# Chapter 37: Distinguished Guests at the Gate (第37章 贵客临门)

—"Not bad. This is indeed [entering the Dao](# "Literal CN: 入道 (rù dào) — attaining sudden enlightenment or breakthrough by aligning with the Dao through specialized mastery or craftsmanship"),"-Lan Pei remarked, gnawing on a roasted chicken leg as he scrutinized Guan Ye.

Because not a single shred of 'renowned physician demeanor' could be detected upon him, all the disciples wore indescribable expressions, glancing at the yellow fox, then back toward their own Master.

—"Not bad,"-Zhou Hui agreed with a gentle smile.

Only then did the other disciples erupt into cheers, showering Guan Ye with heartfelt congratulations:

[—"Fourth Senior Brother, splendidly done!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[—"I knew you could do it!"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

[—"Good food is worth the wait; now that Fourth Junior Brother has crossed this hurdle, there won't be any issues from now on!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan | Literal CN: 好饭不怕晚 — good things take time; late bloomers can achieve great things")

[—"Fourth Senior Brother is the absolute best!"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

[—"Fourth Senior Brother, congratulations."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Everyone chimed in at once. After all, every one of them had witnessed with their own eyes just how grueling Guan Ye's cultivation journey had been; upon catching wind of his breakthrough, they had flocked to their Master's immortal cave at the earliest opportunity. Of course, the news that Eldest Senior Brother had dragged back the Divine Physician Lan Pei and was currently hosting a lavish welcoming banquet inside Master's cave was another inescapable reason they simply had to come.

After a round of congratulations, Qu Qianxing finally asked:

—"How did Fourth Junior Brother manage it? There wasn't the slightest sign during the day, and just now... what did the 'Divine Physician' mean by entering the Dao?"-His utterance of 'Divine Physician' was strained to the extreme.

Only then did Zhou Hui recall that he didn't seem to have ever mentioned the cultivation methodology of 'Entering the Dao through X' to Qu Qianxing.

First, because he didn't know how to do it himself—even if Lan Pei insisted he did—and second, because such [profoundly abstruse](# "Literal CN: 玄之又玄 (xuán zhī yòu xuán) — mystery of mysteries / profoundly abstruse; from the Daodejing") matters were exceedingly difficult to explain clearly and far too prone to breeding blind superstition.

Just as Zhou Hui cleared his throat, preparing to give his disciples a remedial lecture, Lu Shulong spoke up.

—"It's karmic fortune!"-Lu Shulong announced, his eyes still shining brightly as he stared at Guan Ye.

[—"Karmic fortune?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

—"Yes, immense karmic fortune descended upon Fourth Senior Brother!"-Lu Shulong pointed to his pupils, which remained vertical slits.-"I was right there at the time; I saw it with my own eyes!"

—"Fifth Senior Brother, you can actually see karmic fortune?!"-Mo Xi exclaimed in shock.

—"Is this the 'profoundly abstruse' thing you were talking about?"-Zhou Hui asked Lan Pei. Earlier, this wild fox spirit's answer had been so vague and clouded that Zhou Hui had assumed some unknown cosmic entity was at work and had been preparing to investigate thoroughly!

—"Is karmic fortune not profoundly abstruse?"-Lan Pei retorted, his gaze fixed in far greater shock upon Lu Shulong, heedless even of the roasted chicken leg in his paw.-"Where on earth did you pick up this disciple of yours? He's clearly a—"

[—"An egg."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

—"Ah?"-Lan Pei stared dumbly at Yuwen Xuan, who had suddenly cut in.

—"Little Fifth is an egg! He's just an egg!"-Yuwen Xuan insisted emphatically.-"Back when I was very little—probably over a hundred years ago?—I scooped him out from the nest of who-knows-what creature! Master wouldn't let me eat it! He even hatched it!"-Speaking of this, Yuwen Xuan still sounded rather aggrieved.

[It clearly looked so delicious!]

Although even now, Little Fifth still looked exceptionally delicious!

Unaware of his Eldest Senior Brother's lingering culinary designs on him, Lu Shulong puffed his chest out with immense pride:

—"That's right! Master incubated and hatched me himself! He incubated me for over a hundred years; I am Master's biological son!"

—"Get lost!"-Yuwen Xuan glared at him.-"How many times do I have to tell you? First, your surname isn't Zhou; second, Master didn't lay you himself! You're just an egg we picked up!"

—"But Master clearly hatched me,"-Lu Shulong insisted in a small, stubborn voice.

Yet the moment Yuwen Xuan shot him another glare, he immediately shrank behind Guan Ye's back.

Lan Pei stared at this scene with a numb, deadpan expression.

[Fine! An egg it is! Whatever, he's someone else's disciple anyway.]

Zhou Hui was long accustomed to such displays of [fraternal affection and sibling harmony](# "Literal CN: 兄友弟恭 (xiōng yǒu dì gōng) — elder brothers being kind and younger brothers being respectful; classic Confucian ideal of sibling devotion, here used ironically"). Stroking his chin in thought, he drew Guan Ye over for a closer inspection.

He would never comprehend the wild fox's nebulous talk of 'profound and abstruse.' But the moment Shulong uttered the words 'karmic fortune,' Zhou Hui immediately grasped the essence of the matter.

To ordinary cultivators, karmic fortune and the Heavenly Dao were indistinguishable. Yet after cultivating for long enough, one would realize there existed a subtle nuance between the two—likely what Lan Pei meant by 'profound and abstruse.'

If Zhou Hui had to put it into words, the Heavenly Dao was like an Artificial Intelligence—(X) [Artificial Idiot](# "Literal CN: 人工智障 (réngōng zhìzhàng) — playful Chinese netizen derogatory term for flawed, glitchy AI / Artificial Idiocy"), and a poorly trained, thoroughly broken one at that.

Most of the time, It executed fixed subroutines, predetermining the life, death, and destiny of all living beings beneath the heavens, rigorously correcting any deviations from its scripted fate. Yet now and then, overwhelmed by colossal volumes of data, It would spawn massive bugs. Worse, there seemed to be a clumsy, selfish backend developer constantly tampering with the system, patching bugs with even more bugs, leaving one to wonder which day the entire system would suffer a catastrophic full-disk format ([World Calamity](# "Literal CN: 天地大劫 (tiāndì dàjié) — cosmic cataclysm that wipes out epochs in Daoist/Xianxia cosmology")?).

—Of course, he wasn't saying this merely because the Heavenly Dao had scripted a dogshit fate for him.

Karmic fortune, on the other hand, was more akin to an automated reward mechanism—and noticeably fairer.

Whether by accumulating moral merit through good deeds or advancing the civilizational progress of this world, one could obtain a certain measure of karmic fortune. Zhou Hui had merely assumed this parameter only influenced luck; he hadn't anticipated it could be directly converted into [experience points](# "Literal CN: 经验值 (jīngyàn zhí) — gaming concept for EXP").

Thinking along these lines, what 'good deed' or civilizational breakthrough had Guan Ye achieved?

[Running tap water?](# "Literal CN: 自来水 (zìláishuǐ) — modern civil running water / municipal plumbing")

On second thought, that made total sense. In another world, running tap water was an engineering milestone that benefited all mankind. Simply recreating it here was more than enough for Guan Ye to reap a massive windfall of karmic fortune.

Zhou Hui glanced toward the water conduits outside, his determination to have Guan Ye [hand-build a rocket](# "Literal CN: 搓火箭 (cuō huǒjiàn) — modern Chinese netizen slang for hand-crafting or solo-developing a space rocket") in the future burning even brighter.

However, he kept these deductions to himself. Grounding his explanation within the framework understood by the cultivation world, he delivered a concise lecture on the concept of 'Entering the Dao through Technique,' before offering words of encouragement:

—"From this, we see that [Heaven rewards the diligent](# "Literal CN: 天道酬勤 (tiāndào chóuqín) — idiom meaning hard work pays off / providence favors those who persevere"). Keep up the good work!"

—"Yes, Master!"-Guan Ye replied resolutely.

In any case, he had already planned on heading straight back to hammer more water pipes; this revelation merely solidified his determination to hammer pipes.

—"Actually, my own situation doesn't matter much,"-having resolved his own doubts, Guan Ye turned his gaze upon Lan Pei, suddenly recalling what he considered an infinitely more pressing matter.-"Compared to this, has Master Lan Pei completed Master's medical examination? How is Master's condition?"

Instantly, every pair of eyes swiveled back onto Lan Pei.

Lan Pei was just reaching down to retrieve that roasted chicken leg, casually tossing out:

—"Your master has no major issues!"

[—"HUH?!"](# "Speaker: The Seven Direct Disciples in Unison")

A seven-part chorus of disbelief exploded in the immortal cave.

Startled half to death, Lan Pei yanked his claws back, only to find Zhou Hui's disciples glaring at him with ferocious, murderous intensity.

[What is wrong with these little brats?]

[Why do they look like [filial sons](# "Literal CN: 孝子 (xiàozǐ) — here satirical: unfilial sons eager for their father to fall ill so they can perform theatrical filial piety at his deathbed") desperately praying for their old man to fall bedridden so they can display ostentatious filial piety at his deathbed?!]

Yet Master Lan Pei was widely experienced and had seen plenty of such dynamics in prominent aristocratic clans. Moreover, confronting such 'filial sons and virtuous grandsons' was prime harvesting season to milk them for exorbitant fees. His [occupational disease](# "Literal CN: 职业病 (zhíyèbìng) — professional habits / opportunistic reflex") suddenly flared up. Rolling his beady eyes, he hedged:

—"Well... it's not as though he is entirely devoid of illness."

—"Hmph?"-the supposedly bedridden 'old man' let out a frosty grunt.

*Ah, he'd almost forgotten—this one couldn't be offended either!*

Lan Pei scrambled to amend his words in flustered disarray:

—"Failed tribulation, after all! The physical vessel is bound to manifest some adverse indicators—such as hot flushes, profuse sweating, general lethargy, emotional volatility, vertigo, insomnia, declining memory, irregular menstruation, and the like..."

[—"AH?!"](# "Speaker: Everyone in the Cave")

This time, the sound of bewilderment came from every single person in the room.

Lan Pei grabbed a rag from the table to wipe the cold sweat dripping from his brow:

—"In short... the condition is rather intricate. It requires further, more meticulous observation."

—"Then..."-Qu Qianxing pressed, unwilling to drop the inquiry.

—"Speaking of which, we have a genuine patient on our hands—the child of the Plum-Bamboo Immortal Couple,"-Zhou Hui smoothly cut in with a smile.-"He sustained some injuries during the secret realm trial last time. I fear that should anything go awry, it would be difficult to account to the Heavenly Dao Alliance. Would Master Lan Pei be so kind as to examine him?"

—"Let us go together, let us go together!"-Lan Pei piped up without a second's hesitation.

He couldn't endure Zhou Hui's pack of disciples for another second. Nimbly tossing aside his prior stipulation of 'never doing Zhou Hui any favors,' he decisively scurried after Zhou Hui—not forgetting to swipe the final roasted chicken leg from the platter on his way out.

`*`

Corridor—clear!

Outside the window—clear!

In the sky—clear!

*Splendid! The perfect opportunity to infiltrate the secret realm!*

Su Xin wriggled beneath his bed, fishing out the flying sword he had stashed under the bedboards during the day.

Over these past days, he had been nearly bored out of his skull!

Ever since that fateful day in the secret realm when that wicked woman from the Carefree Palace used him as a human shield, leaving him 'severely injured' (?), he had been confined within the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Thankfully, there were no exams during this period, so Su Xin harbored little real resentment; he was simply suffering from sheer boredom.

The most infuriating part was that he was the sole 'severely wounded' casualty forbidden from leaving the Meditation Hall. He could only watch with envious eyes as the other disciples quarantined in the hall were discharged to gallivant wherever they pleased. And as if roaming freely weren't enough, they kept chattering incessantly about the Library Pavilion and Immortal Chess, driving his curiosity to an unbearable itch. Then, just when he was finally permitted to visit the Library Pavilion, everyone began raving about the secret realm—claiming it had been redeveloped into something far more thrilling than Immortal Chess!

Su Xin could no longer lie flat! He already considered Immortal Chess wildly entertaining—what a pity he had missed the tournament earlier, though he had already purchased dozens of championship-lineup piece sets and standard game boards: one set for his personal collection, one set for everyday play, and dozens more to [evangelize](# "Literal CN: 传教 (chuánjiào) — proselytize / spread the gospel (X) promote") the game upon his return to the Heavenly Dao Alliance. How mind-blowingly fun must the secret realm be?!

As the most stylish, dazzling [golden boy](# "Literal CN: 最靓的崽 (zuì liàng de zǎi) — Cantonese/internet slang for the coolest, most fashionable and charismatic kid") in the entire Heavenly Dao Alliance, how could he possibly not have visited it?!

Otherwise, once he returned to the Northern Domain, what if someone among his crew of degenerate friends caught wind of the secret realm and asked him what it was like, only for him to admit that he had been pinned full of needles like a porcupine and seen absolutely nothing? Wouldn't that be [lame as hell](# "Literal CN: 逊毙了 (xùn bì le) — Taiwanese/internet slang for utterly uncool, completely pathetic")?!

He had to go!

Yet this time, his demands had been flatly denied. Fearing he might stumble into further trouble if he wandered about, the Heavenly Spirit Sect adamantly refused to let him roam. Word had it they had even summoned Master Lan Pei to examine him, and that his parents were arriving as well.

The Foremost Divine Physician Lan Pei? He had heard of him—just some old geezer who had lived for tens of thousands of years. What was there to see?!

Su Xin had zero desire to see any doctor. His thoughts were consumed entirely by how to sneak out to the secret realm. Unfortunately, he wasn't a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, so the Sundial Clock offered no intra-sect teleportation for him; he had to travel on his own.

Fortunately, Su Xin was a seasoned veteran when it came to evading adult supervision to run wild outside. Citing 'rehabilitation exercises' as an excuse, he reclaimed his custom flying sword forged by renowned masters—equipped with [auto-pathfinding](# "Literal CN: 自动寻路 (zìdòng xúnlù) — modern gaming feature: auto-navigation") (X) automatic waypoint tracking for places he had previously visited. He then spent days monitoring the guard disciples' shift schedules—hardly a difficult task, as the Sundial Clock kept most Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples operating on strict, predictable routines. At last, he designated tonight as prison break night!

—"Brother, tonight everything depends on you!"-Su Xin whispered beneath the bed, stroking his flying sword.

He was confident that with this sword's velocity, once he took to the skies, nobody could ever catch him.

Except when he clutched the sword and laboriously squirmed out from beneath the bedboards, he found eighteen pairs of eyes staring straight down at him.

`*`

—"Notify the Plum-Bamboo couple to come,"-Master Lan Pei said, shaking his head at Zhou Hui.-"This child is [incurably sick in the vitals](# "Literal CN: 病入膏肓 (bìngrù gāohuāng) — idiom meaning illness has entered the vitals; terminally ill / beyond all hope"); he is beyond saving."

`*`

Several days later.

Heavenly Spirit Sect, [Chen hour](# "Literal CN: 辰时 (chénshí) — traditional Chinese two-hour period from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM").

When the chimes of the Sundial Clock resonated across the peaks, Gan Tang had long finished washing and dressing. Having meticulously packed the textbooks and stationery required for today's lecture, the instant the first chime rang out, she activated teleportation without a moment's hesitation.

The very next second, she materialized before her desk inside the Palace of Silence classroom.

Chen hour, first quarter (7:15 AM) marked the commencement of the morning's first lecture; consequently, most disciples in the Teaching Assistant Class arrived at this precise juncture.

As the seven chimes echoed in sequence, figures appeared one after another at their respective seats.

Yet by no means was everyone as immaculately prepared as Gan Tang. Some bore heavy dark circles under their eyes, looking as though they had [cultivated immortality all night](# "Literal CN: 修仙一整晚 (xiūxiān yī zhěng wǎn) — modern Chinese internet slang: 'cultivating immortality' = pulling an all-nighter / staying awake all night"); some were disheveled, clearly having just tumbled out of bed; some were hunched over their desks scribbling furiously, rushing to finish the homework due today; and one disciple arrived carrying an enormous pouch, shouting:

[—"Who ordered milk tea?"](# "Speaker: Shen Nian'er")

—"Me! I ordered brown sugar fresh milk and a [shredded scallion pancake](# "Literal CN: 手抓饼 (shǒuzhuābǐng) — flaky, savory layered flatbread popular as a Taiwanese/Chinese breakfast street food")!"-Gan Tang hurried to her feet.

[—"Over here, my mango pomelo sago—!"](# "Speaker: Anonymous Classmate")

Instantly, half the classroom swarmed forward.

There was simply no helping it: Delicacy Pavilion—the Heavenly Spirit Sect's cafeteria—was downright atrocious. Even their plain white congee was somehow simultaneously undercooked and scorched, producing an unbearable taste. To make matters worse, Old Huang, head chef of Delicacy Pavilion, defended his atrocious cooking with shameless sophistry:

—"If I made the food too delicious, you'd cling to mortal attachments and refuse to [fast](# "Literal CN: 辟谷 (bìgǔ) — Daoist inedia; abstaining from grain and mortal food to purify the body")—would that not be entirely my fault?!"

Given that logic, the disciples genuinely preferred to skip meals whenever humanly possible.

However, not long ago, Junior Sister Mo Bei's shop, "[A Cup of Sorrow](# "Literal CN: 一悲茶 (Yī Bēi Chá) — Mo Bei's tea shop punning on her name 'Bei' (悲, sorrow) and 'a cup of tea' (一杯茶, yī bēi chá), riffing on modern Chinese boba chains like 'Yi Dian Dian' or 'A Little Tea'")," had opened for business at Qianxing Hall, ushering in salvation for disciples who had yet to achieve inedia.

'A Cup of Sorrow' specialized in novel milk teas that no one had ever tasted before. Not only did they taste sublime, but the tea leaves were meticulously selected supreme-grade spiritual tea (Mo Xi: "..."), while the milk was sourced from spiritual beasts raised right here in the sect, offering significant benefits to cultivation. The moment it launched, it earned universal acclaim; some loose cultivators even skipped entering the secret realm altogether just to savor the tea.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples patronized the shop in droves. Unfortunately, Qianxing Hall was simply too far; even with the Sundial Clock, coordinating schedules proved difficult. The disciples petitioned Delicacy Pavilion to sell milk tea as well—until Old Huang seized the opportunity to debut his newest dark culinary abomination: a milk tea that tasted like pot-washing water dubbed 'Blackberry Fruit Tea,' colloquially known as '[Dark Pot Tea](# "Literal CN: 黑锅茶 (hēi guō chá) — pun on black pot / taking the blame (背黑锅) and burnt pot-scrubbing water")'. Through sheer offensive capability, Old Huang crushed the petition campaign in its tracks, extinguishing everyone's hopes forever.

Naturally, the disciples' yearning for palatable food could never be suppressed.

From that day forward, whenever disciples entered the secret realm during the wee hours of the morning, they were entrusted with breakfast delivery duties, ferrying over a dozen orders daily—and that was merely the quota for the Teaching Assistant Class! One could easily imagine the staggering sales volume once the grand recruitment concluded at the end of the eighth month and the entire sect commenced regular classes.

Gan Tang was one such patron.

However, feeling that drinking tea first thing in the morning was ill-advised, she opted for pure fresh milk instead.

As the sweet, creamy milk washed over her tongue, Gan Tang felt that her day had officially begun.

Over on the other side, Shen Nian'er—the disciple tasked with today's milk tea courier run—finally had a free moment after distributing the beverages to inspect the room. She turned to Gan Tang in confusion:

—"Does the classroom look bigger than usual today?"

In truth, the change was glaringly obvious.

On ordinary days, this classroom could accommodate precisely thirty-three disciples—the exact headcount of the Teaching Assistant Class. Now, however, an expansive clearing had appeared at the rear of the hall, furnished with several jade chairs that looked rather conspicuous.

—"Could something have gone wrong again?"-Shen Nian'er muttered, frowning.

Small wonder she jumped to that conclusion; the Palace of Silence was currently undergoing extensive renovations.

—Though Mo Xi had relied on illusion techniques to weather the sect exam back then, in order to accommodate diverse exam (X) pedagogical requirements, the Palace of Silence still required comprehensive structural remodeling. Consequently, renovation efforts had been underway in sporadic phases over the past several days.

Following the Grand Unified Examination, Zhou Hui had selected a cohort of student-instructors and commenced advanced pedagogy lectures ahead of schedule, necessitating a temporary classroom for these disciples.

Yet due to the ongoing construction, the classroom inevitably suffered bizarre anomalies.

For instance, during the inscription of spatial arrays last time, the classroom had warped into a triangle. The space beyond the doorway led to who-knew-where; at first, the shrill wails of a female ghost could even be heard weeping outside, ceasing only after the Immortal Venerable began his lecture.

—"Not this time,"-Gan Tang replied, taking a sip of fresh milk.-"I heard it's a '[Classroom Observation](# "Literal CN: 教学参观 (jiàoxué cānguān) — teaching observation / classroom inspection; school inspection practice of visitors sitting in the back to observe instruction").'"

—"A classroom observation?"-Shen Nian'er blinked in bewilderment.

—"Just how many days have you been away?"-another disciple piped up.-"Don't you know? It's not just the Foremost Divine Physician Lan Pei—the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Plum-Bamboo Immortal Couple and Array Master Ran Zhen have all arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"

—"I know about Master Lan Pei; when he visited the Meditation Hall for consultations a week ago, I even went to join the crowd! When did the other powerhouses arrive?"-Shen Nian'er wore an expression of [what year is it anyway](# "Literal CN: 今夕是何年 (jīn xī shì hé nián) — classical quote turned modern internet meme: 'what year is tonight?' expressing total disorientation from being out of the loop").-"I only grinded an all-nighter in the secret realm last night!"

—"Just last night,"-Gan Tang explained in her unhurried cadence.-"Which is why Senior Brother Qu issued a notice last night stating that our visiting guests wished to observe Master's teaching methods. They will be sitting in on our class today—what is termed a 'classroom observation.'"

—"Heavens! You all had the leisure to order milk tea, yet not a single person mentioned this to me?!"-Panic flared across Shen Nian'er's face as she frantically patted down her robes.-"With so many supreme powerhouses coming, at least allow me to dress myself properly!"

—"The notice explicitly stated that our ordinary state would suffice,"-Gan Tang consoled her.

Unfortunately, that offered little comfort to Shen Nian'er.

Those were two Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses and two Mahayana grandmasters! The Plum-Bamboo couple were at Tribulation Transcendence, while Lan Pei and Ran Zhen were at the Mahayana stage. To mere Foundation Establishment disciples like them, such figures were celestial beings akin to distant stars.

It was only because they had grown accustomed to seeing the Untiring Immortal Venerable in class lately; in the past, had any of them been granted the privilege of speaking a single word to the Venerable, they would have raced home to offer thanks before their ancestral tablets!

Yet frantic distress availed nothing now; class was about to begin.

And unfortunate souls like Shen Nian'er were hardly few and far between. In a cultivation sect, disciples generally enjoyed tremendous autonomy—some ventured into the secret realm for trials, while others engaged in closed-door seclusion. Even Qu Qianxing could hardly drag every single disciple out to notify them individually. Consequently, over half the disciples in the classroom had never received the announcement, greeting this 'classroom observation' in a thoroughly [raw, unvarnished state](# "Literal CN: 原生态 (yuánshēngtài) — original ecology / raw and unvarnished; uncurated and unprepared").

`*`

In truth, Zhou Hui couldn't care less whether his disciples were 'raw and unvarnished.'

After all, the Plum-Bamboo couple hadn't decided on a 'classroom observation' on an idle whim.

Their precious darling was here. They had originally intended to send subordinates to collect him; upon learning that Divine Physician Lan Pei was also arriving, they felt such an opportunity could not be missed, opting to travel to the Heavenly Spirit Sect in person.

Thus, with the sole exception of Array Master Ran Zhen, the arrival of these supreme powerhouses had been known to Zhou Hui from the very start.

Had Zhou Hui truly wished to roll out the red carpet for these 'distinguished guests of the Heavenly Dao Alliance,' he could easily have suspended classes ten days in advance, barred access to secret realm trials, dressed up every disciple in fine attire, and lined them up along the mountain gate to put on a lavish display of pomp and ceremony.

Yet no matter how many millennia he lived, Zhou Hui harbored zero interest in such colossal wastes of time.

Were guests important? Important.

Were they more important than class? Absolutely not.

Even if the heavens rained flying knives, that was no excuse to cancel class!

If there were guests who simply had to be entertained, then they could sit in on the lecture as auditors.

Such was the true origin of this 'classroom observation.'

Thus, at Chen hour, first quarter, Zhou Hui stepped into the Teaching Assistant Class precisely on time, lesson plans in hand.

Simultaneously, a throng of grand powerhouses rustled in, packing the rear of the classroom.

Aside from Master Lan Pei who was a true lone wolf, the Plum-Bamboo couple and Array Master Ran Zhen had each brought their disciples and retinues.

Given their lofty realms, even their grand-disciples started at the Soul Formation stage. In contrast, the children in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Teaching Assistant Class were mostly at Foundation Establishment, with a few Qi Condensation disciples mixed in. Even though the titans restrained their spiritual pressure, the fledgling students still shivered under the suffocating aura of so many masters.

Worse still, the subject Zhou Hui was lecturing on today happened to be the most foundational cultivation techniques, causing the disciples to worry that their elementary curriculum would appear utterly beneath contempt in front of these supreme seniors.

None of them dared look back. Had they done so, they would have discovered that those grand powerhouses were listening with rapt, spellbound fascination.

Several were not merely scribbling frantic notes, but conversing excitedly through divine sense transmissions:

—"As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable! To expound upon such rudimentary knowledge with such profound innovation! I've harbored a bottleneck since Foundation Establishment that I couldn't even properly identify—yet today, I unexpectedly received the answer."

—"Truly worthy of Untiring! In this mortal realm, nobody possesses firmer fundamentals than him!"

—"Preaching such wisdom to these little brats is truly casting pearls before swine."

—"Indeed. The Immortal Venerable has even deliberately throttled the ambient spiritual energy flow; otherwise, I feel I could advance an entire stage right here on the spot."

—"You're already at your realm and can still advance! If Untiring didn't deliberately suppress it and lectured these brats like this every day, they would have detonated from spiritual overload ages ago!"

Indeed: a lecture delivered by someone of Zhou Hui's caliber was, in essence, a process of bestowing spiritual nourishment back upon Heaven and Earth.

Legend held that at the dawn of creation, it was precisely because True Immortals ascended the dais to preach the Dao that the hierarchy of cultivation realms was established. While later cultivators were not quite so miraculous, upon attaining the Mahayana stage, ascending the pulpit to teach was still potent enough to turn wild foxes into spirits, grant sentience to flora, and elevate low-tier cultivators by several minor stages—to say nothing of a Tribulation Transcendence titan.

However, such cosmic fortune was rare beyond measure, and without sufficient cultivation, one could not indulge in it recklessly.

If the massive spiritual energy absorbed from a single sermon were not assimilated in time, attending another would, at best, damage one's spirit roots, and at worst, cause one to detonate and perish.

It was entirely thanks to Zhou Hui's supreme control—forcibly throttling the volume of spiritual energy down to the exact threshold his disciples could safely absorb—that these fledgling students remained unscathed, thriving in the optimal learning state.

Furthermore, this cohort selected as student-instructors might not be the most naturally gifted in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but they were without doubt the most patient, grounded, and focused minds in the sect.

Take Shen Nian'er, for example: for the first five minutes, she fretted constantly over whether she might lose her composure, feeling the gazes of the titans behind her prickling like daggers against her back. Five minutes later, she was wholly immersed in Zhou Hui's lecture, reluctant even to touch her stationery for fear of missing a single syllable, only lifting her brush in frantic haste when Zhou Hui signaled them to take notes.

As for the grand powerhouses in the rear, she forgot their very existence—recalling them only when the lecture transitioned into the concluding Q&A session.

Zhou Hui typically reserved a quarter-hour at the end of class to resolve students' queries:

—"That will be all for today. Are there any points that remain unclear?"-he asked, concluding the lecture as he did every day.

The disciples were evidently still digesting the day's lesson, and for a moment, nobody raised a hand. Instead, a booming roar like a rampaging bear suddenly shook the hall:

[—"ME!"](# "Speaker: Void Refinement Cultivator from Heavenly Dao Alliance")

Shen Nian'er's ears rang violently. She couldn't help a small complaint in her heart: *Didn't Master say to raise your hand if you had a question?*

Only then did she realize that the sound had originated from the very back of the classroom.

The disciples stole furtive glances backward, discovering that the speaker was a Void Refinement cultivator from the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Bowing respectfully to Zhou Hui, the cultivator petitioned:

—"Immortal Venerable Untiring, might I invite other disciples from the Heavenly Dao Alliance to come 'observe and study' as well?"-In truth, he desperately wished to attend in person, but lacked the thick skin to say so, using junior disciples as a convenient pretext.

The others saw right through his transparent ploy. Array Master Ran Zhen chuckled:

—"The Heavenly Dao Alliance truly is cunning! Trying to [freeload on classes](# "Literal CN: 蹭课 (cèng kè) — modern Chinese university slang: auditing/crashing a lecture without enrolling")—Untiring, I also have several disciples of lower cultivation on hand. Since they have nothing better to do during this period, why not permit them to sit in as well?"

[He only wanted to crash a single session; you're planning to leech the entire semester!]

Lan Pei sneered inwardly at Ran Zhen, though secretly regretting that he hadn't dragged along a few grand-disciples of his own.

—"I naturally welcome academic exchange,"-Zhou Hui replied without the slightest objection.-"Should any of you be interested, you are welcome to audit at any time throughout your stay at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. If you could also offer a bit of guidance to my disciples along the way, that would be all the better."

Hearing this, with the exception of the few standing at the very pinnacle, the visiting cultivators beamed with undisguised excitement—they had conveniently filtered out the Immortal Venerable's final condition—even those at Void Refinement and Body Integration!

Witnessing this spectacle, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples suddenly suffered a jolt of epiphany.

Some clutched their textbooks tightly against their chests; others locked their gazes fiercely upon Zhou Hui, looking for all the world like children terrified their teacher was about to be snatched away.

And when Zhou Hui asked once more if there were any further questions, virtually the entire class shot their hands into the air, clamoring out in unison, resolutely refusing to give those so-called 'grand powerhouses' in the back another inch of speaking room!
