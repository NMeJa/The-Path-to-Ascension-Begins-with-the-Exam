# Chapter 45: New School Uniforms (第45章 新校服)

Regrettably, the Beginner Class's new apparel could not be called identical to the sword cultivators'—they simply had zero relationship.

The Beginner Class attire centered around a moon-white short tunic. The collar was embroidered with an auspicious cloud motif framing the character for "Beginner" (`初`). Across the shoulders, a slender brown leather strap looped behind the back of the neck and hung down along both sides of the chest, tipped with silk tassels. This thin strap was imbued with magic, capable of stretching indefinitely: when necessary, it could be used to roll up and bind sleeves, or hang small magical tools. As for trousers, disciples could choose from three colors: moon-white, light beige, or black.

Because these were autumn and winter garments, each student also received a beige [bijia](# "Literal CN: 比甲 (bǐjiǎ) — a traditional sleeveless, collarless Chinese vest/tunic worn over robes") (with customizable length), a grey-blue straight-collared front-buttoning 80%-sleeve overcoat, and an azure-sky cotton-padded winter coat featuring a rabbit-fur collar trim and 80%-sleeves. All of them were embroidered on the back with the pattern of the Full Moon over Heaven-Cleaving Chasm amid Auspicious Clouds.

In addition, the base version of the Beginner Class belt and wrist cuff bindings were black. However, if a disciple accumulated a certain number of credit hours in a specialized elective course, or passed a vocational skill exam, they could earn corresponding colored belts and bindings:
- Sword Cultivators: Scarlet
- Spell Cultivators: [Snow-Cyan](# "Literal CN: 雪青色 (xuěqīngsè) — a traditional Chinese poetic color, a delicate pale lavender or lilac-blue")
- Array Masters: Bright Yellow
- Alchemists: Pine Green
- Spirit Plant Masters: Peacock Green
- Artifact Refinement Masters: [Dark Indigo](# "Literal CN: 黛色 (dàisè) — a deep bluish-black / dark indigo traditional ink color")
- Talisman Masters: Cinnabar Red

...and so forth. Furthermore, the materials varied slightly, each bearing distinct patterns said to confer minor auxiliary buffs to the respective profession.

—"This is completely different from what I imagined..."

Cheng Ziyi slumped over the desk once again, as though every bone in his body had been sucked out.

Still, he clutched his sword tightly against his chest.

After all, the Beginner Class was temporary. If he gave up now, he would never get to wear that drop-dead handsome sword cultivator set.

—"Is it? I think it looks very nice!"-Jing Chenjun said, adjusting the ties at his cuffs.

Having been born into the imperial family, Jing Chenjun's aesthetic taste had been cultivated since childhood.

When he had seen the sword cultivators' attire earlier, he had thought that while the outfit was undeniably dashing, he would be in serious trouble if they actually issued him one. Jing Chenjun possessed full self-awareness: his personal aura was scholarly and delicate, and his height still had plenty of room to grow. Wearing that brilliant scarlet robe would look utterly absurd on him—ridiculously foolish.

This current moon-white set was wonderful: it suited everyone, and was light and practical.

—"Indeed, I've never worn clothes this fine before!"-Ji Gen said, stroking the fabric with utmost care:—"It's just that the color of this tunic gets dirty easily. Ruining it while working the fields would be terrible."

He had naturally chosen the black trousers.

—"Once you master the Dust-Cleansing Spell, you won't need to fear dirt,"-Huang Xiao offered advice from the side:—"Besides, if you want to care for your garments, there are several other minor spells right in the spell textbook. They're very simple—just read over them a couple of times and you'll get it."

Listening from the side, Jing Chenjun felt a sudden pang of guilt.

He had tried.

Without spirit roots, he naturally couldn't cast any spells whatsoever.

Fortunately, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's curriculum was remarkably abundant. Beyond hardline cultivation classes like spellcasting and meditation, there were pure theoretical courses like mathematical classics and grammar, and even physics and chemistry courses Jing Chenjun had never heard of before. Accumulating credits, his GPA was still sufficient. It was just that upon reaching the Intermediate Class, he probably wouldn't be able to choose practical spiritual energy courses.

Just as Jing Chenjun was furrowing his brows in thought, someone suddenly asked:

[—"You can't even learn such a tiny spell?"](# "Speaker: Cheng Ziyi")

A chill ran down the back of Jing Chenjun's neck. Fortunately, through his trials of traveling to sects and seeking immortality, he had long learned how to mask his emotions—though he couldn't fake spirit roots, so long as his expression remained composed, most cultivators wouldn't probe his background to his face. Striving to keep a calm face, he looked toward the voice, only to see Cheng Ziyi eyeing him with an inquisitive, probing gaze.

*Had he truly been found out?*

[Living together and attending classes every day, my inability to cast spells was bound to be discovered sooner or later anyway,] Jing Chenjun thought, a bitter taste rising in his mouth. He had been mentally prepared, yet when the moment actually arrived, wave after wave of nausea washed over him.

Especially since the first to notice was the sharp-tongued, sarcastic Cheng Ziyi.

[What is he going to say?]

Fortunately, Cheng Ziyi didn't think in the direction of a mortal at all, only asking:

—"You're obviously quite sharp when learning other things. Could it be that you have [trash Five-Element Spirit Roots](# "Literal CN: 五灵根的废灵根 (wǔ línggēn de fèi línggēn) — having all five elemental roots, resulting in extremely slow and diluted cultivation in traditional xianxia"), which is why you can't learn such a tiny spell?"

—"..."

—"In that case, you'd better head to the shop at Tianyi Workshop and buy fixed talismans,"-Cheng Ziyi suggested instead.

Jing Chenjun looked at Cheng Ziyi in surprise, finding no trace of the contempt he had braced for.

Cheng Ziyi even seemed worried that Jing Chenjun wouldn't understand, pulling a talisman from his storage pouch to demonstrate:

—"Like this Dust-Cleansing Talisman. It doesn't require any spiritual energy at all; you just slap it directly onto your clothes."

As he spoke, he slapped it straight onto his left shoulder. Instantly, a white pattern of a horsetail whisk and drifting mist appeared on the shoulder of his moon-white tunic, matching the garment quite nicely.

—"I don't know what standard Tianyi Workshop produces, but this one of mine can keep clothes clean for half a month!"-Cheng Ziyi said.

Jing Chenjun's lips twitched. After a long pause, he finally asked:

—"You didn't buy that at Tianyi Workshop?"

—"No, I brought it from home. My clan has an ancestral heritage of talisman crafting,"-Cheng Ziyi said with a darkening face:—"I couldn't learn that crap, which is why I ran away from... ahem, no! I came to the Heavenly Spirit Sect out of pure admiration for the Untiring Immortal Venerable!"

[Fine, whatever you say!]

A genuine smile appeared on Jing Chenjun's face. He said softly:

—"Actually, I think you're quite suited for drawing talismans."

With Cheng Ziyi's atrocious calligraphy, he didn't even need to practice—his normal handwriting already looked like talisman scribbles.

—"Hmph! My goal is to become a sword cultivator!"-Oblivious to Jing Chenjun's deeper meaning, Cheng Ziyi hugged his sword smugly:—"Once I finish my studies and return home, they'll finally realize what a peerless genius they drove away!"

[—"Still, even with Five-Element Spirit Roots, practicing spells more often is better."](# "Speaker: Huang Xiao")

At this moment, Ji Gen and Huang Xiao looked over as well.

Sitting nearby, they had naturally overheard the conversation between Cheng Ziyi and Jing Chenjun, and evidently held no prejudice against Jing Chenjun's "trash spirit roots."

In fact, if Cheng Ziyi hadn't brought it up, they probably wouldn't have thought anything of it.

Ji Gen originated from a mortal tribe in the Central Domain. In his tribe, mortals could use vines as a medium to perform feats similar to cultivators; thus, Ji Gen still had no self-awareness as a "cultivator" and didn't consider spirit roots anything special—in his mind, Jing Chenjun couldn't cast spells simply because he didn't have plants growing on his body!

Meanwhile, Huang Xiao was a beastman. Demonic beasts had no concept of spirit roots, only monster cores. All monster cores possessed a single attribute, equivalent to a human's Heavenly Spirit Root; hence, demonic beasts cultivated faster than humans. Conversely, from a beastman's perspective, was there any fundamental difference between Three-Element Spirit Roots and Five-Element Spirit Roots? Except for Heavenly Spirit Roots, all humans were weaklings in the early stages! If there was any discrimination, it was discrimination against all humanity.

Naturally, Huang Xiao didn't discriminate against humans; he merely felt that Jing Chenjun being a "trash spirit root" proved his initial observation: this kid really belonged to the category in his species who was tiny, weak, and malnourished—the type who couldn't digest spiritual energy even when fed directly into his mouth! He should take extra care of him whenever possible in the future!

This sort of sympathy had existed ever since Jing Chenjun ate in dainty, tiny bites. Jing Chenjun was long used to it and felt zero offense, only thinking Huang Xiao was rather endearingly rustic.

Demonic beast Huang Xiao, however, was the only one to raise standard cultivation orthodoxy:

—"Even if you aren't good at spells, practicing a few more times and getting proficient is always good."

—"..."

[The problem isn't that I'm not good at it—I literally can't learn it at all!]

Jing Chenjun gave a wry smile, unable to tell the truth to his kind-hearted roommates.

—"Practice, practice, practice! If someone can't learn it, what's the use of practicing!"-Cheng Ziyi snapped in sudden irritation, Huang Xiao's words apparently triggering some deeply unpleasant memory:—"Besides, is using spells absolutely mandatory? I bet you that set Teacher Gan wears is maintained entirely with talismans!"

Hearing him say that, several of them glanced toward the other side of the classroom.

They were chatting here because it was class recess.

Today featured two consecutive Cultivation Method classes. After class ended, Teacher Gan Tang hadn't left; instead, she had been mobbed by a crowd of students asking questions.

Speaking of which, the Beginner Class curriculum was worth mentioning.

For Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, the new class schedule was quite novel.

Generally, during every [ten-day cycle](# "Literal CN: 旬 (xún) — a traditional Chinese 10-day period; a month comprises three xun (early, mid, late)"), five mornings were dedicated to theoretical lectures—after all, disciples needed time for cultivation, drills, and chores. Compared to other sects that held lectures once every ten days or half a month, this class load was already quite heavy. Furthermore, students who couldn't cultivate like Jing Chenjun could elect subjects like physics and chemistry. In any case, if you wanted to [involute](# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn) — hyper-competitive academic grinding"), you never had to worry about lacking classes to take at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Beginner Class disciples did not formally take a master; each course was taught by a different instructor.

For instance, "Teacher Gan"—Gan Tang—whom Cheng Ziyi mentioned was the Cultivation Method instructor for their class.

This newly promoted Foundation Establishment teacher received rave reviews from students.

Whether it was because she had just recently achieved Foundation Establishment or because she had received personal pedagogical training from the Untiring Immortal Venerable—most agreed it was the latter, since other student instructors also taught remarkably well—Gan Tang's lectures were simple and easy to understand, lacking the "profound, abstruse, and nebulous" lecture style common in cultivation sects. At least Cheng Ziyi, who hailed from another sect, stated: "Compared to Teacher Gan, every lecture I heard in the past was complete bullsh*t!" Qi Condensation disciples could quickly grasp concepts, and even Jing Chenjun, who lacked spirit roots, listened with great interest—though the more he listened, the more despair he felt, as he clearly understood the fundamental divide between himself and cultivators.

Naturally, the instructors were not left out of the uniform distribution.

As an instructor and an Intermediate Class disciple, Gan Tang received two sets of garments.

The Intermediate Class uniform was an upgraded version of the Beginner Class:
The short tunic was replaced by a long robe, or one could choose a combination of a short shirt and a pleated skirt. Additionally, what set the Intermediate Class buzzing was that at the end of the year, there would be a Sect Grand Examination: the top student in each subject would receive a cloak in their specialty's color, while the #1 overall student across the entire Intermediate Class would receive a silver-white cloak identical to the Immortal Venerable's own!

Gan Tang was determined to win it!

Meanwhile, the Instructor Edition uniform was a sweet burden for Gan Tang.

That outfit was completely different from the Intermediate and Beginner sets: it was a traditional wide-sleeved Daoist robe typically worn by orthodox cultivators.

Furthermore, as an instructor, the color was not restricted; as a faculty benefit, one could custom-order their preferred color from Tianyi Workshop, request desired embroidered patterns, and even select up to seven powerful defensive and auxiliary spell matrices that were two realms higher than the instructor's own cultivation! The upper limit existed primarily because spell matrices exceeding cultivation by too much couldn't be activated—though those with extraordinary talent could request tailors to stitch matrices up to their personal limit. This was essentially a free magical treasure—and one that could be upgraded for free as one's cultivation rose!

The only problem was that maintaining one's image in daily life while wearing this magical treasure was excruciatingly difficult.

The wide-sleeved robe looked supremely ethereal and celestial, but wearing it while teaching was an absolute nuisance: it was troublesome when writing, troublesome when writing on the blackboard, and even troublesome when lecturing:

Walking around while controlling the jade waist pendants so they wouldn't clink and make noise, disrupting the lecture, was a maneuver of extreme difficulty.

Yet Zhou Hui specifically demanded [Teacher Exemplar](# "Literal CN: 为人师表 (wéirénshībiǎo) — acting as a paragon / role model for students, embodying dignity and decorum"), which was why he had mandated this instructor attire.

Cultivation method teachers like Gan Tang actually had it comparatively easy; word had it that for the physics experiment class, it was an utter catastrophe... practically turning into a sect-wide horror story.

Cheng Ziyi had brought up Gan Tang as an example because he remembered that very incident.

Initially, seeing the instructors arrive for class, the disciples had thought they looked dashing and celestial in a completely different way from sword cultivators—the very ideal of an immortal master. But after that catastrophe, it had flipped into the exact opposite of ideal.

Gan Tang, however, had done well so far and had not lost face before her students due to operational blunders.

Cheng Ziyi, however, claimed to see through it all:

—"Dressing like that to teach us, guess how many talismans she slapped onto her clothes? If she used spells, that combination of spells would completely drain her Foundation Establishment cultivation dry!"

—"...Is it really impossible to use neither spells nor talismans?"-Jing Chenjun asked timidly:—"Can't one simply rely on etiquette and poise to maintain one's posture?"

[I think I could manage that with a bit of effort...] Jing Chenjun thought.

[After all, if one is to be an emperor (of mortals), isn't maintaining regal poise a basic requirement?]

To his surprise, his fellow roommates looked at him in shock:

[—"Are you sure that's called etiquette, and not torture?"](# "Speakers: Cheng Ziyi, Ji Gen & Huang Xiao")

***

Naturally, Gan Tang had no interest in torturing herself, but she hadn't used talismans either.

That was right—she used spells.

She cast Dust-Cleansing Spell on her gown, Silence Spell on the jade pendants at her waist, and [Pass-Through Spell](# "Literal CN: 穿模（X）无障术 (chuānmó / wúzhàngshù) — a gaming joke on 'clipping through models' (穿模) crossed out and replaced by the cultivation term 'Barrierless / Pass-Through Spell' (无障术) so wide sleeves don't knock things over") on her wide sleeves. Maintaining this suite of minor spells perfectly drained all the spiritual energy a Foundation Establishment cultivator could store.

However, continuously maintaining active spells was beneficial both for her personal cultivation and for the world at large.

Every time her spiritual energy was emptied out, it forced her dantian to more aggressively absorb ambient qi and condense spiritual energy. Once that became a habit, her dantian would store more spiritual energy than others, her mana would become more condensed, and her spellcasting speed would be faster.

On the other hand, the process of absorbing ambient qi, condensing mana, and casting spells actually enhanced the circulation of spiritual energy around her, refining and purifying spiritual energy to feed back into heaven and earth while making the surrounding environment richer in qi.

Such an excellent practice was rarely taught or practiced outside of Zhou Hui's lineage.

For one simple reason: it was far too difficult.

Although the Dust-Cleansing Spell was a minor spell, it was momentary, not continuous. To keep it active, one had to constantly channel mana and regulate its intensity, which was equivalent to multitasking. Adding several other spells on top made maintenance exponentially harder.

And that was merely the difficulty of spell execution.

What made cultivators even more uncomfortable was the psychological sensation of an empty dantian.

The sensation was subtle: no pain, no itch. But from the moment a cultivator stepped from mortal into Qi Condensation, they gained the ability to commune with heaven and earth—manifested not only in spellcasting, but also in perception. The moment spiritual energy ran empty, they reverted to mortal senses: like suddenly losing one's eyes and going blind, losing one's ears and going deaf, or a modern person losing their smartphone and unable to access the internet... utterly distressing and anxious.

Compared to this anxiety, being in a mana-depleted state where one couldn't retaliate against a surprise ambush was actually a minor worry.

Therefore, out of pure instinct, cultivators stockpiled massive amounts of spiritual energy in their dantians at all times until full. Even if they knew expending a bit would benefit their cultivation, they would still act like someone seeing their phone battery dip into the red line, obsessively scrambling to recharge.

Gan Tang was the same.

In fact, after doing so today, she had spaced out several times during class, resulting in quite a few students gathering around her after class to ask questions.

Seven, eight... fourteen?

[Did that many people fail to understand a single repeated question? I can't space out next time; I must improve my teaching method for this topic.]

Gan Tang made a note in her lesson plan.

She knew full well that being selected as an instructor with her modest cultivation, and enjoying private lectures from the Immortal Venerable just like direct disciples, was meant to help these new students learn more. She could not fail the Immortal Venerable's expectations.

Still, Gan Tang did not stop maintaining the spells.

Upon discovering that these spells "just happened" to drain her spiritual energy completely, she suspected this was an intentional "trial" from the Immortal Venerable, intended to integrate cultivation into daily life.

What was more, she had an even more important objective:

Saving money!

Indeed! Talismans cost spirit stones! Although talismans for minor spells were cheap, small amounts accumulated into massive expenses!

As an instructor, Gan Tang was no longer poor. Her monthly stipend was quite generous by Heavenly Spirit Sect standards: five medium-grade spirit stones, three hundred low-grade spirit stones, ten bottles of pills, and fifty selectable crafting materials. But having grown up as an impoverished rogue cultivator, she was used to poverty. Since she could use spells, talismans were an avoidable expense, and she refused to waste money on them.

Of course, Gan Tang wasn't purely stingy; in areas where she truly desired something, she was more than willing to spend.

After finishing her class and dismissing the questioning disciples, she headed straight for her destination: Tianyi Pavilion.

***

As the most popular department recently, the front of Tianyi Pavilion was packed with people.

Beyond disciples coming to collect their new uniforms, many disciples who had already received theirs beforehand, like Gan Tang, were loitering around, making the courtyard feel cramped.

To handle the massive crowd, Tianyi Pavilion had implemented a ticketing and queuing system. Even so, it couldn't deter the enthusiastic disciples—merely getting a ticket required queuing.

Several Tianyi Pavilion disciples looking half-dead stood by the entrance specifically to maintain order.

One relatively free disciple saw Gan Tang trying to head inside and quickly blocked her, speaking in a raspy, robotic drone:

—"Beginner Class disciples wanting a ticket, go to the left window at the front gate. Non-Beginner Class disciples, claim a ticket via the 'System' in the Library Pavilion. Disciples whose numbers have been called, enter in numerical order: those wanting Artifact Refinement Hall items proceed through the front door, Alchemy Hall items through the back door, and Array Hall items through the window on that tree by the side..."

[...You've even put windows to use?!]

Gan Tang produced her [peach-wood token](# "Literal CN: 桃符 (táofú) — traditional wooden talisman/slip, here used as a queuing token"), showing her ticket number to the disciple, and explained:

—"I'd like to see the items for the Spirit Beast Enclosure."

[—"...Spirit Beast Enclosure?"](# "Speaker: Tianyi Workshop Disciple")

The disciple seemed to suffer a brain short-circuit, freezing for two full minutes before reacting. She looked at Gan Tang with genuine astonishment.

In the Heavenly Spirit Sect, very few disciples wanted to pursue beast taming. Thousand Beasts Hall was nearby; while their treatment of disciples was subpar, they were the second-largest sect in the Central Domain, with higher fame and easier directions. Rogue cultivators wanting to pursue that path went straight there. Very few came to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and even with the recent expansion, few enrolled in beast taming.

The Spirit Beast Enclosure only existed because Eldest Senior Brother kept some "little animals" in the back mountain that needed tending, usually dragging in underperforming disciples who didn't know what to do with themselves. And as for Eldest Senior Brother... ordinary disciples withheld comment. So it was considered a dreadful chore that everyone in the sect avoided.

Now, while Gan Tang's cultivation wasn't particularly high, she wore the instructor uniform—clearly an honor student recognized by the Immortal Venerable. Wanting items related to the Spirit Beast Enclosure was quite bizarre.

However, standing here on penalty duty had thoroughly extinguished the disciple's gossip instincts. Glancing at the crowd behind Gan Tang, she suppressed her curiosity and said in a listless, muffled tone:

—"There are fewer people in the Spirit Beast Enclosure room. You need to scale the backyard wall onto the third-floor roof, walk along the beam for a bit, and enter through the window beneath the persimmon tree—by the way, we've counted every single persimmon on that tree. No stealing!"

[Doesn't this route sound exactly like a cat burglar? Getting into your Tianyi Pavilion has come to this?!]

Gan Tang wanted to complain, but watching the disciple rasping into the crowd: "Beginner Class blah blah...", she pulled a pear from her storage pouch and pressed it into her hand—since becoming an instructor, she kept fruits in her pouch—before heading to the backyard.

Climbing the wall in her wide-sleeved instructor robe while maintaining "Teacher Exemplar" was a bit problematic, so Gan Tang cast a spell to switch into her Intermediate Class uniform.

[Sooner or later, I'm getting a flying sword,] Gan Tang thought reluctantly.

But that was another expense.

Putting aside thoughts of saving money for the future, Gan Tang followed the instructions: scaling the wall, crossing the roof, climbing down the persimmon tree, and slipping through the window into her destination:

A modest little embroidery chamber.

It was also the display showroom for the Spirit Beast Enclosure disciples' attire.

Upon entering, Gan Tang saw her reflection in an ice mirror opposite the entrance.

However, the figure in the mirror wasn't wearing her Intermediate Class uniform or her instructor robe; instead, she was dressed in a form-fitting hibiscus-red short tunic, draped in a magnificent black fur cape, and holding a leather whip in her hand!

Gan Tang was so startled she nearly backed out of the room:

—"This is the uniform for Spirit Beast Enclosure disciples? Isn't this way too exaggerated?!"

[Should I expect anything less from Eldest Senior Brother's subordinates? Without the resolve to abandon all human shame, who would ever dare wear such an outfit?]

Even though Gan Tang was mentally prepared to head down this path, she still found it unbearable to look at.

Striving to avoid her reflection in the mirror, she moved to the display shelves beside it. Exhibited here were accessory props for the Spirit Beast Enclosure attire: leather whips, beast flutes, specialized beast-taming storage bags, feather ornaments to attract avian spirit beasts, and so forth.

Other Intermediate Class disciples lingered around Tianyi Pavilion precisely for these props.

However, these props couldn't be collected for free. They required completing various quests. Someone had calculated that by the time one completed the quest chain and collected most of the props, one would practically be ready to promote to the Advanced Class.

Of course, there was one exception:

[Microtransactions](# "Literal CN: 氪金 (kèjīn) — gacha/gaming slang for spending real money / spirit stones").

Props one desperately wanted could be purchased outright, and one could even change colors, functions, and special effects—so long as one had spirit stones.

Wanting to become a beast tamer, Gan Tang naturally envied these props. She touched this whip, stroked that storage bag, standing there for a long time.

Fortunately, the Spirit Beast Enclosure room had no other visitors; in a popular department like Alchemy, she would have been shoved out long ago. Then again, with space so tight, the fact that Tianyi Workshop reserved a display room for the Spirit Beast Enclosure likely involved some personal coercion from Eldest Senior Brother.

Gan Tang gazed enraptured for a long while, checking all quest requirements and nearly memorizing them, before finally stopping before the most basic wooden beast flute. Gritting her teeth, she reached out her hand—

[—"You want to buy that beast flute?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Rong")

A sudden voice from behind startled Gan Tang. Turning around, she realized it was an acquaintance:

—"Xiang Rong, what are you doing here?"

—"Because this is the only room with no one around, so I snuck in for a nap,"-Xiang Rong said, rubbing the dark circles under his eyes.

With so many new uniform styles, the busiest people were naturally Tianyi Workshop disciples. Even Xiang Rong, an external non-member, had been dragged in as free labor by his sister. Having just finished today's quota, he had dozed off in the empty Spirit Beast Enclosure room, only to see Gan Tang sneak in and stare for half the day.

Xiang Rong advised:

—"You want to become a beast tamer? As an old acquaintance, I advise you not to make your choice so early. Even the most basic beast flute costs several thousand spirit stones, and it's custom-tuned for the tamer. If you buy it and regret it later, reselling it won't be easy. At early Foundation Establishment, you haven't locked in your core cultivation method yet, right? What if it doesn't match? Better wait until mid Foundation Establishment when your method is settled before picking a flute. By then, completing the quest chain gets you one for free."

—"It's precisely because I haven't chosen a cultivation method that I want a beast flute,"-Gan Tang countered with her own reasoning:—"Beast flutes have nothing to do with cultivation methods, right? It purely depends on which spirit beasts you're compatible with. I want to test it in the secret realm, confirm my beast-taming specialty, and only then choose my cultivation method."

Instead of letting the method choose her!

Seeing her so stubborn, Xiang Rong scratched his head, unsure how to persuade her.

—"Then there's still no need to rush,"-Xiang Yi said, walking over to find her brother:—"In a few days it's the [Winter Clothes Festival](# "Literal CN: 十月朝 (Shíyuèzhāo) — the 1st day of the 10th lunar month, a traditional festival marking the onset of winter and the distribution of seasonal clothing"). Junior Brother Mo mentioned that Thousand Beasts Hall will be visiting. Why not talk with them first before deciding?"

She added in curiosity:

—"Since you want to be a beast tamer so badly, why didn't you choose Thousand Beasts Hall in the first place?"

Gan Tang bit her lip in silence.

—"Sorry, I didn't mean to pry into your personal affairs..."-Xiang Yi waved her hands apologetically upon seeing Gan Tang's expression:—"In any case, don't rush such a major (expensive) decision. Even if you don't want to deal with Thousand Beasts Hall, if you want to buy a beast flute, at least wait until after the Winter Clothes Festival. It's rare enough that new clothes are being issued; perhaps there will be a [great opportunity](# "Literal CN: 大机缘 (dà jīyuán) — massive karmic stroke of cultivation fortune") during the festival?"

[—"A great opportunity at the Winter Clothes Festival?"](# "Speakers: Gan Tang & Xiang Rong")

This time, not only Gan Tang, but even Xiang Rong turned to stare at his sister.

—"Junior Brother Mo said the Immortal Venerable is preparing something,"-Xiang Yi smiled mysteriously:—"You'll know when the time comes."

***

—"The Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall wishes to pay a formal visit?"

On the other side, Zhou Hui inquired as well.

He was also trying on new clothes.

This white undergarment with hidden peony and [double happiness](# "Literal CN: 双喜 (shuāngxǐ) — traditional Chinese ornamental design symbolizing marital joy and good fortune") patterns had been sewn by Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi.

After seeing Zhou Hui's trunk of clothes last time, they seemed to have received quite a shock. They insisted that their Master couldn't possibly sew all his own clothes, could he? As practice, they made this undergarment first—with their skills, they couldn't make outerwear yet, and indeed even the stitches on this undergarment were crooked.

One couldn't expect any defensive functions from such a garment; only that the fabric had been meticulously selected, making it rather comfortable to wear.

Zhou Hui had no plans to face a Heavenly Tribulation anytime soon, so he didn't mind wearing it.

However, hearing that Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi had presented clothing, Yuwen Xuan had whittled a peach-wood hairpin, Ran Deng had crafted a ring, Guan Ye had forged a jade pendant, Lu Shulong had sewn an incense sachet, and Mo Bei had braided a Snow-Glow Grass sash—all delivering them together! Zhou Hui truly couldn't fathom where his disciples' competitive streak had come from.

Naturally, Zhou Hui wouldn't reject his disciples' goodwill; he intended to wear the entire collection for the Winter Clothes Festival.

And it was while he was trying on this pile of gifts that Yuwen Xuan delivered the formal visiting card from Thousand Beasts Hall.

—"We certainly have quite a few guests this year,"-Zhou Hui remarked in perplexity:—"Still, when we 'temporarily detained' their disciples a while back, their Sect Leader never showed up. Now, when we're so busy, what is he coming for?"

When Zhou Hui said "busy," it was genuinely busy.

First recruitment, then new curriculum scheduling, followed by school uniforms—now every department in the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect was operating at full throttle, with nowhere left idle.

Moreover, the Winter Clothes Festival was a holiday, and because the 9th month had been consumed by recruitment, the [Double Ninth Festival](# "Literal CN: 重阳 (Chóngyáng) — 9th day of the 9th lunar month, traditional harvest and elder-honoring festival") hadn't been celebrated. Zhou Hui intended to hold a proper celebration and make up for Double Ninth.

What was Thousand Beasts Hall thinking, running over for a visit at a time like this?

—"If Master finds it troublesome, why not just reject him?"-Yuwen Xuan suggested, his ideas forever sprinting in the direction of self-indulgent whims.

—"Wait a moment,"-Zhou Hui glared at him:—"What did you say you did to Thousand Beasts Hall's disciples earlier? Don't tell me they've come to lodge a complaint?"

—"No way, absolutely not! Even if they found out right now, at most they'd secretly assassinate that kid; they have no evidence to confront us,"-Yuwen Xuan said:—"Besides, that kid is still alive and kicking, and I heard she even got promoted! Furthermore, whenever I stir up trouble, isn't it only discovered after things have spiraled completely out of hand? It's only been a few months; the trouble hasn't even had time to escalate!"

[Listen to that. Is that human speech?!]

With such an eldest disciple, no matter how obedient and sweet the other disciples were, Zhou Hui's blood pressure still spiked from time to time.

He roared:

—"YUWEN XUAN——!"

Yuwen Xuan bolted like a frightened bird.
