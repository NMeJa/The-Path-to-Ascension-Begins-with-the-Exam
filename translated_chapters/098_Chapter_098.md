# Chapter 98: [Karmic Fortune](<# "Literal CN: 气运 (qì yùn) — destiny / karmic fortune; heavenly luck and destiny that accumulates around monumental events and fated individuals; chapter title">) (第98章 气运)

Outside Cloud City.

After departing the tournament grounds, the Untiring Immortal Venerable did not follow Fairy Xia to her Cloud Pool.

Even though Fairy Xia was exceedingly eager to host the Immortal Venerable at her estate:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, having arrived at Cloud City, you are actually unwilling to come sit by my Cloud Pool? Do you disfavor my humble pool?"-Fairy Xia asked with a sigh.

She maintained the appearance of a decrepit, tottering old woman, her eyes glistening with brimming tears, looking for all the world like an [empty-nest elderly parent](<# "Literal CN: 空巢老人 (kōng cháo lǎo rén) — empty-nest elderly parent; a modern Chinese social term for solitary elderly parents whose adult children have left home, here humorously used to describe Fairy Xia playing the guilt-tripping grandma">)—the kind capable of making everyone around her feel like [unfilial descendants](<# "Literal CN: 不肖子孙 (bù xiào zǐ sūn) — unworthy / unfilial descendants who fail to honor their elders">) drowning in unbearable guilt.

Even though chronologically, the Untiring Immortal Venerable was actually older than Fairy Xia, seeing her put on this act still left him somewhat caught between laughter and tears.

—"I would love to accept your gracious invitation, but unfortunately I have many matters to attend to today,"-Zhou Hui declined.

It was impossible for him to go.

It was not that there was anything wrong with the Cloud Pool; rather, it was the rhythm of Fairy Xia's banquets. She hosted them twice a year, each lasting six full months—and that was considered on the shorter side. The most outrageous banquet she had ever hosted reportedly lasted three hundred years, supposedly because Fairy Xia had obtained some exquisite immortal wine, leaving the entire assembly drunkenly slumbering for three centuries without waking. Zhou Hui had little doubt that if he agreed to attend, she would surely produce some new 'exquisite treasure,' and he would truly get nothing else done.

Nonetheless, courteous pleasantries still had to be spoken. Zhou Hui adopted a voice tinged with regret:

—"Next time I will definitely take the initiative to pay a visit... The Cloud Pool is the fairest place in our realm without equal. If only to broaden my disciples' horizons, I intend to visit sooner or later."

Accompanying Zhou Hui's words, Qu Qianxing, standing behind him, flashed an appropriately expectant smile. That countenance, like a delicate peony softly unfurling its petals, completely dazzled Fairy Xia's eyes.

Fairy Xia's aged face could not help blushing faintly.

For the sake of this handsome youth, Fairy Xia did not pester him further:

[—"Very well. With the Immortal Arts Conference opening today, I knew you wouldn't be able to sit still, Immortal Venerable. But the next time you come to Cloud City, you must come to my Cloud Pool!"](<# "Speaker: Fairy Xia">)

—"[[Next time definitely, next time definitely]](<# "Literal CN: 下次一定 (xià cì yí dìng) — popular Chinese internet meme used when politely dodging an invitation or promising content 'next time for sure' without any real intention of doing it">),"-Zhou Hui replied with a smile.

Leading his disciples, he turned into a streak of rosy light, vanishing in the blink of an eye into the snow-white clouds.

Fairy Xia watched the Untiring Immortal Venerable and his entourage depart with lingering regret before turning her gaze toward the 'distinguished guest' from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ritual Court.

Her tone immediately lacked the warmth she had shown Zhou Hui, carrying only a distant courtesy:

—"Elder Siwu, having made a rare journey to Cloud City, do you care to join this old woman at her residence for a chat?"

She did not even mention her Cloud Pool.

When had Siwu ever endured such cold treatment? In past days, he would surely have flown into a rage. Yet perhaps because he had been thoroughly terrorized by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's spiritual pressure moments earlier, he merely let out an indifferent snort:

[—"No need. I am bound for the ruins in the depths of the Abyss Sea. Passing through Cloud City was merely to make brief adjustments, with no intent to disturb your peace. And now, farewell!"](<# "Speaker: Elder Siwu">)

As he spoke, without waiting for Fairy Xia's reply, he too dissolved into a streak of rosy light, streaking off in a direction diametrically opposed to Zhou Hui.

Evidently, he had longed to flee long ago, restrained only by his terror of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's overwhelming might.

Toward Fairy Xia and the others, Siwu naturally felt no dread. His parting words served as an explanation of his itinerary; feeling no further obligation and wishing to suffer no further humiliation, he simply took his leave.

And Fairy Xia naturally had no desire to entertain such an unwelcome guest, sneering as she watched him depart.

Only when Siwu's silhouette completely vanished beyond the horizon did a Mahayana cultivator attending the banquet speak up:

[—"...Although the Ritual Court has always looked down on others, Siwu's conduct today was still a bit excessive. There may well be hidden circumstances behind it."](<# "Speaker: Mahayana Cultivator">)

[—"What hidden circumstances? He simply believes our Central Domain is inferior to the Northern Domain and wished to stir up trouble!"](<# "Speaker: Mahayana Cultivator">)-another Mahayana cultivator snorted.-[—"Had the Untiring Immortal Venerable not happened to expose him on the spot, just imagine what would have happened once word of this leaked! Never mind the Immortal Arts Conference becoming a laughingstock—we Central Domain cultivators would have lost all face!"](<# "Speaker: Mahayana Cultivator">)

[—"Which is precisely why I find it rather strange,"-that Mahayana cultivator said.-"Siwu had no reason to provoke conflict between the Central and Northern Domains, did he? It brings no benefit to the Heavenly Dao Alliance either... In fact, this Immortal Arts Conference is held in cooperation with the Heavenly Dao Alliance."](<# "Speaker: Mahayana Cultivator">)

[—"Mm, but Siwu himself stated that he was heading first to the Abyss Sea. I have heard about this matter—East Hedge Garden seems to have unearthed something grand and specially invited the Ritual Court. To Siwu, that is his genuine business. As for passing by Cloud City and witnessing the Immortal Arts Conference, a spur-of-the-moment whim is a plausible explanation,"](<# "Speaker: Fairy Xia">)-Fairy Xia said with a smile.

Someone wanted to say more.

Yet Fairy Xia clapped her hands and said:

—"Alright, even if an unexpected turn did occur, isn't everything still within the Untiring Immortal Venerable's control? No catastrophe can unfold. Let us resume our banquet!"

This proposal met with unanimous approval.

In truth, all present were ancient monsters on the verge of ascending to immortality; how could they fail to perceive the strangeness of today's events? Yet involving the Ritual Court, Cloud City, the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and even potentially escalating to the peace between the Central and Northern Domains, an investigation was simply far too troublesome. Attributing the incident entirely to Siwu's personal misconduct was far easier.

Since even Fairy Xia expressed no intent to pursue the matter, naturally no one spoke up further.

The flock of high-stage cultivators flew off toward the Cloud Pool in high spirits, appearing to have cast the recent accident entirely to the back of their minds.

Yet after they departed, the voice of Fairy Xia, who led the assembly away, echoed sharply within the dense clouds:

[—"Investigate the route Siwu took into the Central Domain."](<# "Speaker: Fairy Xia">)

[—"Uncover Nie Tongzi's background and experiences over these past years."](<# "Speaker: Fairy Xia">)

[—"Go to East Hedge Garden and ask what manner of invitation they extended to the Ritual Court!"](<# "Speaker: Fairy Xia">)

Accompanying these successive commands, several cloud clusters in the surroundings emitted a low buzzing drone. Then, as if a hornet's nest had been stirred, a massive flock of swifts formed from clouds burst forth from the vapor.

They darted off in every direction.

One swift formed from white cloud streaked across the sky at incredible speed, carving water-like ripples through the wind.

Within those ripples, faint silhouettes gradually emerged—revealing Zhou Hui and his disciples, who had supposedly departed first!

No longer concealing their figures, they simply watched the cloud swift streak past them.

—"It seems Lady Fairy Xia intends to investigate this matter!"-Qingyang Qingyu remarked among them.-"Untiring Master, should we conduct an investigation as well?"

*If you are willing, I can mobilize the personnel of the Qingyang Clan.*

That unvoiced sentence was what Qingyu truly meant to offer.

Unfortunately, the Untiring Immortal Venerable appeared to hold very little interest in Siwu.

[—"No need. Fairy Xia's swifts are clever enough; they will uncover whatever transpired."](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

*That is merely polite rhetoric.*

Qingyang Qingyu thought to herself.

For a widely connected, renowned Mahayana cultivator like Fairy Xia, her primary cultivation techniques and signature arts were hardly secrets, inevitably drawing commentary from other cultivators. Qingyu had heard her own ancestor mention:

Fairy Xia was not renowned for her intelligence-gathering abilities; rather, her cloud swifts were peerlessly formidable at broadcasting information. If Fairy Xia set her mind to inquiring about a piece of gossip, half a day was enough for her to query the entire Central Domain; given a full day, she could comb through every grandmaster across the entire cultivation realm.

*Ah, thinking of it that way, Elder Siwu suddenly seems rather pitiful.*

It was hard to say whether Fairy Xia genuinely intended to investigate. It was more accurate to say that once this flock of swifts flew out, from the depths of the Abyss Sea below to Celestial City above, every last soul would learn of Elder Siwu suffering a humiliating defeat at the Immortal Arts Conference.

Moreover, rumor had it that these swifts could dissolve into a curtain of rain to replay visual scenes...

Thinking to this point, Qingyu resolved not to meddle in the affairs of Mahayana big shots. The unfathomable depth of their machinations was far beyond what a mere Golden Core cultivator like her could fathom.

—"Come, let us return to watch the Immortal Arts Conference,"-Zhou Hui suggested.

Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu followed behind their Master without the slightest objection.

They had realized it: indeed, the 'Untiring' Immortal Venerable never engaged in meaningless actions.

Coming out for a casual excursion only to run into a troublemaking Mahayana elder could never be a mere 'coincidence.' And since the Immortal Venerable had not truly departed Cloud City, he surely had far more momentous matters to attend to.

---

Zhou Hui had no inkling of the awe stirring within his disciples' hearts.

Leading his disciples, he turned back and returned once more to the Immortal Arts Conference venue.

However, Zhou Hui did not head to the gallery promenade this time. Instead, he ascended into the lofty expanse far above the clouds—a realm where not a single wisp of vapor drifted, where flying any higher would lead straight into the void.

Looking down from this vantage point, the entire tournament grounds were laid bare before their eyes.

—"Qianxing, viewing the Immortal Arts Conference from here, what do you feel?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Qu Qianxing was taken aback, gazing downward before replying:

—"It is truly worthy of a grand tournament hosted by Master—exceptionally magnificent. It is only that our security protocols require adjustment to prevent what transpired today from recurring—"

—"You did well enough. Earlier, had I not deliberately sealed off the space, Fairy Xia and the others would have unraveled the restriction and entered the arena long ago. By the standards of handling a Mahayana cultivator, it was more than sufficient,"-Zhou Hui interrupted his second disciple's self-reproach.

*Why must this child take everything so deathly seriously?*

—"I meant your impression upon witnessing the scene below,"-Zhou Hui asked.

Qu Qianxing glanced at Zhou Hui, unable to grasp Master's intent, and answered slowly:

—"...It looks like dinner plates stacked tier upon tier."

—"And what else?"-Zhou Hui asked encouragingly.-"For instance, the people competing down there?"

[—"They look like bugs crawling across the plates."](<# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing">)

Zhou Hui was rendered completely speechless:
—"..."

[Where did your literary talent go, Your Honor former Imperial Tanhua?!]

[Upon beholding such a grand vista, that is your sole impression?!]

Zhou Hui massaged his temples in his mind.

Originally, Qingyang Qingyu had been gazing downward with keen interest. Hearing Qu Qianxing put it that way, she nonchalantly retracted her gaze, clearly having no desire to look upon any 'plates crawling with bugs.'

Qu Qianxing immediately sensed that his 'honest impressions' had failed to satisfy Master. Uncertain what profound lesson Master intended, he could only gaze innocently at Zhou Hui:

—"Master, what guidance do you have for this disciple?"

—"I said that we came out today to have fun, did I not?"-Zhou Hui said helplessly.-"Can you not do something more suited to an outing?"

—"An outing?"

Qu Qianxing hardly felt their itinerary today had anything to do with having fun. But since Master had put it that way, the corner of his mouth twitched, and he composed an expression that met conventional social expectations, brightly proposing:

—"Then, shall I compose a poem for this Immortal Arts Conference...?"

[Are you a little kid being forced into a talent show by his parents?]

—"No, there is really no need,"-Zhou Hui replied, shaking his head.

He was now certain that he was unsuited to be a humanities teacher. Look at what a fine [Wenqu Star](<# "Literal CN: 文曲星 (wén qū xīng) — the Star of Literary Acumen in Chinese astromancy and Daoism, symbol of literary genius and top scholars">) had been reduced to after following him for two hundred years!

—"I had hoped to see you a little happier,"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"Yet looking like this, I have no choice but to resort to magical artifacts."

—"? No! Being able to accompany Master on an outing, I am very happy!"-Qu Qianxing blurted out in a rush.

[Happy like seeing bugs crawling across dinner plates?]

Zhou Hui paid him no heed. Instead, he reached into his sleeve and produced a magical treasure.

It was an exquisite little green jade gourd, carved with delicate talismans.

Qingyang Qingyu's eyes lit up upon seeing it:

—"Untiring Master, could this be the legendary [karmic fortune artifact](<# "Literal CN: 气运法宝 (qì yùn fǎ bǎo) — a rare magical treasure capable of gathering and directing heavenly destiny / karmic fortune">)?!"

[—"Indeed."](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

Zhou Hui gave her an appreciative glance, then tossed the gourd toward the clouds below. The gourd expanded instantly, blowing forth gusts of pure, gentle breezes. As those clear winds swept across the tournament grounds below, shimmering streams of light could be seen rising from the arenas.

—"This is karmic fortune,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"Places such as this, where vast throngs gather and triumphs and defeats are contested, attract the convergence of destiny most easily. It is precisely from such grounds that individuals blessed with immense karmic fortune are born."

—"I have heard my ancestor speak of it, but this is my first time seeing it with my own eyes!"-Qingyu exclaimed in marvel.

[It looks even more like plates crawling with bugs now—the moldy kind.]

Qu Qianxing thought to himself, while maintaining a polite smile on his face:

—"The pure breeze turns to swirl—"

—"Enough, I said there is no need,"-Zhou Hui said, giving the back of Qu Qianxing's head a light swat. He ruthlessly pointed downward:-"Go directly into the clouds above the karmic fortune... no, above your 'bug-infested plates,' and begin circulating your cultivation technique."

[—"???"](<# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing">)

Qu Qianxing stared blankly at Zhou Hui.

Zhou Hui let out a sigh:

—"Go on! Your karmic fortune has always been abysmal. This is an opportunity to gather at least a bit of fortune for yourself. But do try to keep your spirits bright—karmic fortune favors cheerful, optimistic people."

Indeed, this was Zhou Hui's true 'business' for coming out today.

Although Zhou Hui admitted that he had sensed Siwu's massive spiritual energy appearing at the conference grounds—Zhou Hui had no idea what Siwu had been thinking; a Mahayana cultivator actually believing his spiritual presence could escape the notice of a Tribulation Transcendence master, did he think Zhou Hui's divine sense was counterfeit?—and thus arrived at the arena earlier, this trip had genuinely been planned primarily for Qu Qianxing.

In the Heavenly Dao's script, Qu Qianxing, in his role as Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, was destined to be slain by the 'Protagonist.'

Setting aside how he was to be killed, once fate had singled someone out by name, altering that destiny was extraordinarily difficult. What Zhou Hui had conceived back then was simply to have Qu Qianxing 'die' once, using the stratagem to [deceive the heavens to cross the sea](<# "Literal CN: 瞒天过海 (mán tiān guò hǎi) — Stratagem 1 of the Thirty-Six Stratagems: masking one's true purpose beneath a conspicuous, everyday guise to fool the heavens">).

Yet to execute this plan, only someone blessed with immense karmic fortune could hope to deceive the Heavenly Dao.

Unfortunately, Qu Qianxing's luck had always felt rather rotten—setting aside being marked by the Heavenly Dao, consider how he lost the title of [Zhuangyuan](<# "Literal CN: 状元 (zhuàng yuan) — First Rank Scholar / top scorer of the Imperial Examinations">) and ended up as [Tanhua](<# "Literal CN: 探花 (tàn huā) — Third Rank Scholar">) solely because he was too handsome, or how serving as a mortal official led to him being targeted by demonic cultivators; that alone was rotten luck to the extreme.

That was why Zhou Hui had brought Qu Qianxing here to absorb a bit of karmic fortune.

Yet gazing at Qu Qianxing's sour expression—as if 'the entire world owed him ten thousand taels of silver'—Zhou Hui harbored little hope for the outcome.

Qu Qianxing had no idea of Master's internal complaints.

[—"...Yes, Master."](<# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing">)

Hearing this, he wore a complex expression, bowed deeply to Zhou Hui, and flew downward.

Zhou Hui then turned to Qingyang Qingyu:

—"Qingyu, you go as well!"

—"Thank you, Untiring Master,"-Qingyu said, sounding as joyful as she could muster.

Originally, being able to graze upon some karmic fortune was a rare, precious opportunity. But after hearing Qu Qianxing's analogy, watching the torrent of karmic fortune below made her feel as though countless bugs were crawling over her skin.

Such a mindset was obviously detrimental to gathering fortune, so Qingyu could only cling to the resolve not to let an opportunity slip by, absorbing whatever amount she could manage.

Telling herself to pay no heed to whatever Qu Qianxing had said, she too flew downward.

Moreover, she deliberately positioned herself far, far away from Qu Qianxing.

[—"Baa, baa!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb)">)

Seeing his junior brother and (temporary) junior sister fly away, the little black lamb let out an anxious bleat, clearly wanting to head down as well.

[As if it cared about Qu Qianxing's ridiculous nonsense!]

[What bug-infested plates? If Master lets me go down and lick it all over once, never mind the bugs—even the plates wouldn't be left!]

—"Not you,"-Zhou Hui pressed the little lamb down mercilessly.-"Vicious beasts are inherently despised by heaven and earth; they possess no karmic fortune to speak of. Consuming such things is utterly useless to you."

[—"Baa, baa!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb)">)

[I just want a taste!]

Having played a lamb for so long, Yuwen Xuan seemed to have forgotten that with no outsiders present, he was entirely capable of human speech. He merely flailed his four hooves and bleated, desperate to follow along.

Seeing him like this, Zhou Hui did not bother wasting words. Grabbing him by his two hind legs, he tipped the lamb upside down and gave him a vigorous shake.

[—"Baa!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb)">)

Suddenly subjected to such treatment, the little black lamb's eyes went wide, letting out a startled shriek.

Accompanying Zhou Hui's shaking, a whole pile of miscellaneous items tumbled out from the tight black curls of his fleece.

These were all items Yuwen Xuan had scavenged earlier in the tournament arena. Among them were candied hawthorn skewers, teapots, and tea pastries that Zhou Hui had purchased, alongside various pills, medicinal herbs, spiritual delicacies, and the like. It was a complete mystery how he had acquired them all while lounging in Zhou Hui's arms from start to finish.

—"I didn't steal anything!"-Yuwen Xuan argued in [guilty over-explanation](<# "Literal CN: 此地无银三百两 (cǐ dì wú yín sān bǎi liǎng) — 'no three hundred taels of silver buried here'; a famous Chinese fable about someone posting a sign over buried silver claiming nothing is there, meaning a clumsy denial that exposes one's guilt">).-"For all of these, I honestly left behind equivalent spirit stones!"

—"Including this?"-Zhou Hui asked, picking out a magical artifact from the heap.

It was a bronze mirror.

It appeared utterly ordinary, yet emanated a wisp of celestial qi. Even after being greedily licked all over by the little black lamb—or rather, the Taotie—the celestial aura upon it had surprisingly not completely vanished.

In Zhou Hui's eyes, this object was as conspicuous as a Mahayana cultivator sitting smack in the middle of Nascent Soul cultivators at the Immortal Arts Conference.

In fact, this item was distinctly mingled with that very Mahayana cultivator's scent.

—"You stole this from Elder Siwu?"-Zhou Hui demanded.-"You actually dared to steal?!"

—"No, I didn't steal, I just..."-Yuwen Xuan reflexively tried to defend himself.

Yet he abruptly recalled what Siwu had done, and his confidence surged right back:

—"I just wanted to teach him a lesson, what's wrong with that?! Who told him to bully Master and wreck our Immortal Arts Conference?!"

*Who bullied whom? If Siwu heard that, he would certainly disagree.*

—"Even so, you must not resort to such underhanded methods,"-Zhou Hui said, rapping Yuwen Xuan on the head.

The little black lamb whimpered twice in aggrieved distress:

[If Master were truly opposed to it, he should have stopped me back then! The fact that he didn't stop me was because he agreed!]

—"Mm, because I too am curious about this,"-Zhou Hui generously admitted, as if seeing right through Yuwen Xuan's sense of grievance.-"This appears to be an object from the True Immortal Realm."

—"An object from the True Immortal Realm?"-The little black lamb instantly forgot his grievance, his eyes gleaming brightly as he asked:-"Is it delicious?"

—"Whether it is delicious or not I cannot say, but it looks like a communication apparatus..."-Zhou Hui murmured under his breath.-"I wonder if it can establish contact with the True Immortal Realm?"

—"I think it must be delicious! It smelled the most fragrant to me, so I brought it back!"-the little black lamb declared with immense enthusiasm, opening his maw wide in an attempt to gulp down the bronze mirror.

Zhou Hui gave his forehead another sharp rap:

—"I told you it is a communication apparatus, yet you still try to eat it!"

—"Baa, baa! But I'm the one who brought it back!"-the little black lamb wailed indignantly.

Holding the mirror, Zhou Hui questioned his disciple with lingering suspicion:

—"...Did you only steal this?"

Zhou Hui had sensed Yuwen Xuan's sneaky little movements at the time yet had not intervened. His reason was not for the sake of this object, but rather because while Yuwen Xuan was acting, Zhou Hui had felt an intense physiological palpitation.

As mentioned before, for a cultivator of Zhou Hui's stature, 'physiological responses' such as heart palpitations almost never occurred. Thus, this palpitation was akin to Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa instinctively foreseeing his clan's extermination—an instinctual premonition.

It was precisely for this reason that Zhou Hui had not stopped Yuwen Xuan, wanting to see what it truly was.

Yet this mirror was exceedingly ordinary. Even if it was a communication apparatus, it hardly warranted the extermination of the Heavenly Spirit Sect! Zhou Hui had thought it would at least be a cheat weapon belonging to the 'Protagonist,' something like the Immortal-Devouring Sword.

Then again, if it were the Protagonist's cheat weapon, it would hardly be something Yuwen Xuan could steal so easily, would it?

Yuwen Xuan shook his head vigorously:

—"Nothing else! I really didn't steal anything else! It was just because this smelled so fragrant and kept hooking my tongue that it followed me back. It wasn't that I proactively stole... took it... baa!"

—"Stealing is stealing. Do not shirk your responsibility,"-Zhou Hui said.

Holding the mirror, he studied it thoroughly once more, yet still found nothing suspicious. He could only determine that it was an 'ordinary' celestial mirror. As the presider of the Heaven-Worshipping Grand Ceremony for the Ritual Court, theoretically the last entity in this realm capable of reporting affairs to the True Immortal Realm, it was only natural for Siwu to carry such a True Immortal relic.

—"We can show it to Villa Master Qingyang later. He is the true expert on True Immortals... If we confirm there is nothing wrong with it, we can return it to Siwu,"-Zhou Hui could only decide thus.

[—"It was clearly mine..."](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb)">)

The little black lamb buried his head in Zhou Hui's chest, seemingly sobbing fake tears, yet he secretly peeked at Master's expression.

[It seems Master's suspicions are cleared!]

[Although what I 'took' was this mirror, I truly didn't go over there just to steal things! I went over out of the sheer kindness of my heart to 'deliver' a gift to thank our distinguished guest for his 'care'!]

[I wonder if that distinguished guest felt my goodwill?]

[Baa!]

---

[—"Lord Siwu, what shall we do next?"](<# "Speaker: Ritual Court Cultivator">)

—"Master, this matter cannot simply be dropped like this... *Agh!*"

Nie Tongzi took a heavy blow across the face, collapsing to a crouch while clutching his cheek. His facial features, which Zhou Hui had already sculpted rather poorly, became even more grotesque and distorted.

As for Siwu, he was unwilling to cast so much as a second glance at this 'resurrected' disciple of his.

He had suffered immense disgrace today. Given Siwu's temperament, he would normally seek vengeance and reclaim his dignity by any means necessary. Yet the mere thought of the terrifying spiritual might Zhou Hui had unleashed sent an involuntary shudder down Siwu's spine.

*He wasn't afraid! Definitely not!*

—"Go to the Heavenly Spirit Sect for what? To have him [slap my face](<# "Literal CN: 打脸 (dǎ liǎn) — slap in the face / publicly humiliated">) all over again?!"-Siwu barked furiously, feeling his magically healed cheek throb with phantom pain once more.

Siwu declared coldly:

—"Pay no further heed to any Heavenly Spirit Sect or Immortal Arts Conference! This matter was never mine to manage in the first place. We proceed according to our original plan—to the Abyss Sea to find East Hedge Garden!"

As he spoke, so he acted.

Siwu shook his sleeves and streaked off toward the Demon God Ruins.

The flock of disciples and attendants hurriedly scrambled to keep pace.

Nie Tongzi followed behind in a panic, muttering bitterly in his heart:

[Coward.]

He glared resentfully at Siwu's receding back, feeling not the slightest remorse for his own actions, only furious that Siwu would not avenge him.

It was precisely at this moment that he seemed to catch sight of what appeared to be [pastry crumbs](<# "Literal CN: 饼渣 (bǐng zhā) — crumbs from a meat patty or pastry; here secretly planted by Yuwen Xuan, who compressed Taotie resentment into dense edible cakes">) clinging to the hem of Siwu's robe. Yet amidst the billowing white sleeves that tumbled like drifting clouds, they quickly vanished from sight.

[It must be an illusion.]

[Someone with as severe a clean-freak streak as Siwu would never permit a single speck of dust to soil his robes, let alone food crumbs.]

Nie Tongzi thought.
