# Chapter 47: The Winter Clothes Festival (第47章 十月朝)

The Double Tenth, the [Winter Clothes Festival](# "Literal CN: 十月朝 / 寒衣节 (Shíyuèzhāo / Hányījié) — 1st day of the 10th lunar month, traditional festival for distributing winter robes and burning paper clothes for ancestors").

In the mortal realm, this was the day when imperial courts distributed new winter robes to civil and military officials, and when common folk burned paper winter clothes to honor their ancestors.

Naturally, for cultivation sects, their founding patriarchs and ancestral masters were generally still alive—and likely to outlive their disciples by millennia. Consequently, there was no need to burn winter garments; instead, disciples assembled at year's end to deliver comprehensive reports on their annual cultivation progress and sect arrangements. In other words, it was the cultivation world's equivalent of a corporate [year-end review](# "Literal CN: 年终总结 (niánzhōng zǒngjié) — corporate annual performance review").

Honestly speaking, the Thousand Beasts Hall contingent picking this exact day to drop by for a visit was thoroughly obnoxious.

And Yan Bailang had come precisely *to* be obnoxious. In his original blueprint, the optimal outcome was not even laying eyes on the Untiring Immortal Venerable at all, but rather being impatiently brushed off and kicked to the curb by his disciples. That would have been the finest possible development.

Instead, reality unfolded in direct opposition to his calculations. Not only did the Heavenly Spirit Sect refrain from rudely expelling him, but upon hearing his proposal, the Untiring Immortal Venerable actually seemed delighted! He invited Yan Bailang straight into the VIP seat of honor to listen to his disciples' presentations together.

What could Yan Bailang do? He had no choice but to sit down.

Except right now, his mind was utterly consumed by the terrifying dilemma of disaster control—one had to understand, all the sects across the Central Domain currently shared Yan Bailang's dilemma. Coerced by Carefree Palace to choose a side, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had originally exerted zero pressure. But now that Heavenly Spirit Sect intended to host the Winter Solstice gala and banquet and dispatch invitation cards, it was effectively forcing everyone to declare their allegiance: Do you pick Carefree Palace, who is far away but holds your business lifeline? Or do you pick Heavenly Spirit Sect, who is right on your doorstep and can pluck off your head at a moment's notice?! Yan Bailang had no idea which way the other sects would lean, but before anything else, they would definitely lynch him—the idiot who ran over to [pull the tiger's whiskers](# "Literal CN: 撩老虎尾巴 (liáo lǎohǔ wěiba) — recklessly provoking a dangerous predator")! That much was an absolute certainty.

Consequently, Yan Bailang sat in his seat in a total daze, not even daring to rest his weight fully on the chair. When Zhou Hui's eldest disciple and second disciple came up in turn to deliver their presentations, he didn't catch a single word clearly—vaguely gathering that because there were too many new disciples, they needed to construct more immortal cave abodes.

And following those two, a female cultivator stepped forward.

Thousand Beasts Hall's Elder Bai Jun suddenly grew agitated:

—"It's the array masters of the Ran Clan!"-he whispered through divine sense.

`—"?!"`

Yan Bailang was daydreaming when the sudden voice in his sea of consciousness startled him back to reality. He couldn't help shooting a fierce glare at Bai Jun:

[Did you think that speaking through divine sense would keep the Untiring Immortal Venerable from hearing you? Doing this is no different from shouting it out loud!]

Bai Jun, however, failed to notice Yan Bailang's grimace—or perhaps felt that saying it out loud wouldn't matter anyway, since he wasn't speaking ill of anyone. He continued transmitting via divine sense:

—"I heard long ago that the Ran Clan, renowned for their array arts, sent their young miss to study under the Immortal Venerable, but I never expected it to be true! What's more, they even dispatched several of their most outstanding disciples! I've seen the one holding the scroll—he's the Ran Clan's Ran Nine. The perimeter formation of Mount Suzaku in the Southern Domain was designed by his hand!"

[...Since when were you so interested in array families?]

Yan Bailang glanced impatiently at the Ran Clan array masters.

The Ran Clan's genetics were indeed commendable: the men were handsome and the women were gorgeous. Ran Deng possessed bright eyes, white teeth, and a serene, dignified demeanor, belonging to the classic archetype that prominent aristocratic clans favored most when seeking a daughter-in-law.

Such features could certainly earn the praise of being a beauty. Regrettably, the one who had presented before her was the truly transcendent, peerless beauty Qu Qianxing. Even though Yan Bailang had been lost in thought earlier, Qu Qianxing's ravishing looks had seared an imprint into his subconscious, to the point where everyone else he looked at now seemed plain and generic.

Just as Yan Bailang was about to disinterestedly look away, Ran Deng's next sentence caused his eyes to snap wide:

—"...I measured the spiritual energy distribution of the Mount Tianling mountain range and conducted statistical analysis. Based on these findings, I designed the baseline array for the spiritual energy reservoir. Master, please critique,"-Ran Deng said.

Mount Tianling's spiritual energy distribution?!

Baseline array for a spiritual reservoir?!

Yan Bailang didn't know much about arrays, but when it came to spiritual energy distribution coupled with baseline arrays, the only thing that came to mind was a sect's mountain-protecting grand array!

That was the defensive core of every single sect!

If one discovered the weaknesses of the mountain-protecting grand array... no, knowing the distribution of spiritual energy meant one could pinpoint the grand array's flaws, cripple the formation, and assault the Heavenly Spirit Sect without the slightest hindrance!

[Is this something that can be shown to outsiders?!]

Yan Bailang looked uncertainly at Ran Deng, then glanced at Bai Jun—who usually maintained Thousand Beasts Hall's mountain-protecting grand array—only to find the elder staring at Ran Deng, utterly dumbfounded.

Ran Deng seemed to have forgotten Thousand Beasts Hall's existence entirely. She merely waved at her assistants, gesturing for them to unfurl the blueprints in their hands. On the contrary, that Ran Nine mentioned by Bai Jun cast an apprehensive glance in Yan Bailang's direction.

—"No matter. Begin,"-the Untiring Immortal Venerable personally instructed.

Ran Nine hesitated no longer. The massive blueprint billowed in the mountain wind like a banner, displaying a comprehensive topographical map of the Heavenly Spirit Sect domain.

Yan Bailang's eyes opened even wider.

Yet before he could inspect it clearly, he watched the map turn into... circles?

Yes, circles.

Big circles, small circles, big circles nested inside small circles, blue circles, green circles, blue circles sandwiching green circles, along with a massive swarm of bizarre mathematical symbols.

—"This is the [Contour and Spiritual Trace Map](# "Literal CN: 等高线和灵痕线图 (děnggāoxiàn hé línghénxiàntú) — modern topographical contour lines combined with spiritual trace vectors") of Mount Tianling,"-Ran Deng said, drawing out a slender pointer stick and tapping upon the surface.—"Judging from this year's data, the flow of spiritual energy fundamentally corresponds with the ridge orientation of the mountain range. At the 759-meter and 949-meter elevations, localized spiritual energy peaks emerged. From this, we deduce—"

`—"???"` Yan Bailang fell completely silent.

Why did he feel like he understood every single word individually, yet strung together, they were complete gibberish?

Subconsciously, Yan Bailang glanced at the others, only to discover that every single Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple was listening with rapt attention, occasionally raising thoughtful questions. It seemed he was the only person present who hadn't the faintest clue what was being discussed.

Suddenly, out of nowhere, the Untiring Immortal Venerable inquired:

—"What does Sect Leader Yan think?"

—"Ah?"-Yan Bailang froze in place.

Seeing his sect leader looking utterly bewildered, Bai Jun frantically reminded him via divine sense:

—"The Lord Immortal Venerable is asking you what [interval](# "Literal CN: 区间 (qūjiān) — mathematical interval / range of values") the spiritual energy reservoir's baseline values should be set at!"

Yan Bailang had no room to consider anything else, whispering back in immense agony:

—"What is an interval?"

`"..."` Bai Jun's face contorted into an identical expression of sheer suffering.

Since even Bai Jun—who was supposedly proficient in arrays (?)—could not answer, Yan Bailang could only stammer out a couple of vague mumbles.

Fortunately, the Untiring Immortal Venerable did not press the matter.

He merely fixed his silver-gray eyes upon Yan Bailang and his entourage in silence for a few seconds, let out a soft sigh, and averted his gaze.

Yet what on earth was this feeling—where one had clearly avoided a scolding, yet felt far more miserable than if one had been viciously berated?!

Yan Bailang sat in his seat in absolute discomfort, not daring to let his mind wander for even a fraction of a second.

Moreover, a fresh wave of latent dread sprouted in his heart:

[My sect's mountain-protecting elder is so completely useless (Bai Jun: ...) that he can't even comprehend the discussion of ordinary array masters! Could our mountain-protecting grand array be as fragile as paper, crumbling the instant it's poked?!]

If the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples knew what Sect Leader Yan was thinking, they would surely tell him he was overthinking things:

First, their discussion had nothing whatsoever to do with a mountain-protecting grand array. You were the one who jumped to defensive formations the moment you heard 'spiritual energy distribution.' We are discussing a spiritual energy reservoir—ah, your sect doesn't have a Master to help design spiritual reservoirs? Never mind then.

Second, if our Master ever wished to obliterate Thousand Beasts Hall, it would have nothing to do with the thickness of your mountain-protecting grand array or the competence of your grand array elder. He could reach out a single hand and pinch your entire sect—men and beasts alike—into [shattered dregs](# "Literal CN: 捏成渣渣 (niē chéng zhāzhā) — crushed into worthless scraps and dust").

Naturally, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples couldn't care less about what the Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall was pondering. They were all listening intently to Ran Deng's presentation, busy cross-referencing her findings with the data they had collected themselves to see whether their own answers were correct.

Ran Deng continued her report:

—"...Based on the above, I propose that constructing the spiritual energy reservoir in the southwest of Mount Tianling would be optimal."

—"Well done,"-Zhou Hui nodded, though a faint, helpless wry smile touched his lips:—"However, Ah-Ran, you've slacked off again. I told you to measure data from at least thirty fixed points, and you truly measured only thirty!"

—"No, Master, I measured thirty-five!"-Ran Deng chuckled, darting her eyes away:—"Besides, as long as the data is accurate, that should suffice, right?"

[Refuse the rat race, let it begin with me!](# "Literal CN: 拒绝内卷，从我做起 (jùjué nèijuǎn, cóng wǒ zuò qǐ) — modern workplace/academic slogan: 'Refuse the rat race, let it begin with me!'")

At any rate, conducting surveys was fine enough, but when it came to actually building the spiritual reservoir and constructing the Spiritual Energy Dam, Ran Deng had zero desire to lift a finger.

Just think about it! Once the Spiritual Energy Dam was completed and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's territory expanded, a massive horde of disciples would surely want to construct new cave abodes in the outer perimeter—or rather, Zhou Hui's desire to regulate the flow of spiritual energy had originally been intended to build cave abodes for these very people. When that time came, who would be tasked with designing those caves?

Back when Zhou Hui proposed three dam construction blueprints to expand the cave abodes, Ran Deng had been the very first to decline.

Ran Deng felt her workload was already completely saturated; not a single drop could be wrung out of her.

Seizing the momentum, she turned to slip back to her seat, only to find after two steps that her cousin Ran Nine remained rooted to the spot, entirely motionless.

Ran Nine cupped his hands and declared:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, there was a reason my young miss only conducted fixed-point measurements: during this period, she also designed a simplified prototype model of the spiritual gathering formation you provided. Please inspect it!"

[I didn't! I wasn't! Don't talk nonsense!]

[My dad sent you here to assist me, not to heap extra work onto my plate, right?!]

Ran Deng glared at her cousin in utter disbelief, feeling Fourth Senior Brother Guan Ye's gaze piercing her back like a bed of needles:

[Junior Brother, I had no intention of stealing your project! You have to believe me!]

—"Oh?"-Zhou Hui cast a half-smiling glance at Ran Deng. Knowing full well what kind of person his disciple was, he nevertheless nodded toward Ran Nine:—"Then let us take a look."

Ran Nine turned and mercilessly signaled downward with a hand gesture.

Ran Deng watched in despair as the contingent from the Array Hall—who had set up a massive war drum on the mountain slope at some unknown point—stepped forward. One of the array masters raised his mallets and struck the skin:

`"Dong——"`

`"Dong——"`

`"Dong-dong——"`

Accompanied by this booming rhythm, responsive drumbeats echoed from the far distance, and the ambient spiritual energy surged into motion.

[—"The spiritual energy... is gathering."](# "Speaker: Yan Bailang")

This time, Yan Bailang understood. Not only did he understand, but he perceived the movement of spiritual energy far more acutely than most.

He sensed it through his lifebound spirit pet.

Although Yan Bailang's Four-Clawed Five-Eyed Green-Faced Flood Dragon was no True Dragon, it still possessed the instinctive sensitivity of the dragon race. In its perception, the spiritual energy grew noticeably dense. A gale of spiritual energy was sweeping through the mountain valleys, tempting it to soar out, tumble through the clouds, and roar to the heavens. It was only the presence of a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse at the mountain summit that kept this vicious flood dragon from daring to stir up waves.

Naturally, Zhou Hui perceived it as well, offering a slight nod.

Ran Nine, however, was not yet satisfied. Since he had resolved to let Ran Deng bask in this glory, he naturally intended to execute it magnificently so that everyone could bear witness.

—"Lady Mo Bei,"-Ran Nine said, bowing with cupped hands toward Mo Bei.

Mo Bei nodded. Under Ran Deng's incredulous gaze, she stepped onto her flying sword and swooped outward.

Following Mo Bei into the sky were the sword cultivators of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Soaring through the spiritual currents, they fell into sword formations and began their aerial sword dance.

With this living benchmark, the empowering effect of spiritual energy upon cultivators became visible even to ordinary mortals:

Even the outsiders from Thousand Beasts Hall could clearly see that these sword cultivators were executing feats far beyond their nominal cultivation realms. The flowing spiritual energy grew increasingly concentrated amidst their swirling blades, condensing alongside the atmospheric moisture into stark white mist that blanketed the valleys beneath Mount Tianling. The mountain ridges, which had just been bathed in autumn, suddenly appeared frosted over with ice and snow. The vibrant scarlet hue of the sword cultivators' uniforms bloomed with breathtaking allure against the sea of white mist.

—"Well done,"-Zhou Hui praised once the sword cultivators concluded their routine, the array masters' drums fell silent, and Mo Bei returned to Heaven-Cleaving Chasm.

He nodded at Ran Nine, then lauded Mo Bei:

—"Bei'er's sword art has improved as well. You may consider advancing your cultivation realm."

—"Yes, Master,"-Mo Bei responded respectfully.

Although she usually acted nonchalant toward her fellow disciples, the corners of her lips couldn't help curling upward upon receiving Master's praise. Ran Nine, meanwhile, made no effort to conceal his triumphant smile.

Only Ran Deng glared at the pair with rimmed, bloodshot eyes—especially at Ran Nine—before sweeping her sleeves and slumping back into her seat.

***

Following Ran Deng's exit, Guan Ye stepped forward.

Beside him, Qin Xian held a massive, towering stack of documents, causing Yan Bailang's vision to go momentarily black.

—Speaking of which, for some inexplicable reason, even though it was the Immortal Venerable's own disciples presenting their homework, the person most terrified by the ordeal was the outsider—Sect Leader Yan Bailang of Thousand Beasts Hall.

Yan Bailang sat ramrod straight, bracing himself to listen to another round of heavenly scriptures.

And this time, an entire scroll's worth of heavenly scriptures.

Yet what brought Yan Bailang a sigh of relief was that, at Guan Ye's gesture, Qin Xian delivered the documents directly to Zhou Hui's side.

—"We conducted extensive measurements and established a spiritual energy model for the entire Mount Tianling region,"-Guan Ye said with 'humility.'—"Because the volume of data is immense and presenting it would consume excessive time, we shall not delay everyone. The documentation is right here; if anyone is interested in the future, you may examine and consult it at your convenience."

[If you put it that way, doesn't it make those of us who honestly delivered our oral presentation look completely foolish?!]

Ran Nine furrowed his brows in dissatisfaction.

Yet gazing at that colossal stack of documents, he had no choice but to concede that the other party had accomplished far more work than his own side. In the end, he could only shoot a look of [bitter exasperation](# "Literal CN: 恨其不争 (hèn qí bù zhēng) — resent iron for not turning into steel; frustration at someone failing to strive") toward Ran Deng.

Ran Deng sprawled across her seat like a dead dog, completely ignoring him.

—"The data collection is exceptionally thorough. It is evident great effort was expended,"-Zhou Hui praised as he inspected the documentation.—"However, right here on page nine... Well, let us leave that until after today! Once I have finished grading, you may redo it."

—"Yes, Master!"-Guan Ye beamed joyfully.

Judging by his radiant expression, one would think 'redoing it' was some miraculous stroke of fortune.

This expression not only sent a wave of numbness crawling across Yan Bailang's scalp, but even caused the seated Thousand Beasts Hall disciples to shrink backward in unison. Among them, cultivators like Xia Bing—who had once harbored notions of defecting to the Heavenly Spirit Sect—felt an even deeper, shuddering horror.

Not content with merely presenting data, Guan Ye continued:

—"Aside from pure data, I also crafted a magical artifact: a 'Spiritual Energy Regulation and Control Device.' Master, please critique."

As he spoke, Qin Xian directed disciples from the Artifact Refinement Hall to haul over an enormous metal crate.

—"Master, Master! I helped too!"-Mo Xi piped up, seizing the opportunity to squeeze right up to Zhou Hui's side:—"At least two-thirds of the materials inside were provided by me!"

Mo Xi's antics drew sidelong glances from the Thousand Beasts Hall contingent.

Such blatant, shameless credit-grabbing was typically the privilege of the most favored disciple. Sure enough, Zhou Hui smiled and ruffled the adorable youth's hair, while the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples wore expressions of total nonchalance, as if thoroughly accustomed to the sight.

[So even the Untiring Immortal Venerable plays favorites?]

The Thousand Beasts Hall crowd mused. A few individuals with less wholesome inclinations took note of Mo Xi and Mo Bei's identical facial features, sporting inexplicably lewd smiles.

Guan Ye maintained the honest, earnest demeanor of an artisan. Paying no heed to his junior brother's antics, he accompanied the Artifact Refinement disciples to install the metal crate directly at the mountain peak's wind gap—which was also the point of spiritual convergence.

Without anyone seeing what mechanisms he operated, a crisp *clack* rang out. The crate mechanically transformed, erecting a slender, soaring spire. Yet it was not entirely a tower; at its summit were mounted several colossal fan-like turbine blades.

—"What is this...?"-Yan Bailang muttered. He felt as though his side were mortals who had stepped into the cultivation world for the very first time, utterly ignorant of everything.

Zhou Hui's eldest disciple, Yuwen Xuan, smiled cheerfully and explained:

—"A [Spiritual Energy Windmill](# "Literal CN: 灵力风车 (línglì fēngchē) — industrial wind-turbine artifact designed to channel and regulate ambient spiritual energy")."

—"A spiritual energy windmill?"

Before Yan Bailang could inquire further, Zhou Hui's fourth disciple, Guan Ye, activated the so-called 'Spiritual Energy Windmill,' answering his question in real time.

The windmill whirred into motion. Perhaps because it faced away from the void, those colossal blades stirred not a single wisp of atmospheric wind. Yet every cultivator present felt the spiritual energy surge violently, gusting down toward the valleys below.

When that tempest of spiritual energy swept across the mountains, it instantly blew away the spiritual energy the array masters had gathered earlier. The white mist dispersed accordingly, but the spiritual energy had not vanished; rather, it settled evenly across the mountain wilderness.

Down below, the Mount Tianling mountain range abruptly blossomed into a vibrant scene of spring—a dazzling kaleidoscope of crimson blossoms and verdant greens, bursting with life.

—"Magnificent, magnificent—"-Yan Bailang murmured excitedly, eager to strike up a conversation with Guan Ye.

He was intensely tempted by this spiritual energy fan (X) windmill.

No sect in the Central Domain was free from the torment of spiritual energy storms. If one could obtain such a treasure to blow away the chaotic spiritual tempests, wouldn't that be sheer paradise?

Zhou Hui, however, did not seem to share his enthusiasm. Frowning at the windmill, he remarked to Guan Ye:

—"There still appears to be an issue with the core: insufficient energy. You should try blending purple gold with silver jade."

[You're still not satisfied with this?!]

[If my disciple presented such a magical treasure, I would shower him with lavish rewards!]

Yan Bailang thought indignantly.

Naturally, he could not comprehend that this treasure was not something 'presented,' but rather an assignment Zhou Hui had guided Guan Ye to design. Furthermore, such an artifact was merely an initial experimental prototype. Completing the entire Spiritual Energy Dam would require countless finished units, demanding that Guan Ye conduct further experiments and master more advanced principles.

Of course, not a single Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple paid any attention to Yan Bailang's inner thoughts.

Everyone maintained an attitude of earnest learning.

Guan Ye himself answered with complete sincerity:

—"I entertained similar thoughts, but encountered difficulties during metallurgical refinement. I suspect the catalyst—"

Beside them, the Artifact Refinement Hall disciples were already scribbling notes at lightning speed.

Although the process diverged entirely from what Yan Bailang had anticipated, Guan Ye's Q&A session proved to be the longest by far.

***

Following Guan Ye, the fifth disciple, Lu Shulong, walked up to Zhou Hui.

Unlike the previous disciples, Lu Shulong's hands were empty, and he brought no assistants. He did not look at all like someone prepared to deliver a presentation. He merely smiled at Zhou Hui:

—"Master, over the past few days, I monitored the growth of the vital flora on Mount Tianling. They are all well-behaved children, everything is normal. I also measured some data, but I imagine it cannot compare to Fourth Senior Brother's meticulous work, so I shall not embarrass myself here; I will deliver the records to your immortal cave later. The only thing worth mentioning is: I planted all the seeds you entrusted to me!"

Zhou Hui's face brightened with noticeably keener interest than before:

—"All of them?"

—"Every single one."

—"Good, very good, truly excellent!"-Zhou Hui nodded repeatedly, looking profoundly satisfied. None of the earlier disciples had elicited such high praise from him.

This left the Thousand Beasts Hall disciples thoroughly baffled, unable to fathom why the Untiring Immortal Venerable reacted this way.

Naturally, some minds leaned toward less wholesome explanations. For instance, Thousand Beasts Hall's Elder Bai Jun couldn't resist transmitting gossipy divine sense to his sect leader:

—"It seems the Untiring Immortal Venerable's tastes are quite ordinary after all, harboring a fondness for pretty youths... Sect Leader?"

He suddenly noticed his sect leader staring unblinkingly at Zhou Hui's fifth disciple, his nostrils slightly flared.

[No way? Does Sect Leader also have a taste for this type?!]

[But that is the Untiring Immortal Venerable's personal disciple!]

Bai Jun broke out in cold sweat, about to urge restraint, when he heard Yan Bailang mutter:

—"A pretty youth? No, it's not human at all!"

—"Ah?"

—"If only I could possess it, no matter what it requested, I would agree to everything..."-Yan Bailang murmured in a feverish, half-crazed whisper.

Listening to this, Bai Jun wished he could stuff a gag into his sect leader's mouth.

He looked at Lu Shulong in confusion. While he conceded the youth was indeed a rare, stunning beauty, Qu Qianxing—who was also present—was not inferior in the slightest. Why would his sect leader lose his composure to such an extent?

Fortunately, the Untiring Immortal Venerable did not notice Yan Bailang's lapse.

Having praised Lu Shulong, he cast an approving gaze across the rest of his disciples:

—"You are all good children, and you have done splendidly. Because each of you completed your assigned tasks with excellence, we can complete the first phase of the 'Spiritual Energy Dam' earlier than projected today."

At his words, a chorus of cheers erupted from the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples.

Evidently, this 'Spiritual Energy Dam' held immense significance for them.

—"Let Master handle this final step today!"-Zhou Hui smiled.—"The steps that follow will be up to you all."

Saying this, he raised his hand. Colossal waves of ambient spiritual energy instantly surged toward his palm.

With this monumental influx of spiritual currents, Yan Bailang finally snapped out of his trance-like fixation on Lu Shulong. He slumped into his chair with a gasp, while the remaining Thousand Beasts Hall members—who had not been granted seats—could not even remain standing, collapsing to their knees one after another.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples were not faring much better. Though shielded by the Immortal Venerable's protective ward, keeping them from appearing as utterly wretched as Thousand Beasts Hall, their faces still betrayed strain. Yet none were willing to lower their heads, gazing with exhilaration at the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Before their eyes, the spiritual energy converged and compressed within Zhou Hui's grasp, condensing from vapor to liquid, then solidifying into a high-density orb shimmering with mingled gold and silver.

[Is this the power of the Tribulation Transcendence realm?!]

Yan Bailang trembled violently from head to toe. He knew that even if a single fleck were chipped from that spiritual orb, the impact would suffice to obliterate him into ashes and disperse his soul. And if hurled against the earth before them, Heaven-Cleaving Chasm would surely suffer another gaping abyss.

Zhou Hui, naturally, had no intention of casting it downward. Instead, he hurled the orb toward the heavens, calling out to Lu Shulong:

—"Shulong, let me see the seeds you planted!"

—"Yes, Master!"

Lu Shulong arched his slender white neck, unleashing a resounding, piercing roar! His pale, youthful form began to stretch and elongate, sprouting iridescent scales as he soared into the sky.

In that instant, Bai Jun finally understood why his sect leader had lost his composure:

Lu Shulong was a dragon!

A [True Dragon](# "Literal CN: 真龙 (zhēnlóng) — primordial true dragon born of innate celestial energy, far surpassing mundane serpentine flood dragons")!

One had to understand, within the cultivation world, there were vast distinctions among dragons. For example, Yan Bailang's Green-Faced Flood Dragon, incapable of awakening spiritual intelligence, was mere livestock when compared to the dragon race of the Eastern Domain—though naturally, true dragons rarely submitted to human masters. Yet to the Eastern Domain's dragons, the True Dragons of the True Immortal Realm possessed far nobler bloodlines. It was said they were coalesced from innate primordial spiritual energy, capable of attaining the True Immortal realm with virtually no cultivation required.

For a beast tamer who shared cultivation with their spirit beasts, there was truly no greater temptation on earth.

Yet watching the white dragon chase the golden orb in the sky, and recalling the terrifying process by which that orb had been condensed, Bai Jun coughed dryly, forcefully snuffing out that sliver of greed in his heart.

[Out of our league! Completely out of our league!]

Things belonging to the Untiring Immortal Venerable were not something he could ever covet.

Besides, this mortal realm could hardly sustain a True Dragon, could it? While the cultivation realm's spiritual energy was relatively abundant, compared to the upper realm, it was practically a spiritual desert. A True Dragon in this world could only maintain human form to move about; this dragon form was practically a state of semi-suffocation, bound to drive it mad if sustained for too long.

Bai Jun comforted himself with a [sour grapes](# "Literal CN: 酸葡萄的心态 (suānpútao de xīntài) — sour grapes mentality") rationalization.

Yet the white dragon in the heavens showed no signs of spiritual depletion. It cavorted joyfully above the clouds, chasing the golden orb Zhou Hui had forged. And as it swam, something seemed to sprout forth.

[—"Is that a tree?"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"Trees are growing in the sky?"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"Is that an island? Soil is sprouting beneath the trees!"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"No, that's not right! Can't you see? That's Mount Tianling! Growing in the sky! It's Mount Tianling!"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

The Thousand Beasts Hall disciples erupted into chaotic clamor.

Aside from the direct disciples, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples had clearly not anticipated this development either. They gazed in stunned awe at the heavens, watching the embryonic silhouette of a mountain range take shape at the very dome of the sky, forming a mirrored reflection with the forests and ridges below.

Of course, this was merely a rough prototype; fully evolving into an entity identical to Mount Tianling would require vast amounts of time.

Yet in the cultivation world, the one thing never in short supply was time.

Beholding this spectacle, Yan Bailang was completely overwhelmed.

If seeing the spiritual energy orb earlier had instilled sheer terror, in this moment, he lost any lingering notion of ever opposing the Untiring Immortal Venerable—not that he had ever possessed the courage in the first place.

This method of reflecting a mountain range in the sky—even a True Immortal could achieve no more than this, right?

And this was not the end. When the inverted mountain range in the sky had roughly taken shape, the golden orb batted about by the white dragon had shrunk to the size of a chicken egg.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui raised his hand and barked:

—"Break!"

The egg-sized golden orb shattered in an instant, dissolving into a fine golden rain that drifted downward in sweeping veils. One portion flew upward, defying gravity toward the newly formed landmass in the sky (or falling toward it?), while the other drifted downward, scattering evenly across the true Mount Tianling mountain range.

Zhou Hui's voice resonated through the mountain forests:

—"Why aren't you meditating and cultivating?!"

In that instant, the disciples gazing blankly at the scene wore expressions of sudden enlightenment. Scrambling to seat themselves on the spot, they assumed lotus positions.

After all, every thread of this golden rain was pure, refined spiritual energy! Absorbing it properly would allow one's cultivation to leap several minor realms in a single breath!

What was an 'immortal opportunity'? This was it.

Even the disciples of Thousand Beasts Hall showed no hesitation, hurriedly sitting down to meditate.

Only Yan Bailang hesitated for several minutes, unsure whether he should maintain the dignity of a sect leader. But seeing one of his own disciples erupt in the iridescent glow of a cultivation breakthrough, he could no longer resist and began to meditate as well.

However, Yan Bailang was a Body Integration powerhouse after all. The moment he began meditating, the surrounding golden rain was drawn toward him in sweeping currents. The high-ranking Thousand Beasts Hall retainers seated near him suffered the most; Bai Jun, for instance, barely absorbed a few drops of spiritual rain, glaring at his sect leader with suppressed fury.

Yan Bailang, however, could not care less. Ever since entering the Body Integration realm, his cultivation had ground to a halt; his Green-Faced Flood Dragon's foundation was limited, unable to provide further assistance. Consequently, it had been centuries since he had felt the euphoria of surging power.

He greedily yearned for more, more...

—"Junior Brother, you are truly... insatiably greedy!"

At that moment, an ethereal, chilling voice abruptly resonated within Yan Bailang's mind. A coppery taste of blood surged in his throat, and only through swift reaction did he prevent himself from spitting out a mouthful of vital essence!

Yan Bailang's eyes widened in horror, probing into his sea of consciousness in panic:

[What is happening?! I clearly killed her! I slaughtered that inner demon!]

Indeed, there was no inner demon within Yan Bailang's sea of consciousness. Yet what made his soul freeze in terror was that, at some unknown point, a blood-red stone the size of a human head had crystallized within his consciousness. Upon its surface, a silhouette he never wished to see again stared down at him with icy malice.

Yan Bailang frantically withdrew his perception, gritting his teeth as he glared at Zhou Hui with venomous suspicion.

He felt he had been ambushed by Zhou Hui!

Otherwise, why would such a catastrophe manifest in his sea of consciousness? He had clearly slain his inner demon using that forbidden secret method!

Zhou Hui seemed temporarily oblivious to Yan Bailang, looking with concern at his disciple:

—"Bei'er, what is the matter?"

—"Master, a strange object appeared in my sea of consciousness,"-Mo Bei confessed openly.

She presented a stone to Zhou Hui.

From its material texture, it was identical to the object in Yan Bailang's mind. However, it was nowhere near as colossal as Yan Bailang's; Mo Bei's stone was merely the size of a pinky fingernail, and its color was not horrifyingly crimson, but a dull, neutral gray-white.

—"This is a [Heart-Questioning Stone](# "Literal CN: 问心石 (wènxīn shí) — spiritual stone manifesting in the sea of consciousness to expose inner doubts and unresolved psychological obsessions"),"-Zhou Hui explained upon inspecting it.—"When compressing the spiritual energy, I embedded subtle guidance within. If someone happens to possess an [unsteady Dao heart](# "Literal CN: 道心不稳 (dàoxīn bùwěn) — wavering spiritual conviction or subconscious emotional confusion"), it offers guidance."

—"Is that so?"-Mo Bei blinked blankly.—"So my Dao heart is unsteady?"

—"When you sense this stone, can you hear what it is asking you?"-Zhou Hui encouraged.—"Only by knowing where the problem lies can one resolve it."

`"..."` Hearing this, Mo Bei closed her eyes and focused for a moment, then shook her head:

—"No, I can't hear anything at all."

—"That indicates even your subconscious does not know what you are confused about,"-Zhou Hui said, furrowing his brows, though he soon shook his head:—"No rush. Take your time. You are at an age naturally prone to confusion; perhaps as you mature, this stone will dissolve on its own."

—"Understood..."-Mo Bei replied, though she continued to gaze at the stone with a bewildered expression.

Having comforted his disciple, Zhou Hui suddenly turned toward Yan Bailang:

—"Sect Leader Yan, what is the matter?"

—"No, no-nothing at all!"-Yan Bailang stammered, shaking his head frantically.

[So that thing is a Heart-Questioning Stone?!]

[Untiring truly meddles where he shouldn't!]

[Had I known, I would never have absorbed this cursed spiritual rain!]

Yan Bailang thought in fury. But the 'trouble' destabilizing his Dao heart was something that could never be exposed to outsiders! All Yan Bailang wanted now was to flee back to his sect immediately and use his secret method to slay the inner demon once more!

After all, if his current condition deteriorated further, it would no longer be a mere bottleneck—it would be outright [Qi Deviation](# "Literal CN: 走火入魔 (zǒuhuǒ rùmó) — cultivation backlash / descending into madness or demonhood").

Abandoning all pretense of etiquette, Yan Bailang stammered in haste:

—"I... I feel it is about time we take our leave."

—"There is still a banquet scheduled for later this evening..."-Zhou Hui offered, attempting to detain them.

—"No need!"-Yan Bailang barked at his subordinates:—"We are returning!"

With the sect leader's command issued, the Thousand Beasts Hall disciples had no choice but to rise reluctantly to their feet.

They had no desire to leave; after all, merely watching a presentation had yielded such a staggering immortal opportunity—who knew what bounties the evening banquet might hold!

Unfortunately, their sect leader was being tyrannical. Everyone sluggishly packed up and set off. No one noticed that among them, one disciple—Xia Bing—was desperately trying to conceal a crystal-like Heart-Questioning Stone deep into the recesses of her sea of consciousness, as if burying it out of sight could pretend it didn't exist...
