# Chapter 247: Seeking One's Own Death

—"The children of my family are truly simple-minded!"

Having observed the deeds of his eldest and second disciples, Zhou Hui couldn't help but sigh in exclamation.

Hearing this exclamation, Peilan could hardly hold himself back:

—"Simple-minded in what possible way?!"

—"Regardless of what they experienced, the things they do are actually no different,"-Zhou Hui said, his expression turning helpless yet stern.-"Everything Yuwen Xuan does is solely for the sake of 'eating.' As for Qianxing... I previously thought him reasonably clever, but looking at it now, his mental circuitry is really only slightly more complicated than Xuan'er's. No matter how many times he encounters an enemy, his first reaction is always to [drag the other party down in mutual destruction](# "Literal CN: 同归于尽 (tóngguīyújìn) — perish together")—it was so before he began cultivation, it was so in the sixth cycle, and it is still so in this lifetime! Does he think of himself as a landmine?!"

—"You're actually angry?!"-Peilan asked in astonishment.

—"Of course I am angry! If they act like this, reducing themselves to something [neither human nor ghost](# "Literal CN: 人不人鬼不鬼 (rén bù rén guǐ bù guǐ) — in a grotesque, wretched state"), am I supposed to praise them for making something of themselves?"-Zhou Hui arched an eyebrow in retort.

[Your attitude certainly doesn't show it. If anything, I'd say you're quite keen on praising them.]

The corner of Peilan's mouth twitched.

Even Zhou Hui's old friend developed such thoughts, to say nothing of the intensely vigilant "His Majesty the Celestial Emperor." The emperor was finally unable to maintain the so-called dignity of a sovereign, letting out a wretched shriek:

—"Just what on earth do they want to do?!"

Yet for the "Celestial Emperor" to panic like this was also because Yuwen Xuan in gray-lamb form and Qu Qianxing in Demon Sovereign form had directly touched the very bedrock of his existence.

Especially after Tunhuo was swallowed by the blood-jade, speckles of blood began oozing from the "Celestial Emperor's" shadow, erupting and spreading around him like a blood-mist, coalescing into new solid blood-jade entities sprouting in the surroundings, looking as though they were about to devour the "Celestial Emperor" as well.

In truth, had Zhou Hui not plucked the "Celestial Emperor" back a step ahead of time, this blood-jade would have spread even faster; swallowing the "Celestial Emperor" in a single instant was by no means impossible.

Only upon reaching this point did the "Celestial Emperor" finally comprehend his current predicament. He cried in sheer terror:

—"Are they planning to eat me as well?! How dare they do such a thing?! And how could they possibly achieve it?!"

Peilan regarded the "Celestial Emperor" with cold eyes:

—"Why wouldn't they be able to achieve it?"

—"Because We are the embodiment of Heavenly Destiny!"-the "Celestial Emperor" roared.

[What sort of explanation is that?]

Peilan let out a cold scoff.

He looked down on this "His Majesty the Celestial Emperor" all the more, even beginning to suspect whether Zhou Hui had accidentally hauled back an impostor.

—If this was the genuine article, that was even worse. Peilan could not fathom by what principle the Heavenly Court had managed to exist across such vast eons with such an incompetent ruler and such a rotten system. And what was the ascension so many people in the cultivation realm yearned for even worth? When his disciples who ascended departed for a Heavenly Court like this, what sort of end had they met?

The mere thought of this made Peilan feel thoroughly sick to his stomach. He asked Zhou Hui:

—"Why don't you simply let that Taotie of yours eat him?"

—"Y-You people...!"-The "Celestial Emperor" seemed finally to realize that Zhou Hui was the one holding his future fate in his hands, at last sputtering out something that wasn't complete nonsense.-"You cannot do that! If We... if I am killed and eaten, the Heavenly Dao will fall into chaos, and you will also... What I mean is, Untiring... Lord Immortal Venerable! I am willing to take you as my master! I will tell you everything I know about the Primordial God... that is, what you call the 'Creation Machine,' including how to obtain Its acknowledgment!"

—"Pfft,"-Peilan chuckled aloud.-"You certainly [know how to bend and stretch](# "Literal CN: 能屈能伸 (néng qū néng shēn) — adaptable, swallowing pride when outmatched"). Fortunately, Untiring's disciples aren't here—especially that Eldest Disciple who's getting ready to eat you. Otherwise, the moment you voiced taking him as master, he'd carve you up into eighteen dishes."

—"Then... not taking him as master is fine too..."-the "Celestial Emperor" murmured weakly.

He was indeed someone who "knew how to bend and stretch." Laughably enough, this was entirely the [lingering blessing](# "Literal CN: 余泽 (yúzé) — residual benefit or shade left by a predecessor") of Tian Yan—whenever an emperor's court held a few veteran officials who had outlived several generations of monarchs, the emperor would more or less learn to [read the room](# "Literal CN: 识时务 (shí shíwù) — recognize the realities of the situation"). It seemed even the Heavenly Court was no exception to this rule.

Only, the more he acted like this, the more Peilan found him an eyesore.

—"Besides, how dare you continue blabbering about the Heavenly Dao falling into chaos?"-Peilan sneered coldly. Even an outsider like him who hadn't recovered his past-life memories understood how Zhou Hui's Eldest and Second Disciples had been provoked to this state, yet only this "His Majesty"—one of the chief culprits behind it all—still wore an expression of complete incomprehension.-"If the Heavenly Court hadn't forcibly seized control of the Heavenly Dao, and if you hadn't conjured up that divine projection of Untiring, would his disciples have been pushed to such extremes?"

[—"No, what I mean is..."](# "Speaker: Celestial Emperor")

Peilan sneered:

—"Honestly! If you ask me, you people getting eaten is nothing short of well-deserved."

—"All right, Peilan, stop terrifying him,"-in the end, Zhou Hui shook his head.

His gentle attitude caused hope to surge within the "Celestial Emperor's" heart. But without giving the other party the slightest chance to [climb up the pole](# "Literal CN: 顺着杆子往上爬 — push one's luck / seize an opening"), Zhou Hui continued:

—"The 'Heavenly Dao' this 'His Majesty' speaks of right now is not the 'Heavenly Dao' formulated by the Heavenly Court to rule and exploit its subordinate worlds, but rather the underlying code by which the entire Heavenly Court exists in the first place."

—"Underlying... [toad? Code?](# "Literal CN: 底层……带马？ — Peilan mishears 代码 (dàimǎ, code) as 带马 (dàimǎ, carrying a horse)") What sort of thing is that?"-Peilan narrowed his eyes.

Seeing that Zhou Hui looked ready to explain, he frantically waved his bald tail:

—"Stop, stop, stop! You don't need to lecture... Fine, explain it, but keep it as brief as possible. I don't think right now is the time for you to hold class."

[Brief...] That's actually quite difficult.

Zhou Hui's brows furrowed.

Because this would require starting all the way back from the origins of the Creation Machine.

He already knew that the machine was a suppression device specifically manufactured by an advanced civilization of a certain universe to handle the collision along the borders of several universes. The device stabilized the energy generated by the overlapping collisions of cosmic space, thereby producing this "cultivation world" rich in "spiritual energy (cosmic energy)" with nested macro and micro dimensions.

Yet this "cultivation world" had not evolved by mere chance; it had been guided by the machine.

Indeed, the cultivation realm was guided into being.

Whatever thoughts the people who built the machine had harbored—perhaps propaganda and commemoration of their own civilization? (All civilizations loved doing such things anyway)—in short, they had crammed into that unfortunate machine an enormous volume of historical data concerning their own civilization. This included not only various miraculous landmarks, famous figures, and legends, but even web novel adaptations and derivative fiction spun from diverse mythological folklore.

Once this overly energy-rich space began to stabilize, life started to emerge. These living beings alarmed the machine whose sole purpose was spatial stabilization, and it factored these organisms into the variables impacting spatial stability.

—This was entirely natural. Indigenous organisms spawned in such an ultra-high energy concentration zone were extraordinarily hazardous; any single one dropped into a low-energy universe outside would constitute a catastrophic biological hazard. Their very existence could indeed jeopardize spatial stability once more.

Only, what the designers of this stabilization system likely never anticipated was that instead of wiping out those organisms, the machine actually searched its database and located a "matching protocol":

Precisely—that collection of mythological web novels and derivative fiction.

The genre of "immortal cultivation" indeed conformed to the standards of an "ultra-high energy (high-magic) system."

From this, the machine guided the development of living civilization in this universe, formulating for them a complete "cultivation" system.

[...When I have time, I should still research that machine further. It doesn't merely govern energy control; it even encompasses 'apotheosis (dimensional ascension).' Heavens know what bizarre monstrosity its AI system has evolved into.]

Zhou Hui thought to himself, having discovered a brand-new research target.

However, explaining all this to Peilan was far too cumbersome. He hadn't explained it in detail back then, and he had even less time now. Thus, complying with Peilan's request, Zhou Hui stated simply:

—"Just think of that 'Heavenly Dao' as the projection of a certain external civilization. Whether it is the creation of heaven and earth, the glory of the demon court, or the human court supplanting the demon court, all of it is merely a story framework derived from that civilization's history. Even ourselves... Back then, didn't I discover 'another world's me' inside that machine? Thinking carefully, I ought to check everyone. Very likely, everyone has an 'archetype,' and our personalities were molded and nurtured according to those 'archetypes.'"-Zhou Hui said.

—"That's truly disgusting,"-Peilan said with profound revulsion.

No one enjoyed being a mere "copy," and backlash from a clone was an eternal horror story across the cultivation realm.

—"I, on the other hand, care very little,"-Zhou Hui said dismissively. Possessing memories of his "past life," he didn't feel it had much impact. However:—"Whether an archetype or a projection, learn what can be learned, and discard what cannot be inherited. In the final analysis, even if it truly were the same person, placed in different positions and living under different environments, the choices one makes will be starkly different. The 'past' is never an excuse to shackle a person from marching forward."

Speaking to this point, he lifted his eyes toward the "Celestial Emperor":

—"This is true for people, and even more so for worlds."

—"Constructing a history based on a work of fiction is already thoroughly absurd. What is more absurd is treating that story as the 'Heavenly Dao' to be enforced upon that foundation, believing it cannot be defied."

—"However, it has just about reached its end."

—"Stories written by the human race have settings that only draft up to the humans. Once beyond that, there are no further 'reference materials,' and 'history' consequently enters a state of stagnation. Thus, for this joke of a Heavenly Court to sustain itself to this day, nearly half the credit belongs to that machine, does it not? Regrettably, such stability was bound to encounter variables sooner or later. Monsters and demons—chiefly the demon race—have grown ever more numerous. When the machine can no longer sustain it, to say nothing of the Heavenly Court, it will likely initiate a 'Re-Genesis' at the cost of obliterating the three thousand worlds!"

At the mention of "Re-Genesis," the fur on Peilan's tail bristled straight up, and the "Celestial Emperor" shrieked even louder:

—"H-How could that be possible...?!"

Alas, no matter how much he rejected such a possibility, he could not dispute it. Furthermore, without Zhou Hui needing to explain further, the "Creation Machine" itself offered its affirmation:

Even before the "Celestial Emperor" could accept it, an extraordinarily pure divine power descended from the other side.

This divine power was absolute power of rules. So long as one grasped a single shred of it, one possessed the authority to overwrite the rules of the three thousand major and minor worlds. Yet now, this power condensed into an eye-catching, magnificent gossamer veil, draping lightly over Zhou Hui's head and shoulders like mist over a lotus pool bathed in moonlight—mysterious and sacred, compelling onlookers to prostrate in worship.

Seeing this scene, the "Celestial Emperor's" face turned deathly pale in an instant.

He knew better than anyone that this power originated from the "Primordial God (Creation Machine)." It was the machine's backdoor, and also the "method to obtain acknowledgment" he had just touted to win Zhou Hui over.

This was the very reason the "Celestial Emperor" could bypass the process of "discarding humanity, succumbing to divinity, and becoming a higher-dimensional god who was no longer human," while still possessing authority rivaling—or even surpassing—the gods.

As for how it was achieved, it was actually quite "simple."

Just as the "Celestial Emperor" had literally meant: one merely had to obtain the "acknowledgment of the Primordial God."

In other words, after computing, the machine would determine the most suitable candidate to help implement its stabilization protocol, and then grant that candidate a portion of clearance permissions. (As for humans acquiring power, misunderstanding it, and taking it upon themselves to conduct elaborate 'rituals' to appease the machine spirit, that was another matter entirely—the machine did not care.) In short, evaluated from the perspective of "stability," upholding the rule of the Heavenly Court was the most stable option; thus, the lineage of the "Celestial Emperor" had been perpetuated.

Yet even the first Celestial Emperor, upon being selected, had merely received a single slender thread.

That single thread was sufficient for the Celestial Emperor to rule the three thousand major and minor worlds, with enough surplus authority to distribute among his loyal subordinates, such as Tian Yan and Tian Shou.

Now, Zhou Hui had received this power as well.

And it was not a single slender thread, but an entire piece of gossamer veil. It did not look in the least like "receiving the acknowledgment of the Primordial God," but rather like "the Creation Machine was yearning to obtain the acknowledgment of the Untiring Immortal Venerable."

This also indirectly proved that Zhou Hui's previous hypothesis was correct. Perhaps precisely because he was too correct, that machine hoped to enlist his assistance in drafting future protocols for civilizational development (energy stabilization).

Realizing this, how could the "Celestial Emperor" not break into a cold sweat?

He gazed despairingly at Zhou Hui, feeling as though his final trump card was gone (or perhaps had never existed from the start).

Zhou Hui, however, paid it little mind.

Whether apotheosis or a machine backdoor, neither was something he needed or could master at his present stage. Rather than having it forcibly shoved down his throat to digest, he preferred to research it slowly. Besides, the crux of the issue did not lie here.

Thus, Zhou Hui gently brushed aside the gossamer veil atop his head.

—"There is no need for this. Giving me administrative permissions serves little purpose,"-perceiving the origin of the power, Zhou Hui attempted to converse with the other party.-"I cannot help you design protocols."

That machine—who knew whether it was the onboard AI, or a [machine spirit](# "Literal CN: 机精 (jījīng) — machine essence / transformed mechanical spirit") that had gained sentience only after arriving in this universe? (Zhou Hui would have to research this later)—seemed not particularly pleased by Zhou Hui's words.

Fortunately, it did not force him.

The "gossamer veil" was easily brushed aside by Zhou Hui, but as it slid down, it seemed to cling like static electricity, adhering closely to Zhou Hui's white robes, reluctant to part from the hem. At the same time, golden arcs of light flickered within the veil, making that gossamer shroud of power appear all the more alluring.

Watching this, Zhou Hui found it somewhat amusing.

—"I do not mean to refuse you entirely. Even without permissions in this area, I am still quite interested in your energy stabilization protocols,"-Zhou Hui continued.

The "gossamer veil" seemed to brighten up; it wriggled and looped around Zhou Hui's finger.

This time, Zhou Hui did not brush it off entirely, but he had no intention of altering his stance:

—"However, I believe you have already done enough. Besides, if the ultimate fallback is obliteration anyway, why not let go and allow the children of this civilization to stride forward on their own? They are grown enough now; they ought to decide the future of their civilization for themselves, rather than relying solely on your designs... Peilan, what's wrong with your eyes?"

Peilan covered his face.

He had just been frantically winking at Zhou Hui until his eyes nearly cramped, and *this* was Untiring's reaction?!

[He looks more like an automaton—a living NPC—than that strange machine!]

—"What I mean is, don't be so absolute right off the bat,"-Peilan said in sheer resignation, pointing toward the cloud mirror.-"At the very least, your disciples need a solution."

Zhou Hui visibly froze for a second.

He didn't look at the cloud mirror, his gaze drifting with rare evasiveness:

—"Those two children have indeed strayed a bit down the wrong path... But this isn't entirely their fault. In that lifetime, there was no one to teach them or look after them, and 'I' was merely an unfeeling divine projection. They must have been very panicked..."

[Panicked enough to prepare to devour the entire world?!]

—"If they were merely settling grudges and ate the 'chief culprit' or even the Heavenly Court, that wouldn't matter!"-Peilan snapped furiously, glaring at the "chief culprit."

The "Celestial Emperor" had completely lost his nerve. His lips trembled as if wanting to speak, but after glancing at the gossamer veil still wrapped around Zhou Hui, not a single word came out.

Ignoring him, Peilan raged at Zhou Hui:

—"The problem is, just what are they planning to do right now?!"

He pointed at the cloud mirror, refusing to let Zhou Hui evade.

And in that brief span of time while Zhou Hui explained the "origins of the world," the blood-jade crystal within the cloud mirror was expanding at a mind-boggling speed.

First, from human size, it grew to the size of a tree capable of tearing off palace roofs; then, from an ordinary towering tree, it grew to a gigantic sky-piercing timber linking several realms above and below; and finally, from a sky-piercing timber, it became a spiritual vein spanning dimensions and traversing the void—the existence known as a "dragon vein."

When this "blood-jade" grew into a "dragon vein," the Immortal-Demon Realm was merely a tiny scale upon its body.

Seeing this scene, Yuwen Xuan could no longer maintain his chubby gray-lamb form. He ballooned back into his original form, drooling as he stared at this "dragon vein," longing to swallow the entire vein—and every world connected to it—in a single gulp.

—"Is it ready?"-he urged impatiently.

—"It hasn't fully matured..."-Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing frowned, pinching his slender fingers in a posture of divination.-"Theoretically, 'It' should devour Its blood kin—the existence known as the 'Celestial Emperor'—to complete the final link. But the 'Celestial Emperor' doesn't seem to be in the Heavenly Court. I don't know what whisked him away; I cannot pinpoint his location!"

—"You mean, it will fail?"

Taotie's razor-sharp teeth swiveled toward Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing. It seemed that even though the two were currently in a cooperative partnership, this substandard Eldest Senior Brother didn't mind eating his junior brother in the slightest.

The Demon Sovereign didn't care at all; in fact, he was used to it:

—"Stop constantly thinking about eating me. How many times must I say it? If you want to eat, wait until everything is over. Besides, he's merely a 'Celestial Emperor'; we have better materials to replace him... Tsk. To think that even at the very end, something is still protecting a creature like that."

Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing's face darkened, pinching his fingertips hard, clearly aggrieved that the "Celestial Emperor" had escaped.

After all, his revenge targets included the Celestial Emperor; letting the biggest fish get away left him profoundly dissatisfied.

In contrast, Taotie didn't care at all.

Rather than seeking revenge, he merely wanted to eat. In any case, Qu Qianxing had promised he would get to eat. As for whether a missing ingredient during the "cooking process" resulted in a substitution, so long as it didn't ruin the final flavor, it didn't matter. Not to mention, he already knew what Qu Qianxing's "backup ingredient" was; in Taotie's eyes, that thing would only taste even better, so he didn't mind in the slightest.

Rolling his lantern-bright eyes, Taotie laughed:

—"Fine, I'll wait... In the meantime, I can have a little snack, right?"

Saying so, he abruptly slammed a colossal paw toward a certain direction.

The paw whipped up a gale of astral wind, sweeping forward with [crushing momentum](# "Literal CN: 摧枯拉朽 (cuīkūlāxiǔ) — sweeping away dry decay and rotten wood") and even gouging titanic crimson claw marks in midair, ripping through space in a single blow.

Instantly, a screeching grating sound rang out, like claws scraping across ice.

Accompanied by this collision, two familiar figures emerged from behind the spatial rift:

Mo Bei, Lu Shulong.

The maiden and the silver dragon.

Among them, Mo Bei gripped her Frost Soft Sword in a combat stance; that grating noise just now had come from her soft sword deflecting the astral wind. Even now, she did not lower her guard, facing Taotie in full battle readiness, poised to stab her senior brother several times at any moment.

On the contrary, Lu Shulong, the elder brother, cowered behind his junior sister, shouting in disarray:

—"Eldest Senior Brother, you can't do this! Master will be furious!"

—"So what if he's angry?"-Taotie retorted oddly. He paused, sticking out his tongue to lick his razor teeth.-"Ah, the 'Fifth Junior Brother' and 'Little Junior Sister' of our timeline are already gone. So that means you came from another timeline, didn't you? There's another Master... Can I eat him 'too'?"

His saliva dripped down.

Lu Shulong's eyes widened in sheer horror.

Every disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect knew that to their [incorrigible, shameless](# "Literal CN: 混不吝 (hùnbùlìn) — reckless, fear-nothing rogue") Eldest Senior Brother, only Master was special, and only Master's name could keep Eldest Senior Brother in line. But to *this* "Eldest Senior Brother," even Master was useless?!

—"It's no use, Fifth Senior Brother. This Taotie isn't our Eldest Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei spoke up at this moment. Having more memories of that lifetime than Lu Shulong, she had figured everything out.-"Across multiple cycles, Eldest Senior Brother never seemed to recover his memories, which meant Master had to raise him from scratch every single time, didn't he? ...Only this one had a Master who was a fake from beginning to end. In other words, he never once met the true Master! Naturally, he harbors not a shred of affection for Master, and even less for us!"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Listening to her, Lu Shulong's expression turned solemn.

Thinking back carefully, it was indeed as Mo Bei said.

In every lifetime, Eldest Senior Brother had dutifully played the part of Eldest Senior Brother solely for Master's sake.

Even in the cycle where Master gave them away, because Tao Zhu alone couldn't fully keep Yuwen Xuan in check, Master still had to visit and tend to him frequently. That was why Yuwen Xuan had fought Qu Qianxing in that lifetime—in Yuwen Xuan's eyes back then, even though Untiring was not his formal master, he was still his favorite elder. What was best of all: because the Immortal Venerable hadn't accepted a single disciple, *he was his alone*!! No one was allowed to steal him away!!!

But the cycle without Master had been entirely different. Never mind Yuwen Xuan, even Mo Bei and Mo Xi had been affected.

Because the two youngest had been taken as disciples for too short a time and possessed too shallow a cultivation, recovering memories each time was exceedingly difficult, let alone retaining lingering emotional attachments. And yin spirits happened to be the type most sensitive to emotion.

Encountering a "counterfeit Master," Mo Bei and Mo Xi felt none of his benevolence; naturally, they harbored no sentiment toward that Master, to the point of committing [treasonous, patricidal acts](# "Literal CN: 欺师灭祖 (qī shī miè zǔ) — betraying one's master and exterminating the ancestors") later on.

What about Yuwen Xuan?

Lu Shulong wracked his brain to recall. Back then, he had been too consumed by sorrow and regret over Master's disappearance to pay much attention to Eldest Senior Brother. Thinking carefully, Eldest Senior Brother had been surprisingly "obedient" in that lifetime, hardly ever trying to eat him—most of the time, the fellow had simply been drooling over Master.

...So what this Taotie had wanted to eat most of all was that "counterfeit Master"!

Of course, under that premise, he wouldn't spare anything else he fancied eating either. For that lifetime to end up in such utter chaos, with every junior brother and sister exploited to such a degree, this Taotie had undeniably played a part.

The silver dragon drew in a sharp breath.

Instantly disregarding whether his draconic aura might further stimulate Taotie's appetite, he unleashed his dragon might without reservation, enveloping Mo Bei. His dragon tail coiled tightly around her, no longer letting his junior sister face the enemy alone.

Yes—since he wasn't "Eldest Senior Brother," this Taotie was the most dangerous enemy imaginable.

Just as he had proven through everything he had done!

—"Second Senior Brother, are you the same?"-Ignoring the unfeeling Taotie, he turned toward the glamorous Demon Sovereign.-"Do you want to 'eat' Master as well?"

Unlike Taotie, this Demon Sovereign certainly did not lack memories.

On the contrary, this Qu Qianxing ought to possess every memory except for this present lifetime!

If Lu Shulong guessed correctly, the reason Second Senior Brother had been unable to recover his memories in this lifetime was because all his memories had been completely claimed by this Lord Demon Sovereign.

In other words, *this* was the "original Second Senior Brother" who had weathered six full lifetimes of unions and partings, and who had ultimately proposed stripping Lu Shulong's tendons, flaying his skin, and sacrificing his dragon soul to investigate the counterfeit Master!

Thinking of this, Lu Shulong's teeth itched with fury.

Yet precisely because of this, he could even less afford to give up on this Second Senior Brother.

Controlling his temper, he tried to persuade him:

—"I have already returned to the 'true Master's' side. Second Senior Brother, don't you want to come back?"

—"...Can I still go back?"

The Demon Sovereign let out a shrill laugh.

He finally turned his face, looking straight at Lu Shulong:

—"After I drove Third Junior Sister to her death, abandoned Fourth Junior Brother, killed you, and exploited Little Junior Sister and Little Junior Brother until every drop of value was wrung dry? Do you think I still have the face to see Master?"

[So you know full well what you did!]

Lu Shulong said dryly:

—"Master might..."

—"This isn't a question of what Master thinks, but a question of myself! From the moment I killed you in that lifetime... no, from the moment I steeled my resolve to do those things, I was no longer qualified to be Master's disciple,"-the Demon Sovereign stated with resolute finality.

Crimson demonic qi completely shrouded his body, preventing Lu Shulong and Mo Bei from seeing his expression.

Faced with Qu Qianxing's grim resolve, Lu Shulong was momentarily struck dumb.

Memories or no memories, this Second Senior Brother always managed to do things that left him completely speechless. It was a pity Fourth Senior Brother wasn't here, and that Taotie was on his side; there was no one to beat him up.

The Demon Sovereign's tone, however, turned joyous:

—"Yet your presence here proves my choice was correct, does it not? The true Master is truly worthy of being the true Master; he indeed rewound time and pulled everyone back to his side! Therefore, as agreed, leave the rest to me. By sacrificing merely one cycle, I will resolve the 'root of everything' once and for all."

—"Rest assured, I will not let you and Master continue reincarnating,"-the Demon Sovereign said tenderly.

[...Wait, what you want to resolve isn't the 'root of everything,' it's 'everything' period!]

Lu Shulong wanted to roar.

Before he could speak, the Demon Sovereign pulled out an object from within the demonic qi with a heavy *thud*, making Lu Shulong even more terrified.

—"Speaking of which, I promised you I would dispose of 'this,' didn't I?"-the Demon Sovereign said, tapping on the object.

That was..."Master"...

At least, an entity whose outward appearance was identical to their Master.

He... *It* possessed the exact same long silver hair as Zhou Hui, the exact same handsome face, the exact same porcelain-white skin nearly transparent like the surrounding ice, and even an aura that seemed identical at first glance—cold and detached.

Untiring's disciples knew, however, that this was merely the persona Master adopted with strangers. Their true Master was fundamentally a gentle, warm, kind-hearted man; that was why they could never accept that "counterfeit" in the sixth cycle.

Yet evidently, frozen inside the ice coffin right now was the "counterfeit Master" from the sixth cycle.

Seeing It, Lu Shulong's and Mo Bei's expressions turned rather ugly.

Not only because It dredged up memories of regret, but also because of the "counterfeit's" present condition.

Although Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing had tried his best to restore this false Master to a dignified appearance—such that It looked little different from the genuine Zhou Hui—whether it was the undeniable incision marks across the forehead and throat, or the stench of rot that even the ice coffin failed to suppress, both proved that Qu Qianxing had committed unspeakable, sacrilegious deeds upon this fake Master—and more than one. Perhaps not letting Taotie take a few bites out of It was already his final mercy.

Still, this "counterfeit" possessed Master's face!

Both Lu Shulong and Mo Bei found it unbearable to watch.

What was more, this Demon Sovereign looked poised to complete the final item of the [patricidal, master-slaying combo meal](# "Literal CN: 欺师灭祖套餐 (qī shī miè zǔ tàocān) — master-betraying combo set") right before their eyes.

—"This blood-jade is almost fully nurtured; only the final step remains. A pity that Celestial Emperor managed to flee—rest assured, I will not let him go—but because someone did something extraneous, things here cannot be delayed any longer. I can only substitute him with an equivalent 'ingredient,'"-the Demon Sovereign explained, his slender fingers caressing the ice coffin with faux intimacy.-"Conveniently, our 'good Master' is suitable in every regard, even possessing divinity of unknown origin. Freezing the Abyss Sea will be more than sufficient."

Saying so, he exerted force with his hand, toppling the ice coffin toward the "dragon vein."

The collision between ice coffin and blood-jade made no sound, for as if conscious, the blood-jade erupted in plumes of crimson mist before the coffin even touched it. Like tentacles of blood, they swallowed the ice coffin—and the jade-like, moonlit figure within—in an instant.

Even knowing It wasn't the true Master, Mo Bei let out a gasp.

Amidst his own gasp, Lu Shulong caught Qu Qianxing's words:

—"Freezing the Abyss Sea?"

His dragon eyes widened, staring once more at that "dragon vein."

As a True Dragon, his eyes could easily pierce dimensional constraints. He had simply been distracted by Taotie and the Demon Sovereign earlier; coupled with the blood-jade taking the form of a True Dragon—looking akin to Taotie's dining platter—Lu Shulong had been unwilling to look too closely, terrified that a few more glances would turn him into a dish on Taotie's table.

Now, looking closely, Lu Shulong discovered that the blood-jade's shape and trajectory were not merely for "plating"; they were intimately bound to the Abyss Sea.

The Abyss Sea was the fountainhead connecting the three thousand worlds: a cradle of life in lower dimensions, and a conduit of surging energy in higher dimensions. Of course, even Taotie and the Demon Sovereign couldn't devour the entire Abyss Sea in one bite. But Qu Qianxing had somehow frozen a branch of the Abyss Sea across both spatial and temporal dimensions!

Seeing this, Lu Shulong was far more horrified than when suspecting Qu Qianxing wanted Taotie to devour the Heavenly Court. He sucked in a cold breath:

—"You want him to eat the Abyss Sea?!"

—"Indeed!"

—"Have you lost your mind?!"-Lu Shulong couldn't help but roar.-"This is devouring time and space, completely obliterating a past! Even our very existence will be disrupted! How on earth did you come up with—"

—"Isn't that for the best?"-the Demon Sovereign replied as if it were only natural.

His dazzling, glamorous face radiated the sheer madness unique to the demon race:

—"Only by completely obliterating past and future can we leave behind for the 'true Master' a world that has never suffered taint, and is never shackled by any power!"

* * *

—"Never suffered taint? Never shackled by any power?"-Peilan repeated.

Until just now, he had thought Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing merely wanted to destroy the world—which was already dreadful enough. But listening to the conversation between Lu Shulong and Qu Qianxing, he suddenly felt that the [terrible stunt](# "Literal CN: 整烂活 (zhěng lìnhuó) — pulling an atrocious, cringe-worthy stunt") this newly minted Demon Sovereign was pulling was likely far more atrocious than he had imagined.

...Indeed, "merely" destroying the world wouldn't have made Untiring feel so guilty.

However, when it came to multi-dimensional knowledge, Peilan, who harbored zero desire for ascension, couldn't match a True Dragon born as a higher-dimensional organism. Not bothering to get entangled in technicalities, he simply glanced at Zhou Hui:

—"What does that mean?"

—"It roughly means temporally erasing the existence of the Heavenly Court and other worlds, leaving behind solely a minor world free from external interference,"-Zhou Hui said slowly.

Not looking at Peilan's expression, he paused, then defended his disciple:

—"There was no other choice; time was too tight. Qianxing's cultivation was [artificially accelerated](# "Literal CN: 拔苗助长 (bámiáozhùzhǎng) — pulling up seedlings to make them grow faster") through entering the demonic path. No matter how much of a genius he is, he could only achieve this much. He overlooked the macro-level data impact... However, given his abilities, to self-study to this level without me ever teaching him, and to manage to control a small tributary of the Abyss Sea, is already remarkably impressive..."

—"Is *that* the point?!"-Peilan felt as though this master-disciple pair was about to make him spit blood for the nth time.-"If he actually managed to pull it off, were you really planning to become the god of his new world?"

—"How could that be? If he actually managed it, I would hang him from the treetop of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Peilan")

—"Rest assured,"-Zhou Hui soothed Peilan.-"In his current state, he simply channeled too much demonic qi for the sake of boosting cultivation, causing his personality and thoughts to become demonized. That is why he is so extreme."

[...Are you certain it's merely the effect of demonization? That un-demonized disciple at your house isn't much better.]

Peilan rolled his eyes. Even the "Celestial Emperor," who knew little of Qu Qianxing, looked deeply skeptical.

* * *

Even Lu Shulong couldn't take it anymore.

He gave up reasoning with the demonized Qu Qianxing, yet still held Mo Bei tightly in his coils, refusing to let her face that demonic star.

Almost out of sheer desperation, Lu Shulong yelled:

—"Eldest Senior Brother—aren't you going to do something about this—?!"

—"Me?"-Taotie asked in surprise, then burst into laughter.-"Little snake, don't worry, I won't leave you out. It's just that you're too small, never enough to fill the crevices between my teeth. Right now, you can only serve as an after-dinner dessert..."

—"Piss off!"-Lu Shulong sneered rudely.-"You haven't even seen Master's face! What kind of senior brother do you think you are?!"

Saying so, he whipped his tail forcefully, launching a chubby, ball-shaped critter high into the air, and letting it obey the laws of gravity to slam straight down onto his head.

That critter bounced springily several times before coming to a steady rest right between the silver dragon's two horns.

Yet despite such an undignified entrance, Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing's eyes widened slightly. Looking at Taotie and then at that little thing, he subconsciously unleashed demonic qi across his entire body.

—"Yuwen Xuan?"-Qu Qianxing called out.

He was somewhat uncertain.

Because power was not measured by volume, and the ferocious aura radiating from that wad of a creature was far too immense, completely overpowering both the True Dragon and the other (?) Taotie. Even Qu Qianxing, who had completely transformed into a demon, felt as though he occupied the very bottom of the food chain—as if the other party didn't even need to open its mouth, capable of devouring him with its aura alone.

It was a miracle Lu Shulong dared to place such a thing upon his own head.

—"It devoured the true form?!"-Taotie also felt visibly uneasy, staring at that wad of a lamb in contemplation.

At the same time, Taotie secretly sent a divine sense transmission to the Demon Sovereign:

[—"Things are troublesome. I need you to hold it off in a moment."](# "Speaker: Taotie")

—"You can't eat it? Isn't it merely a newborn version of you?"-the Demon Sovereign asked in puzzlement.

—"Not exactly..."-Taotie rolled his massive eyes, swallowing the other half of his words.

In truth, Taotie had never made things entirely clear to his "Second Junior Brother": he was merely one fragment of Taotie. Every time Master remade the world, a new fragment was sent to Master's side. In other words, unlike Qu Qianxing, he did not possess the memories of previous cycles; he had only experienced the counterfeit sect. In fact, his feelings toward his junior brothers—including Qu Qianxing—were completely fabricated, regarding them merely as reserve ingredients.

Conversely, if that 'thing' over there had devoured the true form, the memories of being raised five (six) times by Master had all transferred into that body. Thus, the one over there was the true "main body."

Taotie was certain that if Qu Qianxing knew this, he would definitely consider the other side to be his "Senior Brother."

When it came to obsession with their sect, Qu Qianxing was no different from that dragon.

Naturally, Taotie did not wish for the ingredient he had "reared" for so long to go awry at the final moment. He tried to dupe Qu Qianxing:

—"Who knows what it just ate; it smells foul all over. As for me, I dutifully went hungry for so long under you; my power is bound to be inferior right now. That's why you must stall it. Once I swallow the Abyss Sea, I can naturally devour it!"

—"Fine,"-Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing agreed briskly, seemingly harboring zero suspicion.

In truth, it wasn't that he trusted Taotie all that much; it was simply that having gambled and sacrificed everything, whether this "Eldest Senior Brother" was genuine or not didn't really matter to him. In any case, in the end...

Qu Qianxing lowered his cicada-wing eyelashes.

Conversely, that wad of a lamb atop the silver dragon's head let out a scoff:

—"Aiya, no need to be so secretive! I won't disturb you two in the least—if you want to eat, eat quickly! Once you're done eating, we can settle our accounts, yeah?"

He had visibly "overheard" the divine sense transmission between Taotie and the Demon Sovereign, leaving them startled and uncertain.

Lu Shulong was also deeply dissatisfied:

—"Eldest Senior Brother, what do you mean? Are you going to let them devour the Abyss Sea?"

—"I think it's just fine if they eat it,"-Yuwen Xuan said carelessly, pointing a chubby hoof toward the center of the blood-jade.-"If Master sees that thing, it wouldn't be very good, would it?"

[That thing...?]

Lu Shulong and Mo Bei looked over, finally noticing the human figure at the center of the blood-jade.

That was the "vessel" harboring the Celestial Emperor's bloodline painstakingly nurtured by Tian Yan, as well as the "formation core" designed by Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing to connect to the Heavenly Court. It was precisely because of this presence that the Demon Sovereign had the confidence to let Taotie devour the rules and guardian power represented by the Heavenly Court.

Of course, as the blood-jade expanded, that humanoid was no longer in the form of a youth; it had grown a bit older, taking on the appearance of a young man, looking completely mature.

—"That thing has far too deep an origin with our sect,"-Yuwen Xuan said vigilantly.-"If Master sees it, he will definitely accept it as a disciple! I don't want another junior brother! Let them eat it!"

[...Just for this reason?!]

Lu Shulong was speechless.

Yet this time, he didn't utter a word, because he didn't want another junior brother either.

On the contrary, Mo Bei asked:

—"Then why let that sheep eat it? That doesn't sound like you, Eldest Senior Brother. Don't you consider all your junior brothers and sisters to be your reserve rations? This one isn't even one yet, so why don't you eat it?"

[Do you truly not know or are you feigning ignorance? That's your child!]

Yuwen Xuan glanced at Mo Bei.

His scant remaining humanity actually managed to function with great reluctance at such a moment. Yuwen Xuan did not [poke through the paper window](# "Literal CN: 捅破窗户纸 (tǒngpò chuānghuzhǐ) — lay bare the unvoiced truth"), merely chuckling:

—"I already said there's a deep origin! If I eat it, I might get scolded by Master. So let him eat it! Once he's finished eating, I'll eat him! In any case, we're both 'me'—fragments have to return to the main body sooner or later, don't they?"

—"In the end, you still plan to eat it!"-Taotie sneered coldly. Harboring none of those scruples, he wanted to lay everything bare:-"If you really do that, aren't you still eating your 'Little Junior Sister's'..."

—"Shut up!"

This time, it wasn't Yuwen Xuan, but Qu Qianxing who jabbed Taotie heavily in the gut with demonic qi:

—"Aren't you going to eat? Since the enemy underestimates us, hurry up and eat!"

He averted his eyes from Mo Bei.

Mo Bei said nothing.

Thus, only Taotie let out a couple of sinister sneers, opening his gaping maw toward the blood-jade—

—"Aaaah, finally escaped!"

At this moment, a voice suddenly chimed in.

Everyone froze for a second. Following the sound, they saw a door abruptly open in midair, and a head poked out from behind it:

—"Let me see, is this the way back..."

The person behind the door didn't finish speaking.

Anyone suddenly stared down by a Taotie, a True Dragon, and a demon-qi-cloaked Demon Sovereign (Yuwen Xuan and Mo Bei were so small their presence could be overlooked) would lose their voice.

—"I took the wrong door, my apologies,"-the newcomer swiftly slammed the door shut and retreated inside.

Two seconds later, the door opened again, and that head poked out once more:

—"Wait, you look like Untiring's disciples?"

—"...Sikong Yao,"-Yuwen Xuan also recognized who it was.-"That unlucky bastard from Carefree Palace who was hunted down by Master."

—"Can't you people remember anything good?"-Sikong Yao complained. Noticing the look in the eyes of the group outside seemed distinctly off, he hurried to say:-"The grudges between me and your Master are a thing of the past! Besides, I'm currently together with your Master's friends! Come over here, Jiuzhai, and Old Ran—this is your martial sisters' father, you know!"

He tried to pull over the people beside him, but the unyielding killing intent freezing the air made his movements lock up.

The instincts honed from countless escapes told Sikong Yao that right now was definitely not a good time to put on the airs of an elder. If anything, it wasn't even a good time to be lingering in this godforsaken place.

As if to prove his terrible premonition, the glamorous young man shrouded in demonic qi laughed:

—"They're all people Master knows!"

—"Mhm, Senior Sister's father..."

The True Dragon laughed as well.

Regardless of what opposing sides they had stood on just a moment ago, in this very heartbeat, they reached a complete consensus:

[—"Then we have no choice but to silence them."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing & Lu Shulong")
