# Chapter 145: The Daily Life of a True Immortal (第145章 真仙的日常)

[← Previous Chapter](144_Chapter_144.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](146_Chapter_146.md)

---

Naturally, it was impossible for Zhou Hui to go sit for [the civil service exam](# "Literal CN: 考编 (kǎobiān) — taking the competitive public examination to secure an official government establishment post (bianzhi)").

Although Zhou Hui didn't doubt that he could pass, it simply took far too long.

To begin with, the examination was held only once a year—which translated to over three hundred years in the lower world. By the time he actually passed, the [daylilies would have grown stone-cold](# "Literal CN: 等得黄花菜都凉了 (děng de huánghuācài dōu liáng le) — waiting so long the food has gone cold / far too late").

Even if he entered the Heavenly Court, he wouldn't gain immediate access to its inner core.

Quite the opposite, in fact. Passing the exam merely made one an entry-level civil servant—or rather, a bottom-rung heavenly official. Once sworn into office, one couldn't disobey Heavenly Court decrees. If he were assigned to be a [Protector of the Horses](# "Literal CN: 弼马温 (Bìmǎwēn) — the menial post given to Sun Wukong in Journey to the West; keeper of the heavenly stables"), he wouldn't even have the leeway to decline. If he had to slowly climb the bureaucratic ladder through seniority, heaven only knew how many millennia that would take.

Besides, Zhou Hui knew himself well: he was thoroughly unsuited for bureaucratic politics.

—"We must find a more efficient approach,"-Zhou Hui murmured.

At the very least, he needed to secure baseline intelligence on the upper realm as well as authorization to move about freely.

Stumbling around like a headless fly certainly wouldn't do—especially when the upper realm ran at an accelerated tempo. He couldn't afford to waste time.

At this, Yuwen Xuan's eyes rolled craftily in his woolly head:

—"Why would Master need to lift a finger for something like that? We can just snatch a few True Immortals and infiltrate the Heavenly Court!"

—"Infiltrate?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Precisely, Lord Master,"-Yuwen Xuan prompted.-"A god can't possibly micromanage every single chore personally, right? Surely He has subordinates or something?"

—"He does,"-Zhou Hui frowned, sorting through the remnants of the entity's memories.-"He even has a cohort of heavenly soldiers under his command. But they don't seem particularly useful, as there is zero chance of them being transferred in the short term."

This 'short term' referred to upper-realm time.

According to the 'god's' memories, as beings blessed with infinite lifespans, the Heavenly Court was not merely glacially inefficient; official appointments were practically carved in stone. A single post rarely changed hands once in several thousand years.

For the lower realm, that was long enough for life itself to evolve an entirely new cycle.

This was precisely why the gods and immortals of the upper realm were forbidden from falling in love or bearing children. Immortals had no such concept as retirement. When a single occupant held a post until the [seas dried and rocks crumbled](# "Literal CN: 天荒地老 (tiānhuāng-dìlǎo) — till the end of time / eternity"), there was simply no room left for anyone else, even if those people were their own flesh and blood.

Yuwen Xuan, however, held a differing opinion:

—"If there are no promotion vacancies, we can just manufacture an opportunity! We could grab a few first to inspect them. Perhaps there's a suitable candidate among the bunch?"

—"A suitable candidate? You aren't plotting to eat those True Immortals, are you?"-Zhou Hui asked suspiciously, eyeing Yuwen Xuan's uncharacteristic enthusiasm.

—"How could I? With an entire god already sitting right here, why would I bother eating scraps that wouldn't even [get stuck between my teeth](# "Literal CN: 塞牙缝都不够 (sāi yáfèng dōu bù gòu) — not even enough to fill the gaps between teeth / a measly snack")?!"-Yuwen Xuan scoffed, putting on an arrogant air.-"Master, you shouldn't forget that I am a Beast Tamer after all. When it comes to things like remote pastoral management, I happen to know a thing or two."

[You're wearing a sheep pelt and lecturing me about 'beast taming'? Remote pastoral management, my foot...]

Zhou Hui paused, giving him an odd look:

—"This 'remote pastoral management' of yours wouldn't happen to refer to that young lady from Thousand Beasts Hall, would it?"

He had heard that through Yuwen Xuan's clandestine 'efforts,' that young woman had become a core figure of her sect. She had even reached the grand finals of the Immortal Arts Assembly and reportedly commanded immense prestige within her order.

Come to think of it, when Yuwen Xuan had sneaked off to cause havoc at their sect previously, he had only managed to blend in by following her around.

Zhou Hui had simply never imagined that Yuwen Xuan considered this part of 'beast taming.'

A phantom stomachache twitched inside Zhou Hui.

The Little Black Lamb puffed out its woolly little chest, looking thoroughly pleased with itself:

—"So Master still remembers! Then you can rest easy—I'm exceptionally experienced in this."

[The only thing you're experienced in is hanging from the peach tree!]

[It's only because you haven't been hung up recently that you're getting so full of yourself, isn't it?!]

The corner of Zhou Hui's mouth twitched.

Still, since they couldn't return just yet, he had no choice but to let him posture for a moment.

Besides, those True Immortals and heavenly soldiers couldn't be left unattended.

Although Zhou Hui had laid down restrictions preventing other entities from noticing anything amiss with the 'god,' those True Immortals were the beings closest to Him. There was no telling when one of them might peer past the barrier and spot something irregular.

Zhou Hui had no intention of taking that risk.

—"Very well, let us go take a look,"-Zhou Hui said.

The Little Black Lamb sprang to its feet with a thump:

—"Let me choose! Let me pick! I will definitely select the variety with the finest meat... *cough*, the variety best suited for herding!"

[You really do just want to eat them, don't you?]

—"Why bother choosing?"-Zhou Hui said coolly.-"Their 'god' is already gone; what value do they have left? In that case, let us simply replace them all."

—"Baaah~~"-the Little Black Lamb bleated.

Compared to his master, he felt his own temperament was positively gentle, docile, and adorable.

---

Upper Realm.

The 67th Southern Heavenly Gate.

Xun Zhou was squatting at the threshold of the Southern Heavenly Gate as usual—[lacking the proper posture whether standing or sitting](# "Literal CN: 站没站像，坐没坐像 (zhàn méi zhàn xiàng, zuò méi zuò xiàng) — slouching without decorum / poor bearing"). Had it not been for the golden armor designated for gatekeeper heavenly generals draped over his shoulders, he would have looked virtually indistinguishable from a street beggar. He had been reprimanded for this countless times.

Xun Zhou, however, felt there was no need to fuss over such trivial details.

While the Heavenly Court had an abundance of regulations, they were mostly pointless, not to mention bafflingly shoddy in places.

For instance, what on earth was the 'Sixty-Seventh' Southern Heavenly Gate supposed to be?

And why were all celestial gates leading to the lower realms called 'Southern Heavenly Gates'? This gate didn't even face south!

Xun Zhou's mind leaped from one thought to another, eventually forgetting what he had been pondering in the first place.

His colleague flew down from a higher cloud pavilion and caught sight of his goofy, vacant expression.

—"Xun Zhou, there you go again slouching around. Watch out or the High God will catch you,"-his colleague called out.

Xun Zhou didn't budge an inch, merely lazily tilting his head back to glance upward.

Truth be told, he could see it even without looking up.

A colossal humanoid light source stood towering atop the sea of clouds, outshining even the golden light of the sun, radiating an awe-inspiring, terrifying aura.

When Xun Zhou had laid eyes on this 'god' for the first time, he too had felt genuine reverence from the bottom of his heart. Yet after thousands of years on gate duty, he was thoroughly numb to it.

At times, he would even entertain the blasphemous thought: did that radiant humanoid entity actually care about their existence?

Though He frequently handed down all manner of obnoxious orders, He never concerned Himself with what they were doing or how they behaved. As long as it didn't disrupt their assignments, one could even violate Heavenly Laws without consequence.

Recalling this, Xun Zhou chuckled:

—"How could the High God ever deign to notice tiny ants scuttling at His feet?"

—"You really have no filter on your mouth,"-his colleague warned.-"If this ever reaches the High God's ears, you'll be in for it!"

Xun Zhou paid it no mind. He knew this colleague didn't truly revere the deity; the fellow was simply bored out of his skull and looking for cheap amusement by perching on the moral high ground.

Xun Zhou, however, had zero desire to entertain anyone.

Changing the subject, he asked his colleague:

—"Why did you fly down here? Did the High God issue some new decree?"

—"It's not an order, just a bit of an errand,"-his colleague replied.-"Didn't Fei Huo head down to the lower realm on patrol? He stumbled across an amusing little novelty and claims it might serve as a New Year gift."

—"Oh..."-Xun Zhou nodded offhandedly.

While smuggling items up from the lower realms without authorization technically violated Heavenly Laws, True Immortals who descended repeatedly engaged in it with impunity. As a gatekeeper, Xun Zhou had no wish to make enemies, so he naturally turned a blind eye.

Especially when it was a treasure destined to serve as a New Year gift.

The so-called New Year gift was something the High God presented to His backing patrons in the Heavenly Court whenever a major festive occasion rolled around—no, certainly not a bribe. How could the Heavenly Court ever partake in such crude mortal vulgarities? It was merely a gesture of deep respect toward one's superiors.

Xun Zhou naturally wouldn't dream of obstructing it.

Hearing his colleague's explanation, he subconsciously peered toward the cloud line:

—"Ah, speak of the devil! That should be Fei Huo returning."

His colleague looked over as well.

Seeing a silhouette emerge from the far side of the clouds, darting straight toward the celestial gate without hesitation, he knew one of their own had returned. If it had been a newly ascended newcomer from below, they would inevitably have been stunned by the colossal deity's divine majesty. Those capable of successfully setting foot on the Ascension Platform were individuals of exceptional mental fortitude; quite a few had been so frightened that they tumbled straight back into the void, swept away by temporal-spatial turbulence—effectively throwing away their lives immediately after ascending.

This terrifying divine pressure was guaranteed to take effect on any ascender beholding the deity for the first time, serving as one of the Heavenly Court's methods to cow them into submission. In all his millennia guarding the gate, Xun Zhou had never seen anyone immune to it.

Xun Zhou had guessed right: the newcomer was indeed Fei Huo.

Catching sight of Xun Zhou and their fellow immortal colleague, Fei Huo crowed in triumph:

—"Look at the good stuff I brought back!?"

—"What is it? What is it?"-the colleague piped up eagerly.-"Is it even rarer than that hundred-thousand-year-old demon core you brought back last year?"

—"What did that thing amount to? Nothing more than an ancient beast! What I brought back this time is an entire world!"-Fei Huo proclaimed, opening his mustard-seed artifact.

Intrigued, Xun Zhou leaned over to take a look.

It truly was a 'world.'

Inside that artifact box lay mountains, rivers, and even oceans. Miniature human beings, tinier than ants and resembling specks of dust, inhabited this domain. Only True Immortals possessed the divine sight required to perceive them.

The condition of these tiny beings, however, was dire.

Abruptly wrenched from their native space, unable to see the Golden Crow or Jade Rabbit, and with their world's cosmic laws thrown into chaos, massive numbers of tiny mortals were perishing in droves. Those who remained were in the throes of an apocalypse, weeping and wailing in despair.

As a gatekeeping heavenly general, Xun Zhou naturally commanded extraordinary ocular powers and could see every detail with crystalline clarity. An immediate wave of discomfort washed over him.

—"...So many have died already. Can they even be kept alive?"

—"Plenty are still kicking!"-Fei Huo laughed.-"Don't look down on such a tiny world—they actually know how to cultivate! There are even Mahayana cultivators among them. Even with the change in environment, they should manage to survive for quite a while."

—"Still..."-Xun Zhou pondered how to voice his unease without offending the fellow.

Before Xun Zhou could find the words, however, his colleague let out a piercing shriek—the kind of screech someone would make upon discovering a cockroach skittering across their floor:

—"What kind of foul monstrosity did you bring back?!"

—"A... a pocket world?"-Fei Huo answered in bewilderment, startled by the scream.

—"You know it's a pocket world?! A pocket world with living creatures inside!"-his colleague shrieked in disgust.-"Do you have any idea how filthy this thing is?! What if it's tainted with chaotic demonic qi or wicked miasma? How will you explain this to the High God?!"

—"It won't be..."

—"According to protocol, it must be purified! Immediately, right now, this instant!"

—"Wait a moment! Finding a miniature world with living beings is extraordinarily rare! If you purify it, they'll all drop dead! There won't be anything novel left!"-Fei Huo protested.-"I went through tremendous trouble to secure this!"

Clearly, what he cared about was not the lives within, but the novelty value of the miniature world.

His colleague cared even less:

—"You shouldn't have brought such filth back in the first place! And to present it as a New Year gift—what on earth is rattling around inside your skull?! I don't care! This thing must be purified; that is the regulation!"

—"But..."

—"Are you intending to violate Heavenly Laws?"-the colleague demanded, glaring daggers.

Fei Huo choked on his words.

While nobody cared about Heavenly Laws in daily life, someone deliberately filing a formal complaint was another matter entirely. This colleague was definitely not the type to show mercy in that regard.

Seeing that he had 'persuaded' Fei Huo, the colleague let out a smug scoff.

He turned to Xun Zhou and commanded:

—"You, purify this."

—"Why should I have to do it?"-Xun Zhou replied in dissatisfaction.

He watched as, accompanied by his colleague's screeching tirade, another wave of tiny humans inside the miniature world perished. The tiny beings clearly couldn't withstand noise of that magnitude, to say nothing of those literally blown away by the force of his breath.

Xun Zhou felt that this colleague could wipe out the entire world simply with his bad breath.

Of course, even if his colleague did nothing, under Fei Huo's rough handling, this tiny world wouldn't have lasted long anyway.

Nevertheless, Xun Zhou had no desire to be the executioner.

[Why should two idiot colleagues commit atrocities, only for the karmic slaughter to land on my head?!]

Yet his colleague was remarkably adept at using rules to browbeat others:

—"As a heavenly general guarding the celestial gate, are you neglecting your duties? Don't forget: unless a lower-realm entity possesses cultivation high enough to ascend to the Ascension Platform on their own, entering the celestial gate violates Heavenly Laws! Even taking a step back, since this is an offering to be presented to the deity, it must undergo inspection and purification first!"

—"Purifying it strips away all its value,"-Fei Huo continued to mumble.

Driven to wit's end by the bickering, Xun Zhou raised his hand:

—"Fine, fine... Huh?"

Xun Zhou's hand paused halfway through forming a spell seal.

—"What is it?"-Fei Huo asked.

—"I just felt a sudden palpitation,"-Xun Zhou said.-"I have a feeling something terrible is about to happen."

Even high-level cultivators possessed such premonitions, let alone True Immortals.

Xun Zhou was exceptionally sensitive in this regard.

Depending on their cultivation paths, however, there were also True Immortals who were extraordinarily dull-witted.

Unfortunately, Xun Zhou's colleague was one of them.

—"You're not inventing an excuse just to evade the Heavenly Laws, are you?"-the colleague sneered.

—"You don't need to constantly hold the Heavenly Laws over my head,"-Xun Zhou said impatiently.-"I'm speaking the truth—ah!"

Xun Zhou lifted his head in sheer terror, staring at the sky.

His colleague was still complaining:

—"What do you mean, holding rules over your head? I'm simply telling you to act according to protocol! Is there something wrong with demanding you perform your assigned duties? Stop putting on an act to frighten people... What are you doing?!"

The colleague glared at Fei Huo, who had been tugging frantically at his sleeve.

Fei Huo pointed toward the sky with a trembling finger:

—"N-No! Look up there!"

—"What could possibly be up there? With the High God standing guard, even if Heavenly Demons invaded, they couldn't stir up a single ripple—*Aaaagh! What is that?!*"

The True Immortal let out another ear-splitting shriek.

High in the heavens, a colossal black hole had torn open. Everything below—palaces, clouds, mist, and even True Immortals—was being swept violently into that gaping abyss.

Because Xun Zhou and his two companions were stationed near the Ascension Platform on the lower cloud layers, they were temporarily spared. Soon, however, the 'black hole' seemed to notice them and swiveled in their direction.

In an instant, the three immortals lost all control over their bodies, hurtling skyward. No matter how desperately they channeled their lifelong cultivation, they couldn't resist in the slightest.

And as they drew nearer to that 'black hole,' they realized with shock that it was an opening of cloth!

That 'black hole' was actually the sleeve opening of a wide-sleeved daoist robe!

—"No, what is going on?!"-Fei Huo screamed.

—"High God, save us!"-his colleague shrieked.

Xun Zhou cast one final glance back toward that miniature world. To his astonishment, he found that the fragile little realm—which he could have crushed to dust with a single squeeze of his hand—remained completely motionless amidst the monstrous suction.

Why...? Still, it hardly mattered now.

[When Fei Huo forcibly snatched this tiny world away, did it look something like this?]

Xun Zhou wondered.

The next moment, he and the Southern Heavenly Gate he guarded were sucked into the black hole together.

A few seconds after the massive black hole had completely devoured every True Immortal, a jade-white hand reached down from the heavens, setting the Heavenly Court administrative offices—including the Southern Heavenly Gate—back in their original places, one by one, like someone tidying a set of building blocks.

Except the True Immortals stationed here were gone without a trace, leaving only the radiant humanoid entity still towering silently above the clouds.

It was as if nothing had ever happened.

---

The Little Black Lamb trotted in circles around Zhou Hui's feet, showering him with sweet flattery:

—"As expected of Master, capturing so many True Immortals in one single breath!"

Zhou Hui, however, had no intention of handing the storage pouch in his hand to the Little Black Lamb:

—"Don't try to eat everything you lay eyes on. Consuming such filth will only ruin your stomach."

—"I already said I don't want to eat them! Besides, my stomach isn't that fragile,"-the Little Black Lamb grumbled.

Yet seeing Master frowning as he stared at the miniature world left below the Southern Heavenly Gate—an item dropped by the captured True Immortals—the lamb sensibly shut its mouth.

[There is no question about it: those True Immortals went courting death, and Master is genuinely furious.]

Zhou Hui did not outwardly display his wrath. He simply retrieved another mustard-seed realm from his robes, made rapid adjustments to its environmental parameters, and gently, painstakingly transferred the miniature world inside.

Instantly, the apocalyptic cataclysms raging across that tiny world ground to a halt.

At least, they ceased temporarily.

Regrettably, even Zhou Hui could not fully resurrect the countless lives already lost in this tiny realm.

Zhou Hui sighed softly and stowed the mustard-seed space away.

From start to finish, he had not uttered a single word of criticism, yet the Little Black Lamb felt that those True Immortals might have been better off being eaten by him! At the very least, that would have been a comparatively dignified way to die.

[It seems 'remote pastoral management' is pretty much out of the question.]

The Little Black Lamb thought with a twinge of disappointment.

At a juncture like this, however, not even he dared to stroke Master's scales the wrong way.

Zhou Hui, for his part, kept his anger concealed.

He swept his gaze across the vicinity of the celestial gate.

This gate rested upon the lower cloud tiers. Not far off stood an Ascension Platform identical to the one in the lower realm—it was evident that the Ascension Platform in White Jade Capital back in his home world had indeed been a 1:1 replica of the True Immortal Realm's, exactly as legends claimed.

Ascending to the platform, bowing before the High God, and then passing through the Southern Heavenly Gate—such was the proper procedure for ascension.

Other cultivators beholding the deity upon ascension would feel bone-deep reverence, unlike Zhou Hui, whose instinctive first reaction had been to murder the god to keep his secrets safe.

And slaying the god hadn't been enough; he had scooped up the True Immortals in one fell swoop as well.

After capturing all the True Immortals, Zhou Hui did not interrogate them immediately—lest in a fit of rage he simply crushed them all to death—opting instead to inspect the Heavenly Court administrative office first.

A single tour was sufficient for Zhou Hui to deduce a great deal.

For instance, the primary duty of this Heavenly Court office was clearly to assist that 'god' in managing the lower realms, alongside a modest amount of reception work for newly ascended mortals. The numbers were low because ascension quotas were strictly rationed; while some True Immortals abused their authority to smuggle unscheduled ascenders up, doing so typically cannibalized the quotas allotted to other cultivators.

Furthermore, fortunate as it was, despite being a Heavenly Court branch, their interactions with the central Heavenly Court were far from frequent.

Roughly once a year for an annual evaluation.

Only then would the Heavenly Court dispatch Heavenly Officials to conduct an inspection.

Yet this was one 'year' in the upper realm. Even though nearly half of this upper-realm year had already elapsed, more than two hundred years remained in the lower realm.

Counting the timeline, wouldn't that coincide roughly with when the 'Child of Destiny' was slated to arrive?

In short, matters were not quite as dire as initially feared.

Moreover, while the Heavenly Court was notoriously sluggish and inept, they maintained a habit Zhou Hui rather appreciated: every directive was issued via official written missives. There was no need to worry about being exposed due to prolonged silence from failing to hit predetermined milestones.

—"The problem remains: how do we infiltrate the Heavenly Court..."-Zhou Hui mumbled.

The Little Black Lamb peeked at Master's expression, unsure if it was wise to speak up right now.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui did not remain lost in thought for long, as a fluctuation of spiritual energy near the Ascension Stairs caught his attention.

Borrowing the eyes of the 'god'—which had already been altered into an illusion by Zhou Hui—he looked over and spotted a cultivator flying up from the lower realm.

Without a doubt, this was an ascender.

Like all newly ascended cultivators, this newcomer was initially startled by the colossal humanoid light source. Cowed by the divine pressure, he trembled as he bowed in midair before gingerly landing upon the Ascension Platform.

Yet after landing on the platform, he inexplicably stopped in his tracks, merely staring nervously toward the Southern Heavenly Gate as if waiting for someone.

Before long, a True Immortal clad in similar daoist robes—the [unestablished variety](# "Literal CN: 没有编制的那种 (méiyǒu biānzhì de nà zhǒng) — those without an official government quota / lacking a civil service establishment post")—hurried out from the Southern Heavenly Gate, greeting him with delight:

—"Junior Brother, you're finally here!"

—"Senior Brother! I made it!"

The two fellow disciples clasped hands, their eyes brimming with hot tears.

Evidently, these two were cultivators who had ascended from the same world. This was hardly uncommon; before ties with the True Immortal Realm were severed, ascenders from Zhou Hui's world had likewise been greeted by fellow sect members and compatriots upon arrival.

It was a perfectly normal form of mutual aid.

At this moment, however, Zhou Hui eyed the pair with considerable interest.

—"These must be the main recruits for [the civil service exam](# "Literal CN: 考编 (kǎobiān) — taking the competitive public examination to secure an official government establishment post (bianzhi)"),"-Zhou Hui noted.

—"Indeed?"-Yuwen Xuan blurted.

[Just what on earth is 'sitting for the civil service exam'?]

The Little Black Lamb grasped the general idea and asked cautiously:

—"Does Master feel they have utility value?"

—"At the very least, they appear cleaner than those True Immortals on the official payroll,"-Zhou Hui sneered.

He strolled toward the pair of disciples:

—"Come. Let us go meet them."

As he spoke, his form shifted seamlessly into the appearance of Xun Zhou.
