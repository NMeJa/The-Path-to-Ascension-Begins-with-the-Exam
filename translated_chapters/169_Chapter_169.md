# Chapter 169: Illusions

It seemed this misunderstanding—that "those who don't study diligently will be hung from tree branches"—would remain unresolved for the time being.

Zhou Hui mused with an aching head.

He resolved to find an opportunity to properly explain the matter to Shui Huang'er and the other high-ranking demons, and perhaps discuss "proper pedagogical methods."

Unfortunately, Shui Huang'er had no inkling of Zhou Hui's thoughts.

Feeling as though she had received tremendous validation from Mo Bei, she enthusiastically led Zhou Hui's entourage from the lower branches upward, showcasing how the demon race's [compulsory education](# "Literal CN: 义务教育 (yìwù jiàoyù) — compulsory education; state-mandated basic schooling") was being promoted.

Then again, when it came to dreading school, children all across the world were much the same regardless of race.

Most demons were especially forthright by nature, expressing their emotions with far greater intensity, which made their resistance all the more dramatic.

Thus, as they flew upward along the trunk, they beheld the entire Fire Abyss Sacred Tree in [utter bedlam](# "Literal CN: 鸡飞狗跳 (jī fēi gǒu tiào) — chickens flying and dogs jumping / total chaos and commotion"). Across every single tier, young demons were scurrying frantically in all directions while branches hung heavily laden with dangling captives.

Having seen too much of it, Zhou Hui grew somewhat numb, and a question even sprouted in his mind:

[Does the demon race detest studying that much?]

It couldn't be helped; Zhou Hui had never witnessed such a spectacle before.

Although he knew that most people disliked going to school, his awareness was strictly limited to mere conceptual knowledge. Zhou Hui himself had never harbored any aversion to learning, and aside from Yuwen Xuan, his disciples always feigned absolute obedience before him. Having spent multiple lifetimes as a lifelong bachelor, Zhou Hui had certainly never experienced the tragic and epic drama of human toddlers throwing tantrums, thrashing, and rolling across the floor simply because they refused to go to kindergarten or school. Thus, Zhou Hui had always earnestly assumed that everyone else's "not wanting to go to school" was roughly on par with his own [burnout phases](# "Literal CN: 倦怠期 (juàndài qī) — burnout period / exhaustion phase") of ["not wanting to grind anymore"](# "Literal CN: 不想卷了 (bù xiǎng juǎn le) — refusing to participate in involution / competitive rat-racing") and ["lying flat to coast into second place."](# "Literal CN: 躺平考个第二 (tǎngpíng kǎo gè dì-èr) — slacking off and taking second place with zero effort")

This mass truancy among the demon youth had genuinely opened Zhou Hui's eyes.

[Isn't this basically the same as Yuwen Xuan?]

[In that case... even hanging them from trees might actually be necessary?]

Zhou Hui's principles began their rapid descent down the slippery slope.

His expression grew progressively sterner.

Seeing this expression on his face, the accompanying Tao Zhu could not suppress an involuntary shudder.

Watching this chaotic scene of turmoil along the way, Boss Tao found it utterly impossible to muster the heartbroken indignation of a stern elder. No matter who accompanied Zhou Hui—even someone like Tao Zhu who had watched Zhou Hui grow up—no one could ever feel capable of maintaining the stance of a senior before him. What was more, having recently acted as Yuwen Xuan's "accomplice," Tao Zhu was still terrified of being punished by Zhou Hui!

Thus, confronted with the pitiable demon cubs, Boss Tao felt only [the sorrow of a fox mourning the hare's demise](# "Literal CN: 兔死狐悲 (tù sǐ hú bēi) — the fox laments when the hare dies / sympathy for a fellow victim in similar peril").

Yet as he glanced around, setting aside Zhou Hui, whose stern countenance seemed to suggest that the demon race's punishments were still not severe enough, Zhou Hui's two youngest disciples were likewise completely expressionless, looking entirely unfazed by the spectacles before them. This only made Boss Tao feel all the more terrified.

Naturally, that demon maiden who happily exhibited such execution scenes to visiting guests was exceedingly suspicious as well.

In fact, the entire demon race seemed deeply suspicious.

The more Boss Tao looked, the more he felt as though he were attending a [Feast at Swan Goose Gate](# "Historical / Idiom: 鸿门宴 (Hóngmén Yàn) — the Banquet at Hongmen, an infamous feast masking murderous intent and a lethal trap"):

[Regarding that incident at Langhuan, Zhou Hui only punished his own disciple and didn't string me up... Could he be waiting to do it here?!]

Right as Tao Zhu was pondering in trembling trepidation, he heard the "demon maiden" Shui Huang'er speak:

[—"A bit further up lies the Sun Wheel Palace."](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

—"Wait, you're taking us to the Sun Wheel Palace?!"-Tao Zhu asked, his fur instantly standing on end.

—"Naturally. Only the Sun Wheel Palace is worthy of conveying our respect to the Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Shui Huang'er replied as if it were the most obvious thing in the world.

—"That may be so..."-Tao Zhu grunted.

Lifting his head, he indeed beheld the canopy of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, along with that ball of roaring flames blazing at the very apex.

That ball of "flame" was the very core of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree:

The Sun Wheel Palace.

Though designated as a palace, its exterior manifested as a sphere of fire.

In fact, it truly was a sphere of flame, blazing furiously every second of every hour. Even during the darkest hours when neither sun nor moon graced the skies, it hung suspended atop the canopy, radiating concentrated demonic power that guided and nourished the birth of new demon generations.

This demon flame naturally contained immense energy, capable of incinerating any living creature—even demons were no exception.

Whether it was a demon saintess like Shui Huang'er or the Demon Sovereign themselves, the moment they drew near the flames, they would endure the excruciating agony of the demon fire scorching their flesh. Without sufficiently formidable demonic power, one could not even approach the vicinity.

If that was true for demons, it was even more so for humans.

For humans in particular, the trial was vastly more grueling.

Ordinary mortals lacked the ability to sense demonic power; approaching the Sun Wheel Palace rashly was akin to walking naked through a blizzard without feeling the cold—before they could even register the burning heat of the demon fire, they would be reduced to ash in complete silence.

Even cultivators—even those at the Nascent Soul realm and above—because they possessed no demon core, had to expend dozens of times more spiritual energy to stave off such agony when confronting the demon fire of the Sun Wheel Palace, to say nothing of stepping inside the palace's interior.

Consequently, human cultivators, even high-tier cultivators, would never rashly approach the summit of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

Zhou Hui was no exception.

If Zhou Hui wished to enter the Sun Wheel Palace, he would inevitably undergo the incineration of the demon flame.

It was precisely for this reason that Tao Zhu had scoffed.

Boss Tao had dealt with the demon race for far more than a day or two; he knew the demons harbored no malice. This stood in stark contrast to the demon fiends. If every word and action of the fiends was steeped in boundless hatred and malice toward the world and all living beings, the superficially similar demon race, in truth, held very little malice toward the world or humans for the most part. Even when they devoured humans, it was merely to sate their hunger—the simple [law of the jungle](# "Literal CN: 弱肉强食 (ruò ròu qiáng shí) — the weak are meat, the strong eat / survival of the fittest") where the strong prey upon the weak.

Yet this very purity—this complete absence of malice—was itself the greatest form of malice.

For instance, at this very moment, while the demon race's decision to host Zhou Hui within the Sun Wheel Palace was ostensibly an honor reserved for supreme titans, wasn't the incineration of the demon fire simultaneously a probe and a calculated weakening? Even though someone of Zhou Hui's might would not be harmed by the Sun Wheel Palace's demon fire, once he entered the palace, his strength would suffer massive suppression. And the demons considered this entirely proper and natural: since they revered Zhou Hui purely because he was strong, if he failed to display sufficient strength, they would naturally feel he was unworthy of their respect.

Boss Tao despised this trait of the demon race with a passion.

He wanted to warn Zhou Hui, but just then, Mo Xi, who had remained silent ever since setting foot in the Southern Domain, suddenly spoke up:

—"I've heard that the Sun Wheel Palace is a demon forbidden ground where secret demon treasures are stored. Is it truly alright to let us enter like this?"-Mo Xi asked.

[Oh, right! The secret demon treasure!]

Tao Zhu blinked in surprise.

Only now did he remember that they had ostensibly come seeking medicine for Langhuan. Although he didn't know where the demon race kept that treasure, if one were to hazard a guess, the Sun Wheel Palace fell under heavy suspicion. In that case, this Sun Wheel Palace was a place they had to visit no matter what.

Still... was a "secret treasure" something that could be brought up so bluntly?

For now, Zhou Hui's visit was still framed under the pretext of "inspecting pedagogical results." If the demon race caught wind that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had ulterior motives, things might become needlessly complicated.

[This Mo Xi kid usually seems quite sharp; how did he suddenly become so obtuse?]

Musing thus, Tao Zhu cast a strange glance toward Mo Xi.

Not only Tao Zhu, but Zhou Hui was also looking at Mo Xi with quiet concern.

However, Zhou Hui's thoughts differed from Tao Zhu's. He had been pondering the matter of the "misunderstanding" and "hanging from tree branches" the entire journey; hearing Mo Xi's voice all of a sudden, he only now realized that Mo Xi hadn't uttered a single word the entire way. In fact, Mo Bei had spoken more lines than Mo Xi—even if that was largely because Shui Huang'er was making desperate efforts to chat with her.

For Mo Xi, this was extraordinarily unnatural.

Indeed, even though Mo Xi had taken the initiative to speak just now, his face did not wear its usual endearing smile. Instead, he appeared somewhat uneasy and fearful, as though he were dreading something.

Yet Shui Huang'er was unfamiliar with Mo Xi, and naturally failed to perceive his abnormality.

Paying no mind to Mo Xi's inquiry, she didn't care in the slightest that a human had brought up her clan's secret treasure. Instead, she beamed and said:

—"Precisely because it is where our secret treasures are stored, it is the most fitting place to receive honored guests!"-Shui Huang'er chirped happily.-"We even wish to invite the Lord Immortal Venerable and Sister Mo Bei to appraise those treasures!"

This was indeed the custom of the demon race.

They possessed no notion of [hiding one's wealth](# "Literal CN: 财不露白 (cái bù lù bái) — never flaunt one's riches to the outside world"); if they had something good, they would without fail flaunt it for all to see.

The more distinguished the guest, the more entitled they were to this treatment.

Yet Mo Xi felt a powerful sense of dissonance.

This dissonance was not merely directed at the secret treasure, but stemmed from the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree itself.

Indeed, Mo Xi was acting extraordinarily abnormal. The moment he had followed Zhou Hui into the Southern Domain and caught his very first glimpse of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, he had seen the colossal timber burning in a raging inferno.

Mo Xi knew that was an illusion.

For while he was staring at the burning titan, Master was speaking with his sister. Mo Bei seemed to like that tree very much; no matter how eccentric her tastes might be, she would never take a liking to a horrific scene that seemed poised to incinerate the entirety of the Southern Domain.

Yet that illusion was so terrifyingly real.

The hellish, scorching heat of the burning tree, the wails and curses of the dying demons, and the boundless malice churning within his own heart—all of it felt genuinely real, suffocating him beyond endurance.

Just as Mo Xi was about to turn to Master for help, Shui Huang'er had appeared.

In that split second, Mo Xi felt as if Shui Huang'er had come to seek vengeance against him—the "culprit" who burned the Sacred Tree. He had felt it only natural and right to attack her, kill her, and dismember her to vent the malice in his heart.

Naturally, that too had been an illusion.

The next instant, seeing Shui Huang'er bow respectfully toward Master and gaze with immense affection upon his silly older twin sister, Mo Xi felt his mind clear slightly.

Yet the discomfort wrought by the malice in his chest was not something that dissipated so easily.

Mo Xi had no idea why the bizarre impulse of ["destroy it all!"](# "Pop culture / anime meme: 毁灭吧 (huǐmiè ba) — nihilistic impulse to burn and destroy everything") had suddenly seized him, let alone why he had been influenced by it to the point of genuinely wanting to incinerate the Sacred Tree and butcher Shui Huang'er. It was for this very reason that he had maintained absolute silence throughout the entire journey.

Fortunately, the spectacles along the way had helped him calm down further.

Even though diverse hallucinations had never ceased throughout the flight—either visions of the Sacred Tree engulfed in flames, or demons writhing amidst fire while screaming despairing curses; even Mo Bei in his hallucinations was far from cooperative, seeming constantly eager to slip away from his side to seek out someone else—the sporadic outcries echoing from the boughs—[—"I don't wanna go to class!"](# "Speaker: Demon Child"), [—"Even if I jump off this tree and splatter to death, I still won't study!"](# "Speaker: Demon Child"), [—"Waaah, I want to go home!"](# "Speaker: Demon Child")—always managed to snap him back from his illusions in the nick of time.

Among them, what proved most sobering of all were the branches laden with hanging demons.

Mo Xi could not tell whether the hallucinations were more terrifying, or if that physical reality was.

On second thought, reality was probably slightly more terrifying.

No matter how gleefully he had gloated over Eldest Senior Brother hanging from the tree bough, as one of the [little monkeys being warned by the slaughtered chicken](# "Literal CN: 杀鸡儆猴 (shā jī jǐng hóu) — kill the chicken to scare the monkey / punish someone as a warning to others"), Mo Xi was deathly afraid that one day he would be strung up there too. Unlike Eldest Senior Brother, whose face was thicker than city walls, Mo Xi carried the heavy psychological baggage of being "the Heavenly Spirit Sect's most pampered youngest junior brother." If his fellow disciples were ever to witness him in such an undignified plight, he would rather die on the spot.

And incinerating the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree or assaulting the demon saintess were actions that would guaranteed land him hanging from a tree.

Besides, he harbored no grudge or enmity against them; why on earth would he ever do such a thing?!

Reasoning in this manner, Mo Xi barely managed to suppress that continuous tide of illusions. Arriving at the very summit of the Sacred Tree, he suddenly heard the name "Sun Wheel Palace." This time, no hallucination appeared, yet a bizarre question inexplicably arose in his mind:

[...A place that I couldn't enter no matter how much effort I expended—can it really be entered this easily now?]

Accompanying this question was a bizarre sense of sneering mockery.

It felt directed at himself, but even more so at some entity of which he himself was unaware.

Dominated by this inexplicable emotion, words that even he could not comprehend spilled from his lips:

—"So the demon race is this generous? Back then, even when I was your lover, I wasn't allowed to enter!"-Mo Xi sneered.

After all, back then, there had been no way to get inside no matter what he tried, forcing him to resort to [a desperate last resort](# "Literal CN: 下策 (xiàcè) — inferior strategy / worst plan / desperate measure")...? Wait, what last resort?

Hearing this, even someone as oblivious as Shui Huang'er sensed Mo Xi's inexplicable hostility.

She could not help knitting her brows slightly:

[I didn't offend this... Sister Mo Bei's younger brother, did I?]

[What is with this attitude?]

[He clearly has the exact same face as Sister Mo Bei, yet he speaks so unpleasantly! No, wait—even purely judging by faces, Sister Mo Bei is still much more dashing.]

Though somewhat displeased, Shui Huang'er gave full face to her honored guest's disciple. Still smiling radiantly, she replied:

—"The Sun Wheel Palace selects its own visitors; only the truly powerful can draw near. It is true that if my lover were not strong enough, they would be unable to enter—though I wouldn't choose such a weak lover anyway. No, rather, I have zero interest in looking for a lover at all. At this stage, I only want to support my idol,"-Shui Huang'er said, looking at Mo Bei as she uttered that final sentence.

Mo Bei: "?"

Mo Xi was not the least bit appreciative. Or rather, his chaotic mind was completely incapable of processing external stimuli; driven purely by venomous emotions, he revealed a malicious sneer and prepared to say something more.

Yet before Mo Xi could open his mouth again, a gentle voice resonated:

—"Xi'er, are you very interested in the Sun Wheel Palace?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Yes?"-Mo Xi responded instinctively, reacting a beat too late to realize who had spoken to him.

Blinking his eyes, he turned toward his master as if awakening from a dream. He suddenly felt that his earlier emotions had been utterly baffling—for a supreme being like Master, wasn't whatever hospitality the demon race offered entirely normal? Why had he felt so indignant and aggrieved? What was more, that grievance felt so terrifyingly real that it still choked his throat even now.

Regrettably, Mo Xi had realized it a fraction too late. Zhou Hui had already spoken:

—"If you are so interested, why not go inside and see for yourself?"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Go inside and see?"-Mo Xi repeated blankly.

Even in his muddled state, he sensed that Master's words were far from simple.

—"Yes, go inside and take a look using your own strength,"-Zhou Hui said, looking toward Shui Huang'er and then sweeping his gaze around the surroundings, as though seeking permission from something.

Understanding Zhou Hui's intention, Shui Huang'er hesitated with a troubled look:

—"But he is only at Foundation Establishment..."-Shui Huang'er protested with a troubled expression.

—"Xi'er can do it,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.-"Therefore, please withdraw your protection from him."

—"Ah?"-Hearing this, Mo Xi finally snapped back to total clarity.

Breaking into a cold sweat, he could not fathom how he could have been so foolish moments ago. Yet the resentment in his heart intensified from another direction:

[Standing here... was I already relying on someone else's protection?]

He glared toward Shui Huang'er with reluctant defiance.

Shui Huang'er had indeed cast a protective shroud over both Mo Bei and Mo Xi. This was not merely out of courtesy to Mo Bei, but because the cultivation realms of these two were far too weak; without protection, they simply could not survive. Cubs like them were far below the age where they could endure the trial of the demon flame.

Thus, Zhou Hui's suggestion made Shui Huang'er feel that this Lord Immortal Venerable was perhaps being far too harsh.

While she hesitated, a resonant humming sound echoed forth, accompanied by the trembling vibrations of their surroundings:

[—"Heheh, Lord Immortal Venerable, you possess immense confidence in your disciple, don't you?"](# "Speaker: Tree Spirit / Sacred Tree")

This voice drifted from between the surrounding trunks, sounding as though the colossal tree itself were speaking—no, this was "one of" the voices of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. This tree was alive, naturally possessing countless tree spirits like Elder Tong Ling. As Zhou Hui and his entourage traveled within the Sacred Tree, they were walking inside their very bodies; every movement and breath was sensed by them.

Hence, Zhou Hui had been seeking their consent just now.

Yet only Zhou Hui could perceive them so clearly. Feeling the trunk vibrate and hearing that rumbling voice, Tao Zhu wore an expression akin to a throbbing toothache, while Mo Bei looked dreamy-eyed, displaying a rare look of fascination.

Only Mo Xi, upon hearing these voices, showed a twisted expression of sheer horror:

[This tree... actually possesses sentience?!]

[In that case, everything that I and... did, wasn't all of it seen?!]

[Like a total clown... No, wait, I didn't do anything! Why am I so terrified?!]

His thoughts churned in utter turmoil.

Fortunately, a warm hand rested gently upon his shoulder.

Mo Xi heard that gentle voice declare with resolute certainty:

—"I never assign exam questions beyond my disciples' syllabus. He possesses this strength—why not give it a try?"-Zhou Hui said gently yet resolutely.-"Come, Xi'er. Go on."

Accompanying those words, that hand gave Mo Xi a gentle shove.

Mo Xi instantly regained his composure. Guided by that gentle yet resolute force, he stepped forward to face the malice and illusions that had nearly consumed him entirely.

He felt that he had to fight those "illusions."
