# Chapter 170: Demon Fire

Zhou Hui silently watched Mo Xi walk toward the demon fire.

Within his field of vision, not only was Mo Xi's behavior erratic, but the spiritual energy swirling across his body was extraordinarily abnormal as well.

As a hybrid Yin spirit who had been reared as a [cultivation furnace](# "Literal CN: 鼎炉 (dǐnglú) — vessel for forced dual-cultivation or energy extraction"), Mo Xi's body originally harbored exceptionally dense Yin energy. It had clashed violently with his male, Yang physiology, resulting in a severe cultivation bottleneck. It was precisely to counter this constitution that Zhou Hui had used the [mine spirit](# "Literal CN: 矿灵 (kuànglíng) — sentient spirit beast formed in a spirit stone vein") as a catalyst to alter Mo Xi's spirit roots, transmuting his affinity from Yin to Yang and causing him to manifest the state of a fire spirit root.

Ever since then, Mo Xi's spiritual energy had typically radiated a brilliant golden-crimson hue.

Yet upon arriving at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, the closer Mo Xi drew to the demon fire, the fainter that golden-crimson spiritual energy became. By now, it had dwindled into a pale, ash-white state, intermingled with wisps of murky black qi.

Witnessing this transformation in Mo Xi, Zhou Hui's heart sank on the spot.

He had initially assumed that Mo Xi was being affected by the demon fire, causing the power of the mine spirit to fail and allowing his innate Yin spirit energy to regain the upper hand.

—This was indeed a high-probability event.

Despite the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree blazing so furiously and appearing utterly saturated with Yang energy, [extreme Yang inverts into Yin](# "Philosophical / Cultivation: 极阳反生阴 (jí yáng fǎn shēng yīn) — the peak of Yang inevitably generates Yin"). Both the demon fire it produced and the demon race itself were beings inclined toward Yin, harmonizing exceptionally well with the power of a Yin spirit.

That Mo Bei was so fond of this place was not merely due to aesthetic preferences; the fact that the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree resonated naturally with her constitution was another subtle, underlying factor influencing her.

Yet for Mo Xi, whose cultivation technique and constitution had both been altered, it was by no means suitable.

Zhou Hui immediately intended to use his own spiritual energy to stabilize Mo Xi's condition. But before he made a move, he discovered that the black wisps swirling across Mo Xi were not Yin qi at all, but [demonic fiend qi!](# "Literal CN: 魔气 (móqì) — fiendish / corrupted demonic energy, distinct from benign demon race qi (妖气)")

Not only was there fiend qi upon Mo Xi, but also faint threads of karmic cause and effect.

Hearing Mo Xi awkwardly bring up "Shui Huang'er's lover" just now, Zhou Hui finally understood: Mo Xi's spiritual energy was not destabilizing—he was on the verge of recovering past-life memories.

In this lifetime, Shui Huang'er had no lover—and even if she did, it was neither something Mo Xi would know nor something he would care about. Rather, in the so-called "Heavenly Dao script"—no, in the samsaric cycle of his life-before-last—Mo Xi had once clashed with Shui Huang'er on account of that "fated protagonist." Because of that, his attempt to ally with the demon race had fallen through; instead, during a fierce dispute with the "protagonist," he had set the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree ablaze and suffered grievous wounds, forcing him to intensify his practice of demonic arts. In the end, before the "protagonist" ascended and departed this mortal realm, Mo Xi had descended into complete, unhinged madness, becoming the final [map boss](# "Gaming / Academic: 这个地图的终极BOSS — the ultimate regional boss of this stage / zone") of the protagonist's regional arc.

Viewed from this angle, this ordeal at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had likely inflicted a far deeper trauma on Mo Xi than even his eventual death in that timeline.

And in this current lifetime, though he had reincarnated anew with his memories cleansed and his constitution and cultivation reset to their [factory settings](# "Hardware / Computing: 出厂配置 (chūchǎng pèizhì) — factory default configuration"), like a formatted hard drive that had been heavily written to, residual traces were inevitably left behind.

The scars etched by cultivating demonic arts were particularly severe. After all, demonic techniques altered not merely a practitioner's skeletal roots and physical vessel, but also warped their spirit, mind, and soul.

Given Mo Xi's condition, so long as he remembered nothing, all was well. But the moment he recalled the memories of that past lifetime, his psyche would likely be corrupted by the previous life's [cached data](# "Hardware / Computing: 上一世的数据（X） — data from the previous iteration / life (with an internal mental correction X)") and plunge into [cultivation deviation](# "Cultivation: 走火入魔 (zǒu huǒ rù mó) — qi deviation / falling into demonic madness") once again.

At this thought, Zhou Hui could not help feeling a throbbing headache:

[Why do these children insist on remembering, one after another?]

[Wouldn't it be far better to forget all those awful memories and start fresh in peaceful ignorance?]

[Is there truly anything in those memories worth endlessly dwelling upon and reliving?]

Naturally, Zhou Hui was thoroughly unwilling to let his disciples regain their memories, especially in an extreme case like Mo Xi's.

Fortunately, there was still a way to prevent it.

Disrupted by those chaotic memory fragments, Mo Xi's emotional expression had become far more blunt than usual. From the moment they arrived, his gaze had been fixed unwaveringly upon the Sun Wheel Palace, and he had openly voiced his resentment over Shui Huang'er letting "outsiders" enter and exit the Sun Wheel Palace at will.

[It seems Mo Xi harbors a deep obsession with the Sun Wheel Palace!]

Zhou Hui deduced this much, yet he remained unaware of the root cause.

Because that wretched "Heavenly Dao script" revolved almost entirely around the "protagonist," even though Mo Xi served as a mid-tier boss for that stage, the text devoted very little ink to his internal motives—it merely recorded that he had been [skulking around scheming in the shadows.](# "Literal CN: 狗狗祟祟地搞事情 (gǒugǒu-suìsuì de gǎo shìqing) — suspiciously and stealthily stirring up trouble")

Then again, Zhou Hui did not particularly care what Mo Xi had been plotting two lifetimes ago; knowing that he held an obsession was quite enough.

So long as Mo Xi accomplished what he had failed to achieve in that obsession and made a different choice, he could overwrite those lingering traces of [cached data](# "Hardware / Computing: 数据（X） — data (X)") and memory, preventing him from being compromised by his past life.

Zhou Hui reasoned and calculated all this within his mind in the span of a mere fraction of a second.

Having made his decision, he promptly told Mo Xi to head toward the Sun Wheel Palace.

Regardless of what Mo Xi's obsession was, let him tackle it first; no matter how dire things got, Master was always here to [hold the bottom line.](# "Literal CN: 兜底 (dōudǐ) — provide a safety net / cover all risks")

In making this decision, Zhou Hui had evidently forgotten that he was currently on demon race turf.

And his disciples had always possessed an extraordinary knack for stirring up monumental catastrophes.

Fortunately, the demons had no idea either.

They watched the unfolding spectacle with detached amusement, utterly unconcerned that Mo Xi might cause any serious commotion. In fact, they were even somewhat anticipating the show—after all, this was the Sun Wheel Palace, an entity whose power even the Demon Sovereign could not fully command. Surely even the mighty Untiring Immortal Venerable's power would suffer severe suppression here! What could a mere Foundation Establishment Yin spirit cub possibly hope to achieve?

Shui Huang'er thought the same, though among everyone present, she was the one most genuinely worried that Mo Xi would be injured.

It was not that she cared about Mo Xi himself, but rather that she worried Mo Bei would grieve if her twin brother were scorched by the demon fire.

—"Little Sister Mo Bei, you should really persuade your master and let your younger brother drop this,"-Shui Huang'er whispered to Mo Bei.-"I don't know what rumors he heard outside that made him want to challenge the sacred flame of the Sun Wheel Palace, but that is a sacred relic that attacks even the Demon Sovereign indiscriminately! If he gets injured, the damage will be irreversible."

—"It doesn't matter,"-Mo Bei replied with complete nonchalance.-"Master said he can do it."

—"...Fine, even if he can do it, this is no laughing matter,"-Shui Huang'er said, trying to follow Mo Bei's logic.-"What if he makes a mistake?"

Mo Bei's expression finally turned grave:

—"Then Mo Xi will fail his 'exam.'"-Mo Bei said.

[Is that seriously the point?!]

Shui Huang'er wanted to say something, but out of the corner of her eye, she noticed that the unlikable "Xi'er" suddenly froze in his tracks at Mo Bei's words, his once-resolute stride instantly slowing down.

[He's actually terrified of THAT?!]

Shui Huang'er's eyes went wide.

Mo Xi was terrified, of course.

When he heard that phrase "fail his exam," it was as though a basin of icy water had been dumped over his head, utterly freezing all the lingering chaotic emotions in his mind.

[That's right! Master went so far as to say that—if I can't pull this off, it counts as failing an exam!]

[So what in the world was I doing just now?!]

Mo Xi finally realized that just like over a year ago when he was caught scalping exam papers and ended up assigned that impossible construction mission for the Palace of Silence, he had once again shoved himself into an agonizingly awkward corner.

[Was that really only a year ago? I truly haven't matured one bit!]

Regrettably, it was far too late for Mo Xi to regret it now.

Mo Xi had already arrived before the Sun Wheel Palace's demon fire, and Shui Huang'er had already withdrawn her protective shroud, leaving Mo Xi to face the scorching incineration of the demon fire directly.

Truth be told, Mo Xi could not tell whether this ball of flame was freezing cold or boiling hot.

Intense heat and biting frigidity seemed identical in their manifestation—both presented as excruciating agony. What was more, this agony struck not at the physical body, but directly at the soul.

Evidently, this demon fire was far more terrifying than the demons had warned; it could effortlessly tear the soul of a puny Foundation Establishment cultivator into scattered wisps.

At this critical juncture, Mo Xi was surprisingly calmer than usual.

—"Master said he never assigns exam questions beyond my capabilities. Therefore, I can do it!"-Mo Xi reaffirmed to himself.

He closed his eyes. At the nape of his neck, the golden-crimson dragon markings began to slither and swirl. The spiritual energy that had dimmed across his frame reignited with radiant golden-crimson brilliance, faintly showing signs of resonating in tandem with the demon fire.

* * *

—"Xi'er is performing better than I anticipated,"-Zhou Hui said, sighing.-"He practiced his new cultivation technique diligently and refined more than half of the mine spirit within his body. Given his personality, that he disregarded rapid cultivation leaps to build such a solid foundation comes as somewhat of a surprise to me. But why does he always perform so averagely during exams?!"

His sigh was heartfelt and genuine. In truth, Mo Xi's regular exam scores among the direct disciples were genuinely not very impressive; he merely hovered just above Yuwen Xuan to keep Zhou Hui from getting angry.

—"Master, my brother is simply an idiot,"-Mo Bei exposed him ruthlessly.-"For regular exams, he always acts as though he's studying for someone else, completely satisfied with barely passing marks. Fortunately, he isn't entirely brainless. When you gave him that foundational art to alter his spirit roots, he at least understood that it directly benefited himself—even setting aside cultivation leaps, that's an entire spirit stone vein! How could he not take it to heart?"

—"If that is the case, I must raise my standards for him,"-Zhou Hui sighed.

[Are those seriously your only takeaways?!]

Listening to the exchange between this master and disciple, all the other demons present—including Tao Zhu—were rendered utterly speechless.

—"You're actually conducting pedagogical field practice?!"-Tao Zhu could not help asking. He had assumed Zhou Hui made his disciple do this to claim some secret demon treasure!

Then again, it was entirely consistent with Zhou Hui's usual style—this venerable elder never cared about the occasion.

And his disciples were much the same...

—"Who would have thought this little lass looks so well-behaved, yet delivered such a vicious blow,"-Tao Zhu muttered, glancing strangely at Mo Bei.

—"That's her caring for her younger brother! She's helping her twin brother receive more guidance from their master,"-Shui Huang'er immediately defended her [idol](# "Modern slang / Cultural: 爱豆 (àidòu) — phonetic transliteration of 'idol'; celebrity object of intense fandom").

Yet when she looked toward Mo Xi this time, a genuine trace of admiration appeared in her eyes.

She beheld that youth, who shared a ninety-percent resemblance to Mo Bei, standing ramrod-straight amidst the azure flames. While he lacked the razor-sharp, invincible sword qi that coated Mo Bei, the spiritual energy enveloping his frame resembled molten iron—both scorching hot and malleable—using pure Yang spiritual energy to melt and dissolve the surrounding demon fire.

To say nothing of ordinary demons, even someone of the highest-ranking phoenix lineage like Shui Huang'er could only achieve this much.

The demon race revered the strong, and they instantly acknowledged Mo Xi's strength.

—"He is Mo Bei's twin brother after all; at least he hasn't squandered that shared bloodline,"-Shui Huang'er said, sounding a bit sour.-"Now that he has proven his strength, it's about time to—"

[—"Do not disturb him."](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit Elder")

A deep, vibrating hum resonated directly inside Shui Huang'er's mind.

It was the Sacred Tree's Wood Spirit Elder.

—"This child is about to break through,"-the Wood Spirit Elder said.

—"Break through? In a place like this?!"-Shui Huang'er was startled once more. Glancing toward Mo Xi, her expression turned all the graver.-"Doesn't that make stopping him even more urgent? Although this child is half Yin spirit, he still carries human blood after all. Breaking through on our demon territory makes him exceptionally vulnerable to demonic qi invasion, not to mention doing so right inside the demon fire—this is hardly a wise choice."

[—"Why worry on his behalf? He has his own Parasol Tree standing by."](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit Elder")

—"A Parasol Tree? He's not a phoenix..."-Shui Huang'er murmured blankly.

Yet she quickly caught on, realizing that the Wood Spirit had merely used a metaphor.

Most demons shared a symbiotic relationship with spiritual plants and sacred trees: under normal circumstances, demons safeguarded the flora from external threats, while during the critical moments of a demon's breakthrough, the sacred trees and spiritual plants provided shelter and protection.

For high-ranking demons like Shui Huang'er in particular, Elder Tong Ling was her symbiotic sacred tree; a ten-thousand-year-old tree of this caliber was capable of nurturing and protecting several broods of phoenixes to full maturity.

Mo Xi, a half-Yin-spirit, possessed no such symbiotic guardian. And yet...

Shui Huang'er whipped her head around to look at Zhou Hui, only to see the Lord Immortal Venerable speaking to Mo Bei with a mild, placid expression. Yet when Shui Huang'er activated her [Demon Eye](# "Demon Race Trait: 妖目 (yāomù) — demonic spiritual vision"), she "saw" an immense, steadfast reservoir of spiritual energy unfurling from beneath Zhou Hui's feet. It formed a defensive matrix rivaling the Sacred Tree itself, gently enveloping the entirety of the Sun Wheel Palace.

This was only natural. What if Mo Xi's obsession was to incinerate the Sacred Tree?

Zhou Hui could hardly let him actually burn the Sacred Tree down!

Shui Huang'er was utterly awestruck by Zhou Hui's "plain and unassuming" might:

—"Wait a moment... Lord Immortal Venerable can completely envelop the Sun Wheel Palace? Doesn't that mean he could refine the Sun Wheel Palace?!"-Shui Huang'er gasped.

—"More than that; he could refine all of us if he wished,"-the Wood Spirit grunted with a cold snort.

Yet confronted with such terrifying power, even the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was utterly helpless.

Their home turf was entirely unable to suppress or weaken this human powerhouse. If he so desired, he could even refine this colossal tree whole.

Truth be told, Shui Huang'er found this concept virtually impossible to digest in the moment.

Stunned, she suddenly asked the Wood Spirit:

—"...Then, could this be an opportunity?"-Shui Huang'er asked the Wood Spirit.-"An opportunity for this place to be 'saved'?"
