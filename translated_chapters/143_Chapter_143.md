# Chapter 143: Ascension? (第143章 飞升？)

[← Previous Chapter](142_Chapter_142.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](144_Chapter_144.md)

---

Regardless of how reluctant Sect Master Shen had been, the results of the academic paper were [top-notch](# "Literal CN: 杠杠的 (gànggàng de) — rock-solid / sterling / top-tier").

Particularly at this current stage, when the academic journal *Spells* was aggressively expanding its influence across the realms.

Though founded less than half a year ago, having undergone promotion at the Immortal Arts Assembly alongside the heated academic debates between Central Domain and Northern Domain cultivators, as the cultivation world's very first academic journal, its value had already earned recognition from a growing multitude of cultivators in this realm.

Especially when paired with the publicity of the *White Jade Capital Daily*, it was exerting tangible effects on the cultivation practices of countless cultivators, and even upon entire sects.

Take Ancestor Wushan of Red Maple Cave in the southwest of the Central Domain, for instance.

Back when her sect participated in the Immortal Arts Assembly, she had heard of *Spells*, but thought nothing of it. Later, sparked by the cross-domain polemics between Central and Northern Domain cultivators, she finally bought a copy to see what the fuss was about. From that moment on, while she might not read every single issue of the *White Jade Capital Daily*, whenever a fresh issue of *Spells* was released, she made a point of browsing through it.

After all, without reading *Spells*, one could easily miss out on the latest breakthroughs in one's primary cultivation discipline.

Today was no exception.

Ancestor Wushan was seated beneath her veranda admiring flowers when a disciple brought over the newest issue of the *Spells* journal. She accepted it and casually flipped through the pages.

As an artifact cultivator, she naturally paid closer attention to papers concerning artifact refinement. Yet in this issue, the moment her eyes caught the front-page headline, she let out a puzzled murmur.

—"How could a body cultivation paper make the front-page headline?"-Ancestor Wushan wondered aloud with a peculiar expression.

She didn't discriminate against body cultivators—well, all right, perhaps a little bit—but objectively speaking, the vast majority of body cultivators were truly hopeless when it came to paperwork and written scholarship.

After all, cultivating immortality was fundamentally a process of reinforcing one's physical constitution.

Yet this reinforcement wasn't random or arbitrary; it was targeted. To borrow the gaming terminology of a certain otherworldly realm: with every level gained, a fixed pool of attribute points was distributed. These points were not manually allocated by the player, but automatically assigned based on the skills and physical attributes the player exercised most frequently.

In this regard, 'reality' being a trash game held true regardless of which world one inhabited.

In other words, if someone went their whole life without using their brain, no matter how rigorously they tempered their flesh, their IQ would never increase.

Unfortunately, a substantial portion of body cultivators belonged squarely to this archetype.

[If I can just beat them down, why would I ever use my brain?]

Consequently, since the inception of *Spells*, papers regarding body cultivation were by far the rarest, surfacing once every several issues at best.

Because it was such a rare sight, Ancestor Wushan couldn't help glancing at the author's byline.

—"And it's written by the Sect Master of Blood Demon Hall, no less... Since when did Shen Qingqian know how to write an academic paper?"

—"If it's an article from Blood Demon Hall, then it's hardly surprising,"-said the immortal maid attending at her side.

She was a handmaiden tasked with tending the study's brushes and inkstones. Lately, she had taken on a new duty: reading the news in the *White Jade Capital Daily* every morning and compiling executive summaries so her master would not miss critical developments.

Now, she explained to her mistress:

—"Not long ago, the Heavenly Spirit Sect opened White Jade Capital to publicly demonstrate their disciples' spirit root assessment. Those disciples performed exceptionally well. Word has it this was thanks to the White Jade Capital Academy adopting novel teaching methodologies and newly compiled textbooks. The visiting titans were lavish in their praise, and some are even preparing to adopt those textbooks."

—"Mhm, I remember that—it was on the front page of the *White Jade Capital Daily* about a month ago, wasn't it?"-Ancestor Wushan remarked dismissively.-"But wasn't that purely polite diplomatic flattery?"

[If the Untiring Immortal Venerable went so far as to open White Jade Capital, offering a few polite compliments was only natural, wasn't it?]

[And wouldn't it be just as unsurprising if a few bootlickers bought textbooks just to curry favor?]

Ancestor Wushan reasoned according to the time-honored habits of the cultivation world. It was precisely because she had no patience for such sycophancy that she hadn't bothered attending White Jade Capital in person—though, naturally, a small fry at the Void Refinement stage like her might count for something within her own sect, but in White Jade Capital, she wouldn't amount to anything at all. She had zero interest in joining that vanity circus.

Yet toward Ancestor Wushan's skepticism, the immortal maid shook her head:

—"That might not be entirely true, Mistress. I heard that Fairy Ruopei of the Huanfei Sect placed a massive bulk order for those textbooks."

—"Pfft. She was the one who stoked the feud between the cultivators of both domains earlier,"-Ancestor Wushan scoffed.-"Ten to one, she's merely trying to make amends this time around."

Ancestor Wushan stood firm in her assessment and casually flipped through the journal.

Seeing this, though the maid felt otherwise, she could hardly contradict her master over it. Holding her tongue, she simply poured Ancestor Wushan a fresh cup of spiritual tea.

Yet as Ancestor Wushan began reading, the spiritual tea grew hot, then cooled, replaced five full times—and still, she had not finished.

The maid grew increasingly baffled. Why was her mistress, who had never practiced body cultivation in her life, engrossed for so long?

Finally, just as the maid prepared to pour a sixth cup of tea, Ancestor Wushan set down the journal.

—"...Go summon Wu Xian and Wu Xiang here,"-she instructed the maid.

[—"Yes, Mistress."](# "Speaker: Immortal Maid")-The maid naturally dared not pry further, respectfully bowing.

Wu Xian and Wu Xiang were the Cave Master and Vice Cave Master of Red Maple Cave, as well as Ancestor Wushan's direct descendants and disciples.

Upon hearing their ancestor's summons, Wu Xian and Wu Xiang rushed over post-haste.

Ancestor Wushan did not immediately bring up the academic paper. Instead, she asked:

—"In another month, it will be time for the sect's recruitment drive once more. How are your preparations coming along?"

—"We have already finalized our arrangements, Ancestor,"-Wu Xian replied.

—"If my memory serves me correctly, our sect hasn't recruited enough disciples during the major recruitment drive for fifty years, has it?"-Ancestor Wushan asked.-"Do you have confidence this year?"

This time, neither disciple answered immediately.

Seeing their hesitation, Ancestor Wushan demanded sternly:

—"What is the meaning of this reaction? As I recall, there hasn't been a beast tide in recent decades, has there? By all accounts, you should be replenishing our disciple ranks to the utmost! Why are our numbers dwindling year after year?!"

—"Ancestor, it's not that we don't want to!"-Wu Xiang cried out in anguish upon hearing this.-"It's just that the women in our clan have been so uncooperative—in recent years, they've given birth to nothing but girls!"

—"Then how about simply recruiting girls?"-Ancestor Wushan scoffed with a cold snort.

—"An-Ancestor, not every girl is blessed with Herculean divine strength like you!"-Wu Xiang stammered.-"Besides, our core cultivation technique is far too masculine and unyielding; the vast majority of women cannot endure it."

Ancestor Wushan looked coldly upon her descendants.

Deep down, however, she knew he was speaking the truth.

In principle, once one embarked on the immortal path, so long as one possessed spirit roots, gender should never be an issue.

Yet in the southwest of the Central Domain, a patriarchal system akin to that of mortal realms was stubbornly maintained. And this, of course, was not without cause.

The environmental conditions were simply too harsh.

The southwest of the Central Domain faced the ruins of the God-Demon War head-on. Aside from the countless spatial rifts, chaotic surges of spiritual energy caused beast tides every few decades to ravage the Central Domain—and the southwest bore the initial brunt.

Within such a narrow window of time, no matter how gifted a prodigy might be, it was exceedingly difficult to cultivate so rapidly as to render baseline differences in physical stamina irrelevant.

In fact, to survive the beast tides, men of the southwest were forced to adopt methods that could bolster physical endurance in the shortest time possible: body cultivation. As a result, eight to nine out of ten sects in the southwestern Central Domain were dominated by body cultivators—indeed, it was precisely through encountering body cultivation techniques in her original sect that Shen Qingqian later created the Blood Demon Art. The remaining ten to twenty percent leaned heavily toward artifact refinement, relying on external gear to augment defense and combat prowess.

Red Maple Cave was an artifact refinement sect.

Yet their primary technique was a fire-attribute masculine art that inflicted severe harm upon female constitutions. It was only due to Ancestor Wushan's extraordinary natural physiology that she had managed to achieve her current heights.

Ironically, if one were to conduct a demographic census in the male-centric southwest, women actually formed the overwhelming majority of the population.

The beast tides occurring every few decades claimed far too many young, able-bodied men. In catastrophic cases, an entire sect might be reduced to a handful of survivors. During those dark hours, it was almost always women who gritted their teeth to keep the sect or clan alive. Yet when annual disciple recruitment rolled around, tradition dictated that they must recruit primarily males.

Ancestor Wushan detested this state of affairs, but had been powerless to change it.

Now, she held a third option in her hands.

—"Look over this paper,"-Ancestor Wushan said, handing over the journal.-"If we use this technique as an introductory foundation, wouldn't we be able to recruit female disciples? Even if problems arise, I have ways to mitigate the bodily harm caused by our core technique."

Hearing Ancestor Wushan say this, Wu Xian and Wu Xiang exchanged hesitant glances.

They hastily read through the article.

—"This is indeed an approach,"-Wu Xiang murmured thoughtfully.

—"But..."-Wu Xian looked somewhat dissatisfied. Glancing at Ancestor Wushan, he stammered,-"This article was written by Shen Qingqian of Blood Demon Hall."

—"Is there a problem with that?"-Ancestor Wushan asked.

—"I heard that Shen Qingqian only created the Blood Demon Art because she fell into demonic deviation,"-Wu Xian argued.-"If we use her technique to initiate young children, wouldn't that be..."

—"You stared at it for so long, and you still think this is a demonic art?!"-Ancestor Wushan exclaimed in disbelief.

[Could the disciples of my own sect truly be dimmer than body cultivators?!]

She grabbed Wu Xian's head and slammed it forcefully against the journal.

—"Look at it properly, you idiot!"

Instantly, the burly muscular brute—who could swing several-thousand-pound purple gold warhammers with ease—plummeted headfirst onto the veranda floor with a deafening *thud*, smashing a crater straight into the floorboards.

Wu Xiang took a subtle step back, his face turning away in secondhand agony.

Yet this method was unquestionably the most intuitive.

The academic papers published in *Spells* could not only be read with one's eyes, but also directly experienced through bodily resonance.

By bringing his face—er, forehead—into direct contact with the inscribed characters on the page, Wu Xian was immediately drawn into an illusory simulation, experiencing firsthand the circulation mechanics of the Blood Demon Art.

After quite a while, he finally raised his dizzy head from the floorboards.

—"Well? Do you still think it's a demonic art?"-Ancestor Wushan asked.

—"No..."-Wu Xian answered dryly.

—"Still, regarding the Peerless Alliance..."-Wu Xiang ventured cautiously, attempting to steer the conversation away to save his brother.

—"Boring rumors,"-Ancestor Wushan dismissed nonchalantly.-"That entire affair was because La Jiu and his wife lacked basic decency, wasn't it? He wanted to establish the Peerless Alliance but lacked sufficient strength. Swallowing White Egret Valley whole was bad enough, but chasing down disciples who refused to submit and spreading vicious rumors—what kind of petty nonsense is that?"

—"Wait, is that how it was?"-Wu Xiang asked in surprise.-"How come we never heard you mention this before, Ancestor?"

—"What is there to say about such sordid, disgraceful affairs?"-Ancestor Wushan replied coolly.

After all, back when the Peerless Alliance was established, Ancestor Wushan had personally watched Shen Qingqian defect and depart the sect.

Yet compared to Shen Qingqian, surviving the beast tides every few decades was infinitely more vital. Ancestor Wushan had seen no reason to offend the Alliance Leader of the Peerless Alliance on behalf of a total stranger, which was why she had kept her mouth shut.

Now that Shen Qingqian's technique could alter the future destiny of her own sect, however, that was an entirely different story.

—"Speaking of which, White Egret Valley serves as a cautionary tale as well,"-Ancestor Wushan remarked with dripping sarcasm.-"Back in the day, that sect's male lineage withered away, forcing them to recruit numerous women and appoint the daughter as Valley Master. Unfortunately, the previous Valley Master was a muddle-headed fool. Having decided to let his daughter take the helm, he failed to teach her properly, obsessing only over finding her a suitable son-in-law. In the end, he got himself killed in a beast tide, and his daughter dutifully fulfilled his wish by handing over the entire sect as her wedding dowry."

Sighing deeply, Ancestor Wushan fixed her two disciples with a piercing gaze:

—"Having swallowed White Egret Valley, La Jiu will naturally not let other sects off the hook. He hasn't provoked us yet only because we can still hold our own. But once our sect has no successors left, it will be too late. Are you prepared to recruit new disciples? Or will you cling stubbornly to your rules even if it means watching our sect get swallowed whole?"

Faced with their ancestor's glare, neither dared choose getting annexed.

Before their very eyes, Ancestor Wushan took to the skies, flying straight toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect to consult on adopting the textbooks.

—"What a mess this is!"-Wu Xian grumbled, looking thoroughly disgruntled.-"Even if it isn't a demonic art, is Blood Demon Hall's technique truly effective? Our sect took in disciples who fled Blood Demon Hall in the past, and they all claimed the technique was atrocious."

—"It's atrocious for male cultivators, but isn't it ideal for laying the foundation for female cultivators?"-Wu Xiang persuaded.-"One way or another, recruits need foundational training. Giving it a try isn't a bad idea—that technique was personally endorsed by the Untiring Immortal Venerable, after all."

—"Still, to offend the Alliance Leader over this..."

—"Alliance Leader of the Peerless Alliance, eh?"-Wu Xiang let out a cold chuckle upon hearing that. Pulling Wu Xian aside, he whispered,-"Do you think our ancestor was the only one shaken by that paper?"

—"What? Are other factions looking to adopt the textbooks as well?"

—"Textbooks or not is a secondary matter. Now that the paper has been published, how many people do you think will learn about what happened back then, just like we did today from Ancestor? In the past, everyone kept quiet about it. But now that people are talking, the show is about to get entertaining. There are plenty of pairs of eyes coveting his seat as Alliance Leader!"-Wu Xiang said with deep nuance.

Hearing this, Wu Xian's face broke into a gloating grin as well.

When it came to watching [fireworks in someone else's backyard](# "Literal CN: 别人家的热闹 (biérén jiā de rènào) — other people's drama / watching a house fire from across the street"), one never worried about the flames burning too high.

---

La Jiu, Alliance Leader of the Peerless Alliance, had indeed sensed the shifting atmosphere.

He was a remarkably perceptive man. A few days after the paper's publication, he noticed an increasing number of peculiar, sidelong glances cast in his direction.

Upon investigating more closely, he even overheard an unspeakable conversation near the council chambers:

[—"...So Blood Demon Hall actually has nothing to do with demons?"](# "Speaker: Peerless Alliance Cultivator")

[—"They weren't even called Blood Demon Hall originally. Our Alliance Leader forced their hand."](# "Speaker: Peerless Alliance Cultivator")

[—"Honestly, if someone didn't want to marry into the sect, just let it be! Did he really have to go to such extremes?"](# "Speaker: Peerless Alliance Cultivator")

[—"Who knows if it was really an elder wanting to take her as a wife? Ten to one, La Jiu wanted to swallow the entire sect and keep every woman for himself. After all, word has it White Egret Valley had plenty of beauties back in the day. How many are left today?"](# "Speaker: Peerless Alliance Cultivator")

[—"Tsk, tsk, tsk!"](# "Speaker: Peerless Alliance Cultivators")

Those mocking clicks of the tongue nearly sent La Jiu's blood pressure through the roof.

Yet this was the karma he had sown with his own hands.

Since he had relished spreading slanderous rumors about Shen Qingqian in the past, now that he was on the back foot, others had no reason to show him any mercy. They would only paint him in an even more sordid, despicable light.

Unwilling to swallow this bitter pill, La Jiu roared in fury at his Daoist partner:

—"This is all your fault! Who told you to obsessively persecute Shen Qingqian without letting go?!"

—"I... I was only following the will of the gods!"-whined Lu Ningning, current Daoist partner of the Alliance Leader, former Valley Master of White Egret Valley, and Shen Qingqian's senior martial sister. Her eyes immediately welled with tears, as if she were the one who had been persecuted all along.

Watching her pathetic display gave La Jiu a splitting headache.

[Utterly useless! I have no idea how she ever cultivated to the Void Refinement stage! Why did I ever think she was gentle and lovely back then?!]

Consumed by manic irritation, La Jiu kicked her squarely in the chest:

—"Stop your damn sniveling! At a time like this, why haven't you gone to consult your gods?!"

Lu Ningning went flying from the kick.

Only because she possessed Void Refinement cultivation herself did she survive the blow.

Yet she dared not resist in the slightest. Casting a resentful, aggrieved glance at her Daoist partner, she whimpered:

—"I... I will prepare at once."

So saying, she set up an incense altar.

La Jiu watched from the side with a peculiar expression.

He didn't know whether this was an esoteric secret heritage of White Egret Valley, but Lu Ningning had always been eccentric and mystical. With the aid of her 'gods,' she seemed to possess a degree of divination aptitude, capable of foreseeing future events. It seemed even her initial encounter with La Jiu had been the result of her praying to these gods.

All along, La Jiu had reaped considerable benefits from it. For instance, successfully maintaining his position as Alliance Leader had relied on the divine guidance of the deity Lu Ningning worshipped—though, naturally, La Jiu would never admit to this.

He felt that the god Lu Ningning worshipped was rather sinister. Still, Lu Ningning was the one worshipping it; what did that have to do with him, La Jiu?

Presently, La Jiu had no intention of lending a hand. He even stepped outside the room, refusing to watch Lu Ningning's sacrificial ritual.

A few minutes later, Lu Ningning emerged, reeking of suffocating incense smoke.

—"What did the gods say?"-La Jiu demanded.-"Shen Qingqian has latched onto the Untiring Immortal Venerable now! If she comes seeking revenge, we're in deep trouble!"

—"Th-There is no answer for the moment,"-Lu Ningning replied timidly, shrinking back toward the doorway as if bracing to dodge another fist.-"Sometimes the gods do not respond immediately... B-But by nightfall at the latest, there will definitely be an answer!"

—"There had better be!"-La Jiu spat viciously.

Hunching her shoulders, Lu Ningning took another step back.

She kept her head lowered, not daring to reveal her panicked, guilty expression.

She didn't dare tell her violent partner that during this sacrificial ritual, she had encountered immense cosmic interference. The divine idol had not only fractured with web-like fissures, but crimson blood had seeped from the cracks.

Yet... that was a 'god'!

[A 'god' couldn't possibly be in trouble, right?!]

---

Rewinding to several days before Lu Ningning's sacrificial ritual.

Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Master, you are going to ascend?!"-Qu Qianxing screeched.

Zhou Hui had never seen his second disciple lose his composure to such a degree.

—"It is not ascension, but merely heading up to have a look around temporarily,"-Zhou Hui said, holding the issue of *Spells* in his hand.

Within it lay the final piece of his puzzle:

The academic papers penned by Shen Qingqian and Bai Wubin—descendants of the demon race cursed by the True Immortal—which were the crystallizations of their life's blood and core comprehension.

Indeed, according to Zhou Hui's empirical verification, Shen Qingqian and Bai Wubin were indeed descendants of that True Immortal who had fallen into demonic corruption. This meant that by harvesting their primordial substance, he could establish a definitive karmic tether for ascension.

Thus, Zhou Hui had not urged Shen Qingqian to write an academic paper purely to sell textbooks. Rather, he needed to collect this indispensable material.

Armed with this, according to Zhou Hui's mathematical calculations, he should be capable of successfully traversing to the upper realm.

In reality, when Zhou Hui tested it, there was no longer any deficiency in karmic causality; Shen Qingqian's paper had even yielded a massive influx of providential destiny points.

However, Zhou Hui had not ascended immediately. Instead, he summoned Qu Qianxing.

Hearing that his master was heading to the upper realm, Qu Qianxing had first felt overwhelming excitement, followed quickly by utter bewilderment:

—"What does 'having a look around temporarily' mean?"

—"In other words, I am not officially ascending, but merely making a temporary trip to the upper realm to inspect their situation,"-Zhou Hui explained patiently.-"One can hardly ascend blindly without conducting preliminary field research, can one?"

—"...That does make sense,"-Qu Qianxing agreed.

[Back when I joined the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I conducted thorough field inspections for a very long time. Going to the upper realm naturally requires rigorous field research... like hell it does!]

[Who has ever heard of someone inspecting the environmental conditions of the upper realm before ascending?! Is he treating the immortal plane like his personal backyard?!]

Yet, if it was Master, it somehow seemed entirely reasonable.

[Master gracing the upper realm with his presence is the upper realm's supreme honor.]

Qu Qianxing rationalized this absurd conclusion with blind conviction. Even so, hearing that Zhou Hui was about to ascend to the upper realm, he couldn't suppress a tremor of excitement, feeling as though a long-cherished lifelong dream was finally coming true.

Striving to maintain composure, he asked:

—"Then, Master, why did you summon me? Is there something you require me to do?"

—"Indeed there is,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

Qu Qianxing's heart raced even faster. He desperately hoped he could assist Master with his ascension.

—"I need you to take good care of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. In particular, keep a tight leash on your junior brothers and sisters,"-Zhou Hui instructed.

[...Ah. It's this again?]

Qu Qianxing had to exert immense effort to prevent disappointment from showing on his face:

—"Master need not even command it; I shall naturally see to it."

—"This time is different,"-Zhou Hui spoke sternly.-"I only just recalled that time between the upper realm and our mortal world does not appear to flow equally. Ancient records state that a single day in heaven equates to a full year on earth. By that calculation, if I spend merely one shichen up there, an entire month will have elapsed down here. Even with my cultivation, once I am in the upper realm, my divine sense will be unable to encompass this world below. If anything happens to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I will not be able to return in time."

—"Master, I will protect the Heavenly Spirit Sect with my life,"-Qu Qianxing immediately repeated with utter gravity.-"Please embark without worry!"

[Hearing you say that only makes me worry more!]

Zhou Hui studied Qu Qianxing's face, recalling his track record across past reincarnations. Still, no matter what, this was his most dependable disciple.

Zhou Hui's lips twitched, but in the end, he chose to place his faith in his disciple:

—"Then I leave everything in your hands."

—"Yes, Master!"

Zhou Hui nodded. With one hand, he grasped the metaphysical 'tether' connecting to the upper realm; with the other, he scooped up the Little Black Lamb, who had been lounging nearby pretending none of this concerned him.

—"Baaa?!"-The Little Black Lamb's eyes went wide.

—"Master, you are taking Eldest Senior Brother with you?"-Qu Qianxing asked in astonishment.

—"Leaving him down here is far too dangerous,"-Zhou Hui replied.

Qu Qianxing considered this for a moment and nodded solemnly in agreement.

The Little Black Lamb began flailing wildly:

—"This isn't fair! I am the Eldest Senior Brother! I will take splendid care of my junior brothers and sisters—"

This attitude alone proved the fellow was hatching rotten schemes.

Under normal circumstances, Yuwen Xuan was thrilled to accompany Master anywhere. Even if forbidden, he would sneak along in secret. Yet this time, he was actively protesting!

Naturally—a rare occasion when Master was completely absent (unable to return immediately even if the sky fell down) was a genuine [summer vacation](# "Literal CN: 暑假 (shǔjià) — summer vacation / school break")!

Compared to that freedom, going on a sightseeing trip was completely insignificant.

Unfortunately, Zhou Hui paid no heed to Yuwen Xuan's clamoring. In a flash of light, his figure appeared high in the heavens, ascending ever higher into the cosmos until he vanished entirely past the horizon.

Qu Qianxing stared blankly upward, an indescribable sense of melancholy washing over him.

And even, an inexplicable dread.

A dread that Master might simply never return—in truth, of all those who had ascended to the upper realm throughout history, not a single soul had ever returned.

As if answering that foreboding thought, Mo Xi suddenly sprinted into view:

—"Master, Second Senior Brother! Disaster! Fifth Senior Brother tricked Young Master Jian into going to the Demon Realm! ...Wait, where's Master?"

[...And so the trouble begins already?]

Qu Qianxing thought with an utterly deadpan face.
