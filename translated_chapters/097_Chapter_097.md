# Chapter 97: [A Tit for a Tat](<# "Literal CN: 一报还一报 (yī bào huán yī bào) — an eye for an eye / a tit for a tat / karmic retribution; chapter title">) (第97章 一报还一报)

Along with the descent of this terrifying spiritual pressure, the entire arena fell deathly quiet once more.

Lu Zhen stood isolated and helpless in the center of the arena platform, his back drenched in cold sweat.

As a Nascent Soul cultivator, he had long gained the ability to regulate physiological functions like sweating, yet today, confronted by successive unforeseen dangers, his bodily control had repeatedly failed him.

Yet how could he not feel terror?

To his utter horror, Lu Zhen discovered that he could not perceive the true cultivation depth of this divine sense in the slightest; it had unquestionably transcended the Soul Formation realm, standing above Void Refinement—it might even belong to a Mahayana grandmaster!

At this very moment, that horrific spiritual pressure bore down directly onto the crown of Lu Zhen's head.

Moreover, this spiritual pressure did not crush him in a single instant; rather, it pressed down millimeter by millimeter, making Lu Zhen's bones groan and rattle, as if hairline fractures were creeping downward along his cranium.

An inexplicable realization dawned on Lu Zhen:

That terrifying powerhouse wanted his life!

And that person intended for him to re-enact the exact process of Nie Tongzi's demise, only in agonizing slow motion, grinding him into scattered fragments before the eyes of the entire crowd.

The moment he realized this, a roar of fury tore from Lu Zhen's throat.

He would rather detonate his own core than endure such a humiliating death!

Yet, following close upon that defiant roar, Lu Zhen felt the crushing weight upon his crown suddenly lighten. The dreadful spiritual pressure dissipated in an instant, and the spiritual energy within his dantian surged, brimming with vigorous power once more.

—"Well done,"-a gentle voice spoke within his mind.-"Even when trapped in the depths of a desperate impasse, you refused to yield. You possess great courage."

—"Who's there?!"-Lu Zhen cried in shock, scanning his surroundings.

He found no trace of the voice's origin.

Yet what manner of person could so effortlessly neutralize the might of a grandmaster above the Void Refinement stage?

Pondering this, Lu Zhen suddenly glanced toward the towering skeletal remains on the arena stage, his eyes lighting up:

—"C-Could it be... Lord True Immortal?"

Could the "Immortal's Bones" on this very map be assisting him?

Yet that gentle voice replied:

—"Setting that aside, your battle is not yet over—"

The battle wasn't over?

Lu Zhen wondered in bewilderment, only to see an arrogant cultivator fly forth from the gallery promenade.

Aggressive and menacing, the cultivator headed straight for the arena platform, barking furiously at Lu Zhen:

—"What audacious gall! Having slain my junior brother, you still dare evade my Master's punishment!"

—"Junior brother? You're in league with Nie Tongzi?"-Lu Zhen asked.

Doubt surged in his heart, for the opponent was plainly an orthodox cultivator without the slightest trace of demonic qi—come to think of it, that horrifying spiritual pressure just now hadn't carried a speck of demonic qi either.

At that moment, the words of the Immortals' Gathering host resolved a portion of the confusion:

—"What is going on?! A new challenger has stepped onto the arena?!"-the host shouted at the top of his lungs.

Lu Zhen felt a twinge of admiration for the man; to still carry on hosting in such a perilous situation was no small feat.

Staring at the [teleprompter array](<# "Literal CN: 提词器 (tícíqì) — teleprompter / cue card array">) visible only to his own eyes, the host reported:

—"This one is from... the Northern Domain?! ...The Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court?! ...C-Ceremonial Master..."-the host was evidently so shaken by the opponent's identity that he [flubbed several lines](<# "Literal CN: 吃了几个螺丝 (chīle jǐ gè luósī) — eating screws / choking on bolts; Chinese slang for flubbing lines, stuttering, or tongue-twisting on air">) in succession.

Yet catching the glare shot at him by the challenging cultivator, the host gritted his teeth and read out the full identity:

—"This challenger is Zhengpingzi, from beneath the seat of Elder Siwu, Ceremonial Master of the Ceremony Court in the Northern Domain's Heavenly Dao Alliance! How astonishing—how could it be someone from the Northern Domain?! Could he have betrayed the Heavenly Dao Alliance and colluded with that demon Nie Tongzi?!"

The host added this provocative question, deliberately steering the narrative to cast the newcomer as a villain.

In any case, he was [already so deep in debt that another debt hardly mattered](<# "Literal CN: 债多不愁 (zhài duō bù chóu) — when you owe too much debt, you stop worrying about it; in for a penny, in for a pound">).

The host had actually endured a spiritual pressure nearly identical to Lu Zhen's moments ago. He understood full well that from the moment Nie Tongzi died upon the arena stage, he and Lu Zhen had become [grasshoppers bound to the same string](<# "Literal CN: 一根绳上的蚂蚱 (yī gēn shéng shàng de màzha) — grasshoppers tied to the same string; stuck in the same boat with shared fate">). There was no hope of forgiveness from that mysterious powerhouse claiming to be Nie Tongzi's master—who, according to the teleprompter cue cards, was none other than Elder Siwu of the Ceremony Court!

Fortunately, another supreme powerhouse was clearly holding Elder Siwu in check! The teleprompter array had to be the handiwork of that person as well.

The host naturally clung tightly to that unknown grandmaster's thigh!

Following the host's announcement, the spectators erupted into an uproar:

[—"The Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Wasn't that Nie Tongzi just now a demon?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"The host just said so, didn't he? That person must have betrayed the Ceremony Court!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Wait, that voice earlier claimed to be Nie Tongzi's master, and this man claims to be that master's disciple. Could it be that Elder Siwu of the Ceremony Court is colluding with demons?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

*

Lord Siwu's face turned iron-green.

As a Mahayana cultivator, his divine sense effortlessly enveloped the entire venue; he could naturally hear the crowd's slanderous gossip.

[Those lowly subhumans dare slander me!]

An overwhelming urge seized Siwu to reduce everything here to dust.

Yet he could not move.

Indeed, Siwu had lost all control over his own physical body.

He had lost control of his power the precise instant he attempted to crush that subhuman who had slain his disciple.

The moment that subhuman resisted, Siwu sensed the spiritual energy across the arena turn thick and viscous, like a spiderweb that plastered over his face in reverse and bound his limbs, rendering him entirely immobile.

What infuriated Siwu even more was that after subduing him, the other party hadn't confronted him directly. Instead, they maintained the spiritual pressure stifling the entire venue, freeing only the arena platform.

And then Siwu heard his own voice commanding his disciple:

—"Zhengpingzi, go punish that opponent!"

His disciple naturally harbored no suspicion.

—"Yes,"-Zhengpingzi answered reverently, soaring toward the arena.

*

Of course, Zhengpingzi was also seething with rage at the host's slander.

—"You are the ones colluding with demons!"-Zhengpingzi accused furiously.-"Otherwise, how could a mere rogue cultivator at the Nascent Soul stage possess the power to instantly slay someone of the same rank?!"

His accusation was met with a chorus of boos.

Since when did being stronger than you mean colluding with demons?

—"I didn't..."-Lu Zhen began, attempting to offer an explanation.

Hearing that his opponent hailed from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court, Lu Zhen wondered if there was some misunderstanding; if possible, he did not want to forge a mortal enmity with someone of such elite pedigree.

Yet contemplating such things at this juncture was plainly far too naive of Lu Zhen.

In Zhengpingzi's eyes, their feud was already mortal and irreconcilable.

Without waiting for Lu Zhen to finish, Zhengpingzi raised his longsword and struck.

*Clang! Clang!*

Lu Zhen exchanged a few swift blows, only to be forced steadily backward.

Lu Zhen was originally a talisman cultivator trained in the Xiafei Sect; direct frontal melee combat was hardly his forte. And within those few exchanges, Lu Zhen felt palpable killing intent emanating from his opponent, realizing this matter could not be resolved peacefully.

Right then, that gentle voice resounded once more:

—"Do not hesitate. Isn't this a fine opportunity to test what you've learned?"

[Lord True Immortal...]

Lu Zhen called out in his heart.

Alas, the "True Immortal" provided no further instructions. Zhengpingzi's blade flashed with radiant sunset light, tearing a breach through Lu Zhen's outer spiritual barrier, its razor-sharp killing intent aiming straight for his throat.

In that instant, Lu Zhen made up his mind.

He surged backward, breaking away from the close-quarters melee, and formed a hand-seal from a distance.

Seeing the identical movement that had instantly obliterated Nie Tongzi, Zhengpingzi hastily braced his defenses, yet he saw that the colossal skeletal hand on the stage remained motionless. Only radiant light began gathering within Lu Zhen's hand-seal.

Zhengpingzi breathed a sigh of relief:

[Naturally, how could such an ultimate move be unleashed so easily? Besides, with Master here, he shouldn't be able to pull off anything more formidable...]

Just then, Zhengpingzi sensed the terrifying power coalescing within Lu Zhen's hand-seal.

Shock washed over him as he gasped:

—"You actually stole the power of a True Immortal?!"

—"What 'stole'?!"-Lu Zhen retorted.-"I learned it openly and honestly from Villa Master Qingyang's thesis in *Arts & Sorcery*—"

Along with his shout, the shimmering radiance surged toward Zhengpingzi, sticking fast to his body and blasting him clear out of the arena!

*

—"Our Platform Master has defended his victory once again!"

—"He revealed mid-match that his ultimate move actually originates from Villa Master Qingyang!"

—"Does this finally put a full stop to the long-standing dispute between our Central Domain's Eternal Villa and the Northern Domain's Ceremony Court over the True Immortal's heritage? Judging by this, our Central Domain holds the upper hand!"

The host seemed to have gone completely mad, having discarded all caution regarding whether an almighty grandmaster from the Ceremony Court might be present in the arena, letting his tongue run wild.

The crowd erupted into frenzied cheers as well.

Half of their fervor served as open defiance against Siwu's terrifying spiritual pressure.

Witnessing this scene, another disciple from the Ceremony Court bowed and offered:

—"Master, allow this disciple to go up and teach him a lesson!"

[Go up my ass! Do you have any idea how he defeated Zhengpingzi?!]

Siwu cursed furiously in his heart.

These disciples lacked sufficient cultivation; even the two Combined stage cultivators accompanying him hadn't figured out the secret behind Lu Zhen's overwhelming victory. Yet Siwu had seen right through it:

That petty cultivator didn't possess such power at all! His victory was achieved by borrowing the immortal energy permeating the arena platform!

This was not a fair contest in the slightest!

His disciples, ignorant of the underlying trick, would only be battered one after another if they went up.

In other words, the earlier scenario of [Calabash Brothers saving Grandpa](<# "Literal CN: 葫芦娃救爷爷 (Húluwá jiù yéye) — Calabash Brothers saving Grandpa; classic Chinese animation meme referring to trickling in one by one and getting picked off sequentially">) had been flipped on its head—now it was Siwu's side [feeding kills](<# "Literal CN: 送 (sòng) — feeding / throwing in gaming slang; feeding oneself sequentially to the enemy">)!

Siwu desperately wanted to stop them, yet he felt his head nod gently in assent.

Believing he had received Siwu's approval, that disciple soared straight toward the arena.

Siwu was consumed by incandescent fury:

[Fools! With so many of you, not one noticed anything wrong with me?!]

Yet Siwu's disciples genuinely hadn't noticed.

In their eyes—and in the eyes of anyone acquainted with Siwu—the Ceremony Court's Elder Siwu perpetually wore an impassive, serene demeanor, all while orchestrating the most ruthless deeds from the shadows. Everyone was too terrified of provoking him; who would dare scrutinize whether his calm expression concealed a sinister scheme or merely a bout of facial paralysis?

Consequently, Siwu's side sent several disciples forward in rapid succession.

Meanwhile, on the arena platform, Lu Zhen's technique grew increasingly refined.

At the beginning, his hand-seals had been noticeably clumsy and his control over the power erratic, which was why he had squashed his first opponent into meat paste in a single blow. Yet after being provided several "sparring partners" from Siwu's retinue, Lu Zhen's execution grew fluid and seamless. However, each subsequent bout dragged out longer; Lu Zhen clearly "treasured" these sparring partners, striving to wring every drop of practice from each of them.

By this point, even an idiot among Siwu's disciples could see that they were no match for Lu Zhen.

Yet under the oppressive intimidation of the mercurial Elder Siwu, they could only grit their teeth and step up to be slaughtered one by one.

It was fortunate that among the entourage Siwu had brought on this excursion, aside from a Combined stage deputy—who wasn't present, having been dispatched elsewhere on business—the rest were predominantly at the Nascent Soul and Soul Formation stages, meaning there was no immediate danger of running out of bodies.

Finally, after the ninth disciple was dispatched, Siwu heard the crisp chime of shattering glass.

It was the sound of the spiritual energy barrier he had erected around this arena layer breaking apart.

Originally, Siwu had laid down this barrier to keep Cloud City's patrols away so they wouldn't interrupt his delightful "theater," but hearing the sound now, he felt an impulse to weep tears of joy.

Has someone finally come to put an end to this farce?!

Siwu rotated his eyeballs with fervent hope, looking toward the shattered barrier, only for his face to instantly darken.

Cultivators—so many cultivators!

Led by Fairy Cloudglow, the mistress of Cloud City, followed by a retinue of Combined and Mahayana old monsters totaling over a dozen individuals, including Mahayana powerhouses from both the Southern and Western Domains!

[Did Yunxia bring an entire audience just to watch me make a fool of myself?!]

Siwu thought in boiling resentment.

He knew it—Yunxia was the one who had trapped him!

In fact, Siwu had suspected as much from the very beginning.

As a Mahayana cultivator who regarded himself as an elite even among his peers, Siwu never believed anyone could restrain him—unless... unless the opponent possessed the home-field advantage of a colossal immortal citadel like Cloud City! In that case, even a True Immortal might fall into the trap if caught off guard!

And precisely because he had identified the "culprit," Siwu hadn't felt much panic upon discovering his body was immobilized.

He was smugly certain that Fairy Cloudglow wouldn't dare harm him.

The truth seemed to align with Siwu's conjecture; at that very instant, Siwu felt his divine soul lighten as he regained complete control over his physical body.

Siwu instantly barked:

—"Yunxia! You dare trap me with restrictive formations and injure my disciples—is this your hospitality?!"

—"Ah? Siwu?"-Fairy Cloudglow blurted, caught completely off guard as she was blasted with accusations the moment she stepped inside, utterly bewildered.

She had no inkling of what had occurred.

It was only because the Heavenly Spirit Sect had instituted far too many strict regulations for this Immortals' Gathering. Not only were patrols required to follow timed patrol routes, but they also had to carry "route recording devices" that transmitted real-time data to the "monitoring center," alongside two additional distinct monitoring mechanisms. Only through this redundant surveillance did the staff notice that today's patrols had been unconsciously veering away from a certain Nascent Soul arena platform.

The inspectors overseeing the gathering were powerless to breach a Mahayana-grade barrier, so the issue was escalated level by level until it reached Fairy Cloudglow.

As for why there were so many people, that was simply the everyday state of Fairy Cloudglow's Cloud Pond.

Fairy Cloudglow loved banquets above all else. If something happened, she hosted a banquet; if nothing happened, she hosted a banquet. Today marked the opening of the Immortals' Gathering, which called for grand celebrations, meaning yet another banquet. Thus, her Cloud Pond was perpetually packed with cultivators [flocking together](<# "Literal CN: 扎堆 (zhāduī) — flocking together / congregating in a dense cluster">).

Hearing that an undetectable barrier had manifested at the Immortals' Gathering, everyone tagged along, curious to see who dared stir up trouble.

However, Fairy Cloudglow's party had spent the last fifteen minutes locked in a bitter struggle against that barrier from the outside.

They had arrived a quarter of an hour ago, yet despite exhausting every technique, they couldn't unravel the barrier. Just as everyone was growing frustrated and debating whether to use brute force, the barrier had suddenly dissolved on its own.

Fairy Cloudglow had charged inside aggressively with the crowd, heading straight toward Siwu.

Upon the spectator gallery, Siwu's Mahayana spiritual aura shone as conspicuously as a luminous lantern in the dead of night.

Yet before Fairy Cloudglow could demand an explanation, Siwu [raked back in retaliation](<# "Literal CN: 倒打一耙 (dào dǎ yī pá) — counter-attacking with a rake like Zhu Bajie; turning around to counter-accuse or shift the blame">).

Fairy Cloudglow laughed out of pure exasperation:

—"Entering my territory uninvited without an invitation, setting up a barrier here to block my sight—is this the Ceremony Court's 'etiquette'?"

—"It was merely a game,"-Siwu retorted self-righteously.-"Since Cloud City hosted the Immortals' Gathering, it naturally welcomes everyone! What harm is there in me playing a little joke? I didn't harm anyone."

—"Didn't harm anyone?"-Fairy Cloudglow raised her voice sharply.

By now, the host had already reported the events to the patrols entering to maintain order. For a Mahayana cultivator, multitasking several trains of thought was the most rudimentary of divine abilities.

Fairy Cloudglow had already swept her divine soul across the venue, immediately grasping everything that had transpired.

Fairy Cloudglow demanded in outrage:

—"Instigating a demon to attack contestants—that's what you call not harming anyone?"

—"That disciple of mine cannot be considered a demon! He possesses True Immortal blood; he merely took a wrong turn in his early years and practiced a bit of demonic arts!"-Siwu argued.

A wrong turn in his early years?!

Fairy Cloudglow let out a cold sneer.

Not a single demonic cultivator was innocent, especially one who had pursued demonic arts all the way to the Nascent Soul realm—if not intolerable to heaven and earth, he was certainly the sort who deserved heavenly execution.

One could only wonder what Siwu had seen in him to take him on as a disciple.

Fairy Cloudglow mused.

Although Fairy Cloudglow hadn't interacted with Siwu in the past, she was widely connected and had heard numerous "exploits" regarding this elder.

Because Siwu had been chosen by a True Immortal at birth, blessed with immortal qi channeled through a remnant passage from the True Immortal Realm, he was groomed from infancy to serve as the chief priest for the Heavenly Sacrificial Rites.

He had been raised inside the celestial palaces of the Floating Sky Citadel, drinking only immortal dew and listening only to celestial melodies; rumor had it that prior to reaching Void Refinement, he had never even set eyes upon a cultivator beneath the Combined stage.

Whether a priest raised this way was pure enough remained unproven, but his willful arrogance was beyond dispute.

He had once terminated a grand ritual costing tens of millions of spirit stones simply because a Soul Formation "low-tier cultivator" brushed against a ritual chalice. He had also entered seclusion for two thousand years, abandoning all duties, purely because a minor demon had wandered into the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

With such a person, there was naturally no reasoning.

Fairy Cloudglow wasted no breath on pleasantries, simply demanding:

—"Then what about the cultivators he devoured?"

—"They are perfectly fine,"-Siwu said.

Having prepared in advance, Siwu tore open a spatial rift and dumped those cultivators heavily before Fairy Cloudglow.

Witnessing this, both Lu Zhen and the host felt their heads spin.

They thought they were utterly doomed!

That "demon" hadn't killed anyone, yet their side had slain his disciple; this matter was bound to spiral out of control!

Sure enough, Siwu immediately pressed on with his accusation:

—"My disciple didn't harm anyone, yet your contestant killed my disciple. Shouldn't [a life pay for a life](<# "Literal CN: 杀人偿命 (shā rén cháng mìng) — a life must be paid for taking a life">)?!"

As he spoke, he reached out his hand to seize Lu Zhen.

Lu Zhen's face drained of all color. Trapped beneath the suffocating pressure of a Mahayana grandmaster, there was nowhere to run. He could only watch as a colossal hand bore down toward him—

*Smack!*

A crisp, sharp sound echoed through the air.

Not only was that giant hand swatted aside, but a glaring red palm print blossomed across Siwu's cheek.

—"Your Excellency, who said your disciple died? Isn't he alive and well?"-someone remarked casually.

Along with this voice, a gust of wind swept across the arena, lifting all the Ceremony Court disciples Lu Zhen had defeated one by one. Among them was Nie Tongzi, who had been pulverized into mincemeat; he looked as though an invisible hand had casually kneaded him back into shape, yet his posture remained strangely warped, resembling a [patched-up ragdoll](<# "Literal CN: 破布娃娃 (pòbù wáwá) — patched-up ragdoll; humorously describing Nie Tongzi's lumpy, poorly reassembled body">) cobbled together by a mother clumsy with needlework.

The entire group was tossed like a clutch of chicks straight back onto the second-floor tea terrace, tumbling beside Siwu.

Yet no one paid any attention to these disciples now. Siwu, Fairy Cloudglow, and the assembled grandmasters all stared wide-eyed at the silver-haired young man who had materialized abruptly in the seating area right next to Siwu's tea table.

The youth sat there leisurely cradling a black lamb, flanked by two disciples standing behind him. Three half-emptied cups of tea sat upon the table, proving beyond doubt that he had been sitting there for quite some time.

—"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable!"-Fairy Cloudglow cried in delightful surprise.

—"Un... tiring...?"-Siwu stammered, his expression twisted in sheer horror.

No one would feel comfortable discovering that the empty seat beside them—where they had sat an entire day—had actually been occupied by a person all along, let alone a whole group.

Siwu looked between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Fairy Cloudglow, his voice raspy:

—"Could it be, the one who trapped me earlier was..."

—"Hardly 'trapped',"-Zhou Hui replied with a mild smile.-"I merely didn't wish for the match to be disrupted."

Siwu flared up in rage once more:

—"A match? You call that a match?!"

—"Why shouldn't it be called a match?"-Zhou Hui said.-"Unlike you, who used spatial spellcraft to assist your disciple, I did not intervene in the slightest. Everything relied entirely on the contestant's own ability, completely within the rules!"

—"I didn't order my disciples to go up! It was all your guidance—"

—"And the cultivators who fought Nie Tongzi—were they not misled?"-Zhou Hui's tone turned chillingly cold, his silver-gray pupils fixing upon Siwu.-"Exploiting their righteous hearts to slay demons and defend the Dao, only to toy with them! Lord Siwu, what difference is there between you and those demons?!"

With those words, a terrifying spiritual pressure crashed toward Siwu.

In that instant, Siwu experienced the same crushing suffocation Lu Zhen had felt on the stage earlier, only ten times more overwhelming.

Beneath that horrific spiritual might, Siwu felt like a lowly insect that could be crushed underfoot at any moment.

[This power... how can it be even more terrifying than a True Immortal?!]

[He's "merely" a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator who can't ascend! How did he do this?!]

Siwu thought in sheer panic.

Yet he possessed none of Lu Zhen's courage to fight to the death. Collapsing backward onto the floor, he trembled uncontrollably, waiting for that spiritual pressure to grind him into dust, his chattering teeth unable to utter even a single plea for mercy.

Yet in the very next instant, that crushing spiritual pressure vanished without a trace.

It was as if it had been nothing more than Siwu's hallucination.

—"[Just kidding,](<# "Literal CN: 开玩笑的 (kāi wánxiào de) — just kidding; throwing Lord Siwu's own excuse back in his face">)"-the silver-haired immortal said with a mild smile, picking up the teacup from the table and taking a leisurely sip.

*

And so, this "joke" finally came to an end.

However, after Siwu's disruptive interference, even the Untiring Immortal Venerable found it unseemly to remain seated there.

Under Fairy Cloudglow's warm invitation, the Untiring Immortal Venerable and his entourage departed from the arena.

The spectators in the arena respectfully watched the Untiring Immortal Venerable and his companions leave. Once their figures had completely vanished into the sea of clouds, a deafening uproar erupted across the arena, even louder and more boisterous than the opening ceremony.

No one cared about the continuation of the tournament anymore; everyone was fervently discussing what had just unfolded:

[—"Did you see that? That was the Untiring Immortal Venerable!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"As expected of the strongest cultivator in our Central Domain! Look how terrified that Heavenly Dao Alliance Mahayana elder was!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Haha, isn't the funniest part that they never realized he was sitting right there all along?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"I knew it! No wonder the second half of the tournament was so hilarious—there was a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster overseeing it the whole time! No wonder nobody could pull any dirty tricks."](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Piss off! You think everyone is like that Northern Domain bastard? The Immortal Venerable said so himself—our Platform Master won through his own true ability!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Speaking of which, didn't the Platform Master mention mid-match where he learned that technique? Eternal Villa?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Right! Wait, no, he's not from Eternal Villa! It sounded like an article written by Villa Master Qingyang in some journal!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"What journal?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

People inquired among themselves, while several quick-witted cultivators dashed straight toward Lu Zhen.

Lu Zhen was still gazing dazedly at the "Immortal's Bones" in the arena's backdrop, murmuring to himself:

—"So it wasn't a True Immortal after all, but the Immortal Venerable..."

It wasn't until the host clapped him on the shoulder that he snapped back to reality. Turning around, he was startled by the sight of the fanatic crowd surging toward him.

—"Everyone wants to ask you—where did you get such a formidable killer move?!"-the host laughed, giving Lu Zhen a shove as if pushing him straight into the crowd.

Looking at the mob that seemed ready to swallow him whole, Lu Zhen suddenly felt a terror entirely distinct from facing a Mahayana grandmaster.

In a frantic panic, he hollered at the top of his lungs:

—"*Arts & Sorcery*! My technique came from the journal *Arts & Sorcery*!"
