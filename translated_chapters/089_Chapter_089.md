# Chapter 89: Exchange Student (第89章 交换生)

When Qingyang Qingyu learned that she had become an "[exchange student](<# "Literal CN: 交换生 (jiāohuànshēng) — modern academic term for exchange student">)," she was thoroughly astonished.

She had assumed her clan ancestor had been occupied with the affairs of the Immortal Magic Conference these past few days; she never imagined he would actually arrange an advanced study opportunity for her.

—"Isn't this wonderful, Miss?"-the maidservant attending Qingyang Qingyu said with a smile.-"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's cultivation is even higher than the Villa Master's. He will surely be able to resolve your questions. Besides, rumors say the Heavenly Spirit Sect's book collection is not inferior to Sky City's. You won't have to marry into the Northern Domain to read the books you desire."

[I knew you would say that.]

As expected, this so-called "exchange education" had to be yet another excuse cooked up by their venerable ancestor to block her from going to the Northern Domain—just like all those prior "blind dates."

Qingyang Qingyu thought to herself with a throbbing headache.

In recent days, Qingyang Qingyu had endured no fewer than a hundred blind dates.

In the cultivation realm, this was exceedingly rare.

For dual cultivation, the optimal ages fell before the Nascent Soul stage and after the Body Integration stage.

The former was youthful romance: their longing for love had not yet extinguished, and their cultivation careers were just beginning. Supporting one another and advancing together allowed their progress to far outstrip solitary cultivation. The drawback, however, was that individual talents and immortal destinies differed; couples who could walk all the way to the end like the Mei-Zhu pair were exceedingly rare.

The latter was [sunset romance](<# "Literal CN: 夕阳红 (xīyánghóng) — twilight love / late-in-life romance among the elderly">)~~(scratch that)~~. By that time, both parties had achieved flourishing cultivation careers with no lingering worries. Coming together could dispel loneliness and allow them to traverse the Great Dao side by side. The drawback was that cultivators at this stage had long seen through the mortal world, leaving them devoid of passion; they would rather keep concubines and [male courtesans](<# "Literal CN: 面首 (miànshǒu) — handsome male concubines / gigolos kept by noblewomen in ancient China">) of their own than tolerate the quirks of a lifelong partner.

With Qingyang Qingyu's status, the choice would have naturally been entirely hers.

Whether she wished to marry or remain unwed, whether she desired a like-minded Dao companion or a fleeting mortal, Eternal Villa could easily support her choices. Consequently, as long as the match was not utterly outrageous, clan elders rarely meddled in disciples' marital affairs. Yet Qingyang Qingyu had always shown zero interest in romance, remaining single all the way to the Golden Core Middle Stage, on the verge of aging out of the prime window for dual cultivation.

Yet karmic affinity was the sort of thing that always arrived unannounced.

Last year, this same Qingyang Qingyu made a shopping trip to Sky City in the Northern Domain and encountered Jian Nuo, the son of Sky City's Lord. Young Master Jian fell in love with Qingyu at first sight and launched a ferocious pursuit.

Villa Master Qingyang, however, did not think much of Young Master Jian.

So what if he was the young master of Sky City? Their Eternal Villa was the legacy of the former Holy Sovereign of White Jade Capital! When it came to pedigree, who could look down on whom?!

Furthermore, Jian Nuo had never been an upright, respectable sort. He caroused, lived a life of debauchery, ran wild with a pack of cultivation scions, and was uncomfortably close with Carefree Palace. Ghost knew whether he would keep several cultivation furnaces stashed in the city after marrying Qingyu.

Most importantly: the Northern Domain was simply too far away!

Traveling from the Central Domain to the Northern Domain required starting at the teleportation array in Fengqing City in the southeastern Central Domain, transferring through the Southern Domain, journeying from the Southern Domain to the Eastern Domain, and finally arriving in the Northern Domain—spanning three entire continents!

Not everyone possessed the terrifying might of the Untiring Immortal Venerable; even the Mahayana-stage Villa Master Qingyang could not traverse such distances casually. This meant that once Qingyu married into the Northern Domain, it would be practically impossible for her to return to Eternal Villa in the short term, and should anything happen, there would be no time to rush to her aid.

Thus, from top to bottom, Eternal Villa vehemently opposed this marriage.

Naturally, had this affair merely been [a one-sided infatuation on Young Master Jian's part](<# "Literal CN: 肩挑担子一头热 (jiān tiāo dànzi yītóu rè) — carrying a shoulder pole hot only on one end; an idiom for entirely unreciprocated, one-sided infatuation">), the matter would have ended right there. The trouble was that Qingyang Qingyu was genuinely tempted as well.

First of all, Young Master Jian was exceptionally handsome—the kind of handsome capable of making two-thirds of women with conventional aesthetics swoon.

Second, Young Master Jian's courtship tactics appeared overflowing with "sincerity" (or rather, overflowing with wealth). Leaving aside their time in Sky City, even now, with Qingyang Qingyu having returned to Eternal Villa in the Central Domain, every morning upon waking she would receive a bouquet of dew-kissed [Sky Lilies](<# "Literal CN: 天上百合 (Tiānshàng bǎihé) — specialty immortal flower of Sky City">)—a specialty blossom of Sky City that Qingyang Qingyu had once casually praised.

Finally, and most, most, most, most importantly:

Sky City possessed the most comprehensive collection of cultivation manuals!

As the undisputed heart of the cultivation world in this era, all the finest treasures congregated in the Northern Domain, especially within Sky City. Thus, Sky City possessed not only treasures from across the entire realm, but also the most exhaustive manual archives in existence!

Moreover, Sky City was the sole place that retained a line of communication with the True Immortal Realm; consequently, their manual archives even contained heritages from the True Immortal Realm itself!

This was something Qingyang Qingyu could never refuse.

Yes—the eldest young miss Qingyang Qingyu, next direct lineage successor of Eternal Villa (a clan whose core motto was "enjoy life"), was inexplicably a thoroughbred cultivation maniac.

Her passion differed from the Untiring Immortal Venerable's brand of [hyper-competitive grinding](<# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn) — involution / ruthless academic grinding">); hers was far purer.

Born into immense privilege, Qingyang Qingyu had never once needed to fret over resources. With Eternal Villa's ancestral heritage alone, even if she refused to cultivate a single day and relied purely on medicinal pills, they could pile her all the way to the Nascent Soul stage.

Yet Qingyang Qingyu never relied on external aids, nor did she care whether a technique was deemed good or bad. She cared neither for a manual's prestige nor the material benefits it yielded; she studied techniques purely for the sake of studying them. She had even simultaneously cultivated water and fire techniques—two mutually destructive opposites—just to observe the interaction, resulting in a severe regression of her cultivation base.

This behavior was not entirely reckless folly. All of her endeavors were aimed at delving into her lifebound technique—the very manual Eternal Villa had passed down across generations: the *True Immortal Mind Sutra*.

This sutra reportedly allowed a cultivator to become an existence equivalent to a True Immortal without ever needing to ascend.

However, whether due to the passage of untold epochs or flaws inherent from its inception, the manual was incomplete. Even Villa Master Qingyang, who had attained its highest mastery, had only reached the Mahayana stage, incapable of breaking through to Tribulation Transcendence—or perhaps it lacked a Tribulation Transcendence breakthrough stage altogether. After all, if one did not even need ascension, what was the point of facing lightning tribulations? Was getting struck by heavenly lightning enjoyable?

Worst of all, this manual was extraordinarily difficult to complete.

No matter how fragmented other manuals were, there remained room for deductive research. But the origins of the *True Immortal Mind Sutra* dated back to before the War of Gods and Demons, an era when the cultivation realm maintained close contact with the True Immortal Realm; the sutra itself was a product of research conducted on True Immortals.

Today, however, the cultivation realm had completely severed contact with the True Immortal Realm; even ascended cultivators could never return. Only Sky City retained the final remaining conduit, and rumors held that even they could not establish contact at will.

Naturally, the conundrums Qingyang Qingyu encountered in her cultivation remained unsolvable. The sole reason a cultivation fanatic like her had been willing to leave her immortal cave and visit Sky City was because she heard their archives contained ancient texts regarding True Immortals that were forbidden from being loaned to outsiders. Who could have anticipated that she would catch the eye of the City Lord's young master?

Yet thinking that marrying Jian Nuo would allow her to examine those True Immortal relics however she pleased, and peruse those True Immortal texts whenever she wished, Qingyang Qingyu was thoroughly, shamefully tempted.

To this, Villa Master Qingyang expressed vehement outrage:

—"If you genuinely fancied that brat, so be it! But if you sell yourself out for such trivial nonsense, you will regret it sooner or later!"-Villa Master Qingyang declared vehemently.

To reshape his great-great...-granddaughter's views on marriage, Villa Master Qingyang enthusiastically began arranging blind dates for her. In his eyes, Qingyu's romantic inclinations simply had not yet awakened, which was why she would consider marrying for such an unreliable reason; if she actually fell in love with someone, that would be a different story.

Qingyang Qingyu merely found him utterly exasperating.

—"Has Ancestor shifted tactics? Did he decide to occupy my time by forcing me to study other techniques? Admittedly, this approach is slightly more respectable than those chaotic blind dates, but it feels like an even greater waste of time!"-Qingyu sneered coldly.

The maidservant finally noticed Qingyu's displeasure:

—"Miss, do you believe Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable lacks the qualifications to teach you?"-the maidservant asked.

—"Stop talking nonsense!"-Qingyu tapped the maidservant's forehead lightly.-"He is a Tribulation Transcendence titan! Were it not for Ancestor, I wouldn't even possess the qualifications to stand in his presence! If anyone lacks qualifications, it is me!"

—"Then why do you seem so reluctant, Miss?"-the maidservant asked, rubbing her forehead in bewilderment.

—"['Every domain has its own specialization'](<# "Literal CN: 术业有专攻 (shù yè yǒu zhuān gōng) — from Han Yu's 'On the Teacher' (师说): different people have mastery in different specialized disciplines; even a grandmaster is not omniscient across all fields">),"-Qingyang Qingyu sighed.-"Even a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse is not omniscient and omnipotent!"

Qingyu swore she harbored zero disrespect toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable, but [their specialties did not align](<# "Literal CN: 专业不对口 (zhuānyè bù duìkǒu) — modern college/career idiom: one's academic major doesn't match the job/task at hand">)!

That Immortal Venerable was a purely indigenous cultivator of this realm; no matter how towering his achievements, they were confined to the techniques of this mortal world. Eternal Villa's techniques, on the other hand, pertained directly to True Immortals. What could she possibly learn from the Untiring Immortal Venerable?

[Is there any way to make the Untiring Immortal Venerable refuse to accept me?]

Qingyang Qingyu pondered.

Having spent recent weeks repelling blind dates, she had developed a reflexive habit, scheming ways to reject him before they had even met.

Just then, the wind chimes outside Qingyang Qingyu's immortal cave chimed—the sign of a visiting guest.

—"Miss, Little Young Master Qingyang Yang requests an audience,"-a maidservant hurried in to report.

The "water-radical" (氵) generation like Qingyang Yang belonged to the generation below Qingyang Qingyu. However, as Qingyang Qingyu spent most of her days in seclusion, she was hardly close with these nieces and nephews, making their visit somewhat surprising.

—"Is there something he needs?"-Qingyu asked curiously, bidding the maidservant usher him in.

This was also Qingyang Yang's first time entering Qingyang Qingyu's immortal cave. Toward this legendary aunt who devoted her life to secluded cultivation, he was visibly intimidated. He dared not pose with his folding fan as he usually did, instead standing meekly in place, eyes glued to the tips of his boots.

Yet his tone could not suppress his excitement:

—"Aunt, is it true you are taking Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect as your Master?!"-Qingyang Yang asked, unable to suppress his excitement.

—"It hasn't gone that far,"-Qingyu said with a bizarre expression.-"You know the rules of our villa. The Qingyang bloodline neither takes outside disciples nor bows to outsiders as masters. I am merely going to study for a period and will return once I finish."

—"What will you be learning?"-Qingyang Yang pressed.

[?] Why was this brat so concerned about this? Qingyu grew even more puzzled, though there was nothing to hide:

—"It hasn't been decided yet,"-Qingyu said.-"Lord Immortal Venerable said I may freely pick any one magical technique in which he specializes."

—"Freely?!"-Qingyang Yang gasped loudly.

Qingyu was startled by his shout.

Realizing his breach of etiquette, Qingyang Yang coughed. Recalling something, he revealed a trace of disappointment mixed with eager anticipation:

—"What do you intend to learn from the Untiring Immortal Venerable? Heavenly Spirit techniques?"-he asked nervously.

—"How could that be? I am not a direct disciple; how could I learn core techniques? Even if he were willing to teach, I couldn't possibly abandon our villa's heritage,"-Qingyu said.-"I haven't decided yet."

—"Is that so? I see!"-Qingyang Yang exclaimed, growing even more excited.

After repeating this several times, he finally looked at Qingyu:

—"Since Aunt has not yet decided, might I offer a tiny, humble suggestion?"-he pleaded.

[So that's it. Did this brat come for the Untiring Immortal Venerable's magical arts?]

[Makes sense. He is a Tribulation Transcendence titan, after all; he must possess countless formidable immortal arts! Not everyone takes this opportunity lightly like I do; many in the villa surely dream of studying under Lord Immortal Venerable.]

Thinking this far, Qingyu could not help feeling a twinge of shame.

Indeed, she had never truly looked into the Untiring Immortal Venerable's might, and for now had nothing she particularly wished to learn from him.

In that case, hearing others' opinions wouldn't hurt.

Qingyu nodded:

—"Speak!"-she said.

—"Y-Yes,"-Qingyang Yang stammered incoherently.-"I believe the Untiring Immortal Venerable is exceedingly magnificent. Even judging from the prowess his disciples displayed, the immortal arts he specializes in are peerless! For example, that strike which slew Instant Immortal Monarch appeared unadorned, yet contained spatial thunder prowess within it..."-Qingyang Yang launched straight into a barrage of [lavish flattery](<# "Literal CN: 彩虹屁 (cǎihóngpì) — modern internet slang: rainbow farts / extravagant, over-the-top praise">).

Because Qingyu rarely cared for external affairs, she listened with keen interest, which only fueled her worries:

[What in the world did Ancestor offer to convince such a miraculous powerhouse? Should I really not squander this opportunity?]

[Yet the subject I care about truly does not overlap with what he teaches! Forcing it won't bring happiness!]

[Then again, learning a few other immortal arts wouldn't conflict either; spending a little time is well worth it. For instance, the technique Yang-yang just described sounds exceptionally useful...]

Unaware that his aunt, who had possessed zero interest moments ago, was nearly swayed by his words, Qingyang Yang, after a lengthy [wind-up](<# "Literal CN: 前摇 (qiányáo) — gaming slang: wind-up animation / preparatory delay before unleashing a skill">), finally revealed his true objective:

—"Yet compared to those spells designed for individual use, I believe the most miraculous art in the Immortal Venerable's possession is the Heavenly Spirit Sect's method of secret realm exploration! They seem to call it the 'Secret Realm Guidance System'? Whether for exploring secret realms, guiding numerous disciples to level up, or sustaining secret realms for perpetual reuse, it is extraordinarily complete! Still, I know this is an unreasonable request—asking Aunt to surrender her own personal learning opportunity to benefit our villa. Yet considering the future of our villa, I must brazenly put forward this suggestion. Please, Aunt, give it serious thought!"-he pleaded earnestly.

—"Such a magical art actually exists?"-Qingyu asked, hearing of this for the very first time.

Noticing Qingyu's ignorance, Qingyang Yang breathed a sigh of relief, his tone growing even more animated:

—"Indeed! Our sect even reserved a hundred-year exploration quota! We even wanted to purchase an immortal cave outside their secret realm for our disciples to study, but were sadly turned down!"-he added with great exaggeration.

Qingyu harbored no doubts upon hearing this.

After all, lying about such matters was pointless; a single inquiry would reveal the truth.

Naturally, Qingyu deeply loved her sect; otherwise, she wouldn't have dedicated herself to completing their ancestral mind sutra. Now that she learned of a technique capable of benefiting the entire clan, she was certainly willing to study it.

Yet Qingyu did not commit herself completely. Since Ancestor had not specifically mentioned this, perhaps there were even better options available? Qingyu turned prudent:

—"I will consider it,"-Qingyu said.

Qingyang Yang did not press Qingyu for an immediate decision.

After all, he had rushed over on a sudden whim after hearing Qingyu was bound for Heavenly Spirit Sect; swaying his aunt's heart was already a massive triumph.

Thus, cheering inwardly, Qingyang Yang gazed intently at Aunt Qingyu:

—"Our sect's future rests in your hands, Aunt!"-he proclaimed.

[There is really no need for that.]

[Why act as though this is a matter of life and death for our sect?]

Qingyu [roasted](<# "Literal CN: 吐槽 (tùcháo) — tsukkomi / snarky internal commentary">) inwardly.

Soon, however, Qingyu discovered it might actually be warranted.

Shortly after Qingyang Yang left her cave, faint cheers drifted in from outside.

Curious, Qingyu swept her divine sense outward, only to find over a hundred disciples waiting below her immortal cave for Qingyang Yang to emerge. Upon receiving his progress report, every single face mirrored Qingyang Yang's identical fervor.

[Are they genuinely taking this so seriously?!]

Qingyu's inclination to study that "Secret Realm Guidance System" strengthened by several degrees.

---

While Qingyang Qingyu was embroiled in internal turmoil and second-guessing her ancestor's intentions, Villa Master Qingyang was enjoying himself so immensely that he was ready to trade away his troublesome great-great...-granddaughter entirely.

—"Qianxing-er, will you truly not consider marrying into our clan?"-Villa Master Qingyang pitched to Qu Qianxing for the [Nth time](<# "Literal CN: 第N次 (dì N cì) — modern slang: for the umpteenth time / Nth time">), no longer even restricting the offer to the direct lineage.-"The girls of our Qingyang clan are exceptionally beautiful! You may pick whichever one catches your eye. Whomever you marry, you will be part of our Qingyang clan, and I shall treat you as my direct disciple..."

—"Lord Qingyang, here is today's laboratory report,"-Qu Qianxing said, maintaining a polite, distant smile as he handed a report into Villa Master Qingyang's hands.

Villa Master Qingyang closed his mouth in regret and examined the report.

He knew this was Qu Qianxing's polite refusal, but despite being a Mahayana cultivator rebuffed by a Golden Core disciple, he harbored no real anger.

Villa Master Qingyang had already learned that this strikingly handsome young man was the Untiring Immortal Venerable's designated successor and future Sect Master of Heavenly Spirit Sect; he naturally could never marry into Eternal Villa.

His behavior was practically tantamount to [poaching from another's foundation](<# "Literal CN: 挖墙脚 (wā qiángjiǎo) — digging at the foot of the wall; poaching personnel, talent, or partners from someone else">).

Yet Villa Master Qingyang truly could not resist testing his luck!

Even if he couldn't poach him entirely, getting him to marry a daughter of the Qingyang clan would still be splendid.

After all, he had never encountered such a perfect disciple!

Yes—perfect!

While disciples always dreamed of apprenticing under the most formidable master, from a master's perspective, they equally dreamed of having a perfect disciple.

Qu Qianxing was that very perfect disciple.

The moment he arrived at Villa Master Qingyang's side, he resolved the thesis formatting problem that had given the Villa Master the biggest headache.

Villa Master Qingyang had a belly full of scholarly wisdom to pour forth, but selecting what was suitable, filtering what was not, organizing it into an essay, and formatting it strictly according to the guidelines was something even a Mahayana titan could not achieve overnight!

Yet once Qu Qianxing arrived, none of this was a problem anymore.

Merely by listening to the discussion between Villa Master Qingyang and the Untiring Immortal Venerable, he organized a preliminary outline. Villa Master Qingyang only needed to fill in the blanks according to the outline, while Qu Qianxing handled subsequent editing and formatting polishing.

Although Qu Qianxing had blind spots in theoretical depth and knowledge scope as a Golden Core cultivator, this summarization and editorial refinement alone saved Villa Master Qingyang immense effort.

Of course, had this been his only skill, he would have merely been a [paper-editing tool](<# "Literal CN: 工具人 (gōngjùrén) — utility person / tool used for a specific function without emotional attachment">).

Qu Qianxing also possessed extraordinary powers of observation and learning.

He had only been here for three days, yet had already mapped many of Villa Master Qingyang's habitual idiosyncrasies. For instance: prior to lengthy spellcasting, Villa Master Qingyang liked to condition his folding fan with incense smoke; loosening the censer lid without removing it entirely and passing it over was best, as the Villa Master preferred lifting the lid himself. Another example: while operating the alchemy furnace, Villa Master Qingyang paused briefly when switching techniques; injecting a sliver of spiritual energy into the furnace during that pause shaved off considerable time. Yet another example: the Villa Master had mild germaphobia; handing him spiritual spring water to rinse his hands mid-process while swapping tools during True Immortal blood refinement actually accelerated the experiment.

Villa Master Qingyang had never even noticed these tiny habits himself.

He only felt that with Qu Qianxing standing beside him, the entire process of researching True Immortal blood became unimaginably smooth. It felt as though he had returned to his prime; he didn't even need to use his brain before the next step was already prepared!

At this rate, he might actually break through to Tribulation Transcendence!

This cocky euphoria lasted only about half a day. Soon, Villa Master Qingyang noticed this miraculous efficiency occurred solely when Qu Qianxing was present. A moment's reflection brought sudden clarity.

Villa Master Qingyang was left thoroughly awestruck.

This was far more than mere observation; it required profound familiarity with spell mechanics and experimental procedures. How on earth had the Untiring Immortal Venerable trained his disciple to be so extraordinarily capable?

In comparison, his own disciples were simply...

Thinking this, Villa Master Qingyang glared at several of his disciples.

Most of Villa Master Qingyang's disciples possessed Nascent Soul or Soul Formation cultivation, including a Body Integration ancestor brought along as an assistant. Right now, none felt half as useful as Qu Qianxing.

The disciples were actually deeply aggrieved:

[We want to help too, but our hands aren't as fast as Qu Qianxing's! Besides, half the time we aren't even sure if we should step in. Getting scolded by Master for a mistake is trivial, but ruining precious materials would be the end of us!]

[Furthermore, this Qu Qianxing is an absolute freak! There are things Master never even explained, yet he watches from the side and grasps them instantly. Even if he doesn't know it, by the next attempt he's mastered it! One can hardly tell who the actual direct disciples of Eternal Villa are!]

Naturally, a master would never concern himself with his disciples' grievances.

Seeing his attempt at [matchmaking](<# "Literal CN: 拉皮条 (lā pítiáo) — pimping / pushy matchmaking">) fall flat once more, Villa Master Qingyang could only say:

—"Qianxing-er, if you find time, pray guide my disciples on how to write within the paper formatting as well!"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

After all, relying solely on someone else's child was unsustainable; as Heavenly Spirit Sect's successor and de facto second-in-command, Qu Qianxing was terribly busy and frequently absent.

In fact, because he was so busy, Qu Qianxing had once entertained the notion of sending Fourth Junior Brother to replace him. But that Fourth Junior Brother appeared equally swamped; the two disciples had nearly argued over it. In the end, Villa Master Qingyang set up a temporary laboratory beneath the Abyss Sea near the Immortals' Bazaar, allowing Qu Qianxing to dart back to the sect to manage affairs, finally settling the dispute.

[Though whether it was just Villa Master Qingyang's imagination or not, he felt Qu Qianxing simply looked down on him... Mm, it must be an illusion! How could such a respectful, thoroughly attentive child harbor such arrogant thoughts?!]

See? Aside from marriage proposals, Qu Qianxing executed his every instruction to the letter.

—"Very well, Lord Qingyang. I will ensure the senior masters understand how papers are written,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

—"Heheh, there is no need to call them seniors. When it comes to paper writing, they are far inferior to you! Feel free to order them about as peers,"-Villa Master Qingyang laughed.

Deliberately ignoring the aggrieved glares of his own disciples, he sauntered off cheerfully, waving his fan and carrying his teapot.

If there was a second reason Villa Master Qingyang was enjoying himself so thoroughly that he [had no desire to return home](<# "Literal CN: 乐不思蜀 (lè bù sī Shǔ) — indulging in present pleasures and forgetting one's home / native place">), it was certainly the location of this temporary immortal cave.

Although hastily constructed to accommodate Qu Qianxing, living in it proved delightfully comfortable! Not to mention that the spiritual spring bathhouse system was sheer perfection, traveling between the Immortals' Bazaar and Heavenly Spirit Sect was effortless.

Alongside other cultivators, he could drop by the Academic Hall to debate scholarship, or head to the Floating Water Gardens to exchange gossip, play a few games of chess, and soak up the sun. Nothing could be more relaxed or pleasant.

At times, Villa Master Qingyang even felt the Immortals' Bazaar was a bit far; if not for confidentiality concerns, living in the newly built caves near Heavenly Spirit Sect like Hermit Zui Liu would be outright ideal, with all amenities fully equipped.

[I could truly live like this forever.]

Villa Master Qingyang could not help musing.

Immediately afterward, he shuddered at the thought. Wasn't this precisely the effect the Untiring Immortal Venerable had intended?

If even he, the master of Eternal Villa, was this happy, what about those solitary cultivators who had lived alone for centuries with no one to converse with?

—"What's the matter?"-Hermit Zui Liu asked curiously, sensing Villa Master Qingyang's gaze.

Villa Master Qingyang pondered, but chose not to voice his deduction.

After all, [one was willing to beat and the other willing to suffer](<# "Literal CN: 一个愿打一个愿挨 (yīgè yuàn dǎ, yīgè yuàn ái) — both parties are entirely willing; a mutual arrangement">); what sort of busybody monster was he to interfere?

Villa Master Qingyang merely said:

—"...It is about time I head back,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

—"Heading back already? You haven't even been here for five minutes,"-Hermit Zui Liu remarked in surprise.-"What did you even come over for?"

[To deliberately give the brats room to establish the pecking order, of course!]

[...Ah, his disciples could hardly be called brats anymore.]

Villa Master Qingyang thought with veteran craftiness.

The hierarchy of an immortal cave had to be established early. Just as before, his original disciples were definitely dissatisfied; letting it fester would breed personal grudges, harming future harmony. Thus, he might as well ignite the fuse early and let them hash it out among themselves.

He did feel a pang of pity for Qianxing-er; with the lowest cultivation base, the boy would inevitably suffer a slight disadvantage.

Yet that provided the perfect excuse to shower him with artifacts and spirit stones afterward to buy his loyalty. Perfect!

Musing thus, Villa Master Qingyang returned in high spirits to the temporary laboratory~~(scratch that)~~ immortal cave. He deliberately suppressed his spiritual aura, slipping in soundlessly to inspect the situation.

As expected, the atmosphere inside was far more relaxed than when he had left, without a trace of the initial hostility. Instead, several of his disciples stood obediently before Qu Qianxing, listening to him deliver a lecture...

Wait... a lecture?!

Wait a moment! Based on cultivation hierarchy, shouldn't Qianxing-er have been the one to lose? In the worst-case scenario, Qu Qianxing might have ended up saddled with writing the entire paper—which Villa Master Qingyang naturally wouldn't permit. Villa Master Qingyang had merely wanted the slightly aloof Qu Qianxing to show some deference to his senior disciples, thereby guiding them more diligently in paper writing.

Qu Qianxing was indeed teaching with utmost seriousness, but how had the pecking order become...

Just as Villa Master Qingyang was hopelessly bewildered, Qu Qianxing seemed to sense something and turned his head—as a Golden Core cultivator, he appeared to pierce right through a Mahayana titan's concealment—looking straight toward Villa Master Qingyang's position.

—"Lord Qingyang, you have returned?"-Qu Qianxing stood up with apparent deference.

—"Y-You... what are you all doing...?"-Villa Master Qingyang asked, meeting those stunning eyes with a sudden jolt in his heart, unable to contain his bewilderment.

—"In accordance with your instructions, I was instructing the senior masters on how to write papers,"-Qu Qianxing said with a warm smile.

—"Yes! Senior Brother Qu teaches wonderfully!"-the Eternal Villa disciples chimed in unison.

[Wait, he calls you seniors, but you call him Senior Brother Qu—how does that math work?!]

[Where is your dignity?!]

Villa Master Qingyang's vision went black. Inexplicably, rumors flashed through his mind of how the Untiring Immortal Venerable had [lectured high-cultivation practitioners until they wept](<# "Literal CN: 训哭了高修为修士 (xùn kū le) — berating/lecturing senior cultivators until they burst into tears from academic inadequacy">).

[Ah... he truly is the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple!]

Villa Master Qingyang finally attained enlightenment.
