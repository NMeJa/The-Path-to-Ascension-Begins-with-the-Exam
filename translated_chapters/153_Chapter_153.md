# Chapter 153: Testing (第153章 测试)

What on earth was this nonsense?!

Liu Xuenian desperately wanted to curse a blue streak.

He had always known this pack of [strawbags](# "Literal CN: 草包 (cǎobāo) — strawbags; useless, incompetent good-for-nothings") couldn't accomplish anything of substance. These [cultivation second-generation](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū-èrdài) — spoiled scions born into powerful cultivation clans") scions of Sky City had never been meant for serious operations in the first place. Beyond padding Carefree Palace's sales figures, their so-called "missions" were merely to exploit their convenient backgrounds to pry into the affairs of Sky City and the upper-echelon cultivation world of the Northern Domain, quietly sowing subtle influences to expand Carefree Palace's reach. Such "assignments" relied strictly on leveraging their status to manipulate their powerful ancestors; personal competence was never required of them. In fact, the more useless they were, the better.

Yet he had never imagined they could be quite so utterly, profoundly worthless!

Even Liu Xuenian's current physical vessel was equally incompetent and useless. Both physical constitution and spiritual power were an absolute mess of weakness; why, whenever he used this body's brain to think, he even felt distinctly dim-witted. Aside from a strikingly pretty face, it possessed virtually zero merit.

[If only I could swap into someone like Qu Qianxing, who possessed both stellar intellect and peak talent... *Tch!*]

For a fleeting moment, Liu Xuenian nearly threw in the towel.

Still, he resolved to put up one final struggle.

Opportunities like this were exceedingly rare! If nothing else, he had to ascertain the status of the Demon Realm rift, uncover what had caused the demons to spill out ahead of schedule, and survey the defensive deployments here. Only then would this trip not be an utter waste.

—"Head backward!"-Liu Xuenian's thoughts churned, and he directed Xiao Geng.-"Don't head for the central command tower. Walk in the direction these demonic creatures crawled out from."

—"? Why? 'Everyone' is heading in this direction?"-Xiao Geng asked in utter bafflement.

—"Are you an idiot?!"-Liu Xuenian snapped. Having completely lost his patience, he no longer bothered to maintain his frail, lovely persona.-"Think about the 'game' you were all playing! The more you advance along the prescribed path, the more brutally you get targeted! We might as well head backward."

Fortunately, Xiao Geng was rather thick-skulled. Unaware of the shift in Liu Xuenian's tone, he merely asked in foolish earnest:

—"But... wouldn't doing that make it impossible to test the defensive towers' firepower?"-Xiao Geng muttered.

After all, this was what that Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple calling herself the "Testing Department Manager" had explicitly told him:

> —"Fellow Daoist has surely realized it by now: the demon... monster puppets from earlier possessed very little intellect, capable only of marching mindlessly along the corridor. But real demons aren't all like that! To make the secret realm feel more authentic and enhance the user experience for everyone, we require an intelligent individual to simulate a demon, creating unforeseen crises to stress-test our defensive battle lines. Seeing Fellow Daoist take part in every single examination and brimming with supreme confidence, you are precisely the rare talent we need!"-the Testing Department Manager had said.

Naturally, the "clever" Xiao Geng couldn't possibly turn down such a sincere, flattering mission; he felt a colossal weight of responsibility resting squarely upon his shoulders.

—"You really... whatever,"-Liu Xuenian muttered, swallowing his urge to scoff before nudging him.-"Did they specifically forbid you from walking backward? Think about it: if the monsters suddenly retreat and refuse to advance, how is this secret realm supposed to carry on?"

—"But puppets can't disobey their master's commands, can they?"-Xiao Geng pointed out, proving he still possessed a sliver of intellect.

They had already established these were demonic creatures, for heaven's sake!

—"That only applies to mindless puppets! But sentient puppets are an entirely different matter. If they see those defensive towers are excessively terrifying, they might very well abandon their advance and run straight backward! Wouldn't that create massive trouble?"-Liu Xuenian coaxed patiently.

Xiao Geng was slightly persuaded:

—"True enough! Last time I bought a sword at Carefree Pavilion, rumors claimed it housed a ten-thousand-year-old sword spirit! Completely invincible! But when a real fight broke out, the damn thing actually flew away! To this day, Carefree Pavilion still refuses to compensate me!"-Xiao Geng complained.

[That wasn't Carefree Palace's fault!]

[That was your fault!]

[You were using a flying sword as a meat shield! You should be thanking the heavens the sword spirit didn't turn around and lop your head off!]

Liu Xuenian screamed inwardly, yet had no choice but to redouble his efforts and humor the fool:

—"Right? If even a ten-thousand-year-old sword spirit behaves like that, how could you possibly expect mass-produced puppets to obey orders like good little sheep? Besides, judging by what that Manager Sister implied, your mission is precisely to dig up unforeseen problems outside their original blueprint. They surely never anticipated that demonic beasts might refuse to come out and take a beating, did they?"-Liu Xuenian coaxed.

—"Makes sense!"-Xiao Geng marveled.-"Xuenian, you're so smart!"

[...Compared to you, yes.]

Liu Xuenian accepted the praise without blushing.

Still, while Xiao Geng's brain was severely lacking, his initiative was reasonably decent—otherwise, he wouldn't have foolishly sat for every single test in the first place.

Chattering away, he turned on his heel and began marching against the traffic.

The corridor allocated for his "test" on this floor wasn't bad; aside from himself, there were no other monsters. But the moment Xiao Geng stepped through a doorway and found himself facing another corridor, he saw a grand, surging horde of demonic creatures marching toward him from the far end.

Xiao Geng brushed past them shoulder to shoulder.

—"They made these look so lifelike!"-Xiao Geng whispered, instinctively lowering his voice.

He had previously played dozens of matches of this "game." Standing up in the control tower gazing down at the pathways below was indeed worlds apart from confronting these demonic beasts face-to-face. Just catching a glimpse of their hideous visages was enough to make his calves tremble like jelly.

Particularly as Xiao Geng squeezed past these demonic creatures.

Whether because he was moving against the flow or for some other reason, numerous malicious glares locked onto his figure, seemingly appraising his culinary potential.

—"Th-they look like they really want to eat me,"-Xiao Geng stammered, teeth chattering.

[No shit! Those ARE real demonic beasts!]

[The only reason they haven't pounced on you yet is because your cultivation is actually higher than theirs!]

Liu Xuenian thought in exasperation.

Shadowing Xiao Geng and observing the shifting varieties of demonic creatures, he gradually deciphered the structural logic of this tower:

It was a warped, folded space. After crawling out of the Demon Realm, demonic beasts were routed into separate corridors according to their cultivation realms. Corridors in each sector were defended by different "commanders." Yet even if the demons breached the defensive line on that floor, they never actually breached the command post. Instead, they entered an empty chamber masquerading as a command room, only to be promptly teleported into the next corridor to await the next wave of bombardment.

However, just as that Testing Department manager had noted, sentient demonic beasts would not necessarily cooperate so obediently.

If they realized this was an endless meat grinder, they certainly wouldn't foolishly keep marching forward just to be slaughtered by humans. Thus, advancing along the corridors and tearing down defensive structures rewarded them with assorted treasure chests, packed with equipment, nourishment, and sundry supplies. If they overran a floor's "command tower," they were even awarded a "Splendid Treasure Chest" containing high-grade loot.

Naturally, this meager "resupply" didn't truly make the demons much stronger. Yet this carefully calibrated drip-feed of rewards got the demons utterly hooked. It felt so much like acquiring authentic plunder that they couldn't stop even if they wanted to! Demonic creatures were, by their very nature, crystallizations of raw primal desire; they possessed zero capacity for self-restraint. And so, knowing treasures lay ahead, they simply kept marching down the gauntlet without a second thought.

—"Utterly sinister,"-Liu Xuenian muttered under his breath.

—"Wh-what?"-Xiao Geng stammered, not having caught the words.

—"Nothing... Why have you stopped?"-Liu Xuenian noticed that Xiao Geng had abruptly frozen dead in the middle of the path.

—"I can't do this anymore,"-Xiao Geng whimpered, a sob in his throat.-"These monsters are way too terrifying! Th-they can't actually be real, can they?!"

[You're only realizing this now?! How many times did I already tell you?!]

Seeing Xiao Geng reduced to this pathetic state, Liu Xuenian felt a twisted flash of vindictive pleasure.

Still, he couldn't say that aloud. Instead, he had to keep cajoling him:

—"How could that be possible? How would Eternal Villa dare use real demons as a toy for a game? You're feeling frightened—could it be because the demons on this floor have cultivation comparable to your own?"-Liu Xuenian coaxed.

Indeed, Xiao Geng had already ventured quite deep by this point.

Monsters of his caliber—[Nascent Soul](# "Literal CN: 元婴 (yuányīng) — Nascent Soul stage")—would typically be subjected to concentrated barrage fire in the lower reaches. Even if they managed to break through, they would be battered and dripping with blood, leaving them weakened enough for the rookie commanders up top to dispatch them.

Yet Xiao Geng was marching in reverse. Naturally, the swarms he encountered grew progressively stronger and more terrifying.

Back when Xiao Geng and his buddies were lounging in the command tower, laughing heartily, they had declared it impossible for these to be real demons. But walking through the corridor now, staring at these horribly "lifelike" beasts, suspicion inevitably crept in.

Yet Liu Xuenian had no intention of letting Xiao Geng flee; he still needed Xiao Geng to scout out more intelligence on the frontline defenses!

If his divine senses weren't mistaken, just a little further ahead lay the spatial rift leading straight into the Demon Realm!

He had to at least lay eyes on the terrain over there!

Faced with primal terror, gentle cajoling carried little weight. Liu Xuenian resorted to reverse psychology:

—"Actually, saying your cultivation is comparable isn't quite accurate. You are in the Middle Nascent Soul stage, while most of them are merely equivalent to Early Nascent Soul—a whole minor realm beneath you! And you're actually trembling in fear before them? Hahaha,"-Liu Xuenian laughed caustically.

Unfortunately, the provocation fell flat.

While Xiao Geng's cultivation realm was nominally high enough in the wider cultivation world to earn the title of "Nascent Soul old monster," as a pampered second-generation scion of Sky City, his cultivation had been artificially piled sky-high through every conceivable pill and treasure. His mental fortitude bore zero correlation to his realm. Rather than a [Nascent Soul](# "Literal CN: 元婴 (yuányīng) — Nascent Soul stage"), he was practically an [overgrown toddler](# "Literal CN: 巨婴 (jùyīng) — giant infant; an adult who remains emotionally and mentally a spoiled, helpless infant"). His clan didn't even place much expectation on his shoulders; as long as he sired the next generation when the time came, his duty was fulfilled. In prestigious immortal clans with powerhouse ancestors holding down the fort, descendants enjoyed abundant leeway for "trial and error."

An individual like Xiao Geng had zero combat experience facing life-or-death foes head-on. The moment the stifling demonic qi proved impossible to steamroll with raw cultivation, he decisively resolved to retreat.

Even the taunting of a striking beauty proved powerless.

In fact, under the sheer weight of terror, Xiao Geng miraculously recovered a scrap of wits:

—"Y-You're the one who forced me! I never wanted to run down here in the first place... And you're being so weird! Are you really Xuenian? Xuenian would never speak in such a caustic tone!"-Xiao Geng sputtered.

With that, he spun on his heel and prepared to bolt "back."

—"Wait!"-Liu Xuenian cried in alarm, his voice turning tense.-"Show no fear! Do not turn your back—*tch!*"

His warning came a fraction of a second too late.

Before demonic creatures, projecting negative emotions such as fear was a cardinal taboo.

Originally, his cultivation had been a cut above these monsters. Coupled with the monster gear provided by Eternal Villa, which effectively mimicked demonic aura, it had been more than enough for Xiao Geng to pass as a high-tier demon beast. Demonic creatures adhered strictly to the law of the jungle; out of deference to sheer realm, even if they sensed something slightly off, they had only dared glower without taking action.

Yet now, Xiao Geng had brazenly displayed sheer panic. The beasts that had been eyeing him with malice instantly caught scent:

[—"Such a sweet fragrance..."](# "Speaker: Demonic Beast")

[—"A human... it's the scent of a human!"](# "Speaker: Demonic Beast")

[—"Devour him!"](# "Speaker: Demonic Beast")

Roaring with chaotic, insatiable hunger, the horde surged toward Xiao Geng.

Xiao Geng's terror skyrocketed, and he sprinted forward at breakneck speed—

—"...Useless trash!"-Liu Xuenian spat, sneering coldly.

Even now, he merely stood by with folded arms. If anything, a venomous curiosity stirred within Liu Xuenian: if a second-generation scion of Sky City were mauled to death on Eternal Villa's territory, how on earth would they explain it to his clan?

Unfortunately, Liu Xuenian's malice went unrewarded.

Xiao Geng's cultivation was still marginally higher than the demonic beasts after all. While his combat prowess was non-existent, his luck was remarkably solid: he hadn't crossed paths with any demons specialized in extreme speed. Sprinting for dear life, he actually outpaced the horde, diving through the door he had arrived from, shaking off the beasts, and collapsing back into the previous corridor.

Xiao Geng knew it too; the moment he escaped danger, he slumped onto the floor like a pile of wet dough.

—"Are you alright?"-Liu Xuenian inquired hypocritically.

Xiao Geng gave no reply.

Liu Xuenian looked closer, only to discover that the moment his nerves relaxed, Xiao Geng had actually fainted dead away!

—"...Just where on earth did this fellow's cultivation come from?!"-Liu Xuenian muttered in sheer disbelief.

Even with Carefree Pavilion's potent medicinal pills, someone like Xiao Geng remained entirely beyond his comprehension.

Still, with Xiao Geng knocked out, the operation ground to a screeching halt.

Those demonic creatures wouldn't catch up immediately, as they had to navigate the defensive towers' lines of fire. Meanwhile, the monsters belonging to this corridor had already marched far ahead, leaving this exact spot in an empty vacuum zone.

Furthermore, disciples from Eternal Villa and the Heavenly Spirit Sect had been monitoring Xiao Geng's movements all along. They hadn't intervened previously likely because they assumed it was part of the stress testing, and Xiao Geng possessed sufficient cultivation realm to hold his own.

Now that Xiao Geng was frightened unconscious, they would undoubtedly dispatch someone to check on him.

—"Must it end here?!"-Liu Xuenian gritted his teeth.

He stared down at Xiao Geng for a moment.

Xiao Geng sat on the floor with his head drooping. Pampered from birth, so long as he kept his mouth shut and refrained from thinking, he could pass as a fair, handsome young scholar.

—"This face is barely tolerable. So be it—"-Liu Xuenian murmured.

With that, his paper talisman doll abruptly detonated into scraps, scattering fragments of paper all over Xiao Geng's body.

The very next instant, "Xiao Geng" slowly opened his eyes, an eerie, unnatural smile spreading across his features.

"Xiao Geng" stepped through the doorway once more, arriving in the corridor below.

Seeing him dare return, the demonic creatures prepared to lunge, only to be struck motionless by the bottomless, terrifying malice radiating from "Xiao Geng's" frame.

They smelled the aura of a supreme apex predator.

[—"M-Mercy, my Lord! We were momentarily blinded by greed!"](# "Speaker: Demonic Beast")-the demonic creatures stammered, trembling like leaves.

—"Humph. Follow me!"-"Xiao Geng" commanded, leading the pack of demonic beasts downward toward the rift.

---

—"Test Subject Geng has completely deviated from the designated testing trajectory,"-a cultivator reported.

—"What should we do? Shall we stop it?"-Qingyang Qingyu inquired.

Naturally, not only Xiao Geng, but "Jia, Yi, Bing, and Ding" from before were all firmly within Qingyang Qingyu's operational grasp.

Had Qu Qianxing not arrived a day earlier bearing the "Demon-Revealing Mirror" that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had temporarily refined, Qingyang Qingyu would never have permitted these useless wastes near her team.

Putting them to work now, beyond the pragmatic urge to recycle trash, was also intended to disperse them across different departments, mitigating the risk of these foolish young scions being taken hostage.

In truth, owing to the abysmal performance of this clique, even Jian Nuo had suffered a massive deduction of favorability points in Qingyang Qingyu's estimation.

Previously, no matter what her family urged, Qingyang Qingyu maintained her own rigorous evaluation matrix and remained wholly unmoved. But observing this parade of little blockheads, she experienced profound doubt:

As the saying went, [birds of a feather flock together](# "Idiom: 物以类聚 (wù yǐ lèi jù) — things of a kind come together; like attracts like")—could Jian Nuo be an idiot as well?

Even if he wasn't an idiot, dragging along a pack of [pig teammates](# "Literal CN: 猪队友 (zhū duìyǒu) — deadweight allies who drag the whole team down") meant he was destined for disaster sooner or later. Wouldn't it be far wiser to maintain a healthy distance?

Jian Nuo had not the slightest inkling that he was standing before the single greatest roadblock on his path to matrimony. However, for a variety of reasons, this operation had not been concealed from him, so Jian Nuo was currently standing right beside Qingyang Qingyu, peering intently into the Demon-Revealing Mirror.

The reflection within the mirror was far from gentle.

From the very outset, a length of bloody intestine had been coiled around Xiao Geng's neck, strangling his throat in a death grip. Sprouting from that intestine was a colossal bloody tumor bearing a human face! Xiao Geng had been casually conversing with this grotesque human-faced tumor the entire time—a sight both horrifying and sickening.

Had it stopped at that, it might still have been tolerable.

Yet as Xiao Geng had ventured deeper toward the Demon Realm, the tumor had swelled ever larger. By the time Xiao Geng had tried to turn tail, the bloody growth had bloated to the size of his own skull, making it look as though Xiao Geng had sprouted two heads.

And most terrifying of all, the moment Xiao Geng passed out from fright, that bloody tumor had actually ruptured, instantly drenching Xiao Geng's upper torso in crimson gore!

Now, this half-blood-drenched figure walked forward with an eerie grin, steadily advancing toward the spatial rift.

What was more, he wasn't advancing alone—an enormous swarm of demonic creatures trailed closely behind him.

—"How could it be like this..."-Jian Nuo gritted his teeth, his jaw tight.

This scene breached his psychological baseline.

All along, even though Jian Nuo possessed some inside knowledge regarding True Immortals, the deeply ingrained perceptions of the cultivation world led him to believe that in the absolute worst case, they were merely "petty, narrow-minded high-ranking cultivators." Consequently, even when certain "friends" colluded with Carefree Palace, he could stomach it for the sake of gathering intelligence.

Yet looking at Liu Xuenian in this light, he could not suppress a profound surge of revulsion.

Had he actually been [humoring and playing along with](# "Idiom: 虚与委蛇 (xūyǔwēiyí) — dealing with someone insincerely / pretending to play along") a monstrous thing like this?

—"Is this... truly a 'True Immortal'?"-Jian Nuo asked with a visage contorted in disgust.-"Not some demonic beast or fiend?"

—"No. Theoretically speaking, it should still be a True Immortal, possessing a sliver of divinity, which is why ordinary methods fail to detect it,"-Qu Qianxing said, furrowing his brows.

He turned toward Qingyang Qingyu:

—"Naturally, we must stop it. Furthermore, we must capture it—preferably alive."

—"Because Master wishes to study it,"-Qu Qianxing explained.

[The Untiring Immortal Venerable wants to study something like this? Is his mind in order?] Jian Nuo desperately wanted to ask.

—"Not just the Immortal Venerable, I want it too!"-Qingyang Qingyu said, her voice brimming with fanatical scientific fervor.-"A living True Immortal! With it in hand, I can finally verify what kind of lineage connection my cultivation technique shares with True Immortals!"

—"...Are you certain? Aren't you afraid of turning into something like that yourself?"-Jian Nuo couldn't help questioning.

Qingyang Qingyu remained utterly unshakeable:

—"Of course I'm afraid! But precisely because I'm afraid, I must uncover the root cause! Otherwise, if I continue cultivating blindly without understanding, what happens when disaster strikes?"-she retorted.

Jian Nuo stared dumbly at Qingyang Qingyu's resolute expression.

Only when Qingyang Qingyu cast a cold, detached glance his way did he snap to his senses: *Wait a moment? Why does it feel like Qingyu is looking at me like an idiot?*

In Qingyu's eyes, the crime of stupidity appeared far more heinous than moral failing!—even though Jian Nuo had never done anything genuinely immoral to begin with.

Jian Nuo finally recognized his impending romantic catastrophe.

Unfortunately, it was a bit too late. Qingyang Qingyu paid him no further mind, turning back to Qu Qianxing:

—"Capturing him won't be easy,"-Qingyang Qingyu said.-"This 'test subject' is exceptionally cunning. He doesn't appear inclined to face us head-on at all; rather, it feels like he is probing our defensive strength."

Just as Qingyang Qingyu observed, the gore-drenched "Xiao Geng" had led the swarm of demons to the precipice of the Demon Realm rift. He had halted his advance, instead egging on the demonic creatures to launch a second assault against the forward outpost while concealing himself in the rear, observing from the shadows.

—"It seems he harbors no intention of stepping into the Demon Realm, and is poised to flee at any moment,"-Qingyang Qingyu frowned.

—"Why remain fixated on this 'puppet'?"-Jian Nuo suggested.-"Shouldn't we subdue 'Liu Xuenian' first? That side is obviously the true body."

Speaking of this, Jian Nuo felt another surge of nausea, tangled with an inexplicable weight of guilt.

He did not know whether the "Liu Xuenian" he had originally known was ever human, or had been preserved as a biological specimen from the very beginning. If the real Liu Xuenian had still been alive when they first crossed paths, then turning a blind eye to Carefree Palace's temptations merely to gather intelligence made him little better than an accomplice!

—"Only by capturing this 'puppet' can we apprehend the true body, because the bulk of its consciousness is currently tethered to the 'puppet',"-Qu Qianxing explained.-"If anything, right now is our golden opportunity. If its consciousness were still seated in the true vessel, we would never be able to capture him... capture *them*! At that point, 'he' is actually *them*, capable of severing the link and vanishing at a moment's notice."

—"...You seem remarkably familiar with him?"-Jian Nuo blinked, asking suspiciously.

Relying solely on the Demon-Revealing Mirror could expose an entity's true form, but it could never yield such granular operational intelligence.

—"Ah, if my eyes do not deceive me, he and I... share a bit of history,"-Qu Qianxing smiled brilliantly, yet arcs of golden lightning hissed and crackled along the hem of his robe.

It was precisely this "history" that had prompted Qu Qianxing to plead with Master: he wanted to settle the score with this entity with his own two hands, dissolving his own [inner demon](# "Literal CN: 心魔 (xīnmó) — mental barrier / psychological trauma hindering cultivation breakthrough").

Otherwise, had Zhou Hui intervened personally, there would have been no need for such convoluted maneuvering.

Both Jian Nuo and Qingyang Qingyu instinctively took a step back. Neither dared provoke Qu Qianxing in his current state.

Qu Qianxing paid no heed to the looks around him. He spoke calmly to Qingyang Qingyu:

—"Stage a feigned assault. Throw me out like a 'chess piece' and see if we can bait him over."

—"You're taking the field yourself?!"-Qingyang Qingyu's eyes widened in shock.

A troubled expression crossed her face:

—"But... he possesses realm suppression over you!"

After all, Qu Qianxing was still a Golden Core cultivator. No matter how peerless his prowess, he remained in the Golden Core realm.

Even if the Golden Thunder Qu Qianxing had refined could punch above its weight class to annihilate heretics and fiends, the opponent was no run-of-the-mill villain; he was a "True Immortal," theoretically falling outside Qu Qianxing's "jurisdiction of punishment."

Most crucially, this was Eternal Villa, not the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Without the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-protecting grand array, Qu Qianxing possessed zero outside reinforcement. Formidable as he was, he had not yet broken free from the fundamental common sense of the cultivation world.

—"*Heh*, that is precisely what I want,"-Qu Qianxing sneered.

If he had to rely on the power of a mountain-protecting grand array to slay the creature, how would that differ from running crying to Master for help?

Qingyang Qingyu looked at Qu Qianxing with evident disapproval.

In her eyes, ancestors and clan resources were fundamentally an integral component of one's overall power; there was zero sense in risking one's neck simply out of stubborn pride.

—"How about this? I shall accompany Brother Qianxing,"-Jian Nuo offered voluntarily.-"I am at least a Nascent Soul, of the same realm as the opponent, and can offset his realm suppression. Furthermore, I am also their 'friend.' If he suddenly spots Brother Qianxing, he might suspect an ambush; but with me present, the scene will appear far more 'authentic.'"

—"Who knows, perhaps they might even take the initiative to march right into our hands? *Hahaha*,"-Jian Nuo laughed, but the gaze he directed toward those two "friends" was devoid of any warmth.
