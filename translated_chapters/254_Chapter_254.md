# Chapter 254: Three Hundred Years Later (Part 5)

The teleportation array of the Heavenly Gate was situated deep within the interior of White Jade Capital Academy's Heavenly Gate Branch.

It was also the original site of the divine altar belonging to the deity who had once guarded the Heavenly Gate.

Because an academy was to be built here alongside a teleportation array, the place had naturally undergone an initial scouring. Not only was there not a shred of evidence remaining from the god-slaying, but even the floor was so immaculate it looked as if someone had meticulously licked every square inch with their tongue—Zhou Hui harbored a strong suspicion that a certain glutton, unwilling to let spilled divine power go to waste, truly had licked it all clean—leaving every surface gleaming to a near-mirror shine.

Within this spotless hall stood twelve white marble pillars whose tops vanished into the heights above, their perimeters encircled by luminescent white runes.

These runes coalesced into a colossal, multi-layered grand formation, appearing both sacred and enigmatic. It harmonized so seamlessly with the atmosphere of the Immortal Realm that it looked as if it had been an integral part of the Supreme Heavenly Court from the very beginning.

At the very least, Renran could find no "incriminating evidence" whatsoever.

Even having grown up in the Immortal Realm from childhood, she could not help but feel that the majestic, pristine purity of the celestial spiritual energy circulating within this array was something that could only exist in the Heavenly Court—surpassing what ordinary celestial officials could ever achieve.

Yet why was the Untiring Immortal Venerable able to requisition the Heavenly Court's territory and utilize the Heavenly Court's "facilities" with such brazen openness, acting just as casually at home as if he were in the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Had not a single immortal noticed this place and stepped in to manage it?

Ah! It wasn't that there was no one!

What in the world was the deal with those few celestial officials?!

The doubts in Renran's heart deepened. When she spotted several figures who were conspicuously celestial officials standing right here, this perplexed terror spiked to an absolute peak—

[Could these celestial officials have colluded with Untiring in rebellion?!]

Renran thought in panicked dread, believing she had stumbled headfirst into a monumental treason case against the Heavenly Court.

Yet Renran was still far too naive; reality was vastly more atrocious than she imagined.

Even Goddess Renran, who was already scrutinizing Zhou Hui with the utmost malice, could never have conceived that during Untiring's very first ascent to heaven, he had not merely slaughtered the gatekeeper deity and raided the administrative bureau, but had even let his eldest disciple devour the god's corpse, occupied the Heavenly Gate in broad daylight, peddled Heavenly Court civil service exam prep courses to ascenders, and hoodwinked neighboring celestial officials into joining in "self-service franchise" partnerships.

Indeed, these celestial officials were here to "franchise."

And the franchised enterprise was none other than the teleportation array before them.

—"Master, the advertising campaign has been highly effective,"-stepping inside the building, Yao'er reported obediently within Zhou Hui's mind.-"Within eighteen celestial days, the officials in charge of thirty nearby Heavenly Gates have caught wind of the large-scale teleportation array. Eighty percent of them have shown tremendous interest. Even among those who haven't, ascenders from the immortal sects of their subordinate worlds are exhibiting a wait-and-see disposition."

—"Has no one grown suspicious?"-Zhou Hui asked with an eccentric expression.

In truth, he, like Renran, was somewhat surprised by the Heavenly Court's complete lack of reaction.

Granted, they had even detained the Celestial Emperor, but occupying someone's Heavenly Gate in broad daylight to run a cram school was blatantly audacious. Once his disciples finished constructing the teleportation array, Zhou Hui had been fully prepared for all-out war with the Heavenly Court. Yet eighteen days had already elapsed in heaven without the slightest peep, leaving one to wonder whether they were holding back [to cook up a major storm](# "Literal CN: 憋个大的 (biē ge dà de) — holding in something big / preparing a massive move").

—"You overestimate their administrative efficiency,"-Yao'er stated.

Forget the other disciples; even this newly inducted youngest disciple held zero faith in the Heavenly Court's administrative speed—or rather, as the very architect of the Heavenly Court and the entity that forged this world, it understood the Heavenly Court best and was the most qualified to look down on it.

—"Even under normal circumstances, it would take them hundreds or thousands of celestial days to notice an anomaly at a single minor Heavenly Gate. What is more, they are currently in complete disarray due to the Turmoil of Xuanpu. According to my senior brothers' calculations, our probability of [crossing the sea in disguise](# "Literal CN: 瞒天过海 (mán tiān guò hǎi) — 36 Stratagems: deceiving the heavens to cross the sea; pulling off a monumental deception right under their noses") is one hundred percent. Managed properly, even seating Second Senior Brother upon the throne of the Celestial Emperor would pose no great difficulty."

—"...That is the very last development I wish to see,"-Zhou Hui murmured.

Listening to his disciples scheme how to lay out their pieces across the Heavenly Court and use Heavenly Gates as transit hubs to rope in other worlds—no, perhaps even earlier—Zhou Hui had felt that this band of disciples didn't look like they were promoting educational reform at all, but rather plotting some earth-shattering conspiracy to usurp the Heavenly Court. Judging by the results, that was indeed the case, and he, their revered master, was the mastermind pulling the strings from the shadows.

Yet rather than acting as the shadowy mastermind, what bothered Zhou Hui even more was that such an overt conspiracy had encountered zero resistance—not even discovery. This genuinely violated his worldview.

Zhou Hui was loath to admit that from a certain angle in his heart, he actually wished the Heavenly Court were a bit more "normal."

After all, this miserable place had once been the goal of his cultivation for so many long years.

—"Does Master desire some added turbulence?"-Yao'er inquired keenly.

Yet perhaps because its archetype was, in the end, a machine, Yao'er never rejected any of Zhou Hui's notions. As a consequence, despite being an AI, this youngest disciple had been unanimously voted by the other direct disciples as the "most sycophantic disciple toward Master."

Its suggestion at this moment was no different:

—"In that case, during the promotional campaigns in the Immortal Realm, or while the celestial officials are touring the teleportation array, I can select suitable candidates and plant flaws—for instance, one among those few over there is quite promising—provoking negative emotions such as doubt and greed within them so they commence an investigation into us. I recommend placing the investigative vulnerability during Senior Brother Mo Xi's shift on defense; he is currently probing intelligence on the Heavenly Court, making it easy to orchestrate an opening. If Master wishes to test the other senior brothers and sisters, that can be arranged as well..."

—"There is really no need to go that far,"-Zhou Hui said with a twitch of his lips.

[To the uninformed, one might think this AI harbored personal vendettas against its senior brothers and sisters!]

Zhou Hui reflexively glanced at the celestial officials near the teleportation array, smiling wryly:

—"As long as everything proceeds smoothly... smoothly is good enough!"

Little did those few celestial officials know that in the span of the past few seconds, they had nearly been granted the chance to become the tragic protagonists who unmasked a colossal conspiracy to usurp the Heavenly Court, only to lose that opportunity a few seconds later.

At this moment, these celestial officials could only remain "a cog in the smooth execution of the conspiracy." Completely oblivious, they continued their ignorant and enthusiastic discussion regarding the teleportation array before them:

[—"At last, are the higher-ups preparing to construct teleportation arrays leading down to the lower realms?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"It should have been built long ago. Every descent to the mortal realm requires mountains of red tape, and the temporal flow below is so completely different. By the time you deal with an urgent matter, people down there have already had their great-great-grandsons."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"It can't be helped. Anything touching space-time spellcraft is notoriously difficult. In thousands of years, only a handful possess the talent, and even fewer actually master it. Unless it involves the very survival of the Heavenly Court, they can't even be mobilized, let alone tasked with constructing stable spatio-temporal teleportation formations."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"So why was it suddenly completed out of nowhere?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"It's not considered fully completed. Word has it it's still in the experimental phase. Currently, it can only transmit to a single world—and supposedly that world was originally severed and cast down from the Immortal Realm."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Ah, so the rumors are false? It can't be established in other worlds?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Now you're speaking like rank amateurs. From an array design perspective, a realm that was once part of the Immortal Realm is vastly more difficult! Think about it: the Immortal Realm runs on celestial power; after falling, its energy tier degraded to spiritual power, making the energetic currents utterly chaotic. Add to that the spatial warping endured during the fall—ordinarily, the spatio-temporal conditions of such a place are the most turbulent conceivable. If one can construct a teleportation array there that coordinates axis-to-axis with the Heavenly Realm, it's safe to say it would encounter virtually no issues in any space-time whatsoever."](# "Speaker: Technical Celestial Official")

[—"By that logic, all Three Thousand Worlds would pose no problem at all?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"For the vast majority, no problem—at least judging by the array layout. Look at the arrangement of these runes, observe the architecture of this formation! Tsk-tsk, even the Heavenly Court's Guardian Grand Array is barely on par. This old man has wasted three thousand celestial years, fancying myself somewhat knowledgeable, yet today my eyes have truly been opened. It is the very first time I have witnessed an array of such exquisite, sublime perfection... (three thousand words of effusive praise omitted hereafter)."](# "Speaker: Technical Celestial Official")

Watching their colleague—the technical official well-versed in arraycraft—suddenly slip into a state of hysterical frenzy, the other celestial officials silently stepped back a few paces.

Yet technical nerds of this sort were a minority. That the other celestial officials had been drawn here by rumors (advertisements) meant they naturally had their own calculations.

In truth, as long as one wasn't detached from worldly realities like Renran, anyone who had shuttled between the Heavenly Court and the lower realms a few times knew what a terrifying and indispensable existence a time-synchronized teleportation array truly was—not to mention the monumental commercial profits brought by reciprocal traffic between two worlds.

—Even without a teleportation array, such profits were colossal; it was merely that the Heavenly Court had forbidden it in the past.

Was opening the teleportation array now a signal that "the higher-ups" were loosening their grip?

The celestial officials pondered.

No one suspected for a moment that this teleportation array had been privately erected—what a joke! Could an array of such transcendent caliber ever be developed by the lower realms? It was unquestionably the proprietary asset of the Heavenly Court!

Those who caught the news and rushed here at the first opportunity were naturally well-informed immortals with plenty of private schemes. Shaking off their array-obsessed colleague, they dispersed their divine senses and pulled together a private group chat to continue whispering:

[—"...Indeed, having this teleportation array makes things vastly more convenient. It's even more 'expedient' than the Immortal-Execution Platform, and you don't have to shed a layer of skin."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Far more than that! If it truly achieves 'temporal synchronization' between the Immortal Realm and the lower realms, it means with precise calibration, this array could even rewind time and send you to any 'past temporal point' desired in a world, wouldn't it? After all, if calibrated to the present moment, descending tomorrow means landing in the lower realm a century ago. And as celestial time flows, this temporal delta will widen further—a month from now, teleporting would land us in the 'lower realm three thousand years ago.'"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Ah."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"...Is it really safe to roll out an array like this so casually?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"How is it casual? Don't you have to submit an application?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"That's exactly what I'm talking about! Such a formidable spatio-temporal formation doesn't require filing with the Heavenly Court, but can be processed directly at this Heavenly Gate's branch office. Procedurally speaking, isn't that improper...?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Drop the act. You've already come here fishing for intel—could you really not know? This is precisely the kind of matter where following procedure won't work. Eight out of ten, this array is tied to the factional power struggles among the big shots above. Who knows how many worlds it'll even be permitted to serve."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Power struggles among big shots? You mean the 'Turmoil of Xuanpu'? Is it true that 'the one at the top' is gone?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Worse than that. That day in Xuanpu, a Taotie supposedly ran amok, and even the Celestial Emperor and Heavenly Generals were..."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

Reaching this point, the celestial officials seemed fearful of eavesdroppers; not only did their voices drop to a hush, but they thickened their divine sense barrier by several layers, ensuring not even a speck of dust could drift through.

Alas, such defenses were utterly futile.

Setting aside cheat-like existences of Zhou Hui's caliber, solely in terms of the spatio-temporal interference mechanisms of the teleportation array, the disciples maintaining the formation possessed administrative clearances sufficient to effortlessly suppress a Heavenly-General-level rooster—without such capabilities, Zhou Hui would never have rashly requisitioned the Heavenly Gate as a transit hub—let alone the divine senses of a few petty celestial officials.

Naturally, provided they harbored no malice, Zhou Hui would not take the initiative to intervene.

The one itching to make a move was someone else entirely.

[What are these guys muttering about? First immortal banquets, now the Celestial Emperor... sounds pretty serious?] Long Tianming pondered inwardly.

Indeed, Long Tianming could also hear their conversation.

Yet this had nothing to do with Zhou Hui; it stemmed from the inherent properties of the "diagnostic program." Because certain of its clearance privileges even exceeded the Genesis Machine's administrative permissions, if utilized properly, this "program" could bypass interference from any barrier whatsoever to infiltrate any domain and conduct "diagnostics."

It was precisely to exploit this property to bypass the Genesis Machine that the Heavenly Court designated them "Children of Destiny," steering their execution paths (destinies) so as to plunder forbidden domains that were otherwise inaccessible.

In truth, the "Children of Destiny" encountered by the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the past had served as such guiding beacon signals. They rehearsed the exact same script; even if they failed, it mattered not, because their repetitive behavior would inevitably guide the true Child of Destiny (diagnostic program) to investigate. In this process, the diagnostic program (Long Tianming) would retrace the script's path, tearing open the breach the Heavenly Court desired.

Only this time, the scheme had collapsed in absolute failure.

Yet just as the Heavenly Court continued its sluggish, grinding operations, the consequences wrought by this strategy had not vanished. Long Tianming had still been drawn here, bearing unmistakable signs of "corruption."

[In any case, anything involving the 'Celestial Emperor' must be a major event, right? Could it be the late-game main storyline?] Long Tianming continued applying his rote web novel formulas to every experience he encountered. Yet in truth, he had grown somewhat uncertain; having to take civil service exams after transmigrating had dealt a devastating blow to his confidence. [Hearing NPCs discuss this means I'm on the right track, right? Old Man?]

He reflexively inquired of the "Old Grandpa."

Long Tianming still received no response.

This did not mean the "Old Grandpa" had truly vanished. Although there was no verbal answer, following Long Tianming's inquiry, a surge of dark demonic qi welled up within his core, counteracting his unease and bolstering his resolve.

—"Of course I'm right,"-he told himself.-"Haven't the 'quest rewards' already been paid out?"

As he thought this, he cast a glance at Goddess Renran.

No matter how one looked at it, a peerless beauty was actually willing to give up the opportunity to cultivate at the Heavenly Gate Academy (suspected to be a collegiate gifted youth program?) just for him, following him all the way to the frontlines—if that wasn't a "quest reward," what was?

Had Renran known what was going through Long Tianming's head at this moment, she would have fainted dead away in sheer fury:

[She had clearly followed along only to prevent Long Tianming from being exploited by Untiring—how on earth had that turned into a 'quest reward'?!]

Fortunately (?), Renran was utterly oblivious to Long Tianming's internal delusions.

Noticing Long Tianming looking her way, she forced a stiff, awkward smile, worried that being overly proactive might arouse his suspicion.

Seeing that smile, Long Tianming felt all the more validated in his conviction, even fancying that he had inadvertently unlocked Renran's romance route and affection meter.

Thus, confronted with Renran's frozen smile, Long Tianming tugged at his cheeks, beaming back with an expression he fancied dashing and charming, but which in truth looked thoroughly greasy and sleazy.

Truth be told, the scene was remarkably reminiscent of a cheesy [historical romance idol drama](# "Literal CN: 古偶 (gǔ'ǒu) — ancient costume idol drama; cheesy historical romance series criticized for bad acting, plastic tropes, and heavy filters").

Between the poker-faced female lead and the greasy male lead, it was an eye-searing disaster to behold. Even the script was a shoddy, rushed trainwreck crammed with ideological fluff, where the screenwriter had fled (died) due to force majeure, leaving a plot that satisfied no one except the lead actors themselves.

Fortunately, this cursed production—where the boss (Celestial Emperor) had vanished and the investors (Heavenly Court) had pulled their funding—had zero chance of continuing.

The strained, discordant staring contest between the (pseudo) male and female leads did not last long. The runes upon the white marble pillars suddenly accelerated their rotation, and from their midst echoed a familiar melody and public address broadcast:

> *"Ding-dong—*
>
> *"Ladies, gentlemen, and traveling friends, greetings:*
>
> *"Ticket verification has commenced for Teleportation Array Jia-1111, traveling from Southern Heavenly Gate·Langhuan Station to Langhuan·Ancient Abyss Sea.*
>
> *"Passengers holding tickets for Teleportation Array Jia-1111, please proceed to Red Ticket Gate 01 for ticket verification and teleportation.*
>
> *"The teleportation array is located at Array Platform No. 2. Please verify the teleportation array serial number printed on your ticket, follow the directions on the platform guideboards, and proceed to the corresponding location to await the teleportation array.*
>
> *"For your safety and the safety of others, please walk within the [white safety line](# "Literal CN: 站台白色安全线 — the standard pedestrian safety boundary line on modern Chinese high-speed railway platforms") on the platform, take good care of the elderly and children in your company, and keep watch over your personal luggage."*

Long Tianming jolted at the familiar chime, instantly yanked from his historical idol fantasy back into "reality."

[Here it came again! That godforsaken immersion break!]

From the moment he stepped out of his village—academic encouragement, placement exams, civil service cram schools, and now this teleportation array chime—whenever Long Tianming tried to immerse himself in the standard male-power-fantasy web novel script, it smacked him right in the face, as though some terror buried deep in his subconscious was violently dragging him awake.

[Wait a second... could this really be a dream?]

[Could it be that he hadn't transmigrated at all? That he had merely read too many web novels and was dreaming?]

A shudder rippled through Long Tianming's heart.

Ever since transmigrating, Long Tianming had fancied himself the "male protagonist." Now, for the very first time, he began to doubt his own judgment—testament to the profound psychological trauma that "taking civil service exams" had inflicted upon him.

Even the demonic qi in the depths of his core dissipated by a few fractions.

Unfortunately, a mere break in immersion was not enough to make Long Tianming alter his course. Subjectively, Long Tianming had no desire to "wake up."

Up to this point, Long Tianming still felt this was a wonderful dream:

Look at this default female lead—didn't she smile just like his favorite idol?! Look at these runes—how mystical, how celestial! Look at this teleportation array—even more magnificent than in video games! Look at the beauty presiding over the teleportation...

A beauty?!

Long Tianming reflexively held his breath, eyes widening to dinner plates.

Even having transmigrated from an information-saturated modern world, Long Tianming was left dumbstruck.

[So beautiful!]

[Truly an astonishingly, overwhelmingly gorgeous beauty!]

Long Tianming marveled inwardly with his barren vocabulary.

Indeed, Long Tianming possessed quite a talent for mathematics, but his linguistic aptitude was inversely atrocious, whether in his native tongue or foreign languages. That was why he dreaded civil service examinations. Yet compared to civil service exams, what truly traumatized him were other forms of test-oriented schooling—which explained why, as a (self-proclaimed) top student who had nearly clawed his way into Professor Zhou's [research group](# "Literal CN: 门下 (ménxià) — academic lineage / research laboratory under a professor"), he possessed none of the psychological composure an honor student ought to have.

Long Tianming himself naturally didn't think so. He believed his literary flair was vastly superior to the web novel authors he read; doing poorly in language and foreign literature was merely because the exam questions were too rigid and ill-suited to him.

Today was also the very first time Long Tianming felt his prose lacking. Confronted with a beauty of such magnitude, he couldn't scrounge up a single proper adjective, leaving him standing there in slack-jawed, gormless stupor.

Yet this paralysis was not entirely born of linguistic poverty; in truth, it was predominantly fear.

The instant Long Tianming's gaze was hooked by the beauty, a bone-chilling dread began spreading through his viscera, causing his hackles to rise and his calves to tremble. It was merely because Long Tianming had grown up in a safe modern country and had zero understanding of what physiological reactions accompanied sheer, primal terror that he mistook the near-cessation of his heartbeat for being smitten by the beauty's looks.

Without question, if Long Tianming were a mouse, he would be the exact kind of idiot who went to propose marriage to a cat.

His behavior drove the entity experiencing genuine terror past its breaking point.

If someone were to thoroughly soul-search Long Tianming with spiritual power at this moment, they would discover that the instant Long Tianming laid eyes on the "beauty," the demonic qi clinging to and striving to permeate the depths of his divine soul had shriveled to the size of a sesame seed in a desperate bid to minimize its presence. Yet upon realizing that Long Tianming had actually mistaken mortal terror for lovesickness and was standing frozen in place, it was forced to let out a hushed, frantic snarl:

—"Run! Run now!"

It was the voice of the "Old Grandpa," who hadn't uttered a sound since they arrived at the Heavenly Gate.

At this moment, Long Tianming didn't even have the presence of mind to ask why the other had ignored him earlier only to speak up now, merely muttering in a daze:

—"But, the beauty..."

—"Beauty my ass! You..."—the "Old Grandpa" wanted to curse him out, but fearing that speaking too much would draw that ancestor's gaze, it could only strive ignominiously to extinguish Long Tianming's interest:—"He's a man!"

—"A man?"-Long Tianming's brain, having abandoned all higher cognition, whirred back to life for a fleeting instant.

He scrutinized the beauty once more and realized it was actually quite obvious: the figure was indeed a man. Yet before such devastating, impactful beauty, anyone would likely disregard gender entirely.

As Long Tianming's gaze drifted back to the figure's face, he abandoned higher thought once more:

—"What's wrong with a man?"

—"You can do men too?!"-the "Old Grandpa" nearly raised its voice.

Back when Long Tianming took a fancy to Renran, it had already felt this host's taste was abysmal. Seeing Long Tianming entertain designs on that living ancestor now, it nearly wanted to sever their connection on the spot.

Even if it were a man, he'd be better off eyeing that silver-haired youth who had accompanied them all along. Though that youth's background looked equally shady, he at least appeared approachable and reasonable.

Fortunately, Long Tianming didn't give the "Old Grandpa" the chance to voice this treasonous thought that would have gotten it implicated in [collective guilt punishment](# "Literal CN: 连坐 (liánzuò) — collective punishment / guilt by association where entire clans or associates are executed for one person's crime").

—"Who said I'm interested in men?"-Long Tianming reflexively retorted.-"Is your mind filled with nothing but romance? Even I know that a beauty of that caliber is far beyond anything I could ever dare dream of touching!"

[—"Then what are you standing there like an idiot for?!"](# "Speaker: Old Grandpa")

—"Can't I sneak a few extra looks? Not looking would be such a loss!"-Long Tianming argued. Just like back then when a senior brother from Professor Zhou's research group served as their substitute [teaching assistant](# "Literal CN: 助教 (zhùjiào) — academic TA")—if you didn't chat him up, how could you ever hope for future opportunities? Even though he hadn't dared seize that opportunity back then, even if not for opportunities, it was well worth it just to have bragging rights later!

Perceiving Long Tianming's rationale, the "Old Grandpa" was nearly laughed to tears by sheer exasperation.

It glanced at this disobedient host, then at that terrifying "ancestor" who made its blood run cold. And at that exact moment, the teleportation array truly ignited with brilliance.

No longer caring about Long Tianming's feelings, the "Old Grandpa" mustered all its strength to seize control of the host's limbs.

—"My body... why is it moving on its own...!!!"

Long Tianming couldn't process it in time. Before he could even ask, he felt his body charge headlong into the teleportation array of its own accord. He couldn't even voice a protest, only registering a thought that decidedly did not belong to him:

[Must!]

[It must absolutely leave this godforsaken Immortal Realm before Qu Qianxing discovers it!]

[Wait a damn minute! Who the hell is Qu Qianxing?!]

Long Tianming thought in utter bafflement.

***

After activating the teleportation array, the beauty—Qu Qianxing—did not escort (surveil) the travelers entering the formation as was his daily routine, merely transmitting respectfully via divine sense:

—"Master, you should have informed me of your arrival; I could have made preparations to receive you."

—"No need. I merely wished to observe everyday operations,"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"My meaning was, I could accompany you..."

—"Also unnecessary,"-Zhou Hui cut in.-"I am currently observing the Child of Destiny. Tend to your own duties; there is no need to mind me."

[...How could my duties ever compare to your importance! And what do those Children of Destiny even count for?!]

Qu Qianxing clicked his tongue through exquisite lips. He felt that because of his junior brothers and sisters, he hadn't discussed academic coursework with Master in ages. Yet today, having finally managed to meet, he was denied the chance once more.

Qu Qianxing let out a disgruntled murmur:

—"A Child of Destiny? That one just now?"

He finally lowered his gaze toward Long Tianming's direction, catching only the rear view of the youth charging frantically into the teleportation array like [a mortal in desperate search of a latrine](# "Literal CN: 凡人三急 (fánrén sān jí) — the three urgent mortal bodily needs, referring to needing an urgent bathroom break").

Yet that single glance was enough for Qu Qianxing to see through the other's origins.

—"That is the 'true Child of Destiny'? That thing inside his body..."

—"A demon seed, presumably?"-Zhou Hui remarked, having naturally known of the 'Old Grandpa's' existence all along.-"One so pure is exceptionally rare. Furthermore, it can even interfere with the diagnostic program. I am somewhat curious about its interference mechanism—it seems external radio waves possess no immunity against demonic qi either, resembling a sort of virus."

—"Uh..."

Seeing Master's keenly fascinated expression, Qu Qianxing hesitated for a moment.

Yao'er caught it in an instant:

—"Master, I believe Second Senior Brother truly has something else on his mind this time. He seems to be acquainted with that virus seed."

[This little junior brother... he truly understood why the other martial brothers and sisters despised it so much! Even he and his original form felt like smashing this pile of scrap metal to bits!]

Qu Qianxing forced an awkward arch of his lips:

—"I indeed know it. It was my superior back when I served as the Demon Sovereign—known to the world as..."

—"...the Demon Emperor."
