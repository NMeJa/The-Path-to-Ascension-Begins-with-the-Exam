# Chapter 121: Marketing Strategy (第121章 营销手段)

There was, of course, very good reason for Carefree Palace to be in such an anxious frenzy.

If one were to name the sole victim of this grand debate sweeping across the cultivation realm, it would undoubtedly be Carefree Palace.

Naturally, Carefree Palace was not in the business of academic research. In theory, an academic flame war had absolutely nothing to do with them. At the outset, Carefree Palace had even maintained the detached mindset of [spectators watching a circus](<# "Literal CN: 看戏的心态 (kànxì de xīntài) — mindset of watching a theater show / idle spectator enjoying the drama">), even indulging in petty underhanded tricks to covertly fan the flames and direct the heat toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

It was just that very soon, they could no longer laugh.

Because the explosion of the academic debate sparked massive sales of both *White Jade Capital Weekly* and the journal *Spells*.

Among them, because the barrier to entry for *Spells* was so steep, its circulation gradually tapered off after the initial wave—though essentially any cultivator at or above the Void Refinement Stage found ways to secure a long-term subscription, to the point that even Carefree Palace had secretly tapped third-party channels to subscribe to several copies.

*White Jade Capital Weekly*, on the other hand, was [suitable for all ages](<# "Idiom: 老少咸宜 (lǎoshào-xiányí) — appealing to young and old alike; universally accessible">) and widely beloved. Virtually every cultivator under heaven wanted their hands on a copy, and even certain mortal clans were swept up in the craze, clamoring for annual subscriptions.

As a result, *White Jade Capital Weekly* repeatedly expanded its print run, yet demand still vastly outstripped supply.

If that had been the extent of it, it wouldn't have been a crisis.

Along with the runaway success of *White Jade Capital*, another monumental function of the newspaper medium surfaced:

Advertising!

Sometimes, it wasn't even a deliberate advertisement. A cultivator in the midst of an academic duel might merely ridicule his opponent's brain as resembling a whipped cream cake dropped onto the floor—"mushy, messy, and sickeningly sweet"—and instantly a horde of readers would grow intensely curious:

[—"What on earth is a whipped cream cake?"](<# "Speaker: Curious Reader">)

[—"Milk and eggs combined? Sounds like some kind of delicacy?"](<# "Speaker: Curious Reader">)

[—"I love sweet pastries!"](<# "Speaker: Curious Reader">)

And subsequently, everyone would go into a buying frenzy.

In the past, whenever such shopping crazes struck, the entity that reaped the profits was invariably Carefree Palace.

Carefree Palace boasted the sharpest intelligence network, the most premium procurement channels, and trade routes crisscrossing every corner of the cultivation realm. By all rights, how could those spirit stones belong to anyone other than them?

Yet this time was an exception.

Since *White Jade Capital Weekly* was published by the Heavenly Spirit Sect, whether intentional or accidental, the products mentioned in its pages were invariably the Heavenly Spirit Sect's own "specialties." For instance, that aforementioned cream cake had been conceived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Immortal Bazaar, and only they possessed massive stockpiles of inventory.

It was not entirely impossible for Carefree Palace to get their hands on some, but compared to their usual lines of business, acquiring stock was far more troublesome.

Under normal circumstances, this would hardly stump Carefree Palace.

What they lacked, they could simply "replicate" on the spot! Their bootlegs were guaranteed to be cheaper, more convenient, mass-produced in larger batches, and virtually identical to the original.

In a cultivation world where commerce was underdeveloped, this was practically a [dimensional strike](<# "Literal CN: 降维打击 (jiàngwéi dǎjī) — dimensional strike / dimensional reduction attack; sci-fi/business slang referring to overwhelming unfair competition crushing primitive incumbents">).

In the past, Carefree Palace had relied on precisely this strategy to snuff out countless small craft workshops, securing a de facto monopoly.

Unfortunately, when up against the Heavenly Spirit Sect, many of Carefree Palace's usual tricks proved utterly useless.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's goods were manufactured through standardized production. Even independent merchant vendors operated under stringent quality regulations. As a result, even when produced across multi-person assembly lines, their output was vastly more consistent, uniform, and standardized than "a batch of pills refined by a single alchemist"—making them exceptionally difficult to counterfeit.

Most critical of all, the demographic makeup of the clientele was completely different from before.

This academic debate was top-down: high-tier cultivators followed it first, whose interest subsequently trickled down to influence their disciples, sect members, and descendants.

When it came to the supreme elders standing at the apex of the cultivation world, they cared immensely about whether the items they used were genuine articles—especially anything associated with the Untiring Immortal Venerable. In their eyes, any commodity not produced by the Heavenly Spirit Sect would fail to achieve the desired effect. Furthermore, they possessed ample channels of their own to procure the genuine articles.

In fact, many felt that while buying similar goods made by other third parties was acceptable, using goods manufactured, shipped, or sold by Carefree Palace was strictly out of the question!

After all, who didn't know about the bloody history between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Carefree Palace?!

Consequently, the beneficiaries of this [proxy-shopping craze](<# "Literal CN: 代购 (dàigòu) — surrogate shopping / purchasing agent craze">) were the Heavenly Dao Alliance and another prominent Eastern Domain sect, the Feathered Guest Pavilion—Yuke Xuan. That sect originally did not dabble in commerce, but they maintained branch compounds in the Central Domain. Sourcing and delivering goods was extraordinarily convenient for them—at the very least, far easier than for Carefree Palace, who couldn't purchase directly from the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

As a result, even when ill-informed clients approached Carefree Palace seeking procurement, they discovered Carefree Palace's restocking speed was slower than the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Yuke Xuan! Some deliveries even turned out to be counterfeit!

[—"They can't even get their hands on this? Has Carefree Palace lost its touch?!"](<# "Speaker: Disgruntled Customer">)

Such whispers inevitably spread like wildfire.

For Carefree Palace—which was, in reality, a commercial conglomerate—such a collapse in public reputation was something to be avoided at all costs. Naturally, they were as frantic as [ants on a hot griddle](<# "Idiom: 热锅上的蚂蚁 (règuō shang de mǎyǐ) — ants on a hot griddle; in a state of feverish panic">). They adopted various counter-measures, none of which proved particularly effective.

For example, Carefree Palace jumped on the bandwagon and launched their own newspaper, grandiosely titled *Cultivation World Weekly*, and hired several cultivators to write articles in an attempt to replicate the success of *White Jade Capital Weekly*.

Unfortunately, *White Jade Capital Weekly* was the true main battlefield. The debate was inextricably linked to the journal *Spells*, and the combatants were all renowned titans of the cultivation realm. Who would bother paying attention to a petty academic debate hosted on a tabloid with an uninspired name?

Furthermore, even without the academic debate, sects across the world far preferred subscribing to *White Jade Capital Daily*.

After all, this was established by the Heavenly Spirit Sect under the Tribulation Transcendence titan, the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Particularly when it came to content regarding cultivation research, merely looking at reports in its pages lent them an aura of absolute credibility. On top of that, the name "White Jade Capital" was steeped in elegance, evoking imagery of the ancient cultivation realm—prosperity, romance, and grandeur—which resonated perfectly with the Immortal Venerable's stature (not to imply that the Untiring Immortal Venerable was ancient, of course).

As for *Cultivation World Weekly*? For starters, its title was confined to the "Cultivation World," which carried a distinct undertone of never achieving ascension. Moreover, once people heard it was founded by Carefree Palace, it wasn't that they doubted Carefree Palace's academic caliber—after all, they did have three... well, two Tribulation Transcendence elders!—but given Carefree Palace's customary conduct, the public naturally associated them with quick-fix pills, cultivation furnaces, and assorted shady, illicit cultivation shortcuts.

—Don't misunderstand; this did not mean *Cultivation World Weekly* sold poorly.

Its sales were sensational!

The very first issue sold out completely, surging with a momentum even fiercer than *White Jade Capital Daily* during the height of the Immortal Arts Assembly. The only catch was that readers of *Cultivation World Weekly* behaved with extreme secrecy: not only were they unwilling to purchase long-term subscriptions—preferring to buy single copies in cash—they dared not let friends or family catch them reading it, treating the paper like some sort of illicit contraband.

As for the scholarly essays printed inside, readers naturally showered them with scathing reviews:

[—"I didn't buy Carefree Palace's paper to read this rubbish! Stop printing such appetite-killing drivel!"](<# "Speaker: Disgruntled Reader">)

[—"Where are your cultivation furnaces? Where are the pills? List them out!"](<# "Speaker: Disgruntled Reader">)

[—"The front-page headline of Carefree Palace's newspaper must be a secret code hinting at the winning numbers for their underground lottery!"](<# "Speaker: Disgruntled Reader">)

Reaching this point, it was impossible for the publication's reputation not to be thoroughly in the gutter.

Carefree Palace found itself backed into a corner, forced to confront two choices:

Either cater to consumer demand by turning *Cultivation World Weekly* into a sleazy, illicit tabloid crammed with classified ads and smut;

Or doggedly cling to the scholarly path, [put on lofty airs](<# "Literal CN: 装高大上 (zhuāng gāodàshàng) — putting on high-end, elegant, and classy airs">), and ultimately run themselves into an early grave.

Palace Master Li Hao of Carefree Palace still wanted to put up a fight. He fired the original editor-in-chief of *Cultivation World Weekly* and replaced him with a new one.

After getting a grasp of the current predicament, the new editor-in-chief had no concrete rectification proposals; instead, he skillfully executed a [masterclass in buck-passing](<# "Literal CN: 甩锅 (shuǎiguō) — shifting the blame / passing the buck">).

—"This is all Heavenly Spirit Sect's fault!"-the new editor-in-chief declared with righteous indignation.

It was as though the rancid reputation of *Cultivation World Weekly* had nothing whatsoever to do with Carefree Palace's past misdeeds.

—"It's because Heavenly Spirit Sect founded their newspaper first, snatching the entire cultivation realm's attention, that our paper was put in an unfavorable comparison the moment it launched!"-The editor-in-chief seemed to completely gloss over the fact that *Cultivation World Weekly* was an unvarnished copycat.-"Even worse, they refuse to publish our articles, effectively gagging us! They won't allow us to prove our academic authority in cultivation, which only deepens everyone's prejudice against us!"

Whether this editor-in-chief had any real competence remained to be seen, but his silver tongue was undeniable.

In his telling, Carefree Palace was practically transformed into a pitiful victim of vicious bullying.

And Li Hao bought it hook, line, and sinker.

—"Makes sense,"-Li Hao mused, rubbing his chin.-"As expected, we must seize back control of the public narrative... What do you think if we host a Grand Dao Conference?"

Hosting such "Dao Conferences" was something Carefree Palace had done countless times before.

Typically, they would invite a few highly respected senior cultivators to the Eastern Domain, pampering them with all-inclusive luxury entertainment. They'd have these high-tier figures discourse on scripture, throw in some manufactured oceanic miracles or theatrical displays of "taming sea demons," and lure hordes of low-tier cultivators on a pilgrimage. Several of the famed immortal peaks scattered across the Eastern Seas had been marketed into existence through precisely this routine; they were intimately familiar with the playbook.

Yet this time, the "editor-in-chief" wore a troubled expression:

—"In ordinary years, such an approach would certainly suffice. But currently, the entire cultivation world's attention is fixated exclusively on the outcome of this academic duel..."-the editor-in-chief began.

—"Why are you so rigid?!"-another high-ranking Carefree Palace steward interjected.-"Why not use this very debate as a pretext? We can invite cultivators from both sides... No, invite *only* the Northern Domain cultivators! We'll construct a dedicated Lecture Terrace specifically for them to broadcast their arguments to the world. Do you really think they wouldn't be overjoyed?!"

Hearing this, Li Hao's eyes lit up with excitement.

This scheme was delightfully wicked... brilliant!

Not only would it draw continent-wide attention, it would curry favor with Northern Domain cultivators, undermine the authority of *White Jade Capital*, and suppress those Central Domain cultivators loyal to the Untiring Immortal Venerable. It was killing three birds with one stone!

However, a tactless subordinate raised an objection:

—"Have you considered how many spirit stones this scheme would cost?"-a steward protested.

—"Since when has our Carefree Palace ever lacked spirit stones?!"-Li Hao retorted with lavish swagger.

—"B-but Palace Master, the caliber of guests we must invite this time is entirely unprecedented!"-the steward replied with a grimace.-"According to intelligence reports, the figures participating in this debate are the supreme leadership of major sects—figures like Mahayana cultivator Ruopei, or Tribulation Transcendence Elder Su Mei of the Heavenly Dao Alliance... or rather, his 'disciple'(?)! Every participant is at least at the Void Refinement Stage, with more than a dozen Mahayana titans among them! And that's merely the public tally, excluding the reclusive powerhouses ghostwriting behind their disciples' names! Entertaining such supreme titans is no simple matter. Under ordinary circumstances, pulling in any single one of them would warrant an entire grand preaching assembly of their own. Now we must invite them all..."

Even Carefree Palace wasn't a [gullible cash cow](<# "Literal CN: 冤大头 (yuāndàtóu) — a big sucker / gullible dupe who spends money recklessly">)!

Hearing these lamentations, the grand hall of Carefree Palace fell into momentary silence.

As shrewd merchants, they could naturally calculate the ledger.

Still, the previous steward grumbled unconvinced:

—"However, hasn't Heavenly Spirit Sect already gathered a crowd of high-tier cultivators? Why do they demand nothing from Heavenly Spirit Sect, yet would demand astronomical perks from us? Are our invitations somehow beneath theirs?"-the steward argued.

—"...No. It's because of *that man*,"-someone pointed out.

Indeed. That was the Heavenly Spirit Sect with the Untiring Immortal Venerable at its helm. An invitation from him was an immense honor in its own right; who on earth possessed the standing to nitpick or demand favors from the Heavenly Spirit Sect?

Besides, Heavenly Spirit Sect's hospitality was hardly lacking. In the eyes of worldly high-tier cultivators, the academic atmosphere there was infinitely more meaningful than Carefree Palace's gaudy, artificial stage built purely by throwing spirit stones around.

Some muddle-headed fools were still muttering under their breath:

[—"It's not as though we lack Tribulation Transcendence titans."](<# "Speaker: Muddle-Headed Steward">)

Indeed, they had three!

One had been slain;

One had been relentlessly hunted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable for years—his academic acumen unknown, but his fleeing prowess proven undisputed number one under heaven;

And the last one was currently a pile of minced meat, still nursing his wounds.

Just which of Carefree Palace's Tribulation titans did they propose using to summon the high-tier cultivators of the realm?

—"Perhaps we don't need our own Tribulation titans,"-someone ventured.-"Since they are Northern Domain cultivators, we could directly invite Northern Domain Tribulation titans—"

[—"You mean the Mei-Zhu couple?"](<# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Steward">)

[—"Those two won't do; they're close friends with *that man*! But the Northern Domain has four Tribulation Transcendence titans! The other two are a different story. They have no personal ties with *that man*, and one of them doesn't even think highly of him. We could lobby them to host the Dao Conference while we provide the funding..."](<# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Steward">)

—"Enough."-Li Hao cut in, pressing his palm down to terminate the squabbling.

—"That proposal has some merit, but the cost is far too astronomical. Keep it as a backup option!"-Li Hao declared. He turned a stern glare upon the new editor-in-chief of *Cultivation World Weekly*:-"What about your thoughts?! Surely you aren't relying entirely on your colleagues' ideas?"

[There's no escaping this after all!]

The editor-in-chief offered a bitter smile, lowering his head:

—"My thought is that we still need an article published in *White*... no, published in *Spells*, to establish our authority in the cultivation realm and drive attention to our newspaper."-the editor-in-chief said.

—"Yet we have exhausted every conceivable method! Forget *Spells*, even *White Jade Capital* refuses to print our submissions! Whether by pulling strings and showering spirit stones, hiring ghostwriters, or submitting under unrelated third-party names, every single attempt was rejected—surely you aren't ignorant of even that?"-Li Hao snapped.

—"Indeed. Heavenly Spirit Sect harbors a grudge against us, so naturally they have blacklisted us,"-the editor-in-chief asserted, as though stating an undeniable universal truth.-"Faced with such an unfair blacklisting, surely it is justified for us to employ certain slightly underhanded methods?"

[—"Underhanded methods?"](<# "Speaker: Li Hao">)

—"I wonder if everyone has noticed: Heavenly Spirit Sect is not a commercial enterprise after all, and this entire affair caught them unprepared. Consequently, their newspapers and journals have had an exceedingly erratic release schedule lately, constantly riddled with 'blunders.' Not only did they suffer the embarrassment of selling out completely and rushing reprint runs, but due to overwhelming demand, they were forced to convert *White Jade Capital Weekly* into a daily paper. Even so, shortages persist, forcing them to repeatedly release supplementary special issues,"-the editor-in-chief explained.

The other stewards in the hall listened with reddened, envious eyes.

Did that count as a "blunder"?

They would kill to have "blunders" like that!

—"So?"-Li Hao demanded, glaring at the editor.

—"So, if the journal *Spells* were to suddenly release a supplementary special issue because they received several peerless, epoch-making treatises... wouldn't that appear entirely normal?"-the editor-in-chief asked with an insidious smirk.

Li Hao froze for a moment before the realization dawned on him:

—"You mean forging a supplementary issue of *Spells*?! While technically feasible, do you take the entire world for fools? A single word from the Untiring Immortal Venerable would shatter such a lie instantly!"-Li Hao barked, glaring at the editor.

—"Naturally, naturally! That is why we can only pull this off once. But our true goal has always been [traffic diversion](<# "Literal CN: 引流 (yǐnliú) — funneling traffic / driving user acquisition to our own media">) for our newspaper!"-the editor-in-chief replied in a flurry of panic.

He laid out his [master plan](<# "Author pun: 计（阴）划（谋） (jì [yīn] huà [móu]) — plan (conspiracy)">) in exquisite detail:

—"We shall publish our selected theses under the banner of a *Spells* Supplementary Issue. Before *that man* catches wind of it, we will aggressively hype up these treatises, hiring cultivators to shower them with glowing praise, amplify their reach, and make everyone recognize and revere our papers as undeniably peerless, brilliant, and magnificent! If this brings the debate to a halt, all the better!"-the editor-in-chief elaborated.-"Naturally, this charade cannot last long; *that man* is bound to discover the anomaly. Therefore, while we are [hyping](<# "Author markup: 吹捧（X） (chuīpěng [X]) — flattering / hyping (crossed out)">) promoting them, we will deliberately leave minor discrepancies hinting that this supplement originated elsewhere. Once the entire realm acknowledges the brilliance of our Carefree Palace's papers, we will step forward proactively, reveal the true origin of the 'Supplementary Issue,' and publicly accuse the Heavenly Spirit Sect of blacklisting and suppressing our academic voice! Once that happens, will cultivators ever trust the academic integrity of *Spells* again—"

—"Brilliant!"-Li Hao cheered, slapping his thigh.-"Magnificent!"

He instantly tossed the so-called "backup plan" to the back of his mind, declaring loudly:

—"Execute it precisely as stated!"

The other stewards in the hall chimed in with sycophantic praise, as though a monumental victory had already been secured. One sharp steward even piped up with a report:

—"Speaking of which, rumors say the Untiring Immortal Venerable has recently departed the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Even slipping this 'supplementary issue' into the Heavenly Spirit Sect's distribution channels isn't entirely impossible,"-the steward reported.

—"Hahaha, the heavens favor me!"-Li Hao rejoiced even further.

***

And so, on the very next day, every high-tier cultivator across the realm received a "Supplementary Issue" of *Spells*.

There was no helping it; when it came to execution speed, Carefree Palace was truly in a league of its own within the cultivation realm.

They were also lavishly wealthy.

Because they did not possess the actual subscriber ledger for *Spells*, and feared that someone who subscribed might complain to Heavenly Spirit Sect stewards if they missed the issue—which would blow their cover prematurely—they simply delivered this supplementary issue to every single sect, every cultivator at or above the Void Refinement Stage, and even prominent mortal clans! They prioritized comprehensive, gapless saturation, laying the groundwork for their upcoming promotional blitz.

—Primarily, even if they distributed copies across the entire cultivation realm, the cost of a few sheets of paper was vastly cheaper than hosting a lecture from a single high-tier powerhouse.

Furthermore, Carefree Palace's [counterfeiting](<# "Author markup: 造假（X） (zàojiǎ [X]) — counterfeiting / forging (crossed out)">) replication capabilities were undeniably first-rate.

From the texture of the spirit paper and the font typeface to the academic layout—even down to the short sidebar columns on spellcasting techniques appended to *Spells*—everything was replicated to perfection. (To achieve this, Carefree Palace had genuinely contributed a few exclusive spell manuals of their own; after all, if such details appeared fake, how could they convince anyone the main articles were authentic?)

Had this supplementary issue been handed to Zhou Hui himself, even he might not have been able to tell the difference before reading the actual papers.

Let alone ordinary cultivators.

Take, for example, the initiator of this entire academic debate: Fairy Ruopei, the Mahayana cultivator of the Huanfei Sect.

—"Huh? Why is there another supplementary issue of *Spells* today?"-Fairy Ruopei asked in astonishment upon seeing the booklet.

The fairy maidservant attending her replied:

—"Word has it that *Spells* received several exceptionally outstanding theses, which prompted them to publish this special issue."

This justification was admittedly somewhat bizarre, but Fairy Ruopei suspected nothing amiss.

Cultivators across the realm were accustomed to acting on spontaneous whim, and a formal academic periodical like *Spells* was an unprecedented novelty. Factoring in the previous logistical hiccups, cultivators found nothing strange about a supplementary issue suddenly dropping out of nowhere.

—"Outstanding theses? Then I must examine them closely,"-Fairy Ruopei said.

Fairy Ruopei first changed into fresh ceremonial robes, brewed a pot of invigorating spirit tea, instructed the maidservant to light focus-enhancing incense, and had the page boy grind fresh ink before stepping behind her desk to turn the first page of the supplementary issue.

For someone of Fairy Ruopei's stature, such elaborate preparations were mandatory.

Theses published in *Spells* generally possessed genuine academic merit; even a Mahayana titan like Ruopei had to expend considerable mental focus to thoroughly evaluate them.

Moreover, every time she read *Spells*, even a concise paper demanded an expenditure of concentration comparable to a session of deep meditation, taking at least a day and a night.

Yet today, after reading a mere handful of lines, Fairy Ruopei's expression morphed into pure bewilderment.

She stared blankly at the ceiling for several seconds, then lowered her head to read another few sentences. Her bewilderment deepened.

—"You claim these articles are... 'outstanding'?"-Fairy Ruopei asked.

—"That was what the crane delivering the paper said, and I read several pages myself and found them exceedingly convincing,"-the maid replied, puzzled.-"Are they not? Otherwise, why would Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable specifically produce a supplementary issue for them?"

—"Hmph..."-Fairy Ruopei grunted.

Hearing the Untiring Immortal Venerable's name, Fairy Ruopei composed herself once more. She figured there was simply something wrong with that first article, so she skipped it and turned to the next.

This one felt slightly better—at least it was readable.

Yet as Fairy Ruopei sat there, even after the page boy had ground fresh ink three separate times, she hadn't found a single insight worth recording. On the contrary, her brow furrowed tighter and tighter, and tighter—

*Pfft!*

She suddenly burst into a cold, derisive sneer.

—"M-Mistress?"-The page boy and maidservant looked toward her with nervous apprehension.

—"Are you certain the Untiring Immortal Venerable described them as 'outstanding'?"-Fairy Ruopei asked with a cold sneer.-"Did he not rather say that these arguments are merely [on trend](<# "Literal CN: 潮流 (cháoliú) — on trend / trendy / popular fad; callback to Chapter 120's title and theme">)?"

—"What difference does that make?"-the maid asked, blinking blankly.

—"It means these arguments have merely aggregated the most vulgar, sickening, and lowest-common-denominator opinions in existence! Setting aside their self-contradictory logic, even their formatting isn't remotely proper!"-Fairy Ruopei sneered.-"Though it is also fine that the Untiring Immortal Venerable chose to publish them. It allows everyone to behold what sort of worldview those mediocre, incompetent fools actually hold."

Speaking thus, she tossed the supplementary issue aside and turned her gaze toward her maidservant. Although the woman was technically a maid, she was in truth the scion of an established cultivation clan. For cultivators born with mediocre spirit roots, serving by the side of a supreme powerhouse was their finest path forward; sometimes they enjoyed resources far surpassing inner sect disciples, and many kind-hearted powerhouses treated them as their own disciples. At this moment, Ruopei issued a stern admonition:

—"If one were to adhere to their absurd theories, a Mahayana female cultivator like myself shouldn't even exist in this cultivation world! So if you have no wish to sever your own immortal path, you had better not lay eyes upon such perverse heresies!"-Fairy Ruopei commanded.

—"Y-yes, Mistress!"-The maid broke into a cold sweat, stammering in terror.

***

Having admonished her disciple, Fairy Ruopei considered the matter concluded.

Regardless of what bizarre curiosity had prompted the Untiring Immortal Venerable to publish these repulsive papers, Fairy Ruopei believed that since this was merely a "supplementary issue," it proved the Immortal Venerable himself felt it unnecessary to contaminate the main journal's pages.

[I'll just go wash my eyes and pretend I never saw it.]

Still, having read several utterly talentless papers, Fairy Ruopei was in a foul mood. She exited her cave dwelling and flew toward the sect's interior, hoping the lively buzz of disciple voices would dispel her gloom.

Yet the moment she landed on the stone steps, she overheard disciples conversing in animated discussion:

[—"Did you receive that supplementary issue of *Spells*?"](<# "Speaker: Huanfei Sect Disciple">)

[—"Of course! Although that journal has always been incomprehensibly difficult, today's papers are uncharacteristically clear and easy to understand!"](<# "Speaker: Huanfei Sect Disciple">)

[—"Indeed! Reading it truly makes one feel enlightened! As expected of a journal where only high-tier powerhouses can publish!"](<# "Speaker: Huanfei Sect Disciple">)

[What kind of unholy ghost is this?!]

Fairy Ruopei rang the chime in her hand. Once the two disciples turned toward her, she sneered coldly:

—"Have you completed today's assigned coursework? With your meager cultivation, you dare harbor thoughts about *Spells*?!"

The two disciples turned pale with fright, stammered frantic apologies, and fled in terror.

Little did she expect this was merely the beginning.

As Fairy Ruopei walked along the pathways, she heard similar chorus after chorus of lavish praise. Initially, she still had the patience to discipline them, but eventually she felt like summoning the entire sect for a mass reprimand.

Unable to contain her vexation, she made her way to the sect's council hall, only to find the current Sect Master—her own Nascent Soul grand-disciple—gathered with several Soul Formation elders, all clustered around the journal *Spells*.

They were debating with immense gusto:

[—"Brilliant! Utterly magnificent!"](<# "Speaker: Huanfei Sect Elder">)

[—"The thesis presented here diverges from both warring factions in the debate, yet offers such a distinctive perspective!"](<# "Speaker: Huanfei Sect Elder">)

[—"Indeed, this paper is the one that speaks the truth!"](<# "Speaker: Huanfei Sect Elder">)

[...Even you people are falling for this?!]

Ruopei parted her lips, suddenly losing the will to even be angry. Leaving behind that flock of grand-disciples, she flew out toward the immortal bazaar outside the sect gate, intending to buy a jug of cheap spirit wine to numb her nerves.

Yet the moment she stepped into the tavern, she overheard patrons chatting:

[—"Do you know about the supplementary issue of *Spells*?"](<# "Speaker: Low-Tier Cultivator">)

[—"That heavenly tome that only immortals can comprehend?"](<# "Speaker: Low-Tier Cultivator">)

[—"No, no! There's a supplementary issue this time—even low-tier cultivators like us can make notable breakthroughs after reading it!"](<# "Speaker: Low-Tier Cultivator">)

[—"Truly? Where can one read it?"](<# "Speaker: Low-Tier Cultivator">)

[—"Such a magnificent essay should be spread across the entire world!"](<# "Speaker: Low-Tier Cultivator">)

...

Fairy Ruopei's stomach was bloated with pure rage. Instantly losing all interest in buying wine, she flew straight back to her cave dwelling in incandescent fury.

—"Prepare the brush and ink!"-Fairy Ruopei barked.

—"Mistress?!"-the maid gasped.

—"I want everyone under heaven to see just how preposterous these theses are!"-Fairy Ruopei declared in incandescent fury.

***

Fairy Ruopei was far from the only one who felt this way.

Nor was this confined to the Northern Domain. In the Central Domain, a cultivator finished reading the supplementary issue and let out a blood-curdling shriek of agony:

—"What kind of unholy ghost thesis is this?! Its basic formatting is completely wrong! Why did an essay like this get published in a supplementary issue, while my paper got rejected?!"-the cultivator howled in outrage.-"Unacceptable! I am writing a letter to *White Jade Capital* as well!"

***

Several days later, Li Hao inquired with boundless anticipation:

—"How is it? Did our papers cause a sensation?"

—"They did cause a sensation... Palace Master, the entire cultivation realm is currently cursing us to high heaven,"-the subordinate replied tremulously.

—"What?! Where is that editor-in-chief?!"-Li Hao roared.

—"He has already fled."
