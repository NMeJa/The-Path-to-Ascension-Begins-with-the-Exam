# Chapter 154: Acting (第154章 演)

That Liu Xuenian did not take action in person was less a matter of caution and more that he was utterly spooked.

—"Just what does Untiring intend to do?!"-Liu Xuenian muttered, staring at the spatial rift in utter incomprehension.

Originally, he had assumed the rift below had been torn open by some force majeure. In the most preposterous scenario, perhaps the Qingyang clan had believed the rift was already gone, or suspected a defect in the boundary ward, prompting them to pry open the "floorboards"—merely a metaphor, of course, as reality was far more complex than a simple layer of flooring—to take a look, inadvertently allowing the demonic beasts below to emerge ahead of schedule.

At the very least, observing from the control tower above, even if the defensive wards down here appeared to be pioneering [something entirely new](# "Literal CN: 搞一种很新的东西 — Internet slang for 'trying something bizarrely modern/novel'"), it was still fundamentally a defense.

Yet having actually descended below, Liu Xuenian discovered that "defense" did not exist here whatsoever.

A staggering ninety-nine talismans bound the spatial rift—not to force it closed, but to pry it open as wide as humanly possible! Temporary scaffolding encircled the perimeter of the rift, where a new structure was currently under construction, vaguely taking the shape of a circular ring-shaped outpost. Looking at the materials strewn across the construction site, the vast majority were top-tier artifact-crafting materials like spirit stones, purple gold, and thousand-year dark iron. It did not look like a defensive outpost in the slightest; rather, it looked like an offensive forward staging base.

Building upon this, the control tower plunged downward through the center of the unfinished outpost like the [Ocean-Calming Divine Needle](# "Literal CN: 定海神针 (dìng hǎi shén zhēn) — Sun Wukong's Ruyi Jingu Bang, an immovable pillar anchoring the sea"), spearing straight into the Demon Realm! Countless corridors fanned out around the control tower just as they did on the upper levels. Yet right at this rift, it resembled nothing so much as a colossal spiderweb. Countless demonic creatures threw themselves into this "web" entirely of their own volition, blissfully unaware of what horrors awaited them above, appearing pitifully weak and utterly helpless.

Witnessing this spectacle, Liu Xuenian arrived at virtually the same conclusion Zhou Hui had reached:

—"He couldn't possibly be planning to conquer the Demon Realm, could he?!"-Liu Xuenian gasped in disbelief.

This "he," naturally, referred to the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Liu Xuenian never even suspected that anyone other than Zhou Hui could have orchestrated such a move.

After all, in his estimation, only someone of Zhou Hui's cultivation realm would dare be so recklessly brazen. Any other human would be shivering in abject terror before demonic creatures; who among them would possess the guts to reverse into the demon domains instead?

As for Qu Qianxing, Liu Xuenian never even entertained the possibility that it could be his handiwork.

In the eyes of "Liu Xuenian," Qu Qianxing was nothing more than an article of clothing.

Or rather, in "Liu Xuenian's" perception, whether mortals or cultivators of this realm, they were all merely "consumables." The prettier ones could be worn as garments, while the unsightly ones were expendable materials—in essence, there was zero distinction.

Therefore, although Qu Qianxing had resisted him years ago and even successfully slain "him," it was of little consequence to "Liu Xuenian." He had merely lost an "outfit he had grown tired of wearing."

In fact, as long as Qu Qianxing was still alive, he still possessed the chance to claim this outfit.

A garment so gorgeous, so exquisite, and so "expensive"—one he coveted desperately, yet had failed to obtain.

Of course, right now, he couldn't "afford" it either.

After all, Qu Qianxing's current "owner" was Untiring.

If one were to ask "Liu Xuenian," the Untiring Immortal Venerable was an existence far more bizarre and eldritch than any god or Buddha in heaven or demon in the underworld.

He simply could not fathom how this world had managed to breed a monster like Untiring. If one were to evaluate his innate talent, it wasn't particularly extraordinary, was it? Could that kind of terrifying power truly be attained through mere hard work?

In short, offending such a monstrous terror for the sake of a "pretty outfit" was entirely not worth the price. Thus, "Liu Xuenian" had no choice but to suppress his longing for Qu Qianxing.

Of course, regardless of whatever (he assumed) the Untiring Immortal Venerable intended to do, "Liu Xuenian" had zero intention of personally charging at the vanguard to attack an outpost serving as an invasion bridgehead into the demon domain.

When all was said and done, he was merely a divination cultivator, not frontline combat personnel.

Regarding his background, it was solely because the cultivation technique "Liu Xuenian" practiced happened to be compatible that he could exploit this loophole, descending to a lower realm virtually abandoned by the Heavenly Court, entrusted by the upper realm to monitor the state of this minor realm.

On the other hand, "Liu Xuenian" readily admitted that there was a component of personal volition: after all, only in an ungoverned [no-man's-land](# "Literal CN: 三不管 (sānbùguǎn) — three-way jurisdictional vacuum; a lawless border zone") of a lower realm could he indulge to his heart's content in donning the "pretty clothes" he fancied.

For locals to suddenly [court death](# "Idiom: 作死 (zuòsǐ) — actively seeking death through reckless folly") like this by preparing to invade the Demon Realm was a critical matter that "Liu Xuenian" was obligated to report urgently!

[Even though this minor realm was designated long ago as a flood-discharge zone for the Demon Realm, doing so this many years early is unacceptable! What we want is 'flood discharge,' not handing the demons a fortified staging base!] "Liu Xuenian" mused sourly in his heart.-[I must transmit this intelligence back immediately... Ah, how infuriating! That fellow guarding the gate 'lately' seems exceptionally irritable. I really don't want to communicate with Him; ten to one, I'll just get cursed at again—]

The "fellow guarding the gate" he referred to was, naturally, the very entity Zhou Hui had slain.

Yet owing to the "time dilation," "Liu Xuenian" did not contact the other party frequently. Consequently, he became aware of the "god's" demise even later than that "god's" own mortal worshippers.

In truth, "Liu Xuenian" was not even aware that this world had been repeatedly rewound and reset.

After all, he was currently a component of this world as well. Each temporal rewind purged his memories alongside the rest; as long as his colleagues up in the upper realm refrained from mentioning during this current incarnation that the world was continually rebooting, "Liu Xuenian" possessed zero recollection of those cycles.

Had he known, "Liu Xuenian" would have been infinitely more cautious.

Even without those memories, however, "Liu Xuenian" was already sufficiently cautious.

He generally confined himself to his special private chamber inside the Palace of Carefree Wandering. That room was encircled with specialized talismans, and further fortified with spatial wards that concealed Carefree Palace's true coordinates. Even an existence of Zhou Hui's caliber had been unable to locate Carefree Palace's genuine seat.

"Liu Xuenian's" true vessel virtually never stepped foot outside. He typically acted through the identities of various "garments," and on occasion did not even "wear clothes" (metaphorically speaking), manipulating puppets through specialized imprints whose aura had been scrubbed clean.

This was precisely the "secret" to how "Liu Xuenian" had eluded capture by the Untiring Immortal Venerable all the way until now.

This time, having confirmed the fundamental layout of the outpost, "Liu Xuenian" prepared to withdraw immediately.

—"...Before leaving, let's stir up a grand commotion!"-"Liu Xuenian" chuckled sinisterly.

He planted a suggestion into this current vessel—namely, "Xiao Geng's" physical body. Instantly, the spiritual energy enveloping Xiao Geng dissipated entirely, and with zero defenses whatsoever, his physical body marched straight toward the demonic horde.

If things proceeded this way, Xiao Geng would be instantly devoured by those demons.

—"A second-generation cultivation scion from Sky City dying right here—let's see how you, Untiring, suppress this news!"-"Liu Xuenian" murmured with immense satisfaction.

Next, he prepared to extract his soul and "return" to Liu Xuenian's body, using Liu Xuenian's persona to send an urgent plea for help to the scion's family in Sky City. Naturally, he wouldn't mention Untiring invading the Demon Realm first—that sounded so utterly absurd that even he would find it hard to believe; he would simply claim Xiao Geng had vanished, luring the scion's formidable clan elders over.

Doing this would, of course, burn the "Liu Xuenian" identity as well. Fortunately, he had grown thoroughly sick of wearing this particular outfit anyway, so discarding it was of no consequence. As a finale, he could detonate the curses he had planted across several other "suggested targets."

A whole clique of second-generation scions suffering a collective catastrophe in Eternal Villa, while the secret realm directly beneath them housed a Demon Realm rift—[this basin of sewage](# "Literal CN: 屎盆子 (shǐpénzi) — pot of excrement; blame or dirty scandal dumped on someone") was something neither the Untiring Immortal Venerable nor Villa Master Qingyang could wash their hands of easily.

When that time came, the Palace of Carefree Wandering would only need to manipulate things subtly from the shadows, and this minor realm would descend into self-cannibalizing chaos on its own.

"Liu Xuenian" mused with immense relish.

Yet just as he was poised to execute this venomous scheme, a sudden crack of thunder violently shattered his train of thought:

*BOOM!*

*Isn't this deep underground?*

*Thunder?!*

"Liu Xuenian" froze in bewilderment.

Subconsciously gazing in the direction of the thunderclap, the dazzling glare of lightning forced (Xiao Geng's) body to squint.

Behind the flash of electric arcs, the foremost ranks of demonic creatures were incinerated to ash without so much as a whimper. Above the grand gates of the outpost hovered a peerlessly exquisite figure, while another person rushed frantically out of the outpost in hot pursuit:

—"Qianxing! Brother Qianxing! Wait for me! I can help exterminate demons too!"-Jian Nuo shouted.

These were, naturally, Jian Nuo and Qu Qianxing.

*Why on earth have they emerged?*

"Liu Xuenian" peered suspiciously toward the pair.

The two tugged and bickered mid-air. In theory, their voices should never have traveled to where "Liu Xuenian" (Xiao Geng) was positioned, yet for a body possessing Xiao Geng's cultivation realm, bridging the acoustic distance was no obstacle at all.

With a minor exertion of spellcraft, "Liu Xuenian" caught every word with crystal clarity:

—"Humph! Ever since I began constructing this outpost, not once has a demonic counterattack occurred,"-Qu Qianxing snapped furiously at Jian Nuo.-"This is entirely the fault of your 'friends'!"

—"Which is precisely why I offered to clean up the mess together!"-Jian Nuo protested.

He raised his hand, and several flying swords spiraled into orbit around his frame.

Sword light flared, cleanly finishing off the stray demonic beasts that had survived the golden thunder.

This was well within expectations.

Demonic creatures permitted to ascend to the upper corridors for "novice commanders" to practice on were never particularly formidable. "Liu Xuenian" had commanded them solely to probe the state of the Demon Realm rift, testing the defensive perimeter guarding the tear and seeing if he could tear open a wider breach amidst the chaos.

Who could have guessed that this rift resembled a rift in name only?! They hadn't harbored the slightest intention of sealing the tear; instead, they had converted the rift directly into a colossal outpost! Dispatching this measly swarm of monsters was a trivial breeze.

Yet despite securing victory, Qu Qianxing did not appear the least bit pleased.

He flew a full circuit around the rear of the outpost—the side facing upward toward the mortal cultivation world—looking noticeably tense.

*Could it be that the rear of this outpost harbors a defensive blind spot?*

A thought stirred in "Liu Xuenian's" heart.

Meanwhile, having failed to soothe Qu Qianxing and receiving a cold glare from the beauty instead, Jian Nuo wore an expression of helpless exasperation.

Immediately afterward, however, Jian Nuo's expression hardened into stern severity. He glared directly in the direction of "Xiao Geng" and barked out Xiao Geng's full name:

—"...You're over there, aren't you?!"-Jian Nuo called out sharply.

"Liu Xuenian" was not surprised in the slightest. Since Xiao Geng was here in the capacity of a playtester, his every move had been placed under surveillance; if anything, being called out only after reaching this spot was what surprised "Liu Xuenian."

Surveillance was one thing, but Jian Nuo had evidently failed to see through the fact that Xiao Geng was already possessed.

Naturally: how could expendable consumables like these ever decipher his exquisite art of manipulation?

The question was: should he walk out under Xiao Geng's identity?

"Liu Xuenian" felt it was somewhat unnecessary. The demonic horde had already been reduced to ash by Qu Qianxing, so even if Xiao Geng approached now, he would not be killed; the centerpiece of his planned grand finale had been ruined.

Yet judging by Qu Qianxing's tense posture, the outpost seemed to possess a structural weakness that could not withstand an assault from the rear.

No—the real centerpiece was Qu Qianxing himself!

This "pretty outfit" was hovering right out in the open, utterly unguarded and carefree!

Although "Liu Xuenian" did not dare provoke the Untiring Immortal Venerable head-on, gazing upon a Qu Qianxing like this, a glint of insatiable greed flared in his eyes.

Of course, in Eternal Villa... nay, across the entire Central Domain, on Untiring's home turf, snatching this "garment" away on the spot was out of the question. But Qu Qianxing couldn't possibly stay confined to the Central Domain forever, could he?

Word had it that Untiring was grooming Qu Qianxing as his supreme successor. Naturally, he couldn't keep him tethered for life; sooner or later, Qu Qianxing would leave the Central Domain for various administrative duties. Wouldn't his opportunity arrive then?

Still, back when Qu Qianxing had possessed zero cultivation, he had managed to burn one of his "garments" to cinders. Now that he had attained cultivation, he appeared even more troublesome to handle; planting a clandestine trick on him early on would be far wiser.

Evidently, Qu Qianxing held zero suspicion toward Jian Nuo's "friends."

As a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the vital heir to the sect master seat, Qu Qianxing had seemed out of reach of the idle scions' social circle. During all the days "Liu Xuenian" had spent here, just as they had never met Qingyang Qingyu, they had never laid eyes on Qu Qianxing either.

Moreover, unlike Qingyang Qingyu, who was their "future sister-in-law," the scions had zero excuse to ever seek an audience with Qu Qianxing.

In other words, using "Xiao Geng's" identity, such a golden opportunity to approach Qu Qianxing might never come again!

[Better to strike today while the iron is hot than wait for another day!](# "Idiom: 择日不如撞日 (zé rì bù rú zhuàng rì) — there's no day like today / seizing an immediate chance")

With this thought, "Liu Xuenian" swiftly recalibrated his plan.

He rescinded the suggestion compelling Xiao Geng to commit suicide, resuming manual override of the vessel. Slowly easing out from his concealment spell without stepping entirely into the open, he offered Jian Nuo a fawning smile:

—"Young Master Jian, whatever brings you down here?"-"Xiao Geng" asked obsequiously.

—"..."-Jian Nuo's brow twitched.

Had he not witnessed the grotesque truth of this abomination inside the Demon-Revealing Mirror, he would have undoubtedly mistaken this figure for the genuine Xiao Geng.

Swallowing his revulsion, Jian Nuo played along:

—"That is precisely what I ought to be asking you!"-Jian Nuo barked.-"Never mind disguising yourselves as Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples to build the secret realm—why on earth were you fraternizing with demonic beasts?!"

—"*Heh heh*, isn't this the assignment Lady Qingyu's... subordinates arranged?"-"Liu Xuenian" acted in kind, rubbing his head sheepishly.-"She instructed me to pose as a demon to test the defensive capabilities of this control tower. Seeing as fighting alone was futile, I tricked those demonic beasts into charging along."

At this point, "Liu Xuenian" flashed Xiao Geng's trademark foolish grin:

—"Well? Did you collect sufficient testing data?"-"Xiao Geng" asked with an eager grin.

—"...We never instructed you to launch an assault on the rear,"-Qu Qianxing said coldly, flying over to hover beside Jian Nuo.

"Xiao Geng's" eyes lit up:

—"Ah, Senior Brother Qu is here as well?"-"Xiao Geng" exclaimed.

His expression this time was not an act; Qu Qianxing truly was peerlessly gorgeous. Every single time "Liu Xuenian" laid eyes on him, he salivated over that physical vessel, an urge welling straight from his heart.

—"Why are you calling him 'Senior Brother' too?"-Jian Nuo grumbled in annoyance, before furrowing his brow as he glared at "Xiao Geng".-"What are you standing so far away for? I'm hardly going to blame you over a trivial matter like this! Come over here!"

"Liu Xuenian" had been waiting for those exact words!

He stepped eagerly toward Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing evidently did not treat this minor nobody with any importance. The only reason he spared him a second glance was his previous behavior fraternizing with demonic beasts and instigating an assault on the outpost.

—"Why did those demonic beasts obey you?"-Qu Qianxing questioned suspiciously, staring coldly at "Xiao Geng."

—"*Heh heh*,"-"Xiao Geng" chuckled foolishly, tugging at the demonic monster gear he was wearing.-"It's all thanks to the demonic gear you crafted being so lifelike; it actually duped quite a few demons."

—"Even if it simulates a demon's aura, it shouldn't be enough to make demonic creatures obedient!"-Qu Qianxing pressed with heightened skepticism.

He reached out his hand as if intending to inspect the gear on "Xiao Geng's" frame.

*...Ha! Just like in the past, completely lacking the aloof airs of a beauty! What an absolute waste of such a gorgeous face.*

"Xiao Geng" shrank his shoulders in seeming embarrassment, yet an eerie glint flickered in the depths of his eyes.

—"Ew!"-Qingyang Qingyu gasped in disgust.

Inside the outpost, Qingyang Qingyu, who had been monitoring the situation the entire time, let out an uncomfortable shudder.

Through the Demon-Revealing Mirror, she saw that while "Xiao Geng" appeared to shrink back, the perpetual stream of wet gore drenching his torso slithered forward like a serpent, coiling around Qu Qianxing's finger and branding his ring finger with a dark crimson mark!

*Caught you! This time, I won't let you escape.*

"Liu Xuenian" confirmed with vicious certainty.

Yet the very next instant, he heard Qu Qianxing's chilling voice:

—"Caught you,"-Qu Qianxing said coldly.-"This time, I won't let you escape."

—"Wha—"-"Xiao Geng" gasped, his eyes bulging.

He saw golden-purple lightning coalesce into a thunderous net, descending to engulf him!

*Exposed?!*

*How?!*

*Why?!*

Chaotic thoughts scrambled through "Liu Xuenian's" mind, but his reflexes moved even faster.

This "grand figure" hailing from the upper realm, the venerable ancestral founder of Carefree Palace, was surprisingly adept at running away.

Inside the Demon-Revealing Mirror, one could see the bloody intestine that had been coiled around "Xiao Geng's" throat sever itself almost instantaneously in two. The segment remaining on "Xiao Geng's" side erupted into a cloud of bloody mist, surging violently toward Qu Qianxing and Jian Nuo.

Meanwhile, a bloody tumor surged along the other severed end of the intestine, rolling at hypersonic speed toward Liu Xuenian. The closer it drew to the body, the larger it swelled, vaguely taking the silhouette of a humanoid figure.

Yet something moved even faster than its retreat—

*BOOM!*

What detonated first was the wisp of golden thunder embedded in Liu Xuenian's robe!

This spark of golden thunder had been deliberately left behind when Liu Xuenian and the others had "first" met Qu Qianxing, simply because Qu Qianxing had felt an instinctive revulsion upon laying eyes on Liu Xuenian. Now, this wisp of golden lightning abruptly erupted, completely engulfing that physical vessel... nay, that cadaver!

—"NO!"-the bloody tumor shrieked in shrill agony as it "witnessed" this scene.

Yet before golden thunder capable of sweeping away all evil, any struggle was meaningless. That long-dead body was reduced to ash in an instant. Likewise, righteous golden thunder descended around Qu Qianxing, instantly purifying the erupting blood mist. As for Xiao Geng (the true body), though he had been temporarily possessed, he was not dead after all; his soul remained intact, leaving him entirely unharmed amidst the terrifying golden lightning.

The only other entity that remained undamaged was that colossal bloody tumor.

It was the "true vessel" of a True Immortal, possessing even a sliver of divinity; Qu Qianxing's current power was not yet formidable enough to destroy it outright.

In truth, had this bloody tumor not detached from Xiao Geng's body while failing to reach Liu Xuenian's corpse, Qu Qianxing would not have even been able to rely on the golden thunder's property of vanquishing evil to purify the blood mist and incinerate the corpse.

Of course, it was precisely because Qu Qianxing understood "Liu Xuenian's" nature that he had seized this exact window of opportunity.

—"QU QIANXING!"-Liu Xuenian... no, the "bloody tumor," realizing the situation had turned hopeless, let out a furious howl.

It swelled ever larger, forming a gargantuan humanoid silhouette. Yet because of the swelling, the outer membrane of blood stretched increasingly thin, looking as though it would detonate in the very next second.

No—it truly was about to detonate!

Qu Qianxing had dealt with this thing before. Back then, after he had slain the other party, the bloody tumor had detonated all over his face and body, virtually burning away the skin across his entire frame. Had it not been for Master, he would have likely suffered complete disfigurement of this beautiful mortal vessel!

Qu Qianxing hadn't cared.

At one point, he had felt that his own physical vessel was the root of all his suffering. If he could slay that "monster," no sacrifice was too great; in the moment his flesh was charred by its blood, he had genuinely believed he had killed it.

Who could have guessed that this was merely one of the creature's escape tactics?

Had he not encountered Liu Xuenian this time, Qu Qianxing would have lived on believing he had resolved the grievances of his past.

—In truth, Qu Qianxing suspected Master had known all along that his vengeance had failed back then. Master had likely concealed the truth because he did not want Qu Qianxing to develop an inner demon before possessing sufficient strength.

This time, Qu Qianxing had zero intention of letting it escape.

His golden lightning formed an [inescapable net of heaven and earth](# "Idiom: 天罗地网 (tiānluódìwǎng) — an inescapable net covering heaven and earth"), pressing down upon the mass of bloody tumor and halting its expansion. Precisely because it could not thoroughly purify the essence of a True Immortal, this net of lightning became the ideal cage to capture the bloody tumor instead!

Yet the bloody tumor burst into wild laughter:

—"A mere mortal dares dream of capturing this Immortal?!"-the bloody tumor howled arrogantly.

Accompanying this manic laugh, the golden thunder-net was tainted by a layer of bloody crimson, and the sparkling lightning began to dim.

—"*Tsk*,"-Jian Nuo clicked his tongue.

Looking at Qu Qianxing and then at the bloody tumor, his flying swords returned, their sword light interlacing to form the shape of zither strings!

Jian Nuo strummed the strings. The music resonated as though they had rehearsed together countless times, temporarily suppressing the creature!

—"Jian Nuo! You dare raise your hand against this Immortal?! Do you wish for Sky City to fall from the heavens?!"-the bloody tumor howled in fury.

Jian Nuo paid it no heed, wholly immersed in his strumming.

However, while this melody suppressed the bloody tumor, it could not purify it. The corrosive crimson hue continued to seep across the thunder-net. Even emptying every drop of his spiritual reserves, Qu Qianxing struggled to maintain the net.

Seeing this, the bloody tumor erupted into arrogant laughter once more:

—"Hahaha! Qu Qianxing, had your cultivation realm been a fraction higher, you might truly have trapped this Immortal! But the current you—"-the bloody tumor sneered with arrogant laughter.

*BOOM!*

Its words were cut short by a colossal rumble of thunder.

*A mere Golden Core—how does this fellow still possess strength?!*

The bloody tumor thought in irritation, but it quickly realized something was terribly wrong: this thunderclap had not originated from Qu Qianxing's vicinity, but was reverberating down from the heavens above!

The bloody tumor "looked" upward—assuming it still possessed eyes—and sensed tribulation clouds gathering across the sky. While insignificant compared to Untiring's tribulation lightning, as a [Nascent Soul breakthrough tribulation](# "Literal CN: 结婴之劫 (jiéyīng zhī jié) — the heavenly tribulation faced when forming a Nascent Soul from a Golden Core"), it was already astonishingly vast!

*Wait a moment... Forming a Nascent Soul?!*

—"Who do you think you're looking down on?! You dare attempt a Nascent Soul breakthrough at a time like this?!"-the bloody tumor glared at Qu Qianxing in sheer shock.

Qu Qianxing responded with a smile so radiant it was blinding.

No—what was blinding was the lightning.

Heavenly thunder cascaded from the firmament. Unlike ordinary lightning tribulations sent to test cultivators, the moment this heavenly thunder struck down, it fused seamlessly with Qu Qianxing's innate golden lightning, slamming violently into the bloody tumor!

Two distinct currents of electricity intertwined to weave an entirely new network. As a cosmic ordeal decreed by Heavenly Dao to test cultivators and immortals alike, even a True Immortal could not shrug off the majesty of heavenly thunder!

This time, the bloody tumor could laugh no longer, shrieking in pure agony.

Nevertheless, it was a True Immortal after all; it would not dissolve so easily. Yet Qu Qianxing's power did not run dry this time either. Rather, using himself as a conductor and heavenly thunder as fuel, he wove shimmering runes that repeatedly battered the bloody tumor!

Arcs of lightning danced in a dazzling tempest.

Witnessing this spectacle, Jian Nuo's fingers couldn't help but freeze on the strings.

—"Don't stop,"-Qingyang Qingyu said, cradling a pipa in her arms as she appeared at Jian Nuo's side.-"Play along with me. Senior Brother Qu's cultivation realm is still slightly lacking; he requires harmonized spiritual energy to assist him."

Jian Nuo looked toward Qingyang Qingyu:

—"Brother Qianxing is..."-Jian Nuo began, staring in disbelief.

—"Refining!"-Qingyang Qingyu answered, strumming her pipa.-"He is using our Qingyang clan's secret art to refine that mass of 'True Immortal Blood'!"

—"The refinement methods of a Mahayana grandmaster—he actually learned it for real!"-she added, her gaze brimming with unmistakable jealousy.
