# Chapter 250: Three Hundred Years Later (Part 1)

Long Tianming was a transmigrator.

Naturally, that wasn't anything [to write home about](# "Literal CN: 没什么好稀罕的 — nothing particularly rare or special").

In this day and age, competition had grown far too fierce. Being a mere transmigrator was no longer enough to qualify for the post of Protagonist; at the very least, one had to come packaged with a System or some equivalent [cheat](# "Literal CN: 金手指 (golden finger) — gaming/web novel slang for a protagonist's overpowered cheat ability or unfair advantage").

Even so, Long Tianming felt pretty good about himself.

Because not only was he a transmigrator, he had recently—finally—encountered his own [portable old grandpa](# "Literal CN: 随身老爷爷 — classic xianxia trope of a venerable master's lingering soul residing in a ring, pendant, or the protagonist's mind").

...All right, fine, the whole "portable old grandpa" gimmick was indeed horribly outdated, but a cheat was still a cheat, wasn't it?

Under the guidance of this "Old Grandpa," Long Tianming had already mastered an ancient, supposedly long-lost cultivation method, opened his own meridians, and attained a cultivation base of the third layer of Qi Condensation in a mere three days. Moreover, he had mastered several basic spells corresponding to the technique—and used them to successfully blast a rabbit to death!

Granted, it was just a rabbit! But for a useless university student who had grown up in a peaceful, stable society and had never even slaughtered a fish with his own hands, this was already quite the monumental leap forward!!

—"If things keep going like this, I will definitely transcend mortality and achieve immortality, seize supreme dharma treasures, dominate the immortal realm, and marry a [fair, rich, and beautiful maiden](# "Literal CN: 白富美 (báifùměi) — modern internet buzzword for 'fair-skinned, wealthy, and beautiful woman', the quintessential trophy wife")—"

—"Excuse me, but you only killed a single rabbit. Where on earth did you get all this confidence?"-the "Old Grandpa" inside Long Tianming's mind asked wearily. He was beginning to seriously suspect he had been picked up by a lunatic.

Unfortunately, these days uncertified cultivators were few and far between. It had been no easy feat stumbling upon an [easy mark](# "Literal CN: 冤大头 (yuāndàtóu) — a gullible sucker or dupe who easily falls for scams and spends wastefully") like this; combined with the boy's reasonably decent innate aptitude, he had no choice but to stretch him out and make do.

Yet the easy mark whom he had to ration and use sparingly proclaimed with righteous conviction:

—"You just don't get it! The very instant you crossed paths with me was pretty much the moment the plot kicked into gear. Next up, the female lead should just about make her grand entrance—"

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Old Grandpa")

[—"Ah. Help."](# "Speaker: Woman in the Woods")

As if answering Long Tianming's words, ringing out at the exact same moment as the Old Grandpa's confusion was a woman's cry for help.

Only, this cry for help was somewhat bizarre. It didn't sound urgent in the slightest—in fact, it sounded entirely like a [monotone script reading](# "Literal CN: 捧读 (pěngdú) — reading aloud woodenly from a script with zero emotion or inflection; flat recitative").

Long Tianming grew thrilled at once:

—"Aha! Look! Here it comes!"

Dropping the rabbit in a flash, he followed the sound.

—"Wait a minute..."-the Old Grandpa tried to stop him, but lacked the strength to do so. He could only watch helplessly as Long Tianming—like that fable's foolish hare that threw itself headfirst against a tree trunk—plunged through the bushes and arrived at a clearing in the shallower parts of the woods.

There, indeed, sat a "beautiful young lady."

Clad in pure white robes and a white skirt, she sat limply upon the grass in an attitude of delicate helplessness. Perhaps out of excessive shock, her expression was somewhat blank, devoid of any genuine panic or heartfelt desire for rescue. Yet directly opposite her coiled a massive snake the height of a grown man, wrapped in a mantle of blood-crimson radiance, flicking its tongue with an ominous hiss.

Upon catching sight of this scene, Long Tianming failed to notice his long-yearned-for "female lead" in the slightest, instead crying out in sheer terror:

—"A snake!"

He subconsciously retreated two paces.

It couldn't be helped. For an utterly useless student who had only ever killed a single rabbit since taking up cultivation, demanding that he bravely confront a giant serpent was asking far too much. In truth, the fact that he hadn't turned tail and bolted the very second he laid eyes on the beast was already an act of immense courage.

Actually, Long Tianming wanted to run, but he was still a human being at heart. Twenty years of ideological and moral education made it impossible for him to abandon a girl to face a monstrous serpent on her own.

Especially when that girl was still crying for help:

—"Young master, please save me."

—"Pay no attention to her, she's taking you for a fool,"-the Old Grandpa said, unable to stomach the girl's wretched acting.-"Are female monsters this unprofessional nowadays? Even a child reciting a school textbook wouldn't sound this monotone!"

—"How could you say that about her?!"-Long Tianming protested, disagreeing entirely.

Before his transmigration, his wildly adored [rising young starlet](# "Literal CN: 实力小花 — modern entertainment industry slang for an up-and-coming young lead actress billed as having acting talent") hadn't delivered any better performance in her breakout role either!

Besides, if he fled in a situation like this, what kind of "Protagonist" would he be?!

Swallowing a dry mouthful of saliva, Long Tianming quavered:

—"Miss, hold on, I'm coming to save you!"-Saying so, he forced himself forward, but the foul blast of demonic qi from the great serpent made him shudder violently. Subconsciously squeezing his eyes shut, he mobilized the spiritual energy in his body and blindly hurled a spell forward.

Because of his extreme panic, Long Tianming wasn't even sure which spell he had cast.

Not that it mattered in the least. How could the "Protagonist" ever lose? At any rate, when Long Tianming opened his eyes again, the serpent had already collapsed limply to the ground, and the "beautiful girl" was looking at him with "deep gratitude."

—"I-it worked?"-Long Tianming muttered in disbelief.

—"No. You nearly hit this female demon; she knocked the snake down herself,"-the Old Grandpa stated with brutal honesty.

—"Thank you, young master, for saving my life,"-the "fair maiden" continued her flat recitative.-"[This humble maiden](# "Literal CN: 妾身 (qièshēn) — classical humble first-person self-appellation used by women") is named Renran. Might I ask who you are...?"

—"Renran?!"-the Old Grandpa cried out in shock.

—"Is there something wrong with that name?"-Long Tianming asked curiously in his mind. He had always practiced selective hearing with the Old Grandpa's advice, tuning in only to what he liked; naturally, the fair maiden's name piqued his interest.

The Old Grandpa sounded hesitant:

—"Perhaps I'm mistaken. She shouldn't be here. After all, the 'Renran' I know is a supreme Tribulation Transcendence mighty figure—a goddess descended from Heaven who was stranded in this realm, unable to leave!"

—"Then she must definitely be *that* Renran!"-Long Tianming grew instantly electrified.-"Or maybe she's one of her avatars! Only a goddess of that caliber is worthy of being the female lead!"

[—"Whatever makes you happy."](# "Speaker: Old Grandpa")

The Old Grandpa instantly lost all his strength.

Still, after Long Tianming's wild theorizing, he actually stopped suspecting the identity of this "Renran" before them. On second thought, it was impossible anyway. It had to be some female monster from somewhere who had usurped the goddess's name to swindle a fool like his host, who had read far too many cheap pulp romances!

Long Tianming straightened his robes, saying in all solemnity:

—"My surname is Long, given name Tianming. I am—"

—"Your Ladyship Renran, are you here?"

Just then, someone interrupted Long Tianming's self-introduction.

Accompanied by that masculine voice, an extraordinarily handsome youth with silver hair stepped leisurely out from the forest. With a faint smile, he glanced toward Renran, then cast an appraising look at the "Protagonist" by her side.

Long Tianming froze stiff on the spot.

So this was what they called a [celestial countenance](# "Literal CN: 天人之姿 (tiānrén zhī zī) — the breathtaking bearing and transcendent beauty of a heavenly being")?

Long Tianming thought in a daze, staring blankly at the newcomer, an inexplicable urge to prostrate himself in reverent worship welling up inside him.

Fortunately, someone tugged at his sleeve at that moment.

Looking down numbly, Long Tianming discovered that the "fair maiden" calling herself Renran showed not the slightest hint of maidenly bashfulness or excitement upon seeing the graceful silver-haired youth. On the contrary, she appeared somewhat terrified and repulsed, yet forced her lips to curl upward into a smile—this subtle nuance in expression was countless times more genuine than her wooden cries for help just now.

Ah, as expected of the female lead! Even in this situation, she still chooses me?

Long Tianming thought with deep emotion, only for his brain to catch up a second later:

[Wait a minute. Following standard plot tropes, couldn't this transcendent heavenly being be the arrogant second-generation young master competing for the female lead?! No way! How am I supposed to beat someone like that?!]

Renran had no idea that her "Protagonist" was already [beating a retreat](# "Literal CN: 打退堂鼓 — backing out / getting cold feet"). She merely moved instinctively to shield Long Tianming with her frail frame, calling out with a slight tremble:

—"N-No... Young Master Zhou, why have you come here?"

—"That was what I intended to ask you,"-the silver-haired youth addressed as "Young Master Zhou" replied with a gentle smile.-"The appointed gathering time has arrived, yet you hadn't appeared. I was worried something might have happened to you."

[What could possibly happen to me, a Tribulation Transcendence titan, in a mortal territory like this?!]

[If anything, staying by your side is far more dangerous!]

Goddess Renran's mouth twitched.

Yes, Goddess Renran.

Since she called herself Renran, she was naturally the genuine Goddess Renran. Virtually no one would impersonate her anyway—especially after word spread that she might have offended the Untiring Immortal Venerable, after which everyone simply presumed the goddess was a dead woman walking.

Even Goddess Renran herself had tucked her tail between her legs and lived in secluded hiding for several centuries after fleeing Mount Tianling that day.

That decision had undoubtedly been the right one. That Immortal Arts Assembly had ultimately escalated to the scale of an Immortal-Demon War. The high-tier cultivators returning from that grand assembly had all learned, to varying degrees, of the Heavenly Court's sinister designs. Consequently, they had rebuilt the world's protective barrier restrictions from the ground up, causing this realm to completely sever all ties with the Heavenly Court and other minor worlds.

It wasn't until two hundred and fifty years ago—when Untiring apparently decided developments had reached a satisfactory stage—that he and City Lord Jian reopened the world barrier and proactively re-established connections with the other three thousand worlds, finally allowing Renran to restore contact with the Heavenly Court.

To Renran's bewilderment—leaving her unsure whether to tremble in fear or count her blessings—a "mere" two hundred and fifty years down here was barely two and a half days in the Heavenly Court, yet the Heavenly Court had apparently descended into total pandemonium, leaving them with no spare thought to bother with Renran or this world.

Thus, Renran had spent another fifty years living a life devoid of worldly desires.

Just as she almost believed she would live like that forever, the three-hundred-year term expired. Even amidst the Heavenly Court's chaos, a divine oracle descended from the heavens right on schedule, commanding Goddess Renran to respectfully receive the "Child of Destiny"—complete with an utterly [cringeworthy](# "Literal CN: 一言难尽 (yīyán-nánjìn) — hard to describe in a few words / embarrassing to talk about") "hero saves the damsel in distress" script.

The oracle proclaimed that only the one who appeared in accordance with this script would be the true "Child of Destiny"!

Renran found it rather peculiar. From the phrasing, it almost sounded as though the Heavenly Court itself didn't even know who this "Child of Destiny" was, requiring such an asinine script just to fish him out.

Then again, given those brain-dead, cursed bastards in the Ministry of Celestial Destiny, any idiotic mess they concocted was entirely par for the course!

Entirely unaware that the Ministry of Celestial Destiny had already been wiped out three hundred years ago, Goddess Renran cursed foully in her heart without a shred of divine grace. Soon, she pushed it out of her mind—and indeed, she had no room to ponder it further, because before she could locate the "Child of Destiny," she had the rotten luck of running straight into the Untiring Immortal Venerable!

With the passage of three hundred years, this mighty figure had long since transformed from a recluse known only to a handful of high-tier cultivators into this world's mastermind behind the scenes... or rather, its hidden guiding light.

His every move prompted shifts in policy across the entire cultivation realm.

Yet such a monumental figure had actually concealed his name and identity to appear in this mortal domain. According to the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself, he had merely come to inspect the achievements of the cultivation world's educational reforms over the past three hundred years.

...Like hell Renran would believe that!

Renran was convinced that with Untiring's abilities, he must have known all along about the Heavenly Court's instructions to her, and had come intentionally to throw a wrench into her plans.

However, since the Untiring Immortal Venerable was still giving her face for the time being—refraining from outright tearing off all pretenses and even willing to play along with her acting—Renran wasn't about to be tactless. At the very least, after several centuries in hiding, her temper had mellowed considerably. She resolved to feign total ignorance and keep acting; even if the Heavenly Court tried to hold her accountable later, she would have an excuse.

—"I did run into a minor mishap,"-having thought through to this point, Renran found her composure. Putting on a terrified expression, she glanced at the giant serpent lying thoroughly dead upon the ground, then turned to Long Tianming, murmuring softly:-"Fortunately, this young master came to my rescue."

—"Rescued you?"-Young Master Zhou... that is, Zhou Hui, raised an eyebrow. Amused, he looked toward Long Tianming, deliberately asking what he already knew:-"And this gentleman might be...?"

[—"I—"](# "Speaker: Long Tianming")

—"Young Master Long Tianming,"-Renran gave Long Tianming no opening to introduce himself, deathly afraid that this seemingly brainless "Child of Destiny" would fail to read the room and offend this untouchable ancestor.-"He should be a villager from Long Village, where we are putting up for the night."

["..."](# "Speaker: Long Tianming")

[I haven't even said a word, so how does she know?!]

Long Tianming's eyes widened. A vague feeling of discomfort stirred in his chest; he felt that the label of "villager" lacked prestige, which led him to think this "fair maiden" had rather a big mouth.

Fortunately, Young Master Zhou—that "immortal-second-generation male supporting character"—didn't seem to mind at all, merely chuckling:

—"From Long Village? Then will this esteemed gentleman also be accompanying us on the journey to seek immortality? May I ask which academy you have been admitted to?"

—"Seek immortality? Academy? Admitted?"-Long Tianming repeated blankly.

Strung together, these seemingly unrelated terms caused Long Tianming to temporarily forget about investigating Renran's strange behavior.

Seeing his baffled expression, Zhou Hui also appeared somewhat puzzled:

—"You don't know? But you know spells, so you've naturally embarked on cultivation. At the very least, you must have undergone the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony, haven't you?"

—"Immortality Inquiry Ceremony...?"

[What on earth is that?]

Long Tianming guiltily averted his gaze. For some inexplicable reason, he had the eerie sensation of bumping into school leadership in the hallway and being grilled on why he had skipped an exam. Even though he had no idea who this person was, the back of his neck felt distinctly chilled.

Unfortunately, before Long Tianming could appeal to the Old Grandpa for assistance, Zhou Hui saw right through the youth's guilty conscience, which only magnified his surprise.

—"Wait a moment. Don't tell me you don't even know about the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony?!"-Zhou Hui exclaimed in astonishment.

Renran followed suit, casting a baffled glance his way.

Even the Old Grandpa marveled with immense curiosity:

—"You genuinely didn't know? No wonder! I was wondering why a kid with decent immortal roots like yours hadn't been snapped up already, leaving such a bargain for me to pick up."

["..."](# "Speaker: Long Tianming")

[Is this Immortality Inquiry Ceremony really something everyone has to know about?]

Cold sweat finally beaded on Long Tianming's forehead.

He was consumed by terror, wondering if his identity as a transmigrator had somehow been exposed.

* * *

Several quarters of an hour later, Long Tianming stood woodenly in the courtyard of the ancestral hall.

This was the grandest structure in the village, serving as the communal meeting ground for the villagers.

Only, those gathered at this moment included not just the village chief and clan elders, but also an escort of cultivators with fluttering celestial ribbons. Yet the leader of these cultivators was neither the picture-perfect fairy Renran nor the transcendent Zhou Hui. Instead, it was a dark-complexioned, plain-featured woman; had it not been for the fluttering celestial ribbons adorning her attire, one would almost have mistaken her for a local peasant woman.

At the moment, however, the woman wasn't looking at Long Tianming. She was glaring fiercely at the village chief and clan elders:

—"Folks, this really isn't right of you! Didn't the regulations state that all children in the village must participate in the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony? Why are there still so many boys who didn't attend?!"

Regardless of her rustic appearance, her status as an immortal cultivator remained something ordinary mortals dared not offend.

The village chief merely clutched his long-stemmed tobacco pipe, staring sorrowfully at the dirt ground without uttering a word.

The clan elders, however, muttered amongst themselves in discontent. One of them raised his voice slightly above the rest:

—"What business do boys have cultivating immortality? It's not like they can have spirit roots anyway. Going to the inquiry ceremony only delays their farm chores."

—"What kind of reasoning is that?!"-the woman knitted her brows, glaring toward the clan elders.

One elder stiffened his neck in defiance:

—"Isn't it true? Aren't you all heading to report for registration? How many boys do you even have among you?"

The woman was momentarily left speechless.

What the elder said was indeed the truth. This contingent of cultivators was a reception convoy tasked with collecting young cultivation seedlings who possessed spirit roots. Yet eight out of ten in the squad were young girls, and among the few remaining "boys," one happened to be Zhou Hui!

—"Could it be that in this world, only girls can cultivate immortality?!"-Long Tianming whispered.

He had assumed his secret was blown, but the unfolding situation was nothing like what he had imagined, leaving him thoroughly bewildered.

—"How could that be possible?"-Renran laughed, her tone laced with poorly concealed [gloating schadenfreude](# "Literal CN: 幸灾乐祸 (xìngzāi-lèhuò) — taking pleasure in others' misfortune").-"However, among newly inducted cultivators these days, females truly do make up the vast majority. All of this is because the largest sect in the cultivation world today, the Heavenly Spirit Sect, is somewhat more willing to admit girls!"

As she said this, she deliberately threw a meaningful glance at "Young Master Zhou."

Zhou Hui's expression was somewhat awkward:

—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect possesses no such rule. It was merely that when academies were first promoted back then, people harbored doubts and reservations; hence, the majority of enrolled students were female. The same phenomenon subsequently cropped up in other regions as well, which led to the current state of affairs."

Indeed. Three hundred years was sufficient time for a mortal dynasty to complete an entire cycle of rise, prosperity, decline, and collapse. Yet for cultivators, it was merely the "brief" span between Qi Condensation and Golden Core.

Back then, the populace had been unaccustomed to the cultivation world's educational reforms. Most had treated sending their daughters and female kin to the academies with the mindset of [sacrificing girls to the River God](# "Literal CN: 献祭河神 — ancient superstitious practice of throwing young virgins into rivers to appease water spirits"), reserving the boys who were meant to [carry on the family ancestral line](# "Literal CN: 传宗接代 — patriarchal imperative to bear male heirs") for traditional education. Now, the "dire consequences" had manifested. As early as a century ago, the number of low-tier female cultivators in the cultivation world had already overtaken their male counterparts.

Yet the cultivators with whom mortal commoners had the most contact were precisely these low-tier practitioners in the Qi Condensation and Foundation Establishment realms.

Particularly now, when cultivators intervened in mortal lives far more extensively than in eras past.

When mortals observed that the envoys receiving new recruits were female cultivators, the ones casting spells to teach knowledge were female cultivators, and the ones harvesting spirit herbs and popularizing spiritual seed crops were female cultivators as well, they naturally leaped to the misconception that "cultivation is better suited to women." Consequently, they forfeited the chance for their boys to undergo the Immortality Inquiry, deepening that vicious cycle.

Of course, mortals were loath to part with their households' manual labor force and were all too willing to go along with this misunderstanding, which formed another cog in the vicious cycle.

Zhou Hui had indeed embarked on this journey to inspect the outcomes of the educational reforms—a mere "Child of Destiny" was nowhere near worthy of drawing him away from Mount Tianling—yet plainly, he had hit a snag at his very first stop.

Fortunately, the cultivator tasked with receiving new recruits was quite conscientious.

The woman attempted to reverse the villagers' ignorant prejudice:

—"...Even though our batch is mostly girls, boys have spirit roots too! Look, this... kid from your village, didn't he awaken a spirit root? What a waste it would be to squander talent like that!"

She pointed right at Long Tianming.

Long Tianming instantly became the center of attention.

Unfortunately, the villagers showed no sense of shared pride. Someone even reached out in flustered frustration, attempting to drag Long Tianming away:

—"What does he know?! He's just practicing blind nonsense!"

—"Not at all. Reaching Qi Condensation on his own means he has immense talent,"-the woman grabbed Long Tianming's other arm. Goodness knew what cultivation art she practiced, but it left Long Tianming completely unable to budge.-"Besides, having a child with spirit roots emerge from your village is a blessing for you folks! We already promised: your village will receive an extra twenty percent quota of spiritual crop seeds next year."

—"Immortal Lady, that's not how the math works,"-the village chief countered.-"Spiritual seeds are a matter of a single year, but manual labor is a matter of decades! If the boys all run off to cultivate immortality, who's going to plow our fields?!"

The woman refuted:

—"Times are different now! Cultivating immortality isn't a one-way trip where you never return home. Cultivators in the Qi Condensation and Foundation Establishment realms are required to undergo practical internships! If a few cultivators from your own village return to cast spells and nurture the spiritual fields, won't that boost crop yields even more?"

—"But boys aren't cut out for cultivation. They're clumsy with spells and lack patience and attention to detail. By the time they come back, we might as well have tilled the fields ourselves! Rather than wasting all that effort, they're better off staying in the village..."

—"Why are you circling back to that nonsense again?! It's not like the boys never return..."

...

Pulled between both sides, Long Tianming stood numbly in place as his grand fantasies of plot twists and face-slapping shattered into pieces across the floor.

[What happened to the promised Protagonist storyline?!]

[Why has this suddenly turned into rescuing out-of-school children?!]
