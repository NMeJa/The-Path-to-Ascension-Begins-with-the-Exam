# Chapter 108: [Some Truths](<# "Literal CN: 一些真相 (yīxiē zhēnxiàng) — Some Truths / A Bit of Truth; chapter title revealing the heavenly conspiracy behind the Immortal-Slaying Sword and the hidden eighth reincarnation cycle">) (第108章 一些真相)

[—"How did things ever turn out like this?"](<# "Speaker: Lord Siwu">)

Half an hour before encountering the demon, Lord Siwu was having a full-blown existential crisis.

He felt that ever since arriving in the Central Domain, he had been plagued by non-stop misfortune.

First, at the Immortal Arts Assembly, he had crossed paths with the damnable Untiring Immortal Venerable and was thoroughly made a fool of—Siwu conveniently overlooked that he had been the one to strike first—losing massive face in front of every high-level cultivator in the Central Domain;

Then, after arriving at the Abyss Sea, he was relentlessly harassed and hunted down by a barrage of demon beasts and grotesque creatures the entire way—yet according to others, even in the Abyss Sea, and even during a demon tide, there shouldn't have been such an endless swarm of monsters springing up at every turn;

Next, during a nighttime spiritual energy storm, he was separated from his disciples, leaving only Nie Tongzi by his side;

And finally, the true catalyst that had reduced Siwu, a grand Mahayana cultivator, to his present pathetic state:

Nie Tongzi had actually betrayed him!

At the time, after waiting out the spiritual energy storm with great difficulty and finding a secure refuge, Siwu had instructed Nie Tongzi to stand guard while he divined the whereabouts of his other disciples. Never in his wildest dreams did he imagine that Nie Tongzi would launch a sneak attack while he was wholly engrossed in his calculations. Had the brat not been 'injured' back at the Immortal Arts Assembly, Siwu might have been done for right then and there.

[So why on earth did Untiring Immortal Venerable have to resurrect Nie Tongzi?! He would have been far better off left as a lump of rotten mincemeat!]

Siwu thought in sheer shock and fury.

Yet this was also precisely what puzzled Siwu.

Leaving Nie Tongzi's betrayal aside for a moment, theoretically speaking, Siwu's luck shouldn't have been so wretched.

Because strictly speaking, Siwu was a [Favored of the Immortals](<# "Literal CN: 仙眷者 (xiānjuànzhě) — Favored of the Immortals; practitioners who cultivate by receiving direct empowerment, patronage, and oracles from True Immortals via sacrificial rites">).

Although Siwu specialized in sorcery and could also divine the past and future—sounding somewhat similar to Qu Qianxing—unlike Qu Qianxing, who relied on personally observing stellar spiritual power to cast spells and calculate destiny, Siwu walked the path of offering sacrifices to True Immortals and deriving his power directly from them.

Anyone favored by a True Immortal, even a mere mortal, could instantly obtain immense power.

In the ancient cultivation world, this was an orthodox and prestigious path to ascension.

It was only after the Path to Ascension was severed and True Immortals could rarely interfere in the mortal realm that the number of such 'Favored of the Immortals' dwindled to near zero. Only someone with a unique constitution like Siwu could ever be chosen—an exception among rare exceptions.

Since Siwu enjoyed the grace of True Immortals, served as the chief official of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ritual Court, and oversaw the Grand Heaven-Worshipping Ceremony, he naturally possessed immense karmic fortune.

When someone like him ventured out, he naturally wouldn't act rashly like ordinary cultivators.

Upon receiving intelligence from the Central Domain, Siwu had bathed, changed into pristine ceremonial robes, and petitioned the Upper Realm for divine guidance. Only after receiving the cosmic response of 'Supreme Auspiciousness' did he begin mapping out his itinerary.

His exact route of travel had likewise been cleared with the heavens.

Therefore, heading first to Cloud City for the Immortal Arts Assembly before deliberating whether to visit the Heavenly Spirit Sect was likewise 'heaven's explicit instruction.'

In fact, when Siwu petitioned heaven, he had already noticed that the deities above seemed rather averse to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Every divined route advised detours, forbidding him from traveling to the northeast of the Central Domain too quickly, and certainly prohibiting an early visit to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. The Immortal Arts Assembly, however, was permitted—though an immortal oracle had indicated it would be best not to let it proceed smoothly, as doing so would compromise his entire journey.

It was precisely because of this that Siwu had made his little 'underhanded moves' at the Immortal Arts Assembly.

[Could it be that my interference with the Immortal Arts Assembly brought about all this subsequent bad luck?]

[But I was acting strictly according to the immortal oracle!]

Siwu thought.

Unwittingly, he had stumbled upon the exact truth.

Yet a moment later, Siwu shook his head:—"No, that's impossible. Wouldn't that imply Untiring's power surpasses that of True Immortals? Besides, I've never heard any rumors claiming Untiring is skilled at manipulating karmic fortune. Even if I had the rotten luck to bump into him at the assembly, it shouldn't be that he altered my karmic destiny, leaving me so wretched after reaching the Abyss Sea with monster attacks and spiritual energy storms one after another."-Muttering to himself, he gave his sleeve an irritated flick.

A few pastry crumbs tainted with the aura of an ancient ferocious beast clung tenaciously to the hem of his sleeve, virtually fusing with the fabric.

Siwu didn't notice in the slightest.

Gripping his sleeve, he ground his teeth:—"What's more, Nie Tongzi's actions certainly weren't influenced by karmic luck! That treacherous disciple plotted this long ago!"

Muttering under his breath, Siwu looked down at the blood-crimson longsword in his hand.

This was the *[Immortal-Slaying Sword](<# "Literal CN: 弑仙剑 (Shìxiān Jiàn) — Immortal-Slaying Sword; ancient God-Demon War relic engineered to slaughter True Immortals through stepped cultivator blood sacrifices">)*.

As the name implied, it was a sword capable of slaying True Immortals.

Forged during the ancient God-Demon War, some believed it was crafted by the Demon race, while others claimed it was forged by the final Sacred Lord of White Jade Capital. In either case, so long as one held this blade, even a mere mortal could decapitate a True Immortal.

Fortunately, this blade had an 'activation condition':

Although it could slay a True Immortal, it required stepped sacrifices following the cultivation realms in strict ascension: Qi Condensation, Foundation Establishment, Golden Core, Nascent Soul, Soul Formation, Void Refinement, Body Integration, Mahayana, and Tribulation Transcendence. Only by sacrificing cultivators of every prior stage could it ultimately slay a True Immortal.

When the Heavenly Dao Alliance recovered the sword back then, it had not yet slain any cultivator above Golden Core; thus, it couldn't even kill a Nascent Soul expert.

Naturally, the Heavenly Dao Alliance could not permit such a sinister weapon to continue upgrading. They immediately ordered the Chief of the Ritual Court of that era to seal the blade, storing it within the highest-level restrictive barrier atop the Celestial City, where theoretically no one could ever reach it.

Who could have guessed that Nie Tongzi would somehow lay hands on this sword, and even upgrade it to the point where it could slaughter a Mahayana grandmaster?

Earlier, it was with this very sword that Nie Tongzi had dealt Siwu a grievous wound.

—"Ungrateful wretch!"-Siwu hissed in fury.

He had every reason to be enraged.

Years ago, Nie Tongzi was hunted down for practicing demonic arts. Among his pursuers was an aristocratic young master from the Celestial City who brought this half-demon back as a 'trophy' to 'play with.' Siwu happened upon the scene. Having just received an immortal oracle foretelling that he would acquire a demon-cultivating disciple to assist him in exploring the Abyss Sea—an expedition Siwu believed was this very journey—he rescued Nie Tongzi and took him in as a disciple.

Siwu considered himself to have treated Nie Tongzi exceedingly well, never anticipating he would receive such 'recompense.'

[Doesn't this prove 'yet again' that the immortal oracle was mistaken...? No, perhaps I simply picked the wrong disciple?]

Siwu pondered to himself.

Then he froze in dread, hurriedly offering a reverent bow toward the heavens.

As a Favored of the Immortals, Siwu was the last person who could ever doubt heaven. Doing so would destabilize his very cultivation—especially now, while severely wounded by the Immortal-Slaying Sword!

[No, stop thinking. Lingering in a place like this is corrupting my mind.]

Shaking his head, Siwu stowed the Immortal-Slaying Sword and trudged forward with sluggish steps.

In truth, he didn't even know where he was going.

During the spiritual energy storm, Siwu had lost far more than his disciples—he had lost his supplies, and most importantly, the navigational artifact provided by East Hedge Garden. Only with that artifact could one find the hidden sanctum where East Hedge Garden resided.

That was why Siwu had been so desperate to locate his other disciples.

He had never expected Nie Tongzi to strike a vicious blow precisely when he needed to concentrate his spiritual focus on divination.

Yet as a Mahayana cultivator, Siwu was not completely useless. Furthermore, even while wielding the Immortal-Slaying Sword, if the wielder's own cultivation fell short, their strike was riddled with fatal flaws—which was precisely why Nie Tongzi had to wait until Siwu was absorbed in divination before daring to strike. Thus, while Siwu had been grievously wounded by the Immortal-Slaying Sword, he had managed to evade vital points and strike Nie Tongzi down in retaliation.

However, the wound had already been dealt. Siwu's power was significantly impaired, and he dared not attempt further divination in such a perilous environment, lest demonic beasts drawn by his spiritual emanations swarm him. He had no choice but to hastily divine a general heading and shuffle along in that direction.

This proved that deep down in his subconscious, Siwu still possessed unshakeable faith in 'heaven.'

Although logical deduction told him something was amiss, when choosing a path, he still surrendered his direction to the gods and True Immortals, firmly believing they would guide him toward the most favorable outcome.

Such was Siwu's accumulated 'life experience' over the past several millennia.

He had never been in such a pathetic state in his life. Whenever he had lost his way in the past, he had always returned to the right path under 'heaven's' divine guidance. At the very least, he would stumble upon safe water sources, sheltered resting spots, or even serendipitous encounters in the wilderness.

Siwu held fast to this conviction.

Until half an hour later, when a colossal demon materialized right before his eyes.

*

—"...This is no ordinary demon,"-Zhou Hui stated with certainty.

Having researched and even reared a host of demonic creatures, he possessed considerable expertise regarding these 'organisms.'

According to Zhou Hui's classifications, demons fell into three broad lineages: God-descended, Mortal/Immortal-descended, and Beast-descended.

'God-descended' did not imply demonic creatures carried the actual bloodline of gods; rather, Zhou Hui posited that like nature gods and ancient ferocious beasts, they spontaneously manifested from metaphysical environments of terror, darkness, and malice. They were intrinsic parts of nature and generally the most formidable variant—or, in the worst-case scenario, perhaps they were the dark flip-side of the gods themselves. Unfortunately, due to a dearth of research specimens, Zhou Hui could not say for sure. As for the Mortal/Immortal-descended and Beast-descended variants, they were transformed from humans and beasts, making them comparatively weaker, though some could elevate into Heavenly Demons much like humans ascending to godhood.

Regardless of lineage, the paramount hallmark of all demons was their virulent capacity for emotional pollution.

Merely lingering in the vicinity of a demon would cause even the most composed person to become manic, anxious, distraught, and suspicious. Their base instinctual desires would swell uncontrollably, driving them toward ruthless extremes to satiate their cravings.

Even demons themselves were held hostage by this chaotic emotional turbulence.

Setting aside humans and beasts who cultivated demonic arts—whose descent into bloodthirst and slaughter was glaringly obvious—even the naturally occurring Extraterrestrial Heavenly Demons of the God-descended variety exhibited deeply chaotic emotional expressions, easily seduced by desires and stripped of all reason.

The emotional pollution they generated was inherently irrational, chaotic, and insoluble.

That sensation of one's own feelings slipping from conscious control left an indelible impression on anyone who crossed paths with a demon even once—yet, much like alcohol, it exerted an intoxicating, irresistible pull.

Yet the demon that had surfaced here in the Abyss Sea was entirely different.

The emotional pollution it radiated was just as intense and nauseating, yet it carried an artificiality that made one alert and wary far more easily—

Yuwen Xuan nailed it with an exquisite description:—"This demon feels like [counterfeit alcohol](<# "Literal CN: 假酒 (jiǎjiǔ) — fake liquor / counterfeit alcohol; Yuwen Xuan's vivid analogy for an artificial, adulterated demon lacking authentic metaphysical resonance">)."

Precisely: the stench of cheap, watered-down moonshine.

Zhou Hui nodded in agreement. A second later, realizing something was amiss, he flicked the little black lamb's forehead:—"Children aren't allowed to drink."

—"I'm not a child anymore, *whimper*!"-the little black lamb bleated.

By now, a terrible realization was dawning upon him: his two hundred years of life seemed to have amounted to absolutely nothing.

He had regressed right back to his childhood days when Master forbade him from touching a drop of wine.

And this time, the drinking ban was going to last even longer. At the thought of this, entirely independent of his currently frail lamb tear ducts, Yuwen Xuan felt genuine, heartfelt tears welling up.

[Still, it shouldn't be too late to salvage the situation, right?]

[Whatever happens, don't let Master dwell on this!]

Scheming frantically, the little black lamb attempted to change the subject—or rather, steer the topic back onto the right track:—"Master, that so-called Mahayana grandmaster looks heavily wounded and terribly weak. Are we going to save him?"

He knew his master would certainly step in.

Although Siwu had pulled some foolish stunts at the Immortal Arts Assembly, in Zhou Hui's eyes, it clearly hadn't warranted a death sentence. Furthermore, Zhou Hui had already taught the man a personal lesson. Witnessing him in mortal peril now, Zhou Hui would definitely not stand by and let him perish.

That was precisely why Zhou Hui had been so furious at his eldest disciple's conduct:

Setting aside petty offenses like sneaking around and entrapment schemes, the black lamb's actions theoretically posed varying degrees of lethal danger to both Siwu and the Taotie remnant soul. To put it bluntly, it bordered on premeditated murder.

Fortunately, it had been [foiled](<# "Literal CN: 未遂 (wèisuì) — unconsummated / attempted (unsuccessful) / foiled; referring to Yuwen Xuan's botched plot to feed his other soul-half to Lord Siwu, caught red-handed by Zhou Hui">).

Yet matters down below were escalating fast.

Yuwen Xuan tensely spied on the battle raging below.

While he and his master were leisurely debating whether the demon was authentic or fake, Siwu, who had run headlong into the beast, had no luxury of academic research. He had clashed with the demon right away.

Regrettably, burdened by severe injuries, Siwu held zero advantage and could only fight while retreating.

Meanwhile, the little white lamb had clearly been startled by the demon's sudden appearance as well. Demons were hardly suitable food for ferocious beasts; so long as other fare was available, a Taotie would never stoop to eating demons. Consequently, it hadn't recklessly attacked either side, opting instead to skulk in the shadows, waiting for its moment.

Evidently, it was waiting for Siwu to suffer further injury.

If Siwu were battered again by the demon or exhausted more stamina, it would seize the grandmaster's weakest moment to pounce and swallow him whole.

[This bastard is far craftier than I expected! I thought as a mere remnant soul it would have no brains at all!] the little black lamb thought grumpily.

The little black lamb shot a worried glance at Zhou Hui.

He didn't want Master to notice that wretched white lamb.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui had no time to mind either Taotie for the moment. He was observing the clash between the demon and Siwu, showing no immediate intention of interfering.

—"There's no need to intervene. He's a Mahayana cultivator after all; he should be able to handle it himself,"-Zhou Hui remarked.

The little black lamb, on the other hand, secretly wished Zhou Hui would go save Siwu. That way, the white lamb wouldn't have any opening to launch an ambush right before their eyes, and perhaps his own post-incident punishment might be mitigated.

Putting on an air of deep concern, the little black lamb looked toward Siwu:—"Really? He looks dreadfully battered to me."

—"Probably because he sustained some injuries, though his core foundation remains intact,"-Zhou Hui observed Siwu closely.-"Though what on earth is going on with the karmic fortune around him? It looks utterly abysmal..."

Having said that, Zhou Hui turned his gaze toward the little black lamb.

The little black lamb innocently shifted its gaze away.

After all, this beast was an ancient calamity. Anyone tainted by it suffered a ruinous drop in luck—not to mention here, where the dosage was double.

When one's own disciple stirred up trouble, what else could a master do? He simply had to clean up the mess.

Zhou Hui let out a sigh.

Raising his hand, he made a beckoning gesture toward the miniature 'model' of White Jade Capital. Instantly, the pure azure karmic fortune began to swirl and surge toward the 'pond,' pouring into the Abyss Sea in a boundless torrent straight toward Siwu.

Siwu's strikes became visibly sharper and cleaner.

Or to put it in gamer terms: his [critical strike rate and critical damage](<# "Literal CN: 暴击暴伤 (bàojī bàoshāng) — crit rate and crit damage; modern gaming terminology humorously applied to karmic buffs enhancing martial combat efficiency">) had skyrocketed.

—"Huh?"-Zhou Hui let out a soft exclamation of surprise.

Before his very eyes, a full third of the azure karmic fortune blanketing White Jade Capital vanished in an instant.

Although it didn't seem like much on the 'model,' that immortal city was a real entity anchored somewhere in the Abyss Sea! That was enough providential fortune to shroud an entire metropolis!

While fresh karmic fortune continuously flowed in to replenish it, Siwu's rate of consumption was astronomical—he was practically a [karmic black hole](<# "Literal CN: 气运黑洞 (qìyùn hēidòng) — karmic black hole; an entity that insatiably swallows astronomical amounts of cosmic destiny and providential fortune">).

A few crumbs of bone-ash pancake tainted with a ferocious beast's aura shouldn't have drained his destiny to such an absurd degree!

Especially considering those ashes didn't even belong to the Taotie itself, but to a human!

Zhou Hui glared down at the little black lamb:—"What else did you do?"

The little black lamb shook its head like a rattle-drum:—"I only stuck a few pastry crumbs and tossed a white lamb down there, I didn't do a single other thing...! Could it be that he's burning through so much karmic fortune because he's a [Sacrificial Vessel](<# "Literal CN: 祀体 (sìtǐ) — sacrificial vessel / ritual offering body; human or beast selected as ritual blood food and vessel for deities or ferocious beasts">)?"

—"A Sacrificial Vessel?"-Zhou Hui paused in surprise.

Browsing the Taotie's hereditary memories, he swiftly located the entry for 'Sacrificial Vessel'—it appeared to refer to living blood-food bred specifically to be fed to a Taotie.

The look Zhou Hui directed at Yuwen Xuan grew even sterner.

—"...Master, don't look at me like that! That's how a Taotie operates, but I'm not like that!"-Yuwen Xuan knew how to distance himself when it counted.-"Even if you don't believe me, you have to believe in yourself! Believe in the humanity you instilled in me over two hundred years!"

—"I'm not terribly convinced,"-Zhou Hui replied, gazing toward the white lamb in the distance.

Zhou Hui certainly didn't consider himself a successful master.

Had his teaching been truly exemplary, to say nothing of the absurd world dictated by the Heavenly Dao's script, the sheer chaos of the third and fourth reincarnation cycles would never have occurred. Zhou Hui had never intended to raise his disciples into giant overgrown babies incapable of functioning without their master.

—"*Whimper*..."-the little black lamb uttered a pitiful, aggrieved squeak.

Sniffling and grumbling, it muttered:—"Fine, call it a Taotie then! But honestly, Taoties are relatively straightforward—once a Taotie eats, the matter is settled. Some folks play a far dirtier game: they appoint their Sacrificial Vessels as high priests, granting them power on the surface, but in reality using them as living bait to harvest even more sacrifices."

[—"...Are you saying Siwu's current state was orchestrated by the True Immortals worshipped by the Ritual Court?"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

—"Who knows? In any case, this bottomless drain of karmic fortune wasn't my doing,"-the little black lamb firmly denied.

Together with Zhou Hui, it looked toward Siwu once more.

Having received Zhou Hui's providential support, Siwu had regained the strength expected of his station. With a fierce flick of his hand, he sent the mass of demonic corruption crashing into the distance.

Only now did Siwu find the breathing room to summon his lifebound magical treasure:

A [Sinan](<# "Literal CN: 司南 (sīnán) — Sinan; ancient Chinese south-pointing spoon-and-plate compass, here refined as Siwu's lifebound divination treasure">) ladle-compass.

He had spent nine hundred and ninety-nine years gathering nine hundred and ninety-nine distinct rare materials, refining it under the personal guidance of True Immortals. Because it was so exceedingly precious, he rarely deployed it as a weapon under ordinary circumstances—and Siwu was unaccustomed to actual combat anyway. After all, he had spent most of his life pampered within the Celestial City; there was never any reason for a high priest to fight on the front lines.

Confronted by this demon now, Siwu had only remembered it when seeking to deliver the finishing blow.

—"Die!"-Siwu barked.

Upon his command, the bronze spoon atop the Sinan plate spun with frantic velocity.

A cold smirk appeared on Siwu's lips as he awaited the devastating strike of his lifebound treasure. But suddenly, a wave of paralysis seized his body, and his cultivation drained away like water cascading through a breached dam.

[What's happening?!]

Siwu's eyes widened in horror. To his utter shock, the spoon of the Sinan was pointing straight at himself!

[How could this be?!]

[Why would my own lifebound magical treasure attack me?!]

Siwu thought in utter bewilderment.

Unfortunately, this was hardly the time or place for philosophical contemplation. In that single instant of hesitation, the demon surged forward once more, pinning the powerless, magic-drained Siwu flat against the ground.

Curiously, the demon did not immediately maul him. Instead, something stirred violently within his storage pouch, bursting free in a sudden flash to slash viciously down toward him—

Ah... it was the Immortal-Slaying Sword!

The crimson sword-light was mirrored in Siwu's dilated pupils.

In this split second, the shattered fragments of clues snapped together into a coherent whole within his mind:

The divine oracle that diverged so strangely from reality;

The sudden, inexplicable spiritual energy storm;

Nie Tongzi's treacherous ambush;

The demon that seemed to have been lying in wait right here;

Had everything, from beginning to end, been meticulously calculated all along?

Was he merely the designated blood sacrifice for this sword?

And once it had slaughtered a Mahayana cultivator, for whom was this blade being prepared?

Above Mahayana... lay Tribulation Transcendence!

Siwu's thoughts spiraled in chaotic terror.

His eyes remained wide, unwilling to close in bitter resentment, yet beyond that, he made no further move to resist.

For Siwu understood all too clearly: if the entity orchestrating his demise was the very 'deity above' he had worshipped for thousands of years, there was no room for resistance whatsoever—and not a single soul could ever save him.

How could a mere mortal ever defy a god—?

*Clang!*

At the razor-thin precipice between life and death, both the Immortal-Slaying Sword and the Sinan compass were blasted away by a colossal, irresistible force.

A flash of pristine white abruptly entered Siwu's vision.

Radiating a luminous, bluish-white moonlight, he illuminated every corner of Siwu's surroundings.

Under the bathing of this sacred radiance, the demonic creature let out a despairing shriek. Without even the power to muster basic resistance, it began to melt away, disintegrating into a puddle of foul, crimson blood almost instantaneously.

Just then, a fluffy white blob dashed over and swallowed the pool of blood in a single gulp.

[What in the world is that?]

Siwu had no time to ponder such things. Staring blankly at the white-robed silhouette that had delivered him from death, he heard his own trembling voice cry out:

—"Untiring Immortal...?"

[—"'Master'?!"](<# "Speaker: White Lamb / Taotie Remnant Soul">)

Zhou Hui turned his head, just about to speak to Siwu, only to notice an exceedingly bizarre expression wash over Siwu's face.

Before he could register what was happening, something suddenly clamped around him in a tight hug.

Perplexed, Zhou Hui looked down, only to discover that the person wrapping arms around him was his 'eldest disciple'—completely naked.

'Yuwen Xuan' couldn't have cared less about his appearance, asking with ecstatic joy:—"You're 'Master', right?"

—"What kind of madness are you having...?!"-[Wait, this isn't Xuan'er!]-Zhou Hui realized a beat too late.

Right at that moment, a chubby, round 'black cloud' swooped down from the air:—"You bastard, let go of my Master!"

The young man simply raised a hand and swatted the black cloud away like a fly.

The black lamb tumbled across the ground for several revolutions before crashing against a nearby boulder. That happened to be the very rock where the white lamb had been hiding moments ago—yet now, the white lamb was 'nowhere to be seen.'

No, it wasn't that its whereabouts were unknown.

Zhou Hui looked once more at the 'Yuwen Xuan' clinging to his waist.

—"'Master', 'Master'! 'Master'! I've wanted to meet you for so long!"-'Yuwen Xuan' proclaimed gleefully. He seemed exceedingly fond of the title 'Master,' repeating it several times before continuing his enthusiastic confession:—"Every single time, 'I' kept babbling about you non-stop, longing for you across seven whole reincarnation cycles! It made me want to meet you so badly too, and this time, I've finally seen the 'real deal'!"

—"Let go... Seven cycles?!"-Zhou Hui had initially intended to order him to release him, but upon clearly hearing what the youth said, he asked in confusion:—"Why seven cycles? Isn't that one too many?"

[After all, this current life is only the Seventh Life; the previous ones added together only amount to six, don't they?]

[Strange. When I examined this child's memories last time, he clearly had no recollection of the past reincarnations.]

[Did devouring that bizarre substance just now jog his memory?]

Zhou Hui thought.

Meanwhile, 'Yuwen Xuan'... no, the white lamb, counted on his fingers and affirmed with absolute certainty:—"It's seven cycles! This lifetime is the [Eighth Life](<# "Literal CN: 第八世 (dì bā shì) — Eighth Life / Eighth Cycle; the shocking revelation from the Taotie remnant soul that the current timeline is actually the eighth reincarnation, contradicting Zhou Hui's Seven Lives Transmigration count">)."
