# Chapter 112: [Innate Advantage](<# "Literal CN: 先天优势 (xiāntiān yōushì) — Innate Advantage / Natural Advantages; chapter title">) (第112章 先天优势)

—"Shui Huang'er, maiden of the Southern Domain's Fire Abyss Sacred Tree clan, pays her respects to the Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Shui Huang'er said with deep reverence.

Upon laying eyes on Zhou Hui, she shed every trace of her earlier lively, frivolous demeanor, solemnly performing a [complete grand demon salute](<# "Literal CN: 妖族大礼 (yāozú dàlǐ) — formal grand etiquette / salute of the demon race">). In that instant, she genuinely looked like a dignified Saintess.

Seeing this, Qu Qianxing was slightly astonished.

He held something of a stereotype toward demons, half-expecting this demon maiden to maintain her previous irreverent antics even before Master!

What Qu Qianxing didn't realize was that at this very moment, Shui Huang'er was also privately regretting having been so terribly casual.

She felt she shouldn't have made the impulsive decision to visit the Heavenly Spirit Sect on a spur-of-the-moment whim. Instead, she ought to have returned to the Southern Domain first—or at least contacted her clan so they could dispatch an official retinue of vassals and attendants to accompany her, which would have appeared far more dignified and decorous.

Yet who could have anticipated that the Untiring Immortal Venerable would genuinely grant her an audience?

And in the dead of night, no less.

Based on Shui Huang'er's understanding of humanity, even if the Heavenly Spirit Sect agreed to sell their cultivation methods (textbooks), the Untiring Immortal Venerable wouldn't necessarily deign to meet her.

At least, that was how Carefree Palace operated.

Carefree Palace was considered a vital commercial partner of the demon race.

They operated the primary trade routes in the Southern Domain and maintained substantial business transactions with the demons. Yet, if Shui Huang'er simply flew over to the Eastern Domain for leisure rather than conducting an official state visit, expecting to easily secure an audience with Carefree Palace's core Tribulation Transcendence patriarchs would be practically out of the question.

Human cultivators, after all, regarded demons as [heretical fiends and evil deviants](<# "Literal CN: 邪魔外道 (xiémó wàidào) — evil demons and unorthodox heretics">).

Yet demons had their own pride.

From the demons' perspective, humans and demons were equals. As the Demon Saintess, Shui Huang'er represented the entire demon race. No matter how immense Carefree Palace's influence grew, it remained merely a single human sect. For the Saintess to grace Carefree Palace with a visit was already [condescending to lower herself](<# "Literal CN: 屈尊纡贵 (qūzūn yūguì) — condescending to lower oneself; bestowing one's presence upon social inferiors">)—what right did you humans have to put on haughty airs?

Never mind Carefree Palace—many demons believed that even the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City couldn't truly represent humanity. After all, the Heavenly Dao Alliance only held genuine authority in the Northern Domain. In other regions, the Eastern Domain had Carefree Palace, the Western Domain had Other Shore Heaven, the Southern Domain was co-inhabited by demons and humans, and the Central Domain was an utter mess. In the eyes of the demons, this was equivalent to several Demon Kings carving up the realm among themselves without a single sovereign Demon Emperor whose word was law—[bound to go down in pills](<# "Literal CN: 迟早药丸 (chízǎo yàowán) — internet pun on 迟早要完 (bound to be finished / doomed to collapse), swapping 要完 (doomed) with 药丸 (pills/medicine)">).

Furthermore, the demon race boasted countless long-lived species. Elders like Tong Ling still remembered the glorious era of White Jade Capital, looking down on contemporary humanity with even greater disdain.

While Shui Huang'er didn't harbor such prejudices herself, she was well aware of humanity's general attitude toward demons. Consequently, she had no intention of [throwing herself at them](<# "Literal CN: 倒贴 (dàotiē) — unrequited courting; giving away money or effort without reciprocation / humbling oneself for a partner who looks down on you">), merely planning to treat this as an 'ordinary transaction' much like her dealings with Carefree Palace.

Who would have thought that the Untiring Immortal Venerable would treat the matter with such gravitas?

At first, Shui Huang'er had assumed this was entirely out of consideration for Mo Bei, but his demeanor clearly went far beyond mere politeness to a disciple's acquaintance.

Moreover, Shui Huang'er's perception of the Untiring Immortal Venerable was undergoing a drastic shift.

Before arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, she had viewed him simply as a prominent human cultivator. Even if he was a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster, as a Demon Saintess to whom even Demon Kings were obligated to bow, Shui Huang'er hadn't really cared that much.

Yet the Untiring Immortal Venerable was entirely different!

He wasn't merely a human grandmaster—he was an Immortal Venerable capable of educating a Taotie and a True Dragon!

What's more, a ferocious Taotie beast would hang himself from a tree over mistakes, and a True Dragon would shiver in terror over exams!

It appeared that producing a remarkable yin spirit like Mo Bei was by no means a fluke.

If Shui Huang'er had initially possessed sixty percent confidence in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks, thinking them merely commendable human materials worth importing, that confidence had now soared straight to one hundred percent.

She was more convinced than ever that she had to procure this curriculum.

And because of that, she felt all the more vexed at how flippantly she had presented herself.

[What if the Untiring Immortal Venerable sees me behaving so casually and refuses to provide the textbooks? To be frank, beyond the textbooks, I even wanted to ask whether the Heavenly Spirit Sect's core cultivation manual could be shared! After all, that's a technique capable of instructing dragons and Taoties!]

Shui Huang'er thought anxiously.

Seeing Shui Huang'er's deferential posture, someone suddenly chimed in to question her:

[—"Wait a moment. You've come in your official capacity as the Demon Saintess?"](<# "Speaker: Ancestor Jiuzhai">)

[—"I have."](<# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er">)

[Why does the Untiring Immortal Venerable's voice sound so decrepit and aged? And weirdly familiar?]

Shui Huang'er thought in bewilderment as she lifted her eyes.

She only now noticed another figure standing beside that moonlit, pristine presence:

—"Ah, Lord Jiuzhai?!"-Shui Huang'er gasped.

Residing together in the Southern Domain, Shui Huang'er naturally recognized this pinnacle cultivator of humanity.

In truth, Ancestor Jiuzhai commanded immense respect across the Southern Domain from humans and demons alike. After all, who wouldn't respect a fortune-teller who remained detached from territorial squabbles and possessed the mystical ability to 'glimpse' the future?

Well, except for the times he [spoke in cryptic riddles](<# "Literal CN: 谜语人 (mǐyǔ rén) — 'Riddler'; slang for someone who speaks in frustratingly vague, opaque riddles instead of giving straight answers">)—in those moments, countless people (and demons) harbored a fierce urge to murder him.

Still, the fact that Ancestor Jiuzhai had actually shown up at the Heavenly Spirit Sect elevated her estimation of Zhou Hui another notch.

Particularly because, though both were Tribulation Transcendence cultivators, the Untiring Immortal Venerable looked like the luminous full moon, whereas Ancestor Jiuzhai looked like the dark side of the moon—making Zhou Hui appear all the more radiant by comparison.

—"What's that look supposed to mean? How rude!"-Jiuzhai snapped. He could perceive not only the future, but the present as well.

A single glance at Shui Huang'er was enough to tell him exactly what this [looks-obsessed](<# "Literal CN: 颜控 (yánkòng) — appearance-obsessed / face-con; someone who prioritizes physical beauty above all else">) azure chick was thinking.

Jiuzhai made it clear he couldn't be bothered to bicker with a bratty girl:

—"Where is Tong Ling? Have him step out and speak!"-Jiuzhai demanded.

As he spoke, he raised his hand and slapped a talisman directly onto the shadow trailing behind Shui Huang'er.

The talisman instantly ignited into flames, catching Shui Huang'er's shadow on fire. Yet Shui Huang'er wore a look of complete nonchalance, merely fluttering her tail feathers. A cerulean ember slipped from her tail, tingeing the flames an ethereal azure.

As the azure flames burned down, the shadow morphed into the silhouette of a tree, shakily swaying upright.

It still waved a [bough](<# "Literal CN: 树枝 (shùzhī) — tree branch / bough; used as the Wood Spirit elder's arm">) in greeting toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable first:

—"A pleasure to meet you for the first time, Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Tong Ling greeted.

—"Excellency Tong Ling, I presume? I have long heard of your renown,"-Zhou Hui replied with a gentle smile.

—"Don't ignore me!"-Jiuzhai cried in agitation.

Seeing Shui Huang'er's formal decorum, he was already ninety percent certain that the cosmic karmic shifts across the Southern Domain were tied directly to this Demon Saintess.

He interrogated Tong Ling:

—"Since you're visiting in the capacity of the Demon Saintess, do you plan to promote Zhou Hui's textbooks across the entire demon race?"-Jiuzhai demanded.

—"Is there anything wrong with that?"-Tong Ling countered.-"After all, these are textbooks capable of instructing a Taotie, a True Dragon, and a yin spirit."

On this point, even without prior consultation with Shui Huang'er, the two had reached instant consensus.

—"Those are human textbooks! Compiled by a fellow like Zhou Hui, no less! Do you have any idea that doing this will alter the Southern Domain's... alter your demon race's karmic fortune?!"-Jiuzhai exclaimed.

—"It can alter the karmic fortune of the demon race?!"-Shui Huang'er asked, her eyes lighting up in sheer excitement.

Jiuzhai stared at Shui Huang'er's expression, struck completely speechless.

Tao Zhu, who was casually enjoying the spectacle from the side, asked in puzzlement:

—"Jiuzhai, why are you fretting so frantically over a shift in karmic fortune? Could a change in fortune really destroy heaven and earth?"-Tao Zhu asked.

Although he had initially found the seismic upheaval of the Southern Domain's karmic fortune terrifying, after learning what Zhou Hui had done, Tao Zhu suddenly felt karmic fluctuations were trivial.

Standing right here was a ruthless madman who, if dissatisfied, would [wash the cultivation world clean six whole times](<# "Literal CN: 洗了六遍 (xǐle liù biàn) — washed six times; Jiuzhai's chilling cosmological term for resetting the world and reversing time across six cycles">)!

He'd heard that if the world were washed again, existence itself might cease to be. Compared to that, wouldn't it be far better to let him have his way?

Jiuzhai, however, didn't see it that way.

For Ancestor Jiuzhai, an entity who had always been able to 'see' the present, past, and future, such a violent disruption in cosmic fortune meant he was rendered completely blind to what lay ahead. It was as though a sighted person had suddenly lost their eyes.

Naturally, Jiuzhai couldn't bear it.

—"Karmic shifts of this magnitude are rarely benign,"-Jiuzhai said, eyes darting as he tried to dissuade both sides.-"Don't be fooled because it looks like good fortune right now. Great fortune is exceptionally difficult to control. If the eventual outcome diverges from your intentions, that fortune will instantly backfire, mutating into an unprecedented catastrophe."

Saying this, he cast a pointed look at Shui Huang'er and Tong Ling:

—"It is extraordinarily difficult."

At this, Shui Huang'er pouted, while Tong Ling let out a cold huff.

Watching this, Zhou Hui felt caught between laughter and tears:

—"Why do I get the impression... you hold a prejudice against demons?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"It's not prejudice, it's objective fact,"-Ancestor Jiuzhai retorted.-"Fine, I'll be blunt: there are certain species among the demon race that simply cannot understand human speech! If you want to educate and civilize them, you might as well try reforming the demons of the Demonic Realm!"

—"Isn't humanity the same? There will always be children who simply refuse to absorb knowledge, no matter what,"-Zhou Hui remarked dismissively.

After all, the creature currently hanging from the peach tree outside stood as living proof of his pedagogical shortcomings.

Unaware of Zhou Hui's internal lament, Jiuzhai glared:

—"Is the issue whether they absorb it? The issue is that they refuse to even try learning! Believe me, even if Her Excellency Saintess takes the textbooks back, the only ones willing to study will be the few tribes closest to the Azure Phoenix clan. The rest of the demons probably won't even spare them a glance! Isn't that right?"-he directed his final question at Shui Huang'er.

—"I will do everything in my power to promote them,"-Shui Huang'er pledged.-"Since you said this is tied to karmic fortune, doesn't that prove there is immense potential here? Cosmic fortune is on our side."

Yet this was tantamount to tacitly acknowledging Jiuzhai's words.

There was no helping it; the internal hierarchy of the demon race was far too intricate. Even as the supreme Saintess, she could not command absolute obedience from every beast clan. Shui Huang'er could hardly guarantee that every single demon would sit down and study diligently.

Frankly, Shui Huang'er hadn't even planned to take things to such an extreme at the start!

She truly had been too rash.

She should have arrived fully prepared.

[Who would have guessed Lord Jiuzhai would turn out to be the obstacle?]

Shui Huang'er thought anxiously, casting a worried glance toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable, terrified he might decide against bestowing the curriculum.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui remained completely unmoved by Jiuzhai's warnings.

If anything, Zhou Hui was even more eager than Shui Huang'er.

He required vastly more karmic fortune—especially after discovering that the '[Heavenly Dao script](<# "Literal CN: 天道脚本 (tiāndào jiǎoběn) — the predetermined harem cultivation narrative script staged in the sixth cycle">)' he had long assumed to be mere fiction was actually history that had truly occurred in the past.

Zhou Hui did not believe that just because that story had played out, the matter was settled.

Judging by his experiences in this current life, certain events were still unfolding along predetermined trajectories. Rather than a concluded tale, the lifetime his disciples had experienced was akin to a 'rehearsal.' Quite possibly, someone had staged a simulation of his disciples' actions and reactions while systematically excluding Zhou Hui's interference.

And wherever there was a rehearsal, an actual 'live battle' would follow.

Having run through six (or seven) cycles of reincarnation, the hidden orchestrator had undoubtedly amassed sufficient data. Perhaps they were currently plotting how to rectify the 'errors' of the sixth cycle to polish the Heavenly Dao script to flawless perfection!

Yet Zhou Hui was already bound to White Jade Capital and could no longer ascend. He could not ascend to the True Immortal Realm or the Immortal-Demonic Realm to drag the culprit out and beat them to a pulp, nor could he directly alter the mechanics of the Heavenly Dao.

If he wished to defy heavenly destiny within this mortal realm, astronomical cosmic fortune was an absolute prerequisite.

—One needed only to look at Lord Siwu: merely rescuing him from the demons in the Abyss Sea had incinerated a colossal chunk of fortune in a single stroke. To resist that rehearsed destiny would demand an immeasurably vaster reserve.

Now that the demons had delivered themselves right to his doorstep, Zhou Hui had no intention of letting this opportunity slip away.

Not to mention—

—"In truth, I believe getting the demon race to willingly accept our textbooks and establish more academies might actually prove far easier than building academies among humans in the Central Domain or other regions,"-Zhou Hui said.

Jiuzhai's eyes widened:

—"Easier? You're talking about demons?!"-Jiuzhai blurted out.

—"While the internal structure of the demon race appears complex, they share a common trait, and the current timing happens to be exceptional,"-Zhou Hui said, turning his gaze toward Shui Huang'er.-"For instance, Your Excellency Saintess, where did your initial goodwill toward our Heavenly Spirit Sect originate?"

—"Because of younger sister Mo Bei!"-Shui Huang'er declared without hesitation.

Zhou Hui continued prompting:

—"And why did you develop such affection for Mo Bei?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Because younger sister Mo Bei is just so adorable, of course!"-Shui Huang'er squealed, cradling her cheeks in her hands.

Standing quietly behind Master, Mo Xi's face twisted into an expression of acute toothache.

Unable to watch any longer, Elder Tong Ling interjected:

—"Because that yin spirit maiden displayed ruthless, decisive lethality during the Immortal Arts Conference... Does the Untiring Immortal Venerable mean to utilize the Immortal Arts Conference to promote the Heavenly Spirit Sect among the demon race, thereby facilitating the propagation of your sect's curriculum?"-Elder Tong Ling asked.

—"What a brilliant idea!"-Shui Huang'er clapped her hands together.

Whether it was cultivation techniques or general knowledge, the weak learned from the strong, and the ignorant learned from the knowledgeable.

Only when one was certain of tangible gains would one proactively seek to learn.

Indeed, the single best way to make demons willingly study was to demonstrate the Heavenly Spirit Sect's overwhelming might.

Only after clapping did Shui Huang'er realize her breach of decorum, offering Zhou Hui a bashful smile:

—"As expected of the Immortal Venerable, striking straight to the heart of the matter,"-Shui Huang'er complimented.

Yet Elder Tong Ling remarked with profound subtlety:

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable seems remarkably confident in his sect,"-Tong Ling noted.

If the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect suffered defeat at the Immortal Arts Conference, it would produce the exact opposite effect on their curriculum's promotion. The law of the jungle reigned far more mercilessly among demons than humans; no demon would ever bother learning the techniques of the weak.

—"Rather than confidence, let us say that through a bizarre twist of fate, the disciples participating in this Immortal Arts Conference happen to be exceptionally suitable,"-Zhou Hui mused thoughtfully.-"Aside from that child Mo Bei, the direct disciples who will take part in the competition include Yuwen Xuan and Lu Shulong—that is to say, the Taotie and True Dragon you spoke of..."

—"Ah... in that case, defeat is indeed quite impossible!"-Tong Ling said in shock.

Shui Huang'er was equally astonished:

—"That really is ridiculously fitting! Luck truly is on our side,"-Shui Huang'er agreed.

—"Indeed. For the demon race, those two are truly the most ideal spokespersons imaginable,"-Zhou Hui said.

Judging purely by these two guests' reactions, one could already envision how the rest of the demon race would react upon witnessing a Taotie and a True Dragon taking the stage at the Immortal Arts Conference.

Moreover, one was an ancient ferocious beast and the other a divine True Spirit, effortlessly covering both the light and dark inclinations of the demon race—with a yin spirit thrown in as a bonus!

Even Jiuzhai gave him an odd look:

—"Are you certain you didn't orchestrate this on purpose?"-Jiuzhai asked suspiciously.

—"Not at all,"-Zhou Hui shook his head.-"At the time, I merely wished to hone and temper them."

With Mo Bei, he hoped she would interact more with outsiders to build a grounded sense of self and learn to distinguish between others and herself.

With Lu Shulong, it was because he curled up in the alchemy laboratory day in and day out; if his combat prowess wasn't tempered, people would practically forget he was a dragon.

As for Yuwen Xuan's wretched state, without external pressure, he seemed content to remain a sheep for the rest of his days.

Back then, Zhou Hui hadn't paid attention to the fact that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's competitors were effectively two non-humans—Mo Bei and Yuwen Xuan could each tear away half of their human heritage, which totaled two non-humans.

[Mm, they can still assemble one whole human together. Quite nice.]

Zhou Hui was thoroughly pleased with his arrangements.

Shui Huang'er was thrilled, discovering that her prospect of [fangirling on the sect's dime](<# "Literal CN: 公款追星 (gōngkuǎn zhuīxīng) — pursuing one's celebrity idol using official public/sect expense accounts">) hadn't vanished after all:

—"In that case, I shall return immediately and arrange for the demons to travel to the Central Domain to watch the Immortal Arts Conference! I'm certain that once they see the competition, they will recognize the Heavenly Spirit Sect's true might!"-Shui Huang'er said excitedly.

Especially younger sister Mo Bei's might.

Shui Huang'er felt that the day she founded the Southern Domain branch of the Mo Bei Fan Club was just around the corner.

—"Bring the demons to the Central Domain?"-Zhou Hui asked in return.

—"...Yes, so that we can watch the Immortal Arts Conference,"-Shui Huang'er paused, hesitating.-"Did the Immortal Venerable not mean that?"

Humans, after all, rarely appreciated hordes of demons pouring into their territory.

In truth, the biggest conflict between humans and demons in the Southern Domain was over territory.

When the Untiring Immortal Venerable spoke of having demons watch the conference, she had assumed he tacitly consented to their arrival!

—"I don't mind demons coming to the Central Domain,"-Zhou Hui remarked.

The one who would mind was the City Lord of Fengqing City, seeing as the cross-border teleportation array was located in his backyard.

—"However, I believe having demons travel all the way to the Central Domain is terribly inefficient,"-Zhou Hui continued.-"Furthermore, even if you are willing, how many demons could realistically make the journey? How astronomical would the spirit stone expenditure be?"

Shui Huang'er naturally had a budget in mind, but she turned back to the Untiring Immortal Venerable:

—"What does the Immortal Venerable suggest...?"-Shui Huang'er asked.

—"The reason I believe the demon race is more suited for promotion is that demons possess an innate advantage over humans—no, rather, the Southern Domain possesses a [dimensional strike-level advantage](<# "Literal CN: 降维打击 (jiàngwéi dǎjī) — dimensional strike / outclassing by leagues; technological disruption from a higher dimension">) over every other region in the cultivation world,"-Zhou Hui stated.

Saying this, he shifted his gaze toward Elder Tong Ling:

—"After all, the Southern Domain already possesses a ready-made '[network](<# "Literal CN: 网络 (wǎngluò) — network / internet; Zhou Hui applying modern telecommunications infrastructure concepts to the Wood Spirit / Sacred Tree root system">).'!"

[—"Network?"](<# "Speaker: Jiuzhai & Tao Zhu & Shui Huang'er">)

The others repeated the foreign word, turning to stare at Elder Tong Ling.

Elder Tong Ling: *???*

Southern Domain, Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

[Chi Xue](<# "Literal CN: 赤血 (Chì Xuè) — Scarlet Blood; a serpentine demon inhabiting the middle boughs of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree">) was lazily sprawled across a slender branch in the middle section of the Sacred Tree.

Four shichen ago, it had settled into this half-asleep, half-awake stupor—emptying its mind, eyes half-lidded, hiding its green serpentine body amidst the foliage.

As the distant horizon brightened with dawn, several sparrows fluttered out from nowhere.

One blind little sparrow landed right on top of Chi Xue's head, chirping noisily.

Today's weather was a bit chilly, however, so Chi Xue couldn't be bothered to move.

If the cold persisted and nothing unexpected occurred, Chi Xue could easily hold this posture for three or four months.

Yet just as Chi Xue was about to blend entirely into the branch, it suddenly felt a distinct surge of spiritual energy pulsing from inside the wood.

This signaled that a certain clan upon the Sacred Tree was broadcasting an announcement.

[Did the porcupines and wolverines at the base of the tree finally go to war? Or are the bluebirds up top throwing another dance party?]

Chi Xue mused in utter boredom. Suddenly, its jaws snapped wide into a bloody maw—the sparrow atop its head couldn't react in time, swallowed whole into Chi Xue's mouth and crushed to death.

The other sparrows shrieked in terror, scattering into the sky.

Chi Xue ignored them, leisurely crunching the bones and feathers in its mouth—sparrows barely had two ounces of meat, which was why Chi Xue usually couldn't be bothered with them—while slithering along the bough toward the massive trunk.

As it slithered, its body began to morph.

First, limbs sprouted; then its triangular head rounded out, shedding its scales. By the time Chi Xue reached the junction of the main trunk, it looked almost human—if one overlooked its disproportionately massive maw.

At this elevation, other creatures grew numerous.

Some had transformed into humanoid forms like Chi Xue, while others retained their beastly shapes. Various nests and hollows dotted the massive boughs, resembling something of a human city.

Unlike human settlements constructed of dead, stacked stone, however, the 'city' these demons inhabited was itself alive. The demons shared an even tighter symbiosis with their city; including the living structures themselves, it constituted an intricate, thriving ecosystem.

As one link in this ecosystem, Chi Xue paid no mind to the other creatures along the trunk. Ducking into a tree hollow, it found several of its kin crammed inside. They hadn't assumed human form, merely twisting together in a tangled heap that looked rather unsightly.

Having gone to the trouble of shifting into a human form, Chi Xue had no intention of joining their tangle, asking them:

—"Today isn't the distribution day for spiritual energy, is it? What are you all huddled here for?"-Chi Xue asked.

[—"Didn't you come too?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Xue's Kin">)

Another kin replied:

—"We're here to watch the excitement. Maybe a battle broke out nearby? Perhaps we can [pick up some leftovers](<# "Literal CN: 捡漏 (jiǎnlòu) — scavenge / pick up leftovers; profiting from someone else's missed opportunity or picking up scraps after a clash">),"-one of its kin said.

—"Scavenge leftovers? You lot better pray you don't get eaten by someone else,"-Chi Xue scoffed sarcastically.

Yet its eyes turned expectantly toward the [interior of the trunk](<# "Literal CN: 树干内部 (shùgàn nèibù) — interior of the trunk; the natural vascular fiber-optic channels of the Sacred Tree">). There, spiritual energy flowed through the tree's vascular channels, occasionally flickering with images and script.

Those were the spiritual energy and thoughts of the Wood Spirits.

Indeed, as residents (and parasites) of this colossal tree, the demons would [tap into](<# "Literal CN: 截取 (jiéqǔ) — intercept / tap into">) the Wood Spirits' spiritual energy for cultivation, read their thoughts, and even utilize the Wood Spirits to relay messages.

The Wood Spirits didn't mind the activities of these creatures in the slightest. The energy utilized by the demons was merely a negligible fraction of their vast power. Moreover, while alive, these demons provided services to the Wood Spirits; once dead, their corpses served as nutrient fertilizer, their essence returning to the Wood Spirits and becoming part of the giant tree once more.

Such was the marvelous ecology of the demon race upon the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

Today, however, seemed somewhat different.

Observing closely, Chi Xue discovered that the spiritual energy within the trunk had grown remarkably dense, rendering the projected images and script crystal clear. Such intense transmission was typically the result of high-ranking demons conducting specialized manipulation.

—"Do those bastards in the [canopy](<# "Literal CN: 树冠 (shùguān) — tree canopy / treetop; where high-ranking avian demon aristocrats reside">) have some new edict to announce?"-Chi Xue grumbled in dissatisfaction.

[—"Surely not? Today isn't any significant occasion."](<# "Speaker: Chi Xue's Kin">)

[—"If only we could go to war with the humans."](<# "Speaker: Chi Xue's Kin">)

Chi Xue's kin babbled back and forth, quickly losing interest.

As creatures that crawled along the branches, they felt they could never see eye-to-eye with those winged aristocrats above—though if given the chance to swallow one whole, they would gladly oblige.

It was amidst this idle chatter that a crisp female voice resonated through the wood:

> 【Hello everyone, this is Shui Huang'er of the Azure Phoenix clan! I'm currently in the Central Domain. This has been a thoroughly delightful journey, and so that everyone can share in my joy, I've decided to [live stream](<# "Literal CN: 直播 (zhíbō) — live broadcast / streaming; modern telecommunications livestreaming over the Wood Spirit tree network">) humanity's Immortal Arts Conference!】

[—"A delightful journey?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Xue's Kin">)

[—"Humanity?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Xue's Kin">)

[—"The Immortal Arts Conference?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Xue's Kin">)

Chi Xue and its kin stared at one another in blank bewilderment, wondering whether the Saintess of the Azure Phoenix had lost her mind.

Very soon, however, the visuals of this 'livestream' captured their complete, undivided attention.
