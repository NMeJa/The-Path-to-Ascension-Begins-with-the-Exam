# Chapter 23: The Cursed Yin Spirit

A future?

That was naturally the thing Mo Xi desired most in the world—yet it was also the existence he feared above all else.

Because Mo Xi himself did not know what kind of person he would become once he was no longer a "cultivation furnace."

As a male who possessed an Extreme Yin Constitution, coerced into practicing a pure Yin cultivation art and refined into a living cauldron, Mo Xi certainly never found it pleasant. Nearly every single night, he had to endure a bone-chilling cold that seeped deep into his marrow, keeping him from sleep night after night.

Yet abolishing his Extreme Yin Constitution and switching to another cultivation art?

Mo Xi could not even fathom it.

Because that "Extreme Yin Constitution" was the wellspring of Mo Xi's agony, yet it was also where his greatest "innate talent" lay.

When it came to cultivating immortality, innate talent was everything. A slightly superior spirit root was equivalent to a [guaranteed direct track](# "Literal CN: 保送 (bǎosòng) — academic recommendation/direct admission without entrance exams") from Qi Condensation all the way to Golden Core. To say nothing of an Extreme Yin Constitution—such a physique guaranteed Mo Xi could cultivate to Soul Formation with zero obstacles.

—That was precisely why he and Mo Bei could serve as supreme-grade cauldrons. A pair of furnaces guaranteed to possess Soul Formation aptitude was enough to allow a Void Refinement cultivator to shatter their bottleneck and successfully advance to Body Integration. If this pair of cauldrons were "maintained" a little better, even breaking through to Mahayana was not completely out of the question.

Conversely, if Mo Xi relinquished his Extreme Yin Constitution, whether he could even advance to Golden Core would become an open question.

Thus, on the day he took Zhou Hui as his master, Mo Xi had declined Master's proposal to change his cultivation art. While his ostensible excuse had been that his and Mo Bei's techniques were identical and could stack with each other to accelerate their cultivation pace, for which he was willing to endure that slight bitter chill—in truth, it was simply that he was terrified.

He was terrified of losing his Extreme Yin Constitution.

He was terrified that once he was no longer a furnace, he would possess zero value.

He was terrified that discarding this so-called talent would prove he was nothing more than worthless garbage.

It was only then that Mo Xi had discovered with sheer horror: he had actually become entirely dependent upon the very value of being a "commodity" that he despised to his bones, utterly incapable of tearing himself away from it.

Every time he thought of this, Mo Xi felt that Mo Bei was quite possibly right.

Since he lacked the courage to abandon his past, what was the point of struggling? No matter how many spirit stones he hoarded, it could never alter his destiny. He was, in all likelihood, truly a fool.

Yet Master had seen through his cowardice, placing the opportunity to choose before him for a second time.

Furthermore, he had placed an entire spirit stone vein before him.

Honestly speaking, in the face of spirit stones, no problem was truly a problem.

If one possessed a spirit vein such as this, what did it matter if the shabby talent of an Extreme Yin Constitution were abolished? Even with a five-element trash spirit root, getting [guaranteed direct track](# "Literal CN: 保送 (bǎosòng) — academic recommendation/direct admission without entrance exams") all the way to Soul Formation wouldn't be an issue!

Mo Xi swallowed hard, hearing his own hypocritical voice say:
—"Master, this isn't worth it. Just for me alone, it simply isn't worth it."

—"Is that so?"-Zhou Hui remarked dismissively.-"It was going to be squandered anyway; wherever it gets spent makes little difference. Besides, I do not believe you need to exhaust the entire vein. Even with your Extreme Yin Constitution abolished, you remain a rare single spirit root cultivator. And with your endurance, maintaining your original pace of cultivation will pose no difficulty whatsoever."

—"Haha... Only someone like Master could possess such confidence and courage, right?"-Mo Xi gave a bitter, self-deprecating laugh.

Indeed. If it were Zhou Hui, switching cultivation arts wouldn't cause him a split second of hesitation.

Zhou Hui felt the exact same way.

To his mind, Mo Xi continuing to practice a pure Yin cultivation art had a hundred harms and not a single benefit. An Extreme Yin Constitution offered a bit of help during early-stage cultivation, true, but so long as he retained this physique, he would be regarded as a furnace. In fact, the higher his cultivation grew, the more terrifying the predators coveting him would become. That was why Extreme Yin physiques rarely reached the Mahayana stage safely—they were consumed and used up long before then. Even if he truly reached Mahayana, he would only become prey in the eyes of higher-tier cultivators, demons, or even True Immortals. For instance, when Zhou Hui researched ascension methods, he had heard that seizing a Body Integration stage cauldron was sufficient to achieve ascension.

Seen in this light, in the Heavenly Dao's script, Mo Xi colluding with demons was practically an inevitability. If that physique remained unchanged, demons would have found him sooner or later.

However, Mo Xi's stroke of luck lay in the fact that he was, after all, male. By switching cultivation arts and harmonizing yin and yang, he could ultimately shed his status as a furnace. He was not like Mo Bei. As a female, that constitution had nailed her firmly to the role of prey. Even if she switched techniques, she could never dispel the malice that others might harbor toward her. The Heavenly Dao itself was rife with malice toward her, having arranged her as a mature [freebie EXP care package](# "Literal CN: 经验大礼包 (jīngyàn dà lǐbāo) — gaming meme for a sacrificial character or bundle that delivers massive experience points to the protagonist") for the Child of Destiny. Not only was she meant to generate experience points and help manage the protagonist's harem, but in the end, she was scripted to die for the protagonist's ascension, leaving behind a spiritual soul that was refined into the protagonist's weapon.

Every time Zhou Hui recalled this plotline, he felt utterly disgusted to the point of nausea—ah, he was straying slightly off-topic.

Returning to Mo Xi.

The only reason Zhou Hui had not forced Mo Xi to change his cultivation art previously was that he felt Mo Xi was still young. Switching techniques at that tender age was far too agonizing; it would be better to wait until his body and soul matured and his mental fortitude hardened before making the switch. There would still be plenty of time.

In several previous reincarnations, Zhou Hui had even entrusted a set of cultivation manuals to Yuwen Xuan, just in case he ascended or was gravely injured and unable to look after him, leaving it to Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan to instruct Mo Xi when the time came.

Yet after this sect-wide Grand Unified Examination, Zhou Hui had realized where Mo Xi's inner demon truly lay.

This child was afraid.

He was afraid that if he lost his Extreme Yin Constitution, he would become useless.

He was nowhere near as haughty and willful as he acted. Not only did he lack faith that his Master could [back him up](# "Literal CN: 兜底 (dōudǐ) — provide a safety net / cover all risks"), but he lived in constant dread of being discarded.

Zhou Hui had realized this with a jolt of clarity, even feeling slightly remorseful that he hadn't noticed it sooner.

Zhou Hui found nothing strange about Mo Xi's fear. For a child with no way out, harboring anxiety about one's future was completely natural. Even outside the cultivation world, in the [aerospace sector](# "Literal CN: 航天口 — Zhou Hui reminiscing about engineers fleeing high-pressure modern aerospace/tech jobs") of his former world, plenty of people ran away every single year. Even Zhou Hui himself had wrestled for quite some time back then between [big tech conglomerates](# "Literal CN: 大厂 (dàchǎng) — Chinese slang for major tech giants like Alibaba/Tencent") and his own ideals.

Since Mo Xi lacked a sense of security, he would simply give him one.

It wasn't as if the Heavenly Spirit Sect couldn't afford it.

Besides, the script the Heavenly Dao had handed Mo Xi was originally that of a spoiled young junior brother anyway!

In the novel, Mo Xi was furious that the protagonist dared pursue his older sister, leading Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples to ostracize him. On the other hand, after repeatedly failing to push him out, he grew jealous of the protagonist's cultivation progress, eventually even harboring murderous intent—in short, the quintessential performance of a villainous trust-fund cultivator male supporting character.

If that was the case, why not simply make it a reality?

There was no reason someone else's trust-fund brat could ride around in a White Deer Cloud-Chasing Carriage with "my mom and dad" constantly on his lips, while the most pampered disciple of their Heavenly Spirit Sect had to exhaust all his spiritual energy just to conjure an illusory undersea palace, all for the sake of showing off before his Master!

Zhou Hui encouraged Mo Xi:
—"It doesn't matter if you lack confidence. If you exhaust this entire spirit vein, you are bound to succeed."

[Is that even human speech?]

With reddened rims around his eyes, Mo Xi nodded.

---

[I don't know whether I'm an idiot or not, but I'm definitely a scoundrel to the core,] Mo Xi thought.

He believed that if he possessed even a shred of gratitude and ambition, he ought to prostrate himself on the ground in overwhelming thanks for Master's painstaking intentions, declaring that even without a spirit vein, he could abolish his Extreme Yin Constitution and follow Master's example, pressing forward in his cultivation through sheer hard work alone.

Yet those words merely circled the tip of Mo Xi's tongue without ever being spoken aloud.

Because this was an entire spirit vein!

Sob sob, he couldn't bring himself to refuse!

In a small voice, Mo Xi asked:
—"Master, how does one switch cultivation arts? What preparations must be made?"

—"None. Your preparations are already complete,"-Zhou Hui replied.

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"Earlier, you exhausted all your spiritual energy while conjuring that illusion. Over these past few days, I used a jade bed to nourish your body, flushing your meridians with external spiritual energy so that not a wisp of Yin Qi was generated,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"Lastly, that spiritual peach consolidated your dantian. Right now is the optimal window to change your art."

—"...Master."

Never imagining that even his coma had been a meticulously calculated step planned by Master for his sake, Mo Xi's expression grew all the more ashamed.

Yet Zhou Hui had no need for Mo Xi's shame. As a master, he felt that disciples should simply accept whatever he bestowed upon them with open honesty.

—"Alright. Sit properly, just as you do during regular meditation,"-Zhou Hui instructed.-"Feel the ambient spiritual energy around you—"

Mo Xi complied.

Zhou Hui's instructions were not difficult. Deep in the heart of this spirit vein, abundant spiritual energy surged everywhere, requiring virtually no conscious effort to sense.

The only detail that troubled Mo Xi was that this was a fire-attribute spirit vein—most spirit stones did not possess intrinsic elements, but depending on the subterranean ley lines, a spirit vein could be influenced accordingly—and it clashed directly with his constitution.

In the past, Mo Xi would never have allowed his body to touch such things. Even with high-grade spirit stones, he would process and cleanse them as thoroughly as possible before use. But since he was switching cultivation arts, he had to absorb the spiritual energy here.

Zhou Hui spoke again:
—"Relax. Sense and accept that spiritual energy—and stop overthinking!"

—"..."-Mo Xi complied out of pure reflex.

The outcome was nowhere near as agonizing as he had imagined.

Normally, an Extreme Yin physique touching even the slightest trace of such spiritual energy would inflict burning, searing agony. But now, with his original power completely drained, his empty dantian and limp limbs accepted the surging energy without the slightest resistance.

[So embracing flame feels this warm...?]

—"Wait, Xi'er. Do not be in such a rush to absorb them. You must convert them first—"

—"Guh!"

Zhou Hui's guidance arrived a beat too late. Mo Xi spat out a mouthful of foul, contaminated blood.

Enduring the excruciating agony in his dantian, Mo Xi attempted to regulate the volume of incoming energy just as Zhou Hui instructed. Yet he found that the ambient spiritual energy was like a raging prairie fire, pouring uncontrollably into his dantian beyond any restraint.

—"What is happening?"

Zhou Hui also noticed that the situation was spiraling out of control. Pressing a hand against Mo Xi's shoulder, he channeled his own spiritual power into him. Yet that power was like a [stone ox sinking into the ocean](# "Literal CN: 石牛入海 (shíniú rùhǎi) — disappearing without a trace, swallowed entirely"), devoured whole alongside the rest.

Zhou Hui quickly realized something:
—"...You possess the bloodline of a [Yin Spirit](# "Literal CN: 阴灵 (yīnlíng) — sentient spiritual beings coalesced from condensed yin energy")?!"

[A Yin Spirit?] What was that?

Mo Xi tried to ask, but spat out another mouthful of black blood.

No, that wasn't blood! It was pure, foul water!

Mo Xi had seen this substance before—back when he was very, very young.

Thinking carefully, he had lied to Master—no, it wasn't quite a lie. It was just that when Master inquired about his and Mo Bei's origins, only Mo Bei had answered. As a result, Master had assumed he didn't remember his early childhood either.

Yet Mo Xi did remember.

He vividly remembered that from the earliest age of comprehension, he had been locked inside a pitch-black subterranean palace. There were many, many children just like him and Mo Bei.

Looking back now, it was quite likely a manufacturing workshop for cultivation furnaces.

There, aside from being forced to study cultivation arts he detested, the thing Mo Xi feared most was mealtime. Their only nourishment was pitch-black, filthy water—freezing to the extreme. The instant one drank it down, it felt as though one's internal organs were frozen solid. In fact, many children had directly shattered into shards of ice after consuming that black water.

Mo Xi had constantly avoided drinking it. And that girl who looked identical to him—back then Mo Bei wasn't called Mo Bei, nor was Mo Xi named Mo Xi; they didn't even have numerical serial numbers—would drink his portion for him.

Yet she didn't seem to be consciously saving him either. It was simply that the girl's head seemed exceptionally dull. As long as something was placed before her eyes, she would drink it all down. Her brain was remarkably dim. And after drinking that black water, her already sluggish mind seemed to deteriorate further, to the point where she eventually lost all memory of that period.

Yet thanks to her, Mo Xi had survived.

Later, everyone seemed to view Mo Bei and Mo Xi as "a pair of cauldrons." But Mo Xi never felt that way. He knew that the one who had truly endured the trials and genuinely survived to the end was Mo Bei alone. He was merely a defective reject who had scraped by under Mo Bei's protection.

Precisely because he understood this, Mo Xi was all the more terrified of letting go of his Extreme Yin Constitution. And precisely because of this, even after arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he lived in perpetual anxiety day in and day out, terrified that someone would see through him and realize he had no value.

Sometimes, Mo Xi even envied Mo Bei.

He envied that she possessed no memories, never jolted awake by nightmares in the dead of night; he envied that she possessed genuine aptitude; he even envied that she was heartless enough to [lie flat and rot](# "Literal CN: 躺平摆烂 (tǎngpíng bǎilàn) — modern Chinese internet slang for giving up on ambition and letting things deteriorate at leisure") without the slightest psychological burden.

A creature like him... he was indeed a mongrel, a crossbreed with some unknown foul filth.

In the past, he had merely watched blankly as Mo Bei suffered. Now, having clearly been saved by Master, he shamelessly harbored greed toward this spirit vein.

Vomiting another mouthful of black water, Mo Xi couldn't help letting out a self-mocking laugh:
—"Master, I truly don't deserve to be saved, do I?"

—"Calm down. Though this is unexpected, it is hardly beyond cure,"-Zhou Hui said, gently stroking Mo Xi's back while using his spiritual energy to inspect the boy's dantian.

This was not the first time Zhou Hui had examined Mo Xi's dantian.

Not only when he first brought Mo Bei and Mo Xi back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but even during regular lessons when testing their cultivation progress, Zhou Hui would examine their dantians. Yet he had never detected any anomalies.

[Was it deliberately sealed? If his spiritual energy hadn't been completely drained this time, it wouldn't have shown itself at all,] Zhou Hui thought, furrowing his brow.

He had initially intended to begin unraveling the seal for Mo Xi. But looking at Mo Xi vomiting black water incessantly with a face twisted in agony, Zhou Hui abandoned that relatively time-consuming approach. Instead, he slapped Mo Xi firmly on the back.

One moment Mo Xi was vomiting as if turning inside out; the next moment, his body felt light as a feather, floating up into the air and nearly colliding with the cavern's stone wall.

Yet the bone-chilling cold on his "body" had not dissipated; if anything, it had grown far more severe.

Mo Xi almost believed he was about to be frozen fast to the stone wall.

With great difficulty, he turned his head to look down, only to discover that "himself" was still sitting cross-legged in place—except his physical body was now crawled over with black-and-red runes. Meanwhile, his Master was also seated cross-legged behind "him," yet a "Master" glowing with soft white light was currently rising to his feet from within the seated physical form.

[A soul projection?]

Mo Xi was no novice in cultivation; he instantly understood his circumstances and subconsciously raised a hand to look.

Then, he scared himself half to death.

What Mo Xi saw was not a translucent spectral soul like an ordinary ghost, nor was it a condensed spiritual body radiating gentle, ethereal radiance like his Master's. It was in the form of a black liquid... precisely identical to the foul water he had been vomiting!

If his body had taken this form, what about his face?!

In terror, Mo Xi raised his hand, which was dripping continuously with black fluid, and reached to touch his face.

He cared deeply about his appearance.

It wasn't that Mo Xi was so fond of that face of his which looked no different from a girl's, but rather that he acutely understood how many advantages a pretty, adorable face could afford him.

Yet he felt no facial features at all—only a sticky, slimy texture, like mud.

[What on earth do I look like right now?!]

Mo Xi was on the verge of tears.

Not only did his "body" feel freezing right now, but his heart felt colder still.

At that moment, a warm hand clasped his icy wrist.

—"Come. We are heading into the spirit vein,"-Zhou Hui said.

As he spoke, he casually cast a luminous barrier over his own and Mo Xi's physical bodies to prevent any mishaps, then pulled Mo Xi along as he walked toward the vein.

Mo Xi reflexively tried to pull his hand back.

He felt that he was currently pitch-black and slimy; merely drawing close to that pure, radiant figure felt like sullying him, let alone touching that jade-white hand.

Yet just as he had been unable to refuse Master's earlier gift, Mo Xi found himself equally unable to refuse the warmth radiating from that palm.

Feeling awkward and self-conscious, he allowed Zhou Hui to lead him forward into the spirit vein.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui consoled him:
—"You really need not take it so hard. As I explained to all of you in class, in our realm, all things possess spirit—including 'spiritual energy' itself. Thus, the 'Spirit Race' refers to lifeforms born when spiritual energy develops sentience. The [Yin Spirit](# "Literal CN: 阴灵 (yīnlíng) — sentient spiritual beings coalesced from condensed yin energy") is merely one branch among them, an objective phenomenon of the world."

—"Yet a 'Yin Spirit' doesn't sound like anything good,"-Mo Xi mumbled.

—"That is true,"-Zhou Hui replied, refusing to deceive a student when it came to academic knowledge.-"The spiritual energy of a Yin Spirit is composed of Yin Qi. And you have already studied the origins of Yin Qi on your own, have you not?"

—"..."

[Why are you suddenly pop-quizzing me at a time like this?!]

Mo Xi was so startled that he momentarily forgot the chill racking his form, stammering out an answer:
—"Yin Qi is generated from massive amounts of death, resentment, and hatred... As expected, I really am no good thing, am I?"

Zhou Hui did not answer directly, continuing his questioning:
—"And what happens when a human comes into contact with Yin Qi?"

Mo Xi knitted his brow. Due to his innate constitution, he detested this area of knowledge, yet he had studied it diligently anyway and luckily still remembered it. For some reason, Mo Xi always felt that failing to answer Master's questions was more terrifying than death itself.

—"If the human body is invaded by Yin Qi, in mild cases, one catches cold and falls ill. In severe cases, one is influenced by the resentment within the Yin Qi, triggering underlying obsessions and descending into madness. In the most extreme cases, one is corrupted by Yin Qi and becomes a mindless living corpse that knows only slaughter,"-Mo Xi recited from memory.

—"Correct,"-Zhou Hui nodded in satisfaction.

Only then did Mo Xi let out a sigh of relief.

Zhou Hui resumed their earlier topic:
—"Even mindless Yin Qi can exert such profound influence on humans, to say nothing of a materialized Yin Spirit. Theoretically speaking, a mortal human body cannot conceive a child with a Yin Spirit. Even if it did happen, the proportion of Yin Spirit bloodline would not be high—at most guaranteeing a slightly yin-leaning constitution, carrying less Yin Qi than a ghost fetus. Naturally, what those people wanted was precisely a human vessel." Otherwise, they could never turn him into a furnace.

—"Precisely for this reason, they still needed to feed you [Yin water](# "Literal CN: 阴水 (yīnshuǐ) — freezing black liquid condensed from purest yin energy, fed to child furnaces to force yin accumulation") to amplify the Yin Spirit's power,"-Zhou Hui said with undisguised disgust.

Naturally, his disgust was not directed at Mo Xi, but at the monsters who refined children like Mo Xi into cauldrons.

Zhou Hui even felt a twinge of regret that his handling of Fallen Immortal Palace five years ago had been too clean and simple. He shouldn't have let them die so quickly. He should have subjected them to a round of soul-searching and spirit-scouring, extracted every single link in their network, and only then served them the deluxe ashes-to-ashes total annihilation package.

Yet Zhou Hui did not forget to impartially admonish his disciple:
—"Therefore, if you believe there is any flaw in your moral character, it has absolutely nothing to do with any Yin Spirit bloodline. Reflect upon yourself first!"

—"Y-Yes, Master."

Admonished like this, Mo Xi was stripped of every superfluous thought.

Trembling, he looked around at their surroundings, attempting to change the subject:
—"Master, where are we heading?"

—"We should be nearly there,"-Zhou Hui said, slowing his flight speed.

Finally seeing his surroundings clearly, Mo Xi's eyes went wide—assuming he still possessed eyes in this state.

Judging by Mo Xi's breadth of experience, they had likely arrived at the deepest core of the spirit vein.

Yet this vein was far richer than what he had glimpsed at first. Outside, the flowing river of massive high-grade spirit stones had already been dazzling enough. But venturing deep into the vein, he discovered that the spirit stones in the innermost core could no longer be described by ordinary terms like "high-grade" or "supreme-grade"—they were pure, concentrated essence itself.

A similar [mine essence](# "Literal CN: 矿精 (kuàngjīng) — pure crystallized essence formed within the deepest core of a spirit vein") was something Mo Xi had once seen deep in the vaults of Fallen Immortal Palace. A fragment no larger than a fingernail was said to have auctioned for the price of a high-grade magical treasure. Yet here, it was scattered everywhere!

[So our Heavenly Spirit Sect is actually *this* rich?!]

Mo Xi thought dizzily, his worldview suffering a tremendous shock.

—"Here we are,"-Zhou Hui said with a nod. Releasing Mo Xi's hand, he instructed:-"Just wait here."

[—"Yes?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi responded aloud, his gaze lingering wistfully on Master's hand. Regrettably, Zhou Hui not only released his grip without sentimentality, but his entire figure even vanished into thin air.

A pang of loss struck Mo Xi's heart, yet he obediently sat down in place and assumed a meditative posture.

He believed Master would not abandon him here.

—Even if Master wanted to discard him, he wouldn't drag him all the way to a place like this to dump him! For a money-grubber, dying in a treasure trove like this was practically the ultimate reward!

In Second Senior Brother's words, every single action Master took inevitably possessed deep intent.

Sure enough, Mo Xi did not have to wait long before feeling a searing heat and sharp, stinging pain around his ankle, as though something was slithering up his calf.

Mo Xi's brow twitched, yet he remained motionless in his seated posture.

That creature (?) lingered at Mo Xi's ankle for a moment. Seeing no reaction from Mo Xi, it slithered further up his calf. It appeared to be a serpentine object, coiling in succession around Mo Xi's calf, thigh, hip, and waist.

Mo Xi gritted his teeth and endured it.

Aside from the pain, wherever the thing coiled felt as though it were burning.

Yet Mo Xi waited motionless until the thing had completely encircled his waist and crawled onto his chest. Suddenly, Master's voice echoed in his mind:
—"Strike,"-Master commanded.

Following the command, Mo Xi swiftly reached out and seized the creature in his grip.

The entity thrashed violently. The searing heat and sharp pain in his palm nearly forced Mo Xi to lose his hold. Fortunately, this predicament did not last long; a soft white halo suddenly descended, and the scorching heat vanished.

He could now tighten his grip around the creature (?) without hesitation.

Only now did Mo Xi see clearly that what he held was a tiny dragon formed entirely of crimson spiritual energy. It thrashed relentlessly, trying desperately to break free from Mo Xi's grasp.

—"A [Mine Spirit](# "Literal CN: 矿灵 (kuànglíng) — sentient spiritual beast formed within the core of a massive spiritual stone vein")?"-Mo Xi gasped in delight.

He had once heard that within colossal spiritual stone veins, materialized Mine Spirits could form. Only by refining such an existence could one command the spiritual energy of the entire vein.

—"Correct,"-Zhou Hui manifested at that moment, speaking calmly:-"Eat it."

—"?!"-Mo Xi's eyes widened.-"Can it be eaten?"

—"Normally, it must be refined. But right now, your soul has undergone Yin-spirit transformation. The advantage of the Spirit Race is that consuming one another incurs no penalty, and you can even alter your constitution through elemental suppression,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"There is no existence more suited to altering your Extreme Yin Constitution than this."

Having said this, Zhou Hui paused, then added:
—"However, doing so entails a bit of trouble..."

—"What kind of trouble?"-Mo Xi asked nervously.

He knew that heaven-and-earth spiritual treasures always demanded a price. He wasn't sure whether he could bear it—

—"If you swallow it, unless you completely refine this entire vein, you will likely be unable to ascend,"-Zhou Hui said with a slight frown.

[...How is that even considered trouble?!]

[Isn't that the exact dream most cultivators would kill for?!]

Once again, Mo Xi keenly felt the gaping divide between his Master's worldview and that of ordinary mortals.

Yet Mo Xi, possessing perfectly ordinary mortal values, caught on immediately:
—"Wait, Master! Doesn't that mean if I eat this thing, this entire vein will be bound to my soul, and no one else can ever take it from me?!"

—"Yes?"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"Then doesn't that mean this entire mine truly belongs to me?!"-Mo Xi emphasized.

—"It already belonged to you,"-Zhou Hui looked at his youngest disciple with mild confusion.-"Did I not already tell you I was giving it to you?"

[There was zero sense of reality before... that's such a colossal mine!]

Mo Xi thought in a daze.

But during his daze, the little dragon held fast in Mo Xi's grip suddenly clamped its jaws onto Mo Xi's arm, wrenching free from his grasp in a bid to flee.

Yet with Zhou Hui present, how could it possibly escape? The white barrier firmly enclosed Mo Xi and his immediate surroundings. Finding no way out in any direction, the little dragon wheeled around and burrowed straight into Mo Xi's body!

Mo Xi was currently in a semi-liquid state; he instantly felt a burst of flame incinerating its way inward.

—"It seems it made the choice for you,"-Zhou Hui chuckled.

Reaching out, he flicked his young disciple's forehead.

Mo Xi toppled backward immediately.

Yet he did not truly fall. In the very next instant, he found that he had returned to his physical body. The Mine Spirit that had attempted to devour him from within now rested inside his dantian, bursting into raging flames that instantly evaporated every trace of Yin water inside him!

—"Calm your mind. Refine it,"-Zhou Hui's voice resonated in his consciousness.

Mo Xi naturally would not squander this opportunity. He assumed the meditative posture once more, discarding all stray thoughts. As he moved, a golden-crimson radiance emanated from beneath him, illuminating that lovely face.

Without question, this was a process of purging impurities and retaining the pure essence. Following this serendipitous encounter, Mo Xi would obtain a cultivation physique far superior to his original Extreme Yin Constitution.

[As expected, having talent is wonderful! Even if it's a Yin Spirit bloodline,] Zhou Hui's soul projection remarked as he walked back from outside the cavern.

He gazed enviously at his young disciple for a moment, then broke into a warm, gentle smile.

Three days later. The Library Pavilion, Formation Simulation Chamber.

Over the past few days, the Formation Simulation Chamber in the Library Pavilion had indisputably become the most popular venue in the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect. As long as disciples had free time, they would rush here. Even their most stern and rigid Second Senior Brother, Qu Qianxing, had taken up residence here, camping out by the boards.

Yet two disciples stood watching this spectacle with miserable, furrowed brows.

[—"Ah-Yi, I smell a massive business opportunity."](# "Speaker: Xiang Rong")

[—"Ah-Rong, I also feel this is a golden chance to rake in a fortune."](# "Speaker: Xiang Yi")

The twin brother and sister, Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi, stood shoulder-to-shoulder before the window, gazing at their fellow sect members in distress:
[—"But how exactly do we monetize it?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Rong & Xiang Yi")

Watching a moneymaking opportunity dangle right before their eyes without being able to think of a way to seize it caused them more agony than actually losing spirit stones.

Just then, a familiar voice rang out behind them:
—"You two can't even figure *that* out?"-the newcomer scoffed.

Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi immediately lit up with pleasant surprise:
[—"Junior Brother Mo?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Rong & Xiang Yi")

Turning their heads, however, they froze dead in their tracks.

—"You're... Junior Brother Mo?"-Xiang Rong asked with a bizarre expression.
