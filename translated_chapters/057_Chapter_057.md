# Chapter 57: Trade (第57章 买卖)

Bai Wubin felt that his master had been very strange lately.

As the dignified leader of an entire sect, upon arriving at the immortals' bazaar, she didn't socialize with the elders of other factions at all. Instead, she pushed him—her eldest disciple—out to [hold down the fort](# "Literal CN: 镇场子 (zhèn chǎngzi) — maintain a dignified presence / hold the floor"), while she herself departed at the crack of dawn and returned late into the night, appearing even more diligent than back home at their own sect.

Claiming she was conducting an investigation, she hadn't brought back a single shred of findings. Instead, she had grown inexplicably obsessed with her attire; several times, he had caught her and his junior sisters secretly whispering in corners about dresses, skirts, robes, and similar topics they had never previously discussed.

If not for Master being too ferocious for anyone to ever want (X); if not for Master already being at the Body Integration stage so nobody could beat her in a fight (X); if not for Master being well up in years and lacking interest in that sort of... (X)—in short, if it weren't utterly impossible, Bai Wubin would genuinely suspect Master had found a [step-master](# "Literal CN: 师公 (shīgōng) — master's husband / paternal grandmaster; here humorous slang for master's new husband") to bring back for them.

On this particular day, seeing Shen Qingqian rushing out at dawn once again with two junior sisters in tow, Bai Wubin couldn't resist questioning her:
—"Master, are you truly investigating?"

—"Of course! Didn't I tell you? I am conducting an in-depth investigation of the bazaar, and results cannot be produced overnight! Stop asking every single day, will you?! It's so annoying!"-Shen Qingqian replied roughly.

[That means you're definitely not investigating!]

Bai Wubin was certain of it.

Seeing his two junior sisters dodging his gaze, he knew eighty percent of whatever Master was doing had nothing to do with proper business.

However, considering that up until now, Master had only taken a single storage pouch of spirit stones on the first day and had never asked for another copper since, keeping overall expenditures strictly within budget, so...

It should be fine?

Frankly speaking, given the staggering variety and sheer temptation of the goods in the immortals' bazaar, Bai Wubin had assumed her purse would be completely cleaned out on day one; for her not to come back demanding more funds by now had already exceeded his wildest expectations.

Moreover, there were currently no signs of creditors knocking at their door either. Thus, his master must have uncharacteristically budgeted her expenses. After all, a dignified Body Integration powerhouse couldn't possibly have gone and sold herself, hahaha...

...Ha?

Just as Bai Wubin frightened himself with his own imagination, a greeting drifted across the table:
[—"Brother Bai, what is the matter?"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingli")

The voices belonged to several Nascent Soul and Soul Formation cultivators.

And at this very moment, Bai Wubin was sitting within the Soul Formation Bazaar.

Unlike his flaky master, Bai Wubin was conducting "proper business."

The cultivators accompanying him at present were the backbone figures of several relatively dependable sects and immortal cities in the Central Domain. The annual Winter Solstice Auction was precisely when these factions gathered to exchange insights (dig for gossip) and strengthen bonds ([ineffective socializing](# "Literal CN: 无效社交 (wúxiào shèjiāo) — modern corporate buzzword for unproductive networking")).

Frankly speaking, it was indeed quite dull.

Hence in previous years, Shen Qingqian had lacked the patience to attend these gatherings, dumping them onto Bai Wubin to handle.

—Naturally, Shen Qingqian lacked the patience for it this year as well, ditching her disciple to run wild outside. Blood Demon Hall's official public statement was: "Hall Master Shen prefers peace and quiet; even when traveling abroad, she enters deep meditative seclusion at fixed hours every day."

Fortunately, most powerhouses of the Upper Three Realms (Body Integration, Mahayana, and Tribulation Transcendence) behaved in roughly the same manner, so nobody grew suspicious.

With a master like this, what could a disciple do?

Even though Bai Wubin disliked such low-efficiency rambling, he was still forced to come out and [stand on the street](# "Literal CN: 站街 (zhàn jiē) — modern slang for streetwalking / loitering; here self-deprecating humor for hosting an awkward diplomatic booth") (X).

However, with this year's auction moved to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Bai Wubin found it significantly more entertaining.

Take right now, for instance.

Despite worrying in his heart where his master had gone wandering, when questioned by the other side, Bai Wubin still maintained an air-tight, contemplative expression:
—"I was just pondering: if Immortal Chess releases my piece, which of my signature moves should I choose?"

In truth, Bai Wubin had not paid the slightest attention to what these people had been discussing earlier. But based on his observations over the past few days, this topic was universally applicable.

Sure enough, across the table, Qingyang Qingli of Eternal Villa instantly grew animated:
—"For you body cultivators, every move looks roughly identical, doesn't it? It is us spell cultivators who face true agony! Which set of robes should I wear to look best?"

—"Brother Qingyang's priorities are completely off the mark, aren't they? Isn't the most important aspect of a piece the dazzling visual effects of its spells?"-Yun Mingyao, Vice City Lord of Cloud City, retorted.

—"Tch! You speak as if you're certain to be selected,"-another female cultivator sneered coldly.-"Without even considering how miserably you lost yesterday."

[—"Fellow Daoist Lü, that is uncalled for..."](# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingli")

The cultivators instantly dissolved into heated bickering.

Meanwhile, having successfully ignited the debate, Bai Wubin bowed out gracefully, letting his gaze go blank once more as he looked toward the Immortal Chess board situated in the center of the tea pavilion.

It was said that the game of Immortal Chess had long been popular within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but previously, it had been confined solely within their own borders. With the hosting of this auction, it had finally entered the vision of the entire Central Domain's cultivation world.

To this end, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had also made a few adjustments to the rules of Immortal Chess.

For example, they assigned star ratings to the pieces based on cultivation realm.

Qi Condensation and Foundation Establishment were three-star pieces, Golden Core were four-star pieces, Nascent Soul were five-star, and Soul Formation were six-star.

Considering that the current edition of Immortal Chess featured only disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect—and the strongest six-star piece among them was Yuwen Xuan alone—the Heavenly Spirit Sect decided to produce a set of "Central Domain General Immortal Chess," selecting heroes from among the cultivators of the Central Domain to craft into pieces.

They also announced the regulations for the "Immortal Bazaar Immortal Chess Round-Robin Tournament," declaring their intention to ultimately add seven six-stars, twelve five-stars, thirty-six four-stars, and ninety-nine three-stars.

Cultivators who made the cut would not only receive a generous cash prize ([licensing fee](# "Literal CN: 授权费 (shòuquán fèi) — intellectual property copyright / licensing royalty")), but would also have their images recorded and their life stories chronicled, appended into the standard chessboard instruction manual published following the auction. Henceforth, every customer who purchased an Immortal Chess set could not only view the pieces, but also learn of the great deeds of the cultivators who served as their prototypes through the manual, as well as receive a portrait card personally autographed by them!

This policy naturally sparked immense interest among the cultivators attending the bazaar, particularly those in the Nascent Soul and Soul Formation realms; backbone elders of various sects and representatives of immortal cities fought like madmen over the opportunity.

Anyone with eyes could see that this was a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to make a name for oneself across the cultivation world!

Bai Wubin was actually quite tempted as well. Having already completed one round, judging from his score, he genuinely had a very high chance of being selected as a five-star piece.

Yet what intrigued middle-realm cultivators about the Heavenly Spirit Sect was not merely Immortal Chess.

There was also the atmosphere.

If the bazaar of the Lower Three Realms (Qi Condensation, Foundation Establishment, and Golden Core) was an authentic street market—a massive shopping fair—then upon reaching the Middle Three Realms (Nascent Soul, Soul Formation, and Void Refinement), the bazaar transformed into a luxury commercial boulevard.

Everywhere here exuded an air of elegance ([pretentious flexing](# "Literal CN: 装X (zhuāng bī) — internet slang for showing off / posing ostentatiously")) suited to middle-realm cultivators.

Take, for instance, the tea pavilion Bai Wubin currently occupied. Although it also bore the signboard of *A Cup of Sorrow*, unlike the grab-and-go kiosks down below, this was a gorgeous three-story pavilion providing live tea-brewing services. Cultivators could gather around partitioned booths to watch Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples perform tea-brewing ceremonies, or sit at long tables by the windows overlooking the streets below. Furthermore, in the center of every floor stood an Immortal Chess board; whenever the mood struck, one could sit down for a match at any time.

And throughout the Middle Three Realms, the streets were lined entirely with high-end boutiques of this caliber.

Of course, this did not mean merchandise was any scarcer than in the Lower Three Realms. Not only were the goods sold in each boutique abundant, but their quality was extraordinarily high, with dedicated shopping guides offering meticulous explanations and immaculate service.

If one were genuinely interested in the sundries of the Lower Three Realms, there was no need to make a special trip down to that chaotic market either; every street in the Middle Three Realms featured dedicated Novelty Boutiques that curated and displayed trendy novelties from the Lower Three Realms for effortless purchase.

With service so immaculate, Bai Wubin found his master's behavior all the more incomprehensible:

[What is there to investigate in this bazaar?]

[For the Heavenly Spirit Sect to achieve such heights, isn't their popularity only natural?]

Rather than investigating, it would be far better to discuss proper business as soon as possible:

Naturally, not Immortal Chess—though seeing how manic the other sects had become, many cultivators genuinely treated it as paramount business—but rather deciding when to put their own local specialties up for auction to earn spirit stones, and at what price point to procure the materials their sect required!

Gazing at the Immortal Chess board, Bai Wubin sighed in distress.

***

Shen Qingqian was naturally oblivious to her eldest disciple's distress.

Leading her two female disciples through the bazaar, not daring to look around too much, she arrived at the lowest level of the bazaar's coral reef. Here, a new quarry pit had been excavated to dig up seabed silt from beneath the ocean floor; according to Shen Qingqian's observations, it seemed to be used for firing bricks.

Such manual labor was naturally dominated by male cultivators.

When Shen Qingqian descended, the official shift had not yet begun. A crowd of body cultivators stood around the perimeter of the pit—some squatting while wolfing down breakfast, some huddled in groups chatting, others brooding alone in silent isolation. Every single one appeared brawny and burly, making the scene look like some clandestine underground transaction.

Yet the moment this group caught sight of Shen Qingqian and the two female cultivators beside her, their expressions twisted into pure terror and panic—to use an imperfect analogy, like delinquent punks smoking in a stairwell suddenly bumping into their school disciplinary teacher.

Those still eating instantly set down their meal trays; those chatting scrambled apart; the brooding solitary ones snapped immediately to attention, roaring in unison:
[—"Greetings, Female Hero Shen!"](# "Speaker: Body Cultivator Workers")

Generally speaking, the cultivation world collectively addressed female cultivators as "Fairy"; for some reason, it had morphed into "Female Hero" here. In truth, someone had previously attempted to address Shen Qingqian as "Big Sister," only to be pummeled into a swollen pig's head, unable to speak for days on end.

Shen Qingqian had evidently grown accustomed to this.

Coldly ignoring the crowd, she asked the foreman:
—"Is there any work to do today?"

The foreman's reaction differed little from the other laborers; one glance was enough to tell he had been shoved into a burlap sack and beaten bloody by Shen Qingqian. Bowing and scraping obsequiously, he stammered:
—"The portion excavated last night has already been cleared out separately. We await your eld... Female Hero's move."

Shen Qingqian lowered her fist, nodded, and followed behind the foreman into the depths of the quarry pit. Here, a mountain-sized mound of seabed silt sat piled high, emitting a peculiar odor.

For cultivators, excavating such a colossal mound was child's play—even a spell cultivator could accomplish it in a single breath. But the subsequent work was considerably trickier. While other cultivators could manage it with some effort, the most labor-saving and efficient specialists were body cultivators.

—"Just this?"-Shen Qingqian glanced at it, sounding dismissive.-"This small amount doesn't require my intervention. Miao Dong, you do it!"

—"Yes, Master,"-Miao Dong cupped her fists and bowed.

Stepping forward two paces, she dropped into an offensive stance, then unleashed a rapid flurry of punches with a sharp bark:
—"Hah!"

Miao Dong's physical fists never actually touched the silt. Instead, the gale of her punches materialized into countless invisible fists pounding against the mound. Under the varying shockwaves of her punch force, the silt deformed like modeling clay, separating and sifting apart. By the time Miao Dong pulled back her fists, the giant mound had been neatly sorted into distinct sections of metal, clay, and rock according to their composition. Next, they needed only to collect the desired materials into storage pouches and transport them to wherever they were required.

This was the work of body cultivators. However, depending on their mastery, not all body cultivators could achieve such meticulous separation:
—"Splendid! As expected of Female Hero Miao!"-the nearby foreman cheered.

Hearing this, Miao Dong smiled faintly and looked toward Shen Qingqian.

—"Passable. Your method of force control should be more refined,"-Shen Qingqian instructed.-"For instance, within this chunk of rock, the black iron was not fully separated out."

—"This degree is already plenty sufficient; we never demanded such extreme precision,"-the foreman chuckled.

Just as Shen Qingqian was about to speak, a clamor erupted from outside.

—"What happened?"-the foreman asked in surprise.

—"Someone came over asking if there were a few formidable female body cultivators here,"-someone answered, shooting a pointed glance at Shen Qingqian.

Shen Qingqian raised an eyebrow:
—"Someone looking for trouble again?"

Before that person could answer, a female cultivator clad in a moon-white dress draped in a tender-yellow half-sleeved robe walked over. Through their interactions over this period, Shen Qingqian already knew this attire was the designated uniform of the Heavenly Spirit Sect; for instance, the foreman, being a Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple, wore similar clothing, though tailored into a short tunic for manual convenience.

—"You must be the legendary Female Heroes everyone has been raving about!"-the Heavenly Spirit Sect female cultivator's eyes lit up upon spotting Shen Qingqian and her disciples, beaming brightly:—"Could I trouble you to help us out with a job? We can offer three times the standard wage."

Shen Qingqian did not speak immediately, instead casting an amused glance at the nearby foreman.

Although wages were paid daily, she had technically received his patronage (X); according to cultivation world customs, jumping ship arbitrarily was a major taboo.

Sure enough, the foreman instantly protested:
—"Junior Sister Hua, you can't just poach workers like this!"

—"Senior Brother Qi, how exactly do you manage your laborers?"-'Junior Sister Hua' countered unceremoniously.-"Would you like me to go to Senior Sister Ran Deng and repeat word-for-word what they said to me when I came looking for workers just now? How do you think Senior Sister Ran Deng will react when she hears it?"

The foreman choked instantly.

'Junior Sister Hua' flashed a triumphant smile and merrily led Shen Qingqian's group away, chiming:
—"How many days have you older sisters worked here? What was your base wage? If this senior brother of mine underpaid you, I can help file a complaint on your behalf~"

—"Hua Congyu!"-the foreman bellowed.

Hua Congyu merely let out a haughty huff.

Shen Qingqian and her disciples watched this female cultivator with great amusement, sensing the aura of a kindred spirit.

—"Fellow Daoist Hua..."

—"Just call me Congyu,"-Hua Congyu said cheerfully.

—"Congyu,"-Fu Ning called out naturally, as warmth between girls could spark in a split second:—"What do you need us to do?"

—"I hope you can help us construct immortal cave-dwellings,"-Hua Congyu replied.

—"Cave-dwellings?"

—"Yes—"-Hua Congyu nodded.

As if anticipating what unfamiliar female cultivators might worry about, she explained the job in meticulous detail.

As it turned out, aside from the auction, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had launched numerous engineering projects this year—such as the Buyan Palace renovations, the spiritual qi reservoir dam, and the construction of cave-dwellings for Golden Core disciples like Hua Congyu. Originally, these projects were in no rush; according to the cultivation world's rhythm where a single closed-door meditation lasted centuries, spending several hundred years building an abode was commonplace. Coupled with auction preparations, Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples had fully resigned themselves to moving into new caves decades later.

Unexpectedly, the auction drew a massive influx of cultivators, among whom were numerous body cultivators. The Heavenly Spirit Sect recruited a portion of them for construction work, which dramatically accelerated the previously stalled project schedules, even creating surplus labor capacity. Quite a few disciples dipped into their own pockets, completing their personal cave-dwellings within mere months.

—"However, the temperament of those outsiders, as you know... Ah, I don't mean you, I mean body cultivators... no, that's not it, I mean!"-Hua Congyu tried to explain, accidentally biting her tongue.

Fu Ning patted her shoulder:
—"We understand. When we first said we wanted to work in the quarry, those male cultivators laughed at us too."

Later, Shen Qingqian and her disciples had used their fists to show them [why flowers bloom so red](# "Literal CN: 为什么花儿这样红 (wèishénme huār zhèyàng hóng) — famous Chinese film song line used as idiom for getting beaten black and blue").

—"Exactly that! The female cultivators of our sect do not want those men anywhere near our private abodes. Yet our sect has very few body cultivators, and female body cultivators are even rarer, which is why construction of our cave-dwellings stalled completely,"-Hua Congyu explained.-"Hearing rumors of you, I immediately dashed over to hire you."

—"Leave it entirely to us,"-Miao Dong declared, patting her chest.

Hua Congyu giggled merrily, clearly overjoyed to have hired female body cultivators.

Shen Qingqian was equally thrilled looking at this Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple:
—"See? We've successfully infiltrated the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"-Shen Qingqian boasted via divine sense to Fu Ning and Miao Dong.-"Your master's decision was indeed brilliant."

[Wait a minute, Master!]

[Undercover infiltration of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was never in our plans at all!]

The smiles on Miao Dong and Fu Ning's faces froze solid.

***

—"As expected of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, truly breathtaking!"

Stepping onto the grounds of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Shen Qingqian marveled with heartfelt sincerity.

What she marveled at was neither the wonder of the aerial spiritual energy reservoir dam nor the convenience of the sundial clocks, but purely the "school uniforms" worn by the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Although she had already found them pleasant at the bazaar, an entity like a "school uniform" was only truly at its most orderly and magnificent when all the students of a school—er, disciples of a sect—gathered together.

In such moments, even baggy blue-and-white gym tracksuits could radiate an aura of aesthetic grandeur, let alone the exquisite "school uniforms" of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Particularly the spectacle of hundreds of disciples emerging from all directions, marching in unison toward Buyan Palace.

[This is what an immortal cultivation sect ought to look like.]

Shen Qingqian couldn't help harboring such a thought, though it prompted a hint of curiosity:
—"What are all these disciples going to do? Is there an open lecture today? Is your Sect Master delivering a sermon?"-Shen Qingqian asked, growing somewhat excited.

If it were a lecture by the Untiring Immortal Venerable, even Shen Qingqian would attend without hesitation.

—"Of course not! If it were a lecture by the esteemed Immortal Venerable, how could I possibly be out here?"-Hua Congyu replied cheerfully:—"Though our Immortal Venerable is indeed inside Buyan Palace right now! Because today is the Beginner Class Midterm Exam."

—"Midterm exam? What is that?"-Fu Ning asked, feeling an odd twinge.

Though she didn't comprehend the term, an abrupt wave of unease washed over her.

—"It is the day when... every half-year, we assess the disciples' proficiency,"-Hua Congyu explained.

—"Ah, so it's roughly the same as our sect's martial arena tournament?"-Miao Dong nodded in understanding.

—"Martial arena?"-Hua Congyu blinked blankly at Miao Dong.

—"Is it not?"-Miao Dong asked.

Just days ago, in order to qualify for this auction expedition, they had fought their senior and junior sisters half to death in the arena!

By the way, Miao Dong was the champion of Blood Demon Hall's Winter Solstice arena battles this year, while Fu Ning was the runner-up, which was how they earned the privilege of accompanying their master—though looking at it now, it was hardly an enviable assignment.

Hua Congyu could not comprehend Blood Demon Hall's rules of survival, just as Blood Demon Hall could not fathom the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

However, sensing keenly that these ladies harbored some misconceptions about the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Hua Congyu extended an invitation:
—"If you are interested, would you like to go take a look?"

—"We can watch?"-Miao Dong asked in surprise.-"Isn't this an internal competition of your sect?"

Weren't they afraid of leaking sect secrets?

—"Not a competition, an exam,"-Hua Congyu corrected. Suddenly recalling her unfinished written report, any psychological burden regarding leading outsiders to spectate the Beginner Class exam vanished:—"Our exams welcome spectators; usually everyone in the sect goes to watch! It won't be a problem at all if you go."

Speaking thus, she led Shen Qingqian and the others to the grand hall of Buyan Palace.

Sure enough, just as Hua Congyu had said, a massive crowd had already gathered outside the entrance of Buyan Palace. Judging by their attire, there were sect handymen, Advanced Class disciples, and even outsiders like Shen Qingqian.

Among them, the outsiders gazed with immense curiosity at the palatial grandeur of Buyan Palace and the coral islands floating overhead.

—After six months of renovation, this grand hall was no longer an illusion generated by Mirage Demons, but a genuine physical structure. As a result, it was far sturdier and equipped with brand-new functions—such as a massive screen that magnified disciples' test papers in real time.

—"Even your competition arena is this gorgeous?"-Fu Ning marvelled in astonishment.

—"How will the assessment proceed next? Will they attack one another and throw each other off the coral platforms?"-Miao Dong asked eagerly.

Hua Congyu was utterly horrified:
—"What kind of terrifying place is your Blood Demon Hall?!"

Yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect's examination hall soon showed everyone the meaning of *true* terror.

With the chime of a bell, the examinees raised their brushes in unison and began answering the questions.

Originally, this scene held zero spectacle for anyone other than the examinees themselves—in fact, it was exceedingly boring. Unfortunately, a crowd of "professional" commentators had gathered at the entrance:
[—"This year's batch of freshmen seems even more outstanding than the previous batch!"](# "Speaker: Old Zhang")

Their very first sentence froze the smile right off Hua Congyu's face.

Regrettably, nobody cared about the feelings of a "senior student," continuing their critique:
[—"When the previous disciples took their exams, the direct disciples and the top few inner disciples shot straight to the very peak like kites, but this time there is no dramatic rise or fall."](# "Speaker: Spectator Disciple")

—"Doesn't that just mean this batch lacks top standouts?"-Hua Congyu couldn't resist arguing.

—"Incorrect. Based on my experience, no matter how abysmal a cohort is, standout geniuses always exist—in fact, the worse the batch, the more extreme the polarization. But when they are all clustered tightly at the same elevation like this, it proves their skill levels are closely matched, driving cutthroat competition,"-the "commentator," Old Zhang, the front hall sweeper, stroked his beard and declared decisively.

Hua Congyu had no words to rebut him.

Listening to their dialogue, Fu Ning grasped the rules of the competition (X) examination:
—"Does the elevation of those coral platforms represent the score of each disciple?"

—"Yes."

Fu Ning and Miao Dong exchanged a glance, feeling a deep chill in their hearts.

Seeing a written exam was already dreadful enough, but what felt far more agonizing was this public ranking. While Blood Demon Hall's arena battles were blunt—win and advance, lose and drop—they never ranked every single disciple on a public leaderboard! Carrying the titles of "Champion" and "Runner-up" for a year was certainly glorious, but how on earth was someone bearing the label of dead last supposed to survive?!

Even more terrifyingly, they heard someone among the spectators ask:
[—"Old Huang, where are your nephew and niece?"](# "Speaker: Sect Handyman")

[—"Somewhere near the middle... Gah, that brat Huang Xiao dropped another rank! I told him not to hang around the kitchen all day! How am I supposed to explain this to his father?!"](# "Speaker: Chef Old Huang")

...THERE WERE EVEN PARENTS SPECTATING?!

Miao Dong and Fu Ning began trembling all over.

—"How long does this exam last?"-Fu Ning asked, shuddering.

—"One shichen in the morning, one in the afternoon,"-Hua Congyu answered with an unsightly complexion:—"Three days in total."

[This public execution is going to last for THREE DAYS?!]

Miao Dong and Fu Ning almost fainted on the spot:

They would rather be smashed off a martial arena stage by a direct, bone-crushing punch than endure this kind of [public execution](# "Literal CN: 处刑 (chùxíng) — execution; modern anime/internet slang for agonizing public humiliation")!

—"S-Since it takes so long, sh-shall we go do our work?"-Miao Dong stammered.

—"Alright,"-Hua Congyu nodded, feeling equally unable to stay here any longer.

Fu Ning turned to call her master, only to witness in sheer horror that Shen Qingqian was watching with immense relish:

What crystal-clear, unequivocal scores!

What a breathtakingly elegant spectacle!

Indeed, this was how an immortal cultivation sect ought to be!

Shen Qingqian likewise recalled Blood Demon Hall's recent midterm examination (X) arena tournament.

Body cultivators mostly emphasized flesh-to-bone brawling, and female cultivators were no exception. Thus, that arena tournament had been exceedingly bloody—severed limbs everywhere, shattered teeth flying across the stage, flesh and gore splattered across the floor. Shen Qingqian was a female cultivator with ordinary aesthetics, harboring zero appreciation for the aesthetics of violence—otherwise she would never have purchased that dress. In the past, without a comparison, it had seemed fine; now with a contrast, that previous scene appeared utterly grotesque and unwatchable.

This was not to say she wished to abolish arena tournaments:

Shen Qingqian understood clearly that in the cultivation world, sheer strength was the fundamental root of survival!

However, she also reflected:
—"Were we too focused on brute physical capability in the past? Incorporating a bit of written examinations seems far more conducive to cultivating poise and elegance. I wonder if the Untiring Immortal Venerable would be willing to help draft exam papers for us?"

[—"MASTER?!"](# "Speaker: Miao Dong & Fu Ning")

***

On this day, Bai Wubin waited until the wee hours before his master finally returned.

Furthermore, Shen Qingqian looked completely different from any prior occasion:

Draped in a mane of soaking-wet hair and clad in gossamer silk robes, she strode back beneath the moonlight, steam still wafting from her body.

Witnessing this, Bai Wubin couldn't help asking in shock:
—"Did you fall into the water?! Why didn't you use a Water-Warding Art?!"

—"You're the one who fell into the water!"-Shen Qingqian punched her disciple straight in the face.

She announced smugly:
—"I soaked in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual hot springs before returning! Didn't I tell you I was conducting an investigation? Thus, with my construct... ahem, my talent, I completely conquered the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect! Not only did they invite me on an internal tour of their sect, but they even let me into their newly opened spiritual spring bathhouse!"

—"Truly?"-Bai Wubin asked in disbelief.

He didn't look at his master, instead turning his gaze toward his two junior sisters.

Miao Dong and Fu Ning nodded stiffly.

If judged purely by the results, matters were indeed just as Shen Qingqian had stated.

As a Body Integration titan, moving bricks was utterly effortless. With her assistance, the female cultivators of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had completed their cave-dwellings in a single afternoon. This construction speed had even alerted the abodes' designer, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's third direct disciple, Ran Deng.

Ran Deng had personally visited Shen Qingqian, looking as though she wished she could poach such a formidable body cultivator into the Heavenly Spirit Sect—and the salary she offered genuinely tempted Shen Qingqian for a shameful two seconds! However, as the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall, she regretfully declined. Ran Deng didn't mind, or perhaps felt that fostering good relations with such a body cultivator would benefit future developments; she treated the three of them to dinner and invited them to soak in the aerial spiritual hot springs above the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Those spiritual springs were breathtakingly gorgeous and unimaginably relaxing. It had been, without question, the most wonderful day of Miao Dong and Fu Ning's lives.

—If not for that cursed midterm exam!

Bai Wubin looked suspiciously at his two junior sisters' expressions, which bordered between tears and laughter. Preparing himself for the absolute worst, he asked his master:
—"So then, what did you discover?"

[How many spirit stones do we have to compensate them?]

—"A great deal,"-Shen Qingqian announced triumphantly.-"Furthermore, it is about time we register our goods for the auction. Seizing this opportunity, I have a massive business deal to negotiate with the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"

—"What business?"-Bai Wubin asked, watching in bewilderment as his two junior sisters grew even paler.

—"Naturally—"-Shen Qingqian announced:—"The installation project for Spiritual Spring Running Tap Water!"

[—"Running tap water?!"](# "Speaker: Bai Wubin, Miao Dong & Fu Ning")

All three disciples blurted out simultaneously.

However, the two female disciples' question was completely different from the utterly baffled Bai Wubin's:
—"Master, it's not exam papers?"-they gasped in pleasant surprise.

—"Of course we want exam papers too! Since you two are so enthusiastic, I'll make sure to mention it to the Untiring Immortal Venerable early,"-Shen Qingqian replied, glancing at them curiously.

Miao Dong and Fu Ning instantly lost their voices.

Meanwhile, Shen Qingqian stroked her damp hair:
—"However, right now, what I desire even more is the spiritual spring."

***

—"Master, someone has registered the Flame Crystal Flower for the auction,"-Mo Xi hurried in to report to Zhou Hui the following day.

Indeed. Although countless events had unfolded, the "Flame Crystal Flower" remained their true objective all along.

After all, in several previous lives, Yuwen Xuan had vanished precisely while seeking out this flower for Zhou Hui. And in this life, even without Zhou Hui's command, the brand upon his hand had continued to guide him toward seeking this flower.

However, previous auctions had been co-hosted by the Wanhe Gang and the Palace of Carefree Wandering, leaving Zhou Hui with no trail to investigate. But from the moment the auction was seized into the hands of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they could finally trace the exact provenance of every registered auction item with crystal clarity.

Naturally, including the Flame Crystal Flower.

Zhou Hui set down the disciples' exam papers and asked:
—"Who brought it?"

—"Blood Demon Hall."
