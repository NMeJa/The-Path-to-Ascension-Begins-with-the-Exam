# Chapter 58: Karmic Cycles (第58章 因果循环)

Blood Demon Hall?

The name didn't sound like a proper, respectable sect in the slightest.

However, having reincarnated across multiple lifetimes, Zhou Hui had zero recollection of ever having any dealings with this sect. He even pinched his fingers to divine the [karmic threads](# "Literal CN: 因果 (yīnguǒ) — karma / cause and effect"), confirming that neither his own sect nor Yuwen Xuan shared any karmic ties with them whatsoever.

Since there were no karmic ties, it shouldn't be them who caused Yuwen Xuan to go missing (in previous lifetimes).

In fact, within the Heavenly Dao's script, not a single plot thread related to Blood Demon Hall ever appeared—Zhou Hui had searched it thoroughly; aside from a minor character named the "Fierce Blood Demon Lord" (烈血魔君), not a single keyword overlapped with Blood Demon Hall.

Then why did the "crucial item," the Flame Crystal Flower, originate from this very sect?

—"Do you know what kind of sect they are?"-Zhou Hui asked Mo Xi.

Since Mo Xi had come to report, he had naturally investigated beforehand.

—"Blood Demon Hall is a body-refinement sect,"-Mo Xi replied.

—"Body refinement?"-Zhou Hui frowned. In that split second, countless methods flashed through his mind—tempering the flesh with fresh blood, souls, and vital essence. Such methods might not even violate the Heavenly Dao; after all, in the Central Domain, plenty of wicked arts [danced along regulatory edge lines](# "Literal CN: 打擦边球 (dǎ cābiānqiú) — playing edge balls; skating on the edge of the rules or skirting legal boundaries"), just as Thousand Beasts Hall was hardly upright and orthodox.

Mo Xi assumed Zhou Hui was questioning whether they truly were a body-refinement sect. In truth, when he first heard the words "Blood Demon Hall" coupled with "body refinement," he too had felt the two concepts [couldn't be further apart](# "Literal CN: 八竿子打不着 (bā gānzi dǎ bù zháo) — cannot be reached even with an eight-pole reach; completely unrelated").

To this end, Mo Xi had investigated with meticulous care:
—"Yes, it is confirmed that they are a body-refinement sect. Not only due to testimony from other sects, but also because their eldest disciple, Bai Wubin, is quite well known. More crucially, on the eleventh, three female cultivators from their sect bought a dress at our Golden Core auction house and fell into debt for eight hundred and fifty medium-grade spirit stones. Afterward, they worked continuously in the seabed silt excavation pit, cowing every cultivator there with their muscular physiques and terrifying strength, earning unanimous acclaim! Because of this, they were invited by Hua Congyu of our Array Hall to build cave-dwellings for female cultivators. In the evening, they even visited the Peach Blossom Hot Springs together! Eldest Senior Sister inspected their bodies with her own eyes and declared they were her ideal... ah, no, declared they possessed the bodies of certified, authentic body cultivators without a shred of evil arts! Eldest Senior Sister even added that if we can confirm they pose no threat, she hopes to establish a long-term cooperative partnership with them."

—"Huh?"-Zhou Hui blurted in a daze.

There were simply too many [absurdities to roast](# "Literal CN: 槽点 (cáodiǎn) — points of complaint/ridicule; comedic absurdities in internet subculture") in this account, leaving even Zhou Hui unable to process it for a moment.

Mo Xi's report continued:
—"Additionally, intelligence indicates that the leader of those three female cultivators looks identical to their sect leader, Fairy Shen, a Body Integration titan. It is very likely her in the flesh..."

At this point, Mo Xi furrowed his brows in sheer bewilderment:
—"A sect leader suppressing her cultivation, sneaking into our manual labor zone to move bricks, and even infiltrating our inner sect—it certainly sounds exceedingly suspicious. Yet if she harbors ulterior motives, she strutted about showing her true face in broad daylight without the slightest fear of exposing her identity! I truly cannot fathom what is going on in her head!"

—"Master, what do you think?"-Mo Xi asked Zhou Hui.

—"...I don't understand the thoughts of little girls,"-Zhou Hui said, utterly speechless.

Only Zhou Hui could refer to a Body Integration female powerhouse of the Upper Three Realms as a "little girl."

Zhou Hui flatly refused to ponder the inner workings of "little girls"—primarily because she was also a body cultivator. These two traits were the things Zhou Hui understood least in the world; merely contemplating what went on inside their heads felt like an utter waste of time.

Since no conclusions could be drawn from the dossier on Blood Demon Hall itself, Zhou Hui set down the exam papers and sighed:

—"Let us go inspect the Flame Crystal Flower first,"-Zhou Hui said.

***

All items registered for the auction, once authenticated, were temporarily stored inside the Heavenly Spirit Sect's vaults until purchased at the auction or returned to their original owners if passed over.

This feat alone was something most sects could never accomplish.

Because the cultivation world was overflowing with bizarre, peculiar treasures, failing to identify or handle them properly could result in them losing their original potency at best—or far worse, corroding their surroundings and inflicting catastrophic destruction.

The Flame Crystal Flower happened to be one of them.

However, since the Heavenly Spirit Sect dared to host this auction, they had naturally prepared countermeasures—all the more so for an item that served as their primary target.

Naturally, gaining access to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's auction "vault" required navigating layers of security measures. With Mo Xi's cultivation, even with the highest clearance granted by Zhou Hui, opening it would take around half a shichen.

Yet Zhou Hui was essentially the vault's master "lock" itself; whenever he wished to visit, he could simply step through at will.

With a sweep of Zhou Hui's sleeve, Mo Xi felt a swirl of cloudy mist shroud his eyes. When the mist cleared, he found himself standing in an environment like a blast furnace. Golden-red magma cascaded down like a roaring waterfall, pooling around his feet like a molten lake, while slender, white-snake-like creatures flicked gossamer-thin caudal fins as they swam through the lava.

These "snakes" dwelling within the magma were, of course, also among the registered auction items.

Yet in this grand auction, creatures far rarer than this were a dime a dozen. Mo Xi had already grown numb to such sights. Glancing left and right, he confirmed their orientation:

—"Master, this way. I placed the Flame Crystal Flower inside Magma Lake Number Three at the deepest point. I guarantee no one can touch it,"-Mo Xi said.

Zhou Hui nodded and teleported once more with Mo Xi.

When Mo Xi steadied his footing again, he saw that they hovered above a colossal magma lake. In the very center of the lake blossomed an enormous, water-lily-shaped flower. Its petals were burning cyan flames, yet its stamen was composed of golden crystals—it was hard to say whether it was a plant or a mineral.

Even Zhou Hui found the sight wondrous, all the more so after having traveled through another world. It truly impressed upon him how uniquely blessed the cultivation world was by the heavens:

Just consider it! In that other world, materials science was practically a [gacha game](# "Literal CN: 抽卡游戏 (chōukǎ yóuxì) — gacha card-drawing game; here referring to materials science research feeling like blind gacha pulls") with no rate-up banners—you could pour in millions or tens of millions of funds without producing so much as a ripple!

Here in the cultivation world, materials endowed with all sorts of miraculous properties came ready-made!

What was more, they even came with [built-in visual particle effects](# "Literal CN: 自带特效 (zìdài tèxiào) — coming with innate special visual effects; gaming/CGI trope"), making it infinitely easier for people to discern their properties!

Marveling inwardly, Zhou Hui closely examined this flower that he had failed to obtain across several lifetimes, only to let out a quiet "Huh?"

—"Master, is something amiss?"-Mo Xi asked worriedly.

The appraiser responsible for this item had been brought over by Tao Zhu. As the boss of the Abyssal Ocean Society who hunted treasures in the depths of the Abyss Sea year-round, Tao Zhu commanded an array of seasoned appraisers who rarely made mistakes.

Indeed, it wasn't a mistake on their part. Zhou Hui merely said:
—"The color of these petals is cyan-leaning-purple, not purple-leaning-cyan!"

[—"...Is there a difference between the two?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"There is. The former does not burn as hot as the latter,"-Zhou Hui explained. And what Zhou Hui needed was a Flame Crystal Flower with purple petals.

He remembered clearly that across the previous lifetimes when he had dispatched Yuwen Xuan to find this flower, he had provided detailed descriptions of its required characteristics and harvesting techniques.

In other words, even if Yuwen Xuan had seen this flower at the auction, he would never have bought it.

—"Did Eldest Senior Brother's subconscious suggestion include such detailed requirements?"-Mo Xi suspected nothing, merely rolling his eyes as he tried to throw shade at his senior:—"Still, given Eldest Senior Brother's personality, he might not have noticed the difference, or figured [making do](# "Literal CN: 凑合 (còuhe) — cutting corners or settling for good enough") was fine, right?"

—"In other matters, your Eldest Senior Brother might indeed cut corners, but when it comes to purchasing ingredients and materials, do you truly think he would settle for good enough?"-Zhou Hui looked at the Flame Crystal Flower and countered.

—"Ah, that's true..."-Mo Xi admitted.

Yuwen Xuan was the ultimate gourmet with an extraordinarily picky palate. If he demanded morning dew gathered during the Dragon Hour, a single sniff would tell him if it had been harvested during the Rabbit Hour. If he wanted a hen with a dark red comb, then even if the chicken were minced into fine paste, he could still taste the color of its comb!

This hyper-picky discernment regarding culinary ingredients carried over directly into his purchasing of other goods. All the more so for a "flower"—if the color was even slightly off, Yuwen Xuan was never fool enough to [waste spirit stones on the wrong purchase](# "Literal CN: 花冤枉钱 (huā yuānwang qián) — spending unjust/wasted money").

It was precisely for this reason that in previous lifetimes, Zhou Hui had entrusted the procurement of vital materials tied to his ascension to this most unreliable eldest disciple.

Because none of the other disciples possessed Yuwen Xuan's discerning eye.

So in other words, if this very Flame Crystal Flower had appeared at the Winter Solstice Auction in previous lifetimes, Yuwen Xuan would never have purchased it.

—"Could the suggestion have directed Eldest Senior Brother to buy a different one?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"For heavenly treasures of this caliber, having a single bloom appear at one time is already exceedingly rare,"-Zhou Hui replied.

It suddenly occurred to him: could an "unqualified Flame Crystal Flower" be the *true* trap?

After all, had Yuwen Xuan successfully bid on the exact item Zhou Hui wanted at the auction, he would have brought the flower straight back to the sect. Even if someone had tampered with the flower, the brunt of the trap would have fallen on Zhou Hui, not Yuwen Xuan.

Yet in previous lifetimes—as well as in the Heavenly Dao's script—Yuwen Xuan had vanished.

Could it be that Yuwen Xuan had seen this "immature flower," sought to trace its origin to find a "mature flower," and ended up going missing along the way?

—"Let me look into the source of this flower,"-Zhou Hui said.

He drifted over to the Flame Crystal Flower, submerged his hand directly into the multi-thousand-degree flame petals, and closed his eyes.

Temperatures of this magnitude could injure even a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator. Zhou Hui endured the searing pain as rapid visions flashed across his mind:

The heart of the Central Domain... the ruins of the God-Demon Battlefield... a land of eternal night where day and night blurred together... a spatial rift gnawed open by demons... and a sect situated directly within the rift—

Zhou Hui opened his eyes and pulled his hand back.

—"Master, are you alright?!"-Mo Xi cried, rushing forward in a panic as he retrieved an ointment and tried to apply it to Zhou Hui's charred-black palm.

—"Did you see where this flower came from?"-he asked.

—"No. It was blocked by the mountain-protecting array of a sect... presumably Blood Demon Hall,"-Zhou Hui said, furrowing his brow.

That barrier was unexpectedly formidable; Zhou Hui could not pass through silently without damaging it. Naturally, had Zhou Hui wished to violently tear it apart, he could easily have done so. Yet Blood Demon Hall had never provoked him, nor had they committed atrocities against heaven and earth. Tearing through someone's mountain-protecting array merely to trace an origin would be far too unreasonable.

This matter could be resolved entirely through peaceful means.

—"I wish to meet the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall,"-Zhou Hui said to Mo Xi.

—"Understood, Master. I will make arrangements immediately,"-Mo Xi replied, only to see a transmission talisman flying toward them.

Before he could unravel the talisman, Zhou Hui noted:

—"It seems there is no need. She has arrived on her own,"-Zhou Hui said.

Sure enough, the talisman informed Mo Xi that the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall had come to pay a visit.

***

—"Originally, I intended to discuss a business venture with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Artifact Refinement Hall, but sensing the Immortal Venerable deliberately 'passing by' my doorstep, I figured you must have business with me?"-Shen Qingqian asked, cutting straight to the chase.

As the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall, that mountain-protecting array was essentially an extension of herself; when Zhou Hui perceived the array, she had perceived him in return.

This was actually something worthy of immense pride. Consider Sovereign Immortal Chana's Thought Pavilion: even after Zhou Hui breached it, Chana had noticed nothing. Yet Blood Demon Hall, a mid-tier sect whose leader was "only" at the Body Integration stage, could detect Zhou Hui's approach—proving that Shen Qingqian was undeniably a powerhouse in the cultivation world.

Yet Shen Qingqian was in no mood to celebrate.

Her sensation was as horrifying as having an eldritch god cast a passing glance her way. Although the comparison was somewhat unfair to Zhou Hui—after all, Zhou Hui had done nothing hostile to any sect outside the Palace of Carefree Wandering—under the cultivation world's law of the jungle, referring to a titan two whole realms above oneself as an "eldritch god" was hardly inaccurate, even within the orthodox camp.

And standing before the Untiring Immortal Venerable in person, that sensation deepened even further.

A body cultivator's instincts were always razor-sharp. Even with Zhou Hui deliberately reigning in his aura, Shen Qingqian still sensed an overwhelming pressure—the bone-deep certainty that this man could crush her to death with a single finger.

Had Shen Qingqian possessed a tail, it would have been clamped tightly between her legs by now.

Yet the more frightened Shen Qingqian felt, the more dignified and serious she appeared on the surface—so much so that Bai Wubin glanced at his master repeatedly in bewilderment, amazed that she could ever be this solemn.

Zhou Hui's first impression of Shen Qingqian was quite favorable; he appreciated people who spoke frankly.

Since she had asked candidly, Zhou Hui answered with equal candor:
—"Indeed. I heard your sect registered a Flame Crystal Flower for the auction. As it happens, I require this material, so I went to inspect it. However, I discovered that its blooming stage does not match my needs. Thus, I sought to trace its origin to see if there might be one that meets my requirements,"-Zhou Hui explained.

Naturally, Zhou Hui wouldn't directly mention the bizarre suggestion lingering upon his eldest disciple, Yuwen Xuan.

Mo Xi assumed Master wished to conceal that detail, which was why he used this excuse.

Shen Qingqian, for her part, held zero interest in the fine details. Hearing that Zhou Hui had only peered at her sect's gates because of the auction item, she breathed an immense sigh of relief. As for whether the Untiring Immortal Venerable wanted the flower for himself or for a disciple, that was a trivial detail of no consequence to her.

Instead, she was somewhat astonished:
—"That 'flower' actually has a blooming period?"-she asked, somewhat astonished.

—"Indeed. Its color is cyan-leaning-purple, meaning it has not yet reached full bloom. What I require is the variety that is purple-leaning-cyan,"-Zhou Hui replied.

[—"Ohhh—"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

Shen Qingqian offered a polite yet awkward smile, clearly not understanding in the slightest.

However, since the Immortal Venerable had stated his requirements, business could be discussed:
—"We do indeed have several more flowers back home. While I don't know if they can satisfy the Venerable's requirements, we have nurtured them for—"-Shen Qingqian trailed off, suddenly blanking.

—"The oldest is a thousand-year Flame Crystal Flower, and there are several others around five hundred years,"-Bai Wubin supplemented.

—"To me, the age is not paramount; the crucial factor is the cultivation temperature,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Naturally, we paid great attention to that when nurturing them,"-Shen Qingqian said, glancing at her eldest disciple.

—"We cultivated them with Red Lotus Karmic Fire, supplemented by Phoenix Sand,"-Bai Wubin replied.

—"Those conditions are indeed sufficient,"-Zhou Hui said.

The advantages of the cultivation world's material diversity shone through once again.

Although cultivators had never developed the habit of recording empirical quantitative data, merely hearing "Red Lotus Karmic Fire" conveyed the approximate flame temperature. Perhaps it was precisely because they were so pampered by heaven and earth that people in this world could [get by living so vaguely](# "Literal CN: 含糊的过日子 (hánhu de guò rìzi) — muddling through life in a blur / living vaguely without rigorous precision").

Zhou Hui proceeded to ask several more questions, all of which Bai Wubin answered with thorough precision.

They even discussed what price would be required if there were a suitable Flame Crystal Flower without going through the auction process.

Even when facing a Tribulation Transcendence titan, Bai Wubin did not back down in the slightest. Citing historical auction prices from past years, he presented a firm, cogent case and quoted a price that was thoroughly fair and reasonable.

—"Fairy Shen, you have a fine disciple!"-Zhou Hui couldn't help sighing in praise.

This single compliment caused Mo Xi's heterochromatic cat-eyes to snap wide open. He glared warily at this Blood Demon Hall disciple.

—"Haha, not at all!"-Shen Qingqian smiled nonchalantly, evidently having heard such praise far too many times:—"Truth be told, I often wonder whether I could even survive without this disciple of mine!"

[Master, since you're self-aware enough to realize that, could you please act a little more prudent and serious?!]

Bai Wubin thought, utterly numb.

He cast a slightly envious glance across at the Untiring Immortal Venerable. In the brief exchange regarding the Flame Crystal Flower alone, Bai Wubin had learned countless things he had never noticed or grasped before. He couldn't help feeling that *this* was the ideal master he had always envisioned. By contrast, his own...

[? Why is that kid across from me glaring at me?]

Bai Wubin watched in utter bewilderment as Mo Xi took a deliberate step sideways, physically blocking his view of Zhou Hui.

Zhou Hui paid no mind to his disciple's petty theatrics. Although he had inquired extensively from Bai Wubin—and could even legitimately purchase the flower that had eluded him across several lifetimes through proper channels—for him in this life, having already abandoned ascension, the flower itself held zero value.

What Zhou Hui cared about far more now was the site where the Flame Crystal Flower grew.

—If his deductions were correct, had Yuwen Xuan failed to purchase his ideal material at the auction, he would very likely have traveled directly to the location where the Flame Crystal Flower was cultivated. In all probability, that was precisely where he had gone missing.

The results of drawing information out of Bai Wubin further corroborated this possibility:

Although Blood Demon Hall had presented the Flame Crystal Flower, it was glaringly obvious that neither Shen Qingqian nor Bai Wubin truly understood it. Their sect had simply been fortunate enough to find a spatial rift to establish their sect, discovered this rare treasure within the rift, tended to it casually in ordinary times, and harvested suitable blooms to auction off when the time came.

Judging by the past auction prices cited by Bai Wubin, they had even ruined several unbloomed Flame Crystal Flowers in the early days before gradually figuring out through trial and error what kind of flowers high-level cultivators actually desired.

Naturally, to Blood Demon Hall, this Flame Crystal Flower was simply a "gift of nature" and a "wild local specialty." They had neither the capability nor the courage to transplant it to a safer location, leaving it right where it originally grew and at most shielding it behind their mountain-protecting array.

Under ordinary circumstances, such defenses might suffice to deter petty thieves. But as for his own wild monkey...

It wasn't that Zhou Hui looked down on them, but Yuwen Xuan could sneak into his private study in the dead of night without making a sound; the defenses of a sect guarded merely by a Body Integration cultivator were, to him, no different from non-existent.

As for what he encountered there that caused his disappearance, one would only know after seeing it in person.

—"Since this concerns materials vital to ascension, I remain somewhat uneasy,"-Zhou Hui requested at last:—"Fairy Shen, might I inspect those Flame Crystal Flowers in person?"

—"Of course,"-Shen Qingqian nodded.

Although her calves still trembled looking at Zhou Hui, through the conversation between Zhou Hui and Bai Wubin, she had confirmed that this Immortal Venerable genuinely only wanted the Flame Crystal Flower—otherwise, why would a titan of his stature haggle and banter with her disciple for so long over such a thing?

As for their sect's location, since Zhou Hui had already found their mountain-protecting array, the location was clearly already exposed.

Besides, the "magical artifact" Shen Qingqian originally wished to commission would have exposed their sect anyway:
—"As it happens, I also have a request to trouble the Heavenly Spirit Sect with. Regarding your spiritual spring plumbing, could you perhaps design one for our sect as well—"-Shen Qingqian asked.

***

—"Master, are you truly going to Blood Demon Hall?!"-Mo Xi asked, dripping with jealousy, once Shen Qingqian and her disciple departed.

—"Didn't you hear? I intend to visit after the auction concludes,"-Zhou Hui gave him an odd look.

In truth, making a trip to Blood Demon Hall was effortless; for Zhou Hui, it was merely the work of an instant. However, prior to the Winter Solstice, Shen Qingqian had resolved to remain here at the Tianling Bazaar. Visiting in the absence of the host was exceedingly discourteous, which was why Zhou Hui had agreed with Shen Qingqian to visit only after the auction concluded.

—"Hmph, since it's Eldest Senior Brother's trouble, why not just let him go inspect it himself?"-Mo Xi grumbled, the sourness rolling off him in waves:—"Besides, with that oh-so-reliable 'Eldest Senior Brother' from Blood Demon Hall around, the two Eldest Senior Brothers together shouldn't run into any mishaps."

—"That is hard to say,"-Zhou Hui shook his head dismissively.

—"Is that brand on Eldest Senior Brother truly that dangerous?"-Mo Xi asked, peering at Zhou Hui's expression and immediately tensing up.

It was dangerous indeed; an artifact that even Zhou Hui failed to notice was naturally no ordinary commodity.

However, Zhou Hui could not explain the previous lifetimes to Mo Xi. He merely said:
—"I am simply somewhat concerned. Although that... Bai Wubin appears quite reliable, one never knows how things truly stand."

This was no mere brush-off; it was the honest truth.

Zhou Hui's trust in Blood Demon Hall wouldn't be forged through a few casual words. Thus, after seeing Shen Qingqian and Bai Wubin, he had pinched his fingers to divine their fates.

And this calculation unearthed quite a juicy revelation:

For reasons unknown, Shen Qingqian displayed a physiognomy of [premature demise](# "Literal CN: 命不久矣 (mìng bù jiǔ yǐ) — not long for this world; destined for an early grave"), and this fatal calamity corresponded directly to Bai Wubin!

Yet in Zhou Hui's calculations, Bai Wubin not only pointed toward a future of killing his own master, but posed an even greater mystery—he actually matched the Heavenly Dao's script!

As mentioned earlier, Blood Demon Hall, Shen Qingqian, and Bai Wubin never appeared in the Heavenly Dao's script. However, combining Bai Wubin's various physical traits, he matched the "Fierce Blood Demon Lord" to a tee.

Yet unlike the Heavenly Spirit Sect—where every last member was cast as a villain—the Fierce Blood Demon Lord existed as the protagonist's loyal sidekick! According to the plot synopsis, the Fierce Blood Demon Lord was originally the eldest disciple of some sect, suffered unfair abuse from his master, incurred [qi deviation](# "Literal CN: 走火入魔 (zǒuhuǒ rùmó) — cultivation energy backfire / qi deviation") in a fit of rage, slaughtered his entire sect, and thus degenerated into a demon lord. Yet because of this tragic backstory, he found common ground with the protagonist, loyally gifting artifacts, pills, and beauties along the way! In the end, after the protagonist ascended, he seemed to have completely forgotten his [sworn brother](# "Literal CN: 基友 (jīyǒu) — internet slang for close male friend, sworn bro, or sidekick")—even female cultivators who shared mere flirtatious banter ascended thanks to the protagonist, while this loyal bro was never mentioned again!

This plot was simply... beyond words.

Furthermore, looking at Bai Wubin and Shen Qingqian's dynamic, it hardly looked as though Bai Wubin was being mistreated!

In fact, Zhou Hui felt that while his own disciples showed clear embryonic tendencies toward going astray, this eldest disciple of Blood Demon Hall was an thoroughly upright, filial boy with zero inclination toward villainy. How on earth did the Heavenly Dao's script orchestrate his fall?

Zhou Hui was thoroughly baffled.

Mo Xi had no idea that in Zhou Hui's heart, Bai Wubin ranked as a model [child from another family](# "Literal CN: 别人家的孩子 (biérén jiā de háizi) — the proverbial flawless kid next door that parents endlessly compare their own children to"). Hearing Master declare that Bai Wubin was untrustworthy, he instantly beamed with joy.

—"Indeed! We can't expect outsiders to take our sect's troubles to heart, especially when our own Eldest Senior Brother is so remarkably unreliable,"-Mo Xi did not miss the chance to stomp on Yuwen Xuan again, sighing:—"Still, this appears to be an isolated incident! It must be that Eldest Senior Brother ran wild somewhere and drew an unnecessary temptation upon himself, right? Here I thought it had something to do with the Palace of Carefree Wandering!"

At this point, Mo Xi sounded genuinely disappointed:

—"In that case, the Palace of Carefree Wandering has been so well-behaved this time; they haven't done a single thing,"-Mo Xi added.

He had thought Master might be able to [lop off another of their arms](# "Literal CN: 砍掉他们一只手 — sever one of their limbs/arms; metaphor for eliminating a key faction pillar or high-ranking powerhouse") (another Tribulation Transcendence sovereign).

***

What Mo Xi didn't know was that while he was feeling disappointed, someone else was burning with frantic panic over this very matter:

For instance, at this very moment, Daoist Huang of the Wanhe Gang was shouting into the water mirror:

—"Our plan is a massive success! An absolute triumph!"-he bellowed.


—"Is that so?"-the figure on the other side of the water mirror asked, sounding deeply skeptical:—"Then how have I heard that while the Heavenly Spirit Sect's auction has yet to commence, their Immortal Bazaar has already unfurled an immense spectacle?"

—"That is merely surface-level posturing!"-Daoist Huang stammered in panic, not daring to wipe the cold sweat dripping down his face:—"As you know, our Sect Leader didn't dare offend *that person*, so he brought back a massive haul of teleportation coral beads. To prevent the majority of sects from being coerced by those beads, we distributed them to mortals and demons! That way, the crowd naturally appears large! The Heavenly Spirit Sect, wishing to save face, refused to drive those mortals away. As a result, their bazaar looks bustling on the surface, but in reality, it's nothing to write home about! They're all selling mortal produce and trivial knick-knacks that have virtually nothing to do with us cultivators!"

—"I certainly hope so,"-the voice on the other side of the water mirror sneered coldly.

Seeing that the other party didn't press further, Daoist Huang let out a breath of relief:
—"...My Lord, I can assure you, it is absolutely...!!! M-My Lord, what is this?!"

Daoist Huang stared in sheer terror at a brand that had materialized on the back of his hand.

Just a moment ago, he had seen the figure in the water mirror wave a hand toward him. He had instinctively raised his arm to block—and this brand had appeared upon his hand.

[—"Nothing of great consequence. Merely an extension of my eyes, so that I may witness the auction for myself."](# "Speaker: Shadowy Figure")

Daoist Huang shuddered in horror.

—"I hope you are not lying to me. Otherwise—"-the figure sneered coldly, his silhouette dissolving from the other side of the water mirror.

Daoist Huang collapsed limp onto the floor, clutching the brand on his hand, panic surging through his chest.

Regardless of what Daoist Huang claimed with his lips, having personally stood here at the Immortal Bazaar, his heart knew the truth better than anyone:

[Damn it! I truly did slash the number of orthodox cultivators and major sects!]

Across the entire Central Domain, there were eleven immortal cities and fifty-three sects of medium scale or above. Yet under Daoist Huang's machinations, only five immortal cities and twenty sects were participating in this auction—a fraction far lower than historical turnout!

So he hadn't lied: the vast majority of people here were indeed worthless trash-spirit-root cultivators and mortals!

Yet why was the Heavenly Spirit Sect able to stage a spectacular Immortal Bazaar with just these people?!

And why did it seem vastly more fascinating and bustling than in any prior year?!

—"I-It doesn't matter! It's just superficial hype! Once the truly valuable items at the auction fail to sell and get passed over, they will understand! Trash rogue cultivators and mortals alone cannot possibly sustain an auction of this scale!"-Daoist Huang muttered frantically to himself.

At this point, he not only had to deceive others, but desperately [hypnotize himself](# "Literal CN: 催眠自己 (cuīmián zìjǐ) — modern slang for extreme psychological self-deception and copium") as well.

Yet before he could finish hypnotizing himself, the water mirror flared to life again, and City Lord Feng of Fengqing City appeared on the other side.

Daoist Huang was forced to begin a brand-new round of frantic rationalization:

—"Yes, you need not come! Being swayed by their hype at a time like this would only be your loss! The auction will definitely flop! Yes, yes, those mortals made money? To inflate the bazaar's popularity, the Heavenly Spirit Sect naturally had to dole out a few petty favors! Besides, the spirit stones they bring back will end up right in your tax coffers anyway, won't they?!"-Daoist Huang babbled.

[—"Indeed! This auction is absolutely doomed to fail!"](# "Speaker: Daoist Huang")
