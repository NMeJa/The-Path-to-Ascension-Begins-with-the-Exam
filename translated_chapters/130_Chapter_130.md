# Chapter 130: Divinity (第130章 神格)

[← Previous Chapter](129_Chapter_129.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](131_Chapter_131.md)

---

—"Ah Ran!"-Zhou Hui called out, catching Ran Deng with anxious haste. Taking hold of her wrist, he examined her meridians.

—"She's fine,"-Zhou Hui sighed in relief, speaking to the little black lamb pawing at Ran Deng's hem and looking up with tilted head.-"She must have passed the Ascension Trial and fainted from sheer overwhelming excitement."

[...Master, are you certain she fainted out of excitement?]

Even Yuwen Xuan felt his master was [practically begging to be roasted](# "Literal CN: 满是槽点 (mǎn shì cáodiǎn) — completely riddled with points to roast / full of comedic flaws").

Zhou Hui was completely oblivious.

Sorting through his storage pouch, he selected a low-temperature cloud flecked with frost crystals, kneading it into a cloud bed before carefully settling Ran Deng upon it.

Lying on the squishy, soft cloud bed, the frost crystals subdued her soul flame slightly, allowing her expression to ease into something a bit more peaceful.

Yet this was merely a temporary stopgap.

Though Ran Deng had fainted, the refinement process of her soul flame would not cease. Consequently, even in her dreams, she was bound to continue cultivating. For the next several decades, this would remain a nightmare from which she could never awaken.

Even Yuwen Xuan, watching from the side, found the sight too agonizing to bear.

At this very moment, he genuinely felt that being a silly little sheep... no, a Taotie, wasn't half bad! Just by eating, eating, and eating, he could level up—no exams, no homework, and he could even get cuddled by Master. What bliss!

Zhou Hui had no idea his eldest disciple had taken yet another step down the path of total degeneracy.

Turning to Yuwen Xuan, he asked:

—"Explain in detail. What did the two of you encounter over there?"

—"? Didn't you appear at the very end, Master? I thought you saw everything,"-the little black lamb asked, momentarily taken aback.

—"No, the situation on my end was rather unique..."-Zhou Hui said.-"First, tell me what happened with Ah Ran in detail."

—"Well, I don't really understand it myself,"-Yuwen Xuan admitted.

His perspective differed somewhat from Ran Deng's.

In truth, as half of a ferocious beast, the Ascension Stairway's trial technically did not apply to Yuwen Xuan at all.

As a creature of the upper realm, a Taotie could cross between the two worlds with ease—no exams required—simply by ambling up the Ascension Stairway and slipping through the Immortal Gate.

The little white lamb had previously exploited this exact property of the Ascension Stairway to escape from Zhou Hui's storage pouch.

Hearing this, Zhou Hui patted his storage pouch.

Presently, the little white lamb was muzzled, all four of its hooves trussed up, and tossed into the storage pouch beneath twelve layers of restrictive seals. Even if a Daluo Golden Immortal arrived in person, they would have to defeat Zhou Hui first before releasing it.

Yet this also meant that, compared to the white lamb, the black lamb could not utilize the full breadth of a Taotie's abilities.

The Immortal Gate, for instance.

The reason Yuwen Xuan could not see it was that his human flesh and blood still persisted, fused into his current vessel through a peculiar form. Influenced by this mortal half, he could neither take the examination directly nor perceive the Immortal Gate.

As a result, when Ran Deng "traversed," Yuwen Xuan had followed along, but only incompletely.

—"To be honest, it felt like I was wedged in a crevice—unable to go in and unable to get out,"-the little black lamb complained, sounding thoroughly aggrieved.-"One half was being dragged back and forth by Junior Sister, while the other half couldn't budge at all."

Pitiable though it sounded, Zhou Hui inexplicably found himself picturing a sheep stuck in a crack—scruff gripped by the neck and hoisted upward, its black body stretching out like plasticine... [Ah, that's a feline privilege! Stop letting your thoughts wander!]

Reining in his focus, Zhou Hui continued listening to his eldest disciple.

Yet even with only half of himself there, Yuwen Xuan had still gleaned a few things.

—"You recovered your memories?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Can't really call it recovering!"-Yuwen Xuan answered awkwardly.-"Once I saw it, I just knew."

—"Hmm—"-Zhou Hui narrowed his eyes, staring at his eldest disciple for a long while.

Yuwen Xuan instantly felt a terrible premonition.

Scrambling to steer back to safer conversational waters, he hurriedly continued:

—"Anyway, at the time, Junior Sister was..."

—"Come to think of it,"-Zhou Hui interjected,-"back at the teleportation array beneath Blood Demon Hall, you mentioned that there were Taotie bones and blood there. You swallowed them, suffered indigestion, and turned into this ghostly state, didn't you?"

[—"Y-Yes."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

—"Back then, I was solely focused on the teleportation array and didn't pay much attention to that detail,"-Zhou Hui mused.-"Reflecting on it now, compared to the white lamb's 'memories,' the bulk of the memories should actually reside within the body, shouldn't they? After all, that is where the 'hard drive' is."

Indeed: the physical body was the hard drive. Generally speaking, memories were stored in the brain.

This was also why the ghosts of most mortals had hazy memories, driven solely by their most intense primal emotions.

The cultivation process of cultivators, in essence, involved expanding the capacity of the soul—the 'cache.' This was what allowed someone seizing a body or reincarnating to carry along all their memories. Even so, prior to the Mahayana stage, if an orphaned soul failed to find a host body in time, the memories stored within would eventually disperse.

Upon reaching the rank of True Immortal, the physical body and the soul became equally solid, both capable of storing complete sets of memories. Moreover, these memories were 'synchronized.' At that tier, a higher-dimensional network seemingly existed across their physical tissues; even a single strand of hair could reference the primary body's backup unless severed by restrictive seals.

Zhou Hui did not know the exact mechanics of a Taotie, but as a creature of the upper realm, it was likely extraordinarily similar to a True Immortal.

Judging by how he had probed the little white lamb's memories without finding any trace of seven reincarnations—yet the white lamb had still known his circumstances—Zhou Hui suspected the white lamb had retrieved those memories from somewhere else.

That "somewhere" was in all likelihood its physical body.

As for why Yuwen Xuan lacked this capability, it was probably because his human flesh and blood formed a barrier in between, hindering the read process of the Taotie's memories. Only when an object was directly before his eyes could he "recall" it.

—"Which means,"-Zhou Hui deduced,-"reading your memories would actually offer far greater insight into the Taotie's past?"

—"Do you want to soul-scour me?"-the black lamb asked.

With a soft *thud*, it flopped onto the steps, sticking out its plump little round belly like a carcass resting on a chopping block, entirely at Master's culinary mercy.

Zhou Hui, however, had no interest in cooking it.

—"Soul-scouring is far too crude,"-Zhou Hui said.-"I could do that to the white lamb's half because it is purely a Taotie; scouring its soul wouldn't harm it in the slightest. But you possess half a human soul and are only at Soul Formation. A single soul-scour would ruin you."

The little black lamb instantly grew nervous:

—"Do you need me to break through as soon as possible? I'm actually almost ready to enter the Void Refinement stage anyway. I just need to eat a little more..."-it said, perking its two ears up like a rabbit and rolling its eyes shifty-eyed.

—"There is no rush to advance,"-Zhou Hui said.-"You haven't even digested all the demons, fiends, and ghosts you devoured earlier, have you? Without a suitable trial to temper your breakthrough, blindly forcing an advancement—even if successful—would only drag you closer to becoming a true Taotie—"-here, Zhou Hui fixed the little black lamb with a stern, solemn glare.-"You don't harbor some silly notion that being a Taotie is preferable, do you?"

—"How could I?!"-The little black lamb shook its head so furiously it looked like a rattle drum.-"I would never want to turn into a Taotie!"

Discarding his identity as a human would make Master furious, perhaps even prompting Master to abandon him. Naturally, he would never do such a thing.

Yet in thinking this way, Yuwen Xuan's sole consideration was Master—the last time he had transformed back into a human had also been for Master's sake—without giving a single thought to what *he himself* truly wanted.

Unaware of the messy tangle of thoughts inside the little black lamb's head, Zhou Hui nodded in satisfaction:

—"Good."

—"Right? Anyway, whether we know those Taotie memories or not doesn't really matter,"-Yuwen Xuan tried to steer the topic back.-"Let me continue about Junior Sister's trial. At the time, she—"

This time, Zhou Hui did not interrupt, quietly listening to the black lamb's report in full.

It was just that the look in his eyes made Yuwen Xuan thoroughly uneasy.

At the very end, the little black lamb attempted to claim credit:

—"It was all thanks to me! Otherwise, let alone passing the trial, Junior Sister would have been lucky just to keep her life."

—"And yet,"-Zhou Hui pointed out,-"when it comes to who posed the greatest threat to Ah Ran's life, wasn't it you?"

He was referring to the Taotie half.

Thinking carefully about Ran Deng's trial, whether it was the little white lamb pushing her down the stairs and trying to devour her, or the apocalyptic Taotie chasing her down ten thousand years ago, the only thing that had truly threatened her life was the Taotie!

The other "trials," by contrast, had been utterly trivial to Ran Deng.

Naturally, Yuwen Xuan refused to accept the blame:

—"Those are them, and I am me! Besides, was that Taotie from ten thousand years ago really me? It's not like I'm the only Taotie in the entire universe!"

—"No, it is very likely you, or at the very least directly related to you,"-Zhou Hui analyzed.-"Although there is more than one Taotie across the three thousand worlds, Taoties are hardly easy to come by. Particularly given that Taoties possess an innate habit of prioritizing devouring their own kind."

This was also why that Taotie had chased after Ran Deng: Ran Deng was carrying the little black lamb (or a piece of him). Regardless of whether the little black lamb was wedged inside a temporal fissure, the moment a Taotie sensed its kin, it would prioritize devouring the other above all else—even if that "other" was its own future self. After all, a single world could never sustain two Taoties.

—"Given that this world was ultimately not devoured by that Taotie, it must have been defeated in the end. Its ferocious malice then recongealed into new larval Taoties—namely you and the white lamb. That probability is exceptionally high,"-Zhou Hui concluded.

His deliberations had also yielded a resolution.

—"As expected, we must have you restore your memories,"-Zhou Hui finally concluded.-"Since you can only 'recover' memories upon seeing tangible objects, I will simply devise a way to provide you with phantom projections similar to the Ascension Stairway. You will search the Taotie's memories based on those prompts, and then copy them out by hand—"

—"Hand-copying again?!"-The little black lamb's eyes went wide.

—"Yes, hand-copying,"-Zhou Hui cast a stern glance at his disciple.-"Do not look down on hand-copying. It will be immensely beneficial for organizing your memories."

Whether he was a Taotie or not.

Although cultivation enhanced one's memory—and by the late Foundation Establishment stage, most cultivators achieved [eidetic memory](# "Literal CN: 过目不忘 (guòmù bùwàng) — photographic memory / never forgetting what passes before one's eyes")—in terms of practical efficacy, cultivators rarely acted as if they possessed such a gift.

This was because even if most cultivators retained everything they saw, it was akin to dumping unorganized files onto a hard drive. Without an 'index,' they might never recall the information in their entire lives.

Hand-copying was the process of building that index. It did not strictly demand comprehension; it merely ensured that whenever the data was needed, one could locate the relevant files.

Besides, Qi Condensation cultivators couldn't even achieve eidetic memory. If one failed to cultivate the habit of building their own internal "library" early on, they would never develop such a habit later in their cultivation journey.

Hence, Zhou Hui frequently assigned hand-copying homework.

Especially to the "poor-memory" Yuwen Xuan!

This fellow's brain was like an unorganized bookshelf—a chaotic disaster crammed with all sorts of junk. He had no clue where to find the knowledge he needed, relying purely on "instinct" to act.

Reflecting on it carefully, this little scoundrel hadn't become like this only after turning into a sheep; he had been like this since childhood!

Thinking of this, Zhou Hui revealed a kindly, gentle smile:

—"For this assignment, only you can perceive the Taotie's memories, which means you must copy them entirely by yourself! Don't even think about roping in your junior brothers to find ghostwriters for you!"

Rolling its eyes, the little black lamb collapsed back onto the ground with a soft *thud*, remaining completely motionless.

[*Sob, sob, sob...* If I have to hand-copy texts, I'd rather have Master soul-scour me!]

Zhou Hui let out a cold snort.

He had zero intention of giving the little black lamb the same gentle treatment he gave Ran Deng.

Having finished assigning homework to his foolish eldest disciple, Zhou Hui finally had the peace of mind to contemplate the "serious business."

Zhou Hui murmured in analysis:

—"I see. Judging by Ah Ran's experience, the Ascension Stairway is an entity constructed within a higher spatial dimension. It remains unaffected by time and space; regardless of whether it is physically destroyed, anyone who satisfies the rules can undergo the trial. Naturally. Only in this manner could it bridge two worlds with different spatial dimensions and disparate temporal flow rates."

—"The thing that truly disrupted the old Ascension Stairway,"-Zhou Hui continued,-"was actually Ah Ran's final act."

—"Seizing the window when I was reconstructing the Ascension Stairway, she channeled faith directly to my side, thereby granting me the authority to formulate its rules..."

Speaking thus, Zhou Hui walked toward the Ascension Stairway, arriving at the exact location of the "Immortal Gate" Ran Deng had just crossed.

Naturally, in Zhou Hui's eyes, the "Immortal Gate" still did not exist.

At this position, there was only a dense, thick bank of cloud mist, into which the steps of the Ascension Stairway climbed one by one, appearing endless.

In fact, that was precisely the reality.

Had Zhou Hui continued walking upward, he would have encountered the exact same steps over and over. Even if he walked upon them for a lifetime, he would never find the summit, nor could he ever reach the void beyond this world; he would only wander lost upon the endless stairs.

Therefore, Zhou Hui did not continue climbing.

Standing at the threshold of that Immortal Gate, he made a gesture toward the dense cloud layer as though drawing back a theatrical curtain.

Like a curtain, the clouds parted before Zhou Hui's hands, instantly revealing the scene concealed behind—

—"Master, what is that?!"-the little black lamb cried out in shock, no longer able to bother playing dead upon seeing the sight behind the clouds.

Leaping to its hooves, it asked in a loud voice:

—"The White Jade Capital, of course,"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"I know it's the White Jade Capital, but why is it here?!"-the little black lamb asked.

In sheer disbelief, it stared at the spectacle atop the Ascension Stairway.

Indeed: there stood a brand-new gate.

A grand city gate emblazoned with the characters: **White Jade Capital**.

The White Jade Capital that had formerly lain buried deep underground had somehow materialized in the sky, taking the place of the ancient Immortal Gate that once led to the True Immortal Realm, standing tall once more amidst the high heavens!

—"A moment ago, didn't you ask what I was doing while Ah Ran was undergoing her trial? This was it,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.

It turned out that at the exact instant Ran Deng pushed open the Immortal Gate, Zhou Hui had likewise perceived an anomaly in time and space.

However, unlike Ran Deng's tangible "transmigration" into the temporal fissure, Zhou Hui's "task" as the rebuilder of the Ascension Stairway was to re-weave its "rules."

In other words, Zhou Hui had been granted a single opportunity to re-designate where the Ascension Stairway led.

If it had been merely that, it would have been noteworthy enough.

What brought Zhou Hui true, unbounded ecstasy was that through this opening, he had gained the chance to peek into the Heavenly Dao itself:

To borrow terminology from another world: Zhou Hui had gained the chance to hack into the system backend!

All along, this was precisely why Zhou Hui had felt powerless to oppose the Heavenly Dao: as a native inhabitant of this world, even if his stats broke the ceiling, he still could not modify the Heavenly Dao's underlying code—just as an in-game character could never edit the source program, while the programmers would love nothing more than to scrub him out as a bug.

The only way Zhou Hui could alter the Heavenly Dao was through ascension.

If he became a resident of the upper realm, he "might" gain an opportunity to modify this world's Heavenly Dao.

Unfortunately, after forfeiting his ascension in his very first incarnation, he seemed to have been placed on the upper realm's permanent blacklist.

Unable to step outside this world, Zhou Hui could not interfere with the Heavenly Dao, nor could he alter so-called "Heavenly Destiny." Yet this incident of reconstructing the Ascension Stairway had allowed Zhou Hui to peer through the cracks of time and space, discerning the inner workings of the Heavenly Dao.

Naturally, Zhou Hui had not let this opportunity slip by. On the spot, he had recoded a brand-new set of rules (programs) for the Ascension Stairway, even exceeding the boundaries originally permitted.

And Ran Deng had pressed the "Enter key" for him.

Now, the new rules had completely overwritten the old ones.

The spatio-temporal coordinates were consequently reset.

Thus, anyone climbing the Ascension Stairway in the future would no longer ascend to the True Immortal Realm, but would travel instead to the destination Zhou Hui had designated!

In the cultivation world, there was a far more common term for what Zhou Hui had done:

—"S-So, Master... does this mean you possess divinity?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

—"Not quite. I merely gained administrative rights over the rules of the Ascension Stairway, much like how I gained control of the White Jade Capital. That is why I placed them together,"-Zhou Hui said dismissively.-"My power has not yet reached the level where I can rewrite the Heavenly Dao."

[......You don't need to rewrite the Heavenly Dao to be considered a god!]

Yuwen Xuan thought to himself. He had always felt his master held himself to impossibly exorbitant standards.

In Yuwen Xuan's eyes, Zhou Hui was already a living deity.

He watched his master stand before the gates of the White Jade Capital, lightly pushing open the portal. Iridescent rays of auspicious dawn light poured from beyond the threshold, spilling over his master's immaculate white outer robe like the feathered raiment of a True Immortal... No. Even the True Immortals he had witnessed ten thousand years ago did not possess a fraction of the grace found in a single strand of Master's hair.

Yuwen Xuan could not help but let out an enraptured sigh.

—"Right, what are the new rules of the Ascension Stairway now?"-Yuwen Xuan considered this question trivial, but figured he ought to ask anyway to show some academic initiative.-"Like Junior Sister, passing the trial means returning to Master's side?"

—"Certainly not. Though on that point, I will explain it to everyone,"-Zhou Hui gave the little black lamb a subtle smile, then turned his gaze toward the distance:

—"Well then. It is just about time to invite our guests over."

***

On the other side, the confrontation between the Tribulation Transcendence titans remained in a tense standoff.

Although Jian Haoge had blocked Goddess Renran using his painted city ramparts, as Tribulation Transcendents, engaging in outright combat was far from simple. After all, the slightest carelessness could permanently alter the geography and ecological state of an entire region.

Furthermore, once powers of the Tribulation Transcendence realm clashed, the battle would not conclude easily.

Jian Haoge merely hoped to buy Zhou Hui a bit of time.

And in this regard, Zhou Hui never let Jian Haoge down.

Although the "trial" had felt extraordinarily prolonged for Ran Deng, to these Tribulation Transcendents, that span of time was hardly enough for a warm-up.

Just as Goddess Renran summoned her dharma artifact, preparing to pierce through Jian Haoge's obstruction in a single blow and fly straight toward the Ascension Stairway, Xin Zhu's exclamation rang out:

—"What is that?"

The crowd turned their gazes back toward the Ascension Stairway, only to see that upon the ninth heaven, it was no longer merely a solitary flight of stairs. Several grand celestial cities loomed faintly amidst the churning clouds.

[—"An immortal citadel?"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Cultivators")-exclaimed the cultivators born after the God-Demon War.

[—"The White Jade Capital?!"](# "Speaker: Goddess Renran")-Goddess Renran gasped.

The cultivators all turned to look at Goddess Renran. She let out a cold sneer:

—"What immortal citadel? Can none of you even recognize a city that once stood in your own world?"

—"Indeed, we cannot. After all, we aren't like you—living for ages only to have lived entirely in vain,"-Jian Haoge retorted.

—"You!"-The divine maiden gritted her teeth in fury.

Ignoring her, Jian Haoge turned to ask the others:

—"Supposing for a moment that it truly is the White Jade Capital, how did it appear?"

[—"It must be Untiring again?"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

[—"Naturally, who else could it be? But how on earth did he locate the Ascension Stairway and find the White Jade Capital on top of that?"](# "Speaker: Ancestor Jiuzhai")

—"I suspect the opposite is true. Perhaps while searching for the White Jade Capital, he happened upon the Ascension Stairway by chance?"-Su Mei analyzed, sounding eminently reasonable.-"During New Year, when he discussed the Immortal Arts Assembly with us, he mentioned having uncovered clues regarding the White Jade Capital. In hindsight, while searching for the White Jade Capital, he must have stumbled upon the collapsed Ascension Stairway and restored them together!"

—"Impossible—"-Goddess Renran almost blurted out.

*The Ascension Stairway was clearly handed over to Carefree Palace!*

Yet before the words left her lips, Sikong Yao swiftly grabbed her sleeve.

He hadn't formally resigned from Carefree Palace yet!

He certainly didn't want Carefree Palace's dirty laundry aired in broad daylight!

Glaring daggers at Sikong Yao, the goddess yanked her sleeve free and let out a frigid huff.

The other Tribulation Transcendents tactfully feigned blindness to the silent squabble unfolding between the two.

Ancestor Jiuzhai sighed:

—"Never did I imagine that in this lifetime, I would witness the day the White Jade Capital re-emerged into the world."

—"Still, what is truly going on with the Ascension Stairway and the White Jade Capital?"-Xin Zhu pondered aloud.

Jian Haoge sneered:

—"At any rate, it appears the Ascension Stairway certainly won't be leading to the Demon Realm."

—"Who knows?"-Stung by the barb, Goddess Renran glared at Jian Haoge.-"Regardless of what tricks Zhou Hui is playing, he has seized both the Ascension Stairway and the White Jade Capital for himself. The rest of you can just suck your thumbs and watch from afar! Let's see if he even gives you the time of day—"

Before Goddess Renran could finish her sentence, a resonant voice drifted in from the distance:

—"My apologies for keeping everyone waiting. The Ascension Stairway has now been tidied up, and I have [swept the couch](# "Literal CN: 扫榻以待 (sǎotà yǐdài) — an ancient idiom meaning to sweep clean a guest bed/couch in eager anticipation of welcoming esteemed guests") in anticipation of your arrival."

Along with those words, the clouds parted like the sea, opening a pristine corridor of azure sky leading directly toward the Ascension Stairway.

Goddess Renran's face instantly flushed red and white in rapid succession—every bit as colorful as the shifting clouds.

***

What left Goddess Renran even more utterly struck dumb, however, were Zhou Hui's intentions:

—"You intend to open both the Ascension Stairway and the White Jade Capital to the entire world?!"-Ancestor Jiuzhai exclaimed in shock.

—"Indeed. The Ascension Stairway was originally meant to accept anyone regardless of background, turning none away,"-Zhou Hui replied with a genial smile.-"Moreover, as it stands, the Ascension Stairway seems incapable of leading to the True Immortal Realm—though naturally, it may simply be that my own [modest learning](# "Literal CN: 才疏学浅 (cáishū xuéqiǎn) — sparse talent and shallow learning; a traditional self-deprecating modesty formula") was insufficient to achieve ascension. Perhaps others might pass the trial; one never knows."

Zhou Hui revealed only half the truth. He naturally wouldn't blabber openly about how he had rewritten the system rules, let alone his ongoing research into hacking the Heavenly Dao.

Even so, the Tribulation Transcendents present already felt dizzy.

They weren't blind!

The divine aura of divinity upon Zhou Hui was something they could all perceive with absolute clarity.

Upon beholding the current Zhou Hui, even Renran didn't dare launch an immediate attack.

Everyone understood that Zhou Hui must have attained something profound, yet none knew precisely what heights he had reached.

Yet what earned their deepest reverence was that, even having achieved this, Zhou Hui still intended to open both the Ascension Stairway and the White Jade Capital to the world.

Jian Haoge looked as heartbroken as if his own Sky City were being given away for free:

—"That is the White Jade Capital you uncovered yourself. You're just giving access away like that?"

—"Not quite,"-Zhou Hui said.-"The White Jade Capital consists of [Twelve Towers and Five Cities](# "Literal CN: 十二楼五城 (shí'èr lóu wǔ chéng) — Twelve Towers and Five Cities of the White Jade Capital, from Li Bai's famous poem: 'In the heavenly White Jade Capital, twelve towers and five cities'"). I have positioned them at various intervals along the Ascension Stairway. Only those who pass the Ascension Stairway's trials may enter them. As for the central metropolis of the White Jade Capital, beyond the Ascension Stairway's trial, there is an additional qualification requirement."

[—"A qualification requirement?"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

[—"Cultivation realm?"](# "Speaker: Ancestor Jiuzhai")

—"No. It is one's positive impact upon our realm,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"For the time being, take research published in *Spells* as an example: the more a spell from the journal is utilized by cultivators, and the broader the demographic it benefits, the greater one's impact score."

—"Hah! The journal *Spells* was founded by you! After all that talk, isn't the final qualification still held firmly in your hands?"-Goddess Renran nitpicked.

—"Anyone else is more than welcome to found academic journals as well!"-Zhou Hui chuckled with total nonchalance.-"In the end, the evaluation standard isn't decided by me, but by the citation count and user base of the technique."

Hearing him put it like that, Goddess Renran merely scoffed, while the Plum-Bamboo couple exchanged contemplative glances.

Ancestor Jiuzhai murmured:

—"This... this is monumental!"

Cultivators might not necessarily wish to permanently reside in the White Jade Capital, but driven by their yearning for the upper realm, who among them wouldn't strive to enter it?

Only the little black lamb mimicked a goat, letting out a soft *baa*.

It felt that its master was truly rotten to the core.

Still, he loved it.
