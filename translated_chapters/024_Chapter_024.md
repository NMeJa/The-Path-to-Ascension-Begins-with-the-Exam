# Chapter 24: [A Dog in Cat's Clothing...](# "Literal CN: 披着猫皮的狗…… — playing on 'a wolf in sheep's clothing', contrasting Mo Xi's innocent kitten-like appearance with his predatory 'dog game developer' instincts")

Youngest Junior Brother was adorable.

This was the collective delusion shared by most disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

To put it bluntly, when Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi had first met Mo Xi, they had suffered from the exact same misconception. Later, once they grew familiar with him, it wasn't that they stopped finding Junior Brother Mo cute, but rather that beneath that adorable exterior lurked an undercurrent of something else entirely—something so unsettling that no one dared examine it too closely.

If one were pressed to find an analogy, he was like a [cursed puppet](# "Literal CN: 诅咒人偶 (zǐzhòu réng'ǒu) — unsettling porcelain/voodoo doll vibe").

It didn't help that Mo Xi possessed an androgynous face of ambiguous gender. At first glance, he looked darling, but once you got to know him, that skin-crawling sense of discomfort multiplied twofold.

Yet the Mo Xi standing behind them today had completely shed that feminine, eerie aura.

Having not seen him for just a few days, Mo Xi appeared to have grown several centimeters taller. His exquisite face remained adorable, but he now looked unmistakably like a boy. He was no longer like before, where whenever he deliberately kept silent, it was practically impossible to tell him and Mo Bei apart.

However, what stood out most about Mo Xi now was the addition of a golden-crimson dragon tattoo. The dragon's head was nowhere to be seen; only its tail emerged from the nape of Mo Xi's neck, coiling through the red-streaked strands of his sideburns before its caudal fin playfully peeked onto his right cheek. One could clearly feel the vigorous Yang spiritual energy radiating from it.

Perhaps owing to this, Mo Xi appeared even more innocent and adorable than before—like a pampered young master raised in a respectable, wealthy household, exuding a radiant, sunshiny warmth and the naive, righteous innocence of someone who had never known the harsh realities of the world.

Sporting this very face, Mo Xi asked with a beaming smile:
—"Of course I am Mo Xi,"-he said.-"Does Senior Brother Xiang Rong find something amiss with me?"

[...His condition has clearly gotten worse,] Xiang Rong assessed inwardly.

Naturally, he wouldn't be foolish enough to say that directly:
—"I was just wondering where Junior Brother Mo got this dragon tattooed!"-Xiang Rong forced out a dry laugh.-"It's genuinely handsome!"

—"Very handsome, right?"-Mo Xi's eyes lit up as he stroked the caudal fin on the edge of his cheek, delighted.-"Master gifted it to me. Nobody else has one."

[...Well, that completely kills the conversation!]

Xiang Rong's lips twitched.

Mo Xi couldn't care less about his underling's discontent. Turning toward the Formation Simulation Hall, he cut straight to the chase:
—"Alright, what was that about you two not being able to figure out a way to make money?"-Mo Xi asked.

Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi's attention was instantly yanked back.

[Did whatever had happened to Junior Brother Mo matter more than making money? Obviously not!]

Xiang Yi launched into a detailed description of everything that had happened during the days Mo Xi was absent. In the end, she cast a wistful glance toward the chess—or rather, the Formation Simulation—chamber:
—"Most disciples have already obtained their own chess pieces by now,"-she lamented.-"If you'd seen it a few days ago, the foot traffic was even more terrifying! Disciples had to pull numbered tickets just to queue up for an open spot."

Commercial opportunities were fleeting like that, which was precisely why Xiang Yi and Xiang Rong were in such an anxious fret.

—"Is this chess game really that much fun?"-Mo Xi asked, somewhat unable to wrap his head around it.

His impression of playing chess was limited to Fourth Senior Brother and Fifth Senior Brother hunkering over a Go board. At the shortest, a game lasted several hours; at the longest, it could drag on for an entire month, boring onlookers to tears and yawns. Even Master disdained them for wasting time.

Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi were clearly not that sort of 'refined, cultured gentlemen' either, yet bringing up 'playing chess' this time had gotten both of them thoroughly worked up.

—"Speaking of which, Junior Brother Mo hasn't played it yet, right?"-Xiang Rong asked excitedly.-"How about I teach you how to play?"

By now, every disciple in the Heavenly Spirit Sect had gotten their hands on it for at least a couple of rounds. A completely pristine novice was terribly hard to come by!

And this was Mo Xi, of all people!

The very Junior Brother Mo who routinely bullied and pushed him around!

In reality, he certainly wouldn't dare strike him, nor could he win in a fight. But on the chessboard, hacking him to death fair and square wouldn't be a problem, right?!

Xiang Rong swore he had zero intention of seeking revenge.

Mo Xi stared at Xiang Rong with a half-smile for a moment before bypassing him and strolling straight toward the center of the chess hall:
—"No need for you. I'll find my Second Senior Brother,"-Mo Xi replied.

---

Qu Qianxing stood right in the center of the Formation Simulation Hall.

Ever since losing to Ran Deng last time, Qu Qianxing had attained some profound realization. He had taken up playing chess here day and night, to the point that over this period, he had practically turned into the venue's [permanent NPC](# "Literal CN: 固定NPC — non-player character stationed permanently in one spot"). No matter the hour, whoever stepped into the Formation Hall could challenge Qu Qianxing to a match.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples had found this novel and thrilling for quite a while.

Ran Deng's earlier performance had given them an illusion:

That they, too, could mercilessly stomp the Second Senior Brother of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. An opportunity like that was not something one came across every day!

Yet after countless disciples threw down the gauntlet, they discovered they had grossly overestimated themselves.

Qu Qianxing's initial defeat had stemmed solely from his unfamiliarity with the rules. Once this Second Senior Brother understood the board's mechanics, even Ran Deng had to expend considerable effort whenever she played against him. As for Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan, after losing three matches in a row, he had never set foot in the Formation Simulation Hall again.

What was most terrifying was that Second Senior Brother was not only brilliant, but also exceptionally diligent.

He didn't just cast it aside once he mastered the rules. Instead, he swapped through various formations and assorted chess pieces to spar and test combinations. In this regard, Qu Qianxing was the disciple among Zhou Hui's pupils who resembled Zhou Hui the most.

Several days later, by today, no disciple dared seek out Qu Qianxing for a match anymore. Only Ran Deng would drop by occasionally to provide a few newly generated secret realm formations for Qu Qianxing to study and decipher.

At present, Qu Qianxing stood before the formation board, closely examining a newly formed secret realm map.

—"Second Senior Brother? Second Senior Brother!"-Mo Xi called out.

He was so engrossed that Mo Xi had to call out several times before Qu Qianxing finally snapped back to reality:
—"What? Want a match... Mo Xi?"-Qu Qianxing blurted in surprise.

Just like Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi, Qu Qianxing froze for a second upon seeing Mo Xi.

Anyone who saw their junior brother—unseen for several days—suddenly undergo ~~[secondary gender differentiation](# "Literal CN: 分化了性别（X） — modern web fiction / ABO genre trope where adolescents differentiate into secondary genders, jokingly struck out with （X） by the author")~~ would be taken aback.

Mo Xi didn't mind Qu Qianxing's gaze in the slightest. He stood there openly for inspection, even showing off the dragon mark across his face:
—"Master gave it to me. Pretty, isn't it?"-Mo Xi flaunted.

—"Very pretty,"-Qu Qianxing nodded dismissively before asking with concern,-"Your body should be fully recovered by now?"

This time, it was Mo Xi's turn to freeze.

He refused to believe Qu Qianxing didn't know what the dragon mark on his face signified. Especially since Second Senior Brother assisted Master with the sect's internal accounts, he should know the situation inside the spirit mine better than anyone else.

—"...Could you at least act like you care a little?"-Mo Xi said awkwardly.-"Doesn't this make it look like I'm the only petty one swayed by worldly possessions?"

In other sects, something like inheritance rights was enough to turn senior and junior brothers against one another, wasn't it? Let alone an entire spirit vein so extraordinarily rich!

A question mark practically popped over Qu Qianxing's head. After mulling it over carefully, he finally caught on to what Mo Xi was referring to:
—"You mean the spirit mine? I certainly was opposed to it!"-Qu Qianxing answered.-"You are still young, your temperament is erratic, and you are greedy for money on ordinary days. If it were up to me, it would be best not to give you even your regular pocket money, just to temper your character! But since Master feels you need it, he naturally has his reasons."

At this point, Qu Qianxing turned stern:
—"However, even though it was given to you, it was done so you could cultivate better. You must not fail Master's expectations! Strive diligently!"-Qu Qianxing admonished.

[Are you my father?]

Mo Xi complained inwardly, his face stiffening as he said:
—"Second Senior Brother, let's just play chess!"-Mo Xi urged.

---

—"I see. So this is how my own chess piece is generated?"-Mo Xi asked curiously, looking at the little figurine on the 'chessboard.'

This step was indeed quite fun, far more interesting than boring black-and-white stones.

Qu Qianxing was also noticeably more conscientious than Ran Deng. He harbored zero intention of taking advantage of Mo Xi's ignorance of the rules, explaining everything step-by-step:
—"Correct. You can also choose four additional chess pieces,"-Qu Qianxing explained.-"However, whether direct disciples or outer sect disciples, everyone's cultivation is fixed at the Foundation Establishment realm. That said, there are medicines on the map that boost cultivation, so don't just focus on reaching the objective, you must also—"

—"Second Senior Brother, let's just start directly!"-Mo Xi laughed.

He belonged to the school of players who had zero patience for sitting through rule explanations.

Whatever game it was, jumping in and playing a few rounds was faster than any amount of theoretical research.

Qu Qianxing frowned, disliking this kind of learning attitude:
—"Once you suffer a loss, you'll learn,"-Qu Qianxing grumbled.

—"Then, shall we begin?"-Mo Xi chirped cheerily.

Since Mo Xi was a beginner, Qu Qianxing selected the most fundamental circular formation map—the exact map where he had suffered a crushing defeat at Ran Deng's hands. Qu Qianxing was prepared to give Mo Xi a taste of that exact same medicine.

To that end, Qu Qianxing deployed only Mo Bei's piece, as she was the fastest among all available pieces. As long as she grabbed all the buffs, she could seize victory.

Unexpectedly, Mo Xi complained:
—"Second Senior Brother, swap to a different piece, won't you? Winning like this won't be any fun at all!"-Mo Xi whined.

—"Huh?"-Qu Qianxing knitted his brows.

Only then did he see that Mo Xi hadn't paid the slightest attention to any clearance objectives; he charged straight for Qu Qianxing's piece. In a single clash, Mo Bei's piece was reduced to flying ash!

—"That's not how this game is played,"-Qu Qianxing complained.

Fortunately, he wasn't as overconfident as Ran Deng, who had relied purely on a Sword Immortal rush strategy without touching other pieces. Qu Qianxing immediately ordered his own piece and Guan Ye's piece to flank and corner Mo Xi's piece.

Mo Xi, however, looked at Qu Qianxing's piece and asked with curiosity:
—"Second Senior Brother, your skills don't seem to be the moves you're most skilled at!"-Mo Xi inquired.

—"Ah, that's true,"-Qu Qianxing recalled.-"At the time, Master didn't specify what we should do. He only requested that we use our handiest ordinary techniques."

—"Hehehe, then I won't hold back!"-Mo Xi grinned.

Hearing this, Mo Xi's eyes lit up. He maneuvered his piece forward to clash head-on once again, instantly one-shotting the two pieces representing Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye with a single strike!

[—"So fierce!"](# "Speaker: Onlooker")

[—"Brute force won't work though, look, 'Eldest Senior Brother' has arrived!"](# "Speaker: Onlooker")

[—"Ah, he's definitely lost!"](# "Speaker: Onlooker")

Discussions broke out among the bystanders. Mo Xi glanced up and noticed that, without him realizing it, a crowd had gathered to watch his match against Second Senior Brother.

Moreover, these onlookers clearly didn't favor Mo Xi. After all, during this period, while everyone might not have had their own pieces yet, they had all played with the direct disciples' pieces. Even with their cultivation standardized, Yuwen Xuan was worthy of being Eldest Senior Brother—his piece was undisputed as the strongest. Even with just [normal basic attacks](# "Literal CN: 平A (píng A) — gaming slang for regular auto-attacks without spending mana or cooldowns"), no other piece could withstand a frontal clash against him.

—"Yeah, under normal circumstances I certainly can't beat Eldest Senior Brother!"-Mo Xi admitted.-"However, this piece of mine is using the brand-new [ultimate skill](# "Literal CN: 必杀技 (bìshājì) — signature killer skill") I just acquired!"

As Mo Xi spoke, on the board, 'Mo Xi' and 'Yuwen Xuan' met head-on.

With a single [basic attack](# "Literal CN: 平A (píng A) — normal auto-attack") from 'Yuwen Xuan', 'Mo Xi' indeed lost more than half his HP bar, but a bizarre smirk appeared across 'Mo Xi's' face.

In the very next second, a golden-crimson little dragon soared from his body and lunged fiercely toward 'Yuwen Xuan'! Golden-crimson flames instantly engulfed the entire zone—let alone 'Yuwen Xuan', even the surrounding terrain was incinerated to flying ash!

[—"So cool!"](# "Speaker: Onlooker")

[—"Junior Brother Mo, what move was that?"](# "Speaker: Onlooker")

[—"Can we use Junior Brother Mo's piece?"](# "Speaker: Onlooker")

The surrounding disciples showered praise one after another. Mo Xi—the real-life one, of course—beamed at the deadpan Qu Qianxing.

[Sorry about that, Second Senior Brother.]

[EXP +1.]

---

Knowing when to quit while he was ahead, Mo Xi didn't continue provoking Qu Qianxing. Gathering his chess piece, he exited the chess hall, only to see Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi bustling over, beaming with delight:
—"Junior Brother Mo! We know how to make money!"-Xiang Rong called out.

—"How?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"Just like you did—by crafting stronger chess pieces!"-Xiang Rong smiled.

—"Exactly! As expected of Junior Brother Mo, grasping the crux of the issue in one go,"-Xiang Yi chimed in with flattery.-"In this sort of match, [raw stat power reigns supreme](# "Literal CN: 强度是王道 (qiángdù shì wángdào) — gaming meta slang: stats and tier power reign supreme over aesthetics/lore") after all!"

This was a blind spot in everyone's thinking.

Although everyone had initially noticed that piece strength was crucial, Zhou Hui had led them astray from the start, leaving everyone scrambling to craft pieces representing themselves.

Afterward, when Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples played matches, aside from using their own personal pieces, they mostly used the five pieces of the direct disciples initially provided by Zhou Hui. After all, those were universally acknowledged as the five strongest pieces, so few thought to compare the stat power of pieces crafted by other disciples.

For one, the direct disciples were universally recognized as the sect's strongest; for another, those five pieces were freely selectable on the board, whereas using someone else's piece required asking for their permission first, which felt slightly awkward, so they were generally only shared among close friends and family.

Mo Xi's actions had given Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi a massive wake-up call.

—"No matter how formidable Eldest Senior Brother is, his skill is just a [normal basic attack](# "Literal CN: 平A (píng A) — auto-attack without cooldown")! We can easily find several inner sect disciples with higher cultivation, ask them to unleash their proudest signature moves, and produce a set of far deadlier pieces!"-Xiang Rong said excitedly.

He felt as though a massive mountain of spirit stones was already waving at him.

Mo Xi had been watching him with a beam, but at this moment, his smile vanished abruptly:
—"No,"-Mo Xi cut in.

—"Why?"-Xiang Rong asked nervously.-"Is there something flawed in my thinking?"

—"It's not a matter of flawed thinking,"-Mo Xi said coldly.-"It will break game balance! Master designed this chess board to train formation comprehension. If we do this, it will devolve into a pure contest of combat power."

[Didn't you literally just use raw stat power to crush Second Senior Brother?!] Xiang Yi complained inwardly.

Although she knew this happened because Qu Qianxing hadn't considered the issue of stat creep either—having guided Mo Xi just like any other disciple to input his strongest move—Mo Xi's outright refusal still struck Xiang Yi as exceedingly bizarre.

Because this was Junior Brother Mo!

The Junior Brother Mo who cared only about spirit stones and couldn't care less about rules! In the past, he would never have spared a single thought for such concerns!

[Is he really Junior Brother Mo?]

Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi once again stared at Mo Xi in bewilderment.

Mo Xi, however, stated:
—"Come on, let's go see Master! Stop always thinking about doing things under the table. If we're going to stir up something big, we might as well do it out in the open!"-Mo Xi declared.

---

—"A limited-time event?"-Zhou Hui asked, looking at his youngest disciple with amused interest.

—"Yes, this is something we just discovered,"-Mo Xi reported with solemn formality, giving a brief summary of Xiang Rong's realization while conveniently concealing the part about their scheme to monetize it.-"However, this hidden issue will sooner or later be noticed by the other disciples! Once the novelty of having their own pieces wears off, everyone is bound to focus on the question of stat strength—"

—"Indeed,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

It wasn't that he was oblivious to this 'hidden issue.' But Zhou Hui, after all, was not a game developer. At most, he had simply constructed a game framework based on his memories of the other world; he hadn't given deep thought to internal numerical balancing or live-service event planning. Mainly, there had been no need to. From his standpoint as the Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he only needed a game to stoke his disciples' enthusiasm for studying, and along the way train his second disciple's adaptability to secret realms. He had never intended to run it as a long-term commercial enterprise.

Yet now that Mo Xi had proactively sought him out, Zhou Hui smiled warmly:
—"It seems you have a countermeasure in mind? Go on,"-Zhou Hui encouraged him.

Mo Xi spoke with supreme confidence:
—"Yes, Master,"-Mo Xi answered.

—"Since it's an inevitable issue, I believe we might as well hold regular scheduled events to evaluate and compare the strength of the pieces generated by all disciples,"-Mo Xi proposed.-"We can grant certain rewards to the top rankers, making their pieces public so that anyone can select them."

—"This way, for one, we can regulate the power scaling of the pieces; for another, we can boost everyone's enthusiasm! No matter how fun a game is, once the initial craze passes, the hype is bound to die down. I've heard that everyone's activity levels aren't what they were at the start! Having scheduled events will not only maintain their excitement, but also incentivize everyone to elevate the strength of their own pieces—which in turn forces them to sharpen their own real-world skills!"-Mo Xi explained.

To put it simply: [Daily Active Users](# "Literal CN: 日活 (rìhuó) — modern mobile gaming metric for Daily Active Users (DAU) and player retention").

Zhou Hui nodded slightly. While he hadn't played games often in his past life, he was at least familiar with this concept.

Seeing Zhou Hui's encouraging reaction, Mo Xi pressed on eagerly:
—"Of course, we don't have to limit ourselves to player-crafted pieces either. We can also commission custom pieces to regulate the meta! For instance, we could have Second Senior Brother demonstrate several commonly used spells, or have Fourth Senior Brother showcase a few practical magical treasures. That way, they can serve as model archetypes for everyone."

—"Furthermore, pouring in resources without any returns isn't sustainable either. If I'm not mistaken, running these formation boards actually consumes spirit stones, right? Ah, I'm not saying we should make a profit, but without breaking game balance, recouping a little bit of operational costs should be perfectly fine, right?"-Mo Xi suggested.

—"For example, we could make certain skill animations look flashier, or furnish certain pieces with [special cosmetic outfits](# "Literal CN: 特别的服装 — character skins in live-service games")... Master?"-Mo Xi trailed off.

Mo Xi had been rattling on in a torrential downpour of ideas, only to realize halfway through that his master was staring at him with a profoundly wondrous expression. This prompted him to reflect with unease: had he spoken too much?

Zhou Hui had no intention of scolding Mo Xi. He merely appraised this disciple of his in cat's clothing, breaking into a fond smile:
—"I never imagined that I actually had a [scheming dog of a game designer](# "Literal CN: 狗策划 (gǒu cèhuà) — affectionate yet scathing Chinese gaming community meme for predatory/clever game designers who orchestrate events, power creep, and skins to squeeze engagement and money out of players") right under my roof,"-Zhou Hui chuckled.

—"Woof?"-Mo Xi tilted his head in utter bewilderment, blinking his round, kitten-like eyes.
