# Chapter 5: Behind the Scenes (第5章 黑幕)

Rumors, by their very nature, always grow more outrageous the further they spread.

What's more, as these fabricated tales circulate, they spontaneously evolve an internal logic and narrative completeness of their own, making debunking them infuriatingly difficult—to the point where even eyewitnesses might end up being convinced.

Take Xiang Rong, the illicit exam-paper scalper, for instance.

Xiang Rong rushed frantically into the Inner Sect's Heavenly Clothing Workshop. The moment he laid eyes on his twin younger sister, Xiang Yi, who was an inner disciple there, he blurted out in loud reproach:

—"Why didn't you tell me that the exam papers concealed heavenly secrets?!"-Xiang Rong demanded loudly.

Xiang Yi swayed slightly at his words. Turning around in flustered annoyance, she glared at her twin brother:

—"What heavenly secrets?!"-Xiang Yi snapped.

—"Are you still trying to keep me in the dark at a time like this? I've heard all about it!"-Xiang Rong said.-"The Immortal Venerable hid heavenly secrets inside your inner sect exam papers to search for fated individuals, which is how Gan Tang struck gold and established her foundation!"

As Xiang Rong reached this point, he was practically clutching his chest in bitter agony.

He wasn't jealous of Gan Tang; he simply felt he had sold so many test papers at bargain-basement prices that he had taken a catastrophic loss.

—"Do you have any idea how many more Spirit Stones we could have made?!"-Xiang Rong lamented, his heart bleeding.

Xiang Yi knew nothing of any heavenly secrets. Listening to her brother felt [like listening to gibberish](# "Literal CN: like listening to a heavenly book (听天书) — completely incomprehensible"), yet given the present circumstances, she didn't dare probe further.

She only wanted to shut her brother's mouth:

—"Shut up! I never told you to go sell them in the first place! I only asked you to find a few people to help copy them down!"-she hissed. Yet as she spoke, she didn't look at Xiang Rong at all, casting anxious glances toward the head seat of the embroidery hall instead.

—"It was precisely because you put it that way that I..."-Xiang Rong began.

Xiang Rong originally wanted to argue further, but noticing his sister's stiff, uneasy posture, he had a sudden realization. He promptly followed Xiang Yi's gaze forward—only to discover that there was actually another person sitting in his sister's room.

It was a boy of fourteen or fifteen, exceptionally adorable, especially with his round heterochromatic eyes—one an ordinary brown, the other a glazed, pale gold like colored glass. They appeared crystal-clear and innocent, much like those of a kitten.

Right now, the youth was watching the twin siblings before him with those limpid, mismatched eyes in curious amusement, like a kitten fixated on a feather wand.

Yet the moment Xiang Rong met that innocent, guileless gaze, his knees went weak, and he dropped straight to the floor.

—"M-Mo... Junior Brother Mo,"-Xiang Rong stammered guiltily.-"I... I was only joking just now."

Xiang Yi chimed in hurriedly:

—"Indeed, my brother is a complete idiot,"-Xiang Yi agreed.

—"Quite understandable,"-the boy said with a beam.-"Twins, after all. Sharing a single womb, there's bound to be one whose nutrients fell short, resulting in a slightly stupider brain."

The Xiang siblings hung their heads, not daring to utter a peep.

The youth, however, had no intention of dropping the subject. He pressed Xiang Rong:

—"What was that about heavenly secrets you mentioned just now?"-the youth pressed.

Cold sweat broke out across Xiang Rong's forehead.

—"I-It was all utter nonsense..."-Xiang Rong mumbled.

—"I want to hear it,"-the boy said in a petulant, spoiled tone.

The cold sweat poured down Xiang Rong's brow even faster, yet he didn't dare withhold the truth:

—"Word has it that... although the Immortal Venerable failed his tribulation that day, he comprehended heavenly secrets, which was why he gathered the disciples for an examination to seek out the fated one. Yet the inner sect... cough, instead, it was an outer sect disciple who comprehended the heavenly secret and successfully established her foundation,"-Xiang Rong confessed honestly.

—"Ooooh~~"-the youth sighed in wonder.

—"O-Of course, it's all baseless rumor!"-Xiang Rong added, his missing intellect abruptly flooding back into his head as he stared at the boy. He explained in a frantic fluster,-"So many people worked through that exam paper, and none of them showed signs of enlightenment. Besides, I know that outer disciple who broke through—she was already on the verge of Foundation Establishment anyway, it was merely a stroke of perfect timing! Even if there truly were heavenly secrets, the Lord Immortal Venerable would surely think of his direct disciples first."

—"Hmph,"-the youth gave a soft snort, though the smile on his face widened slightly.

Seeing this, Xiang Rong secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

Immediately after, the youth asked:

—"Still, judging by your tone earlier, those exam papers have been selling quite well?"-the youth inquired.

Xiang Rong broke into a cold sweat once again.

—"F-Fairly well..."-Xiang Rong whispered.

'Fairly well' was an understatement—they were selling like absolute madness.

Ever since word got out that Gan Tang had achieved Foundation Establishment using the "Immortal Venerable's Secret Papers," outer sect disciples had exhausted every conceivable channel trying to get their hands on a copy. It wasn't even limited to outer disciples; even the sect's handymen and servants were desperate to procure a sheet, treating the exam paper as if it were a Foundation Establishment Pill that could grant a breakthrough the moment one completed it.

Xiang Rong had made a staggering fortune from this. His rushing over to Xiang Yi's quarters to make a fuss was nothing more than an attempt to scale up operations:

—Who could have predicted that the Immortal Venerable had drafted so many different exam papers? If he could get his hands on every single inner sect test paper and publish a complete compiled set, the profits would be astronomical.

Xiang Rong had originally planned it all out, never imagining that this "little ancestor" would be sitting right here.

[Then again, with such a massive commotion brewing in the outer sect, the inner sect was bound to have caught wind of it,] Xiang Rong reasoned to himself.

Xiang Yi had clearly realized the same thing. Secretly pinching Xiang Rong's arm, she said to "Junior Brother Mo":

—"However, we won't be selling any more test papers! We never intended to sell them in the first place, right?"-she said, glaring at Xiang Rong.

Xiang Rong was utterly unwilling in his heart, yet at this moment, he dared not show the slightest hint of it:

—"Y-Yes, exactly,"-Xiang Rong agreed.

"Junior Brother Mo" watched the [silent eyebrow-and-eye squabbling](# "Literal CN: eyebrow and eye lawsuit (眉眼官司) — communicating or sparring silently through facial expressions and eye contact") between the twin siblings with great amusement before smiling:

—"Why can't you sell them? In fact, I was just about to say—since everyone outside is so eager to do exam papers, why not just give them what they want?"-the youth laughed.

—"But—"-Xiang Yi started.

"Junior Brother Mo's" face darkened:

—"Or do you think plastering my Eldest Senior Brother's exam paper everywhere is a good thing?"-the youth asked chillingly.

—"No, I never!"-Xiang Rong defended himself, soaked in cold sweat.-"I know the boundaries! Apart from the initial papers that leaked out when I had others help with the punitive copying, everything I sold afterward consisted entirely of regular inner sect papers! The ones circulating out there belonging to Eldest Senior Brother and yourself were leaked by those fools hired to copy lines—it has nothing to do with me—!"

—"Idiot!"-Xiang Yi yanked her brother back, addressing "Junior Brother Mo":-"I understand! I'll have my brother and others procure as many test papers from other inner disciples as possible, and sell them on a much broader scale and in far greater numbers!"

...Until they completely drowned out that initial batch of leaked papers, ensuring that even if anyone tried to investigate, the original source could never be traced.

Xiang Rong caught on as well, hastily sweetening the deal:

—"As for the Spirit Stones earned from the sales... you take seventy... eighty... ninety... You take ninety percent, and I take ten! [T_T](# "Original CN uses the weeping emoticon QAQ")"-Xiang Rong squeaked, trying to salvage the situation.

Only then did "Junior Brother Mo" nod in satisfaction.

This time, Xiang Rong genuinely felt like crying.

"Junior Brother Mo," however, still had something he wasn't satisfied with:

—"As for that disciple who broke through to Foundation Establishment—"-the boy mused.

Xiang Rong immediately felt a dreadful premonition.

He stammered:

—"G-Gan Tang is a very stubborn, rigid person! S-She won't spill anything! In fact, despite all the people seeking her out right now, she hasn't handed over a single one of the test papers she bought from me to anyone else!"-Xiang Rong babbled.

Unfortunately, "Junior Brother Mo" paid no heed to his explanation, zeroing in straight on the key detail:

—"So that person who established her foundation is named Gan Tang?"-the boy asked, seizing on the crux of the matter.

A chill ran straight through Xiang Rong's heart.

Xiang Rong's lips twitched. Ignoring Xiang Yi's ferocious pinching on his arm, he tried to say something, but before the words could leave his mouth, the sight behind "Junior Brother Mo" caused his eyes to go wide.

Not just him—Xiang Yi's face was equally drained of color, frozen in horror.

"Junior Brother Mo," entirely oblivious, flashed that adorable smile once more:

—"What's with those expressions? Relax, it's not like I'm going to silence her by murder. On the contrary, regarding that 'business' of ours, I want her help—after all, only exam papers distributed by the ['Chosen One'](# "Literal CN: chosen of heaven (天选者) — someone singled out by destiny") herself can be considered the real deal!"-the boy chirped brightly.

[—"And what if that child is unwilling to help?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

"Junior Brother Mo" smiled sweetly:

—"How could anyone ever refuse me?"-the boy replied sweetly.

The moment the words left his mouth, he sensed something amiss. The Xiang siblings wouldn't have the guts to question him, and that voice seemed to have come from behind him. Furthermore, why did it sound so dreadfully familiar—

"Junior Brother Mo" froze on the spot.

From behind him came a gentle chuckle:

[—"Xi'er certainly has great confidence in himself."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"...M-Master,"-Mo Xi choked out.

Junior Brother Mo—or rather, Mo Xi, the youngest disciple under Zhou Hui's tutelage—turned his head around with agonizing slowness. Sure enough, standing behind him at some unknown point was a silver-haired young man resembling ethereal moonlight, whose presence framed the striking, gorgeous youth beside him like a peony blooming under the moon.

—"Second Senior Brother is here too, I see..."-Mo Xi squeaked dryly.

Seeing these two, any smile evaporated instantly. Much like Xiang Rong facing him earlier, Mo Xi only felt like bursting into tears.

There was no need to even mention their revered Master, who was famously [strictly disciplined with himself](# "Original CN uses 严于利己 (a playful corruption or typo of 严于律己, 'strictly disciplined with oneself')").

Mo Xi didn't dare meet his gaze at all.

As for Second Senior Brother Qu Qianxing, he was the very last person Mo Xi ever wanted catching him in the middle of mischief.

Because out of all his senior brothers and sisters, Qu Qianxing was by far the most nagging.

Case in point: right now, Qu Qianxing was staring at Mo Xi in utter shock.

He and Zhou Hui had witnessed the outer disciple's breakthrough at the Library Pavilion with their own eyes. Upon hearing that the outer disciple had established her foundation by solving inner sect exam papers, they had set out to track down whoever had leaked the test papers in the first place—never expecting that Master would lead him straight to his youngest junior brother.

Indeed: the "prodigy" who had outsourced his punitive copying lines and charged a fee in reverse was none other than Zhou Hui's youngest disciple:

Mo Xi.

Qu Qianxing hadn't grasped what his usually obedient youngest junior brother was up to at first. But once he pieced the entire picture together, his gaze flared with pure fury:

—"Mo Xi! You actually outsourced your own homework to others?!"-Qu Qianxing roared.

Mo Xi's body stiffened.

Yet in a situation like this, even if beaten to death, he would never admit it.

In an instant, Mo Xi's eyes welled with tears:

—"I didn't... Second Senior Brother, you clearly know that I've been staying by your side these past days correcting my exam papers!"-Mo Xi protested in aggrieved innocence.

Qu Qianxing considered this; it was indeed true, which made Mo Xi's conduct all the more baffling to him:

—"Then what on earth are you doing here?!"-Qu Qianxing demanded, his disbelief deepening.

—"...I-I haven't done anything bad,"-Mo Xi stammered hesitantly.-"I only wanted to earn a little pocket money, s-so I agreed to help the senior brothers copy their exam papers."

His words were half-truth, half-fabrication.

Mo Xi had indeed been dutifully correcting his exam papers, but that was merely playing the good boy in front of Qu Qianxing.

As for the punitive copying lines, Mo Xi had zero intention of writing them himself.

Mo Xi primarily felt there was simply no need.

His innate talent was exceptionally high; by age thirteen, he had already achieved a flawless Foundation Establishment, his cultivation nearly catching up to Fourth Senior Brother's. He was clever, sharp-witted, and nimble-minded, which made "pointless repetitive labor" his absolute most detested chore. Thus, he had delegated his homework to outer sect disciples through the Xiang siblings.

Naturally, an "obedient little junior brother" would never do such a thing.

Mo Xi would never confess to what he had done.

Fortunately, when it came to pulling off mischief, Mo Xi was never foolish enough to do it alone. He had dragged plenty of people into the water with him. Knowing his Second Senior Brother, once a crowd was involved, Qu Qianxing would no longer focus all his firepower on him alone.

Sure enough, Qu Qianxing latched onto the crux of Mo Xi's words:

—"'Senior brothers'?!"-Qu Qianxing repeated sharply.

At this point, Mo Xi stopped defending himself.

He simply widened his watery, round cat-like eyes, staring back at Qu Qianxing with a look of stubborn, mistreated innocence.

This kind of [bratty antics](# "Literal CN: bear-child behavior (熊孩子行为) — spoiled, mischievous brat antics") on anyone else would be begging for a beating. Yet even Zhou Hui had to admit that his youngest disciple was truly adorable; such an utterly punchable expression on him came across only as pitiful and endearing.

—Then again, if he and his older sister hadn't possessed such breathtaking appearances, they wouldn't have nearly been sold off as [top-grade furnaces](# "Literal CN: supreme cauldron/furnace (极品鼎炉) — dual-cultivation partner exploited for their spiritual essence") back in the day.

Unfortunately, the straightforward Qu Qianxing was completely vulnerable to this tactic.

Qu Qianxing possessed a peerless beauty of a face himself, but he loathed his own looks to the extreme, disdaining to ever exploit them, and failed to realize that his youngest junior brother was actively weaponizing his adorable looks to play the innocent victim.

[Seeing Mo Xi like this, could there be some other hidden reason behind it?] Qu Qianxing thought, his heart already softening to the point where he could no longer bear to interrogate him.

—"Do you think I won't find out if you keep your mouth shut?"-Qu Qianxing said coldly, his gaze already landing on the Xiang siblings.-"Whose exam papers did you take?"

—"T-There are a few senior brothers'..."-Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi stammered, drenched in cold sweat. Naturally, they didn't dare name Mo Xi, and as for the others, they had to frantically review the names in their minds to pick out a few inconsequential scapegoats.-"..."

At their hesitation, Qu Qianxing unleashed the overwhelming aura of a Golden Core cultivator:

—"Speak! How dare you scalp inner sect exam papers?!"-Qu Qianxing barked.

That single sentence already reeked of blatant favoritism.

He had absolved Mo Xi of responsibility entirely, dumping the entire blame for scalping the test papers onto these two outer disciples.

Then again, Qu Qianxing had always been like this. In his heart, his direct martial siblings naturally ranked above ordinary inner disciples, and inner disciples ranked above outer disciples.

Xiang Rong was completely incapable of answering; he was terrified of Mo Xi, let alone the crushing spiritual pressure of a Golden Core cultivator.

Fortunately, at that moment, a gentle force buoyed him up, effortlessly brushing away that terrifying pressure.

—"Enough, Qianxing,"-a mild voice sighed.-"It is no grand catastrophe."

—"Yet Master..."-Qu Qianxing frowned deeply.

Mo Xi's eyes remained rimmed in red, but his ears perked right up.

He was confident in handling his biased Second Senior Brother, but had zero confidence when it came to his esteemed Master.

Theoretically, cultivators at the Tribulation Transcendence stage were omniscient. Yet based on Mo Xi's experience, as long as high-ranking cultivators remained human, they had weaknesses and were not truly all-powerful.

Mo Xi was actually itching, with a hint of [courting disaster](# "Literal CN: courting death / seeking death (作死) — recklessly pushing boundaries and asking for trouble"), to probe where his Master's bottom line lay.

—According to all past indications, Zhou Hui was far easier to talk to than Qu Qianxing; most of his strictness was directed toward himself.

Zhou Hui appeared inclined to handle the matter lightly:

—"I never forbade sharing inner sect exam papers with the outer sect in the first place,"-Zhou Hui said calmly.

—"Yet they are scalping them!"-Qu Qianxing gritted out, seething inwardly.-[And scalping Master's personal exam papers at that!]

Zhou Hui, however, cast a curious glance toward the Xiang siblings:

—"Have they been selling well?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Mo Xi had asked the exact same question earlier, yet Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi hardly believed the Lord Immortal Venerable meant it in the same way Mo Xi had. Still, they couldn't ignore an inquiry from a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster.

—"E-Extremely well,"-Xiang Rong braced himself and answered.-"Nearly everyone hopes to obtain a copy."

—"A commendable thirst for learning,"-Zhou Hui said, smiling faintly.

—"Master!"-Qu Qianxing protested.-"Such behavior is hardly worthy of praise!"

Mo Xi breathed a secret sigh of relief, assuming Master wouldn't hold this against them.

Yet Zhou Hui's voice resonated with profound benevolence:

—"Since everyone possesses such a wonderful thirst for learning, let us hold a sect-wide Grand Unified Examination,"-Zhou Hui announced with tender benevolence.
