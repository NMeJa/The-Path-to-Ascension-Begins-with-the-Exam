# Chapter 183: The Demon Realm

—"Achoo!"-Qu Qianxing sneezed.

Cultivators cultivated both body and mind, possessing physical constitutions far exceeding ordinary mortals. From the middle stage of Foundation Establishment onward, they virtually never contracted (ordinary) illnesses, let alone Nascent Soul cultivators—only exotic toxins could induce disease. Naturally, sneezes heralding an ailment were exceedingly rare.

Thus, after sneezing, Qu Qianxing very cautiously swept his spiritual energy through his body for an examination.

Even though he found nothing amiss, he still switched into high alert. Sweeping golden lightning through his meridians, he looked up at the sky in perplexity:

—"Could the temperature fluctuation be too extreme?"

—"Haha, perhaps someone is missing you,"-Young Master Jian Nuo of Sky City chuckled.

Qu Qianxing cast him a bizarre look upon hearing this:

—"Someone is missing me? Young Master Jian, to think you actually believe such a childish superstition. How precious."

[Lord Qianxing, that gorgeous mouth of yours really shouldn't utter such scathing words—it'll get guys with twisted kinks thoroughly addicted!]

[...If I actually said that out loud, I'd probably get beaten to death, wouldn't I?]

Jian Nuo thought to himself.

Still, it wasn't as though he couldn't understand Qu Qianxing's irritation toward him.

If he himself were investigating something of utmost importance and some shameless pest clung to him like a leech, he too would be thoroughly displeased and impatient. But after all, this place led directly to his (unilaterally decided) fiancée's ancestral home; unless he ventured in to conduct an in-depth investigation in person, there was simply no way he could rest easy!

Pondering this, Jian Nuo also raised his head and looked up toward the sky.

At this moment, they were situated in a blackish-crimson realm. Whether the soil or the air, everything resembled congealed blood clots that looked toxic at a mere glance. Yet the most terrifying thing of all was the sky—the sole source of faint illumination.

It was a sky of pale, sickly greenish-white, like a peeled hard-boiled egg. Compared to the black-red air and earth, it was blindingly stark.

Yet across such a sky, there was neither sun nor moon, nor regular celestial bodies like stars. Instead, there was an enormous, ghastly pale face, like a waterlogged corpse bloated from drowning, staring down at the earth with unfocused eyes.

Even though cultivators underwent all manner of trials and were well-accustomed to fiends, demons, monsters, and roaming phantoms, rarely did they ever encounter a scene so colossal and horrifying. The first time Jian Nuo laid eyes on it, he had nearly vomited on the spot.

Yet he could not do so.

Not only could he not display disgust, but he could not even allow that thing to "perceive" that he could see it. Otherwise, he would be assimilated into becoming a part of this Demon Realm.

Indeed—the Demon Realm.

Qu Qianxing, Jian Nuo, and their party were currently within the Demon Realm.

They had entered the Demon Realm nine days ago.

To be more precise, it was on the very day that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grandmaster—Qu Qianxing's Master, the Untiring Immortal Venerable—temporarily left the sect to travel abroad.

Supposedly, an abnormally powerful demonic qi had been detected that day at the junction between the Qingyang clan's secret realm and the Demon Realm. Left unattended, who knew what manner of demonic abominations might crawl out? Yet with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grandmaster happening to be away, there was "no other choice"—as the Heavenly Spirit Sect's most reliable disciple, Qu Qianxing could only venture deep into the Demon Realm to investigate... or so the story went.

Yet after traveling for nine whole days, Jian Nuo had not encountered any "abnormally powerful demonic qi," let alone laid eyes on whatever source had produced it.

On the other hand, Qu Qianxing's logistical preparations were far too abundant; by no stretch of the imagination did it look like something hastily put together on short notice.

[It seems this Second Senior Brother of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, who appears so impeccably upright and rule-abiding on the surface, isn't quite so obedient after all...]

Naturally, these words could never be spoken aloud.

Speaking them aloud wouldn't merely be a matter of being beaten to death; rather, all traces of his corpse would immediately be obliterated without a single flaw. The perfection of the crime would not only thoroughly hoodwink Sky City, but Sky City might even have to formally apologize afterward for Young Master Jian's "reckless misconduct."

At this thought, Jian Nuo couldn't help tightening his collar:

—"...It is a bit chilly. Perhaps the demonic qi has seeped into my body a little,"-he muttered.

At this moment, a feeble voice piped up:

—"Second Senior Brother, in that case, why don't we just head back?"-Lu Shulong asked weakly.

He was the third "person" in this expedition squad.

In fact, apart from Jian Nuo, who had stubbornly tagged along like a shameless pest, Qu Qianxing had brought only his Fifth Junior Brother, Lu Shulong.

After all, in a place like the Demon Realm, low-level cultivators entering were [merely serving themselves up as food](# "Literal CN: 送菜 (sòngcài) — delivering dishes / serving oneself on a platter; gaming slang for feeding the enemy / useless cannon fodder"). Ordinary Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples were nowhere near enough to fill the gap; even Qu Qianxing's own Nascent Soul cultivation was somewhat insignificant here. In contrast, Lu Shulong's True Dragon bloodline suppressed all creation, amply illustrating that no matter how hard one cultivated, it was never as good as being born right.

Yet this True Dragon appeared to be the most delicate and frail of them all.

Having traveled through the Demon Realm for nine days, Lu Shulong's little face was deathly pale. His lustrous silver hair hung limp and lackluster, and his already slender frame looked so frail he could barely bear the weight of his robes—looking for all the world like [a little daughter-in-law tormented by a vicious mother-in-law](# "Literal CN: 恶毒婆婆蹂躏的小媳妇 — a classic Chinese melodrama trope of an abusive mother-in-law bullying a helpless daughter-in-law"), ready to faint by the roadside at any second.

Right now, he was looking imploringly at his Second Senior (Vicious Mother-in-Law) Brother:

—"We've already traveled for so many days, and we've gone deep enough,"-Lu Shulong pleaded.—"Thinking about it, Master should be heading back soon. Shouldn't we hurry and leave this place?"

[At least if you want to stir up trouble, go do it yourself! Take responsibility for your own actions like Eldest Senior Brother does—don't drag me into the water with you!]

Regrettably, although Qu Qianxing was fiercely protective of his own and tender toward the weak—harboring zero resistance against Little Junior Brother Mo Xi's [feigned innocence and green-tea craftiness](# "Literal CN: 装乖茶艺 (zhuāngguāi cháyì) — pretending to be obedient and well-behaved while practicing manipulative 'green tea' theatrics")—he could by no means categorize a True Dragon under the heading of "weak and needing protection." After all, back then he had seen with his own eyes Eldest Senior Brother secretly locking this junior brother inside an alchemy furnace to refine him for several days, only to have a tooth chipped by the dragon's tail when pulling him out.

Consequently, Lu Shulong was also the sole existence among all the martial brothers and sisters who dared to linger stubbornly in Master's embrace for hours right in front of Yuwen Xuan, and even hide inside Master's sleeve to sleep.

Still, doing such things in childhood was one thing; didn't he feel any shame acting like that now that he was all grown up?

It was all Eldest Senior Brother setting a terrible example, causing these junior brothers and sisters to spend every day thinking of nothing but acting spoiled with Master. Barely any of them gave a thought to broader matters, let alone proactively sharing the sect's burdens or relieving Master's worries.

If things continued like this, what was to become of them?!

Looking at Lu Shulong, Qu Qianxing let out a melancholy sigh.

—"...Even if Master has returned, so what? We had 'legitimate grounds' to come to the Demon Realm in the first place, and we didn't come here to play,"-Qu Qianxing said.—"Besides, with an affair this monumental, I would report it to Master immediately anyway. Don't tell me you wanted to hide it from Master?"

—"Not really..."-Lu Shulong mumbled.

[...But wasn't all of this secretly planned by you from the start?]

Lu Shulong thought. He shared the exact same sentiment as Jian Nuo, yet neither of them dared voice it aloud.

Lu Shulong had no choice but to [adopt a roundabout approach](# "Literal CN: 曲线救国 (qūxiàn jiùguó) — saving the country through a curved path; idiom for taking an indirect route to achieve one's goal"):

—"It's just that if we're gone for too long, Master will worry... Second Senior Brother wouldn't want to make Master fret, right? And there's also Eldest Senior Brother..."

—"Master will indeed worry, but is that an excuse to leave things unattended and merely wait for the demon race to come knocking at our door?"-Qu Qianxing countered, his tone growing severe.—"Don't tell me you think that just by doing your homework and scoring full marks on exams, you've fulfilled Master's expectations? Are you a newborn hatchling waiting for Master to spoon-feed you?!"

[By dragon lifespan, I am a hatchling!]

Lu Shulong wanted to say just that, but having clear memories of seven reincarnation cycles, he simply couldn't bring himself to utter such words with a clean conscience.

Most importantly, despite knowing full well that Qu Qianxing possessed no memories of those other cycles, being scolded like that by his Second Senior Brother still caused Lu Shulong's heart to twinge with a pang of guilt.

Fortunately, Qu Qianxing did not continue dressing down his junior brother—showing some restraint for the sake of Jian Nuo, an "outsider."

—"However, your concern is also justified. With Master absent, relying solely on Eldest Senior Brother is indeed rather precarious,"-Qu Qianxing conceded.

[Or rather, Eldest Senior Brother is precisely the single greatest destabilizing factor.]

Qu Qianxing glanced at Jian Nuo and swallowed the second half of that sentence—not to save face for Eldest Senior Brother, but because Yuwen Xuan's true personality was a top-secret scandal; for the sake of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's reputation, even low-ranking disciples could not be informed.

He merely said:

—"I originally intended to collect more intelligence, but I didn't expect the Demon Realm's interior to be so 'vast yet sparsely populated.' We will simply head to the source of that 'abnormal demonic qi' and call it a day."

—"That demonic qi was real?!"-Lu Shulong and Jian Nuo exclaimed in unison.

—"Hah, what do you mean by that?"-Qu Qianxing asked, casting them a strange look.—"Did you two actually think it was fake?"

—"Of course not! Naturally I believe in Lord Qianxing! It's just... we've traveled for so long without spotting that abnormal demonic qi, so it feels a bit odd. Could it be hiding from us?"-Jian Nuo hurriedly smoothed things over.

—"No, it does not move. To be precise, it should be a massive demon colony. That is why it is exceptionally dangerous, and why I had to come see it in person."

—"A massive demon colony?! Really?!"-Jian Nuo blanched in shock.

—"I do not lie about such matters,"-Qu Qianxing said displeasedly.

Hearing this, Jian Nuo lowered his head in shame, feeling he had [measured the heart of a gentleman with the belly of a petty man](# "Literal CN: 以小人之心度君子之腹 — measuring a noble man's generous heart with the narrow mind of a petty rogue").

Only Lu Shulong looked at his Second Senior Brother in speechless exasperation:

[—Yeah, you wouldn't lie, but to rationalize your own actions, you'd deliberately conceal a chunk of key information!]

[Only Young Master Jian doesn't know: although we have 'only' been in the Demon Realm for nine days, we've been traveling via that flight artifact Master gifted you when praising your cultivation progress! Its speed is enough to escape the clutches of a Mahayana powerhouse! Excluding the time spent gathering data, the distance we've flown is enough to take a Soul Formation cultivator three full months of flying at maximum effort!]

[Just how urgent could an 'abnormally powerful demonic qi' located that far away possibly be that you had to rush all the way here to see it?!]

[Even if there were a Demon Sovereign over there, it would be impossible for them to sense our existence!]

[Ah, but given Second Senior Brother's modus operandi, if he dares venture to such a place in person, there certainly won't be any entities above his own realm. Doesn't that make the danger level even lower?!]

Lu Shulong's mind was brimming with endless internal roasts.

He deeply lamented that Fourth Senior Brother Guan Ye wasn't here, leaving him without a single soul to complain to, so frustrated that even his dragon scales had grown dull and lackluster.

Yet Qu Qianxing was hardly the type of person to care about his junior siblings' mental health.

Directing Jian Nuo and Lu Shulong, he collected samples of the local demonic qi and other empirical data, then prepared to rush toward the root of the demonic qi at top speed, bringing out his flight treasure for this purpose.

It was a Lightning-Wheeled Canopy Carriage.

It possessed a frame forged from moonlight, a canopy woven of stellar radiance, and wheels coalesced from wind and thunder. When traveling across the sky, it was like lightning flashing through clouds; by the time someone on the ground snapped out of their daze, the carriage was already tens of millions of miles away.

Yet contrasted with its extreme velocity, passengers riding inside would not feel that insane speed at all. Because the passage of time inside the carriage had been adjusted by Zhou Hui, those aboard felt as though they were moving at a gentle walking pace on the ground, capable of clearly observing and recording all scenery along the way. But precisely for this reason, very few people could ever discern the true value of this treasure.

Even Sky City's well-traveled Young Master Jian had failed to notice anything unusual about the carriage.

Sitting aboard the carriage as he had over the past several days, he looked down upon the earth below and sighed:

—"Come to think of it, the Demon Realm is far more desolate than I imagined!"

"..."

No one took the bait.

Having obtained a rare opportunity to enter the Demon Realm, Qu Qianxing had no interest in idle chatter, frantically cataloging all manner of data. Meanwhile, Lu Shulong was busy brooding and shrinking his presence to avoid becoming Second Senior Brother's study page and free laborer (though he already was one).

Fortunately, Young Master Jian had grown accustomed to this over the course of the journey.

Even with nobody paying him any heed, he could happily rattle on by himself:

—"After all, whenever people mention the Demon Realm, don't they describe skies swarming with ghosts and monsters? Yet after traveling for days, we haven't even run into a handful of bugs..."-though there was indeed an enormous monstrosity floating in the sky, that was currently off-limits for discussion.

—"This is precisely what is normal,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

When it came to topics of interest, Qu Qianxing would still selectively provide answers. Furthermore, Jian Nuo had chosen his topic well, touching directly upon the subject Qu Qianxing was currently investigating. Qu Qianxing didn't mind using the conversation with Jian Nuo to organize his thoughts.

—"The Demon Realm is saturated with demonic qi, making it difficult for ordinary living creatures to survive, including demonic abominations,"-Qu Qianxing explained.—"Although demonic beasts breed within demonic qi and use it as nourishment to advance their cultivation, the majority of demonic creatures require 'food'—namely the vital essence, emotions, flesh, and blood of other living beings. Yet within the Demon Realm, there is simply no adequate supply of matter to sustain them—"

—"So they starved to death?!"-Jian Nuo asked cheerfully.

—"If only that were the case,"-Qu Qianxing said with a frown, the pen in his hand pausing for a brief instant.

He leafed through the notes beside him. Failing to find what he was looking for, he glanced left and right before his gaze finally settled on Jian Nuo's side. Jian Nuo immediately noticed Qu Qianxing's gaze, picking up the draft papers beside him and handing them over.

Thanking him, Qu Qianxing took the sheets, flipped through the draft, copied a few lines onto his own papers, and then continued explaining to Jian Nuo:

—"First, the demon race will, as we already know, exploit every possible 'pathway' to invade the mortal world, dragging mortal cities into the Demon Realm to serve as their blood-food.

—"According to ancient records, surrounding high-ranking demons like Demon Emperors, Demon Sovereigns, and Demon Lords, there are typically full-scale cities or nations. Most of the inhabitants living inside are ordinary mortals. They nourish the demonic abominations with their fear and madness, serving as blood-food in exchange for the right to survive—yet due to the inherently savage nature of the demon race, such cities and kingdoms cannot endure for long. Thus, the demonic armies can never halt their march of invasion, perpetually seizing new territory.

—"However, after the demon armies advance, what remains behind is thoroughly devoured land. The demons stranded on that depleted land begin slaughtering and devouring one another, polluting the area into an absolute wasteland, before entering the third stage—

—"Dormancy."

Qu Qianxing paused here, a chilling smile emerging on his breathtakingly beautiful face.

Jian Nuo couldn't help shivering.

In that split second, he found Qu Qianxing even more terrifying than the colossal corpse floating in the sky.

—"Demons in dormancy dissolve back into demonic qi, but this transformation is merely a temporary state; it does not mean they have perished,"-Qu Qianxing continued with that same bone-chilling smile.—"On the contrary, given sufficient blood-food—the scent of humans, the scent of agony, the scent of slaughter, the scent of death—they can be reawakened, reconstituting once more into demons..."

—"W-Wait a moment!"-Jian Nuo cried out.

He finally grasped the equally hair-raising reality lurking behind Qu Qianxing's smile:

—"Are you saying that all of this is demonic abominations? Everything?!"

Jian Nuo spread his hands, gazing in sheer horror at the blackish-crimson air, the barren earth, and everything around them.

If previously he had found the Demon Realm curiously desolate, bereft of anything except the occasional minor demon, he now understood that the Demon Realm was not so simple. The moment conditions were met, this uncomfortable demonic qi would instantly transform into mouths of all shapes and sizes (demonic beasts), devouring everything in sight...

No, there was no need to wait—the "conditions were met" right now.

Startled by Qu Qianxing's words (and smile), Jian Nuo lost his composure for a split second. Instantly, the demonic qi permeating the entire space transformed into dozens of gaping, blood-red maws, snapping viciously down toward Jian Nuo.

—"Aaaagh!"

Jian Nuo let out a bloodcurdling shriek.

Yet it was not from being bitten, but from being electrocuted.

Before the gaping maws could bite Jian Nuo, a bolt of golden lightning struck down upon his figure. While instantly reducing the maws attempting to devour Jian Nuo into ashes, it also conveniently subjected Jian Nuo to [comprehensive electrotherapy](# "Literal CN: 全面电疗 (quánmiàn diànliáo) — comprehensive electrotherapy; modern psychiatric reference used humorously for being violently shocked by lightning") from body to soul.

Sprawled wretchedly on the floor of the canopy carriage, Jian Nuo had no choice but to tremble and thank the "perpetrator":

—"T-Thank you, Lord Qianxing... But next time, please be a little gentler next time QAQ"

This golden lightning was naturally Qu Qianxing's intrinsic spell.

The might of Qu Qianxing's thunder inherently far surpassed peers of the same realm, possessing the power to enforce cross-realm suppression against monsters, fiends, and ghosts. After his recent breakthrough to Nascent Soul, its power had multiplied several times over. This was precisely why Qu Qianxing dared venture into the Demon Realm with "mere" Nascent Soul cultivation.

Of course, given Qu Qianxing's prudent nature, golden lightning was by no means his sole reliance.

Just as Lu Shulong had perceived, ever since establishing the forward outpost, Qu Qianxing had instructed Fourth Junior Brother Guan Ye to manufacture countless flying mechanical automatons (drones), initiating a comprehensive reconnaissance across the Demon Realm's perimeter.

This journey was essentially meant to verify the gathered intelligence; in truth (in Qu Qianxing's eyes), there was little real danger.

—"There is no need to panic; without massive death, this demonic qi will not reconstitute into demonic beasts so easily,"-Qu Qianxing said to Jian Nuo.—"Moreover, as you have seen, the entrance to the Demon Realm is already under our control. The quantity of awakened demons remains fully manageable."

*So that was it! Was this why the Demon Realm appeared desolate, yet the Qingyang clan's secret realm (tower defense game) never lacked for demonic beasts?* Lu Shulong had previously mentioned to Qingyu the correlations between demonic qi classifications and demon species, using that as a foundation to plan the spawn timing for each wave of monsters—recently even reaching the level of organizing "limited-time in-game events"!

This was truly...

Terrifying!

—"Wouldn't it be better to just seal it shut?"-Jian Nuo asked with a pale face.

He had actually wanted to say as much when he first learned the rift had opened. But Qingyang Qingyu had appeared exceedingly thrilled, and with two supreme titans—the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Clan Head of the Qingyang Clan—personally overseeing the situation, everything had seemed under control. Clearing out the demonic abominations beneath the secret realm before contemplating how to seal it hadn't seemed entirely unreasonable.

Yet in this godforsaken place, even the air itself was demonic beasts! If an accident occurred, could Eternal Villa and the Heavenly Spirit Sect truly hold the line?

Cold sweat beaded on Jian Nuo's forehead:

—"I wonder what the conversion rate of demonic qi to demonic beasts is..."

—"Taking the most basic gray mist as an example, one cubic meter is roughly equivalent to a newly corrupted Foundation Establishment cultivator,"-Lu Shulong chimed in considerately.—"Naturally, you can't calculate it so simply in practice. Depending on the color, density, and scent of the mist, the types of demons produced are completely different. We are currently compiling a related catalog."

[Nobody would ever want a catalog like that!]

—"The issue isn't the catalog, it's the endless monsters!"-Jian Nuo argued agitatedly.—"Demonic qi being able to directly convert into demonic beasts also means that no matter how we defend, the surrounding area will endlessly generate demons. Under these circumstances, the only way is to completely isolate our world from the Demon Realm—"

—"Hasn't that already been done before? And it failed,"-Qu Qianxing said cuttingly.

He hadn't even wanted to bother answering such a foolish question. But after all, his counterpart was the young master of Sky City and an alliance that had to be won over—which was why Qu Qianxing had agreed to let Jian Nuo tag along in the first place. Exercising patience, he explained:

—"After the Fiendgod War ten thousand years ago, the Qingyang clan of Eternal Villa used a secret realm to suppress the final demon eye. Yet in the end, it was proven to be nothing more than a lie—beneath it lay a nest of demon insects! If we hadn't excavated it first and those demon insects had matured, crawled out, and wrought slaughter, do you know what the situation would look like right now?"

Jian Nuo instantly froze.

Indeed: had those demon insects wrought slaughter, the demonic qi here would not be so tranquil. Instead, countless demons would have awakened and surged toward the rift. Just contemplating that scenario made Jian Nuo's scalp tingle.

—"Even if we seal it again now, [burying our heads in the sand](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — plugging one's ears to steal a bell; self-deceit") is utterly pointless,"-Qu Qianxing continued.—"Since a nest of demon insects was planted there, it indicates that location was a targeted attack objective. Leaving aside how effective a temporary seal would even be, even if it held, it would remain paper-thin before a true demonic army. Rather than waiting for catastrophe to strike, understanding the relationship between demonic qi and the demon race and getting our disciples accustomed to battling demonic creatures is the proper course of action."

—"Indeed, that makes sense..."-Jian Nuo said with difficulty.

Yet he found it hard to accept in his heart.

War preparations? Through that game? Theoretically, the entire cultivation world ought to be notified... No, impossible! If even he himself found it hard to accept, it was difficult to imagine how other sects would react—especially when the current center of gravity of the cultivation world was focused on the Northern Domain.

Under these circumstances, could they only place their trust in the Untiring Immortal Venerable?

Jian Nuo pondered. As the young master of Sky City, he possessed a degree of political shrewdness after all; he quickly forced himself to calm down and consider what needed to be done in this scenario.

—"...In that case, investigating the demon gathering grounds is indeed necessary,"-Jian Nuo mused.—"Not only that, but before preparations are complete, we must monitor and control those roving demons, preventing them from leaking news of the fissure (and our presence)..."

—"That is precisely what we are doing,"-Qu Qianxing said.

—"As expected of Lord Qianxing, highly purposeful action,"-Jian Nuo nodded, his thoughts expanding further.—"If I am not mistaken, our safest next step is to seize total control over this entire sector of the Demon Realm, and using that as a springboard, probe into the status of other Demon Realm sectors?"

—"Pretty much."

[How on earth is that 'pretty much'?!]

[Isn't this practically launching a full-scale invasion?!]

Lu Shulong roasted furiously in his heart.

Yet he watched with wide eyes as Jian Nuo, who had been thoroughly reluctant mere minutes ago, quickly became thick as thieves with Second Senior Brother upon realizing the gravity of the situation.

It seemed these two were genuinely well-matched.

In the third cycle, he had thought Second Senior Brother had simply associated with Jian Nuo because he had been "released into the wild" to Sky City and needed a lackey!

Then again, this fellow Jian Nuo truly knew how to [read the room](# "Literal CN: 识时务 (shí shíwù) — recognize the times / be practical and savvy")!

Though he possessed no memories of that cycle, intentionally or unintentionally he avoided clashing with Second Senior Brother. Even regarding Qingyang Qingyu, where they were genuine half-rivals in love (on his side alone), Jian Nuo preferred to steer clear of Second Senior Brother and target Lu Shulong instead. Later on, he heaped all manner of flattery and fawning upon Second Senior Brother. When faced with such a terrifying revelation just now, merely because Second Senior Brother claimed to have a plan, Jian Nuo believed him without hesitation and began strategizing for him. Could it be that this guy simply [had a taste for someone like Second Senior Brother](# "Literal CN: 吃二师兄这口 — into someone's particular type / weak for that specific vibe")?

From this perspective, Jian Nuo was the most suitable [crutch](# "Literal CN: 拐 (guǎi) — crutch; gaming slang for a dedicated support buffer character") for Second Senior Brother. With his assistance, in that cycle Second Senior Brother had gone so far as to seize control of the entire cultivation world, including the Heavenly Dao Alliance, standing toe-to-toe against the Abyssal Ocean Society led by Eldest Senior Brother (even if the one fanning the flames and stirring up trouble between them had been Lu Shulong).

To think that through bizarre twists of fate in this cycle, they had ended up lumped together once more—it truly filled this dragon with anticipation~~

Looking at the two of them, Lu Shulong curled his lips.

Bored out of his mind, he gazed out the carriage window at the land saturated with demonic qi. Suddenly, his senses picked up something.

—"Second Senior Brother, it seems we have arrived,"-Lu Shulong said.

Hearing him say this, Qu Qianxing and Jian Nuo finally halted their conversation (scheming) and looked downward.

Yet the sensory perception of humans was indeed vastly different from that of dragons. It was only half an hour later that Qu Qianxing and Jian Nuo finally laid eyes on their objective—the root of that abnormal cluster of demonic qi.

It was yet another colossal corpse.

Of course, it could not compare to the one floating in the sky, but this corpse on the ground was still startlingly immense—large enough to serve as a city.

No, it was a city.

Visible to the naked eye, numerous fiends and demons inhabited (parasitized) this corpse. In the desolate Demon Realm, there was no better haven or "food" than this.

—"Truly nauseating,"-Qu Qianxing muttered.

Beholding that rotting carcass and the demon race squirming upon it like maggots, it was difficult not to wear such an expression.

—"What should we do? Are we going to infiltrate it?"-Lu Shulong asked with a hint of mischievous schadenfreude.

Although his Second Senior Brother carried the posture of someone about to conquer the Demon Realm, matters had to be taken one step at time. For this current step, they could only honestly enter the demon gathering site and ascertain their circumstances.

Yet unlike Lu Shulong—who was a dragon and excelled at biological dissection and research—while Qu Qianxing's biology marks were undeniably stellar, he had zero fondness for such things and possessed a pronounced germaphobic streak. In particular, having experienced severe physical trauma and festering wounds in his past, Qu Qianxing harbored an intense revulsion toward things like open gashes and maggots—though naturally, no sane person would enjoy such sights anyway. Compared to wretched rotting flesh, Qu Qianxing vastly preferred golden lightning that incinerated everything to ash.

Consequently, Lu Shulong harbored a petty sense of anticipation at the prospect of his Second Senior Brother having to step foot inside a city constructed of a rotting corpse.

Qu Qianxing was oblivious to his junior brother's petty calculations.

Naturally, he would not abandon his meticulously prepared plans over petty personal squeamishness:

—"Of course we must infiltrate it, and you're coming too! I have already prepared your disguise."

Saying so, Qu Qianxing retrieved a tiny censer emitting swirls of violet smoke.

—"It reeks!"-This time, it was Lu Shulong's turn to clamp his nose in revulsion.

Jian Nuo, however, twitched his nose in bewilderment; he didn't smell any stench, only an intoxicating, heady fragrance.

Qu Qianxing paid no mind to whether the violet smoke was foul or fragrant, letting it coil around his figure. In that instant, his hair and eyes darkened to a deep obsidian, his already inhumanly gorgeous features becoming even more unearthly, while blackish-crimson demonic qi coalesced around him, making him look like a genuine high-ranking demon.

—"This too is a fruit of our recent research into the demon race. It allows one to temporarily transform into a demon, even mimicking their power,"-Qu Qianxing said, raising his hand.

His golden lightning turned pitch-black, crackling upon fingers so flawless they elicited sighs of admiration, resembling black serpents capable of swallowing all existence.

[That's a threat, isn't it?!]

[That is definitely a threat!]

[When Master returns, I am absolutely lodging a complaint!]

Tears welling in his eyes, Lu Shulong accepted the violet censer that smelled of rank stench to his senses.

—"...Lord Qianxing, do you plan to disguise yourself as demons to enter their settlement?"-Jian Nuo asked, seemingly a beat too late.

—"Indeed,"-Qu Qianxing had few demands for outsiders.—"If you do not wish to come, you may remain behind."

—"No, what I mean is, even if you disguise yourselves as demons, your and Lord Lu's appearances are simply far too conspicuous..."

—"We will alter our appearances,"-Qu Qianxing said, a slight touch of displeasure creeping into his voice.

Sensing this acutely, Jian Nuo sped up his cadence:

—"It's not an issue of disguise technique... I fully believe that with the capabilities of both lords, you could disguise yourselves flawlessly. However, the more striking someone's features are, the harder it is to disguise them into something ordinary. And once exposed, the ensuing trouble becomes far harder to manage—"

[You still think we're going to get exposed, don't you?]

Lu Shulong thought, pinching his nose to endure the stench.

Fortunately, Qu Qianxing was fundamentally a reasonable man; rather than taking offense, he listened on.

—"Furthermore! I don't believe disguising ourselves as ordinary demons will yield the intelligence we seek. After all, judging from the demons we have observed, immense rifts exist between different species and even individual entities; they devour one another over the slightest disagreement. Even if we succeed in blending in as ordinary demons to gather information, our access will be severely limited!"

—"What do you propose, then?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"I believe you should directly disguise yourself as a high-ranking demon!"

[—"?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

—"The more formidable a demon is, the more humanlike and beautiful they appear. To put it bluntly, in your current state, even without a disguise, you are the absolute spitting image of a high-ranking demon,"-Jian Nuo suggested.—"And the demon race subscribes most devoutly to the law of the jungle. As long as you display your overwhelming might right off the bat, perhaps even striking down several of the strongest figures in this settlement, you can stroll inside as an esteemed guest!"

[Whoa, that hardcore?!]

[Young Master Jian, did you forget that just a moment ago you were desperate to seal the Demon Realm shut?!]

Lu Shulong's mouth twitched.

He saw a pensive expression dawn on Qu Qianxing's face, indicating that Second Senior Brother was indeed tempted.

Yet as an "outsider" who had abruptly tagged along, Young Master Jian Nuo possessed little credit in Qu Qianxing's eyes, making Qu Qianxing hesitate over whether to adopt his advice.

—"...Second Senior Brother, I think it's viable,"-Lu Shulong chimed in.

Qu Qianxing looked at Lu Shulong in surprise.

Lu Shulong averted his eyes, avoiding his gaze:

—"Although Young Master Jian looks like a brainless playboy, he is at least the heir of Sky City. When it comes to putting on airs and playing politics, he counts as an expert. This should be an effective proposal,"-after all, this man had been his partner in a previous cycle!

—"Haha, Lord Shulong, that hardly sounds like a compliment,"-Jian Nuo said with a wry smile.

Regardless of the past, in this cycle Lu Shulong remained the junior brother Qu Qianxing trusted.

With Lu Shulong's vote of endorsement, Qu Qianxing hesitated no longer.

He asked Jian Nuo directly:

—"What do I need to do?"

—"First, taint this carriage with demonic qi as well... no need to alter its exterior!"-Jian Nuo's lips curled into a grin.—"Then, release your lightning—the stronger, the better—to proclaim your arrival to every demon below!"

---

BOOM——

Following Jian Nuo's request, Qu Qianxing's thunder detonated in the very next instant.

The terrifying display was so immense it seemed poised to tear the sky asunder.

[Isn't this going way too far?!]

Even Jian Nuo, who had proposed the idea, was startled out of his wits. It took immense willpower to keep himself from looking up to see if the colossal corpse blotting out half the sky had been awakened by the thunder.

Yet the demons below were undeniably stirred.

Jian Nuo saw countless demons wriggling out from the corpse beneath them like maggots, eliciting visceral disgust.

Several of the largest specimens conferred amongst themselves before flying up toward the canopy carriage.

One of them inquired:

—"May we ask which exalted lord has descended?"

The first step had succeeded: they believed Qu Qianxing, with his dazzling arrival, was a demon. But what followed was the true crux: they could not allow any flaw to be detected.

Assuming the role of a retainer, Jian Nuo snapped back:

—"Is the name of my lord something you are fit to pry into?!"

As Jian Nuo said this, he pulled back the gauze curtain of the canopy, intentionally revealing a sliver of Qu Qianxing's face.

That overly stunning countenance was a temptation, but also a weapon.

He was beautiful enough to make people lose their minds, yet also beautiful enough to make them drop their guard. If this held true even for humans, let alone demons who were inherently slaves to their base desires.

The moment a single demon succumbed to temptation and dared make a move against Qu Qianxing, they would serve as the sacrificial lamb for his all-obliterating thunder to establish authority.

...Provided they could beat them.

Qu Qianxing had surveyed this area with mechanical puppets and concluded that no beings above the Nascent Soul realm resided here before coming to investigate. Indeed, no "Demon Generals" equivalent to Nascent Soul cultivators had appeared; yet over a dozen "Demon Warriors" equivalent to Golden Core cultivators had swarmed up all at once. Would this truly be fine?

Jian Nuo was slightly concerned.

Yet this plan had been proposed by himself; he could hardly turn tail and flee now. Jian Nuo locked onto the demons who drooled most covetously toward Qu Qianxing's face, bracing for battle.

Yet before Jian Nuo could strike, internal discord broke out among the demons.

The Demon Warrior who appeared to be the strongest stared blankly at Qu Qianxing's face. He then yanked back a companion trying to surge forward and bowed his entire body low in midair:

—"Paying respects to Lord Demon Sovereign!"

His booming voice not only conveyed his boundless reverence, but also used the title "Demon Sovereign" to intimidate and suppress the other demons.

Hearing the words "Demon Sovereign," the other demons dropped to their knees by pure reflex.

After all, hierarchy in the Demon Realm was far more brutal than in the human world; if an upper-tier demon grew displeased, they would simply devour lower-tier demons on a whim.

Fortunately, they had dropped to their knees swiftly enough that no demon caught the stunned expression on Qu Qianxing's face.

—From this angle, Qu Qianxing's straightforward, uncompromising personality was truly unsuited for espionage work.

Jian Nuo, on the other hand, was an absolute natural.

Having spent years carousing with dissolute young masters posing as wealthy [Nth-generation cultivators](# "Literal CN: 修N代 (xiū N dài) — Nth-generation cultivator; descendant of an ancient, wealthy cultivation lineage"), even earning VIP privileges at the Palace of Carefree Wandering, the scene before him was trivial.

Though equally baffled by the title "Demon Sovereign," he demanded without batting an eye:

—"You know my lord's prestigious name?!"

—"O-Of course,"-the leading Demon Warrior replied reverently, eager to build connections and speaking loudly for the benefit of any fellow demons harboring illicit thoughts.—"This lowly one once had the honor of serving in Lumbini Holy City, and during the Demon Emperor's grand celebration, was privileged to behold the exalted visage of the Second Demon Sovereign! That peerless, breathtaking countenance is something one could never forget after laying eyes upon it even once—ARGH!"

He let out a miserable shriek, struck by Jian Nuo.

Although the demon's words held no apparent flaw—and upon hearing the title "Second Demon Sovereign," the other demons had pressed themselves even flatter against the ground than their leader, itching to vanish on the spot—a thread of unease still crept into Jian Nuo's chest.

He suddenly remembered: Qu Qianxing didn't seem particularly fond of people commenting on his face.

—Qu Qianxing naturally never stated this aloud, nor did he visibly display it, leading certain foolish Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples to innocently praise Second Senior Brother's stunning beauty (though such notions were thoroughly purged after a few rounds of exams). But who was Jian Nuo? Having stayed in the Heavenly Spirit Sect for so many days, interacting with Qu Qianxing and watching the foolish antics of his fellow aristocratic playboys, he had subtly picked up on this quirk.

Thus, Jian Nuo deemed it worth testing.

He took the gamble of striking the demon, barking:

—"Is Master's appearance something you are qualified to judge?!"

—"Ah!"-The Demon Warrior froze for a moment, then wore an even more terror-stricken expression, as if recalling some horrific past memory. He immediately slapped himself across the cheeks—vigorous, resounding slaps—until his face was swollen like a pig's head.—"Forgive me, forgive me! This lowly one misspoke! What this lowly one meant was that Lord Demon Sovereign's terrifying thunderous might was so unmistakable that this lowly one could never confuse it!"

Evidently, that Demon Sovereign who looked identical to Qu Qianxing didn't seem particularly fond of having his face praised either.

Beholding this scene, Lu Shulong deliberately spoke to Qu Qianxing via divine sense:

—"Second Senior Brother, it sounds like there's a Demon Sovereign whose appearance, powers, and even personality are practically identical to yours!"
