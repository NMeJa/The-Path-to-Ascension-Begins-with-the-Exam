# Chapter 25: A Minor Event (第25章 小活动)

Heavenly Spirit Mountain, Inner Sect Cave Abode.

—"Yu Cong, Yu Cong! There's a tournament!"

Yan Fei clamored as he leaped down from his flying sword, shouting impatiently before he had even crossed the threshold.

Yu Cong was clutching a copy of *[Elementary Arithmetic](# "Literal CN: 启蒙算学 (qǐméng suànxué) — beginner math primer written by Zhou Hui for the newly established tutor class")*, working through practice exercises. Startled half to death by Yan Fei, he asked in knee-jerk, unthinking delight:
—"What? Another exam already?"

—"Not an exam, a tournament,"-Yan Fei corrected him.-"Junior Brother Mo submitted a petition to the Immortal Venerable to host the 'First Immortal Realm Chess Tournament.' It's not just a contest of chess tactics, but a showdown of piece stat power as well..."

—"Stop, stop, don't say another word!"-Yu Cong snapped.-"Didn't I tell you never to mention anything related to chess until I come out of seclusion?"

Yu Cong clapped both hands over his ears, adopting a full-blown posture of [not listening, not listening, like a toad chanting scriptures](# "Literal CN: 不听不听蛤蟆念经 — classic playful nursery rhyme for stubbornly plugging one's ears and refusing to listen").

Watching him, Yan Fei couldn't help finding it hilarious.

Ever since discovering that day that crafting one's own chess piece required minimum exam score thresholds, this friend of his had maintained this state for days on end. Not only did he dodge every conversation regarding chess, but he also claimed to be 'in closed-door seclusion to study,' putting on the airs of a man who would never break seclusion until he achieved top marks.

Then again, Yan Fei had been thoroughly obsessed with Secret Realm Chess these past few days and hadn't paid him much attention. Only now did he urge:
—"Is that really necessary? Even if there were an exam right now, could you actually pass it?"

—"Hard to say! I'm starting to think my parents just picked a terrible tutor for my early childhood schooling, holding me back all these years,"-Yu Cong declared smugly.-"I've realized lately that I'm actually gifted across every academic subject—especially when it comes to mathematics. Give me a couple more days of studying, and I might even conquer the *Book of Changes*!"

—"You don't have a fever, do you?"-Yan Fei asked suspiciously.

He reached out with genuine concern and felt Yu Cong's forehead, momentarily forgetting about the tournament entirely:
—"Not hot at all. How on earth did you start talking out of your mind?"

—"Who's talking out of their mind?!"-Yu Cong shoved his exercise notebook into the air and shook it vigorously.-"I checked the answers myself—seven out of ten were correct!"

—"...You peeked at the answer key, didn't you?"-Yan Fei asked, taking the exercise book.

For anyone else, scoring seven out of ten might be perfectly normal. But Yu Cong belonged to that special breed of person who, when someone tried to buy his flying sword—originally worth 100 spirit stones and depreciated by twenty percent down to 80—for 60 spirit stones, would end up handing the buyer an additional 30 spirit stones in change. Sword cultivators being treated as [chumps and easy marks](# "Literal CN: 凯子 (kǎizi) — someone easily swindled or taken financial advantage of; sword cultivators notoriously bad at math") was almost certainly because there were far too many people like him.

Yet Yu Cong possessed zero self-awareness:
—"It's not a real exam, why would I cheat?!"-Yu Cong protested.

—"So you'd dare to cheat on a real exam, then... Where did you find this arithmetic book?"-Yan Fei asked.

Yan Fei spotted the crucial reason behind Yu Cong's 'breakthrough' in a single glance.

It wasn't because Yan Fei possessed piercing insight, but because his own arithmetic was abysmal as well. While not quite at Yu Cong's catastrophic level, cracking open any mathematics manual still gave him a splitting headache.

Yet this book didn't.

The text was simple and lucid, the exercises clear and engaging. It made Yan Fei feel that if he had started his early education with a textbook like this, he might not have ended up running off to become a sword cultivator in the first place.

Meanwhile, Yu Cong—who was still using a children's primer at his grown age—became inexplicably smug:
—"See? I told you that old man at the private school ruined me back then. I might very well be a mathematical prodigy."

Yan Fei regarded him with a deadpan stare, too exhausted to even roast him.

Only then did Yu Cong concede:
—"What's with that look?... Fine, the textbook was written by the Immortal Venerable."

—"The Immortal Venerable?"-Yan Fei froze.-"Why would someone like him write such a rudimentary textbook?"

—"Rudimentary my foot! I think it's plenty hard!"-Yu Cong protested.-"Besides, I really did copy this textbook from the [Teaching Assistant Class](# "Literal CN: 教习班 (jiàoxí bān) — tutor cohort trained directly by Zhou Hui to teach regular disciples")! Word is it's the newest curriculum, set to be rolled out across the entire sect!"

—"The Teaching Assistant Class?"-Yan Fei raised an eyebrow.

Following the Grand Sect Examination, both the outer and inner sects had been organized into tiered classes. Classes were reportedly scheduled to begin according to each group's academic level, and the most outstanding, patient disciples had been selected as Teaching Assistants. They received personal instruction from the Immortal Venerable, which meant their classes had commenced earlier than everyone else's.

Once these Teaching Assistants completed their training, they would step forward to instruct the regular disciples. Therefore, as their training material, this book was indeed very likely to be distributed among the general student body in the future.

Yan Fei remained perplexed:
—"Even if the Immortal Venerable wrote it, surely no disciple would need something so rudiment—"

Yan Fei paused, his gaze lingering on Yu Cong.

He let out a heartfelt sigh:
—"The Immortal Venerable truly has it rough! To think he has to rack his brain to accommodate things like you."

The aforementioned 'thing' instantly bounced to his feet:
—"What the hell is that supposed to mean?!"

—"Alright, alright. Since you've obtained the Immortal Venerable's secret manual, you won't have to worry about the next exam, right?"-Yan Fei hadn't forgotten his original purpose, cajoling him in a soothing voice.-"In that case, won't you come with me to check out the Immortal Chess tournament?"

—"What in the world is 'Immortal Chess'? I'm gone for a few days, and you guys come up with such an exaggerated name?"-Yu Cong grumbled discontentedly.-"Not going! I can't even play if I go."

—"You just can't craft your own personal piece for now. It's not like you can't play,"-Yan Fei said.

Yu Cong curled his lip:
—"I only want my own piece."

Playing chess with someone else's pieces was utterly boring.

Seeing this, Yan Fei dangled the bait:
—"The prizes this time are incredible! I heard the top ten all get high-grade spirit stones, miraculous pills, and even their choice of a handy weapon!"

—"Still has nothing to do with me. I've barely played a few rounds,"-Yu Cong said.

—"If you don't know how, I can teach you! I can even lend you my piece!"-Yan Fei pleaded earnestly.-"Bro, don't you feel it's such a waste for you to stay cooped up here? If we join forces, we can definitely crush everyone!"

—"...Cut the crap,"-Yu Cong replied coldly.

It was true that he and Yan Fei had a life-and-death friendship, but it was precisely because they were so close that one look at the way the other hitched his rear told him exactly what kind of crap he was about to drop.

—"If it were just about playing chess, you wouldn't need to look for me!"

He hadn't seen this guy show his face once in days! And no matter how good-natured Yan Fei was, he would never wheedle and coax someone this gently for half a day without an ulterior motive.

Yu Cong glared at Yan Fei:
—"Out with your real motive."

—"Ha... hahaha..."-Yan Fei gave a pair of dry chuckles before finally coming clean.-"I want you to spar with me."

—"Spar with swords?"-Yu Cong looked at Yan Fei in astonishment.

Yan Fei's average grades were better than Yu Cong's, but when it came to their specialized discipline—swordsmanship—he fell short of Yu Cong.

Especially recently: Yan Fei had been so addicted to chess that he hadn't touched a sword hilt in days.

Yu Cong asked in confusion:
—"What does that have to do with the tournament?"

—"Ahem, one of the categories in the tournament compares the stat power of the pieces everyone crafted,"-Yan Fei explained awkwardly.-"Word is that the strongest pieces will be inducted into the '[First Generation Immortal Chess Starter Set](# "Literal CN: 初代仙人棋套装 — standardized default set of piece archetypes for the chess hall")', becoming permanent default pieces for Immortal Chess from now on."

*This* was what Yan Fei truly cared about.

Just imagine: from then on, whenever anyone entered the chess hall and selected their pieces, they would inevitably see Yan Fei's piece standing as the proud representative of the sword cultivator class. What glorious prestige!

When that time came, every sword cultivator across the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect would turn green with envy!

At the very least, Yu Cong's eyes were already bloodshot with jealousy.

Being unable to craft his own piece was miserable enough—why the hell should he help someone else take the championship?!

Yet this shameless bastard kept rambling on:
—"Since it's going to become a permanent default piece, I naturally want my performance to be flawless. Didn't you say that move of mine had a flaw? I want to polish it, and I also want to adjust my stance to look much cooler—"

—"Not interested!"-Yu Cong snapped bluntly.

Yan Fei threw his arms around Yu Cong's waist:
—"Bro!"-Yan Fei wailed.

—"Beat it!"-Yu Cong barked.

—"Dad!"-Yan Fei cried.

---

Naturally, not every disciple harbored ambitions like Yan Fei's.

The [dog dev...](# "Literal CN: 狗策…… — catching oneself before saying 'Dog Game Planner' (狗策划)") that is, Junior Brother Mo Xi had prepared an entirely different package for them:

[—"Have you heard? The prize for the Immortal Chess tournament is that the champions' pieces get turned into permanent default pieces."](# "Speaker: Disciple A")

[—"That has nothing to do with someone like me. I know my own cultivation—even if made into a piece, it wouldn't be particularly strong,"](# "Speaker: Disciple B")-the speaker said, wearing the serene expression of someone who had seen through the vanity of mortal dust.-[—"I only plan to enter the tactical chess division."]

[—"Don't be so quick to dismiss it! I heard there are rewards for placing bets on the showdown!"](# "Speaker: Disciple A")

[—"Doesn't our Heavenly Spirit Sect strictly forbid gambling?"](# "Speaker: Disciple B")

[—"Of course, which is why winning a bet doesn't award spirit stones. But!"](# "Speaker: Disciple A")-the other stressed,-[—"If you guess correctly, you get a custom piece!"]

[—"A custom piece?"](# "Speaker: Disciple B")

[—"Exactly. Say you bet on Eldest Senior Brother. Once Eldest Senior Brother wins, the default set will include a standard edition of Eldest Senior Brother's piece. But at the same time, you'll receive a limited special-edition piece featuring Eldest Senior Brother's signature sword technique!"](# "Speaker: Disciple A")

[—"[Holy crap!](# "Literal CN: 窝草 (wō cǎo) — playful phonetic homophone for 我操 / holy shit")"](# "Speaker: Disciple B")

[—"Wait a second, where are you guys going?!"](# "Speaker: Disciple A")

[—"To bet on Eldest Senior Brother, obviously."](# "Speaker: Disciples")

[—"I was just using him as an example! The tournament [bans anyone at Golden Core cultivation or above](# "Literal CN: 禁止金丹修为以下 — author typo for 仅限金丹以下 (restricted to below Golden Core) / 禁止金丹及以上 (banning Golden Core and above), as shown by Yuwen Xuan (Soul Formation) and Qu Qianxing (Golden Core) being excluded while Foundation Establishment disciples are eligible") from entering, so pieces for Eldest Senior Brother, Second Senior Brother, and Eldest Senior Sister are completely out of the running."](# "Speaker: Disciple A")

[—"We can still pick Senior Brother Guan or Senior Brother Lu, right?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"One of them is an artifact refiner and the other is an alchemist. When it comes to raw combat power, they probably won't have the edge, right?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"What about Junior Brother Mo? His piece literally one-shot Eldest Senior Brother's piece! I was there in person, it was insanely cool!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Can't pick him either. He's the host, so he's not participating."](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Doesn't that mean none of the direct disciples can be chosen? Then we can only pick from the inner and outer sects! Somehow that feels a cut below the direct disciples."](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Speaking of sword cultivators, what about Yu Cong?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Don't even mention him. He flunked the Grand Sect Examination so badly he doesn't even have a piece yet. He's currently locked away in seclusion!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Yan Fei?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"He's only good at that one move. Even if he wins, there's no point in getting a special piece for him. I think we'd be better off picking Zhao Xiuxiu."](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"No, if we're talking spell cultivators, wouldn't Xiao Yang be stronger?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Zhao Xiuxiu is pretty!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

...

[—"Wait a minute, did you all forget someone?! She's a direct disciple too! In theory, whatever Junior Brother Mo Xi knows, she should know as well! She's got top-tier stats! She's super cute! And most importantly, unlike Junior Brother Mo Xi, she is a 100% certified, genuine girl—"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Ohhh!"](# "Speaker: Disciples")

The crowd let out a chorus of sudden realization, simultaneously turning their heads toward a certain corner of the sect.

---

Elsewhere, Zhou Hui was setting down a stone upon a board.

He wasn't playing that so-called 'Immortal Chess,' but an ordinary game of Go.

Yuwen Xuan slouched shamelessly across from the board, wailing the moment he saw the stone land:
—"No way, I forfeit! *Sob sob*, Master, you're too ruthless, you didn't show an ounce of mercy!"

—"If one wished for mercy, why play chess in the first place?"-Zhou Hui remarked placidly.

—"But you're just as merciless toward me everywhere else! I was clearly here first, so why do you favor the younger ones? An entire mineral vein, just handed over to Youngest Junior Brother like that!"-Yuwen Xuan whined, rolling around on the floor.

—"Shall I give you the Heavenly Spirit Sect instead?"-Zhou Hui offered nonchalantly.

—"Ah... never mind that. The seat of future sect master is better left for Qu Qianxing to get headaches over!"-Yuwen Xuan waved a dismissive hand.-"I still want to visit other worlds and look around."

—"You certainly don't lack confidence,"-Zhou Hui smiled.

Yet judging from Yuwen Xuan's cultivation progress, he genuinely had the foundation to back up his words. Once he ascended, let alone the modest assets of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even the entire mortal world would hold no meaning for him.

It was just that the Heavenly Dao...

Zhou Hui searched his memories carefully, confirming once again that Yuwen Xuan did not exist within the Heavenly Dao's script.

It was very strange. His eldest disciple was like a ghost who had vanished without a trace, never appearing in the 'novel.' On the other hand, in Zhou Hui's previous reincarnations, he remembered that after entering seclusion, he had required an exceedingly rare medicinal ingredient to heal his wounds and dispatched Yuwen Xuan to retrieve it. As a result, the brat had flown off like a kite with a snapped string, never to be heard from again.

Still, as long as he hadn't perished out there, escaping the calamity of the sect's annihilation was a blessing.

Zhou Hui was quite satisfied with this rare outcome of not needing to worry about Yuwen Xuan. He had even been considering in this life whether he should just toss the fellow out to search for herbs as well.

Yet Yuwen Xuan was, after all, the eldest disciple under Zhou Hui's tutelage. He possessed an even stronger sense of 'whatever belongs to Master is mine' than Qu Qianxing ever did.

Rolling back and forth, he sighed:
—"A mineral vein is different, though! A whole vein! Just imagine how much great stuff we could buy by digging up all those spirit stones. I love nothing more than the blissful joy of spending money without shouldering any responsibility! Master, haven't you thought about how many veins our sect has in total? One for Youngest Junior Brother, one for Youngest Junior Sister—"

—"Bei'er has no need of one,"-Zhou Hui remarked, his eyes resting on the Go board.-"That child's Extreme Yin Constitution is an exquisite rarity among rarities. Tampering with it rashly would completely ruin her. Besides, she is hardly the sort to be swayed by external possessions."

—"What she needs is a heart."

—"Then Master, are you going to give her a heart?"-Yuwen Xuan stopped rolling, perking his head up with curious interest.

Zhou Hui messed up the Go board, sweeping the stones into disarray:
—"Let's test it out. Though I'm not sure if that counts as love, those guys screaming '[waifu!](# "Literal CN: 老婆 (lǎopó) — wife; modern otaku/gaming meme of claiming cute anime/game characters as one's waifu")' might be just the right fit for a heartless puppet."
