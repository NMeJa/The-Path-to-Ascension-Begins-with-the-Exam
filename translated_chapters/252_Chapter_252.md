# Chapter 252: Three Hundred Years Later (Part 3)

The Immortality Inquiry Ceremony.

It was a combined evolution of the traditional cultivation world's "[knocking at the mountain gate](# "Literal CN: 叩山门 (kòu shānmén) — traditional rite where prospective disciples kowtow at a sect's gates to beg for admission")" and the sects' "[cultivation aptitude trials](# "Literal CN: 修仙资质考验 (xiūxiān zīzhì kǎoyàn) — standardized sect entrance tests measuring spirit roots and bone structure")". However, with the universalization of cultivation education, tailored educational mechanisms were now established regardless of whether an applicant possessed spiritual power or not. Consequently, the sense of a grueling "trial" had shrunk to near insignificance; it existed primarily as a [placement exam](# "Literal CN: 分班考试 (fēnbān kǎoshì) — standardized academic test used to stream students into different classes or ability tracks"), and in truth, there was no such thing as [failing the admissions cut](# "Literal CN: 落榜 (luòbǎng) — failing to see one's name on the imperial or university examination rolls").

Naturally, from an individual perspective, everyone still hoped they possessed supreme cultivation talent so they could embark upon the smoothest, most unobstructed Grand Dao of Immortality.

Yet in Long Tianming's eyes, this ceremony was virtually indistinguishable from the mandatory magic-power aptitude test at the start of every retro fantasy web novel's magic academy arc.

It was the obligatory narrative juncture where the protagonist either gave an initial display of overwhelming might, or had their true power obscured by some cosmic anomaly, inviting public mockery and triggering the quintessential "[do not bully the young and poor!](# "Literal CN: 莫欺少年穷 (mò qī shàonián qióng) — iconic web novel battle cry from Battle Through the Heavens: 'Do not look down on a poor youth!'")" subplot. Depending on the author's execution, it branched into either an immediate [power-fantasy romp](# "Literal CN: 爽文 (shuǎngwén) — feel-good / gratification-driven web novels focused on effortless triumph") or a cathartic [face-slapping](# "Literal CN: 打脸 (dǎliǎn) — public humiliation of arrogant antagonists when the protagonist reveals their true power") progression. Regrettably, because the trope had been beaten to death, modern web fiction saw it less and less.

Long Tianming, however, had always been rather fond of this classic trope, even looking forward to it with palpable anticipation.

—At least, that was the case before he stepped into the Immortality Inquiry Hall.

***

Not that there was anything wrong with the Immortality Inquiry Hall or the ceremony itself.

In truth, even for an Immortality Inquiry Hall situated in a remote, backwater town, it was outfitted with every necessary fixture. Regardless of a settlement's scale or geography, every Immortality Inquiry Hall across the realm had been designed and uniformly configured by the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

As the grand hall where the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony took place, it appeared no different from any mundane building at first glance. Yet once one stepped across the threshold, one realized that as the starting point where mortal youths first encountered the cultivation world, it was at once remarkably mundane and breathtakingly vast.

It was mundane because its interior matched every other building in town: four simple walls and a plain floor. If anything, it was even more modest, completely devoid of lavish ornamentation. The sole peculiarity was a sunken hollow in the center of the flagstones, within which rested an enormous bronze cauldron.

This cauldron was currently stewing an enormous pot of rich bone broth. The billowing plumes of steam rose like the physical manifestation of its savory aroma, white vapor rolling forth in thick torrents until it coalesced into the distinct shape of a staircase, spiraling gracefully into the sky.

The vastness, naturally, belonged to the heavens.

Indeed, the hall possessed no ceiling—or at least, none while the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony was underway.

Thus, the jade-blue expanse of the sky lay completely unobstructed before their eyes. When one lifted one's gaze, line of sight was not hindered by the surrounding masonry in the slightest; it felt as though one stood upon an endless prairie, gazing into the distance where heaven and earth converged, looking upward toward the zenith of the sky where the clouds appeared within arm's reach. Adrift amidst those layered white clouds, one could vaguely discern towering, magnificent celestial citadels and immortals flitting in and out of the divine cities. And the white plumes of vapor drifting from the great cauldron formed a cascading flight of steps that led all the way above the sea of clouds, right to the threshold of the citadel gates—paving a physical road into the sky.

Long Tianming stared blankly at the celestial citadel in the sky, swallowing a mouthful of saliva.

A wave of nerves began to grip him.

Owing to the hyper-developed information media of his former era, people of that age perpetually suffered from an illusion: they mistook knowledge gleaned from video clips and text posts for skills they genuinely owned. Yet when forced to confront reality, they invariably discovered the all-too-common syndrome of "[the brain has mastered it, but the body hasn't](# "Literal CN: 大脑会了，身体不会 — popular internet meme describing watching instructional videos and feeling competent, only to fail completely when attempting the physical task")". Worse, because the actual circumstances never quite aligned with what they had watched on screen, it spawned a panic far deeper than knowing nothing at all.

Long Tianming was a textbook victim.

Had he walked in to find a glowing crystal orb or some similar prop, he likely would have continued strutting with smug bravado. But confronted first by the boundless sky and a floating celestial city that so perfectly embodied the grandest cultivation fantasies, while he naturally harbored yearning, as an ordinary mortal, an irrepressible dread crawled from his chest straight into his throat.

[...They call it 'Inquiring of the Immortals,'] Long Tianming asked shakily in the depths of his mind, [but don't tell me they expect me to climb all the way into the clouds along a staircase made of steam?!]

[More or less,] the "Old Grandpa" replied, sounding rather intrigued. [Weren't you brimming with anticipation just moments ago? How come you've turned chicken?]

[That's completely different—!]

Long Tianming tried to calm himself down, to little avail.

He was, after all, a student raised under a strict materialist education. Now that he grasped what "Inquiring of Immortals" actually entailed, even if he firmly believed he was the so-called "protagonist," his brain simply could not comprehend stepping upon wisps of steam with a flesh-and-blood mortal body to ascend into the heavens without the slightest external apparatus.

It was precisely like a student who had survived the Gaokao, believing themselves thoroughly hardened to examinations, who suddenly encountered a problem they had no earthly idea how to solve—panicking far harder than someone who had never sat for a test in their life.

The "Old Grandpa," however, was delighted by this development.

He had long felt this host's manic excitement bordered on abnormal, lacking proper reverence toward him and even harboring subtle suspicions. Right now presented the perfect opportunity to [CPU](# "Literal CN: CPU (网络流行语) — Chinese internet slang meaning to psychologically manipulate, brainwash, or gaslight someone (derived from an actress mistakenly saying 'CPU' instead of 'PUA')") the kid.

[This trial does indeed possess some difficulty,] the "Old Grandpa" coaxed. [However, so long as you have this old master by your side—]

—"Come, come, come! Drink soup!"-Big Sister Huo's hearty voice drowned out the "Old Grandpa," calling out warmly to everyone.

She stood before the great bronze cauldron, waving her hand toward the trio. Beside her, the plump cultivator presiding over the ceremony beamed broadly as he ladled a steaming bowl of fragrant meat broth from the depths of the cauldron.

Long Tianming couldn't help but freeze, subconsciously glancing toward his two companions.

The "pretty maiden" didn't step forward immediately either. Yet her expression wasn't one of astonishment; rather, she looked faintly disgusted. Indeed, an ethereal beauty like her would never deign to stand around sipping soup so uncouthly. Conversely, it was Young Master Zhou—who looked for all the world as though he [didn't eat the food of common mortals](# "Literal CN: 不食人间烟火 — aloof from mortal dust / detached from mundane existence")—who strolled over with complete natural ease, accepting the bowl.

—"[Good food doesn't fear arriving late,](# "Literal CN: 好饭不怕晚 (hǎo fàn bù pà wǎn) — proverb: a good meal is worth waiting for / good things take time")"-the plump cultivator beamed.-"You folks arrived at the perfect hour! After every regular Immortality Inquiry Ceremony, having absorbed the freshest and richest [power of aspiration](# "Literal CN: 愿力 (yuànlì) — power of vows / collective wishing force") as seasoning, this pot of broth is at its most savory and potent!"

As he spoke, he ladled another bowl of soup and shoved it into Long Tianming's hands.

Along with the motion, an intoxicating aroma wafted straight into his nostrils.

Though it was merely a simple bowl of meat broth, it seemed far more alluring than anything Long Tianming had tasted in his entire life. Even having eaten lunch at midday, his mouth watered uncontrollably, his [index finger twitching with appetite](# "Literal CN: 食指大动 (shízhǐ dà dòng) — idiom: one's index finger twitches; an uncontrollable appetite whetted").

Long Tianming struggled mightily to maintain his rationality, stopping himself from gulping it down on the spot.

[You're cautious, at least,] the "Old Grandpa" sneered upon seeing this. [Drink with peace of mind. This concoction qualifies as a supreme tonic; consuming it holds nothing but benefits with zero harm.]

Long Tianming ignored him, keeping his eyes on the others.

The plump cultivator ladled a third bowl and handed it to Renran.

Though Renran wore a face full of disdain, having accepted the meat broth, she didn't refuse taking a dainty sip. Turning toward Zhou Hui, she let out a cold scoff:

—"Quite generous, aren't you! And here I thought three sticks of incense would be enough to dismiss them."

—"For children anxious over an examination, food is the finest tranquilizer,"-Zhou Hui replied with a faint smile.

How could he ever bring himself to admit to outsiders that when he originally drafted the standards for the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony, he had indeed intended to use offering incense? It was inexpensive, convenient, and easily standardized. Who could have foreseen that during the very first public field test, a certain Taotie would surreptitiously alter the target coordinates, anchoring the smoke staircase directly into his own gullet and inhaling the offering incense of the entire experimental testing zone in one single gulp?!

Faced with an emergency and left with no alternative, Zhou Hui had been forced to confiscate that wretched ferocious beast's private stash of lunch ingredients to handle the crisis on the fly, ordering him to boil a massive cauldron of broth as a substitute for offering incense.

Fortunately, this impromptu alteration brought about some delightful surprises.

For mortals who had never truly interacted with the cultivation world, food proved unexpectedly soothing to their frayed nerves. In impoverished backwaters especially, vast numbers of peasants came to attend the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony purely for the free meat broth, which effectively expanded the demographic coverage of the talent search. And thus, it had been finalized as the permanent standard protocol.

[Yet for those who are well-fed and warmly clothed, the efficacy is suboptimal,] Yao'er observed in Zhou Hui's mind. It had been closely monitoring the reactions of both this outsider Long Tianming and Renran. [It appears to trigger their suspicion.]

[Harboring emotions such as vigilance and anxiety is a good thing,] Zhou Hui remarked. [It demonstrates they still retain the capacity for independent thought. Even if one studies out of worldly desires, one will always manage to absorb some knowledge.]

[Is that why the disciples of your house possess an overabundance of 'independent thought'?!]

Yao'er nearly blurted out that retort.

Over these past three hundred years, even a machine had accumulated enough [complaints and roasts](# "Literal CN: 吐槽 (tùcáo) — ACG slang: snarky commentary / roasts / complaints") regarding those senior brothers and sisters to fill volumes.

However, as the "youngest" direct disciple beneath Zhou Hui's tutelage, having lived as a human for three centuries, it had developed a modicum of emotional intelligence after all, and refrained from voicing the question aloud.

On the other side, Long Tianming saw that both Renran and Zhou Hui had finished their soup. He also noticed Big Sister Huo filling a bowl, which she carefully stowed away in her storage pouch without drinking—evidently an annual bonus for leading batches of fresh recruits to the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony. Having finally confirmed this was genuine treasure, he put his worries to rest and took a tentative sip of the broth.

In an instant, an overwhelming sense of comfort suffused Long Tianming from head to toe.

It felt as though he had stepped out of a frozen blizzard directly into a steaming hot spring while downing a cup of warm wine. Every pore across his body burst open in sublime bliss. Not to mention, the flavor of the meat broth was every bit as sensational as its aroma—so delectable one wanted to swallow one's own tongue whole. No longer needing to suppress his appetite, Long Tianming could hardly wait to gulp down the rest of the bowl.

Having emptied the bowl, he felt as if he were [drifting up into immortality](# "Literal CN: 飘飘欲仙 (piāopiāo-yùxiān) — idiom from Su Shi's Red Cliff: light and floating as if becoming an immortal").

No, it wasn't merely a "feeling."

Long Tianming discovered that as the soup settled in his belly, a surge of spiritual power condensed within his dantian—far more obedient to his command than any spiritual energy he had accrued while cultivating alongside the "Old Grandpa" over the past several days!

With merely a flicker of thought, his physical vessel hovered slightly off the ground!

—"Not bad, not bad at all!"-the plump cultivator praised at the sight.

Compared to "hidden clan scions" like Zhou Hui and Renran, who drank their soup at a leisurely pace without showing the slightest reaction, the plump cultivator clearly favored candidates like Long Tianming who wore their responses on their sleeves.

—"To command the spiritual qi in your body right after downing the broth, your talent is quite commendable, young man,"-the plump cultivator said, not forgetting Zhou Hui and Renran either.-"The two of you are also splendid, truly splendid. I haven't laid eyes on such promising seedlings in quite some time. It seems passing the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony won't pose any difficulty for any of you."

—"Heh heh,"-Long Tianming chuckled, praised so heartily that he immediately felt he was [back in top form](# "Literal CN: 我又行了 (wǒ yòu xíng le) — modern internet meme: 'I've got this again' / sudden surge of swagger"). Yet he still couldn't resist verifying:-"The contents of the exam are just climbing into the clouds, right?"

—"Sharp lad!"-The plump cultivator gave a big thumbs-up, then added:-"Though it isn't nearly so demanding. Inquiring of Immortals, Inquiring of Immortals—if you've already scaled the clouds, what would be left to inquire? Therefore, so long as you can channel your spiritual power and step onto the staircase, you've already met the minimum threshold for academy admission."

Hearing this, Long Tianming secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

Though his confidence had somewhat rebounded, he had remained considerably uneasy. But if it only required stepping onto the steam staircase, now that he could channel spiritual energy to levitate, it naturally ceased to be an issue.

The plump cultivator didn't seem to notice Long Tianming's fluctuating emotions, continuing his cheerful explanation:

—"However, the higher you climb, the more academies will vie for your enrollment, and the wider your scope of choice becomes. Naturally, with your intellect and talent, who knows? You might very well become the first in our entire region to climb all the way to the citadel gates! In that case, you would earn the qualifications to enter White Jade Capital Academy."

—"White Jade Capital..."-Long Tianming muttered, his eyes instantly lighting up.

Under the influence of a certain machine, countless terms in the cultivation world originated from Long Tianming's native civilization; naturally, he harbored a profound romantic yearning toward the legendary name of White Jade Capital.

He could hardly wait, asking eagerly:

—"When does the ceremony begin?!"

—"It has already begun,"-the plump cultivator smiled warmly.-"Once you are prepared, you may ascend the staircase at any moment to select the path you wish to walk in the future."

—"Finally,"-Renran muttered impatiently.

This Immortality Inquiry Ceremony was utterly unlike any novice selection trial in her comprehension. It possessed not an ounce of the solemn gravity an orthodox ceremony ought to have; calling it a ceremony was practically an insult. The presiding proctor showed zero divine majesty, coddling candidates as if coaxing toddlers. Even Renran, commanded to act out a script beside the Child of Destiny, could never bring herself to perform such degrading pampering.

[Wait a moment... Don't tell me Untiring arranged this?!]

[Is he trying to win over the Child of Destiny?!]

Renran thought suspiciously, reluctantly taking a step closer to Long Tianming.

Zhou Hui showed no reaction, and Long Tianming barely paid any heed to Renran's movements. At this juncture, his entire focus was captivated by the Immortality Inquiry Ceremony itself, having tossed all thoughts of face-slapping, [flexing his might before mortal crowds](# "Literal CN: 人前显圣 (rénqián-xiǎnshèng) — web novel trope: manifesting divinity / flexing grand power in public for prestige"), or courting gorgeous maidens clean out of his mind.

—"I'm fully prepared!"-Long Tianming exclaimed excitedly.

—"Splendid, please!"-The plump cultivator beamed, waving his sleeve with grand flourish.

***

The white steam rising from the bronze cauldron swirled in a gentle arc toward Long Tianming and his party, dipping down slightly as though waiting for them to board.

This time, Long Tianming didn't hesitate. He channeled his spiritual power to levitate without delay, alighting firmly upon the steam staircase.

His footing was remarkably solid.

Though it appeared to be ethereal, insubstantial vapor, stepping onto it felt no different from walking upon flat flagstones.

[Just treat it like a glass skybridge at a scenic tourist park. Don't look down, I've got this,] Long Tianming muttered in his heart, keeping his eyes glued straight ahead as he continued upward.

Seeing this, Renran hurried to follow closely behind Long Tianming.

Zhou Hui cast a glance at the plump cultivator, smiled faintly, and brought up the rear.

The plump cultivator didn't pay much attention to Zhou Hui. Watching the trio vanish into the misty staircase after merely a few paces, he merrily ladled himself a bowl of meat broth, only for Big Sister Huo's sarcastic voice to chime in beside him:

—"'A promising seedling you haven't seen in ages'? 'Qualified to enter White Jade Capital Academy'? Old Huang, your skill at [drawing big pies](# "Literal CN: 画大饼 (huà dà bǐng) — painting unrealistic visions / dangling empty promises") has reached absolute perfection, hasn't it?!"

—"What else could I do? Country kids are too timid; if I don't heap praises on them, they won't dare take a single step. Wouldn't that ruin my [quarterly performance KPI](# "Literal CN: 绩效 (jìxiào) — performance metrics / KPI evaluation")?"-Old Huang sighed, taking a sip of hot soup.-"The times have truly changed. Back in my day, people begged and wept for a chance at immortality; whoever heard of coddling brats into cultivating?!"

—"Aren't you afraid of drawing the pie so big that the kid chokes on it?"-Big Sister Huo asked, still gazing upward with genuine concern.

—"Aiming high is a virtue; you must have faith in them. Besides—"-Old Huang replied.

—"Besides what?"-Big Sister Huo pressed.

—"Besides, you know, don't you? My grandfather's third grand-aunt's cousin's... wife's maternal grandfather's third younger brother's household has a clan relative who is an actual Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple—and from the very first graduating class after the Cultivation Education Reform, no less! Naturally, I possess quite a bit of insider information!"-Old Huang puffed out his round belly with immense pride.

Big Sister Huo had heard this absurdly convoluted family tree hundreds of times already. Rolling her eyes in exasperation, she snapped:

—"Cut the nonsense! What insider info? Could it be White Jade Capital Academy is [expanding enrollment](# "Literal CN: 扩招 (kuòzhāo) — university enrollment expansion / increasing student intake") this year?"

—"More or less."

[!—]

Seeing Big Sister Huo's stunned expression, Old Huang hurriedly waved his hands:

—"Not in the way you're thinking. Rather, this year's Immortality Inquiry Ceremony is a bit bizarre. You know how difficult it is to get into White Jade Capital Academy, especially after the new cultivation education was rolled out across the Five Domains and Six Abysses. Screening every single child across the entire world has driven the admission standards through the roof—without one-in-ten-thousand talent, you can't even dream of it. Yet in this year's ceremony, our remote corner alone has already produced three candidates..."

—"Three?!"-Big Sister Huo's eyes widened like saucers.

The territory Huo Yingqing oversaw was the northwestern corner of the Eastern Domain. Though vast—larger in geographic area than the greatest mortal human empire in the heart of the domain—its population didn't equal a single province there. From ancient antiquity to the present, promising seedlings had always migrated to resource-rich metropolitan centers, leaving behind mortals of mediocre aptitude. Even with the new cultivation education having been implemented for three hundred years, this entire region had only ever produced two individuals qualified for White Jade Capital Academy.

To produce three in one single intake—how could she not be astounded?

Yet while Huo Yingqing seemed rough around the edges, she possessed a remarkably keen mind. Once the initial shock faded, rather than delight, her brows knitted together in worry:

—"Won't that mean White Jade Capital Academy will raise the [cutoff score](# "Literal CN: 录取线 (lùqǔxiàn) — university admission cutoff mark / score threshold")? Our preschool and primary cultivation tutoring out here is atrocious; forget White Jade Capital Academy, barely any of our kids can even qualify for intermediate academies! If this keeps up..."

—"No need to fret about that for now. According to my grandfather's... *cough*, my clan relative,"-Old Huang was about to [recite the full menu of ancestral titles](# "Literal CN: 报菜名 (bào cài míng) — famous crosstalk piece: rattling off long lists of dishes; rattling off endless family ties") again, but caught Big Sister Huo's murderous glare and coughed hastily,-"this year appears to be an exceptional situation. The admission standards won't be altered—not this year, nor for the next few years. Instead, they are increasing the total number of academies!"

—"Isn't that an enrollment expansion in all but name?!"-Huo Yingqing gasped in delight.

Old Huang tilted his head back, gazing up at the celestial staircase vanishing into the sea of clouds:

—"Indeed. A once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. Who knows, perhaps these children truly possess that stroke of immortal fate?"

***

Immortal fate?

That was a phrase only ancient fossils who had lived over three hundred years still tossed around.

In today's cultivation system, the weight of "immortal fate" had diminished to near insignificance. Not that it was entirely useless—good luck was always welcome at any juncture—but it was no longer capable of easily deciding the success or failure of one's cultivation as it had before the education reform. Modern young cultivators possessed an entirely different set of [subcultural jargon](# "Literal CN: 黑话 (hēihuà) — argot / insider slang / jargon").

At the very least, Long Tianming believed his progress had nothing to do with immortal fate; it was purely a matter of strength.

—"These questions are way too simple. I could solve them with my eyes closed,"-Long Tianming declared with triumphant swagger.

Yes—exam questions.

While scaling the celestial staircase served as the very first test for children entering the cultivation world in this backwater realm—ordinarily an endurance climb testing raw spiritual power, physical stamina, and mental fortitude—there was also a contingency protocol. If a candidate fell short in stamina and mindset, the system probed their intellectual talent and generated targeted exam papers, ensuring no diamond in the rough slipped through the cracks.

Long Tianming belonged squarely to the latter category.

His physical stamina and mental fortitude were simply too pathetic—though that wasn't entirely fair.

Long Tianming's physical constitution wasn't actually half bad; it was vastly superior to his pre-transmigration body.

After all, surrounded by dense concentrations of spiritual energy in the cultivation world, even ordinary mortals devoid of spirit roots were far sturdier than organisms of a purely physical world—vastly sturdier. To put it bluntly, compared to creatures of a purely physical world, even the common livestock here were practically mutated super-specimens, capable of performing extreme athletic feats with effortless ease.

Regrettably, while Long Tianming arrogantly cast himself as a web novel protagonist, his rigid mindset imposed its own mental chains.

He simply couldn't conceive of his physical body endlessly scaling stairs for hours on end.

In truth, as a sedentary [dead-tree otaku](# "Literal CN: 死宅 (sǐzhái) — hardcore shut-in / otaku who never leaves the house") who had engaged in pure mental labor in a high-tech society, after climbing the staircase for less than half a shichen ([one hour](# "1 shichen (时辰) = 2 modern hours; half a shichen = under 1 hour")), Long Tianming felt he was [completely done for](# "Literal CN: 觉得自己不行了 — felt he couldn't go on / stamina depleted").

—"Just how long do we have to climb?"-Long Tianming grumbled, his initial thrill at experiencing novel wonders rapidly eroded by the mind-numbing, repetitive physical exertion.-"It feels like we haven't gotten any closer at all... If I stop to take a breather, I won't tumble off, will I?"

—"You will not. As long as you do not surrender, you may climb indefinitely,"-Zhou Hui replied with a gentle smile.

—"Indefinitely..."-Long Tianming groaned, already wanting to throw in the towel.

Renran's brows knitted.

While she had no desire for the Child of Destiny to attend the Heavenly Spirit Sect's White Jade Capital Academy, this gutless tendency to give up so readily filled her with profound contempt. Furthermore, an ordinary academy was hardly befitting of a Child of Destiny's exalted station; he needed to attend an elite institution on par with White Jade Capital at the very least.

—"Young Master, please persevere a little longer. The higher one climbs, the finer the academies available for selection,"-Renran urged, forcing herself to maintain a patient facade.

Zhou Hui was less than pleased with that formulation:

—"While instructional quality does vary somewhat across academies, it bears no correlation with altitude. The academies situated on different cloud levels merely correspond to varying depths of cultivation curricula. Furthermore, even if one exits at a lower cloud tier and enrolls in a different tier of academy, one's future is by no means set in stone. Should a student desire a more preferred institution later on, there remain established channels to undergo testing for academic advancement and transfer."

—"Academic advancement... transfer?"-Long Tianming shivered violently.

That all-too-familiar academic terminology triggered an ominous premonition.

The "Old Grandpa" sensed his psychological state.

Though unaware of why Long Tianming had suffered this existential shudder, he wasn't about to let the opportunity slip away.

[Indeed, persevere!] the "Old Grandpa" chimed in, echoing Renran. [In the cultivation world, if you fail to reach a sufficiently lofty vantage, your future will be bleak. At the very least, these two 'new friends' you've just met will look down upon you with absolute scorn.]

As he spoke, he channeled subtle wisps of demonic qi, stimulating emotions of indolence, rebelliousness, and irritation within Long Tianming's soul, amplifying the shadowy recesses of his heart.

In an instant, the impatience in Long Tianming's heart exploded tenfold.

[Why the hell do I still have to sit for EXAMS even after transmigrating to another world?!]

[And who wants to read such a boring, tedious plot anyway?! At a time like this, shouldn't an arrogant young master jump out—ah, that's a bit much, but at least touch a crystal orb to measure aptitude and walk the 'Child of Destiny' or 'Do Not Bully the Young and Poor' routine!]

[At the very least, where's my golden finger...?!]

Thinking up to here, Long Tianming couldn't resist asking the "Old Grandpa" in his mind:

[Cut the empty platitudes. Do you have any cheat to let me breeze through this trial right now?]

[That would be cheating. If caught, it might sever your immortal path forever,] the "Old Grandpa" warned with mock severity, before immediately dangling the bait: [However, it isn't as though there are no methods whatsoever. Certain cultivation manuals can allow you to pass the exam legitimately—even under scrutiny, it would register as your own capability. It's just that you'll have to suffer a bit of hardship down the line.]

[What kind of hardship?]

[You'll become rather emotional.] *And prone to demonic corruption.*

The "Old Grandpa" swallowed the second half of the thought, withholding the genuine peril.

Hearing this, Long Tianming adopted a contemplative look as if weighing the advice, but in truth, the warning merely slid off his [smooth brain](# "Literal CN: 平滑的大脑 (pínghuá de dànǎo) — modern internet slang: smooth brain / lack of critical thinking") without triggering a single thought.

This was the chronic affliction of children raised in peaceful, sheltered eras.

Exposed to endless internet media, they deluded themselves into believing they understood everything. In reality, "knowing of a concept" and "actually experiencing it" were entirely different dimensions. Their vigilance in the face of mortal danger was woefully inadequate; even when they inwardly suspected an Old Grandpa (or System) was untrustworthy, it was usually framed through the lens of a gamer whining that game developers were stingy with gacha rewards—never once realizing the entity was genuinely plotting to harvest their life.

[What do I need to do...?]-he asked at last.

The "Old Grandpa" revealed a sinister smirk invisible to Long Tianming, preparing to impart the corrupting art. Yet right at that very second, Long Tianming felt something abruptly plaster itself across his face with a sharp *smack*!

—"What the hell is this?!"-Long Tianming flailed in utter chaos, nearly tumbling head over heels off the celestial staircase. Fortunately, Zhou Hui discreetly summoned a wisp of clear breeze from behind to prop him up, preventing a grisly tragedy.

[There is a safety hazard here. Note it down,] Zhou Hui instructed his youngest disciple within his consciousness.

[Understood, Master.]

Renran took two steps forward, peeling off the object plastered across Long Tianming's face.

It was a sheet of blank paper.

—"Blank paper?"-Renran knitted her brows, glancing at what she held, then looking toward the sky.

—"Blank paper?"-Long Tianming wiped his face, annoyed at his embarrassing loss of composure. Seeking to change the subject, he peeked at the "blank paper" in Renran's hand:-"That's not blank paper. There's clearly a... question on it?"

As he spoke, he subconsciously lifted his eyes toward the sky.

Unbeknownst to them, countless sheets of paper had begun fluttering down from the sea of clouds like falling snow, drifting everywhere—instantly evoking within Long Tianming memories of his high school teaching building on the afternoon the Gaokao ended.

Renran experienced her own association, though vastly different from Long Tianming's.

—"A question? You can see it...?"-Renran froze, then whipped around to glare at Zhou Hui.-"Could this be... a Heavenly Book?!"

A Heavenly Book was the Heavenly Court's instrument for [special direct admission](# "Literal CN: 保招 (bǎozhāo) — guaranteed recruitment / special direct recruitment bypassing normal entrance exams").

Whenever the lower realms produced extraordinary talents urgently required by the Heavenly Court, high-risk targets, or high-value nascent worlds beyond direct celestial interference, the Heavenly Court would bestow a Heavenly Book to recruit the target or prominent figures of that realm, permitting them to bypass the orthodox tribulation ascension route and become celestial officials on the spot.

However, a Heavenly Book shouldn't appear here—at least, Renran had received no prior oracle.

So this was Zhou Hui's doing?!

He wanted to poach the Child of Destiny before the Heavenly Court could?!

Her latent anxieties instantly flooded her mind.

Zhou Hui met her gaze with an easy smile:

—"It hardly qualifies as a Heavenly Book. In truth, it is merely a supplementary mechanism of the celestial staircase: specialized exam questions tailored for gifted youths. Should one answer correctly, it saves the time required to climb the staircase."

It could also funnel them toward specialized academies better aligned with their unique aptitudes.

This served as a supplementary problem set for youths with [unbalanced academic profiles](# "Literal CN: 偏科 (piānkē) — subject favoritism / leaning heavily toward one subject while failing others; e.g. math genius who fails gym"). After all, due to congenital or environmental factors, there were always candidates who lacked stamina or mental fortitude. Yet compared to a purely physical world, immortal spellcraft could evaluate human potential far more expediently—particularly with supercomputer-tier entities like the Divine Tree calculating future trajectories, formulating an optimal developmental roadmap for every child who cleared the stairs. (Though ultimately, their achievement still depended on personal diligence.)

And those who learn of prophecies inevitably tend to fulfill them.

Long Tianming was no exception.

Though his soul was hardly that of a child, hearing words like "talent" and "supplementary exam questions," his ears pricked up immediately.

—"Talent, you say!"-Snatching the "Heavenly Book" from Renran's reluctant hands, he peered at it with curiosity, and promptly burst into a smug grin:-"Ah, it's just a math problem! And junior high level at that!"

—"Mathematics?"-Renran's brows furrowed. She had never heard such a word in the Heavenly Court.

—"You are proficient in mathematics?"-Zhou Hui remarked, rather surprised.

—"Naturally! That was my major, and I nearly..."-If Long Tianming had a tail, it would have been wagging high in the air. This surge of pride was even more intoxicating than when he had first blasted a wild rabbit with a spell. But noticing Renran's baffled expression, he finally recalled his identity as a transmigrator, and begrudgingly swallowed his proudest boast back down:

[*I nearly got accepted into Professor Zhou's research group!*]

Renran hadn't heard his inner thought, but the "Old Grandpa" lurking within his soul caught it:

[What is this 'Professor Zhou'?]

[None of your business, you wouldn't understand even if I told you,] Long Tianming brushed him off, though he couldn't help adding: [He is a god-tier luminary in my heart, and not just in my field either.]

[A god?!] The "Old Grandpa" couldn't pretend not to care anymore, asking nervously: [What god? How did you meet Him? Did He ever disclose His full name?]

[Not from your world!] Long Tianming had no desire to chat further with the Old Grandpa.

Only, when the "Old Grandpa" inquired about the full name, Long Tianming paused, subconsciously glancing at Zhou Hui.

*Wait... the same name?*

*Must be a coincidence. But everyone always called him 'Professor, Professor'—for a moment, I actually can't recall the full name.*

Long Tianming achieved internal rationalization, utterly failing to connect the elderly professor of his memories with this youthful-looking second-generation immortal scion before him, merely offering Zhou Hui a silly grin.

Zhou Hui returned the smile without understanding why:

—"Mathematical talent is quite rare indeed."

*It seems this Child of Destiny is actually somewhat useful after all.*

[Since he is a diagnostic test program, being proficient in mathematics is hardly surprising,] Yao'er reasoned plausibly. [Perhaps the simulated personality was an elite from a relevant industry.]

[That is splendid. Qianxing has been complaining of an acute shortage of assistants.]

[Second Senior Brother is perpetually short-handed,] Yao'er asked. [Shall we assign him a special quota?]

[Let us inspect his exam scores first,] Zhou Hui replied, having no intention of bypassing the standard metrics.

Long Tianming shuddered violently.

In a daze, he seemed to hallucinate senior brothers and sisters smiling creepily at him, coaxing him to join their lab. Alas, back then Long Tianming's grades had fallen just shy; he hadn't even earned the qualification to be an [academic draft beast](# "Literal CN: 做牛马 (zuò niúmǎ) — modern slang: working like a beast of burden / corporate or lab slave"), and had been forced to seek employment elsewhere, becoming a beast of burden for capitalists instead. Yet this did not stop Long Tianming from treating "almost joining the lab" as a badge of lifelong glory—much like people who spend their entire adult lives bragging about their Gaokao score.

At this moment, however, Long Tianming brushed aside the eerie dread in his gut.

After all, while he despised exams, that was solely because he disliked questions he couldn't solve. Who on earth could hate a test where a guaranteed perfect score was in the bag—especially at a middle school level?

Seeing how Miss Goddess hadn't even heard of "mathematics," the mathematical development of this world had to be laughably primitive!

*Makes sense! Everyone's cultivating immortality; who studies math?!*

*This is practically the classic trope of [transmigrating to a world where the math level dropped by a thousandfold](# "Literal CN: 穿越到了数学水平下降一千倍 — popular web novel subgenre where common knowledge is degraded a thousandfold, making the protagonist an instant genius")! It's tailor-made for me!*

*Now THIS is the treatment a transmigrator deserves!*

Long Tianming managed to suppress the smug glee in his heart with great effort, scrutinizing the problem. Instantly, his deductive steps materialized upon the "Heavenly Book."

This time, Renran could see the writing, but to her eyes, those algebraic symbols were genuinely no different from an indecipherable heavenly cipher.

—"What on earth are these?"-Renran frowned. She ground her teeth as she glared at Zhou Hui, but lacking the courage to confront him directly, she could only target Long Tianming:-"Do not place blind faith in whatever is written there. Even if it is a Heavenly Book, there are perils. [Haste makes waste](# "Literal CN: 欲速则不达 (yù sù zé bù dá) — Analects: desiring speed results in not arriving / more haste, less speed")."

[She speaks the truth,] the "Old Grandpa" chimed in.

Long Tianming's lips curled upward higher and higher.

If the question had been purely textual, he might genuinely have worried about hidden tricks or verbal traps laced with mystical spells producing the effect of a devil's contract. But how could *math* have any tricks?

Math was math: either you knew it, or you didn't.

Long Tianming thought triumphantly, effortlessly solving the middle-school math problem in a split second.

Instantly, the cloud beneath Long Tianming's feet spiraled upward, lifting him dozens of meters into the azure sky!

Unable to hold back his laughter, Long Tianming crowed with unbridled delight:

—"Again!"

And as though answering his summons, another "Heavenly Book" fluttered into his hands.

***

[Ah... it seems there is no stopping him,] the "Old Grandpa" thought in despair.

This was why he utterly detested the new cultivation education!

Things had never been like this in the past!

To say nothing of his prime, even back when he was merely a wisp of demonic qi freshly spawned from mortal negative emotions during the era when cultivation relied solely on "immortal fate," he could effortlessly possess any ignorant fool of a cultivator. Feeding on their ignorance and desires, he could hook out their greed, anger, and delusion to guide them into corruption. How could it compare to this current age, where every child possessed academies as a [universal safety net](# "Literal CN: 保底 (bǎodǐ) — guaranteed minimum / gacha pity pull / baseline security"), leaving fewer and fewer willing to put their trust in him?!

Yet he was powerless to stop Long Tianming from taking the test. Watching his near-captured prey slip from his grasp once again, he could only choke down his fury and quietly bide his time for the next opportunity.

*With this kid's mental fortitude being so abysmal, another chance will surely present itself... right?*

Yet while the "Old Grandpa" gave up, that didn't mean Renran was throwing in the towel.

Unaware that the Celestial Emperor had already been extinguished and the Heavenly Court was on a countdown to total oblivion, her sole desire was to complete her mission as swiftly as possible and bid farewell to this realm. Gritting her teeth, Renran glared at Zhou Hui, declaring war:

—"I will never hand the Child of Destiny over to you!"

Having spoken her piece, as though terrified Zhou Hui might strike her dead with a bolt of lightning, she summoned an auspicious cloud and hurriedly gave chase toward Long Tianming.

—"I had no intention of snatching him..."-Zhou Hui murmured, wearing a faintly aggrieved expression.

He made no move to pursue Renran, merely standing upon the celestial staircase and gazing upward.

Long Tianming's progress was gratifying. It appeared middle-school mathematics suited his palate splendidly—vastly swifter than scaling stairs, at any rate. By the looks of it, after the duration of a single standard exam period, he would reach the cloud platform closest to him—the very location housing the teleportation formation leading to White Jade Capital Academy, destination of cultivators possessing the highest aptitude in astromancy and mathematics.

In contrast, though Renran flew with great speed, she could not catch up to Long Tianming no matter how hard she tried.

After all, Renran's flight constituted "cheating"; her method failed to satisfy the ascent criteria of the celestial staircase.

Fortunately, Goddess Renran was a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator. While such rules, penned by Zhou Hui himself, posed some hindrance to her, they could not completely halt her. Thus, she remained stubbornly locked onto the heading of her designated Child of Destiny, resisting the staircase's spatial spells from diverting her elsewhere.

Yao'er was displeased by this; its foundational programming harbored zero tolerance for such rule-breakers.

[Master, shall we stop her?]

—"Humor her a little,"-Zhou Hui chuckled.-"She has the right to choose whichever academy she desires to attend."

[...Does that mean this Goddess is slated to receive an education as well?!]

[Though in truth, she is indeed in dire need of education.]

Yao'er caught on in a flash, promptly executing a few minor adjustments to calibrate the curriculum for a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse.

Zhou Hui, meanwhile, strolled leisurely up the celestial staircase.

Naturally, the rules of the staircase did not bind its architect. Zhou Hui faced no barriers whichever academy he wished to visit. Thus, ascending along the staircase, he arrived upon the cloud platform receiving Long Tianming virtually neck-and-neck with Goddess Renran.

Above the sea of clouds stood towering ramparts and a monumental portal, resembling an imposing floating metropolis.

In reality, however, this was not the case; the massive gateway was merely the external form of a teleportation array. These cloud gates would transport the cream-of-the-crop youths to academies across various cities based on their differing performances, ensuring they studied within the pedagogical environment best suited to their growth.

The portal before them bore the inscription "Heavenly Gate." A prominent red banner reading "Welcome New Students" was draped across it, almost entirely obscuring the plaque, while beneath it, the plaza was completely empty without a soul in sight.

Under normal circumstances, reception personnel would be stationed here. However, the peak admissions rush had already concluded; the few remaining orientation cultivators were merely holding down the fort for latecomers. The moment they spotted Long Tianming ascending, they had jubilantly escorted the newcomer inside, never imagining anyone else followed behind him—to say nothing of the fact that Renran had ascended via irregular means and would never have triggered the official reception protocol.

Renran paid none of this any heed. Desperate to catch her designated Child of Destiny and equally desperate to evade Zhou Hui, seeing the open gate, then glancing back at the staircase behind her and Zhou Hui standing upon it, she dashed through the portal without a second thought.

The deafening cacophony of voices erupting beyond the portal nearly sent her stumbling head over heels!

To her utter astonishment, Renran found that behind the gate lay a bustling ferry port. Crowds surged to and fro, forming a vibrant marketplace, making it impossible to determine where Long Tianming had vanished in the throng.

Renran naturally refused to admit defeat. She possessed specialized divine tracking arts specifically designed to locate the "Child of Destiny"!

She swiftly cast her spell—and watched as the golden light points representing the Child of Destiny illuminated within her line of sight.

*Ah, found him! One... two... three......*

*???*

Goddess Renran paled in absolute horror. Whirling around, she confronted Zhou Hui, who had strolled in right behind her:

—"Untiring! What have you done?!"

—"?"-Zhou Hui offered a mild, innocent blink.

—"Why are there so many Children of Destiny?!"-she shrieked in utter disbelief.
