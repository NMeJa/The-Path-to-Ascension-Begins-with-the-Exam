# Chapter 212: Fox Borrowing the Tiger's Might

—"Power... is accumulating?"

On the other side, somewhere along the turbulent boundary between the Celestial Realm and Demon Domain, Qu Qianxing opened his eyes.

Even amidst the most perilous circumstances, he had never abandoned his daily cultivation regimen. And as the designated successor of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Qu Qianxing's soul was intrinsically linked to the mountain grand array; even separated by light-years, he maintained faint resonance. Consequently, just as his Master had hoped, Qu Qianxing detected the subtle shifts within the mountain grand array at the very first instant.

Yet Qu Qianxing's initial reaction was to furrow his handsome brows in acute anxiety:

—"Did something happen to the mountain grand array?"

After all, that grand array had been engineered by his Master; its energy inflow and outflow were calibrated with absolute mathematical precision, theoretically immune to violent fluctuations.

However, recent surges in the grand array's power had occurred more than once. Not long ago, there had been a massive energy drain, making Qu Qianxing severely suspect that a certain gluttonous black sheep had sneaked inside.

A sudden drop in energy could be rationalized that way, but an abrupt surge was far harder to explain.

According to Qu Qianxing's understanding of that black sheep, anything that entered its stomach was never coming back out. It would never use its private stash to replenish the sect's warehouses; even if Master strung it up from the mountain gates, it would stubbornly refuse! And once the hanging concluded, it considered the slate wiped clean, boldly sneaking back into the Heavenly Spirit Sect's storerooms to steal food again! Before laying eyes on that black sheep, Qu Qianxing had perpetually suspected it possessed rat lineage.

Therefore, this sudden surge in power was deeply bizarre.

—"Speaking of which, the Immortal Arts Assembly should have commenced by now, shouldn't it? Could a crisis have erupted, forcing Master to reinforce the grand array?!"

Qu Qianxing was accustomed to preparing for worst-case scenarios.

He began probing and diagnosing the grand array's status.

This was an excruciatingly arduous endeavor.

Particularly when operating 'abroad.'

Although Qu Qianxing's soul was tethered to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain grand array, the physical distance was simply too vast, separated by multiple dimensional planes. It was only because the synergy between the grand array engineered by Zhou Hui and the foundational cultivation techniques of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was profound and stable that the "signal" had not vanished entirely.

Yet with a "signal" of this nature, let alone performing a memory-intensive 'full system diagnostic,' merely attempting to access the 'surveillance feed' burned a massive portion of Qu Qianxing's spiritual energy.

Frankly, this was an exceptionally reckless gambit.

At this moment, Qu Qianxing was seated inside the primary command pavilion at the absolute epicenter of the demon encampment.

Though nominally hailed as the Supreme Commander, everyone understood the nature of demons: they operated strictly by the law of the jungle, treacherous and deceitful to the bone. They would never genuinely submit out of fealty to titles like Demon Sovereign or Supreme Commander, spending every waking second plotting to devour their peers to elevate their own cultivation.

That Qu Qianxing had maintained his position to this day was largely thanks to that original 'Second Demon Sovereign.' That entity appeared to have been a sufficiently ruthless and cold-blooded tyrant, such that most demons had heard of his terrifying prestige; laying eyes upon Qu Qianxing's inhumanly gorgeous face, none dared act out of turn.

However, this truce was purely temporary.

Qu Qianxing knew all too well that the instant he displayed the slightest hint of weakness, he would be torn to ribbons and devoured by the ravenous demons circling him. In truth, because he had refrained from taking the field in recent days, several demon warlords were already growing restless.

Yet compared to his personal safety, Qu Qianxing worried far more about his sect—and above all, his Master.

Steeling his resolve, Qu Qianxing channeled his full spiritual reserves to probe the status of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grand array.

To his utter astonishment, while establishing the connection drained a portion of his reserves, the moment the link solidified, a torrent of majestic power flowed backward into Qu Qianxing's dantian, instantly replenishing his depleted reserves to the brim!

—"How could this be..."

Qu Qianxing scrambled to sever the link.

He would never sneak food like a certain black sheep.

Even though everyone knew he was the next Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect; even though his soul was tethered to the grand array, granting him unconditional authorization to mobilize the array and the sect's assets at will; even though he managed all the sect's logistical resources—Qu Qianxing had always regarded every blade of grass in the sect as Master's property. He merely managed them as a steward, and would never appropriate public resources for private benefit.

Yet before he could finish severing the link, Qu Qianxing snapped back to reality:

Even with an operational blunder, the energy of the mountain grand array could never backflow this easily—let alone across multiple worlds. Even if backflow occurred, it could never travel at such astronomical speed. It could only mean someone had deliberately orchestrated this conduit.

Moreover, the power cascading into him was no ordinary spiritual energy, but a vastly more complex hybrid infused with temporal and spatial mechanics, feeling distinctly like an overdraft from the future—much like how Qu Qianxing had inscribed advanced calculus problems as defensive barrier wards for the mortal villages under the sect's protection. He hadn't expected the villagers to solve them on the spot; he was loaning from future time.

Yet executing such a feat across the void outside the mountain grand array was far beyond Qu Qianxing's current comprehension.

—"Master,"-Qu Qianxing murmured with absolute certainty.

Lu Shulong and Jian Nuo must have successfully returned to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Qu Qianxing had previously harbored mild anxiety that his Fifth Junior Brother might hide the truth out of fear of Master's wrath, merely stealing the Heavenly Robe before scampering back. It seemed he had overthought matters.

*(Lu Shulong, back at the sect: sweating profusely with a guilty conscience...)*

Qu Qianxing breathed a massive sigh of relief.

Not because of the immense energy channeled from the grand array, but purely due to the fact that "his entrapment within the demon horde was now known to Master."

Though his serene exterior betrayed nothing, Qu Qianxing had been shouldering crushing psychological pressure, teetering on the edge of his endurance.

Yet having a mountain to lean on changed everything!

Even if he died here, he had no fear of the fallout engulfing the Heavenly Spirit Sect, for Master would unquestionably clean up every loose thread!

*Finally, I can act without holding anything back!*

Qu Qianxing thought with buoyant delight, the corners of his lips curving into a dazzling, breathtaking smile.

It was fortunate there was no other living soul inside his command pavilion; otherwise, any entity possessing standard aesthetic perception would have had their flesh and bones melted into a puddle of spring water by that radiant smile.

Wearing this ethereal smile, Qu Qianxing quietly savored the energy flowing from the mountain grand array into his dantian.

To Qu Qianxing, this felt akin to resting in his Master's embrace.

He felt reluctant to let go. Yet in the very next heartbeat, having grasped the situation, he decisively severed the spiritual feed without hesitation.

—"It is too far away. Channeling energy across such vast spatial expanses is far too wasteful,"-Qu Qianxing reasoned softly, as though his Master stood right before him,-"Master, that you lent me the aura of the mountain grand array so I could [borrow the tiger's might like a fox](# "Literal CN: 狐假虎威 (hújiǎ-hǔwēi) — the fox borrowing the tiger's terror; intimidating others with borrowed authority") is already more than enough."

Just as Qu Qianxing was murmuring to himself, an aggressive commotion erupted outside the pavilion.

Qu Qianxing instantly wiped the smile from his face, turning his gaze toward the entrance, where heated bickering filtered through the heavy flaps:

—"...The Demon Sovereign is currently meditating."

—"When is he ever *not* meditating?!"

—"We carry urgent military intelligence to report! If a disaster occurs due to delay, can you shoulder the blame?!"

—"However..."

Qu Qianxing let out a cold snort, declaring in a resonant voice:

—"What is the ruckus? Let them in."

—"Understood..."

The guard's voice sounded hesitant, as though wanting to add something.

Yet before it could finish, arrogant laughter boomed outside. In the next breath, the pavilion curtain was violently ripped aside as several demon warlords stomped insolently inside.

They were a cabal of obnoxious, swaggering demons.

Inherently grotesque and misshapen, they sprouted numerous aberrant organs that would give an adult human recurring nightmares for weeks. To flaunt their might, they unleashed their suffocating demonic miasma without restraint, resembling roving conduits of pollution.

Facing these monstrosities daily, it was entirely natural that Qu Qianxing's psychological stress had skyrocketed.

Fortunately, they failed to draw anywhere near Qu Qianxing this time.

The very instant these demons tore the curtain aside, a crushing, apocalyptic aura crashed down upon them like a collapsing mountain range! Before they could even wipe the insolent smirks from their hideous faces, they were slammed flat against the floor!

One of the weaker demons couldn't even maintain its physical form, liquefying into a quivering puddle of black sludge with a sickening squelch.

—"D-Demon Sovereign... Y-Your Excellency... what is the meaning of..."

The one barely managing to squeeze words through its throat was the guard who had attempted to halt them. Standing outside the threshold, although it had also been flattened by the aura, it wasn't as close to the epicenter, allowing it to croak hoarsely from its throat.

And it wasn't just them: every demon within a ten-mile radius felt the crushing weight. The perpetually deafening, chaotic demon encampment abruptly descended into dead silence, rippling outward from the command tent!

Qu Qianxing was somewhat startled by the sheer magnitude of the effect.

He had been reluctant to siphon raw energy from the grand array, merely borrowing the grand array's aura to intimidate these unruly demons; never had he anticipated the outcome would be this staggering.

Had Qu Qianxing known that Yuwen Xuan had recently used the mountain grand array to hunt a Heavenly General, his astonishment would have multiplied tenfold. For that alone proved the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grand array surpassed the tier of Heavenly Generals—not to mention that was before the assembly commenced. Now that the trial system had launched, bolstered by the computational 'external aid' of the contestants, the grand array's output had surged by an entire magnitude.

If Qu Qianxing stood within the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grand array right now, he would legitimately command the firepower to upend the heavens.

Regrettably, Qu Qianxing was not at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Even if he were, he would never utilize the grand array for such 'meaningless' theatrics.

At this moment, riding the apocalyptic pressure radiated by the grand array, Qu Qianxing let out a frosty sneer:

—"Did this seat not state I was meditating? My circulation has not yet concluded; endure it!"

—"I-It is this humble one's supreme honor!"-the guard shrieked, barely holding back tears.

It felt on the verge of shattering to pieces, wanting nothing more than to flee this horrific pressure this very second.

Yet it dared not antagonize a Demon Sovereign commanding such godlike might, merely glaring with venomous hatred toward the foolish demons who had dared trespass:

*It's all their fault!*

*Had I known His Excellency's cultivation was this terrifying, I would have fought to the death to stop these idiots!*

As for the offending demons, they were weeping tears of genuine, agonizing regret.

They had only heard legends of the 'Second Demon Sovereign's prestige,' never having witnessed him draw a blade. Observing Qu Qianxing's seemingly modest spiritual reserves, they had harbored skepticism toward the rumors. They had never suspected Qu Qianxing was a fraud—for that peerless, ethereal face was something even demons specializing in shapeshifting could never emulate—they had merely burned to provoke him, testing the 'Demon Sovereign's' true caliber.

Yet even with such insolence, demons naturally feared the Second Demon Sovereign.

Thus, they had tracked the 'Second Demon Sovereign's' daily meditation schedule, calculating that he might expose vulnerabilities while cycling his energy. Seizing this window under the pretext of delivering military intelligence, they had barged in—only to be struck head-on by the 'unleashed aura of the Demon Sovereign's cultivation,' nearly crushed into mincemeat.

Consequently, their hearts bled with bitter remorse:

*Lord Demon Sovereign, if you commanded this level of power, you should have said so earlier!*

*We would never have dared offend you!*

Alas, regret came far too late. Pinned beneath this terrifying aura, they could only struggle in agony until several other demon commanders stationed at the frontlines arrived.

This war was a titanic conflict governing the sovereign dominion over multiple mortal worlds; consequently, the Demon Domain had mobilized numerous warlords.

Currently stationed at the forward encampment were the Supreme Commander Second Demon Sovereign, followed by the Third Demon General, Seventh Demon General, Ninth Demon General, and Thirteenth Demon General—totaling one Demon Sovereign and four Demon Generals. The reason there were no other Demon Sovereigns was reportedly because the Second Demon Sovereign was too formidable; the sovereigns beneath him dared not style themselves Demon Sovereigns, demoting their titles to Demon Generals. Rumor had it that the Demon Emperor might personally grace the battlefield alongside other high-ranking commanders in the near future.

This was another catalyst that had caused Qu Qianxing's stress to soar.

While Qu Qianxing was genuinely curious to witness what the Demon Emperor and First Demon Sovereign looked like, he dared not gamble with his life in such an environment. That was why he had dispatched Lu Shulong to retrieve the Heavenly Robe, planning his escape.

Alas, following Lu Shulong and Jian Nuo's departure, battlefield developments had left Qu Qianxing zero room to slack off.

The simultaneous arrival of the four Demon Generals proved this beyond doubt.

—Through Qu Qianxing's observations, while the Second Demon Sovereign theoretically held supreme authority, the generals harbored fierce mutual disdain, rarely visiting his pavilion unless an emergency arose.

Gazing upon these four Demon Generals, Qu Qianxing asked with playful mockery:

—"Why have you all assembled? Could there truly be some critical intelligence?"

Qu Qianxing's tone remained icy and composed. Having lingered in the demon camp for so long, whether his acting chops had improved or he had truly assimilated into the persona, he blended into the environment with seamless grace.

The Demon Generals naturally suspected nothing. Dropping to their knees in reverent terror, they saluted:

—"Greetings, Lord Supreme Commander!"

—"These insolent wretches have soiled Your Excellency's eyes; it is our dereliction of duty!"-a general barked.

Directing a furious glare toward the quivering demons flattened upon the floor, before the offenders could even beg for mercy, they detonated into sprays of bloody foam and shredded organs!

Witnessing this, Qu Qianxing's brows twitched faintly.

He had stayed here for weeks, witnessing similar carnage virtually every day, yet found himself fundamentally incapable of growing numb to it.

A demon noticed Qu Qianxing's subtle reaction, though it evidently misread the context:

—"Did I not command you never to soil Lord Supreme Commander's carpets?"-the demon snarled, instantly incinerating the gore and organs into ash with demonic fire.

While still a gruesome spectacle, the room was at least clean again.

The Demon General offered a fawning smile:

—"However, regarding the war, we dare not deceive you. Precisely as Your Excellency predicted, the Heavenly Court has deployed reinforcements! They intend to bypass our strongholds through spatial rifts, attempting a pincer strike against our flank."

—"Hmph. Anyone with eyes could have deduced that,"-Qu Qianxing replied dismissively, though inwardly he breathed a sigh of relief.

He hadn't survived to this day relying solely on his face. Even ordinary demons doubted him due to his lack of dense demonic miasma, let alone these cunning generals.

Thus, Qu Qianxing had occasionally displayed his 'prophetic' foresight, analyzing the movements of the celestial host. Because his predictions hit the mark time and again, these generals, despite their private doubts, had never dared make a move against him.

—Naturally, the lingering dread of the authentic 'Second Demon Sovereign' was immense, and predictive strategic analysis appeared to be that entity's specialty. Even if the generals suspected their commander's cultivation had suffered an anomaly, they had only dared send disposable scouts to harass him.

At this moment, facing the crushing aura radiating from Qu Qianxing, the last lingering shred of doubt in their hearts evaporated completely.

Seeing he had achieved his desired outcome, Qu Qianxing refrained from suffocating them further, withdrawing the grand array's aura.

The demons felt the crushing weight lift from their shoulders, overwhelmed by the euphoric relief of cheating death.

Even the generals, who nominally stood near Qu Qianxing's rank, felt their knees tremble.

Gathering a scrap of courage, a general gingerly flattered:

—"Lord Supreme Commander, your divine might has progressed astonishingly!"

—"It is nothing. I have not fully mastered it yet; consequently, it tends to strike friend and foe indiscriminately,"-Qu Qianxing casually leaked a scrap of 'insider lore,'-"In entering the field this time, I was merely seeking an opportunity to test it."

—"I-I see! No wonder Your Excellency was willing to grace the frontlines in person!"

The Demon Generals offered fawning smiles, but collectively took a synchronized step backward.

They couldn't begin to fathom how horrific it would be if that crushing pressure detonated as an offensive blast upon the battlefield. Particularly that "indiscriminate" clause—it felt entirely capable of cleaving them in two alongside the enemy.

The crux was that given the Second Demon Sovereign's temperament, he would never give a damn about collateral damage among his own troops.

Thus, the demons who had spent days plotting to lure Qu Qianxing into taking the field to test his strength underwent an instantaneous 180-degree reversal. None of them wished to face that nightmarish pressure again!

They stammered frantically:

—"I-It hasn't reached the point where Your Excellency needs to intervene!"

—"Indeed! We haven't even deployed; how could mere cannon fodder merit Lord Supreme Commander's personal blade?"

—"Handling those Heavenly Court trash is more than enough for us!"

Qu Qianxing listened to their frantic flattery with icy detachment, before asking with a half-smile:

—"You sound exceptionally confident in triumphing over the Heavenly Court."

—"Naturally!"-the generals declared in unison.

—"Have you devised a strategy to counter the Five Heavenly Generals dispatched by the Heavenly Court?"-Qu Qianxing pressed,-"I recall they beat you into wailing submission not long ago, did they not?"

A general stammered:

—"That was a momentary blunder! Next time... next time we shall surely prevail!"

Qu Qianxing asked again:

—"Then have you calculated how to repel the multi-fold heavenly reinforcements flanking our rear?"

A general swallowed:

—"W-We will find a way!"

—"Indeed! Didn't Lord Supreme Commander previously mention a tactical battle array to counter them?"

Qu Qianxing continued:

—"Then have you drilled that battle array? Or rather... did you even understand the schematics?"

The generals wore pained, constipated expressions:

—"..."

Qu Qianxing delivered the final strike:

—"You must possess absolute confidence that before His Majesty the Demon Emperor arrives, you will secure at least one or two glorious victories, correct?"

The Demon Generals dropped to their knees once more, howling in terror:

—"Lord Supreme Commander, save us!"

Qu Qianxing let out a cold smirk.

In his frank assessment, the demon troops and generals here were genuinely an unruly rabble of headless chickens.

Lacking brains and lacking discipline, they had zero concept of operational strategy and zero defensive doctrine. Stepping onto the battlefield, they engaged in chaotic slashing; if they won, they stopped to gorge themselves on corpses, causing formations to collapse; if they lost, they routed instantly, causing formations to collapse again.

That they had held the line for so many years was entirely thanks to the celestial server's exceptional matchmaking system:

—The heavenly host was hardly any better. Nominally styled heavenly soldiers and celestial generals, the lower ranks were mostly uncontracted ascenders from minor worlds. They fought merely in hopes of securing an official celestial post, yet possessed zero avenues for promotion, harboring endless resentment. Meanwhile, the Heavenly Generals were crony 'insiders' dispatched by the Heavenly Court, looking down upon mortal ascenders while ruling through paranoid, abusive control, resulting in every battle turning into an even 50/50 brawl against trash-tier demon hordes.

Qu Qianxing felt these fools were inferior even to his sect's rat... no, black sheep spirit.

Naturally, from Qu Qianxing's original standpoint, he held zero desire for the demon race to win.

While his encounters with heavenly soldiers revealed catastrophic systemic rot within the Heavenly Court, the Heavenly Court was ultimately the Heavenly Court. On a fundamental level, they maintained basic cosmic order across the three thousand worlds, qualifying as an entity too big to fail.

Conversely, chaotic, evil demons were a plague wherever they roamed. Even if one wished to overhaul the Heavenly Court, relying on such depraved monsters was out of the question.

Until Qu Qianxing deduced the Heavenly Court's broader strategic blueprint.

Qu Qianxing had noticed that the frontline between the Heavenly Court and Demon Domain was stretched unimaginably thin. (This puzzled Qu Qianxing greatly: why did the Demon Domain share so many borders with the celestial realm? Ascending from minor worlds was nearly impossible, yet the Demon Domain sat directly adjacent to heaven). Along such an expansive frontline, vulnerabilities were inevitable. Whenever demons breached the perimeter, the Heavenly Court's standard protocol was to sever the infected sector and divert the demonic flood elsewhere.

And judging by spatial coordinates and frontline trajectories, no matter how Qu Qianxing modeled it, his native world was the next scheduled sacrifice.

Thus, the so-called 'prophecy' was highly likely no natural prophecy at all, but a premeditated, deliberate slaughter.

Having uncovered this truth, Qu Qianxing naturally refused to tolerate it, even conceiving the ambition to lead the demon armies to storm the Heavenly Court.

It was merely that previously, his cultivation had been insufficient to command the demon armies. A premature move would invite his own death at best; directing demonic attention toward his home world ahead of schedule was the true catastrophe, prompting Qu Qianxing to suppress his ambitions.

Yet now, with Master providing backing, Qu Qianxing felt he could do whatever the hell he pleased!

Armed with this resolve, Qu Qianxing determined that before the Demon Emperor arrived, he had to secure an unprecedented, sweeping triumph—not to curry favor with the Demon Emperor or evade punishment, but to cement his absolute authority as 'Supreme Commander,' seizing true operational control over the military and whipping this disorganized rabble into shape, securing more bargaining chips for himself.

From this perspective, auditing the assets under his command was of paramount importance.

Through weeks of observation, Qu Qianxing had noted that demonic species and origins varied wildly, resulting in vastly disparate combat capabilities and ecologies. Certain demonic lineages intrigued Qu Qianxing immensely; these were mysteries he needed to unravel.

Thinking of this, Qu Qianxing gazed down upon the groveling generals:

—"As Supreme Commander, it is my solemn duty to assume responsibility for your survival and guide you to victory."

—"Although due to my cultivation, I will refrain from drawing my blade until a suitable battlefield is chosen—" (the generals exhaled in collective relief) "—we require a glorious victory to welcome His Majesty's arrival. To that end, I must select the strongest, most formidable, most cunning, and most loyal warriors to serve as my adjutants." (The generals' eyes lit up with blazing anticipation.)

—"Therefore... let us commence with a Diagnostic Assessment Examination!"

Saying this, Qu Qianxing tapped the command desk with his slender fingers.

The Demon Generals shivered in visceral, inexplicable terror. They had zero inkling of what a "Diagnostic Assessment Examination" was, yet an instinctual dread gripped their souls—why did that phrase sound infinitely more evil and horrifying than the apocalyptic aura the Second Demon Sovereign had just displayed?!
