# Chapter 204: *Hiccup* (第204章 嗝)

—"I can't find it! How can it not be anywhere?!"-Ren Ran roared in fury.

While Lin Qi was diligently playing his dungeon-crawling puzzle game (self-preparation culinary course), Ren Ran was ransacking her private quarters aboard the grand treasure ship into complete disarray. A crushing Tribulation Transcendence spiritual pressure radiated from her, flattening her maidservants virtually prostrate against the floorboards.

Goddess Ren Ran had returned to the treasure ship immediately following the qualification examination.

—Naturally, Ren Ran would never admit she had fled.

By her own rationale: although Guan Xinguang had arrived in her company, he was an official of the Heavenly Dao Alliance. Whatever he did, what did it have to do with her, Goddess Ren Ran?!

Regarding the 'Dao Bone,' no matter how foolish Ren Ran might be, she would never confess to it.

Yet as an exalted Goddess, being interrogated in public was intensely humiliating in itself. Furthermore, with Untiring and Ruwang looking primed to settle old scores, Ren Ran naturally possessed neither the stomach nor the courage to linger inside Buyan Palace.

Thus, the moment the examination concluded—while the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Ruwang Ghost Emperor were absorbed in Guan Ye's condition and other cultivators surged forward to offer congratulations amidst the chaotic hubbub—she had slipped away like greased lightning, scurrying straight back to the treasure ship.

In truth, Ren Ran had wanted nothing more than to depart the Heavenly Spirit Sect altogether. The dilemma was that alongside Guan Xinguang's demise, Lin Qi had vanished into thin air.

Lin Qi was, after all, an exalted 'Heavenly General.' Whether he had been misplaced or met with foul play, it spelled catastrophic trouble.

If Ren Ran wished to take any future action—even if merely petitioning the Heavenly Court for reinforcements—she at least needed to account for Lin Qi's whereabouts. It was by no means a matter that could be brushed off with a breezy declaration of 'missing in action.'

It was for this very reason that Ren Ran had been forced to bite the bullet and return to the treasure ship.

She had left several trunks of luggage aboard, among which was a specific Dharma artifact capable of locating Lin Qi's aura.

Yet Ren Ran could find nothing.

The artifact was nowhere near its remembered location; even after tearing the entire chamber apart, not a trace could be found. Compounded by her frantic anxiety and the ceaseless dread that Untiring would come knocking to demand answers at any moment, she vented her boiling fury upon her personal maidservants.

—"Where is my array disc?!"-Ren Ran glared at them with venomous fury,-"Which of you stole it?!"

—"Your... your humble servants... would never dare!"-the maids stammered in agony under the crushing aura.

Many of the Goddess's personal items were specially enchanted; without a certain tier of cultivation base, one couldn't even use them, let alone touch them. The maidservants were only permitted to handle certain implements during routine service under special dispensations. With their meager cultivation, touching such items normally was impossible—how could they have stolen one?

Furthermore, Ren Ran's array disc had been stashed in the deepest hidden compartment of her dressing vanity. Not to mention the layers upon layers of restrictive wards woven over it, Ren Ran rarely ever took it out. Her closest personal maids had never even known such an array disc existed—how could they possibly steal something they were utterly oblivious to?!

Naturally, such logical arguments could never be voiced to the unreasonable Goddess.

While Ren Ran was nothing special in the eyes of monsters like Untiring, her might remained terrifyingly lethal to these maidservants.

Yet remaining silent was equally fatal; lingering under Ren Ran's spiritual pressure for another moment meant permanent maiming, if not instant death.

—"Your humble servant... your humble servant once saw..."-a maidservant struggled to pass the buck,-"that previous guest emerging from your quarters."

—"Guest?"-Ren Ran froze momentarily,-"Guan Xinguang?"

During these recent days, Ren Ran had kept her doors firmly shut to visitors. The only person who qualified as a guest and had been granted audience was Guan Xinguang—possessed by Lin Qi.

Mentioning Guan Xinguang only stoked her rage further:

—"Why didn't you speak up back then?! Besides, what skill did that useless trash possess to steal an array disc through my restrictive wards?!"-Ren Ran snapped.

[If an esteemed Mahayana cultivator lacked the skill, would we lowly servants have it?](# "Maidservant's unspoken thought")

Swallowing her grievance, the maidservant whispered tremulously:

—"At the time, I overheard voices conversing inside. It sounded like two men. But when the door opened, he walked out alone, so your servant assumed her ears were playing tricks, mistaking your voice..."

—"What sort of defective ears could mistake my voice for a man's?!"-Ren Ran flared with even greater fury. Fortunately, she hadn't completely parted with her wits; pausing a beat, she stiffened,-"Wait a moment... two men?"

Could it be... Lin Qi?

Guan Xinguang had been alone; the only entity capable of conversing with him was Lin Qi.

Moreover, compared to the incompetent Guan Xinguang or these useless maidservants, only Heavenly General Lin Qi possessed the ability to filch her belongings undetected.

The question was: what did the exalted Heavenly General steal... take that thing for?!

Could he possibly believe that array disc held some extraordinary, omnipotent function? Ah, that was genuinely possible; when celestial soldiers and heavenly generals campaigned, military strategists often wielded comparable divination discs.

But her array disc was nothing of the sort! That was a Heavenly Court 'instrument of penal discipline'!

Ren Ran bit her lip in mounting panic.

The "legends" surrounding Goddess Ren Ran were largely grounded in truth.

As a second-generation immortal scion, she was the offspring of a descending True Immortal and a mortal woman in a minor world. However, her father had become deeply enamored with the mortal maiden, refusing to return to the Heavenly Court. Consequently, while she was still a tender child, her father had suffered celestial retribution from the Heavenly Court.

Ren Ran, however, was permitted to enter the Heavenly Court due to her extraordinarily rare spell aptitude. Individuals like her constituted the core backbone of second-generation immortal scions; after all, ascending to the Heavenly Court was notoriously arduous. The Heavens did not grant exceptions merely because one parent was a True Immortal. Unless one possessed exceptional talent like Ren Ran or bore other singular traits, [ascending to the heavens alongside the dogs and chickens](# "Literal CN: 鸡犬升天 (jīquǎn-shēngtiān) — even poultry ascends when a master achieves the Dao; riding coattails") was out of the question—they couldn't even set foot into the Immortal-Demon Realm.

Raised in the Heavenly Court, Ren Ran took immense pride in being an official member of the celestial order and revelled in her extraordinary gifts, viewing herself as fundamentally superior to mortal beings. Consequently, she could never fathom why her father had willingly accepted celestial punishment just to linger in the mortal realm for a mortal woman. Yet her parents had long since aged and died in the lower world (as recorded by the Heavenly Court), leaving Ren Ran without answers.

It was not until ten thousand years ago, during the previous God-Demon War, that Ren Ran—driven by curiosity regarding this very enigma—had descended to this world as part of the celestial army's logistical corps to battle the demon race.

That had been her very first time setting foot in a minor world. Perhaps because this world had once been an integral part of the Heavenly Court, it featured grand floating celestial cities like White Jade Capital and colossal teleportation arrays providing peerless convenience, appearing every bit as magnificent as the Heavenly Court.

All of this had momentarily enchanted Ren Ran. She had thought: if this was what the mortal world was like, perhaps her father's choice wasn't entirely incomprehensible. Swayed by such musings during the decisive campaign, the Heavenly Court had commanded that White Jade Capital be deployed as the main battlefield. Yet White Jade Capital was still teeming with living mortals at the time; unable to bear watching them perish, Ren Ran had defied the celestial edict, forcibly evacuating the mortal populace and using her own body to seal the Demon Sovereign. In the process, her spells had spiraled out of control, causing White Jade Capital to vanish into spatial oblivion—until Untiring somehow fished it out of some forgotten cosmic gutter millennia later, falsely claiming he had discovered it alongside the Ascension Stairway.

As a result of overexerting her power, Ren Ran had been gravely crippled. Not only was she branded a disgraced convict, but she was permanently barred from returning to the Heavenly Court.

Initially, Ren Ran hadn't cared much. She believed she had stayed true to her heart by refusing to carry out the Heavenly Court's ruthless slaughter; accepting punishment and lingering in this minor world for ten thousand years to atone seemed entirely reasonable.

Yet after dwelling in this world for so long, she had begun to feel that she had truly been [blinded by pig lard](# "Literal CN: 猪油蒙了心 (zhūyóu méngle xīn) — heart blinded by pig fat; completely addled and foolish") back then!

First of all, after severing ties with the Heavenly Court, this world had not embraced glorious prosperity as the ancient heroes had proclaimed; on the contrary, it had spiraled into swift, catastrophic decline. Whether magnificent floating cities like White Jade Capital or large-scale inter-domain teleportation arrays, none could ever be built again. On this point, Ren Ran conceded that the complete eradication of that wartime generation of high-tier cultivators played a role; yet if this world possessed genuine capability, shouldn't they have reinvented them from scratch? Ultimately, they simply couldn't survive without the Heavenly Court! The lower realm was just the lower realm!

On the other hand, the attitude of mortal beings had also soured over time.

In the beginning, they had been so effusively grateful, revering her as a savior. Yet with the inexorable passage of time, their reverence had decayed into disrespect. Mortal lifespans were so tragically brief; once the old generation died off, their descendants began doubting whether she had ever accomplished anything at all, or merely sought to squeeze favors out of her, such as secrets of celestial ascension.

Most intolerable of all was that Untiring! Back when he had come to visit her, he had asked several questions that sounded profoundly arcane. In truth, Ren Ran believed not understanding them was irrelevant—they were merely technical pedantry regarding ascension. So she had answered them offhandedly. Although she couldn't recall her exact responses, the look Untiring had cast upon her afterward was overflowing with incredulity and profound pity, as if gazing upon a bumbling fool holding a golden begging bowl while begging for scraps.

Even Ren Ran could read social cues!

She had felt thoroughly insulted!

Thus, Ren Ran was consumed by bottomless regret. She often wondered: had her father, who willingly abandoned the celestial realm for a mortal woman, regretted his fateful choice just as bitterly?

Yet all regret came far too late. Ren Ran could not return to the Heavenly Court; not only was return impossible, but her every action in the lower world was subjected to rigorous surveillance and assessment. That very 'array disc' was the mechanism of her confinement.

The array disc 'Thousand Eyes'—a standardized disciplinary implement forged by the Bureau of Heavenly Dao specifically to monitor and command exiled celestial sinners.

Although the disc possessed basic utilitarian functions to assist Ren Ran in executing tasks, every single headache-inducing directive assigned by the Heavenly Court was broadcast through it. Worse, it continuously monitored her actions and could administer punitive electro-spiritual shocks at will.

Ren Ran had developed a deep psychological trauma toward that cursed disc.

She dreaded hearing the disc emit that sharp *ding* sound, feeling as though every *ding* was chipping away at her very soul.

What Ren Ran detested most was that despite recording and transmitting everything, the disc was strictly a one-way communication tool. Ren Ran could not use it to reach out to the Heavenly Court, nor would it ever lift a finger to aid her in mortal peril; she suspected that even if she perished in the mortal realm, the disc would merely look the other way.

Consequently, despite strict orders to keep it on her person, Ren Ran had always refused to store the disc in her interspatial pouch, deliberately sealing it inside her vanity cabinet. Even knowing this could never truly evade surveillance, [burying her head in the sand](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — plugging one's ears while stealing a bell; self-deception") made her feel slightly less watched.

Had Lin Qi not suddenly 'vanished,' Ren Ran would have never dreamed of retrieving it.

Yet now, it was gone.

The most probable culprit was the very reinforcement she had summoned from above.

—"In truth, Lord Lin Qi using it wouldn't be catastrophic; we are both Heavenly Court personnel, so even if there's a minor regulatory breach, the punishment wouldn't be severe. But right now, both Guan Xinguang and the Heavenly General are missing..."-Ren Ran reasoned in mounting panic.

(Guan Xinguang had indeed been incinerated and refined, yet that scene had unfolded at blinding speed amidst chaotic spiritual crosswinds. Furthermore, Guan Ye was a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator; claiming a Foundation Establishment junior had incinerated and refined a Mahayana titan was far too preposterous. Consequently, aside from supreme powerhouses like Untiring and Ruwang who perceived every microscopic detail from start to finish, the vast majority of onlookers—including Ren Ran—believed that only Guan Xinguang's physical shell had been destroyed, while his soul had either fled or vanished.)

—"The Heavenly General wouldn't have let the array disc fall into Untiring's hands, would he?!"-Ren Ran gasped, shuddering violently.

An artifact belonging to the Heavenly Court—especially an implement belonging to the Bureau of Heavenly Dao that governed the cosmic laws—falling into the hands of a lower-realm mortal was an absolute taboo among taboos!

At this moment, Ren Ran had zero doubt that the disc had fallen into Untiring's grasp following Lin Qi's disappearance.

Although her lips perpetually derided Untiring as a 'base mortal,' Ren Ran harbored deep, visceral dread toward a "mortal" who had single-handedly repaired the Ascension Stairway, excavated White Jade Capital, attained true godhood, and operated entirely outside the Heavenly Court's calculated trajectory. It was precisely because she feared him that upon discovering the disappearance of Carefree Palace's master, she hadn't dared confront Untiring directly, instead navigating countless backchannels to summon the strongest combat powerhouse she could enlist.

And now, this 'strongest powerhouse' had vanished on Untiring's home turf alongside her disciplinary array disc!

—"No! The array disc records everything! If the Heavenly General meets with foul play, the Heavenly Court will know without fail... I cannot sit here waiting for death!"-Ren Ran stammered frantically.

Worse yet, long before the Heavenly Court's wrath arrived, Untiring's retribution would strike first. If Lin Qi was in his clutches, Untiring undoubtedly knew everything. Without the array disc, Ren Ran possessed zero leverage to resist.

Thinking of this, Ren Ran raised her eyes, gazing upon the maidservants kneeling and trembling before her in an irritable blur.

These maidservants were mortals she had plucked from this realm.

They clearly possessed celestial seeds (latent cultivation aptitude), yet had lived lives worse than beasts. Ren Ran felt she had 'saved' them and ushered them onto the immortal path; they ought to be brimming with eternal gratitude, yet their faces were etched solely with visceral terror.

—"Hmph, maintain formation. We are leaving this place immediately,"-Ren Ran ordered curtly.

—"Eh?"-the maids gasped in surprise.

A quick-witted maid spoke up:

—"I shall pack the luggage!"

—"Forget the luggage! Dragging you lot along is burdensome enough!"-Ren Ran snapped impatiently.

The maidservants exchanged bewildered glances. Their primary utility to the Goddess had always been carrying her opulent luggage and maintaining her magnificent parade; now the Goddess was abruptly abandoning her baggage, leaving them thoroughly at a loss. Besides, what did she mean by 'burdensome'? They didn't require the Goddess to carry them on her back, did they?

Ren Ran had no intention of elaborating, merely letting out a bitter sigh:

[Ah, how I wish I could leave them behind as decoys to pretend I'm still aboard, buying time against Untiring! But these useless wretches would probably spill unnecessary intelligence to Untiring the second they're caught... better drag them along!](# "Ren Ran's mental monologue")

A few seconds later, Ren Ran and her maidservants vanished without a whisper from the grand treasure ship. No one witnessed how they departed; even Yuwen Xuan dangling from the bough at the mountain gate caught not the slightest glimpse.

***

Ren Ran presumed that Lin Qi had been captured by the Heavenly Spirit Sect and that her array disc had fallen into Untiring's hands.

In a bizarre, roundabout fashion, she was actually speaking the absolute truth.

Lin Qi, however, remained utterly oblivious to his true predicament, completely unaware that Ren Ran had fled the scene. At this very moment, he was gingerly pushing open the front doors of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's second direct disciple's private residence.

—"This must be the final hurdle!"-Lin Qi mused with immense gratification.

Looking back on his journey, Lin Qi felt as though he had just [passed five passes and slain six generals](# "Literal CN: 过五关斩六将 (guò wǔ guān zhǎn liù jiàng) — Romance of the Three Kingdoms: Guan Yu overcoming all obstacles").

Every single trial had been fraught with mortal peril!

Particularly the Formation Pavilion of the third disciple, Ran Deng. Located right inside Buyan Palace where the final array eye resided, setting foot inside risked triggering the ultimate mechanisms to begin with. Furthermore, the interior of that Formation Pavilion resembled an entire miniature pocket realm; Lin Qi had nearly lost himself within the expanse. After finally destroying the pavilion's array eye, he had alerted Ran Deng's lingering phantasm, nearly getting refined into a go chess stone. Only by bolting headlong into the eldest disciple's quarters had he narrowly escaped disaster!

—"That young maiden looked so remarkably well-behaved sitting at the commentary desk! And that fourth disciple Guan Ye—he looks like a completely normal fellow, yet in the mountain grand array he manifested as a colossal fire giant! Does that make any sense?!"-Lin Qi complained bitterly,-"Fortunately, this is the very last one... No, I cannot afford to drop my guard."

Muttering thus, he patted his own cheeks.

Yes, his cheeks.

Lin Qi was no longer a feeble ball of golden light; he was now a humanoid silhouette radiating dazzling golden luster.

He had regained substantial power!

Having been marinated and soaked (Water Mirror Pavilion), carved and sliced (Sword Mountain), washed and rinsed (Pill Concoction Chamber), roasted and seared (Artifact Refinement Workshop), steamed and boiled (Formation Pavilion), and aged in cold storage (Rare Beast Enclosure), he had shattered six array eyes of the mountain-protecting grand array.

With each broken array eye, Lin Qi (convinced himself he) could siphon a portion of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual energy. By now, he was finally capable of condensing a humanoid body, possessing genuine combat efficacy once more.

This was precisely why Lin Qi had managed to endure such harrowing trials and persevere until now!

—"Now, as long as I retrieve an item belonging to Qu Qianxing from here, I can safely infiltrate the Academic Hall atop Buyan Palace, activate the final array eye, and seize absolute control over the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-protecting grand array,"-Lin Qi whispered to cheer himself on, cautiously surveying Qu Qianxing's living quarters.

This place also warranted his utmost vigilance.

Qu Qianxing resided closest to Buyan Palace and the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Lin Qi regarded this as ironclad proof that Qu Qianxing was the master's favored pet—though Qu Qianxing merely lived here for logistical efficiency. Yet as the (in Lin Qi's mind) most pampered direct disciple, Qu Qianxing's residence was shockingly spartan: books, books, astrolabes, books, star charts, books, and endless mountains of unsorted scrolls! For a moment, one couldn't tell if this was a residence or an unorganized library archive.

Looking at the towering stacks gave Lin Qi an instant migraine, let alone searching for an item among them.

—"Thank heavens for this array disc,"-Lin Qi sighed in relief.

Throughout this entire trek, his ability to unravel every riddle was largely credited to the array disc gifted by Ren Ran.

With Ren Ran's modest intellect, it was an unprecedented miracle that she could make such an enlightened decision.

Caressing the array disc, Lin Qi channeled energy. Instantly, the disc illuminated the precise location of the targeted artifact. Not only that, it dispatched a guiding star ahead, tracing a winding, tortuous fifty-meter path that required half a shichen to navigate through the cluttered chamber.

Lin Qi showed zero arrogance; he could distinctly sense that this bedroom was densely laden with lethal wards and magical traps. Following the array disc's guidance was undeniably the safest path.

And the result proved it.

Though it consumed time, Lin Qi arrived unharmed at the designated coordinate, discovering an inconspicuous wardrobe chest beneath a towering mound of books.

Inside lay a celestial garment, dazzling and resplendent—unquestionably an attire tailored for a peerless beauty like Qu Qianxing.

—"Why does this aura feel so familiar? Smells like a Heavenly Robe...?"-Lin Qi murmured, but didn't dwell on it.

The garment was breathtakingly gorgeous, casting a hypnotic allure that made one itch to put it on immediately.

—"I'm hardly suited for such delicate attire,"-Lin Qi protested with false modesty, yet gleefully draped the celestial robe over his shoulders. He even twirled around, looking for a mirror to admire his magnificent posture.

Yet bafflingly, while Water Mirror Pavilion was choked with endless mirrors, the personal quarters of a beauty like Qu Qianxing did not contain a single looking glass, leaving Lin Qi unable to admire his reflection.

Shortly after Lin Qi donned the garment, however, a deafening dragon roar thundered from outside the courtyard!

It was an earth-shattering bellow that made the very ground convulse violently! The teetering mounds of books inside Qu Qianxing's room could naturally not withstand such tremors, collapsing like avalanches and triggering the defensive mechanisms in rapid succession, setting off a chain reaction of deafening explosions!

—"What in the world?!"-Lin Qi squeaked, nearly blown to bits. Only his lightning-fast reflexes and deploying a defensive barrier saved his hide.

Scrambling out of the detonating immortal cave with immense difficulty, Lin Qi sensed that something had gone terribly wrong:

Before he entered Qu Qianxing's cave, the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect had seemed peacefully slumbering beneath the moonlight—quiet, desolate, devoid of movement. Yet following that dragon roar, everything had shifted. Though no people were visible, the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect had 'awakened'!

Lin Qi could feel it: the trees were staring at him, the pavilions were staring at him, the roads were staring at him, and even Buyan Palace was glaring down upon him! In an instant, he had become the most scrutinized entity under heaven!

Naturally, being stared at was no mortal wound; yet atop Buyan Palace, both the coiled white dragon and the reclining beauty had opened their eyes, fixing their icy, murderous gazes straight toward him!

—"Damn it, why did they have to wake up now?!"-Lin Qi cursed inwardly.

Yet he did not turn tail and flee as he had during their first encounter. Instead, he dusted off the sleeves of his magnificent robe and puffed out his chest proudly:

—"Hmph! Do you truly believe I fear you?! We are both celestial immortals descended from above! You were stripped of all your cultivation as punishment, whereas I descended voluntarily! Furthermore, I have already conquered the power of six array eyes! Even if you have a dragon at your side—"

*BOOM!*

A bolt of golden divine lightning crashed right behind Lin Qi, instantaneously vaporizing one-third of the Heavenly Spirit Sect into nothingness!

—"T-This is your own sect, you know?!"-Lin Qi broke into a torrential cold sweat. Glancing back at the smoldering crater, he squeezed out a stiff, terrified smile,-"Lord Star Monarch, we are celestial colleagues hailing from the same Heavenly Court! We should cherish harmony and fraternal love..."

*BOOM!*

Another apocalyptic detonation rent the heavens!

Only Lin Qi's hypersonic agility saved him; the spot he had stood upon a microsecond prior had been erased from existence.

And Qu Qianxing's barrage was clearly far from over. Countless thunderbolts plunged from the heavens like a meteor shower, radiating the absolute fury of someone willing to obliterate the entire world just to wipe Lin Qi off the map!

—"Is he completely insane?!"-Lin Qi shrieked in horror.

He had presumed that by revealing his True Immortal pedigree, even the Untiring Immortal Venerable would show some deference. Who could have imagined that this "Wenqu Star Monarch" wouldn't give a damn about protocol?!

More importantly, how could this power be so terrifyingly colossal?!

Rumors claimed he was merely at the Nascent Soul stage—could this apocalyptic devastation possibly belong to a Nascent Soul cultivator?!

—"Exalted True Immortal, over here! Over here!"-a voice called out.

As Lin Qi dodged frantically left and right, an unexpected hand reached out to him. Looking toward the entrance of the Rare Beast Enclosure, Lin Qi saw someone waving urgently at him—it was none other than the eldest disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

Throughout this grueling ordeal, Lin Qi had inexplicably forged absolute trust in Yuwen Xuan. Without a second thought, he bolted straight toward the Rare Beast Enclosure!

And virtually the microsecond he crossed the threshold of the Rare Beast Enclosure, Lin Qi watched in horror as the entire enclosure transformed into the titanic head of a gargantuan beast, a bloody maw gaping wide to receive him! In the center of that gaping maw stood 'Yuwen Xuan,' beaming with a warm, radiant smile.

—"A f-ferocious primordial beast?!"-Lin Qi stammered in shock. He did not immediately identify it as a Taotie; after all, even in the modern Heavenly Court, Taotie were extraordinarily rare creatures.

Nor did Lin Qi particularly care what breed of beast it was; his astonishment lay elsewhere:

—"First a True Dragon, and now a primordial ferocious beast... can this puny mortal realm truly accommodate such entities?!"

—"Even if you are a True Immortal, being devoured by a wild ferocious beast can hardly be blamed on the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-'Yuwen Xuan' remarked delightfully.

—"Hahaha! A ferocious beast?! Excellent! Wonderful!"-Lin Qi erupted into manic, frenzied laughter,-"Since this realm can hold a ferocious beast and a dragon, accommodating one more True Immortal won't make a damn bit of difference!"

With that roar, Lin Qi "summoned" his true physical body!

Perhaps because Lin Qi's soul had been thoroughly replenished and nourished by pure spiritual energy, this summoning was extraordinarily seamless. His physical shell breached the void instantly, fusing with his divine soul like milk blending with water—smoother than it had ever been in his entire existence!

Most bizarre of all, even with a True Immortal's complete physical entity forcing its way across the boundary, this world showed zero resistance, appearing completely devoid of the usual high-energy rejection barriers!

Could the capacity of this world truly be classified as a lower realm?!

[Who cares! It doesn't matter what this world is! Now that I possess my true physical vessel, I can unleash my supreme might! Be it a Star Monarch, a True Dragon, or a ferocious beast, watch how I use the power of a True Immortal to crush them all into—](# "Lin Qi's mental monologue")

[To crush them into...](# "Lin Qi's mental monologue")

Before Lin Qi could savor his triumph, his eyes bulged in absolute terror.

He saw everything melting: the Heavenly Spirit Sect and Buyan Palace were melting, the Star Monarch and the True Dragon were melting, the ferocious beast was melting, and of course, the Yuwen Xuan standing in the beast's maw was melting as well!

Yuwen Xuan resembled a half-melted tallow candle, his face twisted into an impossibly grotesque grin, his throat emitting an even more eerie, muffled voice:

—"Plating complete. Ready to eat."

Alongside those words, Lin Qi watched as the melting scenery peeled away, unveiling moist, glistening fleshy stomach walls.

He was already inside something's belly!

***

—"*Hiccup.*"

Inside the Rare Beast Enclosure, a little black lamb let out a thunderous, deeply satisfied burp.

—No, calling it a 'little' black lamb was no longer accurate.

This black lamb was now the size of the entire Rare Beast Enclosure, resembling a colossal cube of black woolly fleece that plugged the room from floor to ceiling. Whether peeking through the windows or the doorway, one saw nothing but dense, curly black fleece.

When Zhou Hui stepped into the courtyard of the Rare Beast Enclosure, this was the exact spectacle that greeted him.

Unable to bear the sight, Zhou Hui plucked the newly forged Immortal Binding Rope from the hands of the nearby Guan Ye, whipping it sharply toward the unsightly giant ball of black fluff:

—"What random garbage did you eat this time?! Spit it out!"-Zhou Hui scolded sternly.

Struck by the Immortal Binding Rope, the giant black sheep deflated instantly back to its normal miniature stature, retching violently:

—"*Blegh!*"

Yet the little black sheep did not spit out a True Immortal. It merely regurgitated a celestial robe drenched in copious, slobbering saliva.

—"Master, hear me out!"-ignoring Fourth Junior Brother's deeply disturbed side-eye, the little black sheep pleaded frantically,-"I was merely apprehending a thief! He stole Second Junior Brother's clothes! If you don't believe me, you can check the mountain grand array's surveillance logs!"

—"Really! I was strictly expelling an unlawful trespasser!"-Yuwen Xuan bleated innocently.
