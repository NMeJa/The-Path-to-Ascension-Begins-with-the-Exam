# Chapter 255: Three Hundred Years Later (Part 6)

The Qu Qianxing currently standing guard at the Heavenly Gate was the very Qu Qianxing who had run away from home to become the Demon Sovereign, imprisoned that cycle's Child of Destiny, sought to overthrow the Heavenly Court's rule, and aimed to install his own Master as the Celestial Emperor.

Yet Qu Qianxing himself felt there was hardly any difference.

Granted, in the vast majority of such cases, prolonged separation between a true self and an avatar, coupled with the differing experiences and memories of two physical bodies, would render re-fusion impossible or even induce severe psychological disorders. But for Qu Qianxing, this was not an issue in the slightest.

In the words of Lu Shulong, his Second Senior Brother's temperament was stiffer than the fire poker Fourth Senior Brother used to stoke the artifact refinement furnace. Across multiple cycles, whether blessed with memories or not, he never once bent or wavered; what he did was always virtually identical. Whether it was the avatar or the original body made no fundamental difference whatsoever. Look no further: hadn't the other Second Senior Brother—the one who hadn't run away from home—seamlessly stepped up to become the Demon Sovereign under completely bewildering circumstances anyway?

The only real issue lay in the disparity between the two Qu Qianxings' academic knowledge. The real Heavenly Spirit Sect had implemented educational reforms, whereas out in the wild, the Demon Sovereign had studied a hodgepodge of bizarre demonic cultivation techniques. This was the sole portion that required digestion and integration, which was why it had taken three hundred years without complete fusion.

More importantly, both the original Qu Qianxing and the avatar felt there was zero urgency to fuse anytime soon; in fact, they had actively split into two bodies to hustle about everywhere.

Because having two physical bodies while maintaining telepathic synchronization of mind and memory actually doubled their learning efficiency! One could preside over the sect while the other oversaw the Immortal Realm they had dreamed of occupying—and indeed had occupied. Even if they never fused back together, it wouldn't matter in the least.

Of course, it was not entirely without its downsides.

Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing was still somewhat troubled by his "dark history."

—"I didn't seriously consider it a superior!"-Qu Qianxing hurried to explain.-"It was Eldest Senior Brother who threatened me, saying that as long as he was the boss, I could only be second-in-command. But because he wanted to head to the Immortal-Demon Realm to 'play' Tunhuo, the Demon Emperor's throne could not be left vacant, forcing us to preserve the original Demon Emperor."

This was an excuse.

Back then, Qu Qianxing's decision to preserve a puppet Demon Emperor was rooted in immensely intricate calculations. After all, the foe he intended to confront was the Heavenly Court, yet his partner was an insatiably ravenous Taotie that wanted to devour even its own allies. Having an extra chess piece in hand was always advantageous—who knew when it might turn into a [handy little gadget](# "Literal CN: 妙妙小工具 (miàomiào xiǎogōngjù) — a reference to the problem-solving gadget (Mouseketool) from 'Mickey Mouse Clubhouse'; an unexpected Swiss Army knife tool") for dealing with the Heavenly Court or the Taotie?

It was simply that he could not voice this openly.

Even though he had already served his punishment, what if Master finally realized the punishment back then had been far too lenient?

Even a star student like Qu Qianxing had no desire to hang from the tree a second time. That high honor was best left entirely to Eldest Senior Brother—after all, the amount of time that beast spent dangling from the bough was practically enough for it to become Dao companions with that peach tree.

While slandering his Eldest Senior Brother in the privacy of his heart, Qu Qianxing volunteered verbally to silence the demon:

—"...In the end, it is a disaster I left behind, and now it is even attempting to pollute Master's observation subject. Please allow me to resolve it!"

—"Qianxing,"-Zhou Hui merely called his second disciple's name, saying nothing more.

Qu Qianxing instantly behaved.

—"Master, I overstepped,"-Qu Qianxing admitted his fault.

Truth be told, it wasn't as though he absolutely had to kill the Demon Emperor. It was merely a puppet to begin with, completely oblivious to what Qu Qianxing had originally planned to use it for, and its power had shriveled to such a pathetic state that it posed no real threat.

It was just that this piece of "criminal evidence" had somehow attached itself to Master's observation subject, constantly reminding Master of what he, Qu Qianxing, had done back then—which was thoroughly vexing.

—"The demon seed is no great issue, but Qianxing, you..."-Zhou Hui shook his head with a sigh.-"Very well. Be it the demon seed or the Heavenly Court, you need not concern yourself with matters regarding that transmigrator. I plan to accompany him for a while longer."

—"But..."-Qu Qianxing started.

Zhou Hui did not wait for his second disciple to finish, continuing:

—"By comparison, promoting the large-scale teleportation arrays is far more important. Judging by the Heavenly Court's state, it will take them a long time to figure out the situation. As a result, the minor worlds we can contact and connect will be more numerous than anticipated, and you must monitor them all closely."

Indeed, supervising the Heavenly Gate teleportation arrays was a monumentally arduous undertaking.

Among the duties, managing travelers and the Children of Destiny in the academies was a minor affair. The true headaches lay in monitoring Heavenly Court movements, gathering intelligence on the Heavenly Court and other minor worlds, trading diplomatic pleasantries with the immortal officials governing various minor worlds, wooing ascenders arriving from other realms, screening and selecting optimal minor worlds for array construction, and countless other intricate affairs. It was a massive portfolio that only Qu Qianxing—the public face and designated next Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect—could oversee.

Zhou Hui felt this was also an exceptional crucible for Qu Qianxing.

By interacting with all sorts of people and witnessing various minor worlds, this stubborn disciple might be polished into someone a bit smoother and more adaptable... right?

—"Yes, Master,"-the moment official business was mentioned, Qu Qianxing's expression turned solemn.-"I will comprehensively gather intelligence on other minor worlds, rigorously audit them, and select the optimal minor worlds to spread the Dao, impart coursework, construct teleportation arrays, and establish alliances!"

With that tone, no one would doubt that this former Demon Sovereign's very next step would be marching an army straight back into the Heavenly Court.

Zhou Hui, however, did not view it that way.

As long as his disciples attended to their assigned duties, they were all good children in his eyes, and Zhou Hui invariably prioritized positive encouragement:

—"Do a good job. Master believes you can certainly do it."

[...There is really no need to coax me in the tone you use for the youngest disciple, especially since your youngest disciple (that broken machine) needs humanistic care even less.]

Qu Qianxing's face flushed slightly. Seeing Master strolling toward the teleportation array—as the boarding window was nearly drawing to a close amidst their conversation—he asked hurriedly:

—"Master, how long will this inspection tour last? I... your junior disciples have accumulated quite a few questions to ask you! And with you away, Eldest Senior Brother has scurried off who knows where."

[? I don't believe I've been away from the Heavenly Spirit Sect for very long, have I?]

This time, Zhou Hui did not indulge his disciple:

—"When I return depends on the observation subject's actions. Your cultivation is sufficient now; rather than verifying questions with me, put them into practice first. As for your Eldest Senior Brother... let him scurry around for a while! We will settle all accounts together afterwards."

Having said this, Zhou Hui paused, glancing at his second disciple who wore a rare expression of aggrieved longing.

In the end, his heart softened:

—"Judging from that child's performance along the way, I estimate I will return sooner than expected."

A certain "observation subject" was utterly oblivious to the discussion behind him. He had finally recovered from the critical strike dealt by Qu Qianxing's devastating beauty, yet remained terrified and incensed by the "Old Grandpa's" actions in defying his free will:

—"You actually took control of my body?!"

—"It was out of sheer necessity..."-the "Old Grandpa"—no, the former Demon Emperor—defended itself aggrievedly.

And it had every right to feel aggrieved.

A dignified Demon Emperor, reduced not only to having no physical vessel and being forced to parasitize a human, but having to sweet-talk and cajole the other party with pleasantries! If the host's hostility climbed too high, it would be unable to smoothly control him to siphon energy, and should the host seize the right opportunity, it might even be forcibly expunged.

And the ones responsible for reducing the supreme Demon Emperor of an entire realm to such a pathetic state were none other than Qu Qianxing and that ravenous beast of his.

Back then, when Qu Qianxing descended upon the demon army's encampment in transcendent celestial majesty accompanied by a colossal lump of Taotie, the Demon Emperor had recognized at first glance that the newcomer was a genuine celestial being, possessing blood ties to Tian Yan of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny and even the Celestial Emperor himself. As the sovereign of the Celestial Court's greatest enemy, the Demon Emperor had naturally seen them before, and even its very birth shared ancient origins with them. It had guessed that Qu Qianxing had likely had a falling out with the Heavenly Court and chosen to fall into demonhood.

To the Demon Emperor's eternal remorse, it had been possessed by demonic greed and failed to seize the chance to slaughter Qu Qianxing on the spot, even dreaming of using him to oppose the Heavenly Court. Who could have imagined that beneath that angelically divine face lurked an entity far more fiendish than orthodox demons? It had completely [broken all martial ethics to bite, cheat, and sneak-attack](# "Literal CN: 不讲武德，来咬，来骗，来偷袭 — famous Chinese internet meme originating from viral martial artist Ma Baoguo (马保国), adapted here to include Taotie's biting") an "elderly senior" who had lived for tens of thousands of years! Within a few short years, Qu Qianxing had reduced it to a puppet emperor, threatening to feed it to the Taotie whenever it stepped out of line—as if that Taotie didn't already sneak bites of its flesh and steal its demonic qi on a daily basis anyway.

The accursed "turning point" arrived three hundred years ago (Langhuan time).

Back then, Qu Qianxing had inexplicably split into two, with one of them marching straight to war against the Heavenly Court's grand army. The Demon Emperor had been overjoyed. Pretending to reinforce the battlefield, it notified one of the Qu Qianxings while lurking in the shadows to spectate, waiting for an opportune moment to reap the fisherman's spoils. Unfortunately, it never got the chance, watching in sheer horror as Qu Qianxing actually beat Tian Yan into retreat.

Not only that: when Qu Qianxing was fine, it suffered; when Qu Qianxing ran into trouble, it suffered even more!

In truth, the Demon Emperor had no idea what had actually transpired. It only knew that upon seeing even Tian Yan vanish, it panicked and tried to flee so Qu Qianxing wouldn't catch it at the scene. Yet before it had fled very far, it was ensnared by the Demon Summoning Banner, and its power began to unravel.

—"I didn't do it on purpose,"-a certain fourth disciple (Guan Ye), who had planted the Demon Summoning Banner to accumulate demonic qi, had remarked.-"Had I known there was such a massive fish, my array ought to have been even stronger."

—"I didn't do it on purpose either,"-a certain second disciple avatar (Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing), who had nearly been devoured by Eldest Senior Brother, had likewise said.-"I never intended to leave this future calamity behind, so I designed a contingency: if I were ever destroyed, the Demon Emperor would be gravely stricken and slain by my curse. But who knew Eldest Senior Brother would hold me in his mouth? Trapping me between life and death kept it alive instead."

In short, through layers of bizarre coincidences while Qu Qianxing had completely forgotten about its existence, the Demon Emperor had been caught in the crossfire, regressing into an insignificant demon seed.

Naturally, the Demon Emperor—no, the present demon seed—refused to resign itself to this state. Even if it couldn't recover its peak condition, merely regaining enough strength to flee this minor world where Qu Qianxing resided would be a blessing.

To that end, it had no choice but to coax and cater to this arrogant, narcissistic host.

Yet this host was not entirely devoid of merit. Although he had foolishly marched up to the Heavenly Gate on his own whim, nearly exposing them beneath the dual gazes of Qu Qianxing and the immortal officials, high risk had indeed yielded high reward. For reasons unknown, this host had suffered an acute brain malfunction, adamantly forfeiting the opportunity to study at the Heavenly Gate Academy to head straight for the Immortal-Demon frontlines!

The Immortal-Demon frontlines!

That was the homeland the demon seed knew best and longed for most!

Such battlefields were constantly irradiated with high concentrations of demonic qi, plagued by turbulent magnetic fields and rampaging demonic barriers that severely corroded the physical and non-physical vessels of virtually every existence—animals, plants, living creatures, non-living constructs, souls, and soulless matter alike.

Only for the demon race was that place the "cradle of ascension."

Upon a battlefield shrouded in death and despair, even the faintest sliver of malice would be nourished by demonic qi to spawn terrifying monstrosities, let alone a demon seed that had once been the Demon Emperor!

Moreover, the demonic domain connected to Langhuan... was in fact the deep rear of the demon domain, a horrifying zone where even ordinary Heavenly Generals dared not casually tread.

The demon seed was confident that as long as it returned there, it could reclaim a portion of its power. Although Qu Qianxing's cursed mark prevented it from reshaping its original body, that was of no consequence—it would simply seize this host's vessel.

Considering this foolish host had voluntarily charged to the frontlines to seek his own death, the demon seed resolved to devour his soul completely when the time came, sparing him the excruciating agony of soul-shredding so he could vanish swiftly and cleanly.

Scheming thus, the demon seed's demeanor toward Long Tianming grew increasingly benevolent, exactly like a devoted, biological grandfather.

—"If I hadn't taken control of you back then, what would you have done if you missed the teleportation array?"-the Old Grandpa reasoned earnestly, putting on an air of looking out entirely for Long Tianming's sake.-"Besides, no matter how pretty that living ancestor is, is he someone you can just gawk at carelessly? Careful he gouges out your [peepers to steep in wine](# "Literal CN: 招子 (zhāozi) — archaic martial arts slang for eyes; 挖出招子泡酒 is a classic wuxia villainess/femme fatale threat")!"

—"Whoa!"-Long Tianming gasped.

Gouging out peepers to steep in wine?!

What kind of retro wuxia female lead behavior was this?!

Although the popular female archetypes in male-oriented web novels had cycled through countless iterations—growing increasingly hollow like inflatable dolls—and modern soft men had grown ever more glass-hearted, unable to tolerate the slightest questioning or pushback, labeling every concession as [pathetic simping](# "Literal CN: 舔狗 (tiǎngǒu) — simp / licking dog"), the moment you actually tossed them in front of an overwhelmingly gorgeous woman, all they knew how to do was bark like obedient dogs. In fact, if the beauty wasn't a woman, it was even better.

Long Tianming wasn't threatened in the least, gossiping with keen enthusiasm instead:

—"You know him?"

—"I don't!"-the demon seed swiftly denied.

—"Oh? Then who is Qu Qianxing?"-Long Tianming rolled his eyes.-"Why do I get the feeling you're terrified of him?"

[...I really want to possess and devour him right this instant!]

The demon seed thought in incandescent irritation. Regrettably, it still relied on this host to exist; before taking over the body, it needed him to accumulate demonic qi and unseal the demon domain.

The demon seed had no choice but to continue its gentle defense:

—"I'm not afraid, I simply wouldn't dare provoke such an exalted figure... Fine, I admit I know who he is! His overt identity right now is the second disciple of this Langhuan world's Heavenly Spirit Sect, but in truth he is a grand figure from the Celestial Realm. Far more elevated than that questionable demoted goddess following you, he is the child of Lord Tian Yan himself, who governs the Heavenly Dao. In other words, he can alter destiny!"

The demon seed mingled truth with falsehood, using that final lie as an implicit intimidation tactic.

For beings who fancied themselves "protagonists" and "chosen ones of heaven," no matter how fearless they sounded on the surface, the fact that they constantly prattled about destiny meant they were undeniably the ones who revered—and feared—"heavenly destiny" most of all.

*Someone like you, with your 'my fate is governed by me' arrogance, is his prime target for eradication!*

—"Someone like me is his tribulation target, right!"

Long Tianming didn't dare entertain delusions about someone like Qu Qianxing, so he could only conclude that Qu Qianxing was his love rival.

Long Tianming sucked in a breath of cold air as though nursing a phantom toothache.

—"Exactly! So don't foolishly be an eyesore before him."

If Qu Qianxing was his love rival, who was the "lover"? Renran? Didn't seem like it. Besides, Renran had already brought along Young Master Zhou when she made her entrance. Then what about Qu Qianxing? If the love rival was already that breathtakingly gorgeous, what on earth did the "lover" look like?!

Long Tianming couldn't help but wonder.

He swore! It was pure curiosity! He had zero intention of offending Qu Qianxing or the Heavenly Dao; he just wanted to sneak a single look out of curiosity.

So where would that person be?

Following standard "plot development," since he was the love rival, that "lover" was likely somewhere very close to Qu Qianxing—for instance, in that whatever-it's-called Heavenly Spirit Sect. Right! Since Qu Qianxing was said to be the second disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, could that person perhaps be the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Little Junior Sister?! Come to think of it, "Heavenly Spirit Sect" sounded awfully familiar; ever since running into Renran, he seemed to hear this sect's name mentioned non-stop.

Thinking of this, Long Tianming turned to Renran and asked:

—"What kind of sect is the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"Huh? Ah?"-Renran was preoccupied with her own thoughts, caught entirely off guard by the abrupt question.

The demon seed's heart leapt into its throat in an instant (though it currently possessed neither heart nor throat). Unable to comprehend why the topic had circled back, it could only pray that Renran wouldn't utter anything superfluous.

Fortunately, regarding the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Renran's stance was entirely aligned with the demon seed's: she, too, had zero desire for Long Tianming to get entangled with the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect is a remote sect located in Langhuan's Central Domain,"-Renran weighed her words carefully, striving to sound objective while dissuasive.-"The spiritual field there is turbulent and ambient spiritual energy is inferior, unsuited for cultivation..."

—"Haha, maiden, what ancient calendar are you reciting from?"

Someone abruptly interrupted Renran's speech.

Renran glanced over in displeasure, discovering a cultivator attired in Heavenly Gate Academy robes. He was evidently the guide assigned to that group of immortal officials; however, because the officials had been whispering in their private divine-sense chat group and paying him little heed, he had been content to act as if he didn't exist—until he overheard Renran "slandering" the Heavenly Spirit Sect, at which point he couldn't help speaking up:

—"The Central Domain hasn't looked like that for ages. You will understand once you arrive at the Abyssal Ocean."

He had zero desire to be delayed for that long.

Teleportation arrays are instruments of utmost precision! *DONG————!*

Cutting off the official's tirade was a long, resonant toll of a bell.

The guide let out a sigh of relief, speaking in a tone that was practically a cheer:

—"We have arrived."

Yet contrasting with his jovial tone, following immediately upon the bell was a titanic, cataclysmic roar—like the earth-shattering din of an impending apocalypse where heaven and earth collapsed! [—"Teleportation failure?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")-a celestial official questioned.

They were immortal civil servants holding official state posts after all, and wouldn't lose their composure over a minor tremor. By unspoken consensus, they prepared to unleash their various divine arts for self-preservation, only to discover their spiritual energies were sluggish and obstructed—and *that* was when true panic began to set in.

Fortunately, amidst the roaring rumble, the guide's voice echoed with crystalline clarity within everyone's minds:

—"Please do not panic, everyone. This is a normal phenomenon of space-time docking. Rapidly entering a world with differing space-time causes slight physical discomfort; our phantasmal projection will assist you in acclimatizing, while helping you better understand the world you are about to enter."

Accompanying this explanation, Long Tianming discovered that the white glow representing the teleportation array had dissolved. He was suspended in midair, while the heavens and earth all around were collapsing, tumbling headlong into a bottomless abyss!

—"...Th-there's even an opening cinematic to watch?"-Long Tianming chuckled weakly.

He had been in a state of terror moments ago, unable to hear or comprehend what the others were saying.

Fortunately, the guide's words were evidently infused with spellcraft; the moment they were uttered, they forcefully conveyed what was happening in a manner Long Tianming could comprehend, forcibly calming his nerves.

Yet Long Tianming's dread did not dissipate entirely.

The sense of immersion was simply too overwhelming!

Even if one prattled about transmigrating or experiencing the end of the world, when actually placed inside a genuine "sky is falling" cataclysm, knowing it was merely an 'animation' did little to truly put one's mind at ease.

While Long Tianming was petrified, the immortal officials were gripped by solemn awe.

Not because of the apocalyptic illusion of heaven and earth crumbling.

When they first discovered their power suppressed, they had been exceedingly disgruntled. As immortal officials governing entire worlds, they were rarely subjected to such constraints, and several had even considered forcibly bursting out regardless of the array's status. Yet at that moment, the guide's voice had struck their seas of consciousness like a bolt of lightning, carrying the absolute deterrence of supreme power.

How could a mere guide, whose cultivation didn't even reach the level of an immortal servant, possess spellcraft of this caliber?!

If even a guide was like this, just how terrifying was the grandmaster who had constructed this teleportation array...?!

Only now did the immortals look properly upon that humble guide for the first time, their gazes vacillating with shock and suspicion.

Recalling their earlier speculations (gossip), they broke into cold sweat and hastily stowed away their playful, judgmental attitudes, genuinely feeling the weight of standing before the creation of an "exalted titan of unfathomable origins."

Along with this shift, their postures straightened into strict propriety.

Viewing that "opening cinematic" again, they found profound meaning embedded in every single frame.

Of course, the grandeur and hyper-realism of the cinematic was another reason.

Beside them, the guide narrated that the illusion originated from the history of "Langhuan."

Tens of thousands of years ago, this minor world was originally the Heavenly Court's library. To plug the Demon Eye corrupting the Celestial Realm, it had chosen to fall into the lower realm; the opening cataclysm was a reenactment of that ancient catastrophe.

Yet the world had not perished. So long as living beings endured ceaselessly like wild grass, civilization would inevitably ignite and spread like wildfire. Thus, even though the Demon Eye had never vanished, the spiritual energy of the central Abyssal Ocean remained utterly chaotic, and the demon race could return at any moment, colossal trees had sprouted upon the corpses of fallen immortals into towering behemoths reaching the heavens. Humankind had constructed cities in the clouds, taking up arms once more against the demonic hordes and even expanding boundaries outward.

Naturally, in a history of such magnificent proportions, [generations of talents emerged, each leading the epoch for millennia](# "Literal CN: 代有人才出，各领风骚数千年 — adapted from Zhao Yi's Qing dynasty poem: 'Generations of talents emerge, each leading the literary scene for hundreds of years'").

Among them were ingenious artisans who designed the Five Cities and Twelve Towers in the sky; exiled formation grandmasters cast down from the Heavenly Court; divination masters capable of peering through reincarnation; and brilliant commanders who marched onto battlefields to combat demons. In so brief a projection, it was naturally impossible to depict their entire lives—there were only fleeting snippets of combat and a few lines of description—yet this brevity only stirred the imagination all the more.

The immortal officials were local natives of the upper realms after all. Though they had started off sitting upright with stiff dignity, by the end their blood was boiling, and they couldn't resist wanting to chat again. Yet this time, they didn't dare congregate in group chats, opting instead to whisper in one-on-one transmissions with trusted acquaintances:

[—"Worthy of having once been part of the Immortal Realm; truly a land of outstanding heroes."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official A")

[—"Ah, I can't help but reminisce about the years before my ascension; back then, I too was..."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official B")

[—"Spare me, were you even half as dashing as them?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official C")

[—"Speaking of which, if we construct teleportation arrays in the future, could we also adapt our world's history into a phantasmal cinematic?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official B")

[—"Don't tell me you want to stuff yourself in there?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official C")

There were also those who were politically astute:

[—"This is quite fine; at least travelers gain an understanding of the minor world they are entering."](# "Speaker: Politically Astute Official A")

[—"I suspect it is more than a mere introduction."](# "Speaker: Politically Astute Official B")

[—"How so?"](# "Speaker: Politically Astute Official A")

[—"Don't you feel your blood boiling after watching this, making you want to enlist and fight on the Immortal-Demon frontlines?"](# "Speaker: Politically Astute Official B")

[—"You're not wrong! Just for the chance to appear in this cinematic, quite a few people might genuinely be tempted."](# "Speaker: Politically Astute Official A")

...Recruitment propaganda?!

Long Tianming had caught every word of the immortal officials' murmurs once more, but his thoughts were on an entirely different wavelength from these upper-realm natives:

[You guys missed the entire point!]

[Looking at this motley crew (x) of heroes showcasing their unique ultimate abilities, if I hadn't transmigrated to a cultivation realm, I would swear this was a promotional trailer for some MOBA game!]

Wait a damn minute—what on earth did:

> *"Immortal Chess: Historical Edition Now on Hot Sale!"*

...even mean?!
