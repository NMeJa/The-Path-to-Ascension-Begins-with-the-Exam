# Synopsis & Introduction (文案与简介)

After [grinding relentlessly for several lifetimes](# "Literal CN: 卷了几辈子 (involution / grinding for several lifetimes) — modern Chinese slang for exhausting, hyper-competitive academic or workplace overwork"), Zhou Hui finally accepted the reality that he was nothing more than a side dish of cannon-fodder fish.

No matter how hard he ground, he could never alter the Heavenly Dao's script in which his sect was annihilated, his disciples betrayed or perished, and his own soul was scattered to the winds.

So Zhou Hui decided to salt himself properly, lie flat as a [salted-fish slacker](# "Literal CN: 咸鱼 (salted fish) — slang for a total slacker who has completely given up on ambition and just wants to lie flat"), and start his retirement early—tending flowers, teasing spirit birds, and casually tutoring his disciples.

Of course, he also intended to squander every last bit of the fortune he had accumulated over his previous lives, leaving not a single copper coin for that so-called Child of Heavenly Fortune!

The only problem was... something seemed slightly off about Zhou Hui's method of squandering.

His good-for-nothing disciples suddenly started grinding like mad; next, the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect started grinding; and finally, the whole cultivation world was dragged into the academic rat race.

> **Immortal Realm Educational Reform Milestones**
> - Universal Nine-Year Compulsory Cultivation Education rolled out across all provinces;
> - The [Project 211 & Project 985 Elite Sect Initiative](# "Cultural Parody: 门派211，985工程 — parodies China's Project 211 and Project 985 national elite university tier programs") formally enacted;
> - The Mortal World Collective Ascension Plan officially launched—

By the time Zhou Hui snapped out of it, his sect had become the number-one sect in the world, his disciples had grown into supreme powerhouses of the Three Thousand Worlds who refused to leave his side, the fortune he had tried to squander had multiplied several times over, the Heavenly Dao's script had completely crashed, and the entire mortal realm had ascended into an Upper Realm.

Many years later, the Protagonist chosen by the Heavenly Dao, Long Tianming, finally arrived in this world, stared at the newly distributed copy of *[Five Thousand Years of Ascension, Three Thousand Years of Cultivation](# "Book Parody: 《五千年飞升三千年修仙》 — parody of China's notorious college entrance exam workbook 'Five Years of Gaokao, Three Years of Mock Exams' (《五年高考三年模拟》)")*, and let out a despairing wail:

[—"I want to go home!"](# "Speaker: Long Tianming (The Heavenly Dao's Chosen Protagonist)")

***
