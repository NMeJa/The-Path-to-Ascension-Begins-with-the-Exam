# Chapter 17: Post-Exam Arrangements

[Time is just about up,]
Gan Tang thought.

Unlike a certain fellow who had indulged in both cheating and peeping, Gan Tang had naturally made full preparations for the exam's conclusion.

Unrushed, she conducted one final check. Once she confirmed that nothing was amiss, she neatly stacked her examination paper, washed her ink brush and inkstone clean, packed her things, and looked up toward the very apex of the palace.

Upon that crystal ceiling resembling the surface of water, a colossal "silver moon" was being refracted.

That, of course, was no real moon, but rather a newly added sect treasure of the Heavenly Spirit Sect:

The [Sundial Clock](# "Literal CN: 日晷钟 (rìguǐ zhōng) — sect timekeeping magical treasure dividing a two-hour shichen into modern minutes and seconds").

Unlike ordinary sundials, it further divided each of the twelve [shichen](# "Traditional Chinese two-hour time unit (时辰)") into 'first half' and 'second half,' breaking each half-shichen down into sixty minutes, and each minute into sixty seconds. Two newly added hands—a minute hand and a second hand—ticked rhythmically as they indicated the passage of time. This examination, for instance, had been calibrated with pinpoint precision to conclude at exactly forty minutes past the second half of the You Hour [6:40 PM].

Gan Tang had no idea why anyone would subdivide time in such an elaborate manner; it had taken the disciples across the sect several whole days just to grow accustomed to it.

Yet such temporal divisions were a trivial matter (in Gan Tang's estimation). What made this magical treasure truly extraordinary was the upright gnomon pin it originally possessed.

Refined from the purest spiritual energy and the celestial essence of the sun and moon, this gnomon was utterly pure and invisible, undetectable without spiritual power. Yet whether through cloud, rain, or snow, through dawn, dusk, night, or day, it cast a needle-thin shadow across the sundial face to mark the exact hour.

Furthermore, this shadow was refracted by the gleaming surface of the sundial, falling upon different locations throughout Mount Tianling depending on the time of day.

Take this very moment, for instance: the You Hour [5:00 PM – 7:00 PM].

As the sun dipped beneath the mountains, the shadow of the gnomon was refracted directly over the Palace of Silence. Outside the palace, the light was visibly dimming; yet simultaneously, the [Peach Talisman](# "Literal CN: 桃符 (táofú) — traditional protective peach-wood talisman, upgraded with campus RFID / teleportation card functionality") tied beside the jade pendant at Gan Tang's waist began to radiate a faint, ethereal glow.

This Peach Talisman served as proof of identity for Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples.

Whenever an aspiring cultivator was accepted into the sect, they received a peach talisman carved from the boughs of the [ancient peach tree](# "The tens-of-thousands-of-years-old peach tree standing at the mountain gate") at the mountain entrance. It was inscribed not only with the disciple's name, but also provided minor benefits: attracting fortune and auspicious luck (hardly any), warning of impending peril (unreliable), replenishing spiritual energy (negligible amounts), and transmitting messages between fellow disciples (limited to mere fragments of phrases).

Now, alongside the operation of the Sundial Clock, this humble talisman had been upgraded with a brand-new function:

Intra-sect teleportation.

For example, right now, the shadow of the gnomon rested squarely atop the Palace of Silence, signifying the end of the day's curriculum. So long as the shadow lingered, disciples inside the Palace of Silence could activate their Peach Talismans to select their desired destination: dormitories, the dining hall, the Library Pavilion, and so forth.

For disciples like Gan Tang who had yet to forge their own flying swords, this was an absolute lifesaver.

After all, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was nestled amidst rugged mountains. Without a flying sword, every single day meant trekking up and down peaks, hiking endless slopes, and marching across thousands of stone steps—a grueling rhythm capable of wearing a person to death.

Granted, traditional cultivation dogma held that 'tempering the body precedes tempering the mind' and that physical hardship was a necessary trial for fledgling seekers of the Dao. But prior to mastering flying sword flight, which disciple hadn't secretly cursed such nonsense in their hearts?

Now, with the Sundial Clock in place, even if it required fixed schedules and designated pick-up points, it eliminated countless headaches.

At the very least, whenever Gan Tang wished to return to her outer sect quarters from the Palace of Silence, it now took merely an instant.

Today, however, an announcement had been issued beforehand: upon the exam's conclusion, all disciples were required to assemble at the Library Pavilion. As a result, individual destination selection was disabled, and everyone would be forcibly teleported en masse.

Thus, when the bell announcing the end of the examination rang out with a resonant *clang——!*, Gan Tang bore witness to the very same sight that Su Xin had seen.

She saw the test papers swim away like silver minnows, the coral platforms pop and vanish like bubbles, and the entire palace dissolve into drifting clouds, dispersing into nothingness in the blink of an eye.

Yet unlike Su Xin, Gan Tang was not abruptly dumped out onto the dirt road.

Black shadows cast by the gnomon soaked through every inch of space. Only the protective bubble encasing Gan Tang remained intact; yet all color—both around her and upon her very person—was drained away, leaving only shades of gray, white, and black, as though suspended in a crevice between moments.

No—at this exact instant, time had genuinely stopped.

When the Sundial Clock was first erected, some meddlesome disciples had experimented with it and discovered that one could remain within this temporal seam for up to three minutes—a buffer deliberately reserved to allow students to prepare themselves for transit.

—"No matter how many times I see it, it's still miraculous,"-Gan Tang marveled softly.

Word had it that this Sundial Clock had been forged by none other than the Immortal Venerable's fourth direct disciple, Senior Brother Guan Ye.

To think that at merely the Foundation Establishment Middle Stage, he was already capable of crafting a magical treasure that manipulated both time and space! Such talent in artifact refinement truly lived up to the reputation of a disciple handpicked by the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself!

[I truly have to work even harder!]

Musing thus, Gan Tang gave her sleeve a gentle wave.

Time instantly resumed its flow. The black shadows and everything around her dissipated just like the underwater palace. When Gan Tang lifted her eyes once more, she was already standing inside the newly renovated main hall of the Library Pavilion, where fellow disciples were being teleported in at that very same second.

An acquaintance spotted Gan Tang and called out in greeting:

—"Gan Tang, how did you do on the exam?"

—"Not bad,"-Gan Tang replied, before asking in turn:-"What about you?"

—"Lucky break—every single problem was something I reviewed!"-the other disciple said with undisguised pride.

Gan Tang nodded, thinking that it was just as she had anticipated. Exactly as she had deduced from the testing mechanics, that examination paper had been tailored specifically to everyone's individual strengths.

Yet another disciple chimed in:

—"I was completely hopeless—not a single thing I crammed for showed up."-(Though had he not been grinning so broadly, his lament might have sounded slightly more convincing.)

Another laughed heartily:

—"None of what I reviewed showed up either, but they tested everything I'm actually good at!"

In short, not everyone possessed Gan Tang's sharp perception to deduce the underlying testing algorithm. Consequently, virtually everyone felt they had performed exceptionally well; in high spirits, they bantered back and forth with half-serious, half-playful guesses about their scores, filling the hall with harmonious merriment.

It was not until the figure of the Untiring Immortal Venerable materialized high above the Library Pavilion that the chatter abruptly ceased.

\*

—"Everyone performed quite well in this examination,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.

Since the primary objective of this exam had been encouragement, Zhou Hui naturally had no intention of wearing a stern face to lecture the disciples. He commended them warmly:

—"The inner sect showed considerable progress compared to the previous examination, and quite a few talented individuals with great potential emerged from the outer sect as well. It is evident that all of you put forth diligent effort and demonstrated your respective strengths."

Standing to the side, Qu Qianxing felt the corner of his mouth twitch uncontrollably.

[Given that Master literally handpicked questions with targeted precision, of course everyone demonstrated their strengths!]

[Had I known the exam paper would be like that, I should have obediently followed Master's instructions and served as an exam proctor instead of wasting my time in the examination hall trying to reclaim the face I lost during the previous exam!]

Indeed, Qu Qianxing, just like Gan Tang, had seen straight through the question-generation mechanics, and he was currently steeped in bitter regret.

However, people like him were few and far between.

For ordinary disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, opportunities to even catch a glimpse of the Untiring Immortal Venerable were extraordinarily rare, to say nothing of receiving his personal praise.

Thus, with merely those few brief remarks from Zhou Hui, the vast majority of disciples present—Gan Tang included—wore wide, goofy grins, sending the already cheerful atmosphere soaring even higher.

Seizing upon this delightful mood, Zhou Hui made his next announcement:

—"In order to ensure that none of your diligence and potential go to waste, this Venerable has decided to implement [ability-based tracking and leveled classes](# "Literal CN: 分级分班 (fēnjí fēnbān) — standardized academic tracking sorting students into tiered classes based on exam scores") based upon the results of this examination. Different grades and classes will be assigned differentiated curricula. The specific proposals and implementation timeline shall be posted here at the Library Pavilion for public notice in the coming days."

Regarding this, the disciples raised not a single objection.

Unaware of what horror lay in store for them, their cheerful mood remained entirely unaffected. In fact, the silly grins upon some disciples' faces grew even broader, thrilled that they would be granted even more learning opportunities.

Seeing their reaction, Zhou Hui nodded in satisfaction, proceeding with his next announcement:

—"Furthermore, based on the performance in this examination, several disciples shall be selected to assume the post of [Instructor](# "Literal CN: 教习 (jiàoxí) — traditional title for schoolmaster, tutor, or teaching assistant")."

This arrangement was what truly arrested the disciples' attention.

After all, within a cultivation sect, the official post of Instructor carried substantial weight. It was typically held by cultivators at or above the Golden Core or Nascent Soul stages; at the very least, it fell to direct disciples—in the past, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had relied on Qu Qianxing and his peers to hold the post concurrently. Appointing ordinary disciples as Instructors was something unheard of until today.

Could they teach properly?

A few disciples felt somewhat apprehensive.

Yet those students who felt confident in their own academic excellence puffed out their chests, gazing up at Zhou Hui with eager anticipation, praying that they would be among the chosen.

—"Silence!"-Qu Qianxing stepped forward at this moment.

This handsome Second Senior Brother clearly wielded an authority within the sect far exceeding his pretty face; with merely one frosty glance swept across the crowd, everyone ducked their heads [like frightened quail](# "Literal CN: 像是鹌鹑一样 — cowering in silence out of fear and respect").

—"I shall now announce the roster of selected disciples,"-Qu Qianxing said coolly.-"Zhang Qian——"

[—"Present!"](# "Speaker: Zhang Qian")

—"Wang Xiaoye."

[—"Present!"](# "Speaker: Wang Xiaoye")

...

—"Gan Tang."

—"Present!"-Gan Tang answered in sheer delight.

She couldn't help but be ecstatic. The status of an Instructor was elevated, and the monthly stipend was naturally generous; for sect disciples, it was arguably the best job available, bar none. This was especially true for Gan Tang, who hailed from the outer sect. Without connections in the inner sect or any prior 'work experience,' even if she reached Foundation Establishment, finding a comfortable posting that allowed her to cultivate in peace would have been extraordinarily difficult.

In truth, Gan Tang was somewhat surprised that she had been selected. After all, once she figured out the exam's mechanics, she had [deliberately courted hardship](# "Literal CN: 自找苦吃 (zì zhǎo kǔ chī) — asking for trouble; deliberately making things harder for oneself") by tampering with the question-generation algorithm, saddling herself with needless difficulty.

—(It was only later, when these top students conducted a post-exam review, that they realized selection was not determined solely by total score. Rather, every single chosen disciple was someone like Gan Tang: possessed of rock-solid foundational knowledge, meticulous in character, and perceptive enough to see through the examination rules.)

And what excited Gan Tang even further was what came next.

—"...The disciples named above shall report to the Palace of Silence starting tomorrow,"-Qu Qianxing continued once the roll call concluded.-"You will undergo personal 'training' conducted by Master in advance; only after passing shall you be qualified to instruct other disciples."

This announcement provoked another wave of breathless gasps.

Personal instruction from a [Tribulation Transcendence titan](# "Literal CN: 渡劫期大佬 — supreme powerhouse at the highest mortal cultivation realm")!

Mere casual praise from Zhou Hui had already been enough to send these disciples over the moon, let alone direct personal tutelage!

Even those who had harbored doubts about ordinary disciples serving as Instructors no longer had the slightest objection.

What could possibly go wrong with Instructors trained personally by the Untiring Immortal Venerable?

Everyone found themselves looking forward to future classes with even greater relish!

\*

[What adorable disciples!]

[In that other world of mine, students with such passion for learning were truly few and far between.]

Zhou Hui beamed warmly at the disciples below, who resembled a herd of [silly roe deer](# "Literal CN: 傻狍子 (shǎ páozi) — northeastern Chinese slang for adorably gullible, innocent, and oblivious creatures"), feeling that the painstaking effort he had poured into authoring introductory textbooks lately had not been in vain.

He could hardly wait to fulfill the eager expectations of these lovely children and begin their lessons!

Yet before Zhou Hui could capitalize on the momentum to finalize all arrangements, Yuwen Xuan suddenly snuck into the Library Pavilion like a thief, darting up to his side.

Zhou Hui's good mood instantly evaporated by half.

—"Why are you back here again? Where are the guests?"

—"Ah, they said they had urgent business and left without staying for dinner,"-Yuwen Xuan replied, putting on an innocent and adorable expression.

Zhou Hui frowned in utter bewilderment.

—"Then what in the world did they come all this way for?"

[Did they make the trip just to sit for an exam?!]
