# Chapter 214: Loving Master, Filial Disciple

The moment Yuwen Xuan began contemplating culinary matters, he exhibited a degree of rapt dedication far surpassing any academic lecture. Zhou Hui frequently remarked that had he devoted even half of this zeal toward studying, he would have ascended to the celestial realm aeons ago.

Yet what was celestial ascension? Could you eat it?

Yuwen Xuan actually scrutinized Qu Qianxing's exam papers twice from top to bottom, comparing them against the exam question repository designed by his Master, carefully evaluating how much psychological trauma these questions would inflict upon the demon race.

—"...Speaking of which, the disparity between different question types is colossal! Certain exam problems are simply too difficult, leading those blockheads to glance at the dismal scores of those around them and give up entirely. Yet other questions appear deceptively simple, only to be lethal trap problems that leave even Old Second gnashing his teeth in regret! The latter variety of high-intensity torment (marination) cultivates far more succulent, tender brain matter. No, merely inspecting them isn't enough; I must select a few problems and hunt down several... 'lucky volunteers' to run live experiments."

A certain hopeless academic layabout muttered to himself.

His cognitive faculties experienced a parabolic spike exclusively when applied to such endeavors. Even in the form of a black sheep, nothing impeded his train of thought. Endowed with terrifying execution speed, the moment the thought solidified, he dashed off to track down his 'lucky volunteers.'

Left behind, the Heart Demon listened as his voice faded into the distance, thoroughly bewildered:

—"Senior Brother Yuwen? Senior Brother Yuwen... has he left?"

However, she had long grown accustomed to this.

From her vantage point, Yuwen Xuan materialized without warning and dissolved into thin air just as abruptly. Most of the time, he did nothing at all, merely dropping by to inspect her—resembling a farmer stepping into his coop to verify whether his penned hens had laid eggs, whether they had fattened up, and when they would be ripe for the stew pot.

—"Though his husbandry is far too rough,"-the Heart Demon grumbled softly,-"Even culinary livestock reared for slaughter will fail to fatten up if they are made aware of their impending doom from day one. Senior Brother Yuwen understands this principle full well, yet insists on terrifying people with every visit! Even for the sake of tenderizing the meat, wouldn't coaxing me, humoring me, and acknowledging me as Patriarch Yu Si make me far happier, enabling me to fatten up much faster?"

Indeed: the vast majority of Heart Demons suffered from cognitive identity crises.

Under normal circumstances, Heart Demons were fundamentally incapable of recognizing their nature as Heart Demons. They either hallucinated that they were an organic aspect of the host's psyche, or believed themselves to be the vengeful wraith or spiritual avatar of another being.

This cognitive dissonance was inextricably tied to their genesis.

The so-called Heart Demon—demons born from the heart—broadly bifurcated into two distinct archetypes.

The first category arose from an inability to achieve inner reconciliation with oneself: the chasm between ideals and reality, lust and restraint, ego and alter-ego. The mighty could not stomach their own vulnerability; the perfectionist could not bear to perceive their own flaws; the righteous could not forgive their momentary cowardice; the covetous could not bridle their insatiable desires. From these fissures, a Heart Demon inevitably spawned.

Because this category of Heart Demon originated directly from the host's own soul, their physical appearance was typically identical to the host. Believing themselves to be the disowned fragment of the host's true identity, they tempted, cursed, and guided the host to surrender to and confront this suppressed 'self,' ultimately dragging the host into demonic cultivation. Only then could they merge smoothly with the host, truly becoming the 'primary self.'

Frankly, even if this path led to demonic corruption, it was considered a relatively 'merciful ending.'

Whether the cultivator admitted it or secretly yearned for such release, having fallen to demonic corruption, they remained 'themselves'—an uninhibited, hedonistic iteration of themselves.

The second category of Heart Demon was vastly more horrifying:

They were born entirely from dread!

Certain terrors were merely psychological trauma capable of being conquered; yet other terrors stemmed purely from having perpetrated heinous, unforgivable atrocities, [inviting ghosts to knock upon the midnight door](# "Literal CN: 平时不做亏心事，半夜不怕鬼敲门 — do no evil by day, fear no ghost knocking by night").

This class of Heart Demon was the living incarnation of terror. They assumed the exact appearance and temperament the host dreaded most under heaven. Particularly the vengeful wraith archetype seeking bloody retribution: such Heart Demons would never spare the host merely because the host 'fell to demonic cultivation.' Firmly convinced they were the authentic victim, possessed by an obsession far more harrowing than any wandering ghost, they would not merely drive the host into demonic madness—even after the host succumbed to corruption, they would continue torturing them for eternity until their soul was extinguished into oblivion.

Yan Bailang's Heart Demon was originally destined to belong to this second archetype.

Within her foundational self-conception, she was supposed to be the senior sister Yan Bailang had backstabbed in cold blood—the predecessor Sect Master of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, Patriarch Yu Si. She could even point to a flawless origin story justifying her birth:

Yu Si had been murdered within the Beast Soul Tower. As the sacred trial ground of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, that tower preserved a multitude of beast souls, fortified by countless warding arrays binding ethereal entities. Dying within that tower, a sliver of Yu Si's remnant soul had been preserved, finally seeking vengeance when Yan Bailang reopened the Beast Soul Tower to exploit disciples just as his predecessors had.

Such was the Heart Demon's self-conception.

Yet Yuwen Xuan had brutally shattered her reality.

To be precise, the very instant the Heart Demon awakened to her consciousness and the bottomless hatred directed at Yan Bailang, she opened her eyes—only to be met with a drooling sheep face.

The Heart Demon had been scared out of her wits on the spot.

For a Heart Demon, this was an unprecedented anomaly.

As ethereal parasites rooted within the host's heart, lacking physical form and independent souls, sustained entirely by feeding upon negative emotions, unless the host perished or achieved genuine enlightenment, even True Immortals were powerless against them.

Compounded by their predatory nature of tempting and corrupting mortals, Heart Demons were innately devoid of fear. Given the slightest opening, they dared seduce gods!

Yet the moment she laid eyes on that sheep face, she trembled instinctively.

It was a primal terror rooted at the absolute apex of the cosmic food chain.

In truth, that sheep was indeed preparing to devour her.

—"However, you're still far too small,"-the black sheep had remarked with disdain,-"Better to eat you once you've grown as large as the original host."

*If you phrase it like that, who would ever want to 'grow up'?!*

—"Did you know me when I was alive?"-the Heart Demon—or rather, the entity who still believed herself to be 'Yu Si'—had gathered her courage to ask. If this sheep had seen her while she lived, she resolved never to grow into that form!

—"Alive?"-the black sheep had scoffed with mockery.

Knowing full well the Heart Demon's delusion, it had bluntly dismantled her identity:

—"You are definitely not Yu Si; you don't even recognize me. The original host I refer to is that white-eyed wolf. You are merely his Heart Demon. Once you grow as large as him and drag him into demonic corruption, I can eat you; Master won't fault me for devouring demons."

...*Who on earth are you? Was I supposed to know you?!*

"Yu Si" had thought in indignant fury, though she dared not voice it.

And the black sheep couldn't be bothered to entertain her further, strutting off with its stubby tail wagging smugly.

However, spurred by the black sheep's disdain, "Yu Si" had developed burning curiosity. And because the senior sister in Yan Bailang's heart had been an extraordinary genius, "Yu Si" was far sharper than standard Heart Demons; utilizing Yan Bailang's memories, she audited 'her own' historical records.

*Disaster... I genuinely am not "Yu Si"!*

The moment the Heart Demon realized this truth, she nearly dissipated into thin air from existential shock.

Yet! The authentic Yu Si had been a far more brilliant, radiant, and magnificent woman than the caricature imagined by Yan Bailang.

To murder such a woman—Yan Bailang truly deserved ten thousand deaths!

The Heart Demon firmly concluded.

This murderous resolve stabilized the Heart Demon's ethereal form, cementing her objective to slaughter Yan Bailang—even if it meant being devoured by the black sheep afterward.

—And through this investigation, the Heart Demon discovered who that "black sheep" was.

The Eldest Disciple of the adjacent Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yuwen Xuan.

Within Yan Bailang's memories, he had caught several glimpses of his senior sister clandestinely conferring with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Yuwen Xuan, nearly caught by their master on one occasion. Yan Bailang had even suspected his senior sister harbored an unrequited romance, for Yu Si had named her primary 'beast pet' Senior Brother Yuwen; that beast pet had been terrifyingly formidable, accompanying Yu Si to conquer the Beast Soul Tower.

Yet all evidence pointed to the fact that the 'beast pet' had likely been Yuwen Xuan himself, deliberately disguising himself as a spirit beast to infiltrate the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall for snacks—though back then, he hadn't assumed the form of a black sheep, but rather a majestic, double-horned, white-browed black tiger.

Deducing this, the Heart Demon had tested the waters upon the black sheep's next appearance:

—"Senior Brother Yuwen, since you signed a covenant with Yu Si back then, why not sign one with me as well? I will deliver Yan Bailang and the entire Ten Thousand Beasts Hall straight to your jaws."

—"Who on earth signs covenants with food?"-the black sheep had scoffed,-"Yu Si at least qualified as a human; acting as my servant wasn't beneath my dignity. Besides, I was young and gluttonous back then, foolishly tricked into signing a pact. Now that I have grown up, I would never pull such a brainless stunt—regardless, once Yan Bailang falls to demonic corruption and I devour you, wouldn't the entire cafeteria... the Beast Soul Tower belong to me anyway?"

*I can't see a single trace of you having 'grown up.'*

The Heart Demon had thought.

She found Yuwen Xuan's behavior deeply contradictory.

He genuinely burned to eat her, yet lingered in patient anticipation, seemingly driven by motivations far deeper than merely waiting for her to 'fatten up'—even dismantling her delusions, a risky gamble that could have caused his food to evaporate entirely!

*Or could this be an integral phase of his specialized culinary preparation?*

The Heart Demon couldn't help but shiver.

She had witnessed through Yan Bailang's memories the spectacle of the double-horned black tiger devouring beast souls; frankly, she held zero desire to become his meal.

Yet the black sheep watched her like a hawk, and Yan Bailang was remarkably 'diligent' in digging his own grave.

Knowing she was earmarked as livestock, the Heart Demon lacked operational motivation. While she agreed Yan Bailang deserved death, she had barely lifted a finger; whispering a few cryptic words into his mind was enough to drive Yan Bailang into paroxysms of paranoid delusion.

No—Yan Bailang had long since gone insane.

From the instant he gazed upon his senior sister in the Heart-Interrogating Stone; from the moment he resolved to reopen the Beast Soul Tower—nay, from the day he murdered the senior sister who had treated him like her own blood brother to usurp the Sect Master's seat, Yan Bailang had become his own greatest Heart Demon.

Thus, even if his Heart Demon remained passive, he was frantically shoveling dirt over his own grave.

Just like this very moment!

While the Heart Demon fretted over Yuwen Xuan's phantom-like visits, Yan Bailang continued his hysterical tantrum.

Convinced his palpitations were an assassination plot, beating his gatekeepers senseless was insufficient; he summoned the remaining disciples to interrogate them, butchering a disciple's spirit beast on the spot when it instinctively moved to shield its master. Disregarding the disciples' grief, he bellowed hysterically, forcing them to reconfigure the chamber's defensive wards from scratch.

Following this ordeal, even without the Heart Demon's emotional perception, one could see the smoldering hatred and suppressed killing intent blazing within the disciples' eyes.

The Heart Demon suspected that before Yan Bailang could even be lured into demonic corruption, he might well be backstabbed to death by his own disciples.

For example—

—"Sect Master, Senior Sister Xia Bing and the female disciples have arrived to pay their morning respects..."

Amidst the chaos, a disciple entered from outside, reporting with quivering trepidation.

It was the customary hour.

The cultivation world was rife with rigid decorum, particularly regarding generational hierarchies. Even though the Athletes' Village separated male and female quarters, disciples were obligated to pay respects to their sect elder at the appointed hour.

At least within the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, this was absolute law.

After all, mundane actions of surrounding disciples were warped by Yan Bailang's paranoia into treasonous conspiracies; had the female disciples missed a single morning, heaven knew what grand betrayal he would hallucinate.

Yet even for a daily routine, hearing the report caused Yan Bailang to freeze visibly:

—"Xia Bing?"

Instantly, his expression softened with uncanny serenity.

—"Enough, cease this reconfiguration. Sit down and welcome your senior sister,"-Yan Bailang's tone turned remarkably gentle as he asked his disciples,-"Speaking of which, your senior sister won yesterday's duel as well, correct?"

—"Indeed, Sect Master!"-a disciple flattered hastily,-"Senior Sister Xia Bing swept her opponent decisively, utilizing the very beast-taming arts you excel in, displaying eighty percent of Your Excellency's majestic grace!"

—"...Good child. A splendid child."

Yan Bailang murmured, a faint, seemingly proud smile touching his lips.

Seeing this, the surrounding disciples exhaled in collective relief, though complex undercurrents swirled through their thoughts:

[The Sect Master truly favors Senior Sister Xia Bing!]

[It seems the next Sect Master seat is practically locked in for Senior Sister Xia Bing.]

[Damn it, Xia Bing isn't even his direct disciple; is this favoritism necessary?!]

[Should I seize this chance to curry favor with Senior Sister?]

[If he favors Xia Bing this much, why doesn't he abdicate already? We could all breathe easier.]

A storm of stray thoughts bubbled up, swallowed seamlessly by the Heart Demon.

The Heart Demon couldn't resist snickering.

Mortals were truly farcical creatures!

Living in collective societies, they remained perpetually blind to the inner thoughts of the people standing before them!

Was Yan Bailang displaying favoritism toward Xia Bing? It was pure, unadulterated dread!

For reasons unknown, Yan Bailang saw the reflection of his murdered senior sister, Yu Si, within Xia Bing: her brilliance, her soaring popularity among peers, and her inexplicable commercial ties to the Heavenly Spirit Sect—indeed, Yan Bailang obsessed over Xia Bing's dairy transactions, and obsessed even more over her amicable rapport with Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, every detail reminding him of the sister he had slaughtered.

To this, the Heart Demon could only say: Yan Bailang was hallucinating.

While Yuwen Xuan had shattered her identity, auditing Yan Bailang's memories proved that the authentic 'Yu Si' was undeniably brilliant—otherwise she could never have authored the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*—yet her temperament had been far too gentle, compassionate, and burdened by deep-seated inferiority.

Because she believed a young girl serving as Sect Master could never command authority, she had agreed to the elders' proposal to adopt the archaic title 'Patriarch Yu Si,' leading future generations to confuse her with her master, facilitating Yan Bailang's erasure of her legacy.

Not to mention her fatal flaw of trusting others unconditionally; even after her master tossed her into the Beast Soul Tower as monster bait, she spent years agonizing over having (accidentally) slain her master in self-defense, doting excessively on her junior brother and relying naively on corrupt elders until she was stabbed in the back.

By contrast, that girl Xia Bing...

—"Disciple Xia Bing pays respects to the Sect Master!"

Xia Bing stepped gracefully into the chamber.

Leading a retinue of female disciples, she bowed deeply in greeting.

Her posture appeared impeccably deferential, yet the instant she crossed the threshold, her gaze swept across the chamber's layout with razor sharpness, lingering for several seconds upon the freshly altered array nodes, the vigilance within her eyes hardening perceptibly.

The Heart Demon knew Xia Bing had instantly deduced what had transpired; she had been monitoring the Sect Master's movements with acute precision.

This was the fundamental divergence between Xia Bing and Yu Si.

During the Heart-Interrogating Stone trial, Xia Bing had also wavered over secretly practicing the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* without authorization; yet once she entered the Beast Soul Tower and sniffed out Yan Bailang's murderous intent, she discarded every shred of filial sentiment toward her master, shifting her focus from survival to actively plotting a counter-kill.

At least from the emotional resonance the Heart Demon detected, Xia Bing exuded latent, persistent killing intent toward Yan Bailang.

This girl possessed boundless ambition and decisive ruthlessness; she and Yu Si were entirely different species.

Yet to the paranoid Yan Bailang, such nuances were irrelevant.

He had blamed all his failures and his inability to break through to Mahayana upon his senior sister's lingering curse, convinced her vengeful wraith was stalking him—despite having obliterated her soul beyond reincarnation back then, such that without Zhou Hui's Heart-Interrogating Stone, a Heart Demon wouldn't even have formed—yet Yan Bailang viewed everyone as a vessel possessed by his sister's ghost.

Consequently, even without Xia Bing, he would have projected his sister onto someone else.

Naturally, Yan Bailang harbored intense killing intent toward Xia Bing. He hadn't struck yet partly because Xia Bing was far too sharp, leaving him zero viable openings, and partly due to a sinister calculation brewing in his heart.

He had reached the Body Integration stage by murdering his sister and plundering her *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*; though haunted by a Heart Demon, in the depths of his soul, he had never felt a shred of remorse, regarding Yu Si purely as cultivation fertilizer.

Thus, it was only natural that Yan Bailang conceived identical designs toward Xia Bing.

[Could Xia Bing... also serve as cultivation fertilizer?]

Particularly since Xia Bing had conquered the trials of the Beast Soul Tower, achieving her Golden Core breakthrough within its depths, emerging unscathed from the primordial beast slumbering at the bottom—a feat unprecedented in history! (Regarding this, Xia Bing maintained there was no beast at the bottom, or that it had been asleep; yet Yan Bailang refused to believe it, convinced Xia Bing concealed a monumental secret, while lacking the courage to venture down himself lest he be slain just as his master had been).

Armed with this premise, Yan Bailang dreaded Xia Bing, yet 'anticipated' her even more.

Within his warped, demented brain, a twisted epiphany took root:

[Just like back then—if I seize a suitable opportunity to slaughter Xia Bing, I will surely break through to the Mahayana stage!]

Obsessed with this delusion, he treated Xia Bing with magnified regard and soothing gentleness, as though genuinely preparing to take her as his primary disciple and bequeath the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall to her hands. The surging killing intent flooding his soul was so rich that even the Heart Demon felt thoroughly sated.

Fortunately, whether Xia Bing sensed his designs or not, she did not lower her guard in the slightest. On the contrary, her vigilance spiked, and her latent killing intent thickened proportionally.

Naturally, neither revealed a scrap of their inner malice.

The two monsters beamed warm smiles upon each other, presenting a picture of absolute [loving master and filial disciple](# "Satirical trope: X慈X孝 (parody of 父慈子孝) — hypocritical elders and deceitful juniors smiling sweetly while plotting mutual slaughter").

Alas, the dagger was destined to reveal itself eventually.

Even without the Heart Demon's meddling, the fatal clash approached.

After pleasantries concluded, Yan Bailang asked with paternal warmth:

—"I heard you achieved total victory in yesterday's match?"

—"Merely the preliminaries; the opponent was modest,"-Xia Bing replied with cautious modesty, offering an explanation divergent from the other disciples,-"Furthermore, I secured several potent Scenario Cards within the trial system, capable of emulating the Sect Master's supreme techniques."

—"The trial system?"

—"Indeed, Sect Master. The regulations of this Immortal Arts Assembly... are remarkably innovative,"-Xia Bing reported faithfully, striving to keep her tone neutral,-"Combat prowess on the stage is merely one dimension; the rewards bestowed by the trial system constitute another. For instance, by triumphing in the trial system, I secured diverse auxiliary cards... essentially specialized Dharma artifacts that granted tremendous tactical utility in the arena."

—"And not just myself; numerous practitioners relied on mastering artifacts and trial mechanics to achieve victory against superior adversaries!"-Xia Bing added.

Yan Bailang listened with an air of dignified gravitas, yet his vacant gaze betrayed his utter disinterest.

Adopting an elder's haughty posture, he scoffed:

—"Gimmicks and parlor tricks! The Tireless Immortal Venerable was renowned for his steady, pragmatic composure; now that he commands the Stairway to Heaven, instead of focusing on celestial ascension, he meddles in academic journals and educational reforms, heaven knows what foul schemes he plots."

Sighing grandly as though he held the moral high ground, Yan Bailang delivered paternal instruction:

—"Studying tournament rules to secure victory is acceptable, but you must never take such child's play seriously. A contest of cultivation is decided strictly by raw power."

—"The Sect Master speaks true,"-Xia Bing assented deferentially, scoffing inwardly.

The principle was sound, but coming from Yan Bailang's lips, it was rank hypocrisy.

Xia Bing knew full well this man was posturing; he preached raw power, yet his foundational comprehension of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* was inferior even to hers.

And this superficial arrogance extended to the Immortal Arts Assembly.

Yan Bailang dreamed of using the assembly to gain universal acclaim and expand his social network, yet dragged his feet until the absolute final hour, failing to study a single tournament rule upon arrival, blindly assuming victory was his by right.

When such a charlatan spouted righteous wisdom, nobody could genuinely revere him as a mentor.

[It's no wonder... this tournament is the golden opportunity to end his life.]

Xia Bing thought.

As that thought crossed her mind, the horrifying shadow of the Heart Demon behind Yan Bailang shuddered violently, opening countless eyes—both human and beast—glaring malevolently toward Xia Bing.

Oblivious to the spectral eyes, Xia Bing smiled brightly at Yan Bailang:

—"Against a supreme titan such as the Sect Master, the Heavenly Spirit Sect made a grave error attempting to use you as a stepping stone! You will unquestionably sweep the Exhibition Match!"

—"Exhibition Match?"-Yan Bailang blinked, feeling he had finally uncovered the source of his earlier palpitations.

—"The exhibition matches where titans of the Void Refinement stage and above duel outside official brackets without affecting tournament standings!"-Xia Bing elaborated,-"I heard opponents were drawn during the opening ceremonies. Though we arrived late, did they genuinely fail to notify the Sect Master? How disgraceful of them!"

—"..."-Yan Bailang held his tongue.

Naturally, he would never admit that a grand tournament schedule had hung upon his wall, complete with detailed guidelines, which he had discarded after a single glance, expecting his disciples to remind him on the day of the duel.

Accustomed to her Sect Master's brainless arrogance, Xia Bing pressed no further, delivering the final, fatal blow:

—"Your exhibition match opponent is the Eldest Disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect... Yuwen Xuan."
