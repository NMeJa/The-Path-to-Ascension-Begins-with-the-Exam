# Chapter 193: Continuous 'Surprises'

Cultivators from across various domains were converging upon the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and naturally, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect couldn't remain idle either.

Rather, they were the ones under the heaviest pressure.

Especially the Artifact Refinement Workshop.

—"Senior Brother Guan issued a dead order: every single official disciple of the Artifact Refinement Workshop must obtain a qualification certificate!"-a disciple announced.

—"Uwah——!"-the gathered disciples wailed in unison upon hearing this news.

Although the Artifact Refinement Workshop was where the Heavenly Spirit Sect's most elite disciples congregated, what student under the heavens actually liked taking exams?

The disciples of the Artifact Refinement Workshop voiced their sheer agony:

[—"Where are we supposed to find time for certification exams? The backlog of tasks on our hands is already scheduled fifty-eight years into the future!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"I haven't slept a wink for half a month straight."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"You're already a Golden Core cultivator, what the hell are you sleeping for?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"It must be that crowd from the Formation Pavilion stirring up trouble. Word is they claim the teleportation array is entirely their project."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Tch, then let Senior Sister Ran design the teleportation array herself!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Exactly! We're already swamped as it is—it's not like we're as leisurely as Senior Sister Ran."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Wait a second, aren't you planning to participate in the large-scale teleportation array project?"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Even if I wanted to go, it wouldn't be easy, right? Heard the requirements over there are absurdly high, and it gets in the way of our existing tasks."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"I also feel it's not worth the hassle. After all, it's a teleportation array! It makes sense for the Formation Pavilion folks to be so proactive, but if our Artifact Refinement Workshop goes, we'd just be doing grunt work refining raw materials—completely useless for padding our credentials."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Since you aren't planning to join the large-scale teleportation array project, aren't the qualification certificates a breeze to pass?"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Come to think of it, that's true. Heard this qualification examination is open to the public; considering the level of those outsiders, the lower-tier certificates can't possibly be hard—probably even easier than our monthly exams."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Ah, indeed! If it's just to scrape by with a certificate, the lowest tier, 'Tier 1 Artifact Refinement Worker' certificate, shouldn't even require reviewing to pass, right? Just remember to show up for the test on exam day."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"What a relief, scared me half to death."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Here I thought it was some crucial exam!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Disperse, disperse!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

Relieved expressions broke out across the faces of the Artifact Refinement Hall disciples. Laughing heartily, they scattered in a swarm.

When Huang Xiao from Delicacy Pavilion came by to deliver meals, he happened upon this very scene.

Within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the Artifact Refinement Workshop was practically the only department that faithfully patronized Delicacy Pavilion.

Ever since Huang Xiao and his companions unearthed culinary recipes and manufacturing blueprints for miniature household appliances in the Library Pavilion, most Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples had set up private little kitchenettes inside their dormitories and cave dwellings to [treat their tastebuds](# "Literal CN: 打牙祭 (dǎ yájì) — treat oneself to a lavish feast / satisfy meat cravings"). In particular, the Alchemy Hall seemed to have received immense inspiration: every single day, no one knew what they were baking inside their alchemy cauldrons, but the sweet, novel aroma of cakes perpetually wafted through the air, joined at meal times by the savory fragrance of fried chicken—driving their neighbors opposite them in the Artifact Refinement Workshop to near-tears of ravenous longing.

Yet the Artifact Refinement Workshop was the single busiest department across the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect. The disciples genuinely had no time to cook for themselves, nor did they have the face to constantly mooch snacks from the Alchemy Hall; they could only honestly place delivery orders from Delicacy Pavilion (the campus cafeteria).

Fortunately, with new disciples like Huang Xiao handling chores, Eldest Senior Brother drafting several meal-prep recipes, and the franchise addition of 'One Sorrow Tea' (一悲茶), Delicacy Pavilion's meals were no longer so utterly inedible as before, sparing the disciples of the Artifact Refinement Workshop from losing their will to live.

Delivering meals according to routine today, Huang Xiao witnessed the Artifact Refinement Workshop disciples' nonchalant attitude toward the qualification exam.

Seeing this, Huang Xiao couldn't help but ask an acquainted Artifact Refinement Workshop disciple, Zhao Yuegui, in curiosity:
—"Senior Zhao, do you truly believe that just testing for a Tier 1 certificate is enough?"-Huang Xiao asked.

Hearing this, Zhao Yuegui's expression turned mysterious and profound:
—"I..."-He paused, noticing the sideways glances darting over from around him, before instantly flashing a hearty, sunny grin.-"Of course! This qualification examination is being held for the very first year, and no one knows how things will turn out. Isn't it far better to start from the most elementary exam? Don't you all agree?"

He turned to his fellow workshop peers beside him, eliciting another chorus of cheerful agreement.

Having heard this, Huang Xiao returned to the dormitory and recounted the scene to his roommates.

He voiced his concerns to his dorm mates:
—"If that's the case, all the senior brothers in the Artifact Refinement Workshop will be taking the beginner exams. But I heard each exam hall has a quota on the certificates awarded—should we just observe the situation this year and wait until next year?"-Huang Xiao asked.

Indeed: the disciples of the Beginner Class also had plans to participate in the examinations.

It wasn't that the sect had placed any mandatory targets upon them; rather, 'clever individuals' deduced that similar qualification exams would only multiply going forward, and chances were class advancement evaluations might look at how many certificates one held. To avoid piling a crushing burden onto their future selves, it was best to test for whatever certificates they could ahead of time.

Only, seeing the Artifact Refinement Workshop's overwhelming posture, Huang Xiao couldn't help shrinking back.

To this, Jing Chenjun let out a scoff:
—"If you listen to their nonsense, you're doomed,"-Jing Chenjun scoffed.

—"What do you mean?"-his roommates turned to look at Jing Chenjun.

Jing Chenjun was the most enthusiastic about sect activities in this dormitory: from teaching internships at the White Jade Capital Academy to preparatory operations for the Immortal Arts Assembly, and even the management committee of the Immortals' Bazaar, he had his hand in everything. He knew plenty of people, and his information was by far the best connected.

In fact, he was the one who had urged the Beginner Class disciples to register for the qualification exams in the first place.

Yet regarding the Artifact Refinement Workshop's bizarrely dismissive attitude, Jing Chenjun merely smiled mysteriously, offering no explanation.

—"Once the day of the Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination arrives, you'll see. There'll definitely be a fantastic show to watch,"-Jing Chenjun said.

As it turned out, they didn't even need to wait until the day of the qualification examination.

A few days prior to the qualification exam—and the day before the Tenth Month Festival—Huang Xiao and the others abruptly received a flying crane transmission from Jing Chenjun.

Jing Chenjun was incapable of casting spells himself, yet his paper crane was quite a fine magical treasure. In particular, its recorded voice sounded uncannily lifelike and steeped in emotion:

[—"Help! Help! Something's gone down in the Artifact Refinement Workshop, get over here and save me!"](# "Speaker: Jing Chenjun")

Assuming he had run into some life-threatening crisis, Huang Xiao and the others hastily rounded up their dorm buddies and sprinted to the Artifact Refinement Workshop—only to find their senior brothers and sisters inside locked in an absolute brawl:

[—"...Didn't we all agree to register for Tier 1 Artifact Refinement Worker?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Look who's talking! No wonder you've been so cozy with that rabbit demon next door lately—you knew all along it came from the Southern Domain, didn't you?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Is there anyone here who didn't lie about their registration?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

...

The swarm of Beginner Class disciples looked at each other in bewildered silence. They didn't understand what was going on, nor did they dare mediate the brawl. In the end, Cheng Ziyi contributed two teleportation talismans to fish Jing Chenjun out from under a desk deep inside the workshop.

—"What are they doing?"-Cheng Ziyi asked Jing Chenjun with an odd look.-"If they're bullying you, we can go find the Instructors..."

—"No, that's not it. I merely went to collect the qualification exam registration forms, and they accidentally caught a glimpse,"-Jing Chenjun answered with great difficulty, showing the registration forms clutched in his arms.

Lately, immense numbers of people were registering for the qualification examinations, particularly external cultivators over at the Immortals' Bazaar. As a result, all the Instructors had been mobilized, and even an intern Instructor like Jing Chenjun, who was assigned to the White Jade Capital Academy, was being run ragged all over the place.

His roommates knew about Jing Chenjun's duties, making them all the more puzzled:

—"What's the big deal if they saw the registration forms..."-Cheng Ziyi began.

—"Ah!"-Huang Xiao gasped.

That small gasp had escaped from Huang Xiao. He noticed that the very top form in Jing Chenjun's stack belonged to their familiar Senior Zhao Yuegui. However, unlike what he had publicly claimed about registering for a Tier 1 certificate, written starkly across the application was:

> **Tier 4 Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination Registration Form**

This examination was divided into eight tiers. To prevent sect disciples from recklessly registering beyond their depth, the Beginner Class had an initial registration cap of Tier 2, while the Intermediate Class had an initial cap of Tier 4; only after passing Tier 4 could one proceed to Tier 5 and Tier 6.

Zhao Yuegui was an Intermediate Class disciple—and he had blatantly registered for the highest ceiling available to him.

—"B-But Senior Zhao clearly said last time..."-Huang Xiao couldn't wrap his head around it.

—"Because they wanted to [eliminate competitors](# "Literal CN: 想要竞争对手 (xiǎng yào jìngzhēng duìshǒu) — lit. 'wanted competitors' / wanted to weed out rivals"), of course!"-Jing Chenjun smoothed his ruffled robes.-"In truth, all of them want to take part in that large-scale teleportation array project, so they wanted to hoodwink a few fools into sitting it out. That's exactly why they started tearing into each other just now."

Huang Xiao and the others were struck instantly speechless. Glancing through the stack of registration forms, they discovered practically no one had registered below Tier 4.

—"Why go through all that trouble?"-Huang Xiao muttered.

—"It's not just the Artifact Refinement Workshop. Over at the Formation Pavilion, they're nearly [beating each other's brains out](# "Literal CN: 快要打出狗脑子了 (kuài yào dǎ chū gǒu nǎozǐ le) — fighting so violently they're about to knock out dog brains; a vivid slang idiom for furious infighting") to decide who gets to build the teleportation array, not to mention external cultivators,"-Jing Chenjun sighed.-"That's why I said: take the exam as early as possible! Think about how many junior brothers and sisters joined this year, how many people want to enter our sect, and how many are prepping over at the White Jade Capital Academy! Given our sect's 'wholesome academic atmosphere'—"-he ground his teeth,-"—the qualification exams a few years down the line will only get harder and harder."

Hearing those words, the second-year Beginner Class disciples felt their entire futures plunge into pitch darkness.

Yet there was also someone reveling in others' misery.

—"Thank the heavens I'm a sword cultivator,"-Cheng Ziyi beamed, clutching his spirit sword with glee.

—"Gloat while you can,"-Jing Chenjun sneered coldly.-"Sooner or later, Master will roll out a Tier 8 Sword Cultivator Standardized Exam."

—"Shut your jinx of a mouth!"-Cheng Ziyi snapped.

Cheng Ziyi reached out to pinch Jing Chenjun's lips shut, but froze halfway through the motion as if sensing something, whipping his head toward the mountain gate.

—"What's wrong?"-Jing Chenjun had been smiling pleasantly, but found Cheng Ziyi's reaction rather odd. Only then did he notice his other roommates—even their lowest-profile roommate, Ji Gen, was gazing toward the mountain gate, the vines atop his head standing straight up.

[Ah, they must be sensing something a mortal like me can't perceive, right? Like spiritual energy?]

So Jing Chenjun reasoned, putting on a show of gazing intently toward the mountain gate as well.

Fortunately, Jing Chenjun didn't need to pretend for long. From the mountain gate, the welcoming guest drums resounded. Accompanied by the drumbeats, a faint wind whistled through the sky as a colossal behemoth breached the cloud layer, resembling a whale diving beneath the ocean's surface.

It was, astonishingly, a gargantuan treasure ship.

In the past, such a sight would certainly have awed the sect's disciples—in fact, as the treasure ship journeyed through the Northern, Eastern, and Southern Domains, sects large and small were struck with shock upon seeing it—yet arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, that intimidation didn't work so well.

After all, everyone here had even ridden upon a Kunpeng; in their eyes, this treasure ship was merely mediocre.

Jing Chenjun also breathed a sigh of relief, explaining to his companions:
—"This is likely the treasure ship from Sky City! We received a notice earlier that cultivators from the Northern and Eastern Domains would be visiting our sect for an academic pedagogical tour—"-Jing Chenjun said.

—"Then that's way too arrogant!"-Cheng Ziyi grumbled in displeasure.-"Since they are guests, they should honestly disembark at the outer Qianxing Hall first, and then be escorted inside! How is what they're doing any different from barging straight into someone's home?!"

—"Ah, including the Heavenly Dao Alliance, practically every prominent sect in the cultivation world is aboard; it's only natural they get special treatment, right?"-Huang Xiao whispered. As a demon beast, he wasn't accustomed to the spiritual energy exuded by the treasure ship—particularly since it seemed blended with something far more exalted than ordinary spiritual energy—prompting this little beast to shrink behind Ji Gen.

Cheng Ziyi clicked his tongue.

Jing Chenjun understood what Cheng Ziyi was annoyed about.

Cheng Ziyi hailed from a subordinate cultivation clan belonging to a renowned talisman sect in the Northern Domain, having run away from home to cultivate the sword at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Right now, that grand ship was bound to carry disciples from that sect, or perhaps even Cheng Ziyi's blood relatives.

At present, Cheng Ziyi had only transitioned to sword cultivation for a single year and naturally had achieved nothing grand; if caught by them, no matter how great the Heavenly Spirit Sect was, he would face a severe dressing-down, or in the worst case, even get dragged back home. That was why Cheng Ziyi was so disgruntled.

However, looking at that treasure ship, Jing Chenjun also felt a twinge of disappointment.

Unlike his companions, Jing Chenjun was a complete mortal, hailing from the largest mortal empire in the Northern Domain as an imperial prince and direct heir. Yet in this cultivation realm, mortals were naturally not worth mentioning, not to mention the Northern Domain was home to the highest concentration of cultivation sects. Thus, to a mortal empire, Sky City and the Heavenly Dao Alliance were akin to what the Heavenly Court was to the cultivation world—beings mortals yearned for and could never refuse.

Yet a treasure ship carrying immortals from the great sects was only of this caliber, leaving Jing Chenjun somewhat underwhelmed.

*Or could it be... that the Heavenly Spirit Sect is actually the strongest sect in this world?*

Jing Chenjun suddenly realized.

It wasn't that Jing Chenjun looked down upon his own sect.

Prior to this, Jing Chenjun genuinely loved the Heavenly Spirit Sect—after all, it was the only sect willing to take him in and teach him so much—but growing up in the Northern Domain, even if he wasn't among the immortal sects, he had been steeped in the Northern Domain's environment. Moreover, when he joined the Heavenly Spirit Sect, many of its grounds had yet to be constructed, and Jing Chenjun had never truly entered the inner sanctum of any great sect to study. Subconsciously, he had always felt the Northern Domain's Heavenly Dao Alliance was the true immortal orthodoxy.

Never had Jing Chenjun expected that in a mere year, his horizons would broaden to the point where he looked down even on such a treasure ship.

[Have I... stumbled into an extraordinarily terrifying sect?]

Jing Chenjun mused in belated realization.

Thus, replacing his disappointment in the treasure ship, unprecedented sect pride swelled within Jing Chenjun's chest. Suddenly, his confidence in the true worth of the qualification certificate skyrocketed.

—"Let's go, let's head back,"-Jing Chenjun tugged at Cheng Ziyi's hem.-"Back to prepping for the exams."

—"You could've left out that last sentence,"-Cheng Ziyi grumbled.

He naturally had no desire to watch the treasure ship flaunt its majesty, obediently trailing behind Jing Chenjun.

Huang Xiao also clutched onto Ji Gen, hurriedly following along.

Yet they had only walked a few paces when faint, ethereal, melodious sounds drifted down from the sky—somewhat like music, and somewhat like bird calls—accompanied by a gust of wind carrying a raw, feral scent.

Even Jing Chenjun felt it. He shuddered, feeling the fine hairs on the back of his neck stand on end:
—"What is this..."-Jing Chenjun stammered.

—"A demonic wind?"-Cheng Ziyi gripped his sword hilt.

—"Wait, I don't sense any hostility, and—"-Huang Xiao's eyes widened—though his beady little eyes remained just as tiny no matter how wide he stretched them—his voice turning delightfully surprised:-"—it's the demon race from the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree!"

Just as he cried out, led by several azure phoenixes, countless spirit birds and spirit beasts stepped upon auspicious clouds, surging out from the heavens; some even collided head-on into the sails of the treasure ship!

—This was no joke: the spirit birds and beasts capable of serving as cultivators' mounts were of no small stature; smashing headlong into the ship sent violent tremors through the grand hull.

—"Ah, right! I do recall hearing that in honor of our alliance, the demon race would arrive to celebrate during this Tenth Month Festival. This must be their contingent, right?"-Jing Chenjun said, a beat behind.

—"Not only that,"-the Ghost-Parasite Vines upon Ji Gen's body snaked toward the ground,-"there seems to be something else... far more terrifying..."

As if answering his words, shrill ghostly wails blended into the chorus of hundreds of birds. The low clouds outside the mountain gate were dyed pitch black, accompanied by a ghostly howl laden with operatic cadence:

[—"Immortal Venerable, Zhen has come to visit you, ah, hahahaha——!"](# "Speaker: Ruwang Ghost Emperor")

Amidst that bizarre voice, pattering raindrops pelted down from the clouds.

Only, that "rain" was viscous and pitch black, akin to freshly boiled asphalt. Raining down indiscriminately upon the treasure ship, it glued the ship so firmly in midair that it couldn't advance half a pace closer to the mountain gate.

For a time, the treasure ship resembled a beached whale, stuck in midair in utter disarray, while carrion birds hopped about on its deck as if looking for scraps.

Finally, a hysterical female voice erupted from aboard the treasure ship:

—"Ruwang! Fire Abyss! What is the meaning of this?!"-Goddess Ren Ran shrieked.

Absolute chaos.

* * *

—"Master, the guests have all arrived,"-Ran Deng reported obediently.

She naturally understood that the moment those 'guests' crossed into the borders of the Central Domain, Master was already well aware of their movements. Yet as a disciple, what needed reporting still had to be reported.

Not to mention those guests were truly... somewhat unsightly.

—"The Northern Domain treasure ship arrived this morning, but they disregarded our guides' instructions and refused to dock at Qianxing Hall. Goddess Ren Ran insisted on paying a direct visit to our mountain gate,"-Ran Deng curled her lips in secret annoyance at this point, continuing:-"The demon beasts from the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree were quite obedient, but their comprehension and intellectual capacity are a bit... well, somehow they wandered into the fog and only just emerged from the mountain defense array, slamming right into the treasure ship. Furthermore, there is an unexpected guest: His Excellency Ruwang Ghost Emperor from the Western Domain. He claimed he shared an old bond with you, and without waiting for disciples to announce him, barged straight inside..."

—"Ruwang, is it... He truly doesn't listen to reason. Go comfort the disciples who were frightened by him,"-Zhou Hui instructed. Seeing Ran Deng staring at him in curiosity, he added:-"Ruwang and I do indeed share something of an old acquaintance."

[Master, your explanation explains absolutely nothing.]

Ran Deng grumbled inwardly.

This was the first time she learned that Master also shared an 'old bond' with the notoriously infamous Tribulation Transcendence titan of the Western Domain.

Pity that her master didn't appear inclined to elaborate.

Instead, a mournful weep filled with operatic cadence rang out:
—"'Something of an acquaintance'? How can it be merely 'something'? My former master, how heartless you are, ah~~~~"-the ghostly voice trilled melodramatically.

Those final words were sung out, winding through seven or eight trills, twisting so much that goosebumps broke out all over Ran Deng's body.

Ran Deng couldn't help taking a step back, discovering that Zhou Hui's shadow had turned unnaturally pitch-black, bubbling and gurgling like a swamp. As the bubbles popped, they emitted chilling ghostly qi, eventually coalescing into a humanoid figure.

The figure appeared male, possessing floor-length hair that seemed woven from the black night, with eyes as dark as an abyss. Only his complexion was as jade-white as Zhou Hui's, his height nearly identical to Zhou Hui's, and his face upon closer inspection was also identical to Zhou Hui's...?!

—"Master?!"-Ran Deng retreated two paces.

Zhou Hui glanced with displeasure at that face identical to his own:
—"Don't use my face,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Cruel, cruel! You clearly know Zhen has no face, ah~~~~"-the mournful operatic tune piped up once more.

However, this time Ran Deng saw clearly: that bizarre voice didn't come from the mouth of that 'black-haired Master', but was sung by an odd black bird perched upon his shoulder. Fortunately so—if the other party had used Master's face to emit such bizarre singing, Ran Deng might not have been able to control herself, slipping and smashing a chair right over his head.

Only, that 'black-haired Master' possessed none of Zhou Hui's gentle, cultured warmth, looking as icy and ruthless as a stone statue. Anyone less intimately familiar with their master than Ran Deng wouldn't have realized right away that he had adopted Zhou Hui's features.

Exuding an aura that warned all living beings away, he remained silent upon hearing Zhou Hui's words. He merely waited for the odd bird on his shoulder to completely finish its drawn-out operatic note before reaching out and flicking the bird once.

The odd bird instantly dispersed like black mist and lunged onto its master's face, condensing into a weeping theatrical opera mask that veiled that face identical to Zhou Hui's.

Evidently, this was his greatest 'concession.'

That opera mask also opened its mouth to sing:
—"[Fated foe](# "Literal CN: 冤家 (yuān jiā) — lit. 'enemy/foe', used theatrically/affectionately in traditional Chinese opera to mean 'bitter beloved', 'bane of my existence', or 'heartless darling'"), are you satisfied this time, ah~~~?"-the opera mask sang out.

Ran Deng felt she had sprouted so many [goosebumps](# "Literal CN: 鸡皮疙瘩 (jīpí gēda) — 'chicken skin bumps' (goosebumps); Ran Deng jokes she had so much chicken skin she was turning into a chicken") she was about to turn into a chicken herself.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui let out a sigh as well.

He wondered: back in the third cycle, how on earth had he felt reassured entrusting Guan Ye, Mo Bei, and Mo Xi to this fellow?

Indeed: this was the supreme Tribulation Transcendence titan ruling the Western Domain, Ruwang Ghost Emperor.

And once upon a time, he had been Zhou Hui's ghost servant.

Though Zhou Hui personally felt there was nothing noteworthy about this matter. After all, countless cultivators in the realm kept pets, ghosts, or demons to aid them in battle. In his early years, Zhou Hui was merely an insignificant, powerless Foundation Establishment cultivator with rather unremarkable luck; he had never scored divine-grade pets at bargain prices in immortal markets. Instead, while undertaking a sect mission, he picked up a ghost in an opera playhouse that was just as weak as himself. Seeing that the ghost had never committed evil, yet staying there prevented the mission from concluding, he took it as a servant and studied ghost-taming arts for a time. Only, ghost-related techniques were comparatively hasty and utilitarian, clashing with Zhou Hui's temperament and demanding exorbitant resources, so Zhou Hui later released this ghost servant in a peaceful parting of ways.

Such was the utterly unglamorous backstory between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Ruwang Ghost Emperor.

Zhou Hui assumed Ruwang surely wouldn't like others knowing about his past as a servant; thus, he never brought it up to anyone, nor did he ever seek him out. Consequently, even without counting the cycles of reincarnation, he hadn't seen Ruwang for several thousand years.

Ruwang rarely sought out Zhou Hui either; his abrupt arrival to join the excitement today came as quite a surprise to Zhou Hui.

Zhou Hui asked directly:
—"Ruwang, what did you come here for?"-Zhou Hui asked.

That mask was about to break into song again:
—"[Fated foe](# "Literal CN: 冤家 (yuān jiā) — theatrical opera trope for 'bitter beloved' or 'bane of my existence'")——"-the mask trilled.

—"Speak in your own voice!"-Zhou Hui's spiritual pressure swept over without mercy, muting that annoying mask on the spot.

Ruwang Ghost Emperor tilted his head, wearing the look of someone struggling to organize his words:
—"..., ......, ........."-he stammered in complete silence, utterly lost for words.

Evidently, this fellow would be incapable of producing any coherent output in the short term. The corner of Zhou Hui's mouth twitched, feeling it would be much faster to interrogate him directly.

Yet before Zhou Hui could say anything more, another Tribulation Transcendence titan barged in:

—"Untiring, is this how you treat your guests?!"-Goddess Ren Ran snapped as she stormed inside.

She appeared to have suffered no small humiliation outside the mountain gate, looking thoroughly flustered and exasperated. Yet having come with the intention of causing trouble for Zhou Hui and determined to deliver a display of dominance, she hadn't forgotten her lavish regalia.

Thus, one could see this goddess balancing a headdress resembling an [illegal construction project](# "Literal CN: 违章建筑 (wéizhāng jiànzhù) — humorous modern Chinese slang describing an absurdly tall, over-engineered, towering architectural hairstyle or headdress") atop her head, draped in shimmering robes, trailing a long procession of handmaidens holding flowers, incense, lanterns, fans, and parasols—looking for all the world like a courtesan parade through the pleasure quarters.

Pity that Goddess Ren Ran barging in so recklessly had disrupted her formation, leaving only a single handmaiden holding a grand imperial canopy closely flanking the goddess.

Worst of all, someone was actually perched atop that canopy!

A young man dressed in magnificent multicolored robes—garments even more ostentatious than the illegal construction atop the goddess's head. Strangely enough, not a single soul noticed him! Neither the handmaiden holding up the canopy, nor the Tribulation Transcendence goddess below.

The young man clearly knew this. Thus, lounging like a monkey, he leaned nonchalantly against the canopy, his two long legs dangling straight down, happening to obscure more than half of the goddess's face.

Now that the goddess had entered the hall, the young man showed zero intention of adjusting his posture. He merely peered nervously at Zhou Hui for several moments; seeing Zhou Hui looking helplessly at Goddess Ren Ran without noticing him in the slightest, the young man grew even more relaxed, grinning and winking at Ran Deng—whose face had turned ashen the second he appeared.

Ran Deng nearly passed out on the spot:

[Yuwen Xuan, you motherfucker, I let you down, NOT so you could pull this kind of shit!!!]
