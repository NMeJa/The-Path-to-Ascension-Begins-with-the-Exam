# Chapter 134: The Parents (第134章 家长们)

[← Previous Chapter](133_Chapter_133.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](135_Chapter_135.md)

---

—"Why can't any of these kids ever give me a single moment of peace?"-Jian Haoge grumbled.

He sat at the highest vantage point of Sky City, watching an arc of flying rainbow leap across the firmament.

Those were the "footprints" left behind by Jian Nuo's flying horses.

Even in the ancient cultivation era, the vicinity of Heavenly Lake in the Northern Domain—the very site where Sky City now stood—was the prime breeding ground for the finest winged celestial steeds.

Yet when even the immortal cranes of this region were mercenary profit-seekers, let alone the wild, untamable celestial horses, procuring a steed commensurate with one's cultivation was no easy task, even for a Void Refinement cultivator.

Yet Jian Nuo owned an entire ranch of them. Whenever this herd of celestial steeds surged forth en masse, the entire sky was painted in iridescent rainbow hues, breathtakingly spectacular.

Naturally, there were those who were bitter about it. Su Xin, for instance, felt it was only natural for Young Master Jian of Sky City to possess such a ranch—much like the flock of immortal cranes hitched to his own cloud carriage. Hadn't he gotten those simply by acting spoiled before Lord Mei and Lady Zhu?

What few people knew was that Jian Nuo had procured those celestial steeds entirely on his own.

Not just the celestial steeds, but all of Jian Nuo's artifacts, daily expenses, and spirit stones had been earned with his own two hands.

Because back when Jian Nuo was still in the Qi Condensation stage, he had a massive blowout row with his great-grandfather, Sky City Lord Jian Haoge, after which Jian Haoge cut off all of Jian Nuo's allowances and supplies.

Jian Haoge had originally assumed that Jian Nuo would swiftly bend the knee, just like his other sons and grandsons. He never imagined that Jian Nuo would stubbornly claw his way up to Nascent Soul entirely by himself, showing not the slightest intention of admitting defeat.

—Granted, one could hardly deny that the bonus clout of being "Young Master Jian" made "working hard" considerably easier for someone like him than for ordinary cultivators. Even so, for an exalted young lord of Sky City, achieving this much was genuinely remarkable.

For this reason, Jian Haoge could not interfere with Jian Nuo's itinerary.

Or more precisely, if Jian Haoge insisted on interfering, he certainly possessed the power to do so—provided he was willing to risk Jian Nuo severing ties with him completely and abandoning Sky City forever.

Jian Haoge, of course, had no desire to push things that far.

In fact, the very fact that he tacitly regarded Jian Nuo as his successor already proved that he acknowledged Jian Nuo's capability.

In response to Jian Haoge's convoluted inner musings, a chuckling voice chimed in:

[—"He's the one who takes after you the most!"](# "Speaker: City Spirit of Sky City")

—"As if! Back in my day, I was—"-Jian Haoge retorted with a touch of [tsundere pride](# "Literal CN: 傲娇 (àojiāo) — tsundere; haughty and standoffish while secretly caring"), only to halt mid-sentence and eye the speaker with deep suspicion.-"You aren't mocking me, are you?"

—"Not at all! I'm praising you as hard as I possibly can,"-the other voice replied.

The speaker was an exquisite beauty.

Her features were like Sky City itself, perched high above the glacial peaks—pure white, divine, and breathtaking. And indeed, that impression was entirely accurate: she was the City Spirit of Sky City, a being known as a Land Deity.

It was just that this beautiful Land Deity held very little reverence for the City Lord of Sky City. Beaming sweetly, she said:
—"I can hardly say you were actually worse than him, can I? Before Golden Core, you were an utterly hopeless cause! You only buckled down after losing to Untiring in a duel—ah, 'buckling down' is too generous. You pulled plenty of dirty tricks, only to end up thoroughly conquered by him in the end and trailing right behind his heels, scaring your father into thinking you swung that way..."

—"Shut up, shut up, shut up! We agreed never to bring up the past!"-Jian Haoge shouted, his face flushed crimson.

Sky City's spirit giggled, sinking half of her body into the wall.

This was precisely why "Land Deities" were so exasperating.

Though their personal combat prowess was far from formidable, even Tribulation Transcendence titans had once been young and green. None of the embarrassing blunders from their youth could escape the eyes of a local deity. No wonder in the ancient cultivation era, when this world was still tethered to the True Immortal Realm, the Land Deities held [official celestial civil service posts](# "Literal CN: 编制 (biānzhì) — government staff quota / official establishment post") in the upper court.

However, across the long millennia since the Ascension Stairway fractured, Land Deities could no longer report to the Heavenly Court above, and many had sunk into slumber.

Sky City's spirit, on the other hand, was a new Land Deity born after the God-Demon War, meaning she fell outside that archaic bureaucratic hierarchy entirely.

Jian Haoge glared at her, thoroughly disinclined to take a trip down memory lane.

Forcing the conversation back on track, he asked stiffly:
—"Speaking of which, who did Nuo'er take with him?"

—"What do you mean, 'who'?"-the City Spirit asked with a cheerful smile.

—"The ones accompanying him to the Central Domain. He couldn't have actually gone all by himself, could he?"-Jian Haoge asked. With the City Spirit present, nothing that transpired within the city could escape Jian Haoge's eyes.-"Who did he take? His gang of fair-weather cronies?"

—"Not really. Since it seemed to be a spur-of-the-moment decision, and chasing after a girl felt a bit embarrassing, he didn't even tell his friends. Right now, barely anyone in the city knows he went to the Central Domain,"-the City Spirit replied, her eyes rolling as she surveyed the city's happenings.-"And like I said just now! He's actually far more dependable than you were back then. He did bring along a few high-tier cultivators: aside from bodyguards, there are several array masters and artifact refiners. By the looks of it, he plans to check out the Ascension Stairway on the way."

Hearing this, Jian Haoge's expression softened slightly.

He appeared to filter out the part where the City Spirit disparaged him, asking further:
—"Array masters and artifact refiners? Did he bring craftsmen who construct airships, then? Or related specialists?"

[—"None of those either. On the contrary, because all the prominent artifact refiners in the city have been dispatched to build airships, he only took a handful of sidelined old pedants."](# "Speaker: City Spirit of Sky City")

—"What can they possibly accomplish?"-Jian Haoge's face crumpled in annoyance once more.-"He couldn't have bought into the nonsense spouted by certain factions in the city, thinking the Ascension Stairway will trigger a grand migration of cultivation sects, and gone to pick flaws in the Ascension Stairway just to offend the Heavenly Spirit Sect, did he?"

—"Eh? It won't trigger a migration?"-the City Spirit asked.

—"Of course not,"-Jian Haoge paused in surprise.-"Don't tell me you believed that rubbish too?"

—"Heheh,"-the City Spirit chuckled guiltily, averting her eyes.

Over the past few days, listening to the humans' endless discussions, she had grown somewhat uneasy.

After all, appearing alongside the Ascension Stairway was White Jade Capital!

Ever since Sky City came into being, people had constantly drawn comparisons between it and White Jade Capital.

Those praising it would marvel: "Surely the legendary White Jade Capital was merely something like Sky City!" Those who disliked it would scoff: "If this were White Jade Capital, it would never do such-and-such."

Even for a city spirit, hearing such comparisons day in and day out would instill fear!

What was more, she was not a Land Deity born from the actual earth, but an airborne castle—quite literally—reliant entirely on cultivators to sustain it. If all the cultivators packed up and left, that would mark her demise.

Seeing the City Spirit's expression, Jian Haoge sighed:
—"You don't need to worry. The spiritual energy pollution in the Central Domain is far too severe. Even if the Ascension Stairway has reappeared, that miasma cannot simply be purged overnight. Taking ten thousand steps back, even if Zhou Hui possessed heaven-defying might to restore the Central Domain, he couldn't single-handedly give birth to hundreds of millions of people."

[—"Population? Do you mean the mortal dynasties of the Northern Domain? But they're just mortals; they don't even qualify to set foot up here."](# "Speaker: City Spirit of Sky City")

—"Yet cultivators emerge from mortals. Even Untiring was merely a mortal at the beginning!"-Jian Haoge said.-"As long as there are mortals, there is a steady stream of cultivators. Therefore, as long as the largest mortal populations remain in the Northern Domain, the geopolitical landscape of the cultivation realm will not shift. Even if the Central Domain truly recovers one day, it will merely mean the Central Domain's mortals have birthed a new center; it will never erase the existence of the Northern Domain's cultivation world."

—"Oh,"-the City Spirit nodded, half-comprehending.

—"Still, if even you bought into those rumors, those fellows must be stirring up quite a racket,"-Jian Haoge snorted coldly.

This was a predictable state of affairs.

Although Zhou Hui had opened access to the Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital, that was only alluring to those [hyper-competitive grinders](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — involution champions / relentless tryhards") who still clung to dreams of ascension. Not all cultivators wished to trudge endlessly along that arduous path.

In particular, those clinging to earthly power and thoroughly contented with their current status and privileges—such as the upper-echelon cultivators of Sky City and the Heavenly Dao Alliance, or the prominent cultivation clans of the Northern Domain—felt profound panic at this abrupt disruption. Consequently, all manner of rumors sprouted like weeds.

The reason Jian Haoge and the Mei-Zhu couple had coordinated to proactively construct grand airships—allowing those eager to see for themselves to visit the Central Domain—was precisely to use harsh reality to demonstrate to everyone:

Ascension? You're daydreaming!

Even with the Ascension Stairway, you lot might not even keep up with *that man's* footsteps.

After all, that was the Untiring Immortal Venerable!

Yet evidently, certain factions were already itching to stir up trouble in the shadows.

Jian Haoge muttered:
—"I thought Nuo'er was merely talking tough, and would in fact bring along a few array masters skilled in airship construction to survey the route on the sly!"

—"Don't take my saying he takes after you seriously! That was mere flattery. Little Jian Nuo isn't nearly as perverse and awkward as you,"-the City Spirit roasted, unfamiliar with modern terms like 'tsundere.'-"Besides, aren't you overthinking things? He already made it clear he didn't care about building airships—the boy just went to chase after his future wife!"

—"*Tsk!*"

[—"I thought you were quite fond of that little miss from the Qingyang Clan?"](# "Speaker: City Spirit of Sky City")

—"She's fine. The moment she laid eyes on you, she recognized you as the City Spirit instead of getting jealous over bizarre misunderstandings. Furthermore, her cultivation and talent match Nuo'er's, so she won't suffer an early death midway,"-Jian Haoge shook his head.-"However, the Qingyang Clan felt from the very beginning that Sky City was too far away and wasn't keen on the match. And if his rival happens to be Untiring's disciple—"

[—"Haha, [you're chickening out](# "Literal CN: 怂 (sǒng) — cowardly / chickening out")!"](# "Speaker: City Spirit of Sky City")

—"Huh?!"

—"You're admitting defeat! You think your boy can't beat Untiring's disciple!"-the City Spirit crowed gleefully.

—"The hell I am! He's at Tribulation Transcendence, but am I not at Tribulation Transcendence too?! I haven't lost—"

[—"You're chickening out! You're scared!"](# "Speaker: City Spirit of Sky City")

—"Damn it all, I never should have spoken with a brainless spirit! I need to notify the Central Domain and tell them to receive that brat... Hopefully Zhou Hui isn't engrossed in studying the Ascension Stairway; knowing that fellow, entering secluded cultivation right on top of it is entirely within character,"-Jian Haoge hollered at the top of his lungs, attempting to drown out the City Spirit's taunts with sheer volume.

---

Thus, a quarter of an hour later, Zhou Hui found himself staring at a fuming Jian Haoge through the cloud mirror—which was hardly surprising.

Not that Zhou Hui thought much of it.

Ever since he first made Jian Haoge's acquaintance, every time he saw the man, Jian Haoge was either actively throwing a tantrum or getting ready to throw one. To borrow a certain trope from another world, Jian Haoge was practically an outdated-version tsundere young lady.

Zhou Hui had never harbored any interest in coddling high-maintenance young ladies.

He cut straight to the chase:
—"Is something the matter? Speak quickly."

—"What do you mean by that?! Am I forbidden from contacting you unless something's the matter?"-Jian Haoge rambled on as usual, even glancing around the projection.-"I knew it, the Ascension Stairway! Don't tell me you haven't stepped foot down from there once this entire time?"

—"If there is nothing, I am hanging up,"-Zhou Hui said, sweeping his sleeve toward the cloud mirror.

—"Wait! Of course there's something!"-Jian Haoge bellowed, knowing full well Zhou Hui would genuinely sever the connection.-"My kid went to the Central Domain! He might head for the Heavenly Spirit Sect—keep an eye on him for me!"

—"? Your kid?"-Zhou Hui blinked in mild surprise.

He regarded Jian Haoge with an expression that suggested he wanted to speak, yet hesitated out of tact.

Jian Haoge had been scrutinizing Zhou Hui's expression and naturally knew what he was thinking:
—"My wife and son had mediocre aptitudes and passed away long ago. There's nothing taboo about saying that! But what you probably don't know is that sons have grandsons, and grandsons have great-grandsons!"

—"...I am well aware of how biology works,"-Zhou Hui murmured dryly.

—"Among my great-grandsons, there is a boy with exceptional talent. Barring accidents, he should be my successor,"-Jian Haoge stated, publicly acknowledging Jian Nuo for the first time—even if it was before his (unilateral) arch-rival.-"The boy is named Jian Nuo, which is precisely the one who ran off to the Central Domain."

At this point, Jian Haoge suddenly remembered the City Spirit's mocking cries of "admitting defeat." Flushing red, he deliberately provoked:
—"He went to find his beloved, the young miss of the Qingyang Clan! I hear you accepted her as a temporary disciple!"

He glared intently at Zhou Hui as he spoke, waiting for Zhou Hui to argue about the betrothal so he could stand up for his great-grandson.

Zhou Hui's reaction, however, left him utterly bewildered.

—"Qingyu's... Ah, *that* Jian Nuo!"-Zhou Hui said, enlightened.

Truth be told, had Jian Haoge not brought it up, Zhou Hui truly hadn't connected "the Father of the Red Rose" with "Jian Haoge's descendant."

Yet Zhou Hui didn't harbor many thoughts about the Father of the Red Rose.

After all, the fathers of such high-born young ladies were essentially another walking trope.

For some inexplicable reason, the fathers of various harem members in stallion novels—especially those belonging to [fair, rich, and beautiful](# "Literal CN: 白富美 (báifùměi) — fair-skinned, rich, and beautiful; ideal wealthy maiden") young misses—were doting fathers who loved their daughters dearly and remained devoted to their wives, thus having no sons, stopping just short of packaging their entire family estates and handing them to the protagonist on a silver platter.

The Father of the Red Rose was no exception.

As the City Lord of Sky City who concurrently held the title of Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, directing the entire cultivation realm in repelling the demon invasion, he practically gave off the vibe that even if the protagonist achieved nothing, he would gladly gift the entire cultivation world to him anyway.

However, the Heavenly Dao's script hadn't dedicated much ink to him. Most of the time, he was merely an NPC handing out quests and subsidies to the protagonist. Although some readers in the book's comment section had argued that this character harbored hidden motives, Zhou Hui never read web novel comments, so he had never bothered studying such marginal, peripheral characters who seemed forgotten in the late-game arcs.

Thinking about it now, though, it was rather strange.

And so Zhou Hui asked directly:
—"Speaking of which, where did you go?"

—"Huh?"

—"If Jian Nuo became the City Lord of Sky City, where were you? Did you ascend?"-Zhou Hui asked, puzzled.

—"Well, well! Zhou Hui, are you cursing me to die?!"-Jian Haoge roared, spitting out Zhou Hui's true name in fury.

Ignoring him, Zhou Hui murmured:
—"Indeed, death is the far more probable outcome."

—"Zhou Hui!"-Jian Haoge bellowed.

Zhou Hui was merely making an objective deduction.

After all, less than three hundred years remained until the events of the Heavenly Dao's script—events that had already occurred in his previous incarnation. The protagonist was due to arrive soon. Right now, Jian Haoge was alive and kicking, yet by the time the original story began, Jian Nuo was already serving as the City Lord of Sky City. Hence, Jian Haoge's whereabouts were genuinely baffling.

Nevertheless, Zhou Hui believed Jian Haoge had still remained in this world; at the very least, his "corpse" had.

After all, a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator was an entity of astronomical spiritual mass. Had he ascended, then when the world was reset, pulling an object of such colossal mass as Jian Haoge back down from the upper realm would have certainly been felt by Zhou Hui.

So, what could have caused Jian Haoge to "die" during this intervening period?

Perhaps the answer lay with Jian Nuo?

And that brought up another peculiarity regarding Jian Nuo: how on earth had he become the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance?

The incumbent Alliance Leader was naturally alive and well. That part was trivial, of course—the Heavenly Dao Alliance operated much like a corporation, where the Alliance Leader was merely a CEO, while the controlling board members were the Mei-Zhu couple; if they truly wished to replace him, they could.

The problem was that Jian Nuo was the Lord of Sky City!

Although both the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City were based in the Northern Domain, they belonged to two distinct factions with inherent conflicts of interest regarding the division of cultivation turf. Thus, Jian Nuo becoming the Alliance Leader felt somewhat like the big boss of a family enterprise running next door to become CEO of a rival firm. Had the Heavenly Dao Alliance been acquired by Sky City?

Zhou Hui stared intently at Jian Haoge, lost in contemplation.

Under Zhou Hui's unblinking gaze, goosebumps broke out across Jian Haoge's skin.

He vividly remembered that whenever Zhou Hui discovered some peculiar demonic beast or strange creature, he had this exact same look in his eyes.

[This lunatic couldn't have suddenly gotten the urge to dissect a Tribulation Transcendence titan just to see what's inside, could he?]

[I swear he is genuinely capable of doing that!]

Jian Haoge promptly forgot all about the custody battle over Qingyang Qingyu. Before departing, however, he did not forget to follow standard protocol and drop a parting threat:
—"A-Anyway, once my boy reaches your territory, if he loses so much as a single hair, I'll hold you personally responsible!"

With that bluster, his silhouette vanished cleanly from the other end of the cloud mirror.

Seeing Jian Haoge flee, Zhou Hui finally snapped out of his reverie.

He turned to look at Mo Xi, who was idling beside him:
—"Xi'er, why don't you make a trip back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"Sure!"-Mo Xi agreed with unprecedented enthusiasm this time.

In truth, ever since White Jade Capital was relocated to orbit the Ascension Stairway, traveling between the Ascension Stairway and the Heavenly Spirit Sect had become exceedingly convenient, which was why Mo Xi had been able to lounge lazily by his master's side.

This time, however, was different.

Going back meant getting front-row seats to the juicy drama between Senior Sister Qingyu and Second Senior Brother! Mo Xi was naturally thrilled to scurry back and stir up trouble.

After all, opportunities to enjoy gossip about Second Senior Brother were exceedingly rare!

—"Should I go back to notify Second Senior Brother to prepare to receive the Young Master of Sky City?"-Mo Xi asked excitedly.-"Should we summon Senior Sister Qingyang over as well? She's been spending quite a lot of time in the secret realms lately; it'd be awful if Young Master Jian couldn't find her at either place."

Zhou Hui cast a bemused glance at his overly excited youngest disciple.

Mo Xi hastily reined in his grin, trying his best to look grave:
—"*Ahem*, on second thought, specially calling Senior Sister Qingyang back might be a bit dramatic. It'd be enough to have Second Senior Brother escort the man over to Eternal Villa."

[When that happens, should I go watch the [drama battlefield](# "Literal CN: 修罗场 (xiūluóchǎng) — Asura field / messy romantic battlefield") in person?]

[Although Second Senior Brother would never fight some young master over Senior Sister Qingyang, seeing how Senior Sister Qingyang is hopelessly addicted to homework and has zero interest in play, she clearly has no intention of returning to the Northern Domain. At that point, wouldn't Second Senior Brother serve as the perfect on-site human shield?]

[And if Senior Sister Qingyang is too earnest to think of that herself, little Xi'er can helpfully remind her!]

[When that happens, the look on Second Senior Brother's face will definitely be... Hahaha!]

Mo Xi's mind spun an entire theatrical blockbuster of romantic rivalry and chaos.

Of course, Mo Xi harbored no genuine malice toward his Second Senior Brother. It was merely petty grievances like Qu Qianxing docking his budget, bossing him around for labor under the guise of seniority, confiscating his pocket money, reprimanding him for poor test scores... minor trifles like that. Mo Xi wasn't petty enough to hold a grudge!

—"No, let's call Qingyang over after all. Since Jian Nuo came for her sake, it is best that she be present,"-Zhou Hui said.-"Though then again, Jian Nuo might not visit the Heavenly Spirit Sect first. He may very well head straight to Eternal Villa, in which case there will be no need for Qingyang to come."

[...It seems Master has zero intention of playing matchmaker for Second Senior Brother and Senior Sister Qingyang at all!]

[Then again, the maiden had no interest and the gentleman harbored no desire; it was merely Villa Master Qingyang acting on a one-sided impulse.]

Mo Xi felt a slight twinge of disappointment.

—"More importantly, the final exams just wrapped up recently, so your Second Senior Brother should be quite swamped. Go give him a hand,"-Zhou Hui instructed casually.-"Also, call Shulong over."

—"Fifth Senior Brother? Why him?"-Mo Xi's ears pricked up once more.

—"There is a small matter,"-Zhou Hui replied without elaborating.

---

Zhou Hui sought Lu Shulong, naturally, for information regarding "what transpired in the past."

After all, in that Heavenly Dao script that had already unfolded once in their previous lives, Lu Shulong might remember what had happened to Jian Haoge.

Hearing Zhou Hui's inquiry, Lu Shulong shook his head:
—"The City Lord of Sky City? I apologize, Master. In that life, I lived in a muddle-headed haze and had no heart to concern myself with worldly affairs. I didn't even know Jian Nuo had become the Lord of Sky City... I am truly utterly useless."-Lu Shulong's face filled with profound shame.

—"There is no need to be so hard on yourself; I was merely asking casually,"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"We are situated far from the Northern Domain. It is only natural that you wouldn't know."

[—"Master..."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

The more comforting Zhou Hui tried to be, the deeper the shame grew on Lu Shulong's face.

Yet speaking of Sky City, Lu Shulong was not entirely devoid of memories. In fact, a deep impression from an entirely different dimension reminded him of some bizarre possibilities.

—"If I really had to say, Master... It's not that I hold any grudge against Second Senior Brother! Nor do I mean to slander him,"-Lu Shulong stammered hesitantly.-"B-But do you remember? In the third reincarnation, where did you entrust Second Senior Brother? I feel that if an accident truly occurred, could it be... Second Senior Brother's doing?"

—"But wasn't that back in the third life?"-Zhou Hui remarked with a peculiar expression.

It was true: in the third reincarnation, Qu Qianxing had indeed been entrusted by Zhou Hui to Jian Haoge.

After all, for Qu Qianxing, who hailed from a mortal human background, a place like Sky City was far more suitable... As if!

In that life, Qu Qianxing fully leveraged his status as Jian Haoge's "adopted son" to claw his way into the seat of Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance. Then, like a cat toying with a cornered mouse, he exacted ruthless vengeance upon the imperial family of the mortal dynasty, throwing the mortal realms of the Northern Domain into utter chaos. He enraged Jian Haoge so thoroughly that the man complained to Zhou Hui every other day—yet even so, Jian Haoge never disguised his admiration for Qu Qianxing, declaring outright that he was countless times better than his own offspring! It was also this very Qu Qianxing who mobilized the entire cultivation world to scour the earth for the "benefactor" who had saved him back then (that life's Zhou Hui had been very careful not to leave behind a shred of evidence regarding himself, acting entirely like [the Little Mermaid](# "Literal CN: 整个小美人鱼的状态 — referring to Hans Christian Andersen's Little Mermaid saving the prince unseen and leaving no trace")). One could almost say that in leading that life's "protagonist" straight to the side of a Heavenly-Spirit-Sect-less Zhou Hui, Qu Qianxing's "meritorious service" was undeniable.

Wait a minute. Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance?

Qu Qianxing becoming Alliance Leader as Jian Haoge's adopted son?

That career trajectory bore an uncanny resemblance to Jian Nuo's! Had the Heavenly Dao's script plagiarized Qu Qianxing's exploits and deliberately assigned a similar trajectory to Jian Nuo, or had Qu Qianxing done something?

Zhou Hui felt a headache brewing, his stomach practically knotting in sympathetic phantom pain.

Lu Shulong asked astutely:
—"Master, should I summon Second Senior Brother over?"
