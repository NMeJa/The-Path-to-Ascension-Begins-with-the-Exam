# Chapter 49: All According to Plan (第49章 计划通)

—"Just what on earth is Thousand Beasts Hall doing...?"-Zhang Hongjing groaned, his face buried flat against his desk amidst a room buzzing with clamoring paper cranes and talisman slips.

He was so troubled—so agonizingly troubled that he had to fight with every fiber of his being not to split his face into a massive grin and burst out laughing.

Yes, laughing.

Although everyone assumed that he, as Sect Leader of the Thousand Harmony Gang, must be suffering immensely caught in the crossfire between titans, Zhang Hongjing privately felt Thousand Beasts Hall had performed a magnificent deed:

He had finally found an ironclad excuse not to [take sides](# "Literal CN: 站队 (zhànduì) — line up behind a faction; pick a side in factional politics") with Carefree Palace, haha!

One had to know: prior to today, as the premier faction of the Central Domain, the Thousand Harmony Gang had faced the exact same extortion from Carefree Palace, demanding he take the lead in severing diplomatic ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Yet unlike Yan Bailang—who had chosen Carefree Palace without a second thought and merely fretted over how to sever ties at the lowest cost—Zhang Hongjing had zero desire to take sides.

After all, the Thousand Harmony Gang and Carefree Palace held no genuine cooperative partnership.

As a loose organization primarily comprised of rogue cultivators and structured akin to a merchant guild, the Thousand Harmony Gang was virtually self-sufficient within the Central Domain. They had none of Thousand Beasts Hall's vital, life-or-death dependence on external imports like beast fodder. Although they engaged in commercial cooperation with Carefree Palace on public affairs like annual auctions, they possessed their own internal supply channels and interdependent networks across the Central Domain. To a certain extent, they were even direct competitors with Carefree Palace. Naturally, they had no compelling reason to line up behind them.

In fact, had the Thousand Harmony Gang possessed even an ounce of internal unity, they could have seized this golden opportunity to drive Carefree Palace's influence completely out of the Central Domain and replace them.

Regrettably, the Thousand Harmony Gang was not [harmonious](# "Wordplay on 万合帮 (Wanhe Gang) — the Gang of Ten Thousand Harmonies / Ten Thousand Mergers is anything but united") in the slightest.

Despite having a prime opening to expand their power, ever since Carefree Palace severed the trade routes, one day this protector would report that so-and-so merchant caravan running the border was cut off from supplies and facing ruin, and the next day that elder would whine that without Carefree Palace's subsidies and funding, so-and-so immortal city was running a deficit—as if the Central Domain's number one gang only existed on Carefree Palace's charity!

Zhang Hongjing simply couldn't fathom it:

Back when he had first befriended these individuals, everyone had been candid, forthright, and righteous souls. How had several decades of serving as elders and protectors of the Thousand Harmony Gang transformed them into Carefree Palace's [lackeys](# "Literal CN: 马仔 (mǎzǎi) — Cantonese/slang for errand boys, goons, or stooges")?

It wasn't as if they were blind to Carefree Palace's clandestine atrocities!

Everyone had climbed up from the bottom as humble rogue cultivators; Zhang Hongjing felt their sect ought to be the most acutely aware of the sordid deeds Carefree Palace perpetrated under the guise of Fallen Immortal Palace. Driving up prices through speculative hoarding, dumping low-price goods, and unscrupulous racketeering were mere trifles. Even trafficking mortals, poaching spirit beasts, and refining human furnaces were hardly the worst of it. The truly horrifying horrors were those "breeding farms" and "alchemy cauldrons" they operated deep within the Abyss Sea to artificially spike cultivation bases!

Zhang Hongjing never considered himself an awe-inspiring paragon of justice who hated evil like an enemy. He lacked both the courage and the capability to openly oppose Carefree Palace; he didn't even dare leak their dirty laundry. Yet he maintained his own bottom line: at the very least, he would not collude in evil or become their [ghostly accomplice](# "Literal CN: 伥鬼 (chāngguǐ) — chang ghost; the ghost of someone eaten by a tiger who lures more victims to the tiger; a sycophantic henchman aiding a monster").

Yet even this modest bottom line looked increasingly untenable.

And the pressure stemmed not from outsiders, but from his own trusted peers.

When all was said and done, Zhang Hongjing was simply too weak.

As Sect Leader of the Central Domain's largest faction, he possessed a mere Void Refinement cultivation base. Several elders within the gang could counterbalance and constrain him. The Thousand Harmony Gang had been erected entirely on the foundation of Zhang Hongjing's personal prestige and popularity among rogue cultivators.

—In truth, Zhang Hongjing harbored private suspicions that the gang's present stature had little to do with his supposed popularity, and everything to do with Carefree Palace needing a surrogate faction to manage the Central Domain's cultivation scene—a faction they wished to keep fragmented and loose so it could never grow into a threat. That was why they had chosen the Thousand Harmony Gang.

Yet suspicion was merely suspicion; Zhang Hongjing had no desire to uncover the truth.

Ignorance permitted self-deception.

Regrettably, Carefree Palace's blockade of the trade routes had backed Zhang Hongjing to the very edge of the precipice. He faced only two choices: either line up behind Carefree Palace, feigning ignorance until he was entirely reduced to their puppet; or refuse to pick a side, and witness [harsh reality](# "Literal CN: 人间真实 (rénjiān zhēnshí) — internet slang for bitter, unvarnished real-world truth") unfold before his eyes.

It was precisely at this juncture that Thousand Beasts Hall's [moronic](# "Literal CN: 2X掌门 (èrxī zhǎngmén) — 2B/dumbass sect leader, platform self-censored slang for an utter idiot") sect leader, for reasons known only to heaven, dashed off to invite the Untiring Immortal Venerable, sparking Heavenly Spirit Sect's intention to host this year's auction. Were Yan Bailang standing before him right now, Zhang Hongjing would have planted a massive, slobbering kiss on his cheeks:

[My great benefactor!]

[My survival is entirely thanks to you, big brother!]

Compared to refusing to take sides and ending up dead, what was a room buzzing with frantic paper cranes? A trifle!

Naturally, jubilation over cheating death aside, superficial misery had to be staged with consummate skill. Condemning Thousand Beasts Hall's spiteful folly that harmed others without benefiting itself was a public performance he owed to everyone.

Just as the paper cranes were screeching in cacophony, the doors to the Thousand Harmony Gang's council chamber burst open. Several pro-Carefree Palace elders and protectors rushed in as usual:

—"Sect Leader, regarding the resolution to sever ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, what exactly are you—"

They froze.

Because the instant the words "Heavenly Spirit Sect" left their mouths, the roomful of paper cranes, talisman slips, and communication artifacts fell dead silent. In eerie unison, every single paper crane swiveled its paper "head," "staring" fixatedly toward the figures at the entrance.

Though mere paper constructs, their overwhelming numbers rendered the scene profoundly unnerving.

The elders and protectors instinctively took two steps back. The protector who had pushed open the door even attempted to pull it shut again.

Regrettably, he was a beat too slow. The paper cranes surged forward like a cloud of hornets, swarming straight into their faces with a barrage of frantic interrogations:

[—"What?! The Thousand Harmony Gang wants to sever ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"](# "Speaker: Outraged Central Domain Cultivator")

[—"Then what happens to the auction?!"](# "Speaker: Outraged Central Domain Cultivator")

[—"Fuck! The situation is disastrous enough as it is! You bastards in the Thousand Harmony Gang want to drop the burden and walk away now?!"](# "Speaker: Outraged Central Domain Cultivator")

[—"I'll fuck your entire family, you goddamn Thousand Harmony Gang dogshit!"](# "Speaker: Outraged Central Domain Cultivator")

Such exquisite greetings instantly engulfed the elders and protectors, persisting until the talismans' spiritual power was entirely exhausted and they disintegrated into flying ash, finally releasing the battered souls from their torment.

—"You have seen the situation for yourselves,"-Zhang Hongjing lamented, his voice dripping with sorrow:—"Thousand Beasts Hall, driven by heaven-knows-what madness, went out of their way to provoke the Untiring Immortal Venerable. To bring up severing ties now—do you all wish for my death?"

As he spoke, his eyes welled, and two streams of crystal-clear tears coursed down his cheeks.

Recalling the indignities and terror he had endured up to this day, Zhang Hongjing wept with genuine, heartfelt emotion.

Had he been a dainty, delicate maiden rather than a burly, bearded giant, he might have elicited profound sympathy. As it stood, he merely made everyone's scalps tingle with dread.

Worse yet, even as he wept, Zhang Hongjing began singling people out:

—"Brother Huang, how do you see this?"

—"I... I don't know!"-Huang stammered.

—"Brother Chen, would you be willing on my behalf to visit our fellow sects and explain our gang's hardships?"

—"Y-your junior's qualifications are meager; I am unworthy of such an assignment!"-Chen blurted.

—"Elder Qiu, you are a figure of prestige and moral authority..."

—"This old man... this old man is preparing for secluded cultivation; I cannot attend to such trivial trifles!"-Elder Qiu sputtered.

Instantly, everyone retreated [three paces back](# "Literal CN: 退避三尺 (tuìbì sānchǐ) — shrinking away in panic to keep a safe distance").

Clamoring for severance when the Untiring Immortal Venerable had yet to step into the fray was one thing; continuing to holler for severance after the Immortal Venerable himself had stepped forward was another entirely. Everyone was well aware that Lord Immortal Venerable was not someone to be trifled with. If he could cleave Carefree Palace in twain and crush Fallen Immortal Palace into dust yesterday, he certainly wouldn't break a sweat plowing the Thousand Harmony Gang seven or eight times over today.

Yet this hardly meant the crisis was resolved.

Someone still asked resentfully:

—"Then do we simply submit to the Untiring Immortal Venerable just like that?"

—"Do you wish to go refuse Lord Immortal Venerable?"-Zhang Hongjing asked, gazing over with tear-filled eyes.

The fellow scrambled like a scalded dog back into the crowd, while those around him scattered like chaff, unwilling to touch the topic with a ten-foot pole.

Zhang Hongjing sneered inwardly, yet maintained a sorrowful countenance.

—"Naturally, yielding outright is equally impermissible,"-Zhang Hongjing fished out a massive handkerchief from his sleeve to dab at his reddened eyes, sighing heavily:—"This Winter Solstice Auction has a history of thousands of years in our Central Domain. Though it began as an informal year-end gathering organized by rogue cultivators and only gained prominence in recent centuries, it remains a vital nexus where Daoist peers across the Central Domain gather to exchange intelligence and trade resources. Even Carefree Palace merely provided sponsorship! We cannot simply surrender hosting rights like this."

[—"Well said!"](# "Speaker: Thousand Harmony Gang Elder")

Zhang Hongjing clutched his handkerchief and looked toward those cheering:

—"Therefore, are you all willing to accompany me to Mount Tianling to [reason things out](# "Literal CN: 说理 (shuōlǐ) — to plead one's case, debate, or reason with someone")?"

—"..."

The room fell dead silent once more.

Seeing this, Zhang Hongjing let out a long sigh:

—"Fine. Since you are unwilling to go, I shall have to go in person."

—"Sect Leader's righteousness knows no bounds!"-someone shouted.

The look they gave Zhang Hongjing was identical to looking upon a martyr marching to the execution grounds.

—"However, if only our Thousand Harmony Gang goes, it will appear as though we are stubbornly clinging to the hosting rights,"-Zhang Hongjing added smoothly:—"Why not contact merchants, sects, and suppliers who participated in past auctions and have them accompany me? I trust the Untiring Immortal Venerable is not an unreasonable man. Seeing everyone united in opposition, perhaps he may change his mind."

Hearing this, the elders and protectors exchanged glances.

Most assumed Zhang Hongjing merely wanted to drag extra meat shields down with him. Those terrified of dying naturally shrank back, already preparing to warn their cultivator friends to feign secluded cultivation and ignore any communications from Zhang Hongjing. Others felt something seemed slightly off, yet couldn't put their finger on any fault. Even Carefree Palace moles hell-bent on sabotaging the auction could find zero flaw in Zhang Hongjing's posture.

Thus, the proposal to form the ["Untiring Immortal Venerable Persuasion Delegation"](# "Literal CN: 不倦仙尊说服团 (Bùjuàn Xiānzūn Shuōfú Tuán) — 1,000-man delegation assembled under the pretext of reasoning with Zhou Hui") was passed unanimously.

***

It had to be said: Zhang Hongjing's social network was truly formidable.

Even for something as terrifying as "reasoning with the Untiring Immortal Venerable," he managed to drum up a delegation of a thousand people.

Moreover, virtually every faction of note in the Central Domain took part.

Within this delegation were administrators of major immortal cities, renowned rogue cultivators, and prominent merchants from the domain's foremost trading syndicates.

Naturally, Zhang Hongjing had vetted them with utmost care: he screened out the die-hard Carefree Palace loyalists, retaining individuals who, even if not entirely anti-Carefree Palace, were at least relatively neutral and open to persuasion.

Thus, Zhang Hongjing led his entourage flying toward Mount Tianling.

Their first stop, however, was not Heavenly Spirit Sect itself, but the perimeter mountains—what was now known as the "Qianxing Mountain Range."

Indeed, this was the location of the secret realm: ground zero of the disaster where the Untiring Immortal Venerable had slain Sovereign Immortal Chana, triggering this entire cascade of crises.

Zhang Hongjing explained it to the group thus:

—"We have received no formal invitation from the Immortal Venerable, making this an unannounced visit. For such a massive crowd to descend uninvited—what kind of decorum would that show? Should the Immortal Venerable misunderstand this as an attempt to coerce him, things would end badly. Hence, we shall pause at his doorstep, submit our visiting card, and proceed only upon receiving permission."

—"Fellow Daoist Zhang's considerations are thorough,"-a white-bearded elder nodded in approval.

Yet a plump man sporting a round belly furrowed his brows:

—"Can we not find a proper town? Old Tao here suffered internal injuries in his youth; I cannot withstand chilly mountain drafts."

Zhang Hongjing glanced at the plump man.

This was a heavyweight figure in every sense of the word—both physically and socially.

This fat man named Tao Zhu might not look impressive, but he was a Mahayana realm cultivator and the secret boss behind the Abyssal Ocean Society, the Central Domain's largest artifact trade syndicate. Their primary trade lay in excavating and brokering relic treasures unearthed from the ancient Fiendgod Battlefield, an entity even Carefree Palace had to treat with utmost courtesy.

Zhang Hongjing had never expected to recruit such a titan. To his shock, upon hearing of the "Persuasion Delegation," Tao Zhu had approached him proactively and insisted on attending in person.

Amidst his shock, Zhang Hongjing worried this man might prove the biggest wild card. What if he sided with Carefree Palace...?

Sure enough, before they had even reached their first stop, Tao Zhu began making trouble.

Zhang Hongjing kept his inner thoughts concealed, wearing his signature affable smile as he prepared to smooth things over.

Yet before Zhang Hongjing could speak, beside them, Fifth Master Qingyang Qingli of Eternal Villa unfurled his folding fan with a chuckle:

—"Boss Tao, you haven't visited this region recently, have you? According to my sect disciples, this secret realm is infinitely more entertaining than any ordinary town."

—"Heh. Old Tao has explored more secret realms than the roads you brat have walked. Only kids like you would find a secret realm novel,"-Tao Zhu scoffed dismissively.

He was indeed here to stir up trouble.

Fortunately, his true motive was far milder than Zhang Hongjing's worst fears.

While the Abyssal Ocean Society did maintain deep financial ties with Carefree Palace, it was purely business.

Tao Zhu similarly looked down upon Carefree Palace's sordid subterranean operations. It was simply that having wandered the Central Domain's harshest, most desolate reaches for millennia, he had grown numb to every variety of grim tragedy and couldn't be bothered to intervene.

Consequently, he couldn't comprehend why Zhou Hui seized every available opportunity to beat Carefree Palace half to death. Even if you overturned Carefree Palace, what of it? Another Fallen Immortal Palace, Fallen God Palace, or Fiendgod Palace would inevitably crawl out to take its place. Meanwhile, without Carefree Palace to integrate commercial resources, his Abyssal Ocean Society would have to shoulder vastly more tedious legwork.

Nor was Carefree Palace the only faction Tao Zhu found irksome; he couldn't stand Heavenly Spirit Sect either.

Millennia ago, Zhou Hui had turned down the Abyssal Ocean Society's recruitment offer, running off to the East Hedge Garden to hang out with a bunch of spellcasting nerds. That was foolish enough, but as he mingled, he had actually scooped up a little brat from the depths of the ruins and brought it home!

Tao Zhu had warned Zhou Hui back then: you must never adopt brats! Adopt one, and you won't be able to resist adopting a second; adopt a second, and a third will follow, until your entire life is completely ruined!

Zhou Hui had refused to listen. And what was the result? He had picked up a whole string of four, five, or was it seven or eight of them? He had even established Heavenly Spirit Sect for these little whelps! Erecting all this right on the cusp of ascension—was that not sheer, unadulterated madness?!

With brats and a sect came fatal weaknesses.

And now, hadn't Carefree Palace—that colossal parasitic leech—latched right onto him?

And this current nuisance was merely the tip of the iceberg; the real terror was having someone target him during his Heavenly Tribulation of Ascension. That would be the ruin of a millennium of peerless renown in a single morning!

Yet Tao Zhu knew all too well how stubborn Zhou Hui was.

Hence, Tao Zhu harbored zero illusion that he could talk sense into Zhou Hui. He simply felt the title "Persuasion Delegation" made for a convenient cover to tag along and give Zhou Hui an earful of curses before the end—a final farewell to an old friend!

Yes, in Tao Zhu's mind, Zhou Hui was already as good as dead.

To a certain extent, Tao Zhu was remarkably prescient.

Still, Tao Zhu and Zhou Hui had known each other for an eternity, dating back to when Zhou Hui, as a reckless Golden Core fledgling with zero regard for his own life, had first barged into the Abyssal Ocean ruins. Back then, Tao Zhu was merely a Soul Formation cultivator; several thousand years had slipped by since.

Reflecting on how his old acquaintances had departed one by one—two-thirds of them through self-inflicted courting of death—Tao Zhu felt utterly suffocated.

He had initially thought traveling leisurely alongside these juniors might be a tolerable diversion, but now he had lost all patience for itineraries, snapping gruffly at Zhang Hongjing:

—"Visiting Heavenly Spirit Sect directly is fine as well; Untiring will give me that much face!"

At his declaration, several voices chimed in:

[—"Indeed, who wouldn't give face to Boss Tao!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Merchant")

[—"Even Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable would surely take no offense."](# "Speaker: Central Domain Merchant")

These flatterers were primarily merchants within the entourage who clearly deemed the boss of the Abyssal Ocean Society far more valuable to their business than the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Yet there were also peace-loving souls seeking compromise. The white-bearded elder who had praised Zhang Hongjing earlier suggested:

—"Since Boss Tao dislikes secret realms, we could rest at a nearby town first! I saw numerous rogue cultivators flying past outside just now; surely there must be a sizable immortal city in the vicinity."

—"What immortal city could there possibly be out here?"-someone familiar with the geography retorted immediately:—"Heading northeast leads straight into a hundred thousand li of barren wilderness—no villages, no inns, dull as ditchwater. Exactly as Boss Tao suggested, better to visit Heavenly Spirit Sect early and get this over with."

—"Could we have taken a wrong turn?"-another wondered aloud:—"We've seen swarms of rogue cultivators passing by since moments ago. Were there no immortal city, how could there be so many people?"

Hearing the debate, curious passengers peered out the windows.

Because this was a "Persuasion Delegation," Zhang Hongjing had deliberately avoided excessive ostentation to prevent misunderstandings with the Untiring Immortal Venerable, selecting ordinary [cloud carriages](# "Literal CN: 云车 (yúnchē) — flying carriage / aerial yacht") as transportation. Naturally, as Sect Leader of the Thousand Harmony Gang and hosting luminaries like Boss Tao, while the carriage exterior appeared as a standard six-crane carriage, the interior was in truth a three-story flying villa featuring multiple grand council chambers, studies, and private lounges.

Moreover, a thousand people could never fit inside a single vessel; the Thousand Harmony Gang had readied over a dozen such carriages.

Judging by interior capacity, the thousand-plus travelers could comfortably lodge inside with room to spare.

Over a dozen cloud carriages soaring through the skies should have been a conspicuous sight.

Yet as passengers peered out, they discovered the skyways were bustling like a teeming thoroughfare. Rogue cultivators on flying swords streaked through the clouds; even more luxurious aerial carriages and exotic spirit beasts sailed past. Their fleet was utterly ordinary amidst the traffic.

And upon closer inspection, every single one of these cultivators was heading toward the exact same destination.

Zhang Hongjing glanced outside as well, smiling at Tao Zhu and the others:

—"It appears we are approaching the Qianxing Secret Realm."

—"A secret realm? Sect Leader Zhang, you mean to say so many people are heading to a secret realm?"-incredulous voices arose.

Ordinary secret realms rarely attracted such crowds.

Private realms monopolized by sects were off-limits to outsiders, while even public realms managed by the largest immortal cities rarely saw this density of cultivators milling about.

After all, exploring a secret realm was an exhausting ordeal. Even resource-starved rogue cultivators wouldn't dive in daily. Furthermore, rogue cultivators never lingered near a realm: the wounded hurried back to heal; the empty-handed craved rest; and those lucky enough to strike gold fled in terror of treasure-hunting muggers, desperate to hide inside their cave abodes. Thus, cultivators near secret realms typically came and went in a frantic hurry.

Yet the cultivators soaring outside were not only multitudinous, but remarkably relaxed.

Some meandered through the clouds doing lazy loops; others lingered to admire the vista; still others grouped together chatting and laughing, appearing as carefree as if strolling through their own private gardens.

Observing this spectacle, even Tao Zhu found it odd:

—"Could it be that some peerless treasure recently emerged from this realm?"

Only the birth of an extraordinary relic typically drew such multitudes.

Yet Tao Zhu paused, shaking his head:

—"No. Were a relic to appear, they wouldn't be this leisurely."

To put it bluntly, had a supreme treasure surfaced, a crowd might gather, but the air would reek of blood and murderous intent.

—"Boss Tao possesses sharp discernment,"-Zhang Hongjing praised casually:—"Naturally, no relic has appeared. In fact, the harvest within the secret realm isn't particularly extraordinary. Many come purely to [have a bit of fun](# "Literal CN: 找点乐子 (zhǎo diǎn lèzi) — leisure and recreation"), hence their relaxed demeanor."

—"Having fun in a secret realm?"-Tao Zhu grumbled that he was getting too old, having never heard of such bizarre recreation.

Zhang Hongjing offered no further explanation, merely chuckling:

—"As we draw closer to the realm, it will be even livelier!"

The fleet of cloud carriages descended toward the mountain range below.

Just as Zhang Hongjing had promised, the closer they drew to the realm, the more bustling it became.

Yet this bustle differed fundamentally from a sprawling immortal city crammed with row upon row of contiguous pavilions and packed shoulder-to-shoulder with throngs of pedestrians; it was a far more leisurely, pastoral vitality.

The Qianxing Mountain Range bore none of its former barren desolation. Dozens of exquisite cottages were perched upon mountain peaks or nestled along hillsides, blending harmoniously with craggy cliffs into scenic landscapes.

Cultivators swooped down from the clouds, drifting casually toward these hillside lodges as though residing here.

Had these visitors hailed from another world, they would have possessed a far more precise term for it:

A [resort](# "Literal CN: 度假村 (dùjiàcūn) — vacation resort / holiday villa village").

Regrettably, people of this world held no such concept. It was not that they never vacationed; rather, leisure was a luxury reserved exclusively for the wealthy, who erected private villas and imperial palaces rather than congregating in such a "plebeian" fashion.

Thus, Tao Zhu was utterly bewildered.

He sensed that the ambient spiritual energy in this area was hardly dense—far from ideal for cultivation cave abodes.

Perceiving his perplexity, Zhang Hongjing offered a timely explanation:

—"These are not their permanent cave abodes; they are merely leased rentals."

—"Leased rentals?"

—"Indeed. Word has it the Immortal Venerable instituted strict trial durations for the secret realm: each cultivator is allotted only a few hours of exploration per day. To avoid wasting the next day's quota and spare themselves the hassle of flying back and forth, many cultivators simply lease cottages nearby."

[There was such an operation?!](# "Literal CN: 还有这种操作 (hái yǒu zhè zhǒng cāozuò) — internet meme for an unexpected, galaxy-brain maneuver") The merchants within the delegation were thunderstruck.

Fifth Master Qingyang Qingli of Eternal Villa chimed in:

—"Because the demand for rentals is so fierce, leasing rates recently surged! The closer a cottage is to the realm, the steeper the rent. I hear prime spots have climbed to five thousand spirit stones a month! What on earth are they thinking? That's almost on par with the monthly admission pass to the realm itself."

—"?!" The merchants' eyes bulged even wider.

—"Your Eternal Villa needn't worry about this, surely?"-Zhang Hongjing smiled:—"I heard you signed a long-term contract with the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"We only signed a long-term pass for the secret realm, not for housing rentals—we missed out,"-Qingyang sighed:—"With business booming like this, no wonder when we offered to purchase an entire mountain peak back then, they adamantly refused!"

Hearing this, the gaze with which the merchants beheld the craggy wilderness underwent a drastic transformation.

These were no barren mountains; these were literal mountains of spirit stones!

Someone gulped, asking:

—"Sect Leader Zhang, are we staying here?"

—"Of course not,"-Zhang Hongjing replied:—"We have come to pay court to the Untiring Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect; at the very least, lodging inside Qianxing Hall is the bare minimum of proper etiquette!"

As they spoke, the cloud carriages sailed through the mountains of spirit stones—*ahem*, barren peaks—and arrived before Qianxing Hall.

As the central edifice of the Qianxing Mountain Range, Qianxing Hall was exquisitely magnificent, though before a delegation of a thousand people, it appeared slightly diminutive.

Naturally, just as with Zhang Hongjing's cloud carriages, spatial dimensions in the cultivation world could never be judged by exterior appearances. The visitors had prepared themselves for spatial enchantment arts within Qianxing Hall. Yet upon stepping inside, they were still thoroughly astounded.

Qianxing Hall employed no spatial magic; rather, the entire mountain had been hollowed out with brute simplicity.

Stepping through the portal, one beheld the mountain interior sculpted into a vast, conical subterranean spire, tapering at the pinnacle and broadening toward the base. The entrance of Qianxing Hall was perched at the very summit of the cone, looking down into an abyss of dizzying depth.

Yet this man-made chasm evoked not the slightest dread. Every tiered gallery was ablaze with radiant illumination, draped with colossal holographic banners suspended across the tiers. Some featured an ethereal maiden seated in a tea pavilion amidst fragrant steam:
> *A Cup of Sorrow, A Cup of Fate.*

Others showcased a dashing cultivator in serene meditation:
> *Member Meditation Rooms Now Open: 20% Off for Long-Term Memberships!*

Other tiers advertised spiritual weapons, alchemy pills, and equipment enhancements, dazzling the eyes.

Furthermore, cultivators rushed back and forth between galleries in lively commotion, some broadcasting divine sense shouts across the open corridors:

[—"Five-man party, two sword cultivators, looking for one alchemist! A support is fine too—!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator LFG")

[—"Ice Eye brick-hauling bounty quest, anyone want in?!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator LFG")

[—"Need one beast tamer!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator LFG")

[—"Level 5 Guild recruiting! Seeking permanent stationed members!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator LFG")

The delegation stood slack-jawed at the spectacle.

Zhang Hongjing naturally wouldn't leave his hard-won guests standing in a daze. Pre-instructed disciples of the Thousand Harmony Gang stepped forward smoothly, ushering different contingents in various directions:

—"Gentlemen, we have reserved guest accommodations here; please follow me."

—"Why is this secret realm so popular? You may experience it for yourselves! We have reserved 'Tourist Passes' for everyone—completely safe, treat it as a scenic tour, wonderfully fascinating."

—"The spiritual energy inside Qianxing Hall is abundant, is it not? If interested, you may experience cultivating here."

—"Indeed, most commercial storefronts remain vacant; they are currently soliciting merchant tenants."

They pitched the facilities with practiced ease, radiating even more enthusiasm than Heavenly Spirit Sect's own disciples.

Meanwhile, Zhang Hongjing escorted Tao Zhu, Qingyang, and other VIPs whose status could not be ignored, providing a guided briefing along the way:

—"Though exterior cottages are pricey, they pale before Qianxing Hall's private meditation chambers. A single chamber here rents for a hundred spirit stones per hour! But it is worth every crystal: not only is the spiritual energy exceptionally dense, but the Immortal Venerable's supreme authority presides over the hall. Player-versus-player attacks between cultivators are strictly impossible, and attempting a cultivation breakthrough here actually lowers tribulation difficulty... Rumor has it a demon beast even had one of its heavenly lightning bolts deflected by the Immortal Venerable."

—"Yet meditation rooms inside Qianxing Hall are not the most exorbitant. What is truly expensive lies inside the secret realm itself."

—"Indeed, the very floating island where we now stand: lodging here starts at tens of thousands of spirit stones per night... Boss Tao, what is the matter?"

Zhang Hongjing turned around, jolted by Tao Zhu's appearance.

Tao Zhu stood there with a face black as soot, his belly swelling to more than double its original girth, looking ready to detonate at any second.

—"I regret it with all my soul!"-Tao Zhu wailed in agony:

—"Had I known little Zhou Hui possessed this caliber of genius for commerce, why didn't I push just a bit harder back then?! Why did I let him wander off to the East Hedge Garden instead of dragging him into the Abyssal Ocean Society?! Just how many mountains of spirit stones have I missed out on, *waaaaah*—!"
