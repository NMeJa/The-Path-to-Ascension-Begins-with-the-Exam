# Chapter 220: Ran Family Bloodline

—"Why would Ran Zhen be in the Heavenly Court?"

Tao Zhu questioned with deep skepticism:

—"Was he not exploring ancient ruins in the Abyssal Ocean alongside the Dongli Garden?"

Regarding the endeavors Ran Zhen was currently undertaking, Tao Zhu was exceptionally well-informed.

After all, the Abyssal Ocean was essentially Tao Zhu's private domain. The Abyssal Ocean Society he had established operated in the maritime depths year-round, unearthing ancient Dharma relics and hunting rare spiritual beasts. Naturally, they also purchased spoils and artifacts harvested by independent cultivators exploring the abyss. Because the Abyssal Ocean Society offered fair, honest prices, never engaged in highway robbery or slaughter like the Fallen Immortal Palace (Xiaoyao Palace), and routinely mounted search-and-rescue operations when circumstances warranted, they enjoyed an immaculate reputation among high-tier cultivators, with many practitioners becoming regular suppliers to the society.

Among them, the Dongli Garden was one of the Abyssal Ocean Society's premier long-term trading partners.

Although the Dongli Garden was an academic research society—focusing primarily on ancient history and forbidden esoteric arts, plunging headfirst wherever peril was thickest to the point where members were widely branded as "lunatics"—it was precisely this obsession that enabled them to excavate unimaginable treasures from the deepest ruins of the Abyssal Ocean.

Treasures with modest research utility but astronomical commercial value were routinely bought out in bulk by Boss Tao.

Consequently, Boss Tao maintained a keen interest in the Dongli Garden's "research expeditions," and the monumental project currently spearheaded by Ran Zhen, the Clan Head of the Ran Family, was naturally one of the Abyssal Ocean Society's priority interests.

While Ran Zhen was not an official member of the Dongli Garden, the Ran Family was a renowned clan of formation masters spanning countless generations; inevitably, ambitious disciples hungry for cutting-edge theory would join the society.

This instance was no exception. A certain disciple of Ran Zhen within the Dongli Garden had discovered a primordial ruin in the abyss, only to be stymied by the impenetrable outer grand array, prompting him to rush home and enlist his master as emergency reinforcement.

Conversely, for an array to confound a syndicate of obsessive research maniacs, the ruin shielded at its core had to be of extraordinary historical pedigree—a project Boss Tao had been eagerly awaiting updates on.

Now, instead of receiving news of archaeological spoils, he was told Ran Zhen had ascended to heaven?!

Boss Tao found the claim utterly absurd:

—"Was Ran Zhen not deciphering the abyssal arrays? I heard progress was remarkably smooth, and they even unearthed some relic, formally inviting a dignitary from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ritual Ministry to come inspect it...?"

Tao Zhu's voice trailed off.

He suddenly remembered that a certain official from the Ritual Ministry had been residing at the Heavenly Spirit Sect for quite some time.

Because cultivators of the Ritual Ministry were notoriously aloof—especially Si Wu, who constantly looked down his nose at everyone—Tao Zhu had never bothered conversing with him, nor had he investigated why Fujuan had escorted him back. Yet he vaguely recalled Si Wu claiming he had fallen into a "trap," rescued from the jaws of death in the abyss by Fujuan.

More crucially, shortly after rescuing Si Wu, Fujuan had ventured into the Abyssal Ocean once again, restoring the legendary "Ascension Stairway"!

A faint realization clicked within Tao Zhu's mind:

—"Could that Ascension Stairway...?"

—"It was naturally no coincidence,"-Zhou Hui confirmed.

He had indeed sought out Ran Zhen solely because of Si Wu.

Back then, commissioned by the Dongli Garden, Si Wu had arrived in the Middle Realm to inspect a "divine descent relic." En route, however, manipulated by a "celestial decree," he had strayed off course, betrayed by his own disciple, the Wicked Child, and nearly perished in the Abyssal Ocean.

Si Wu's death was inconsequential in itself; the core complication was that upon dying, he would drop an orange-tier legendary artifact: the Immortal Slaying Sword.

In previous reincarnation cycles, that sword had served as the linchpin for the so-called "protagonist" to exterminate the Heavenly Spirit Sect. In other words, it was his (their) ultimate cheat code; years down the line, he (they) would serendipitously unearth the blade, embarking upon his grand epic.

Simply put, Si Wu had been deliberately positioned by the Heavenly Court to deliver end-game gear to the "protagonist." Had Si Wu not been too young and inappropriately handsome, he would have been bundled as an elderly mentor spirit trapped inside a ring, offering a "buy-one-get-one-free" package.

Because the narrative was so thoroughly cliché, Zhou Hui—operating with limited intelligence at the time—had assumed the heavenly Dao was merely a third-rate fantasy novel riddled with plot holes, with the Immortal Slaying Sword existing solely to service the "protagonist." Yet now, understanding the full cosmic context, looking upon that blade revealed it was entirely calculated—likely engineered to assassinate "those who needed to be assassinated."

After all, the Immortal Slaying Sword didn't merely execute True Immortals; it possessed the absolute function of severing one's connection to the Heavenly Court. If a True Immortal fell beneath its edge, even if their residual soul was gathered for reincarnation, they could never return to the Heavenly Court... meaning they were permanently barred from ascension.

And several of Zhou Hui's disciples were intimately tied to the Heavenly Court:

First and foremost was Qu Qianxing; in a broader sense, Guan Ye with his Dao Bone and Lu Shulong with his True Dragon heritage qualified as well.

Yet judging by the outcome of the "heavenly Dao script," the one directly targeted was Ran Deng, who had been butchered by the "protagonist" like disposable cannon fodder.

In that third-rate novel, because Ran Deng was executed the instant she showed her face, readers later completely forgot who she was. When the television adaptation was produced, the screenwriters had crudely fabricated an unrequited crush subplot where Ran Deng harbored secret feelings for her younger junior brother, chiming in with Mo Xi to bully the "protagonist" and Mo Bei. Because that romantic pairing was so thoroughly cursed and [unholy](# "Internet slang: 邪道 (xiédào) — cursed/heretical shipping or narrative trope"), Zhou Hui refused to squander mental storage recalling such garbage.

As for why the television adaptation had preferred concocting nonsensical romance rather than deleting Ran Deng, it was not purely due to the screenwriters' [romance-addled brains](# "Internet slang: 恋爱脑 (liàn'àinǎo) — love-struck brain"); rather, the plotline concerning Ran Zhen and the secret realm he unearthed was critically indispensable.

It was the final massive dungeon raid prior to the "protagonist's" ultimate ascension.

According to the narrative, the Clan Head of the Ran Family opened the gates to the abyssal secret realm, only to receive word that Ran Deng had been slain. "By sheer coincidence," he crossed paths with the "protagonist" treasure-hunting within the realm. Attempting to avenge his beloved daughter, Ran Zhen was counter-slain by the protagonist, gifting all of his supreme, celestial-grade defensive arrays to the hero, securing the protagonist's immortal cave dwelling against all future threats.

Misled by that storyline, Zhou Hui had originally assumed this was a mundane trope of killing monsters to loot treasures, with the author merely fabricating a veneer of righteousness so the audience found Ran Zhen's death palatable.

Concerned for Ran Zhen's safety, Zhou Hui had sought to bring Ran Deng to reunite with her father. Yet while searching for Ran Zhen, he had serendipitously stumbled upon the legendary "Ascension Stairway" within the Xiaoyao Palace's maritime encampment, subsequently restoring it.

Only through that restoration did Zhou Hui truly grasp the cosmic significance of that secret realm.

After all, the Ascension Stairway could not be constructed haphazardly.

That celestial stairway simultaneously existed and did not exist within this physical world; rather, it was anchored in the interstitial seams bridging several distinct dimensions. Utilizing the unique physics of spatial fractures, it enabled mortals to achieve smooth, stabilized "ascension" into another world (the Heavenly Court) with mortal flesh, without violently shattering dimensional barriers through raw brute force.

This also meant that the secret realm surrounding the stairway was by no means an ordinary spatial pocket attached to this world. Depending on the geometry, it could very well be an entirely separate micro-world.

Subsequent investigations confirmed this hypothesis.

Afterward, Zhou Hui attempted to locate Ran Zhen multiple times. While specialized transmission artifacts from the Dongli Garden maintained continuous communication—with Ran Zhen's expedition regularly transmitting real-time progress on breaking the array—the coordinates recorded by the expedition and those handed to Si Wu were utterly untraceable within the Abyssal Ocean.

Zhou Hui had attempted to calculate their location via the Ascension Stairway, but lacking essential dimensional metrics—particularly parameters belonging to external micro-worlds—the equations remained insoluble.

Fortunately, through the bloodline resonance carried within Ran Deng, he confirmed that Ran Zhen was hale and hearty. (Thinking back, it made sense: as a pivotal antagonist in the late-game script tasked with unlocking the grand dungeon, he wouldn't be allowed to perish so prematurely.) Zhou Hui had no choice but to bide his time.

Until recently, when Zhou Hui acquired the Heavenly Court's array disc.

Beyond divining future timelines, that array disc contained astronomical volumes of spatial coordinates spanning countless micro-worlds, including the absolute celestial coordinates of the Heavenly Court. Armed with this dataset, Zhou Hui finally deduced the spatial formulas governing the Ascension Stairway, pinning down the precise location of the realm.

The result of those calculations caught even Zhou Hui off guard:

—"The secret realm Ran Zhen is currently deciphering is, in truth, an actual precinct of the Heavenly Court,"-Zhou Hui revealed to Tao Zhu and the gathering:—"To be precise, it was once a structural wing of the Ascension Stairway. In the ancient celestial era, after mortals scaled the stairway and opened the immortal gates, this was the spatial sanctuary where they waited for the deities to receive them—the authentic 'Ascension Platform.'"

Hearing this, a collective gasp rippled through the gathering.

The Ascension Platform!

Unlike Zhou Hui, none of them had encountered True Immortals seeking to butcher them for their Dao Bones, nor had they ventured into the True Immortal Realm. As cultivators—even those like Peilan who had foresworn ascension—they harbored an unspoken, romantic yearning toward the celestial firmament.

Tao Zhu pressed eagerly:

—"What does that mean? Does stepping into that realm mean one has achieved ascension?"

—"How could it be that effortless? Stepping onto the Ascension Platform without enduring the trials of the stairway would invite instant obliteration by heavenly tribulation lightning in ancient times,"-Zhou Hui shook his head:—"That Ran Zhen could ascend is intimately linked to the unique bloodline of the Ran Family."

Back then, on the Ascension Stairway, Ran Deng had been the sole individual capable of beholding the Celestial Gate.

While Zhou Hui had originally assumed he and Yuwen Xuan had failed to meet the criteria due to administrative discrepancies, upon gaining administrative authority over the stairway and conducting deep analysis, he realized that Ran Deng's qualification as a True Immortal was essentially a "cosmic inevitability."

Ignorant of the backstory, Tao Zhu gasped:

—"Bloodline? That cursed bloodline of short-lived mortals?"

—"Rather, why has the Ran Family suffered short lifespans for generations?"-Zhou Hui countered softly.

For millennia, longevity had been the fatal shackle strangling the Ran Family. While they could pass their cultivation down through generations, the moment a master achieved a breakthrough or crossed a certain age, they aged rapidly and perished, virtually severing their path to ascension.

Yet even under such handicaps, the Heavenly Court remained unsatisfied, demanding the complete extermination of the Ran Family in the narrative "script."

Therefore, Zhou Hui deduced that the Ran Family possessed an intrinsic "administrative clearance"—a celestial authority that even the Heavenly Court could not revoke—enabling Ran Zhen and Ran Deng to push open the Celestial Gate while bypassing the tribulations of the stairway entirely.

Ran Deng had traversed the orthodox path, and because she defined ascension as "standing beside Master," she posed no threat to celestial hegemony, earning legitimate True Immortal certification. Ran Zhen, however, was fundamentally different: having bypassed the stairway entirely, he had breached the wreckage of the fallen Ascension Platform, picking the lock through raw formation mastery.

In other words, Ran Zhen was currently standing in the Heavenly Court's front courtyard, busily trying to pry open their front door.

And for reasons unknown, the Heavenly Court was powerless to halt him directly, resorting instead to dispatching assassins to purge him and wipe out his entire bloodline.

—"To be marked for execution by the Heavenly Court... the Ran Family has truly brought the sky down upon their heads,"-Peilan murmured, having assembled the pieces:—"Then bringing Ran Zhen back is imperative. Allowing him to tamper with that ruin will spark a cosmic catastrophe."

—"Whether he is brought back or not, a catastrophe is already guaranteed,"-Sikong Yao interjected, having witnessed countless such power struggles within the Xiaoyao Palace:—"As long as this lineage of formation masters retains this capability, the Heavenly Court will never sleep peacefully. It is as if a stranger possesses a master key to your front gate! Regardless of whether the head of the house manages to pick the lock today, the paranoia will never abate. Compounded by how absurdly ambitious the Ran Family is—continuing their fanatical research despite being cursed with shortened lifespans—how could the celestials ever rest easy? Either the Ran Family is wiped out down to the infant... or they all achieve godhood. There is no middle ground."

Reaching this point, Sikong Yao's brow furrowed in sudden horror:

—"If we align ourselves with him, won't we be targeted by the Heavenly Court as well?!"

He instantly recoiled from participating in this endeavor, though he had never been enthusiastic to begin with.

Yet the moment the words left his lips, he found every eye in the gathering trained dead upon him.

The atmosphere turned palpably hostile.

—"Hey! This means offending the Heavenly Court!"-Sikong Yao shouted:—"Do you people not intend to ascend anymore?! Even if you forfeit ascension, you could be cursed like the Ran Family, having your lifespans severed and your toil stolen to [serve as wedding gowns for others](# "Idiom: 为他人作嫁衣裳 — toiling endlessly only for others to reap the rewards")!"

—"This old one does not care in the slightest,"-Peilan replied, tugging gently at his fine yellow whiskers:—"I have lived long enough, and my medical arts have been thoroughly passed down to my disciples; I harbor zero regrets. Besides, I never intended to ascend in the first place."

Born as a wild spirit, Peilan's cultivation journey had been an authentic exercise in "defying heaven," suffering endless discrimination.

Consequently, while he lacked Zhou Hui's omniscience regarding celestial atrocities, he had long harbored an intuitive premonition that ascension was no blessing. In particular, one of his apprentices had achieved ascension centuries ago, yet judging by reports filtering through the Ritual Ministry, that disciple's circumstances were deeply unsettling.

More recently, the revelation that Guan Ye's spine had been harvested by Guan Xinguang came to light.

Years ago, when Guan Ye was first carried back to the mountain gates, Peilan had treated his wounds personally; he knew intimately that there was nothing in Guan Ye's body worthy of an imperial grandmaster's greed, let alone involving Goddess Ranran! The sole factor that made Guan Ye a target after his qualification exams was that he had crystallized a Dao Bone.

Peilan had instantly deduced the horrifying truth.

Peilan possessed a Dao Bone as well.

Entering the Dao through the path of medicine, he had forged his Dao Bone through honest, painstaking toil. Because he was a transformed wild fox, his bone lacked the universal compatibility of human bone, nor did it offer the intoxicating vitality of nine-tailed celestial foxes, but it was an authentic Dao Bone nonetheless. While mortal cultivators could not utilize such transcendent "materials," True Immortals in the Heavenly Court certainly could.

Therefore, this micro-world was Peilan's safest sanctuary. Only within this realm did Peilan possess the confidence to outmaneuver predators and preserve his own bone.

Peilan stated with iron resolve:

—"Ran Zhen's situation cannot be ignored. Setting aside all else, to eliminate the vector for a second demonic invasion, we must drag him back."

It was a necessary gamble.

—"Hm, while that is true..."-Tao Zhu hesitated slightly. While he held zero desire for celestial ascension—believing the accumulation of spirit stones was the supreme pursuit of life—commerce valued harmony above all; he had no appetite for provoking the Heavenly Court without cause.

Yet rubbing his belly, another thought struck him, dissolving his reluctance:

—"Then again, it is the courtyard of the Heavenly Court. Perhaps we might slip inside the gate..."

You just want to sneak into the Heavenly Court's yard to loot divine artifacts to sell, don't you?!

Greed has truly rotted your brain!

Sikong Yao rolled his eyes, ignoring these two unambitious Mahayana grandmasters, turning his gaze toward the remaining three Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses.

—"Do you three truly not care?"-Sikong Yao pressed:—"You stand merely half a step from ultimate ascension!"

—"Master has issued his command; how dare this servant decline?"-the crimson mask covering Ruwang's face spoke on his behalf:—"Not to mention the Heavenly Court, even were it the eighteen layers of purgatory, this Emperor would march through without hesitation!"

—"This old one does not wish to go! It is far too perilous!"-Jiuzhai maintained his eternal posture of seeking passive detachment while physically inserting himself into every apocalyptic catastrophe:—"Yet Fujuan has already drafted his schemes. If I do not watch over him in person, I fear he will commit atrocities far beyond my psychological threshold."

Yet all you ever do is watch from the sidelines!

Sikong Yao complained inwardly, turning his gaze finally upon Fujuan.

Evidently, both Jiuzhai and Ruwang deferred entirely to Fujuan.

Zhou Hui offered no grandiose moral rhetoric to persuade Sikong Yao, nor did he declare his ultimate cosmic allegiance. He merely remarked serenely:

—"I generally refrain from morally coercing others. How one chooses is an individual prerogative; I possess no right to alter another's conviction."

—"Oh—"-Sikong Yao scrutinized Fujuan's countenance, finding the grandmaster seemingly sincere.

He instantly declared with crisp finality:

—"I decline to participate. Farewell!"

With those words, he turned on his heel, striding briskly toward the arena exits.

Zhou Hui made no move to block his path, continuing in an unhurried, gentle drawl:

—"However, there is the matter of allegiances in this world: one either stands with me, or stands with my enemies. While I do not interfere with another's autonomy, once someone chooses my enemies, they naturally become my enemy... would they not?"

Reaching this sentence, Zhou Hui lifted his eyes, offering a warm, radiant smile toward Sikong Yao's retreating back.

Sikong Yao possessed no eyes on the back of his skull, yet he froze dead in his tracks.

[Becoming Fujuan's enemy...?]

[Wait, isn't this laying all the cards on the table?! What do you mean by "choosing my enemies"?!]

[The "enemy" you are referring to is the HEAVENLY COURT!]

[Do you intend to wage war against the Heavenly Court?! Are you planning to storm the celestial palace?!]

[Wasn't the original topic how to prevent a demonic invasion?! How did this escalate into open rebellion against the Heavenly Court?!]

[Why don't you just become a Demon King already?!]

Besides, what kind of choice was this?!

On one side stood the Heavenly Court, governing myriad micro-worlds across the cosmos; on the other stood Fujuan, who hadn't even achieved ascension. Logically speaking, there was no deliberation required—

—"I am naturally on your side!"-Sikong Yao executed a flawless, friction-free [sliding kowtow](# "Modern slang: 滑跪 (huáguì) — sliding on one's knees into immediate, shameless submission"), sliding back to Zhou Hui's boots with a fawning beam:—"Immortal Master, please be clear next time! When it comes to taking sides, who else could I ever choose besides your esteemed self?!"

His posture would have left his former colleagues in the Xiaoyao Palace utterly flabbergasted.

After all, Sikong Yao was infamous for being slicker than an oiled eel; regardless of factional strife, coercion, or bribery within the palace, nobody had ever forced him to pledge allegiance to a single faction—the fundamental reason he had been politically marginalized for centuries. Nobody could imagine he would take a knee with such shameless alacrity.

Yet Sikong Yao had no alternative. The Heavenly Court was far away; Fujuan was right before his nose.

Sikong Yao harbored total conviction that even if he ascended to heaven tomorrow, Zhou Hui would track him down and hunt him across the cosmos for another eight years! He couldn't stomach that brand of relentless devotion. To make him stand with the Heavenly Court, the celestials would have to hunt him with identical ferocity for nine years; otherwise, negotiations were off the table.

With even the "non-friend" Sikong Yao pledging enthusiastic cooperation, zero dissent remained.

A harmonious consensus was achieved.

Zhou Hui was deeply gratified.

—"Splendid. Let us go retrieve Ran Zhen!"-Zhou Hui clapped his hands cheerfully, as though addressing toddlers in a kindergarten.

—"Right now?!"-not merely Sikong Yao, but even Tao Zhu cried out in shock.

He had assumed today was merely an exploratory strategy session, never anticipating immediate tactical deployment. He asked in bewilderment:

—"From here?!"

Zhou Hui never harbored a habit of procrastination:

—"From right here. I have already locked onto the spatial coordinates; crossing over will take merely an instant."

As he spoke, he swept his wide sleeve. An ancient, weathered white marble stele materialized at the center of the arena, surrounded by fractured masonry and crumbled architectural debris—the unmistakable wreckage of a collapsed primordial structure. Seasoned abyssal explorers recognized this as the outer perimeter of a grand ruin, where lethal arrays and subterranean deathtraps guarded the true entrance below.

The white jade stele exhibited unmistakable traces of array deciphering, yet the grandmasters present could sense zero spiritual resonance indicating an entryway.

—"Deciphering the grand array alone is insufficient to grant entry,"-Zhou Hui noted.

Beside him, an exquisite celestial palace lantern materialized out of thin air. It was the natal soul lamp belonging to his Third Disciple, Ran Deng.

Within the lantern rested Ran Deng's soul fire. While predominantly dim and muted, several embers burned with fierce, dazzling brilliance—the physical testament of Ran Deng refining her primordial flame, a discipline requiring centuries of solitary cultivation.

Under normal circumstances, such intimate artifacts could not be handled even by a master; corrupt mentors frequently seized them by force to enslave their disciples.

Yet in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Ran Deng had surrendered her lamp without a second thought, and the grandmasters watching the exchange registered zero surprise.

After all, it was Fujuan. Not merely his disciples, but even his "friends" would gladly lend their life-bound treasures were Zhou Hui to make the request.

Holding the lantern aloft, Zhou Hui cast its illumination upon the white jade stele. Beneath the radiant glow, the surrounding space began to warp, revealing celestial vistas alien to this realm. Behind the stele, the towering, ruined gateway of an opulent celestial palace faded into shimmering existence.

Zhou Hui stepped toward the gate.

Seeing this, Sikong Yao mounted a desperate last-ditch delay—he had pledged his loyalty without hesitation, but marching directly into an apocalyptic ruin was another matter:

—"Isn't this far too rushed?! At least grant us time to pack provisions!"

He invoked the time-honored tactic of stalling.

Then again, entering an ancient ruin demanded thorough logistical preparation!

Not to mention that this was the Ascension Platform—the front courtyard of the Heavenly Court itself!

Sikong Yao shot desperate, frantic glances toward Tao Zhu, knowing the merchant had the lowest appetite for danger:

—"What if we are trapped inside for ten days or a half-month?"

Certain colossal ruins required expeditions measured in years.

—"Indeed! If you vanish into the void, what becomes of your Immortal Arts Assembly?"-Tao Zhu was instantly persuaded:—"Even if you can abandon your duties, my Abyssal Ocean Society has vital logistical operations to delegate first!"

—"Rest assured. I shall grant you ample time for preparation. This foray is merely a preliminary reconnaissance,"-Zhou Hui reassured them.

He pressed his palms against the massive bronze doors, pushing them open.

Tao Zhu was grumbling under his breath, preparing to voice further objections, but the scene that greeted his eyes caused every sound to choke in his throat.

Demons!

Dense, boundless, sky-blotting swarms of demons—!

Like a suffocating tidal tsunami, an infinite ocean of ravenous, snarling fiends was surging in a terrifying torrent straight toward the palace gates, screaming with primordial bloodlust!

Even for a Mahayana titan, Tao Zhu's cognitive faculties paralyzed for an entire second.

Zhou Hui merely let out a soft, cold snort. Yet contrary to his indifferent, serene visage, an irresistible, primordial pressure resembling an ancient cosmic behemoth detonated from his physical form! The aura washed over the demon tide—and in that singular instant, millions upon millions of screeching fiends were crushed into microscopic dust!

It took merely the blink of an eye.

Had an observer blinked, they would have seen an inescapable, lethal ocean of demons one second, and absolute, pristine emptiness the next—with nothing remaining save for luminous moon-white pearls of purified energy drifting downward like gentle snowflakes, devoid of a single wisp of demonic miasma.

The grandmasters stood frozen in absolute petrification.

Though everyone knew Fujuan was terrifyingly powerful, never had they witnessed his martial supremacy in such raw, visceral clarity. With a mere breath, an unquantifiable army of demons—including warlords and demon sovereigns—had been reduced to cosmic vapor.

After an agonizing silence, Peilan rasped in a dry, trembling voice:

—"...You purified the demonic miasma as well?"

—"Merely a casual byproduct,"-Zhou Hui answered serenely. With the Demon Domain opened recently, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had captured innumerable demons; Lu Shulong had practically authored an entire anatomical encyclopedia on demon biology, let alone Zhou Hui.—"There is a specific technique to it, though explaining the mechanics is somewhat lengthy..."

—"You can refrain from speaking,"-Peilan cut him off decisively.

He had zero desire to hear Zhou Hui's lecture; his skull was throbbing.

—"This terrain... appears to be the Primordial God-Demon Battlefield from ten thousand years ago?"-Sikong Yao inquired.

Reluctant though he was, the moment the gates opened, his instinct was to observe every environmental variable—deliberately scrubbing Fujuan's godlike slaughter from his memory to prevent cognitive breakdown—in order to map out optimal escape routes.

Yet the towering Ascension Stairway, the celestial citadels hovering in the distant heavens, and the lush emerald plains below filled him with profound deja vu.

—"How do you know this is the God-Demon Battlefield?"-Tao Zhu asked suspiciously.

—"Ah, within the assembly's Trial System, this battleground scenario is featured. Did you not explore it?"-Sikong Yao countered.

—"Is it?"-Tao Zhu rubbed his belly with a guilty grimace. He had been consumed with managing the assembly's commercial logistics, failing to pay attention to trial minutiae... Wait a second:—"You are a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster, and you actually spent your days playing inside the trial simulations?!"

—"Is a Tribulation cultivator barred from participating?"-Sikong Yao complained:—"Even the Mahayana elders were having the time of their lives in there."

Tao Zhu found no words to reply.

Fortunately, Jiuzhai scrutinized the landscape, offering an authoritative diagnosis:

—"This is not the authentic God-Demon Battlefield, but a historical reenactment."

—"An illusion? That is manageable..."-Tao Zhu breathed a sigh of relief.

—"Manageable my foot!"-Jiuzhai cursed, glaring anxiously toward the valleys below:—"It is a *reenactment*! Not a phantom illusion! That means that unless the source of the demonic miasma is eradicated, demons here will manifest endlessly without cease, perpetually reenacting the slaughter of the ancient war!"

Hearing his warning, the gathering looked down in the direction Jiuzhai indicated. Sure enough, the seemingly fertile, verdant plains were boiling with plumes of thick black miasma, continuously corrupting the sky that Zhou Hui had just purified. Within the air, brand-new demonic entities were crystallizing into existence.

—"The reproduction rate is absurdly fast,"-Peilan noted, his brow furrowing tightly:—"Does this not mean an operative must remain stationed here indefinitely, slaughtering demons without pause to draw their aggro? Otherwise, we cannot maneuver, and demons might even breach the gate and invade our world!"

—"Or we could follow Ran Zhen's example: shut the doors and lock ourselves inside,"-Tao Zhu muttered sourly:—"Though considering we have zero inkling what lurks in the deeper sectors, I strongly advise against it. Otherwise, we might end up like those Dongli Garden idiots, trapping ourselves with no way for outsiders to locate us."

—"Tsk, this is ostensibly a celestial precinct of the Heavenly Court, so why is it reenacting such an apocalyptic nightmare...?"-Sikong Yao began to grumble, but choked mid-sentence.

No matter how thick-skinned, he sensed an ominous familiarity; from the terrain behind the gate, he detected the unmistakable stench of the Xiaoyao Palace, while Fujuan's posture toward the Heavenly Court was identical to how he had methodically dismantled the Xiaoyao Palace centuries ago!

Sikong Yao suffered a sudden, agonizing wave of acute stomach cramps.

As a Tribulation powerhouse, he had clung to the naive fantasy that achieving celestial ascension would free him from worldly tribulations. Now it appeared that no matter where he fled across the cosmos, endless nightmares awaited. Where on earth was he supposed to run?!

Just as Sikong Yao was questioning the meaning of existence, a newly crystallized demon let out a bloodcurdling shriek, bursting into a cloud of crimson gore.

—"There is no need for such convoluted fretfulness. Leave this threshold to Us—"-Ruwang announced, the opera mask across his face morphing into a wide, laughing maw.

Zhou Hui, however, shook his head:

—"Precious high-tier combat power should not be squandered on holding a doorway. While these hordes are troublesome, as long as others hold their attention, we can locate the demonic font and permanently terminate the loop."

—"Yet pinning down an endless legion of this magnitude is practically impossible,"-Jiuzhai noted:—"It requires an entire disciplined army."

—"Why do you think I chose the 'God-Demon War' as the simulated trial theme for the Immortal Arts Assembly?"-Zhou Hui asked in return, his lips curving into a gentle, enigmatic smile.

The grandmasters froze in stunned realization.

—"...You orchestrated the entire tournament just for this?!"
