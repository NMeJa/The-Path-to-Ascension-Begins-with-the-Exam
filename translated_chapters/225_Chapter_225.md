# Chapter 225: Destined by Fate

Zhou Hui was profoundly stirred.

He had not imagined that he would truly arrive at the place of origin.

To be precise, he had never imagined he would find himself so tantalizingly close to it.

Earlier, through the True Immortal's array disc, Zhou Hui had deduced the history of the Heavenly Court and this very cosmos. He knew this universe was born from the friction, collisions, and catastrophic explosions between several distinct cosmic planes. Consequently, he also knew that near the center of the universe—the epicenter of that primal detonation—lay a colossal machine installed by an advanced civilization. It was precisely because this machine cushioned the cosmic blast and broke down hazardous substances that the raging, violent energies of this universe were calmed into relative tranquility, allowing life to emerge—specifically life capable of flourishing amidst high concentrations of energy (spiritual qi). Humanity, too, had grasped the Laws (the Great Dao) through deciphering this machine, subsequently establishing the Heavenly Court.

Such a monumental entity, Zhou Hui had assumed, would be nigh-impossible to locate. Perhaps he would need to blast his way straight through the Heavenly Court and wrench its precise coordinates from the lips of some cryptic keeper of secrets. Indeed, the array disc had noted that the machine was situated in the deepest recesses of the cosmos, within the most enigmatic forbidden grounds of the Heavenly Court, in a place known to none. Who could have foreseen...

Wait. Did this secret realm not fulfill every single one of those conditions?!

Zhou Hui had an abrupt epiphany!

First, it lay exceedingly close to the deepest heart of the cosmos.

According to the origin of the universe, the closer one drew to the center, the more abundant the spiritual qi (energy) would become. As the supreme sovereign ruling over countless worlds, the Heavenly Court was naturally the most energy-saturated sanctuary across all realms; thus, it stood nearest to the cosmic center. (Of course, not the absolute center itself—that ground zero was the grinding confluence of several colliding universes, perpetually undergoing violent cataclysms where no biological organism could possibly survive. The Heavenly Court had occupied the optimal sweet spot: saturated with boundless spiritual energy, yet hospitable to life).

And the world Zhou Hui inhabited was originally a fractured piece that had plummeted from the Heavenly Court; naturally, it was not far from the machine either.

Second, this realm was indeed one of the Heavenly Court's forbidden domains: the Langhuan Forbidden Ground.

As the imperial archive of the Heavenly Court, Langhuan was worlds apart from the libraries of the Heavenly Spirit Sect or that other modern world; by no means was just anyone permitted entry. Moreover, it housed countless forbidden tomes wholly unsuitable for general viewing.

Thinking along those lines, it was entirely logical for this forbidden ground to serve as the research archive dedicated to the "Genesis Machine." And if its purpose was an archive and documentation repository, it stood to reason it would not be situated particularly far from the machine itself.

Finally: a place known to none.

While this criterion was somewhat subjective, what place could be more perfect than a shattered world fallen from the Heavenly Court, teetering on the brink of total annihilation at the hands of the demon race? Once this world crumbled into oblivion, the entrance to the secret realm would vanish as well, and all pathways leading from the outside would cease to exist forever.

This also explained why, within the "Heavenly Dao script," that "protagonist" had entered this secret realm without proceeding straight to the Heavenly Court—because his objective from the very beginning had been to destroy it! It was just that the "protagonist" was akin to a ravenous locust, wrecking wherever he went. When the script documented him laying waste to this secret realm, the "audience" had simply assumed it was routine locust behavior.

The only lingering puzzle was that collapsing the outer secret realm meant severing the passage connecting the Heavenly Court to this machine. What on earth was the Heavenly Court scheming? Had they completely abandoned research into this machine?

There didn't appear to be any researchers stationed in the vicinity either—not even an automated station logging data!

This was a machine tethered to the very survival of the cosmos!

Was leaving it wholly unattended truly wise?

Zhou Hui mused with furrowed brows, taking several steps toward the machine.

In tandem with his movement, almost simultaneously, several hands lunged from various angles to seize Zhou Hui's robes.

Zhou Hui turned in bewilderment to his companions.

—"Don't go any closer. That metal contraption looks utterly bizarre; it resembles some sort of arcane mechanism, and could well be lethal,"-Peilan warned.

—"Indeed! Even if you have no regard for your own skin, think of your children!"-Boss Tao pleaded, fraught with worry.

Though Ruwang remained silent, the mask over his visage morphed into a weeping face.

[What in heaven's name are they on about?]

Zhou Hui was caught between amusement and exasperation:

—"Don't make it sound as if I'm seeking an early grave. I understand its inner workings."

—"You understand them?"-Jiu Zhai gasped in astonishment.

—"In a manner of speaking!"-Zhou Hui replied.

Zhou Hui "simply" outlined the cosmic history he had deduced via the array disc.

Because it concerned the fundamental genesis of their world, no one complained about the complexity this time. All listened with rapt attention, unconsciously loosening their grip on Zhou Hui's robes, thereby allowing him to approach the machine.

True to its pedigree as the creation of an advanced civilization, the machine was marvelous.

Although it appeared vastly complex—and was indeed staggeringly intricate under the hood—its user interface was remarkably crisp and intuitive. It fell well within the threshold where intelligent beings from a lower civilization could readily comprehend and execute basic operations, provided they deciphered the written script.

—In all likelihood, was this not precisely how ancient humanity had decoded the machine and founded the Heavenly Court?

To this day, the so-called "Celestial Script" remained merely a mutated derivation of the characters inscribed upon the machine's interface. Any cultivator under the dominion of the Heavenly Court who sought to cultivate and ascend was obliged to use a variant of this writing—amounting to a cosmic [unification of the written word](# "Literal CN: 书同文 — historical allusion to the Qin dynasty's standardized writing system, 'All books written with the same characters'").

As for the Heavenly Court subsequently layering esoteric spiritual wards over Celestial Script to bar outsiders from reading their texts and engineer fresh barriers to knowledge—that was another matter entirely.

In short, as a cultivator—and one who had attained the Dao through deciphering the Heavenly Scripture—Zhou Hui naturally recognized these characters.

In truth, during his "transmigration" to that other world, the written language employed there was identical to this very "Celestial Script."

Hence, standing before this machine, Zhou Hui encountered not the slightest linguistic barrier.

However, Zhou Hui did not rush into inspecting the machine's operational functions. Instead, he pulled up the information interface, which documented the engineering process and underlying rationale behind the machine's construction.

Evidently, for the consortium of advanced civilizations that forged this apparatus, mitigating and halting the friction and collision of multiple universes was hardly a trivial endeavor! Consequently, they had not only logged the technical specifications in exhaustive detail, but had also "incidentally" appended the history and culture of their respective civilizations, even including biographical memoirs of notable figures. The data totaled several [yottabytes](# "Literal CN: 尧 (yotta) — SI unit prefix for 10^24, here referring to yottabytes of digital data")—a gargantuan archive sufficient to occupy a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster for tens of thousands of years.

[Splendid. At least it proves the outside isn't a Dark Forest,] Zhou Hui mused inwardly, indulging in a private joke only he could appreciate.

Naturally, Zhou Hui could not digest such a library in a brief sitting. Yet after studying the directory in silence for a moment, on some inexplicable whim, he entered his own name into the search bar using "Celestial Script."

The name "Zhou Hui" was not extraordinarily unique, yet neither was it particularly commonplace—especially among luminaries of other universes. Following his entry, exactly one line popped up:

> Zhou Hui, Aerospace Engineering Scientist; constructor and lead engineer of Universe XX's premier extraterrestrial base.

Zhou Hui's eyes widened slightly.

He attempted to click open the entry, but beneath the header lay nothing more than that single sentence—not a crumb of additional data.

It seemed that particular "Zhou Hui" had not been an earth-shaking titan within his own universe. Had this machine not been an inter-cosmic enterprise that cataloged the stellar exploration chronicles of various dimensions in meticulous detail, a figure of his standing would not have qualified for inclusion in the machine's archives at all. At this moment, even lodged within the catalog, he resembled nothing more than a trifling footnote, a fleeting comma—and following that comma came far more magnificent expansions.

Perceiving this, Zhou Hui could not help but smile. His joy and pride surpassed even the moment he had achieved Tribulation Transcendence.

The "child" that had merely taken its first tentative step toward the starry expanse upon his departure—when news of it reached him once more, had matured to the point of averting the annihilation of multiple universes and serving as the cradle for a newborn civilization! Could there possibly exist tidings more exhilarating than this?

Naturally, this memory might not represent literal objective truth.

It could have been Zhou Hui's genuine "past life."

Alternatively, it might have been an aggregation of information synthesized by the machine: when past Zhou Hui was racking his brain for a solution to the "Seven-Life Reincarnation," just as humans unconsciously consolidate past experiences during slumber and project them in the form of dreams, his immense power as a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster had inadvertently synthesized these disparate fragments, even weaving in data from the Heavenly Dao script. Ultimately, it had selected the life of this scientist—summarized in a single line—as a vessel, illustrating the methodologies of other civilizations through the guise of "transmigration" to illuminate a path forward for Zhou Hui.

Yet regardless of the truth, Zhou Hui felt boundless joy and harbored fresh anticipation:

If a universe devoid of spiritual energy could evolve to such heights, what of this present cosmos? What of his disciples? To what heights might they ascend?

Moreover, unlike that scientist who personally designed the extraterrestrial base yet, constrained by the frailties of age, ultimately failed to step beyond his mother planet, Zhou Hui—as a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster standing merely half a step from True Immortality—commanded an unfathomably vast lifespan. This time, he might truly witness the future of this civilization, and perhaps even reunite with his "homeland"—

—"Untiring... Untiring!"

Once more, several hands gripped the hem of Zhou Hui's robes, dragging him back to reality.

—"Are you certain there is truly nothing amiss with this 'mechanism'?"-Peilan inquired anxiously, daring not look at the machine's interface, much less its text:—"Your state appears somewhat... abnormal."

The others gazed at Zhou Hui with equal concern. In all their years of acquaintance, they had never once seen Untiring wear such a brilliant, profoundly intoxicated smile. It was enough to make one dread whether he had fallen under some sinister mind-controlling hex.

Zhou Hui, oblivious to their worries, replied with uncharacteristic exuberance:

—"There is nothing abnormal about me whatsoever! I am perfectly sound! In fact, I am contemplating how to live as long as humanly possible, so that I may watch my 'children' grow up!"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Companions")

[His mind has truly come unhinged.]

Every single soul present shared that identical thought.

When handling an unfortunate wretch suspected of being [possessed by demonic evils](# "Literal CN: 中邪 — stricken by evil spirits or demonic hexes"), the standard remedy was slapping several mind-clearing talismans onto their forehead to ascertain the ailment. Alas, no one on the scene dared attempt that on Zhou Hui; Untiring's combat prowess was so preposterously terrifying that everyone dreaded triggering a violent backlash that would culminate in a total party wipeout.

Thus, the companions traded uneasy glances. In the end, the seasoned healer Peilan cleared his throat and probed once more:

—"Ahem. Discovering the place of origin is magnificent indeed, Untiring, but do not forget: our purpose in venturing here was to locate Ran Zhen. Yet we appear to have found not a single trace of him!"

When dealing with the "possessed," if forceful remedies were off the table, the only alternative was invoking their original objective and familiar matters to anchor their wandering sanity.

—"That is simple enough,"-Zhou Hui said:—"This machine possesses surveillance capabilities. While its monitoring radius is not vast, it is more than sufficient."

So saying, he began operating the controls on the machine.

The others watched his movements with bated breath.

In their eyes, Untiring had merely glanced at this contraption a few times before losing his marbles. Even if this mechanism truly pinpointed Ran Zhen's whereabouts, ought they really follow instructions dispensed by such a monstrosity?

Yet as fate would have it, what one feared most was precisely what arrived.

—"Ah, located him,"-moments later, Zhou Hui announced cheerfully.

Not only had he pinpointed Ran Zhen, he had secured a complete topological map of the secret realm, and even full surveillance archives from when the Heavenly Court originally constructed it! Because this was a purely electronic apparatus devoid of a single shred of spiritual energy, the Heavenly Court had never realized the machine could monitor their conduct, nor that automated electronic eyes had blanketed the entirety of the secret realm and its outer perimeter.

Zhou Hui had no intention of dwelling on the Heavenly Court's historical antics for the moment. Prioritizing Ran Zhen, he turned to his companions—no, his veteran comrades:

—"Ran Zhen is currently... Huh?"

—"What is it?"-the others asked, tensing instantly.

—"We appear to be inside the belly of a Pixiu,"-Zhou Hui replied, frowning.

Departing from his earlier exuberance, his demeanor turned abruptly somber. This mercurial mood swing left everyone thoroughly baffled.

Without offering an explanation, Zhou Hui simply routed the surveillance feed for the others to view.

Thereupon, the assembly beheld not only the Pixiu, but also a cluster of True Immortals encircling it.

***

—"How ought we extract the treasures from its belly to inspect them?"-the True Immortals of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny debated, ringing around the Pixiu.

Though these True Immortals had initially come merely to pass the buck, now that they stood beside the Pixiu, they found themselves swept up in extraordinary enthusiasm. After all, rare was the opportunity to "tour" the Heavenly Emperor's private vault!

Yet on the matter of *how* to conduct this "tour," the gathered immortals hit a wall.

Everyone had heard of the Pixiu's trait of [taking in everything, expelling nothing](# "Literal CN: 只进不出 — legendary trait of the Pixiu / Tianlu, which possesses no anus and only hoards wealth"), but as to how to extract "wealth" already lodged inside its gut, even True Immortals possessed zero practical experience. A divine beast of this caliber, after all, was hardly something an ordinary immortal was qualified to keep.

They clamored in discord:

[—"Can we not simply have Lord Tianlu vomit it out?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"If he could just throw up whatever is upsetting his stomach, wouldn't all our worries vanish?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"How about feeding him some emetics?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Wait, might that not damage the treasures inside?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"This won't work, that won't work—what do you propose, then?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"I say we slice his belly open, inspect them, and stitch him back up with spells afterward."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

...

Listening to the discussion devolve into utter idiocy, the Pixiu could bear it no longer. It unleashed a ferocious roar that blasted the gathered immortals backward several paces.

—"What demonic rubbish are you spewing?!"-the Pixiu roared in fury:—"Is my belly something you can carve open whenever the whim strikes you?! Furthermore, once something has been swallowed down my gullet, how in the nine hells could it ever be vomited back up?!"

—"If what is swallowed cannot be spat back out, does that not render your belly utterly useless as a treasury?"-an immortal asked in bewilderment.

—"Fool! While what I swallow is never spat back up, it is transmuted into fortune corresponding to the treasure!"-the Pixiu proclaimed haughtily:—"Do not presume this lord is some useless, unproductive waste like a Taotie that only knows how to stuff its face... ?!"

Speaking up to this point, the Pixiu shuddered violently.

In the midst of [dragging and stepping on the Taotie](# "Literal CN: 拉踩 (lācǎi) — internet slang: elevating oneself by putting down or disparaging another; here, Tianlu trash-talking Taotie"), it had suddenly sensed an inexplicable wave of venomous hostility. Baffled, it peered around the vicinity, yet found no source for the animus—only the cluster of True Immortals continuing to play the fool.

Having grasped the Pixiu's operating mechanism, an immortal remarked dismissively:

—"In that case, what you swallow isn't a 'treasure' at all, but discarded junk, no?"

This time, before the Pixiu could erupt, another immortal snapped in rebuttal:

—"Idiot! Do you think this is one of those third-rate power-fantasy web novels you write, where you can feed it garbage and reap a windfall?! If you truly wish for Lord Pixiu's powers to take effect, it must be an authentic, bona fide treasure in every sense of the word."

—"...What's wrong with power-fantasy novels? Everyone descending for mortal tribulations loves them; they all beg me to write them!"-that immortal muttered under his breath:—"Besides, if it has to consume authentic treasures every single time, the Pixiu's maintenance costs are far too steep—it wouldn't even make a decent cheat finger."

—"Nobody asked you to use it as a cheat finger! Furthermore, you vastly underestimate the Heavenly Emperor's 'treasures.' How could a treasure fed to the Pixiu ever be something that could genuinely be consumed?"-yet another True Immortal scoffed.

A treasure that could not be consumed?

What did that mean?

The immortal was eager to press for clarification, only to discover that the one who had just answered him was the most venerable senior immortal in the Ministry of Celestial Destiny—a figure whose seniority surpassed even Lord Tianyan, the head of the department. Moreover, this ancient immortal was glaring at him with a chilling, sinister smirk. The younger immortal shuddered and clamped his mouth shut.

As facts proved, even celestial officials of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny knew how to read the room.

Seeing this, the remaining officials dared not pry further. They realized the "treasures" within the Pixiu's belly might not be so simple, and in all likelihood could not serve as a convenient scapegoat for passing the buck as they had envisioned.

Naturally, an attempt to pass the buck was still mandatory.

The immortals resumed their murmuring:

[—"Since it cannot be extracted, how ought we treat it? Surely we must summon a physician for this?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Wrong. By the looks of it, a treasure has malfunctioned and requires maintenance. What we need is not a physician, but an artifact refiner!"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Even if we summon an artifact refiner, how is he supposed to service the treasure? Crawl into Lord Tianlu's belly?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Whoever enters can never come back out!"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Since it is such a vital treasure, sacrificing an artifact refiner or two is hardly..."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

—"Bullshit! If devouring a few celestial officials would solve anything, I'd have eaten you lot first!"-the Pixiu roared.

It had stood guard over this place since before the Heavenly Court—no, even before the Demon Court had been established. It knew these bureaucrats inside out; the moment they arched their backs, it knew exactly what brand of filth they were about to discharge. But Lord Tianlu had zero intention of letting these celestial officials shirk their responsibilities.

—"What do you imagine is inside my belly? Is that something an artifact refiner could forge, let alone repair?"-the Pixiu barked testily:—"I already told you: celestial destiny has gone awry! Why are you crowding around me? Once you rectify celestial destiny, my stomach will naturally be cured!"

[Don't talk about celestial destiny as if it were stomach medicine.]

And wait: was it because celestial destiny went awry that the treasure inside the Pixiu malfunctioned, rather than the treasure malfunctioning causing celestial destiny to shift?

Did that not mean the "buck" had traveled in a complete circle right back to where it started?

The celestial officials traded glances, their expressions turning exceedingly troubled.

The entire reason they had scurried here in the first place was to dodge accountability for the drifting celestial destiny; in the end, were they still stuck having to fix celestial destiny?

Ultimately, it was that ancient celestial official who bowed deeply toward the Pixiu and inquired:

—"Presently, the customary Grand Fengshan Rite draws near, and all senior ministers have entered seclusion alongside the Heavenly Emperor, including Lord Tianyan of our Ministry of Celestial Destiny. Those of us remaining behind are merely insignificant minor immortals of meager cultivation, unfit to shoulder monumental burdens. Lord Tianlu, won't you point out a bright path? What must we do to rectify celestial destiny?"

[Passing the buck right back to me, are you?]

[If my suggestion fails, will you lot simply butcher this old Pixiu?]

Tianlu cracked open one eye and glared at the ancient official.

The old official maintained a posture of deferential humility, bowing low with eyes lowered, not daring to meet the Pixiu's gaze.

Behind him, the junior celestial officials exchanged glances, caught on, and mimicked the old official's posture in unison:

[—"We implore Lord Tianlu to grant us guidance!"](# "Speaker: Scribes of Celestial Destiny")

[Tsk. It seems the human Heavenly Court is nearing its twilight as well.]

—"A bright path, is it? Heh-heh,"-the Pixiu did not decline, actually offering a proposal:—"To rectify celestial destiny, wouldn't relying on your most routine method suffice? That so-called... Protagonist! Isn't handling such messes his entire purpose?"

[—"Protagonist?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

The celestial officials blinked in surprise.

One immortal remarked with an odd expression:

[—"It's not as though this is one of the stories we author; where would we procure a 'protagonist'..."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

—"No, hold on! If the Heavenly Dao of Langhuan Forbidden Ground truly went awry, we actually do have a protagonist!"-another True Immortal chimed in shrewdly:—"Do you recall? That Heavenly Dao ran through multiple cycles without success, yet on the sixth attempt, it succeeded—"

[—"Ah, yes! Who was that fellow again?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"I can't recall off the top of my head. But at the time, after repeated failures, no insider was willing to descend. As I recall, they simply grabbed a random, insignificant minor immortal who had committed an infraction and threw him down to test the waters. Who could have imagined he would actually pull it off! Even Lord Tianyan was astounded, directly rewarding him with the seat of Immortal Sovereign of the Immortal-Demon Realm."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"That reward was excessively lavish, even for a 'protagonist'..."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Chiefly because he obtained something of immense importance and merged it into his own bloodline. In that state, had he returned to assume an office in the Heavenly Court, it would have been awkward to handle with a routine promotion, so they let him preside as Immortal Sovereign for the time being."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"That certainly sounds very 'protagonist'!"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Then sending him to resolve it is indeed the best course? Let us return and see how to script it."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"We must first ascertain where the glitch occurred. I reviewed the logs of Langhuan Forbidden Ground just now: this current cycle's script hasn't truly commenced yet; it is merely in the preparatory stage."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Preparation for what?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Three critical milestones in the early phase: first, prepare the Immortal Slaying Sword; second, have the formation master unseal the secret realm designated for destruction; third, eradicate the bloodline of the preeminent cultivation clan in that region, rendering them incapable of breeding qualified cultivators."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"That Lord 'Protagonist' already succeeded in the previous run; shouldn't those have been achieved already?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Hard to say. With time rewound, everything reverted to its prior state. 'That Lord' slated to be slain by the Immortal Slaying Sword has undoubtedly revived, meaning it counts as incomplete. As for the cultivation clan's bloodline... although he seduced their daughter into ascending and rendered her barren, once time rewound, that also counts as incomplete."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Doesn't that mean not a single milestone is prepared?!"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Not entirely. The core of the second secret realm was destroyed. Strictly speaking, its location does not reside within the forbidden ground, so rewinding time had no effect upon it. Even if that formation master ventures in once more, he will merely trap himself inside..."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Stop dawdling here chatting! Let us go inspect the situation and determine how to pen the script!"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"And what about the 'lead actor'? His status has skyrocketed now; hauling him back out will prove quite troublesome..."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Ah, I remember now! His name was 'Tunhuo,' wasn't it?"](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

[—"Why does that name sound somewhat like a demon beast's..."](# "Speaker: Scribe of Celestial Destiny")

The flock of celestial officials chattered noisily.

In the end, they refrained from further pestering Lord Pixiu, hurrying back to the Ministry of Celestial Destiny to draft a new script.

The Pixiu gave a cold snort and sprawled onto the ground. Just now, it felt as though its stomach didn't ache quite as fiercely anymore. Regardless, judging by the antics of that pack of celestial officials, it had a distinct premonition that disaster was only a matter of time.

***

—"These are True Immortals?"-on the other side, beside the Genesis Machine, several cultivators who had witnessed the entire spectacle furrowed their brows deeply.

Although some among them had long abandoned aspirations of ascension, beholding True Immortals so thoroughly [grounded in bureaucratic mundanity](# "Literal CN: 接地气 (down-to-earth) — here used sarcastically to describe how petty, lazy, and pedestrian the True Immortals behave") struck them as utterly preposterous.

Zhou Hui gave a cold snort.

He possessed a mountain of commentary he could voice, yet ultimately uttered none of it, stating merely:

—"Regardless of their antics, we must first retrieve Ran Zhen."

—"Indeed, Ran Zhen,"-Peilan nodded in agreement:—"I didn't quite follow most of what those True Immortals discussed, but that 'formation master in the secret realm' they mentioned at the end—could that be Ran Zhen? It sounds exceedingly perilous."

—"We depart at once,"-Zhou Hui said decisively.

—"Then this mechanism..."-Jiu Zhai remarked, his gaze lingering upon the Genesis Machine.

Aside from Zhou Hui, he was arguably the person most captivated by this "mechanism."

—"Although no one can destroy this mechanism, the Immortal Realm clearly sought to obliterate the secret realm precisely because they did not wish anyone to approach it, correct? Thus, to guard against unforeseen mishaps, it would be prudent to station someone here to stand guard,"-Jiu Zhai proposed.

Jiu Zhai stopped just short of explicitly declaring, "Let me stay behind."

Zhou Hui cast a profound, searching look at Jiu Zhai.

—"That makes sense... Ruwang, I shall trouble you to stand guard here,"-Zhou Hui said to Ruwang instead:—"By the way, take my command token. Should any unexpected contingency arise, you may summon me."

—"As you command,"-Ruwang nodded, accepting the token.

Thereafter, he watched Zhou Hui lead the other cultivation titans away, Jiu Zhai's spirit orb trembling with lingering reluctance as he departed.

Once the group had departed, Ruwang tapped lightly upon the command token:

—"Did you hear all that, Xi'er?"-Ruwang asked.

—"Yes, Foster Father,"-a shadow manifested from within the token.

Yet it was not the "ordinary" Mo Xi, but the glamorous incarnation of Mo Xi hailing from the sixth cycle—having awakened memories spanning several lifetimes, he retained clear recollection of having been fostered by Ruwang during the third cycle as well.

This "Mo Xi" remarked:

—"Just now, Master instructed the 'me' of this lifetime to link this machine with the Langhuan Library of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree to guard against contingencies... Judging from the spiritual qi conduits, they were once interconnected; it shouldn't prove overly difficult. Even the present 'me' is fully capable of accomplishing it."

—"And then? What are your thoughts?"-Ruwang inquired.

—"I harbor no particular thoughts; after all, I am merely the residual silhouette of a bygone cycle,"-this "Mo Xi" smiled faintly:—"However, the 'me' of this lifetime is exceedingly furious! He feels that since someone possessed the audacity to author our destinies, we ought to return the courtesy—and script 'their destinies' properly in kind!"
