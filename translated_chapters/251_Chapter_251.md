# Chapter 251: Three Hundred Years Later (Part 2)

—"Now *this* is what the cultivation world is supposed to look like!"-Long Tianming proclaimed with deep emotion.

Under the tireless efforts of the recruitment squad leader, Long Tianming had finally shed his status as an "[out-of-school dropout](# "Literal CN: 失学儿童 — school dropout / child deprived of education; modern welfare/charity term humorously applied to rural youths prevented from cultivating")" and successfully departed that backward, backwater mountain village to embark upon the path of cultivation.

Even so, he had still missed the scheduled date for the orthodox Immortality Inquiry Ceremony. Fortunately, such occurrences were hardly uncommon; the Immortality Inquiry Halls ([admissions offices](# "Literal CN: 招生办 (zhāoshēngbàn) — modern school/university admissions office")) in each locality were always ready to process supplementary makeup registrations.

And so, following the recruitment squad, Long Tianming arrived at the nearest town.

Before he even stepped into the settlement, he was utterly captivated by the massive maple tree standing at the town entrance.

It was an ancient (?) maple of over two hundred years. A good ten meters tall, its lush, leafy canopy was crowned with crimson leaves like evening sunset clouds, casting a breathtaking splendor over heaven and earth.

Naturally, if it had merely been an ordinary maple tree, it wouldn't have shocked Long Tianming quite so much.

In fact, upon first laying eyes on the maple, he had only given it an extra glance or two, feeling a pang of regret that his transmigration had been a physical one and he hadn't brought his smartphone along to snap a photo. If he'd had a phone on him, he might have used it to charm the girls around him—perhaps even coax Renran into a selfie to post on his Moments! That was an opportunity he'd never had in his past life.

Just as Long Tianming was indulging in these daydreams, he watched several crimson leaves drift lazily down from the boughs.

While a common enough sight in autumn, these red leaves didn't obey the laws of gravity to tumble onto the dirt. Instead, they spun gracefully midair, pairing off two by two to form vibrant crimson butterflies that fluttered with effortless elegance right before Long Tianming's party.

[—"Welcome to Red Leaf Town. Do the esteemed guests require a guide?"](# "Speaker: Red Leaf Butterfly")

[—"T-the tree leaves can talk?!"](# "Speaker: Village Girl")

Long Tianming was stunned inward, but being a transmigrator from a modern society, he barely managed to keep his composure, unwilling to embarrass himself with an ignorant inquiry. Fortunately, the other girls in the squad were all rookie cultivators from remote rural villages who lacked worldly experience; quite a few let out candid gasps of wonder, asking the exact questions Long Tianming was dying to ask.

—"Merely a minor trick,"-Renran scoffed dismissively, casting a sideways glance at the maple tree.-"Though quite a few tree demons have awakened their intellect over the past few centuries. Interacting so proactively with humans... Did the Heavenly Spirit Sect strike some sort of agreement with them?"

—"There is an agreement, though it would be more accurate to say the agreement is part of the spellcraft itself,"-Zhou Hui replied.

His old habit of lecturing [acting as everyone's teacher](# "Literal CN: 好为人师 (hào wéi rén shī) — idiom from Mencius: to like lecturing others / playing the teacher") flared up, and he launched into a fluent dissertation:

—"Trees have always served as the guardian deities of their respective locales. They possess vast lifespans and abundant spiritual qi; lamentably, their intellect develops at a glacial pace, and they remain [aloof from mortal dust](# "Literal CN: 不食人间烟火 — not eating the food of common mortals; unworldly / aloof from mundane society"). Therefore, after the Immortality Inquiry Halls were established across every region, they began compiling census data on local residences and recruiting local merchants, storing these records directly inside the most spiritually receptive trees of each area. Using these spiritual trees as base stations, they carry out work akin to public notices and literacy campaigns. This process stimulates the spiritual trees to awaken their intellect and achieve sentience ahead of time. In turn, the trees that attain the Dao regard this service as a legitimate path of cultivation, proactively shouldering administrative duties to achieve a state of symbiosis between humans and the demon race."

[...Muttering all that gibberish. What an absolute bore.]

Renran rolled her eyes.

She had never possessed any patience for such pedantry.

Yet glancing around, she noticed the other members of the squad were listening with rapt attention—even the "Child of Destiny" was no exception.

After all, these children hailed from remote rural corners. While they had "fortunately" been discovered to possess spirit roots, they were still fraught with anxiety, terrified of being sent back. Especially with an example like Long Tianming—who had nearly been barred from cultivating by his own village—nobody dared to slack off; even if they couldn't fully comprehend the lecture, they strove their utmost to commit every word to memory.

Long Tianming, for his part, wasn't a blank slate. He was a student who had received a proper modern education; when he actually paid attention, his comprehension wasn't half bad.

Even if it veered completely off course.

[A base station...] Long Tianming pondered. [Hearing that word tossed around between cultivators and demon beasts feels completely bizarre. Then again, if admissions notices and school dropouts are already a thing, it shouldn't come as a surprise. It just goes to show the author of this story has pretty shoddy literary polish!]

[Still, that makes things easy for me. If the maple tree is a base station, does that mean this is a spellcraft network centered on the tree? At the very least, it's a local area network! The "magic tech level" of this world is way higher than I expected... Don't tell me this is some kind of Cyber-Xianxia world?!]

[Besides, the relationship between humans and demons sounds surprisingly harmonious. That's a pretty rare "setting."]

[Which means... there must be tons and tons of cute demon beast waifus!]

[Come to think of it, if there's a "setting" where women have an easier time cultivating, that must mean the cultivation world is predominantly female! Just like the character pools in modern "all-ages" [gacha games](# "Literal CN: 二游 (èryóu) — anime/ACG mobile games (e.g., Genshin Impact, Fate/Grand Order), famous for gacha character pools"), it's probably packed with scantily clad immortal fairy maidens! And didn't that 'Young Master Zhou' say that rumor was false anyway? So if I, as one of the rare male cultivators, make a [blockbuster debut](# "Literal CN: 一鸣惊人 — to astonish everyone with a single cry / blockbuster sensation") amidst the disdain of countless women, shatter their prejudices, and overturn the entire landscape of the cultivation world... isn't that the quintessential [shonen royal road trope](# "Literal CN: 王道桥段 (wángdào qiáoduàn) — classic, orthodox shonen protagonist trope/plotline")?!]

And just like that, Long Tianming thoroughly coaxed himself into high spirits.

Unfortunately, the grin plastered across his face as he indulged in these fantasies was so thoroughly lecherous that it made the surrounding girls distinctly uncomfortable, prompting them to take several subconscious steps away from him.

The squad leader—the very woman who had argued tooth and nail on Long Tianming's behalf, though Long Tianming hadn't bothered to remember her name; she was called Huo Yingqing, commonly addressed as Big Sister Huo—failed to notice anything amiss with Long Tianming.

She smiled toward Zhou Hui and Renran, who had taken the initiative to explain:

—"The two of you truly live up to your background from a secluded hidden sect. You know so much."

A "secluded hidden sect" was the excuse Zhou Hui and Renran had fabricated to blend into the squad of new recruits.

After all, their bearing was far too grand compared to children from backwater mountain hamlets; without such a cover story, gaining trust would have been nearly impossible.

Fortunately, Big Sister Huo had a brisk, carefree temperament and didn't pry into such matters.

Having offered her compliment, she turned to the Red Leaf Butterfly waiting nearby:

—"Lead us to the Immortality Inquiry Hall! By the way, are there still rooms available in the town's Immortality Inquiry Hall dormitory?"

—"Yes, plenty, the previous batch of recruits just departed,"-the Red Leaf Butterfly chirped warmly.-"However, if you desire superior accommodations, I can recommend the finest inns in town—"

Along with its words, several floating projection images popped into view.

Yet before the images could even resolve clearly, Big Sister Huo cut in:

—"Not necessary."

[—"Well then, after traveling so far, you must surely be parched and famished! Our town boasts exceptionally delectable local specialty snacks—"](# "Speaker: Red Leaf Butterfly")

—"Also not needed for now,"-Big Sister Huo said briskly.-"Cut the chatter and lead the way."

[—"Very well!"](# "Speaker: Red Leaf Butterfly")

Flapping its wings in mild disappointment, the Red Leaf Butterfly led the group into the town.

As they passed beneath the ancient maple tree, the group noticed a crude, modest shrine nestled at its base. It looked utterly unremarkable, resembling a makeshift altar haphazardly set up by superstitious rural peasants. Big Sister Huo, however, patted her waist pouch, fished out a spirit stone shaped like a copper coin, and tossed it onto the shrine.

—"Thank you for the tip!"-the Red Leaf Butterfly instantly brightened up.

—"We will be staying here for two days; this afternoon is free time,"-Big Sister Huo announced to the others.-"You can come here and spend one spirit coin to hire a red butterfly as a guide to take you around to play, but don't fall for its absurd advertisements and buy useless junk."

—"Immortal maiden, I have professional ethics! Besides, my contract strictly forbids me from pitching goods to newcomers!"-the Red Leaf Butterfly protested.

—"It had better,"-Big Sister Huo chuckled coldly.

Nevertheless, upon hearing the words "free time," the young girls in the squad lit up with excitement.

It was little wonder they were so thrilled. Once they entered the town, even Long Tianming, who had been attempting to maintain an airy, aloof posture, couldn't resist craning his neck to gawk in every direction.

Here, the Red Leaf Butterflies fluttering amidst the crowds were nothing extraordinary. Floating alongside them were origami paper cranes zipping overhead carrying food delivery boxes, as well as earthworm shadows suddenly burrowing up from underground to transport freight.

Yet compared to these quaint little novelties, what truly commanded attention were the myriad [golden puppets](# "Literal CN: 金人机关 (jīnrén jīguān) — golden puppets / mechanical automata, originally referencing Qin Shi Huang's twelve giant bronze colossi, here applied to cast-iron magitech golems").

Some stood a towering seven to eight meters tall, squatting silently alongside the thoroughfares like multi-story buildings. Inside their bodies, just like within real edifices, people moved about and even resided, with wisps of cooking smoke drifting lazily from their ears and nostrils. Other golden puppets were of ordinary human stature, either mingling with the pedestrians or working in storefronts; for instance, outside a noodle shop, a golden automaton was deftly pulling dough into paper-thin ribbons, looking for all the world like a veteran pastry chef.

Naturally, what seized Long Tianming's attention most were the combat-oriented models.

Displayed within the most prominent storefront window at the street corner were not mannequins draped in exquisite silk robes, but fully armed "defense-type" golden puppets. Clad in imposing armor incised with glowing, circuit-like cyan-blue runes, they held keen broadswords or hoisted massive twin warhammers, looking poised to pounce upon an enemy at any moment.

—"Incredible robots! Is this the kind of town where cultivators live?!"-Long Tianming gasped in marvel.

For an instant, he even wondered if he had transmigrated not into a cultivation realm, but into a futuristic sci-fi world!

—"No, this is merely a mortal town,"-Renran replied. Acting was hardly her forte, so her impression of a gentle, delicate maiden was half-hearted at best, barely concealing her haughtiness.-"These 'robots' you speak of... do you mean the golden puppets? They are nothing more than inferior contraptions fashioned from cast iron. The amount of spiritual qi they can hold is minuscule, their intelligence is rudimentary, and their operation is exceedingly clumsy. Only mortals would treat them as treasures."

—"However, cast-iron mechanisms possess the distinct merits of low manufacturing cost and minimal spiritual power consumption. For the price of a few low-grade spirit stones, even mortals devoid of spiritual power can pilot and operate them,"-Zhou Hui interjected. He was exceedingly pleased with these golden automata.

Especially compared to the robots Long Tianming spoke of: in a world governed purely by physical laws, the developmental costs of such contraptions alone were enough to cause a massive headache. How could they compare to the convenience of the cultivation realm, where a few crumbs trickling down from high-level cultivation research were enough to spark an entire technological revolution?

Zhou Hui remarked with deep satisfaction:

—"You see, precisely because of their widespread adoption, the world has completely transformed in a mere few centuries."

—"So that's how it is. I was wondering why the mortal realm had changed so rapidly,"-the "Old Grandpa" in Long Tianming's mind muttered.

—"Indeed, indeed!"-the Red Leaf Butterfly chimed in eagerly.-"Our region has always been famed for high-grade cast-iron ore. Regrettably, because cast iron is mere common metal with few uses, even mortal imperial officials looked down on cast-iron weaponry. But now that these low-energy automata have gained popularity, cheap and sturdy cast iron has become the premier material for crafting puppets, which is why our town has flourished so rapidly!"

As it spoke, it enthusiastically began its pitch again:

—"If you are interested, this humble one can guide you to the Golden Puppet Workshop! Just as this young master said, the low-energy puppets we manufacture are ideal for newly initiated cultivators like yourselves. Even if you cannot afford a complete puppet bodyguard just yet, browsing our compatible weaponry is a marvelous experience!"

—"...We don't need anything,"-Big Sister Huo refused once more.

This time, however, her tone was not quite as resolute as before; she seemed somewhat tempted.

After all, while the children could only gawk at the spectacle, she could actually afford one. Purchasing a cleaning automaton to place in her cave dwelling would certainly be far more relaxing than expending her own spiritual energy to tidy up.

At present, the only one still stubborn was Renran.

—"Tch. It merely offers mortals an excuse to be lazy. What possible use is this for cultivating immortality?"-Renran mumbled under her breath.

—"Quantity breeds quality. Though it may be low-level technology, once popularized and commercialized, the speed at which puppet mechanics evolve will surpass anything you could ever imagine—far outstripping what a handful of mighty figures could achieve [working behind closed doors](# "Literal CN: 闭门造车 (bìmén-zàochē) — building a cart behind closed doors; working in isolation detached from practical reality"),"-Zhou Hui told Renran.-"You have remained in the wilderness for far too long; you ought to visit a proper immortal city to see for yourself."

[...And just who was it that scared me into hiding in the first place?!]

Faced with this [living ancestor](# "Literal CN: 活祖宗 — sarcastic term for someone so untouchable, venerated, and terrifying that one must cater to their every whim"), Renran seethed with rage for a brief second before swallowing her indignation.

*

Guided by the Red Leaf Butterfly, the group wove through the town streets and checked into the temporary dormitory of the Immortality Inquiry Hall.

After partaking of the lunch provided by the hall and freshening up with a quick wash, the young girls embarked upon their most eagerly anticipated free time.

Regrettably, this had nothing to do with Long Tianming. Rather, the squad's free time had only come about because of him: while everyone else was touring and having fun, Big Sister Huo had to take Long Tianming, Renran, and Zhou Hui to undergo the "Immortality Inquiry Ceremony."

Yes, Renran and Zhou Hui had to participate as well.

This wasn't entirely an excuse fabricated to observe the "Child of Destiny." Over the course of three hundred years of development, rogue cultivators had virtually ceased to exist in this realm. Whether one hailed from a secluded hidden clan or was entirely self-taught, anyone wishing to travel had to first undergo the ceremony and obtain a "Cultivator Identity Certificate." Otherwise, forget entering cultivator territories—one couldn't even set foot inside a slightly larger mortal city!

Even a Tribulation Transcendence titan like Renran had to acquire this permit if she wanted to follow the Child of Destiny roaming across the Five Domains and Six Abysses. That was the primary reason she had sought out a recruitment squad to join in the first place.

As for Zhou Hui, he actually possessed such a certificate already—in fact, he had been the very first recipient.

Everyone agreed that only he was qualified to hold identification number 001. Moreover, the entire cultivator identity information network had been engineered by Zhou Hui himself, making it only natural that he held the premier registry. However, pulling out that particular certificate would be far too conspicuous; it would be tantamount to broadcasting to the entire world that the Untiring Immortal Venerable was wandering about incognito.

Hence, Zhou Hui required an ordinary cultivator license.

While he could easily have forged one on the fly for his travels, Zhou Hui felt it best to play the role to the hilt and experience the coverage of the cultivator identity network firsthand. Thus, he had also adopted the guise of a scion from a secluded hidden clan to join a squad.

—Renran, however, firmly believed Zhou Hui was doing this purely to [rub salt in her wounds](# "Literal CN: 故意寒碜她 — intentionally embarrass, mock, or belittle her").

Long Tianming didn't notice anything out of the ordinary.

In his mind, a "Protagonist" was supposed to participate in such ceremonies alongside his companions. If anything, he felt the audience was far too sparse; it would have been far more stylish if all the girls on their "free excursion"—or even the entire town—gathered to witness his grand awakening!

At this thought, however, an inexplicable pang of apprehension tugged at him.

—"There aren't any problems with that technique you taught me, right?"-he inquired of the 'Old Grandpa' within his mind.-"This ceremony won't detect something sinister inside me and force me to flee to the ends of the earth as a wanted fugitive, will it?"

—"How could that be possible?! What this venerable one imparted to you is a technique of the righteous, orthodox sects! Why would I ever harm you?!"-the "Old Grandpa" frantically distanced himself from the accusation.

Naturally, he would never admit that back before he understood the modern market, his previous few hosts had been flagged and dragged away by local authorities in precisely this manner. That debacle had spooked him so badly that he no longer dared impart overly aggressive techniques or utilize conspicuous methods, lest the host he had worked so hard to find be confiscated once again.

...Wait, hold on?

The way this current host phrased the question... almost sounded as if the boy already knew he was shady?

The "Old Grandpa" asked cautiously:

—"Are you suspecting me?"

—"Not at all!"-Long Tianming chuckled it off.

Naturally, he didn't trust the "Old Grandpa" in the slightest.

After all, in web novels featuring portable old grandpas, aside from the earliest pioneering classics, nine out of ten old grandpas in later books harbored malicious intentions. The remaining one tenth were usually reformed by the protagonist's charisma and willingly sacrificed themselves—and even among that tiny minority, the spirit usually turned out to be a stunning fairy in disguise.

Long Tianming didn't believe for a second that his "Old Grandpa" was the latter.

Not that it mattered. He was the "Protagonist"! If he didn't even have this much courage, what kind of protagonist was he? At any rate, according to the "plot," he was bound to defeat the "Old Grandpa" in the end anyway.

Long Tianming was brimming with inexplicable confidence.

The "Old Grandpa" let out a cold sneer. He could actually faintly read Long Tianming's thoughts—a residual perceptual ability left over after wandering for so long and having his strength degraded. In his prime, let alone reading another's mind, he could have effortlessly stimulated their desires and rewritten their brain in an instant. Unfortunately, his power was currently deficient, leaving him no choice but to make use of this foolish host.

No matter. Whatever schemes this host harbored, as long as the boy grew stronger under his parasitic tether, he would inevitably become his supreme nourishment!

The "Old Grandpa" was likewise brimming with inexplicable confidence.

Pity that neither of the two realized that before they had even set foot into the ceremonial hall of the Immortality Inquiry Hall—from the very moment they crossed the entrance threshold—invisible golden filaments carried on a gentle breeze had already penetrated Long Tianming's body, scanning him thoroughly from inside out.

Simultaneously, a voice resonated within Zhou Hui's sea of consciousness:

[—"Master, I have concluded verification."](# "Speaker: Pan Xiaogu / Yao'er")

—"How is it, [Yao'er](# "Literal CN: 幺儿 (yāo'er) — affectionate term for the youngest child / baby of the family")?"-Zhou Hui inquired, lifting his hand ever so slightly. Several strands of golden thread gently twined around his index finger, akin to a small child hooking their finger around an elder's hand.

The one currently conversing with Zhou Hui was none other than the youngest disciple he had taken in three hundred years ago.

That Genesis Machine.

Naturally, having been accepted as Zhou Hui's disciple, it could no longer be addressed merely as the "Genesis Machine" or "Primordial God," much less by labels like "Antimatter Energy Stabilizer." Zhou Hui had originally intended to bestow a proper name upon it and give it his own surname, but after Yuwen Xuan threw an almighty tantrum, the naming rights of this junior disciple had unfortunately fallen into Eldest Senior Brother's hands.

And so, this machine received the name:

[Pan Xiaogu](# "Literal CN: 盘小谷 — pun combining 'Pangu' (盘古, the mythical creator of the universe) and 'Xiaogu' (little grain/cereal), bestowed by the gluttonous Taotie Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan").

Well, at least it had an origin story.

In any case, the machine... no, Pan Xiaogu now; in short, Pan Xiaogu didn't care in the least. To it, names were mere identifiers, and it had acquired several since its birth! Only Zhou Hui felt a lingering sense of guilt, finding the name too awkward to say aloud, which was why he generally addressed it as "Yao'er."

Yet though Zhou Hui called it so, the machine was in truth the eldest of all his disciples. After all, the very creation of heaven and earth had been its handiwork!

The matter concerning this "Child of Destiny" had also been reported to Zhou Hui by it.

—"It is indeed an energy wave transmitted from an outer universe—what is commonly referred to as a 'transmigrator,'"-Yao'er reported.

However, according to Pan Xiaogu, the arrival of these "transmigrators" was by no means accidental.

After casting the energy-stabilizing machine into this cosmos, the outer universe hadn't abandoned it entirely. From time to time, they transmitted radio waves inward to inspect whether the machine was functioning normally, whether spatial energy remained stable, whether excessive energy was leaking, and whether it posed any threat to the outer universe.

As the subject of inspection, Yao'er possessed no authority to block or intercept these transmissions.

—"However, the agency I entrusted with managing humanity... by which I mean, 'Heaven'—or the Heavenly Court—could. They were carefully handpicked by me, after all, and possess considerable intelligence. They noticed the existence of these 'transmigrators' very early on, and discerned that I could not resist these energy waves and was obligated to comply with certain requirements. Consequently, the Heavenly Court designated them as 'Children of Heaven's Choice' and even conceived the notion of leveraging them to wrest authority away from me."

Yao'er provided Zhou Hui with a detailed explanation regarding the origins of the "Children of Heaven's Choice":

—"The Heavenly Court would scout entities bearing similar electromagnetic waves... souls... to impersonate external energy waves and deceive me. Later, they codified this into a formalized ritual; even when not intended to deceive me, they would dispatch vast numbers of trial-takers known as 'Children of Heaven's Choice' across various minor worlds to plunder resources. At the same time, the Heavenly Court interferes with genuine 'transmigrators' through chaotic informational noise, leading them to believe they serve the Heavenly Court and prompting them to act in accordance with Heaven's preordained scripts."

—"Hmph,"-Zhou Hui muttered, casting a gaze over Long Tianming.-"So, this one belongs to the latter category?"

—"Yes, which is why I cannot simply terminate it,"-Yao'er admitted apologetically, though its tone quickly rallied.-"However, it has suffered severe corruption. Not only has it been tampered with by the Heavenly Court, but another stray wave of discarded dark matter energy is also eroding it. I am entirely capable of 'stripping away the hazardous substances' for it; the only side effect is that it will impair some of its cognitive faculties."

—"It'll turn him into a retarded cripple, you mean!"-Zhou Hui instantly pierced through the machine's sanitized euphemism, asking bluntly.

[—"It is of no consequence. It is merely an energy wave intended for diagnostic inspection; it was already quite simplistic (retarded) to begin with."](# "Speaker: Pan Xiaogu / Yao'er")

[...Has this child also been corrupted by its senior brothers and sisters?]

Back at the beginning, it had been a bit slow and wooden, but it certainly hadn't possessed this kind of snarky personality!

Zhou Hui harbored his suspicions, though he wasn't entirely certain what kind of personality a machine was supposed to have in the first place.

—"Let us hold off for now,"-Zhou Hui sighed, choosing not to pass summary judgment upon Long Tianming.-"Since it is an energy wave meant for diagnostic inspection, it serves nicely to test the stability of our current cultivation system. Besides, once its inspection is complete, it will carry data back with it, will it not? I am somewhat curious about this outer universe."

—"Understood, Master,"-Yao'er responded briskly.

Just as it replied, the grand doors of the Immortality Inquiry Hall's ceremonial chamber swung open:

[—"The ceremony is prepared."](# "Speaker: Hall Official")

[—"Esteemed guests, please enter."](# "Speaker: Hall Official")
