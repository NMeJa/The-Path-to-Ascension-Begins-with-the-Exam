# Chapter 244: Settling Accounts

—"It's settled..."-Qu Qianxing murmured in a daze, gazing up at the sky.

To be honest, Qu Qianxing felt no profound emotion regarding the fact that he had just slain a True Immortal—and one of exalted status who oversaw the Heavenly Dao, no less.

For one, Qu Qianxing had not recovered the memories of the previous cycles. For another, he had no idea who Tian Yan was, let alone that this very person was the culprit who had orchestrated the annihilation of the Heavenly Spirit Sect multiple times and pitted the martial siblings against one another. His resolve to kill Tian Yan had stemmed partly from the utter revulsion he felt upon sensing the connection between them, but mostly from the simple desire to protect that world... or more accurately, to protect the world his Master was guarding.

Thus, let alone a Chief of Celestial Destiny, even if he had slain the Celestial Emperor himself, Qu Qianxing's impression would likely have been no different from having solved a difficult problem that Master hadn't taught yet.

What truly left Qu Qianxing stunned was the power continuously surging into his body.

Power bestowed by his junior martial brothers and sisters.

Heaven only knew what methods they had used to gather it; the energy was vast, heterogeneous, and wildly chaotic. Yet had it not provided the most critical reserve of resources, relying solely on Qu Qianxing's own strength would truly have left him with no choice but mutual destruction with Tian Yan—and even then, he would have had to gamble on the odds of success, for the other party was no ordinary True Immortal.

Qu Qianxing understood every single step required to "solve the problem" with absolute clarity, and precisely because of that, he knew better than anyone just how much this power had aided him.

And precisely because he understood, he was somewhat in a daze.

For a person like Qu Qianxing, opportunities that could leave him so dumbfounded were exceedingly rare.

And as he stood there in a stupor, lost in unknown thoughts, a familiar voice rang out behind him:

[—"Second Senior Brother, have you completely settled it?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Qu Qianxing answered subconsciously:

—"Not necessarily. He didn't seem so easy to kill, and even if it succeeded, it is merely the beginning."

—"True enough. It's the Heavenly Court, after all..."-the person (?) behind him sighed.

—"...If you knew it was the Heavenly Court, why did you get involved?!"-At this moment, Qu Qianxing finally snapped out of his daze.

Yet before he could sort out his own thoughts, he habitually assumed his usual commanding posture, furrowing his brows as he turned to glare behind him.

Behind him stood two of his junior brothers: Guan Ye and Lu Shulong.

To be precise, Guan Ye was riding atop Lu Shulong in his dragon form. His expression was as gloomy as ever, but the look in his eyes was faintly terrifying, appearing markedly different from normal.

Qu Qianxing sensed something amiss, but didn't dwell on it. At this moment, his emotions were a tangled mess, and only his mouth remained stubborn:

—"Provoking deities! Never mind that you might not escape with your lives—what is to become of our sect?!"

[In the past, we never provoked them, and they never let us off easy either!]

Guan Ye and Lu Shulong, the two who had recovered their memories, exchanged a glance. They confirmed that this Second Senior Brother of theirs had not recovered his past memories. This was rather bizarre. One had to know that Qu Qianxing had an extraordinarily phenomenal memory—he was someone capable of scoring top honors in the imperial civil exams, after all. Before he embarked on immortal cultivation, he could still recite backwards books he had read at the age of five, remembering with crystal clarity the exact date he had read them and what had happened that day.

Thus, in the previous cycles, even when Master painstakingly wiped everyone's memories, all it took was the slightest trigger for the first one to remember to inevitably be this Second Senior Brother with the best memory (and the biggest grudges).

In this lifetime, things had already escalated to such a degree, yet Second Senior Brother still hadn't remembered. It was truly strange.

Unfortunately, there was no answer to this question for the moment. Lu Shulong inquired:

—"Since Second Senior Brother knew provoking the Heavenly Court would cause trouble, why did you still do it?"

—"Because my plan ensured it would bring no trouble to the sect,"-Qu Qianxing replied full of confidence, sounding as righteous and assured as when he normally explained cultivation concepts to his junior brothers.-"According to my plan, so long as the demon race defeated the celestial soldiers and the 'Demon Sovereign' slew the heavenly generals, this matter would remain strictly between the Heavenly Court and the demons, having nothing to do with us."

Hearing this, Lu Shulong ground his teeth.

Seeing this Second Senior Brother looking so matter-of-fact infuriated it, and reminded it of why it had rushed over in the first place.

Yet before Lu Shulong could voice a few biting sarcasms, Fourth Senior Brother's sinister voice rang out:

—"The Demon Sovereign slays the heavenly general? Were you that confident you would win?"

[It's over, Fourth Senior Brother is furious!]

Lu Shulong shuddered.

Unfortunately, only Lu Shulong was familiar with Guan Ye's emotional shifts. Qu Qianxing was not. To Qu Qianxing, Guan Ye was merely a low-maintenance, honest junior brother; he had rarely paid attention to the other's feelings.

Or rather, with the sole exception of Master, Qu Qianxing did not care about anyone's emotions—including his own.

Even so, Qu Qianxing was not a complete [automaton](# "Literal CN: 人机 (NPC/bot) — someone devoid of human emotion"). Guan Ye's blatant grievance was something even he could perceive. What was more, the power provided by Guan Ye and Ran Deng was currently coursing through his body. Without that energy, Qu Qianxing could never have triumphed so easily and without injury. From any perspective, he had to feel grateful for his junior siblings' assistance.

Because of this, Qu Qianxing, who originally hadn't wanted to elaborate, still explained:

—"I had a measure of certainty. I seem to... share a connection with the Heavenly Court,"-Qu Qianxing said, unable to suppress a look of utter disgust as he reached this point.-"Furthermore, that 'deity' happened to be tied to the causality of the Heavenly Dao. You know what I begged Master to teach me; it was the perfect chance to test the results and sever this thread entirely to prevent future troubles."

—"As expected of Second Senior Brother,"-Guan Ye gritted out.

His words were no different from usual, but his tone was so sinister he sounded like a vengeful ghoul crawling up from the eighteenth level of hell, every single syllable oozing black malice:

—"Did Senior Sister and I meddle where we weren't wanted?"

Lu Shulong's scalp went completely numb.

It remembered that in the fourth cycle, it had been flayed alive while Fourth Senior Brother spoke in this exact tone!

Had Guan Ye not been sitting on its back at this very moment, it would practically have swished its tail and bolted.

Qu Qianxing was taken aback as well.

Regrettably, unlike his other martial siblings, Qu Qianxing not only lacked memories, but even residual emotional echoes had not remained within him. It was as though his previous lifetimes had never existed at all. Consequently, he had zero comprehension of his fourth junior brother's deep-seated resentment, feeling only a sense of bewilderment.

Yes, bewilderment.

Beyond Guan Ye's "baffling" fury, what perplexed Qu Qianxing even more was precisely what Guan Ye had referred to: the "help from his junior siblings."

He did not know how he ought to respond.

Of course, of course. As the public face of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the appointed successor to the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Qu Qianxing knew that by conventional reason, when his junior siblings helped him, he knew how he was supposed to act. He wasn't some unreasonable, feral beast cub like Eldest Senior Brother, after all. Normally, Qu Qianxing observed etiquette; whenever he troubled his junior siblings with anything, he would thank them properly.

This time, thanks were needed all the more.

After all, his junior siblings had saved his life.

After all, after he had stubbornly severed contact with the sect, provoked the Heavenly Court, dragged in Guan Ye and Mo Bei who were still stranded here, and nearly detonated himself in mutual destruction, his junior siblings had still chosen to bestow power upon him to save his life.

And yet, that feather-light "thank you" felt heavy as a thousand catties at this moment, pressing down upon Qu Qianxing's handsome lips, refusing to be uttered no matter what.

Qu Qianxing hadn't even realized it himself, but he was actually dreadful at expressing gratitude to others.

Those he struggled to thank even included his most revered Master.

Back then, when Zhou Hui had saved him, Qu Qianxing had arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect harboring doubt and scrutiny. He had observed for several years before finally kneeling to become his disciple in earnest, thereby missing the opportunity to speak his gratitude aloud. As a result, that gratitude had been locked deep within his heart alongside his other emotions, fermenting and growing until it became too heavy to ever extract.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui didn't care.

This protective master had never needed a disciple's gratitude to begin with. What was more, in his eyes, even the eldest disciple's gluttony was endearing, and the second disciple's arrogance and fierce self-esteem were far from flaws—without that very pride, Qu Qianxing likely would never have endured his darkest days.

Yet when this pride was displayed in front of his junior brothers, matters took a turn for the worse.

Qu Qianxing stared straight into Guan Ye's face.

He already had no idea how to handle this, and now, confronted with his junior brother's inexplicable anger, his instinct was to fire back with the exact same haughty attitude:

—"Of course it was meddling! Since I had already decided to handle it myself, I never needed you to take it upon yourselves to—!!!"

*SMACK!*

Before Qu Qianxing could finish his sentence, he took a heavy "slap" to the face.

No, it wasn't a slap either.

What lashed across Qu Qianxing's face was the tail end of Guan Ye's tailbone spine. Refined by Guan Ye into something akin to an unbreakable purple-gold whip—thick and rock-hard—it shattered Qu Qianxing's protective barrier in a single blow, its sharp bone spurs slicing open his skin to carve a massive gash from above his left eye all the way down to the corner of his left mouth.

—"*Hiss...*"-Lu Shulong sucked in a breath of phantom pain.

Even watching that bloody gash made it throb with ache.

Qu Qianxing himself hadn't processed it, staring at Guan Ye in utter disbelief. He clearly had never imagined, nor could he fathom, why Guan Ye had suddenly struck him.

From this angle, Fourth Senior Brother was truly the one existence in the entire sect against whom nobody raised their guard.

Otherwise, when Fourth Senior Brother went mad and purged the sect in the fourth cycle, it wouldn't have gone so smoothly.

Lu Shulong thought.

Even after experiencing the fourth cycle, no one dared to blame Guan Ye—when an honest, dutiful man was pushed to such an extreme, any sensible person would reflect on whether they themselves had gone too far.

In this life, Guan Ye hadn't been driven to that brink, but the way he looked at Qu Qianxing was nearly identical to the fourth cycle.

—"'Take it upon ourselves'? Isn't the one who loves acting on his own the most Second Senior Brother yourself?"-Guan Ye accused in a frigid voice.

His resentment coiled toward Qu Qianxing like a man-eating phantom:

—"The one who disregarded Master's wishes to fight Eldest Senior Brother and trigger a war across the cultivation world was you! The one who went mad trying to find Master, fell into becoming a Demon Sovereign, and sacrificed the entire sect to feed Eldest Senior Brother was also you! Not to mention what you just did—proclaiming with every breath that it was for the sect, only to turn around, abandon everyone, and prepare to blow yourself up! You'd be done with it once you died, but never mind what Master would feel upon learning of it—did you ever consider the future of the sect?! Were you planning on dumping everything onto Eldest Senior Brother?!"

—"I didn't..."-Qu Qianxing attempted to deny it.

He remembered none of the first two-thirds of Guan Ye's accusations, which could be set aside for now, but the final accusation was something Qu Qianxing absolutely refused to acknowledge.

Dumping the sect onto Eldest Senior Brother?!

Was there any practical difference between that and sect annihilation?!

[Still, Master is still around. If I died, he should have enough time to train another... right?]

Thinking up to this point, Qu Qianxing finally felt, for the very first time, a sliver of guilt over his reckless actions.

He pondered back and forth. Not a single one of his junior brothers or sisters could put his mind at ease. What was worse, looking at the disciples Master had taken in, each felt more hopeless than the last—he didn't dare place any hopes on a newcomer either.

Yet this meager degree of reflection from Qu Qianxing was nowhere near enough for a fourth junior brother whom he had utterly enraged!

—"You didn't what? Did you not take the sect to heart, or have you simply never regarded us at all?"-Guan Ye demanded, laughing out of sheer fury as his resentment thickened by several layers.-"*Jie-jie*, Second Senior Brother, don't rush to deny it! I never said you intended to betray the sect. I also know that under *normal* circumstances, you genuinely want to be a good Second Senior Brother. But! 'Inheriting the sect' is, in your heart, merely 'homework' assigned by Master, isn't it?! And we junior brothers and sisters are nothing more than [tools](# "Literal CN: 工具人 (tool persons / utility pawns) — people used purely to accomplish an objective") for you to complete your assignment—"

As he spoke, Guan Ye's grievance condensed into a physical manifestation, transforming into countless whip shadows that lashed toward Qu Qianxing.

By now, Qu Qianxing had regained his senses.

Taking one "whip" from his junior brother was enough; taking a few more would be sheer idiocy.

Qu Qianxing naturally wouldn't let himself be an idiot. What was more, his golden lightning was the ultimate counter against resentment; before that dark malice could graze his robes, it was shattered into shreds by thunderbolts.

Regrettably, Qu Qianxing couldn't simply blast the source of the grievance—his dear junior brother Guan Ye—with golden lightning the way he had done to Tian Yan. That resentment billowed endlessly from Guan Ye's body as though connected straight to the underworld, coalescing into vast dark clouds that refused to disperse.

And Guan Ye's words, like that churning malice, coiled around Qu Qianxing and poured straight into his ears:

—"No, not even tools! A handy tool at least has utility. Most of the time, we're probably nothing more than troublesome burdens to you, aren't we? You neither acknowledge our abilities nor trust our feelings. *Tch.* You act as if in the entire sect from top to bottom, only you understand Master's plight! But after so many repeated cycles, when Master sees that among all the disciples of our sect, we haven't united a single time—what must Master be feeling?!"

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing's heart skipped a beat.

Rationally, he still wanted to retort and reprimand him, because he still couldn't comprehend Guan Ye's accusations. At most, he had only wanted to abandon his junior siblings "this once"—after all, the opponent was the Heavenly Court! Not cutting ties completely would have brought ruin upon the sect.

Yet those explanations circled in his mouth several times without ever being spoken.

Unlike his inability to "say thanks," as Guan Ye laid out his accusations, Qu Qianxing felt demonic qi within his body rush straight to his crown—

This demonic qi was something Qu Qianxing had intentionally tainted himself with to masquerade as the "Demon Sovereign." He had assumed he could control it, yet never anticipated that at this moment, it would suddenly swell—as if "another self" resided inside his body, reacting to Guan Ye's accusations. A wave of inexplicable shame and indignation seized his thoughts, as though he truly had done countless unforgivable things and had no face left to look upon Master.

And to make matters worse, gloating from a certain obnoxious bastard faintly echoed from within the demonic qi:

—"*Hahahahaha!* Fourth is completely right! Second, you truly failed your duty to protect this bunch of brats, and you're pretentious as hell to boot! As expected of someone descended from the Heavenly Court—there's no fundamental difference between you and that wretched thing that created you! Ah, speaking of which, back then you hated those mortals so much, yet when you discarded your sect, what difference was there between you and them?! *Hahaha!* Just what will Master think of you?!"

[What is this bastard babbling about?!]

Qu Qianxing clutched his forehead in agony, attempting to suppress the demonic qi with golden lightning.

Yet in that fleeting lapse of concentration, when Qu Qianxing raised his eyes once more, he saw Guan Ye had already closed the distance until they could feel each other's breath, staring at him with pitch-black, hollow eyes.

Subconsciously, Qu Qianxing hurled golden lightning out.

Guan Ye sneered, the resentment around him condensing into a whip, lashing out at Qu Qianxing without the slightest courtesy.

In an instant, dark clouds forged of resentment churned across the sky, laced with lightning and thunder that threatened to obliterate everything.

Even though the celestial soldiers and demon hordes had already completely exited the stage, the entire minor world seemed plunged into another crisis of impending destruction.

\*

[—"Second Senior Brother, Fourth Senior Brother, please stop fighting!"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Lu Shulong voiced a futile plea from the corner.

It couldn't fathom it. It had clearly been the one who rushed over in a fit of rage to settle accounts with Second Senior Brother—so why had it turned into Fourth Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother brawling, and fighting so terrifyingly that it couldn't even intervene?

Just as Lu Shulong was hesitating over whether to risk its life to step in, a clear, cool voice rang out:

[—"Fifth Senior Brother."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Lu Shulong turned its head and discovered it was Youngest Junior Sister Mo Bei. Behind her followed temporary companion Qingyang Qingyu and a dazed, foolish Jian Nuo—the latter of whom could be completely ignored.

Even so, facing his junior sister, Lu Shulong felt a twinge of guilt.

She had clearly been trapped here as well, yet he and Fourth Senior Brother had been so consumed with confronting Second Senior Brother that they hadn't checked on her safety in time.

—"Junior Sister, are you all right?"-it asked apologetically.-"You weren't caught in the crossfire just now, were you?"

—"I'm fine. Senior Sister Qingyu, Young Master Jian, and I took shelter in White Jade Capital,"-Mo Bei replied, glancing at the floating city not far away, which had been battered into a mess by lightning yet stubbornly remained aloft.-"There is a core formation over there; it was unexpectedly safe."

—"What core formation?"-Lu Shulong seemed to catch a critical term.

Mo Bei did not explain, looking instead toward the sky-blotting dark clouds:

—"However, we were trapped as a result. By the time we figured it out and emerged, Fourth Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother were already like this."

—"*Haha*, it's a misunderstanding,"-Lu Shulong gave a dry laugh, attempting to preserve a senior brother's dignity before his junior sister.-"I'll go stop them right now..."

Despite saying so, Lu Shulong still lacked the courage to charge in and break up the fight.

Fortunately, Mo Bei spoke up immediately:

—"Let Fourth Senior Brother beat him!"

Lu Shulong looked at her in surprise.

Mo Bei said:

—"We brought it upon ourselves. Only Fourth Senior Brother had it the worst—deliberately isolated from the truth by Second Senior Brother. It's only natural he harbors grievances."

Although Mo Bei hadn't cared much about the other martial siblings in that lifetime, she had still known more or less what everyone was doing. In particular, Guan Ye had pursued the "protagonist" with such ruthless ferocity to avenge the sect, only for it all to seemingly fall within Second Senior Brother's calculations. Thus, Mo Bei could understand Guan Ye's resentment.

What was more, Mo Bei's own ordeal in that lifetime seemed to have received Qu Qianxing's tacit consent, and Mo Xi had been run ragged under his orders.

Lu Shulong had died early, knowing even less than Mo Bei.

Still, he had died only after making a "transaction" with Qu Qianxing to begin with.

Mo Bei looked at Lu Shulong:

—"Fifth Senior Brother, you have no objections, right?"

—"...Of course not."-Lu Shulong blinked, then curled its lips into a cold smile.

On the matter of beating Qu Qianxing, the junior siblings reached complete consensus.

As for Eldest Senior Brother...

If the chance arose, they wanted to [throw a burlap sack over his head](# "Literal CN: 套麻袋 (tào mádài) — slang for ambushing someone from behind with a sack over their head to beat them anonymously") and beat him as well. Regrettably, that shameless beast was utterly brazen; he didn't even care about being strung up on a tree by Master. It felt like simply beating him wouldn't settle anything.

Mo Bei sighed inwardly.

Yet from her own standpoint, she felt she had little right to judge Eldest and Second Senior Brothers. If accounts were to be settled for that lifetime, her own culpability was no lighter than theirs. And if those two senior brothers were malicious, she had been stupid—stupid beyond belief. As Master's disciple, Mo Bei felt her own foolishness was even more unforgivable than pure malice; it was only that her senior brothers wouldn't hold it against their youngest junior sister.

Now that her senior brothers were conducting an internal audit, she would have to act in their stead to properly bring this matter to a complete close.

Besides, she had a real account to settle with "those above"!

Mo Bei's cool eyes gazed toward the sky, darkening instantly like the abyssal pool in that lifetime where she had lost her purity and sanity—piercingly cold to the bone yet impossible to freeze over, wanting nothing more than to drag those above down into the abyss to become frozen corpses below.

Qu Qianxing did not remember the hatred, but Mo Bei remembered.

—"Senior Sister, Fifth Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei stroked the hilt of the flexible sword at her waist, staring at Lu Shulong as she asked,-"since Second Senior Brother is tied up, could you lend that spiritual energy to me?"

—"Eh? Ah?"-Lu Shulong failed to react.

—"Did Second Senior Brother fail to clean things up thoroughly?"-Ran Deng's voice rang out.

Ran Deng had re-established contact with this world after Qu Qianxing accepted and used the spiritual energy she had channeled through the array. However, she was busy maintaining the formation and handling matters on her end—Qu Qianxing defeating the invaders could be [broadcast live](# "Colloquial/Gaming: 直播 (zhíbō) — streaming live across the tournament water mirrors"), but martial siblings squabbling afterward absolutely could not! Thus, Ran Deng had temporarily been unable to attend to this side.

Even now, she and Mo Xi were still pondering how to give the Immortal Arts Assembly a flawless conclusion.

Yet compared to the battlefield of the Heavenly Court, the Immortal Arts Assembly was no longer a major affair; bringing the immortal-demon invasion to a decisive finish was obviously far more critical. Regrettably, her martial brothers, led by Eldest Senior Brother, [dropped the ball](# "Idiom: 掉链子 (diào liànzi) — drop the bicycle chain; colloquial for failing or flaking at a critical moment") more enthusiastically than anyone the moment a critical juncture arrived! At such a tense moment, with the situation in the Heavenly Court still uncertain, the seemingly most reliable Second Senior Brother and Fourth Junior Brother were actually brawling on the spot, while Fifth Junior Brother was foolishly watching from the sidelines!

Ran Deng was truly furious enough to cough blood at these useless martial brothers.

Fortunately, Youngest Junior Sister was still here.

—"I'm not entirely sure how thoroughly Second Senior Brother handled it, but I can sense that something wasn't wiped clean..."-Mo Bei said, gazing coldly at the sky.

To be precise, she sensed an existence tied to her bloodline.

Yet Mo Bei's blood kin began and ended with Mo Xi alone. If there existed another bloodline-related entity that was connected to outside invaders, even without recovering her memories, she would know it was no good thing.

At this moment, Mo Bei shared some of Qu Qianxing's earlier sentiment, instinctively feeling that it had to be purged thoroughly. Furthermore:

—"I don't know what the situation is 'above'; it would be best to confirm things. I was more or less crafted from immortal remains, so slipping up there under the radar to take a look shouldn't cause any problems."

—"Fine,"-Ran Deng said without hesitation upon hearing this.

The power that had previously surged toward Qu Qianxing shifted course, flooding into Mo Bei's body.

She merely cautioned with slight worry:

—"Just go up and take a look. If you sense danger, return immediately..."

—"I know,"-Mo Bei wasn't one to overestimate herself.-"I'll just take a look first. If I can't handle it, I'll return and report... Senior Sister, don't give me all the spiritual energy either. I only know swordsmanship and can't use that much. On the contrary, we might need a great deal of spiritual energy later on."

—"Later on?"-Ran Deng was taken aback.

Mo Bei said no more, turning her gaze toward Qingyang Qingyu.

—"We found some things in White Jade Capital,"-Qingyang Qingyu said.

While Qu Qianxing was battling the celestial soldiers and heavenly generals, Mo Bei and Qingyang Qingyu inside White Jade Capital naturally hadn't remained idle. Although their original objective had been to retrieve Sky City's primordial core, the battle outside was so cataclysmic that running out would have turned them into living targets. They had no choice but to relocate the core from its original position and seek out a safer spot within White Jade Capital.

Fortunately, precisely because the battle outside was raging so fiercely, no one paid any heed to the interior of White Jade Capital, allowing Mo Bei and the others to move without major hindrance.

Qingyang Qingyu, on the other hand, had always been fascinated by Sky City's core and its associated cultivation manuals. Given this opportunity, she wished she could [root herself in the manuals](# "Literal CN: 长在功法上 — grow directly onto the cultivation techniques"), content to stay buried in them for centuries without ever emerging.

"Unfortunately," while browsing the manuals, she had discovered some superfluous matters within.

—"It is a formation regarding the world's grand restrictions."

Indeed, this world had once been the "Langhuan Forbidden Grounds"—an existence that even in the primordial Heavenly Court wasn't accessible to just anyone. Consequently, this world possessed a complete set of grand restrictions. It was also because of this that when the Heavenly Court prepared to abandon this place entirely, the realm possessed the leverage to resist the Heavenly Court's plundering, forcing even Tian Yan to resort to underhanded schemes from the shadows.

Explaining the details would be overly complicated, so Qingyang Qingyu kept it as brief as possible:

—"This set of restrictions was originally governed by White Jade Capital. Ten thousand years ago during the demon invasion, it was transferred to Sky City. If applied properly, it can completely bar any outside enemy from entering this world."

Conversely, losing Sky City would likely cause the world's barrier to collapse entirely.

Though Qingyang Qingyu didn't think things would get so dire, Ran Deng grasped the gravity of the situation instantly.

Jian Nuo spoke up at this point:

—"I hope to relay this news to my ancestor as soon as possible."

For the first time, this Young City Lord of Sky City recognized his own profound powerlessness. Never mind celestial soldiers, heavenly generals, and demon armies—even with Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye brawling right in front of him, he couldn't even step between them.

Yet through the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Jian Nuo had also learned that when you couldn't do something yourself, asking your family's elders for help was not shameful in the slightest!

Particularly when an unwelcome "past" foreshadowed a terrifying "future."

—"I think under these circumstances, things will go much smoother if Sky City steps forward,"-Jian Nuo said.

Indeed. Before Master returned, having the City Lord of Sky City present would save a great deal of trouble.

Ran Deng's tone turned solemn:

—"Understood. I will immediately dedicate all power to establishing a pathway for your return."

—"No need for such trouble,"-Lu Shulong finally found a place where it could be useful.-"I'll just carry them back."

—"You follow Youngest Junior Sister!"-Ran Deng snapped coldly.-"If Youngest Junior Sister doesn't come back, don't bother coming back either!"

—"Eh?!"-Lu Shulong squawked in dismay.

Mo Bei didn't mind the decision, merely nodding:

—"Let's go, Fifth Senior Brother."

As she spoke, she flew straight up into the clouds.

Her speed was blazingly swift, like an unsheathed sword.

Only then did Lu Shulong follow after.

On the other side, Mo Xi silently watched his twin sister fly out of that world. He said nothing about that, asking only:

—"Senior Sister, what about Second Senior Brother and Fourth Senior Brother...?"

[—"Let them die!"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")
