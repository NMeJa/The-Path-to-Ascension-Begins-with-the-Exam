# Chapter 196: Before the Exam

[—"Isn't that Fairy Maogui of Moon-Welcoming Valley? Why is she sitting up there?"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

Not only disciples hailing from Moon-Welcoming Valley, but cultivators from various other sects recognized Maogui as well.

It was not as though everyone recognized Fairy Maogui.

In truth, while Mahayana powerhouses were not quite as vanishingly rare as Tribulation Transcendents, their numbers were hardly vast. In any sect, they stood as supreme pillars equivalent to guardian treasures of the lineage. Even newly admitted disciples of their own sects had rarely laid eyes upon their founding ancestors, let alone disciples from other sects. And for someone like Maogui, who cloistered herself in deep seclusion to cultivate in solitude, those capable of recognizing her were even fewer.

At this moment, the reason Maogui was recognized the second she showed her face was that she simply had far too many 'acquaintances' present.

Seated at the commentary desk, Fairy Maogui was equally startled by the 'spectator stands.'

Good heavens! Upon those 'spectator stands' were unexpectedly four Tribulation Transcendents, more than a dozen Mahayana titans, over forty Body Integration experts, and hundreds of Void Refinement masters. Even if the Heavenly Dao Alliance were to issue an edict summoning every cultivator under heaven to convene a realm-wide summit, nowhere near this many high-ranking powerhouses would have shown up!

[Could there be something extraordinarily special about this examination?]

[No, it seems the other exams are drawing plenty of onlookers as well. Could this be some brand-new form of entertainment?]

Fairy Maogui thought in utter bewilderment.

Caring solely about the Ascension Stairway, Fairy Maogui had no idea that a considerable number of these high-ranking cultivators had genuinely come for the qualification examination.

And this, ironically enough, was Guan Xinguang's "achievement."

Originally, when the Heavenly Spirit Sect announced news of the qualification examination, time had been tight and promotion minimal. Furthermore, Ran Deng's connections lay predominantly within the formation masters' circle, giving her no direct inroads among artifact refiners. Consequently, even with the large-scale teleportation array dangled as bait, it had only gained traction among low-to-mid-tier cultivators. High-ranking cultivators—especially the world-renowned master artifact refiners—either remained completely oblivious or, even if they knew, maintained a cautious wait-and-see stance.

After all, these 'master refiners' possessed pride, and because artifact refiners were exceedingly scarce, their pride was particularly lofty.

In their view, if they were willing to contribute their efforts toward the large-scale teleportation array, even the Untiring Immortal Venerable would have to [rush out with inside-out slippers to welcome them](# "Literal CN: 倒履相迎 (dào lǚ xiāng yíng) — rushing out with shoes turned backward / inside-out in haste to warmly receive an honored guest, from an ancient historical anecdote of Cai Yong greeting Wang Can"); how could they possibly sit for a qualification exam and let others certify their competence? It was simply too [beneath their dignity](# "Literal CN: 掉价 (diào jià) — dropping price/value; losing face, beneath one's station or prestige")!

Until they caught wind of the news that Guan Xinguang was preparing to lead the entire Department of Works—and even register to sit for the qualification exam in person.

Whatever one might say about Guan Xinguang's moral caliber, as the Chief Director of the Department of Works, his renown as an artifact refiner was undeniably illustrious.

Particularly in a cultivation realm lacking any unified formal government, the Heavenly Dao Alliance served as a quasi-official authority. Thus, Guan Xinguang was effectively a semi-officially certified master artifact refiner, and his position as Chief Director of the Department of Works was roughly equivalent to the president of the cultivation realm's academy of sciences.

For such an eminent master to personally choose to sit for an obscure 'Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination' naturally sent shockwaves across the realm.

[—"If even this grandmaster has to get certified, doesn't that mean those of us without certificates don't even count as artifact refiners?"](# "Speaker: Master Refiners")

All at once, numerous master refiners and artifact refining sects sprang into motion upon catching wind of the news, eager to pry details out of Guan Xinguang. But what could Guan Xinguang possibly say? He certainly couldn't tell them: *I extracted the Dao Bone from Untiring's fourth disciple years ago, and now I'm being coerced by two deities to extract it all over again, could he?* Guan Xinguang had no choice but to shut his doors and decline all visitors. Even when cornered by a few acquaintances he couldn't refuse, he hemmed and hawed, adamantly refusing to disclose the real reason, only telling them there was nothing whatsoever worth paying attention to regarding this qualification exam. If possible, they shouldn't even set foot in the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

Guan Xinguang was speaking from the bottom of his heart, yet the more mysterious his demeanor seemed, the less anyone believed him.

The prevailing ethos of the cultivation realm had always been like this:

Every last person spoke in [cryptic riddles](# "Literal CN: 谜语人 (mǐyǔrén) — 'riddler'; modern pop-culture slang for people who refuse to speak plainly, hoarding critical information behind vague hints"), and every last person dreaded someone else snatching their fortuitous encounters.

This climate had improved slightly of late thanks to the emergence of academic journals, but on the whole, not by much. Most cultivators still loved hoarding knowledge, clutching the petty secrets they possessed tightly to their chests, then glaring at others with bloodshot eyes, forever suspecting everyone else was concealing some secret opportunity they themselves didn't know about.

Thus, Guan Xinguang's evasive attitude played right into this exact paranoia.

These people loved overanalyzing everything. In an instant, they stitched together all the recent realm-shaking events: the Untiring Immortal Venerable had repaired the Ascension Stairway; his fourth disciple who designed the teleportation array was sitting for the exam; the Heavenly Spirit Sect's educational dissemination initiatives; the doctrinal disputes in academic journals; the internal upheaval in the Palace of Carefree Wandering, and so on and so forth. Clearly, this qualification exam was no ordinary test! There had to be some profound, deeply hidden secret behind it—it might even conceal a tremendous fortuitous opportunity! *That was why Guan Xinguang refused to say a word!*

Most importantly, Guan Xinguang's social network was vast!

Having risen to Chief Director of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works, Guan Xinguang clearly belonged to the breed of bureaucrat whose interpersonal politicking far outstripped his technical prowess. As a result, he was intimately familiar with most cultivators, which also meant that whenever he made a move, over half the cultivation realm's high-ranking figures would instantly learn about it.

This was practically the ultimate endorsement for the qualification exam!

As a consequence, once the news broke of Guan Xinguang's decision to sit for the exam, registrations skyrocketed by fifty percent in an instant, and several master refiners who had no intention of traveling to the Central Domain packed their bags and followed along.

Naturally, these master refiners weren't brainless followers. They weren't about to impulsively sign up for the exam themselves on a whim. But they could easily dispatch their disciples and grand-disciples to compete! And observing was always an option—what was more, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's examinations were actually open to spectators!

All at once, the number of people clamoring to watch the exam reached unprecedented heights, to the point where the Heavenly Spirit Sect was forced to erect tiered amphitheater seating around the examination grounds for the 'spectators.'

Even then, seats were still nowhere near enough.

In particular, the Level 8 Master Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination—which both Guan Xinguang and Guan Ye would be taking—was swarming with seas of people. The number of spectators exceeded the examinees several times over, rivaling even the grand spectacles of the Immortal Arts Assembly! It reached the point where securing a seat in the stands required raw cultivation strength—anyone below the Soul Formation stage was not even qualified to enter the spectator gallery!

And that was precisely why the spectator stands presented such a star-studded, powerhouse-packed vista.

These grand powerhouses rarely gathered in such numbers. Their cultivation levels were comparable, meaning they had no need to put on aloof airs before juniors. Even those with old grudges dared not act rashly with the stifling spiritual pressure of four Tribulation Transcendents looming over their heads. Consequently, the atmosphere became surprisingly harmonious and relaxed.

They leisurely indulged in gossip:

—"How truly rare,"-one elder remarked.-"To see Fairy Maogui make a public appearance outside Moon-Welcoming Valley. She wouldn't even attend the grand auctions in Sky City in person."

—"First Chief Director Guan registered for the exam, and now we have Fairy Maogui providing live commentary,"-another chimed in.-"It seems this examination is far from ordinary!"

—"Not that I intend any disrespect toward Fairy Maogui, but isn't she an alchemist?"-a cultivator asked.-"Why is she commentating on an artifact refinement exam?"

—"What's wrong with that? She's a Mahayana Peak titan, after all,"-someone countered.

—"Still, [different trades are like separated mountains](# "Literal CN: 隔行如隔山 (gé háng rú gé shān) — different professions are divided as if by mountains; expertise in one field does not translate to another")!"-the first cultivator pointed out.-"Can she really provide proper commentary?"

—"On the contrary, I believe this reflects the Untiring Immortal Venerable's profound foresight!"-another senior mused.

[—"How so?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator")

—"After all, this is a 'Master Artifact Refiner' exam,"-the senior explained.-"When even the Chief Director of the Department of Works is sitting on the exam floor, who could possibly judge him? None of us old fogies would accept each other's verdicts anyway, so having an outsider alchemist serve as the commentator is actually far safer."

[—"That makes sense."](# "Speaker: Cultivator")

—"Far more than just sense!"-another chimed in.-"Haven't you noticed? This examination doesn't have a single examiner! While the Untiring Immortal Venerable might have recused himself because his own disciple is competing, he also stated: 'The craft of master artifact refiners cannot be objectively graded by a proctor,' and 'An artifact refiner must let their creations speak for themselves!'"

—"What a line—'let their creations speak for themselves'!"-an elder exclaimed.-"I wonder what these 'master refiners' will forge today!"

While the titans up in the stands chatted leisurely, the examinees down on the exam floor were feeling anything but relaxed.

Not only the disciples of Moon-Welcoming Valley, but examinees from other sects looked up at the spectator gallery [as though their parents had died](# "Literal CN: 如丧考妣 (rú sàng kǎo bǐ) — mourning as if both father and mother had died; wearing a face of utter doom and bereavement"):

[—"Why on earth did Master, Martial Uncle, and Martial Grand-Uncle all come to watch?!"](# "Speaker: Examinee")

[—"Our sect specializes in talismans, doesn't it?! Why is my founding ancestor up there joining the circus?!"](# "Speaker: Examinee")

[—"We're finished... If I mess up this time, I'll be humiliating myself in front of the entire cultivation realm!"](# "Speaker: Examinee")

[—"Why on earth did I ever think taking an exam sounded 'fun'?!"](# "Speaker: Examinee")

...

Amid the chaotic swirl of frantic thoughts, the most shocked person of all was the very culprit who had engineered this state of affairs in the first place.

[How in the world did the entire cultivation realm end up here?!]-Guan Xinguang thought in pure horror.

More than half of the people here had shown up because of him—all old acquaintances of his. To him, was this not virtually the 'entire cultivation realm'?

Having kept his doors firmly shut these past few days, Guan Xinguang truly had no inkling of the frenzy he had ignited. Yet staring up at the overwhelming spectacle in the stands, his already wavering resolve trembled once more:

—"Under the watchful eyes of the entire world... are we really still going through with this?"-Guan Xinguang asked, his voice trembling.

—"[We're already here](# "Literal CN: 来都来了 (lái dōu lái le) — 'Since we've already come all this way / we're already here'; the quintessential Chinese cultural excuse and inescapable sunken-cost phrase used to rationalize seeing an ordeal through no matter how awful"),"-Lin Qi snapped, thoroughly annoyed with him.

Lin Qi felt he had never encountered an asset quite as troublesome as Guan Xinguang.

Back in the day, even when marching against the Demon Emperor, he had only needed to cast his 'subconscious suggestion' three times upon celestial soldiers suffering from low morale, and that had been more than enough to fill the most cowardly soldier with courage to charge into battle against the Demon Emperor (and straight to their deaths). Yet with this so-called Chief Director of the Department of Works from the lower realm, he had layered countless suggestions on the man throughout their journey, only for him to still contemplate running away at the critical hour. He truly couldn't tell whether this Guan Xinguang was simply too pathetically cowardly and incompetent, or if the prestige of that 'Untiring Immortal Venerable' was just that overwhelming.

However, Lin Qi had no intention of backing down.

If, back when Ren Ran invited him, he had merely wanted to find a random destination for a paid vacation—anywhere would do—now that he had actually arrived in this world, Lin Qi discovered that this minor world truly lived up to its heritage as a severed fragment of the Upper Realm. It completely lacked the stifling, impoverished spiritual energy typical of other minor worlds that cramped one's style; instead, resources of every kind were abundantly stocked.

Particularly after stepping into the Heavenly Spirit Sect's domain, the spiritual energy here was not merely rich and pure—it had even condensed into celestial dew. Lower-realm mortals were ultimately just lower-realm mortals; never having experienced an immortal physique, they had no idea what an exquisite treasure this was.

Then again, this state of affairs seemed rather inconsistent with the dossier records!

Lin Qi felt a flicker of doubt.

Before descending, he had carefully perused the official records documenting the status of this world. He knew that tens of thousands of years ago (by this world's chronology), it had severed from the Immortal Realm and plummeted down. It had subsequently endured several catastrophic God-Demon Wars, to the point where the native inhabitants' trust in the Heavenly Court had completely dissolved. They had proactively destroyed the Ascension Stairway, shutting both immortals and demons outside their world.

Naturally, the opinions of the natives had never factored into the Heavenly Court's considerations.

What had genuinely caused the Heavenly Court to abandon this world was that throughout the prolonged warfare, catastrophic anomalies had developed in its time and space.

The time in this realm was not only utterly chaotic, surging with backwards and forwards temporal currents, but its space was also riddled with fissures leading into the void, causing spiritual energy to bleed outward without the ability to self-circulate.

Under such conditions, destruction became an inevitable certainty, and the Heavenly Court had naturally washed its hands of this realm.

Yet when Lin Qi descended this time, he found the spiritual energy quite abundant! The natural circulation hadn't collapsed at all. If he reported to the Heavenly Court that this world could even condense celestial dew, the Heavenly Court might very well reverse its decision entirely.

Then again, why would he ever do that?

Even if this world were reclaimed by the Heavenly Court, immortal officials like him wouldn't reap any personal benefits; on the contrary, he would lose a prime off-the-books travel destination for mortal tribulations and paid vacations. Furthermore, since the Heavenly Court intended to abandon this place, it meant any resources unrecorded in the celestial archives could be squandered and plundered at will before the world's eventual destruction, without needing to care in the slightest about the life or death of the local inhabitants.

Viewed from this angle, Lin Qi suddenly saw nothing wrong with the fact that this realm's spiritual energy contradicted the official dossier.

*Evidently, other colleagues intentionally left this behind as an off-the-books 'perk.'*

Now that this 'perk' had been discovered by him, Lin Qi, he naturally had to claim his fair share!

With these calculations in mind, Lin Qi found a touch more patience for a coward like Guan Xinguang:

—"What does a big crowd matter? As a master artifact refiner, are you afraid of people watching you?"-Lin Qi coaxed him.-"Besides, I never asked you to make a move under everyone's gaze! All you need to do is place a mark on him, and leave the rest to our handiwork."

Hearing this, Guan Xinguang finally took a step toward his assigned examination platform.

Lin Qi let out a breath of relief, growing all the more invested in 'his Dao Bone':

—"Speaking of which, where is the vessel holding the Dao Bone?"-Lin Qi asked.

—"At [Platform Forty-Four](# "Literal CN: 四十四号 (sìshísì hào) — 'Number 44'; in Chinese culture, the number 4 (四, sì) is an extreme homophone for death (死, sǐ), making 44 ('double death') an exceptionally ominous number"),"-Guan Xinguang replied without glancing aside.

Knowing the Untiring Immortal Venerable was seated at the highest apex of the examination arena, Guan Xinguang dared not cast his gaze about recklessly.

In truth, had he not spent these past few days hiding aboard the flying treasure ship without Untiring ever sparing him a single glance—reassuring Guan Xinguang that in Untiring's mind, this examination was far more important than executing him—he wouldn't have dared set foot in this arena at all.

Fortunately, fear aside, Guan Xinguang was still a Mahayana cultivator. Even without looking, he could easily pinpoint Guan Ye's location through his divine sense.

Yet... was that truly Guan Ye?

Guan Xinguang felt another surge of uncertainty.

It wasn't that Guan Ye's features had changed—in fact, Guan Xinguang had never met Guan Ye face-to-face. His only knowledge of Guan Ye's appearance came from a jade slip recording clandestinely retrieved after Zhou Hui had accepted Guan Ye as a disciple. Rather, it was Guan Ye's spirit and presence that differed completely from what Guan Xinguang had imagined.

In Guan Xinguang's mind, someone whose spine had been ripped out and whose spirit roots had been crippled ought to have suffered devastating, irreversible destruction to their psyche and spirit. Even if taken in by the Untiring Immortal Venerable, they should never have been able to recover.

Yet Guan Ye stood there. Though his exterior remained as silent and somber as in the recording, one could not detect the faintest trace of inferiority or resentment in his expression. Even with that dark-crimson spine trailing behind him like a monster's tail, standing in full view of the public under the scrutiny of the cultivation realm's most preeminent titans, he radiated an unshakable, steadfast aura.

[This man... no wonder he condensed a Dao Bone.]

[Only, will ripping the spine out of him a second time truly be that simple?]

Guan Xinguang thought to himself.

At that moment, as if sensing Guan Xinguang's divine sense, Guan Ye suddenly shifted his dark irises, casting a glance in Guan Xinguang's direction—causing Guan Xinguang's entire body to freeze stiff.

Lin Qi paid no heed to Guan Xinguang's tangled psychological state.

—"A master possessing a godhead, a disciple possessing a Dao Bone, and even the air condensing celestial dew... isn't this land a bit too [blessed by heaven and earth](# "Literal CN: 人杰地灵 (rén jié dì líng) — outstanding people and blessed land; a place endowed with fine spirits and fertile soil that breeds remarkable talents")?"-Lin Qi remarked with amusement.-"Pity their luck isn't so great. Number Forty-Four is hardly a good omen!"

In the cultivation realm, omens portended by numbers were naturally very real.

Yet Guan Ye receiving such an inauspicious number was no accident either; it was the result of fate and karmic luck having been deliberately manipulated.

—"Alright, what are you standing there spacing out for? It's about time you took your place,"-Lin Qi urged. Seeing Guan Xinguang hesitate again, he grew even more impatient:-"Still standing here—are you trying to make Untiring notice you?"

That wasn't quite the case.

Although Guan Xinguang and Lin Qi had 'chatted' for quite some time, Lin Qi was currently in a state of possessing Guan Xinguang's body. Thus, their conversation had taken place directly within the mind, spanning mere fractions of a second.

To outside onlookers, Guan Xinguang had merely paused slightly upon stepping into the examination arena to take in his surroundings—the inevitable, composed reaction of an 'unfathomable Mahayana cultivator.'

Pity that the moment this 'grand powerhouse' heard Untiring's name, a flicker of panic broke across his face, and he scrambled up to his examination platform in hurried strides.

Following Guan Xinguang's arrival, the examination platform emitted a series of crisp *ding-ding* chimes.

Amid these wind-chime-like tones, the decorative seaweed surrounding the platform began to wildly proliferate and morph, forming thick, solid walls. In the blink of an eye, the entire platform transformed into an enclosed forging chamber.

[—"! It's... it's an illusion!"](# "Speaker: Guan Xinguang")

Startled at first, Guan Xinguang quickly stammered—whether to soothe his own nerves or inform Lin Qi:

—"The walls of this forging chamber are illusory,"-Guan Xinguang said.-"People on the outside should still be able to see me. This design of theirs must be intended to let examinees focus on their work, right?"

—"You're calmer than expected. I thought you were about to scream out loud just now,"-Lin Qi sneered, though a touch of concern crept in.-"Still, with line of sight blocked like this, closing in on the Dao Bone will be tricky."

At this point, Guan Xinguang unexpectedly showed some initiative.

Or rather, he had run out of avenues of retreat. Now that he stood under the watchful gaze of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, he could only rely on Lin Qi, which meant he had to fulfill this Celestial General's demands no matter what.

—"No, it won't be,"-Guan Xinguang said.-"I've already inquired about this. These examination platforms rise and fall automatically based on exam performance. As long as I reach the highest apex of all the platforms, pinpointing the positions of the others will be effortless."

—"The highest position? Then you'll have to take first place!"-Lin Qi noted.

—"Heh, I dare not make grand boasts about other matters,"-Guan Xinguang replied with smug confidence.-"But as the Chief Director of the Department of Works, my artifact refining craft in this world ranks second to none!"

Walking before the forging cauldron in the center of the chamber, he flicked his sleeve with practiced ease. With a whoosh, purple-gold flames flared fiercely from the heart of the furnace.
