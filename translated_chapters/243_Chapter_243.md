# Chapter 243: Cause and Effect

While Ran Deng was inscribing the transmission array, Qu Qianxing was gritting his teeth and desperately holding out.

Clearly, no matter how much of a genius he was, he could not truly transcend the hard constraints of his cultivation realm to forcefully and completely obliterate an enemy whose power exceeded his own by multiple orders of magnitude.

Yet Qu Qianxing had not fallen into this predicament because he failed to understand a principle as simple as [acting within one's limits](# "Idiom: 量力而行 (liànglì-érxíng) — act according to one's capabilities / know one's bounds").

No matter how domineering and autocratic a tyrant Qu Qianxing was by nature, he had never deluded himself from the outset into believing he could sweep through the Heavenly Court and hunt True Immortals through sheer individual might. Rather, according to his original blueprint, he had merely planned to pit the demon legions against the celestial soldiers, orchestrating a mutually destructive bloodbath. If luck was on his side, he would teach the celestial generals a sharp little lesson, thereby forcing the bigwigs of the Heavenly Court to direct their attention toward the "proper business" of exterminating the demon race, rather than perpetually fixing their crooked designs upon the resources of innocent minor worlds.

Yet when the Immortal-Demon battlefield was abruptly shifted to the exact location of his own family's "trial system," Qu Qianxing had vaguely realized that none of this was a coincidence, but an orchestrated conspiracy.

—He certainly did not believe in any coincidence that happened to drop an apocalyptic Immortal-Demon battlefield right at his own front door.

And after Ran Deng probed the spatial rift data and caught the mastermind's slipping tail, this suspicion had been proven. Or worse: when Qu Qianxing bombarded the spatial fissures with thunderbolts in an attempt to sever the opposing party's grip, he had caught the faintest wisp of an aura—ethereal yet intimately familiar.

No! It wasn't merely intimately familiar—it was virtually identical to his own!

For a fleeting instant, Qu Qianxing had almost believed that he was the one standing on the far side of that spatial fissure!

Yet "virtually identical" was not entirely identical. Qu Qianxing soon noticed that the aura was far richer and infinitely purer than his own—unmistakably the aura of a True Immortal.

Furthermore, this True Immortal clearly shared an extraordinary connection with Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing could feel an unnatural palpitation stirring within himself. His entire divine soul and power experienced a violent, irresistible pull—like a tiny child catching sight of its parents, like a newborn creation looking upon its creator, like a stray avatar gazing upon its original true form, instinctively yearning to return to that presence.

He had no idea how an ordinary person would react upon learning they possessed an inseparable connection to a True Immortal or the Celestial Realm itself. But for Qu Qianxing personally, the moment he realized this, he was sickened to the point of wanting to extract his own soul and scour his own marrow.

[This body and this soul are far too filthy. They cannot be kept!]

Because of that striking face of his, Qu Qianxing had been praised since childhood as "resembling a celestial being" and "as radiant as a heavenly immortal." Unfortunately, that face had never brought him good fortune; rather, it had forced him onto the path of cultivation against his will, birthing the conviction that "the Heavenly Dao is unjust." Even his past willingness to support Master's ascension at any cost had been rooted in the belief that the Heavenly Court was utterly corrupt and immoral, desperately needing someone of Master's caliber to reform it from within.

How could a man like Qu Qianxing ever tolerate having some murky, sordid connection with a True Immortal?!

Especially if one considered the worst-case scenario: every hardship and torment Qu Qianxing had suffered had been a predetermined destiny—a premeditated conspiracy. Even the sudden relocation of this Immortal-Demon battlefield to his own front door was very likely the result of that mastermind tracking him down as their target!

Qu Qianxing had never hesitated to anticipate the worst possible outcomes for the future. Thus, in virtually an instant, his resolve hardened:

He had to kill that True Immortal who shared this murky connection with him!

Even if it cost his own life in mutual destruction, he would not hesitate.

Given Qu Qianxing's stubborn, unyielding temperament, Zhou Hui had been entirely correct in wishing he would never regain his past memories. Yet precisely because Qu Qianxing had zero recollection of the lengths Master would go to avenge his death or rewind time on his behalf, he dared to act with such ruthless finality.

And worst of all—when it came to mutual destruction, Qu Qianxing was actually capable of pulling it off!

After all, a "connection" was a two-way street.

Even in something like a "master-servant contract"—an ironclad bond where one party ostensibly held absolute dominion over the other's life—so long as one seized hold of the crucial "thread," even an enslaved puppet stripped of self-awareness possessed a sliver of a chance to backlash and devour its master.

And even more unfortunately for his enemy, Qu Qianxing had conducted exhaustive research into this exact field.

Due to his past mortal experiences, Qu Qianxing harbored lingering trauma regarding controlling contracts and subordinate relationships. And Zhou Hui was a master who unconditionally doted upon his disciples; so long as a student showed genuine interest in a subject, there was nothing he would refuse to teach. Besides, given Qu Qianxing's stunning visage, harboring anxieties of this nature was entirely understandable. Thus, beyond contractual arts, Zhou Hui's instruction to Qu Qianxing had delved into deeper dimensions of causality—techniques capable of severing connections across temporal dimensions or even annihilating the opposing party outright.

Even Zhou Hui, however, had likely never anticipated that his Second Disciple would be this ferocious: the very first target he tested this on was a True Immortal, and rather than merely severing the connection, he intended to snipe and eliminate the entity through mutual destruction!

Yes—a calculated assassination.

On the surface, Qu Qianxing appeared to be using lightning to sever the enemy's "tentacles" in order to eliminate outside interference within this spatial domain. In reality, his electrical arcs served offense under the guise of defense: tracing along those golden threads, he had located and seized the mastermind, intending to blast the entity to ash.

Lightning, after all, was never an instrument of defense—it was an instrument of slaughter.

\* \* \*

Faced with the lightning severing his destiny threads, the "all-knowing" Tian Yan had not even realized Qu Qianxing's true intent at first.

He was currently consumed by a fury that rivaled Qu Qianxing's own.

For the more Tian Yan engaged with the battlefield and with Qu Qianxing, the more he discovered that everything had spiraled beyond his control:

Whether it was Qu Qianxing himself, or the trajectory of events.

Tian Yan was incandescent with rage.

He felt like a master who had cast an ugly-feathered fledgeling into a dense briar patch. Originally, he had only needed to wait for the chick to slowly starve to death, luring nearby "critters"—such as demonic beasts—to gnaw at the surrounding thorns in their scramble to devour the bird. Once the briars withered and died, a single torch would incinerate the briars and corpses together, allowing him to reclaim that fertile soil.

Yet events had refused to unfold according to his design. Looking back after an interval, that damnable chick had not only grown, but evolved, its plumage radiant and fully fledged! Had he known this would happen, he would never have cast it away, but instead refined it into his next physical vessel.

Nor had the briar patch suffered the slightest damage. On the contrary, it flourished with ever-greater vitality, its luxuriant foliage concealing its roots and blocking Tian Yan's gaze from discerning the true foundation of this world. There were even eerie, muffled cries echoing from beneath the boughs, suggesting that besides that fledgling, other little monstrosities were lurking in the shade. If he rashly reached in his hand now, it wouldn't merely be scratched by thorns—his fingers might very well be bitten off.

Of course, "bitten off"—that was merely a figure of speech.

What could possibly harm a "genuine" True Immortal? Tian Yan was supremely confident that he and those who had relied on various mortal methods to ascend were fundamentally different species.

This confidence, however, did nothing to stop his fury from burning hotter.

As the Chief of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny overseeing the Heavenly Dao across myriad realms, Tian Yan did not merely consider himself the architect of the Heavenly Dao—he fundamentally believed he was "Heavenly Destiny" itself!

Consequently, what Tian Yan detested most in the cosmos were entities that slipped beyond the dominion of the "destiny" he had decreed!

In his eyes, those who defied "Heavenly Destiny" had only one path: death!

—"I originally considered this land a territory of the Celestial Realm, still possessing value for reclamation. But since it has been ruined to such a degree, I might as well obliterate it entirely!"-Tian Yan muttered in venomous resentment.

He selectively ignored the fact of who was actually trying to ruin others, preparing to wipe out the defier of destiny along with their entire world—precisely as he had done countless times throughout antiquity.

Accompanying his killing intent, ethereal celestial melodies echoed from the layered clouds above. Divine mist radiating a luminous pearlescent white descended like silken veils, draping over the Heavenly Soldiers and Celestial Generals and coating them in a fluorescent mantle of blessing. These blessings polished their armor and weapons to a brilliant sheen, multiplying their combat prowess several-fold.

These "veils" also seemed to wipe away the soldiers' negative emotions. They not only purged the fatigue of their flesh and spirits, but also washed away their terror and panic. Even as heavenly lightning continued to pour down like torrential rain, even as their supreme commander failed to mount any respectable counterattack, and even as the demon legions bared their fangs and claws, a delusion took root in their minds: *We are invincible. We are peerless under heaven.* The physical and equipment enhancements further validated this delusion, causing their morale to soar as they lunged fearlessly toward the demon hordes.

[—"Hahaha, reinforcements have arrived!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Soldier")

[—"The Celestial Emperor must be watching over us!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Soldier")

[—"Heavenly Destiny is on our side!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Soldier")

They clamored in euphoric exaltation.

Alas, their "invincibility" was merely an illusion.

Even Zhou Hui, who possessed divine empowerment and was willing to bestow it selflessly, could not instantly elevate his closest disciples to a state of absolute invulnerability, let alone a True Immortal driven by ruthless, selfish malice.

The enhancement granted to these Heavenly Soldiers and Generals clearly had an upper ceiling. While they were somewhat stronger than before, that strength was limited: they remained unable to withstand heavenly lightning, nor could they truly break through the encirclement of the boundless demon hordes.

Yet this warped perception and the stripping away of their emotions also robbed them of their instinct to seek advantage and avoid harm. Their frenzied minds blinded them to failure and agony, and even the deaths cascading around them could no longer elicit fear.

They began clinging inexplicably to a manic delusion: [I have already ascended! I have attained immortality! As long as I achieve military merits, I will be listed among the celestial ranks! How could the Heavenly Court ever allow me—how could I possibly perish in a place like this with zero value?!]

Indeed, even Tian Yan had no desire for them to die "without value."

When these soldiers suffered injuries, the seemingly restorative white light enveloped their wounds, numbing pain and mending flesh like medicine, even seeping like celestial qi through their wounds into skin and sinew to further fortify their strength and spirit for more ferocious combat. Unfortunately, this treatment was reserved only for those whose initial wounds were shallow and brief.

When a wound was deep enough to defy healing, or when a healed soldier was wounded anew after a short interval, blinding white radiance erupted from their flesh! In a single breath, it assimilated the celestial bodies and immortal power they had painstakingly cultivated across millennia, transmuting the entire person into a blade of light that ripped through everything in its vicinity!

Including space itself.

A fresh spatial rift was torn wide open.

Thus, however many heavenly soldiers were critically wounded or slain, that exact number of new spatial rifts was torn open. Given the scale of this "illusion realm," by the time this entire legion of Heavenly Soldiers and Generals was wiped out, the entire spatial fabric would be shredded to pieces, exposing "Langhuan" where Qu Qianxing truly resided nearby.

Fortunately (?), faced with soldiers who fought with such reckless disregard for death, even the demon hordes began feeling dread.

They were a rabble to begin with. Though Qu Qianxing had temporarily whipped them into some semblance of discipline, that order was merely provisional. Seeing the situation turn grim, they immediately wanted to [scatter like birds and beasts](# "Idiom: 作鸟兽散 (zuò niǎoshòu sàn) — scatter in panic in all directions").

Forget the demons; even the commander of the celestial soldiers—their former commander—could not accept this.

—"Tian Yan! I handed you the Tiger Tally, not so you could feed every single person into the meat grinder!!"-Tian Shou screamed.

His protest achieved nothing.

—"They were created for this exact purpose to begin with,"-Tian Yan replied coldly.-"Otherwise, why would we allow them to ascend in the first place? Power is a commodity that is never sufficient to divide among many, and the Heavenly Court has no need for so many idle hands."

—"Even so, you can't 'use' them all in one go!"-Tian Shou was apoplectic with fury.-"Even cannon fodder takes time to cultivate! If you squander them all at once, how am I supposed to account for this to His Majesty later?!"

[—"No matter. The dead require no explanations."](# "Speaker: Tian Yan")

[—"Wh—?!"](# "Speaker: Tian Shou")

Tian Shou's mind failed to comprehend Tian Yan's words in time. The very next instant, his physical vessel was hoisted into the air by golden threads, dangling like a puppet.

Tian Shou was terrified and enraged. He wanted to curse, but those golden threads seemed to have sprouted directly from his divine soul, seizing control not merely of his physical body, but of his power and his very existence.

Naturally, he could no longer utter a sound. He could only stand dumbly at the densest convergence of thunderbolts, waiting for lightning to strike his flesh and harness his own power to tear open the most gargantuan spatial fissure.

Having reached this stage, Tian Shou could no longer muster even fury or terror.

[...So this day has truly arrived.]

Tian Shou thought dimly.

He had never been adept at deep contemplation, but his warrior's intuition had always caused him to instinctively loathe Tian Yan. Previously, Tian Shou had believed his distaste stemmed merely from the man's schemes to usurp his military authority. Now, gifted with unprecedented clarity, he could finally view his revulsion and dread with objectivity:

How could a control freak who permitted no living soul to escape predetermined destiny ever spare those around him?

No wonder the "veterans" of the Heavenly Court were becoming fewer and fewer...

—"That wasn't my doing,"-Tian Yan sneered.

Having seized control of Tian Shou, he could naturally perceive Tian Shou's thoughts.

Whether out of "hospice care" or simply an overflowing urge to vent, Tian Yan did not extinguish his counterpart's consciousness. Instead, he babbled incessantly to this former colleague:

—"You don't truly believe that True Immortals endure for all eternity, do you? Even immortals must either abandon their humanity completely to become gods, or wither into decrepitude and die. What 'eternity'? Deceiving the mortals of the lower realms is one thing; don't go deceiving yourself! Even the Heavenly Court is doomed to fall sooner or later!"

—"Sensationalizing alarmism? This is an immutable, established fact—a future I could see from the very moment I took office, though it was hardly convenient to share with others. I know that neither His Majesty the Celestial Emperor nor any of you would ever want to hear it. But the human race is inherently a species that thrives by having the new devour the old. No matter how the rules are rewritten, so long as it remains a Heavenly Court of humankind, that fundamental reality cannot be altered. Otherwise, why do you think I am perpetually cultivating different physical vessels?"

—"Ah, when? You're asking for the exact hour of the Heavenly Court's doom? I'd love to know that myself. Unfortunately, essential clues seem to be missing, and the temporal currents involved are extraordinarily chaotic; even I cannot divine the precise date. I can only vaguely sense that it lies in the near future—very, very near indeed... Who knows, perhaps it will arrive unexpectedly this very day?"

—"Of course, of course. I am, after all, the 'Heavenly Dao'—the Heavenly Dao of three thousand worlds! How could I possibly stand by with open eyes and watch the Heavenly Court perish? That is precisely why I am currently correcting every anomaly that has slipped beyond control, is it not?"

—"Therefore, you will willingly 'sacrifice' yourself for this noble cause, won't you?"

At the conclusion of this dialogue, Tian Shou's countenance was dyed in the hues of despair—a despair so profound that even suppressed emotions could not stifle it.

Sensing this despair, Tian Yan found his mood inexplicably lifting.

This was the familiar sensation of holding absolute dominion over all things!

Yet merely manipulating a colleague who had long rested within his grasp was not enough to fully appease Tian Yan's rage. He was resolved to obliterate everything that had escaped his control—especially Qu Qianxing!

Thus, he reined in Tian Shou's destiny threads:

—"Come! For the future of the Heavenly Court,"-he uttered high-sounding platitudes, though his hands dragged Tian Shou like a tattered rag doll, compelling the once-formidable Celestial General to plunge heedlessly toward the most chaotic nexus of the battlefield.

Tian Shou could not resist. He hung limply, manipulated at whim, the only luxury permitted him being the naked despair, rage, and pleading etched upon his face.

Yet such expressions served only to delight Tian Yan further.

In this manner, Tian Yan shoved his disagreeable colleague straight toward the coordinate where spiritual energy was most turbulent, and where his golden threads were woven thickest.

Tian Yan "knew" that next, a thunderbolt of sufficient ferocity would crash down upon that exact position.

That thunderbolt would certainly shatter the destiny threads he had painstakingly woven, but it would also leave a wound upon a Celestial General that could not be mended in time. Using this wound as a primer, all the power of the Heavenly Court's supreme warrior—a pure True Immortal carefully tailored and cultivated—would be thoroughly channeled outward, transmuting into an immortal blade capable of annihilating all things, cleaving this "illusion realm" and every world bordering it into flying dust.

Doubtless, at the moment of total devastation, that discarded defective good (Qu Qianxing) would wear a fascinating expression.

A pity that destruction always transpired too swiftly, denying that bastard a longer taste of the agony of personally obliterating everything he sought to protect.

Tian Yan curved his lips, looking toward Qu Qianxing with eager anticipation.

His face was dripping with venomous malice. Had anyone witnessed Tian Yan at this moment, they would never have believed that he was a lofty True Immortal who governed the Heavenly Dao across three thousand worlds; his expression resembled a hideous amalgamation of demonic qi, or a black hole devouring the cosmos.

Fortunately, that revolting smirk lasted only for a fleeting instant upon Tian Yan's face.

In the next breath, he violently yanked Tian Shou back, narrowly evading the thunderbolt that seemed destined to pierce heaven and earth.

Tian Shou had assumed Tian Yan experienced a change of heart and was about to "speak" a few words to further dissuade him from sacrificing a colleague to heaven, only to hear Tian Yan roar in hysterical fury:

—"How is this possible?! He's nothing more than a discarded defective good...!"

—"What? Did a future appear outside your divinations?"-Tian Shou realized the truth. The man hadn't suddenly softened his heart; rather, this 'Lord of Celestial Destiny' had almost certainly glimpsed an outcome he despised, halting the execution at the last second.

The corner of Tian Shou's mouth twitched. Although most of his emotions had been forcefully stripped away, being irritatingly insolent was evidently his fundamental nature rather than a mere personality trait. Fully aware that the other was preparing to claim his life, he couldn't resist jabbing at him:

—"It seems simply killing me isn't a good move after all,"-Tian Shou sneered.-"Who knows, perhaps you'll have to sacrifice yourself to 'save the Heavenly Court'!"

[If that were truly the case, it would be simple!]

Tian Yan thought in exasperation.

It was not that he harbored profound loyalty toward the Heavenly Court, but rather that Tian Yan had refined his true lifespan into the rules of the Heavenly Court—namely, the active Heavenly Dao itself. So long as the Heavenly Court prospered... no, even if the Heavenly Court declined, falling into the wretched state of today's Demon Court, so long as there were still beings willing to ascend to the Heavenly Court, Tian Yan could survive forever.

As for physical vessels, they were even less of an issue. Though none compared to Qu Qianxing, Tian Yan had prepared hundreds of similar vessels across the realms.

With such precautions in place, Tian Yan had believed he was near "eternal."

Until this very moment.

Tian Yan had no leisure to heed Tian Shou's sarcastic mockery. In agony, he clawed at his own eyes with both hands as if attempting to gouge out his eyeballs. Yet those eye sockets had long been completely hollow, resembling two black holes capable of swallowing a myriad of stars, appearing grotesquely horrifying upon that handsome, refined face.

His vision had not vanished. On the contrary, countless golden threads unfurled across Tian Yan's sight—every golden thread representing a path, and every path representing a future!

This ability was precisely the supreme foundation of his arrogance.

Manipulating these golden threads, Tian Yan could not only perceive the past, present, and future, but could even rewind into the past to alter the trajectory of what was to come. Thus, his claim of being the overseer of the Heavenly Dao was not entirely without merit.

Now, however, these golden threads (futures)—bending, curving, reaching near and far—converged at a point not far ahead regardless of their direction, knotting into a dead end!

A dead knot that even the Chief of Celestial Destiny could not untie!

Evidently, this was the truly "predetermined," "immutable" future!

Futures of this kind were exceedingly rare. Even with a control freak like Tian Yan serving as Chief of Celestial Destiny, incidents of [human effort defying destiny](# "Idiom: 人定胜天 (réndìng-shèngtiān) — human willpower triumphs over heaven") still occurred from time to time. As for a true "future" that even the Chief of Celestial Destiny could not alter, Tian Yan had witnessed it only once before in his entire existence.

That had been the prelude to the fall of the Demon Court and the rise of humankind.

Consequently, Tian Yan had always assumed that the next immutable future he could not alter would inevitably be the decline of the human race and the ascendance of other species. To this end, he had kept vigilant watch over all sentient races beneath the heavens, refusing to overlook even ancient divine beasts on the brink of extinction. Especially following the recent failure of the Celestial Emperor's Sky-Tour Ceremony, with the future growing dim and opaque, Tian Yan had suspected the moment was drawing near. Yet after observing endlessly, Tian Yan had always believed that the inexhaustible demon race was the most plausible candidate to supplant humankind.

Now, he had finally caught sight of that future:

The Heavenly Court had fallen, but the human race had not declined.

On the contrary—without the Heavenly Court, humankind had flourished like never before. And it was all "humans"! Even mortals could cultivate and wield immortal arts, rendering spirit roots—while still significant—no longer decisively absolute.

Humankind would transition completely from the era of "Immortals" into the era of "Humans"!

—"Utterly disgraceful! [Turning heaven and earth upside down](# "Idiom: 倒反天罡 (dǎofǎn-tiāngāng) — turning the heavenly celestial order upside down; utter subversion of authority or natural hierarchy")!!"

Tian Yan cursed in a low snarl, clenching the threads binding Tian Shou.

Tian Shou had assumed he was being "disciplined" for his loose tongue, silently lamenting his bitter fate while remaining powerless to resist. He could only stare at the thunderbolts crashing endlessly from the heavens. Since his "colleague" was wholly untrustworthy, his only hope was that the enemy would prove weaker.

Yet one glance was enough for Tian Shou to comprehend what Tian Yan's "future outside divination" truly entailed.

—"His thunderbolts... have they gotten stronger?"-Tian Shou asked in a trembling voice.

Naturally, Qu Qianxing's lightning had been formidable from the very start.

Had this "Demon Sovereign" not been so terrifying, and had his visage not made his connection to Tian Yan glaringly obvious, Tian Shou would never have sought Tian Yan's assistance in the first place, only to fall into his clutches.

Yet that previous "formidability" had still been within manageable limits.

Tian Shou (in his own mind) had only turned to Tian Yan because he was unwilling to pay too steep a personal price. Had he known he would be puppeted by Tian Yan, so long as he was willing to thoroughly sacrifice his subordinate celestial soldiers, he could have completely eradicated this demon army. And naturally, had he not been bound, it would have been equally impossible for that "Demon Sovereign" to claim his life.

Yet after that single blast, lightning poured like rain from the heavens—each bolt more blinding than the last, each arc more devastating than the one before, faintly embodying the power of rules capable of opposing the Heavenly Court itself!

A chill ran down Tian Shou's spine.

At this juncture, it was no longer a matter of paying a price. Tian Shou harbored zero doubt that if the other's strength continued to escalate, even in his peak state as the supreme Celestial General, clashing head-on with this lightning would likely result in his body perishing and his Dao extinguishing.

As these thoughts crossed his mind, peculiar notions stirred within Tian Shou. Out loud, he merely sought confirmation from Tian Yan:

—"He's merely a 'vessel' you crafted, isn't he? Just how did he become this terrifying?"

Indeed—where on earth had such colossal power originated?!

Tian Yan also stared coldly at Qu Qianxing.

Evidently, that "predetermined future" had nothing to do with whether he sent Tian Shou to his death. The proof lay in the fact that even after he yanked Tian Shou back, the future remained entirely unchanged—an occurrence he had zero power to alter:

That sudden surge of lightning, terrifying enough to send shivers through a Celestial General!

As for the source of this power, Tian Yan could naturally "see" it.

He "saw" that part of this power stemmed from this "illusion realm" itself: an immense formation array blanketed the core formation aperture of the realm—an aperture that would have cost even Tian Yan effort to locate—converting the recurring power of the past and the demonic qi of the demon realm into vigorous spiritual energy, funneling it unceasingly to Qu Qianxing.

Another portion of the power consisted of spiritual energy transmitted by vast numbers of cultivators pooling their strength together. To an ordinary True Immortal, this was already a staggering reservoir of power—the combined might of titans spanning half an intermediate cultivation world.

Under normal circumstances, such power amounted to nothing before Tian Yan. So long as he willed it, scripting a few narratives and designating a few "Children of Destiny," gathering the spiritual energy of an entire realm—let alone half of one—was child's play.

This time, however, Tian Yan discovered that he could not interfere with this power, because mingled within it was "Divine Power."

In Zhou Hui's terms: power imbued with "rules."

In the past, this was a force wielded exclusively by the Heavenly Court.

No—even the Heavenly Court could not... Or more accurately, even the Celestial Emperor, who possessed the authority to "bestow divine offices," could not conjure it from nothingness, granting an authority to a celestial official who completely lacked the requisite talent and credentials, thereby allowing a "god" beyond Heavenly Court control to be born.

It was precisely for this reason that the "Ministry of Celestial Destiny" existed. Rather than claiming Tian Yan's authority controlled the destiny of three thousand worlds, it was more accurate to say he guided candidates possessing the requisite capacities and qualifications into the Heavenly Court.

And it was precisely for this reason that the Heavenly Court had sealed the uncontrollable "Genesis Machine."

Yet now, the power surging from Langhuan contained "Divine Power" outside the Heavenly Court's dominion. Had someone else made contact with the "Genesis Machine"? Had a new Celestial Emperor (God) been born?!

No, that wasn't right. Why was this power so fragmented? It did not feel as though it originated from a single individual, but from countless people—so much so that even he could not trace it back to a single root!

Tian Yan's expression flickered with dark uncertainty.

He proceeded to cast several of his most formidable divinatory arts, yet none could probe the state of the "Genesis Machine," let alone any newly born "Celestial God." On the contrary, if he continued divining at this rate, he would exhaust his own life force and die first.

Tian Yan did not push himself further; in an instant, the urge to retreat took root.

The dignified Chief of Celestial Destiny was cowardly to such an extent.

He was an immortal who wielded Heavenly Destiny, and therefore understood better than anyone that destiny was absolute. Furthermore, as an entity who had always been "all-knowing and all-seeing," the moment he could no longer perceive the future, he felt like a sighted man suddenly struck blind—there was no way he would leave himself vulnerable upon a battlefield in such a state.

Naturally, Tian Yan would never admit that he intended to "flee."

—"The mandate of heaven cannot be defied. Rather than agonizing over how to alter an unchangeable future, it is far wiser to plan a retreat in advance. I cannot be expected to go down with a sinking ship, can I? However..."

Tian Yan's gaze snapped viciously back toward Qu Qianxing:

—"Even if it cannot be defied, I must test his depth before making my plans."

With that thought, Tian Shou abruptly felt his previously stripped power surging back into his vessel. Not only that, but Tian Yan funneled an additional surplus—equivalent roughly to the plundered spiritual energy of several minor worlds!

Tian Shou felt no joy; he felt only terror.

For along with the power, the emotions that had been suppressed within him were also poured back into his being—especially negative passions like fury and resentment. Like his power, they inflated several-fold, crushing what little rationality Tian Shou possessed and asserting total dominance over his mind and flesh.

In Tian Shou's vision, the entire world twisted into something bizarre and grotesque. Extreme malice drove him, birthing a ravenous desire to annihilate all things, caring not in the least if he were blasted to cinders by lightning.

Using the last shred of his sanity, Tian Shou roared:

—"Tian Yan, you f—king bastard... You planned to use me! I..."

He seemed intent on hurling venomous threats at Tian Yan, or perhaps uttering something else, but his words were ultimately swallowed by runaway malice. As his power skyrocketed, his physical form ballooned until it was the size of dozens of Giant Spirit Gods combined—propping up the sky and treading upon the earth, poised to tear the entire illusion realm apart, resembling a titanic golden bullseye.

Witnessing this sight, the demons fled even faster. Even the Heavenly Soldiers and Generals who had lost their minds to bloodlust revealed terrified expressions, scrambling to escape—though barely any remained. Yet in the very next instant, all of them melted into golden liquid, assimilated into components of that colossal golden entity.

—"Roar!!"

Tian Shou unleashed a beast-like howl, lunging a massive claw toward Qu Qianxing.

Confronted by such a celestial general, Qu Qianxing dared not take the blow directly. The boundless thunderbolts across the firmament coalesced into a shield, blocking Tian Shou's assault while sizzling and crackling against the Giant Spirit God's arm.

Unfortunately, Tian Shou had lost both the sensation of pain and the rationality to weigh risks and rewards, colliding head-on against the shield of lightning regardless of cost, locking them into a temporary stalemate.

Tian Yan, however, was far from satisfied.

—"Locked in a stalemate with a cultivator who hasn't even ascended, let alone reached the high-tier realms—Tian Shou, [you've really outdone yourself](# "Sarcasm: 真是出息了 (zhēnshi chūxi le) — quite the achievement / really made something of yourself"),"-Tian Yan remarked coldly.-"Very well. I never placed my hopes in you anyway; it seems I must do this myself after all."

As he spoke, he drew forth every destiny thread he could mobilize, lashing them toward Qu Qianxing in the midst of the confrontation.

If those destiny threads, capable of cleaving space itself, firmly ensnared Qu Qianxing, not even a wisp of his divine soul would survive—to say nothing of the fact that Tian Yan immediately followed up by casting several grand formations upon him.

Yet Tian Yan did not linger to observe the outcome of his assault.

On the contrary, having executed these actions, he slapped a final spell onto himself. His physical vessel remained upon the sacrificial altar, maintaining control over the far end of the destiny threads and Tian Shou, while his true divine soul drifted weightlessly toward another direction.

Yes—Tian Yan was fleeing.

With an immutable future looming, preserving his core divine soul in a location undetectable by others—after all, he possessed hundreds of vessels similar to Qu Qianxing across the three thousand worlds—recuperating and biding his time was the only sound strategy. Once the trajectory of the future stabilized, he would select the most suitable physical vessel, reemerge, and reclaim the optimal post.

Naturally, fleeing in such a fashion would inevitably invite suspicion. Thus, splitting off a portion of his soul to report to the Celestial Emperor and observe his reaction was also necessary. Beyond that, there was cleanup: packing his assets, severing previous covert lines, concealing his magical artifacts, and other subsequent tasks...

Tian Yan was calculating how many shards to divide his soul into, yet before he could act, he felt himself ensnared by something. A searing agony tore through his divine soul, sending curls of acrid green smoke wafting into the air.

Only then did Tian Yan discover that at some point, he had been encircled by a densely woven electric net.

These electrical currents had crawled up along his destiny threads, intertwining layer upon layer around Tian Yan. Neither his physical vessel nor his divine soul could easily rupture them.

Most critically, the "Divine Power (rules)" embedded within this force bound Tian Yan with inescapable solidity.

—"Bastard!"-Tian Yan roared in incandescent fury—though he had been furious from the start. He did not even attempt to sever the electric net, temporarily abandoning his flight to return to his physical body.

Tian Yan felt that while he could flee from Heavenly Destiny, having a useless shell he had discarded at will dare to plot against him was utterly unforgivable!

He was even slightly worried that his earlier strike had been too ruthless, allowing Qu Qianxing to die "too painlessly" at Tian Shou's hands.

Such worry, however, was glaringly superfluous.

Tian Yan glanced over, only to discover that in the fleeting instant he had "departed," a hairline spatial fissure had breached the altar, bridging several space-time coordinates.

And on the far side of that fissure was Tian Shou's colossal visage.

—"Tian Yan, die!"-he roared.

The power Tian Yan had bestowed only moments before surged from the opposite side of the fissure, transmuting into sharp swords that cleaved toward Tian Yan!

Tian Yan dodged the strike with great difficulty, scrambling to yank the destiny threads controlling Tian Shou:

—"You... useless piece of trash!"-Tian Yan spat.-[In merely a breath, he had actually been turned by the other side?!]

Yet no matter how Tian Yan jerked the destiny threads, there was zero response.

Just like the electric net encircling him and striving to purge him, those golden destiny threads had at some unknown point been corroded by lightning, inverted into Qu Qianxing's instruments.

[How could he possibly accomplish this...?]

Only at this moment did Tian Yan, for the very first time, experience a creeping dread toward Qu Qianxing.

Tian Yan had no choice but to try stirring the rationality he had suppressed within Tian Shou, attempting to pull him back to his side:

—"Tian Shou! To think you call yourself a Celestial General—controlled by a lowly mongrel from the lower realm, do you feel no shame?!"

—"*Heh*, control? Yours truly was never controlled! I have never been more sober in my life!"-Tian Shou laughed mockingly.-"Tian Yan, admit it! You're about to be devoured alive by the very vessel you created! That lightning, that power, that divine authority—*hahahaha!* You've controlled so many people; how does it feel to have your own destiny replaced?!"

—"What are you babbling about...?!"-Tian Yan froze for a moment, then understood Tian Shou's meaning.

Tian Shou seemed to believe that Qu Qianxing's power originated from Tian Yan's side—that as Tian Yan's spare vessel, Qu Qianxing had usurped the master's position, seizing Tian Yan's power to become the true Tian Yan!

Disastrously, part of this deduction was true.

At the very least, Tian Shou being controlled by Qu Qianxing was almost certainly accomplished by relying on the "threads" between Tian Yan and Qu Qianxing; precisely because Tian Shou was bound to Tian Yan, he could be seized by Qu Qianxing.

Even those destiny threads—

Precisely because a connection existed between Tian Yan and Qu Qianxing, Qu Qianxing was able to corrode the destiny threads!

The causality behind every event flashed rapidly through Tian Yan's mind. Alas, realizing this after the fact was far too late. Moreover, Tian Yan was accustomed to laying plans only after knowing all variables; even as Chief of Celestial Destiny, processing these causal chains and improvising countermeasures in an instant was impossible.

In his haste, Tian Yan could only urge Tian Shou:

—"Since you know, why—"

—"You wanted me dead! Why would I stand on your side?"-Tian Shou sneered in fury.-"In any case, I don't give a damn who Tian Yan is!"

[...But the Celestial Emperor cares!]

Tian Yan wanted to retort, but knew words were futile.

Tian Shou appeared to be rationalizing, but in reality, he was still being controlled—and ironically, this form of manipulation that appeared to retain personal logic was vastly more dangerous than crude puppetry.

—"In that case, you might as well die!"-Tian Yan declared.

He snapped Tian Shou's destiny threads and attempted to kick Tian Shou back down into the lower realm.

Regrettably, Tian Yan had underestimated Tian Shou, just as he had always looked down upon Qu Qianxing.

Whatever else he was, Tian Shou was still the number-one warrior of the Celestial Realm (in his own mind). Incompetent though he often was, the instant he saw his destiny threads severed, he erupted with strength that surpassed even the Chief of Celestial Destiny's expectations, lunging furiously toward Tian Yan.

Tian Yan scoffed and moved to dodge, but the instant his feet shifted, he felt once more the lash-like agony inflicted by the electrical current. He then realized that he remained enveloped within that golden electric net, resembling a moth trapped in spider silk.

How utterly laughable: the weaver of destiny threads ensnared by destiny threads.

Tian Yan's eyes widened.

He saw an ocean of golden luminescence completely drown him.
