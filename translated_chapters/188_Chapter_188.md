# Chapter 188: Specific Methods for Slacking Off

[So exhausted... I don't want to work!]

[Did I painstakingly cultivate and ascend all the way up here just to torture myself like this?!]

Lin Qi clutched his temperature-regulated jade mug, staring with utter nihilism at the mountainous stacks of official dossiers piled high upon his desk.

Back in the day, when he was still in his native minor world, he had been a renowned Guardian Immortal Lord. From the Void Refining Stage onward, he stood as a supreme figure—[below one and above ten thousand](# "Literal CN: 一人之下万人之上 (yīrén zhīxià wànrén zhīshàng) — traditional idiom meaning second only to the emperor, outranking everyone else"). That "one," of course, referred to the local agency dispatched by the Heavenly Court. Even then, taking into account Lin Qi's sheer potential and high-level connections, the immortal officials stationed there scarcely dared to cross him.

Those had been the most carefree, glorious days of Lin Qi's life.

Regrettably, [childhood must come to an end eventually](# "Meme: 童年总有结束的一天 (X) — playful pop-culture riff indicating the carefree golden era has expired"). With the continuous increase of Lin Qi's cultivation base, his native minor world could no longer accommodate his presence. Coupled with passing the Heavenly Court's qualification assessment with flying colors, he ultimately "ascended."

Of course, this wasn't to say Lin Qi hated the Heavenly Court.

The Heavenly Court resided within the most flawless "Major World" known across existence.

This "Major World" was an absolute sanctuary blessed by gods and buddhas, where even inner demons could not intrude. This "Major World" possessed inexhaustible resources, boasting exotic treasures, wondrous flowers, and miraculous trees that all the minor worlds possessed or lacked. This "Major World" was so colossal that no one could reach its bounds, teeming with endless spiritual qi to sustain immortals across infinite lifespans.

—At least, that was how Lin Qi perceived it.

When he had first ascended, he too had been ecstatic, believing he had arrived in paradise. But no matter how perfect a world was, lingering there for several centuries or millennia would bore anyone to tears. What was more, the Heavenly Court was exceptionally peaceful, obsessively regulated; everything was strictly governed down to the finest detail by the Heavenly Statutes. That meant there were zero thrills to seek, zero novelties to be found.

Not to mention, as a [civil servant of the Heavenly Court](# "Literal CN: 天庭公务员 (tiāntíng gōngwùyuán) — modern corporate humor contrasting divine ascension with grueling white-collar office bureaucracy"), Lin Qi had to face never-ending administrative paperwork and an even more mind-numbing workplace day in and day out.

Most terrifying of all, there was no end to such days.

An immortal's lifespan was infinite.

Barring any accidents, they would live on for thousands, tens of thousands, hundreds of millions of years, repeating the exact same daily routine forever.

—"I really want to find a minor world and take a proper vacation!"-Lin Qi muttered to himself.-"For instance, that minor world invaded by the demon race last time wasn't bad. I wonder if claiming my [mortal heart was stirred](# "Literal CN: 动了凡心 (dòngle fánxīn) — developing romantic feelings for a mortal, a classic violation of heavenly rules") down there would let me violate the Heavenly Statutes and get demoted down... Ah, but it's only been fifty years since my last '[love tribulation](# "Literal CN: 情劫 (qíngjié) — karmic trial of mortal romance in Taoist mythology").' Repeatedly breaking celestial law and getting demoted would be a pain. Besides, the [Immortal Execution Stage](# "Literal CN: 诛仙台 (zhūxiāntái) — execution terrace in Taoist celestial myth used to strip immortals of divinity and cast them into the mortal realm") has apparently been fully booked for the past several centuries! There are way too many bastards looking for excuses to get banished! If this keeps up, the higher-ups might crack down [yet again, and again, and again](# "Internet slang: 又双叒叕 (yòu shuāng ruò zhuó) — four characters featuring increasing repetitions of 又, slang for endless recurring cycles)! At that point, it won't just be a simple demotion—I'll be flat-out fired!"

Though Lin Qi found life as a heavenly civil servant unbearable, when it actually came to being demoted or fired, he wasn't willing to let that happen either.

Lin Qi let out a long sigh:

—"If only something—anything—would happen,"-he muttered.

As though in answer to his wish, an immortal boy attendant guarding the doorway reported:

—"My Lord Heavenly General, Envoy Ren Ran of Minor World No. 67 requests a transmission with you?"-the attendant asked.

—"An Envoy?"-Lin Qi frowned.

[Heavenly Envoy](# "Literal CN: 天使 (tiānshǐ) — literally 'Envoy of Heaven'; the author playfully uses the Chinese word for Angel/Heavenly Messenger as a bureaucratic title for low-tier overseers"), as the name implied: an envoy of the Heavenly Court.

Yet it was merely a fine-sounding title.

The worlds to which "Heavenly Envoys" were dispatched were impoverished backwaters—minor worlds whose usable value had already been completely hollowed out. If a realm was like Lin Qi's homeland, possessing abundant spiritual qi and distinctive local specialties, the Heavenly Court would directly establish an official agency for direct administrative oversight, rather than merely stationing a lone watcher to keep an eye on things.

Without a second thought, Lin Qi said:

—"Why does every random person come looking for me? Doesn't she have her own dedicated liaison officer? I'm busy to death over here. I won't see her!"-Lin Qi barked.

—"But she claims she tried contacting her dedicated liaison several times without receiving any response,"-the immortal attendant said awkwardly.

—"Then tell her to try contacting them a few more times. Could a dedicated liaison officer simply vanish into thin air?"-Lin Qi retorted dismissively.-"Every world has a different flow of time; failing to establish real-time communication is completely normal. If everyone came looking for me every time a call didn't go through, wouldn't I be doing nothing else all day?"

[—"...Understood."](# "Speaker: Immortal Attendant")

With his superior speaking to such a degree, the subordinate naturally dared not press the matter further.

Holding his breath, the immortal attendant backed away, intending to leave. But before his foot could cross the threshold, a voice from above halted him:

—"Wait. Number Sixty-Seven... isn't that the minor world that has dragged its feet for ten whole years without completely reclaiming its resources?"-Lin Qi called out.

—"Indeed!"-the attendant replied quickly. Goddess Ren Ran had evidently vented her grievances to him quite a bit, granting him some familiarity with the situation.-"That world was originally a part of the Heavenly Court, where the Langhuan Archive was situated. According to the original plan, the relevant resources were supposed to have been reclaimed ten years ago, but the effort met with repeated setbacks. It appears to be the same this time. Not only has Lady Ren Ran run into trouble, but she is also unable to reach her liaison officer, leaving her no choice but to seek Your Lordship's aid."

—"Hmph, useless trash,"-Lin Qi sneered coldly.-"['A day in heaven is a year on earth'](# "Literal CN: 天上一天，地上一年 (tiānshàng yītiān, dìshang yīnián) — time flows vastly slower in the celestial realm compared to the mortal world"). Ten years in the Heavenly Court translates to a full three thousand years down in her realm! To fail to complete an assigned task after three thousand years, and she still has the nerve to beg me for help?"

The attendant lowered his head, not daring to chime in.

Yet Lin Qi showed little genuine anger.

Three thousand years sounded like an eternity—long enough for a mortal civilization to rise and fall. But to immortals possessing limitless lifespans, it amounted to practically nothing.

Not to mention, owing to the disparate flow of time, "only" ten years had elapsed in the Heavenly Court.

To Lin Qi, ten years felt like ten days.

A subordinate department staffer failing to complete an assignment properly and stalling for "ten days" fell well within tolerable limits.

In fact, it was a ubiquitous phenomenon.

The passage of time in the Heavenly Court was boundless and boring. Immortal officials and heavenly generals were constantly seeking amusement. Even a heavenly general like Lin Qi plotted every other day to descend to the mortal realm for fun, to say nothing of the officials who had already secured a coveted quota to descend.

Among them, someone like Ren Ran had the sweetest gig of all.

After all, if one violated the Heavenly Statutes and was demoted to the mortal dust, one would inevitably lose memories and power to some degree. In that state, descending offered zero pleasure; if luck was bad, one might not be restored to their original post upon return, or might never make it back at all—turning joy into sorrow.

A special commissioner like Ren Ran, tasked with monitoring a minor world and recovering usable resources, faced no restrictions on her memories or physical form. Her power was merely sealed to a threshold the world would not reject—which happened to be the absolute apex tier of that realm. Furthermore, as long as the mission was eventually completed, there was virtually no supervisor breathing down her neck. It was an assignment every immortal official dreamed of.

What was more, if the task was carried out well, securing a promotion upon return wasn't out of the question.

However, considering that immortal officials possessed infinite lifespans and the old fogies above wouldn't easily drop dead, even if one qualified for a promotion, one would have to wait several centuries, millennia, or even tens of thousands of years. Consequently, most immortal officials were far more inclined to sacrifice their performance metrics, dragging out the completion time just a bit so they could enjoy a few thousand or tens of thousands of years in the lower realm.

Among them, stalling for ten years was nothing; there were plenty who delayed for a century (Heavenly Court time), staying until the minor world's resources were utterly drained dry.

—"Claiming she ran into difficulties? She's undoubtedly doing the exact same thing!"-Lin Qi sneered.

For this reason, Lin Qi felt no interest in whatever Ren Ran wanted to report.

—At most, she had slacked off for too long, realized things were heading south, and wanted Lin Qi to help clean up her mess.

Lin Qi naturally had no desire to do that. Yet the reason he changed his mind was that he suddenly remembered something:

—"Hasn't that world already been mostly reclaimed? What kind of trouble could she possibly have encountered?"-Lin Qi asked.

—"This..."-the attendant murmured awkwardly, equally in the dark.

The other party had whined for ages without getting to the point. It was purely because she was being such a persistent nuisance that the attendant had been forced to report it.

Lin Qi didn't mind, waving his hand:

—"Forget it. Tell her to speak to me directly,"-Lin Qi said.

The attendant nodded in acknowledgement and hurried away.

Shortly after, he returned, cradling an incense burner in his hands.

Three sticks of incense burned within it, the rising white smoke coalescing into a cloud of mist. In the mist, a woman could be faintly seen gazing over with nervous apprehension—none other than Goddess Ren Ran.

—"Greetings, My Lord Heavenly General,"-Goddess Ren Ran bowed.

Her face was fraught with extreme anxiety and tension. The moment they connected, she launched straight into venting her woes:

—"This humble one did not wish to disturb Your Lordship, but some utterly bizarre matters have transpired within this minor world that must be reported to the Heavenly Court. Yet the emergency liaison official has failed to respond, leaving this humble one no choice but to—"-Goddess Ren Ran began in a rush.

—"Halt!"-Lin Qi cut her off rudely.

Lin Qi had zero interest in listening to her babble; in truth, he couldn't care less about what had happened in the lower realm:

—"Get to the point! I have piles of business to handle and no time to parse every trivial detail. I only want to know: what is the reclamation progress of the Langhuan Forbidden Grounds? How much is left there?"-Lin Qi demanded.

—"Langhuan? Progress?"-Goddess Ren Ran blinked, looking utterly blank.

Lin Qi berated her at once:

—"Didn't you go down there precisely to reclaim that world's resources? How can you know nothing? What on earth have you been doing down there?!"-Lin Qi scolded sharply.

—"Th-this humble one's primary duty was never that,"-Goddess Ren Ran explained in a fluster. Standing before a superior immortal official of the Heavenly Court, she displayed none of the imperious grandeur she wielded in the lower realm.-"My duty is to oversee the minor world and provide assistance to descending celestial officials. The official tasked with reclaiming this world's resources is scheduled to arrive at the next execution on the Immortal Execution Stage—which is three hun... one year from now in Heavenly Court time."

[Just as I thought.]

Lin Qi's eyes lit up.

This was the real reason he had agreed to see Goddess Ren Ran.

It was also why the "eternally occupied" Guardian Demon-Subduing Heavenly General had remembered the "unremarkable" Minor World No. 67.

From what Lin Qi knew, Minor World No. 67 had once formed part of the Heavenly Court and possessed thousands of tangled ties with the celestial realm, whether in bloodlines or remnant treasures. Therefore, even though the higher-ups had decided to abandon the world, they wished to retrieve as many portable treasures as possible and resolve whatever bloodlines could not be brought along before severing it entirely.

To that end, the Heavenly Court had left several immortal officials like Goddess Ren Ran in that world to conduct "preliminary cataloging," after which "porters" would be sent to haul everything up.

Yet this plan seemed to have run into complications.

Every year, the Immortal Execution Stage would cast down a few immortal officials on pretexts such as "mortal affections stirred," "undergoing tribulation," or "unfinished karmic ties." Yet none of them stayed long before returning, unable to even clearly explain why they had come back.

Until two years ago (Heavenly Court time)—

—"Two years ago, who was it again... Lord Tunhuo? Didn't he already succeed?"-Lin Qi asked pointedly.-"Not only did Tunhuo retrieve the desired archives, he even returned with a wife and child (chuckle). His sole mistake was failing to slay a certain exiled Wenqu Star, driving him into demonic corruption instead. That triggered a demon invasion of the Immortal-Demon Realm, during which the Celestial Emperor of the Immortal-Demon Realm perished in battle. Yet Tunhuo managed to redeem his fault with meritorious service by repelling the demon race, ultimately becoming ruler of the Immortal-Demon Realm."

[And became my boss's boss!]

By the end of his sentence, Lin Qi was practically gnashing his teeth.

Positions in the Heavenly Court were precious beyond measure, to say nothing of the substantive authority to govern an entire medium-sized world that even immortals were not rejected from. Lin Qi had every reason to suspect that Tunhuo had deliberately driven the Wenqu Star into demonic corruption, conspiring with him to seize control of the Immortal-Demon Realm!

Goddess Ren Ran was still completely out of the loop:

—"Two years ago? The Immortal-Demon Realm? Eh? Eh?!"-Goddess Ren Ran stammered.

She had so many questions that she didn't even know where to begin. Only upon catching the menacing glare of the "Lord Heavenly General" did she shudder, realizing that knowing nothing in front of a superior was definitely not a good look.

Daring not press further, Ren Ran offered another defense:

—"My Lord Heavenly General, this humble one has strictly adhered to her assigned duties—guarding that world! Without orders from the Heavenly Court, this humble one dared not pry. However, as far as I know, isn't the final contingent of immortal officials responsible for inventorying this world's resources not scheduled to arrive until three hun... one year from now (Heavenly Court time)? In the several years (Heavenly Court time) prior to this, no other immortals or demons should have arrived,"-Ren Ran argued defensively.

—"No other immortals or demons have visited?"-Lin Qi asked, casting a half-smiling glance at his attendant.

The clever attendant had already rummaged through the archives and pulled up all the information regarding that world:

—"According to the failure reports submitted by the preceding immortal officials, that world has already undergone at least five temporal cycles. They claimed that temporal loops were precisely what caused their mission failures,"-the attendant reported promptly.

—"And yet, you had no idea about the time loops? My Lady 'Ren Ran'?"-Lin Qi said, fixing his gaze upon Goddess Ren Ran.

"...!!!"

Goddess Ren Ran's face instantly drained of color.

As the saying went: [there might be mistaken names, but never a mistaken nickname](# "Proverb: 有取错的名字，没有叫错的外号 — people may be given improper names at birth, but their sobriquets always reflect their true nature"). To some extent, an immortal's title operated on the exact same principle.

Though most cultivators in their native worlds loved piling lavish honorifics upon themselves the moment they gained a shred of cultivation—titles like "Immortal Venerable," "Immortal Lord," "Great Sage," "Heavenly Venerable," "Goddess," or "Fairy"—for the title itself to win universal respect and receive official recognition from the Heavenly Court upon ascension, it had to align with their core attributes.

Goddess Ren Ran was precisely such an example.

True to her title of "Ren Ran," [she possessed the ability to control the passage of time](# "Literal CN: 荏苒 (rěnrǎn) — from 光阴荏苒 (guāngyīn rěnrǎn), the fleeting passage of time; her title reflects her authority over temporal flow").

Lin Qi didn't look at Goddess Ren Ran. Taking the dossier from the attendant's hands, he read aloud in a slow, deliberate cadence:

> "Minor World No. 67, formerly a sector of the Heavenly Court's 'Academy.' Thousands of years ago, it fell to the lower realm to seal the Demon Eye. This process shattered the area's space into fragments and created fissures in time, giving rise to unstable temporal backtracking and resets. In particular, during the War between Gods and Demons thirty years ago, both combatant factions pushed deep into the Abyssal Ocean, entangling [Zhujiuyin](# "Mythology: 烛九阴 (Zhújiǔyīn) — Torch Dragon / Illuminating Nine Yin; ancient primordial solar-temporal dragon deity whose opening and closing of eyes governs day, night, and seasons") coiled beneath the sea and inflicting severe injuries upon it, rendering the region's time increasingly chaotic. Combined with the encroaching influence of the demon race, the sector is projected to be completely destroyed by spatio-temporal fissures within one hundred years. Consequently, the Heavenly Court had no choice but to abandon this region and reclaim all resources and bloodlines.
> 
> "To ensure the smooth execution of this mission, 【classified】 skilled in spatial arts and artisans of Clan Ran were dispatched to stabilize the realm's space, while Ren Ran and Cha Na, masters of temporal immortal arts, were dispatched to stabilize time. Among them, Cha Na was recalled early due to a 【classified】 mission, leaving Goddess Ren Ran behind to continue fulfilling her duties—"

Hearing this, Goddess Ren Ran's face turned from green to pale white.

As a goddess appointed to safeguard time, failing to notice that the world she oversaw had reset more than five times was something that could no longer be brushed off as mere carelessness—it constituted gross dereliction of duty!

—"No, this humble one had no idea! I... no, Untiring! That's it! It must be Untiring's fault!"-Goddess Ren Ran stammered, scrambling for an excuse. After a few flustered words, something suddenly occurred to her, and her expression turned vicious.-"He was the one who killed Immortal Lord Cha Na! It must be him who scrambled time!"

—"Wasn't Cha Na already reassigned?"-Lin Qi asked.

—"He returned to the Heavenly Court first, but he left behind a bloodline in that realm to inherit his arts and magical treasures, assisting me in safeguarding the world! Yet one year ago—one year in this world's time—he was slain by Untiring, and his treasures were seized!"-Goddess Ren Ran accused furiously.-"Not only that, Untiring has been relentlessly targeting Carefree Palace! Recently, 【classified】 has also gone missing! It's very likely... no, it was definitely Untiring's doing!"

—"Who is Untiring...?"-Lin Qi inquired.

—"Untiring is the cultivator with the highest cultivation base in this world,"-Ren Ran gritted her teeth.-"With poor talent and zero hope of ascension, he somehow possesses an inexplicably monstrous strength. Not long ago, he even repaired the Ascension Stairway and subjugated the tribulation lightning!"

[...Pfft. She's already dead-set on pinning the blame on a local native?]

[Doesn't she have a brain? You're celestials, while he's merely a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator. How could he possibly slay a True Immortal without a sound, much less manipulate time?]

Lin Qi thought dismissively, not believing a single word out of Goddess Ren Ran's mouth.

Yet outwardly, he assumed a grave expression, casually flipping through the file:

—"Now that you mention it, past reports did mention this individual. Born of mortal flesh and blood, he indeed lacks the qualifications to ascend. The corrupted Wenqu Star was once taken in as his disciple, which led to his inclusion on the roster. Two years ago (Heavenly Court time), he was slain by Tunhuo, the current Celestial Emperor of the Immortal-Demon Realm, in accordance with the Mandate of Heaven. In theory, he shouldn't possess the capability to rewind time, should he? Even if he could manage it, it wouldn't come without immense cost—his power should be severely depleted,"-Lin Qi noted solemnly.

—"B-but the way he behaves doesn't look like his strength was depleted at all..."-Goddess Ren Ran stammered in panic.

—"Don't panic, I never said I didn't believe you,"-Lin Qi said earnestly, his tone turning inexplicably gentle.-"Regardless of whether it has anything to do with this Untiring, judging by your account, there are indeed numerous points of suspicion... No, even prior to this, the matter of the Wenqu Star falling into demonic corruption was exceedingly suspicious. Not to mention, such a 'simple' mission has dragged on for ten celestial years—three thousand mortal years—and multiple rotations of celestial officials have failed to finish it."

Lin Qi feigned a solemn sigh:

—"Ah, very well. One must see things through from start to finish; it would not do for something to go awry at the final juncture. Hurry and submit a formal requisition file. I might as well descend personally to investigate,"-Lin Qi added.

—"My Lord Heavenly General!"-Ren Ran cried, touched to tears.

—"My Lord Heavenly General!"-the immortal attendant gasped in panic.-"This violates protocol!"

—"Where matters concerning the demon race are involved, no amount of caution is excessive,"-Lin Qi replied.

—"Even so, we should inform the Southern Heavenly Gate liaison official overseeing that sector first—"-the attendant protested.

[The Southern Heavenly Gate? The celestial official overseeing Minor World No. 67 isn't just an immortal official—he's a minor deity, isn't he?]

[Filing a requisition through him would be an absolute nightmare.]

[With the Heavenly Court's snail-paced bureaucratic efficiency, delays lasting months until the end of the year would be inevitable. By then, the Immortal Execution Stage would already be releasing the designated convicts, and other chosen officials would take the spot. How could it ever be his turn to descend for a vacation?!]

Lin Qi thought with intense dissatisfaction.

That was right: vacation.

Or rather, [paid leave](# "Literal CN: 带薪休假 (dàixīn xiūjià) — paid vacation / paid time off; modern corporate employment concept humorously applied to heavenly immortals").

This was Lin Qi's ultimate objective.

At any rate, that world had already seen several immortal officials sent down to [run a campaign](# "Gaming slang: 跑团 (pǎotuán) — tabletop RPG session / running a TRPG quest; gaming slang humorously applied to divine missions to the mortal world") (and fail); one more wouldn't make a difference!

Of course, if he could genuinely uncover the truth behind the Wenqu Star's fall to demonic corruption and grab some leverage over Tunhuo, the Celestial Emperor of the Immortal-Demon Realm, Lin Qi certainly wouldn't pass up such an opportunity for promotion.

With his mind brimming with private calculations, Lin Qi naturally had no desire to let anyone else meddle:

—"Since Lady Goddess has contacted them several times without response, it likely indicates the Southern Heavenly Gate is swamped with work. There is no need to disturb His Divinity,"-Lin Qi said smoothly.-"Furthermore, as a Heavenly General, I possess the authority to act at my discretion regarding matters concerning the demon race! It's just that during my absence, you'll need to keep an eye on some of the workload."

The immortal attendant glanced at the towering dossiers on the desk, his face twisting into a grimace of bitter despair, yet he dared not voice any objection.

Sadly, no one cared about his suffering.

Goddess Ren Ran exclaimed with delight:

—"This humble one shall immediately order the lower realm's cultivators to prepare a grand reception for Your Lordship's arrival!"-she offered eagerly.

—"No need for such theatrics,"-Lin Qi said reservedly, mindful that he was, after all, slacking off from work.-"Rather than useless ceremonies, I am more concerned that the minor world cannot withstand my power. I will require a vessel such as a 'Dao Bone' or 'Immortal Remains' to shoulder a portion of the burden."

—"Eh? Is Your Lordship not planning to seal your cultivation base before descending...?"-Goddess Ren Ran asked blankly.

[What kind of 'vacation' involves sealing one's own power?!]

Lin Qi glared irritably at the clueless goddess.

She shuddered under his glare, instantly catching on:

—"A D-Dao Bone? We have one! This humble one recalls that the Heavenly Dao Alliance possesses one! I shall prepare it immediately!"-she stammered hurriedly.

[Laziness can truly drive a person to do anything.]

Yuwen Xuan reflected.

He followed Ran Deng to her cave dwelling.

Naturally, this was not the "complete" Yuwen Xuan.

If he dared climb down from the peach tree in his entirety, Master would materialize before him the very next second, skewer him onto the highest bough of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, and slow-roast him over the flames of the Primordial Sun.

Given that his junior sister had brought such an exceptional magical treasure, Yuwen Xuan figured it was best not to trouble Master to demonstrate his rather lackluster culinary arts.

Thus, Yuwen Xuan had detached a small sliver of his soul—his true form had done this countless times anyway, so it wasn't the least bit difficult—and shaped it into a complete humanoid figure.

However, such a petty trick still couldn't fool Zhou Hui. The moment Master returned, he would immediately notice that his disciple differed from when he had been strung up: his soul was missing a corner.

This was where the Heavenly Robe proved its worth.

The robe's function was not mere "invisibility." Rather, it operated on the cognitive level of the target, manipulating perception so that they completely disregarded the wearer's existence.

In other words, the Heavenly Robe reduced a person's presence to something akin to the question: [what did you have for breakfast seventy-three days ago?](# "Metaphor: 七十三天前的早饭 — an obscure memory that technically exists in one's mind but is utterly impossible to recall or notice")—the information technically existed in the brain, yet one could never fish it out.

This meant that even if Zhou Hui saw that Yuwen Xuan's soul was missing a chunk, or even if this soul sliver stood right in front of Zhou Hui's eyes, as long as he wore the Heavenly Robe, Zhou Hui would simply fail to register the sliver's existence.

Yet this Heavenly Robe could also be worn inside out... or rather, worn right-side out.

When the side with the exquisite embroidered patterns faced outward, whoever wore the robe became the most conspicuous person in all creation. Even when not in their presence, everyone's thoughts would be completely consumed by them.

Such an inherent property was remarkably rare. It felt less like an effect achievable through immortal arts or array formations and more like the innate attribute of some divine beast or mythical demon.

Yuwen Xuan felt a flicker of curiosity about this.

Yet the question merely slid through the intestinal convolutions of his brain like indigestible gristle. After all, the material used to craft the Heavenly Robe had come from Second Brother's enemy, not his. Yuwen Xuan would never waste precious mental energy pondering information useless to him.

At this moment, Yuwen Xuan was gazing with mild astonishment at a sand table inside Ran Deng's cave dwelling.

As an array master, Ran Deng possessed many sand tables in her abode. But the one Yuwen Xuan was examining was a panoramic model of this minor world's Five Domains and Six Abysses.

Such a sand table was naturally not intended to display architecture or formations, nor was it meant to depict mountains, rivers, and geomantic feng shui. The dense clusters of red dots covering its surface cataloged various cultivation clans alongside their respective specialties; it was, astoundingly, a strategic distribution map of all cultivation powers across the realm.

—"Are you planning to conquer the cultivation world?"-Yuwen Xuan asked suspiciously.

—"Who would do something so bothersome?"-Ran Deng said, massaging her temples.-"Though I suppose I must have lost my mind, because what I'm aiming to do isn't much different: I want to take this opportunity to integrate all engineering-related human resources across the cultivation world."

—"Heh,"-Yuwen Xuan let out an ambiguous scoff, glancing back at the sand table.

Integrate human resources?

Yuwen Xuan felt that conquering the cultivation world would honestly be easier!

After all, array formations, artifact crafting, and even alchemy were considered mere "side hustles" by cultivators. Everyone cultivated with the goal of achieving immortality; so long as there remained a sliver of hope for advancement, no one was willing to squander time on such unorthodox crafts. Consequently, aside from a handful of poached-proof specialists cultivated by major sects and giant organizations, other cultivators merely dabbled—knowing a bit of everything while mastering nothing. Even if someone did master a craft, they would never advertise it, for fear of being abducted by higher-realm powerhouses and locked away in a dungeon to hammer iron forever.

In Master's words: the social division of labor was shockingly primitive, worse than mortal societies, lingering in the primary stage of social development.

Heaven knew what Ran Deng was thinking, actually wanting to undertake something so excruciatingly troublesome.

—"I want to solve this once and for all!"-Ran Deng added defensively.-"I've finally figured it out: it isn't just Guan Ye. Given our sect's work culture, I will never have a day of peace! Last year it was the waterworks project; this year it's a massive teleportation array; next year heaven knows what even more terrifying madness they'll cook up! If I just lie flat and wait, they'll dump more and more 'surprises' on my head! Only by striking first and seizing overall control can I subcontract these 'surprises' out to other people when they hit!"

—"Splendid ambition! You have my full support,"-Yuwen Xuan applauded.

—"However, you also know this isn't an easy task, right?"-he said, clapping a hand affectionately onto his junior sister's shoulder and peering straight into her eyes.-"[If you want my help, you've got to add more money](# "Meme: 得加钱 (dé jiā qián) — 'You have to add more money'; famous line from the film Brotherhood of Blades (绣春刀), widely used across Chinese internet culture for mercenaries demanding higher pay for extra work")."

A single Heavenly Robe—and one stolen from Second Brother at that—was hardly worth him getting tangled up in such an exhausting hassle!

—"...Rest assured, Eldest Senior Brother, I will compensate you handsomely,"-Ran Deng replied.-"Besides, I have my own plan; I don't intend to make you do everything in my stead."

After all, if she let this lunatic loose, it might genuinely turn into a conquest of the cultivation world.

Ran Deng merely wanted to become the cultivation world's supreme general contractor, not its empress.

Tapping the sand table, she said:

—"Just as I mentioned earlier, I only need you to act as an assassin and help me take care of the most troublesome targets first. For example, the Department of Works of the Heavenly Dao Alliance—"-she said.

As she spoke, the map of the Northern Domain on the sand table zoomed in, displaying the detailed layout of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, down to the exact location of the Department of Works.

—"Pfft! Here I thought you'd want to eliminate Fourth Brother!"-Yuwen Xuan burst into laughter upon hearing this.-"Turns out you're not only dutifully preparing to work, but you're even helping avenge Fourth Brother?"

—"I don't know anything about revenge or grudges! It's simply that they are standing in my path, so I need Eldest Senior Brother's help,"-Ran Deng said. Gazing at the Heavenly Dao Alliance upon the sand table, she let out a quiet sigh:

—"Still, they really have lived a bit too long."
