# Chapter 94: [Unforgivable](<# "Literal CN: 不可饶恕 (bù kě ráoshù) — unforgivable / unpardonable; here humorously referencing the ultimate sin of deleting someone's video game save file and clearing their high score">) (第94章 不可饶恕)

The Demon Eye seemed to have opened.

Overhead, a beam of light suddenly appeared.

The Brown-Winged Fei Mother felt a twinge of bewilderment.

She remembered that "Master" had once said the Demon Eye would open sooner or later, but that it was a long, arduous process. She had to fill the entire cavern with eggs nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine times; those eggs had to hatch nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine times; they had to slaughter one another nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine times to produce the strongest Fei Kings; and those Fei Kings would fight one another yet again. Only when the nine hundred and ninety-nine most formidable Fei Generals were selected would the Demon Eye reopen, and the path leading to the "hunting grounds" would unlock once more.

Yet now, she had not laid enough Fei Kings, nor had her children's slaughter reached its final conclusion, yet the Demon Eye had opened anyway.

[Did 'Master's' prophecy err?]

[Or has some unexpected turn of events occurred that even 'Master' failed to foresee?]

The Brown-Winged Fei Mother wondered hesitantly.

Right at this moment, she sensed a faint wisp of spiritual energy wafting from that beam of light.

The Brown-Winged Fei Mother cared little for the scent of spiritual energy. That taste diluted her demonic qi, dulled her wings, and slowed her movements. Yet she also knew that her favorite prey—human beings—were exceedingly fond of spiritual energy. The more spiritual energy was concentrated in an area, the more succulent human flesh became, and the sweeter their souls tasted.

At the mere thought of the taste of humans, the Brown-Winged Fei Mother felt her already sunken belly grow even more hollow.

Ever since she was sealed inside this [insect urn](<# "Literal CN: 虫壶 (chóng hú) — an insect urn or Gu jar used to seal poisonous insects together to fight until only the deadliest survives">) by "Master" thousands of years ago, she had eaten nothing aside from her own offspring, to say nothing of delicious humans.

In that instant, the Brown-Winged Fei Mother ceased troubling a brain that was hardly meant for thinking in the first place.

Spreading her wings, she rubbed them together, producing a shrill frequency entirely inaudible to human ears.

Instantly, the entire insect hive stirred into a violent commotion.

The Fei bugs locked in perpetual combat ceased their endless butchery, coiling in anticipation as they awaited their mother's command.

It was fortunate that these demon bugs had not yet reached maturity or inherited ancestral memories; otherwise, the moment light shone from above, they would have ignored the Fei Mother's direction entirely, surging outward like a dense black storm.

For Fei bugs starved for thousands of years, nothing could suppress their desire to devour all things.

Yet this Fei Mother had lived through the previous war; she was far more cautious than ordinary Fei bugs.

She did not order all her offspring to rush out through the Demon Eye immediately. Instead, she singled out several dozen of the smallest Fei bugs to fly toward the Demon Eye, resolved to scout the situation first.

—Naturally, part of the reason behind this decision was that the opening of the Demon Eye was far too narrow; insects that were not sufficiently tiny couldn't squeeze through at all. If she had left those brainless offspring to decide for themselves, they would likely have engaged in another frenzy of fratricide and bred for dozens more generations until spawning a massive swarm of miniature Fei bugs before surging out en masse.

Now, under the Fei Mother's direct command, they managed to avoid an unnecessary bout of [involution](<# "Literal CN: 内卷 (nèijuǎn) — hyper-competitive rat race / cutthroat internal zero-sum competition">).

Unfurling their wings, several dozen Fei bugs took flight toward the beam of light.

*

[—"Huh? Has the Demon Eye opened?"](<# "Speaker: Demon Guard">)

At the exact same time, a demon soldier remarked with a sudden premonition.

He was a humanoid demon who, aside from bearing two extra eyes, looked little different from an ordinary human.

Yet the face he turned toward that faint glimmer of light was etched with savage greed and ravenous hunger:
[—"Can we head to the 'hunting grounds' to hunt already?"](<# "Speaker: Demon Guard">)

The other demon soldier on duty with him shook his head:
—"It's not time yet,"-he said.-"There are still several centuries to go!"

—"Did an accident happen?"-the first demon soldier asked nervously.

Yet right after, a grin split his face all the way to the corners of his ears:
—"Wait, isn't that even better?!"-he crowed.

Most demons harbored virtually no concept of "obeying orders" or "reporting anomalies upon discovery."

Even when stationed as "sentries" dutifully guarding a post, they were either like the Fei Mother—forcibly sealed near their guard post by far mightier demons—or opportunistic demon soldiers and demonic servants lingering around spatial rifts. They loitered about in hopes of ambushing and devouring whatever unlucky, feeble creatures slipped through the cracks—whether wandering vengeful souls, stray low-tier demons, or newly 'ascended' demon cultivators who had practiced demonic arts.

An early opening of the "hunting grounds" was nothing short of a joyous occasion for them.

They certainly wouldn't notify any other demons—least of all high-ranking nobility—and would instead slip across first to feast to their hearts' content.

Saliva began dripping uncontrollably from the demon soldier's jaws:
—"It has been ages since I last tasted humans,"-he muttered hungrily.

—"Don't get too excited,"-the other demon soldier warned with greater caution, even though his own drool was nearly trickling down.-"Who's to say it isn't a trap? Let those bugs go ahead and scout first."

Besides, with their current size, they couldn't squeeze through right now anyway.

—"Haha, if it's a trap, that's even better!"-the first demon soldier chuckled with manic glee.-"Those puny humans always think they can slay us, yet they never understand that they are actually the finest [spiritual nourishment](<# "Literal CN: 养料 (yǎngliào) — fertilizer / nutrients; demons view humans as emotional nutrients">)."

Indeed: to demons, humans were their greatest nourishment.

This held true not merely in the culinary sense, but even more so in terms of demonic pollution.

Demonic qi propagated through the conduit of human negative emotions. The more intense the negative emotion, the more potent the demonic qi it nurtured, corrupting humans into ever more terrifying demons.

This was precisely why immortal cultivation stressed "purity of heart and moderation of desire."

Without love, there was no hatred. Devoid of other desires, negative emotions naturally could not arise, preventing qi deviation and failed ascensions.

Conversely, mortals who had yet to ascend could never truly eradicate emotion.

Even if one harbored righteous resolve and prepared to battle demons to the death, both "killing intent" and "hatred" were inherently negative emotions capable of propagating demonic qi.

In other words, as long as those demon bugs flew into the mortal realm, whether they devoured weak mortals or were slaughtered by cultivators burning with hatred, negative emotions would inevitably be spawned.

And if there were enough bugs—and there were certainly plenty, bred in isolation for thousands of years, with the insect mother constantly laying eggs and her generations of offspring continually birthing, devouring one another's corpses, and breeding fresh broods like a perpetual-motion non-scientific species—a never-ending stream squeezing through would amplify those negative emotions. Sooner or later, a localized zone of demonic contamination would form.

When that happened, the Demon Eye would tear open even wider, and the demon soldiers could cross over.

Picturing this glorious future, both demon soldiers drooled even more copiously.

*

The Brown-Winged Fei Mother was fully aware that other demons treated the insect swarms as expendable cannon fodder.

She didn't care in the slightest.

In fact, she did the exact same thing herself.

[...The aura of the 'vanguard' has vanished? Were they slain? Or did they fly farther afield?]

The Brown-Winged Fei Mother monitored the faint auras outside, lost in thought.

Yet she was not surprised that her first wave of "offspring" had failed to return.

Demon insects adhered to no rules whatsoever. If there were no formidable foes outside, they would abandon the hive without hesitation, flying far and wide to wreak havoc across the land. If powerful enemies awaited, they would naturally all be wiped out, leaving none to deliver word. The sole scenario where one might return was if the enemy outside was not overwhelmingly strong but sufficient to slaughter them, allowing one survivor to flee back for reinforcements—only for that weakling to be promptly devoured by its own kin.

With the first probe yielding no return, the insect mother dispatched a second wave.

This batch was slightly larger in build and far greater in number, totaling a full hundred bugs.

—"You lot, advance!"-the Brown-Winged Fei Mother commanded, watching her offspring swarm toward the Demon Eye.

[My offspring, go and devour your enemies!]

[If you encounter those you cannot eat, corrupt them with demonic qi and demonize them!]

[Use your corpses to forge a brand-new demonic domain!]

The Brown-Winged Fei Mother envisioned with immense anticipation.

*

On the other side, the moment the Fei bugs squeezed out of the Demon Eye, they were greeted by a baptism of roaring flames.

Yet they were demon beasts after all, constantly slaughtering one another in their spare time; among them were mutants endowed with fire attributes, meaning flames were hardly intolerable.

Thus, aside from a handful of useless weaklings that were incinerated on the spot, the majority surged forward.

Next, they were pelted by a volley of water bombs.

The freezing liquid drenched the newly scorched insects from head to toe, causing several more frail-winged weaklings to tumble helplessly down.

Then came ice arrows, wind spheres, lightning strikes...

By the time the Fei bugs rounded the fourth corner, only a single insect remained—the strongest of them all.

With hundreds of millions of offspring, the Fei Mother naturally couldn't bother naming each child. Consequently, Fei bugs usually named themselves; if they managed to survive internal infighting for a few months, their chosen moniker would be recognized by their peers. Of course, there were rather unfortunate cases: if an insect's mutation was too conspicuous—such as growing a shiny golden rear end—it mattered not what grand name it chose or how powerful it became; all the other bugs would invariably brand it "Gold Butt," or at most "King Gold Butt" once it grew strong.

This particular Fei bug, however, was quite fortunate. Void of bizarre deformities, it had instead grown a pair of majestic, imposing golden antennae, prompting it to name itself "King Golden Whiskers."

King Golden Whiskers flew alone down the long, winding corridor.

It felt not a shred of sentiment over the demise of its comrades. Among Fei bugs, failing in battle merely proved one's weakness; even if enemies didn't kill them, kin would devour them. Only itself was exceptional, of course.

King Golden Whiskers firmly believed that it alone would attain ultimate triumph.

And indeed, when it finally reached the end of the corridor, an intense fragrance suddenly struck its senses:

It was the aroma of vexation.

And the [four-legged white meat bug](<# "Literal CN: 四足白色肉虫子 — how the insect demon perceives a human">) emitting this intoxicating fragrance was glaring straight at it:

[—"Aaaagh! Why did one leak past?!"](<# "Speaker: Qingyang Yang">)

Although King Golden Whiskers had received no ancestral memories and did not know these meat bugs were called "humans," it instinctively recognized it as an ultra-delicacy.

It lunged straight toward the "white meat bug" without a second thought.

Yet the "meat bug" showed no hint of terror, merely lifting a "foreleg":

A gale of biting, frigid wind instantly engulfed King Golden Whiskers, bristling with five or six thick icicles that skewered it into a hedgehog in a flash. Yet King Golden Whiskers' body detonated at that very instant, billowing a torrential wave of demonic qi straight toward the "meat bug."

King Golden Whiskers knew that as long as the demonic qi contaminated this "meat bug," it would sooner or later resurrect within the creature's soul.

And the "vexation" the "meat bug" felt at this exact moment was the prime parasite medium!

Yet before the demonic qi could truly seep in, an overwhelming wave of joy completely drowned out that fleeting vexation, causing the demonic cloud to plunge into nothingness and linger aimlessly in midair.

If it failed to locate a fresh host of negative emotion within one shichen, it would dissipate into oblivion.

[Why?!]

The final remnant of King Golden Whiskers' consciousness cried in despair.

*

—"Hey, this is pretty fun!"-the four-legged white meat bug... no, Qingyang Yang, declared gleefully.

Waving his fan with a brisk rustle, he turned excitedly toward Qingyang Qingyu:
—"Though these bugs are way too weak,"-he said.-"Auntie, you can send something stronger to test me!"

Hearing this, Qingyu raised an eyebrow slightly.

Yet before Qingyang Qingyu could reply, the other disciples of Eternal Villa who had accompanied Qingyang Yang couldn't wait any longer.

[—"Come off it! You clearly leaked a bug just now!"](<# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple">)

[—"Next round is mine!"](<# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple">)

[—"Yeah, those are demon bugs! Yang-yang, you might have been contaminated by demonic qi, so vacate your seat already!"](<# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple">)

Watching this crowd clamoring in unison in an attempt to "usurp his throne," Qingyang Yang instantly flared up in indignation:
—"Scram! A single round gives at least three to five chances of failure! Besides, do you think I get contaminated that easily? Even if there was contamination, running a single cycle of the White Jade Immortal Technique wipes it completely clean!"-he snapped.

The White Jade Immortal Technique was one of Eternal Villa's core inheritance arts.

Having originated from a True Immortal, it possessed a natural purifying effect against demonic qi.

Take the demonic qi from King Golden Whiskers just now: even if it had truly contaminated Qingyang Yang's soul through that moment of vexation, running a single cycle of his technique would have purged it entirely, making him practically a [human air purifier](<# "Literal CN: 人肉净化器 (rénròu jìnghuàqì) — humorous metaphor for someone whose body passively cleanses toxins or demonic corruption">).

In this regard, Eternal Villa was indeed the natural nemesis of demons.

—However, during a massive demonic invasion when demonic qi spread on a catastrophic scale, even Eternal Villa might fail to hold the line.

For Foundation Establishment cultivators like Qingyang Yang, dealing with three to five low-grade demon bugs a day was already his upper limit.

Fortunately, this defense system had never intended to place its hopes on fellows like Qingyang Yang in the first place.

Qingyang Qingyu paid no heed to the squabbling of her clan's juniors.

Sitting in a meditative posture beneath a pillar of spirit stone, she attuned her senses to the surrounding spiritual energy.

Qingyang Qingyu possessed rare Triple Mutant Spirit Roots: Wind, Lightning, and Ice. Most miraculous of all, these three roots were identical in both "length and breadth," creating zero mutual interference that might stall her cultivation progress.

Such aptitude was already one in ten thousand, let alone being born into Eternal Villa—the inherent "drawback" of rare elemental spiritual energies being difficult to locate and requiring extended time to reach maximum capacity simply didn't exist for her.

Consequently, from the moment she first touched cultivation, Qingyang Qingyu had found meditation to be a thoroughly delightful affair.

She could sense the presence of spiritual energy with effortless ease, watching them, playing with them, drawing them into her body to convert into portions of her strength, and dispatching them farther afield to attract even more spiritual energy to coalesce and bolster her might anew.

Within such a virtuous cycle, Qingyang Qingyu could never fathom why anyone would ever find cultivation arduous.

[This is clearly the most interesting thing in the entire world! It's ten thousand times more fun than boring marriage and family!]

Moreover, through this exchange study, Qingyang Qingyu had learned a novel way to play with spiritual energy... ahem, cultivate, from the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

First, identify a living entity capable of generating massive spiritual energy while possessing a baseline backend (computing) capability to act as a Central Processing Unit (CPU)—whether a cultivator or some other spiritual being.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable's advice was that spiritual flora served best.

After all, cultivators and spirit beasts moved about; if they strayed too far, a "network disconnection" would occur, which was detrimental to system users. Furthermore, "animals" possessed temperamental dispositions that could destabilize the execution of specific spells (programs), easily triggering bugs.

By comparison, spiritual plants generated the most abundant spiritual energy. Although their computing (thinking) capacity was relatively weak, in the absence of spiritual sentience (and even if sentience arose, plant emotions were generally sluggish), the spells (programs) stored within them were far more stable. Unless literally chewed away by insect bugs, few problems would ever arise.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's internal cultivation system seemed to be anchored upon the ancient peach tree guarding the mountain gate.

The Qianxing Secret Realm system, meanwhile, utilized the carpet of Snow Glistening Grass blanketing the mountains as its Central Processing Unit—though the Untiring Immortal Venerable did not recommend this. He had merely tested it as an experiment back then; the computing throughput of Snow Glistening Grass was far too minuscule, though fortunately compensated by sheer numbers, forcing him to establish a miniature "local network" to conduct distributed cloud computing. Ah, that belonged to advanced coursework.

As for Qingyu's very first hands-on assignment, her homework was relatively straightforward: utilizing the Spirit Stone Palace itself.

Spirit stones held colossal spiritual energy and possessed formidable innate storage capacity, making them an ideal storage medium for spells (programs). The only minor drawback was that this medium lacked computing (thinking) capabilities. However, Eternal Villa's mineral vein boasted a history spanning tens of thousands of years; while resisting demonic qi had prevented it from birthing a mineral spirit like Mo Xi's, it possessed abundant spirit nodes. With minor reorganization, it was fully operational.

In the Untiring Immortal Venerable's words, it was more than sufficient to construct a simple [Tower Defense System](<# "Literal CN: 塔防系统 (tǎfáng xìtǒng) — modern gaming genre adapted into sect defense architecture">).

Indeed—simple.

Although Qingyu felt slightly aggrieved, the Spirit Stone Palace's tower defense system was nowhere near as complex as Qianxing Secret Realm, let alone Mount Tianling's comprehensive cultivation system that coordinated with all disciples.

Still, it was ideal for a novice.

Once Qingyu actually rolled up her sleeves, however, she discovered that calibrating the design was remarkably difficult.

First, making players feel it was "fun" was a daunting challenge; second was calculating the volume of demonic qi pouring through the corridor, allocating sufficient spells to eliminate them while reserving a comfortable safety margin.

Take just now: after only four waves of bugs, a flaw had surfaced.

In truth, Qingyang Yang's operation of the attack towers had been nearly flawless; at the very least, when Qingyu ran tests herself, she hadn't thought of using elemental counters to exterminate demonic beasts. Qingyu had simply misjudged the spellcasting capacity of Foundation Establishment cultivators, resulting in a single bug slipping into the Command Tower despite his exceptional performance.

Yet during a [closed beta](<# "Literal CN: 内测 (nèicè) — closed beta testing">), was such a bug truly a problem?

Qingyang Yang and the others had experienced far too little, evidently failing to understand the sacred principle of [compensation ten-pull, nothing happened](<# "Literal CN: 补偿十连，无事发生 — famous gacha gaming meme where developers give players 10 free gacha pulls as apology compensation for game-breaking bugs, acting as if nothing happened">)! Besides, far too few monsters were crawling out of the Demon Eye right now; closed beta access was an exceedingly rare privilege.

While Qingyu was measuring the residual demonic qi in the corridor and fine-tuning the towers' attack output, Qingyang Yang and the others nearly broke into a fistfight.

Qingyang Yang clutched the bronze mirror overseeing the entire corridor, refusing to let go.

—At that moment, the bronze mirror displayed a one-star rating alongside an unlocked new attack tower.

—"I won that round just now! I'm continuing!"-Qingyang Yang shouted.

[—"What kind of reward is a measly one-star rating?! We have to at least restart and retry!"](<# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple">)

—"The demons haven't even split into multiple lanes yet! It's still in the closed beta phase, how do you expect to restart?! You might as well let me clear all the stages in one go!"-Qingyang Yang retorted.

[—"You're already [leaking monsters](<# "Literal CN: 漏怪 (lòuguài) — tower defense gaming term for letting enemies slip past defenses">) by what, round four? Out of the way, let me do it!"](<# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple">)

The group was squabbling in a noisy brawl when a voice suddenly boomed:

—"What are you all doing?!"

[—"Fifth Uncle..."](<# "Speakers: Eternal Villa Disciples">)

Seeing their elder arrive, the juniors instantly became as meek and well-behaved as quail.

Hearing the voice, Qingyu opened her eyes as well, looking toward the newcomer:
—"Fifth Cousin,"-she greeted.

The newcomer was none other than the Fifth Master of Eternal Villa, Qingyang Qingli.

He shared the "Qing" generational name with Qingyu. However, in such immortal cultivation clans, sharing the same generational character while having astronomical age gaps was completely normal. Qingyang Qingli was currently a Soul Formation ancestor; in Qingyu's eyes, he belonged squarely to the ancestor generation, far from intimate.

Furthermore, Qingyang Qingli had previously served as the villa's representative at the Immortals' Bazaar and clinched first place in the Immortals' Chess tournament, becoming the "Soul Formation Representative." Afterwards, having concealed crucial information, he was soundly thrashed by relatives inside and outside the villa, spending the subsequent months "nursing injuries" and only daring to show his face when the Immortal Gathering tournament reopened. Since then, he had been gallivanting around flaunting the airs of a "First Champion Senior," making another sound thrashing seem practically inevitable.

Qingyu had no idea what this jinxed fifth cousin had come here for.

Qingyang Qingli paid no heed to Qingyu's skeptical gaze. After barking at the juniors to stand down, he cast a seemingly nonchalant glance at the bronze mirror before turning his head:
—"Qingyu, you seem to have studied rather well at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, eh?"-he asked.

—"I have merely scratched the surface,"-Qingyu replied.-"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable possesses boundless divine arts. What I have learned is nothing more than fur and skin; to truly master it will require far more study."

—"Mm, you are willing to study more of the art of weaving 'secret realm systems'? Truly rare,"-Qingli remarked, sounding somewhat surprised.

Noticing her cousin's expression, Qingyu sensed there was more beneath his words:
—"Is it strange that I am studying this?"-she asked.

Qingli's expression grew increasingly eccentric, yet he did not explain immediately. Instead, he pulled a slender "booklet" from his robes.

—"This is the new cultivation journal issued by the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Qingli explained.-"The trial edition is a monthly periodical that compiles academic papers from cultivators who engaged in the scholarly debates at the Tianling Immortal Gathering. Our Ancestor is featured in the very first issue!"

—"I know,"-Qingyu said, accepting the journal.

She had already heard of this while staying at the Heavenly Spirit Sect; if anything, she understood the entire compilation process even more intimately than her fifth cousin, even knowing that Ancestor's thesis had likely been formatted and edited by that Qu Qianxing.

Yet so what?

Qu Qianxing was the Untiring Immortal Venerable's direct disciple and had encountered academic papers far earlier than she had; it was only natural that he could manage such feats.

Qingyu wasn't about to panic over that. She firmly believed that if she continued studying, she would achieve the same sooner or later.

Yet what was Fifth Cousin's purpose in specifically bringing it over?

Puzzled, Qingyu muttered:
—"I was just about to have someone fetch a copy to see what Ancestor wrote..."

She froze.

Her eyes went wide.

—"True Immortal's Blood?!"-Qingyu cried out in utter shock.-"Why is Ancestor researching *this*?!"

One had to understand: the entire reason Qingyu had suddenly contemplated marrying into Sky City was purely for True Immortal connections! How on earth was Ancestor's project already centering on this very subject?!

—"You didn't know? Master Untiring provided the materials,"-Qingli said.-"Actually, Ancestor's side isn't in any rush; you should be able to join whenever you wish. But did you really never ask Master Untiring? Since he was able to obtain that drop of blood, he surely possesses relevant information, and perhaps even related heritages... I thought you knew all along, which was why I wondered why on earth you chose systems."

Hearing him say this, Qingyang Yang and the other disciples shrank into a dead blind spot, desperately pretending they didn't exist.

For the moment, Qingyu had no leisure to care about these juniors, her face alternating between pale and green as she stared at the journal's contents.

She remembered!

Back then, when the Untiring Immortal Venerable had brought out that first diagnostic test paper, he had seemed to harbor different plans! Yet because she chose the Secret Realm Guidance System, Master Untiring switched to something else!

[Just what on earth did I miss out on?!]

Leaping to her feet, Qingyu moved hurriedly to leave the Spirit Stone Palace:
—"I'm heading back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect for a moment..."-she blurted out.

—"Wait, the Demon Eye here hasn't been resolved yet, has it?"-Qingyang Qingli reminded her with a show of 'concern.'

Qingyu knew she couldn't leave it completely unattended either. Groping her person, she pulled out the secret realm's jade lock and tossed it to Qingli:
—"Fifth Cousin, look after it for me. I'll be right back!"-she called out.

So saying, she hurried away.

And Qingyang Qingli, watching his little cousin's departing back with a smirk, carried the jade lock over to the bronze mirror the juniors had just been fighting over. Then, under the watchful, horrified eyes of Qingyang Yang and the rest, he took the score Qingyang Yang had accumulated over the previous rounds and—

[Wiped it clean to zero](<# "Literal CN: 清零 (qīnglíng) — reset to zero / wiping save data and high scores; in gamer culture, deleting someone's save file and wiping their high scores is the ultimate unforgivable crime">)!!!!!

Ignoring his nephews' furious glares, Qingyang Qingli waved a hand at them dismissively:
—"Alright, this place belongs to me now."
