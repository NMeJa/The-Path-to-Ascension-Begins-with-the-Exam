# Chapter 78: Contest (第78章 较量)

[← Previous Chapter](077_Chapter_077.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](079_Chapter_079.md)

---

[Could this young lady be the key to my ascension?!]

Jing Chenjun thought in racing disbelief, gripping the imperial jade seal tightly in his hand.

Looking toward Eleventh Lady Mei once more, the corners of his mouth couldn't help but curl upward:

[Interesting.]

Jing Chenjun decided that Eleventh Lady Mei had thoroughly caught his eye, though he had no intention of recklessly making contact with her just yet. Instead, he planned to observe her from the shadows for a while.

Naturally, if Eleventh Lady Mei were bullied or harassed again during his observations, he wouldn't mind [playing the hero saving the beauty](<# "Literal CN: hero saving the beauty (英雄救美) — classic romantic rescue trope">).

—Or so Jing Chenjun planned, in all his [domineering, arrogant swagger](<# "Literal CN: kuang-ba-zhuai (狂霸拽) — domineering, tyrannical, and swaggering CEO trope">) *(delusion)*.

Yet very quickly, Jing Chenjun discovered that Eleventh Lady Mei had absolutely no need for his assistance.

None whatsoever!

That day's incident seemed to have flipped some sort of switch inside Eleventh Lady Mei.

The very next day after the incident, Fourteenth Lady Mei—Eleventh Lady's half-sister and one of the regular lackeys who had shadowed the 'Grand-Aunt' in harassing Eleventh Lady—ran in tears to the instructors, wailing that she had been beaten by Eleventh Lady Mei.

She had no proof.

The school's monitoring arrays contained zero recordings of it either.

Not a single instructor, including Jing Chenjun, believed a word she said, assuming these girls were merely stirring up endless [petty drama](<# "Literal CN: yao'ezi (幺蛾子) — eccentric antics or trouble">).

Yet Jing Chenjun soon noticed that Fourteenth Lady genuinely seemed terrified of Eleventh Lady from that day forward. Not only did she stop joining in the "Grand-Aunt's" jeering, but she even took detours to avoid Eleventh Lady whenever they crossed paths.

And it wasn't just Fourteenth Lady. As time progressed, more and more girls began acting just like her.

Jing Chenjun grew faintly suspicious, but subconsciously dismissed the thought that it had anything to do with Eleventh Lady. After all, by any measure, the 'anti-study faction' had overwhelming numbers on their side. Even if one of them suffered a minor setback during a spat with Eleventh Lady, wouldn't they gang up and retaliate later? Besides, some of them even had maidservants at their beck and call!

—"Is that so?"-Instructor Miao Dong from Blood Demon Hall said upon hearing Jing Chenjun's remarks. (Truth be told, Blood Demon Hall placed immense importance on this collaborative venture, going so far as to dispatch Golden Core cultivators to serve as instructors—a fact that made Jing Chenjun's heart flutter with quiet intimidation.) Miao Dong spoke of Eleventh Lady with high praise:—"Eleventh Mei has been remarkably earnest in my class lately! Moreover, for a mortal, her bone structure and physical constitution are exceptionally fine. While starting physical training now is a bit late, a few years of tempering will be more than enough for self-defense."

—"Eleventh Lady Mei is earnest in every single subject,"-Jing Chenjun remarked casually.

He hadn't paid the slightest heed to what a Golden Core cultivator actually meant by "exceptionally fine bone structure" and "enough for self-defense."

Until one day, Jing Chenjun happened to wander into the coral garden behind the dormitory.

This coral garden was purely ornamental landscaping meant for students to relax and stroll in after class. To an instructor like Jing Chenjun, it was merely an illusion projection; besides, every school's garden looked virtually identical. As long as the school's monitoring arrays exhibited no anomalies, Jing Chenjun generally never set foot there.

That day, Jing Chenjun had been pondering the test paper for the upcoming first pop quiz when he absentmindedly wandered into the area.

Seeing two figures embraced tightly in the distance, he snapped out of his reverie. Thinking ['see no impropriety'](<# "Literal CN: fei li wu shi (非礼勿视) — Confucian adage from the Analects: see no evil / look not at what is improper">), he turned to quietly slip away...

Wait, no!

[Within the school grounds, romance is strictly prohibited, just like fighting!]

Not to mention physical contact below the neck!

Thinking this, Jing Chenjun crept closer on tiptoe, determined to catch them red-handed.

Creeping closer, however, a familiar voice drifted over in a low murmur:

—"...You wouldn't want your family to find out you've taken a fancy to someone else, would you?"

[Whoa, not only are they dating, but someone's actively poaching a partner?!]

Wait a second, this voice was... Eleventh Lady Mei?!

Jing Chenjun's eyes widened in shock. Peering behind the coral tree, he saw that the person pinning someone against the rockery was indeed the slender Eleventh Lady Mei.

And pinned beneath Eleventh Lady was a girl slightly taller and sturdier than her.

Jing Chenjun remembered that her name was Lou Qiniang—to tell the truth, the names of the girls in these schools were all jumbles of birth-order numbers, leaving Jing Chenjun perpetually struggling to remember his students' names. She seemed to be the child of ordinary Longmen Town folk, not belonging to the same social circle as young mistresses like Eleventh Lady Mei (though from Jing Chenjun's royal perspective, they were all equally rustic country girls).

Though the girl was far taller and broader than Eleventh Lady, both of her hands were pinned flat against the artificial rock by Eleventh Lady, utterly unable to budge.

—"You're non-nonsense! Young Master Zhao and I are completely innocent!"-the girl stammered, struggling stubbornly.

—"Oh? Did I ever mention Little Zhao the Second?"-Eleventh Lady Mei asked innocently.

Lou Qiniang's face instantly flushed crimson.

—"Not that I ever thought you and Little Zhao the Second could amount to anything anyway,"-Eleventh Lady Mei beamed, staring down at Lou Qiniang with a faint smile.-"Little Zhao the Second is his entire family's darling treasure! I heard his parents liquidated their family fortune just to enroll him in the Shengmo Sect as a secular disciple rather than sending him to this school. How could his family ever permit him to marry you? Why, even my father wants to arrange a marriage alliance between him and Fourteenth Lady!"

This time, not only did Lou Qiniang's cheeks burn red, but her eyes welled up with crimson tears as well.

She seemed about to speak, but her shoulders slumped and she fell silent.

—"What a pity,"-Eleventh Lady let out a soft, melancholic sigh.-"I was actually quite optimistic about you."

Lou Qiniang offered no reply.

—"You're Lou Wu's niece, aren't you? I heard that because he struck it rich at the Immortals' Bazaar, our town caught the Heavenly Spirit Sect's eye in the first place,"-Eleventh Lady continued, completely unbothered by the other girl's miserable expression.-"I thought that just like your uncle, you might earn the Heavenly Spirit Sect's esteem. Ah, come to think of it, Instructor Jing praised you during the very first class, didn't he? And in the end... scoff!"

—"G-Girls can't do business,"-Lou Qiniang muttered weakly.

—"Is that so? Yet the Immortals' Bazaar places no restrictions on women! Instructor Miao from the Physical Conditioning class is a woman too,"-Eleventh Lady countered.-"Besides, what kind of notable figure was Lou Wu in town originally? He didn't even have the qualifications to step inside my family's courtyard! Wasn't it only by [latching onto the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grand thigh](<# "Literal CN: hug the thigh (抱大腿) — cling to a powerful patron for support">) that he became so cocky? Even now, after he dragged our entire town into such a mess, nobody dares to truly cause him trouble! They still have to flatter and pamper him!"

Hearing this, Lou Qiniang's expression flickered with conflicted emotions.

As Lou Wu's niece, she was the one who could feel most acutely the dramatic shift in her unreliable uncle's status throughout the town.

Take this school incident, for instance: at home, her parents wept and cursed Lou Wu for bringing ruin upon the family, yet to his face, neither dared utter a single cross word.

—"Even though the townsfolk say the Heavenly Spirit Sect is wicked, it is still a genuine immortal sect!"-Eleventh Lady pressed on.-"If you truly possess the ability to secure a post at the Immortals' Bazaar, everyone will only envy you. And don't you think that holding an internship qualification for the Immortals' Bazaar would make you fully worthy of matching a secular disciple from the Shengmo Sect?"

—"B-But my parents..."-Lou Qiniang's objection was already fading into a whisper.

—"They don't want you marrying Little Zhao the Second anyway. If they found out you secretly gifted him an embroidered pouch, they would only curse you for ruining the family reputation! And then they'd marry you off as quickly as possible to some old cripple in town to squeeze out a hefty bride price to pay for your younger brother's betrothal gifts."

The grim future Eleventh Lady painted drained every drop of color from Lou Qiniang's face.

—"With things as they are, I truly don't understand why you're being so obedient,"-Eleventh Lady said.-"In any case, your parents have no authority over you here at school, nor can they drag you back home. Why not study with everything you have? Perhaps you might earn the Heavenly Spirit Sect's favor. Instead, why run around after the 'Grand-Aunt' and her lot? Surely you don't believe that by flattering my petty younger sister, she'd kindly lift you up into the Zhao household as a concubine?"

—"Of course not!"-Lou Qiniang's face flushed once more, this time with the shame of having her secret thoughts laid bare. But having been thoroughly swayed by Eleventh Lady, she didn't argue further, offering only a strained excuse:—"I was only... only worried my cousin would tattle to my parents about what I do in school."

—"Your cousin... isn't she Third Lady Zhao's maidservant? The one who constantly receives praise in the Six Arts of Music class?"

—"Y-Yes, that's her."

—"She's a fool as well. Knowing full well she's most likely to be married off as a dowry concubine alongside Third Lady Zhao, she still shows off such brilliant musical talent—does she really think Third Lady Zhao won't get jealous? If only she put that effort into Mathematics instead!"-Eleventh Lady sighed.

Lou Qiniang nodded in fervent agreement.

By now, Eleventh Lady was no longer pinning Lou Qiniang down. Instead, she took Lou Qiniang's hand in hers and said:—"Next time, arrange for her to meet us, alright? I'll speak with her in private."

—"B-But..."

—"This is for your cousin's own good,"-Eleventh Lady said solemnly.

Lou Qiniang gave a dazed nod.

Having reached a consensus *(or something like it)*, the two young women finally emerged from behind the coral tree, bumping squarely into a dumbfounded Jing Chenjun.

Lou Qiniang's face registered pure panic, but Eleventh Lady merely smiled faintly, betraying not the slightest awkwardness of being caught in the middle of a "misdeed."

With the exact same polite reverence she showed whenever passing Jing Chenjun in the corridors, she greeted:—"Instructor Jing."

—"W-What are you doing?"-Jing Chenjun managed to squeeze out with difficulty.

—"Exhorting a classmate to study,"-Eleventh Lady replied.

Her unshakably resolute tone stripped Jing Chenjun of the ability to speak once more.

Yet Eleventh Lady Mei had no intention of offering further explanations to Jing Chenjun; she simply turned and strolled away.

Lou Qiniang glanced nervously at Jing Chenjun, then at Eleventh Lady Mei's departing back, before hurriedly scurrying after Eleventh Lady.

From that day onward, Lou Qiniang became Eleventh Lady's little follower. Soon afterward, several other girls joined their ranks, gradually forming an opposing faction confronting the 'Grand-Aunt.'

Just as Jing Chenjun was worrying that a clash between the two sides would further degrade the school's academic climate, the issue was resolved far faster—and with far more crude, violent simplicity—than he could have ever imagined.

The school held its monthly examination.

Lacking prior testing experience, the instructor from Blood Demon Hall seemed to harbor some profound misunderstanding regarding examinations. Consequently, the test for Physical Conditioning took the form of direct, one-on-one combat between students.

And Eleventh Lady Mei dragged the 'Grand-Aunt' straight onto the sparring arena.

This 'Grand-Aunt' was not actually strong.

Having been dispatched as an undercover mole by Carefree Palace, she naturally possessed no immortal roots. Previously, she had relied entirely on magical artifacts supplied by Carefree Palace to stir up trouble, but inside the school, all such artifacts were strictly confiscated and banned.

In terms of physical fitness, she had practiced dance for a time, so originally she was indeed stronger than Eleventh Lady Mei, who had rarely stepped outside her boudoir.

Yet she hadn't continued her physical training within the school.

First, the Modified Blood Demon Manual taught in the Physical Conditioning class sounded bizarre and ominous, making her worry she might fall into a trap. Second, Blood Demon Hall was an orthodox body-refinement sect whose sole objective was elevating raw combat prowess, which fundamentally diverged from Carefree Palace's training regimen for female disciples; the 'Grand-Aunt' feared that practicing this art would ruin her feminine charm and destroy her core 'value.'

Eleventh Lady Mei, on the other hand, harbored no such reservations—she wished for nothing more than to grow stronger!

And stronger she had indeed become.

This delicate young mistress who originally grew dizzy from standing in the sun too long had, after two months of training at the school, grabbed the 'Grand-Aunt' by the collar and unleashed [fifteen crisp, resounding slaps](<# "Literal CN: pa-pa-pa-pa-pa (啪啪啪啪啪) — onomatopoeia for a rapid succession of slaps">) across her cheeks, knocking out several teeth. In the end, it was the spectating instructors who realized something was amiss and stepped in to halt the one-sided beatdown.

Instructor Miao pouted in aggrieved innocence:—"What's a few knocked-out teeth? Back in our Blood Demon Hall, having one's intestines beaten out isn't uncommon at all."

[...Is your Blood Demon Hall truly an orthodox sect?]

Heavenly Spirit Sect trembled in silence.

Eleventh Lady Mei was subsequently punished with an afternoon of detention. Yet from that moment on, the 'Grand-Aunt's' clique collapsed like scattered sand, and every girl who had ever harassed Eleventh Lady took wide detours whenever they saw her coming.

Naturally, they no longer possessed the courage to badger students who wanted to study either.

And even if they did, there was nothing a good thrashing couldn't fix.

And if one thrashing didn't fix it, two would.

The academic atmosphere of Northwest Sixth Elementary was instantly revitalized and purified.

—"Tsk, I originally thought we'd have to teach them a lesson after the midterm exam. Who knew these children would resolve the problem themselves?"-an instructor remarked with approval.-"This Eleventh Lady Mei is quite the promising talent!"

—"Still, violence isn't good,"-Jing Chenjun said.-"Especially among young ladies." Whether it was because his dream of [saving the damsel in distress](<# "Literal CN: hero saving the beauty (英雄救美) — classic romantic rescue trope">) had fallen flat or due to other factors, he simply couldn't muster any enthusiasm for this outcome.

—"Violence isn't good?"-Miao Dong beamed at Jing Chenjun with a sweet smile.

Jing Chenjun shrank back into his chair.

What left Jing Chenjun utterly speechless was that not only did his colleagues disagree with his views, but even that jade seal appeared to disagree with him.

Back when Eleventh Lady Mei was "exhorting classmates to study," the jade seal had brightened noticeably. And once Northwest Sixth Elementary's academic discipline was fully "rectified," the seal glowed a full tier brighter. The first stroke of the indistinct intaglio inscription carved on the seal's base grew faintly clearer, revealing a downward left-falling slash—the left half of the character *'Ren'* (人, Person/Human).

This left Jing Chenjun thoroughly bewildered.

Because the ancestral imperial seal of his dynasty was inscribed with *"Having Received the Mandate of Heaven, May Longevity and Prosperity Endure"* (受命于天，既寿永昌), completely different from this one.

—"So then, what exactly makes you glow?"-Jing Chenjun asked the jade seal in a murmur.

He had clearly done nothing at all, merely bearing witness to Eleventh Lady Mei's rise into a schoolyard boss!

Unfortunately, the jade seal offered no reply.

Instead, another cluster of glowing light spheres drifted leisurely toward him.

[Could Eleventh Lady Mei be bullying someone else already?!]

Jing Chenjun thought in alarm. Lifting his head to trace the trajectory of the light orbs, he discovered they hadn't drifted from his own classroom at all, but rather from a classroom on the far side of the corridor.

Jing Chenjun walked over to investigate, finding several sect brothers gathered there, murmuring in animated conversation.

Jing Chenjun was well-liked and had grown well-acquainted with all his fellow disciples serving as part-time instructors over this period.

Seeing Jing Chenjun approach, the disciple in charge of this class waved excitedly:—"Jing Chenjun, come take a look at our school's Eleventh Lady's masterpiece!"

—"Eleventh Lady Mei? What has she done now?"-Jing Chenjun asked nervously.

—"Eleventh Lady Mei?"-his sect brother gave Jing Chenjun a bizarre look.-"No, it's Eleventh Lady Qiao!"

Only then did Jing Chenjun realize this wasn't the Eleventh Lady from his own school.

Though it made sense.

While from the vantage of Buyan Palace these classrooms stood side by side, separated at most by a corridor, in reality they were all remote projections. The actual physical locations of the various schools were scattered across several Abyssal Oceans beneath the Central Domain, separated by entire continental tectonic plates.

Jing Chenjun took a deliberate look at the plaque outside the classroom: Southeast Second Elementary. Indeed, it was separated from Northwest Sixth Elementary by the entire breadth of the Central Domain.

[Why on earth are they both named 'Eleventh Lady'?!]

[Can't these people put a shred of effort into naming their children?!]

[Even the names in Huang Xiao and Huang Xiaoxiao's household sound far more reliable than this!]

Jing Chenjun grumbled silently in his heart, casting an anxious glance toward this 'Eleventh Lady Qiao.'

Though both were called Eleventh Lady, they were blatantly two completely different girls.

Eleventh Lady Mei had left a rather good first impression on Jing Chenjun because she was a delicate, graceful young lady who closely matched the refined aesthetic standards drilled into him since childhood—though naturally, the closer she matched them, the more jarring the contrast became, and Jing Chenjun had to admit he now harbored lingering psychological trauma.

This Eleventh Lady Qiao, meanwhile, possessed the features of an ordinary peasant girl: thick hands, sturdy feet, and a robust, muscular build that would give grown men a run for their money.

[Whoa, this Eleventh Lady looks like her punches would hurt like hell.]

Yet the glowing light spheres this time emanated directly from this unassuming Eleventh Lady Qiao. Moreover, they were far more numerous and dazzlingly bright, instantly illuminating the first stroke beneath the jade seal in its entirety!

[So what exactly is this jade seal's criteria for glowing?]

[Is it the name 'Eleventh Lady'? Or is it being better at beating up one's classmates?!]

—"Speaking of which, if she starts hitting people, wouldn't that count as school bullying?"-Jing Chenjun muttered under his breath.

Eleventh Lady Mei had honestly been skirting the border of bullying herself.

Fortunately, after rectifying the school's academic atmosphere, she never engaged in petty retaliation against those who had formerly bullied her.

*(Eleventh Lady Mei: I have books to study, where would I find the time to waste on them?)*

—"What?"-someone glanced at Jing Chenjun in bewilderment.-"What bullying?! Who dares bully our Eleventh Lady?!"

At those words, several students inside the classroom glared over indignantly, looking ready to fight anyone who dared lay a finger on this burly, hardworking girl.

Jing Chenjun finally realized things were not as he had imagined. He asked:—"Then what are you all doing?"

—"Commending the [fish feeder](<# "Literal CN: automated fish feeder (喂鱼器) — an aquaculture dispensing mechanism">) Eleventh Lady engineered, of course!"

—"Fish feeder?"-Jing Chenjun repeated dumbly.

At that moment, someone hoisted the model resting before Eleventh Lady Qiao high into the air, presenting it for all to see.

It was a remarkably intricate contraption. Jing Chenjun couldn't even make heads or tails of its blueprint, let alone craft one himself.

A cluster of disciples from the Artifact Refinement Hall had already gathered around, whispering animatedly in technical appraisal:

[—"It truly is exceptionally clever. It combs through the spiritual energy dispersed in seawater and condenses it."](<# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple">)

[—"The automated dispersal of spiritual energy into the water is seamless as well, achieving fully automated aquaculture."](<# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple">)

[—"Most importantly, mortals can operate it with ease!"](<# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple">)

Listening to their discussion, Jing Chenjun finally learned that beyond attending daily classes, this Eleventh Lady had also applied for a work-study post, taking charge of managing the school's vegetable plots. Every school provided meals, and to cultivators, feeding a handful of mortals was negligible; after all, for mortal crops or low-tier spirit herbs, a standard Wood-element spell could easily boost yields tenfold. In contrast, mundane chores like daily cleaning and replacing depleted spirit stones were tedious and repetitive, which was precisely why many sects recruited hordes of mortal-adjacent outer disciples.

Eleventh Lady Qiao belonged to the exceptionally conscientious sort.

Not only did she work to supplement her family's income, but feeling that the cafeteria fare was dreadfully monotonous, she set her sights on the wild fish shoals that swam past the school perimeter every day. Using leftover spiritual fluid to bait and gather the schools of fish, she began cultivating them. Later, feeling that manual maintenance devoured too much precious study time, she engineered a fully automated contraption.

Lacking any talent for naming, she simply dubbed it a 'fish feeder,' though in reality it integrated spiritual energy distribution, shoal attraction, and enclosed [fish farming](<# "Literal CN: yangyu (养鱼) — aquaculture; also modern slang for keeping a roster of admirers/suitors">) operations.

It was only because her aquaculture operation had grown so massive—reclaiming hundreds of acres of 'fish paddies' in the sea outside the school—that she finally caught the instructors' attention.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect had always actively encouraged practical innovation, so Eleventh Lady Qiao's invention naturally earned everyone's glowing praise.

Despite her cheerful and forthright demeanor, being suddenly surrounded by a throng of "immortals" left Eleventh Lady Qiao flustered and tongue-tied.

—"I-It wasn't entirely made by myself,"-Eleventh Lady Qiao stammered, twisting her hands.-"Th-There are similar mechanisms in the textbook. I merely followed the blueprints and made slight adjustments."

She was referring to the *Five Charioteering Arts* textbook.

This was an introductory textbook covering common machinery and magical artifacts.

Although Zhou Hui categorized these institutions as 'elementary schools,' the textbooks he authored were intensely pragmatic, modeled directly after practical handbooks from his past world like *Dual-Use Talents for Military and Civilian Service* from the early founding era.

After all, there was no luxury of time to let these children study at leisure.

That inefficient pedagogical model—nine years of basic education topped off with high school and university, where graduates still couldn't apply what they studied directly to practical production and had to relearn everything from scratch—could only exist in a peaceful modern society. To put it bluntly, extended schooling there was often merely a buffer to mitigate employment pressures.

While the cultivation world boasted endless millennia, that was a luxury experienced solely by immortal cultivators. Ordinary mortals still possessed brief, fleeting lifespans; peasant girls like Eleventh Lady Qiao were already at an age to be betrothed and bear children in their teens, making it impossible to let them advance at a leisurely pace.

These students had no immediate need for esoteric academic theory; what they needed above all was practical utility.

Thus, in the *Agronomy* and *Machinery* volumes of the Five Charioteering Arts, Zhou Hui explained almost zero theoretical principles—those would only come later for those who advanced further. From page one, it was all hands-on operation, teaching assembly, maintenance, and deployment straight away.

Yet this was precisely the distinct advantage of the cultivation realm:

The cost of hands-on experimentation here was dirt cheap.

In his past world, having novice students operate heavy industrial machinery right off the bat was bottlenecked by equipment shortages, not to mention severe safety hazards. But in the cultivation world, a single illusion array solved everything.

Eleventh Lady Qiao was naturally studious and possessed exceptional hands-on mechanical talent. Even though the instructors hadn't mandated studying this advanced section yet, she had previewed every single textbook before classes even began. She was particularly captivated by the machinery curriculum, where she discovered countless practical tools.

And today's 'fish feeder' was merely one of her experiments.

The reason Eleventh Lady Qiao didn't think it was any big deal was because the cultivation world already possessed devices that gathered spiritual energy. Their structure was simple enough to be assembled using standard scrap components provided by the school. Nobody had thought to do it before purely because mortals had zero access to such devices, while immortal cultivators had long since ceased consuming ordinary fish, leaving no one with reason to bother.

—"I feel this can be promoted across all schools,"-an Artifact Refinement disciple noted in satisfaction.

—"Indeed. Hasn't Junior Brother Mo been complaining that the elementary schools are a drain on the sect budget?"

—"With this, they can essentially achieve self-sufficiency."

Jing Chenjun listened numbly to their banter, still finding it utterly incomprehensible.

He had resided in the Heavenly Spirit Sect for quite some time and had even interned at the Artifact Refinement Hall, confirming that without a spirit root, he could never forge immortal treasures. Yet who could have imagined that this mortal peasant girl before him had engineered a mechanism capable of sustaining human livelihood without relying on immortal spellcasting—even if it was merely an adaptation.

Eleventh Lady Qiao was still dazed by the instructors' talk of rewards. Seizing the opportunity, she timidly inquired:—"Th-Then, Lord Immortals..."

—"Just call us 'Instructor' like everyone else,"-the Artifact Refinement disciples replied warmly, showing utmost courtesy to such a promising seedling.

—"Immortal Instructors, could the reward I ask for... be this sugarcane press?"-Eleventh Lady Qiao asked, flipping through her textbook.-"Y-You won't need to trouble yourselves to forge it, sirs! I can ask our village blacksmith to hammer out the parts... But with this, our village's sugar-making will be vastly more efficient."

—"Of course you may,"-the lead instructor for this class decided on the spot.-"Since the Immortal Venerable included it in the curriculum, it was meant for you to use in the first place. However, we must report this matter to the sect before we can officially transfer the blueprints and parts to you in a short while."

—"Thank you!"-Eleventh Lady Qiao beamed, her cheeks flushed with excitement.

Watching Eleventh Lady Qiao's radiant face, then gazing down at the luminescent jade seal, Jing Chenjun vaguely grasped something profound.

[Not enough. A sample size of two is still far too small.]

—"If we educate a few more students, perhaps a clearer conclusion will emerge,"-Jing Chenjun murmured to himself.

***

While everyone crowded around Eleventh Lady Qiao, nobody noticed that in a far corner of the classroom, a delicate, glamorous young girl's face had darkened with venomous envy:

[—"Nothing more than an unrefined village bumpkin."](<# "Speaker: Rou Niang">)

Like the 'Grand-Aunt,' she was an undercover sleeper agent sent by Carefree Palace, named Rou Niang.

Only Rou Niang's undercover espionage had proven even rougher and more miserable than the 'Grand-Aunt's.'

To begin with, the cultivation factions in Fengqing City had been purged for various transgressions, leaving them terrified of offending the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Naturally, not a single local dared pave the way or pull strings for Rou Niang, forcing her to infiltrate the school entirely on her own.

Furthermore, in Southeast Second Elementary, under the leadership of Qiao Family Village and especially with the presence of an academic god like Eleventh Lady Qiao, the students' enthusiasm for learning had been roaring hot from day one, leaving Rou Niang virtually zero opportunity to stir up trouble.

Rou Niang had originally planned to rely on acting coy, cute, and helpless to seduce and charm the instructors. Who could have foreseen that the instructors all taught via remote projection, rendering her physical charms virtually useless? Besides, no matter how pretty and coquettish she acted, could it possibly compare to high-scoring honor students who brought concrete benefits to the teachers?—The students' exam scores were directly factored into these Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples' own practical teaching evaluations!

Over here, anything Eleventh Lady Qiao did effortlessly commanded all the instructors' attention.

Rou Niang was left thoroughly helpless.

After classes ended that day, she slipped out of the school perimeter and found a secluded spot beyond the artifact-jamming barrier to contact Carefree Palace. Lately, however, something strange seemed to have happened within Carefree Palace, making communications extraordinarily difficult.

Only after countless attempts did the water mirror on the other end finally flicker with a response. For some bizarre reason, the operative on the other side had draped a thick veil over her face:

—"...Notice from Chief Steward of Carefree Palace: affairs in the Eastern Domain have grown overwhelming lately, so the palace has decided to adopt a shelf-and-hold strategy toward the Central Domain. We need only remain dormant. Even if we must abandon controlling the schools, do not provoke any incidents."

—"Is that you, A-Niao? Why does your voice sound so weird?"-Rou Niang asked in confusion.

—"None of your business,"-"A-Niao" muttered in a muffled, garbled slur, sounding like a toothless old crone whose mouth was leaking air.

Fortunately, Rou Niang held zero interest in the other girl's personal affairs, pressing only on the mission:—"What do you mean, 'do not provoke incidents'? My situation here is atrocious! There's a peasant girl... Her village has ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect that are far too tight, leaving me completely incapable of doing anything!"

—"Then do nothing,"-A-Niao replied through her leaky, missing teeth.-"Dormancy is the top priority. I heard that even the Carefree Pavilions across several immortal cities have suspended all market intervention! Much less us." *(So I didn't fail at all—I'm merely remaining dormant under orders!)*

—"Then are we supposed to just sit here like wooden statues? If we remain dormant for years, I fear headquarters will completely forget we exist,"-Rou Niang protested.

—"Then work hard on your own!"-A-Niao scoffed coldly.

She seemed about to say more, but a gust of wind swept past, lifting a corner of her veil.

Rou Niang's eyes flew wide. She thought she caught a glimpse of something horrific:—"A-Niao, why is your face..." *swollen like a pig's head?!*

—"It's nothing! If there's nothing else, that's all!"-A-Niao clamped her hands frantically over her veil and terminated the transmission.

Rou Niang stared blankly at the dark water mirror, uncertain whether her eyes had deceived her.

Though she didn't know what misfortune had befallen A-Niao, nor why Carefree Palace had suspended operations in the Central Domain, Rou Niang was utterly unwilling to give up just like that.

Gritting her teeth in ruthless determination, Rou Niang glared down at her storage pouch and reached inside to pull out...

A textbook on the *Five Charioteering Arts*.

Under these circumstances, she could only rely on raw 'academic prowess' to fight for her standing!
