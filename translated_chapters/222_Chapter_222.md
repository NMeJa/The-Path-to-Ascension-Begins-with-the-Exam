# Chapter 222: Insignificant Memories

What, in the end, was the meaning of a mortal's existence?

Jing Chenjun pondered.

His entire life seemed to have been spent contemplating this very question.

—When he first learned of the existence of immortal cultivators; when he learned that bearing the bloodline of the "Human Sovereign" meant he could never achieve immortality; when, refusing to believe in fate, he ventured abroad in search of the immortal path; when, rejected by every major immortal sect, he had no choice but to travel east across the Boundless Ocean seeking miraculous elixirs; when, his quest having failed, he found himself stranded upon the Boundless Ocean; when he was rescued by the Palace of Carefree Wandering; when he agreed to become Carefree Palace's proxy to contend for the imperial succession; when, having slain all his royal brothers, he sat upon the imperial throne and swallowed the "Elixir of Immortality"; when, bound by contract, he was forced to sell out national interests to immortal sects; when, unable to stomach sinking into total degeneracy by selling out his own people, he sought to defy Carefree Palace; when, having breached his contract with Carefree Palace, he was transformed into a grotesque half-human, half-ghost creature; when, trapped in that wretched state, he watched his own offspring reenact his own tragedy; when he believed the mortal world was nothing more than an endless cycle of misery; when the demonic hordes invaded, annihilating everything...

Yes. The demon invasion.

Jing Chenjun sat upon the marble palace steps, staring blankly at a world drenched in scarlet.

The sky was scarlet. Dense clouds resembled congealed pools of bruised blood, pressing down so heavily they seemed to brush the crests of the palace walls. Far in the distance, a jagged fissure was faintly visible across the firmament, from which endless swarms of demons poured forth.

The palace was scarlet. Tongues of fire danced across the tiled eaves. The magnificent halls—legend held they had been erected with the aid of immortals, destined never to crumble—were already more than half reduced to charred ruins. Scarlet flocculent filaments, indistinguishable whether ash or condensed demonic miasma, drifted everywhere.

The earth was scarlet. The blood of the slain, mingled with falling embers, saturated the soil. This was a merciless, one-sided slaughter, for there were only mortals here. Before the demon race, even the most valiant warriors possessed zero ability to fight back; in fact, they did not even require a demon's touch—merely brushing against the drifting airborne filaments caused them to dissolve into puddles of bloody gore.

If even the palace of the mortal realm's greatest empire, fortified with multiple layers of defensive arrays, was reduced to this, one could readily imagine the plight of the world outside.

Perhaps all mortals were already extinct.

After all, powerless mortals who resembled mere vermin—in a catastrophe of this magnitude, would they not be snuffed out in an instant, like ants drowning in molten iron poured into their nest?

What, then, of the immortal cultivators? What of those soaring immortal cities suspended high in the clouds?

Would they also perish in a flash like mortals? Were they struggling bitterly? Or were they fighting demons with effortless grace, only to gaze down at the smoldering ruins of mortal kingdoms after the war had ended and offer a casual sigh: *We arrived too late*?

—"Why... is it so unfair?"-Jing Chenjun murmured tonelessly.

Throughout his life, he had posed this question countless times—in anger, in grief, in exhaustion, in indignation, in helplessness, in despair, and now, in this hollow state of total void. Yet never once had he received an answer.

Furthermore, as though the Heavens deemed his predicament not yet wretched enough, a colossal shadow swept across the sky, completely blotting out the glare of the burning palace. Subconsciously, Jing Chenjun lifted his gaze.

And nearly slumped limp to the pavement.

It was... an army of the demon race, was it not?

Jing Chenjun could not be certain.

Dense swarms of demons completely obscured the scarlet firmament, resembling a colossal migrating flock of birds—yet even birds possessed far more order. "A wild revelry of dancing fiends" fell woefully short of describing so horrific a spectacle. Even Jing Chenjun, who believed he had witnessed every revolting thing under heaven, could not quell the terror surging within his chest.

—"No, this won't do... I cannot feel fear. Fear will only attract them..."-Jing Chenjun mumbled under his breath.

He hunched his shoulders tightly, desperately hoping to conceal himself. Alas, it was already too late. The moment he peered upward in terror, the monstrous fiends in the sky spotted him.

Instantly, a hair-raising din erupted across the firmament.

It sounded like a crowd roaring in laughter, yet also like a multitude wailing in despair; it sounded like wet chewing, the grinding of fangs, the gulping of saliva, and the gurgling rush of blood. Amidst this horrific cacophony, only a stray phrase or two emerged that Jing Chenjun could barely decipher:

[—"...Human?"](# "Speaker: Demon")

[—"Doesn't seem entirely so. Smells a bit expired."](# "Speaker: Demon")

[—"Who cares! I've been flying all this way and haven't eaten a single bite! Expired will do just fine!"](# "Speaker: Demon")

Even if he understood those words, they held zero significance.

The blot-out-the-sky demon horde had already plunged downward, surging like a tidal wave.

Yet, with the palace blazing and nearly every attendant slaughtered, there was obviously a reason Jing Chenjun was able to sit alone upon the steps completely unharmed until now.

The foremost demon never truly reached him. Mid-flight, it unleashed an agonized shriek, bursting into flames just like the burning palace. Simultaneously, a muffled groan slipped from Jing Chenjun's lips as a chunk of skin was savagely ripped from his body.

Looking closely, not a single patch of intact skin remained on Jing Chenjun's frame.

What made it truly horrifying was that this tier of resistance was utterly useless against the demon race—especially against an entire demonic vanguard! They cared nothing for the deaths of their kin. On the contrary, the copper-scented stench of fresh blood radiating from Jing Chenjun's stripped flesh only spurred the demons into a wild frenzy. Instantly, like a dense swarm of moths circling a candle flame in midsummer, they plunged in a suffocating black cloud, caring nothing if they were reduced to ash. Or rather, they were gambling—gambling that another demon would peel away that bizarre human's skin completely, stripping him of any capacity to resist.

Fortunately—or perhaps unfortunately—the Elixir of Immortality that Jing Chenjun had obtained from the Palace of Carefree Wandering in the Boundless Ocean chose this moment to take effect.

That elixir invariably took effect precisely when it shouldn't.

For instance, it had enabled Jing Chenjun to survive for over three hundred years—while to an immortal cultivator, three centuries was merely the span of a single closed-door meditation, to a mortal, it was an agonizingly interminable eternity. It had also allowed him to continue living as a half-human, half-ghost creature after tearing up his contract with Carefree Palace. And now, at this worst possible juncture, it sustained Jing Chenjun's ragdoll-like frame in the worst possible fashion.

As demons perished in flames, the skin across Jing Chenjun's body was torn away alive. Yet the moment the destruction neared a fatal threshold, fresh skin knitted with unnatural speed across his flesh, like patches stitched onto a battered cloth doll.

Alas, Jing Chenjun was no cloth doll. Beyond granting him biological immortality, the elixir bestowed zero additional faculties. He collapsed pitifully into the pool of his own blood, the excruciating agony nearly tearing his sanity to shreds:

[Why bother...?]

[What am I even holding on for? Just let them devour me.]

[I wonder if I'll still be 'alive' after being ground into minced meat?]

Jing Chenjun thought in disoriented delirium. He attempted to let the agony overwhelm his consciousness, knowing that only when the pain exceeded all human endurance could he pass out into sweet oblivion.

How bitterly ironic! For a being cursed with eternal life, a coma had become his greatest salvation.

At that moment, a voice rang out above his head:

[—"What is this...?"](# "Speaker: Second Demon Sovereign")

[A demon?! How could it get close to me?!]

Jing Chenjun jolted in horror.

Yet wracked by the relentless cycle of flayed skin, he lacked the strength to even register shock. Through eyes streaming with bloody tears, he could only hazily discern a demon standing before him.

A peerlessly exquisite demon.

He was like a star gleaming in the abyss, a blossom unfolding in the deep sea, pristine snow falling upon embers—so breathtakingly beautiful, yet so uncanny. Merely by standing there, the scarlet air, the blazing, crumbling palace, and the grotesque fiends all felt like mere ornaments and nourishment for this transcendent entity. It evoked the calamitous sense of a fatal beauty—as if, should it nourish such perfection, the annihilation of countless worlds would be a trifling price to pay.

Anyone blessed with eyes could tell that a creature so extraordinarily beautiful could never be an ordinary demon.

Jing Chenjun faintly heard other demons addressing him as "Demon Sovereign."

And because of this "Lord Demon Sovereign's" presence, the fiends ceased their reckless dives toward Jing Chenjun. The agonizing cycle of flayed skin and rapid regeneration finally ground to a halt.

Yet Jing Chenjun could breathe no sigh of relief, for the Demon Sovereign remained standing before him.

The fact that this figure could draw close without being immolated made it obvious that Jing Chenjun's power could not constrain him in the slightest. Which also meant... this entity might actually possess the power to kill him.

Realizing this, Jing Chenjun felt a faint, twisted flicker of anticipation.

Regrettably, the Demon Sovereign showed zero inclination to attack. He merely inquired:

—"Are you this generation's Emperor?"-the Demon Sovereign asked.

[Why should I answer a demon's questions?!]

Jing Chenjun wished to defy him. Alas, as a powerless mortal, whether facing demons or immortal cultivators, he commanded zero strength to resist.

Though he possessed neither the desire nor the strength to speak, Jing Chenjun heard his own throat rasp out words:

—"Sort of... I committed every filthy deed imaginable to seize this throne; it is only right that I pay the price."

—"Hmph,"-the Demon Sovereign snorted dismissively, though he inquired further:—"Yet the throne of the Human Sovereign and an Elixir of Immortality do not bestow magical arts. Where did your spellcraft come from? Not the immortality part—I mean the technique of sacrificing yourself to incinerate your enemies."

—"...Found in the imperial archives,"-Jing Chenjun did not hide it, feeling a faint sliver of pride:—"It was a technique employed over three hundred years ago by a minister of our dynasty, who refused to submit to demons! His notes were discovered afterward and preserved in the Hanlin Academy, where he served the longest..."

—"Ha! Hahahahaha!"-the Demon Sovereign burst into laughter hearing this:—"You cast him aside, yet you still recorded his deeds in your histories?! Hahahahahaha—"

[What is so funny about that?!]

Jing Chenjun thought indignantly, yet a sudden flicker of hesitation gripped him.

He recalled the margin annotations he had seen upon those notes. The commentary stated that the minister was peerlessly handsome, which was why he caught the eye of an evil demon that disguised itself as an immortal, demanding the ruling emperor surrender him. Unwilling to endure humiliation, the minister resolutely sacrificed himself in a conflagration that forced the demon to reveal its true form, allowing the world to learn the truth of the affair.

*(In truth, Carefree Palace was far too ashamed to let the cultivation world discover that their founding ancestor had been wounded by a mere 'mortal', and would rather be regarded as an evil demon.)*

This demon before him was also breathtakingly beautiful... Surely it couldn't be?!

The thought struck Jing Chenjun abruptly, but he dismissed it almost immediately:

That minister would rather die than become a demon's minion; how could he possibly have fallen to the demonic path?

Meanwhile, the Demon Sovereign asked with a mocking sneer:

—"So, Your Imperial Majesty, what do you hope to accomplish by employing the spellcraft of the minister you discarded? You wouldn't happen to be thinking of perishing alongside the demon race to save your country, would you?"

Hearing this, Jing Chenjun let out a wretched, hollow laugh:

—"With so many demons, how could I ever finish dying with all of you?"

—"I am merely indulging in delusions, committing an act of utter folly."

—"I thought, by sacrificing myself, sacrificing this broken husk, sacrificing this abominable bloodline, perhaps I could drag a fraction of you down to hell with me? Though this resistance is utterly insignificant, perhaps a handful of human mortals might escape death?"

—"So long as we are not completely wiped out, the race can always carry on. Perhaps that is the sole reason for a mortal's existence."

...

Jing Chenjun had bottled this up for far too long.

In truth, after falling out with Carefree Palace, he had ceased being Emperor. Imprisoned deep within the palace walls, there was no one left to hear him speak.

Presented with a rare chance before death, he poured out the thoughts he had harbored for over three centuries—his reflections on the value of life, and the reasons he stubbornly held his ground in this ruin.

Yet speech was merely speech; Jing Chenjun assumed his thoughts were the most inconsequential matters imaginable to a demon.

Sure enough, the peerless Demon Sovereign appeared to think exactly that. He listened to Jing Chenjun's speech with surprising patience, yet a mocking curl touched his lips.

—"Well done. Then keep persevering!"-the Demon Sovereign said.

He rose into the air, lightly flicking a hand.

In truth, even without his command, the demons that had cowered in fear of the Demon Sovereign surged forward once more. Jing Chenjun plunged right back into the skin-flaying purgatory.

Yet amidst the searing agony, Jing Chenjun heard a voice resonate:

[—"Since that is the case, keep enduring!"](# "Speaker: Second Demon Sovereign")

[—"Your persistence is not entirely without meaning. For every demon you detain, for every speck of substance you preserve, you preserve one more fragment of matter for this already destroyed timeline and world."](# "Speaker: Second Demon Sovereign")

[—"'That Person' will certainly resurrect everyone—provided you are still here."](# "Speaker: Second Demon Sovereign")

[—"Speaking of which, that spell was originally gifted by 'That Person' as well. Perhaps if you accumulate enough karmic affinity, in your next life you might reach his side and discover the answer you truly seek."](# "Speaker: Second Demon Sovereign")

[What... does that mean...?]

Jing Chenjun thought in a haze. But this time, rather than willing himself to pass out, he faced the agonizing pain head-on—

***

—"Aaaaaah—!"

From the second-year disciples' dormitory of the Heavenly Spirit Sect erupted a blood-curdling scream.

Half the residence hall was jolted awake in fright.

—"Wh-What happened?!"-Huang Xiao tumbled out of bed in sheer panic, but paying zero mind to his bruises, he scrambled up and looked nervously at his roommate:—"Jing Chenjun? Did you have a nightmare again?"

—"Probably..."-Jing Chenjun answered blankly.

At this moment, however, he possessed none of the weathered weariness from the dream; his unfocused eyes held only the [clear-eyed innocence and foolishness](# "Literal CN: 清澈和愚蠢 (qīngchè hé yúchǔn) — popular Chinese meme describing naive university students with a pure, uncorrupted, adorably clueless look") characteristic of low-tier disciples.

—"Just what kind of dream is it anyway?"-Cheng Ziyi grumbled through a massive yawn:—"Ever since the Immortal Arts Assembly began, I haven't had a single peaceful night's sleep."

—"..."-Jing Chenjun offered no reply. He sat dazedly upon his bed, straining with all his might to grasp the fleeting memories of the dream.

He felt it was an extraordinarily vital memory.

Alas, dream memories to a mortal were like rainwater cupped in two hands: the harder one clutched to retain it, the faster it slipped through the gaps between fingers. No matter how Jing Chenjun strained, he could not truly seize it.

After sitting frozen for quite some time, Jing Chenjun finally replied:

—"I don't remember."

His roommates were not the least bit surprised by this answer.

Jing Chenjun was far from the only one plagued by nightmares. Ever since the Immortal Arts Assembly began, a great many had begun dreaming. Particularly after the latest update to the trial system, practically every disciple had experienced forgotten nightmares to varying degrees, jolting awake amidst grief, wrath, and sorrow.

—"It shouldn't be an issue, right?"-Cheng Ziyi remarked:—"Buyan Palace posted an announcement stating that this trial system update simulates large-scale campaigns of the God-Demon War. To maintain authenticity, sensory cognitive manipulation via spiritual phantoms was deployed, which induces a certain degree of emotional aftereffects."

—"Still, I have nightmares too, but none as severe as Jing Chenjun's,"-Ji Gen noted:—"Besides, Jing Chenjun has been swamped with volunteer work lately; he hasn't even used the trial system, has he?"

—"You haven't used the trial system?!"-Cheng Ziyi cried out in disbelief.

—"...I test-ran it, and I've memorized the *Emergency Operating Manual for the Trial System*,"-Jing Chenjun stammered softly:—"Because as volunteers, when visitors encounter issues, we have to help resolve them."

—"That doesn't count as playing,"-Cheng Ziyi grumbled under his breath.

—"Then you've used it far less than the rest of us. Why is your reaction so intense?"-Ji Gen asked, genuinely puzzled.

—"Could it be too much stress?"-Huang Xiao asked with concern. He fished a piece of candy from a small tin by the head of his bed and offered it to Jing Chenjun:—"Jing Chenjun has taken on far too many responsibilities: an instructor at the academy, a tour guide at the Immortals' Bazaar, and recently a volunteer for the Immortal Arts Assembly. On top of that, midterm examinations are right around the corner. Even a True Immortal would collapse under that workload!"

—"Just what are you rushing for?"-Cheng Ziyi asked, scrutinizing Jing Chenjun.

—"I..."-Jing Chenjun parted his lips, unable to reply.

He had initially plunged into diverse endeavors because he was a mortal.

He refused to resign himself to fate, hoping that one could attain immortality even without a spirit root, and sought to carve out a path. The Heavenly Spirit Sect's rich, multifaceted curricula and practical internships seemed to fulfill his aspirations; even as a mortal, he could accomplish a vast array of tasks.

Yet the dream just now... Though the specifics had slipped from his grasp, Jing Chenjun felt that familiar anxiety surge once more—no, an even more acute restlessness. Was what he was doing genuinely meaningful?

He had studied so many subjects, yet was the vast majority not made possible solely through the magical artifacts and talismans of the Heavenly Spirit Sect? He still had not achieved Qi Condensation. Could he truly attain authentic strength? Could he truly alter his destiny? Could he truly avoid resembling that reincarnation...???

Watching Jing Chenjun lapse into stony silence once more, uneasy expressions crept across his roommates' faces.

Ji Gen and Huang Xiao traded several discreet glances. They were acquainted with a few demon clansmen adept at illusory arts and heart-manipulation—which, conversely, made for excellent "psychological counseling" tools. Though cultivators had no formal concept of "psychological therapy," it did not prevent them from occasionally employing such methods to resolve mental bottlenecks.

Ji Gen and Huang Xiao were debating whether to have Jing Chenjun give it a try.

Right then, Cheng Ziyi blurted out:

—"In that case, why don't we dive into the trial system?"

—"Huh?"-everyone in the dormitory turned to gawk at Cheng Ziyi.

—"Truth be told, I've had nightmares these past two days as well, waking up with overwhelming agony and bitter regret,"-Cheng Ziyi confessed.

He had no idea what he had been grieving or regretting; it seemed that in the dream, he resented being a talisman master ill-suited for combat, yearning instead for overwhelming martial prowess.

Then again, he had run away from home in the first place and was currently sprinting full-tilt down the path of a sword cultivator, eager to don the sword cultivator robes exclusive to the intermediate class! Consequently, he had swiftly shaken off that melancholy funk.

Cheng Ziyi resolved to share his personal remedy with his sworn dormitory brothers:

—"Carrying those useless emotions, I entered the trial system just as the update rolled out. I plunged into a hearty, exhilarating battle, and now all my regrets have evaporated! I think you lot ought to give it a shot too."

—"That actually makes sense,"-Ji Gen concurred:—"Combat vents unnecessary emotions, and the new update is exceptionally practical. I think it will build solid experience against beast tides."

—"A new update?"-Huang Xiao asked.

In truth, he disliked combat. Furthermore, during the Immortal Arts Assembly, the Delicacy Pavilion was swamped; riding the bizarre festival atmosphere, it felt as though even the darkest culinary abominations could sell out in seconds, so he had rarely tested the system.

Though Jing Chenjun did not play either, as a volunteer, he was well-informed:

—"It must be the latest major expansion patch,"-Jing Chenjun explained:—"It depicts the God-Demon War, where the demonic army pours into this world through a spatial-temporal rift, and cultivators from all regions mobilize to engage in battle. Unlike previous simulations featuring lone demons breaching temporal rifts, this update focuses on grand group warfare and team raids."

—"Exactly! It's brutally hard,"-Cheng Ziyi enthused:—"At first I didn't get the hang of it and got mobbed to death several times, but after grouping up, it felt so much better! Let's go together—[I'll carry you all!](# "Literal CN: 带飞 (dài fēi) — gaming slang: to carry one's teammates to victory")"

[You just want an excuse to play, don't you?]

Huang Xiao thought dryly.

Jing Chenjun was equally unenthusiastic:

—"No, I..."

—"Don't refuse! You've had nightmares for days anyway—what's the harm in giving it a shot?"-Cheng Ziyi urged.

Poking his head out into the hallway, he shouted down the dormitory corridor:

—"Anyone else awake? Who wants to jump into the trial system together?"

[—"Count me in!"](# "Speaker: Neighboring Dormitory Disciples")

Enthusiastic replies echoed from several neighboring rooms.

With matters having progressed to this point, Jing Chenjun could hardly decline. Shrugging helplessly toward Huang Xiao, he followed Cheng Ziyi into the trial system.

The scenery within the trial had indeed undergone a total overhaul. It resembled the lush, vibrant terrain of the Central Domain, yet possessed an unfamiliar cadence. As a volunteer, Jing Chenjun had entered during the system update to familiarize himself with the layout; at the time, he had been quite thrilled, because compared to daily lectures on mathematics and physics, an epic simulation like this felt far closer to authentic "immortality cultivation."

Yet at this moment, Jing Chenjun felt a sudden urge to retreat.

It was merely an illusory realm constructed by the power of magical artifacts and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-protecting grand formation; Jing Chenjun was still an insignificant mortal. What was more, with the official rollout complete, the enemies were fully "configured"—swarms of demons breached the sky in suffocating numbers, blotting out the heavens like flocks of migrating birds.

In that instant, scarlet flashed across Jing Chenjun's vision. A phantom sting pricked every inch of his skin.

—"What are you standing there for? Charge!"-Cheng Ziyi jabbed Jing Chenjun's shoulder.

Before Jing Chenjun could reply, Huang Xiao was already so terrified by the spectacle that his bushy weasel tail popped straight out:

—"Charge? Charge how?! We're only second-years, and we're not sword cultivators like you! We can't fight like this at all!"

—"If you're not a sword cultivator, there are other ways,"-Ji Gen said, patting a massive iron contraption beside them:—"See these Spell-Armor Cannons? Firing these is far more satisfying than swinging a sword."

—"Eh?"-Huang Xiao's beady little eyes widened in astonishment.

Cheng Ziyi, who aspired to become a peerless sword cultivator, let out a disdainful snort:

—"Do you even know how to fire it? Operating that thing demands formidable computational prowess, doesn't it?"

—"My arithmetic might be lacking, but don't we have Jing Chenjun right here?"-Ji Gen dragged Jing Chenjun forward and planted him firmly before the control console of the armored cannon:—"Lord Instructor of the White Jade Capital, the time has come to unleash your talents!"

—"Ah... right,"-Jing Chenjun responded blankly.

Though initially disoriented, as he gazed upon the familiar numerical interface and operating manuals, Jing Chenjun's eyes gradually steadied into cool composure.

This was no despairing mutual destruction. Slowly, it seemed, he too had found something he was truly capable of doing.

***

Yet the low-tier disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were unmistakably the most inconspicuous element of this war.

The true backbone forming the frontline consisted of higher-tier figures like Dragon Prince Dansha. Driven by inexplicable surges of emotion, they plunged back into the battlefield of the demon invasion, launching merciless counterattacks against the demonic hordes that had once laid waste to this world.

Witnessing this, Patriarch Jiuzhai could not help questioning:

—"Are you preparing to let everyone recover their memories of the sixth cycle?"

—"Not really,"-Zhou Hui replied nonchalantly, gazing toward the distant battlefield:—"Memories lost during a temporal rewind are essentially akin to formatting a hard drive, while memories of this lifetime are rewritten upon that formatted drive. Once overwritten, they are virtually impossible to retrieve. Thus, even if I wished for everyone to regain their sixth-cycle memories, theoretically it is impossible. As for why several of my direct disciples recovered theirs, I suspect it remains tied to me—because they are positioned so close to me, the 'server', their data was preserved."

At this point, Zhou Hui's gaze grew solemn:

—"However! The demonic qi lingering within the fissures of time served as a reminder: even if certain memories are completely forgotten, the emotions carved deep into their marrow still endure! Do not assume lingering demonic qi merely granted external forces an opportunity to invade; every soul in this world has long since braced themselves psychologically to resist that very invasion."

—"This also serves as a dress rehearsal for the worst-case scenario. If we truly prove unable to hold back the 'future', this time around, not a single person will march into battle unprepared!"

[...That wasn't what you said when you were so reluctant to let your own disciples recover their past-life memories.]

[So your heart only aches when your own precious brats regain their past-life trauma, is that it?]

Tao Zhu rolled his eyes beside him.

Unbothered by Tao Zhu's silent exasperation, Zhou Hui cast another sweeping glance over the chaotic battlefield in the distance, before turning toward his companions:

—"In any case, there are others to hold off the manifested demon hordes here. You all should be adequately prepared by now, yes?"

—"It is about time we step through the second gate."

As he spoke, his eyes swept over Jiuzhai, Tao Zhu, Ruwang, Peilan, and Sikong Yao. Then, he patted the "Gate" towering behind him.

It was the "Immortal Gate" situated upon the Ascension Stairway within this illusory realm.
