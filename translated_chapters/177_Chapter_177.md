# Chapter 177: The Sun

Zhou Hui did not immediately bring up the recent developments concerning the Demon Eye with the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

For one, it was taboo to [speak intimately on shallow acquaintance](# "Literal CN: 交浅言深 (jiāo qiǎn yán shēn) — discussing deep or critical matters before trust and rapport are established"); for another, Zhou Hui had yet to fully grasp the situation on the demons' side. Rashly disclosing that the Demon Eye had already opened would likely provoke nothing but needless suspicion and panic.

One needed only look at Tao Zhu and Shui Huang'er to see why.

One was the boss of the Abyssal Ocean Society, a Mahayana cultivator; the other was a noble demon beast, the Saintess of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. Yet upon seeing that the Demon Realm lay right beneath this world and realizing a demonic invasion was imminent, both wore expressions far more laden with terror than astonishment. It was difficult to imagine how others would react if they learned the truth of the Demon Eye.

At the very least, those silver-spoon scions of immortal dynasties still lingering at Eternal Villa would be frightened away from the very 'game' they had just been fiercely squabbling over. They would instantly crawl and wail in a frantic flight from the Central Domain, rushing home to tattle to their elders.

When that happened, long before the demons even arrived, this world would fall into chaotic turmoil first.

Therefore, even if Zhou Hui intended to discuss the matter with the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, he would have to wait until deeper cooperation was established.

What was more, when the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree dredged up ancient history and offered to gift the sun, its underlying subtext was likely not solely for the sake of the "demons."

Zhou Hui sized up that crimson sun, asking with curiosity:

—"Can it replace a true 'sun'?"

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree burst into hearty laughter:

—"Naturally it can!"

It knew Untiring Immortal Venerable had grasped its meaning.

The shrewd Boss Tao understood as well. A violent shiver ran down his spine, his bead-like eyes bulging wide:

[Are you two trying to rebel against the Heavenly Court?!]

Why Boss Tao would arrive at such a conclusion required starting from "what a sun is."

Speaking of the sun—after all, this was the cultivation realm; whether sun, moon, or stars, none were conventional celestial bodies.

They could be Three-Legged Golden Crows sweeping methodically across the heavens on schedule; they could be resplendent divine chariots radiating blinding brilliance driven by goddesses; or they could even be the colossal eyeballs of giants peering downward from spatial rifts.

One could say that among the three thousand worlds, every realm possessed its own sun.

Yet for the minor worlds that had submitted to the Heavenly Court, their suns were all mere projections of the Heavenly Court's 'sun'.

This was considered a welfare benefit.

After all, "suns" were wildly diverse, which resulted in notoriously poor environmental stability across various minor realms. In recorded history alone, during the Demon Court era, Golden Crow suns had brought about the catastrophe of ten suns scorching the earth. Across the three thousand worlds, various bizarre suns had concocted every conceivable method to court disaster—enough material to fill an entire book.

The trajectory and illumination of the Heavenly Court's sun projection were fixed, which at least guaranteed the baseline functioning of a minor world. No matter how creatively the local inhabitants courted death, they wouldn't accidentally extinguish their own sun.

Take the Heavenly Spirit Sect, for instance.

When Zhou Hui constructed the Spirit Reservoir Pool and reflected the Heavenly Spirit Sect inverted in the sky, it was practically equivalent to placing a lid over his own territory. Had the sun been a physical star or something similar, its light would have been completely blocked out. Yet because this realm's sun was merely a projection, it traversed a fixed altitude in the sky each day, rising and setting between the two 'Heavenly Spirit Sects' without suffering the slightest interference.

This was an advantage, but also a drawback.

Possessing this sun also meant that this region would perpetually remain within the Heavenly Court's sphere of influence.

The moment someone violated the Heavenly Laws and heavenly punishment descended, it could strike down whomever it pleased.

—Whether it could actually land the strike was another matter entirely.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree knew these intricacies well. Having resolved to take Untiring Immortal Venerable's side, it naturally acted far more decisively and thoroughly than Shui Huang'er.

It practically spelled it out:

—"This sun of this old man may be somewhat dim, but illuminating the entire Central Domain is more than within its power,"-the Sacred Tree said.

[You really want Untiring to rebel?!]

Tao Zhu nearly shrieked aloud. Instinctively clamping his hands over his mouth, he swallowed the shock down his throat so hard that his round belly ballooned once again.

Paying no mind to his swollen stomach, Tao Zhu rolled his small eyes and turned his gaze toward the sun as well.

Rebellion aside, this "sun" was [truly irresistible](# "Literal CN: 真香 (zhēn xiāng) — internet slang for something so tempting one cannot resist, even after prior reservations").

Never mind that the Demon Eye in the Central Domain genuinely required a sun to suppress it; even without the Demon Eye and without a demonic incursion, a treasure capable of producing spirit dew was something no one could afford to miss.

Therefore, regardless of what sinister motives this old tree monster harbored, it was best to agree first and secure the prize.

Boss Tao calculated slyly. Unfortunately, his words carried zero weight here. In fact, because the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's demigod manifestation was present in person, he did not even dare transmit his divine sense to whisper scheming advice to Untiring—Tao Zhu's cultivation was far from impressive before a ten-thousand-year-old tree spirit. He could only stare at Untiring with pathetic, pleading eyes.

At this moment, Boss Tao only dreaded that Untiring's stubborn streak would flare up:

He feared Untiring would be too rigid and, having no desire to rebel, flatly turn it down;

He also feared Untiring would be too rigid and, agreeing on the spot, truly charge off alongside the tree monster to confront the Heavenly Court.

[Sigh, how could swindling the demon race even count as swindling? If only Untiring were as "clever" as old Tao.]

Tao Zhu mused fretfully.

Boss Tao could devise such underhanded schemes, and naturally Shui Huang'er could too:

[And Grand Elder called me gullible!]

[Look who's talking!]

[Who scrambles to give things away like this?!]

[Even if there's a chance of a demonic incursion, doesn't that situation simply mean the human race's vulnerability lies right in our hands? This is the prime opportunity to bestow a favor! At the very least, we should fight for the demon race's interests—such as having Lord Immortal Venerable participate more deeply in our educational reforms, strengthening the ties between the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, and the like—he can't just take our supreme treasure for nothing!]

At this juncture, the Saintess of the demon race dutifully weighed the gains and losses for her kind.

Unfortunately, she had no say either.

Thus, Shui Huang'er could only join Boss Tao in staring tensely at Zhou Hui, longing to know what choice this Lord Immortal Venerable would make before such an incomparable treasure.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was gazing at Zhou Hui as well:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, what do You think?"

—"Then I shall not stand on ceremony,"-Zhou Hui replied without the slightest hesitation. He clearly had no intention of declining this gift.

At this outcome, both Shui Huang'er and Tao Zhu let out a sigh of relief.

Yet alongside her relief, Shui Huang'er felt an inexplicable surge of complicated emotions, tinged with a faint trace of disappointment.

She figured these sentiments stemmed from her anxiety over the future of the demon race.

The demon race had surrendered a supreme secret treasure, yet Untiring Immortal Venerable had made no promises in return. Even though Shui Huang'er believed Lord Immortal Venerable was noble of character and dedicated to [educating all without discrimination](# "Literal CN: 有教无类 (yǒujiào wúlèi) — Confucius's principle from the Analects: providing education to all regardless of origin or species"), she could not help worrying: did he simply desire the demon race's treasure without wanting any deeper entanglement with their kind?

Meanwhile, Tao Zhu, who knew Zhou Hui far better, found his heart tightening with even greater panic after that initial breath of relief.

His brain spun in frantic overdrive, racking his wits for a chance to persuade Untiring: cooperating temporarily with the demon race was fine, but under no circumstances should he take any of their demands to heart—especially defying the Heavenly Court! No matter how the Heavenly Court had once abandoned this world, when facing demons, the immortals of the Upper Realm were still vastly more reliable than the native demons!

Tao Zhu reasoned. This line of thought reflected the conventional wisdom of most mortals and cultivators alike.

Only, Boss Tao had completely misjudged the situation: the one who had thoroughly offended the Heavenly Court right now was Untiring Immortal Venerable, not the demon race.

Naturally, whatever Boss Tao and Shui Huang'er were thinking was wholly irrelevant.

Zhou Hui paid them no heed. Fixing his gaze upon the sun, he asked:

—"May I test it?"

—"It is already Yours; You may dispose of it as You please,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree said.

It even went so far as to provide a most considerate walkthrough on how the sun produced spirit dew, how to extract it, and how to utilize it.

Zhou Hui listened with absolute gravity. He even retrieved a roll of bamboo slips, jotting down notes upon them, which caused Tao Zhu to glance sideways in disbelief.

Once cultivators reached Nascent Soul, most possessed photographic memory, able to retain everything they wished without copying or transcribing, let alone an unfathomable figure like Untiring.

So what in the world was he writing?

Curious, Boss Tao craned his neck to peek, only to discover that upon Zhou Hui's brush tip of soft, silvery-white hairs, there was not a single drop of ink. Only vigorous yet exquisitely precise spiritual energy flowed alongside the crisp strokes, quickly permeating the slips carved from thousand-year-old aged bamboo. It was impossible to discern what this wordless heavenly book contained.

Boss Tao had no choice but to sullenly retract his neck and listen to the Sacred Tree continue its lecture:

—"...Although the spiritual energy of Taiyi Spirit Dew is exceptionally abundant and sufficient to meet most requirements, when speaking of spiritual purity, nothing surpasses the unextracted Solar Essence..."

—"Pure? Is this sun actually this realm's 'Primordial Sun'?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"The Primordial Sun... You truly know so much!"-the Sacred Tree marveled, gazing upon Zhou Hui with open admiration.

Its lavish praise was enough to make Boss Tao's teeth ache with sour envy. Still, Jian Haoge had once grumbled that if one wished to travel alongside Untiring and learn anything from him, one simply had to get used to this sort of spectacle.

Fortunately, while Zhou Hui cared little for flattery, he [loved lecturing others](# "Literal CN: 好为人师 (hào wéi rén shī) — an idiom from Mencius meaning having an incorrigible pedagogical urge to instruct") and would patiently explain unfamiliar concepts to those who didn't understand (Tao Zhu felt this was primarily to indulge the little Phoenix maiden):

—"A so-called Primordial Sun is the very first pure energy... body of spiritual gas formed by the power of creation when a world has just coalesced,"-Zhou Hui elucidated.-"It typically manifests in the shape of a sun, possessing pure spiritual energy converted directly from primordial chaos, capable of nourishing the flourishing growth of all living things."

Thanks to its vast lifespan, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree knew even more than Zhou Hui on this subject:

—"However, most 'Primordial Suns' do not endure for long. Living beings are born greedy; once they develop sentience, they scheme by hook or by crook to monopolize this 'Primordial Sun'. Little do they know that swallowing a 'Primordial Sun' means becoming the replacement for the sun itself."

This was also the origin of the bizarre, kaleidoscopic suns across the various minor worlds.

—"Later on, the Heavenly Court standardized the 'specifications of suns',"-the Sacred Tree continued.-"In exchange, and as proof of submission to the Heavenly Court, regardless of what form a minor world's sun took, it had to be surrendered to the Heavenly Court."

This was, of course, an unequal treaty.

Unless a world had reached its apocalypse, a sun was teeming with immense power, far beyond what a mere projection could match. Yet whether to seek the Heavenly Court's protection, or cowed by the Heavenly Court's overwhelming military might, or simply because that realm's high-tier cultivators wished to trade for the qualifications to ascend to the Upper Realm, they all surrendered their suns and became part of the Heavenly Court's dominion.

Because these reasons were somewhat humiliating, most cultivation realms never recorded them in their histories. Everyone merely took it for granted that ascension was a natural, matter-of-course reality.

Zhou Hui had also only learned of this after making the acquaintance of a Creation Goddess.

—"Why was this realm's 'Primordial Sun' never surrendered?"-Zhou Hui asked in wonder.

—"Perhaps the Heavenly Court never knew?"-the Sacred Tree chuckled.

In fact, speaking of this matter, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree harbored no small amount of smug satisfaction:

—"After all, this world was once a fragment of the Upper Realm and was not initially treated as an independent world. In truth, that was indeed the case—compared to other Primordial Suns, this sun's power tier was vastly inferior. It was likely converted from chaotic qi only during the cataclysmic descent, its spiritual energy far from sufficient to sustain an entire world. Therefore, neither the Heavenly Court nor the inhabitants of this realm ever imagined that such a shattered world could harbor a 'Primordial Sun'.

—"Moreover, back then, this world had just crashed down, fractured into pieces, with demons running rampant and the original spirit veins shattered into complete disarray. Nobody noticed the presence of the Primordial Sun's energy.

—"Most importantly, that Primordial Sun happened to coalesce near this old man, perfectly veiled by my spiritual aura. Such a serendipitous fortune delivered right to my doorstep—naturally this old man wouldn't refuse. As the saying goes, [the pavilion closest to the water catches the sun first](# "Literal CN: 近水楼台先得日 (jìnshuǐ lóutái xiān dé rì) — a pun on the proverb 近水楼台先得月 (the pavilion closest to the water catches the moon first), meaning being in prime position to reap a windfall")—so I gladly scooped it up."

Hearing this, Boss Tao could do nothing but roll his eyes.

Put simply: when this realm fell, chaos reigned supreme and nobody had the time or attention to realize a new sun had been born, allowing the nearby Fire Abyss Sacred Tree to scoop up an outrageous bargain!

Only, Boss Tao was unaware that after tens of thousands of years had elapsed, the Heavenly Court had more or less caught wind of anomalies.

In the "previous cycle of reincarnation," that brat who came to seduce Shui Huang'er had likely been a pawn of the Heavenly Court. Although he was chased away that time, the Sacred Tree did not believe the Heavenly Court had given up. At the very least, after the entire world fell, leaving only the Southern Domain as the sole survivor had been glaringly suspicious. Back then, the demons could easily have overrun the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree directly, yet they never made a move—almost as if deliberately leaving an opening to force the demon race to flee with the pure, untainted 'Primordial Sun'.

This was also why the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, upon hearing of Untiring's "illustrious deeds" in cycling the world through reincarnation, was so eager and willing to give away this secret treasure.

Since there was a high probability it couldn't keep it anyway, why not [dump the hot potato onto the Human Sovereign](# "Literal CN: 甩锅 (shuǎi guō) — passing the buck / dumping the burden / shifting blame") and let this Immortal Venerable clash with the Heavenly Court!

In this respect, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was an ancient monster countless times more cunning than its Saintess.

—"This Primordial Sun has accompanied this old man for tens of thousands of years; this karmic tie ought to conclude,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree proclaimed with high-sounding righteousness.-"I have full faith that once Untiring Immortal Venerable acquires this Primordial Sun, You will bring out its greatest value."

It even warmly suggested ideas to Zhou Hui:

—"For instance, You could use it to revive the spiritual energy circulation that was once destroyed in the Central Domain."

—"No rush for that,"-Zhou Hui had other plans for the Central Domain; he cared far more about an alternative application.-"If one nourishes trees with Solar Essence, could one cultivate a biological compu... *cough*, a tree like you?"

—"This old man?"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree blanked.

Shui Huang'er, however, felt she understood Zhou Hui's requirement:

—"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable, do you wish to cultivate that peach tree at the mountain gate of the Heavenly Spirit Sect?—At the Heavenly Spirit Sect, there is a several-hundred-year-old peach tree that serves as a symbol, and disobedient disciples are also hung from it,"-Shui Huang'er explained to the Sacred Tree.

—"Oh, so that's how it is. That peach tree of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, this old man has indeed heard Tong Ling mention it,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree nodded, speaking with dignified pride.-"However, for a junior of merely several hundred years, even with the aid of the Primordial Sun, growing to this old man's stature would likely require no less than several thousand years."

Reaching this point, it paused and added with genuine concern:

—"Furthermore, this old man does not recommend using the Primordial Sun to artificially force plant growth—[pulling up sprouts to help them grow](# "Literal CN: 拔苗助长 (bámiáo zhùzhǎng) — idiom: spoiling things through excessive zeal or forcing premature, unnatural growth"). Although your human race might fail to discern any difference in artificially accelerated flora, over the long term, whether in the fruits they bear or the pills refined from them, there will be indescribable consequences."

The Sacred Tree had done its utmost to issue a warning from a human perspective.

Yet it deeply doubted whether humans could restrain themselves. After all, causing spiritual herbs that took thousands of years to bear fruit to mature in an instant was a temptation no cultivator could refuse.

In any case, humans wouldn't care whether an artificially force-ripened plant was completely insane, would they?

Only, the gift had already been handed over...

—"Several thousand years! Truly, no matter how extraordinary the secret treasure, the passage of time remains unavoidable,"-Zhou Hui let out a sigh of regret.-"Moreover, spiritual botanics is indeed not the Heavenly Spirit Sect's forte."

Not that it was completely impossible.

Zhou Hui knew a bit of everything, so he possessed a considerable degree of skill in spiritual plant botany, not inferior to an average botanist.

And among his disciples, his Fifth Disciple Lu Shulong had inherited this lineage.

As an alchemy master and a member of the dragon race, Lu Shulong was adept at calling the clouds and summoning rain; those few mu of spiritual fields and herb gardens were tended far better than those of ordinary alchemy sects.

Only, that was solely from the perspective of ordinary mortals.

By Zhou Hui's exacting standards, it could only be described as "utterly mediocre."

—"In particular, when it comes to the genuine needs of flora, I truly know very little." After all, conventional spiritual plant techniques contained no such knowledge.

Zhou Hui humbly consulted the Sacred Tree:

—"If I wish to utilize spirit dew to heal and nurture flora, might I trouble You to help take a look?"

—"This old man..."-the Sacred Tree hesitated for an instant.

It normally never departed from the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. Yet its intent to take Untiring Immortal Venerable's side was genuine. While there was a measure of exploitation involved, had it not genuinely favored him, how could it have given away even the "Primordial Sun"?

Besides, the Sacred Tree was genuinely anxious that humans would abuse the spirit dew.

The Sacred Tree ultimately nodded:

—"If You require it, this old man shall do my utmost to assist."

—"Then I am most grateful,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.

He raised his hand. The bamboo slips he had been recording upon floated into the air, plunging into the midst of the Taiyi Spirit Dew. In a flash, they regenerated from bound slips back into the form of living bamboo, then burst open with a crisp *crack*, and a tender sapling sprouted from within.

Tao Zhu stared dumbfounded, feeling that the sapling looked remarkably familiar. Only after a good while did he react:

[—"Isn't that—"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

—"Langhuan?!"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree cried out in recognition even before Tao Zhu.

Naturally, it recognized Langhuan. Back when the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was not yet a sacred tree, but merely an outer little Wood Spirit, the Langhuan Library had stood in this very territory. The tallest tree within had been the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's idol; precisely because that ancient tree embraced humanity and preserved human history, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had been inspired to emulate it.

Yet when this land crashed down, Langhuan's sacred tree refused to abandon the library to the very end, resulting in it shattering to pieces—a fate the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree could never understand.

For all these years, it had heard no word of Langhuan and assumed it was utterly destroyed. It never expected to witness Langhuan's lineage so abruptly. The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree trembled from head to toe with excitement...

No, this trembling wasn't from excitement!

The Sacred Tree felt a massive torrent of knowledge and memories surging into its body.

This was the "symbiotic network" unique to Wood Spirits.

From this day forth, Langhuan would become a part of it, and it would become a part of Langhuan.

—"No!"-the Sacred Tree cried out to Zhou Hui.-"If You wish to nurture Langhuan, You must return to the Heavenly Spirit Sect! If it stays here with me, once it establishes root networks, it will be exceedingly difficult to transplant!"

It assumed Zhou Hui had failed to understand how to use the spirit dew and had brought Langhuan out by mistake.

Boss Tao also panicked, babbling to Zhou Hui:

—"Right, no rush with Langhuan, let us return to the Heavenly Spirit Sect first—"

How could a treasury like Langhuan be brandished so casually?! And displayed right before the demon race?!

Boss Tao paused.

A thought suddenly struck him: if even he understood this, could Untiring possibly be oblivious?

This fellow had deliberately planted Langhuan here. Could it be...

Boss Tao watched in utter despair as Untiring turned a tender smile upon the Sacred Tree, his gaze as passionately affectionate as if gazing upon a lover in the heat of romance.

—"Since it cannot be transplanted, then we simply won't transplant it."

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Sacred Tree")

The Sacred Tree was struck dumb.

Zhou Hui said, eyes brimming with boundless affection:

—"Let us raise it together!"

[I knew it!]

[This bastard Untiring isn't just after the Primordial Sun and the Taiyi Spirit Dew!]

[He wants the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree itself!]

No wonder, given Untiring's usual temperament, he had accepted someone else's treasure so decisively—it had felt bizarre all along.

Turns out he never intended to take the treasure away; he planned to take the owner along with the treasure!

[Only... isn't this tree merely capable of fabricating illusions?]

[Does it warrant Untiring valuing it so much?]

Tao Zhu felt a terrible headache coming on.

Had Zhou Hui known Tao Zhu's thoughts, he would have solemnly retorted:

[That is a super biological computer! A super biological computer! The only unit in the entire world, meticulously nurtured with Solar Essence over who knows how many tens of thousands of years! To acquire it, never mind parting with a treasure or two—even if I were sucked completely dry myself, it would be totally worth it!]

Naturally, even without voicing these words, the Immortal Venerable expressed them thoroughly through his actions.

Zhou Hui clasped the Sacred Tree's hands with immense fervor:

—"I said from the beginning, your database is insufficiently large, nor do you possess a rational classification directory,"-Zhou Hui said earnestly.-"Once Langhuan is imported, I believe that issue can be temporarily set aside. Next, our primary focus is purging redundant data, expanding memory capacity, and—"

[—"Wait, Lord Immortal Venerable?"](# "Speaker: Sacred Tree")

The Sacred Tree didn't understand; the Sacred Tree was terrified.

It wanted to cut him off, but didn't know how to interrupt.

It had an ominous feeling that this Immortal Venerable was cooking up some terrifying scheme.

Shui Huang'er, on the other hand, watched this scene in a daze. Suddenly a spark of realization struck her:

—"Could it be that Lord Immortal Venerable does not intend to take the Primordial Sun and Taiyi Spirit Dew away, but plans to place this... Langhuan Library directly here with us?"

—"Precisely. I wish to center upon the demon race to construct a Virtual Experimental Teaching Center!"-Zhou Hui said ardently.-"On this foundation, I hope the demon race will cooperate with me in executing an even deeper educational reform program."

[—"Woah!"](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

Shui Huang'er let out a delighted cry.

[I completely misunderstood Lord Immortal Venerable just now!]

[He is clearly a sage seen once in ten thousand years!]

[Faced with supreme treasures, all he thinks of is how to innovate teaching methodologies!]

Shui Huang'er thought with boundless veneration, eagerly blurting out:

—"Any cooperation is fine! Lord Immortal Venerable, do you need me to do anything?"

—"There is something... Speaking of which, Mo Xi's 'homework' over there should be about ready to turn in, shouldn't it?"

[—"Homework?"](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

—"Yes. A piece of homework that both satisfies his desires and serves to repay your demon race's generous gift,"-Zhou Hui said.

[Before your disciple was even immersed in the trial of desires, we hadn't said we'd make a gift yet, had we?]

[Wait a moment... back then, Untiring Immortal Venerable was frantically ordering about (stress-testing) the Wood Spirits (system performance).]

[Remembering what that kid was currently doing...]

Hearing this, the Sacred Tree's heart sank.

The white tree shadows in the surroundings swayed, projecting the current state of Zhou Hui's two disciples.
