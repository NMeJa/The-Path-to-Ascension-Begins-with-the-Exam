# Chapter 228: Being Too Clever for One's Own Good

While the lower realm began acting out Mo Xi's "script," the Heavenly Court wasn't idling either.

Although the Heavenly Court's administrative efficiency was notoriously dismal as a rule, whenever accountability and blame-shifting were on the line, immortal officials across every department suddenly overflowed with burning enthusiasm for their work—and the immortal officials of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny were no exception.

Having received the Pixiu's "guidance," this flock of immortal officials rushed frantically back to the Ministry of Celestial Destiny and entered the Grotto-Heaven of Mystic Transformation.

This grotto-heaven was the sanctum where immortal officials authored and compiled the Heavenly Dao of the subordinate worlds—the crucible where Heavenly Destiny was forged.

Grand though it sounded, the interior of the Grotto-Heaven of Mystic Transformation was actually pitch-black. If one stood just slightly apart—and not even that far, merely two paces away—it became impossible to sense the aura or presence of another "colleague," as though only oneself remained in the entire expanse of heaven and earth.

Such a grotto-heaven felt somewhat eerie, hardly resembling the Heavenly Court at all, yet it was undeniably the most fitting workplace for the Ministry of Celestial Destiny.

Leaving aside the fact that its gloomy silence and absence of interruptions allowed the celestial officials to settle into their working groove far faster, the single most marvelous feature of this grotto-heaven was its ability to synchronize its temporal flow with various lower worlds.

After all, it took three days to write a single chapter, but readers devoured it in three minutes. Given the time dilation of the Immortal Realm, by the time the celestial officials drafted a new script, [the daylilies in the lower realm would already be stone cold](# "Literal CN: 黄花菜都凉了 (huánghuācài dōu liáng le) — an idiom meaning everything is far too late / the ship has sailed").

It was precisely thanks to this grotto-heaven that the "Heavenly Dao" authored by the Ministry of Celestial Destiny could be synchronized down to subordinate minor worlds. If necessary, time within the grotto-heaven could even be brought to a dead halt, allowing celestial officials to catch up before their [deadlines](# "Modern satire: 死线 (sǐxiàn) — deadline; corporate office slang applied to celestial officials drafting fate").

Furthermore, the grotto-heaven housed magical artifacts such as the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation, which allowed direct observation of the lower realms. This made it convenient for officials to track the execution of their scripted destinies in real time and patch discrepancies whenever errors cropped up.

That being said, the celestial officials rarely cared to use this particular artifact. For one thing, although it could observe the lower realm, it was by no means a "cinema viewing tool"; rather, it was equipped with an automated error-reporting function. Any discrepancy from the Heavenly Dao script—regardless of magnitude, regardless of whether it genuinely affected the lower world or not—would be meticulously cataloged into tens of thousands of dense error notices, enough to inflict near-myocardial infarction on a True Immortal. Consequently, everyone preferred flipping through the Wordless Heavenly Scripture rather than touching this artifact. For another, re-parsing a script to untangle and rectify minute errors within a bloated Heavenly Dao codebase was an excruciating ordeal. Even if this [mountain of legacy spaghetti crap](# "Tech slang: 屎山 (shǐshān) — mountain of shit; developer slang for bloated, convoluted legacy codebases") was their own creation, out of sight was out of mind—as long as it managed to run, it was deemed a success.

Now, however, the corrupted lower-realm script had caused errors to propagate into the Heavenly Court's own Heavenly Dao. Unable to sit idly by any longer, a cohort of celestial officials finally steeled their resolve and congregated before the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation.

An official pinched a hand seal, activating the precious mirror.

—"Show me the status of Minor World No. 67,"-he commanded.

Instantly, glimpses of multiple worlds flashed across the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation as it scanned through them searching for the target. In the end, however, the mirror remained completely blank, looking exceedingly ripe for a "404 Not Found" stamp.

—"The designated world does not exist,"-a voice from within the precious mirror replied.

—"How could it not exist?!"-a True Immortal exclaimed.

—"Did someone remember the serial number wrong?"-asked another official, who harbored zero confidence in his colleagues' memories—or his own.

—"If it doesn't exist, then what the blazes have we been writing all this time?!"-another attempted to reason with the mirror.

To this, the precious mirror remained completely unresponsive, keeping quiet and pretending to be nothing more than an ordinary, mindless piece of glass.

The True Immortals were so infuriated they nearly smashed it on the spot.

—"Connect to the 'Langhuan Forbidden Grounds',"-the veteran celestial official commanded, having experienced a sudden stroke of inspiration.

Admittedly, compared to the colloquial names of minor worlds, serial numbers were generally far more definitive. With so many minor worlds in existence, duplicate names or complicated local politics often rendered names fluid and ambiguous. Langhuan, however, was different. It had originally been an integral part of the Heavenly Court, assigned a serial number only after its fall. Perhaps—just perhaps—its serial number had simply failed to be entered into the registry, even if they had never encountered such a glitch when using the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation before.

This time, the mirror indeed did not claim ignorance. Instead, it swiftly locked onto a certain world. Yet the surface of the mirror appeared veiled behind a layer of dense fog, murky and impenetrable.

—"Insufficient permissions,"-the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation announced.

—"How are our permissions insufficient? We're the very ones who author this world's Heavenly Dao—how could our permissions be insufficient?!"-the celestial officials demanded.

The mirror maintained its silence.

—"I suspect that due to the alterations in the Heavenly Court's own Heavenly Dao, a portion of the Great Dao—the data—was restored to a prior state,"-the veteran celestial official theorized:—"That caused the serial number for Sector Sixty-Seven to be lost, and it has been reclassified as part of the Heavenly Court—the upper realm."

—"Put that way, it does make sense,"-the celestial officials murmured thoughtfully.

—"So what are we supposed to do now?"-asked those who couldn't care less about the underlying cause.

—"Insufficient permissions means higher credentials are required, does it not?"-the veteran celestial official said:—"Our only option is to contact Lord Tian Yan directly and let him make the call."

—"..."

Faced with this proposal, the celestial officials fell dead silent.

Much as they longed to cleanly pass the buck to anyone—including Tian Yan—Tian Yan was, after all, their supreme direct superior. Scheming against him was no easy feat. What was more, he was currently stationed at the side of His Imperial Majesty the Celestial Emperor alongside the other high ministers of the Heavenly Court. If things went sideways and the distortion of the Heavenly Court's Heavenly Dao became public knowledge, they would bypass the phase of [making amends for past faults](# "Literal CN: 将功补过 (jiāng gōng bǔ guò) — using future achievements to compensate for past mistakes") entirely. They would be stripped of their immortal registry on the spot, banished to the mortal dust, and turned into playthings of fate for the incoming freshmen of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny!

The atmosphere ground to a tense stalemate.

—"Lord Tian Yan doesn't necessarily have to be present in person! Can't we just use Lord Tian Yan's official seal to grant us clearance?"-a young celestial official with exceptionally [untainted, naive eyes](# "Internet meme: 清澈的愚蠢 (qīngchè de yúchǔn) — pure and untainted stupidity; popular slang for the earnest, clueless gaze of recent graduates") suddenly suggested in a 'flash of brilliance.'

—"Using Lord Tian Yan's personal property without authorization... isn't that rather inappropriate?"-the veteran official hesitated.

—"It hardly counts as unauthorized,"-another official said, clearly tempted as well, chiming in to persuade him:—"Before Lord Tian Yan departed, he left his official seal on the Celestial Destiny Terrace. For one, it symbolizes that he permanently presides over the terrace, standing united with us; for another, isn't it precisely so that if an emergency arises and he cannot return in time, we would have a contingency measure at our disposal?"

—"Except the last time an emergency occurred was a thousand years ago,"-the veteran official mumbled.

Yet he voiced no further objections. Instead, he accompanied the flock of celestial officials to the Celestial Destiny Terrace to "invite" down Tian Yan's official seal of celestial governance.

They acted with the utmost delicacy and caution, not daring to disturb even the faintest wisp of Tian Yan's residual divine sense nestled within the jade seal. Evidently, keeping their own boss in the dark until the crisis was resolved had become an unspoken consensus among them. Fortunately, the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation cared nothing for the seal holder's state of consciousness; as long as the formal protocol was satisfied, it executed the command.

Thus, the moment the official seal bearing Tian Yan's name was reverently enshrined before the precious mirror, the murky imagery within began to clear.

Perhaps because it had been blurred for far too long, the mirror screen flickered several times. The images passed so swiftly that not even the immortal officials present could make out what had flashed across the glass: it looked like a meditating (sleeping) cultivator, a dazzling sun, the hateful glare of a young girl, and an Ascension Stairway towering into the clouds.

—"Is something wrong with this mirror?"-a True Immortal asked uneasily.

The moment his words fell, the scenery within the mirror stabilized, locking with crystalline clarity onto a certain battlefield.

At the sight, the celestial officials breathed a sigh of relief. Yet upon discerning the scene, their expressions morphed into bewilderment:

[—"A demonic invasion?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Officials")

Judging by the vista, it appeared to be an "ordinary" demonic invasion.

Countless demons swarmed forth, crawling from the sky, burrowing from the soil, and surging from the seas. Their numbers were so staggering that the local cultivators had no time to mount an effective defense. One unfortunate cultivator was seized by a demon, letting out a shriek that sounded half like laughter and half like weeping, before being torn to shreds in an instant. A patter of dripping blood dyed a stretch of the ocean crimson.

Though the opening scene was gruesome, the celestial officials of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny remained entirely impassive. Clearly accustomed to such spectacles, their surprise stemmed solely from the fact that the "script" diverged from what they had authored:

[—"Has this cycle's 'Protagonist' descended already? Why is there already a demonic invasion?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Probably not yet?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Is there even a 'Protagonist' this time? Don't tell me they assumed that just because Tunhuo returned, this script was considered finished?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Splendid!... Ahem, I mean, if the error was caused by the lack of a 'Protagonist', then it isn't our fault."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Get in touch with the Immortal Execution Stage..."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Wait, according to the script, it doesn't seem to be time for this cycle's 'Protagonist' to make his debut yet."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Then what is going on with these demons?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Could it be that the pathway opened by the previous 'Protagonist' wasn't properly sealed by the temporal reset, causing the demons to invade prematurely?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"But a premature demon invasion shouldn't impact much, should it? It's an abandoned world anyway."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Hard to say! That's Langhuan down there, after all—a massive pile of forbidden items needs to be reclaimed. We gave the 'Protagonist' every possible cheat within our power, yet they failed time and again, and even the single one who succeeded can hardly be said to have recovered everything. What if some demons retained their full memories, invaded ahead of schedule during the temporal rollback, snatched away some forbidden relic, and caused the Heavenly Dao to diverge?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Ugh, please no! That kind of bug is the hardest to patch! Standard Divine Descent Arts can't fix it at all!"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Can't we just rewind that world's time?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"If you had that kind of power, why would you still be working in the Ministry of Celestial Destiny? Wouldn't becoming a God directly be [much sweeter](# \"Slang: 不香吗 (bù xiāng ma) — 'isn't that fragrant?'; internet slang meaning 'isn't that far better / more appealing?'\")?"](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

[—"Tch, God? What's so great about becoming a God nowadays? Becoming one of *Them* means having all your desires stripped away, living increasingly dreary days—not to mention Their power is also..."](# "Speaker: Celestial Official")

—"Cough, cough, cough!"

The veteran celestial official let out a series of deafening coughs, cutting short the increasingly outrageous conversation.

The fellow who had sharply critiqued the divine realized he had spoken out of turn. He slunk sheepishly behind the crowd of immortals, pretending he could conceal himself from sight.

Yet the veteran official said nothing further. For one, he lacked the authority to lecture the other; for another, the Ministry of Celestial Destiny possessed certain unique privileges that shielded it from the gaze of deities, so dropping a few anonymous critiques was of little consequence so long as they didn't leak out; for a third, he naturally harbored no great disagreement with the sentiment himself—it was simply that the situation was dire, and this was neither the time nor the place for such banter.

—"Scroll the footage back a little. Yes, right there,"-the veteran official commanded the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation, once the whispering among the officials subsided, his gaze fixed gravely upon the glass.

—"What's wrong with that spot?"-a True Immortal asked, puzzled.

[—"Do you not see the stairway and the city in the sky over there?"](# "Speaker: Veteran Celestial Official")

—"A city in the sky? What of it?"-the True Immortal blinked in confusion:—"I recall that Langhuan, being part of the Heavenly Court, possessed the craft to construct floating cities in the sky. They even had a Sky City—though after that world was abandoned, to prevent architecture of that caliber from leaking out, we specifically scripted a storyline detailing how to destroy Sky City along with its master."

The reason this gentleman was so intimately familiar was, evidently, because he had been the very author of that particular plotline.

—"Take a closer look—is that Sky City? And that stairway leading straight into the firmament—"-the veteran official glared at him.

—"Ah..."-the True Immortal stared dumbly at the mirror. Although he had scripted the tragedy of their city's ruin and clan's destruction, he had never cared in the slightest what the citadel actually looked like. He merely thought the fortress in the mirror was somewhat excessively magnificent, scarcely inferior to the celestial palaces themselves. Was that why orders had been given to destroy it...?

Fortunately, the Ministry of Celestial Destiny wasn't populated entirely by empty-headed fools.

—"Setting the city aside for a moment, that stairway... could it be the Ascension Stairway?!"-another True Immortal gasped, grasping the veteran official's meaning.

—"The Ascension Stairway?!"-the other True Immortals cried in disbelief.

At last, they realized something was deeply amiss. In this day and age, preserved Ascension Stairways were exceedingly rare.

After all, gone were the days when the Heavenly Court was newly established and desperately understaffed, opening ascension pathways and constructing Ascension Stairways for allied minor worlds to bolster celestial ranks. Nowadays, the Heavenly Court suffered from severe bureaucratic overstaffing and personnel redundancy (though no one would admit it openly on the surface) and had no need for so many immortals. Consequently, the recruitment policies of the Heavenly Court had shifted: not only were quotas for True Immortals allocated strictly according to the spiritual energy abundance of each world, but the Ascension Stairways of nearly all minor worlds had been deliberately severed. Only a handful of resource-rich yet fiercely resistant minor worlds retained a few Ascension Stairways to bestow "celestial enlightenment"—and even those were strictly numbered and recorded in official registries.

Unquestionably, Langhuan met none of the criteria for retaining an Ascension Stairway. Thus, the Heavenly Court had issued an edict commanding Langhuan to return the library's archived tomes and resources, assessing ascension eligibility strictly by "contributions"—the standard imposed upon all other ascenders as well: the more useful assets and resources one carried up to the Heavenly Court, the easier it was to ascend. Yet the cultivators of Langhuan deemed the Heavenly Court's demands to be blatant extortion and exploitation. In defiance, they had taken the initiative to sever the Ascension Stairway themselves, breaking ties with the Heavenly Court entirely.

—"Right, wasn't their Ascension Stairway supposed to have been severed? His Imperial Majesty the Celestial Emperor was furious back then, thinking them utterly [ungrateful](# \"Literal CN: 不识抬举 (bù shí táiju) — failing to appreciate favors/elevation\"),"-recalled another older official who had lived through the era of Langhuan's God-Demon War.

—"Yet the Ascension Stairway in this world has been 'restored.' And not just the stairway, but the city as well... That is no 'Sky City.' That is White Jade Capital,"-the veteran official nodded.

—"White Jade Capital?!"-several senior officials exclaimed.

The younger officials, meanwhile, wore expressions of complete mystification.

—"In antiquity, Langhuan was not merely an imperial archive, but also the academy where the royal bloodline of the Celestial Emperor studied; hence, imperial traveling palaces were constructed there,"-the veteran official explained:—"Although those palaces were shattered to powder when Langhuan fell, the cultivators of that realm used the ruins as a foundation to construct a 'White Jade Capital' that was by no means inferior to the original palaces."

—"Were they trying to rebel?"-an official muttered.

—"It wasn't so much rebelling as that they simply never accepted the fact that they were no longer immortals,"-another official remarked with a sneer:—"No wonder the Heavenly Court abandoned that world."

—"...Cough, cough."

The veteran official coughed twice more.

In truth, he didn't believe the inhabitants were in denial of reality at all.

When all was said and done, Langhuan had plummeted in the first place for the sake of the Heavenly Court. Furthermore, the fact that the local cultivators possessed the capability to rebuild White Jade Capital proved their strength was already comparable to True Immortals. Wishing for Langhuan—which had sacrificed so much—to return to the Heavenly Court was only natural; they certainly had no reason to surrender everything they owned just to barter for a single measly ascension ticket. Breaking ties with the Heavenly Court was entirely justified.

In fact, had the God-Demon War not claimed so many lives... (Speaking of which, that ancient God-Demon War had been exceedingly suspicious. The initial fall of Langhuan had already suppressed the demon race, affording that world an opportunity to recover and perhaps even return. Yet right at the critical juncture, the demons had invaded once again!) Many peerless titans perished in that war. Even then, the Celestial Emperor hadn't dared to make a move against that minor world immediately. Instead, he commanded the Ministry of Celestial Destiny to covertly tamper with that world's Heavenly Dao for ten thousand full years, wearing down and outliving every single ancient powerhouse before finally issuing the decree to harvest Langhuan's resources!

If one were to speak of ingratitude and treacherous perfidy, the Heavenly Court was... Ahem!

The veteran official coughed twice more.

Naturally, he could not divulge these ancient, scandalous secrets to the juniors before him. (Come to think of it, staff turnover within the Ministry of Celestial Destiny had been rather frequent; barely anyone remained who understood the true causes and consequences, even in a Heavenly Court where everyone ostensibly possessed infinite lifespan!)

Having silently cautioned himself, the veteran official steered the conversation back on track for what felt like the hundredth time:

—"At any rate, the question before us is this: why have the Ascension Stairway that collapsed ten thousand years ago and the fallen White Jade Capital reappeared in that world?"

—"Indeed,"-a reckless True Immortal chimed in, eyeing the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation:—"You didn't display the wrong place, did you?"

The precious mirror ignored the idiot.

—"Even if it were the wrong place, there wouldn't be an Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital. Besides, the precious mirror doesn't possess the function to rewind another world by ten thousand years, does it?"-another True Immortal said, eyeing the mirror uncertainly:—"You don't have that feature, right?"

The mirror ignored him as well.

—"On the other hand, speaking of Langhuan—ungrateful though it was, His Imperial Majesty would hardly be so petty. The real reason it was abandoned was surely that its world had already become unstable, yes? That was what caused the repeated temporal resets; didn't the script we authored have to run a full six times before succeeding once? So, could the reappearance of the Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital mean that this time, the rollback jumped back an excessive span—reverting straight to ten thousand years ago?"-the True Immortal pressed on with his analysis, taking the mirror's silence as assent.

—"That rollback is far too vast,"-the veteran official frowned:—"It is virtually impossible."

—"Virtually impossible does not mean completely impossible!"-the True Immortal insisted stubbornly:—"Across the three thousand worlds, nothing that happens is out of the realm of possibility!"

—"Still, a temporal rollback requires raw materials! That war back then involved not only True Immortals and demons, but countless local cultivators whose souls were completely obliterated! To faithfully reenact a historical scene is practically impossible!"-the veteran official remained unconvinced.

—"It isn't entirely impossible,"-another True Immortal offered in support:—"A dying minor world once exhibited a similar phenomenon. Granted, it wasn't a natural temporal rollback—the entire world was marching toward apocalypse, and the local deity couldn't accept it, so he forcibly rewound time with divine power. Even though countless souls had been completely scattered into oblivion, 'history' was still reenacted. All those roles were filled by the souls of currently living beings, reenacting 'history' like mindless puppets."

—"That tragedy sounds suspiciously saturated with your personal taste. Are you sure you didn't write it yourself?"-his colleague questioned.

—"Yet it succeeded! That proves the concept is viable,"-the True Immortal held his head high.

The veteran official's brow furrowed deeper.

He had no idea when the Ministry of Celestial Destiny—which had originally been established to help various minor worlds familiarize themselves with celestial rules and integrate into the cultivation system—had devolved into an agency that toyed with those worlds with such reckless abandon.

Yet he had long grown numb. One simply could not survive here otherwise.

He merely disliked the way the other substituted subjective, fabricated plots for objective reality. In the absence of an author's script, how could any world possibly summon the colossal might to abruptly rewind across so many millennia?

Could Langhuan have birthed some unnameable cosmic entity...?!

—"Alright, alright, stop arguing,"-a True Immortal mediated, cutting in just as the veteran official was about to deduce the correct conclusion from erroneous premises:—"Setting the theoretical mechanics aside for a moment, let us observe the state of White Jade Capital and the Ascension Stairway. Can't we determine whether history is rolling back simply by checking if they are reenacting 'history'?"

With that, he commanded the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation:

—"Show us the interior of White Jade Capital."

Obediently, the precious mirror shifted into a long panning shot, zooming in toward White Jade Capital and the Ascension Stairway.

That sector was evidently the epicenter of the battlefield, where combat raged even more fiercely. Yet the cultivators engaged in battle looked somewhat peculiar. They seemed inadvertently suffused with a manic excitement and a suicidal disregard for life and limb; countless cultivators charged brainlessly straight into the demon hordes, flailing wildly in chaotic brawls before being promptly shredded to pieces by the demons.

Even a True Immortal who had never set foot on a battlefield could sense a profound discordance after watching a few rounds. When forcing their "Children of Destiny" through scripted paces, they had occasionally witnessed similarly shoddy performances.

[—"These cultivators look utterly fake!"](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

[—"Exactly! Facing an authentic demon invasion, nobody could possibly be that thrilled!"](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

[—"Actually, I felt that way right from the start. That cultivator we saw earlier—didn't he sound almost joyful as he died?"](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

[—"Then again, if the hypothesis holds true—that because souls were missing, current 'history' is being cobbled together by crushing the existing world to fill the gaps—then they truly are just puppets putting on a play."](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

[—"Even for puppets, their facial expressions are overly theatrical. Some of them are practically striking dramatic poses—how revolting."](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

[—"Ugh, I'm starting to lose my appetite for this."](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

Amid the True Immortals' complaints, the imagery within the Precious Mirror of Mystic Transformation finally transitioned from the chaotic, absurd battlefield toward White Jade Capital. Before fully entering the citadel, however, they spotted a group of cultivators conducting what appeared to be a ritual atop the highest terrace.

—"Eh?"-the veteran official couldn't help but murmur upon seeing it:—"An Immortal Summoning Rite? It's been an eternity since I last saw one."

[—"What is that?"](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

—"Literally what it sounds like: a ritual petitioning our Celestial Soldiers and Heavenly Generals to descend to the mortal dust. Except this is an advanced variant—it forcibly opens a specialized conduit outside the Celestial Gate, allowing us to bypass cultivation realm restrictions and descend into that world,"-another True Immortal who possessed some knowledge of the God-Demon War explained:—"If I recall correctly, that was precisely how our immortals went to White Jade Capital back then!"

[—"So it really did roll back ten thousand years?"](# "Speaker: True Immortal")

The True Immortals immediately defaulted to this hypothesis, leaving even the veteran official at a loss for words before the "evidence."

—"Even if this world alone rewound time, even if they conduct a summoning, there wouldn't be a Heavenly Court answering their call, would there? Or could it be... they might alert the present-day Celestial Soldiers and Heavenly Generals?"-a low-ranking immortal ventured with a bizarre expression.

—"How could that be possible? Leaving aside the fact that they severed contact with the Heavenly Court long ago and there are no 'immortals' left who would answer them, the sheer fact that the Immortal Summoning Rite requires colossal energy is enough to shut out any malicious schemers. How could mortal worms of the lower realm possibly possess that caliber of pow—?!"-scoffed an official.

Right at that very moment, a terrifying suction force erupted from within the mirror!

Before the gathered immortals could react, they were violently dragged into the glass!

—"Why is this happening?!"-a True Immortal shrieked in terror.

His landing point was anything but fortunate; he plunged directly into a dense swarm of demonic beasts. Before he could raise a defensive barrier, he was ripped to shreds by demons in the blink of an eye—precisely like the cultivators he had mocked moments before.

The other True Immortals were likewise hurled in every direction across the chaotic battlefield.

The precious mirror, however, showed not the slightest inclination to sound an alarm.

Instead, it let out a satisfied burp.

A tuft of gray fur scrambled out from the mirror. With a casual flick of its hoof, it "accidentally" kicked Tian Yan's official seal—which sat reverently enshrined before the mirror—straight into the glass, before happily bounding and scampering away into the shadows.
