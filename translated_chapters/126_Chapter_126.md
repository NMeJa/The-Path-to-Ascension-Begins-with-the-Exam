# Chapter 126: The Stairway (第126章 阶梯)

The speed of the palace lantern was neither exceptionally fast nor particularly slow.

Though it drifted unhurriedly through the air, appearing as though it could dawdle along like this for an entire lifetime, in merely a quarter of an hour, it had already guided Ran Deng to the front of a building.

The structure's exterior was utterly unremarkable, looking no different from any of the other buildings around it. Tucked away in a quiet corner of the complex, it drew no attention whatsoever.

Even peering inward from outside, one could see at a glance its dilapidated interior layout, hardly resembling the dwelling of the "Hall Master" who held dominion over this entire precinct.

Had the palace lantern not led the way, Ran Deng would never have guessed this was the [array core](# "Literal CN: 阵心 (zhènxīn) — core node/center of a formation") of this entire architectural expanse.

Still, now that she had the answer, [solving the problem](# "Literal CN: 解题 (jiětí) — solving the exam problem / math problem") was child's play.

Ran Deng sized up the surrounding terrain and the cottage's exterior, then pulled out paper and brush to scribble calculations, quickly sketching an eccentric geometric diagram.

Next, retrieving several small lamps from her storage pouch, she commanded them to fly to specific coordinates outside the building according to the diagram, forming a formation. With a suave snap of her fingers, the little lamps flared to life one by one.

As the small lamps ignited, the building let out a low hum, and the floor groaned with a deep vibration. When she looked again, the floor had vanished, replaced by a flight of pristine white jade stairs where the floorboards had once rested.

[—"Now *this* looks a lot more like an 'Ascension Stairway'!"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")
Ran Deng muttered under her breath.

Watching the palace lantern drift toward the staircase, she prepared to follow.

Right then, a voice echoed from the shadows:

[—"You'd best not go over there."](# "Speaker: White Lamb")

Ran Deng paused and turned her head. In the darkness, a pair of glowing eyes was watching her.

Though slightly eerie, just like before, the little lamb-shaped silhouette betrayed its identity.

[...Ah, I almost forgot. I came here specifically to catch this critter in the first place,] Ran Deng thought.

Then again, by comparison, the room full of children was clearly far more urgent. Ran Deng hadn't even had time to seek out Master, which was why she had abandoned (forgotten about) the little lamb and followed the palace lantern straight here.

Yet the direction the palace lantern guided was unequivocally correct.

Since Ran Deng's inquiry had been "how to catch the little lamb by the tail," the lantern naturally wouldn't just lead her to rescue a bunch of bizarre children.

Sure enough, upon arriving here, the little lamb had popped up on its own.

Only, it seemed reluctant to expose its identity to Ran Deng. Squeezing its throat into a pinched falsetto, it said:

—"If you step onto that, you'll enter the trial of the Ascension Stairway—though you're already in the middle of a trial anyway."

If the sheep hadn't spoken, it might have held out; the moment it opened its mouth, Ran Deng felt a certain suspicion of hers thoroughly confirmed.

After all, they had spent nearly two centuries together!

—"...Eldest Senior Brother,"-Ran Deng called out.

—"I am not your Eldest Senior Brother,"-the little lamb continued in its pinched falsetto,-"unless you can speak my name."

[...With that tone, it's definitely him.]

Ran Deng's lips curled into a cold smirk.

—"Drop the act, Yuwen Xuan."

The moment she spoke his name, the little lamb's silhouette slowly shifted into humanoid form.

Ran Deng's heart suddenly jolted.

She inexplicably recalled the terrifying folklore regarding demons taking human shape. A 'name' was of paramount significance in the cultivation world—not merely for cultivators, but equally for demons and beasts. Furthermore, the more powerful a demon (or spirit beast) was, the more likely it was born innately possessing its own name. For instance, her Fifth Junior Brother had clearly been scavenged from who-knew-where by Eldest Senior Brother and subsequently incubated by Master. Theoretically, he should have viewed Master as his parent and taken Master's surname Zhou. Yet the instant he learned to speak, he already knew his true name was "Lu Shulong."

And Eldest Senior Brother seemed to be a similar case. No one knew where the name 'Yuwen Xuan' originated, but given this 'Eldest Senior Brother's' litany of decidedly inhuman behaviors, Ran Deng and Qu Qianxing had privately speculated that it, too, had come pre-packaged with him at birth.

[...Wait, why am I suddenly thinking about this?]

Granted, Eldest Senior Brother didn't act human, but that was back when he was young. Nowadays, Eldest Senior Brother put on a very convincing human act; he simply chose [not to be human](# "Literal CN: 不做人 (bù zuò rén) — refusing to act human / acting like a beast or troll") on occasion.

At the very least, she no longer had to worry about him eating her, right?

Ran Deng reassured herself.

Yet her nerves remained on high alert as she watched "Yuwen Xuan" step forward out of the shadows—

—"Aaaah! What on earth are you showing an [unmarried maiden](# "Literal CN: 黄花闺女 (huánghuā guīnü) — a young unmarried virgin woman")?!"-Ran Deng shrieked. Instantly forgetting all her unease, she whipped her head away with a crimson blush spreading across her cheeks.-"I'm telling Master!"

—"No, Master will get angry,"-"Yuwen Xuan" replied with absolute solemnity.

—"If you know that, then put on some clothes!"

—"I don't have clothes."

—"If you knew you'd turn into a sheep, you should have prepared clothes in advance! Is your brain filled with nothing but food?!"-Ran Deng complained. Rummaging through her storage pouch with her back to him, she hurled a garment over her shoulder.-"Hurry up and put it on!"

—"...This is different from what 'I' usually wear."

—"No kidding! Where would I get your clothes?! Put it on this instant!"-Ran Deng commanded.-"Don't flash your filthy filth at me! My eyes, wuuu wuuu wuuu..."

—"I'm not filthy filth... It's done,"-"Yuwen Xuan" said.

Hearing his declaration, Ran Deng turned her head while shielding her face, peeking through the gaps between her fingers. She was worried Yuwen Xuan had deliberately lied about being dressed while remaining stark naked. This wasn't paranoia on her part—this shameless Eldest Senior Brother of hers was completely capable of such a stunt.

Fortunately, he had properly donned the garment she had provided:

A tender-yellow, wide-sleeved [immortal-staying gown](# "Literal CN: 留仙裙 (liúxiān qún) — classic pleated silk gown named after Han dynasty court lore").

This wasn't Ran Deng playing a prank on him either. After all, just as she had pointed out, why would an unmarried maiden keep men's clothing in her storage pouch? Even this dress was only packed because she knew she was traveling with Master; she had stashed it away to avoid wearing the exact same outfit day after day if they couldn't return within a few days, which might leave a poor impression on Master. Now that Yuwen Xuan had worn it directly against his bare skin, it was essentially ruined. At the very least, recalling his naked state just moments ago, Ran Deng had zero desire to ever wear it again—and she had actually quite liked this dress, too.

In that case, she might as well make the best of a bad bargain!

For instance, capturing Yuwen Xuan's [dark history](# "Literal CN: 黑历史 (hēi lìshǐ) — embarrassing past / cringe moments") from this very moment and bringing it back for the other junior brothers and sisters to admire.

With that thought in mind, Ran Deng deliberately aimed an image-recording magical artifact at Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan cooperated completely, striking a bold pose.

[...Does this man have no self-respect whatsoever? Why is he acting so thoroughly righteous?!]

Ran Deng lowered the recording artifact, all interest evaporating.

Then again, watching Yuwen Xuan in drag wasn't particularly amusing to begin with—he looked far too natural.

And this "natural" was entirely different from Qu Qianxing's potential brand of "natural."

Ran Deng was confident Qu Qianxing would look stunning in women's attire, probably even prettier than she did. But that beauty held its own unique comedic value—especially considering that unless Master explicitly commanded it, Second Senior Brother would rather die than compromise his pride by donning a dress, which made the prospect of Qu Qianxing in drag all the more delectable.

Yuwen Xuan's "natural," on the other hand, was utterly bland.

The man's features bore zero resemblance to a woman's, and wearing a dress did nothing to change that. Paired with his unrepentant, chest-puffing swagger, the exquisite immortal-staying gown on his frame was reduced to a mere scrap of cloth, devoid of even a shred of comedic contrast.

On him, virtually any garment transformed into the battle attire of an imposing, broad-shouldered young hero.

Seeing Ran Deng put away the recording artifact, "Yuwen Xuan" actually had the gall to ask:

—"Aren't you going to continue?"

[Continue what, exactly?!]

Still, possessing skin this thick was definitely Eldest Senior Brother.

Ran Deng had no desire to feel the comforting reality of her senior brother's presence in such a bizarre fashion. Refusing to entangle herself in this pointless banter, she resumed the earlier topic:

—"What was that test you were talking about just now?"

"Yuwen Xuan" appeared quite disappointed.

Nevertheless, he answered her question obediently:

—"The trial of the Ascension Stairway, of course! You should know about it, right? Whoever walks upon the Ascension Stairway will undergo a trial, embarking upon the path to ascension."

—"Ah? I think there is such a tale,"-Ran Deng said blankly.

She nervously glanced around.

—"Is that trial still functional? How do you know about it? Wait a second, I'm not even on the Ascension Stairway right now! If anyone is, Master is the one on the Ascension Stairway!"

—"The moment your feet touch the Ascension Stairway, it signifies that the trial has commenced,"-"Yuwen Xuan" explained in detail.-"Furthermore, in order to maximize its power, foolish mortals expanded its boundaries. Nearly all the architecture here was constructed from it, so as long as you remain within this perimeter, you will undergo the trial. As for Master, he is an exception because he has already passed another trial and become the master of that place. I am also an exception, because I am inherently a higher-order being from the upper realm. Only you—"

"Yuwen Xuan" pointed right at Ran Deng:

—"—an ordinary mortal, are naturally a subject of the test."

[A higher-order being from the upper realm?]

[Have you finally stopped hiding your true monster nature?]

Ran Deng thought, rendered completely speechless.

The current Yuwen Xuan repeatedly reminded her of the Yuwen Xuan of two hundred years ago. Back then, although Master insisted he was human, and anatomically speaking he roughly passed as one, his behavior couldn't be described as human by any stretch of the imagination.

This was precisely why both Qu Qianxing and Ran Deng held zero reverence for this "Eldest Senior Brother."

Who would have thought he truly wasn't human!

It had taken Master two centuries of painstaking education to make him resemble a person.

Now, it seemed Master's efforts had all been in vain.

[This guy belongs hanging from a peach tree for the rest of his life.]

Ran Deng rolled her eyes and ignored Yuwen Xuan, turning toward the stairs.

—"I already told you it's a test, and you're still going down?"-"Yuwen Xuan" asked, eyeing her actions with a bizarre expression.

—"Out of my way, Eldest Senior Brother,"-Ran Deng said with zero respect.-"Unlike you, I'm very busy. There's an entire room of children waiting for me to save them! As for you, running around wherever you please—watch out or Master will get angry."

Now that she knew the little black lamb was Eldest Senior Brother, Ran Deng wasn't worried about him in the slightest.

In any case, this creature would trot back to Master on its own.

The children were far more important.

—"It's not just one room of children. If you're referring to what you saw earlier, there are more than a dozen similar facilities here,"-"Yuwen Xuan" said, his eyes darting.-"The experiments differ, but you surely weren't naive enough to believe there was only one?"

—"Doesn't that make it even more urgent?!"-Ran Deng said.

She strode briskly onto the stairs.

In that very instant, her mind flashed to the five pounds she had gained recently.

As a Golden Core female cultivator, she had actually gained five whole pounds!

It had to be the consequence of recent late-night manuscript reviews + milk tea + endless snacking while editing + neglecting cultivation for far too long!

Even though she had resolved to swallow [inedia pills](# "Literal CN: 辟谷丹 (bìgǔ dān) — fasting pills that sustain the body without food"), the moment she arrived at Master's courtyard, she couldn't resist devouring an entire box of pastries for breakfast. It was all Fifth Junior Brother's fault for being such an incredible baker, wuuu wuuu wuuu...?!

—"What on earth am I thinking about?!"

Ran Deng mumbled, stepping down onto the second step.

Consequently, her thoughts immediately drifted to how she had published Ruopei Fairy's gossip article in *White Jade Capital Weekly* purely for the drama, knowing full well it would create a headache for Master and infuriate Second Senior Brother. She wondered how chaotic things had gotten by now...

—"*Hiss*, does this staircase force people to remember whatever they're worried about?"-Ran Deng said, the corner of her mouth twitching.

—"Precisely. That's what I was about to tell you,"-"Yuwen Xuan" said.-"Because the Ascension Stairway induces all sorts of trials and negative illusions that foolish mortals cannot resist, they sought ways to expand its territory while shunting the unmanageable side effects to a fixed location—which is right here."

"Yuwen Xuan" watched Ran Deng's feet with keen interest.

—"Walking down this flight of stairs, mortals are inevitably forced to look back upon their entire lives. Some may lose the courage to ever ascend the heavenly stairs; others, conversely, receive unprecedented inspiration. Which one are you? Personally, I think—"

—"Is whoever runs this place insane?!"-Ran Deng interrupted before he could finish gloating, her face filled with shock.-"Putting a contraption like this right at the entrance they walk through every single day?!"

—"Because he doesn't need to tread these stairs to enter! This was originally an anti-theft security measure against intruders. As for the master himself, he has other methods of descending."-"Yuwen Xuan" leaned close to Ran Deng's face as he spoke.-"For example, down below lies a [life tablet](# "Literal CN: 命牌 (mìngpái) — jade tablet binding a cultivator's life force or authority"). As long as you ignite it with your soul fire, you can seize control over this entire realm, eliminating the need to trudge down these stairs."

—"And then?"-Ran Deng took a step back.

—"If you're hellbent on saving those brats, you can light your lamp with your soul fire, and I'll take it down below. Remember? I'm immune to the stairway's effects. I can ignite the life tablet on your behalf, handing you full authority over this precinct."

—"Why don't you light the life tablet with your own soul fire?"

—"Because *you're* the one who wants to do it, not me,"-"Yuwen Xuan" replied.

[...] [Eldest Senior Brother really is more obnoxious than Second Senior Brother.]

With a stiff expression, Ran Deng stepped down onto the third step.

She remembered how, a week ago, she had secretly coaxed Master's ink monkey into helping her print the newspaper.

She had had no choice—the print run had simply been too enormous.

She wondered if Master had found out yet.

—"Don't need a hand?"-"Yuwen Xuan" circled around his junior sister.-"Handing over your soul fire is so much easier! No walking, super fast—aren't you in a rush to get down?"

—"No thanks. Refining this palace lantern with spiritual energy is already exhausting enough; if I had to use soul fire, wouldn't that be even more draining? I'd rather take my time walking down."

—"That might not hold true,"-"Yuwen Xuan" chuckled.-"The memories you think you have aren't the entirety of your recollections. Walking down like this, you might just unearth things you'd rather keep buried."

—"How could that be?"-Ran Deng retorted as she continued downward.

Another suppressed memory flared in her mind.

Yet it was still a "trifling" little detail:

Ran Deng remembered that half a month ago, she had swiped one of Fourth Junior Brother's magical artifacts to use and hadn't found an opportunity to return it. She hoped that guy Ye-zi wouldn't whine if he found out—he complained far too much as it was.

—Then again, this was Ran Deng.

She considered herself completely free of psychological trauma; all her troubles were trifling matters of everyday life.

In this regard, Ran Deng was well aware that she was actually the most "unique" disciple under Master's tutelage.

After all, under the Untiring Immortal Venerable's lineage, the disciples were either orphans of unknown origin with neither father nor mother (Yuwen Xuan and Lu Shulong: *...*), wretched souls betrayed by the entire world (Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye: *...*), or both at once—orphaned *and* betrayed by the world (Mo Xi and Mo Bei: *...*).

Ran Deng, however, was different.

Ran Deng had a family, relatives, friends, and healthy social relationships.

Though her family brought a certain degree of pressure, it was undeniable that her parents and kin loved her dearly, just as Master, her senior brothers, and her junior siblings loved her.

Ran Deng knew this was the reason Master was particularly "gentle" with her.

For her fellow disciples to obtain what they desired and secure a brighter future, they had to strive with monstrous effort. But Ran Deng was different. As the heiress of an esteemed array-master clan, once she came of age, she would directly inherit her father's Mahayana cultivation realm. She could effortlessly attain everything others bled and sweated to achieve.

Furthermore, while Ran Deng loved slacking off, at the end of the day she merely felt overwhelmed by everyone's high expectations. She had never rebelled with notions like *I refuse to inherit the family business, I refuse to marry, I'm going to start my own venture!* On the contrary, she actually felt everything in the Ran Clan was quite wonderful.

Though the Untiring Immortal Venerable was the undisputed master of [hyper-involution](# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn) — academic grinding / hyper-competitive striving"), he maintained that involution must have a clear purpose.

In Ran Deng's case, had she harbored personal ambitions, that would be one thing. But since she lacked the inclination, there was no reason to sacrifice her life upon the altar of other people's expectations.

She only needed to master sufficient knowledge and technical skill, methodically becoming a competent head of the Ran Clan. When the timing was right, she could find a partner she liked to produce the next "A-Ran." And if she fancied no one, that was perfectly fine too—after all, every descendant of the Ran Clan was an "A-Ran"; there was no absolute necessity to pin the clan's future onto Ran Deng's biological offspring.

As A-Ran, as Ran Deng, she needed only to live a life she found satisfying and without regret.

This was Zhou Hui's wish for all of his disciples.

And for that, Ran Deng was profoundly grateful to Master.

She just wasn't sure why Master seemed to have ratcheted up his academic demands on everyone lately.

[Still, it's Master. Everything he does has a reason. As long as I follow his instructions, nothing can go wrong!]

Ran Deng thought with absolute trust.

Hitching up the skirt of her gown and ignoring Yuwen Xuan's nonsensical warnings, she descended several more steps, dredging up a series of inconsequential memories that caused her more embarrassment than pain.

To outrun these awkward recollections, Ran Deng actually quickened her pace.

She took the opportunity to toss a warning over her shoulder:

—"Since you know it's a trial, stop pestering me! If anything happens to me, it'll be entirely your fault, and I'll go back and tell Master."

—"Hmph, telling Master again, is it?"

—"Because that's the only thing that works on you!"

—"Then again, since your snitching will definitely make Master angry, and Master will be angry either way, wouldn't it be simpler to just eliminate you—a loose variable?"-"Yuwen Xuan" mused.-"Frankly, seeing how terrified that fellow is of 'Master getting angry' only makes me wonder what would actually happen if Master were thoroughly enraged."

—"What?"

That sounded completely wrong. Ran Deng tried to turn around, but a violent shove slammed into her back.

Her footing vanished, and she tumbled headlong down the stairs.

Naturally, as a Golden Core cultivator, Ran Deng wasn't going to perish from something as absurd as falling down a flight of stairs. Otherwise, wouldn't her two centuries of life be a laughingstock?

Ran Deng's body immediately turned light as a feather, drifting downward in gentle swings. She even had the presence of mind to adjust the palace lantern's flame so it wouldn't be extinguished by the fall.

What genuinely stunned Ran Deng was Yuwen Xuan's action.

Although this Eldest Senior Brother was wildly unreliable and constantly caused chaos, he had never once made a move to genuinely harm her—or any of their siblings! In that split second just now, she had clearly felt palpable malice radiating from him!

Even as Ran Deng pondered this, a massive shadow loomed over her from behind.

Ran Deng whipped around in terror, only to see a gaping, black-hole maw opening directly behind her.

—"Baaa-baaa!"—*Don't touch my junior sister!*

Just then, a strange cry rang out.

A black silhouette plummeted downward like a cannonball, slamming into the giant mouth! The impact knocked the maw back into the likeness of Yuwen Xuan, before collapsing him from Yuwen Xuan into a tiny furball that, along with the black silhouette, plunged straight into Ran Deng's bosom!

—"Aaaah!"-Ran Deng shrieked.

Though the two lumps were tiny, for reasons unknown their weight felt heavier than two full-grown men! Ran Deng could no longer maintain her feather-light state; she could only slap a defensive barrier around her physical body and tumble down the stairs in utter despair.

Worse yet, as she rolled downward, suppressed memories flared to life one after another within her mind:

One year ago: that feathered robe she had hesitated to buy, regretting it to this very day;

Ten years ago: taking advantage of Master's absence to sleep for three solid months without touching a single shred of homework, only to be hung from a tree by an enraged Master right next to Eldest Senior Brother, who had caused another disaster;

One hundred years ago: misjudging a calculation in the library pavilion and nearly starving herself to death;

Two hundred years ago: clinging to Master's thigh and refusing to go home;

Three hundred years ago: a previous life...

A previous life?

Why would there be a previous life?!

No—not just one previous life. A life before that, and a life before that life, and a life before that...

—"Wuuu... Aaaaaaah!"

Ran Deng clutched her head and screamed.

***

A flight of stairs that felt several lifetimes long to Ran Deng was a matter of mere seconds for the little black lamb... Yuwen Xuan.

The instant it hit the ground, it shook its fleece, scrambled to its feet, and glared to the side.

Over there, the little white lamb had been beaten back to its original form by his flawless strike. Still dizzy from the forced reversion, it wobbled unsteadily on its hooves.

The little black lamb had zero intention of being polite. Opening its mouth wide, it lunged straight in for a vicious bite.

[—"Baaa!"](# "Speaker: White Lamb")

The white lamb reacted instantly, dodging the black lamb's snapping teeth and biting right back without hesitation.

Thus, the black lamb and white lamb descended into a kindergarten-tier brawl, black and white tumbling together into a messy heap, churning like a pot of boiling Taiji.

Right as their skirmish reached a stalemate, a commotion nearby caught the black lamb's attention.

The black lamb stole a glance and froze, getting a mouthful of wool bitten off by the little white lamb in the process.

Yet the little black lamb couldn't care less about that, staring nervously at Ran Deng:

[L-Little Junior Sister is crying?!]

[Why is she crying so miserably?!]

[Back when I swallowed her entire storage pouch, she didn't cry like this!]

[Did she bump her head just now?]

[Or is she furious?!]

[I'm dead! If she keeps crying like this, Master is going to boil me into a mutton hotpot!]

Frantic to reach Ran Deng's side, the little black lamb completely overlooked the white lamb opening its gaping maw right behind him. Right at that moment, still weeping uncontrollably, Ran Deng abruptly whipped her arm around and hurled a lantern pole straight at them, ramming it with pinpoint precision directly into the white lamb's open mouth!

The lantern pole instantly expanded, skewering the white lamb into a [mutton skewer](# "Literal CN: 羊肉串 (yángròu chuàn) — lamb kebab / skewer"), and burst into roaring flames.

The little black lamb shot a terrified glance at the flaming white mutton skewer, then turned back to his hysterically sobbing junior sister:

[Is she hurt, or is she fine?!]
