# Chapter 156: Chain Reaction (第156章 连锁反应)

Thunder.

Thunder potent enough to shatter all of creation.

Light.

Light fierce enough to obliterate everything into ash.

Beneath a rock, Pichi cowered, shivering uncontrollably.

Pichi was originally a native lesser fiend born in the Demon Realm. Typically, it drifted aimlessly about like a mayfly, scavenging whatever emotional dregs tumbled down from the mortal realm to feed upon.

It was also from these emotional dregs that Pichi had learned of the concept known as "human."

Naturally, this understanding was thoroughly rudimentary. Then again, Pichi did not even possess a head in the human sense; it neither cared nor wondered what manner of creature a human truly was. To it, knowing that "human" equated to "food" was more than enough.

In truth, the vast majority of demonic entities were just like this, which made it practically impossible for mortals to glean any coherent picture of what the Demon Realm was truly like from the mouths of fiends.

If Zhou Hui were to explain it, the Demon Realm and the mortal realm were two dimensional spaces overlapping with one another, separated merely by a spatial "membrane." This membrane was exceedingly thin and utterly incapable of completely isolating the two worlds; consequently, they exerted constant mutual interference upon each other.

Among all things, emotion and desire were the least hindered by this spatial "membrane."

Should human emotions run just a fraction stronger, they could permeate right through the "membrane" and seep into the Demon Realm.

Thus, in the eyes of lesser fiends like Pichi who sprouted bizarre sensory organs, these emotions were akin to some manner of fruit, ripening in fixed territories (corresponding to regions of human activity) for them to devour.

Some lesser fiends had even evolved specialized organs to harvest these "fruits" more efficiently, allowing them to parasitize the specific zones where the "fruits" grew, using their own secretions as fertilizer to nourish the extra-dimensional creatures bearing the "fruits."

These secretions were known as "desire"—and these fiends were precisely what were termed [inner demons](# "Literal CN: 心魔 (xīnmó) — mental demons, inner devils, or psychological fiends").

Since inner demons could secrete desire, they were naturally agglomerations of desire themselves. Greedy and devoid of restraint, they craved more and more fruit. Once they learned of humanity through these fruits, they no longer coveted merely the harvest, but hungered to seize the very humans who produced it.

Yet for the most part, constrained by dimensions and spatial boundaries, inner demons were separated from mortals by that barrier. They could only stimulate mortals with desire, driving their prey mad, and then orchestrate slaughter by hijacking their emotions—spurring the yield of even more emotional dregs.

What truly sent demonic creatures into frenzied ecstasy, however, was when the spatial membrane warped and ruptured.

Such opportunities were exceedingly rare. Yet every emergence of a spatial fissure heralded a savage carnival for fiends: they would flood into that world in full force, devouring and parasitizing every living organism within, until that realm too degenerated into part of the Demon Realm.

Regarding such a "banquet," Pichi had merely heard rumors. If it had ever partaken in one, it would at least be the chieftain of a swarm by now—rumor had it that the matriarch-chieftain of its own brood had spawned her entire lineage after invading a minor realm. Pichi yearned desperately for such a day to arrive.

Recently, that long-awaited opportunity seemed to have finally arrived.

The distant winds carried a delicious scent Pichi had never smelled before. It seemed an immense abundance of "fruit" was blooming, and rumors quickly spread that the pathway to the mortal realm had blown open. Demons and fiends swarmed toward that direction like locusts.

Pichi was no exception.

It could hardly wait to flutter into the mortal realm and gorge itself silly.

However, Pichi's flight was far from swift, leaving it lagging behind the other fiends.

Moreover, what made Pichi mutter with unease was that it caught the scent of an insect hive from afar. A nest of Brown-winged Fei bugs seemed to reside near the spatial fissure. According to Pichi's knowledge, the Brown-winged clan was notoriously domineering; if they occupied the entrance, they should have packed the vicinity of the fissure entirely with their spawn, leaving no room for a puny fiend like Pichi to swoop in and reap any scraps! What seemed even stranger was that of all the other fiends that had surged into the mortal realm, not a single one had returned.

When Pichi voiced its doubts, the other fiends sneered at its cowardice. In their eyes, the mortal realm was overflowing with delicacies—why would anyone return to the cramped, desolate Demon Realm? The fact that no fiend had returned was entirely normal!

Pichi half-believed this explanation for the time being, yet a lingering sense of foreboding gnawed at it, prompting it to fly even slower.

And fortunate it was that it flew so sluggishly. On this day, just as it spotted the fissure from the distance alongside a colossal structure erected by mortals—another point that puzzled Pichi, as this was the first time it had ever seen mortals constructing buildings inside the Demon Realm—before Pichi could even flutter close, a blinding light flared from the direction of the mortal realm, accompanied by a deafening roar.

Terrified out of its wits, Pichi scrambled to find cover and hide.

It proved to be the right decision, for alongside the ignition of that brilliant luminescence, all those foolish fiends still hovering or crawling out in the open were instantaneously incinerated into ash.

Pichi pressed flat beneath its rock, petrified, not daring to twitch a muscle, let alone steal a glance outside.

The thunder pealed in ceaseless, rolling concussions—to Pichi, it felt as long as an entire lifetime.

Yet this was not what frightened Pichi the most. What terrified it to the core was that after the sixth round of thunderous strikes, something vaguely slammed down near the rock where Pichi hid, rolling over with a hollow *clack-clack-clack*.

Pichi dared not indulge its curiosity; it wanted nothing more than to pretend it didn't exist. Yet that object kept tumbling closer and closer until it came to a dead stop right next to its hiding spot.

It was then that Pichi caught sight of the thing that had rolled over.

It was a head.

The severed head of a Demon General.

In the taxonomy of fiends, Demon Generals were high-ranking demonic entities possessing humanoid forms, sitting comfortably at the absolute apex of the food chain.

Strangely enough, entities of this caliber rarely appeared at such sealed mortal-demon borders. They either dwelled in the abyssal depths of the Demon Realm where demonic miasma was thickest, or waged war on the frontlines of various battlefields.

Yet Pichi had no luxury for curiosity; after all, a mere exhale from this creature could blow it to smithereens.

Pichi immediately squeaked in terror, frantically trying to burrow into the deeper soil beneath.

Alas, burrowing was not its forte. Despite clawing and digging frantically for ages, it failed to sink even an inch. Fortunately, the Demon General's severed head seemed to have no intention of devouring it to replenish demonic power:

—"Stop digging,"-the Demon General said with helpless exasperation.-"A puny worm like you wouldn't restore much power even if I swallowed you whole. Still, since you know enough to hide, you must possess at least a sliver of sapience! If you survive this, I need you to travel to the western frontline and deliver a message—"

—"I—I can't do that!"-Pichi shrieked.

The Demon General sneered mockingly at the tiny insect, seemingly about to say something.

At that exact moment, another burst of thunder pealed, accompanied by blinding radiance.

This flash of light was so overwhelming it threatened to annihilate everything in existence, shearing away three full feet of topsoil in an instant.

Nor did Pichi escape unscathed.

It felt the rock above crumble into dust, while its own pitiful little wings disintegrated. Yet at the critical juncture, the Demon General collapsed entirely into a cloud of black mist, blanketing the tiny insect and shielding it from complete obliteration.

—It wasn't out of any fraternal affection between fiends, but rather that the Demon General's dying power was only sufficient to shield such a minuscule bug; it couldn't protect a head of its own size at all.

What was more, its preserving this little bug came with no benevolent intentions whatsoever.

In fact, once the blinding glare faded, Pichi peered at its surroundings with eyes stripped of lucid thought.

Having lost its wings, its vitality had plummeted to the absolute brink, leaving behind nothing but raw survival instinct.

And so, it spotted a tiny demon core resting right before it—the Demon General's final remnant, preserved within that black mist. Driven almost purely by instinct, Pichi swallowed the demon core whole.

As expected of a Demon General's core, Pichi's wings sprouted back instantaneously, grander and far more gorgeous than before.

Sapience reignited within its eyes as well, though far more chaotic than before:

[Who am I? I am Pichi, the remnant of a Demon General.]

[Where did I come from? I came from the depths of the Demon Realm, sent to inspect the condition of the spatial fissure and confirm the territory promised to us by the True Immortals following the last war.]

[Where am I going? I must go to the frontlines of the Demon-God Battlefield, find Lord Demon Sovereign, and inform him that everything has spiraled out of control! The mortals of this world have gone utterly mad! They actually want to invade the Demon Realm! We need military reinforcement!]

Pichi ran these memories through its nonexistent brain, thoroughly convinced to the marrow of its being that these were its own genuine thoughts.

Accompanying these realizations, an exquisite, peerlessly handsome figure of a human manifested within its memories.

Do not be surprised; fiends possessed their own sense of aesthetics too.

In truth, in order to deceive mortals more effectively, the aesthetics they cultivated were not only closely aligned with human standards, but went even deeper—designed precisely to stimulate humanity's deepest, most primal desires.

And whenever a fiend assumed human form, the more flawlessly it aligned with this aesthetic, the more it proved its formidable strength.

Therefore, purely from appearance alone, that exquisite human ignited an instinctual urge within Pichi: it *wanted* him.

Yet immediately afterward, Pichi shuddered violently.

For it had simultaneously realized that this breathtaking human was an existence it must never, under any circumstance, approach.

For at the moment the heavenly thunder had detonated, it—the Demon General—had managed with great difficulty to confirm this fact right at the brink of the fissure.

Not only was this human the lunatic invading the Demon Realm, but he was also the very one who had summoned the heavenly lightning moments ago, incinerating every fiend inside and outside this region into ash—and bringing down even a mighty Demon General.

...No, the general hadn't completely perished. After all, wasn't Pichi still standing right here?!

—"I have to report this to Lord Demon Sovereign,"-Pichi muttered under its breath.

Fluttering its newly grown, gorgeous wings, it took off in the opposite direction of the Demon Realm fissure, heading toward the "frontline of the Demon-God Battlefield" etched in its memories.

Yet along the way, a massive horde of fiends was mindlessly surging toward the fissure.

Although the heavenly thunder and radiant glare had obliterated all fiends within that zone, they had also alerted fiends across much greater distances to the existence of this spatial fissure.

Unaware of their kin's deaths—or simply not caring if they knew—these demonic beasts were like [insects drawn to light](# "Literal CN: 趋光的虫子 — phototactic bugs rushing blindly toward illumination, heedless of the flame"), throwing themselves forward in endless waves toward the borders of the mortal realm, heedless of paving the path with their own lives.

In an instant, tremors of unrest rippled across the entire Demon Realm.

---

—"Huh? This 'reward'... even I get one?"-Zhou Hui remarked in surprise as the Demon Realm roiled in unrest.

Originally, he had been delighted that his disciple had successfully advanced realms and was just preparing to map out the next stage of study curriculum for him, only to sense that, in tandem with Qu Qianxing's breakthrough, his own power had also surged forward by an entire tier.

To be precise, it was his "godhead" that had suddenly received an upgrade.

—"Well of course you do!"-that mysterious voice piped up once again.-"Lord Immortal, did you truly not know? This is the reward your world is gifting back to you."
