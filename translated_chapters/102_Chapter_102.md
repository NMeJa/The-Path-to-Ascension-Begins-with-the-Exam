# Chapter 102: [Yin Spirit, Heroic Spirit](<# "Literal CN: 阴灵英灵 (yīnlíng yīnglíng) — Yin Spirit, Heroic Spirit; homophone pun on malicious wraiths (阴灵) vs noble departed warriors (英灵); chapter title">) (第102章 阴灵英灵)

Fengqing City, a city marching toward its death.

Not by murder.

Rather, of natural old age.

It had weathered tens of thousands of years.

Taking the city walls alone, they had undergone several major overhauls and countless minor repairs. Not only had their appearance and materials long ceased to resemble the original, but even their physical location had shifted from where it once stood.

The interior of the city was even more so. Whether roads or residences, patching the new atop the old, restoring the old atop the new—the result was an inability to preserve its original form, yet nothing new could sprout forth. The entire city resembled an elderly soul with [half their body already in the dirt](<# "Literal CN: 半截入土 (bàn jié rù tǔ) — half buried in the earth; with one foot in the grave">), gray and dreary, sustained only by the final breath hanging from the massive cross-realm teleportation array within the City Lord's estate.

Thus, despite bearing the name Fengqing—City of Pure Breeze—there was not a single speck of "purity" to be found.

Witnessing this scene, a voice sighed:

—"Human dwellings are always like this. No matter how magnificent in their prime, after so many years, they are no longer what they once were."

—"Is that so? I think it's quite lively!"-the young girl replied cheerfully.

This girl bore an azure feathered crest atop her head, a pair of ears formed entirely of plumage, and long tail feathers trailing behind her. One look made it abundantly clear she was no human, drawing constant sideways glances from people on both sides of the street.

Yet it was hard to tell whether what captured people's gaze was the girl's demon race heritage, or her lovely face.

After all, demons were hardly a rare sight in Fengqing City.

The last massive cross-realm teleportation array in Fengqing City connected directly to the Southern Realm. The Southern Realm was a continent where humans and demons lived side by side, with the demon population even outnumbering the humans—the demons held that the Southern Realm had originally been their ancestral land, and humans were merely interlopers. During the Demon God War, it was precisely through the teleportation array here that they arrived in the Middle Realm to take part in the conflict. Consequently, the surrounding regions of Fengqing City were home to many humans bearing traces of demon blood, such as Qiao Family Village.

Nowadays, merchants arriving in the Middle Realm via the teleportation array were predominantly demons or half-demons due to geographic convenience. Strolling along the streets of Fengqing City, one could frequently spot figures displaying beast-like traits.

The girl was one of them.

However, her features and traits were evidently quite exceptional even among demons; simply walking down the street was enough to draw everyone's eyes.

The girl seemed accustomed to such treatment. Paying no heed to the surrounding stares, she peered curiously around the city, murmuring to herself from time to time:

—"Though their living quarters aren't great, humans have always loved building their dwellings out of dead matter! As long as they themselves are lively, isn't that nice enough?"

—"That is because you have visited far too few human cities,"-a voice chimed in the girl's mind. The timbre was melodious, yet carried the nagging cadence of an old elder.-"Though humans perpetually dream of detaching from the earth, they were born of the earth after all. Their vitality is intertwined with their dwellings. When the city they inhabit is dying, they fare no better; they wither away alongside it."

—"Those people too?"-The girl casually pointed toward a crowd gathered outside a shop not far away.

Those people were engaged in heated discussion.

Some argued until their faces flushed red, some beamed with broad smiles, and others bowed their heads in deep contemplation. They looked remarkably vibrant. Even from afar, words like "periodical," "map," "cultivation method," and "victor" drifted over, making it sound like they were debating something immensely entertaining.

And such people were by no means rare.

Coming out of the teleportation array, the area around the City Lord's estate had indeed felt somewhat decrepit and gloomy, but that was perhaps what human nobility called 'decorum'? Once past that quarter, one found these vibrant humans growing ever more numerous.

Or so the girl believed.

Yet the voice in her mind did not agree with her view in the slightest, prattling on endlessly:

—"Those people are merely the final [terminal lucidity](<# "Literal CN: 回光返照 (huí guāng fǎn zhào) — the last radiance of the setting sun / sudden rally before death">) of this dying city. Clearly some event is being held recently, which stirred up their enthusiasm. But even with an event, it pales in comparison to the past! I still remember when I came to the Middle Realm all those years ago—also right here in Fengqing City. There was an event back then too, but it was incomparably more bustling than now! Even the daughter of the Holy Sovereign attended in person, passing overhead through the sky—"

The girl continued craning her neck in every direction, treating the voice as mere [wind passing by her ears](<# "Literal CN: 耳边风 (ěr biān fēng) — in one ear and out the other">).

There was no helping it; she had heard this story so many times she felt she could recite it by heart.

It was precisely because of this that she had developed an enormous fascination with humanity and ancient history, intentionally choosing the decayed Middle Realm for her trial.

For now, though this city was not as wondrous as she had envisioned, the novelty of visiting a human settlement for the very first time was more than enough for her to overlook its flaws.

Yet the girl's naive, world-ignorant demeanor seemed to attract some unsavory stares.

Several [street loafers](<# "Literal CN: 街溜子 (jiēliūzi) — street ruffians / idling hooligans">) exchanged malicious glances and ambled toward the girl. Before they could close the distance, however, magnificent celestial music resonated from the sky. It caused them to freeze in their tracks and crane their necks upward, only to be met by a gust of wind laced with sparkling, crystalline snowflakes blowing against their faces.

In southern Fengqing City, where snow never fell year-round, this was an exceedingly rare sight.

[—"What happened?"](<# "Speaker: Bystander">)

—"H-Heavens, there's an army in the sky!"-someone gasped in shock.

Just as everyone witnessed, accompanied by celestial music and dancing snowflakes, an army of over a hundred troops soared across the sky.

Perhaps in the cultivation world this was not an extraordinary spectacle; the private legions of some immortal cities were colossal. Fengqing City, for instance, maintained a standing army that nominally boasted a hundred thousand soldiers—though everyone knew it would be a miracle if the actual headcount even reached one thousand.

Yet this army in the heavens was entirely different.

Every single soldier stood over two meters tall, postures erect and majestic, radiating a soft golden glow. Even under the bright daylight, they resembled a moving string of radiant pearls. What astonished onlookers most was their absolute uniformity of movement—it was as if they shared a single body. The friction of their armor clinked in identical rhythm, blending seamlessly as part of that grand celestial symphony.

Thus, this hundred-man unit flew through the heavens with the overwhelming momentum of tens of thousands.

At first, the denizens of Fengqing City clamored noisily, speculating about the origin of this army. Gradually, their voices quieted into hushed whispers. By the end, everyone seemed to forget how to speak, staring dumbfoundedly at the sky like [ducks held by the neck](<# "Literal CN: 被提着脖子的鸭子 (bèi tí zhe bōzi de yāzi) — stunned and silenced like ducks lifted by their scruffs">).

The demon girl was an exception.

Gazing at the firmament, the demon girl grew even more excited:

—"Such formidable spiritual pressure! So this is a human army! Elder Tong Ling, that daughter of the Holy Sovereign you saw back then—did she possess this kind of grandeur too? Just like the commander of this army?"

This was the most astonishing aspect of all: the commander of this formidable army was a girl.

A petite girl.

Had she not been mounted atop a white steed that likewise radiated golden light, her diminutive figure would have been completely swallowed up by the towering soldiers... No, not necessarily, for a beam of blinding spotlight poured straight down from directly above her head, as if reminding the entire world of the commander-in-chief's presence!

In the eyes of others, this sight was rather bizarre, but the demon girl held no such preconceived notions.

—"So majestic! I want an army like that too,"-the demon girl said enviously.

The voice in her mind addressed as "Elder Tong Ling" paused, then said in a strange tone:

—"Something is wrong."

—"What's wrong?"-she asked.

—"That girl is a [yin spirit](<# "Literal CN: 阴灵 (yīnlíng) — yin spirit / wraith / ghost born of extreme negative yin energy">)!"

—"What's wrong with the commander of [heroic spirits](<# "Literal CN: 英灵 (yīnglíng) — heroic spirits / valorous souls of fallen heroes (homophone with 阴灵 yīnlíng)">) being a heroic spirit?"-the demon girl asked, even more bewildered.

Indeed, the demon girl had already discerned that this army was not composed of living mortals, but heroic spirits.

Heroic spirits were generally formed from fallen heroes.

Unlike ordinary human ghosts that lingered out of resentment and unwillingness to pass on, heroic spirits typically coalesced from a powerful will to safeguard a realm. Their true forms had long since reincarnated or dissolved into nothingness, yet a fraction of their soul persisted through sheer willpower. If continuously supplied with spiritual power, they could be preserved indefinitely.

However, in a cultivation world with loose social structures and no mortal nations to speak of, the conditions required to birth heroic spirits were exceptionally harsh. Their numbers were exceedingly sparse, let alone gathering enough to form an entire legion.

Hence, ordinary cultivators dared not even consider that the aerial unit was an army of heroic spirits. Only the demon girl, unburdened by common sense and blessed with ancestral memories of fighting alongside human heroic spirits during antiquity, recognized them immediately.

Elder Tong Ling retorted:

—"Not *that* kind of spirit! The girl is a *yin* spirit... the 'yin' of yin energy! The vile, malicious wraiths spawned only in wretched places crawling with vengeful specters and foul demons!"

—"?! Then this army—is it an illusion disguised by vengeful ghosts?"-the demon girl asked in shock.

—"No, the army is genuine heroic spirits, and their attire even matches the soldiers of ancient White Jade Capital... But how could that be? Why would soldiers of White Jade Capital obey the command of a yin spirit? Even the daughter of the Holy Sovereign back then couldn't command them! Could I be mistaken?"-Elder Tong Ling's voice wavered with hesitation before he sighed helplessly.-"Shui Huang'er, open your [Heavenly Eye](<# "Literal CN: 天目 (tiānmù) — spiritual vision of the Azure Phoenix Clan">) and take a look."

—"..."-Shui Huang'er nodded.

She widened her azure eyes, wherein flame-like luminescence ignited.

Beneath the gaze of these eyes, the soldiers appeared even more radiant, resembling orbs of golden light. Only the "commanding girl" proved to be an exception.

The girl's physical vessel was semi-translucent—half human flesh, half spirit body. Together, the two formed a medium akin to black glazed glass, holding within it a soul as pitch-black and viscous as liquid ink.

Witnessing this, Elder Tong Ling offered a bizarre analysis:

—"She actually has half a human body! A half-blood? No, that's impossible. How could a yin spirit crossbreed with a human? They don't even have physical bodies, how could they possibly copulate with mortals?!"

Evidently, the perversity of humanity far exceeded the demon race's wildest imagination.

In truth, humans always mocked demons for lacking moral restraint, but that was merely a misunderstanding born of demons disliking human clothing and dressing more revealingly. (Nonsense, their true forms grew thick fur and feathers—why on earth should they bother wearing clothes?) Demons were far more principled than humans; they typically only intermarried within their own kind and would never engage in indiscriminate affairs with other races. After all, if demons fooled around indiscriminately, creatures that flew in the heavens, walked on land, and swam in the waters would have long fused into a single chaotic breed or produced an endless variety of bizarre hybrids, making it impossible to preserve the purity of so many lineages.

The ones truly devoid of restraint were humans; the vast majority of crossbreeds across the realms were tied to human exploits.

At the very least, no sane demon would ever dream of conceiving a child with a bodiless yin spirit.

Therefore, the yin spirit half-blood in the sky was completely beyond the boundaries of Elder Tong Ling's comprehension.

The demon elder could only speculate based on his understanding of humanity:

—"I know! This is body-snatching! The legendary [possession](<# "Literal CN: 夺舍 (duóshě) — seizing the dwelling; body-snatching / soul possession">)!"

[—"Possession?"](<# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er">)

—"Indeed! Human bodies are so fragile, lacking the slightest self-defense mechanism; any external soul can snatch one and use it right away. Thus, after certain human cultivators or demon practitioners perish, they seek out bodies with exceptional aptitude to seize, extending their own lifespan. But for entities like ghosts and yin spirits that inherently lack physical vessels, they don't even need to die first—they can simply possess directly!"-Elder Tong Ling analyzed with utter conviction.-"This girl's situation must be a possession by a yin spirit! It makes total sense if you think about it: for this girl to command the soldiers of White Jade Capital, she is eighty to ninety percent likely a descendant of the human Holy Sovereign! It's no wonder she was targeted by evil forces. Someone must be plotting to use her to usurp the position of Holy Sovereign!"

Having said that, Elder Tong Ling lowered his voice, though a sliver of poorly concealed excitement leaked through:

—"Who would have thought! I haven't left the Divine Tree in over ten thousand years, and the moment I step out, I stumble into a monumental conspiracy! Our Azure Phoenix Clan has always been the symbol of benevolence and love. We cannot simply sit by and let such evil run rampant in broad daylight!"

Had any other demon heard this, they would have scoffed with disdain. Even within the Azure Phoenix Clan, everyone would know their elder's mental illness was flaring up again:

The human White Jade Capital had vanished ten thousand years ago! What Holy Sovereign could possibly exist?!

Yet the one present happened to be Shui Huang'er.

If this young Saintess of the Azure Phoenix Clan possessed that kind of rationality, she wouldn't have run away from home with this senile old coot Tong Ling in tow, traveling all the way to the far-off Middle Realm.

—"Right! We must [exterminate demons and defend the Dao](<# "Literal CN: 除魔卫道 (chú mó wèi dào) — eradicate evil and defend the righteous path">)!"-the demon Saintess proclaimed, before humbly seeking guidance from the elder.-"What should we do?"

—"First... let us tail that yin spirit! We must discover what she intends to do before making our move!"-Elder Tong Ling instructed.

Shui Huang'er nodded vigorously, fixing her gaze upon the sky.

By now, the celestial army had completed a majestic circuit over Fengqing City and was descending toward the opposite end of the city.

Seeing this, Shui Huang'er immediately transformed into a burst of azure flame, pursuing in their wake.

A few individuals who had been eyeing the demon girl with ill intent realized she was far from an ordinary demon and sheepishly abandoned their original designs.

---

On the other side, that miraculous army of heroic spirits did not descend upon Fengqing City's City Lord estate, nor did it head for the military barracks. Instead, it touched down directly before the entrance of a tiny milk tea shop.

This shop conspicuously bore the signboard: "Yi Bei Cha."

Elder Tong Ling had not been mistaken; this army truly was an army of heroic spirits from White Jade Capital. It had been dispatched by the "current Holy Sovereign" Zhou Hui to accompany his youngest disciple, who was traveling away from home for the very first time—and who was also the proprietor of "Yi Bei Cha," Mo Bei.

Following the Immortal Fair, Yi Bei Cha's business expansion had progressed smoothly. Even in Fengqing City, which maintained rather frosty relations with Heavenly Spirit Sect, a branch had been established under the direct management of Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples. Thus, the socially timid Mo Bei had headed straight here upon arriving in Fengqing City.

The commander of that heroic spirit army was naturally the yin spirit half-blood, Mo Bei.

Whether chilled from soaring through the high winds or prickled by strange gazes, the moment Mo Bei stepped into the shop, she let out a listless sneeze:

—"Achoo..."

Seeing this, someone immediately offered a handkerchief:

—"Senior Sister Mo Bei, please use this."

[Senior Sister, huh...]

Mo Bei looked toward the young woman offering the handkerchief.

The other party was actually slightly older than Mo Bei, but because she was a disciple of the introductory class who had only joined the sect the previous year, rules dictated that she address Mo Bei as "Senior Sister."

Yet Mo Bei did not like this form of address in the least.

Having an unlucky younger brother, Mo Bei knew all too well what troublesome creatures younger siblings were. When nothing was going on, they would call you "Mo Bei" with every breath, showing zero respect; only when they needed something did it suddenly become "Elder Sister."

—"Just call me Mo Bei,"-Mo Bei requested, before inquiring:-"What are we doing next?"

—"Senior Sister Mo Bei may arrange as she pleases,"-the other replied with an obedient smile.

Receiving no answer, Mo Bei could only look toward the others.

Regrettably, for this excursion to Fengqing City, Heavenly Spirit Sect's sword cultivators were either eager to showcase their prowess at the Immortal Arts Assembly or occupied with other duties, so none had accompanied her—Mo Bei suspected Master had orchestrated this intentionally. The disciples brought along this time were all apprentice sword cultivators from the introductory class.

These youngsters were like [timid quails](<# "Literal CN: 鹌鹑 (ānchún) — quail; slang for shy, submissive people who shrink into themselves and don't speak up">), obediently trailing Mo Bei and looking after her needs, yet completely devoid of any inclination to act independently or dispute her judgment. They wouldn't even venture an opinion.

Seeing Mo Bei look their way, they hurriedly offered identical, well-behaved smiles.

[Looks like I won't be getting any help from them.]

Mo Bei thought helplessly, then turned her gaze toward the heroic spirit soldiers standing beside her.

Naturally, Yi Bei Cha's small shop could never accommodate an entire army.

Mo Bei didn't wish to obstruct her shop's daily business either, so she kept only two soldiers by her side, dismissing the rest for the time being until needed.

In fact, Mo Bei had never wanted to summon them in the first place, but this was a strict mandate from both Master and Mo Xi. They insisted that whenever she appeared in public, she had to bring this army along to prevent anyone from [harboring ill intentions](<# "Literal CN: 图谋不轨 (túmóu bùguǐ) — plotting treacherous schemes / ill designs">).

Mo Xi was a fool, so that was that, but for Master to do the same left Mo Bei completely baffled.

No, sending her out to resolve the crisis in Fengqing City in the first place was strange enough. In the past, Master would never have let her go out alone; even if he did, he would have arranged everything meticulously.

Now, there wasn't a single instruction, only saddling her with a massive troop of heroic spirits.

Aside from serving as an honor guard, these soldiers were utterly useless.

They could not think, nor could they speak. Even when Mo Bei looked at them, they stood like two stone pillars, incapable of offering her any operational guidance.

Mo Bei retracted her gaze in despair.

Finally, she turned toward the shop clerk of Yi Bei Cha, imitating Mo Xi's demeanor as she inquired:

—"Could you brief me once more on the situation with Fengqing City's Immortal Arts Assembly?"

—"Right away, Lady Mo Bei!"-the clerk replied with extraordinary fervor.-"Fengqing City's Immortal Arts Assembly is utilizing an arena renovated from an abandoned old fortress. Because the City Lord of Fengqing City has adopted an attitude of neither cooperating nor interfering with this tournament, we only invited personnel from the Wanhe Gang and the Heavenly Dao Alliance to assist. Lacking a mighty expert to preside over the venue, even with a supporting formation array system, security remains relatively weak, leaving the arena rather chaotic. According to our intelligence, at least five major factions are stirring up trouble inside. They are..."

[...This intelligence is awfully detailed!]

[Do you people actually spend any time selling milk tea normally?!]

Listening along, Mo Bei racked her brains trying to figure out how she was supposed to accomplish her mission:

[Why organize an Immortal Arts Assembly in Fengqing City in the first place?]

[Though it isn't incomprehensible—as the gateway to the Middle Realm, Heavenly Spirit Sect cannot bypass this place if it wishes to expand its influence.]

[Still, precisely because it is the gateway, it is riddled with endless complications! Hearing about these convoluted factional politics makes my head ache. Why did Master send me to handle this? Wouldn't it be far better to send Mo Xi? How does that fellow usually handle these things anyway... No, even if I knew how he did it, I could never match his slippery knack for wheeling and dealing with people.]

[...Wait a minute, why am I imitating that idiot Mo Xi?!]

[Even though we look identical, his style is the polar opposite of mine! If I'm going to imitate someone, I should imitate the one person truly worth emulating.]

[If it were Master, how would he instruct me in this situation?]

Mo Bei abruptly stood up.

—"Lady Mo Bei?"-The reporting clerk was startled by Mo Bei's sudden movement, asking in surprise.

—"I am going to the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-Mo Bei declared decisively.

Precisely! Listening to intelligence reports she would never understand was meaningless; Mo Bei only knew the simplest, most direct solution.

Her fingers tightened around the hilt of the flexible sword at her waist.

---

While Mo Bei was preparing to head for the Immortal Arts Assembly, Shui Huang'er had also tracked her way to the vicinity of "Yi Bei Cha."

—"Are you certain? That is a... teahouse, isn't it?"-Elder Tong Ling asked.

Even having stayed away from human cities for ages, he didn't think this was the kind of place an evil-intentioned yin spirit ought to frequent.

Then again, it wasn't the kind of place a heroic spirit army should visit either.

—"Positive. I see her right upstairs in this building,"-Shui Huang'er said, raising her eyes blazing with azure flames.

This was a special innate talent of the Azure Phoenix Clan.

They could effortlessly see through the soul essence of living beings—secrets capable of deceiving even True Immortals—and locate hidden entities with ease.

Besides, Mo Bei had never once thought of hiding.

Even without opening her Heavenly Eye, Shui Huang'er could rely on the acute senses of the demon race to know her target was right here the moment she stepped into this area; doing so now was merely adding another layer of assurance.

—"Perhaps the teahouse is a cover? I'll go inside and see!"-Shui Huang'er offered.

[My first time exterminating demons and defending the Dao—this is so exciting!]

For this purpose, she deliberately tucked away her conspicuous feathered crest and tail plumage, stepping inside in as human an appearance as possible.

Yet upon entering the teahouse, Shui Huang'er quickly discovered there had been no need to conceal her features at all.

So many girls.

[Is this where human female cultivators gather?]-Shui Huang'er wondered to herself.

Shui Huang'er didn't know why, but despite knowing that female human cultivators were by no means few, whether cooperating with human cultivators, engaging in battle, or merely walking through human cities, it always felt as though they were nowhere to be seen.

Yet that was not the point. The point was that this group of girls was openly and boldly discussing Shui Huang'er's target of investigation:

[—"...So that was Junior Sister Mo Bei of Heavenly Spirit Sect?"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"She's also the proprietor of this Yi Bei Cha, right?"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"Waah, she's even cuter than her piece in Immortal Chess! Just like a porcelain doll!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"Do you think we'll have a chance to talk to her?"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

Listening for a moment, Shui Huang'er asked hesitantly:

—"Are you talking about... the one who just flew through the sky commanding that army?"

—"Of course!"-a girl replied candidly, looking at her with curiosity.-"A new face?"

—"Yes, it's my first time here. Because I saw that girl—"

—"Don't tell them straight away! Humans won't easily believe bad news. You must win their trust first,"-Elder Tong Ling directed in Shui Huang'er's mind.

[Of course I know that!]

Shui Huang'er answered inwardly, while wearing a beaming smile on her face:

—"That girl looked so majestic, so strong! It made one... look up to her in admiration, so I couldn't help following along. She is here, isn't she?"-she pried gently.

She had not anticipated that these words would ignite glittering sparkles in the other's eyes!

And not just hers, but the other girls' as well:

[—"Right?! Junior Sister Mo Bei is amazing, isn't she!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"She's the champion of the very first Immortal Chess tournament, after all!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"Exactly, exactly! She is the true, undisputed representative of Foundation Establishment cultivators. With her around, there was no need to hold another Immortal Arts Assembly at all!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"I know, right! The competition over there is so filthy, using all sorts of underhanded tricks. How is that even a proper tournament?!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

[—"Here, let me replay the recording of Heavenly Spirit Sect's internal tournament for you! Junior Sister Mo Bei was so incredibly cool!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivator">)

The girls chimed in one after another in a flurry of voices.

[Immortal Chess? Immortal Arts Assembly? What on earth are those?]

Shui Huang'er listened in utter bewilderment.

She did not know that in Fengqing City, "Yi Bei Cha" served as an Immortal Chess parlor for female cultivators.

Although Heavenly Spirit Sect had dedicated Immortal Chess parlors in Fengqing City just as in other cities, the more chaotic a region was, the more a fiercely competitive game became hostile toward women.

Female cultivators were pushed out and marginalized over there, so they naturally gravitated toward Yi Bei Cha.

As one of the proprietors of Yi Bei Cha, Mo Bei held undeniable home-court advantage here. Especially after these girls faced frustration and foul play at the Immortal Arts Assembly, they regarded Mo Bei as an idol.

Thus, completely unbeknownst to Mo Bei, Heavenly Spirit Sect's bizarre fan club had been quietly expanding.

At this moment, seeing a new "fellow fan," they were overflowing with enthusiasm.

Yet before Shui Huang'er could even react to this passionate [fangirl shilling](<# "Literal CN: 安利 (ānlì) — Amway; internet slang for enthusiastically evangelizing or recommending something you love to someone else">), someone rushed down from the upper floor of the teahouse bearing breaking news:

—"Are you all still watching those old replays?! Lady Mo Bei is here in the flesh! And what's more, she's heading to Fengqing City's Immortal Arts Assembly right now! Aren't you going to the venue to watch?!"

[—"What?! Junior Sister Mo Bei is going to our local Immortal Arts Assembly?!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivators">)

[—"Of course we're going!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivators">)

The female cultivators erupted with excitement.

Scrambling to get ready, they did not forget to sweep Shui Huang'er along:

—"You are so lucky! The moment you arrive, you get to watch her live! Come on, let's go cheer for Junior Sister Mo Bei together!"

[???]

Utterly bewildered, Shui Huang'er found herself draped in a coat emblazoned with the character [Sorrow](<# "Literal CN: 悲 (bēi) — Sorrow; character in Mo Bei's name and 'Yi Bei Cha'">), and handed several magical firework charms, purportedly meant for [cheering on](<# "Literal CN: 助威 / 应援 (yìngyuán) — cheering / fan support">) Junior Sister Mo Bei.

—"She is an evil spirit without a shadow of a doubt!"-Elder Tong Ling declared with absolute certainty.-"Look at how thoroughly bewitched they are! Tsk tsk, completely beyond saving."
