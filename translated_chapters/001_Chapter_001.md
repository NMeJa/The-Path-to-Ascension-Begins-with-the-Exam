# Chapter 1: Who Wakens First from the Grand Dream? (第1章 大梦谁先觉)

The heavenly tribulation had just passed.

The purplish-red thunderclouds had yet to disperse entirely. The pitch-black mountain ranges exhaled a dense, heavy mist, while fragmented shards of shattered jade hovered and tumbled across the ashen sky. Yet atop those cloud-wreathed peaks, though trembling on the verge of collapse, the jade platform still stood firm.

Several streaks of sword light shot toward the jade platform.

They were disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect rushing over to investigate the aftermath.

Yet none of them dared to actually set foot upon the platform. They merely circled its perimeter, craning their necks anxiously toward the center.

The tribulation-crosser sat cross-legged right there.

In appearance, he was already indistinguishable from a true immortal: brows like distant mountain ridges, skin like mutton-fat jade. Even the robes draping his frame were just like the jade platform beneath him—shattered into tatters, utterly wrecked. Fortunately, a mane of frost-white hair cascaded down his shoulders, veiling the greater part of his body.

He was still here. He had not ascended. Clearly, the heavenly tribulation had failed.

Even so, no one dared disrespect him. They simply waited in the surrounding air with bated breath.

Who knew how long they waited, but at last, the tribulation-crosser's frost-tipped eyelashes stirred.

Someone noticed this minuscule movement at once and cried out in delight:

—"He's awake! Master is awake!"-someone shouted.

Accompanied by these shouts, the tribulation-crosser addressed as "Master" fluttered his eyelashes and opened his eyes.

Yet having only just regained consciousness, he was visibly disoriented:

—"Where... am I...?"-he murmured.

—"Master, did you forget? You failed your tribulation again!"-the most dashing young man among the disciples replied in a boisterous, cheerful tone.

He nimbly vaulted off his flying sword onto the jade platform, shrugged off his outer robe, and draped it over his master's shoulders.

With him leading the way, the other disciples finally landed on the jade platform one after another.

However, several took immediate offense at the lead youth's choice of words:

[—"Eldest Senior Brother, if you don't know how to speak, keep your mouth shut! What do you mean Master 'failed again'?!"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

[—"What failure? It was just another 'practice run'!"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

[—"Exactly! Even though Master has already 'practiced' a full nine times now, as long as he perseveres, he will definitely ascend!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"May Master ascend soon!"-someone bellowed.

—"May the Untiring Immortal Venerable ascend soon!"-the rest chorused in unison.

......

[What kind of [cult](# "Original CN self-censored as 'X-religion' (X教) for platform filters — means evil cult (邪教)") gathering is this?]

The corners of the tribulation-crosser's mouth twitched. If surviving the heavenly tribulation hadn't drained every ounce of his strength, he would have gladly given this bunch of foolish disciples a comprehensive lesson on what it meant to "ascend" in the purely physical, violent sense of the word.

Yet thanks to these boneheaded disciples, the tribulation-crosser finally sorted through his jumbled memories.

His name was Zhou Hui, known to the world as the "Untiring Immortal Venerable."

Though brazenly styled a "Venerable," he remained eternally half a step away from becoming a true immortal.

Case in point: right now, he had [failed his heavenly tribulation yet again, and again, and again](# "Literal CN: 又双叒叕 (yòu shuāng ruò zhuó) — internet slang repeating the character '又' (again) in escalating clusters to mean 'over and over and over again'").

And this particular failure had been exceptionally catastrophic. Not only had more than half of his cultivation been blasted away, but his very soul had nearly been pulverized. In the end, he had been forced to sacrifice his [Lifebound Treasure](# "A cultivator's natal artifact bound directly to their soul") just to barely scrape by alive. Yet the violent backlash from losing his Lifebound Treasure had knocked him completely unconscious.

[Still, as long as I'm alive, I'll definitely succeed next time... right?]

—That was certainly the thought Zhou Hui would have clung to in the past.

That was how he had lived his entire life.

Zhou Hui's innate talent for cultivation was not good; in fact, one could say it was terrible.

Born into an ordinary mortal family, his spirit roots were a muddy mess of Four-Element Spirit Roots. At the beginning, not a single sect was willing to take him in. He had zero resources and zero fated opportunities. Yet through sheer relentless effort—by [grinding himself to the bone](# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn / involution) — hyper-competitive overworking and grinding")—Zhou Hui had crawled his way up step by step from a humble wandering cultivator all the way to the Tribulation Transcendence Stage, just half a step shy of ascension.

Yet that half step felt like a chasm spanning worlds.

He had reincarnated six times, and every single time, his tribulation had failed.

Yes, reincarnated six times.

Zhou Hui hadn't merely failed his tribulations; he had lost his life six times over.

In his very first life, his first tribulation had severely wounded him. While he was in seclusion recuperating, an enemy faction [backdoored and sacked his sect's headquarters](# "Literal CN: 偷了家 (stole the home base) — gaming slang for backdoor base raiding"). Nearly slain, he had been forced to execute his self-created forbidden art, the *Seven-Life Reincarnation Art*, to reincarnate.

Yet whether this forbidden art had offended the Heavenly Dao or not, each of Zhou Hui's reincarnations was more wretched than the last.

Sect wiped out, betrayed and abandoned by everyone, soul shattered to dust—he had experienced virtually every tragedy imaginable.

The most absurd was his fifth life. Perhaps because his preparations had been too thorough, that heavenly tribulation was unprecedentedly colossal, directly obliterating half the world. Zhou Hui's soul was almost blasted into nothingness; only a sliver of his remnant soul had "luckily" slipped into an "accidentally discovered" spatial rift to escape. Consequently, he spent almost his entire sixth life nursing that battered remnant soul.

Zhou Hui had begun to suspect that ascending through normal means was impossible for him, yet he still never thought of giving up.

Thus, in this current lifetime, while preparing for his tribulation, Zhou Hui had simultaneously been researching the spatial rift he had encountered in his fifth life. In the worst-case scenario, he planned to ascend by [smuggling himself across the border](# "Literal CN: 偷渡 (tōudù / illegal border crossing) — bypassing the Heavenly Gate by slipping through spatial rifts").

After all, this was his seventh life—his final chance at reincarnation.

As expected, the Heavenly Dao refused to let him off. The tribulation struck right on schedule, unceremoniously battering him into critical condition.

Yet Zhou Hui's efforts hadn't been entirely in vain. Although he hadn't found the spatial rift he sought, during the lightning tribulation just now, he had caught a glimpse of another world.

That world was utterly devoid of spiritual Qi. There were no spells and no immortals; people there could only painstakingly decipher and apply the physical laws of their planet, striving to venture beyond their home world.

Because his soul was incomplete, Zhou Hui had lost his memories, believing himself to be an ordinary resident of that world. Yet he had never let go of his obsession with ascension. In that world, just as in the cultivation realm, he began [grinding like mad](# "Literal CN: 卷了起来 (started involuting) — threw himself into cutthroat academic competition").

Zhou Hui ground his way from a rural village primary school to a town middle school; from the town middle school to a prestigious city high school; from the prestigious high school into the country's top university; and kept grinding all the way up until he personally launched a spacecraft of his own design toward another planet.

Regrettably, in a world devoid of immortal arts, human lifespan inevitably had its limit. On the very day the astronauts stepped onto extraterrestrial soil, Zhou Hui closed his eyes forever in that world and returned to the cultivation realm.

Up to that point, it could only be called a bizarre adventure.

The only problem was that upon waking and sorting through these memories, Zhou Hui noticed a detail he had previously overlooked: in that world, he had read a cultivation web novel.

The story itself was utterly cliché—nothing more than a Child of Destiny transmigrating into a cultivation world, obtaining a system, steadily leveling up, collecting beauties to build a sprawling harem, and ultimately becoming a True Immortal.

Naturally, along the way, there was no shortage of brainless, arrogant villains.

And the biggest [fall-guy scapegoat](# "Literal CN: 冤种 (yuānzhǒng) — internet slang for an unfortunate, exploited sucker who gets taken advantage of or suffers unjustly") in the entire book was called the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

This blind sect treated the Protagonist as a disposable outer sect disciple to be exploited. The Protagonist had no choice but to swallow his pride and keep a low profile, while the entire sect treated him as trash. Inner sect disciples mocked him, direct disciples bullied him, and only the youngest junior sister showed him a shred of kindness. Consequently, a certain senior brother grew jealous, attempting to murder the Protagonist inside a secret realm—only to be slain by the Protagonist instead, prompting the Protagonist to rebel and defect from the sect.

Yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect refused to let matters rest. They relentlessly hunted the Protagonist across the world: from ordinary disciples to their masters, from their masters to sect protectors, and from protectors to the sect master himself, all slain one by one by the Protagonist. Finally, this enraged the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grandmaster—the first-stage final boss of this realm: the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Yes, the novel also had an Untiring Immortal Venerable.

At this point, how could Zhou Hui not understand? That so-called "novel" was in truth the script written by the Heavenly Dao, and everything Zhou Hui had ever done was merely to serve as a stepping stone for that so-called "Protagonist."

Even including the spatial rift he had been researching.

According to the novel's plot, when Zhou Hui was killed by the Protagonist, Zhou Hui once again "luckily" found a spatial rift, scrambling to send his remnant soul through. He finally entered the Immortal-Demon Realm, only to fall into the clutches of a powerful Extraterrestrial Demon and become its slave. Later, once the Protagonist unlocked the Immortal-Demon Realm map, Zhou Hui would relentlessly continue opposing the Protagonist until his soul was finally obliterated for good. Meanwhile, Zhou Hui's "master," seeking vengeance on his behalf, would drag his entire faction along to serve as fodder to [feed exp to the Protagonist](# "Literal CN: 送经验 (deliver EXP) — gaming slang for rushing in to get killed and grant experience points to the player").

In other words, whether it was Zhou Hui's "forbidden reincarnation art," the "spatial rift," or his plan to "smuggle himself across"—all of it was within the Heavenly Dao's calculations, neatly preparing the transition for the Protagonist to unlock the next world map!

Having pieced together the full picture, Zhou Hui couldn't help muttering a low curse:

—"To hell with the [fucking](# "Original CN self-censored as 'XX' — uncensored to natural swearing") Heavenly Dao!"-Zhou Hui cursed under his breath.

—"Master?!"-a disciple standing beside Zhou Hui blurted in confusion, having caught the muttered curse.

Zhou Hui raised his head and looked at the disciple.

It was a handsome young man of exquisite features and ethereal grace. Regrettably, the whites of his fine eyes outweighed the irises, and his lips were perpetually pursed in a venomous sneer, bearing an expression as if the entire world owed him several million spirit stones—oozing villainous aura from head to toe.

And in fact, that assessment wasn't wrong.

In the novel, this very man was the sect master of the ill-fated Heavenly Spirit Sect, as well as Zhou Hui's second disciple: Qu Qianxing.

To Zhou Hui, Qu Qianxing was actually among the more reliable disciples. But in the eyes of outsiders, Sect Master Qu possessed only two defining traits:

First, a razor-sharp tongue; second, fierce, shameless nepotism.

Consequently, across the six reincarnations Zhou Hui had lived through, Qu Qianxing had indulged his disciples in offending the Protagonist on three separate occasions, relentlessly hunting the Protagonist down and ultimately bringing about the sect's total annihilation.

Looking at him now, Zhou Hui couldn't help but sigh:

—"Qianxing, you really need to keep a tighter rein on our sect members,"-Zhou Hui sighed.

—"M-Master?"-Qu Qianxing stammered in panic, completely unprepared for this to be Zhou Hui's first words.-"Did I do something wrong?"

A flush rose to his cheeks, making his already handsome face look even more striking—hardly resembling the spiteful sect master depicted in the novel.

Seeing Qu Qianxing like this, Zhou Hui suddenly realized that this disciple of his was only at the late Golden Core Stage. Whether as the sect master or as the disciple of a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster, his cultivation was indeed rather lacking.

However, the blame lay primarily with Zhou Hui himself.

—In his previous lives, Zhou Hui had been grinding so maniacally that only upon reaching the Tribulation Transcendence Stage had it even crossed his mind to pass down his legacy. Aside from his eldest disciple, the cultivation bases of his other direct disciples were not high at all. No wonder they had been slain by the so-called Protagonist in a classic case of "the weak defeating the strong," resulting in the slaughtered sect.

Zhou Hui let out a weary sigh:

—"How about this: starting tomorrow, I will give you [remedial tutoring](# "Literal CN: 补课 (bǔkè) — make-up classes / remedial tutoring; a modern Chinese school term Zhou Hui picked up from his mortal lifetime")."-Due to his jumbled memories, Zhou Hui inadvertently used vocabulary from that other world.

Qu Qianxing froze, unable to process the term:

[—"Remedial tutoring?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Zhou Hui didn't bother explaining. Noticing the gaze of the other disciples, he suddenly felt he shouldn't play favorites:

—"Not just Qianxing. The rest of you should come as well,"-Zhou Hui added.

The disciples instantly felt their skin prickle.

For Master to wake up from a failed tribulation and, instead of resting, immediately announce remedial tutoring for everyone—somehow, this did not bode well.

The eldest disciple, Yuwen Xuan, asked with concern:

—"Master, you've only just finished the tribulation and suffered severe injuries. Shouldn't you enter secluded cultivation to heal?"-Yuwen Xuan asked worriedly.

Indeed, the price of failing a tribulation was catastrophic. Without immediate secluded meditation, one could not stabilize their mental state or consolidate their cultivation; in severe cases, it would jeopardize the next tribulation attempt. That was why after every (failed) tribulation, Zhou Hui had always entered seclusion for an extended period.

In each previous life, after being grievously injured by the heavenly tribulation, Zhou Hui had even passed the position of Sect Master to Qu Qianxing precisely so he could enter seclusion.

Hearing Yuwen Xuan's reminder, Qu Qianxing came to his senses as well:

—"This unfilial disciple would never dare delay Master's cultivation,"-Qu Qianxing hastily said.

—"It's fine. Seclusion isn't strictly necessary,"-Zhou Hui replied, shaking his head.-"Besides, I don't plan on facing another tribulation anyway."

—"No intention of facing another tribulation?!"-Qu Qianxing's eyes widened in disbelief.-"...Master, do you mean... you don't plan to attempt it again in the short term...?"

—"No. I don't plan to face another tribulation ever again,"-Zhou Hui said.

Yes, Zhou Hui had given up on ascension.

[Zhou Hui was done with the grind!]

Mainly because grinding was utterly pointless.

Faced with the Heavenly Dao's script, even a True Immortal could not overturn it, let alone a Mahayana cultivator who hadn't even passed his tribulation.

Zhou Hui had ground through seven lifetimes, failing tribulations dozens of times. The culmination of all his blood, sweat, and tears was discovering that the entire world was merely a web novel—a preordained script written by the Heavenly Dao, an absolute reality that could not be defied.

So what was the point of continuing to grind?

As a true [Involution King](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — the champion of grinding; someone who out-works, out-studies, and out-competes everyone else"), one had to know which direction to grind and how to grind with maximum efficiency in order to win. Blindly grinding without rhyme or reason wasn't being an Involution King—it was being an idiot.

Zhou Hui might have wanted to bellow [—"My fate is determined by me, not by Heaven!"](# "Famous Xianxia trope quote from *Ba Lian Cheng Xian* / *Ne Zha*") like a typical protagonist, but that was just another script in itself. Rather than dancing as Heaven's puppet, he would rather [lie flat](# "Literal CN: 躺平 (tǎngpíng) — modern slang for giving up the rat race, refusing to compete, and living with minimal effort").

Most importantly, Zhou Hui felt exhausted.

Across several lifetimes, he had lived for nearly a thousand years. His existence had consisted of nothing but cultivation, cultivation, and more cultivation. It was grinding all the same, yet it had never brought the sense of fulfillment he had experienced during those few short decades in that other world.

Zhou Hui didn't know the root of this emptiness—he suspected it was the sheer nausea of seeing the Heavenly Dao's idiotic script. He simply felt that, just like people in that other world, he ought to retire to a sanatorium and leave the future in the hands of the younger generation.

With that thought, Zhou Hui let out a long breath and smiled gently:

—"It's about time I rested as well,"-Zhou Hui murmured with a gentle smile.

***

Little did Zhou Hui know that his decision had sparked a massive uproar among his disciples.

After escorting Zhou Hui back to his immortal cave abode, the disciples did not disperse. Instead, they gathered together in grave solemnity to hold an emergency conference.

—"Master feels completely wrong!"-Second Disciple Qu Qianxing declared, his expression dark and heavy.

The Eldest Disciple, Yuwen Xuan, was slightly bewildered:

—"Does he?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

—"It's bleeding obvious!"-Qu Qianxing snapped, shooting him a sidelong glare.-"Ascension is the one thing Master has thought about day and night! For someone like him to suddenly give up—no matter how you look at it, something is seriously wrong!"

—"Exactly! Anyone else giving up is one thing, but how could our Master ever give up on ascending?!"-Seventh Disciple Mo Xi chimed in from the side.

—"I suspect... could he have been [body-snatched](# "Literal CN: 夺舍 (duóshě) — soul possession / seizing another cultivator's body")?"-Fourth Disciple Guan Ye speculated in a low murmur.

—"N-No way!"-Sixth Disciple Mo Bei gasped in fright.-"Master is so powerful! How could he possibly be possessed?!"

—"It is hard to say,"-Guan Ye's tone grew increasingly chilly.-"I have heard that the lightning tribulations of ascension attract Extraterrestrial Demons. They interfere with a cultivator's tribulation, devour the soul, and cause drastic personality shifts. In severe cases, they might even orchestrate an invasion of our world!"

Third Disciple Ran Deng offered a different perspective:

—"It's also possible that Master fell into Qi deviation during the tribulation!"-Ran Deng suggested.

—"Eeeek!"-Mo Xi and Mo Bei shrieked together.

—"Come to think of it, this lightning tribulation was far more terrifying than any before,"-Fifth Disciple Lu Shulong agreed.

The thunder tribulation this time had indeed been monstrously apocalyptic. The disciples had genuinely thought Master was [a goner](# "Literal CN: 寄了 (jì le / GG'd) — modern Chinese gaming slang meaning 'dead / game over'"), which was why they had rushed en masse to the Ascension Platform. Their earlier "comforting" of their master had really been an effort to comfort themselves.

Recalling the apocalyptic devastation of the lightning storm, everyone's complexion turned sickly pale.

If an Extraterrestrial Demon had invaded back then, or if Zhou Hui had suffered Qi deviation amidst the lightning, both were entirely plausible scenarios.

As every grim possibility raced through their minds, silence fell over the gathering.

Only Yuwen Xuan remained skeptical:

—"No way, right? Given Master's sheer determination, could he really be possessed? Or suffer Qi deviation?"-Yuwen Xuan muttered half in disbelief.

Ignoring their eldest senior brother, the rest turned their eyes toward their second senior brother.

Qu Qianxing pondered for a moment before speaking:

—"Don't act rashly. Tomorrow when he lectures, we will observe him closely,"-Qu Qianxing said grimly.-"However, do not slack off either. Keep a tight rein on the sect disciples and prepare for battle! If Master truly has been possessed... we will avenge him!"

His tone was resolute and unyielding.

He seemed not to give a single thought to the fact that whether Zhou Hui had succumbed to Qi deviation or been possessed by an Extraterrestrial Demon, they would be facing an entity at or beyond the Tribulation Transcendence Stage. With the meager cultivation of Qu Qianxing and the others, exacting vengeance was utterly impossible.

Yet not a single person objected.

At least in this moment, if anything had truly happened to their master, every one of them was ready to lay down their lives.

***

The next day, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect walked into the Palace of Silence—the hall designated for sermons and instruction—with the solemn dread of men marching to the execution grounds.

And here, it truly was an execution ground.

Zhou Hui had been waiting for them since dawn. Beside his low table sat a miniature mountain of papers.

—"It is truly shameful. As your master, I have remained ignorant of your academic progress all this time,"-Zhou Hui said warmly and kindly.-"Fortunately, it is not too late. Before we begin our formal lessons, let us hold a small [baseline assessment exam](# "Literal CN: 摸底考试 (mōdǐ kǎoshì) — diagnostic / baseline placement test used in Chinese schools at the start of a term to gauge students' true levels")!"

As he spoke, he distributed portions of the paper mountain to each disciple.

Although the disciples had no idea what a "baseline assessment exam" was, the sight of the densely packed questions covering the sheets made their vision go black.

[—"Second Senior Brother, this is definitely Master!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[—"You're right. Aside from Master, who else could draft this many questions in a single night?"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

[—"As expected of Master... [truly terrifying to this degree](# "Literal CN: 恐怖如斯 (kǒngbù rú sī) — iconic web novel catchphrase popularized by *Battle Through the Heavens* (斗破苍穹) meaning 'terrifying to such an extent'")!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

Compared to the suspicion that their master had been possessed, the test papers before their eyes plunged the disciples into far deeper sorrow and agony.
