# Chapter 29: Claiming Ownership (第29章 认主)

[—"The Palace of Fallen Immortals is really the Palace of Carefree Wandering?!"](# "Speaker: Surrounding Rogue Cultivators")

Qian Er's words caused the surrounding rogue cultivators to glance sideways in shock. Yet several sneered, quietly steering clear of him to move further away.

—"You claim that Fallen Immortal Palace is really the Carefree Palace—do you have any shred of evidence?"-the burly man from earlier asked with half-belief and half-doubt.

In the eyes of ordinary cultivators, the Palace of Carefree Wandering actually enjoyed quite a stellar reputation.

After all, their branch halls were scattered across every district of the cultivation world, and they even operated businesses within mortal kingdoms. For the vast majority of cultivators, the first time they purchased a magical treasure or flying sword in a respectable immortal goods shop, the first time they attended an auction, or the first time they visited an immortal fair to participate in large-scale Daoist events—even if the establishment was not directly under the Carefree Palace's name, they would still see the Carefree Palace listed as a sponsor. To the public, it was an accredited brand universally acknowledged for guaranteed quality and fair pricing.

As for the trafficking of '[cultivation furnaces](# "Human cultivators exploited as vessels for dual-cultivation or forced spiritual energy extraction")' that had provoked Zhou Hui's utmost fury, as long as the Carefree Palace could prove those 'furnaces' had clean origins, virtually no one found any issue with it. Some would even consider Zhou Hui meddlesome:

—"How do you know those 'furnaces' didn't do it voluntarily just to [hug the golden thighs](# "Literal CN: 抱大腿 (bào dàtuǐ) — hitching oneself to someone powerful or wealthy for protection and advancement") of high-stage cultivators? Think about it: a female cultivator stuck at Foundation Establishment or Golden Core with zero hope of breakthrough only has to cultivate a few matching manuals to become the concubine of an exalted master, enjoying a lavish life she could never otherwise dream of... As for the Carefree Palace, they're even more blameless! At their Grand Curio Fair, they could sell [a lump of shit](# "Literal CN: 一坨X (yī tuó X) — internet censorship euphemism for shit/crap") for an astronomical fortune; those 'furnaces' probably couldn't wait to get in there just to [gild their credentials](# "Literal CN: 镀金 (dùjīn) — acquiring prestigious labels to inflate one's market value")!"-the common consensus argued.

Naturally, the Palace of Carefree Wandering also took meticulous pains in tending to its public image.

The sordid deeds committed by the Fallen Immortal Palace—abducting and trafficking mortals and low-tier cultivators, refining human furnaces, murdering people to seize their treasures—were things the Carefree Palace would never touch on the surface. On the contrary, they frequently sponsored low-stage cultivators, gifted treasures to promising talents, conducted relatively fair trade, and even served as respected witnesses and arbiters for commercial contracts between cultivators, enjoying exceptionally high credibility.

It was only in the Central Domain—where conditions were brutal, settlements few and far between, and the Carefree Palace's institutional reach limited—that the Fallen Immortal Palace operated with brazen impunity in this lawless territory beyond the grasp of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, utterly unconcerned about exposing its true face. Hence the burly man's skepticism.

To an Eastern Domain native like Zhao Yuegui, he didn't believe a word of it.

Especially for a rogue cultivator like him who spent his days hunting sea beasts: if he was lucky enough to encounter a Carefree Palace treasure ship while wounded or exhausted, not only would he receive attentive care, but they would also purchase his hunted demon beast cores at a relatively fair price. If fortune favored him, he might even purchase a handy weapon. It was practically considered a stroke of good karmic fortune.

Furthermore, Zhao Yuegui had already gleaned from everyone's reactions that the Fallen Immortal Palace was utterly vile. Incensed that Qian Er had tried to dupe him into joining such an outfit, he questioned him in dissatisfaction:

—"Exactly! What proof do you have?! The Carefree Palace possesses legitimate supply chains across the entire realm, and their brand alone commands universal respect. Why on earth would they resort to underhanded tricks to conceal their identity?"-Zhao Yuegui demanded.

—"Heh, only folks from outside domains are that naive. In the Central Domain, it's an open secret to anyone with eyes!"-Qian Er sneered.

Having had his lies exposed earlier and facing repeated questioning, the blood rushed to his head, and in his agitation, he completely forgot his taboos.

—"Setting aside everything else,"-Qian Er said,-"if you sell even a scrap of raw materials to the Fallen Immortal Palace, it'll show up on the shelves of a Carefree Palace shop the very next morning! Not to mention the last Abyssal Ocean storm: everyone saw with their own eyes that the Fallen Immortal Palace came out on top, yet the ones who arrived to collect the goods were actually—*hurk*!!"

Qian Er's words never finished.

A vibrant emerald vine suddenly speared straight through Qian Er's chest. Qian Er's eyes bulged in disbelief. His lips twitched, as if trying to plead for mercy, but before he could utter a single syllable, his entire body disintegrated into withered grass and leaves, scattering instantly in the wind.

Only then did an enchanting young maiden aboard one of the light skiffs closest to Zhao Yuegui and the others project her voice across the plateau:

—"A mere rogue cultivator dares to slander the honor of our Palace of Carefree Wandering! A minor punishment has been meted out. I trust everyone will watch their words and deeds, and refrain from causing offense,"-the maiden proclaimed in a ringing voice.

Seeing this, the surrounding rogue cultivators hurriedly backed away from the Carefree Palace's fleet, retreating into the craggy shadows of the mountain.

Even Zhao Yuegui, who had previously harbored favorable impressions of the Carefree Palace, now dimly realized that the Carefree Palace in this domain operated far differently from elsewhere. He swiftly ducked into an inconspicuous corner alongside the others.

The Carefree Palace disciples seemed thoroughly pleased by this reaction.

With the colossal treasure ship at the center, their light skiffs fanned out, driving away the rogue cultivators and seizing full control of the entire summit, settling in to await the arrival of the other sects.

---

—"Acting like this, I'm now inclined to believe they really are [in bed with](# "Literal CN: 有一腿 (yǒu yī tuǐ) — to have an illicit liaison or corrupt covert connection") the Fallen Immortal Palace,"-the thick-browed, bright-eyed female cultivator complained in a hushed whisper as they retreated to the corner.

This muttered complaint startled the surrounding cultivators into frantic winks and cautionary glares. The female cultivator evidently knew she shouldn't press her luck, and clamped her mouth shut.

—"No. Making such a grand spectacle actually makes me believe they're clean. The Fallen Immortal Palace wouldn't dare pull off this kind of pomp when they know the Heavenly Spirit Sect is coming,"-the burly man countered.

[—"That's true too."](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

The rogue cultivators present exchanged knowing, conspiratorial smiles.

Zhao Yuegui could finally no longer suppress the burning curiosity in his chest. Cupping his hands toward the two, he bowed:

—"Fellow Daoists, may I ask... just what kind of sect is this Heavenly Spirit Sect—"-Zhao Yuegui inquired.

---

—"Sovereign Immortal, are we being a bit too... ostentatious?"-within the treasure ship, Perfected Master Zhao of the 'Fallen Immortal Palace' asked, his heart thumping in terror.

He attempted a tactful plea:

—"It is merely a minor secret realm; it is hardly worth mobilizing so much firepower on Your Exalted's part..."-he added cautiously.

—"Heh. You're terrified of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, aren't you?!"-an androgynous voice, neither distinctly male nor female, sneered from the grand master seat.

Along with that voice, a crushing spiritual pressure blanketed the entire vessel. An existence at the Nascent Soul stage like Perfected Master Zhao was pressed flat against the deck, prostrating in sheer helplessness.

If anyone had dared to raise their head, they would have discovered that the grand master seat was completely vacant. In its place stood only a water mirror. The person within the mirror had not even arrived in person, yet their spiritual pressure held every soul aboard the vessel paralyzed in fear.

The figure in the mirror ground their teeth, sneering:

—"What does a little pomp matter? Our Carefree Palace moves freely across all Five Domains—where is there any place we fear?! Is a mere Untiring Immortal Venerable worth being so terrified of?! If he dares to show up in person, this seat will gladly [debate the Dao](# "Literal CN: 论道 (lùn dào) — discuss/debate the Dao; in cultivation contexts, often a dignified euphemism for a high-stakes duel between grandmasters") with him!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana declared.

—"The Sovereign Immortal is mighty,"-Perfected Master Zhao could only chime in helplessly.

He felt aggrieved to the extreme.

That day, who knew what kind of fit the Heavenly Dao Alliance's emissary Su Xin had thrown? He had fled without even stepping foot inside the Fallen Immortal Palace, and upon returning, gods knew what he told his parents, leaving the Fallen Immortal Palace with virtually zero slots to explore the secret realm.

Having no other recourse, Perfected Master Zhao had reported up to the Carefree Palace. Little did he expect that the Carefree Palace headquarters would attach bizarrely immense importance to this matter. They had directly intervened under their official banner to secure exploration credentials, even dispatching a Tribulation Transcendence heavyweight known as 'Sovereign Immortal Chana' to hold the fort.

The only problem was that Sovereign Immortal Chana had broken through to Tribulation Transcendence barely a century ago. (From this, one could also glimpse the terrifying foundation of the Carefree Palace: Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses across the entire cultivation realm could be counted on two hands, yet three of them belonged to the Carefree Palace, and they possessed no small number of elite disciples qualified to strike for Mahayana and Tribulation Transcendence.) Zhou Hui's clash with the Carefree Palace, on the other hand, had occurred four hundred years ago. Back then, Chana was merely at the Body Integration stage, coddled and shielded by the Carefree Palace as a prized breakthrough seed, and had never directly faced Zhou Hui's terrifying might.

After ascending to Tribulation Transcendence, Sovereign Immortal Chana believed that the Tribulation Transcendence elder stationed at the Carefree Palace back then had simply been incompetent. Had he encountered Zhou Hui himself, things would surely have turned out entirely differently.

Perfected Master Zhao offered zero comment on this.

As a tiny Nascent Soul [small fry](# "Literal CN: 小虾米 (xiǎo xiāmǐ) — little shrimp; insignificant small fry"), he had zero desire to find out who was stronger between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Sovereign Immortal Chana. [When gods fight, mortals suffer](# "Literal CN: 神仙打架，小鬼遭殃 (shénxiān dǎjià, xiǎoguǐ zāoyāng) — when deities clash, the little ghosts suffer; innocent underlings get crushed when titans fight")—even if they didn't brawl, a stray spark from their friction would be [more than enough to make him choke](# "Literal CN: 够他喝一壶 (gòu tā hē yī hú) — enough for him to drink a pot of; enough trouble to give someone a massive headache or kill them").

At this moment, Perfected Master Zhao prayed with all his soul that the Untiring Immortal Venerable's 'Untiring' title was literal—that he was obsessed with cultivation as usual, locked away in secluded meditation, and that during this entire secret realm expedition, he wouldn't cast a single glance in this direction!

---

On the other side, the rogue cultivators had finished briefing Zhao Yuegui on the grudge between the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Fallen Immortal Palace.

—"That is how our generation ought to live!"-Zhao Yuegui exclaimed in admiration.

He still didn't believe the Carefree Palace had ties to the Fallen Immortal Palace, viewing the latter as a rogue den of pure villainy. Hearing how Zhou Hui had overturned the Fallen Immortal Palace's halls and blasted their stewards into the sky over two little girls, his spirit surged with exhilaration, rekindling a spark of the original passion he had felt upon first establishing his foundation.

Consequently, his disgust toward Qian Er's scheme to dupe him only deepened.

Yet with Qian Er dead, dwelling on it was pointless. Zhao Yuegui turned to the two cultivators before him:

—"May I ask, Fellow Daoists, if one wishes to join the Heavenly Spirit Sect, what requirements must be met?"-he asked.

The burly man and the female cultivator exchanged a glance, their expressions growing somewhat complicated.

—"If it's the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I wouldn't recommend it,"-the burly man said.-"While Lord Immortal Venerable is indeed noble and righteous, carrying the title 'Untiring' naturally means he is obsessed with cultivation and neglects internal sect affairs. Thus, while the Heavenly Spirit Sect has a Tribulation Transcendence master holding the fort in name, the disciples below are mostly Qi Condensation and Foundation Establishment cultivators—scarcely better than an ordinary sect."

—"Indeed. Though Qian Er harbored ill intentions, aside from pitching the Fallen Immortal Palace, his advice wasn't wrong,"-the female cultivator advised.-"If you truly wish to join a sect, your best options are the Myriad Coalition Gang or Myriad Beasts Hall. Or finding a way to commission the Carefree Palace to carry you out of this domain is also an option."

—"The Carefree Palace here..."-Zhao Yuegui shook his head with a wry smile.

He was no fledgling who had just begun cultivating yesterday. The anomalies surrounding the Carefree Palace here were plain to see. Even if the Carefree Palace here was unrelated to the Fallen Immortal Palace, could it really be the same entity as the Carefree Palace in the Eastern Domain? Communications in the cultivation world were primitive; rumors frequently circulated about some branch steward of a prominent sect suffering cultivation deviation and embarking on private slaughter.

Zhao Yuegui felt he needed to observe further before deciding.

Of course, he couldn't find an avenue to connect with a behemoth like the Carefree Palace right now anyway!

As for the other sects Qian Er had recommended, Zhao Yuegui felt they were somewhat lacking.

While they were discussing the Heavenly Spirit Sect, several other sects that had secured exploration credentials had arrived one after another, each bringing the pomp expected of major factions:

For instance, the Myriad Beasts Hall arrived atop a colossal white python large enough to coil around an entire mountain peak; and the Myriad Coalition Gang made their entrance piloting a flock of mechanical wooden-and-metal birds. Regrettably, against the dazzling five-colored radiance of the Carefree Palace's treasure ship, they all looked somewhat [petty and provincial](# "Literal CN: 小家子气 (xiǎojiāziqì) — petty, narrow, lacking grand stature").

And Zhao Yuegui, having hailed from the Eastern Domain where exotic schools and unorthodox sects abounded, couldn't help feeling that the sects of the Central Domain were rather underwhelming.

Even the Heavenly Dao Alliance had, for some reason, arrived in ordinary flying carriages today, devoid of their usual spectacle of cloud carriages and soaring cranes.

Refusing to give up, Zhao Yuegui asked:

—"Speaking of which, what about the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Didn't people say they were coming today as well?"-Zhao Yuegui pressed.

—"That is precisely why I don't recommend them,"-the female cultivator sighed.-"Rumor has it the Heavenly Spirit Sect secured the single largest allocation of exploration slots this time, but whether they'll actually show up is anyone's guess. That Lord Immortal Venerable has always been detached from fame and fortune; he likely doesn't care about the meager yields of a secret realm—"

*BOOM!!*

Right as the female cultivator was speaking, a thunderclap abruptly detonated.

Startled, everyone present looked up in unison, only to see the sky—which had been crystal-clear moments ago—plunged into pitch-black darkness as if an inkpot had been overturned. Flashes of brilliant silver light snaked directly above the mountain summit like dancing serpents, threatening to rip the heavens apart at any moment.

[—"H-Heavenly tribulation lightning?!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

When had the rogue cultivators ever witnessed such terrifying thunderous fury? They scrambled backward in panic.

Even the major sects present were forced to [retreat three paces](# "Literal CN: 退避三尺 (tuìbì sānchǐ) — step back in deference or caution"). And amidst this lightning tempest, the one bearing the brunt of the onslaught was naturally the Carefree Palace's colossal treasure ship.

Its gargantuan bulk made it impossible to dodge the lightning strikes like the agile mounts of the other sects. And whether by chance or design, the vast majority of thunderbolts crashed down precisely in the direction of the Carefree Palace! In an instant, the entire summit resembled a tempestuous ocean: the Carefree Palace's treasure ship and escort skiffs were tossed violently up and down amid the storm's fury, looking like toys battered by the claws of dragons.

Finally, after a bolt of lightning shattered the ship's protective barrier and smashed clean through one of its mainmasts, an enraged roar erupted from within the vessel:

—"Insolent brat, how dare you insult me!"-an enraged roar erupted from within the vessel.

Along with that roar, nearly everyone present was crushed beneath a colossal spiritual pressure, driven nearly to their knees.

[Mahayana, or Tribulation Transcendence?!]

[What on earth is inside this secret realm that prompted the Carefree Palace to dispatch such a monster?!]

Hall Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Central Domain Branch, the Body Integration cultivator He Yong, thought in abject terror. He strained to hold his ground, yet even with his formidable cultivation, he was on the verge of collapsing to his knees.

Fortunately, at that very moment, another crack of thunder resounded, violently sweeping away the suffocating pressure. It allowed cultivators like He Yong to preserve their dignity, and spared the lives of the weaker rogue cultivators from being crushed.

He Yong realized then that this was no ordinary lightning. Widening his eyes, he stared toward the epicenter of the storm:

[Could it be...?!]

—"Senior, pray quell your anger,"-a voice drifted from within the lightning, though it was not the person He Yong had anticipated.-"Junior meant no offense. It was merely that such a gigantic monstrosity was blocking the thoroughfare, Junior thought it was a [pile of garbage from who-knows-where](# "Literal CN: 哪里来的垃圾 (nǎlǐ lái de lājī) — Qu Qianxing's iconic deadpan insult treating the Carefree Palace's prized five-colored treasure ship as roadside trash")."

Accompanied by these thoroughly impolite words, the lightning coiled and flattened into a shimmering silver disc, descending straight into the airspace the treasure ship had occupied.

Someone actually walked out from the raging lightning storm, standing proudly upon the silver disc. And the moment the crowd caught sight of the leader's countenance, a collective gasp of awe rippled through the plateau:

Peerless beauty!

They lacked words to describe this breathtaking beauty. Even the roaring lightning could not detract from his splendor in the slightest; if anything, those breathtakingly gorgeous features complemented the wrath of the heavens, conferring an aura so dignified, sacred, and magnificent that no one dared harbor impure thoughts. What was more, behind him followed a boy and girl who looked like identical celestial attendants. Surely, the descent of True Immortals could be nothing more than this!

Such striking looks, once seen, could never be forgotten. Certain rogue cultivators who had dealt with the Heavenly Spirit Sect immediately recognized his identity:

[—"It's Qu Qianxing!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

[—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Second Disciple!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

[—"Who would have imagined the Heavenly Spirit Sect possessed such grandeur, completely overshadowing the Carefree Palace!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

Whispered discussions erupted in waves.

Zhao Yuegui stared at the Heavenly Spirit Sect cohort, touched his own cheek, and suddenly suffered a total collapse of self-confidence:

[Does the Heavenly Spirit Sect recruit disciples based on looks?! Putting aside that peerless beauty at the head and those two immortal attendants, even the disciples behind them possess striking features and extraordinary grace! Even if I used to be quite confident in my own appearance, I doubt they'd even look at me.]

[What happened to recruitment being a two-way street?!]

Yet the internal lamentations of an insignificant rogue cultivator mattered little; the standoff in the sky was on the verge of exploding.

No matter how dazzling Qu Qianxing looked, it could not mask the reality that he was merely at the Golden Core stage.

The voice from the Carefree Palace's treasure ship grew even more furious:

—"Ignorant whelp! You think you are worthy of speaking to this seat?! Tell your master to come out!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana bellowed.

—"It is merely a minor trial; how could it possibly warrant troubling Lord Master to come in person?"-Qu Qianxing smiled serenely, his expression blossoming like silver petals amid the lightning.-"Since Senior desires so intensely to meet my master, Junior boldly asks for Senior's esteemed name, so that Junior may report it to Master."

The treasure ship fell instantly, dead silent.

Anyone with eyes could see that the Carefree Palace had just [eaten dirt](# "Literal CN: 吃了瘪 (chī le biē) — suffered a humiliating setback / been backed into a corner").

After all, the Carefree Palace's shadowy titan had been forced to speak, yet on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's side, the Untiring Immortal Venerable hadn't even stirred.

After a long pause, someone finally spoke from the ship:

—"Insolent brat! It is one thing to arrive, but why strike our treasure ship with lightning? Do you mistake this mountain summit for the Heavenly Spirit Sect's private courtyard?"-a woman's voice scolded.

This time it was a woman's voice; the earlier 'titan' had vanished into silence.

Clearly, had Zhou Hui appeared in person, two Tribulation Transcendence seniors could at least engage as peers. But with Zhou Hui absent, if Chana dared lay a hand on Qu Qianxing, it would be tantamount to declaring war on the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Recalling that the events of four hundred years ago would surely repeat themselves, the Carefree Palace—which lived by the maxim of [harmony breeds wealth](# "Literal CN: 和气生财 (héqì shēngcái) — traditional merchant maxim: peaceful relations generate profit")—naturally refused to let such a catastrophe occur.

—"This mountain naturally does not belong to my Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Qu Qianxing said, refusing to concede an inch of courtesy.-"However, with your Carefree Palace blocking the entrance to the secret realm, I naturally assumed the entrance had been commandeered by you!"

With that, he extended his palm. A crackling ball of lightning coalesced in his hand, which he tossed toward the airspace the Carefree Palace's treasure ship had previously occupied.

The lightning orb detonated instantly, scattering brilliant silver light that illuminated the surroundings like a sheet of white paper. In the center, however, a shadow bloomed like soaked ink, resembling an inverted mountain peak—one that, if flipped upside down, aligned perfectly with the sheared flat-topped mountain beneath everyone's feet.

Along the inverted peak ran an unmistakable spatial fissure, harboring what seemed to be a pocket dimension inside.

[—"It really is the secret realm?!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

[—"It was hidden in that exact spot?!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

[—"Is Qu Qianxing truly only at Golden Core? I thought spatial rifts like this could only be detected by Nascent Soul cultivators and above!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

The onlookers erupted in astonishment.

Qu Qianxing paid no heed to the chatter. Ignoring the Carefree Palace, he turned his gaze toward the Heavenly Dao Alliance:

—"We trouble Hall Master He to preside over the proceedings,"-Qu Qianxing said.

—"..."-He Yong felt his tongue turn dry and bitter.

On one side was a (suspected) Tribulation Transcendence titan; on the other was the direct disciple of another Tribulation Transcendence supreme. Both sides were revered ancestors, neither of whom he could afford to offend.

Just as He Yong was writhing in misery, a figure strode past him.

Seeing him, He Yong was overjoyed:

[That's right! We have a little ancestor on our side too!]

[As the saying goes, fight magic with magic, and counter ancestors with ancestors!]

The Heavenly Dao Alliance's 'ancestor,' Su Xin, was entirely oblivious to He Yong's desperate hopes. His eyes were shining with starry radiance as he stared fixedly at Qu Qianxing.

Over the past few days, Su Xin had lived in utter gloom. He hadn't dared wander around or speak out of turn, terrified that his parents would remember his blunders and ship him off to Zhou Hui's sect for 're-education.' For this secret realm expedition, he hadn't even dared bring out his prized cloud carriage.

Fortunately, he hadn't. The size of his cloud carriage could never compare to the colossal treasure ship.

Thus, as a [cultivation second-generation](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — child of powerful, wealthy cultivators; equivalent of rich second generation") obsessed with prestige and face, Su Xin had actually been thoroughly annoyed by the Carefree Palace's earlier arrogance. Now that Qu Qianxing had descended from the heavens clad in thunder, Su Xin felt his swagger surging back!

[I didn't seem to notice last time! The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Second Disciple is actually such a breathtaking beauty! Losing an exam to a peerless celestial beauty like him—even when I brag about it later, it won't be shameful at all!]

(Su Xin consciously conveniently forgot that he had lost to *every single disciple* of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, not just Qu Qianxing.)

With his spirits restored, he actually behaved somewhat like a proper human being.

—"Senior Brother Qu is too courteous,"-Su Xin said, putting on the airs of a Heavenly Dao Alliance emissary, though his form of address followed the Heavenly Spirit Sect's seniority. (Then again, Qu Qianxing's master was at Tribulation Transcendence, and his own parents were also at Tribulation Transcendence, so Su Xin felt calling him 'Senior Brother' was no slight to his pride.)-"This secret realm was originally cleaved open by Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's heavenly tribulation; taking the first spot for the Heavenly Spirit Sect is only right and proper."

Even though Su Xin held petty grievances against the Carefree Palace in his heart, he understood their close relationship with the Heavenly Dao Alliance and refrained from offending them:

—"The Palace of Carefree Wandering has traveled from afar as a guest, so they shall enter the secret realm in second position. What does Senior think?"-Su Xin added, turning toward the treasure ship.

—"This little woman hardly dares accept the title of 'Senior,'"-the female voice from earlier spoke from the ship.

A ribbon of rosy twilight mist glided out of the vessel, carrying a retinue of delicate, charming female cultivators. The one who spoke was their leader, appearing to be merely at the Golden Core stage as well.

Evidently, this was the Carefree Palace's actual expedition team for the secret realm.

Under normal circumstances, even if such beauties acted impolitely, their charm would mollify most men's tempers. Unfortunately, amid the lightning and thunder, standing directly opposite them was Qu Qianxing, who looked like a celestial immortal descended to earth. In comparison, the Carefree Palace's painted beauties instantly lost all their luster.

The Carefree Palace maiden took no offense, merely offering a radiant, smiling curtsy toward Qu Qianxing and Su Xin:

—"It was merely that the situation was perilous moments ago, and our venerated elder was worried for my safety, thus intervening through the cloud mirror. I trust Senior Brothers will forgive the intrusion,"-the leading Carefree Palace maiden said with a smiling curtsy.

These words were naturally uttered merely to salvage some scrap of face; everyone laughed politely and let it pass.

Su Xin proceeded to arrange the order of entry for the other sects according to the Heavenly Dao Alliance's original schedule. As this had already been pre-negotiated—in fact, the Carefree Palace purchasing qualifications to enter early was also pre-arranged, with only the Heavenly Spirit Sect truly cutting in uninvited, though no one dared utter a word of complaint—naturally no one raised any objections.

Su Xin addressed Qu Qianxing:

—"Senior Brother Qu, please—"

Qu Qianxing gave him a nod, leading the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples as they stepped into the secret realm.

---

—"What an arrogant second disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana's frantic, buzzing voice reverberated through the hall.-"Let us see if that master of yours can protect you inside the secret realm! Zhao Shun! The preparations this seat ordered you to make—have you carried them all out properly?!"

Inside the Carefree Palace's treasure ship, the scene looked as though a true apocalyptic lightning strike had ripped through it. Near the grand master seat in particular, a deep fissure nearly cleaved the entire hall in two. And Perfected Master Zhao—Zhao Shun—having absorbed the direct brunt of a Tribulation Transcendence sovereign's wrath, was now sprawled on the deck like a battered blood gourd.

Even in this state, Perfected Master Zhao dared not ignore the Sovereign's interrogation.

Trembling violently, he answered:

—"All arrangements... have been completed. I just confirmed it: our planted agents have followed right behind Qu Qianxing, and that pair of furnaces is present as well. Everything is... just as Exalted Sovereign anticipated,"-Zhao Shun stammered.

Right as he spoke, a transmission rune suddenly flared to life.

Struggling, Perfected Master Zhao extended a blood-drenched hand to receive the rune:

—"It is Hongying. Having entered the secret realm, she must be reporting their progress..."-he muttered.

Saying so, he activated the rune, only to hear the panicked voice of the woman named Hongying shrieking from the other end:

—"Steward Zhao, disaster! Qu Qianxing, he... he actually—"-Hongying shrieked in panic from the other end of the rune.

—"Hongying? Hongying! Finish your damn sentence!"-Zhao Shun shrieked in terror, casting a panicked glance toward the cloud mirror on the throne.

Truthfully, whatever happened to the disciples who entered the secret realm mattered nothing to him, but if this provoked the terrifying Sovereign, he was utterly doomed.

And just as Perfected Master Zhao dreaded, that horrific spiritual pressure descended upon him once more:

—"What are you doing, dilly-dallying?! Hand the rune to me!"-the androgynous voice roared.

Along with that command, a vicious gale swept across Perfected Master Zhao. A layer of bloody mist condensed around his body, and with a sickening *pop*, it detonated. The Nascent Soul-stage Fallen Immortal Palace steward had his breath extinguished just that effortlessly.

The shattered rune drifted toward the cloud mirror, yet before it could arrive, the entire treasure ship rocked violently, sending the cloud mirror tumbling off the master throne onto the deck.

—"What happened?!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana bellowed in fury.

This time, there was no one left to answer him.

Fortunately, the cloud mirror was ultimately an artifact connecting two spaces. As long as it existed, regardless of where it rolled or what angle it faced, a Tribulation Transcendence sovereign like Chana could still project his consciousness through it to intervene on this side of the vessel.

Thus, Sovereign Immortal Chana swept his divine sense outside the treasure ship, only to discover that the source of the violent shaking was the very secret realm undergoing exploration!

The secret realm formed from the sheared mountain peak was radiating blinding light, trembling violently as if attempting to tear free from this spatial dimension and soar away. Yet a swirling chain of runic inscriptions danced around its perimeter like glowing shackles, binding it firmly in place. And among them, a rune representing the Heavenly Spirit Sect shone with extraordinary, blinding brilliance.

[—"...Claimed ownership! The secret realm has recognized a master!"](# "Speaker: Knowledgeable Cultivator")

At this moment, knowledgeable cultivators present couldn't help screaming at the top of their lungs:

[—"It recognized the Heavenly Spirit Sect as its master!"](# "Speaker: Knowledgeable Cultivator")
