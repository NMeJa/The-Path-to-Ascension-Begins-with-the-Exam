# Chapter 111: [Observations and Impressions](<# "Literal CN: 见闻 (jiànwén) — Observations and Impressions / Things Seen and Heard; chapter title referring to Shui Huang'er's hilarious and shocking discoveries upon visiting the Heavenly Spirit Sect">) (第111章 见闻)

Whether Mo Bei had actually changed remained unclear, but her status had indisputably transformed.

This wasn't to say her original standing had been lacking.

As a direct disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Youngest Junior Sister of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mo Bei was in every respect a [flower on a high peak](<# "Literal CN: 高岭之花 (gāolǐng zhī huā) — an aloof, unattainable beauty beyond reach">), scarcely inferior to the likes of Qingyang Qingli of Eternal Villa. If she declared her identity, even the haughtiest cultivation clans of Sky City would be forced to treat her with profound deference.

Yet this status was entirely bestowed by her Master.

No one respected Mo Bei herself.

Rather, due to her stunning beauty and her Extreme Yin Constitution, if she failed to promptly declare her background—and at times, even declaring it proved useless—she would inevitably attract no shortage of scoundrels with nefarious designs.

Even her younger twin brother felt this way.

Mo Xi had always believed Mo Bei was far too foolish. Thus, in his eyes, every single male creature that approached his elder sister was either harboring dirty intentions toward her Extreme Yin Constitution, or was a degenerate criminal seeking to take advantage of someone mentally deficient—and in the sixth cycle, this absurd prejudice of his had actually been somewhat validated, which was downright outrageous—so they had to be severely punished.

Consequently, whether it was Mo Xi's fierce protection, the protective affection of other Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, or the awe of outsiders, none of it had anything to do with Mo Bei's own will. No one ever took her genuine thoughts to heart.

At least throughout the previous six cycles, and even during the first half of this present cycle, it had always been this way.

Mo Bei herself took no issue with it.

Strictly speaking, she had never really realized this in the first place, and naturally paid it no mind.

Nor did she feel she had undergone any change at all.

That day, when Mo Bei heard the request from the Southern Domain Saintess, Shui Huang'er, regarding the textbooks and an audience with the Immortal Venerable, she wasn't surprised in the least.

—Although she always mocked her senior brothers and younger brother for being [hopeless Master-simps](<# "Literal CN: 师尊控 (shīzūn kòng) — Master-complex / people obsessed with their master">), she tacitly agreed in her heart that her Master was the greatest under the heavens, and the textbooks written by Master were the finest in the world. For someone to take a liking to them and want them was the most natural thing imaginable.

Besides, wasn't Master intending to promote them everywhere anyway?

Mo Bei nodded promptly:

—"Textbooks? That should be fine..."-Mo Bei said.-"How about this: I'll find Mo Xi—ah, my twin brother—and have him guide you to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. You can ask him for the details!"

This was Mo Bei's habitual reaction.

Whenever it came to explanations and entertaining outsiders, Mo Bei always pawned it off on Mo Xi. For one, Mo Xi loved handling such matters; for another, Mo Xi was far more charming, possessed a silver tongue, and people generally preferred being hosted by him.

Mo Bei initially assumed this time would be no exception.

Yet she saw the azure demon maiden clasp her hands behind her back, fidgeting bashfully as she looked at her with a flushed face and asked:

—"Can't it be you?"-Shui Huang'er asked.

[—"Eh?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Bei">)

—"I wish for Your Excellency Mo Bei to personally introduce me to the Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Shui Huang'er requested even more bluntly.

She admitted she had a bit of selfish motivation in this request! Who wouldn't want an opportunity to [stick close and cuddle with their idol](<# "Literal CN: 贴贴 (tiētiē) — modern slang for hugging, cuddling, or sticking close to someone you adore">)?

Yet purely from an objective standpoint, Shui Huang'er didn't want to switch people either:

Shui Huang'er didn't know what the Heavenly Spirit Sect was like, but even on a single outstretched hand, the five fingers were of different lengths! Parents always had favorites among their children. Thus, whether looking from the perspective of human sects or demon tribes, there would always be favored disciples and unfavored disciples.

Although Mo Bei offered to introduce her twin brother, the reality Shui Huang'er witnessed was that only Mo Bei had been dispatched to Fengqing City, leading an entire army of heroic spirits to maintain security. To claim the Untiring Immortal Venerable didn't value her was utterly impossible.

Most importantly of all, Mo Bei was formidable!

Absurdly, overwhelmingly strong.

If such a disciple wasn't the sect's premier talent being cultivated with maximum focus, who else could be?

Based on these considerations, Shui Huang'er insisted on Mo Bei acting as her introducer.

Had Mo Bei known what was going through Shui Huang'er's mind, she would have certainly told her that her treatment was by no means the most outrageous among the disciples, nor could her strength be considered high among the direct disciples.

Yet before Mo Bei could speak, the exchange between her and Shui Huang'er had already drawn the attention of those around them.

Upon learning that the young girl who had been running around with them was actually from the demon race—and an exalted demon figurehead at that—the female cultivators of Fengqing City couldn't help feeling unsettled. They scrutinized Shui Huang'er from a distance, no one daring to speak for a moment.

Even so, their esteem for Mo Bei and the Heavenly Spirit Sect was undeniably skyrocketing. If they weren't sufficiently extraordinary, how could they have attracted the Saintess of the Southern Domain? They felt an inexplicable sense of shared pride.

As for the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, they were somewhat disgruntled.

—Before leaving the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they had been sternly warned by Mo Xi to watch out and never let suspicious individuals approach Mo Bei (Mo Xi: [That girl gets abducted way too easily!]). Although Shui Huang'er was a girl, her rabid fangirl behavior over recent days had not gone unnoticed by them. Furthermore, rumors said the demon race [played wild and loose](<# "Literal CN: 玩得花 (wán de huā) — indulged in wild, kinky, or unconventional romantic/sexual antics">); who knew whether she harbored untoward desires toward Senior Sister Mo Bei?

[—"Senior Sister Mo Bei already turned you down,"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"Exactly. Matters like this aren't Senior Sister Mo Bei's responsibility in the first place,"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"Are you truly the demon Saintess?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

Several disciples voiced their dissatisfaction.

Hearing this, Mo Bei cast a chilly glare over:—"Do not be insolent!"

Those few disciples instantly froze in terror.

Such deference was something they had never displayed back at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Although these disciples had always respected Mo Bei in the past, when it came to matters like this, they had leaned toward obeying Mo Xi, whose status within the sect appeared higher (purely because he bragged louder).

Yet during these days in Fengqing City, Mo Bei's slaughter had carved out the awe-inspiring prestige of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and with it, forged her absolute authority among her sect's disciples.

With a single sentence, Mo Bei silenced them completely.

Whether they were convinced in their hearts or not, they stammered and bowed in apology to Shui Huang'er.

Evidently, if Mo Bei ever found a boyfriend in the future, even if Mo Xi threw a temper tantrum that reached the heavens, Mo Bei could guarantee her partner wouldn't suffer any bullying within their own sect.

Yet the Mo Bei of this cycle, just like in every preceding cycle, possessed a mind cleaner than if it had been washed with pure water.

That previously bone-deep (physically literal) romance hadn't left the faintest trace in her mind. At this moment, Mo Bei only wanted to safeguard Shui Huang'er, this prospective partner of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Even if she disliked thinking, Mo Bei had watched Master's actions over this period; she understood that an entity like Shui Huang'er held immense value for Master.

Since the other party refused to be received by Mo Xi, she would simply have to do it herself.

—"My apologies. Please forgive these juniors,"-Mo Bei attempted to say politely.-"I haven't handled matters like this before, but if you wish, I will do my best."

—"I don't mind at all,"-Shui Huang'er said sweetly.

She didn't find anything amiss with Mo Bei's attitude; rather, it only deepened her misunderstanding:

As the Immortal Venerable's most cherished disciple, wasn't it completely normal for her not to be adept at entertaining guests?

—"Take your time,"-Shui Huang'er offered considerately.-"Once you've completely resolved matters here in Fengqing City, introducing me to Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable when you return to the Heavenly Spirit Sect will be perfectly fine. There is no rush on our end."

[How could that be permitted?] You aren't in a hurry, but when it comes to 'academies' and 'textbooks', our Master is more anxious than anyone alive!

The corners of Mo Bei's lips curled up, revealing the professional commercial smile drilled into her by Fallen Immortal Palace—a smile usually reserved for paired appearances with Mo Xi, now gifted as a solo exclusive:

—"I will arrange everything."

*

Unlike Mo Xi, Mo Bei was a woman of her word.

A mere three hours later, Mo Bei had settled every detail and ushered Shui Huang'er aboard the Kunpeng bound for the Heavenly Spirit Sect—a process even swifter than if it had been handed over to Mo Xi.

—"I told you there was no need to rush so much,"-Shui Huang'er mumbled.

Elder Tong Ling erupted into thunderous mockery within her consciousness:—"Hahaha! That girl is clearly of a straightforward, resolute temper. How could she possibly fool around with you and waste half the day ignoring proper business?"

"Tsk!" Seeing that her dream of [fangirling on the company dime](<# "Literal CN: 公款追星 (gōngkuǎn zhuīxīng) — pursuing one's celebrity idol using official public/sect expense accounts">) had shattered, Shui Huang'er let out a helpless click of her tongue.

Yet she immediately cheered up again:—"Doesn't that just prove younger sister Mo Bei is genuinely candid and true to herself? She doesn't act like a human at all! So utterly adorable,"-Shui Huang'er gushed, cupping her cheeks like a starry-eyed fangirl.

—"She was never human to begin with; she's half a yin spirit..."-Elder Tong Ling began, but paused mid-sentence, realizing something was off.

Yin spirits were entities steeped in malice, jealousy, and slaughter—deceitful to the bone and fond of trickery. They had nothing whatsoever in common with the word 'straightforward'.

—"Fine, a rather peculiar yin spirit,"-Elder Tong Ling muttered.

Shui Huang'er paid Elder Tong Ling's words no mind.

Her attention had already been diverted.

Although her status as Saintess of the Azure Phoenix Clan of the Southern Realm's Fiery Abyss Divine Tree hadn't brought her any closer to Mo Bei, Shui Huang'er had zero complaints about Mo Bei's lightning-fast arrangements.

Disregarding her fan-goggles, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's transit and reception services were simply too impeccable.

After all, they had originally been designed to transport Void Refinement and higher powerhouses; even when entertaining a Southern Realm Saintess, they were by no means lacking. Furthermore, knowing Shui Huang'er's love for bustling excitement, Mo Bei had specifically arranged a VIP spectator lounge for the Immortal Arts Assembly.

—Following the launch of the Immortal Arts Assembly, Central Domain cultivators frequently hopped between different arenas, leading many to utilize the "Kunpeng Abyss Sea Transit System". As passenger numbers swelled, Ran Deng had remodeled the deck space atop the Kunpeng's back, constructing something akin to an Immortals' Chess clubhouse. Cultivators could play Immortals' Chess, watch thrilling highlights of matches across various arenas via illusory projections, listen to designated commentators read academic journals and dissect match developments, and, naturally, purchase copies of *White Jade Capital Weekly*.

Yet even without all that, Shui Huang'er was thrilled.

Who would have thought that on this journey to the Central Domain, she would have the chance to ride a Kunpeng?

—"Not even in my wildest dreams did I imagine I'd get to voyage through the depths of the Abyss Sea aboard a Kunpeng! A sect capable of achieving this must be truly extraordinary,"-Shui Huang'er exclaimed happily.-"Elder, how come I never heard you mention the Heavenly Spirit Sect before?"

—"Ahem, ahem. Rather than sects, we place greater value upon the beings who founded them,"-Elder Tong Ling coughed, trying to [salvage face](<# "Literal CN: 挽尊 (wǎnzūn) — internet slang for saving face / recovering dignity after an embarrassing blunder">).

—"Then I've rarely heard you mention the Untiring Immortal Venerable either!"-Shui Huang'er pressed.

Elder Tong Ling was struck speechless.

Could he really admit that rumors described the Untiring Immortal Venerable as an austere ascetic obsessed solely with cultivation who never meddled in outside affairs, leading them to believe that even if he ascended, it wouldn't impact the Fiery Abyss Divine Tree? Could such 'fabricated' intelligence even be spoken aloud?

Judging purely by the organization of this Immortal Arts Assembly, Elder Tong Ling certainly didn't believe the Untiring Immortal Venerable was some solitary hermit who knew only bitter cultivation.

To say nothing of the conduct of his disciple, Mo Bei.

[Perhaps we ought to curtail our contact with Carefree Palace in the future! Not a single word they uttered regarding the Untiring Immortal Venerable was true,] Elder Tong Ling thought irritably.

This conviction swelled to its absolute zenith once they disembarked from the Kunpeng, transferred to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's cloud carriage, and arrived before the sect's mountain gate.

—"Shui Huang'er, you have made an exceptionally wise decision!"-Elder Tong Ling suddenly announced.-"We should indeed form an alliance with the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

—"We haven't reached the point of an alliance yet; for now, I'm only interested in their textbooks—or so I'd like to say, but the surprises from the Heavenly Spirit Sect far exceed expectations,"-Shui Huang'er replied.-"Did you discover another surprise?"

—"No. I merely feel that someone who would plant such a 'breathtaking beauty' right at their front gate cannot possibly be a bad person."

[—"Beauty?"](<# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er">)

Shui Huang'er blinked in confusion, peering out of the cloud carriage. Beholding the ancient peach tree fluttering with soft pink petals, she fell silent for a moment.

—"It's not like you two could [crossbreed](<# "Literal CN: □□ (交配) — mate / reproduce / crossbreed; uncensored Chinese text">), right?"-Shui Huang'er asked deadpan.

—"Why are the thoughts of you beasts and birds always so filthy?! I am merely appreciating a fellow wood spirit!"-Elder Tong Ling retorted.

—"At least we don't grow our reproductive organs on the tops of our heads,"-Shui Huang'er countered.

As Shui Huang'er and Elder Tong Ling bickered back and forth, they suddenly spotted a tiny figure trotting out from the mountain gate.

It was a miniature black lamb.

—In the dark of night, had it not been for the superior eyesight of the Azure Phoenix Clan, it would have been nearly impossible to spot.

Shui Huang'er's eyes sparkled:—"Aww, how adorable!"

She could hardly resist leaping out of the carriage to hug the little lamb, when Elder Tong Ling cried out in horror:

—"Adorable?! That is a... Taotie!"

—"A Taotie?!"-Hearing the terrifying name of an ancient ferocious beast, Shui Huang'er instinctively shrank back into her seat, readying herself for battle.

She asked in panic:—"Why does the Heavenly Spirit Sect have a Taotie?!"

No one answered her question.

Yet the Taotie showed not the slightest intention of attacking anyone.

In fact, the creature didn't even spare Shui Huang'er a single glance. It halted beneath the peach tree, nudged its head into its curly fleece, retrieved a long rope from within its wool, and hurled it upward.

As if possessing a life of its own, the rope looped smoothly over a sturdy bough.

The little black lamb clamped one end of the rope in its teeth and tugged hard, seemingly testing its tautness. Having confirmed it was securely anchored, it tied a noose, slipped it around its own neck, and hauled itself upward.

And so, with its neck firmly tied, the little black lamb [hung itself from the tree branch](<# "Literal CN: 把自己挂在树枝上 — Yuwen Xuan hanging himself from the peach tree as a bizarre self-punishment">).

—"What is it doing?"-Shui Huang'er stared in utter bewilderment.-"Is it hanging itself?"

—"Perhaps it's an innovative hunting method of the Taotie?"-Elder Tong Ling found it equally incomprehensible.

Yet dangling from a tree branch was clearly useless for hunting, nor did it look remotely comfortable. At the very least, the little black lamb appeared deeply miserable; it twisted its neck, kicked its stubby little legs, and seemed to struggle for breath.

—"It looks rather pitiful..."-Shui Huang'er murmured, unable to bear the sight even knowing it was a fearsome Taotie.

After a moment's thought, she leaned out of the cloud carriage and inquired of the Heavenly Spirit Sect driver—a sword cultivator who was also one of Mo Bei's subordinates:—"This... sheep, what is going on with it?"

—"Probably [bombed an exam](<# "Literal CN: 没考好 (méi kǎohǎo) — failed or underperformed on an examination">)?"-the disciple replied uncertainly.

—"Bombed an exam?!"-Shui Huang'er exclaimed in shock.

—"It hung up there for a while once before. Back then, we were already guessing it wasn't an ordinary sheep. Seeing this now, it truly isn't ordinary—it's likely a sheep demon enrolled in one of the classes,"-the sword cultivator deduced, having clearly witnessed the little black lamb's self-hanging routine.-"Midterm exams just rolled around again. Having botched its test, it must have hung itself up according to the 'rules'?"

—"Your sect actually has such a 'rule'?!"-Shui Huang'er was flabbergasted.

"..." [Well, not exactly... Could this be a beginner-class student misled by wild rumors?]

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple was about to make that guess.

Yet before he could speak, a voice drifted out from beyond the mountain gate:

—"May I ask if this is the Azure Phoenix Saintess of the Southern Realm's Fiery Abyss Divine Tree?"

Following this inquiry, a peerless beauty accompanied by a youth bearing an uncanny resemblance to Mo Bei strode out from the mountain gate.

Amidst the night, this beauty appeared even more mesmerizing than a mountain spirit.

Yet as this beauty passed through the gate, he looked up and froze at the sight of the black lamb hanging overhead. Meeting his gaze, the black lamb's tears began to patter downward, splashing into the soil beneath the tree, looking beyond pitiful.

—"Second Senior Brother, why is this sheep hanging up there again?"-Mo Xi said upon seeing the scene.-"Should we go plead for leniency?"

—"...I'll ask Master later?"-Qu Qianxing replied uncertainly.

Hearing their conversation, the disciple driving the carriage wore an enlightened expression, murmuring in a low voice:—"So that's how it is! By hanging oneself up, one at least preserves some face; furthermore, seeing how miserable it looks, there might even be a chance for a [sentence reduction](<# "Literal CN: 减刑 (jiǎnxíng) — commutation of punishment / reduction of penal sentence">)."

[...Is bombing a midterm exam in your Heavenly Spirit Sect truly such a terrifying catastrophe?!]

Shui Huang'er thought for the second time.

Although she had vaguely sensed something back at Qiao Family Village, arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect made it feel infinitely more terrifying!

—"If we import the Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks, theoretically we should also import their pedagogical methodology! Including their methods of punishment..."-Shui Huang'er whispered softly.

—"Shui Huang'er, do you find it inappropriate?"-Elder Tong Ling asked.

—"No, I think it's even more magnificent!"-Shui Huang'er declared gleefully.

If they only had textbooks, how could they possibly intimidate that unruly horde of demons back home?! With the accompanying 'pedagogical methods', everything felt reassuringly secure all of a sudden!

Beaming with satisfaction, Shui Huang'er stepped forward toward Qu Qianxing.

*

—"Your Excellency Shui Huang'er, why do you wish to import our curriculum?"-Mo Xi inquired curiously.

He scrutinized Shui Huang'er with an evaluating gaze.

In truth, what he really wanted to ask was: 'Can a single person like you actually make the final call?'

Not that Mo Xi doubted Shui Huang'er's identity—her credentials had already been authenticated by Master, after all. Rather, Mo Xi found it bizarre that Shui Huang'er was completely alone (a lone demon).

Importing educational manuals, even if solely for the Azure Phoenix Clan, could hardly be considered a trivial matter. Sending only a young girl like her—wasn't that a bit too frivolous?

Sensing Mo Xi's skepticism, Shui Huang'er couldn't help curling her lips into a smile:—"Although you are twin siblings, you and younger sister Mo Bei are entirely different!"

—"Ah, now this is the human (yin spirit) I am familiar with!"-Tong Ling chimed in.

...?

Mo Xi felt inexplicably offended.

—"Xi'er, do not be impolite!"-Qu Qianxing shot Mo Xi a warning glance.-"The demon race imports human teaching manuals every year. We are nothing extraordinary."

—"Indeed. Who told us to be incapable of ascending on our own?"-Shui Huang'er said with a faint smile.

Qu Qianxing looked at Shui Huang'er with an indescribable expression.

Shui Huang'er took no offense; she even took the opportunity to educate Mo Xi on the matter:

The demon race possessed no native path of ascension.

Although classifications like Demon Emperors existed, and a Demon Realm was acknowledged, it was by no means a higher realm; at best, it could only be considered a neutral territory, and one heavily contaminated by demonic qi.

For demons to ascend, one method was taking the human path: shedding all demon characteristics, cultivating intellect, assuming human form, [purging away bestial instincts](<# "Literal CN: 修掉□□ (兽性/欲念) — shedding animal instincts and primal desires; uncensored text">), and finally becoming a True Immortal just like a human.

Another method was taking the spirit beast path: relinquishing intellect, casting aside dignity, degenerating completely into beasts, and existing merely as pets or mounts for True Immortals.

The final method was falling into the demonic path: [indulging base lusts and malice](<# "Literal CN: 扩大□□ (魔念/兽欲) — expanding demonic cravings and unbridled desires; uncensored text">), plunging into utter madness.

—"Leaving the demonic path aside for the moment, between the human route and the beast route, when one can be a master, who would willingly be a slave?"-Shui Huang'er explained.-"Thus, the majority of the demon race chooses the path of human transformation. This naturally means we must study human cultivation methods, so importing relevant manuals is entirely routine."

—"Our Heavenly Spirit Sect's academy textbooks cannot grant you ascension,"-Mo Xi reminded her.

After all, those were elementary primers—not even meant for cultivators, but designed for ordinary mortals to study.

—"I know, but I feel they might well be more useful than methods that 'grant ascension,'"-Shui Huang'er replied.

In truth, studying human cultivation methods was an act of pure necessity.

Demons had their own arts; unless driven to desperate straits, who would willingly erase their own heritage and transform completely into another species?

Those textbooks, however, were not cultivation manuals, but blueprints for societal construction.

Shui Huang'er smiled:—"It reminded me that sometimes, we shouldn't merely gaze up at the canopy of the tree; we must also examine its deep roots. That is what truly matters."

—"You have remarkable vision,"-Qu Qianxing appraised her.

—"Heheh, I think so too!"-Shui Huang'er giggled, cupping her cheeks.-"Where are we heading next?"-she asked Qu Qianxing.

—"You arrived at the perfect time. Master should be free now; I'll take you to see him,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

—"So quickly?!"-Shui Huang'er's eyes widened.

She had assumed that according to human etiquette—no, even among demons, no guest who arrived in the dead of night was immediately ushered to meet the chieftain unless it was a matter of dire life-and-death emergency.

Mo Xi curled his lips:—"Didn't you ask my sister to arrange it?"

—"Ah, as expected of younger sister Mo Bei!"-Shui Huang'er gushed instantly.

Mo Xi felt subtly insulted yet again.

Lowering his voice, he whispered to Qu Qianxing:—"This one is acting weird. She couldn't be harboring ulterior motives, could she?"

Qu Qianxing pressed down on his head:—"Put a limit on your possessiveness over your sister."

—"I'm not doing that!"-Mo Xi protested.

Qu Qianxing, on the other hand, felt Mo Bei's arrangement was spot on. Master appeared just as eager to meet this young Saintess; the timing aligned flawlessly.

As if echoing Qu Qianxing's thoughts, when they arrived beneath Zhou Hui's cave dwelling, Qu Qianxing saw Lu Shulong emerging to greet them.

Contrasting with Qu Qianxing's moonlit epiphyllum-like splendor, Lu Shulong resembled a secluded forest orchid illuminated by fireflies—pure and refined, yet tinged with a melancholy hue.

Tonight in particular, for reasons unknown, his melancholy had deepened significantly.

Seeing him like this, Qu Qianxing furrowed his brows in worry.

Shui Huang'er, however, had no room for such delicate sentiments; she was frozen in sheer shock:—"...A dragon?!"

—"What on earth is this sect?! As if a yin spirit and a Taotie weren't enough, they even have a True Dragon?!"-Elder Tong Ling cried in utter disbelief.

—"Your Excellency Saintess, welcome to the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-this dragon said, displaying none of the legendary draconic majesty, but rather offering a frail, weary smile.-"My Master is currently waiting for you all."

—"I'll bring them inside,"-Qu Qianxing said.

Though he couldn't bear to do it, obeying Master's voice transmission, he retrieved a thick stack of test papers from his storage pouch:—"Go finish these papers first... Master said you are to retake the [diagnostic baseline assessment exam](<# "Literal CN: 摸底考试 (mōdǐ kǎoshì) — diagnostic baseline assessment exam / placement test used to gauge a student's true level">)."

Lu Shulong froze petrified on the spot.

Even a dragon was subjected to this?!

At long last, Shui Huang'er empathized down to her very soul with the sheer, unadulterated terror of 'examinations'.
