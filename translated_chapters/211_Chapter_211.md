# Chapter 211: Trial System

*Is this... Mo Bei of the Heavenly Spirit Sect?*

Xia Bing thought with immense uncertainty.

While she had never possessed the opportunity to speak with Mo Bei directly, she had glimpsed her from afar on numerous occasions. Moreover, having made the acquaintance of a fanatical devotee like Yu Feifei, every major festival as "business partners" saw her forcefully gifted a mountain of Mo Bei merchandise—ranging from Immortal Chess figurines to decorative folding fans and hanging posters emblazoned with Mo Bei's portrait—to the point where failing to recognize Mo Bei's face was practically impossible.

In truth, as the two most favored frontrunners of the Immortal Arts Assembly, bolstered by the Heavenly Spirit Sect's overwhelming home-court advantage, promotional merchandise of Mo Bei and Lu Shulong was plastered everywhere, ensuring virtually everyone under heaven recognized their features.

The reason Xia Bing felt uncertain now was that the "enemy" before her looked like Mo Bei, yet simultaneously was not Mo Bei.

To be precise, it was a corpse.

A colossal corpse.

This corpse stood a staggering five meters tall. It appeared to have plummeted from catastrophic heights, such that its spine, waist, and thighs were crushed into a pulpy paste, exposing the skeletal framework within. Yet even so, it did not appear gruesome.

For the flesh and blood of this corpse resembled pristine ice and snow, while its bones possessed the translucent texture of flawless crystal. Even spilling across the floor, it radiated an aura of pure, untouched chastity—bordering on breathtaking beauty, akin to a peerless crystal masterwork shattered upon the ground, evoking heartfelt lamentation.

Particularly since this shattered 'work of art' possessed a delicate visage virtually identical to Mo Bei's.

That head rested awkwardly upon the floor, its neck twisted at an unnatural, broken angle. Enormous, hollow eyes gazed aimlessly ahead like a ruined puppet doll. A single trail of bloody tears trickled from the corner of its eye, invoking an inexplicable surge of sorrow.

Witnessing this corpse-puppet doll after seeing Mo Bei dance so gallantly with her sword in real life made the tragedy feel all the more visceral.

Xia Bing couldn't resist taking two steps toward the corpse.

Instantly, a sequence of foreign memories that did not belong to her surged into her mind.

Xia Bing's heart tightened, reprimanding herself for her carelessness.

When facing adversaries associated with corpses, ghosts, and deceased wraiths, direct frontal attacks were never the true hazard; the deadliest threats were psychic memory tampering and spiritual soul manipulation. In such predicaments, forgetting one's true identity was merely the mildest outcome; in worst-case scenarios, one was directly subjugated into a [chang ghost](# "Literal CN: 伥鬼 (chāngguǐ) — a ghost of someone eaten by a tiger that helps the tiger lure new victims; a thrall").

Xia Bing frantically circulated her spiritual energy to erect defenses, only to discover that while this memory stream was impossible to repel, it harbored zero malice.

It functioned purely like an instructional tome—a paragraph of text informing Xia Bing of a 'lore premise.'

Indeed, a lore setting!

Through this memory stream, Xia Bing learned that "she" was an ancient Mahayana cultivator hailing from White Jade Capital during the God-Demon War ten thousand years ago. Wounded in battle, her cultivation had plummeted; separated from the main vanguard, she had inadvertently plunged into the deepest abyss of the Primordial Sea, discovering this colossal corpse that had slumbered in the oceanic depths for untold aeons.

Xia Bing finally understood:

—"So that's it... The trial objective is to defeat this corpse? Why does this even have a narrative campaign?"

Even without experiencing the Thousand Star Secret Realm, she could readily deduce this much.

In truth, cultivators were remarkably experienced in this domain.

While they had never played tabletop roleplaying games, whether dealing with ghosts and heart demons or descending to the mortal realm to undergo karmic tribulations, occasions where one had to assume another persona to resolve a crisis were commonplace. Even novices who had never experienced it had heard similar tales from their elders; as long as they grasped the premise, they understood their objective, sparing them from feeling lost.

Yet after absorbing the memory and understanding the trial's premise, Xia Bing did not relax; on the contrary, she observed the corpse with intensified vigilance.

After all, whether it was the 'lore cues'—the God-Demon War, a Mahayana elder, the depths of the Primordial Sea—or that face bearing uncanny resemblance to Mo Bei, all signaled that this opponent was extraordinarily formidable.

—"Still, why use Junior Sister Mo Bei's face? For a simulated combat trial, any appearance would do!"

Xia Bing felt slightly bewildered.

Yet she was granted no further leisure to ponder. The instant Xia Bing fully absorbed the memory, the unfocused, heterochromatic eyes upon that exquisite face twitched violently. Rolling unnaturally in their sockets, they locked their glare squarely upon Xia Bing! Immediately following, a suffocating, bone-chilling aura enveloped her entire being.

Even though Xia Bing had braced herself, she was startled half to death.

Hundreds of Fire Flying Ants surged from her robes like airborne sparks of flame, dancing around her perimeter in disciplined battle formation.

Across the arena, the colossal corpse began to move.

Yet a broken corpse could not move naturally. Its limbs and joints shuddered erratically, twisting into awkward angles as it rose—resembling a shattered marionette yanked upright by invisible marionette strings. Its torso and legs moved on completely independent trajectories, lacking fluid connection.

Seeing this opening, Xia Bing commanded her Fire Flying Ants to assault the corpse without the slightest hesitation.

—In the cultivation world, battles were waged with life and death on the line; there was no chivalric rule granting 'prep time' to an adversary.

Furthermore, Xia Bing recognized that once this corpse achieved fluid motion, opportunities to direct her swarm would vanish!

Reality unfolded precisely as Xia Bing anticipated.

As the dense swarm of Fire Flying Ants descended upon that snowy flesh, scorching patches of blistering burns erupted across the corpse. It was a heartbreaking sight to behold, yet the corpse remained completely expressionless, entirely oblivious to physical pain.

Lifting an arm with disjointed stiffness, Mo Bei's customary frost broadsword coalesced in an instant. Accompanied by a blizzard of whistling sword winds, it cleaved straight toward Xia Bing!

*This is... Mo Bei's signature sword technique?!*

*She is genuinely terrifying!*

*It looked formidable when watching from the sidelines, but experiencing it firsthand is terrifying beyond belief!*

*Fortunately, its gigantic proportions mean the spatial gaps between sword arts have widened proportionally, granting me room to maneuver. What would happen if I faced the real girl?!*

*Fairy Mo Bei publicized her signature techniques so openly within this trial—isn't she worried about being thoroughly analyzed and countered?*

*Isn't this far too arrogant?!*

Xia Bing's mind spun in chaotic turbulence.

However, she was clearly miles away from 'seeing through' Mo Bei's sword arts. Losing focus for a split second, an icy sword sweep nearly slammed directly into her face!

Though Xia Bing dodged with razor-thin margin, more than half of her Fire Flying Ants were instantly obliterated.

Xia Bing hastily focused her concentration, throwing herself entirely into combating the corpse.

***

While competitors like Xia Bing were pleasantly (?) undergoing their trials, Ran Deng was watching Mo Bei with profound concern:

—"Beier, why don't you take a rest?"

They were currently stationed within Mo Xi's Water Mirror Tower.

Naturally, unlike the haunted, illusory Water Mirror Tower in the pocket dimension of the mountain grand array that Lin Qi had infiltrated, the authentic Water Mirror Tower within the Heavenly Spirit Sect was brilliantly illuminated day and night due to the bustling commerce of the Immortal Bazaar. Endless crowds gathered here to negotiate business deals; in Mo Xi's words, tens of thousands of spirit stones circulated through here every minute.

Beyond that, the Water Mirror Tower served as both a surveillance nexus and an engineering command hub.

Whether the Thousand Star Secret Realm nearby or the Spirit Stone Secret Realm bordering the Demon Domain, all were linked to mirrors routed here, allowing Mo Xi and his personnel to monitor conditions in real time and calibrate realm mechanics on the fly.

Capitalizing on this necessity, Mo Xi had aggressively expanded the Water Mirror Tower.

Thanks to this expansion, abundant reserve equipment was available when sudden emergencies arose.

For instance, at this moment, hundreds of mirrors of diverse crystalline materials floated before Mo Bei, their reflective surfaces depicting the very combat trials Xia Bing and other competitors were actively undergoing!

Xia Bing was merely one among many. Before Mo Bei's eyes unfolded countless concurrent battlefields; every competitor in the tournament who had "randomly" rolled the 'Ancient Immortal Remains' encounter was mirrored across these crystalline surfaces.

Mo Bei was staring intently at these combat feeds.

No, she wasn't merely staring.

In the colossal liuli glaze mirror at the center, an early-stage Nascent Soul sword cultivator had grown thoroughly enraged after repeatedly struggling against the corpse's assaults. With a thunderous roar, he unleashed his ultimate technique; a blazing broadsword slipped through the gaps in the corpse's sword form, striking its torso and blasting a spray of crystalline bloody sparks!

At the opposite end of the mirror, an identical wound sliced across Mo Bei's body.

Although the injury was not as horrific as the shattered corpse within the mirror, a long sword furrow opened across Mo Bei's arm, crimson beads of blood trickling down her skin.

Mo Bei was not an unfeeling corpse after all; a muffled groan of agony escaped her lips.

Yet her actions mirrored the corpse itself. Paying the wound zero heed and disregarding Ran Deng's concern, she locked her gaze upon the screen, manipulating her flexible blade to counterstrike with frigid resolve.

Yes—manipulating.

In the cultivation world, a "mirror" was never a simple looking glass, nor was it a mere display terminal; it was a 'pathway.'

Especially for energetic beings like wraiths and Yin spirits, what was seen was where one existed.

They could effortlessly permeate the boundary of the mirror, projecting their true form and raw spiritual power into the world reflected on the other side.

As a half-Yin spirit, Mo Bei naturally commanded this power.

It was merely that she had always cultivated as an orthodox sword cultivator; prior to this current cycle, even Zhou Hui had never discovered the Yin spirit constitution of Mo Bei and Mo Xi, naturally never instructing them in corresponding occult arts, leaving Mo Bei somewhat clumsy in wielding this power.

Yet what felt far clumsier to Mo Bei was the Ancient Immortal Remains stationed at the other end of the mirror.

This Immortal Remains was an entity derived from the 'prophecy.'

In the 'prophecy' Mo Bei had witnessed, Qu Qianxing fell to demonic corruption, Guan Ye became a ghost cultivator, Lu Shulong was reduced to a bone dragon, and Mo Bei and Mo Xi manifested as colossal immortal remains and spectral spirits, acting as a fourth faction waging war against the Celestial Realm.

Yet to say these immortal remains and spectral spirits were Mo Bei and Mo Xi was both true and untrue.

Naturally, Zhou Hui would never wish for his precious disciples to become such grotesque horrors.

Consequently, Zhou Hui's directive to Guan Ye had been a far more straightforward 'God-Demon War expansion pack.' While competitors and spectators could enter trials through meditation during the assembly to experience the 'God-Demon War' campaign—allowing them to comprehend the origins of the world and uncover historical truths while harvesting 'essential data' for Zhou Hui—Master had never ordered his disciples to personally act as final dungeon bosses!

The concept of acting as the final dungeon bosses was an initiative Mo Bei had devised herself.

Utilizing the 'Future Simulator' of the Fire Abyss Divine Tree, she sought to simulate 'the projected forms of her martial siblings and herself in the prophecy' to duel against competitors, thereby seizing that portion of 'future power' ahead of schedule—ensuring that if the prophecy ever came to pass, they wouldn't be caught entirely without countermeasures.

Mo Bei's proposal had yielded remarkable results.

Setting aside whether she had truly secured 'future power,' during the process of 'simulating the future,' the direct disciples discovered that manifesting this 'future' appeared to require specific prerequisite conditions.

Among them, setting aside Qu Qianxing who was absent, the simulation ran smoothest for Guan Ye.

As everyone knew, the 'Academic Guidance System' of the Fire Abyss Divine Tree extracted all characteristics of the target to simulate their prospective trajectories; Guan Ye undeniably satisfied every prerequisite to become a 'Ghost Sovereign,' succeeding on his very first attempt.

Regarding this, Lu Shulong—who understood the dark truth—could barely bear to watch.

The next to succeed were Mo Bei and Mo Xi, though their simulation encountered minor hiccups.

No matter how they modeled the twins' future, it mirrored the trials they had already overcome at the Fire Abyss Divine Tree—bright and glorious—lacking any prerequisite to transform into immortal remains or spectral spirits.

It was Mo Xi who had a 'stroke of genius,' feeding data from the corpses resting in the 'Snow Glow Grass Cemetery' near the Divine Tree into the simulation, finally generating the colossal corpse seen in the 'prophecy'!

This was a 'future' forged from authentic True Immortal remains, possessing staggering might.

In raw cultivation alone, it commanded True Immortal caliber, compounded by the unbreakable physical density of celestial bones and the debilitating curses of Yin spirits. Moreover, the immortal remains within the 'Snow Glow Grass Cemetery' were virtually inexhaustible.

Such a legion was hardly inferior to the celestial host or Qu Qianxing's demonic horde.

—Conversely, from this army of immortal remains, one could extrapolate the terrifying might of Qu Qianxing's demon army and Guan Ye's ghost legion.

At the time, Mo Xi had couldn't resist roasting:

—"What in the world is this future? Are we staging an uprising to overthrow the Heavenly Court?"

—"That sounds rather entertaining. Jie-jie,"-Guan Ye had chuckled.

While Guan Ye felt guilty about hiding this from Master, he cared nothing for the survival of the Heavenly Court. If anything, even without prior memories, he felt that a Heavenly Court that persistently barred Master from ascending was definitely a rotten enterprise.

When even the most 'honest' Guan Ye felt this way, the other disciples naturally shared identical sentiments.

Alas, even though this 'Immortal Remains' was successfully fabricated, it remained fundamentally incompatible with both Mo Bei and Mo Xi.

Mo Xi in particular: having swapped his spiritual roots, he could no longer assume Yin spirit form.

Meanwhile, Mo Bei retained her pristine Ultimate Yin Constitution, yet she discovered that as long as she maintained conscious awareness, she could never merge smoothly with the celestial corpse. It felt as though a veil separated them; consequently, while the corpse commanded True Immortal power, it moved like a clumsy hand-puppet wielded by an inexperienced little girl, leaving gaping holes in its sword arts and power flow—precisely what Xia Bing was experiencing.

However, if she relinquished her conscious mind, would she still be herself?

Having regained the tragic memories of the sixth cycle, Mo Bei refused to reenact that pathetic, helpless existence.

Especially since this corpse was the 'future'! If she and Mo Xi degenerated into this state, didn't that mean she and Mo Xi had been stripped of their souls, reduced once more to puppet playthings?!

Such a fate must never come to pass!

So what if she was clumsy? So what if they were incompatible?!

If she lacked mastery, she would practice again and again!

As long as she manipulated it a thousand times, ten thousand times, she would eventually achieve total mastery!

Armed with this unbreakable resolve, Mo Bei forcibly funneled her consciousness into those reflective panes, clashing with competitors across the trials again and again, steadily deepening her mastery over this 'future.'

In this state of hyper-focus, she was completely deaf to external voices.

Yet as Mo Bei entered this transcendental state, a faint golden shadow steadily coalesced beneath her feet.

Mo Bei herself was utterly oblivious; only Ran Deng, observing from the side, widened her eyes in astonishment.

Ran Deng examined that shadow with meticulous care.

She noticed its contour mirrored the sect's sundial clock. Yet the shadow representing the passage of time was not moving clockwise; it was frozen in place, faintly ticking backward.

And alongside this reverse passage of time, the golden radiance radiating from the sundial intensified steadily.

*Speaking of which... Master intended to harvest something through this trial system, correct?*

*Could Little Junior Sister be responding directly to Master's requirements?*

Realizing this, Ran Deng refrained from disturbing Mo Bei further.

Turning around, she walked toward the other side of the chamber.

Over there, Mo Xi, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong were locked in an intense, animated debate.

—"Is it still impossible to forge?"-Ran Deng asked,-"The bone dragon."

Indeed. Despite being a prophesied future, every attempt to simulate Lu Shulong's 'bone dragon' ended in total failure. At this moment, the mirrors before Guan Ye and the others merely displayed an anthology of Lu Shulong's gruesome deaths, resembling an exhaustive catalog of fatal demises that was simply unbearable to witness.

—"I told you! Even if that future is authentic, that bone dragon isn't Shulong!"-Guan Ye exhaled a sigh of relief,-"How could I ever butcher my own junior brother?!"

—"Then you found yourself another dragon?!"-Lu Shulong protested with even greater indignation.

Was being refined into a bone dragon some glorious honor?!

Mo Xi rolled his eyes, refusing to entertain this idiot senior brother:

—"If there isn't one, then there isn't one. I feel the data from the Immortal Remains is more than enough to populate the trial grounds. Besides, the Fire Abyss Divine Tree is synchronizing with the trial system as we speak; if something goes haywire, I won't be able to account to Master. Let's call it a day!"

—"Out of the question!"-Lu Shulong was on the verge of throwing a tantrum on the floor,-"Everyone else can simulate their forms, proving this future can exist! Why am I the only one who can't?!"

Saying this, Lu Shulong inadvertently blurted out his true feelings:

—"It looked so unbelievably cool!"

—"Cool?!"-Mo Xi and Guan Ye cried out in unison.

They turned their eyes once more toward the footage extracted from Mo Bei's memories—which Mo Xi had looped on repeat for analysis.

On the screen hovered the visage of that bone dragon: a savage, rotting dragon skull, with two pitch-black hollow eye sockets glaring straight at the viewer, as terrifying and hideous as anything imaginable.

—"Look how magnificent it is!"-Lu Shulong praised with heartfelt admiration, before casting a look of utter disgust toward his own reflection in the adjacent mirror.

Even when pulling a scowl, that gorgeous face radiated a fragile, melancholic elegance, not to mention the delicate ice-blue scales along his cheeks and the exquisite dragon horns upon his brow.

It didn't look cool in the slightest!

Guan Ye had never imagined Lu Shulong would obsess over lacking intimidating martial ferocity to the point of willingly becoming a bone dragon; he assumed only Second Senior Brother suffered from that complex! By comparison, Mo Xi possessed the healthiest detachment; as long as profits were made, he had zero qualms crossdressing as Mo Bei—naturally, such a mindset wasn't entirely wholesome either, but having shed his Ultimate Yin Constitution, once Mo Xi grew a bit taller, he would lose the opportunity to crossdress anyway.

Guan Ye sighed:

—"Fine, I will fetch several other materials to see if we can simulate refining that bone dragon—Eldest Senior Sister, what's the matter?"-Guan Ye changed the subject, turning his gaze toward Ran Deng.

Ran Deng let out a wistful sigh:

—"Watching you all roleplay as final dungeon bosses looks rather entertaining."

—"Does Eldest Senior Sister wish to become a corpse too?"-Mo Xi grinned with mischievous malice,-"Or do you wish to fall to demonic corruption?"

There was no helping it; they had scrutinized that 'future' dozens of times, yet had never found a trace of Ran Deng, indicating she was absent from that timeline.

—"What is so great about becoming a ghost or a corpse?"-Guan Ye tapped the back of Mo Xi's head, reassuring Ran Deng,-"Your absence from that future is a blessing; it proves you remained by Master's side throughout."

*Or perhaps I simply perished prematurely, just like all those previous lifetimes.*

Ran Deng thought, lowering her eyelashes. Then, turning a resolute gaze upon Guan Ye, she declared:

—"However, I have zero sense of participation! Why don't you add me in as well, to give these competitors a proper 'surprise'?"

As she spoke, a wisp of flame sparked between her brows, descending onto the mirror.

—"Tsk... What is wrong with you people? Is being a final boss genuinely that thrilling?"

Guan Ye grumbled, shaking his head.

***

*Truthfully, being the villain might actually be quite entertaining.*

Zhou Hui thought, gazing up into the starry sky.

What he was observing at this moment was the exact same 'future' his disciples were scrutinizing.

Naturally, Zhou Hui knew this future would never come to pass. If anything, it was precisely because the Heavenly Court had foreseen this 'future' that they had resolved to annihilate this world and purge these 'threats.'

The primary threat in the eyes of the Heavenly Court was actually Mo Bei and Mo Xi.

Or rather, the boundless Immortal Remains represented by Mo Bei and Mo Xi.

Those immortal remains were the corpses of ancient True Immortals who had plummeted from the heavens while defending against the demon realm. As celestial bodies, even in death they commanded extraordinary value, and were typically recycled.

Yet the immortal remains of this realm had endured for far too many aeons, generating colossal Yin spirits like unrefined radioactive ore. No matter how the Heavenly Court extrapolated the future, those immortal remains inevitably morphed into apocalyptic weapons threatening the celestial host. Compounded by the temporal and spatial instability of this realm, they became the foremost priority marked for liquidation.

Regrettably, while the Heavenly Court maintained this dogmatic stance, the True Immortals who learned of this refused to abandon such priceless treasures.

They understood the hazards of immortal corpses, yet each arrogant celestial assumed that skimming 'a little bit' in secret would cause no harm. Thus arose the Yin spirit human experimentation of Xiaoyao Palace; thus arose the deceptive exploitation of Mo Bei during the sixth cycle.

From this perspective, the 'threat' of the Immortal Remains was indeed an inescapable doom within the Heavenly Court's prophecies.

Born entirely from celestial greed.

Next, the Heavenly Court's perceived threats were True Dragons and genius artisans.

These two belonged to the Heavenly Court's 'routine' category of threats.

Given the Heavenly Court's tyrannical habit of looking down upon mortal realms with contempt, where there was oppression, there was resistance. In the vast majority of minor worlds, even Creator Gods were powerless against the celestial host, brutally suppressed into silence.

Yet among these rebels, extraordinary prodigies occasionally arose, capable of dealing severe shocks to the Heavenly Court. The Heavenly Court's response was crude and ruthless: calculating their locations, exiling them to doomed worlds, and obliterating them en masse.

Guan Ye was the prime exemplar.

His bone structure and natural genius destined his life to be fraught with tribulations, coupled with an uncompromising, unyielding stubbornness. Without a transcendent master to guide him, he was virtually guaranteed to embark on the path of open rebellion.

The Heavenly Court lacked the patience to tame him, seeking only to crush him and harvest his Dao Bone.

As for Lu Shulong... Lu Shulong was completely innocent. His father had been a 'criminal dragon' of the celestial realm, who during the ancient God-Demon War could not stomach the Heavenly Court abandoning this mortal realm to slaughter. He had aided humanity in severing the celestial umbilical cord binding this world to the heavens, for which he was executed and discarded here—only to be refined into a bone dragon by Guan Ye in the 'prophesied future,' becoming an instrument of cosmic vengeance.

—Indeed: that bone dragon was not Lu Shulong; it was Lu Shulong's father.

The Heavenly Court had merely applied guilt by association to Lu Shulong.

Besides, would the son of a criminal dragon fail to rebel against the Heavenly Court?

In the eyes of the Heavenly Court, there was no difference: both Lu Shulong and Guan Ye were 'threats' marked for eradication.

The final threat lay in 'sinful ministers.'

Where there were criminal dragons, there were naturally criminal ministers—and quite a few.

The Ran Clan, to which Ran Deng belonged, was one such line.

Strictly speaking, they belonged to the Heavenly Court's faction; but due to an engineering blunder during grand construction—a mundane occurrence in engineering projects—they were stripped of their longevity and exiled to the mortal realm to atone for their sins.

Yet while labeled atonement, insignificant formation masters held zero weight, and their destruction was inconsequential.

Ran Deng had simply been swept up as collateral damage.

And compared to the Ran Clan, the one whose crimes were truly unpardonable was Qu Qianxing!

A pillar of the Big Dipper, yet daring to betray the Heavenly Court!

This Wenqu Star, born with rebellious bones, would inevitably harbor disgust toward the stagnant Heavenly Court across every conceivable future. He could not be reformed and adamantly resisted indoctrination; to the Heavenly Court, he was the ultimate blasphemy.

Naturally, he had to be purged!

Thus, the Heavenly Court gathered all these "threats" into a doomed world, scripting Heavenly Dao to obliterate them all.

*Wait... Did I forget someone?*

*Ah, right. Yuwen Xuan.*

However, in that alternate timeline, no human woman had ever given birth to him, so it was unknown if he was even named Yuwen Xuan.

Regardless, this Taotie was not the Heavenly Court's responsibility; he had never been on their hit list. The problem lay in the fact that in the future the Heavenly Court desperately sought to avert—just as the celestial host, Qu Qianxing's demon army, Guan Ye's ghost legion, and legions of immortal remains were locked in apocalyptic slaughter threatening the very heart of the Heavenly Court...

A titanic Taotie popped out of nowhere and—*GULP!*—swallowed everything in a single bite!

Every single thing, completely devoured!

The entire future concluded in utter bewilderment.

Aside from that gluttonous Taotie who [reaped the fisherman's spoils](# "Literal CN: 坐收渔翁之利 (zuò shōu yúwēng zhī lì) — profiting from others' conflict like the fisherman catching both the snipe and the clam") and stuffed its belly to bursting, nobody gained a shred of benefit!

Witnessing this conclusion, Zhou Hui felt he could empathize with the Heavenly Court's frustration:

Dying at the hands of rebels like Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye was at least an honorable, glorious defeat; perishing to destiny was a poetic death. But getting swallowed whole by a rogue beast—what kind of garbage was that?!

*Is this a trash web novel with an abrupt ending? The readers would riot!*

No wonder the Heavenly Court had expended immense effort to unearth the Taotie first, seeking to eliminate it ahead of schedule.

Though it seemed they had still underestimated Yuwen Xuan.

Even though Yuwen Xuan ended up missing in every reincarnation cycle, it was precisely because he existed outside destiny that nobody knew what mischief he had wrought behind the scenes.

Thinking of this, Zhou Hui smiled faintly, waving his sleeve to wipe away this projection—particularly the fat Taotie sprawled across the cosmic void with its swollen belly in the final frame.

While the Heavenly Court detested this future, Zhou Hui held little affection for it either.

—A future where only Yuwen Xuan ate his fill—what kind of good future was that?

—"However, even to avert such a future, manipulating the destinies of living beings—even an entire minor world—is utterly unpardonable,"-Zhou Hui said coldly,-"To assume that altering Heavenly Dao grants absolute control over a world is the height of celestial arrogance! Especially when this world has always harbored the seeds of defiance!"

Saying this, he turned his gaze toward the sundial clock.

At this moment, alongside the commencement of the Immortal Arts Assembly and the activation of the trial system, countless cultivators were plunging into the trials. What they did not realize was that as they underwent their trials, golden divine luminescence flickered upon the face of the sundial clock.

—"The harvesting of temporal 'causality' is proceeding remarkably smoothly,"-Zhou Hui chuckled softly,-"I wonder if Qianxing can feel it."
