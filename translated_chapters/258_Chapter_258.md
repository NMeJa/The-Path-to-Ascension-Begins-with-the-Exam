# Chapter 258: Three Hundred Years Later (Conclusion)

When Long Tianming regained consciousness once more, he thought he had transmigrated yet again.

First came the vaguely familiar scent of traditional Chinese herbal medicine. Opening his eyes, what greeted his vision was a plain white ceiling and blue privacy curtains, evoking the distinct sensation that he was back in the small community clinic downstairs from his home.

Or perhaps... it wouldn't be half bad if everything before had simply been a dream.

After all, nearly being possessed and used as a self-detonating meat bomb by a [portable Old Grandpa](# "Literal CN: 随身老爷爷 — a classic Xianxia web novel trope of an ancient master's spirit trapped in a ring/artifact guiding the protagonist") was far too humiliating. It made him feel like some disposable cannon fodder in an [anti-Gary Stu novel](# "Literal CN: 反杰克苏文 — a subgenre of web novels that deconstructs or punishes the invincible, harem-collecting male protagonist trope"). Dying under the blade of a breathtaking beauty was practically a happy ending by comparison.

Or so Long Tianming reasoned.

Yet a nagging sense of unease lingered. A moment later, he discovered in mounting horror that his entire body was bound fast, completely unable to move.

Could it be that it wasn't entirely a dream after all?

Had all his prior "immortality cultivation" merely been a bout of acute psychosis, and he had actually been committed to a psychiatric hospital?!

As expected of a seasoned web novel aficionado well-versed in every imaginable plot twist, Long Tianming quickly arrived at an alternative hypothesis that made his hair stand on end.

Fortunately, his terror did not last long before the curtain was drawn aside.

[—"Time's up."](# "Speaker: Jian Qingyang")

[—"He's awake."](# "Speaker: Qingyang Jian")

The speakers were a pair of fraternal twins who appeared thirteen or fourteen years old. They shared identically exquisite, handsome faces—in pure appearance, they were the literal manifestation of a legendary golden boy and jade maiden. Yet their temperaments could not have been more divergent.

Though young and dressed in white robes, the girl sported a radiant smile and vivid, ruby-red lips that made her look like a crimson rose on the verge of full bloom. The boy beside her, however, was so gloomy he looked as though he might lie down in a coffin at any second. His face was devoid of color, and even a vibrant set of crimson robes did little to improve his deathly pallor.

Without a doubt, modern society could never produce twins radiating such an aura.

—"Ah... so it wasn't a dream after all!"-Long Tianming murmured slowly.

—"Of course it wasn't a dream,"-a third voice chimed in,-"whether it's the fact that you were possessed by a demon seed, or that you were pierced clean through by Untiring's disciple."

Long Tianming rolled his eyes toward the sound and found Renran sitting in the distance.

Yet this Miss Renran displayed none of the affected, coy bashfulness she had maintained throughout their journey. Instead, she wore a frosty expression, looking for all the world as if someone owed her millions of spirit stones—reminding Long Tianming vividly of the enthusiastic, bubbly senior student during his freshman college orientation who had answered every question with a warm smile, only to completely ignore his existence the instant she failed to sell him a campus phone card.

...Ah. That felt right.

Long Tianming let out a long, heavy sigh of relief.

It wasn't that he was a masochist. It was simply that earlier, with a portable Old Grandpa on one side and a clinging young beauty fawning over him on the other, he had felt an airy, floating illusion that the entire universe was pampering him. Yet the sensations had been far too vivid to dismiss as a dream. Now that he had finally been stabbed through the chest, everything seemed to have snapped back to reality—even the pretty girl's dismissive attitude was delightfully authentic.

Long Tianming's brain finally regained a measure of normal cognitive function, seizing upon the key phrase in Renran's statement:

—"Untiring's disciple?"

—"The one who pierced you through was Aunt Mo Bei,"-the rose-like girl before the bed declared, puffing out her chest proudly.-"My (prospective) master!"

[Don't look so proud about it!]

[And even if you say that, I have no idea who she is!]

[All I remember from my final glimpse was an ice-cold, peerless beauty...]

[Not good, I'm starting to daydream again...]

Severing his wandering fantasies, Long Tianming looked toward the twins beside his bed:

—"Then who are you two...?"

—"I am [Jian Qingyang](# "Named by combining her parents' lineages: Jian (Sky City) and Qingyang (Eternal Villa)"),"-the girl replied brightly.

—"I am [Qingyang Jian](# "Literally 'Qingyang Cocoon' (茧), playing on his twin sister's name Qingyang (青扬) while reflecting his yin, cocooned constitution"),"-the boy beside her followed in a somber tone.

—"Top-ranked freshmen of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's three-hundredth cohort,"-they announced in unison.

—"Oh..."-Long Tianming murmured blankly.

—"The one surnamed Jian is the eldest daughter of the Sky City Lord,"-Renran supplied coolly, providing background context.-"The one surnamed Qingyang is a direct descendant of Eternal Villa. The two of them are the heirs born of a titan alliance between two illustrious cultivation lineages. Not that it matters to you—all you need to know is that in this realm, they are little young masters that ordinary cultivators can never afford to provoke."

—"We are not young masters,"-the boy, Qingyang Jian, protested coldly.

—"Right, not *your* ancestors,"-the girl, Jian Qingyang, chimed in, her eyes dancing with a mischievous grin.-"Still, it truly doesn't matter. What matters is that as members of the same cohort—and since you were delivered by Aunt Mo Bei herself—we have an obligation to look after you."

—"Huh?"

Long Tianming blinked, struggling to decipher her meaning:

—"Does that mean... I can still go to school... ahem, cultivate immortality?"

—"Why couldn't you?"-Jian Qingyang asked, looking at him as if he were asking why water was wet.

—"Ahem, I was run through by a sword, wasn't I? Shouldn't my cultivation be completely crippled?"-Long Tianming asked. In web novels, that was standard procedure: if one were lucky (?), after an agonizing gauntlet of torture and misery, the protagonist would claw his way back through a triumphant revival match.

Long Tianming was daydreaming again.

This time, the delusion was short-lived. The twins exchanged a glance and ruthlessly shattered every shred of his fantasy:

[—"It was merely digging out a demon seed. Why on earth would you be crippled?"](# "Speaker: Jian Qingyang")

[—"Are you underestimating Aunt Mo Bei?"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Jian")

[—"Besides, did you actually think being crippled would exempt you from cultivation? Your spirit root is merely temporarily unusable. You already possess a massive advantage over ordinary mortals born without spirit roots; work a bit harder and you can easily make up for lost ground."](# "Speaker: Jian Qingyang")

[—"Furthermore, even if you die, you still have to cultivate!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Jian")

[—"Exactly! The Western Domain provides introductory education for departed souls. Because they lack physical vessels, their cultivation curriculum is even harsher than elsewhere!"](# "Speaker: Jian Qingyang")

[...Are you two even speaking human language?!]

Long Tianming no longer saw an adorable golden boy and jade maiden before him. No matter how he looked at them, they were a pair of vicious little devils.

He slumped heavily backward, burying his face deep into the pillow and shutting his eyes, desperately attempting to will himself to transmigrate back to his original world.

Unfortunately, transmigrating back was equally delusional. Renran, unwilling to squander any more time, strolled over:

—"Young Master Long appears fully lucid; his divine soul seems undamaged. Can we dispel the binding spell on his body now?"

—"True. If he's already scheming how to ditch school, he's definitely fine,"-Jian Qingyang muttered.

She made a subtle gesture, and Long Tianming felt an immediate weight lift from his frame. Freedom returned to his limbs, allowing him to sit up—though he had zero desire to leave the bed.

—"This was an unavoidable precaution,"-Jian Qingyang explained.-"Even though Aunt Mo Bei struck with lightning speed, the demon seed had taken root in your soul for quite some time. Extracting it inevitably leaves spiritual wounds, which can trigger temporary amnesia, cognitive confusion, or outright madness. We had no choice but to bind your physical vessel first while monitoring your condition."

Her words jogged Long Tianming's memory.

Reluctantly pushing himself upright, Long Tianming hesitated for a moment before he couldn't resist asking:

—"What exactly was that thing you called a 'demon seed'...?"

—"Greed, anger, delusion,"-Qingyang Jian answered coldly.-"The obsessions buried deep within human hearts. Given the slightest temptation, they become demons."

—"Though the one inhabiting you was slightly more special,"-Jian Qingyang added.-"That was the former sovereign of the demon race, an entity referring to itself as the Demon Emperor. Following a certain war, Second Senior Uncle let it slip away due to a momentary lapse of oversight. No one expected it to possess your body, nor that it would suffer a violent stress response from your visit to the immortal-demon frontlines. The situation was critical, which was why Aunt Mo Bei had to resort to drastic measures."

—"The Demon Emperor..."

[So my 'Old Grandpa' really did have an illustrious pedigree.]

Long Tianming squeezed out a bitter, self-deprecating smile.

Qingyang Jian observed his shifting expression and suddenly asked:

—"Do you want to go see it?"

—"No!"-Long Tianming instantly shook his head like a rattle drum.-"No, no, no, absolutely not!"

Had it been his former self, he would likely have indulged in grandiose delusions once again. But nearly being puppeteered into a human suicide bomb was an ordeal he had experienced firsthand; he could no longer treat it like some harmless power-fantasy web novel trope. Whenever the incident was mentioned, Long Tianming felt deeply that he simply lacked the mettle to be a protagonist. This sort of "karmic fortune" was best left for a genuine Chosen One to shoulder.

—"No need, I don't want to touch that thing ever again in my life!"-Long Tianming declared vehemently.

—"You don't need to be so terrified; it can't harm you anymore,"-Jian Qingyang chuckled.

They had no sadistic desire to force Long Tianming to inspect the foul parasite carved from his soul:

—"Since you're fine now, shouldn't we head over to report for registration?"

—"Report?"

—"Report to the academy, of course,"-Jian Qingyang said as if it were the most obvious thing in the world.

Seeing Long Tianming's dazed expression, the gloomy-looking Qingyang Jian provided some helpful background:

—"Apologies, I forgot to mention. Because you were parasitized by a demon seed, we do not recommend approaching places like the immortal-demon battlefield anytime soon. Even though it has been purified, it remains a contaminated zone and could inflict irreversible damage on your physical and spiritual state. Furthermore, since our Heavenly Spirit Sect shares responsibility for the demon seed incident, you have the option to enter the Heavenly Spirit Sect for advanced studies."

—"You're not actually going to abandon cultivation just because of one little stabbing, are you?"-Jian Qingyang asked suspiciously.

Hearing this, Renran also lifted her gaze, fixing her eyes squarely on Long Tianming.

A shiver ran down Long Tianming's spine.

He was no genuine fool. Although he despised examinations, dreaded civil service exams, and loathed test-oriented education, he understood perfectly well that this was the highest-probability avenue for an ordinary person without family pedigree, powerful patrons, or accumulated resources to seize control of his own destiny. At present, having no clue how to transmigrate back—or whether returning was even possible—straining every nerve to seize this opportunity was paramount.

In truth, Long Tianming now found it bizarre how he had ever dared to abandon the opportunity to study in the Heavenly Court merely out of dread for civil service exams, running off to the god-demon frontlines instead—even if that place hadn't been anywhere near as lethal as he had imagined.

Could it have been the corrupting whispers of the demon seed that was once the Demon Emperor?

[It must be the demon seed's bewitchment!]

Reasoning along these lines was not entirely mistaken; a demon seed did indeed amplify dormant fears and obsessions, steering its host down the path of self-destruction.

With the demon seed purged from his soul, Long Tianming's mind had cleared up considerably. Facing the twins' scrutiny, he responded with instant conviction:

—"Of course I want to join the Heavenly Spirit Sect! It's the greatest sect in this entire world, isn't it?! I've heard endless tales about this sect along the journey—I couldn't ask for anything better!"

If only his voice hadn't sounded so utterly unconvincing.

Nevertheless, his answer proved satisfactory to the twins.

—"That's more like it,"-Jian Qingyang tilted her chin up haughtily.-"Don't be like those disgraceful idiots who cried and begged to come with me, only to study for half a month before crying and begging to go home, completely humiliating Sky City!"

—"Sister!"-Qingyang Jian called out helplessly.

—"Sister my foot! Did I say anything wrong?"-Jian Qingyang retorted in indignation.

Her presence here naturally wasn't born of any special fascination with a "Child of Destiny." Aside from the fact that Long Tianming had been brought back by Mo Bei, her hometown peers whom she had previously escorted had fled after merely a few days of classes. This had thoroughly infuriated the young heiress of Sky City, making her eager to prove that the fault didn't lie with her.

Jian Qingyang glared at her brother:

—"They thought even the Heavenly Spirit Sect's basic cultivation was too strict, then went home whining as if I had abused them! I'm going to properly orient a new recruit right in front of them, just to show them not everyone is a spineless coward like them!"

[...You really don't need to place such high hopes on me.]

[You're making me deeply anxious about how strict basic cultivation actually is!]

Long Tianming fretted internally.

Unfortunately, Jian Qingyang had completely stopped paying attention to him:

—"Not only spineless, but arrogant! The scions from Sky City, the ones from the Qingyang clan—every single one of those cultivation clans is the exact same!"

—"Sister..."-Qingyang Jian sighed.

[Sister... you just insulted yourself.]

—"At the end of the day, they just had better luck during reincarnation! Do they honestly believe they're genuine immortals?"-Jian Qingyang continued her tirade, blissfully oblivious; once this topic was broached, she could rant for ten consecutive days.-"Even if they were real immortals, once they arrive at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, don't they still have to learn from the basics like good, obedient students? Knocking that snooty, nose-in-the-air attitude right out of them—"

*THUD!*

A sharp sound from the side abruptly cut off Jian Qingyang's tirade.

Everyone looked over in surprise, only to see Renran keeping her head lowered:

—"Apologies. I accidentally nudged my stool."

—"Are you alright?"-Qingyang Jian asked with feigned concern, seizing the chance to redirect his sister's focus.-"I can sense that your spirits are quite low. Were you frightened by the demon-purging procedure?"

—"Now that you mention it, Miss Renran was unusually downcast back then too,"-Long Tianming recalled suddenly. Back when he had been hijacked by the demon seed, Renran hadn't displayed any reaction either. He gasped in horror:-"Could you have been affected by the demon seed as well?"

—"No,"-Renran rolled her eyes at them.-"I merely..."

She hesitated for a beat, yet spoke nonetheless:

—"Encountered an old acquaintance."

—"An old acquaintance?"

—"A maidservant,"-Renran said, leaning back against her chair. She didn't know why she was confiding in irrelevant people, let alone mere juniors—or perhaps precisely because they were detached juniors, she felt no inhibitions. If she were standing before Untiring, she would rather tear her own mouth to shreds before offering him ammunition for ridicule.-"She was originally a mortal, born in a small village much like Young Master Long's. She had several younger brothers; even with spirit roots, cultivation was out of the question for someone in her station. In lean years, she was nearly sold off. I took pity on her plight, saved her, and guided her onto the path of cultivation... This time, I ran into her at Myriad Treasure Pavilion. She's done quite well for herself—already attained Nascent Soul."

—"Did she refuse to recognize you?"-Long Tianming asked curiously, fully prepared to share in righteous indignation on her behalf.

It sounded like a clichéd tale of perfidy and betrayal where one could guess the ending from the opening lines—so much so that Long Tianming completely overlooked the chronological impossibility of someone looking Renran's age having rescued a cultivator who had now risen to become an executive manager at Myriad Treasure Pavilion.

—"No. She still regards me as her master. In truth, throughout all these years, she has continually sent me correspondence."

During Renran's "seclusion" to evade Untiring, news of the outside world had been supplied entirely by several servants she had once fostered.

Renran sighed softly:

—"She is loyal to me, and fiercely loyal to the cultivation hierarchy of the past—she even despises the current cultivation system. She told me that nowadays, anyone can sit in the same classroom alongside the scions of Immortal Lords. To her ears, it represents a complete inversion of superior and inferior, a destruction of all decorum."

In truth, the maidservant's emotions had been far more impassioned—she had seemed practically ready to rally behind Renran the very next second to defeat Untiring, overthrow the Heavenly Spirit Sect's educational reforms, and restore order to the cultivation world.

In ordinary times, Renran had heard her servants whisper such sentiments countless times, and had thoroughly relished hearing them.

Yet this time, with genuine True Immortals from the authentic Celestial Realm engaged in a manic shopping spree on one side, and a former mortal maidservant of an ex-goddess indulging in delusions of restoring the ancient order on the other, the contrast was simply...

Utterly, exquisitely farcical.

It was an absurdity so glaring that even someone as stubborn as Renran could perceive it.

All of it was absurd.

Whether it was True Immortals who deceived themselves while rationalizing their own subjugation, a female cultivator who considered herself part of the upper crust yet stubbornly insisted on remaining another's handmaiden, or a foolish Child of Destiny who credulously trusted a demon seed while believing he could tame it... every single one of them was absurd to the extreme, foolish to the extreme, arrogant to the extreme.

Just like Renran herself.

—"Young Mistress Jian wasn't wrong earlier. In the Heavenly Spirit Sect, immortals and mortals are no different,"-Renran said quietly.-"In the eyes of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, I suppose I am no different from my maidservant."

Long Tianming listened, uncomprehending. The country he had inhabited in his previous life was peaceful and dynamic, its channels of social mobility unobstructed. While he could play the keyboard political pundit online with solemn gravity, in reality he possessed zero genuine sensitivity toward rigid class divides.

Now, however, a sudden flash of inspiration struck Long Tianming:

—"That's a textbook case of [people who got on the train first wanting to weld the doors shut](# "Literal CN: 先上车的把车门焊死 — Chinese internet slang for established elites kicking away the ladder to prevent newcomers from rising")! Miss Renran, how could you possibly be like her? The mere fact that it made you uncomfortable proves you aren't that kind of person at all."

Having delivered this observation, he looked at Renran with bated breath, as if waiting for floating text reading *Favorability +5* to materialize above her head.

Unfortunately, far from gaining favorability, Renran merely gazed at Long Tianming for a long moment with a deeply peculiar expression.

Such Children of Destiny, such celestial officials...

Renran understood with crystal clarity: it wasn't that she had suddenly found enlightenment or begun genuine self-reflection—it was that she had run out of avenues of retreat.

Her mind had always operated on a single track. As long as a carrot was dangled before her nose, she was the type who could turn a millstone until she dropped dead. That was the sole reason she had been able to stand guard in the lower realm for over ten thousand years with her pristine divine essence intact, cherishing the fantasy that once her mission was accomplished, she could return to the Heavenly Court.

Yet witnessing the monumental transformations unfolding across the god-demon frontline, Renran realized that even if she were to successfully return to the Heavenly Court, she would merely be running away for another three hundred years... No, in less than three hundred years, she would almost certainly have to face Untiring once more—or perhaps wouldn't even possess the qualifications to stand before him.

Recognizing this reality, Renran developed a completely resigned slacker mindset where anything went. Her emotional equilibrium had never been more stable in her entire existence.

Jian Qingyang's remarks earlier had simply stirred a passing pang of melancholy within her.

Jian Qingyang herself remained equally oblivious. Mo Bei had never informed them of Renran's true identity—in fact, Mo Bei had barely registered Renran's presence at all—nor did Jian Qingyang particularly care:

—"Is being viewed as equal by the Immortal Venerable really such a bad thing? Before a figure like him, all living beings ought to be equal. His occasional bouts of favoritism are already terrifying enough—take his direct disciples, for instance..."

—"Sister,"-Qingyang Jian raised his voice, glancing nervously toward the window as if terrified something might suddenly materialize from the shadows.

—"Ahem. In any case, you don't need to dwell so much on what other people say,"-Jian Qingyang declared, adopting the solemn air of a miniature adult. Having clearly absorbed endless grand ideological lectures, she recited them verbatim now:-"Second Senior Uncle said that this is merely a case of [spiritual civilization failing to keep pace with material civilization](# "Literal CN: 精神建设没跟上物质建设 — classic ideological phrasing regarding cultural/ethical progress lagging behind economic/technological development"). Once everyone can cultivate immortality, no one will harbor the delusion that they are superior to others."

—"Very well,"-Renran scoffed lightly.-"Then I shall watch and see!"

Having already lingered in this lower realm for ten thousand years, another stretch of time hardly mattered. And Untiring, by all accounts, was not a man to waste time.

Reflecting on this, in that singular moment, she felt the invisible shackles that had bound her for ten thousand years dissolve into thin air.

Satisfied, she rose to her feet, sparing Long Tianming no further glance as she stretched her arms lazily.

—"Wait! How could you merely watch?!"-Jian Qingyang seized the hem of her skirt as if terrified she might bolt.-"You are part of 'everyone,' so naturally you must cultivate immortality as well! You're coming with us to report for registration right now!"

—"Report?"-Renran's face froze in the exact same stunned stupor Long Tianming had displayed.

—"Of course you must report! Why else would we have brought you along to the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"-Jian Qingyang insisted.

—"I have to attend school as well?"

—"Yes! Both of your student registrations were transferred over from the Heavenly Gate. I hear the Immortal Venerable handled the paperwork personally!"

—"I have to begin from the fundamentals as well?"

—"Yes."

—"..."

Renran's lips twitched. She had anticipated that she would be incandescent with rage, that she would feel mortally insulted, that she would recklessly storm off to settle scores with Untiring just as she had in the past. Yet in this instant, all she felt was profound, overwhelming helplessness.

She murmured softly:

—"Ah... I've been set up..."

***

Once the reality of enrolling in the Heavenly Spirit Sect was accepted, there seemed to be little else to fret over.

After all, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was universally acknowledged as possessing the most bountiful educational resources in this entire realm. Setting aside Renran's true identity, even under the current standardized education system, securing admission into this sect was an astronomical karmic blessing.

The first inkling of this became apparent the moment they stepped out of the "infirmary."

The instant Long Tianming crossed the threshold of the chamber, the illumination behind him snuffed out like spent candles. The dazzling white walls faded with startling speed, revealing moss-encrusted bronze surfaces beneath. While the cot he had occupied remained clean, heavy iron shackles were fastened to its legs—resembling nothing less than a maximum-security prison cell.

—"It is indeed a prison cell,"-Qingyang Jian explained.-"Apologies. Securing the demon seed was of paramount importance at the time, so we had no choice but to bring you here as well. However, this cell is enchanted with specialized arts capable of transforming into whatever healing sanctuary brings the crim... er, resident peace of mind."

Clearly, had Long Tianming been too severely corrupted by the demon seed, he would have remained permanently confined within this cell.

Fortunately, Long Tianming's delightfully smooth brain never reached that conclusion. He merely peered back at the chamber with wide-eyed fascination:

—"Incredible."

No wonder it had felt so remarkably like the community clinic downstairs from his home—that was the very place he frequented whenever he caught an illness and needed to [get an IV drip](# "Literal CN: 挂水 (gua shui) — colloquial Chinese for intravenous fluid therapy, ubiquitous in local clinics").

Having concluded his tour of the cell that had nearly served as his permanent cage, Long Tianming turned his gaze outward with escalating excitement.

His reaction was entirely understandable, for they were currently positioned within a colossal hexagonal lightwell. Layer upon layer of countless concentric corridors and walkways lined the sheer bronze walls. The cell Long Tianming had occupied sat on the uppermost perimeter walkway; a few paces further along, massive bronze portals permitted slivers of daylight to seep through from the outside world.

In no rush to depart, Long Tianming gripped the stone balustrade, peering curiously into the depths below.

Similar cells honeycombed the lower walkways in dense, dizzying arrays. Some chamber doors were sealed shut, their interiors invisible yet vibrating with bone-chilling roars and eerie scraping; others stood inexplicably ajar, vague silhouettes shifting within the gloom. Most terrifying of all was the abyss at the heart of the lightwell: the spiraling walkways plunged downward into unfathomable, pitch-black depths, resembling a primordial void. Staring into it for more than a few seconds made Long Tianming feel as though phantom eyes were sprouting across his flesh. Fortunately, titanic iron chains draped in glowing talismans coiled layer upon layer across the central shaft, locking the abyss down in an iron grip.

—"The demon seed extracted from your soul is also imprisoned here,"-Qingyang Jian informed Long Tianming.

—"On the very bottom floor?"-Long Tianming asked expectantly. After all, it had once been an entity revered as the Demon Emperor; if such a fortress-prison deemed it worthy of maximum containment, Long Tianming felt getting tricked by it wouldn't have been in vain.

—"How could it possibly have the qualifications?!"-Jian Qingyang laughed.-"It's merely locked in a cell on the tier right below us. The second tier is reserved for negligible demons used for researching the physiological impacts of demonic qi. Those imprisoned on the deeper levels are colossal behemoths captured by our senior uncles from beyond the celestial domains—terrifying primordial beasts born exclusively within the depths of primordial chaos! Containing them requires strict adherence to elaborate protocols."

It sounded remarkably like some SCP Foundation containment facility. Yet in Xianxia video games, there existed a far more culturally grounded architectural landmark—

—"Isn't this the [Demon Locking Pagoda](# "Literal CN: 锁妖塔 — iconic Xianxia trope from Chinese Paladin / Xianjian Qi Xia Zhuan; an ancient tower holding demons that protagonists inevitably bring crashing down")?!"-Long Tianming exclaimed in sudden enlightenment.-"The iconic landmark in video games that is guaranteed to be collapsed by the protagonist!"

[—"Huh?"](# "Speakers: Jian Qingyang & Qingyang Jian")

The twins turned their heads in unison, staring at Long Tianming.

Long Tianming immediately shrank his shoulders:

—"No, I was just babbling... this sacred place will surely endure for ten thousand generations..."

—"Just a pen of crooked fiends and demons—what ten thousand generations?"-Jian Qingyang snorted dismissively.-"Who knows, maybe when [a certain someone](# "Referring to Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan") gets hungry tonight, he'll sneak in and snack on a few of them."

[—"?"](# "Speaker: Long Tianming")

—"Alright, demonic qi and hostile malice mingle too heavily here; we shouldn't linger,"-Qingyang Jian intervened.-"We should take our leave."

Saying so, he strode toward the bronze portals.

Jian Qingyang moved to the opposite side of the towering gates.

The twins exchanged a brief glance and pressed their palms against the metal simultaneously. The towering portals, dozens of meters high, glided open as weightlessly as silk. In an instant, the evening breeze rushed inward, carrying churning billows of mist gilded in sunset gold.

Standing framed against the glowing golden clouds, the gorgeous twins spoke in perfect unison:

—"Welcome to the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

***

The Heavenly Spirit Sect bathed in the morning sun is at its most beautiful (so decreed the Untiring Immortal Venerable).

However, by the time Long Tianming and Renran emerged from the Demon Locking Pagoda (provisional name), the hour was already late. It had nothing to do with the morning sun, but it afforded them a breathtaking panorama of the Heavenly Spirit Sect under the twilight glow.

And the Heavenly Spirit Sect at this hour was indeed breathtakingly sublime.

Before their eyes, boundless seas of golden clouds surged and crested. Nine tiers of ethereal celestial palaces hung suspended upside-down from the heavens, forming an impeccable mirror image with the grand palaces crowning the immortal peaks below. Overhanging eaves and intricate corbel brackets in the sky aligned symmetrically with glazed roof tiles on earth. The entire immortal sect resembled a pair of ancient green-jade seals—one stamped firmly into the clouds, the other pressed deep into the sacred mountains.

If only the twins hadn't helpfully pointed out that those magnificent celestial palaces were merely the newly expanded academic lecture halls!

At the dead center, the monumental Sundial Clock hung suspended between heaven and earth. Its bronze dial spanned as wide as an imperial walled city, the shadow cast by its gnomon sweeping across a different mountain peak every two hours. With dusk falling, its elongated shadow fell across a monumental waterfall cascading from the sky palaces (academic halls). That sheet of golden gossamer dissolved upon striking the ground into thermal hot-spring vapor, shrouding the entire grand plaza.

This was also the most relaxed hour of the day for the students—after all, evening self-study commenced in another two hours.

Scores of disciples sat cross-legged in meditation amidst the rainbow hues refracting through the thermal mist. Occasionally, white cranes soared forth from the spray, their beating wings scattering not droplets of water, but shimmering grains of starlight.

—"Now *this* is what an immortal cultivation sect is supposed to look like!"-Long Tianming exclaimed, deeply moved.

Although he had uttered similar sentiments back in that steampunk-flavored border town, what in the world had those subsequent sights been?! A Heavenly Gate resembling a civil service examination plaza, teleportation terminals resembling high-speed rail stations, and a god-demon frontline fortress resembling a commercial mega-mall! While undeniably impressive, they possessed none of the transcendent immortal flavor that Xianxia novels were supposed to have!

Even Renran could not help but nod in silent agreement, feeling that this was indeed the spectacle she had spent thousands of years expecting to see.

Enveloped by this majestic vista, Long Tianming completely forgot his identity as an otherworldly transmigrator, seemingly regressing into an excited child hollering at every marvel:

—"Whoa! A white crane!"

—"Whoa! Sword cultivators!"

—"Whoa! Monkeys!"

—"Whoa! A dra—"

His earlier cries had merely been casual exclamations, but the instant he spotted a "golden dragon"—presumably golden, glistening radiantly beneath the sunset—soaring through the twilight clouds, his neck craned outward so far he looked ready to stretch it as long as the dragon itself.

Who on earth could say no to a real dragon?

Unfortunately, the majestic beast didn't spare them a single glance, darting straight toward the western corner of the immortal peaks. A split second later, a deafening *BOOM!* echoed across the mountains.

Long Tianming froze on the spot:

—"Th-That dragon..."

—"Nothing to worry about. That's the direction of the alchemy and artifact-refining chambers; an explosion now and then is standard operating procedure,"-Jian Qingyang said casually. Seeing Long Tianming craning his neck relentlessly toward the smoke, she offered a helpful recommendation:-"If you're interested, once you finish your foundational coursework, you can pursue advanced studies in alchemy or artifact refinement. Having visited Myriad Treasure Pavilion, you already know those two majors print the most spirit stones."

[I care much more about the dragon.]

Long Tianming thought to himself, asking curiously:

—"Are you two planning to specialize in those two majors as well?"

—"I don't lack spirit stones!"-the young mistress of Sky City puffed out her chest proudly.

She turned her gaze toward the sky above the plaza, where a squad of cultivators clad in flying-fish robes soared past in crisp, synchronized sword flight—the very "sword cultivators" Long Tianming had cheered at earlier.

—"Didn't I tell you already? I came to the Heavenly Spirit Sect purely out of admiration for Aunt Mo Bei,"-Jian Qingyang said.-"How breathtaking was that sword thrust piercing straight through your chest?! How crisp and clean was her technique extracting the demon seed?! I could watch it a thousand times without ever growing tired of it!"

—"It was indeed beautiful..."-Long Tianming said weakly.

[...Though a thousand times won't be necessary! Once was more than enough.]

Jian Qingyang shot him a chilly sneer:

—"So sword cultivators are not something to lump together with monkeys and birds!"

—"S-Sorry,"-Long Tianming took a cautious step backward, hastily attempting to change the subject.-"What about Miss Renran? What do you wish to study?"

Renran deflected the conversational spotlight, turning toward Qingyang Jian:

—"It's no surprise that someone from Sky City wants to cultivate the sword—City Lord Jian's sword talent is renowned. Does the Qingyang clan share the same inclination?"

Qingyang Jian furrowed his brow, clearly disinclined to answer.

Seeing his reluctance, Jian Qingyang huffed in dissatisfaction:

—"Hmph, what's wrong with the Qingyang clan practicing the sword? We came from the exact same womb anyway, yet for some reason people keep claiming Ah Jian has more affinity with Aunt Mo Bei!"-She sounded unmistakably envious.

—"I am not suited for sword arts. If we speak of affinity, it is more with Uncle Mo Xi. After all, my body bears excessive yin qi and an abnormal soul constitution; studying under Senior Uncle Guan Ye is also an option,"-Qingyang Jian said, glancing at the sister who adamantly refused to call him elder brother, attempting to pacify her indignation.-"Though my maternal grandfather prefers that I study Astromancy with Senior Uncle Qu."

—"If you want to study it, study it yourself—don't drag Grandfather into this! Grandfather currently wishes Mother would divorce Father and remarry Senior Uncle Qu!"-Jian Qingyang snapped back, shooting a sharp glare at Long Tianming who was pricking up his ears to feast on the gossip.-"Asking for trouble."

It was anyone's guess which person she was referring to.

Long Tianming let out a couple of dry chuckles, asking with genuine interest:

—"Is Astromancy truly that formidable?"

—"Of course it is!"-Upon arriving at his favorite subject, Qingyang Jian launched into an impassioned sales pitch:-"Astromancy discerns the past, calculates the future, channels the power of celestial bodies, and grasps the authority of the Heavenly Dao! Even Little Senior Uncle might not calculate the destiny governing every single star! It is the single most technical and intellectually demanding discipline in all of cultivation!"

To seal the pitch, he leaned in to recruit Long Tianming:

—"I heard that when ascending the celestial stairs, your talents leaned heavily toward mathematics. You should definitely consider this discipline! Senior Uncle Qu is a grandmaster of mathematical arts! And mathematics is the foundation of all things—even if you fail to reach mastery, the effort will never go to waste!"

[What a horribly familiar sales pitch!]

—"I..."

Long Tianming instinctively began to decline, but then a realization suddenly struck him:

[Wait a minute, I'm in a cultivation world!]

[The mathematical edifice here can't possibly be taller or more terrifying than back home!]

[In fact, how is coming here any different from transmigrating into a world where [the entire world's mathematical ability dropped by a hundred times](# "Literal CN: 全世界数学能力下降一百倍 — a popular web novel premise where the protagonist retains normal modern high-school math skills in a world where everyone else has devolved into elementary arithmetic")?!]

[Didn't I reach the highest Heavenly Gate with effortless ease on the celestial stairs purely through mathematical talent?!]

[So I was heading in the wrong direction all along! I'm not some outdated [Do not bully the youth for being poor](# "Literal CN: 莫欺少年穷 — the iconic catchphrase of traditional Xianxia underdog protagonists, famously from Battle Through the Heavens (斗破苍穹)") Xuanhuan protagonist—I'm the protagonist of an academic scholar novel!]

Long Tianming sank once more into grandiose delusions, a silly, blissful grin spreading across his face.

Unfortunately, no one bothered to shatter Long Tianming's reverie this time.

The twins guided the two "illustrious" freshmen to the registration office to collect their foundational textbooks and school supplies. Qingyang Jian even enthusiastically prepared extra study guides for Qu Qianxing's advanced courses, stuffing them into the standard-issue storage pouch provided by the sect.

Long Tianming failed to notice that throughout the entire process, Jian Qingyang had been watching her twin brother with the exact gaze one would reserve for a [tiger's accomplice](# "Literal CN: 伥鬼 (chang gui) — in Chinese folklore, the ghost of a person eaten by a tiger who is compelled to lure new victims to be devoured").

Just like most new recruits, Long Tianming cradled the compact, coin-purse-sized storage pouch affectionately in his hands, wishing he could sprint into a private room to inspect every treasure inside.

And with that, they finally arrived at the final destination of this strangely prolonged pilgrimage toward immortality:

The Freshman Dormitories.

The freshman dormitories of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were situated a short distance from the sect's grand mountain gate. Even though the Heavenly Spirit Sect had expanded dramatically over the centuries and the grand gate had been renovated repeatedly, growing increasingly imposing, the ancient peach tree beside the gate had likewise expanded with every renovation—ensuring that every single freshman could clearly view its boughs right from their dormitory windows.

This was a time-honored tradition that had persisted for three hundred years. If asked why, veteran disciples would merely offer a bizarre smile without uttering a word, eagerly anticipating the incoming freshmen's confrontation with the "unique cultural shock of the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

Yet Long Tianming and Renran had arrived at an awkward moment.

As they approached the front of the dormitories, they happened upon the sight of a transcendent, silver-haired immortal standing beneath the ancient peach tree. Like an official hoisting a flag, he was hauling a pitch-black object smoothly toward the highest boughs, leaving it swinging there like a pendulum.

Peering closer, that black bundle was actually a doll-like little lamb, a noose fastened around its neck, looking utterly miserable and pathetic.

What was more, the little lamb was weeping in wretched agony:

—"Master! I didn't do it on purpose! I was only trying to expand our influence more effectively, I swear! I never intended to treat it like an all-you-can-eat buffet!"

Witnessing this spectacle, the twins couldn't resist leaning in to whisper:

[—"...What on earth did Senior Uncle Yuwen do to provoke the Immortal Venerable this time?"](# "Speaker: Jian Qingyang")

[—"Judging from what he said, ate something he shouldn't have again."](# "Speaker: Qingyang Jian")

[—"Ate what?"](# "Speaker: Jian Qingyang")

[—"Don't want to know, don't dare to know, must not know."](# "Speaker: Qingyang Jian")

Only then did the twins remember the two freshmen standing beside them, hurriedly turning their eyes back.

Renran displayed little reaction, merely offering a cold scoff. Long Tianming, however, was staring transfixed toward the peach tree, his face stark white, as though he had witnessed an unspeakable horror.

*Is this scene really that frightening?*

*He hasn't even seen the tree when it's hung full of people like gourds!*

Jian Qingyang mused to herself.

When she had visited the Heavenly Spirit Sect as a child, she had been thoroughly traumatized by the sight; consequently, like all veteran students, she relished this fixed tradition with quiet anticipation.

Still, the main performance hadn't even commenced! Hoping Long Tianming wouldn't flee in panic before the show began, she offered soothing words:

—"It's nothing serious, just Grandmaster's customary punishment for his Eldest Disciple; it has nothing to do with us."

—"Indeed. That black lamb is Grandmaster's Eldest Disciple; it's merely playing the victim, so don't be fooled,"-Qingyang Jian added.

—"Black lamb?"-Long Tianming stammered, his voice quivering.

He saw no black lamb. All he could see was a colossal, horrific monstrosity covered in countless ravenous maws, every single mouth slavering torrents of saliva. The visual was so unspeakably eldritch that gazing at it for a second longer felt like signing a warrant for lifelong nightmares.

Yet the monster was not what truly paralyzed Long Tianming with dread. What commanded his pinned gaze was the exiled immortal beneath the boughs... No, calling him a mere immortal felt like an insult; in short, he was an ethereal, silver-haired figure beyond worldly description.

—"Th-That silver-haired gentleman is..."

—"Oh, you recognized him?"-Renran laughed with pure malice. Having zero desire to speak with Untiring herself, she was more than delighted to introduce him to Long Tianming at this moment.-"Yes, he accompanied us the entire way! Zhou Hui, Young Master Zhou—and the Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the illustrious Untiring Immortal Venerable!"

Young Master Zhou was the Immortal Venerable?!

Long Tianming's brain crashed completely. He could not process the words Renran was speaking. While the youth he had met was undeniably striking and poised, he had never drawn the connection—because Long Tianming could never conceive what "that person" looked like in his youth. It felt as though "that person" shouldn't have had a childhood, shouldn't have even had a youth; he was born to be a revered master whose gentle spring rain nurtured all living beings, whose immense virtue bore the weight of the world, just like...

Just like the silver-haired immortal standing beneath the ancient peach tree.

—"Zhou Hui... Professor Zhou?"

The final circuit in Long Tianming's head connected. A shriek of pure horror ripped from his throat, and in his panic, he violently yanked at the storage pouch in his hands—

One had to admit, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was not without its flaws.

The shoddy quality of their freshman storage pouches was one of them.

In keeping with the principle of zero waste, the storage pouches issued to incoming freshmen were practice pieces stitched together by artifact-refining apprentices, notoriously prone to ripping. Fortunately, submitting an application immediately yielded a replacement, mitigating the flaw.

At this very moment, however, under Long Tianming's panicked tug, the seam burst, and an avalanche of assorted textbooks spilled across the cobblestones.

The thickest, heaviest tome among them slammed directly onto Long Tianming's foot, hitting with the impact of being stomped on by a primordial beast.

Dully, sluggishly, Long Tianming looked down at the floor, catching sight of the book's title:

[*Five Thousand Years of Ascension, Three Thousand Years of Cultivation*](# "Literal CN: 《五千年飞升三千年修仙》 — a terrifying Xianxia parody of China's most notorious Gaokao prep book, Five Years of Gaokao, Three Years of Simulation (《五年高考三年模拟》)")

From Long Tianming's throat tore a despairing wail that eclipsed any sound he had made when possessed by the Demon Emperor:

—"I want to go home!"

From this day forth:

Three days remained until Long Tianming learned that failing his exams meant hanging from the peach tree alongside the monster.

Three years remained until he discovered that mathematics in this world was equally impossible to master.

Thirty years remained until he became an errand-boy apprentice under Qu Qianxing.

Three hundred years remained until he completed his foundational coursework.

And until he returned home...

Indefinite.
