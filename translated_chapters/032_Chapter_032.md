# Chapter 32: Filial Devotion (第32章 孝心)

From that day forth, Zhou Hui felt as though he had lost his hands.

Whenever he returned to his immortal abode, Ran Deng lit fragrant incense and presented spirit spring water to cleanse his hands; Lu Shulong arranged his meditation cushion, sat before the tea stove, and began brewing tea. When his meditation concluded, Mo Bei had garments perfumed with spiritual incense ready at hand, while Mo Xi offered meticulously curated spirit delicacies. When he wished to soak in the spirit hot spring, Qu Qianxing stood vigil by the water's edge cradling a bath towel, while Yuwen Xuan even served spiritual wine on a tray.

And this lineup, word had it, was the result of fierce [competitive job placement](# "Literal CN: 竞争上岗 (jìngzhēng shànggǎng) — modern HR/corporate practice of competing for job postings").

For instance, Guan Ye, whose lifestyle lacked refinement and whose clumsy hands were spurned by the others, didn't even get the chance to approach Zhou Hui, having been banished to sweeping and logistical chores. Meanwhile, Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing blatantly abused their senior disciple privileges, suppressing a restlessly eager Mo Xi to secure the coveted duty of attending Zhou Hui's bath. Ran Deng and Mo Bei, who had never once burned incense or touched needlework in their lives, somehow abruptly transformed into [stereotypical virtuous wives and devoted mothers](# "Literal CN: 良妻贤母 (liángqī xiánmǔ) — traditional archetype of the gentle, devoted homemaker"), contracting out all interior decor and wardrobe accessories for Zhou Hui. In truth, the one genuinely skilled in incense was Lu Shulong; however, Lu Shulong had a mild, accommodating temperament and never fought for anything, thus degenerating into a mere technical consultant for Ran Deng and Mo Bei.

What gave Zhou Hui a genuine migraine was that while this flock of disciples was utterly mediocre at cultivation, when it came to pampering people, they acted as if they had graduated from a [level-ten domestic service class](# "Literal CN: 家政专业十级班 (jiāzhèng zhuānyè shí jí bān) — modern vocational college joke: certified master-class domestic caretakers / elite housekeepers").

Mainly, it was because they were intimately familiar with Zhou Hui's daily routine, and Zhou Hui was exceptionally disciplined himself.

Consequently, Qu Qianxing, who was in charge of the master schedule, concluded that before a qualified specialist made a formal diagnosis, it was best not to rashly alter their master's living habits and cultivation schedule, but rather provide supplementary care so Master could live and cultivate in optimal comfort.

Naturally, there was real science behind this.

Granted, as a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, there was no need to worry about Zhou Hui shaving years off his lifespan by pulling all-nighters, nor that eating the wrong food would worsen his condition, nor even that over-cultivating would drain his mental vitality. In fact, the disciples' only worries were that their master would refuse his medicine or cultivate too little. Nevertheless, ensuring he maintained peak condition during his cultivation hours was an exceedingly intricate affair.

Qu Qianxing and the others had put in tremendous effort.

The moment Zhou Hui sat down to meditate, they would regulate the temperature around his immortal abode, ensuring fluctuations never exceeded ten degrees. They monitored the spiritual energy flow across Mount Tianling to ensure no violent spiritual storms disturbed their master's practice, and whenever the ambient spiritual qi thinned, they stimulated the spirit vein to boost its intensity. Furthermore, based on the hour of the day, Zhou Hui's cultivation progress, the weather, and other environmental variables, they rotated different aromatherapy blends to keep Zhou Hui in prime condition.

Living in such luxury was terrifyingly decadent; Zhou Hui genuinely worried he would be completely spoiled rotten.

Even under these conditions, the disciples frequently convened review meetings:

[—"Sensing spiritual energy fluctuations is harder than anticipated. I believe we must devise a method for dedicated surveillance."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

[—"Speaking of which, I found a feasibility report on [high-altitude meteorological stations](# "Literal CN: 高空气象站 (gāokōng qìxiàngzhàn) — modern meteorological engineering document from Zhou Hui's modern academic memories") in the 'Library.' Perhaps we could run a trial based on that proposal? It seems that with appropriate magical apparatuses, even lower-cultivation disciples could handle the duties."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

[—"Compared to all that, Mount Tianling truly lives up to its reputation as a blessed cave-heaven personally handpicked by Master; the external conditions are already stellar. The real issue is that Master's everyday lifestyle is far too austere! What on earth does he usually wear? Even if he is an apex powerhouse, he can't be this careless about appearances!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

[—"What matters even more is Master's mood. Every time he looks at me, he clutches his chest in agonizing grief, as if reproaching me for slacking on my cultivation."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

[—"Back a few centuries ago, I urged Master to take in a few attendants to care for his daily life, but he wouldn't listen."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

[—"I wouldn't trust them either. Mortals are the least dependable creatures of all."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

[—"Pre-made puppets would be better, wouldn't they? I heard there's a sect in the Western Domain exceptionally versed in that craft..."](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"Since you know I dislike it, why persist?"-Zhou Hui spoke in a frosty tone. Looking at the disciples squatting in a crowded circle across his courtyard, he felt viscerally as though he had been reduced to the topic of an academic [group meeting](# "Literal CN: 组会材料 (zǔhuì cáiliào) — graduate research group meeting agenda/presentation materials").

—"We were also concerned about Master's dissatisfaction, which is why we are presenting our future milestones. If Master has any feedback, we can discuss and iterate on improvements,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

—"My biggest dissatisfaction is that I don't need you attending to me! I am not injured!"-Zhou Hui emphasized.

If this bunch were obedient enough to listen to that, today's farce wouldn't have happened in the first place.

—"I want to believe Master's words, yet I cannot ignore Master's recent conduct,"-Qu Qianxing said stubbornly.-"No matter. Eldest Senior Brother has already confirmed the whereabouts of Master Lan Pei, and will soon invite him to visit our mountain."

—"You actually managed to locate Lan Pei?"-Zhou Hui was mildly surprised.

Master Lan Pei was a medical cultivator. Though he was "merely" at the Mahayana stage, he had already attained Mahayana before Zhou Hui had even set foot upon the immortal path. No one knew his exact age. He was the foremost divine physician in this mortal realm; even Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses gravely wounded by heavenly tribulations longed for medicine bestowed by his hands.

However, Lan Pei's whereabouts were perpetually elusive. Spotting him was a colossal stroke of serendipity, and it was quite an accomplishment that Yuwen Xuan actually knew where he was.

Qu Qianxing, however, felt that only a physician of this caliber was worthy of treating their master:

—"Indeed. With Master's physical condition, Master Lan Pei will discern everything at a single glance."

—"If I am not injured, will you cease this fuss?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"How could this possibly be called a fuss?"-Qu Qianxing countered.-"Even if Master is uninjured, there are still many inconveniences in your daily life and cultivation. We disciples have been negligent in failing to notice them sooner. Moving forward, we must dedicate our utmost hearts and strength to attending Master."

[...Hopeless.]

Flicking his sleeves, Zhou Hui strode toward the exit of his immortal abode.

—"Master, where are you going?"-Mo Xi asked in a panic.

—"I'm going to inspect that secret realm you dragged back,"-Zhou Hui said, resolving that he couldn't keep marching to the beat of these little fools' drum, or he really would be turned into an invalid.-"Let's see what condition that secret realm is actually in."

*

Hearing that Zhou Hui was heading to the secret realm, the few disciples who were already intrigued by it shamelessly tagged along. The remaining disciples sat in place exchanging blank stares, and the gathering quickly disbanded.

Mo Bei, naturally, belonged to the faction interested in absolutely nothing.

Calculating that Master wouldn't be meditating today and thus wouldn't require her to hold incense, she prepared to head back to her own cave abode, change clothes, and practice swordplay. Yet the moment she descended the peak, she caught sight of her twin brother sneaking outward like a thief.

Mo Bei dearly wished she could pretend not to have seen him. But whether owing to that cursed twin telepathy or sheer misfortune, Mo Xi happened to glance over, locking eyes squarely with Mo Bei.

Mo Xi made a beckoning gesture and pressed a finger to his lips. Mo Bei had no choice but to trudge over at a sluggish pace.

—"What are you up to?"-Mo Bei asked, convinced her brother was an irrepressible troublemaker.-"I thought you'd be far more interested in sticking to Master's side to explore the secret realm."

—"The senior brothers beat me to the punch,"-Mo Xi said with deep indignation.-"Eldest Senior Sister is the best after all; whenever something good happens, she remembers to bring you along. The senior brothers on my side are practically subhuman, only knowing how to bully the youngest."

Even as his biological older sister, she felt this brother's worldview was rather warped. Showing filial piety to Master was a disciple's bounden duty, not some "perk," wasn't it?

Of course, to certain people, waiting on Master hand and foot might genuinely be the greatest "perk" under heaven.

Mo Bei wouldn't name names. In any case, she had seen through it long ago: this Heavenly Spirit Sect was populated by nothing but [Master Simps](# "Literal CN: 师尊厨 (shīzūn chú) — otaku/fandom slang (厨 / chu) for an obsessive fanatic or simp for Master") and [Twisted Master Simps](# "Literal CN: 扭曲的师尊厨 (niǔqū de shīzūn chú) — toxic, possessive, or unhinged fandom fanatic"). Aside from herself, there wasn't a single normal human being in sight!

Mo Bei merely wondered:

—"I thought exploring the secret realm didn't have a quota."

It wasn't like serving tea to Master—once someone offered tea, there was no need for others to offer another cup.

—"With several senior brothers hovering around, there won't be any chance for me to stand out,"-Mo Xi calculated with pure pragmatism.-"If I can't be the most outstanding one, I might as well not go."

—"Oh,"-Mo Bei grunted. Deciding that talking to this creature was an absolute waste of time, she turned to leave, only to hear her brother speak:

—"I heard that Abyss Sea City is holding its regular grand auction. This season happens to be when travelers from the Southern Domain arrive at the Abyssal Ocean. Their lands produce excellent tea, and top-tier harvest will definitely hit the auction block. When Master emerges from the secret realm and I present him with fresh tea, wouldn't that be infinitely better than those useless freeloaders exhausting Master?"

—"How do you plan on buying it?"-Mo Bei asked.

She furrowed her brows in rare severity, glaring at her brother:

—"The Fallen Immortal Palace has never given up on coming after us, and Master just made an enemy out of Carefree Palace. Don't go running outside and get captured."

What Mo Bei detested most in storybooks was the brain-dead trope where the heroine runs around trying to help the hero, only to get captured as a hostage.

In her eyes, her foolish little brother showed immense potential for that exact role.

—"If even your empty skull can think of that, how could I possibly make such an elementary mistake?"-Mo Xi sneered, crooked a finger at Mo Bei.-"Follow me. I'll show you how I buy things... By the way, seeing as we're blood siblings, I can share some tea leaves with you, provided you share the new tea-brewing technique Fifth Senior Brother taught you."

[You were angling for that all along, weren't you?!]

Though inwardly disgruntled, Mo Bei followed him anyway, genuinely terrified that Mo Xi might stir up trouble.

Mo Xi indeed had no intention of leaving Mount Tianling; instead, he made his way to the Water Mirror Pavilion.

The pavilion was situated within a subterranean karst cavern beneath Mount Tianling. Due to its unique geological structure, underground springs had formed tiered puddles of varying elevations and sizes, glistening like polished mirrors. Zhou Hui had enchanted these "water mirrors" to serve as communication relays with the outside world and opened them to all disciples.

On that note, the Heavenly Spirit Sect did not advocate the practice of "severing mortal ties." Zhou Hui actively encouraged his disciples to stay in touch with old acquaintances and cherish every bond. Regrettably, even setting aside the direct disciples, ordinary inner-sect and outer-sect disciples rarely had anyone outside they felt compelled to contact, leaving this subterranean pavilion perpetually deserted.

Yet upon entering the Water Mirror Pavilion, Mo Xi acted like he was returning to his hometown. With a flurry of snaps, he activated a string of water mirrors. Moments later, figures materialized in each pool: the Heavenly Spirit Sect's traveling disciples and purchasing agents out in the field, including the Xiang siblings who had been running the Immortal Chess tournament inside the sect just days prior.

Mo Xi didn't immediately inquire about tea leaves. Instead, he asked:

—"I already notified you last time that we have completely broken ties with Carefree Palace. How do things feel out there?"

—"I feel it's alright so far. Low-level disciples of Carefree Palace don't seem to know about it yet, so things remain peaceful,"-reported a disciple traveling in a distant region.-"However, when visiting Carefree Palace's larger commercial shops, I sensed palpable hostility, and was even tailed after stepping outside."

—"Thanks to Junior Brother Mo's warning on my end, some Fallen Immortal Palace scoundrels actually tried to ambush me!"-another disciple chimed in.

—"Things are pretty grim here in the Eastern Domain,"-frowned another disciple traveling in the east.-"Quite a few people witnessed the disappearance of the Thought Pavilion. Even if Carefree Palace wanted to suppress the news, they couldn't. So they're intentionally smearing Master's reputation."

—"Then Senior Brother Zhang, what about you..."-Mo Xi inquired with concern.

—"I'm fine,"-that 'Senior Brother Zhang' offered a bitter smile.-"To put it bluntly, they haven't the faintest idea what our Heavenly Spirit Sect is. Even if I climbed atop Carefree Palace's rooftop, barely anyone would spare me a glance."

—"That's good, but we cannot afford to be careless,"-Mo Xi exhaled in relief, then addressed the other disciples:-"Senior Brother Li, Senior Sister Qian, Senior Sister Wei... Given the current climate, it's hard to predict what countermeasures Carefree Palace will adopt. If possible, I hope you can temporarily return to Mount Tianling to observe the situation first."

—"Right. Being outside isn't as interesting as we imagined anyway; we were indeed planning to head back—"

The senior brothers and sisters on that side concurred; six or seven out of ten were ready to return.

Mo Bei surmised this was likely a task delegated by Qu Qianxing. Even so, she knew she could never match her brother in this regard. Mo Xi was an adept social butterfly, capable of fostering smooth relations across both inner and outer sects. Had it been Mo Bei, she wouldn't even remember who was who, let alone know their sect possessed such disciples.

Only after inquiring about the well-being of all the traveling disciples did Mo Xi finally ask about the item he desired.

—"I'm sorry, we couldn't buy it,"-Xiang Rong said with a fallen face.-"Even though Junior Brother Mo lent us a teleportation array allowing us to arrive three days before the auction, we discovered that most regular merchant caravans from previous years haven't shown up."

—"Not only that, but aside from tea, many of the regional specialties you asked us to purchase are in short supply,"-Xiang Yi reported.-"Someone seems to be buying up various commodities at high prices, and rumors are circulating in secret."

—"Rumors?"-Mo Xi arched an eyebrow.

—"They say our Heavenly Spirit Sect attacked Carefree Palace, damaging their trade routes so that merchants might not make it through."

—"What kind of bullcrap is that?!"-Before Mo Xi could even react, the other senior brothers and sisters flared with fury.-"Our Immortal Venerable merely slew one of their Tribulation Transcendence elders! He didn't disrupt their commerce in the slightest! What do their trade routes have to do with our Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"

[—"This is shifting the blame! They want to ruin our Heavenly Spirit Sect's reputation across the Central Domain!"](# "Speaker: Sect Purchasing Agent")

[—"I fear when merchants can't acquire goods, they'll pin the blame entirely on us."](# "Speaker: Sect Purchasing Agent")

—"What should we do? Should we report this to Master?"-Mo Bei asked worriedly.

—"Of course we must, but certain arrangements need to be set in motion first,"-Mo Xi was exceptionally composed at this moment.-"Xiang Rong, Xiang Yi, you're still monitoring the rumors in Abyss Sea City, correct?"

—"Yes,"-Xiang Yi replied.-"Should we find a way to suppress them?"

—"No. Amplify it,"-Mo Xi declared.

[—"Amplify it?"](# "Speaker: Sect Disciples")

—"Yes, amplify it!"-Mo Xi repeated.-"I'll go find Fourth Senior Brother in a moment; he should have jade slips recording Master's heroic demeanor. I'll make copies for you... No, not just Abyss Sea City, but everywhere else! Let everyone witness with their own eyes how effortlessly our Master swatted that Carefree Palace scum!"

—"Heheh, not a bad idea at all,"-a senior brother praised.

—"Since we've already offended Carefree Palace, we're not afraid of offending them further,"-Mo Xi said.

Yet others wore troubled expressions, appearing unconvinced.

—"But they blockaded the trade routes themselves and framed us for it..."-a senior sister worried aloud.

—"That's the crux of it,"-Mo Xi continued plotting.-"We will take credit for it!"

[—"Take credit for it?"](# "Speaker: Sect Disciples")

—"Speaking of which, with Carefree Palace abruptly blockading the trade routes, there must be quite a few merchant caravans stranded outside the Central Domain, right?"-Mo Xi abruptly pivoted the topic, looking toward one of Heavenly Spirit Sect's purchasing agents.

The man, whose specialty was procuring goods along the border regions, blinked in surprise before answering:

—"No obvious signs yet, but prices for certain commodities have indeed begun fluctuating."

—"Lao Zhu, your work ethic leaves much to be desired,"-Mo Xi sighed.

That Lao Zhu's face blanched:

—"B-but I genuinely haven't heard any whispers on my end..."

—"Just because you haven't heard it means it doesn't exist? As a merchant, how can you be so oblivious to the market? If you can't even handle this much, how convenient: Master's Library... Master recently brought in several business textbooks, and I happen to be begging Master to launch research projects in that field! Why don't you come back, study for another two years, and then head back out?"-Mo Xi said with a beaming smile.

—"I-I'll investigate immediately!"-Lao Zhu shuddered in terror.

The other purchasing agents instantly stiffened in solemn vigilance. Every one of them had heard about the Grand Sect Examination. Those who cared had even bought copies of the exam papers to review, reaching a unanimous conclusion: thank heavens they were stationed outside and exempt from testing! But if they crossed this little ancestor and got recalled for mandatory examinations, they would be utterly ruined!

Mo Xi was actually toying with that exact idea. Regrettably, the timing wasn't quite ripe; he would have to scout promising seedlings within the sect first, train them up, and dispatch them to replace these folks for corporate re-education.

For now, Mo Xi simply instructed:

—"Keep a close eye on the border routes. If they blockade the roads, aside from Carefree Palace's own shipments, plenty of independent merchants should be stopped. When the time comes, we will step in and buy up all their inventory."

—"Buy up all of it?"-the purchasing agents repeated like parrots.

—"Of course! The conditions in our Central Domain are perilously harsh; bulk commodities can only pass through designated trade routes during the summer and autumn seasons. Merchants rely on this window for their entire next year's earnings. How pitiful for them to be stranded on the road by Carefree Palace! Naturally, we should show empathy and purchase all their stock,"-Mo Xi said with a sweet smile.-"Then, whenever major cities run low on supplies, we can provide for them!"

[Junior Brother Mo, you're planning to replace Carefree Palace entirely!]

Yet this plan was genuinely viable.

As an anomaly of a region, the Central Domain had enormous demand for supplies, yet safe corridors were only open during specific seasons. Outside merchants thus hoarded massive stock before embarking on their journey to the Central Domain. With Carefree Palace pulling this stunt, droves of merchants were barred from entry. While the Central Domain would certainly suffer shortages, the merchants themselves would be seething with resentment.

At this juncture, if the Heavenly Spirit Sect took credit for the blockade and stepped up to purchase their inventory and facilitate trade, while they might not instantly conquer the entire Central Domain market, ensuring market stability around Mount Tianling was well within reach. Best of all, once the jade slip recordings of Untiring Immortal Venerable crushing Sovereign Immortal Chana were leaked, the public narrative would flip upside down overnight.

After all, in a cultivation realm where the strong preyed upon the weak, weakness was the ultimate original sin.

Mo Xi's scheme was ruthlessly brilliant.

Stunned into speechlessness, everyone stared at the adorable little junior brother before them. One purchasing agent hesitantly asked:

—"B-but the spirit stones..."

—"As much as you need, I will give!"-Mo Xi proclaimed with filthy-rich swagger.

He was a man who owned a whole spirit stone mine now!

Mo Xi turned dead serious:

—"The most important objective is expanding the Heavenly Spirit Sect's influence across the Central Domain and eating up the markets of several major cities—at the very least, the cities near us can no longer remain under the Fallen Immortal Palace's thumb!"

—"Understood!"-The traveling disciples and purchasing agents put on grave expressions.

Considering the astronomical amount of spirit stones this endeavor would consume, they naturally assumed the Untiring Immortal Venerable had foreseen everything long ago.

[As expected of the Immortal Venerable!]

And so, they delved into detailed deliberations regarding regional commodity prices and supply conditions.

Mo Bei, meanwhile, watched all this with a completely deadpan expression:

[My foolish little brother... do you even remember the tea you wanted to buy?!]

*

If even Mo Xi knew about Carefree Palace blockading the trade routes, the Heavenly Dao Alliance naturally knew as well.

Yet unlike the Heavenly Spirit Sect, where one or two direct disciples could decide major sect policy, the Heavenly Dao Alliance had a sprawling council of elders, a legion of protectors, and countless disciples and families tethered to those elders and protectors. Fractured into various factions with conflicting viewpoints, they could turn the tiniest triviality into a brawl, let alone a "grand crisis" of this scale.

At such moments, the stance of the two Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses carried immense weight.

[—"Heh! The Heavenly Spirit Sect hasn't even made a peep, yet his Carefree Palace is already screaming bloody murder?"](# "Speaker: Su Mei")

—"There is no principle under heaven that permits snatching away an entire secret realm while disciples are undergoing a trial! In my eyes, Zhou Hui struck magnificently! That hand deserved to be chopped right off!"-the Mei-Zhu couple declared in incandescent rage.

Evidently, they had already viewed the full recording of Sovereign Immortal Chana attempting to seize the secret realm only to be battered back by Zhou Hui.

Carefree Palace had wished to cover it up, of course, but the Heavenly Dao Alliance's He Yong had been presiding as arbiter right outside the secret realm, alongside countless other sects and rogue cultivators. Just like the disappearance of the Thought Pavilion, the incident was already common knowledge across the entire world.

Seeing that scene, Su Mei and Xin Zhu were pushed beyond the brink of fury.

Su Xin had been inside, after all!

When juniors were undergoing trials within a secret realm, their greatest fear was an outside powerhouse directly tampering with the entire realm.

Setting aside whether Sovereign Immortal Chana's technique—[Hand Turning Heaven and Earth](# "Literal CN: 手转乾坤 (shǒu zhuǎn qiánkūn)")—would damage the secret realm itself, permanently altering its internal structure and trapping the disciples inside to die; on a basic level, had Su Xin been in the middle of battle while the realm was being shifted, a mere tremor in the ground would have been enough for a demonic beast to slay him multiple times over.

Recalling the Heavenly Book's prophecy that their child faced a great tribulation in this secret realm, didn't it match up perfectly?!

When the Mei-Zhu couple received the report, they had nearly fainted from sheer rage.

—"I say we should impose sanctions on Carefree Palace!"-Su Mei advocated.

—"Ahem. But Zhou Hui not only severed his hand, he killed the man outright and destroyed the Thought Pavilion,"-another elder of the Heavenly Dao Alliance cautioned.-"Isn't that perhaps a bit excessive?"

—"And Carefree Palace blockading the Central Domain's trade routes isn't excessive?!"-Xin Zhu retorted sharply.

Whoever it was, facing two Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses sharing one mind and spirit, one couldn't help but lose confidence.

In the end, it was the Alliance Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance who stepped in to smooth things over:

—"Ahem, ahem. Both sides have let temper get to their heads; it is best to persuade them to each take a step back. After all, Carefree Palace needs to conduct commerce as well; what benefit does blockading the Central Domain's trade routes bring to itself? And Untiring Immortal Venerable is always so driven by impulse—at the very least, he ought to spare some consideration for his own sect!"

How convenient: dispensing fifty lashes to each side, clearly preparing to [muddle through and smooth over the mess](# "Literal CN: 和稀泥 (huò xīní) — literally 'mixing wet mud'; smoothing over a dispute without resolving right or wrong, playing both sides to keep the peace").

Then again, for the Heavenly Dao Alliance, muddling through was the only option. As long as the two behemoths didn't plunge into open warfare, heaven and earth remained at peace.

The Mei-Zhu couple was thoroughly dissatisfied with this response. However, anxious over their son's condition and seeing that Carefree Palace hadn't breathed a single word regarding their boy's well-being, they rushed off to contact Zhou Hui.

*

Inside the secret realm, Qu Qianxing was also bringing up this very matter with Zhou Hui:

—"When the secret realm acknowledged its master, disciples from various sects were still inside, so they followed me back. I initially assumed the other sects would immediately arrive to reclaim their disciples, but unexpectedly, they have made no move to this day."

—"They likely want to see Carefree Palace's reaction first,"-Zhou Hui noted.

In truth, Qu Qianxing believed those people had been scared out of their wits by the terrifying power their master had displayed. Coming immediately to demand their disciples back might appear disrespectful, yet with Carefree Palace looming over them, they dared not fawn over their master directly lest they be branded allies of the Heavenly Spirit Sect—hence their dawdling.

Zhou Hui was merely remarking in passing, not caring in the least about the reactions of those sect higher-ups.

He was only concerned for the trial disciples:

—"Treat those trial disciples with hospitality, look after them as esteemed guests, and if any are injured, administer prompt medical treatment."

Reflecting on those children, they were truly unfortunate little scapegoats. Handpicked to compete in the trial, they hadn't even qualified to serve as foils; shortly after stepping into the secret realm, they had watched helplessly as the realm acknowledged an owner, and were now stranded in another sect. They were bound to feel terribly aggrieved.

—"I will see to their hospitality,"-Qu Qianxing nodded.-"Though the situation was somewhat chaotic at the time, because Master intervened with such swiftness, most disciples from other sects hadn't even processed what occurred before it was all over. Those who sustained the heaviest casualties were actually the Carefree Palace disciples who attempted to attack us, as well as... His Excellency Su Xin."

Su Xin was genuinely cursed with rotten luck.

He had already been subdued by Carefree Palace's Hongying and flung out as a meat shield against needles. And when Sovereign Immortal Chana seized the secret realm, Su Xin had been left utterly defenseless, pinned like a porcupine by snow needles. Even now, he lay in bed shivering uncontrollably.

—"I shall explain this matter to the Mei-Zhu couple personally later,"-Zhou Hui said, caught between amusement and dismay at the thought of Su Xin's plight.-"I expect they will be contacting us shortly."

—"Ah, Master, we're almost there,"-Guan Ye called out from the side at that moment.

Zhou Hui nodded, looking alongside Qu Qianxing at the dozen-odd divergent paths sprawling before them.

—"Speaking of which, the terrain in this secret realm is extraordinarily convoluted,"-Qu Qianxing said awkwardly.-"Even though I have become the master of this realm, I still find myself getting lost."

—"This secret realm appears neither designed to hoard treasure nor built upon some sect's ruins. Rather, it seems to be an experiment by a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator attempting to forge their own pocket world—much like the Thought Pavilion. His ambitions were grand indeed; he likely sought to encompass landscapes from across all Five Domains and Six Abysses into a complete realm. That explains the sheer variety of terrain and the labyrinthine paths,"-Zhou Hui deduced.

Even walking along the orthodox routes, Zhou Hui had been hiking for several hours.

Had Zhou Hui not desired to see what the spring looked like—the one in the 'novel' that his disciples obsessed over, trekking back every few years without fail to fetch water for him—he would have lost all interest in navigating such an exhausting, tedious secret realm long ago.

—"Indeed. To explore it in its entirety would take decades. It's rather [chicken ribs](# "Literal CN: 鸡肋 (jīlèi) — chicken ribs; a classical idiom for something of little value that is too good to discard but not worth keeping (tasteless yet wasteful to throw away)"),"-Qu Qianxing furrowed his brow.

—"Mm, there is no need to go to such trouble,"-Zhou Hui began, glancing at their surroundings. Just as he was about to elaborate, his peripheral vision caught his fourth disciple's movements, and he couldn't help shouting:-"Ye'er, do not touch the water inside!"

—"Huh?"-Startled by the shout, Guan Ye almost tumbled straight in.

With Zhou Hui present, he naturally wouldn't let the boy suffer such rotten luck—otherwise, the one trekking here every ten years to take a frigid bath would have been Guan Ye.

Zhou Hui caught Guan Ye with a wave of spiritual energy.

Perched beside the "Ice Eye," Guan Ye scratched his head and laughed sheepishly:

—"I was just wondering why the water here doesn't freeze. It feels far colder than anywhere else."

—"Such is the nature of an Ice Eye—infinitely frigid yet never freezing. Situated as it is in a microclimate mimicking the scorching Southern Domain, for the realm's creator to craft such a natural wonder took tremendous effort,"-Zhou Hui appraised the "Ice Eye" with admiration.-"No wonder (in the book) I required water from here to aid my secluded healing."

—"To aid secluded healing?"-Qu Qianxing's ears perked up instantly.

—"..."-Zhou Hui averted his gaze.

—"Master, you truly are hiding things from me!"-Qu Qianxing pressed.

—"Not at all,"-Zhou Hui deflected.-"That is only a consideration when preparing for ascension. Since I have abandoned ascension, that naturally ceases to be an issue."

Truth be told, the Heavenly Dao's script did possess a certain internal logic:

The sequence was roughly: Mo Bei explores the secret realm $\rightarrow$ falls into a trap, plunges into the Ice Eye, and contracts heat poison $\rightarrow$ Zhou Hui heals Mo Bei and discovers anomalies in her spiritual power $\rightarrow$ Zhou Hui orders Mo Bei to fetch water, using the ultra-frigid liquid to assist his own cultivation $\rightarrow$ Mo Bei thus learns Zhou Hui's fatal weakness $\rightarrow$ Mo Bei is turned by the protagonist and reveals Zhou Hui's weakness to him.

In this lifetime, this Ice Eye was firmly in Qu Qianxing's grasp, making it impossible for Mo Bei to learn Zhou Hui's weakness. Could it be that Qianxing...?

Zhou Hui was somewhat curious as to how destiny would fulfill itself.

Yet Qu Qianxing was doggedly relentless:

—"Then what does 'ceases to be an issue if you don't ascend' even mean?! Master, your body, what on earth—"

—"Master, the seniors of the Plum-Bamboo Immortals are requesting a communication link."

Just as Qu Qianxing cornered Zhou Hui, Ran Deng's communication talisman arrived.

Zhou Hui instantly found his escape hatch:

—"They're here after all."

—"Wait, Master!"-Qu Qianxing chased after him.

Guan Ye stared blankly at the two departing figures, then turned back toward the "Ice Eye" before him, a contemplative expression dawning across his face.

*

[—"So that was how it happened?!"](# "Speaker: Su Mei")

[—"Carefree Palace! We are irreconcilable enemies with them!"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

When Zhou Hui returned to his immortal abode, the enraged shouts of the Mei-Zhu couple rang out.

Evidently, Ran Deng had already relayed the entire sequence of events to them.

Seeing Zhou Hui return, they were in no mood for idle pleasantries:

—"We have troubled you in this matter. In a few days, we will dispatch envoys to Mount Tianling to bring our son home."

Seeing the Mei-Zhu couple so fraught with anxiety, Zhou Hui instead offered comforting words:

—"There is no need to rush. This matter can be considered my responsibility. In some time, Lan Pei will be visiting my mountain; when he arrives, let him examine Su Xin."

Zhou Hui blithely ignored the sharp glare Qu Qianxing shot his way.

He had nothing for Lan Pei to treat in the first place; he couldn't very well let the divine physician make a trip for nothing!

The only catch was that Lan Pei had better arrive quickly, or Zhou Hui would have already healed Su Xin himself.

—"Master Lan Pei?!"-The Mei-Zhu couple was deeply moved. Ignorant of the underlying context, they believed the Heavenly Spirit Sect had specially retained a divine physician for Su Xin's sake—and given Su Xin's meager cultivation, merely receiving a single glance from Lan Pei was an unfathomable blessing.

If they had harbored any lingering resentment toward Mo Bei for accidentally injuring Su Xin earlier, it had now vanished without a trace.

The Mei-Zhu couple solemnly pledged:

—"Bujuan, we owe you a tremendous favor this time. In the future, whatever request you have, do not hesitate to ask."

—"As a matter of fact, there is one thing I need right now—"-Zhou Hui smiled.
