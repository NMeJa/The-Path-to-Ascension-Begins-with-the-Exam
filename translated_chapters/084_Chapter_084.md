# Chapter 84: The Neighbor's Chicken (第84章 隔壁家的鸡肉)

—"Having already missed the New Year courtesy visits, we naturally cannot pretend nothing happened,"-Yan Bailang said coldly.

At the mention of this "Immortal Arts Tournament," Yan Bailang's belly flared with burning fury.

Because he had invited the Heavenly Spirit Sect to participate in the auction—which ultimately resulted in the entire auction being commandeered and hosted by the Heavenly Spirit Sect—Thousand Beasts Hall had spent the better part of the past six months suffering the [cold shoulder](<# "Literal CN: 冷暴力 (lěng bàolì) — cold violence / passive-aggressive ostracization and silent treatment">) from the Central Domain's cultivation world.

Not only had the Palace of Carefree Wandering suspended all trade and transactions with Thousand Beasts Hall, but cultivators from most sects and immortal cities in the Central Domain had also treated Thousand Beasts Hall with lukewarm detachment, feeling that they had invited completely unnecessary trouble upon everyone.

Had it only been that, Yan Bailang would have pinched his nose and swallowed it.

He admitted that out of "cowardice" and "impulsiveness," his attempt at [belling the cat](<# "Literal CN: 给猫挂铃铛 (gěi māo guà língdang) — belling the cat; attempting an impossible, suicidal task or trying to appease/monitor a dangerous predator">) had indeed been rather foolish.

However!

The Wanhe Gang had directly assisted the Heavenly Spirit Sect in hosting the auction—did anyone raise an objection to that? Sects like Eternal Villa and Blood Demon Hall had strutted in with grand swagger to attend the auction—did anyone condemn them? Even when Thousand Beasts Hall sneaked over to the Immortal Bazaar to purchase beast feed, they caught sight of a whole crowd of sects who clung to Carefree Palace's thigh and had solemnly declared they would never deal with the Heavenly Spirit Sect secretly slipping over too! Did everyone suddenly decide to play blind at that moment?!

What was most infuriating was that once the auction concluded and the New Year arrived, the various major sects and immortal cities all rushed over to Mount Tianling to pay New Year greetings (apologize). And now, as they prepared to host the Immortal Arts Tournament, STILL NO ONE CALLED UPON THOUSAND BEASTS HALL!!!

[Are they bullying our family just because we don't have a Mahayana cultivator?!]

Yan Bailang was practically ready to burst with rage.

Their Thousand Beasts Hall was at least one of the foremost sects in the Central Domain. Excluding sheer head count, their comprehensive combat strength surpassed even the Wanhe Gang. Why was it that every single time, no one ever invited them to play?!

[You won't play with me? Fine, I won't play with you either—]

Alas, whether as the master of a sect or as a Body Integration titan, one could never afford to act on such childish spite.

Although Yan Bailang desperately wanted to ignore that damned Immortal Arts Tournament, he was well aware that if Thousand Beasts Hall failed to take action now, they would be thoroughly marginalized by the Central Domain's cultivation world in the days ahead.

He naturally could not sit by and watch such a catastrophe unfold.

—"Dispatch someone to establish contact with the Heavenly Spirit Sect and see if that 'Organizing Committee' of theirs still needs people,"-Yan Bailang instructed.-"Furthermore, we will naturally participate in the Immortal Arts Tournament—and across every single cultivation realm! We shall sweep every first-place title!"

Yan Bailang was by no means boasting.

When it came to head count and branch halls, Thousand Beasts Hall could not match the Wanhe Gang; in historical pedigree and overbearing opulence, they could not compare with Sky City or Eternal Villa; and in top-tier combat power, they did not possess a single Mahayana cultivator, leading high-stage powerhouses to look down upon them. Yet no matter who evaluated the cultivation factions of the Central Domain, Thousand Beasts Hall was the genuine [second-rate (X) second-largest sect](<# "Literal CN: 老二（X）第二大门派 — humorous wordplay: 'lao er' can mean second-rate/number two, or slang for male genitalia, self-corrected to 'second-largest sect'">).

Because their combat prowess was genuinely formidable.

Within the Central Domain, due to the ceaseless roiling of spiritual energy storms, spirit beasts and demon beasts were inherently far more ferocious than in other realms. In terrible years, they could even coalesce into catastrophic beast tides.

Yet Thousand Beasts Hall had successfully adapted to local conditions. Their cultivation doctrine of "Walking Alongside Beasts" ensured the sect's baseline combat power. What was more, their cultivation method enabled disciples to rarely encounter cultivation bottlenecks prior to the Void Refinement stage. Consequently, Thousand Beasts Hall boasted a staggering thirty Soul Formation cultivators and over fifty Nascent Souls, to say nothing of the ranks beneath the Golden Core realm.

What made other sects turn green with envy was that Thousand Beasts Hall could actually afford to maintain them!

Although feed for spirit beasts caused Thousand Beasts Hall immense headaches, so long as the feed supply was secured, the hall could essentially achieve self-sufficiency. Not to mention that the peripheral byproducts harvested from spirit beasts each year yielded a bounty of spirit stones and indispensable cultivation materials, thereby guaranteeing a massive baseline population of cultivators at every single realm. Other sects simply lacked such uniquely endowed conditions.

Therefore, Yan Bailang possessed genuine confidence in sweeping the board of Immortal Chess piece slots... cough, at least claiming the lion's share was well within reach.

—"When that time arrives, who will still consider the Wanhe Gang number one?"-Yan Bailang declared with burning ambition.

He had long found the Wanhe Gang's signboard of "Number One Sect of the Central Domain" an utter eyesore. It was nothing more than an agglomeration of motley rabble. Setting aside the fact that their loose cultivators looked numerous while their hearts were completely divided, even their sect leader, Zhang Hongjing, was merely at the Void Refinement stage. What qualifications did he have to vie with Thousand Beasts Hall for the title of "Central Domain's Number One"?!

—"Naturally,"-Elder Bai Jun chimed in beside him.-"A loose cultivator sect like the Wanhe Gang was never qualified to compare with our Thousand Beasts Hall... However, if we are to participate across all cultivation tiers, Hall Master, do you intend to enter the competition in person?"

—"Can I not? I heard the leaders of the other sects turned down judge qualifications specifically to compete themselves. So it hardly matters if I enter directly, does it?"-Yan Bailang chuckled, casting a deeply pregnant glance at Bai Jun.-"Or could it be that Elder Bai intends to enter independently?"

Bai Jun shook his head in frantic haste.

Both he and Yan Bailang were at the Body Integration stage. Leaving aside the disparity in raw combat power, purely from the perspective of sect hierarchy, if he managed to snag the credential of "Central Domain Body Integration Representative Cultivator" before his own sect master, he could kiss his career goodbye.

—"I heard that this tournament is not purely [martial combat](<# "Literal CN: 武斗 (wǔdòu) — physical martial dueling and spell combat">),"-Bai Jun explained flusteredly.-"At least for cultivators at the Soul Formation stage and above, it isn't. There is also an [intellectual debate](<# "Literal CN: 文斗 (wéndòu) — intellectual debate / scholarly symposium / lecture defense">) segment."

[—"Intellectual debate?"](<# "Speaker: Yan Bailang">)

—"Yes. Word has it they will ascend the altar to lecture on scriptures and expound their Grand Dao,"-Bai Jun said, stealing a cautious glance at Yan Bailang's countenance.

For the vast majority of high-stage cultivators, they were certainly more than willing to embrace such a format.

After all, as venerable powerhouses, rolling up their sleeves to bicker and brawl like ordinary disciples was thoroughly beneath their dignity. Adding the academic presentation segment instantly elevated the whole affair into something immensely refined and high-end!

Not to mention that expounding the Dao brought celestial merit—especially preaching before a congregation of fellow high-stage powerhouses, where a moment of epiphany might very well shatter their current cultivation bottleneck.

Yet this was the mindset of cultivators who had ascended through "normal" paths.

Even those who relied on popping medicinal pills had at least accumulated some personal insights on pill-popping regimens.

Thousand Beasts Hall's breakthroughs, however, relied entirely upon spirit beasts—relying upon that very *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*—and that included Sect Master Yan Bailang himself.

No, it would be more accurate to say that Yan Bailang was the one who relied most desperately upon the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*.

The versions of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* obtained by the other disciples were incomplete, forcing them to grope in the dark and study portions on their own. Yan Bailang, however, held the "complete" *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*. He treated this technique purely as a miraculous cheat code, having virtually never researched or deconstructed it himself. This fostered a mental inertia of total dependence on external crutches, leaving him thoroughly inept at independent thought. Even now, stuck at the Body Integration bottleneck, his thoughts were entirely consumed by how to locate the final volume of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, never once crossing his mind to complete the cultivation technique on his own.

Naturally, Yan Bailang would never confess his humiliating predicament to another living soul.

Yet others were no fools. Especially someone like Bai Jun, who hovered around Yan Bailang day and night; having perceived Yan Bailang's unstable state to some degree, he had deliberately broached the topic of "intellectual debate."

On the surface, Yan Bailang appeared entirely nonchalant.

—"Let them debate then!"-Yan Bailang declared, feigning complete fearlessness.-"My *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* can tame even the souls of ancient ferocious beasts; otherwise, Thousand Beasts Hall would never have become the greatest power in the Central Domain! When it comes to expounding scriptures, I truly fear no one!"

[—"Worthy of Hall Master, the foremost beast tamer in the entire cultivation realm!"](<# "Speaker: Bai Jun">)

Bai Jun appeared thoroughly convinced, showering Yan Bailang with a dizzying cascade of extravagant praise.

Yan Bailang seemed immensely pleased as well, soaking in a full quarter-hour of shameless flattery before instructing Bai Jun to prepare the registration matters for the Immortal Arts Tournament.

Yet the instant Bai Jun stepped away, Yan Bailang's expression plummeted into pitch-black gloom.

He spat: "Ptooey!" and cursed:

—"This old rascal truly harbors rebellious thoughts!"

Yan Bailang was indeed atrocious when it came to researching cultivation techniques, but he was extraordinarily adept at reading faces and discerning intent—otherwise, with his mediocre innate talent back then, he never would have caught the eye of the former-former sect master, been taken into the bloody trial grounds as an heir, latched onto the right thigh, won the absolute trust of his Senior Sister, and subsequently murdered that very Senior Sister to seize the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and the sect master's throne.

Thus, Yan Bailang had long perceived his subordinate's subtle probes and simmering doubts.

Had this been Yan Bailang in his earlier years, he would have decisively executed the man at the very first spark of dissent to [kill the chicken to scare the monkeys](<# "Literal CN: 杀一儆百 (shā yī jǐng bǎi) — kill one to warn a hundred / kill the chicken to scare the monkeys">). Yet he had not done so, not only because nurturing a Body Integration cultivator required an astronomical expenditure of resources, but also because their sect, as the second-largest power in the Central Domain—unlike the loose, motley structure of the Wanhe Gang—required sufficient high-stage powerhouses to uphold its facade. If not a Bai Jun, there would simply be a Hei Jun!

[Damn it all! Outsiders look down on me, and now even the people inside my own sect dare question me!]

[If I were at the Mahayana stage, or even Tribulation Transcendence, how could they ever possess such audacious guts?!]

Yan Bailang mused for the hundredth time.

—As always, he selectively chose to forget that his personal strength had had virtually nothing to do with himself from the very beginning. That was precisely why, outside of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, he could not find a single path toward a breakthrough, and why attending an academic symposium (X) immortal arts tournament filled him with utter panic and dread.

Yet after six months of being ostracized and looked down upon, Yan Bailang could no longer afford to pin all his hopes solely on locating the final portion of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*.

He had even begun to suspect that his own memories were playing tricks on him. Could it be that his "Senior Sister" had never created that final portion in the first place?

[—"No, you clearly saw with your own eyes how I wrote that final portion, and how I used it to tame the beast soul."](<# "Speaker: Senior Sister (Inner Demon)">)

[—"If not for me, you wouldn't have even survived the trial!"](<# "Speaker: Senior Sister (Inner Demon)">)

[—"Yet not only did you betray me, you were foolish enough to lose that final portion—"](<# "Speaker: Senior Sister (Inner Demon)">)

—"Shut up!"-Yan Bailang roared.

Yet the relentless, chattering voice inside his brain did not cease in the slightest; instead, it erupted into mocking, piercing laughter.

This was the [inner demon](<# "Literal CN: 心魔 (xīnmó) — mental demon / demonic manifestation of unresolved guilt, fear, and sin">) re-ignited by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's boundless pressure during Yan Bailang's last visit to Mount Tianling!

Damn that Untiring Immortal Venerable! Damn that Heavenly Spirit Sect!

—"Don't think I must rely on you to advance to Mahayana!"-Yan Bailang growled in a low, furious hiss.-"There are other ways to... That's right, even without the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, aren't there other paths? A method that existed from the very beginning..."

Rather, he had been contemplating that very method ever since he hit his bottleneck at the Body Integration realm.

It was merely that he was unwilling to bear the gruesome consequences and devastating price of that method, leaving him perpetually hesitant. After all, his remaining lifespan was still ample, and he believed he would eventually unearth the final volume of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*.

Today, he suddenly had no desire to wait any longer.

Not merely because of the relentless, chattering voice within his skull, but even more so due to the suffocating dread that welled up whenever he contemplated that "intellectual debate."

His earlier display of effortless confidence before Bai Jun had, of course, been entirely an act staged for the man's benefit.

Yan Bailang possessed zero genuine foundation.

In fact, the moment Bai Jun mentioned lecturing on the Dao, Yan Bailang had stood there feeling as if he were already standing upon a deathly silent lecture podium, surrounded on all sides by scrutinizing, mocking gazes.

That excruciating humiliation had felt so visceral that it filled Yan Bailang with an overwhelming urge to turn tail and flee.

Yan Bailang had not the slightest wish to encounter such a nightmare in reality when he took the stage for the intellectual debate.

[I must become a Mahayana...]

Consumed by fanatical obsession, he flicked his fingers and transmitted a divine sense summon to several targets he had scrutinized over the past half year:

[—"...Come over."](<# "Speaker: Yan Bailang">)

[—"Regarding the Immortal Arts Tournament, I have certain matters to instruct you in person."](<# "Speaker: Yan Bailang">)

---

Xia Bing received the summons from Yan Bailang, Hall Master of Thousand Beasts Hall, right as she was preparing to step out of her immortal cave.

Had it been just a few minutes later, she might already have departed for the Qianxing Secret Realm.

Yet upon receiving Yan Bailang's order, Xia Bing did not feel annoyed in the slightest; instead, her eyes sparkled with excitement:

—"The Immortal Arts Tournament—as expected, our Thousand Beasts Hall is participating too?"-Xia Bing murmured.

She had heard Qingyang Yang of Eternal Villa bring this matter up countless times. Word had it that Eternal Villa had been intensely preparing for the Immortal Arts Tournament ever since the New Year, to the point where Qingyang Yang had barely shown any enthusiasm for exploring the secret realm lately. Every single one of them dreamed of securing the qualification to become an Immortal Chess piece just like their Martial Uncle Qingyang Qingli, [fighting until their dog brains get beaten out](<# "Literal CN: 连狗脑子都要打出来了 (lián gǒu nǎozi dōu yào dǎ chūlái le) — vivid Chinese colloquial hyperbole meaning fighting ferociously, savagely, and without restraint">) in internal qualifiers.

Xia Bing had felt immense envy listening to him.

Their Thousand Beasts Hall would never foster such an enthusiastic atmosphere. Fortunately, taking into account sect prestige and the sect master's vanity, Xia Bing had wagered that Thousand Beasts Hall would not miss out on this grand tournament.

Sure enough, after waiting for two full months, just when Xia Bing feared Thousand Beasts Hall might sit on the sidelines, the sect master's command had finally arrived.

Xia Bing was overjoyed.

Although in truth she did not particularly care for Yan Bailang as a sect master, even a fool understood that entering the Immortal Arts Tournament carried zero downsides and infinite benefits:

If nothing else, in order to guarantee the contestants' combat strength, magical artifacts and medicinal pills would certainly flow in abundance, perhaps accompanied by secret specialized training.

Perhaps the sect master's summons today was for this exact purpose.

This was the privilege of clinging to the sect master's golden thigh.

In the past, an ordinary disciple like Xia Bing would never have possessed the qualifications to even be considered.

[—"So, this is how you repay the Hall Master?"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone">)

[—"Not only colluding with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but privately harboring this sect's secret scripture, the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*."](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone">)

[—"Are you worthy of his expectations?"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone">)

It was the Heart-Interrogating Stone.

During the Double Tenth Festival last year, Xia Bing had accompanied the sect master to Mount Tianling. While receiving the Untiring Immortal Venerable's celestial qi to temper and deepen her cultivation, she had also received this Heart-Interrogating Stone.

Yet after spending half a year with it, Xia Bing was no longer panicky as she had been at the start.

She had realized that the Heart-Interrogating Stone bore her no malice; rather, it was an introspective tool designed to identify deficiencies and mend flaws. Whenever negative emotions arose within her, this stone would issue sharp interrogations as warnings, prompting her to resolve the underlying root cause of those emotions so she could cultivate more purely.

Yet certain deeds, once committed, could never be undone or neatly resolved.

Such as the matter of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*.

Failing in their formal visit to Mount Tianling, and instead receiving the sect's supreme classic, the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, from the hands of a bizarre "demon"—this was Xia Bing's greatest secret, as well as her deepest source of guilt. That was precisely why the Heart-Interrogating Stone incessantly interrogated her conscience.

[Still, one cannot give up on ascension because of that!] Xia Bing thought.

Stepping onto this path of immortal cultivation, cultivators possessed the resolution to discard everything. For certain individuals, morality was the most trivial among the things abandoned; for the sake of a superior magic treasure, they would not hesitate to murder and pillage without a second thought. Xia Bing's acquisition of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* was essentially a fortuitous encounter; she did not even need to feel guilty in the first place.

That being said, guilt was still guilt.

Xia Bing habitually buried the Heart-Interrogating Stone deeper into the recesses of her sea of consciousness. She then dispatched a transmission talisman to apologize to her secret realm exploration teammates, informing them that she could not make the trip, before flying toward Yan Bailang's immortal cave.

---

[—"The Beast Soul Trial?!"](<# "Speaker: Xia Bing">)

Arriving before Yan Bailang, Xia Bing was utterly dumbfounded by the command that greeted her ears.

She stared at Yan Bailang in absolute disbelief.

Naturally, Xia Bing was entirely unaware of the sinister reality lurking behind the Beast Soul Trial.

As an ordinary disciple of Thousand Beasts Hall, Xia Bing only knew that beneath the Beast Soul Tower at the center of Thousand Beasts Hall lay the suppressed soul of a ferocious beast from the upper realm. So long as one entered the tower, passed the trial, and devoured a fragment of that beast soul, their cultivation would experience an astronomical surge, which also signified qualifying as a candidate for sect master.

Thus, among the disciples, the Beast Soul Trial was also renowned as the "Succession Trial."

To be permitted entry into this trial was essentially equivalent to being acknowledged as the sect master's designated heir.

What Xia Bing and the other duped disciples did not know was that even if one successfully cleared the preliminary trials, when swallowing even the tiniest speck of the beast soul at the end, not even one in a thousand could survive—it was a pure game of suicidal gambling.

Originally, the former-former sect master had painstakingly resolved this lethal gamble through the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*; yet due to her murder and the disappearance of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, this deadly game of chance was about to commence once more.

And this current sect master harbored an entirely different scheme altogether.

Yan Bailang scrutinized the disciples standing before him, as though appraising fish laid out upon a serving platter.

—"This is not the entirety of the trial,"-Yan Bailang said, leading this group of disciples to the base of the Beast Soul Tower and speaking in his customary tone of detached coolness laced with a veneer of warmth.-"You are the most elite disciples of our Thousand Beasts Hall. Only for the sake of this Immortal Arts Tournament are you exceptionally granted entry into the Beast Soul Tower—"

Hearing his words, the summoned disciples still could not suppress the proud curls tugging at their lips.

Even if granted exceptional entry into the Beast Soul Tower solely for the tournament, it still proved that they held the sect master's profound favor!

After all, this was the ancestral Beast Soul Tower of the sect's inheritance!

—"Are you worthy of this trust?"-the voice of the Heart-Interrogating Stone rang out in Xia Bing's mind once more.

Yet this time, Xia Bing did not bury it.

Although she was fully conscious of having secured the sect master's favor under the Heavenly Spirit Sect's subtle guidance, she had always assumed it was nothing more than an ordinary transactional relationship between superior and subordinate. She never dreamed that the sect master would trust her to the extent of welcoming her into the sect's sacred grounds of legacy!

Her former one part of guilt instantly multiplied tenfold.

Especially when her sect's supreme *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* was resting right inside her storage pouch.

Whether due to the weight of her guilt or something else entirely, as Xia Bing stepped into the Beast Soul Tower, she felt that the storage pouch concealing the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* was growing distinctly scorching against her hip.

At the exact same time the sect master of Thousand Beasts Hall was using his own sect's disciples to experiment with breakthrough methods, the sect master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was likewise utilizing his disciple's blood to test a brand-new cultivation technique.

—"It's about ready to give it a try,"-Zhou Hui murmured, gazing with profound satisfaction at the physical form seated opposite him.

It was another "Zhou Hui."

Its features closely mirrored Zhou Hui's—flowing silver hair, jade-toned skin—yet its age was visibly far younger, appearing to be only three or four years old. What was more, a pair of enormous ram horns curled proudly from its head.

This vessel had been hand-sculpted from Zhou Hui's pure spiritual energy and Yuwen Xuan's essence heart-blood.

Since Zhou Hui had resolved to test the Taotie's legacy cultivation technique, nothing surpassed the efficacy of experiencing it firsthand. So long as he circulated that technique for a single complete heavenly cycle, he would grasp the foundational essence of this ferocious beast heritage, as well as whatever lingering effects it might impose upon his disciple.

When it came to the methodologies of cultivation, Zhou Hui had always been a staunch practitioner of empirical action.

No sooner said than done. Having finished sculpting the vessel, Zhou Hui's physical form turned translucent, possessing the diminutive body. The little vessel then blinked open a pair of striking, silver-gray eyes—

[So hungry.]

This was Zhou Hui's very first sensation upon occupying this body.

Although Zhou Hui had been mentally prepared prior to taking over this form, he was still startled by the sheer, all-consuming intensity of this starvation, even feeling a rare twinge of remorse:

[Has Xuan'er been enduring this kind of hunger all along?]

Perhaps he shouldn't have been so strict in forbidding him from snacking in the past... Ah, of course, wanting to devour Shulong was a categorical exception—don't even think about it.

Following that gnawing hunger, Zhou Hui's second sensation was:

[So fragrant.]

Every single corner of the room was suffused with the scent of mouthwatering food.

The tea upon the low desk was fragrant, the desk itself was fragrant, the floorboards were fragrant, and even his own physical body was overwhelmingly fragrant... Rather, his own body smelled the most intoxicating of all, radiating an instinctive conviction that consuming it would amplify his cultivation by several hundredfold.

Zhou Hui understood that this was likely the Taotie's primal evaluation of Zhou Hui's celestial power.

As the greediest of the ancient ferocious beasts, even when faced with the power of a Tribulation Transcendence titan far mightier than itself, it felt only ravenous drool, without an ounce of fear.

However, this was Yuwen Xuan's essence heart-blood after all. Consequently, the aroma Zhou Hui perceived from his own flesh was infused with deep filial warmth and lingering attachment, which thoroughly suppressed the voracious appetite.

Zhou Hui felt a touch of gratification, comforted by the sense that the boy was reasonably sensible after all.

Yet right as this thought crossed his mind, he sensed through the essence heart-blood the existence of another entity that had been branded with a mark.

It was not inside Zhou Hui's immortal cave, but somewhere far, far away.

Through the primal instincts of a Taotie, Zhou Hui could perceive that it was a prey—a prey stamped with a Taotie's proprietary seal, designed to proclaim to its brethren and all other ferocious beasts:

*This is my food. Hands off!*

—"This little bastard didn't stamp this mark onto Shulong, did he?!"-Zhou Hui furrowed his brows, casting his gaze in the direction of the brand.-"I already told him his junior brother cannot be eaten..."

And then, even Zhou Hui froze in utter disbelief.

The "prey" Yuwen Xuan had marked was not his fifth junior brother Lu Shulong, nor was it anywhere within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but situated far, far in the distance.

To mortal perception, it was a place no human eye could ever behold, nor somewhere reachable within a day or two's journey. Yet with the supreme divine abilities of a Tribulation Transcendence titan, Zhou Hui effortlessly pinpointed the exact location of the brand:

Thousand Beasts Hall.

And specifically, the Beast Soul Tower at the very heart of Thousand Beasts Hall.

Employing his [Clairvoyance](<# "Literal CN: 千里目 (qiānlǐmù) — Thousand-Li Eyes; clairvoyance divine sight allowing high-stage powerhouses to peer across vast distances">) divine ability, Zhou Hui could see that inside the Beast Soul Tower was an expanse of roiling blood-red, fully stamped with an active hunting mark—in the present continuous tense!

In other words, his unruly child had not only branded the neighbor's "chicken" with his personal mark, but had already sprinted over there right this very second!

[Ha, no way?!]

[Here I was thinking he'd been quite well-behaved lately, and the little bastard actually snuck over to the neighbor's estate to [steal their chicken](<# "Literal CN: 偷鸡 (tōu jī) — stealing a chicken; humorous metaphor for Yuwen Xuan sneaking into Thousand Beasts Hall to devour their ancient ancestral beast soul">)?!]

[I explicitly ordered him to rest properly inside his immortal cave!]

[And the neighbor's "chicken" has been reared for thousands of years, hasn't it?! Just when the hell did that little bastard set his sights on their "chicken"?!]

[He's only around two hundred years old this lifetime—could it be he's been plotting to eat their "chicken" ever since he learned to walk?!]

Zhou Hui rose to his feet. Casting aside all thoughts of practicing the cultivation technique, he took flight in the direction of Thousand Beasts Hall.

Before departing, he did not forget to snap a disciplinary switch from the ancient peach tree before the mountain gate.

The thickest, longest one of them all.
