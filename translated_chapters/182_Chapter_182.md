# Chapter 182: [Phantasm](# "Literal CN: 幻形 (huànxíng) — phantasm / phantom form / illusory manifestation; karmic traces and lingering soul fragments preserved by the Sacred Tree")

Mo Bei did not indulge her emotions for too long.

Rather, the warmer and more reassuring Master's embrace was, the more self-loathing Mo Bei felt—loathing her past self who had once betrayed Master.

[Just how brain-dead did I have to be to [throw myself away for free](# "Literal CN: 白送 (báisòng) — delivering oneself up for free / throwing oneself at someone without requiring any effort on their part") like that?!]

...Even if she truly had not intended to give herself away for nothing at the start.

One had to admit that degeneration was a step-by-step descent.

Failing to slaughter him the instant intimacy occurred had been her greatest blunder—though given the upbringing Mo Bei had received, she could never have brought herself to do such a thing anyway. After all, objectively speaking, influenced by that monster (that [dropout who slipped through nine-year compulsory education](# "Literal CN: 九漏鱼 (jiǔ lòu yú) — short for 九年义务教育漏网之鱼; popular Chinese internet slang for an illiterate slacker or uneducated ruffian who slipped through the net of nine-year compulsory education")), having relations had been an accidental incident; it seemed she couldn't entirely blame him. Then, because of that intimacy, a delicate psychological shift had taken place: she began paying slight attention to his movements, bestowing petty favors upon him. Those petty favors had drawn the attention ([bullying](# "Literal CN: 霸凌 (bàlíng) — bullying / targeted harassment")) of other disciples toward him, which in turn sparked her pity, leading her to grant him even more favors...

How to put it? It was a pure, unadulterated [toxic investment](# "Literal CN: 纯纯的负面投资 (chúnchún de fùmiàn tóuzī) — a completely negative, value-destroying investment").

Just like certain failed merchants and treasure-hunters at the Immortal Arts Assembly who, unwilling to write off their initial petty investments and desperate for a greater return, continuously sank more capital into the venture, only to end up losing their shirt, down to the last penny!

[Ah, isn't that being so idiotic I'm not even fit to compare to Mo Xi?!]

Thus, by the time she finally came to her senses, she had already committed an irredeemable cardinal sin:

She had revealed Master's weakness to him!

Never mind that she had harbored no ill intent, and never mind weaknesses—merely disclosing the dates of Master's secluded cultivation to an outsider had delivered Master's private affairs straight into enemy hands. And in truth, that [bleep—](# "Literal CN: 哔—— (bì) — audio bleep sensor sound used in Chinese media for self-censored profanity") (*profanity*) had indeed struck against Master and the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

[—"——!!!"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"Bei'er?"-Zhou Hui called out in concern.

He had just been shoved away abruptly by Mo Bei.

Paying no mind to her disrespectful action, it was Mo Bei's current state that worried Zhou Hui far more:

His normally emotionless disciple was deathly pale. Her delicate yet doll-like wooden little face was crumpled into a tight knot, her breathing came in ragged gasps, and the flexible sword in her grip was turned horizontally toward herself—looking as though she might run herself through at any second, so much so that Zhou Hui was forced to shroud her in spiritual energy to keep her from doing something [drastic](# "Literal CN: 想不开 (xiǎngbùkāi) — taking things too hard / being unable to get over a grievance, usually leading to suicide").

Fortunately, Mo Bei did not do so.

—It was not that she didn't want to, but rather that she felt killing herself would be pointless.

After all, killing herself wouldn't solve any problem. Most importantly, the memories told her that reincarnation cycle had been no accident; it had clearly been a conspiracy targeting Master, the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and perhaps even the entire world.

For the sake of vengeance, and for Master and the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mo Bei had to live at least until that time came.

Theoretically, this was true, but...

It felt utterly awful!

In the past, Mo Bei had loved nothing more than emptying her mind. To her, having an empty head and thinking of nothing was the most comfortable state imaginable, leaving her spirit completely relaxed. Yet right now... no, from this moment onward, Mo Bei would likely never be able to return to that state again.

The moment she calmed down, the [dead memories launched their assault](# "Literal CN: 死去的记忆在攻击我 — a popular Chinese internet meme about cringe, painful, or traumatic memories from the past suddenly resurfacing to attack one's psyche")!

Every single frame and scene made her want to die.

Mo Bei had no choice but to find something else to think about to divert her attention:

—"M-Master, where is 'that'?"-Mo Bei asked.

—"'That'?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"'That' bastard who crammed those memories into my head!"-Mo Bei gritted her teeth.-"At the final moment, you rescued that thing, didn't you?"

—"...You sensed it?"-Zhou Hui said, looking pleased.

He had thought Mo Bei's final strike had been an indiscriminate area-of-effect attack, but it seemed she had properly sensed the location of the most critical enemy.

[—"Since you sensed it, why didn't you weigh your options?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"...Blood rushed to my head,"-Mo Bei said, averting her gaze.

Zhou Hui let out a helpless chuckle. With a flick of his wide sleeve, a bead of blood-colored light drifted out.

That bead of blood-colored light landed on the ground and condensed into human shape.

That figure was a being both most familiar and most foreign to Mo Bei:

Mo Xi: Past-Life Edition.

Indeed. The one who had persistently entangled Mo Xi after entering the Sacred Tree, only to turn toward Mo Bei after being rejected by Mo Xi, was "Mo Xi" himself.

In the previous cycle, Mo Xi and the Child of Destiny had attempted to seize the Sacred Tree's secret treasure and had even fought a duel here. Both had their "criminal records" retained by the Sacred Tree, which could be considered a form of "phantasm."

Yet the Child of Destiny's phantasm was surprisingly safe. Or rather, whether in terms of will or power, he possessed nothing capable of leaving behind a strong imprint—ordinary to a degree that practically did a disservice to the title "Child of Destiny."

It was only when Mo Bei encountered danger—no, that couldn't even be called danger; when real danger struck, that fellow hadn't shown a trace of a shadow. Rather, after the danger had already been resolved, right at the moment of that final strike, he had suddenly popped out wanting to [rescue the damsel in distress](# "Literal CN: 英雄救美 (yīngxióng jiùměi) — hero saves the beauty; classic romance trope"), which seemed to be his instinctual reflex.

Compared to this harmless fellow, the phantasm left behind by the past-life Mo Xi was the living embodiment of a [female vengeful ghost](# "Literal CN: 女鬼 (nǚguǐ) — female ghost / banshee; humorous exaggeration for a spiteful, vengeful wraith")!

Brimming with bitter resentment and dripping with murderous intent, itching to destroy the world by tomorrow.

Only because of the Sacred Tree's suppression had he been unable to stir up any major trouble. But when Mo Xi and Mo Bei arrived here, as the phantasm's original body and that body's direct bloodline, they were exceedingly prone to interference even beneath the Sacred Tree's suppression. Thus, this phantasm had struck at 'himself' without the slightest hesitation.

As the true body, Mo Xi was the most vulnerable to interference.

Furthermore, because he was also "Mo Xi," even Zhou Hui had been unable to distinguish between them.

Fortunately, Mo Xi had made some progress compared to his past life. At least the fortitude of his will and heart was stronger than in the previous life. Coupled with utilizing the Sacred Tree's demon fire to achieve an early breakthrough, he had astonishingly left his phantasm with nowhere to sink its claws.

Refusing to give up, the phantasm had continued trying to influence Mo Xi on one hand, while reluctantly turning to deal with Mo Bei on the other.

The phantasm had not struck at Mo Bei right from the start, not because he couldn't bear to, but because he simply couldn't do it.

Compared to Mo Xi, Mo Bei's progress in power and mental resolve over the previous cycle was actually far more pronounced. In particular, with the dense layers of sword intent shrouding her, no fiends, demons, ghosts, or monsters could draw near. It was only because the runic mathematical "simulation program" had left her slightly dizzy and she had relaxed her guard that she had given the enemy an opportunity to attack.

—"Then again, someone who could grasp everything about me so clearly... aside from Mo Xi, there wouldn't be a second person,"-Mo Bei muttered.

She looked with agony toward that phantasm.

This phantasm—or rather, the past-life Mo Xi—looked like Mo Xi on the surface, yet not entirely like Mo Xi. It was the fully grown version of the soft, alluringly enchanting form Mo Xi had assumed after entering the Sacred Tree: more [femme fatale](# "Literal CN: 红颜祸水 (hóngyán huòshuǐ) — a beauty that causes national disaster / fatal woman") than a femme fatale, more peerless demoness than a peerless demoness.

Seeing his ghostly appearance, Mo Bei also found it strange how that 'fated person' could have bypassed Mo Xi to come looking for her.

[Could it be because Mo Xi was too difficult to [conquer](# "Literal CN: 攻略 (gōnglüè) — gaming slang for capturing/clearing a romance route or boss")?]

[—"Tsk!"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

At this thought, Mo Bei clicked her tongue in displeasure once more.

However, the past-life Mo Xi clearly harbored no goodwill toward Mo Bei either.

—His familial affection for Mo Bei had already been completely ground away amidst her repeated betrayals and flights.

Meeting Mo Bei's gaze, he narrowed his seductive eyes and let out a sneer:

—"My, my, has the puppet finally learned to make expressions? Is it that painful? In that case, why don't you obediently slice up your own soul to atone for your sins? That way, you could still contribute a bit of value—*oomph!*"

Mo Xi No. 2 let out a pathetic wail.

It wasn't Mo Bei—Mo Bei actually thought what he said made quite a bit of sense—but Zhou Hui.

Zhou Hui raised a hand and flicked him on the forehead with a force the boy could withstand:

—"Xi'er, don't say strange things to your sister,"-he warned helplessly.

Only then did "Mo Xi" look toward Zhou Hui.

His expression, however, was rather strange.

Clearly he was one of the disciples most fond of acting spoiled, yet his eyes held neither fondness nor aggrieved longing; instead, they were filled with wariness, vigilance, and... fear.

—"Are you... the 'real' Master?"-'Mo Xi' asked in a dry, hoarse voice.

[What kind of ghostly question is that?]

Mo Bei was somewhat puzzled, feeling this wasn't a question Mo Xi would ever ask.

—"I am real,"-Zhou Hui replied unconcernedly.

He showed not the slightest intention of blaming his disciple for questioning him; instead, he asked quite earnestly:

—"Do you need me to do something to prove it?"

—"Heh, what meaning is there in proving it?"-'Mo Xi' sneered, seemingly unappreciative.—"Isn't the counterfeit Master as well? A pawn like me can't see through anything anyway."

This was getting more and more out of hand.

[Is this thing really my idiot younger brother who loves Master most?]

Mo Bei looked oddly at "Mo Xi," only to find the fear on his face deepening.

That was not fear of Zhou Hui's power, but a far more intricate dread born of agonizing over gains and losses.

Even though this was Mo Xi's phantasm—and a past-life phantasm at that—meaning Mo Bei could not share the same telepathic resonance and emotional connection she had with his true body, they were twins who had grown up together since childhood after all. Mo Bei took one look at "Mo Xi's" demeanor and understood:

This child had actually confirmed whether Master was real or fake long ago; he simply didn't dare admit it.

As if he feared that the moment he acknowledged it, Master would vanish.

Seeing "Mo Xi" like this, Mo Bei felt a twinge of pity.

Yet as those hateful memories replayed and she recalled the "Master" in that cycle, Mo Bei could understand 'Mo Xi's' reaction...

[Wait, no... why was that Master like that?!]

Mo Bei clutched her forehead; having just received those memories, she was somewhat chaotic and confused.

Zhou Hui also felt a surge of emotion; he had never imagined his youngest disciple would look at him with such eyes. Yet he did not miss the hidden information embedded in the boy's words:

—"The counterfeit Master is also Master? You seem to know what the counterfeit is?!"

This was intelligence that even Lu Shulong, who clearly remembered all the reincarnation cycles, did not know.

What exactly was the "Zhou Hui" in the script?

This was information that even after inspecting the Heavenly Dao, Zhou Hui had been unable to obtain, yet he had to know!

After all, whether it was the Heavenly Court's conspiracy or other sinister plots, if something could completely emulate him—even with a mere ten percent of Zhou Hui's power—the devastation it could cause would be terrifying.

—"...I'm not particularly clear on it either!"-'Mo Xi' said, rolling his heterochromatic eyes—incidentally, in the sixth cycle, he had not obtained the fire mineral vein but had clearly cultivated demonic arts, so his brown eye had not turned red, but had shifted into an eerie purple instead.

Wide-eyed with this eerie pair, he made no effort to conceal his mischievous, malicious intent.

Sure enough, 'Mo Xi' spoke in a tone that seemed to [pass the buck](# "Literal CN: 甩锅 (shuǎiguō) — shifting blame / tossing the wok"):

—"Because all the investigations into the counterfeit's identity were carried out by Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother!"

[—"Xuan'er and Qianxing?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"That's right! Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother cared *so* much about 'you'! They did all sorts of things to 'Master'! For instance, capturing him, locking him up, and conducting all kinds of magical, physical, and other experiments on him. At first, they merely wanted to discover the counterfeit's true identity and where the real Master was. But Second Senior Brother clearly got addicted to playing with it! It was the first time I'd ever seen him like that! The things he did... calling it [defying the master and destroying the ancestral lineage](# "Literal CN: 欺师灭祖 (qī shī miè zǔ) — betraying one's master and destroying the founding ancestors; the supreme taboo in Chinese sects") wouldn't be an exaggeration at all~~~"

'Mo Xi' stared fixedly at Zhou Hui, narrating deliberately in an ambiguous, suggestive tone.

Looking at his actions, was he trying to lodge a complaint with Master? Or did he want to frighten Master and sow discord between Master and Eldest and Second Senior Brothers? Or perhaps both?

In any case, Mo Bei felt that no matter what this fellow "Mo Xi" had gone through, his nature remained truly identical.

Unfortunately, as always, it proved completely useless against Master.

—"Qianxing conducted many experiments?"-Zhou Hui said upon hearing this, astonishingly showing a touch of gratification.—"Tell me in detail—"

*Crash—!*

*Crash! Crash! Crash!*

Zhou Hui had originally intended to pursue the details, but a rapid cascade of massive crashes cut his words short.

It sounded akin to shattering glass.

Accompanying this noise, the white shaded promenade once again exhibited a light-warping distortion. Countless shards of desire cascaded down in chaotic disarray like shattered glass, and amidst them, Mo Xi stood upright and proud.

Naturally, this Mo Xi was the genuine, authentic Mo Xi of this lifetime.

While Zhou Hui, Mo Bei, and the other Mo Xi had been conversing, the real Mo Xi had still been undergoing his trial of desire (mixed with the Exhortation to Learning exam), and like Mo Bei, he had achieved victory.

—Though given the current turn of events, that achievement had become rather inconsequential.

Mo Xi knew none of this. First, he shot Mo Bei a smug glance, then spotted his revered Master:

—"Master, I won..."

Finally, his smugness ground to a halt upon seeing another 'himself'.

Mo Xi snapped his mouth shut, staring at that "himself."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

He did not speak right away. Instead, he carefully skirted around the figure, taking the route farthest from him until he reached Zhou Hui's side. Clinging tightly to Zhou Hui's arm, only then did he ask:

—"Master, what is 'that thing'?"-Mo Xi asked.

He referred to himself as "that thing."

—"That is your phantasm,"-Zhou Hui said helplessly.

[—"Phantasm?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"You broke through and stabilized your cultivation, casting out that portion of power from your Extreme Yin Constitution. It carried a portion of your personality, which, amplified by the Sacred Tree's magnification of desires, produced a phantasm,"-Zhou Hui explained in a seemingly well-reasoned manner, offering his youngest disciple a warm smile.—"In other words, this signifies that you have completely shaken off the influence of the Extreme Yin Constitution and the Yin Spirit bloodline. You did very well."

[—"Master!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi's eyes lit up.

Rare praise and validation from Master caused him to completely forget his usual petty calculations. Nor did he notice that upon hearing Master's explanation, his older sister wore an even rarer expression of utter shock.

[W-Wait a moment, Master!]

[That's not where this "Mo Xi" came from, is it?!]

[Isn't he a fragment of Mo Xi from the previous cycle?!]

[Why did Master say that? Master is lying... No, how could Master lie? And his analysis sounded so reasonable and logical too? Could it be that I was mistaken?!]

Mo Bei was thoroughly bewildered.

Fortunately, her confusion did not last long; Master's voice resonated directly within her mind:

—"Forgive me, Bei'er, but it is better not to tell Xi'er about the events of that cycle,"-Zhou Hui's voice echoed gently in her consciousness.—"For you, and for him as well, it is far too painful. Truthfully, I really do not wish for you two to be burdened by those memories."

[Just for that?!]

[Just for Mo Xi's and my insignificant little emotions, a person like Master was actually made to lie?!]

Mo Bei's eyes widened. She felt it wasn't worth it.

—"Whew, 'Master', that is hardly an appropriate choice,"-the other 'Mo Xi' sneered as well.

He had been trying to say something to the real Mo Xi just now, but had been gagged by Zhou Hui.

Yet Zhou Hui had not completely blocked his divine sense transmission to himself and Mo Bei. Thus, while shielded from the real Mo Xi, "Mo Xi" could only whisper privately to Zhou Hui and Mo Bei:

—"'Master' wants to know what happened in the previous cycle, right? Then having 'me' recover my memories would be the best option! After all, this 'me' is merely a fragment retained by the Sacred Tree; my memories aren't even complete. Even the memories crammed into Mo Bei only go up to the Southern Domain, don't they?"

Mo Bei nodded in agreement.

—"Plenty of things must have happened afterward, which only the true body 'me' would know,"-'Mo Xi' continued, ruthless even when throwing himself under the bus.—"Although what 'I' knew on the macro level might not match Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother, as the person who actually did the dirty work on the ground—whether it was your murder, the destruction of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, or the Demon Realm's invasion—all of it is firsthand intelligence! Its value might well surpass what Eldest and Second Senior Brothers know!"

—"Is that so?"-The emotion carried by Zhou Hui's divine sense was somewhat complicated.

"Mo Xi" urged him on even more vigorously:

—"What's more, even if those two wouldn't dare lie to you, they wouldn't necessarily give an honest and complete report either. In comparison, 'I' am just a little fool and the best-behaved child! If my memories are restored, whatever you want to know, 'I' will obediently report it all, even allowing you to [search my soul](# "Cultivation technique: 搜魂 (sōuhún) — soul-searching; prying into memories directly")—"

[—"Xi'er, you have suffered."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Past-Life Phantasm Mo Xi")

—"Even under those circumstances, you never gave up on your sister. Good child,"-Zhou Hui's divine sense enveloped "Mo Xi," as warm as an embrace.—"It was all Master's failing for not noticing you had been pushed to such extremes... You have already done very well, and it is more than enough. Whether as a fragment or as the true body, there is no need to torment yourself over things that have already passed, Xi'er."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Past-Life Phantasm Mo Xi")

Mo Bei watched as that "Mo Xi" trembled violently, as if struck by lightning.

She didn't know whether he had taken Master's words to heart, but after standing dazed on the spot for a moment, "Mo Xi" suddenly turned and walked toward Master.

He wrapped his arms around Master's waist and buried his face in Master's shoulder—this "Mo Xi" possessed the height of his fully grown self, nearly identical to Master's own height.

Instantly, the real Mo Xi jumped up like a cat with its fur bristling:

—"What are you doing?! Let go of Master!"-Mo Xi shouted.

"Mo Xi" naturally paid him no heed, instead hugging Zhou Hui even tighter.

In contrast, Mo Xi's true body released Zhou Hui's arm and moved to wrap around Zhou Hui's waist instead, desperately trying to shoulder his phantasm out of the way.

Regrettably, although the "future Mo Xi" simulated in the "Exhortation to Learning test" had been tall, handsome, and far more masculine than his incarnations in previous cycles, he was currently still an un-grown youth, entirely unable to budge his counterpart.

Murderous intent flared within Mo Xi:

—"Master! Since he's just a phantasm and a cocktail of Extreme Yin Constitution dregs that I d-i-d n-o-t w-a-n-t, it shouldn't matter if we dispose of him right now, right?!"

—"Ah, no. He is a part of you; we must be more prudent,"-Zhou Hui said with a headache.

[Why is this kid always trying to dig a pit for himself?]

—"Master, I'm scared,"-the other "Mo Xi" seized the opportunity to act spoiled, nuzzling his head against the crook of Zhou Hui's neck.

Having seemingly realized something just now, he had inexplicably reclaimed his original self-identity. Not only did he refuse to let go of Master, but from an angle Master couldn't see, he flashed the real Mo Xi a smile that was one part provocative, two parts contemptuous, and seven parts [green tea](# "Literal CN: 茶味 (cháwèi) — 'tea flavor'; slang for manipulative, deceptively innocent 'green tea bitch' behavior").

The real Mo Xi wanted nothing more than to slaughter "himself":

—"Master, let's just dispose of him right now! I'd rather suffer severe backlash and injury than leave him be!"

He practiced what he preached: flames erupted across his body, blazing a full three feet high.

Yet before the flames could touch the other "Mo Xi," that fellow practically liquefied, clinging adhesively to Zhou Hui:

—"Master, save me! I want to kill me!"

...*Wow, this is truly unbearable to watch.*

In the end, it was Mo Bei who couldn't take it anymore, letting out a cold sneer:

—"Go right ahead and cling to Master all you like! It's just us here anyway, but once we head back and 'that fellow' catches your scent on Master, let's see if your hides are as thick and beat-proof as Fifth Senior Brother's, you [thieving cats](# "Literal CN: 小偷猫 (xiǎotōumāo) — homewrecker / sneaky vixen who steals someone's man/affection")."

Both Mo Xis froze instantly in sheer terror.

Zhou Hui didn't know whether to laugh or cry as he peeled both of them off his body:

—"All right, stop fooling around. We still have matters to discuss with the Sacred Tree demon clan! Don't lose our manners on other people's territory."

This excuse provided the Mo Xis with an out, and they finally stopped their squabbling.

[Isn't it a bit too late to remember 'manners' now?!]

Mo Bei roasted inwardly.

She thought this topic was settled, but heard Master's voice ring out once more:

—"By the way, Bei'er, you mentioned earlier that you wanted to kill Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing, didn't you? Did they do something?"

[—"Uh?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"Once we return, tell me about this in detail,"-Zhou Hui said gently through divine sense, including "Mo Xi" in his final sentence:—"Including the part about 'defying the master and destroying the ancestral lineage.' I need to know every single detail."
