# Chapter 19: [Lingering PTSD](# "Literal CN: 心理阴影 (xīnlǐ yīnyǐng) — psychological trauma / mental shadow; Su Xin's visceral exam PTSD")

—"Please convey to Protector Liu that we will never fail to repay his efforts. We will certainly pamper Elder Su until he is thoroughly satisfied,"-Perfected Master Zhao, General Manager of the Palace of Fallen Immortals, said with a genial chuckle.

In his mouth, Su Xin's generational seniority had climbed yet another rung, officially promoting him to 'Elder.'

Yet Perfected Master Zhao paid it no mind. If sweet-talking this precious [trust-fund cultivator](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — child of powerful, wealthy cultivators; second-generation cultivator") could coax him into handing over a few more exploration slots for the secret realm, he would gladly kneel and call the brat 'Great-Grandfather' without a second thought.

Cutting off the transmission, Perfected Master Zhao bustled off to oversee the reception preparations:

—"Our distinguished guest will be arriving any moment,"-Perfected Master Zhao called out.-"Is everything I ordered prepared?"

—"Of course, it is already—"-a servant replied, nodding eagerly.

—"No! Spare me! Spare my life, I beg you!"-a woman's shrill, agonizing scream suddenly pierced through from the rear palace hall.

A female cultivator came stumbling out in disarray. She possessed striking, delicate beauty, and her cultivation had reached the Foundation Establishment Late Stage. In an ordinary sect, she would have been an elite maiden surrounded like [stars cupping the moon](# "Literal CN: 众星拱月 (zhòng xīng gǒng yuè) — surrounded by admirers like stars revolving around the moon"); here, however, she was pursued by several servants, utterly disheveled and frantic.

Spotting Perfected Master Zhao, she lunged toward him as if catching sight of a savior, dropping to her knees before him in a flurry:

—"My lord, spare me! Whatever you bid me do in the future, I will do it! I will be obedient, I swear it! I beg you—*mmph!*"

Before she could finish, Perfected Master Zhao slapped a Silence Talisman across her mouth, muffling her cries, followed by a vicious kick straight to her lower abdomen. The kick was laced with spiritual energy, leaving her writhing in agony, unable to rise.

Without sparing the woman a single glance, Perfected Master Zhao snapped at the servants:

—"Can you useless fools not even keep hold of a single woman?"

—"This servant was momentarily careless..."-the servants stammered.

Seizing the collapsed woman by the arms, they dragged her toward the courtyard behind the hall.

There, in the center of the courtyard, sat a colossal clam.

It stood fully two stories tall, its shell a chalky, gypsum-like grayish-white. The massive bivalve hung slightly ajar, exuding the briny stench of seawater. Its mere appearance was profoundly eerie.

Even the servants dared not approach the monstrosity directly.

Not daring to step into the courtyard itself, they shoved the woman with all their might toward the giant clam, pitching her right before its gaping maw.

A muffled wail of despair tore from the woman's throat. Disregarding the searing pain in her abdomen, she scrambled desperately to crawl back toward the hall.

She had crawled only a few paces when a cluster of glistening water droplets sprayed from between the clam's shells, showering over her.

The woman's eyes went wide with terror, but her expression swiftly glazed over into a vacant trance. Trembling, she collapsed flat against the stone floor as streams of pure, refined spiritual energy began leaking from every pore of her body, wafting like steam toward the giant clam.

Looking closer, several other female cultivators lay scattered around the giant clam in identical states.

Some sat while others reclined, every one of them wearing an ethereal, blissful smile as if lost in an unbreakable sweet dream. They blindly drained their own spiritual energy, allowing it to converge into rivers of misty luminescence that nourished the bizarre [Shen demon clam](# "Literal CN: 巨蚌 / 蜃妖 (shènyāo) — a mythical giant mirage clam that exhales illusions and drains essence").

Perfected Master Zhao stood beneath the covered walkway, observing the scene with cold detachment, his expression far from satisfied.

—"Bring another three... no, four [cultivation furnaces](# "Literal CN: 炉鼎 (lúdǐng) — human cultivation vessel exploited for dual-cultivation or spiritual extraction")! And make sure they are of higher quality,"-Perfected Master Zhao commanded.

Moments later, the servants dragged over four more weeping, exquisitely beautiful female cultivators.

Watching them, Perfected Master Zhao could not help feeling a pang of regret:

[What a waste! Kept alive and intact, they could have fetched a fortune in spirit stones! If only that supreme-grade pair was still in our hands...]

Come to think of it, this was all that Zhou Hui's fault!

Five years ago, their Palace of Fallen Immortals had merely 'accidentally' abducted two little girls on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's territory. They were children with the absolute wretchedest spiritual aptitudes, unfit even to serve as medicinal dross, yet Zhou Hui had seized upon it as a pretext to make trouble! As an exalted powerhouse at the Tribulation Transcendence Stage, he had actually deigned to act in person, steamrolling the entire Palace of Fallen Immortals into dust—the kind where literally not a single brick or roof tile was left standing! Even the chief supervisor and servants of the time had been incinerated into flying ash. (While that turned out to be a stroke of luck for Perfected Master Zhao, he had only survived because he had been away visiting their northern headquarters at the time.) Zhou Hui had also liberated all the 'merchandise' they had painstakingly gathered, including that finest pair of supreme-grade cultivation furnaces!

Even today, the mere memory made Perfected Master Zhao's heart bleed.

Reaching into his robes, he pulled out a bottle of qi-nourishing medicinal pills and swallowed one in a gulp. Still feeling unsettled, he waved at a servant:

—"Bring that over to me!"

—"My lord, though these pills replenish qi, taking too many is harmful—"-the servant hesitated.

—"Who asked you for pills? Bring me that merchandise catalog I've been reading lately!"-Perfected Master Zhao snarled.

The servant scrambled to fetch the catalog.

The items listed within were mostly flying-sword treasures and magical artifacts, alongside a handful of rare sword-art manuals. While extraordinarily valuable in the eyes of outsiders, they were nothing extraordinary to the Palace of Fallen Immortals. What made Perfected Master Zhao view them with great importance was the fact that every single one of these goods had been unearthed in this exact region. Consequently, someone had deduced that an ancient secret realm lay concealed nearby—and in all likelihood, an ancient Sword Tomb of Grade Three or higher!

This was the sole reason why the Palace of Fallen Immortals, despite having suffered such a devastating disaster at Zhou Hui's hands, still insisted on reestablishing their camp right beside the Heavenly Spirit Sect's doorstep.

Fortunately, the Heavenly Dao spun in cycles! The secret realm that the Palace of Fallen Immortals had searched for in vain for years had actually been blasted wide open in a single breath by Zhou Hui's heavenly lightning tribulation! The thought alone made Perfected Master Zhao want to laugh out loud in his sleep.

Furthermore, having researched this area for so long, even if they had lacked the power to shatter the seal previously, they had already formed a solid hypothesis regarding the secret realm's interior! That gave them a tremendous head start over everyone else!

—"So long as we retrieve the treasures from that secret realm, what need is there to worry about rebuilding our Palace of Fallen Immortals!"-Perfected Master Zhao fantasized aloud.

[And it will be even grander and more magnificent than the one Zhou Hui destroyed!]

Carried away by his grand vision, he had blurted it out unawares—though naturally, he did not dare utter Zhou Hui's name aloud.

The surrounding servants immediately echoed in obsequious agreement:

—"There is no need for the true Palace of Fallen Immortals! Even this present manor of ours is more than enough to make that unsophisticated little young master from the Northern Domain obediently hand over all his secret realm exploration slots!"

—"Naturally!"-Perfected Master Zhao said smugly.

Pleased with himself, he turned his gaze back toward the giant clam.

Now supplied with the spiritual energy of several additional female cultivators, the colossal clam seemed to bask in warm, soothing seawater, opening and closing its shells with utter contentment. Prismatic, iridescent mists billowed from between the valves, coalescing in midair into tangible, breathtaking scenery.

Suspended before them, resplendent and towering, was none other than a magnificent palace!

---

*

—"Truly worthy of the Palace of Fallen Immortals, conjuring such an incredible palace out of thin air!"-Liu Chang exclaimed in exaggerated admiration.

This time, his praise was entirely genuine.

Though Liu Chang had previously hyped the Palace of Fallen Immortals to high heaven, conditions in the Central Domain were far inferior to the Northern Domain, and the Palace of Fallen Immortals could hardly rival the true Palace of Carefree Wandering. He had merely taken their money and blown smoke with his eyes shut, fully prepared for the little ancestor to sneer at the estate's exterior. (He wasn't worried about whether Su Xin would be pleased once they stepped inside—the Palace of Fallen Immortals possessed endless illicit amusements; forget coaxing a naive young master who knew nothing of worldly pleasures, they had enough tricks to entertain even the Sect Master of the Hehuan Sect).

Yet the 'naive young master who knew nothing of worldly pleasures' was evidently not entirely ignorant.

Liu Chang heard Su Xin remark in a stiff, strained tone:

—"That is a Sea Clan palace. It must be formed from illusory [mirage-vapors](# "Literal CN: 蜃影 (shènyǐng) — mirage exhaled by a mythical Shen clam/demon")."

—"So that's what it is! No wonder it looks so lavish!"-Liu Chang gasped in awe.

[The Palace of Fallen Immortals is far too stingy—hiding such a magnificent wonder and never taking it out on ordinary days,] Liu Chang thought to himself.

Having grown up in the Central Domain, he had never laid eyes on an authentic Sea Clan palace in his life. At this moment, he leaned so far out of the cloud carriage he nearly fell out, entirely oblivious to the exceedingly complicated expression plastering Su Xin's face.

Staring at the distant palace—an aesthetic he had encountered not long ago—Su Xin asked in a voice that trembled uncontrollably:

—"The exciting spectacle you mentioned... it couldn't possibly be that the Palace of Fallen Immortals is also holding a sect tournament?"

—"You've already heard?"-Liu Chang replied casually, his eyes still glued to the palace in the distance.

He assumed someone had leaked the 'entertainment program' beforehand to curry favor with Su Xin.

Not that leaking it mattered in the least. When Perfected Master Zhao had first pitched the idea to him, Liu Chang had slapped the table in admiration at the sheer brilliance of the scheme. The title sounded thoroughly 'safe' and wholesome; even if Su Xin's parents caught wind of it, they wouldn't accuse him of corrupting their child.

And as for what a 'sect tournament' hosted by the Palace of Fallen Immortals actually entailed...

—"It is precisely for the sake of exploring the secret realm that the Palace of Fallen Immortals is holding a sect tournament,"-Liu Chang said, feeling the timing was ripe to lay the groundwork for extracting exploration slots.-"However, the Palace of Fallen Immortals is comprised entirely of fairy maidens. Their so-called tournament is merely little girls playing around. Hearing that Martial Uncle Su loved inter-sect rivalries back in the Northern Domain, why not grace them with a few pointers... Martial Uncle Su?"

Liu Chang turned his head—and finally caught sight of Su Xin's face, bleached as pale as a sheet of paper.

---

*

—"Our distinguished guest has arrived?!"-Perfected Master Zhao asked in delighted surprise on the other side.

Conducting one final inspection of his robes and crown, he soared out of the freshly conjured palace. Sure enough, accompanied by ethereal celestial music, a sparkling convoy of cloud carriages drawn by snow-white cranes and white deer appeared on the horizon, radiating dazzling immortal luminescence.

Even Perfected Master Zhao, who had seen countless wealthy guests flaunt their riches, could not help but marvel at the sheer grandeur of Su Xin's procession:

—"Truly worthy of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's most pampered little elder."

Naturally, so extravagant a procession warranted a reception of equal magnificence.

Perfected Master Zhao had long stationed two flanking rows of delicate, breathtaking fairy maidens at the palace gates, raining down sea-beds of flower petals that transformed into a fragrant airborne carpet.

Yet just as the lead cloud carriage was about to touch down onto the flower carpet, merely paces away, it abruptly ground to a halt.

Yes. It stopped dead.

The celestial music stopped. The white cranes stopped. The white deer stopped. The cloud carriage, naturally, stopped as well.

The entire luxurious convoy froze in place, as if struck by a collective Body-Freezing Talisman, utterly motionless in midair.

—"What is the meaning of this?"-Perfected Master Zhao muttered, gesturing for a servant to step forward and investigate.

Yet before the servant could even draw close, the white cranes beat their wings furiously, the white deer kicked their hooves, and the cloud carriage executed a violent, instant one-hundred-and-eighty-degree U-turn, rocketing back in the exact direction it had come!

Perfected Master Zhao was struck completely dumb.

Unable to bother sending servants, he channeled his spiritual energy to amplify his voice and called out across the sky:

—"Elder Su, why are you departing? This humble one has already prepared a grand feast—"

Had Perfected Master Zhao kept his mouth shut, it might have been fine; the moment his voice boomed out, the carriage bolted even faster!

The cloud carriage transformed into a streak of blazing white lightning, vanishing beyond the horizon in the blink of an eye!
