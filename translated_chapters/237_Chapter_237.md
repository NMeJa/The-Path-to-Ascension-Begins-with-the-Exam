# Chapter 237: Blinding Radiance

[← Previous Chapter](236_Chapter_236.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](238_Chapter_238.md)

—"It really is Sky City?"-Jian Nuo exclaimed in surprise.

As the current heir to Sky City, he naturally knew the history of his family's floating metropolis. Consequently, upon laying eyes on the embryonic prototype of Sky City, he didn't harbor many grand sentiments, only a degree of perplexity:

—"How did the Immortal Venerable know the embryonic prototype of Sky City?"-Jian Nuo wondered aloud.-"My old man is stingy to a fault and generally never lets outsiders see it. Even I was only permitted into the core a handful of times during my training."

No one present answered his query.

Jian Nuo didn't even know that his grandfather was on quite good terms with the Untiring Immortal Venerable, so it was hardly surprising that Untiring's disciples didn't know either. Moreover, the foundation of this illusion realm wasn't constructed from scratch by the Untiring Immortal Venerable in the first place, but was the genuine residual echo of the God-Demon War ten thousand years ago. Such a Sky City was naturally the most authentic visual record possible.

Then again, neither Qingyang Qingyu nor Mo Bei cared about this matter anyway.

Qingyang Qingyu couldn't care less about the relationship between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Lord of Sky City, but she was rather intrigued by Sky City's core.

Although this fell under the domain of artifact refinement, the core of Sky City was intrinsically tied to the ancestral cultivation art passed down through Sky City's ruling Jian clan; thus, only Jian clan descendants who had practiced this technique could control Sky City.

Furthermore, both Sky City and White Jade Capital drifted high in the heavens. Persistent rumors held that Sky City, like White Jade Capital, utilized True Immortal technology, meaning the Jian clan's technique was also linked to True Immortals. For Qingyang Qingyu—who had likewise studied True Immortal-related techniques and was herself a researcher of cultivation arts—this was immensely captivating, stoking an urge to investigate its inner mysteries.

Truth be told, a major reason Qingyang Qingyu had never unequivocally rejected Jian Nuo the way she rebuffed other suitors was that Sky City's cultivation arts were simply too fascinating.

Still, it was someone else's family heritage after all. Without having made up her mind to become their daughter-in-law, it was improper for her to delve too deeply into their secrets, making the present opportunity exceedingly rare.

That she could pause to ask a polite, formulaic question at this moment was already the finest fruit of her noble cultivation clan's etiquette training:

—"It's an exact reproduction? That is truly impressive,"-Qingyang Qingyu asked.-"Is it really all right for Junior Sister Mo Bei and me to enter like this?"

—"Of course it's all right,"-Jian Nuo replied, utterly incapable of denying any request from Qingyang Qingyu.-"Though no matter how perfect the reproduction, it is merely the prototype from ten thousand years ago. If Qingyu is interested in our actual Sky City, you can always visit Sky City again. When the time comes, I'll personally take you on a tour."

[You literally just said yourself that you've barely been allowed into the core area a handful of times during training.]

Qingyang Qingyu silently roasted him in her heart, though her face maintained a polite yet distant smile.

If the Jian clan's mental cultivation method was a point in his favor, Jian Nuo's overzealous enthusiasm was a massive deduction.

Qingyang Qingyu didn't dislike Jian Nuo, but she occasionally felt that the young lord of Sky City was far too irrational when it came to her.

For instance, offering to show her Sky City's core just now—were those words a city lord's heir ought to utter? As a direct descendant of a ten-thousand-year cultivation clan herself, Qingyang Qingyu naturally placed clan duty before personal inclinations; she could muster no goodwill for such reckless declarations.

Not to mention, based on her observations, Jian Nuo was not the frivolous, dissolute playboy her ancestors assumed him to be. On the contrary, Jian Nuo possessed his own shrewd depth and calculation. Yet whenever this shrewd young man laid eyes on her, he resembled a [peacock displaying its feathers](# "Literal CN: 开屏的孔雀 — showing off enthusiastically to impress a love interest"), practically itching to count out every individual plume on his tail for her to see. Qingyang Qingyu felt that unless he harbored some hidden motive, he had to be possessed by evil spirits.

Between the two possibilities, the former seemed far more likely. Occasionally, Qingyang Qingyu caught a faint trace of overbearing guilt in Jian Nuo's fawning eyes and subservient tone. If her biological father weren't still alive and well, she would almost suspect Jian Nuo was her father's murderer trying to atone for past sins.

Regrettably, Jian Nuo seemed completely oblivious to his own behavior. Qingyang Qingyu had no way of probing for the cause, leaving their relationship stranded in this awkward, neither-here-nor-there limbo.

Regarding all this, Mo Bei harbored zero thoughts.

In her view, this illusion realm belonged to her Master, which meant Sky City inside the illusion realm also belonged to her Master; Qingyang Qingyu shouldn't even be asking Jian Nuo for permission in the first place.

Nor did Mo Bei have the patience to bother with the subtle romantic tension between Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo—she didn't even want to manage her own biological twin brother, let alone outsiders. She merely stated coldly:

—"Whatever the real Sky City is like doesn't matter,"-Mo Bei said.-"Right now, we must complete our mission."

Saying so, she paid no heed to their reactions, striding forward into the depths of Sky City of her own accord.

Qingyang Qingyu hurriedly followed in her footsteps, and seeing this, Jian Nuo scrambled to chase after them.

***

There was little worth admiring inside the original Sky City. It possessed neither the majestic grandeur of White Jade Capital nor the awe-inspiring spectacle of modern Sky City. It was even inferior to the colossal treasure ship that had sailed to Mount Tianling earlier this year to visit the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Its interior structures and facilities were exceedingly crude, and some were not even fully finished.

One could easily tell that back then, Sky City was merely an expedient measure designed to evacuate the vital forces of the cultivation realm from the central vortex of the God-Demon War, prepared in tremendous haste on every front.

In fact, ten thousand years ago, Sky City had indeed departed the Central Domain at the final critical hour. It was precisely for this reason that during the final crisis of the demon invasion in the sixth reincarnation cycle, Villa Master Qingyang had adopted the exact same measure, sending away the great ship that carried his final hope—never imagining he was delivering the absolute worst "protagonist" right before his great-great-great-great-granddaughter.

Fortunately, that tragedy had not yet come to pass in this cycle.

As for Mo Bei and the others, their current task was merely a segment of the "Immortal Arts Assembly Trial System Script." Following the course of history to launch Sky City into flight was also meant to add a bit of "flavor" to the exhibition match between the two Mahayana powerhouses, preventing their performance from appearing overly staged. Put simply, it was about cranking up the difficulty, compelling the two titans—like the ancient cultivators who defended White Jade Capital in history—to safely escort Sky City out of the Central Domain amidst the crossfire between celestial soldiers and demonic hordes.

At least, that was how Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo understood the script.

Only Jian Nuo grumbled inwardly. He felt this assignment could have easily been handled by just him and Qingyu alone; there was no need to send Mo Bei along to disrupt their "two-person world." In particular, Mo Bei seemed in an extraordinary hurry, bolting straight toward Sky City's core without giving Jian Nuo any time to properly introduce the sights to Qingyang Qingyu.

Jian Nuo's personal grievances, however, were the most inconsequential detail. Before long, the group reached Sky City's central core hall. It was here that Mo Bei's footsteps abruptly halted, pausing before stepping inside.

—"We've arrived?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked, following right behind Mo Bei. Peeking curiously into the hall, a subtle expression surfaced on her face:—"It's so empty."

It was indeed barren.

One would have thought the outer sectors of Sky City were deserted enough, but the core hall was emptier still. Aside from a blood-red, ice-crystal-like pillar towering in the center of the hall, there was literally nothing else.

Not only were there no basic furnishings or decorations, but there wasn't even a single formation array inscribed on the ground.

Yet without formation arrays, how was one supposed to steer Sky City's flight?

—"This is Sky City's core? Did we take a wrong turn?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked in bewilderment.

—"...The map indicates this is the place,"-Mo Bei said.

She paused, turning in silence to look at Jian Nuo.

Mo Bei knew what needed to be done next, but moments ago, the voice of Fifth Senior Brother Lu Shulong had resonated within her sea of consciousness, instructing her to leave the next step to Jian Nuo.

Clueless about this exchange, Qingyang Qingyu assumed only Jian Nuo could resolve her doubt, and turned her gaze toward him as well.

Jian Nuo—who had spent the entire journey trying to strike up awkward small talk with Qingyang Qingyu—was now staring blankly at the blood-red pillar at the center of the chamber, his expression dazed and visibly lost in thought.

—"Lord Jian Nuo?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked, puzzled.

—"...Ah? Oh! We didn't take a wrong turn,"-Jian Nuo snapped out of his daze, an embarrassed expression flashing across his features.

He couldn't fathom what had just come over him. The instant he laid eyes on this hall—which ought to have felt reasonably familiar—an inexplicable torrent of emotions had surged within his chest: remorse, hatred, indignation, and despair. Yet he couldn't comprehend where these emotions originated, as though he had forgotten something of paramount importance that refused to resurface no matter how hard he racked his brain.

Jian Nuo could only suppress the surge of emotion, answering in a daze:

—"It is said that during the God-Demon War back then, the situation was exceedingly dire,"-Jian Nuo explained.-"Construction on Sky City was nowhere near finished; even the auxiliary formations to control the city's flight hadn't been inscribed. Therefore, the City Lord of that era had no choice but to bind his physical body to the still-embryonic City Spirit, burning through his own magical power to steer the city to the Northern Domain."

This was considered one of the monumental feats of the Jian clan's ancestors, mandatory reading material for all Jian clan offspring.

Yet Jian Nuo spoke of it without the slightest trace of pride; his tone was even somber:

—"And because of this, Sky City's City Spirit never fully matured, forcing successive generations of City Lords to supplement it using their own cultivation arts,"-Jian Nuo continued.-"In other words, once a disciple of the Jian clan assumes the mantle of City Lord, they enter a symbiotic bond with the City Spirit and can never leave Sky City for any extended period...!"

At this point, Jian Nuo abruptly halted his explanation, glancing nervously at Qingyang Qingyu.

After all, this reality meant that if he inherited Sky City, he would be shackled to that airborne fortress, and his wife would naturally be tethered alongside him. He had no idea whether Qingyang Qingyu could accept such a future.

Then again, even if she couldn't accept it, that would at least imply Qingyu had contemplated marrying him.

A glimmer of hope sprouted in Jian Nuo's chest.

Regrettably, Qingyang Qingyu hadn't thought that far ahead in the slightest.

Having listened to the explanation, she merely assumed a contemplative expression:

—"So, only a City Lord of the Jian clan can control Sky City."

—"That is how the present, real Sky City works..."-Jian Nuo admitted.

—"Ah!"-Qingyang Qingyu's eyes widened as understanding dawned.-"I see! Because this is the original Sky City, your clan's bloodline hasn't formed a connection with the City Spirit yet! Does that mean others can enter symbiosis with the City Spirit as well? Without requiring any specialized cultivation technique?"

—"No specialized technique is needed. If anything, our clan's current technique was gradually adjusted and formed based on the practical reality of coexisting with the City Spirit,"-Jian Nuo said. Knowing she was passionate about this field, he not only explained in rich detail, but even took the initiative to invite her:—"Would you like to give it a try?"

—"Huh?"-Mo Bei blurted out.

[But Fifth Senior Brother said we have to make Jian Nuo do it?]

Mo Bei's questioning gasp prompted both Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo to glance at her in bewilderment.

If Mo Xi had been here, he would have smoothly cooked up a ready-made excuse on the spot to herd Jian Nuo over. Unfortunately, the one carrying out this mission was Mo Bei, whose mouth remained as tightly shut as a marionette doll's most of the time. Her social reflexes were not even a tenth as swift as her sword draw; caught completely flat-footed, she stood frozen on the spot with her small mouth half-open.

Seeing her fall silent and resume "zoning out," Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo retracted their gazes.

Although they hadn't interacted with Mo Bei much, both assumed she simply possessed this seemingly aloof but actually "airheaded" temperament.

Moreover, compared to Mo Bei's odd behavior, Sky City's cultivation secrets held far greater allure for Qingyang Qingyu.

Yet Jian Nuo's sudden generosity made Qingyang Qingyu deeply suspicious.

—"Wouldn't doing that reveal the core secrets of your clan's cultivation arts...?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked cautiously. *She couldn't help suspecting: once I enter symbiosis with the City Spirit—even a simulated one—and learn secrets I shouldn't know, won't I be pressured into becoming your clan's daughter-in-law?*

—"Not in the slightest,"-Jian Nuo replied with a wry smile. Discerning her apprehension, he felt a twinge of disappointment, but offered an even more meticulous explanation:—"As I mentioned earlier, our family's technique underwent massive alterations solely to accommodate symbiosis with the City Spirit. There are no secret manuals or predetermined pathways for such modifications; it is by no means a universal formula. Therefore, if a cultivator practicing an entirely different art attempts it, their adaptive responses will be completely distinct, evolving into an entirely different technique."

—"Oh—?"-Qingyang Qingyu's eyes sparkled at his words.

The influence of external entities on cultivation arts was her absolute favorite subject of study. Even so, she still hesitated.

Seeing her hesitation, Jian Nuo pressed on with his persuasion:

—"Rest assured,"-Jian Nuo added.-"Even if some secret were unveiled as a result, I would never use it to coerce you into doing anything against your will. Though... to be honest, in letting you bond with the City Spirit and pilot Sky City, I do harbor a bit of selfish motivation..."

—"Hmm?"-Qingyang Qingyu furrowed her brows.

—"Qingyu, you possess profound research into cultivation arts, don't you?"-Jian Nuo continued.-"When it comes to cultivation methods, most cultivators merely practice without comprehending the underlying principles, dedicating their entire lives solely to arts that directly elevate their personal cultivation. Those genuinely intrigued by the evolution and mechanics of cultivation arts are exceedingly few, and fewer still possess your research resources. To put it frankly, your expertise in this domain surpasses even many high-tier cultivators."

He lavished praise upon Qingyang Qingyu, yet every word was the honest truth; even if other cultivators were interested, none possessed the vast resources of the Eternal Villa's eldest young mistress to pursue theoretical cultivation research.

The corners of Qingyang Qingyu's lips curved upward slightly. She considered this the most pleasing remark among all the foolish sweet talk Jian Nuo had ever spouted.

Noticing her softening demeanor, Jian Nuo wagged his invisible tail with renewed enthusiasm:

—"If it's you, you might just be able to lift my curse! After all, being tethered to Sky City through symbiosis with the City Spirit was never what the Jian clan truly desired. Yet the symbiotic process is so protracted and complex that we have never been able to find a suitable cultivation grandmaster to sever the bond. But with this rare opportunity—and with a cultivation scholar like you here, Qingyu—perhaps an answer can finally be found!"

Saying so, he stared at Qingyang Qingyu with pleading eyes, looking for all the world like a terminally ill patient afflicted with a lethal curse looking upon his sole savior.

In reality, had it not been for Qingyang Qingyu, Jian Nuo wouldn't have cared much about being unable to leave Sky City after inheriting it. This was evident from the fact that he had always resided quietly inside Sky City, never chasing after Zhou Hui across the four seas under the pretext of "broadening his horizons" the way Jian Haoge did in his youth.

However, feelings blossomed through shared time.

Compared to their current state of having zero interaction—with the lady's family displeased and the lady herself reluctant to entertain him—he might as well offer himself up as a guinea pig for her research! That was certainly a valid way of "spending time together," wasn't it?!

Jian Nuo schemed in his heart.

He gazed upon Qingyang Qingyu with an increasingly pitiful puppy-dog look.

With the other party opening his heart to such an extent, Qingyang Qingyu felt that refusing him any further would simply be too discourteous.

—"Then I will... give it a try!"-Qingyang Qingyu said, her cheeks blushing slightly.

As it turned out, that Jian Nuo had managed to win over Qingyang Qingyu in a certain reincarnation cycle was by no means pure luck.

Completely oblivious that she had walked straight into a calculated routine, Qingyang Qingyu looked around:

—"So, where is the City Spirit we are supposed to bond with?"-she asked, though her gaze settled naturally upon the blood-red ice-crystal pillar in the center of the hall.

There was no helping it; while the eldest young mistress of the Qingyang clan had low resistance against romantic courtship tactics, she was by no means stupid. In an otherwise empty hall, anyone with half a brain could tell that pillar was the sole anomaly.

—"As expected of Qingyu,"-Jian Nuo beamed. He stepped up to the pillar, maintaining a delicate, respectful distance:—"According to our clan's records, this 'pillar' is the embryonic incubation vat of the City Spirit. It was originally supposed to be nurtured inside with all manner of heavenly treasures and earthly wonders. But with time of the essence back then, the clan head of that era channeled his art and poured his spiritual power directly into the incubation vat."

—"Like this?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked.

She strode before the pillar and pressed her palm against the blood-red surface, watching as her hand sank straight into it like dipping into a pool of thick, coagulated blood.

Qingyang Qingyu's brows arched slightly. The very next instant, her robes fluttered as radiant silver motes of light drifted from her sleeves toward the pillar—the unmistakable hallmark of the Qingyang family's natal cultivation technique.

Seeing this, Jian Nuo's eyes widened in shock.

He had intended to provide a more detailed breakdown first, never expecting Qingyang Qingyu to be so decisive—or rather, so reckless.

He couldn't resist crying out in alarm:

—"Qingyu!"

—"Eh?"-Mo Bei gasped in a tiny squeak from beside the door.

Moments earlier, Mo Bei had been secretly conversing with Lu Shulong via divine sense, asking how to prompt Jian Nuo to take control of Sky City. On the other end, Lu Shulong hadn't expected to encounter difficulties on Mo Bei's end; after pitching several conversational strategies, he realized his youngest junior sister possessed zero social rhetoric, forcing him to teach her line by line. Mo Bei had just barely memorized her "lines" when she turned around, only to discover that Qingyang Qingyu had already acted!

Yet what drew Mo Bei's squeak was not Qingyang Qingyu's movement itself, but rather that the moment Qingyu's spiritual power surged into the crimson pillar, the blood-red coating dissolved into a downpour of gore! It showered over Qingyang Qingyu's face and body, staining her porcelain-white skin with foul, corrosive crimson.

And as this shower of blood cascaded down, the object sealed within the center of the pillar was laid bare.

It was unexpectedly a human spine.

And... an immortal skeleton!

Not long ago, Mo Bei had seen an immense multitude of immortal remains in the Southern Domain. However, those ancient immortal skeletons were practically petrified into fossils and had coalesced into titanic yin spirits that ordinary mortals could never manipulate. Even when the Palace of Carefree Wandering sought to exploit those celestial corpses, all they could manage was severing a tiny sliver of yin spirits to mold marionettes like Mo Bei and Mo Xi in hopes of achieving indirect control.

Yet the immortal skeleton here was exceptionally fresh—so fresh it bristled with predatory aggression.

Having just shed the suspicious crimson fluid to reveal its true form, the spine emitted a brittle *clack*. As though coming alive, it twisted its rigid segments before coiling aggressively toward Qingyang Qingyu!

—"Qingyu!"-Jian Nuo instinctively clutched the sword hilt at his hip.

—"Don't come over!"-Qingyang Qingyu commanded sharply, her eyes staring straight into the coiled spine.-"This is the critical phase of master-recognition and symbiosis! Do not disrupt it!"

—"B-But..."-Jian Nuo stammered in panic.

To his horror, the spine coiled around Qingyang Qingyu like a titanic centipede, sinking the sharp lateral spikes along its vertebrae straight into Qingyu's blood-stained skin!

A grimace of pain crossed Qingyu's face, yet her resolve did not waver in the slightest. She even took the breath to explain:

—"It's fine. I can feel... its memories,"-Qingyang Qingyu said.-"Though it looks terrifying, it is actually much easier than theoretical models predicted."

—"Theory? Easier?"-Jian Nuo repeated, bewildered.

Gritting her teeth against the agony, Qingyang Qingyu nonetheless articulated with crisp clarity:

—"Earlier, our ancestor conducted research on True Immortal Blood with the help of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and I read his paper,"-Qingyang Qingyu explained.-"While True Immortal Blood elevates one's power by astronomical leaps, because the physical constitution of True Immortals differs entirely from mortal cultivators, the blood alone induces violent graft rejection. Rashly attempting to harness that power only [pulls up seedlings to help them grow](# "Literal CN: 拔苗助长 (bámiáo zhùzhǎng) — destroying potential through reckless, forced acceleration"), and can even shatter one's spirit roots entirely."

—"However, this immortal skeleton is different—it is a Dao Bone."

—"A Dao Bone?"-Mo Bei asked.

Qingyang Qingyu didn't mind who asked the question; explaining it aloud helped organize her thoughts, aligning them with Villa Master Qingyang's research paper so she could better assimilate the symbiosis:

—"Yes, a Dao Bone,"-she answered.-"You must have heard that those who possess a Dao Bone, even if they aren't cultivators, can be received into the Heavenly Court to become True Immortals, right? While such individuals are technically True Immortals, the resilience of their immortal bodies is vastly inferior to beings who ascended naturally."

—"The original owner of this Dao Bone was likely such an existence. He... or she? They must have been an artifact refiner who descended from the Upper Realm. Taking charge of the Sky City project seemed to be a personal cultivation trial for their own forging theories, so much so that they did not hesitate to sacrifice their own flesh and blood. I can feel their... madness, passion, and despair. One must assimilate these emotions to better enter symbiosis with the immortal bone..."

Qingyang Qingyu spoke softly.

Yet she failed to realize that her own stubborn refusal to back down in the face of excruciating agony bore an uncanny resemblance to that mad artifact refiner she had just described.

Watching this scene unfold, Jian Nuo's mind fell into a sudden daze.

[—"No! Father! I need that drop of True Immortal Blood!"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Daughter")

[—"As long as I can stay by that person's side, I am willing to pay any price!"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Daughter")

Desperate voices echoed beside Jian Nuo's ears, and a silhouette bearing an uncanny resemblance to Qingyu flashed across his mind. He vaguely recalled something, yet could grasp nothing concrete, feeling only an overwhelming tide of frantic desperation seizing his chest:

[Stop her! Quickly!]

[You cannot let her do this!]

[If I stand by and watch her walk toward her own destruction, how could I ever face Qingyu, who died giving birth to her...?!]

*Wait... isn't this Qingyu right before my eyes?!*

Chaotic thoughts battered Jian Nuo's mind, his temples throbbing violently. He was paralyzed in place, unable to move—until a resounding, piercing sword hum shattered his spiraling thoughts.

Jian Nuo looked blankly toward the sound, only to see that Mo Bei had drawn her blade at some unknown moment, flickering like an ethereal phantom to Qingyang Qingyu's side, raising her sword to slash downward!

Once again, this scene overlapped with a phantom memory.

For reasons he couldn't fathom, Jian Nuo felt he had seen Mo Bei's frigid, remorseless gaze before—as though she were deciding to strike down someone of paramount importance to him...

Jian Nuo's eyes nearly tore at the corners, and he shrieked in mortal terror:

—"STOP—!"

Regrettably, Mo Bei's blade was infinitely faster than his voice.

Before Jian Nuo could even finish the first syllable, Mo Bei's strike had already descended with thunderous finality!

Yet the edge of her blade did not fall upon Qingyang Qingyu. Instead, it slammed into the empty "air" beside her! The air produced a resounding thud, like a heavy cleaver hacking into bone and sinew. By this point, any cultivator could perceive the violent fluctuation of spiritual energy; something appeared to emit a muffled groan before being sent flying by her razor-sharp sword qi!

It all happened in a flash. By the time the word "STOP!" finished echoing from Jian Nuo's throat, Mo Bei was already embroiled in ferocious combat against unseen presences—and her targets were not one, but a swarming multitude.

At this juncture, even a complete fool would realize he had misunderstood.

Jian Nuo instantly discarded the bizarre phantoms in his mind. He finally came to his senses, yet grew all the more bewildered:

—"What is going on?!"

Mo Bei didn't bother offering explanations in the slightest. Clashing continuously against the invisible entities, she commanded sharply:

—"Erect a barrier!"

Indeed, this was hardly the time for explanations. Just as she spoke, Jian Nuo abruptly felt a suffocating weight press down upon the back of his neck, as though some horrifying presence were crushing down upon him. Fortunately, the very next second, Mo Bei flickered behind him, cleaving the grotesque thing apart with a single sweep of her blade and reducing a massive cluster of indistinct entities into flying ash with her sword qi.

If even Jian Nuo was subjected to this, the situation was far more perilous for Qingyang Qingyu, who was in the midst of fusing with the immortal skeleton.

Although Mo Bei cleared away the "unidentified entities" that dared approach Qingyu, the swirling sword qi and fierce clashes threw the hall's spiritual energy into turmoil. This caused the previously docile immortal skeleton to thrash violently. Qingyang Qingyu could no longer maintain a standing posture, collapsing onto her knees as she desperately held down the bone spikes piercing her flesh.

Seeing this, Jian Nuo wasted no more breath on words. Quickly opening his storage pouch, he hurled a dozen defensive magical treasures around Qingyang Qingyu.

Yet they yielded little effect. Without even knowing what their foes were, ordinary treasures offered minimal utility.

Jian Nuo only used them to buy time; what he truly needed was to construct a formation array.

Fortunately, while Jian Nuo was no formation grandmaster, as the young lord of Sky City, he never lacked magical treasures or resources. His storage pouch contained seven or eight sets of high-grade formation arrays alone, every single one a premium treasure capable of fetching astronomical sums at auctions.

Jian Nuo originally intended to draw out the set that could be deployed fastest, followed by the most expensive one for added insurance. But he abruptly remembered that both of those arrays seemed to have been purchased at the Carefree Palace's treasure auctions, causing him to hesitate.

In the end, he selected the formation array he had sworn never to deploy outside Sky City.

It was a miniature version of the formation array genuinely utilized at the heart of Sky City!

It proved to be the correct choice.

Once Jian Nuo laid down the formation, although Mo Bei was still clashing with thunderous impacts outside the perimeter, the immortal skeleton at the center of the array calmed down, returning to its prior state.

Jian Nuo was far from reassured. Looking with heartache at the pool of blood beneath Qingyang Qingyu, yet fearing his approach would interfere with her process, he could only call out from a distance:

—"Qingyu, are you all right? If it's too much, let's just withdraw from this damned trial!"

—"...I-I'm fine,"-Qingyang Qingyu gritted her teeth and persevered.-"This is invaluable experience. I don't want to give up."

[Is it really necessary to go this far?]

Jian Nuo vehemently disapproved. He had no idea what those prior sensations were, concluding that his frantic concern had made him vulnerable to hallucinations induced by whatever was attacking them. Regardless, his worry for Qingyu was completely genuine.

Even if this was an "illusion realm," an excessively authentic illusion could inflict real, physical trauma upon cultivators! As peerlessly powerful as the Untiring Immortal Venerable was, what if he had miscalculated the strength of the trial?

At this moment, Jian Nuo deeply regretted letting Qingyang Qingyu touch the core in the first place.

Qingyang Qingyu instantly detected his emotional shift—not because her emotional intelligence had suddenly spiked, but because she possessed ample experience with how people around her reacted whenever she undertook dangerous experiments.

She had no doubt Jian Nuo would intervene just like her elders, running to fetch her ancestor at the most inconvenient moment.

Ah, speaking of Ancestor...

And so, while continuing to subdue the immortal skeleton, Qingyang Qingyu attempted to divert Jian Nuo's attention:

—"Still, this situation is indeed abnormal,"-Qingyang Qingyu said.-"Theoretically, we are merely setting up the stage. Even if it faithfully reproduces the past, we shouldn't be encountering such lethal peril. More importantly, if even we have been pushed to this state, who knows how Ancestor and the others are faring outside? Could you check on them for me, please~~?"

Her tone grew intentionally soft and sweet at the end.

With the girl he adored pleading with him like this, Jian Nuo was naturally powerless to refuse.

—"All right! I'll check right away!"-Jian Nuo agreed. If necessary, having Villa Master Qingyang persuade Qingyu would be far more effective anyway.

Harboring his own calculations, Jian Nuo habitually fished a mirror-shaped magical treasure from his storage pouch.

Only when images began manifesting upon the mirror's surface did Jian Nuo recall that this treasure was designed exclusively for observing external surroundings from aboard the real Sky City; yet inside this "illusion realm," it actually functioned just the same!

—"The illusion realm crafted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable can even make specialized clan treasures work?"-Jian Nuo muttered in astonishment.

After this relentless barrage of shocks, Jian Nuo was so stunned he barely had the energy to be surprised anymore. He merely stared numbly at the artifact in his hands—until the next scene reflected upon the glass caused his face to turn ashen and his jaw to drop.

—"How can this be?!"

—"...What is it?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked with effort.-"Did something really happen to Ancestor?"

—"I wouldn't call them safe... He and the Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum are completely surrounded,"-Jian Nuo said tensely.

—"By demons?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked.

According to the "script," it was true that the Mahayana exhibition match involved battling demons alongside their duel.

Jian Nuo, however, shook his head:

—"No... it looks like celestial soldiers and heavenly generals?"

—"What?"

Qingyang Qingyu finally couldn't resist glancing over at Jian Nuo.

Finding it difficult to explain, Jian Nuo made a series of hand seals toward the magical treasure, activating the "shared viewing" function. This technique—theoretically effective only upon the real Sky City—worked just as seamlessly on this "counterfeit Sky City."

The floor, ceiling, and surrounding walls of the core hall instantly turned crystal-clear and transparent, revealing the outside world.

Consequently, they saw with terrifying clarity that atop the churning sea of clouds outside, a formidable, imposing legion was laying siege to the floating city. Dignified in bearing and radiating majestic heavenly authority, they looked nothing like demons—they were unmistakably the legendary celestial soldiers!

Yet these celestial soldiers and heavenly generals were not merely battling the demon race; they were simultaneously fighting the cultivators defending the city and the Mahayana powerhouses who had summoned them, bearing the unmistakable demeanor of ruthless executioners intent on silencing every living soul.

—"What on earth is going on?!"-Qingyang Qingyu exclaimed in shock.

—"Could the entire illusion realm have malfunctioned?"-Jian Nuo questioned.

—"Never!"-Mo Bei executed a lethal sword form, answering with unshakeable conviction.-"How could Master's illusion realm ever malfunction? This only happens because whatever occurred in real history is what is transpiring right here."

[You trust your master a bit too much, don't you?]

Although Jian Nuo deeply revered the Untiring Immortal Venerable as well, he wouldn't dare possess such blind, absolute confidence.

Before he could speak, however, Mo Bei's movements abruptly hitched, as though she were listening to something. The next instant, an expression of genuine panic surfaced on her face for the first time as she shouted at Jian Nuo:

—"Put your magical treasure away! They can see us from the outside!"

—"We're the ones looking out—"-Jian Nuo began to reply uncomprehendingly.

He lifted his gaze, only to discover Mo Bei's warning had come too late. Countless celestial soldiers were already turning their eyes toward their location, clearly gazing straight through the walls from outside the building!

*...Right! They are celestial soldiers—True Immortals! Naturally, they possess the genuine power of True Immortals!*

*While this is Sky City, it lacks the perception-shielding arrays of the true Sky City!*

*So while we are looking out at them, they can look straight in at us!*

Only now did Jian Nuo realize the horrific truth.

He scrambled frantically to stow the magical treasure, but the celestial soldiers outside were already swarming toward them like ants smelling honey, no less frenzied than demons! Worse still, towering amidst the celestial ranks was a Giant Spirit God as colossal as a city wall. Brandishing a titanic cleaver, he swept it horizontally straight toward their core hall!

[—"Ah!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingyu")

The entire hall violently shuddered as fractures spider-webbed across the surrounding air—unmistakable proof that the entire chamber was on the verge of collapsing! Jian Nuo nearly tumbled to the floor, the magical treasure slipping from his grasp and clattering down.

In this instant, Jian Nuo finally understood what Mo Bei had been fighting all along:

She was battling the raw "killing intent" brought forth by the descent of the celestial soldiers.

True Immortals descending from the upper realm into the lower mortal world possessed this exact capability! The unconscious intent radiating from their consciousness was sufficient to coalesce into tangible, physical assaults that affected lower-realm beings.

He simply couldn't fathom how Mo Bei—a mere Golden Core cultivator who had only recently achieved breakthrough—was capable of withstanding such pressure.

*Ought one to expect nothing less from a disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable?*

Jian Nuo's thoughts were a tangled mess, an attempt to escape the brutal reality unfolding before him: he saw the Giant Spirit God hoisting that titanic cleaver once more, poised to strike a second devastating blow—

Against an attack of such magnitude, no matter how exquisite Mo Bei's swordsmanship was, it was entirely powerless. Even Mo Bei's perpetually expressionless face revealed a look of helpless indignation.

*Is there truly no way out?*

At this moment, Jian Nuo was prepared to release every single magical treasure in his possession, even using his own physical body as a shield, to protect Qingyang Qingyu.

*BOOM!*

At that exact heartbeat, a deafening thunderclap detonated across the heavens.

A brilliant flare of purple-gold light nearly blinded Jian Nuo's eyes.

And that thunder strike was merely the prelude. The next instant, lightning cascaded from the heavens like torrential rain, relentlessly blasting into the Giant Spirit God and the celestial legions besieging White Jade Capital, instantly reducing them all to flying ash!

In the span of a single breath, everything had been wiped clean.

—"Master!?"-Mo Bei cried out in joyful surprise upon witnessing the scene.

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable has arrived as well?"-Jian Nuo and Qingyang Qingyu were equally thrilled.

Yet with sharp eyes, Jian Nuo noticed that the radiant smile blooming on Mo Bei's lips turned peculiarly stiff and bizarre a moment later. Uncomprehending of her reaction, Jian Nuo followed Mo Bei's gaze.

And then, Jian Nuo froze as well.

For amidst that terrifying tempest of lightning outside stood a figure—yet it was not the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

It was...

Qu Qianxing.
