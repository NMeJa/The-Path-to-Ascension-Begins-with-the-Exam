# Chapter 109: [Perception](<# "Literal CN: 认知 (rènzhī) — Perception / Cognition / Awareness; chapter title referring to differing perspectives on Zhou Hui's sacrifices and the hidden awareness carried across reincarnations">) (第109章 认知)

—"Why is it you?"-Tao Zhu asked, furrowing his brow.

He puffed out his potbelly, which was swollen with indignation, glaring at the 'divine beast' before him.

This was supposed to have been an ordinary business transaction.

Six months ago at the Tianling Winter Solstice Auction, the Abyssal Ocean Society had sold off a precious ancient magical artifact fragment. However, instead of paying in cash, the other party requested to exchange it for the remains of a divine beast—allegedly the remains of the omniscient divine beast, the [Baize](<# "Literal CN: 白泽 (báizé) — Baize; legendary omniscient divine beast of antiquity capable of understanding the speech of all living things">).

Tao Zhu had been quite pleased with the trade. However, because the Palace of Carefree Wandering was no longer trustworthy, Tao Zhu exerted tremendous effort to safely escort his side's magical artifact to the Western Domain and welcome back the divine beast's remains.

Yet a month ago, a subordinate reported to him that the divine beast's remains had... 'come to life'?!

When dealing with a living creature—especially a 'divine beast'—one naturally couldn't use standard shipping methods. Tao Zhu instructed his subordinates to satisfy every single demand of the 'divine beast' while dropping everything at hand to greet it in person. But due to various disruptions along the journey, a full month elapsed before Tao Zhu finally met the 'divine beast' that had arrived in Fengqing City.

And as it turned out... this was the 'divine beast'?!

This thing?!

Inside the extravagantly opulent golden sedan carriage, there indeed sat a white creature.

Yet apart from the color 'white,' he didn't have half a cent's worth of relation to a divine beast.

This was glaringly an old man.

A human.

He was skinny and frail, looking like a [wrinkled ball](<# "Literal CN: 皱巴巴的球 (zhòubābā de qiú) — a crumpled/wrinkled ball; Tao Zhu's irreverent visual comparison for Ancestor Jiuzhai's shriveled, diminutive physique">), yet he wore a long beard that was snowy white and silky smooth. Thanks to this beard, the wrinkled old geezer somehow looked somewhat celestial and sage-like... No, perhaps more than somewhat. When he opened his golden eyes and looked over with a benevolent gaze, you felt as though every secret you harbored was being laid bare. That was hardly a pleasant sensation. The old man clearly knew this, so he had masked his own eyes with a talisman.

Yet Tao Zhu knew that the talisman was completely useless—Ancestor Jiuzhai could still see whatever he wished to see.

Yes, this old geezer was Ancestor Jiuzhai.

Who else could it possibly be?

—"Where did you stash my Baize?"-Tao Zhu demanded.

He treated all Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses with equal impartiality—without the slightest shred of respect.

Jiuzhai didn't mind Tao Zhu's attitude in the slightest, shaking his head sluggishly:—"What Baize? I haven't the foggiest idea. I was merely passing by, and Heaven guided me to board that carriage, indicating it would carry me to my desired destination, that's all."

—"Ha!"-Tao Zhu sneered coldly.

Ignoring Jiuzhai, he spun on his heel to leave.

It wasn't that Tao Zhu doubted Jiuzhai's words. Rather, precisely because Jiuzhai strictly spoke only the truth, before Jiuzhai posed as the Baize, the remains had likely vanished for real.

Tao Zhu was naturally anxious to dispatch people to investigate the entire transport route and figure out how his goods had gone missing into thin air.

—He wouldn't ask Jiuzhai. Even though Jiuzhai could certainly divine the answer, the karmic price was unpredictable. Through long years of dealing with him, Tao Zhu had learned his lesson: never proactively ask Jiuzhai for anything.

Yet this time, Jiuzhai took the initiative to call him back:—"As long as you deliver me to Untiring's place, I'll tell you how to find what you're looking for."

—"...Why didn't you 'merely pass by' or let 'Heaven guide you' into having Heaven carry you there?"-Tao Zhu asked.

[You're the very [vehicle](<# "Literal CN: 交通工具 (jiāotōng gōngjù) — transit vehicle / conveyance; Jiuzhai humorously viewing Tao Zhu as his heaven-ordained Uber">) guided by Heaven!]

Jiuzhai didn't say this aloud to avoid provoking Tao Zhu further, remarking only:—"Matters arose somewhat abruptly. Untiring altered the karmic fortune of the Southern Domain in these past two days."

[—"Untiring altered the Southern Domain's karmic fortune?"](<# "Speaker: Tao Zhu">)

—"Indeed. Even the Heavenly Dao itself may be rewritten because of it,"-Jiuzhai said gravely.

—"How could he..."-Tao Zhu didn't know what to say for a moment. Stroking his towering, bulging belly, he felt something was off.-"Wait a minute. If this happened in the past two days, how did you already disguise yourself as the Baize and board my carriage a month ago...?"

At this point, Tao Zhu realized he was asking nonsense.

It wasn't the first time Jiuzhai had pulled this. Sometimes, he hadn't necessarily divined the future, but merely calculated that 'taking this road brings misfortune,' 'destined to ride this carriage,' or 'heading in this direction brings luck,' and simply acted on it. As for whether anything would actually transpire, that still depended on the Heavens. In any case, divination cultivators under heaven always had reasons to explain their bizarre antics.

Tao Zhu remarked sheepishly:—"Still, why would Untiring start altering destiny like you diviners do?"

—"Precisely because he is perpetually [defying heaven to alter destiny](<# "Literal CN: 逆天改命 (nì tiān gǎi mìng) — defying heaven to alter destiny / fate; fundamental Xianxia trope of opposing celestial order to forge one's own path">), he is no diviner,"-Jiuzhai grumbled displeasedly.-"We diviners would never dream of altering heavenly destiny; even disclosing it is strictly forbidden. After all, fate is intricately interlocked. We can only leverage a few trivial details. For example, if I had told you beforehand that your transaction was doomed to fail, wouldn't that mean I wouldn't have caught this ride?"

—"Aha! You really do know where my shipment went—"-Tao Zhu started.

—"Yet this time, he has finally succeeded,"-Jiuzhai said through gritted teeth.-"After renouncing his ascension and [washing this world](<# "Literal CN: 洗了六遍 (xǐle liù biàn) — washed six times; Jiuzhai's chilling cosmological term for resetting the world and reversing time across six cycles">) six times over."

—"Wait, so my Baize... ah, ah! Ah?! What did you just say?!"-Tao Zhu stared at Jiuzhai in horror.-"Renounced ascension?! Washed six times?!"

*

—"Untiring, I heard that you... What on earth is going on here?!"

A quarter of an hour later, Tao Zhu arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect with Jiuzhai in tow.

The moment he flew outside the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate, he began clamoring at the top of his lungs. But the moment Tao Zhu barged into the immortal cave, he swallowed the second half of his sentence right back down, causing his belly to swell twofold and jiggle heavily.

Yet it was hardly surprising that Tao Zhu was so shocked: at this very moment inside Zhou Hui's immortal cave, two naked men—bearing identical appearances—were grappling with Zhou Hui. Tao Zhu seemed to recall that he (they) was Zhou Hui's eldest disciple? But why were there two of them? On top of that, there was a Lord Siwu kneeling before Zhou Hui, clinging tightly to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's thigh!

—"Apologies, pardon the intrusion,"-Tao Zhu backed away decisively.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui had no intention of making excuses.

Instead, seizing the opportunity, he addressed the three individuals around him:—"Are you satisfied now? My [pure reputation](<# "Literal CN: 清誉 (qīngyù) — pure/unsullied reputation; Zhou Hui's deadpan lament over his scholarly dignity being demolished">) in this life is completely ruined right here."

—"S-Sorry, Immortal Venerable,"-Siwu stammered, releasing his grip subconsciously.

Yuwen Xuan (A), however, had no intention of letting go, glaring murderously at Tao Zhu:—"We should just kill him."

—"Eh? Can we eat him? Can we eat him?"-Yuwen Xuan (B) asked with a grin, his mouth splitting all the way to his earlobes to reveal razor-sharp fangs.

Despite being merely two Soul Formation cultivators, they sent an inexplicable chill right through Tao Zhu's belly.

—"You lot really are..."-Zhou Hui sighed, raising his hand and suddenly sweeping it downward.-"You won't learn without a proper beating!"

Along with these words, a tremendous force slammed down upon the heads of both Yuwen Xuans, smashing them directly into the ground until their entire heads were embedded into the jade stone floor.

Having dealt with them, Zhou Hui smoothed his sleeves and called out to Tao Zhu and Jiuzhai:—"Come in!"

Only then did Tao Zhu and Jiuzhai gingerly step inside.

Tao Zhu even scrutinized the two specimens embedded in the floor:—"While I have no desire to pass judgment on your private predilections, next time, spare an old man like me from seeing such spectacles. It's far too stimulating."

—"You know very well that isn't the case,"-Zhou Hui said, shooting Tao Zhu a bizarre look.-"Besides, what's so stimulating about this? I imagined you've witnessed far worse scenes more times than you can count?"

—"Sure, I'm used to far more [hardcore and depraved](<# "Literal CN: 更XX和XXX的 (gèng XX hé XXX de) — Chinese net novel self-censored shorthand for explicit/unmentionable erotic or degenerate acts">) sights, but those people weren't you,"-Tao Zhu complained.-"If even you were fooling around with scandalous debauchery and messing around with your own disciples, I might suffer qi deviation from sheer shock."

Zhou Hui frowned:—"Just what do you take me for, Boss Tao? Messing around with disciples is naturally out of the question. As for not seeking a partner, it's purely because I've never met anyone suitable. I am an ordinary person."

Leave aside Tao Zhu—even Jiuzhai couldn't help but scoff.

Zhou Hui had no interest in continuing this discussion with a [belly spirit](<# "Literal CN: 肚子精 (dùzi jīng) — belly spirit / potbelly monster; Zhou Hui's deadpan internal moniker for the round, fat Tao Zhu">) and a [wrinkled ball](<# "Literal CN: 长皱纹的球 (zhǎng zhòuwén de qiú) — wrinkled ball; Zhou Hui's internal moniker for the shriveled, diminutive Ancestor Jiuzhai">). Resigned, he asked:—"Just what did you two come here for?"

—"That karmic fortune—"-Jiuzhai began to ask.

—"Ah!"-Tao Zhu recalled his purpose, shouting anew:—"I heard you gave up on ascension?!"

—"Indeed. Haven't I told you that long ago?"-Zhou Hui replied in pleasant surprise.

He had repeated it several times, yet seemingly no one believed him. Last time, Tao Zhu had spun a web of bizarre theories, driving Zhou Hui to abandon candor altogether and simply humored their wild speculations.

Now that Tao Zhu had finally seen the light, Zhou Hui felt quite pleased.

In truth, he had hoped to inquire with old friends about what retirement in the cultivation world ought to look like, as Zhou Hui felt his current days were somehow wildly off track:—"I wanted to give up on ascension long ago. After all, slaving away for pointless matters is utterly devoid of meaning."

—"Hahaha, that's hilarious!"-Tao Zhu let out a few fake chuckles before adopting a solemn expression.-"Don't change the subject. I had no intention of prying into your future plans—unlike Jiuzhai, even if you overhauled every shred of this world's karmic fortune, I wouldn't have the slightest objection. So there's no need to brush me off with such nonsense!"

[...I wasn't brushing you off.]

Zhou Hui thought with a headache. Evidently, what Tao Zhu was referring to wasn't the same thing at all.

Sure enough, Tao Zhu pressed on:—"I heard from Jiuzhai that you once gave up on ascension in the past? Something that already happened!"

—"...Surely not?"-Zhou Hui remained silent for a moment before replying.-"If an opportunity to ascend presented itself, how could I possibly give it up?"

Hearing this, Tao Zhu breathed a sigh of relief instead:—"I knew it was impossible—"

—"How could you not have?!"-Jiuzhai interjected indignantly.-"When you washed this world for the very first time, you clearly renounced your ascension!"

As he spoke, he made a hand seal, and an illusory light screen materialized before everyone.

Faintly visible was Mount Tianling, yet engulfed in roaring flames. Sect disciples could be vaguely seen collapsed across the ground, while the Untiring Immortal Venerable hovered furiously mid-air, locked in combat with someone.

No, it couldn't even be called a battle.

That 'enemy' wielded a familiar longsword in hand.

Siwu noticed this and glanced toward the study of the immortal cave, where the Immortal-Slaying Sword had been brought back, discarded on the floor like a piece of trash.

Yet that Immortal-Slaying Sword had failed to slay even Siwu, to say nothing of the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

In the illusion, however, the Untiring Immortal Venerable took a direct strike from the Immortal-Slaying Sword with his bare body. Only after allowing the opponent to pierce a gaping hole through his chest did he kill that 'enemy' as easily as crushing an insect.

Then, with sorrowful and furious eyes, he looked down upon the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Above him, thunder roared and lightning flashed—he was right in the midst of Heavenly Tribulation, though that tribulation lightning appeared to be drawing to a close, lacking immense ferocity.

Yet Zhou Hui didn't spare the tribulation lightning a second glance. Making a single hand gesture, a torrential flood of light erupted from his sword-pierced chest, instantly submerging everything—even the tribulation lightning itself.

—"Does this not count as renouncing it?"-Jiuzhai demanded.-"You were a mere single tribulation lightning bolt away from ascending, yet you sacrificed yourself to wash the entire world, forcing time to reverse!"

—"Is this for real?!"-Tao Zhu asked.

Watching everything recorded within the illusion, Zhou Hui's expression gradually turned icy. His silver-gray pupils hardened like permafrost, terrifyingly cold.

This time, Zhou Hui did not deny it, stating in an icy tone:—"How could I ascend under such circumstances? Several of my children were dead."

—"They weren't all dead!"-Jiuzhai argued.-"And shouldn't the standard procedure be ascending to the [Immortal-Demon Realm](<# "Literal CN: 仙魔界 (xiānmó jiè) — Immortal-Demon Realm; the intermediate upper plane above the mortal cultivation world, distinct from the True Immortal Realm">) to hunt down the mastermind for revenge? That was originally the initial Heavenly Destiny—"

The second half of his words froze in his throat under Zhou Hui's chilling gaze.

—"Revenge?"-Zhou Hui asked softly.-"Can revenge resurrect my children?"

[—"..."](<# "Speaker: Jiuzhai">)

Under the Untiring Immortal Venerable's spiritual pressure, Tao Zhu and Jiuzhai froze stiff on the spot.

Zhou Hui, in foul spirits, looked toward the illusion conjured by Jiuzhai, which had now dissolved into a blank white screen.

He was reminded of odious memories.

Indeed, if one were to summarize Zhou Hui's reincarnation experiences across these seven lives, the time he had been closest to ascension was, ironically, the first life.

That Heavenly Tribulation had been the easiest of all the cycles. If not for sensing the news of Mo Xi's death right before the final lightning strike, Zhou Hui would have practically ascended.

Yet when he rushed back while braving the tribulation lightning, what met his eyes was a Nascent Soul cultivator holding the Immortal-Slaying Sword, having slaughtered Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye.

[So! Was that any time to ascend with peace of mind?!]

[My home base had been turned into ruins!]

[I had been forced to take a sword strike myself just to restart the entire world. How was that not a failed ascension?]

Zhou Hui thought.

Still, this was timely. Having felt somewhat perplexed by the white lamb's words earlier, Zhou Hui took the opportunity to review his previous six lifetimes:

In the First Life: the sect was ambushed, his disciples were slaughtered, he took a sword strike himself, and restarted the world.

In the Second Life: learning his lesson, he reinforced the mountain-protecting array, helped disciples like Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi break through to higher realms, tracked down the 'enemy' from the first life, and straightened out that man's life to ensure he had zero motive to assault the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Zhou Hui then prepared for his second ascension. This tribulation lightning was stronger than the first, yet posed no immense obstacle to Zhou Hui. What truly shocked Zhou Hui was that 'another' enemy arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect wielding the Immortal-Slaying Sword, reenacting the tragedy of the first life.

In the Third Life: Zhou Hui confirmed that someone was deliberately targeting him, which was why multiple attempts with the 'Immortal-Slaying Sword' took place. Even if one puppet was eliminated, another would appear. To prevent his disciples from being implicated, Zhou Hui chose not to establish the Heavenly Spirit Sect at all, fostering all his direct disciples out to others. As it turned out, perhaps because things deviated too drastically from the first two lives, those disciples either awakened their past memories or blackened into malice, throwing the places fostering them into utter chaos. To make matters worse, some of them—by coincidence or cruel inevitability—wandered near Zhou Hui's secluded cultivation grounds. Thus, the Immortal-Slaying Sword emerged once more, bringing an end to the third life.

The Fourth Life was best left unmentioned—an utterly botched restart. The disciples' memories hadn't been wiped clean, so they reincarnated burdened with the grudges and grievances of the previous life. Even though Zhou Hui no longer dared foster them out to torment others, the disciples stirred up a storm of internal strife within the Heavenly Spirit Sect. In the face of relentless civil warfare, Zhou Hui didn't dare undergo tribulation at all, opting to reset the world directly.

In the Fifth Life: although Zhou Hui suspected a few disciples still held residual memories, after the harrowing shura field of the previous life, they seemed relatively subdued in this one—at least maintaining surface harmony without stirring up trouble. Zhou Hui moved with utmost speed to enter Tribulation Transcendence before the Immortal-Slaying Sword could be prepared—having realized through the previous cycles that the enemy required time to orchestrate an assassination. To his horror, the celestial lightning cleaved half the world away.

The Sixth Life was a tranquil lifetime. Because the cultivation world had not merely been restarted, and even time reversal could not fully mend the cosmic fractures of the fifth life, this cycle was devoted to recuperation. Zhou Hui's own soul required rest, and he needed to research how to smuggle across spatial rifts. Consequently, after repairing the entire world in this lifetime, lacking absolute confidence in ascending safely and unwilling to risk lightning cleaving half the world again, he chose to execute another restart.

The Seventh Life was the current lifetime. Zhou Hui's strategy had initially been similar to the fifth life: he intended to fight a swift war of resolution.

However, taking into account the destructive power of Heavenly Tribulation, the Zhou Hui of this life opted to smuggle across via spatial rifts. Each time he summoned tribulation lightning, he strictly controlled it to an intensity that would not destabilize heaven and earth. Such incomplete tribulations naturally could not lead to successful ascension, but they sufficed for Zhou Hui to locate suitable spatial rifts. Who could have foreseen that instead of reaching the Immortal-Demon Realm or the [True Immortal Realm](<# "Literal CN: 真仙界 (zhēnxiān jiè) — True Immortal Realm; the supreme celestial realm of genuine immortals, distinct from the mortal world and lower immortal planes">), his soul ended up flung into an entirely different world.

It was precisely during that detour that Zhou Hui caught sight of the Heavenly Dao's script. Lacking the authority of a True Immortal to edit the script, Zhou Hui chose to lie flat and renounce ascension, carrying on peacefully until the present day.

—"That's how I recall it,"-having finished his count, Zhou Hui confirmed with Jiuzhai.-"Am I correct?"

—"...Is this really something a mere 'correct' can settle?"-Tao Zhu muttered.

Dragging over a chair, he collapsed heavily into it, feeling as though he couldn't take much more of this.

Before rushing over, Tao Zhu had thought the tales he heard from Jiuzhai were preposterous enough. He never imagined that what Untiring had actually done was infinitely more unbelievable than anything Jiuzhai described.

Just what was this world to him?!

Starting over whenever he felt like it! Washing it clean whenever he pleased! Was this kind of thing even within the realm of what Tribulation Transcendence could achieve?!

Tao Zhu felt that the other Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses he knew were nothing like this.

At the very least, Jiuzhai certainly wasn't.

Jiuzhai, too, wore an expression that defied description.

—"Jiuzhai?"-Zhou Hui pressed.

Jiuzhai didn't answer directly, speaking like a riddle instead:—"...Just as I feel you gave up on ascension while you believe you failed your tribulation, when it comes to the exact same event, everyone's perception is entirely different."

The moment Tao Zhu saw the old man acting like this, he knew Jiuzhai definitely held a different view regarding Zhou Hui's accounting. Yet the old diviner's habit of beating around the bush had flared up again, as though speaking a word too many would invite celestial lightning down upon his head.

Zhou Hui realized this as well, furrowing his brow.

Still, Zhou Hui didn't press Jiuzhai. If the other party was unwilling to speak, he naturally had his reasons. For divination cultivators in particular, words spoken casually could directly jeopardize their lifespan and cultivation base. Zhou Hui felt this matter was ultimately his own concern, and had no wish to shift the risk onto a friend.

[Better to ask someone who won't get struck by lightning.]

Thinking this, Zhou Hui turned his gaze, only to see that Siwu was still there.

This fellow was another headache.

Zhou Hui had assumed that saving him would bring the matter to a close. To his dismay, the man had clung to Zhou Hui's leg all the way back, gazing at him with an exceedingly bizarre look in his eyes.

Tao Zhu finally noticed Siwu's presence, asking in puzzlement:—"Isn't this Siwu from the Ceremony Court? I heard he was taught a lesson by you not long ago. Why is he here?"

—"That was due to my own foolishness in falling into a trap,"-Siwu said without taking offense at Tao Zhu picking at his scab. Instead, looking at the Untiring Immortal Venerable with sparkling eyes, he declared:—"To make amends, from this day forth, I shall worship Immortal Venerable as a god!"

—"...Where did he bump his head?"-Tao Zhu asked.

Zhou Hui likewise suspected Siwu's brain had suffered trauma during the demon attack—though theoretically, a Mahayana cultivator shouldn't suffer brain damage from a mere knock.

[I'll have Peilan examine his head later.]

Zhou Hui thought.

Since serious business was currently being discussed, Zhou Hui had no time to attend to Siwu for the moment.

Averting his gaze from Siwu, he looked toward his 'two' eldest disciples whom he had forcefully planted into the floor earlier.

Only now did Zhou Hui realize that his hand had been a bit too heavy just now. The Yuwen Xuans had been unable to maintain human form, reverting to the shapes of lambs (Taoties) in record time, lying unconscious inside the craters they had created.

Gripping the white lamb by its hind leg, Zhou Hui lifted it into the air and gave it a vigorous shake.

—"Those seven lives you mentioned earlier—can you count them out for me? Which seven lives were they?"-Zhou Hui asked.

He had wanted to ask this question long ago. But earlier in the Abyss Sea, the black lamb had awakened, explosively transforming back into human form and getting into a heated brawl with the white lamb over 'who Master belonged to.' In a place like the Abyss Sea, Zhou Hui feared their commotion would attract other fiends and monsters, leaving him no choice but to bring them back first.

—Which was precisely the scene Tao Zhu and Jiuzhai had walked in on.

To his surprise, once awakened, the white lamb didn't answer. It merely let out a series of whines, snapping its tooth-filled maw in an attempt to bite Zhou Hui.

It appeared that turning into a lamb caused a drop in IQ—both Yuwen Xuans were identical in this regard.

Furthermore, the white lamb's intelligence was noticeably lower—even in human form, it possessed far less human common sense.

—"It's futile. Ferocious beasts are chaotic by nature. Even if you read its mind directly, you won't decipher what it's thinking,"-Jiuzhai suddenly advised.-"Since you care so deeply, why don't you ask that [True Dragon](<# "Literal CN: 真龙 (zhēnlóng) — True Dragon; primordial sacred dragon of the Upper Realm, possessing innate immunity to memory-wiping time reversals">) disciple of yours?"

[—"Shulong?"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

—"Indeed,"-Jiuzhai nodded. While he couldn't speak freely himself, he clearly hoped Zhou Hui would learn the full truth from the one who could.-"A True Dragon is a sacred entity of the Upper Realm. Even trapped in the lower realm, completely wiping his memories is impossible."

—"Wait. Are you saying Shulong might possess all the memories of my repeated reincarnations?"-Zhou Hui was shaken.-"All six or seven lives... every single one of them?! Why did that child never show the slightest hint of it?!"

No one could answer.

Nor did Zhou Hui require an answer from anyone other than Lu Shulong. In asking, he was merely feeling a master's deep shame.

Those memories were akin to a shameful dark history; Zhou Hui had never wished for any disciple to remember those dreadful pasts. He never imagined that Lu Shulong had quietly carried the burden of all those memories in complete silence.

Shaking his head, Zhou Hui walked toward the entrance of the cave dwelling:

[—"I'll go ask Shulong."](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

[—"Master, were you looking for me?"](<# "Speaker: Lu Shulong">)

In response to those words, Lu Shulong was already standing at the cave entrance, though no one had noticed when he arrived.

Everyone was startled, but considering they had been so deeply engrossed in such bizarre revelations, and that the newcomer was a True Dragon, failing to perceive him was understandable, so they relaxed.

Only Zhou Hui gazed at Lu Shulong with a profoundly complicated expression.

At a loss for how to broach the subject, he asked only:—"Shulong, did you come here for something?"

Lu Shulong, however, wore his usual gentle smile. Not a single shadow lingered upon that handsome face that rivaled Qu Qianxing's. Looking as though he knew nothing at all, he consulted Zhou Hui:

—"Master, Junior Sister sent word that the Southern Domain wishes to introduce the Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks."

—"She has already arranged for the Saintess of the Qinghuang Clan from the Divine Fire Abyss Tree to board a Kunpeng. She should arrive tonight."

—"How should we receive her?"
