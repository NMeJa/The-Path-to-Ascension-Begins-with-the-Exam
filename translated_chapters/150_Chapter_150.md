# Chapter 150: The Hint (第150章 暗示)

[← Previous Chapter](149_Chapter_149.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](151_Chapter_151.md)

---

[—"What the heck?! And here I thought we were actually going to fight demons!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The second-generation scions chattered and laughed among themselves in giddy amusement.

Seeing them behave like this, Liu Xuenian anxiously retorted:

—"No, these *are* demons..."-Liu Xuenian said with hurried urgency.

—"Hahaha, Xuenian, you're so easy to fool! You didn't actually take Young Master Jian's words seriously, did you?"-someone laughed dismissively.-"No matter how you look at it, this is obviously just a game like Immortal Chess."

[—"No, it's way more fun than Immortal Chess!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"I heard that the Heavenly Spirit Sect previously renovated secret realms, using phantoms to train disciples and familiarize them with exploration protocols. They called it an 'advanced edition' of Immortal Chess—this must be it!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The second-generation cultivators babbled eagerly, peering downward with intense fascination.

At this moment, they were naturally standing inside the Spirit Stone Secret Realm.

To be precise, they were standing atop a sparkling causeway carved from pure spirit stone.

Nor was there merely one such causeway; there were countless paths sprawling in every direction.

They intersected and crisscrossed across the vast subterranean expanse, yet every single one ultimately converged upon the colossal tower stationed at the heart of the palace. They resembled heavy chains binding the giant tower, or perhaps endless, unsweepable layers of spiderwebs draped around it, lending the entire structure an eerie aura akin to a legendary Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Down below on the spirit-stone causeways, demons were genuinely marching past.

And not just a stray beast or two, but whole swarms!

Fortunately, bizarre restrictions seemed to govern these spirit-stone causeways, preventing the demonic fiends from straying far from the pathway whether flying or running. They could only stagger forward in a rigid, predetermined line along the causeways.

Whenever they advanced, various "chess pieces" would rain down from the central tower: defensive arrow towers unleashing relentless volleys of spirit arrows, mechanical contraptions spewing roaring torrents of flame, or valiant, battle-hardened soldiers and commanders.

Under normal circumstances, even a single demon would cause cultivators immense headaches. Here, however, they were herded along like sheep, methodically butchered every step of the way until not a single fiend remained.

Naturally, there were failures as well.

Whenever demonic swarms breached the defenses and surged into the tower, that particular tier would flash with alarming crimson light. The cultivators garrisoning inside would come tumbling out amidst a storm of foul curses, and that entire floor would be sealed off, emblazoned with the stark characters for "DEFEAT."

Having all played Immortal Chess before, the second-generation cultivators present naturally understood what this signified.

Consequently, they immediately felt they "understood" the whole picture.

In fact, they grew so excited they could hardly wait to dive in and experience it for themselves.

After all, while they had heard from childhood that demons were the mortal enemies of immortal cultivators, a "game" centered on slaughtering demons was something none of them had ever played!

—There was a slight misunderstanding on the scions' part here: in the peaceful environment of the Northern Domain, spotting even a single demon was a monumental event. What's more, any demon capable of surviving in the cultivator-dominated north was a formidable menace not to be underestimated—a completely different beast from these dim-witted, low-level abyss demons who had never even laid eyes on a mortal.

Thus, the second-generation cultivators naturally assumed these demons were pre-programmed phantoms, identical to the illusory enemies on an Immortal Chess board—merely part of the "game."

Only Liu Xuenian found them completely beyond reason:

—"No, this is genuine combat against demons! Can't you sense the demonic qi?"-Liu Xuenian pressed.

[—"Huh? Demonic qi? You mean that black smoke they dissolve into?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"The spiritual energy in this palace does feel a bit uncanny... Truly impressive! The immersion is incredible."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Yeah, feels just like the real thing."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

...It wasn't just *like* the real thing! This *was* real!

Liu Xuenian desperately tried to persuade this bunch whose brains were stuffed with nothing but games:

—"Have you forgotten what Young Master Jian told us on the way here? This spirit-stone palace was constructed to seal the very last passage to the Demon Realm left behind from the God-Demon War! Looking at the current situation, that passage has clearly breached, and demons are pouring out!"-Liu Xuenian pleaded.

But why?

The designated "time" clearly hadn't arrived yet!

And what on earth was Untiring trying to pull this time?!

Even if the Demon Realm passage had sprung a leak prematurely, with his capabilities, it wasn't as though he couldn't patch it shut!

What was the meaning of this grand posture, acting as though he intended to ignite a Second Human-Demon War?

Yet in this lifetime, far too many unexpected events had already occurred. The entire situation was spiraling out of control. He could not sit back and allow Untiring to continue indulging his own whims.

Above all, the current predicament was simply too bizarre!

Faced with an invading demon horde, this flock of young brats wasn't the least bit terrified—they actually wanted to treat it as a "game"!

Liu Xuenian thought in mounting agitation, attempting to put forward a suggestion:

—"This is a catastrophic crisis that could lead to the invasion of our entire realm! It cannot be resolved by us alone—we must inform everyone!"-Liu Xuenian urged.

At the very least, get the rest of the world to rein in the Untiring Immortal Venerable!

Yet hearing his warning, this bunch of Northern Domain young masters merely sighed in admiration:

[—"Whoa, what a realistic lore backstory!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Well, it's rooted in real history, right? I heard before that Eternal Villa has been using vast quantities of spirit stones to seal demons, and they wouldn't be fully purified until ten thousand years had passed."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"It's been well over ten thousand years since the God-Demon War, hasn't it? So the purification must be complete!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Even if purification isn't 100% finished, after such a long time, there can't be many demons left."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"That explains why the spiritual energy here feels a bit unusual."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Most likely. Eternal Villa probably saw that the seal was expiring and felt leaving all those spirit stones idle was a waste. But using spirit stones that had sealed demons directly for normal cultivation would feel pretty [unsettling](# "Literal CN: 膈应 (gèying) — uncomfortable / nauseating / grossed out"), so they commissioned Heavenly Spirit Sect to renovate it!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Makes total sense!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Listening to their chatter, Liu Xuenian nearly coughed up blood in fury:

—"But demonic qi isn't something you can play with!"-Liu Xuenian cried.

Though he had long known this group were fools, he had never imagined they were moronic to such an astronomical degree.

Right at that moment, someone suddenly had a burst of "intellect":

—"Xuenian, are you familiar with demonic qi?"-a scion asked curiously.

The others turned their gazes toward Liu Xuenian as well.

Right—the "character setting" of his current identity was a delicate, frail young scion of an immortal cultivation clan. Naturally, he could never have come into contact with demons, nor should he know what demonic qi felt like.

—"A-A little bit, perhaps?"-Liu Xuenian coughed twice, his already pale complexion turning almost translucent.-"Due to my frail constitution, I have been hypersensitive to the purity of spiritual energy since childhood. In an environment like this... *cough, cough, cough!*"

He erupted into a violent fit of coughing. A smear of blood stained his bloodless lips, unexpectedly imbuing him with a bewitching, delicate charm.

Catching sight of that trace of blood, the gazes of everyone present grew hazy and unfocused:

*What were we about to do just now?*

*It didn't seem important anymore. What mattered right now was Xuenian.*

*We must take him away from here, and just as he said, inform everyone...*

—"What are you all standing around here for?"-a voice called out.

Right then, Jian Nuo's voice rang out.

Jian Nuo had originally been leading the way, but he had walked a bit briskly, while the group behind him had been dazzled by the spectacle below. As a result, by the time Jian Nuo reached the command tower, he looked back and saw them still loitering on the causeway, forcing him to backtrack to collect them.

At this moment, the crowd that had been buzzing with excitement just moments before inexplicably developed an urge to depart.

Turning to Jian Nuo, they said:

—"Young Master Jian, Xuenian isn't feeling well, so—"-one began. *So... we should leave?*

That second half of the sentence was abruptly swallowed back down.

Every single person who turned toward Jian Nuo froze, staring blankly past him... at Qu Qianxing standing right behind him.

He was so breathtakingly gorgeous that words failed to do him justice; wherever he stood, he naturally commanded the absolute center of everyone's attention.

And Qu Qianxing was never one to shy away from scrutiny. He lifted his head with poise, meeting everyone's gaze directly.

Instantly, everyone felt as though they had been pricked by a sharp rose thorn, a sudden jolt shivering through their bodies.

Watching their bizarre, stunned expressions, Jian Nuo muttered suspiciously to himself:

[Sure, Qu Qianxing is exceptionally handsome, but there's no reason for these guys to look like they've been struck by Gu poison, right?... No, wait. Even before seeing Qu Qianxing, this bunch was already acting rather odd.]

—"What are you doing?"-Jian Nuo asked for the second time.

—"Xuenian feels a bit unwell, so we..."-a scion stammered. *Wait, didn't I come here to play? Why on earth was I thinking of leaving with Xuenian just now? It's not like I can cure him!*

Under Qu Qianxing's penetrating gaze, the group inexplicably regained their sanity, just as inexplicably as they had wanted to leave moments ago.

It was merely a fleeting thought, so they didn't dwell on it, hastily shifting their story:

—"So we wanted to find a place for Xuenian to rest. His health is poor, and he can't adjust to the environment here,"-the scion corrected smoothly.

—"Qianxing, what do you think..."-Jian Nuo turned to Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing nodded:

—"Let us proceed to the command tower first. Inside the tower, safe rooms have been constructed. Not only do they completely isolate demonic qi, but they also offer abundant spiritual energy to treat minor physical ailments,"-Qu Qianxing said.

—"That is wonderful,"-Jian Nuo said.

Yet only Jian Nuo bothered to express such courtesy. The moment Qu Qianxing appeared, Xuenian had ceased to matter to the rest.

Someone seized the opportunity to ask Jian Nuo:

—"Young Master Jian, won't you introduce this beauti... young gentleman?"-a scion asked. Under Qu Qianxing's steady gaze, no one dared act too frivolous to his face.

—"This is Qu Qianxing, direct disciple under the Untiring Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-since asked, Jian Nuo introduced him, then continued.-"And these gentlemen are—"

One by one, he introduced each second-generation cultivator from the Northern Domain.

Because he had previously spoken with Qu Qianxing regarding "investigating the Curse of the True Immortals," and was well aware that this circle of [fair-weather scion buddies](# "Literal CN: 狐朋狗友 (húpéng-gǒuyǒu) — disreputable friends / scoundrel companions") was far from squeaky-clean, Jian Nuo provided exceptionally detailed introductions. He essentially hinted to Qu Qianxing each person's respective faction, background, and allegiance to prevent them from causing unnecessary trouble.

Qu Qianxing listened attentively as well.

However, when he finally arrived at Liu Xuenian, Jian Nuo merely said:

—"This is Liu Xuenian, youngest son of the Liu family of Sky City."

—"And?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"And what?"-Jian Nuo blinked in confusion.

*Don't you feel that compared to everyone else's, his introduction is conspicuously brief?*

Qu Qianxing arched a beautifully shaped eyebrow.

Yet Jian Nuo appeared utterly oblivious to anything amiss with his introduction. Xuenian, on the other hand, seemed to sense something, offering a faint, delicate smile:

—"I have been frail since childhood and remained secluded at home. I possess neither grand reputation nor distinguished achievements to speak of,"-Liu Xuenian said softly.

Qu Qianxing remained silent, his gaze fixed steadily upon Liu Xuenian.

He did not buy that explanation for a second.

On the contrary, the instant Liu Xuenian spoke, a momentary haze had drifted across Qu Qianxing's eyes for half a second. Immediately following, however, tiny sparks of purple-gold electric current flashed deep within Qu Qianxing's pupils, dispelling the fog. What remained was a viscous, nauseating discomfort, as though he were sinking deep into the murky bog of a nightmare.

Qu Qianxing found this sensation eerily familiar.

There had once been a period when he was trapped in this very nightmarish sensation. It was not until he arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect and stayed by Master's side that his soul had found true, unshakeable tranquility.

But that made no sense!

The one who had plunged Qu Qianxing into that feeling in the past was an elder from the Palace of Carefree Wandering. Under the pretext of accepting Qu Qianxing as a disciple, that elder had demanded the mortal emperor surrender Qu Qianxing to him. The emperor dared not disobey an immortal. Nay, it was more accurate to say that from the imperial court down to the common populace, everyone considered it Qu Qianxing's supreme honor. Only Qu Qianxing himself was unwilling. Having noticed several deeply anomalous, sinister behaviors from that elder while finding no one willing to aid him, he had ultimately resorted to an archaic sacrificial rite of dubious efficacy, sacrificing himself to a deity in order to obtain the power to drag his foe into mutual destruction.

—As it turned out, Qu Qianxing's sacrifice had not functioned as a true divine ritual. That archaic rite merely targeted the most powerful immortal cultivator in the vicinity. Yet that had been his greatest fortune: the "deity" who responded to Qu Qianxing was his Master, and thus Qu Qianxing had survived.

But that elder was dead... wasn't he? Probably?

In any case, an elder of Carefree Palace should have zero connection to an immortal cultivation clan of Sky City.

*Could I be mistaken?*

Pondering this, Qu Qianxing offered Liu Xuenian a faint smile:

—"No, I merely meant that without understanding the specific nature of your condition, it is difficult for me to arrange an appropriate recuperative regimen,"-Qu Qianxing said.

—"Huh? Oh yeah! Xuenian, what exactly is wrong with you anyway?"-someone asked in genuine puzzlement.

They had always been exceptionally attentive to Liu Xuenian, yet only now did they seem to remember to actually ask.

[You're the ones who are sick!]

Liu Xuenian's expression stiffened slightly:

—"I am not sick, merely innately frail since birth,"-Liu Xuenian replied.

He failed to notice that a subtle arc of golden lightning had slipped down from Qu Qianxing's sleeve. It popped three or four tiny sparks upon the floor before clinging invisibly to the hem of Xuenian's robe.

Liu Xuenian merely glared in dissatisfaction at this pack of [fickle fence-sitters](# "Literal CN: 墙头草 (qiángtóucǎo) — grass atop a wall swaying with the wind; fickle opportunists"), watching them fawn around Qu Qianxing as they strode forward:

[Tsk, that skin and vessel really are flawless!]

[What a pity I failed to claim it back then.]

[A shame Untiring is around; I still cannot make a move.]

[Compared to that, I need to figure out how to get away.]

He hadn't been lying—in his current state, it was genuinely unsuitable for him to come into contact with demonic qi.

[Given the present circumstances, I can only wait until these idiots have had their fill of this 'game,' then find an opportunity to depart.]

Liu Xuenian brooded in dark resentment.

Yet as it turned out, their stay lasted a full half-month.

---

Liu Xuenian sat blankly inside the "safe room."

Exactly as Qu Qianxing had guaranteed, this safe room was fortified by a staggering fifty protective formations; not a single wisp of demonic qi could penetrate within.

The catch was that having established his "cannot touch demonic qi" persona, he couldn't leave either.

As a result, Liu Xuenian was trapped like a caged bird. Only after waiting for what felt like an eternity did a few figures finally wander in:

[—"Xuenian, how are you holding up?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"We brought you some spirit tea."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"And check out this plush doll! It was the drop reward for successfully defending the thirteenth floor."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Liu Xuenian stared at them.

In theory, these few individuals were the ones under his deepest "control" among the second-generation clique.

This "control" was not some crude, rigid evil art that hijacked soul and flesh—after all, these were high-born cultivation scions of Sky City, each hailing from illustrious lineages with exhaustive defensive wards. Anything overt would easily trigger alarms, nor did he need complete puppetry. Thus, he had merely employed a touch of "suggestion."

A tiny, subtle hint.

Merely amplifying an emotion that already existed.

For example, Liu Xuenian was exceptionally delicate and pretty, possessed of a frail, [heart-melting vulnerability](# "Literal CN: 我见犹怜 (wǒjiàn-yóulián) — 'even I feel pity upon seeing her/him' — evoking tender, protective affection"). Even among peers of the same sex, it was natural to feel a degree of protective instinct. By merely magnifying that existing sentiment, it was more than enough to plant an obsessive urge in their minds to shield and protect Liu Xuenian at all times.

Even if such a suggestion were temporarily overshadowed by a more intense shock—such as Qu Qianxing's breathtaking beauty—it would never dissipate.

Or so it was supposed to be.

Yet lately, things had gone terribly off the rails.

Liu Xuenian rested his chin on his palm and sighed softly, asking in a tender voice:

—"Are you all going to continue today?"-Liu Xuenian asked, gazing deeply into their eyes to reinforce his psychological suggestion.

Qu Qianxing was not present right now; by all accounts, this suggestion should have taken hold seamlessly.

Indeed, the scions appeared dazed for a few seconds. But the moment they registered what Liu Xuenian was asking, their expressions abruptly lit up with wild excitement:

[—"Of course we're continuing! I'm about to level up!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"My Demon Dust is almost capped out—I can upgrade my gear any minute now!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Xuenian, you have to hear this, I popped off so hard today..."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The group babbled on in an overlapping frenzy before finally noticing Liu Xuenian staring at them with a hollow, mournful gaze, making them feel slightly sheepish.

—"It really is such a shame you can't play,"-one of them actually had the nerve to say.

Indeed! He could certainly feel it.

From their initial *"I'll leave right after clearing the next floor,"* to *"I'm just a sliver away from Level 50, I'll log off as soon as I level up,"* all the way to *"We're almost qualified to raid the Demon Realm Outpost!"*

Clearly, this bunch was having [so much fun they forgot home entirely](# "Literal CN: 乐不思蜀 (lèbùsīshǔ) — indulging in pleasure to the point of forgetting one's home/duties; historical idiom from Liu Shan"); they had zero intention of leaving.

*Is fighting demons really that fun?*

*Once you ascend, you can fight to your heart's content!*

*Assuming you even have the guts to set foot on a battlefield, that is.*

Though with their pathetic combat power, ascending was an absolute impossibility in the first place.

While mocking them relentlessly in his mind, Liu Xuenian inquired with gentle solicitude:

—"Still, battling demons for so many days on end—even if it is not real—doesn't it feel a bit exhausting?"-Liu Xuenian asked softly.

[—"Not at all! Xuenian, you just don't get it—this game is ridiculously fun."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Yeah, what a shame it can't be commercialized and sold like Immortal Chess."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Which means we have to grind our fill while we're still in the Central Domain!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"I already sent a letter home telling my family I'm undergoing intensive cultivation for half a year to a year under the direct mentorship of grandmasters from Eternal Villa and Heavenly Spirit Sect."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Oh, that's a brilliant excuse!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Seeing them erupt into another lively debate, Liu Xuenian cleared his throat:

—"However..."-Liu Xuenian began.

[—"What is it?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Liu Xuenian had intended to utter a few cautionary words, but he acutely detected a flick of impatience in their demeanor.

They were actually showing impatience toward *him*!

When they were nothing more than a few puppets!

Had the sheer addiction of that "game" actually overpowered his psychological suggestion?!

Anxiety gripped Liu Xuenian's heart.

He could not afford to wait any longer.

Waiting like this was a bottomless rabbit hole.

Liu Xuenian produced a fragile yet eerie smile, abruptly speaking up:

—"Yet I was thinking... is this 'game' truly impossible to buy?"-Liu Xuenian asked softly.

The moment a topic of mutual interest was broached, the group's resistance to suggestion plummeted visibly.

[—"How could we buy it? This is an entire secret realm!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

—"Yet wasn't this secret realm retrofitted from an existing foundation?"-Liu Xuenian said.-"While sitting here, I chatted with the disciples of Eternal Villa. I heard this secret realm was engineered by Qingyang Qingyu."

[—"Qingyang Qingyu? Isn't that Young Master Jian's..."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

—"Precisely, Young Master Jian's beloved,"-Liu Xuenian elaborated.-"She is currently a provisional disciple under that Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and that was reportedly how she mastered the secret realm construction system."

[—"Incredible!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Young Master Jian certainly has an eye for talent!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"What an absolute gem of a wife!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The scions marveled in unison, seemingly having completely forgotten that not long ago, they had scoffed dismissively at Jian Nuo's romantic obsession.

[Truly, bought off with laughable ease.]

Sneering coldly in his heart, Liu Xuenian amplified his seductive, coaxing tone:

—"Indeed. All your families and sects possess their own secret realms, do they not? Just imagine: if Miss Qingyang marries Young Master Jian, given your close bond with Young Master Jian, if you were to ask Miss Qingyang to help remodel your secret realms, she could hardly refuse—"-Liu Xuenian whispered.

[—"Brilliant idea!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Their eyes bulged wide.

Liu Xuenian redoubled his suggestion once more:

—"If only Young Master Jian could marry Miss Qingyang that much sooner!"-Liu Xuenian murmured.

[You still remember, don't you? You were planning to help Jian Nuo abduct her for a wedding back in the Northern Domain—even if back then, you just wanted an excuse to ditch the Central Domain.]

[—"Right... We have to make Young Master Jian marry Miss Qingyang sooner."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Several voices echoed blankly in a daze.

This time, the suggestion was undeniably a resounding success.
