# Chapter 56: Going on a Shopping Spree (第56章 逛街)

November 11th. Clear skies.

The Kunpeng swam unhurriedly through the depths of the Abyss Sea.

Recently, a star chart had manifested within its mind; each day, it was required to cruise past every luminous "star" marked upon the celestial map at least once before its daily circuit was deemed complete.

Why must it do this? To the Kunpeng's chaotic, primordial brain, no answer existed.

It knew only that along this cruising route, it could feast upon all manner of gelatinous, succulent delicacies. That was more than enough.

For a physique as colossal as the Kunpeng's, opportunities to satiate its bottomless appetite were vanishingly rare. Fortunately, the beast's physiological functions were extraordinarily sluggish. Ordinarily, when unable to scavenge sufficient food, it would sink to the ocean floor, suppress its metabolic energy demands to the absolute minimum, and slumber peacefully for centuries or millennia. Only when hunger became genuinely unbearable would it unfurl its wings, soar into the high heavens, migrate to a sea domain teeming with prey, and gorge its gullet once more.

Without question, the Central Domain's Abyss Sea, overflowing with swarms of luminescent jellyfish, fulfilled this condition to perfection.

The Kunpeng had no objections to patrolling daily in exchange for food. As for the minuscule creatures boarding and disembarking along the journey, treating it as a free hitchhiking shuttle, the Kunpeng paid them no heed. After all, similar creatures existed in other waters—schools of fish, octopuses, sea monsters, and the like. At any rate, in the colossal Kunpeng's eyes, these minuscule organisms possessed no fundamental difference.

On this day, as the Kunpeng drifted past one of the glowing "stars," creatures once again flew onto its back.

[—"Whoa-ho, so this is what riding a Kunpeng feels like?"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

Shen Qingqian, Sect Leader of the Blood Demon Hall, glanced around in all directions like an excited little girl before slumping her shoulders in mild disappointment:
[—"It's not nearly as fun as I imagined!"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

—"That's because the Kunpeng is simply too colossal, which ironically robs you of the feeling of 'riding' anything,"-Bai Wubin chuckled.-"Walking around here feels virtually identical to ordinary dry land."

He had little leisure to pamper his master, busying himself instead with directing his junior brothers and sisters to stow away their luggage properly.

As the very first sect to plant a coral tree, Blood Demon Hall had nevertheless delayed their departure for the Heavenly Spirit Sect until November. There was no profoundly esoteric rationale behind this; it was purely because when an entire sect mobilized, there were myriad logistical affairs to settle. Preparations were infinitely more cumbersome than those of ordinary itinerant peddlers. Furthermore, arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect under their official sect banner constituted formal diplomatic engagement; casual back-and-forth travel was out of the question. Consequently, once Shen Qingqian arrived, she intended to reside at the Heavenly Spirit Sect straight through to the conclusion of the Winter Solstice Auction. Naturally, nothing could be treated lightly.

Even so, Blood Demon Hall was still among the earliest sects to journey to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Only once December arrived would the peak wave of major sects arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect truly begin. Many sects planned merely to drop in and [punch the clock](# "Literal CN: 打卡 (dǎkǎ) — modern corporate slang for clocking in; doing a perfunctory courtesy visit") at the auction venue during the final three days of the Winter Solstice before departing.

In truth, the Kunpeng's shuttle service had spared numerous sects an immense amount of toil this year.

Otherwise, according to past conventions, depending on their respective strengths, certain minor sects would have had to embark on their journey a full month in advance. Along the perilous route, they might even lose several disciples to deadly mishaps.

Of course, even with the Kunpeng available, Blood Demon Hall dared not lower their guard.

Bai Wubin placated his master while taking inventory of their accompanying disciples and cargo.

For this expedition, they had brought a total of fifty disciples. Among them, twenty-one burly, muscle-bound disciples, along with four junior sisters, served as the entourage to [hold up the sect's face](# "Literal CN: 撑场子 (chēng chǎngzi) — showing up in force to display muscle, prestige, and intimidating presence") by Shen Qingqian's side—the permanent honor guard she brought on every official outing. The remaining twenty-five disciples were assigned to procurement duties, and the vast majority of them were women.

This represented an unprecedented revolution within the Blood Demon Hall.

In previous auctions, they would never bring so many people, and female disciples were kept to even fewer numbers—generally never exceeding seven—specifically to avoid provoking violent incidents.

But this time was different. Leaving aside the fact that the Heavenly Spirit Sect had a titan like Zhou Hui holding the fort, which exponentially elevated overall security, the scout disciples sent ahead had reported back that this bazaar was extraordinarily safe and bustling; bringing a few more female disciples posed no issue whatsoever. Only then had Shen Qingqian exercised her discretion to bring a larger contingent. Otherwise, Blood Demon Hall's internal "Auction Slot Sparring Tournament" would have been dozens of times more cutthroat!

[Even though Master complains that riding the Kunpeng lacks novelty, this is honestly so much easier!]

Bai Wubin, the perennial "tour group leader," thought with heartfelt gratitude.

One had to understand that luxury cloud carriages resembling flying villas were privileges reserved exclusively for sect masters of premier sects or wealthy second-generation scions. Ordinary cultivators traveled by diligently riding their flying swords through wind and rain.

In the past, even when leading merely seven or eight junior brothers and sisters to the auction, only the first two days after stepping out the front gates were somewhat bearable. Everything that followed was practically a survival trial: if they weren't ambushed by demonic beasts, then some village they sought lodging in had suffered demonic corruption. Managing to arrive at their destination with the entire team in one piece was considered a miraculous blessing; where on earth would anyone find the leisure to be picky about transportation?

Yet the Kunpeng was not only rock-steady, but blazingly fast. Rumor had it they would arrive in merely half a day! This was easily the most pleasant journey of Bai Wubin's entire life. If the auction could secure the goods they desperately needed, Bai Wubin would wish with all his heart that the Heavenly Spirit Sect hosted the auction every single year!

Not only did Bai Wubin feel this way, but the other disciples of Blood Demon Hall shared the exact same sentiment.

Having heard tales of past horrors, everyone had mentally prepared themselves for this trip to resemble a bloody trial deep within the Abyss Sea. None had anticipated the journey would be this relaxing, leaving them gasping in awe and praise all the way.

Yet what captivated them most of all were the [holographic projections (advertisements)](# "Literal CN: 幻影 (广告) — magical illusions used as targeted modern commercial ads") displayed outside the pavilions perched atop the Kunpeng's back.

These holographic projections were evidently targeted displays. Based on the differing cultivation realms of the passengers, they showcased everything from the market regulations to the most popular shop offerings in the specific bazaar sector they were heading toward.

Among these, the grand auction that cultivators had originally been obsessing over was by no means the star of the show. Instead, massive poster-style illustrations displayed shrimp and crab casseroles, pastries and tea snacks, elegant garments, beauty and youth-retaining pills, and the like—enthralling the passengers long before their arrival. Meticulous disciples even went so far as to jot down shop addresses in their notebooks, fully intent on tracking them down to investigate.

Even Shen Qingqian, who had initially complained about the Kunpeng lacking amusement, stared fixated at the advertisement billboards for quite a long time.

—"What a pity. The pictures look delightful, but once it enters the mouth, it will almost certainly feel dreadfully boring, just like most foods I once considered delicious,"-Shen Qingqian noticed that the vast majority of those ads originated from the Foundation Establishment Bazaar, Golden Core Bazaar, or at highest the Soul Formation Bazaar, prompting her to let out a rueful sigh.

In truth, among all cultivation paths, Body Cultivators were the least restrictive regarding diet. Shen Qingqian had originally chosen the path of body refinement precisely because she adored fine food. Even to this day, Shen Qingqian had never practiced inedia (bigu); nevertheless, she had lost the pleasure of enjoying culinary delicacies a very long time ago.

After all, for a cultivator, the higher one's realm, the more purely refined energy one required. The physical body grew increasingly "perfect," and sensory perception became exponentially more acute.

Ordinary mortal food, completely devoid of spiritual energy, could not only fail to provide them with adequate energy, but even the microscopic impurities within were easily detected by the palates of high-tier cultivators—turning eating into an utterly tedious and repulsive chore.

This was true not only of food, but also of clothing and other mundane comforts.

—Perhaps this was the Heavenly Dao's way of maintaining cosmological balance.

After all, if high-tier cultivators could still relish the joys of mortal goods, the scale of energy they consumed would be tens of millions of times that of a mortal. Much like a Kunpeng compared to a tiny fish, if cultivators ate with unrestricted appetites, mortals would have zero chance of survival. Thus, when high-tier cultivators reached that ultimate state of "extreme sensory acuity," where only items saturated with dense spiritual qi could satisfy them, it produced the exact same effect as the Kunpeng's "sluggish metabolism": compressing bodily functions and forcing them into a state of low desire.

Certain cultivators had noticed this principle, such as Shen Qingqian, yet were utterly powerless to change it.

[The Untiring Immortal Venerable is truly too much! Knowing full well that these pleasures have long abandoned us, why must he plaster them everywhere just to torment my cravings?!]

Shen Qingqian grumbled in the depths of her heart.

Watching her disciples' blissful expressions, she deliberately rapped the table with her knuckles:
—"Quit standing there drooling like fools. Let us discuss our concrete arrangements once we reach the Heavenly Spirit Sect bazaar."

—"Master, didn't we already discuss this at home dozens of times?"-a disciple asked helplessly, still casting reluctant glances at the billboard. Seeing her master act like this, she knew her master's childish temper was flaring again.

Shen Qingqian was not merely throwing a tantrum; she had legitimate reasons:
—"I feel adjustments may be necessary, because this auction seems to have attracted far more people than I imagined."

[—"Far more than you imagined?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

—"Indeed. I just swept the area with my divine sense. Do you know how many people are currently aboard this Kunpeng's back?"-Shen Qingqian asked, answering her own question:—"A full several tens of thousands!"

[—"Tens of thousands?!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciples")

The disciples exchanged bewildered, disoriented glances.

Due to the presence of demonic beasts, fiends, and celestial calamities, the population of the cultivation world was by no means vast. Even the grand unified mortal empire of the Northern Domain boasted merely four hundred million people.

Calculating by the statistical probability that only one in a hundred mortals possessed a spirit root, four million cultivators would be the ideal theoretical ceiling—which explained why the Northern Domain was the most densely populated cultivation territory, as other domains lacked the massive demographic foundation required to foster such numbers.

In reality, once one subtracted those buried among the masses who were never discovered, those whose spirit roots were too chaotic to cultivate, and those who perished in brutal trials, managing to reach Foundation Establishment and truly embark upon the immortal path yielded at best perhaps one million souls. When divided among thousands of sects, even White Jade Capital, the largest immortal city in existence, maintained a permanent population of merely seventy thousand residents!

And right now, a single trip of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's transit shuttle was ferrying ten thousand people?!

That was equivalent to transplanting one-seventh of White Jade Capital's entire populace in a single migration!

What was more, there was still over a month left before the official grand auction began; they were nowhere near the true demographic peak!

—"Master, could you have made a mistake?"-Bai Wubin asked.

—"No, I verified it. Although there are indeed quite a few mortals aboard, those possessing spiritual energy still number in the tens of thousands,"-Shen Qingqian stated with certainty.

Bai Wubin became even more baffled.

[From Master's words, she hasn't even counted the mortals?!]

[Just what scale of grand congregation is this Tianling Auction? The Untiring Immortal Venerable hasn't gone and invited people from other regional domains, has he?!]

In truth, the crowd from Blood Demon Hall had made a fundamental miscalculation.

While the attendees of the Heavenly Spirit Sect bazaar were indeed several times more numerous than in previous years for various reasons, the passengers aboard the Kunpeng at this moment were by no means embarking on a one-way expedition like them.

Even though the Kunpeng made only one circuit around the Central Domain's Abyss Sea per day—meaning guests visiting the immortals' bazaar today could only return home tomorrow—for low-tier cultivators and mortals, this transit service was astronomically convenient. Furthermore, ticket fares were shockingly dirt-cheap: the very first round trip was completely free of charge, subsequent rides cost a mere ten spirit stones, and transit commuter passes offered a 30% discount throughout the duration of the immortals' bazaar! As a result, countless traveling peddlers and merchants commuted continuously between their hometowns and the immortals' bazaar, generating foot traffic that appeared mind-bogglingly exaggerated to traditional cultivators.

—"With so many people, the scale of this auction is naturally extraordinary. We cannot act rashly according to our original plan,"-Shen Qingqian stated solemnly.-"For instance, the items we prepared for the auction cannot be registered prematurely. We must first investigate what other sects have brought to the table, and only reveal our wares when the timing is optimal."

—"Yes, Master,"-Bai Wubin and the other disciples adopted stern expressions.

—"As for the materials we intend to procure, we must not purchase them immediately as we did in previous years. Inquire thoroughly about prevailing market prices, and [compare goods across three vendors](# "Literal CN: 货比三家 (huò bǐ sān jiā) — traditional Chinese consumer idiom: shop around and compare before buying") before committing,"-Shen Qingqian continued.

—"Understood, Master. We shall conduct rigorous comparisons,"-the procurement disciples replied.

—"Naturally, the most vital objective is to find out why there are so many people, and what on earth drew them here,"-Shen Qingqian mused.-"According to preliminary reports, this bazaar has been stratified by cultivation realm. Although similar setups were attempted in the past, in my experience, the results were consistently abysmal. In high-tier bazaars, goods were too sparse, counterfeits were spotted at a single glance, and there was zero entertainment value; meanwhile, in low-tier bazaars, valuable goods found no buyers. Some overly ambitious sellers assumed high-tier cultivators would never visit low-tier markets, so buyers assumed nothing good existed there, culminating in a lose-lose deadlock. Yet this event has managed to captivate such massive crowds—there must be profound rationale behind it! We must inspect every single bazaar sector with utmost precision."

—"As expected of Master! To glean so many profound insights purely from attendance numbers,"-Bai Wubin praised earnestly.-"I shall immediately reorganize our itinerary and dispatch our junior brothers and sisters to inspect every market."

—"No good,"-Shen Qingqian countered solemnly.-"I had not anticipated this development beforehand. The disciples I brought have relatively modest cultivation bases; furthermore, if so many people scatter too widely, catching the Heavenly Spirit Sect's attention and triggering misunderstandings would be terrible."

—"Then what does Master propose...?"-Bai Wubin asked for instructions.

—"Lead your junior brothers and sisters to proceed as originally planned!"-Shen Qingqian commanded.-"The mission of scouting the bazaars—I shall handle it personally."

As she spoke, she stood up, and her soul [detached from her body](# "Literal CN: 神魂离体 (shénhún lí tǐ) — soul projection / out-of-body spiritual detachment")!

As expected of a Body Integration stage titan, Shen Qingqian's soul was as solid and tangible as a physical entity. Standing beside her mortal frame, she and her body looked like identical twins; were it not for the slightly vacant expression on her soulless flesh, it was virtually impossible to distinguish which was the soul and which was the body.

Witnessing this, the disciples of Blood Demon Hall wore expressions as if struck by lightning:

[Master, you just purely want to ditch us and go goof off on your own, don't you?!]

It wasn't that they lacked trust in their master; it was that Shen Qingqian had never provided a shred of foundation for them to trust her in the first place!

—"Master, but upon arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, diplomatic etiquette dictates that we pay our formal respects to the exalted Immortal Venerable,"-Bai Wubin reminded with a throbbing headache.-"What if he sees right through you?"

—"What does it matter? For us body cultivators, the physical flesh embodies one's entire lifetime of cultivation anyway! The body has arrived, so the person has arrived—tell him not to sweat the minor details!"-Shen Qingqian dismissed nonchalantly.-"Besides, in such a boring... er, solemn diplomatic occasion, there's no need for me to do anything superfluous anyway. Just follow the protocol step by step. Whether there is a soul inside or how much soul is present makes zero difference!"

[Didn't you just say you were worried about causing misunderstandings?!]

[What happened to acting with utmost caution?!]

***

Unfortunately, no matter how outrageous a master might be, disciples in most sects were utterly powerless to resist.

Blood Demon Hall was no exception.

In the end, Bai Wubin dutifully led his junior brothers and sisters through the sect-dedicated ferry dock to pay formal respects to the Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, while Shen Qingqian took two female disciples and departed ahead of time for the bazaar.

—"Master, where shall we begin?"-asked Miao Dong, one of the disciples accompanying Shen Qingqian.

—"Since both of you are Golden Cores, we might as well start with the Golden Core Bazaar,"-Shen Qingqian replied casually.

Utilizing unknown arts, she suppressed her cultivation base down to the Golden Core stage, whereupon their passenger bubble wobbled leisurely toward the Golden Core Bazaar.

—In truth, suppressing her cultivation was completely unnecessary. Sorting visitors into markets matching their realm was merely the default setting. If one wished, one could select any destination at the ferry dock; whether a low-realm cultivator wished to visit a high-realm bazaar, or a high-realm cultivator wished to explore a low-realm bazaar, it made no difference. After all, before Zhou Hui—a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse—everyone was practically identical, and all were required to obediently comply with market regulations.

Still, Shen Qingqian's decision was hardly mistaken.

Blended in with average cultivation, she was spared unnecessary scrutiny. At the entrance of the Golden Core Bazaar, Shen Qingqian spent merely five minutes completing the tourist registration formalities.

—"It turns out this registration is far more lenient than the rumors claimed. There wasn't even an identity background check; it felt like one could write whatever nonsense one pleased!"-remarked Fu Ning, the other disciple accompanying Shen Qingqian.

—"The significance lies not in the registered details, but in the act of registration itself,"-Shen Qingqian lectured her disciples.-"Regardless of whether you lied, the instant you completed registration, you were officially logged on that Immortal Venerable's ledger. Henceforth, the moment any of you commit a violation, the entire cause and effect can be traced back in a flash."

—"That terrifying?!"-Fu Ning gasped in fright.

Indeed, that terrifying.

Not only could he trace the origins, but for those with lower cultivation, the Untiring Immortal Venerable could even exert total dominion over everything about them through that single registration step!

Shen Qingqian chose not to voice this to avoid scaring her disciples.

She felt somewhat perplexed. Such matters were hardly secrets in the cultivation world; any cultivator possessing an ounce of vigilance would actively avoid such procedures. Consequently, in the cultivation world, whether in small-scale bazaars or major auctions, guest information was guarded with paranoid secrecy. Participants in such gatherings concealed their identities to the extreme; most wished they could purchase a black cloak to shroud themselves from head to toe, terrified that buying or selling rare treasures would invite murder and highway robbery.

For the Untiring Immortal Venerable to introduce such a counter-intuitive measure that seemingly drove customers away—was it truly fine?

***

In truth, Tao Zhu had asked Zhou Hui a similar question.

—"I know you want to guard against the Palace of Carefree Wandering, but isn't this rule excessively unreasonable?"-Tao Zhu had argued.-"With mandatory registration as a prerequisite, half the merchants won't dare bring out their genuinely prized treasures."

—"For the inaugural auction, it makes no difference if they hold back,"-Zhou Hui had replied.-"At the end of the day, isn't the current state of affairs caused precisely because previous organizers failed to maintain fairness and impartiality, unable to even guarantee sellers' safety, plunging everyone into paranoia and total lawlessness? Allowing this to persist actively stifles commercial development. A new order must be forged, acclimating people to a safe trading environment and fostering healthy supply and demand. Only then will more and superior goods be produced."

—"A splendid ideal, but you will bleed money—wait a moment! If you bleed money in this auction, you are bleeding MY ancestral fortune!"-Tao Zhu had sputtered, instantly puffing up in fury.

Zhou Hui had shoved his rotund belly aside with mild disgust:
—"Hardly. Hidden trading has its ways to play, and open trading has its ways to play. Besides, having lived all the way to the Mahayana stage, do you honestly find sneaking around in cloaks fun?"

***

Shen Qingqian was naturally unaware of Zhou Hui's grand design.

However, as a Body Cultivator who abhorred deep contemplation, Shen Qingqian pondered the matter for merely a fleeting second before her attention was instantly derailed.

—"Ah, it's *A Cup of Sorrow*—I heard this is the Heavenly Spirit Sect's signature brand—shall we begin here?"-Shen Qingqian said, looking at the signboard across the street.

The two disciples naturally raised no objections; one immediately dashed over to queue up and returned with milk tea.

—"The line here is even longer than at the registration counter,"-Shen Qingqian smiled, taking the cup.-"Don't tell me there's one at every single bazaar entrance? The Heavenly Spirit Sect is truly stingy, reserving all the prime real estate for their own flagship product."

—"But it's so pretty!"-Miao Dong beamed, cradling her Strawberry Cheezo with starry eyes.

—"And so delicious!"-Fu Ning took a sip of her Caramel Milk Tea, wearing an expression of pure bliss.

Evidently, *A Cup of Sorrow* had thoroughly conquered both female cultivators right at the opening bell.

Shen Qingqian shrugged, taking a sip without much expectation.

Oh my? It was actually quite delicious!

Hers was the most basic Pearl Milk Tea. Although brewed from premium spiritual milk and tea, to a Body Integration cultivator, the spiritual energy contained within—calibrated only for the Golden Core realm—could not conceal the raw ingredients' flaws. It wasn't that the ingredients were poor, but rather that spiritual tea that had grown for "only" fifty years was considered fine tea for a Golden Core cultivator, whereas a Body Integration powerhouse could easily discern the tea leaves' microscopic impurities. The same held true for the spiritual milk.

However, the creator of this beverage had evidently anticipated this exact issue, skillfully blending the components so the impurities were not jarringly noticeable. Coupled with the masking effect of sugar, it spared Shen Qingqian the distaste she experienced with other foods.

Naturally, to genuine Golden Core cultivators, it was nothing short of an otherworldly delicacy.

[—"Master, I want another cup!"](# "Speaker: Miao Dong")

[—"Me too!"](# "Speaker: Fu Ning")

—"We've only just begun shopping! Don't drink too much,"-Shen Qingqian declared with "dignified restraint":—"Just buy one cup per flavor."

Thus, fifteen minutes later, numerous Golden Core cultivators watched three female cultivators stroll past clutching dozens of cups of milk tea.

The female cultivators of Blood Demon Hall were thoroughly accustomed to such stares:

[What are you staring at? Haven't you ever seen body cultivators before?!]

[Body cultivators simply eat a lot, alright?!]

Consequently, wherever body cultivators went, they easily became the center of public attention. If it were burly male cultivators, bystanders would simply step aside out of caution upon recognizing their brute strength. But for female cultivators traveling abroad, it caused endless inconvenience, which was why Blood Demon Hall's female disciples avoided gathering in packs.

Yet to the girls' immense relief, the frequent harassment and provocations they typically encountered were entirely absent here.

Although several people cast curious glances, they quickly moved on; nobody poked their nose into others' business. On the contrary, watching the body cultivators drink tea with such blissful relish, the queue outside *A Cup of Sorrow* seemed to grow noticeably longer.

Moreover, unlike typical bazaars dominated overwhelmingly by male cultivators, Shen Qingqian noticed that—perhaps due to the presence of *A Cup of Sorrow*—the proportion of female cultivators in this market was significantly higher than in other gatherings, completely devoid of the usual sight where an entire street was wall-to-wall men.

This was an encouraging phenomenon.

While theoretically speaking, once one embarked upon the immortal path, male and female cultivators were largely equal, in practice disparities persisted—especially in lawless, unregulated bazaars.

First, during the initial stages of cultivation, the physical strength gap between male and female cultivators had not yet been eliminated, leaving men with natural advantages. Female cultivators were prone to premature demise from various dangers, leading them to instinctively avoid public gatherings like bazaars and auctions. This caused the proportion of men in public spaces to appear even higher than it actually was, further exacerbating female cultivators' sense of insecurity.

Even upon reaching the Golden Core stage, where female cultivators possessed formidable personal strength and rarely needed to fear for their safety, the trauma accumulated during their weaker days lingered. They remained accustomed to such unspoken rules, striving to avoid drawing attention to themselves.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's bazaar suffered no such ailments. The increased presence of peers allowed female cultivators to let down their guard; they no longer hurried along frantically searching only for their target goods, but possessed the leisure to slow their pace, browsing as they strolled.

Barely a dozen paces forward, their senses were captured by a rich, sweet aroma.

[—"It smells like... sweet pastries?"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"It smells so fragrant and delicious; is it this shop?"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"Whoa, what a gorgeous 'tower'!"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"Wait, the sweet aroma is wafting from that thing; could it be food?"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"There's a label next to it, called... 'Cream Fresh Flower Cake Tower'? Since it has both cream and cake in the name, it must be food, right?"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"I saw the hologram aboard the Kunpeng; I thought it was a decorative ornament, but it turns out to be edible!"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

Aside from Shen Qingqian's party, several other female cultivators stood outside the transparent glazed window, chattering curiously as they gazed at the exquisite cake tower.

Noticing the girls outside, the shop clerk called out with warm hospitality:
—"Since this is a brand-new pastry category, our shop is currently running a promotion! If you ladies are interested, please step inside for a free tasting!"

Could any girl refuse food that was both sweetly fragrant and breathtakingly gorgeous?

Even if one could resist that, nobody could resist the word "free."

Instantly, the female cultivators outside filed in as if hypnotized. When they emerged, each held a pastry box in her hands. Blood Demon Hall's cultivators carried no boxes this time—instead, they had placed an order for a giant twelve-tier cake, instructing the shop to deliver it straight to their sect's designated inn.

Next, they happily moved on to the next shop.

Then another shop.

And the next shop after that.

...

By the time the group arrived before the entrance of the Golden Core Auction House, dusk had already settled over the horizon.

At this point, they hadn't even covered half of the Golden Core Bazaar.

It was only upon spotting the auction house signboard that master and disciples remembered the actual purpose of their excursion.

—"The Heavenly Spirit Bazaar... [terrifying indeed](# "Literal CN: 恐怖如斯 (kǒngbù rú sī) — iconic xianxia internet meme: 'terrifying to such an extent'")."-Shen Qingqian murmured.

In her hands, she still held [takoyaki](# "Literal CN: 章鱼小丸子 (zhāngyú xiǎowánzi) — Japanese octopus balls") and a massive handful of lamb skewers and squid skewers, while carrying boxes of fried chicken cutlets, beefsteaks, and crispy pork belly, three cups of fruit tea, and an assortment of large and small bags, looking like she had reaped a bumper harvest.

—"Shall we call it a day here?"-Shen Qingqian suggested, sounding uncharacteristically guilty.

Behind her, Fu Ning secretly peeked into her storage pouch. They had clearly set out with a bulging pouch, yet now it was more than half empty! And they hadn't bought a single essential item—it had all been blown on eating, drinking, and snacking!

Returning like this, Master might not care, but she and Miao Dong would definitely be reprimanded by Senior Brother Bai!

—"M-Master, why don't we at least look through the auction catalog before leaving?"-Fu Ning proposed the suggestion she would regret for the rest of her life:—"Otherwise, if we return like this and everyone asks about the auction situation, we won't have any explanation!"

—"Yes, exactly! Let's take a look inside before heading back!"-Miao Dong thought of the exact same dilemma.

—"True enough. Little Bin-er can be quite a nag,"-Shen Qingqian furrowed her brows.

The three stepped inside the auction house.

An hour later, Shen Qingqian emerged with her two dazed, soul-shattered disciples.

—"We... should head back,"-Shen Qingqian muttered.

—"B-But Master, that item you won at auction... Senior Brother Bai is going to be furious, isn't he?!"-Miao Dong whimpered, half in tears.

—"What does he have to be angry about?"-Shen Qingqian closed her eyes serenely.-"Your master merely bought a dress, that's all."

—"A dress with ZERO added skills!"-Miao Dong pointed out.

[—"BUT IT'S PRETTY!"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

—"A dress crafted from the lowest-grade mortal cotton, devoid of even a trace of spiritual energy!"-Fu Ning felt her head spinning.

[—"BUT IT'S PRETTY!"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

[—"It cost ONE THOUSAND HIGH-GRADE SPIRIT STONES! Not only did it drain all our existing spirit stones, but you bought it on credit under the Blood Demon Hall's name!"](# "Speaker: Fu Ning")

[—"BUT IT'S PRETTY!"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

As Shen Qingqian spoke, she shook out the dress they had won at auction right there on the street.

It was indeed a breathtakingly gorgeous gown.

Crucially, nobody in the cultivation world had ever seen this design before.

Yet that voluminous skirt, embellished with ruffled [lace](# "Literal CN: 蕾丝 (lěisī) — modern phonetic loanword for lace") borders and shimmering gemstones, instantly captivated the hearts of every female cultivator across the entire street:
[—"Could any of you refuse this?"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

No. Truly, nobody could refuse.

Miao Dong and Fu Ning were on the verge of tears:
[—"B-But it's too expensive! And completely useless!"](# "Speaker: Miao Dong & Fu Ning")

[...In truth, the best part about it is precisely that it is expensive and completely useless!]

[What kind of flex is buying clothes for mere utility?! Donning this gown, she, Shen Qingqian, would be the undisputed most formidable female cultivator at this auction—her sheer might transcended any need for gear buffs!]

Of course, she couldn't say this aloud to her disciples.

[Not just these two; if I break the news to the rest of the disciples back at the inn, they will probably lose their minds as well, won't they?]

Shen Qingqian thought with a pang of guilt.

At that moment, Shen Qingqian suddenly noticed the building directly opposite the auction house.

—"Alright, stop crying. For this dress's expense, I'll pay out of my own pocket, okay?"-Shen Qingqian said.

[—"Give me a break, Master, you've eaten away all your money!"](# "Speaker: Miao Dong")

[—"Yeah, Master, where on earth would you have any private stash?"](# "Speaker: Fu Ning")

At this point, Miao Dong and Fu Ning couldn't even care about respecting their master, unceremoniously laying bare her financial ruin.

Shen Qingqian declared:
—"Even if I don't have savings, I can sell myself!"

[—"Sell yourself?!"](# "Speaker: Miao Dong & Fu Ning")

The two disciples gasped in sheer horror. Following Shen Qingqian's gaze, they discovered a peculiar signboard hanging across from the auction house:

[Talent Exchange Center](# "Literal CN: 人才交流中心 (réncái jiāoliú zhōngxīn) — modern corporate/government labor exchange and job agency")

And in the most prominent display area was a recruitment notice:

> **Urgent Recruitment: Body Cultivators**
>
> **Requirements:** Colossal strength, efficient worker, can follow instructions
>
> **Job Content:** [Moving Bricks](# "Literal CN: 搬砖 (bānzhuān) — classic Chinese internet slang for grueling manual labor / corporate wage-slaving")
>
> **Compensation:** Piece-rate, more work more pay, daily payout!
