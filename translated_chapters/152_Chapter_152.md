# Chapter 152: A Foolish Conspiracy (第152章 愚蠢的阴谋)

How exactly does one carry out a bride kidnapping?

First and foremost, one must begin by finding the target in question.

—"Does anyone here actually know where the Qingyang clan's young lady is?"-someone finally asked, a full half-shichen after they had decisively resolved to "kidnap the bride."

The remaining young masters looked at one another in blank dismay.

—"I don't know. What about you lot?"-one asked.

[—"Jian Nuo treats his sweetheart like some precious treasure. Back in Sky City, he wished he could lock her away in a secret vault where no one could lay eyes on her! How could I possibly know where she is?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Then why on earth did you lead us in this direction?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Because there's a road in this direction!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"There was a path in the other direction just now too! Why didn't you head that way?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Stop bickering! What are we supposed to do now?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

Reaching this point in their deliberation, the group fell dead silent, staring at each other in blank bewilderment.

Eavesdropping on this profoundly moronic exchange, Liu Xuenian nearly threw his current physical vessel to the floor in sheer fury.

[So after running around like headless chickens for half the day, you don't even know where to look for her?! Why in the world did I end up choosing such an unfathomably idiotic pack of puppets?! I swear, even possessing these fools makes me feel like my own brain is rotting!]

The most infuriating part was that not only were these fools hopelessly dense, they were also prone to giving up at the drop of a hat.

Sure enough, someone immediately chimed in:

—"How about we just forget it..."-one scion muttered.

Liu Xuenian cut in without hesitation:

—"*Cough, cough*... I believe I know,"-Liu Xuenian said.

The voice speaking on Liu Xuenian's behalf came from a paper talisman doll, which had originally been dispatched to keep watch over this bunch.

Naturally, Liu Xuenian had not been foolish enough to label it "surveillance." Instead, he claimed it was an expression of pure fraternal devotion—that although his delicate constitution rendered him unsuited to exposure to demonic qi, he still yearned to stand alongside his companions. The gesture had moved the young scions to tears.

As events had demonstrated, this had been a remarkably prudent decision.

Liu Xuenian had always known these fellows were a gathering of absolute simpletons, but he had never imagined they could be brainless to such an astronomical extent. Had he not shadowed them, this entire escapade would likely have dissolved into thin air before anything could even begin.

He had originally hoped these puppets might exhibit a sliver of autonomous agency. Now it was painfully clear he would have to switch to direct manual control.

—"Although I haven't stepped outside once these past few days, I have heard plenty of fascinating tidbits from the Qingyang clan's immortal attendants,"-Liu Xuenian provided by way of a casual excuse, feeding them intelligence.-"For instance, the fact that Qingyang Qingyu is engineering a secret realm system was shared with me by them."

This, of course, was an outright lie.

While Qingyang Qingyu had never harbored any desire to conceal her movements, neither she nor Qu Qianxing would ever permit loose-lipped servants anywhere near their guests—even a guest who appeared frail, ailing, and utterly harmless.

Liu Xuenian naturally possessed his own esoteric avenues, none of which were fit for outside ears.

He continued through the doll:

—"Precisely because she must calibrate the secret realm system, Qingyang Qingyu spends most of her time inside this very secret realm,"-Liu Xuenian explained.-"Do you see that position situated at the dead center of the control tower? That level where every single pathway connects? That is the central control chamber."

—"Ohhh! So that's how it is!"-the crowd exclaimed in sudden realization.

The fellow who had been leading the way at random even puffed up with smug pride:

—"See? Looks like I didn't lead us astray after all!"-he boasted.-"Overall, we were heading in that general direction anyway!"

Nonsense! He had just explicitly stated that *every single pathway* connected to the control tower! As long as they didn't exit the tower, whichever direction they walked would ultimately funnel them toward the central control hub! In other words, if a demon were seeking an escape route to the outside world, no matter how it navigated, the corridors would inevitably channel it straight to the central control tower—which was precisely why the layout had been designed that way in the first place! Yet the reason this pack of little imbeciles was wandering through the tower like lost demon beasts was not that they couldn't locate the exit, but rather that they simply couldn't bear to leave!

A wave of suffocating mental exhaustion washed over Liu Xuenian.

Yet he could not rest just yet; his "manually steered puppets" were glaringly, painfully defective.

Having learned of Qingyang Qingyu's location, they actually began swaggering brazenly and openly straight toward the central control tower!

Liu Xuenian frantically intervened:

—"Wait... wait, wait, wait! You're just marching straight over?!"-Liu Xuenian cried in alarm.-"What if you're discovered?!"

[—"It shouldn't matter, right? We're visiting guests! If we say we're just touring the central control chamber, would they actually bar us at the door?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Besides, the whole point of 'kidnapping the bride' is the 'kidnap' part! We have to carry the Qingyang clan's young lady away fair and square, in broad daylight! That way Jian Nuo will know exactly what went down, and there won't be any misunderstandings!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Exactly, exactly! If we stealthily sneak her out under the cover of darkness, what if Jian Nuo suspects something illicit went on between us and Fairy Qingyang? We'd never be able to wash ourselves clean!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Ah, now that you mention it, we should have just had a servant lead us over from the very beginning."](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

...

The group chattered among themselves.

Bizarrely enough, their brain-dead notions actually possessed their own internally consistent logic.

Liu Xuenian had no idea how to direct them anymore:

—"Do you take Fairy Qingyang for a corpse?!"-Liu Xuenian snapped in disbelief.-"As if she'll just stand there and let you snatch her at will?!"

—"Heheh, Xuenian, aren't you underestimating us a bit too much?"-someone chuckled.-"We are Nascent Soul cultivators, after all! Snatching a Golden Core is more than within our capabilities."

—"Except Qingyang Qingyu is unlikely to be alone!"-Liu Xuenian argued.-"The Qingyang clan is a bona fide ancient cultivation powerhouse! Not only do they boast several Void Refinement and Body Integration titans, but they even have a Mahayana patriarch presiding over the estate! Do you truly believe they would sit idly by and watch you carry off a descendant of their direct bloodline?!"

—"Aren't those grand titans usually in secluded cultivation anyway?"-someone countered.-"Besides, precisely because we are openly 'inviting' Qingyang Qingyu to visit the Northern Domain for some fun, they'll surely treat it as nothing more than youngsters roughhousing!"

They weren't entirely devoid of brains after all.

As [cultivation second-generation scions](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū-èrdài) — the privileged, sheltered offspring of elite immortal families"), they understood intimately how to lull their elders into dropping their guard.

For instance, in this matter of "kidnapping the bride": if Qingyang Qingyu were to vanish silently without a trace, Villa Master Qingyang would certainly not leave the matter unattended. But if a pack of high-born scions accompanied Qingyang Qingyu out on an excursion, and the clan was temporarily unsure whether it was Qingyang Qingyu's own voluntary decision, at most it would be her peers and fellow disciples—along with Jian Nuo—racing out in pursuit.

—As for whether they could ultimately [cook the raw rice into porridge](# "Literal CN: 生米煮成熟饭 (shēngmǐ zhǔchéng shúfàn) — make it an irreversible fait accompli"), that was entirely Jian Nuo's problem.

They patted their storage pouches with supreme confidence:

—"As for the rest of the Qingyang clan, we brought quite an arsenal of Dharma treasures on this outing. Whisking one person away will be child's play,"-they declared.

—"And what about Young Master Jian?"-Liu Xuenian pressed.-"If Young Master Jian is standing right by Fairy Qingyang's side, how do you intend to make your move? Furthermore, if Young Master Jian sees you pestering Fairy Qingyang, he might very well find an excuse to kick you all out!"

—"How could that be? What kind of bond do we share with Jian Nuo..."-the speaker began to brag, only to choke on his own words.

Because every single one of them knew in their hearts: Jian Nuo was completely capable of doing exactly that! That shameless fellow who practiced [bros before hoes in reverse](# "Literal CN: 重色轻友 (zhòngsè-qīngyǒu) — prioritizing romance over friendship / abandoning one's buddies for a lover")!

However, with their proposals shot down one after another by Liu Xuenian, someone finally grew irritable:

—"Xuenian, this won't do, that won't work! Did you get cold feet at the last second or something?"-one scion grumbled.

[If you weren't all so staggeringly brain-dead, would I ever have needed to intervene in person?!]

Liu Xuenian felt as though his very intestines were twisting in agony. Yet he still had to coax this pack of idiots along:

—"Actually, I think your plan is excellent; it's simply a bit too reckless,"-Liu Xuenian appeased them gently.-"After all, if we barge straight into the center of the control tower without knowing what the situation is around Fairy Qingyang, the variables are far too great. Not to mention, we likely only have a single opportunity. Why not devise a way to sneak in covertly, verify that Fairy Qingyang is unaccompanied, and then pay a visit under that pretext to whisk her away?"

[—"Sneak in covertly?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

—"Correct. I have made inquiries,"-Liu Xuenian said, laying out a meticulous plan down to the finest detail for this bunch of simpletons.-"Maintaining such a colossal secret realm is no small feat. Consequently, numerous disciples from both Eternal Villa and the Heavenly Spirit Sect have been temporarily mobilized here. Fairy Qingyang will be selecting and dispatching suitable manpower from among them to assist in managing the realm."

Hearing Liu Xuenian's detailed blueprint, someone wore a peculiar expression.

Why was Liu Xuenian informed in such excruciating detail, almost as though he had orchestrated this from the very start?

Besides, the little young master of the Liu clan had always been frail, delicate, and endearing. Was he truly the type to scheme something like this?

Yet the suspicion flickered past in an instant and vanished.

Seizing the opportune moment, Liu Xuenian deftly hooked into their own underlying desires, dialing up his [subconscious suggestion](# "Literal CN: 暗示 (ànshì) — psychological suggestion / subtle mental manipulation"):

—"Don't you all wish to see firsthand how such an entertaining secret realm is crafted in the first place?"-Liu Xuenian asked.

[—"That's so true!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Brilliant idea!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"If we infiltrate the team, can we actually watch how a secret realm comes to life?"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

The scions chimed in with jubilant enthusiasm, finally mustering some genuine drive.

Only then did their deliberations begin to resemble a functional operation. They began hashing out how to infiltrate the ranks, who would go in first, how to confirm Qingyang Qingyu's status, how to call in backup for the getaway, and which flying artifacts would offer the swiftest escape velocity.

Listening to them organize their thoughts with some semblance of coherence, Liu Xuenian finally breathed an inward sigh of relief.

No, in truth, his heart was still far from at ease. However, his frailty was not entirely a fabrication; situated here at the boundary of the Demon Realm, the drain on his power was undeniably real.

The paper doll of Liu Xuenian slowly bowed its head:

—"My spiritual energy is nearly depleted. Let me rest a while, and I shall come check on you again later,"-Liu Xuenian said.

To this, nobody gave it a second thought. If anything, this was simply par for the course for Liu Xuenian:

[—"Get some good rest, Xuenian!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"Yeah, there's no need to push yourself to come over!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

[—"You'd be better off preparing for our departure! Once we secure Fairy Qingyang, we'll definitely be bolting at breakneck speed. The methods used to whisk you away might be a little rough, so you must get your body into fighting shape!"](# "Speaker: Sky City Scion")

They urged him with earnest solicitude.

---

—"So! Why on earth are you still sitting here?!"

That very evening, Liu Xuenian snapped in sheer exasperation.

His sudden outburst startled Xiao Geng so violently that the young man nearly tumbled out of his chair.

By the way, "Xiao Geng" was an alias.

These cultivation second-generation scions naturally had elegant formal names, and had even coined dazzlingly grandiose Daoist sobriquets for themselves which they addressed each other by daily—though scarcely anyone outside their circle recognized them.

This time, however, following Liu Xuenian's instructions to infiltrate the secret realm's management team, they naturally could not blithely announce their real names as they had originally planned. Instead, they had to disguise themselves as disciples of Eternal Villa or the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Thus, they adopted pseudonyms derived from the [Heavenly Stems](# "Literal CN: 天干 (tiāngān) — the ten Heavenly Stems used for cyclical counting: Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui").

—What these naive children failed to realize was that the Heavenly Spirit Sect meticulously archived and cataloged every single disciple's identity. With a simple query, Qingyang Qingyu could pull up their historical examination records in seconds. Furthermore, Eternal Villa had recently adopted the Heavenly Spirit Sect's model, conducting their own comprehensive census and registration.

Consequently, whether these silly brats used pseudonyms or not, they stood out as blatantly as [huskies blending in among a pack of wolves](# "Literal CN: 混在狼群中的哈士奇 — internet meme describing an oblivious, goofy dog standing out glaringly among fierce wolves").

Of course, this oversight was not entirely their fault, for Liu Xuenian had been equally ignorant.

After all, this was simply not how things were done in the orthodox cultivation world.

While cultivation sects maintained yellow registries of their disciples, they never tracked every individual with such granular precision. Smaller sects were manageable since everyone knew each other; in larger sects, it was not the least bit unusual for a few outsiders to slip in when picking up mission assignments.

Yet for a certain "reason," they had not been rooted out.

In fact, they had even been permitted to take part in the recruitment screening.

Yet Xiao Geng held little affection for the contents of said screening:

—"Of course I wanted to get it over with quickly!"-Xiao Geng complained, brimming with resentment.-"But didn't you know there are examinations?!"

[—"Examinations?"](# "Speaker: Liu Xuenian")

—"Yeah! You're the one who told us to infiltrate the place!"-Xiao Geng snapped bitterly.-"I sat through a full dozen examinations today!"

To make matters worse, Liu Xuenian had been lounging as an esteemed guest inside a safe room stocked with tea and pastries, whereas Xiao Geng had endured an entire day of relentless psychological beatdowns under standardized testing. His resentment only deepened.

Seeing his venomous state, Liu Xuenian felt a twinge of apprehension, alongside a trace of genuine curiosity:

—"Why would there be so many examinations?"-Liu Xuenian asked.

—"Because the job responsibilities inside the secret realm are divided up!"-Xiao Geng explained.-"I hear they have numerical balance design, level design, combat systems design, narrative design, art design, and all sorts of different tracks! Every single category has its own specialized testing criteria!"

And so, this gang of cultivation scions had charged in recklessly, and while they hadn't been kicked out, they were subjected to a battery of technical interviews.

Yet this had not caused the young masters to [beat the retreat drum](# "Literal CN: 打退堂鼓 (dǎ tuìtánggǔ) — back out / get cold feet").

After all, in the eyes of laymen, making games looked immensely entertaining—especially since they had just tasted the intoxicating fun of playing the game firsthand.

Hearing that numerical balance could dictate the destructive parameters of Dharma artifacts, level design could map out the architectural layout of each floor, and combat designers could forge customized combat skill rotations, everyone's enthusiasm had instantly caught fire.

Originally, they had planned to send in just one scout to assess the terrain. But with their [fingers itching to show off their talents](# "Literal CN: 一时技痒 (yīshí jìyǎng) — itching to show off one's skills"), every last one of them had scrambled to take the exams!

—"Xiao Jia's arithmetic is decent, so he became a numerical balancer. Xiao Yi's poetry and literature are sharp, so he became a narrative writer. Xiao Bing..."-Xiao Geng counted off one by one.-"Once they went in, they never came back out! They must be having way too much fun playing around! They completely forgot about our original plan!"

And so, unwilling to be left behind, he had stubbornly sat for every single test in a desperate bid to get hired!

Xiao Geng had been brooding outside the testing grounds in bitter resentment. Had Liu Xuenian's paper doll not suddenly spoken up, he might have forgotten why he had come here in the first place.

Naturally, he was not stupid enough to admit that to Liu Xuenian's face.

Liu Xuenian failed to realize that he had been [stood up](# "Literal CN: 放鸽子 (fàng gēzi) — stood up / flaked on") for a second time. Hearing Xiao Geng's account, he merely found the situation rather suspicious:

—"Are you certain they are merely playing?"-Liu Xuenian mused.-"Could something have gone wrong?"

—"What could possibly go wrong? This is the Qingyang clan, and we are Jian Nuo's guests!"-Xiao Geng scoffed.-"Even if we were exposed, the worst they could do is toss us out!"

There was logic in that.

After all, they were high-born young masters of Sky City. Even if they succeeded in abducting Qingyang Qingyu, at worst they would face a few centuries of grounding as punishment. Unless the Qingyang clan genuinely wished to ignite a second Human-Demon War and make themselves the target of universal condemnation from day one, they would never execute them.

[Then why hasn't a single person come out to deliver word?]

Liu Xuenian pondered.

Yet to Xiao Geng, this question wasn't even worth pondering. He ground his teeth in pure malice:

—"Ditching me to go have fun! They'd be better off getting kicked out!"-Xiao Geng hissed.

—"No, designing a secret realm is hardly child's play,"-Liu Xuenian murmured.

He couldn't help suspecting whether those little imbeciles had caught a glimpse of the truth regarding the Demon Realm and had been imprisoned by the Qingyang clan. Still, such a development would actually work in Liu Xuenian's favor: so long as he found a way to release them, he could solidify the narrative that the Qingyang clan and the Heavenly Spirit Sect were colluding with the demons.

Liu Xuenian offered:

—"What is your next test? Let me assist you,"-Liu Xuenian said.

—"Xuenian, you're the best!"-Xiao Geng exclaimed, deeply touched. Having been battered all day long, he had completely lost faith in himself and was practically begging for outside help.-"The next test is—"

Right then, approaching footsteps echoed down the hall. Clearly, someone was coming.

Xiao Geng snapped his mouth shut, and Liu Xuenian's paper doll hurriedly vanished into Xiao Geng's sleeve.

From outside, Liu Xuenian heard a bright, cheerful voice announce:

—"Are you Xiao Geng? We heard that you took every single examination today! Commendable courage! Our Testing Department happens to be in dire need of talent just like you!"

---

This was precisely why, fifteen minutes later, Xiao Geng was clad in demonic beast monster gear, standing dumbly in a dark stone corridor and frantically dodging incoming barrage attacks in utter humiliation.

And tucked securely inside his sleeve was Liu Xuenian.
