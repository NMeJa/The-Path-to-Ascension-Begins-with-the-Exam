# Chapter 52: Exploiting Loopholes (第52章 钻空子)

—"[A heinous waste of heaven's gifts](# "Literal CN: 暴殄天物 (bàotiǎn-tiānwù) — squandering heaven's precious bounty; egregious waste of rare resources")! A heinous waste of heaven's gifts!"-Tao Zhu yelled in furious indignation.

His belly was currently swollen like a massive drum, producing rhythmic *thump-thump* booming sounds in tandem with the cadence of his speech—expanding and contracting in pulses, looking as though it could burst open at any moment, making onlookers thoroughly unsettled.

Boss Tao, however, couldn't care less about his own image (or belly), continuing to bellow:

—"It's merely an ordinary auction! Yet you actually mobilized an entity like a Kunpeng! This is an absolute, heinous waste of heaven's gifts!"

Zhou Hui's head throbbed from his (belly's) racket, and he could only offer a wry smile:

—"It isn't really that wasteful, is it? A Kunpeng isn't truly some spiritual beast—it's merely grown to an extraordinary size. My little disciple caught one from who-knows-where to serve as emergency rations. I simply borrowed it for temporary use."

—"Emergency rations? You feed your kids stuff like this?!"-Tao Zhu exclaimed.

For a fleeting second, Tao Zhu wondered just how on earth Zhou Hui reared his disciples. But in the end, he didn't care all that much about that question, quickly shaking his head:

—"Wrong! When I said 'a heinous waste of heaven's gifts,' I wasn't talking about the Kunpeng—I was talking about you actually controlling a Kunpeng to use as a transit vehicle! Do you no longer wish to ascend?!"

[I don't wish to at all!]

Zhou Hui thought.

Yet someone present was far more sensitive to the word "ascension" than Zhou Hui himself.

Qu Qianxing had originally been in charge of receiving the guests. Who could have foreseen that an old friend of Master's would be mixed among the entourage—well, presumably an 'old friend'? Looking at Tao Zhu's bizarre belly, Qu Qianxing was actually somewhat reluctant to acknowledge him as Master's friend—and what was more, this fellow had the audacity to openly complain about Master's conduct.

Qu Qianxing had naturally sided with Zhou Hui from the outset, thinking, *Whatever my Master chooses to carry people with, what business is it of yours?* But the moment he heard "unable to ascend," he could no longer sit still.

—"This... Senior,"-Qu Qianxing inquired:—"what was that about being unable to ascend? Does steering a Kunpeng lead to an inability to ascend?"

Tao Zhu had been thoroughly irritated by Zhou Hui's impervious, lukewarm attitude. Seeing the man's disciple finally grow anxious over his words, he felt a faint twinge of delight.

—"Steering a Kunpeng naturally won't prevent ascension on its own, but the excessive spiritual energy consumed in steering a Kunpeng certainly will!"-Tao Zhu huffed in explanation:—"Your master just said it himself, didn't he? A Kunpeng cannot be considered a spiritual creature—it has merely grown exceptionally mammoth, unable to even produce spiritual sapience. Controlling a creature devoid of sapience and lacking spiritual qi consumes an astronomical amount of spiritual power. To be frank, only your master could pull it off. Legend has it that in ancient times, a True Immortal wished to raise a Kunpeng as a pet, yet after controlling it for a mere seven days and seven nights, he was forced to abandon the attempt due to spiritual exhaustion."

Hearing him speak thus, Qu Qianxing took it as praise for his Master, unable to resist curling the corners of his lips into an exquisite smile.

Yet accompanying the thunderous drumming of his belly, Tao Zhu immediately bellowed:

—"Therefore! That makes it all the more wasteful! Squandering such vast reserves of spiritual power in one go on an untamable, idiotic beast! What talk of ascension remains?!"

—"That cannot be right, Senior,"-Qu Qianxing said, furrowing his brows.

True to his standing as an honor student personally educated by Zhou Hui, he didn't shrink back in the slightest merely because the other party was a Mahayana powerhouse. Instead, he engaged in a thoroughly academic inquiry:

—"Controlling a Kunpeng indeed demands immense spiritual power. Yet once spiritual power is expended, it is not as though the dantian ceases to generate more. On the contrary, when an existence of Master's caliber gathers ambient spiritual qi to produce spiritual power, it accelerates the circulation of spiritual qi between Heaven and Earth, enriching our world's spiritual atmosphere. Is that not a monumental act of merit? Why do you claim it hampers ascension?"

—"That must be your Heavenly Spirit Sect's theory, eh?"-Tao Zhu sneered coldly:—"On the surface, it indeed appears that way. Your lineage discourages reliance on medicinal pills and rarely consumes external artifacts, producing far more spiritual energy than you draw in. For this world, that is indeed a deed of great merit. But think about it: over a single cycle, how much spiritual qi does your master generate? That spiritual qi is absorbed by the myriad living beings of this world. How many worldly karmic bonds have they formed with your master because of it? Will that not irrevocably bind your master to this realm?"

Qu Qianxing's eyes widened in shock.

Still feeling unsatisfied, Tao Zhu looked toward Zhou Hui and continued:

—"This isn't just my opinion—it was a deduction made by that old charlatan Jiuzhai of the [Altar of Divine Decrees](# "Literal CN: 神旨坛 (Shénzhǐ Tán) — esoteric, prophetic cultivation sect renowned for divination and cosmic omens")! During our last gathering—the one you claimed you had no time to attend because you had to cross tribulations—we were discussing how, given that your cultivation and merit have reached such heights, you still haven't been able to ascend. And that was precisely what he said."

—"Moreover, everyone suspects that if you keep this up, you might very well end up just like that [Zhujiuyin](# "Literal CN: 烛九阴 (Zhújiǔyīn) — mythical primordial torch dragon of the Abyss Sea whose breath controls seasonal cycles and light") in the deepest trenches of the Abyss Sea, becoming a permanent part of this realm. Except Zhujiuyin turned into a giant candle beneath the Abyss Sea, whereas you will likely become something akin to the [World Tree Jianmu](# "Literal CN: 建木 (Jiànmù) — legendary divine cosmic tree connecting Heaven and Earth, felled during the ancient God-Demon War") that was cleaved down during the God-Demon War, capable of automatically generating spiritual energy! Who knows, maybe when the time comes, the rest of us will be able to step upon your back to ascend! Hahaha!"

—"How could that be?!"-Qu Qianxing gasped in horror, leaping to his feet to look at Zhou Hui.

He didn't find Tao Zhu's "joke" the least bit amusing.

The mere thought of Master turning into a tree sent a chilling dread straight down to the marrow of his bones.

The other disciples present also turned anxious, distraught gazes toward their Master.

—"Stop frightening my disciples,"-Zhou Hui said helplessly.

He cast a reassuring glance at his disciples:

—"That Zhujiuyin is a genuine dragon, an innate primordial creature. That is why it can merge with the cosmos and become one with this world. But what am I? Merely an ordinary mortal who attained the Dao, with spirit roots so terribly mottled. Unless I successfully ascend and thoroughly transform into a True Immortal's physique, even if I wished to become a part of this world, the world itself would disdain me!"

Speaking to this point, Zhou Hui recalled the scripted demise the Heavenly Dao had drafted for him, and let out a soft chuckle:

—"If I truly could become Jianmu and assist many people in entering the Upper Realm, that wouldn't be half bad either."

*Certainly far better than that rock-bottom clown of a villain.*

—"No! Unless Master ascends, it will never do!"-Qu Qianxing vehemently objected.

—"Indeed! Never mind ascension—even if only one single person were left in this world, so long as it is Master, that would be enough!"-Mo Xi chimed in with even more radical devotion.

—"If Heaven and Earth are unkind, why should we repay Heaven and Earth?!"-Lu Shulong exclaimed, the rims of his eyes reddening.

Tao Zhu watched this scene with cold detachment.

These little brats were merely spouting pretty words. If their master truly turned into the World Tree, these youngsters would likely be the very first to brawl over who controlled the quota for ascension. That was simply how the human heart worked in this world.

Believing he had long seen through the ways of the world, Tao Zhu had no patience for such hypocritical displays of filial devotion.

He merely said to Zhou Hui:

—"Regardless of what you think, you cannot deny that this hypothesis is a distinct possibility!"

—"If that is the case, Master, let us forget about the auction!"-Qu Qianxing declared on the spot:—"There is no need to squander such vast spiritual power for the sake of a potential conspiracy."

—"Well, there's no need to go that far,"-Tao Zhu revealed his true colors, grinning:—"You just don't have to control that Kunpeng. But if you need other methods to transport passengers, you can always buy from our Abyssal Ocean Society! It just so happens that we recently excavated several ancient relics from the ruins that could serve as special sponsorships..."

However, Tao Zhu had overplayed his hand just now, thoroughly terrifying Zhou Hui's disciples.

They now vehemently rejected anything that required Master to expend spiritual power:

[—"Yes, let us cancel the auction!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"When all is said and done, the only reason Master wanted to host this auction was because Eldest Senior Brother got contaminated with something foul and insisted on going, right? Let Eldest Senior Brother resolve that kind of mess himself! In any case, it's mostly his own suicidal antics again."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[—"Exactly, exactly! Who cares if Eldest Senior Brother drops dead!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

*Is this how you treat your Eldest Senior Brother?!*

Zhou Hui thought with a wry smile. Turning to Tao Zhu, he remarked:

—"Old Tao, you've caused me a massive headache!"

With those remarks dropped, his disciples clearly had lost all motivation to work.

—"I was doing it for your own good,"-Tao Zhu said, cradling his massive belly and merely grunting:—"Do you still want to ascend or not?"

[I don't.]

Zhou Hui wanted to answer with absolute, unwavering conviction. But catching sight of Qu Qianxing's agonizingly distraught expression, he swallowed the words back down.

He sensed that his disciples would hardly be able to stomach such a decision.

Fortunately, at that moment, a voice suddenly piped up:

—"I, on the contrary, believe quite the opposite. This series of actions by the Untiring Immortal Venerable is undertaken precisely to better facilitate ascension."

Zhou Hui raised his eyebrows in surprise. The others also turned toward the voice.

Catching sight of the newcomer, Tao Zhu asked in mild surprise:

—"Lan Pei, why are you here?"

—"Ah, Master Lan Pei, why are you still here?"-Mo Xi also gasped in astonishment.

He had clearly forgotten this divine physician's existence long ago.

Lan Pei coughed twice, stroking his sparse, yellowish beard as he strove to project the aura of a transcendent master:

—"This old man is naturally still here. Not only that, during this time I have been observing your master and observing your Heavenly Spirit Sect, seeking to discover a method to save your master."

*...Don't make it sound as if I'm on the verge of dying!*

Zhou Hui complained internally.

Hearing him speak thus, the disciples' expressions turned instantly solemn. Qu Qianxing even bowed deeply to the old fox:

—"Master Lan Pei has worked hard."

—"Tch!"-Tao Zhu looked at Lan Pei dismissively:—"If you have a theory, take it up with Jiuzhai. I was merely relaying his words."

He was here to conduct business, not participate in an academic symposium.

—"I never said what you reported was incorrect. Even Untiring didn't entirely deny it just now,"-Lan Pei stated:—"He indeed generated excessive spiritual qi back into the world, thereby forging too many karmic bonds with this realm, impeding his ascension."

*Not at all. It's purely the Heavenly Dao playing tricks.*

Zhou Hui thought coldly.

Though after several cycles of reincarnation, he had indeed become tangled in excessive intersections with this world—especially the apocalyptic celestial lightning of the fifth cycle. That was why he had estimated he couldn't ascend. In the sixth cycle and the early part of this current cycle, he had considered 'smuggling' himself across, until he discovered that even 'smuggling' was part of the Heavenly Dao's script, prompting him to give up and lie flat.

Lan Pei, this old fox, was probably bored out of his mind staying at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, or perhaps scheming to swindle a sum of spirit stones before skipping town.

Following the theory Tao Zhu had put forth, he began spinning pure nonsense:

—"However, our lord Immortal Venerable has evidently discovered a solution: the reason he preaches the Dao, imparts knowledge, and recruits disciples on a grand scale is precisely to transmute these worldly karmic bonds into cosmic merit!"

—"Ah? Is that so?"-Tao Zhu exclaimed in astonishment.

*Ah, is that so?* Zhou Hui thought.

Lan Pei elaborated in detail:

—"If spiritual energy is merely returned to Heaven and Earth in an ordinary fashion, once absorbed by worldly creatures, it is simply gone. Heaven and Earth possess no heart; karmic bonds exist without awareness, serving only to obstruct Untiring's ascension."

—"However, recruiting disciples on a vast scale is fundamentally different. The entire lineage of the Heavenly Spirit Sect shares in this gift, and the karmic bonds are given concrete form through specific disciples. In this way, Untiring can resolve these karmic bonds in a tangible manner, paving a smooth path for his ascension."

—"Furthermore, this upcoming auction operates on the exact same principle."

—"Originally, this was a karmic grievance between Untiring and Carefree Palace. Dragging the multitude into the fray should have manifested as malevolent karma. Yet Untiring uses the auction to pacify grievances, and mobilizes the Kunpeng to pioneer a new trade route, transmuting what would have been ill-fated karma into boundless domain-wide merit. Is that not immensely advantageous to his cultivation?"

*...This utter nonsense actually sounds astonishingly plausible.*

Worthy of a divine physician who had lived for thousands of years and possessed abundant experience handling [medical dispute riots](# "Literal CN: 医闹 (yīnào) — agitated hospital riots or violent confrontations staged by patients' families against doctors; here Lan Pei's peerless bedside sophistry")!

Zhou Hui felt genuine admiration.

With even Zhou Hui acknowledging it in his heart, Qu Qianxing was completely won over.

Revealing a look of profound shame, Qu Qianxing confessed to Zhou Hui:

—"With my shallow understanding, to actually doubt Master's decisions over a few words from an outsider—it is truly unfilial and treasonous!"

—"I shall immediately proceed according to Master's instructions and prepare the auction arrangements!"-Qu Qianxing declared, bowing deeply.

—"Wait a moment! Now you're making me look like the villain here!"-Tao Zhu complained, turning stubbornly back to Zhou Hui:—"Fine, you have your logic. If you're happy using a Kunpeng, go right ahead and use it! But my spiritual treasures can still be placed in the auction, right? And those items you gave to the people of the Thousand Harmony Gang—give me a share too! Oh, right, right, I also want a few dozen VIP cards for the secret realm. Speaking of which, I have several decent secret realms on hand. Little Zhou Hui, given our longstanding friendship, shouldn't we collaborate on developing them together—"

Tao Zhu chattered incessantly, pestering Zhou Hui without end, acting as though he wouldn't let the matter rest until a deal was struck.

He seemed to have completely forgotten that he had initially rushed here to curse Zhou Hui out, turning instead into an eager merchant desperate to buy a stake in the business.

—Although, to be fair, he had indeed delivered a thorough tongue-lashing to Zhou Hui from various angles just moments before.

Zhou Hui fended him off with a wry smile, privately transmitting his divine sense to Lan Pei:

—"Master Lan Pei, my thanks for smoothing things over for me. I'll treat you to fine wine later."

[No, I was being completely serious!]

Lan Pei thought, thoroughly bewildered.

***

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's resolve had once again unified.

Along with it, all personnel from other organizations connected to the auction plunged into frantic activity.

Among them, naturally, was the Thousand Harmony Gang, tasked with overall coordination of the auction.

However, much like the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the Thousand Harmony Gang also descended into heated squabbles.

They didn't have troublemakers like Tao Zhu bursting in from outside (or if they did, they hadn't shown their faces yet); their conflict stemmed from within.

In truth, there was little worth recounting about Zhang Hongjing leading his thousand-man delegation to pay respects to the Untiring Immortal Venerable. No matter how rebellious someone's heart might be, after witnessing with their own eyes the spectacle of Mount Tianling's inverted reflection and that celestial Kunpeng, all thoughts of causing mischief vanished completely. In fact, they didn't even possess the courage to gaze upon the Immortal Venerable in person; they merely prostrated themselves at the foot of Mount Tianling, reverently listening to the Immortal Venerable's teachings (X), collected their respective assignments, and returned.

Zhang Hongjing had done the same.

However, as the leader of the Thousand "Harmony" Gang, upon returning to the headquarters, he inevitably encountered obstinate fellows who hadn't directly faced the Immortal Venerable's might, dragging the debate right back to square one:

—"You just came back like this? If I recall correctly, you went there to 'persuade,' not to 'surrender'!"

—"That was a collective decision made by everyone present at the time,"-Zhang Hongjing replied listlessly:—"You didn't see the spectacle at Mount Tianling. Facing power rivaling that of a True Immortal, what was I supposed to do?"

—"At the very least, you could have demurred and claimed we couldn't handle it! We aren't subordinates of the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

—"Go tell that to the Immortal Venerable yourself!"-Zhang Hongjing sneered.

That person shut his mouth immediately.

Zhang Hongjing added:

—"Besides, the Heavenly Spirit Sect doesn't actually lack for anything. They possess supplies, venues, and even a Kunpeng! After cultivating for all these years, have you ever heard of anyone capable of domesticating a Kunpeng? Not even a True Immortal could! Yet the Immortal Venerable did! If he wanted to flip the entire Central Domain upside down right now, it wouldn't pose the slightest difficulty. The fact that he's still willing to let us handle some mundane logistics is already showing immense respect—or do you believe it would be better if we surrendered full hosting rights for all future auctions to the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

Hearing him put it that way, quite a few individuals wore embarrassed, sheepish expressions.

The Thousand Harmony Gang considered itself the largest sect in the Central Domain. Organizing and running the annual auction was vital proof of their guild's strength; they naturally didn't want to surrender it, which meant they couldn't stiffly declare their refusal to participate.

Yet there were still those unwilling to give up even at this stage:

—"Then must we obey his every command?"

—"You can naturally refuse!"-Zhang Hongjing snorted coldly, slamming his Sect Leader Token onto the table:—"Take the token and go tell him yourself! Whichever of you has a death wish is more than welcome to go!"

This time, absolute silence fell across the room.

Quite a few people even took several steps back, as though terrified the Sect Leader Token might somehow cling to them.

Zhang Hongjing glared at the crowd and let out a cold grunt.

[Ah, the feeling of hugging a giant patron's thigh is so delightfully satisfying!]

He mused privately in his heart.

Ever since founding the Thousand Harmony Gang, Zhang Hongjing had never felt so thoroughly carefree. No matter what issue arose, simply invoking the Untiring Immortal Venerable's name was enough to immediately silence this unruly mob.

He only hoped that when the Immortal Venerable found out, he wouldn't blame him for [borrowing the tiger's majesty](# "Literal CN: 狐假虎威 (hújiǎ-hǔwēi) — the fox borrowing the tiger's terror; using a superior's prestige to cow underlings").

Zhang Hongjing offered a silent apology in his heart, softened his tone, and conveyed the Immortal Venerable's requirements:

—"In truth, the Immortal Venerable did not issue any harsh demands. He merely gave us a batch of seeds, instructing us to distribute them to those who truly need to attend the auction."

—"Seeds?"

—"Correct, seeds."

Zhang Hongjing fished out a handful of colorful, coral-bead-like objects from his storage pouch and displayed them to the crowd:

—"They do not appear to be ordinary spiritual tree seeds. According to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's explanation, they are meant to guide the Kunpeng to designated locations to pick up passengers. The planting method is remarkably simple: just bury them in the dirt. They require no special care."

As he spoke, he decided to demonstrate.

Choosing the summit of an outer peak belonging to the Thousand Harmony Gang—Zhang Hongjing had originally planned to plant it in the main courtyard, but faced widespread opposition, forcing him to select this location a certain distance from headquarters—he planted a bright red seed into the earth.

Sure enough, the seed grew upon contact with the wind.

In merely an instant, a red coral tree over five meters tall materialized before everyone. Every coral branch radiated luminous light, and at the very summit hung a halo resembling a lunar disc, faintly marked with tick graduations dividing time.

Beneath the illumination of the halo, the coral tree cast its shadow upon the ground day and night. Miraculously, however, it displayed the shadows of only three branches, much like a sundial—yet two of those shadow-branches were constantly in motion.

—"This is the Heavenly Spirit Sect's [Improved Sundial Clock](# "Literal CN: 改进型日晷钟 (Gǎijìnxíng Rìguǐzhōng) — an enchanted sundial clock using three coral branch shadows to track hours, minutes, and seconds"),"-Zhang Hongjing observed and explained:—"When the appointed time arrives, it will teleport us to a location convenient for boarding the Kunpeng."

The crowd reflected on this and found it logical.

What a gargantuan creature a Kunpeng was! It naturally couldn't easily descend to the ground, especially in densely populated areas. Teleporting passengers directly aboard using a magical artifact was only natural.

The onlookers circled the coral tree to examine it.

Seeing that Zhang Hongjing said nothing further, someone couldn't help asking:

—"And then? That's all?"

—"Yes, that's all,"-Zhang Hongjing answered. Pausing briefly, he added:—"Oh, right. There are also several blueprint designs for passenger ferry docks. The Heavenly Spirit Sect stated that if the participants' financial conditions permit, they can consider whether to construct dedicated docks to receive and welcome the Kunpeng. They do not enforce it."

*Why build that useless junk? Could it be that after this auction, we'll be riding Kunpengs regularly?!*

Many in the opposition faction muttered inwardly.

If they didn't lack the courage, they would have loved to uproot this coral tree on the spot.

However, that wasn't what they truly wanted to ask:

—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect only had us distribute these seeds? What about the other matters?"

—"There are no other matters,"-Zhang Hongjing replied innocently.

—"What about the specific logistics of the auction?!"-someone pressed anxiously:—"Could it be that they've monopolized everything?! In that case, how is this any different from us being completely sidelined?!"

*Just a moment ago you were all shouting to refuse!*

Zhang Hongjing toyed with them for several minutes before slapping his forehead:

—"Ah, I almost forgot to mention. Because the Heavenly Spirit Sect lacks experience in hosting auctions, they do not intend to replace us entirely. Therefore, they have instructed us to conduct an on-site survey and submit an auction [project proposal](# "Literal CN: 方案书 (fāng'àn shū) — formal corporate business/operational proposal"), detailing venue requirements, pavilion specifications—"

Hearing this, quite a few individuals revealed scheming, sinister expressions.

Zhang Hongjing knew what they were plotting the moment he looked at them. He immediately extinguished their hopes:

—"Do not even think about making things difficult for the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Do you truly believe the Immortal Venerable would listen to our demands? We have a total of only nine chances to revise and submit the project proposal. If they remain unsatisfied after nine submissions, they will seek another venue or handle it entirely on their own."

Instantly, numerous faces turned pale, struck as if by a bolt from the blue.

Anyone who suddenly realized they had become [Party B](# "Literal CN: 乙方 (yǐfāng) — the contractor/vendor in Chinese corporate and contract law, subjected to endless client demands and revisions") would feel equally crushed.

Zhang Hongjing, however, thought nothing of it. In truth, they had been playing Party B every single year—it was just that in previous years, [Party A](# "Literal CN: 甲方 (jiǎfāng) — the client/buyer who holds ultimate decision-making power") was the Palace of Carefree Wandering. These fools simply lacked self-awareness!

He even added a brutal parting blow:

—"Furthermore, those seeds have been enchanted by the Immortal Venerable's divine arts. They can only be bestowed upon eligible participants who meet the criteria; we cannot play many tricks. If we fail to distribute the required quota, they will deem us unsuitable partners and reject our proposal outright."

—"This is an outright plot to seize our hosting rights!"-someone spat through gritted teeth.

*No, it's merely to prevent you from doing stupid things.*

*In fact, quite a few of those conditions were suggestions I personally advised Fellow Daoist Mo to include.*

Zhang Hongjing thought inwardly.

He had finally managed to smoothly delegate the distribution of coral seeds and the drafting of the proposal.

Of course, the matter was far from settled.

Once the crowd dispersed, Zhang Hongjing swept his sleeves and headed toward the rear courtyard of the Thousand Harmony Gang.

***

There stood a peculiar, colossal boulder, densely carved with names.

This was the [Witness Stone](# "Literal CN: 见证石 (Jiànzhèng Shí) — monumental inscribed oath stone") that Zhang Hongjing and his close companions had erected when they [swore an oath of alliance by smearing blood](# "Literal CN: 歃血为盟 (shàxuè wéiméng) — ancient Chinese ritual of smearing animal blood on lips to solemnize a brotherhood pact") to establish the Thousand Harmony Gang all those years ago.

Because of the Central Domain's harsh, cutthroat environment, rogue cultivators could not survive alone. Nor was everyone as fortunate as Zhao Yuegui, happening to stumble upon a suitable sect. Having witnessed this bleak reality firsthand, Zhang Hongjing and his friends founded the Thousand Harmony Gang—setting no restrictive admission requirements and imposing no harsh rules, intending merely to offer fellow rogue cultivators mutual aid so everyone could survive a little better.

On the surface, they had indeed succeeded. The Thousand Harmony Gang had not only become the largest sect in the Central Domain, but had also won over numerous immortal cities. Thanks to its loose regulations, whenever rogue cultivators found themselves with no alternatives, it was the first haven they could turn to.

But among the highest echelon, how many still remembered their original intent?

—"I am still watching."

—"I hope you will not let me down."

Zhang Hongjing reached his hand toward the boulder covered in names. Azure arcs of lightning flickered. As the electrical current flowed across specific names, the faint sound of hushed conversation echoed from within the depths of the stone.

***

—"Are we truly going to comply?"

After leaving the Thousand Harmony Gang, Daoist Huang, Perfected Master Chen, and several other high-ranking gang figures belonging to the pro-Carefree Palace faction gathered at their usual pleasure den. Yet this time, they called for none of their familiar courtesans, huddling together solely for a secret conference.

—"I fear we have no other choice,"-Perfected Master Chen sighed:—"Never mind that Zhang Hongjing was wavering all along—even the representatives we dispatched had their courage completely terrified out of them, refusing to push forward under any circumstances."

—"Then how do we account for this to Carefree Palace?"

—"We tell the truth! Surely Carefree Palace cannot force us to throw away our lives, can they?"

—"Hard to say! They still hold dirt on you, don't they?!"

—"But I've already done everything I possibly could! Besides, once the auction is held, those sects and tribes will obtain the resources they desperately need—will they still heed Carefree Palace's demands? Their entire scheme won't hold together... *Pfft!*"

The one who had been loudly clamoring to back out suddenly vomited a mouthful of blood and collapsed across the table. An alien eye that did not belong to him sprouted upon his face, coldly glaring at the assembled crowd.

The atmosphere froze into instant, chilling silence.

The others present trembled in terror as they stared at that eye, petrified that they might suffer the exact same fate.

In this moment, their resentment toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable burned even hotter—why did he have to oppose Carefree Palace in the first place?! The ones suffering misfortune now were merely disposable cannon fodder like them!

—"It isn't completely hopeless,"-Daoist Huang spoke with a stiff voice:—"We only need to agree on the surface. As long as those seeds never reach the hands of the people genuinely attending the auction, what does it matter if the auction takes place? When the time comes, those people will still have no choice but to turn to Carefree Palace."

—"But didn't Zhang Hongjing say the seeds are refined magical treasures bound by restrictive conditions?"-Perfected Master Chen asked:—"Can we truly defy the rules of a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator?"

Saying so, he nervously touched his own face, terrified that an extra eyeball might erupt from his skin.

—"I never said we should defy him directly,"-Daoist Huang said:—"Don't you know how to [comply in public while flouting in secret](# "Literal CN: 阳奉阴违 (yángfèng-yīnwéi) — outwardly compliant but privately rebellious")? Even a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse cannot possibly monitor the whereabouts of every single seed, can he? The so-called 'rules' are merely parameters he established to keep people from violating his will. But as everyone knows, wherever there are 'rules,' there are bound to be [loopholes to exploit](# "Literal CN: 钻空子 (zuān kòngzi) — gaming the system or finding loopholes in rules")!"

Saying so, he glanced at the eyeball on the face of the man slumped across the table.

Daoist Huang had confirmed this principle with the powerhouses of Carefree Palace. Though he had never directly faced a Tribulation Transcendence titan, Carefree Palace had plenty of Mahayana masters—and hadn't he still found loopholes to exploit among them?

This time was no different. He felt certain he could pull the wool over the Untiring Immortal Venerable's eyes.

Daoist Huang asked the crowd:

—"What were the rules established by that person?"

—"The conditions for planting the coral trees require meeting three criteria,"-someone read:

> **Conditions for Planting Coral Trees**:
> 1. Individuals who possess an urgent need for items that are certain to be purchasable at the auction;
> 2. Individuals who possess sufficient capital and material resources to participate in the auction (whether as buyer or seller);
> 3. Locations where the two aforementioned categories are sufficiently concentrated (a total of at least thirty individuals).
>
> *Note: These tree seeds can only survive within the Central Domain and must not be taken into territories outside the Central Domain...*

—"Good! That is more than enough!"-Daoist Huang slapped his thigh without waiting for him to finish.

—"Enough?"-The others stared at him in a daze.

—"Have you lost your minds?! There is an enormous loophole in those three rules!"

—"A loophole?"

—"Un-Roll... *Ahem*, that person actually forgot to specify the participants' cultivation realm! Nor did he specify the participants' social background!"-Daoist Huang declared with smug triumph:—"If we were forced to select exclusively among immortal cultivators, these three conditions would be extremely rigid. We would likely have to distribute seeds to every single sect and immortal city, and even then we might not fulfill his distribution quota. But what if we distribute these seeds to the mortals of the Central Domain?!"

—"However, rule number two requires sufficient capital... Mortals cannot achieve that, can they?"-someone voiced with concern.

—"How could that be impossible? This is the Central Domain! Any mortal tribe capable of surviving here invariably hoards some accumulated reserves. In ordinary auctions, neighboring sects would purchase goods on their behalf, and their tribal chiefs would subsequently buy from those sects. This time, we'll take the extra trouble to deliver the seeds directly into their hands!"-Daoist Huang said gloatingly.

He added:

—"And it doesn't necessarily have to be mortals. There are also secluded sects and demon clans in remote regions! In the past, it wasn't that they didn't wish to participate—they simply had no means of traveling! This time, with the Immortal Venerable providing the transportation, they can easily attend! Gathering thirty people won't be difficult in the slightest!"

This was precisely where the Thousand Harmony Gang held immense power. Loose as they were, their guild members were scattered across every corner of the Central Domain; when it came to dredging up entities hidden in deep, forgotten mountain crevices, no one surpassed them.

This was also the reason Zhou Hui had entrusted them with distributing the seeds.

—"Brilliant! Utterly brilliant!"-Perfected Master Chen's eyes lit up as he chortled with laughter:—"In this manner, this auction will become a third-rate circus of messy, ragtag demons and mortals! I fear by the time the Heavenly Spirit Sect sees these attendees, their regret will come far too late!"

—"Precisely! And we can also curry favor with certain sects,"-someone added:—"I know several sects that stand firmly with Carefree Palace. They don't wish to attend, but are agonizing over how to resist that person's tyrannical might! If we withhold the seeds from them, they will naturally have a legitimate pretext not to attend."

—"And when reporting to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, we can simply claim those sects refused to attend of their own accord. The Heavenly Spirit Sect won't be able to blame us—after all, we executed everything strictly according to their guidelines! We will have even distributed a sufficient quantity!"

—"We can even exceed the quota required by the Heavenly Spirit Sect! If we factor in all those remote demon beast clans and spiritual tribes, I wager the Heavenly Spirit Sect's territory won't even be capable of accommodating so many people!"

...

The more the group discussed, the more excited they became.

In their minds, the failure of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's auction was already right around the corner.
