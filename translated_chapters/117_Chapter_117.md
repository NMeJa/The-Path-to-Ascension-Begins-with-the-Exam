# Chapter 117: [Master and Disciples](<# "Literal CN: 师徒 (shītú) — Master and Disciples; chapter title referring to Zhou Hui and his disciples gathering warmly in the cave, contrasted with Northern Domain masters and disciples suffering academic peer-review trauma">) (第117章 师徒)

When Qu Qianxing arrived at Zhou Hui's immortal cave, his fellow martial brothers and sisters had indeed already gathered.

Among them, Mo Xi was clinging to Master's side as usual, holding a mirror in both hands as reverently as if [holding a funeral portrait](<# "Literal CN: 捧着遗照 (pěngzhe yízhào) — holding a deceased ancestor's funeral portrait; comedic exaggeration for Mo Xi's solemnity holding the mirror showing Mo Bei">). Reflected within the mirror was Mo Bei, far away in Fengqing City and unable to return anytime soon.

Lu Shulong sat behind Master, holding a comb to gently untangle Master's long silver hair while simultaneously utilizing his True Dragon physique to assist Master in regulating and smoothing his spiritual energy.

Ran Deng and Guan Ye sat one by the window and one in the corner, holding burning incense and a caged lantern respectively; they were using fragrance and light to calibrate the flow of spiritual energy within the cave, weaving a new formation array.

As for the "Eldest Senior Brother," draped in a black outer robe and cradling a long sword, he stood guard at the cave entrance—on the surface, looking more like an "Eldest Senior Brother" than he ever had in his entire life.

Seeing Qu Qianxing enter, the "Eldest Senior Brother" raised his eyelids slightly and shot Qu Qianxing a glance.

Looking at this "Eldest Senior Brother," Qu Qianxing finally recalled the sorrowful, resentful gaze of the little black lamb [hanging (by its own neck)](<# "Literal CN: (自)挂在桃树上 — hanging (oneself) from the peach tree; Yuwen Xuan hanging from the peach tree branch to elicit sympathy and face-saving leniency">) from the peach tree that day when he had gone out to welcome Shui Huang'er.

Thus, Qu Qianxing feigned a glance at the torrential downpour outside and respectfully asked Zhou Hui:

—"Master, this spiritual rain looks like it will keep falling for quite a while. Shouldn't we bring the peach tree back inside?"

—"Ah, that lamb is still on the peach tree?"-Mo Xi blurted out in shock.

When the heavenly lightning descended, Mo Xi had sensed Mo Bei's communication request and rushed off to the Water Mirror Tower, never imagining that the black lamb on the peach tree had remained hanging there all this time.

He felt a twinge of guilt, wondering if he should have gone to take the black lamb down.

Unexpectedly, his Master said:

—"Pay him no mind,"-Zhou Hui said.-"When the time comes for him to get down, he will naturally come down on his own."

At this point, Zhou Hui let out a chuckle:

—"Perhaps he is quite delighted to be hanging there right now!"

Upon the peach tree, the black lamb—currently opening its mouth wide and [vacuuming down](<# "Literal CN: 暴风吸入 (bàofēng xīrù) — hurricane inhalation / vacuuming down food at gale-force speed; internet slang for ravenously inhaling food/drink">) the spiritual rain—suddenly sneezed. It closed its mouth in aggrieved indignance, letting out a whimpering *ying-ying* sound. Yet whether it was the spiritual rain being sucked in like a vortex or the black curly fleece on its body writhing restlessly like tentacles, everything indicated that it had no intention whatsoever of halting its "feeding."

If anyone were to walk up to the mountain gate at this moment, they would surely be scared weak in the knees by this "monster" on the peach tree, which could no longer even be recognized as a black lamb.

Fortunately, at the present moment, no one had the spare time to pay attention to the mountain gate.

Qu Qianxing had only been asking casually. Since Master put it that way, he naturally held no objections. He merely shook his head toward the "Eldest Senior Brother," who lowered his eyes, seemingly unbothered.

Meanwhile, the other disciples wore thoughtful expressions, though what they were speculating remained anyone's guess.

Only Mo Xi cast a worried glance in the direction of the mountain gate.

Although Master certainly had his reasons for everything he did, he still felt that lamb was a bit... pitiful.

Qu Qianxing glanced at Mo Xi and swiftly deduced from the mirror in the youth's hands what Master had been discussing with his junior brother and sister before his arrival.

—"Did Little Junior Sister also see the heavenly tribulation from Fengqing City?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Not only did I see it, I faced it head-on,"-Mo Bei said.

She was clearly in the pavilion atop the rooftop terrace of the First Sorrow Tea shop at this moment. Simply by turning sideways, she could see the spiritual energy falling from the sky like a fine drizzle.

—"Fengqing City was affected as well? Then the entire Central Domain...?!"-Qu Qianxing blurted out in alarm, hurriedly looking toward Master.

Prior to this, Qu Qianxing had just assured the cultivators that with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Spirit Storage Pools, no greater disaster would occur. But if the entire Central Domain had been affected, wouldn't the territories beyond the reach of the Spirit Storage Pools be devastated?

By stopping the cultivators from mounting disaster-relief efforts, had he ended up making things worse instead?

—"Do not panic. The tribulation lightning was primarily concentrated in the Heavenly Spirit Mountain region; only about fifteen percent of the energy spilled outward,"-Zhou Hui said slowly.-"Furthermore, in the short term, this rain of spiritual energy is a good thing. Particularly for immortal cities like Fengqing City and Cloud City, it is a tremendous fortuitous opportunity for the cultivators within."

—"Indeed. There is nobody on the streets right now,"-Mo Bei said, panning her transmission angle toward the street below.

It was not entirely devoid of people as she claimed; several cultivators were meditating cross-legged right on the streets, looking as though terrified of missing even a single drop of the spiritual rain.

Yet their courage to meditate in the middle of the street was not sheer recklessness, for not far from where they sat stood a White Jade Capital heroic spirit soldier standing straight as an iron lance.

Evidently, this spiritual rain also conferred direct benefits upon heroic spirits. That heroic spirit soldier was over ten centimeters taller than before, and the armor encasing its body gleamed pristine white, sparkling just like Zhou Hui's hair.

Faced with such soldiers, those cultivators were actually not afraid, even daring to meditate near them. It seemed his Little Junior Sister had already won considerable trust from the residents of Fengqing City!

Qu Qianxing assessed. However, he did not miss the subtext in Mo Bei's words:

—"There is 'nobody' now, but does that mean there were many people during the heavenly tribulation?"

Fengqing City lacked Master holding the fort—only Mo Bei and a handful of Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples were stationed there. That apocalyptic spectacle during the heavenly tribulation, where everything seemed on the verge of destruction, must have ignited no small panic, right?

Come to think of it, if nothing had happened, why would those heroic spirit soldiers be standing sentry out on the streets?

Sure enough, Mo Bei nodded:

—"Indeed. Some people were running around frantically, screaming that it was heaven's wrath, and there were also opportunists attempting to loot and rob amidst the chaos, so I rounded them up—"

—"Did you kill people again?"-Mo Xi asked in displeasure.

It was not that he felt any pity for those who died; rather, he was worried about Mo Bei.

Although Mo Xi felt his elder sister's head was almost as empty as her pet carp's, as bearers of half-Yin spirit lineage, their nature was inherently inclined toward slaughter and toying with people's hearts—ah, though toying with people's hearts was probably too high a difficulty for Mo Bei. Therefore, whether killing out of principle and justice or not, their emotions were bound to be tainted by blood and slaughter, walking step by step toward the abyss.

In fact, Mo Xi had been having recurring dreams lately. He dreamt that he slaughtered countless people, and in recent dreams, his dream-self could no longer control his emotions with reason, slaughtering innocents indiscriminately just to derive satisfaction.

Mo Xi did not understand the origin of these dreams, and he worried whether Mo Bei's emotions had spilled over and affected him.

Naturally, this made him even more worried about Mo Bei's mental state.

Yet Mo Bei always wore that poker-faced deadpan expression. Even sharing the exact same face, Mo Xi could not decipher what on earth his sister was thinking just by looking at her expression. In the past, they could still share consciousness, but now that Mo Bei was in Fengqing City, Mo Xi had completely lost grasp of her thoughts. This left him profoundly anxious and terribly unaccustomed.

Yet Mo Bei appeared completely unaffected.

Fortunately, she did not put on her "I already killed them, why are you so nagging" attitude this time. She merely said:

—"The one who caused the worst ruckus was decapitated by me; he harmed innocent mortals right in the street. As for the rest, I used the Spirit-Binding Array Eldest Senior Sister gave me to string them up in the city center."

—"Huh, I recall that the Spirit-Binding Array seals the target's spiritual meridians, rendering them unable to circulate any cultivation methods so as to achieve restraint, right?"-Lu Shulong asked upon hearing this.-"Doesn't that mean even though they are currently standing in the spiritual rain, they cannot absorb a single shred of spiritual energy?"

—"Is that so?"-Mo Bei asked nonchalantly.

—"That is so,"-Ran Deng confirmed.-"Those trapped within the Spirit-Binding Array are no different from ordinary mortals."

—"Oh,"-Mo Bei nodded with zero interest.

Guan Ye, on the other hand, gave her a thumbs-up:

—"Jie-jie, beautifully done!"

[Beautiful my foot! This girl didn't even do it intentionally!]

Looking at Mo Bei's expressionless face, Mo Xi felt annoyance surge within him.

Before Mo Xi could voice any complaints toward Mo Bei, however, Zhou Hui spoke up:

—"I had originally intended to place a ward on you, but since you have a Spirit-Binding Array on hand, that works out just as well. Bei'er, have the heroic spirit soldiers guard the house properly, and apply that Spirit-Binding Array to yourself as well!"

—"Eh? Why?"-Mo Xi looked toward Master, his face blanching in shock.

—"This spiritual rain carries remnants of tribulation lightning,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"It will exert negative effects on an Extreme Yin Constitution. Using the Spirit-Binding Array to isolate the absorption of spiritual energy is just what is needed."

—"So that's why. No wonder I felt a slight stinging pain when the rainwater landed on me just now,"-Mo Bei murmured in realization.

[Are you an idiot?! You only realize it after Master points it out?!]

Mo Xi glared at Mo Bei.

—"It is fine as long as you can endure it,"-Zhou Hui said, shooting Mo Bei an approving glance.-"While absorbing too much is harmful, absorbing a moderate amount poses no danger. Moreover, heavenly lightning will aid you in comprehending the Dao of Slaughter. Didn't you mention last time that you wished to cultivate in this direction? Then before the spiritual rain ceases, try absorbing a wisp of spiritual energy every two hours to temper your sword intent. But do not force yourself—if it becomes truly unbearable, stop immediately."

—"Yes, Master,"-Mo Bei accepted the instruction.

Mo Xi, however, was anxious enough to leap to his feet:

—"Wait a second! You want to cultivate the Dao of Slaughter?!"

—"Is that not allowed?"-Mo Bei raised her eyes.

—"The Dao of Slaughter is not necessarily a bad thing,"-Guan Ye chimed in.-"Although it sounds terrifying, if the direction is right, it is actually a Dao of cultivating the heart. Examining oneself before examining others—that way, one will not lose themselves."

Mo Xi naturally knew this.

The merits and pitfalls of various Daos had been taught to the disciples by Zhou Hui through his own insights back during their very first introductory lesson.

If Mo Xi couldn't recite them from memory, he would be hanging at the mountain gate alongside Eldest Senior Brother by tomorrow.

—"B-But Mo Bei is a girl..."-Mo Xi voiced his worry.

—"Does that have anything to do with being male or female?"-Mo Bei countered.-"I wish to cultivate the Dao of Slaughter solely because I do not wish to be controlled by anything. Whether it is Yin spirit bloodline, an Extreme Yin Constitution, or anything else, I only ever want to grip my own sword with my own will!"

"..." Mo Xi was momentarily struck dumb.

Since when had such resolve appeared in Mo Bei's usually nonchalant eyes?

For some reason, strange murmurs echoed in his mind:

[—"He is only treating you as a tool?"](<# "Speaker: Inner Voice / Karmic Echo">)

[—"If used as a tool by one's destined soulmate, isn't that romantic?"](<# "Speaker: Inner Voice / Karmic Echo">)

[—"I think your brain has rotted away!"](<# "Speaker: Inner Voice / Karmic Echo">)

Listening to these voices, Mo Xi asked as if possessed by a ghost:

—"What if it is your destined soulmate?"

—"...Destined soulmate?"-Mo Bei froze for a moment, as though she had completely forgotten such a concept even existed.

—"A destined soulmate... ought to be special, right?"-Mo Bei said slowly and hesitantly.

Her words caused Mo Xi's heart to clench.

Yet Mo Bei continued:

—"However, wanting to control my sword, wanting to control my very foundation of survival—can such a person... really be called a 'destined soulmate'?"

—"Pfft!"-Lu Shulong let out a burst of laughter.

The others shot him peculiar glances.

—"Sorry... pfft-haha..."-Lu Shulong apologized, yet as though he had heard the most hilarious joke in the world, he still snorted with laughter. He buried his face in Master's hair, his shoulders trembling with suppressed mirth.

—"What is so funny?"-Mo Xi muttered.

Yet without knowing why, he also felt a long sigh of relief escape him. Those nightmares of consecutive days suddenly did not seem quite so terrifying anymore.

Only, alongside that relief, Mo Xi faintly felt a trace of envy.

Mo Bei seemed to have found the path she wanted to walk. What about him?

What about his "Grand Dao"?

—"Xi'er, you need not rush. Take your time,"-at this moment, a pair of gentle hands cupped Mo Xi's face. Silver-gray eyes, resembling a deity's, gazed upon him.-"Ponder slowly, take it one step at a time. No matter when you begin, it is never too late."

—"Master..."-Mo Xi called out, deeply moved.

He moved to lean against Master's shoulder, but before he could actually touch Master, a hand grabbed the scruff of his neck and tossed him aside.

"!"

Mo Xi's eyes widened. He whipped his head around in fury, only to see Qu Qianxing sitting right in the spot he had just occupied.

—"Master, you mentioned earlier that this spiritual rain is harmless in the short term. Does that imply it will be rather troublesome in the long run?"-Qu Qianxing asked with utmost solemnity, making it hard to imagine he had just so "cruelly" tossed his adorable little junior brother aside.

—"With such a massive scale of spiritual energy fluctuation, an impact on the Central Domain is unavoidable,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

What he did not say was that, had Zhou Hui been willing at the time, he could have kept from leaking a single drop of spiritual energy, storing it all within the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Spirit Storage Pools for his own use. Yet Zhou Hui had deliberately allowed that spiritual rain carrying the purifying power of heavenly lightning to leak out. He wanted to observe what kind of impact such spiritual rain would have on the environment of the Central Domain—especially on the ancient God-Demon Battlefield corrupted by demonic qi.

Naturally, Zhou Hui was not aiming without a target.

Before doing so, he had calculated the worst-case scenario and ensured everything remained firmly within his control.

Zhou Hui instructed:

—"Therefore, we must construct a spiritual energy monitoring system across the Central Domain, similar to our Heavenly Spirit Sect's..."

A sharp gasp resounded through the room. Without even thinking, one knew who it came from.

—"Rest easy, I have no intention of making you build it,"-Zhou Hui said, shooting Ran Deng an exasperated glare between laughter and tears.-"The entire Central Domain is so vast, you wouldn't be able to finish building it anyway!"

Ran Deng stuck out her tongue and feigned worry:

—"Then is Master planning to...?"

—"Have the demon race assist! They currently wish to cooperate with us in establishing schools. A minor condition like this costs them little effort; they will surely agree,"-Zhou Hui had clearly planned it all out. He turned to Qu Qianxing.-"However, the demon race can at most help us collect data. Analyzing the data will be the tricky part. How much manpower can our academy still spare?"

—"A few dozen people at most... Setting aside the Immortal Arts Assembly, the demon academies require training instructors. We are already facing a severe manpower shortage,"-Qu Qianxing said, furrowing his brow.

—"Is that so? But waiting until this year's major recruitment drive to train them will be a bit too late. Let us see if we can borrow some people from someone..."-Zhou Hui mentally tallied his "old friends," but after calculating back and forth, he felt somewhat uneasy about all of them. He then inquired:-"What do you think of your Eldest Senior Brother?"

—"Eh?!"-Qu Qianxing's eyes went wide.

He saw the "Eldest Senior Brother" guarding the doorway snap his head up as well, his eyes gleaming brightly as he looked over:

Although it meant having an assignment to complete, this signaled that Master was finally willing to let him down from the tree!

Qu Qianxing, however, felt his scalp tingle with dread.

He swore he held no prejudice against Eldest Senior Brother. He was even willing to support him with the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect after Master ascended—releasing that glutton out into the wild felt like unleashing a calamity upon the entire cultivation realm—but collaborating with Eldest Senior Brother?

Only if the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect was annihilated would that be possible!

Yet Zhou Hui seemed to consider his eldest disciple quite usable:

—"Presently, out of the entire sect from top to bottom, he is the idlest of all. It is about time he..."

—"M-Master! I do not think we need to trouble Eldest Senior Brother!"-Qu Qianxing hurriedly intervened.-"Actually, there are still some kind-hearted folks willing to help... probably!"

—"Kind-hearted folks?"-Zhou Hui asked in surprise.

—"Y-Yes, exactly!"-Qu Qianxing thought for a moment, citing how the high-tier powerhouses in the academic lecture hall had automatically and spontaneously prepared to mount disaster relief after Zhou Hui crossed his tribulation. He persuaded:-"At the time, I placated them. But thinking carefully about it, the various venerable seniors hold a noble heart to rescue the Central Domain, and the Central Domain belongs to everyone—how could we possibly exclude them? Moreover, they seem extraordinarily interested in learning how to construct the Spirit Storage Pools. I believe this [kills two birds with one stone](<# "Literal CN: 一石二鸟 (yī shí èr niǎo) — one stone two birds">)!"

Hearing Qu Qianxing say that, the "Eldest Senior Brother" glared viciously at Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing pretended not to notice.

In any case, even if Eldest Senior Brother hung from that tree for the rest of his life, he would never agree!

Zhou Hui did not notice the undercurrents surging between his first two disciples. He actually found Qu Qianxing's idea rather viable, though his conscience twinged slightly:

—"They clearly came to participate in the Immortal Arts Assembly, yet somehow end up doing labor for us!"

—"I am certain they are more than willing!"-Qu Qianxing declared with firm conviction on behalf of those powerhouses.

He treated this matter as finalized, refusing to grant "Eldest Senior Brother" any chance of turning the tables.

Qu Qianxing stiffly changed the subject:

—"Then it is settled! I shall go negotiate with those seniors later... More importantly, Master, since this heavenly tribulation affected the entire Central Domain, will it cause repercussions in other regions of the cultivation realm?"

—"With the Abyss Sea acting as a buffer, it will not cause any repercussions,"-Zhou Hui said.

After all, this time was not the Annihilation Lightning of the fifth reincarnation cycle.

He was actually somewhat puzzled as to why the other party had not destroyed the world this time. After all, that final strike had clearly carried a hint of furious humiliation. Yet the volume of Annihilation Lightning had merely been confined to the northeastern portion of the Central Domain; tearing apart the entire Central Domain evenly with that amount would have been quite difficult.

[Could it be that they ran out of lightning?]

Speaking of which, having expended such an astronomical quantity of Annihilation Lightning in the fifth cycle, how much "inventory" did they actually have left?

Just how on earth was an entity like heavenly lightning generated?

The Untiring Immortal Venerable's head was teeming with questions. Yet answers to these inquiries could only be found upon reaching the True Immortal Realm; mere contemplation was insufficient, so Zhou Hui could only suppress his thirst for knowledge.

Zhou Hui simply analyzed his second disciple's question:

—"If there is any impact to speak of, it is merely that the heavenly lightning generated an exceedingly colossal celestial phenomenon that can be perceived even across the Abyss Sea! Especially for the Eastern Domain and Northern Domain, which lie very close to our bearing, any slightly higher-tier cultivator ought to have 'seen' it."

—"However, it is merely seeing it; there will be no actual impact,"-Zhou Hui said lightheartedly.

At this moment, Qu Qianxing felt that Master truly did not care in the slightest about what others thought of him!

Think about it: a heavenly tribulation capable of nearly shattering an entire territory suddenly appeared in the Central Domain. Even across the Abyss Sea on another continental plate, suddenly witnessing such a spectacle would surely trigger an uproar!

Yet the mastermind who caused this monumental uproar did not offer a single word of explanation.

Qu Qianxing could practically envision the utter chaos and turmoil sweeping through the cultivation worlds of the Northern and Eastern Domains.

---

And just as Qu Qianxing guessed, the Eastern and Northern Domains—especially the Northern Domain—were in complete chaos.

In fact, the brilliance of the heavenly tribulation was even more dazzling than Zhou Hui had calculated. Even mortals in the Northern Domain witnessed a [second "sun"](<# "Literal CN: 第二轮太阳 (dì-èr lún tàiyáng) — a second sun; referring to the blinding white light of the ascension tribulation visible across continents">) rise in their southeastern sky.

All manner of celestial phenomenon researchers offered differing theories. The major sects scrambled frantically to contact their friends or disciples in the Central Domain. Unfortunately, due to the interference of the spiritual rain, communications were anything but smooth—in fact, cross-border communications were rarely smooth under normal circumstances anyway, as even Mahayana cultivators found communicating across domain boundaries arduous unless one was a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator. As a result, the temporary inability to receive replies amplified all sorts of dire speculations. The major sects had no choice but to frantically barrage the Heavenly Dao Alliance with inquiries.

[Lord Mei and Lady Zhu](<# "Literal CN: 梅竹夫妇 (Méi Zhú fūfù) — the Mei Zhu couple; senior leadership figures in the Northern Domain Heavenly Dao Alliance">) of the Heavenly Dao Alliance were hounded into dizzying circles by the harassment. Yet their energy was entirely consumed by attempting to contact the Central Domain branch of the Heavenly Dao Alliance. For the moment, it did not even cross their minds that this bizarre phenomenon had been caused by their "old friend." Meanwhile, the Central Domain branch's primary personnel were dispersed across various territories organizing the Immortal Arts Assembly; even those stationed in the Central Domain were uncertain of the true cause behind the blinding white light and could not explain what had happened, making the situation even more chaotic.

Amidst this state of chaos, even the venerable ancestor of the [Huanfei Sect](<# "Literal CN: 幻飞门 (Huànfēi Mén) — Illusory Soaring Sect; one of the premier sects of the Northern Domain">)—ranked among the Northern Domain's foremost ["Three Sects, Six Factions, One Valley"](<# "Literal CN: 三门六派一谷 (sān mén liù pài yī gǔ) — Three Sects, Six Factions, One Valley; the elite ruling tier of cultivation sects in the Northern Domain">)—the Mahayana powerhouse [Fairy Ruopei](<# "Literal CN: 若佩仙子 (Ruòpèi Xiānzǐ) — Fairy Ruopei; ancient Mahayana ancestor of the Huanfei Sect in the Northern Domain">), was alarmed and invited out of seclusion.

Because Fairy Ruopei possessed a [Moon Phase Mirror](<# "Literal CN: 月相镜 (Yuèxiàng Jìng) — Moon Phase Mirror; an ancient divining mirror capable of peering into scenes across the cultivation world">), said to be capable of peering into scenes across various parts of the cultivation realm, everyone begged her to look into what major catastrophe had unfolded in the Central Domain.

This Moon Phase Mirror truly lived up to its illustrious reputation. On its very first activation, it successfully established a connection with [Daojun Bo Jiu](<# "Literal CN: 薄九道君 (Bó Jiǔ Dàojūn) — Daojun Bo Jiu; Void Refinement cultivator of the Huanfei Sect traveling in the Central Domain">), a cultivator of the Huanfei Sect who had been traveling in the Central Domain for many years.

It did not encounter the slightest hindrance.

—"Is this not quite easy to contact?"-Fairy Ruopei remarked to the others.

However, when she looked through the mirror and caught sight of her grand-disciple, she received a massive shock.

This Daojun did not possess the slightest demeanor of a Void Refinement powerhouse. His eye sockets were ringed with deep dark circles, his complexion was deathly pale, and his frame was emaciated—looking as though his vital essence and blood had been completely drained dry by some succubus or vixen demon!

Seeing this, Fairy Ruopei could not help asking:

—"Bo Jiu, what is the meaning of this state?! What happened to you?!"

—"Venerable Grandmaster, you must [redress my grievances](<# "Literal CN: 为我做主 (wèi wǒ zuòzhǔ) — make decisions for me / stand up for me / right my wrongs">)!"-Upon seeing Fairy Ruopei, Bo Jiu burst into a wailing *waaaah*.

A man nearly two thousand years old, bawling like an infant—no matter how one looked at it, it felt somewhat sickening.

Yet Fairy Ruopei felt a twinge of pity in her heart, resolving that no matter what had occurred, she would demand justice for her grand-disciple.

—"Do not panic. Speak slowly. Your Grandmaster will certainly stand up for you!"-Fairy Ruopei promised.

Yet she saw Bo Jiu pull out a thick stack of scrolls and papers, weeping out an aggrieved accusation:

—"I have revised this academic paper [thirteen times](<# "Literal CN: 改了十三遍 (gǎi le shí-sān biàn) — revised thirteen times; the eternal graduate student nightmare of infinite peer review revisions and rejections">)! Thirteen times! But they just won't let me pass! They claim there are too many overlapping topics and my paper lacks novelty, but this topic was clearly conceived by me first and submitted by me first! There must be shady corruption at play here! Grandmaster, you have to help me!"

[—"Ah?"](<# "Speaker: Fairy Ruopei">)

Fairy Ruopei froze on the spot, utterly dumbfounded.
