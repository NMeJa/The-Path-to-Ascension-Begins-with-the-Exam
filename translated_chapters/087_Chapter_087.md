# Chapter 87: The Setting (第87章 设定)

[—"What is that hanging from the mountain gate?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"A... lamb?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"And here I thought only Eldest Senior Brother ever got hung from that tree!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

On this day, the sight at the mountain gate of Heavenly Spirit Sect once again sparked fierce discussion among the disciples.

After all, having the grand ancient peach tree by the mountain gate skewer an Eldest Senior Brother or some monster was a remarkably common occurrence~~(scratch that)~~, but skewering a diminutive lamb was exceedingly rare. Furthermore, a little lamb's deterrence was obviously far smaller than that of an Eldest Senior Brother or a demonic monster; even the disciples of the Beginner Class dared gather beneath the peach tree, chirping and gossiping away.

Whether because it had been a very long time since he was gawked at by so many people, the little lamb wore an expression of sheer humiliation. It was just that all four of his little hooves were tied together and hung from the peach branch, moreover placed under a spell so he couldn't move an inch. Naturally unable to dodge, he could only let tears fall pitter-patter, stubbornly refusing to make a sound.

Seeing the little lamb's pitiful plight, the female disciples of Heavenly Spirit Sect let out sighs of tender pity.

Alas, this peach tree was special. Aside from the Immortal Venerable himself, who could hang things upon it at whim, no one could casually take anything down. They could only sigh in vain.

Confronting this spectacle, this time even the direct disciples uncharacteristically joined the discussion:

[—"Is that the little black lamb that's been clinging to Master lately?"](<# "Speaker: Direct Disciple">)

[—"Why is it up a tree too? Hanging in the exact posture where lighting a fire underneath would make a whole roasted lamb (laugh)."](<# "Speaker: Direct Disciple">)

[—"According to eyewitnesses, half a shichen ago, a child carrying a peach branch larger than himself with a lamb dangling from it walked through the mountain gate. After grafting that branch with the lamb back onto the peach tree, the child vanished."](<# "Speaker: Direct Disciple">)

—"...Could it be Eldest Senior Brother?"-Lu Shulong guessed.

He guessed that Eldest Senior Brother was the "child" who had tied the lamb to the tree.

Lu Shulong never once entertained the thought that the lamb was Eldest Senior Brother, while the "child" was actually his master.

After all, in Lu Shulong's heart, Master was Master. Forget imagining Master transformed into a toddler—he couldn't even picture what Master looked like at the Soul Formation stage.

In Lu Shulong's eyes, his master had been an apocalyptic Tribulation Transcendence titan from the very moment he was born.

Naturally, it wasn't just Lu Shulong who felt this way; the other disciples held much the same impression of Zhou Hui. Consequently, not a single soul realized that the lamb was their Eldest Senior Brother.

That, however, didn't stop Mo Xi from gloating:

—"Eldest Senior Brother has finally returned to normal. He should have done that long ago."

[It's clearly just a sheep, yet it clung to Master all day long—it should have been strung up in the tree to turn into wind-dried mutton jerky!]

—"If Eldest Senior Brother found that sheep an eyesore, wouldn't tossing it straight into a cooking pot have been even better?"-Mo Bei said.

Mo Xi stared at his elder sister in utter disbelief:

—"How could you be so cruel?!"

—"..."

[This younger brother of mine truly is an idiot,] Mo Bei thought.

***

While the disciples were gossiping about the little lamb on the branch, Zhou Hui was currently reviewing Xia Bing's dossier.

As expected, he hadn't quite felt at ease letting Yuwen Xuan handle things, so he had summoned Xia Bing's files to assess the situation.

To his surprise, the other party turned out to be an "acquaintance."

This wasn't to say Zhou Hui knew her personally, but rather that he had seen her name in that damned "Heavenly Dao script."

Xia Bing—one of the Heavenly Dao protagonist's harem members.

Even though Mo Bei had been the protagonist's "first love" and his [official consort](<# "Literal CN: 正宫 (zhènggōng) — primary wife / official consort; in harem web novels, the main, first, or legitimate wife holding supreme status over secondary concubines">) in that story, how could the protagonist of this sort of web novel flirt with only a single girl in any given period? Yet Heavenly Spirit Sect had been bullying the protagonist and was fated for total annihilation, making it ill-suited for the protagonist to cultivate too many indispensable complications there; hence, the protagonist had no choice but to expand outward.

Thousand Beasts Hall, being the closest sect to Heavenly Spirit Sect, was naturally a prime direction for expansion.

However, in that "novel," Thousand Beasts Hall wasn't described in much detail. The sect only existed as a background prop during events like secret realm explorations and the Immortal Magic Conference, without in-depth writing on its internal affairs—which was also one of the reasons Zhou Hui had never paid much attention to Thousand Beasts Hall.

Xia Bing, on the other hand, had been written in great detail.

Not only Xia Bing; regarding Thousand Beasts Hall, there was also a girl named Rong Rong.

The protagonist had encountered Rong Rong in the wild. At the time, this girl was being hunted down by ferocious beasts. Thus, after a textbook display of a hero rescuing a damsel in distress, Rong Rong developed romantic feelings for the protagonist and proactively invited him on an expedition. Through this, the protagonist became entangled with the girls of Thousand Beasts Hall—judging purely from the book's descriptions, one would almost think Thousand Beasts Hall was a sect of moe maidens raising fluffy pets!—and even made the acquaintance of Thousand Beasts Hall's Holy Maiden (?), Xia Bing. He was then invited by her to participate in a certain secret realm trial, where he uncovered an immortal artifact capable of teleportation, which ultimately provided the means for the protagonist to evade the Heavenly Dao Alliance's investigation after he slaughtered the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

It was easy to see: if the Heavenly Dao script was a pure [power-fantasy web novel](<# "Literal CN: 爽文 (shuǎngwén) — feel-good / power-trip web novel designed for instant gratification, face-slapping, and OP progression">), then the girls of Thousand Beasts Hall existed solely to soothe the protagonist's frustration from being stifled at Heavenly Spirit Sect:

*You at Heavenly Spirit Sect look down on me? Well, the sect right next door has hordes of girls falling head over heels for me, their sect heir appreciates me, and they even take me to participate in higher-tier events!*

Consequently, once Heavenly Spirit Sect was annihilated and such contrast was no longer needed, the protagonist promptly forgot these [romantic companions](<# "Literal CN: 红颜知己 (hóngyán zhījǐ) — female confidantes / romantic companions; intimate female friends or romantic interests">) and departed for the Western Domain, then the Southern and Northern Domains. After a series of adventures, the protagonist leveled up and grew stronger. Meanwhile, the Central Domain—where Heavenly Spirit Sect had been wiped out—was plunged into smoky chaos by Mo Xi colluding with the Demon Clan, eventually forming a tense standoff against the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

The protagonist met Xia Bing again on the battlefield against the demons. By that time, Xia Bing had already been corrupted by demonic qi, becoming a high-ranking demonic creature. Yet as if bewitched, she still harbored an undying obsession for the protagonist. In a certain secret realm, she teamed up with the protagonist to backstab everyone, allowing the protagonist to obtain ascension-related elixirs.

Judging from this trajectory, Xia Bing followed the demonic path and should have had a corresponding role in the Immortal-Demon Realm. However, whether the "author"—if that cursed thing even had an author—grew tired of writing her or simply forgot, Xia Bing never made another appearance. The Immortal-Demon Arc ended in a hurried rush: the protagonist slew Zhou Hui's remnant soul, entered the final climactic war, sacrificed Mo Bei to defeat the Demon Venerable who sought to avenge Zhou Hui, swiftly ascended to the True Immortal Realm, and the book came to a complete end.

Based on the initial foreshadowing, Zhou Hui had assumed the protagonist was planning to achieve godhood in the Immortal-Demon Realm before ascending to the True Immortal Realm!

—"No, I cannot view this story entirely as a 'novel.'"-Zhou Hui pinched the bridge of his nose as he reviewed Xia Bing's plotline once more, suddenly sensing something amiss.

In a "novel"—especially a power-fantasy web novel—every item and secondary character existed to serve the protagonist; naturally, there were no good treasures the protagonist missed out on. But with the cultivation world being an actual living world, the phenomenal fortunes the protagonist never obtained were far too numerous to count.

Other places were too remote—such as Blood Demon Palace and White Jade Capital, which was understandable given the early protagonist's lack of strength to discover them. But Thousand Beasts Hall's treatment had been far too "favorable," hadn't it?

Granted, Xia Bing and the other female characters of Thousand Beasts Hall suffered collateral misfortune by flirting with a scumbag for a time, and the entire sect fell to demonic corruption in the late stages to be driven by demons. Yet viewed as a whole, Thousand Beasts Hall hadn't suffered losses in its dealings with the protagonist. Even Xia Bing's scenes with the protagonist were primarily cooperative, and she obtained what she wanted—a starkly different fate from Mo Bei having her bones crushed and [drained to the marrow](<# "Literal CN: 敲骨吸髓 (qiāo gǔ xī suǐ) — smashing the bones and sucking the marrow; ruthlessly and completely exploiting someone to death">).

Yet Thousand Beasts Hall was hardly devoid of grand treasures!

Setting aside everything else, the fact that the Beast Soul Tower—practically a ready-made game dungeon—was never even mentioned was exceedingly bizarre. It wasn't as if the protagonist didn't rear spirit beasts! Not to mention that beneath the Beast Soul Tower lay sealed a drop of True Immortal blood!

Given the Heavenly Dao's greedy habit of wanting to peel a layer of skin off even a stone to feed the protagonist, overlooking the Beast Soul Tower and that drop of True Immortal blood was extraordinarily strange.

—"Could it be that the 'True Immortal blood' served another purpose and was consumed before that?"-Zhou Hui pondered.

That possibility wasn't out of the question. For instance, right now, that drop of True Immortal blood was already in Zhou Hui's hands; once taken, it was gone, and the Heavenly Dao couldn't conjure another drop for the protagonist.

Furthermore, according to the Heavenly Dao's original script, even without Zhou Hui's trip, Thousand Beasts Hall should have been targeted by Yuwen Xuan long ago. He wondered whether Yuwen Xuan's scheming against Xia Bing would still have occurred... Wait, no. According to the original script's progression, Yuwen Xuan had gone to Blood Demon Palace right after the auction; even if he had constantly craved the Beast Soul Tower, he shouldn't have been able to obtain the True Immortal blood, right?

After he left, could someone else have taken over his reckless shenanigans?

—"Master?"

Just as Zhou Hui was thinking up to this point, Qu Qianxing's voice interrupted his contemplation.

Unlike Yuwen Xuan, who barged recklessly into the immortal cave, even though Qu Qianxing knew Zhou Hui's divine sense perpetually enveloped the dwelling, he would still knock upon arriving at the doorway to announce his presence.

Zhou Hui appreciated this about him very much:

—"Enter."

Only then did Qu Qianxing step inside, carrying a stack of scrolls in his arms:

—"Master, these are the relevant papers submitted by the various figures interested in joining the 'Organizing Committee'."

—"Everyone is quite enthusiastic!"-Zhou Hui said happily, instantly setting aside his contemplation of that tangled knot.

While Zhou Hui had been worrying his heart out over his eldest disciple, the preparatory work for the Immortal Magic Conference had by no means ground to a halt.

In fact, progress here was proceeding exceptionally smoothly.

To say nothing of the eager applicants itching for action, even the members of the "Organizing Committee" had erupted with immense zeal.

These cultivation realm titans at the Body Integration and Void Refinement stages put on no airs whatsoever. Not only did they accommodate the Heavenly Spirit Sect's requirements by making all sorts of adjustments, but they also enthusiastically penned one "paper" after another, boisterously occupying the Academic Hall to put forward countless opinions regarding the formatting specifications of the entire conference's theses.

In a cultivation world where every sect and faction traditionally looked out only for itself, this was an almost unbelievable spectacle.

In response, Qu Qianxing said respectfully:

—"...Master's methods are truly formidable."

—"I didn't do anything at all."

—"Merely by proposing the model of academic presentations, you seized those grandmasters right by their vital artery!"-Qu Qianxing's tone grew even more reverent.-"As expected of Master!"

Presumably, even those grandmasters had never anticipated they would harbor such passion today, had they?

***

—"Academic presentations? Doesn't that only begin when the 'Scholarly Contest' commences?"-Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa was likewise inquiring with furrowed brows at that very moment.

Unlike those news-deprived Mahayana cultivators who remained oblivious even when their disciples and grand-disciples triggered colossal catastrophes, Villa Master Qingyang had always paid close attention to affairs both grand and petty across the cultivation realm. Consequently, not only had he learned immediately when the Untiring Immortal Venerable obliterated Sovereign Immortal Chana of the Carefree Palace, but he also knew the specific details of the secret realm and the auction, and had keenly observed the shifting winds across the Central Domain's cultivation world.

Thus, even though his own estate had never provoked the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Villa Master Qingyang had followed the crowd to pay New Year greetings to the Untiring Immortal Venerable, displayed immense enthusiasm toward Immortal Chess, secured the "Mahayana Representative Slot" for the inaugural Immortal Magic Conference, and recommended numerous talents for the "Organizing Committee."

Nevertheless, Villa Master Qingyang had also been well aware that many cultivators were likely rather unwilling.

By the traditions of the cultivation world, cultivators were generally reluctant to meddle in affairs that had nothing to do with them and offered no personal benefit, even if it were serving as a judge.

Described charitably, this attitude was "aloofness from fame and fortune"; put bluntly, it was "[sweeping the snow from one's own doorstep](<# "Literal CN: 各人自扫门前雪，休管他人瓦上霜 — everyone sweeps the snow from their own doorstep and ignores the frost on others' tiles; minding one's own business">)"—the normal state of affairs in any case.

Villa Master Qingyang hadn't particularly cared whether those "friends" of his were truly willing to come. His recommendations had merely been a gesture to display his support for the Immortal Magic Conference.

What left him dumbfounded, however, was that while those cultivators had initially appeared reluctant, once they arrived at Heavenly Spirit Sect, it was as if they had been brainwashed by the Untiring Immortal Venerable! Not a single one declined; what was more, they even began squabbling over the quota of seats on the "Organizing Committee"! This ended up making Villa Master Qingyang, who had penned a whole stack of recommendation letters, look like the villain of the piece, prompting quite a few people to write letters lodging complaints against him.

Villa Master Qingyang had had his disciples inquire for ages without reaching any clear conclusion. In the end, taking advantage of his status as a conference participant, he came in person to see for himself.

This time, however, it was an "[incognito inspection](<# "Literal CN: 微服私访 (wēifú sīfǎng) — an emperor or high official touring common areas in ordinary clothes to observe reality firsthand without pomp or warning">)." He had merely submitted an ordinary visiting card without alarming the Untiring Immortal Venerable—mainly because that Immortal Venerable's power was far too overwhelming, leaving him distinctly uncomfortable in his presence. Instead, he entered normally through the mountain gate and spent a long while studying that little black lamb hanging from the mountain gate's peach tree... That was a "sheep," right? Given the sensory perception of Villa Master Qingyang's Mahayana cultivation, something felt faintly amiss.

—"Stop staring, I hear it's a Heavenly Spirit Sect tradition,"-Hermit Zui Liu said with a hearty chuckle as he came out to welcome Villa Master Qingyang.-"Word has it that disciples with poor academic performance get hung from the tree."

—"Is this 'lamb' one as well?"-Villa Master Qingyang asked.

—"Who knows? Perhaps its meat isn't flavorful enough?"-Hermit Zui Liu replied.

—"Oh!"

Villa Master Qingyang glanced once more at that little lamb shedding tears pitter-patter under the gaze of the surrounding disciples.

So this was the Untiring Immortal Venerable's method of disciplining disciples? Come to think of it, that Immortal Venerable's approach was probably far more effective than conventional methods! It didn't qualify as overly severe corporal punishment, yet under the public gaze of fellow disciples, one would surely reflect on one's errors with far greater depth, wouldn't they? Quite excellent indeed.

Several disciples following behind Villa Master Qingyang shivered involuntarily for reasons they couldn't comprehend.

Fortunately, Villa Master Qingyang had [fasted from grain](<# "Literal CN: 辟谷 (bìgǔ) — inedia / grain fasting; Daoist practice of abstaining from mortal food, sustaining life purely on spiritual qi">) for ages and had no particular craving for mutton. After watching for a brief moment, he walked inward alongside Hermit Zui Liu, asking about the matter of the "academic presentations."

The moment Hermit Zui Liu heard this, he immediately demanded in cross-examination:

—"I was just about to ask you! My lord Villa Master, just how many people did you recommend to serve as judges?! You've made things so competitive that we now have to [compete for posts](<# "Literal CN: 竞争上岗 (jìngzhēng shànggǎng) — corporate and civil service appointment via open, merit-based performance competition">)!"

[Compete for posts? What on earth does that mean?]

Villa Master Qingyang wondered in bewilderment.

Before he could ask, he saw Hermit Zui Liu grin foolishly:

—"Fortunately, my lecture the day before yesterday was a resounding success! I've already been upgraded from the small seminar room to the grand hall, securing a confirmed spot."

—"???"

Villa Master Qingyang listened in utter confusion. Only upon arriving at the Academic Hall did he finally understand what was going on.

As it turned out, upon arriving at Heavenly Spirit Sect, the other cultivators—just like Hermit Zui Liu—had been introduced to the new report-writing format for the very first time, and every single one felt it was marvelous.

To popularize this new model, Zhou Hui had arranged several presentation sessions for his own disciples, selecting relatively intriguing topics for these seniors to critique and evaluate.

However, having watched so many, it was inevitable that someone's hands would itch to try.

The first to take the stage was a visiting Void Refinement cultivator who gave a presentation on minor techniques for refining pills.

His cultivation was naturally higher than that of Heavenly Spirit Sect's disciples, and the subject matter he discussed was slightly deeper. Yet his topic selection and presentation skills were utterly shambolic, inevitably giving everyone in the audience the distinct impression: [‘If he can do it, I can do it too!’](<# "Literal CN: 我上我也行 (wǒ shàng wǒ yě xíng) — internet gaming/sports meme: 'If you put me in coach, even I could do that!' expressing confidence that one could easily match or surpass someone else's mediocre performance">) This ended up provoking the other grandmasters far more than the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples' lectures ever had.

Thus, on the second day, three grandmasters gave it a shot.

On the third day, five grandmasters took to the podium.

...

By the fifth day, even if the main podium were scheduled across all twelve double-hours of the day, it was no longer enough.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect, however, appeared to have long anticipated this development. They fully opened the Academic Hall, categorized the presentation topics by discipline, distributed them across different seminar rooms, and published the various lecture topics and schedules in advance, delivering them to the various cultivators' temporary guest caves early in the morning so everyone could choose which ones to attend.

While this service was remarkably convenient, it also cleanly drew the line between fascinating topics and dull ones, brilliant presentations and atrocious ones. Human nature being what it was, nothing terrified people more than comparison—and even Body Integration and Void Refinement titans were no exception. Consequently, an implicit rivalry began brewing beneath the surface.

At this juncture, even more cultivators arrived—some were invited seniors, while others were disciples summoned by the earlier arrivals to come and learn—making the rivalry in the Academic Hall livelier and far more public.

What finally drove the competition into a white-hot frenzy was the realization that the "Organizing Committee" was oversubscribed.

At first, nobody had wanted to come, and everyone assumed others wouldn't want to come either, so recommendation letters had flown about willy-nilly. Yet once they rushed over and saw how fascinating it was, none of them wanted to leave! After all the back-and-forth, the roster of the Organizing Committee ended up vastly exceeding the quota.

In the end, everyone pooled their heads together and simply decided: the final roster of the Organizing Committee would be determined based on the popularity of their academic presentations.

—"...You lot are certainly having quite a grand time!"-Villa Master Qingyang marveled with a sigh.

Gazing at the topic board in the Academic Hall, he spotted quite a few fascinating titles and felt an urge to sit down and listen to a few sessions himself.

Come to think of it, this crowd's primary objective probably wasn't some mere "Organizing Committee" quota at all, but rather this very Academic Hall!

At the Void Refinement and Body Integration stages, it was exceedingly rare to encounter such a peaceful (?) and harmonious (?) academic discussion atmosphere! (The term "academic discussion atmosphere" was one Villa Master Qingyang had only just learned!)

Hermit Zui Liu, of course, would never admit to that:

—"We are conducting serious scholarly discourse!"

To prove his point, he even rummaged through the topic board, declaring:

—"Look! Our topics primarily revolve around the specialized research fields of yourself and the Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum. Only by debating them clearly and thoroughly can we properly evaluate others when serving as judges in the future."

After all, ostensibly, this was still the Immortal Magic Conference!

The two Mahayana representatives were Villa Master Qingyang and the Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum. Therefore, according to the rules, the Scholarly Contest would use their respective primary areas of study as subject matter, accepting submissions from participants.

Hearing this, Villa Master Qingyang's interest was piqued.

He hadn't expected there was such a way to play this.

Although Villa Master Qingyang had known about the Scholarly Contest long ago, he had always felt it had little to do with him. After all, aside from claiming ancestral ties to White Jade Capital of the ancient cultivation world, he and the Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had zero common ground.

The "Dao" of the two held no conflicting points, yet neither did they share any correlation. Even if they wished to debate, there was nothing to debate.

Thus, Villa Master Qingyang had always regarded it as merely a promotional gimmick for the tournament.

After all, low-tier cultivators listening to high-tier cultivators expound the Dao could also boost their cultivation. As long as Villa Master Qingyang were willing to lecture, there would be people willing to camp outside his mountain estate—even if they had to wait for a decade—and they would likely even come to blows over seating spots.

With such a gimmick, even if the Immortal Magic Conference were an utter disaster, people would still flock to participate.

But for Villa Master Qingyang, there was essentially no benefit.

While expounding the Dao could indeed accumulate hidden karmic virtue, that was predicated on the recipients being genuinely willing to accept this "Dao." Coming merely to boost cultivation was practically equivalent to [freeloading](<# "Literal CN: 白嫖 (bái piáo) — modern slang for getting goods, services, or knowledge for free without contributing or paying">).

Therefore, aside from certain powerhouses whose cultivation methods were inherently meant for widespread dissemination, Mahayana titans like Villa Master Qingyang who led niche sects like Eternal Villa generally had no desire to expound the Dao.

His agreement this time had purely been out of respect for the Untiring Immortal Venerable—an act of adding icing to the cake.

—After all, as a gimmick, if the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself gave a lecture, it would attract far more people.

But now that this "Topic Selection" system had emerged, everything was different. Even if only to win the tournament, people would actively study his, Qingyang's, Dao! This immediately stirred Villa Master Qingyang's enthusiasm.

He took another careful look at the topics, only for his facial expression to instantly collapse.

—"Why do I feel none of these have anything to do with me?"-Villa Master Qingyang roasted.

If one had to say, among these topics, there were far more regarding the Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's "Epigraphy and Bronze-Stone Inscription Studies"!

After all, cultivation in this era relied on archaeology; having explored numerous secret realms, everyone possessed some research into ancient scripts and steles, so they could more or less produce something. But what on earth was Eternal Villa's cultivation technique even about?

Hermit Zui Liu, considering himself a close friend of Villa Master Qingyang, felt he understood him thoroughly:

—"Though they aren't numerous, aren't this one, this one, and that one all related? However, I feel their research is a bit too shallow. Now MY topic, *A Study on the History of Folding Fans in White Jade Capital*, is what truly connects with your estate!"

This was also the very reason Hermit Zui Liu had summoned Villa Master Qingyang over.

When it came to evaluating content, the actual subject in question was naturally the ultimate authority!

Villa Master Qingyang was infuriated to the point of laughing at this title:

—"In your mind, what on earth is our Eternal Villa?!"

—"A [folding fan spirit](<# "Literal CN: 折扇精 (zhéshàn jīng) — folding fan spirit / demon essence; jokingly accusing someone of being an inanimate folding fan that cultivated into a demon">)?"

...

[—"My lords, do not fight here!"](<# "Speaker: Academic Hall Attendant / Disciple">)

[—"That's right! This is the Academic Hall, fighting is strictly forbidden!"](<# "Speaker: Academic Hall Attendant / Disciple">)

***

—"...And so, Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa has changed his mind. He wishes to deliver the inaugural academic presentation right when the Immortal Magic Conference begins,"-Qu Qianxing reported to Zhou Hui.

Originally, Villa Master Qingyang had planned to deliver his academic lecture after the grand finals of the Immortal Magic Conference, feeling that appearing as the "grand finale" would grant him far more prestige.

Clearly, he could no longer hold back.

To this, Zhou Hui was utterly unbothered:

—"Such great academic enthusiasm. Splendid."

[Master, are you truly not doing this on purpose?]

Qu Qianxing roasted inwardly. Turning to Zhou Hui, he asked:

—"Then, Master, should I convey the requirements for the inaugural presentation to Lord Villa Master Qingyang now?"

—"That is f... Wait,"-Zhou Hui had initially intended to let everything proceed according to plan, but remembering the vexing matters from earlier, he suddenly changed his mind.

First came Blood Demon Palace, followed by Thousand Beasts Hall.

Zhou Hui had never been interested in the so-called "protagonist's" movements because he was certain his true enemy was merely the Heavenly Dao. Even without this "protagonist," the Heavenly Dao could easily find another "protagonist."

That was why Zhou Hui had adopted a semi-slacking strategy.

Yet after lying flat (?) for over half a year, Zhou Hui suddenly realized that the novel was merely the surface; behind it lay the entire cultivation world, where pulling a single hair moved the whole body. Continuously waiting for events to unfold without understanding the underlying causes and effects was rather frustrating. He might as well treat it as studying the [lore sourcebook](<# "Literal CN: 设定集 (shèdìng jí) — setting bible / lore sourcebook containing worldbuilding rules, character profiles, and lore details in fiction/gaming">)—at the very least, he would grasp the complete picture of affairs!

—"Eternal Villa, correct? I seem to recall the '[Red Rose](<# "Literal CN: 红玫瑰 (Hóng Méigui) — literary trope from Eileen Chang's novel 'Red Rose, White Rose', symbolizing the passionate, tempestuous lover in contrast to the gentle White Rose">)' was from his family,"-Zhou Hui muttered in words Qu Qianxing couldn't comprehend. Then, he instructed:-"Please invite Villa Master Qingyang over for a visit. I wish to gain a deeper understanding of his cultivation technique and the history of Eternal Villa."
