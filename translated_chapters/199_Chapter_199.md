# Chapter 199: Reputation

[—"Didn't you claim that you were the greatest artifact refiner?"](<# "Speaker: Lin Qi">)

[—"How is it that you can't even best a Foundation Establishment junior?"](<# "Speaker: Lin Qi">)

—"Is this what you call the 'greatest'?!"-Lin Qi demanded in blistering fury.

On the surface, his temperament appeared far gentler than Ren Ran's. Yet when he truly lost his temper, he was infinitely more terrifying than the blustering, posturing Ren Ran. At the very least, Guan Xinguang felt as though his entire skull were vibrating violently, giving him the distinct sensation that his head would detonate in the very next instant.

This only made Guan Xinguang's already muddled brain even more utterly bewildered:

[Indeed!]

[Why did things turn out like this?!]

[According to the plan, the one disrupted by the spiritual energy field—the one who should have plunged from the sky—was supposed to be that brat!]

Yes, the "plan."

Guan Xinguang had deliberately disrupted the spiritual energy field to orchestrate this very accident.

Only through an accident could his examination platform interact directly with Guan Ye's. Only then could he make a move on Guan Ye, following Lin Qi's and Ren Ran's demands to plant a 'True Immortal Mark' into Guan Ye's body so that they could extract the boy's Dao Bone down the line and forge a suitable physical vessel for Lin Qi.

Furthermore, Guan Xinguang harbored his own petty calculations:

First, Guan Xinguang believed that if he intentionally blew the matter up into a massive spectacle, the Untiring Immortal Venerable might find it difficult to pursue him for it.

After all, in his view, once Guan Ye was branded with the 'True Immortal Mark,' regardless of whether the boy truly possessed a Dao Bone or not, his spine would subsequently be ripped out once more. That would undoubtedly provoke the Untiring Immortal Venerable into thunderous fury.

He didn't know whether this Heavenly General Lin Qi was truly powerful enough—whether he could actually slay the Untiring Immortal Venerable as he had so boastfully bragged.

Yet even if Lin Qi were genuinely that formidable, a Tribulation Transcendence titan was hardly that easy to kill.

Never mind Tribulation Transcendents—hadn't Guan Xinguang himself been slain once by Untiring? Even a Mahayana cultivator like him could reconstruct his physical body; with the Untiring Immortal Venerable's terrifying might, even if he were temporarily slain, as long as his soul went into hiding until Lin Qi and the others departed, he could basically [resurrect with full health](# "Literal CN: 满血复活 (mǎnxiě fùhuó) — gamer slang for reviving with a full health bar").

In the worst-case scenario, what if the spine ripped from Guan Ye turned out not to be a Dao Bone once again?

When that time came, the Untiring Immortal Venerable would have lost his disciple—with a Foundation Establishment cultivator's fragile flesh and soul, Zhou Hui saving Guan Ye once was already an astonishing feat, but reconstructing a spine twice was out of the question; even if the flesh could endure it, the soul would shatter under the agony. Hence, Guan Xinguang firmly believed that if Guan Ye's bone were ripped out a second time, the boy would be dead without a shadow of a doubt. The Immortal Venerable would inevitably be incandescent with rage. The Venerable might not be able to seek vengeance against Lin Qi and Ren Ran, but in his heart, Guan Xinguang was already a prime suspect; [venting his fury](# "Literal CN: 迁怒 (qiānnù) — redirecting anger onto an easier target or scapegoat") upon him was an absolute certainty.

No matter how Guan Xinguang turned it over in his mind, he felt he could never escape the fate of being liquidated by the Untiring Immortal Venerable in the future.

[In that case, I might as well blow the whole affair wide open!]

In any event, if Guan Ye's spine were extracted a second time, the Untiring Immortal Venerable would still have "no evidence." Even if Untiring flew into a berserk rage and butchered Guan Xinguang once more, if he wished to obliterate Guan Xinguang's body and soul entirely, with Guan Xinguang's prestigious status and vast network as Chief Director of the Department of Works, the Heavenly Dao Alliance and the entire cultivation world would unquestionably stand on Guan Xinguang's side, making it awkward for Untiring to make a move.

Didn't you see how miserably that scoundrel Sikong Yao had been hunted back then? Yet wasn't he currently seated safe and sound right beside the Untiring Immortal Venerable?

Evidently, even Untiring had to give face to public opinion!

Second, Guan Xinguang desired a quick, decisive resolution.

He felt that being forced to participate in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's examination as a dignified Mahayana cultivator was already humiliating and disgraceful enough. If he couldn't secure victory with an overwhelming, crushing advantage, he would become the laughingstock of the realm.

Such an overwhelming advantage, naturally, could not be a mere ordinary perfect score.

Just a 'perfect score'? How could that possibly showcase the supreme prowess of the Chief Director of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works?!

As the realm's foremost master of artifact refinement, he had to deliver a performance that thoroughly crushed every single candidate—a performance that proved all other examinees were unworthy of even sharing the same testing arena with him! Only that would suffice to command the mortal realm's worship and awe.

Therefore, regardless of who his opponent was, Guan Xinguang had planned from the very outset to forge a magical treasure capable of generating an immense spiritual resonance, creating a suffocating spiritual energy field to blast every examination platform in the arena straight out of the sky! That way, he would achieve a visual, undeniable 'first place' without even needing a numerical score.

Moreover, the faster this occurred, the better.

Ideally, he would ignite the spiritual energy field shortly after the examination commenced.

For Guan Xinguang, the sooner this exam ended, the more effectively everyone would recognize his 'master stature.'

If the publicity and narrative were spun properly, people wouldn't condemn him for disrupting other candidates' exams; they would only claim that other examinees were wholly unqualified to compete on the same stage as the Chief Director of the Department of Works!

No—from the very beginning, Untiring had zero qualifications to host an artifact refiner qualification examination in the first place!

Consequently, even if Untiring pursued Guan Xinguang afterward under the premise of "having no evidence"—even if Untiring's disciple perished—the public would merely view it as Untiring venting his petty humiliation after having his and his disciple's faces thoroughly dragged through the dirt during the qualification exam.

Such was Guan Xinguang's line of reasoning.

[Ah, that's right! After 'aweing' the entire examination hall, I should casually and magnanimously declare that I disdain to construct any large-scale teleportation array, nor do I believe a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator who cannot even pass his own sect's qualification exam could possibly draft legitimate blueprints for one.]

[With such a display, if Untiring tries to cause trouble for me in the future, people will only view him as petty and narrow-minded.]

[Meanwhile, my reputation as a grandmaster of artifact refinement will only soar to even greater heights!]

Reaching this delightful realization in his thoughts, Guan Xinguang had let out a cold sneer.

During these past days cooped up inside the treasure ship, he had spent all his waking hours daydreaming about precisely this brand of delusions.

It was precisely because he harbored such grand designs that, the moment he ignited his mutated flame, Guan Xinguang had shoved all his pre-selected materials—including Ten-Thousand-Year Dark Iron and Thousand-Year Moon Essence—into the refining cauldron in one reckless heap. Furthermore, he had deployed amplifying magical artifacts, cranking the furnace fire up to its absolute maximum.

Afterward, everything had proceeded with utmost smoothness.

Under the blessing of karmic fortune, Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field had expanded to its zenith, slamming squarely into Guan Ye's examination platform in a single stroke.

Yet!

Yet what Guan Xinguang could never have anticipated in his wildest dreams was that when the two spiritual fields violently collided, the one trembling and plummeting straight out of the sky wasn't Guan Ye—it was his own examination platform!

[Why did it turn out like this?!]

Guan Xinguang thought in his mind, his expression violently contorting... internally.

His sanity had not entirely unraveled; he still remembered that he was under the scrutiny of the public eye.

More critically, shaken by the impact of the colliding spiritual fields, fractures had surfaced across the defensive array enclosing his platform. This caused the illusory walls—designed to help examinees concentrate—to flicker unsteadily, allowing him to faintly glimpse the external spectator stands.

Although he couldn't clearly see the expressions of the titans in the spectator gallery, the mere consciousness of their presence was enough to give Guan Xinguang a cerebral hemorrhage.

[This makes no sense!] he roared internally. [Even setting aside technical skill and speaking purely of raw power, no Foundation Establishment junior—no matter how monstrously gifted—could possibly shake the spiritual energy field forged by a Mahayana cultivator!]

[He doesn't even attempt to defend his own craftsmanship?!]

[Prior to the exam, the man was proudly proclaiming himself the realm's number-one artifact refiner.]

[Or could it be... he was well aware all along that his skills were inferior to others?!]

Lin Qi thought in utter disgust, his sensation of having been swindled deepening by the second.

How could he have known that while Guan Xinguang went around styling himself a "Grandmaster of Artifact Refinement" and the "Number-One Artifact Refiner in This Realm," in Guan Xinguang's own eyes, those two titles had zero correlation with actual forging capability?

The Department of Works of the Heavenly Dao Alliance operated as a semi-official institution in the cultivation world, naturally attracting hordes of artifact refiners to its ranks. Guan Xinguang had long grown sick of seeing teenage prodigies and monstrous talents. There were even individuals like Guan Ye—of utterly mediocre aptitude, yet confirmed by a Celestial Edict to possess a Dao Bone! But so what? Once they entered the Department of Works, wouldn't they still have to obediently toil beneath the Chief Director, forging whatever the Chief Director ordered them to forge and stamping the Department of Works' name onto their proudest masterpieces?

Over the years, Guan Xinguang had long treated the mantle of 'Grandmaster of Artifact Refinement' as merely a component of personal reputation management. Just like this time: while figuring out how to survive, he was simultaneously calculating how to gild his own prestige in gold.

Yet to the descended Lin Qi, none of this mattered in the slightest.

This was not to say that True Immortals were inherently noble, or that similar bureaucratic nonsense did not exist within the Heavenly Court. Rather, when an issue did not personally affect someone, humans naturally tended to perch themselves atop the moral high ground.

Lin Qi was thoroughly nauseated by this useless trash Guan Xinguang, yet for the moment, this insect was the only tool at his disposal.

—"Maintain the spiritual energy field!"-Lin Qi barked at Guan Xinguang in command.-"Useless trash—must this General intervene in person?!"

—"This..."-Guan Xinguang glanced at the refining cauldron in complete disarray, hesitating slightly.

After being battered and dropped like this, the materials inside the cauldron had unquestionably suffered severe disruptions. Even with miraculous luck, even if he could force the refinement to continue, the resulting quality of the forged artifact would inevitably be compromised.

In such a situation, the optimal choice was to [cut one's losses in time](# "Literal CN: 及时止损 (jíshí zhǐsǔn) — stop-loss; mitigating damage before it spirals").

As long as he claimed that his refinement had not truly begun before being disrupted by his opponent, he could terminate the exam. Even if suspicions lingered in everyone's hearts, most would chalk it up as an unforeseen accident. Conversely, if a flawed magical treasure were forged, regardless of the extenuating circumstances, public opinion would only remember that he had crafted a defective artifact—even though this entire debacle was already...

*Hiss!*

Guan Xinguang let out an agonized, muffled gasp.

He felt as though an invisible hand had ruthlessly squeezed his brain. His skull was not merely buzzing—it felt as if it were about to shatter into pieces.

Yet before Guan Xinguang could even comprehend the source of his agony, the Lord Heavenly General's chilling voice rang out:

—"I ordered you to maintain the spiritual energy field and stay interlocked with your opponent—do you not understand human speech?!"-Lin Qi's voice snarled.

Evidently, just like being forced to participate in the examination, there was zero room for choice.

—"Had I known you were such worthless trash—styling yourself a grandmaster of artifact refinement, yet unable to suppress a mere Foundation Establishment refiner—I never should have granted you the faculty of thought!"-Lin Qi's sinister voice continued.

A dense wave of goosebumps instantly erupted across the back of Guan Xinguang's neck.

Wiping away someone's thoughts, controlling their mind—why were this Lord Heavenly General's abilities so chillingly demonic?

Guan Xinguang dared not ponder it deeply, nor did he wish to.

Never had he realized more acutely than at this very moment just what a terrifying nightmare it was to allow such a "True Immortal" to possess his body.

—"L-Lord Heavenly General, spare my life! I will do it immediately! Right away!"-Guan Xinguang stammered in panic, throwing himself before the refining cauldron.

What brought him immense relief was that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's testing formations had clearly been laid out with exquisite precision. Even with an examination platform plummeting from midair, no catastrophic damage had occurred; the raw materials inside the cauldron had only suffered minor imperfections, barely allowing the refinement to continue.

Guan Xinguang's artifact refinement had always adhered to the philosophy that [sufficient thrust can make a brick fly](# "Literal CN: 力大砖飞 (lì dà zhuān fēi) — with enough brute force, even a brick can achieve flight; relying on raw power and overwhelming resources rather than delicate technique"). He hastily tossed in a handful of high-tier repair materials, readjusted the number of amplifying magical implements, and cranked the mutated flame to its absolute highest tier.

In an instant, the spiritual energy surrounding Platform Six—where Guan Xinguang stood—erupted into violent chaos once more.

\*

—"Splendid! Platform Six has stabilized! Chief Director Guan appears completely unharmed!"-Fairy Maogui commentated.

When the platform plunged just moments ago, she had feared the absolute worst!

Having such a catastrophic mishap occur during the very first qualification examination was, to some degree, a public scandal. Who could have expected that the Heavenly Spirit Sect seemed to have anticipated this very scenario from the beginning? The defensive arrays across the arena had functioned with rock-solid dependability, steadily catching and arresting the falling Platform Six while neutralizing the shockwaves generated by the clash aloft, preventing them from even disturbing the platforms below.

Fairy Maogui let out a massive sigh of relief, remarking with a hint of admiration:

—"Chief Director Guan appears exceedingly persistent as well; harboring no intention of giving up, he has already resumed refining his artifact,"-Fairy Maogui observed.

Fairy Maogui's praise carried no deeper agenda.

She had previously possessed little contact with the artifact refinement sphere and was largely unacquainted with Guan Xinguang. She merely felt a natural fondness for the virtue of perseverance in the face of adversity; thus, viewing Guan Xinguang's actions at face value, she had voiced an innocent compliment without understanding the context.

Yet the only people capable of feeling admiration were likely those who, like her, were completely in the dark. Refiners familiar with Guan Xinguang wore expressions of profound bewilderment:

[Is Guan Xinguang truly the kind of person who perseveres?]

"Regrettably," those who knew Guan Xinguang constituted the vast majority present; consequently, murmurs began to ripple among the artifact refiners.

Even setting them aside, seeing that the one who plunged from the sky was the platform of a Mahayana titan rather than a Foundation Establishment junior, even bystanders entirely oblivious to the backstory couldn't help harboring suspicion and voicing criticism amidst their confusion.

A commotion began to stir across the spectator stands.

Fairy Maogui wasn't entirely privy to the mood of the spectators in the stands, but she herself had numerous questions awaiting answers.

Attempting to keep the conversation flowing with further praise, Fairy Maogui remarked:

—"Still, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's defensive formations truly command newfound respect. It gives one the impression that no matter what transpires, the exam can proceed uninterrupted..."-Fairy Maogui began.

—"It is no mere impression; no matter what transpires, the examination can indeed proceed,"-Ran Deng stated.-"When construction on the Buyan Palace testing arena concluded, Master personally conducted safety tests. Even if heavenly tribulation lightning were to strike, it would not disrupt the examination's progress."

[...Just how obsessive is the Immortal Venerable about examinations?!]

Fairy Maogui felt intensely fortunate that she was not a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Yet amidst her relief, she felt a twinge of envy, musing that Moon-Welcoming Valley ought to commission a matching set of such arrays.

Not for conducting examinations, of course, but because cauldron blowouts were a routine occupational hazard in Moon-Welcoming Valley; they genuinely required stronger safety measures.

—"Your Excellency Ran Deng, I have heard that most of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's architecture was designed and constructed under your supervision?"-Fairy Maogui inquired further.

—"Most of it was, yes. However, Buyan Palace was overseen by my Youngest Junior Brother; I was only responsible for designing the formation arrays for this testing arena,"-Ran Deng nodded in explanation.

—"As expected of the scion of a renowned array lineage,"-Fairy Maogui's eyes brightened considerably; this was precisely what she had wanted to ask.-"This array setup seems remarkably targeted. Did you anticipate this very scenario?"

—"Indeed. However, my own research into artifact refinement is rather shallow, so my Fourth Junior Brother—the one currently down in the testing arena, Guan Ye—drafted a detailed list for me outlining every potential emergency that could arise during the forging process. Using that as a foundation, I designed the arrays,"-Ran Deng explained.-"This formation not only defends against unforeseen accidents, but can also display real-time telemetry from across the testing grounds."

Saying so, she snapped her fingers toward the examination arena.

In response to Ran Deng's gesture, multicolored rays of light illuminated the arena, dazzling and resplendent. Looking closely, one could see floating textual descriptions and numerical metrics annotating the quantity, density, and origins of diverse energies—spiritual energy, baleful qi, demonic qi, and more.

—"How magnificent!"-Fairy Maogui marveled in praise.-"Crystal clear at a single glance."

—"You flatter me; someone of your caliber could have sensed it naturally, could you not, Fairy?"-Ran Deng offered a bashful smile.-"I merely took the liberty of adding a few modest features to make it more convenient for debriefing and breaking down exams for our disciples in the future."

—"Having it active right now is exceedingly convenient as well,"-Fairy Maogui chuckled.

[*[Keikaku doori](# "Literal CN: 计划通 (jìhuà tōng) — Japanese loan phrase meaning 'all according to plan'").*]

The smile on Ran Deng's face deepened.

In truth, even had Fairy Maogui not brought it up, she had already planned to activate this 'Observation Array.'

It wasn't that Ran Deng had rigged any deceptive tricks into the formation. Just as Ran Deng had confirmed with Fairy Maogui, high-tier cultivators like Maogui did not require the assistance of such an array; relying purely on their own spiritual senses, they could effortlessly discern the movement of spiritual energies within the testing grounds.

Yet that was a 'subjective' perception.

No matter how high high-tier cultivators cultivated—even if they attained True Immortality—they were still human beings at their core. And being human meant harboring biases, likes, and dislikes. Thus, although they possessed the ability to perceive everything, they were inevitably influenced by their own moods, seeing only what they wished to see. According to a book Ran Deng had read a couple of days ago, this phenomenon seemed to be called an [information cocoon](# "Literal CN: 信息茧房 (xìnxī jiǎnfáng) — echo chamber / filter bubble")?

Take just now, for instance: had these high-tier powerhouses simultaneously monitored the movements of both Guan Ye and Guan Xinguang while cross-referencing the spiritual shockwaves they produced, predicting the violent collision of spiritual fields would have been elementary. Regrettably, no one had paid attention.

Or rather, perhaps someone had noticed, but refrained from speaking up.

Ran Deng's Observation Array eliminated such loopholes, laying out the spiritual energy flows and quantitative data in crystal-clear fashion, forcing people to take note of what lay outside their conscious focus. To accomplish this, Ran Deng had even borrowed a portion of Langhuan's divine power to bolster her array.

Yet Ran Deng hadn't done this out of the goodness of her heart.

She had zero intention of allowing people to view the examination objectively. On the contrary, she wanted to steer this crowd toward looking at what *she* wanted them to look at, dragging matters that would otherwise never be spoken aloud straight onto the tabletop for open debate.

For instance: Guan Xinguang's "artifact refinement technique," as well as his character and integrity.

The reason Guan Xinguang had been able to bounce around alive and kicking to this day after ripping out Guan Ye's spine—and the reason Guan Ye spouted foolish nonsense like wanting to resolve this with his own hands so Master wouldn't have to intervene—wasn't it purely because Guan Ye worried that rashly slaying the man would cause certain fools to misunderstand Master due to Guan Xinguang's exalted reputation and Chief Director title, potentially driving a wedge between Master and the Meizhu couple?

—Although in Ran Deng's view, Master didn't care about such trivialities in the slightest; only Guan Ye tormented himself over them.

In that case, she might as well obliterate those protective shields entirely!

That was the true reason Ran Deng had seized Yuwen Xuan and insisted that he drag Guan Xinguang here to take the exam.

Speaking of which, where was that bastard Yuwen Xuan?

Ran Deng suddenly recalled something, bolting upright and scanning the testing arena with wide eyes.

Theoretically, Yuwen Xuan had been constantly babbling about some "sumptuous feast" and tailing Ren Ran's vicinity—he wouldn't actually try to devour Ren Ran, would he? That was 'food' strictly forbidden by Master!—so he naturally shouldn't be absent from this exam. Yet ever since the examination began, not a single trace of his figure had been seen. This made Ran Deng feel as though a ticking time bomb had been planted in the arena, liable to detonate at any given second.

Regrettably, Ran Deng ultimately failed to locate Yuwen Xuan.

Instead, Fairy Maogui was startled by Ran Deng's abrupt movement, glancing at her curiously:

—"What is the matter, Your Excellency Ran Deng?"-Fairy Maogui asked.

—"Ah, nothing! I merely felt that the spiritual fields in the testing grounds are rather dim, making it difficult to see clearly, ahaha..."-Ran Deng stammered with a guilty conscience.

—"Now that you mention it, that is indeed true!"-Fairy Maogui harbored no suspicions, turning her gaze toward the arena with furrowed brows.-"An exceptionally magnificent spiritual field. It appears to emanate primarily from Chief Director Guan's platform."

Ran Deng feigned innocence and directed her gaze toward Guan Xinguang's platform as well.

Just as Fairy Maogui noted, once the spiritual energy field was visually manifested, one could observe a halo of violet luminescence radiating outward from Guan Xinguang's platform, plunging the entire testing grounds into an eerie gloom. Even with defensive arrays dampening the shockwaves, the neighboring platforms were still dragged into the vortex, causing several artifact refiners on adjacent platforms to commit operational errors due to the turbulent spiritual interference.

And as though unsatisfied, that spiritual energy field continued to creep upward like a massive shadowy hand, attempting to engulf Platform Forty-Four above—which was radiating a golden-crimson glow like a morning sun that had yet to fully rise.

Visually, it evoked profound discomfort in anyone watching.

Witnessing this scene, a glint of chilling frost flashed across Ran Deng's eyes.

[It seems this fellow is actively courting death once more!] Ran Deng didn't show her thoughts on her face, reflecting internally: [Then again, today's progress feels a bit too ridiculously smooth. I haven't even done anything yet, and he's already detonating himself. At this rate, if I'm even a second slower, His Excellency the Chief Director might just manage to off himself before I can even strike.]

[No, for someone like that, even if he dies, he must die with his reputation thoroughly in ruins!]

—"We cannot allow his filthy blood to splatter Fourth Junior Brother instead!"-Ran Deng muttered under her breath.

Reaching this thought, she put on an earnest smile:

—"Chief Director Guan must surely be forging an extraordinarily formidable magical treasure!"-Ran Deng exclaimed.

—"Mmh..."-Fairy Maogui let out an ambiguous hum.

An alchemist might not understand artifact refinement, but the methods of manipulating spiritual energy and furnace fire were fundamentally interconnected. Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field was glaringly abnormal.

Even Fairy Maogui refrained from mouthing polite pleasantries this time, remarking with modest diplomacy:

—"I know little of artifact refinement; perhaps Chief Director Guan possesses some unique forging technique?"-Fairy Maogui offered.

Because of her remark, another flurry of discussions broke out across the spectator stands.

A cultivator who knew nothing of artifact refinement and was purely here to watch the spectacle asked the person next to him:

[—"Fairy Maogui's words sound rather peculiar! Could his forging method be improper? To my eyes, it looks extraordinarily impressive!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Cultivator">)

Three-quarters of those present were grandmasters of artifact refinement, and upon hearing this, quite a few wore expressions of utter disdain.

—"In artifact refinement, bigger commotion does not equate to greater quality,"-the cultivator seated to the inquirer's left noted tactfully.

—"Precisely. The moon waxes only to wane,"-the cultivator to his right stroked his beard in disapproval.-"He just suffered disruptions from the spiritual field; what he should be doing right now is remediating the flaws in his materials, not violently tossing in aggressive stimulants."

An even more blunt refiner sneered coldly:

—"It's merely a flying sword; anyone unaware would think he was refining some peerless heavenly treasure!"-a refiner scoffed.

Indeed, what Guan Xinguang was forging was a flying sword.

Flying swords were the most common 'consumables' in the cultivation world.

From sword-riding flight and slaying foes to wilderness exploration and even prying open treasure chests, flying swords were used for everything. Consequently, flying swords suffered the highest attrition rate of any magical treasure in the cultivation world. Even sword cultivators forged several spare flying swords as consumables rather than constantly brandishing their life-bound swords in public. Naturally, such treasures also commanded the highest transaction volume. Cultivators didn't bother hunting down premium items at auction houses; they typically custom-ordered several blades from a trusted establishment to make do. Among these, the 'fair and impartial' Department of Works was the premier choice.

It could be said that two-thirds of the goods sold by the Department of Works each year were flying swords, and Guan Xinguang was likewise most adept at forging them. His debut masterpiece back in the day was forging a flying sword for the reigning Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance; that sword had allegedly gouged out the eye of an ancient dragon, cementing Guan Xinguang's reputation as a "Grandmaster of Artifact Refinement."

Yet precisely because everyone used them, refiners forged them in massive quantities and harbored their own expert insights.

Perhaps previously they had refrained from critiquing him out of courtesy to Guan Xinguang's face, and laymen couldn't see through the facade; but now that the spiritual energy field was so glaringly manifested for all to see, everyone naturally spoke their minds aloud.

[—"Why do I get the feeling he isn't forging a sword, but rather ambushing other cultivators?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Cultivator">)

[—"It's glaringly obvious, isn't it? Such an exaggerated outward burst of spiritual energy."](<# "Speaker: Spectator Cultivator">)

[—"Wait a moment—if that was his plan, then the accident just now was no coincidence at all?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Cultivator">)

[—"Ha! So he tried to [pick a fraudulent fight](# "Literal CN: 碰瓷 (pèngcí) — staged collision / fraudulent foul-baiting"), only to get knocked flat out of the sky by a Foundation Establishment junior?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Cultivator">)

...

The crowd debated without the slightest restraint.

They might not have thought along these lines initially, but beholding that eerie violet spiritual field, listening to the commentary from the broadcast booth, and swayed by the emotional cues of the colors and dialogue, the conversation began veering wildly toward the worst possible interpretations.

This was precisely the effect Ran Deng wanted.

\*

—"[Suspicion breeds phantom demons](# "Literal CN: 疑心生暗鬼 (yíxīn shēng ànguǐ) — a suspicious mind creates its own ghosts"),"-Ruwang murmured.

Listening to the chatter below and sensing the ebb and flow of emotions, the mask on Ruwang's face shifted into the sneering visage of a ferocious ghoul, spinning around to the back of his head as he addressed Zhou Hui:

—"This female disciple of yours is truly a prodigy at manipulating human hearts. Why not send her to our Bi'an Tian for advanced studies~~"-Ruwang suggested with a grin.

—"Cease your nonsense; my A-Ran is a good child,"-Zhou Hui replied nonchalantly.-"She is not suited for Bi'an Tian."

Indeed!

Across seven entire lifetimes, she had literally lazed herself to death in every single cycle.

How could a Ran Deng who never stirred up trouble or whipped up waves possibly be a bad child?

Even if she harbored any petty wicked thoughts, it was purely the fault of that scoundrel who had provoked her to anger in the first place.
