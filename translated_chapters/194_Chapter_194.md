# Chapter 194: The Tribulation Transcendents

Ran Deng naturally didn't dare pass out.

Right now, she didn't dare do anything that might arouse Master's suspicion. She could only strive to maintain a proper, polite smile on her face, completely ignoring Yuwen Xuan who was vigorously winking and making faces at her, not even daring to take another glance in Master's direction.

Fortunately, Master genuinely seemed not to have noticed his eldest disciple clowning around, waiting with an expression of impeccable breeding for Goddess Ren Ran to finish throwing her tantrum—meanwhile, Yuwen Xuan's two legs continued to dangle right in front of the goddess's face, tempting anyone who saw them to reach out and yank him down.

Seeing this, Ran Deng felt a breath lodge in her throat—she couldn't swallow it down, nor could she exhale it.

At this moment, she was filled with profound regret.

Yet Ran Deng wasn't regretting letting Eldest Senior Brother (or a part of him) down. The disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were remarkably 'accountable' when it came to mischief—having done the deed, they never harbored an ounce of remorse, only regretting that they hadn't executed it with flawless perfection. Thus, what Ran Deng truly regretted was: why on earth had she insisted on wanting to see this goddamn thing in the first place?

The Heavenly Robe came with a built-in 'invisibility' function, but that didn't mean wearing it passively made the user invisible to everyone with no off-switch unless stripped off. Rather, as a magic treasure, its owner could freely allow whomever they wanted to see them, while concealing themselves from whomever they wished to hide from.

As a responsible, normal human being, Ran Deng naturally hadn't dared to simply let Yuwen Xuan down and call it a day:

[What if Eldest Senior Brother decides to poke a hole in the heavens?!]

Therefore, before handing the Heavenly Robe over to Yuwen Xuan, Ran Deng had first branded it with her own spiritual energy. That way, the Heavenly Robe remained Ran Deng's property, merely lent to Yuwen Xuan temporarily; unless Yuwen Xuan forcibly wiped her mark, the Heavenly Robe's concealment effect wouldn't work on Ran Deng.

Ran Deng thought she had set up the ultimate safeguard, but now she felt like she had [given birth without labor pains](# "Literal CN: 无痛当了娘 (wútòng dāngle niáng) — Chinese slang for suddenly finding oneself burdened with the exhausting, exasperating maternal responsibility of managing a chaotic child without ever giving birth").

Worse, the mother of a [troublemaking brat](# "Literal CN: 熊孩子 (xióng háizi) — 'bear child'; Chinese slang for a chaotic, unruly, undisciplined child who constantly causes havoc"):

When he was out of sight, she was worried sick; when he was in plain sight, she was driven mad.

Had she known earlier, she would have rather suffocated him in his crib—ah, right, she hadn't birthed him anyway.

Ran Deng's temples throbbed violently. Ultimately, it was Master's voice that liberated her:

—"...Why haven't the demon clan guests arrived yet?"-Zhou Hui asked.-"A-Ran, go take a look."

—"Untiring, don't change the subject——"-Goddess Ren Ran snapped in fury.

Before Ren Ran could finish her words, a terrifying spiritual pressure suddenly descended over her, instantly shutting her mouth.

Perched atop the canopy above her head, even Yuwen Xuan hadn't anticipated this—while the Heavenly Robe could grant him 'invisibility', it couldn't shield him from another's spiritual pressure, attacks, or damage. His body instantly seized up, and with a resounding *splat*, he crashed to the floor, shivering violently all over, unable to scramble back up for the moment.

This was also the very first time Yuwen Xuan had directly faced Zhou Hui's merciless spiritual pressure. He finally experienced what it felt like to stand on the opposite side of Master.

However, Zhou Hui had merely released spiritual might without directly striking. Moreover, Yuwen Xuan had been raised by Zhou Hui and was long accustomed to Zhou Hui's spiritual energy, so he was merely stunned and awed, suffering no actual physical harm.

The one truly injured was Goddess Ren Ran.

The instant that tremendous spiritual might bore down upon her, her own strength dissipated completely. Her entire dantian was stripped bare and hollow; before she could gather spiritual energy anew, she would be completely unable to cast spells.

Ren Ran stared at him in sheer horror:

—"You... you..."-Ren Ran stammered.

[How could you?!]

She desperately wanted to ask.

Even though her cultivation was locked at the Tribulation Transcendence stage due to the worldly constraints of this minor realm, she possessed the body of a True Immortal after all. What circulated in her dantian was not mere spiritual energy, but celestial energy (which consequently consumed far more ambient spiritual energy from this world just to sustain its circulation). While this might not display any obvious distinction in routine skirmishes, because it belonged to a higher dimensional hierarchy governed directly by the Heavenly Court, ordinary controlling or disruptive spells could never take effect on her—even if the opponent were this world's Creation God!

This was precisely why immortals who descended to the lower realm did not fear native lower-realm deities. Even if there was a gap in sheer power, the worst that could happen was death; after reincarnation, they would simply return to the Heavenly Court. But you could never dominate or control them!

Yet now, Zhou Hui had scattered her celestial energy in a single breath. How could she not be terrified?

Zhou Hui, however, paid her no heed. He simply turned back to Ran Deng and continued his instructions:

—"When you meet the demon clan emissaries, there's no need to bring them here either. You may escort them straight to their lodgings to rest. But if they take an interest in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the Immortals' Market, or the Immortal Arts Assembly, you may arrange for disciples to entertain them,"-Zhou Hui instructed.

—"Ah, right. There are also guests from the various major sects,"-Zhou Hui added, glancing past Goddess Ren Ran toward the courtyard outside the main hall.

The cultivators from the Northern Domain were not out there.

For Goddess Ren Ran and Ruwang Ghost Emperor to barge headlong into Zhou Hui's personal residence was their own breach of etiquette. The cultivators of the Northern Domain, however, wouldn't dare treat the present-day Untiring Immortal Venerable with such insolence. Consequently, some had remained aboard the treasure ship, while others waited respectfully before the mountain gate.

Nevertheless, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had long made thorough preparations to welcome them. Had Goddess Ren Ran not barged inside, an orderly protocol of reception—one that left both sides delighted and satisfied—would have been waiting for them.

Naturally, that reception protocol wasn't scrapped now.

It was just that, with things unfolding like this, the Heavenly Spirit Sect needed a direct disciple to go out and oversee the proceedings.

The only one who knew he couldn't escape and had followed Ren Ran inside was Sikong Yao.

He squatted stealthily by the hall entrance, looking like a man prepared to bolt the instant things took a turn for the worse.

Zhou Hui didn't immediately smite him with lightning either. He merely advised Ran Deng:

—"Just proceed according to the standard protocol,"-Zhou Hui instructed.

—"Exalted Immortal Venerable,"-the mask on Ruwang's face interjected,-"Zhen has also brought along some of Zhen's children. They've been clamoring since dawn to tour the market. But rest assured, Zhen has already disciplined them. They wouldn't dare cause trouble on your territory."

—"Entertain them together,"-Zhou Hui said to Ran Deng.

—"Yes, Master,"-Ran Deng answered.

She walked straight out without looking sideways, even planting a firm step squarely onto Yuwen Xuan's calf, as if completely blind to his presence.

Only after Ran Deng had departed did Zhou Hui turn with a mild smile to Ren Ran, Ruwang, and Sikong Yao:

—"Whether it be the Immortal Arts Assembly or the qualification examinations, they are merely friendly spars and academic games among juniors. To have several distinguished guests grace my humble abode is truly an immense honor,"-Zhou Hui said.

[...You'd better genuinely think so.]

Ren Ran, Sikong Yao, and the mask thought in unison.

Zhou Hui resolved to resolve his guests one by one (or rather, resolve his problems).

He first looked toward Goddess Ren Ran, who seemed the most troublesome to deal with.

—"Particularly the Heavenly Dao Alliance,"-Zhou Hui said, employing polite formalities with unpracticed stiffness—though fortunately, no one present cared whether his etiquette was slightly lacking.-"The Heavenly Dao Alliance has continuously rendered immense support to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Especially for this Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination, even going so far as to dispatch the Department of Works to participate, validating the true worth of this qualification certification. I would very much like to thank His Excellency the Chief Director of the Department of Works in person. Where might he be at present?"

—"You truly do..."-Ren Ran began in alarm.

[*...want to kill him!*]

Ren Ran suffered a spike of trauma response. Yet catching Zhou Hui's icy gray eyes and recalling that horrifying spiritual pressure from moments ago, she forced herself to swallow those words back down. Mimicking Zhou Hui's formal cadence, she replied:

—"Chief Director Guan of the Department of Works had no wish to be discourteous either. However, he places paramount importance upon this qualification examination. At this very moment, he is instructing his subordinates to prepare for the test. He will naturally participate when the time comes."

—"Then I shall look forward to the Department of Works' results,"-Zhou Hui replied without pressing the matter.

Ren Ran inwardly let out a sigh of relief, thanking her stars that she hadn't let hubris get the better of her by forcing Guan Xinguang to come along.

Otherwise, not only would she have failed to accomplish her objective of seeing the owner of the Dao Bone, but she would have also forfeited that crucial chess piece on the spot.

Speaking of the Dao Bone...

Ren Ran recalled the true reason for her arrival. Scanning her surroundings in secret, she failed to spot the figure she sought, leaving her no choice but to bite the bullet and ask Zhou Hui:

—"Speaking of examinations, where might your esteemed disciple who drafted the teleportation array be? Word has it he will also participate in the qualification examination. Would it be possible to meet him?"-Ren Ran inquired.

—"You will naturally see him during the examination,"-Zhou Hui said neither warmly nor coldly.

Having hit a stone wall and lacking the courage to push her luck, Ren Ran could only press her lips together in resentful silence, having zero desire to speak with Zhou Hui any further.

Zhou Hui had no intention of wasting time trading verbal barbs with her either.

He then turned his gaze toward Sikong Yao, who had shrunk into the doorway at some unknown point, his tone growing rather casual:

—"Quite a rare guest. I assumed you would never approach my territory in this lifetime. Have you finally seen the light and come to accept your execution?"

—"Of course not!"-Sikong Yao cried out in alarm, stumbling several steps backward.

Yet right before Ren Ran's eyes, Sikong Yao didn't dare confess that he had defected from the Palace of Carefree Wandering—much less that he had absconded with a whole contingent of craftsmen.

Rolling his eyes, he quickly improvised with slick cunning:

—"Some minor issues cropped up at the Carefree Palace recently, making it inconvenient for me to stay there. I merely stepped out to clear my head. Along the way, I happened across a group of artifact craftsmen wishing to register for the qualification examination, so I escorted them for part of the journey."

—"Oh?"-Zhou Hui responded, the corners of his mouth curving into a faint smile.-"What sort of issues cropped up at Carefree Palace?"

Sikong Yao inwardly breathed a sigh of relief, knowing he had passed this hurdle. Still, he didn't lower his guard, offering a vague explanation:

—"Merely some minor squabbling over power distribution. After all... the master isn't home!"-as he spoke, he shot a deliberate glance at Ren Ran.

Sure enough, Ren Ran took the bait.

This was the exact reason she had summoned Heavenly General Lin Qi in the first place. Now that Sikong Yao had provided an opening—she didn't doubt Sikong Yao's motive for visiting the Heavenly Spirit Sect, assuming he had simply concocted an excuse to investigate the whereabouts of his sect's missing 'Old Ancestor', a truly commendable display of courage—she proactively spoke up once more:

—"Lower-realm insects are always like this, squabbling endlessly over mere pastry crumbs. One wonders how they will react once their master returns,"-Ren Ran scoffed coldly, turning her gaze toward Zhou Hui.-"Only, 'that person' of the Carefree Palace has always been elusive in his movements, making his actions difficult to predict. Perhaps the Exalted Immortal Venerable has met him before?"

Both Ren Ran and Sikong Yao looked toward Zhou Hui, though neither expected him to give an actual answer.

Sure enough, Zhou Hui shook his head:

—"That person from Carefree Palace? I would quite like to see what his true body looks like myself—to think he managed to shield Carefree Palace from my perception for so many years. However, my second disciple did happen across quite an intriguing matter."

At this point, Zhou Hui's tone grew unprecedentedly cheerful, tinged with a subtle trace of bragging—resembling a proud parent boasting that his child had once again secured first place in the entire academic year. The sight sent an icy shiver down both Ren Ran and Sikong Yao's spines.

Ren Ran was relatively fine; haughty and dismissive of all worldly affairs, she had no idea who Zhou Hui's second disciple even was. But ever since he had been hunted across the realm, Sikong Yao had spent all these years quietly monitoring everything surrounding Zhou Hui to prevent himself from accidentally stepping on one of the Immortal Venerable's landmines. He was naturally well aware of the grudge between Qu Qianxing and Carefree Palace's 'Old Ancestor'.

The instant he heard Zhou Hui's tone, he had a faint inkling of what had transpired.

Yet Zhou Hui seemed worried someone might not understand, explaining in meticulous detail:

—"That child encountered a monster possessing a mortal's body. Who would have guessed that the monster happened to be an old enemy who had once coveted his physical vessel? So, he slew the fellow, refined it, and crafted it into a Heavenly Robe magic treasure capable of shielding perception. I was even hoping to invite everyone to admire it!"

Reaching this point, Zhou Hui paused.

Meanwhile, Yuwen Xuan had finally recovered from Master's spiritual pressure after great difficulty. Having observed for quite a while, he had concluded that Master genuinely hadn't seen him and that his fall had merely been 'collateral friendly fire'—after all, while Master was speaking just now, he had pulled several goofy faces to Master's left, right, front, and back, even getting almost face-to-face with Master at one point without eliciting the slightest reaction. Thus, he was just preparing to clamber back onto the canopy when Zhou Hui's abrupt pause startled him, causing his foot to slip and sending him crashing right back down.

This time, Yuwen Xuan adopted the [ostrich strategy](# "Literal CN: 鸵鸟战术 (tuóniǎo zhànshù) — ostrich strategy; burying one's head in the sand to pretend danger doesn't exist"), lying flat on the floor and playing dead.

Fortunately, Master truly seemed not to see this idiot.

He continued with apparent regret:

—"Pity that my disciple has gone away on business, and heaven knows where he tucked that Heavenly Robe away. That item is exceedingly bizarre; unless brought out by someone in the know, even I cannot locate its whereabouts, so naturally I cannot present it for everyone's viewing."

—"It's just as well we lack the fate, just as well we lack the fate,"-Sikong Yao chanted like a monk reciting sutras.

This time, Ren Ran finally understood as well.

Her face turned a mottled shade of green and pale, caught between sheer terror and disbelief. Staring daggers at Zhou Hui, she managed to squeeze out:

—"You... you will face cosmic retribution."

—"When that thing peeled away other people's bodies to fashion garments for itself, did it ever contemplate retribution?"-the smile on Zhou Hui's face faded.-"Ah, pardon me, not 'he'—'it'."

—"How could the mere husk of a lower-realm creature ever compare to a True Immortal?!"-Ren Ran [shrieked in a total meltdown](# "Literal CN: 破防 (pòfáng) — 'defense breached'; modern internet slang for completely losing emotional composure, suffering an emotional breakdown").

[Your Ladyship Goddess, do you actually possess a brain?]

Sikong Yao shot Ren Ran a profoundly skeptical look.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui did not take offense. He regarded Goddess Ren Ran with aloof, silver-gray eyes for a long moment before asking in a peculiar tone:

—"Your Ladyship Goddess, I have had a question I wished to ask you for a very long time: were you born in the Upper Realm?"

—"What if I was?"-Ren Ran retorted.

Zhou Hui rapidly rattled off a barrage of questions; it appeared he was genuinely curious:

—"Who are your parents? No, rather, what manner of creatures are your parents? Are they True Immortals as well? Are they [Nth-generation immortal nepo-babies](# "Literal CN: 仙N代 (xiān N dài) — modern internet slang parodying 'second-generation rich' (富二代) or 'second-generation official' (官二代); multi-generational descendants born into privilege without earning it"), or did they ascend from below? On what grounds did they unite to give birth to you? Or could it be that you do not reproduce through biological means? ...And finally, are 'innocent' children like you commonplace in the Upper Realm?"

—"You... you! Insolent!"-Goddess Ren Ran roared in outrage.

She stormed off just as furiously as she had arrived.

Yuwen Xuan lay on the floor for a few moments longer, glancing between Ren Ran's departing back and Master. In the end, utterly incapable of abandoning the delicious feast dangling right within his grasp, he bolted out like a streak of lightning.

[...That fellow's brain seems to have been nibbled on by sheep lately; his behavior is resembling a sheep more and more by the day.]

Zhou Hui retracted his gaze, unable to bear the sight. He then noticed that, following Ren Ran's departure, the two remaining Tribulation Transcendents were staring at him.

—"Exalted Immortal Venerable, have you recently developed a penchant for humiliating people?"-Sikong Yao inquired with trepidation.

On the other side, half of Ruwang Ghost Emperor's mask vanished. He stared at Zhou Hui in silence with abyss-black eyes, seemingly expressing mild disapproval.

—"Don't reward her~~ If there's something you wish to ask, simply slaughter her and be done with it~~"-the mask sang in an operatic trill.

—"I am genuinely curious,"-Zhou Hui said, utterly baffled by what these fellows were thinking.-"Are you two not curious? According to records across various ancient texts, aside from those True Immortals who reincarnated after having their memories wiped, most True Immortals descending by celestial decree like her appear profoundly ignorant of worldly matters, possessing warped values. They simply do not look like individuals who cultivated and ascended from below."

—"Wherever there are mortals, there shall be new life~~"-Ruwang's mask trilled.

—"You speak of mortals, but consider the circumstances of cultivators,"-Zhou Hui said, casting a glance at the other two [bachelors](# "Literal CN: 单身狗 (dānshēngǒu) — 'single dogs'; ubiquitous Chinese internet slang for unmarried or unpartnered individuals") present.-"As cultivators climb higher, Daoist couples grow increasingly scarce. Especially at our level of cultivation: aside from [lifelong singles](# "Literal CN: 母胎单身 (mǔtāi dānshēn) — 'single since the mother's womb'; individuals who have never been in a romantic relationship since birth"), the rest are widows or widowers. A harmonious couple like Su Mei and Xin Zhu who stay together through eternity is literally one in ten thousand. Even if folks like us ascend, do you think they'll suddenly see the light, find a partner, and pop out a whole string of children?"

—"..."-the other two fell dead silent.

Zhou Hui continued to mutter:

—"Furthermore, it feels as though the number of female cultivators who ascend is rather small. Though no one has compiled formal statistics, during recent assemblies, I noticed that the number of Mahayana female cultivators across various sects is quite substantial. Why is it that, upon reaching Tribulation Transcendence, there are only two females among us, and one of them is even a celestial descent from the Upper Realm? Yet in the Heavenly Dao's scripts, as long as a female cultivator gets tangled up with the 'protagonist', they ascend in whole batches. Did they truly ascend, or..."

—"Master, please exercise restraint (with your curiosity),"-Ruwang said expressionlessly.

This (former) master always harbored all manner of bizarre curiosities in the most inappropriate places.

Zhou Hui retorted in dissatisfaction:

—"I am not indulging in mere idle curiosity."

[After all, one of my disciples' children might well be in the Immortal-Demon Realm; figuring out the living conditions of second-generation immortals in the Upper Realm would make it far easier to track them down.]

Nevertheless, Zhou Hui also knew that if Ruwang had reached the limit of his patience and spoken in his own voice, it proved he genuinely detested this topic. Besides, Ren Ran had already fled; discussing such matters with these remaining two was rather pointless.

—"By the way, what brought you here?"-Zhou Hui asked once more.

By this time, Ruwang seemed to have finally organized his words. He replied:

—"An omen."

[What does that mean?]

Sikong Yao looked at him in confusion.

Zhou Hui, on the other hand, was well accustomed to this:

—"You came because of an omen? An omen of what?"

Ruwang pointed a finger toward the sky.

—"An omen of ascension? Or an omen of the Upper Realm?"-Zhou Hui pressed.

—"Both,"-Ruwang said.

Zhou Hui furrowed his brows.

Unlike Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai, who was a professional fortune-teller, the 'omens' Ruwang perceived were not predictable futures or fated trajectories. Rather, they were sudden, unforeseeable catastrophes—the 'extreme calamities' that diviners frequently foresaw yet were powerless to resolve. Ghosts, however, relished pursuing these extreme calamities to augment their own power.

Yet what 'extreme calamity' could there be in the Heavenly Spirit Sect? And one connected to ascension and the Upper Realm, no less.

[Come to think of it, Jiu Zhai recently 'fled' back to the Southern Domain.]

Zhou Hui mused. He was just about to inquire in greater detail when he heard Ruwang continue:

—"Also... unfair... demon clan."

—"You speak of my alliance with the demon clan?"-Zhou Hui asked.-"Could it be that Bi'an Tian wishes to form an alliance with the Heavenly Spirit Sect as well?"

Ruwang nodded.

—"However, my alliance with the demon clan was not formed for combat or military cooperation; it was purely to exchange pedagogical and study methodologies. Would that be acceptable to you?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Ruwang nodded once more.

Hearing this, Sikong Yao couldn't resist interjecting:

—"Wait, your place is entirely populated by dead people waiting to reincarnate! What's the point of sharing study methods?!"

Indeed: Ruwang Ghost Emperor's Bi'an Tian, as its name implied, was a sanctuary for wandering souls.

Yet ghost cultivators there were exceedingly scarce—or rather, ghost cultivators across the entire cultivation realm were rare by nature. After all, to become a ghost cultivator, one not only had to die once, but also required the right celestial alignment, geographical advantage, and human harmony. Even if every karmic condition was satisfied and one finally became a ghost cultivator, they would discover that the needs and traits of ghosts were completely different from living beings: a true state of the living dead, nothing but pure agony.

Consequently, what drifted through Bi'an Tian were mostly maimed, incomplete vengeful spirits and souls. Most were merely fractured wandering ghosts who had perished in the Great Wilderness, waiting for a karmic opportunity to mend their souls and reincarnate anew.

Moreover, as the most desolate region among the Five Domains and Six Abysses, both mortal tribes and cultivator sects in the Western Domain were scattered far and wide. Thus, Zhou Hui hadn't even considered promoting educational reforms there.

Yet upon Ruwang's reminder, Zhou Hui actually found it rather sensible.

Zhou Hui mused aloud:

—"The populace there is widely dispersed, which I felt made widespread promotion difficult. But indeed, your sanctuary is truly the destination where all living beings in the Western Domain will eventually end up. Using it as a starting point isn't a bad idea,"-Zhou Hui mused.

[That's not a starting point, that's the literal terminus of existence!]

[The people are already dead, and you still want them to study?! What on earth are they going to use that studying for?!]

Sikong Yao's mind was bursting with complaints.

Pity that neither human nor ghost paid him any mind.

Ruwang nodded again, appearing quite satisfied.

—"Then let us discuss this matter later. Before that, why not watch the children's examination first?"-Zhou Hui extended an invitation.

—"Ye-zi?"-Ruwang asked.

—"You remember him?"-Zhou Hui asked, not surprised in the least.

As his former ghost servant, Ruwang shared the same origin of power as Zhou Hui. What Zhou Hui had done across these several cycles of reincarnation could hardly be concealed from Ruwang; for him to remember Guan Ye was only natural.

Ruwang nodded in silence, pausing for several seconds before adding:

—"Long time no see. Miss him dearly."

—"Then all the more reason you should see him,"-Zhou Hui said.-"His hard work and accumulated foundation."

[Accumulated foundation?]

[The 'Ye-zi' they're talking about is Untiring's fourth disciple, Guan Ye, right?]

[Also the one who drafted the blueprints for the large-scale teleportation array.]

[If I recall correctly, he's only at Foundation Establishment—where would he get any 'accumulated foundation'?]

[...Wait, no. The very fact that he could draft and design a large-scale teleportation array is supernatural in itself.]

[Or could it concern the core heritage of Untiring's lineage?]

Sikong Yao listened from the side, pondering absently in his heart.

It wasn't that he deliberately sought to pry into another clan's private secrets, but to say Sikong Yao had zero interest in the source of Zhou Hui's power would be an outright lie—at the very least, he yearned to learn a way to completely avoid Untiring.

Yet just as Sikong Yao was musing over this, Zhou Hui finally remembered that there was another guest present:

—"You come along as well,"-he said to Sikong Yao.

[At a time like this, you could really just forget about me.]

Sikong Yao smiled wryly.

*

—"Infuriating! How dare he speak to me like that?!"-Ren Ran fumed, returning to the treasure ship in a towering rage.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect had naturally prepared lodgings for the cultivators arriving from the Northern Domain, but with Ren Ran storming off in such a huff, arrangements naturally couldn't be carried out. Furthermore, she had never intended to stay within the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the first place—after all, she had someone by her side who wouldn't dare approach the Untiring Immortal Venerable alone under any circumstances.

Naturally, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had no intention of going out of their way to invite her either.

Goddess Ren Ran fumed:

—"What on earth does he mean?!"-Ren Ran fumed in outrage.

—"Perhaps after observing you, he developed a holistic skepticism regarding the average intelligence of Heavenly Court officials,"-Lin Qi spoke for the first time since arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Precisely! To insult me is to insult the Heavenly Court!"-Ren Ran chimed in agreement. She paused.-"Lord Heavenly General?"

Why did that sentence sound slightly off?

Lin Qi paid her no heed.

Truth be told, having observed Ren Ran, he was rather worried about the Heavenly Court's next generation as well.

Having run into a wall with Lin Qi, Goddess Ren Ran finally regained a semblance of calm.

She asked sheepishly:

—"Lord Heavenly General, why did you give no instructions while we were at the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Ah, I do not mean to blame you, but... what is this?!"

Ren Ran's eyes went wide as she stared at Guan Xinguang, who had stayed behind aboard the treasure ship.

She saw a layer of pale golden light enshrouding the top of Guan Xinguang's head, bearing a striking resemblance to her imperial canopy.

—"You actually dare reproach me?"-Lin Qi sneered without answering immediately.-"I have yet to ask you: why does this 'Untiring Immortal Venerable' possess a divine spark?! Had my reactions not been swift enough, I fear my disguise would have been pierced on the spot."

—"I... I assumed it was merely a local divine spark..."-Ren Ran said, losing another three parts of her confidence as she realized she had indeed neglected to mention it.-"He likely acquired it from repairing the Ascension Stairway. It didn't feel very important."

—"How is it not important?! The Ascension Stairway is an official hallmark certified by the Heavenly Court! The divine spark he acquired from repairing the Ascension Stairway is without question a Heavenly Court-certified divinity! If he manages to ascend, his celestial rank will surpass ours without a shadow of a doubt!"-Lin Qi snapped.

—"Then..."-Ren Ran was dumbfounded.-"Are we still going to investigate him...?"

—"Of course. Has he ascended yet?"-Lin Qi sneered.

[And he never will ascend.]

[The grand figures of the Upper Realm would never wish for a fellow like this to climb up.]

Lin Qi let out a sneer:

—"However, because he possesses a divine spark, rashly participating in the competition would likely put us at a disadvantage. Thus, I had no choice but to bestow a bit of fortune upon His Excellency the Artifact Refiner."

—"Fortune?"-Ren Ran echoed.

She naturally knew what fortune was. However, as auxiliary personnel dispatched to the lower realm, Ren Ran had no authority to mobilize substantial fortune. Furthermore, dilute fortune was of little practical use; only when concentrated could it overturn the heavenly mechanics of an entire world. Therefore, such fortune was generally allocated by specialized departments in the Heavenly Court, concentrated onto a designated operative to turn them into a Child of Fortune.

It was just that this world's Child of Fortune had yet to arrive.

Ren Ran inquired:

—"Could it be you contacted the Bureau of Heavenly Dao...?"

—"The Dao Bone was what Tian Yan wanted in the first place. Isn't borrowing a bit of fortune from him only natural?"-Lin Qi said dismissively, before turning to Guan Xinguang:-"With the blessing of fortune, I trust you will perform even better?"

—"O-Of course!"-Guan Xinguang stammered in excitement.

For the craft of artifact refinement, fortune—or rather, that one-in-a-hundred-billion probability—was of paramount importance. Even among grandmaster artifact refiners, minute discrepancies existed in finished products, and that slight discrepancy was the discrepancy in fortune.

Guan Xinguang declared with supreme confidence:

—"I will surely seize victory!"

—"That is well,"-Ren Ran said, finally letting out a faint smile.

Only, none of the people present could hear the scoffing sneer.

—"Fortune? Luck?"-leaning against the doorway with arms crossed, Yuwen Xuan watched them.-"Junior Brother Guan's luck truly isn't all that great!"
