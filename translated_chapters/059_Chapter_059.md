# Chapter 59: The Auction Begins (第59章 拍卖会开始)

Through sheer hook and crook, sweet-talking and stammering excuses, Daoist Huang finally managed to fool them and pull the wool over their eyes.

His current situation was actually not too terrible.

After all, the cultivation world lacked efficient mass communication channels; information gathering relied almost entirely on the divine abilities and scrying spells of each faction's high-tier powerhouses. This made it extraordinarily easy to form an [information cocoon](# "Literal CN: 信息茧房 (xìnxī jiǎnfáng) — sociological/internet term for echo chamber or information cocoon, where groups only consume self-reinforcing information"). Aside from the handful of factions that had closely monitored the Tianling Auction from the start, those sects and immortal cities that chose to side with the Palace of Carefree Wandering and complied with Daoist Huang's boycott arrangements remained completely in the dark regarding the true conditions of the Immortal Bazaar.

Daoist Huang felt exactly like a beginner-class disciple who scored a miserable twenty percent on his midterm exam and was desperately trying to hide his test paper beneath his mattress—praying with every fiber of his being that those sects and immortal cities would remain blissfully ignorant forever.

In fact, it would be best if a heavenly lightning bolt crashed down tomorrow and cleaved the entire Central Domain in half, forcing the Tianling Auction into an emergency cancellation!

Regrettably, what was destined to happen would always come to pass.

A hidden failing exam would inevitably see the light of day, and an auction slated to unfold would inevitably commence.

By the time the Heavenly Spirit Sect's beginner-class disciples suffered through their midterm exams, the intermediate and advanced students submitted their practicum reports, the Immortal Venerable finished grading the papers, every disciple across the sect received their final scores, were assigned to new internships based on their academic ranking, and completed a full month of field training—the Immortal Bazaar had also drawn near its finale. The Winter Solstice had finally arrived, every guest who was meant to come had gathered, and the grandest auction in the Central Domain opened on schedule.

Naturally, people like Daoist Huang were an extreme minority; the overwhelming majority looked forward to the auction's grand opening with bated breath.

Yet alongside the glorious run of the Immortal Bazaar, countless attendees secretly wished this bustling market could continue forever and ever. Toward the grand auction that served as the bazaar's grand finale, they harbored a bittersweet melancholia—a tangled mix of fervent anticipation and reluctant sorrow.

For instance, at this very moment, someone was having an absolute [melodramatic breakdown](# "Literal CN: 发癫 (fādiān) — acting crazy / having a hysterical meltdown / pitching a fit") right at the entrance of the auction venue—which was also the front threshold of Qianxing Hall:

—"Brother Qingyang! Brother Qingyang! How could you keep me in the dark so cruelly?!"

A gorgeous youth dressed in crimson robes wailed at the top of his lungs, clutching the flowing sleeve of an elegant, handsome young master clad in pristine white. Surrounding them was a cluster of high-tier cultivators, clearly the elite disciples of prominent sects; even though every last one of them boasted a Nascent Soul cultivation base, their spectacle still drew the curious stares of passing pedestrians.

Qingyang Qingli of Eternal Villa yanked his sleeve several times without breaking free. He could only force an exasperated smile:
—"Brother Jiawen, what on earth did I keep from you?"

—"A bazaar this marvelous and fun, and you didn't urge me to come?!"-the crimson-robed youth named Jiawen accused dramatically.

His accusation was seventy percent theatrical banter, but thirty percent genuinely indignant.

Shu Jiawen was a disciple hailing from a prominent sect known as Yuke Xuan.

Similar to Eternal Villa, this sect was a cultivation clan centered primarily around an aristocratic family. However, its scale was vastly larger, and it did not belong to the Central Domain—their ancestral headquarters resided in the Eastern Domain.

Consequently, on matters concerning the Palace of Carefree Wandering and the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yuke Xuan naturally leaned slightly toward the Carefree Palace. Yet such aristocratic family-sects typically possessed their own aloof pride; they customarily prided themselves on being strictly neutral and never considered themselves puppets under any other faction's thumb.

Therefore, regarding Carefree Palace's isolation of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and Heavenly Spirit Sect's hosting of the auction, Yuke Xuan's stance had been: *If you can genuinely rally every immortal sect beneath heaven to crush the Heavenly Spirit Sect together, we certainly won't object. But we are not Carefree Palace's hunting hounds, so we will only join at the very end. By the same token, if the Heavenly Spirit Sect wants to hold an auction, we won't boycott it just because of Carefree Palace. Still, what could possibly be entertaining about an auction held by a minor backwater sect? Showing up in the final two days is already doing the Heavenly Spirit Sect an immense courtesy.*

Thus, Shu Jiawen had only arrived on the morning of the auction's grand opening.

Furthermore, to showcase Yuke Xuan's distinguished pedigree, they didn't even deign to ride the Kunpeng or travel through the subterranean Abyss Sea; instead, adhering strictly to classical aristocratic routes, they rode an immortal crane-feather carriage, flying for three days and three nights non-stop before finally arriving at Qianxing Hall.

And this happened to be Qianxing Hall at its most bustling peak.

At the beginning of the twelfth lunar month, the "Best Stalls" across the various cultivation tiers of the Immortal Bazaar had already concluded their evaluation. The winners had relocated into Qianxing Hall with ten years of waived rent; stall owners could operate long-term or choose to collect a hefty cash prize and operate for a brief season after the auction.

As a result, Qianxing Hall at this moment was a dazzling treasure trove, brimming with shops of every imaginable variety, leaving spectators thoroughly dazzled.

Shu Jiawen hadn't even set foot in the deep seabed bazaar of the Abyss Sea yet, but right here in Qianxing Hall, his intestines turned green with regret:

[Why on earth did I dawdle on the road?! Why didn't I come earlier?!]

He completely forgot that just this very morning, he had perched upon a cloud peak for half an hour composing poetry and sending transmission runes to friends across the realms, utterly enamored with his own peerless elegance and refinement!

The end result was that Shu Jiawen had only browsed the market for half a day, and right when his excitement reached its peak, countless shops began packing up their stalls to prepare for the afternoon auction.

And thus, the dramatic scene earlier unfolded.

Shu Jiawen's lamentation actually resonated with quite a few people around them—primarily because the cultivators still wandering the upper shopping pavilions of Qianxing Hall at this hour were all late arrivals who had only shown up in the last two days and hadn't gotten their fill of fun. Those who had stayed for a while and understood the drill had long since headed down to secure their seats for the grand opening.

[—"Indeed, I haven't even browsed half of it."](# "Speaker: Late-Arriving Cultivator")

[—"Had I known, I would have arrived several days earlier."](# "Speaker: Late-Arriving Cultivator")

[—"Truth be told, the shops could easily keep operating during the auction! It wouldn't interfere at all."](# "Speaker: Late-Arriving Cultivator")

[—"Precisely! Hear, hear!"](# "Speaker: Late-Arriving Cultivator")

[If you have complaints, go lodge them with the Heavenly Spirit Sect! My Eternal Villa isn't the one hosting this auction!]

Qingyang Qingli thought, caught between exasperation and amusement.

It was only because Eternal Villa commanded considerable prestige in "high-society cultivation circles"—among those prestigious aristocratic clans and great sects that held their heads high—that Qingyang Qingli associated with these 'young elite talents' in the first place. Little did he expect that stepping out to receive his guests would result in walking straight into an ambush of whining complaints.

—"What kind of talk is that?!"-Qingyang Qingli retorted:—"Back when the Heavenly Spirit Sect first announced the auction, didn't I write to invite you from the very beginning?"

—"I thought you were merely drooling over their Kunpeng!"-Shu Jiawen argued self-righteously:—"I hail from the Eastern Domain; what wondrous spectacle have I not witnessed? As for Kunpengs, I grew sick of looking at them centuries ago!"

[You've only looked at them from afar—have you ever actually ridden one?!]

[Nowadays in our Central Domain, even ordinary mortals have ridden that beast more times than they can count.]

Qingyang Qingli thought with a cold sneer.

He didn't bother bursting his bubble, also fearing that provoking Shu Jiawen further would only trigger another round of theatrical dramatics—

Shu Jiawen and his cohort hadn't seen the true marvels and couldn't bear to leave the storefronts above; Qingyang Qingli, however, had thoroughly enjoyed every single minute of it over the past month. Right now, he was itching to race down to the grand hall below and await the opening ceremony.

Yet Shu Jiawen still dragged his sleeve, continuing his grievance:
—"Afterward, whenever I asked you about the Immortal Bazaar, you never gave a single positive review! Naturally, I assumed it was thoroughly mediocre!"

—"That was... wait, didn't I say it was extraordinarily fascinating?"-Qingyang Qingli attempted to shirk responsibility.

—"That lukewarm, indifferent tone looked like polite pleasantries no matter how one sliced it!"-Shu Jiawen argued with utter unreasonableness:—"Knowing your disposition, if you genuinely thought it was marvelous, you should have moved heaven and earth to convince me! You should have badgered me relentlessly until I packed my bags early! *That* would be a true positive review!"

[Are you my Daoist partner or something? Why on earth would I go to such lengths for you?!]

Qingyang Qingli roasted internally.

Yet he did feel a twinge of guilt. After experiencing the Immortal Bazaar, Qingyang Qingli held it in the highest esteem, yet he had deliberately refrained from aggressively [hyping it up](# "Literal CN: 强烈安利 (qiángliè ānlì) — modern internet slang: passionately pitching / recommending / evangelizing a product or hobby to friends") to his friends in other sects—strictly due to his own selfish motives.

It was just rather awkward to speak of it right now.

Suppressing his urge to gloat, Qingyang Qingli said to Shu Jiawen:
—"Brother Jiawen, you lament here that you didn't arrive at the bazaar sooner, but isn't harboring such regret an insult to the present moment? Wasting a single second standing around here means missing one more glance at the auction below!"

Throughout his time at the Immortal Bazaar, Qingyang Qingli had already grown thoroughly accustomed to using "seconds" as a unit of time.

Fortunately, Shu Jiawen had no time to ponder Qingyang Qingli's bizarre choice of vocabulary. Seeing that Qingyang Qingli was genuinely frantic to head down to the auction hall, his curiosity was piqued:

[Could the auction truly be even more entertaining than the bazaar?]

—"In that case, let us head down and see!"-Shu Jiawen agreed, though his mouth remained stubbornly haughty:—"Just remember, our Yuke Xuan hails from the ethereal and romantic Eastern Domain. Setting aside Carefree Palace, our major and minor islands are carpeted with bustling street markets, and auctions take place right on open street corners. An ordinary auction will never satisfy my refined palate."

—"After all, what grand spectacle beneath heaven have I not witnessed?"

***

As it turned out, the spectacle before his eyes was something Shu Jiawen had genuinely never seen in his entire life!

Shu Jiawen hadn't been boasting blindly. As an aristocratic scion hailing from a major Eastern Domain sect, he had not only attended the Carefree Palace's Treasure Fair and the Sea Market of the Dragon King of the Eastern Sea, but had even traveled to the Northern Domain's greatest immortal metropolis, Sky City, to participate in the Ascension Fair held once every five hundred years. Compared to the vast majority of cultivators, he was undeniably a man of high society who had seen the grandest stages of the world.

Consequently, Shu Jiawen held plenty of seasoned opinions regarding such auctions.

In his view, most auctions were fundamentally identical: at most, the architecture was slightly more exquisite, the serving attendants' attire was a touch more opulent, the attendees were somewhat bigger luminaries, and the rarities gathered from across the realms were slightly more numerous. The routine remained strictly the same: take your seat, wait for the items of personal interest to appear, and if the auction had nothing you desired, it would quickly become thoroughly dull.

And such boredom was, in fact, the norm.

After all, even spiritual flames in the cultivation world could be categorized into ninety-nine distinct variations, with artifact forging and alchemy demanding precise specifications. While mainstream varieties fared well enough, niche items—no matter how rare or exorbitant at auction—only ever attracted bids from a handful of specialists. The vast majority of spectators watched the bidding in complete, bewildered apathy.

Thus, organizing an auction that never lacked buzz and excitement was an extraordinarily Herculean task.

The few renowned grand auctions across the realms had all developed their own distinct trademarks over countless centuries:

Sky City's Ascension Fair exclusively auctioned relics left behind by ascended immortals; each item boasted an epic historical provenance, offering cultivators rare glimpses into the secrets of the True Immortal Realm. It belonged to the ultra-elite tier, where invitations were strictly reserved for high-ranking luminaries.

The Eastern Sea's Sea Market leaned heavily toward exotic raw materials and mystical treasures. Cultivators might not recognize esoteric crafting components, but everyone certainly recognized spirit stones! It reveled in sheer, unadulterated decadence, typically attended by tycoon cultivators, though ordinary cultivators could enter just to hear the clinking chorus of astronomical fortunes changing hands.

Carefree Palace's Treasure Fair was the most popular among ordinary cultivators because it was "pragmatic." It focused primarily on treasures that directly boosted cultivation: bluntly stating how many years of cultivation base an item could provide was something even a novice Foundation Establishment cultivator could easily grasp. Furthermore, they would intersperse the event with items the crowd [universally adored](# "Literal CN: 喜闻乐见 (xǐwénlèjiàn) — a popular delight; something everyone loves to see and hear")—such as cultivation furnaces—to warm up the room. Whenever the atmosphere cooled after several esoteric lots, rolling out a few strikingly gorgeous female cultivators instantly sent the hype through the roof.

Yet even Carefree Palace had never directly lent its official name to the Central Domain's annual auctions; at most, they provided a bit of spirit stones and goods as "sponsorship" to boost their brand reputation.

Because this godforsaken place called the Central Domain was far too chaotic in resources.

To call it destitute would be unfair: as the center of the ancient cultivation world, the original site of the greatest ancient immortal city White Jade Capital, and the ruins of the God-Demon Battlefield, the Central Domain never lacked bizarre rarities. From time to time, excavators could even unearth immortal treasures worthy of being auctioned at the Ascension Fair.

Yet to call it rich in resources was equally misleading: the Central Domain's spiritual energy was turbulent, human settlements were sparse, mortals carried demon bloodlines, and even an ordinary blade of grass suffered grotesque mutations. Consequently, one could easily stumble upon bizarre curios that appeared to be peerless treasures on the surface, but turned out to be completely worthless junk that drove their buyers into total bankruptcy.

And the Central Domain's Winter Solstice Auction was originally established by rogue cultivators specifically to trade and offload items of unknown origin. Bizarre, unidentifiable objects were abundant, the auction process was notoriously sluggish, and lots frequently went unsold. There was virtually zero entertainment value; it had only grown into an annual event because rogue cultivators genuinely needed an outlet for their obscure finds.

Thus, to disciples of major sects, attending this was merely an annual chore to be endured—just show up, [punch your timecard](# "Literal CN: 打个卡 (dǎ ge kǎ) — modern corporate slang for clocking in; doing the bare minimum attendance"), and leave.

Shu Jiawen had originally thought the exact same thing.

Yet as he descended the grand, winding staircase of Qianxing Hall, Shu Jiawen realized he had been catastrophically mistaken.

This was truly the first time in his life he had ever laid eyes on such an astonishing exhibition.

Yes—an exhibition.

Unlike other auction houses that merely compiled all items into printed booklets beforehand to pitch exclusively to high-net-worth VIPs—though similar jade-slip catalogs were available here as well—the Heavenly Spirit Sect had actually displayed every single commodity, either as physical specimens or radiant holograms, directly along both sides of the descending staircase leading into the auction hall! Floating above each exhibit was not only a detailed description and a holographic replay tracing how the item was excavated, but also curious, fluttering little tags drifting around each display.

—"People are placing bets,"-Qingyang Qingli explained.

—"Placing bets?"-Shu Jiawen echoed in bewilderment.

—"Correct. Take this stone, for instance: even after the Heavenly Spirit Sect enlisted the most veteran appraisers from the Abyssal Ocean Society, none could identify its origins,"-Qingyang Qingli explained:—"Yet it pulses with abundant spiritual energy and clearly isn't an ordinary rock. Attendees can place bets based on their personal expertise; whoever guesses closest to the final transaction price scoops the entire betting pool."

—"Whoa!"-Shu Jiawen gasped, unable to resist giving the mysterious stone several more appraising glances.

Beside them, an eager companion chimed in:
—"Brother Jiawen, aren't you a man of vast worldly experience? Why not appraise this stone and hazard an estimate?"

—"If even the appraisers of the Abyssal Ocean Society are stumped, how could I possibly know?"-Shu Jiawen replied modestly.

Yet when he inspected the subsequent exhibits, his scrutiny grew noticeably sharper.

After all, based on his past experience, he had always managed to spot a few unidentified materials or magical artifacts at previous auctions. In the past, after mocking the appraisers for being incompetent hacks and scoffing at Carefree Palace's uselessness, that was the end of it; this time, however, he could actually turn a tidy profit!

And even for those without Shu Jiawen's mercenary ambitions, the other cultivators browsed the exhibits with boundless relish.

Because the Heavenly Spirit Sect hadn't merely slapped down text descriptions; they had recorded holographic appraisals from veteran evaluators. Each appraiser broke down the lot with sound logic, detailed metrics, and historical comps to determine a reserve price. This thorough, masterclass breakdown made every spectator feel as though they had instantly become a connoisseur of ancient treasures, unconsciously loosening their storage pouches to place a wager.

As a consequence, following the shopping promenade above, Qingyang Qingli discovered that his entourage of high-society friends was once again rooted to the spot. They lingered endlessly before the exhibition cases, scrutinizing every single relic as though it were their own family heirloom about to be hammered away.

—"See?! If you had just come earlier, you wouldn't be stuck like this!"-Qingyang Qingli grew anxious:—"On one hand, you turned your noses up at them, dragging your feet until the very last minute; on the other hand, the moment you set foot here, your eyes pop out of your heads and you refuse to take another step!"

—"Aren't we simply fascinated by the novelty?"-Shu Jiawen laughed, before abruptly adopting a solemn expression to resurrect his earlier grievance:—"Still, Brother Qingyang, I was only bantering earlier, but now I speak in earnest. This exhibition format is nothing short of ingenious; watching these master appraisers' analyses alone is [well worth the price of admission](# "Literal CN: 回票值 (huípiào zhí) — modern slang for getting one's money's worth on a ticket / recouping ticket value"). You truly should have recorded a segment onto jade slips and distributed them among the major sects! Hasn't your Eternal Villa sought to befriend the Heavenly Spirit Sect from the very beginning? The other sects were merely wavering; had they seen such an unprecedented spectacle, countless people would have gladly rushed over! That way, Eternal Villa could have showcased its vast connections while cementing its alliance with the Heavenly Spirit Sect—a win-win on all fronts, so why didn't you do it?"

—"Heh... heh,"-Qingyang Qingli chuckled hollowly, shifting his gaze in pure guilt.

The days when the auction exhibits were first arranged happened to be Qingyang Qingli's absolute most "critical" days. Forget notifying outsiders like Shu Jiawen—he hadn't even called his own sect's junior disciples, and had slapped a total gag order on everyone!

As for the reason...

Qingyang Qingli didn't bother explaining, merely continuing:
—"Actually, none of you need to rush. If you can't examine these exhibits now, you can view them later all the same. This auction has been divided into distinct sessions, with different batches of lots presented in separate time slots. You can search for the items you desire and attend whichever session suits your schedule."

Saying so, Qingyang Qingli produced a jade-slip catalog, which indeed displayed the detailed session batches and schedule for all auction lots.

—"The rules this time are remarkably convenient,"-Shu Jiawen praised.

With much dragging and coaxing, Qingyang Qingli finally managed to haul this bunch—whose boots seemed glued to the floorboards—into the private VIP box he had reserved for the auction.

Peering outside from the VIP box, the "worldly and experienced" Shu Jiawen let out his third thoroughly bumpkin-like gasp of the day:
—"So many people?!"

There truly were a staggering number of people; looking down from above, the dense sea of dark heads numbered in the tens of thousands.

Even at Carefree Palace's wildly popular Treasure Fair, drawing a few thousand attendees per session was already considered a roaring success.

—"The vast majority down there are low-tier cultivators, mixed with a fair share of mortals,"-Qingyang Qingli said dismissively.

As a Nascent Soul cultivator and the Fifth Master of Eternal Villa, Qingyang Qingli felt that while this Immortal Bazaar was marvelous in every respect, having too many mortals and low-tier cultivators around somewhat diluted the high-society prestige.

However, because the Nascent Soul-tier bazaar was adequately tailored to his status, and tonight was a special occasion, he tolerated it gracefully.

Naturally, the Qingyang Qingli who harbored such thoughts had yet to realize how [quantitative accumulation sparks qualitative transformation](# "Literal CN: 量变引起质变 (text wrote 质变引起量变 as humorous slip/reversal) — the Marxist/economic principle of quantitative change leading to qualitative leaps"), nor did he fathom what monumental economic impact tens of thousands of participants—even low-tier cultivators and mortals—would unleash upon the auction.

He merely turned his eyes toward the center of the auction hall with burning anticipation:
—"Never mind the crowd—the true highlight is about to begin!"

—"Oh?"-Shu Jiawen asked, intrigued:—"I've wanted to ask since the start: you seem extraordinarily hyped for this auction! Could there be some treasure you're determined to secure at all costs?"

—"Not quite the bullseye, but not far off!"-Qingyang Qingli replied smugly, keeping him in suspense.

Seeing his smug posture, Shu Jiawen was just about to press further when a thunderous, rapid roll of drums cut him off.

This was the signal that the auction had commenced.

Shu Jiawen turned his head toward the central auction stage. At some point, an exquisitely crafted incense burner had been placed upon the dais. Swirling tendrils of aromatic smoke rose upward, condensing into a vast expanse of billowy clouds above the auditorium; within the misty sea, glimpses of delicate pavilions, grand terraces, and glittering rarities flickered in and out of sight, transforming the hall into a celestial paradise.

At that moment, the drumbeats swelled with earth-shaking resonance, joined by the solemn chiming of bronze bells, infusing the atmosphere with grandeur and antiquity.

To the rhythm of this cosmic hymn, colossal silhouettes of ancient gods and primordial fiends materialized within the mist, clashing in apocalyptic warfare. Amid their cataclysmic struggle, magnificent palaces shattered into smoldering ruins, priceless treasures were buried beneath dust, mortal creatures mutated, and demonic beasts and monstrous abominations ran rampant across the battered earth.

Just then, a profound sigh echoed through the hall. A figure bent down and retrieved a shattered ancient wine goblet from the muddied earth, then slowly looked up—revealing a round, chubby, yet undeniably dignified countenance projected with razor-sharp clarity in the holographic illusion.

Ordinary cultivators might not recognize this grand luminary, but an elite disciple from a major sect like Shu Jiawen would never mistake that bulbous belly:
—"Boss Tao of the Abyssal Ocean Society?!"

[—"It truly is Boss Tao!"](# "Speaker: Sect Elite")

[—"Why do I feel he looks far more handsome than usual?"](# "Speaker: Sect Elite")

Because of visual effects filters.

Tao Zhu had slapped on a mountain of beauty filters! As a result, his complexion was fair and glowing, and even his potbelly had been slimmed down several sizes, lending him an unexpectedly cuddly charm.

Not only that, when he turned his torso, his silhouette froze in an elegant, stylized ink-wash freeze-frame, accompanied by a sleek, floating name placard. Down in the lower seats, a spectator helpfully explained:

[—"This senior is a Mahayana grandmaster from the Abyssal Ocean Society; he personally sponsored this entire auction!"](# "Speaker: Audience Member")

A chorus of awed gasps rippled through the tens of thousands in the crowd; mingled with the rolling drums, the collective sound swelled like roaring thunderous applause.

Of course, the Boss Tao in the projection offered no response. Following the freeze-frame, he returned to his filter-drenched 'realistic' (?) self, seemed to murmur a parting line toward the projection array, and extended the battered wine goblet forward.

A hand reached out and received the goblet.

It was a breathtakingly gorgeous hand—slender fingers, distinct knuckles, fair and pristine as white jade. Merely holding the battered wine vessel, it imparted an aura that made the relic appear as priceless as an ancient divine artifact.

One could not help but imagine the peerless grace and transcendent stature of its owner.

[—"Could this be... the Lord Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"](# "Speaker: Audience Member")

Someone whispered under their breath, as though raising their voice even a fraction would blaspheme the owner of that celestial hand.

Regrettably, the legendary Untiring Immortal Venerable had zero intention of showing his face like Boss Tao. That elegant hand merely flicked upward with a graceful wave, tossing the wine goblet into the firmament.

In midair, the encrusted grime and heavy mud were swept clean from the vessel, revealing its pristine form, before it transformed into a radiant chess piece that plummeted from the heavens—landing squarely in front of a ferocious abomination poised to slaughter innocent mortals!

Shielding the mortals, that chess piece shifted once more, morphing into a young master clad in fluttering white robes, wielding a delicate folding fan!

The youth snapped his folding fan open with practiced panache, effortlessly batting the beast away with a single debonair stroke!

The masses in the auction hall erupted into a roaring cheer: [—"Ahhh!"](# "Speaker: Audience")

Shu Jiawen and his companions let out a stunned cry: [—"Ah?!"](# "Speaker: Shu Jiawen")

While Qingyang Qingli let out a dreamy sigh of pure self-intoxication:
—"Ahhh..."

Just like Boss Tao, the projection of the white-robed youth froze at his absolute most dashing angle in an ink-wash portrait, beside which floated a sleek biographical tag:

> Eternal Villa: Qingyang Qingli.

—"Why on earth is it *you*?!"-Shu Jiawen demanded in utter disbelief.

Boss Tao appearing in the opening cinematic made complete sense—after all, the man had funded the entire event with hard cash. But what in the world did Qingyang Qingli have to do with anything?!

Qingyang Qingli gave his folding fan a smug, leisurely flutter, refusing to spoil the mystery.

Yet Shu Jiawen had no time to interrogate him, for right after the projection of Qingyang Qingli struck his heroic stance, another monstrous abomination lunged from the shadows to flank him from behind! At that critical instant, a pair of thick, ironclad arms blocked the creature's clawed assault, obliterating the beast in a single crushing counterstrike!

A strapping, battle-hardened figure stood firm as an ancient mountain pine directly behind Qingyang Qingli.

[—"It's Bai Wubin of Blood Demon Hall!"](# "Speaker: Audience Member")

[—"Why is he in this too—?!"](# "Speaker: Audience Member")

Spectators murmured in bewilderment as more faces—some famous, some obscure—flashed across the holographic projection. There were rogue cultivators, sect disciples, and clan heirs; the only common denominator was that every single one of them was a cultivator from the Central Domain!

Finally, the portraits of these heroic cultivators converged into a glorious mosaic poster, beneath which blazed three monumental characters: *Immortal Chess*.

This cinematic promotional poster settled behind the grand dais like an imposing backdrop.

—"Fellow Daoists, welcome, one and all, to the Tianling Auction,"-a clear, melodious voice rang across the auditorium.

A breathtakingly gorgeous figure stepped to the center of the auction stage. He was so dazzlingly handsome that everyone whose eyes had been glued to the cinematic backdrop instantly snapped their full attention onto him.

—"That is the second disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Qu Qianxing,"-Qingyang Qingli introduced.

—"I had long heard he was an exceptional beauty, but seeing him with my own eyes, he is vastly more breathtaking than the rumors,"-Shu Jiawen sighed in awe:—"Brother Qingyang, did you manage to get acquainted with him during your stay?"

Shu Jiawen harbored no nefarious intentions.

Faced with a paragon of beauty like Qu Qianxing, any normal human being would naturally wish to strike up an acquaintance.

Yet Qingyang Qingli's expression turned awkward:
—"Er... I forgot..."

Clearly, when he first arrived at Mount Tianling and saw Qu Qianxing, he had fully intended to introduce himself. But afterward, the Immortal Bazaar had barraged him with so many mind-blowing delights that he had clean forgotten all about it!

Shu Jiawen stared in utter disbelief:
—"A beauty of that caliber, and you *forgot*?! What on earth were you doing here all this time?! Wait, you still haven't told me: how in the world did you end up in that opening cinematic—?!"

—"*Ahem!* Isn't Qu Qianxing explaining it right now?"-Qingyang Qingli coughed, barely suppressing his overwhelming urge to brag as he feigned an aloof wave of his fan toward Qu Qianxing on the stage.

Shu Jiawen shot him a deeply suspicious glare, then turned his gaze toward Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing had just concluded his opening remarks, cutting straight to the chase:
—"...Without further ado, let us unveil our very first auction lot—"

—"Immortal Chess."

—"Most of you have undoubtedly heard of it at the bazaar. This game was merely a casual leisure creation of my Master, yet it garnered universal adoration across the realms. In accordance with your passionate feedback, over the course of a rigorous month-and-a-half round-robin tournament, we selected the finest talents best suited to represent our Central Domain from among the various immortal factions attending the bazaar, crafting them into this premier set of figurines."

As Qu Qianxing spoke, he gave a graceful wave toward the background projection. The grand mosaic poster separated once more, transforming into a ring of dashing, holographic cards orbiting the auction dais, drawing another wave of collective gasps from the audience.

Shu Jiawen, however, couldn't stomach it any longer:
—"'Finest talents'?!"-he glared furiously at Qingyang Qingli.

—"Indeed! None other than yours truly!"

Qingyang Qingli finally dropped all pretenses, lifting his chin in supreme triumph:
—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect launched this game called Immortal Chess, but struggled to find suitable prototypes for the character pieces. Through a month and a half of relentless grinding and struggle, I vanquished all contenders, forcing them to acknowledge me as the premier representative of the Central Domain's elite cultivators!"

—"'Premier representative of elite cultivators'?!"-Shu Jiawen echoed like a broken record.

—"Precisely,"-Qingyang Qingli nodded smugly.

—"Absolute hogwash! The only reason you were chosen is because I wasn't here!"-Shu Jiawen bellowed.

The other sect scions chimed in with noisy agreement.

Qingyang Qingli eyed them with utter disdain:
—"Pure, unadulterated jealousy."

He would rather die than admit that what Shu Jiawen said was the gospel truth!

It was precisely because the competition for Immortal Chess roster slots had grown cutthroat that Qingyang Qingli—who had initially penned letters urging Shu Jiawen and the others to visit the bazaar—abruptly changed his tune and gave evasive, lukewarm non-answers whenever they inquired about the market!

He hadn't recorded the exhibition displays onto jade slips solely because the Immortal Chess round-robin tournament had entered its fiercest climax at the time, leaving him zero bandwidth for anything else in his obsessive quest to secure the championship crown!

And all that sweat and tears had paid off in spades!

Just seeing his glorious visage featured front and center in the auction's opening cinematic before tens of thousands of viewers—at this moment, Qingyang Qingli certainly didn't look down on the crowd's low cultivation bases—was enough to make him swell with pride for the next three centuries.

Meanwhile, on the auction stage, Qu Qianxing presented the very first lot:
—"...Aside from the standard and deluxe editions sold in the storefronts, tonight, as our inaugural opening lot, we present the one-and-only Limited Edition Crystal Immortal Chess! The raw materials were handpicked by the Mahayana grandmaster Tao Zhu, and personally blessed and empowered by my venerable Master. It is unique beneath heaven, peerlessly noble, and incomparably elegant."

—"And now, we shall commence the first round of bidding—"

Following Qu Qianxing's announcement, a set of crystal-clear, translucent figurines materialized upon the auction dais. The craftsmanship was extraordinarily exquisite, every character sculpted with lifelike precision; paired with a set of sleek, foil-embossed holographic cards, even those who had never played the game felt their fingers itch with the urge to place a bid.

Shu Jiawen glared at Qingyang Qingli with seething jealousy, his knuckles turning white as he clenched his fists:
