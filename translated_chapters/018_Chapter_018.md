# Chapter 18: Secret Realm Slots

The emissaries of the Heavenly Dao Alliance certainly hadn't traveled all this way just to sit for an examination.

While Zhou Hui was puzzling over the matter on his end, elsewhere—as the cloud carriage returned to the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Central Domain Abyssal Ocean Branch Hall—someone was inquiring with He Yong about Zhou Hui as well.

Still, when it came to a [Tribulation Transcendence titan](# "Literal CN: 渡劫期大佬 — supreme powerhouse at the pinnacle of mortal cultivation"), even though the Alliance's Abyssal Ocean Branch Hall was thousands of kilometers away from Mount Tianling, nobody possessed Su Xin's [dog-like audacity](# "Literal CN: 狗胆子 (gǒudǎnzi) — reckless, foolish nerve; brazen insolence") to speak ill of him.

In fact, throughout the entire Central Domain, none of them even dared to utter Zhou Hui's true name aloud.

Liu Chang—He Yong's subordinate and a Protector of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Abyssal Ocean Hall—dared only verify in cautious, hushed tones:

—"Hall Master, regarding the Heavenly Spirit Sect..."-Liu Chang began tentatively.

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable is doing just fine. It appears he suffered not the slightest harm during the heavenly tribulation,"-He Yong replied in utter exhaustion.

His startlingly blunt answer gave Liu Chang quite a fright.

—"He... he might 'hear' us!"-Liu Chang stammered.

—"It doesn't matter anymore,"-He Yong muttered. In any case, far worse words had already been recited verbatim by the man's direct disciple. While He Yong had no idea what the Immortal Venerable himself actually thought, he had already thrown in the towel and [given up on life](# "Literal CN: 自暴自弃 (zì bào zì qì) — resigned to one's fate; completely abandoning oneself in despair").-"Besides, it's not like he would actually annihilate our Heavenly Dao Alliance, right?!"

[...Has the situation escalated to the point of total annihilation?!]

Realizing that the outcome of this diplomatic visit had been far from ideal, Liu Chang grew deeply concerned.

—"Then what should we do about the quota distribution for the secret realm?"-Liu Chang asked.

—"The secret realm..."-He Yong murmured.

Then, a sheet of cold sweat instantly broke across his back.

[The secret realm... I completely forgot to bring it up!]

[No, rather, I never found a single chance to bring it up in the first place.]

He Yong thought, clutching his aching temples.

His visit to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, while primarily intended to probe Zhou Hui's current condition, still required diplomatic decorum. As an emissary of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, barging onto someone's mountain gate and saying outright, "We came to check whether you were struck dead by lightning," would have been atrociously tactless—the sort of boorish idiocy only a [trust-fund cultivator](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — child of powerful, wealthy cultivators; equivalent of rich second generation (富二代)") like Su Xin was capable of pulling off. On the surface, therefore, He Yong had arranged a perfectly legitimate pretense for his visit.

And "exploring the secret realm" was precisely that legitimate pretense.

In fact, this secret realm had a direct connection to Zhou Hui himself.

When Zhou Hui underwent his heavenly tribulation, the summoned lightning had been so utterly earth-shattering that a colossal spiritual storm radiated outward across the Central Domain with Mount Tianling as its epicenter. The gale had blown away the seal on a certain mountain peak, laying bare the entrance to a subterranean secret realm beneath.

This was, naturally, a stroke of immense good fortune.

In the cultivation world, every secret realm represented a treasure trove of serendipity and resources—something cultivators dreamed of finding.

The only hitch was that once the seal blew wide open, a surge of sword qi accompanied by rosy celestial radiance shot straight into the ninth heaven, alerting every cultivation sect within a thousand-mile radius!

Past experience dictated that any secret realm whose mere [cracking open of a canned lid](# "Literal CN: 开个罐头 (kāi gè guàntou) — hilarious modern colloquialism for popping open a sealed ancient dungeon/tomb")... *ahem*, unsealing produced such spectacular fanfare was clearly no mundane pocket realm, but a discovery of monumental value!

In an instant, the entire Central Domain [erupted like a bubbling cauldron](# "Literal CN: 跟炸了锅似的 — boiling over in sudden uproar; in total chaos"). Every single sect was desperate to [claim a slice of the pie](# "Literal CN: 分杯羹 (fēn bēi gēng) — share a cup of broth; take a cut of the spoils or profits").

Yet occurrences like this were fairly commonplace in the cultivation world, governed by established convention:

Whenever an ownerless realm was uncovered without any single sect securing the initial advantage, standard protocol dictated that they [shelve disputes for joint exploration](# "Literal CN: 搁置争议，共同探索 — cultivation spoof of modern diplomatic policy 'shelving differences and pursuing joint development'"). Every sect within a thousand miles wishing to join would dispatch an expedition team to enter simultaneously. A strict time limit would be imposed, during which everyone relied upon their own prowess to plun—*cough*, find whatever treasures they could carry.

As for how many cultivators each sect could deploy, the order of entry, whether anyone was smuggling in excessively overpowered magical treasures, and a myriad of similar disputes—mediating such matters fell squarely under the Heavenly Dao Alliance's jurisdiction.

Naturally, the Heavenly Dao Alliance never performed charity work.

They would naturally dispatch their own agents to 'assess' the secret realm's value, procuring a few 'samples' along the way. Securing those spoils was precisely why Su Xin had come along in the first place.

Before anyone could step foot inside, however, the paramount issue on the table was the quota of slots allocated to each sect's exploration team—a number that directly dictated how much of an early lead they could seize within the realm.

Half of He Yong's reason for visiting Mount Tianling had been to address this very issue.

No matter how one looked at it, the discovery of this secret realm was tied directly to Zhou Hui's tribulation; the Heavenly Spirit Sect was undeniably entitled to exploration slots.

Who could have imagined that the Heavenly Spirit Sect would be holding a grand unified examination? Both He Yong and Su Xin had gotten so completely wrapped up in sitting for exams that neither of them remembered to utter a single word about the matter to Zhou Hui!

He Yong naturally had no desire to recount such a humiliating ordeal in front of his subordinate.

—"Ahem, we will discuss this matter later,"-He Yong hedged.-"The Untiring Immortal Venerable just finished crossing his heavenly tribulation and is terribly busy at the moment!"
...Busy giving his disciples written exams, that is.

—"How can we put it off until later?"-Liu Chang protested anxiously.-"The other sects care deeply about this realm! Inquiries have been pouring in non-stop over the past few days, and some sects have even pitched camp right outside our gates, praying we will announce the expedition quotas as soon as possible!"

Liu Chang paused, lowering his voice:

—"Since the Heavenly Spirit Sect doesn't seem to care, perhaps their slots could be..."-Liu Chang trailed off suggestively.

—"What utter nonsense are you spouting?!"-He Yong snapped.
The Untiring Immortal Venerable hadn't 'ascended' yet, for heaven's sake!

Of course, if the Heavenly Spirit Sect's supreme pillar of support were no longer around, that would be an entirely different story.

—"Until the Heavenly Spirit Sect explicitly states they have no need for them, we cannot arbitrarily strip away their eligibility,"-He Yong said coldly.

[—"And what if they never state their position?"](# "Speaker: Liu Chang")

—"Then we shall proceed according to precedent,"-He Yong muttered evasively.

A look of instant comprehension dawned on Liu Chang's face.

The so-called 'precedent' was that because the Untiring Immortal Venerable was deeply immersed in cultivation and never meddled in outside affairs, yet face had to be accorded to a Tribulation Transcendence titan's sect, they would allocate the bare minimum quota as a token gesture to demonstrate that formal notice had been served.

In truth, that was precisely what He Yong intended.

[I am not setting foot in the Heavenly Spirit Sect ever again!]

—"I shall go see the representatives from the other sects first,"-He Yong said, looking for an excuse to escape.-"We will finalize whatever arrangements can be settled for now."

With that, he lifted his foot to head toward the front hall, but Liu Chang chimed in once more:

—"Ah, should we not summon Martial Uncle Su?"-Liu Chang asked.

Liu Chang did not actually belong to Su Xin's branch of the sect, but he shamelessly followed He Yong's lead in addressing Su Xin as "Martial Uncle," making his desperate eagerness to [cling to a golden thigh](# "Literal CN: 抱大腿 (bào dàtuǐ) — curry favor with an influential patron or backer") abundantly clear.

Yet ever since returning from the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Su Xin had locked himself inside his quarters for reasons unknown, barely sparing Liu Chang the time of day.

"..." Knowing full well what Liu Chang was scheming, He Yong sneered:

[—"He won't be coming. He is in no mood to deal with this right now."](# "Speaker: He Yong")

In He Yong's view, Su Xin shouldn't have been meddling in this business to begin with.

As a [cultivation trust-fund brat](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — child of powerful, wealthy cultivators; equivalent of rich second generation (富二代)"), Su Xin was merely tagging along to leach treasures from the secret realm, yet he carried himself as though he were the true authority of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, attempting to dictate the quota distribution itself.

From a certain perspective, the fact that Su Xin's ego had taken a catastrophic beating was the sole silver lining of this journey to Mount Tianling.

He Yong certainly had no fondness for having an entitled, willful [deadweight burden](# "Literal CN: 拖油瓶 (tuōyóupíng) — drag-oil-bottle; colloquial term for a troublesome dependent or burdensome tagalong") interfering with his official duties.

At that thought, He Yong instructed Liu Chang:

—"You may go humor Little Martial Uncle. Taking him out for some amusement is fine, but do not venture anywhere overly dangerous."

[—"Understood!"](# "Speaker: Liu Chang")

Liu Chang answered eagerly, though a subtle, unusual gleam flickered deep within his eyes.

*

[—"What could there possibly be to enjoy in this godforsaken dump?"](# "Speaker: Su Xin")

Su Xin grumbled listlessly.

He would never admit that the reason he dared not step outside was fear of that damned Un-Grinding Immortal! Nor would he ever confess that he felt too utterly humiliated to show his face because he couldn't even outperform ordinary disciples on an examination!

—"Heheh, this place naturally cannot compare to the flourishing Southern Domain,"-Liu Chang coaxed with a conciliatory chuckle.-"Ever since the ancient War of Gods and Demons, most of the Central Domain's lands have been eroded by the Abyssal Ocean. It can't even sustain a decently sized mortal dynasty, let alone rival the prosperity of the South—yet chaos possesses its own distinct charms!"

He lowered his voice as he delivered that final remark.

Su Xin had originally lacked any interest in the Central Domain's dull history, but upon hearing the conspiratorial pitch of Liu Chang's voice, he couldn't help casting a curious glance his way. Rather than elaborating, however, Liu Chang shot a cautious look toward the bevy of lovely maidservants serving tea and pouring water around Su Xin.

Su Xin instantly caught his drift.

Drawing upon his wealth of experience as a [cultivation second-generation heir](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — child of powerful, wealthy cultivators; equivalent of rich second generation (富二代)"), he knew full well what kind of 'amusement' Liu Chang was hinting at, and promptly waved the maidservants away.

Liu Chang broke into a sly chuckle:

—"Martial Uncle Su, have you ever heard of the [Palace of Carefree Wandering](# "Literal CN: 逍遥宫 (Xiāoyáo Gōng) — major merchant guild and commercial entertainment network")?"-Liu Chang asked.

—"The Palace of Carefree Wandering?"-Su Xin's brow furrowed, his budding interest snuffed out in a heartbeat.-"That's it? I've been there countless times. I grew bored of them long ago."

Such blithe dismissal was something only a [trust-fund cultivator](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — child of powerful, wealthy cultivators; equivalent of rich second generation (富二代)") of his caliber could utter.

Even in the Northern Domain where cultivators gathered in legions, the Palace of Carefree Wandering ranked among the foremost commercial trading guilds. They dealt in every manner of exotic treasure and miraculous elixir, establishing guildhalls, fine taverns, and auction houses across major cultivation settlements. Most celebrated of all was their decennial Curio Fair—a grand convergence of commerce, leisure, and entertainment that every cultivator yearned to attend. It was even lauded with the saying: ["Above lies the Heavenly Immortal Realm; below sits the Carefree Palace."](# "Literal CN: 上有天仙境，下有逍遥宫 — play on the famous Chinese proverb 'Up above is heaven; down below are Suzhou and Hangzhou' (上有天堂，下有苏杭)")

—"What bores you is merely the Carefree Palace in the Southern Domain,"-Liu Chang said with a knowing grin.-"That is the home turf of our Heavenly Dao Alliance, teeming with prestigious orthodox sects. Naturally, the rules there are stiflingly strict; indulging in anything remotely thrilling is immediately decried as scandalous and depraved. But the Central Domain is another matter entirely! There are no decent factions to speak of here. Thanks to the relics of the ancient gods and demons, bizarre creatures abound, and strange treasures frequently surface from the depths. The possibilities for entertainment are endless! Even our Heavenly Dao Alliance can only ensure that things remain orderly on the surface."

—"Heh, the Heavenly Dao Alliance's influence is feeble in the Central Domain, yet you make it sound like a blessing in disguise,"-Su Xin laughed.

Yet the very fact that he laughed meant his interest had indeed been piqued.

Sensing an opening, Liu Chang pressed his advantage:

—"Naturally, it isn't an admirable state of affairs, yet as a venue for occasional novel thrills and illicit pleasures, it is delightfully entertaining. Take the Palace of Carefree Wandering, for instance: a great deal of their inventory is sourced directly from the Central Domain. To avoid tarnishing their pristine reputation, however, they operate under an alternate name here: the [Palace of Fallen Immortals](# "Literal CN: 堕仙宫 (Duòxiān Gōng) — illicit, shady alter-ego of Xiaoyao Palace operating in the lawless Central Domain"). Tsk tsk, with a name like that, you can already guess what kind of sordid business they conduct."

At this point, Liu Chang let out a leering chuckle.

Tempted by Liu Chang's pitch, Su Xin still maintained an aloof posture:

—"I refuse to set foot in any place that is excessively filthy."

—"My good Little Martial Uncle, what are you saying? I wouldn't dare recommend a truly vile dive to you! I still value my life!"-Liu Chang immediately [climbed the trellis](# "Literal CN: 顺着杆子爬 (shùnzhe gānzi pá) — taking advantage of an opening; running with the cue").-"The Palace of Fallen Immortals is, at the end of the day, merely the [sockpuppet identity](# "Literal CN: 马甲 (mǎjiǎ) — modern Chinese internet slang for an alter-ego, disguise, or front organization") of the Palace of Carefree Wandering. No matter how unruly it gets, there is a limit to how base it can be—it is simply far bolder and unconstrained than usual. What is more, they recently completed construction on a new cave-manor, and with the sudden unearthing of the new secret realm, they are currently hosting an inter-sect assembly! It is at the absolute height of bustling excitement, which is why I dared suggest taking a look."

—"In that case, let us go have a look,"-Su Xin said.

Su Xin also felt that he desperately needed to go out and indulge in some proper revelry, if only to purge every last trace of his ordeal at the Heavenly Spirit Sect from his mind.

Little did Su Xin suspect that after showering him with another round of lavish flattery and taking his leave, the fawning smile vanished from Liu Chang's face. Turning to a servant, he commanded in a chilling voice:

[—"Notify the Palace of Fallen Immortals that I have delivered what they requested! As for whether they can pry additional secret realm exploration slots out of this young master's hands, that will depend entirely on their own abilities."](# "Speaker: Liu Chang")
