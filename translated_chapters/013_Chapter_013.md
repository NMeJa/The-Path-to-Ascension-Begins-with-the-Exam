# Chapter 13: Such a Bizarre Request

—"This guest wants to sit for the exam?"-Zhou Hui raised an eyebrow.

Even as a veteran [King of Involution](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — modern slang for someone who out-works, out-studies, and out-grinds everyone else in hyper-competitive environments"), he had never once done something like sprinting over to a neighboring school demanding to take their final exams. This bizarre request compelled the Untiring Immortal Venerable to scrutinize this visiting guest a few extra times.

He Yong and Su Xin were standing respectfully at the foot of the jade stairs inside the Palace of Silence.

Even someone as arrogant and willful as Su Xin wore an expression of dutiful, endearing innocence before the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Just as Su Xin himself had claimed, he possessed extensive experience when it came to handling Tribulation Transcendence seniors.

In short, nothing about the boy suggested he harbored the ambition to conquer a neighboring school.

Zhou Hui immediately deduced the identity of the true culprit, glaring sharply at his eldest disciple, Yuwen Xuan, who stood by wearing a look of complete innocence.

[I shouldn't have taken him down just because guests were arriving and having him strung up on the mountain gate looked unsightly!]

Zhou Hui thought, nursing a throbbing headache.

Over the course of any teacher's pedagogical career, they were bound to encounter a few banes of their existence. Without question, Yuwen Xuan was the one [dancing on his nerves](# "Literal CN: 在心头蹦迪 (zài xīntóu bèngdí) — dancing on someone's heart/nerves, pushing all their buttons").

This fellow was an [agent of pure chaos](# "Literal CN: 乐子人 (lèzǐrén) — someone who lives purely for the amusement, drama, and chaotic entertainment of a situation"), having taken bringing disaster upon all living beings as his personal mission since childhood.

What was worse, he was impervious to reason and bowed only to force. Exhausting every gentle word in the language proved useless; only a savage thrashing could ever make him behave.

Zhou Hui was already about as averse to corporal punishment as a teacher could get, yet he had been driven by this unfilial disciple into resorting to coercive measures.

When Yuwen Xuan was small—specifically, before age three—things had been manageable; Zhou Hui only needed to pinch the scruff of his neck to quiet him down. When he grew a bit older, Zhou Hui had to find a spot to hang him up so he could reflect on his actions. Later on, Immortal-Binding Ropes had to be deployed. Later still, even Immortal-Binding Ropes couldn't hold him, leaving physical incapacitation as the sole recourse—such as snapping his cervical vertebrae.

Yet ever since Yuwen Xuan entered the peak of the Soul Formation Stage, snapping his neck no longer seemed particularly effective either.

This time, he had been suspended from the tree for half a month. When brought down, not only did he fail to show the slightest hint of having learned his lesson, but his cultivation had actually surged a notch, showing faint signs of breaking through his bottleneck.

Seeing him like this, Zhou Hui hadn't dared let Yuwen Xuan take the exam.

In another world, Zhou Hui had learned an essential truth: never lump geniuses and ordinary mortals together.

Lumping the two together might not necessarily affect the genius, but it would unquestionably wreck the ordinary people.

In particular, Zhou Hui had only just begun rolling out the examination system. If this bastard led the disciples to believe that 'exams have nothing to do with cultivation,' or worse, 'the poorer one's exam scores, the higher one's cultivation grows,' everything would be ruined.

After all, if everyone followed Yuwen Xuan's method of cultivation, every single disciple would have to be strung up on trees.

Zhou Hui had exempted Yuwen Xuan from the exam, yet feared he would wander off elsewhere to stir up trouble. Ultimately, considering the fellow was a seasoned connoisseur of feasting, drinking, and merrymaking, Zhou Hui had dispatched him to receive the guests.

Evidently, that had been a colossal mistake.

Zhou Hui had never dreamed that Yuwen Xuan would escort the guests straight into the examination hall.

—"What is the meaning of this? Didn't I tell you to entertain them? I didn't tell you to drag people in to sit for an exam!"-Zhou Hui interrogated him directly inside his mind, enveloping Yuwen Xuan in his divine sense.

—"Come now, the guests spotted the commotion around the Palace of Silence from miles away and looked eager to interrogate us the second they stepped out of the carriage. I figured explaining it wouldn't clear anything up, and giving them a tour would disrupt everyone's test-taking performance. So why not just let them take the exam as well?"-Yuwen Xuan rationalized with self-righteous confidence in his mind.-"That way, we spare the Heavenly Dao Alliance from harboring unnecessary misunderstandings about us."

—"You certainly always have an excuse,"-Zhou Hui sneered coldly under his breath.

Turning toward the visitors from the Heavenly Dao Alliance, he said candidly:

—"Our guests may have misunderstood. This Venerable merely wished to evaluate the cultivation progress of our sect's disciples; this is nothing more than an ordinary examination. My disciple here is unruly and stubborn. In our last assessment, he even placed [dead last](# "Literal CN: 倒数第一 (dàoshǔ dì-yī) — bottom of the ranks / dead last"), losing to his younger brothers and sisters. Fearing he harbors bitter resentment, he likely dragged you here purely to seek amusement at your expense."

—"Hurry up and apologize to our guests!"-Zhou Hui snapped, glaring at Yuwen Xuan once more.

Unfortunately, the guests didn't buy a single word.

[An internal sect exam creating such an immense spectacle, with every single disciple participating and a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster personally presiding over it?!]

[There's definitely some unrevealable heavenly secret at play, without a shadow of a doubt!]

Su Xin felt all the more thrilled.

Yet none of that showed on his face. Instead, he put on the demeanor of an earnest, studious, well-behaved boy—the exact model elders adored most:

—"Though it was a misunderstanding, this junior is deeply grateful to Senior Brother Yuwen for granting this opportunity,"-Su Xin said.-"My father and mother have always taught me never to [work behind closed doors](# "Literal CN: 闭门造车 (bì mén zào chē) — working in isolation without consulting the real world"), but to absorb the strengths of various schools. This junior has long revered the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and hopes to experience the pedagogical style of the Heavenly Spirit Sect firsthand."

Su Xin delivered this speech so smoothly that goosebumps erupted all over He Yong beside him.

Had he not witnessed this little ancestor cause catastrophes in the Heavenly Dao Alliance and play the dutiful child before his parents, He Yong might actually have believed him.

Zhou Hui turned his full attention to Su Xin:

—"You are...?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"The son of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Guardian Elders, Su Mei and Xin Zhu,"-Su Xin replied. Before Zhou Hui, he dared not conceal his true identity.

—"Fellow Daoists Mei and Zhu?"-Zhou Hui murmured thoughtfully.

Once one cultivated to the Tribulation Transcendence Stage, age and social standing ceased to matter.

Zhou Hui was several thousand years younger than Su Mei and Xin Zhu, belonging to a different generation, and was thus not particularly close with them. Yet, being fellow Tribulation Transcendence peers, he had attended several gatherings of Daoists at the same level, leaving him with a deep impression of the profound devotion between the couple.

However, at their most recent gathering—which, by his count, had occurred over twenty years ago—the couple had uncharacteristically refrained from [stuffing dog food down everyone's throats](# "Literal CN: 塞狗粮 (sāi gǒuliáng) — public displays of affection, forcing single people ('single dogs') to eat dog food"). Instead, from the moment the gathering began, they had endlessly boasted about how adorable, exceptionally gifted, and obediently sweet their newborn child was. Back then, that had made Zhou Hui—perpetually driven to wit's end by Yuwen Xuan—ferociously envious.

[So, this must be that child?]

He truly did look quite well-behaved, and possessed quite a thirst for learning. Even upon traveling out to such a remote countryside for leisure, he hadn't forgotten to sit for an exam at the local school.

Zhou Hui was deeply touched.

It just so happened that he also wanted to see the caliber of an honor student produced by a [prestigious key academy](# "Literal CN: 重点院校 (zhòngdiǎn yuànxiào) — top tier university or school") like the Heavenly Dao Alliance, lest his own standards become disconnected from the cultivation world's normal educational baseline. He nodded.

—"In that case, you may give it a try,"-Zhou Hui said, nodding.

—"Yes!"-Su Xin chirped in delight.

He Yong watched helplessly as Su Xin trotted off, wanting to intervene yet hesitating.

Earlier, Zhou Hui's heavenly tribulation had swept through like a violent tornado. Mount Tianling, situated at the eye of the storm, had actually suffered the least damage. Outside Mount Tianling, however, across the entire Abyssal Ocean and extending into half of the Central Domain, a spiritual energy tempest had raged for a full week. Many cultivators had been affected, and a major incident had even occurred—which was precisely why Su Xin had been dispatched to the Abyssal Ocean.

Consequently, He Yong had been keeping a close eye on Mount Tianling throughout this period. Naturally, he had caught wind of the rumors regarding the 'heavenly secrets,' which was why he had hurried over now under the guise of a 'coincidental visit'—all to gauge the Untiring Immortal Venerable's actual condition.

Judging by appearances now, the Untiring Immortal Venerable was clearly unharmed, even if his behavior was rather abnormal.

For someone who abhorred extravagant fanfare to stage a grand sect examination and blow it up into such an immense spectacle—surely it had something to do with the 'heavenly secrets,' didn't it?

He Yong was intensely curious about what those 'heavenly secrets' were, yet simultaneously worried this might be a trap. If his grandmaster's precious son met with any mishap here, he was as good as dead.

Torn in an agonizing dilemma, He Yong gritted his teeth and petitioned:

—"Immortal Venerable, please grant me a copy of the exam paper as well!"-He Yong requested.

—"You wish to sit for the exam too?"-Zhou Hui looked at He Yong in astonishment.

—"Ahem. I... I merely wish to take a look,"-He Yong said awkwardly.

[Understood. It's just a peer in the field wanting to inspect a colleague's exam paper!] Truly worthy of the Heavenly Dao Alliance: not only were the students eager to learn, but the teachers never forgot academic exchange either.

Zhou Hui nodded, gesturing for a disciple to hand over a test paper.

—"This exam paper can be synchronized with Worthy Nephew Su's... ahem, it has the exact same questions,"-Zhou Hui said.

He Yong didn't quite grasp what 'synchronized' or 'the exact same questions' meant:

[If everyone is sitting for an exam together, could it be that each test paper has different questions?]

Perplexed, he scrutinized the test paper:

[Hmm? Why are these all questions for the Qi Condensation and Foundation Establishment stages? And they seem deceptively simple?]

He Yong knitted his brows. Like most people who knew nothing about modern pedagogy, he formed a certain misunderstanding regarding multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions. Furthermore, his thoughts took another turn: if Zhou Hui had truly comprehended 'heavenly secrets,' how could he disclose them so casually?

[He probably deliberately gave a simplified paper to Su Xin, offering it as a gesture of goodwill to my grandmasters!]

He Yong deduced.

Small wonder he thought that way.

Most sects, upon learning of Su Xin's background, would gladly extend such courtesy. Thus were born the rumors of this honor student—that 'Fellow Daoist Su was endowed with extraordinary natural talent, inheriting his parents' supreme cultivation gifts.'

He Yong instantly breathed a sigh of relief. Even if he hadn't uncovered the heavenly secrets, as long as the little ancestor came to no harm, all was well.
