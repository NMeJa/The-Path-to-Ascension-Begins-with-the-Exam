# Chapter 46: Suspicion and Inner Demons (第46章 暗鬼疑心)

Thousand Beasts Hall was certainly not here to lodge a complaint.

In fact, Yan Bailang, Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall, desperately wished the Heavenly Spirit Sect would secretly stir up trouble—instigating some reckless disciple who didn't know life from death to provoke him, whom he could strike down, uncover some incriminating clue, and then march up to their mountain gate with righteous indignation to [sever diplomatic ties](# "Literal CN: 断交 (duànjiāo) — to break off relations / sever diplomatic ties").

Yes, sever ties!

Thousand Beasts Hall wanted to sever ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Even though, aside from being geographical neighbors, the two sects ordinarily had zero dealings—neither between sect leaders nor between disciples. (Oh, recently some Thousand Beasts Hall disciples would sneak off to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's secret realm to spend money, but that was a purely commercial exchange of spirit stones without any real personal connection.) There was no communication whatsoever, yet Thousand Beasts Hall still desperately wanted to announce a formal severance of ties.

Without a doubt, this so-called "severance" was purely a matter of political alignment.

Naturally, Thousand Beasts Hall didn't want to do this without cause; the whole affair went right back to Carefree Palace.

Carefree Palace's trade route blockade had no direct impact on the Heavenly Spirit Sect, which had always kept to itself and operated in solitude. But for Thousand Beasts Hall, it was a massive blow.

As the name implied, Thousand Beasts Hall specialized in rearing and taming spirit beasts.

Due to the Central Domain's chaotic environmental problems, spirit beasts and demonic beasts abounded. Every fifty years, a [beast tide](# "Literal CN: 兽潮 (shòucháo) — beast stampede/surge caused by spiritual energy storms") would even erupt—a horrific calamity where surplus demonic beasts, driven mad by spiritual energy storms, rampaged through human settlements. It was precisely this environment that turned Thousand Beasts Hall—practicing beast taming, a cultivation branch considered niche and unorthodox in other domains—into the second-largest sect in the Central Domain.

The trouble was: human cultivators could undergo [inedia](# "Literal CN: 辟谷 (bìgǔ) — fasting from mortal food through cultivation"), but no matter how high a spirit beast or demonic beast's cultivation reached, it could never fast!

Consequently, for Thousand Beasts Hall to rear myriad beasts, it required astronomical quantities of animal feed.

Yet, also due to these chaotic environmental problems, the Central Domain was thoroughly inhospitable to normal plant growth. It wasn't that plants would wither and die; rather, they mutated with alarming ease. When that happened, whether plants ate animals or animals ate plants became a very real question. What was worse, even if a Central Domain plant could be eaten and tasted quite delicious, who knew what it would do to an animal's brain?

To ensure their reared spirit beasts could grow without going raving mad, rabid, or mutating into abominations, Thousand Beasts Hall had to purchase vast shipments of ordinary grain from other domains each year to serve as animal feed.

The moment Carefree Palace blockaded the trade routes, Thousand Beasts Hall was in dire straits.

Thousand Beasts Hall had no choice but to negotiate with Carefree Palace. Carefree Palace's stance was crystal clear:

"Publicly announce that you have severed diplomatic ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and we will ship your grain—with a [0.01% discount](# "Literal CN: 九点九九折 (jiǔ diǎn jiǔ jiǔ zhé) — 9.99-fold price, i.e., 99.9% of full price, a laughable 0.1% off")!"

In truth, this was Carefree Palace's real objective all along.

They weren't fools; they knew blockading trade routes wouldn't affect the Heavenly Spirit Sect. The reason they blockaded them was to [hold the emperor hostage to command the feudal lords](# "Literal CN: 挟天子以令诸侯 (xié tiānzǐ yǐ lìng zhūhóu) — historical idiom from the Three Kingdoms era, coercing everyone via strategic leverage")—forcing the major sects of the Central Domain to pick a side and isolating the Heavenly Spirit Sect into complete embargo.

Don't think this move was useless; it was actually an extraordinarily venomous gambit.

"A sect that keeps to itself, self-contained and with no interaction with others" and "a sect that is actively despised and ostracized by everyone else" were two completely different concepts—it could even influence [karmic providence](# "Literal CN: 气运 (qìyùn) — cosmic fortune, sect destiny, and heavenly favor")!

After all, this was the cultivation world. "When the Son of Heaven lacks virtue, disaster falls upon the people" was not empty rhetoric. If a mortal emperor acted perversely and lost the hearts of his people, he would inevitably lose the blessing of heaven's mandate; the nation's providence would wither, and droughts, floods, and plagues would erupt everywhere.

The exact same principle applied to cultivation sects.

If a sect was universally despised by all other sects, it would naturally be cast aside by heavenly mandate, stripped bare of its providence. Not only would its disciples find cultivation vastly more difficult, but their daily lives would also be plagued with misfortune—[even drinking cold water would lodge between their teeth](# "Literal CN: 喝凉水都塞牙 (hē liángshuǐ dōu sāi yá) — Chinese idiom for peak bad luck where even the simplest act brings misfortune") wouldn't be a mere figure of speech. And when the Untiring Immortal Venerable sought to ascend, the lethal ferocity of his lightning tribulation would multiply!

The Untiring Immortal Venerable's sole obsession was ascension (or so Carefree Palace believed). Once matters reached the point where the hearts of all people had turned against him, he would have no choice but to reflect on his "wrongs" and take the initiative to sue for peace with Carefree Palace!

Of course, if he flew into a rage, suffered qi deviation, and went mad, forcing the entire cultivation world to band together and exterminate him, that would be even better.

In fact, driven to that point, most sects would choose to fall into demonhood.

Carefree Palace's calculation was an absolute [open scheme](# "Literal CN: 阳谋 (yángmóu) — an overt, transparent gambit whose trap cannot be avoided even when seen").

Although the sects of the Central Domain were dissatisfied with this, none were willing to openly fall out with Carefree Palace over it—not to mention the trade routes remained in Carefree Palace's hands. As far as Thousand Beasts Hall knew, several sects situated farther from the Heavenly Spirit Sect had already acquiesced to Carefree Palace's demands. The only reason they hadn't immediately declared a severance of ties was that nobody wanted to be [the bird that sticks its head out](# "Literal CN: 出头鸟 (chūtóuniǎo) — the prominent target, the first to take the hit"). Thus, they agreed to wait until Carefree Palace persuaded the largest sects in the Central Domain, after which they would issue a joint condemnation of the Heavenly Spirit Sect for using violence against Carefree Palace and forcing them out of the Central Domain market, cutting off the livelihood of every sect in the region.

And Thousand Beasts Hall was precisely that "large sect."

This was why Yan Bailang, Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall, found himself in such an agonizing dilemma.

He didn't want to fall out with Carefree Palace, but did he dare fall out with the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!

You had to understand: that Untiring Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect slaughtered Carefree Palace's Tribulation Transcendence elder without batting an eye! What was he, the mere Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall, in comparison?! He was only at the Soul Integration stage!

What was most unfortunate was that Thousand Beasts Hall was Heavenly Spirit Sect's immediate neighbor. Even if they announced the severance of ties alongside other sects, if the Untiring Immortal Venerable erupted in fury and decided to pick off a [first bird](# "Literal CN: 出头鸟 (chūtóuniǎo) — the prominent target, the first to take the hit"), it would inevitably be Thousand Beasts Hall!

(Or so Yan Bailang believed.)

Therefore, they couldn't just sever ties willy-nilly. They needed a righteous, unassailable excuse.

An excuse that, as long as the Untiring Immortal Venerable hadn't fallen into demonhood and still intended to walk the righteous path, he would have no choice but to accept!

At this moment, Thousand Beasts Hall genuinely wished the Heavenly Spirit Sect would stir up trouble so they could sever ties.

Alas, they had no idea what Heavenly Spirit Sect's eldest disciple Yuwen Xuan had done. They could only harden their scalps and head to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, manufacturing a conflict where none existed.

Naturally, even taking this step made Yan Bailang's calves tremble.

The closer he got to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the more violently Yan Bailang's calves shook.

—"Dammit! Where on earth did that woman hide the complete edition of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*?!"-Yan Bailang complained fretfully:—"If only I could devour the Beast Soul, why would I ever be reduced to this state?!"

Sensing his agitated mood, the Four-Clawed Five-Eyed Green-Faced Flood Dragon beneath him let out a long, rumbling cry, as if echoing his sentiment.

This was the fundamental cultivation method of Thousand Beasts Hall.

The cultivation progress of a Thousand Beasts Hall cultivator was tethered to their spirit pet.

Because spirit beasts and demonic beasts were innate spiritual creatures, their cultivation speed was far faster than that of humans. Once a cultivator tamed a spiritual creature, they gained a bonus from their pet's cultivation speed, advancing their cultivation progress much faster than other cultivators.

Adding to that, the spirit pet itself was a formidable combat force, making beast tamers an exceptionally powerful profession in both early and late stages.

But this method had its inherent drawbacks.

A beast tamer's cultivation relied on their spirit pet. The human body was not necessarily adapted to this process; spiritual energy was essentially [pulled up like sprouts to help them grow](# "Literal CN: 拔苗助长 (bámiáozhùzhǎng) — ancient fable about ruining crops through impatience / forcing unnatural growth"), leaving the beast tamer's physical body fragile and their personal spellcasting feeble. Worst of all, a frail spiritual foundation made breaking through cultivation realms extraordinarily difficult. Essentially, they couldn't rely on themselves to break through; they could only rely on their spirit pet's cultivation to forcibly drag them across the threshold.

With a single spirit pet responsible for advancing both its own cultivation and its master's, things inevitably grew increasingly difficult in the later stages. Consequently, Thousand Beasts Hall had swarms of Golden Core, Nascent Soul, and even Soul Formation combatants, appearing far more flourishing than other sects. But once they hit Void Refinement, they suffered a massive cliff-dive; cultivators who could enter the final three realms—Soul Integration, Mahayana, and Tribulation Transcendence—were vanishingly few.

Even their Sect Leader was merely at the Soul Integration stage.

Fortunately, this was not entirely insoluble.

For Thousand Beasts Hall to gain a foothold in such a perilous environment as the Central Domain and develop into the second-largest sect, it naturally didn't rely on a Soul Integration Sect Leader, but on an entity known as the "Beast Soul."

According to Thousand Beasts Hall records, during an exploration of the Abyss Sea, their first-generation sect leader, Patriarch Qubi, encountered a terrifying ferocious beast that even accompanying Tribulation Transcendence cultivators could not subdue. In the end, only thanks to the aid of a True Immortal was it slain. Patriarch Qubi chose no other spoils of war, taking back its Beast Soul. From then on, as long as a Sect Leader candidate devoured a portion of the Beast Soul, they could forcibly elevate both themselves and their spirit pet to the Tribulation Transcendence realm!

However, this method had a fatal flaw: the soul of a slain ferocious beast was incomparably sinister and perilous. With the slightest carelessness, one would be devoured in reverse, becoming part of the Beast Soul itself. Thus, Thousand Beasts Hall's previous practice was to select gifted young boys and girls and fill the meat grinder with their lives; eventually, a qualified sect leader seedling would emerge. The ancient "Beast Soul Trial" of Thousand Beasts Hall was a bloody trial in the truest sense.

Only because survival in the Central Domain was impossible without extreme measures was Thousand Beasts Hall spared from being branded an evil demonic cult.

This practice only came to an end under the previous sect leader, Patriarch Yusi.

Patriarch Yusi was a peerless genius. She discovered the secret of taming the Beast Soul; not only did she survive, but she also ensured that every junior brother and sister in her cohort survived the trial. Little did she know that this act would bring calamity upon herself. She did not die at the hands of the previous sect leader, but fell by the hand of the junior brother she had saved—Yan Bailang! The core mental manual she had created, the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, was stolen by her junior brother.

Had it not been that creating a cultivation manual was an act of great karmic merit in the cultivation world—where plagiarizing and claiming it as one's own required deceiving the Heavenly Dao, a feat of astronomical difficulty—she likely wouldn't even have left her name behind as the author of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*.

Of course, stolen goods remained stolen goods.

The *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* Yan Bailang obtained was missing the final two pages, leaving him unable to find the safe method to devour the Beast Soul.

And the bloody trial of yesteryear had left Yan Bailang with immense psychological trauma. Under no circumstances was he willing to risk his life again to devour the Beast Soul. He could only [scrape by](# "Literal CN: 苟着 (gǒuzhe) — internet slang for playing it safe, turtle-crawling, hunkering down"), resulting in this awkward, half-baked predicament.

Remembering this, Yan Bailang was filled with bitter resentment:

—"If only I had the complete *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and could reach the Tribulation Transcendence stage, even if I visited that person at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I'd have some backbone!"

[But the last person the Untiring Immortal Venerable killed was a Tribulation Transcendence master!]

The others thought to themselves, though none dared voice it to court disaster.

—"It is said that person is busy cultivating and rarely receives guests,"-Thousand Beasts Hall Elder Bai Jun consoled Yan Bailang:—"When we arrive at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, we will likely only see his disciples."

—"Besides, the request we are bringing isn't excessive in the least. It's just that the Heavenly Spirit Sect has always been arrogant, so they are bound to ignore it! When that happens, our severance of ties will appear completely justified. Later, when more sects rise up en masse to squabble with the Heavenly Spirit Sect without warning, it won't be pinned on our heads,"-Bai Jun added meticulously. In truth, he had repeated these words more than once, unsure whether he was convincing Yan Bailang or reassuring himself.

—"Let us hope so,"-Yan Bailang muttered.

As they spoke, the silhouette of Mount Tianling had already appeared far in the distance amidst the misty horizon.

***

People always get whatever they fear most.

The Thousand Beasts Hall delegation didn't actually enter the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Not because they were slighted, but because the moment they stepped into the borders of Mount Tianling, Zhou Hui's eldest disciple arrived riding a giant eagle to inform them: to make up for missing the [Double Ninth Festival](# "Literal CN: 重阳 (Chóngyáng) — 9th day of the 9th lunar month, traditional harvest and elder-honoring festival"), the Heavenly Spirit Sect had decided to hold a grand assembly for the [Winter Clothes Festival](# "Literal CN: 十月朝 (Shíyuèzhāo) — the 1st day of the 10th lunar month, a traditional festival marking the onset of winter and the distribution of seasonal clothing"). At this moment, Zhou Hui had gone to the summit of Heaven-Cleaving Chasm at the northeast corner of Mount Tianling—which was also the northeast corner of the Central Domain—to conduct a blessing ceremony, and invited the Thousand Beasts Hall delegation to join them there.

In other words: they had arrived right during their festival, and the master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was directly hosting a banquet at the highest peak.

Under ordinary circumstances, this would have been a stroke of great fortune. But for Thousand Beasts Hall, which had come specifically to "sever diplomatic ties," this was hardly a good omen.

—"By the way, does the Heavenly Spirit Sect have this many disciples?"-Yan Bailang asked.

Since it was a festival, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had naturally turned out in full force.

However, as Heaven-Cleaving Chasm served as the Central Domain's barrier, ordinary disciples lacked the cultivation to scale it. To prevent a holiday festival from turning into an endurance trial, Zhou Hui had Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing release various spirit beasts and magical artifacts for disciples to ride.

Along the way, Thousand Beasts Hall could see Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples who had changed into their new uniforms: some gathering in groups of three to five on rocky ledges to play and snap commemorative holograms; some riding high-grade artifacts they usually couldn't access, wandering through the mountain valleys; while bold ones even raced their flying swords against spirit beasts. It looked extraordinarily lively.

Alas, human joys and sorrows are never shared; Yan Bailang only found them noisy.

—"I heard that riding the momentum of slaying Carefree Palace's Sovereign Immortal Chana, the Heavenly Spirit Sect made quite a splash. Not long ago, they held a minor recruitment, and many rogue cultivators flocked to their mountain,"-Bai Jun said with a hint of jealousy.

Right now, they could only feel sour.

They had no idea they had already missed a completely legitimate reason to sever ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect—if Zhou Hui had been willing to recruit disciples from other sects, Thousand Beasts Hall would have had a massive chunk poached away.

It was just unknown whether the Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall would have agreed?

But if "sacrificing" a few disciples could allow them to occupy the moral high ground and sever ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yan Bailang would surely have been willing.

Unfortunately, none of that happened.

Oblivious to all of this, Yan Bailang looked at the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, letting out a disdainful sneer:

—"Reckless insects who know nothing of death. They only see the Heavenly Spirit Sect's limelight, but never consider: is Carefree Palace so easily offended?"

Clearly, it was Carefree Palace that initiated the provocation, Carefree Palace that severed the trade routes out of spite against the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and Carefree Palace that coerced all sects to cut ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Yet most sects in the Central Domain still chose to compromise for their own interests.

And this was even with the Heavenly Spirit Sect possessing a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse!

But being unable to purchase goods was a tangible, immediate problem, whereas incurring Heavenly Spirit Sect's enmity carried no immediate practical cost. So why fight Carefree Palace?

Even if Carefree Palace lost one Tribulation Transcendence elder, they still had two left! Moreover, their strength was so immense that even if everyone united to force open the trade routes this time, they would still have to do business in the future!

By comparison, offending the Heavenly Spirit Sect was admittedly a bit risky, but nothing major.

Only Thousand Beasts Hall, who might end up as the "first bird," felt anxious.

Even so, Thousand Beasts Hall still chose Carefree Palace.

Yan Bailang believed that the major sects agreeing to Carefree Palace's demands was merely a matter of time.

Once all Central Domain sects announced their severance of ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Carefree Palace's siege encirclement would solidify. The Heavenly Spirit Sect would be forced onto the demonic path, and these fledglings would merely be cannon fodder buried alongside them.

However, this circled right back to the problem of "severing ties."

Thousand Beasts Hall needed an excuse first.

Just thinking about this gave Yan Bailang a stomachache. Unwilling to look any further at this lively throng of foolish young Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, he cast a fretful gaze into the far distance.

***

But just because Yan Bailang had no desire to look didn't mean other Thousand Beasts Hall disciples weren't interested.

[—"I just saw quite a few Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples wearing identical clothes. Could those be their new uniforms for this season?"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"Must be. Isn't the Winter Clothes Festival the day for distributing new clothing?"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"Whoa! See that team of sword cultivators? So dashing!"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"The ordinary disciples' robes are gorgeous too! That shade of blue is so crisp and refreshing. Whereas us—"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

—"We literally look like dirt clods,"-the speaking disciple muttered under his breath, unable to resist tugging at the gray uniform on his body.

Hearing this, all the low-ranking Thousand Beasts Hall disciples present couldn't help shrinking their shoulders.

They felt that their current outfits were simply unworthy of appearing on Heavenly Spirit Sect territory.

—"By the way, won't the Sect Leader and elders feel ashamed when they see Heavenly Spirit Sect's new clothes?"-a disciple remarked.

—"Hush! Clothes don't mean anything,"-a tall female cultivator reprimanded at that moment, winking at the surrounding disciples and gesturing toward the spirit beasts beneath them.

On this journey, Thousand Beasts Hall wasn't lacking in pomp either.

Aside from Sect Leader Yan Bailang's imposing Four-Clawed Five-Eyed Green-Faced Flood Dragon, they had also deployed eight silver-gray giant pythons. Flying through the sky, while not quite matching true dragons, their aura was practically on par.

These spirit beasts possessed a degree of intelligence; if they overheard disciples complaining, they might very well tattle to their masters.

Only then did the disciples snap to attention. One disciple even chimed in, catching on and winking back at Xia Bing:

—"Right you are, Senior Sister Xia Bing! Clothes are just external possessions."

Xia Bing offered a smile, but as she looked at the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, a complex expression surfaced on her face.

Looking back on what had transpired over the past month, she still found it hard to believe:

She got promoted!

Xia Bing had always been capable—otherwise she wouldn't have snagged a slot for the secret realm trial. But having no family or connections, zero ties to Thousand Beasts Hall's upper echelons, and being an upright person who didn't know how to flatter others, she had always languished in Thousand Beasts Hall's tangled web of nepotism—otherwise she wouldn't have contemplated defecting to the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the first place.

That day during Heavenly Spirit Sect's minor recruitment, she obtained the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, met Zhou Hui's direct disciple Mo Bei, and landed the contract to supply beast milk for "A Cup of Sorrow."

Xia Bing originally assumed this business would mean fighting alone, toiling to satisfy customer requirements. It turned out completely different.

She had merely described the working styles of several Thousand Beasts Hall managing stewards to Mo Bei's maidservant (?) and manager of "A Cup of Sorrow," Yu Feifei. Under the other's guidance, she hooked up with one of them. Then, through several batches of beast milk purchases—without even revealing who the client was—she earned the manager's trust. The manager praised her sales performance right in front of the Sect Leader, catching the Sect Leader's eye.

As a result, the Sect Leader brought her along on this delegation!

Although this visit wasn't likely a pleasant affair, being included in the Sect Leader's travel party meant Xia Bing was closer to Thousand Beasts Hall's center of power than ever before!

Xia Bing was certain that with this credential on her resume, she could secure a minor promotion upon returning—not only snagging a position as a junior manager, but also being counted as one of the Sect Leader's people from now on!

[Terrifying!]

[Simply too terrifying!]

Xia Bing had always thought Thousand Beasts Hall's internal relationships were labyrinthine, with the Sect Leader and elders practicing blatant nepotism, making it impossible to advance without connections. Who could have thought that simply latching onto the right thigh would make promotions and pay raises so laughably simple!

And this all came from just a few pointers from a "minor disciple" of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

[Was every single Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple this terrifying? What kind of ruthless survival environment did Heavenly Spirit Sect have?!]

Thinking this, Xia Bing looked ahead.

Serving as guides, a flight of giant eagles reared by Yuwen Xuan flew on either flank of the Thousand Beasts Hall delegation. (Unfortunately, that day Xia Bing hadn't even had the courage to lift her head and look; otherwise she would have been astonished to discover who her imagined 'demon' actually was.) And as part of the reception team, Yu Feifei rode a giant eagle hovering near Xia Bing's position.

Seeing Xia Bing look over, Yu Feifei—former Carefree Palace disciple, loser of Carefree Palace's previous palace battle, Carefree Palace sleeper spy (defected), current double agent, Mo Bei fansite master, and head of "A Cup of Sorrow"—flashed a bright, cheerful smile and even waved at Xia Bing.

—"Someone you know?"-a Thousand Beasts Hall disciple asked keenly.

[—"No, don't know her."](# "Speaker: Xia Bing")

Recalling the cover story she had coordinated with Yu Feifei, Xia Bing withdrew her gaze with an expressionless face.

***

After flying like this for half an hour, the Thousand Beasts Hall delegation finally arrived at a certain peak of Heaven-Cleaving Chasm.

This was also the very edge of the Central Domain.

Looking down from this vantage point, the Mount Tianling mountain range below resembled the grid of an Immortal Chess board—miniature, square, and brimming with vitality. Meanwhile, the other side of Heaven-Cleaving Chasm was an endless void: pitch-black, fathomless, terrifying as if it would swallow anyone whole.

And the Untiring Immortal Venerable sat at the snowy apex of this peak that divided two worlds.

His white robes were pure as snow, his long silver hair draped behind him, blending seamlessly with Heaven-Cleaving Chasm, which was blanketed in perpetual frost and snow. He looked like the mountain god of this divine peak—holding back the vast cosmic void from the mortal realm, while cupping this mortal world gently within the palm of his hand.

The moment Yan Bailang laid eyes upon him, his knees went weak, and he collapsed into a kowtow completely against his own will:

—"Thousand Beasts Hall's Yan Bailang greets the Untiring Immortal Venerable!"

—"No need for such excessive ceremony,"-Zhou Hui said. He was somewhat surprised by Yan Bailang's extravagant grand kowtow, but toward this uninvited guest who had almost disrupted his pedagogical schedule, he had no desire to waste time and only wanted to deal with him as quickly as possible. Thus, Zhou Hui cut straight to the chase:—"However, our two sects have never had any dealings. May I ask what business brings Sect Leader Yan here?"

[Asking right off the bat?!]

[Shouldn't you at least exchange two pleasantries, sit down to enjoy a performance, and ask when the mood is good? By then, you'd be in high spirits and might not take my question to heart, perhaps even forgetting about me altogether!]

Cold sweat beaded on Yan Bailang's forehead, freezing solid against his skull an instant later.

—"It... it is nothing major,"-Yan Bailang stammered:—"I... I came to inquire about the Winter Solstice Auction."

—"The Winter Solstice Auction?"-Zhou Hui asked in puzzlement.

Beside him, Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing exchanged a meaningful glance.

The "providential mark" on Yuwen Xuan's hand was still there right now!

They still didn't know who had guided him to that auction to buy strange items. Who would have thought that before they could launch an investigation, Thousand Beasts Hall would deliver itself right to their doorstep?

Yan Bailang was completely oblivious to what was going on in the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Adopting the mindset that [stretching out your neck or shrinking it back still meant taking a blade](# "Literal CN: 伸头是一刀，缩头也是一刀 — Chinese idiom meaning facing an unavoidable ordeal, so you might as well get it over with"), he finally spoke:

—"According to past tradition, on the Winter Solstice—the day when Yang energy is reborn—our Central Domain hosts a grand gala banquet and auction so everyone can stock up on New Year goods and welcome the new year. However, in previous years, this gala and auction mostly had... the generous sponsorship of Carefree Palace. This year, due to a certain... well-known reason, Carefree Palace does not plan to participate, so..."

—"Are you blaming our Master?"-Mo Xi asked with a frown.

—"I dare not, I dare not! It's just that without Carefree Palace, the Winter Solstice Auction will likely be terribly cold and deserted. Therefore, we hoped to invite Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable to hold the fort, lest everyone grow anxious!"-Yan Bailang recited like reciting a memorized script.

But he knew Zhou Hui would never go.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable was famous for despising all time-wasting social rituals. Something like banquets and holiday galas—he would never attend... Uh, wait?

Thinking up to here, Yan Bailang suddenly felt something was logically inconsistent.

Because he failed to realize: this Untiring Immortal Venerable who "despised holidays" was currently hosting a holiday festival for his disciples!

Of course, that was an insignificant detail. The crux of the matter was: "Carefree Palace isn't going, so we're inviting the Untiring Immortal Venerable." That made the Untiring Immortal Venerable seem like some second-rate substitute! In a cultivation world obsessed with face, no powerhouse with the slightest pride would ever accept such an invitation.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable obviously didn't belong to the spineless, mild-tempered sort. Especially when the comparison was Carefree Palace, even if he had originally harbored some intention to go, he would now erupt in thunderous fury and refuse!

Yan Bailang had already braced himself for Zhou Hui's wrath.

Yet in his heart, he was trembling:

[Just asking a question... I shouldn't be executed, right? This person is an 'Immortal Venerable,' not a Demon Venerable, right?]

As Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall personally visiting to invite the Immortal Venerable to the Winter Solstice Festival, getting kicked out because he was "clumsy with words" and offended the Immortal Venerable—the more he thought about it afterward, the more humiliated he felt, making it entirely justified to unilaterally sever diplomatic ties!

Even before the Heavenly Dao Alliance, Thousand Beasts Hall would have the moral advantage.

Later, when the other sects united under Carefree Palace's instigation to declare a severance of ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even if the Untiring Immortal Venerable flew into a rage, he wouldn't recall such a tiny slight from Thousand Beasts Hall.

Yan Bailang had come here with only this modest aspiration.

That was the limit of his ambition.

Now that the act of pulling the tiger's whiskers was complete, all that was left was to wait for the Immortal Venerable to kick him out.

Yan Bailang closed his eyes, waiting for the crushing pressure of a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator. Instead, he heard Zhou Hui ask:

—"That makes sense. Without Carefree Palace, preparing the venue, the merchandise, and everything else must be extraordinarily difficult for you all, wouldn't you say?"

—"Indeed it is, which is why we wished to invite Lord Immortal Venerable to..."

—"Very well. In that case, how about our Heavenly Spirit Sect hosts it?"

—"Understood, since you do not plan to g..."-Yan Bailang began, about to recite his pre-packaged lines, but the moment the words left his mouth he sensed something wrong. His eyes widened as he snapped his head up:—"You're going?!"

—"Not going. We are going to host it,"-Zhou Hui corrected, enunciating each word deliberately.

Talk about [handing someone a pillow right as they're nodding off](# "Literal CN: 瞌睡遇到了枕头 (kēshuì yùdàole zhěntou) — perfect, serendipitous timing; receiving exactly what one needed most")—could there be such a stroke of fortune?

Originally, Zhou Hui had been somewhat troubled over Yuwen Xuan attending that auction:

First, he didn't know who was plotting against his disciple. The thorniest possibility was that the Heavenly Dao itself was taking the field. Because Zhou Hui had always remained aloof and estranged from the mainstream cultivation world, he lacked leads to intervene and investigate.

Second, that fellow Yuwen Xuan was like a wild monkey—the moment you let him loose, he vanished without a trace! Who knew if running off to the auction would end with him going missing, just like in previous lifetimes? That wouldn't be an investigation; that would be serving oneself up on a silver platter!

Now, everything was solved. Someone had delivered the perfect excuse right before his eyes: an away game had suddenly turned into a home game!

As long as Heavenly Spirit Sect hosted the Winter Solstice Auction, all organizers and all visiting guests would be right beneath his eyes. Even if the Heavenly Dao wanted to pull something, it would never escape his gaze.

Thinking of this, Zhou Hui beamed with genuine joy, finding this Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall suddenly looking quite agreeable.

But in Yan Bailang's eyes, the Untiring Immortal Venerable's smile was as blinding as sunlight glaring off the snow-capped mountain, nearly blinding him and forcing uncontrollable tears from his eyes!

Amidst this dazzling radiance, Yan Bailang heard the Immortal Venerable continue his instructions:

—"Have the auction organizers come speak with me directly. I shall provide all the assistance they desire. Additionally, write down a roster of all sects that attend this Winter Solstice gala and auction every year. I will have my disciples dispatch invitation cards immediately—"

[It's over!]

[Before Heavenly Spirit Sect is isolated, the first one to be excommunicated by every major sect in the Central Domain will definitely be our Thousand Beasts Hall!]

Yan Bailang thought in utter despair.
