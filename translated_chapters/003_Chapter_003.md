# Chapter 3: Punitive Copying?! (第3章 罚抄？)

A sudden chill ran down the spines of the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples.

They had no inkling of the terrifying resolution their Master had just reached. Yet facing the examination papers before them, they knew by instinct that if they scored poorly, their fate would be anything but pleasant.

Regrettably, knowledge one never mastered beforehand does not magically conjure itself into one's brain during an examination.

Time continued its merciless march.

As cultivators, the disciples had never felt the flow of time pass so rapidly as they did today. Every tick emanating from Zhou Hui's 'magical treasure' felt like draining a drop of their vital essence blood.

Two *shichen* flashed by in the blink of an eye.

—"Time is up,"-Zhou Hui announced punctually once the two *shichen* had elapsed.

The disciples were still burying their heads in frantic toil.

As if the correct answers might suddenly strike them in the final seconds, they desperately waged their last-ditch struggle on the paper.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui let out a sigh:

—"Hand in your papers,"-Zhou Hui said, shaking his head.-"This last moment will hardly raise your scores."

Not a single person put down their brush.

Zhou Hui tapped his desk without mercy. His slender index finger rapped against the white-jade desk, which shared nearly the identical tone as his fair skin, producing a crisp, resonant clack.

To the cadence of that sound, the disciples discovered in horror that their ink-drenched brush tips could no longer leave the slightest mark on the paper. The exam papers themselves seemed to awaken with a life of their own, rustling as they moved across the desks and fluttering toward the dais like a flock of white birds.

There on the dais, Brush Monkeys had already prepared cinnabar ink to assist Zhou Hui in grading the papers.

And they graded extraordinarily fast.

Although this exam featured a massive number of questions to conduct a comprehensive [baseline assessment](# "Literal CN: 摸底 (mōdǐ) — feeling the bottom; diagnostic testing to assess fundamental competency")—a full five hundred questions, a quantity practically designed so no one could finish—fortunately (?), the number of disciples was rather small.

Or rather, the Heavenly Spirit Sect currently had very few members.

Zhou Hui had always lacked interest in managing sect affairs, so the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect numbered barely three hundred people. Those qualified to participate in this exam—the direct disciples and inner sect disciples—totaled just over fifty in all, roughly the size of a single classroom.

For Zhou Hui, even in another world devoid of magic, grading this handful of exam papers would take merely an instant, to say nothing of the cultivation world.

Thus, the exam papers merely drifted in a brief circle across his desk before every single one was completely graded.

Seeing that the die was cast, the disciples let out collective sighs of despair.

Looking over these exam papers, Zhou Hui's mood actually improved slightly compared to earlier.

During the examination, Zhou Hui had primarily watched the papers of his few direct disciples. Now that all the results were tallied, he found that the inner sect disciples' performance was fairly decent.

Merely 'fairly decent.'

It was not that the inner disciples had surpassed the direct disciples personally instructed by Zhou Hui—in truth, the direct disciples' scores remained higher than the inner disciples', though Zhou Hui held his direct disciples to far higher standards. The inner disciples also suffered from shaky foundations and narrow breadths of knowledge, but on the whole, their mastery over their 'elective disciplines' was quite respectable, slightly above that of ordinary cultivators of equivalent rank in the wider cultivation world.

At the very least, they still had salvage value.

Pondering this inwardly, Zhou Hui turned a milder gaze upon his disciples:

[—"It is evident that everyone worked hard—worked hard in your respective primary disciplines."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"However, are you satisfied with just this?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"The Grand Dao of cultivation is no simple matter! A single careless misstep can cost you your life. What you lack today is precisely what will claim your lives tomorrow!"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"Naturally, I also bear responsibility. My teaching has not been comprehensive enough..."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"Master,"-the youngest junior brother Mo Xi piped up in a fawning tone,-"you have taught us splendidly."

—"Of course I teach splendidly,"-Zhou Hui said as if stating an undeniable truth.-"What is flawed is the educational curriculum. Therefore, I shall reinforce your foundations."

Mo Xi: "..."

—"However, before that—"-Zhou Hui's tone shifted abruptly as his expression darkened, his gaze sweeping over his disciples.

The disciples instantly became [quiet as quail](# "Literal CN: 安静如鸡 (quiet as a chicken) — modern internet slang for falling dead silent out of fear or awkwardness").

Though their Master normally appeared as gentle and warm as polished jade, once his face fell, that warm jade turned to solid ice; the freezing chill radiating from him was enough to freeze a man to death.

Even more terrifying were the words that followed:

—"First, you will correct every erroneous question on your exam papers yourselves,"-Zhou Hui commanded.-"These are all questions strictly within the purview of your cultivation realms. Even if you were never formally taught them, you should be able to find them in the Library Pavilion! Track down the original source for every question you got wrong, and copy it out one hundred times!"

[—"Those who truly cannot comprehend may come find me."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

As he said this, he swept his gaze across all the disciples once more.

Now the atmosphere wasn't merely quiet as quail—it felt like keeping vigil at a funeral wake.

Zhou Hui, however, nodded in satisfaction:

—"Qu Qianxing, record the scores and distribute the papers,"-Zhou Hui instructed.-"In one week, collect all the corrected papers back."

—"...Yes, Master,"-Qu Qianxing replied, head lowered.

* * *

Once Qu Qianxing finished returning the exam papers, Zhou Hui finally departed the Palace of Silence, leaving behind a hall of utterly drained disciples.

Only after Zhou Hui had been gone for a full [half cup of tea's time](# "A traditional Chinese measure of time, roughly 5–10 minutes") did the disciples dare speak:

[—"I'm finished! I only completed eighty percent of the questions! That means I have to punitive-copy over ten thousand times!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"Eighty percent? I didn't even finish half of mine!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"I have it the worst! I got stuck on a question early on, and didn't have time for a bunch of questions near the end that I actually knew how to do! Do I have to copy those as punishment too?!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"Master's scope was way too broad! I'm an alchemy cultivator—isn't it completely natural that I know nothing about formations?! Why do I have to copy those?!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"If you have the guts, don't copy them then!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

In truth, they were merely [plugging their ears while stealing a bell](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — deceiving oneself; burying one's head in the sand").

Everyone knew that with Zhou Hui's cultivation, if he wished, even the fluttering of a butterfly's wings within a thousand-li radius could not escape his divine perception.

Yet that was not why the disciples held Zhou Hui in such awe.

What they revered was Zhou Hui's character.

The disciples all understood that their Master never considered his 'punishments' to be punishments at all, because that was precisely how he held himself to standard. He was even self-aware of his own exacting standards, which was why when dealing with his disciples, he usually lightened their workload. Even so, for ordinary cultivators, it already bordered on punitive levels of homework.

Take this exam, for instance:

[—"...By the way, what was the answer to Question 15?"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"Nine-Ingredient Powder?"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"No way, wasn't that a cultivation manual question? How could it be Nine-Ingredient Powder?"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"Wait a moment... our exam papers don't seem to be the same."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"They shouldn't be the same to begin with. Your cultivation realms are different."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"Still, they were all Qi Condensation questions. I assumed they'd be identical..."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"You think Master is as lazy as you?"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"Does that mean... Master drafted a personalized exam paper for every single person?"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"You've got to be kidding me! Five hundred questions per paper—with this many of us, the grand total of questions is..."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"And didn't Master only decide on this spur of the moment yesterday? Drafting that many questions in a single night?!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciples")

Silence descended over the Palace of Silence once more.

After a long pause, Qu Qianxing suddenly slammed his palm onto his desk:

—"Now that you realize how much blood and sweat Master poured into this, what are you standing around daydreaming for?"-Qu Qianxing barked, his voice ringing out.-"Why haven't you rushed to the Library Pavilion to correct the questions you failed?!"

With a flick of his wide sleeves, Qu Qianxing swept out of the Palace of Silence.

The majority of the remaining disciples exchanged glances. Whatever private grievances they harbored, they obediently fell in behind their Second Senior Brother, flying toward the Library Pavilion.

After all, with a Master like this, the slightest slackening felt like betrayal.

* * *

Over the next several days, inner sect disciples burying their heads in transcribing books with rapt concentration could be seen everywhere across the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Such an unusual sight inevitably drew the attention of the outer sect disciples.

[—"These past few days, doesn't the atmosphere in our sect feel a bit off?"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Word has it the Immortal Venerable failed his tribulation yet again. It'd be weird if the atmosphere *were* good."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"It's hardly the first time the Immortal Venerable failed a tribulation. Shouldn't everyone be used to it by now?"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Not necessarily. Didn't you think the heavenly tribulation last time was exceptionally terrifying? It's quite possible the Immortal Venerable suffered a severe setback this round."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

—"...Ptui, ptui! Stop talking nonsense! An Immortal Venerable so kind-hearted could never suffer any mishap!"-outer sect disciple Gan Tang said nervously.

Her words left the others at a loss for what expression to make.

—"Little Gan, you don't even know how to flatter properly,"-someone teased unceremoniously.-"Even if you want to butter up the Immortal Venerable, 'kind-hearted' is hardly the right virtue!"

—"Who's buttering anyone up?"-Gan Tang rolled her eyes.-"If you went to other sects, would they ever grant you a Library Pavilion like this?"

Among the outer disciples, those from [rogue cultivator backgrounds](# "Literal CN: 散仙 (loose immortal) — used colloquially here for unaffiliated / rogue cultivators from outside established sects") fell silent.

Only an outer disciple native to the local region asked curiously:

—"Is a Library Pavilion really that rare? I heard every sect has one."

Hearing him ask this, the outer disciples of rogue cultivator origin could not help sighing:

[—"You truly do not realize how blessed you are!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"You think just any sect boasts this vast an ocean of books?"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Even the grandest sects in the Eastern Province might not possess a repository as magnificent as our Heavenly Spirit Sect's."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

As they spoke, they tilted their heads back in collective awe, gazing upward.

They were currently inside the Library Pavilion. Tier upon tier of towering bookshelves rose like mountain ranges, every level packed to overflowing with bamboo slips, jade slips, wooden tablets, thread-bound tomes, hardcover volumes, and records bound in every conceivable casing.

And this immense ocean of literature stood as living proof of Zhou Hui's status as the [Champion of Grinding](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — Involution King; someone who out-works, out-studies, and out-grinds everyone else in hyper-competitive environments").

Zhou Hui possessed poor innate talent and poor karmic fortune. Consequently, in his early years, he was perpetually deprived of suitable cultivation techniques. Though he attained some cultivation through sheer dogged effort, with every breakthrough, he always discovered that his hard-won finish line was merely where others started. Even so, Zhou Hui never surrendered; he quietly and methodically made up for everything he had lacked in the past. Thus, even after attaining the Golden Core realm, he still collected Qi Condensation manuals; even upon reaching the Mahayana realm, he would feel genuine delight upon acquiring a Foundation Establishment spell slip he had never read before.

All the manuals and spell books Zhou Hui had read through over the millennia accumulated to form the Library Pavilion of today.

Opening any book in this pavilion, one could see the dense margin notes left behind by Zhou Hui.

Any disciple who stepped foot into this Library Pavilion would be utterly awed by this colossal mountain of knowledge.

The locally born disciple was similarly captivated by this Library Pavilion, yet he stubbornly insisted:

—"H-how could the grandest sects possibly lose to our Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-the locally born disciple stubbornly retorted.-"Take the Cloud Firmament Sect—they even have palaces suspended in the sky, how could their collection be small? More like you lot wouldn't even qualify to cross their threshold."

The other disciples nodded in agreement:

[—"Putting it that way, you're not wrong. Aside from our Immortal Venerable, which sect would ever open their Library Pavilion to everyone? Other sects wish they could hoard every precious technique away, where even inner disciples cannot lay eyes on them, let alone outer disciples like us."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Word has it the Immortal Venerable was also a rogue cultivator in his early years. That is why he empathizes with the struggles of us juniors and threw open the Library Pavilion."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"He even constructed the Library Pavilion right beside the mountain gate, so that even a mortal on foot could reach it."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Phrasing it that way feels a bit unusual for an Immortal Venerable, but indeed, it truly warrants the word 'kind-hearted'."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"...May the kind-hearted Immortal Venerable live as long as heaven and earth!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"May the Immortal Venerable live as long as heaven and earth."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciples")

For reasons unknown, the disciples present spontaneously began to pray.

When another disciple arrived on the scene, he was inevitably greeted by this bizarre spectacle.

That disciple took two steps backward, asking in genuine alarm:

—"What on earth are you doing?"

Gan Tang and the others racked their memories for a second before recalling where the conversation had started:

—"We were discussing why the atmosphere inside the sect has been so weird lately,"-Gan Tang and the others replied.-"We were worried something bad happened."

—"Not as weird as you lot! I thought you'd joined some demon cult!"-the newcomer roasted them, before lowering his voice.-"Rather than weird rituals, legitimate channels are far more reliable."

[We are not performing any weird rituals!]

Gan Tang was about to retort, but someone tugged at her sleeve, while another disciple chimed in with a cheeky grin:

—"Xiang Rong, did you manage to get your hands on some good merchandise through 'legitimate channels' again?"

Only then did Gan Tang realize what was happening.

The outer sect disciple before them named 'Xiang Rong'—his words just now sounded somewhat like earnest counsel, but the fellow was hardly an upright sort. Relying on having a younger sister in the Inner Sect, he spent year-round scalping perks and provisions exclusive to inner disciples.

The others were already asking with practiced ease:

[—"Did you score any good magical artifacts this time?"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"I asked you to ask around about a Foundation Establishment Pill earlier—any news?"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Tch, how could Foundation Establishment Pills be so easy to get? You'd be better off buying some Crimson Essence Pills and practicing honestly!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

Xiang Rong clearly relished this atmosphere. Smirking with pride, he scoffed:

—"What pills? What magical artifacts? How can one ever attain the Grand Dao by relying on such external crutches?"

While spouting this 'righteous doctrine', Xiang Rong's eyes darted vigilantly around the perimeter. Only after verifying that no one was eavesdropping did he stealthily extract several sheets of paper from the storage pouch at his breast:

—"Behold this! Exam papers from the inner sect examination!"

A collective question mark practically materialized above the others' heads as they stared back in utter bewilderment.

As cultivators, examinations had virtually nothing to do with their lives. Even if their abilities were ever tested, it would never take such a format.

It was a disciple of mortal upbringing who asked in perplexity:

—"Exam papers? Like the imperial civil service exams?"

—"Ptui, ptui! How could mere mortal exams ever compare to this?!"-Xiang Rong hissed.-"These are papers capable of putting direct disciples through their paces! Word is the Immortal Venerable personally drafted the questions himself."

—"Drafted by the Immortal Venerable himself?!"-someone cried out in shock.

Xiang Rong hurriedly swatted the speaker, frantically miming for silence.

The group hastily ducked their heads and clamped their mouths shut. Yet the look in their eyes as they gazed upon those sheets of paper—(X) exam papers—was completely different from before, gleaming as if they were beholding peerless divine scriptures.

And no wonder.

Although Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion was open to all, that did not alter the reality that the sect remained a third-rate minor outfit. Resource allocation could never be truly equitable for everyone—particularly when it came to educational resources.

In other words, the Heavenly Spirit Sect lacked enough 'teachers.'

Zhou Hui could not possibly lecture every disciple personally. Thus, those privileged to remain by his side, free to seek guidance whenever doubts arose, were solely the direct disciples who had formally bowed to him as Master.

Inner sect disciples rarely had the opportunity to see Zhou Hui, only able to audit his open lectures when luck smiled upon them—a privilege that was already extraordinary compared to other sects, where Tribulation Transcendence patriarchs would never deign to deliver public lectures. Inner disciples primarily relied on direct disciples and other senior members to impart knowledge.

Outer sect disciples, on the other hand, were virtually indistinguishable from odd-job handymen. Although open lectures existed for them as well, most were delivered by senior inner disciples covering basic rudiments, with wildly uneven instructional quality. In essence, outer disciples had to rely almost entirely on self-study.

An examination like the one just held was reserved strictly for elite disciples; outer sect disciples did not even possess the clearance to know it was taking place.

Under such circumstances, any crumb of guidance from a senior cultivator was priceless to an outer disciple—let alone questions personally crafted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

—"How many spirit stones?"-someone asked eagerly.

—"Heh-heh, I had to expend an immense effort to procure these,"-Xiang Rong began, but catching the ravenous glints in their eyes, he simply flashed a hand gesture.-"Seeing as we entered the sect in the same batch, a mere ten spirit stones."

—"That cheap?"-someone asked in doubt.

Another disciple reached out impatiently to snatch the papers straight from Xiang Rong's hands:

—"I'll take it."

—"Hold your horses! If you want to buy, a few conditions must be made clear first,"-Xiang Rong said, clutching the test papers firmly to his chest.

Though disgruntled, the others exercised patience and listened.

Xiang Rong stated:

—"First, this transaction must be kept strictly confidential."

This was elemental; even for the usual 'goods' Xiang Rong peddled, neither buyer nor seller would ever breathe a word, lest they sever future supply lines.

Xiang Rong continued:

—"Second, some of these exam papers come without answer keys. But rest assured, the questions are not impossibly obscure; every answer can be unearthed in the Library Pavilion. Everyone gathered here is among the most formidable talents in our outer sect. I believe you are in no way inferior to the inner disciples and should easily be able to solve them yourselves."

Nobody raised any objection.

Cultivation was an endeavor that rarely possessed standard answers. Thus, before laying eyes upon the actual questions on the test paper, not a soul realized the trap.

Flattered by Xiang Rong's smooth compliments, several disciples could not help curling their lips into satisfied smirks.

Yet Xiang Rong was also speaking the truth.

This particular batch of 'goods' was unique; his 'supplier' had explicitly stipulated that at this stage, they could only be sold to top-tier honor students. Otherwise, he wouldn't have spent so much time scouring the labyrinthine Library Pavilion just to single out these specific individuals.

Xiang Rong finally dropped the most crucial requirement:

—"Third, and most important of all: whoever purchases an exam paper must answer fifty questions—and copy them out one hundred times."

[—"...Copy them out one hundred times?!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciples")

The crowd froze in stunned disbelief, and immediately someone cried out:

[—"Isn't that punitive copying?!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")
