# Chapter 132: Stirrings of Restlessness (第132章 蠢蠢欲动)

[← Previous Chapter](131_Chapter_131.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](133_Chapter_133.md)

---

Eastern Domain.

[—"Hahaha! I pulled him!"](# "Speaker: Flood Dragon Crown Prince")

A deafening roar shattered the air without warning, startling a passing Featherfolk so violently she nearly plummeted from the sky.

Flapping her pale blue wings in a flurry, she finally managed to stabilize her flight.

The Featherfolk couldn't help but grumble:

—"Are those [Seekers of Immortality](# "Literal CN: 寻仙客 (xúnxiān kè) — Seekers of Immortality; the indigenous Eastern Domain term for foreign cultivators") at it again? What has gotten into them lately, screaming and shouting like lunatics over a bunch of toys..."

Before she could finish her sentence, her companion yanked her back.

Raising a wing in a hushed gesture, the companion whispered:

—"That's no Seeker of Immortality."

—"If it's not a Seeker of Immortality, then what is it?"-the blue-winged Featherfolk asked in bewilderment, though she instinctively lowered her voice.

Her companion tugged her downward, landing upon a pine tree growing atop the "island" below.

Concealing themselves among the pine needles, the two Featherfolk peered down.

The moment the blue-winged Featherfolk took in the scene below, a scream nearly tore from her throat. Fortunately, her companion clamped a wing over her mouth in the nick of time.

Tense and trembling, the Featherfolk spied on the scene below.

Protruding from beneath the makeshift reef on the eastern edge of the "island" was a gargantuan turtle head. The head was larger even than the nearby thousand-year-old pine, leaving one to wonder where its body could possibly be hiding beneath the reef... No, there was no hiding! The entire island was its body. Looking closely, this "small island" was no island at all, but the carapace of an enormous turtle.

Naturally, there was nothing unusual about that.

Across the [Boundless Sea](# "Literal CN: 无尽海 (wújìn hǎi)") of the Eastern Domain, such "islands" were everywhere.

Some belonged to spirit beasts that were benevolent or neutral toward other living creatures; simply too lazy to stir, their backs gradually sprouted self-contained biomes capable of hosting other species, with some even maintaining true symbiotic relationships with the creatures living upon them. Yet others belonged to demonic beasts and vicious fiends that disguised themselves as islands, tricking passing creatures into landing upon their shores before devouring them whole.

As indigenous natives of the region, the Featherfolk naturally possessed their own methods of telling them apart.

Even without being a Featherfolk, however, anyone could see that the "master" of this island was [bad news](# "Literal CN: 不是个善茬 (bù shì gè shànchá) — not a gentle customer / not someone to be trifled with").

For at that very moment, the turtle's maw was stretched to its absolute limit, exposing three dense, razor-sharp rows of jagged teeth that looked capable of snapping through the defenses of a Golden Core cultivator in a single crunch.

Yet that was not even the most grotesque sight.

Right now, two "humans" were emerging straight from the depths of the turtle's throat.

One was an elder with snowy hair and a flowing white beard; the other was a stunning beauty with mist-veiled temples and cloud-coiled hair. Both exuded an aura of ethereal celestial grace. Anyone crossing the boundless ocean who stepped onto this island and encountered either of them would surely believe they had met a genuine immortal!

Upon closer inspection, however, the lower halves of their bodies were fused seamlessly to the turtle's tongue. They were not human beings at all, but biomimetic angler lures grown by this gargantuan turtle.

Yet at this moment, these two humanoid lures were not putting on an immortal act to ensnare unsuspecting prey.

Instead, both were sprawled over a flat reef, using it as a table. Resting atop the rock was none other than the Immortal Chess set that had recently swept the [Five Domains and Six Abysses](# "Literal CN: 五境六渊 (wǔ jìng liù yuān)") like wildfire. The "two" of them were squabbling until their faces turned red:

—"This round is [concubine's](# "Literal CN: 妾身 (qièshēn) — humble self-referential pronoun used by women in ancient China") turn!"-the fairy snapped.

—"Nonsense! This round belongs to [this old man](# "Literal CN: 老夫 (lǎofū) — humble/proud self-referential term used by elderly men")!"-the elder barked.

—"Shut up, both of you! Aren't you two literally the exact same person?!"-their opponent seated across the reef barked in annoyance.

Anyone capable of playing chess against such an eerie, colossal turtle was naturally no ordinary figure either.

His upper body was vaguely humanoid, albeit covered in greenish-blue scales with hands resembling talons. His lower half, however, remained entirely that of a flood dragon, dangling limp and submerged in the seawater.

Presently, he had only subjected himself to the indignity of assuming a humanoid form for the sole purpose of playing chess.

The flood dragon sneered impatiently at the turtle:

—"You're not even using different brains anyway!"

The "fairy" mimicked by the turtle's tongue wore a hurt, aggrieved pout:

—"We are entirely different! Concubine is clearly far cleverer—I lure in far more food than he does!"

—"Rubbish! This old man possesses far greater wisdom! Even the Seekers of Immortality agree!"-the "venerable immortal" huffed.

The flood dragon was utterly speechless:

—"If you were like my brother and grew two separate heads, fine. But you two grew off a single split tongue and you still manage to brawl? I am genuinely impressed."

Had the flood dragon known clinical terms like "schizophrenia" or "dissociative identity disorder," he would have used them to diagnose the turtle on the spot.

The flood dragon harbored zero interest in the turtle's psychological afflictions, however. Smacking the reef, he urged:

—"Alright, hurry up and get started! I didn't spend all that effort pulling the 'Fifth Prince' just to sit here talking! I want to take him for a spin!"

As he spoke, he gingerly placed a chess piece onto the stone table.

The figurine featured long, shimmering silver-blue hair and porcelain-pale skin—none other than the Untiring Immortal Venerable's fifth disciple, Lu Shulong.

—"His Highness the Fifth Prince is truly magnificent!"

The flood dragon sighed in pure intoxication, looking for all the world as if he desperately wanted to lick the figurine.

Of course, he couldn't lick it.

After all, in a cultivation realm where knowing someone's true name was enough to place a lethal curse upon them, figurines crafted with concrete physical likenesses carried inherent hazards. Consequently, every chess piece was encased in a protective barrier; even the owner could not use them for strange or improper purposes.

Then again, chess pieces were never meant to be licked in the first place. Merely possessing this piece was more than enough to make other "people" burn with envy.

The "fairy" remarked sourly:

—"His Highness the Fifth Prince may be breathtaking, but he clearly didn't unleash the true might of the Dragon Clan during the tournament. All the skills recorded onto his piece are human spells; he won't necessarily have the upper hand on the board!"

—"Hmph! As a True Dragon, unleashing dragon might in preliminary rounds before the grand finals would be humiliating! I have absolute faith that His Highness will take the championship!"-the flood dragon retorted dismissively.

Fixing his murky vertical pupils upon the turtle, he sneered:

—"Besides, with His Highness the Fifth Prince under my command, [this Crown Prince](# "Literal CN: 本太子 (běn tàizǐ) — this Crown Prince; royal self-referential term used by the flood dragon") has more than enough firepower to crush your rabble of [shrimp soldiers and crab generals](# "Literal CN: 虾兵蟹将 (xiābīng xièjiàng) — shrimp soldiers and crab generals; worthless minions")!"

—"What arrogant boasting!"-the "venerable immortal" roared in fury.-"Let us put it to the test, then!"

Following his shout, the entire island shuddered violently, sending the branches of the nearby pine tree swaying with exceptional turbulence.

The flood dragon's movements paused for a split second.

Glancing toward the pine tree, he noted with an amused grin:

—"Those two little birds seem to have flown away."

—"Who cares about them! Battle!"

The turtle cared nothing for two morsels of prey, declaring aggressively:

—"Don't think you're the only one blessed with lucky hands! Behold the booster pack I cracked this time—"

---

Flapping their wings frantically, the two Featherfolk fled the island in haste.

Only after flying thousands of nautical miles in a single breath did they finally dare exhale.

It wasn't the turtle they feared, but that flood dragon. Had he taken an interest in hunting them, escaping his pursuit would have been utterly impossible.

As things stood, perhaps they hadn't been noticed—or perhaps the flood dragon simply couldn't be bothered.

The latter was far more likely.

Given how utterly engrossed the flood dragon was with Immortal Chess, he had no bandwidth to spare for anything else.

Breathing a sigh of relief, the blue-winged Featherfolk asked in bewilderment:

—"Ugh, why is everyone playing this chess everywhere we go? The Seekers of Immortality, our own clansmen, and now even Great Demons are hooked on it!"

—"You've got it backward. It was the Great Demons and the Seeker powerhouses who got hooked first before it trickled down to us,"-her companion corrected earnestly.-"You saw that Ascension Stairway suspended above the Ninth Heaven, didn't you? It belongs to the creator of Immortal Chess, Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable! To earn the qualifications to climb that staircase, let alone the Seekers of Immortality, even our tribal chieftains have begun researching Immortal Chess! They're even preparing to visit the Central Domain in person. Haven't you noticed? There's already a dedicated Immortal Chess hall right in our tribal market!"

—"That's going way too far,"-the blue-winged Featherfolk muttered.-"Those who can actually get close to the Ascension Stairway are a tiny minority! Why is everyone acting as unrealistic as those Seekers of Immortality?"

The term "Seekers of Immortality" was what the foreign races of the Eastern Domain called cultivators.

These so-called "foreign races" were essentially human, yet differed from standard mankind in distinct ways: Featherfolk had wings instead of arms, while others included Giants, Dwarves, and more. Yet they were neither demon beasts nor demon hybrids—lacking demon cores and demon physiology, they could not practice demon heritage techniques, leaning far closer to humans from a biological standpoint.

In the Eastern Domain, these foreign races were the true native inhabitants.

Just as they called cultivators "Seekers of Immortality," the cultivators were the true outsiders who ventured into the Eastern Domain in pursuit of karmic opportunities.

Legend had it that in the ancient cultivation realm, immortals who refused to ascend yet possessed power rivaling the True Immortals of the upper realm chose to live in seclusion within the Eastern Domain. Such was the origin of the "Seekers of Immortality."

However, although the ethnic makeup of the Eastern Domain was even more convoluted than that of the Southern and Western Domains, relations between the various factions remained surprisingly cordial.

After all, nearly eighty percent of the Eastern Domain's territory consisted of an ocean known as the "Boundless Sea." This environment was exceedingly rich, yet perilous in equal measure. Even ancient Great Demons could find themselves utterly helpless before the wrath of natural calamities. Thus, while every living creature formed a link in the food chain and regarded one another with mutual hostility, they would readily unite whenever necessary to confront unknowable catastrophes.

In theory, aside from the Seekers of Immortality (the cultivators), other races ought to have had zero interest in the Ascension Stairway. Clearly, however, that was not the case.

—"Who says it's unrealistic?! Rumor has it that beyond our mortal realm lie Three Thousand Great Worlds—perhaps there is even a world ruled by Featherfolk! It is rare enough that Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable harbors no prejudice against non-human races and has opened the Ascension Stairway to everyone; why shouldn't we go take a look?!"-her companion argued.-"I heard the chieftain say that this might very well involve a complete redistribution of power!"

—"A redistribution of power?!"

—"That's right. In the ancient White Jade Capital, there was never a seat for foreign races. But this time might be different! Many tribes are already [stirring with restlessness](# "Literal CN: 蠢蠢欲动 (chǔnchǔn yù dòng) — restless and eager to move / stirring into action"), especially after word spread that the demon clans of the Southern Domain reached an accord with the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

—"Everyone actually harbors such ambitions?"-Hearing this, the blue-winged Featherfolk looked up once more toward the Ascension Stairway suspended in the firmament.

Even an insignificant nonentity like herself felt the staggering rallying power of the Ascension Stairway for the very first time.

Without a shadow of a doubt, Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable, holding both the Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital in his grasp, possessed the power to reshuffle every faction in the entire world—and every one of them would submit willingly.

Just as a surge of profound awe welled up in the blue-winged Featherfolk's chest, her companion added:

—"Besides, Immortal Chess is genuinely super fun."

—"Huh?"

—"I have no interest in chess itself, but even if you don't play it as chess, treating it as a puzzle-solving game is an absolute blast,"-the companion explained.-"In the past, I always thought humans from the Northern Domain were pathetic weaklings. They have no wings, can't fly, and have to risk their lives hunting demon beasts and 'cultivating' just to take to the air! But after playing Immortal Chess, I realized their spellcraft is ridiculously varied, and those secret realm maps are utterly fascinating."

At this point, the companion continued:

—"Even our chieftain said so! In the past, she used to disguise herself as a Seeker of Immortality to sneak into those human sects for fun. But the operational costs were astronomical, and whenever real danger struck, she couldn't resist using our clan's secret techniques, blowing her cover. With Immortal Chess, she can experience the exact same thrilling rush of a Seeker of Immortality without risking her identity in the slightest! Can you really blame the Great Demons for falling in love with it?"

—"Wait a second... From the sound of that, don't tell me you and the chieftain are both hopelessly addicted?!"-the blue-winged girl cried out.

—"Wh-Who's addicted?!"-the companion stammered defensively.

The two Featherfolk flew in silence through the open skies for a while.

Suddenly, the companion broke the quiet:

—"Speaking of which, those two Great Demons back there seemed to be cracking open booster packs. That must mean fresh stock just arrived! Why don't we drop by the market to take a look?"

—"You ARE addicted, aren't you?!"-the blue-winged Featherfolk shrieked.

---

[—"Sold out again?!"](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple A")

[—"Just how many packs did we restock this time? The moment they hit the shelves, they vanished!"](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple B")

[—"And it isn't just human cultivators buying them lately! The spirit races, demon clans, and foreign tribes are going wild. Even the Old Dragon King of the Western Sea pre-ordered several hundred sets from us!"](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple C")

[—"The Ascension Stairway is the ultimate advertisement."](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple A")

[—"Or rather, everyone is doing it purely for the Ascension Stairway! Plenty of old fogies in the Northern Domain have been begging us to proxy-purchase Immortal Chess sets for them, even though not long ago they were scoffing about how 'playing with trifles saps the will'!"](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple D")

[—"Why even single out northern old fogies? Even our own Protector Qiu—"](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple B")

[—"*Cough, cough, cough!*"](# "Speaker: Yuke Xuan Disciple C")

Disciples of Yuke Xuan were gathered on the ground floor of the Immortal Chess Hall, fervently debating the latest sales figures.

The instant their own protector was named, someone coughed violently and jabbed a furtive finger toward the upper floor. The hall instantly fell dead silent, though stifled snickers and knowing glances rippled through the crowd.

Up on the second floor, a middle-aged man with a stern, righteous countenance let out a cold snort:

—"Utterly scandalous!"

—"Let them have their chatter,"-seated across from the middle-aged man, an elegant Daoist in azure robes remarked with a mild smile.-"Had you not cracked down on them so harshly when Immortal Chess first appeared, why would they bother gossiping behind your back?"

The middle-aged man—known among the disciples as "Protector Qiu"—wore an aggrieved scowl:

—"When the elders and Sect Master acquired Immortal Chess, it was to analyze methods of climbing the Ascension Stairway; they naturally had their reasons. But what do these brats know? All they care about is messing around! If you ask me, Third Wen shouldn't have shipped these contraptions over in the first place!"

—"Now that is biased,"-the azure-robed Daoist replied gently.-"First of all, Jiawen acted entirely out of goodwill. Furthermore, precisely because Jiawen did so, our Yuke Xuan has been able to [reap advantages from all sides](# "Literal CN: 左右逢源 (zuǒyòuféngyuán) — navigate smoothly in all directions / gain favor from both sides"), whether during the previous academic debates or now with the Ascension Stairway."

Protector Qiu did not respond immediately, though his expression softened slightly.

The azure-robed Daoist continued:

—"Besides, Immortal Chess is far from a mere toy. Haven't you tried playing it yourself? It offers immense value in helping disciples familiarize themselves with secret realms and train their combat maneuvers."

—"Hmph! Those are Central Domain secret realms! Other territories might find them useful, but they hardly apply to our Eastern Domain,"-Protector Qiu retorted, lifting his chin with stubborn pride.

Seeing this, the azure-robed Daoist smiled:

—"Then we shall simply have to consult Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable to see if they can release a few secret realm maps based on our Eastern Domain."

Hearing those words, Protector Qiu's expression turned grave:

—"Does that mean, Sect Master, that you intend to travel to the Central Domain?"

—"The Ascension Stairway stands there; going is naturally an inevitability sooner or later,"-the azure-robed Daoist said.-"However, as for our Eastern Domain... there is no rush."

As he spoke, the azure-robed Daoist turned his gaze toward a certain direction.

Protector Qiu followed his line of sight, his eyes equally solemn.

That was the direction where the Palace of Carefree Wandering was located.

Although Yuke Xuan had not done so intentionally, they had virtually become the exclusive distributor for the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the Eastern Domain—a reality that directly clashed with the commercial interests of the Palace of Carefree Wandering.

However, having harbored no historical grudges and both standing as the foremost cultivation factions of the Eastern Domain, Yuke Xuan instinctively wished to avoid antagonizing Carefree Palace beyond repair.

That was precisely why Protector Qiu had strictly forbidden his disciples from playing Immortal Chess in public prior to the emergence of the Ascension Stairway. Helping the Northern Domain with proxy-purchases could be excused as an unavoidable favor among peers, but having their own disciples openly idolize Immortal Chess was impossible to justify.

Only after the Ascension Stairway manifested did Yuke Xuan lift the ban, compelled by strategic long-term planning.

On that matter, Protector Qiu harbored his own grievances:

[Our Yuke Xuan owes Carefree Palace nothing! Even if we openly act as sales brokers, so what?! What right do they have to give us cold shoulders? Back when they operated their businesses, didn't we extend every courtesy and convenience possible?!]

Deep down, however, he knew all too well that the Palace of Carefree Wandering was not a faction that listened to reason.

Regardless of how generously and charitably Carefree Palace pretended to act within the Eastern Domain, high-ranking elders of sects like Yuke Xuan were intimately acquainted with just how overbearing they truly were. They wouldn't even need to strike openly; a few covert saboteurs would be enough to decimate disciples venturing out to sea for trials. Where would Yuke Xuan go to demand justice then?

They certainly couldn't ignite an all-out war with Carefree Palace over this!

In a frontier like the Eastern Domain, teeming with demon clans, spirit races, and foreign tribes alongside catastrophic weather anomalies, human cultivators could ill afford internal conflict.

The azure-robed Daoist—the Sect Master of Yuke Xuan—evidently shared the same thoughts.

He let out a sigh:

—"There is no need for our Yuke Xuan to [be the bird that sticks out](# "Literal CN: 做出头鸟 (zuò chūtóuniǎo) — act as the leading bird that gets shot"). Once the cultivators of the Northern Domain make their way to the Central Domain, we can invite the other sects of the Eastern Domain and simply [hitch a ride](# "Literal CN: 搭个便车 (dā gè biànchē) — hitch a ride / join the bandwagon")."

Yet as one of the great sects of the Eastern Domain, being unable to act decisively in the face of a monument like the Ascension Stairway carried a bitter sting of humiliation.

Protector Qiu's brow twitched:

—"The northern cultivators are already preparing to go?"

—"Even setting aside the Ascension Stairway, White Jade Capital is there! That is the ancestral orthodox seat of the entire cultivation world! How could they possibly stay away?"-the Sect Master of Yuke Xuan sighed.-"Not only are they going, but they are preparing to go with maximum fanfare!"

—"Maximum fanfare?"

—"Indeed. Rumor has it that the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City are spearheading the delegation, assembling every renowned sect across the Northern Domain into a grand procession to pay formal respects in the Central Domain."

—"...Isn't that far too extravagant?!"-Protector Qiu asked in disbelief.-"Are they certain they won't offend Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable?"

—"Rest assured, their calculations are far more meticulous than yours,"-the Sect Master of Yuke Xuan smiled wryly.-"First and foremost, despite the sheer size of the delegation, the highest cultivation among them will only be a few Mahayana cultivators; not a single northern Tribulation Transcendence titan will attend. In any case, judging by current rumors, following the manifestation of the Ascension Stairway, the supreme powerhouses of the north—including Her Eminence the Goddess—have already paid their private visits to the Untiring Immortal Venerable."

—"I see..."-Protector Qiu nodded in understanding.

Without the "Venerables" present, no matter how many cultivators gathered, they were merely juniors. Under such circumstances, a massive delegation did not appear threatening; rather, it conveyed the utmost sincerity.

—"Furthermore, their stated pretext for the visit is not the Ascension Stairway,"-the Sect Master noted.-"While everyone knows full well they are going for the Stairway, rushing over explicitly for it would sound too much like an interrogation. Thus, they concocted an alternative excuse."

—"What excuse?"

—"The Immortal Arts Assembly and the Tianling Auction,"-the Sect Master said, a rather bizarre smile touching his lips.-"With so many people and so many factions, by the time their preparations are complete, winter will be upon us. That happens to coincide with the grand finals of the Immortal Arts Assembly as well as the opening of the Tianling Auction. Consequently, they can claim in full righteousness that they are merely traveling to attend the tournament finals and the auction!"

—"Hold on... The Immortal Arts Assembly? Ha, hahaha!"-Protector Qiu burst into uproarious laughter.-"I actually caught wind of a most amusing rumor: this Immortal Arts Assembly was originally hosted by the Central Domain sects merely as a pretext to appease the Immortal Venerable after Carefree Palace offended him! Now the Northern Domain is using that exact same tournament as their pretext to come knocking?!"

—"Indeed. The timing couldn't be more perfect,"-the Sect Master of Yuke Xuan remarked.

—"Hahahaha... hah."-Protector Qiu's laughter gradually tapered off. Lowering his voice, he asked:

—"Is it truly a coincidence?"

The Sect Master of Yuke Xuan offered no answer.

He had heard another rumor: back during the New Year when the Central Domain cultivators traveled to Mount Tianling to "apologize," the Untiring Immortal Venerable had already dropped casual hints regarding White Jade Capital.

In other words, whether it was the Ascension Stairway or White Jade Capital, everything had been resting firmly in the Untiring Immortal Venerable's palm from the very beginning.

Come to think of it, restoring both the Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital was hardly something that could be accomplished overnight. For the Untiring Immortal Venerable to unveil them at this precise juncture, perfectly synchronized with *The White Jade Capital Daily* and the journal *Spells*, carried the unmistakable impression of a terrifyingly elaborate grand strategy.

Yet whether intentionally or inadvertently, by acting as the regional distributor for these goods, Yuke Xuan had already been drawn onto the board.

Could they, too, be a calculated piece in the Immortal Venerable's overarching plan?

After that [hair-raising realization](# "Literal CN: 细思恐极 (xì sī kǒng jí) — terrifying upon deeper reflection"), Protector Qiu remarked with heavy gravity:

—"It seems a trip to the Central Domain is unavoidable."

—"Indeed. We must make the journey,"-the Sect Master nodded in accord.

Pausing, the Sect Master forced a faint smile:

—"Still, the most exaggerated part about the Northern Domain is this: to demonstrate the solemnity of their visit, they have even suspended this autumn's [Grand Recruitment](# "Literal CN: 大招 (dàzhāo) — major disciple recruitment")! They are currently burning the midnight oil to forge a colossal flying ark capable of transporting several thousand passengers specifically for this journey!"

—"A flying ark?"-Protector Qiu attempted to lighten the mood with a laugh.-"Haha, why bother building a new one? Isn't the Palace of Carefree Wandering famous for manufacturing those?"

He managed two dry chuckles before faltering under his Sect Master's chilly, unamused gaze.

Putting the Heavenly Spirit Sect and Carefree Palace in the same sentence was indeed nothing to joke about.

If anything, it was deeply unsettling.

—"May disaster not strike,"-Protector Qiu murmured under his breath.

—"A difficult wish,"-the Sect Master of Yuke Xuan sighed, turning a chess piece over in his fingers.-"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable has already laid out the board. The match has begun."

[And we are merely the chess pieces upon it, aren't we?] he thought gloomily, staring down at the board.

---

Meanwhile, in the Northern Domain:

—"I'm supposed to oversee the construction of the flying ark?!"

The young master of Sky City, Jian Nuo, cried out in sheer shock.

Normally, he was never one to shirk administrative duties, but this time he hollered at the top of his lungs:

—"No way! Absolutely not! I was already packing to leave for the Central Domain! If I don't go right this second, my [wife](# "Literal CN: 老婆 (lǎopó) — wife; modern slang used presumptuously for one's crush or waifu") is going to be snatched away—!"

Right alongside that frantic wail, Lu Shulong's chess piece rolled off the table and clattered onto the floor.
