# Chapter 66: Raised in Vain (第66章 白养了)

Yet how could these six skeletons exist here?

Given that he had already reincarnated, shouldn't everything have [reset to zero](# "Literal CN: 归零 (guī líng) — reset to zero / returned to the starting state")?!

Zhou Hui sank deep into contemplation.

Were it anyone else standing here—take that transmigrated 'protagonist,' for instance—they likely could do nothing beyond 'thinking,' seeing as they didn't even understand how they had transmigrated in the first place. Zhou Hui, however, was fundamentally different. Reincarnation, the system, transmigration—every single one was something he had personally engineered, coded, and controlled with his own hands. When walking a path paved step-by-step by one's own design, encountering a [bug](# "English in original: BUG — software glitch/defect") at least left actionable traces to follow.

At the very least, he understood the operating principles behind his own rebirth with crystalline clarity.

The *Seven Cycles Revolving Wheel Method* he had authored was his vehicle for rebirth. The underlying mechanic of this cultivation technique was harnessing the sheer deluge of spiritual energy unleashed upon the demise of a Tribulation Transcendence supreme powerhouse to execute a temporal leap—which conveniently served as a recycling mechanism for that apocalyptic destructive power, preventing the vicinity of Mount Tianling from degenerating into a scorched wasteland upon his death.

Yet Zhou Hui, after all, had not attained True Immortality; he possessed no absolute authority to manipulate time at will. Consequently, this temporal leap was strictly bounded, incapable of reshaping chronology arbitrarily. Thus, Zhou Hui had established a [save point](# "Literal CN: 存档点 (cúndàng diǎn) — save point / save state"):

The exact instant he founded Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Back then, Zhou Hui had mobilized the power of heaven and earth to construct the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array for Heavenly Spirit Sect, creating an enormous beacon of spiritual coordinates. Even across the sprawling timeline, it blazed with incandescent radiance—an exceptionally conspicuous waypoint.

Following each cycle of death, Zhou Hui would guide his soul to backtrack toward those exact coordinates, simultaneously channeling spiritual energy to scrub away the traces of the elapsed timeline. It was much like an eraser passing over a sheet of paper filled with pencil sketches, or formatting a hard drive that had already been loaded with data.

Indeed: what had occurred had occurred. What Zhou Hui jumped was time, not space. Branching into an alternate worldline—even a parallel branch forged by his own actions—was beneath his consideration. He demanded solely his own native worldline. Therefore, obliterating every footprint of 'what had occurred' was an absolute necessity.

This was also the genesis behind the *Seven Cycles* Revolving Wheel Method.

This worldline could withstand being wiped and overwritten by Zhou Hui only seven times at most. Any further tampering, even if Zhou Hui's divine soul survived intact, would cause the fabric of this worldline to unravel into total collapse.

Naturally, in places where the 'pencil strokes' had pressed especially hard, residual indentations were bound to linger.

Among those, the mutual cannibalization of Taotie beast souls leaving behind impressions was hardly shocking.

What was bizarre was why these six skeletons remained so flawlessly intact—and at this precise juncture in time!

Indeed: at this exact juncture in 'historical time,' Yuwen Xuan shouldn't have been eaten yet!

After all, time was linear. Even if residual impressions were left behind, they should only manifest at the precise moment the event transpired. It was just like a novel's manuscript: if the Taotie head incident took place on page 172, then even if Zhou Hui failed to delete it cleanly, a reader would still have to turn to page 172 before catching sight of any residual marks.

Yet according to the timelines of the prior six lives, the 'Winter Solstice' milestone was fixed in stone: Yuwen Xuan had to attend the auction before ever laying eyes on the Flame Crystal Flower. Only afterward—whether by tailing Bai Wubin in secret or conducting a post-hoc investigation—would he trace his way to Blood Demon Hall, where he was subsequently devoured.

Given the desolate, remote coordinates of Blood Demon Hall, the journey alone was enough to eat up several months of travel. Thus, at this current moment, the Yuwen Xuan of previous timelines shouldn't have even arrived at Blood Demon Hall yet!

In this seventh life, however, Zhou Hui had unlocked a subterranean map beneath the Abyss Sea and utilized a Kunpeng as their mode of transit. Even with all his dillydallying along the way and the substantial time spent erecting defensive formations upon reaching Blood Demon Hall, a mere [ten days](# "Literal CN: 一旬 (yī xún) — a ten-day period in traditional reckoning") had elapsed in total.

In other words: the novel hadn't even reached page 172, yet Yuwen Xuan's bones were already lying here!

*This* was the true source of Zhou Hui's astonishment.

No, at this stage, a novel could no longer adequately describe the phenomenon. This circumstance bore far closer resemblance to a hard drive.

Zhou Hui had already formatted it clean, yet before ever executing the designated program, extraneous data had materialized out of thin air.

This scenario looked either like someone had performed data recovery on deleted sectors, or outright resembled a BIOS-level virus—a corrupted state where a simple hard drive format and clean operating system reinstallation proved wholly powerless to wipe or overwrite the viral code!

Now *this* was getting interesting.

What was the BIOS of the cultivation world?

The Heavenly Dao?

Had someone injected a virus into the Heavenly Dao itself, such that no matter how many times the system was reinstalled, a specific viral script would trigger and execute regardless?

Zhou Hui let out a faint chuckle.

—"Since the data has been restored anyway, let us see what actually transpired,"-Zhou Hui sneered coldly.

He pressed his palm against one of the "Yuwen Xuans" to read the residual "memories."

Of course, impressions were merely impressions. Yuwen Xuan's soul was not present; what Zhou Hui could extract was solely the events witnessed by this skeleton. He could not perceive Yuwen Xuan's thoughts at the time, only observe the actions he had taken.

Across the previous six lives, the sequence was broadly identical:

Yuwen Xuan had either tailed Blood Demon Hall in secret or swindled Bai Wubin's trust to tag along. Upon arriving at the spatial rift, he did not strike immediately; instead, he merely scouted the location of the Flame Crystal Flowers. Seizing a moment when the Blood Demon Hall crowd wasn't paying attention, he prepared to slip into the "cave (nostril)" of the extraterrestrial fiend's head.

It was at this precise juncture that Yuwen Xuan pulled a [bafflingly bizarre maneuver](# "Literal CN: 迷惑操作 (míhuò cāozuò) — confusing / bewildering / baffling move"):

For reasons unknown, he left his mortal flesh behind on the outside, sending only his divine soul flying in!

Yes! He was not eaten at all!

Rather, like shedding a suit of clothes, he had stripped off his physical body, found a hiding spot to tuck it away safely, and then flown his remarkably sturdy, highly cultivated divine soul straight into the cavernous nostril!

If Zhou Hui had initially thought Yuwen Xuan did this out of fear of alerting Blood Demon Hall during his first viewing, later witnessing Yuwen Xuan discard his physical shell and venture in solely as a soul even at the imminent risk of discovery by Blood Demon Hall completely baffled him.

In short, the moment Yuwen Xuan's divine soul entered, the entire "mountain" began quaking violently. It didn't look like feeding; it looked like an all-out brawl inside! But with such violent tremors shaking a place lacking pre-established protective formations, Blood Demon Hall met with sheer catastrophe. Every single time, more than half the hall collapsed. Even a Body Integration grandmaster like Shen Qingqian could do no more than shield a fraction of her disciples and evacuate outward—

And that was the precise moment Bai Wubin was abandoned by his master.

Although Shen Qingqian had not done so intentionally, Bai Wubin's luck was evidently abysmal: every time disaster struck Blood Demon Hall, he was critically injured in the most awkward, inaccessible corner imaginable. He could only watch helplessly as his master ushered away his junior brothers and sisters, unable even to cry out for help as Shen Qingqian and the others departed.

—"Is this part of the [destiny](# "Literal CN: 命运 (mìngyùn) — destiny / fate") (virus) script as well?"-Zhou Hui scoffed coldly.

Still, even from the silent memory projections, one could tell that the Bai Wubin of that period remained thoroughly sane and normal. He bore no grudge against anyone; he had even breathed a sigh of relief upon seeing his master and siblings escape the calamity.

The crux of the matter was that "mountain" (the head).

After collapsing Blood Demon Hall, the titanic head kept shaking incessantly until it finally pried open the spatial conduit behind it, tumbling downward just as it had when kicked by Zhou Hui moments ago.

Yet that was as far as the vision went.

In the prior six lives, Yuwen Xuan had stashed his physical shell with remarkable "cleverness." Each time the head rolled down, the skeleton did not tumble along with it as it had in this life, remaining stranded beside Blood Demon Hall.

Zhou Hui waited with immense patience. Approximately half a shichen later, a suffocating, heaven-blotting surge of demonic qi gushed forth from the conduit, engulfing the entire spatial realm—and engulfing Bai Wubin as well.

—"Tch!"-Zhou Hui clicked his tongue coldly.

He lingered for quite some time, ensuring no further 'plot points' remained, before terminating the temporal reconstruction.

However, the six skeletons evidently could not endure such spiritual probing; with a brittle clatter, they crumbled into a heap of dust and bone fragments across the floor.

Zhou Hui showed no disgust. Procuring an empty storage pouch, he painstakingly collected the remains of his eldest disciple from prior lives—wondering wryly whether this qualified as a [white-haired elder burying the black-haired young](# "Literal CN: 白发人送黑发人 (bái fà rén sòng hēi fà rén) — a traditional Chinese idiom for an elder burying someone from a younger generation, a tragic inversion of nature").

Once the ashes were gathered, Zhou Hui casually took that persistent Taotie soul fragment—the one stubbornly trying to sprout a mouth to bite him—stuffed it into the pouch alongside the bone dust, slapped more than a dozen layers of restrictive seals over it, and stowed it away securely.

He planned to deal with all of this once he returned.

At present, Zhou Hui judged that overwhelming surge of demonic qi to be the most pressing concern.

He had no idea where that demonic qi had originated. In the past six lives, it had overrun Blood Demon Hall; he certainly could not permit a repeat performance here—though the arrays he had instructed Ran Deng to engrave already included provisions for warding off demonic qi, Zhou Hui had always believed in being thoroughly prepared.

Zhou Hui first went to inspect the colossal head.

As the severed head of an extraterrestrial fiend, it was unquestionably the prime suspect.

Yet this was, after all, a skull that had served as the dwelling of a Taotie soul fragment. Zhou Hui stepped inside to investigate, only to find the interior licked spotlessly clean. Forget demonic qi—if even the faintest whiff of any other living organism's aura lingered within the bone marrow, it would be an insult to a Taotie's gluttony!

With the sole exception of the Flame Crystal Flowers.

Though designated a 'flower,' the Flame Crystal Flower was in truth an organic aggregate of concentrated energy. While cultivation and modern physics followed entirely different paradigms, purely in terms of energetic yield, a single flower was equivalent to hundreds of tons of radioactive nuclear fuel—merely an analogy, of course, as Flame Crystal Flowers possessed zero radiation and caused no harm prior to refining.

Zhou Hui's reason for seeking out highest-grade Flame Crystal Flowers was exceptionally straightforward:

If no path to ascension existed, he would simply blast one open.

Yes: simple, crude, and brutish.

Yet undeniably effective.

Regrettably, all the way through his sixth life, Zhou Hui had never managed to secure a single Flame Crystal Flower.

Naturally, Zhou Hui hadn't been particularly obsessed either; such a blunt, violent contingency possessed far too many logical loopholes and ranked merely as the ultimate backup among backups in his grand ascension architecture. Back during his first life, dispatching Yuwen Xuan to search for this flower had been driven purely by how monumentally annoying the brat was, constantly disrupting his cultivation. Zhou Hui had merely assigned Yuwen Xuan to hunt down something with such impossibly harsh growth requirements that it was practically nonexistent.

Consequently, Zhou Hui had never paid much heed to Yuwen Xuan's subsequent "disappearances"—he figured that if the wild monkey were genuinely scouring the world for Flame Crystal Flowers, vanishing without a trace was par for the course.

Who could have imagined that the Flame Crystal Flowers had been this close all along?

(To Zhou Hui's standards, being within the same mortal realm counted as practically next door.)

Zhou Hui scrutinized the skull's interior and discovered that someone had engineered an artificial micro-domain tailored specifically to cultivate Flame Crystal Flowers: torrid, sealed, with its only two apertures—the nostrils—aimed directly toward Shen Qingqian's chambers in Blood Demon Hall. In truth, it was tapping into the cyclical spiritual energy Shen Qingqian circulated during her cultivation sessions to nourish the blooms.

This spiritual horticulture technique alone was so sophisticated that even Zhou Hui felt humbled.

Yet why? Why would someone deliberately construct such a specialized micro-domain inside a skull housing a Taotie soul?

It looked for all the world like emergency rations thoughtfully prepared for a Taotie!

However, a Taotie possessing merely a remnant soul could never digest these Flame Crystal Flowers. It was far too fragile; a single lick would blow it to pieces. Unless it reclaimed its missing soul fragments and became whole once more...

—"Hmph,"-Zhou Hui scoffed coldly once more.

Since the skull harbored no demonic qi, that demonic surge had to originate from elsewhere.

Stepping out of the colossal head, Zhou Hui proceeded to inspect the entire hall.

Just as his initial impression suggested, aside from the spatial conduit overhead, the sprawling hall appeared entirely mundane, scarcely different from the countless teleportation halls scattered across the Abyss Sea. Furthermore, this place also looked as though it had been thoroughly licked clean by a Taotie; not a whisper of residual aura remained, yielding zero clues at first glance.

Under such circumstances, theory dictated pushing open the hall's doors to see what lay beyond.

Yet Zhou Hui did not give up so easily.

Bringing two fingers to his lips, he whistled a sharp gust of wind. The thick carpet of dust covering the entire hall whipped into the air, leaving the stone paving as pristine as if it had been washed with fresh water.

Gazing at the teleportation array engraved into the floor, Zhou Hui first took a spiritual rubbing of the complete formation, layered several protective barriers over it, and only then began studying the rubbing in depth.

One careful glance was all it took for alarm to seize him.

Zhou Hui had initially assumed this array was merely one of those ancient teleportation formations constructed to summon cultivators during the fiendish invasion—countless remnants of such broken arrays dotted the Abyss Sea, yet virtually none remained intact, and restoring them was virtually impossible, which was why Zhou Hui had barely spared it a glance.

Yet the formation in Zhou Hui's hands was entirely intact!

Worse still, relying on his supposedly 'shallow' knowledge of formations, Zhou Hui deduced that the destination of this teleportation array was not the Central Domain, nor the Eastern, Western, Southern, or Northern territories—in fact, it didn't even lead anywhere in this world!

Indeed: it led directly to the **True Immortal Realm**!

—"...Legend had it that during the God-Demon War, the cultivators of White Jade Capital directly summoned True Immortals. So it was actually true?..."-Zhou Hui murmured under his breath, as if merely reviewing an obscure historical tidbit.

Yet that was hardly the crux.

First the Flame Crystal Flowers, now a teleportation array leading to the True Immortal Realm—treasures he had dreamed of across his prior lives had suddenly landed right into his lap.

Had it been anyone else, they would have been ecstatic beyond measure!

Zhou Hui, however, didn't feel the slightest temptation; he merely knitted his brows tightly.

—"No matter how I look at it, this smells like a trap,"-he muttered.

Zhou Hui inspected the array, made hundreds of meticulous copies through various recording techniques, and then directly produced a specialized set of stone-carving tools. He chiseled off the entire teleportation array alongside the hall's stone floor tiles, packed the whole assembly into a storage pouch, and slapped multiple layers of heavy restrictions over it!

Having executed this operation with the vigilance of facing a mortal foe, Zhou Hui let out a sigh of relief.

Up to this point, he still hadn't located the origin of the demonic qi.

In that case, the only remaining option was to step outside and check.

Steeling himself for combat, Zhou Hui strode toward the doors of the grand hall.

Curiously enough, he felt not the slightest premonition of danger.

And in fact, reality proved just as peaceful: Zhou Hui pushed open the double doors with effortless ease and stepped through to the other side.

Behind the doors lay no demonic qi, no ferocious monsters, no hazardous traps whatsoever—only an elongated white jade gallery stretching toward a void-like darkness. In the ruins of the God-Demon Battlefield, such architecture was a common sight.

Yet if no demonic qi lay behind the doors, where had the surge that engulfed Blood Demon Hall come from?

The True Immortal Realm?

Zhou Hui furrowed his brows in contemplation.

Still, having already ventured out, he naturally intended to walk to the far end of the gallery to verify whether anything uncanny lurked at its terminus.

Yet Zhou Hui had taken merely a few steps when he suddenly detected a faint wisp of aura leaking from the door.

Very light.

Very faint.

Very familiar.

Without even thinking, Zhou Hui flicked his sleeve backhanded:

*SLAP!*

The "door" immediately tilted sideways, teetering on the verge of collapse, as even more aura leaked out from its frame.

Evidently realizing it could no longer keep up the pretense, the "door" burst into loud, wailing grievances:

—"Master, you hit me again!"-Yuwen Xuan wailed in complaint, as though he had suffered the greatest injustice under heaven.

Transforming into a plume of black smoke, "it" attempted to flee upward toward the spatial conduit. But how could such childish tricks catch Zhou Hui off guard? With a single snatch, he seized "it" right by the nape:

—"Yuwen Xuan! Why are you here?!"-Zhou Hui demanded.

Yes. This time, it was the real deal.

The genuine Yuwen Xuan.

Yet Zhou Hui didn't recall bringing this disciple along; he had ordered him to stay at Heavenly Spirit Sect, and the boy had obediently agreed...

Obediently...

Fine. Yuwen Xuan had never possessed an obedient bone in his entire body.

—"You didn't tail me the entire way here, did you?"-Zhou Hui said, glaring at the black smoke that was Yuwen Xuan.

—"...Yes,"-seeing no avenue of escape, Yuwen Xuan could only answer "obediently," not forgetting to offer excuses:-"But after all, that brand is directly tied to me! Master, how could I let you risk your life for my sake while I sat idly in my cave abode doing nothing?"

—"Heh!"-ignoring his rationalizations, Zhou Hui merely demanded:-"And then you followed me down here?"

[—"Yes."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

[—"Everything I just did, you saw it all?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"Yes."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

[—"When you followed me down, you turned into a door to watch from the sidelines?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"You're lying!"-Zhou Hui grabbed the wisp of black smoke, intending to knead it into a ball just like that Taotie soul remnant.-"If you had followed me down, you wouldn't have had the time to disguise yourself as a door... Wait a second, what on earth do you look like right now?!"

Zhou Hui suddenly noticed the texture in his hand felt completely wrong.

Flinging his hand, he tossed the plume of black smoke onto the ground. The black vapor condensed into a solid form—yet it was not the tall, handsome young man he had painstakingly raised for over two centuries until he stood taller than Zhou Hui himself.

Instead, it was a tiny, pitch-black, plump little—

Lamb?!

—"What did you do?"-Zhou Hui demanded, his tone carrying the ominous weight of an impending tempest.

Seeing that evasion was impossible, Yuwen Xuan had no choice but to confess his escapade in full.

As it turned out, Yuwen Xuan truly had tagged along with Zhou Hui the entire way.

The day he had agreed to Zhou Hui's face, he had secretly crafted an avatar and parked it back at Heavenly Spirit Sect to fool Qu Qianxing, Mo Xi, and the others. Meanwhile, his true body slipped onto the Kunpeng, following Zhou Hui all the way to Blood Demon Hall.

While Zhou Hui had Ran Deng busy laying out the grand array, Yuwen Xuan was still loitering aimlessly around the perimeter.

[—"Just loitering aimlessly?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"Of course! Even if I wanted to do something, if I ended up implicating my junior brothers and sisters or the Blood Demon Hall folks, I know I wouldn't just get docked cultivation—Master would beat me to death!"-Yuwen Xuan cried out in grievance.

His moral bottom line evidently consisted entirely of not being beaten to death by Zhou Hui.

—"Keep talking,"-Zhou Hui sighed, massaging his temples.

Yuwen Xuan had caught the itch when overhearing Zhou Hui analyze the spatial layout for Ran Deng. Hearing Zhou Hui deduce that a spatial conduit likely lay behind the colossal head, he couldn't sit still any longer. Taking advantage of the gap before Ran Deng could finish the formations, he had snuck down here all by himself.

—In that regard, he was virtually identical to his past lifetimes.

—"And then?"-Zhou Hui asked, his eyebrow twitching violently as he suppressed the urge to beat him black and blue on the spot.

—"And then I saw a pile of bones lying over here that looked exceptionally delicious, so I couldn't resist eating them,"-Yuwen Xuan confessed.-"And then I turned into this."

Saying this, tears fell *pitter-patter* from his eyes. Because he was currently in the form of a plump, round little lamb, it looked rather adorable.

Even though Zhou Hui knew this [little bastard](# "Literal CN: 王八羔子 (wángbā gāozi) — affectionate curse for an unruly rascal / brat") was fake-crying—after turning three, this brat had never cried easily; or rather, he never cried in front of anyone else, reserving his tears exclusively for his master—Zhou Hui still couldn't help softening slightly.

Hoisting the little black lamb by the scruff of its neck, Zhou Hui pinched and palpated its frame from head to hoof.

—"These aren't sheep bones; they are Taotie bones,"-Zhou Hui said, frowning.

—"Ying, ying,"-Yuwen Xuan whimpered. Thoroughly enjoying the massage, he sprawled all four limbs flat in Zhou Hui's palm while putting on an aggrieved act:-"Why did I end up like this? Do I turn into whatever I eat? I don't want this!"

Zhou Hui tossed it back onto the ground with a wet *plop*, asking:

—"...You thought those bones looked delicious. Don't tell me you also found the Taotie soul... the thing I just extracted from that head, appetizing as well?"

—"How could I? Such an eerie, creepy thing..."-Yuwen Xuan replied.

It would have sounded convincing had he not been drooling uncontrollably from the corner of his snout.

Yuwen Xuan naturally thought it was immensely delicious! He had smelled the scent from thousands of li away; had it not been for his terror of Zhou Hui, he would have charged into that skull and swallowed it in a single gulp.

Of course, it was truly a blessing in disguise that he hadn't.

Recalling the brutal way Zhou Hui had slapped that colossal head, Yuwen Xuan shivered in terror, though a trace of pettiness lingered:

Usually, he was the sole recipient of that special treatment... wait, no, who in their right mind would want that kind of treatment?!

Thinking of this, Yuwen Xuan blurted out:

—"Master, why did you call it 'Xuan'er' too?!"-Yuwen Xuan asked directly.

[You rarely even call me that these days!]

—"Speaking of which, how did you mask your aura?"-Zhou Hui countered, ignoring his whining entirely.

Not only had Zhou Hui failed to detect him, even that Taotie soul hadn't noticed his presence.

—"You haven't answered my question yet,"-Yuwen Xuan attempted to play dumb, but catching the look in Zhou Hui's eyes, he sheepishly confessed:-"I ate it... I ate my own aura."

What a straightforward answer.

If a Taotie could devour demonic qi, why couldn't it devour its own aura?

The final piece of the puzzle had clicked into place.

Zhou Hui had always assumed Yuwen Xuan was lured to Blood Demon Hall, roused that titanic head, and was swallowed in a single bite. But when it came to Yuwen Xuan, how could anything ever be that simple?

Who ate whom was entirely up in the air!

The bizarre actions of the Yuwen Xuan from previous timelines now had a logical explanation: this [troublesome brat](# "Literal CN: 熊孩子 (xióng háizi) — bear child, Chinese slang for a terribly naughty, destructive child") most likely thought the Taotie soul smelled delicious. He proactively shed his mortal vessel—which would have been devoured down to the dregs the instant it contacted the beast soul—and charged into the skull purely as a divine soul, locking into a fierce brawl over who was eating whom!

Yet regardless of whether Yuwen Xuan was devoured or devoured the other party, both outcomes culminated in tumbling down into this very hall.

And unlike the empty hall Zhou Hui currently saw, this hall originally housed the skeletal remains of a Taotie!

Thus, soul and skeleton were assembled in full. Someone had even thoughtfully prepared emergency rations in the form of "Flame Crystal Flowers."

The Taotie would successfully revive.

However, following the God-Demon War, an armistice between the True Immortal Realm and the extraterrestrial fiends stipulated that to allow this mortal realm to recuperate, neither party was permitted to descend into this world in their true physical forms. An entity like a Taotie, capable of ravaging an entire world with ease, naturally could not remain either.

"Coincidentally," an ancient teleportation array sat directly beneath the Taotie.

Its titanic power was guaranteed to activate the array, banishing it far away.

And just like that, Zhou Hui's eldest disciple had "vanished without a trace."

—"What an exquisitely crafted scheme!"-Zhou Hui muttered, the fury in his heart deepening.

If he ever discovered who had orchestrated this plot against his disciple, he would tear them into a thousand pieces!

Yuwen Xuan stared silently at his master.

Even he did not dare provoke Zhou Hui at a time like this, for Zhou Hui was evidently furious for real.

—"Haven't I told you time and again not to eat everything you see?!"-Zhou Hui scolded, tapping the lamb's skull.-"Now that your soul and bone have fused into one, you can't even transform back!"

Fortunately, the Taotie soul hadn't been completed; otherwise, this disciple would have ascended on the spot, just like in prior lifetimes.

—"With Master here, I'll definitely be able to transform back!"-the little lamb declared, rolling four or five times across the floor after being flicked on the forehead, yet brimming with unshakeable faith in its master.

[Is that your justification for courting death?!]

Zhou Hui glared at it, yet after thinking with resigned helplessness, he rummaged through his storage pouch—speaking of which, he had prepared an absurd variety of items this time, and every single one had found a use, which was ridiculous in its own right—producing some flour and spiritual water. He then poured out the bone dust collected from the skeletons of his disciple's prior lives, mixed it in, kneaded the dough, heated a pan, and fried it up, swiftly producing a donut-shaped flatbread pancake.

Zhou Hui looped this pancake—infused with the skeletal ashes of Yuwen Xuan's past lives—around the little black lamb's neck.

Because the pancake was excessively large, the little lamb tilted under the weight, nearly stumbling over.

—"Starting today, you are forbidden from eating anything else!"-Zhou Hui declared.

[—"What?!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

—"Whenever you feel hungry, you may only nibble on this pancake,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"As you digest it, focus your senses intently on your human form and reconstruct your original body."

—"B-But it's so tiny, I'll finish it in one gulp!"-Yuwen Xuan protested in distress.

He actually opened his snout and took a bite.

*Hm? Pretty tasty.*

Another bite.

*It's really delicious.*

Another...

When Yuwen Xuan went to take a third bite, he realized his little belly had puffed up into a taut sphere; he couldn't swallow another crumb!

[—"Only two bites?! I'm stuffed after only two bites?!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

Suffering a devastating psychological blow, Yuwen Xuan burst into tears once more, weeping *pitter-patter*.

This time, he was crying for real.

Zhou Hui felt he had gotten exactly what he deserved.

He couldn't help wondering about the true age of the Taotie soul dwelling within Yuwen Xuan—judging from Yuwen Xuan's behavior and the petite size of this black lamb, wasn't this beast just an unruly Taotie cub?

He had [raised this brat for two hundred years in vain](# "Literal CN: 白养了两百年 (bái yǎngle liǎng bǎi nián) — raised for 200 years for nothing / wasted effort"), under the delusion that he had nurtured him into an adult!

Zhou Hui sighed, pushing this vexing parental dilemma aside to return to 'proper business.'

Though he knew the teleportation array harbored the greatest secret, what this hall actually was still warranted investigation.

Zhou Hui was indeed somewhat curious:

What kind of place would construct a grand hall dedicated to summoning True Immortals?

Harboring such questions, Zhou Hui stepped out through the main doors once more and strolled along the white jade corridor.

Yuwen Xuan hastily trotted his tiny hooves. Encased in that oversized flatbread pancake like a kitten fitted with an ill-fitting [Elizabeth collar](# "Literal CN: 伊丽莎白项圈 (Yīlìshābái xiàngquān) — Elizabethan cone / collar used on pets to prevent licking"), he wobbled clumsily behind Zhou Hui, miraculously managing to keep pace.

—"What lies at the end of this corridor?... Don't tell me you were so busy gnawing on bones that you never even checked?!"-Zhou Hui asked, refusing to look at his disciple's comical display.

—"Of course I checked!"-Yuwen Xuan retorted.

Naturally, he was too embarrassed to admit that after swallowing the bones in a few gulps, he had turned into a sheep, failed completely to transform back, and didn't dare face his master in such a state, thus scouring every corner of the surroundings instead.

Recalling what lay along the gallery, Yuwen Xuan abruptly forgot his pitiful predicament and brightened up:

—"Master, Master! We're rich! The end of the corridor is packed with incredible treasures! Finding this place means we've struck it rich!"-Yuwen Xuan exclaimed excitedly.

—"Struck it rich?"-Zhou Hui repeated, at a loss as to what could possibly make someone like him—who possessed multiple high-grade spiritual veins—feel 'rich.'

Yet when Zhou Hui truly arrived at the terminus of the gallery, his eyes widened in spite of himself.

—"Ah. We truly have struck it rich,"-Zhou Hui murmured, gazing out at the breathtaking vista before him, momentarily unable to describe the exhilaration surging through his chest.

For at the broken precipice of this fractured gallery lay neither a demonic den nor an empty abyss. Looking downward from this vantage point, there sprawled an entire city!

A breathtaking, magnificent celestial city!

This was unquestionably the ancient capital of cultivators that had fallen during the God-Demon War, vanished without a trace for tens of thousands of years:

**White Jade Capital.**
