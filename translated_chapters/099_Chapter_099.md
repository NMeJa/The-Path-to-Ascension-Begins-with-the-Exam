# Chapter 99: [Nightmare](<# "Literal CN: 噩梦 (èmèng) — nightmare; chapter title">) (第99章 噩梦)

[What is Master saying to that "black lamb"?]

Qu Qianxing gazed up from below at Zhou Hui high in the sky, thinking with a strange feeling.

Yet this was merely a stray thought drifting through Qu Qianxing's mind; he had no real desire to know the answer.

For an honor student, a teacher reprimanding a classmate during class was naturally far less important than the lesson itself. Qu Qianxing merely cast an incidental glance at his teacher before refocusing his entire mind on his "coursework."

Karmic fortune, star charts, and celestial phenomena were, in truth, Qu Qianxing's "required courses."

Qu Qianxing was a spell cultivator, but strictly speaking, he leaned toward the path of a [hexagram spell cultivator](<# "Literal CN: 卦修 (guà xiū) — a divinatory or hexagram cultivator specializing in astromancy, fortune-telling, and calculating cosmic fate">).

In the cultivation world, this belonged to the "Great Dao."

Even in mortal minds, [pinching one's knuckles to divine](<# "Literal CN: 掐指一算 (qiā zhǐ yī suàn) — pinching knuckles to divine; calculating past, present, and future using Chinese astromantic finger-reckoning methods">) past, present, and future was considered an essential immortal skill. In the cultivation realm, observing celestial phenomena, reading star charts, and calculating the future was orthodox Great Dao. That was why the Heavenly Dao Alliance maintained the Ritual Court, tasked exclusively with presiding over the Grand Sacrificial Rites to Heaven.

In terms of prestige and status, hexagram and spell cultivators were the professions that exuded the [grandest immortal aura](<# "Literal CN: 最有仙人逼格 (zuì yǒu xiān rén bī gé) — possessing the most transcendent immortal swagger / elite aesthetic prestige">).

Yet once one truly entered the cultivation world, one discovered that alchemy cultivators and artifact refinement cultivators were by far the most ubiquitous.

The reason was simple: alchemy and artifact refinement yielded quick cash—that was, spirit stones!

Whatever one's primary specialty, one inevitably encountered times when pills and magical artifacts were necessary. Thus, alchemical and artifact cultivators raked in fortune, and every sect's Alchemy Hall and Artifact Refinement Hall remained the most fiercely contested choices.

Lately, for reasons unknown, sword cultivators had developed a trend of [crying poverty](<# "Literal CN: 哭穷 (kū qióng) — constantly complaining about being broke">). But in truth, sword cultivators were the least strapped for cash of all!

They only had one vital natal sword. Harsh forging conditions aside, it was essentially a one-time purchase—hardly comparable to the cost of an alchemist replacing an alchemy furnace. Moreover, sword cultivators nourished their swords through battle, constantly on the front lines exploring secret realms and slaying demons; their material rewards and bounties flowed in with virtually nothing flowing out—they were practically [acting like a Pixiu](<# "Literal CN: 属貔貅的 (shǔ pí xiū de) — referring to the mythical beast Pixiu, which devours wealth and has no anus, meaning wealth flows in with zero outgoing expenses">).

Therefore, those sword cultivators crying poverty were eighty percent likely complaining because they had [chopped off their hands](<# "Literal CN: 剁手 (duò shǒu) — Chinese internet slang for impulsive overspending / shopping addiction to the point of wanting to cut off one's buying hands">) from compulsive shopping.

The ones who were truly dirt-poor were the orthodox spell cultivators.

After all, magical research was the exact opposite of sword cultivation: all expenses, zero returns. Whether crafting magical implements or practicing spell formulas, immense volumes of consumables were consumed. Even if one succeeded, there was little tangible gain—spells could hardly be packaged and sold on market stalls like pills or artifacts. As a result, spell cultivators could never earn any real money.

Among them, "fortune-telling," as a branch of spellcraft, at least left spell cultivators a tiny avenue for revenue.

Yet fortune-telling sounded like a zero-capital business—setting up a street stall without even needing a beggar's bowl! In reality, anyone who tried it knew the truth: those unlucky souls seeking readings were just like [unlucky scholars writing serialized storybooks](<# "Literal CN: 写话本的倒霉书生 (xiě huà běn de dǎo méi shū shēng) — frustrated authors who can only stomach praise and meltdown at criticism">)—they could only bear to hear sweet praise, never a word of ill omen.

[—"You dare say my fate holds no sons, and my spouse has taken a concubine on the side?! Fantastic! Stinking charlatan, cursing me to my face! I paid you good money just to hear this garbage?! See if I don't smash your stall to pieces!"](<# "Speaker: Outraged Customer">)

Thus, fortune-telling had nothing to do with accuracy; it had devolved into pure psychology.

Consequently, spell cultivation, despite being a Great Dao, had declined.

Nowadays, those who still persisted on this Great Dao had to not only come from wealthy families, but also be [blessed by the heavens](<# "Literal CN: 老天爷赏饭吃 (lǎo tiān yé shǎng fàn chī) — the heavens bestowing a rice bowl; blessed with innate, god-given talent">).

Both Qingyang Qingyu and Qu Qianxing were like this.

Especially Qu Qianxing.

Qingyang Qingyu's aptitude was already a genius among geniuses, but Qu Qianxing was one step above. Not only was his cultivation like a game brimming with positive feedback, but when absorbing karmic fortune, the fortune practically chased after him.

After all, he possessed the "Talent of Wenqu"!

Even if Qu Qianxing had never cultivated, the moment he stepped into an imperial examination hall, the immense karmic fortune gathered from countless candidates would shower upon him, granting him supreme luck.

While the Immortal Arts Conference was not a civil examination hall, it was similarly a fiercely competitive venue testing the cultivation standards of cultivators. It was practically Qu Qianxing's home turf!

With the assistance of the karmic fortune artifact, this blessing became even more pronounced.

Qu Qianxing had merely approached the clouds of karmic fortune when golden streams swirled into a golden vortex, sweeping toward him and bathing him in a river of golden stars.

Yet at this moment, the sole obstacle turned out to be Qu Qianxing himself.

Just as Zhou Hui had said, karmic fortune was attracted to "cheerful, optimistic emotions." The fortunate became more fortunate, and the unlucky grew unluckier.

As for the exact scientific mechanics, Zhou Hui didn't quite understand them either.

After all, Zhou Hui himself had never been blessed by the heavens. He was a ruthless individual who had spent his entire life defying heaven—a man capable of studying the *Book of Changes* as pure mathematics and turning metaphysics into physics!

Yet ironically, his disciples were all blessed with the aptitudes of [Protagonists](<# "Literal CN: 天命之子 (tiān mìng zhī zǐ) — Child of Heavenly Destiny; Chosen One / Protagonist">)... or rather, final bosses.

When it came to studies, what Zhou Hui could do was lay the most solid foundation and point the direction. But how far his disciples could go relying on innate talents Zhou Hui had only heard rumors of depended on their own choices.

In experiencing karmic fortune, Qu Qianxing's practical intuition was sharper than Zhou Hui's.

For instance, Qu Qianxing knew that his highest, most efficient utilization of karmic fortune had been during the mortal imperial exams. Back then, he had been brimming with high spirits, believing himself the Chosen of Heaven. The karmic fortune he attracted was equally vibrant and energetic. Even after testing for three consecutive days in miserable exam stalls, his mental state remained peaked, enabling him to perform beyond his limits and draft the finest papers—even if, in the end, it was undone by his own face, landing him as the Tanhua.

Now, with Master's magical treasure assisting, he could theoretically harvest more karmic fortune, but what Qu Qianxing sensed in this moment was the anxiety, hostility, and fury radiating from the tournament contestants. The fortune he attracted was precisely the variety that magnified such negative emotions.

Yet karmic fortune born of negative emotions was not entirely without value—

[This karmic fortune would be quite suitable for demonic cultivation.]

Qu Qianxing thought, cold and objective.

In fact, the moment he thought this, a ring of karmic fortune was filtered out, circulating smoothly along the pathways of a certain cultivation method. It formed purplish-red blocks of color that looked rather sinister.

Qu Qianxing was jolted with sudden horror!

[Why did I think of demonic cultivation?!]

[And what kind of technique was that? I have no memory of ever practicing such a dangerous art that harvests negative emotions! Yet channeling it felt bafflingly familiar, as though I had practiced it countless times...]

Even as he thought this, his body involuntarily circulated the technique for two more cycles. Those discolored patches grew even more garish.

Simultaneously, Qu Qianxing felt his cultivation surge alongside the swelling fortune. In merely a split second, his cultivation jumped by an entire sub-tier! It seemed that if he continued, he could instantly reach the peak of Golden Core and strike toward Nascent Soul!

Qu Qianxing furrowed his brows.

He felt more and more that whatever technique he was using was not on the up-and-up.

Anything with rapid-progression traits was rarely an orthodox technique.

Qu Qianxing had never been in a rush to raise his cultivation.

Mainly because rushing was impossible anyway.

Although he possessed a single Gold Heavenly Spirit Root, he had missed the prime age for entering cultivation. On top of that, he had burned his own flesh and blood in an archaic sacrificial ritual, requiring his entire meridians to be remolded. Zhou Hui had once sighed that had his spirit root been any lesser, he wouldn't even have had a chance to cultivate at all.

But that wasn't even the deadliest part.

What truly hindered Qu Qianxing's cultivation speed was his unique talent: the Talent of Wenqu.

This destiny was that of a "Celestial Official," following the path of "Descending to the Mortal Realm to Undergo Tribulation." In other words, Qu Qianxing never needed to cultivate; so long as he lived out a virtuous mortal life with accumulated merits, he would ascend to immortalhood upon death.

But that path belonged to the era before the Celestial Ladder was severed!

In this era, mortals no longer possessed such an ascension path. Furthermore, Qu Qianxing had defied imperial orders and mutilated his own body—a grave cosmic sin. Even if he completed a mortal life, he would never become an immortal. Let alone the fact that he ultimately chose to defy heaven and change his fate by walking the path of immortal cultivation—a path diametrically opposed to his heavenly destiny!

Therefore, whenever someone like Qu Qianxing cultivated, he received the highest "VIP treatment" from heavenly lightning:

Every single tribulation was a full Nine-Nine Heavenly Tribulation!

Without thorough preparation, such a tribulation was lethal.

Fortunately, he found Zhou Hui as his master—a master with the richest experience in defying heaven and altering fate.

—"There are two choices,"-Zhou Hui had said back then. He had not yet been Qu Qianxing's master, merely offering advice.-"One is rather orthodox. Are you not a Gold Spirit Root? The metal element is well-suited for defensive spells, and there are many such techniques. I can introduce a few to you..."

—"And the other choice?"-Qu Qianxing had asked with great difficulty.

Back then, he was hiding battered and wretched in a subterranean cave, his face and body covered in massive scabs that had not fully healed, revealing crimson flesh beneath like molten magma. Not a single trace of his handsome looks remained; he resembled an all-devouring demon.

Back then, Qu Qianxing had no desire to become a shield to protect anyone, not even himself. He only wanted to become the sharpest spear, piercing through every wicked, unjust existence.

And Zhou Hui's alternate plan suited Qu Qianxing's thoughts perfectly:

—"The other is much harder. It requires thoroughly mutating your metal spiritual energy into offensive lightning spiritual energy, confronting heavenly thunder head-on. This mutation process is slow and protracted, but the advantage is extreme offensive power, highly suited for combat. Because each time your lightning is tempered by heavenly tribulation, it will become the strongest lightning. At the pinnacle of cultivation, you will be a walking heavenly tribulation—"

—"I choose to become the heavenly tribulation!"-Qu Qianxing had answered without the slightest hesitation.

For this, he had chosen a protracted path, reaching "only" the peak of Golden Core after two full centuries of cultivation.

Such a Qu Qianxing would naturally never act rashly.

Although he had felt restless lately, inexplicably wanting to accelerate his cultivation speed, facing this sudden "epiphany" of an unknown technique and a doubling of cultivation, Qu Qianxing's very first reaction was:

—"Master—"

—"What happened?"

Turning around, Qu Qianxing saw Master already standing beside him.

Clearly, before Qu Qianxing even [called for backup](<# "Literal CN: 摇人 (yáo rén) — modern Northeast Chinese slang for summoning one's crew or calling for reinforcements">), Zhou Hui had already noticed something amiss and rushed down immediately.

High above, the little black lamb was still paddling its little hooves, desperately riding a black cloud down!

A wave of warmth flowed through Qu Qianxing's heart.

Regarding cultivation techniques, Qu Qianxing harbored none of that foolish pride about being "embarrassed to trouble Master." Reckless meddling only invited catastrophic problems.

Qu Qianxing promptly described his issue, seeking Zhou Hui's guidance.

—"Write down that technique and let me see,"-Zhou Hui said, furrowing his brows.

—"Yes, Master,"-Qu Qianxing replied. Producing a jade slip from his storage pouch, he pressed it against his forehead, instantly recording the circulation pathway, and handed it over.

Yet regarding this technique of unknown origin, he still worried Master might misunderstand:

—"I swear I have never practiced anything like this before—I have never even heard of it!"-Yet it was bafflingly familiar.

—"I know,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

[If he didn't practice it in this life, isn't that even worse?]

[Then this technique likely originates from—]

[The previous life?]

That was the only explanation.

Although Zhou Hui had wiped the memories of core figures like Qu Qianxing with every world reset—especially after that time of being "abandoned and betrayed by all," Zhou Hui had been meticulous about this—some traces could never be fully erased, particularly techniques that had been deeply practiced.

Just like learning to swim or ride a bicycle: even if neglected for years, toss someone into water or onto a bike, and the body's instincts took over.

Qu Qianxing was an honors student whose diligence rivaled Zhou Hui's own.

For someone like him, the body moved automatically without even needing conscious recall. Even if you [threw his soul into a washing machine for a few spin cycles](<# "Literal CN: 丢进洗衣机甩几遍 (diū jìn xǐ yī jī shuǎi jǐ biàn) — humorous modern analogy for aggressively scrubbing and spin-drying memories from a soul">), it wouldn't help!

The question was: where had Qu Qianxing learned this technique?

Zhou Hui had certainly never taught it. He was certain he hadn't taught it in several lifetimes; nor did he recall Qu Qianxing ever practicing such a technique in previous timelines.

Could it be the Heavenly Dao's fault again?

But the Heavenly Dao's script hadn't unfolded yet; that was future events. Even if calculated by the Heavenly Dao, in that script, Qu Qianxing was destined to be slain by the "Protagonist" when the Heavenly Spirit Sect fell! There was no mention of Qu Qianxing cultivating demonic arts.

—"Master?"-Qu Qianxing called out.

—"...Let me study it,"-Zhou Hui mused.

Looking at the mangled karmic fortune, he didn't force Qu Qianxing to keep absorbing it in this poor state:

—"Since you are in no mood for this, why not switch to another method?"

—"Master means...?"

—"I recall that according to Villa Master Qingyang's research paper, karmic fortune is a component of celestial energy, and it includes methods for purifying celestial force,"-Zhou Hui said thoughtfully.-"Why not try refining and extracting this karmic fortune to play around with? Turn in an academic report to me later."

—"Understood!"-Qu Qianxing's eyes lit up.

Truly worthy of Master—always discovering fresh angles of academic inquiry!

Compared to absorbing karmic fortune, Qu Qianxing had long wished to conduct deeper research on this force that had influenced his entire life.

Meanwhile, the little black lamb, who had just paddled its hooves down with great effort, heard the exchange and immediately curled into a tight ball, masquerading as a plump, round black cloud!

[What do you mean, "play around with"?!]

[Stop making technique research sound like some fun game, alright?! Not to mention writing a research paper!]

[Only Second Senior Brother would be happy about that!]

[If you ask me, Second Senior Brother is the least human of all Master's disciples!]

Yuwen Xuan's belly was bursting with complaints, but he curled himself into a tight little cloud, terrified Master would notice him and rope him in to "play around" and "turn in a report later."

Fortunately, Zhou Hui was in no mood to pay him attention.

After settling his second disciple, Zhou Hui pressed the jade slip against his glabella, instantly imprinting the technique into his mind.

Zhou Hui contemplated it carefully.

It was indeed a demonic art.

Its core mechanism relied on karmic fortune to supercharge cultivation speed. But because positive karmic fortune was difficult to gather, the "negative karmic fortune" required by this technique was far easier to procure: one "simply" needed to kill.

Just as when a sect was annihilated, a massive surge of karmic fortune was generated. Cultivating at such a time would skyrocket one's cultivation.

Naturally, such cultivation was not without steep costs: first, one would be led onto the Dao of Slaughter, relying on bloodshed to amass fortune; second, the cultivator would be eroded by negative emotions, descending completely into demonic madness.

After reviewing it, Zhou Hui grew even more perplexed.

Yet overall, this demonic technique was a relatively "orthodox" karmic fortune method.

It successfully piqued Zhou Hui's academic curiosity.

He was certain he had never seen a similar method before. In the cultivation world, karmic fortune techniques were the rarest category; even after centuries, Zhou Hui had collected very few. Even a demonic manual was an extraordinary rarity.

Naturally, Zhou Hui would never commit something like slaughtering an entire sect to harvest fortune.

Fortunately, this technique did not strictly mandate slaughter; high-stakes contests like the Immortal Arts Conference already accumulated sufficient karmic values.

No research was more thorough than hands-on experimentation.

Hesitating no longer, Zhou Hui closed his eyes and circulated his spiritual energy according to the manual.

Influenced by his vast, boundless spiritual energy, not only the skies above the tournament grounds, but the karmic fortune across half the Central Domain began to surge and churn—

---

Karmic fortune was churning.

Purplish-red, blackened, dirty clotted-blood karmic fortune congealed into the shape of clouds, churning in the sky, staining the sky above the Heavenly Spirit Sect into the likeness of a pool of blood.

But if the sky was a pool of blood, the interior of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was an outright purgatory.

Shattered architecture, slaughtered disciples, rivers of blood everywhere. And the cause of all this was the so-called "Child of Heavenly Destiny."

*Heh, "Child of Heavenly Destiny" indeed!*

*Heaven's Destiny favored a monstrous thing like that?!*

*Then such a "Heavenly Destiny" was better off discarded entirely!*

So Mo Xi thought, flying toward the Alchemy Hall.

Though not the epicenter of the slaughter, this place was the most tragic.

A scorched wasteland where barely a trace of the Alchemy Hall remained. Its master had been reduced to dust along with the hall's obliteration. And the blood-clotted karmic fortune spread outward from here—if the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect had become a sacrificial altar of filthy blood, this was the altar's very center.

A figure stood in the center of the charred, filthy ruins.

That figure was breathtakingly striking, like a blood-red peony blossoming from hell, condensing all tears and blood.

Mo Xi heard himself utter a cold sneer.

—"Second Senior Brother, you truly are still alive,"-he heard himself say, as though unable to restrain the rage in his heart.-"Then again, with Fifth Senior Brother's true dragon body as a sacrifice, with the disciples of the entire sect as a blood catalyst, and even tossing innocent Eldest Senior Sister out as bait, deceiving the Heavenly Dao must have been an easy task, wasn't it?"

Hearing his sneer, Qu Qianxing lifted his face.

Mo Xi instantly let out a gasp of dread, taking a frightened step backward.

For no other reason than Qu Qianxing's pitch-black eyes had turned even darker, like bottomless abysses.

Those were the eyes of a demon.

It added a terrifying veneer to Qu Qianxing's already gorgeous face, making him look like an ominous evil fiend walking the mortal world, capable of devouring all things.

Staring at the junior brother he had once doted on most with those dreadful eyes, Qu Qianxing spoke coldly:

—"Xi'er, you now have two choices."

[—"?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi">)

—"One choice is to take this cultivation manual and leave,"-Qu Qianxing said.

He lifted the object in his hands.

Only then did Mo Xi notice that Qu Qianxing was clutching a mirror. Because Qu Qianxing's appearance was too striking, that worn, ancient mirror had not drawn much attention.

Inside this mirror was a manual.

Qu Qianxing explained:

—"This is a cultivation manual. Master gave it to Eldest Senior Brother, telling him to pass it to you..."

—"You found Eldest Senior Brother?!"-Mo Xi asked in astonishment.

Qu Qianxing did not answer him, continuing in an icy tone:

—"That manual can harmonize an [Extreme Yin Body](<# "Literal CN: 极阴之体 (jí yīn zhī tǐ) — a rare, fragile constitution of pure yin energy, highly sought after as a cultivation furnace">). Once that is gone, you will be nothing more than an ordinary mortal. You will have no need to embroil yourself in this mess; find an ordinary place to live out an ordinary life."

—"Master..."-Mo Xi muttered blankly.

Glancing toward Master's immortal cave dwelling, a ferocious expression surfaced on his face.

—"And the other choice?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"Practice the same demonic art as me, until we overturn destiny,"-Qu Qianxing produced another manual.

—"Hahahahaha! Do I even have a choice?!"

Mo Xi laughed bitterly and shrilly.

Without the slightest hesitation, he reached his hand toward one of the manuals—

---

Mo Xi opened his eyes.

Blankly lifting unfocused eyes, he looked around. A flurry of chaotic voices buzzed in his ears:

[—"How are things at Cloud City? Has the Nascent Soul arena resumed?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"Things on Longmen Town's side are wrapping up already?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"Why so fast? Were the contestant numbers that low?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"The turnout at Fengqing City exceeded capacity! It's even higher than Cloud City!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

[—"A bunch of them are definitely outsiders posing as Central Domain locals!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

The clamor made Mo Xi's head buzz, but it brought the comforting warmth of the mortal world.

This was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's [Water Mirror Tower](<# "Literal CN: 水镜楼 (shuǐ jìng lóu) — Heavenly Spirit Sect facility dedicated to communications, live array broadcasts, and commercial operations">).

Usually desolate, but right now every water mirror was broadcasting live footage from the Immortal Arts Conference. Communication requests poured in from the arenas; Commercial Department disciples were running in circles—logging match statuses, relaying messages, and handling commercial advertising requests from external third parties.

Ah, right. Today was Day 1 of the conference. He had worked for months, even juggling orientation duties for the temporary Senior Sister from the Qingyang clan exploring secret realms. In the past week, he hadn't even found time to meditate. Once all arenas opened smoothly, he had sat down to supervise and slipped into sleep (or rather, passed out).

Mo Xi thought blankly.

Looking around, he suddenly burst into laughter: "Pfft!"

[Right! This is my Heavenly Spirit Sect! No slaughter, no ashes, only disciples bustling about.]

[Why did I have such a terrifying nightmare just now?]

[Heavenly Spirit Sect destroyed, Second Senior Brother falling to demonhood.]

[It even came with hidden lore: Eldest Senior Brother missing? Fifth Senior Brother sacrificed?]

[Those two most carefree, heartless senior brothers—as if that could ever happen!]

So Mo Xi admonished himself, yet for some reason, an anxious flutter persisted in his heart.

—"Might as well go check on Senior Brothers,"-Mo Xi muttered.

Gathering the small blanket someone had draped over him, he stood up and asked the crowd:

—"Nothing major, right? Then I'm stepping out for a bit..."

—"Ah, Junior Brother Mo, you're finally awake?"-a disciple hurried over, reporting anxiously.-"Something happened at Cloud City earlier; no matter how we tried, we couldn't wake you..."

—"What happened?"-Mo Xi yawned.

—"Just a Mahayana cultivator causing trouble, but the Untiring Immortal Venerable happened to be there and resolved it."

—"Then what is there to—wait, what did you say?! Master?! Master went to Cloud City to resolve an incident?!"

Mo Xi's eyes widened, whipping his head around to glare at the disciple.

—"N-no, he didn't go specifically for that? It seems he just happened to be there, along with Senior Brother Qu and the fairy from the Qingyang clan..."-the disciple attempted to explain.

Mo Xi pressed closer:

—"Master went to Cloud City, and took Second Senior Brother and the temporary Senior Sister along?!"

—"Y-yes?!"

—"Damn it! Such a massive affair! Why didn't you wake me up?! You could have slapped me awake!"-Mo Xi seethed, his face almost twisting in frustration.

The disciples exchanged glances.

They felt Junior Brother Mo's definition of "massive affair" and their definition of "a Mahayana powerhouse crashing the event" were two completely different things!

Seeing the "crisis" resolved, they hadn't wanted to disturb his rest.

After all, everyone saw how exhausted Junior Brother Mo had been.

Alas, Junior Brother Mo was not having it.

—"No, you all watch things here!"-Mo Xi decided after a moment's thought.-"I'm heading to Cloud City!"

—"B-but Cloud City has already been resolved..."-the disciple tried a third time.

But Mo Xi was already sprinting out the door, three times faster than when he had merely intended to stroll over and check on his senior brothers.

In the blink of an eye, he dashed out of the Water Mirror Tower.

Yet Mo Xi was a step too slow.

As he flew over the Alchemy Hall, he looked down instinctively.

Sure enough, unlike the ash-strewn ruins in his nightmare, the Alchemy Hall stood proud and intact. Fifth Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother happened to be standing by the gates; two breathtaking beauties making the hall entrance look as magnificent as an imperial palace courtyard...

[Second Senior Brother is here too?]

[Didn't they say Second Senior Brother went with Master to Cloud City?]

Hesitating as he looked at Qu Qianxing's back, Mo Xi flew down toward the Alchemy Hall.

Landing at the courtyard entrance, Mo Xi opened his mouth, but found himself hesitating to call out to Qu Qianxing. Those demonic eyes from the nightmare were too terrifying. Even seeing Fifth Senior Brother standing safely beside Qu Qianxing, Mo Xi still felt a lingering chill of fear.

Just then, a gleam of light reflecting from deeper inside the Alchemy Hall caught Mo Xi's eye.

Looking over instinctively, in the next instant, his blood seemed to freeze solid.

It was a mirror.

A mirror identical to the one Qu Qianxing had held in the dream.

But why would the mirror from the dream—held by the demonic Qu Qianxing—appear in "reality"?

[Is this really not just another nightmare?]

Mo Xi thought in terror. He didn't know why he felt such dread, as though a single glance at that mirror would shatter every beautiful thing in his life.

But the person holding the mirror noticed Mo Xi and stepped out from the Alchemy Hall:

—"Xi'er?"

The silver-haired immortal smiled warmly. The radiance surrounding him was as pure and immaculate as moonlight, shining upon the mirror until it looked like thin ice on the verge of melting away.

Suddenly, Mo Xi was no longer afraid.

As long as it was in that person's hands—be it mirrors, misfortune, or demonic arts—there was nothing to fear!

Putting on his most charming smile, Mo Xi dashed toward him, complaining in a sweet, coaxing voice:

—"Master, you are far too biased! You clearly could have taken me along to Cloud City as well—"
