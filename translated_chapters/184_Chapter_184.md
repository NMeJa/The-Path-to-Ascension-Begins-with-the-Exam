# Chapter 184: Karmic Ties

—"The demonic technique I practiced was given to me by Second Senior Brother,"-the other "Mo Xi" tattled.

Qu Qianxing had not anticipated in the slightest that while he was conducting his enjoyable (not) journey through the Demon Realm, not only had his docile Little Junior Brother produced an extra avatar, but said avatar was also perseveringly [giving his senior brothers eye drops](# "Literal CN: 给...上眼药 (gěi... shàng yǎnyào) — applying eye drops; Chinese slang meaning subtly badmouthing, smearing, or lodging petty complaints against someone before a superior to get them into trouble").

—"Back when the Heavenly Spirit Sect was annihilated,"-"Mo Xi" was naturally referring to events from his own reincarnation cycle,-"Second Senior Brother gave me two cultivation methods to choose from. One was left behind by you, Master—which is presumably the method that [innocently foolish](# "Literal CN: 单蠢 (dānchǔn) — a derogatory pun on 单纯 (dānchún, pure/innocent) replacing chún with 蠢 (stupid/foolish)") present me is practicing. The other was a demonic technique Second Senior Brother obtained from heaven knows where."

As he spoke, he drew a cultivation manual from his sleeve.

The volume was a seemingly ordinary thread-bound book titled [*Heart-Tailoring and Tongue-Carving Art*](# "Literal CN: 《裁心镂舌功》 (Cái Xīn Lòu Shé Gōng) — originates from a literary idiom describing painstaking craftsmanship of words and sentiments, here taking on a literal and sinister demonic meaning"). Among the cultivation world's flood of grandiloquent styles like *Grand Art of This* and *Precious Canon of That*, this title seemed quite refreshing, resembling an auxiliary internal art or secondary technique.

Its contents were not particularly complete either.

After all, this manual was not a physical object, but rather extracted from the memories of "Mo Xi," which were fragmentary to begin with.

In the sixth cycle, when "Mo Xi" and the protagonist wreaked havoc across the Southern Domain, his cultivation had only reached Early Nascent Soul. What he remembered most clearly was merely the portion up to Early Nascent Soul. As reading ahead risked qi deviation, he had deliberately avoided memorizing the subsequent stages of the demonic art.

Yet for a titan like Zhou Hui, this was more than enough.

Browsing through the manual's contents, Zhou Hui furrowed his brows.

This was actually a high-tier demon clan cultivation art. Based merely on the fragments "Mo Xi" remembered, it could already be deduced up to at least the Demon Commander level (equivalent to Soul Formation) or higher. For someone with slightly superior talent, cultivating it to Demon Sovereign (Mahayana level or above) was not out of the question. If someone of Zhou Hui's caliber were to extrapolate and complete it, it might even shatter the constraints of extraterrestrial heavenly demons to attain the status of a Demon God.

Naturally, such a high-tier demonic art—even as an incomplete fragment—should never have appeared in this minor world!

The demonic qi in this world was fundamentally incapable of sustaining a demonic art of this caliber.

—"Where did he obtain this?"-Zhou Hui muttered with a headache as he flipped through the manual.

—"I don't know, Second Senior Brother would never tell me such things,"-"Mo Xi" said in a [green tea tone](# "Literal CN: 茶茶的 (cháchá de) — behaving like a 'green tea bitch' (绿茶), acting sweet, innocent, and pure while maliciously scheming behind the scenes"), playing innocent while firmly resolving to drag even more senior brothers into the water.—"However, I suspect it has something to do with Eldest Senior Brother."

Zhou Hui's hand paused as he turned the pages.

"Mo Xi" knew full well that whenever any bad deed was pinned on Eldest Senior Brother, Master would believe eighty percent of it right off the bat.

Besides, he was not lying; he was making an honest report:

—"Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother are extremely suspicious, and so is Fifth Senior Brother!"-"Mo Xi" refused to spare a single senior brother, adopting an all-out posture of dragging everyone down into the water.—"They realized very early on that 'Master' was a counterfeit. Even my 'true memories' were awakened with their assistance. Yet they stubbornly insisted on treating that 'counterfeit' as the real Master, catering to every single whim of that creature! Especially Eldest Senior Brother—can you believe he would yield precious food ingredients to anyone other than you? Refusing to cook for that counterfeit seemed to be his final baseline!"

"..." It was indeed somewhat difficult to imagine, but wasn't this entire yardstick a bit bizarre?

Wait a moment, something wasn't right?

—"I thought he had already gone missing when the Heavenly Spirit Sect was wiped out?"-Zhou Hui asked with a frown.

After all (due to the trap Zhou Hui himself had laid), in every reincarnation cycle, Yuwen Xuan would be forced to revert to his original Taotie form and transmitted back to the Upper Realm to be devoured by his former self.

—"Master knows about Eldest Senior Brother's 'disappearance'?"-"Mo Xi" asked curiously, before explaining further:—"However, although Eldest Senior Brother vanished on the surface, various signs indicate that Second Senior Brother knew where he was, and they were colluding."

[—"Qianxing knew?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

"Mo Xi" nodded:

—"Indeed. After Eldest Senior Brother disappeared, the bills for his expenses continued to pile up. And as for his emergency food rations, Second Senior Brother claimed they had to be liquidated gradually to prevent chaos, but according to my observations, the rate of consumption was virtually identical to when Eldest Senior Brother was present. Occasionally, Second Senior Brother would even secretly restock the specific types Eldest Senior Brother loved most.

—"In contrast to the food reserves, our sect's treasury gained quite a few treasures of unknown origin, alongside massive expenditures and inflows whose purposes could not be explained. While Second Senior Brother used these funds to aggressively expand the sect, he also—for reasons unknown—funneled resources to that 'Master' whom he clearly knew to be a counterfeit, appearing exceedingly eager to see him ascend.

—"However, the most important detail is that a strange mirror appeared at Second Senior Brother's side.

—"Before the sect was annihilated, it wasn't as noticeable; he simply kept it in his room and rarely used it. But recently—ah, I mean in my cycle, after the sect was destroyed—Second Senior Brother carried that mirror everywhere. When he wasn't cultivating demonic arts in front of it, he was speaking to it. There were times I genuinely wondered if Second Senior Brother had lost his mind, but once, I heard Eldest Senior Brother's voice coming from within.

—"Therefore, I have ample reason to suspect that whether it was the demonic technique or Second Senior Brother's schemes, both were connected to Eldest Senior Brother."

Zhou Hui listened to "Mo Xi's" description with a look of phantom stomach ache on his face.

Among these accounts, some details aligned with the "Heavenly Dao script" he was familiar with.

For instance, the parts regarding Qu Qianxing expanding the sect. However, certain elements were entirely absent from the script—the script had no idea, for example, that the expansion of the Heavenly Spirit Sect actually involved Yuwen Xuan's assistance!

His Eldest and Second Disciples had actually worked in sincere collaboration to expand the sect and help "Master" ascend!

What a touching development that sounded like. Even if the process was somewhat crude and unfeeling, according to the script, they had built their master's sect into one of the largest factions in the Central Domain.

At the very least, back when Zhou Hui had assumed this cycle was merely the "script" or "the future intended by the Heavenly Dao," the pity and gratification in his heart had outweighed everything else:

[Even if the outcome was tragic, the children tried their best! If anything went wrong, it was surely the Heavenly Dao's fault, never the children's!]

Zhou Hui had once thought so.

Yet now, having listened to "Mo Xi's" comprehensive explanation, Zhou Hui only felt a phantom pain in his stomach.

[What in the world did this pack of unruly brats do?!]

[Especially those two leading troublemakers!]

[Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing may have been building up the sect, but they clearly harbored no good intentions whatsoever! They practically look as if they knew the entire 'script.' Worst of all, not only did they have no intention of derailing the script, they deliberately ran the plot forward—caring not in the slightest if the 'script' killed every junior brother and sister, even playing along with the script to offer sacrifices!]

[To go this far, what on earth were they plotting?!]

Zhou Hui clutched his stomach, lost in thought.

Yet once suspicion took root, numerous doubts that had previously been overlooked began to surface.

Zhou Hui suddenly recalled something:

Speaking of Yuwen Xuan and the "script," when one thought about it carefully, in one cycle (and according to Lu Shulong, more than one), Yuwen Xuan had recovered his memories of the prior reincarnations, hadn't he? In that case, he must have known there was a trap laid specifically for him, designed to force him back into his original form and transmit him to the Upper Realm. Why, then, across several cycles, were there just as many of Yuwen Xuan's skeletons buried before the threshold of the Blood Demon Palace? Having recalled his memories, did he never think to avoid it?

Don't be fooled by that lamb's goofy exterior—when it came to his own vital interests, he was shrewd beyond belief!

Zhou Hui regretted growing overly excited upon seeing Langhuan, rushing off to the Southern Domain with Mo Bei and Mo Xi. He really should have thoroughly interrogated his [dutiful eldest son](# "Literal CN: 好大儿 (hǎo dà ér) — 'good big son'; modern ironic slang for a troublesome or prodigal child whom one loves yet wants to strangle") first!

Fortunately, it wouldn't be too late to question him once he returned.

\*

At this very moment, Yuwen Xuan, who was still hanging from the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate, abruptly jolted awake from a sweet dream of savoring poached dragon eggs.

He looked around in apprehension, yet failed to find the source of his unease.

However, Yuwen Xuan was Yuwen Xuan—his ability to lock onto imaginary enemies across the void had always been uncannily precise:

[Could Master be missing me?]

[At a time like this?]

[Eighty percent chance that little snitch Mo Xi said something again!]

As Yuwen Xuan pondered what his junior brother might have tattled about to devise a countermeasure and dodge punishment, he also plotted how to retaliate against his beloved Little Junior Brother after the fact. At this moment, he failed to realize the sheer gravity of the situation, assuming it was merely an ordinary round of brothers 'lovingly tripping each other up.' Consequently, he squandered his chance to flee.

Naturally, that was a story for later.

\*

Returning to Zhou Hui's side, he was currently contemplating how to handle those two leading disciples of his:

Besides Yuwen Xuan, there was Qu Qianxing.

Unfortunately, Qu Qianxing had not recovered the memories of the previous cycles; even if questioned right now, he could provide no answers.

At this moment, for the very first time, Zhou Hui genuinely wished Qu Qianxing would recover his memories as well.

Yet upon careful reflection, he ultimately couldn't bear to do it.

It wasn't out of favoritism; Qu Qianxing's early life was simply too tragic. Experiencing it once was already cruel enough, but due to Zhou Hui's own mistake, Qu Qianxing had endured it a full eight times. Zhou Hui truly could not bring himself to make Qu Qianxing relive all of that.

Furthermore, considering Qu Qianxing's mental health, even if he were a saint, after repeating such a tragic first half of life eight times, wouldn't anyone turn twisted even if they weren't twisted to begin with?

The most troublesome part was that Qianxing was exceptionally stubborn—Guan Ye was the same in this regard. Clearly their circumstances were already so miserable, yet if Zhou Hui attempted to take them in as disciples early, they would refuse to leave their homeland or sect. So long as a sliver of hope remained, they would stubbornly strive to protect the place they once belonged to, with the inevitable result that they had to exhaust all despair before finally grasping the hand Zhou Hui reached out to them.

Viewed from this angle, Qianxing abandoning the Heavenly Spirit Sect and dutifully following the "script" must have had his own reasons; he had simply lost his memories and was unable to speak in his own defense.

At this point, Zhou Hui had virtually acquitted his Second Disciple unilaterally.

That was how top students were: the reservoir of trust they accumulated was enough to make even a mentor like Zhou Hui unable to resist taking their side.

Provided no evidence was left behind.

Evidence of Qu Qianxing "turning bad" was something that, even scouring this entire world, shouldn't exist... right?

Zhou Hui let out a sigh.

—"I understand. I will investigate once we return,"-Zhou Hui said to "Mo Xi," casually adding:—"However, since there is no concrete evidence, perhaps it has nothing to do with your Eldest Senior Brother?"

Even he lacked conviction when uttering those words.

That was how problem students were. While in theory one knew not to view them through biased lenses, when nine and a half out of ten misdeeds were undoubtedly committed by that wretched child, being 'fair' to him was actually being unfair to the rest.

Zhou Hui felt that if this continued, he would certainly become the first Tribulation Transcendence cultivator in history to suffer from a perforated stomach ulcer.

Fortunately, Mo Bei couldn't bear to listen any longer either, speaking up to ask:

—"Master, are we not there yet?"

Throughout the journey, she had quietly listened to "Mo Xi" lodge his complaints. Although her memories had "returned," in terms of the sheer volume of information in her mind, Mo Bei felt as though she and "Mo Xi" had lived through two entirely different cycles.

While she had faintly sensed that in the previous cycle her plight had been met with Eldest and Second Senior Brothers' tacit approval or even covert instigation, had they truly done so many other things besides that?

Mo Bei expressed that she did not know, nor did she wish to know.

—Knowing too clearly would inevitably spawn the urge to run a sword through both Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother.

Though she already felt like doing so right now.

Mo Bei let out a sigh as well:

—"We have already followed the earth veins for so long,"-she asked, attempting to change the subject.-"Where exactly are we going?"

—"You don't know?!"-"Mo Xi" exclaimed, looking at her in feigned shock.

"..." Mo Bei ignored him.

Whenever Master told her to move, she simply walked without ever asking questions; it wasn't as if this was the first day "Mo Xi" knew this.

Still, this habit truly wasn't great.

Precisely because she had never [sought to understand thoroughly](# "Literal CN: 不求甚解 (bù qiú shèn jiě) — not seeking deep comprehension; glossing over details without digging deeper"), she had ended up being swindled so wretchedly. While Mo Bei believed her [lovestruck brain](# "Literal CN: 恋爱脑 (liàn'ài nǎo) — 'love brain'; slang for someone whose infatuation overrides all reason and self-preservation") bore unavoidable responsibility for her tragic fate in the previous cycle, never asking or caring about anything was also a major flaw.

In the future, aside from Master's words, she would have to think through everything carefully.

Mo Bei reluctantly resolved to start growing a brain.

Seeing Mo Bei's somewhat gloomy expression, Zhou Hui spoke reassuringly:

—"We are nearly there,"-he said.-"It should be a place you will like."

—"Like?"-Mo Bei asked, lifting her face in surprise.

At this moment, a cool breeze brushed against their faces. In the sweltering Southern Domain, encountering such a refreshing breeze was exceedingly rare, particularly when it carried the familiar fragrance of grass tinged with the scent of sleet, a scent Mo Bei knew best of all.

Mo Bei recognized it instantly:

—"The scent of Snow Glow Grass?!"-Mo Bei blurted out.

—"Yes,"-Zhou Hui nodded slightly.

Mo Bei remained expressionless.

She truly had liked it—before coming to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree and encountering another 'Mo Xi.'

Rather, she had always harbored doubts about Master's nerves.

Having just experienced the "sea of blood-drenched Snow Glow Grass," the "rarely blooming flowers dyed in crimson gore," and the "countless versions of herself," Mo Bei had developed quite the psychological trauma toward Snow Glow Grass. How could she possibly still like it?

Then again, her master probably didn't understand the concept of "psychological trauma," did he?

After all, Master was someone who could watch Eldest Senior Brother (in human form) devour a ferocious ghost in one gulp and calmly praise him with a straight face: "Good boy!"

Mo Bei complained inwardly.

Her footsteps began to slow, feeling just like a child who had once adored instant ramen, eaten it by themselves for four years in college until they were ready to vomit, and finally returned home only to find their loving mother had specially brewed a bowl of instant ramen to "reward" them.

Thankfully, the aroma of Snow Glow Grass smelled far better than instant noodles (though Mo Bei had never actually eaten instant noodles either).

Strictly speaking, Snow Glow Grass possessed no overpowering fragrance of its own; only when vast expanses grew together, mingling with frigid moisture, would it release a faint grassy scent carrying the crisp essence of winter frost and snow. Mo Bei was only so sensitive to this scent because she had watered her own Snow Glow Grass year in and year out.

Yet having endured such a brutal "breakthrough ritual," Mo Bei half-suspected this delicate scent would transform the very next second into a pungent reek of blood, complete with phantasms of several lovestruck versions of herself glaring back at her—

"!"

Mo Bei's eyes suddenly widened.

A boundless sea of Snow Glow Grass abruptly surged into her vision—shades deep and shallow, kaleidoscopic and resplendent... Yes, there was red as well, but even the crimson carried the texture of a translucent watercolor painting. Luminous and clear, like a dream bathed in winter sunlight, warm and gentle, it instantly brought peace to the heart.

At least for Mo Bei, whose mind had been battered by agonizing memories of regret, it felt like a soothing brain massage.

She stared in a daze at the sight, forgetting her distaste from moments ago. She didn't even notice the transformed humanoid figure of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree stepping out from the earth veins beside them:

[—"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable, you have arrived?"](# "Speaker: Fire Abyss Sacred Tree")

As the guardian sacred tree of the Southern Domain, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's root system spanned the entirety of the Southern Domain. Reaching even the southernmost expanse at the edge of the domain was merely a matter of a single thought.

Zhou Hui, by contrast, was a step slower.

—"Ah, following the earth veins made it a bit tricky to find,"-Zhou Hui said, looking at the sea of Snow Glow Grass with slight amazement.-"Still, I truly didn't expect this grassy sea to be so vast!"

Compared to this, Mo Bei's little mountain peak was hardly worth mentioning; this was a veritable "ocean of grass."

—"Master, have you never been here before?"-Mo Bei asked in surprise.

She had assumed this world held no secrets from Master!

—"I merely heard the Mei-Zhu couple mention it and examined the Snow Glow Grass they cultivated. Because the grass itself had nothing extraordinary about it, I assumed it was merely a specialized weed produced by a unique microclimate, and thus bypassed this place during my travels,"-Zhou Hui shook his head, his jade-fair cheeks flushing slightly with embarrassment.-"Indeed, I was careless after all! No matter how inconspicuous a thing might be, the cause behind its existence is never simple, and it can form breathtaking scenery."

There really was no need for that.

Mo Bei felt that as a several-thousand-year-old [single dog](# "Literal CN: 单身狗 (dānshēn gǒu) — single dog; Chinese internet slang for someone who is chronically single"), Master had surely been slapped in the face with a mountain of [dog food](# "Literal CN: 狗粮 (gǒuliáng) — dog food; slang for sickening public displays of affection by couples") by the Mei-Zhu couple, which was why he had avoided visiting such a textbook romance check-in spot.

She wracked her brain, at a loss for how to console Master, only to hear him sigh:

—"However, had I visited earlier,"-Zhou Hui said,-"I could have learned the origin of you and Xi'er much sooner, and spared you a lifetime of suffering."

—"Eh?"-Mo Bei froze in astonishment.

Unfortunately, Zhou Hui was frowning as he gazed at the sea of flowers, immersed in self-reproach, leaving him no time for a detailed explanation.

Hearing this, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree took a closer look at the twins who possessed souls born of half-yin spirits:

—"I see,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree murmured.-"Were their souls and physical bodies shaped from the yin spirits here?"

Mo Bei turned to look at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

Although Mo Bei's expression was near-blank, the Sacred Tree differed from humanity; it did not communicate by reading facial expressions. Possessing an entirely different sensory system for the emotions of living beings, it immediately understood Mo Bei's bewilderment.

The Sacred Tree stepped in on Zhou Hui's behalf:

—"Back when this land fell from the Heavenly Court, there were naturally heavy casualties. In truth, aside from old fossils like me, scarcely one in ten living beings survived. Their corpses... or in human terms, immortal remains? Those immortal remains still possessed immense power that could wreak havoc upon the mortal world. The people of that era cast spells to seal—or rather, freeze—them here, hoping to use the power of vegetation to cause them to fade away. That is also the origin of this sea of grass. However, while the immortal remains were sealed, the resentment of their departed souls could not dissipate so easily, coalescing here into persistent yin spirits that even this old man was unwilling to approach. I simply never imagined that humans would be so audacious as to sneak here and steal yin spirits..."-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree explained.

Hearing up to this point, Mo Bei understood.

She crossed her arms over her chest, hugging her supposedly supreme Extreme Yin Constitution.

—"That's right. This place is the source of our souls and bodies, so it could even be counted as our 'parents,'"-"Mo Xi" looked at Mo Bei, the malicious grin on his face widening.-"Surprise! The grass you love most isn't just the dry straw used to pack mannequins, but the veritable [graveyard grass](# "Literal CN: 坟头草 (féntóu cǎo) — grass growing on a grave; dark slang for the dead") of immortals. Your taste is truly impeccable."
