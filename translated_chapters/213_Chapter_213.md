# Chapter 213: Heart Demon

—"Hm? He rejected it?"

Zhou Hui murmured in mild bewilderment.

Ever since the mountain grand array reestablished a smooth "signal" with Qu Qianxing, Zhou Hui had naturally been able to indirectly gauge his second disciple's condition through the flow of array energy. Consequently, he had detected Qu Qianxing's refusal of the spiritual feed at the very first instant.

This left Zhou Hui profoundly perplexed.

—"Could it be he feels his own reserves are sufficient? Even so, hoarding it for a rainy day is always prudent!"

—"Master, you sound precisely like an overprotective mother agonizing that her traveling child doesn't have enough pocket money, wishing she could pack up the entire family estate for him to carry,"-a sour, petulant voice piped up from behind Zhou Hui,-"However, you're worrying far too much! Old Second is sly as a ghost and never overestimates his abilities. If he refused the power, it merely proves he has zero need for it. He'd never suffer a loss anywhere under the sun!"

—"...Have you finished configuring the formation array I assigned you?"

Zhou Hui did not turn around, merely inquiring coolly.

Instantly, a heavy *duang* of a deadweight collapsing to the floor echoed behind him, followed by dead silence.

Hovering between exasperation and amusement, Zhou Hui turned his head, only to see his Eldest Disciple assumed in the form of a black sheep, foaming at the mouth with all four cloven hooves pointed straight toward the rafters—wearing the insolent expression of *'If you make me study again, I will drop dead of the pestilence right this second.'*

*Is he nearing his limit already?*

Zhou Hui thought helplessly.

Reality proved that an incorrigible brat remained thoroughly incorrigible no matter how much force was applied. Originally, Zhou Hui had merely noticed that chaotic as Yuwen Xuan's brain was, it appeared capable of unconsciously absorbing data and autonomously reorganizing it into viable answers, prompting Zhou Hui to experiment whether this specimen possessed the operational characteristics of an 'antimatter computer.'

In the beginning, the experiment proceeded smoothly; Yuwen Xuan's brain appeared capable of converting forcibly injected knowledge into answers without going through orthodox calculation pathways. Yet by the third iteration, Yuwen Xuan's subconscious seemed to have sniffed out Zhou Hui's pedagogical designs; after that, no matter what data was injected, nothing registered. If pressed further, it produced wildly absurd outputs.

For instance, morphing from a black sheep into an indescribable clump of dark, fuzzball sludge.

This disciple was already sufficiently detached from humanity; there was no need to pressure him to mutate in bizarre directions.

*Yet a primordial beast's cognitive architecture is so remarkably sensitive and eccentric—it genuinely makes one want to pursue the research further!*

Zhou Hui pondered with conflicted fascination, falling into uncharacteristic indecision.

Meanwhile, after being stared down for too long, Yuwen Xuan found it difficult to maintain the charade, his little hooves twitching faintly.

Cracking a slit of an eye to peek upward, he found Master examining him with a deeply unsettling gaze—resembling a chef scrutinizing an oyster that needed to be soaked in brine for days to purge its sand before being tossed into the cooking wok.

The little black sheep shuddered violently. Daring to play dead no longer, it scrambled upright in a single flip, tucking its little tummy out of sight.

—Whatever was stored inside this little belly, it held zero desire to vomit it back out!

Not a single crumb!

Thick-skinned to the core, Yuwen Xuan instantly pivoted the topic, adopting a posture of righteous solemnity:

—"Regardless, poor Second Junior Brother is trapped amidst demon hordes without accepting external power. He does seem rather pitiful,"-he smoothly resumed their earlier conversation, as though playing dead on the floor had never occurred.

Zhou Hui saw right through his little schemes, yet being reminded of Qu Qianxing, genuine concern resurfaced:

—"For Qianxing, whether warring against demon troops or defying celestial hosts, it is somewhat premature,"-Zhou Hui sighed,-"I feel he ought to have elevated his cultivation further before stepping into such a storm."

*Elevate his cultivation further so he can directly upend the Heavenly Court?*

Yuwen Xuan scoffed inwardly.

Leaving aside whether he possessed any fraternal affection—truthfully, Yuwen Xuan had never believed he possessed such sentiment; who on earth developed familial bonds with potential rations?—in his assessment, among all his junior brothers and sisters, Qu Qianxing was the single existence least worth worrying over.

After all, that fellow was cunning beyond measure. Back then, he had clearly promised never to compete for Master's favor, yet somehow ended up seamlessly installed as Second Senior Brother. It was this very bastard who had set a terrible precedent, paving the way for an entire parade of delectable yet completely inedible junior siblings trailing behind!

Furthermore, having "recovered" the memories of previous cycles from his true body, he knew Qu Qianxing hadn't merely deceived him once—he had swindled him in every single reincarnation! He was rotten to the core!

*Nothing good ever comes out of the Heavenly Court!*

Yuwen Xuan thought with petty indignation. Rolling his beady black eyes, he wheedled ingratiatingly:

—"Since that's the case, shouldn't we hurry to bring Second Junior Brother back?"

—"Indeed,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

The little black sheep's spirits soared instantly. Tugging at the hem of Zhou Hui's robes with its cloven hooves, it bleated eagerly:

—"Master! Master! Let me go!"

—"You?"

—"Yes! Because everyone else is swamped with work!"-Yuwen Xuan pleaded with absolute earnestness,-"Particularly Shulong—isn't he competing in the Immortal Arts Assembly? If we wait until the assembly concludes before letting him deliver the Heavenly Robe to Second Junior Brother, Second Junior Brother will already be cold in the ground!"

—"Can you speak a single benevolent word?" Besides, the Immortal Arts Assembly wasn't truly that paramount, was it? The primary reason was that Lu Shulong concealed memories of multiple cycles, shielded by a True Dragon's racial barrier; even Zhou Hui couldn't ascertain what heights Shulong had reached across those cycles. Seeing that the fellow was perpetually slacking off, Zhou Hui had commanded him to enter the assembly to see what potential could be squeezed out.

Yet compared to Qianxing's survival, the tournament was naturally inconsequential.

In truth, Zhou Hui planned that once he finished stabilizing the mountain grand array and confirmed his second disciple was safe for the moment, he would summon his fifth disciple, dispatching Lu Shulong with the Heavenly Robe to extract Qu Qianxing.

Yuwen Xuan naturally deduced Master's thoughts.

Without waiting for Master to speak, he rattled off in haste:

—"Little Sixth is in the exact same boat, competing in the assembly! Old Third and Old Fourth are orchestrating event logistics, while Little Seventh is managing the broadcast feeds at the Fire Abyss Divine Tree. In short, before the assembly concludes, nobody has a free moment! Even Master—setting aside researching the array disc, you must oversee the Heavenly Spirit Sect during the assembly! So many titans have converged upon our mountain; if they fail to catch a glimpse of Master, everyone will be terribly heartbroken!"

—"Therefore, isn't it obvious that only I have free time?"-Yuwen Xuan concluded triumphantly.

He pretended he hadn't been lounging around Master's side for leisure, but rather serving his penance for swallowing unidentified objects by studying predictive array mechanics under Master's tutelage.

Using his junior brother's rescue as an excuse to dodge homework was his true, magnificent masterplan.

Yuwen Xuan would rather charge into an active warzone than do another math problem!

Alas, a disciple's devious intentions were virtually transparent before their Master—especially such blatant, undisguised schemes.

Zhou Hui naturally did not grant Yuwen Xuan's wish, asking abruptly:

—"You abandoned my formation homework solely to display concern for your junior brother?"

*Why does the topic always loop back to this cursed homework?!*

Yuwen Xuan's expression froze—though it was hard to tell on a sheep's face. Pausing for a heartbeat, he muttered aggrievedly:

—"Naturally not. Someone has petitioned for an audience with Master."

Saying this, he shook his thick black fleece, and a bronze mirror popped out from his curls, landing squarely in Zhou Hui's palm.

—"Why didn't you mention this earlier?"-Zhou Hui scolded lightly.

Looking into the mirror, he was met with a face resembling withered tree bark. Zhou Hui offered an apology:

—"My apologies, Ancestor Jiuzhai. The child in my household is simply too undisciplined—"

—"Forget that! What on earth did you do this time?!"-Ancestor Jiuzhai had zero bandwidth to pursue petty etiquette, barking an interrogation at Zhou Hui,-"Why has karmic luck fluctuated so bizarrely again?!"

—"Karmic luck?"-Zhou Hui blinked in surprise.

A similar interrogation had occurred not long ago, back when Zhou Hui resolved to ally with the demon race and implement compulsory education for beast tribes; Ancestor Jiuzhai had practically scrambled to the Central Domain in a panic to cross-examine him.

Zhou Hui reviewed his recent actions:

—"I integrated a trial system into the Immortal Arts Assembly, though I hardly believe that could generate significant karmic luck."

—"Hmph! You think so lightly of it! Where would so much excess karmic luck even come from?!"-Ancestor Jiuzhai rubbed his temples in frustration,-"Furthermore, this is not an anomaly confined to your Central Domain; the karmic luck of the entire world is violently seesawing right now, impossible to forecast!"

—"That profound?"-Zhou Hui furrowed his brows.

He recalled that Ruwang had also mentioned sensing an omen of 'great calamity.'

Yet the qualification exams had concluded smoothly, Guan Ye had successfully manifested his Dao Bone, and ultimately no 'catastrophe' had transpired—aside from Yuwen Xuan scavenging unknown junk to eat. While he couldn't vomit it back up, he didn't appear to suffer indigestion, leading Zhou Hui and Ruwang to dismiss it as minor.

Now it seemed... was that 'calamity' related to the karmic turbulence Jiuzhai described?

Ancestor Jiuzhai was convinced that whether fortune or calamity, it was inextricably linked to Zhou Hui:

—"What did you just say about a trial system? Explain it to me in detail!"

—"You actually should have encountered it at the Fire Abyss Divine Tree; it is merely an adaptation of the 'Academic Guidance System,'"-Zhou Hui explained patiently,-"In essence, it extracts the latent potential of contestants to calculate their future trajectories..."

He was remarkably patient, breaking down the mechanics to Jiuzhai in meticulous detail.

As the two titans engaged in philosophical debate over cosmic causality, a certain troublemaker was temporarily forgotten.

The black sheep flattened its belly against the floor like a mop, slowly, silently sliding toward the doorway.

*The situation is turning sour!*

*Little Sixth's secret scheme is about to be exposed!*

Yuwen Xuan schemed inwardly.

He was perpetually the single person—or rather, the single beast—who possessed complete visibility over all secrets within the Heavenly Spirit Sect. From the exact tally of Master's private treasury down to how many mouse holes riddled the mountain gates, he knew everything! Naturally, he had watched the current 'entertainment' unfold from inception to execution. Though spurned by his junior siblings, Yuwen Xuan understood every detail of the saga.

He knew Master had only commissioned a standard 'trial system' to stimulate the potential of athletes and spectators, encouraging frequent utilization of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's 'operating system' to siphon spiritual energy into the central sundial clock, thereby accumulating causality to establish contact with Qu Qianxing.

However, driven by misunderstandings and dread of the future, the junior siblings sought to familiarize themselves with the power of their "darkened future personas," sneaking a dungeon boss system into the simulation to temper themselves.

Yuwen Xuan knew Master would never be pleased to see this.

Setting aside whether that future was authentic, Master would never tolerate seeing his precious disciples degenerate into such grotesque abominations. If Master uncovered Mo Bei's rogue modifications, his displeasure would be palpable.

Remarkably, Yuwen Xuan had not participated in this conspiracy.

Primarily because regarding that final fat Taotie gorging itself in the void, the junior siblings hadn't recognized it as their Eldest Senior Brother, assuming that doomed realm was ultimately annihilated by an unknown cosmic horror—and if Master was no longer around, the disciples didn't particularly mind such an apocalypse.

Consequently, even if Master caught wind of the disciples' rogue project and meted out discipline, it had zero connection to Yuwen Xuan.

—"It feels rather unnatural..."-Yuwen Xuan muttered.

Naturally, no matter how shameless he was, he wouldn't volunteer to share punishment with his siblings.

Yet before Master 'corrected' the errors within the trial system, Yuwen Xuan had loose ends of his own to clean up.

—Indeed: while Yuwen Xuan hadn't participated in the scheme, that didn't mean he hadn't co-opted the project to set up his own hunting snares! Meat tenderized through repeated beatings yielded the finest flavor, after all! Not to mention the natural feed provided:

The siblings hadn't realized that while their darkened personas were undeniably powerful, that strength did not merely stem from forsaking their lives. Whether bone dragons, immortal remains, demonic corruption, or ghostly wraiths, these forms radiated intense corruptive miasma. Cultivators possessing unstable mental states who crossed paths with them would be genuinely infected, dragged straight into the abyss!

The siblings might fail to notice, but Master certainly wouldn't.

Once Master realized what the disciples had done, he would likely audit the competitors' spiritual conditions—which would spell trouble for Yuwen Xuan's private pantry.

*I originally planned to marinate it longer, but I'd better reel in the net before Master discovers it!*

Yuwen Xuan calculated.

Concealing his presence, he slithered past the threshold like a mop while the two grand masters were engaged in debate. The instant he cleared the doors, he kicked his cloven hooves into high gear, darting toward his personal quarters. Digging through a messy pile of personal belongings, he retrieved a dazzling, multi-faceted gemstone.

Rotating the gemstone, every polished facet mirrored the projection of a distinct living entity: ordinary wild beasts, penned spirit livestock, exotic monsters guarding immortal herbs, primordial beasts matching his lineage, and even demons, spirits, fiends, and True Immortals.

These were the 'prey' Yuwen Xuan had monitored over aeons.

Rotating the gem to a specific facet, a savage, contorted male face materialized upon the crystal.

It was an 'old acquaintance.'

Yan Bailang, Sect Master of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall adjacent to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

While the neighbor's chicken had been snatched and devoured long ago—with the prized drop of True Immortal blood gifted to Old Second!—the remaining chicken broth was still worth savoring!

Yuwen Xuan had always been an exceptionally frugal Taotie.

***

Yan Bailang was struck by a violent surge of palpitations.

As though bitten on the backside by a venomous serpent, he sprang up from his meditation mat, glaring with bloodshot eyes as he swept the perimeter with frantic vigilance.

The physical chamber he occupied was extraordinarily secure.

For Yan Bailang was currently quartered inside an 'Executive VIP Suite' for athletes of the Immortal Arts Assembly.

This was by no means special favoritism granted by the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but rather because competitors of the upper three realms could whip up storms with their breathing alone. Furthermore, figures like Yan Bailang brought dangerous combat beasts; housing them alongside lower-tier contestants would invite disaster.

Fortunately, competitors of the upper three realms were scarce, leaving ample space for lavish accommodations.

*(Decades later, when even paragons of the upper three realms took immense pride in entering the Immortal Arts Assembly, providing accommodations for them became the ultimate nightmare of tournament organizers.)*

During this maiden assembly, such bottlenecks were nonexistent; thus, living quarters for upper-three-realm athletes were situated at the highest pinnacle of the Athletes' Village.

Colossal floral vines penetrated the cloud sea, ascending into the sky to bloom with radiant blossoms, each flower serving as an executive suite for an upper-three-realm competitor.

These blossoms were naturally the size of Ascension Platforms, and Zhou Hui had woven spatial expansions upon them, rendering their interior volume far vaster than appeared from without—spacious enough for an elder of a grand sect to assemble all his disciples for street dancing without feeling cramped.

Such expansive dimensions allowed upper-three-realm cultivators to unleash their arts at will, not to mention the multi-layered defensive formations isolating the rooms, ensuring internal spiritual fluctuations never leaked outward, sparing practitioners from worrying about exposing their signature techniques prematurely.

The upper-three-realm competitors praised these accommodations lavishly, with several thick-skinned masters even inquiring whether they could lease the suites permanently as private immortal caves after the tournament.

Naturally, having engineered accommodations to this tier, the Heavenly Spirit Sect strictly forbade competitors from coming to harm outside official matches—and that included the sect's Eldest Disciple, Yuwen Xuan, who would dutifully devour any intruder threatening the competitors, even if that intruder was himself.

Yet even under such safeguards, certain paranoid neurotics remained chronically anxious.

Particularly Yan Bailang, Sect Master of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall.

Not only had he dragged his feet until the absolute final hour before arriving with his disciples, but upon checking in, he had disregarded the robust defensive arrays of the suite, deploying three inner and three outer layers of personal traps and warding arrays, releasing swarms of ferocious beasts to stand guard. Consequently, the cavernous suite had been transformed into a suffocating labyrinth, with his personal living space constricted to the immediate radius of a single prayer mat.

The male disciples forced to attend him suffered immense misery.

A luxurious sanctuary had been converted into a foul beast pen littered with lethal traps. The disciples were only informed of the safe corridors within their assigned operational sectors; a single careless step meant either tripping a deadly mechanism or being mauled by guard beasts, rendering their existence no different from convicts in a maximum-security prison.

Yet compared to horrific living conditions, what terrified the disciples far more was their Sect Master himself.

For instance, the two disciples currently standing guard outside Yan Bailang's chamber:

Hearing the rustle of Yan Bailang leaping to his feet, their bodies trembled violently, wearing expressions of dread yet zero surprise, standing rigid as statues without daring to exchange a glance.

In this suffocating tension, they clearly heard Yan Bailang pacing back and forth, the hum of repeated array diagnostic checks, and the sweeping flare of spiritual senses scanning the room. Every rustle stretched the disciples' nerves to the snapping point, terrified the Sect Master would spot an imperfection and vent his wrath upon them—the previous watch had their eyes slashed open by the Sect Master merely because the entrance mat was shifted by a finger's width (Yan Bailang insisted).

Fortunately, this time, Yan Bailang appeared to find nothing amiss.

After rustling about for what felt like an eternity without discovering any discrepancy, he slowly quieted down, allowing the two disciples to exhale secretly in relief.

Yet before their breath settled, the Sect Master's sinister, raspy voice hissed through the door:

—"You two standing guard outside—did you not detect anything amiss?"

—"...N-No,"-the two disciples froze, gathering their courage to reply,-"Reporting to the Sect Master, we have maintained vigilant watch throughout; there has been no anomaly—!!"

—"No anomaly?! How could there be no anomaly?!"

A suffocating wave of spiritual pressure crashed down upon their skulls!

Although Yan Bailang's personal combat prowess ranked among the lower tier of Body Integration cultivators—the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall relied on beast pets to fight—he was nonetheless a genuine Body Integration titan. His raw spiritual aura packed devastating lethality against low-tier practitioners.

The two disciples were slammed flat against the floor, spitting mouthfuls of blood as sensation drained from their limbs. Only their Sect Master's manic howling buzzed above their heads:

—"I felt it! How could there be nothing?!"

—"I know it! Something is watching me! Something wants to kill me!"

—"I know you are lurking there! Stop hiding! If you have the guts, face me in a duel!"

Yan Bailang shrieked hysterically, kicking aside a mountain of furniture.

Listening to his deranged bellows, the disciples collapsed upon the floor thought through the haze:

[The sect rumors... that the Sect Master has suffered cultivation deviation... it appears to be entirely true...]

[...Even if it isn't deviation, he has developed a Heart Demon.]

[With our Ten Thousand Beasts Hall governed by such a madman, we are truly finished...]

Drifting through fragmented thoughts, they ultimately lost consciousness.

When even low-tier outer disciples recognized the truth, "Sect Master Yan has suffered cultivation deviation" was virtually an open secret within the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall—it was merely that nobody dared voice it aloud.

Only Yan Bailang remained oblivious.

No—he knew. He simply refused to confront it.

While Yan Bailang screamed frantically, seeking invisible enemies, a chaotic shadow loomed behind his back: a grotesque chimera stitched together from the corpses and wraiths of countless spirit beasts, flayed hides, oozing entrails, dripping gore, mingled with the rancorous faces and severed limbs of human disciples. They were the crystallization of beast souls reared inside the Beast Soul Tower and the disciples murdered within its trials—the manifestation of Yan Bailang's Heart Demon.

And naturally, the face of Yu Si—the senior sister Yan Bailang had murdered two hundred years ago to usurp the Sect Master seat and steal the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*—floated prominently within this writhing shadow.

She watched Yan Bailang's psychotic tantrum with cold detachment, uttering not a single syllable, as though observing a tedious farce.

Suddenly, the woman seemed to sense something, lifting her head toward the ceiling to reveal a dazed, radiant smile:

—"Senior Brother Yuwen, you have arrived?"

—"Don't call me Senior Brother. I don't belong to your Ten Thousand Beasts Hall; I am of the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-an impatient voice replied.

It was none other than Yuwen Xuan's voice.

The woman smiled sweetly:

—"Senior Brother Yuwen is far too modest. Had you not lent a hand back then, I could never have survived the Beast Soul Tower, could never have ascended to Sect Master, and could never have created the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*. It was merely that I was too useless, failing to defend my seat, allowing this fool to steal everything away..."

—"I told you, that is your sect's private business. I didn't help her back then out of benevolence; I merely wanted to sneak into the Beast Soul Tower to scavenge for snacks. Whatever she achieved was through her own competence,"-Yuwen Xuan dismissed coolly,-"Setting that aside, even if I allowed her to call me Senior Brother, you are not her—you are merely the Heart Demon born from her murderer."

The Heart Demon did not deny it, giggling softly.

—"Therefore, how long do you intend to stall? Why haven't you devoured him already?"-Yuwen Xuan urged,-"So I can hurry up and devour you."

—"Didn't Your Excellency previously state there was no rush?"-the Heart Demon asked curiously.

Naturally, that was because he had wanted Yan Bailang to undergo several rounds of trials, elevating his strength so his Heart Demon would fatten up and taste far more delectable.

However, if Master audited the trial system, he might notice Yan Bailang's condition. What if Master discovered he was secretly fattening up a Heart Demon to eat?!

Yuwen Xuan naturally wouldn't confess his anxieties before his prey, stating simply:

—"I am hungry."

He paused, feeling that urging his food to fatten itself up so he could eat it sounded somewhat ridiculous. Yet Yuwen Xuan never engaged in self-reflection, asking the Heart Demon suspiciously:

—"You haven't had second thoughts, have you? You're not planning to flee?"

—"How could I? I have always been deeply grateful to Senior Brother Yuwen,"-the Heart Demon gasped,-"Besides, as a Heart Demon, I require a host to exist. Even if I wished to flee, where could I possibly run?"

—"Hard to say. If separation were impossible, the primordial Extraterrestrial Fiends would never have come to be,"-Yuwen Xuan sneered.

—"You even know of that?"-the Heart Demon was genuinely astonished.

Yet she laughed once more:

—"However, even if that were possible, separating requires both host and demon to command terrifying power. As for this trash... forget it!" She cast a disdainful glare toward Yan Bailang.

Fearing Yuwen Xuan remained unconvinced, the Heart Demon added:

—"Furthermore, life in the Heart Demon Realm isn't easy these days. Many sisters have retreated from the 'hunting grounds,' complaining it's far too difficult."

—"Difficult?"

—"Indeed. It seems 'examinations' have suddenly become trendy among the demons in the Demon Domain, looking remarkably similar to your Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-the Heart Demon remarked. Rummaging through the writhing shadows beneath her form, she actually fished out an exam scroll, handing it to Yuwen Xuan.

Laying eyes upon that object, Yuwen Xuan felt his vision go completely dark.

Yet he examined it closely, sniffing its scent:

—"Ah... Qu Qianxing!"-Yuwen Xuan identified the culprit instantly.

[Isn't that guy on the frontlines fighting a war?!]

[What on earth did he do to the demons?!]

[At this rate, even demons will be tormented into sprouting heart demons!]

[Wait a second... Can one eat the heart demon of a heart demon?!]

Yuwen Xuan pondered with absolute seriousness.
