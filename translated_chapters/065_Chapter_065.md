# Chapter 65: Soul of the Taotie (第65章 饕餮之魂)

—"W-we've been living right next to the mouth of such a thing?!"-Shen Qingqian asked in a daze.

Even with Shen Qingqian's notoriously thick nerves, she could not accept this reality.

In truth, the lingering terror churning in her gut ran even deeper than that of the Blood Demon Hall disciples:

Back when Shen Qingqian was still at the Golden Core stage, she had set out with fellow cultivators to explore the ruins of the God-Demon War along the edges of the Abyss Sea. They were swept up by a ferocious spiritual storm and blown all the way down, whereupon she stumbled across this hidden spatial rift purely by chance.

At the time, having lost all her teammates, Shen Qingqian didn't dare venture out recklessly. Discovering that this spatial rift was exceptionally suited to her cultivation, she simply settled down in the void pocket and immersed herself in seclusion. She cultivated here for a full one thousand five hundred years, even inventing her own unique cultivation technique, finally breaking through to the [Spirit Severing stage](# "Literal CN: 化神期 (Huàshén-qī) — Soul Formation / Spirit Severing stage") before she was at last able to leave.

This harrowing ordeal was practically enough to pen a cultivation world edition of [*Robinson Shen-soe*](# "Literal CN: 《沈滨逊漂流记》 (Shěn bīn xùn piāoliú jì) — parody of 《鲁滨逊漂流记》 (Robinson Crusoe), substituting Shen Qingqian's surname Shen for Lu").

Yet the tale had not ended with Robinson Shen... with Shen Qingqian returning to the mortal world. Back then, upon returning to her original sect, Shen Qingqian had enjoyed several years of leisurely days with the esteemed status of a Spirit Severing elder. Then, a once-in-a-century massive beast tide descended.

Her original sect was merely a modest, minor sect, naturally [unable to hold up the sky alone](# "Literal CN: 独木难支 (dúmù-nánzhī) — a single log cannot support a tottering building; unable to sustain the burden alone"). Thus, they banded together with several neighboring small sects in the same dire predicament to establish the [Peerless Alliance](# "Literal CN: 无双盟 (Wúshuāng Méng) — Wushuang Alliance / Peerless Alliance") to jointly weather the beast tide.

In the end, once the beast tide passed, the Sect Master of Shen Qingqian's sect—who was also Shen Qingqian's senior sister—hooked up with the Alliance Leader of the Peerless Alliance. Normally, this wouldn't have been an issue; if two people found each other pleasing to the eye, becoming Daoist companions was a fine arrangement. However, the Alliance Leader actually sought to exploit this opportunity to annex all the other minor sects and turn the Peerless Alliance into a permanent hegemony. Worse still, Shen Qingqian's senior sister [meekly obeyed her partner's every whim](# "Literal CN: 夫唱妇随 (fūchàng-fùsuí) — the husband sings and the wife chimes in; traditional submissive spousal obedience"). Not only did she consent to unequal coalition treaties, but she even attempted to marry off the female elders and managers of her own sect to the Alliance Leader's subordinate disciples to transfer authority. This included Shen Qingqian herself. Shen Qingqian was so utterly enraged she nearly fell over backwards.

As she put it: she never imagined that after cultivating all the way to immortalhood, she would still be treated as [dowry goods](# "Literal CN: 嫁妆 (jiàzhuang) — bridal dowry; bartered off as matrimonial property")!

Shen Qingqian led a group of equally defiant disciples and broke away, only to face relentless harassment and pursuit by the Peerless Alliance. Even after fleeing to other territories, they could not escape the alliance's clutches. In a fit of furious exasperation, Shen Qingqian led these disciples straight into this secret, undiscovered spatial rift beneath the ancient ruins. Only then did they finally find true peace and stability. It was here that she founded Blood Demon Hall. In the blink of an eye, another five hundred years had slipped by.

Adding it all up, Shen Qingqian had spent a full two thousand years inside this very rift.

—"For two thousand years... I've been living alongside such a gargantuan head?"-Shen Qingqian murmured.

At the thought that this head could have opened its jaws and swallowed her whole at any given second over those two thousand years, she began to [question her entire existence](# "Literal CN: 怀疑人生 (huáiyí rénshēng) — modern slang: questioning one's life / existential dread").

Worse yet, they had even treated the Flame Crystal Flowers growing inside its nostrils as a sect 'specialty product,' venturing in several times every year to inspect and pluck them... Speaking of which, whenever flames belched out of those caves, wasn't it because she had tickled the creature and nearly woken it up?!

Shen Qingqian suddenly found it rather difficult to breathe.

Zhou Hui, on the other hand, felt that for her to only experience lingering terror now, her nervous system's reflex delay was simply far too sluggish—her nerves were probably as thick as utility poles!

—"A normal person wouldn't build a sect next to a 'mountain' when they clearly knew there was something wrong with it, would they?"-Zhou Hui remarked.

—"I thought it was nature's blessing!"-Shen Qingqian countered.

[—"A blessing?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"...Our sect's cultivation method progresses extraordinarily fast when practicing here,"-Shen Qingqian admitted reluctantly.-"So even if there were a few minor anomalies, I figured they were just the natural price to pay. Under normal circumstances, I simply restrained the disciples from approaching it."

If the situation were not truly critical, she never would have disclosed such an internal sect secret to an outsider.

Zhou Hui looked at Shen Qingqian with an exceedingly strange expression, then shifted his gaze toward the Blood Demon Hall disciples.

—"Of course your progress would be fast. Resisting baleful qi day in and day out is equivalent to circulating your techniques every waking second without pause. It would be bizarre if you didn't advance under such conditions,"-Zhou Hui said.-"But didn't you ever feel that living in this environment left your bodies faintly chilled, aching, depleted of strength, and feeling as though you were constantly suffering from blood loss?"

—"I thought it was [uterine cold](# \"Literal CN: 宫寒 (gōnghán) — traditional Chinese medical concept of uterine coldness / menstrual chills, causing severe dysmenorrhea, fatigue, and lower abdominal pain\"),"-Shen Qingqian answered purely out of instinct.

The entire spatial rift instantly plunged into a dead silence.

In the cultivation realm, female awareness had never awakened, and female cultivators rarely, if ever, discussed such matters. Consequently, while everyone else was enveloped in acute embarrassment, Zhou Hui had fallen into deep contemplation.

Because women belonged to the Yin polarity, female cultivators inherently progressed faster when practicing Yin-aligned techniques. This naturally led them to pair their cultivation with water- and ice-attribute spells, and because of such elemental arts, they often chose dwellings near glacial peaks or frozen lakes. As a result, several renowned all-female sects were predominantly Yin and delicate, to the point of becoming a rigid stereotype.

It was only because Zhou Hui had lived in another world and attained a foundational understanding of the tribulations women experienced that hearing Shen Qingqian's words suddenly brought this question to the forefront of his mind:

Speaking of which... do female cultivators truly not feel pain?

Even if a female cultivator learned to [sever the red dragon](# "Literal CN: 斩赤龙 (zhǎn chìlóng) — ancient Daoist physiological term for female cultivators halting menstruation to conserve vital essence"), she hadn't undergone a complete anatomical transformation; whatever discomfort was meant to be felt in the early stages would still be felt.

Especially in the early tiers of cultivation, when the mortal body had not yet been fully reinforced and reconstructed by spiritual energy—it was usually not until the Nascent Soul stage that they could completely shed this torment.

Yet judging from the circumstances of Blood Demon Hall, Shen Qingqian appeared to have treated this chronic agony as nothing more than a routine part of daily cultivation.

Zhou Hui had never realized this before. Even the sword technique he had bestowed upon Mo Bei was quite stereotyped—though with Mo Bei's unique constitution, she couldn't practice anything else anyway; fortunately, the physical training of a sword cultivator strengthened the constitution and lessened physical distress.

...Ah, well. Regardless of whether they felt pain or not, he must remember going forward: newly admitted female disciples would first build their foundations with Yang-aligned techniques, preferably acquiring a groundwork in body cultivation and sword cultivation to bolster their physical constitutions before choosing their ultimate specializations.

Pondering thus, Zhou Hui naturally and smoothly added two whole mandatory courses to his female disciples' curriculum in one breath.

Zhou Hui instantly felt as though he had resolved a monumental pedagogical issue.

He then turned his head toward Bai Wubin:
—"What about you men? No sensation whatsoever?"

—"It... it certainly felt awful,"-Bai Wubin's face flushed bright red, looking as though he had just overheard something he had no right hearing.-"We always assumed this cultivation method was only suited for girls, so suffering was inevitable. In the end, only those who could endure the pain stayed behind."

—"Wait a second,"-Shen Qingqian snapped, whirling her head to glare at Bai Wubin.-"Does that mean the agony you claimed male disciples couldn't endure every year was actually identical to what the female disciples were suffering?!"

Bai Wubin's face turned even redder.

Shen Qingqian let out a frigid harrumph:
—"Splendid! A pack of useless trash, every single one of them! Good riddance!"

Well then. No wonder Blood Demon Hall was predominantly female.

—"In that case, do you still desire this 'blessing'?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Shen Qingqian instantly shook her head like a rattle-drum:
—"No, no, absolutely not!"

If this head were truly dead, even if it exuded baleful qi contamination—or rather, precisely because that 'contamination' was what she required—Shen Qingqian might have been able to endure it. But the monstrosity was undeniably alive, and no one knew when it might awaken. That was something she could never tolerate.

In any case, her cultivation technique did not rely entirely on baleful qi. Although their future progress would inevitably slow down, they could still continue practicing.

Zhou Hui nodded:
—"I shall move it away shortly."

—"Immortal Venerable, if you fancy it, you could also transport it back to Mount Tianling,"-Shen Qingqian offered immediately.-"Look, its nostrils even hold the Flame Crystal Flowers you require... Why are you pulling my sleeve?"

Shen Qingqian glared at Bai Wubin, who had frantically tried to stop her.

Ran Deng and the others stared at her in utter speechlessness:

[A monstrosity you dread to your very marrow, and you want us to drag it back home?! The sheer nerve to suggest that!]

Yet Master didn't seem to be the sort of person who cared about superstition or conventional sensibilities; what if he genuinely decided it possessed immense research value and hauled it back?

Ran Deng nervously shifted her gaze to Zhou Hui.

Taking every possible factor into account, Ran Deng desperately wished for this thing NOT to be moved back to their mountain.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui harbored no such inclination.

He summoned a white light screen, displaying a meticulously drafted structural diagram of the spatial rift—during the time Ran Deng and the others had spent designing the formations, Zhou Hui naturally hadn't idled; he had mapped every nook and cranny of this rift.

Zhou Hui explained the layout in detail to Shen Qingqian:

—"This rift is an isolated void pocket suspended along the outer periphery of the Abyss Sea.

—"Judging from the conditions within, it was likely formed during the ancient God-Demon War. Back when our realm served as the battlefield, the outermost spiritual barrier collapsed, allowing countless extraterrestrial fiends to tear open spatial rifts and invade. One such invading fiend—the very one whose remaining half-skeleton you fashioned into Blood Demon Hall—tore this rift open.

—"For reasons unknown, another 'head' followed closely on that fiend's heels. With a single snap of its jaws, it tore off the fiend's lower torso, killing it on the spot. But before the rift the fiend carved could fully stabilize, it ended up jamming the 'head' firmly in place. Consequently, this 'head' has remained wedged within this rift for tens of thousands of years."

[...That head sounds kind of stupid!]

What kind of absurd operation was biting the fiend tearing open the rift, only to wedge oneself immovably into the gap?!

Every single person present thought the exact same thing.

Curious, Shen Qingqian asked:
—"Immortal Venerable, what is the origin of this stup... this head? Since it fought against an extraterrestrial fiend, could it be that it wasn't an evil 'person'?"

—"Such ferocious beasts recognize no distinction between good and evil; they act purely upon primal desire,"-Zhou Hui reminded her before elaborating:-"Judging strictly by its outward appearance, it too resembles an extraterrestrial fiend. However, given that this place exudes only baleful qi, while even the demonic qi of the devoured fiend has completely vanished, it is quite likely that the soul of a ferocious beast resides within."

—"A ferocious beast's soul inside an extraterrestrial fiend's head?"-Shen Qingqian felt a little dizzy.

—"That was simply the nature of the God-Demon War—absolute, unfathomable chaos,"-seeing her confusion, Zhou Hui had no choice but to provide a remedial history lecture.-"Whether god or demon, neither was a being that perished simply from being killed. Even if torn to shreds, they could still survive, seizing any chance to possess surrounding corpses and re-enter the fray until pulverized beyond all cohesion. Therefore, within the ruins of the God-Demon Battlefield, judging an entity solely by outward appearance is exceedingly difficult; you can never truly know what it actually is."

Saying this, Zhou Hui cast a contemplative sigh toward the titanic head.

Thus, relying on outward appearance alone made it impossible to determine whether this monstrosity was the true culprit behind Yuwen Xuan's disappearance.

Theoretically speaking, Yuwen Xuan's mere Spirit Severing cultivation shouldn't have possessed the power to awaken it!

After all, Shen Qingqian, a Body Integration cultivator, had pranced about in its vicinity for ages, even climbing into its nostrils year after year to harvest Flame Crystal Flowers, without ever showing the slightest sign of rousing it.

He needed further experimentation to confirm.

Contemplating thus, Zhou Hui addressed the Blood Demon Hall crowd once more:

—"Furthermore, having been wedged open by it for tens of thousands of years, this rift has long since set into a fixed form. Even if we remove it, there is no need to fear spatial collapse, and you may continue residing here—if you are still willing to do so, that is.

—"As for the direction of removal, I believe we can be a little lazy: wherever it crawled out from, we shall simply shove it right back in!

—"Theoretically, since it chased the extraterrestrial fiend here, there ought to be a spatial conduit behind or beneath it through which both entities passed. We shall eject it through that very conduit."

Hearing this, even with the structural diagrams before her, Shen Qingqian felt her head spin.

—"What do we need to do?"-Shen Qingqian showed zero interest in theory, desiring only action.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui refrained from further explanation.

He soared into midair, hovering directly before the forehead of the colossal head:

—"Like this."

Zhou Hui raised his foot and delivered a ferocious kick straight into the head's brow—

*BOOM!*

The head emitted a thunderous roar as it rolled diagonally downward.

It smashed a gargantuan hole straight through the fabric of space—according to Zhou Hui, this 'conduit' had existed originally and was merely plugged by the head; however, from Shen Qingqian's perspective, the spiderweb-like fractures spreading across the periphery of the hole looked like anything but an existing tunnel.

In short, the head tumbled out along the cavity, leaving behind a massive black void.

Instantly, the earth shook and mountains trembled.

The entire rift quaked violently. Yet the arrays Zhou Hui had instructed Ran Deng to engrave proved their worth: the very instant the tremors began, the formations blazed with dazzling radiance, swiftly locking the spatial realm into stability.

Only the gargantuan black hole remained, from within which echoed the distant *thud, thud, thud* of heavy impacts, indicating that the head was still rolling downward.

—"How did you do that?!"-Shen Qingqian gasped in utter veneration.

In the past, she had entertained thoughts of moving this 'mountain' or smashing it to bits—reminiscing on it now, that was truly a suicidal impulse that made her heart seize in fright—yet she hadn't even been able to make it budge an inch.

And yet, the Untiring Immortal Venerable accomplished it with a single kick!

And he wasn't even a body cultivator!

Shen Qingqian possessed zero resistance against such absolute, unadulterated brute force!

She resolved right then and there: starting today, she was an ardent devotee of the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Zhou Hui was completely oblivious to the fact that his single kick had produced a fanatical fangirl. Gazing down into the colossal cavity, he tracked the tumbling head with his divine sense.

Contrary to Zhou Hui's expectations, the head did not plunge into the infinite void connected to the conduit, nor did it wedge itself somewhere along the spatial fold. Instead, a dull *thud* soon reverberated upward, as though it had landed within an expansive, empty space.

What was more, it was remarkably close?

Zhou Hui knitted his brows.

—"A-Ran, overlay this cavity with a formation as well, just in case,"-Zhou Hui instructed.-"I am heading down to investigate."

—"Immortal Venerable, is there anything you need me to do?"-Shen Qingqian offered hastily.

She felt it terribly improper to let their honored guest do all the heavy lifting.

Lu Shulong also rushed forward:
—"Master, should I accompany you?"

—"You all remain here."

Leaving that instruction behind, Zhou Hui had already plunged into the depths of the cavity.

Zhou Hui's disciples were clearly well-accustomed to their master's decisiveness.

—"Senior Shen, may I trouble the Blood Demon Hall disciples to lend a hand as well?"-Ran Deng said to Shen Qingqian.-"This spatial conduit was violently smashed open and must be reinforced immediately, or the consequences could be dire."

Shen Qingqian understood this as well.

Glancing at the black hole that resembled a gaping maw, she rolled up her sleeves and barked at the Blood Demon Hall disciples:

—"Alright, let's get to work!"

* * *

Zhou Hui descended along the black hole.

Sure enough, just as his divine sense had detected, the spot where the head landed was not far away.

Flying for only five minutes or so, Zhou Hui caught the wind blowing from the end of the conduit. Emerging from the passage, he found himself standing inside an enormous hall.

—"It's still within the Abyss Sea?"-Zhou Hui murmured, surveying his surroundings.

Back during his days in the East Hedge Garden, he had explored God-Demon ruins alongside fellow cultivators and was quite familiar with halls of this nature: this was likely an ancient teleportation hall. According to historical records, prior to the invasion of the extraterrestrial fiends, human royal dynasties that had foreseen the catastrophe constructed numerous teleportation array halls throughout the Central Domain to facilitate cultivators deploying against the fiends.

However, the vast majority of these halls were beyond repair.

This place was no exception. Dust lay thick over everything, interspersed with ancient skeletons that had long since lost even the barest trace of rotted flesh. Evidently, it had suffered an assault back in that ancient era, destroying the teleportation array.

—"Still, how did this head fly out from a place like this and bite off half an extraterrestrial fiend?"

Zhou Hui wondered in bewilderment.

He descended slowly, flying up beside the colossal head.

The head breathed with steady, rhythmic inhalations, clearly still wrapped in peaceful slumber.

—"Even the full force of a Tribulation Transcendence blow couldn't stir it?"

Witnessing this, Zhou Hui almost felt he had meddled unnecessarily.

Given the depth of this creature's slumber, it might not have awakened even after another ten thousand years; perhaps there had been no need to assist Blood Demon Hall in moving it at all.

Then again... perhaps not.

Zhou Hui retrieved a storage pouch and wiped away the restrictive seal on its surface.

This seal was primarily designed to mask the aura of the contents within, preventing spirit beasts, monster beasts... or ferocious beasts with keen noses from detecting the items inside.

The moment the seal dissolved, the effect was immediate.

Zhou Hui watched as the head—which had remained dead to the world despite being struck, kicked, and rolled—twitched its nostrils. Its heavy eyelids showed signs of fluttering open.

Zhou Hui simply withdrew the item from the storage pouch and tossed it high into the air.

*ROAR—!*

The head suddenly unleashed a deafening howl, springing up from the ground to catch the object Zhou Hui had thrown in its jaws:

It was a flimsy infant shirt, embroidered with the pattern of a Taotie alongside the character "Xuan" (轩).

—"...So the key to awakening it was indeed Xuan'er?"-Zhou Hui frowned.

Indeed, that tiny garment was clothing Yuwen Xuan had worn in his infancy.

Naturally, the present-day Yuwen Xuan couldn't fit into it, but Zhou Hui had no habit of discarding belongings—furthermore, in the cultivation world, carelessly throwing away clothing that had touched a person's flesh and belonged to a master could lead to catastrophic consequences. Thus, Zhou Hui had kept it pressed beneath the wardrobe chests in his storeroom.

This excursion to Blood Demon Hall was originally an incident connected to Yuwen Xuan, and Zhou Hui had considered bringing him along, but ultimately decided against it upon reflection.

After all, one could never know how the enemy—if one existed—had laid their trap.

What if Yuwen Xuan's mere presence was the trigger?

Therefore, Zhou Hui decided to assess the situation first. Nonetheless, to be thoroughly prepared, he not only brought Lu Shulong, but also an assortment of items bearing Yuwen Xuan's aura: including the baby hair shaved when he first took him in, his infant clothing, his childhood feeding bowl, and a mishmash of magical artifacts he had recently broken, ensuring he could handle any eventuality.

This precaution had proved utterly correct.

The head showed zero reaction to anything else, yet the moment an item belonging to Yuwen Xuan appeared, it was inevitably roused!

Could it be that in the past, his eldest disciple had been devoured by this monstrosity?

And that by awakening it, he had brought disaster upon Blood Demon Hall as well?!

Guessing thus, Zhou Hui suddenly felt a pang of guilt toward Blood Demon Hall. After all, the troublemaker was his own eldest disciple... No, rather, it was the person who had branded Xuan'er with that mark!

Who was that person? Why did they send Xuan'er here specifically to feed this head?!

Zhou Hui glared coldly at the head.

He saw a gaping maw.

While dormant, this head might have responded exclusively to Yuwen Xuan's scent—aside from those unfortunate souls sucked in by its breathing—but now that it was awake, it certainly wouldn't be satisfied with a tiny rag that couldn't even floss its teeth.

Back then, it had devoured an extraterrestrial fiend; a 'mere' Tribulation Transcendence cultivator naturally seemed like light work.

The head snapped its titanic jaws wide, lunging to swallow Zhou Hui, who stood right beside its mouth.

Zhou Hui stood rooted in place, not flinching in the slightest. He merely raised a single hand and flicked his wrist:
—"Scram!"

Instantly, the breeze stirred by his fingers transformed into a howling tempest before coalescing into the shape of a colossal palm. With a crisp *SLAP!*, it struck the left cheek of the head.

The head reeled, but refused to concede. Snapping its jaws wide, it swung around—

*SLAP!*

This time, its right cheek took the blow. Because it was spherical, it spun several times across the floor.

*SLAP! SLAP! SLAP!*

Zhou Hui flicked his fingers three more times, whipping the monstrosity like a spinning top. It spun several hundred rotations on the spot before rolling limply into the corner.

[Should I seal it and take it back to study its connection to Yuwen Xuan?]

[Still... even I don't want to drag such a repulsive thing back home!]

Zhou Hui pondered.

He stepped toward the head, only to find that having rolled into the corner, it was no longer stubbornly attempting to bite him. Instead, it lay motionless against the wall, its aura seemingly extinguished.

[...No way? Did it die just like that?]

Zhou Hui approached the head, making a show of inspecting it. Yet behind him, his shadow slowly rose upright, forming a massive black void that looked just like a gaping maw—

*SLAP!*

Zhou Hui backhanded the air, sending the shadow-like black maw tilting sideways.

*SLAP! SLAP!*

Two more slaps followed, slamming the black-hole maw (or maw-like black hole) flat onto the pavement.

—"A wisp of a remnant soul? As expected, something was hiding inside this head!"-Zhou Hui sneered coldly. A formation pattern illuminated in his palm, and just as he prepared to cast it over the black hole to seal it completely, he noticed the flattened shadow trembling violently. Liquid began pooling beneath it, as though it were weeping.

This thing could weep? No... this manner of weeping was eerily familiar!

Zhou Hui froze internally. Reaching down with two fingers, he pinched the skin-like remnant of a soul and lifted it up like a sheet of dough.

His gaze met a pair of enormous golden eyes, streaming with a torrential flow of silent tears.

Though the hue differed, the shape and size were undeniably familiar.

—"Xuan'er?"-Zhou Hui called out.

The golden eyes blinked innocently, tears streaming down even faster. Yet the black portion pinned against the floor obstinately peeled open its gaping maw once more, lunging to swallow Zhou Hui whole.

*SLAP!*

It was flattened into a pancake yet again.

—"No, you aren't Xuan'er. But you ought to be a fragment of Xuan'er's soul..."-Zhou Hui stated with certainty.-"A [Soul of the Taotie](# \"Literal CN: 饕餮之魂 (Tāotiè zhī hún) — soul of the ancient ferocious beast Taotie, embodiment of insatiable gluttony\")?"

Indeed: physically speaking, Zhou Hui's eldest disciple Yuwen Xuan was one hundred percent human, but his soul was that of a Taotie.

This likely traced back to Yuwen Xuan's origins.

—Though in truth, Zhou Hui didn't know all that much about them either.

What was certain was that centuries ago, during an expedition into the Abyss Sea, Zhou Hui had inadvertently raided an illegal black workshop. Inside lay countless dead pregnant women, yet from the womb of one deceased woman crawled an infant radiating the ferocious aura of a primordial beast.

That infant was Yuwen Xuan.

Prior to that, one verified rumor regarding the God-Demon Battlefield was that an ancient Taotie had fallen in this very region; people had recovered numerous relics from its demise. Evidently, the soul dwelling within that infant was merely a portion of it.

Who had obtained this soul and stuffed it into a human embryo remained a mystery.

(In any case, Carefree Palace, having committed every wickedness under the sun, was under heavy suspicion.)

Back then, when the other cultivators saw the infant, all agreed that such a creature could never be permitted to live, demanding its execution. Sensing something, the infant wept—yet made not a single sound, shedding silent, streaming tears.

Zhou Hui had felt deeply moved by how quietly and obediently the child cried. Overriding all objections, he adopted the boy.

And the result...?

The truth proved that this brat wasn't the least bit obedient; he was [monstrously mischievous](# "Literal CN: 熊到不能再熊 (xióng dào bùnéng zài xióng) — modern Chinese slang: as unruly and troublesome as a 'bear child' (naughty brat) could possibly get") to the absolute extreme!

—"He's already that much of a terror, and his soul wasn't even whole?... Then again, a mortal embryo could never withstand the complete soul of a primordial ferocious beast."

Hence, other soul remnants had to exist.

Take the one before him: hiding within the head of an extraterrestrial fiend, it had devoured another fiend before entering an eternal slumber—whether to conserve energy or purely because it had choked itself.

Could it be that someone had lured Yuwen Xuan toward this head in order to fuse the Taotie's soul remnants into one, obtaining a complete beast soul?

Zhou Hui pondered, only to sense a bizarre tugging force in his hand.

Looking down, he saw that this remnant soul had wised up: it no longer attempted to bite him. Instead, while he was lost in thought, it stretched itself out like a strand of noodles, desperately worming toward the skeletons as though seeking to return to its body...

Wait? Its body?

Zhou Hui suddenly noticed an exceedingly bizarre detail:

The direction the soul was stretching toward was not the colossal head, but toward several human skeletons!

What was going on? By a soul's natural instinct, shouldn't it return to the head it had inhabited for tens of thousands of years? Why was it fixated on those human skeletons? Come to think of it, those bones hadn't originally belonged to this hall; they had been scattered near the head from the very start, seemingly the remains of unfortunate souls it had devoured.

...!

Wait a second. A head that titanic spitting out bones after devouring an extraterrestrial fiend was one thing... but why on earth would it spit out bones after eating a human?! Compared to the colossal stature of an extraterrestrial fiend, a human being wouldn't even suffice to floss its teeth!

Zhou Hui's eyes widened. Grabbing the remnant soul, he kneaded and squashed it until it turned into a little black ball, then walked over to the skeletons.

To all appearances, they looked like ordinary human bones. Much like the remains of that extraterrestrial fiend, not a shred of aura lingered upon them—the classic hallmark of being eaten clean by a Taotie, which never left behind the slightest trace of residual demonic qi or spiritual energy.

Consequently, Zhou Hui hadn't paid them much heed earlier, assuming they were Blood Demon Hall disciples who had gone missing over the centuries.

Yet inspecting them closely now, a glaring anomaly emerged:

These skeletons were completely identical!

They originated from the exact same person!

How could one person be devoured multiple times?!

And leave behind a skeleton every single time!

Moreover, they felt inexplicably familiar—just like those eyes and that silent weeping.

The corner of Zhou Hui's mouth twitched. Channeling his mana, he hoisted the skeletons to their feet, then coated their frames with spiritual energy, reconstructing how they had looked when clad in living flesh and blood.

Thereupon, a row of "Yuwen Xuans" stood before Zhou Hui.

[—"Ying, ying."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul")

Spotting the line of "Yuwen Xuans," the soul remnant squashed into a black ball emitted an excited, infant-like whimper, parting its jaws as if eager to devour them whole.

Zhou Hui casually pinched its mouth shut once again.

—"As expected of you... you actually ate yourself? Just how many times did you eat yourself?!"

Zhou Hui gave a bitter smile. Though the truth was plain as day at a single glance, he couldn't resist counting aloud:

One, two, three, four, five, six... Six skeletons.

The exact number of the six reincarnations Zhou Hui had already lived through.

Only missing this current lifetime.
