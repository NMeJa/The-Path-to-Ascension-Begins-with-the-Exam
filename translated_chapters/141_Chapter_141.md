# Chapter 141: Teaching Achievements (第141章 教学成果)

[← Previous Chapter](140_Chapter_140.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](142_Chapter_142.md)

---

—"Aiya, these children look quite promising!"-Fairy Ruopei remarked.

As an elder of one of the Northern Domain's premier sects, Fairy Ruopei had not accompanied Jian Nuo here—even as a young master of Sky City, he was hardly qualified to have a Mahayana cultivator of her caliber act as his escort. Rather, the instant the Ascension Stairway manifested in the world, she had rushed straight to the Central Domain at top speed, arriving even earlier than Jian Nuo.

That said, her reason for rushing over was a touch [frivolous](# "Literal CN: 轻佻 (qīngtiāo) — frivolous / lacking solemnity"):

She was gathering material for the next round of flame wars.

Indeed, the great debate was far from over.

Nor could it ever truly end.

Although the meddling of the Palace of Carefree Wandering and the sudden manifestation of the Ascension Stairway had drastically reduced the number of polemical essays in mainstream broadsheets like *White Jade Capital Daily*, the flames of war between Central and Northern Domain cultivators had already been kindled. Furthermore, their subject—"Ascension"—was the eternal obsession of all immortal seekers. At present, both factions were merely [biding their time and recouping their strength](# "Literal CN: 养精蓄锐 (yǎng jīng xù ruì) — conserving energy and building up strength"), waiting for the next spark to ignite another round of verbal warfare.

As a leading figure among the Northern Domain's scholars, Fairy Ruopei had naturally discerned this.

Moreover, the existence of the Ascension Stairway triggered a sharp sense of crisis within her:

The Ascension Stairway was directly tied to "Ascension," yet it sat squarely in the Central Domain. Central Domain cultivators could effortlessly obtain firsthand research material. If they preemptively published some definitive, groundbreaking paper, the Northern Domain would be thrust into a severely disadvantaged position in the academic flame war.

By this point, Fairy Ruopei had already developed a wholly unnecessary sense of responsibility:

Ascension was no longer what mattered; what mattered was that they could never lose an argument!

Driven by this mindset, Fairy Ruopei hadn't even waited for the Northern Domain's Ascension Stairway Tour Group [—struck through—](# "Literal CN: 旅游团（x） — tour group with comedic (x) strike marker") to get organized before setting out for the Central Domain entirely on her own.

However, the Huanfei Sect held no prior ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and Fairy Ruopei didn't dare rashly disturb the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Consequently, she had first gone to visit an old acquaintance: Fairy Xia of Cloud City.

How could one not say Fairy Xia had friends across the four seas?

Even though she enthusiastically stoked the flames in her own publication, *Weekly Gossip*, it hadn't hindered her real-world social networking in the slightest. On this day, the two of them appeared side by side in White Jade Capital, chatting with sisterly warmth.

Standing atop the gatehouse of the city walls, they looked down upon the children preparing to scale the Ascension Stairway.

Instantly, Fairy Ruopei's [occupational hazard](# "Literal CN: 职业病 (zhíyè bìng) — professional deformation / occupational habit") flared up.

—Although Fairy Ruopei was famed across the cultivation realm for her Moon Phase Mirror, she was far more renowned for her [penchant for lecturing others](# "Literal CN: 好为人师 (hào wéi rén shī) — love of acting as everyone's teacher / being fond of teaching people"). One could easily see this from how she still willingly gave one-on-one thesis guidance to her grand-disciples even after reaching the Mahayana stage.

Yet compared to such minor matters, what made Fairy Ruopei truly legendary was that during her Soul Formation and Void Refinement stages, she had served as her sect's Rector of Studies. Under her tutelage, she had instructed over a thousand disciples, more than ten percent of whom successfully broke through to Nascent Soul. In the cultivation realm, this was an astonishing achievement. That the Huanfei Sect stood among the front ranks of the Northern Domain was largely thanks to her monumental contributions.

At this moment, Fairy Ruopei scrutinized the cohort of children below and let out an appreciative exclamation:

—"Setting aside their latent potential for a moment, to not freeze up with stage fright in an occasion of this scale proves their mental fortitude is at least up to par. I wonder where the Heavenly Spirit Sect managed to unearth so many fine seedlings?"

—"Are they really that exceptional?"-Fairy Xia asked, craning her neck in curiosity.

Compared to Fairy Ruopei, Fairy Xia rarely took part in sect recruitment. Still, not long ago, she had heard her own descendants complaining that promising seedlings in the Central Domain were far too scarce—and the one or two that did show up had invariably migrated from other domains.

Yet that was simply inevitable.

The living conditions of the Central Domain were far too harsh; it struggled even to support mortal kingdoms, to say nothing of nurturing quality talent.

Consequently, sect recruitment across the Central Domain was crude and simplistic: they checked only spirit roots, disregarding everything else.

Fairy Xia followed Fairy Ruopei's gaze downward.

From the ramparts of White Jade Capital, the Ascension Stairway resembled a slender silk thread, and the children appeared like tiny insects crawling along it. For Mahayana powerhouses, however, this distance hardly prevented them from taking in every minute detail of the children below.

Looking over the youngsters, Fairy Xia remarked:

—"To me, their spiritual energy seems terribly thin. Some look like outright mortals!"

—"The majority are indeed mortals. It is a bit of a pity, given how rare it is to find children with such spirited vitality,"-Fairy Ruopei sighed regretfully.-"Still, so long as one possesses a spirit root capable of cultivation, neither the number nor the raw quality of those spirit roots determines everything."

—"So it hinges on mental fortitude?"-Fairy Xia asked, her tone carrying a hint of skepticism.

Indeed, once one attained their realm, the quantity and quality of one's spirit roots no longer mattered, as higher stages allowed practitioners to patch and perfect them anyway. For low-tier cultivators, however, spirit roots remained paramount.

It was just that the master of this domain, the Untiring Immortal Venerable, famously possessed mediocre spirit roots, so Fairy Xia couldn't openly dispute it.

Yet how many figures like the Untiring Immortal Venerable ever existed?

Becoming an [Involution King](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — supreme champion of grinding / hyper-competitive overachiever") wasn't just a matter of working hard; one had to out-grind and out-compete every single living soul.

—"No. Children this young have barely begun development; their mental fortitude is only average,"-Fairy Ruopei corrected.-"What matters far more are good study habits and the ability to define their learning objectives as early as possible."

—"...Can you even tell those two things at a glance?"-Fairy Xia asked in disbelief.

—"Of course. Once you have taught enough students, you will find that children with clear goals and learning drive are strikingly obvious,"-Fairy Ruopei said. As she spoke, she turned her eyes toward the Ascension Stairway.-"Take the young lady coming up first, for example. She is quite remarkable. Although a mortal, her willpower is unquestionably the strongest among this entire group."

—"Oh?"-Fairy Xia replied, unimpressed.

She held zero interest in mortal willpower and suffered from none of Fairy Ruopei's pedagogical obsessions. Feeling the conversation had run its course, she was about to change the subject when gasps of astonishment erupted from the surrounding cultivators:

[—"Isn't she a mortal?! How on earth can she mobilize spiritual energy?!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

Fairy Xia hurriedly looked down.

---

Eleventh Lady Mei strode steadily across the Ascension Stairway.

Just as Qu Qianxing had explained to Jian Nuo, the trial of the Ascension Stairway tailored itself to every individual. Depending on one's physique, cultivation realm, and life experiences, each person who tread upon the stairway faced a unique trial.

For example, as Eleventh Lady Mei climbed, the magnitude of the spiritual energy storm was drastically reduced; she did not require defensive spellcraft simply to hold her footing like Jian Nuo. Yet for a mortal physique, merely standing amidst such turbulent, chaotic spiritual currents caused needle-like stabs of pain across the entire body, let alone walking.

[If this were me half a year ago, I would have fainted the moment I set foot on the very first step, wouldn't I?] Eleventh Lady Mei thought to herself.

Half a year ago, after all, she was still a cloistered young miss confined to her boudoir who felt dizzy at the sight of direct sunlight. Setting aside her frail health, she hadn't even possessed the courage to walk unaccompanied outside, let alone tread upon the legendary Ascension Stairway where only immortals walked.

Yet walking it now, it wasn't all that difficult!

Just as her instructor in the Five Charioteering Arts class taught every day: stabilize your center of gravity, clear your mind, and attempt to perceive the ambient spiritual energy around you—and as a mortal, if you cannot perceive spiritual energy, then perceive your own pain instead.

Carefully distinguish the nuances between the sensations, then breathe in rhythmic cadence...

While reviewing the foundational concepts she had long committed to memory, Eleventh Lady Mei climbed step after step.

Her footfalls were solid and steady, planted with deliberate force. Rather than the airy, delicate grace demanded of mythical fairies, she moved like a mortal grounded firmly to the earth—yet every single step carried the distinct aesthetic beauty of strength.

With each step she took, multicolored spiritual energy welled up from Eleventh Lady Mei's body.

Compared to Jian Nuo's magnificent ice palace earlier, Eleventh Lady Mei's spiritual energy was undeniably minuscule—merely delicate little blossoms swirling around her. Yet amidst the high-altitude spiritual vortex, they remained unyielding, drifting outwards of their own accord and leaving a subtle, lingering fragrance along the white jade stairway.

Eleventh Lady Mei was oblivious to all of this. She could not even perceive the spiritual energy; she simply continued climbing upward.

---

High above on the city walls, the senior cultivators stirred into a commotion.

For titans who had crossed into the upper three realms, not even the sight of a Heavenly Spirit Root would incite such agitation:

What was so special about a Heavenly Spirit Root?

Leaving aside someone like Jian Nuo, many of these grandmasters possessed extraordinarily rare aptitudes themselves. Along their lengthy paths of cultivation, they had witnessed countless geniuses of every stripe fall by the wayside.

Yet standing before them right now was a mortal girl!

A mortal manipulating spiritual energy was like the blind reading script, the deaf composing symphonies, or the mute singing melodies—it belonged firmly in the realm of medical miracles. For cultivators, the former was arguably even more miraculous.

Certain spells could heal deafness and muteness, after all, but no one had ever heard of anyone cultivating a spirit root inside a mortal from scratch.

—"There is no need to 'plant' one from scratch,"-Qu Qianxing said with a composed smile.-"Theoretically, every living creature possesses a spirit root. It is merely that certain beings have incomplete spirit roots or dantian, rendering them unable to practice cultivation."

—"The theory holds, but could there actually exist an acquired method to remedy a mortal's spirit root?"-a cultivator asked.

Here, the emphasis was specifically on *mortals*.

For cultivators in the later realms, remedying spirit roots was relatively manageable: after undergoing the body-tempering of earlier stages, their physical vessels were sturdy enough to endure the scouring of raw spiritual energy. Forget about rounding out the fundamental five elements; even stimulating mutant spirit roots was entirely within reach.

A mortal body, however, was far too frail. Attempting to augment even an innate spirit root entailed agony equivalent to slicing open every blood vessel in the body—and no amount of sheer willpower could endure it; the mortal would inevitably burst and perish.

Thus, the cultivators cast peculiar glances at Qu Qianxing, suspecting the Heavenly Spirit Sect had developed some miraculous new spellcraft.

Yet an even sharper, more observant cultivator noticed:

—"No, that's not it. The child is still a mortal. The spirit root within her remains inadequate for cultivation. Rather than cultivating, she is utilizing the ambient spiritual energy around her to stabilize her body."

[—"Stop joking around! Mortals cannot sense spiritual energy in the first place, so how could she possibly utilize it?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Yet she clearly did!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

After several inconclusive rounds of debate, the cultivators all turned their gazes toward Qu Qianxing.

Someone asked cautiously:

—"Young Qianxing, did the Untiring Immortal Venerable invent some specialized technique that allows mortals to perceive spiritual energy?... Ah, if it's a sect secret, please pay it no mind."

Qu Qianxing replied:

—"This is not a technique of our Heavenly Spirit Sect. That this student could achieve this is the fruit of her studies in the Five Charioteering Arts class. And the textbook for that curriculum was generously provided by Sect Master Shen of Blood Demon Hall."

As he spoke, his gaze turned toward Shen Qingqian.

Every head whipped toward Shen Qingqian in unison.

Sect Master Shen was currently munching on cake.

Since no one was paying her any mind and she had no desire to mingle with others, Shen Qingqian had nestled into an inconspicuous corner after brushing off Qu Qianxing's offers of introductions.

She was no stranger to collaborating with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and with several of her disciples serving as instructors at White Jade Capital's academies, she felt entirely at ease. What thrilled Shen Qingqian most of all was that the Untiring Immortal Venerable showed no sign of making a personal appearance.

Thus, while the other cultivators stood on the ramparts buffeted by the turbulent spiritual winds to observe the mortal trial, Sect Master Shen had her disciples set up a plush armchair in the wind-sheltered shade of the gatehouse. With a spread of fresh fruit and her favorite cake delivered to her side, she was merrily sipping hot tea and enjoying herself.

According to Shen Qingqian's plan, once she finished her tea, she could slip away and go straight home. Absolute perfection.

Who could have guessed that Qu Qianxing would say whatever he just said over by the White Jade Capital tour group, causing everyone to turn and stare at her in unison? The shock sent Shen Qingqian's cake tumbling with a wet *splat* back onto her plate.

Beside her, Bai Wubin made an expression that suggested he could hardly bear to watch.

Fortunately, the cream hadn't smeared across her robes, barely salvaging the dignity of a Body Integration grandmaster—assuming that dignity existed in the first place.

Shen Qingqian asked dryly:

—"W-What is it?"

—"We were just discussing the textbook you authored,"-Qu Qianxing said with a warm smile.-"Everyone is immensely curious: how did you enable mortals to perceive spiritual energy?"

—"Can they even do that?"-Shen Qingqian blurted out dumbly in response.

Qu Qianxing had touched upon the very matter that gave Shen Qingqian the biggest headache. Having zero desire to dwell on that cursed textbook, she hurriedly deflected:

—"While the core technique originated from me, Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable merely consulted it because he wasn't familiar with female body-refinement practices! The textbook was actually compiled and written by him! I don't understand a single thing about it."

Hearing this, many cultivators adopted expressions of sudden comprehension.

Only Qu Qianxing looked as though he wanted to add something, but after receiving a fierce glare from Shen Qingqian, he could only close his mouth with a wry smile.

Just as the cultivators lost interest in Shen Qingqian and turned back to question Qu Qianxing, Eleventh Lady Mei stepped onto the final stair.

At that exact instant, everyone watched as the blossoms swirling around Eleventh Lady Mei took to the air, ninety percent of them hurtling straight toward Shen Qingqian.

In a flash, Shen Qingqian was bathed in a gentle rain of fragrant blossoms.

—"What kind of ghost trick is this?"-Shen Qingqian asked, thoroughly ruining the poetic atmosphere.

Yet immediately afterward, she felt her cultivation surge upward by a noticeable notch.

Shen Qingqian's expression underwent a drastic change:

—"Wait a moment! I didn't do anything to that girl!"

Yet she saw the expressions of the distant cultivators shift, several of them breaking into hushed whispers.

[It's over! Has the day finally come where I can't even survive in the Central Domain?!]

[These people couldn't possibly think I've practiced some vile demonic art, could they?!]

[Theoretically, my technique shouldn't have any ability to siphon cultivation from disciples!]

[No! Most importantly, that child—is that child alright?!]

In complete panic, Shen Qingqian scrambled to her feet and peered downward.

The other cultivators followed suit, gazing down once more.

To Shen Qingqian's relief, Eleventh Lady Mei appeared completely unharmed.

Not only was she unharmed, she looked overjoyed.

Having passed the trial of the Ascension Stairway, she stood upon the platform leading to the city gates. Several Heavenly Spirit Sect instructors waiting there welcomed her, speaking warmly to her.

Upon Eleventh Lady Mei's forehead, a distinct mark had materialized.

—"What will happen to that child?"-Shen Qingqian asked.

What she truly wanted to ask was whether the disappearance of those blossoms that had boosted her cultivation would leave any adverse side effects on the girl, even if she appeared fine for now.

Qu Qianxing seemed to misread the core of Shen Qingqian's concern, answering:

—"This was merely a baseline spirit-root assessment; she still has far more to learn! Passing this examination, however, means she possesses sufficient capability to assist with certain tasks. Master plans to host this year's Grand Immortal Arts Assembly, Immortals' Bazaar, and Auction right here in White Jade Capital. When the time comes, children who pass the assessment may come to the bazaar to assist."

[That wasn't what I was asking!]

[Still... if they're going to continue cultivating her, she's probably fine, right?]

[Then where on earth did that sudden surge of cultivation come from...?]

Shen Qingqian pondered anxiously. She wanted to press for clarity, but with so many bystanders present and several cultivators staring inquisitively at her, she had no choice but to swallow her questions and pretend to be nonchalant as she looked back down.

By this time, the second student had already stepped onto the Ascension Stairway.

His footing was not nearly as steady as the first student's; buffeted by the turbulent eddies of spiritual energy, he swayed from side to side, appearing several times on the verge of tumbling off the stairs. At the critical junctures, however, he held his ground, ultimately reaching the stairway's end.

This time, Shen Qingqian saw clearly: what the youth utilized at those critical moments was indeed the body-refinement technique she had developed, bearing no signs of distortion, mutation, or demonic corruption—it was entirely standard execution.

Yet due to the Ascension Stairway's influence, his spiritual energy was likewise manifested into physical form as gentle, colorful breezes. Furthermore, while he was also a mortal, his innate spirit root was slightly superior to the previous girl's, making his spiritual energy relatively richer.

As this student reached the top of the stairway, those colorful breezes blew straight toward Shen Qingqian as well.

Shen Qingqian felt her cultivation increase once more.

Not nearly as much as from the first girl, however—only a slight sliver.

—"What on earth is going on?"-Shen Qingqian muttered, thoroughly baffled.

Watching this spectacle unfold, the cultivators erupted into passionate discussion once more:

[—"Is it karmic cause and effect?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"It truly is karmic fruit!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"This must be the 'impact' the Untiring Immortal Venerable spoke of!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"To think such a method even existed!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

Listening to their murmurs, Shen Qingqian could finally hold back no longer:

—"What are you all whispering about? If you have doubts about my technique, say it to my face!"

Since she had to face it sooner or later, laying it bare was far better.

Yet in response to Shen Qingqian's defensive outburst, the cultivators wore bizarre expressions. Even with an emotional quotient as rock-bottom as Shen Qingqian's, she could read pure envy upon several of their faces... Envy?!

—"Fellow fairy, you have misunderstood,"-Fairy Xia said at this moment, gliding over. She attempted to loop her arm through Shen Qingqian's, but Shen Qingqian dodged aside. Unfazed, Fairy Xia smiled sweetly:-"Everyone is actually deeply grateful to you. You may very well have helped us discover a brand-new method to earn Impact Factor."

—"Impact Factor?"

—"Indeed,"-Fairy Xia nodded.

Without probing into why Shen Qingqian was unaware, she explained in detail the conditions decreed by the Untiring Immortal Venerable for qualifying for residency in White Jade Capital.

—"Everyone had only considered influencing other cultivators through the journal *Spells*; no one had thought about the ramifications of popularizing these spells among mortals... From the look of things, this might very well be the premier method,"-Fairy Xia remarked.

Hearing this, Shen Qingqian finally breathed an immense sigh of relief.

—"So that boost in cultivation is the [tuition](# "Literal CN: 束脩 (shùxiū) — traditional dried meat bundles presented by students as tuition to a teacher") they offered me?"-Shen Qingqian had little interest in ascension or taking up residency in White Jade Capital; her sole concern was this surplus cultivation.

—"Cultivation?"-Fairy Xia asked, looking at Shen Qingqian with a strange expression.

[...Did I receive something I wasn't supposed to get after all?] Shen Qingqian froze solid.

—"You may interpret it that way,"-fortunately, Qu Qianxing did not misunderstand Shen Qingqian's meaning this time—he too hadn't caught on to her train of thought earlier; who could have known a grandmaster of her standing would be completely oblivious to such basics?

Qu Qianxing explained:

—"Mortals cannot store spiritual energy. Thus, the spiritual energy they condense while traversing the Ascension Stairway is bequeathed to you."

This explanation sent the surrounding cultivators into an even more fanatical fervor, looking for all the world as though they couldn't wait to herd and pen mortals like livestock to harvest spiritual energy.

Qu Qianxing merely offered a cold, imperceptible sneer.

It was only this specific section of the Ascension Stairway that possessed such an effect. To accumulate cultivation here, one not only had to escort mortals to this exact point, but those mortals also had to pass the trial. The effort required would yield far less than simply practicing honest, diligent cultivation. Most crucial of all, White Jade Capital had only granted special access today; at any other time, even a Mahayana cultivator who hadn't passed the trials couldn't approach this place, let alone pen mortals here.

Naturally, given Zhou Hui's personality, anyone who dared harbor such ideas would be permanently barred from White Jade Capital.

Still, the present situation served as an excellent incentive.

Everyone watched as student after student scaled the Ascension Stairway, delivering various types of spiritual energy into Shen Qingqian's hands.

—"Fellow Daoist Qu! Does Lord Immortal Venerable have any plans to compile another batch of textbooks in the near future?"-a cultivator asked, [shamelessly pressing his luck](# "Literal CN: 腆着脸 (tiǎn zhe liǎn) — brazenly / putting on a thick face").

—"None. This is not something just anyone can accomplish,"-Qu Qianxing's refusal was utterly ruthless, leaving zero face.

Other cultivators opened their mouths to chime in, only to be laughed off by Fairy Xia:

—"None of you can even teach your own disciples properly, yet you dare dream of authoring textbooks? Why not think of more practical approaches? This is hardly the only place you can popularize your spellcraft, is it?"

Hearing her words, many cultivators fell into deep contemplation.

Ignoring them, Fairy Xia pulled Shen Qingqian aside to chat:

—"Little Sister Shen, regarding your technique, I noticed..."

[Promoted from 'Fellow Fairy' to 'Little Sister' just like that?]

Left standing alone to the side, Fairy Ruopei shook her head with a wry smile.

She knew Fairy Xia's old habit was flaring up again.

Ruopei loved acting as a teacher, whereas Fairy Xia loved scouting promising powerhouses of tomorrow.

Then again, this Fairy Shen hardly needed any "scouting." Solely by virtue of this technique enabling mortals to manipulate spiritual energy, she was destined to qualify for the Mahayana stage sooner or later. If anything, that Fairy Xia had overlooked Shen Qingqian's existence for so long was the real anomaly—something Ruopei could mock her for later.

Before that, though: should she introduce Shen Qingqian's body-refinement method to the towns under her sect's jurisdiction?

—No, not just the body-refinement method. Given Shen Qingqian's temperament, there was no way she could have trained mortals into what they were today on her own. Which meant they needed the Untiring Immortal Venerable's complete textbook curriculum!

And if they acquired the textbooks, wouldn't they also need to import his pedagogical methodology?

Fairy Ruopei pondered deeply.

She wasn't planning on dragging mortals from her sect's domain all the way here to undergo the Ascension Stairway trial—besides, even if they passed, it would only benefit Shen Qingqian anyway. Rather, Ruopei felt that if she could popularize certain spells and cultivation arts among mortals, she could publish excellent papers and harvest substantial Impact Factor. But the prerequisite was that the mortals had to be able to withstand it!

In this scenario, Shen Qingqian's technique... no, the educational curriculum of White Jade Capital's academies would be of colossal assistance.

[Taking ten thousand steps back](# "Literal CN: 退一万步来说 (tuì yī wàn bù lái shuō) — even in the absolute worst-case scenario / looking at it from every angle"), even if Fairy Ruopei held no interest in the Ascension Stairway, if the academy's textbooks could improve the quality of prospective disciple recruits across her region, it was already a massive win. It meant the future of her sect was guaranteed.

At this thought, Fairy Ruopei could sit still no longer.

She stepped forward and asked Qu Qianxing:

—"Fellow Daoist Qu, may I inquire into the teaching methodologies of White Jade Capital's academies in greater detail?"

[—"Me! Me too!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

A swarm of eager cultivators surged forward.
