# Chapter 160: Spectating (第160章 围观)

[—"What staggering thunder."](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

Across the Heavenly Spirit Sect, numerous cultivators gazed up at the sky in awe.

Among their ranks were no small number of Body Integration and Mahayana powerhouses. Yet precisely because they had already weathered the highest tiers of lightning tribulations themselves, they understood all the more acutely just how horrifying the spectacle unfolding before their eyes truly was.

As for those at the Void Refinement stage and below, their expressions were graver still.

After all, Immortal Venerable Untiring's ascension tribulation was far too distant and unattainable for them to fathom. But Yuwen Xuan was "merely" breaking through from Soul Formation to Void Refinement—a milestone many present had only recently undergone, or were on the verge of facing themselves.

This tribulation demonstrated the terrifying upper limit of what a breakthrough tribulation could be.

—"I recall that back when I broke through to the Body Integration Stage, even my tribulation lightning wasn't nearly this exaggerated,"-Fairy Xia remarked with a sigh.

—"This tribulation lightning already carries the terrifying might of the Upper Three Realms!"-other cultivators chimed in agreement.

[—"Judging by the silhouette of those thunderclouds, does Untiring's eldest disciple possess half-demon blood? Tribulations for the demon race are supposed to be far more terrifying than for humans, aren't they?"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"Judging by this ferocious momentum, he doesn't seem to be any benevolent demon!"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"Not a benevolent demon? But look at the hue of those thunderclouds—there isn't even a trace of blood-red! That proves he hasn't committed a single crime violating the Heavenly Dao. Such purity is rare even among human cultivators! Or could it be that demon tribulation clouds adhere to different standards?"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"How could that be? The Heavenly Dao is only harsher toward the demon race! When human cultivators slay wicked individuals, it isn't counted as a sin. But for us demonkind, so long as it is human—regardless of whether the person was utterly heinous—it is chalked up as a karmic sin! That is precisely why demon tribulations are always vastly more terrifying than those of humans."](# "Speaker: Visiting Demon Cultivator")

[—"There's actually a rule like that?"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"Indeed. And so... wait, wouldn't that make for a rather splendid thesis topic?"](# "Speaker: Visiting Demon Cultivator")

The cultivators chattered back and forth in animated discussion.

Yet no matter what angle they approached the matter from, every discussion inevitably circled back to a single conclusion: Immortal Venerable Untiring possessed a formidable eldest disciple.

This only stoked their curiosity further.

After all, alongside the Heavenly Spirit Sect repeatedly stealing the continental spotlight—especially with the ongoing Immortal Arts Assembly—the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had earned considerable renown across the cultivation realm.

Among them, the ones outsiders relished gossiping about most were naturally Lu Shulong and Mo Bei, both of whom had competed in the Immortal Arts Assembly and delivered spectacular performances.

However, any visiting cultivator with a modicum of status and cultivation who set foot on Mount Tianling came away most impressed by Qu Qianxing. The prospective successor's competence left countless senior powerhouses wishing desperately that he were their own disciple—to say nothing of the fact that Qu Qianxing was strikingly handsome.

Yet while the grandmasters of various prominent sects heaped lavish praise upon Qu Qianxing and magnanimously sought to partner with the Heavenly Spirit Sect to license their textbooks and pursue other joint ventures, these titans remained blissfully unaware that their own administrative managers considered the Heavenly Spirit Sect an exceptionally tough bone to gnaw. This was entirely due to the presence of Heavenly Spirit Sect's youngest junior brother, Mo Xi. Under his watch, extracting even the slightest commercial advantage from any covenant or contract was virtually impossible. The proverb [little devils are impossible to appease](# "Chinese proverb: 阎王好见，小鬼难缠 (Yánwáng hǎojiàn, xiǎoguǐ nánchán) — 'The King of Hell is easy to deal with, but the petty devils are a nightmare' — high officials are reasonable, but low-level bureaucrats are ruthless and impossible to bribe") had become a literal description of Mo Xi, and his notorious reputation spread across the cultivation world in an entirely different manner.

Behind these four disciples, Ran Deng and Guan Ye might have appeared inconspicuous at a glance, but the Heavenly Spirit Sect's formations and magical artifacts were omnipresent. Any cultivator paying the slightest attention knew that two formidable rising stars had emerged among array masters and artifact refiners.

Highlighted by the brilliance of all these disciples, Immortal Venerable Untiring's pedagogical methods appeared all the more worthy of study and emulation.

Nevertheless, even as the crowd sang praises of how exceptionally accomplished every single one of Immortal Venerable Untiring's direct disciples had turned out to be, someone would inevitably notice a glaring anomaly:

[—"Why is the second disciple, Qu Qianxing, the one inheriting the sect? Where is their eldest disciple?"](# "Speaker: Inquiring Cultivator")

[—"Could there be something wrong with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's eldest disciple?"](# "Speaker: Inquiring Cultivator")

[—"Indeed! How come we've never heard a peep about their eldest disciple?"](# "Speaker: Inquiring Cultivator")

Regarding this, Su Xin—a certain [cultivation second-generation scion](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū-èrdài) — spoiled child of high-tier cultivators / nepo baby") of the Heavenly Dao Alliance—would have protested in a tearful voice:

—"You people have no idea how terrifying he is! (*sob*)"-Su Xin whimpered.

Unfortunately, exceedingly few people had ever interacted with Yuwen Xuan the way Su Xin had.

Particularly over the past half year, due to a certain [force majeure](# "Literal CN: 不可抗力 — humorous reference to Zhou Hui cursing Yuwen Xuan into a black lamb as punishment"), Yuwen Xuan had rarely shown his face before outsiders, cloaking this eldest disciple in an aura of ever-deepening mystery.

The public only knew that Yuwen Xuan was slated to compete in the Immortal Arts Assembly. But because his cultivation was already beyond Soul Formation—a realm that qualified cultivators in smaller sects to serve as elders or even sect leaders—very few individuals at this tier entered the competition. Furthermore, the Soul Formation powerhouses of major sects were seeded contestants who would only take the stage during the grand finals, leaving onlookers unable to probe the depths of his true strength anytime soon.

Under these circumstances, various individuals with ulterior motives began circulating wild speculations about the Heavenly Spirit Sect's eldest disciple.

Some claimed this eldest disciple had defied Immortal Venerable Untiring's teachings and was an utter good-for-nothing.

Others claimed he had incensed Master and was locked away in the sect's forbidden grounds for long-term self-reflection.

Still others claimed Immortal Venerable Untiring favored the second disciple and had consequently placed the eldest under house arrest. The rumors were endless and thoroughly absurd.

Today, while this heavenly tribulation had drawn everyone's gaze and shattered many of those old rumors, it simultaneously gave off the distinct premonition that an entirely fresh crop of bizarre rumors was about to sprout.

Amidst the commotion, several individuals sought to pry the truth from an "insider":

[—"Boss Tao, you're quite familiar with Immortal Venerable Untiring, aren't you? Then you must surely know what his eldest disciple is like!"](# "Speaker: Inquiring Cultivator")

—"I'm only familiar with Untiring, not with his disciples,"-Boss Tao Zhu replied testily.

Yet he obviously didn't truly know nothing; rather, he felt it was something that could not be spoken aloud:

[Untiring's eldest disciple... is that Taotie, isn't he?]-Tao Zhu thought, furrowing his brow.

He naturally retained a vivid, indelible impression of that calamitous beast.

After all, back when they had explored that unidentified secret realm deep beneath the Abyssal Ocean, had it not been for Untiring, Tao Zhu and a dozen elite experts under his command would have nearly been "devoured" alive by that secret realm.

Only after unraveling a series of arcane puzzles did they discover that the place was a man-made hell designed to breed and give birth to a ferocious beast's soul. There, they uncovered the mangled, shredded corpses of dozens of pregnant women who had been consumed from within by the very fetuses in their wombs. The sole survivor was a delirious madwoman who stubbornly clung to the delusion that the thing inside her belly was her own precious child, barely clinging to life. Yet once the secret realm was dismantled, her soul too dispersed, leaving her to birth the infant carrying the ferocious beast's soul with her final breath.

Perhaps because the scene was simply too tragic, Untiring had gazed upon that infant—silently shedding tears without uttering even a single newborn cry—and unexpectedly conceived the notion of raising the child himself.

—"We can't entrust him to anyone else,"-Untiring had sighed.-"This is the soul of a Taotie. One lapse in vigilance, and he would devour his foster parents!"

[So, wouldn't putting it to death right now be far safer?]

At the time, every insider present—Tao Zhu included—had thought precisely that.

Yet under Untiring's insistence, this tiny infant harboring the soul of a Taotie had ultimately survived.

[...Who could have imagined that in a mere two hundred years, that infant would already reach the realm of breaking through to Void Refining! Good grief! That fellow Untiring actually just let him cross his tribulation out in the open like this! At the very least, he should have hidden him beneath the Abyssal Ocean. That way, no matter how colossal the thunderclouds grew, outsiders wouldn't have noticed!]-Tao Zhu thought with a throbbing headache.

His present mood shared an uncanny similarity to how Villa Master Qingyang had felt upon witnessing Qu Qianxing cross his tribulation.

It was just that Tao Zhu had no idea a demonic rift had opened beneath Eternal Villa. Thus, in his eyes, a ferocious beast Taotie crossing its tribulation was already a catastrophe capable of annihilating the pristine reputation the Heavenly Spirit Sect had painstakingly established!

After all, that was a Taotie!

If things slipped out of control and the Taotie's feral ferocity erupted under the lightning tribulation, it could trigger an apocalyptic disaster.

Naturally, Tao Zhu trusted Zhou Hui's discipline. The fact that the Taotie had never stirred up trouble to this day, and seemingly hadn't even eaten many people, was already a staggering miracle in itself.

Yet Tao Zhu understood even better: just because he trusted Untiring did not mean others would trust Untiring.

Even if the Taotie crossed the tribulation safely, the moment it revealed its true form, meddlesome instigators and factions harboring malicious designs would seize upon the pretext to launch venomous attacks against the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Thus, Tao Zhu was as frantic as [ants on a hot pan](# "Chinese idiom: 热锅上的蚂蚁 (règuō shang de mǎyǐ) — in a state of utter panic and frantic anxiety"), wishing desperately that he could take Yuwen Xuan's place to cross the tribulation himself.

—It was hardly because Boss Tao possessed an overabundance of empathy. Rather, summer had already arrived; following the Heavenly Spirit Sect's general recruitment, it would soon be the Winter Clothes Festival on the first of the tenth month, followed immediately by the Immortals' Market. The prime annual window for doing business was right around the corner!

Particularly this year, riding the momentum drummed up by the Immortal Arts Assembly, cultivators from across the domains were flocking eagerly to the Central Domain and White Jade Capital. It was plain as day that this year's auction would be vastly more magnificent than the last.

Boss Tao did not want any unexpected mishap to jeopardize the auction.

In truth, Boss Tao's journey to the Heavenly Spirit Sect wasn't like ordinary cultivators coming to inspect textbooks; rather, inventory allocation for the Immortals' Market had already commenced, and Tao Zhu had arrived to confirm business arrangements.

Who could have anticipated that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Taotie would pick this precise moment to cross its tribulation?

Hearing someone call out Yuwen Xuan's demon nature, Boss Tao was so anxious [blisters nearly erupted on his lips](# "Chinese idiom: 急得嘴上起泡 (jí de zuǐ shang qǐ pào) — fret so intensely that stress sores/blisters break out").

Especially when he watched that preposterous tribulation lightning crash against the protective barrier hovering above Yuwen Xuan's cave-dwelling.

The other cultivators could not empathize with Boss Tao's inner turmoil, but they gained an even deeper appreciation for Yuwen Xuan's strength:

[—"This protective barrier is truly formidable!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"It has actually withstood six heavenly lightning strikes without the slightest damage!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"Is it thanks to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's formations? I heard Immortal Venerable Untiring's third disciple is a child of the Ran clan."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"I've seen that child. She's only at the Golden Core stage. There's no way her formations were designed to withstand a tribulation of this magnitude!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"Tch, what kind of eyes do you people have?! Open your eyes wide and look closely! Where is there any trace of a formation in that ward? He hasn't even summoned an artifact! The fellow is obviously using a makeshift defensive spell to tough it out!"](# "Speaker: Discerning Cultivator")

[—"Th-that's being far too arrogant..."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"Still, isn't that just how the demon race is? They have always disdained external tools, relying entirely on their physical bodies to endure tribulations. Or rather, their fleshly bodies are their most formidable magical treasures."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"Then he'll be forced to reveal his true form sooner or later!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

The cultivators debated back and forth, making Boss Tao grow tenser with every passing word.

He now prayed that wretched Taotie cub would act more like a human—don't tank it head-on, and whatever you do, don't revert to your true form!

Yet as if deliberately defying his wishes, someone shouted at that exact instant:

—"Ah, isn't that Yuwen Xuan? Why is he flying straight up toward the heavenly lightning?!"-someone cried out.

Hearing those words, the crowd looked up in unison, while Boss Tao nearly fainted dead away.

Plain to see, each bolt of heavenly lightning was exponentially more lethal than the last. Why couldn't this Taotie cub stay properly sheltered? What on earth possessed him to come charging out?!

Yet the very next scene left him dumbfounded.

Not only him—the surrounding cultivators fell deathly silent as well.

Only after a long pause did someone shriek in disbelief:

[—"Am I seeing things?! He just ate that bolt of heavenly lightning!"](# "Speaker: Stunned Cultivator")

[—"Has he lost his mind?! How can heavenly lightning be swallowed like that? Especially as a demon—is he not afraid his soul will scatter into oblivion?!"](# "Speaker: Horrified Cultivator")

[—"No, isn't he a half-demon? Perhaps the human portion..."](# "Speaker: Speculating Cultivator")

[—"You're human! Why don't you go give it a try?!"](# "Speaker: Retorting Cultivator")

[—"Naturally I can't! But that is Immortal Venerable Untiring's disciple! The Immortal Venerable himself can harness heavenly lightning for his own use; what's so strange about his disciple eating a bolt or two? For all we know, his disciples were raised on a diet of heavenly lightning!"](# "Speaker: Rationalizing Cultivator")

[—"How could that pos—"](# "Speaker: Skeptical Cultivator")

Someone tried to scoff in rebuttal, but having evidently witnessed Immortal Venerable Untiring's ascension tribulation in person, the cultivator recalled that absurd, world-shaking spectacle and quietly swallowed the retort back down.

And if those who had witnessed Immortal Venerable Untiring's tribulation reacted like this, the cultivators seeing such a bizarre phenomenon for the very first time were shaken even more profoundly.

—"Is this how Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples cross their tribulations?"-Fairy Ruopei murmured.

She suddenly recalled the first time she had encountered the academic journal *Spells*, when she revised that research paper for her grand-disciple.

Fairy Ruopei's cheeks and ears flushed crimson.

[That idiot witnessed an even more exaggerated spectacle than this, yet wrote *that* kind of paper?! Both are disciples, how could the difference be this astronomical?! No, we absolutely must introduce the Heavenly Spirit Sect's pedagogical methods—not just for the beginner classes, but advanced classes as well, as long as the Heavenly Spirit Sect agrees!]

[Otherwise, when disciples of the same cohort are compared side-by-side, I won't have the face to show myself!]-Fairy Ruopei thought in deep mortification.

—"To instruct a half-demon to such an extraordinary extent, Immortal Venerable Untiring is truly peerless!"-other cultivators shared similar sentiments.

[—"To be capable of devouring thunder and lightning, is Yuwen Xuan truly not the bloodline of an evil-dispelling auspicious beast?"](# "Speaker: Puzzled Cultivator")

[—"If he were an auspicious beast, how could he possibly draw a tribulation like this? I would bet my life he is a ferocious beast!"](# "Speaker: Confident Cultivator")

[—"Tsk, tsk. If he can achieve this with a ferocious beast, then with the [bratty rascals](# "Literal CN: 熊孩子 (xióngháizi) — bear children / bratty, mischievous kids") in my clan, the results should be even more miraculous!"](# "Speaker: Clan Elder")

[—"As expected of Immortal Venerable Untiring!"](# "Speaker: Admiring Cultivator")

Listening to these discussions, Tao Zhu suddenly felt [completely out of the loop](# "Slang: 麻瓜 (mǎguā / Muggle) — dumbfounded / felt like a clueless fool who worried over nothing").

Why had he ever worried about Untiring in the first place? What an utter waste of anxiety.
