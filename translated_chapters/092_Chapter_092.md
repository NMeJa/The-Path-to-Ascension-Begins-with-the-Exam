# Chapter 92: Obsession (第92章 执念)

**Abyssal Ocean — Villa Master Qingyang's Temporary Cave-Dwelling**

—"So, that 'temporary senior sister's' bizarre antics were instigated by Second Senior Brother, weren't they?"-Mo Xi demanded accusingly.

In the beginning, that eldest young miss from Eternal Villa had been perfectly "normal."

Although having someone suddenly drop in as a "transfer student" was annoying, during her first month she had been thoroughly well-behaved, merely listening to lectures honestly. She hadn't tried to excessively cozy up to Master or curry favor with him, and she knew how to read the room without deliberately showing off, looking for all the world like someone who would scurry right back home the instant her studies were concluded.

Mo Xi had not only been thoroughly satisfied; he had even secretly rejoiced, feeling she was far better than the Second Senior Brother traded away in the exchange.

*—If only Second Senior Brother could stay at Eternal Villa and not come back for a long time, that would be even better!*

[Then Master and the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect would belong all to his little Xi-er!]

Yet the honeymoon didn't last. Once her initial adjustment period ended, Qingyang Qingyu suddenly became burning with enthusiasm for Master and his curriculum, racking her brains to curry favor with Master (at least, through Mo Xi's eyes).

At first, she merely prepared some ordinary valuables, like extra magical artifacts and a resplendent [cloud chariot](<# "Literal CN: 云车 (yúnchē) — luxurious flying chariot traveling upon clouds">). But those were things ordinary spirit stones could buy. Was Master ascetic because he lacked spirit stones? Certainly not! Mo Xi had even smugly watched on, waiting for her to make a fool of herself.

Yet as the young lady unfurled her spirit-stone-splurging tactics—one day a verdant jade ancient lotus censer, the next day a gold-woven nine-tailed fox fur cloak—Mo Xi gradually found himself unable to smile.

As the saying went: *whether the gift is something the recipient actually needs is unimportant; what matters is the thought behind it.* No matter how little Master lacked spirit stones, seeing someone bestow gifts like this, even he would surely sense her ardent devotion!

And what finally triggered Mo Xi's highest alarm was this:

The day before, Master had casually remarked:
—"For this material, a five-thousand-year [Unburning Fire](<# "Literal CN: 不燃火 (bùránhuǒ) — mythical flame from the Great Desolation that produces neither smoke nor light, yet sustains immense spiritual heat">) from the depths of the Western Domain's Great Desolation would be ideal. Alas, procuring it on short notice is rather difficult."

The very next day, that "temporary senior sister" presented the legendary Unburning Fire—and a ten-thousand-year-old specimen, no less!

Only then did Mo Xi belatedly realize that this young miss seemed to belong to an archetype that had never before appeared among the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect:

A filthy rich pay-to-win tycoon!

Prior to this, among Master's direct disciples, there was a bizarre battle-maniac pervert, a mortal civil exam top scholar, an heir to a cultivation clan, a mediocre talent with crippled spirit roots, and even a dragon! But there had never been a truly rich person.

Everyone had been plucked up from the gutters by Master, so they could only rely on their own craftsmanship (X) to vie for favor.

The only one even tangentially associated with wealth, Ran Deng, didn't come from a landed aristocratic family either, but rather a clan of [civil engineering concrete-mixers](<# "Literal CN: 打灰的 (dǎhuī de) — modern Chinese engineering/construction slang for pouring concrete; here applied to formation masters who lay down array foundations and masonry">). Although nominally an heir, she actually had few resources at her disposal to squander; besides, she was a total slacker herself, posing zero competitive threat.

But Qingyang Qingyu was a completely different breed!

She was [filthy, disgustingly rich!](<# "Literal CN: 超有钱的 (chāo yǒuqián de) — super rich">) Her family possessed ancient heritage as nobility of the prior dynasty, equipped with deep connections and boundless resources! Rare materials and antiques that ordinary cultivators couldn't unearth in a lifetime, she casually pulled from her sleeve!

Most infuriating of all, this loaded young lady didn't lounge around and slack off—she was insanely hardworking! When Master assigned an eight-thousand-word essay, she could churn out fifty thousand words! She was [involuting the academic rat race so hard she could grind everyone to dust!](<# "Literal CN: 能把人卷没了 (néng bǎ rén juǎn méi le) — involuting so severely as to obliterate competitors">)

Watching her, Mo Xi felt his stomach convulse with stress.

Yet he also fairly (?) observed that Qingyang Qingyu seemed to harbor no romantic or treacherous designs on their Master; she showed no signs of defecting to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, either. She purely wanted to rely on [throwing cash](<# "Literal CN: 砸钱 (zá qián) — pay-to-win spending / whale spending">) to secure a more pivotal role in Master's research projects, master higher-level knowledge, and prove her own worth.

Still, why had she suddenly undergone such a 180-degree metamorphosis?

Mo Xi grew deeply suspicious.

Deducing the timeline carefully, he realized that the day before Qingyang Qingyu's sudden stylistic shift, she had apparently paid a visit to Villa Master Qingyang's temporary alchemy laboratory. Of course, Mo Xi was not so all-powerful that he could surveil the temporary residence of a Mahayana patriarch. But because Mo Xi managed the Immortals' Bazaar, he routinely tracked the registry of incoming and outgoing personnel, making it child's play to retrieve Qingyang Qingyu's movement logs.

Mo Xi had promptly stormed all the way to Villa Master Qingyang's temporary cave-dwelling to interrogate his Second Senior Brother!

—"Instigate? Whatever makes you say that?"-Qu Qianxing replied coldly.

In truth, he had always held deep disdain for Eldest Senior Brother's and Youngest Junior Brother's petty "scrambles for affection."

In his view, it was only natural for disciples to crave Master's attention. But Guan Ye's approach—studying diligently and generating solid technical output—was the correct, upright, normal method! Even failing that, having minor eccentricities like Ran Deng to draw a little extra pastoral care from Master was tolerable.

But what on earth were Yuwen Xuan and Mo Xi doing all day long?!

[Are you two pets?!]

[You practically want to graft yourselves onto Master's body!]

[Are you both that starved of maternal affection?!]

[If given the chance, would you two actually try to reincarnate into Master's belly so he could give birth to you?!]

Qu Qianxing cast a contemptuous glance at his junior brother:
—"I didn't say a single word to Qingyang Qingyu, nor did I have the slightest intention of instigating anything! But what is so terrible about her wanting to contribute to our Heavenly Spirit Sect? Isn't this precisely what exchange students are for?"

[Second Senior Brother is clearly an exchange student too! And traded away to someone else's house to boot!]

Mo Xi internally roasted him, his face flushing crimson:
—"W-What if Master gets stolen away..."-Noticing his senior brother's frigid glare, Mo Xi hastily reconsidered and pivoted his argument.-"What if she performs too brilliantly, making Master feel that we direct disciples are actually inferior to an outsider? What then?!"

—"Then that naturally proves you aren't working hard enough! If you can't even outcompete an outsider, you only bring shame upon Master!"-Qu Qianxing retorted.

—"Wugu..."-Mo Xi let out a helpless, kitten-like whimper.

[Who on earth said Second Senior Brother's personality resembles Master's?!]

Master was so gentle and considerate, always weighing others' circumstances before acting.

And Second Senior Brother? Aside from endlessly grinding and driving the rat race, he had zero concept of showing empathy for others! He self-righteously took it for granted that because he could accomplish something, everyone else ought to be able to do it too! Standing beside him exerted suffocating pressure!

Mo Xi even suspected that if Master truly ascended one day and let Qu Qianxing take the Sect Master's seat, he would push everyone to full-blown mutiny!

In fact, Mo Xi wanted to mutiny right this second!

Rolling his watery eyes, Mo Xi glowered resentfully around Villa Master Qingyang's temporary residence.

This cave-dwelling dripped with Eternal Villa's signature flair. Even though it was merely a temporary abode established on the seabed of the Abyssal Ocean—slated for abandonment once this period ended, as the vicinity lacked resources or landmarks—it was constructed with breathtaking opulence, resembling a crystalline palace beneath the sea.

At that moment, Mo Xi and Qu Qianxing were seated in the coral gardens of this palace.

Disciples occasionally traversed the distant corridors, but upon noticing someone sitting there—especially upon spotting Qu Qianxing—they respectfully steered clear from afar.

It was identical to life inside the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

When disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect saw Qu Qianxing, they gave him a wide berth as if encountering a terrifying [dean of students](<# "Literal CN: 教导主任 (jiàodǎo zhǔrèn) — school dean of discipline / head of academic affairs, notorious for enforcing strict rules">).

It was abundantly clear that Second Senior Brother was thriving on other people's turf as well.

Quite likely, he had seized total administrative control over someone else's cave-dwelling.

...No, he had definitely taken over their dwelling!

The more Mo Xi pondered it, the more disgruntled he felt.

Yet watching Mo Xi sitting there with puffed cheeks and wide, round eyes, Qu Qianxing felt his heart soften slightly.

At least, compared to the utterly hopeless Eldest Senior Brother, this youngest junior brother still had room for salvation.

Reaching out, he ruffled the boy's hair:
—"Xi-er, stop obsessing over such pointless nonsense all day,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"Think about it: no matter how much Master values her, she's still someone else's child. With Master's temperament, how could he ever possibly abandon you?"

Mo Xi fell silent. Of course he knew Master wasn't that kind of person.

Yet he had always felt uneasy—profoundly uneasy.

He had no idea where this gnawing emotion came from.

In the past, Mo Xi thought it was caused by his lack of personal wealth. But now the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spirit stone veins and the Immortals' Bazaar were both under his control. Theoretically, even if Master were to suddenly ascend tomorrow, he wouldn't be left destitute or helpless. And yet, an instinctive dread lingered in the back of his mind.

...Ah, he had almost let Second Senior Brother derail the conversation!

—"I'm not afraid of Master abandoning me!"-Mo Xi put on a brave front, swatting Qu Qianxing's hand away.-"Quite the opposite! It's just that Master has always taken care of us without us offering any return—we aren't even matching up to an outsider! How could we have the face to stay by Master's side like this...?"

He paused, then suddenly blurted out:
—"Second Senior Brother, you aren't actually planning a marriage alliance with Eternal Villa, are you?"

[—"?!"](<# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing">)

—"I mean, Senior Sister Qingyang is rich and hardworking. If you marry her, Eternal Villa becomes Heavenly Spirit Sect's property, and you won't have to worry about lacking contributions to Master—*Mmph! Mmph!*"-Mo Xi mumbled as Qu Qianxing clamped a hand over his mouth.

—"Cease your nonsense! Don't go tarnishing a young lady's reputation!"-Qu Qianxing scolded sharply.

[...It's clearly the optimal solution! Given Second Senior Brother's utilitarian nature, he is absolutely capable of doing it!]

Mo Xi glared at Qu Qianxing, thinking internally.

—"I would do no such thing! If I used such underhanded methods to deceive a maiden, what would Master think of me?!"-Qu Qianxing defended himself.

[The problem is what Master thinks of you?!]

Still, that was indeed Second Senior Brother's current moral bottom line.

Heaven only knew what trauma Second Senior Brother had suffered in his past, but his current moral baseline was terrifyingly low.

Mo Xi mumbled a couple of muffled whimpers, conceding to Qu Qianxing's point.

—"Besides,"-Qu Qianxing continued,-"why would I need a marriage alliance to make her hand over resources?"

[—"Mmph?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi">)

Mo Xi's eyes widened once more.

—"That young lady is surprisingly competitive. In truth, I did nothing at all—I merely flashed her a smile,"-Qu Qianxing finally explained.-"Who knew she would actually misunderstand?"

[That was definitely not a misunderstanding!]

Mo Xi thought.

—"Then again, it's hardly surprising. Just as I strive to perform impeccably here so as not to bring shame upon Master, that young miss doesn't wish to lose face for Eternal Villa either. It is what Master calls 'healthy competition,'"-Qu Qianxing remarked.

[How in the world does this count as healthy competition?!]

Mo Xi wanted to scoff.

Yet he watched as his Second Senior Brother sighed, casting down his cicada-wing lashes.

When a sharp, striking beauty revealed a sorrowful expression, it was always heart-wrenching.

Mo Xi felt that it would be even better if his senior brother actually released him, rather than pinning him securely against his chest with one hand clamping his mouth and the other throttling his neck.

Qu Qianxing had no intention of altering his posture, continuing calmly:
—"Still, I am rather sorry toward that young lady, for victory shall certainly belong to my side. Even without Master's explicit orders, looking at the True Immortal's Blood alone, it is something beneficial to ascension. For Master's sake, I must obtain it at all costs!"

[—"Mmph mmph?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi">)

—"It's just that Eternal Villa is excessively extravagant. Many of the ingredients they require to refine that drop of blood are unheard of,"-Qu Qianxing noted.

[—"Mmph?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi — 'Our Heavenly Spirit Sect doesn't have them either?'">)

—"We do not,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

Heaven only knew how Qu Qianxing deciphered Mo Xi's muffled groans.

Qu Qianxing explained:
—"Unlike Master's pragmatism, this belongs to the so-called heritage of cultivation clans."-At this point, Qu Qianxing let out a cold sneer.-"They amass countless expensive yet utterly impractical rare materials, many of which have mediocre efficacy and are simply costly for the sake of being costly. Although I believe many can be substituted, we lack sufficient blood for repeated trial and error. To ensure absolute precision at this stage, we must follow their recipe to the letter."

—"Conveniently, that young lady has a fierce competitive streak, does she not? She wants to achieve the absolute best, doesn't she? Then let us indulge her!"-Qu Qianxing instructed.-"I hear she wishes to study the 'Secret Realm Guidance System.' That sector in our sect happens to be under your purview. There is no need to withhold data from her, but in exchange—as her tuition fee—find a way to make her deliver all these materials into our hands."

Saying this, Qu Qianxing finally released Mo Xi and shoved a densely packed catalog of materials into Mo Xi's hands.

Mo Xi stood utterly dumbfounded.

[So you came to someone else's cave-dwelling, not only learning their secret techniques for refining immortal blood, but you're even making them supply all the raw materials?!]

[...And this is what you call 'not deceiving a maiden'?!]

Mo Xi felt that a person like Second Senior Brother deserved to remain a bachelor for all eternity—he could never be let loose into the world to harm innocent girls!

---

Qu Qianxing did not know what Mo Xi was internally roasting him for.

Even if he knew, he wouldn't care.

In any case, he had long resolved that for that person to achieve ascension, he was willing to do anything.

And only that person was worthy of ascending—

Qu Qianxing believed this to his very core. After seeing Mo Xi off, he knitted his brows and walked toward the subterranean fire chamber.

*—For a mere temporary dwelling, Villa Master Qingyang actually channeled subterranean fire all the way through the seabed! Eternal Villa is truly that absurdly extravagant!*

Having abandoned all hope of roasting these abhorrent rich people, Qu Qianxing stepped straight into the earth-fire chamber.

At this moment, the earth-fire chamber was heavily guarded by seals and immortal servants, and that drop of "True Immortal's Blood" was stored right here.

Several disciples at or above the Void Refinement stage were working inside the chamber. Seeing Qu Qianxing enter with only Golden Core cultivation, they merely displayed respectful expressions, none making any move to bar him.

Some even stepped back a few paces so Qu Qianxing could better observe the True Immortal's Blood.

Qu Qianxing turned his gaze toward the center of the earth-fire chamber.

It was merely a single drop of blood, yet it was bound by nine thick golden chains within a massive bronze alchemy furnace. Hovering around it were concentric circles of dancing talismans—ninety-nine layers of restrictive seals.

Only through this could the True Immortal's Blood be prevented from escaping.

And yet, despite being sealed so securely, the eerie reality was that whenever anyone looked upon that heavily restricted furnace, they could distinctly perceive that drop of True Immortal's Blood.

From a human perspective, it did not resemble blood at all.

While the core of this True Immortal's Blood was a crimson solid, it possessed the characteristics of a fluid, morphing into various shapes beneath the influence of potent spiritual energy, with golden glyphic striations covering its surface.

If one scrutinized those golden patterns closely, they appeared like some arcane script or mystical iconography. Gazing upon them, one felt on the verge of comprehending some cosmic truth, yet simultaneously understood nothing at all—until one's entire mind descended into chaotic turmoil, as if a thick, elongated probe were thrust through one's temple, violently churning one's brain.

According to Qu Qianxing's observations, even the Mahayana-stage Villa Master Qingyang was not immune.

It was simply that cultivators with greater spiritual power and higher cultivation could endure its influence slightly longer.

The sole exception seemed to be the Untiring Immortal Venerable alone.

When this drop of blood rested in Master's palm, Qu Qianxing had never felt it required such stringent confinement. Master had casually rolled it across his fingers like a plaything, and back then, Qu Qianxing hadn't even realized it was True Immortal's Blood, assuming it was merely a pretty pebble; naturally, he had suffered zero psychic backlash.

*Indeed, only Master is worthy of this blood!*

*Only Master is fit to ascend!*

Qu Qianxing thought.

Gazing intently at that drop of True Immortal's Blood, the fervor in his eyes burned even hotter.

---

**Heavenly Spirit Sect — Zhou Hui's Cave-Dwelling**

—"So, this is the report Qianxing authored?"-Zhou Hui remarked with paternal gratification, taking the report from Villa Master Qingyang's hands and browsing through it.

This was the Phase One report of the experiment.

Although the scheduled review date had not yet arrived, Villa Master Qingyang figured that since it had been compiled, he might as well let the financial backer (even though this backer hadn't actually contributed a single spirit stone) look over their findings.

To be sure, this preliminary phase couldn't boast any monumental breakthroughs yet, but Qu Qianxing's performance had been nothing short of dazzling.

Just as Zhou Hui had anticipated, Qu Qianxing's learning aptitude was more than sufficient to participate in the refinement of high-tier materials. Even his cultivation stage was no obstacle, for his innate talent and past experiences had accrued him ample resilience and immunity to coordinate everything seamlessly.

Yet compared to the quiet pride of Zhou Hui, the genuine master, Villa Master Qingyang wore a bitter smile.

Having interacted with him over such an extended period, Villa Master Qingyang had witnessed Qu Qianxing's true capability firsthand, and thus no longer possessed the brash confidence to assert that Qu Qianxing couldn't master it:
—"I have suffered a colossal loss this time! A truly catastrophic loss! For a Golden Core cultivator to grasp the refinement process to such an extent is virtually handing our Qingyang Clan's ancestral secrets to an outsider! My Lord Immortal Venerable, you ought to have at least warned me beforehand that this disciple of yours possesses the [Talent of Wenqu](<# "Literal CN: 文曲之资 (wénqū zhī zī) — innate destiny and talent aligned with the Wenqu Star, governing supreme scholarship, intellect, and imperial examination mastery">)!"

—"Oh? If I had told you in advance, would you have refused to teach him?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Well, not quite,"-Villa Master Qingyang blinked, shaking his head.-"I always assumed that the 'Talent of Wenqu' was only useful for mortal imperial examinations, and that once the [Celestial Path](<# "Literal CN: 通天之路 (tōngtiān zhī lù) — Path to Heaven / Stairway to Heaven connecting the mortal and immortal realms">) was severed, the capacity of scholar's qi to resonate with celestial stars was extinguished, rendering it impossible to cultivate!"

Speaking up to this point, Villa Master Qingyang muttered to himself:
—"Indeed, the Talent of Wenqu was [heaven feeding one with a golden spoon](<# "Literal CN: 老天爷赏饭吃 (lǎotiānyé shǎng fàn chī) — bestowed with innate genius by the heavens">). Even without cultivating, so long as one accumulated boundless merit, one could ascend smoothly... But that was ancient history. In this day and age, shouldn't those with the Talent of Wenqu have their path to ascension utterly severed?"

—"...If you wish to view it that way, you may,"-Zhou Hui nodded hesitantly.

Personally, he put little stock in such claims.

Cultivation was fundamentally about defying heaven to alter one's destiny—why dwell on one's predetermined fate? One could hardly claim that the Wenqu Star was originally destined to ascend, but because its destiny was altered, ascension became impossible.

Still, Villa Master Qingyang's remarks reminded Zhou Hui of something:

*Could Qianxing be aware of this pointless myth?*

Moreover, when Zhou Hui first took in Qianxing, the youth's circumstances were unlike any of his other disciples.

Zhou Hui knew little of the specifics behind Qu Qianxing's original ordeal, only that a "great demon" (as Qu Qianxing called it) had coerced mortals into offering him as a sacrifice.

Yet Qu Qianxing had not passively waited for someone to rescue him like Guan Ye, Mo Bei, or Mo Xi. While researching and constructing irrigation aqueducts, that boy had casually researched ancient mortal paths of ascension on the side—do not ask why studying civil irrigation canals led him to learn so much tangential lore; the answer was simply that his learning ability was formidable, comprehending all principles once one was unlocked. From who-knows-where, he had unearthed an archaic sacrificial rite, incinerating his own flesh and blood in an attempt to drag that "great demon" down with him in mutual destruction.

Alas, Qu Qianxing ultimately lacked sufficient understanding of the cultivation world and the demon race (?). His spellcraft lacked power, merely wounding the "great demon" and causing it to flee. Meanwhile, that sacrificial rite failed to connect with the long-lost ancient deities, but was intercepted by Zhou Hui instead.

Intrigued by who could possibly enact such an ancient ritual, Zhou Hui had answered Qu Qianxing's summons.

At the beginning, Qu Qianxing did not trust Zhou Hui in the slightest.

Employing nearly deceitful methods, he gradually learned from Zhou Hui how to treat injuries, how to cultivate immortality, and how to harness his spirit roots. Finally, relying on clues provided by Zhou Hui, he traversed countless hardships at the bottom of the Qi Condensation stage to reach the newly founded Heavenly Spirit Sect. Mingling among the low-tier disciples, he observed Zhou Hui for a long time before formally kneeling to become his disciple.

Zhou Hui had always considered this disciple to be the one with the strongest independent mind.

Yet Qu Qianxing had remained utterly obsessed with Zhou Hui's ascension, doing every manner of evil deed across previous lifetimes just to push Zhou Hui to ascend.

Looking at it now, that boy was perhaps "someone who knew too much."

In antiquity, when relations between the cultivation realm and the True Immortal Realm were harmonious, nearly anyone could ascend.

Even mortals without spirit roots harbored the potential for ascension, to say nothing of beings blessed with special abilities like the "Talent of Wenqu"—who had an even easier path than ordinary cultivators with subpar roots.

Yet once the [Stairway to Heaven](<# "Literal CN: 登天梯 (dēng tiāntī) — the mythological Stairway to Heaven / Celestial Ladder connecting mortal earth to the celestial realm">) vanished, everything faded into myth. The cultivation world struggled merely to look after itself, while mortal realms spawned myriad folklore and superstitions.

Considering that Qu Qianxing had mastered even such an ancient ritual and possessed the sheer resolve to sacrifice himself, he might truly have believed, based on those legends, that he was incapable of ascending. Having pinned his hopes onto his master, Zhou Hui could finally comprehend why Qianxing had exhibited such manic obsession with his ascension in the past: convinced he could never ascend himself, he hoped his master would fulfill that dream in his stead!

*Goodness, one often hears of parents [wishing for their son to become a dragon](<# "Literal CN: 望子成龙 (wàng zǐ chéng lóng) — traditional Chinese idiom for hoping one's offspring achieves greatness">), but rarely does one see a disciple [wishing for his master to become a dragon!](<# "Literal CN: 望师成龙 (wàng shī chéng lóng) — humorous inversion: a disciple desperately pushing his master to achieve legendary success/ascension">)*

Zhou Hui shook his head, remarking to Villa Master Qingyang:
—"My second disciple has adequate learning capacity, but his personality is somewhat prone to [drilling into ox horns](<# "Literal CN: 钻牛角尖 (zuān niújiǎojiān) — obsessing over dead-ends / hyper-fixating on stubborn extremes">) and going to extremes for his goals. I must trouble you to bear with him."

—"Haha! Having such a brilliant disciple, I am green with envy before I can even complain!"-Villa Master Qingyang replied.

The two engaged in a round of [polite mutual business flattery](<# "Literal CN: 商业互吹 (shāngyè hùchuī) — modern Chinese internet slang for mutual back-scratching flattery between peers">).

Zhou Hui then asked:
—"That being the case, do you still permit Qu Qianxing to continue his studies?"

Previously, it was Villa Master Qingyang who had insisted on taking Qu Qianxing before Zhou Hui consented. Now that the other party had seen through Qu Qianxing's "true colors," leaving his disciple to continue studying their clan's true lineage secrets would be somewhat improper.

Besides, given Qu Qianxing's terrifying learning rate, it was hard to say just how much of their knowledge he had already absorbed.

Fortunately, Villa Master Qingyang showed no anger or anxiety over trade secrets leaking.

*—Had it been a truly classified secret manual that could never be exposed to outsiders, he would never have invited Qu Qianxing from the start, let alone grandly proclaimed that 'he may learn as much as he can.'*

In truth, research into True Immortal's Blood was a relatively niche discipline within Eternal Villa. After all, even Eternal Villa hadn't procured a single drop of immortal blood in thousands of years; everything was based on ancient texts, occasionally substituting weird beasts at best. Thus, their research teetered on the brink of being lost. Compared to having their True Immortal blood studies fade into oblivion, securing the assistance of a brilliant helper to boost efficiency—and gaining the backing of the titan behind him if necessary—was far more vital.

—"I do not mind letting him continue his studies,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.-"However, the old issue remains: his cultivation stage is simply not high enough. That is his bottleneck... Of course, I wouldn't dare claim he cannot learn it anymore. With Qianxing-er's terrifying aptitude, he will certainly learn it and master it thoroughly. But that is precisely what is most terrifying!"

At this point, Villa Master Qingyang put away his playful demeanor, looking solemnly at Zhou Hui:
—"Yet as you know, sometimes 'knowledge' is far more perilous than 'ignorance.' It is precisely because he can comprehend it that trouble will brew."

—"Mmm..."-Zhou Hui let out a soft sigh.

Indeed, given Qianxing's temperament, it was precisely because he knew too much that he meddled in so many superfluous schemes.

Zhou Hui pondered for a moment, then parted his lips and exhaled a single drop of blood.

At the sight, Villa Master Qingyang's eyes went wide.

The higher a cultivator climbed in realm, the less likely they were to bleed. Consequently, the rarity of a high-stage powerhouse's blood was virtually comparable to that of a True Immortal.

Such blood could only be obtained through voluntary offering, and was almost invariably [essence heart-blood](<# "Literal CN: 心头血 (xīntóuxuè) — the most concentrated vital essence drawn directly from a cultivator's heart, carrying their soul imprint and life force">).

—"Villa Master Qingyang, may I trouble you with a request?"-Having exhaled this drop of blood, Zhou Hui appeared entirely unaffected, merely remarking calmly,-"Have this drop of blood incorporated as one of the seals over the True Immortal's Blood... Ah, and do not let Qianxing know."

Villa Master Qingyang understood Zhou Hui's intention in a flash.

This drop of blood was intended to neutralize the lethal offensive aura of the True Immortal's Blood; in moments of dire crisis, it would serve as Qu Qianxing's protective umbrella.

—"...Since you are willing to go to such lengths for your disciple,"-Villa Master Qingyang smiled,-"how could I possibly refuse?" After all, this protective umbrella would shield him in an emergency as well.

Still, he hadn't imagined the Untiring Immortal Venerable would sacrifice this much for a disciple.

*Was it because Qu Qianxing was his chosen successor?*

Thinking of this, Villa Master Qingyang's heart warmed again:
—"Speaking of which, since Qianxing-er cannot ascend, doesn't that make him an ideal match for our Eternal Villa? You have met our Qingyu as well; don't you think she and Qianxing-er make a splendid couple—"

—"...By the way, Excellency Qingyang,"-Zhou Hui smiled, smoothly cutting him off.

He held no personal objection to the match, but Zhou Hui had no intention of playing matchmaker. It required Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu to harbor mutual interest first, and from what he could see, neither showed the slightest inclination—especially his second disciple.

Changing the subject, Zhou Hui said:
—"My Qianxing has learned your clan's core inheritance, and it would weigh on my conscience to do nothing in return. Therefore, do you have any requests? In exchange, I shall certainly fulfill it."

This was the second time Zhou Hui had offered this.

The first time, Villa Master Qingyang assumed Qu Qianxing would never learn it and brushed it aside. But now that he knew Qu Qianxing could master it, he had no reason to be bashful.

Glancing at that drop of essence heart-blood, even knowing what Qu Qianxing meant to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Villa Master Qingyang did not artificially inflate his price. After pondering carefully, he replied:

—"Is that child of mine not studying the 'Secret Realm Guidance System'? I never expected she would be willing to learn that, but it fits my expectations perfectly. In fact, our Eternal Villa has..."

Slowly, he laid out his request.
