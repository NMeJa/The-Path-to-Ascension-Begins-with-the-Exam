# Chapter 62: A Pleasant Journey (第62章 愉快的旅行)

Of course, claiming that certain apparatuses and experimental instruments were unsuitable for storage pouches was merely an excuse.

With Zhou Hui's divine powers, never mind these instruments, even teleporting the entire Mount Tianling to Blood Demon Hall and back again would take no more than an instant.

In fact, Zhou Hui had originally planned to do precisely that.

It was just that while he was checking the luggage manifest and confirming what to bring for this outing, that old fox spirit Lan Pei strolled past, eyeing the mountain of gear with an indescribable expression.

—"Are those preparations for a holiday?"-Lan Pei asked.-"Anyone who didn't know better would think you were setting off to excavate ancient archdemon ruins!"

"..." [I *am* going out to do research!]

Zhou Hui wanted to answer just like that.

Yet Lan Pei rattled on with his commentary:
—"You've been like this since ancient times—always in such a rush, flashing instantly from one place to another. What joy is there in that? The scenery along the road is far more interesting. You set that Kunpeng loose, yet haven't even ridden it once yourself, have you?"

Zhou Hui mulled it over and found it quite reasonable:
—"Indeed. Now that I have retired, I ought to squander time more lavishly."

And so, he instructed his disciples to move several experimental instruments directly aboard the Kunpeng.

Whether something had gotten lost in transmission was anyone's guess, but when Zhou Hui and the others arrived before the Kunpeng, they discovered a "coronet" woven of coral adorning the behemoth's head.

Stepping into the interior of the coral coronet, it turned out to be an exquisite little traveling palace! Not only had it been laid out according to Zhou Hui's preferences with a study, a meditation chamber, and an alchemy chamber, it even featured a bubbling little spiritual energy fountain.

—"Master, this is a water circulation artifact I found in the texts you provided. It maintains the flow of the spiritual spring, turning stagnant water into living currents,"-Guan Ye reported.-"However, the purification artifact's efficacy isn't quite satisfactory, and the auxiliary spirit vein attached is rather small—likely only sufficient for you to wash your hands. I shall continue refining it in the future."

—"Master, please sit over here,"-Ran Deng called out, placing a plush throw pillow beside the railing of the study at the far end of the spiritual fountain.

Because this was perched upon the Kunpeng's back, the entire area was enveloped in a protective array, forming a sturdy bubble of air. While sealing out seawater to provide ample oxygen for mortals, it also mounted robust defense against predatory sea beasts.

Beneath this protective dome, Ran Deng had boldly opted for an open-air pavilion design. The view from the study was particularly magnificent—as Zhou Hui would unquestionably spend the longest hours here. It sat right beside the coral garden featuring the spiritual fountain, bordered only by a low balustrade like a circlet of jewels draped across the Kunpeng's brow. To prevent obstructing the panoramic vista, the bookshelves were designed at low heights so one could reach any volume while seated. Sitting in this study, whether gazing upward or looking ahead, one enjoyed an unobstructed, breathtaking view of the oceanic realm. If one grew weary of admiring the scenery or reading, one could even feed the schools of multicolored fish swimming over from the spiritual fountain.

As for the squishy, plush cushions scattered about the study, that was purely Ran Deng's personal indulgence.

—"This spot is the coziest,"-Ran Deng said, steering Zhou Hui to sit before the low desk.

Across the desk, Lu Shulong lit calming incense and served freshly steeped tea:
—"Eldest Senior Brother didn't accompany us this time, and my craft cannot compare to his. I hope Master will forgive any shortcomings."

Saying so, he laid out over a dozen plates of tea pastries—sweet and savory alike, with not a single repeated variety. Furthermore, their styling echoed the miniature coral garden, sculpted with such exquisite artistry that one could hardly bear to swallow them.

Shen Qingqian silently observed Zhou Hui's disciples, then cast a sideways glance at her own disciples.

—"Master, one must possess self-awareness,"-Bai Wubin said.

[Are you implying I don't deserve this?!]

Shen Qingqian raised her brows in indignation, but then looked at the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and then down at the leviathan Kunpeng beneath their feet.

All right, she truly didn't deserve it.

* * *

Setting aside Shen Qingqian's sour envy, Zhou Hui, on the whole, was exceedingly satisfied with this "relaxation trip."

—"Lan Pei spoke the truth. The scenery along the road is indeed important!"-Zhou Hui remarked, gazing into the distance.

At this moment, the Kunpeng was swimming directly above an abyssal trench in the deep sea. Situated near the center of the continental plate, this region was heavily tainted by demonic qi. Even the deep-sea jellyfish capable of devouring demonic qi could not survive under such noxious density. To mortal eyes, there was only pitch-black void; and even through the unobstructed divine vision of a Tribulation Transcendence sovereign, it was merely a dead, barren wasteland, devoid of anything remotely resembling scenery.

Yet inside the study, whether Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples or Blood Demon Hall disciples, everyone was bustling in a frenzy.

Some were inflating measurement balloons; some were maneuvering these balloons to dive through the ocean currents; some were reeling them back in to log the readings; while others were cataloging data and crunching formulas.

Ran Deng and Guan Ye were buried in calculation sheets when Guan Ye suddenly reported:
—"Master, the demonic qi here differs significantly from the ambient spiritual qi values captured by the gauges aboard the Kunpeng. The demonic qi density is 3.67 times the baseline."

—"It must be emanating from beneath the trench,"-Zhou Hui said.

Zhou Hui swept his divine sense down into the abyss and noted that the demonic qi concentration was indeed astonishing. But he had stepped out this time merely to "travel," not to hunt down root causes—if he stopped to investigate every anomaly along the path, even he would likely take decades to reach Blood Demon Hall.

Zhou Hui picked up a luminescent colored orb and tossed it outward:
—"Shulong, deploy the measuring apparatus to the designated coordinates."

—"Yes, Master,"-Lu Shulong replied.

A shimmering silver-white dragon darted past the Kunpeng's flank, bounding down into the abyssal trench after the glowing sphere like an eager giant puppy.

Zhou Hui didn't watch further, merely taking the data parchment Guan Ye had just filled out, pulling over the nautical chart, and marking it up. At that moment, Ran Deng spoke up again:
—"Master, the demonic qi in this sector seems anomalous as well."

"..." Zhou Hui scanned it with his divine sense, reached for another glowing orb, and called out out of habit:
—"Shulong..."

He paused; evidently, Lu Shulong wouldn't return so quickly.

—"Immortal Venerable, let me go!"-Shen Qingqian offered.

Guan Ye and Ran Deng both looked up, exchanging astonished glances.

Especially Ran Deng.

Discovering that this body cultivator who had run over to Heavenly Spirit Sect for odd jobs was actually a Body Integration powerhouse and the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall had already stunned her; she never imagined this great sovereign would actively volunteer to run errands like Fifth Junior Brother.

—"Haha, well, idling about is rather boring anyway,"-Shen Qingqian explained.-"Besides, I've long wanted to test myself against the dragon race—to see whether their innate spiritual physiques are superior, or if the body tempered by my cultivation technique is sturdier. The deep sea happens to be an exceptional breakthrough environment."

Hearing her say this, Bai Wubin, who was inflating measurement balloons, popped a bubble with a loud snap.

He knew full well his master was spouting pure nonsense.

Yet academic atmospheres are contagious.

Even with such a breathtaking coral garden, from the moment they boarded the Kunpeng, Heavenly Spirit Sect's disciples had been endlessly busy, making Blood Demon Hall's disciples look like an incompetent bunch of [deadweights](# "Literal CN: 渣渣 (zhāzhā) — dregs, deadweights, absolute trash"). As a result, before anyone realized it, all of them had pitched in to assist.

In such an atmosphere, even if Shen Qingqian had previously glared at her disciples with the gaze of ['look at other people's disciples,'](# "Literal CN: 看看别人家的孩子 — classic Chinese parenting trope of unfavorably comparing one's own children to the prodigies next door") now that everyone was working furiously, she had become the sole idler. Every disciple's passing glance seemed to scream *'look at other people's masters,'* leaving her unable to sit still for another second.

Even so, forcing Shen Qingqian to calculate data alongside Zhou Hui was far too cruel for a body cultivator. Shen Qingqian would much rather swim with the jellyfish!

In fact, that was precisely what she chose to do.

Hearing this bizarre request, Zhou Hui was actually quite pleased:
—"That is a splendid research topic. Fellow Daoist Shen, I look forward to reading your analytical report comparing the two in the future."

Shen Qingqian's expression froze instantly.

She hadn't interacted with Heavenly Spirit Sect for very long, but she already understood crystal clear what a so-called "analytical report" entailed.

Still, sitting in Zhou Hui's vicinity was visibly even more excruciating, so she fled the Kunpeng's back at top speed:
—"All right, I'm heading out then."

—"Fellow Daoist Shen, you haven't taken the measuring apparatus,"-Zhou Hui called after her, tossing the luminescent orb out.

Thus, the disciples of Blood Demon Hall experienced an unprecedentedly "enriching" journey. Especially on the return trip, they felt they had crunched more arithmetic problems under Zhou Hui's watch than in the entire first halves of their lives. Had their cultivation not been insufficient, they would have loved nothing more than to soak in the deep sea alongside Shen Qingqian.

Meanwhile, Zhou Hui acquired a far more comprehensive nautical chart.

—"You lot, take this nautical chart and conduct a comparative analysis against both the previous survey chart generated by the Kunpeng and my initial drafted chart,"-Zhou Hui instructed his direct disciples.-"Choose your comparative angles yourselves. In [ten days](# "Literal CN: 一旬 (yī xún) — a ten-day period in the traditional lunar calendar"), I want to see your reports."

[—"Understood."](# "Speakers: Ran Deng, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong")

Ran Deng and the others replied with practiced resignation.

Even Ran Deng had known before departing that she couldn't escape the fate of writing reports.

With that expectation set in advance, it wasn't quite as agonizing.

Besides, she was actually rather intrigued by Blood Demon Hall!

* * *

Blood Demon Hall.

True to its name.

This palace was not only situated within a rift in the Archdemon Ruins, but built directly inside the colossal skeleton of an ancient demon fiend.

It was a blood-red skeleton.

Although completely stripped of flesh and viscera, it remained ferocious and terrifying enough to induce nightmares. Particularly the skull: its anterior half was wedged into the mouth of the spatial rift—or rather, it would be more accurate to say that this demon beast had forcefully smashed open a spatial rift using its own head.

Behind the demon's skull, the back of the head appeared to have been completely chewed apart. Blood Demon Hall had constructed a watchtower and Shen Qingqian's immortal cave atop it. Following down the cervical spine were a series of functional halls, extending to the shattered ribcage, where the ribs and the disciple dormitories built between them enclosed an open plaza.

The Black Coral tree gifted by Zhou Hui was planted right in this plaza.

—"Rather drab, isn't it? Nowhere near as beautiful as Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Shen Qingqian said with a bashful look as she noticed Zhou Hui and his disciples inspecting her sect.-"Fortunately, thanks to the Immortal Bazaar this time, I purchased plenty of coral seeds. Once we plant them, it will become vibrant and multicolored."

[No matter how multicolored it gets, it doesn't change the fact that this is a giant skeleton!]

Guan Ye thought to himself.

He pictured those blood-red ribs covered in multicolored coral, and instantly felt his stomach twist into knots.

—"A wonderful idea!"-Ran Deng praised enthusiastically.-"However, I suggest planting the coral seeds at your dock down in the Abyss Sea. Here, I feel blood-red climbing roses would leave a far more striking impression."

—"I think so too! Unfortunately, we've tried planting vegetation here before, but nothing survived,"-Fu Ning, a disciple of Blood Demon Hall, lamented.

—"It must be because the demonic qi is too potent,"-Ran Deng said.-"At any rate, Master will surely test the demonic qi levels shortly. Our Fifth Junior Brother has cultivated several plant species capable of thriving in demonic qi; you can give them a try."

—"That would be wonderful!"-the Blood Demon Hall disciples chimed in excitedly, chattering lively about which flowers would look prettiest.

Lu Shulong showed a helpless smile at the side:
—"Blood roses! If they need to climb vines, that will take quite some effort. Speaking of which, the orientation of these bones is somewhat bizarre."

He curiously inspected the skeleton and remarked:
—"Was this skeleton left behind from the Archdemon War? Judging by the trauma, it appeared to be fleeing pursuit from some spiritual beast, attempting to tear through space to escape. But if that were the case, it should have been rushing from outside the spatial rift inward, rather than fleeing outward."

—"Now that you mention it, that's true!"-Ran Deng wondered.-"What could have caused this?"

Unfortunately, Blood Demon Hall had no answer either.

—"I'd like to know the reason as well!"-Shen Qingqian chuckled.-"I stumbled upon this spatial rift years ago when I tumbled down from the upper Archdemon Ruins. The skeleton was already here back then. I don't know why it's facing this way either—just as you said, it looked as though it were being chased by something terrifying, forced to tear open a spatial rift to flee for its life, only for its lower half to be bitten off by something."

—"Yet that seems rather improbable,"-Lu Shulong said, looking behind the skeleton.

Indeed, the skeleton's spine broke off just below the ribcage, and a towering mountain—actually not excessively tall, only about twice the height of the demon's raised skull—completely blocked the rear of this rift.

Moreover, this mountain was remarkably vertical, like a natural rampart. If an enemy had pounced down from the summit, smashing the demon's skull might have been plausible, but biting off its lower body would be rather difficult.

—"Could this mountain be the petrified remains of the demon's own body?"-Guan Ye joined the speculation.

—"Its ass is higher than its head?!"-Lu Shulong exclaimed in astonishment.

—"Not necessarily the buttocks. Think about it: perhaps its torso was snapped in two, so this mountain is actually the demon's leg?"-Guan Ye deduced in all seriousness.

—"That posture is even weirder, all right?!"-Ran Deng retorted.

—"Dying in a posture like that is worse than being eaten whole!"-Lu Shulong chimed in.

The other Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples began whispering among themselves as well.

This was practically a hallmark of Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples: having been repeatedly tormented by Zhou Hui, they had developed a reflex of getting to the very bottom of every strange phenomenon they encountered.

Their heated academic debate left the Blood Demon Hall disciples exchanging bewildered glances.

Everyone had lived here for so long; while they had occasionally wondered about it, they had never analyzed it so rigorously. After all, to the disciples of Blood Demon Hall, they had long since grown accustomed to it.

Shen Qingqian, however, felt a sudden chill.

Watching this bunch debate, why did it look suspiciously identical to what happened on the Kunpeng just now?!

To avoid repeating that nightmare, Shen Qingqian resolved to let Zhou Hui swiftly conclude his inspection, lest further complications arise.

—"Immortal Venerable,"-Shen Qingqian spoke up,-"the Flame Crystal Flowers grow upon this mountain. Following up along this slope, one can see two caves that connect within. Whichever cave you enter, you will see where the Flame Crystal Flowers grow."

Zhou Hui seemed to have sensed something long ago. He hadn't paid much attention to the skeleton, gazing at the mountain behind it all along.

Now that Shen Qingqian told him the Flame Crystal Flowers were on the mountain, Zhou Hui showed no rush to proceed. Instead, he asked:
—"...Is there something amiss with this mountain?"

—"As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, seeing through it with a single glance,"-Shen Qingqian confessed with a bitter smile.-"You must have noticed. The reason I would rather carve immortal caves into a skeleton than touch this mountain is naturally not out of some bizarre personal kink, but because this mountain 'eats people.'"

—"Whether finished structures or temporary campsites on the mountain, everything faces the risk of vanishing without a trace. Once I confirmed this, I had no choice but to build Blood Demon Hall inside the skeleton,"-Shen Qingqian sighed.-"If the environmental aura here were not so ideally suited for my sect's cultivation, I wouldn't have chosen this place for our sect grounds at all."

—"I see. No wonder it's so cramped over here by the skeleton, yet you leave the space on the mountain untouched,"-Ran Deng murmured.

Ran Deng murmured, oblivious to the strange looks from the Blood Demon Hall disciples:

[Master, that's completely different from what you usually tell us!]

[You clearly always bragged that you founded the sect here because you thought this skeleton looked so badass!]

[You even smugly christened it 'Blood Demon Hall'!]

[And later, when you realized the name invited massive misunderstandings in the outside world, you still refused to change it!]

The Blood Demon Hall disciples roasted frantically in their hearts.

They watched speechlessly as Shen Qingqian, adopting an unprecedentedly delicate, helpless posture, addressed Zhou Hui:
—"Immortal Venerable, perhaps... you could assist us?"

After all, with such a terrifying titan present, not taking advantage of his presence would be doing oneself a disservice.

Zhou Hui gazed at the back mountain and gave a subtle nod:
—"This mountain is indeed a problem."

—"Then does Immortal Venerable intend to..."-Shen Qingqian began. [Move it away? Or destroy it?]

[Still, the Flame Crystal Flowers are on it, so destroying it is out of the question, right?]

Shen Qingqian felt her demands were modest; she merely wanted to shift this mountain backward by several hundred meters. She had originally intended to do this herself, but having attained the Body Integration stage, theoretically she could heave ordinary mountain peaks with ease, yet this mountain was extraordinarily bizarre—she couldn't budge it by even half an inch.

Shen Qingqian widened her eyes at Zhou Hui, eager to witness how a Tribulation Transcendence titan worked his magic, hoping to learn a thing or two.

Zhou Hui, however, turned to look at his three disciples.

The reason he had brought these three along—Ran Deng, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong—was that Blood Demon Hall wanted running tap water (X) spiritual spring plumbing installed, so Ran Deng and Guan Ye came as engineers, while Lu Shulong served as a contingency weapon. Considering that the divine brand on Yuwen Xuan was related to ancient "gods," a true dragon could handle many unexpected situations.

As it turned out, this happened to be the most convenient combination.

—"It seems the plumbing project will have to wait,"-Zhou Hui said.-"Ah-Ran, lay down the formations first. Encircle the perimeter with three layers of defensive arrays, then set up a spatial array just in case. Ye-zi, don't take out the surveying gear yet; first bring out that hypnosis artifact you forged last time..."

—"Immortal Venerable, what are you...?"-Shen Qingqian asked, the corners of her mouth twitching.

—"Better safe than sorry,"-Zhou Hui replied.-"To prevent any unforeseen accidents when contacting this mountain, laying down formations first is the proper procedure."

[...Is this truly your operational style?!]

[Why was it that when beating up Carefree Palace, you rolled up your sleeves and jumped straight in?!]

Shen Qingqian found it utterly incomprehensible.

* * *

—"I want to go out with Master so badly, aah!"-Mo Xi wailed, slumping over the corridor railing at Mount Tianling and gazing up at the sky.

For the first time, he regretted choosing commerce. If he had chosen formations like Eldest Senior Sister, wouldn't he have been qualified to be taken along by Master?!

Qu Qianxing emerged carrying bamboo slips and rapped him on the head.

—"Stop slacking off. The auction may be over, but even more post-event cleanup is waiting for us!"

—"Besides, precisely because Master is away, we must safeguard the Heavenly Spirit Sect even better on his behalf! Otherwise, what if he returns only to find we can accomplish nothing without him?"-Qu Qianxing added.

Mo Xi rolled his sluggish, cat-like eyes toward Qu Qianxing.

—"Get up, quickly!"-Qu Qianxing urged, exasperated by his half-dead demeanor.-"Today, we still have to sign the relevant contracts with the villages affiliating with us. Do you intend to let those mortals see you like this?"

Speaking up to here, Qu Qianxing sighed:
—"Honestly speaking, how much karmic merit can those mortals possibly return to Master? Even though Master Lan Pei claimed as much, will it truly have any effect?"

[...Never mind karmic merit, it's mountains of spirit stones!]

[Those mortals are the very bedrock of the Immortal Bazaar! Second Senior Brother, what on earth have you been reading lately?!]

Mo Xi thought to himself.

He suddenly felt a surge of nervous tension.

Among the four people currently remaining in the sect, Eldest Senior Brother and his silly older sister were visibly useless—in fact, it was a blessing if they didn't actively make things worse. Second Senior Brother was far too rigid. When all was said and done, the only one capable of flawlessly handling the aftermath of the auction and Immortal Bazaar was him, Xi'er.

This Heavenly Spirit Sect couldn't function without Xi'er for even a single day!

—"Understood,"-Mo Xi said, straightening up.-"I'll go right..."

As he spoke, he tilted his head up in bewilderment to glance at the sky.

—"What is it?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Nothing. When I was sitting here just now, it was clearly bright and sunny. Why did it suddenly turn overcast?"-Mo Xi murmured, glancing at the back of his hand. He had just felt a droplet of water land on it.

Yet when he focused his eyes to look, there was nothing.

Mo Xi flicked his hand:
—"All right, let's go meet our 'collaborators'!"

* * *

Qiao Linggen sat in the reception hall of Heavenly Spirit Sect with a heart filled with four parts delight, three parts nervousness, and two parts unease.

To this day, he still felt as though he were dreaming.

Not only had the white sugar produced by Qiao Family Village caught the favor of Heavenly Spirit Sect's little fairy—rumor had it she was Heavenly Spirit Sect's Youngest Junior Sister, though Qiao Linggen didn't know how to address her, so he defaulted to the respectful term for a noble young lady of the cultivation world—selling out completely, but they had also become their "long-term collaborator."

What filled Qiao Linggen with trepidation was that when he first heard the words "long-term collaborator," he had actually hesitated, showing a shocking lack of appreciation.

Because he had heard that a village two doors down had been duped by a cultivation clan in precisely that manner: they planted excessive amounts of crops their own village had no use for, only for the other party to tear up the agreement, leaving the harvest unsold and resulting in the entire village's farmland being seized by that cultivation clan.

Yet that fairy truly was an immortal fairy. Taking no offense, she told him to consider it carefully and take a good look around this bazaar, deferring discussions until after the auction concluded.

And so, Qiao Linggen took a thorough look at this bazaar.

He discovered he was by no means the sole lucky soul. Many low-tier cultivators like himself, and even mortals, and practically worthless settlements like their village, had found their own fortunes at the Immortal Bazaar.

From the explosive sales of cakes and pastries, Qiao Linggen could even firmly conclude that even if Heavenly Spirit Sect didn't sign a long-term contract, the white sugar they produced in the future would never lack buyers.

Conversely, the greatest pressure came from their local cultivation clans.

Such as the Zheng Clan.

It was said that during the days Qiao Linggen visited the Immortal Bazaar, domestic servants of the Zheng Clan had come to Qiao Family Village to stir up trouble, demanding they sell their land to the Zheng Clan. Qiao Linggen returned with spirit stones from selling white sugar and slapped their faces hard.

After that, the Zheng Clan didn't bring up purchasing land again, but their servants didn't leave either. Villagers saw them lingering outside the village perimeter, up to no good.

Qiao Linggen exchanged information with friends he met at the Immortal Bazaar and discovered this was not unique to his village, but the reality for most settlements around Fengqing City.

In fact, villages in similar circumstances around other immortal cities faced the exact same predicament.

Qiao Linggen keenly realized at once:

Those cultivation clans likely no longer cared about keeping up appearances.

Previously, they hadn't resorted to direct violence partly because a single clan doing so would damage their reputation, and these villages had no backing anyway—even without direct violence, they possessed countless means to coerce mortal villagers into submission, so why do something on the surface that compromised their refinement?

However, the Immortal Bazaar gave everyone an opportunity, allowing them a way to survive without depending on cultivation clans. Those clans couldn't sit still anymore. In the worst-case scenario, they might very well resort to open violence.

At this juncture, Heavenly Spirit Sect proposed the "Partner Plan," declaring they would protect all "partners" willing to do business at the Immortal Bazaar.

This time, Qiao Linggen did not hesitate.

Decisively, even impulsively, he submitted a [pledge of allegiance](# "Literal CN: 投名状 (tóumíngzhuàng) — a blood oath or token of submission traditionally required to join an outlaw brotherhood or sovereign lord") to Heavenly Spirit Sect, willing to become their subordinate village.

Or rather, [vassals](# "Literal CN: 眷属 (juànshǔ) — subordinate kin or vassal dependents under a demon king's domain").

This was common practice among demon villages in the Southern Domain: in regions where no mortal empire could form, settlements submitted to greater demon kings to ensure safety, handing over the entire village's [life stones](# "Literal CN: 命石 (mìng shí) — soul-bound stones that hold the life essence or spiritual tether of a clan, allowing their lord to control or execute them at will") as collateral.

The Central Domain originally had no such custom, but because it could never form a unified mortal dynasty, several villages adopted measures similar to the demon race toward nearby immortal cities.

Qiao Family Village, possessing half-demon bloodlines, naturally shared similar traditions. However, for various reasons, they had always remained skeptical of the City Lord of Fengqing City and refused to do so. Yet everything at the Immortal Bazaar had completely won over Qiao Linggen, prompting him to take this risky gamble.

—Of course, Qiao Linggen also knew that even if he was willing to submit, Heavenly Spirit Sect might not be willing to accept them.

Fortunately, Heavenly Spirit Sect did not look down on them.

They even promised that upon signing the contract, fulfilling mutual commercial cooperation would suffice. Heavenly Spirit Sect did not require their partners to surrender life stones, yet promised to protect them.

Evidently, many shared Qiao Linggen's thinking.

Within just one day of Qiao Linggen making this decision, dozens of merchant households made similar choices. And now, sitting inside the Heavenly Spirit Sect, were several hundred people—who knew how many villages like Qiao Family Village they represented.

Looking at the crowd, Qiao Linggen even felt a trace of worry: Could Heavenly Spirit Sect truly protect all of them?

Immediately after, he shook his head.

To be capable of hosting an auction of that magnitude, he shouldn't doubt Heavenly Spirit Sect's capabilities!

Just as Qiao Linggen was thinking uneasily, a dazzling, striking figure stepped into the reception hall.

Immortal Qu.

The host of Heavenly Spirit Sect's auction.

Rumors said he was likely the next Sect Leader of Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Qiao Linggen didn't know if the rumor was true, but for some reason, looking at that gorgeous face, he felt an inexplicable dread.

Qiao Linggen wasn't alone in this sentiment; upon seeing Qu Qianxing enter, the entire reception hall instantly fell dead silent. It was only when Mo Xi behind him offered everyone a warm smile that the tension eased slightly.

Qu Qianxing had never had the habit of wasting words. He announced coldly:
—"We invited everyone today to discuss the contracts—"

*BOOM——!*

Accompanied by Qu Qianxing's words, a colossal roar of thunder suddenly exploded outside!

Startled by the thunder, Qiao Linggen shuddered violently and instinctively looked outside Huaixu Hall. He saw lightning flashing and thunder crashing across the heavens, carrying the terrifying momentum of a heavenly tribulation!

[What is happening?]

[Clearly it was bright and sunny not long ago! Why did it suddenly turn into this?]

Even mortals could tell something was dreadfully wrong. The crowd inside Huaixu Hall couldn't help showing expressions of dread, murmuring in agitated turmoil.

At that moment, someone cried out in panic:
[—"An o-omen of disaster!"](# "Speaker: Unknown Cultivator")

[—"The heavens are opposing this!"](# "Speaker: Unknown Cultivator")
