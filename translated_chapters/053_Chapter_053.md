# Chapter 53: To Each According to Their Needs (第53章 各取所需)

Naturally, not everyone was scheming to stir up trouble.

Bai Wubin, the Eldest Disciple of Blood Demon Hall, did not linger after paying his respects to the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Flying at top speed for a mere twenty-eight [shichen](# "A traditional Chinese unit of time equal to two hours; twenty-eight shichen equals fifty-six hours"), he returned to Blood Demon Hall. After reporting the entire sequence of events to the Sect Leader, he planted the coral tree that very afternoon—several days faster than the Thousand Harmony Gang, who were located considerably closer to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Furthermore, not a single soul within Blood Demon Hall looked upon this tree with suspicious eyes. They merely offered objective evaluations:

[—"So pretty!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"I've been saying for ages that our sect ought to plant some flowers and greenery."](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Ordinary flowers and plants couldn't possibly survive here, could they? Besides, coral isn't even flora!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"I wonder if we could ask the Heavenly Spirit Sect for a few more corals to plant?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

A cluster of female cultivators huddled around the coral, chattering and twittering excitedly.

Completely contrary to the ominous name "Blood Demon Hall" and the worldly impression of "body cultivators," aside from Bai Wubin and a handful of muscle-bound men who fit the public stereotype of body refinement, eight or nine out of every ten members in Blood Demon Hall were actually girls! What was more, though their looks were not aloof and ethereal like those of the cultivation realm's famous all-female sects, neither did they pursue male aesthetics by packing on bulging muscle until they were [hulking and burly](# "Literal CN: 三大五粗 (sāndà-wǔcū) — thick-waisted, broad-shouldered, and burly"). They appeared merely as a group of ordinarily healthy, ordinarily vibrant girls who harbored ordinary affections for beautiful things—such as right now, thoroughly smitten with the black coral tree their "Senior Brother Bai" had brought back.

And this tree was indeed exquisite.

Although dubbed "Black Coral," only its skeletal core was black; its outer mantle was pristine white. It possessed a single main trunk standing nine meters tall, its slender branches spreading out in bilateral symmetry like a colossal white feather.

Were this "feather" bathed in sunlight, it might have been merely ordinarily pretty. Yet Blood Demon Hall was situated in a spatial rift along the periphery of the ancient God-Demon ruins. Day or night, its surroundings were perpetually enveloped in the absolute darkness of the void. Thus, this "white feather" radiated a soft, translucent white glow against the pitch-black backdrop, just as it had in its deep-sea homeland.

Amidst the eerie, desolate ruins of gods and demons, this tree was a rare beacon of immaculate beauty.

Consequently, the moment this coral tree was planted, the disciples of Blood Demon Hall swarmed around to marvel at it.

Quite a few disciples rushed back from their duties specifically to see the tree, shoving Bai Wubin and the other tree-planters straight into the corner.

In the end, it took two coughs from Shen Qingqian, the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall:

—"All right, settle down. This tree isn't meant for sightseeing; it directly affects our cultivation resources for the coming year. Let your senior brother come forward and explain its purpose."

Only then did the disciples of Blood Demon Hall cease their chatter and part to make a path.

Bai Wubin did not move from where he stood, addressing his junior sisters from the back:

—"This tree is likely a miniature teleportation formation capable of sending us aboard the Kunpeng bound for the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

As he spoke, he gave another detailed report of his trip to Mount Tianling.

Upon hearing of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Kunpeng, the disciples of Blood Demon Hall gasped in wonder once again.

—"So when we go to the auction this year, we get to ride a Kunpeng?"-a disciple asked excitedly.

—"Indeed. The Heavenly Spirit Sect explicitly stated they would dispatch the Kunpeng to pick us up,"-Bai Wubin said.

—"Wow!"-the disciple exclaimed in delight:—"I want to go!"

[—"I want to go too!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Sign me up!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

The disciples clamored to sign up, eyeing one another with competitive glints:

[—"Looks like the competition this year is going to be fierce!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

In contrast, the few male disciples present appeared remarkably serene. In any case, their quotas were guaranteed.

It was not that Blood Demon Hall favored men over women. Rather, the name "Blood Demon Hall" was already sensational enough; if outsiders discovered this sect was comprised almost entirely of female cultivators, it would likely spawn even more outrageous rumors (which had indeed occurred in the past). Thus, out of concern for sect security, external affairs were predominantly handled by male disciples like Bai Wubin who matched public perceptions. This resulted in relatively few travel quotas for female disciples. Yet female disciples vastly outnumbered males in Blood Demon Hall, meaning whenever an event like an auction arose that offered massive shopping sprees, the competition was always cutthroat.

Fierce enough that every year, they had to fight [martial arena tourneys](# "Literal CN: 打擂台 (dǎ lèitái) — fighting on raised tournament platforms to duel for victory or contested slots") to settle it.

And hearing that this year they could travel by Kunpeng, everyone grew all the more ecstatic.

Although the quotas had yet to be decided, everyone harbored countless questions about riding the Kunpeng:

[—"When will the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Kunpeng arrive?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Can something that huge even enter our space?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"When the time comes, will we just be teleported onto the Kunpeng's back?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Won't doing that expose our sect's coordinates?!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

Bai Wubin answered patiently:

—"It will not expose our sect's location. Supposedly, the coral here is actually a projection of the coral in the Abyss Sea. When the time comes, we will board the Kunpeng as it swims through the Abyss Sea beneath us, rather than traveling through the skies above."

—"Hold on. Traveling from beneath the Abyss Sea?"-Shen Qingqian, the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall, asked in astonishment.

—"Yes... Master, when I was explaining earlier, were you not listening at all?"-Bai Wubin said helplessly.

—"You rambled on and on with a mountain of boring details—who would have the patience to listen?!"-Shen Qingqian said dismissively.

Besides, she wasn't afraid of exposing the sect's location in the first place.

After all, Blood Demon Hall kept its location confidential and never invited outside guests solely because they hated strange rumors slandering their disciples. From a purely defensive standpoint, this spatial rift was easy to defend and perilous to attack; once the grand protective array was activated, even a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator couldn't dream of barging in. And from the belligerent perspective of body cultivators, Shen Qingqian had actually always wanted to experience firsthand how a sect siege battle played out.

Therefore, once Bai Wubin explained the coral tree's purpose, she had simply told him to plant it right in the main courtyard, where it formed a rather lovely landscape feature. She was even contemplating planting a few more.

Bai Wubin looked at his master with deep resignation.

He knew his master couldn't be bothered to ponder overly complicated matters. And it wasn't just his master; influenced by their body cultivation techniques, most body cultivators possessed straightforward, blunt temperaments.

Bai Wubin abandoned verbal explanations and decided to let everyone experience it directly.

—"In that case, let's simply head down and take a look,"-he said.

—"Eh? We can go down right now?"-Shen Qingqian exclaimed in surprise.

Bai Wubin didn't explain immediately. Instead, he reached out toward the lunar halo at the crown of the white-feather coral tree and made a dial-turning motion. Instantly, the ground began to glow.

This was far clearer than what had been seen at the Thousand Harmony Gang.

Because this pocket space lacked a sun to begin with, the sundial patterns etched across the ground radiated a sharper, clearer white light. The sundial face was divided into several concentric rings, the innermost ring split into two halves: one marked "Surface" (`表`), the other marked "Inner" (`里`).

Bai Wubin's nudge moved a tiny pointer from "Surface" over to "Inner."

Accompanying this adjustment, everyone felt the ground shudder violently. Amidst the startled, flustered shrieks of the Blood Demon Hall disciples, the earth suddenly flipped upside down, sending everyone's world spinning topsy-turvy.

When they sensed their surroundings stabilizing once more, they discovered that nothing remained unchanged before them except the white coral tree. Blood Demon Hall had completely vanished. In its place, undulating sand dunes resembling the deep seabed stretched before their eyes, while overhead, scattered deep-sea fish drifted leisurely past.

—This was their subjective perception. From a third-person perspective, their orientation had undergone a full 180-degree inversion, standing in an inverted posture upon the seabed of the Abyss Sea.

Yes, the Abyss Sea.

The geography of the cultivation world adhered to the classic cosmological model of "Heaven Round, Earth Square." The Central Domain, for instance, rested upon a massive square continental plate. And if one flipped this continental plate upside down, beneath it lay the Abyss Sea.

When cultivators spoke of the "Five Domains and Six Abysses," the "Six Abysses" referred to the oceans beneath the five continental plates, plus the "Sixth Abyss Sea" visible beyond the outermost edges of the landmasses. However, supreme powerhouses had verified that these "Six Abysses" were actually interconnected into a single colossal ocean. It was rather the continental plates that rested upon varying planes above this Abyss Sea, separated without direct overland roads, traversable only via teleportation formations or natural spatial rifts.

And the cultivators of Blood Demon Hall had just been transported to the Abyss Sea beneath the Central Domain:

Naturally, the earth had not literally flipped upside down, nor was this spot directly beneath Blood Demon Hall. The spell just now had merely projected a psychological suggestion of inversion into their minds, preventing a sudden disruption in perceived gravitational direction from placing unnecessary stress on their physical bodies.

—"I see. So the teleportation still sends us to a fixed coordinate, rather than directly onto the Kunpeng's back,"-Shen Qingqian murmured.

As a Body Integration powerhouse, while she disliked convoluted thinking, that didn't mean she was dense.

Besides, when one gave it proper thought, teleporting directly onto a moving object—especially a Kunpeng flying at breakneck speed—was far too perilous. Yet if one sought a fixed landing site on the mainland, there might not even be terrain capable of accommodating a Kunpeng's descent. At least within a thousand-li radius of Blood Demon Hall, there was no such space. Not only did they lack a landing zone for a Kunpeng, they didn't even have a harbor large enough for grand cloud-carriages, forcing them to trek immense distances every year just to haul back necessary supplies.

If landing on the ground was impossible, the standard convention was to anchor coordinates in midair.

Yet the sky offered no footing; constructing corresponding floating islands required legions of cultivators, mountains of spirit stones, and astronomical materials. Beyond mega-sects, no one could shoulder such costs. One could hardly expect the Heavenly Spirit Sect to foot the entire bill alone! No matter how many mineral veins the Untiring Immortal Venerable possessed, it wouldn't be enough!

Thus, the seabed was an ingenious alternative.

All one needed was an anchored coordinate and a water-repelling enchantment centered around it, and passengers could be teleported without concern.

The reason other cultivators had never conceived such a "simple" solution was purely that cultivators understood next to nothing about the Abyss Sea. Nor did they possess the spiritual reserves to dive beneath the Abyss Sea for extended voyages, let alone access to teleportation points leading directly back above the continental plates. Naturally, they never looked downward.

For the Kunpeng, however, no such issues existed.

The creature was naturally amphibious in sea and sky!

—"Still, that Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Shen Qingqian mused:—"Does he possess a chart of the Abyss Sea floor? How could he map coordinates across the entire domain?"

Shen Qingqian swiftly abandoned the thought.

Things like maps were closely guarded strategic secrets in major sects. It wouldn't be surprising in the least for a Tribulation Transcendence titan to hold such treasures in hand; racking her brains wouldn't yield an answer anyway.

Shen Qingqian confirmed with Bai Wubin:

—"We just wait for the Kunpeng here?"

—"Indeed,"-Bai Wubin nodded:-"Not only that, they also provided blueprint designs, stating that if we are willing, we can construct a dedicated ferry dock here to await the Kunpeng's arrival. Shall we build it?"

—"Build it! Why wouldn't we?!"-Shen Qingqian's eyes widened.

If not for the Untiring Immortal Venerable's supreme divine powers, ordinary people like them would never have had the chance to be teleported into the depths of the Abyss Sea. Now that they possessed secure coordinates, building a dock meant Blood Demon Hall would have an undersea outpost! In the future, even if they couldn't ride the Kunpeng, sending disciples down to hunt a few deep-sea beasts for trial expeditions would be fantastic!

This was practically gaining a brand-new secret realm!

Just as Shen Qingqian was daydreaming, the disciples curiously scanning their surroundings erupted into a commotion:

[—"A light?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"There's light in that direction!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Something is drifting over!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"What is that? So gorgeous! It looks just like a necklace!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"I don't know, I've never been to the ocean either. Maybe... a fish?"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"No, not fish—those are jellyfish!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

Amidst the disciples' twittering chatter, Shen Qingqian stared wide-eyed as jellyfish radiating ethereal luminescent light linked into long, shimmering chains, drifting gracefully overhead.

The Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall promptly declared with immense delight:

—"Forget just a dock! We can completely build an undersea vacation palace right here!"

—"Master, I misunderstood you! You're so good to me,"-Yuwen Xuan said, deeply moved.

He too was currently standing before a massive expanse of glowing jellyfish. However, unlike the swooning admiration of the female cultivators in Blood Demon Hall, his face was awash with voracious appetite as he swallowed hard.

Hearing this, Zhou Hui gave him a deeply suspicious glare:

—"These are not edible."

—"I know! Of course I wouldn't eat them; it's not like I'll eat anything that moves!"-Yuwen Xuan protested, aggrieved:—"But my A-Da will!"

Just as he spoke, a colossal black silhouette swept past high above, instantly vacuuming up more than half the jellyfish swarm.

Yuwen Xuan gazed at the sweeping shadow with a drooling, covetous expression, purring in satisfaction:

—"A-Da eats way too much. But since he's emergency food rations, feeding him too much premium grub is a terrible return on investment. I was just fretting over how to raise him! Look at him—he's been [starved skinny](# "Literal CN: 饿瘦 (è shòu) — becoming thin from hunger; hilarious comedic irony when applied to a planet-sized Kunpeng")!"

*Starved skinny?!*

Everyone turned their heads toward the colossal shadow.

The humans here weren't even as large as a single fish scale on the Kunpeng, and in the murky depths of the seabed, one couldn't even glimpse its full silhouette. God only knew where Yuwen Xuan managed to see that a Kunpeng could look "starved skinny."

Yuwen Xuan chortled gleefully:

—"If I'd known Master had such a jellyfish farm, I wouldn't have stressed at all!"

His tone sounded for all the world like a man discovering his deceased father had left him a private gold mine.

Qu Qianxing glared at him, then looked with concern toward the jellyfish.

The jellyfish before them were far more numerous than those near Blood Demon Hall. Every variety imaginable was present, floating in orderly, stratified layers throughout the water column—at a glance, unmistakably not a natural occurrence.

Qu Qianxing asked worriedly:

—"Master must have expended considerable effort here, right? Is it truly all right to let the Kunpeng eat them?"

—"These jellyfish are actually nothing rare; they are ubiquitous beneath the Central Domain's Abyss Sea,"-Zhou Hui said unconcernedly.

He casually gave the disciples a brief lecture on the ecology of the Abyss Sea:

—"You are familiar with the God-Demon War tens of thousands of years ago, correct? Not only was the Stairway to Heaven shattered, but a colossal crater was smashed straight through the center of the Central Domain, causing the Abyss Sea beneath—that is, the waters here—to pour upward, forming a bottomless abyss. Demonic qi from above also leaked down through that fissure, causing mass extinction among the organisms in the Central Domain's Abyss Sea. The few that barely survived mutated into demonic beasts. Only a single creature remained unaffected."

—"These jellyfish?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Correct. Their physiological structure is far too rudimentary. Aside from a few mutated, gigantic varieties—which I suspect might be an evolutionary branch—they are virtually incapable of undergoing demonic transformation,"-Zhou Hui explained in detail.-"Ah, though saying they are 'unaffected' isn't entirely accurate. They feed on demonic qi to multiply, which caused them to proliferate throughout the Abyss Sea. Some even drifted past the perimeter barrier of the Central Domain, becoming an invasive plague across the Sixth Sea."

Zhou Hui paused here, casting a disappointed glance over the jellyfish farm:

—"When I first discovered this trait, I assumed they possessed the ability to purify demonic qi, so I cultured a batch for experimentation. Regrettably, given their simple biology, however much demonic qi they ingest, they excrete the exact same amount. Utterly useless. And so, I abandoned this facility."

—"Then is it truly safe for the Kunpeng to eat these jellyfish?"-Qu Qianxing asked. After all, these jellyfish were fed on demonic qi, weren't they?

—"It doesn't matter in the slightest! The Kunpeng's mass is astronomical; whether it ingests demonic beasts or spiritual treasures makes zero difference to its gargantuan body,"-Yuwen Xuan announced proudly:—"It's the safest food imaginable!"

[Actually, I'm more curious about which end you'd even start eating such a colossus from.]-Qu Qianxing thought.

Pity that Qu Qianxing wouldn't be witnessing Yuwen Xuan eating the Kunpeng anytime soon—Yuwen Xuan seemingly had no plans to devour this emergency food stash in the near future. Thus, upon discovering that Master was merely "borrowing" the Kunpeng rather than accepting it as an Eighth Junior Brother, and that as compensation for the loan Master would provide feed for this gluttonous behemoth, Yuwen Xuan became instantly thrilled. He had even cheerfully volunteered to serve as the Kunpeng's pilot, responsible for shuttling passengers throughout the auction.

Naturally, for him to be this enthusiastic, he seemed to harbor other calculations.

—"I never expected Master to possess even a seabed map of the Abyss Sea,"-Yuwen Xuan fawned, sounding for all the world like a greedy heir trying to wheedle out more inheritance:—"Master is truly peerless!"

—"Hm? You didn't know? I assumed you had stolen it..."-Zhou Hui paused, looking at Yuwen Xuan in mild surprise before his tone grew stern:—"Then how on earth did you capture the Kunpeng and all those other beasts?"

Yuwen Xuan froze on the spot, chuckling dryly:

—"Master, what are you talking about? I don't follow."

—"The items stored alongside my sea charts—I had always assumed you took them... No, that isn't the point,"-Zhou Hui said sharply:—"If you didn't take them, what methods did you use to capture so many beasts? With your cultivation base, that should have been impossible!"

—"...Haha, guess?"-Yuwen Xuan's eyes darted left and right.

Before Zhou Hui could say another word, with a *swoosh*, he vanished into thin air!

Zhou Hui massaged his temples:

—"That scoundrel..."

—"Don't laugh; not just your disciple, even I'm salivating!"-At that moment, Tao Zhu walked over holding architectural blueprints:—"I truly had no idea you possessed even a chart of the Abyss Sea bed. Untiring, just how many treasures are you hoarding?"

—"This is hardly rare,"-Zhou Hui said dismissively:—"The Central Domain's Abyss Sea didn't suffer the direct impact of the God-Demon War. In fact, the demonic qi wiped out most aquatic life, leaving only the central rift somewhat dangerous. Overall, it's quite safe. As long as one has the patience to slowly walk through it once, one can always draft a rough map."

[...Is that something mere "patience" can accomplish?!]-Tao Zhu thought.

Never mind how astronomical an amount of spiritual power such a journey required—the simple fact that one could not fly across the ocean floor, could not use spells to cut corners, and that walking it would take centuries, meaning several hundred years of total isolation without speaking to a single living soul... That alone was enough to send ordinary cultivators fleeing in terror!

Ah, but this fellow had never been an "ordinary cultivator" to begin with.

Tao Zhu took two deep breaths, willing his swelling belly back down.

—"Forget it, I refuse to discuss your cultivation habits with you. Every time I listen, my stomach aches,"-Tao Zhu grumbled, thrusting the blueprints directly in front of Zhou Hui:—"What is the meaning of this ferry dock design proposal? You aren't swindling my spirit stones, are you?"

Indeed. Boss Tao had ultimately succeeded in investing in the construction of the auction hall and associated facilities.

Thus, their visit here today was not merely to watch the Kunpeng devour jellyfish, but to plan the construction of a grand ferry terminal near this teleportation node.

Naturally, as the primary transit hub for the Heavenly Spirit Sect's auction—and the very first stop for visitors arriving by Kunpeng—it could not be treated lightly. Even Zhou Hui had not carelessly tossed down an ordinary coral to make do; he had selected a colossal coral reef.

This coral reef was so gargantuan that it grew all the way from the ocean floor up to the water's surface. Viewed from the seabed, it resembled nothing less than a [Sea-Calming Needle](# "Literal CN: 定海神针 (Dìnghǎi Shénzhēn) — the legendary pillar that calms the ocean, famous as Sun Wukong's Ruyi Jingu Bang in Journey to the West"). Furthermore, it was stratified in tiers, each layer forming a natural, expansive terrace.

Back then, Zhou Hui had chosen this location to "farm jellyfish" precisely because of its convenience. But utilizing it now as a landing hub for the Kunpeng was undeniably an even superior choice.

Tao Zhu, as the "investor," had inspected it and found it exceptionally pleasing. Yet like all corporate investors, he harbored a few "minor issues" regarding the project's specific arrangements.

Tao Zhu grabbed Zhou Hui and demanded:

—"Partitioning distinct entrances for cultivators is one thing, but why on earth are there mortal entrances, demon tribe entrances, and spirit tribe entrances?!"

—"Squeezing creatures of completely different tiers and species into the same bottleneck would hardly be ideal, would it?"-Zhou Hui answered as if stating the obvious:—"First, the merchandise would become overly cluttered and chaotic, making it difficult for visitors to browse what they desire or locate the services they need. Second, their power levels vary drastically, and some even exist in natural food chains. Shoving them together without protective barriers would inevitably invite unpleasant incidents. Even when my disciple keeps a few small beasts, he knows to house them in separate painted scrolls!"

—"That does sound reasonable... Wait, no! You're actually inviting mortals and demon tribes?!"-Tao Zhu challenged.

—"Is that so strange? Doesn't Carefree Palace's Treasure Fair also decline to restrict race?"

—"While they don't restrict race in name, without an immense stroke of serendipity, ordinary demons can't go, and mortals even less so! Even ordinary cultivators can't make it without extraordinary fortune—of course, if one possesses truly special fortune, they might even encounter someone like you crashing the party,"-Tao Zhu barbed:—"But here in the Central Domain, this is merely an ordinary cultivator trade exchange! What is the point of dragging mortals and demon tribes into this? There's zero profit in it!"

This was the universal consensus across the cultivation world: cultivators looked down on mortals, and looked down on demons just the same.

Zhou Hui had no interest in debating dogmas with someone like Tao Zhu. He merely said:

—"While there may be no immediate profit, there might be delightful discoveries."

—"Delightful discoveries?"

—"Let me ask you, Boss Tao: why do you think everyone yearns to ascend?"-Zhou Hui parried with a question instead of answering.

—"For greater power, naturally! Longer lifespan! Supreme authority!"

—"Incorrect. In my view, it is purely out of boredom."

Tao Zhu sneered:

—"Hey, Little Zhou Hui, now you're looking down on people! While I admit you possess more perseverance than anyone else, when it comes to pursuing higher realms, everyone is identical."

—"Perhaps roughly so,"-Zhou Hui still didn't argue, only continuing calmly:—"Yet you must concede that boredom is the ultimate driving force behind ascension. Beyond Void Refinement, cultivators discover that it becomes nearly impossible to purchase the equipment they desire, impossible to forage suitable herbs, and impossible to make perceptible progress. Instead, as old friends depart one by one, their social circles perpetually shrink. Peers of comparable standing dwindle to a handful. Even combat becomes utterly devoid of flavor—opponents are either bugs crushed with a pinch, or entities impossible to contend with. Even exerting a fraction more power prompts concerns, just as you reproached me the other day, of forging karmic entanglements that bind one inextricably to this world. In the end, us old fogeys have no choice but to find a secluded corner to seal ourselves away, left with no exit save ascension..."

—"Hey, that's enough,"-Tao Zhu waved his hands frantically:—"When even you talk like that, you make me despair of the future! Besides, what does any of this have to do with an auction?!"

—"It's all child's play anyway. Do you truly believe a mortal marketplace, a demon marketplace, and a cultivator marketplace are fundamentally different? Is there any difference in the spirit stones earned?"-Zhou Hui said.-"Since there is no difference, why not expand our sample size? When diverse populations become sufficiently abundant, anomalies inevitably emerge, and the world becomes infinitely more amusing, doesn't it? Or perhaps humans from an exceedingly remote tribe might bring forth treasures you have never laid eyes upon?"

—"Mmh..."-Tao Zhu felt himself teetering on the verge of being persuaded.

—"Of course, this holds immense benefits for me as well,"-Zhou Hui borrowed Lan Pei's sophistry:—"Regardless, the spiritual qi I return to Heaven and Earth makes no distinction between realms, and thus the creatures with whom I establish karmic ties make no distinction between races. Providing them convenience is accumulating merit for myself. I am merely preparing for my ascension!"

Zhou Hui concluded:

—"If you are unwilling, I can easily fund it myself. It's merely a handful of spirit stones, after all!"

—"No, I'm in! I'll invest, all right?!"-Boss Tao conceded.

He always had the nagging feeling Zhou Hui was plotting something monumental; if he didn't buy in, he'd miss out big time.

Having settled that, Tao Zhu's worries shifted in a new direction:

—"Still, fine and dandy as your calculations are, how much can that rabble in the Thousand Harmony Gang actually accomplish for you? Their ranks are crawling with people dragging their feet."

—"No, they are doing exceptionally well,"-Zhou Hui offered a genuinely warm smile:—"Originally, I was concerned they might fail to grasp my intentions. But judging by the coordinates currently lighting up across the map, they are performing far better than I ever anticipated!"

Indeed. The Thousand Harmony Gang was notoriously inefficient at everything else, forever spinning in bureaucratic circles. Yet in the distribution of the Kunpeng's coordinate-anchoring seeds, they displayed astonishing, unprecedented efficiency.

In all likelihood, this was the very first time the Thousand Harmony Gang had ever "worked with one heart and united strength"!

Even if their vectors pointed in diametrically opposite directions.

The supporters of the Tianling Auction distributed coral seeds to established partners—major sects and prominent immortal cities—securing their existing clientele. Meanwhile, the opposition faction busily distributed coral seeds to communities that had never before participated in auctions: low-tier demon tribes, secluded hermit clans, and ordinary mortals, vigorously acquiring new customers. For a brief moment, [their united hearts were like a fortress, and their combined sharpness could cut through metal](# "Literal CN: 众志成城，其利断金 (zhòngzhì-chéngchéng, qí lì duàn jīn) — two classic Chinese idioms combined: 'unity of will is an impregnable fortress' and 'when two people are of one heart, their sharpness can cut metal'; here sarcastically applied to the warring factions accidentally executing a flawless two-pronged market expansion strategy") (X).

Although the sects of the Central Domain certainly included practical pragmatists like Blood Demon Hall—who cared only about obtaining their desired goods and couldn't care less who organized the event—there were also factions that, just as Daoist Huang and his cohort hoped, resolutely boycotted this auction.

Such as Fengqing City, nestled in the southeastern reaches of the Central Domain.

The city possessed high-grade teleportation arrays linking the Central Domain to other continents. Since ancient times, it had served as a vital thoroughfare of commerce, bustling with congregating merchants and cultivators. In previous years, the annual auction was naturally held within its borders.

Regarding the Carefree Palace's trade embargo, the one squeezed hardest was naturally the City Lord of Fengqing City, Feng Lang.

After all, every passing merchant represented taxable revenue!

With Carefree Palace's blockade in place, immense quantities of Central Domain commodities couldn't leave, outside goods couldn't enter, and transaction volumes plummeted catastrophically, causing Feng Lang to shed hair by the fistful.

Yet Feng Lang didn't blame Carefree Palace for blockading the routes; instead, his heart burned with venom toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Had he not lacked the martial might, he might well have taken the initiative to declare war against the Heavenly Spirit Sect and force them to grovel in apology to Carefree Palace.

Thus, the moment Daoist Huang pitched his scheme, Feng Lang leapt at it immediately.

He not only hosted a lavish banquet in Daoist Huang's honor, but also summoned the leading merchant magnates of Fengqing City, instigating them to publicly swear off attending the Tianling Auction. As for the coral seeds they received, they were promptly dumped according to Daoist Huang's plot.

—"Where were those corals sent?"-Daoist Huang asked, drunkenly slurring his words.

Drunk as he was, he remained surprisingly conscientious, remembering to confirm the destination of the coral seeds.

—"Dumped on a few disobedient country bumpkins!"-a prominent clan patriarch from Fengqing City chortled, equally half-sloshed:—"Those [low-tier mixed-blood little demons](# "Literal CN: 低等的混血小妖 (dīděng de hùnxuè xiǎoyāo) — derogatory slur used by arrogant human merchant clans against impoverished demi-human/hybrid beastfolk")! Their soil produces next to nothing, yet they obstinately refused to sell us their worthless plots of land, nor would they cultivate the crops we ordered them to grow! It's high time they suffered a bit of hardship, so they learn that without us, they are nothing but helpless trash!"

[—"Well said!"](# "Speaker: Fengqing City Merchant")

The clan patriarch's declaration drew raucous cheers as wine cups clinked together in boisterous celebration.
