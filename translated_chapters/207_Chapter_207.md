# Chapter 207: Tower (第207章 楼)

—"Demon Sovereign?! Second Senior Brother cultivated demonic arts again?!"

Startled out of her wits by Lu Shulong's news, Mo Bei blurted out the question on pure reflex.

Yet the moment the words left her lips, she noticed her Fifth Senior Brother staring back at her with a look of *just as I suspected*.

—"You've recovered your memories?"-Lu Shulong countered instead, his gaze scrutinizing Mo Bei's countenance,-"How did you regain them?"

Lu Shulong was profoundly astonished.

In his memory, this Sixth Junior Sister had always possessed the 'flimsiest' memory of them all. Reincarnating across so many lifetimes and enduring such horrific tragedies, even with Master deliberately scouring their memories, everyone else retained at least a few residual fragments—save for Mo Bei alone.

This was something Lu Shulong had 'verified' back in the third reincarnation cycle.

Furious that Master had "gifted him away," Lu Shulong had expended immense effort to restore the memories of his martial siblings and incite a bloody civil war among them. That lunatic scheme had actually proven far simpler than he originally envisioned; evidently, none of the other siblings truly wished to forget the past, so with merely a gentle nudge, even the most inscrutable Eldest Senior Brother and the most detached Eldest Senior Sister had recovered their memories to varying degrees.

Only this youngest junior sister had remained completely impervious; no matter what arcane arts, spells, or miraculous pills Lu Shulong deployed, he couldn't coax out a single trace of recollection.

Mo Bei's mind had been so pristine, so utterly scrubbed, that had Lu Shulong not verified through esoteric arts that her divine soul had never altered across lifetimes, he would have suspected whether the little junior sister of each reincarnation was even the same person.

Yet in this lifetime, Mo Bei actually possessed "memories"?!

Lu Shulong found it utterly inconceivable, burning to know what had transpired.

Fortunately, Mo Bei harbored few conniving schemes and never played coy. In her view, her experiences were hardly state secrets; since her senior brother asked, she answered with unvarnished honesty.

Prompted by Lu Shulong's inquiries, Mo Bei recounted the memories she had "recovered" at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree in exhaustive detail.

—"So after I 'died,' so many things actually happened...?"-Lu Shulong murmured mournfully, his frosty, silver-white eyelashes fluttering downward like glistening frost crystals, once again bitterly lamenting that he had bowed out far too early in that past life.

He looked at Mo Bei:

—"In other words, the memories you recovered actually belonged to Mo Xi?"

—"They were fragments from the past-life Mo Xi, so the memories are incomplete,"-Mo Bei nodded, before looking back at Lu Shulong with sudden curiosity,-"Fifth Senior Brother, what about you? How did you deduce that I had 'recovered' memories? No, wait—how did *you* recover your memories?"

—"..."-Lu Shulong averted his gaze in acute guilt.

He was hardly shameless enough to confess that he had remembered everything from the very beginning.

Moreover, despite remembering everything, because he had exited the stage far too prematurely in that crucial cycle, his knowledge was virtually useless.

As Lu Shulong wracked his brain trying to salvage his dignity as an elder senior brother before his junior sister, he paused abruptly, asking Mo Bei:

—"Wait a moment... does that mean Master knows you have memories?"

—"More accurately, I recovered my memories under Master's personal protection,"-even if those memories turned out to be the bitter shame of backstabbing Master.

Mo Bei answered with lingering remorse. Yet she did not permit herself to wallow in sorrow, quickly latching onto the critical pivot in Lu Shulong's question.

Mo Bei asked with a bizarre expression:

—"Fifth Senior Brother... Master doesn't know you recovered your memories, does he?"

—"No, he found out long ago,"-it was precisely because Master had caught him that he had been forced to rush back for the Immortal Arts Assembly.

Lu Shulong grew even more mournful.

He understood now: although both possessed past-cycle memories, his junior sister's situation was light-years apart from his own. One could saunter home for boba milk tea whenever she pleased; the other was teetering on the brink of being strung up from the mountain gate peach tree alongside Eldest Senior Brother!

*Tsk, and here I thought I had caught a piece of leverage!*

*Looks like I can't drag Little Junior Sister into the mud after all.*

Lu Shulong pondered with his melancholic prince-like visage.

Clearing his throat twice, he pivoted his stance entirely:

—"Ahem, Little Junior Sister, regarding what I said just now... pretend you never heard a word!"

—"Starting from which part?"

—"Starting from 'Second Senior Brother became the Demon Sovereign.'"-in truth, Lu Shulong sorely wished Mo Bei could pretend she had never laid eyes on him in the first place.

—"I would love nothing more than to pretend I heard nothing,"-Mo Bei stated deadpan.

She meant every word. Had she lacked past-cycle memories, Mo Bei genuinely would have played deaf and dumb without hesitation; after all, she had zero desire to join this pack of idiotic senior brothers in dangling from the mountain gate. Yet with memories restored, Mo Bei felt a profound sense of protective duty toward her sect and Master; she refused to let her own head-in-the-sand willful ignorance lead to repeating past tragedies.

Particularly this catastrophe of 'Second Senior Brother becoming the Demon Sovereign'—it was blatantly tethered to the crises of past reincarnations.

—"However, considering Second Senior Brother is on the verge of marching upon the Heavenly Realm, do you truly believe keeping everyone in the dark is wise?"-Mo Bei asked,-"Furthermore, what were you originally hoping I would do?"

—"You're willing to help?!"-Lu Shulong's eyes lit up instantly.

His mist-shrouded, silver-blue eyes gazed upon Mo Bei with brimming anticipation; surely no mortal or dragon under heaven could ever say no to such a breathtaking creature.

[The male creatures of our sect are truly...](# "Mo Bei's exasperated inner sigh")

Mo Bei let out an internal scoff, answering noncommittally:

—"Tell me the details first."

Lu Shulong immediately took that as consent.

—There was no helping it; Mo Bei had an impeccable track record. Whenever a favor was asked of her, she rarely refused, and she was famously straightforward (in the eyes of outsiders). Lu Shulong felt his little junior sister would surely lend a hand.

Modulating his tone to better tug at Mo Bei's heartstrings, Lu Shulong began:

—"This entire affair traces back to the moment we arrived in the Demon Realm..."

As it turned out, on the day Qu Qianxing and his companions slipped into a demon metropolis, they stumbled upon the news that the demon race was aggressively drafting an army to invade the Heavenly Realm.

—In truth, this revelation wasn't particularly startling. According to subsequent investigations, the Demon Realm and the Heavenly Realm waged war every single day without pause; conscription was practically a mundane daily routine.

As demons residing in a "remote border province," they had originally possessed zero intention of traveling to the front lines as cannon fodder. Yet the arrival of the 'Lord Demon Sovereign' seemed to have sparked ambitious notions in their minds.

The castellan of that demon citadel had approached Qu Qianxing, inquiring with obsequious deference:

—"We have heard Lord Demon Sovereign has been absent from the Lumbini Holy City for recent years, absent even from the Demon Realm itself. To think Your Lordship would grace our remote hinterlands today... could it be Your Lordship has returned specifically for this grand campaign?"

Qu Qianxing hadn't known how to reply, nor had he needed to. He had merely cast a sidelong glance at the castellan with his peerless, icy eyes—and the demon lord had dropped to his knees, shivering violently in terror.

The castellan had stammered a frantic explanation:

—"Your humble servant would never dare pry into Your Lordship's celestial movements! It is merely that if Lord Demon Sovereign intends to partake in this campaign, your servant is eager to serve as your vanguard, dedicating everything to Your Lordship's glory!"

Naturally, the castellan hadn't fallen madly in love with Qu Qianxing's ethereal beauty at first sight; he was pursuing his own petty calculations:

The clash between demons and the Heavenly Court naturally involved territorial spoils. Yet the demons of this backwater city were weak and lacked influential backers—evident from their exile in the hinterlands; even if they joined the war, spoils would never trickle down to them. Encountering the "Lord Demon Sovereign" changed the entire playing field! Enlisting as the "personal vanguard of the Second Demon Sovereign," even if the Demon Sovereign lifted not a finger, no demon in the realm would dare embezzle their military provisions!

These demons naturally had no intention of letting such a golden opportunity slip away.

Besides, even if rejected, they had nothing to lose.

Qu Qianxing, however, seemed disinclined to refuse:

—"War, is it...?"

[Second Senior Brother, you can't genuinely be contemplating going to war, can you?!](# "Lu Shulong's horrified memory")

Both Lu Shulong and Jian Nuo had stared at Qu Qianxing in absolute terror.

—"It's not that I wish to wage war, but rather to seize this chance to inspect this so-called Holy City,"-Qu Qianxing had explained afterward,-"The demon race's Holy City must be their paramount headquarters, right? Don't you wish to gather intelligence? Furthermore, this 'Second Demon Sovereign' title intrigues me greatly. If the Holy City is too distant and consumes excessive time, we can reconsider our approach."

As it turned out, it consumed zero time.

The spatial mechanics of the Demon Realm were profoundly bizarre: astronomically distant locations could be bridged in a single heartbeat. Particularly the 'Holy City'—in the words of the demon castellan: *"It resides in the depths of every living creature's heart; one may arrive at any moment."* Thus, in the very next instant, while Qu Qianxing's group was merely asking for directions to the Holy City, they found themselves standing right inside it!

From there, Qu Qianxing smoothly infiltrated the palace of the Second Demon Sovereign.

—"...Wait a moment,"-hearing this, Mo Bei couldn't help calling a halt, eyeing Lu Shulong with deep skepticism,-"You infiltrated it just like that? Isn't that far too simple?!"

Lu Shulong chuckled vaguely:

—"Haha, probably because Second Senior Brother looks so remarkably identical to that Demon Sovereign... and I glossed over a few minor details..."

In truth, calling it 'identical' was an understatement!

He was literally him!

Rumors claimed the Second Demon Sovereign detested being served; thus, not a single living creature inhabited his palace. Even the palace's grand gateway was guarded by a singular array cipher that only he alone could unravel.

When Qu Qianxing arrived, he had dismantled the cipher effortlessly.

Because that array cipher... was completely identical to the defensive wards on Qu Qianxing's own immortal cave back at the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

—"Hah, perhaps that Demon Sovereign truly is me,"-having reached that stage, even Qu Qianxing had muttered with dry self-mockery.

Thus, everything following had been dreadfully 'effortless.' Everything inside the palace aligned meticulously with Qu Qianxing's personal living habits, allowing them to retrieve whatever information they desired without breaking a sweat.

Not only within the palace, but among the demon lords 'intimately acquainted' with the Second Demon Sovereign as well.

Although Qu Qianxing was notoriously inept at acting, as long as he operated according to his normal, everyday habits, not a single demon harbored the slightest suspicion.

By the time they came to their senses, Qu Qianxing was already commanding a colossal demon legion stationed on the front lines of the war against the Heavenly Realm!

—"You people realized this far too late,"-Mo Bei complained dryly.

—"Indeed,"-Lu Shulong forced a bitter smile.

What he couldn't easily confess to Mo Bei was that they hadn't been slow to react; rather, as they advanced, they uncovered endless anomalies that seized their rapt attention—mysteries touching not only past reincarnations, but possibly tracing back to their "original lives" before reincarnating into this world, shedding light on why the Heavenly Court was hunting them down and determined to erase the Heavenly Spirit Sect! This made it impossible for Qu Qianxing and his companions to look away.

—"In short, Second Senior Brother is now [riding a tiger, unable to dismount](# "Literal CN: 骑虎难下 (qí hǔ nán xià) — riding a tiger with no way to get off safely; trapped in a dilemma"),"-Lu Shulong skirted the heavier truths,-"No matter how much he 'resembles' the Second Demon Sovereign, he isn't him after all. There is an immense gulf in their raw power levels. The moment he takes the field, that discrepancy will be exposed instantly, inviting catastrophic doom. Yet as the supreme commander, slipping away openly is virtually impossible."

[Does one truly need to step onto the battlefield to realize that?! More to the point, how in the world did you lot manage to bluff your way to the battlefield with your meager cultivation bases?! Is becoming a supreme demon commander really that easy?!](# "Mo Bei's speechlessness")

Mo Bei was thoroughly at a loss for words.

Yet Lu Shulong had felt much the same way.

He sorely wanted to lodge a formal complaint against the demon military's abysmal management standards.

It could be said that the demon army possessed zero administrative oversight and zero organizational hierarchy. Adhering strictly to the law of the jungle and viewing slaughter and pillage as their supreme purpose in life, they lacked all discipline and refused all governance. Merely hoisting the banner of the 'Demon Sovereign,' swarms of millions assembled like locusts, charging headlong to plunder wherever they pleased.

Naturally, such a so-called 'army' was equally prone to catastrophic routs.

Even if Qu Qianxing genuinely were the 'Second Demon Sovereign,' the slightest misstep during combat would cause more than half of the assembled horde to scatter into the winds just as swiftly as they had gathered.

The fact that celestial soldiers and heavenly generals waged endless back-and-forth warfare against such a rabble could only be described as the unique matchmaking algorithm of the Immortal-Demon server!

Lu Shulong sighed:

—"Thus, even without the Immortal Arts Assembly, I was obligated to return."

—"Will returning allow you to rescue Second Senior Brother?"-Mo Bei asked.

Yet looking at her Fifth Senior Brother, he clearly had no intention of petitioning Master for aid.

Not only did he refuse to ask, but he lived in mortal terror of their 'failure' reaching Master's ears.

Mo Bei understood that this was precisely where Lu Shulong wanted her to intervene:

—"So what do you need me to do?"

—"Under the current circumstances, Second Senior Brother actually possesses a Dharma treasure capable of extricating him. It's just that he deemed it an ill omen, so he never carried it on his person. You may have heard of it,"-Lu Shulong explained,-"It is the treasure Second Senior Brother refined after incinerating his mortal enemy—"

—"The Heavenly Robe."

Mo Bei:

—"..."

Lu Shulong noticed Mo Bei's expression had turned exceedingly strange, yet didn't think much of it:

—"That Heavenly Robe can completely sever divine perception. As long as we deliver it to Second Senior Brother, he can slip away from the battlefield with effortless ease,"-Lu Shulong explained.

He clutched his temples in distress:

—"The sole dilemma is that the artifact resides in Second Senior Brother's immortal cave, stuffed into the deepest recesses of his storeroom. Although he instructed me on how to retrieve it, frankly, I truly have no desire to set foot inside his cave. That cursed place is no different from the [Web-Spun Cave of Spider Demons](# "Literal CN: 盘丝洞 (pánsī dòng) — Journey to the West: the spider demons' webbed labyrinth"), choked with deadly mechanisms and mountains of books. Without the owner guiding the way, one can't even find a place to put a foot down."

Particularly since Lu Shulong had only recently finished exploring the "demon lair of the Second Demon Sovereign"; he had zero appetite to repeat that traumatic experience.

Lu Shulong looked pleadingly at Mo Bei:

—"Second Senior Brother also mentioned that if push came to shove, we could ask Eldest Senior Sister for aid. However, I truly don't want Eldest Senior Sister to learn of this. Fortunately, Little Junior Sister, you share an exceptional bond with her, and girls speak more easily among themselves. If it were you, convincing Eldest Senior Sister to retrieve the Heavenly Robe should be elementary—Little Junior Sister?"

—"My apologies, Fifth Senior Brother. It's not that I'm unwilling to help, but rather... you returned far too late,"-Mo Bei stated with an utterly expressionless countenance.

Upon returning to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mo Bei had wondered why Eldest Senior Sister was presiding over the public greeting reception. If she had guessed correctly, it had to be some form of punishment. And indeed, her deduction was spot-on; Mo Bei had subsequently pieced together the entire saga of the 'Heavenly Robe Incident' from Eldest Senior Sister and Fourth Senior Brother.

—Including the part where Eldest Senior Brother transformed into a little black lamb.

Mo Bei felt her emotional affections had been thoroughly swindled.

She cursed Eldest Senior Brother to hang from that peach tree for the rest of his natural life!

Yet discovering another 'fellow victim' right before her eyes offered Mo Bei considerable emotional consolation.

Striving with all her might to squeeze out a tone of heartfelt sympathy, Mo Bei announced:

—"The Heavenly Robe is no longer in Second Senior Brother's cave. It is currently in Master's hands."

***

Master was not at the Heavenly Spirit Sect; he was at White Jade Capital.

Zhou Hui had taken flight toward White Jade Capital immediately after assigning duties—administering discipline—to his disciples that fateful day.

His primary objective was to facilitate deeper research into the array disc.

After all, this array disc hailed from the celestial realm—an entirely unfamiliar domain to Zhou Hui. The standard equipment he routinely wielded was ill-suited for the task; in contrast, White Jade Capital, as a lost city hailing from the ancient era before the severance from the Heavenly Court, preserved numerous celestial-tier refinement and alchemy facilities. Zhou Hui had harbored intense interest in these relics from the very beginning, having expended considerable effort to restore them to workable order, lacking only a suitable high-tier artifact to analyze.

Now, serendipitously acquiring this array disc was akin to [finding a soft pillow just as one grew drowsy](# "Literal CN: 瞌睡遇到了枕头 (kēshuì yùdàole zhěntou) — meeting a pillow when sleepy; a timely blessing").

Dragging the little black lamb into the Dharma Treasure Tower within White Jade Capital, Zhou Hui had eagerly commenced the disassembly of the array disc.

Only the little black sheep suffered.

Not only was it forced to act as Master's research assistant, using its stubby cloven hooves to scribe telemetry logs and manipulate arcane refinement levers, but it was also obligated to fulfill its academic duties as Master's disciple, using its tiny lamb brain to memorize complex theories and wrack its wits over the agonizing mathematical proofs Master assigned.

However, Yuwen Xuan had brought this entirely upon himself.

Over the past half-year, his 'ash flatbread' had largely been digested, and having reclaimed the Taotie's true form, Yuwen Xuan had long since possessed the ability to shift freely between human and beast forms. It was Yuwen Xuan himself who adamantly refused to shift back.

Yuwen Xuan felt that studying was excruciatingly agonizing regardless of whether he assumed human or lamb form. Particularly when knowledge drifted through his mind seeking to leave an imprint; his cerebral cortex was far too impenetrable for anything to stick, meaning the sheer impact of knowledge colliding with his skull induced a concussion-like dizziness that made him want to faint dead away.

Compared to that, a lamb's smaller cranial capacity allowed knowledge to slip straight through and exit faster, concluding the agony much sooner.

*(Zhou Hui: So you have completely abandoned all pretense of studying, haven't you?)*

Furthermore, in the form of a tiny black lamb, he was visibly more pitiable and endearing—completely helpless and bumbling. Perhaps Master would soften his heart—grow thoroughly annoyed—and release him?

Yuwen Xuan harbored such cunning schemes, which explained why a few days later, he had reduced himself to a filthy, limp, squashed little fleece mat, sprawling half-dead right across the threshold of the Dharma Treasure Tower.

When Mo Bei accompanied Guan Ye to White Jade Capital, this was the exact spectacle that greeted her.

Mo Bei had originally been quite fond of the little black lamb, constantly carrying it in her arms. Yet discovering that this creature was Eldest Senior Brother all along had triggered unprecedented psychological trauma—leaving her feeling thoroughly cheated of her heart and body by a deceitful scoundrel.

—"My, look at that. There's a doormat here. Must one clean one's boots before entering?"-Mo Bei murmured with deadpan theatricality.

Without a shred of mercy, she brought her boot down squarely upon the little sheep mat!

—"Ying!"-the sheep mat let out a pathetic squeal.

Simultaneously, however, its body liquefied into a viscous, tar-like mire, resembling the bottomless silt of a primordial swamp, latching onto Mo Bei's boots and dragging her downward! Beneath the black mat, rows upon rows of jagged, razor fangs sprouted in dizzying layers, snapping shut as though primed to tear their prey into ribbons!

Fortunately, Mo Bei's reflexes were razor-sharp. Making a split-second decision, she kicked her boots free, soaring straight into the air! Spiraling gracefully mid-descent, she landed lithely upon Guan Ye's broad shoulders.

Supporting Mo Bei's legs, Guan Ye sighed helplessly at the "sheep blanket"—no, the tooth-lined swamp:

—"Eldest Senior Brother, please cease teasing Little Junior Sister."

—"Ying-humph! She kicked me first!"-the "swamp" grumbled indignantly.

In the next heartbeat, its body expanded rapidly into a colossal puff of black woolly fleece, and Mo Bei's discarded boots popped out from the black fur with a crisp *poof*.

Supporting Mo Bei, Guan Ye retrieved the boots, allowing her to slip them back on.

Meanwhile, the giant black furball seemed to feel that rolling on the floor in front of his junior siblings lacked dignity; shaking its dark fleece, it flipped over, transforming into a handsome young man seated upon the threshold. He wore a face of profound exhaustion, his hair and robes in complete disarray, looking as though he had been trampled by a migrating herd of wild sheep.

—"What did you come here for?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

—"Did I not make an appointment with Master?"-Guan Ye replied,-"Master mentioned he had several conceptual adjustments regarding the Immortal Arts Assembly that require my implementation."

—"No, I'm not asking you; I'm asking her,"-Yuwen Xuan tilted his head, scrutinizing Mo Bei,-"Master didn't summon you, did he?"

—"...Even if Master didn't summon me, can I not come visit?"-Mo Bei retorted stiffly.

—"Naturally, you may. However, if Little Seventh pulled this stunt, it wouldn't be surprising; but for you, it is profoundly bizarre. After all, you rarely ever cling to Master!"

Yuwen Xuan leaned in close to Mo Bei, his nostrils flaring slightly as though scenting the breeze:

—"I smell the scent of an incident brewing!"

—"Eek!"

Leaving her boot half-fastened, Mo Bei twisted around and slid off Guan Ye's shoulder, darting behind his back to peek warily at Yuwen Xuan.

[This guy is exceptionally sharp only when it comes to trouble!]

Mo Bei watched him with extreme vigilance.

Guan Ye remained utterly oblivious.

When Little Junior Sister had asked to accompany him on this visit to Master, Guan Ye had naturally agreed. In truth, Guan Ye had spent recent days cloistered inside the Artifact Refinement Workshop; although he had heard rumors of Lu Shulong's return, he hadn't crossed paths with him yet, and thus had no inkling of what Lu Shulong was scheming.

Regardless, Guan Ye had no desire to provoke Yuwen Xuan; it was far too troublesome and an immense waste of time.

Guan Ye asked directly:

—"Eldest Senior Brother, is it convenient for us to see Master right now?"

—"Otherwise, why would I be lounging here?"-Yuwen Xuan yawned broadly, pushing the grand doors open,-"Since Master instructed you to come, he is naturally awaiting you."

Yet Yuwen Xuan himself showed zero intention of following them inside.

Flashing a bared-teeth grin at Mo Bei, he wheedled:

—"However, Master never said he wished to see Little Junior Sister. Little Sixth, why don't you stay out here and chat with me..."

Before Yuwen Xuan could finish his sentence, Mo Bei's silhouette flashed past like a phantom, vanishing right through the doorway!

She had zero intention of heeding Yuwen Xuan's nonsense about "Master didn't say he wanted to see you."

As long as Master wasn't in closed-door seclusion, he would never turn away any of his disciples.

Yet Mo Bei's lightning-fast agility startled both of her senior brothers; before their eyes could track her movement, their little junior sister had dissolved into thin air.

Gazing at the doorway, Yuwen Xuan rubbed his nose:

—"She made another breakthrough, didn't she?"

At this rate, catching her would become impossible—and eating her was out of the question.

Though Little Junior Sister had never been edible to begin with, so there was no real loss... like hell there wasn't!

Both Little Sixth and Little Seventh had originally been mere wisps of spiritual energy; eating them would have been akin to cotton candy—looking voluminous but failing to fill the belly. Yet recently, they seemed to have solidified, evolving from cotton candy into generous portions of chilled sweet cream cheese, appearing more delectable by the day!

Not being able to eat them was rather tragic.

Furthermore, there was also the unfortunate Fourth Junior Brother, who had suddenly become exceptionally well-suited for simmering bone broth...

Yuwen Xuan stared at Guan Ye with predatory longing.

Oblivious to the horrific recipes brewing in Yuwen Xuan's head, Guan Ye merely sighed with mild exasperation:

—"I told you not to tease Little Junior Sister."

With that, Guan Ye stepped through the doorway in pursuit of Mo Bei.

Naturally, Yuwen Xuan could never genuinely turn Guan Ye into bone broth, nor did he truly wish to enter; leaning back against the doorframe, he let out a mournful wail:

—"I am so hungry..."

***

Stepping through the threshold, Guan Ye discovered that Mo Bei hadn't rushed to Master's side; instead, she was standing frozen near the entrance, staring upward in a daze.

—"Waiting for me?"-he asked.

Mo Bei did not turn around, her chin tilted toward the rafters:

—"Fourth Senior Brother, look above."

—"Above...?!"

Guan Ye lifted his head—and froze completely.

Stretching above their crowns was an infinite starry sky.

A complete, boundless cosmos was draped above them; countless constellations mingled with sprawling nebulae in magnificent grandeur, appearing close enough to touch, yet unimaginably transcendent, stealing their breath away in an instant.

And above this breathtaking expanse of stars, one could faintly perceive the titanic silhouette of a white-robed immortal. He manipulated the stars like go stones on a board; cascades of silver hair spilled over his shoulders, draping across the cosmos like the celestial River of Heaven. His eyes, radiant as the sun and moon, beamed with boundless compassion and warmth. He gazed upon the cosmos, his eyes coming to rest upon the two disciples standing beneath the stars.

—"You have arrived?"-the silver-haired immortal spoke with serene gentleness, extending a celestial palm downward.

From Guan Ye's and Mo Bei's vantage point, that hand eclipsed nearly one-third of the entire cosmic sky. Beneath that palm, they were as minuscule as ants, effortlessly crushed into dust; yet neither felt the slightest urge to dodge. On the contrary, they gazed upon the hand in dazed veneration, willing to be manipulated in whatever manner he decreed.

Naturally, their Master could never harm them.

Guan Ye and Mo Bei felt themselves gently scooped up and set down by that colossal hand. By the time their senses reassembled, they were standing amidst the sea of stars, with their normal-sized Master smiling warmly before them.
