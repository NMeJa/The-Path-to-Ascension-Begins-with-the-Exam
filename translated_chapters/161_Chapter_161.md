# Chapter 161: An Outstanding Youth (第161章 大好青年)

A few days later, when Qu Qianxing rushed back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect from Eternal Villa alongside Lu Shulong and Mo Xi, his ears were greeted by an unbroken chorus of lavish praise for Yuwen Xuan:

[—"As expected of the eldest disciple."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"When you really think about it, reaching Soul Formation in a mere two hundred years—even among pureblood demon clans, that qualifies as a genius among geniuses."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"The reason he kept a low profile before must have been because he was cultivating with painstaking diligence, right?"](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"Naturally! He is the eldest disciple, after all! He must have inherited Immortal Venerable Untiring's tireless grit and perseverance."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

[—"I can hardly wait to see his performance."](# "Speaker: Spectating Cultivator")

Qu Qianxing had listened to these comments all along the journey. Upon finally meeting Guan Ye and Mo Bei, he could no longer hold back from asking:

—"Who on earth are they talking about?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Eldest Senior Brother, I believe?"-Guan Ye replied slowly.

—"They're talking about *our* Eldest Senior Brother?"-Qu Qianxing pressed, seeking verification.

—"It should indeed be our Eldest Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei affirmed.

[—"......"](# "Speaker: Direct Disciples")

The direct disciples present plunged into dead silence.

Whether he resembled Master was another matter entirely—though to Qu Qianxing, even entertaining the comparison felt like supreme blasphemy against Master.

Yet a "painstaking and persevering" Eldest Senior Brother?

If their Eldest Senior Brother ever heard those words, his very first instinct would probably be to swallow that virtue whole before offering his review: [—"Where's the pain? Where's the perseverance? It tastes terrible!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Imagined)")

—"At any rate, this misunderstanding is a blessing,"-Lu Shulong remarked, scanning the expressions of his fellow disciples.-"Compared to being misunderstood, surely none of us wants outsiders discovering what kind of temperament our Eldest Senior Brother actually possesses?"

[—"...Put that way, fair enough."](# "Speaker: Direct Disciples")

The direct disciples exchanged looks and arrived at a sorrowful consensus:

Rather than letting the outside world know that the head disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was *that* kind of monstrosity, it was far better to let everyone thoroughly misunderstand. At least that way, Master's sterling reputation would not be dragged through the mud.

—"It truly is a good thing; even more sects now wish to introduce our Heavenly Spirit Sect's pedagogical methods,"-Mo Xi said, entirely unburdened by psychological qualms as he immediately calculated the practical applications.-"Furthermore, it has drastically heightened public engagement for the Immortal Arts Assembly."

—"Then let us direct the publicity along those lines!"-Qu Qianxing conceded through gritted teeth.

He despised lying.

Yet when weighing 'telling lies' against 'Master's reputation', Master's reputation won out every single time.

—"Eldest Senior Brother gave those exact instructions to the [Daily paper](# "Heavenly Spirit Daily / 天灵日报") himself,"-Guan Ye said.-"He also cautioned them not to overdo the propaganda. After all, his true form and inherent nature are what they are; if exposed down the line, the backlash would be a thousandfold worse. So the publicity should focus primarily on praising Master, without exaggerating him into a saint."

—"Literally nobody wanted to boast about him to begin with!"-Qu Qianxing retorted in rare, agitated exasperation.

There was no helping it. While rationality told him this was indeed the most advantageous course of action, actually thinking about *that* Eldest Senior Brother still left him feeling profoundly aggrieved.

[Painstaking and persevering like Master... On what conceivable grounds?!]

Qu Qianxing drew a deep breath, fighting to calm his swirling emotions.

—"So,"-he inquired,-"where is our 'painstaking and persevering' Eldest Senior Brother right now?"

The others subconsciously shrank back an inch.

—"Well, seeing as Mo Xi hadn't returned, he went to coordinate matters regarding the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-Mo Bei reported.

—"The Immortal Arts Assembly?"-Qu Qianxing echoed.

—"Yes,"-Mo Bei nodded.-"In fact, after Eldest Senior Brother broke through to Void Refinement, the tournament committee received an avalanche of complaints, so he went in person to resolve them."

—"Wait, why on earth would there be complaints?"-Mo Xi asked anxiously.

—"Certain parties felt that since Eldest Senior Brother had broken through to Void Refinement, it was no longer appropriate for him to compete in the Soul Formation bracket,"-Mo Bei explained.

—"...*That's* all they're worried about?"-Qu Qianxing's expression turned exceedingly complicated.

—"Yes, that is quite literally all they are worried about,"-Mo Bei nodded.

Qu Qianxing felt that this world was truly beyond saving.

***

—"Naturally, I shall participate in the Void Refinement bracket,"-Yuwen Xuan declared with an impeccably decorous smile.

Ran Deng sat behind Yuwen Xuan, fighting a massive urge to roll her eyes. Regrettably, not a single person present saw through Yuwen Xuan's true colors; quite a few venerable powerhouses beamed back in warm appreciation.

What was more, Yuwen Xuan followed up with modest deference:

—"To be completely candid, I felt I ought to withdraw from the competition to consolidate my cultivation base,"-Yuwen Xuan continued with humble grace.-"However, Master believes that regardless of winning or losing, I must participate in the Immortal Arts Assembly to accumulate experience. I also believe the true spirit lies in participation. When the time comes, I must ask all senior powerhouses to please show mercy."

Once these remarks circulated, the Soul Formation cultivators naturally breathed a collective sigh of relief, rejoicing that they had dodged a brutal fight against a Void Refinement titan. Meanwhile, even the Void Refinement cultivators who had suddenly gained an extra competitor began harboring petty schemes:

Indeed! No matter how terrifying that heavenly tribulation had been, hadn't this Yuwen Xuan only just broken through?

Judging by his tone, he seemed to have cultivated in perpetual seclusion without ever stepping out into the world for trials. In that case, his real-combat proficiency couldn't possibly be that formidable, right?

Come to think of it, although the Heavenly Spirit Sect's internal edition of Immortals' Chess featured a Yuwen Xuan piece, all of his moves appeared to be artless, brute-force basic attacks. Perhaps his combat parameters had only been dialed up out of deference to his status as the Eldest Senior Brother?

Regardless, defeating Yuwen Xuan meant taking down the first direct disciple of Immortal Venerable Untiring!

Striking while he had just broken through, in his most unsolidified and fragile state—was this not the golden opportunity of a lifetime to establish an illustrious name across the five domains?!

...

A cocktail of stray ambitions coalesced, tempting quite a few cultivators to entertain notions they had no business harboring.

Even higher-stage grandmasters were taken in by Yuwen Xuan's veneer. Only Tao Zhu stared at him with an utterly baffled expression:

[Does this Taotie cub really have this kind of personality?]

Boss Tao had crossed paths with Yuwen Xuan several times, but in his memory, the brat had never been anywhere near this well-behaved, had he?

No—saying he was 'not well-behaved' was an understatement. If anything, how did he recall this fellow being an extraordinarily troublesome menace...?

Tao Zhu squinted suspiciously at Yuwen Xuan.

As if sensing something, Yuwen Xuan lifted his gaze and cast what seemed to be a deeply meaningful look back at Tao Zhu.

Tao Zhu instantly felt the golden toad inside his belly thrash violently, slamming so hard against his stomach lining that it nearly made him double over in pain. Ever since he had formed a symbiotic bond with this golden toad, he had never felt the creature this terrified.

Afterward, Tao Zhu could not resist grumbling to Jiuzhai:

—"That Yuwen Xuan is definitely a bit abnormal, isn't he?"-Tao Zhu asked.

—"Is that so? You two shared the karmic bond of master and disciple in one lifetime, you know,"-Jiuzhai remarked offhandedly.-"You even passed the Abyssal Ocean Society down for him to inherit."

—"What?! Have I lost my bloody mind?!"-Tao Zhu cried out in disbelief.

Jiuzhai lifted a single eyelid:

—"Yet across countless timelines, that lifetime saw the Abyssal Ocean Society expand the fastest and grow into the most gargantuan force, even standing toe-to-toe with the combined alliance of the Northern Domain's Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City."

—"I'm heading over right now to demand Untiring hand that Taotie cub over to me!"-Tao Zhu blurted out.

He actually took two strides toward the door before abruptly freezing in his tracks. He turned around and eyed Jiuzhai suspiciously:

—"You aren't pulling my leg, are you? Did you truly witness such a past?"-Tao Zhu asked.

—"If you ask whether I saw it with my own eyes, of course I didn't. When all is said and done, despite both of us being Tribulation Transcendence, my dharmic power falls far short of Untiring's. Therefore, even though I know time has wound back across several lifetimes, I cannot retain memories of the past,"-Ancestor Jiuzhai said in that maddeningly sluggish drawl unique to diviners, speaking in a way that made listeners itch to punch him.-"However, traces of what once occurred can always be traced back. You can examine them yourself."

Hearing this, Tao Zhu followed Ancestor Jiuzhai's gaze toward the adjacent table.

Laid out across the tabletop was a colossal Chart of Heaven and Earth and the Eight Trigrams, accompanied by an assortment of measuring instruments. At first glance, it looked less like casting divinations and more like drafting cartographic surveys and sandbox models. Although Boss Tao possessed a passing familiarity with divination, witnessing this spectacle still made his belly swell with irritation.

—"I've been meaning to ask since earlier—what on earth are you doing?"-Tao Zhu asked, straining to decipher the Heaven-and-Earth chart.

—"I am recalculating the Heavenly Dao,"-Jiuzhai answered vaguely.

—"Hmph."

Tao Zhu cast one more glance at the table and refrained from probing further.

At any rate, casting hexes was a diviner's primary vocation. Jiuzhai had always calculated hexes before lifting a finger, so measuring the Heavenly Dao a thousand or ten thousand times over was completely normal for him.

While Boss Tao paid close attention to fortune and auspices when brokering deals, once things reached Jiuzhai's dizzying level of complexity, he lost all interest.

—"Forget it. I'll just go find Untiring directly,"-he muttered.

It wasn't that he genuinely bought Jiuzhai's claims and actually intended to adopt Yuwen Xuan—even if he wanted to adopt him, the age window had long since passed. The fellow was already at the Void Refinement stage, an existence on par with mighty figures throughout the cultivation realm; what business would he have being adopted? Rather, Boss Tao instinctively felt uneasy about the current situation, constantly sensing that some catastrophic event was brewing, so he wanted to check in with Untiring to be certain.

Jiuzhai paid him no heed.

Yet Boss Tao had apparently not even stepped out of the courtyard when his startled, booming shout drifted back from just outside the doorway:

—"Yuwen Xuan?! Why are you...?"

[—"Ah, looking for me?"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

[—"Oh, right, right..."](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

The conversation outside dwindled into a muted murmur. Ancestor Jiuzhai kept his eyes cast downward onto the Heaven-and-Earth chart, remaining thoroughly silent.

A short while later, Boss Tao returned to the room beaming from ear to ear, declaring to Jiuzhai:

—"You were entirely right—Ah-Xuan is truly an exceptional boy, hahaha!"-Tao Zhu said, patting his protruding belly in deep satisfaction as he clapped Yuwen Xuan on the shoulder.-"Do me a favor and let Untiring know: I have business and am taking him on a trip to the Abyssal Ocean Society. I will return him shortly."

Jiuzhai lifted his drooping eyelids to look toward Boss Tao. He found Boss Tao grinning so brightly it looked as though he had struck a mountain of spirit stone mines, while Yuwen Xuan stood right beside him, wearing the humble smile found only on [truly outstanding youths](# "CN: 大好青年 (dàhǎo qīngnián) — fine, upright, promising youth / model citizen; ironic here given Yuwen Xuan's true monstrous nature").

Whatever secret transaction the two had brokered remained unknown, but the sight of them together was deeply unsettling.

Jiuzhai felt uneasy as well, offering only a noncommittal:

—"Mm."

Taking that as consent, Boss Tao departed in triumphant high spirits, ushering Yuwen Xuan along with him.

After Boss Tao departed, a scruffy yellow fox that had been lounging inside the room all along with virtually zero presence finally spoke up:

—"Jiuzhai, why did you have to swindle Old Tao?"-Peilan asked.

—"How did I swindle him?"-Jiuzhai asked.

—"Heh. If you hadn't claimed Yuwen Xuan could expand the Abyssal Ocean Society to unprecedented heights, he wouldn't have fallen for it so easily,"-Peilan scoffed.

Peilan naturally possessed zero faith in Yuwen Xuan's obedient veneer.

Setting aside how he had spent the past half year loafing around the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even in years past he had frequently lodged on Mount Tianling. As such, he understood that brat's rotten temperament all too well.

He was utterly convinced that if Zhou Hui were not keeping watch, that Taotie cub would have swallowed him in a single gulp.

The predatory hunting mark secretly branded onto his fur was indisputable proof!

No matter where Peilan hid, Yuwen Xuan could always sniff him out—which was precisely how Yuwen Xuan had 'invited' Peilan back a year ago.

Peilan refused to believe that Yuwen Xuan harbored no designs on that golden toad.

For all anyone knew, the brat had been craving [stir-fried bullfrog](# "Literal CN: 爆炒田鸡 (bàochǎo tiánjī) — a popular spicy Chinese frog dish; Peilan suspects Yuwen Xuan wants to cook Tao Zhu's golden toad") for years!

To this, Ancestor Jiuzhai replied in his leisurely drawl:

—"When a fish is determined to bite, it will impale itself even on a [straight, barbless hook](# "Allusion: 姜太公钓鱼，愿者上钩 (Jiang Ziya fishing with a straight hook) — those who wish to take the bait will do so of their own accord"). What is more, I spoke no falsehood—Yuwen Xuan truly owes Boss Tao a karmic debt."

—"...Besides, while Untiring raised these disciples, it is only with these disciples around that he can be constrained,"-Jiuzhai sighed.-"Back before he exercised self-restraint, this world's timeline had already been washed clean by him six times over! My ancient bones cannot endure him thrashing reality any further. Reaching an equilibrium as soon as possible is for the best."

—"So you believe Untiring is even worse than those disciples of his?"-Peilan asked in wonder.

—"Is that not the case?"-Jiuzhai countered.

—"...That is undeniably true."

***

—"Where is Yuwen Xuan?"

Meanwhile, the very first words Zhou Hui uttered to his disciples upon 'emerging from seclusion' were to ask for him.

—"Eldest Senior Brother volunteered to accompany Boss Tao to make preparations for this year's Immortals' Market,"-Ran Deng reported.

...*Tell that to a ghost!*

Hearing this report, the other disciples thought in unison:

[He is definitely off stirring up catastrophic trouble!]
