# Chapter 16: [From Cheating to Complete Shutdown](# "Literal CN: 从作弊到自闭 (cóng zuòbì dào zìbì) — modern internet slang for spiraling from petty cheating into total existential despair and shutting down")

[—"A leaderboard?"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

—"Indeed. A common and remarkably effective system of competition, wouldn't you say?"-Zhou Hui said, his voice laced with profound nostalgia.

No matter which world he was in, he had always rather enjoyed exam rankings. Whether one made progress or fell behind, it allowed one to examine oneself with far greater clarity—and thereby [grind even harder](# "Literal CN: 内卷 (nèijuǎn) / 卷 — modern slang for hyper-competitive academic or workplace grinding").

[...Master, can't you spare a single thought for how the [slacker students](# "Literal CN: 学渣 (xuézhā) — academic underachievers / struggling students") feel?!]

The disciples wailed in the depths of their hearts, their gazes toward the examination hall brimming with sheer dread.

From their vantage point, they had a far clearer view than Su Xin.

After all, the Palace of Silence currently utilized the Dragon Palace template: the examination ground occupied the stage, while the platform where Zhou Hui and the others sat was the palace's throne of honor.

From this high seat, one could clearly see some coral platforms drifting high into the air like bubbles rising to the water's surface, while others were sinking downward, descending as though plunging into an abyss.

Even Yuwen Xuan's complexion turned slightly green at the sight:

—"Thank the heavens I didn't enter the field,"-Yuwen Xuan muttered. Even he had some shred of face to preserve.

Being strung up from a tree for causing mischief was one thing; being sunk to the bottom of the ocean for failing an exam was an entirely different matter.

—Though in Zhou Hui's eyes, the two were fundamentally the exact same thing.

Lu Shulong watched the bobbing, sinking coral platforms, a look of worry creeping across his face:

—"Controlling the coral platforms like this must consume an immense amount of spiritual energy, right? Even if he doesn't have to provide the spiritual power directly, acting as the transit conduit with Youngest Junior Brother's cultivation level—"

—"...Hall Master He, why have you suddenly started working on an exam paper?"-Zhou Hui said, suddenly lifting his frost-white eyelashes to look toward the other side.

Lu Shulong choked on his words.

Following Zhou Hui's gaze, they saw the Heavenly Dao Alliance emissary—who had still been watching their side just moments ago—huddled over the desk, busily scribbling away at the exam paper in front of him before anyone realized it.

Hearing Zhou Hui's inquiry, He Yong offered a hasty, face-saving laugh:

—"Haha! Seeing the questions, my hands couldn't help but itch."

Yet on the other side of the examination grounds, the Light-Stealing Mirror in Su Xin's hands was currently displaying the exact contents of the test paper before He Yong.

This was the only loophole Su Xin had managed to unearth.

Originally, in order to let He Yong understand how the exam worked, Zhou Hui had given him a synchronized test paper linked with Su Xin's.

When Su Xin discovered the secret of the coral platforms and was teetering on the brink of despair, he finally noticed this detail. Secretly activating another magical treasure, he coerced He Yong into providing him with [off-court assistance](# "Literal CN: 场外援助 (chǎngwài yuánzhù) — game show / sports term for phoning a friend or getting outside help").

—Fleeing was out of the question. Much as Su Xin wanted to cut and run, he couldn't bear the sheer humiliation of having it exposed that his grades were dead last.

That was why He Yong had abruptly started writing.

Unfortunately, He Yong had no idea that several anti-cheating formations had been inscribed beneath the Palace of Silence. The very instant Su Xin pulled out the Light-Stealing Mirror, Zhou Hui and the others already knew he was cheating.

Yet Zhou Hui merely looked at He Yong with a meaningful gaze:

—"In that case, do your best."

—"Heheh..."-A cold sweat broke out across He Yong's back, exactly like any student caught cheating when the teacher abruptly called their name.

He harbored a faint suspicion that he had been exposed, agonizing over whether he should put down his brush. But on the other side, his Little Martial Uncle was pressing him relentlessly; he had no choice but to dutifully carry on.

Still, seeing that Zhou Hui showed no sign of pursuing the matter, he bit the bullet and kept writing—

[Even Untiring Immortal Venerable wouldn't want to see a guest from the Heavenly Dao Alliance score dead last in his own sect!]

[Wouldn't that be tantamount to deliberately slapping us in the face?]

Naturally, Zhou Hui had no intention of deliberately slapping anyone's face. He didn't actually care what score someone else's child got; he was merely somewhat perplexed by the Heavenly Dao Alliance's educational culture.

Whipping out magical treasures the moment the exam began, then providing off-court lifelines midway through... Did the Heavenly Dao Alliance have some sort of peculiar testing regulations?

However, Zhou Hui had refrained from mentioning this earlier; bringing it up now just as Ran Deng had brought up Mo Xi was plainly an intentional maneuver to steer the subject away.

Lu Shulong naturally understood Master's intention, instinctively shooting a pleading look toward Yuwen Xuan and Ran Deng.

Yuwen Xuan shrugged, simply playing along with Zhou Hui's diversion:

—"Actually, it's quite reasonable. When it comes to exams, everyone relies on their own capabilities!"

—"If I had made you take the exam, wouldn't you have planned to do the exact same thing?"-Zhou Hui had no qualms about assuming the worst of Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan chuckled sheepishly:

—"In cultivation, aren't we fundamentally breaking the rules anyway? Could there possibly be any fairness in the eyes of the Heavenly Dao? When that time comes, cheating or not cheating hardly makes any difference!"

Zhou Hui froze upon hearing those words.

At any other time, Zhou Hui would have sneered at Yuwen Xuan's crooked fallacies. Yet at this precise moment, keywords like 'Heavenly Dao' and 'fairness' struck a chord deep within Zhou Hui's preoccupations.

—"Indeed... As cultivators, should we perhaps refrain from measuring things by mortal standards...?"-Zhou Hui murmured, lost in thought.

He gazed out at the examination grounds, sinking into profound contemplation.

Only, this reaction of Zhou Hui's scared the living daylights out of Ran Deng and Lu Shulong.

Both stared in sheer horror at their Master, never imagining that in their lifetimes they would witness the moment Master was [dragged off course in reverse](# "Literal CN: 反向带偏 (fǎnxiàng dài piān) — being led astray or influenced into bad habits in reverse by someone who should be learning from you") by Eldest Senior Brother.

Even Yuwen Xuan himself was caught off guard.

He subsequently leaned over and whispered to his junior sister and brother for confirmation:

—"Does this mean... from now on, we can all cheat openly and with clear consciences?"

—"Eldest Senior Brother, would you please just shut your mouth!"-Ran Deng snapped, her patience completely exhausted.

*

While Zhou Hui was lost in contemplation over there, Su Xin was copying at lightning speed over here.

Yet Su Xin wasn't happy in the slightest.

He had always carried the aura of a blessed favorite of heaven. Regardless of whether he possessed true competence or not, he was lavished with praise and flattery no matter which sect he visited. Backed by the magical treasures bestowed by his parents, he had never tasted a single defeat in any contest, leading him to take it for granted that he would inevitably reach the Mahayana Stage in the future, just like his mother and father.

Yet upon arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Su Xin discovered that he couldn't even answer the questions that ordinary sect disciples could solve—he couldn't even manage to cheat off them. His pride took a severe battering.

And this sensation was unexpectedly magnified while copying the exam paper of his [off-court lifeline](# "Literal CN: 场外援助 (chǎngwài yuánzhù) — game show / sports term for phoning a friend or getting outside help").

Su Xin discovered that the more he copied his paper, the harder it became.

Most terrifying of all, toward the end, every single problem revolved around his sect's specialized cultivation methods!

Su Xin had no idea that this test paper dynamically adapted to the examinee's specialties and competence. The "outside help" he had found was his fellow sect member, his "Martial Nephew" He Yong; naturally, the exam paper morphed into a specialized Heavenly Dao Alliance cultivation technique anthology—and its difficulty was skyrocketing vertically!

It was only because Zhou Hui had hard-capped the test questions at the Foundation Establishment Stage. Otherwise, given He Yong's Body Integration cultivation, it wouldn't have been surprising at all if Void Refinement Stage questions started popping up.

Even so, this was more than enough to deal Su Xin a catastrophic [dimensional strike](# "Literal CN: 降维打击 (jiàngwéi dǎjī) — sci-fi / Three-Body Problem meme for an attack from a higher dimension that completely annihilates lower-level competition").

When Su Xin looked at the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples' papers, he couldn't understand them, and thus couldn't fathom what realm of cultivators was required to study such problems. Although disheartened, the blow wasn't visceral, and he still had room for rationalization.

He Yong's paper, however, consisted of the exact material Su Xin studied every single day. While he couldn't solve them himself, he understood in broad strokes just what level of mastery was required to crack such problems.

Comparing this paper to those of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, a horrifying misunderstanding blossomed in Su Xin's mind:

[Could it be that the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect have thoroughly digested their own cultivation arts to such an absurd degree?!]

[Has the academic curriculum of other sects advanced that far ahead?!]

[Could it be that... I am the only [slacker](# "Literal CN: 学渣 (xuézhā) — academic underachievers / struggling students") in the entire world?!]

[Is this world even real?!]

The more Su Xin pondered, the colder he felt all over. However, he was fundamentally not the sort of person prone to self-reflection; on the contrary, he was exceptionally adept at deflecting his own shortcomings onto others.

—"No! This is my clan's cultivation art; this paper was generated specifically for me! That [backcountry rogue immortal](# "Literal CN: 乡下散仙 (xiāngxià sǎnxian) — backwater loose immortal") must be trying to humiliate me on purpose—that's why he issued such absurd questions! I refuse to believe it! There's no way his direct disciples are doing problems this hard!"

Reasoning thus, Su Xin deliberately disregarded the answers of the other Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, turning his Light-Stealing Mirror instead toward Zhou Hui's direct disciples.

By this point, the exam was drawing to a close, and everyone had nearly finished their questions.

Moreover, since the test papers were so "simple" (dynamically tailored to their proficiencies), everyone across both the inner and outer sects had tested rather well. The direct disciples naturally wouldn't lag behind either; the coral platforms belonging to Qu Qianxing and the others had long since drifted to the very apex of the palace, looking as though they were about to float straight through the ceiling.

Su Xin's Light-Stealing Mirror wavered for ages, yet he didn't dare settle on any of them, seemingly terrified of witnessing something his psyche couldn't endure.

Just then, Su Xin spotted an exception.

The coral platform belonging to one of the twin girls among the direct disciples whom he had noticed earlier was still teetering precariously, hovering only a tiny fraction above its original position. Inside, the young girl (?) seemed incapable of answering the problems; her face was deathly pale and drenched in cold sweat, presenting a sight that could evoke pity in anyone who beheld her.

Su Xin fancied himself a tender-hearted gentleman who cherished beauty. Under normal circumstances, he would have felt no small amount of sorrow for the poor young girl (?); yet seeing her miserable state now, he was overjoyed:

[Now that is the unmistakable look of a fellow [slacker](# "Literal CN: 学渣 (xuézhā) — academic underachievers / struggling students")!]

Decisively, Su Xin aimed his Light-Stealing Mirror at the other party's test paper—and instantly froze completely.

That examination paper bearing the name "Mo Xi" was packed wall-to-wall with mathematical problems from arithmetic classics, such as [Pheasant and Rabbit in the Same Cage](# "Literal CN: 鸡兔同笼 / 雉兔同笼 — classic Chinese arithmetic riddle dating back to the 4th-century Sunzi Suanjing"). The scratch paper beside it was filled with distorted handwriting covered in all manner of calculation formulas. The sight was enough to induce far more tears than any cultivation technique question.

Su Xin even felt that being able to keep one's platform floating at such a height while facing such problems was already a miraculous feat.

And yet, this wasn't even what inspired the deepest awe in Su Xin. What left him utterly dumbstruck was that while this young girl (?) named "Mo Xi" was answering questions, a continuous stream of spiritual energy was endlessly pouring from her scallion-white wrists.

Terrified, Su Xin vigorously rubbed the surface of the Light-Stealing Mirror and looked at Mo Xi once more.

This time he saw it with even greater clarity: not just the wrists—spiritual energy in various colors was radiating continuously from Mo Xi's wrists, ankles, waist, neck, and the crown of her head, sprawling outward like the strings manipulating a marionette puppet.

Yet these ribbons of spiritual energy were not controlling Mo Xi; rather, Mo Xi was controlling the spiritual energy attached to her body.

She (?) was directing these streams of spiritual energy to interweave, blend, and diffuse outward, ultimately weaving together this exquisite, breathtaking underwater palace!

—"A direct disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect... has to do this sort of thing in the middle of taking an exam?!"-Su Xin muttered, staring blankly at the young girl (?) in the mirror, his mind completely incapable of thought.

It was at this exact moment that the tolling of a bell resonated from high above, announcing the conclusion of the examination:

*Clang——!*

Amidst the lingering chimes of the bell, Su Xin saw the young girl (?) upon the coral platform collapse backward like a marionette whose strings had been snapped. He instinctively reached out to catch her, only to witness the desk and exam paper beneath him, the surrounding coral platforms and bubbles, the opulent palace, and even that pitiful young girl (?) all disperse into thin air like dissipating smoke.

By the time Su Xin came back to his senses, he found himself standing before several crude, tile-roofed houses, facing a rustic dirt road that wound toward the mountain woods.

Unable to comprehend what had just happened, Su Xin stared at the bronze mirror in his hands, then gazed at the dilapidated tiled cottages. He looked for all the world like a [poor scholar swindled out of his money and virtue by a fox spirit](# "Literal CN: 被狐狸精骗色骗钱的倒霉书生 — classic Pu Songling / Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio trope") after seeking shelter in an abandoned temple on a rainy night.

Fortunately, He Yong's angry bellow jarred him back to reality:

—"...Who in the world dumps people out halfway through like this?!"

Dazed, Su Xin lifted his head to see He Yong flying down before him alongside Yuwen Xuan and the others.

Among them, Yuwen Xuan was smiling cheerfully as he offered an apology:

—"Our apologies, truly! It seems when we were preparing for the exam, it was decided that right after the exam concluded, we would go directly to the Library Pavilion to announce the results. We really didn't take our guests' feelings into consideration."

Su Xin couldn't process any of it. He asked blankly:

—"The exam? The palace?"-"And... the young girl (?)...?"

—"There was never any palace to begin with!"-He Yong exposed the truth flatly.-"That entire examination hall was nothing more than an illusion! This right here is the true appearance of the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

[Ah. So... the young girl (?) I saw weaving the illusion at the very end was real?!]

[A direct disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect genuinely had to maintain an illusion while taking an exam at the same time!]

Su Xin's complexion paled even further.

Seeing his Little Martial Uncle's expression, He Yong assumed Su Xin was equally outraged by the indignity of being abruptly tossed out into the dirt after sitting comfortably just moments ago. Turning back to Yuwen Xuan, he demanded furiously:

—"Is this how your Heavenly Spirit Sect treats honored guests?!"

—"Aiya, the sect of a [wild cultivator from a rogue background](# "Literal CN: 野路子出身的散仙 — Su Xin's insult in the carriage, now quoted right back to his face") can only muster this meager standard!"-Yuwen Xuan replied with a beaming smile.

He Yong instantly froze.

Su Xin snapped his head up in shock, staring at Yuwen Xuan.

'A wild cultivator from a rogue background'—wasn't that the exact phrase he had used when discussing Zhou Hui with He Yong inside the cloud carriage? How could he possibly know?! That cloud carriage was unmistakably a True Immortal treasure, fully capable of shielding against divine sense even from Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses!

—"Our Heavenly Spirit Sect doesn't have any True Immortal treasures, nor do we possess any real palaces. Still, as a [frugal and peace-loving](# "Literal CN: 生活简朴，不喜享受 — Su Xin's exact words mocked back at him") [backcountry rogue immortal](# "Literal CN: 乡下散仙 — backwater loose immortal"), we can certainly afford to treat you to a meal,"-Yuwen Xuan said, the smile on his face deepening as he met the guests' suspicious, unsettled gazes.-"Now that the exam is over, don't be shy—let's go have dinner together!"

—"Come, right this way, please,"-Yuwen Xuan said, making an inviting gesture with a beaming smile.

In the shadow cast from who-knew-where, his smile seemed distinctly ominous.
