# Chapter 28: The Palace of Carefree Wandering

—"The Palace of Fallen Immortals again? Why are they like cockroaches—impossible to eradicate no matter how many you kill?"-Zhou Hui grumbled, knitting his brows.

Yu Feifei, who could still technically be classified as one such un-exterminated "cockroach" having not yet cleanly severed ties with the Fallen Immortal Palace, shrank her shoulders in sheer terror.

Zhou Hui, for his part, was genuinely annoyed by the sheer hassle.

His grievances with the "Palace of Fallen Immortals" were hardly a one- or two-time affair; in fact, he had clashed directly with the Palace of Carefree Wandering itself.

Ever since his youth, when Zhou Hui had first stepped into an auction hosted by the Carefree Palace and witnessed living human beings being auctioned off as [cultivation furnaces](# "Literal CN: 炉鼎 (lúdǐng) — living cauldrons / human cultivation vessels whose essence is harvested through dual cultivation"), he had detested this faction to the marrow of his bones. Back then, Zhou Hui was merely in the Foundation Establishment stage, utterly powerless to challenge such a behemoth. But Zhou Hui had kept that grudge firmly committed to memory. So much so that the very moment he attained the Tribulation Transcendence stage, his very first act of business was to cleave the Carefree Palace's premier flagship auction—the Grand Curio Fair—straight in half with heavenly lightning, before relentlessly hunting down one of their Tribulation Transcendence elders for seven solid years! In the end, virtually every high-tier grandmaster across the entire cultivation realm had to step in as frantic mediators before the matter was finally put to rest.

Zhou Hui's ultimate decision to establish his sect in the remote and barren Central Domain was also tied to those ancient grudges.

—What left Zhou Hui both amused and exasperated, however, was that even after he had wrought all that havoc, neither the Carefree Palace nor the rest of the cultivation world had the foggiest idea *why* he had attacked them. To this day, wild rumors still circulated: *"The Untiring Immortal Venerable's beloved was turned into a furnace by the Carefree Palace,"* *"Zhou Hui's kin were abducted by the Carefree Palace,"* *"Disciples of the Carefree Palace massacred Zhou Hui's hometown,"* and other absurd fabrications. The Carefree Palace had even sheepishly accepted the blame and meekly delivered formal apologies to his doorstep—simply because they had committed so many heinous atrocities that they genuinely couldn't remember whether they had wronged this terrifying powerhouse in his youth. Not a single person in the entire world thought that the very existence of the Carefree Palace was fundamentally wrong!

It seemed Zhou Hui was the only one who believed: regardless of the rationale, treating human beings as commodities to be bought and sold was intrinsically inhumane.

Zhou Hui couldn't tell whether it was this world that was broken, or his own cognition that was out of place.

Nevertheless, after weathering several millennia of time, Zhou Hui had long since lost the fiery blood of his youth.

He understood all too clearly that beasts like these could never be wiped out.

Cleaving the Grand Curio Fair all those years ago had merely been a way to settle a youthful obsession and banish his inner demons; it hadn't been done out of some grand crusade for justice, nor could it alter the entrenched norms of this mortal coil.

Even if the Palace of Carefree Wandering fell, there would still be the Palace of Fallen Immortals, the Palace of Plummeting Immortals, or whatever other vile spawns crawling out to replace it.

For that matter, even if one ascended, was the "Immortal Realm" truly so glorious?

Never having visited, Zhou Hui naturally didn't know what it looked like. Yet according to the Heavenly Dao's novel script, that place was nothing more than a larger cultivation world endowed with denser spiritual energy; what the protagonist did after ascending was functionally identical.

Upon reading that, Zhou Hui's only thought had been: *Ah, of course.*

His desire to lie flat and live as a [total slacker](# "Literal CN: 咸鱼 (xiányú) — salted fish; slang for someone with zero ambition who just wants to rest") had more than a little to do with this disillusionment.

Consequently, Zhou Hui felt he had grown remarkably placid over the past few centuries. So long as the Fallen Immortal Palace kept their dirty claws out of his domain, he did his best to turn a blind eye to these scuttling vermin.

Unfortunately, the scoundrels stubbornly insisted on sticking their claws into his business anyway.

—"Master, do you want me to go level the newly built Fallen Immortal Palace hideout?"-Yuwen Xuan chimed in, never one to pass up an opportunity for absolute mayhem.

—"Just the Fallen Immortal Palace won't cut it. It would have to be at least the Palace of Carefree Wandering,"-Zhou Hui muttered under his breath.

—"Master! Eldest Senior Brother!"-Qu Qianxing shouted.-"Such reckless action will never work! If we are to eradicate the Palace of Carefree Wandering, we must at least map out the precise coordinates of every single one of their branch headquarters, so that we can [pull them up by the roots](# "Literal CN: 斩草除根 (zhǎncǎo chúgēn) — cut the grass and dig out the roots; utterly eradicate an evil so it cannot revive") and ensure they never return!"

[...Senior Brother Qu, your proposal is by far the most radical of all, isn't it?!]

Yu Feifei shivered violently from head to toe.

In her impression, Zhou Hui was a gentle mentor, and Eldest Senior Brother... no, Senior Brother Qu was a reasonable senior. Even upon discovering her identity as a spy, so long as she took the initiative to [abandon the darkness for the light](# "Literal CN: 弃暗投明 (qì'àn tóumíng) — renounce evil to join the righteous path"), they shouldn't make things difficult for her.

She hadn't anticipated walking into such an intensely 'stimulating' war council the moment she arrived, leaving her suddenly questioning whether handing over that secret missive had been a wise decision after all.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui had no genuine intention of waging an all-out war against the Palace of Carefree Wandering:
—"It hasn't come to that yet. Besides, the matter outlined in this letter... cannot be resolved merely by eradicating the Carefree Palace."

—"Are other factions involved?"-Qu Qianxing asked tensely.

—"Indeed,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"What faction could be larger than the Carefree Palace? Could it be... the Heavenly Dao Alliance?"-Qu Qianxing pressed.

—"Far graver than that,"-Zhou Hui replied slowly.-"In all likelihood... it is the Heavenly Dao itself."

He examined the secret letter Yu Feifei had surrendered once more.

Naturally, the encrypted missive contained very little operational detail. It merely stated that upon entering the secret realm, an undercover agent would release a wisp of subtle fragrance undetectable to anyone outside the Fallen Immortal Palace, instructing Yu Feifei to lure Mo Bei along that scent trail to a prearranged rendezvous point.

From Yu Feifei's perspective, the Carefree Palace's conspiracy was glaringly obvious:

They simply couldn't bear to part with a supreme cultivation furnace possessing an Extreme Yin Constitution, and thus sought to exploit the chaotic environment inside the secret realm to isolate Mo Bei and 'reclaim' their lost property.

Zhou Hui, however, didn't view it that way.

Because this letter was predicated on a fundamental premise: *Mo Bei would definitely enter the secret realm!*

Yet precisely because the Extreme Yin Constitution shared by Mo Bei and Mo Xi posed an irresistible temptation to malicious cultivators, Zhou Hui took great pains to conceal their condition even within the sect. Until the twins had mastered their techniques and could defend themselves, Zhou Hui would never casually permit them to leave the mountain gates—let alone venture into a battle royale slaughterhouse like an ancient secret realm.

Turning to Qu Qianxing, Zhou Hui inquired:
—"Qianxing, for this secret realm expedition, were you planning on taking Bei'er along?"

—"Ah, I'm still finalizing the roster,"-Qu Qianxing answered blankly.-"I did plan to bring the disciples who performed exceptionally well in the recent Immortal Chess tournament, but as for Youngest Junior Sister..."

Qu Qianxing glanced over at Mo Bei, who wore a breezy, detached expression as though she weren't the target of this vile conspiracy in the slightest, his face twisting into a conflicted grimace.

Clearly, the thought of taking this junior sister along had never even crossed his mind.

[Sure enough, this 'plot point' makes no sense at all.]

Zhou Hui mused.

In the Heavenly Dao's original script, deep within the secret realm lay a hot spring whose waters were miraculously therapeutic for Zhou Hui's injuries. As a result, the Heavenly Spirit Sect dispatched teams here on regular intervals to draw water—until the protagonist secretly dove beneath the pool, unraveled an ancient seal, and ended up trapped in the lowest subterranean depths of the secret realm alongside Mo Bei, sparking their 'fated romance.'

When reading this passage back then, Zhou Hui had been profoundly baffled: *Why on earth was Mo Bei leading the expedition?*

Neither he nor Qu Qianxing would ever let Mo Bei out of the mountain gates!

—"Then let us posit a hypothetical question,"-Zhou Hui continued.-"Purely hypothetical, mind you—"

—"Yes?"-Qu Qianxing acknowledged.

—"Hypothetically, suppose I had been gravely injured during my heavenly tribulation..."

—"Master, you were gravely injured?!"-Qu Qianxing shrieked.

Every single disciple present broke out in abject horror. Even Mo Bei looked thoroughly panicked.

—"...I said it was a hypothetical!"-Zhou Hui roared in exasperation.-"If I had appointed you to act as Sect Master on my behalf, and a secret realm expedition arose, whom would you have dispatched?"

—"Eldest Senior Brother, naturally?"

—"Pretend he doesn't exist."

—"Ah. In that case, Fifth Junior Brother would lead the team with Fourth Junior Brother assisting. If they were occupied, I would have Third Junior Sister lead, accompanied by several inner sect disciples with high combat prowess—someone like Yan Fei or Yu Cong would do nicely,"-Qu Qianxing pondered.

Sure enough, in Qu Qianxing's mind, keeping Mo Bei and Mo Xi out of sight was the top priority.

Zhou Hui asked:
—"Under what circumstances would you dispatch Bei'er?"

—"Under no circumstances would I... wait."

Qu Qianxing was no fool; he quickly caught on to the underlying crux. After mulling it over carefully, he answered:
—"Under our present circumstances, I cannot conceive of any reason why Youngest Junior Sister would be indispensable. But under your hypothetical scenario—if you were critically injured, the secret realm happened to contain the exact medicinal ingredient you needed, and the internal environment favored Junior Sister's specific powers—only then might I consider sending her at the head of the team."

Reaching this conclusion, Qu Qianxing still found it impossible to reconcile:
—"Yet we never received any such intel! Why on earth would they be so certain we'd dispatch Youngest Junior Sister?"

This was precisely what was so bizarre about the Palace of Carefree Wandering.

Why were they so utterly confident—almost as if they, too, had read the Heavenly Dao's script?

—"Whatever their reasoning, wouldn't everything be solved if we simply don't send Youngest Junior Sister out?"-Lu Shulong suggested.

—"Second Senior Brother wasn't planning on taking her anyway, right?"-Guan Ye chimed in.

Qu Qianxing looked toward Zhou Hui.

—"No. Take her along after all,"-Zhou Hui said after prolonged consideration, deciding not to alter the 'plot.'

After all, 'Heavenly Mandate' was not so easily defied. If he altered this specific detail, the Heavenly Dao would inevitably balance the ledger elsewhere, devising some other contrived scheme to trap Mo Bei and the 'protagonist' in the same room. Rather than trigger an unpredictable retaliation, it was far better to play along with the 'plot,' unearth whatever pitfall the Heavenly Dao had dug for Mo Bei, and mount a calculated countermeasure.

In all likelihood, the Carefree Palace's conspiracy was simply an off-screen piece of backstory that the original 'novel' had omitted.

Calculating his moves, Zhou Hui addressed Yu Feifei:
—"You shall accompany them as well. Do exactly as they instructed. There is no need to 'betray' them—after all, if they were to swap you out for another agent, that would only be more troublesome."

—"Yes! Understood!"-Yu Feifei replied, bowing her head in flustered haste.

—"Master, I will protect Youngest Junior Sister with my life!"-Qu Qianxing declared firmly at this juncture.-"Be it the Carefree Palace or the Fallen Immortal Palace, I will unmask their conspiracy and never allow them to succeed!"

Double Seventh Festival, the Nameless Mountain Range.

The rogue cultivator Zhao Yuegui flew across the jagged mountain peaks, standing atop his flying sword.

Gazing down, he saw nothing beneath him but gnarled demonic foliage and swarming specters, without a single trace of human habitation. Zhao Yuegui couldn't help accelerating his sword flight, speeding forward until a certain flat-topped mountain came into view, allowing him to exhale a breath of relief.

The base of that peak differed little from the surrounding crags, but its summit appeared as though it had been sheared clean off by a colossal sword, exposing a vast expanse of barren rock.

Upon that rocky plateau, quite a few people had already gathered.

Some stood at the cliff edges with blades cradled in their arms; others lingered among the stunted tree line clad in Daoist vestments. Most kept to themselves in isolated vigilance, with only a scattered few huddled in conversation. Evidently, just like Zhao Yuegui, they were all [unaffiliated rogue cultivators](# "Literal CN: 散仙 (sǎnxiān) — here used in the broader colloquial sense of unaffiliated, wandering cultivators without sect backing").

Taking in the scene, Zhao Yuegui lowered his airspeed, drifting toward the perimeter of the peak while keeping his presence as unobtrusive as possible.

Yet descending from the open sky, he had naturally drawn glances from the moment he appeared. While most cultivators merely sized him up, dismissed him as an ordinary rogue cultivator, and turned away in disinterest, one particularly enthusiastic fellow called out a greeting:

[—"Where does Fellow Daoist hail from?"](# "Speaker: Qian Er")

—"I come from the east. Having heard that a new secret realm was opening here, I came to [join the excitement](# "Literal CN: 凑个热闹 (còu gè rènào) — participate in or spectate a bustling gathering"),"-Zhao Yuegui replied candidly, seeing no way to avoid the conversation.

The man chuckled:
—"Hehe, does Fellow Daoist hope to [claim a slice of the pie](# "Literal CN: 分一杯羹 (fēn yī bēi gēng) — take a share of the spoils / feast") inside the secret realm?"

—"A mere outsider like myself wouldn't dare entertain such extravagant dreams,"-Zhao Yuegui said. Even if he did nurture such ambitions, he certainly wouldn't voice them aloud!

To tell the truth, his luck had been genuinely abysmal.

Zhao Yuegui was originally an unaligned cultivator from the Eastern Domain. One day, while hunting demonic core beasts out at sea, he had pursued his quarry so far from the mainland that night fell before he could return. Finding a desolate reef island, he decided to bed down for the night.

Who could have imagined that was no island at all, but the colossal fish-back of a transformed [Kunpeng](# "A mythical leviathan-roc capable of transforming from a gargantuan fish into a giant bird that ascends to the highest heavens")!

In the dead of night, the Kunpeng suddenly unfurled its titanic wings, soared into the ninth heaven, and carried Zhao Yuegui all the way across continents to the Central Domain.

And this Central Domain truly lived up to its reputation as an ancient battlefield of gods and demons—the most unforgiving terrain across the Five Domains, where conditions were so harsh that mortals couldn't even establish kingdoms. Zhao Yuegui had considered himself quite well-off back in the Eastern Domain, enjoying ample resources and total carefree freedom without ever having to join a sect. Little did he expect that upon landing in this godforsaken wasteland, he couldn't even scrape together the travel fare to journey home.

Zhao Yuegui offered a wry smile:
—"I merely wish to see which sects are reputable, so that I might [hang my register](# "Literal CN: 挂单 (guàdān) — originally a Buddhist/Daoist term for a wandering monk registering at a temple for lodging; here meaning taking up guest/itinerant service in a sect") with one in the future."

—"Astute!"-The man gave a hearty thumbs-up upon hearing that.-"Brother, you aren't like those out-of-domain dreamers with their heads in the clouds. Your luck is solid too—you arrived at the perfect time. This is a once-in-a-blue-moon opportunity to choose a sect!"

—"Choose a sect?"-Zhao Yuegui asked in bewilderment.

According to Zhao Yuegui's common sense, an unaligned cultivator like him who had never achieved the stature of a venerated elder would usually have to [beg his ancestors on bended knees](# "Literal CN: 求爷爷告奶奶 (qiú yéye gào nǎinai) — plead with grandparents; beg every authority figure imaginable") just to enter an orthodox sect. How could there ever be an opportunity for him to 'choose'?

The man chatting with him was evidently well-traveled and perceptive, seeing right through Zhao Yuegui's confusion.

He chuckled:
—"That's how folks from outside domains think. In our Central Domain, choosing a sect is a two-way street!"

With that, he enthusiastically launched into an explanation.

As it turned out, whether in the affluent Eastern and Southern Domains, the frigid Northern Domain, or even the arid Western Domain, vast mortal kingdoms always flourished. Even if possessing a spirit root was a one-in-a-thousand rarity, so long as the population base existed, most sects had no trouble finding new disciples.

Furthermore, outside rogue cultivators could never match the loyalty of disciples raised from childhood. Consequently, sects in other lands relied primarily on in-house rearing (much like vocational academies) and treated mid-career external recruitment (essentially [social hiring](# "Literal CN: 社招 (shèzhāo) — corporate jargon for hiring experienced talent from society/market rather than campus graduates (校招)")) as a secondary supplement, never treating rogue cultivators as their primary talent pipeline.

The Central Domain, however, was an entirely different beast.

As an ancient battleground of gods and demons littered with demonic beasts, fiends, and spatial rifts, mortals couldn't even form nations, surviving only in scattered hamlets. Trying to scout disciples among them was almost as rare as stumbling across a free spirit stone on the roadside. Conversely, a steady influx of rogue cultivators poured into the Central Domain—either seeking ancient treasures or fleeing pursuit. (Naturally, there was also no shortage of unlucky bastards like Zhao Yuegui; rumor had it that the end of spatial-temporal pathways from every corner of the world converged on the Central Domain.) Those who managed to survive, regardless of their cultivation realm, possessed some real capability, making them the primary recruitment pool for Central Domain sects.

With a seller's market, rogue cultivators naturally [waited for the right price](# "Literal CN: 待价而沽 (dàijià'érgū) — hold out for a handsome offer"), weighing the study environments, pedagogical quality, and compensation packages of various sects.

—"And secret realm expeditions are the ultimate litmus test for a sect's true strength,"-the man continued.-"Whatever compensation or working environment they advertise is all smoke and mirrors! How many secret realms they control for disciple training is the only real metric! Especially with newly discovered realms: seizing the initiative against rival sects and securing a larger allocation of quotas—*that* is when a sect's muscle is truly put on display."

—"I see,"-Zhao Yuegui praised.

—"Take today's expedition, for instance: the number of quota slots a sect secures directly reflects the scale of its power."

Swayed by the man's compelling pitch, Zhao Yuegui couldn't help asking:
—"Do you happen to know which sects are entering the secret realm today?"

—"Hehe, that's not information just anyone can get their hands on,"-the man said, miming the motion of counting spirit stones with his fingers.-"If Fellow Daoist is interested, why not step over here and let me lay it out in detail? If you're particularly keen, I happen to have a few connections in the Central Domain and can even provide a personal referral."

[...No wonder he was so friendly. Turns out I ran into a professional headhunter.]

Zhao Yuegui complained inwardly, yet felt an odd sense of relief.

After all, the cultivation world was rife with sinister malice and lurking traps. Unprovoked warmth was far more terrifying; someone being upfront about their commercial motives was infinitely more reassuring.

Besides, Zhao Yuegui happened to be in dire need of such 'services.'

Seeing as a direct return to the Eastern Domain was out of the question, taking up employment at a wealthy sect for a few years, getting the lay of the Central Domain's geography, and then planning his voyage home was by far the safer route.

Yet survival was never cheap. Rather than agreeing immediately, Zhao Yuegui engaged in a fierce round of haggling before finally parting with two spirit stones as an 'information fee.'

This 'broker' didn't scoff at the meager sum, his demeanor remaining as cheerful as ever. Clearly, [even a fly's thigh was meat](# "Literal CN: 再小的蚂蚁也是肉 (even the smallest ant is meat) / 苍蝇腿也是肉 — every scrap of profit counts"). Besides, it was only because Zhao Yuegui had arrived late that he was so desperate for intel; everyone else was either a Central Domain native or had staked out the site since the realm's discovery, already privy to whatever news there was. Finding another paying customer was practically impossible.

Moreover, being an inveterate chatterbox, he happily launched into his briefing:
—"When I said your luck was good, this was why: this secret realm manifested with tremendous fanfare, stirring up every major sect. Nearly all the prominent sects of the Central Domain have secured entry quotas. If you're curious about the smaller outfits, you can ask me later, but let me first walk you through the heavyweight contenders you should focus on!"

—"First, the premier choice for rogue cultivators is naturally the Myriad Coalition Gang. It's an alliance formed by rogue cultivators themselves. The entry threshold is low, their demands are few, and you enjoy great freedom—though correspondingly, the compensation is rather... mediocre."

—"Next is the Myriad Beasts Hall. They possess unique ancestral techniques and lucrative benefits, but unless you intend to walk the path of a beast tamer, you won't receive favorable cultivation conditions and will just end up wasting your years, so weigh that carefully."

—"Then come the Abyssal Ocean Society and Eternal Villa. Both are exceedingly wealthy, with top-tier manuals and training environments. Unfortunately, their admission standards are steep, and once you join, it is exceedingly difficult to leave."

—"The one I recommend most highly, however, is the Palace of Fallen Immortals."

—"Don't be put off by the ominous name. While their core business is commerce, looking at it from another angle, they maintain the broadest network with other territories and operate dedicated commercial shipping routes. For an outsider looking to leave this place, they are by far your best bet."

This final pitch struck right at the heart of Zhao Yuegui's desires.

Zhao Yuegui's sole priority was returning to the Eastern Domain; naturally, a sect with established shipping routes was ideal. Eager for more details, he asked:
—"Then this Fallen Immortal Palace..."

—"Qian Er! Just how many spirit stones did the Fallen Immortal Palace grease your palms with?! Abducting good folks into that trap again!"

A booming voice suddenly cut in.

The speaker was a tall, burly man. After bellowing at the broker named 'Qian Er,' he turned to Zhao Yuegui:
—"Hey, kid! Don't listen to this swindler's drivel! What kind of decent place is the Fallen Immortal Palace?! They deal exclusively in rotten, conscience-stripping businesses. Go there, and three years from now, they'll have harvested your spirit roots and sold them off piecemeal!"

—"Besides, would the Fallen Immortal Palace even dare show their faces at this secret realm expedition?"-another female cultivator with thick brows and bright eyes chimed in.-"I hear the Heavenly Spirit Sect secured the single largest allocation of entry quotas, while the Fallen Immortal Palace offended the Heavenly Dao Alliance's emissary. They don't even have the credentials to enter the realm, let alone kick up trouble right under the Heavenly Spirit Sect's nose."

—"You're the ones talking nonsense!"-Qian Er protested, casting an anxious glance at Zhao Yuegui before flusteredly countering:-"The Fallen Immortal Palace is a major powerhouse in our Central Domain! How could they forfeit their qualifications just because of pressure from the Heavenly Dao Alliance? I've spotted Fallen Immortal Palace personnel in this vicinity just over the past few days. They're clearly still preparing for the expedition!"

[—"Come to think of it, people really have spotted their movements,"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")-someone corroborated.

Qian Er puffed up with swagger instantly:
—"See, what did I tell you?! In the Central Domain, when it comes to sheer influence, the Fallen Immortal Palace reigns supreme!"

—"Tch. Even so, I bet the Fallen Immortal Palace won't dare act half as arrogant as they used to,"-the earlier female cultivator sneered.-"Aren't they terrified that the Heavenly Spirit Sect might plow through them all over again?"

—"E-Even the Untiring Immortal Venerable can't just slaughter people indiscriminately without cause!"-Qian Er retorted, still stubborn.-"Besides, the Untiring Immortal Venerable spends most of his time in secluded cultivation; there's no way he would attend this secret realm in person. Why wouldn't the Fallen Immortal Palace show up?"

[In other words, if the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Untiring Immortal Venerable actually showed up, the Fallen Immortal Palace wouldn't even dare poke their heads out!]

[What kind of 'supreme influence' is that?!]

Zhao Yuegui was both amused and gripped by the irritation of being conned.

Clearly, even Qian Er conceded that there was a Heavenly Spirit Sect that the Fallen Immortal Palace feared above all else—yet when rattling off the great sects earlier, he hadn't even uttered their name.

Just as Zhao Yuegui opened his mouth to confront him, someone shouted:

[—"A sect is arriving!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

At those words, the rogue cultivators on the plateau perked up in unison, turning their gazes toward the horizon. Zhao Yuegui followed suit.

He saw the distant mountains and forests veiled beneath billowing mist and clouds, creating a boundless sea of white. From the horizon at the edge of that sea of clouds, the ethereal notes of celestial music faintly drifted over.

Riding the celestial music, a gentle breeze swept across the plateau, carrying a floating wisp of exquisite fragrance. Through this perfumed gale, several spiritual skiffs glided into view over the sea of clouds, each piloted by a captivating maiden. And trailing behind those vanguard skiffs, a colossal twelve-sail treasure ship radiating five-colored splendor made its majestic, awe-inspiring entrance.

The ship was so immense that the spiritual skiffs clearing its path resembled mere ants. As it pulled alongside the mountain peak, it loomed like a floating metropolis, casting its shadow across the entire mountain range.

Beholding this spectacle, gasps of astonishment erupted among the gathered rogue cultivators:

[—"Could it be...?!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

[—"Why would they come here?!"](# "Speaker: Rogue Cultivator")

Even Zhao Yuegui was no longer in the dark. Hailing from the Eastern Domain, he recognized that treasure ship on sight—or rather, there was not a single soul across the entire cultivation realm who did not recognize that vessel:

[—"It's actually... the Palace of Carefree Wandering!"](# "Speaker: Zhao Yuegui")

—"Hehe, see, what did I tell you?"-Qian Er boasted smugly.-"Who doesn't know that the Fallen Immortal Palace is really just the Palace of Carefree Wandering?"
