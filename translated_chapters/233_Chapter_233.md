# Chapter 233: Remote Call

—"Spiritual energy from the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array? No, this power...?"-Qu Qianxing said, snapping to attention.

He lifted his slender, scallion-white fingers, feeling boundless power overflowing from his fingertips.

This power was so immense that ordinary spiritual energy could not even begin to compare. Yet it felt both familiar and intimate—akin to the aura one could perceive only when standing right at Master's side while he cultivated. Furthermore, an indescribable essence seemed woven into the power, filling Qu Qianxing with equal parts awe and dread.

He had a distinct premonition that if he could master this power, he might well be able to pull and shift celestial stars—in the literal, physical sense.

[—"Only... why?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Qu Qianxing had already refused to draw upon the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array's reserves earlier, so he felt no joy at this sudden deluge of power. Instead, he immediately reached out to contact Master and the grand array to ascertain what had occurred.

Master gave no response. Qu Qianxing could faintly sense that the grand array was currently overseen by his junior sister Ran Deng. Moreover, a continuous torrent of power was being channeled into the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array, which then dispersed and fed back into Mount Heavenly Spirit—and perhaps even the entire world.

Qu Qianxing's heart sank like lead.

—"Master... Master couldn't have [feathered into an immortal](# "Literal CN: feathering (羽化) — traditional Daoist euphemism for death or ascension; here Qu Qianxing assumes Master has passed away and dissipated his power"), could he?"-he blurted out in horror.

—"Ptooey, ptooey, ptooey! What kind of nonsense are you babbling?!"-Ran Deng snapped back at once.-"Eldest Senior Brother having no filter on his mouth is bad enough, but Second Senior Brother, since when did you start cursing Master out of nowhere?!"

—"I didn't, that's not what I meant... But where did this power come from?"-Qu Qianxing asked, anxious and frantic.

Naturally, he had no intention of jinxing the Master who mattered more to him than anyone else. But by sheer force of habit, Qu Qianxing's mind always raced toward the absolute worst-case scenario to make contingency plans. When it came to this unidentified surge of power flooding into the mountain array, his first instinct was that it belonged to Master. And under what circumstances would Master pour his power without reservation into the grand array and distribute it across the world? The only explanation was that Master had "feathered."

Even just entertaining the possibility made Qu Qianxing's limbs run so icy cold that his mind nearly seized.

Ran Deng's state of mind was hardly any better:

—"I don't know, but—in any case! Master is definitely not gone! Before Master entered the mystic realm, he specifically lit that lamp, and it's completely intact! The moment I sensed the anomaly, I rushed over to check! It's brighter than it has ever been at any point in the past! That's more than enough proof Master is doing wonderfully right now!"-Ran Deng declared.

So Ran Deng claimed, yet the fact that she had sprinted over to check the soul lamp the instant she felt the influx inadvertently betrayed that she had harbored the exact same morbid suspicion.

Qu Qianxing had no time to quibble over details; his sole concern was Master's safety:

—"Mystic realm? What mystic realm did Master go to? Is it dangerous? Why did he deliberately leave a life lamp behind? Wait a minute... how are you having a remote call with me?"-Qu Qianxing asked, suddenly bewildered.

Ran Deng simply did not possess such power.

Qu Qianxing was stationed on the vanguard of the God-Demon battlefield, separated by multiple spatial layers. Never mind Ran Deng—even Qu Qianxing himself, or even other Transcending Tribulation powerhouses who seized full ownership of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Mountain-Protecting Grand Array, could never pull this off. Only Zhou Hui possessed such absurd spatial control.

—"You're asking so many questions at once, I don't even know where to begin,"-Ran Deng grumbled.

Truth be told, she felt somewhat guilty regarding where Master had gone. She knew Master had set off to find her father. Even though Zhou Hui had stated before departing that he had other matters to attend to, Ran Deng could not shake the guilty feeling that her family affairs were burdening Master. Faced with Qu Qianxing's barrage of questions, she opted to answer the easier one first:

—"I don't know how I can talk to you either. If pushed for an explanation, it's probably thanks to that newly injected power,"-Ran Deng said. She, too, had recognized the power's abnormality, and had even gone a step further than Qu Qianxing by grasping the authority embedded within it much faster. This was not because her learning capability exceeded Qu Qianxing's, but due to a peculiar reason:—"The moment that power poured into my body, I felt my mastery over formations skyrocket. I could even perform feats with the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array that previously only you and Master could manage. But this isn't my own ability—it's part of my clan's inheritance. You know about it, right? Because our Ran clan suffers from short lifespans, whenever the previous clan head dies, their cultivation and knowledge are passed down to the successor through a specific method. I think this sudden leap in my array comprehension is drawing upon that power."

Qu Qianxing felt a deeper sense of foreboding:

—"Then your father..."-Qu Qianxing trailed off, a sinking feeling in his gut.

—"He's alive too!"-Ran Deng cried out, cutting off this [crow-mouthed](# "Literal CN: crow's mouth (乌鸦嘴) — harbinger of bad luck / someone whose pessimistic predictions come true") Second Senior Brother before he could utter anything more distressing:—"Though his soul-fire is faint, he is still alive! Besides, Master went to find him, so nothing will go wrong."

—"Master went to find... your father?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

[—"Yeah."](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

Seeing that she had let the cat out of the bag and could stall no longer, Ran Deng laid out Zhou Hui's whereabouts and purpose in full detail.

—"So, Master went to retrieve your father to construct arrays in order to prevent a demonic invasion, is that it?"-Qu Qianxing summarized.

Naturally, he took no issue with Master going to rescue Ran Deng's father. If anything, given Master's character, delaying until an absolute crisis arose before looking for someone was already uncharacteristic of him. It was simply that this past year had been far too hectic, leaving Master with too many burdens to shoulder—while he himself was fooling around out here, utterly unable to assist.

Qu Qianxing reflected on his shortcomings with utmost solemnity.

Were it not for the fact that holding the frontlines of the God-Demon battlefield served to pin down the demon forces, he would have bolted straight back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect this instant.

Ran Deng, on the other hand, sounded somewhat uneasy:

—"That's the gist of it. So I can't reach Master right now either. But he's only been inside the mystic realm for less than half a day, and this power is already flooding back..."

—"If that's the case, it's far more likely Master obtained something inside the mystic realm,"-Qu Qianxing offered in comfort. Although he tended to assume the worst, his faith in Master's absolute power remained unshakable.

Even so, Master was the most important person in Qu Qianxing's life; he could not help but worry.

Pausing, Qu Qianxing suddenly remembered:

—"Speaking of which, where is Eldest Senior Brother? What did he have to say about this power? Couldn't he sniff out a sliver of Master's condition from it?"

—"Eldest Senior Brother isn't a dog,"-Ran Deng retorted. Yet while the man wasn't a canine, he was better than one. If he were around, he genuinely might have sniffed out Master's exact situation. Remembering this, she grumbled:—"And you know him. Whenever he's actually needed, he's nowhere to be found... Come to think of it, Master's decision to enter the mystic realm seemed to have something to do with him too."

Ran Deng recounted Yuwen Xuan's earlier tournament match:

—"After the match, who knows if his conscience got the better of him, but the last time I saw him, he looked like he was packing his bags and skipping town."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Qu Qianxing offered no commentary.

The stunts Eldest Senior Brother pulled were forever beyond mortal comprehension or critique.

He even began to suspect whether this wildly overflowing power had something to do with Eldest Senior Brother as well—after all, since Yuwen Xuan was no longer at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he could be anywhere, including right at Master's side.

—"Second Senior Brother, what should we do now?"-Ran Deng asked, unwilling to waste further breath on Yuwen Xuan. When push came to shove, Second Senior Brother was still the most reliable of all the siblings. She asked in concern:—"Setting aside what this power actually is, right now it's not only flooding the grand array, but also diffusing outward through it. I can't stop it at all. What should we do?"

—"Mm..."-Qu Qianxing knitted his brows.

Not being on the scene, he had no way to accurately gauge the grand array's status. But if Ran Deng, a dedicated formation master, couldn't halt it, then even if he were there in person, he likely couldn't stop it either.

Recognizing this reality, Qu Qianxing instructed:

—"If you truly can't halt it, do your best to collect and store the power! Also, compile statistical records of both the influx and the escaped energy. I—"

—"Demon Sovereign, My Lord."

Just as Qu Qianxing was deliberating over the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array with Ran Deng, an entity abruptly cut into their conversation.

Qu Qianxing frowned and glanced down to see a demon kneeling submissively at his feet.

No—far more than just this single demon.

At this moment, Qu Qianxing was seated atop a crimson dais forged from bleached bones and raw sinew. Flanking him stood several non-humanoid demons—some bristling with squirming tentacles, some covered in oozing pustules, some grotesque, colossal spheres of meat—each horrifying beyond words. Beneath the dais, hundreds of thousands of similarly monstrous demons clustered in a vast, dark expanse. Though filthy, chaotic, and miasmic, they somehow held a faint semblance of a military formation. They were noticeably quieter than standard demonic hordes, which only rendered the atmosphere infinitely more eerie—a spectacle terrifying enough to drive any ordinary onlooker completely insane at a single glance.

Ran Deng's [sanity meter](# "Literal CN: SAN值 (Sanity Value) — Cthulhu mythos term commonly used in Chinese internet culture for mental stability") came within a hair's breadth of collapsing entirely.

While conversing with her, Qu Qianxing had allowed the newly supercharged grand array to extend to his side, which inadvertently granted Ran Deng a visual feed of his surroundings. Engrossed in their conversation, she hadn't noticed the expanded array permissions until that demon spoke. Peeking curiously at Qu Qianxing's environment, she was nearly scared to death.

Fortunately, Master's life lamp was right beside her. Ran Deng threw her arms around the lamp, clinging to it for dear life. Only its gentle, luminous radiance kept her heart from stopping and slowly restored warmth to her freezing limbs.

Needless to say, Ran Deng didn't dare cast another glance anywhere else.

Fixing her eyes strictly on Qu Qianxing's gorgeous face for several minutes, she finally managed to choke out a question:

—"What... what is going on over on your end?"-Ran Deng asked shakily.

—"War,"-Qu Qianxing replied nonchalantly.-"Didn't Fifth Junior Brother tell you? I'm stationed on the frontlines of the Immortal-Demon War. These are the troops under the 'Demon Sovereign'."

[No! My question was, why the hell are you so calm?!]

The corners of Ran Deng's mouth twitched violently.

She knew Qu Qianxing was masquerading as some Demon Sovereign on the frontlines of the Immortal-Demon War commanding demonic hordes, but she never imagined the so-called demon army looked so profoundly [grotesque](# "Literal CN: 猎奇 (lieqi) — bizarre, freakish, or eldritch"). An ordinary human standing amidst them would feel their marrow dissolve into bloody water. It was a miracle Qu Qianxing could blend in with such a stunning face... Or rather, it wasn't unfitting at all.

Qu Qianxing's features had always been extraordinarily breathtaking. Back at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, with Master above whose transcendent spiritual aura suppressed the mortal realm, and Lu Shulong below whose True Dragon blood rivaled his splendor, he had seemed somewhat "ordinary." Yet standing here among the demons, whether through his innate nature or the sharp contrast with their hideous forms, Qu Qianxing's beauty was heart-stopping—resembling an exquisite masterpiece sculpted from primordial terror. Standing among the demons, he looked not out of place, but as though he belonged here by divine right.

Once that realization dawned on her, Ran Deng couldn't even look him in the face anymore.

Qu Qianxing was oblivious to the fact that he had thoroughly traumatized his junior sister. Having squatted in this godforsaken pit for so long, he had seen every conceivable variety of demon and was thoroughly desensitized.

Seeing that Ran Deng fell silent, Qu Qianxing turned his gaze toward the demon who had interrupted them.

He recalled this creature was an intelligence officer he had dispatched to the absolute vanguard. It had no business being here; its sudden return meant something significant must have transpired on the frontlines.

—"What? Routed again?"-Qu Qianxing asked, completely unbothered.

—"H-How could that be? Under the formations personally directed by Demon Sovereign, My Lord, our demonic legions have been invincible these days!"-the demon intelligence officer flattered in trembling reverence.

It was not entirely empty sycophancy.

Demons were naturally ferocious and born for slaughter, their individual prowess far outstripping that of ordinary celestial soldiers—which was precisely why they had caused the Heavenly Court endless headaches across protracted wars. Their fatal flaw was a universal lack of intellect and cohesive tactics. Even the Demon Emperor could command only a handful of mid-to-high-tier demons capable of understanding human speech, which alone granted the Heavenly Court breathing room.

The examinations introduced by this "Demon Sovereign" had, to a certain degree, solved that very problem.

Before written test papers, the demons were effortlessly categorized into:
- [Completely brainless line-fillers](# "Literal CN: 填线宝宝 (line-filling teletubbies) — Chinese gaming slang for expendable cannon fodder infantry deployed merely to plug gaps on the frontline")
- Useful pawns with a shred of wit, suitable for maintaining formations
- Combat bioweapons endowed with intellect but stripped of humanity
- Highly intelligent technical specialists capable of high-precision operations
...and so forth!

Categorizing them strictly by intellectual tier, Qu Qianxing adjusted their ranks, tossed them onto the battlefield, and reaped instantaneous, devastating results.

Yet in Qu Qianxing's eyes, the utility was pitifully modest.

So much so that the demon's flattering praise sounded virtually indistinguishable from sarcasm.

—"Formations? Those so-called formations are simple enough for human children to master, yet you lot can't even obey basic commands. And with this you expect victory? It's only because time is running short; otherwise, I would thoroughly educate every last one of you,"-Qu Qianxing snapped, deeply displeased.

At his words, every demon present—no matter how hideous or horrifying their visage—paled in sheer terror.

[Haven't we been "educated" enough?!]

The demons thought in sheer panic.

Initially, upon hearing that military ranks would be assigned according to test scores, and that high-scoring demons would earn the Demon Sovereign's favor and even audience with the Demon Emperor, those self-proclaimed "intellectual" demons had rejoiced, believing their day of glory had arrived.

As it turned out, that so-called "examination" was merely a novel method of torture devised by Demon Sovereign, My Lord!

Score poorly, and you were hurled into the most lethal meat grinders. Score well, and you were detained to study mind-numbing, dry lore that felt like pouring wet concrete directly into your skull! Either outcome was pure agony. Had this Demon Sovereign not radiated such terrifying might, the demons would have mutinied long ago.

In fact, they had nearly launched a revolt today. But moments ago, the "demonic aura" emanating from the Demon Sovereign had abruptly surged several tiers higher. Merely standing nearby made them feel as though they would be crushed to paste by the sheer gravitational weight of his power, forcing them to tuck away their treasonous schemes once and for all.

Fortunately, Qu Qianxing harbored little genuine passion for educating demons.

His motive for examining the demons was never to defeat the Heavenly Court in the first place, but to figure out what this "Demon Sovereign" identity truly entailed, while concurrently scouting the demon race's nature. Judging by the fact that Master had personally journeyed to a mystic realm to recruit Ran Zhen for array design, this intelligence gathering was critically essential.

And the [Diagnostic Placement Exam](# "Literal CN: 摸底考试 (mōdǐ kǎoshì) — initial assessment test used to determine students' baseline levels") had proven remarkably effective!

Qu Qianxing already had foundational datasets on the demonic race in his grasp.

He discovered that the human race's broad categorization of all hazardous entities as "demons" was extraordinarily crude. Based on differences in origin, power, and inherent nature, demons spanned countless distinct factions: some could be reformed through education, some had to be slaughtered on sight, and some were doomed to spontaneously generate within a world no matter how rigorously one sealed its borders. Blindly shunning them while awaiting salvation from the Heavenly Court would only trap humans in an increasingly disadvantageous position.

[—"To a certain extent, they are existences that 'inevitably come into being.' Perhaps we must understand them, divide them, and assimilate them before we can resolve the threat once and for all... though, not right now."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Qu Qianxing pondered, jotting down a footnote in his research ledger.

Since he had no intention of aiding the demons in vanquishing the Heavenly Court, the casualties and outcome of the war were of zero consequence to him. Qu Qianxing was prosecuting this conflict purely with the detached mindset of an academic researcher.

He didn't even bother concealing this attitude from the demons, questioning the intelligence officer with frosty detachment:

—"Speak! What occurred at the vanguard? If not a defeat, was it an insurrection? A mutiny? Or something else?"-Here, Qu Qianxing glanced toward another demon stationed beside the stairs:—"Have the Third Reference Squad stand by to plug any personnel deficits at a moment's notice."

—"Understood,"-the demon answered reverently.

They held no grievances against Qu Qianxing's cold indifference and cruelty.

If anything, every Demon Sovereign and Demon Emperor across history had been identical: regarding the lives of low-tier demons as lighter than dust. Seeing as the demons didn't value their own lives either, Qu Qianxing's behavior actually commanded their complete, unquestioning submission. Even if countless demons could not stomach the exams, not a single one dared doubt him anymore. Or rather, it was precisely the examinations that solidified their reverence for Demon Sovereign, My Lord's terrifying might and merciless cruelty.

The intelligence officer didn't dare dispute Qu Qianxing's words, groveling obsequiously:

—"As expected of Demon Sovereign, My Lord! Small-scale skirmishes and desertions did indeed occur at the front, but they are standard routine, well within your calculations. Th-The only unexpected development is that Lord Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King has arrived!"

—"Lord Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King?"-Qu Qianxing lifted his eyes, casting a cool glance down.

Though it was merely a glance from a stunning beauty, the intelligence officer melted like warm butter onto the floorboards:

—"N-No, not a 'Lord'... j-just a Celestial General."

—"A Celestial General?"-Qu Qianxing raised an eyebrow.-"Judging by your tone, not an ordinary one?"

While gathering data on the demon race, he had naturally cataloged information regarding celestial soldiers and generals.

Yet in Qu Qianxing's evaluation, harvestable intelligence on the Heavenly Court remained scarce. Just as he utilized brainless low-tier demons as biological specimens and line-fillers, the Heavenly Court likewise deployed ascenders who had yet to secure official appointments as cannon fodder. Because these ascenders were not genuine celestial officials, their deaths were disposable and provided Qu Qianxing with virtually no valuable data.

The only marginally useful assets were the "Celestial Generals." Unfortunately, they were shockingly frail—sometimes even weaker than unappointed ascenders. Once their outer defenses were breached and they fell into the demon ranks, the hordes tore them to ribbons in an instant before any intact "samples" could be gathered. To make matters worse, squabbling over consuming these generals frequently incited mutinies among the demon troops Qu Qianxing had painstakingly corralled.

Qu Qianxing found this deeply frustrating; he had been longing to capture a complete, intact specimen for ages.

Now that a seemingly unique specimen had presented itself, Qu Qianxing took an interest:

—"What rank is this Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King?"

—"He is one of the highest-ranking Heavenly Kings in the Heavenly Court,"-the intelligence officer reported. It couldn't fathom why Demon Sovereign wouldn't know something common knowledge to every demon on the battlefield, but dared not question him, whimpering softly:—"And he is exceptionally formidable. The previous Demon Emperor was defeated and slain by his hand."

—"Oh? Sounds like a grand commander,"-Qu Qianxing murmured.

—"However, he rarely graces the battlefield, usually appearing only to confront the Demon Emperor in person. This time, your military achievements were simply too dazzling, which actually baited him to the frontlines,"-the intelligence officer prostrated itself and reported.

—"Whoa. Sounds like you're pretty incredible, Second Senior Brother,"-Ran Deng chimed in, having recovered her wits just in time to hear the demon's report, unable to resist teasing him.

Qu Qianxing felt no pride in his accomplishments:

—"Is that really necessary? I don't feel like I've won many times. This demon mob has zero military discipline; no matter how I drill them, they never learn. I barely advanced a fraction of a step, and at least a third of them deserted."

[But you advanced and conquered three entire uninhabited minor worlds!]

[And strategically pivotal ones at that! Even someone without a military bone in their body can see that if you advance just a hair further, you'll be chewing straight into the Heavenly Court's backyard!]

The demons screamed inwardly.

They would have loved nothing more than to sing hymns of praise, but they knew all too well that Demon Sovereign, My Lord genuinely despised the chaotic performance of the demonic horde. Even when securing "a modest victory," it fell leagues short of his pedagogical expectations. Last time, in a fit of irritation, he had decapitated a sycophant for unsolicited flattery and hung the head from the flagpole outside the command pavilion.

Even now, Demon Sovereign did not believe it was about him:

—"That Heavenly King rushing over out of nowhere—could it be because the Heavenly Court caught wind that the Demon Emperor is personally leading an expedition? Speaking of which, where is His Imperial Majesty the Demon Emperor? When exactly will he arrive?"

—"Word has it he has already entered the Immortal-Demon Boundary, and should arrive in roughly [one ten-day cycle](# "Literal CN: 一旬 (yī xún) — a ten-day period in the traditional Chinese lunar calendar"),"-the officer answered.

—"Is that so?"-Qu Qianxing muttered.

Having whipped the demonic forces into shape, Qu Qianxing enjoyed far greater operational freedom than before. He could now slip away undetected without alerting the other demons. Qu Qianxing even suspected that if he suddenly "went missing," these wretches might celebrate the cessation of formation drills and pop quizzes with unbridled joy. Thus, he had been hesitating on whether to meet the Demon Emperor at all.

His cautious nature urged him not to take reckless gambles, yet sensing the bizarre connection between the true Demon Sovereign and himself, he was tempted to investigate further.

It left Qu Qianxing in a rare state of indecision.

Watching Qu Qianxing sink into contemplation, the surrounding demons exchanged glances before one ventured timidly:

—"Since the Heavenly Court might be aiming for His Imperial Majesty, should we... perhaps avoid engagement for now?"

Naturally, they believed no such thing in their hearts.

Every demon present was convinced the Heavenly Court was coming strictly for Demon Sovereign, My Lord, but that didn't prevent them from deflecting responsibility.

It wasn't that they feared the battlefield. If anything, they would rather march straight into combat—under this Demon Sovereign, the odds of victory were at an all-time high. What terrified them was "training"! Ordinary skirmishes required drilling multiple formations and tactical coordination; facing the Heavenly Court's elite deployments, with Second Demon Sovereign's pathological perfectionism, he would drill them within an inch of their lives.

Yet precisely because the Demon Emperor was en route, whether to hoard military glory or to display battle achievements, the Demon Sovereign surely wouldn't forgo combat.

Word even had it that this war had been initiated partly to intimidate the Second Demon Sovereign. But if the Second Demon Sovereign crushed the Heavenly Court single-handedly, the Demon Emperor's already precarious throne would likely...

Just as the demons were convincing themselves of this, they heard Demon Sovereign declare:

—"Fine. We'll avoid engagement."

—"Ah, as expected we must fight... Wait, avoid?!"-The demons stared in horror at the "Demon Sovereign," hearing him choose evasion for the first time in memory.

—"Yes, avoid,"-Qu Qianxing confirmed.

Qu Qianxing paid no heed to the demons' bewildered expressions; he only saw a golden opportunity:

A prime opportunity to [reap the spoils without lifting a finger](# "Literal CN: fisherman's profit (渔翁得利) — from the fable of the snipe and clam grappling while the fisherman nets both").

If the Heavenly Court's commander and the inspecting Demon Emperor collided head-on, wouldn't he glean intelligence on both factions in a single stroke? To Qu Qianxing, that was the optimal outcome.

A thought flashed through Qu Qianxing's mind, and the orders rolled out smoothly:

—"What are you standing around gaping for? Commence withdrawal... No, don't make the retreat too conspicuous. Execute Protocol Fifty-Six: the vanguard continues skirmishing to stall for time, while we take the other flank to welcome His Imperial Majesty the Demon Emperor—"

—"Demon Sovereign, My Lord!"

Just as Qu Qianxing was plotting how to orchestrate a direct clash between the Demon Emperor and the Heavenly King, another demon came sprinting over in frantic haste.

This newcomer was a surveyor Qu Qianxing had dispatched to map the battlefield. Among the brainless demon hordes, such creatures were rare endangered specimens: they could actually learn mathematics!

For that reason, Qu Qianxing granted them preferential treatment. Yet right now, the surveyor appeared thoroughly panicked:

—"Demon Sovereign, My Lord! For reasons unknown, starting from [three shichen](# "Literal CN: 三个时辰 — traditional Chinese time unit, equivalent to six hours") ago, spatial telemetry has undergone colossal fluctuations!"

—"On a battlefield, spatial fluctuations are perfectly normal, aren't they? Especially with the Heavenly Court dispatching a commander of Heavenly King caliber,"-another demon scoffed dismissively.

Ignoring the heckler, the surveyor handed a thick stack of telemetry records directly to Qu Qianxing:

—"It does not resemble disturbances caused by mere individual power; rather, a colossal force is tugging the spatial coordinates of the entire battlefield, shifting them wholesale!"

—"The entire battlefield?"-Qu Qianxing knitted his brows, leafing through the numbers.

He, too, found the data somewhat outrageous:

—"Could you lot..."-have made an error?

—"Wait! Second Senior Brother, don't move!"-Ran Deng, who had been silently listening in, suddenly shrieked in Qu Qianxing's mind.

Startled, Qu Qianxing checked his words and spread the documents flat, making it convenient for his junior sister to inspect them through the scrying link.

—"Is there an issue?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"The coordinates... these spatial coordinates look terrifyingly familiar! They resemble the coordinates of the Immortal-Demon battlefield on our side,"-Ran Deng said.-"Hold on, let me go fetch Mo Xi."

—"Immortal-Demon battlefield?"-Qu Qianxing froze, completely in the dark regarding the sect's current affairs.-"A war is raging on your end too?"

Unfortunately, Ran Deng had no time to answer him.

A short while later, Mo Xi's voice rang out through the communication line:

—"Those are indeed the coordinates of the Immortal-Demon War... [Holy shit!](# "Literal CN: 艹 (cǎo) — internet profanity, short for 操 (fuck)") Not only that, someone is hacking and rewriting my script! The two battlefields are overlapping!"

—"What on earth are you talking about?"-Qu Qianxing's confusion deepened.

—"What I mean is... Second Senior Brother, are you willing to fulfill a certain prophecy and become the Demon Sovereign who annihilates the Heavenly Court?"-Mo Xi asked out of the blue.

Ran Deng gasped in sudden realization:

—"Ah! No wonder when I looked at your battlefield just now, it felt bizarrely familiar and eerily fitting for you, Second Senior Brother! So it was that 'prophecy'?!"

*Huh?*

Qu Qianxing could only conclude that having been away from the sect for less than a single month, he was already incapable of comprehending a single word his junior brother and sister were saying.
