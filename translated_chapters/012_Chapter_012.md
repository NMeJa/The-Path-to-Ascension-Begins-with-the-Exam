# Chapter 12: [Since We're Already Here](# "Literal CN: 来都来了 (lái dōu lái le) — one of China's iconic Four Great Excuses: 'Since we've already come all this way, we might as well...'")

[The sixth day of the sixth lunar month: the Festival of Heavenly Gifts.](# "Literal CN: 天贶日 (tiān kuàng rì) — a traditional holiday commemorating the Heavenly Emperor granting sacred scriptures; traditionally designated for airing out books and robes under the bright summer sun")

This was an auspicious day.

From the crack of dawn, not a single cloud marred the sky for ten thousand li. The brilliant sun shone overhead—a perfect day for drying out books, and an equally splendid day for the Heavenly Spirit Sect to hold its first-ever Grand Unified Examination.

Although the sect disciples had not yet realized what 'examinations' would truly signify for their lives, none dared defy the decree of an Immortal Venerable. Early in the morning, everyone headed toward the examination hall to make their preparations.

Even the handymen and servants exempt from testing gathered in front of the Palace of Silence whenever they had a spare moment to [gawk at the spectacle](# "Literal CN: 看热闹(X)观摩 — web novel formatting joke crossing out the crude truth ('watching the commotion') to replace it with a dignified excuse ('scholarly observation')") (cross that out—*conduct scholarly observation*).

In the distant future, once they finally grasped the paramount significance of the Grand Unified Examination, this custom would be handed down across endless generations, eventually crystallizing into a cherished Heavenly Spirit Sect tradition.

Yet as everyone's focus remained locked upon the Grand Unified Examination, none noticed that an object was steadily approaching through the sky.

It was a procession of carriages.

Leading the way was a flock of white cranes—sixty-six in total. Their snowy wings stretched wide, unfurling like vast drifts of white clouds.

Tethered to the foremost twelve white cranes with five-colored reins were three small red war chariots arranged in a wedge formation—one in front, two behind. Upon each chariot stood two silver-armored cultivators.

Directly behind the small chariots rolled a massive silver war chariot carrying six gold-armored cultivators, requiring twenty-six white cranes just to haul it forward.

And behind this colossal silver chariot trailed an exquisitely compact cloud carriage.

This cloud carriage was drawn by eight spiritual deer, each entirely snow-white without a single blemish of color. Whenever their hooves came down, accompanied by the crisp chiming of deer bells, ripples of multicolored luminescence bloomed beneath their feet. These glowing streams drifted backward to envelop the canopy of the exquisite carriage, weaving a halo of resplendent rainbow light.

From this sheer spectacle alone, one could tell this was a pomp and majesty no ordinary cultivator could ever possess.

And indeed, it was so. Atop the trailing escort carriages fluttered an array of banners. Embroidered upon the grandest banner among them was the character 'Alliance' (*Meng*)—the emblem of the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

The emissaries of the Heavenly Dao Alliance were seated precisely within that cloud carriage.

There were two of them: one old, one young.

The elder had [leopard-like round eyes](# "Literal CN: 豹眼苍髯 (bào yǎn cāng rán) — fierce, round leopard eyes and graying beard; classic physical trope for a gruff, imposing warrior like Zhang Fei") and a grizzled beard, his shoulders broad and waist thick. He looked less like a refined cultivator and more like a rugged martial warrior, yet the pressure radiating from his frame was the genuine, unadulterated aura of a Body Integration stage powerhouse.

The youth, by contrast, possessed merely the cultivation of the Foundation Establishment stage. He wore an Eight-Treasure Gold Crown studded with luminous pearls, possessing a delicate, powder-pale countenance. Clad in brocade and jade robes, he reclined languidly against the carriage window while an entourage of lovely maidservants served tea and poured water. Despite the carriage's deceptively compact exterior, its interior was as opulent and spacious as a grand hall, holding over a dozen maidservants with room to spare.

Yet even pampered by such a bevy of beauties, the youth still wore an expression of utter boredom.

—"What kind of destitute backwater is this?"-the youth grumbled, gazing down at the scenery.-"Does that so-called [Un-Grinding Immortal Venerable](# "Literal CN: 不卷仙尊 (Bùjuǎn Xiānzūn) — Su Xin derisively mispronounces Zhou Hui's title 'Untiring Immortal Venerable' (不倦仙尊, Bùjuàn Xiānzūn), replacing '倦' (untiring) with '卷' (juǎn, involution/grinding) to mock it as an uncompetitive backwater bumpkin title") actually live in a godforsaken dump like this?!"

—"Little Martial Uncle, please watch your words!"-the elder cautioned, his leopard eyes widening in panic.

—"Tch. Why? What's he going to do, kill me?"-the youth scoffed flippantly.

The elder clenched his fists, completely helpless against the boy.

For just as he had addressed him, this boy was his 'Little Martial Uncle.'

He Yong, the elder in question, hailed from the Revenue Hall of the Heavenly Dao Alliance. He was the disciple of the Revenue Hall Master, Heyunzi. And Heyunzi's masters were none other than the legendary immortal Daoist couple of the Heavenly Dao Alliance: Su Mei and Xin Zhu.

This Daoist couple was renowned not only because both were supreme Tribulation Transcendence stage heavyweights, but even more so for their legendary devotion to each other.

Born on the exact same year, month, and day, they had been childhood sweethearts who worshipped the same master and entered the same sect. Supporting each other through thick and thin as they pursued the Grand Dao together, both astonishingly broke through Mahayana and stepped into the Tribulation Transcendence stage. Not only that, but on their joint 8,888th birthday, they were blessed with a precious son whom they named Su Xin—the very youth He Yong addressed as 'Little Martial Uncle.'

Su Xin's seniority in the generational hierarchy was, naturally, terrifyingly immense.

From the moment of his birth, he possessed a pair of Tribulation Transcendence parents and several Mahayana senior martial brothers. Martial nephews at the Body Integration and Void Refinement stages like He Yong numbered more than a dozen, while grand-disciples and great-grand-disciples at the Soul Formation and Nascent Soul stages were too numerous to count.

In the entire cultivation world, almost no one possessed the authority to discipline Su Xin. To make matters worse, Su Mei and Xin Zhu had conceived him so late in their lifespans that they doted on him unconditionally, spoiling Su Xin into an untouchable little ancestor.

Yet instead of throwing his weight around at the Heavenly Dao Alliance headquarters, this little ancestor had run off to this 'destitute backwater' and insisted on coming along to pay respects to the Untiring Immortal Venerable—a headache of catastrophic proportions for He Yong.

Particularly now that their cloud carriage had arrived over the master's mountain peak. Gossiping about a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse in their own domain—especially speaking their sacred Daoist title aloud—was practically identical to whispering slander right into their ear. It was impossible for him not to hear.

He Yong was naturally beside himself with anxiety:

—"His Eminence is an eminent senior who does not stoop to the level of juniors, and out of respect for your parents, he likely won't take offense,"-He Yong pleaded.-"Even so, disparaging another's sect behind their back is, at the end of the day, terribly ill-mannered."

These words of He Yong's were, in truth, spoken for Zhou Hui's ears.

He was terrified that in a fit of rage, Zhou Hui might obliterate this little ancestor on the spot. If that happened, not even Tribulation Transcendence elders of the Heavenly Dao Alliance could protect their son from ten thousand li away.

And taking ten thousand steps back, even if this ancestor didn't perish and merely suffered a scratch, the one taking the fall would still be him, the 'martial nephew.'

He Yong could only redouble his earnest pleading:

—"Furthermore, the Untiring Immortal Venerable lives frugally, disdains indulgence, and cherishes tranquility. That is why he chose Mount Tianling, an earthly paradise untainted by the mundane world, as his sanctuary for peaceful cultivation. We have already disturbed his peace by coming to seek his counsel—how could we possibly—"

Yet Su Xin showed not the slightest appreciation for his efforts:

—"Heh, what earthly paradise?"-Su Xin sneered.-"Only [wild cultivators](# "Literal CN: 野路子出身的散修 (yě lùzi chūshēn de sǎnxiū) — wild, unorthodox rogue cultivators lacking aristocratic or premier sect lineage") from rogue, unrefined backgrounds would treat a [place where birds don't even shit](# "Literal CN: 鸟不拉屎的地方 (niǎo bù lā shǐ de dìfang) — colorful Chinese idiom for an isolated, godforsaken backwater") like it's something special."

—"Su Xin!"-He Yong bellowed.

That phrase 'wild cultivators from rogue backgrounds' scared a cold sweat straight through He Yong's robes.

Although cultivators hailing from premier 'orthodox' sects often muttered such petty slurs out of green-eyed envy whenever Zhou Hui was brought up, not a single one among them possessed the sheer audacity to utter it atop the Untiring Immortal Venerable's own mountain.

How was this any different from an outright declaration of war?

—"Hahaha! Are you really that terrified?"-Su Xin burst into spiteful laughter.-"Relax. I'm not an idiot. Words spoken in here won't ever be overheard by that [Un-Grinding Immortal](# "Literal CN: 不卷仙人 (Bùjuǎn Xiānrén) — mocking title replacing 'untiring' with 'non-involuted/un-grinding'")!"

[—"Won't be overheard?"](# "Speaker: He Yong")

—"Naturally! What do you think we're riding in?"-Su Xin declared smugly.

Patting the carriage wall, his face beamed with boastful pride:

—"This White Deer Cloud-Chasing Carriage was forged by a True Immortal of the upper realm—a genuine, unadulterated immortal treasure! Never mind a backwater rogue immortal, even if my parents attacked it together, they couldn't damage this carriage in short order, let alone breach its protective barrier and use divine sense to eavesdrop on us... Aaargh?!"

Su Xin shrieked as the cloud carriage violently lurched, frightening the surrounding maidservants until their faces turned pale with terror.

Not only that, but a frantic chorus of crane cries echoed from outside. Clearly, the carriage was not the only thing caught in this 'assault.'

At this moment, He Yong actually regained his composure.

—"No, this isn't the Untiring Immortal Venerable. If he took action, it would be far cleaner than this,"-He Yong muttered. He shouted toward the outside:-"What happened?!"

—"True Monarch He, we appear to have run into the spiritual turbulence surrounding the Heavenly Spirit Palace!"-a gold-armored warrior replied from outside.

[—"Heavenly Spirit Palace?"](# "Speaker: He Yong")

—"It... it seems to be the Heavenly Spirit Sect's palace complex!"-the warrior stammered, evidently bewildered.

—"Since when does the Heavenly Spirit Sect have a palace?"-He Yong asked, thoroughly baffled.

As the Hall Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Central Domain Abyssal Ocean Branch, he was tasked with overseeing the cultivation sects of this territory. And the Heavenly Spirit Sect, harboring a supreme Tribulation Transcendence heavyweight, was classified as a Grade-A entity of supreme concern.

This was far from He Yong's first visit to Mount Tianling. He considered himself intimately familiar with the layout of the sect, well aware that aside from the Library Pavilion and the Ascension Platform, which were passably decent, the rest of the sect's architecture could practically be described as rough concrete shells. There were no palaces to speak of—unless one felt compelled to flatter the Untiring Immortal Venerable by shutting one's eyes and blowing hot air about those few large halls bearing names like 'XX Palace.'

Spreading his spiritual energy outward to soothe the panicked spirit beasts, He Yong pushed open the carriage door and peered outside.

And then, he froze.

There, suspended above the mountain peaks beneath the blazing afternoon sun, hung a dazzling 'silver moon.' Only upon squinting did he realize that it was an enormous... sundial?

It was probably a sundial, though somewhat different from ordinary ones. He Yong could not be entirely certain. Yet what captured his gaze far more was the 'palace' situated within the folds of Mount Tianling beneath that gigantic dial.

Wreathed in misty clouds, half-illusion and half-reality, exquisite pavilions, terraces, and towers floated hazily amid the mountains—seemingly ethereal and insubstantial, yet undeniably, tangibly present.

[—"A Sea Clan palace complex?! How could such a thing appear in the middle of mountains?!"](# "Speaker: He Yong")

By now, Su Xin had also made his way to the carriage door, staring at the palace in utter astonishment.

Su Xin quickly realized it wasn't constructed by the Sea Clan. Pausing, he turned to He Yong with a bizarre expression:

—"I always thought only the Dragon Clan could afford to be this lavish,"-Su Xin remarked, his tone dripping with skepticism.-"Are you positive that belongs to the 'frugal and peace-loving' Un-Grinding Immortal?"

—"...It is the Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-He Yong replied dryly.

He had no idea how to explain it either; this was utterly unlike Zhou Hui's style.

Still, hovering here accomplished nothing. They would know once they went over to see.

Reaching into his robes, He Yong retrieved an exquisite visiting card and puffed a gentle breath across it.

The visiting card instantly transformed into a delicate lark, winging its way toward the misty palace. At the same time, He Yong channeled his technique and declared in a resonant voice:

—"He Yong, Hall Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Central Domain Abyssal Ocean Branch, comes to pay respects to the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"

Soon enough, a hearty, laughing voice echoed from within Mount Tianling:

—"A guest is a guest! Welcome, welcome!"

—"...That is the Untiring Immortal Venerable's eldest disciple, Yuwen Xuan,"-He Yong introduced to Su Xin.

The hierarchy of the cultivation world was strict. On occasions such as this, Zhou Hui, as a supreme Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, would never come out in person to greet guests. Having his eldest disciple present was more than enough courtesy to the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Furthermore, alongside Yuwen Xuan's greeting, a bridge of rainbow light vaulted out from the palace nestled in the mountains, extending all the way beneath the convoy to guide He Yong's party forward.

—"Tch. Nothing special,"-Su Xin clicked his tongue in dissatisfaction.

He was evidently displeased that a 'backcountry rogue immortal' could put on such an ostentatious display.

He Yong did not waste breath on this impossible-to-please little ancestor, simply ordering the cloud carriage to proceed along the rainbow road.

Before long, at the terminus of the rainbow, they spotted Yuwen Xuan waiting alongside two of his junior martial siblings and a retinue of servants.

—"Hall Master He, long time no see!"-Yuwen Xuan stepped forward with boisterous warmth.

Anyone unfamiliar with them would have assumed he and He Yong were bosom friends, when in truth they had met only three or four times.

Yet Yuwen Xuan acted as if he were greeting his own long-lost kin:

—"You couldn't have arrived at a better time! In his recent heavenly tribulation, my master comprehended the secrets of heaven! He is currently holding a Grand Unified Examination to select the fated individual destined for these heavenly revelations!"

—"Heavenly secrets? A Grand Unified Examination?"-He Yong gasped in feigned surprise, as if hearing of this for the very first time.

In reality, he had already caught wind of the rumors; confirming their veracity was half the reason for his visit. He had hardly expected Yuwen Xuan to blurt it out the moment they met.

[Had Zhou Hui truly discovered the path to Ascension?]

He Yong's curiosity burned, eager to press further, but Yuwen Xuan said nothing more on the subject, turning his gaze toward Su Xin instead:

—"And this is...?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

—"...This is an emissary newly dispatched from the Heavenly Dao Alliance headquarters,"-He Yong stated stiffly.

This was the cover story he and Su Xin had agreed upon beforehand.

After all, Su Xin's background was so extraordinary that revealing it would escalate this visit to an exceedingly complicated diplomatic level.

Though looking at Su Xin's extravagant get-up and foul temper, He Yong was deeply worried whether anyone would actually buy such an explanation.

Fortunately, Yuwen Xuan appeared entirely devoid of suspicion:

—"Lord Emissary? What an immense honor! One look at your noble bearing reveals you are a [dragon among men](# "Literal CN: 人中龙凤 (rén zhōng lóng fèng) — dragon and phoenix among men, an outstanding genius destined for greatness")! Your age is just right as well—would you care to sit for our Grand Unified Examination? Who knows, perhaps you are the [Child of Destiny](# "Literal CN: 天命之子 (tiānmìng zhī zǐ) — the chosen one of destiny / protagonist of heaven")!"

—"Hmph. You certainly have a discerning eye!"-Su Xin tilted his chin up proudly.

—"Outsiders may participate as well?"-He Yong asked, growing slightly suspicious at how readily Yuwen Xuan had extended the invitation.

Yuwen Xuan waved a hand breezily:

—"The heavenly revelation never specified that it had to be a disciple of our Heavenly Spirit Sect. His Lordship arriving precisely on the day of the exam proves there is a bond of fate,"-Yuwen Xuan explained. Then he paused, as if belatedly realizing the impropriety:-"Of course, if Lord Emissary is unwilling, we can pretend I never mentioned it. I have already prepared wine and a banquet in the Hall of Embracing Emptiness. We could simply..."

—"No! I will take it!"-Su Xin cut in.

He didn't find Yuwen Xuan's behavior suspicious in the slightest.

To his mind, an affair like a 'sect grand examination' was likely just the typical 'sect tournament' that prominent cultivation sects regularly held. Many sects engaged in such friendly exchanges, and Su Xin 'won' practically every single time. He adored tournaments like this.

Even if there were no heavenly secrets involved, he wouldn't mind showing off a bit.

Just as this man had said, didn't he, Su Xin, look exceptionally peerless, endowed with natural immortal bones, a dragon among men, exquisitely clever, overflowing with talent, and standing head and shoulders above the masses?

—"If we're testing, then let's test!"-Su Xin declared smugly.-"I'll let you witness the true might of this Immortal Venerable. Lead the way!"

Su Xin was entirely oblivious to the exceedingly bizarre expressions that had crept onto the faces of Yuwen Xuan's junior brother and sister the moment those words left his mouth.
