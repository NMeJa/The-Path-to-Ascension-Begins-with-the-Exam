# Chapter 140: The Preparatory Bell (第140章 准备铃)

[← Previous Chapter](139_Chapter_139.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](141_Chapter_141.md)

---

Inside White Jade Capital, preparations to receive the students were already complete.

Yet aside from the members of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the other cultivators clearly [had ulterior motives](# "Literal CN: 醉翁之意不在酒 (zuì wēng zhī yì bù zài jiǔ) — the drunkard's interest lies not in the wine; having an ulterior motive or unspoken agenda"), curiously peeking at everything within White Jade Capital.

[—"To think White Jade Capital would see the light of day once more."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Its state of preservation is far too immaculate—it hardly looks like it survived a war."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"If this isn't White Jade Capital, what else could it be? Even the very first tier is this magnificent; one can scarcely fathom the majesty of the subsequent cities! Could you find any grander architecture in this entire realm?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Hey!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

One of the chatting cultivators tugged the other's sleeve, casting a surreptitious glance not far away.

At the other end of the avenue, Young Master Jian Nuo of Sky City—widely hailed as the "Premier Immortal City of the Modern Era"—was strolling past under Qu Qianxing's guidance, trailed by a listless Lu Shulong.

Flanked by two peerless beauties, Young Master Jian seemed oblivious to the cultivators' hushed whispers, appraising everything in the city with admiring eyes and showing little concern that White Jade Capital might challenge Sky City's status.

—"As expected of Sky City's young master—noble in bearing and magnanimous in conduct,"-a cultivator praised.

He wasn't merely kissing up; rather, he was genuinely relieved.

The vast majority of cultivators permitted to enter White Jade Capital this time were high-level powerhouses of the Void Refinement stage and above. Having already been gathered in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's academic lecture hall, they had naturally [taken advantage of their proximity to reap early benefits](# "Literal CN: 近水楼台先得月 (jìn shuǐ lóu tái xiān dé yuè) — the pavilion closest to the water catches the moonlight first; benefiting from proximity") once again. There was certainly no need for them to fawn over a mere Nascent Soul cultivator like Jian Nuo.

Yet Jian Nuo represented Sky City after all. In particular, the shadowy rumors of discord between Castellan Jian Haoge of Sky City and the Untiring Immortal Venerable had caused certain cultivators to worry that they might once again be forced to pick sides between White Jade Capital and Sky City.

...Wait, why "once again"?

At any rate, for Young Master Jian to get along harmoniously with the Heavenly Spirit Sect was the best possible outcome.

Unlike the one-sided situation when the Central Domain cultivators had previously had to choose sides between the Palace of Carefree Wandering and the Heavenly Spirit Sect, while some were concerned about Sky City's stance, they were a distinct minority. Upon hearing this, most cultivators merely showed dismissive expressions.

—"Young Master Jian's bearing is certainly fine, but don't you think the Immortal Venerable's two disciples possess far superior grace and beauty?"-someone chuckled.

—"You can't compare them like that,"-the earlier cultivator mumbled.

—"Why can't we compare them?"-another cultivator chimed in.-"Back in the day, the Immortal Venerable and the Castellan were constantly competing against each other. Why shouldn't their descendants and disciples be compared as well?"

[—"Speaking of which, between the Castellan and the Immortal Venerable, who actually won?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

—"*Cough, cough*—both are Tribulation Transcendence titans, so how could there be a clear winner or loser?!"-a cultivator spluttered.

[—"Right, exactly! Besides, weren't we discussing looks and charisma?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Then between Castellan Jian and the Untiring Immortal Venerable, who is more handsome?"](# "Speaker: Young Cultivator Spectator")

[—"*Hsss...*"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Crowd")

The air instantly plunged into silence. The gathered cultivators glared at the reckless young hothead who had asked, baffled as to how such an unhinged question could possibly have tumbled out of a person's mouth.

Yet after that heavy silence, someone actually ventured to answer this godforsaken question:

—"Naturally, both seniors possessed flawless visages; back then, people described them as brilliant as the sun and moon... However, Castellan Jian seemed to be slightly more popular?"-an older cultivator offered tentatively.

—"That was only because the Immortal Venerable was more focused on cultivation! Speaking of popularity, I heard that Castellan Jian's Dao companion, Fairy Yutian, was actually in love with the Untiring Immortal Venerable at first!"-another added.

[—"Really? Then how did Castellan Jian manage to [snatch her away](# "Literal CN: 横刀夺爱 (héng dāo duó ài) — brandish a blade to rob love; steal someone's beloved")?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

—"Oh, come off it. Yutian was harboring unrequited love; Lord Untiring never gave her a second glance. Later on, when she realized Castellan Jian shared a common hobby with her, the two kindred spirits got together,"-Fairy Xia joined the conversation.

[—"What common hobby?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Ah, probably... the Untiring Immortal Venerable?"](# "Speaker: Fairy Xia")

"..."

Fairy Xia's remark plunged the cultivators into yet another stunned silence.

---

While the cultivators on that side gradually forgot their original purpose as the chatter devolved into scandalous gossip, over on the other side, Jian Nuo and Qu Qianxing were also discussing the visiting cultivators and White Jade Capital—though their topic was vastly more serious and dignified:

—"Is passing the trial truly a mandatory requirement to reside in White Jade Capital?"-Jian Nuo asked, looking at the cultivators lining both sides of the avenue.

Even though White Jade Capital was open today, that hardly meant the Untiring Immortal Venerable felt comfortable letting these cultivators wander about unchecked. Consequently, everyone's roaming range was strictly restricted to the vicinity of the First City's gates.

Yet White Jade Capital was indeed worthy of its title as the center of the ancient cultivation world and the most colossal metropolis recorded in history. Even with only such a tiny area accessible, the cultivators still felt like tiny goldfish released into a vast lake, ridiculously miniscule.

This only accentuated how empty and devoid of life the entire city was.

Thus, even if someone were to ask Jian Nuo bluntly: *Are you worried White Jade Capital will eclipse Sky City?*, Jian Nuo would answer with absolute sincerity: *No.*

Because in his eyes, the life of a city lay in its "people."

Just as he felt every single morning when walking inward from Sky City's gates, only as the people within awoke did the city truly come alive.

As for White Jade Capital, its preservation—was it truly "preserved"? Although the Untiring Immortal Venerable claimed as much, everyone privately suspected that the Immortal Venerable had painstakingly "repaired" it, just like the Ascension Stairway. But what an unfathomable magnitude of power would that require! So while everyone speculated, none dared make a definitive conclusion. Well, regardless of how impeccably it was "preserved," the original inhabitants of White Jade Capital were long gone. Perhaps heroic phantoms still patrolled the ramparts in endless loops, but they were merely lingering projections of the past, possessing no self-awareness.

Jian Nuo felt that to truly restore White Jade Capital, having living people reside within was the only proper path forward.

Setting aside the entourage Jian Nuo had brought, the rest of the cultivators present today were mostly those who had recently congregated at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's academic lecture hall, or figures like Fairy Xia. It was precisely because they had been close at hand that they were able to rush over the moment the Untiring Immortal Venerable suddenly opened White Jade Capital.

Looked at another way, these individuals represented the topmost echelon of power in the Central Domain—and the faction most favorably inclined toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect at that. If the Untiring Immortal Venerable opened a special channel to allow them to take up residence in White Jade Capital, it would be equivalent to cementing his supreme authority over the Central Domain.

With utmost sincerity, Jian Nuo offered a suggestion on behalf of his idol:

—"If these grandmasters were permitted to reside in White Jade Capital, they would surely be immensely grateful to the Immortal Venerable, offering substantial assistance toward rebuilding White Jade Capital."

—"That sounds wonderful indeed,"-Qu Qianxing said with a gentle smile.-"Regrettably, the assessment criteria of the Ascension Stairway are not determined by the Heavenly Spirit Sect. It is, after all, the path to ascension; it has its own fundamental laws. Our Master, as its restorer, can merely utilize certain functions."

—"Yet given the Immortal Venerable's boundless might, I imagine altering the assessment criteria wouldn't be impossible,"-Jian Nuo said with an innocent look.

Listening to the two trade subtle barbs from behind, Lu Shulong silently cheered in his heart:

[Fight! Fight each other!]

He wished Qu Qianxing would immediately beat Jian Nuo to a pulp.

After all, today had originally been none of his business. It was solely because Jian Nuo had issued a "challenge" against him that Lu Shulong had been dragged out here for no good reason, saddled with a surprise "supplementary exam."

Unfortunately, even if Qu Qianxing harbored a few petty grievances toward Jian Nuo, it amounted at most to the urge to [toss a burlap sack over his head and beat him in an alley](# "Literal CN: 套麻袋 (tào mádài) — put a burlap sack over someone's head; ambush and beat someone covertly"). He had no intention of embarrassing Young Master Jian in public.

Performing his duties with meticulous care, he guided Jian Nuo through the permitted tour areas, placing special emphasis on today's focal point:

—"Young Master Jian, please look this way. This is the 'Ascension Terrace' we intend to utilize today."

—"The Ascension Terrace?"-Jian Nuo asked, looking over.

Beyond the city gates, an expansive platform adorned with exquisite engravings stretched outward into the open sky. Approaching the edge of the terrace and gazing downward, one could see turbulent currents of spiritual energy and demonic qi swirling above the Abyssal Ocean ruins into a colossal vortex that looked capable of devouring all living beings. From the heart of that terrifying maelstrom, the Ascension Stairway wound upward like a slender ribbon, connecting directly to the opposite edge of the platform.

The mere sight was enough to turn one's knees to jelly.

—"According to historical research, in the ancient cultivation realm, all living beings possessed the capacity to cultivate,"-Qu Qianxing explained.-"Thus, White Jade Capital modeled itself after the upper realm, constructing this Ascension Terrace to welcome all living beings who reached this celestial city without discrimination."

—"Oh, I think I've heard something like that..."-Jian Nuo muttered.

Still... were "all things can cultivate," "White Jade Capital," and "the Ascension Stairway" truly part of the same package?

Would White Jade Capital welcome mortals?

Jian Nuo mused in bewilderment. He felt as though he had encountered every one of those individual concepts before, yet stringing them together felt distinctly off.

However, looking at the deadpan, serious expression on Qu Qianxing's handsome face, Young Master Jian was instantly swayed:

[This is the Untiring Immortal Venerable's proud disciple. If he says so, there must be a valid reason.]

The thoroughly "reasonable" Qu Qianxing looked at Jian Nuo and asked:

—"Young Master Jian, would you like to go down and take a look?"

—"Go down and take a look?"-Jian Nuo repeated.

—"Indeed. You have likely heard that the Ascension Stairway serves as a trial for immortal seekers. In the process of scaling the stairway, one must question the path, question the heart, and question the Dao,"-Qu Qianxing said with a warm smile.-"While one does not need to traverse the entire stairway right now, each section carries distinct trials. For example, this section connecting to White Jade Capital tests 'potential' and 'growth'."

Qu Qianxing paused momentarily.

Noticing that not only Jian Nuo was listening intently, but other surrounding cultivators had also drawn near to eavesdrop, an exquisitely radiant smile blossomed across Qu Qianxing's face. Yet that smile never reached his eyes, lending that ethereal beauty an inorganic, chilling quality.

Framed against the perilous vortex of spiritual energy churning below, Qu Qianxing's smile made him look like a heartless celestial deity testing mortal souls.

Qu Qianxing continued:

—"To put it simply, walking upon this flight of stairs will trace back the development of one's spirit root, scrutinizing the pathways of one's spiritual energy."

—"...So that's how it is,"-a cultivator realized in sudden enlightenment.-"No wonder the Immortal Venerable chose this location to test spirit roots."

—"What do you think?"-Qu Qianxing asked Jian Nuo.-"Is Young Master Jian interested?"

Hearing this, the surrounding cultivators turned eager, expectant gazes toward Jian Nuo as well.

After all, merely listening to theoretical principles was dreadfully dull. Having someone demonstrate it—especially Sky City's young master—would be infinitely more entertaining.

The corner of Jian Nuo's mouth twitched at the spectacle.

For some reason, he felt that Qu Qianxing was [luring him into a trap](# "Literal CN: 请君入瓮 (qǐng jūn rù wèng) — invite the gentleman into the ceramic jar; coax someone into their own trap").

[...Still, it must be an illusion, right?]

[I haven't offended Qu Qianxing, and I felt an inexplicable sense of familiarity the moment I saw him, like he's someone I can trust.] Jian Nuo had always trusted his own intuition—for instance, the very first instant he laid eyes on Qingyang Qingyu, he had firmly decided she was his future wife. Thus, he figured he was simply overthinking.

[Perhaps he's just kindly offering me a chance to try out the Ascension Stairway?]

[After all, having come all this way, it would be a crying shame not to experience it firsthand.]

Jian Nuo quickly talked himself into it, nodding cheerfully:

—"Alright! Where should I begin?"

—"No need to go too far down. Starting from the six-thousand-six-hundred-and-sixty-sixth step—right around that corner below—will suffice,"-Qu Qianxing said.

Jian Nuo gave an affirming nod. He intended to step down, yet hesitated as he peered at the stairs.

Whenever the Ascension Stairway was mentioned, it was always in the context of climbing upward. Who knew if walking downward would trigger some unforeseen disaster?

—"It's quite alright; simply walk down,"-Qu Qianxing said with a reassuring smile.-"Descending the Ascension Stairway does not trigger the trials."

Only then did Jian Nuo proceed downward.

Just as Qu Qianxing had said, nothing occurred during the descent. It was merely a span of a hundred-odd steps, so he reached the spot in no time. Yet the moment he turned around and looked back up at the Ascension Stairway, astonishment washed over his face.

This segment of the stairway had grown extraordinarily long and impossibly narrow. Coupled with the tempestuous vortices of spiritual energy churning above and below, walking upon it felt like treading a tightrope suspended in the high heavens, lashed by ferocious gales from every direction.

Even more nerve-wracking was that White Jade Capital above had completely vanished into the billowing mist, leaving not a trace to be seen. Scaling heights without a visible target undoubtedly multiplied the psychological pressure exponentially.

—"An impressive trial indeed!"-Jian Nuo muttered, a competitive smile breaking across his lips.

He took steady, deliberate strides, stepping firmly onto the stairs. At that very instant, a gale of turbulent spiritual energy blasted into his face, violent enough to sweep him straight off the steps.

Yet Jian Nuo did not waver. Crystalline ice blooms shaped like snowflakes burst forth beneath his feet, anchoring him securely to the stairway.

The higher he climbed, the more savage the storm of spiritual energy became; yet Jian Nuo's ice crystals grew correspondingly harder and more resilient, even piercing through the stone steps to coalesce into hanging icicles beneath. In the end, these icicles fused into a continuous canopy, resembling an inverted, suspended palace.

The spectacle drew gasps of admiration from the cultivators observing from above.

Naturally, they could not feel the trials Jian Nuo was enduring; they merely saw Young Master Jian ascending the stairs with robes fluttering elegantly in the wind, pure-white ice spiritual energy swirling about him like falling snowflakes. More breathtaking still was that with every step he took, the inverted palace beneath the stairway gradually crystallized into shape, unmistakably outlining the early silhouette of Sky City.

[—"As expected of Sky City's heir."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"A pure Single Ice Spirit Root, isn't it? Such crystalline, translucent spiritual energy."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"To possess spiritual power of this caliber while still at the Nascent Soul stage is truly extraordinary!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

Amid the cultivators' choruses of praise, Qu Qianxing glanced sideways at Lu Shulong.

Because there were far too many high-tier cultivators present, Qu Qianxing refrained from using divine sense transmission—no matter how profound their spiritual sense or how exquisite their technique, in the eyes of true powerhouses, such transmissions were riddled with glaring flaws.

However, eye contact was more than enough.

Lu Shulong understood Qu Qianxing's unspoken question instantly: *Have you recorded Jian Nuo's spiritual energy parameters?*

*Recorded,* Lu Shulong signaled with a nod, narrowing his eyes as he smiled back at Qu Qianxing. In reality, he was asking: *Do you want me to make him suffer later?*

Qu Qianxing shook his head, turning his gaze toward the direction of Eternal Villa.

*Huh? You want to leave him to Qingyang Qingyu? Let Senior Sister Qingyang deal with him? Isn't that just rewarding the guy?!* Lu Shulong's eyes widened in disbelief.

Watching Lu Shulong's exaggerated expression, Qu Qianxing finally spoke aloud:

—"Shulong, sometimes I truly cannot fathom what goes on inside your head."

[—"?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Lu Shulong tilted his head with an innocent blink.

---

While the two martial brothers communicated through glances, Jian Nuo concluded his "trial."

Turning around to see the frozen palace he had manifested, an inexplicable surge of pride welled up within him:

[To think my power could achieve something like this!]

Qu Qianxing praised warmly:

—"As expected of Young Master Jian. Your spirit root is pure enough to leave one in awe."

—"Heh, I'm sure you wouldn't do poorly either,"-Jian Nuo said.

As he spoke, a thought struck him—he genuinely wanted to see what kind of phenomenon Qu Qianxing would produce if he walked the stairs.

Regrettably, Qu Qianxing gave him no such opportunity, suddenly glancing downward:

—"They have arrived."

Jian Nuo blinked in surprise before realizing what he meant.

"They" naturally referred to the children arriving for the spirit root assessment.

Recalling the primary purpose of today's gathering, however, a hint of concern crept into Jian Nuo's brow:

—"I heard those children are predominantly mortals, right? Even if there are promising seedlings among them, can they truly withstand such a trial?"

It wasn't that Jian Nuo looked down on mortals, but even with his cultivation, walking that path had felt fraught with peril!

—"They will not face the same difficulty as you,"-Qu Qianxing explained.-"Since the Ascension Stairway can be climbed by anyone, there is no such thing as a trial that only a specific cultivation level can pass. When mortals walk upon it, they will face trials tailored strictly to mortals."

—"I see,"-Jian Nuo said, nodding.

He glanced back at the Ascension Stairway, lingering fear still fluttering in his chest:

—"Setting aside the trials for a moment, the physical structure of the stairs remains the same, doesn't it? There aren't even handrails, suspended so terrifyingly high in midair. Would mortals devoid of spiritual power truly dare to walk upon it?"

—"Never underestimate our students,"-Qu Qianxing replied, this time with a genuine, gentle smile.

Even as his words fell, tiny specks of light began to flicker within the spiritual energy vortex below.

One, then another, then countless more.

Instantly, the pitch-black, terrifying vortex of spiritual energy was illuminated like a cosmic nebula, dazzling and breathtakingly beautiful.

As those "starlights" drifted upward, Jian Nuo finally discerned that they were translucent bubbles, each carrying a young boy or girl inside.

These bubbles did not fly directly onto White Jade Capital's Ascension Terrace.

Instead, a colossal, auspicious cloud unrolled near the Ascension Stairway like a soft, fluffy mat. Several Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples were already waiting atop it to receive the arriving children.

Evidently, just like Jian Nuo, the children were required to walk up to the Ascension Terrace on their own feet from below.

—"Those are the children participating in the trial?"-Jian Nuo asked in surprise.-"Their vitality and posture truly are remarkable!"

He almost felt as though these children had been meticulously groomed by elite cultivation clans. Yet on some of them, indeed, not a sliver of spiritual energy could be felt. Why bring children who were so obviously mortal that they didn't even need testing?

Jian Nuo was somewhat bewildered.

Like Shen Qingqian, he had assumed this was an early-admission round of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grand Recruitment.

Qu Qianxing had no intention of elaborating, merely saying:

—"However, while the Ascension Stairway's trial is open to everyone, we must ensure the children's absolute safety. Therefore, Young Master Jian, I'm afraid we must trouble you."

—"Leave it to me!"-Jian Nuo said, nodding gravely as understanding dawned.

He had heard about the teaching evaluations tied to this spirit root assessment.

The instructors participating in this assessment—though Jian Nuo was not officially one—were tasked with guaranteeing the children's safety and stabilizing the ambient spiritual energy around the Ascension Stairway, creating an undisturbed environment for the examination.

Previously, Jian Nuo had had no concrete concept of the task; but having just walked the path himself, he felt he could already envision children being blown off the stairway [like dumplings tumbling into a boiling pot](# "Literal CN: 下饺子 (xià jiǎoxi zi) — dumplings dropping into the pot; falling or plunging in rapid succession").

[I certainly won't let them actually fall, but isn't this trial a bit too harsh for mortals?]

Jian Nuo pondered inwardly.

At this moment, Lu Shulong gestured toward Jian Nuo:

—"Young Master Jian, this way, please."

Qu Qianxing cupped his hands in farewell:

—"Pardon me, Young Master Jian, but I will not be accompanying you further. Master has invited several crucial guests."

—"Please, go ahead,"-Jian Nuo nodded absentmindedly.

Yet as he followed Lu Shulong, he couldn't resist glancing in the direction Qu Qianxing had flown, curious as to who these "crucial guests" might be.

He saw that once the first batch of children had arrived atop the cloud carpet, another wave of bubbles surfaced.

This time, it was a cohort of high-tier cultivators, all possessing formidable cultivation.

For example, the female cultivator flying at the vanguard with a handful of disciples possessed Body Integration stage cultivation.

[So that's it—other high-level powerhouses of the Central Domain?] Jian Nuo observed quietly. [It seems the reality differs from past intelligence. The Untiring Immortal Venerable's ties with Central Domain cultivators are actually quite cordial. Well, with the Immortal Assembly having been convened, the geopolitical landscape of the Central Domain is naturally no longer what it once was. There seems to be a discrepancy in Carefree Palace's intelligence as well—which is an excellent thing!]

With that thought, he turned his gaze away.

Meanwhile, Qu Qianxing addressed the leading female cultivator:

—"Sect Master Shen, we have awaited your arrival for some time."

—"There was no need for you to go out of your way to attend to me,"-Sect Master Shen Qingqian of Blood Demon Hall said, rolling her eyes.-"I'm doing perfectly fine on my own."

...Except she looked rather lonely.

After all, Shen Qingqian had been ostracized by the southwestern cultivation world right from the start. Add to that Blood Demon Hall's prolonged seclusion inside a spatial rift, and Shen Qingqian had virtually zero social network to speak of. Bai Wubin's connections were considerably broader than his master's, but with his master standing beside him, other cultivators hesitated to approach and exchange pleasantries. Among the throng of cultivators, Blood Demon Hall stood out as distinctly isolated.

Shen Qingqian had also felt she looked excessively solitary, which was why she assumed Qu Qianxing had come over specifically to bail her out.

Yet Qu Qianxing merely smiled gently:

—"Sect Master Shen is too polite. You are our honored guest. This way, please."

—"Seriously, there's no need. Having you standing next to me makes me feel like I'm being [roasted over an open fire](# "Literal CN: 在火架上烤 (zài huǒ jià shang kǎo) — roasted on a grill rack; placed on the hot seat / under scorching scrutiny"),"-Shen Qingqian curled her lip.

And indeed, Qu Qianxing's gesture had instantly drawn the eyes of numerous cultivators toward the Blood Demon Hall contingent:

[—"Who is that?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"That seems to be Blood Demon Hall, doesn't it?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Blood Demon Hall? Why does that name reek of such sinister demonic malice? Does it have ties to the demon race?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Don't panic, it's just an ordinary body-cultivation sect."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Is that so? I heard their sect master had something to do with the demon race?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"Hardly. Isn't the one speaking with young Qianxing their sect master? She looks like an ordinary young woman."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

[—"A woman practicing body refinement is rather peculiar to begin with."](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

The cultivators on that side chattered in low murmurs.

All residing within the Central Domain, they had naturally caught wind of the rumors surrounding the southwest. But having had zero dealings with Blood Demon Hall, and given how exceedingly rare female body cultivators were—even if Shen Qingqian hadn't succumbed to demonic corruption, her cultivation arts were hardly something male body cultivators could discuss with her—none of them had ever interacted with her. Naturally, no one was interested in probing to the bottom of it.

Not that Shen Qingqian liked having people probe into her affairs anyway.

Fortunately, their hushed discussions did not last long before words echoed from the deepest recesses of White Jade Capital.

Words from the Untiring Immortal Venerable:

—"Is everyone gathered? Then let us begin."

His voice resonated with a deep, echoing thrum, as if the entire colossal expanse of White Jade Capital itself were speaking.
