# Chapter 176: Giving Gifts Is So Hard!

With the Sacred Tree itself acting as guide, this time Zhou Hui and his entourage encountered zero obstacles as they arrived at the demon race's secret treasury.

In truth, the demon race's treasury hung in a place where anyone and everyone could see it.

It was a... sun.

A crimson sun.

It hung high in the sky above the Southern Domain, rising and setting in a manner that seemed no different from an ordinary sun. Furthermore, with the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree blazing like a colossal "torch" in the Southern Domain, turning day and night equally bright, the sun's presence was, truthfully speaking, hardly conspicuous at all.

Even Zhou Hui had never imagined that the Southern Domain's sun concealed any secrets.

Yet at this very moment, they were standing right in front of that sun.

Though called a sun, it radiated very little brilliance—one could gaze directly upon it with the naked eye. It was so dim it seemed on the verge of snuffing out at any second. Yet the spiritual energy radiating from it was genuine and unmistakable: surging, torrential, incomparably scorching. It erupted outward, forming particularly magnificent solar prominences that spewed fragments of the sun into the sky, igniting the canopy of the towering Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

Thus, the summit of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was not only burning; magma-like fragments of the sun also gurgled and flowed across it like streams, or like the weeping blood and tears of the sky, dripping downward and vanishing into the white arbor shadows, becoming part of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's nourishment.

And these "fragments" were none other than the secret treasure of the demon race:

Taiyi Spirit Dew.

It was said that a single drop—merely one drop of this essence—could restore a withered ancient tree to its prime, cloak barren deserts in verdant forests, cause spirit herbs that bloomed once a century to bear fruit, and instantly mature thousand-year spiritual medicines. Furthermore, legends held that refining spiritual pills with a sufficient quantity of Taiyi True Water could not only bring the dead back to life and grow flesh upon bare bones, but could even grant an ordinary being instant Immortal Bones, endowing them with the innate talent to ascend to immortality.

Zhou Hui had come to the Southern Domain not only to inspect the expansion of his educational curriculum, but also specifically for this Taiyi Spirit Dew.

The Langhuan Library he had just acquired was currently reduced to the state of a tender sapling whose leaves had been gnawed off, desperately requiring the nourishment of Taiyi Spirit Dew. Meanwhile, Boss Tao had previously been "accidentally injured" by Zhou Hui and also needed Taiyi Spirit Dew to concoct spiritual medicine to treat the golden toad inside his belly.

Yet no one could have anticipated that this spiritual dew was actually the fragments of a sun.

—"Will pills refined from this stuff burn a hole right through my stomach?"-Boss Tao murmured upon seeing this, reflexively rubbing his belly.

His belly—which looked slightly deflated compared to before, yet remained undeniably round—quivered uneasily.

However, such worry was merely self-deception born of Boss Tao's attempt to restrain his greed.

Every immortal cultivator knew that alchemy was a process that fundamentally altered the substance itself, meaning the raw material's properties and the finished pill were two entirely different matters. What was more, given the properties and rarity of Taiyi Spirit Dew, even without any refinement whatsoever, offering just a trace of it would be enough for Sky City and the Palace of Carefree Wandering to convene an exclusive high-end auction; it was an existence [priceless beyond any market value](# "Literal CN: 有价无市 (yǒujià-wúshì) — having a theoretical price but no market availability / practically impossible to buy").

With Boss Tao's mercantile disposition, he practically wished he could sweep away every last drop of the spiritual dew—if only he possessed the ability to do so.

Of course, another of Boss Tao's virtues was that he possessed exceptional self-awareness.

He knew full well that, surrounded by the demon race, even a Mahayana cultivator could not possibly pilfer a single drop of spiritual dew—to say nothing of the fact that the true body of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, that "ancient tree demon" who had lived for heaven knew how many eons, was standing right beside them.

Boss Tao could only use his beady little eyes to frantically signal Zhou Hui, praying that his old friend would not display his customary [lofty integrity](# "Literal CN: 高风亮节 (gāofēng-liàngjié) — lofty character and noble integrity / unworldly moral rectitude") on such an occasion, and would instead take as much of the spiritual dew as possible.

Regrettably, his old friend remained as delightfully oblivious to mortal sentiment and indifferent to personal desire as ever.

From the moment Zhou Hui stepped into the demon race's forbidden grounds, he had not spared a second glance for the spiritual dew radiating overwhelming spiritual energy. His gaze was fixed unwaveringly upon that sun.

Furthermore, it held not a single shred of greed—only scholarly inquiry.

[Untiring isn't trying to research the formation mechanics of Taiyi Spirit Dew, is he?!]

[Please, anything but that! Once this guy's research itch flares up, he might very well sit here meditating for a thousand years! What would be the difference between getting the treasure and not getting it then?!]

Peering at Zhou Hui's expression, Tao Zhu thought in deep dread.

In truth, his worry was entirely superfluous.

Zhou Hui's gaze had lingered on the sun for merely a few moments when the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree spoke up:

—"As expected of Lord Immortal Venerable! A single glance, and you immediately perceive where the true value lies!"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree exclaimed.

Tao Zhu: "..."

[No, literally anyone could see that the sun is obviously the most valuable thing here, right?]

[But no matter how valuable the sun is, would you actually be willing to give it away?]

—"If you desire it, I'll just give it to you,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree announced with immense magnanimity.

"Pfft!" Boss Tao nearly spat out the symbiotic golden toad inside his belly.

—"Ah?! Grand Elder?!"-Shui Huang'er cried out, her face draining of all color.

Although Shui Huang'er had constantly spoken of gifting the secret treasure to Zhou Hui as an investment in the future, what she had meant was the 'Taiyi Spirit Dew', not the sun in the sky!

Even that would have been a staggering sacrifice.

Despite the raging fires atop the Sacred Tree making it look as though Taiyi Spirit Dew were abundant, merely approaching this place was enough to reduce low-cultivation beings to ashes. Even if one safely entered the forbidden grounds, harvesting the Taiyi Spirit Dew was no simple task—these were solar prominences, after all! While it appeared plentiful, collecting it in liquid form while ensuring the spiritual energy did not disperse yielded merely a single small vial over several decades.

—If Taiyi Spirit Dew could be mass-produced, the Heavenly Court likely would have been unable to resist seizing it long ago; they never would have allowed the demon race to guard such a treasure for all these millennia.

Shui Huang'er had never imagined that the Grand Elder would be even more lavish than she had ever dreamed, actually offering to give away the very source of the spiritual dew!

And besides, how did one even "give away" a sun?!

At this moment, Boss Tao also snapped out of his shock.

The thought that they could actually haul away a sun caused his greed to momentarily overpower his reason. Ducking behind Zhou Hui, he tested the waters:

—"Heheh, even if you wish to gift it, we wouldn't have the faintest idea how to take it away. You surely wouldn't expect Untiring to pluck it down, would you?"-Tao Zhu probed.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree shot Boss Tao a half-smiling, enigmatic look:

—"Since I have said I will gift it, how could I trouble Lord Immortal Venerable to lift a finger? Naturally, I shall personally deliver it to the Central Domain."-the Sacred Tree replied.

—"Wait a moment! Grand Elder!"-Shui Huang'er finally found her voice, exclaiming in utter panic.-"B-But if you give the sun away, what will happen to us?!"

—"How many millennia do you think this sun has been scorching me? Even if I just burn for another tens of thousands of years, that will be more than enough to illuminate the Southern Domain,"-the old tree said dismissively.

—"But..."-Shui Huang'er wore an expression as if nursing a severe toothache.

She naturally knew that the Sacred Tree's flames were sufficient to illuminate the Southern Domain. Most flora and fauna in the Southern Domain currently relied on the Sacred Tree for survival; one could say the Sacred Tree was the bedrock of their very existence.

Yet having a sun versus not having a sun were two completely different matters!

Even if this sun had zero practical utility, simply hanging there gave all living beings peace of mind.

Because that represented a "normal" world.

Compared to the sun itself, Taiyi Spirit Dew was utterly trivial.

If Shui Huang'er had known what a "computer" was, she would have unquestionably suspected that Untiring Immortal Venerable had just completely hacked the Sacred Tree's operating system.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was no ordinary block of wood. Having observed the desires of living beings for millennia, it naturally understood its Saintess's anxiety. Even more, it understood that under normal circumstances, offering such a colossal gift without rhyme or reason would certainly make the recipient suspicious—at the very least, that toad was glaringly suspicious. As for Untiring Immortal Venerable... the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree admitted it couldn't see through him at all. It was just that Lord Immortal Venerable's gaze made it somewhat terrified—why did it feel as though the other party were itching to set up permanent residence inside its trunk?!

The Sacred Tree was desperate to prevent such a scenario from coming to pass!

It just so happened to have a pretext that would allow the other party to take the treasure without any psychological burden—giving gifts was truly an exhausting chore! Especially when one had to ensure the recipient took it happily.

—"Of course, this old man also has his own selfish motives,"-the Sacred Tree said with an air of theatrical gravity.

It watched as the toad revealed an "I knew it" expression, while Untiring Immortal Venerable remained completely unmoved.

The Sacred Tree deliberately asked Zhou Hui:

—"Having cycled this world through reincarnation so many times, Lord Immortal Venerable has surely witnessed the disaster about to unfold next, yes? The catastrophe that destroys this minor realm."

[—"Destroy the world?!"](# "Speakers: Shui Huang'er & Tao Zhu")

Shui Huang'er and Tao Zhu cried out in unison.

Zhou Hui blinked in surprise before answering:

—"Are you referring to... the demonic incursion?"

[—"A demonic incursion?!"](# "Speakers: Shui Huang'er & Tao Zhu")

Shui Huang'er and Tao Zhu cried out once more.

Zhou Hui looked at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree somewhat uncertainly.

After all, Zhou Hui truthfully had very little concept of what constituted a "world-destroying catastrophe."

If one were talking about destroying the world, all he needed to do was roll the world back through reincarnation one more time, and this realm would be practically finished.

Even if he refrained from [washing the world](# "Literal CN: 洗这个世界 (xǐ zhège shìjiè) — wiping, formatting, and resetting the cosmos through reincarnation") again, there were countless other ways to bring about the apocalypse.

To Zhou Hui, the greatest catastrophe was the destruction of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the slaughter of his disciples. Yet even he knew that this matter was of no consequence to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree; consequently, the ancient tree had no reason to join him in opposing the Heavenly Court over that.

Yet seeing the ancient tree so dead serious, Zhou Hui gave it some careful thought and realized that the catastrophe the tree spoke of was something much more superficial:

For example, the ostensible "main storyline" that the so-called protagonist faced in the Heavenly Dao's script:

The demonic incursion.

Indeed, that third-rate novel did, for better or worse, possess a main storyline called "demonic incursion."

Because of this main plotline, the "protagonist" had acted like a harbinger of doom—wherever he went, the demons of the Demon Realm followed right behind:

The "protagonist" destroyed the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and just as he was about to be held accountable, the Demon Realm portal at Eternal Villa opened and the Central Domain was laid waste. The "protagonist" fled to the Northern Domain, and the Northern Domain was assaulted by demons. The "protagonist" visited the Southern Domain, and the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was burned down. The "protagonist" went to the Western Domain, and the Western Domain was overrun by demons. The "protagonist" traveled to the Eastern Domain, and the True Dragons of the Eastern Domain were corrupted by demonic qi. Finally, the "protagonist" returned to the Northern Domain, which had become the primary theater of war against the demons, culminating in the destruction of even Sky City itself.

As the "righteous protagonist," he naturally could not sit idly by; he indeed fought the demons all along the way.

This was primarily manifested in him relentlessly battling Mo Xi, who had succumbed to demonic deviation.

Even in the final battle of Sky City, the "protagonist" ascended only after slaying Mo Xi, who had completely transformed into a demon—and that was achieved with Mo Bei's assist, no less!

It was as though slaying Mo Xi somehow miraculously resolved the entire demonic calamity afflicting this minor world.

However, because the "protagonist" ascended to the higher realm, the script made zero mention of what ultimately became of the unsealed gaping hole leading to the Demon Realm, the uncleared demonic legions, or the scorched world ravaged by war.

Presumably, it was no longer deemed important. After all, throughout the "protagonist's" personal trials, this world had already been wrecked into an utter shambles.

That was the perspective of a reader.

For the eyewitnesses who actually lived through it, however, reality was different—because after the "protagonist" ascended, this world did not simply end.

While the "protagonist" was in the Upper Realm, casually slaying an "Untiring Immortal Venerable" who had ascended only to be enslaved by some nameless Demon Venerable, time was presumably still ticking away in this demon-ravaged minor world.

And that meant the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had remained there the entire time.

—"I recall that in that cycle of reincarnation, the Southern Domain seemed to fare reasonably well,"-Zhou Hui inquired.-"It hardly counted as the destruction of the world, did it?"

[No, a demonic invasion is already horrific enough, alright?! How does only the Southern Domain surviving count as 'reasonably well'?!]

[What about the Central Domain?]

[How did our Central Domain fare?!]

Tao Zhu glared at Zhou Hui.

He had heard from Patriarch Jiuzhai that Untiring had rewound the world through reincarnation several times, but he had never known that across those cycles, monumental events like a Demon Realm invasion and the total devastation of the world had actually occurred!

Even the Sacred Tree possessed far more human warmth than Zhou Hui:

—"[When the nest is overturned, how could any egg remain unbroken?](# "Idiom: 覆巢之下，焉有完卵 (fùcháo zhī xià, yān yǒu wánluǎn) — when an entire society or collective collapses, no individual can escape unharmed")"-the Sacred Tree sighed.

This time, its lamentation was filled with genuine emotion:

—"Although this place was fated to undergo such a tribulation, when it actually arrived, it still filled this old man with profound loneliness."

—"Wait, Grand Elder! What do you mean by 'fated to undergo such a tribulation'?!"-Shui Huang'er could finally restrain herself no longer and questioned sharply.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree looked at her, recalling how this little bird had stubbornly refused to leave its side.

—The Sacred Tree hadn't entirely been bluffing Zhou Hui. In the previous lifetime, under conditions where the entire world was invaded by demons, no matter how minor the damage sustained by the Southern Domain, it could not have remained completely unscathed. In that situation, the demon race could either choose to become vassals of the demons, or flee this place altogether for another minor world.

Although the fiendish demons had historically been considered one possible evolutionary path for the demon race, most demons were still unwilling to let base desires dictate their souls; hence, the majority chose to flee.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had not been surprised by the arrival of that day.

Truth be told, its transformation from a Wood Spirit into a demigod had been precisely so that when that day arrived, it could attain "freedom."

After all, a demigod who lost its divinity could still survive, but a Wood Spirit that lost its true body would perish without exception.

What it had never anticipated was that while the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree itself did not care, a massive flock of demons led by Shui Huang'er cared immensely. While some demon wood spirits truly had nowhere else to go and could not leave this world, a phoenix maiden like Shui Huang'er could completely maintain her status and prestige if she returned to the ancestral gathering grounds of the demon race. Yet this stubborn little bird had been willing to cast everything aside just to stand guard until the very end.

—"I am the Saintess of the Sacred Tree!"-she had declared.

At the time, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had been unable to persuade Shui Huang'er, nor could it convince the other stubborn demons who shared her mindset. In fact, in that fleeting moment, it had been so deeply moved by those little fools that it had almost agreed to shelter them until their dying breath.

Fortunately, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree ultimately did not have to make such a tragic choice.

Because, just like the previous cycles, time reset once again, reverting to a point where nothing had happened yet.

For that, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had breathed a massive sigh of relief.

This was also why the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree didn't have (or rather, didn't dare have) any objections to Untiring Immortal Venerable repeatedly resetting this world through reincarnation.

Now, looking into Shui Huang'er's eyes, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree felt rather conflicted. It suspected that if it were to happen again, this silly bird would likely do something just as foolish as in the past life. Worst of all, it found itself unable to refuse these children.

[It must be because these silly kids know nothing that they can be so naive!]

The Sacred Tree decided to explain the catastrophe in greater detail—and execute a thoroughly clean [pass of the buck](# "Literal CN: 甩锅 (shuǎiguō) — shifting blame / passing the buck to the Heavenly Court").

—"From the very moment it collapsed, this world was already fated for destruction,"-the Sacred Tree declared.

*

Accompanied by the Sacred Tree's words, the surrounding scenery shifted abruptly.

It transitioned from the dim sun into a lush, verdant forest.

Zhou Hui was not surprised.

Since the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree possessed the ability to induce desire-based illusions, constructing a projection like this was child's play. Clearly, this ancient tree wished to recount a piece of history known only to itself.

[Now this is the proper way to handle things!]

Zhou Hui looked at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree with greater earnestness than ever before.

Unnerved by Zhou Hui's intense stare, the Sacred Tree cleared its throat and continued:

—"Back when I was still a Wood Spirit, this 'world' was not an independent world, but a corner of the Heavenly Realm."

—"The Heavenly Realm?!"-Shui Huang'er's eyes widened in shock. This was the very first time she had ever learned that their world had such an illustrious origin.

Zhou Hui, on the other hand, remained entirely unfazed—he had clearly known this all along.

Seeing this, the Sacred Tree chuckled and continued:

—"Although it was a corner of the Heavenly Realm, whether during the reign of the Divine Court, the Demon Court, or the Heavenly Court, this area was never considered a central location. As a matter of convention, it was designated as an Academy, established to educate ignorant youngsters."

—"...Is that how the various 'Heavenly Scriptures' scattered across the realms were left behind?"-Zhou Hui suddenly asked.

With a wave of his sleeve, a collection of Heavenly Scriptures inscribed with various scripts appeared within the phantom forest. Some were carved upon boulders perched on precipices, some on weathered, nearly eroded stone steles, and some were even inscribed directly onto flesh and blood—looking eerily like the internal organs of colossal behemoths?!

Tao Zhu did not recognize all of them, but he was familiar with a few: those Heavenly Scriptures were the "study materials" Untiring had collected in his youth.

It was precisely by relying on these as reference materials that Untiring had created his own immortal arts and walked the path that brought him to where he stood today.

Zhou Hui added:

—"In the past, I always assumed that immortals had intentionally left these behind in this world to enlighten the people..."-He had thus developed a yearning for the Heavenly Realm, believing the Upper Realm held the answers to all problems, which was why he had once yearned so intensely for ascension.

—"No, these are merely the [children's scratch notes](# "Literal CN: 蒙童学习留下的痕迹 (méngtóng xuéxí liúxià de hénjì) — scribbles and homework scratch notes left behind by young elementary students") left behind while studying,"-the Sacred Tree corrected.

It scrutinized those "Heavenly Scriptures" closely and couldn't help but roast:

—"These scripts are completely different, and their frameworks are entirely incompatible. There are remnants from the most ancient nature deities, secret manuals from the Demon Court, and institutional rules from the Heavenly Court—how could they possibly have been left behind on purpose? Besides, whenever the ruling power in the Heavenly Realm changed hands, a new batch of textbooks and a new batch of students were brought in! These traces that weren't erased were undoubtedly dumped in some forgotten corner and abandoned by every faction. Not to mention this world fell from the heavens once—how on earth did you humans manage to dig all of this up?!"

—"Don't lump all of us humans together; we aren't that abnormal,"-Tao Zhu retorted, refusing to shoulder the blame.-"Untiring traveled to the ends of the earth and dug every single one of them up with his own two hands! That was the foundation of his studies!"

—"How are you still alive?!"-the Sacred Tree was flabbergasted.

[—"Good luck, perhaps?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"Even if you didn't die, you should have gone completely mad!"-the Sacred Tree shouted.

—"That was probably luck too..."-Zhou Hui replied, thinking it was perfectly ordinary.

—"If you truly had good luck, you would have stumbled upon a complete, unified cultivation method!"-the Sacred Tree huffed.

After all, the imperial library of the Heavenly Realm had been located in this very vicinity... Speaking of which, it wondered what had become of that library.

The thought flashed through the Sacred Tree's mind.

However, it had no leisure to dwell on the past; instead, it scrutinized Untiring Immortal Venerable once more from head to toe.

[To be able to self-teach to this level using nothing but fragmented junk... he might actually be able to overturn this world's fate and alter the destiny of the demon race.]

For the very first time, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree harbored such a thought.

Even when hearing that Zhou Hui had cycled this world through reincarnation six times, the Sacred Tree had not dared to believe it; yet now, the gaze it directed toward Zhou Hui turned dead serious.

—"None of that is important, is it?"-Zhou Hui remarked, having zero interest in analyzing why he hadn't died or gone mad. He steered the conversation back to the previous topic:-"I understand the origin of these Heavenly Scriptures now. So, how did our world 'fall' from the Heavenly Realm in the first place?"

—"Ah... that. It was mainly due to the Immortal-Demon War,"-the Sacred Tree said.

Clearing its throat, the Sacred Tree also returned to the main topic:

—"Back then, the Heavenly Court had not been established for very long, and they did not control nearly as many minor worlds as they do now. As a result, the frontline between the Heavenly Realm and the demons lacked a buffer zone, leading to frequent direct clashes. During the most perilous crisis, the Demon Realm constructed an Ascension Stairway and nearly stormed straight up into the Heavenly Court itself!"

Zhou Hui's eyes widened slightly as he turned his head to look in a specific direction.

It was the direction of the Central Domain.

Over there stood the Ascension Stairway that Zhou Hui had personally repaired with his own hands.

—"Indeed, that was originally built by the demons,"-the Sacred Tree nodded.-"In order to prevent the Demon Realm from truly assaulting the Heavenly Court, the people of the Academy at that time made the choice to personally sever this region from the Heavenly Realm, transforming it into the absolute front line against the demons!"

As the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree spoke, it waved its hand.

The surrounding scene shifted once more.

They were now positioned in the void of space. Above, they beheld the majestic Heavenly Court and continent-sized floating islands. Down below, however, lay a pitch-black abyssal sea. Within that ocean was a bottomless Sea Eye. Countless grotesque and terrifying creatures crawled out of the abyss, stacking their own corpses and bones to construct a towering celestial stairway, attempting to climb toward the heavens.

At the very instant they were about to touch the Upper Realm, the connection between a piece of land in the Heavenly Realm and the Heavenly Court fractured. It plummeted downward, crushing the stairway of bones beneath it, smashing into the abyssal ocean, and plugging that bottomless Sea Eye!

That falling realm was also shattered into five pieces upon impact.

—"This is our world? Then doesn't that mean our world has been sitting right atop a demonic lair all along?!"-Shui Huang'er asked.

—"Precisely,"-the Sacred Tree nodded.

It gazed coldly toward the direction of the Heavenly Court:

—"Naturally, such a sacrifice was not made for nothing. The Heavenly Court praised the bravery of the people of the Academy, and right after it fell, the Heavenly Court still acknowledged this place as part of the Heavenly Realm. They dispatched countless heavenly soldiers and divine generals to eradicate the demons. The heavenly beings here also continued their ancient traditions, constructing a place called 'White Jade Capital', striving to educate every child to excellence and send them back to the Heavenly Court.

[—"However..."](# "Speaker: Sacred Tree")

—"However what?"-Shui Huang'er asked, looking at the Sacred Tree nervously.

—"However, this old man doesn't know either,"-the Sacred Tree shook its head.-"At that time, this old man was merely an ignorant Wood Spirit who only cared about my own affairs, so I know nothing of the inner workings. All I know is that after this world plugged the Demon Eye, things took a turn for the better. The Heavenly Court grew increasingly powerful, gathering more and more minor worlds under its dominion. Yet the immortals dispatched here by the Heavenly Court grew fewer and fewer, until this world degenerated into just another ordinary minor world."

—"Ugh! Grand Elder, why is it that every time you reach the crucial part, you don't know anything?!"-Shui Huang'er complained in disappointment.

Zhou Hui and Tao Zhu exchanged a glance.

Even without the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's elaboration, they could already deduce what had happened.

Moreover, since Zhou Hui had personally traveled to the Heavenly Realm and witnessed past history, the Sacred Tree's account merely corroborated certain attitudes of the Heavenly Court.

—"Setting aside the Heavenly Court's attitude, the real crux is that Demon Eye,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree coughed twice before continuing.-"Although it was sealed, in a certain cycle of reincarnation... simulated by Lord Immortal Venerable, it was opened once more in the future—and right in the Central Domain at that!"

As it spoke, it shifted the scene once again.

This time, however, it was a projection of the demons running rampant from the previous cycle of reincarnation. The only difference from history was that a crimson sun now hung in the sky above.

The Sacred Tree declared with immense righteous gravity:

—"Rather than our Southern Domain, I believe Lord Immortal Venerable has far greater need of this sun to suppress the demonic fiends!"

[No, I don't really need it that much.]

[My kids have already modified that Demon Eye into a tower defense game and are currently farming demons there for fun!]

Zhou Hui thought to himself.
