# Chapter 20: Heavenly Destiny

—"Father! Mother! They humiliated me!"-Su Xin wailed at the cloud mirror treasure.

Never mind whether a Foundation Establishment cultivator had any business bawling his eyes out—Su Xin had long concluded from experience that this trick worked best on his parents.

And indeed, on the other end of the cloud mirror, the [Mei-Zhu couple](# "Literal CN: 梅竹夫妇 — couple portmanteau combining Su Mei (苏枚, 'plum') and Xin Zhu (辛烛, punning on 竹 'bamboo')") flew into a rage:

[—"What colossal nerve! How dare they humiliate my son!"](# "Speaker: Su Mei")

[—"What sort of rubbish is this Palace of Fallen Immortals anyway? It doesn't sound like a proper, upright place at all! We must conduct a thorough investigation into their sect's historical record!"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

Without even asking for details, they instantly convicted the Palace of Fallen Immortals on the spot.

This couple had always treated Su Xin like this.

Having lived as a [DINK couple](# "Literal CN: 丁克 (dīngkè) — Double Income No Kids; here describing cultivators who lived childless for nearly 10,000 years before having a late child") for nearly ten thousand years, the last time they had personally taught disciples was already several millennia ago. Now, well up in their advanced years, they had suddenly had a child. Their [delayed parental love hit like a landslide](# "Literal CN: 迟到的父爱母爱如山体滑坡 — modern internet meme describing overwhelming, smothering parental affection that arrives late in life"), treating this late-born son of theirs like an unenlightened spirit pet: *Our baby is so adorable; the problem must definitely be someone else!*

Seeing this reaction, Su Xin cried even louder.

After all, while the Palace of Fallen Immortals rubbing salt in his wounds (Perfected Master Zhao: ???) was undeniably hateful, the most detestable one of all was that Zhou Hui!

Originally, Su Xin had cared about saving face and hesitated over whether to run crying to his parents. But judging from the grand reception the Palace of Fallen Immortals had put on, was it possible the entire Central Domain already knew he had [cushioned the bottom of the ranks](# "Literal CN: 垫底 (diàndǐ) — being dead last in score rankings, acting as a cushion for everyone else") in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's exam?!

Su Xin concocted a massive web of catastrophic delusions all on his own, his tears now infused with seventy percent genuine distress:

—"It's not just the Palace of Fallen Immortals! It's the Heavenly Spirit Sect too!"-he howled.

—"Then we'll investigate the Heavenly Spirit Sect together with them!"-Su Mei declared, completely disregarding the facts in favor of blatant favoritism.-"My poor son has suffered so much! I don't know what that scoundrel He Yong is doing, letting some obscure, no-name little sect run wild like this!"

Hearing him say this, it was obvious he didn't even know whose sect the Heavenly Spirit Sect was.

Naturally, Su Xin wasn't about to remind him. Fortunately, Xin Zhu hadn't thrown her brain away completely. She was just about to chime in with her husband to denounce them when she paused, asking in confusion:

—"Wait a moment. Isn't the Heavenly Spirit Sect the Untiring Immortal Venerable's sect?"-Xin Zhu asked.

Su Mei's expression instantly froze:

—"Zhou Hui's sect?"-Su Mei asked, stunned.

—"That's right, it's that very Immortal Venerable's sect!"-Su Xin wasn't afraid of the truth coming out; he decisively [lodged the complaint first like a true villain](# "Literal CN: 恶人先告状 (è rén xiān gào zhuàng) — the guilty party filing a lawsuit or grievance first to seize the moral high ground and deflect blame"), striving not to give them any time to think.-"I thought he was a senior friend of my parents, so I personally prepared gifts to pay him a visit. Who could have guessed he'd spout some nonsense about '[since you've already come, it must be destiny](# "Literal CN: 来了就是机缘 — cultivation parody of 'since we're already here' (来都来了) and the famous slogan 'once you come, you are a Shenzhener' (来了就是深圳人)")', forcibly dragging me to take an exam alongside his elite personal disciples, and humiliated me ruthlessly!"

—"You weren't injured, were you?"-Xin Zhu grew tense the moment she heard that.

—"...No. It was just a written exam on paper,"-Su Xin answered in a small, muted voice.

He didn't dare lie about that part. A physical injury was something that would be exposed the instant anyone checked. If he incited his parents to confront Zhou Hui directly to demand accountability, wouldn't the whole facade crumble? Therefore, if he was going to lie, he could only tell half-truths—but he absolutely had to make his parents despise the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

Relying on rich past experience, Su Xin put on an intensely aggrieved expression and wept:

—"However, he dispatched elite disciples with extraordinarily high cultivations and deliberately forced me to test alongside them! That paper was ridiculously hard! If you don't believe me, you can ask He Yong! It was fundamentally an intentional humiliation!"

Sure enough, Su Mei was successfully provoked into another flare of fury:

—"Those Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples truly are too abominable!"-Su Mei fumed.

—"It wasn't the disciples, it was the Immortal Venerable himself! He proctored the exam in person!"-Su Xin insisted stubbornly, pinning the blame firmly on Zhou Hui.

He would never let his parents' anger be deflected onto the Heavenly Spirit Sect's disciples! Although that Yuwen Xuan was by far the most loathsome of the bunch, if his parents went to question the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Zhou Hui would at most dish out a couple of mild, perfunctory scoldings to appease them! After all, that was exactly how Su Xin's own parents handled things.

Therefore, the focal point of the grievance had to be managed with surgical precision—he had to tether all of his parents' aggro directly onto Zhou Hui himself!

Su Xin was exceptionally clever only when it came to schemes like this, and his tongue was well-practiced at sowing discord:

—"He definitely did it on purpose! Writing such an impossibly difficult test paper and making me sit for the exam with his proud personal disciples—he just wanted to prove that he's a better teacher than Father and Mother! It doesn't matter if I lost face, but I simply cannot swallow this indignation! Because what I disgraced was Father's and Mother's reputation! Right now, rumors must be flying across the entire Central Domain, saying the disciple taught by Father and Mother can't even compare to the ones taught by that Untiring guy! I have failed Father and Mother, *waaaaah*—"

Su Xin knew that the more he blamed himself, the angrier his parents would become at the other party. That was how it had always worked.

Only this time, after crying for a good while, Su Xin didn't hear any explosive outburst of parental rage. He had no choice but to peek up through teary eyes to steal a glance at their expressions, only to discover that the looks on his parents' faces were not fury, but... resignation?

—"If it's Untiring..."-Su Mei sighed.

—"Mhm. If it's him, it really wouldn't be a deliberate attempt to make things difficult,"-Xin Zhu agreed.

—"Father! Mother!"-Su Xin cried in disbelief.-"You don't know just how impossible that test paper was—"

—"If it's hard, that's normal. If it weren't hard, he wouldn't be Untiring,"-Xin Zhu said with a wry smile.-"Son, do you know the origin of his title 'Untiring'?"

[...I don't want to know.]
Su Xin thought to himself.

Unfortunately, once elderly folk started reminiscing, there was no stopping them. His mother had already begun recounting the tale:

—"Back in the day, when he was merely at the Golden Core stage, he claimed first place in the [Grand Sect Tournament](# "Literal CN: 仙门大比 (xiānmén dàbǐ) — grand tournament and martial gathering held among major cultivation sects"). By the rules, he was permitted to choose one cultivation manual from any participating sect to study. Who would have thought that an elder of the Yuqing Sect would deliberately make things difficult for him? The elder deceived him into heading to Severing-Heaven Cliff in the Abyssal Ocean to study an art left behind by a True Immortal."

—"A Golden Core cultivator naturally couldn't learn such a thing; simply staring at it for a few extra moments carried the risk of suffering qi deviation. Yet Zhou Hui sat upon Severing-Heaven Cliff for nine hundred and ninety-nine days. Defying all odds, character by character, he committed the entire manual to memory."

—"Had it ended there, one could only praise his perseverance. But over the next six hundred and sixty-six years, without rest or sleep, he traveled to every single corner of this realm, visiting every sect, until he miraculously deciphered the full contents of that art! Shattering the limitations of natural talent, he attained the Great Dao—and was thus given the title 'Untiring'."

—"Later, someone once asked him: 'Did you not think that a True Immortal's art was far too difficult? That it was completely beyond what could be learned?' To which he answered: 'There is no truly difficult knowledge, only knowledge that has not yet been studied.'"

Reaching this point, Xin Zhu shook her head:

—"Given Untiring's personality, I fear the concept of 'deliberately making things difficult' doesn't even exist in his mind. On the contrary, he probably believes that the harder the questions are, the more genuinely he is acting for your own good."

—"B-But...!"-Su Xin wanted to refute her, yet found no ground to stand on.

He cast his mind back to that Heavenly Spirit Sect examination paper. Because of his own warped perspective, looking back now, it really did feel exactly as his mother had described.

At this point, Su Mei was also swept up in nostalgia by Xin Zhu's words:

—"Come to think of it, he must surely teach his own disciples with the same rigorous standard... Speaking of which, wasn't that child from the Ran clan sent under his tutelage?"-Su Mei asked.

[—"The Ran clan?"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

—"The Ran clan of talisman-array masters,"-Su Mei said.-"Their bloodline has never been particularly prolific. I heard the main branch of this generation produced only a single daughter, but her temperament was chronically lazy. No matter what, she refused to study array formations. Beatings proved entirely useless, so as a last resort, they packed her off and sent her under Untiring's door."

—"Oh? How has she learned?"-Xin Zhu asked with interest.

—"She hasn't completed her apprenticeship yet, so who knows? But hearing Xin'er describe things, she must be learning quite well!"-Su Mei said.

As the two discussed this, their gazes turned simultaneously toward Su Xin.

The hairs on the back of Su Xin's neck stood straight on end. A violently ominous premonition suddenly seized his heart.

—"Speaking of which, shouldn't Xin'er also apprentice himself under Untiring?"-Xin Zhu pondered aloud.-"Since he found the Heavenly Spirit Sect's test paper impossibly hard, doesn't that mean his cultivation progress is severely lagging behind? Why don't we let Untiring [whip him into shape](# "Literal CN: 调教 (tiáojiào) — train, discipline, or drill strictly") for a few years..."

—"NOOOOOOO!"-Su Xin shrieked at the top of his lungs, bursting into tears.

This time, his crying was one hundred percent authentic.

He had no idea how the Heavenly Spirit Sect conducted their regular studies, but that single 'live 3D Coral Platform leaderboard' was more than enough to ensure Su Xin never wanted to experience such a thing a second time in his life!

If it weren't for the cloud mirror separating them preventing him from physically reaching his mother, he would have clamped onto her thighs with both arms and legs:

—"Father! Mother! I promise I'll cultivate properly from now on! I'll study hard! Please, whatever you do, don't send me to the Heavenly Spirit Sect! I don't want to go, *waaaaah*—"

—"All right, all right, if you don't want to go, you don't have to! We'll teach you ourselves!"-Su Mei panicked the moment his son wailed, hastily coaxing him.-"Our household boasts two Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses—are we still afraid we can't teach our own boy?"

—"Tsk. [A doting father ruins the son](# "Literal CN: 慈父多败儿 (cí fù duō bài ér) — traditional Chinese idiom meaning an overly indulgent father spoils and ruins his child"),"-Xin Zhu muttered huffily.

However, now that Xin Zhu had entertained such a terrifying notion, Su Xin didn't dare push his luck any further.

What if his mother suddenly hardened her resolve and actually packed him off to the Heavenly Spirit Sect?

Su Xin dared not say another word about the Heavenly Spirit Sect or Zhou Hui lest he re-trigger his mother's inspiration. He hurriedly terminated the transmission, resolving right then and there not to contact home again until he was safely back at the Heavenly Dao Alliance headquarters in the Northern Domain!

Left behind after the call ended, the Mei-Zhu couple continued discussing the matter for a while.

—"Speaking of which, should we grant the Heavenly Spirit Sect a few extra entry quotas for that secret realm?"-Su Mei brought up proactively.

—"Is that necessary?"-Xin Zhu hadn't thought that deeply.-"I believe Untiring organizing sect exams is simply standard routine for the Heavenly Spirit Sect; it has nothing to do with that secret realm. After all, knowing Untiring's style, if he truly cared about that realm, he'd take matters into his own hands, wouldn't he?"

—"I think so too. That secret realm is capped below the Golden Core stage anyway; at most, it's just a playground for youngsters to get some practical drills,"-Su Mei explained his calculations.-"However, by doing Untiring this favor, we can have his disciples protect our Xin'er!"

[—"Protect Xin'er?"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

—"Precisely. The ['Heavenly Book'](# "Literal CN: 天书 (tiānshū) — Heavenly Book / Book of Destiny; in meta-web-novels, this refers to glimpses of the original plot script or heavenly prophecy possessed by high-level figures") prophesied that our son is destined to face a tribulation within the secret realm. Although we've dispatched ample manpower to guard him, we cannot alter the heavenly destiny inscribed in the 'Heavenly Book', nor can we personally enter, and we don't even know what specific tribulation it will be! Meanwhile, that useless He Yong actually allowed underlings to escort our boy into a sleazy den like the Palace of Fallen Immortals!"-Su Mei complained in displeasure.

His revulsion toward the Palace of Fallen Immortals was entirely genuine. It wasn't merely because of Su Xin's whining; rather, just as Su Mei had flared up at the very start, this sect clearly wasn't any good egg from the sound of its name alone. With He Yong keeping watch, some blind idiot had actually dragged his precious child to such a den of iniquity—how could any doting parent rest easy?

Su Mei analyzed the situation with reasoned conviction:

—"Under these circumstances, having the Heavenly Spirit Sect watch over him becomes paramount. While Untiring won't enter personally either, if his disciples possess even thirty percent of Untiring's meticulousness and conscientiousness, it will be more than enough for them to notice our Xin'er's difficulties."

—"Very true! In that case, let's grant the Heavenly Spirit Sect a few more slots!"-Xin Zhu said, enlightened.-"I shall issue the orders immediately."

[—"And another thing! Don't forget to have He Yong investigate that Palace of Fallen Immortals!"](# "Speaker: Su Mei")

[—"Of course! Any trash attempting to lead our child astray cannot be tolerated!"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

*

The bureaucratic efficiency of the cultivation world could be sluggish beyond belief, yet it could also be blindingly fast.

No sooner had the Mei-Zhu couple made up their minds than, a single day later, Zhou Hui received five massive cartloads of gifts alongside a personal handwritten letter from the couple. Those in the know looked on with utter bewilderment; anyone out of the loop would have thought the Untiring Immortal Venerable had saved Su Xin's life!

Zhou Hui originally hadn't paid it much mind. He instructed Qu Qianxing to store the gifts in the sect treasury, then picked up the couple's letter and browsed through it, finally learning the true purpose of He Yong's earlier visit.

—"The Blade-Flying Peak Secret Realm? So that's how it is,"-Zhou Hui murmured softly.

This secret realm was an extraordinarily pivotal location in the "novel."

Not this current expedition, however.

Rather, several centuries later, after the so-called "protagonist" became a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he would register to explore this very secret realm. Through a series of bizarre coincidences, he and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's youngest junior sister, Mo Bei, would be trapped inside together. Romantic feelings would secretly sprout between them, setting off a cascading chain of tragic causes and karmic consequences downstream.

A location custom-tailored purely for a web-novel protagonist to [flirt with girls](# "Literal CN: 撩妹 (liáomèi) — modern Chinese slang for hitting on or flirting with girls") was something Zhou Hui didn't want in the slightest!

Yet the expedition quotas had still been delivered directly into Zhou Hui's hands. This was completely unlike the usual MO of the cultivation world's Heavenly Dao Alliance:

In Zhou Hui's experience, when it came to lucrative windfalls like exploring a newly unearthed secret realm, if he didn't proactively demand a share, the Heavenly Dao Alliance would always muddle through the matter and toss him a single token honorary slot to brush him off. Yet this time, they had been eerily generous, delivering a stack of quotas right to his doorstep unprompted.

Zhou Hui murmured softly:

—"Heavenly destiny truly cannot be evaded."

If that was the case, he might as well face it head-on!
