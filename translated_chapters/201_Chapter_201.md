# Chapter 201: Reaping What One Sows

[Just where on earth did Little Long and Second Senior Brother go? They still haven't returned even now.]

Guan Ye thought to himself.

At this moment, his eyes remained glued to the furnace fire, the muscles along his arms corded and bulging. With one hand, he held the dragon tendon steadily clamped with heavy tongs, while his hammer rose and fell in relentless, rhythmic strikes.

This was an exceptionally grueling task.

Because dragon tendon was an extraordinarily rigid material—even harder than defensive dragon scales. Ordinary flames couldn't leave the faintest scratch upon it; only high-tier flames of Samadhi True Fire or above could hope to refine it.

Yet even when dragon tendon entered a suitable fire, its physical properties flipped into the exact opposite of its prior hardness, turning exceptionally brittle and unstable. The slightest fluctuation in the flame's temperature or volume would reduce the dragon tendon to flying ash.

Under such conditions, forging the dragon tendon was doubly difficult. Not only did the hammer strokes have to be light and rhythmic, but the smith also had to maintain unwavering stamina and endurance. The faintest deviation in force midway through would result in catastrophic failure.

In short, this was simply not a task a Foundation Establishment cultivator could accomplish.

Had the Guan Ye of back then possessed even the slightest understanding of forging dragon tendon, the moment the foreman brought that tendon back, he would have realized there was a conspiracy afoot, rather than ending up having his bone extracted... No, even so, would he really have been able to escape?

Thinking thus, Guan Ye suddenly recalled Lu Shulong's reaction when they had discussed how his spine had been extracted:

—"Eh? Was it dragon tendon 'this time'?"-Lu Shulong had asked.

[—"This time?"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"...What I mean is, dragon tendon is such a precious material; how generous of them to actually bring it out! I assumed that even if they went out of their way to frame you, it would have been fledgling spirit bird feathers, water-spirit ice crystals, star-dust petals, thousand-cold subterranean water, divine fish sea-fins... items like that."-Lu Shulong explained.

—"Your examples are entirely too specific, aren't they? Besides, I actually refined all of those before,"-Guan Ye noted.

[—"Ah?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Guan Ye had mused:

—"I'm not sure if it was because our clan head had ties to the Heavenly Dao Alliance, so our forge frequently received bizarre materials for refinement. I handled everything you just listed. Perhaps because of that, when I was ordered to watch over the dragon tendon, I didn't think too much of it. Who knew I wouldn't have the same 'good luck' as before? Previously, I fumbled through rare materials blindly and somehow managed to treat them properly; but with dragon tendon, I had zero feel for it whatsoever, failing completely."-Guan Ye recalled.

—"I don't think that's good luck; that's building muscle mem... wait a minute! Why on earth was dragon tendon the *only* thing you had zero feel for?! We've been close for so long!"-Lu Shulong protested.

—"Precisely because we're close... what kind of [dogshit](# "Literal CN: 狗P (gǒu pì) — bullshit / nonsense") sentence pattern is that?"-Guan Ye clicked his tongue, reflecting on himself.-"Speaking of which, it seems because I get along well with you, I subconsciously avoided materials related to dragons."

Although dragon materials were of the highest grade, because they were adjacent to Lu Shulong, Guan Ye ultimately harbored a psychological barrier.

Guan Ye felt this mindset was quite improper.

Lu Shulong cared even more than Guan Ye:

—"That won't do! You don't mind handling materials related to humans at all, so how can you have this kind of blind spot?! Besides! Are other dragons the same as me?! I'm one of a kind!"-Lu Shulong insisted.

From then on, Lu Shulong scoured who-knew-where to dig up an endless supply of dragon materials for Guan Ye to practice on.

By now, Guan Ye's forging of dragon tendon was already thoroughly second nature.

Whether it was the heat management or the force of the hammer strikes, everything was immaculate. Among all the high-tier artifact refinement masters present, not a single one could find a flaw. In fact, half the refiners desperately wished they could record the process onto jade slips to take home and study in detail; some were so entranced that they hardly cared what was happening on Guan Xinguang's side.

Guan Ye's sole weakness was his lack of cultivation.

Foundation Establishment was simply too, too low. To dragons—whether a true dragon like Lu Shulong or the realm's distant draconic cousins whose claws and tails were imperfect—a Foundation Establishment cultivator was barely distinguishable from a mortal: a fragile creature that could be snuffed out by a single breath of dragon flame. Even in death, the residual spiritual power lingering within their formidable corpses was more than enough to drag such an opponent down into hell.

Naturally, the spiritual reserves of an ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivator couldn't even control Samadhi True Fire, let alone refine dragon tendon. As for Guan Ye's mangled body—with its extracted spine and crippled spirit roots—it was even less capable.

The reason Guan Ye was currently able to refine the dragon tendon was that he was drawing upon and borrowing the ambient spiritual energy around him.

More accurately, in this refinement process, Guan Ye treated himself as a semiconductor component. He had refined the replacement spine Master gifted him, enabling it to absorb every usable scrap of surrounding spiritual energy to refine the dragon tendon.

Naturally, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual energy was more than sufficient.

Leaving aside the fact that this territory possessed Zhou Hui—a supreme existence on par with the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree who continuously nourished the world—the mere fact that Zhou Hui had designed a 'Spiritual Energy Reservoir' to hoard the chaotic energy storms of the Central Domain was enough to keep the region's spiritual energy exceptionally lush and abundant.

Furthermore, the high, middle, and low-tier cultivators gathered at the Heavenly Spirit Sect were not allowed to go to waste.

Whenever they utilized the Heavenly Spirit Sect's public magical artifacts—such as projecting their research papers onto the massive jade screens in Buyan Palace's academic lecture halls—or sat in meditation, they were required to pay a modest 'spiritual energy tax,' siphoning a fraction of the energy they expended and generated to replenish the spiritual reservoir.

On this basis, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual energy was sufficient not merely to refine a segment of dragon tendon, but even to refine a True God.

As a direct disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's master, Guan Ye could requisition this spiritual energy however he pleased.

However, at this moment, Guan Ye's "tail"—the very tip of his new spine—dragged along the floor of the examination platform, trembling faintly. What it was absorbing was not the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual energy, but rather the energy of Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field.

The energy within this spiritual field was likewise more than sufficient.

After all, Guan Xinguang was a Mahayana titan, and the materials he was using to forge his artifact were top-tier treasures. Although the resulting spiritual energy field was somewhat turbulent, possessed terrible compatibility, and carried properties completely opposite to the materials Guan Ye was refining, in terms of sheer concentration and volume, it was entirely adequate.

Besides, those shortcomings weren't true drawbacks at all.

Had he collected it heedlessly and stuffed it foolishly into the furnace, it would indeed have triggered a catastrophic furnace explosion. But with Guan Ye acting as an intermediary relay, once he seized the energy from the opposing spiritual field, he passed it through an additional refining cycle, thoroughly transmuting it into a usable state that wouldn't disrupt his crafting in the slightest.

This step wasn't difficult—at least, not for Guan Ye.

Aside from expending a bit more personal effort, requiring an extra processing step, and enduring a bit more spiritual pressure, the only consequence of excessively draining the opposing spiritual field would be causing Guan Xinguang to fail his forging, suffer a furnace detonation, sustain severe injuries, or—if handled even worse—suffer spiritual energy backlash and bodily destruction.

It wasn't as though his spirit roots were being crippled; for a Mahayana cultivator, that surely wouldn't count for much.

Right?

Guan Ye looked cold and detached as he stared at the segment of dragon tendon, musing inwardly.

Indeed, what Guan Ye sought was revenge!

[—"Do you truly want to take revenge?"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

That day, Ran Deng—thoroughly startled by Guan Ye's reaction—had asked him with utter disbelief.

Guan Ye had found his Eldest Senior Sister's reaction far more unbelievable:

—"I am no saint like Master! Why wouldn't I want revenge?"-Guan Ye replied.

—"Didn't you always say that perhaps they weren't targeting you specifically?"-Ran Deng asked.

—"I still think so,"-Guan Ye frowned. Whenever he spoke of his past, his tone remained objective, calm, and constantly inclined toward self-reflection.-"Before Master took me in, I was merely a mortal, the most useless kind—and even now, not much has changed. People like that are everywhere; there was nothing special about my spine. There is no reason they would waste an item as rare as dragon tendon just to swap it out."-Guan Ye analyzed.

Indeed, even now, Guan Ye did not believe this was the 'deliberate framing' Lu Shulong had claimed.

As an artifact refiner, Guan Ye did not believe a mortal's spine could ever compare to any piece of dragon tendon, nor to any of the rare materials Lu Shulong had rattled off earlier. It simply wasn't worth it.

—"I've always believed the truth behind my spine being extracted was that an artifact refiner from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works botched the dragon tendon, causing it to become irreversibly ruined. Unwilling to bear responsibility, he dumped it down into the lowest forge and picked a scapegoat like me,"-Guan Ye explained, a vengeful sneer finally surfacing on his face.

—"Doesn't that sound like it has nothing to do with Guan Xinguang, then?"-Ran Deng pressed.

It was precisely because Guan Ye had always said this that Ran Deng had worried he might think she was meddling in affairs that didn't concern him.

Of course, Ran Deng hardly believed Guan Xinguang was innocent. Guan Ye might think his spine was unremarkable, but in Ran Deng's eyes, if even Master couldn't find his original spine, there had to be an extraordinary reason; under no circumstances could Guan Xinguang be acquitted of blame.

Fortunately, Guan Ye possessed his own iron logic:

—"How could it have nothing to do with him?! If someone in your Formation Pavilion murdered a random passerby to use as a foundation stone just to set an array or patch a formation's flaw, would you let them off?"-Guan Ye demanded.

—"...I would kill him just as he killed that passerby,"-Ran Deng said.

—"Same for me,"-Guan Ye nodded.-"Therefore, it's only natural for me to resent the Chief Director of the Department of Works, isn't it?"

Moreover, Guan Ye would remember the day he 'died' for all eternity.

Because it was simply too painful!

A pain so excruciating it could be remembered across several lifetimes!

He didn't know if having one's spine extracted always felt like that, but Guan Ye remembered every single nerve ending in his body screaming in agony, every tissue struggling and recounting its grievances. Under such conditions, peaceful death was impossible; even time itself was subjectively dragged out.

A single second became as long as an entire lifetime.

And within that 'endless' torment, Guan Ye had looked back upon his fleeting, brief life.

Perhaps because the pain was entirely too immense, or perhaps because his nature was inherently gloomy, Guan Ye's recollections contained not a single pleasant memory—not one! Only resentment!

He remembered the sneers and mockery surrounding him the moment he was tested with trash spirit roots;

He remembered the endless overtime, sleepless nights, and the beatings and abuse from the forge foreman during work;

He remembered the sinister gazes and forced labor from the Department of Works when he was coerced into handling rare materials;

And more, and more... resentments he couldn't even consciously recall...

In that instant, faint traces of agony and hatred from several lifetimes seemed to assault Guan Ye, almost transforming him into the most terrifying vengeful ghoul before he had even fully died, driving him to butcher every single existence he resented:

For instance, the foreman who faked illness to force him to forge the dragon tendon;

For instance, the high-tier cultivator who tore out his bones;

For instance, the negligent Chief Director of the Department of Works;

For instance, the fellow disciples who ignored and abandoned him—wait, did he even have fellow disciples back then? They were just coworkers, weren't they?

For instance, the unfair Heavenly Dao that caused his death and repeatedly struck at...

[The Heavenly Dao? Struck at what?]

Guan Ye thought in confusion. He seemed on the verge of recalling something exceptionally, monumentally important; yet precisely because of this momentary doubt, his transformation into a vengeful ghoul was interrupted.

After that, Guan Ye never had the chance to turn into a vengeful ghoul again, because in the very next second, he was enveloped by a slightly cool embrace.

That cool sensation was wonderfully soothing, gently quelling Guan Ye's agony and granting him a sliver of strength to pull his mind back from hatred, looking toward the figure holding him.

He saw a pair of silver-grey eyes.

That silver-grey hue resembled ice and metal, appearing cold and emotionless at first glance, yet in truth it was unimaginably gentle and tender—reminiscent of the moon hanging over a shadowy mountain path, softly enveloping all travelers and illuminating the way for the lost.

Guan Ye didn't know why, but he felt those eyes were extraordinarily familiar.

Clearly, the owner of those eyes was neither kin nor friend—after he had ruined the dragon tendon and offended the 'big shot' in the Department of Works, everyone connected to Guan Ye had severed all ties—nor was it anyone he had ever met; yet he inexplicably felt as though he had looked into those eyes countless times before.

Then, he heard the owner of those familiar eyes speak in a familiar voice, trembling as he uttered words Guan Ye did not recognize:

—"I'm sorry. The seventh time... I'm sorry..."

[No, why are you apologizing?!]

[Even if the entire world is in the wrong, you alone are never in the wrong!]

Guan Ye thought dimly.

He wanted to reach out his hand to touch those gentle eyes, but because his spine had been extracted, he couldn't even perform such a simple action. Instead, the impulse triggered a wave of overwhelming pain that tore through his body, finally plunging him into unconsciousness.

When he awoke, Guan Ye had already arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

He remembered everything that occurred before his blackout vividly. Yet regarding those illogical aspects—such as his inexplicable familiarity with Master, and the immense resentment mingled with other foreign memories—Guan Ye could never fathom them. Nor was he the type to obsess over abstract thoughts, simply chalking those anomalies up to the mental confusion brought on by blinding pain.

The only certainty was that Guan Ye never wished to hear Master speak to him in that "I'm sorry" tone again. Whether it was a hallucination born of agony or not, that sorrowful tone and expression should never appear on Master.

This was also why Guan Ye had refused to let Master take revenge on his behalf:

If it caused Master's relationship with the Mei-Zhu couple to sour, would Master be saddened?

In short, he could never place Master in a difficult position.

Guan Ye had always believed this.

Yet that hardly meant that when his Eldest Senior Sister delivered his enemy right to his doorstep, Guan Ye would refuse to act.

Especially when this setting was exceptionally suited for orchestrating 'accidents.'

Even if he truly killed Guan Xinguang, the one ridiculed would only be the Mahayana-stage Guan Xinguang.

Of course, an excuse was still required:

—"I had no choice. My platform was completely encircled by 'Chief Director Guan's' spiritual field; to acquire spiritual energy, I could only resort to this method."-Guan Ye murmured.

...Blegh, disgusting.

That sounded like something Little Long or Little Xi'er would say.

Guan Ye possessed full self-awareness regarding his linguistic expression and acting skills; he deeply doubted whether he could deliver an appropriate explanation after the exam concluded.

However...

Guan Ye's gloomy gaze drifted down toward Guan Xinguang on the platform below.

Or perhaps, toward something else lingering upon that platform.

Ever since the exam began, Guan Ye had sensed that just like the day his tendons were severed and his bones ripped out, the accumulated grievances of several lifetimes seemed to press heavily upon that examination platform. He wished nothing more than to crush the other party into powder immediately.

This resentment seemed completely groundless and excessively vicious; even Guan Ye hadn't expected to harbor such violent hatred toward this 'Chief Director of the Department of Works' whom he had never met before.

Worse still, he didn't want to restrain his resentment in the slightest.

—"The terrible thing isn't revenge, but making revenge your entire life... Jie-jie, that is what your beloved Master told Zhen back then, and he even helped Zhen take revenge."-Ruwang Ghost Emperor's eerie voice echoed in Guan Ye's memory.

—"To be frank, only a vengeful ghoul like Zhen requires more resentment as a source of power. As for you, thanks to your Master, you are still a living human. If you don't wish to degenerate into a ghost cultivator like Zhen, Zhen actually advises you to resolve those resentments; only then can you lay down your burdens and journey onward with a light heart."-Ruwang had told him.

Guan Ye agreed with that perspective.

He felt his current resentment had grown so dense it was bordering on an inner demon. If this continued, he might not even advance smoothly to the Golden Core Stage. But that wouldn't do: the large-scale teleportation array wasn't finished, Master still had to teach him how to build the cloud server, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's engineering projects remained incomplete, and he had far too many things left to finish.

Therefore, this trifling 'past' had to be cleaned up as quickly as possible.

Guan Ye resolved inwardly.

Yet the force and cadence of his hammer striking the dragon tendon did not waver in the slightest. Only his fierce hatred and resentment—transmuted alongside the spiritual energy plundered from Guan Xinguang's spiritual field—hammered into the dragon tendon stroke by stroke.

When the dragon tendon's color shifted from corpse-pale to a resilient, translucent luster, Guan Ye let out a slight breath of relief. It was nearly time for the next step: quenching the dragon tendon.

Guan Ye reached for the tongs, but his movement paused slightly.

He sensed a change in the spiritual energy field, prompting him to look down toward the lower platform once more:

—"Worthy of a Mahayana cultivator, discovering I was refining your spiritual field so quickly? Come then, let's contest and see what tricks you have to dismantle my power, jie-jie,"-Guan Ye muttered, flashing the quintessential dark smirk of a classic villain.

***

Regrettably, the "protagonist" of this world had yet to be born.

Even if a "protagonist" truly existed, someone like Guan Xinguang would at best be a background mob strutting around an auction to display pomp, not even qualified to craft weapons for the protagonist—after all, anyone crafting weapons for a 'protagonist' had to be at least a master refiner capable of hand-crafting divine artifacts. Consequently, Guan Xinguang was exceptionally suited to be slain by the mini-boss Guan Ye here today, serving as future evidence to grant the "protagonist" a righteous pretext to eradicate the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

From this perspective, Guan Xinguang seemed remarkably well-suited to die today.

Of course, Guan Xinguang had no desire to die so casually.

Even if his brain had already been somewhat scrambled by Lin Qi.

—"A mere Foundation Establishment junior—does he truly think he can refine my spiritual energy field?!"-Guan Xinguang gnashed his teeth.

He scrambled to stuff a pile of premium materials into his refining furnace, attempting to reinforce his spiritual energy field.

Guan Xinguang's scrambled brain failed to realize that his actions were being observed with absolute clarity by the spectator stands.

At the very least, Fairy Maogui's live commentary took on a hesitant tone:

—"We can see that Chief Director Guan has once again placed refined fire and snow-essence charcoal into the furnace. Er, I haven't studied artifact refinement myself... is artifact forging truly this material-intensive? That's enough fuel to keep me warm for an entire year, haha..."-Fairy Maogui chuckled awkwardly.

[No, we artifact refiners do not squander materials like that!]

[Rather, what on earth is Guan Xinguang refining? Is he truly forging a flying sword?!]

The refiners in attendance wore thoroughly complicated expressions.

Conversely, an observant sword cultivator watching the spectacle spotted the abnormality:

—"He wouldn't actually be trying to use his spiritual energy field to attack that Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple, would he?"-a sword cultivator asked.

—"This isn't the Immortal Arts Assembly!"-someone barked.

—"Exactly, this is an 'examination,' not a combat tournament!"-an artifact refiner shouted with a flushed, indignant face.-"Besides, relying solely on a spiritual field to suppress and disrupt another person's forging—even if he secures the so-called top score, he is unworthy of being called an artifact refiner!"

This refiner's words garnered nods of agreement from all around.

Yet not a syllable reached Guan Xinguang's ears.

Only Lin Qi heard it all. At this point, Lin Qi wished Guan Xinguang weren't some damned artifact refiner at all. He suggested to him:

—"Must you rely on artifact-refinement methods to expand your spiritual field? As a Mahayana cultivator, do you have no practical spells to broaden your field?"-Lin Qi urged.

He had completely figured it out: this "master refiner" was mostly hot air. If he relied honestly on forging methods to bolster his spiritual field, he would likely be thoroughly refined by the opponent without inflicting a scratch.

Guan Xinguang hesitated:

—"I... I do, but under the gaze of the crowd..."-Guan Xinguang stammered.

—"However, you are an 'artifact refinement master.' Can those shallow fools truly comprehend your profound operations?"-Lin Qi deliberately provoked.

The hesitation on Guan Xinguang's face deepened.

Lin Qi sneered secretly. He didn't forcefully cast spells to compel Guan Xinguang further, but he knew that as long as a suitable opportunity presented itself, Guan Xinguang would act exactly as he desired.

After all, he, Lin Qi, was no heart demon, but a 'True Immortal'!

His guidance was no vile demonic temptation, but rather the 'courage' and 'wisdom' arising from people's own hearts—life would always find a way!

—"Think it over yourself. I'll test the brat's condition for you in the meantime,"-Lin Qi said with feigned nonchalance.-"Things on that side seem a bit abnormal; perhaps he has already cheated, otherwise how could he contest so effortlessly against a Mahayana titan like you?"

Saying so, several golden threads crept toward the upper platform once more.

This time, however, the golden threads were virtually transparent, blending almost seamlessly into Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field, taking utmost care to avoid detection.

Lin Qi wasn't entirely fooling Guan Xinguang; he genuinely sensed the overwhelming hatred brimming upon Platform Forty-Four. For someone like Lin Qi who excelled at manipulating others' emotions, this presented a prime vulnerability.

—"...Though I thought so, what is with this brat? His mental state is clearly like this, yet why can't I find the slightest opening? Resentment is supposed to be the easiest catalyst for soul instability."-Lin Qi grumbled inwardly.

Lin Qi's golden threads hovered around Platform Forty-Four for several rounds, yet it was like facing a curled-up hedgehog—he simply couldn't find anywhere to sink his teeth in.

Even the opponent's forging rhythm remained immaculate. Even a layman like Lin Qi could see it was completely unassailable.

Was this kid truly a Foundation Establishment cultivator?

Leaving aside that counterfeit 'master refiner' below, none of the other artifact refiners he had seen possessed such gorgeous, practiced technique; it looked as though he had hammered metal for thousands of years.

[However, if such a rhythm is disrupted, a crack will appear in the refiner's mind as well, won't it?]

Lin Qi calculated.

Only, Lin Qi's physical flesh wasn't here, rendering him incapable of physical interference. Thus, he could only look toward his puppet, deciding to utilize him once more.

And just as Lin Qi anticipated, Guan Xinguang began hesitantly executing 'petty maneuvers only Mahayana cultivators could pull off.'

Unfortunately, this was the Heavenly Spirit Sect—the Untiring Immortal Venerable's domain—and an examination overseen directly by the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself. The actions Guan Xinguang could take were severely limited; at most, he merely threw in 'materials' completely useless for forging, and his execution was so clumsy that anyone could deduce his intent.

Whether the spectator stands realized it was uncertain, but a faint commotion had indeed begun.

It seemed he was incompetent not only at forging, but at cheating as well.

Lin Qi sighed at this useless puppet, yet once more reinforced Guan Xinguang's 'conviction' to make him continue. Not only that, but he also enhanced the man's karmic fortune.

Naturally, this wasn't because Lin Qi favored Guan Xinguang, but simply because the more fortune Guan Xinguang possessed, the greater the disruptive influence upon his 'enemy,' thereby suppressing the 'enemy's' luck.

In other words, the luckier Guan Xinguang was, the unluckier Guan Ye would be.

And artifact refiners were heavily reliant on luck. Even if he couldn't disrupt Guan Ye's forging directly, under extreme bad luck, wouldn't a tiny operational error snowball into a catastrophe, creating a massive fissure?

Lin Qi planned cheerfully. And then, he witnessed the most unbelievable scene of his life:

That Foundation Establishment refiner was not merely refining the spiritual energy field—he was goddamned refining karmic luck!

No, it wasn't strictly refinement.

Karmic fortune naturally favored the truly talented; this was the foundational origin of 'Entering the Dao through Craftsmanship.' Evidently, this refiner's operations were so immaculate that Lin Qi couldn't shake Guan Ye's fortune in the slightest; instead, the fortune previously gathered for Guan Xinguang was steadily transferring onto Guan Ye's body!

—"Worthy of one who possesses a Dao Bone,"-Lin Qi muttered.

Killing such an artifact refiner was truly a pity.

For a moment, Lin Qi wavered. Even for a True Immortal, acquiring a magical treasure perfectly suited to one's desires was extraordinarily difficult; otherwise, the Heavenly Court wouldn't scour the lower realms for various prodigies.

In contrast, look at his current puppet!

Lin Qi thought with growing fury. In a fit of displaced rage, he violently yanked the golden threads anchored to Guan Xinguang.

Guan Xinguang froze instantly.

Yet Guan Xinguang did not realize this was Lin Qi's doing. He merely felt a flash of inspiration in his mind, a marvelous idea suddenly popping into his head:

[Why didn't I think of this earlier? To counter that brat, isn't it simple? Just use *that*!]

With his mind consumed entirely by victory and devoid of any capacity to weigh pros and cons, Guan Xinguang hurriedly retrieved a long, pale-white 'material' from his storage pouch and tossed it into the furnace.

Following the addition of this 'material,' the spiritual energy field across the arena abruptly reversed. The spiritual field originally being refined by Guan Ye suddenly surged with power, counter-devouring Guan Ye's spiritual energy field!

—"Well done!"-Lin Qi praised.

He finally sensed a hairline fracture appear upon Platform Forty-Four. Instantly transforming into a streak of golden light, he flew toward the opening.

After Lin Qi departed, Guan Xinguang felt his mind and body lighten considerably. A sinister smile broke across his face, anticipating Lin Qi directly annihilating that irritating Foundation Establishment brat.

Yet his joy didn't last long. With Lin Qi's sensory shielding gone, Guan Xinguang's perception restored, allowing him to clearly hear the spectator stands erupting into an absolute uproar:

[—"What did he just throw in? A human bone?!"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

[—"It looks like a spine..."](# "Speaker: Spectator")

[—"No, it doesn't matter what he threw in! He is blatantly using his spiritual energy field to attack another examinee! And against a Foundation Establishment child, no less!"](# "Speaker: Spectator Refiner")

The refiners who had previously attempted to speak on Guan Xinguang's behalf felt completely humiliated:

[—"How could he?!"](# "Speaker: Spectator Refiner")

[—"Does this even count as an examination?!"](# "Speaker: Spectator Refiner")

—"I think it's fine; quite fascinating, actually!"-someone chimed in. Most of these spectators were not artifact refiners, but cultivators who had come purely for the excitement.-"A contest of spiritual energy fields—isn't this just as entertaining as the Immortal Arts Assembly?"

[—"This is not the Immortal Arts Assembly! This is an exam! And he is desecrating the entire profession of artifact refinement!"](# "Speaker: Spectator Refiner")

[—"How did a scoundrel like this ever become Chief Director of the Department of Works?!"](# "Speaker: Spectator Refiner")

—"However, I see that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's disciple hasn't shown the slightest weakness. If anything, his spiritual energy field remains exceptionally stable under these conditions, continuing to expand. You refiners aren't seeing that the Chief Director is in trouble and trying to shield him, are you?"-a bystander countered.

The refiners were left red-faced and speechless.

One refiner gave up arguing entirely, looking up toward the dais:

[—"Untiring Immortal Venerable, please disqualify Chief Director Guan... Guan Xinguang from the exam! Such a man is unworthy of being called an artifact refiner!"](# "Speaker: Spectator Refiner")

—"Indeed, it ought to be severed—severing this thief's life!"-Before the Untiring Immortal Venerable could reply, Ruwang Ghost Emperor's eerie voice rang out.-"How dare he refine artifacts right in front of the victim using that very victim's extracted spine?!"

[—"The victim's... spine?!"](# "Speaker: Spectators")

The spectator stands exploded completely. Even Fairy Maogui at the commentary booth widened her eyes, incapable of uttering a sound.

[No, I didn't...]

Guan Xinguang's vision went black as he realized the situation had spiraled entirely out of control.

His heart was filled with frantic regret:

[Why did it turn out like this?!]

[I was clearly sheltered by a True Immortal, yet it felt as though I was controlled by a demon... Right, I can claim I was possessed by an evil demon! Otherwise, how stupid would I have to be to pull out the other party's spine in broad daylight?!]

Indeed, what Guan Xinguang had tossed into the fire was Guan Ye's original spine.

Back then, after confirming from Lin Qi that the extracted bone was merely useless mortal bone, he had intended to discard it. Yet out of some inexplicable impulse, he had carried it with him, and finally, as if guided by ghosts, he had used it.

That sensation of being manipulated by something in the dark...

It truly had to be an evil demon!

Guan Xinguang frantically rolled his eyes, concocting excuses to absolve himself.

It was at this moment that the Untiring Immortal Venerable's voice resonated across the venue—miraculously, Guan Xinguang only now realized that after stepping onto the platform, he had almost forgotten the existence of this terrifying titan:

—"Calm yourselves. For now, let the examination proceed; after all, my disciple has reached a crucial juncture,"-Zhou Hui said calmly.

[...What does that mean?]

*BOOM——!*

As if answering the question, the refining furnace beside Guan Xinguang abruptly detonated! Surging spiritual materials and anomalous flames fueled by potent karmic fortune blasted all over Guan Xinguang's head and body. Had he been a mortal, he would have instantly disintegrated into ash; yet Guan Xinguang's Mahayana flesh was extraordinarily resilient, resembling a newly lit log of firewood, burning slowly.

The agony of this moment was infinitely more excruciating than when Zhou Hui had obliterated him into powder with a single bolt of heavenly lightning at the auction centuries ago.

—"NO!"-Guan Xinguang screamed.

Yet he had no intention of extinguishing the flames. Instead, he decisively chose self-detonation:

He prepared to flee!

It was merely a physical body. While Guan Xinguang's status as a master refiner remained questionable, he undeniably possessed considerable expertise in crafting backup physical vessels—which was precisely why he had been able to 'resurrect' after the Untiring Immortal Venerable wiped him out during the auction upheaval.

Forced to come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect by two immortals, Guan Xinguang had secretly made preparations.

He had concealed a backup body in a location known only to himself. Even if he "died" today, so what? At worst, he would simply resurrect there! Besides, Lin Qi had already flown toward Guan Ye; before long, he would extract the brat's bone, forge a body, and perhaps even teach the Untiring Immortal Venerable a lesson. When the world fell into turmoil, Guan Xinguang could hide away for a few years until everyone forgot his disgrace.

With these filthy calculations settled in his mind, Guan Xinguang wished only to blow himself into the finest dust possible.

And his physical body detonated into powder exactly as he hoped, forcing Zhou Hui to erect a temporary protective barrier around the platform to prevent the blast from affecting neighboring examinees.

However, Guan Xinguang's divine soul failed to escape.

This time, it wasn't the Untiring Immortal Venerable's doing. Rather, the instant his soul attempted to transfer, something hooked onto him.

Guan Xinguang looked back and discovered, to his utter horror, that it was a segment of spine!

Reaching out from the shattered furnace, the spine locked firmly around Guan Xinguang's neck, trapping his divine soul completely within his pulverized flesh and refining him alongside all the materials on the platform!

It was a slow, agonizing process. The refinement amplified all sensory perception, no different from having bones slowly extracted one by one while forced to remain wide awake. Even a Mahayana cultivator was not immune.

No—precisely because he was a Mahayana titan, the pain was magnified hundreds, thousands of times over! Moreover, with his physical flesh pulverized, his divine soul met the torment without the slightest buffer.

—"To be frank, I truly never imagined someone could be foolish to this degree,"-Zhou Hui summarized.-"Personally placing the finest conductor—Guan Ye's spine—into his own furnace, allowing Ye-zi's spiritual energy to refine him directly from within... what on earth was he thinking?"

Yet at this stage, nobody cared what Guan Xinguang was thinking.

Even the spectator stands fell into stunned silence, everyone watching this spectacle with wide, slack-jawed eyes.

They watched Guan Xinguang's furnace explode; they watched Guan Xinguang detonate his flesh; they watched Guan Xinguang's platform reduce to a molten slurry; they watched Guan Ye refine the spiritual field and that entire slurry; they watched Guan Ye's furnace erupt with extraordinary brilliance, to the point where even the weather outside the arena was affected:

The moment the artifact emerged from the furnace, the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect was enveloped in breathtaking, seven-colored celestial radiance.

[—"A divine artifact! It's a divine artifact, isn't it?!"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

[—"Just what did he forge?!"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

The crowd discussed in feverish curiosity, collectively ignoring Guan Xinguang's existence—or rather, at this juncture, nobody particularly cared to know what had become of him.

Guan Ye did not keep anyone in suspense.

Stepping down from the examination platform, a rare, radiant smile broke across his face as he looked toward Zhou Hui:

—"Master, I succeeded in forging it!"-Guan Ye announced proudly.

[—"The Immortal Binding Rope!"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

A coil of crystal-clear, sparkling rope radiated soft luminescence in Guan Ye's hands.

Merely gazing upon the artifact's appearance, likely no one would doubt its prowess—it gave the impression that let alone celestials, perhaps even divine gods could be bound firmly in place.

Zhou Hui, however, didn't cast a second glance at the Immortal Binding Rope. Instead, his gaze fell behind Guan Ye.

That oversized spine still trailed along the floor, but it had transformed from a lifeless, murky dark-red into a vibrant, soft coral crimson. Though its form remained formidable, it was now wrapped in a sacred luminescence that made onlookers hesitate to look directly upon it.

Zhou Hui knew that as long as Guan Ye wished, he could withdraw this bone into his body at any time, or shape it into whatever form he desired.

—"Indeed. You accomplished it,"-Zhou Hui said with deep gratification.

Guan Ye's Dao Bone was complete.
