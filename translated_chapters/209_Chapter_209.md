# Chapter 209: A Future That Will Never Happen

Arriving at this impasse, what choice did Lu Shulong have?

The sole silver lining was that the matter had not yet reached Master's ears, meaning the situation wasn't entirely beyond salvation.

Desperately wracking his wits, Lu Shulong came clean about everything.

However, upon learning what Qu Qianxing had orchestrated in the Demon Realm, the other disciples did not share the slightest conviction that this was 'salvageable.'

The only one present still capable of laughing and enjoying the show was Yuwen Xuan:

—"Hahaha! As expected of Old Second—every time he causes a stir, it's an earth-shattering affair!"-the so-called Eldest Senior Brother remarked with profound admiration, his face beaming with proud satisfaction,-"It is precisely because he is such an exceptional talent that I can rest easy entrusting the Heavenly Spirit Sect to his hands."

*Don't make sect succession sound like you magnanimously yielded it to Second Senior Brother!*

*Isn't it precisely because you're such a hopeless layabout that Master was forced to groom Second Senior Brother to take over the sect in the first place?!*

The junior siblings thought in unison.

With seamless harmony, they collectively ignored their useless Eldest Senior Brother.

—"We must tell Master!"-Guan Ye declared with an intensely grave expression,-"This is beyond anything we can resolve; we must report this to Master immediately!"

As he spoke, he directed a stern, reprimanding glare toward Lu Shulong:

—"When you were in the Demon Realm, you should have restrained Second Senior Brother! Speaking of which, Second Senior Brother is equally at fault! He is usually so steady and prudent—how could he become so reckless in such a perilous place?"

Lu Shulong bowed his head in apparent shame, but in truth, he stuck out his tongue and pulled a secret grimace.

He shared the closest bond with Guan Ye; Lu Shulong could predict every word out of Guan Ye's mouth using his tonsils alone. Consequently, he didn't take the lecture to heart at all, merely viewing Guan Ye as overly rigid and pedantic.

—"I also believe we must inform Master,"-Ran Deng chimed in at this moment,-"This matter exceeds the scope of our abilities."

Yet she clearly wasn't voicing pure support for Guan Ye; she simply found the entire ordeal bothersome.

Lu Shulong wasn't surprised.

This woman didn't even care about her own survival, [slacking off and rotting in place](# "Literal CN: 摆烂 (bǎilàn) — letting things rot; giving up and doing the bare minimum") in every lifetime; how could she ever risk Master's disciplinary wrath on Second Senior Brother's behalf? If anything, hearing that she had run off to steal the Heavenly Robe to let Eldest Senior Brother run amok had scared Lu Shulong half to death, making him severely suspect she had been brainwashed by Yuwen Xuan.

Now, gazing upon Senior Sister Ran Deng's half-dead, exhausted expression, Lu Shulong felt even more validated in his assessment.

Regardless, excluding the thoroughly useless Eldest Senior Brother, both the Eldest Senior Sister and Fourth Senior Brother had planted their flags. Whether the final remaining brat agreed or objected was ultimately inconsequential—and knowing that little twerp's opportunistic habit of sailing with the wind, nothing benevolent would come out of his mouth anyway.

Lu Shulong thought, glaring sideways at Mo Xi.

—"Actually, it's not as though I can't empathize with Fifth Senior Brother's feelings,"-Mo Xi rolled his large eyes, feigning distress, yet the twitching corners of his lips betrayed his identity as a chaos-loving spectator cut from the same cloth as Eldest Senior Brother,-"Although the course of action was admittedly somewhat reckless, returning safely laden with rich bounties is completely different from getting pinned down in enemy lines waiting for Master to bail you out. It's no wonder Fifth Senior Brother made a special trip back to steal—"

—"I returned to *retrieve* it,"-Lu Shulong emphasized fiercely,-"The Heavenly Robe was originally Second Senior Brother's property! One can only say his preparations were lacking; failing to carry it with him was his greatest blunder! Never did we anticipate someone else would use it first, reducing us to having to reclaim it from Master's hands!"

As he spoke, he cast an intensely accusatory glare toward the sect's Eldest Senior Brother and Eldest Senior Sister.

Ran Deng instantly wore a look of guilty conscience and shame; she truly held zero moral high ground in this debacle.

However, Yuwen Xuan was thick-skinned and shameless; having zero intention of reflecting on his sins, his grin only widened.

Mo Xi, loving nothing more than fanning the flames of chaos, continued his breakdown:

—"Very well, Fifth Senior Brother returned to 'retrieve' the Heavenly Robe to rescue Second Senior Brother. Provided everything unfolds smoothly without Master catching wind, this Demon Realm expedition remains a glorious triumph! Master wouldn't even discover the 'great deeds' you perpetrated in the Demon Realm, and would merely praise you for securing invaluable intelligence for the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

—"...The problem remains that Second Senior Brother is trapped,"-Guan Ye furrowed his brows.

He was indeed the most difficult to sway.

—"Trapped amidst a demonic horde, actively warring against the Heavenly Court. Regardless of which side discovers his true identity, the outcome will be catastrophic! If someone traces his affiliation back to our Heavenly Spirit Sect, the fallout will be even worse! When that happens, our entire realm might face extermination!"-Guan Ye argued.

He was by no means engaging in alarmism.

From his vantage point, if the demon race unmasked Qu Qianxing as a fraud, getting devoured alive on the spot would be considered a relatively 'merciful' demise. If any demon probed Qu Qianxing's origins and uncovered the dimensional rift beneath the Spirit Stone Secret Realm, the next world facing a full-scale demonic invasion would be their own.

Conversely, if Qu Qianxing's identity was exposed to the Celestial Realm, the consequences would be equally dire. At the very least, they would suspect this realm was conspiring with demons, which might prompt them to obliterate the Heavenly Spirit Sect entirely.

At this juncture, Guan Ye, who had not yet recovered his memories of the prior cycles, remained blissfully ignorant of the fact that their master and disciples had long been blacklisted by the Celestial Realm.

Up to this point, the only disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect who had not recovered their memories of the previous reincarnations were Qu Qianxing, Guan Ye, and Mo Xi. Among them, Mo Xi's sixth-life avatar was situated in close proximity to him, making his state practically identical to full recollection; while Qu Qianxing was undeniably drawing closer step by step to the truth of the previous cycles... and his historical ties to the Celestial Realm.

As a result, Guan Ye was the sole existence furthest removed from the memories of the previous reincarnations.

Yet Guan Ye was also the one person whom nobody wished to burden with the gruesome tragedies of those past cycles.

All the disciples who had reclaimed their memories agreed that the prior lifetimes were far too harrowing; whether one remembered or not made little difference. Furthermore, Master himself did not wish for them to recover those memories, so there was no need to go out of their way to enlighten anyone.

—Of course, certain individuals possessed complete recollections—particularly of the fourth cycle—and understood all too well what horrific deeds this Fourth Senior Brother could commit, having zero desire to witness his "awakening."

At least, Lu Shulong harbored this sentiment acutely, accompanied by a heavy mantle of guilt.

Guan Ye was oblivious to Lu Shulong's turbulent inner turmoil. Having finished his pragmatic assessment, he patiently urged Lu Shulong:

—"In such a precarious predicament, notifying Master is the only prudent course. Moreover, Master will not necessarily punish you. Knowing Master's temperament, he is far more likely to observe the situation from afar, allowing you to proceed with your plan while intervening only when you have truly exhausted all options."

*And then force us to write a fifty-thousand-character self-reflection essay upon our return, right?!*

*While that might be trivial for Second Senior Brother, there is no way in hell I am writing that!*

*Otherwise, why on earth would I want to conceal this from everyone in the first place?!*

Lu Shulong thought, weeping tears of despair.

To be fair, Zhou Hui was not generally a 'draconian' master. At times, when his disciples undertook 'bold' gambits, Master would even provide backing and logistical safeguards. Yet the disciples still preferred to act recklessly without reporting their circumstances beforehand. Setting aside their inherently defiant temperaments, the primary reason lay right here.

Who would want to go through a hair-raising brush with death, only to return home and have to draft an essay analyzing their personal shortcomings during their suicide mission?

However, while Lu Shulong dared to pull sneaky stunts behind Master's back, he did not dare defy the words of his closest Fourth Senior Brother.

To this day, he vividly remembered how in the fourth cycle, Fourth Senior Brother had uncovered the underhanded sabotage he had wrought during the third cycle, slaying him with an expression of shattered disappointment and utter despair. Even now, whenever he recalled that moment, Lu Shulong felt every scale on his body ache.

—"Fine, six against one, I concede,"-Lu Shulong sighed. Inadvertently exposing the fact that 'concealing everything' had been his own rogue initiative while Qu Qianxing had actually leaned toward reporting to Master, he picked up the Heavenly Robe and grumbled,-"Honestly, if Little Junior Sister didn't want me to hide it, she could have just said so directly. What was the need to round up everyone for an ambush?!"

—"Then you've misunderstood,"-it was Mo Xi who answered,-"My dear sister is incapable of something as sophisticated as 'irony.' She is likely the sole person here who genuinely agrees with you about keeping Master in the dark."

—"Huh?"-Lu Shulong blinked in bewilderment.

The others also instinctively turned their eyes toward the expressionless Mo Bei.

—"Come to think of it, Little Junior Sister truly didn't say a word, and didn't even report Fifth Junior Brother's return,"-Guan Ye noted in hindsight, finally realizing that the few instances where he had intended to report to Master had been deliberately interrupted by Mo Bei.

—"I originally intended to tell Master,"-Mo Bei admitted plainly,-"Everything."

—"Hey!"

*You actually went to Master with the express intention of snitching?!*

*Though compared to dragging all our brothers and sisters here for an inquisition only to conclude we should snitch anyway, snitching directly would have been far better!*

*So why didn't you just snitch?!*

Lu Shulong glared at his thoroughly unendearing Little Junior Sister.

—"However... I saw the 'future,'"-Mo Bei lowered her eyelashes, speaking in a measured cadence.

—"The future?"

The others wore peculiar expressions.

—"Master has been studying the array disc Eldest Senior Brother seized from the True Immortal, correct? That array disc appeared to be deducing certain realities,"-Mo Bei explained,-"Whether due to my unique constitution or some other factor, when I approached Master, I could distinctly feel those currents flooding into my mind, allowing me to perceive the projected deductions..."

—"That sounds fascinating,"-Yuwen Xuan's vitality instantly resurged,-"What sort of future did it show?"

—"It was—"-Mo Bei parted her lips, but promptly frowned in visible difficulty. The words hovered at the tip of her tongue, yet she couldn't articulate them, looking thoroughly lost on how to express the concepts.

Mo Bei had never been articulate to begin with.

Opening and closing her mouth several times, she ultimately abandoned the attempt to narrate it through spoken prose:

—"Describing it with words is far too troublesome. Mo Xi, use the Yin spirit cloud network to synchronize my memories and project them for everyone to see!"

—"Ugh, you're so demanding,"-Mo Xi grumbled.

Nonetheless, he did not refuse. Swiftly flicking open a glowing spiritual interface, he inscribed several arcane runes.

Instantly, a bone-chilling aura enveloped the room. It felt as though malevolent ghosts were possessing their physical forms; a draft of freezing air blew directly against the napes of their necks, and their shoulders grew unimaginably heavy.

Naturally, ordinary ghosts were trivial to cultivators; merely circulating spiritual energy was sufficient to repel the vast majority of Yin corruption. Yet precisely because of this, when this Yin chill licked against their skin, actively suppressing the instinct to cycle their cultivation and dampening their divine sense to willingly allow the Yin energy to latch onto them was extraordinarily difficult.

Ran Deng couldn't resist complaining:

—"Little Xi'er, what foul new technique is this? Why is it so disgust—"

Before she could finish her sentence, her pupils trembled violently as she stared blankly into the empty void.

Mirroring the spectacle Zhou Hui had witnessed, the grand, apocalyptic clash between celestial hosts and demonic legions was funneled directly into Ran Deng's mind. Moreover, compared to the detached cosmic prophecy of the starry sky, the vision experienced through a Yin spirit felt viscerally empathetic—especially when the imagery locked onto Qu Qianxing.

Ran Deng felt as though she were standing right beside Qu Qianxing, capable of palpably feeling the horrific, suffocating malice rolling off her Second Senior Brother.

This was a degree of bloodthirsty hostility that Qu Qianxing had only possessed when he first arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. While that hostility had terrified the very young Ran Deng back then—compounded by the fact that half of Qu Qianxing's body had not yet regenerated, leaving him horribly disfigured whenever his spiritual energy spiraled out of control, causing young Ran Deng to constantly mistake him for a monster who had abducted Master—compared to the Qu Qianxing she witnessed now, that former hostility seemed astonishingly faint!

The bloodlust exuded by the Qu Qianxing upon this celestial-demon battlefield was so dense it seemed to congeal into crimson clots of gore, making his entire being resemble an aberrant demonic flower blooming from a swamp of blood.

Ran Deng was momentarily paralyzed by this sheer malice, her long-dormant childhood trauma violently reawakened.

She remained frozen for several seconds before hoarsely seeking confirmation from Lu Shulong:

—"Qu... Second Senior Brother, is he currently like this?" *How did he revert to this ghastly, malice-ridden state? Wasn't he completely healed by Master? Even if he journeyed to the Demon Realm, he shouldn't have suffered a relapse!*

Lu Shulong failed to grasp the emotional crux of Ran Deng's question. While he had also been startled by Qu Qianxing's murderous aura, he was ultimately a True Dragon, inherently immune to human malice to a large degree; furthermore, he had never laid eyes on the Qu Qianxing of the distant past—every time Qu Qianxing arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Lu Shulong had not yet been picked up by Yuwen Xuan, sparing him that childhood trauma.

At most, he felt Second Senior Brother's 'acting skills' had become terrifyingly refined, frightening even him.

Lu Shulong nodded:

—"It is indeed highly probable that he is in this state... Ah, that demon beside him is the chieftain of the first settlement we encountered. Qu Qianxing truly led him and his demon forces toward the frontlines. If our time dilation calculations hold true, in three months, Second Senior Brother should be confronting the Celestial Realm upon the primary battlefield just like this."

Bolstered by Lu Shulong's corroboration, the other disciples fully accepted that this was indeed the 'future.'

—Qu Qianxing left them no choice but to believe.

Even though this Second Senior Brother appeared hundreds of times more terrifying than the one they knew, only Second Senior Brother possessed such a peerless, breathtaking aura; it was inconceivable that anyone else could impersonate him.

Only Yuwen Xuan curled the corners of his lips, as though seeing through something profound.

—"...Is that so,"-Ran Deng had no leisure to notice Yuwen Xuan, her parched lips parting faintly,-"However..." She wanted to inquire more deeply into Qu Qianxing's psychological state.

Yet the unfolding vision abruptly severed her voice.

While Ran Deng was conferring with Lu Shulong, the heavenly host and demonic hordes had already clashed, churning into a meat grinder of slaughter. This was the scene Zhou Hui had observed, containing little out of the ordinary; the true horror lay in what followed.

Just as the demons and heavenly soldiers were locked in a stalemate, a thunderous, sky-shattering dragon roar echoed across the void, interrupting Ran Deng's inquiries and seizing the undivided attention of every disciple.

—"Fifth Senior Brother?"-Mo Xi asked upon hearing the roar.

Guan Ye shook his head:

—"It sounds somewhat similar, but only vaguely. The timbre is considerably deeper; I don't believe it's him."

—"On the contrary, I feel it's definitely me!"-such a magnificent, awe-inspiring roar!

Lu Shulong joyfully 'watched' a swarm of demons and celestial troops instantly disintegrate into ash beneath the sonic dragon roar, feeling that this was precisely the majestic dragon might he was destined to command.

—"In three months, I must have successfully reached the battlefield, swooping in at the critical juncture to rescue Second Senior Brother!"-Lu Shulong declared with soaring confidence.

*With this level of overwhelming might, why would you even need the Heavenly Robe?!*

Everyone present burned to roast him, but they swiftly realized words were unnecessary.

Accompanied by that thunderous roar, the chaotic spatial boundaries between the Celestial Realm and Demon Domain warped violently, tearing open a colossal dimensional rift. First, razor-sharp claws thrust outward from the fissure, followed by a savage, terrifying dragon skull, a serpentine dragon torso flickering amidst billowing black fog, and an endless dragon tail stretching beyond sight.

Before this titanic dragon, both immortals and demons appeared utterly insignificant, precisely matching the majestic glory Lu Shulong had envisioned.

The sole problem was... this dragon was not alive!

It was not even an intact dragon—it was a zombie dragon.

The scales upon its body were virtually gone, exposing rotting, putrid flesh and gleaming white skeletal ribs beneath. Its eye sockets were two hollow black voids, glaring upon all creation with bottomless, rancorous hatred.

—"Ah... judging by the bone structure, I still think it looks remarkably like Fifth Senior Brother,"-Mo Xi muttered.

One couldn't tell if he was deliberately stirring the pot.

The dragon was rotted so thoroughly that not a single scale remained, rendering its species unidentifiable; yet from the curvature of its horns and skeletal framework, it was undeniably a True Dragon.

Lu Shulong also felt an eerie kinship with it:

—"Why would I end up like this?!"-he asked in visceral horror, casting an aggrieved, accusatory look toward Guan Ye.

Guan Ye was startled by Lu Shulong's glare:

—"I still believe it shouldn't be you... Wait a second, why are you staring at me? You don't actually believe I did this to you, do you? I would never..."

—"Ah, it's Fourth Senior Brother..."-Mo Xi gasped in a hushed squeak.

Indeed, following the arrival of the bone dragon, a figure stepped onto the battlefield right beside it—and that figure was none other than Guan Ye!

However, compared to Qu Qianxing who had merely gained an aura of intense malice, Guan Ye's transformation was utterly grotesque:

Like the bone dragon, his body was in a state of semi-putrefied zombification. Corrupted flesh peeled away to reveal stark bones beneath... no, his entire skeletal frame gleamed a sinister, blood-crimson hue. He looked entirely like a sovereign of ghosts, radiating an unholy, terrifying miasma.

In fact, trailing behind him was a vast, writhing tide of wandering souls and vengeful wraiths, surging into the battlefield like a black tidal wave, tearing into celestial soldiers and demonic fiends alike.

Facing such an entity, had that gaunt, scarred face not faintly retained Guan Ye's features, none of them would have dared recognize him.

Guan Ye himself refused to accept it:

—"Impossible... How could I ever..."

Arriving at this point, his voice hitched, his expression descending into chaotic turmoil.

Lu Shulong's pupils constricted violently, shifting from his disguised human roundness into the enormous, circular golden pupils of a True Dragon.

His vision shifted alongside his pupils, allowing him to perceive faint, wispy threads of black energy coiling around Guan Ye's skull.

This was a technique Guan Ye had mastered during the third cycle under Ghost Emperor Ruwang:

Soul Threads.

It was an esoteric method ghosts utilized to preserve their memories.

When a mortal died, their soul was akin to software stripped of its hardware, incapable of operating independently. It had to rely on carriers like Yin energy to barely sustain its final obsession—killing for vengeance, harvesting rancor and Yin mist, forging a ghostly womb to embark on the path of a ghost cultivator.

Throughout this process, lacking a proper 'storage drive,' the memories of the vast majority of ghosts became fragmented and corrupted, making it virtually impossible to retain their original selfhood, occasionally erasing their identity entirely until they became 'someone else.'

To combat this degeneration, a specialized art named 'Ghost Threads' had been engineered by figures led by Ghost Emperor Ruwang: condensing Yin energy into intangible filaments, infusing one's memories within them, and weaving them seamlessly into the soul. Whether passing through the cycle of reincarnation or getting shattered into dust, these threads would never dissipate; upon encountering predefined triggers, one's true memories would instantly resurface.

Back then, Zhou Hui had entrusted Guan Ye to Ghost Emperor Ruwang; Ruwang had mentored Guan Ye like his own son, naturally passing this technique down to him.

This was also the very foundation that had allowed Guan Ye to fully awaken his memories during the fourth cycle, embarking on a merciless slaughter of the martial siblings who had obstructed Master's ascension.

Lu Shulong had suffered under his hand, and felt his scales crawl the instant he saw those threads.

[Not good! Fourth Senior Brother is about to recover his memories!]

Lu Shulong thought in sheer panic.

Yet he did not sit waiting for doom. Having suffered once, he would never trip in the same ditch twice. Ever since the fifth cycle, Lu Shulong had meticulously researched 'Soul Threads,' engineering countermeasures against them. Never had he imagined the day would come when they would actually be put to use! Instantly whipping up a localized gale around Guan Ye, he sucked the drifting soul threads toward himself, swallowing them in a single gulp!

Instantly, alongside the 'future,' Lu Shulong saw the 'past.'

Yet this 'past' was not his own shameful history from the third cycle—it was the 'past' of the sixth cycle.

Indeed, that was the lifetime where Mo Bei turned traitor, Lu Shulong sacrificed himself prematurely, Ran Deng perished, Mo Xi and Qu Qianxing fell to demonic corruption—the very lifetime Zhou Hui assumed was the 'Heavenly Dao Script'!

[Strange... In this lifetime, Fourth Senior Brother should have been fine! Before I died, I struck a pact with Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother: do whatever the hell you want, but under no circumstances are you to drag Fourth Senior Brother into this mess!]

Lu Shulong thought in bewilderment.

He swiftly learned from the ingested memories that Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing had indeed upheld their vow. Even while driving Ran Deng to her death and exploiting Mo Bei and Mo Xi, they had never laid a finger on Guan Ye.

Yet Guan Ye had refused to spare himself.

Powerlessly watching the Heavenly Spirit Sect collapse, Master perish, and his Fifth Junior Brother incinerate himself, how could he possibly live out the remainder of his days in tranquil peace as an ordinary cultivator? Having recovered fragmented, incomplete memories, he had trudged agonizingly toward the Western Frontier, seeking Ghost Emperor Ruwang.

Yet the demon hordes had already invaded. Amidst harrowing tribulations, Guan Ye never reached Paramita Heaven; instead, he encountered a half-human, half-ghost mechanist. Apprenticing under him, Guan Ye mastered twisted, heretical refinement arts, refining his own flesh into a grotesque half-corpse puppet to exact vengeance upon the 'Protagonist.'

The culmination mirrored the 'Script.' Guan Ye's vengeance ended in catastrophic defeat; not only did he die a horrific death, but the revolutionary refinement methodologies he had synthesized across his lifetime were plundered by the 'Protagonist,' serving as bragging capital for his celestial ascension.

Merely witnessing this memory sequence made Lu Shulong feel sick to his stomach, wishing he could gouge his own brain out.

Yet this 'past' corroborated the 'future.'

The Guan Ye of the sixth cycle, who had refined himself into a monster solely to avenge the Heavenly Spirit Sect, was virtually identical to the half-human, half-corpse sovereign seen in the 'future'—lacking only that zombie bone dragon.

—"Shulong?"

When Lu Shulong snapped back to reality, he discovered the others staring at him with bewildered eyes. Even Mo Xi had halted the playback of Mo Bei's vision.

Only then did Lu Shulong realize his face was drenched in tears.

Wiping his face with his sleeve, Lu Shulong averted his eyes:

—"No, it's nothing..."

Like Ran Deng and Mo Bei before him, he was struck by an unprecedented, crushing wave of guilt—perhaps even deeper.

Lu Shulong had already felt indebted to Fourth Senior Brother over the events of the third cycle, and lived in mortal terror of the fourth cycle repeating itself. That was why he had specifically struck that bargain with Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother, forcing them to swear never to implicate Fourth Senior Brother. Yet seeing Guan Ye's memories of the sixth cycle, Lu Shulong felt it might have been better to let Fourth Senior Brother join Eldest Senior Brother... Wait, what were Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother doing in the sixth cycle?! Fourth Senior Brother had reduced himself to that state—why didn't they do anything?!

—"Old Second attempted to intervene, but is Old Fourth someone who ever listens? The moment he realized Little Seventh had fallen to demonic cultivation, he tried to butcher Little Seventh on the spot. It's terrifying to imagine what he would have done if he laid eyes on Old Second; the slaughter of the fourth cycle would have repeated itself. Naturally, we couldn't take that risk back then."

—"Though agreeing to your condition was a terrible deal; Old Fourth has immense utility value, after all!"

Lu Shulong's body stiffened, glaring fiercely toward Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan remained leaning against the doorframe with that half-mocking smirk, his face wearing an expression of omniscient detachment; those words had just been transmitted directly into Lu Shulong's mind via divine sense.

*Eldest Senior Brother recovered his memories too?! When?!*

Lu Shulong's mind spun in chaotic turbulence. But he couldn't care less whether Eldest Senior Brother lived or died, gritting his teeth to transmit via divine sense:

—"The oath remains binding! You are forbidden from dragging Fourth Senior Brother into this mess! You are forbidden from letting him recover his memories!"

Such agonizing memories—Lu Shulong could barely endure them himself, let alone Guan Ye.

Just as Lu Shulong was thinking this, Guan Ye's face suddenly leaned in close:

—"Shulong, what is genuinely wrong with you?"

—"No, truly, nothing,"-Lu Shulong denied once more.

Yet Lu Shulong's tear-streaked face was far too dramatic; judging by the suspicious looks from the others, such a deflection couldn't fool anyone.

—Particularly Senior Sister Ran Deng and Junior Sister Mo Bei, who wore deeply contemplative expressions for some inexplicable reason.

—"I was simply terrified,"-Lu Shulong dared not meet Guan Ye's eyes, staring past him as he spoke,-"The thought of Fourth Senior Brother turning into such a terrifying monster three months from now, even refining me into a zombie bone dragon... it scared me half to death."

—"Who on earth would do that?!"

—"However, this 'future' is destined to happen, isn't it?"-Lu Shulong's tone turned deadly serious.

—"It certainly won't..."-Guan Ye attempted to refute.

Yet he paused, glancing toward Mo Bei, his brow knitting in profound anguish.

Guan Ye recalled Mo Bei repeatedly verifying the authenticity of this 'future' with Master. How had Master replied back then? He seemed to have affirmed that it was an 'inevitable future,' hadn't he?

He could treat anyone else's words as hot air, but Master's decree was something Guan Ye could never ignore.

Lu Shulong was actively corroborating this "future":

—"I don't want to believe it either, but..." *Given Fourth Senior Brother's past, it's impossible not to suspect this 'future' is inextricably linked to that 'past'!*

Naturally, Lu Shulong could not voice this testimony aloud.

Yet Lu Shulong possessed other evidence:

—"However... seeing myself reduced to this half-dragon, half-human state, it's impossible not to worry!"

—"Speaking of which, Shulong, how did you turn into this?"-Ran Deng asked in genuine curiosity.

—"Overdraft of power,"-Lu Shulong sighed,-"Although Second Senior Brother impersonating the Demon Sovereign went 'smoothly,' occasions inevitably arose where displaying overwhelming force was required. Compared to Second Senior Brother, a True Dragon's aura is less prone to immediate exposure. I pushed my power somewhat beyond the limit; as a result, whether due to demonic miasma or other factors, overdrawing my reserves left me like this. Furthermore, I can no longer suppress my True Dragon aura. If another disaster strikes midway, getting refined into an undead dragon in real time is entirely within the realm of possibility."

—"I said I would never! How could I ever refine my own junior brother?!"-Guan Ye snapped in frustration.

—"However, you don't even spare yourself!"-Lu Shulong looked sorrowfully upon Guan Ye,-"If an irrecoverable catastrophe truly strikes in the future—if the Heavenly Spirit Sect is wiped out, and I die—would you genuinely refuse to turn yourself into that monster?"

Guan Ye opened his mouth. Even without his memories, he found himself unable to deny that he would take that exact path.

Consequently, Guan Ye flared with embarrassed anger:

—"Stop spouting nonsense about things that will never happen!"

Saying this, Guan Ye turned his gaze back to Mo Bei:

—"This 'future' lacks head and tail! Even if it is destined to occur, there must be cause and effect!"

—"I don't know; I didn't see the 'causes.' I only know this was the 'future' Master perceived while disassembling the astral disc,"-Mo Bei lowered her eyelashes,-"At the time, I was wondering: as long as Master is here, how could we ever walk toward such a 'future'?"

—"Right! Where was Master? Was Master absent from that 'future'?!"-Lu Shulong asked anxiously.

Mo Bei shook her head:

—"There was no Master in that 'future.' It felt as though Master was nowhere to be found..."

Hearing this, every disciple present who possessed prior memories shuddered violently.

A world without Master—they had already endured it once, and refused to experience it a second time at all costs.

Even Guan Ye and Mo Xi, who lacked prior memories, wore profoundly unsettled expressions.

Mo Xi instinctively tried to deny it:

—"How could Master possibly be absent..."

Ran Deng suddenly spoke up:

—"Speaking of which, Ghost Emperor Ruwang came here. I heard Master mention he arrived because he foresaw an omen of 'great calamity'... Furthermore, Ancestor Jiuzhai lingered in our sect for so long, yet with the Immortal Arts Assembly on the verge of commencing, he abruptly fled back to his mountain. Isn't that deeply suspicious?"

Such was human nature: once dread took root, thoughts spiraled down the worst possible corridors, becoming darker and darker.

Ran Deng was currently in that exact spiral.

She speculated:

—"Could something catastrophic have transpired, causing Master to no longer be here?"

—"How could that be?!"-Guan Ye remained stubborn.

—"...Regardless, we must take precautions,"-Mo Bei spoke at this juncture.

Her perpetually blank face displayed uncharacteristic gravity. Gazing upon the others with her heterochromatic eyes, she stated with absolute seriousness:

—"I did not project this memory to terrify my senior brothers and sisters, but rather in hopes that everyone prepares mentally."

*What mental preparation?*

*Mental preparation for Master being gone again?*

Both Ran Deng and Lu Shulong thought in horror, repelling that horrific possibility from the depths of their souls.

—"Actually, I don't believe this 'future' will come to pass,"-Mo Bei continued, uncharacteristically comforting everyone,-"Because if I could perceive this 'future,' Master must have seen it as well. He will unquestionably implement corresponding countermeasures."

—"Indeed! If it is Master, there will never be a problem!"

—"Yes, absolutely!"

Several voices chimed in agreement immediately.

Yet nobody genuinely relaxed. Especially the disciples who possessed prior memories; they all knew that when it came to defying destiny, even Master was not entirely omnipotent, and could be dragged down by their burdens.

—"Wanting to alter the future, only to become the very instrument that causes it—isn't that a classic trope?"-Yuwen Xuan piped up nonchalantly,-"Say, could Master be desperately striving to twist destiny, thereby incurring the wrath of Heavenly Dao, leading to his demise, whereupon Old Second, Old Fourth, and Old Fifth..."

—"SHUT YOUR MOUTH!"

Everyone roared at Yuwen Xuan in unison.

Yuwen Xuan let out a pathetic squeak, and beneath Mo Xi's thunderstruck gaze, transformed into a little black lamb, scurrying outside the door in huffy indignation.

—"Eldest Senior Brother... he..."-Mo Xi found reality impossible to accept.

The little black lamb he had intensely envied for weeks—the one pampered in Master's embrace—was Eldest Senior Brother all along!

This was completely foul play!

Had he known, he would have directly learned shapeshifting arts!

*Could one simply transform into a small critter to sleep in Master's arms?!*

Mo Xi thought in bitter lamentation.

Ran Deng was oblivious to her junior brother's 'Master-obsessed brain,' snapping:

—"Ignore him!"-turning to her little junior sister, she encouraged,-"Continue."

—"In short, I firmly believe Master can save everyone!"-Mo Bei declared resolutely,-"Therefore, we must not act rashly. We cannot interfere with Master's maneuvers, and above all, we cannot distract Master with trivial burdens—especially those who have already stepped onto the 'trajectory of the future'..."

As Mo Bei spoke, her gaze landed squarely upon the Heavenly Robe.

—"You intend to abandon Second Senior Brother?"-Ran Deng gasped in shock.

—"I am not abandoning him. But reporting to Master and disrupting Master's judgment is out of the question, isn't it? Isn't allowing Fifth Senior Brother to proceed according to plan far better?"-Mo Bei glared coldly at Lu Shulong,-"If he can be rescued, rescue him!"

—"I feel like you want to abandon me right alongside him!"-Lu Shulong shivered.

—"It isn't abandonment,"-Mo Bei's logic was not only self-contradictory, but remarkably cryptic,-"Master will unquestionably rewrite the future. Pawns must not move recklessly; on the surface, they can follow the current of destiny, ready to respond to the chess master at the critical moment. In fact, Master established a unique condition for the Immortal Arts Assembly, which I believe is a hint left for us."

—"For example... perhaps we can claim the power of the 'future' in advance!"-Mo Bei declared.

—"The power of the future?"

—"Indeed. Although our circumstances in that future are horrific, we are unimaginably powerful—powerful enough to wage war against the Celestial Realm! That much is undeniably true, isn't it?"-Mo Bei stated.

—"Second Senior Brother and Ye-zi are indeed terrifyingly strong... Wait, does this involve us too?"-Ran Deng asked.

—"Because Fifth Senior Brother was bawling his eyes out just now, I hadn't finished the projection,"-Mo Xi explained.

He proceeded to broadcast the latter half of the memory.

The remaining footage depicted the 'futures' of Ran Deng, Mo Bei, and Mo Xi, alongside the 'future' of the Celestial Realm.

Ran Deng, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong fell into stunned silence after witnessing the conclusion.

Regarding this 'future'...

—"It isn't necessarily a bad ending,"-Mo Bei remarked,-"If Master truly ceases to be."

She proceeded to detail her counter-plan meticulously.

—"It truly possesses feasibility,"-Ran Deng conceded after hearing it out.

Lu Shulong nodded in agreement:

—"The worst case is merely that 'future'; I find it acceptable."

—"Wait, are you all accepting that as the 'future' by default?!"-Guan Ye still struggled to resist.

—"Then Fourth Senior Brother, can you genuinely guarantee none of it will happen?"-Mo Xi countered.

Truthfully, Mo Xi shared Guan Ye's skepticism; lacking memories of the prior cycles, he harbored immense doubt toward this sudden 'bad ending' future.

Yet Mo Xi decided to endorse Mo Bei's plan.

Not out of twin telepathy, but purely because Mo Bei so rarely utilized her brain to strategize. Even if he doubted whether any of it would transpire, going along with it wasn't a bad idea.

Naturally, it wasn't because leaving Second Senior Brother stranded on the demon battlefield sounded hilarious.

He certainly wasn't eagerly anticipating the chaos to come!

Plotting inwardly, Mo Xi nonetheless persuaded Guan Ye:

—"That is a horrific future calculated by a True Immortal's array disc, destined to happen. Even if you don't believe it or understand how it comes to pass, we must still prepare for it, mustn't we?"

—"Furthermore, I am somewhat worried about Master,"-arriving at this point, Mo Xi sighed with heartfelt sincerity,-"He witnessed this future, yet refused to breathe a single word to us—not even a warning. That indicates he doesn't believe we can be of any help, intending to shoulder everything entirely on his own! If only we were stronger..."

Guan Ye snapped his head up, staring intently at Mo Xi.

Mo Xi met his gaze with absolute earnestness.

—"To overdraft the 'power of the future' will require specialized methods,"-Guan Ye stated,-"Although Master instructed me that certain techniques could be utilized during the competition, I will require your technical support."

Saying this, he was evidently swayed.

The other disciples exhaled in relief, launching into a flurry of animated discussion.

***

Outside the door, the forgotten little black lamb poked its head in, eavesdropping.

—"Baa-hah, they actually took that seriously,"-the little black lamb chuckled amusedly.

It obviously understood the complete truth.

Yet it had zero intention of correcting them.

—"However, the direction is entirely correct,"-Yuwen Xuan laughed,-"After all, that is the exact 'future' the Celestial Realm fears most—the very 'future' they erased our Heavenly Spirit Sect seven or eight times to avert."

It was precisely because every single disciple here was an existential threat to the Heavenly Court, capable of bringing ruin to the celestial host upon the battlefield, that they had been exiled to this realm and bound by scripted destinies.

Yet that was also a future without Master—a 'future' the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect would never choose.

The instant Master took them under his wing, that 'future' ceased to exist.

Yuwen Xuan had never anticipated that their Master would idly simulate this timeline, only for Little Junior Sister to resonate with it and mistake it for the authentic future, running back in sheer panic to engineer a counterstrike.

—"This plan is quite entertaining."

—"Though everyone abandoned that 'future,' scaring the Celestial Realm and letting them watch their worst nightmare come to life isn't a bad idea."

—"Especially that *other* 'Old Second'—I wonder what his reaction will be?"

The little black lamb bared its fangs, breaking into a thoroughly wicked grin.
