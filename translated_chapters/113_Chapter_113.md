# Chapter 113: [Demonstration Effect](<# "Literal CN: 示范效果 (shìfàn xiàoguǒ) — Demonstration Effect / Exemplary Effect; chapter title referring to Lu Shulong's textbook demonstration match at the tournament inspiring rival disciples to study academic papers">) (第113章 示范效果)

—"Did you do something bad?"-Guan Ye asked suspiciously.

Lu Shulong offered a feeble smile:

—"How could that be? I'm not Eldest Senior Brother,"-Lu Shulong said.

—"Be that as it may,"-Guan Ye said, fiddling with the object in his hands.-"I also assumed all along that this set of magical artifacts was forged for Eldest Senior Brother. I never imagined it would end up being used on you."

Hearing this, Lu Shulong's smile grew even feebler.

He cast a melancholy gaze toward the 'magical artifact.'

It was a set of [shackles](<# "Literal CN: 镣铐 (liàokào) — shackles / manacles; forged by Guan Ye to suppress a cultivator's internal spiritual energy circulation">).

Forged from black steel, it consisted of several components—handcuffs, leg irons, and slender decorative chains linking them together. Clearly designed not to impede ordinary movement, they looked almost like ornamental jewelry at first glance. Yet once worn, the wearer would immediately discover that they severely sealed and suppressed the circulation of internal spiritual energy. Most insidious of all was the [muzzle](<# "Literal CN: 嘴铐 (zuǐkào) — muzzle / mouth restraint; intricately etched with Taotie motifs to prevent speech or biting">), intricately etched with Taotie motifs, which prevented the wearer from opening their mouth once fastened.

Lu Shulong stared at that muzzle for a long time with an expression that defied description before finally failing to hold back:

—"Just what kind of grudge do you have against Eldest Senior Brother?"-Lu Shulong asked.

—"Ahem,"-Guan Ye coughed lightly, putting the muzzle away.-"You won't be needing this one... You can make do with the rest of the set for now. I'll design a set that suits you better later on."

[......That is completely unnecessary, alright?!]

Lu Shulong thought inwardly, yet he couldn't bring himself to refuse.

Because this was his 'exam content.'

Ever since the secret of his seven reincarnations and fully retained memories had been exposed, Master had, quite naturally, administered another [baseline diagnostic exam](<# "Literal CN: 摸底考试 (mōdǐ kǎoshì) — diagnostic baseline assessment exam / placement test used to gauge a student's true level">). To be fair, Lu Shulong hadn't spent all seven lifetimes merely slacking off; he had genuinely applied himself to the subjects that interested him. But the greatest difference in this lifetime was that the exam syllabus was entirely different!

The questions were technically still within Lu Shulong's scope of study, but they were monstrously difficult and fiendishly tricky—evidently tailored specifically for a Lu Shulong who had 'studied six times before.'

Lu Shulong had barely managed to scrape a [passing grade](<# "Literal CN: 及格 (jígé) — passing grade / 60-point cutoff on exams">).

After grading the paper, Master hadn't said a harsh word. He had merely looked at Lu Shulong and let out a long, heavy sigh.

Evidently, in Master's eyes, all that trivial nonsense about seven cycles of reincarnation couldn't hold a candle to the profound disappointment of a disciple who, having studied the curriculum six times over, was still hovering on the verge of failing.

Under Master's disappointed gaze, Lu Shulong felt his heart shatter into pieces.

There was simply no helping it. Unlike Eldest Senior Brother, even if Lu Shulong occasionally had his slips, he was usually a well-behaved little dragon capable of masquerading as an honor student. He had no desire whatsoever to be lumped together with Eldest Senior Brother into Master's category of [organic waste](<# "Literal CN: 有机垃圾 (yǒujī lājī) — organic waste / bio-trash; Zhou Hui's deadpan classification for irredeemable slacker students">)!

Fortunately, the written test wasn't the entirety of the assessment—there was still the Immortal Arts Assembly!

Master hadn't devised a separate exam for that; instead, he treated the Immortal Arts Assembly as a practical assessment, establishing various project parameters for evaluation.

Lu Shulong felt this was his final opportunity for redemption.

If he met the requirements at the Immortal Arts Assembly, he could at least offer Master the plausible excuse: "In my past lifetimes, I was determined to defy the heavens and change my fate, so my focus was entirely devoted to martial prowess and combat strength (a blatant lie)."

At that thought, Lu Shulong drew a deep breath:

—"Fasten these onto me!"-Lu Shulong said.

—"Are you certain?"-Guan Ye frowned, eyeing Lu Shulong.-"Fighting while wearing these means you won't be able to utilize your full spiritual power at all. It's practically equivalent to a punishment... What exactly did you do? Should I go plead with Master on your behalf?"

—"...I already told you, I didn't do anything,"-Lu Shulong stubbornly insisted.

After all, across several reincarnations, right after hatching from his shell, he had intentionally or unintentionally caused considerable trouble for this Fourth Senior Brother. If Guan Ye discovered that he had been feigning amnesia all along, his reaction would likely be even more explosive than Master's.

Lu Shulong had already pleaded with Master to keep Fourth Senior Brother in the dark about this matter as much as possible.

Now, confronted with Guan Ye's skeptical gaze, Lu Shulong forced an explanation:

—"Besides, Fourth Senior Brother, don't you think this set of shackles was intended for me by Master from the very beginning?"-Lu Shulong said.

—"Huh?"-Guan Ye raised an eyebrow.

When he had initially received Master's request, he had naturally assumed Eldest Senior Brother had committed some atrocity that incited public outrage and provoked Master's ire. It had never crossed his mind that the shackles were meant to bind Lu Shulong.

Xiao Long had always been well-behaved; why would anyone need to shackle him?

—"Because as a True Dragon, I cannot unleash my full power in this world to begin with! That's why Master has constantly been training me in combat styles that restrain spiritual energy,"-Lu Shulong argued, sounding entirely reasonable and grounded in logic.-"The Immortal Arts Assembly is the perfect venue for this. Every single arena has been designed according to the academic papers published by senior powerhouses. If executed correctly, one can win matches relying purely on technique without exerting one's own power at all."

Having spoken to this point, Lu Shulong had practically convinced himself.

He exhaled softly and said with deep earnestness:

—"Youngest Junior Sister chose for herself the trial of 'defeating everyone in the same realm using pure swordsmanship.' As her senior brother, it is only proper that I defeat everyone relying purely on technique, isn't it?"-Lu Shulong said.

Moreover, if he botched this exam, he genuinely wouldn't have the face to see Master ever again!

Lu Shulong's genuine, heartfelt passion successfully convinced Guan Ye as well.

—"So Master harbored such profound expectations for you? Had I known earlier, I would have consulted Master for the detailed specifications,"-Guan Ye replied, his expression turning solemn. He patted his junior brother on the shoulder.-"Endure this set of artifacts for today! I'll pull an all-nighter to custom-forge you a brand-new set of shackles, guaranteed to bind every shred of your power so not a single drop of spiritual energy leaks out."

[That really isn't necessary.]

Lu Shulong thought with a bitter smile.

He watched as his Fourth Senior Brother, with an expression of sheer delight, held up the Taotie muzzle and measured it against his face:

—"Though in your case, this doesn't quite suit you. Perhaps crafting a dragon-patterned half-mask would be more appropriate?"-Guan Ye mused.

Lu Shulong's bitter smile deepened.

For the very first time, he realized that his Fourth Senior Brother seemed to harbor an unnerving passion for crafting torture instruments!

*

And so, bound in shackles, Lu Shulong stepped onto the tournament grounds of the Immortal Arts Assembly.

The moment he appeared, he was met with a deafening roar of cheers.

Lu Shulong was simply too handsome. Even without knowledge of his identity as a True Dragon, his sheer facial aesthetics alone were enough to make him the focal point of the entire crowd.

Lu Shulong had long grown accustomed to such treatment.

This stemmed primarily from his experiences in his third lifetime—the one where he had been 'abandoned' by Master. Raised as a True Dragon among the Sea Clan, every gesture and movement he made had been subjected to obsessive scrutiny.

Lu Shulong smiled toward the spectator stands. Once the gesture elicited an even more frenzied wave of shrieks, he finally turned to regard his opponent.

—"My, it's Thousand Beasts Hall?"-Lu Shulong remarked in surprise.

—"Indeed it is!"-Bai Tuo of Thousand Beasts Hall sneered with a chilling smile.-"As neighboring sects, please show mercy!"

Even as he spoke, his hand covertly brushed against his storage bag.

Inside rested a magical artifact forged specifically to deal with dragons.

Back when Thousand Beasts Hall had been debating how to secure victory in the Immortal Arts Assembly, they had learned of Heavenly Spirit Sect's tournament roster through the newspapers. Upon seeing Lu Shulong's name, Yan Bailang had conceived other ambitions.

—"Isn't that the dragon? They actually sent it out to compete?"-Yan Bailang's eyes had gleamed with greed.

Xia Bing had despised that look in Yan Bailang's eyes, attempting to shut down his delusions:

—"However, that is the Heavenly Spirit Sect's dragon..."-Xia Bing had pointed out.

—"Naturally, naturally. We couldn't possibly strike at a dragon 'in broad daylight,'"-Yan Bailang had replied.

He and Bai Jun exchanged a knowing glance:

—"However, if such an invaluable dragon happens to suffer a slight injury during the tournament, leaving behind an imprint that even the Immortal Venerable fails to notice... and much, much later—perhaps after the Immortal Venerable ascends—it becomes Thousand Beasts Hall's guardian divine beast due to that subtle karmic thread, wouldn't that be perfectly reasonable?"-Yan Bailang had proposed.

[......I think you lot are courting death.]

Xia Bing had thought.

Yet she knew full well that whatever she said would fall on deaf ears.

She had resolved to find an opportunity to warn Yu Feifei of the Heavenly Spirit Sect so they could be on guard.

Unfortunately, Yu Feifei was swamped with the newspaper, busy to the point of sprouting wings. Furthermore, their business partnership regarding the milk supply for Yibei Tea was already running like clockwork, requiring no frequent meetings. As a result, even when the regional rounds of the tournament commenced, Xia Bing hadn't managed to meet Yu Feifei. Reluctant to entrust such sensitive news to ordinary Tianling disciples, the warning had never been delivered.

Xia Bing had hoped to find a way once she arrived at the tournament venue.

Once the tournament reached the regional stage, it no longer operated under the chaotic open-challenge ring format of the individual immortal cities. Instead, disciples from all sects were shuffled for one-on-one matches. Who could have foreseen that their luck would be so 'splendid'—Thousand Beasts Hall's very first match pitted them directly against Heavenly Spirit Sect, with Bai Tuo facing Lu Shulong.

In Xia Bing's eyes, this was the worst possible luck.

Bai Tuo was of Elder Bai Jun's direct bloodline; grandfather and grandson were cut from the exact same cloth of petty spite and insidious malice. Bai Jun would never allow his descendant to pass up such a golden opportunity to hunt a dragon!

Just as Xia Bing had anticipated, prior to entering the ring, Bai Tuo had already learned of Lu Shulong's true identity from his elder ancestor and received an artifact purportedly capable of countering dragons.

Staring at Lu Shulong, Bai Tuo could no longer conceal the covetous hunger burning in his eyes:

[—"This dragon is mine!"](<# "Speaker: Bai Tuo">)

*

[—"A dragon! That's a True Dragon!"](<# "Speaker: Demons of the Sacred Tree">)

At that very moment, atop the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree in the Southern Domain, numerous demons watching the phantom projection of this duel—their Saintess called it a '[live stream](<# "Literal CN: 直播 (zhíbō) — live broadcast / streaming; modern telecommunications livestreaming over the Wood Spirit tree network">)'—gasped in awe.

This was especially true for demons like Chi Xue.

Their species was considered distant, distant, distant... distant kin to the dragon race, and they took immense pride in their draconic bloodline.

Seeing a True Dragon locked in battle with a human now, despite having zero understanding of what the Immortal Arts Assembly actually was, they unconditionally took the True Dragon's side. Some were even itching to leap onto the stage to fight in his stead:

[—"What is wrong with this human? How dare he provoke the dragon race?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Lord True Dragon, bite them to death!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Eat them!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

Chi Xue and its kin hollered with fiery passion.

Had this not been the Wood Spirits' very first attempt at 'livestreaming,' rushed into service due to the tournament's impending start with many features still incomplete, Chi Xue and the others would certainly have found other ways to show their support for the True Dragon.

Naturally, those features would be added down the line.

Yet as this was their first time watching a livestream, Chi Xue and its kin had no leisure to explore other functions. Their full attention was captivated by Lu Shulong, and they gradually sensed that something was amiss:

[—"Wait a moment, why isn't Lord True Dragon using draconic power?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"For an insect-like human, a single dragon roar ought to scare him to death!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Could it be that Lord True Dragon is too young and doesn't know how to use his power yet?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

Listening to this, Chi Xue felt the blood throughout its entire body boil:

—"Vile humans! Where did they kidnap Lord True Dragon from? He's even bound in shackles! As expected, we must declare war on humanity and reclaim Lord True Dragon—!"-Chi Xue snarled.

*Smack!*

The eldest kin in the tree hollow whipped its tail across Chi Xue's face, delivering a stinging slap.

—"What drivel are you barking? Look closer!"-the elder kin scolded sharply.-"Does Lord True Dragon look anywhere close to losing? It's you pack of idiots who don't know how to fight!"

Rebuked like this, Chi Xue and the other demons finally calmed their minds and observed with care.

Sure enough, just as their elder had pointed out, while Lord True Dragon appeared to be locked in a stalemate with the opponent, his dodges were exceptionally nimble. Not once had he allowed the other party to land a blow. Conversely, that human was hopping up and down so frantically that it reminded Chi Xue of the chirping sparrows dancing atop its head earlier.

If he truly wanted to eat the human, it would take no more than a single bite.

The other kin noticed this as well, and the heated tension in the tree hollow swiftly gave way to easy laughter:

[—"Haha, so he's merely playing! Lord True Dragon is quite mischievous."](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Well, of course. That human's meat looks so tough and stringy, and his face is unsightly. He doesn't look tasty at all."](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"As expected of Lord True Dragon, even without using his power he can... The Dragon-Locking Cauldron?!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

Yet that relaxed atmosphere didn't last long before the scene on the arena provoked terrified shrieks from Chi Xue and its kin.

Because that human cultivator had summoned a small bronze cauldron!

Catching sight of that seemingly unassuming cauldron, the color drained from their faces:

[—"Is this cultivator a beast tamer?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"To actually use a Dragon-Locking Cauldron against Lord True Dragon!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

As demons, Chi Xue and its kind had suffered bitter grievances at the hands of beast cultivators. After all, human beast cultivators loved nothing more than capturing formidable demons to tame as their spiritual pets. And the [Chi clan](<# "Literal CN: 螭族 (chī zú) — Chi clan / Hornless Dragon clan; a demon species distantly related to dragons with formidable combat prowess">), possessing extraordinarily ferocious combat power, was a prime target of harassment.

Among the various underhanded techniques employed by beast cultivators, the Chi clan harbored an exceptionally vivid trauma regarding the magical treasure known as the [Dragon-Locking Cauldron](<# "Literal CN: 锁龙鼎 (suǒ lóng dǐng) — Dragon-Locking Cauldron; an ancient bronze artifact forged from methods passed down by True Immortals specifically to restrain, siphon, and poison dragon power">).

As the name implied, the Dragon-Locking Cauldron had been forged specifically to target the dragon race and their kin. Legend held that its refinement method had been imparted by ancient True Immortals. A complete Dragon-Locking Cauldron could not only bind a True Dragon's power, but far more venomously, it contained a toxic draught concocted specifically against draconic biology. Once it siphoned draconic power, it would corrupt the energy and infiltrate the Dragon Core. Thus, even if a dragon managed to slip the cauldron's bindings, it would be left with a lingering poison that doomed its future cultivation.

Countless members of the Chi clan had fallen to this very cauldron.

[—"We haven't heard of this cauldron for centuries. How has it surfaced again?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Lord True Dragon, watch out!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

Chi Xue and its kin cried out in dread, the more timid among them even shutting their eyes in horror.

Yet when they looked again, they discovered that Lord True Dragon was completely unaffected by the Dragon-Locking Cauldron. Instead, he was staring at the bronze cauldron with an expression of sheer bewilderment.

[—"No effect? Is he not a True Dragon?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Are you blind?! How is he anything other than a dragon?!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"! I get it! Of course Lord True Dragon isn't affected—he hasn't used a single shred of draconic power!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"So that's how it is! The Dragon-Locking Cauldron requires draconic spiritual energy as bait!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

The Chi demons babbled in disjointed revelation.

*

As if confirming their speculations, Bai Tuo's expression contorted into a venomous snarl:

—"Why aren't you using any spells?!"-Bai Tuo roared.

—"On this map, your attacks haven't reached the threshold where I need to resort to spells,"-Lu Shulong replied with a gentle smile.-"Are you finished with your techniques? Then it's my turn—"

Instinctively, Bai Tuo dropped into a defensive stance.

Yet Lu Shulong chuckled softly:

—"Defending now is already far too late, you know?"-Lu Shulong said.

With a flick of his wrist, he snapped open the folding fan in his grip and raised it with effortless elegance.

Following his motion, Bai Tuo suddenly realized that a faint teal mist had silently enveloped the surroundings without his noticing. His own limbs turned to jelly, as though the one dropped into the Dragon-Locking Cauldron was himself, every ounce of strength draining from his body.

Bai Tuo's eyes widened in horror:

—"H-How can this be?!"-Bai Tuo stammered.

—"Merely a minor trick,"-Lu Shulong explained cordially.-"Had you read today's academic journal, you would know that this arena features a standard enclosed structure, perfectly suited for [aerosolized agents](<# "Literal CN: 雾状的药剂 (wùzhuàng de yàojì) — mist/aerosolized pharmaceutical agents; applying chemical vapor dispersal to combat">)—hello? Are you listening?"

Naturally, Bai Tuo could no longer hear him; he had already collapsed unconscious onto the ground.

In response, a deafening cheer erupted from the spectator stands, followed by the announcer's impassioned proclamation:

> 【First match of the Immortal Arts Assembly: Lu Shulong of Heavenly Spirit Sect is victorious!】

*

Accompanying Lu Shulong's victory, the demons upon the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree let loose a deafening roar of cheers no less thunderous than that of the live arena.

Yet amidst their celebrations, the Chi demons harbored a pressing question:

[—"How exactly did Lord True Dragon win?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"He's a True Dragon! Isn't being powerful completely natural?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Yes, but Lord True Dragon didn't use a shred of draconic power from start to finish! In other words, if we followed his combat method, even if we ran into a Dragon-Locking Cauldron, we might actually have a shot at escaping or even winning!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"With your capabilities?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

As the Chi demons argued back and forth, words escalated into fists. Chi Xue had long abandoned maintaining a human guise, reverting to its original form and tangling into a writhing heap with its kin.

In the end, it was the voice of the Demon Saintess that resolved their debate:

> 【This concludes the trial broadcast of the first match.】
> 
> 【Follow the [Sacred Tree Channel](<# "Literal CN: 神树频道 (shénshù píndào) — Sacred Tree Channel; the official Wood Spirit television/livestream broadcast network">) for more live streams.】
> 
> 【Additionally, we intend to introduce the cultivation manual tutorials of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the sect of His Excellency True Dragon Lu Shulong. Slots for the initial pilot batch are strictly limited. Any clan with interest may register at [Azure Phoenix Hall](<# "Literal CN: 青凰殿 (Qīnghuáng Diàn) — Azure Phoenix Hall; the royal palace/seat of the Azure Phoenix Clan atop the Sacred Tree">)—】

[—"Holy crap! What did she just say?!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Lord True Dragon's sect tutorials?"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Does that mean we can also learn that power to win without relying on spiritual energy?!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Forget that! Why are you lot still huddled here like idiots?! Didn't you hear what the Saintess said? Pilot slots are limited! If we don't hurry, the other clans will snatch them all!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

[—"Like hell they will!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

A collective epiphany dawned upon the Chi clan. Panic setting in, they scrambled to crawl out of the tree hollow. However, having tangled themselves so thoroughly during their earlier squabble, they couldn't untangle in time. In their frantic rush to squeeze through the opening, they formed a massive, knotted ball, wedging themselves completely stuck at the mouth of the hollow.

Seeing this, the eldest Chi wriggled with all its might, only to find the knot tightening further. It let out a voice of utter despair:

—"Honestly speaking, with your cranial capacity, even if you secure a spot, could you lot actually learn it?"-the elder sighed.

[—"Shut up, old man! Aren't you one of us?!"](<# "Speaker: Chi Clan Members">)

*

While the Chi demons debated matters of intellect, the human cultivators had already arrived at a consensus:

Whether one could master the material or not, studying was an attitude in and of itself.

If all else failed, one could always [copy someone's homework](<# "Literal CN: 抄作业 (chāo zuòyè) — copying homework; slang for replicating someone else's winning strategy or formula">)!

At that moment, in the spectator stands of the Immortal Arts Assembly, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect watched their Senior Brother Lu's triumph. Yet rather than joining the raucous celebrations of the surrounding crowd, they were earnestly verifying data with one another:

—"Did you record it?"-one disciple asked.

—"All captured from the northeast angle,"-replied another, clutching three or four jade slips.-"We've got both wide-angle panoramas and close-ups. We didn't miss a single one of Senior Brother Lu's movements."

—"Excellent!"-The first disciple gave him a firm thumbs-up.

—"Still, Senior Brother Lu truly lives up to his status as a direct disciple. His utilization of the arena today was simply as gorgeous as a [textbook demonstration](<# "Literal CN: 教科书级别的 (jiàokēshū jíbíé de) — textbook-level / exemplary standard-setting demonstration">),"-another Tianling disciple praised.-"Had we not recorded the entire match, I wouldn't even have been able to tell when Senior Brother Lu dispersed the [pill poison agent](<# "Literal CN: 丹毒药剂 (dāndú yàojì) — pill poison agent; pharmaceutical toxin distilled from alchemical impurities used as combat chemical weaponry">)."

[—"Isn't the most formidable part the way he diffused the poison? It was completely silent and imperceptible."](<# "Speaker: Tianling Disciple">)

—"Indeed. Although the academic journal detailed the method for [aerosolization](<# "Literal CN: 雾化 (wùhuà) — aerosolization / atomization; converting liquid or solid chemical compounds into fine airborne vapor">), even after consulting the Instructors and experimenting a few times myself, I still wasn't confident I could pull it off in a real match,"-another disciple said.

[—"No matter, we can just 'learn' directly from Senior Brother Lu!"](<# "Speaker: Tianling Disciple">)

[—"Exactly! Watching the recorded phantoms, Senior Brother Lu's movements were actually quite measured—every step was practically a live demonstration. If we dissect it a few more times, should we encounter the same arena map against an opponent of comparable cultivation, we'll have a seventy-percent chance of passing."](<# "Speaker: Tianling Disciple">)

[—"And passing the preliminaries earns us tournament points, at the very least!"](<# "Speaker: Tianling Disciple">)

*

Thus the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect deliberated.

Though they deliberately lowered their voices, cultivators from other sects blessed with sharper divine senses still picked up on the commotion among them.

—"Did you hear that?"-a disciple from another sect asked his companions.

—"I heard!"-replied his fellow disciple in deep chagrin.-"Damn it, why didn't we think of recording the match?!"

[—"Who could have guessed that this match of Lu Shulong's would be so classic? It was practically on the level of a masterclass demonstration."](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

[—"As expected of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's fifth disciple."](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

[—"He's terribly good-looking, too."](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

As those disciples chatted, their discussion promptly devolved into [starry-eyed swooning](<# "Literal CN: 花痴 (huāchī) — starry-eyed fanboys/fangirls / infatuated idolization">).

The disciple who had spoken first couldn't bear to watch any longer, barking in exasperation:

—"Hey! Did you lot get poisoned by the pill toxin too?! Aside from Lu Shulong's looks, don't you have any other thoughts?!"-he yelled.

[—"Tee-hee, Senior Brother, aren't you admitting that Lu Shulong is gorgeous yourself?"](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

—"I told you we're not discussing that!"-the disciple bellowed.-"What I'm saying is, are you blind to how hard the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples are working?! They're already so far ahead, yet they're still dissecting every single match! If this goes on, how are we ever supposed to beat them?!"

Chastised by this roar, the surrounding disciples fell silent.

Only after a good while did one disciple stammer sheepishly:

—"B-But we didn't record the footage of that match just now,"-the disciple said.

—"It's too late for this round, but we can make up for it next time,"-another disciple offered in self-reflection.

Though judging by his sparkling eyes, it was difficult to tell whether his enthusiasm was directed toward the tournament matches or toward Lu Shulong himself.

Fortunately, there were those who genuinely wished to learn:

[—"Lu Shulong mentioned that his battle was tied to a paper published in the academic journal? Perhaps we should study it more thoroughly?"](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

—"I've already studied it, and I even read the analysis in the newspaper,"-another disciple sighed.-"Yet there are still parts I don't understand."

[—"Are you dense? Their Heavenly Spirit Sect has Instructors, but don't we? We have our Master!"](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

[—"Right!"](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciples">)

The eyes of those disciples lit up. Instantly, they lost all desire to watch the subsequent matches—primarily because after witnessing Lu Shulong's performance, they had completely lost interest in the bouts of ordinary cultivators—and hurried away from the arena.

Their Master had also attended the Immortal Arts Assembly. However, as an elder, his concern lay strictly with matches at his own cultivation level, having naturally skipped the junior bouts. Seeing his disciples return so prematurely, he asked with a genial smile:

—"Why back so early? If I recall correctly, today's matches haven't concluded yet, have they?"-their Master asked.

In response, he watched in utter bewilderment as his disciples pulled out academic journals, newspapers, and their sect's core manuals:

[—"Master, we have several questions we'd like to ask."](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

[—"Regarding the paper in this journal issue on the combat applications of aerosolizing pill poisons, what spells from our sect's manuals can achieve the desired effect?"](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

[—"Regarding the newspaper's analysis..."](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciple">)

[—"Master..."](<# "Speaker: Rival Sect Disciples">)

Master: *???*
