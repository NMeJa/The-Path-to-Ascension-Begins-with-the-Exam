# Chapter 21: [Auto Chess](# "Literal CN: 自走棋 (zìzǒuqí) — auto-battler / auto chess genre where players assemble lineups of autonomous figurines")

However, the ones who had to step up to meet it were Zhou Hui's disciples, not Zhou Hui himself.

How could anyone expect an old retiree to shoulder everything?

He wasn't like the Mei-Zhu couple, popping out a son in his golden years just to fret over—though Zhou Hui felt that if you grabbed any single little brat from his own litter at random, they would be several times more trouble than other people's kids.

Yet everyone had to walk their own path; all he could do was teach them to the utmost of his ability.

Thinking thus, Zhou Hui confirmed the date for exploring the secret realm.

The date was set for one month from now.

Whether born of the Heavenly Dao's twisted sense of humor or not, that day happened to fall on the [Double Seventh Festival](# "Literal CN: 七月七 (qī yuè qī) — the 7th day of the 7th lunar month (Qixi Festival), China's traditional lovers' festival commemorating the annual reunion of the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl").

According to the conventions of the cultivation world, if the expedition proceeded smoothly, this date would eventually become the recurring trial day for major sects exploring the secret realm—which was to say, the exact day the [Child of Destiny](# "Literal CN: 天命之子 (tiānmìng zhī zǐ) — the Chosen One of destiny / traditional overpowered Xianxia protagonist trope") and Mo Bei would first meet.

—"Heh,"-Zhou Hui let out a cold sneer.

Truly revolting enough.

He might want to [lie flat](# "Literal CN: 躺平 (tǎngpíng) — modern Chinese youth philosophy of refusing to participate in the hyper-competitive rat race"), but the children of his household were neither marionettes nor good actors.

In the past, with him keeping a lid on things while the kids still lacked seasoning, their weaknesses were easily exploited into dutifully acting out their tragic parts. But what if he reinforced them? That pack of unruly brats wouldn't even listen properly to their own Master; he refused to believe they would ever willingly dance to the tune of the Heavenly Dao.

Watching how the Heavenly Dao scrambled to patch its plot script ought to make for fine retirement entertainment, wouldn't it?

Gazing out the window at the sky, Zhou Hui calculated the syllabus schedule:

—"One month's time. That will be plenty."

*

The disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were indeed no marionettes.

While the Immortal Venerable was still mapping out the syllabus on his end, certain disciples on the other end didn't even want to attend the very first lecture.

[—"Ah-Cong! Yu Cong! Get up, quick! We're going to be late for the open lecture!"](# "Speaker: Yan Fei")

[—"...I'm not going."](# "Speaker: Yu Cong")

Burrowing furiously into his quilt, Yu Cong muttered:
—"I'm exhausted. All I want right now is to sleep."

Every sect harbored disciples of this caliber: immune to judging glares, deaf to whispered gossip, happily [lying flat and rotting](# "Literal CN: 躺平摆烂 (tǎngpíng bǎilàn) — modern Chinese internet slang for lying flat and letting things go to waste") at the sect's very bottom. As long as one self-evolved into the [foundation stone of the sect](# "Literal CN: 门派的基石 — sarcastic joke about dead-last slackers anchoring the bottom of the sect rankings"), their standing remained utterly unshakeable.

Yu Cong was precisely such a disciple.

In the previous sect-wide Grand Unified Examination, he had finished dead last—not only ranking dead last in scores, but in attitude as well.

Yu Cong had slept through the entire exam. Even the steadily plunging Coral Platform couldn't rouse him into putting up a token struggle. He had brazenly snoozed through the entire examination under the full view of fellow disciples and handymen alike, prompting everyone to lament what a waste it was that he didn't pursue body cultivation—without needing any tempering at all, the skin on his face was already as thick as a fortress city wall.

Within a Heavenly Spirit Sect steeped in the Untiring Immortal Venerable's ethos of relentless diligence, Yu Cong was an incomparable singular marvel.

Naturally, Yu Cong didn't see it that way himself.

He considered himself exceptionally hardworking. But he was a sword cultivator!

As a sword cultivator, all he needed was to hone his sword arts. If not for the unfortunate reality that illiteracy would prevent him from reading sword manuals, Yu Cong felt that remaining illiterate would have been perfectly fine. Why on earth did he have to sit for exams?!

Today was no exception.

He had risen at the [Chou hour](# "Literal CN: 丑时 (chǒushí) — traditional Chinese hour division, 1:00 AM to 3:00 AM") to practice sword forms, returning drenched in morning dew, and was just about to [catch a second round of sleep](# "Literal CN: 睡回笼觉 (shuì huílóngjiào) — going back to sleep after waking up early in the morning")! Yet here he was, being dragged out of bed for some open lecture. Yu Cong felt not the slightest need for it.

Yu Cong's friend knew his temperament well and knew exactly how to persuade him:

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable is definitely attending this open lecture in person. Are you sure you're skipping?"-his friend asked.

Yu Cong reluctantly poked his head out from under the quilt.

Truth be told, sword arts were hardly the Heavenly Spirit Sect's strong suit. That a sword cultivator like Yu Cong had chosen the Heavenly Spirit Sect was purely because he idolized the Untiring Immortal Venerable's overwhelming might.

It was just that in the presence of sleep, even the Immortal Venerable had to step aside temporarily.

—"Still... it's just an open lecture. The Immortal Venerable probably won't teach anything related to sword arts anyway,"-Yu Cong reasoned with his meager brain capacity.-"It'll just be generic cultivation manuals and common spells. It's all the same whether I go or not."

—"That's where you're wrong. Consider our Lord Immortal Venerable's temperament: he will definitely take meticulous note of who attended and who didn't,"-Yu Cong's friend countered.-"You wouldn't want to get on the Immortal Venerable's radar like that, would you? Ah, wait... with your test scores, maybe you're already on his radar!"

That remark jolted Yu Cong completely awake in sheer terror.

Abysmal paper scores could still be excused on the grounds of being an unrefined sword cultivator, but if he left the Immortal Venerable with the impression of being a lazy, insubordinate slacker, his future would be thoroughly doomed!

Scrambling out of bed in a panic, Yu Cong hastily tidied his robes and appearance.

Fortunately, thanks to the newly constructed Sundial Clock, the travel time was shaved down to nothing.

Thus, the very moment the shadows cast by the morning sun fell upon the disciples' residential quarters, Yu Cong and the others arrived at the Library Pavilion in the blink of an eye.

Because the Palace of Silence hadn't, strictly speaking, undergone full renovation yet, holding a grand lecture capable of accommodating everyone still required convening at the Library Pavilion.

This time, however, the gathering spot wasn't the previous main hall, but the right-wing turret of the Library Pavilion.

The renovated Library Pavilion was colossal; Yu Cong had never set foot here before.

—In fact, as a sword cultivator (once again!), Yu Cong had never harbored the faintest interest in the Library Pavilion.

His friend, however, let out a startled sound:
—"Huh? Why are we in the Formations wing?"

*What wing?*

Yu Cong thought dismissively: as long as it wasn't sword arts, it had nothing to do with him anyway.

With eyes shut like a sleepwalker, he trailed behind his friend: when the other walked, he walked; when the other sat, he sat. Not even Zhou Hui's voice could make him open his eyes:

—"...I trust the previous examination has allowed everyone to appreciate the importance of study, so this Venerable will not repeat the usual platitudes. For the majority of you, formal classes have yet to begin. For this open lecture, let us play a little game..."-Zhou Hui's voice resonated through the hall.

Yu Cong stifled a yawn.

Lethargically, he whispered under his breath:
—"Am I dreaming? Did the Lord Immortal Venerable just say 'game'?"

—"You're not dreaming. It really is a game,"-his friend answered.

[As expected, it really is a dream!]

Yu Cong let his consciousness sink deeper into the [Black Sweet Country of sleep](# "Literal CN: 黑甜乡 (hēitiánxiāng) — traditional Chinese poetic idiom for the land of deep, sweet dreams (sleep)"). Falling completely asleep was out of the question—if the Lord Immortal Venerable caught him, the consequences would be catastrophic—yet the Immortal Venerable's warm, gentle, melodious voice made for the most exquisitely soothing lullaby:

—"...A game of chess. Given that this is likely everyone's first time playing this form of chess, for our first round, we shall proceed according to a standard [team comp](# "Standard MMO/RPG party composition: Mage/Dharma cultivator, Tank/Body cultivator, Support/Alchemist-Artificer, DPS/Sword cultivator")."

—"First, I require a standard [Dharma cultivator](# "Literal CN: 法修 (fǎxiū) — spellcasting cultivator focusing on element/Dao arts (mage/caster)")... Qianxing."

—"Next, we need a [Body cultivator](# "Literal CN: 体修 (tǐxiū) — physical/body-tempering cultivator focusing on close-quarters combat and extreme durability (tank/brawler)")... Yuwen, step forward... I know you are not a body cultivator, but with your regenerative capabilities, you are more than qualified to moonlight as one."

—"Then, two supports—namely, an [Artifact cultivator](# "Literal CN: 器修 (qìxiū) — artifact-refining / weapon-crafting cultivator") and a [Pill cultivator](# "Literal CN: 丹修 (dānxiū) — alchemist / medicinal pill cultivator (healer/support)")—Ye-zi, Shulong."

—"And lastly, we require a [Sword cultivator](# "Literal CN: 剑修 (jiànxiū) — sword cultivator specializing in lethal offensive blade techniques (DPS/carry)")..."

*Snap!* Yu Cong's eyes flew wide open.

—"Did the Lord Immortal Venerable just mention a sword cultivator?"-Yu Cong asked sideways.

His friend didn't pay him the slightest heed.

To be precise, every single disciple was craning their neck, eyes glued intently to the front of the hall. Not a soul had the spare attention to bother with Yu Cong.

Yu Cong had no choice but to widen his own eyes and look for himself.

Only then did he discover that this lecture genuinely wasn't some dry, tedious theoretical sermon; it truly appeared to be a "game."

At the front of the lecture hall sat a chessboard. Several chess pieces rested upon its grid—not the Go stones popular among cultivators, nor Chinese xiangqi pieces, but rather lifelike, vividly detailed little figurines.

Upon closer inspection, the appearance of those figurines corresponded precisely to the direct disciples Yu Cong had heard Zhou Hui summon in his daze:

Qu Qianxing, Yuwen Xuan, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong.

Only one blank piece remained on the black side. Scanning his surroundings, Zhou Hui patted the shoulder of his youngest female disciple, Mo Bei:

—"Bei'er, take the field."

Whatever Zhou Hui did, the blank piece on the board shifted and morphed, taking on Mo Bei's exact likeness.

Nodding, Zhou Hui addressed the assembly:
—"Next, I require an opponent—"

—"Hold on, Master, that's not fair!"-Yuwen Xuan piped up right then, bouncing forward.-"Even if this is an [instructional match](# "Literal CN: 指导棋 (zhǐdǎo qí) — game where a master or stronger player plays to instruct and demonstrate concepts to students"), the opponent is Master yourself! Isn't this a guaranteed loss?"

—"In that case, let us have someone else play the instructional match,"-Zhou Hui glanced at Yuwen Xuan, then averted his gaze.-"Ran Deng, you remember the rules I explained earlier, yes? You shall take the pieces."

—"Yes, Master,"-Ran Deng stepped up beside the board.

—"Third Junior Sister is a formations master! If I'm not mistaken, this match involves formations, so she holds an inherent advantage!"-Yuwen Xuan pressed persistently.-"And then there are the pieces! Junior brothers and sisters aside, not to brag, but whoever holds my piece is guaranteed victory!"

—"Then we shall let Ah-Ran's opponent command this set of pieces,"-Zhou Hui turned to Ran Deng.-"Ah-Ran, would that be acceptable?"

—"Of course, Master,"-Ran Deng replied, beaming at Yuwen Xuan.-"Eldest Senior Brother, do you intend to match wits against me using your 'guaranteed-victory self'?"

—"I don't bully my junior sisters,"-Yuwen Xuan chuckled heartily.-"Second Junior Brother, why don't you play the instructional match against Third Junior Sister?"

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Qu Qianxing had clearly been lost in thought. Abruptly singled out by Yuwen Xuan, he was shoved in front of the chessboard before he could even register what was happening.

Knitting his brows, Qu Qianxing moved to step back:
—"I don't need to..."
[Is Eldest Senior Brother in that much of a hurry to hang from the tree again?]

To everyone's surprise, Zhou Hui seemed remarkably tolerant of Yuwen Xuan's antics today:
—"Let it be so, then,"-he murmured, glancing at the board.-"However, I will need to find five suitable pieces for Ah-Ran."

With that, Zhou Hui turned toward the assembled disciples:
—"Is anyone willing?"

The disciples froze in momentary shock. Some hadn't grasped what was happening; others were cautiously testing the waters. Yet several bold souls raised their hands high:

[—"Me!"](# "Speaker: Eager Disciples")

[—"Immortal Venerable, please choose me!"](# "Speaker: Eager Disciples")

Zhou Hui's gaze swept across the crowd before finally settling right beside Yu Cong.

Yu Cong blinked blankly, only then realizing that the fellow sitting beside him had also raised his hand.

—"Yan Fei, correct?"-Zhou Hui addressed Yu Cong's friend. He had evidently memorized every disciple's name and specialty.-"I recall your sword arts are quite solid. Do you intend to become a sword cultivator?"

—"Yes, Immortal Venerable!"-Yan Fei answered, lightheaded with disbelief.

Yu Cong stared at Yan Fei in sheer envy, kicking himself for not reacting faster.

Not noticing Yu Cong, Zhou Hui handed a peach-wood chess piece to Yan Fei:
—"Imbue it with the single sword technique you excel at most."

—"Ah, what I'm best at is the third stance of your self-created Flying Spirit Sword Technique: the Spirit Cloud Sword,"-Yan Fei confessed, his face flushing crimson.

As he spoke, an additional piece materialized on the board before Ran Deng, sculpted in the exact likeness of a miniature Yan Fei.

The little figurine struck a remarkably crisp stance and piped up:
—"Spirit Cloud Sword!"
Not only did it mirror Yan Fei's appearance down to the finest detail, but its posture, motions, and even the subtle subconscious quirks of how he wielded his blade were replicated to perfection.

Yu Cong could even spot the common flaws Yan Fei habitually exposed!

[—"Whoa!"](# "Speaker: Disciples in the Hall")
A collective wave of awe rippled through the surrounding disciples.

Yan Fei stood frozen with a face that screamed utter [social death](# "Literal CN: 社死 (shè sǐ) — social death; extreme, dying-of-embarrassment public humiliation").

Yu Cong, however, was consumed by envy. He desperately wanted a figurine of his own too—and what better way to show off before the Immortal Venerable?

Then a sudden realization struck him: [Wait, did those direct disciples' figurines just demonstrate their moves earlier too?]
Regret pierced Yu Cong's heart even deeper for having slept through the beginning.

Evidently, Yu Cong was far from the only one thinking this.

When Zhou Hui asked:
—"We have our sword cultivator. Next, a body cultivator—"

[—"Me!"](# "Speaker: Eager Disciples")

[—"Me! Pick me!"](# "Speaker: Eager Disciples")

This time, nearly half the lecture hall shot their hands into the air, making it seem as though the Heavenly Spirit Sect were some grand sanctuary for body-tempering brutes—though, naturally, plenty were raising their hands blindly. Who wouldn't jump at the chance to show off before the Immortal Venerable?

Zhou Hui swiftly assembled all five pieces for Ran Deng.
