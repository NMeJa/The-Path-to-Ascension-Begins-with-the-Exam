# Chapter 186: Fourth Senior Brother

—"They're definitely hiding something from me,"-Mo Xi—the version from this current cycle—muttered with a bellyful of resentment.

He had realized this the instant he laid eyes on the "other me."

Although Master had praised him back then with a string of reasonable-sounding nonsense, Mo Xi was hardly his foolish elder sister. Adept at reading expressions and assessing atmosphere, he knew full well that Master occasionally lied too. Usually, that was reserved exclusively for Eldest Senior Brother—whenever Master found Eldest Senior Brother too bothersome and couldn't be bothered to reason with him, Master would adopt a seemingly earnest tone to hoodwink Eldest Senior Brother with a few phrases, diverting the fellow's attention while shooing him away.

Who would have thought he'd enjoy Eldest Senior Brother's treatment one day too? Heh heh...

—"Wait, no! I don't want to become Eldest Senior Brother!"-Mo Xi yelped.

Abruptly realizing what utter idiocy he had just been daydreaming about, every fine hair on his body stood on end.

While Mo Xi was still reeling from the shock, an unceremonious sneer of mockery came from behind him:

—"It's not that you don't want to, you just wouldn't dare, wouldn't you?"-a voice mocked.-"After all, one [deadbeat freeloading off the sect](# "Literal CN: 吃闲饭 (chī xiánfàn) — eating idle rice; living as a freeloader or parasite") is more than enough for our Heavenly Spirit Sect. Anyone else delusional enough to try becoming a second will simply end up becoming part of Eldest Senior Brother's idle meal. *Jie-jie.*"

[—"Fourth Senior Brother?!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Hearing that familiar voice, Mo Xi forgot all about being angry. Whirling around, he discovered a certain gloomy youth trailing a vivid crimson tailbone standing there, having appeared who-knew-when.

—"Why are you here?"-Mo Xi asked in surprise.

—"Master told me to come, of course. Otherwise, with my cultivation, did you expect me to teleport all the way here from the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-Guan Ye scoffed. But when his gaze settled upon his youngest junior brother, it narrowed slightly.-"Speaking of cultivation, we haven't seen each other for only a few days, and it looks like you've broken through?"

—"Ah..."-Mo Xi muttered, immediately feeling a twinge of guilt.

Naturally, Mo Xi wasn't feeling guilty about successfully breaking through.

No cultivator would ever think breaking through a cultivation realm was a bad thing, nor was there any need to conceal one's prowess in the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Regarding his successful breakthrough, Mo Xi's only regret was that advancing from Foundation Establishment to Golden Core didn't come with heavenly tribulation lightning or anything of the sort—something like Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother's breakthroughs, which would have allowed the entire demon race across the Sacred Tree to gaze upon his glorious success in awe.

Still, just because Old Man Heaven wouldn't cooperate didn't mean one couldn't take matters into one's own hands.

Mo Xi had already made up his mind: the moment he returned, he would assemble all his trusted lackeys, inform them of his promotion to the Golden Core stage, and drop heavy hints for them to plaster the Heavenly Spirit Sect with celebration banners, letting every single person know that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's youngest junior brother had successfully advanced to Golden Core at the tender age of merely fifteen (in reality, he was already seventeen by [nominal age calculation](# "Literal CN: 虚岁 (xūsuì) — traditional East Asian age reckoning where a person is one year old at birth and gains a year on Lunar New Year"))!

Granted, his realm wasn't as high as Eldest Senior Brother's or Second Senior Brother's, but his youth was unmatched!

A fifteen-year-old Golden Core was virtually unprecedented in the cultivation world! Even the genius Second Senior Brother and the non-human Fifth Senior Brother hadn't matched this speed! Who could behold that and not shower him with praise?!

At this thought, Mo Xi could hardly wait to cock his (non-existent) tail straight into the heavens.

It was only upon Guan Ye's sudden appearance that Mo Xi remembered: his Fourth Senior Brother was still stuck at the late Foundation Establishment stage. Wouldn't his and Mo Bei's breakthrough to Golden Core make Fourth Senior Brother lose face?

Had it been any other senior brother, even Fifth Senior Brother, Mo Xi wouldn't give a damn if they choked on their own spit. But Fourth Senior Brother...

The most earnest, hardworking Fourth Senior Brother...

Facing Guan Ye's pitch-black pupils, Mo Xi felt an inexplicable sensation of having committed some wicked deed.

After hemming and hawing for quite some time, he finally whispered in a tiny voice:

—"I... and Mo Bei... broke through,"-Mo Xi admitted softly. Bringing Mo Bei into it wasn't to drag someone down with him, but rather because he didn't want Fourth Senior Brother to suffer another emotional shock later upon discovering Mo Bei had broken through to Golden Core as well.

—"Youngest Junior Sister broke through too?"-Sure enough, Guan Ye froze for a moment upon hearing that, a flash of complicated emotion passing through his eyes.

Yet it lasted for only a fleeting instant.

Immediately following, Guan Ye broke into a gratified smile, clapping Mo Xi on the back:

—"Isn't that a wonderful thing? Why do you look like you just got caught red-handed causing trouble?"-Guan Ye asked.

At that moment, Mo Xi deeply felt that his Fourth Senior Brother was truly an artisan specialized in artifact refinement—his physical strength was absurdly monstrous. Even having advanced to Golden Core, Mo Xi felt as though he were on the verge of coughing up internal injuries from being patted by a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator.

Fortunately, Guan Ye had no intention of seizing the opportunity to shatter his junior brother's shoulder blades.

As if suddenly remembering something, he withdrew his hand, rummaged through his Mustard Seed Pouch, and produced two bronze bells—one tied with a blue silk ribbon, the other with a red silk ribbon.

Guan Ye handed the one with the red silk ribbon to Mo Xi:

—"This is your breakthrough gift,"-Guan Ye said.

—"There's actually a gift?"-Mo Xi asked, his eyes instantly sparkling.

Guilt was guilt, but when it came to collecting gifts, he felt not a single shred of shame.

Or rather, he loved gifts above all else!

Guan Ye also liked this about his youngest junior brother best. He beamed chuckledly, watching Mo Xi infuse spiritual energy into the bell.

—"It's a mustard seed spatial realm!"-Mo Xi exclaimed.

"Looking" into the space inside the bell, he found it resembled an artifact shop, packed with all kinds of magical artifacts a Golden Core cultivator might need. Among them, an abacus forged from top-grade crimson dragon marrow jade was particularly eye-catching. Not only was its exterior gorgeous, able to expand or shrink at Mo Xi's will and compute automatically, but each individual abacus bead could also store a spell for instant discharge—an absolute masterpiece of a treasure for someone like Mo Xi, whose practical combat prowess was not his forte.

Mo Xi gleefully inventoried every single artifact, deliberately pulling out the abacus to caress it several times before reluctantly fastening the bell to the tip of his braided hair, intentionally giving it a shake to show it wouldn't easily leave his person.

Yet his mouth tsundere-ly complained:

—"Fourth Senior Brother's gifts really lack any novelty,"-Mo Xi grumbled.-"Aside from magical artifacts, it's just more magical artifacts."

—"I'm just a blacksmith, and this is the only sort of thing I know how to make,"-Guan Ye sneered.-"If you want novelty, go find your Eldest Senior Sister and Fifth Senior Brother!"

—"I can get gifts from other senior brothers and sisters too?"-Mo Xi asked, his eyes shining even brighter.

—"Customarily, yes,"-Guan Ye nodded.-"Back when I re-established my foundation, I also received gifts from the other senior brothers and sister; even Lu Shulong, who was still a little dragon at the time, plucked a scale to give me. Therefore, as early as when you and Mo Bei were establishing your foundation, I had already begun preparing gifts for my younger siblings. Originally, when you two broke through to Foundation Establishment, we wanted to give gifts, but firstly, you had just joined the sect and none of us had suitable gifts on hand, and secondly, with how you two were back then... we decided to wait until your next advancement."

—"I see!"-Mo Xi nodded in profound agreement.

He and Mo Bei had advanced to Foundation Establishment only a few months after entering the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Back then, neither he nor Mo Bei had fully come to terms with the reality that they were no longer commodities, but the youngest junior sister and junior brother of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Had they received a pile of gifts from their seniors back then, who knew what they might have done with them.

—Not that his current self was plotting anything virtuous either.

For instance: if even his senior brothers and sister gave gifts, what about his biological sister? Though he didn't know by how many minutes Mo Bei had been born before him, she was still his "elder sister"—at the very least, she had to give a gift! What about the other Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples? Once they learned Mo Xi had advanced, shouldn't they present gifts? Most importantly, those grand figures from outside sects who freeloaded food and drinks at the Heavenly Spirit Sect—they couldn't possibly expect to get away with merely tossing out a few words of empty praise, could they?

Mo Xi's train of thought branched wilder and wilder, having already progressed to [scraping the entire Central Domain three feet deep](# "Literal CN: 刮地三尺 (guādì sānchǐ) — plundering and squeezing every last drop of wealth").

Guan Ye had no inkling that his youngest junior brother harbored such grandiose ambitions over merely advancing to Golden Core. Having handed the bell to Mo Xi, he surveyed their surroundings.

Guan Ye had also been summoned abruptly.

He had originally been carrying out routine work in the artifact refinement workshop when Master's little bird suddenly flew over, delivering an order for him to come to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. Then, without waiting for him to ask what it was about or giving him any time to prepare, it had directly dragged him over.

Thus, Guan Ye had emerged from Mo Xi's shadow just moments ago with a head full of bewilderment, having no clue what had transpired. It was only because the advancement gifts for Mo Bei and Mo Xi had long since been prepared—from Guan Ye's perspective, it was only a matter of time before the peerlessly gifted twins surpassed him in cultivation; just as Lu Shulong had been destined from birth to outstrip him, there was simply nothing to brood over—that he had been able to hand over the gift the moment he saw his junior brother.

Even so, Guan Ye still had no idea why Master had summoned him here.

And Mo Xi was visibly just as clueless.

—"Where is Master?"-Guan Ye asked with a strange expression, glancing around when he failed to find the person he wished to see.-"Why aren't you clinging to the old gentleman like usual?"

—"He took Mo Bei out early this morning,"-Mo Xi replied.

*[And took the other "me" too.]*

Mentioning Master, Mo Xi lapsed back into his previous listless gloom:

—"I don't know what he went off to do either. He just left me behind all by myself to do chores."

—"No wonder you look like an abandoned, resentful widow,"-Guan Ye mocked unceremoniously.

He didn't bother asking what Master had gone to do—judging by Mo Xi's pathetic look, he almost certainly had no clue; besides, whatever Master did naturally had its reasons, and it was better not to pry unnecessarily. Guan Ye simply leaned over toward Mo Xi's light screen and peered at it:

—"What's this?"-Guan Ye asked.

—"The demon race's 'Exhortation to Learning' system,"-Mo Xi replied.

—"Why does this system feel even more formal and standardized than the one at the White Jade Capital Academy?"-Guan Ye curled his lips, scanning the rune code ten lines at a glance.-"*Hiss*, this function can actually be implemented?"

—"Their hardware is top-tier,"-Mo Xi glanced at the interface, growing even more lethargic.-"Who knows how Master managed to swindle the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree itself into serving as his processor."

—"Isn't that the highest possible spec?"-Guan Ye said enviously.

With a rustle, he flipped through the runes across the light screen, casually rectifying several errors along the way.

Mo Xi watched without stopping him, even taking the initiative to clear the way for him.

After all, when it came to runes, his Fourth Senior Brother was the genuine powerhouse.

And he was the rare variety proficient in [both software and hardware](# "Literal CN: 软硬兼修 (ruǎn yìng jiān xiū) — proficient in both soft and hard aspects; here playfully applied to computing software and hardware in artifact refinement").

One only needed to look at the magical artifacts Guan Ye forged to understand this—if a fifteen-year-old Golden Core cultivator might occasionally appear among the prodigies of the human race, while demonic and spirit races were sometimes born at the Golden Core stage, in the field of artifact refinement, no one had ever heard of a Foundation Establishment cultivator capable of forging weapons for the Golden Core realm.

This was only natural. A Foundation Establishment cultivator didn't even know how much spiritual energy a Golden Core held; how could they forge artifacts tailored for them? Unless they directly used Nascent Soul dragon cores or phoenix bones as raw materials, downgrading the grade by an entire realm just to churn out some substandard products.

For this very reason, once cultivation reached the Void Refinement stage and beyond, there was no such thing as "normal commerce."

Aside from high-tier treasures crafted by grand artifact masters that only massive commercial syndicates like Carefree Palace could heap mountains of wealth to nurture, all other treasures could only be acquired by digging up antiques from ancient ruins or waiting for celestial treasures to fall from the Upper Realm. The odds of that were essentially on par with being the Child of Destiny. Whenever such items surfaced at an auction, their prices would skyrocket hundreds of times above the raw material costs, forcing grand powerhouses at Void Refinement and above to become all-rounder cultivators—needing to master alchemy, artifact refinement, formation arrays... they had to master every single discipline themselves.

Guan Ye, however, was different.

First, he had a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse as his master, giving him an impeccable foundation and theoretical knowledge superior even to certain Nascent Soul artifact masters. Second, he had an endless variety of rare and common materials to practice on, and even had a living Golden Core dragon willingly serving as a live test subject, allowing Guan Ye to conduct real-time research and grasp the most optimal circulation models of a Golden Core system. Third, and most crucially: Guan Ye was ruthless enough toward himself. If his own spiritual energy couldn't keep pace, he directly upgraded the refinement chamber's power supply to the Nascent Soul level, hammering out artifacts as a Foundation Establishment cultivator while braving Nascent Soul-grade spiritual pressure. Since he possessed trash spirit roots with abysmal spiritual energy perception, he directly implanted sensory artifacts into his own body, braving the risk of physical body mutation to read data. If his talent was lacking, the solution was simpler still: multiplying his practice by several times, dozens of times, hundreds of times—even Zhou Hui couldn't stop him from ruining his own body, though it was equally possible Zhou Hui had walked that very path himself and understood Guan Ye's mindset.

In any case, through Guan Ye's various death-defying antics, not only could he forge Golden Core artifacts, he had even forged that massive cauldron which Eldest Senior Brother could only wield at the late Nascent Soul stage.

In other words, comparing it to another industry: Mo Xi was merely a systems designer, whereas Guan Ye was a master who could [hand-craft silicon microchips from scratch](# "Literal CN: 手搓芯片的大能 — modern tech metaphor: a grandmaster capable of fabricating computer chips by hand").

At this thought, Mo Xi suddenly felt his earlier guilt had been entirely unwarranted.

Fourth Senior Brother would always be his Fourth Senior Brother. Before Senior Brother Guan's artifact refinement prowess, what did a measly Golden Core even amount to?

On the contrary, for Master to summon Senior Brother at this critical juncture probably boded ill for himself!

Thinking thus, Mo Xi stared at Guan Ye as if facing a mortal enemy.

Guan Ye had already immersed himself completely in the rune programming. Several ambiguous floating windows hovered around him, seemingly bearing no relation to the "Exhortation to Learning system" whatsoever, drifting with symbols that even Mo Xi couldn't decipher.

Seeing this, Mo Xi called out:

—"Fourth Senior Brother..."-Mo Xi called.

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"Fourth Senior Brother,"-Mo Xi called again.

[—"..., ......"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"Fourth Senior Brother!"-Mo Xi shouted.

—"...I know! I know... I'm just looking!"-Guan Ye responded, his words making it blatantly clear he had no clue why Mo Xi was calling him, his eyes glued intently to the screen.-"As expected of a tree spirit that has lived for tens of thousands of years! This [spiritual energy capacity] is truly massive! Really so big! Even bigger than the dragon race!"

—"What's the use of being big? It can't even last a few minutes! And if Fifth Senior Brother heard that, he'd definitely be jealous,"-Mo Xi retorted.

[Ah, what kind of cursed conversation is this?!]

Seeing that shouting was useless and knowing his physical strength was no match for the other, Mo Xi simply plopped his backside right onto Guan Ye's lap, shoving his face directly before the man's eyes to monopolize his entire field of vision.

—"What are you doing?!"-Guan Ye finally "saw" his youngest junior brother.

Puffing his cheeks, Mo Xi looked at him suspiciously:

—"Senior Brother Guan, have you figured out what you're reporting for the [Tenth Month Festival](# "Literal CN: 十月朝 (shíyuècháo) — the first day of the tenth lunar month, a traditional festival where the sect conducts annual reports and reviews")?"-Mo Xi asked crossly.

—"The Tenth Month Festival?"-Guan Ye's thoughts were still lingering on the "tree spirit supercomputer." He blinked, only then regaining his senses.-"With the Immortal Arts Assembly this year, and Master mentioning he wants to host an opening ceremony for the grand finals, didn't he say we weren't going to make a big fuss?"

—"Master isn't planning to throw a grand celebration, but there's no escaping our annual debrief, right?"-Mo Xi said.-"After all, around this time every year, someone always wants to [pull off some massive performance stunt](# "Literal CN: 整大活 (zhěng dà huó) — modern internet slang: staging a show-stopping spectacle or groundbreaking flex")."

—"You think everyone is as petty and scheming as you?"-Guan Ye scoffed.

—"Hmph, hard to say!"-Mo Xi huffed. Leaning his face even closer in an attempt to manufacture a sense of intimidation, he pressed:-"So, Fourth Senior Brother, is your annual report written yet?! Do you have a flagship project to report to Master?!"

[You aren't trying to snatch my project, are you?!]

Mo Xi had practically plastered this question across his face.

Amused by Mo Xi's reaction, Guan Ye was just about to tease him when a shocked voice rang out:

[—"What in the world are you two doing?!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

Guan Ye and Mo Xi turned their heads, only to find Tao Zhu staring at them in utter bewilderment.

Behind Tao Zhu, their master had returned at some point as well. Mo Bei was trailing behind him, her face dark as thunder as she glared at them.

Mo Bei naturally had every reason for her vision to go black:

The instant she stepped through the door, she saw her moronic younger brother sitting on Fourth Senior Brother's lap, their faces so close they were practically kissing! Anyone unfamiliar with them would assume these two martial brothers were engaged in some scandalous, forbidden affair! Just look at Boss Tao—he had clearly misunderstood!

Fortunately, Master didn't overthink it, maintaining his emotionally stable smile.

—Though Mo Bei had good reason to suspect that even if two of his disciples genuinely hooked up, as long as there was no coercion involved, Master wouldn't care in the slightest.

[After all, that man has been single for thousands of years; he's surely long grown accustomed to seeing the men and women—or whatever combination one prefers—around him pairing off, hasn't he?]

Mo Bei grumbled inwardly.

—"Master!"-Mo Xi cried out.

On the other side, Mo Xi hadn't noticed how intimate his posture with Fourth Senior Brother looked in the slightest. The moment he spotted Master, he darted over like an arrow, circling Zhou Hui several times. Confirming that the "other me" wasn't around, he was finally satisfied, vigorously shaking the newly attached bell at the end of his braid so it jingled merrily. He looked for all the world like an eager puppy currying favor, making Mo Bei's expression darken even further.

Subsequently, Guan Ye walked over:

—"Disciple pays respects to Master,"-Guan Ye greeted, bowing conscientiously to Zhou Hui.

He then produced the blue-ribboned bell from his sleeve and handed it to Mo Bei:

—"This is a breakthrough gift for Youngest Junior Sister,"-Guan Ye said.

—"...Thank you,"-Mo Bei hesitated briefly before accepting it.

Unlike Mo Xi, she didn't immediately inspect the internal space of the bell. Instead, she examined its exterior carefully, offering a customary polite compliment:

—"This bell is truly beautiful. As expected of Fourth Senior Brother, exquisite craftsmanship... Huh? This bell has a summoning function?"-Mo Bei asked.

—"Youngest Junior Sister truly has sharp eyes; a certain someone didn't notice at all,"-Guan Ye said, casting a pointed glance at Mo Xi.

—"Summoning?"-Mo Xi spun around to grab his braid.-"Ah, really?! All our senior brothers' and sisters' names are engraved on it?!"

—"Everyone felt that once our junior sister and brother reached Golden Core, you would inevitably have to travel outside for trials and experience,"-Guan Ye explained.-"It would be terrible if you encountered danger. So we each left our true names on it so you can summon us in times of peril. When necessary, you can even alert and summon the entire sect."

—"...Thank you, Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei thanked him once more upon hearing this.

She carefully tied the bell to the jade pendant at her waist.

With her senior brother having gone to such lengths, she wasn't foolish enough to utter proud nonsense like "I can protect myself."

Mo Xi, on the other hand, made a face as though suffering from a toothache upon discovering Eldest Senior Brother's name on the bell, hurriedly slapping several restriction seals onto it—as if terrified he might accidentally summon his Eldest Senior Brother out into the open.

Zhou Hui watched this loving scene between his disciples with a beaming smile, not thinking for a second that having the entire sect swarm out like a kicked hornet's nest whenever someone provoked his disciples was a bad thing at all.

Seeing Zhou Hui like this, Tao Zhu could only swallow the bellyful of words back into his plump stomach. Saying them would be useless anyway.

—"Untiring, what did you call me here for?"-Tao Zhu asked grumpily.

[Surely not just to watch your disciples display [brotherly love and filial devotion](# "Literal CN: 兄友弟恭 (xiōngyǒu-dìgōng) — traditional Confucian ideal of elder brothers being kind and younger brothers being respectful")?]

Zhou Hui naturally wouldn't summon Tao Zhu just for this.

Even before returning in person, he had summoned his fourth disciple and Boss Tao together solely for that "cloud server" intended to expand the Sacred Tree's capacity.

Without wasting words, Zhou Hui cut straight to the chase:

—"Boss Tao, how much jade do you have on hand?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"What quality are you looking for?"-Tao Zhu asked, instantly perking up upon hearing that business was involved.

In the cultivation world, jade was actually used primarily as a structural material. While its spiritual energy storage was admittedly inferior to spirit stones, it exhibited different "fields" of spiritual energy under environments of varying spiritual qi concentrations—indeed, if one were to make an analogy, jade was the semiconductor material of spiritual energy. Cultivators had conducted extensive research into jade as a result, utilizing it to store all manner of spells, which had given birth to jade slips of every kind.

Glancing at Mo Xi's light screens, Boss Tao inquired:

—"Is it for storing this 'pedagogical spell'?"-Tao Zhu asked.-"I agree a set is needed. Those wood spirits are far too rigid; letting them store data easily leads to packet loss. Having a backup is much better. I happen to have a batch of premium ice jade—"

—"One batch won't be nearly enough,"-Zhou Hui interrupted Boss Tao's sales pitch.-"This system needs some, but another place requires far more."

—"How much is 'far more'?"-Boss Tao asked.

—"I will take you to see it first, and then you can appraise it,"-Zhou Hui said. After all, without seeing that massive mass of yin spirits in person, one could never comprehend the scale.

Saying so, Zhou Hui turned toward Guan Ye:

—"Ye-zi, how are things over at your artifact refinement workshop?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Mo Xi's ears instantly pricked up, looking terrified that Fourth Senior Brother might butt in.

While Guan Ye might tease Mo Xi on purpose, before Master, he spoke nothing but the plain truth:

—"The artifact refinement workshop is already swamped beyond belief, Master,"-Guan Ye answered.-"Are you short on hands? If you need manpower, I can try to give an emergency training course to a batch of disciples—"

—"In other words, you can free yourself up, correct?"-Zhou Hui nodded.-"In that case, hand off the workshop duties for this period and wrap up your current coursework. Starting next spring, stay by my side."

Neither immortal remains nor towering yin spirit cloudbanks were things the Heavenly Spirit Sect's artifact refinement workshop could touch. In fact, even a Mahayana powerhouse like Tao Zhu, who wasn't specialized in artifact refinement, might not be able to handle them. Therefore, Zhou Hui had decided from the very beginning to take charge of this project personally.

Yet with such a massive project, it would be a crying shame not to use the opportunity to mentor a student.

Among his disciples, Guan Ye was undeniably the most suitable; his cultivation realm was just absurdly low.

Naturally, Zhou Hui wouldn't give up over a "minor detail" like cultivation. He had pulled Guan Ye over immediately and would figure out the plan as they went.

Crucially, the boy Guan Ye was hardworking and ambitious enough.

Hearing Master's words, Guan Ye's entire face lit up, his gloomy demeanor dissipating by more than half:

—"Yes, Master!"-he responded in a loud, clear voice.

For Master's curriculum, everything on hand could naturally be set aside.

Yet Guan Ye had no intention of completely abandoning his current coursework either. He even thoughtfully considered Master's and the sect's requirements:

—"Master, does this mean our interactions with the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree will increase significantly in the future?"-Guan Ye asked.-"If so, wouldn't it be better to have a large-scale teleportation array directly established?"

—"It is indeed needed, but time is somewhat tight..."-Zhou Hui mused.

The Sacred Tree, which had been standing half-dead in the shadows, suddenly perked up:

—"You even know how to build large-scale teleportation arrays?!"-the Sacred Tree exclaimed. It had assumed this human craft had long been lost to history!

—"Of course he knows how; he even prepared the materials,"-Tao Zhu said, recalling last year's annual meeting.

Ever since then, Zhou Hui had been gathering relevant materials. Although he hadn't decided where the first teleportation array should lead, completing all the preparatory work in advance was an absolute necessity.

And now, the first region requiring a large-scale teleportation array had presented itself.

Although the Central Domain's teleportation array connected to the Southern Domain, and that border city had come under the Heavenly Spirit Sect's de facto control following the Immortal Arts Assembly, the distance between the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was still far too vast. If only Zhou Hui were capable of instant teleportation, regular communication and traffic between the two regions would be exceedingly troublesome.

A large-scale teleportation array was a hard requirement.

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree itself was far more enthusiastic about this than about having its capacity "expanded":

—"Venerable Immortal, given our current relationship, a large-scale teleportation array..."-the Sacred Tree chimed in.

—"I agree it is necessary, but time is truly too tight,"-Zhou Hui reiterated for the second time.-"It is not difficult, per se, but constructing a large-scale teleportation array is fundamentally manual labor. Designing and erecting the formation array simply takes too much time..."

[Can you even call that human speech?]

Tao Zhu's lips twitched, swallowing his snark unspoken.

In any case, he had long grown accustomed to it.

Unexpectedly, Guan Ye intervened at this moment:

—"Indeed,"-Guan Ye said.-"Master's energy shouldn't be squandered on such physical labor. Therefore, please allow me to undertake the engineering of this teleportation array."

—"Wait, you know how?!"-Tao Zhu exclaimed in genuine astonishment this time.

For the very first time, he looked squarely at this disciple of Zhou Hui, who possessed a mere Foundation Establishment cultivation and whose spirit roots had been virtually ruined.

Guan Ye paid no mind to Tao Zhu's attitude. Nodding, he explained in detail:

—"At the beginning of the year, Master placed materials regarding large-scale teleportation arrays into the Library Pagoda,"-Guan Ye explained.-"Whenever I had idle time, I would go read them. Although I cannot manage the most crucial tasks—such as using spiritual energy to tear open spatial rifts or processing certain high-grade spiritual materials—I have studied the intricacies of how to calibrate the spatial entrance, stabilize the aperture's spiritual energy, and compute transmission energy loads. I even tried drafting several blueprints myself."

Saying so, Guan Ye produced several tons of jade slips from his Mustard Seed Pouch.

Yes, the unit of measurement was correct.

Several tons.

A jade slip's storage capacity was actually quite immense, sufficient to document the entire life of a thousand-year-old cultivator. Yet Guan Ye had used them for drafting blueprints, filling several tons of them—and that was merely drawn during his "idle time."

Tao Zhu stared, feeling his Mahayana-grade brain somewhat overloaded.

—"Well done,"-Zhou Hui praised, glancing once more at Mo Bei and Mo Xi's bells.-"In that case, could it be that the runes for this summoning technique were also developed by you?"

—"It was merely a bit of peripheral inspiration gained from researching teleportation arrays,"-Guan Ye replied modestly.

—"Then give it a try!"-Zhou Hui smiled warmly.-"Whatever materials you need, ask Qianxing... No, go directly to the treasury and take them yourself; just record it afterward."-Saying so, Zhou Hui handed the Heavenly Spirit Sect secret treasury's clearance jade pendant to Guan Ye.

With the Heavenly Spirit Sect showing such commitment, the Sacred Tree at the other end of the teleportation link naturally couldn't remain empty-handed.

—"Worthy of being the Immortal Venerable's disciple,"-the Sacred Tree sighed in admiration.-"I will inform Shui Huang'er shortly to have the Sacred Tree's demon race fully cooperate with the teleportation array's construction. You may mobilize whichever demons you require!"

—"Then I leave it in your hands,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Disciple obeys!"-Guan Ye cupped his fists.

Mo Xi watched this scene in dead silence:

[I knew it! I just knew it! Every time the Tenth Month Festival draws near, Senior Brother Guan starts [pulling extraordinary stunts](# "Literal CN: 作妖 (zuòyāo) — stirring up demonic trouble / making waves / flexing unexpectedly")!]

[And what does our usual squabbling for favor even amount to? Next to Senior Brother Guan, it's child's play.]

[To be frank, among all the disciples, the one Master finds most headache-inducing is Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan; the one he trusts most is Second Senior Brother Qu Qianxing; the one he feels most helpless about is Eldest Senior Sister Ran Deng; the one he worries least about is Fifth Senior Brother Lu Shulong; the ones he worries most about are Mo Bei and me; but the one he dotes on most is without question Fourth Senior Brother Guan Ye!]

[Basically, only Senior Brother Guan gets whatever he wants from Master, no questions asked!]
