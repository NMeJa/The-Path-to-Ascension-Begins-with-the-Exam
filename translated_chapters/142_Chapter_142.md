# Chapter 142: Only Just Begun (第142章 才刚刚开始)

[← Previous Chapter](141_Chapter_141.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](143_Chapter_143.md)

---

—"Not a single one has fallen off..."-Jian Nuo said in astonished disbelief.

He was speaking of the students climbing the Ascension Stairway.

By now, the exam was more than half over. One after another, students from the White Jade Capital Academy crossed the Ascension Stairway. Though most did not walk as steadily as Eleventh Lady Mei at the front—several teetered and swayed like paper caught in a gale, and one had even scrambled up the steps in an undignified, frantic dog-paddle—not a single one had actually plunged off the edge.

Other cultivators might not think much of it, but Jian Nuo had tested the stairway himself. Knowing firsthand that the Ascension Stairway was nowhere near as simple as it appeared, his surprise only deepened until he couldn't help but blurt out:

—"Could it be that every last one of them will pass?!"

Lu Shulong smiled faintly:

—"Why, Young Master Jian? Were you hoping someone would fall?"

—"Of course not! I'm merely marveling at how remarkable these children are,"-Jian Nuo said.-"To educate mortals lacking spirit roots to such a caliber... the Heavenly Spirit Sect is truly extraordinary."

These words were by no means mere polite flattery.

Although no one had fallen so far, waiting until someone fell would be far too late. Thus, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had deployed disciples to spread a defensive safety net near the Ascension Stairway.

This was far easier said than done.

Despite hanging isolated in the empty sky looking like anyone could just fly up, in truth anyone *could* fly up—yet unless one climbed from the very first step, the effort was completely in vain. One might step onto a staircase suspended high in the heavens, only to climb thousands of steps and find oneself standing on the lowest tier. Furthermore, once someone tumbled off the Ascension Stairway, it was treated as a failed trial run, requiring them to start all over from the beginning.

As for how to survive a fall from the Ascension Stairway? That was basically a [matter of pure metaphysics](# "Literal CN: 玄学问题 (xuánxué wèntí) — a metaphysical gamble / pure RNG").

Knowing how to fly was entirely useless.

The Ascension Stairway treating all living beings equally didn't merely mean anyone could attempt the climb—it also meant the fate awaiting anyone who fell off was virtually identical. Forget cultivators who had learned to fly through cultivation arts; even a demon beast born with wings would inevitably enter an anomalous state of gravitational disarray upon tumbling off the stairway, rendered completely incapable of flight.

What was worse, mutations occurred during the descent.

While most victims were pulverized into meat paste so mangled that not even the remnants of a corpse could be identified, historical records documented wilder outcomes: some fell and morphed into packs of monsters; some plunged down and transformed into mountain ranges; some shattered into severed chunks that came back to life once pieced together; others landed completely unscathed. The luckiest of all plummeted straight onto a lower flight of stairs, able to resume their trial.

The aftermath of a fall seemed to have nothing to do with cultivation realm—it came down strictly to luck.

Of course, Zhou Hui wasn't about to let his academy students gamble on cosmic RNG.

As the master of the Ascension Stairway, after studying its underlying rules, he discovered that so long as anyone tumbling off didn't hit the ground—if they were intercepted in midair—the mutation could be prevented entirely.

Yet waiting directly underneath that particular section of the stairway was strictly out of the question.

A peculiar restriction enveloped the perimeter of the Ascension Stairway. If someone got too close, whether seeking to assist or protect, they would be affected by the barrier and dragged into the trial themselves.

—Naturally so; otherwise, the cultivation world could have opened [guaranteed-admission ascension concierge services](# "Literal CN: 保送升仙服务 — guaranteed recommendation/fast-track service for ascension, parodying China's university guaranteed admission quotas (保送)").

Conversely, stationed too far outside the restricted perimeter, trying to catch a falling object across such a vast expanse was practically like playing a pinball game—and blind pinball at that, where the ball was completely invisible. It placed an absurd demand on flight speed and reflex timing that ordinary Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples couldn't possibly manage.

Therefore, the disciples wove a gargantuan surveillance-and-defense net out of spiritual energy in midair. Endowed with the sticky, decelerating properties of a spiderweb, it could pinpoint the exact coordinates of any falling student the instant they slipped, while simultaneously arresting their descent speed so rescue personnel could intervene in time.

In Jian Nuo's eyes, this solution was hardly easier than playing pinball.

After all, maintaining a stable spiritual energy net was no small feat. Not only did the cultivators weaving it need to maintain a continuous spiritual output, but they also required extraordinary tacit coordination to keep the network's energy evenly balanced.

To this, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples responded: *How could anyone rely on mere gut feeling?*

They crafted a swarm of spiritual energy detectors operable even at the Qi Condensation stage and dispersed them across the sky, monitored by a central magical apparatus. The remaining disciples simply manned their assigned nodes and inputted a fixed quota of spiritual energy according to specification. If anyone ran low on spiritual energy, they even rotated shifts!

At this very moment, Jian Nuo was stationed right at the central monitoring hub. His "function" was to observe spiritual fluctuations around the Ascension Stairway, perform real-time calculations, and calibrate the data parameters across the network nodes.

[...This is completely different from the exam I imagined!]

Jian Nuo had been [utterly dumbfounded](# "Literal CN: 彻底麻瓜了 (chèdǐ máguā le) — totally stunned / turned into a Muggle") on the spot.

Yet Lu Shulong had transformed into a flesh-and-blood dragon calculator—though Jian Nuo had no idea what a "calculator" was; in truth, if they had calculators, he wouldn't need to crunch numbers here either—rattling off numerical values at breakneck speed.

And it wasn't just Lu Shulong. Even ordinary disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were doing the exact same thing.

Only then did Jian Nuo realize just how reckless he had been in issuing a "challenge" to Lu Shulong!

Challenging an opponent without even knowing what the Heavenly Spirit Sect primarily specialized in was truly as foolish as it got.

[Still, why arithmetic?!]

[The Heavenly Spirit Sect isn't a divination sect, is it?]

Jian Nuo wondered in bewilderment.

As an ardent [fanboy](# "Literal CN: 迷弟 (mídì) — fanboy / devoted admirer") of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Jian Nuo had naturally conducted an exhaustive investigation into the Immortal Venerable's cultivation history.

Rumor had it that when the Untiring Immortal Venerable first made a name for himself, he was a sword cultivator—of the thoroughly unorthodox variety.

It couldn't be helped; Zhou Hui had started out destitute with zero background. Because his spirit roots were so wretched, no sect was willing to accept him, and he naturally possessed no funds to purchase ingredients for alchemy or artifact refinement. He had no choice but to carry a battered sword and surreptitiously learn techniques wherever he could. In this scrappy manner, he clawed his way up to the Golden Core peak. After clinching unexpected victories in several sect martial tournaments, he was schemed against and exiled to the Abyssal Ocean of the Central Domain. Only after deciphering the Heavenly Book did he transition into a spell cultivator.

Consequently, the Heavenly Spirit Sect primarily focused on spell cultivation plus sword cultivation.

Jian Nuo had assumed that whether he challenged Lu Shulong in swordsmanship or spellcraft, he would hold a fair degree of confidence. Never did he anticipate being tripped up by mathematical treatises.

—"To achieve such staggering results using merely simple magical apparatuses... I am thoroughly outmatched,"-Jian Nuo sighed.

Lu Shulong was taken aback for a moment.

He hadn't expected Jian Nuo to "concede defeat" so readily.

In truth, Lu Shulong had prepared himself for Jian Nuo to kick up a fuss about unfairness and demand a different competition.

—"Young Master Jian, why did you want to compete against me in the first place?"-Lu Shulong asked.

—"Didn't I tell you earlier? I wanted to gauge the disparity between myself and the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect..."-Jian Nuo began.

"..."

"..."

Stared down by Lu Shulong's pale golden pupils, Jian Nuo felt inexplicably guilty.

—"Fine, I was being an idiot,"-Jian Nuo admitted.-"Because the ancestors of the Qingyang Clan once forged a covenant with the True Dragons, when I heard Qingyu had joined the Untiring Immortal Venerable's tutelage—even if only temporarily—I worried that she and you might... Wait, Lord Shulong, where are you going?!"

[...I wasted my breath on such idiotic nonsense.]

[How did I never notice before that this guy is a total lovebrain?]

Lu Shulong couldn't be bothered with Jian Nuo. Clutching his data sheets, he walked off to consult his fellow disciples, asking how many students had yet to test their spirit roots and roughly when the trials would conclude.

The disciples were also in the midst of discussing this:

[—"How many are left?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Almost done—only five or six more. Once the Second Academy finishes, it'll be about wrapped up."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

—"Why is the Second Academy at the very end?"-Jing Chenjun asked.-"By the way, hasn't it reached Eleventh Lady yet?"

[—"Jing Chenjun, didn't your academy's Eleventh Lady already test first?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Why do you care so much about the Second Academy's Eleventh Lady, anyway?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

—"Can't you people think along normal lines for once?"-Jing Chenjun retorted.-"What if your baseless rumors cause trouble for the young lady?! Besides, I'm keeping an eye on her because her marks pose a direct threat to our academy's Eleventh Lady... In this final exam, if we look at total academy scores, the two Eleventh Ladies took first and second place, didn't they?"

—"That is true,"-another instructor chimed in.-"Though I don't believe they can be directly compared. Their fortes lie in completely different fields."

—"It's actually quite fascinating,"-another remarked.-"Eleventh Lady Qiao from a peasant farming household excels at [STEM subjects](# "Literal CN: 理科 (lǐkē) — science and engineering disciplines / STEM subjects"), whereas Eleventh Lady Mei from an aristocratic background is vastly superior in combat."

—"Not necessarily,"-someone countered.-"Their other subjects are actually top-tier as well. Neither has any noticeable shortcomings."

"..."

The instructors began debating their students' respective aptitudes.

Having unexpectedly overheard the answer he wanted, Lu Shulong wasn't planning on interrupting them. Yet the moment he turned his head, Jian Nuo's oversized face suddenly loomed directly behind him.

Startled, Lu Shulong sprang back three or four paces to put distance between them.

Jian Nuo barely noticed Lu Shulong's distaste. Instead, he stared in astonishment toward one of the disciples:

—"Jing Chenjun... 'Jing'? By the way, Lord Shulong, do you know that person's true identity?"

—"A prince of a mortal dynasty, isn't he?"-Lu Shulong replied.

—"You knew?!"-Jian Nuo's eyes widened in shock. He gaped at Lu Shulong.-"Then how could you still—"

—"Even if he is a prince, in your phrasing, he is merely a mortal,"-Lu Shulong said, giving Jian Nuo a puzzled look.-"I'm more surprised that you would bother remembering the face of a mortal."

—"For what it's worth, Sky City is required to send representatives to their sacrificial rites to Heaven. I attended once, and it just so happens I'm rather skilled at remembering faces,"-Jian Nuo answered circumspectly.

What Jian Nuo couldn't say aloud was that the Palace of Carefree Wandering had meddled deeply in that mortal dynasty, prompting him to investigate the imperial lineage.

On this matter, Jian Nuo had long harbored a headache.

Originally, the imperial clan of this mortal dynasty was of Central Domain descent. Back before the God-Demon War, they had been escorted north to the Northern Domain by the ancestor of the Jian Clan. Consequently, those mortals revered the Jian Clan with immense deference; even their earliest sacrificial rites were established purely to express gratitude to the Jian Clan, having nothing whatsoever to do with upper-realm True Immortals.

However, the Jian Clan Ancestor believed cultivators should not excessively meddle in mortal affairs, so he never paid them much mind.

By the time Jian Nuo took notice, the mortal dynasty was already riddled with interference from various cultivation sects. Carefree Palace's clandestine presence among them wasn't even particularly prominent.

Take Jing Chenjun's "running away from home," for instance—Jian Nuo strongly suspected it was the collaborative "handiwork" of several cultivation sects. Because they wanted to prop up a successor more suited to their tastes, beguiling Jing Chenjun into an obsession with seeking immortality and then staging a fatal mishap was as easy as turning one's palm.

In fact, the imperial court currently believed Jing Chenjun was long dead.

Never did Jian Nuo expect this high-ranking imperial heir to be alive and well in the Heavenly Spirit Sect—and living quite comfortably, by the looks of it!

—"Is he also studying at the White Jade Capital Academy?"-Jian Nuo asked.

—"No, he is an official disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Lu Shulong replied.-"And he studies quite well. You can tell simply by seeing him stationed here—he is actually an instructor for the White Jade Capital academies."

—"But..."

Jian Nuo opened his mouth to say something, but abruptly fell silent, his gaze snapping upward.

Not just Jian Nuo—everyone else raised their heads, looking into the sky above.

At this moment, this group was below holding the safety net, which naturally meant they were looking at the underside of the Ascension Stairway. The vantage point was terrible, far inferior to the city ramparts. While they could still observe the students' performance, it was exceedingly distant and blurred.

However, when a trial-taker possessed formidable strength, large-scale phenomena would manifest.

Jian Nuo, for example, had manifested palaces beneath his footsteps.

Of course, for mortal students devoid of spiritual energy, achieving such a feat was virtually impossible.

The reason it was termed "virtually" was because under specific circumstances, there were always "exceptions." And right now, that "exception" had arrived.

First came an excruciating, tooth-grating screech of grinding metal from high atop the Ascension Stairway. The sound swelled steadily into the clanking of interlocking gears and the sharp impact of metal plates. Then, a titanic metallic construct hung suspended upside down from the apex of the Ascension Stairway, as if intent on altering the stairway's trajectory, unfurling toward the far horizon into a colossal floating metal island.

[—"What is that?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Is that Eleventh Lady Qiao?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Still, isn't this a bit too exaggerated..."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

—"No, it's not an exaggeration at all,"-an instructor exclaimed.-"That child's talent in mechanical engineering is terrifyingly high! My teaching syllabus couldn't even keep pace with her. Her current textbooks are advanced upper-grade volumes she's been teaching herself!"

[—"Do you mean to say she might enter the Dao through machinery?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Instructor")

—"Well, as for that..."-the instructor from the Second Academy made a conflicted face.-"Eleventh Lady Qiao is exceptional in every regard, but in my assessment, her mental state isn't particularly stable. She is nowhere near the threshold of 'Entering the Dao.'"

As if corroborating his words, accompanied by the deafening roar of machinery, the Ascension Stairway jolted violently. Eleventh Lady Qiao's body swayed precariously, dangling like a tiny insect on a thread of spider silk, looking as though she might plummet at any second.

No—something had already fallen.

Several cogs snapped loose, tumbling downward.

Witnessing this, every instructor tensed up instantly.

—"What are you standing around staring for?!"-shedding his usual soft-spoken demeanor, Lu Shulong glared with wide eyes.-"Prepare to catch her!"

[—"Yes!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

The disciples scrambled to their respective stations in a flurry. Although the spiritual net had remained operational all along, no one had actually plummeted off yet, leaving everyone on edge about its real-world efficacy.

High above, terror flashed across Eleventh Lady Qiao's face. She quickened her pace, desperate to clear the trial as quickly as possible. Yet the moment her movements sped up, the floating metal island beneath her disintegrated even faster. Even the Ascension Stairway began collapsing alongside the crumbling island.

Of course, the Ascension Stairway hadn't genuinely collapsed—this was merely an "illusory phantasm" generated because Eleventh Lady Qiao failed to meet some criteria of the exam. The problem was that this "phantasm" was fully capable of pitching a person straight into the void.

Despair washed over Eleventh Lady Qiao's face. Flailing desperately, she reached out, trying to find something to cling to on a handrail-less staircase to stabilize her footing.

And she truly did catch hold.

Multiple hands reached out in unison, seizing Eleventh Lady Qiao's arms and yanking her forward.

Eleventh Lady Qiao didn't squander the lifeline. Steel resolve hardening in her eyes, she deftly planted her foot upon a fragment of falling metal before it could drop, leaping through the peril to land safely upon the upper tier of the Ascension Stairway.

[—"She passed..."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Instructor")

[—"She passed!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Instructor")

[—"Ah, she made it through!!!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

The instructors watching below erupted into cheers, more thrilled than if they had passed an exam themselves.

Amidst the excitement, someone inquired with scholarly curiosity:

[—"Can this trial be passed with help from companions?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Presumably so? It's said that fellow challengers on the Ascension Stairway are permitted to assist one another."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Doesn't that mean someone could just carry you up?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"You're welcome to try. Given that different flights of the Ascension Stairway feature different trials, who knows whether the companion helping you now might become part of your trial later on."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"True enough. I've heard the Ascension Stairway tests human nature, including sowing doubts about kinship and friendship. If a friend is right beside you, the trial would only become harsher and more terrifying!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Fortunately, we're only testing spirit roots here. By the way, Eleventh Lady Qiao has a Gold Spirit Root. I never knew Gold Spirit Roots possessed such traits! Then again, Senior Brother Qu is also immensely formidable and brilliant—does the Gold attribute grant some special innate buff?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Ah, I'm also a Gold Spirit Root..."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Keep dreaming."](# "Literal CN: 你在想P吃 (nǐ zài xiǎng P chī) — internet slang for 'you're daydreaming / wishing for the impossible'")

The crowd buzzed with lively chatter.

This time, Jing Chenjun did not join in their discussion. He merely caressed the imperial jade seal inside his pocket.

He did not draw it out, but he already knew that with Eleventh Lady Qiao passing the trial, the carved characters upon his jade seal had undoubtedly grown brighter. He was one step closer to gathering his [Human Ministers](# "Literal CN: 人臣 (rénchén) — ministers of the human realm / imperial subjects").

This was the true reason Jing Chenjun cared so deeply about Eleventh Lady Qiao's test.

Yet seeing Eleventh Lady Qiao actually pass the trial brought him none of the anticipated joy.

If anything, watching the metallic floating island she had constructed filled him with sudden dread:

He was inferior to her.

He, an imperial prince, was inferior to a peasant village girl.

This was not something he could simply pretend not to notice by shutting his eyes. Jing Chenjun had also undergone the Ascension Stairway's spirit root assessment. Although he had scraped a passing mark, he had not walked as steadily as Eleventh Lady Mei, nor had he unleashed an awe-inspiring spectacle like Eleventh Lady Qiao.

Without a direct comparison, Jing Chenjun could have continued deceiving himself. But after witnessing the scene just now, doubt began creeping into his heart:

[What truly constitutes the standard of a Human Emperor?]

[Devoid of spirit roots, inferior in capability even to my own students... do I truly possess the qualifications to become the Human Emperor? Or is having this bloodline enough on its own?]

Jing Chenjun stared blankly into space, lost in thought.

He was so consumed by apprehension that he failed to notice two pairs of eyes observing him.

—"He is beginning to waver,"-Jian Nuo observed coolly, before asking,-"Did your sect accept him as a disciple hoping to leverage Jing Chenjun's imperial pedigree to roll out the White Jade Capital Academy's educational model?"

Lu Shulong found Young Master Jian's sharp perception somewhat irritating. For an insufferable lovebrain, he certainly stuck his nose into far too many matters.

—"Then he is ill-suited... No, more accurately, mortal monarchs are fundamentally ill-suited. They will never welcome anything that threatens their imperial reign,"-Jian Nuo said.-"From that standpoint, partnering directly with major cultivation sects or our Sky City is vastly superior."

—"It would naturally be wonderful if your faction is willing. But giving up now is a bit premature, isn't it? Much like that young maiden just now, his trial has only just begun,"-Lu Shulong said.

Besides, when it came to treating the mortal populace, was there any fundamental difference between high-and-mighty sects like Sky City and a mortal emperor?

---

—"Everything has only just begun,"-Zhou Hui murmured softly.

He reached into the air and grasped something. When he opened his hand, a metallic gear rested in his palm.

A cog originating from Eleventh Lady Qiao.

Just as the vast majority of Eleventh Lady Mei's spiritual feedback had flowed toward Shen Qingqian—the creator of the Blood Demon Art who exerted the deepest influence upon her—the spiritual energy generated by Eleventh Lady Qiao's mechanical aptitude flowed predominantly toward Zhou Hui, who had compiled the engineering textbooks.

Yet Zhou Hui didn't feel this spiritual energy rightfully belonged to him. Rather, it belonged to certain figures from another world.

Zhou Hui did not absorb this spiritual energy. Instead, he condensed the wisps into a solid cluster, manifesting it into physical form, and set it upon his low desk.

Spotting this, the Little Black Lamb poked its head out from beneath the desk, its bead-like eyes rolling in their sockets as they fixated hungrily on the ball of spiritual energy.

—"No eating. That isn't for you,"-Zhou Hui warned.

—"As if I'd eat that! Sure, it smells delicious, but an appetizer that tiny wouldn't even [get stuck between my teeth](# "Literal CN: 塞牙缝都不够 (sāi yáfèng dōu bùgòu) — not even enough to fill the gap between teeth / a measly morsel"),"-the Little Black Lamb scoffed dismissively.

His retort would have sounded far more convincing had he not let out an audible slurping sound.

Zhou Hui stared at him for a quiet moment, then retrieved a storage pouch and packed the spiritual energy away.

The Little Black Lamb clicked his tongue in disappointment and flopped back beneath the desk.

To be precise, he knelt before the desk, resting his head comfortably upon Zhou Hui's lap.

When Qu Qianxing entered escorting Shen Qingqian and Bai Wubin, this scene of "wanton decadence" from his Eldest Senior Brother was the first thing that greeted his eyes.

—"...Master, precisely as you foresaw, numerous senior titans have shown immense interest, requesting to tour White Jade Capital Academy's classes. Several have even placed orders for the academy's textbooks,"-Qu Qianxing reported.-"However, a few seem rather dissatisfied."

—"No matter. There is no need to force those who are unwilling; let them figure it out on their own,"-Zhou Hui said mildly.

Indeed, they would come around sooner or later.

They might scoff now, but once neighboring sects upgraded their prospective student pool by a full tier and recruited vastly superior disciples, those stubborn elders would be forced to adapt whether they understood the logic or not.

Once the [involution](# "Literal CN: 卷起来 (juǎn qǐlái) — hyper-competitive rat race / academic arms race") began, there was no stopping it.

Qu Qianxing understood this principle well. Nodding in agreement, he stepped aside, revealing a thoroughly dazed Shen Qingqian.

—"Imm-Immortal Venerable, is it truly wise to disseminate the Blood Demon Art so freely like this?"-Shen Qingqian stammered.

She felt as though she were floating in a dream.

Leaving aside the boost to her cultivation, every single student today brought a share of karmic merit. Shen Qingqian felt that even if she [lay flat](# "Literal CN: 躺着 (tǎngzhe) — lie flat / completely slack off") and never practiced cultivation again, the sheer passive influx of karmic merit would suffice to propel her straight to the Mahayana realm.

And now, multiple major sects were actually preparing to adopt her curriculum!

—"What could be unwise about it? Your technique is fitting, and therefore it is worthy,"-Zhou Hui replied.

Hearing this, Shen Qingqian felt dizzier than ever.

It felt as though she might ascend to heaven the very next second.

Watching this unfold, Bai Wubin felt a sense of eerie déjà vu. Back when the Untiring Immortal Venerable persuaded Shen Qingqian to compile the textbook, he had encouraged her in this exact same manner.

Sure enough, the Untiring Immortal Venerable continued:

—"To maximize this impact, you would do well to draft an academic paper immediately elucidating your cultivation technique."

"!" Shen Qingqian froze solid.

She crashed headfirst from the clouds back down to reality.

Yet before Shen Qingqian could muster a word, the Untiring Immortal Venerable turned his gaze toward Bai Wubin:

—"As your master's eldest disciple, you should write one as well. Consider it building promotional momentum."

—"...This junior thanks the Immortal Venerable for your guidance,"-Bai Wubin said with a wry smile, discreetly tugging at the sleeve of Shen Qingqian, whose entire face screamed visceral rejection.

Was this something they could actually refuse?

The Untiring Immortal Venerable had paved the road for Blood Demon Hall to this extent; refusing now would be [utterly oblivious to social cues](# "Literal CN: 不会看眼色 (bù huì kàn yǎnsè) — unable to read the room / lack tact").

—"Master, having this academic paper will serve to vindicate yourself and your technique! So long as you publish it, no one in the world will ever dare look down upon you again!"-Bai Wubin urged in a hushed whisper.-"Even if only for the development of our sect, you must write it!"

—"That's exactly what I don't understand! Why does solving every single problem in this world turn into writing an academic paper?!"-Shen Qingqian cried out in tragic, furious despair.
