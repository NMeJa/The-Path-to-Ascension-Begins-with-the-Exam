# Chapter 129: Ten Thousand Years Ago (第129章 万年前)

[← Previous Chapter](128_Chapter_128.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](130_Chapter_130.md)

[—"Ying."](# "Speaker: Little Black Lamb (Yuwen Xuan)")

While the Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses clashed in battle over on that side, the little black lamb on this side widened his eyes. Poking his head out from beneath Zhou Hui's elbow, he extended his snout and sniffed vigorously in that direction.

He wasn't actually a lamb, after all, but a Taotie.

As an ancient calamity beast, he was exceptionally sensitive to conflict—especially high-energy clashes between supreme cultivators of such caliber.

The black lamb (strikethrough) Taotie almost instinctively spread his divine sense outward like tentacles, seeking the source of the skirmish (food).

Unfortunately, whether due to his pitifully low cultivation or some other cause, no sooner had the Taotie's divine sense extended several dozen li beyond the Ascension Stairway than it collided with something indistinct—like striking a transparent wall—unable to reach any further.

Instead, Zhou Hui patted him on the head:

—"Don't drip your drool on my sleeve,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Master, perhaps I should lead Eldest Senior Brother instead!"-Ran Deng pleaded, unable to watch any longer.

Ever since a moment ago, Zhou Hui had been carrying the little black lamb in his arms as he climbed.

It couldn't be helped. The little black lamb's body was far too diminutive; having to hop upward step by step made his pace excruciatingly sluggish, leaving Zhou Hui no choice but to scoop him up.

Ran Deng mused that it was only because she was the one present that the atmosphere remained so remarkably tranquil. Had it been Qu Qianxing, Mo Xi, or even Guan Ye and Lu Shulong, they would have chucked this wretched sheep off the Ascension Stairway ages ago.

Yet the ungrateful little black lamb actually protested at her:

—"Ying, ying!"-the little black lamb bleated ungratefully.

[Eldest Senior Brother, you know how to speak human words! What are you doing putting on an act of playing cute with 'ying ying ying'?!]

Ran Deng rolled her eyes.

Slurping back a mouthful of drool, Yuwen Xuan indeed opened his mouth. Craftily, he picked a topic Ran Deng clearly had neither the sensory range to perceive nor the standing to interrupt:

—"Master, Master, why did they start fighting over there?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

—"No idea,"-Zhou Hui replied disinterestedly.

Though he said so, his pace quickened slightly once again.

Zhou Hui had naturally sensed those Tribulation Transcendence titans—far earlier than the black lamb, right from the very beginning.

After all, this realm held only a handful of such titans. In Zhou Hui's eyes, their surges of spiritual power were as conspicuous as the Heavenly Chasm Mountains; rocketing through the sky, they were like whole mountain ranges hurtling through the air, making it far more difficult *not* to notice them.

Yet this was only natural.

Putting himself in their shoes, had Zhou Hui suddenly seen the Ascension Stairway fully repaired, standing so prominently at the very center of the world, he too would have come over to inspect it.

Zhou Hui had no intention of turning visitors away.

In fact, depending on who arrived, he had several different research topics he wished to discuss with them. From the look of things, aside from the one in the west whose mind was shattered and the one from Carefree Palace who perpetually [hid his head while showing no tail](# "Literal CN: 藏头不露尾 (cáng tóu bù lù wěi) — secretive, elusive, and never showing his true colors"), virtually everyone else seemed to have assembled.

Yet among them lay a single exception.

Goddess Renran.

Indeed: Zhou Hui was wary of Goddess Renran.

And it wasn't merely Zhou Hui; when it came to the matter of ascension, other high-ranking cultivators held a comparable attitude.

Make no mistake: it wasn't that Zhou Hui had uncovered some earth-shattering conspiracy of hers, nor was there anything fishy about Goddess Renran's historic sacrifice, much less an urge among this realm's cultivators to be ungrateful. Rather, the goddess's stance toward ascension and exploring the Abyss was simply too glaringly apparent:

Her Ladyship believed exploring the Abyss might invite catastrophe, while ascension should simply [follow natural selection](# "Literal CN: 顺应天择 (shùnyìng tiānzé) — adapt to nature's choice / leave it to heaven's will")—one should never do anything superfluous.

As a result, cultivators universally felt that when it came to something like the Ascension Stairway, the goddess would only prove an obstacle.

In truth, setting aside ascension, everyone held a reasonably high opinion of her.

Even Zhou Hui had gone to see Goddess Renran in his youth.

—She was a genuine, bona fide True Immortal, after all. What cultivator aspiring toward ascension wouldn't wish to approach Goddess Renran, learn the secrets of ascension from her, or perhaps even be granted an ascension manual?

Nor was Goddess Renran aloof. She put on airs, naturally, but so long as someone caught her eye, securing an audience was fairly straightforward. She was quite willing to offer guidance and would happily reminisce about her ancestral home.

Back then, Zhou Hui had accompanied Jian Haoge to see her (though Jian Haoge vehemently insisted it was a pure coincidence! A pure coincidence!).

Yet after meeting the goddess, Zhou Hui had felt profoundly disappointed.

How to describe Her Ladyship the Goddess? She struck one as extraordinarily...

Shallow.

Yes, applying such a word to a lady who had made monumental sacrifices to end the war felt rather improper, yet Zhou Hui found the sensation difficult to characterize otherwise. If forced to draw a comparison, she felt very much like... a volunteer from a developed nation sent to provide aid to an underdeveloped region.

Her intentions were pure, benevolent, and self-sacrificing; she had truly rendered immense sacrifices for this world. Yet the technologies and funding she carried bore little real connection to her own faculties. She had merely received an education in another world where she [knew the what without knowing the why](# "Literal CN: 知其然不知其所以然 (zhī qí rán bù zhī qí suǒyǐ rán) — knowing what happens without understanding the underlying principles"), and driven by certain personal biases—such as an ardent devotion to environmentalism—she would strictly insist that the underdeveloped locals would rather starve to death than be permitted to use certain technologies, even if they had developed them entirely on their own.

—Looking back now, Zhou Hui even harbored slight doubts regarding the true intentions of the 'organization' behind this volunteer.

Zhou Hui had no desire to waste words with an 'immortal' of this sort.

He resolved to reach the summit of the Ascension Stairway before she could arrive.

Fortunately, he was already not far from the top.

Ran Deng panted as she trailed behind Zhou Hui. It wasn't that her physical stamina was lacking—as a Golden Core cultivator, no matter how chronically lazy she was, her physical resilience was beyond anything a mortal could fathom. It was simply that after following Master for so many consecutive days while simultaneously using her soul flame to refine the palace lantern, Ran Deng was nearing her absolute limit. Raising her head to follow Master's silhouette, she suddenly let out a delighted cry:

—"Master, there's a door up there! Are we almost at the top?"-Ran Deng called out in surprise.

[—"A door?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

Zhou Hui and the little black lamb both looked up.

In their vision, the stairs above simply vanished into the cloud sea, appearing utterly endless.

—"I see no door,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Hee, finally hallucinating, are we?"-the little black lamb gloated.-"Since you're out of steam, just stay right here and wait for us."

—"A hallucination?"-Ran Deng asked, bewildered.

She didn't think it was a hallucination in the slightest.

That door was brimming with spiritual energy; genuine, fragrant breezes wafted down from it, instantly re-energizing the exhausted Ran Deng. She had zero feeling that she was trapped in an illusion.

Yet even Master couldn't see it...

—"A-Ran, go up and take a look,"-Zhou Hui pondered aloud.-"Viewed optimistically, perhaps you have passed the trial."

[—"?!"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng & Yuwen Xuan")

Ran Deng and the little black lamb widened their eyes in unison.

Zhou Hui explained:

—"Due to White Jade Capital, it seems I was ineligible to participate in the Ascension Stairway's trial from the outset. As for your senior brother..."-Zhou Hui glanced at the black lamb lounging shamelessly in his embrace.-"Setting aside whether he even possesses the qualifications, having been carried all the way up like this, he can hardly be said to have climbed the Ascension Stairway under his own power. I fear he was disqualified long ago."

—"Ying?!"-The little black lamb wore an expression as though struck by a [bolt from the blue](# "Literal CN: 晴天霹雳 (qíngtiān pīlì) — thunderclap from a clear sky / completely unexpected shock").

[Does that mean I climbed all this way for nothing?!]

[Across the entire trek, the time I spent being carried was barely anything! Setting aside the stretches where Ran Deng couldn't stand the sight of me and hauled me by the scruff, Master only carried me for this tiny final bit! Why count me out?!]

[Then doesn't that mean I'll be the only one (sheep) left behind in—]

[Wait a second. Even if someone ascends, it's just Ran Deng alone? Master can't ascend either! Then whatever, all good.]

The little black lamb lazily flopped back into Zhou Hui's arms.

Ran Deng arrived at the very same realization.

—"Master, I haven't made any preparations to ascend,"-Ran Deng said with a crumpled face.

—"Rest assured, you won't be left on your own,"-Zhou Hui comforted her.

—"Master..."-Ran Deng tilted her face upward, only to feel a soft, fluffy bundle shoved straight into her hands.

Looking down, she found herself staring eye-to-eye with the little black lamb.

—"Take your Eldest Senior Brother up, push the door open, and see what the situation is,"-Zhou Hui said, casually imprinting a mark of his own spiritual energy onto his disciple.-"I will be watching over you as well."

—"Ying, ying!"-the little black lamb bleated in furious protest. [Why is it me again?!]

—"...Understood, Master,"-Ran Deng nodded, her expression hardening with resolve.

She knew this was no time to shrink back. Besides, Master would never send her into genuine peril. With Master's spiritual imprint anchored upon her, more than half of her anxieties vanished.

Clutching the thrashing little lamb, Ran Deng bypassed Zhou Hui.

Stepping onto the upper stairs, she arrived before that colossal door and pressed her hand against its surface.

Yet before she could exert any force, the door swung open without warning.

Accompanied by a colossal suction, Ran Deng flew uncontrollably toward the interior of the gate.

—"Ah!"-Ran Deng cried out, the soul flame between her brows trembling violently.

Instinctively squeezing her eyes shut, she circulated the entirety of her cultivation to resist the dragging force.

Although the little black lamb couldn't see the door, he too felt the pulling force. Biting down viciously on his junior sister's sleeve, he attempted to anchor Ran Deng with his tiny frame, only to be dragged along, plunging headlong in that direction.

At that precise instant, the mark from Zhou Hui upon Ran Deng erupted with dazzling luminescence. Moon-white brilliance illuminated the surrounding sea of clouds and lit up the interior of the grand gate, firmly anchoring Ran Deng's form in place.

When Ran Deng opened her eyes again, she found herself seemingly still standing on the Ascension Stairway, the little black lamb still clamped onto her arm.

Yet Ran Deng had no time to demand her Eldest Senior Brother let go.

For all around the Ascension Stairway, iridescent auspicious clouds and billowing demonic qi churned together in chaos. Soaring through the heavens were countless flying dharma treasures of every shape and size, piloted by Immortals, mortal cultivators, and demons of every variety.

Ran Deng watched as they fought in a frantic frenzy:

Cultivators battled demons, Immortals battled demons, cultivators battled Immortals, and even cultivators fought cultivators, Immortals fought Immortals, and demons fought demons—all tangled in a colossal, chaotic melee.

Among them, an exceptionally gigantic antlered demon beast happened to open its gaping maw right toward Ran Deng's location, biting down with savage force!

Ran Deng's eyes widened in sheer horror, only to see the demon beast's bloody maw pass clean through her body, striking exclusively against the Ascension Stairway.

No—the demon beast's intended target had been the Ascension Stairway all along. Unleashing a thunderous howl, it clamped its teeth down hard upon the heavenly stairs.

Under its savage chewing, the Ascension Stairway shuddered on the brink of collapse, fractures spiderwebbing along its surface.

—"No! Master worked so hard to repair it—"-Ran Deng cried, instinctively moving to summon her dharma artifact.

Just then, a voice rang out inside her mind:

—"Hey, calm down. That isn't the present Ascension Stairway."

Along with those words, something seemed to take a gentle bite inside Ran Deng's mind, devouring the anxious portion of her thoughts and restoring clarity to her consciousness. Only then did Ran Deng realize that her mind had been muddied by the chaotic spiritual currents around them.

Furthermore, her body was semi-transparent. Even the little black lamb clamped onto her arm was in a state of suspended time—frozen motionless, as though not entirely existing in this space-time continuum.

—"E-Eldest Senior Brother?"-Ran Deng asked.-"What is this..."

—"It's likely the Ascension Stairway's 'memory regression',"-Yuwen Xuan's voice resonated in her mind.-"This is probably the God-Demon Battlefield from back then."

—"The God-Demon Battlefield?"-Ran Deng echoed uneasily, lifting her gaze.

She happened to see the demon beast's bloody maw gaping wide toward her once more.

Letting out a reflexive shriek, Ran Deng bolted several steps upward.

Even if the demon beast couldn't harm her, the spectacle was simply too horrifyingly real.

Once she came to a halt, she asked in a panic:

—"But why am I here?"

—"The Ascension Stairway must still be frozen on the day it was destroyed!"-Yuwen Xuan replied.-"Its rules haven't been updated yet; it's still anchored in its original state."

—"Why do you know so much?!"-Ran Deng pressed. Yet taking in the foul, chaotic carnage around them, she promptly abandoned her interrogation.-"Never mind! More importantly, how do I get back?!"

If Master were the one standing here, he would probably be ecstatic.

This was primary source material for researching the God-Demon War!

If it were Zhou Hui conversing in her head right now, Ran Deng might have indulged him with some field research. But with Eldest Senior Brother lodged in her brain, Ran Deng dared not linger for another second.

She felt that if she didn't perish in the God-Demon War, her brains would end up eaten clean by this black sheep!

—"You can... wait,"-Yuwen Xuan began to give directions, only to halt abruptly.-"Junior Sister, run up a few more steps and hide inside that bank of cloud mist. Yes, right over there."

[Why?]

Ran Deng wondered, but obediently followed Yuwen Xuan's instructions nonetheless.

No sooner had Ran Deng concealed herself than a voice rang out:

—"Brother Bai, attacking like that is futile,"-accompanied by this voice, a cultivator riding a dragon flew over.-"Allow me!"

Demons and cultivators working together...?

Ran Deng gazed in bewilderment at the dragon-riding cultivator, finding his features oddly familiar—

*Wait, he looks strikingly like Qingyang Qingyu!*

The shape of his eyes and the curve of his lips were identical! Could he be an ancestor of the Qingyang clan?

*Yet aren't they descendants of the Sacred Lords of White Jade Capital? Why would they be 'colluding' with demons?*

Ran Deng puzzled.

She watched as that cultivator—suspected to be an ancestor of the Qingyang clan—patted the head of the dragon beneath him:

—"Brother Lu, I leave it to you."

—"Leave it to me!"-the dragon called "Brother Lu" bellowed. From its maw, it spewed torrents of azure flame.

The flames surged toward the Ascension Stairway, yet a golden protective barrier manifested around the stairway's perimeter, repelling the blaze.

—"This is the Ascension Stairway's true core,"-the suspected Qingyang ancestor explained to the demon who had assaulted the stairway first.-"This Ascension Stairway cannot be broken by ordinary destruction. In fact, when many ascended the stairway in antiquity, they endured not only its trials but also ambushes from bitter foes; the stairway treats external attacks as part of the ascension trial, putting on a show of appearing shattered, but unless its internal rules are damaged, it will automatically repair itself."

—"Tsk, what an utter nuisance,"-the demon snorted coldly.

—"Naturally. How else could it have survived from the primordial wilderness to the present day?"-the cultivator replied.

Saying so, he waved his wide sleeve. A shower of golden powder drifted into the flames spewed by the dragon, stoking the blaze into an inferno until the barrier enveloping the Ascension Stairway was scorched to the verge of vanishing.

The cultivator sighed with emotion:

—"Unable to rewrite the rules of the Ascension Stairway, we had no choice but to research for thousands of years to devise a method to temporarily disrupt its mechanism—back then, it was all thanks to the assistance of the Dragon Clan."

—"Thousands of years? You people are certainly [desperate to the bone](# "Literal CN: 够拼命的 (gòu pīnmìng de) — putting your lives on the line / risking everything")!"

—"Who would ever desire a pipe inserted into their flesh like a mosquito, constantly siphoning away blood and marrow..."-The cultivator let out a self-deprecating laugh. Suddenly, he tilted his head back, gazing upward:-"Wouldn't you agree, Lord True Immortal?"

Accompanying his words, Ran Deng sensed a brilliant, blinding radiance emanating from above.

Hurriedly shrinking deeper into the cloud mist, Ran Deng cautiously peeked upward.

She saw that at the summit of the Ascension Stairway stood the very same colossal gate she had just pushed open. Standing before the gate were several shimmering figures whose celestial glow made it instantly apparent that they were not merely Immortals, but of an exceptionally exalted echelon.

—"How utterly insolent,"-the leading True Immortal remarked.-"You speak as though we forced you to ascend."

—"Naturally, you never forced us. It was our own [cheap servility](# "Literal CN: 犯贱 (fànjiàn) — glutton for punishment / groveling self-debasement")—draining this world's heaven-and-earth spiritual qi and celestial treasures, all in the pathetic hope of seeking refuge in the Heavenly Court one day,"-the cultivator said with biting mockery.-"Therefore, now that we sever the Ascension Stairway ourselves and seal this world, ensuring we no longer cause trouble for the Heavenly Court—surely that is acceptable, no?"

—"...Naturally, it is,"-the True Immortal replied with an eerie smile.-"And there is no need for you to toil so bitterly. I can lend you a helping hand."

Hearing his words, the cultivator, the demon, and even the True Dragon ceased their attack and looked upward.

Through that grand gate emerged a grotesque, horrifyingly colossal sheep beast.

—"A Taotie!"-the cultivator exclaimed.

Yet that Taotie opened its cavernous bloody maw and, in a single gulp, first swallowed the True Immortal whole! Then, lunging downward, it bit straight into the Ascension Stairway below. The stairway crunched loudly between its fangs, sounding like someone chewing on an ice pop.

The cultivator had originally set out to destroy the Ascension Stairway, yet seeing this scene, he panicked for reasons unknown, shouting:

—"Stop! We cannot let it keep devouring! Eating like this will destroy the Heavenly Dao!"

—"It won't just destroy it; the Heavenly Dao will be eaten alive!"-the demon cried in horror.-"It has been ages since I last laid eyes on a fully grown Taotie... Is this its final state?!"

—"Final state?"

—"Starved to the absolute limit—forget devouring worlds, it's the kind that can devour itself!"

—"Then what do we do?!"

While the demon and cultivator were still debating, the Taotie abruptly ceased chewing the Ascension Stairway. Glinting, ravenous eyes turned downward, locking onto them.

In the next instant, the Taotie gaped wide, lunging straight toward the position where Ran Deng was hiding!

—"...Why me?!"-Ran Deng shrieked, bolting frantically from the cloud mist.

The demon and cultivator seemed unable to perceive Ran Deng, remaining completely oblivious. Yet the True Dragon's eyes widened as it stared right at her:

—"A person of later generations... Why do you carry the scent of my bloodline?!"-the dragon roared.

—"Waaah!"-Ran Deng shrieked in terror.

Ran Deng had no leisure to answer the dragon; she was scrambling for her life before the pursuing Taotie! Why on earth did she have to endure this?! She had only just resolved [not to be a salted fish](# "Literal CN: 不咸鱼了 (bù xiányú le) — giving up being a slacker / resolving to work hard"), yet suddenly being thrown into a deathmatch against a legendary calamity beast was [completely beyond the syllabus](# "Literal CN: 太超纲了 (tài chāogāng le) — wildly out-of-syllabus / far beyond test scope")!

Even Master's exam questions were never this absurd!

—"Eldest Senior Brother! How do I get out of here?! Tell me! Yuwen Xuan!"-Ran Deng screamed frantically.

When she cried out the name "Yuwen Xuan," the colossal Taotie paused for a fraction of a second. Yet immediately afterward, overcome by ravenous hunger, it resumed snapping its jaws at Ran Deng, as though she were some irresistible delicacy.

Amidst the Taotie's relentless chase, Ran Deng spotted a pathway winding upward toward the gate atop the Ascension Stairway.

—"Climb upward. So long as you ascend to the True Immortal Realm, you will be saved,"-a guiding voice whispered—sounding eerily like the voice of the True Immortal that had just been devoured.

Yet the True Dragon noticed as well. Unleashing tremendous draconic pressure, it roared to stop Ran Deng:

—"Do not go up! Do you wish for all our efforts up until now to be rendered in vain?!"

Ran Deng was plunged into utter confusion.

She vaguely realized she had been pulled into the great war of ten thousand years ago, and her decision seemed of paramount significance. Yet with a Taotie snapping at her heels, she had no space to think.

—"Climb up! But keep it crystal clear in your mind: where do you truly wish to 'ascend'?"-a third voice commanded.

It sounded somewhat like Yuwen Xuan, yet also like Zhou Hui.

Hearing this voice, Ran Deng hesitated no longer. She shot toward that grand gate at breakneck speed.

—"No!"-the True Dragon bellowed.

Startled by the dragon's roar, the cultivator and demon looked up in bewilderment. At the exact moment Ran Deng flew into the gate, their eyes finally caught sight of her:

[—"No!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Clan Ancestor & Antlered Demon")-they cried out in unison.

Yet their intervention came a step too late. Ran Deng had already charged through the radiant gate.

—"What was that? How could..."-The cultivator was utterly bewildered.

—"Has the 'passage' been re-established? Everything we worked for..."-the True Dragon uttered in despair.

—"No, wait. That girl didn't go to the True Immortal Realm,"-the demon said, surprisingly calm.

—"Not the True Immortal Realm? Then where?!"

—"It's..."

Before the demon could answer, a deafening roar erupted from the Ascension Stairway.

Snapping into several segments, the Ascension Stairway collapsed downward in ruin.

This time, both the cultivator and the True Dragon realized the truth:

—"It's the rules!"

—"Someone is establishing new rules for the Ascension Stairway!"

—"That girl went to the origin of the new rules!"

—"Wait—if someone can establish rules for the Ascension Stairway, could he modify this world's Heavenly Dao as well—"

Just as they were marveling at this, a wave of horrific pressure swept over them.

One human, one demon, and one dragon turned their heads to find the Taotie glaring down at them with predatory hunger.

Naturally. Their previous quarry was gone, and the Ascension Stairway was gone; the largest prey remaining in this space was now them.

Yet none of the three showed the slightest intention of fleeing. Instead, they exchanged glances:

—"We must dispose of this thing,"-the cultivator said.

—"Indeed. If the people of future generations have not given up on this world, we certainly cannot leave behind an irreparably ruined world for them,"-the demon agreed.

—"Whew. If this beast is left running loose, there won't be any future generation to speak of,"-the dragon exhaled.

***

Naturally, Ran Deng had no idea what transpired after her departure.

All she knew was that she had passed through that radiant gate, and upon emerging on the other side, the very first sight greeting her eyes was Master.

—"Master!"-Ran Deng cried, throwing her arms around Zhou Hui as if returning from another lifetime.

*Indeed, the true place of ascension should be by Master's side all along!*

Ran Deng was on the verge of bursting into tears once more.

Zhou Hui patted her head, smiling wryly:

—"Having already obtained an Immortal Seal, acting like such a spoiled child will hardly do,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"No matter what cultivation realm I reach, I am still Master's disciple... Immortal Seal?"-Ran Deng blinked, tilting her face up.

Zhou Hui waved his hand, conjuring a mirror before Ran Deng.

Ran Deng saw that the flame between her brows had altered its shape, transforming into a glowing, auspicious golden-red cloud that shimmered with divine light.

—"One tradition holds that the Golden Immortal Seal is the hallmark of an immortal. So long as you bear this mark, you require no bottleneck breakthroughs and no lightning tribulations; as long as you continue cultivating, you will naturally attain the power of a True Immortal,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"Though, your cultivation base is simply too low at present; otherwise, you would have inherited the might of a True Immortal right this moment."

—"Oh,"-Ran Deng murmured, touching her forehead with utter lack of interest.-"It's not like I care about becoming an immortal."

Especially after witnessing the scenes of the past, she harbored grave doubts regarding the nature of True Immortals.

—"Possessing strength is always a good thing. Besides, with this, your soul flame has grown vastly more potent,"-Zhou Hui said, attempting to cheer her up.

Yet hearing these words, Ran Deng felt an overwhelmingly ominous premonition.

Directing her divine sense inward, she peered at her soul flame. Sure enough, it had expanded countless times over! In comparison, the strand linked to the palace lantern was now merely a slender, wispy thread!

*BUT!*

*She was currently in the middle of refining her soul flame!*

*That original tiny wisp was already going to take nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine days. Now with this astronomical volume...*

Ran Deng's body swayed precariously, and she fainted backward on the spot.
