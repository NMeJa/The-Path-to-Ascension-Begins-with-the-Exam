# Chapter 27: Yearning (第27章 憧憬)

Whether Mo Bei was an idiot or not remained to be seen, but she certainly came with guaranteed [stat checks / meta strength](# "Literal CN: 强度 (qiángdù) — gaming slang for raw combat potency and tier-list viability").

In the cultivation world, the Extreme Yin Constitution was an intensely stigmatized physiological trait.

The prevailing consensus held that male cultivators cursed with this constitution were born sinister and deceitful, prone to qi deviation and demonic corruption; female cultivators with it were supposedly wanton, promiscuous, and incapable of staying domestic—yet made the most exquisite [cultivation furnaces](# "Literal CN: 炉鼎 (lúdǐng) — cultivators exploited as human vessels for dual-cultivation or energy extraction").

Conversely, setting aside whether one's temperament was actually tainted by their constitution, whether it was "succumbing to qi deviation" or being prized as a "top-tier cultivation furnace," both prerequisites demanded a substantially high cultivation base. Common practitioners who spent every waking hour panicking over whether they could even break through to Foundation Establishment or Golden Core had no qualifications to worry about such luxury problems.

Consequently, so long as a sword technique, cultivation art, or spell suited her, Mo Bei mastered it at blinding speed.

Had she not encountered a teacher like Zhou Hui, who placed near-maniacal emphasis on academic fundamentals—requiring his disciples to chew every single stance to pulp and digest it completely before moving on to the next (with the sole exception of Yuwen Xuan, who leveled up on autopilot even when left entirely to his own devices)—Mo Bei and Mo Xi's cultivation would never have lingered at merely the Foundation Establishment Middle Stage.

And Mo Bei's very first ~~[fansite master / site sister](# "Literal CN: 站姐（X） (zhànjiě) — modern idol fandom slang for a dedicated fansite admin who photographs and promotes an idol")~~ [X](# "Author's humorous strikeout note"), Yu Feifei, had a front-row seat to the terrifying progression of a true prodigy.

---

On the first day, she only thought Junior Sister Mo's sword forms were graceful and fluid—truly worthy of being Master's direct disciple. Even though she was young and had started learning sword routines relatively late compared to the sect's dedicated sword cultivators, her execution was still far more practiced than most. There was merely a trace of stiffness between the transitions, and as the flexible sword fluttered, the condensed droplets of moisture formed in sporadic, intermittent bursts.

On the second day, Junior Sister Mo's transitions were notably smoother than the day before. Although several glaring flaws remained, the forming droplets had already thickened considerably.

On the third day, the flaws between Junior Sister Mo's stances had dwindled, and the water droplets grew even denser.

On the fourth day...

On the fifth day...

On the sixth day, Junior Sister Mo's sword forms were virtually flawless. The water droplets had coalesced into a shimmering curtain of rain, cascading across the surrounding Snow-Glow Grass.

On the seventh day, Junior Sister Mo's sword routines appeared unchanged on the surface, yet the rain curtain fluctuated between torrents and drizzles, erratic and difficult to rein in.

On the eighth day, the sword forms still showed no outward change, nor did the rain curtain.

On the ninth day...

On the tenth day...

On the eleventh day, the subtle whistling of sword wind hummed within every strike; mastery of the grand completion stage seemed just around the corner...

---

Watching from the sidelines, Yu Feifei felt her heart leap into her throat.

What shocked her was not that Mo Bei showed signs of nearing grand completion within eleven days—while certainly extraordinary, for someone like Yu Feifei who knew Mo Bei's background, it was somewhat expected. What truly shook her was Mo Bei's sheer, relentless effort.

The Snow-Glow Grass in front of Mo Bei's cave abode was not merely a humble patch of lawn. To produce that breathtaking, magnificent spectacle, one had to claim an entire hillside so that gazing from afar, a rainbow-tinted mountain peak stood enveloped in drifting mist, conjuring the illusion of a celestial wonderland.

Watering this vast expanse of Snow-Glow Grass was no small undertaking.

Back when she watered them with spiritual buckets, it was somewhat manageable—Mo Bei could cast a wide-area spell to irrigate an entire slope at once. But now that she had switched to using her unfamiliar sword art, she drilled relentlessly from the [Mao Hour (5:00–7:00 AM)](# "Literal CN: 卯时 (mǎoshí) — dawn period from 5:00 to 7:00 AM in the traditional 12 two-hour Chinese watch system") all the way to noon. After that, she would meditate and review her cultivation mantras for four hours before immediately launching into her second round of 'watering.'

In other words, whenever Yu Feifei and the others came by to check on Mo Bei, nine times out of ten they found her furiously swinging her sword.

At first, everyone chattered and giggled about how breathtaking Junior Sister Mo's sword stances looked, debating which exact technique should be chosen for her game piece; some even lugged stone stools and chessboards up the slope to kill time while waiting.

Three or four days later, someone grew concerned about Junior Sister Mo's physical strain, and they began plotting ~~health maintenance~~ [skincare and preservation routines](# "Literal CN: 保养（X） — joke playing on skincare / beauty preservation for an idol vs. maintaining a vehicle") [X](# "Author's humorous strikeout note").

Five or six days in, as they waited for Junior Sister Mo to conclude her drills, the fooling around dwindled, replaced by sober, solemn spectators.

Seven or eight days in, disciples began analyzing technical sword mechanics among themselves. When Mo Bei finished her drills, instead of merely stepping forward to offer greetings and hand her tea, they approached like humble junior disciples seeking guidance from 'Junior Sister Mo' on sword practice.

On the eleventh day, someone arrived with their own sword and spiritual implements strapped to their back.

—"You can't be serious! You're going to practice right alongside her?!"-Yu Feifei nearly broke character on the spot.

—"But just sitting here watching her gives me such immense guilt!"-someone answered.

Clearly, more than one person shared the sentiment:
—"Exactly! With Junior Sister Mo's staggering talent, she doesn't waste a single second of her day. How are we supposed to sit around goofing off when we're nowhere near her level?!"

[—"I feel like if this keeps up, I won't have the face to call her Junior Sister anymore."](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"At this point, I don't feel ashamed to call her Senior Sister at all."](# "Speaker: Disciple")

The disciples let out heavy sighs. Yu Feifei clutched her chest, utterly unable to refute them.

Because deep down, she felt the exact same way.

—"Wait, do you people even remember why we came to watch Junior Sister Mo practice in the first place?!"-Yu Feifei pressed on valiantly.-"We came for the Immortal Chess tournament!"

—"Those two aren't mutually exclusive! Once practice wraps up here, we can still go back and play a couple of rounds."

—"I think understanding her sword forms better will help us pilot her in battle."

—"Right! I'm a formation cultivator. I never looked into the Flying Spirit Sword Art before, but after practicing a few stances here, I got a whole new perspective on how to crack formations in combat."

Her fellow 'club members' chimed in all at once, leaving Yu Feifei completely speechless.

[Terrifying!]

[The Heavenly Spirit Sect is absolutely terrifying!]

---

Back when Yu Feifei was in her mortal cultivation clan, she had already been an [involution champion](# "Literal CN: 能卷 (néng juǎn) — exceptionally skilled at cutthroat academic or workplace grinding"). That was precisely how she had stood out among her father's seventeen or eighteen sons and daughters to gain entry into the immortal sect. Later, she out-grinded the competition again to catch the eye of a director within the palace. Although her lowly birth dragged her down—causing her to be tossed into the Heavenly Spirit Sect as a planted nail—it still represented a rare turn of fortune.

—Indeed, Yu Feifei was no ordinary inner sect disciple. She was a [sleeper agent / undercover spy](# "Literal CN: 细作 (xìzuò) — undercover operative planted by Xiaoyao Palace") for the Palace of Carefree Wandering.

Years ago, when Zhou Hui flattened the Palace of Fallen Immortals in a fit of divine fury, the Palace of Carefree Wandering—the true headquarters operating behind the veil—scrambled to wash their hands of the mess in public: 'What on earth is a Fallen Immortal Palace? Never heard of it, nothing to do with us!' 'Perhaps a few rogue stewards hailed from our Carefree Palace, but they committed atrocities while [the sky was high and the emperor was far away](# "Literal CN: 天高皇帝远 (tiān gāo huángdì yuǎn) — beyond the reach of authority"); we had zero knowledge of their misdeeds!' 'We offer our deepest gratitude to the Untiring Immortal Venerable for exterminating evil and excising the cancerous tumor that leeched off our reputation!'

Yet beneath the surface, they were seething with indignation.

Helpless against a Tribulation Transcendence supreme powerhouse, all they could do was insert moles like Yu Feifei into the Heavenly Spirit Sect. For the time being, they hadn't tasked her with anything drastic—merely to give them advance warning before the grand elder went on his next rampage.

Upon arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yu Feifei discovered that this sect might lack many things, but its academic study atmosphere was unparalleled.

At the very top stood the towering paragon of the Untiring Immortal Venerable; below him, discounting the outlier Yuwen Xuan, everyone led by Second Senior Brother Qu Qianxing was entirely devoted to the Dao and studious to the bone. Factoring in the Library Pavilion open twenty-four hours a day and an open study culture where disciples freely observed and audited one another's cultivation, the overarching atmosphere made you feel as if failing to study diligently was a personal insult to eighteen generations of your ancestors.

Objectively speaking, the fact that the previous inner sect test escalated into a sect-wide Grand Unified Examination was by no means an accident.

This was standard operating procedure in the Heavenly Spirit Sect: whenever Zhou Hui taught his direct disciples or the inner sect a new learning methodology, it would sweep across the entire sect at blazing speed like [a single spark setting off a prairie fire](# "Literal CN: 星火燎原 (xīnghuǒ liáoyuán) — a small spark spreading rapidly into a massive movement"), plunging everyone into a collective frenzy of cutthroat grinding.

In such an environment, Yu Feifei should have taken to it like a fish to water, yet she felt nothing of the sort.

To her horror, she realized that her past reputation for 'excellence' had merely been built on the fact that everyone around her had been slacking off and dodging work. When every single disciple was [twisted like fried dough twists / hyper-involuted](# "Literal CN: 卷成麻花 (juǎn chéng máhuā) — modern Chinese slang for extreme, obsessive academic grinding"), her modest talent wasn't nearly enough to keep up!

Take the Grand Unified Examination, for instance.

When all the disciples began circulating past papers and memorizing syllabus topics, Yu Feifei had filled out her exam booklet with breezy confidence. But when the score rankings were posted, she found herself stuck squarely in the mediocre middle.

Worse still, before she could even conduct a self-critique on her testing errors, a secret missive from the Fallen Immortal Palace arrived out of the blue, demanding: "What on earth did Su Xin, son of the Plum-Bamboo Couple, experience while visiting the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"

At the time, Yu Feifei had been completely baffled:

[Who in the world is Su Xin?]

[What visit?]

[We were in the middle of a sect-wide exam! We didn't see anyone visiting!]

[Your so-called honored guest couldn't possibly have come to take our exams, could he?!]

Naturally, that reply failed to satisfy the Fallen Immortal Palace. First, her contact cursed her out; then her handler docked her monthly stipend; finally, her father sent an enraged letter threatening to sever their father-daughter relationship if she didn't diligently serve the palace.

Heh. As if that father had ever given a damn about their relationship before.

Yu Feifei tore the letter to shreds, but was nonetheless forced to lower her head and draft an earnest self-reflection, promising she would closely monitor all developments across the Heavenly Spirit Sect and report back thoroughly next time.

Fortunately, 'next time' didn't arrive so quickly.

Following the Grand Unified Examination, the Immortal Venerable seemed to finally realize the disciples needed to unwind, rolling out Immortal Chess and hosting a grand tournament around it.

On the surface, Yu Feifei acted passionately invested in the event—enthusiastically registering, joining discussions, and dropping heavy bets. In reality, every single night she was [hanging her hair from the beam and jabbing her thigh with an awl](# "Literal CN: 头悬梁锥刺股 — classic Chinese idiom for torturous, relentless all-night studying"), desperately grinding her cultivation behind closed doors.

However, her faith in Mo Bei was one hundred percent sincere.

After all, having also originated from the Fallen Immortal Palace (Carefree Palace), Yu Feifei's espionage duties included keeping tabs on Mo Bei and Mo Xi—Yu Feifei suspected the palace brass couldn't bear to write off such high-grade commodities and were biding their time to repossess them—which was how she knew about their Extreme Yin Constitutions.

In Yu Feifei's eyes, with such god-tier talent, Mo Bei was undeniably destined to be the strongest piece on the board.

Of course, if the pretext held up, using her proximity to Mo Bei to squeeze an expense account out of the Fallen Immortal Palace made it even better.

Whether for duty or personal gain, Yu Feifei went all-in on Mo Bei.

She just never anticipated this!

Who could have guessed that in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even an Extreme Yin Constitution worked this hard?!

Back in the Fallen Immortal Palace and Carefree Palace, those 'senior sisters' spent their days preening before mirrors or comparing clients, occasionally lamenting their tragic lot in life, never once believing that bitter cultivation could yield spiritual energy faster than bedding a few wealthy patrons.

Yet this Extreme Yin Constitution here drilled every single stance hundreds of times a day without the slightest hint of fatigue!

Who could witness such a spectacle without spiraling into acute academic anxiety?

At any rate, Yu Feifei had only slept for four hours last night, spending the rest of the night secretly drilling her spells.

Exasperatingly, she clearly wasn't the only one suffocating from anxiety; the other 'enthusiasts' who had supposedly been obsessed with chess could no longer sit still either.

[No! This cannot continue.]

Having exhausted her spiritual energy after a whole night of secret drilling, she simply lacked the stamina to grind alongside these maniacs.

Yu Feifei cleared her throat and put on a solemn face:
—"Did you all come here just to freeload off Junior Sister Mo's spiritual aura?! Don't forget, our true objective is assisting Junior Sister Mo in winning the tournament!"

—"But aside from pouring tea and handing her cloaks, there isn't much else we can do."

—"Even cheering on the sidelines feels like we're disturbing her sword drills."

—"Hmph, you people lack vision,"-Yu Feifei declared.-"Momentum is part of psychological warfare!"

—"What do you mean?"

—"If just watching Junior Sister Mo practice fills us with this much anxiety, what about her opponents? What about the players who didn't draft her piece?"-Yu Feifei pointed out.-"As long as we build an aura of Mo Bei's inevitable, crushing victory, we can psychologically steamroll the opposition!"

—"That makes so much sense! What should we do?"

—"Does anyone have an ocular recording treasure on them? First, we pick an angle to record Junior Sister Mo's sword forms. Oh, and if anyone has a temporal playback treasure, even better—we can splice together all of Junior Sister Mo's drills over the past few days into a compilation..."

Recalling the promotional marketing campaigns from the Carefree Palace auctions, Yu Feifei clumsily directed the other disciples into action.

A day later, when Mo Bei visited the Library Pavilion and saw a colossal holographic replay of her sword practice projected across the hall, she was so mortified she nearly tumbled off her flying sword—but that was a story for later.

---

The psychological pressure was supposed to land on Mo Bei's opponents.

—"How on earth did they come up with this? There are illusions of Junior Sister Mo literally everywhere,"-Yan Fei grumbled.

Stepping out of his cave abode, he looked up only to see the girl's expressionless face and lithe, graceful figure hovering overhead.

The projected sword swept out with such vicious crispness that even knowing it was an illusion, Yan Fei couldn't resist flinching to dodge.

Lately, these phantoms could frequently be spotted across the Library Pavilion and the Palace of Silence, plastered with banners reading "Junior Sister Mo Is the Strongest!" and "Fairy Mo Will Reign Supreme!" Yet the densest concentration was not in public squares, but directly outside the cave abodes of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's top sword cultivators—basically blasting the propaganda right into their faces.

During broad daylight it was tolerable, but stepping outside at night, you'd think it was either a vengeful ghost or an assassin. Just this morning at dawn, Yu Cong had stepped out to wash up and was startled so badly by a pop-up hologram that he nearly tumbled headfirst into his water vat.

As a fellow victim, Yu Cong showed zero sympathy for Yan Fei, instead mocking him:
—"Junior Sister Mo is one thing. If those holograms were all of you, that would be a genuine nightmare."

—"Can't argue with that,"-Yan Fei actually agreed.

Gazing at the Mo Bei projection nearest his cave, he mimicked her wrist flick into a sword bloom and couldn't help admiring:
—"Junior Sister Mo executed this stance so flawlessly. I never imagined it could produce this kind of effect."

—"Right?! If that were you, it'd be a [public execution](# "Literal CN: 公开处刑 (gōngkāi chùxíng) — internet meme for extreme public humiliation or being exposed on display"),"-Yu Cong chimed in.

His fingers itching, Yu Cong drew his blade and began gesturing on the spot:
—"Your strike is too hurried, which makes the transition to the next stance clunky."

—"But I don't want to abandon that opening,"-Yan Fei agonized.-"Even if it sacrifices fluidity later, seizing the initiative first in actual combat should be better, shouldn't it?"

—"Hard to say..."

The two sword cultivators stood right there engrossed in technical debate, leaving Yu Feifei—who had snuck over to inspect the 'enemy lines'—at a total loss for words.

She couldn't resist asking them:
—"Aren't you two afraid of losing?"

—"...Of course we are!"-Yan Fei shot Yu Feifei a strange look.

—"Then why haven't you given up?!"-Yu Feifei demanded.-"Or do you actually think you can win?"

—"No, this isn't about winning or losing. A sword cultivator could never pass up such an incredible opportunity,"-Yan Fei said, turning to Yu Cong.-"If you're terrified of losing, you'll never have the chance to improve, right?"

—"Exactly. The stronger the opponent, the sharper you're honed,"-Yu Cong nodded in firm agreement.-"I'm actually jealous that Ah-Fei gets to duel an opponent like that. I wonder if Junior Sister Mo would agree if we asked to spar on ordinary days?"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Yu Feifei")

[The cognitive faculties of these Heavenly Spirit Sect people are completely broken!]

In the Carefree Palace, no one was like this. Nobody would ever bust their ass for something doomed to fail.

Yu Feifei rubbed her temples in a pounding headache.

Right then, Yan Fei and Yu Cong locked their eyes directly onto Yu Feifei:
—"Are you the one projecting these illusions?"

—"What of it?"-Yu Feifei countered.-"Didn't you just say you refuse to give up?"

—"Refusing to give up is one thing, but blasting them right in front of our doors is crossing the line!"-Yu Cong complained.-"If we didn't know this had nothing to do with Junior Sister Mo, we'd practically end up hating her."

(Had he known the term [anti-fan](# "Literal CN: 黑粉 (hēifěn) — modern fandom slang for an anti-fan"), he would have had far more to grumble about).

—"Besides, Junior Sister Mo has no need for such cheap tricks,"-Yan Fei flicked his sword tip toward Yu Feifei.-"You're a contestant in the chess tournament, aren't you? These projections only make players who didn't pick Junior Sister Mo anxious. You aren't purely cheering for her—you're exploiting Junior Sister Mo's image to heap psychological pressure onto your own competitors!"

—"It's not against the rules!"-Yu Feifei took a step back.-"If you're dissatisfied, go report me!"

—"It's not that serious. In any case, petty tricks won't work! I will win!"-With righteous dignity, Yan Fei declared.-"Whether in swordsmanship or chess!"

With that, he sheathed his sword, executing a dashing turn alongside Yu Cong to depart.

Yu Feifei watched their departing backs with eyes wide in disbelief.

For some inexplicable reason, the corners of her mouth tugged upward; she even felt a strange prickle of anticipation.

[Wait, anticipation for what?]

[Anticipating defeat?]

[Mine? Or Junior Sister Mo's?]

Yu Feifei thought blankly, staring at their silhouettes.

Yet before the pair had taken more than a few paces, Yan Fei suddenly whipped around and called out:
—"By the way, do you have any extra copies of that giant portrait of Junior Sister Mo hanging in the Palace of Silence?"

—"...Ten spirit stones per copy,"-Yu Feifei answered numbly.

This man was beyond saving.

---

Yan Fei was indeed beyond saving.

Facing Mo Bei, he failed to survive even three moves before being defeated.

To be precise, every single one of Mo Bei's opponents was [one-shot](# "Literal CN: 秒杀 (miǎoshā) — insta-killed / eliminated in a split second"); some hadn't even registered what happened before they were knocked out.

The fact that Yan Fei managed to struggle against Mo Bei for a full minute was already an impressive feat.

In short, Mo Bei's overwhelming presence reduced the sword-cultivator bracket of the piece selection tournament into the most anticlimactic contest of all.

With this, Mo Bei solidified her undisputed title as the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Number One Sword Cultivator.

Her terrifying strength forced anyone who had previously harbored grievances against her to swallow their words whole.

Meanwhile, every single jade slip recording her holographic drills sold out completely afterward. If Mo Xi hadn't swooped in out of nowhere to extort a massive cut of the profits, the earnings would have funded the "Mo Bei Fan Club" for years to come.

---

[It's finally over!]

Mo Bei thought with the relief of a survivor crawling out of a disaster.

Sheathing her sword, she glanced up at the giant portrait of herself looming above the arena and felt her vision darken all over again.

Heaven only knew how she had survived these past few days!

With these giant portraits and holographic replays on endless loop, Mo Bei felt she could no longer survive in the Heavenly Spirit Sect. She had even submitted a formal petition to Master requesting a ten-year solitary seclusion—which was summarily rejected.

[Now that the tournament is over, they should finally take down these damned portraits, right?]

Mo Bei thought hopefully. Dodging the crowds rushing to congratulate her on her victory, she slipped into an empty pedestrian walkway to escape the Library Pavilion, only to spot someone sitting slumped on the staircase after a few steps.

Furthermore, it was the ringleader of her fanbase—Mo Bei seemed to recall her name was Yu Feifei?

Mo Bei instinctively spun around to flee, but seeing the girl's ashen, sickly complexion, she couldn't help halting her steps.

—"...Did you lose?"-Mo Bei ventured a guess.

She recalled that before the finals, this girl had rushed over to apologize, saying her own match coincided with Mo Bei's finals, so she wouldn't be able to come cheer.

At the time, Mo Bei had only felt profound relief.

To be honest, the main reason Mo Bei opted for one-shotting every opponent in record time was precisely because she couldn't bear the sight of a mob waving promotional banners for her—it was far too humiliating.

Right now, Yu Feifei possessed none of her bubbly enthusiasm from twenty minutes ago. Only now did she seem to notice Mo Bei, squeezing out a bitter smile:
—"Yeah. Lost. Brutally. To an opponent I previously provoked."

Indeed, the competition in the Immortal Chess division was vastly fiercer, and Yu Feifei hadn't lasted until the final rounds at all—given that she hadn't studied the game in depth and only approached it half-heartedly, her skill level wasn't high to begin with. She had been eliminated in the round of sixteen, losing to Yu Cong—who had piloted Yan Fei's piece!

Naturally, that was entirely Yu Feifei's own fault. Yet Mo Bei stared at her for a moment, her face deadpan as she spoke:
—"I'm sorry."

Yu Feifei flinched as if lashed by a whip, staring at Mo Bei in utter disbelief:
—"What on earth do you have to apologize for?!"

—"Because... you used my piece?"-Mo Bei said slowly.

Yet this was clearly not the piece's fault.

Mo Bei had already proved her overwhelming power in the tournament. Losing while piloting Mo Bei's piece was blatantly a skill issue on Yu Feifei's part.

Even so, Mo Bei still apologized:
—"Next time... I will strive to become stronger."

—"...You want to become even stronger?!"-Yu Feifei asked, dazed.-"You really... really... Pfft, hahahaha!"

She suddenly burst into loud laughter.

The kind of laughter that simply couldn't be stopped.

Mo Bei watched her with genuine concern, finally deciding:
—"I'll go fetch a physician."

—"No, I'm fine! I'm not sitting here sulking over losing a game,"-Yu Feifei hurriedly grabbed Mo Bei's sleeve, wiping away the tears at the corners of her eyes.

She realized that Mo Bei probably had no clue about her petty schemes and ulterior motives.

Yet it was precisely this innocence that was so extraordinarily...

Yu Feifei took a deep breath and drew a sealed letter from her robes:
—"It's from my family... No, they don't even qualify as family, merely the people who raised me. Their letter orders me to frame and sabotage you inside the secret realm!"

Yu Feifei's voice trembled slightly.

Clever as she was, she naturally understood that carrying out the deed outlined in that letter meant she could never stay in the Heavenly Spirit Sect again. In fact, the missive explicitly stated that upon successful completion, she would earn the right to return to the Carefree Palace.

Yet Yu Feifei hesitated.

This was clearly an opportunity to choose her allegiance anew.

The Fallen Immortal Palace?

Or the Heavenly Spirit Sect?

If it were her former self, she would have obediently followed orders to accumulate merits and climb the ranks. But now, she couldn't help asking herself: if she returned to the Fallen Immortal Palace, would she truly have a future?

Over there, it was nothing but petty scoundrels [buzzing like flies and scurrying like dogs](# "Literal CN: 蝇营狗苟 (yíngyíng gǒugǒu) — shameless scheming and sordid pursuit of personal gain"). Even the director she had exhausted so much effort to flatter was merely a contemptible villain.

In contrast, within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even ordinary disciples possessed the indomitable willpower to never back down—to say nothing of direct disciples like Mo Bei.

A girl who would have been treated as nothing more than a disposable commodity in the Fallen Immortal Palace held the potential to become the realm's greatest Sword Immortal.

Why should such exquisite jade be dragged back down into the filth and mire?!

Yu Feifei made her decision. Pushing the secret letter toward Mo Bei—who still appeared completely bewildered by the sudden turn of events—she declared:
—"Please report this to the Immortal Venerable! I am willing to accept any punishment!"
