# Chapter 253: Three Hundred Years Later (Part 4)

—"?"-Zhou Hui countered with utter innocence.-"Weren't there always multiple Children of Destiny to begin with?"

Indeed, there were plenty of Children of Destiny; this had never been news in the first place.

Back at the very beginning of beginnings, Zhou Hui had also assumed that eliminating the Child of Destiny would avert the worst-case scenario. Yet under the guidance of the predetermined Heavenly Dao (the script), the "actors" were blatantly the most disposable link. No matter how many were slain, the next reincarnation cycle would inevitably sprout new ones. There were even several "backup" Children of Destiny in a single cycle—resembling nothing so much as cockroaches.

In fact, even with Tian Yan (the compiler of the Heavenly Dao) gone, the issue could not be thoroughly resolved.

After all, "Children of Destiny" had originally been an objectively existing, external "diagnostic program."

The Heavenly Court had merely piggybacked on this mechanism. What was even more absurd was that with Tian Yan gone and the script interrupted, whether because the Heavenly Court's administrative efficiency was utterly abysmal or because three celestial days (three hundred mortal years) was too short a window, the Immortal-Execution Platform in charge of descending to the mortal realm hadn't reacted in time. Far from grinding to a halt, the Heavenly Court's operations had spiraled out of control, escalating with compounding recklessness.

Now that the three-hundred-year deadline had arrived, the so-called Children of Destiny tumbled into the mortal realm one after another, [falling like dumplings into boiling broth](# "Literal CN: 下饺子 (xià jiǎoxiang) — dropping like dumplings into a pot; raining down in droves")!

Their numbers were even greater than before. Aside from genuine Children of Destiny like Long Tianming, between those tossed down by the Heavenly Court to [fish in troubled waters](# "Literal CN: 浑水摸鱼 (hún shuǐ mō yú) — idiom: to fish in muddy waters / profit from chaos"), the number of descended entities already located and verified had surpassed a hundred.

Even Zhou Hui found it giving him a headache, to the point where he had actually considered tossing the captive Celestial Emperor back to take charge of the mess.

Renran, however, was privy to none of this.

When all was said and done, she was merely a minor pawn acting on orders. Forget interfering with the "script"—prior to the curtain rising, she wouldn't even know who the "lead actor" was.

Renran merely suspected that these Children of Destiny were all Zhou Hui's doing.

—"A Child of Destiny isn't some wholesale commodity sold in bulk! How could there be more than one?!"-Renran demanded indignantly.-"Confounding my senses—Untiring, what kind of conspiracy are you concocting?!"

—"I don't have one..."-Zhou Hui offered a wry smile.

The affair involved far too many moving parts, and Zhou Hui had no desire to explain. What was more, experience told him Renran lacked the patience to listen anyway.

And Renran indeed had no intention of listening.

Without waiting for Zhou Hui to finish, she continued on her own:

—"Whatever your schemes may be, I have already verified the true Child of Destiny. I won't be so easily bewitched by you!"

With that declaration, without pausing for Zhou Hui's reply, she spun on her heel and strode away with resolute finality.

Had her pace not been so brisk that it bordered on an all-out sprint to flee, it might have possessed a touch more majesty.

Zhou Hui smiled helplessly, making no move to stop her.

Yet Renran had taken only a few paces before halting abruptly. As if sensing something, she looked around in stunned bewilderment, then scurried right back, shrieking at Zhou Hui:

—"Th-this is the Heavenly Realm?!"

—"Not entirely. It is merely one of the transit junctions following ascension,"-Zhou Hui replied, casting a glance toward the gatehouse inscribed with 'Heavenly Gate.'-"By the Heavenly Court's original designation, this is Southern Heavenly Gate Ascension Platform No. 67."

—"The Southern Heavenly Gate?!"-Renran shrieked even louder.

Just moments ago, she had been fretting over how to complete her mission so she could return to the Heavenly Realm. Who could have imagined that simply chaperoning someone to an [entrance exam](# "Literal CN: 升学考试 (shēngxué kǎoshì) — school entrance examination") would land her right back here?!

Even the celestial restrictions binding her had lost their effect!

—One had to understand that owing to the covenants and punishments from the Immortal-Demon War ten thousand years ago, Renran had been branded with a restriction: before completing her duty of guiding the Child of Destiny, she could never return to the Heavenly Realm. Not only was her power suppressed, but had she used other means to force her way back, the restriction would have incinerated her into flying ash the instant she set foot in the Heavenly Realm.

Yet here she stood, completely intact within the Heavenly Gate, devoid of the slightest restrictive friction. If the dense concentration of immortal spiritual energy didn't confirm beyond doubt that this was indeed the Heavenly Realm, she wouldn't even have realized she was back in the celestial court she longed for day and night.

And yet, Renran felt not an ounce of joy.

She felt only sheer, unadulterated terror:

*Does this count as encroaching on Heavenly Realm territory?!*

*How on earth did Untiring pull this off?!*

*Does the Heavenly Court know about this?!*

*Does my presence count as an unauthorized return, violating heavenly laws?! When the reckoning comes, will I be branded as Untiring's accomplice?!*

Anxieties of every stripe flooded Renran's mind, driving her to scream, or better yet, to flee at top speed just like she had last time at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Yet she hadn't completely lost her head; she noticed that her shrieks had drawn a fair number of curious glances from passersby.

Choking down the urge to scream, Renran bit her lip in humiliation, stepped closer to Zhou Hui, and hissed in a hushed voice:

—"You! What in the world did you do?! Do you have any idea?! Infiltrating the Immortal Realm, seizing a celestial gate, and obstructing ascenders is an act of war against the Heavenly Court and all Three Thousand Worlds under its dominion!"

Renran attempted to dissuade Untiring from his "conspiracy."

Though she didn't know how long Untiring had been up to this, at most it had been three hundred mortal years—merely three days in the Immortal Realm. Perhaps there was still room to salvage the situation... probably.

—"Is there such a rule?"-Zhou Hui heard of this for the first time.

He had never been terribly interested in heavenly laws anyway. Fortunately, certain disciples of his were quite keen on them. Although they had captured the Celestial Emperor and purged the Heavenly Court's most senior civil official, Tian Yan, and supreme military commander, Tian Shou, they hadn't quite reached the point of declaring outright war against the Heavenly Court.

For the stability of the Three Thousand Worlds, the Heavenly Court still needed to exist for a certain period.

So Zhou Hui pacified Renran:

—"No need to worry; we have no intention of obstructing ascension. Quite the contrary—our purpose here at this Heavenly Gate is to assist ascenders in integrating into the Heavenly Court far swifter!"

Saying so, Zhou Hui pointed straight ahead.

*Integrating into the Heavenly Court?!* An un-ascended mortal—wait, could his current state really be called 'un-ascended'? Never mind! At any rate, Untiring's current state looked like anything but normal ascension! How did he have the gall to shamelessly proclaim he was helping ascenders integrate into the Heavenly Court?! It wasn't as if he were an official of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny!

Renran's bellyful of grievances swelled further. Yet her gaze obediently followed Zhou Hui's finger. Perched high above on the opposite side of the Heavenly Gate, several lines of text leapt into her vision:

> **Heavenly Court Heavenly Official Examination Training Class**
>
> Policy Breakdown | Post Analysis
> Interview Coaching | Examination Trends
>
> *"Ascension is merely the starting point; becoming a Heavenly Official is [true shore-landing](# "Literal CN: 上岸 (shàng'àn) — landing on shore; modern Chinese buzzword meaning successfully passing a competitive civil service or postgraduate exam to secure a stable iron rice bowl")!"*

These lines of text were massive and eye-catching. Had Renran not been distracted first by the horde of Children of Destiny and then fixated on Untiring's motives, she would have spotted such a glaring billboard long ago.

Yet even seeing those massive characters now, even knowing every single character, Renran's brain simply could not process it. In the end, she could only let out a helpless wail:

—"What in the world is all this nonsense?!"

***

—"What is this?"-Long Tianming uttered in bewilderment.

With such an enormous sign, Long Tianming naturally spotted it too. What was worse, it induced a deeply unsettling sense of déjà vu, prompting him to ask:

—"Why... do Heavenly Officials have to take exams?"

—"Of course Heavenly Officials must take exams!"-the senior brother responsible for receiving new students laughed upon hearing this.-"The Heavenly Court governs the Three Thousand Worlds! Heaven knows how many Heavenly Gates there are; this location of ours is merely Number 67. Think about how many ascenders there are—without a reasonable evaluation system, how could everyone just become a Heavenly Official?"

—"..."

[...The logic holds, but why does it feel so damn wrong?]

Long Tianming pursed his lips.

It gave him the distinct feeling of graduating with a bachelor's and a master's, still being unable to find an ideal job, and getting nagged by one's parents to [take the civil service exam](# "Literal CN: 考公 (kǎogōng) — sit for China's notoriously grueling national civil service examinations for stable state employment").

Unfortunately, education reform in this world hadn't been around long—or rather, the lifespan of cultivators was so vast that three hundred years of reform hadn't yet carved the sheer dread of examinations into their marrow, especially for raw youths who had barely touched the threshold of cultivation like those from White Jade Capital Academy.

Seeing Long Tianming listening so intently, the reception senior brother assumed he was keenly interested in the Heavenly Official Examination, clapping him on the shoulder with a chuckle:

—"You've come to the right place! While they're all White Jade Capital Academies, this branch of ours at the Heavenly Gate is the hardest to enter and has the highest standards—but it's also where you progress fastest and get closest to the Heavenly Court! Who knows, the day you graduate from the academy, you might be able to sit directly for the Heavenly Official Examination!"

[I really don't want to. I already transmigrated, why do I still have to take exams...]

With twitching eyelids, Long Tianming wanted to decline.

Yet before he could open his mouth, two passersby drawn by their conversation approached. Bowing in greeting, they asked:

—"Young friends, pray stay your steps. Might we consult you on a few matters?"

—"We wouldn't dare claim to teach seniors,"-the reception senior brother returned the courtesy.

Long Tianming was at a loss, but this senior brother understood the situation clearly. Though this was one of the campus branches of White Jade Capital Academy, it had already exited the Langhuan Realm and stood within the true Immortal Realm. Consequently, the cultivators able to reach this Heavenly Gate were either high-ranking cultivators vetted and dispatched by the Heavenly Spirit Sect, or ascenders arriving from other worlds.

Clearly, those from the Heavenly Spirit Sect—even if unfamiliar—would never fail to recognize White Jade Capital Academy disciples. Anyone here addressing them as "young friends" was naturally one of those ascenders.

Those capable of ascending were all titans at or above Tribulation Transcendence. Raw academy disciples who had just entered the immortal path would never dare presume to be their "young friends."

Yet stationed here, even academy disciples had grown accustomed to dealing with such formidable powerhouses. The reception senior brother replied neither obsequiously nor arrogantly:

—"Seniors, please ask whatever you wish."

Seeing his composed demeanor, the ascenders became even less inclined to underestimate them.

—To encounter two children in the Immortal Realm whose cultivation bases were nearly indistinguishable from mortals, they could hardly be ordinary mortals by any stretch of the imagination.

One of the ascenders inquired cautiously:

—"We overheard you mentioning White Jade Capital Academy just now. Could it be an academy established specifically to test for Heavenly Officials?"

—"Not quite. I was merely motivating my new junior brother!"-the reception senior brother admitted candidly, casting a wry glance at Long Tianming.-"Though it's no idle boast either. As the saying goes, [the pavilion closest to the water catches the moonlight first](# "Literal CN: 近水楼台先得月 (jìn shuǐ lóutái xiān dé yuè) — idiom: benefiting from proximity / being first in line due to vantage point"). Catching wind of news regarding the Heavenly Official Exams early allows apprentices like us to target our cultivation much better. But you two seniors have no need for such worries—"

Pausing here, the reception senior brother sized up the two ascenders curiously, asking while knowing the answer full well:

—"You two must be ascenders from below, correct? May I ask where you seniors hail from?"

The two ascenders exchanged glances and offered no answer.

After all, regardless of the minor world, ascending to the Heavenly Realm entailed cutthroat competition for resources; ascenders were inherently one-in-ten-thousand anomalies. Even having ascended, they would not disclose their backgrounds so easily.

The guiding senior brother clearly understood this. He had only asked in passing and had no need for their reply, continuing smoothly:

—"Regardless of where you seniors hail from, ascending alone proves you possess ample qualifications to remain in the Heavenly Realm and test for a Heavenly Official post."

—"As if it were that easy!"-the ascender who had remained silent suddenly sneered coldly.-"Those of us who ascended are essentially no different from loose immortals. We know next to nothing about the Immortal Realm or Heavenly Officials, nor have we received your Immortal Realm education. How could we ever compete against the scions of noble clans and [immortal second-generation](# "Literal CN: 仙门二代 (xiānmén èrdài) — nepo babies of immortal sects / cultivation silver-spoon elites") nurtured by your Immortal Realm academies?!"

He had evidently mistaken Long Tianming and his senior brother for native inhabitants of the Heavenly Court.

Yet this ascender's words also betrayed the fact that he was by no means a fresh ascender.

In truth, back when Zhou Hui and Yuwen Xuan ascended and wrecked the place, Yuwen Xuan had brandished receipts from the Heavenly Court registry to spread rumors, aiming to dupe loose immortals into providing intelligence.

It had been an idle move, yet it quickly yielded results. These two had likely traveled here from elsewhere after catching wind of the news.

Consequently, they had lingered in the Heavenly Realm for quite some time—long enough to accumulate a deep-seated well of grievances.

This, too, was perfectly normal.

Given the Heavenly Court's administrative efficiency and recruitment practices, it would have been difficult not to harbor grievances.

Rolling his eyes thoughtfully, the guiding senior brother refrained from clearing up the ascenders' misunderstanding, merely continuing:

—"That is only because Senior is temporarily unfamiliar with the situation..."

—"This seat ascended fifty celestial years ago—amounting to a full five thousand years in the lower realm! How could I still not understand?!"-the furious ascender raged.-"You clan scions of celestial bloodline have had famous masters instructing you from childhood, spirit elixirs nurturing you, top-grade cultivation methods at your disposal, and can even directly inherit Heavenly Official posts! What do we ascenders have?! We have our lives! That's it! Good for nothing except being shoved into the meat grinder of the Immortal-Demon frontlines, right?!"

—"Brother Fu, mind your words,"-the other ascender anxiously tugged his companion's sleeve, flashing a nervous, appeasing smile toward the senior brother.

For a Tribulation Transcendence titan to offer such a humble smile to an academy disciple with negligible cultivation spoke volumes.

It was enough to show how much bitterness they had swallowed in the Heavenly Realm.

—"I can understand Senior's feelings; anyone would feel aggrieved after being stalled for so long,"-the senior brother said soothingly. In truth, with his seniority, he understood nothing of the sort; yet when dealing with ascenders bursting with grievances, he had been drilled in a complete script.-"However, times have changed. Word has it the Heavenly Court is in desperate need of a batch of Heavenly Officials to fill vacant posts! Isn't this a tremendous opportunity for you all?"

The two ascenders exchanged a look upon hearing this. The friendlier ascender eagerly stepped closer toward Long Tianming and his companion:

—"Why are there vacancies in the Heavenly Court? Could you elaborate?"

—"Supposedly an issue occurred at the Sky-Tour Immortal Banquet, while others claim personnel from the Ministry of Celestial Destiny have gone missing. They sound like lucrative posts far out of reach in ordinary times—almost too good to be true, making them somewhat unbelievable. The only certainty is that the Heavenly Court will definitely recruit Heavenly Officials in recent years; otherwise, this training class wouldn't exist."

The senior brother shifted his gaze with deliberate hesitation, but his words resonated deeply with the two ascenders:

—"If you seniors wish to hear more detailed official information, you can inquire at the training class over there. They possess specialized internal intelligence. If you don't know the way, I could..."

The guiding senior brother paused here, casting a glance at Long Tianming as though suddenly remembering he still had to escort his junior brother back to White Jade Capital Academy.

Long Tianming was normally terrible at reading the room, yet at this moment, [inspiration struck](# "Literal CN: 福至心灵 (fú zhì xīn líng) — idiom: when good fortune arrives, the mind turns nimble; a sudden flash of insight"):

—"Senior Brother, why don't we show the two seniors the way? It just so happens I've never visited either, and I'd like to broaden my horizons."

—"Many thanks, young friends!"-both ascenders exclaimed in delight.

Led by the two academy disciples, they made their way toward the Heavenly Official Training Class—leaving aside for now the generous "tokens of appreciation" they slipped the boys along the journey.

***

Though Long Tianming had reaped some benefits before even stepping through the academy doors, he wasn't particularly thrilled.

In fact, he was somewhat disappointed.

After all, the atmosphere had been built up so grandly beforehand. Long Tianming had assumed that upon crossing the Heavenly Gate, even if he didn't witness white jade staircases, golden-tiled roofs, flocks of immortal cranes circling amid auspicious clouds, and air suffused with the fragrant aroma of ten-thousand-year spiritual herbs—where a single casual breath rivaled ten years of bitter cultivation below—at the very least, there ought to be a cinematic CG animation showcasing a colossal xianxia metropolis modeled to Hollywood blockbuster standards!

Yet what lay before his eyes...

An excessively flat bluestone plaza, a rigidly standardized and stiff architectural complex, uniform three-story blocks huddled one against another, outer walls painted a drab, monotonous grayish-white—bluntly advertising boringness for all to see.

The only elements related to immortal arts were the various holographic projections of celestial beings and spells hovering above the rigid buildings. While they looked bustling, their actual audio and text were:

> *"Three Years of Simulations, Five Years of Immortal Exams—Exclusively Authorized by the Ministry of Celestial Destiny Teaching and Research Group!"*
>
> *"Secrets to High Scores on the Essay Exam: Taught Directly by Disciples of Lord Wenqu!"*
>
> *"Administrative Aptitude Crash Course: Break Through Bottlenecks in Ten Days—Full Refund if You Don't Pass!"*
>
> *"Interview Image Consulting: Special Guidance from the Red Thread Pavilion of Yue Lao, Helping You Catch the Examiner's Eye!"*

What in the world was all this?!

It was far too familiar!

So familiar that it provoked acute physical nausea in Long Tianming.

—"Senior Brother, did we come to the wrong place?"-Long Tianming glanced at the ascenders murmuring in hushed tones, unable to stop himself from asking the guiding senior brother.

The guiding senior brother glanced at Long Tianming, immediately grasping why he asked:

—"No mistake, this is the direction of the Heavenly Official Training Class. Does Junior Brother feel... it's not grand enough?"

Long Tianming nodded honestly.

—"That is because from the Heavenly Gate's construction until now, it has only been 'one day,'"-the guiding senior brother explained.-"You might not know, but prior to the 'New Education Reform,' cultivators sought their own fortunes and carved their own paths. It wasn't until 'three days' ago, when a certain opportunity created a massive shortage of Heavenly Officials, that this transit station was re-established."

"One day?" The history here was only one day?! That was actually rather impressive!

Long Tianming was taken aback.

Yet in his heart, he couldn't shake the feeling that something was off.

The two ascenders, however, were not as easily duped as Long Tianming.

They chimed in:

—"One day? For immortals, isn't that more than enough? Any True Immortal could erect jade towers and vermilion pavilions with a wave of their hand. Why make it so austere?"

It seemed they harbored doubts as well.

This was indeed a flaw.

Back when Zhou Hui ascended to the Heavenly Realm to slay gods, he had returned to resolve issues in his own realm and thrown himself into educational reform, paying no heed to this transit hub. It was only later, realizing the Heavenly Court was doomed and that connections with other minor worlds had to be forged in advance, that he remembered this place.

—The Southern Heavenly Gate received ascenders from every realm; what finer window to the outside world could there be?!

As for borrowing the Heavenly Court's name and setting up a Heavenly Official Training Class—that was the brainchild of Untiring Immortal Venerable's "clever disciples." They always brimmed with whimsical notions of this sort.

However, time was tight, and "one day" in the heavens equaled a century in the lower realm. They had no choice but to construct several batches of [prefabricated houses](# "Literal CN: 预制房屋 (yùzhì fángwū) — modular prefabricated buildings hastily assembled on-site") down below and haul them straight up, making them look somewhat austere amidst the lavish magnificence of the Heavenly Realm.

Fortunately, academy disciples like this guiding senior brother were "prefabricated" as well.

"Senior brothers and sisters" like him had all arrived in the Heavenly Realm with missions in hand, so every response had been systematically drilled into them.

—"It is easy to go from frugality to luxury, but hard to go from luxury to frugality! You seniors have come to study and test for Heavenly Official posts, not to serve as pampered officials yet. Hence, the master of this place uniformly planned the architecture and established new standards,"-the guiding senior brother said, lowering his voice as he looked at the two ascenders.-"Besides, didn't you two feel before that Upper Realm immortals were far too undisciplined? Once they became Heavenly Officials, all they did was play chess, drink wine, and admire flowers. The temporal flow between Upper and Lower realms is unequal to begin with; this made Heavenly Court efficiency outrageously sluggish. With this rare opportunity opening so many vacancies, shouldn't the rules be overhauled? Shouldn't a batch of more professional, standardized officials be cultivated?"

—"So that's how it is!"-both ascenders' eyes lit up upon hearing this.

They hurriedly inquired:

—"Who is the master of this place...?"

—"Cannot be spoken, cannot be spoken!"-the senior brother sighed deeply, yet glanced around with conspicuous hints.-"However, looking at all these exams and training courses, you seniors must have a few ideas in mind!"

—"Oh! Ohh!"-both ascenders wore expressions of sudden enlightenment.

Long Tianming had no idea what they had realized, only turning his head away in mounting frustration.

He had no interest in Heavenly Official training, and even less in the master of this place. Just like that familiar revulsion he felt the moment he laid eyes on the sign, he had transmigrated! He thought the world would be boundless and free, yet after going in circles, he had landed right back before the word "Exam."

Long Tianming felt a chill in his marrow, even harboring a faint urge to flee.

[Can I just not attend this academy? Cultivating on my own would be better,] Long Tianming pondered in his mind as if muttering to himself. [Being a loose cultivator should work too, right?]

He was actually speaking to the "Old Grandpa."

Unfortunately, after passing through the Heavenly Gate, for reasons unknown, the "Old Grandpa" had fallen dead silent, as though he had lost his voice entirely.

Left with no choice, Long Tianming nervously continued scanning his surroundings. Suddenly, amidst the endless sea of glaring signs, his eyes caught one that held considerably more appeal than the rest:

> **Enlistment Office**

***

—"You want to become a Heavenly Soldier?!"

When Renran tracked down Long Tianming once more and caught wind of this news, she nearly blacked out on the spot.

She grew increasingly convinced that everything was Untiring's conspiracy:

Don't assume she was too stupid to see through it! What Heavenly Official Training Class? Wasn't this a scheme to hollow out and infiltrate the Heavenly Court from within?!

Leaving other ascenders aside, what if some among those "Children of Destiny" were truly agents descended from the Heavenly Court? Now they were being hauled back to the Heavenly Realm, trained, and made to test back into the Heavenly Court to serve as Heavenly Officials again?! What kind of sinister operation was this—sending enemy spies back into their original faction as sleeper agents?!

Worst of all, Long Tianming—the "true Child of Destiny" she had chosen—had actually birthed an ambition that made her vision go darker than testing into the Heavenly Court:

—"Do you have any idea what a Heavenly Soldier is?"-Renran suppressed her fury, striving to keep her voice steady.-"Stationed on the Immortal-Demon frontlines, fighting the demon race, with countless casualties year after year! So-called Heavenly Soldiers are the lowest tier of cannon fodder. For a mortal like you who hasn't even entered the sect, going there is pure suicide!"

Indeed—tricking a mortal child into fighting demons made Untiring utterly unforgivable!

Renran glared daggers at Zhou Hui.

This time, Zhou Hui averted his gaze with a guilty conscience.

There was nothing wrong with Renran's perception. He couldn't deny it; even he found those Children of Destiny far too troublesome. Especially the ones who refused to study and lacked any practical utility—better to educate them and dispatch them back to the Heavenly Court. Besides, the Heavenly Court was indeed short on hands, so conducting a little infiltration on the side wasn't crossing the line, was it?

It was just that whenever his two senior disciples, Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing, joined forces, the stunts they pulled were always impossible to appraise with a straight face.

—"Ahem, little maiden, even if it's the truth, you shouldn't be so blunt,"-to the side, the two ascenders who hadn't walked away yet couldn't help but advise.

Yet their views on "Heavenly Soldiers" evidently aligned with Renran's.

—"This... Junior Sister? You needn't put it so harshly,"-the guiding senior brother offered a wry smile.

He sized up Renran and Zhou Hui curiously, annoyed at himself for failing to notice that this batch included two more juniors. Unfortunately, now was hardly the ideal moment for mutual introductions. Seeing how furious this "junior sister" was, he hurriedly added:

—"The recruitment here is somewhat different. The destination is a newly opened battlefield. Word has it they've had continuous major victories after major victories, making it far safer than elsewhere."

—"Even to coax a young girl, that tale is too bogus,"-the ascender sighed.

—"I speak the absolute truth! It's near the Abyssal Ocean, yet there hasn't been a single demonic tide for at least three hundred years!"-the guiding senior brother shouted.

—"Near the Abyssal Ocean and no demonic tides?!"-the ascenders finally wore expressions of sheer disbelief. They naturally understood the reality and knew how brutal the Immortal-Demon frontlines were, especially for worlds bordering the Abyssal Ocean, where demonic tides were a ubiquitous plague.

—"I do not speak falsehoods. If you don't believe me, you may go see for yourselves; you don't even have to enlist,"-the guiding senior brother said.

—"Young friend, we truly will go take a look,"-the ascender said earnestly.

Though they were interested in becoming Heavenly Officials, that didn't prevent them from being intrigued by the Immortal-Demon frontlines—or more precisely, by the disappearance of demonic tides. After all,

For an immortal who had ascended from the lower realm, whose homeland didn't suffer from similar tribulations?

Therefore, regardless of whether they intended to test for Heavenly Official posts, hearing that demonic tides had vanished would compel them to investigate. Wouldn't that draw the immortals of other realms straight over?

And the strongest among the ascenders at that.

Rather than calling this entirely the Heavenly Spirit Sect's "conspiracy," it was far more accurate to call it an [open gambit](# "Literal CN: 阳谋 (yángmóu) — open plot / overt stratagem where the target willingly walks into the trap knowing full well it exists").

Sensing this, Renran watched her vision black out again and again, overwhelmed by a sense of utter powerlessness against fate.

The ascenders were oblivious to the Goddess's inner turmoil, quickly latching onto another temptation they found impossible to resist:

—"Come to think of it, if the demonic tides really vanished, wouldn't that front be the easiest place to rack up military merits?"

—"You could certainly say so, but that is something only formidable ascenders like yourselves can accomplish,"-the guiding senior brother chimed in with a beaming smile, laying on the bait thick.

He turned to his "junior brother," not nearly as polite:

—"However, for raw recruits like you who haven't even enrolled in an academy, no matter how relaxed the front may be, it is still a front! There is an academy there too, and you must study first! Moreover, compared to the Heavenly Gate here which leads directly to the Heavenly Court, that place is a trial of blood and fire! The difficulty of graduating there is dozens, hundreds of times greater than other academies! It demands not merely immortal arts mastery, but profound combat experience!"

—"Just what I've been waiting for!"-Long Tianming roared with soaring spirit.

He was here to be the "protagonist"! Practical combat suited his tastes far better. Besides, why transmigrate to xianxia if not to enjoy spectacular immortal arts battles?

In short, he would rather die than [take the civil service exam](# "Literal CN: 考公 (kǎogōng) — sit for China's notoriously grueling national civil service examinations for stable state employment")!

He hadn't taken it before transmigrating, and after transmigrating, fat chance!

For this, Long Tianming felt he could even renounce the currently trending light-comedy harem romance route.

Women could not disrupt the rhythm of his leveling!

Reaching this thought, Long Tianming felt the blood rush to his head even more, saying to Renran:

—"Forgive me, our fated bond ends here for now."

—"No!"-Renran shut her eyes, gritting her teeth in fury.-"I'm going to the frontline with you—"
