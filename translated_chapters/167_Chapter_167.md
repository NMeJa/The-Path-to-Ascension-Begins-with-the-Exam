# Chapter 167: Historical Origins

Zhou Hui's desire to travel to the Southern Domain was naturally to save that tree—that book—in Langhuan.

It was a newborn tree.

It had only sprouted not long ago; the history it had recorded was too brief, and Yuwen Xuan had gnawed off several mouthfuls of its leaves to boot, leaving it woefully deficient in energy.

In other words, its "bandwidth" was still far too low.

Although according to Zhou Hui's initial plans, securing the transmission conduit of the Heavenly Scripture was sufficient for him to [test the waters](# "Literal CN: 练手 (liànshǒu) — practice run / warm up one's skills"), human beings were perpetually insatiable, and Zhou Hui was no exception. Having obtained the genuine Heavenly Scripture, he naturally wished to acquire the complete, full-fledged Heavenly Scripture configuration.

Most importantly, judging from the intelligence provided by the Heavenly Scripture and Yuwen Xuan, Zhou Hui discovered that interfering with the Upper Realm was not merely a matter of "practicing"—it was an absolute necessity.

Because there was no "final boss."

It wasn't that there was no sinister mastermind behind the curtain, but rather that there was no single "final boss."

After all, reality was not some third-rate Heavenly Dao script. It was impossible for things to play out like a protagonist's leveling progression in a web novel, where they first [devastated the Heavenly Spirit Sect](# "Literal CN: 霍霍 (huòhuo) — northern colloquialism meaning to ruin, torment, or wreck"), then engaged in a showdown against Mo Xi until ascending, and once upstairs, found a "Demon Sovereign" waiting as the final boss.

As if the "protagonist's" life had only been miserable because of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mo Xi, and that "Demon Sovereign"—such that so long as one struck down these "villains," everything would magically sail smoothly.

Truth be told, Zhou Hui also wished there were such a villain to serve as the "final boss."

That way, whether it was the extermination of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the adulteration of the Heavenly Dao, or even the grand conspiracy of the God-Demon War, every last crime could be pinned onto their head. Then Zhou Hui could obliterate them in one fell swoop, [settling everything once and for all](# "Literal CN: 一了百了 (yī liǎo bǎi liǎo) — once the main thing is finished, all associated matters are resolved / settled once and for all").

Regrettably: there wasn't one!

In fact, this point could already be detected from the celestial departments Zhou Hui had witnessed when he temporarily "ascended" to the Upper Realm.

The Heavenly Court was a functional bureaucratic apparatus—bloated, redundant, with each department minding only its own narrow remit. Even if there were so-called deities, such a god was merely one terminal at the end of a nerve ending. Take the one Zhou Hui had killed, for instance: although the Heavenly Court had yet to notice its demise, once they did, dispatching a replacement would hardly be difficult. The newcomer might even seek retribution for their predecessor's death, inflicting even more outrageous atrocities upon this mortal world.

The adulteration of the Heavenly Dao operated in the exact same manner. It was not some specific True Immortal or deity deliberately targeting this world out of personal malice; rather, it was a bureaucratic measure implemented by the Heavenly Court after this world was designated as a buffer zone.

—Though designating it as a buffer zone in the first place did carry a certain degree of deliberate malice.

Although the records in the Heavenly Scripture were incomplete, as Langhuan's presence in this world demonstrated, this realm had once been part of the "Upper Realm." Consequently, right after it originally plummeted into the lower dimension, this realm had maintained rather enthusiastic, close ties with the Heavenly Court.

Yet [the tea cools once the guest departs](# "Idiom: 人走茶凉 (rén zǒu chá liáng) — when people leave, the tea grows cold; out of sight, out of mind / worldly relationships fade when distance or status changes")—a universal truth applicable to every world.

As former territory of the Upper Realm, in the beginning, cultivators ascending from this world were still entitled to enjoy their original [official establishment quotas](# "Literal CN: 编制 (biānzhì) — government establishment / civil service quota; authorized personnel staffing in state-owned institutions"). This had already sparked dissatisfaction among certain factions within the Heavenly Court. And the fact that the cultivators of this world refused to resign themselves to their fate, obsessively contemplating how to [ascend back up once more](# "Literal CN: 再次飞升 (zàicì fēishēng) — lifting the entire fallen realm back into the Upper Realm / ascending again"), vexed them all the more.

Yet what ultimately drove them to make up their minds was, shockingly, the Langhuan Forbidden Grounds.

Because Langhuan contained far too many archives that "should not exist," including scandalous [black files](# "Literal CN: 黑料 (hēiliào) — compromising dirt / scandalous secrets / incriminating dossiers") on numerous gods and immortals. When it was originally the personal courtyard garden of the Heavenly Emperor, they could still grit their teeth and tolerate it. But having such dirt fall into the hands of "lowly lower-realm organisms" was absolutely intolerable.

Combined with several rounds of political purges and personnel reshuffles inside the Heavenly Court, certain deities and True Immortals hatched an agenda. Thus was born the plan to completely eradicate Langhuan alongside this entire world. In fact, during the previous demon invasion, the Heavenly Court had actively turned a blind eye, deliberately letting the demons run rampant.

It was merely that the ties between the Heavenly Court and this realm had still been quite close back then, making it inconvenient for the Heavenly Court to act too brazenly. Moreover, certain cultivators in this world caught wind of the Heavenly Court's malicious intentions and sealed off the realm, attempting to thwart the Heavenly Court's conspiracy.

Their endeavors succeeded, yet also failed.

Success lay in repelling both the True Immortals and the demon race beyond the boundaries of the world, winning an era of peace and development spanning over ten thousand years for this realm. Yet failure lay in the catastrophic casualties suffered during the God-Demon War. Furthermore, after sealing the realm, they never completely tore off all pretense with the Heavenly Court. Instead, this gave the Heavenly Court the opportunity to relentlessly infiltrate over the following ten thousand years; numerous insiders perished in mysterious "accidents," culminating ten thousand years later in the present state of affairs—a realm teetering on the brink of total annihilation without even realizing it.

And the so-called "Heavenly Dao script" was presumably the final wave of harvesting.

Just as Zhou Hui had sensed, it did not matter who the "protagonist" was—though having said that, the candidates chosen were generally individuals with ties to the Heavenly Court, such as those possessing True Immortal bloodlines or reincarnated celestial souls. According to the blueprint, they would carry off the final remaining assets desired by the Heavenly Court.

For this purpose, this world's Heavenly Dao had been altered into an incoherent, tangled mess.

Likewise, this was not the handiwork of a single "individual." Aside from the deity directly surveilling the Heavenly Dao whom Zhou Hui had slain, the Heavenly Court actually possessed a dedicated [Lantai](# "Historical/Institutional: 兰台 (Lántái) — Orchid Pavilion / Imperial Archival Bureau, the official imperial library and records department in ancient China; here, the celestial clerical bureau overseeing cosmic records and destinies") responsible for overseeing such clerical affairs. The True Immortals serving within it rotated constantly, which was precisely why the Heavenly Dao had been butchered into such an unintelligible mess with no single person held accountable.

Tallying it all up, the most stable "sinister mastermind" across all this time had unexpectedly turned out to be Yuwen Xuan (cross that out—the Taotie).

Because that Taotie wanted to swap into a fresh physical vessel, and because it knew the true origins of this world, it had wished to claim this realm as its new personal lair. Consequently, it had been the one keeping the tightest watch along the entire way, and many of the terrible, rotten schemes directed against the Heavenly Spirit Sect had originated directly from it.

That was why, once Yuwen Xuan discovered what his soul's true form had done, he had been scared half to death and desperately rushed to destroy all incriminating evidence, terrified that Master would slice him into pieces and hang him from the peach tree.

Actually, Zhou Hui didn't particularly mind that Taotie.

After all, it was a physical entity that could be beaten into submission; if it truly proved unteachable, slaughtering it would merely count as ridding the world of a pest.

However, as for the Heavenly Court...

—"It's not that there was completely no person in charge,"-Yuwen Xuan had confessed with utmost honesty before being hung up in the tree.-"Originally, there was a specific True Immortal spearheading the strategy regarding the cultivation realm—he was the one the Taotie was cooperating with. But he [got wiped out](# "Colloquial/slang: 被交代了 (bèi jiāodài le) — was finished off / died / perished") during the God-Demon War. All that was left was his operational proposal, which has been chaotically executed ever since."

This was the most infuriating aspect of it all.

Due to the sheer immensity of the Heavenly Court, even when the project manager was dead, no matter how atrocious the plan was, it would blindly grind onward regardless.

—"It seems the only way is to overthrow the Heavenly Court,"-Zhou Hui considered once again.

Yet even for Zhou Hui, at this stage, such words could only be spoken in passing.

Confronting an entire Heavenly Court by one's own power alone—no matter how arrogant Zhou Hui might be, he would never believe himself capable of such a feat. He was not a certain famous monkey, after all. And even that monkey had ultimately reached an indirect compromise in the end.

Yet this world could not afford to compromise.

Furthermore, even if Zhou Hui did overthrow the Heavenly Court, matters would hardly conclude overnight. Setting aside the catastrophic fallout that would strike the countless minor worlds governed by the Heavenly Court, regardless of the true relationship between the demon race and the deities, the gods and True Immortals remained a crucial force containing the expansion of the Demon Domain. Wiping them out rashly would trigger a catastrophic collapse of cosmic equilibrium.

Therefore, even if the day arrived when Zhou Hui resolved to overthrow the Heavenly Court, he could not simply walk away; he would have to make full preparations for the reconstruction of universal order.

—"Ah, that is far too distant, and far too exhausting for a retired old fellow like me. Better to focus on cultivating the disciples first,"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"Matters like these must ultimately be entrusted to the next generation."

Resistance without generational inheritance was nothing but fleeting smoke—just like the White Jade Capital of yesteryear, which had left nothing behind.

However, there were still things that could be accomplished at the current stage.

For example, Zhou Hui had originally planned to launch an intervention to counter the Upper Realm's Heavenly Dao, keeping them too swamped with trouble to meddle here.

After learning that the Heavenly Scripture in this realm had originally belonged to the Upper Realm, the viability of this plan had clearly surged. It might even allow him to counter-intervene against them just as the Upper Realm had intervened in this world.

Particularly given that within the destroyed sections of the Heavenly Scripture lay copious amounts of the Heavenly Court's [black files](# "Literal CN: 黑料 (hēiliào) — compromising dirt / scandalous secrets / incriminating dossiers") (archival records), which would assist Zhou Hui even further in manipulating the Upper Realm's Heavenly Dao.

This made repairing the Heavenly Scripture an absolute imperative.

Naturally, even without these practical considerations, out of sheer thirst for knowledge alone, Zhou Hui would still have chosen to restore the Heavenly Scripture.

Which brought him to the unavoidable necessity of traveling to the Southern Domain.

Because this "Heavenly Scripture" preserved knowledge in the form of a plant, and the Southern Domain happened to possess a secret treasure capable of pouring out spring water that [made withered wood bloom in spring](# "Idiom: 枯木逢春 (kūmù-féngchūn) — bringing dead wood back to life / rejuvenating the decrepit"). Medicines refined from it held extraordinary restorative properties, such that countless people had always journeyed to the Southern Domain to seek medicine.

Employing it to heal the "Heavenly Scripture" was a match made in heaven.

In fact, judging from the "plotline" that had unfolded in the sixth cycle, this secret treasure seemed to have been one of the Upper Realm's primary objectives.

Because the so-called protagonist had journeyed to the Southern Domain to seek medicine specifically to heal the injuries of his [red rose](# "Literary/Pop-culture allusion: 红玫瑰 (hóng méiguì) — the 'red rose' archetype from Eileen Chang's novel 'Red Rose, White Rose', representing the passionate, vivid romantic love interest"), and Shui Huang'er had fallen head over heels for the "protagonist" at first sight as if bewitched by Gu poison, nearly gifting the secret treasure to him outright. Fortunately, she had come to her senses in time later on and avoided foolishly giving it away. Yet precisely because of this, the clashing "protagonist" and Mo Xi had burned down the Sacred Tree in their battle, and the "protagonist" had snatched away a portion of the medicine amidst the chaos.

—"The 'protagonist' of this cycle should be the same person, right?"-Zhou Hui mused.

After all, across all seven cycles, this particular "protagonist" had come closest to success.

—"However, the Southern Domain's 'script' may very well undergo changes. After all, during the sixth cycle, Shui Huang'er's behavior broke completely free from their control,"-Zhou Hui added.

As for what specific calibrations had been made, Zhou Hui was rather eager to see how they intended to implement them.

The Southern Domain of this cycle would be vastly different from that of the sixth cycle.

The concrete catalyst for this transformation began precisely with this grand recruitment drive.

At this thought, Zhou Hui finally revealed a faint, pleased smile.

Meanwhile, his divine sense picked up that Boss Tao had already arrived outside his immortal cave. Accompanying him were the twins, Mo Bei and Mo Xi—the reason those two had bumped into Guan Ye and Lu Shulong on the mountainside path earlier was precisely because Master had summoned them to his cave.

—"Untiring, you wouldn't happen to be heading to the Southern Domain to demand their secret treasure, would you?"-Tao Zhu asked.

He had turned it over in his mind all along the way and concluded that the most optimistic reason for Zhou Hui inviting him to the Southern Domain was to seek medicine to heal the Heavenly Scripture.

And Tao Zhu had indeed hit the mark.

Yet the moment Boss Tao stepped into Zhou Hui's immortal cave, he still shouted preemptively in an attempt to leave Untiring zero room to press charges:

[—"For something like that, you can just go by yourself! I have zero ties with the folks at the Sacred Tree!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

—"Even without personal ties, taking a look is still worthwhile, isn't it?"-Zhou Hui chuckled.-"Actually, I'm not going purely to seek medicine. It is currently the [back-to-school season](# "Literal CN: 开学季 (kāixué jì) — back-to-school season; academic calendar term applied to the grand disciple recruitment of cultivation sects"). Not only is our Heavenly Spirit Sect conducting its grand recruitment, but the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree is doing the same. Their Saintess invited me to observe the ceremonies. Conveniently, since I 'accidentally' injured you earlier, you can also seek a little medicine to heal your wounds, can't you?"

—"Ugh,"-Tao Zhu grunted upon hearing this, rubbing his belly.

His stomach was currently deflated and sunken, appearing far healthier than his usual pregnant-looking silhouette.

Yet for Boss Tao, this state was entirely abnormal—it signified that something had gone wrong with the Golden Toad residing in his abdomen.

And this was precisely the injury inflicted by Untiring when they had contested ownership of the Heavenly Scripture earlier. If he had medicine from the Southern Domain, there might indeed be hope for a speedy recovery.

Yet gazing upon Yuwen Xuan's grim fate, Tao Zhu still felt immensely guilty and apprehensive about the whole affair:

—"N-no, there's no need,"-Tao Zhu stammered.-"My injuries aren't severe; they'll heal after recuperating for a while. Besides, they invited you. What right do I have to butt in..."

—"Actually, the one who wishes for Boss Tao to travel to the Southern Domain is me,"-Mo Xi piped up at this moment with a radiant smile.

Tao Zhu looked toward Mo Xi in bewilderment.

—"The Immortal Bazaar's grand opening isn't far off this year, and before that comes the universally anticipated grand finals of the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-Mo Xi said.-"I want to head down to the Southern Domain to negotiate a business deal. However, my experience is lacking, so I was hoping Boss Tao could [cast an expert eye over it](# "Literal CN: 掌掌眼 (zhǎngzhǎng yǎn) — antique/commercial slang for having an expert inspect goods for authenticity") and [vet the details for me](# "Literal CN: 把把关 (bǎbǎ guān) — guard the pass / oversee quality control and gatekeeping")."

These words sounded exceedingly pleasant, but...

[Why does this sound so painfully familiar?!]

[Right—not long ago, when Yuwen Xuan invited me to the Langhuan Forbidden Grounds, he used this exact same tone!]

[Isn't this just another brand-new trap?!]

Boss Tao practically wanted to [bristle like an angry cat](# "Literal CN: 炸毛 (zhàmáo) — fur standing on end / bristling with indignation")—if he had any fur to bristle with, that was.
