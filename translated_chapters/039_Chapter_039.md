# Chapter 39: Minor Sect Recruitment (第39章 门派小招)

Faced with Mo Xi's frantic yelling, Qu Qianxing merely cast him a cold, sidelong glance, not bothering to pay him any mind.

Zhou Hui, on the other hand, broke into a faint smile, reaching out to tap his youngest disciple on the forehead.

—"Stop playing dumb,"-Zhou Hui said.

This wasn't some video game; the rogue cultivators coming to petition the mountain gate hadn't randomly spawned in at four o'clock this morning. Over the past several days, people had already been trickling steadily into the vicinity of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and Zhou Hui and Qu Qianxing were in the middle of discussing this very situation.

According to Qu Qianxing's investigation and statistical breakdown, this applicant pool fell primarily into three categories:

The first category consisted of rogue cultivators who had been near the secret realm on the day of the July 7th trial, along with a handful of disciples from other sects. Having witnessed with their own eyes how the Untiring Immortal Venerable crushed Sovereign Immortal Chana and dealt Carefree Palace a devastating head-on blow, and having subsequently experienced the benefits of the reopened secret realm, they were utterly won over by the Heavenly Spirit Sect and thus arrived to petition the mountain gate.

These were precisely the prospective students Qu Qianxing had originally projected as their primary recruitment target.

Little did anyone expect that they would now turn out to be the smallest group by head count.

The second category, ironically, was directly tied to Carefree Palace's "propaganda" campaign.

Having lost Yinian Pavilion and Sovereign Immortal Chana, Carefree Palace blockaded the trade routes and launched a smear campaign against the Untiring Immortal Venerable's reputation. While plenty of gullible souls bought their nonsense, what Carefree Palace failed to anticipate was that the cultivation world [revered raw strength above all else](# "Literal CN: 慕强 (mù qiáng) — the psychological drive in the cultivation world to revere and flock to sheer, overwhelming strength")! Not to mention that Carefree Palace already carried a notorious dark history—having had their grand Treasure Appraisal Convention cleaved to splinters by Zhou Hui and their Tribulation Transcendence elder chased across realms by him. Chana's demise merely jogged everyone's memory. Merging past and present into one, Carefree Palace was reduced to a laughingstock. Coupled with illicit ["short videos"](# "Literal CN: 小视频 (xiǎo shìpín) — modern internet slang for short video clips, here referring to illicit spiritual projection talisman recordings") of the Untiring Immortal Venerable disciplining Sovereign Immortal Chana circulating in secret, it ended up triggering an explosive surge of new fans for the Untiring Immortal Venerable!

Among those who rushed to petition the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate this time, the vast majority were these very [strength-tier devotees](# "Literal CN: 强度粉 (qiángdù fěn) — gaming/internet slang for meta-slaves or power-chasers who worship characters purely based on top-tier combat viability"), with some having traveled thousands of miles across rugged terrain from the outer edges of the Great Wasteland in the Western Domain just for this.

The third category was rather bizarre, originating from the batch of supplies Mo Xi had hoarded and resold.

That day, upon hearing that Carefree Palace was blockading the trade routes, Mo Xi had jumped at the opportune moment to snatch up a massive stockpile of goods, planning to flip them for a tidy profit in the Central Domain. In actual practice, however, he ran into a bit of a snag.

The cultivation realm was a standard medieval world, and its civilizational characteristics closely resembled the ancient Cathayan civilization from the other world Zhou Hui had lived in—an agrarian society that prioritized agriculture while suppressing merchant commerce. This also meant that its commercial distribution channels were heavily regulated by the "authorities." In mortal realms, "authorities" naturally referred to secular mortal dynasties; in the cultivation realm, they meant regional sects (and the Heavenly Dao Alliance in territories where sects intermingled). Goods could not simply circulate without restriction—one required official trade permits.

Consequently, after securing that merchandise, Mo Xi could only release scattered, retail goods to supply the general market. As for supplies vital to certain sects, Mo Xi did not approach them right away. Carefree Palace was currently blockading the Heavenly Spirit Sect; even if those sects weren't on cozy terms with Carefree Palace, they certainly wouldn't give face to the Heavenly Spirit Sect either. Delivering goods to their doorsteps at this juncture would only get them treated as fat sheep ripe for shearing. Thus, Mo Xi merely had word leaked that the Heavenly Spirit Sect possessed inventory, waiting for those sects to come begging of their own accord.

Finally, there remained a batch of daily living supplies that cultivators had no need for whatsoever. Only after conducting field research did Mo Xi discover that life in the Central Domain was exceptionally brutal. Mortals here were unable to form unified states and could only dwell in scattered settlements. Most of these small settlements could not achieve self-sufficiency, relying entirely on trade with the outside world. Fortunately, the Central Domain possessed many peculiar local specialties found nowhere else, leading a handful of merchants to specialize in this trade. However, such transactions were strictly mortal commerce handled only by low-level cultivators. As a result, this category comprised the largest volume of goods yet held the least monetary value—merchandise so worthless that not even Carefree Palace bothered to seize it. Yet if the trade routes were severed, the ones who suffered the most were these mortal settlements; for them, it was an immediate question of basic survival!

Yet transporting these goods to mortal settlements was not only an enormous hassle, but the mortals—seeing unfamiliar faces instead of the traders they knew—might not even be receptive!

At that juncture, Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi had taken the initiative to ask Mo Xi for the assignment.

The brother and sister were originally born in a Central Domain mortal village, having fled after nearly being sold off due to famine and material shortages. This predicament seemed to strike a chord deep in their hearts; unwilling to see tragedies like their own unfold again, they shouldered the burden. Over the past month, they had traveled between numerous mortal settlements, successfully distributing that batch of supplies while bringing back a cohort of young boys and girls endowed with spirit roots.

Such was the origin of this recruitment's applicant pool.

By this calculation, two out of the three applicant pools were more or less linked to Mo Xi! This left Mo Xi unimaginably smug; if he had a tail, it would have been wagging high in the clouds, making him itch to hang a wooden placard reading "Heavenly Spirit Sect Admissions Director" around his neck and swagger everywhere.

Qu Qianxing paid his posturing no mind, continuing his report to Zhou Hui:

—"This one, this one, and this one appear to be from Thousand Beasts Hall. They disguised their appearances and identities, attempting to join our sect. Besides them, there are also applicants from the Wanhe Gang..."

What he and Zhou Hui were actually scrutinizing was still that smallest first group of applicants.

Specifically, the disciples from other sects mingled among them.

In the cultivation realm, this was a cardinal taboo!

Once one entered a sect, regardless of whether one had a master, unless one was formally expelled from the sect, one could never lightly [change sect allegiance](# "Literal CN: 换门派 / 改换门庭 (gǎi huàn mén tíng) — changing ancestral door/allegiance; defecting from one's sect to another"). If one already possessed a master, doing so was considered an outright act of treason and filial rebellion.

Mo Xi's reaction was textbook pragmatic:

—"These people ran over to us simply because our sect offers better benefits. If another sect offers better perks tomorrow, won't they just jump ship to them instead? There are barely any of them anyway, let's just reject them!"

—"Not necessarily,"-Qu Qianxing said equitably.-"A few of them are remarkably fine seedling material. If their own sects' conditions weren't excessively harsh, they might never have come."

—"Then let's just recruit them! There are barely any of them anyway."

Using the exact same excuse whether arguing for or against, Qu Qianxing couldn't resist reaching over to tap Mo Xi's forehead as well.

—"No,"-Zhou Hui pondered for a long moment, ultimately shaking his head.

He wished he could accept them all, but objective reality did not allow it.

After all, this world differed from that other world: there was no vast array of schools and teachers to choose from, and switching sects was far from as simple as transferring schools.

—"It is already fortunate for an entire region to have a single sect,"-Zhou Hui said, furrowing his brows.-"At the very least, it ensures that children with spirit roots in that territory won't be overlooked—especially in a place like the Central Domain. Think about how many children Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi brought back: those youths possessed spirit roots, yet had never encountered a suitable master! Those with better luck might become rogue cultivators; those with worse luck simply have their talents squandered. No matter how harsh those sects may be, they at least offer a chance. If disciples bolt for another sect the moment they gain a modicum of capability, it will deal a crushing blow to those sects' recruitment, making them even more reluctant to accept disciples of ordinary mortal origin, preferring only [clan-born retainers](# "Literal CN: 家生子 (jiāshēngzǐ) — children born to hereditary domestic bondservants, fully bound to the master clan from birth")." In truth, quite a few sects were already doing precisely that.

When all was said and done, it stemmed from a severe shortage of educational resources; at this stage, they could not afford to encourage such practices.

No matter how much injustice those disciples suffered, they could only abide by the "rules" of the cultivation world for now.

Zhou Hui let out a long, quiet sigh.

Qu Qianxing nodded, striking through the names with his brush before circling several others:

—"These few hail from outside the Central Domain. Rumor has it they committed grave atrocities in the past. For example, this one murdered the son of the Heavenly Crane Sect Leader; and this one single-handedly brought ruin to over a dozen sects..."

—"Whoa! What kind of criminal rap sheet is this?! How do these people even have the guts to show up?"-Mo Xi gasped in sheer disbelief.

And so, with the assistance (and meddling) of Temporary Admissions Director Mo Xi, Qu Qianxing and Zhou Hui continued reviewing and investigating the backgrounds of their prospective disciples.

`*`

Outside the mountain gate.

The applicants who had come to petition the mountain had no idea the Untiring Immortal Venerable was already screening their qualifications ahead of time. Nonetheless, they remained reverently prostrated on the ground, kowtowing toward the mountain gate at regular intervals.

This was not the Heavenly Spirit Sect deliberately creating difficulties; rather, it was standard procedure for petitioning a mountain gate.

In sects that placed heavy emphasis on etiquette, there were rigid regulations specifying how many paces separated each person, the exact posture of their prostration, and how many times they had to kowtow per [shichen](# "Two-hour time block in traditional Chinese timekeeping"). The moment one failed to comply, one would be eliminated from the running.

Naturally, there were those who couldn't stand this rigid protocol.

—"How much longer do we have to kneel?!"-Zhao Yuegui whispered under his breath.

He despised this kind of ritual above all else, which was why he had never joined a sect back in the Eastern Domain, where multiple immortal gates were readily available.

Beside Zhao Yuegui, a young man draped in fine silks and satins—looking like a young master from a wealthy clan—whispered back:

—"At the shortest, until noon; at the longest, midnight tonight. Generally speaking, half a day is standard."

—"Good heavens!"-Zhao Yuegui almost wanted to wail toward the sky.

Yet his posture did not waver in the slightest, following the crowd in performing a standard kowtow.

There was no helping it. If heading to the secret realm to scout out future sect options back then had been partially born of desperation, after witnessing the Untiring Immortal Venerable's overwhelming might that day and the sheer speed with which Qu Qianxing subdued the secret realm, he had become utterly devoted to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

He felt the Heavenly Spirit Sect was well worth a few bows and prostrations.

It was just that Zhao Yuegui had lived free and unfettered for far too long; simply kneeling in silence made him feel horribly awkward, so he couldn't resist striking up a conversation with those around him.

Curious, he asked the young master beside him:

—"You sound quite familiar with petitioning sects! Have you petitioned other mountain gates before?"

—"I... I suppose?"-the young master mumbled, clearly unwilling to elaborate.

To Zhao Yuegui's left, someone chuckled:

—"Who asks a question like that! If someone petitioned other sects and is still kneeling here, isn't that basically admitting they were rejected?"

The young master immediately hung his head, his face flushing crimson, essentially confirming it.

Seeing this, Zhao Yuegui felt a twinge of guilt and glanced to his left. Those two, at least, were familiar faces—the burly cultivator and the thick-eyebrowed female cultivator he had run into outside the secret realm. The three of them truly shared some strange twist of fate: meeting at the secret realm entrance that day, and now kneeling together before the Heavenly Spirit Sect's gates.

Zhao Yuegui poked fun at them without ceremony:

—"Didn't you two earnestly warn me back then that the Heavenly Spirit Sect was an absolute dead end? Why did you come anyway?"

—"Heh, [a great woman knows when she was wrong and corrects her ways](# "Playful gender inversion of the classic Chinese proverb: 'A great man knows when he is wrong and corrects it' (君子/大男子知错能改)"),"-the thick-eyebrowed female cultivator declared with utter shamelessness.

The burly cultivator, however, remained stiff and silent, only hurriedly bending down when it was time to kowtow, his movements noticeably clumsy.

—"What's wrong with him?"-Zhao Yuegui asked, puzzled.

—"His old ailment acting up,"-the thick-eyebrowed woman said dismissively.-"Don't let his hulking frame fool you—the moment an important occasion rolls around, he turns into this bundle of nerves. Heaven only knows what he'll look like when the formal discipleship ceremony actually begins."

—"Aren't you two nervous at all?!"-the burly man snapped, flustered and irritated.-"The Immortal Venerable here is renowned for his strictness! I heard there's even an exam to enter the sect, and heaven knows what will be tested—ah, but you two probably aren't afraid at all, chattering away like magpies! You're already prepared to get screened out for lack of decorum, aren't you?!"

Stung by his rebuke, the other three hastily clamped their mouths shut, straightening their kneeling postures to appear more standard.

Yet once they turned serious, the anxiety crept right back in.

[...Damn it, now I'm getting worried too,]-Zhao Yuegui muttered internally, unable to resist glancing around.

In truth, the burly man was far from the only one sweating bullets. Looking forward, a female cultivator's face was chalk-white and her body rigid, prompting Zhao Yuegui to ask under his breath:

—"Hey, are you alright?"

"..."

—"Hey?"-Zhao Yuegui hesitated, unsure whether he should tap her shoulder.

This female cultivator looked on the verge of fainting. He didn't want to leave her unattended, but if he shifted, his prostration posture would surely break—would that be deemed insincere?

He did not have to deliberate for long, however, as a sound shattered his train of thought.

It was a soft *pop*.

The sound of a flower blooming.

Such a faint sound, unless in the dead silence of late night, shouldn't have been audible to anyone. Yet it detonated in Zhao Yuegui's ears both delicately and clearly.

Subconsciously, Zhao Yuegui lifted his head to look. A splash of vivid pink burst into his vision.

Ah. It bloomed.

Peach blossoms.

Zhao Yuegui thought dumbly.

His mind went blank as he watched a single petal twirl down from the branch like a weightless dancer or a vernal flower sprite, swaying gracefully right before his eyes.

Zhao Yuegui instinctively reached out to catch it. But when he opened his palm, there was no physical petal—only a glowing pink petal mark etched into his skin.

At that moment, another sound resonated:

*DONG——*

This chime was deep, long, and resonant, striking straight into Zhao Yuegui's brain. Jarred awake, he raised his gaze to see an open pathway materializing through the ancient peach forest that had previously shrouded the mountain, sprawling wide into the depths of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

—"The mountain gate! The mountain gate has opened!"-someone cried out in astonishment.

—"Ah, the gate is open? So fast?"-Zhao Yuegui exclaimed in shock.

Though he had been grumbling moments ago, he knew discipleship rituals weren't short—just as the young master beside him had noted, half a day at minimum, a full day at most. Yet they had been kneeling outside the gate for less than one shichen!

Just then, Zhao Yuegui felt a scorching warmth in his palm. Looking down in surprise, he discovered the peach petal mark he had caught was glowing brightly.

It struck Zhao Yuegui in an instant: this was an acknowledgment.

Acceptance by the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

Before Zhao Yuegui could fully savor the joy, the young master beside him cheered ecstatically:

—"I passed! I passed! I was chosen!"

[No, I heard there's still an exam—this is only step one!]-Zhao Yuegui wanted to say.

Yet he wasn't so tactless as to pour cold water on someone at such a moment. Turning toward his other two acquaintances, the thick-eyebrowed female cultivator generously held out her palm to show him the glowing petal mark, while the burly man stared at his own palm with a goofy, ear-to-ear grin, clearly having made the cut as well.

Seeing the burly man's grin, Zhao Yuegui remembered the girl he had been worried about before the blossoms bloomed. Hurriedly looking forward, he saw she had already stood up; though her complexion remained pale, it was no longer as deathly as before.

—"Fellow Daoist, you seemed rather unwell earlier..."-Zhao Yuegui asked after a brief hesitation.

—"It's nothing, I was just too tense,"-the girl replied.-"It seems I... failed? But I actually feel relieved."

—"Ah..."-Zhao Yuegui was momentarily at a loss for words.

Yet not everyone took rejection with such equanimity. Before Zhao Yuegui could find his words, a spiritual pressure thick with malice surged from the rear of the crowd.

Zhao Yuegui and the others turned in horror to look, only to see the crowd parting amid panicked shrieks, revealing a cultivator with decent facial features but drenched in sinister, suffocating bloodlust.

—"I refuse to accept this!"-the cultivator roared at the top of his lungs.-"I possess a Fire Heavenly Spirit Root! How can supreme talent such as mine not even qualify to enter your Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"

His outcry instantly ignited heated murmurs throughout the crowd.

Zhao Yuegui's eyes widened in shock.

A Heavenly Spirit Root!

If even someone of that caliber couldn't enter the Heavenly Spirit Sect, why on earth was he chosen...? Confused, he stared down at his own palm, while the young master beside him looked equally rattled and unsettled.

Voices began chiming in from the crowd:

[—"That's right! Why?"](# "Speaker: Agitated Applicant")

[—"I traveled all the way from the Northern Domain for this kind of result?!"](# "Speaker: Agitated Applicant")

[—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable is nothing special after all!"](# "Speaker: Agitated Applicant")

Amid these rising doubts, the scene began to descend into turmoil. But in the end, a voice echoing from deep within the Heavenly Spirit Sect overpowered all the clamor:

—"Jin Yibing. Origin: Western Domain. Once, purely for your own amusement, you slaughtered an entire city of mortal civilians, and when cultivators stepped forward to stop you, you disemboweled them and hung their entrails from the city walls. Truly devoid of humanity and cruel to the extreme! Such 'talent' is something our Heavenly Spirit Sect cannot afford."

Along with the voice, a slender figure drifted down like a falling petal, alighting delicately upon a branch of the peach tree.

That breathtaking, ethereal grace completely eclipsed the sea of blossoming peach flowers across the tree, causing the cultivators gathered before the gate to hold their breath.

A countenance like that was impossible to forget once seen. Zhao Yuegui recognized at first glance that this beauty was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Second Senior Brother, Qu Qianxing. However, unlike his holy, inviolable presence amidst the thunder that day, Qu Qianxing beneath the peach blossoms appeared more delicate than the flowers themselves, seemingly harmless, making it deceptively easy to underestimate his power.

Only Zhao Yuegui, having witnessed Second Senior Brother's awe-inspiring display with his own eyes that day, quickly realized that the "city-slaughtering butcher" Qu Qianxing referred to had to be the very cultivator protesting right now.

He stole a glance toward the man.

The cultivator was visibly stunned for a moment by Qu Qianxing's appearance, but he quickly flashed a contemptuous sneer. Far from denying his bloody atrocities, he barked boldly:

—"It wasn't just one city! When the mood strikes me, the mortal cities I've wiped out are too many to count! That day, only a few blind fools poked their noses into my business, which was the only reason the world found out. But so what? When all is said and done, to people like us, what difference is there between mortals and ants? Compared to the Untiring Immortal Venerable, my deeds are nothing. He even dared execute an Immortal Sovereign of the Tribulation Transcendence stage, yet somehow he reared a spineless coward like you!"

—"Excuse me?!"-Qu Qianxing's eyes widened in disbelief, purple lightning detonating violently around him.

Clearly, Qu Qianxing was furious.

This Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple, who had conversed calmly even before the Tribulation Transcendence Sovereign Immortal Chana, was genuinely enraged at this moment.

Qu Qianxing's pupils contracted as he hissed icily:

—"What right does a beast like you have to let my Master's revered title pass through that filthy mouth—much less presume to compare yourself to him?!"

[Ah... so that's what set him off?]-Zhao Yuegui thought.

In the next heartbeat, purple lightning poured down toward the cultivator like torrential rain.

The cultivator was provoked into fury as well:

—"A mere Golden Core brat dares insult me?!"-his spiritual pressure swelled violently, crushing nearby low-level cultivators so hard they couldn't even stand upright.

—"Nascent Soul! He has Nascent Soul cultivation!"-someone screamed in horror.

Transforming into a plume of black fire, the Nascent Soul cultivator charged straight toward Qu Qianxing, who possessed only a Golden Core cultivation!

Seeing this, some shut their eyes, unable to bear watching the beauty get obliterated; others instinctively raised defensive barriers, dreading collateral damage; still others watched in breathless suspense, expecting higher-level seniors from the Heavenly Spirit Sect to intervene and rescue him.

Yet Qu Qianxing neither dodged nor yielded, merely fixing his cold gaze upon that ball of black fire.

The instant the black fire reached his face, a bolt of celestial purple lightning slammed down from the heavens, instantly disintegrating the Nascent Soul cultivator into flying ash—before he could even let out a dying shriek!

Yet... a Golden Core... directly striking a Nascent Soul dead?!

Just what kind of cultivation art did this Second Senior Brother practice to be so terrifying?!

When the crowd looked back at the beauty standing weightlessly upon the peach branch, their gazes were drenched in reverence and dread, devoid of the slightest irreverence.

Qu Qianxing then raised his voice, his tone echoing across the mountain:

—"To prevent anyone from wasting their efforts in the future, let me clarify beforehand: our Heavenly Spirit Sect maintains 'Three Rejections':"

> 1. Those who commit heinous atrocities for selfish gain or fleeting pleasure will not be accepted;
> 2. Those who tread evil paths and use unscrupulous methods to raise their cultivation will not be accepted;
> 3. Those who already possess a master and have joined another sect will not be accepted!

—"Furthermore: if anyone from the first two categories dares set foot in Heavenly Spirit Sect territory, they will be slain without mercy—"

As Qu Qianxing's final words fell, several individuals in the crowd who had harbored ill intent realized things had gone south and turned to flee. But Qu Qianxing's lightning was far swifter than their heels, striking down with blinding, split-second speed.

Over a dozen more people were turned into flying ash just like that!

Even those who had already been "acknowledged" watched this scene with shaken souls—some seized with terror, others trembling in awe. Zhao Yuegui, however, had no spare thoughts for that; he merely looked forward in worry.

He remembered that the girl had also failed. Did she also fall under one of the "Three Rejections"? Yet since lightning hadn't struck beside him, she must be...?

Just as Zhao Yuegui pondered this, he realized the space ahead was completely empty. The girl had vanished without a trace when nobody was looking.

Yet he had no time to dwell on such a minor detail.

Having purged the beasts mingled among the applicants, Qu Qianxing opened the mountain gate and signaled the new recruits to enter. Zhao Yuegui hurried along with the crowd, never discovering in the end why the girl had been rejected.

`*`

[As expected of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Second Senior Brother!]

[So strikingly gorgeous, yet so terrifyingly strong!]

[Thank goodness he doesn't punish the third category. Otherwise, wouldn't a traitorous wretch like me who 'betrayed her sect' have been turned to flying ash as well?]

Xia Bing thought with immense relief.

She walked against the tide of people entering the Heavenly Spirit Sect, slipping into the adjacent woods.

Fortunately, hailing from Thousand Beasts Hall, she was accustomed to tracking and hunting spiritual beasts, recognizing the beast trail through the forest at a glance, allowing her to navigate the dense woods without losing her way.

Yet having learned such skills from Thousand Beasts Hall, she had still come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to "try her luck." Not being arrested and repatriated to her original sect was already giving her immense face; how could they possibly accept her?

Xia Bing told herself with a self-deprecating, bitter smile.

Indeed, she was the very girl who had knelt in front of Zhao Yuegui.

Coming to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to petition the mountain gate this time hadn't been an impulse. Ever since the secret realm acknowledged its master that day and Xia Bing was detained in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, she had harbored this vague notion. But she had practiced Thousand Beasts Hall's cultivation art, which made switching sects difficult, so she suppressed the thought. Yet later, when the Heavenly Spirit Sect reopened the secret realm and Xia Bing discovered that the realm could also boost cultivation, her desire to change sects could no longer be contained.

After all, having witnessed a far superior sect, who would willingly remain in a hellhole like Thousand Beasts Hall?

Xia Bing knew that several disciples in her sect harbored the exact same thought.

However, in the cultivation realm, [changing sect allegiance](# "Literal CN: 改换门庭 (gǎi huàn mén tíng) — changing ancestral door/allegiance; defecting from one's sect to another") was an exceptionally grave matter. Once caught, one faced harsh retribution from both sects. Furthermore, Thousand Beasts Hall truly held the grace of rearing and instructing her, leaving Xia Bing torn and unable to fully commit even while kneeling before the Heavenly Spirit Sect's gates.

Now that she was rejected, she actually felt a weight lifted from her shoulders.

[Give up. Thousand Beasts Hall is your fate after all.]

Xia Bing told herself, walking slowly down the mountain. Inadvertently, she caught sight of a tall, imposing figure ahead.

A "man"?

Xia Bing thought in bewilderment, warily eyeing his back. She instinctively lightened her footsteps, trying her best not to attract his attention.

She hadn't forgotten that she was walking along a beast trail!

Even for a disciple from Thousand Beasts Hall like herself, treading such rugged terrain was arduous, yet that man walked as though strolling along a sunlit grand boulevard, utterly leisurely and unhurried.

In Xia Bing's experience, if this wasn't a spectral wraith, it had to be a fiend or demon.

Most terrifying of all, Xia Bing could not sense his cultivation in the slightest. Even with Qu Qianxing, who could strike down ten of her at once, she could clearly perceive he was at the Golden Core stage—so just imagine how terrifying this entity must be!

Yet how could such a foul thing exist within Heavenly Spirit Sect territory?

Since she had already been rejected, Xia Bing had no desire to meddle in another sect's business, nor did she possess the cultivation to do so. Xia Bing attempted to alter her path, fleeing before drawing the attention of whatever that creature was.

Unfortunately, the more nervous one is, the easier it is to make a mistake.

She hadn't taken many steps when she felt something soft beneath her foot, letting out a sharp *snap*.

[It's over!]

Xia Bing looked toward the "demon" in panic, but saw that he didn't seem to notice her. Only then did she slowly pull her foot away, taking a second to look down.

With just that one glance, she could no longer tear her eyes away.

It was actually a book!

A book bearing the title *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*!

Anyone reading the title would know this book was inextricably bound to Thousand Beasts Hall. In truth, it was indeed so.

This technique was the core, foundational cultivation art of Thousand Beasts Hall!

Being the core manual, it wasn't something just anyone could read. In reality, the full manual was accessible only to core direct disciples; even inner sect disciples were only granted "permissible study materials" based on their cultivation tier.

For instance, the two fragmented pages Xia Bing possessed—she didn't even know which part of the manual they belonged to. Only upon successfully breaking through could one see the subsequent pages. Naturally, if one accidentally offended an elder or backed the wrong faction, all hope was lost. That was also why Xia Bing had gritted her teeth and come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect—just their Library Pavilion, where disciples could freely browse and select manuals, was infinitely superior.

Yet right now, the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* on the ground was a complete, intact volume!

Though Xia Bing couldn't verify the contents inside, judging by the thickness alone, it was impossible to refuse.

Just as Xia Bing hesitated, a voice spoke:

—"This was something I borrowed back then after crossing paths with your sect's Patriarch Yusi. Meeting you today by fate, it should be returned to its rightful owner. Hand it back to the sect leader of Thousand Beasts Hall."

That voice came from none other than that "demon"!

So she had been discovered all along!

Xia Bing almost wanted to lie down and play dead—though that obviously wouldn't work; playing dead when encountering a bear was pure nonsense anyway, more likely to get one's belly ripped open, and wasn't this starting to look like a karmic opportunity?

Breaking into a cold sweat, Xia Bing didn't dare test whether it was real or a trap, politely declining:

—"This junior is merely an insignificant disciple in Thousand Beasts Hall. Even if entrusted by Senior, I have no chance of meeting the Sect Leader and cannot return the book on Senior's behalf."

Indeed, had she encountered such an "opportunity" under normal circumstances, Xia Bing might have gritted her teeth and tried her luck—perhaps the sect would even reward her. But she was currently at the Heavenly Spirit Sect! If the Sect Leader or superiors asked where she encountered this opportunity and retrieved their sect's core manual, wouldn't uttering the words 'Heavenly Spirit Sect' expose everything?

The "demon" was puzzled:

—"Aren't you a disciple of Thousand Beasts Hall?"

—"Y-Yes,"-there was no lying about this.

—"If you are, why can't you become the Sect Leader?"-the "demon" said willfully.-"So long as you become Sect Leader, consider my promise fulfilled!"

—"B-But I... this humble girl cannot!"-Xia Bing stammered frantically. She had never dreamed of the possibility of becoming Sect Leader:—"Thousand Beasts Hall has never had a female Sect Leader."

—"Who said so? Patriarch Yusi was a woman,"-the "demon" sneered.-"She just lacked backbone. After painstakingly authoring the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, she let her junior brother usurp her position. If you don't even fight for it, how do you know you can't?"

[!] How was that possible?!

Xia Bing's eyes widened in disbelief.

She looked up, but the "demon" was nowhere to be seen.

Xia Bing looked around in bewilderment, finding no trace of anyone. Everything felt like a dream, save for the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* at her feet, proving that what just happened was not her imagination.

—"No, I can't do it..."-Xia Bing murmured softly.

She stared at the book on the ground as if looking at something defiled, not daring to touch it.

Just then, a voice drifted from the other side of the bushes:

[—"I think I saw... over this way."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Someone was coming!

Xia Bing leaped as if someone had jabbed her in the back. Reflexively wanting to flee, she hesitated, glanced down at the book on the earth, and at the final second bent down, snatched it up, and shoved it into her robes.

Just as she finished, amidst rustling footsteps, two girls emerged from the foliage:

—"I saw that Thousand Beasts Hall disciple head this way... Ah, found you!"

—"N-No, I didn't come to petition the mountain!"-Xia Bing stammered, bewildered as to why they had tracked her down. Had Qu Qianxing changed his mind? Was he sending someone to strike down this treacherous wretch from another sect with lightning?

Her terror peaked when she recognized who the extraordinarily adorable girl among the two was.

That was Mo Bei.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Youngest Junior Sister.

Though Xia Bing hadn't interacted with her directly before, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Immortal Chess featured her piece; as the prettiest and strongest piece, Mo Bei was wildly sought after by all Immortal Chess players. Disciples from other sects who loved the game, even if they lacked the spirit stones to purchase a complete set, would buy a standalone Mo Bei figurine to carry around with them.

Mo Bei looked at Xia Bing with innocent eyes, tilting her head in confusion:

—"Petition the mountain? You're from Thousand Beasts Hall, right?"

—"Y-Yes."

—"Then that's right,"-Mo Bei nodded.-"I came over precisely because I heard a Thousand Beasts Hall disciple was around here. I want to discuss a business deal with Thousand Beasts Hall."

—"A business deal?"

—"Yes. You've been to the secret realm, right? Have you tasted A Cup of Sorrow?"

—"...I have. It was very delicious."

—"Thank you. A Cup of Sorrow specifically selects premium tea leaves and spiritual beast milk to achieve that flavor,"-Mo Bei said earnestly.-"However, as you know, our Heavenly Spirit Sect does not excel at beast taming. With sales surging, our beast milk supply can't keep up. So I wanted to ask you to bridge the connection between us and Thousand Beasts Hall for a long-term partnership to order a batch of beast milk."

—"I'm sorry, I don't really..."-Xia Bing instinctively moved to decline.

She raised spiritual insects; she had nothing to do with mammalian spirit beasts.

Yet Xia Bing suddenly realized: how strikingly similar was this moment to the "demon" entrusting her with returning the book!

Just over there, someone egged her on to become Sect Leader, and right here, a lucrative business deal fell straight into her lap?

Could this truly be a coincidence?

Xia Bing looked at Mo Bei. Mo Bei was gazing back at her with wide, cat-like eyes full of innocent curiosity. Xia Bing didn't suspect such an adorable girl harbored malicious intentions, but she strongly suspected this was a karmic opportunity deliberately arranged for her by that "demon."

Having entered and exited the Heavenly Spirit Sect's secret realm so many times recently, Xia Bing had naturally noticed how roaringly popular A Cup of Sorrow was; even she had occasionally been willing to wait a quarter of an hour to buy a cup of milk tea.

Yes—nowadays in Thousand Stars Hall, buying a cup of milk tea required waiting a full quarter of an hour!

Business was already booming, and with so many new disciples petitioning the sect today, milk tea would only become more popular.

For Thousand Beasts Hall, this was indeed a major deal too lucrative to refuse.

It was just that Xia Bing had previously thought it had nothing to do with her.

Xia Bing harbored no good impression of Thousand Beasts Hall's stewards and had no intention of acting as a liaison, not to mention dreading the exposure of her visit to the Heavenly Spirit Sect—which was why she had wanted to refuse.

Yet recalling the "demon's" words, Xia Bing thought:

[That's right... why should I hand this opportunity to someone else?]

[I don't necessarily have to become Sect Leader, but I can certainly leverage this chance to secure more resources, can't I? Since I can't leave that sect anyway, I must plan properly for my own future.]

—"H-How much do you need? Perhaps I could give it a try?"-Xia Bing stammered.

Mo Bei's eyes lit up instantly, filling Xia Bing with a deep sense of satisfaction, feeling that helping such an adorable girl was a super-correct decision.

Meanwhile, the girl beside Mo Bei warmly linked arms with Xia Bing:

—"Wonderful! Sister Xia, when you were staying at the Heavenly Spirit Sect earlier, I already thought you were dependable! But this isn't the place to talk—we can chat inside. By the way, I'm Yu Feifei, the manager of A Cup of Sorrow..."

Just like that, Yu Feifei led Xia Bing away arm-in-arm.

Mo Bei did not follow, merely standing blankly where she was.

The moment Xia Bing was out of sight, however, the innocent, adorable smile on Mo Bei's face vanished, reverting to her usual deadpan poker face.

—"Eldest Senior Brother, will this suffice?"-she asked into the air.

—"Splendidly done!"-a voice called from the tree. The very "demon" Xia Bing had dreaded leaped down, stroking his handsome face:—"Though girls these days are truly bizarre. When a handsome guy like me talks to her, she won't even spare me a glance; yet swap in a cute little sister, and she melts completely."

—"..."-Mo Bei cast Yuwen Xuan a speechless look.-"Still, was it really necessary for us to go to such lengths?"

—"Of course it's necessary!"-Yuwen Xuan declared firmly.-"As you said, our sect doesn't have enough spirit beasts. A massive livestock breeding powerhouse like Thousand Beasts Hall is an ally we cannot bypass! This directly impacts our disciples' future diet—we need a healthy supply of milk!"

—"Does Master know about this?"-Mo Bei asked again.

Yuwen Xuan averted his gaze:

—"Right, now that this one is handled, there's another rascal from the Wanhe Gang on the other side of the woods. Senior Brother will be off to swindle... ahem, guide him onto the righteous path!"

[—"Eldest Senior Brother?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")
