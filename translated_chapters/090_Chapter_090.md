# Chapter 90: The Test (第90章 考验)

While Qu Qianxing was progressively occupying~~(scratch that)~~ Villa Master Qingyang's temporary immortal cave and establishing his own authority, Eternal Villa's Qingyang Qingyu finally arrived at Mount Tianling.

Her foremost task was, of course, to pay respects to the master of this domain—who would also serve as her teacher for the foreseeable future: the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

As a disciple hailing from Eternal Villa—a clan that prided itself on ancient immortal clan lineage—even though Qingyang Qingyu was obsessed with cultivation, she had completed the full curriculum of etiquette, mastering the most cumbersome ancient rites. Thus, no matter where she traveled, she never feared breaching decorum; even if she went to Sky City, the current heart of the cultivation realm, her poise was enough to intimidate the "upstart" cultivation clans into silence.

Yet the moment this very Qingyang Qingyu laid eyes upon the Untiring Immortal Venerable, she experienced an instantaneous lapse of composure, so much so that when she bowed in greeting, the chiming jades at her waist clashed in disordered harmony.

Qingyang Qingyu did not even notice; in her mind, she was thinking in a daze:

[Is this the Untiring Immortal Venerable?]

[He is so good-looking.]

Yet compared to his appearance, what left a far deeper impression was his temperament.

Extraordinarily majestic.

Although high-cultivation practitioners all possessed considerable majesty that rendered mortals and low-tier cultivators terrified of looking them in the eye, that sort of spiritual pressure was typically generated by densely accumulated spiritual energy—a suppression purely at the level of brute power, from which one could be exempted simply by possessing equivalent strength.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable, however, was not like that. Likely out of consideration for her as a new student, he had completely restrained all spiritual pressure about his person, appearing at first glance like an ordinary mortal. Yet from within, he still radiated an awe-inspiring authority that made one hold their breath. Particularly when held beneath the gaze of those silver-gray pupils, Qingyang Qingyu still subconsciously lowered her head, not daring to meet his eyes.

Qingyang Qingyu felt that even if a bona fide True Immortal were seated here, it would be nothing more than this!

At this moment, the Untiring Immortal Venerable spoke.

His voice differed from that majestic aura, gentle and warm as polished jade:
—"...As expected of a direct lineage successor of Eternal Villa,"-the Untiring Immortal Venerable said.-"His Excellency the Villa Master claimed your aptitudes were decent, but now it seems he was overly modest. Decent is an understatement—your divine sense and cultivation base have been tempered with immense solidity. On the contrary, your meridians appear slightly depleted?"

—"Master Untiring overpraises me,"-Qingyu said nervously.

Inside, however, a jolt of alarm surged through her.

[As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable!] With merely a single glance, he had discerned so many things, even discovering her damaged meridians—this could not have been something Villa Master Qingyang told him, because the Villa Master didn't know either!

Years ago, Qingyu had recklessly cultivated conflicting techniques, damaging her meridians. But terrified that her ancestor would scold her if he found out—especially since he was already displeased with her locking herself in her immortal cave to cultivate every day—Qingyang Qingyu had quietly sealed herself in closed-door seclusion to heal her injuries. She only emerged once she had fully recovered; even her closest maidservant never knew what she had done. Yet now, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had seen through it at a single glance.

Feigning bashfulness, Qingyu attempted to gloss over the matter of her damaged meridians.

Fortunately, the Untiring Immortal Venerable did not press the issue to the bottom.

—Yet Qingyu sensed his gaze lingering upon her for quite a while, giving her the eerie feeling that she had been thoroughly seen through.

—"However, I require a clearer understanding of your cultivation progress,"-the Untiring Immortal Venerable stated.

With a wave of his hand, a long test paper slithered down from his desk, crawling like an elongated serpent until it arrived before Qingyang Qingyu.

—"Let us do some questions first!"-he said.

[?]

Qingyang Qingyu froze for a second before realization dawned:

[Is he testing me?]

[That makes sense. Even if it is merely temporary, a Tribulation Transcendence titan accepting a student would naturally be selective!]

[...Perhaps this is an excellent opportunity to have him "return the goods"?]

Qingyu thought to herself.

Naturally, Qingyu was merely entertaining the thought. Regardless of how dissatisfied she felt about this "exchange," she would never deliberately bomb the exam and bring shame upon Eternal Villa.

Besides, before Qingyu could accept the test paper, a voice brimming with laughter rang out:

[—"Master, may Fourth Senior Brother, Fifth Senior Brother, and I participate as well?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi">)

Accompanying these words, a remarkably cute young boy hopped out. Flanking him were two young men—one looking rather terribly gloomy, the other heartbreakingly handsome.

Only now did Qingyang Qingyu realize that she had been so engrossed in gazing at the Untiring Immortal Venerable earlier that she had completely overlooked the several youths attending by his side.

Judging from that cute boy's tone, they were likely the Untiring Immortal Venerable's direct disciples.

Qingyu had previously heard Qingyang Yang mention that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had seven direct disciples: five males and two females. The ones who had stepped forward were the young boy and the two young men he addressed as "Fourth Senior Brother" and "Fifth Senior Brother," while remaining stationary beside the Immortal Venerable were two female cultivators. Evidently, aside from Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan (still hanging from a tree) and Second Senior Brother Qu Qianxing (sent away on exchange), the rest had gathered in full.

Moreover, their gazes carried an undisguised element of scrutiny.

In particular, when that boy smiling so cutely looked over, his gaze was scarcely mere scrutiny—it was bare, undisguised hostility.

[Did I offend him?]

Qingyu wondered in bewilderment.

—"Xi-er, stop messing around,"-the Untiring Immortal Venerable scolded gently.

—"But Master, I want to see the prowess of this 'elder sister' from Eternal Villa too!"-Mo Xi wheedled in a coaxing tone, though the sidelong glance he shot at Qingyu was anything but polite.-"To be exchanged with our most outstanding Second Senior Brother, she must be extraordinarily formidable, right?"

[...I see. Is this the start of a hazing ritual?]

Qingyu formed a hypothesis:

Although Eternal Villa lacked neither resources nor spirit stones, wherever there were people, there would inevitably be a Jianghu. Even among blood relatives, the disciples of the Qingyang clan would still [pull hair in petty squabbles](<# "Literal CN: 扯头花 (chě tóuhuā) — pulling hair ornaments / tearing hair; modern internet slang for petty infighting and jealousy among siblings or palace consorts">) over trivialities like whom the ancestor favored more or who secured first-class treatment first.

Under the tutelage of a Tribulation Transcendence titan, the infighting would surely be far fiercer. Now that was...

[Utterly delightful!]

The corners of Qingyu's lips curved into a faint smile.

As a rare [top academic ace](<# "Literal CN: 学霸 (xuébà) — top student / study god / academic overachiever">) in Eternal Villa's laid-back atmosphere, Qingyang Qingyu loved nothing more than engaging in various competitions with her fellow disciples—for that always heralded the moment she could pry even more precious treasures from the hands of the clan elders.

If Qingyang Qingyu's initial willingness to be tested was a three out of ten, it was now at least a seven.

Qingyu looked toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable was furrowing his brows slightly, gazing at Mo Xi.

Zhou Hui naturally understood his youngest disciple very well, which was why he felt a twinge of displeasure.

After Villa Master Qingyang departed that day, Qu Qianxing had voiced his reluctance regarding the "exchange." Subsequently, upon hearing that an "elder sister (or junior sister)" was arriving on exchange, all his other disciples—save for Yuwen Xuan—had their fur stand on end. (As for Yuwen Xuan, he remained blissfully ignorant to this day, still hanging from the peach tree! Zhou Hui had no intention of taking him down anytime soon.) These brats insisted on coming to see this "elder (or junior) sister."

Coming to look was one thing, but intentionally provoking her was another.

[Are these children too possessive and overly dependent on me?]

Zhou Hui reflected self-critically.

Speaking of which, during one of his past reincarnations, Zhou Hui had once tried "releasing" this flock of disciples into the wild—not that he abandoned them completely, but rather, after resolving their immediate crises, he entrusted them to others. As it turned out, that lifetime and the one immediately following it (counting them up, they should have been the third and fourth reincarnations) turned into an enormous [field of carnage](<# "Literal CN: 修罗场 (xiūluóchǎng) — Sanskrit Asura battlefield; modern slang for a catastrophic mess of emotional jealousy, chaotic infighting, and traumatic drama">). Even karmic memory regressions failed to cleanse the "psychological trauma of being abandoned" from their minds. His achievement of "deserted by all and betrayed by kin" was also unlocked during those two lifetimes, forcing Zhou Hui to expend immense effort trying to correct it—without even knowing if he had succeeded. In the end, it was the fifth reincarnation, where heavenly lightning obliterated half the world, that inadvertently reset everything back to zero to start anew.

Reflecting on it now, perhaps he needed to foster their self-reliance more vigorously.

While Zhou Hui was pondering his pedagogical plan and preparing to dismiss the troublemaking disciples, he saw the young lady of the Qingyang clan turn toward him. Her gaze, which had been somewhat evasive moments ago, grew resolute:

—"Since this Junior Brother has spoken thus, Master Untiring, please allow us to be tested together,"-Qingyu said.-"I too am exceedingly curious about the caliber of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's direct disciples! For a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator to dare challenge a Golden Core, he must surely possess some extraordinary trump card up his sleeve, does he not?"

[My, this young lady is quite competitive!]

[Still, letting her teach Mo Xi and the others a lesson might not be a bad idea.]

With an inward sigh, Zhou Hui nodded:
—"Very well, since you agree as well."

He tapped his desk, and several test papers flew toward Mo Xi and the others.

Mo Xi winked mischievously at his senior brothers.

Yet the instant Mo Xi and the others reached out to take the test papers, they found the sheets suspended motionless in midair. Meanwhile, their Master's spiritual pressure bore down like a colossal mountain range:

—"Guan Ye, Lu Shulong, Mo Xi,"-Zhou Hui said.-"Since you actively volunteered to sit for this exam, if your scores are unsatisfactory, you know the consequences, do you not?"

—"...Yes!"-they chorused.

Instantly shedding his smug expression, Mo Xi dropped to his knees on the floor.

---

[I'm done for! Why are the questions this time so insanely deep?!]

After roughly scanning the exam questions, Mo Xi wailed inwardly in despair.

—"You truly are a fool, aren't you?"-Mo Bei sneered coldly.

Naturally, she was not speaking aloud to Mo Xi, but rather communicating through the telepathy unique to the twins.

In ordinary exams, this was naturally forbidden by Zhou Hui. However, as Zhou Hui had not originally planned to test Mo Xi today, he had not imposed a ban—though the twins secretly suspected that if Master wished, he could overhear their conversation whenever he pleased.

Hence, Mo Bei naturally had zero intention of helping Mo Xi cheat.

Not only did she refuse to help Mo Xi, she even mocked him mercilessly in their mental link:
—"You're a half-baked amateur yourself, yet you wanted to teach an elite disciple on exchange to our sect a lesson? I have never seen anyone as brainless as you!"

—"But when Second Senior Brother sat for this kind of test paper the first time, he was stumped too,"-Mo Xi protested defensively.

This was the true source of his confidence when issuing the challenge.

Mo Xi naturally never imagined he could breeze through Golden Core-level questions with ease. But with the enemy forces~~(scratch that)~~ guest playing on an away field and unfamiliar with the question formats, delivering an intimidating head-on blow should have been more than sufficient.

Mo Bei was hardly optimistic:
—"Second Senior Brother's sole setback? That time was purely Master wanting everyone to realize their breadth of knowledge was insufficient, so he designed the questions specifically targeting everyone's blind spots! That was the only reason Second Senior Brother slipped up—and even then, Second Senior Brother still took first place! This time is completely different. This 'temporary senior sister' has to be taught regardless, so Master merely wishes to see her strengths and determine what is suitable to instruct her in. Why would he deliberately make things difficult for her?"

[...Hmph. Even so, Master's test papers are not that easy to pass,] Mo Xi thought, unwilling to concede.

[I'm definitely not giving up!]

[I must let this 'temporary senior sister' know that even if her cultivation base is higher, she can never jump ahead of Xi-er!]

[I was the one who arrived first!]

Gripping his writing brush, Mo Xi struggled amidst a sea of Golden Core cultivation technique questions, searching desperately for any problem he was capable of solving.

Mo Bei rolled her eyes.

---

[—"This 'senior sister' is quite formidable!"](<# "Speaker: Lu Shulong">)

[—"...Don't talk to me. We are taking an exam right now!"](<# "Speaker: Guan Ye">)

[—"It doesn't matter. It's not like we're cheating anyway!"](<# "Speaker: Lu Shulong">)

Lu Shulong "openly and honorably" conversed with Guan Ye using divine sense, lifting his face to flash a fawning smile at Master.

As long as the newly arrived "senior sister" didn't overhear them, it was fine!

It so happened that both Guan Ye and Lu Shulong possessed extraordinary divine senses. Lu Shulong in particular could utilize dragon might as a veil, thoroughly screening out other mortals. (Against Zhou Hui, of course, this was utterly useless.) Thus, even though Qingyang Qingyu possessed a higher cultivation base, she remained unable to intercept their telepathic exchange.

Feeling secure with nothing to fear, Lu Shulong discussed the matter with Guan Ye:
—"The difficulty of this test paper was likely calibrated to 'Senior Sister's' scope of knowledge, right? Setting aside a few questions specific to Eternal Villa, strictly in terms of the depth of cultivation technique theory, she is actually on par with Second Senior Brother... No, in certain directions, her research goes even deeper than Second Senior Brother's. Take the third-to-last question: that topic offers little practical benefit to ordinary cultivation, so Second Senior Brother would never have researched it in depth..."

—"Are you trying to say Second Senior Brother is inferior to her?"-Guan Ye questioned.

—"Not to that extent,"-Lu Shulong replied.-"If Master issued an assignment, Second Senior Brother would certainly outshine her. But in terms of self-directed exploratory depth, Second Senior Brother loses."

—"Hmph, and what about you?"-Guan Ye questioned.-"Compared to Eldest Senior Brother—who at least makes efforts in advantageous directions—you have zero interest in anything outside medicinal pills, don't you? Every time your cultivation base increased, it was only temporarily boosted because you needed to refine pills. Only recently, with Master instituting exams, did you start studying properly. So, how about it? Can you solve them?"

—"I will... probably, maybe, try my utmost..."-Lu Shulong's voice diminished to a whisper.

Guan Ye's lips curled into a gloomy smirk, but the moment his gaze fell upon the exam questions, his expression hardened into solemn earnestness.

Lu Shulong was already at Golden Core; even if he couldn't solve the problems, he could still objectively assess Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu's theoretical caliber. Guan Ye, however, as Lu Shulong's senior brother, was merely at Foundation Establishment Late Stage. Moreover, cultivation techniques were decidedly not his forte, rendering him incapable of even comprehending some of the questions.

[I let success go to my head recently!]

[Because Master's instruction on thesis formatting gave us an early advantage, we gained the authority to speak before senior cultivators of higher cultivation bases. In truth, however, my foundational competence is still sorely lacking!]

Guan Ye reflected in solemn self-examination.

Naturally, he would not give up because of this. At the very least, he had to complete what he could.

Quieting his mind, Guan Ye began answering the questions he knew with meticulous care.

---

[My, these questions are rather intriguing!]

Qingyang Qingyu thought as she held the test paper.

Upon encountering these never-before-seen question formats, much like Qu Qianxing back then, her eyes lit up, quickly discerning the pedagogical benefits of such problems.

[This type of question can assess a disciple's mastery over the minute nuances of a technique with surgical precision, can it not? The slightest carelessness will plunge one straight into a 'trap.']

That she thought this way was naturally because she had already spotted the "traps."

During the first fifty questions, Qingyu still had the leisure to offer such assessments.

Yet past Question Fifty, her expression grew grave:
[This technique... actually possesses this possibility as well? I distinctly believed I had explored every conceivable permutation! Still, no matter—it can be deduced.]

[Ah, I must have practiced this before, but this is far too granular. Let me think... is it roughly like this...?]

Qingyu's brushstrokes grew hesitant. With a startled heart, she glanced at the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples. Seeing that their progress also appeared fitful and halting, she breathed a covert sigh of relief and pressed onward.

Yet past Question One Hundred, Qingyu was no longer merely hesitant—she was teetering on the verge of chaos:

[It can even be interpreted in this direction?! What do I do? Why am I suddenly feeling that everything I learned previously was wrong?!]

[Did I truly ever practice this technique?]

Even though eighty percent of the test paper comprised cultivation technique questions—her greatest specialty—Qingyu grew thoroughly uncertain. By this point, she was practically half-guessing and half-fumbling her way through.

Yet this was merely the beginning; the difficulty of the questions continued its relentless ascent.

These were clearly techniques Qingyu knew like the back of her hand, yet at this moment, they became utterly alien, standing before her like towering monstrosities demanding to know what on earth she had been studying all these years.

Staring at these questions, Qingyu suddenly felt she had been far too arrogant!

[Why did I always assume that I understood all the techniques I practiced completely? That there was nothing left to study! That I needed to travel to Sky City to comprehend higher-level truths, when in reality, I haven't even mastered the most foundational concepts!]

With reddened eyes, Qingyu lifted her face. At this instant, she felt a desperate urge to give up, only to see the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples likewise flushed crimson, locked in fierce mortal combat with their papers.

Among them, that remarkably cute boy happened to look up at that exact moment, revealing a pair of rimmed, bloodshot eyes remarkably similar to Qingyu's own.

Abruptly locking eyes, the boy was visibly startled. He rubbed the corner of his eye and ducked his head to resume his desperate struggle.

Watching his reaction, then observing the hesitant expressions of the other two disciples, Qingyang Qingyu breathed an inexplicable sigh of relief.

This was not the usual schadenfreude she felt during clan assessments when she answered questions others failed; rather, it was a profound sense of comfort born from knowing that someone else was suffering alongside her.

At this moment, Qingyang Qingyu even felt a twinge of gratitude toward these direct disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Had they not insisted on competing together, sitting here all by herself wrestling with these questions would have been utterly despair-inducing! She might well have lost the courage to continue cultivating altogether.

Yet now, she inexplicably discovered the fortitude to soldier on.

Dabbing the corners of her eyes with a silk handkerchief, Qingyu refocused her attention squarely upon the test paper.

---

[So this 'senior sister' finds it agonizingly difficult too?] Mo Xi thought.

He, too, breathed an inexplicable sigh of relief.

Yet looking at the densely packed questions on the paper, although he couldn't comprehend most of them, as someone who was always present whenever Master tested his senior brothers and sisters, Mo Xi had a fair sense of the exam's terrifying difficulty.

Consequently, rather than gloating, a sliver of admiration inexplicably surfaced within him:

[To be able to reach this extent, this 'senior sister' is at least not inferior to Second Senior Brother in cultivation technique theory, right?]

[As a temporary 'senior sister,' she is... barely qualified, I suppose.]

[B-But long-term is absolutely out of the question! Our Heavenly Spirit Sect already has more than enough disciples; there is zero need for one more!]

As if seeking to disguise his churning emotions, Mo Xi rustled through his papers with a loud clatter. In his mind, however, a sharp voice rang out:

—"Wait, don't flip the page! I think I can still take a crack at this question!"-Mo Bei cried out.

—"...No, why on earth are you getting all serious?!"-Mo Xi complained.

---

—"Aren't these questions a bit too brutal?"-Lu Shulong couldn't resist complaining to Guan Ye once more.-"New Senior Sister is looking rather pitiful!"

—"If Master merely tested her haphazardly, that would be truly pitiful,"-Guan Ye said solemnly, gazing down at the questions he could no longer answer.-"However, it is indeed exceedingly difficult."

—"Right? Exactly! If it were me trying to apprentice under Master and having to endure an assessment like this, I would have burst into tears long ago,"-Lu Shulong said with lingering dread.-"I am truly so fortunate that Master hatched me from an egg; I have been by Master's side right from the very start."

—"...Should I rejoice that my spine was ripped out back then? At least it spared me an entrance exam, [jie-jie](<# "Literal CN: 桀桀 (jiéjié) — classic sinister villain chuckle in xianxia web novels">)."-Guan Ye muttered darkly.

—"That's hardly necessary... getting your spine ripped out must have hurt terribly..."-Lu Shulong shuddered.

—"Precisely. So having an exam is far better,"-Guan Ye said.-"Don't give up so quickly either. If there are questions you can solve, put in some effort. You don't want to end up in Eldest Senior Brother's wretched state, do you?"

—"...I'm trying already,"-Lu Shulong's voice diminished to a whisper once more.

---

[Hmph. A total wipeout.]

Seated beside Zhou Hui, Ran Deng was lazily keeping count of the exact timestamps when everyone present met their doom.

These were, after all, rock-solid cultivation technique problems testing actual training progress; there was zero room for opportunistic shortcuts.

The first to crash and burn was Mo Xi. He only lasted five minutes before hitting a wall, spending the rest of the time cheating off his sister. (As Mo Bei's swordplay and cultivation technique complemented one another, her theoretical comprehension had actually reached the Golden Core tier; she had merely suppressed her cultivation base previously due to her Extreme Yin Constitution. Seeing that she was bound to make monumental breakthroughs within the next couple of years, she held out for a quarter of an hour.)

The second to perish was Guan Ye. His [specialty was completely misaligned](<# "Literal CN: 专业不对口 (zhuānyè bù duìkǒu) — modern college/career idiom: one's academic major doesn't match the job/task at hand">); cultivation techniques were precisely the area he could not practice. Thus, he lasted ten minutes. Afterward, he did not resort to cheating off Lu Shulong; instead, true to his meticulous, earnest nature, he diligently copied down the exam questions, evidently preparing to attend remedial lessons later.

The third was Lu Shulong. This kid belonged to the archetype possessing immense capability but zero diligence. Since exams were instituted, merely to dodge punishment, he would grudgingly study whatever fell within Master's announced testing scope—and managed to score full marks anyway, how detestable! This time, confronted with a test paper far exceeding his syllabus, his true colors were laid bare, yet he still held out for half an hour—even more detestable!

Only at the very end was this newly arrived Junior Sister (or Senior Sister?). As expected of a direct lineage successor from a prestigious orthodox titan like Eternal Villa, she certainly possessed genuine talent, with formidable depth to boot. It was merely that, due to traditional clan teaching methods, her scope of knowledge was somewhat narrow. On top of that, her psychological stress tolerance was slightly lacking—tearing up just from this, quite pitiful.

Yet this sort of exam was an absolute necessity for Master!

Although Mo Bei believed Master would not deliberately make things difficult for her—and Master indeed harbored no intention of doing so—given Master's pedantic rigor, how could he possibly instruct someone without first conducting an exhaustive [diagnostic baseline exam](<# "Literal CN: 摸底 (mōdǐ) — diagnostic baseline exam / assessing someone's foundational depth">)?

[The reason our daily pressure isn't that suffocating is because we spend far more time together, so the academic torture is distributed across the semesters!]

[So why on earth did these idiots willingly seek their own doom by volunteering for the exam?!]

Ran Deng reclined leisurely against her bolster like a [lump of salted fish](<# "Literal CN: 咸鱼 (xiányú) — salted fish; slang for a complete slacker with zero ambition">), rejoicing that she alone was safely detached from this worldly catastrophe.

Naturally, if even Ran Deng could see that they were completely incapable of solving anything more, Zhou Hui had recognized it even earlier. Yet seeing that these disciples had not thrown in the towel, Zhou Hui gently~~(?)~~ granted them an additional ten minutes of struggling~~(torment)~~.

—"All right, pens down,"-Zhou Hui finally announced.

Instantly, deep sighs of profound liberation echoed throughout his immortal cave.

The disciples watched with immense relief as the exam papers slithered away from their grasp. Yet the moment those sheets came to rest in Zhou Hui's hands, their nerves wound tight once more.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui showed no inclination to settle scores immediately.

He first stacked his own direct disciples' test papers on one side of his desk, bestowing a mild smile upon them:
—"I shall discuss your scores with the three of you at a later date."

Mo Xi immediately slumped face-down onto the table. Guan Ye and Lu Shulong inwardly regretted indulging his reckless mischief.

Zhou Hui, meanwhile, carefully scrutinized Qingyang Qingyu's test paper. Whether to save face for his own disciples or preserve Qingyu's dignity, he merely remarked:
—"I have a general grasp of your proficiency. Now then, what immortal art do you wish to study?"

[!] [He's asking right away?!]

Qingyu eagerly raised her reddened eyes.

If, upon entering this immortal cave, she still held that ['every domain has its own specialization'](<# "Literal CN: 术业有专攻 (shù yè yǒu zhuān gōng) — from Han Yu's 'On the Teacher' (师说): different people have mastery in different specialized disciplines; even a grandmaster is not omniscient across all fields">) and Zhou Hui would be unable to teach her, Qingyu was now utterly and completely conquered.

Almost abandoning all deliberate calculation, Qingyu blurted out:
—"I want to study XXXX, XXX, XXXXX..."-she reeled off a continuous barrage of names, all related to the techniques featured on the test paper just now.

Zhou Hui understood immediately:
—"No need to rush. I will review and explain your test paper to you in the future,"-Zhou Hui said, shaking his head gently.-"What I am inquiring about is your original intended subject... prior to arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

—"Ah... my apologies..."-Qingyu's face flushed crimson instantly.

Yet she did not believe she had spoken out of turn. Her ambition was far from merely correcting exam mistakes; having glimpsed the colossal iceberg from a single exposed corner, it was glaringly obvious that Master commanded an even more encyclopedic ocean of knowledge. But could she truly only pick one? [Why isn't he my true master?!]

This predicament of entering a mountain of treasures only to be permitted a single choice left Qingyu simultaneously thrilled and crestfallen.

Comparing the merits and flaws of several techniques in her heart, she found herself momentarily paralyzed by indecision. She honestly confessed:
—"I originally intended to study the 'Secret Realm Guidance System.'"

[Even for Eternal Villa's sake, I can never abandon such a golden academic opportunity!]

—"The Secret Realm Guidance System? Why do you wish to study that?"-Zhou Hui's eyes widened in astonishment.

[That is completely unrelated to this child's research specialization!]

—"I... I also didn't know what was best to study at the time,"-Qingyu explained in a fluster.-"But I've already..." [given up on that.]

Upon second thought, however, Zhou Hui mused:
—"Still, it is not entirely unfeasible. Using that as hands-on practicum, we can conveniently integrate and elucidate the cultivation techniques from this test paper alongside it."

[There's actually such a benefit?!]

—"Yes! I want to study the 'Secret Realm Guidance System'!"-Qingyu exclaimed with fervent excitement.

[—"Are you certain?"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

—"Certain!"-Qingyu affirmed resolutely.

—"Very well,"-Zhou Hui nodded. Although he still could not fathom why this child wanted to study something so distant from her academic major, since he had promised Villa Master Qingyang to instruct her in an immortal art of her interest, he would honor his pledge.

It was merely that he had previously taken for granted that her choice would align with her specialty; shifting to a new subject revealed a few gaps in her foundational baseline.

Zhou Hui promptly retrieved another thick stack of test papers from beneath his desk:
—"However, that system encompasses far more disciplines. You will need to complete this exam paper first..."

"..." Qingyang Qingyu's vision went pitch-black.

No matter how much she used to relish exams, she could not endure such high-intensity testing back-to-back!

—"Master! Wait a moment!"-someone abruptly cried out at this juncture.

Zhou Hui looked over. Discovering it was Mo Xi once more, he knitted his brows:
—"What is it now?"

—"I... I think..."-Mo Xi shot a glare at Qingyang Qingyu, muttering reluctantly,-"testing her again right away isn't very good, is it?"

[—"Hm?"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

—"Indeed, Master!"-Mo Bei chimed in at the opportune moment.-"This... Senior Sister just finished an arduous exam; her mind is probably still spinning. Let her rest for a bit first!"

—"That's right. I can prepare some refreshments to welcome Senior Sister,"-Lu Shulong offered with a warm smile.-"How about we go eat first before discussing this further?"

—"Right now... is too grueling,"-Guan Ye agreed quietly.

With several disciples pleading her case in unison, Zhou Hui suddenly felt he had indeed been rushing things too hastily.

—"Fair enough. Take a rest first; we shall resume tomorrow,"-Zhou Hui conceded gently.

The direct disciples immediately escorted Qingyang Qingyu out, as if lingering a single minute longer would invite further testing from their Master.

Watching their retreating backs, Zhou Hui's previous displeasure melted into mild amusement:
—"Young people after all—they bonded in the blink of an eye. It seems I misjudged them."

[...No, Master, why on earth do you think they bonded so quickly?]

[It's because they are [comrades-in-arms forged in the shared trauma of the very same exam](<# "Literal CN: 战友 (zhànyǒu) — comrades-in-arms / battle companions; here referring to fellow students bonded through shared academic torture">)~~(torment)~~!]

At his side, Ran Deng swallowed her inward screech back down her throat.
