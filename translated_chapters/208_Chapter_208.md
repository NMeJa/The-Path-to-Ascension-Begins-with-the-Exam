# Chapter 208: Amateur Troupe

—"We truly have stumbled upon something extraordinarily formidable!"

Zhou Hui hummed with cheerful satisfaction.

For several days, Zhou Hui had been meticulously disassembling this array disc.

Although Zhou Hui had recognized upon first acquiring it that its core was exquisitely perfected, now that he had dismantled all the slapdash, messy peripheral components to leave behind only the pristine, precision-machined array core, the more he examined it, the more he marveled at the sheer structural genius of its inner mechanism.

Zhou Hui was even somewhat reluctant to dismantle it further. Only after fabricating seven or eight replicated core samples in a single breath and running extensive simulations did he gingerly unseat a jade wedge pin from the Jade Pivot.

As that jade pin detached from the array disc, it instantly dissolved into a shower of luminous starlight, scattering around the Immortal Venerable.

Zhou Hui exhaled a sigh of profound appreciation.

It was the first time Yuwen Xuan had ever seen Master this elated; instantly, his stubby black tail stood straight up in smug triumph:

—"I knew Master would adore this treasure!"

He failed to realize that his tongue had slipped.

Since there was a "treasure Master would surely adore," by definition, there had to be "another treasure Master might not necessarily appreciate."

It was only because Zhou Hui was so thoroughly absorbed in studying the array disc that he lacked the bandwidth to interrogate him.

In fact, it wasn't just Yuwen Xuan; judging by Zhou Hui's rapt concentration, none of his disciples could likely pry him away from researching the array disc right now.

—And if Zhou Hui's own disciples couldn't accomplish it, then literally no one under heaven could.

Yet this array disc genuinely merited such rapt dedication, for the foundation powering its continuous calculation was actually 'Heavenly Secrets'—and not the fleeting, superficial 'heavenly secrets' that Jiuzhai or Ruwang could extrapolate or foresee. What sustained this array disc was a vastly deeper existence capable of deducing the very origins of the cosmos.

Consequently, the starlight scattering from the disassembled core was by no means haphazard. Through these astral particles, Zhou Hui could perceive far greater revelations:

Zhou Hui could see the parallel progression of multiple cosmic planes, the shifting of constellations, magnificent and orderly. Alas, they exerted gravitational pull upon one another, drawing nearer and nearer, until they violently collided in an unprecedented, cataclysmic Big Bang;

Zhou Hui could see that horrific explosion annihilate everything in the surrounding void, tearing apart time and space, fracturing the continuum into countless shattered dimensional planes. The advanced civilizations and higher-dimensional entities native to those colliding universes banded together, engineering a specialized apparatus to buffer the explosion, subsequently sealing off the perimeter to prevent the detonation from propagating further. Regrettably, even beings of their stature were powerless to halt the explosion itself;

Zhou Hui could see the detonation rage across tens of millions, hundreds of millions of years. Under the stabilizing influence of the buffering apparatus, the release of explosive energy gradually trended toward equilibrium. Consequently, within this stable, energy-dense sanctuary, brand-new life-forms unexpectedly emerged;

Zhou Hui could see that some of these life-forms possessed energy cores, while others were pure beings of concentrated energy. In the beginning, they resembled the indigenous organisms of the detonated universes, but through convergent evolution across aeons, they gradually manifested into the entities Zhou Hui was intimately familiar with: primordial beasts, spirit beasts, demon tribes, spirit races, and the human race... Among them, the demon race, endowed by birth with the innate ability to command terrifying raw power, took the initiative to conquer the majority of dimensional planes, establishing the mighty Demon Court;

Zhou Hui could see the frail human race struggling desperately to survive in the worlds subjugated by the Demon Court. They attempted to imitate the cultivation methods of the demons and copy the esoteric arts of the spirits; though they failed to grasp the underlying principles, the humans refused to yield. With relentless persistence, they explored and surveyed each dimensional plane until they ultimately arrived at the very epicenter of the primordial explosion, laying eyes upon the cosmic energy-stabilizing apparatus;

Zhou Hui could see that primitive humanity had no understanding of the machine's intended function, let alone its scientific mechanics. Yet that ignorance did not prevent humans from observing and learning the flow of energy through the apparatus. Over vast stretches of time, they actually synthesized a distinct, human-specific methodology for circulating energy: Immortal Arts;

Zhou Hui could see humanity, having mastered Immortal Arts, steadily growing in might. They disseminated these arts and cultivation doctrines far and wide, guiding more mortals onto the path of cultivation. Inevitably, this sparked a war of extermination against the Demon Court, culminating in the overthrow of demonic dominion and the founding of the Heavenly Court;

Zhou Hui could see that following the establishment of the Heavenly Court, humanity... no, the immortals, grew shockingly hidebound and self-satisfied. On one hand, they concealed the whereabouts of the ancient apparatus, fiercely determined to prevent any other species from gazing upon it and deciphering so-called 'Immortal Arts';

On the other hand, they reverse-engineered the buffering apparatus to mass-produce batches of crude cores, fabricating array discs. Yet these array discs were never intended to deduce cosmic physics—they were manufactured solely to calculate and identify beings that might pose a 'threat' to the Heavenly Court's absolute rule.

They rounded up these 'threats,' gathered them into worlds teetering on the brink of annihilation, and systematically liquidated them—

—"What in the blazes is this garbage?!"

Arriving at this point, every trace of Zhou Hui's smile had vanished, replaced entirely by profound exasperation at such pathetic squandering of potential.

Resentment born straight from the depths of his soul.

His spiritual energy wavered, slightly turbulent, causing the surrounding starlight to cascade into a dense meteor shower that sent the little black lamb scampering in all directions.

—"Master, Master, please calm down!"-Yuwen Xuan bleated in alarm.

—"I am perfectly calm,"-Zhou Hui replied frigidly, casting a sideways glare at the little black sheep,-"It is merely that seeing such staggering power persistently wasted on petty nonsense fills me with disgust."

The little black sheep felt immensely wronged. It felt Master was venting collateral fury upon it, yet it had zero standing to argue back.

—"Fourth Junior Brother should be arriving shortly. I'll go receive him!"-digging in its stubby little cloven hooves, the lamb scrambled out the door in ignominious flight.

Zhou Hui paid it no mind, gazing upon the half-dismantled array disc with a frustrated sigh:

—"Since you went through the trouble of forging this array disc, why didn't you calculate the fundamental truth? In terms of innate talent and biological aptitude, the demon race and spirit race outclassed humans by orders of magnitude. Why was humanity the sole race capable of comprehending 'Immortal Arts'?"

Naturally, it was because of humanity's cognitive capacity for learning and their inexhaustible spirit of perseverance.

That was the supreme 'variable.'

It was also the one component the array disc was fundamentally incapable of calculating.

If this 'variable' was excluded, forward extrapolations of the array disc would never have predicted the rise of the Heavenly Court. But if this 'variable' was factored in, the emergence of the Heavenly Court became an inevitability—just as it became an inevitability that the Heavenly Court could never permanently eradicate its 'threats.' For to a stagnant, paranoid Heavenly Court, its true mortal 'threat' was nothing other than that very 'variable'!

Naturally, the moment Zhou Hui engaged in this line of thought, the array disc began factoring the 'variable' into its matrix.

In an obscure corner of the astral river, a brand-new star sparked into existence. At first, its radiance was faint and inconspicuous, easily overlooked; yet it steadily began perturbing the trajectories of all surrounding stars. In particular, the select few stars designated as 'threats' flew toward that newborn star as though compelled by an irresistible gravitational pull.

Zhou Hui shifted his gaze toward that newly manifested star.

Within it, he saw himself.

In the realm hitherto uncalculated by the astral array disc, the Zhou Hui manifested by that star was treading resolutely along his Grand Dao—mirroring the weak yet unyielding humanity that had once defied the dominion of the ancient Demon Court.

No, precisely because he was merely an ordinary human, he embodied the quintessential attributes of humanity.

In other words, even without him, someone else would have stepped forward to walk this path.

Such was Zhou Hui's conviction.

He watched the reflection of himself within the starry matrix with cool detachment. The other Zhou Hui seemed to sense his gaze, lifting pupils that appeared to see through all creation to peer across the void. Yet before their gazes could truly align, both indifferently looked away, utterly devoid of vanity.

To be sure, if this array disc continued its deduction, it could even extrapolate Zhou Hui's personal future. But that was the single domain Zhou Hui cared about least.

—"There isn't a single mention of gods and demons!"

—"And the raw computational data is the most boring drivel imaginable!"

—"Who cares about the Heavenly Court? Where is the operational data of the buffering apparatus?! Where are the energy metrics of the primordial detonation?! What is the current status of the outer universes?!"

—"With such a transcendent foundation in your hands, all you could obsess over was consolidating your tyrannical rule?"

—"Is being the king of an ant hill genuinely that thrilling?!"

Zhou Hui's belly was bloated with complaints.

If the brainless higher-ups of the Heavenly Court were standing before him this instant, he would unquestionably dunk their skulls into a bucket of water, pluck out every strand of sodden straw from their brains, and inspect what useless junk was scribbled on them.

—"Though inspecting them would be pointless. Wet straw is at best fit for toilet paper; it certainly couldn't record anything worthwhile. In the end, I'll likely have to ascend and see it with my own eyes,"-Zhou Hui sighed.

His gaze returned reluctantly to the array disc, still baffled by how anyone could squander their intellect on such petty politicking.

Speaking of which, the current supreme echelons of the Heavenly Court—that cabal of celestial deities and Heavenly Emperors—had originally been the very vanguard who rebelled against the Demon Court. Back then, they had risked absolute obliteration to reach the primal apparatus at the center of existence, observing its flow to comprehend 'Immortal Arts.' How on earth had they degenerated into this pathetic state?

The more Zhou Hui pondered it, the more his heartache deepened.

Just then, a dazzling star drew Zhou Hui's attention.

That star was the Tianquan Star, presiding over literary culture—the Wenqu Star. It ought to have been one of the primary pillars of the Heavenly Court, yet it was classified by the matrix as part of the 'threats.'

That was hardly surprising.

Given the stagnant, putrid cesspool the Heavenly Court had become, anyone possessing a shred of intellect would realize this state of affairs was unsustainable. Let alone the Wenqu Star, who inherently governed wisdom and scholarly attainment; he would understand the systemic rot far more acutely than anyone else.

Consequently, a Wenqu Star contracting the 'Delusional Malady' was neither the first occurrence nor a singular anomaly.

According to the array disc's logs, although the celestial stellar deities of the Heavenly Court were never formally deposed, the Heavenly Court showed zero mercy toward any existence that might jeopardize their dominion. Once all therapeutic methods to cure the 'Delusional Malady' proved futile, they would ultimately resolve to purge the diseased star.

Fortunately, the current Wenqu Star was newly born. Even though he had contracted the Delusional Malady at a tender age, the Heavenly Court had refrained from passing an immediate death sentence—primarily because regenerating a new stellar deity was extraordinarily difficult. Even with countless mortals ascending across the myriad planes, they refused to entrust sovereign pillar stars to 'outsiders.' Factoring in that this Wenqu Star understood nothing and had experienced little of reality, they merely exiled him to the mortal realm, meticulously scripting his mortal life trajectory so this 'naive' star would witness the boundless ignorance and malice of mortal fools, coming to realize that upholding the Heavenly Court was the supreme virtue.

Except the script was utter trash.

The Wenqu Star had never been a fool. Even if his traumatic experiences had warped his personality into cynical extremism, he remained exceptionally sharp at discerning primary and secondary contradictions. He certainly despised the vulgarity of mortals, but he loathed the chaotic disparity of power among demons, spirits, cultivators, and mortals far more—a disparity that inevitably warped the human heart. He had even harbored ambitions to radically overhaul the Heavenly Court's foundational hierarchy.

It was merely that his power had been insufficient at the time, prompting him to temporarily place his hopes upon someone else. Yet after personally incinerating his mortal enemy, the lad appeared to have figured things out; very likely, he intended to take matters into his own hands.

For instance, that Wenqu Star was currently situated remarkably close to the Heavenly Court.

—"...Wait. Why is Qianxing near the Heavenly Court? Wasn't he supposed to have traveled to the Demon Realm?"

Zhou Hui paused.

Though Zhou Hui could easily deduce the exact situation of his disciple with a simple calculation, ominous scenarios began uncontrollable brewing within his mind:

Speaking of which, why hadn't Qianxing and Shulong returned yet?

Truthfully, it wouldn't be surprising if Shulong got swept up having fun and lost track of time (Lu Shulong: *I didn't! That's slander...!*). But given Qianxing's responsible nature, how could he possibly feel at ease leaving the Immortal Arts Assembly, which he had meticulously planned for so long, entirely in the hands of his younger siblings?

Could he have been captured by something in the Demon Realm and forcibly dragged off to the Celestial Realm?

Zhou Hui worried inwardly.

Fortunately, as though responding to his concern, the starry array disc initiated an autonomous deduction to ascertain Qu Qianxing's true location.

Immediately following, war drums mingled with thunderous battle cries shattered Zhou Hui's worries.

He looked up; the entire astral dome had transformed into a colossal, blood-soaked battlefield.

Upon this battlefield, dense cloud banks weighed heavily over a bottomless abyss. Upon every single cloud stood ranks of heavenly soldiers and celestial generals, their radiant armor gleaming brilliantly beneath the sacred luminance of the Heavenly Court. In the abyss below, every billowing swirl of foul black mist teemed with grotesque, hideous demons. They glared toward the celestial host with lecherous, ravenous eyes, burning to drag the haughty immortals down from the heavens and reduce them to the same depraved state.

Yet at the absolute center of these fiendish demons stood an exquisite beauty who appeared thoroughly out of place, yet shockingly harmonious with his surroundings.

This beauty was so ethereal and striking that he looked as though he had no business standing amidst horrific demons;

Yet this beauty possessed an eerie, breathtaking allure that made it seem as though only the primordial abyss could truly embrace him.

The beauty himself cared nothing for his physical appearance. With frigid, unfeeling eyes, he gazed upon the heavens, lifting a slender arm:

—"Attack!"

Instantly, legions of demon hordes surged skyward, clashing violently with the heavenly host in a meat grinder of slaughter.

Witnessing this spectacle, Zhou Hui couldn't resist raising a hand to rub his eyes vigorously. Opening them once more, he found that his [ostrich-like self-deception](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — plugging one's ears while stealing a bell; willful self-delusion") could not alter the deduction's verdict; the gory carnage upon the astral battlefield raged on unabated.

—"Is there an error in this simulation's logic?"-Zhou Hui found himself uncharacteristically unwilling to confront reality,-"I assumed that introducing myself as a 'variable' would alter the deduced outcome to some degree... Did an operational blunder cause the original timeline to cross-contaminate the present?"

Murmuring thus, Zhou Hui waved his billowing sleeve, sweeping the horrific battlefield away into oblivion.

He paused for a heartbeat, lifting his hand to pinch a chess piece. He removed the newly introduced 'variable'—his own existence—reverting the astral sky to the baseline projection extrapolated by the Heavenly Court.

Without the gravitational tether of the 'variable,' the stars representing 'threats' rolled away into the far corners of the cosmos like loose marbles, no longer converging.

Yet Qu Qianxing's star remained virtually identical in position to before.

—"Still in the same spot? What was Qianxing's original destiny supposed to be—presuming the Heavenly Court failed to eradicate him?"

Zhou Hui felt genuinely curious.

Not just regarding Qu Qianxing; Zhou Hui was also curious about what earth-shattering deeds his other disciples would have wrought in his absence to drive the Heavenly Court into viewing them as existential threats, exhausting every foul scheme to purge them.

This was perhaps the sole scrap of amusing intelligence this horribly mispurposed array disc could offer him.

Yet before Zhou Hui could inspect further, he sensed the spiritual aura of two other disciples approaching.

Not within the fabricated astral simulation, but in real life.

—"You have arrived?"

Zhou Hui smiled warmly.

He refused to allow the simulated apocalypse to cloud his mood, receiving his disciples with breezy composure as though nothing had transpired.

In contrast, his two disciples appeared utterly spellbound, evidently stunned into silence by the disassembled cosmos revolving within the Dharma Treasure Tower.

—"Is this the array disc from before?"-Guan Ye was the first to recover his senses; his fascination was purely technological,-"How did it turn into this? What functions does it actually possess?"

—"Indeed, this is the array disc,"-Zhou Hui replied vaguely.

He had no desire to burden his disciples with the cosmic truths and apocalyptic extrapolations he had just witnessed—at least, not before he personally ascended to the Heavenly Court, reached the epicenter of the universal collision, and examined the primal buffering apparatus with his own eyes. Until then, he would not treat the array disc's projections as absolute gospel.

Zhou Hui merely advised Guan Ye:

—"If you wish to craft a tool of this nature, you must observe and log atmospheric spiritual currents and leyline fluctuations over vast periods, deepen your mastery of mathematics, and construct corresponding predictive models. Only then can you forecast future trajectories with reasonable accuracy."

As for copying an ancient energy core without comprehending the underlying principles, Zhou Hui adamantly discouraged it.

The Heavenly Court had already demonstrated that knowing the *what* without understanding the *why* would only produce cascade errors the moment unforeseen variables emerged.

*Speaking of which... is Qianxing genuinely on the frontlines of a war between the Celestial and Demon Realms?*

*How did he even get there?*

*It completely defies all logic!*

Zhou Hui's skepticism regarding the array disc's accuracy deepened further.

To verify reality, he asked Guan Ye:

—"How is the Immortal Arts Assembly progressing?" *Have your Second Senior Brother and the others returned yet?*

—"The opening ceremonies concluded without a hitch. The guests are full of praise for Eldest Senior Sister,"-Guan Ye attempted to speak favorably of Ran Deng to lighten her punishment, before preparing to report the other news,-"Also..." *Shulong has safely returned ahead of the matches.*

—"Indeed, everyone is looking forward to it immensely,"-Mo Bei interjected suddenly.

—Ever since entering the chamber, her face had been pale as she stared at the starry sky, her expression exceedingly strange.

However, Zhou Hui assumed she was merely overwhelmed seeing such a celestial artifact for the first time, compounded by her Yin spirit constitution naturally repelling Heavenly Court artifacts, so he didn't dwell on it.

Sure enough, once Mo Bei gathered her wits, her spirits rallied visibly.

With uncharacteristic enthusiasm, she pressed:

—"Master, you mentioned wanting to implement certain adjustments to the tournament format. How do you intend to execute it? As a competitor, I feel terribly nervous!"

—"There is no need to be nervous; simply perform to your customary standard,"-Zhou Hui smiled warmly,-"Besides, it is hardly a drastic overhaul. Do you recall the 'Academic Guidance System' of the Fire Abyss Divine Tree? With the Divine Tree's assistance, one can extrapolate the prospective futures of students to a certain degree. I felt introducing this element into the Immortal Arts Assembly would be quite beneficial. It was just that the Divine Tree is situated too far away and its computational support is constrained; I was originally concerned about insufficient computing power, but the array disc we acquired can readily bridge that gap."

Having said this, Zhou Hui glanced toward the star representing Qu Qianxing.

While he remained deeply skeptical of the projection regarding Qu Qianxing's current circumstances, that did not mean Zhou Hui dismissed it out of hand. He had always been accustomed to preparing for worst-case scenarios.

—"With this computational throughput, perhaps we can assign a 'theme' to the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-Zhou Hui remarked.

—"A theme?"

—"Indeed. Monotonous sparring matches are somewhat dull. We could establish a theme centered around a God-Demon War."

Zhou Hui rapidly outlined a blueprint in his mind, beginning to deliberate technical specifications with Guan Ye.

Their conversation steadily transitioned into an esoteric engineering exchange.

Mo Bei stood off to the side listening absentmindedly, her eyes darting involuntarily toward the starry projection again and again.

Only when Master and Fourth Senior Brother concluded their discussion did Mo Bei suddenly chime in:

—"Master, you mentioned multiple times that this array disc possesses the ability to deduce the future. Is this capability truly accurate? What I mean is... is the future it predicts guaranteed to happen?"

—"It is guaranteed,"-a trace of subtle derision touched Zhou Hui's lips,-"Provided there are no variables."

—"Variables?"

—"...Beier, did you come here with a specific matter in mind?"-Zhou Hui asked instead of answering. Like Yuwen Xuan, he recognized that Mo Bei was hardly the sort to [cross the threshold of the Three Treasures hall without a plea](# "Literal CN: 无事不登三宝殿 (wú shì bù dēng sānbǎodiàn) — visiting a temple only when one has a request; never visiting without a reason").

Mo Bei indeed had an agenda.

She had originally intended to lodge a formal complaint with Master regarding Second Senior Brother's catastrophic situation.

Guan Ye also recalled Lu Shulong's return, but figuring Mo Bei was about to report it since Master was questioning her, he held his tongue.

Yet Mo Bei asked:

—"Master, may I borrow the Heavenly Robe?"

—"The Heavenly Robe?"

—"Yes, the one belonging to Second Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei kept her face completely deadpan, resembling an honest child speaking plain truth,-"In the Immortal Arts Assembly, isn't there no restriction on utilizing Dharma treasures? I wish to borrow that artifact. But please be assured, Master, I will never abuse it. I have already heard of what Eldest Senior Brother and Eldest Senior Sister did; I swear on my Dao heart, I shall never—"

—"Certainly."

—"I shall never resort to opportunistic cheating... eh?"-Mo Bei's eyes widened in shock.

Zhou Hui looked upon Mo Bei with gentle warmth. Whether it was the scripted narrative of Heavenly Dao or the genuine past life he had never truly experienced, Zhou Hui had never harbored the slightest suspicion toward Mo Bei on account of her supposed 'betrayal.'

—After all, Mo Bei was not Yuwen Xuan.

—"If it is Beier asking, you may borrow it,"-Zhou Hui nodded,-"I trust you."

—"Master..."

Mo Bei lowered her eyelashes. Then, lifting her heterochromatic gaze, she looked resolutely upon Zhou Hui:

—"Master, I will never let you down."

Zhou Hui nodded with a smile, assuming Mo Bei was referring to the tournament.

He had wanted to tell Mo Bei not to stress over competition rankings, but considering that having competitive fire was a positive development, he swallowed his words.

Mo Bei said nothing further either.

Right until the very end, she did not utter a single syllable regarding the 'colossal mess' Second Senior Brother had orchestrated.

She was unaware that Master already knew—he simply refused to believe it. For Zhou Hui's faith in Qu Qianxing was hardly inferior to his trust in Mo Bei.

***

—"Fifth Senior Brother, I have retrieved the Heavenly Robe."

Upon departing White Jade Capital, Mo Bei headed straight for Lu Shulong's immortal cave.

Lu Shulong was currently hiding inside his quarters sorting through masks and robes, attempting to conceal his half-transformed dragon physique. Hearing Mo Bei's announcement, he spun around, eager to envelop her in a massive bear hug:

—"Little Junior Sister, I knew you were the most dependable—eh?!"

—"Shulong wanted the Heavenly Robe? Wait, hold on a second, Lu Shulong, why have you turned into this?!"-Guan Ye, who had trailed behind Mo Bei, cried out in utter shock upon laying eyes on Lu Shulong's current appearance.

—"Hehe, disaster has indeed struck!"

Yuwen Xuan stood there with a face full of gloating delight, leaning bonelessly against the doorframe like a demonic door guardian.

[Why did Little Junior Sister invite this ultimate nuisance along?!]

Lu Shulong recoiled several steps in horror, banging his hip against the table.

And the gathering was far from over. A moment later, Lu Shulong witnessed a thoroughly drained, half-dead Ran Deng trudge into the room, followed closely by a projection of Mo Xi—manifested as a phantom, his true body still anchored at the Fire Abyss Divine Tree. Laying eyes upon Lu Shulong's scaly dragon form, Mo Xi's eyes flared with intense curiosity and unadulterated schadenfreude, though he wisely kept his mouth shut.

—"What happened here?"-Ran Deng paused, visibly bewildered.

Lu Shulong was momentarily at a loss for words.

He had explicitly begged Little Junior Sister not to breathe a word to anyone! How had she ended up summoning the entire sect over?!

Lu Shulong glared at Mo Bei in sorrowful indignation.

Mo Bei paid her Fifth Senior Brother's glare zero mind, stating with deadpan solemnity:

—"It seems we haven't convened a 'Junior Siblings Conference' in a very long time. Therefore, I deemed it necessary to hold one."

—"What on earth is a Junior Siblings Conference?"-the Eldest Senior Brother asked in genuine confusion.

—"Generally speaking, it is a council convened by Second Senior Brother with you strictly excluded,"-Mo Xi 'helpfully' elaborated,-"Designed to deliberate how to resolve Master's worries without dragging the rest of us down whenever you cause a massive catastrophe."

—"Oh... HUH?!"-Yuwen Xuan's handsome visage twisted in righteous outrage.

Nobody paid him the slightest attention.

—"The crisis this time concerns Second Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei declared, tossing the 'Heavenly Robe' into the center of the chamber. Turning her gaze to Lu Shulong, she concluded,-"Fifth Senior Brother, I promised you that I would not divulge Second Senior Brother's situation to anyone else. Therefore, please explain the entire debacle to everyone yourself!"

[...Little Junior Sister, were you always this kind of devious Little Junior Sister?!]

Lu Shulong thought in profound agony.
