# Chapter 248: Assemble

—"Where the hell did you blast us off to?"-Ran Deng complained with a bizarre expression.

She had no idea that her own flesh and blood had just been delivered right in front of Taotie and a True Dragon by Sikong Yao, and was currently slated to be silenced to boot. At this moment, her bewilderment stemmed entirely from Sky City.

After Mo Bei and Qingyang Qingyu discovered the ancient heaven-and-earth restriction within the White Jade Capital of the past, Ran Deng had prepared, just as she promised, to send Jian Nuo back to Sky City. The plan was for him to inform City Lord Jian Haoge about everything concerning the demon invasion, so they could activate that long-forgotten restriction and permanently seal the spatial fissures through which the demons might invade.

The problem was, she couldn't find Sky City.

Or rather, to be precise, it wasn't that she was entirely unable to locate it, but that there were discrepancies in both the teleportation coordinates and the energy consumption.

In fact, strictly from the standpoint of a teleportation array, everything appeared completely fine. Had it been any ordinary array master, they likely would have muddleheadedly ushered Jian Nuo and Qingyang Qingyu straight onto the array center. Fortunately, after enduring a whole series of headache-inducing ordeals today, Ran Deng harbored superfluous anxieties regarding spiritual energy circulations. She kept wanting to verify how much accumulated spiritual energy was being expended. And with that single glance, she discovered the numerical telemetry had drifted wildly—by no means pointing to the original coordinates.

Curiously enough, communication hadn't been severed.

At least for the moment, it seemed perfectly normal.

City Lord Jian Haoge of Sky City didn't believe there was any problem whatsoever:

—"Sky City is naturally right where Sky City belongs. Where else could it possibly go?"

—"But the coordinates are wrong..."-Ran Deng pressed.

—"Either you miscalculated or you misread it. Since the teleportation array itself has no issues, what is there to hesitate about?"-Jian Haoge raised an eyebrow. He possessed little patience to debate technical details with a junior like Ran Deng.-"If you truly aren't reassured, go fetch your Master over... Speaking of which, where is Untiring? You're currently holding the Immortal Arts Assembly over there, aren't you? Where did that fellow run off to at a time like this?"

—"Uh, Master, he..."-Ran Deng stammered.

If she could find Master, would she be wasting her breath here?

Jian Haoge was, after all, an old acquaintance of Zhou Hui for many years. One look at Ran Deng's face, and he understood completely:

—"Heh, as expected of Untiring! Running off midway through hosting such a monumental occasion—what does that fellow take the guests from every sect for?! Fine, since that's the case, I'll come over."

—"Eh?!"-Ran Deng's eyes widened.

Beside her, Jian Nuo also let out an "Eh?!"

—"What are you 'Eh'-ing about?"-Jian Haoge said as if it were only natural.-"For such a massive occasion, without even an adult who can take charge, we can't just let you kids keep running around in circles! It's also a good opportunity to see what this 'restriction' you mentioned is all about."

—"But... aren't you unable to leave Sky City?"-Jian Nuo asked awkwardly.

He was practically half-running away from home. Although circumstances now forced him to return, facing City Lord Jian made him feel awkward down to his bones.

Jian Haoge didn't mind his attitude in the least:

—"I cannot step away for long, but leaving for half a shichen or an hour is no issue. This is certainly better than letting you take risks on a 'faulty teleportation array'."

Saying so, Jian Haoge stepped into the center of the large-scale teleportation array.

This array had been temporarily erected by Zhou Hui to experiment with cross-domain teleportation. With the success of the Southern Domain teleportation array as a precedent, there was theoretically no flaw, yet it had never been put to genuine practical use. If what Qingyang Qingyu and the others found hadn't been so critical, Ran Deng wouldn't have thought to utilize it so soon.

Jian Haoge, on the other hand, harbored zero doubt regarding the success rate of Untiring's teleportation arrays. Even though Untiring's own disciple insisted there was an issue, he stood right upon the array core without a shred of hesitation, waved his sleeve, and illuminated every spirit stone surrounding the formation.

—"Don't! I'll come over instead!"-Jian Nuo yelled.

—"Wait a moment..."-Ran Deng's eyelids twitched violently as she also tried to stop him.

Regrettably, their intervention came too late. As the spirit stones flared, the runic tracery of the teleportation array ignited alongside them. During long-distance transit across vast spatial dimensions, even a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator could not maintain an active communication link. Naturally, the call severed.

Of course, had the teleportation proceeded smoothly, Jian Haoge should have materialized within the array on this side the very next instant, rendering the call's termination trivial. The problem was, he did not appear right away.

The teleportation array on Ran Deng's side flared with light as well, yet amidst the surging tide of spiritual energy, not a single figure emerged from the formation's core. Only the dharma artifact calculating the flow of spiritual energy kept flashing digits in rapid succession.

—"Tsk! That old bastard!"-seeing this, Jian Nuo grew frantic, uncharacteristically letting slip a curse.-"I told him not to come!"

—"...He was just worried about you,"-Ran Deng consoled him at this moment.-"Besides, there might not be a problem. The array's operation is smooth at least. It might simply be that the distance is too vast, so it takes more time..."

—"...This is bad! City Lord Jian really seems to have gone missing!"

At this moment, Qingyang Qingyu suddenly spoke up in sheer panic.

—"Yeah, he went missing inside the array,"-Jian Nuo snapped grumpily, only then realizing who had spoken to him.-"No, I mean..."

—"? What I mean is, City Lord Jian seems to have gone missing yesterday,"-Qingyang Qingyu explained.

Qingyang hadn't noticed any issue with Jian Nuo's attitude. The moment Ran Deng detected the array anomaly and opened the call with City Lord Jian, Qingyang had contacted her own clan ancestor, leveraging Eternal Villa's extensive network to inquire about news from Sky City.

The result had shocked her profoundly:

—"My ancestor inquired with acquaintances over at Sky City. No one has seen City Lord Jian since yesterday afternoon! But just now when Senior Sister Ran called City Lord Jian, he was standing right at Sky City's teleportation array, wasn't he?"

—"Ah?"-Jian Nuo froze completely.

Stunned, he hurriedly retrieved his own sound-transmission artifact to contact Sky City. As the heir to Sky City, Jian Nuo's channels were naturally far broader and more accurate.

—"Ah, how could this be?! The servants and subordinates also said Old Ancestor disappeared two days ago! But we were clearly just talking..."-Jian Nuo muttered in a daze.

In truth, the subordinate he contacted spoke in an even graver tone—bordering on: "The City Lord may be missing! Young Master Jian, hurry back to guard the city and inherit the family estate!"

Yet they seemed visibly relieved.

After all, the time Jian Haoge had been absent wasn't long—merely two days. In an ordinary city, that wouldn't be an issue at all. It was just that Sky City could not function without its City Lord, and City Lord Jian had never vanished before. Worse, Jian Nuo had previously been inside the illusionary White Jade Capital and unreachable, unable to disclose his whereabouts to anyone, plunging the city into nervous panic. Now that Jian Nuo reached out proactively, their anxieties eased, leaving Jian Nuo to panic in their stead.

—"No, I must return immediately!"

Saying so, Jian Nuo moved toward the still-glowing teleportation array, but Qingyang Qingyu grabbed him firmly:

—"We already said the teleportation array is faulty! Even if you must go back, take a safer route!"-she said, turning to look at Ran Deng.-"Senior Sister Ran, is there a faster method? I heard the Southern Domain's teleportation array works? Can we travel from there..."

—"Don't panic yet,"-Ran Deng rubbed her temples, casting another glance at the teleportation array.

From a formation master's perspective, the array was functioning properly without structural fault, yet the teleportation telemetry was completely erratic, and the person had failed to appear.

—"He is Master's friend after all, he won't meet with mishap so easily,"-Ran Deng said.-"If you rashly take another route, getting delayed on the road might cause even more trouble. Why not give me one shichen? I'll diagnose the array's problem right now. If there really is a critical defect or I can't figure it out, it won't be too late for you to leave then."

—"All right, we trust Senior Sister Ran,"-Qingyang Qingyu nodded. Jian Nuo parted his lips to say something, but hearing Qingyang say that, he kept his mouth shut.

Shouldering their expectations, Ran Deng took a deep breath.

However, she possessed ample confidence to make this decision.

Not only was it her confidence as an array master, but because even though Master was absent, their Heavenly Spirit Sect's "technical personnel" were all gathered right here! Leaving only Lu Shulong and Mo Bei to depart earlier was truly the wisest decision she had made.

Thinking so, she turned toward the illusion realm where thunder rumbled and lightning flashed, and raised an amplification artifact:

—"You two [fucking](# "Literal CN: 他XXX的 — platform-censored profanity for 'fucking' / 'damn it'") stop fighting! Get your asses back here and work for [this lady](# "Literal CN: 老娘 (lǎoniáng) — assertive, fiery self-referential term used by women when infuriated")!"

Hearing her furious roar, Jian Nuo and Qingyang Qingyu froze stiff. Not only them, but Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye over there were even more terrified—in this lifetime, no, across several lifetimes, they had never heard Ran Deng speak in such a ferocious tone! Fortunately, their brawl had been nearing its end anyway, neither knowing how to back down gracefully. Taking this convenient out, they obediently listened to their martial sister and returned to help.

—"The teleportation array appears fine,"-Guan Ye reported to Ran Deng after inspecting it.

—"Yet the data is intensely chaotic,"-Ran Deng frowned, looking at Qu Qianxing.-"Can you calculate it?"

—"The vectors are extremely chaotic; calculating them will take a considerable amount of time,"-Qu Qianxing said, though his handsome face was black and blue with bruises. Not caring about the swelling, he merely pinched his fingers to divine.-"However, considering a human life is at stake, I'm afraid we can't afford to calculate slowly."

—"Then what do you mean...?"-Ran Deng asked.

Qu Qianxing looked at Jian Nuo:

—"Don't we have a blood relative right here? Can we use him to calculate his old ancestor's location?"

—"That same trick you used earlier to drag the celestial official?"-Guan Ye let out a cold sneer.

Qu Qianxing's bruised mouth corner twitched, his face instantly scowling in pain. But as long as it didn't involve his own background or Master, he was basically reliable (?). Not swayed by his junior siblings' sarcasm, he merely insisted:

—"This is the fastest method."

—"...Let's give it a try then!"-Jian Nuo offered proactively.

He trusted Qu Qianxing quite a bit.

Since the party involved had agreed, and Guan Ye had already beaten Qu Qianxing once and wasn't as enraged as before, he didn't pursue the dispute. He even coordinated with Qu Qianxing, preparing dharma artifacts to monitor the spiritual energy shifts.

Qu Qianxing grasped Jian Nuo's outstretched hand, channeling the "clearance" he had obtained from beating down Tian Yan not long ago, murmuring complex and obscure incantations under his breath.

Along with his chant, a thin scarlet thread crept out from Jian Nuo's wrist, crawling like a blood vessel toward the still-operating teleportation array, seemingly extending all the way to the other end of the formation.

—"Ah, found him!"-Jian Nuo cried out in delight.

Though it was merely a slender thread, he felt as if he were catching hold of Jian Haoge's sleeve as a child.

Yet before Jian Nuo could tighten his grip and pull his ancestor over, the ground beneath their feet shook violently—no, the teleportation array, even the entire space was trembling.

—"What's happening? These values keep changing!"-Guan Ye shouted.

—"It's not the data, it's space!"-Qu Qianxing said calmly.-"We're being teleported—"

—"Damn it, where is this—"-Ran Deng grabbed the edge of a table, barely keeping her footing. Catching a glimpse from the corner of her eye, she noticed the surrounding walls had vanished—no, not vanished, but their very location had shifted.

Yet before Ran Deng could confirm where she was, she heard a familiar voice say:

—"Then we have no choice but to silence them."

—"Huh?"-Ran Deng's eyes widened, looking up.

Several pairs of eyes met in utter, awkward bewilderment.

* * *

—"What is the situation?"-Peilan couldn't help asking as several cloud mirrors merged into one, congregating scattered individuals together and turning a secretive spatial fissure into something resembling a bustling vegetable market.

Zhou Hui's expression wasn't particularly pleasant either.

After all, his disciples were currently preparing to silence his friends—including the father of one of his own disciples.

However, Zhou Hui wasn't ignorant of the situation. On the contrary, after nearly "ascending to godhood," he had already perceived all the causes and effects through the river of time. The current development even included elements of his deliberate guidance.

Zhou Hui did not conceal it:

—"The Sky City Lord's 'disappearance' and Ran Zhen's death are, just like before, a 'script' designed by the Heavenly Court. And these two are not isolated events; they are strongly correlated."

—"What do you mean?"-Peilan asked.

—"Sky City's core was inherited from White Jade Capital; it is the hardware required to activate the restriction isolating Heavenly Court interference. And Ran Zhen of the Ran Clan, as a demoted array master who could wield forbidden arts by overdrawing his life force, was the sole array master capable of repairing that restriction—at least that was the case in the first several cycles. In other words, to prevent Langhuan's restriction from being activated anew, they had to either destroy Sky City's core or destroy Ran Zhen. And in the Heavenly Court's 'script', neither was spared,"-Zhou Hui explained coldly.

He gazed toward the "Celestial Emperor." At this point, the "Celestial Emperor" didn't even have the energy left to claim, "This was all Tian Yan's scheme." Besides, as Tian Yan's superior, he was there to shoulder the blame at times like this. Thus, he remained silent, quietly listening to Zhou Hui enumerate the Heavenly Court's crimes.

—"Therefore, to prevent the restriction from taking effect—or rather, to unravel the restriction guarding this world—Ran Zhen was deceived into the illusion realm across several cycles and perished there. Nor was it a swift death, but a deliberate torment in an extremely harsh environment, a slow death while attempting to unravel the restriction to escape..."-Zhou Hui continued.

—"Unraveling the restriction... Could it be?"-Peilan murmured.

—"Precisely. The restriction Ran Zhen was trying to unravel was actually the very restriction guarding this world. His array inevitably destabilized the core of the restriction—which was the City Lord of Sky City, Jian Haoge."

—"Thus, the moment Ran Zhen succeeded was usually the moment of his death, as well as the moment Jian Haoge, due to the shattering of the restriction, was forcibly transposed into the illusion realm to replace him as the new captive, eventually perishing there as well."

—"After destroying the restriction, just like the sixth cycle, the entire world became a playground for the [Child of Destiny](# "Literal CN: 天命之子 (tiānmìng zhī zǐ) — fated protagonist favored by the heavens"),"-Zhou Hui said in a chilling voice.

—"Is that so?"-Peilan hesitated, looking in another direction.-"City Lord Jian?"

Yes, City Lord Jian.

Jian Haoge.

Somehow, he had not been teleported to his intended location, nor had he vanished completely as in past cycles, but had instead manifested inside this space.

However, his expression was terrible—not only was his complexion sickly pale green, but his temples were throbbing violently.

Seeing Jian Haoge looking as if blood were rushing straight to his heart, on the verge of being enraged to death at any moment, Peilan felt somewhat concerned:

—"City Lord Jian? R-Right, you don't know what specifically transpired yet, do you? Let me explain..."

—"What is there to explain?!"-Jian Haoge rubbed his temples and scoffed.-"In any case, it roughly boils down to me being in for bad luck, but before anything actually happened, Untiring saved me, right?"

—"You recovered your memories?!"-Peilan asked in astonishment.

—"What recovered memories?"-Jian Haoge replied.

—"Then what you just said..."-Peilan began.

—"I was merely guessing,"-Jian Haoge's expression turned even fouler.-"Since my teleportation didn't reach the designated location, and instead I encountered Untiring who isn't even at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, eight or nine out of ten it's some terrible sudden disaster, isn't it? Based on past experience, wherever Untiring is, there is trouble—the only difference is whether it's big trouble or small trouble. Since you people seem so suddenly certain something would happen to me, eight or nine out of ten it's some trap or conspiracy targeting this [unlucky bastard](# "Literal CN: 倒霉鬼 (dǎoméi guǐ) — jinx / wretchedly unlucky soul"), and then I was saved by Untiring again?"

[...City Lord Jian, what on earth did you go through in the past? You seem remarkably experienced.]-Peilan thought sympathetically.

Suddenly having someone around who was even more clueless than himself, Peilan felt strangely comforted.

And Jian Haoge seemed thoroughly accustomed to his status of "never being clear on the situation." Glancing at Zhou Hui with an ugly look, he asked:

—"So, did I guess correctly?"

—"No,"-Zhou Hui sighed, answering dryly.-"I tried several times, but never succeeded in saving you."

To be precise, prior to this lifetime, he hadn't even discovered that Jian Haoge had met with disaster, so saving him was out of the question.

—"...*Hiss*, is the matter that severe? Even you couldn't solve it?"-Jian Haoge's eyes widened.-"Could the three thousand worlds be on the verge of destruction?"

To reiterate: City Lord Jian was truly experienced.

—"No, this time I will not permit it,"-Zhou Hui said.

He pondered how to explain so City Lord Jian could grasp the situation fastest—forcibly cramming memories seemed like an effective method, but putting aside whether Jian Haoge's head would explode, with Jian Haoge's temperament, he would likely explode with rage first. Zhou Hui considered with difficulty, attempting to persuade him:

—"Explaining specifically is somewhat troublesome, so..."

—"So don't explain! I don't want to know,"-Jian Haoge cut him off with blinding speed, emphasizing:-"As long as the world won't be destroyed tomorrow—or even if it is destroyed, whatever—in any case, I don't want to know a single thing!"

Zhou Hui choked into silence.

Seeing Zhou Hui looking thoroughly choked, Jian Haoge's mood improved considerably. He inquired:

—"Since the world won't be destroyed 'this time', what are you waiting for here?"

—"Indeed, what are we waiting for?"-Peilan asked, looking at the chaotic scene in the cloud mirror.-"If you stall any longer, your disciples might end up fighting again."

—"I am waiting for them to make a decision! Whether to fight, or to continue destroying the world,"-Zhou Hui smiled.

His smile was neither the gentle warmth he usually showed his disciples nor the icy sneer reserved for unforgivable enemies, but a sinister, eerie grin rarely ever seen on Zhou Hui's face.

Seeing this smile, both Jian Haoge and Peilan shuddered.

—"After all, in this reincarnation cycle, thwarting the Heavenly Court's conspiracy and saving the world are all the achievements of these children. Naturally, I want them to carry it out to the very end,"-Zhou Hui smiled with chilling eeriness, his expression looking remarkably gentle as he gazed into the cloud mirror.-"I only hope that in the end, they can hand me a satisfactory 'answer'."

[An answer? Understood. This is an 'exam', right? The kind of 'exam' where if Master isn't satisfied, these little brats will suffer catastrophic misfortune, right?]

Jian Haoge snorted coldly. Although he knew nothing, he still looked at the cloud mirror with provisional sympathy:

—"Then I wish these little brats good luck!"

—"Don't bother wishing them luck,"-Peilan knew the causes and effects, and thus harbored zero sympathy.-"Every single one of them has already flunked. It's merely a matter of deciding how they'll be cleaned up in the end."

—"Oh!"-Jian Haoge said. His gaze toward the cloud mirror grew even more sympathetic.

* * *

—"Achoo!"

While Zhou Hui calmly observed his foolish disciples, Yuwen Xuan—of course, the version belonging to this reincarnation cycle—sneezed loudly, shaking the little curly fleece across his body.

—"Eldest Senior Brother, don't get your drool on my head,"-Lu Shulong protested in a small voice.

—"Shut up,"-Yuwen Xuan tapped his Fifth Junior Brother's head with a hoof.-"I suddenly have a very bad premonition. I keep feeling that continuing like this will bring disaster."

—"It's already a disaster,"-Lu Shulong said, looking toward Ran Deng and the others still standing in the array center.-"S-Senior Sister, I think there might be a bit of a misunderstanding here."

Ran Deng ignored him.

Or rather, Ran Deng didn't have the time to pay attention to him.

Because having been teleported here so abruptly, Ran Deng hadn't even reacted to who it was her martial brothers were planning to "silence," and before she could process the scene, she was embraced firmly!

—"Deng'er!"-Ran Zhen cried out emotionally.

—"Mmh? Let go of me, who are you... Ah, Dad?!"-it took Ran Deng a full half-minute to recognize that [bloody gourd](# "Literal CN: 血葫芦 (xuè húlu) — drenched head-to-toe in blood like a gourd dipped in gore") was her own father. Oblivious to her soiled robes, she hurriedly hugged him back.-"What on earth happened? How did you end up like this?"

Saying so, she looked up at the others, finding this entire group was in no better shape.

The Ran Clan array masters went without saying: they were all in a similar state to Ran Deng's father Ran Zhen, almost all "bloody gourds," several seemingly unable to maintain their sanity—actually Ran Zhen wasn't much better, after emotionally hugging his daughter, he practically passed out. And Jiuzhai and Tao Zhu, who had gone out with Master, looked terribly disheveled. Especially Patriarch Jiuzhai, who originally looked like a dried-up orange, now looked like a dried orange thrown into water and grown moldy, appearing nearly completely rotted.

—"What happened to you all?"-Ran Deng inquired.

—"Ahem, little girl, you are Ran Zhen's daughter?"-Sikong Yao, who was in the best condition, asked knowingly.-"Entrusted by your Master, we located your father deep within the illusion realm and went through immense hardships to bring him out. Unfortunately, midway through, something went wrong with the teleportation array, and we were sent to this place."

Sikong Yao briefly summarized their journey, especially emphasizing his heroic rescue of Ran Deng's father.

Yet this was also the truth.

Although Zhou Hui had delivered Sikong Yao and the others near Ran Zhen back then, promising that as long as they brought him out he could teleport them directly back, how could an illusion realm capable of trapping the supreme array master be an ordinary place? Once Sikong Yao and the others entered, extricating themselves proved extraordinarily difficult. Worse, at that time Zhou Hui was in an "ascending to godhood" state and could not pay attention to their situation, and several of them nearly perished in the predicament.

Fortunately, Sikong Yao was there.

As a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse—especially one who could escape with his life from Zhou Hui—if even he couldn't escape, then no one in the three thousand worlds could. Fortunately, though the conditions were harsh, it hadn't reached that level. In the end, Sikong Yao, who had been [conscripted as a laborer](# "Literal CN: 抓壮丁 (zhuā zhuàngdīng) — forcibly drafted / press-ganged into service"), became the primary force of the rescue. By his own power, he activated the arrays Ran Zhen had drawn repeatedly across several cycles of imprisonment, finally dragging everyone out. But whether due to an issue with the array or external interference, they were teleported to this inexplicable place.

Sikong Yao did not feel they had succeeded.

He originally disliked saving people, and disliked being dragged into this mess even more. In his view, from the beginning he shouldn't have agreed to Untiring's request to take two burdens to save people. Although he had no choice, and if he were to reflect, perhaps he shouldn't have joined Carefree Palace and offended Untiring in the first place.

[Doing things I'm not accustomed to really brings bad luck.]-Sikong Yao thought, glancing at the two (?) Taoties present.

His instincts were still warning him: he felt even more uneasy than inside the illusion realm that trapped Ran Zhen.

However, even Sikong Yao possessed the dignity of a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse. He had no interest in explaining too much to juniors. Unfortunately, he wasn't a good person either, especially hailing from Carefree Palace. Since the omen of danger stemmed from Untiring's disciples, naturally it should be resolved internally among them—if things grew chaotic, it would be easier to slip away, wouldn't it?

Musing inwardly, Sikong Yao said to Ran Deng:

—"I don't know where this place is either, but your martial brothers and sisters are all here—only your Master is absent. Hearing that I know your Master, they grew anxious instead! Even after I said this is your father, they still said they wanted to silence us."

—"Oh,"-Ran Deng responded expressionlessly.

She gave no reaction to Sikong Yao's "tattling," merely turning her head to look at the other "people."

Mainly referring to the other Taotie and Qu Qianxing radiating demonic qi.

Of course, two such conspicuous figures were hard to overlook. It was just that Ran Deng had been shocked by her father's wretched state and intentionally overlooked them. The others caught inside the teleportation array—such as Qu Qianxing, Guan Ye, Jian Nuo, and Qingyang Qingyu—especially this human Qu Qianxing—looked at "himself" right away.

Fortunately, Qu Qianxing had long heard that a Demon Sovereign looked identical to him, and had even borrowed the other's name to lead demons in battle against celestial soldiers. Seeing the same face wasn't terribly surprising, just somewhat disgusting.

Qu Qianxing felt that having one face like this was already disgusting enough.

Guan Ye thought so as well:

—"Tsk, another Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother? Having one of each was already more than enough."

—"I think so too,"-responding to Guan Ye's disgust was Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing. Sizing up everyone present, he laughed magnanimously:-"Truly don't know what kind of fated coincidence this is, that at this critical juncture, our sect is actually reunited—it seems apart from Little Junior Brother, everyone is here?"

Actually, Little Junior Brother is probably here too.

Ran Deng rolled her eyes, looking toward the void.

Although Mo Xi was still guarding the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree and hadn't arrived in person, the "web" hadn't broken. Even if transferred, as long as power traces remained, he should be able to find his way over... right?

Of course, Ran Deng wouldn't say it aloud.

If something unexpected happened, having Mo Xi as a backup was necessary, and easier to account to Master later. Speaking of Master...

—"A pity 'our Master' isn't here,"-Taotie chimed in at this moment, curling into a malicious grin:-"I truly want to meet the 'True Master' you all yearn for day and night!"

The other disciples might not think so.

Qu Qianxing—the Qu Qianxing of this reincarnation cycle—stared warily at Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing, while questioning Sikong Yao and the others:

—"Hey, whoever you are, wasn't our Master supposedly out with you? Where is he?"

—"? You're asking me?"-Sikong Yao froze, pointing to himself.

He belatedly realized that along with this question, everyone's eyes suddenly fell upon him!

Sikong Yao broke into a cold sweat.

Others aside, the gaze of those two Taoties was more than he could bear—just being looked at felt like being licked with a tongue.

Sikong Yao retreated behind Tao Zhu, attempting to use the latter's rotund body to shield himself:

—"Your Master has his own business to attend to..."

—"Indeed, your Master is busy,"-Tao Zhu finally came to his senses.

His gaze toward Taotie was complicated.

Because earlier in the illusion realm, when attacked by demons, getting injured was minor, but Tao Zhu's memories of several cycles had been replayed once. Although he had heard a lot from Jiuzhai, having them dumped back all at once had a huge psychological impact. And unlike others, Tao Zhu harbored zero regrets about the demon invasion cycle; what caused him massive psychological trauma was the cycle where he was forced to adopt Yuwen Xuan from Zhou Hui!

Now, there were actually two of these jinxes!

Tao Zhu felt a stomach ache. Managing Yuwen Xuan for an entire cycle had taught him zero useful experience; when helpless, he could only use Master to scare it:

—"Your Master found something truly extraordinary in the previous illusion realm. Once he returns, his power will likely surge by another massive leap, and he might even deduce what 'fine deeds' you're planning! You had best behave yourselves, or else you'll have [good fruit to eat](# "Literal CN: 没你好果子吃 (méi nǐ hǎoguǒzi chī) — a classic Chinese idiom meaning 'you'll face severe consequences / suffer for this'") when you go back!"

—"Pfft,"-the giant Taotie scoffed dismissively.

The little black lamb rolled its eyes, looking full of wicked tricks:

—"That means Master temporarily can't spare attention for this place, eh?"

—"I didn't mean that,"-Tao Zhu said weakly.

—"Heh!"-Yuwen Xuan ignored him, turning toward Taotie and Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing.-"Hear that? Master is busy! Weren't you going to destroy the world and eat the timeline? If you're going to do it, hurry up! And silencing them too!!!"

—"Wait, what did you say they're going to do?"-this cycle's Qu Qianxing asked in disbelief, reaching out to grasp the little lamb's soft horns.

—"Don't ask him, he doesn't know as clearly as 'you' do—because 'I' should have 'remembered' by now, right?"-this time it was Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing who spoke. Looking at the self of this reincarnation cycle, his eyes were full of scrutiny.-"'I' plan to devour past and future, leaving only 'the uncorrupted present where only the True Master exists.' That way, Master and the Heavenly Spirit Sect will never again suffer unfair treatment in time or space. 'You' are also 'me'—you should be able to understand 'my' actions, right!"

—"..."-Qu Qianxing was expressionless.

Yes, he "remembered."

The moment he saw the other self, he "remembered" everything.

Yet saying he "remembered" wasn't quite accurate. To be precise, he had severed himself from his demonized self.

Unlike Mo Xi who was manipulated and didn't truly know, Qu Qianxing knew from beginning to end... in fact, a third of it had been planned by him, and the remainder belonged to Taotie.

In that cycle, upon discovering that even Master was not the Master in memory, Qu Qianxing had fallen into demonhood on the spot.

In truth, Qu Qianxing had always possessed the seeds of demonhood: betrayed by humans, sacrificed to demons, gaining power by destroying himself. If not for his innate moral perfectionism that forbade him from degenerating, falling into demonhood would have been a matter of minutes. He held out until taking Master and entering the Dao, seemingly walking the righteous path, only to be reincarnated again and again, watching the sect destroyed again and again—the last few times even related to him. Qu Qianxing finally failed to resist the demon seed in his heart and completely fell into demonhood.

Qu Qianxing despised such a self, and despised a world without Master. But unlike Lu Shulong, Qu Qianxing didn't like evading problems. Although he firmly believed the True Master would restart reincarnation and find them back, after repeating so many times, there was no reason to leave all the hardship to Master alone. Better to solve the problem once and for all while Master was busy.

Thus, he negotiated with Taotie: at the cost of sacrificing himself, the sect, and even the entire world, thoroughly severing time and space to feed to Taotie, to leave behind a clean, pure world for Master.

Of course, through this series of actions, Qu Qianxing ended up demonized beyond recognition. The current Demon Sovereign form was merely a superficial appearance; if he showed his true form, it would be an unwatchable state even to himself.

And because he knew he would face such an end, Qu Qianxing from the very beginning severed the relatively clean portion of his soul to die as "himself."

This was Qu Qianxing's selfish motive.

He hoped that when Master restarted reincarnation, there would be a "pure and clean" second disciple who knew nothing, meeting Master's hopes, serving at Master's side.

Qu Qianxing's plan was ninety-nine percent successful.

Now only the final step remained.

However, [kings do not meet kings](# "Literal CN: 王不见王 (wáng bù jiàn wáng) — an ancient rule from Chinese chess (Xiangqi) where two kings/generals cannot face each other directly along the same file without intermediate pieces; here referring to two identical sovereign identities colliding"). Even with the severance, Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing and the Qu Qianxing of this cycle shared one soul. It was not like the avatar or incarnation tricks of cultivators with primary and secondary distinctions. As soon as he and "himself" met, memories, power, and even cultivation would be shared!

Therefore, the moment he saw the demonized "himself," Qu Qianxing "remembered" everything, and along with it, a surge of demonic qi rushed into his dantian, nearly causing him to instantly demonize. Fortunately, the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array was still operating, and Master's power was still blessing him. Qu Qianxing circulated his power several times and barely suppressed the demonization.

Yet in Qu Qianxing's current state, as long as he entered closed-door seclusion for a few months, even away from the mountain array, he could skip three levels to challenge the cultivation limit. If he wanted, he could create a miracle of the fastest ascension.

Although the ascension destination, the Heavenly Court, was about to be fed to Taotie by "himself."

Qu Qianxing naturally didn't care about the Heavenly Court's fate, especially having just killed a celestial official. If anything, he wished the Heavenly Court would perish immediately.

And having sacrificed so much, even sacrificing the entire sect, on the verge of success, he truly shouldn't give up at the final step... right?

If it were right after beating Tian Yan, he would definitely have decided so. But having just been beaten by Fourth Junior Brother, with the corner of his mouth still burning with pain, Qu Qianxing was somewhat hesitant.

Looking at Guan Ye brimming with resentment, remembering what fine things he did in past cycles, looking from an objective perspective, Qu Qianxing felt he truly hadn't done many human things. Being beaten by his junior brother was actually letting him off easy. If Master found out... From this perspective, he really wanted Taotie to swallow everything and thoroughly silence everyone, pretending he never "remembered" anything.

Thinking of this, Qu Qianxing uncharacteristically didn't retort immediately, but somewhat guiltily shifted his gaze, subconsciously glancing at the others.

Then Qu Qianxing's heart skipped a beat.

He realized that along with the Demon Sovereign's question, his martial brothers and sisters were looking at "himself," seemingly waiting for him to make a choice.

[Ah, it really is my turn to answer.]-Qu Qianxing thought.

Because he was the successor to the Heavenly Spirit Sect—though Qu Qianxing doubted whether he still possessed that qualification. His siblings probably harbored the same doubt, so their gazes weren't simply waiting for Second Senior Brother's order as usual, but clearly contained scrutiny, even Mo Bei.

Qu Qianxing uncharacteristically didn't dare face this scrutiny.

His gaze passed Guan Ye, Lu Shulong who were watching quietly, and the little black lamb (Eldest Senior Brother) watching the drama, finally landing on Ran Deng.

This junior sister whom he had personally led to her death.

She was indeed the only one whose gaze lacked scrutiny, merely waiting, even with her bloody father slung over her shoulder, still silently waiting for Second Senior Brother's answer.

—"No,"-Qu Qianxing heard himself speak.-"Perhaps for 'me' it was the best choice, but for the others, it is by no means a good choice."

—"Others?"-the Demon Sovereign raised his brows with a faint, mocking smile.-"Master?"

—"Not just Master, but 'everyone',"-Qu Qianxing answered.-"Don't play dumb. 'You' should have seen 'my' memories as well. In this cycle, 'everyone' did a great deal. There might not necessarily be such a tragic end."

—"After 'I' slew a celestial official?"-the Demon Sovereign deepened his sarcastic smile.

—"Jie-jie, as if the Heavenly Court would have spared us if I hadn't killed that celestial official,"-Guan Ye chuckled eerily.

As a victim of that cycle, Guan Ye could never support the Demon Sovereign's approach.

He merely wanted to see "this cycle's Qu Qianxing's" stance. This was related to whether he counted as "Second Senior Brother" or "Demon Sovereign." Guan Ye didn't mind beating "both" together, or even packing them up and destroying them together.

And although Lu Shulong wasn't so radical, he held the same view:

—"Exactly! To fight enemies, we should do it together! Besides, it's not impossible—didn't Second Senior Brother manage to repel the celestial host with the help of Eldest Senior Sister and Fourth Senior Brother? If we did it once, we can do it a second time."

—"Pfft,"-the Demon Sovereign sneered coldly.-"Relying on you?"

Having reached this stage, he clearly wasn't someone who could be convinced by a couple of sentences from junior brothers.

Seeing him like this, let alone Guan Ye, even Lu Shulong wanted to whip his tail to thrash him.

Ran Deng stopped Lu Shulong, asking plainly:

—"Or is it, Second Senior Brother, that you wish for me to die once more?"

—"Or use us again?"-Mo Bei said coldly, the soft sword in her hand emitting sharp whistling humming vibrations.

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing")

Facing his junior sisters' interrogation, Qu Qianxing lowered his cicada-wing eyelashes. When he manipulated these two junior sisters, he obviously hadn't envisioned the scene of the victims questioning him to his face. Thus, even with demonic qi eroding his humanity, facing them, he couldn't help averting his gaze.

Unfortunately, Qu Qianxing was Qu Qianxing after all.

He could do wrong, but would never give up halfway.

The Demon Sovereign would not change his stance because of his junior sisters, merely stating coldly:

—"I do not wish for a repeat either. Therefore, I advise you to return to Master's side immediately—truthfully, if Master rewinds time once more because of your absence, I will find it a headache as well."

—"Qu Qianxing, you've really got guts!"-Ran Deng laughed out of pure fury.

—"Let's beat him up!"-Lu Shulong offered eagerly. Having missed an opportunity earlier, now that even Senior Sister was furious, they could gang up on him together, which he looked forward to immensely.

—"The junior brothers and sisters seem very angry, oh,"-seeing that a fight was about to break out, Taotie fanned the flames, harboring unnecessary anticipation:-"Why not let me eat them all together? Wouldn't that be just perfect?"

—"Not good. Mixed with demonic qi and celestial qi, you will get diarrhea,"-Qu Qianxing... this cycle's Qu Qianxing said coldly.

He didn't want to be beaten again!

Although the one beaten would be his Demon Sovereign self, since he and "he" had met, sharing the same soul, sensory perceptions were also shared! Beating either "him" was beating Qu Qianxing himself, and it would hurt!

Even if it didn't hurt, his pride wouldn't allow it.

Fortunately, as "Qu Qianxing," he knew how to stop "himself":

—"By the same logic, involving both the demon race and the Heavenly Court, if you let Taotie swallow it all down in the end, are you truly certain it will not affect Master? Calculating everything through one person's sole strength—I didn't know 'I' was so conceited!"

Qu Qianxing paused here, but without waiting for the other to reply, continued:

—"In this reincarnation cycle, Master taught a substantial amount of useful knowledge. Look over it first before speaking!"

—"Is *that* the point?!"-Ran Deng roasted upon hearing this.-"As if if Master hadn't taught that pile of stuff in this cycle, you would still support the original plan... Qu Qianxing! Look into my eyes! Don't look away!"

Ran Deng was thoroughly incensed, yet Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing visibly revealed a tempted expression.

He had received the shared memories of this cycle, but hadn't looked closely yet—for the same reason this cycle's Qu Qianxing suppressed the demonic qi: if consciousness and power synchronized too much, they might merge the next moment. Currently remaining "two people" was entirely relying on tiny differences to buy time.

Taotie obviously didn't want this (Demon Sovereign) Qu Qianxing, who had promised to let himself be eaten, to vanish:

—"Don't listen to his nonsense, he's coaxing you into vanishing! But even if 'you' vanish, 'Qu Qianxing' is someone I am definitely going to eat!"-it glared at both Qu Qianxings as it said this.

Saying so, it even opened its colossal maw, attempting to swallow both at once!

—"Pah, pah! Even I don't have the qualification to eat my family's junior brother, what the [fuck](# "Literal CN: 吃个P (chī gè P) — polite internet slang for 吃个屁 (eat a fart / eat shit)") do you think you're eating?!"-the little black lamb bristled in an instant.

It extended a tiny hoof and swung it forcefully—actually sending the Taotie, which was hundreds of times larger than itself, flying across the void with a single blow!

The sheer disparity in strength hadn't even been realized by its clone. But in Yuwen Xuan's eyes, it was only natural: having swallowed the main body and consumed the celestial feast, if he still couldn't thrash a clone that had only lived one cycle, never met the True Master, and never been taught by Master, he would have lived all those years in vain and might as well eat himself.

Earlier he hadn't acted simply because he didn't care to.

Everyone revealed stunned expressions.

Yet remembering that this was their Eldest Senior Brother, it didn't seem strange anymore.

Eldest Senior Brother—whatever he did was entirely within reason.

—"Didn't expect him to be just as ruthless when beating himself,"-someone muttered under their breath.

In the next heartbeat, the little black lamb transformed into a titanic Taotie, while that other Taotie turned into a gray sheep, bloody and pinned firmly beneath the hoof of the Taotie transformed from the black lamb, unable to utter a sound—completing a total reversal in size and status!

Then Yuwen Xuan rolled his massive eyes, looking toward his junior brothers and sisters.

The junior siblings obediently withdrew their gazes, pretending they hadn't seen a thing, turning back to look at Demon Sovereign Qu Qianxing, waiting for this Qu Qianxing's final choice!
