# Chapter 236: The Ancestor Has Already Been Enraged Back to Life

—"Qianxing-er? How could it... be him...?!"-Villa Master Qingyang gasped in utter astonishment.

Since Jing Chenjun and the defenders of the other cities could see the demonic vanguard and Qu Qianxing, the other cultivators inside this "illusion" could naturally see them as well—especially Villa Master Qingyang and Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum, who stood at the very epicenter of the illusion realm.

According to the "Script of the Great Immortal-Demon War," the two of them were currently engaged in an exhibition match circling the Altar of Heavenly Prayer in White Jade Capital.

An exhibition match, naturally, was heavy on the "exhibition."

Neither of the two Mahayana powerhouses could possibly go all out in an event like this. Thus, after trading blows for half the day, the two Mahayana titans had not only meticulously demarcated their combat zone to avoid having their spiritual ripples collateral-damage the defending cultivators along the ramparts, but had also kept their power strictly contained—to the point where they hadn't chipped off so much as a single brick from the city battlements.

During the fight, they even had ample leisure to glance about and rubberneck.

Consequently, Villa Master Qingyang had noticed the spatial anomaly, the endless demonic legions surging through the interdimensional fissure, and Qu Qianxing leading at the vanguard of that abyssal horde the very instant they emerged.

Villa Master Qingyang had always harbored immense goodwill toward Qu Qianxing—to the point of scheming to marry off his favorite great-great-great-granddaughter to him. Thus, catching sight of Qu Qianxing amidst the demonic legions was no different from watching your favorite star straight-A honor student suddenly turning up as [a rainbow-haired shamate delinquent revving a ghost-fire moped](# "Literal CN: 染着七色头发的杀马特造型的鬼火少年 — 'shamate' was an exaggerated 2000s Chinese youth subculture known for flamboyant neon spiky anime hair, while 'ghost-fire youth' refers to reckless teenage delinquents racing around on flashy, glow-modded scooters").

In that single instant, Villa Master Qingyang's entire worldview nearly collapsed.

In a clash between peak powerhouses, such a lapse in concentration was utterly forbidden. Thus, in the brief second he froze in bewilderment, the runes encircling him abruptly detonated, bursting into a cascade of sparks that looked immensely grandiose but possessed negligible lethal force.

Thanks to it being merely an "exhibition," Villa Master Qingyang only suffered a minor indignity without sustaining any actual injury.

Still, the attack jolted him back to his senses.

From across the arena, his opponent, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum, scolded sharply:

—"Daoist Qingyang, you dare let your attention wander while fighting me?! Aren't you underestimating me far too much?!"

[—"Ah..."](# "Speaker: Villa Master Qingyang")

—"Besides, what do you have to get so distracted over? Isn't it perfectly normal for the Heavenly Spirit Sect's direct disciples to play the villains in this war?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum added with pointed insinuation.

Jarred by her reminder, Villa Master Qingyang—as well as the countless spectators watching this duel—suddenly remembered:

*Oh, right! This is the trial system of the Immortal Arts Assembly!*

Back in the early phases of the trial system during single combat, the direct disciples had all taken turns roleplaying the opponents. For instance, Fourth Disciple Guan Ye had played a fallen ghost cultivator, Fifth Disciple Lu Shulong had played a skeletal dragon, and Seventh Disciple Mo Bei had played a bone puppet. Although no one had spotted Second Disciple Qu Qianxing's role until now, seeing as the trial had reached the "Grand Climax," having the next-in-line successor of the Heavenly Spirit Sect step forward to play the supreme commander of the demonic army was actually perfectly reasonable...?

Instantly, the vast majority of the onlookers accepted this "lore setting."

Villa Master Qingyang, however, still looked thoroughly unsettled:

—"Still, there was no need to make Qianxing-er do it..."

—"To go so far as deploying his own direct disciple, perhaps the Untiring Immortal Venerable wishes to warn us of something?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said. This time she did not speak aloud, projecting her voice through a strand of divine sense instead.

Villa Master Qingyang froze for a moment upon hearing her words before the underlying meaning struck him with full force.

—"You recovered yours as well?"-he inquired via divine sense, deliberately keeping his phrasing cryptic and vague.

Yet Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum understood him in a heartbeat:

—"Yes. The memories of the previous cycle."

Indeed. Both of them had recovered their memories.

This was only natural.

When confronted with the boundless demonic legions, even a mere mortal like Jing Chenjun had caught glimpses of past karmic entanglements in his dreams and carried that unyielding grievance back into waking reality—let alone two Mahayana powerhouses.

They possessed vastly deeper cultivation bases and infinitely stronger divine souls. Reclaiming suppressed memories was trivial by comparison. Moreover, their bitterness and unresolved grievances from that cycle were second to none; all they had ever lacked to awaken their memories was an external "catalyst."

Thus, both had regained their memories, though at different junctures.

Villa Master Qingyang had recovered his earlier—largely because he had died relatively early in the sixth reincarnation cycle. In that life, Eternal Villa had ultimately perished alongside the invading demons down to the last soul, and Villa Master Qingyang had fallen in that very battle. Consequently, the moment he witnessed the siege of his own ancestral manor in the trial, his memories had already begun to resurface in fragmented echoes.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum, on the other hand, had recovered hers considerably later.

Although she operated primarily in the Central Domain, her ancestral foundation was not situated directly atop a Demon Eye like Eternal Villa's. In fact, in that previous cycle—unburdened by any grand event like the Immortal Arts Assembly—she, as a talisman and rune master unsuited for direct frontline combat, had departed the Central Domain when the demons first invaded. She had journeyed north to deliver warnings of the demonic incursions and petition the major sects for reinforcements. Yet by the time she had barely crossed into the northern frontier, the entire Central Domain had already fallen completely due to the annihilation of Eternal Villa.

From that point onward, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had never faced the main demonic army head-on. Instead, she had followed her original itinerary to Sky City in the Northern Domain, serving as a logistical support pillar who continuously mass-produced endless talismans and defensive runes for the frontline cultivators... until the very end.

...Until the moment Sky City was utterly annihilated.

Recalling this, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum turned her gaze toward the Altar of Heavenly Prayer and the unnatural spatial fissure suspended above it.

According to the "Script," she was currently roleplaying the ancient cultivator from the God-Demon War ten thousand years ago who had summoned Heavenly Soldiers and Celestial Generals to reinforce the mortal realm. By strange coincidence, in her past life, she had likewise participated in Sky City's final rite of heavenly prayer.

No, it likely wasn't a coincidence at all, but rather a deliberate "reminder." Without this scene before her, her memories would never have snapped back into place.

The only thing that still puzzled Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum was that in her "previous life"—what she believed to be her previous life, as she could never have imagined that Untiring had rewound time seven or eight times purely for the sake of his disciples, leaving her convinced there had only ever been that single cycle of demonic invasion—the Heavenly Spirit Sect had seemingly been obliterated right at the onset of the demonic invasion, including the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself...

Come to think of it, for someone as peerlessly supreme as the Untiring Immortal Venerable to die so easily in that lifetime was inherently bizarre.

*Could the rewinding of time have been his doing?*

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum pondered, accidentally stumbling upon the absolute truth.

For now, however, none of that mattered. If everything within this "illusion realm" was intended as a warning, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum felt there were truths that Villa Master Qingyang—who had died far too early in that life—and the others desperately needed to know.

—"Yes, I remembered. I remembered everything,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said.-"And because of that, I know that rather than the demons, the true enemy we must confront may very well be... 'Up Above.'"

—"What?!"-Villa Master Qingyang asked in bewilderment.

Right at that moment, a talisman detonated before Villa Master Qingyang's eyes, and a sequence of vivid memory fragments flashed through his consciousness.

He saw an identical sky stained pitch-black and crimson by demonic corruption; an identical magnificent city floating high in the firmament; an identical sprawling Altar of Heavenly Prayer. Yet this was not White Jade Capital—it was Sky City. Although Villa Master Qingyang rarely departed Eternal Villa, in his youth as an itinerant Mahayana cultivator, he had visited Sky City numerous times and recognized it instantly. Only, he had never seen Sky City in such a battered, desperate state of ruin.

...Ah. These were Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's memories.

So after the demonic invasion of the "previous life," even Sky City... even the entire Northern Domain had been reduced to this wretched state?!

Villa Master Qingyang quickly processed the reality.

A profound sorrow welled up within his chest.

Having only recently recalled the total annihilation of Eternal Villa, to now witness the tragic end of Sky City and the entire Northern Domain—it was impossible not to feel heartbroken.

—"It doesn't matter! The Demon Head [Mo Xi] has already been defeated by my hand. Now, as long as I successfully ascend and open the gateway to the Immortal Realm, Heavenly Soldiers and Celestial Generals will naturally descend to deliver this world from peril!"

Right then, someone in the memory declared with boundless confidence.

An immediate wave of irritation washed over Villa Master Qingyang.

He wasn't displeased because the speaker proposed calling upon celestial reinforcements to save the world—at that desperate stage, there was hardly any other choice left. What grated on his nerves was the speaker's impossibly frivolous and flippant tone.

As if routing the demonic horde and saving the cosmos were some trivial, effortless chore.

[And just who do you think you are?]

[You can ascend just because you say so?]

[Even a supreme titan like Untiring has never succeeded!]

While muttering these blistering critiques in his heart, Villa Master Qingyang followed the sound to look at the speaker. The moment he discovered the person talking was merely a greenhorn brat with only Soul Formation cultivation, his distaste escalated tenfold.

Yet at that very moment, a surge of profound admiration and unshakeable trust crept up Villa Master Qingyang's spine. The sudden emotional influx sent a cold shudder down his back before he realized it was the lingering residue of Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's own feelings in the memory.

And it wasn't just Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum. The other cultivators present on that altar—having survived the demonic onslaught this far, their cultivation levels were extraordinarily high, mostly Void Refinement powerhouses and above, with Mahayana titans like Sacred Mausoleum standing in abundance—were all gazing at this Soul Formation brat with eyes full of doting trust and starry-eyed reverence, genuinely convinced that he could ascend on command!

Many of those cultivators were old acquaintances of Villa Master Qingyang. What bewildered him most was that these people had never struck him as foolish or lacking basic judgment.

Yet among the crowd, there was one particular person who caught Villa Master Qingyang's eye.

Aside from that boastful Soul Formation brat, she was the "only other" Soul Formation cultivator present.

A young maiden... no, a young married woman.

She appeared extraordinarily young, bearing the delicate visage of a sixteen- or seventeen-year-old girl, yet she was dressed in the garments of a wedded matron. In her arms, she cradled an infant wrapped in swaddling clothes, gazing up at that Soul Formation cultivator with a face drowned in helpless adoration.

Evidently, this young woman was that Soul Formation brat's... Dao companion?

[I've never seen anyone dragging along their whole family when ascending!]

Yet that wasn't the reason Villa Master Qingyang was paying attention to her. Rather, the instant he laid eyes upon her, he detected that she carried his family's bloodline.

The direct lineage.

Recognizing this, Villa Master Qingyang's displeasure surged past its breaking point.

One had to understand: Eternal Villa was famous throughout the realm for doting excessively on its younger generation. If they wanted spirit stones, they got spirit stones; if they wanted rare treasures, they got rare treasures. Even if spoiling them turned them into haughty, extravagant playboys, the elders spared no expense, all to ensure their children possessed the one quality the clan prized above all else:

Pride.

A self-esteem and unyielding dignity higher than the heavens.

It was precisely this unyielding pride that drove Eternal Villa to resist to the bitter end during the God-Demon War ten thousand years ago, and it was this very same trait that doomed Eternal Villa to be eradicated in the future demonic invasion three centuries later.

Even so, Villa Master Qingyang felt zero regret. He took immense pride in it:

A child of the Qingyang clan would never bow their head to anyone—be it immortal or demon!

Thus, seeing a young woman carrying the direct bloodline of the Qingyang clan standing behind that Soul Formation cultivator like [a helpless dodder vine](# "Literal CN: 菟丝花 (tùsīhuā) — Chinese dodder, a parasitic plant used as a metaphor for a woman who has no independence and clings utterly to a man"), her eyes overflowing with pathetic infatuation, the blood rushed straight to his head. He couldn't resist looking closer.

Those few extra glances, however, proved catastrophic. Villa Master Qingyang felt his blood vessels were about to detonate!

First, he noticed that while the young woman was indeed of his direct bloodline, the swaddled infant in her arms was unmistakably not! It carried the lingering aura of that Soul Formation cultivator, but possessed zero connection to the Qingyang family.

[How could a daughter of my Qingyang family sink to raising someone else's bastard?!]

Second, this young woman was fully decked out in several supreme-grade spiritual treasures—every single one of them ancient heirlooms of Eternal Villa and Sky City! While Villa Master Qingyang wasn't entirely familiar with the specific functions of Sky City's treasures, he knew his own family's heirlooms like the back of his hand. Most of them were paired defensive artifacts meant to be worn exclusively when guarding another cultivator's breakthrough tribulation.

Right now, that brat was claiming he was about to ascend, making it glaringly obvious whom she was wearing those defensive treasures to protect. Not to mention, that Soul Formation brat himself was sporting several of the Qingyang family's own sacred treasures—specifically the kind refined to assist in breakthroughs and ascension!

Villa Master Qingyang simply couldn't wrap his mind around it:

[You share the exact same cultivation stage, and the treasures belong to our family—why the hell are you shielding him instead of ascending yourself?!]

Finally, what Villa Master Qingyang found most unforgivable of all was the young woman's cultivation base.

It wasn't too low—it was far too high.

Upon closer inspection, Villa Master Qingyang realized that not only did she look youthful, but her actual chronological age was shockingly young—she wasn't even fifty years old! One had to realize that ordinary Soul Formation cultivators were typically three to four hundred years old; even a peerless genius like Qu Qianxing had required nearly a century. Reaching the Soul Formation stage at such an age was impossible without employing horrific, shortcut methods to force rapid advancement.

The devastating sequelae of such methods were visibly stamped upon her body.

Villa Master Qingyang was no spiritual physician like Peilan and could not diagnose her internal condition at a single glance, but anyone—even without medical expertise—could see that her vital life force was completely extinguished. She was surviving purely as a living corpse sustained by external artifacts.

A living corpse was utterly incapable of bearing children.

Naturally, for a cultivator, bearing offspring wasn't particularly vital.

Although Villa Master Qingyang never condoned his children ruining their own bodies, if they made such a sacrifice for the sake of cultivation or some righteous purpose with a clear head, he wouldn't completely condemn it.

He had always worried that his most cherished great-great-great-granddaughter, Qingyang Qingyu, might one day do something reckless like this in the name of academic research. Villa Master Qingyang had even mentally prepared himself, knowing that with Qingyu's personality, once she set her mind to a goal, wild dragons couldn't turn her back.

Yet the young woman before him was nothing like that.

She had reduced herself to a living corpse, yet she gazed upon another man with tender, lingering devotion, looking down at an infant carrying none of her bloodline with gentle yet heartbroken sorrow. Anyone could see what kind of future she desperately craved, yet everyone knew she could never possess that future again.

Villa Master Qingyang could not bear seeing a direct descendant of his clan reduced to such a tragic, wretched state. He could not resist snapping in a harsh voice:

—"Who is she?!"

—"Tunhuo,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum replied, completely misunderstanding the target of his inquiry. Assuming he was asking about the Soul Formation cultivator preparing to perform the ascension rite, she offered an irrelevant name before explaining:—"He's your great... however many generations granddaughter's grandson-in-law."

[—"Grandson-in-law?!"](# "Speaker: Villa Master Qingyang")

—"You didn't see? Or don't you remember?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum asked, somewhat puzzled.-"Your great... granddaughter married the Lord of Sky City... Well, he isn't the City Lord just yet, but you should know it happens later. They had a daughter. Look over there—the one standing behind him holding the infant."

...As expected!

Villa Master Qingyang took a deep, shuddering breath.

Having recalled the previous life, he naturally remembered that in that cycle, Qingyang Qingyu had ultimately married Jian Nuo. Because he had always disapproved of the match, he had rarely visited Sky City. Even after Qingyu gave birth, he had only stopped by a couple of times right after delivery when the baby was still a tiny meatball. Consequently, he had never seen that meatball grown into adulthood, which was why he hadn't recognized the young woman right away—or rather, his subconscious had recognized her, but he had simply refused to let his thoughts wander toward such a horrifying conclusion.

Alas, denial was futile. He still had to face the brutal reality: this young woman—whose eyes were entirely filled with another person, having completely lost herself—was indeed Qingyu's daughter.

*Still, having grown up in Sky City without receiving the Qingyang clan's education, it's almost inevitable she'd turn out this way.*

*Besides, her condition might not necessarily be connected to that man. With the demonic invasion bearing down on them, perhaps she was forced to raise her cultivation just to fight off the demons?*

[Don't get angry, don't get angry, don't get angry...]

Villa Master Qingyang tried desperately to pacify his surging fury.

—"Your great... granddaughter is actually quite decent,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said, noticing his darkening expression and attempting to comfort him.-"Though originally, I also felt that with your clan's girl constantly gifting him sacred treasures, traveling the entire world at his side, paying no heed to his endless scandalous rumors with other women, and even handing Sky City over to him on a silver platter, she was indeed [paying out of pocket to simp](# "Literal CN: 倒贴 (dàotiē) — subsidizing a partner with one's own treasures and status while receiving nothing in return") a bit excessively, but..."

—"BUT WHAT?!"-Villa Master Qingyang felt he [could barely keep his coffin lid pinned down](# "Literal CN: 棺材板快压不住了 — an internet meme meaning an ancestor is so incandescent with rage at their descendants' foolishness that even death and a sealed coffin cannot hold them down").

He strongly suspected that after living through the last cycle, the only reason he was able to come back to life in this one was that he was enraged back to life by this great... granddaughter!

Regrettably, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had always possessed a notoriously low emotional intelligence. Otherwise, she would never have uttered such "comfort" in the first place. Seeing that Villa Master Qingyang's anger had far from subsided, she forged ahead with her attempt to console him:

—"Well, he is the great hero who stopped the demon invasion after all!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum argued.

Recovering her memories had done nothing to dampen her high opinion of that Soul Formation brat.

After all, had she not trusted him implicitly, she would never have agreed to assist his ascension. As for what transpired afterward, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum didn't believe it was his fault.

Villa Master Qingyang, however, lacked any such sentimental foundation.

He found her high regard utterly absurd:

—"Hero? What can a mere Soul Formation cultivator possibly accomplish?!"

—"Don't underestimate him. Although he only possessed Soul Formation cultivation, he earned the recognition of the Immortal-Slaying Sword,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum instinctively began reciting his legendary achievements.-"In the previous cycle, after the Central Domain fell and the demons invaded en masse, it was this very hero who braved mortal peril to travel everywhere and sound the alarm—[though wherever he went, that place immediately fell]—quelled numerous demonic disasters—[which subsequently rebounded ten times worse]—and even personally slew the Demon Head—[Heavenly Spirit Sect's Mo Xi]..."

Recounting the sequence in plain words, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum suddenly fell completely silent.

A profound bewilderment gripped her: *Why didn't my past-life self notice how many glaring plot holes there were in his "heroic exploits"?!*

Villa Master Qingyang, however, was oblivious to her internal crisis and the dark truth behind those "heroic feats." Hearing her recounting, his expression softened by a fraction, though his suspicion only deepened.

—"The Immortal-Slaying Sword? How did he acquire the Immortal-Slaying Sword?"-Villa Master Qingyang interrogated, conducting a thorough background check.-"What is his lineage?"

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum snapped out of her daze and replied:

—"I'm not entirely certain either. I believe he was originally a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Because the sect was wiped out..."

*The Heavenly Spirit Sect?*

*Wait a moment! Why was the Heavenly Spirit Sect wiped out?!*

*And how did he kill the Untiring Immortal Venerable?!*

*Could it have been that Immortal-Slaying Sword...? But why did he want to kill Untiring in the first place?!*

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's heart thudded in wild terror.

In her previous life, when she heard the news, she hadn't reacted strongly. Back then, she had zero familiarity with the Untiring Immortal Venerable; if the Heavenly Spirit Sect ceased to exist, it was simply gone—it had nothing to do with her.

In this life, however, thanks to the Immortal Arts Assembly and the joint research on ancient scripts, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had spent considerable time residing within the Heavenly Spirit Sect. She knew intimately what kind of sect it was, what kind of titan the Untiring Immortal Venerable was, and what caliber of disciples they reared. Even if the Heavenly Spirit Sect of the previous life hadn't undergone educational reform, its fundamental roots couldn't have differed that drastically, could they?! So! How could such a sect be wiped out? And was it truly something that could be wiped out so easily?!

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum realized with escalating dread that every detail of that past life reeked of sinister absurdity.

Yet because she had cut her sentence off midway, Villa Master Qingyang remained in the dark about her terrifying realization.

A blessing in disguise, really—otherwise, his blood vessels truly would have popped.

Listening to her stammer and hedge, Villa Master Qingyang already had a dreadful premonition, but he tentatively steered his thoughts toward something more palatable:

—"A Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple? Well, that's somewhat acceptable, but what's this about being wiped out? Ah, right... in the previous cycle, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was indeed eradicated... Tsk, how could the Heavenly Spirit Sect possibly be wiped out? In particular, how could the Untiring Immortal Venerable ever die? I found it strange in the last cycle, but I never had the time to investigate, nor did I ever learn what became of Qianxing-er in that life. Wait, did that brat flee simply because his sect fell? To not even possess the resolve to live and die alongside his own sect—what utter cowardice!"

Even without knowing that this punk was the true culprit behind the Heavenly Spirit Sect's downfall, Villa Master Qingyang still despised him to his bones.

He even redirected his fiery resentment:

—"If you ask me, since they were both Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, why wasn't it Qianxing-er?! In fact, Qingyu should have married Qianxing-er! Not only would she be close to the Villa, but looking at how Qianxing-er trains and instructs his junior disciples, he would never let a child turn out ruined!"

At that moment, Villa Master Qingyang conveniently forgot that his cherished "Qianxing-er" was currently playing the Demon Sovereign amidst the demonic horde right before his eyes!

Yet speaking of children, Villa Master Qingyang recalled another critical issue that demanded strict interrogation:

—"Where did that infant come from? No matter how I look at it, it carries none of my Qingyang family's blood."

—"Ah, the infant?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's mind was reeling from the myriad bizarre discrepancies of the previous life, unable to give his question thorough thought, and answered reflexively:-"I'm not entirely sure either. There seemed to be a rumor that it was fathered with Heavenly Spirit Sect's Mo Bei..."

—"Mo Bei? That airheaded-looking child?!"-Villa Master Qingyang turned pale with sheer horror.-"Were our family's children not enough?! He even laid his hands on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's children?!"

***

[—"Achoo!"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo")

[—"A-tchoo!"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Somewhere inside White Jade Capital, two people sneezed at the exact same instant.

Qingyang Qingyu couldn't help looking at them with a bewildered expression:

—"What's wrong with you two? Is it really that cold here?"

—"Qingyu, you're concerned about me?!"-Jian Nuo gasped, tears of profound gratitude practically welling in his eyes.

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Mo Bei stared blankly into the empty void ahead, as though the tiny sneeze hadn't come from her at all.

*Well then. One is a fool, and the other is a complete airhead. Clearly, no meaningful response is going to be forthcoming.*

Qingyang Qingyu instantly felt that wasting concern on these two was a tragic misallocation of emotional energy.

She decisively ignored Jian Nuo—who had never once behaved like a sane, functional adult in her presence—and turned to Mo Bei:

—"Junior Sister Mo Bei, are we supposed to make that thing down there take flight?"

—"...Yes,"-Mo Bei finally responded. Her vacant, glassy-eyed expression remained unchanged as she continued staring downward.-"That is how the script goes."

—"The script, huh!"

Qingyang Qingyu murmured, following Mo Bei's gaze toward the depths below.

Within the interior of the airborne White Jade Capital, there was unexpectedly another tiny city nestled inside.

It was so small that if one called it an oversized barge, people might well believe it. Yet ten thousand years later, it would truly blossom into a genuine floating metropolis.

It was Sky City.
