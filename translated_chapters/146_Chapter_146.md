# Chapter 146: The Questionnaire (第146章 问卷)

[← Previous Chapter](145_Chapter_145.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](147_Chapter_147.md)

---

[Is this the Heavenly Realm?]

[Can this be considered a successful ascension?]

Kong Xiangfei wondered with profound unease.

In theory, he had weathered the trial of the ultimate lightning tribulation, the spatial corridor leading to the upper realm had opened before him, he had indeed ascended to the higher realm, and he had even landed safely upon the Ascension Platform—by every definition, his ascension was a triumph. Yet he felt not the slightest shred of post-ascension joy; instead, a crushing, suffocating pressure bore down upon him.

It had been an eternity since Kong Xiangfei had experienced such acute discomfort.

Ever since reaching the Tribulation Transcendence stage, aside from the True Immortals who occasionally descended on inspection tours, no one in his world had possessed the power to make him feel so wretched.

It was like clawing one's way with blood, sweat, and tears to the very apex of the food chain, only to be slapped back down to the bottom rung in an instant.

To comfort him, Xiang Fudou, who had come to receive him, offered reassuring words:

—"Don't worry; this is the reaction anyone has upon beholding a deity with their own eyes. After all, that is a god!"-Xiang Fudou said.

—"It seems I was too full of myself,"-Kong Xiangfei replied with a wry smile.-"I assumed that once I ascended, even if I still revered the divine, I wouldn't look as pathetic as I did back in the lower realm when welcoming the High Gods on their inspection tours. Who would have thought that at the very first glance, I would nearly lose all my strength and tumble right back down the ascension corridor?"

Xiang Fudou jumped in fright at Kong Xiangfei's words:

—"[You can't go dropping like that!](# "Literal CN: 这可不兴掉 (zhè kě bù xīng diào) — Northern slang: 'don't jinx it / that's not something you should ever do'") Now that you've flown up, the ascension corridor is already closing. If you tried to fly back now, not only would you fail to return to our minor world, you'd end up lost in the void—or worse, encounter terrifying extraterrestrial heavenly demons. It's essentially a one-way trip to oblivion!"

—"I know, of course; it was merely a figure of speech,"-Kong Xiangfei said with a helpless laugh.

He tilted his head back, gazing up at the colossal humanoid figure of radiant light:

—"It seems my luck is atrocious. The moment I arrived, I managed to offend a deity."

[“...”](# "Speaker: Xiang Fudou")

[This has nothing to do with bad luck.]

Xiang Fudou thought with a bitter tang in his mouth.

Rumor had it that regardless of which Southern Heavenly Gate one ascended through, the very first sight would inevitably be the deity stationed to guard the post. It was, for all intents and purposes, [the customary opening thrashing](# "Literal CN: 威杀棒 / 杀威棒 (shāwēibàng) — the customary fifty blows administered to new prisoners upon entry to break their spirit; an opening intimidation display").

After all, every cultivator capable of ascending was a favored darling of the heavens. Even if they had originally possessed a mild temperament, during their centuries as Tribulation Transcendence titans, their personalities inevitably grew... 'confident.'

And upon a successful ascension, heaven only knew what heights of insufferable arrogance some might reach.

Thus, being suppressed by divine might at first glance forced the vast majority of cultivators to realize immediately that they were as insignificant as insects, knocking them back into humble, obedient mortals.

As for the sort so arrogant they sought to challenge a god the moment they arrived, they generally didn't survive long in the upper realm anyway.

This was a theory Xiang Fudou had pieced together himself over the years.

Naturally, the Ascension Platform was hardly the place to voice it.

—Even though, in theory, a god could listen in whenever He pleased regardless of location, saying it behind defensive wards in the safety of one's own quarters felt infinitely more reassuring.

Xiang Fudou could only squeeze out two dry chuckles.

Kong Xiangfei failed to notice his senior brother's bitter gaze.

A flicker of anticipation still lingered in his chest:

—"I wonder when I will truly attain the bearing of an exalted immortal."

—"Heh, you've got a long, long way to go!"-Xiang Fudou laughed wryly.-"Attaining godhood isn't that simple."

—"Senior Brother...!"-Kong Xiangfei froze in shock, nervously tugging at his sleeve.

That colossal humanoid light source was still towering above the clouds!

Was the phrase 'attaining godhood' something one could casually utter right in front of a 'god'?!

—"Naturally you couldn't back in the lower realm. Cultivation there was about defying the heavens to alter one's fate,"-Xiang Fudou said with a wry smile.-"But now that you've ascended, such matters are no longer taboo. In fact, the exalted immortals wish nothing more than for us to figure it out as quickly as possible!"

He then proceeded to explain the upper realm's promotion system to his junior brother in painstaking detail.

As it turned out, ascending was merely the beginning.

Even with a successful ascension, someone like Kong Xiangfei was nothing more than a [rogue immortal without an official post](# "Literal CN: 没有编制的散仙 (méiyǒu biānzhì de sǎnxiān) — an unestablished wandering immortal lacking a government headcount quota (bianzhi)"). In reality, his fundamental power had undergone no qualitative transformation; he still faced an agonizingly long journey to convert the spiritual energy in his body into immortal energy.

Yet upon reaching the upper realm, lower-realm cultivation methods ceased to function.

Lower-realm manuals could not absorb immortal energy. For cultivators, converting this energy was actually far more troublesome than it was for ordinary mortals.

Mortals possessed 'pure' physical vessels. Soaked in the omnipresent immortal qi of the upper realm, their bodies naturally acclimated with ease, allowing them to integrate as immortals in short order.

Ascended cultivators, however, had spent lifetimes having their physical bodies reconstructed and tempered by lower-realm spiritual energy to the absolute limit. Converting that accumulated spiritual energy into immortal energy not only added an entire prerequisite step, but demanded an exponentially greater workload.

If one relied solely on slowly breathing immortal qi to convert it bit by bit, it would take several thousand—if not tens of thousands—of years.

—"Then what are we supposed to do?"-Kong Xiangfei asked in horror.

Xiang Fudou sighed:

—"Since relying on oneself won't work, naturally one must rely on the Heavenly Court. If you become a member of the Heavenly Court, you can receive resources that accelerate the conversion."

Indeed. If breathing alone was insufficient, the only recourse was consuming immortal pills, drinking immortal dew, and eating immortal fruits to rebuild one's constitution.

The lethal catch was that while commerce in most minor cultivation worlds was merely underdeveloped, the Heavenly Court had no concept of 'commerce' whatsoever, operating instead under a strict [rationed allocation system](# "Literal CN: 按需供给 (àn xū gōngjǐ) — supply according to need / centralized planned distribution without free trade").

Consequently, aside from securing an official post in the Heavenly Court, there was virtually no legitimate channel to acquire those treasures.

—"*Hiss...*"-Kong Xiangfei drew in a sharp breath, feeling the pressure mount even further.

Yet Xiang Fudou's 'Upper Realm Survival Tutorial' was far from over:

—"And to attain godhood, one must first become a Heavenly Official."

Only after completely converting spiritual energy into immortal energy could one become partially immune to the crushing oppression of divine might. At that stage, one could finally be considered a true immortal—a so-called 'True Immortal.'

However, a True Immortal was merely one tier higher in raw power than a mortal cultivator. Since even mortals brought up to the upper realm could swiftly become True Immortals, they were naturally a dime a dozen.

Beyond that lay the cultivation of divinity.

Unfortunately, divine sparks could not be attained through meditation or cultivation either; they required holding an [official post with real authority](# "Literal CN: 实职 (shízhí) — a substantive post with executive power, as opposed to an honorary or sinecure title").

Specifically, even if one [passed the exam for an official post](# "Literal CN: 考上编制 (kǎoshàng biānzhì) — securing an official government establishment quota"), becoming a True Immortal made one nothing more than a bottom-rung odd-job laborer. One had to strive for promotions into positions of actual authority, accumulating karmic merit and achievements for the Heavenly Court before immortal energy could be transmuted into divine power commensurate with that merit, gradually ascending to supreme divine existence.

—"So that is how it is,"-Kong Xiangfei marveled, stealing a reverent glance up at the towering radiant humanoid in the sky.-"This High God must have walked the same path!"

[“...”](# "Speaker: Xiang Fudou")

[Far more than that.]

According to what a drunken True Immortal had once leaked, overseeing lower-realm worlds was a [tremendously lucrative assignment](# "Literal CN: 肥差 (féichāi) — a fat job / lucrative posting with endless kickbacks").

Setting everything else aside, every single cultivator who ascended carried the immense spiritual energy of their native world within their flesh and bones.

Cumbersome as converting that spiritual energy into immortal energy might be for the individual, when a mortal became a True Immortal, they consumed upper-realm immortal qi; but when an ascended cultivator converted their spiritual energy, they were effectively injecting fresh immortal qi into the realm.

Not to mention, they served as ready-made military recruits.

Still, such truths could never be spoken aloud—not here, not anywhere.

Even if anyone who lived in the upper realm long enough would eventually figure it out for themselves.

The bitterness in Xiang Fudou's mouth deepened. He hesitated before asking:

—"The letter I sent you last time—did you receive it?"

—"The letter telling me not to rush ascending?"-Kong Xiangfei asked.-"Of course I received it! Back then, I had failed several ascension attempts in a row, and my anxiety nearly caused my mental state to collapse. It was all thanks to Senior Brother's advice to take things slowly at my own pace that I managed to calm my mind, which finally led to this success!"

[“...”](# "Speaker: Xiang Fudou")

[I'm doomed! The letter was a total waste! And after going through so much trouble to bribe a True Immortal to carry it down, too!]

Of course, precisely because a True Immortal had been delivering it, he hadn't dared to be too explicit.

Who could have guessed it would produce the exact opposite result?!

Xiang Fudou nearly blacked out on the spot.

Yet he still didn't dare state it bluntly now, asking only with a bitter sigh:

—"Why did you have to push yourself? When I ascended back then, didn't you say you had no desire for immortality, that you wanted to live a carefree, leisurely life down below?"

—"Senior Brother, there is no leisure left to be had,"-Kong Xiangfei said with a helpless smile.-"Signs of an extraterrestrial demonic invasion have appeared in our world!"

—"What?!"-Xiang Fudou's eyes flew wide.

Having spent all these years in the upper realm, he understood all too well what that signified—

It meant their native world might be reduced to a Demon-God battlefield. Even if they somehow managed to repel the demonic hordes, the world would fall under the complete, unyielding control of the upper realm!

Xiang Fudou instinctively glanced down toward the cloud banks, looking as though he were ready to leap right off the cloud tier:

—"How could there be a demonic invasion?! Our world is clearly positioned far from the Demon-God battlefield!"

—"Senior Brother, don't panic. We only discovered a few initial traces; if a full invasion does occur, our world still has several thousand years of leeway,"-Kong Xiangfei explained.-"However, those demonic domains cannot be purged. If left to expand, our world is doomed to be eaten away until it is riddled with holes. As the one closest to ascension, it fell upon me to fulfill my duty."

Saying this, Kong Xiangfei looked at his senior brother, his eyes shimmering with [the sparkling innocence of fresh foolishness](# "Literal CN: 清澈的愚蠢 (qīngchè de yúchǔn) — 'clear stupidity / pure, unadulterated foolishness', a popular meme describing naive college graduates entering society"):

—"Senior Brother, you explained so much just now, so how exactly does one become a Heavenly Official? By the way, our sect has seen so many great seniors ascend throughout history; surely many of them have become Heavenly Officials, right? For instance, what about Grandmaster? How is he doing now? He must have attained godhood by now and can easily purge those demonic domains, right...?"

—"*Cough, cough*... Grandmaster is a rather hot-blooded individual; he went to the front lines of the Demon-God battlefield,"-Xiang Fudou replied, his voice completely dry.

What else could he say? He couldn't very well blurt out: [Grandmaster took the civil service exam for over thirty years and failed every single time, until his life savings ran dry and he had no choice but to throw his life away on the Demon-God battlefield!]

Yet Kong Xiangfei picked up on none of the underlying despair, marveling instead:

—"As expected of Grandmaster! Even after ascending, his blood still runs so hot! Then what about Third Martial Grand-Uncle...?"

—"Martial Uncle, he..."-Xiang Fudou stammered.

—"What about Martial Aunt...?"-Kong Xiangfei pressed.

Kong Xiangfei and Xiang Fudou clearly hailed from a prestigious ancient sect. Because their world had not yet suffered the devastation of a Demon-God war, countless generations of forebears had successfully ascended.

After inquiring about every single one of those elders, Kong Xiangfei naturally turned his concern to the person standing right before him:

—"Then what about you, Senior Brother? It is understandable that the elders were headstrong and unwilling to be bound by Heavenly Court regulations! But with your temperament, you must have passed the exam to become an Immortal Official, right?"

—"Me...?"-Having just wracked his brains fabricating heroic excuses to gloss over their elders' fates, Xiang Fudou was at his absolute mental limit, and the truth slipped out.-"I took the exam for two years. I failed both times, and I'm already thinking of giving up."

—"Only two years? That doesn't sound like you at all, Senior Brother!"-Kong Xiangfei exclaimed in shock, his tone tinged with unavoidable reproach.-"We cultivators lack many things, but patience is never one of them! What does taking the exam for a mere two years even amount to?!"

—"Heh, heheh!"-Xiang Fudou let out a pair of hollow, rasping chuckles.

[Only someone who has never sat for the exam could utter such nonsense!]

[Can cultivating immortality even be mentioned in the same breath as taking an exam?!]

Xiang Fudou had no idea how to explain this to his foolish junior brother. Right at that moment, however, an unfamiliar voice drifted over from nearby:

—"Indeed! Giving up after a mere two years? You people lack perseverance entirely."

Accompanied by these words, two 'True Immortals' descended from the higher tiers.

—"What is that supposed to mean?"-Kong Xiangfei bristled immediately. While he had just been chiding his own senior brother, he took no pleasure in seeing an outsider reprimand him. Stepping forward to defend Xiang Fudou, he snapped:-"Just who do you think you—*Mmph, mmph!*"

Xiang Fudou clamped a hand over Kong Xiangfei's mouth with lightning reflexes, turning toward the gold-armored True Immortal with an ingratiating smile:

—"Lord Xun Zhou."

Every time he passed by, he would see this True Immortal leaning against the celestial gate without a shred of immortal dignity, and over time, he had committed his face to memory.

While this could hardly be called a friendship, an unestablished rogue immortal could never afford to offend an immortal holding an official post in the Heavenly Court. What was more, this man was a celestial general; if Xiang Fudou ever decided to head to the Demon-God front lines in the future, he might well need to consult him regarding suitable celestial army units. Naturally, getting on his bad side was out of the question.

Then again, was there any True Immortal a rogue could afford to offend?

After greeting 'Xun Zhou,' Xiang Fudou shifted his gaze to the dashing, valiant young man standing beside him.

This was the first time he had seen this 'True Immortal.' Yet taking in the aura radiating from him, Xiang Fudou's eyelids twitched uncontrollably, feeling as though he might be swallowed whole at any moment. And lethally enough, that provocative remark had come straight from this young man's lips.

Xiang Fudou cautiously inquired of 'Xun Zhou':

—"This immortal lord is...?"

—"I am Yuwen Xuan,"-the 'True Immortal' calling himself Yuwen Xuan answered before 'Xun Zhou' could even speak.-"An Education Official dispatched to inspect this sector! I originally intended to investigate why the exam pass rate in this sector is perpetually so abysmal, only to step out and immediately overhear such defeatist drivel! With an attitude like yours, how could you ever hope to pass?!"

Subjected to this tongue-lashing, even Kong Xiangfei—who had little grasp of the situation—looked thoroughly ashamed, to say nothing of Xiang Fudou.

Xiang Fudou hung his head, but on second thought, something felt amiss:

—"W-Why is our sector's pass rate the lowest? Doesn't every sector have a fixed quota?"

If their numbers were lower than other sectors, wasn't that the Heavenly Court's fault?!

As an examinee, Xiang Fudou felt like filing an appeal!

—"Because your sector's candidate quality is atrocious, of course!"-Yuwen Xuan fabricated without batting an eye.-"The quotas allocated to each sector by the Heavenly Court are naturally based on the historical performance of its ascended immortals! When you constantly perform like this, it would be strange if your quota were high!"

[Is that so?!]

[Every year the Heavenly Court only admits a handful of people—could that actually be our own fault?!]

Xiang Fudou's eyes bulged in shock.

Yuwen Xuan pressed on:

—"If things continue like this, the admission quota for your sector will be slashed even further!"

—"It can't be like that, Exalted Immortal!"-Xiang Fudou cried out.

Though Kong Xiangfei didn't fully comprehend the stakes, hearing this made him panic as well:

—"Exalted Immortal, we will make amends! What must we do?!"

—"Let us first inspect your attitude!"-Yuwen Xuan declared.

He glanced at 'Xun Zhou,' who produced two examination papers seemingly out of thin air.

Yuwen Xuan took the papers and shoved them into the pair of fellow disciples' hands:

—"As it happens, I intend to survey your thoughts regarding previous examinations. Fill out these questionnaires!"

Xiang Fudou looked down with trepidation, discovering an assortment of questions such as:

> Did you participate in last year's Heavenly Court Examination?
> 
> How many times have you taken the exam?
> 
> What were your impressions of the examination?
> 
> What category of questions predominated in last year's Heavenly Court Examination?
> 
> What was the final question?
> 
> What answer did you submit?
> 
> What are your current thoughts regarding that question?

Kong Xiangfei stared at the paper in distress:

—"B-But I've never taken the exam... I only just ascended."

—"You may still fill it out. Furthermore, there is no need to be nervous; these are questions anyone can answer,"-'Xun Zhou' said gently.-"His Excellency the 'Education Official' merely wishes to gather feedback to determine whether adjustments should be made for the next assessment. After all, word has reached us that everyone harbors significant grievances regarding the exam."

[“...”](# "Speaker: Xiang Fudou")

Hearing this, Xiang Fudou intended to [blow a few rainbow farts](# "Literal CN: 吹两句彩虹屁 (chuī liǎng jù cǎihóng pì) — lay on thick, sycophantic praise"), but even he couldn't bring himself to violate his conscience and claim that 'people don't have many grievances.'

—"Afterward, based on prior records, we will have all immortals residing in this sector collect a questionnaire. In addition, in... three days' time, His Excellency the Education Official will convene a special symposium for this purpose,"-'Xun Zhou' added.

—"It is of paramount importance! It concerns reforms to the examination system and this year's admission quotas,"-the 'Education Official' stated haughtily.-"In principle, everyone ought to attend, but my itinerary is exceptionally tight! I have no time to squander here, so those who can make it should do their utmost to attend!"

Hearing such an arrogant tone, Kong Xiangfei furrowed his brows in displeasure.

Xiang Fudou, however, found his junior brother's reaction odd—in his eyes, this was precisely how a Heavenly Official was supposed to speak.

If anything, 'Xun Zhou's' gentle demeanor was what set him on edge:

—"Please do your best to help spread the word as well,"-'Xun Zhou' said.-"Furthermore, I will place a questionnaire drop box beside the celestial gate. We expect you to answer conscientiously before submitting them."

Even so, Xiang Fudou dared not dismiss 'Xun Zhou' merely because of his courteous tone.

For reasons he couldn't articulate, this 'Xun Zhou' felt rather peculiar. Though his words were gentler than before, they carried an inexplicable aura that brooked zero refusal—an authority far more imposing than that of the 'Education Official.'

Xiang Fudou stammered:

—"O-Of course we will."

—"Very good,"-'Xun Zhou' smiled mildly.-"By the way, this is a newly arrived ascender, yes? In accordance with standard protocol, you must register your details in the ledger."

Kong Xiangfei promptly recorded his identity, whereupon he and Xiang Fudou scurried away as quickly as their legs could carry them.

—"We shall return immediately to complete the questionnaires,"-they said deferentially before departing.

Neither dared linger near the celestial gate a moment longer. The presence radiating from that 'celestial general' made them feel as though they were standing before their late, terrifyingly stern master.

Naturally, they would fill out the questionnaires with utmost diligence and notify everyone in the area.

After all, this concerned [sitting for the civil service exam](# "Literal CN: 考编 (kǎobiān) — taking the competitive public examination to secure an official government establishment post (bianzhi)")!

Little did the pair of fellow disciples know that once their backs were turned, the colors cloaking 'Xun Zhou' rapidly faded away like washed-out dye. His features and physique shifted, and moments later, a silver-haired immortal as pristine as white jade stood in his place.

Naturally, this was Zhou Hui.

Meanwhile, the 'Education Official' beside him underwent no transformation whatsoever. Yuwen Xuan was Yuwen Xuan; he had strutted out to conduct fraud using his very own face, utterly devoid of any fear of exposure.

Grinning cheekily, Yuwen Xuan asked Zhou Hui:

—"How was that, Master? Didn't I perform brilliantly? I got them to accept your questionnaires without breaking a sweat, and once they get back, they'll definitely spread the word of their own accord!"

—"You did well,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

Yuwen Xuan beamed with even greater pride.

Then Zhou Hui added:

—"Now then, we can head back."

—"Back where?"-Yuwen Xuan asked blankly.-"Shouldn't our next step be visiting nearby immortal townships to spread the news to even more people?"

—"The administrative office has a roster of registered rogue immortals. Mailing a copy to each rogue immortal will suffice; they wouldn't dare ignore it,"-Zhou Hui planned methodically.-"Time is pressing on this occasion, so it matters little if the sample size is somewhat incomplete. In any case, they will gather here three days from now, and there will be ample time to promote it then. Thus, for the moment, we have no further business in the upper realm."

At this, Zhou Hui cast his gaze downward:

—"As for heading back, we are naturally returning to our own world. We can hardly stay here for three full days, can we? In our world, that amounts to three whole years!"

—"*Ying~*"-Yuwen Xuan let out the whimpering cry of a Taotie.-"But we still need to monitor progress! Besides, there's still that enormous god..."

—"I will bring that 'divine corpse' back with us, but you are not allowed to eat another bite! Do you wish to turn into what you were just a moment ago?!"-Zhou Hui snapped, glaring sternly at him.

Yuwen Xuan rubbed his own face with a guilty conscience.

The role of 'Education Official Yuwen Xuan' had been a spur-of-the-moment addition. Originally, Zhou Hui had intended to approach the pair alone under the guise of 'Xun Zhou, Celestial General Guarding the Heavenly Gate.' However, before he could even draw near, Yuwen Xuan had suddenly suffered an 'episode.'

His body had abruptly ballooned, black woolly fur filling the entire office chamber like a colossal skein of black yarn.

It was the adverse side-effect of gorging on too much 'divine flesh.'

While a Taotie could devour deities without fear of karmic backlash, such an immature juvenile was bound to suffer indigestion.

Fortunately, as his body grew, his appetite expanded accordingly. The gigantic furball had whimpered and sniveled *ying ying ying* while clumsily fumbling through its own thick black fur to fish out scraps of flatbread crumbs he carried on his person. Stuffing them into his mouth, he had finally managed to revert to human form.

It was solely due to this accident that Yuwen Xuan was able to appear before the two fellow disciples in human guise.

—"Fine, I won't eat it for now,"-Yuwen Xuan muttered.-"But do we truly have to return?"

—"Naturally. Three years is not a period of time to be squandered, and I have concerns regarding the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Zhou Hui said. He glanced at the magical chronometer he had specially refined, which displayed the differing flow of time between the upper and lower realms.-"We have been here for half a shichen already; roughly half a month has elapsed down below."

—"Only half a shichen,"-Yuwen Xuan mumbled.

Still, even he had to admit that in a mere half a shichen, Zhou Hui had accomplished a staggering amount.

Not only had he slain a deity and abducted an entire department of True Immortals, he had even drafted an examination questionnaire for rogue immortals aspiring to enter the civil service!

Yuwen Xuan fidgeted restlessly:

—"No, I still feel this isn't secure enough. How about this? Master, you head back first, and I will stay behind to set up the board here, welcoming you back with perfection three days from now—*Aaaagh! Don't drag me by the neck! Even if you insist on grabbing me, grab the scruff of my neck, don't choke the front!*"

Yuwen Xuan screeched.

Even in human form, he was hauled away by Zhou Hui just like a scruffed black lamb.

---

The return journey was instantaneous.

Because he had never traversed the ascension corridor in the first place, Zhou Hui faced no risk of becoming lost in the void, reappearing within the Heavenly Spirit Sect in a single breath.

Yet upon returning to his familiar immortal cave, Zhou Hui nonetheless exhaled a breath of relief.

Ever since attaining sufficient cultivation, Zhou Hui had been capable of sensing virtually anything occurring anywhere in this world. Rare was the experience of being in the upper realm, completely severed from perception of the mortal domain; it had felt as though a vital sensory organ had been excised from his body, deeply unsettling him.

Now that he was back, that perception was fully restored. With this—

—"Where are the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples?"-Zhou Hui asked, puzzled.

—"You're right, there's a lot of people missing!"-Yuwen Xuan twitched his nose, sensing the anomaly.-"At least two-thirds of the rations... *cough*, disciples are absent from the sect."

[Don't tell me the cycle of sect destruction has started all over again?!]

With another teleportation, Zhou Hui arrived at Buyan Palace at the center of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Fortunately, circumstances at Buyan Palace remained as tranquil as ever. A few disciples were still wandering casually along the corridors, showing no signs of a cataclysmic crisis; there were simply far fewer people than usual, lending the halls an unusually leisurely air.

Yet this only deepened the confusion of Zhou Hui and Yuwen Xuan.

—"Bizarre. Where did everyone go?"-Trailing behind Zhou Hui, Yuwen Xuan glanced left and right before snatching a passing disciple by the sleeve.-"Hey! Do you know what happened to everyone in the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"Immortal Venerable, Senior Brother Yuwen,"-Gan Tang bowed respectfully toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable before explaining:-"Everyone went to Eternal Villa's Spirit Stone Secret Realm. Word has it that... a fissure to the Demon Realm opened up over there."

—"?!"

Even Zhou Hui froze in utter disbelief at this revelation.
