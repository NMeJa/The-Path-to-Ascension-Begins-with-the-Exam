# Chapter 137: A Curse from Ten Thousand Years Ago? (第137章 万年前的诅咒？)

[← Previous Chapter](136_Chapter_136.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](138_Chapter_138.md)

---

—"Did he really say that?"-Zhou Hui inquired thoughtfully.

—"Yes, Master,"-Qu Qianxing nodded in confirmation.

After settling Jian Nuo, he had returned and reported their conversation verbatim to his master without missing a single detail. Yet Qu Qianxing had offered no personal opinions on the matter; once the report concluded, he simply stood quietly to the side, waiting for Master's inquiry.

Beside him, Lu Shulong looked as though he wanted to say something upon hearing this. But though his mouth parted, the words failed to emerge.

In any case, Mo Xi had already caught wind of the news and rushed over. He could always be counted on to blurt out the most obnoxious remarks before anyone else.

—"Why on earth did he tell Second Senior Brother about this?!"-Mo Xi demanded, eyeing Qu Qianxing's face with deep suspicion—though this suspicion was directed not at Qu Qianxing, but at Jian Nuo.-"According to his story, if this secret is truly of such earth-shattering magnitude, why did he tell Second Senior Brother instead of reporting directly to Master? It couldn't be that he fell in [love at first sight and infatuation at second](# "Literal CN: 一见倾心二见钟情 (yījiàn-qīngxīn èrjiàn-zhōngqíng) — falling in love at first glance and utterly smitten on the second; a comedic exaggeration of romantic tropes") with Second Senior Brother, could it?!"

[What in the world is this nonsense?!]

—"I asked him,"-Qu Qianxing rolled his eyes at Mo Xi.-"Jian Nuo has been conducting his investigation under the guise of being close with the Palace of Carefree Wandering. He hasn't been exposed to this day and is even treated by them as an honored guest. He has no desire to ruin that arrangement, which is why he hoped I would pass the message along."

—"He publicly proclaimed his reverence for Master—how is that not suspicious?"-Mo Xi snorted coldly.

—"It's different,"-Qu Qianxing analyzed.-"He and Carefree Palace are not partners or allies; he is an 'honored guest,' an identity that is rather cleverly advantageous. Carefree Palace has no right to restrict his social circle and must even flatter and cater to him. Even if he adopts a posture leaning toward Carefree Palace's enemies, Carefree Palace must concede even greater benefits just to win back their 'honored guest.' Of course, this is also because his initial positioning was exceptionally advantageous—after all, the entire cultivation world knows that City Lord Jian and Master do not get along."

—"Hmph, annoying [second-generation cultivation brat](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — children of high-ranking cultivators born with silver spoons and immense inherited privilege"),"-Mo Xi muttered under his breath.

He naturally understood the logic Qu Qianxing laid out, but that didn't make him any less annoyed.

Mo Xi continued nitpicking:—"Still, did he really trust Second Senior Brother that easily? I would have thought he'd prefer seeking out the 'cursed Fifth Senior Brother'!"

—"He already tested Shulong—namely with that golden crown. Finding that Shulong knew nothing whatsoever, he came looking for me,"-Qu Qianxing said. Before returning to report, he had naturally coaxed the full details out of Jian Nuo.-"As for why he trusted me... hmph, he investigated my background long ago and knew of the bad blood between Carefree Palace and me."

—"Whoa!"-Mo Xi let out an awe-struck sigh.

[A true warrior!]

[Did this guy manage to step on every single one of Second Senior Brother's absolute taboos in record time? Whether it's wanting to become Master's disciple or digging into Second Senior Brother's past... tsk, impressive!]

Mo Xi obediently shut his mouth.

At this moment, he no longer found Jian Nuo an eyesore; he actually felt a twinge of sympathy for him.

However, the others weren't concerned with Jian Nuo's ultimate fate.

—"Second Senior Brother, regarding Shulong's curse, do you think what he said is true?"-Guan Ye asked with furrowed brows.

—"Ah! That!"-Ran Deng piped up.

The others turned their gaze toward Ran Deng.

Ever since the Ascension Stairway had been repaired, Ran Deng had been perpetually half-dead due to the aftermath of her trial.

In order to suppress the soul flame, she had even carved an ice cavern for herself, usually hiding inside wrapped in thick quilts looking utterly pitiful—to the point where even her own clan didn't dare pressure her to make academic progress.

The other disciples felt it inappropriate to ask her directly how Master had repaired the Ascension Stairway or what had happened to her.

Furthermore, with Zhou Hui having been occupied these past days deciphering the Heavenly Dao, even direct disciples like Qu Qianxing remained ignorant of the exact process behind Master's restoration of the Stairway.

Only because Jian Nuo brought up the curse had Zhou Hui specifically summoned Ran Deng over today.

Now it appeared this summon was by no means without cause.

—"How is this related to you as well?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

Hearing the unmistakable distaste in Qu Qianxing's voice, Ran Deng let out a feeble, half-dead snort, disdaining to waste breath on him.

Before completely refining the soul flame, she had zero desire to speak to anyone.

Unfortunately, Master commanded:—"Ah-Ran, tell everyone about what you experienced."

—"Yes,"-Ran Deng answered helplessly.

After all, she was the only one who knew the details firsthand.

Thus, Ran Deng explained in detail the trial she had undergone and the ten-thousand-year-old projections she had witnessed upon the Ascension Stairway. Throughout her recounting, Ran Deng omitted Eldest Senior Brother's current condition—because afterward, the little black lamb had paid her a hefty hush-money bribe accompanied by fierce threats, forbidding her from revealing his state to anyone.

Ran Deng felt Eldest Senior Brother was merely struggling in vain at death's door, but she had tentatively agreed.

Fortunately, the crux of this trial did not hinge on Eldest Senior Brother.

—"Wait a moment! You obtained the qualification to ascend?!"-Qu Qianxing stared intently at Ran Deng, his eyes narrowing.

—"What does that count for?"-Ran Deng said.-"According to Eldest Senior Brother, Master obtained godhood!"

—"Master obtaining godhood is only natural,"-Qu Qianxing declared matter-of-factly. He glared at Ran Deng.-"The question is, why on earth were *you* able to obtain the qualification to ascend?!"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

Things had indeed turned troublesome.

Ran Deng rolled her eyes.

This was precisely one of the reasons she had been loath to mention this matter to the others—especially Qu Qianxing.

To a [second-generation involution king](# "Literal CN: 二代卷王 (èrdài juǎnwáng) — hyper-competitive overachiever; someone fiercely obsessed with coming out on top") like Qu Qianxing, having "first place snatched away by another disciple" reduced matters like "new disciples" or "having his past exposed" to mere trivialities.

Especially when that very "first place" had been snatched by Ran Deng, universally acknowledged as the laziest slacker in the entire sect.

Had it been Guan Ye, perhaps... well, no, regardless of who it was, Qu Qianxing would never accept it lying down.

—"How was I supposed to know the trial would turn out like that?"-Ran Deng said, curling her lip.

Qu Qianxing glared resentfully at Ran Deng's [shameless apathy](# "Literal CN: 摆烂 (bǎilàn) — letting things rot; giving up effort / slacking off with zero remorse"), then turned toward Zhou Hui:—"Master, if I climb the Ascension Stairway once, could I also undergo the trial?"

—"Do you wish to ascend as well?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Ascension is a minor matter—"-Qu Qianxing ground out through gritted teeth.

[So taking first place is the major matter?!]

Ran Deng let out a cold snort.

Zhou Hui cast a helpless look at his second disciple:—"There is no need to climb it purely for the sake of rankings. Ah-Ran's circumstances at the time were exceptional. As for the Stairway's current state, the main issue is that I haven't fully mastered that 'godhood' of mine. Once everything is finalized, I will likely need your assistance to run diagnostic tests."

—"Is that so?"-Qu Qianxing murmured.

Zhou Hui's explanation made it somewhat more palatable, yet the look he directed toward Ran Deng remained laced with lingering indignation.

—"Besides, you have plenty on your plate recently, do you not? The scheduling wouldn't align anyway,"-Zhou Hui added, attempting to placate Qu Qianxing.

—"[Time is like water in a sponge; squeeze a little and—](# "Literal CN: 时间这种东西，挤挤总有 — referencing Lu Xun's famous quote: time is like water in a sponge, there is always some if you squeeze")"

—"Master, Second Senior Brother has his hands full, but my coursework simply proceeds step-by-step,"-Guan Ye suddenly chimed in.-"If there are any tests to be run, you can call upon me at any time."

Qu Qianxing instantly shot daggers at Guan Ye.

Mo Xi and Lu Shulong exchanged a glance, quietly edging closer to the corner where Mo Bei stood holding the black lamb—having reached the tournament finals, Mo Bei had returned from Fengqing City half a month ago.

—"Here I was thinking that Young Master Jian must harbor some grand conspiracy by dashing all this way for exams!"-Mo Xi grumbled.-"The truly bizarre ones are right here on our side!"

—"Indeed!"-Lu Shulong looked at Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye with a flicker of hopeful anticipation.

Judging by how Master had previously explained the Heavenly Dao to him in private, Master clearly intended to make Lu Shulong his research assistant.

Yet Lu Shulong harbored little enthusiasm for "first place."

He currently wished Second Senior Brother and Fourth Senior Brother would duke it out and snatch the assistant spot for themselves.

However, the reigning "first place" holder, Ran Deng, had zero desire to be roasted over an open fire.

She was already physically and mentally exhausted from being scorched by her own soul flame; why should she have to endure her martial brothers glaring at her like starving tigers?!

Ran Deng glanced at Lu Shulong, attempting to steer the topic back on track:—"Rather than the Ascension Stairway, isn't Shulong's 'curse' far more important?"

These words finally brought everyone back to their senses, and their collective gazes turned once more toward Lu Shulong.

[Keep going, please! You can completely ignore me!]

Lu Shulong's entire posture screamed resistance:—"I've grown this big without ever feeling cursed! Could that Jian Nuo be talking complete nonsense? Or perhaps he was deceived as well?"

—"He shouldn't have any reason to lie,"-Qu Qianxing analyzed.-"Yet the likelihood of him being duped is equally slim. According to his account, this curse is something known to both the Ceremony Court and Carefree Palace. Certain clans offended True Immortals during the God-Demon War and were subsequently cursed."

—"That matches Senior Sister's account,"-Guan Ye said thoughtfully.-"'Lu' is not a common surname among dragonkin in this realm. What's more, Shulong is a True Dragon. We have never been able to locate Shulong's parents all this time. Perhaps he is indeed the descendant of the dragonkin who destroyed the Ascension Stairway ten thousand years ago."

—"But I'm perfectly hale and healthy! If there were truly a curse on me, that would practically be an insult to Master!"-Lu Shulong protested.

He was visibly unenthusiastic about the prospect of tracking down his biological parents.

To his mind, since Master had hatched him, Master *was* his parent.

—"Perhaps it's precisely because you have always stayed by Master's side that others could never lay a hand on you?"-Qu Qianxing pointed out.-"Especially since Jian Nuo mentioned that Carefree Palace is in fact one of the Upper Realm's proxies. Everyone knows the relationship between Carefree Palace and Master—so long as you remain here by Master's side, you are safe!"

—"Hmm,"-Lu Shulong was somewhat swayed by the argument.

Even so, he didn't care for the sensation.

Although outwardly Lu Shulong appeared to be one of the milder-tempered among Zhou Hui's direct disciples, he was nonetheless a dragon—a creature perched at the pinnacle of apex predators.

Lu Shulong thoroughly loathed the feeling of being treated as prey.

He turned toward Zhou Hui:—"Master, what do you think...?"

—"Well, rather than guessing blindly, we might as well ask someone who actually knows,"-Zhou Hui said.

[—"Someone who knows?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

—"Indeed. You didn't truly believe that the little things you children do in secret are completely hidden from the adults, did you?"-Zhou Hui said with a faint smile.

As he spoke, he initiated a communication link with Jian Haoge.

In truth, Zhou Hui had intended to do so the moment he heard Qu Qianxing's report. However, considering that the disciples—primarily Lu Shulong, one of the parties involved—knew nothing of the causes and consequences, he had summoned Ran Deng first.

After all, Zhou Hui was not the person who underwent that trial; having Ran Deng explain it directly made things much clearer.

Now that all the disciples understood the situation, it was naturally time to contact the parent of the child who brought the news to inquire about the details.

Faced with Zhou Hui's impeccably logical rationale, the disciples present shuddered involuntarily for reasons they couldn't quite name.

Suddenly, every single one of them felt a twinge of pity for Jian Nuo.

---

—"Did something happen to Nuo'er?!"-Jian Haoge inquired in a frantic rush the very instant he saw Zhou Hui.

—"No, not at all. He arrived safely at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Someone should have already reported this to you,"-Zhou Hui countered with mild curiosity.-"Why are you so worried?"

—"Because of you, of course!"-Jian Haoge immediately slipped into his standard routine, flying into a huff.-"It has been three thousand six hundred ninety-seven days and four [double-hours](# "Literal CN: 时辰 (shíchen) — traditional Chinese two-hour time unit; four shichen equals eight hours") since the last time you initiated contact with me! And that time was only to borrow a book found exclusively in Sky City! Basically, if I don't reach out to you, you'd never in your life remember to contact me!"-Jian Haoge accused heatedly.-"So for you to actively seek me out, how could it possibly be anything good?!"

Having finished his tirade, he immediately checked once more:—"So, my Nuo'er really didn't get into trouble?"

—"He's fine. He merely mentioned a few things that I would like to verify with you,"-Zhou Hui said.-"It is somewhat important, so could I trouble you to find a secluded spot without people around?"

—"Oh?"-Jian Haoge's expression turned grave upon hearing this.

Although he constantly bickered with Zhou Hui (entirely one-sidedly), business was business.

Especially when Zhou Hui described something as "important," it was generally best understood as a catastrophe on par with the sky collapsing.

Thus, following Zhou Hui's request, Jian Haoge flew thousands of meters up into the sky above the clouds. Ensuring that even the City Spirit who followed him everywhere could not perceive his presence, he finally asked:

—"All right, what do you want to say?"

—"Are you certain there is no one nearby?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"You are so long-winded! This is the Ninth Heaven approaching the void; not even a bird could—"

Jian Haoge's voice lodged abruptly in his throat.

A terrifying restriction surged across from Zhou Hui's end, completely sealing off everything around him—even the Three Thousand Great Daos were thoroughly shut down!

As a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, Jian Haoge could neither radiate the slightest breath outward nor perceive any aura from the outside world. He felt as though he had been reduced to a severed piece of the void itself.

—"How... did you do that?!"-Jian Haoge asked in utter shock.

He had always known there was a certain gap in power between himself and Zhou Hui, but no matter how wide that gap, there had to be a limit!

A restriction technique like this—even if a True Immortal descended in person, they couldn't pull this off!

He believed Zhou Hui was capable of sealing him, but sealing the Three Thousand Great Daos as well?! How was that remotely possible? That was rewriting the fundamental laws of the world!

Infuriatingly, Zhou Hui's tone remained as effortlessly casual as every single time he had defeated him in the past:

—"How? This restriction is merely a very ordinary—"-Zhou Hui began.

—"I'm not asking what restriction you used!"-Hearing that cadence, Jian Haoge knew immediately that Zhou Hui was about to launch into a lecture, and cut him off impatiently.-"I'm asking, how on earth do you have the authority to seal the Three Thousand Great Daos?!"

—"Oh, that?"-Zhou Hui lost interest somewhat.-"Didn't you see it last time? I obtained godhood."

—"I know you obtained godhood... Wait a minute, you obtained the godhood of a *True God*?!"

—"Is there such a thing as a fake god?"

—"Something even lower than a pseudo-god! We all assumed that because you repaired the Ascension Stairway, you were granted some measure of temporary godhood! Was that not the case?!"-Jian Haoge gasped in disbelief.-"That was PERMANENT?!"

—"I suppose so?"-Zhou Hui replied nonchalantly.

He hadn't merely repaired the Ascension Stairway; he had also acquired the administrative authority over the Stairway's underlying laws, making the godhood genuine and indisputable.

He had assumed Jian Haoge and the others already knew.

—"[Like hell I knew!](# "Literal CN: 我知道个P (wǒ zhīdào gè P) — polite bowdlerization of 我知道个屁 ('I know a fart!'; I didn't know shit!)")"-Jian Haoge finally abandoned all semblance of poise befitting the City Lord of Sky City—not that such poise had ever existed in front of Zhou Hui anyway.-"That is *godhood*! Godhood! Why do you act like you just picked up a pebble on the side of the road?!"

Thinking carefully, it was all Zhou Hui's fault for reacting far too casually atop the Ascension Stairway.

Who on earth receives divine godhood and acts as though nothing had happened?!

Consequently, every Tribulation Transcendence titan present at the time had assumed it was merely temporary godhood—given Zhou Hui's monumental achievements, being bestowed a boon of that level seemed entirely plausible.

Yet it turned out to be permanent! Didn't that mean Zhou Hui had become an actual True God?!

And all this time, he had been shouting and yelling at a literal god...?!

Jian Haoge descended into utter mental chaos.

In the cultivation world, everyone strove toward becoming an immortal or a god, but once one succeeded, one ascended to a higher plane of existence. How could anyone treat such a reality with casual indifference?!

Yet Zhou Hui remained entirely nonchalant.

—"Every single time I took first place in an immortal arts duel, refined a magical treasure, mastered a new technique, picked up a dragon, or overturned the Palace of Carefree Wandering, you had this exact same reaction,"-Zhou Hui noted.-"Doesn't that prove it's really nothing to write home about? Stop acting like one of my disciples finding out another disciple's cultivation has surpassed his."

(Qu Qianxing: "...")

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Jian Haoge")

Reminded by Zhou Hui, Jian Haoge suddenly recalled his own "tragic" life.

Right... was something like this remotely unusual when it happened to Untiring?

Isn't it just attaining godhood?

[If Zhou Hui set his mind to doing something, he could definitely pull it off... like hell he could!]

Jian Haoge silently swallowed a mouthful of blood, facing Zhou Hui with the same "placid serenity" as in the old days.

—"Then why did you call me out here? Surely not just to flaunt this *unimportant* (heavy emphasis) godhood of yours!"

—"Well, it concerns what Jian Nuo said..."

Seeing that Jian Haoge had "calmed down," Zhou Hui recounted Jian Nuo's claims to him in full.

Upon hearing the recounting, Jian Haoge was consumed by jealousy on an entirely different level:

—"What is this nonsense?! Being surpassed by you yet again in cultivation is one thing, but why is a child from my clan so infatuated with you that he even told you about the detective game he's been playing?"

[—"A detective game?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"Ah... Though he doesn't think he's playing a game; he genuinely believes he is avenging his father,"-Jian Haoge said with biting sarcasm.

As expected of a parent, he naturally knew what his own descendant was up to.

Whether he was willing to understand the boy, empathize with him, or lend a helping hand, however, was another matter entirely.

—"Vengeance? His father and grandfather... could it be?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"No, it has nothing to do with True Immortals. Those children in my household simply possessed wretched aptitude,"-Jian Haoge explained.-"Yet none of them were willing to accept reality. They believed that carrying the bloodline of Sky City entitled them to achieve great things. Unable to walk the righteous path, and with countless wolves seeking to lure them into depravity, they resorted to foul shortcuts. Nuo'er witnessed all of that, and thus stubbornly convinced himself that his father... and even his grandfather... were murdered by Carefree Palace."

Jian Haoge spoke of these matters as though reciting the affairs of strangers.

Zhou Hui stared at him in silence.

—"Don't look at me like that. In the beginning, of course I wanted to correct their course. But once children are born, they will live their own lives and inevitably grow into people you never wished them to be,"-Jian Haoge sighed. At this moment, he looked neither like the arrogant young master vying for supremacy against Zhou Hui nor the aloof, frosty City Lord of Sky City, but an ordinary father. Upon that face frozen at the height of dashing youth, there emerged an exhaustion seen only in the elderly.-"Especially for us cultivators—our lifespans are measured in millennia, yet those children grow up in the blink of an eye. One moment they are well-behaved toddlers; the next, complete strangers. I have already..."

Jian Haoge shook his head with a bitter smile:—"If Nuo'er believes everything is Carefree Palace's fault, perhaps that isn't such a bad thing. At least in his heart, he still clings to the belief that his father was redeemable! —Of course, Carefree Palace is hardly innocent. It is entirely true that they constantly scheme to exert vile influences upon those youngsters."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

Zhou Hui was at a loss for words.

He had never known that Jian Haoge harbored troubles of this nature.

[Fortunately, the children in my own household... don't seem much better either.]

Recalling those past reincarnations when he wasn't by his disciples' sides, Zhou Hui felt phantom spasms of ache in his stomach.

—"Looking at it that way, it's only natural Nuo'er worships you,"-Jian Haoge continued.-"To him, I am an unreasonable great-grandfather who turned a blind eye to Carefree Palace's atrocities and stood idly by while his own flesh and blood perished, whereas you are the great hero who dared to tear Carefree Palace apart!"

—"Then what about the True Dragon and the Qingyang clan...?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"That is an entirely different matter,"-Jian Haoge said solemnly.

He glanced around subconsciously, but upon recalling that he was thoroughly insulated within Zhou Hui's restriction, a flicker of relief crossed his face, followed immediately by another quiet snort.

Jian Haoge knitted his brows:—"This secret is likely known only to Sky City. After all, it was my grandfather who led the cultivators fleeing the Central Domain into the Northern Domain. A certain account circulated among those cultivators: that True Immortals, just like the Demon race, were foreign invaders. The main lineage of the Qingyang clan—the ones alive today are merely a branch family—once attempted to resist, only to be completely exterminated."

—"Furthermore, the one who sealed this world was not the True Immortals, but Saint Lord Qingyang of White Jade Capital back then! And because of that decision, the descendants of the Saint Lord and the bloodlines of his companions were marked by the Upper Realm. In particular, the defeated True Immortal generals swore an oath to annihilate every last trace of those bloodlines."

—"However, because this involved True Immortals, my grandfather didn't dare recount it to us with excessive clarity back then, leaving behind only fragmented records... Nuo'er most likely dug up these scraps and deduced that our household must be on the blacklist as well!"-Jian Haoge chuckled.

—"That might actually be true. Don't you think you know a bit too much?"-Zhou Hui said.

He still found Jian Haoge's mysterious disappearance in those previous timelines suspicious, and felt a warning was in order.

Jian Haoge was about to flare up:—"You! ...Are you serious?"

Taking in Zhou Hui's expression, Jian Haoge reined in his temper and asked.

Zhou Hui nodded.

Had this come from Jian Nuo, or indeed anyone else, Jian Haoge would never have paid it any heed. But coming from Zhou Hui, the situation was entirely different.

Jian Haoge fell silent for a moment before clicking his tongue:—"Fine! I'll look into it."

—"Before that, hand over the list of names you know who were 'cursed' by the True Immortals,"-Zhou Hui said, extending his hand.

Right here, a suspicion suddenly struck Zhou Hui.

A rather unpleasant suspicion:

Perhaps the script had never been targeting him in the first place, which was why its attitude toward him had been so perfunctory and careless.

That script was crafted to target his disciples, as well as those other specific bloodlines.

And he, Zhou Hui, was merely the wild card and game-breaker who fell completely outside the calculations of the Heavenly Dao—or rather, the True Immortals of the Upper Realm.
