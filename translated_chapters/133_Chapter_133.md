# Chapter 133: Young Master Jian of Sky City (第133章 天上城的简公子)

[← Previous Chapter](132_Chapter_132.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](134_Chapter_134.md)

---

To speak of Young Master Jian Nuo's decision, one has to start from his experiences earlier that very day.

During the [Hour of the Tiger](# "Literal CN: 寅时 (yínshí) — 3:00 AM to 5:00 AM in the traditional Chinese two-hour system"), Jian Nuo appeared at the city gates just as he always did.

As the young master of Sky City and arguably the most pedigree-blessed second-generation cultivator in the entire cultivation world, Jian Nuo's day began with surprising regularity.

Ever since establishing his foundation, so long as he was present in Sky City and not in closed-door seclusion, every single morning without fail, he would start from the city gates and walk a complete circuit around Sky City along its main thoroughfares. Neither rain nor storm could deter him, nor could lightning strikes budge him, to the point that people considered it an urban legend... *cough*, an amusing anecdote.

Regarding this routine of his, some suspected it was an esoteric mental art passed down within the Jian Clan, though no one ever saw another member of the Jian family doing the same. Some cultivators attempted to imitate him, yet noticed zero cultivation benefits; moreover, none could walk day in and day out for several centuries through rain and shine like Young Master Jian. Eventually, everyone grew accustomed to the sight and lost all curiosity.

Jian Nuo was exceedingly satisfied with that.

Truth be told, he had no profound motive whatsoever; he simply felt that heading out at dawn to breathe the crisp morning air was quite pleasant.

Furthermore, dawn at the Hour of the Tiger was precisely the moment Sky City "woke up."

At this hour, there was still some time before broad daylight. The [Golden Crow](# "Literal CN: 金乌 (jīnwū) — mythological three-legged crow representing the sun") was still marshaling its daily journey at the far edge of the Abyssal Ocean, yet the spiritual energy between heaven and earth had already begun its cycling shift—the most vigorous transition of spiritual energy in a twenty-four-hour cycle.

This juncture was ill-suited for meditative sitting, but exceptionally well-suited for practicing spellcraft.

Consequently, ambitious cultivators would all awaken at this hour: sword cultivators drilled their sword forms, spell cultivators honed their arts, and even those who did nothing at all benefited merely from taking in a few breaths of fresh spiritual qi.

Alongside the awakening of the cultivators, various activities in the outer city commenced as well: cultivators tasked with Dust-Cleansing spells began sweeping the avenues, water-bearers hauling spiritual spring water entered the gates, vendors hawking spiritual-food breakfast bites set up their stalls... Oh, and recently, a brand-new "profession" had been added to the mix:

—"Good morning, Young Master Jian!"

A greeting drifted down from above Jian Nuo's head.

He lifted his gaze and saw a flock of white cranes gliding gracefully toward the city interior.

Sky City had always been home to countless immortal cranes.

In fact, prior to the construction of Sky City, this very spot in the Northern Domain had been a natural habitat for immortal cranes. Thus, all sorts of immortal cranes could regularly be seen soaring through the azure skies and dancing outside the ramparts, counting among the region's famous attractions.

In the past, however, these cranes generally kept their distance from the cultivators of Sky City—

—"Don't think for a second we don't know what you're plotting! You either want to drag us off as personal mounts or enslave us to pull your carriages!"-the crane chieftain had once snorted coldly.-"Without sufficient spirit stones and competitive compensation, don't even dream about it!"

Yes, this flock of cranes was precisely that snobbish.

And their willingness to fly into the city nowadays naturally meant they had been offered ample "spirit stones and compensation."

—"Young Master Jian, care for a newspaper? The latest edition of *The White Jade Capital Daily*!"-the immortal crane who had greeted him chimed in enthusiastically.-"Oh, and we also have *The Cultivator Weekly*, as well as the recent smash hit, *Weekly Gossip*!"

—"So you lot have monopolized distribution for every single newspaper from the Central Domain,"-Jian Nuo remarked.

—"Heheh, the news from the Central Domain is so much more juicy! We enjoy reading it ourselves,"-the crane chirped.

No particular emotion showed on Jian Nuo's face, yet he inquired with seeming curiosity:

—"...Is there anything interesting in today's news?"

—"Naturally!"-the immortal crane exclaimed excitedly.-"The final examinations at the Heavenly Spirit Sect have begun!"

—"Huh?"-Jian Nuo blinked.

—"I heard the scene was utterly wild! A human wall ten meters thick was packed outside Buyan Palace, dozens of news outlets were recording holograms on the spot, and some examinees cracked under the sheer pressure right there, hanging themselves from the ancient peach tree at the mountain gates! The sheer tragedy of it all, *tsk tsk tsk*,"-the crane shook its feathered head.-"It frightened away quite a few people who were planning to take part in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grand Recruitment!"

It did sound truly pitiable.

—"Wait a moment. The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grand Recruitment?"-Jian Nuo asked.-"Aren't there still two months left before that? Or did the Heavenly Spirit Sect move it up early?"

—"Not quite,"-the crane shook its head.-"However, the moment the Ascension Stairway manifested, swarms of Central Domain cultivators took advantage of their geographic proximity and rushed over to the Heavenly Spirit Sect ahead of everyone else! Word has it that applicants wanting to register for the Grand Recruitment are already queued from the mountain peak straight down to the base! The Heavenly Spirit Sect probably won't even need to canvass regional mortal villages for eligible children this time; their admissions quota is already bursting at the seams! As for the finer details, why not buy a copy of *White Jade Capital* and see for yourself? It has a much more in-depth analysis of the Ascension Stairway!"

—"Fine, fine! Give me a copy,"-Jian Nuo conceded.-"Throw in one copy of each of the other new papers as well."

The immortal crane instantly let out a triumphant cry.

Counting out the fresh issues of the day, it asked with renewed zeal:

—"Do you want Carefree Palace's *The Cultivator Weekly*? If I recall correctly, your relationship with them is pretty good, isn't it?"

—"...Give me one,"-Jian Nuo said, then asked:-"How is it selling in the Northern Domain? Do many people subscribe in Sky City?"

—"Decent enough. For shopping, it's quite handy, so a good number of people in Sky City subscribe,"-the crane said. Then, as if an idea had just struck it, it inquired:-"Young Master Jian, would you like to set up a subscription while you're at it?"

—"No need. A mere shopping catalog holds little appeal,"-Jian Nuo declined.

—"True, someone of your stature has other channels!"-the crane chuckled, then added with cheeky cheek:-"Still, compared to *The Cultivator Weekly* which you buy only on a whim, you purchase *White Jade Capital* every single morning anyway. You really might as well take out a subscription—there's even a discount!"

Having delivered its pitch, it didn't press the matter further. As if afraid Jian Nuo might turn defensive and lash out in embarrassment, it snickered and swiftly flapped away.

Jian Nuo wasn't angry in the slightest. Holding the freshly purchased newspapers in hand, he simply watched the crane's receding figure.

He saw that the crane had not flown far; like the other cranes, it merely hovered and circled above the municipal wards.

As it passed over a certain courtyard residence, it suddenly gave an elegant flick of its wing. A thick newspaper went sailing toward the main gate, producing a dull *thump* as it landed cleanly inside the front courtyard.

Evidently, that was a household with an active subscription.

Such households were clearly plentiful, as dull *thump-thump* sounds echoed incessantly across the city.

Word had it that the City Guard had received a barrage of complaints lately, demanding that the immortal cranes improve their delivery methods.

Yet it was far from just residential households subscribing. As Jian Nuo continued along his habitual path, discussions regarding the various articles in the papers could be heard everywhere:

[—"Why haven't the tournament schedules for the Immortal Arts Assembly finals come out yet?"](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator A")

[—"Soon, soon. I heard the finalists have all traveled to Cloud City to prepare; the lot drawing will take place any day now."](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator B")

[—"Are they still holding it on schedule? I assumed that once the Ascension Stairway manifested, the almighty titans would divert their attention elsewhere..."](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator C")

[—"No way, didn't you read the papers a few days ago? It's precisely because the Ascension Stairway appeared that the almighty titans are paying *more* attention to Immortal Chess, hoping to boost the citation rates of their papers in *Spells*!"](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator B")

[—"Though Lord Immortal Venerable doesn't seem particularly fond of that practice."](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator D")

[—"That's true. The Immortal Arts Assembly will eventually come to an end; what will they do then?"](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator C")

[—"Host our own Northern Domain Immortal Arts Assembly?"](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator A")

[—"Hahaha! Those old masters are absolutely shameless enough to pull that off!"](# "Speaker: Pedestrian Cultivator B")

...

Listening to these bustling discussions, Jian Nuo felt a slight sense of unfamiliarity.

Although this was Sky City—where even the youngest children possessed cultivation potential and the entire population consisted of cultivators—in the past, matters such as the Ascension Stairway belonged strictly to the realm of high-tier powerhouses. Even if low-tier cultivators could see the Stairway from afar, they would know virtually nothing about it. Never would they have gathered in the streets like this to debate the Ascension Stairway and gossip about events in the Central Domain.

And it was not merely the low-tier cultivators; the high-tier cultivators were even more swept up in the frenzy.

Jian Nuo walked along, reading as he strolled.

Because he did not use spellcraft during his walks, and instead read his papers, observed passersby, and paused occasionally to greet acquaintances along the way, by the time the sky turned a pale grayish dawn, he had only reached the vicinity of the Heavenly Dao Alliance near the inner city.

This was the headquarters of the Heavenly Dao Alliance. Although the Tribulation Transcendence couple, Lord Mei and Lady Zhu, did not reside within the city proper to avoid clashing with their fellow Tribulation Transcendence peer, City Lord Jian Haoge of Sky City, high-tier cultivators were by no means scarce.

As Jian Nuo approached, he spotted several high-tier cultivators' cloud carriages parked nearby—the morning assemblies of the Heavenly Dao Alliance seemed to carry over the traditions of mortal imperial dynasties, commencing well before daybreak. Faint snatches of debate drifted out from within:

[—"Perfected Master Lai wants to go as well?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator A")

[—"That is the Ascension Stairway; why on earth wouldn't he go?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator B")

[—"Yes, but if this continues, the Void Refinement elders wishing to depart will make up three-quarters of our Heavenly Dao Alliance! Surely our Heavenly Dao Alliance can't just shutter its doors during that period?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator C")

[—"Aren't Lord Mei and Lady Zhu staying behind? It hardly matters if more of us subordinates go."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator B")

[—"You actually have the nerve to make Lord Mei and Lady Zhu cover your shifts?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator A")

[—"Besides, that flying ark from Sky City must have a passenger capacity, right?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator D")

[—"By the way, how many quotas did they allocate to us?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator C")

[—"No one knows yet! After all, construction hasn't even begun, and nobody knows who will oversee it."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Cultivator B")

...

Jian Nuo narrowed his eyes and gave an irritated shake of his head, having no patience to listen any further. Nor did he have any desire to enter the inner city—venturing further meant stepping into the territory of Sky City's high-ranking leadership. Those fellows didn't just debate amongst themselves; they would pester him incessantly. Recently in particular, hordes of people [itching with restlessness](# "Literal CN: 蠢蠢欲动 (chǔnchǔn yù dòng) — restless and eager to move / stirring into action") had come looking for him. Having zero interest in dealing with them, Jian Nuo pivoted on his heel and headed in a different direction.

Along the boulevard, he noticed that this morning seemed livelier than usual, with a substantial crowd heading toward the most bustling district in the north of the city—Pingkang Ward.

Jian Nuo was somewhat puzzled, but having no particular destination in mind at the moment, he followed along.

Just as he arrived outside Pingkang Ward, someone clapped him on the shoulder:

—"Old Jian, you came too?"

Jian Nuo turned around and saw several of his "close friends" gathered at the ward entrance.

Yet these gentlemen were truly not people accustomed to rising early. Despite possessing Golden Core and Nascent Soul cultivation, each sported a pair of dark circles beneath their eyes and looked thoroughly lethargic, as if they had just engaged in unspeakable debauchery at Carefree Pavilion.

Seeing them here of all places, Jian Nuo asked:

—"What are you guys doing here?"

[—"? You came all the way over without even knowing?"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend A")

[—"Can't be helped; Old Jian has had zero interest in the Immortal Arts Assembly or Immortal Chess from the very start."](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend B")

[—"*Tsk tsk*, you're truly missing out on so much fun!"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend C")

Hearing this, Jian Nuo instantly grasped seventy to eighty percent of the situation:

—"Don't tell me you lot are queuing up to buy those chess booster packs again?"

[—"How could that be?"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend A")

[—"We leave that sort of chore to the servants."](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend B")

[—"Exactly, why would we ever queue up in person?"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend C")

[Hard to say. Who was it that waited out here all night just ten days ago, I wonder?]

Jian Nuo complained inwardly, but asked aloud:

—"If not booster packs, then what for?"

—"The Immortal Chess Hall! The Immortal Chess Hall is finally opening in Sky City!"

Someone "answered" in a loud, carrying voice.

The voice came not from Jian Nuo's "friends," but from the opposite direction.

—"Ah, it's the little young master of the Mei-Zhu household..."-someone murmured.

Jian Nuo looked over, and sure enough, he saw Su Xin and his gang of fair-weather cronies swaggering in excitedly from outside the ward.

Even though both were descendants of Tribulation Transcendence titans and the Heavenly Dao Alliance headquarters was located right here in Sky City, due to various reasons, Jian Nuo and Su Xin had never run in the same circles. In fact, precisely because neither group lacked status, a mutual friction lingered between their respective cliques.

Of course, differences in cultivation and age were very real factors.

Between Jian Nuo at Nascent Soul and Su Xin at Foundation Establishment lay an age gap of several hundred years—virtually several generations' worth of generational divide.

Consequently, the two groups had maintained virtually no real contact. Only recently, with the sweeping craze of Immortal Chess, did Jian Nuo feel that his chances of bumping into Su Xin were steadily multiplying.

—"Are you guys certain you want to hang out with little kids?"-Seeing Su Xin, Jian Nuo instinctively wanted to steer clear.-"If you're looking for new novelties, wouldn't it be better to head over to Carefree Pavilion?"

Under normal circumstances, Jian Nuo's requests were granted without objection.

This time, however, Jian Nuo's "friends" reacted with immense excitement:

[—"But this is the Immortal Chess Hall!"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend A")

[—"What's the point of playing Immortal Chess if you don't play it at the Immortal Chess Hall?!"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo's Friend B")

[Given how you lot used to act, I thought you were planning to rot to death inside Carefree Pavilion!]

Just as Jian Nuo was about to speak, one of his "friends" asked curiously:

—"Old Jian, why do you always avoid the Mei-Zhu brat?"

—"...It's not that I'm avoiding him. It's just that if his parents think I'm a bad influence on him, it'll become an enormous headache. His parents are far too nagging,"-Jian Nuo replied.

Seeing that his "friend" still seemed intent on pressing the subject, he sighed helplessly:

—"If you're dead set on going, let's go take a look then."

---

—"Ah, it's Young Master Jian."

While Jian Nuo's group had been discussing Su Xin, Su Xin's side had likewise spotted Jian Nuo and company.

[—"Seems like we've been bumping into their crowd constantly lately!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony A")-someone let out a similar sigh.

[—"More than just bumping into them! Last time when Ah Xing was buying booster packs, he got into a fistfight with them."](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony B")

[—"That's right, they even agreed to settle it across the chessboard... Ah Xing, who ended up winning?"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony C")

[—"The match hasn't started yet, but Ah Xing is in serious danger! The other guy bought three hundred booster packs in one go and brute-forced his way into pulling the Youngest Junior Sister!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony D")

[—"Three hundred packs?! Just how [cursed is his luck](# "Literal CN: 多黑 (duō hēi) — how black/unlucky; gaming slang for terrible RNG or gacha luck")?! Didn't people say an average pull takes around fifty?!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony B")

[—"Well, the man has spirit stones!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony C")

[—"It's all thanks to his family anyway; he's just flexing because he's a few years older!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony A")

Listening to his cronies' banter, Su Xin ground his teeth in irritation.

If Jian Nuo's side looked down on Su Xin's clique as mere "little kids" and avoided them because Lord Mei and Lady Zhu were too troublesome to provoke, then the hostility on Su Xin's side was the classic resentment, jealousy, and envy of children toward adults.

Even among second-generation cultivators, their "grade levels" were different, the "allowances" provided by their families were different, the strictness of parental supervision was different, and the worldly pleasures accessible to them were different—making it inevitable that the "kids" envied the older group's "freedom."

And in Su Xin's case, there was an added layer.

To Su Xin, Jian Nuo was the quintessential ["child of other families"](# "Literal CN: 别人家的孩子 (biérén jiā de háizi) — the idealized child other parents constantly compare their own kids to").

It wasn't that Lord Mei and Lady Zhu did so maliciously, but in the Northern Domain, very few Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses still had cubs to raise—Zhou Hui and his disciples were far away in the Central Domain—so Jian Nuo seemed quite respectable by comparison.

At the very least, Jian Nuo was handsome, possessed refined elegance, and boasted commendable cultivation. Although he held somewhat ambiguous ties with the Palace of Carefree Wandering, on the whole, he harbored no egregious vices; his only apparent flaw was keeping poor company. Those were precisely the modest standards Lord Mei and Lady Zhu held for Su Xin. Consequently, in the eyes of the couple, Jian Nuo became the "older brother next door" used to motivate Su Xin.

Recently in particular, Su Xin, who was hopelessly addicted to Immortal Chess, desperately wanted to gamble his way into pulling a "Youngest Junior Sister" of his own.

[—Speaking of which, Mo Bei must be that direct disciple I saw during the exam, the one who controlled Buyan Palace's examination illusion while taking the test herself! She really is amazing! And so cute too...]

Having mistaken Mo Xi for a girl back then, and now mistaking Mo Bei for Mo Xi, Su Xin thought with a blush flushing his cheeks.

Alas, Lord Mei and Lady Zhu kept him on a very tight leash.

Playing Immortal Chess was fine—they were even willing to fetch brand-new maps directly from Zhou Hui the moment they were made for Su Xin to play—but spending exorbitant amounts of spirit stones to gamble on blind booster packs? Absolutely not! That was gambling!

Su Xin had thrown several tantrums over this.

Irritated by his whining, Lord Mei and Lady Zhu scolded Su Xin:

—"Even the child of the Jian Clan isn't addicted to Immortal Chess!"

—"In terms of seniority, he's not even as high as you!"

Such reprimands did nothing to dissuade Su Xin; instead, they only deepened his grudge against Jian Nuo.

[And now, isn't this fellow who supposedly 'isn't addicted to Immortal Chess' standing right here at the crack of dawn at the grand opening of the Immortal Chess Hall?!]

Su Xin glared resentfully toward Jian Nuo.

Just as Su Xin was brooding and quietly nursing his grudge, a cheer erupted from the crowd:

—"Ah, it's starting!"

It was the new promotional video.

For the grand opening of the Sky City Immortal Chess Hall, the Heavenly Spirit Sect (or rather Mo Xi—who evidently treated this as the first step in expanding his commercial empire into the Northern Domain) had spared no expense. Not only had he invited the finalists of the Grand Tournament to film a new promotional video, but he had also compiled their match highlights and interwoven them with contestant interviews, creating the cultivation world's very first "documentary."

Although the doors to the Immortal Chess Hall had yet to open fully, a colossal illusion was already being projected into the firmament above the building to seize the spotlight, drawing in ever more residents of Sky City.

Even Young Master Jian, who had been indifferent from the beginning, couldn't help lifting his head to gaze curiously at the footage in the sky.

Yet Su Xin, as a true [connected insider](# "Literal CN: 关系户 (guānxìhù) — someone with special connections or nepotistic privilege"), had already watched it beforehand.

In fact, to pamper him, Lord Mei and Lady Zhu had even brought back the behind-the-scenes documentary of the Immortal Arts Assembly contestants.

Su Xin possessed zero concept of "no spoilers"; having watched such footage, his sole intention was to brag about it to his cronies. But at this moment, glancing at Young Master Jian, the urge to give the man a thoroughly unpleasant jolt suddenly welled up within him.

And as if accommodating his scheme, the very scene he wanted flashed across the celestial screen.

—"Ah, it's Qingyang Qingyu!"-Su Xin shouted loudly all of a sudden.

Because of that name, several gazes instantly snapped toward him.

Naturally including Jian Nuo's.

—"Who?"-Su Xin's companion hadn't heard clearly for a moment.

—"Didn't you see? In the clip of Lu Zhen in Cloud City just now, the one who flashed past was Fairy Qingyang of Eternal Villa!"-Su Xin deliberately raised his voice.

—"...Ah, *that* lady!"-At this point, Su Xin's cronies caught on as well.

They cast snickering glances toward Jian Nuo.

After all, for quite some time, the fact that Young Master Jian of Sky City had fallen head-over-heels for the young miss of Eternal Villa had been massive news. Even in the era before newspapers, it had spread across the entire Northern Domain—enough to keep everyone happily [munching melon](# "Literal CN: 吃瓜 (chīguā) — eating melon / enjoying gossip as a bystander") for over a decade if no other major scandals occurred.

Having spent so much time with Su Xin, this bunch naturally knew what he was angling for.

One of them played along:

—"Wait, why would Fairy Qingyang be in Lu Zhen's footage? Don't tell me the two of them...?"

—"No, no! Not Lu Zhen—it's Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's second disciple, Qu Qianxing!"-Su Xin immediately relayed the gossip he had eavesdropped from his parents.-"I heard Villa Master Qingyang is exceedingly fond of him, and it seems the rumors are true! In the footage just now, Qu Qianxing and Fairy Qingyang were sitting together in the spectator stands!"

[—"Whoa! Does that mean Eternal Villa plans to form a marriage alliance with the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony B")

[—"For the sake of the Ascension Stairway?"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony C")

—"Stop talking nonsense! Eternal Villa's intentions were made long before the Ascension Stairway appeared, alright?!"-Su Xin corrected.-"Besides, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect are all exceptional; it's a completely [equal match in status](# "Literal CN: 门当户对 (méndānghùduì) — matching social status and lineage in marriage")!"

[—"True, that Lu Shulong is suuuper gorgeous."](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony D")

—"Hmph, that's only because you haven't seen Qu Qianxing! While you can't say Lu Shulong loses to him, Qu Qianxing is even more striking! No wonder Villa Master Qingyang wanted Qingyu to marry him,"-Su Xin said, so thoroughly immersed in the bliss of gossiping that he nearly forgot his original objective.-"Moreover, Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable seems very pleased as well. I heard he even exchanged disciples with Villa Master Qingyang—specifically Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu! That's practically parental approval already—"

—"Could you elaborate on that in detail?"

Right at that moment, a hand rested firmly on Su Xin's shoulder.

Su Xin spun his head around, only to be met with Jian Nuo's magnified face.

Su Xin jumped out of his skin.

He had merely wanted to bait the man a little; he hadn't intended to provoke him into beating him to a pulp!

Jian Nuo stared unblinkingly at Su Xin:

—"Could you please explain that part about exchanging disciples in detail?"

—"I-I didn't lie! Everyone in the Central Domain knows about this!"-Su Xin stammered in defense.-"If you don't believe me, you can watch the promotional videos for the Immortal Chess contestants! Not only are Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu in the promos, but they also appear in Lu Shulong's documentary segment, featured as Lu Shulong's senior brother and senior sister!"

—"Senior sister?"-Jian Nuo's brow twitched.

—"Y-Yes!"-Su Xin squeaked.

[—"Come to think of it, Lu Shulong and Fairy Qingyang make a pretty good pair too!"](# "Speaker: Su Xin's Crony A")-At this moment, one of Su Xin's companions started [recklessly playing matchmaker](# "Literal CN: 拉郎配 (lālānpèi) — force an arbitrary match / random shipping"):-["A talented man and a beautiful woman... wait, no, a beautiful man and a talented woman!"](# "Literal CN: 男才女貌……不对，男貌女才 (nán cái nǚ mào... bù duì, nán mào nǚ cái) — reversal of traditional idiom 'talented man, beautiful woman' (郎才女貌)")

Su Xin glared venomously at his companions:

[What the hell are you babbling about?! Are you trying to get me killed right now?!]

Fortunately, Jian Nuo did not actually throw a punch.

Instead, once the Immortal Chess Hall officially opened its doors, Jian Nuo bought an entire set of Immortal Chess merchandise to take home. And for some inexplicable reason, he even pulled Lu Zhen and Lu Shulong right there on the spot.

Especially Lu Shulong.

[He wouldn't actually consider him a love rival, would he?]

Su Xin thought nervously.

He only prayed that Jian Nuo wouldn't stir up any catastrophic incidents; otherwise, as the "prime instigator," he would be in deep trouble.

---

—"So, you want to go to the Central Domain?!"

After listening to the entire account, Jian Haoge glared at Jian Nuo in disbelief, then roared:

—"I forbid it!"

Facing the fury of a Tribulation Transcendence titan, Jian Nuo stood his ground and fired right back without yielding an inch:

—"I am informing you, not seeking your permission! Great-Grandfather!"
