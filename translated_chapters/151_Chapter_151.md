# Chapter 151: Research on Immortals (第151章 仙的研究)

While Jian Nuo's "friends" were [skulking about sneakily](# "Literal CN: 狗狗祟祟 (gǒugou-suìsuì) — a humorous pun on 鬼鬼祟祟 (guǐguǐ-suìsuì), meaning shady or skulking like a guilty dog") and plotting mischief, Zhou Hui was meditating.

A mere handful of juniors; they were hardly worth his attention.

Besides, ever since returning from his "ascension," Zhou Hui had noticed that his power had achieved a "breakthrough."

No—to be precise, the change had already begun after he acquired the "godhead."

Zhou Hui had previously sensed that his consumption of spiritual energy was decreasing.

However, that change had been far too minuscule.

Given Zhou Hui's vast, ocean-like reservoir of spiritual energy, the amount he expended each day could practically be measured in "micrograms." Therefore, even if his daily expenditure decreased, it was virtually impossible to detect.

In truth, had Zhou Hui not consistently set aside a dedicated block of time each day—even in his "retirement"—to meditate, perform inner introspection, and regulate his spiritual qi, he would have overlooked the change entirely.

Even so, it was difficult for Zhou Hui to ascertain what factor had caused those few micrograms of reduction. Given his level of cultivation, perhaps exercising just a fraction more mindfulness during spellcasting could reduce spiritual energy expenditure by that much.

Thus, Zhou Hui had simply shelved the question for the time being.

Until his "ascension"—when he stood face-to-face with deities and True Immortals!

When Zhou Hui told Yuwen Xuan that "the battle had been extraordinarily difficult," he naturally hadn't been being modest or [humblebragging](# "Literal CN: 凡尔赛 (Versailles) — Chinese internet slang for humblebragging or flexing subtly"); it was an objective description. After all, those were genuine deities and True Immortals! While Zhou Hui's combat and spellcasting had appeared effortless—defeating the god with apparent simplicity and capturing a whole flock of immortals in one clean sweep—the spiritual energy he expended could easily equal several millennia of his past consumption.

Yet it was precisely due to such colossal spiritual expenditure that Zhou Hui confirmed his true condition:

It wasn't that spellcasting reduced his spiritual energy requirement—rather, under the influence of the godhead, he could cast spells without using spiritual energy at all!

Indeed! Since when did gods ever require spiritual energy to channel their divine power?!

The reason Zhou Hui had never noticed that his godhead could provide power unconditionally was, ironically, because of his own astronomical spiritual reserves!

Zhou Hui's spiritual energy was not only colossal, but it had also been repeatedly compressed through cultivation into something exceedingly viscous. As a result, it acted like thick glue, cementing the godhead firmly shut.

In comparison, immortal power was like lubricant, greatly beneficial to the operation of the godhead.

Furthermore, after arriving in the Immortal Realm, Zhou Hui discovered that accompanying his "ascension," all of his spirit roots showed a tendency to merge into one, transforming into the legendary "Immortal Root."

Yet a "tendency" was merely a "tendency."

Obtaining a complete Immortal Root was not something accomplished overnight.

An immortal cultivator needed to use this embryonic "Immortal Root" to repeatedly process the spiritual qi in their body, converting spiritual qi into immortal power. Throughout this process, the spirit roots would thoroughly fuse into one, becoming a true Immortal Root, which would then convert all remaining spiritual energy into immortal power in one fell swoop.

At that point, the cultivator would have fully transformed into a True Immortal.

If they subsequently forged a godhead successfully, the accumulated immortal power would establish the might of the godhead far faster and more smoothly.

Given the sheer density of Zhou Hui's spiritual energy, if he could convert it all into immortal power, he could undoubtedly unleash the godhead's maximum power!

Yet Zhou Hui did not do so.

The reason an [involution king](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — king of involution / the ultimate hyper-competitive overachiever who grinds relentlessly") was an involution king was that one couldn't just mindlessly grind without thinking.

If one sensed a change in their power and foolishly dove straight into seclusion for thousands or tens of thousands of years, by the time the Immortal Root was finished refining, their humanity would probably have been completely worn away by time.

Therefore, not only did Zhou Hui refrain from immediately converting his immortal power, but he also isolated his spiritual energy, keeping this newly formed Immortal Root in a vacuum state. He even carved out a separate space in his dantian, storing immortal power and spiritual energy completely apart.

Until he gained a deeper understanding of immortal power, he had no intention of cultivating it rashly.

Finally, Zhou Hui used his spiritual energy to seal the godhead once more.

He had empirically verified the principle of the "Conservation of Energy." Even before dream-transmigrating to the other world and learning the formal theory, Zhou Hui had derived a similar conclusion based on the fundamental laws of the cultivation world. Consequently, Zhou Hui was deeply puzzled by the "unconditional, limitless divine power" produced by the godhead.

He felt that he had to ascertain its source before he could use it with peace of mind.

And just as Zhou Hui was concentrating on regulating his power, he suddenly sensed a gaze upon him:

—"Who's there?!"-Zhou Hui demanded.

Zhou Hui lifted his eyes and swept his gaze across his surroundings, yet found no source for the gaze.

Given the density of Zhou Hui's divine sense coverage, this was an exceedingly rare occurrence.

Very little could spy on Zhou Hui without being detected.

Even the brand on Yuwen Xuan had only avoided detection under the strict condition that it never targeted Zhou Hui directly.

However, Zhou Hui currently had no time to search for the origin of that gaze. For the moment he swept his divine sense outward, he "saw" Villa Master Qingyang flying toward his immortal cave-dwelling, bringing Qu Qianxing along.

Villa Master Qingyang had not come unprompted on a whim; Zhou Hui had specifically invited him.

Qu Qianxing's presence, however, was a slight surprise to Zhou Hui.

These past few days, Qu Qianxing was supposed to be busy [conquering](# "Literal CN: 征服（x） — author's comedic crossed-out mark, implying 'conquering (strike that)'") the Demon Realm!

Or could it be that something had happened?

Zhou Hui mused to himself.

Zhou Hui had originally intended to receive Villa Master Qingyang inside his cave-dwelling, but recalling that bizarre, inexplicable gaze from just now, he couldn't help but frown slightly.

In the end, Zhou Hui waved his hand, sealing his entire cave-dwelling, and changed the location to receive Villa Master Qingyang elsewhere.

---

Qu Qianxing found it a bit strange.

His Master rarely entertained guests outside his cave-dwelling.

Especially in a gazebo halfway up the mountain; it seemed far too informal.

—Except for the foxes and toads that crawled into Zhou Hui's spiritual spring on their own, of course.

Villa Master Qingyang, on the other hand, didn't mind in the slightest.

Compared to the meeting location, he was far more terrified of the questions the Untiring Immortal Venerable was about to ask.

Thus, the moment Villa Master Qingyang met Zhou Hui, he immediately retrieved a vessel:

—"Immortal Venerable, this is the refined True Immortal Blood,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

Indeed, this was why Zhou Hui had summoned Villa Master Qingyang.

Earlier, Zhou Hui and Yuwen Xuan had found a drop of True Immortal Blood at the Thousand Beasts Hall. Because Zhou Hui lacked the research methodologies and experimental techniques for this domain at the time, he had entrusted it to Villa Master Qingyang, a descendant of White Jade Capital, asking him to help research it.

Now that Zhou Hui had ascended once, slain a deity, and captured a whole bunch of True Immortals, he had acquired a solid understanding of the Upper Realm. Since he currently needed to investigate the Immortal Root and immortal power within his own body, he naturally recalled this drop of True Immortal Blood.

He also wanted to use the refinement status of this True Immortal Blood to see what progress the descendant of White Jade Capital had made in this area.

Therefore, Zhou Hui had instructed Villa Master Qingyang to bring the True Immortal Blood over.

In Villa Master Qingyang's eyes, this was practically his [corporate sponsor](# "Literal CN: 金主爸爸（x） — humorous Chinese modern business slang for investor / financial sugar daddy (with strikethrough)") suddenly dropping in to inspect his research progress—one misstep, and all subsequent funding (True Immortal Blood) would go up in smoke.

Consequently, Villa Master Qingyang was trembling with trepidation.

Yet unlike the academic slacker Shen Qingqian, Villa Master Qingyang hailed from a pedigree of academic overachievers; when it came to handling [investors](# "Literal CN: 投资人（x） — venture capital investor (with strikethrough)"), he was exceptionally seasoned.

Rule number one: never surrender the initiative to the other party.

Especially when the other party was a mighty powerhouse whose abilities far exceeded his own and was more than qualified to be his master—allowing someone like that to ask open-ended questions without boundaries was a surefire recipe for suicide!

Thus, Villa Master Qingyang reported with immense enthusiasm:

—"Thanks to your True Immortal Blood, I have verified many ancestral secret arts, my cultivation has improved, and the refinement process has proceeded with extraordinary smoothness! Look at the immortal patterns upon it! They have practically been fully refined out by me! You can also look over my papers—the latest one was published in the previous issue of the magical arts journal. I estimate that with just one final step, the refinement will be completely successful!"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

Standing behind him, Qu Qianxing wore a rather peculiar expression.

Judging by Villa Master Qingyang's confident tone, one would think he could succeed tomorrow. In reality, however, Villa Master Qingyang had been stuck on this "final step" for an entire month.

Although in the cultivation realm it was perfectly normal for the crafting of artifacts or pills to take hundreds or thousands of years, the Qingyang clan's refinement of True Immortal Blood emphasized speed above all else. Supposedly, this was because the immortal power contained within True Immortal Blood was nearly impossible for mortal flesh to constrain; if one was not swift, failure was inevitable.

Therefore, if Villa Master Qingyang kept stalling like this, this drop of True Immortal Blood would soon be ruined.

Yet Villa Master Qingyang was unwilling to lose the opportunity to refine True Immortal Blood. He had repeatedly urged Qu Qianxing not to inform the Untiring Immortal Venerable right away, pleading to "wait a few more days to see the progress first."

As Villa Master Qingyang was technically Qu Qianxing's teacher, Qu Qianxing had naturally agreed.

—Of course, if this matter had posed any real detriment to his Master, Qu Qianxing would have turned around and snitched in a heartbeat.

However, his Master had previously instructed that Villa Master Qingyang was free to research this drop of True Immortal Blood however he wished; even if he destroyed it completely, it didn't matter.

As long as Villa Master Qingyang could publish enough papers before the True Immortal Blood was wasted, that was all that mattered.

As many papers as he could manage.

Yet Qu Qianxing hadn't shared those words with Villa Master Qingyang to reassure him.

Because he had noticed that ever since realizing his refinement might fail, Villa Master Qingyang's productivity had skyrocketed. He no longer spent every day hanging out in the academic lecture hall sipping tea and bragging with peers; instead, he had unleashed all kinds of formidable secret techniques in a desperate bid to salvage his research results.

Qu Qianxing had promptly and obediently kept his mouth shut.

He felt that Master Qingyang's current state of mind was exceptionally productive.

Villa Master Qingyang remained completely oblivious to the fact that he was being exploited.

In fact, he even assumed that with that brat Jian Nuo rushing over, Qu Qianxing's willingness to help Qingyang Qingyu renovate the secret realm was proof of their deepening relationship. However, since Qingyu's secret realm system had reportedly achieved major breakthroughs—Villa Master Qingyang had been so engrossed in refining the True Immortal Blood that he didn't even realize the ground beneath his estate had been drilled straight through—it would be utterly humiliating if he produced zero results himself. Thus, he had proactively accelerated his own [self-juicing grind](# "Literal CN: 榨汁 (zhàzhī) — juicing / extracting every drop of productivity from oneself").

Even without Qu Qianxing's supervision, he had accomplished quite a lot recently. At this moment, he proactively produced a thesis draft and said:

—"This is regarding the final refinement stage. I plan to publish it once the refinement succeeds. Please grant your guidance, Immortal Venerable,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

Zhou Hui accepted the manuscript.

Villa Master Qingyang let out an inward sigh of relief:

[As long as the Untiring Immortal Venerable's attention is diverted to the paper, he surely won't notice that my refinement progress has hit a brick wall!]

Little did he expect that in Zhou Hui's eyes, this petty trick was something he had outgrown ages ago.

Zhou Hui held the manuscript without even glancing at it, cutting straight to the heart of the matter:

—"So, you're stuck?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Ah, oh? Ah..."-Villa Master Qingyang choked out a string of bizarre squawks, looking like a goose that had just been seized by the neck.

Yet even as a descendant of the former Holy Lord of White Jade Capital, he dared not utter a single lie before the current Holy Lord of White Jade Capital.

Villa Master Qingyang's shoulders slumped:

—"...Yes. I can go no further,"-Villa Master Qingyang admitted.

—"Can you explain the specific reason?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Villa Master Qingyang let out a heavy sigh:

—"Immortal Venerable, you have read my previous papers, so you should know that White Jade Capital researched True Immortal Blood in order to obtain the immortal power of a True Immortal with a mortal body without having to ascend,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

This sounded somewhat arrogant, yet it was not impossible.

In fact, there had historically been mortals who were directly brought to the True Immortal Realm and became immortals on the spot. Because they had no spiritual energy conversion process to undergo, they were actually assimilated by the Immortal Realm even faster, condensing an Immortal Root directly and producing pure immortal qi.

The Lower Realm lacked the mist-shrouded immortal environment of the Upper Realm. To skip the cultivation step entirely, the ancient immortals of White Jade Capital had devised a method: utilizing the immortal qi residing within True Immortal Blood.

However, the immortal power in True Immortal Blood was far too potent. For mortals of flesh and blood—or even cultivators who had reforged their physical bodies—it was pure poison. Thus, it had to be repeatedly refined and diluted. This was precisely what Villa Master Qingyang had been working on.

Villa Master Qingyang explained honestly:

—"The initial phase went very smoothly, but the later stage diverged from our ancestral clan records,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.-"A True Immortal's blood is not merely blood. Just as when we cultivate to our current stage where soul and physical body unite as one, a True Immortal's blood contains their soul memories and technique skills. Theoretically, during the refinement process, I should have been able to uncover the origins of this drop of blood for you. Yet this blood behaves like 'soulless blood'—I cannot extract any information whatsoever regarding its original owner..."

—"Perhaps it is due to my limited knowledge, or an error in my technique,"-Villa Master Qingyang continued.-"However, if True Immortal Blood is to be refined to a usable state, this information is indispensable—because not just anyone who ingests True Immortal Blood can endure it. According to the research left behind by White Jade Capital, even if refinement succeeds, only someone most similar in nature to the blood's original owner can ingest it and reap the benefits. Otherwise, even if it doesn't kill them, it will inflict catastrophic damage upon their body."

Indeed! In the Heavenly Dao's original script, his descendant—the daughter of Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo, the "Red Rose"—had ingested True Immortal Blood, ruining her body and rendering her infertile, turning her into [the ultimate sucker](# "Literal CN: 大冤种 (dàyuānzhǒng) — internet slang for a colossal chump / aggrieved patsy taken advantage of by others") who raised other people's children for them.

Inspecting the drop of True Immortal Blood, Zhou Hui remarked:

—"In other words, you have already completed the refinement, but haven't found a suitable test subject."

For some reason, Villa Master Qingyang sensed a faintly unsettling undertone in the Untiring Immortal Venerable's voice.

Villa Master Qingyang attempted to do damage control:

—"No, I am not certain whether the refinement truly succeeded either. Right now, I am gathering blood from various cultivators and refining it to see if any can blend harmoniously with this True Immortal Blood,"-Villa Master Qingyang said.

If he got lucky and hit a match, it would be a resounding success! If not, it was failure.

This was a common occurrence in research.

Yet Villa Master Qingyang also understood that "investors" despised such uncertainty.

Sure enough, Zhou Hui spoke up:

—"Testing them one by one—a single drop of True Immortal Blood isn't nearly enough, is it? But in any case, I never intended to use this blood to empower anyone; I have other uses for it. Since you've finished refining it, I'll take it back then!"-Zhou Hui said.

Villa Master Qingyang struggled to protest:

—"No! I still... Alright,"-Villa Master Qingyang said, his shoulders slumping.

Just as the Untiring Immortal Venerable had said, a single drop of True Immortal Blood was far too small a sample size to gamble on, and it might well be destroyed in the process. It was only natural that the other party wanted to reclaim it.

[What a pity for this paper—looks like it'll be left without a conclusion.]

Villa Master Qingyang thought despairingly.

Right at that moment, Zhou Hui continued:

—"Speaking of which, the issue is simply that your sample size is too small. Villa Master Qingyang, are you still willing to continue?"-Zhou Hui asked.

[You've already taken the True Immortal Blood back, how on earth am I supposed to continue?!]

Villa Master Qingyang subconsciously shook his head:

—"I..."-Villa Master Qingyang began. Then, his eyes bulged wide.

He saw the Untiring Immortal Venerable pat his storage pouch. From within flew out a full ring of transparent glass phials, each containing several drops of True Immortal Blood!

—"I-Immortal Venerable, how did you obtain all these?!"-Villa Master Qingyang stammered in disbelief.

Seeing the Untiring Immortal Venerable smiling without a word, Villa Master Qingyang had no idea what horrifying scenario his own imagination concocted, stammering frantically:

—"If you cannot say, please disregard my question! Surely you couldn't have captured a flock of True Immortals just to bleed them, hahahaha... ha,"-Villa Master Qingyang chuckled nervously.

Yet as Villa Master Qingyang laughed, the joke suddenly stopped being funny.

After all, this was the Immortal Venerable who had literally captured the Nine Heavens Lightning Tribulation. Doing something like that... actually seemed completely in-character for him?!

Zhou Hui did not answer, merely asking:

—"Do you not want them? All this True Immortal Blood?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"No, I want them! I want them all!"-Villa Master Qingyang cried excitedly.

He scooped up the glass phials on the table in one massive embrace, shoving them all into his sleeves in a frenzy:

—"Just you wait for my papers!"-Villa Master Qingyang declared.

Saying so, as if terrified Zhou Hui might change his mind, he sprinted away at top speed.

---

Having mastered the art of self-involution, Villa Master Qingyang swept up the True Immortal Blood and bolted, but Qu Qianxing remained behind.

—"Master,"-Qu Qianxing greeted respectfully.

Seeing his demeanor, Zhou Hui instantly realized that his second disciple had come seeking him for a separate matter, having only bumped into Villa Master Qingyang by chance and accompanied him up.

However, regarding that spatial rift leading to the Demon Realm: no matter how much Zhou Hui trusted Qu Qianxing, when world peace was at stake, he had to ensure those demons wouldn't cause any unforeseen disasters. Thus, he had stationed a strand of divine sense there.

That strand of divine sense had sounded no alarm, so the problem clearly did not lie with the demons.

—"Is it regarding those 'friends' of Jian Nuo?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Master's insight is peerless,"-Qu Qianxing smiled wryly.-"Among those 'friends,' for some reason, I feel as if I have met an 'old acquaintance,'"-Qu Qianxing said, practically grinding his teeth on the words 'old acquaintance.'-"However, the refined golden lightning I planted on him showed no reaction. I observed him for several days, and he doesn't seem to show any abnormalities... and yet, that sickening discomfort refuses to dissipate."

This was why Qu Qianxing had been hesitating and beating around the bush.

He hadn't caught the person's [fox tail](# "Literal CN: 狐狸尾巴 (húli wěiba) — telltale clue / hidden sinister motive"). By Qu Qianxing's nature, even if the person hadn't caused trouble yet, he would have preferred to preemptively neutralize and control them. But his Master was at home, and the target was not a Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple; if his Master found out, it would look bad.

Yet Qu Qianxing felt that leaving the matter unattended was out of the question, leaving him torn over whether to seek Master's permission.

Furthermore, coming to Master over such a "trivial matter" felt distinctly childish.

That was why Qu Qianxing had been hesitating the entire way. He had even stopped by to check on Villa Master Qingyang's experimental progress without reaching a resolution, only to be dragged along when Villa Master Qingyang arrived for his scheduled appointment with Master.

[I suppose this counts as 'fate'!]

Qu Qianxing consoled himself.

Fortunately, even if it was merely a "gut feeling," his Master would never treat it lightly.

—"It makes you feel uncomfortable? Then there is indeed something amiss,"-Zhou Hui mused.-"Where is he right now?"

—"In Safe Room Ding,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"Supposedly due to a frail constitution, he is unsuited to contact with demonic qi, so he has stayed obediently inside these past few days."-Because of that, however, Qu Qianxing found him all the more suspicious.

—"Room Ding, is it?"-Zhou Hui tapped the tabletop.

The stone tabletop instantly rippled like the surface of water. Within the rippling reflection, a pale, slender youth sat serenely inside an enclosed chamber, quietly reading a book like a bird trapped in a cage.

—"...He appears entirely normal,"-Zhou Hui remarked after inspecting him thoroughly.

Qu Qianxing gave a bitter smile:

—"Even Master finds nothing wrong? Then it seems I was overthinking things,"-Qu Qianxing murmured.

[—"Look with your godhead."](# "Speaker: Mysterious Voice")

Right then, a voice rang out beside Zhou Hui's ear, offering a reminder.

Just like the gaze from earlier, this voice arrived abruptly and inexplicably. Zhou Hui instantly swept his divine sense across a ten-thousand-li radius, yet found nothing suspicious.

Sensing Zhou Hui's divine sense, Qu Qianxing called out in confusion:

—"Master?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

Zhou Hui glanced at Qu Qianxing.

Frankly speaking, although the voice and gaze had appeared without warning from an unknown origin, Zhou Hui inexplicably sensed no malice from them. He was far more concerned about his disciple's condition.

[Look with the godhead?]

Thinking this, Zhou Hui withdrew the spiritual energy sealing his godhead and tapped the table once more.

The imagery upon the table instantly transformed.

The serene, peaceful youth had vanished. In his place was a human specimen whose internal organs had been completely hollowed out, eyes glassy and dull. And a clump of... some horrifying monstrosity beyond description was nestled inside that cadaverous vessel.

It was drenched in gore, blood dripping and splashing everywhere. Several intestine-like, [pixelated tendrils](# "Literal CN: 马赛克物体 (mǎsàikè wùtǐ) — mosaic/pixelated censored objects, comedic authorial description of cosmic horror") dangled downward from it, sprawling outward like puppet strings.

—"'An immortal'?!"-Qu Qianxing's gorgeous face twisted in horror.-"How could it be *that* thing?!"

Zhou Hui fell silent, his brows furrowing as he stared at that grotesque abomination.

Upon that monstrosity, he saw a peculiar brand—identical to the one on Yuwen Xuan, identical to the one on Mengshan Old Freak, and identical even to the "divine flame" he had captured previously. Yet in the past, those marks had appeared to be entirely different entities.

[Is this something that can only be perceived through a 'godhead'?]

[...Still, even that 'god' was slain by me. How can it still be stirring up trouble?]

Thinking this, Zhou Hui shifted the viewing angle.

—"Master?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Do not worry, it will be dealt with,"-Zhou Hui replied.-"I am more interested in where those intestines lead."-Zhou Hui was referring to the things sprawling outward from the grotesque entity.

—"Indeed. Leaving severed threads outside would be messy,"-Qu Qianxing agreed with ferocious ruthlessness.

Zhou Hui glanced at his second disciple, tracing the path of those intestines as he shifted the viewing perspective—and located a pack of unlucky bastards currently plotting to kidnap Qingyang Qingyu.
