# Chapter 10: Secretly... (第10章 偷偷的……)

[—"Ah, Eldest Senior Brother has been hung up at the mountain gate again."](# "Speaker: Mountain Spirit")

[—"How did he provoke the Immortal Venerable to anger this time?"](# "Speaker: Mountain Spirit")

[—"Isn't this a bit too fast? The Immortal Venerable only crossed his heavenly tribulation a few days ago; I thought he'd let him run loose for quite a while longer!"](# "Speaker: Mountain Spirit")

Across the valleys of Mount Tianling, the trees rustled softly in the wind, their leaves blending into a chorus of hushed whispers.

At the entrance to this valley stood a colossal peach tree. It had grown for tens of thousands of years, having long since surpassed the ordinary height and girth of any standard peach wood. It blossomed once every three hundred years and bore fruit once every six hundred years.

In ordinary years when it neither flowered nor fruited, it appeared merely as an excessively gargantuan withered tree. Consequently, few people ever realized that this ancient peach tree was actually the grand mountain gate of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

On this particular day, however, dangling from one of the peach tree's thick branches was a young man—or at least, someone who outwardly appeared to be a young man.

The young man possessed a handsome face, with sharp sword-brows and bright starry eyes, along with a pair of long, straight legs. He was the kind of fine young gentleman who would elicit involuntary praise from anyone who laid eyes upon him, regardless of gender or age.

Unfortunately, no matter how dashing a man might be, having his cervical spine twisted at a crisp ninety-degree angle rendered him utterly horrifying to behold. To say nothing of the fact that he was dangling from the branch of an ancient peach tree resembling a dead trunk, making the scene all the more macabre.

Had an outsider stumbled into this area by mistake, they would undoubtedly have mistaken Mount Tianling for an ominous land of pure malevolence.

Within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, however, everyone had long grown accustomed to this sight.

Only the spirits and menial servants who were exempt from examinations still gossiped about the underlying cause. The formal disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect didn't even spare it a second glance, pacing hurried footsteps back and forth between the Library Pavilion and their dormitories.

They still had an exam to prepare for!

Furthermore, if they did poorly on the exam, who knew if they wouldn't be the next ones [hanging from the mountain gate](# "Literal CN: 挂山门 (guà shānmén) — traditional martial punishment of stringing an errant disciple from the sect gate")!

—There was no secret under heaven that could stay concealed forever. Quite a few people had already caught wind that the one who scored dead last on the previous exam was none other than their Eldest Senior Brother. Although no one knew for certain whether Eldest Senior Brother's [punitive suspension from the mountain gate](# "Literal CN: 挂山门 (guà shānmén) — traditional martial punishment of stringing an errant disciple from the sect gate") was related to his abysmal grades, working that much harder was guaranteed never to be a mistake.

Precisely for this reason, when Gan Tang saw Xiang Rong, she was extraordinarily surprised.

—"The... [punitive copies](# "Literal CN: penal copying (罚抄) — assigning lines or copying mistakes as academic punishment") of the exam papers?"-Gan Tang asked.

The test papers she had obtained back then hadn't all been purchased; she had also helped copy a portion of the assigned penalty lines. Gan Tang had finished copying them long ago, but after being interrupted by her Foundation Establishment breakthrough and the announcement of the Grand Unified Examination, she had completely forgotten to hand them over to Xiang Rong.

Xiang Rong had never come to ask for them either, leading Gan Tang to assume they were no longer needed!

Gan Tang instinctively glanced in the direction of the mountain gate:

—"Are they still needed now?"-Gan Tang asked.

—"Yes, absolutely needed, absolutely!"-Xiang Rong said with unprecedented politeness.-"If you feel the price isn't right, I can refund the spirit stones for those papers to you."

His sudden politeness toward Gan Tang was not because she had advanced to Foundation Establishment—Xiang Rong regularly dealt with disciples across all kinds of cultivation realms and didn't care in the slightest about Gan Tang's minor milestone. Rather, it was because Gan Tang had [registered on the ledger](# "Literal CN: 挂了号 (guàle hào) — registered on the radar / gained the personal attention of someone in power") with Untiring Immortal Venerable himself.

Zhou Hui obviously knew about Gan Tang and had even implicitly permitted her actions. If anything were to happen to Gan Tang now, both he and Mo Xi would be finished. How could Xiang Rong dare to be anything less than courteous?

Furthermore, Xiang Rong had his own little calculating scheme:

—"In fact, I could even refund all your money in full,"-Xiang Rong wheedled.-"Could you let me copy your answers, pretty please?"

—"...I'd rather not sell my answers,"-Gan Tang said hesitantly.

As the origin of the rumor regarding the 'Exam Paper Heavenly Secret Theory,' countless disciples had approached Gan Tang previously attempting to buy her papers. Gan Tang had turned every single one away. Beyond her confidentiality agreement with Xiang Rong regarding the contents of the test papers, she also didn't believe herself capable of instructing others; avoiding misleading them was already fortunate enough.

She didn't wish to sell them to Xiang Rong either, so she advised him:

—"You've handled so many exam papers, so you should have looked at the contents by now, right?"-Gan Tang advised.-"No two questions formulated by the Immortal Venerable are ever identical. If this is purely for the sect-wide examination, I think past exam papers can only serve as reference for question formats at best; there definitely won't be repeat questions."

—"No selling, no selling! I just want to [leech some of your karmic luck](# "Literal CN: 蹭气运 (cèng qìyùn) — gaming/cultivation slang for borrowing or rubbing off on someone else's lucky aura"),"-Xiang Rong said, rubbing his hands together with a flattering grin.

[—"My karmic luck?"](# "Speaker: Gan Tang")

—"Exactly! The moment you got the papers, you successfully broke through to Foundation Establishment, so your karmic luck is clearly exceptional,"-Xiang Rong said.-"If I rub off a bit of that luck, maybe I can achieve a decent score on the Grand Unified Examination as well."

—"I really don't think this has anything to do with luck,"-Gan Tang said awkwardly.

Still, with Xiang Rong pleading to such an extent, she ultimately handed over her test papers. After all, Gan Tang remained genuinely grateful to him. Although those exam papers had nearly emptied her purse back then, they had genuinely helped her reach Foundation Establishment. Besides, compared to the current market price of exam papers, the fee back then had practically been giving them away for free.

Xiang Rong was overjoyed when Gan Tang handed over the papers.

He glanced through the contents of the exam papers, carefully tucked them away into his storage pouch, bid Gan Tang farewell, and stepped outside. True to his word and just as he had promised Gan Tang, he did not sell the papers to anyone. Instead, he made his way toward the Inner Sect.

However, Xiang Rong did not do as he had done in the past, strutting boldly inside as though he were an Inner Sect disciple himself simply because his sister was stationed there.

He made a cautious detour and, like a [lecherous scoundrel](# "Literal CN: 登徒子 (Dēngtúzǐ) — traditional literary archetype of a lascivious rake or wall-scaling peeping Tom") scaling a wall, sneaked along a secluded path and climbed right into his sister's boudoir.

As misfortune would have it, this exact scene was witnessed by the person they dreaded most—Mo Xi.

* * *

—"What on earth is that fellow up to?"-Mo Xi wondered, feeling a flicker of petty excitement at having caught his subordinate red-handed.

Originally, Mo Xi had only come out here to take a breather.

Lately, in his eagerness to prove himself before Master, he had accepted far too many quests, resulting in crushing pressure. Yet even more obnoxious than the quests themselves was the fact that the vicinity of the Palace of Silence, which he was tasked with renovating, happened to be one of the areas with the thickest academic atmosphere in the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect, second only to the Library Pavilion.

Stepping into the Palace of Silence now, no matter how secluded a corner one ducked into, one would find people reciting texts, people solving problem sets, and people frantically scribbling copies.

In all his years since joining the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mo Xi had never seen the disciples study with such ferocity. It was as if they were all scheduled to face their heavenly lightning tribulations tomorrow!

[Master never even promised any rewards for scoring well on the exam. Is it really necessary to grind this hard?!]

Mo Xi grumbled inwardly.

Given his personality of [refusing to rise early without personal profit](# "Literal CN: 无利不起早 (wú lì bù qǐ zǎo) — never doing anything unless there is personal gain to be had"), he naturally regarded this sort of effort as the most pointless waste of time imaginable.

However, human beings were easily swayed by their surroundings. Mo Xi had certainly not cultivated his mental fortitude to the level where he was completely impervious to external distractions. Whenever he raised his eyes and saw a swarm of people grinding away while he alone wasn't studying, he felt bizarrely out of place.

What was worse, Mo Xi couldn't even stop or chase them away.

—Setting aside the fact that Qu Qianxing would never permit it, the very test papers and practice problems clutched in those disciples' hands had been distributed by the Xiang siblings under Mo Xi's own instructions. He could hardly go and sabotage his own business.

Trapped within that eerie atmosphere, Mo Xi finally reached his breaking point. He shoved a few miscellaneous chores onto other disciples and slipped away to Xiang Yi's quarters, intending to look over the account ledgers to soothe his frayed nerves—for while the System existed, having a physical ledger in his hands always felt far more reassuring.

Yet before he could even catch a glimpse of the ledger, Mo Xi had spotted Xiang Rong's suspicious, skulking behavior.

Mo Xi's curiosity was instantly piqued.

It wasn't that he cared about maintaining the sect's moral discipline—if anything, he felt that particular sect rule was overly meddlesome. But as a self-styled mastermind who loved pulling sly stunts himself, like repelled like. Mo Xi vehemently detested anyone pulling tricks behind his back, let alone the Xiang siblings whom he viewed as his subordinates.

Prompted by even more mischievous schemes, however, Mo Xi did not call out to stop Xiang Rong immediately. Instead, he activated several magical treasures that concealed his breath and hid his form, tailing quietly behind Xiang Rong's steps.

Entirely oblivious, Xiang Rong deftly hopped over the windowsill of his sister's boudoir with practiced familiarity.

Xiang Yi had been waiting inside the room for a while, furtively occupied with who-knew-what. At the sound of the disturbance, she instinctively shoved whatever was in her hands out of sight. Turning her head and seeing her twin brother, she let out an audible sigh of relief.

—"Did you get your hands on them?"-Xiang Yi asked, lowering her voice.

—"Got them,"-Xiang Rong said in the same hushed whisper.-"All the answer sheets from the most outstanding disciples in the Outer Sect are right here. But I still recommend Gan Tang's the most. There's good reason she managed to break through to Foundation Establishment! Her papers are actually easier to understand than those of several Direct Disciples, because she didn't just summarize the question archetypes—she even categorized them."

Saying so, Xiang Rong retrieved a thick stack of test papers from his storage pouch, then asked Xiang Yi:

—"What about your side?"-Xiang Rong asked.

—"Not bad. I managed to get Senior Brother Guan's test papers, which are strictly forbidden from being sold outside,"-Xiang Yi said.-"Although Senior Brother Guan's cultivation realm isn't particularly high among the Direct Disciples, his test papers are the easiest to understand. The notes he copied down even include relevant background knowledge that isn't found in any standard manuals."

—"Splendid work,"-Xiang Rong said, barely suppressing his excitement.

Outside the window, Mo Xi wore an expression of smug revelation:

[Ha! These two really are secretly scalping exam papers behind my back!]

Although Mo Xi had authorized the Xiang siblings to sell the papers, his commission cut was notoriously vicious. In particular, because he was eager to prove himself before Master lately, he had accepted the quest to renovate the Palace of Silence without receiving any funding from Qu Qianxing. Consequently, he had poured every last spirit stone earned from scalping exam papers straight into the renovations, leaving virtually zero profit for the Xiang siblings.

What was more, Mo Xi practiced blatant price discrimination between the Outer and Inner Sects.

Calibrating against the spending power of the two demographics, he had produced several tiers of exam compilations, ruthlessly squeezing every last drop of spirit stones from the pockets of Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples.

However, the most terrible thing about Mo Xi was that he was acutely aware of how unscrupulous his actions were.

Because of this, Mo Xi didn't trust his two subordinates in the slightest, suspecting that under his relentless exploitation, they would inevitably violate his directives to hoard private slush funds.

Now, the secretive antics of the Xiang siblings appeared to confirm Mo Xi's suspicions.

[Should I give them a little warning?]

While Mo Xi was turning over the thought in his mind, his attention was abruptly arrested by what the pair inside the room were doing next.

Having swapped their off-the-books exam papers, the Xiang siblings did not begin duplicating, collating, or pricing them like standard 'merchandise.' Instead, they began scrutinizing them with rapt attention.

They even began conversing in hushed, earnest murmurs:

—"Perfect. Let's go over the problems we couldn't solve earlier first!"-Xiang Rong said.-"Then we'll check the similar question archetypes on Senior Brother Guan's and Gan Tang's papers."

—"Alright,"-Xiang Yi nodded, retrieving the item she had hidden away moments ago.

It was unmistakably a handmade little notebook. Covered in densely copied script, its meticulous craftsmanship rivaled even the precious ledgers Xiang Yi normally hoarded like treasure. Except what filled its pages were not figures and balances, but miscellaneous subjects she previously never cared about—knowledge that 'couldn't earn a single spirit stone.'

Right now, however, Xiang Yi cradled the notebook in her hands, quizzing Xiang Rong with utmost seriousness:

—"Which of the following demon beasts frequently appears along the shoals near the Abyssal Ocean? A, Bo Fish; B, Shen Demon; C, Heluo; D, Gu Diao?"-Xiang Yi asked.

[—"Gu Diao."](# "Speaker: Xiang Rong")

[—"What classification of demonic creature does the Gu Diao belong to?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Yi")

—"A bird... No, wait, it lives in the water, right? I remember getting this exact question wrong last time,"-Xiang Rong muttered with a furrowed brow.-"I guess... a fish?"

[—"Are you sure?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Yi")

—"Let me think some more,"-Xiang Rong held up a hand, his entire face scrunching into a knot.

His expression was not at all like someone pondering a mythical beast that had virtually zero bearing on his daily life, but rather as deadly serious as if this creature were about to bestow a massive fortune upon him in the next instant.

Watching this unfold, Mo Xi couldn't help but be struck dumb with shock:

[Wait... did I misunderstand?! These two aren't cooking the books—they're actually...]

—"You two are actually... [secretly studying](# "Literal CN: 偷偷的学习 — classic Chinese high school trope of classmates pretending to slack off while secretly pulling all-nighters to study")?!"-Mo Xi roared, unable to contain himself as he smashed straight through the window.
