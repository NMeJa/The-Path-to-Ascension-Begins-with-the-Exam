# Chapter 115: [A Top Powerhouse's Playbook](<# "Literal CN: 大佬的操作 (dàlǎo de cāozuò) — A Big Boss / Top Powerhouse's Playbook / Flextastic Moves; chapter title referring to Zhou Hui casually capturing, milking, and chasing away the Ascension Heavenly Tribulation like helpless prey">) (第115章 大佬的操作)

[—"What on earth happened?! Why did the weather suddenly change?"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

[—"Th-Thunder, a lightning tribulation?"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

[—"Why would there be a lightning tribulation?!"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

Confronted by a sky abruptly enveloped in thunderclouds, even the Mahayana cultivators fell into a state of utter disarray, to say nothing of the low-rank disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. At this very moment, like small critters before an impending earthquake, they scuttled about in every direction.

And one could hardly blame them.

Although they had already witnessed the Kunpeng and the True Dragon, witnessed the bazaar and academy on the seabed of the Abyss Sea, witnessed Integration and Mahayana powerhouses [ripping each other's hair out](<# "Literal CN: 扯头花 (chě tóuhuā) — pulling hair / ripping out hair flowers; humorous slang for petty catfights or undignified brawls among prestigious figures">) in the academic lecture hall, witnessed a sect and secret realm built with its own System, and witnessed their Eldest Senior Brother frequently suspended from the mountain gate tree (X)—ranking them as exceptionally well-traveled and experienced among disciples of their generation—up to this point, they had never once witnessed heavenly thunder or a lightning tribulation.

However, that only applied to the disciples who had joined the Heavenly Spirit Sect just last year.

The senior students of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, gazing at the thunderclouds rolling across the sky, actually found the composure to casually critique the spectacle:

[—"It finally arrived this year, huh!"](<# "Speaker: Senior Disciple">)

[—"Feels like it's not quite as exaggerated as last year's."](<# "Speaker: Senior Disciple">)

[—"The color looks a bit different too. It probably isn't an ascension this year, right?"](<# "Speaker: Senior Disciple">)

[—"We're in the middle of hosting the Immortal Arts Assembly right now! There's no way the Lord Immortal Venerable would ascend at a time like this!"](<# "Speaker: Senior Disciple">)

[—"Then what is it now?"](<# "Speaker: Senior Disciple">)

—"Who cares what it is?! You guys still have time to stand around shooting the breeze when junior brothers and sisters are scuttling all over the ground?!"-someone barked at the top of his lungs, sprinting past them upon overhearing their idle chatter.

The group jumped in fright.

One of them, slightly more quick-witted, promptly summoned the System quest panel—in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, students in the Intermediate Class and above could unlock the quest panel to view sect quests in real time—and discovered an extra time-limited public quest:

> **【(Emergency) Calm the bewildered disciples and escort them to a safe location to shelter from the lightning tribulation.】**

This quest even unlocked the function to view other disciples' locations on the map.

The disciple immediately activated the tracking function, only to blurt out a curse at the sea of red dots swarming across the entire map:

—"[Holy crap!](<# "Literal CN: 窝草 (wō cǎo) — playful phonetic homophone for 我操 / holy shit / WTF">) Are these brats a bunch of [little rabbits](<# "Literal CN: 兔崽子 (tùzǎizi) — little rabbits / young rascals / little brats">)? It's just a bit of thunder—did they really need to panic and scatter all over the place like that?!"

—"Hey, listen to you talking—did you forget your own first year at the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-a [sarcastic friend](<# "Literal CN: 损友 (sǔnyǒu) — damaging friend; a close friend who constantly roasts and exposes your shortcomings">) beside him unceremoniously roasted him, exposing his past shortcomings.

—"That was..."-The disciple choked for a second.

However, glancing at the quest light screen, he tried to [salvage some dignity](<# "Literal CN: 挽尊 (wǎnzūn) — salvaging face / preserving one's dignity after being humiliated">):

—"I never troubled any senior brothers or senior sisters back then! Nor did we have such a convenient quest tablet! How could I have been as fragile as them?!"

Yet several of his companions had already soared into the sky on their flying swords.

Having no time to argue over "which batch was the absolute worst," he hastily summoned his own flying sword as well, flew up into the airspace, drew a long flute from his wide sleeve, and pressed it to his lips.

The very next instant, the long flute unleashed an ear-splitting, excruciating din.

No, that was hardly a sound at all—it was a direct assault on the eardrums, completely drowning out even the intermittent booms of thunder in the heavens, leaving only raw, throbbing agony behind.

This "flute music" immediately attracted the bewildered gaze of several visiting cultivators.

[—"What is that? An attack amidst the chaos?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Does the Heavenly Spirit Sect intend to strike at us?!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

The visiting cultivators questioned in complete confusion.

Fortunately, they did not rashly make a move, as they noticed that the low-rank disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were also suffering from this "indiscriminate attack." Instead of fleeing or counterattacking, however, the young disciples gathered together like a flock of sheep.

What the visiting cultivators did not know was that this was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's emergency assembly signal.

Although under ordinary circumstances, the Heavenly Spirit Sect relied on the chimes of the [Sundial Clock](<# "Literal CN: 日晷钟 (rìguǐ zhōng) — Sundial Clock / Sundial Bell; the sect's central timekeeping instrument dividing hours into minutes">) echoing throughout the mountains to summon disciples, in extraordinary situations—such as the arrival of a lightning tribulation, where spiritual energy turbulence rendered the Sundial Clock unusable—high-rank disciples would step in to rally them instead. Naturally, a normal assembly signal was by no means this horrific to listen to, but this particular senior was an exception.

This was also part of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's "emergency preparedness drills."

Considering that every single reincarnation cycle featured a scheduled "assault by the Child of Destiny," Zhou Hui felt that even if destiny could not be overturned, he had to at least grant ordinary disciples a chance to escape. Thus, he regularly scheduled these emergency evacuation drills across the Heavenly Spirit Sect (under the guise of "resisting foreign invaders"). Though these disciples had never had the opportunity to practice in a real crisis and thus panicked slightly upon meeting heavenly thunder, the moment they heard their senior brother's ghastly "flute playing," their muscle memory finally kicked in.

Some of them even grew excited.

They hailed the flute-playing senior brother:

—"Senior Brother Fan, are enemies invading? Should we head for the Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation?"

—"Protect your head! Are you blind?! This is clearly a heavenly tribulation!"-That "Senior Brother Fan" cursed at them.-"An Ascension Lightning Tribulation!"

[—"Ascension?"](<# "Speakers: Junior Disciples">)

The little disciples' eyes went wide.

It took them half a minute to fully process it, yet they remained somewhat bewildered:

—"Could it be that the Lord Immortal Venerable is about to ascend?"

Theoretically, this was a cause for supreme celebration, wasn't it?

In any other sect, if their founding ancestor in the Tribulation Transcendence stage were about to undergo a lightning tribulation, it was a momentous joyous occasion that would be celebrated for three years beforehand and fifty years afterward.

In the Heavenly Spirit Sect, however, things were a bit peculiar. While the disciples held boundless faith in their direct disciples, even Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan's Soul Formation cultivation was slightly low on the grand stage, while Qu Qianxing, who actually managed the sect's affairs, was merely at the Golden Core stage. If the Lord Immortal Venerable were no longer around, everyone couldn't help feeling a tinge of anxiety—not to mention that a substantial portion of this batch of disciples had joined specifically for the sake of the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Evidently, however, their worries were entirely superfluous.

—"What on earth are you daydreaming about?!"-Senior Brother Fan scolded.-"The Immortal Arts Assembly is still going on. With the Immortal Venerable's temperament, do you really think he would just abandon you all to your own devices? Besides, we haven't even taken this semester's final examinations yet."

The young disciples' faces instantly darkened.

They had only just finished their midterms; they hadn't the slightest desire to even think about finals.

Though speaking of examinations, every single one of them suddenly wished the Immortal Venerable could ascend smoothly!

Alas, that remained pure wishful thinking.

—"This is merely a routine [preview](<# "Literal CN: 预习 (yùxí) — preview / pre-study / practice run; academic slang for reading textbook material before class, here used for triggering a practice heavenly tribulation">),"-Senior Brother Fan explained.-"Every few years, the Lord Immortal Venerable triggers a heavenly tribulation to repeatedly experiment on how to ascend."

—"You can preview a tribulation?!"-This time, a visiting cultivator couldn't hold back from blurting out.

In the cultivation world, lightning tribulations descended strictly according to a cultivator's cultivation progress.

While some almighty figures had premonitions prior to their breakthroughs and suppressed their cultivation to choose an opportune moment to face the tribulation, no one had ever heard of someone undergoing repeated tribulations over and over again.

Of course, the Tribulation Transcendence realm was perhaps a slight exception.

When a cultivator attained Tribulation Transcendence, it signified that their Grand Dao was complete. Like an overflowing pitcher of water, their spiritual energy surpassed the threshold the mortal world could contain; thus, they could take the initiative to trigger a lightning tribulation and ascend to the True Immortal Realm.

Yet precisely because of this, Tribulation Transcendence cultivators were all the more cautious.

After all, a Tribulation Transcendence thundercloud was no laughing matter. It was the Nine Heavens Thunder capable of tearing through spatial fabric. At its pinnacle, it could blast even a True Immortal to death. Even if one survived, taking a mere stray spark could strip away half of one's cultivation. And if one barely weathered the storm only to find oneself completely depleted of spiritual energy the moment the celestial path opened, powerless to fly up—what kind of horrifying dark comedy would that be?

Therefore, unless Tribulation Transcendence almighty figures had made absolute, foolproof preparations, they would never dare summon a lightning tribulation.

Moreover, they essentially had only one shot. Starting over was an ordeal infinitely more brutal than succeeding on the first try.

—"Well, our Immortal Venerable can!"-Senior Brother Fan declared with immense pride.-"You should have seen last year's heavenly thunder—it blasted the Ascension Terrace into smithereens, yet our Immortal Venerable was completely unharmed."

Senior Brother Fan then turned to his junior disciples and continued:

—"When you paid respects at the mountain gates, you all witnessed Senior Brother Qu's lightning, didn't you? I heard his Golden Lightning was refined from the remnant thunder left over from the Lord Immortal Venerable's tribulation. That's why he can cross realms to strike down cultivators of far higher cultivation, and it even possesses the divine might to traverse through space."

Hearing this, the little disciples revealed expressions of reverent awe.

Involuntarily, they recalled the awe-inspiring might they had witnessed on the day they bowed before the mountain gates.

Among them, a disciple with a Lightning Spirit Root couldn't help asking:

—"How can we collect the Lord Immortal Venerable's remnant thunder?"

—"Wait until you reach Senior Brother Qu's level!"-Senior Brother Fan shot him a glare.

The disciple stuck out his tongue.

A godlike figure like Senior Brother Qu—how could an ordinary mortal like him ever hope to imitate him?

At that moment, as though affirming this reality, a deafening crash of heavenly thunder detonated across the firmament, distinctly closer to their position than before.

—"Alright, stop standing around like statues! Hurry up and follow me to the Library Pavilion!"-Senior Brother Fan barked, as if suddenly remembering his duty.-"This heavenly thunder won't do a thing to the Lord Immortal Venerable, but blasting you or me to cinders is child's play! Nobody run around outside right now!"

Saying so, he led his junior brothers and sisters flying toward the Library Pavilion, not forgetting to call out to the visiting cultivators:

—"You folks come along too! Our Library Pavilion was constructed using materials from the Ascension Terrace—it's more than sturdy enough to defend against heavenly thunder!"

—"...Wait, didn't you just say the Ascension Terrace got blasted to smithereens by heavenly thunder?"

—"Yeah. If it hadn't been blasted to smithereens, why would it have been repurposed to build the Library Pavilion?"

[No, what I mean is... can materials from an Ascension Terrace that was already blasted to smithereens actually protect us?!]

The visiting cultivator thought with dread.

However, hearing the thunder claps detonating directly above his head, he dared not linger outside for another second and hurriedly scrambled after the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples.

*

—"During the heavenly tribulation, we kindly request that all seniors refrain from leaving the Palace of Silence."

—"Our Palace of Silence was constructed using [Thousand-Species Jade](<# "Literal CN: 千种玉 (qiānzhǒng yù) — Thousand-Species Jade / Myriad-Species Jade; supreme structural jade used for the Ascension Terrace and Buyan Palace, capable of withstanding heavenly lightning">) as its primary building material; it is more than sufficient to withstand the lightning tribulation."

Meanwhile, in the Academic Lecture Hall of the Palace of Silence, Qu Qianxing was soothing the crowd.

Compared to his strict demeanour toward his own disciples, his tone was naturally far more tactful toward these Integration and Void Refinement almighty figures—of course, should any powerhouse dare act out of line, Qu Qianxing would not hesitate to activate the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation to teach them the meaning of "politeness."

Fortunately, there were no such reckless individuals present.

High-rank cultivators were all the more acutely aware of just how terrifying a tribulation thunderbolt was. Never mind causing trouble; brushing against even a single stray spark meant eternal damnation.

Certain cultivators among them felt a pang of bitter regret, lamenting that they never should have come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the first place, only to end up embroiled in a lightning tribulation.

Yet at least until the heavenly thunder ceased, none of them would dare voice their grievances aloud.

Instead, a cultivator exclaimed in surprise:

—"Thousand-Species Jade? Isn't that the material for the Ascension Terrace?"

—"Indeed,"-Qu Qianxing nodded.

He did not disclose any superfluous information to them.

Nevertheless, upon hearing that the Palace of Silence had been forged with materials from the Ascension Terrace, quite a few people revealed visibly relieved expressions.

After all, as an indispensable architectural construct for cultivators confronting ascension tribulations, the structural hardness of an Ascension Terrace was universally acknowledged to withstand the vast majority of heavenly tribulations. (Pity these cultivators had no idea that Zhou Hui's heavenly tribulations were perennially anything but ordinary—never mind the tribulation that shattered the Ascension Terrace last year, there was once a tribulation that shattered the entire cultivation realm!) Thus, they felt their safety had at least some guarantee.

Someone also remarked with deep regret:

—"Yet why did the Lord Immortal Venerable not use Thousand-Species Jade to build an Ascension Terrace? If an Ascension Terrace were present, wouldn't the Lord Immortal Venerable's chances of ascending this time be substantially higher?"

[Because this opportunity for ascension isn't what Master wants!]

Qu Qianxing thought to himself.

He had not the slightest doubt that his master's ascension would be achieved under harmonious cosmic alignment—at the opportune time, favorable terrain, and unified will—rather than an ascension handed down like some charitable reward upon completing a karmic merit quest.

[Who is worthy of bestowing a reward upon Master?!]

Qu Qianxing thought with unabashed arrogance.

Yet even with complete confidence, Qu Qianxing, just as he had during every past heavenly tribulation, gazed up at the sky with anxious vigilance, hoping that Master would once again achieve his objective this time around.

*

Beneath the watchful eyes of all, Zhou Hui remained completely composed, his expression even bordering on indifferent.

—"What a beautiful cloud,"-Zhou Hui said, sizing up the gathering thunderclouds with subtle mockery.

Across seven entire reincarnations, Zhou Hui had continuously attempted various methods to ascend. Consequently, he had researched every conceivable morphology of heavenly tribulation, to the extent that merely by observing the color and shape of the thunderclouds, he could gauge the probability of an ascension's success.

For instance, this particular heavenly tribulation was more suited for ascension than any prior occasion.

It was even more opportune than the very first attempt he had abandoned by his own choice.

This stemmed from the fundamental mechanics of heavenly tribulations.

It was generally believed that heavenly thunder descended from the True Immortal Realm high above the Nine Heavens.

After reaching the Nascent Soul realm, a cultivator encountered a heavenly tribulation at every breakthrough.

However, tribulations prior to the Mahayana stage were primarily intended to temper the physical body, burning away impurities to evolve the mortal form toward an immortal physique. Throughout this process, the body established a metaphysical link with heavenly thunder, functioning akin to a tracking beacon.

Upon reaching Mahayana, the immortal physique was virtually complete; at this stage, entering Tribulation Transcendence allowed one to trigger the ascension thunder.

This thunder was essentially a space-tearing force designed to carve out a passage leading to the True Immortal Realm.

Based on past records, the heavenly thunder locked onto the tribulation cultivator and ripped through spatial fabric downwards. Thus, as a matter of course, those bolts of heavenly lightning would inevitably strike the tribulation-taker until the passage was completely opened.

The mightier the cultivator, the sturdier the passage required, and the more heavenly thunder needed to blast open the gateway.

Following this theory, the greater the power of the heavenly thunder and the larger its volume, the better—because the resulting ascension passage would be far more solid, ensuring the ascension process remained free from spatial interference:

—Provided, of course, that the cultivator could withstand those lightning tribulations themselves and not get blasted until their spiritual energy ran dry.

When researching historical records, Zhou Hui had read an account of a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator who endured the entire barrage of thunder, only to run completely out of spiritual energy at the final moment. He could only watch helplessly as the celestial passage swung wide while lacking the strength to fly upward—ultimately resulting in his spirit pet seizing the opportunity to ascend instead.

Regrettably, this historical case was of little practical relevance to Zhou Hui.

Because Zhou Hui had never once encountered a situation where his spiritual energy ran dry.

Even that heavenly thunder that cleaved the entire world in two had occurred because, from the very beginning, it aimed to shatter the world rather than construct a passage—it was a thunder of annihilation, not a thunder of creation. Even Zhou Hui had never anticipated encountering such an apocalyptic cataclysm, falling victim to it purely due to a momentary lapse of oversight.

Given a second chance, Zhou Hui would never grant it such an opportunity again.

However, "those above" currently showed no intention of repeating that catastrophic tribulation. The heavenly thunder they dispensed this time was exceedingly merciful; judging from the thunderclouds, it almost carried an air of [guaranteed admission](<# "Literal CN: 保送 (bǎosòng) — academic recommendation or guaranteed direct track without taking exams; here meaning the heavens are practically handing Zhou Hui a free, effortless ascension pass">), faintly manifesting the aura of auspicious five-colored clouds.

Zhou Hui knew that this kind of iridescent cloud possessed spatial attributes.

Contrary to the annihilation-type heavenly thunder, the greater the volume of this thunder, the easier it was to construct a celestial passage.

Yet to the Upper Realm, this type of lightning was seemingly an extraordinarily rare commodity as well.

At the very least, the True Immortals were remarkably stingy with it.

In ordinary lightning tribulations, spatial power of this caliber was typically sprinkled like a dash of seasoning—just a tiny pinch. That was precisely why past cultivators had formulated the rule of substituting quantity for quality.

The reason Zhou Hui had failed to ascend in previous lifetimes was partly because spatial heavenly thunder of this variety was far too scarce.

That was why in every reincarnation cycle, Zhou Hui would dispatch Yuwen Xuan to search for the Flame Crystal Flower—his fallback contingency was to use the Flame Crystal Flower to forcibly blow open a passage.

He truly hadn't expected that this time around, the heavens would deliver a lavish giveaway of this exact spatial thunder.

—"Does educating the Demon Race truly garner such immense commendation?"-Zhou Hui smiled faintly.

Seemingly, yes.

The thundercloud slowly condensed above Zhou Hui's head, so gentle that it seemed as though the Untiring Immortal Venerable could step right upon it and soar directly into the True Immortal Realm.

Yet Zhou Hui promptly let out a cold scoff:

—"If it truly is such a monumental merit, then taking me away at this very juncture makes even less sense!"

Indeed. The Demon Race was currently far from fully educated; if anything, the undertaking had only just begun.

While Qu Qianxing was present, the underlying problem remained: Qu Qianxing's cultivation was still too low to hold down the fort, and the Demon Race was notorious for its unyielding wildness. The moment Zhou Hui ascended, the grand enterprise of educating the Demon Race would nine times out of ten collapse entirely.

Then how would this ascension be accounted for?!

Once he arrived in the Upper Realm, would they strip away his karmic merit and knock him back to square one?

Zhou Hui scoffed coldly. Raising his arm, he let drift from his wide sleeve a mist radiating a faint, luminescent glow. Shockingly, this luminous mist swept outward in reverse, flanking and encircling the thundercloud from all sides!

The thundercloud seemed to sense that something was amiss. Unleashing a ferocious rumble, it finally cast down its first bolt of lightning, crashing straight toward Zhou Hui:

*Rumble—!*

—"So this is the heavenly thunder of an ascension tribulation?"-Witnessing this spectacle, the high-rank cultivators inside the Palace of Silence gasped in awe.

They were all at least at the Void Refinement stage, having weathered multiple tribulations themselves, yet this was the first time they had ever laid eyes on such terrifying heavenly thunder. Even sheltered within the Palace of Silence built from Ascension Terrace materials, they felt a shudder of primal dread.

[—"Only the Untiring Immortal Venerable could stand fearlessly amidst such heavenly thunder!"](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

[—"Though... hasn't this bolt of lightning lasted a bit too long?"](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

[—"Come to think of it, yeah..."](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

Straining their eyes wide for several moments, they realized that the lightning enveloping the Untiring Immortal Venerable showed no sign of dissipating. Instead, it glowed continuously, almost resembling a solid pillar of light rather than a fleeting flash of lightning.

[—"What is happening?"](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

[—"Could it be that the Immortal Venerable has failed..."](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

[—"No! Look closer, look at that lightning! Hasn't it... been captured?!"](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

Someone shrieked in shock. The other cultivators froze, but within half a minute, even those with the lowest cultivation could see it clearly: that bolt of lightning had neither struck the Untiring Immortal Venerable nor been blocked by a defensive artifact in a stalemate. Rather, it had been engulfed by the "luminous mist" released by the Untiring Immortal Venerable—and rolled up into a neat ball!

Before long, a radiant sphere of electricity floated serenely by Zhou Hui's side.

Zhou Hui tilted his head upward, looking up with keen anticipation for the second strike of heavenly thunder.

The thundercloud seemed somewhat "hesitant" due to this bizarre turn of events. After brewing for an exceptionally long time, it finally unleashed its second bolt.

This bolt was thicker, grander, and...

And captured even faster.

The cultivators below couldn't even make out what had transpired. All they saw was a colossal flash of lightning flickering within the thundercloud before abruptly "dissipating" into thin air—while beside Zhou Hui, an even larger sphere of electricity appeared.

[—"Wait a minute, you can do that?!"](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

The cultivators exchanged bewildered glances.

Enraged by this insolence, the thundercloud hesitated no longer. *Rumble!* The "third strike" crashed down.

This strike was not a single concentrated bolt; instead, it deployed nine hundred and ninety-nine slender filaments of [scattered lightning](<# "Literal CN: 散雷 (sànléi) — scattered lightning / split lightning barrage; a wide-area carpet bombardment of hundreds of lightning bolts">), bombarding the entire Mount Tianling mountain range.

Yet across the skies of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, a pale barrier of light manifested, gently yet immutably catching every single streak of lightning before scooping them up in reverse—producing nine hundred and ninety-nine tiny glowing orbs of various iridescent hues that clustered around Zhou Hui.

—"Preposterous, utterly preposterous! What manner of facing a tribulation is this?!"-A cultivator couldn't help crying out.-"What if he fails to control all that heavenly lightning and it detonates in one massive burst?!"

—"Detonate?"-Villa Master Qingyang scoffed.-"Rest easy. Even if all that heavenly lightning exploded at once, it wouldn't matter in the slightest. Haven't you noticed? This entire heavenly tribulation is completely within the Lord Immortal Venerable's grasp."

[—"Within his grasp?"](<# "Speakers: Bewildered Cultivators">)

Prompted by Villa Master Qingyang's reminder, they finally realized: three strikes of ascension tribulation thunder had already fallen—including a wide-area barrage of scattered lightning—yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect had not suffered the faintest scratch.

It was one thing for those sheltered inside the Palace of Silence, but even outside the palace walls, not a single speck of remnant lightning had fallen.

Upon realizing this, the cultivators' faces turned pale, far more stunned than if they were facing the lightning themselves.

Precisely because they were all high-rank cultivators who had weathered tribulations before, they knew all too well just how brutally difficult it was to control the scattering of heavenly thunder during a tribulation.

In truth, safeguarding oneself during a tribulation was already an excruciating ordeal, to say nothing of protecting the surroundings; if anything, most cultivators desperately prayed for someone else to shoulder a portion of the lightning.

That was why even Dao companions kept a distance of at least ten li from one another during tribulations to avoid collateral damage.

Yet here, in the midst of an even vaster and far more terrifying ascension tribulation, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had not only captured the heavenly thunder with effortless grace, but had not allowed a single speck of remnant lightning to escape. The Heavenly Spirit Sect rested directly beneath his feet, utterly unscathed!

[—"With this level of mastery, an Ascension Terrace is completely redundant!"](<# "Speaker: High-Rank Cultivator">)

Someone muttered in disbelief.

This time, every single person gazed upward with eyes full of sheer veneration.

Yet the Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's subsequent actions shattered their expectations even further.

After the third strike was captured, the fourth bolt did not immediately descend. It seemed the thundercloud itself had suffered heavy trauma, requiring a much longer time to brew new lightning.

However, Zhou Hui showed none of the patience he had displayed while awaiting the second strike. With a casual flick of his wide sleeve, a net of light surged upward, actively charging straight toward the heavenly thunder!

Although he couldn't swallow the thundercloud in a single gulp, he [fleeced and milked](<# "Literal CN: 薅羊毛 (hāo yángmáo) — fleecing sheep / squeezing and milking resources; Zhou Hui ruthlessly harvesting spatial heavenly lightning from the tribulation clouds like an aggressive resource farmer">) a fourth bolt of lightning right out of the cloud, devouring it whole.

Thus, beside Zhou Hui appeared a fourth complete orb of light.

Yet Zhou Hui was clearly far from satisfied. His net of light constricted around the entire thundercloud, seemingly intent on devouring it down to the last wisp.

And at that very moment, a scene materialized that left every cultivator utterly slack-jawed:

The thundercloud suddenly began to thrash violently. Tearing through the blockade of the light net, it actually turned tail and fled in reverse toward the highest heavens!

Yet the light net refused to let it escape. Like an [arrow loosed from a bowstring](<# "Literal CN: 离弦的光箭 (líxián de guāngjiàn) — an arrow of light loosed from a bowstring; moving at blinding speed">), it shot upward even faster in hot pursuit.

Amidst this chase—one fleeing, the other hunting—a muffled crash of thunder erupted from the highest reaches of the cosmos:

*Rumble—!*

To all the watching cultivators, that roar of thunder sounded remarkably like someone [hurling furious profanities in the street](<# "Literal CN: 骂街 (màjiē) — shouting curses in the street / hurling profanities; the indignant, humiliated bellow of the Heavenly Tribulation as it is hunted and fleeced like helpless prey">).
