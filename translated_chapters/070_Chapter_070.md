# Chapter 70: The School (第70章 学堂)

Regarding going to school: The school announces it, the parents decide it, and the student is usually the last to know.

Such was the case for Eleventh Lady Mei.

When she was held in the embrace of Ninth Concubine—her birth mother—who wept inconsolably, she still had no clue what had transpired.

—"My poor child, why is your fate so bitter—?!"-Ninth Concubine was an opera troupe singer by trade, and her wailing habitually carried an operatic cadence, resembling a theatrical performance meant to face others while deceiving herself.-"Never loved by anyone since childhood, finally picked for a good household, yet before enjoying even a single day of comfort, you're being sent off as a sacrificial offering—!"

[A sacrificial offering?]

[Is this some new scheme they came up with to sell off another daughter?]

Eleventh Lady Mei thought with cold detachment.

After listening to her concubine-mother weep in rambling circles for half a day, she finally pieced together that the town had somehow provoked an evil sect called the "Heavenly Spirit Sect," and the other party had demanded that the town surrender all [virgin boys and girls](# "Literal CN: 童男童女 (tóngnán tóngnǚ) — ancient trope of offering virgin boys and girls to demonic deities or evil sects") as sacrificial offerings.

—"...Then Twelfth Brother has to go too?"-Eleventh Lady Mei asked.

Ninth Concubine instantly ceased her rhythmic, melodramatic wailing and cursed with venomous spite:

—"Who knows what kind of [sweet-talk soup](# "Literal CN: 灌米汤 (guàn mǐtāng) — to pour rice broth; to flatter or bewitch someone into compliance") that wretched vixen fed the master to keep her brat safe and sound behind?!"

Speaking to this point, Ninth Concubine viciously pinched Eleventh Lady Mei's arm:

—"It's all because you're useless! If you had been born a son, would you face this today?!"

With that, she broke into mournful wailing once more!

Eleventh Lady Mei numbly registered the sharp pain in her arm, watching her mother weep with cold, detached eyes.

[What of it?]

[Isn't it virtually identical to how things were before?]

Longmen Town had always been "protected" by Shengmo Sect, and Mayor Mei's clan had always maintained cozy ties with Shengmo Sect. For the daughters of the Mei clan, those who were plain were left alone, but those blessed with beauty—regardless of whether they possessed spirit roots—would be carried off to Shengmo Sect in bridal sedan chairs.

It was said that Mei Xianyu obtained his post as town mayor precisely because his eldest daughter had caught the eye of a Shengmo Sect direct disciple. Because of this, Mei Xianyu was proud to no end, feeling that this had fiercely slapped the faces of all those who mocked him for [lacking immortal destiny](# "Wordplay: The name Mei Xianyu (梅仙缘) is a homophone for 'Lacks Immortal Destiny' (没仙缘)"). His surname Mei (梅) was destined to align with the character for [matchmaker](# "Wordplay: Mei (梅) sounds like Mei (媒) as in 媒人 (matchmaker/go-between)")! From then on, he was fanatically keen on shipping his daughters off to Shengmo Sect.

Yet Eleventh Lady Mei had never once laid eyes on that eldest sister who had secured their father the mayoral seat. What she had seen was their third sister, who had been married to a certain manager in Shengmo Sect. That third sister's face had been gaunt and haggard, yet she forced an agonizing smile, insisting that everything was wonderful—only to secretly trace the words "RUN" upon Eleventh Lady Mei's palm as she took her leave. Less than two years later, Third Sister was dead. And Eleventh Lady Mei was selected to become that very manager's successor wife.

This was what her mother called a "good household."

Eleventh Lady Mei refused to dwell on what had truly transpired; she only felt a bone-chilling cold throughout her body.

Third Sister told her to run, but where could she possibly run to?

Without Shengmo Sect, would her father not simply marry her off to someone else?

Like this "Heavenly Spirit Sect" that had abruptly cropped up out of nowhere?

Eleventh Lady Mei lowered her eyelashes, fingers brushing against the sharpened hairpin concealed inside her sleeve.

Reality swiftly proved that Eleventh Lady Mei possessed no room for resistance. No matter how much Ninth Concubine wailed and threw a fit, when the appointed hour arrived, the main madam's head maid arrived, flanked by several burly elder matrons.

—"Ninth Concubine, you were told to dress Eleventh Young Lady up, and this is how you dressed her?"-the head maid sneered with biting sarcasm.

The elder matrons instantly pounced forward like wolves and tigers.

Some forcibly peeled Ninth Concubine away; others roughly stripped and changed Eleventh Lady Mei's garments. Eleventh Lady Mei had zero room to resist, clinging tightly only to her hairpin. Fortunately, pressed for time, the matrons paid little heed to what she clutched in her hand. After a whirlwind of rough bustling, they bundled the dazed Eleventh Lady Mei out of the house as fast as humanly possible, dragging her directly to the center of town.

The brilliant light outside stung Eleventh Lady Mei's eyes so intensely she could barely open them.

It was laughably absurd: growing up to fourteen years old, aside from a handful of visits to relatives, Eleventh Lady Mei had virtually never set foot outside her boudoir tower. She had only ever entered the family's main ceremonial hall during the New Year to pay respects to clan elders.

As a native of Longmen Town, Eleventh Lady Mei possessed zero concept of what Longmen Town actually was.

Even up to the very end, she had no concept of it.

Reflecting upon it afterward, Eleventh Lady Mei only felt that the light outside had been overwhelmingly glaring, blurring everything into strange, flickering patches of glare that left not a single indelible impression in her memory.

Eleventh Lady Mei's true memory began only with the tolling of a bell.

Accompanied by the noon bell, Eleventh Lady Mei felt the world spin violently upside down. By the time her senses returned, she found herself standing in pitch darkness. Before her eyes stood a colossal, tender-yellow, leafless tree—only later did Eleventh Lady Mei learn that it was coral—glowing softly in the dark.

Eleventh Lady Mei found the tree breathtakingly beautiful, unable to resist gazing at it several times.

Unfortunately, this was hardly the time for sightseeing. For by the gentle luminescence of that "tree," Eleventh Lady Mei took in everything around her.

So many people! And the vast majority were young girls.

Following Ninth Concubine's words, aside from those sheltered and preserved, practically every child in the entire town had been brought here, hadn't they?

Everyone seemed to have realized their grim fate as "sacrificial offerings." Most of the children were weeping, differing only in the volume of their sobs. A rare few stood frozen with numb or utterly bewildered expressions, just like Eleventh Lady Mei.

—"Shut up!"-a harsh bark cracked through the air.

Eleventh Lady Mei did not even bother lifting her eyelids; she recognized the voice—it was her father.

Mayor Mei Xianyu, nursing a throbbing headache from the clamorous wailing of the children, roared in fury:

—"Look at you lot, sniffling and bawling like this! What if the immortal masters see you and take offense?!"

Hearing him say this, older, sensible youths like Eleventh Lady Mei managed to swallow back their whimpers, muffling their cries. But among the crowd, a cluster of seven- and eight-year-old youngsters only wailed all the louder.

The coral tree had cold-bloodedly and mercilessly transported all eligible children in one fell swoop. The parents had not been permitted to follow; the only adults responsible for escorting them here were Mei Xianyu and Lou Wu. Otherwise, the scene would have devolved into utter pandemonium.

Mei Xianyu barked several more orders, only to discover that the little brats paid him no heed whatsoever. Shouting and beating proved entirely useless; it only made them cry harder.

Fuming with anxiety, he suddenly heard a delicate, dainty voice complain:

—"So noisy."

Accompanied by that voice, every single child seemed to have their throat clamped by an invisible hand, unable to utter another sound.

No... they *were* being choked by the neck.

Even Eleventh Lady Mei, who hadn't been crying, felt something constrict around her throat at that very moment, suffocating her to the point where she could scarcely draw breath.

Instantly, looks of sheer horror washed over the children's faces. Several fragile young girls even fainted dead away on the spot.

Unable to suppress her terror, Eleventh Lady Mei forced herself to look forward.

Only then did she notice that aside from her father Mei Xianyu and a few hired lackeys, there stood a strikingly delicate and enchanting young lady.

Eleventh Lady Mei had often been praised as the most beautiful maiden in Longmen Town—which was probably true, or else she wouldn't have been selected to be sent off to Shengmo Sect. Yet compared to this maiden, the contrast was literally that of a rustic village girl beside a celestial fairy.

It was only because Eleventh Lady Mei's attention had initially been stolen away by the glowing coral tree that she hadn't noticed her earlier.

Could this maiden be an immortal fairy from that "Heavenly Spirit Sect"?

Eleventh Lady Mei guessed silently.

—"Many thanks, Immortal Maiden,"-Mei Xianyu said obsequiously to the maiden. Turning around, his gaze landed upon his own daughters.-"Why haven't you come forward to greet your Grand-Aunt?!"

Grand-Aunt? Isn't she an emissary from Heavenly Spirit Sect?

Eleventh Lady Mei found it thoroughly bizarre, but followed her sisters forward to curtsy in greeting.

Oddly (or perhaps not so oddly) enough, the moment they curtsied to this "Grand-Aunt," the constriction around their throats abruptly vanished, allowing them to speak normally again.

Only Fourteenth Lady—the youngest among the sisters—failed to perceive the danger. Because she had a brother born of the same concubine mother, she was relatively pampered at home, and thus blurted out with foolish innocence:

—"Dad, since when do we have a Grand-Aunt?"

—"Of course you do,"-the "fairy Grand-Aunt" said with a beaming smile.-"You just forgot."

She curved her alluring [peach-blossom eyes](# "Peach-blossom eyes (桃花眼) — an attractive, flirtatious eye shape associated with charm and romantic fascination"), fixing her gaze upon the young girls.

A wave of dizziness washed over Eleventh Lady Mei.

Ah, she remembered now. She indeed had a grand-aunt who, due to her delicate health, had been raised on an estate outside town since childhood. For the sake of Heavenly Spirit Sect's decree, she had specifically been brought back...

Wait, no!

Eleventh Lady Mei exerted all her strength, viciously slashing her palm with the sharpened hairpin inside her sleeve!

A jolt of agony pierced through her, snapping her mind awake.

She remembered with crystal clarity that her family's great-grandfather had passed away decades ago—how on earth could a "grand-aunt" suddenly materialize out of thin air?!

Besides, with just a few daughters marrying low-tier cultivators as concubines, her father already boasted as if he were an in-law of Shengmo Sect itself! If he truly possessed an immortal fairy for an aunt, wouldn't his tail have soared into the ninth heavens?!

Then... who was this "Grand-Aunt"?

From her tone, she intended to blend into this group of children and sneak along to that so-called White Jade Capital school?

Then she was definitely not from Heavenly Spirit Sect! Where had she come from?

Eleventh Lady Mei's thoughts swirled in chaos, inexplicably recalling how Ninth Concubine had once attempted to plant personal maids beside the master's newly favored concubine.

Having survived in the inner courtyard for fourteen years, Eleventh Lady Mei had long honed her own brand of survival wisdom. She promptly lowered her eyelashes, pretending she had been thoroughly hypnotized and acting as if this truly were her long-lost grand-aunt.

Fortunately, this newly minted Grand-Aunt held little interest in Eleventh Lady Mei and the others.

Once she confirmed she had hypnotized Eleventh Lady Mei and her sisters and secured her false identity, she dismissed them.

And right at that moment, an immense black shadow swept across the waters above the coral tree.

—"Is that... a Kunpeng?"-Eleventh Lady Mei heard the "Grand-Aunt" murmur in astonishment.

Eleventh Lady Mei had no idea what a "Kunpeng" was, nor did she dare or desire to look up. She stood motionless like a wooden puppet stripped of all curiosity—until transparent bubbles enveloped her body.

—"Eek?!"-Eleventh Lady Mei gasped, unable to hold back.

[What is this?]

Her eyes went wide as the bubble cocooned her, floating gently upward.

And as the bubble ascended, Eleventh Lady Mei finally realized she was actually inside a "river"—she couldn't help it; Eleventh Lady Mei had never even heard of the concept of an "ocean"—she noticed bubbles floating outside, along with vast shoals of fish swimming past. Dazzling and kaleidoscopic, they were far more exquisite than the fat carps in her family's courtyard pond.

Eleventh Lady Mei couldn't resist pressing against the edge of the bubble, peering closely at the schools of fish.

If even the steady Eleventh Lady Mei was so captivated, one could only imagine the reaction of the other children.

Especially the younger ones.

Children truly were easy to coax.

Only moments ago, these seven- and eight-year-olds had been weeping in terror at being separated from their parents. Now, seeing themselves cocooned inside bubbles, swaying gently through the water surrounded by gorgeous little fish, they instantly forgot their fear and burst into giggles. The moment they had been scooped into the bubbles, the constriction clamping their throats had completely dissolved. The bolder ones even reached their hands out toward the fish shoals.

The children failed to notice that their bubbles were actually moving forward.

Enfolded by the massive underwater current stirred by that colossal shadow, the bubbles drifted swiftly ahead.

Of course, even had the children noticed, they wouldn't have cared. Even Eleventh Lady Mei secretly wished she could linger inside this bubble a little longer.

Only that mysteriously appeared "Grand-Aunt" communicated with Lou Wu via divine sense, asking nervously:

—"Didn't you say we would ride the Kunpeng? Why have we been in these bubbles for so long without boarding?"

—"I-I don't know either,"-Lou Wu replied in a panic.-"Normally, people board right away!"

—"Could something have gone wrong?"-she asked, her face paling.

[Was my identity exposed?!]

The "Grand-Aunt's" face turned distinctly pale. She stared anxiously at the surrounding seawater, terrified that at any moment the bubble would pop, casting her into the deepest abyss of the Abyssal Ocean to perish without even a corpse remaining.

Such fears, however, were entirely superfluous.

The reason these "passengers" had not boarded the Kunpeng was simply that this was an ultra-short-distance commute.

Carrying the children along the currents stirred by the Kunpeng, the bubbles traveled for roughly [one quarter-hour](# "A quarter-hour (一刻钟) is about fifteen minutes")—which happened to be fifteen minutes of pure terror for the "Grand-Aunt" and Lou Wu—before an undersea coral structure of kaleidoscopic colors came into view on the seabed.

In front of the building stood a colossal white jade stele, upon which was inscribed:

> White Jade Capital Central Domain Northwest Sixth School

Carrying the enchanted children whose curiosity was far from sated, the bubbles slowly descended, landing beside the structure.

Several cultivators were already waiting there.

Taking in the appearance of these cultivators, Eleventh Lady Mei gasped in admiration.

She suddenly felt that calling that "Grand-Aunt" an immortal fairy earlier had been terribly presumptuous. *These* were true immortals! Had they stood there from the start, let alone a single tree, not even the sun in the sky could have outshone their radiance.

And the cultivator leading them revealed a gentle smile to all the children:

—"Welcome to White Jade Capital School."

* * *

White Jade Capital Central Domain Southeast Second School.

Another young maiden was gazing upon the exact same spectacle:

[Ah, so this is what a true immortal looks like?!]

[No wonder Mother said that if one could latch onto any immortal, they'd never have to worry for the rest of their days.]

[Ah, what am I thinking?! I clearly have a betrothed already—how shameful!]

Eleventh Lady Qiao thought, covering her blushing cheeks with dainty embarrassment.

Perhaps her reaction had been too conspicuous, for Eleventh Lady Qiao heard a soft, mocking sneer from beside her:

—"Pfft."

The one scoffing was an exquisite young maiden.

Before seeing the several immortals of the school, this maiden had been the most breathtaking girl Eleventh Lady Qiao had ever laid eyes on.

In fact, the very first moment she saw this girl, Eleventh Lady Qiao had even felt a trace of relief that her betrothed lacked the qualifications to attend the school. Her fiancé was a boy from a neighboring village to Qiao Family Village. Because that village had long subordinated itself to a cultivation clan under Fengqing City, they had missed the Immortals' Bazaar, naturally failing to obtain partnership status and thus ineligible to attend this immortal school. She knew full well that her fiancé had a slightly roving eye; seeing a maiden like this, he certainly wouldn't have been able to walk away. But then again, which man wasn't like that?

Still, for a woman, virtue and capability were what truly mattered, right?!

If she became the most virtuous woman in the ten surrounding miles and eight neighboring villages—just as her mother taught—her fiancé surely wouldn't dare look at other women! In fact, Eleventh Lady Qiao was already the most virtuous and capable girl in Qiao Family Village!

Not only did she look after her younger brothers and sisters at home and work the fields with great skill, but simply by listening attentively while delivering younger siblings and trading with traveling peddlers, she had not only learned to write simple characters, but could even help the village chief weigh goods and balance accounts! During the recent Immortals' Bazaar trade, Eleventh Lady Qiao had been a tremendous help! She was the one who weighed, calculated, and packaged an entire warehouse of goods; whenever anyone in Qiao Family Village heard Eleventh Lady Qiao's name, who wouldn't give a resounding thumbs-up?!

Now that she had obtained the rare opportunity to enter the school, Eleventh Lady Qiao was determined to do her absolute best! Only then would her future in-laws realize that marrying Eleventh Lady Qiao was the most profitable bargain imaginable!

Of course, achieving this wouldn't be easy.

Before arriving at the school, Eleventh Lady Qiao had assumed only children from Qiao Family Village would attend. Only upon arriving at this "White Jade Capital Central Domain Southeast Second School" did she discover that children from all partner villages in the Fengqing City region had been sent here.

That lovely maiden, for instance, came from another village, and looked at a glance like an educated young lady from a proper family.

Eleventh Lady Qiao instantly felt a bit tense.

Trembling slightly, she followed the immortal's instructions and pressed her palm against the stone stele. Instantly, her name—"Qiao Shiyiniang"—appeared in the center of the stele.

Before Eleventh Lady Qiao could admire her name, she was pushed aside by the girl behind her.

Registering her own name on the stele, that maiden turned and asked the immortals with graceful poise:

—"Immortal Sir, does this mean my registration is complete? May we begin classes now?"

—"[Rector](# "Literal CN: 祭酒 (jìjiǔ) — Libationer / Rector, an ancient official title for the head of the Imperial Academy (Guozijian)")—you may call me Rector,"-the immortal, pure and bright as moonlight, replied.-"Today is merely registration. Formal classes will begin after the New Year."

After all, he wasn't so unreasonable; for mortals, the New Year was of paramount importance.

—"Eh? We must return home for the New Year?"-the maiden asked with a slight furrow of her brow, looking utterly pitiful and aggrieved.-"To tell the truth, I have lost both my parents and kin; I am merely an orphan in my village. It is entirely thanks to Heavenly Spirit Sect that I have the privilege to come here! I cannot wait to begin classes tomorrow."

Eleventh Lady Qiao's ears pricked up immediately.

[I-Is she taking it that seriously?!]

Although Uncle Gen had told them to listen obediently to the immortals and study hard, Eleventh Lady Qiao had assumed that only applied during class hours. Who would have thought other students wanted to start studying before school even opened?!

—"Having such dedication is commendable, but diligent study is not a matter of rushing,"-the Rector said in an easygoing, amiable manner.-"Besides, textbooks will be distributed today. Review them thoroughly before classes start, and strive for high marks on the entrance placement examination!"

—"By the way, for children who find it inconvenient to commute home and wish to study more, the school will provide boarding services in the future,"-the Rector added with a warm smile.-"Students aged twelve and above may register."

[Reviewing the textbooks right now?]

[Boarding at school to study?]

Eleventh Lady Qiao listened intently to the Rector's briefing.

She had originally planned to commute daily between home and school; after all, there were so many household chores to attend to and younger siblings to care for. But studying was critically important too! Uncle Gen had even said it was far more important than chores at home! At the very least, she could just return home during the [ten-day rest breaks](# "Literal CN: 旬休 (xúnxiū) — traditional ten-day rest break in ancient academies and imperial bureaucracy")!

[I refuse to lose to anyone else!]

Eleventh Lady Qiao resolved firmly.

* * *

Back at Northwest Sixth School:

—"...Those requesting boarding accommodations may register right now. However, before the official start of term, you may only purchase meals with spirit stones or work in exchange for sustenance, assisting with the school's preparatory setup..."-the cultivator announced.

It was actually a school?

It was really a school!

Did her father even understand what this place was before packing them off here?

Now that she was here, she'd have to be an utter fool to go back!

Without a second thought, Eleventh Lady Mei checked her name on the "Boarding Registration" and even added the word "FOREVER" beside it.

Only when submitting her registration form did she notice her freshly minted "Grand-Aunt" was also there, making her hesitate for two moments.

In the end, Eleventh Lady Mei gritted her teeth and handed in the "Boarding Registration."

She did not know where that "Grand-Aunt" hailed from, but for such a person to be here—didn't that prove this place represented an entirely new realm of possibility?

* * *

Eastern Domain, Palace of Carefree Wandering.

—"Lord Palace Master, all our 'chess pieces' have successfully entered their destinations,"-a subordinate reported through a water mirror.

Upon hearing this news, Palace Master Li Hao of Carefree Palace became exceedingly agitated instead:

—"Not a single one was weeded out?!"

—"Perhaps it was never a major affair to begin with,"-said the gorgeous youth attending Li Hao.-"They are merely mortal children after all. Perhaps Heavenly Spirit Sect simply wants to control their 'partners in cooperation.'"

—"To control a few mortals, why would he personally take the field?!"-Li Hao grew even more frantic.-"And according to reports, that man actually deployed avatar techniques to attend the enrollment ceremonies of every single school in person, yet failed to spot our 'chess pieces'! Could he be plotting some conspiracy—?!"

Although Carefree Palace had been thwarted by Qu Qianxing during their previous meddling, that hardly meant they had given up. In fact, they had maintained an unrelenting surveillance over Heavenly Spirit Sect, keeping track of every single movement.

—"You are far too high-strung,"-the youth chuckled.-"Whether those were true avatars remains up in the air; Untiring is famously detached from worldly affairs, so perhaps he merely cast a few illusions as window dressing. Besides, our chess pieces were meticulously handpicked, their backgrounds and identities completely tailored to regulations. Unless Untiring personally investigated every single individual, he would have no reason to suspect them."

—"So you're certain that person knows nothing?"-Li Hao glared at the youth.

The youth froze for a beat.

Evidently, even he found the notion of "Untiring Immortal Venerable knowing nothing" rather implausible.

—"Still, given Untiring's temperament, had he cared, he would have acted long ago!"-the youth argued, still feeling Li Hao was making a mountain out of a molehill, thoroughly traumatized into neurotic paranoia by Untiring Immortal Venerable lately.-"While it is indeed strange for Untiring to suddenly establish schools, you are overly anxious. What on earth are you worried about?"

—"What am I worried about? I'm worried about him **CONQUERING THE WORLD!**"-Li Hao shrieked.

—"Huh?"-the youth was dumbfounded.

—"Look at the locations of the villages and towns he selected—spread across every corner of the Central Domain! I have every reason to believe he has finally grown sick of our Carefree Palace's influence and wants to annex the entire Central Domain!"-Li Hao ranted incessantly, spewing out all his observations and deductions over the past days.-"AND! Did you notice? He used the name White Jade Capital for these schools! What kind of place was White Jade Capital? It was the ancient center of cultivation! This is irrefutable proof of his ambition!"

...Put that way, it actually sounded somewhat plausible.

The youth still found it hard to believe:

—"But that's Untiring! How could he possibly conquer the world?"

—"Then why did he use the name White Jade Capital? Could he have discovered the ruins of White Jade Capital?"-Li Hao countered.

—"You're spouting more and more nonsense,"-the youth retorted without thinking.-"It is impossible for anyone to find White Jade Capital, because it was utterly annihilated—not even its faintest traces remain."

—"Then explain the meaning behind naming the school 'White Jade Capital'?!"-Li Hao demanded coldly.-"He couldn't have picked it just because he thought the name sounded pretty, could he?!"

The youth fell silent.

Indeed, in the cultivation world, names were never used lightly. Any name invoked carried definitive significance.

—"...How about this,"-the youth conceded at last.-"The New Year is approaching, and the ancient powerhouses of this land hold an annual gathering as custom dictates. Since Untiring hasn't entered secluded cultivation lately, in theory he will give the host face. Regarding the matter of 'White Jade Capital', I shall ask him in person."
