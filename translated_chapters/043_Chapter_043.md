# Chapter 43: Natural History Studies of the Heavenly Spirit Sect (第43章 天灵派博物考)

Autumn of this year, yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect was like spring, bursting with exuberant vitality.

Not only were the freshmen striving their utmost to adapt to their new sect life, but the veteran disciples were also competing fiercely to earn the praise of their teachers and elders. As their master, Zhou Hui naturally could not slack off either—

No, even in the past, regardless of the season, Zhou Hui had never had a moment of slacking off. This year, he was actually relatively relaxed.

At least Zhou Hui himself felt so.

First, he wasn't in closed-door seclusion; second, he wasn't meditating or cultivating. While all the children were bustling in a frantic frenzy, he even had the leisure to sit in the Sect Master's Office of Buyan Palace [Palace of Silence](# "Literal CN: 不言宫 (Bùyán Gōng) — Palace of Silence/Unspoken Words, from 'peaches and plums do not speak, yet a path forms beneath them'"), facing a wall densely plastered with course schedules, contemplating whether there was anything he had overlooked. In this world, could there possibly be any behavior more "slack" than this?

—"I am certain I must have overlooked something,"-Zhou Hui murmured to himself.

He brought up the system interface, deciding to cross-check his pedagogical curriculum once more.

Master Lan Pei sat on a sofa to the side, cradling a wine jar in his arms. Speaking of which, this variety of cushioned seat called a "sofa" had earned rave reviews from this fox spirit, who judged it to be the most relaxing seat under heaven—regrettably situated in the room of Zhou Hui, a man who possessed not the slightest concept of what "relaxation" even meant. Lan Pei complained:

—"Isn't it completely normal to have overlooked something? Just looking at that wall of yours makes my head spin!"-Fortunately, the divine physician remained blissfully ignorant of the existence of [trypophobia](# "Literal CN: 密集恐惧症 (mìjí kǒngjù zhèng) — fear of clustered patterns or dense repetition"); otherwise, he certainly would have used it to diagnose himself.

Lan Pei lectured with earnest, well-meaning patience:

—"There is no such thing as a perfect plan in this world, just as there is no living organism that never falls ill. So long as the body maintains basic equilibrium, the presence of minor lesions is hardly unacceptable. There is no need for you to demand absolute perfection. Paranoia of this caliber makes it look like you've contracted a major affliction—something akin to hysteria."-Or rather, a psychiatric disorder.

Zhou Hui paid him no heed, continuing to attend to his business with unwavering dedication.

Seeing this, Lan Pei sighed and took a swig of wine.

Reeking of alcohol fumes, he grumbled in deep distress:

—"If you keep acting like this, your disciples will only worry more. And if I cannot produce a plausible 'treatment protocol,' they will never let me leave."

—"If you truly wished to flee, could they stop you?"-Zhou Hui finally spared Lan Pei a glance.

—"They would drag me right back!"-Lan Pei squawked.-"Do you still not know what your eldest disciple is like?!"

Zhou Hui considered it for a moment, ultimately conceding the point:

—"Fair enough. Heaven only knows where that boy picked up those methods of his..."

Lan Pei's expression turned exceedingly subtle:

—"You..."

Lan Pei had wanted to say something, but before the words could leave his mouth, a knock sounded outside the Sect Master's Office:

[—"Master."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

Among the direct disciples, fully aware that Zhou Hui could sense his approach with divine sense yet still conscientiously knocking on the door in strict accordance with decorum—only Qu Qianxing, who placed supreme value on propriety, would do such a thing.

With frantic, [self-deceiving haste](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — plugging one's ears to steal a bell; willful blindness"), Lan Pei shoved the wine jar beneath the sofa.

—"And here I thought the only one you feared was Yuwen Xuan,"-Zhou Hui remarked at the sight.

—"Bah, that second disciple of yours is plenty troublesome too,"-Lan Pei grumbled.-"Or rather, disciples of that archetype are universally troublesome. Fortunately, the one in my clan has already graduated."

—"Is that so?"-Zhou Hui remarked noncommittally. Only then did he turn toward the door with a gentle smile:—"Enter."

Qu Qianxing opened the door. The instant the scent of alcohol in the office hit him, his brow furrowed, and he shot a sharp glare at Lan Pei. Lan Pei promptly offered an obedient, innocent smile.

Qu Qianxing glared at him. Ultimately, because this was the divine physician treating his master, he refrained from speaking out, merely setting the file in his hands onto Zhou Hui's desk:

—"Master, these are the statistical results from the beginner class's first department intent survey."

—"Good, I shall examine them shortly,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

Having set down the documents, Qu Qianxing lingered in place for two seconds, seemingly hesitating over something, not speaking immediately.

Zhou Hui instantly caught his second disciple's slight abnormality, turning to ask:

—"What is the matter?"

—"It is nothing worth alarming Master over,"-Qu Qianxing said, furrowing his brows.

Ever since "school opened," he had taken over all internal sect administrative affairs, refusing to allow 'irrelevant entities' to disturb Master, who was injured and required peaceful convalescence (presumably). The matter at hand fell squarely under administrative duties; originally, Qu Qianxing felt he shouldn't bring it here, but because the party involved was rather peculiar, he felt compelled to seek guidance.

Qu Qianxing spoke deliberately:

—"Mainly... 'that entity' Master keeps in the caverns near Heaven-Cleaving Chasm has come to lodge a complaint."

—"Disciples have complained about it already?"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"I shall transplant it further away later... Ah, it did not harm anyone, did it? If it harmed someone, I might as well dispose of it entirely."

—"...No, Master, you misunderstand,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"It is not that someone came to complain about it. It came to lodge a complaint against our disciples."

—"Eh?"-Zhou Hui blinked in surprise, looking at Qu Qianxing with a peculiar expression:—"What could it possibly have to complain about?"

Qu Qianxing did not speak. He glanced at Lan Pei, found the fellow had no intention of leaving, and saw that Master showed no intention of dismissing him either. Thus, ignoring the fox's presence, he turned his gaze downward to his own shadow:

—"Come out."

Along with those words, Qu Qianxing's shadow turned thick and viscous like dark sludge, bubbling with churning, rainbow-hued bubbles. A black entity resembling a tangle of plant roots burrowed out from within, studded with three or four protruding eyeballs.

[—"Holy crap! A demon!"](# "Speaker: Master Lan Pei")

Lan Pei shrieked in terror, bouncing straight off the sofa and diving behind it in a single leap.

—"Zhou Hui, we haven't offended you recently, have we?!"-Those eyeballs did not spare Lan Pei so much as a glance. Tears pattered down from them in bitter streams as a raspy, static-filled voice cried out in accusation:—"We were growing perfectly fine in the void! You were the one who forcefully abducted us here! Even after entering this realm, we never attacked the living creatures here, and we worked so hard to restrain ourselves from reproducing or expanding our territory! All this time..."

—"Get to the point,"-Zhou Hui said coldly. He hardly felt his precious time ought to be squandered on this thing's blubbering lamentations.

Admonished by him, the entity known as a demon wailed in overwhelming grievance:

—"We obeyed every command so meekly, so why did you send your disciples to dig me up?! Three times in a single night! Every single time, they stripped my flesh, chopped my root filaments, and drained my demonic qi with sealing artifacts! Even for a demon, who can withstand you [sucking marrow and scraping bones](# "Literal CN: 敲骨吸髓 (qiāo gǔ xī suǐ) — squeezing someone dry; extreme exploitation") like this?!"

—"Three times in one night?"-Zhou Hui blinked, looking toward Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing said helplessly:

—"Were you not preparing to construct the spiritual energy dam, Master? It is likely... the surveying teams led by Junior Sister and Junior Brothers."

***

[—"Do you see this vine corrupted by demonic qi?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

[—"I shall now demonstrate how to handle it and collect data on the demonic qi within its form."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

[—"First, we must establish a defensive perimeter. A standard demon-repelling solution made from plant ash mixed with peach wood and pure water will suffice. Pour it into a spray canister, and centered around the mutated flora, spray a full circle..."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Lu Shulong explained with meticulous patience, demonstrating each and every step with flawless precision.

By this time, however, the hour was already late. The disciples trailing behind Lu Shulong were visibly flagging in spirits. Furthermore, not only was Lu Shulong remarkably delicate and handsome, his voice was clear and melodious with an androgynous softness, leading several individuals to treat his discourse as a lullaby, nodding off in a drowsy stupor.

At this juncture, the stark contrast between new and veteran members of the Alchemy Department became blindingly conspicuous.

The senior disciples fished out Spirit-Refreshing Pills and shoved them directly into their nostrils, not daring to miss a single syllable—though the practiced fluidity of their movements was frankly heartbreaking to behold.

The new disciples, on the other hand, indulged themselves in a half-asleep, half-awake daze, and several among them even harbored growing dissatisfaction.

Such as Zhu Rong and Liu Gong.

Both were Golden Core cultivators who had roamed the Central Domain for decades, boasting extensive wilderness expedition experience. They considered themselves fully qualified to assume elder positions in any other sect; it was solely out of reverence for Zhou Hui's peerless might that they had deigned to come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect as ordinary disciples.

Their confidence was not without justification.

The pair were already illustrious among the rogue cultivators of the Central Domain. Mention "Daoist Zhu" and "Hermit Liu," and virtually no local rogue cultivator was unfamiliar with their names. It had taken considerable painstaking effort for them to enter the Untiring Immortal Venerable's sect: they had hunted down past examination papers from the Heavenly Spirit Sect and scrutinized them meticulously. As a result, they performed brilliantly on the sect's placement examination, leading this year's freshmen cohort by a wide margin to claim first and second place respectively, successfully entering the advanced class and securing their coveted assignment to the Alchemy Department.

Yet the moment their eyes fell upon Lu Shulong's soft, melancholy visage, they began to doubt their life decisions.

—Though both served as the visual figureheads of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Lu Shulong was distinct from Qu Qianxing. His beauty lacked the slightest trace of aggression. What was more, he was merely at the late stage of Foundation Establishment; compared to Qu Qianxing, who was as intoxicatingly striking as a blooming peony, Lu Shulong resembled a completely harmless white lotus.

In tranquil times, such features might be thoroughly endearing, but in the position of a "teacher" or "superior," they lacked authority, inviting condescension from students and subordinates alike.

Zhu Rong grumbled to Liu Gong via divine sense:

—"This [pretty boy](# "Literal CN: 小白脸 (xiǎobáiliǎn) — fair-skinned youth; derogatory for lacking masculinity") is as [fussy as an old woman](# "Literal CN: 婆婆妈妈 (pópomāmā) — naggy, indecisive, overly meticulous")."

—"Precisely. It's just a vine corrupted by demonic qi, isn't it? We've seen hundreds in ancient battlefield ruins!"-Liu Gong remarked.-"Is it truly worth treating like a mortal nemesis?"

—"Probably protected too well by the Immortal Venerable,"-another voice chimed into the conversation via divine sense.

It turned out the two had left their divine sense open to the other disciples, essentially operating on a [public frequency](# "Literal CN: 公频 (gōngpín) — open global chat frequency") that deliberately excluded the instructor.

[—"Had I known, I would have chosen the Artifact Refinement Hall."](# "Speaker: New Disciple")

[—"Indeed. Guan Ye over there appears far more dependable; I hear he oversees numerous vital projects."](# "Speaker: New Disciple")

[—"Regrettably, I only know alchemy, not artifact forging."](# "Speaker: New Disciple")

The disciples buzzed with discussion.

They failed to notice that only freshmen participated in this chatter. Though the veteran disciples could hear this public broadcast loud and clear, they merely kept silent and smiled, the corners of their lips curving into knowing, ambiguous smirks.

Lu Shulong, too, seemed to notice nothing at all.

Having gently, softly explained the entire process of handling demonic-polluted vines, he smiled warmly at the Alchemy disciples:

—"Alright, now you may all give it a try!"

The disciples instantly sprang into action, the freshmen particularly eager.

After all, private complaints were private complaints; having newly arrived, one still had to put on a good performance. Even if they intended to usurp authority later, they would wait until they earned the fellow's trust!

It was just that this group hadn't listened attentively during the lecture. Whether they could execute the steps properly was another question entirely.

Of course, omitting a few steps hardly seemed to matter anyway.

It was merely a vine corrupted by demonic qi. No matter how formidable, could it actually devour a man?

At least Zhu Rong and Liu Gong—who believed their wilderness exploration experience far surpassed that of some pretty boy—firmly held this conviction.

And in fact, they did successfully complete Lu Shulong's assignment, retrieving the experimental specimen without incident; the demonic qi data they recorded was merely somewhat irregular.

Fortunately, perhaps because the hour was so late, Lu Shulong did not even inspect their results.

He did not even demand that the disciples submit their collected data, merely saying:

—"It is getting late, and tomorrow there will be even more tasks to attend to. Shall we rest here for tonight? Ah, by the way, do you still remember the wilderness bivouac tips I mentioned on the first day? If not, I can repeat them once more..."

Liu Gong rolled his eyes at Zhu Rong:

—"What a chatterbox."

—"Indeed, [naggy as an old woman](# "Literal CN: 跟个婆娘似的 (gēn ge póniáng shìde) — fussing like a meddlesome wife")."

Disregarding whatever Lu Shulong was saying, the pair skillfully selected a spot slightly distanced from the main group yet offering an open vantage point, sat down, and splashed a defensive perimeter of potion around themselves.

As once-illustrious rogue cultivators, they certainly did not believe their wilderness survival prowess would lose to some pretty boy.

At the Golden Core stage, one had little need for sleep; meditating alone was sufficient to restore one's vigor.

And so, while meditating, the pair continued their idle banter:

—"Speaking of which, what is the purpose of this outing?"

—"A Heavenly Spirit Sect tradition, most likely? I noticed that not only our Alchemy Department, but the Artifact Refinement Hall and Array Hall came out afterward as well."

—"A bizarre tradition."

—"Still far more reliable than other sects."

—"That is true enough. Who knows what those other sects are thinking—protecting their disciples like fragile porcelain on ordinary days, acting as though a single mutant vine within the sect grounds could end their lives, only to dump them all into trials without warning when the time comes. With that style of teaching, it would be strange if several didn't die every single time!"

—"The sole problem with the Heavenly Spirit Sect is that the direct disciples are too young—and far too soft."

—"Indeed. One glance tells you he has zero wilderness survival experience. By the time he finishes that whole routine of his, the sun will already be up."

—"Still, occasionally demonic mutant flora can be genuinely horrifying."

—"Huh?"

—"I once heard a friend recount that while exploring the Abyssal Ocean Secret Realm, someone had their skin snagged and torn by a mutant briar. From then on, he developed an affliction—an endless, persistent itch across his body. Thinking he had suffered minor poisoning, he paid it no heed. Two or three years passed, and during a drunken argument, someone slapped him across the face. Who could have imagined his head would roll right off *thump-thump-thump*, and briars spewed from the severed neck like vipers! Only then did people realize his insides had long been hollowed out and packed full of briars."

—"...What kind of textbook ghost story is that?!"

—"Hahaha, pretty creepy, right?"

—"Having one's body hollowed out like that is impossible! At most, it would look like this."

—"Look like what...?!"

Zhu Rong chuckled and turned his head, only to see Liu Gong smiling at him—except where his eyes should have been were two gaping black chasms, from which vines were currently squirming outward, swaying at him like waving hands.

—"Holy crap! What the hell?!"-Zhu Rong shouted. He instinctively tried to draw his sword, only to discover his body was frozen stiff as if trapped in [sleep paralysis](# "Literal CN: 鬼压床 (guǐ yā chuáng) — ghost pressing down on the bed; sleep paralysis"), completely incapable of moving a muscle.

[Sleep paralysis?!]

Realizing what was happening, Zhu Rong concentrated all his spiritual energy and forced his eyes wide open—

The gray-blue dawn sky, still retaining the remnants of night, entered his vision.

—"I-it was a dream after all,"-Zhu Rong gasped in heavy, ragged pants.

Hearing a rustling sound beside him, he turned with a relieved laugh:

—"That mutant vine's demonic qi really is formidable. To think I hallucina—"

He froze.

For he saw coils upon coils of vines wrapped tightly around Liu Gong's body.

Zhu Rong instantly moved to draw his sword just as he had in the dream, only to discover in mounting horror that he could not move. And this time, it was not sleep paralysis—his own body was bound in coils of vines, exactly like Liu Gong.

—"Cease your struggling,"-Liu Gong said, having accepted the situation with surprising equanimity:—"Just wait for the medicine."

—"Medicine?"

Zhu Rong asked in bewilderment.

In the next moment, the medicinal fragrance drifting through the air dispelled his terror and cleared his head considerably. From afar, the same soft, leisurely voice carried over:

—"Do not panic, everyone. This is a normal symptom of demonic qi corruption. I have already brewed the decoction; so long as you drink it, regardless of your current condition, you can be rescued."

Thanks to the prime vantage point they had selected the previous night, even while his body remained bound, Zhu Rong could look across to see that pretty boy... no, Senior Brother Lu Shulong, standing gracefully and delicately beside a cauldron filled with bubbling green decoction, gazing upon his surroundings with the exact same gentle, benevolent smile as before.

Yet the disciples surrounding him were in far less graceful shape.

When even Golden Core cultivators like Zhu Rong and Liu Gong had fallen victim, one could easily imagine the plight of disciples with lower cultivation realms.

Some were truly living out his nightmare—parasitized by vines erupting from bodily orifices; some had been half-assimilated, their lower halves or limbs turned completely into vegetation; others showed no external changes but were convulsing violently upon the ground.

The miraculous thing was that, despite such horrific conditions, not a single person had died.

—"S-save me..."-The disciple closest to Lu Shulong struggled forward, reaching out to grasp Lu Shulong's hem, his lower body having already transformed entirely into vines.

—"Certainly,"-Lu Shulong said, pressing an object into the disciple's trembling hand. It was not medicine, but a complete set of specimen collection instruments:—"However, before that, please take a sample from yourself and measure your demonic qi levels. With this firsthand experience, you will surely gain a far deeper appreciation of what impact various concentrations of demonic qi exert upon the body."

—"..."-The disciple stared with wide, incredulous eyes at Lu Shulong's beautiful, delicate visage.

Lu Shulong then turned his gaze toward the others beside him:

—"Go assist those disciples who are temporarily unable to answer the questions, so that they are at least in a condition to get up and complete their homework."

Only then did Zhu Rong realize that every single victim this time was a freshman; the original Alchemy disciples were completely unscathed.

—"Right away, Senior Brother Lu,"-they answered respectfully. Filling spray canisters with potion, they spritzed it onto the faces of several unconscious freshmen, ruthlessly jarring them awake:—"Did you hear that?! If you wish to lessen the agony, finish the assignment first!"

Naturally, 'seniors' also arrived beside Zhu Rong and Liu Gong, who were ensnared in briars.

They obeyed Lu Shulong's orders with absolute, scrupulous fidelity—spraying merely a tiny squirt of potion onto the pair's arm sections. The vines immediately writhed in agony, loosening just enough space for Zhu Rong and Liu Gong to extend a single hand.

—"Here, your tools,"-the senior offered considerately, handing over the instruments.

—"Did you do this on purpose?!"-Zhu Rong demanded through gritted teeth.

—"Hardly on purpose. If you had honestly followed Senior Brother Lu's instructions, you would have been completely fine,"-the disciple scoffed without the slightest pretense of sympathy:—"Yet it seems you didn't follow a single one! Every trap you could trigger, you triggered in full. If not for your sufficiently high cultivation, you would have been turned into [human swine](# "Literal CN: 人彘 (rénzhì) — a mutilated human stump, referencing Empress Lü's horrific punishment of Consort Qi") by now."

—"You are [fishing](# "Literal CN: 钓鱼 (diàoyú) — entrapment; deliberately setting bait to lure fools into a trap")!"-Zhu Rong roared in fury.

—"Precisely, fishing! Senior Brother Lu loves foolish little fish like you the most,"-the disciple chuckled.

Another disciple chimed in:

—"Speaking of which, Senior Brother Lu is truly wicked. Every single time, he intentionally selects campsites beside creatures that appear low in demonic qi but are actually starved half to death and itching to ambush humans. Fishing for disciples is one thing, but he even fishes for demonic entities."

—"? What do you mean?"-Liu Gong asked.

Yet he soon required no explanation, for a shrill, piercing shriek rang out in the distance.

It came from the vine.

—"You promised Master, didn't you? Never to harm humans,"-Lu Shulong said, pouring potion over the vine:—"Bad children who sneak midnight snacks will absolutely not be tolerated!"

As he spoke, he poured the potion without a shred of mercy. The vine thrashed frantically, scattering leaves all over the ground.

Watching it, Zhu Rong felt phantom pains on the plant's behalf.

—"By the way, the territory of the Heavenly Spirit Sect is absolutely secure, because the Immortal Venerable placed seals upon all dangerous creatures here, forbidding them from taking human lives,"-the two seniors took the opportunity to admonish the newly arrived disciples:—"Still, they have to survive! Midnight snacks are inevitable. And because they are starved to the extreme, their ferocity approaches the level of demons near the Abyssal Ocean. You would do well to take note."

Zhu Rong uttered two dry chuckles, obediently conducting specimen sampling on the shivering vine wrapped around his body.

Having passed through this ordeal, everyone in the Alchemy Department was visibly far more "united."

Once they completed their homework and received medical treatment, they followed Lu Shulong toward their next destination as meekly as sheep, daring not voice the slightest objection, leaving behind only the dying, half-dead vine.

Yet the vine's respite did not last long. Barely half a shichen later, another team marched up to its vicinity.

—"Today, we shall discuss the fundamental applications of demon-exorcism arrays. Let us use this vine as our demonstration model. It looks like its demonic qi is just about depleted, so perhaps we can wrap up early."

Ran Deng said lazily.

—"...Shulong and the others did go a bit too far this time. At the very least, they shouldn't keep [plucking wool from the same sheep](# "Literal CN: 逮着一株薅 (dǎizhe yī zhū hāo) — variant of 逮着一只羊薅羊毛, repeatedly milking or exploiting the exact same target")!"

Zhou Hui sighed after learning the full story.

—"Is that the issue here?!"-Lan Pei couldn't help shouting. After hearing the account, even he couldn't help feeling sympathy for this demonic entity.

Wait, no!

That wasn't the issue at all!

Hiding behind the sofa, Lan Pei hollered:

—"You are actually keeping demons?!"

—"It hardly counts as raising them. I merely wished to evaluate their contamination levels,"-Zhou Hui said.

Indeed. To borrow the terminology of another world, demonic entities were a pollutant to the cultivation world.

Generally speaking, spiritual energy, as a form of energy, could be directly absorbed, refined, and nurtured by cultivators, spirit beasts, and spiritual flora alike, generating even more spiritual energy.

According to this principle, spiritual energy should increase the more it was utilized.

In reality, this was not the case. Aside from spells expending spiritual power, purifying demonic qi also consumed vast quantities of spiritual power. In the worst-case scenario, spiritual energy was corrupted by demonic qi, becoming part of a demonic domain.

This invariably brought one to the origins of demonic qi.

It was universally believed that demonic creatures and demonic qi did not belong to this world, typically summoned forth by the negative emotions of living beings. Once they manifested, however, they would severely contaminate the surrounding spiritual energy, converting spiritual energy into demonic qi and polluting the entire region into a demonic domain.

Within a demonic domain, no normal living organism could survive.

Consequently, for cultivators, preventing the genesis of demonic entities was a matter of paramount importance. The major sects monitored their respective territories, dispatching disciples on patrols to eliminate potential sources of resentment capable of summoning demons. When necessary, they would even impose restraints upon mortal governments and rulers, preventing them from exploiting their subjects too ruthlessly and manufacturing battlefields ripe for demon spawning.

The discrimination faced by the demon beast clan stemmed from this very dynamic.

Because beasts were ill-equipped for restraint and relatively prone to indulgence, making them exceptionally susceptible to demonic qi corruption.

Thus, cultivators slaughtered beasts and demons alike.

Yet the cultivators went no further than this.

They never specifically investigated the underlying mechanics of how demonic qi corrupted spiritual energy, nor how to thoroughly eradicate demonic qi.

—The conventional method of purging a demonic domain was simply to slaughter all the demons within, resolve the root cause of resentment, and then seal the entire expanse for several centuries or millennia, leaving time to resolve the problem—or leaving time to ferment even greater trouble.

In truth, Zhou Hui had not originally pondered such lofty environmental concerns either.

The pure reason he researched demonic entities was that certain individuals intentionally absorbed demonic qi and degenerated into demonhood. A tiny minority among them would not be overwhelmed by negative emotions into mindless monstrosities, retaining their sanity to cultivate, become so-called "Demon Venerables," and ascend to the Demon Realm as beings parallel to celestial immortals.

Obsessed with ascension as he was, Zhou Hui had naturally considered this avenue, wondering if it might earn him some [extra credit](# "Literal CN: 加分 (jiā fēn) — bonus exam points, reflecting Zhou Hui's academic obsession").

However, the indulgence of desire provoked by demonic qi was diametrically opposed to Zhou Hui's personal disposition of restraint and patience. Thus, Zhou Hui would never rashly inhale demonic qi. Yet he wondered what consequences would ensue if demons practiced sexual and emotional abstinence. Therefore, he planted the demons, forcing them to suppress their desires to observe the outcome, using the findings as empirical reference to glean insights.

—"Are you a devil?!"-Lan Pei was utterly awestruck after hearing the rationale:—"And you truly have no fear! Incurring qi deviation, being branded a demonic cohort, and getting besieged by the entire cultivation world!"

—"Surely it would not come to that?"

—"How could it not?! Just harboring this notion is unorthodox enough, yet you actually went and executed it! It is a sheer miracle you haven't fallen into demonhood, yet here you are still dreaming of ascending!"-Lan Pei yelled.

—"...What you just said, repeat it?"

—"Dreaming of ascending?"

—"The sentence before that?"

—"Just harboring this notion is unorthodox enough, yet you actually went and executed it! It is a sheer miracle you haven't fallen into demonhood, yet here you are still dreaming of ascending!"-Lan Pei repeated, asking in confusion:—"What is the matter?"

—"No, I merely recalled something exceedingly peculiar,"-Zhou Hui murmured, touching his lips.

In the Heavenly Dao's script, Mo Xi's crime was colluding with demons, correct? That was why the protagonist annihilated the Heavenly Spirit Sect under this banner, and not a single soul in the cultivation world stepped forward to defend them—yet thinking carefully, that was an exceptionally flimsy pretext.

Because rather than making accusations about Mo Xi, weren't the entities right here on Mount Tianling far easier to convict him with?

Why wasn't the Untiring Immortal Venerable accused of colluding with demons?

Come to think of it, when the protagonist wiped out the Heavenly Spirit Sect, where did these creatures go? Were they truly so well-behaved? Wouldn't they have seized the opportunity to avenge themselves upon him?

Or rather, did someone take them away beforehand?

What kind of demonic entity could make Mo Xi obey so submissively...? He felt as though that name was lingering right on the tip of his tongue.

Zhou Hui pinched one of the demon's eyeballs, lost in thought.

The demonic entity shivered violently, its several eyes practically crying themselves blind.

—"Master?"-Observing this, Qu Qianxing asked:—"Should we simply dispose of this demon? Lest the disciples misunderstand?"

The demon wailed in terror:

—"You cannot do this! You are less humane than demons! We are starving out of our minds, yet we served as experimental subjects so obediently, never doing anything evil—"

—"Enough!"-Zhou Hui plucked off one of its eyes and crushed it to dust.

Black demonic qi instantly corroded his fingertips, leaving behind a dark gray smudge.

This fellow hadn't refrained from doing evil out of virtue; it simply didn't dare.

—"In consideration of your relatively obedient conduct, I shall not [eradicate you root and branch](# "Literal CN: 斩草除根 (zhǎn cǎo chú gēn) — completely wipe out an enemy")—in fact, we may well need to research pollution remediation down the road. However, my disciples will indeed be rather active in the coming days. You had best stay far, far away!"

The demon cried in grievance:

—"W-we want to avoid them too! But all you humans look virtually identical in our eyes; we cannot tell you apart at all!"-Otherwise, would it have charged headlong into the lair of such a terrifying monster?

—"Then provide them with identifying markers... Ah, I remember!"-Zhou Hui looked up.

—"What?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"I remember what I overlooked,"-Zhou Hui said cheerfully:—"School uniforms!"

—"School uniforms? What are those?"

—"Standardized attire for our sect,"-Zhou Hui added, casting a glance at the demon:—"Depending on the occasion, special functions can be incorporated! Such as demon repellent!"

[Is *that* the kind of thing you remembered?!]

[Besides, other sects hunt demons, so why has your Heavenly Spirit Sect done the exact opposite?]

[Are you going to start protecting rare, endangered demons next?]

[Ah, in that case, this old fox will be the first to sign up.]
