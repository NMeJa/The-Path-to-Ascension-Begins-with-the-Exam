# Chapter 83: Calculating Agendas (第83章 算盘)

[—"Master, eleven cultivators have already expressed their intent to collaborate."](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan">)

[—"Here are their names, backgrounds, and primary research directions. Among them, the ones marked with cinnabar have a stronger desire to present, and their research directions are relatively accessible while carrying great topical appeal. I believe they fit our initial need to attract other scholars and cultivate an academic atmosphere."](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan">)

[—"However, I have not agreed to their proposals yet. I request Master to make the final decision."](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan">)

...

Zhou Hui listened to the report with an expression of deep suspicion, while the disciples beside him exchanged bewildered glances, looking distinctly panicked.

There was, of course, nothing wrong with the report itself.

One could even say it was exceptionally thorough and impeccably comprehensive—even if Qu Qianxing, the most outstanding among Zhou Hui's disciples, had handled it, it would have been at this level at best. The sole problem was that this report had been delivered by Yuwen Xuan.

Standing in the center of Zhou Hui's immortal cave, Yuwen Xuan recounted the entire reception of their guests with deadpan solemnity, right down to the details of how he had baited and incited the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys. On the outside, he looked every bit like a normal, dependable Eldest Senior Brother.

Yet such behavior was so unprecedented for Yuwen Xuan that his junior brothers and sisters were terrified, constantly peering outside the immortal cave to check whether the sun had somehow [flown in the wrong direction](<# "Literal CN: 太阳今天是不是飞错了方向 — Chinese equivalent of checking if the sun rose in the west">) today.

Looking at his eldest disciple, Zhou Hui also felt an inexplicable sense of bizarreness.

—He had known the very instant Yuwen Xuan changed back into human form.

After all, Yuwen Xuan's condition during this period had been far too volatile. Without keeping a close eye on him, it would have been disastrous if he were dominated by the flesh and bone of the Taotie, lost his sanity, and devoured an ordinary disciple.

Fortunately, Yuwen Xuan seemed largely unaffected by the transformation. After reverting to human form, he remained as willfully reckless as ever; in fact, right now he appeared slightly more rational and sharp-witted than before...

*Ah, is that how it is?*

[Could this child merely be trying to prove to me that even after devouring the flesh and bone of a Taotie and being [molded into the shape of a juvenile Taotie](<# "Literal CN: 变成了幼年饕餮的形状 — humorous callback to Yuwen Xuan's cursed black lamb form">), he hasn't lost his humanity?]

Thinking this, Zhou Hui could not help but feel a touch of gratification:

It seemed two hundred years of teaching had not gone entirely to waste—Yuwen Xuan at least firmly recognized himself as a "human."

It was just that he almost never [acted like a decent human being](<# "Literal CN: 总不干人事 (zǒng bù gān rénshì) — idiom meaning someone constantly does outrageous, non-human, or unhinged things">).

For instance, during this guest reception, he had not acted like a human at all—he had ruthlessly set up the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys, yet delivered the report with such solemn earnestness. Did this fellow actually believe that giving a proper report meant the matter would just blow over?

Zhou Hui mused to himself.

Once his eldest disciple's report reached a natural pause, he inquired:

—"Why did you play tricks on the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys? They came here to preside over the tournament, not for your personal entertainment."

—"Precisely for that reason, I felt we should let them experience Master's intent in a more grounded fashion!"-Yuwen Xuan replied.-"Just standing by and watching is meaningless. Especially since they are serving as judges, only by rolling up their sleeves and experiencing it personally will they truly take academic presentations seriously."

—"You always have an excuse,"-Zhou Hui said with a faint, knowing smile.

Any other disciple would have felt guilty and reflected on their conduct, but who was Yuwen Xuan? He took Zhou Hui's remark entirely as praise. Had he still been in his little black lamb form, he would have wagged his stubby little tail straight up into the air:

—"Of course! Everything I do is out of utmost consideration for Master!"

Seeing him act this way, Zhou Hui was about to say something, but spotted an origami paper crane fluttering into the cave.

Qu Qianxing intercepted the paper crane, unfolded it, and wore a peculiar expression after skimming its contents.

He immediately reported to Zhou Hui:

—"Master, the Heavenly Dao Alliance has conveyed their apologies. Feeling they underestimated the significance of the Central Domain Immortal Arts Tournament, they intend to dispatch higher-cultivation cultivators to reinforce the delegation."

—"Higher cultivation?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Yes, they plan to send Lord Siwu, who oversees the Grand Sacrificial Rites to Heaven,"-Qu Qianxing said.

—"Ha..."-Zhou Hui muttered slowly.

The tournament's official stature had abruptly ballooned.

Though the Heavenly Dao Alliance held little influence within the Central Domain, it was the legitimate coalition of the cultivation world's orthodox sects. Whenever massive disasters struck—such as an invasion of extraterrestrial demons, demon clan uprisings, or beast tide rampages—it was usually the Alliance that organized the major sects to mount a resistance.

In other words, it was the cultivation world's "officialdom."

Therefore, when Zhou Hui launched the Immortal Arts Tournament, he had casually mentioned it to the Plum-Bamboo Couple, asking the Heavenly Dao Alliance to send a few representatives to bear witness. This counted as an "official certification," lending an air of greater legitimacy to the title of "Central Domain Representative Cultivator."

Yet even so, it had never been intended to be particularly formal.

Tournaments like this were rare in the Central Domain primarily because sects were sparse and scattered. Up in the Northern Domain, where a single mountain peak might host five or six minor sects, contests of all kinds occurred daily; at least half of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's routine business consisted of witnessing inter-sect competitions, making it ordinary daily routine.

Yet dispatching the grand official in charge of celestial rites elevated the tournament's tier by several notches in one stroke.

It suddenly felt as if a commercial exhibition match had been upgraded into an official state tournament.

Naturally, the Heavenly Dao Alliance would not abruptly change its stance without reason. The most likely cause had to be linked to Yuwen Xuan's instigation... no, rather, the "practical experience" he had gifted the envoys.

Zhou Hui and the other disciples turned their gazes toward Yuwen Xuan in unison.

—"Didn't I tell you? Everything I do is out of consideration for Master!"-Yuwen Xuan boasted smugly.

He lifted his invisible stubby tail even higher.

---

As far as the Heavenly Dao Alliance was concerned, the chief of rites' abrupt departure for the Central Domain actually had little to do with Yuwen Xuan's antics.

It was purely a coincidence.

Though strictly speaking, the catalyst truly had begun with Su Xin's entourage.

After returning that day, the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys had braced themselves to contact headquarters, requesting that cultivators of higher cultivation be dispatched to supervise the event.

—Following that "unforgettable" academic presentation, these Soul Formation cultivators simply lacked the courage to sit at the judges' bench evaluating contestants—especially alongside those Body Integration titans. To think that with every evaluation, they would be forced to relive that afternoon's mortifying embarrassment... hell itself was hardly worse than this.

Regarding their request, the Heavenly Dao Alliance had initially brushed it aside.

[—"I heard this tournament is merely a 'game'."](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Steward">)

While titles like "Central Domain Representative Cultivator" sounded grand, they hardly came across as serious, to say nothing of the fact that the prize was simply being cast as a figurine in a set of chess pieces.

The Heavenly Dao Alliance steward sought to reject the envoys' petition:

—"Our Heavenly Dao Alliance is merely providing witness. Your presence on site is more than sufficient."

—"How could it possibly be sufficient?!"-Qi Jie blurted out, his face flushing crimson.

It was just that he would rather die than let anyone learn of what had transpired today.

Qi Jie pivoted to a different angle of justification:

—"When the Patriarch and Matriarch [the Plum-Bamboo Couple] originally dispatched us, it was because they mistakenly assumed the tournament judges would be sect leaders and city lords of the Central Domain, for which our cultivation was appropriately matched. But they have brought out a gathering of Void Refinement and Body Integration grandmasters! We Soul Formation cultivators have no standing to sit alongside them!"

—"Hardly so. You represent the Heavenly Dao Alliance,"-the steward dismissed casually.

As the quasi-official authority of the cultivation realm, if every affair required dispatching cultivators of superior cultivation to succeed, where would their institutional prestige lie?

The steward paused, then gossiped with a chuckle:

—"Still, none of the sect leaders or city lords in the Central Domain took charge of organizing the judges? If they aren't simply indifferent, could it be that they want to participate themselves? Hahaha."

—"I believe it is precisely the latter!"-Qi Jie seized upon the latter half of the remark at once, asserting solemnly:-"You haven't been here, so you don't understand. The Central Domain is taking this extraordinarily seriously! Not only are there spell duels, but they are also ascending the altar to lecture on scriptures. It truly seems they intend to determine the strongest cultivator in the Central Domain."

—"So what? It's not as if you people have to step up and compete,"-the steward replied.

—"Even if we don't have to step up, we must at least be able to command respect and [hold down the fort](<# "Literal CN: 镇得住场子 (zhèn de zhù chǎngzi) — have the gravitas, authority, and status to command the room and maintain control">)!"-Qi Jie argued theatrically.-"Just think about who organized this tournament! The illustrious Untiring Immortal Venerable! This immortal is famed across the cultivation realm for his love of learning—even Sky City's archives cannot compare to the richness of his pavilion! What's more, he has formulated an entirely new paradigm of researching and writing cultivation techniques specifically for this tournament. Without a heavyweight grandmaster present, how could we possibly hold the fort?!"

At this moment, Qi Jie seemed to have completely forgotten his initial sneers about "backwater bumpkins."

This was only natural. Having suffered such a bruising lesson, he wished for nothing more than to hype the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Central Domain as formidable as humanly possible, so that his own earlier defeat would look far less humiliating.

The steward was startled by Qi Jie's impassioned fervor:

—"Is it really that serious?"

—"Of course it's that serious! This tournament is the largest event in the Central Domain in the past century... no, the past millennium! Originally, our Heavenly Dao Alliance had little influence in the Central Domain. If we appear negligent toward this tournament, I fear the Central Domain will refuse to acknowledge our authority!"-Qi Jie warned, playing the fear card.-"To say nothing of the fact that they seem prepared to record all the presentations delivered during this tournament and compile them into a dedicated... journal? That is, an official scholarly publication! Surely you wouldn't want the Heavenly Dao Alliance to be dismissed as a mere passing footnote in such a historical record?"

*...Just what kind of tournament is this anyway?*

*Ascending altars to lecture on the Dao, and authoring dedicated books for it... are you truly hosting an immortal arts tournament, rather than some scholarly conference on cultivation methods?*

*Still, considering it was organized by that legendary Untiring Immortal Venerable, it hardly seemed surprising.*

The steward grumbled inwardly.

Yet, overwhelmed by Qi Jie's relentless momentum, he conceded nonetheless:

—"Fine, fine. I will ask around and see which grandmaster is willing to head over."

However, neither he nor Qi Jie believed they could find someone so quickly.

After all, even within the Heavenly Dao Alliance, powerhouses of the upper three realms were scarce resources, not so easily summoned for errands.

—The Plum-Bamboo Couple might well have been willing, but as peak existences of the cultivation realm, paying an occasional social call to the Untiring Immortal Venerable was one thing; staying long-term at another sect was out of the question. Besides, not even the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself had stepped forward to personally manage the tournament's day-to-day operations!

Therefore, the steward could at most report the matter upward and circulate a notice to the immortal caves of various grandmasters, seeing if anyone was bored enough to accept the commission.

To everyone's surprise, barely had the steward posted the commission when a response arrived from the Ceremony Court.

Word had it that deep within the Fiendgod ruins of the Abyss Sea, the old eccentrics of the Eastern Hedge Garden had unearthed an artifact suspected to be an ancient [deity-descending relic](<# "Literal CN: 降神之物 (jiàngshén zhī wù) — an ancient sacred vessel or medium used to channel or summon deities from higher celestial planes">) from White Jade Capital. As the presiding authority over the tri-centennial [Grand Sacrificial Rites to Heaven](<# "Literal CN: 祭天大典 (jìtiān dàdiǎn) — supreme ceremonial rite held every 300 years by the Heavenly Dao Alliance to worship the Heavenly Dao">), the Ceremony Court of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had been invited to inspect it.

It was precisely at this juncture that they received the commission to visit the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable? That Tribulation Transcending powerhouse renowned for being [untiring in learning](<# "Literal CN: 学而不倦 (xué ér bù juàn) — from the Analects of Confucius: 'learning without satiety, teaching without tiring'">)? Does he intend to seize this occasion to consolidate the Central Domain's cultivation world?"-Lord Siwu, Head of the Ceremony Court, laughed softly.-"Speaking of which, I have never had an opportunity to pay him a visit. This makes for a fine occasion. However, being fond of learning and possessing genuine scholarship are two entirely different matters. I hope he does not disappoint me."

---

Zhou Hui showed very little interest in the upcoming visit from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's supreme ceremonial official.

—"Mmh, if it can expand the reach of our academic conference, that is naturally well and good, but there is no great rush,"-Zhou Hui remarked.-"We have only just laid the groundwork here. Taking our time is perfectly fine."

[Master, you just said "academic conference," didn't you! You really are [hanging a sheep's head to sell dog meat](<# "Literal CN: 挂羊头卖狗肉 (guà yángtóu mài gǒuròu) — bait-and-switch; peddling one thing under the banner of another">), using an immortal arts tournament as a facade to push an academic symposium!]

The disciples thought to themselves, thoroughly accustomed to such revelations... No, they still grumbled inwardly; after all, apart from Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye, none of the other disciples had the slightest desire to be saddled with a mountain of research reports.

In truth, even Qu Qianxing and Guan Ye felt a slight headache over the prospect.

Yet the disciples did not realize that they had no need to stress so much at their current stage. Zhou Hui saw little reason to make these kids write endless dissertations before [completing their undergraduate studies](<# "Literal CN: 本科（X）毕业 — humorous academic parallel to undergraduate graduation">). After all, they had not even properly mastered their fundamental curriculum yet! What could they possibly research? For this stage, mastering the thesis formatting structure and practicing a few mock exercises was more than enough.

Calculating thus, Zhou Hui reviewed the other disciples' assignments, conducted a "simple" question-and-answer session, and brought the day's instruction to a close.

Just as the disciples were preparing to depart, Zhou Hui spoke up:

—"By the way, Yuwen Xuan, Lu Shulong, you two stay behind..."

—"Master, I was completely well-behaved this time!"-Yuwen Xuan protested on the spot.

Lu Shulong looked rather uneasy and apprehensive as well.

—"I hardly consider inciting Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys to be 'well-behaved,'"-Zhou Hui roasted dryly. However, keeping these two behind was not to settle scores.

Once the other disciples had exited, Zhou Hui said directly to the pair:

—"I require a drop of your [essence heart-blood](<# "Literal CN: 心头血 (xīntóuxuè) — the most concentrated vital essence drawn directly from a cultivator's heart, holding their soul brand and life force">)."

This was an extraordinarily severe, almost outrageous demand.

In a cultivation realm where merely knowing someone's "True Name" was enough to orchestrate their demise, if essence heart-blood fell into another's hands, being slain, mind-controlled, or refined into a puppet were the mildest outcomes. Every aspect of their existence—from soul to flesh—would fall under total dominion.

Hence, even ferocious demon beasts subjected to having their essence heart-blood extracted would choose self-detonation to taint the blood, to say nothing of human beings possessing far greater pride—

However, neither Yuwen Xuan nor Lu Shulong truly counted as human.

Upon hearing their Master's demand, their eyes widened slightly. Then, without the faintest hesitation, both shut their eyes, circulated their spiritual energy, and coughed up a single drop of blood.

Yuwen Xuan even looked somewhat relieved.

—"Master, what do you need this blood for?"-After expelling that drop of blood, his face turned deathly pale. Clearly, even with a Taotie soul that had devoured the bones of a Taotie, expelling essence heart-blood took a severe toll on his vitality.

Yet Yuwen Xuan still had the energy to joke with his Master:

—"If you want to control me, you don't need to go through all this trouble. Just tie a leash around me."

—"Rest assured, I truly have considered forging a pair of chiming bells for you two [specifically referring to the two sheep],"-Zhou Hui remarked ambiguously. Using jade vessels prepared in advance, he gingerly gathered the two drops of essence heart-blood.-"However, this blood is for research. I recently obtained a legacy that might be suitable for your cultivation."

That legacy was, of course, the ancient heritage extracted from the soul of the white sheep (Taotie).

Zhou Hui had long fretted over how to teach these two disciples with non-human constitutions—a dilemma for which the Library Pavilion held no answers. This legacy resolved many hurdles, but Zhou Hui was human after all; he could not guarantee with absolute certainty that his comprehension was entirely flawless, which was why he needed two drops of blood to conduct experiments.

Hearing Zhou Hui's intention, Lu Shulong's eyes lit up like stars:

—"Master, with this technique, will I be able to break through my current cultivation and revert to my true form more often?"

Lu Shulong's current cultivation realm was the Golden Core stage—on the surface.

In reality, as a True Dragon, Lu Shulong had possessed Soul Formation stage cultivation from the moment of his birth. Regrettably, the ambient spiritual energy of this mortal realm was insufficient to sustain a True Dragon's hereditary technique, forcibly suppressing him into his present diminished state.

Thus, Lu Shulong had always felt like someone forcibly crammed inside a cramped, suffocating box, able to revert to his true form only on rare occasions.

Acquiring a new cultivation technique meant that this box could at least become a little larger.

Zhou Hui glanced at Lu Shulong's pale yet ecstatic face, reluctant to let him harbor expectations that were too high:

—"I am still researching it. It may not necessarily be usable for you."

After all, it was a Taotie legacy.

Though mythology claimed the Taotie shared blood ties with dragon kind, no one knew how much of it was genuinely compatible.

Yet Lu Shulong harbored not the slightest doubt regarding Zhou Hui's abilities:

—"Master will never fail!"

—"No, I fail all the time. Think about it: how many tribulations have I undergone?"-Zhou Hui sighed. Had he truly been incapable of failure, he would never have chosen to abandon ascension.

—"Yet no one else could ever 'fail' so many times and remain alive!"-Lu Shulong protested. To him, Zhou Hui's tribulations did not count as failures in the slightest. Especially from the perspective of dragon kind, so long as one weathered the Nine Heavens Lightning Tribulation without perishing, it counted as a triumphant success.

—"Failing to ascend is failure,"-Zhou Hui replied plainly.

He produced a porcelain vial of medicinal pills and thrust it into the arms of Lu Shulong, who was still trying to argue:

—"Enough. Extracting essence heart-blood must have exhausted you. For the next [ten-day cycle](<# "Literal CN: 一旬 (yī xún) — a standard ten-day period in the traditional lunar calendar">), rest properly inside your immortal cave. You are excused from writing this cycle's research report."

—"Really?!"-Lu Shulong gasped in ecstatic shock.

His expression was actually even more radiant than when he had just heard he might receive a compatible cultivation technique.

*Is it really that extreme?*

Beholding this, Zhou Hui could not help but self-reflect for a single second on whether he usually assigned these children far too much homework. But turning his head, he saw Yuwen Xuan extending an open palm toward him.

—"What is it?"-Zhou Hui asked, staring at Yuwen Xuan's flattened palm.

—"Master, where is my share?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

—"You cannot take medicinal pills."

—"Eh?"

—"Have you forgotten what state you are currently in?"-Zhou Hui stated pitilessly.-"Finish eating that [pancake](<# "Literal CN: 那块饼 (nà kuài bǐng) — referring to the corpse-ash donut pancake (骨灰煎饼) looped around Yuwen Xuan's neck to force his physical reconstruction">) first before we talk."

—"Pancake?"-Lu Shulong asked curiously.

Yuwen Xuan clamped his mouth shut instantly.

There was no way in hell he would ever let his junior brother find out he had been turned into a little lamb!

—"All right, neither of you should run around during this period,"-Zhou Hui said, feeling a twinge of heartache at the pale complexions of his two disciples.-"Having extracted essence heart-blood, you are both in a weakened state. Avoid exerting your strength as much as possible and meditate properly—given your constitutions, you should simply sleep soundly until you are fully recovered."

—"Yes,"-Yuwen Xuan and Lu Shulong answered respectfully.

---

Regarding Zhou Hui's arrangements, Lu Shulong naturally obeyed without the slightest reservation.

Exiting his Master's immortal cave, he immediately flew obediently toward his own abode, resolving to consume the medicinal pills, submerge himself in the spirit spring, and sleep through his entire holiday without interruption.

However, Lu Shulong suddenly noticed that the direction Yuwen Xuan was flying was entirely wrong—it looked nothing like heading toward his immortal cave.

—"Eldest Senior Brother, where are you going?"-Lu Shulong called out.

—"Finding a bite to eat."

—"But didn't Master just tell you not to eat strange things?"-Lu Shulong asked suspiciously.

Yuwen Xuan halted abruptly in midair and caught hold of Lu Shulong.

—"Master forbade me from snacking because impure spiritual energy might affect my constitution. But upon closer thought, pure True Spirit qi is not only harmless to me, but tremendously beneficial!"-Pinching a lock of hair by Lu Shulong's temple, Yuwen Xuan bared his teeth with a beaming grin.-"Conveniently, Fifth Junior Brother, why don't you let me take a bite?"

—"...I-I am going back to rest!"-Lu Shulong cried in sheer panic.

Yanking himself forcefully from Yuwen Xuan's grip, he bolted toward his immortal cave at top speed, not daring to pry into this terrifying Eldest Senior Brother's itinerary for another second.

Yuwen Xuan let out a cold snort.

He raised his hand and looked down: several icy-blue strands of hair were coiled around his fingertips.

These had been left behind in flustered haste when Lu Shulong tore away.

Yuwen Xuan gave them an exploratory lick, then crunched them down with crisp snaps.

Much like their color, they were chilly with a faint taste of crushed ice. Worthy of being part of a True Dragon, they were indeed exceptionally delicious.

—"A pity this cannot be eaten,"-Yuwen Xuan sighed.-"If I could swallow an entire one whole, I wouldn't have to worry about power at all... But then everything I wish to protect would vanish completely too, wouldn't it!"

Yuwen Xuan let out a deep, heavy sigh, gazing in the direction of his Master's immortal cave.

He stared wistfully for several minutes before finally turning around and slowly drifting toward his own immortal cave.

Yet while "this Yuwen Xuan" was flying, *another* Yuwen Xuan materialized right at the Academic Hall of the Palace of Silence.

—"Excellent. It seems Master's divine sense didn't follow,"-Yuwen Xuan murmured with a relieved sigh.

Naturally, *he* was the "true Yuwen Xuan."

As the eldest disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yuwen Xuan treated moving about the sect almost like playing a stealth game; judging from his practiced maneuvers, he was intimately familiar with the routine—so thoroughly familiar that he even knew how to deceive a Tribulation Transcending Master.

Right now, without attracting a single soul's notice, he slipped into the Academic Hall, completely evading the senses of several high-tier cultivators who were still gathered discussing matters, and reached the back of the lecture podium.

Precisely the spot where those Soul Formation cultivators from the Heavenly Dao Alliance had stood a few days ago.

Standing in that exact position, Yuwen Xuan reached deftly beneath the podium.

He fished out several pitch-black, squirming things from underneath.

The creatures wriggled their bodies, vaguely resembling small mice, yet a closer inspection revealed they had no physical substance—they were merely condensations of black mist.

—"They truly bred: ['Resentment Bugs'](<# "Literal CN: 怨气虫 (yuànqì chóng) — spiritual vermin coalesced from the lingering resentment and humiliation of high-level cultivators">),"-Yuwen Xuan whispered gleefully, licking his lips.-"Scared me half to death today. When Master kept me behind, I thought I had been exposed!"

Yes, *this* was the true reason Yuwen Xuan had set those people up.

Resentment Bugs.

In the cultivation realm, the emotions of living beings possessed intrinsic power and could even coalesce into independent entities. For example, dying with overwhelming rancor could birth vengeful wraiths, while harboring excessive malice could trigger qi deviation into inner demons.

Generally speaking, the emotions of most living beings were not so violent, merely leaving ordinary ethereal residue behind. These "Resentment Bugs" were precisely such emotional residue; the higher a cultivator's realm, the more prominent the traces they left behind.

Such tiny bugs posed virtually no danger and would naturally dissipate after a few days if left alone. But to a Taotie, they served a very particular purpose.

Do not misunderstand—they were not meant to be eaten.

Bugs that minuscule would not even suffice to [fill the gaps between Yuwen Xuan's teeth](<# "Literal CN: 塞牙缝 (sāi yáfèng) — idiom for a portion so tiny it wouldn't even get stuck in one's teeth">).

—"The neighbor's meat patty should be almost ready, shouldn't it?"-Carrying these bugs, Yuwen Xuan slipped out of the Academic Hall, singing under his breath as he strolled:—"Chop it fine, coat with flour, dip in liquid, [the kid next door can hardly wait](<# "Literal CN: 隔壁的孩子快要等不及了 — parody of the viral Chinese culinary meme: 'coat in flour and egg wash, fry until golden, and the kid next door is crying from cravings' (裹上面包糠，隔壁小孩都馋哭了)">)~~"

He sauntered all the way to the Water Mirror Pavilion, picked out a water mirror, tossed the bugs straight inside, and flashed a deeply satisfied grin.

And on the opposite side of the water mirror, there shockingly emerged the face of Yan Bailang, Sect Leader of Thousand Beasts Hall!

---

Yan Bailang scratched at his cheek.

Just now, an inexplicable itch had crawled across his face, as though tiny insects were creeping over his skin.

Naturally, that was impossible.

As a Body Integration cultivator, not even stray dust could adhere to his flesh, let alone bugs.

Yet he felt inexplicably restless and irritable.

It was likely due to "that thing"—for some unknown reason, "that thing" had been roaring incessantly of late, filling him with an unrelenting sense of dread.

—"Sect Master?"-Seeming to sense his unease, Elder Bai Jun, who stood beside him delivering a report, called out in puzzlement.

—"No, it is nothing,"-Yan Bailang shook his head.-"Where were we?"

—"Regarding the Immortal Arts Tournament,"-Bai Jun continued reporting, though confusion lingered on his face.-"It has now been confirmed that Cloud City, Eternal Villa, and the Thousand Harmony Gang have all participated to varying degrees. As one of the preeminent sects in the Central Domain, for our Thousand Beasts Hall to make no gesture whatsoever... wouldn't that be somewhat improper?"
