# Chapter 200: Offense and Defense of the Spiritual Energy Field

[Matters are looking somewhat unfavorable!]

Lin Qi thought inwardly.

The moment the wind shifted across the spectator stands, Lin Qi keenly noticed it.

Although Lin Qi hadn't detected Ran Deng's petty maneuvers—Ran Deng's steering had been entirely too 'physical,' devoid of any spellcraft or magical methods, thereby evading Lin Qi's perception—judging purely by Guan Xinguang's abysmal performance, a deterioration in public sentiment was inevitable. It was just that this public opinion had fermented entirely too quickly; at the very least, it should have been triggered only after Guan Xinguang committed another catastrophic blunder.

Naturally, Lin Qi couldn't care less about Guan Xinguang's public reputation; the man's total ruin and disgrace had nothing to do with him.

The trouble was that Guan Xinguang cared—and cared to an extraordinary degree.

Cultivation's severing of emotions and desires seemed to have only severed Guan Xinguang's physiological 'desires,' yet failed completely to sever his psychological 'desires.' Rather, precisely because he had possessed zero conviction or ideals from the very beginning, once his physiological desires vanished, his meaning of existence as a living creature disappeared alongside them; consequently, he took that very last remaining shred of vanity and treated it as his own 'Dao.'

Fools like this were plentiful, whether in the lower realms or the Heavenly Court.

Lin Qi never denied that he, too, was much the same.

Lin Qi knew full well that people like himself (and Guan Xinguang), upon discovering they were being discussed and questioned behind their backs, would inevitably fly into a furious rage, followed by flustered panic, exhausting every possible scheme to salvage their reputation and break free from their immediate predicament—even going so far as fleeing to abandon the exam. In fact, Guan Xinguang had nearly done precisely that just moments ago. Had Lin Qi not intervened and Guan Xinguang dropped out mid-competition, even Ran Deng likely would have been helpless to stop him. One could only say Guan Xinguang had cultivated considerable expertise in managing his own public prestige.

Yet if matters unfolded like that, it would pose an immense disruption to Lin Qi, who had yet to secure the Dao Bone.

—"It won't do without shedding a bit of blood,"-Lin Qi muttered.

Yet sensing the spiritual energy of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and contemplating the potential resources of this world, he ultimately steeled his resolve:

—"Very well, this world is well worth the risk of an attempt,"-Lin Qi decided.

Calculating thus, he began focusing his consciousness.

Under the command of his focused mind, his physical body—still lingering in the upper realm—stirred into motion.

—As an aside, Lin Qi's physical flesh did not reside in the Heavenly Court, but rather in the Immortal-Demon Realm.

Because the "time lag" between the Heavenly Court and this world was entirely too immense: whether it was his consciousness issuing commands to the body in the Heavenly Court, or the body sending feedback back to his consciousness, a gap of mere minutes up above would cause a temporal discrepancy of several two-hour shichen down below—altogether too inconvenient.

To prevent such an occurrence, Lin Qi had made a special trip to the Immortal-Demon Realm prior to descending to the mortal world.

The 'Immortal-Demon Realm' was merely a broad, general geographical concept. It was actually the collective name for a vast expanse of sub-spaces nestled adjacent to the Heavenly Court's peripheries. Because this space connected directly to the Celestial Realm, it possessed abundant celestial spiritual energy; however, because it was riddled with holes and riddled with spatial rifts connecting to numerous minor worlds and even the Demon Domain—all while shifting continuously—the Heavenly Court was unable to exert absolute dominion over it, and demons even inhabited it.

Nevertheless, the 'Celestial Emperors' who governed the Immortal-Demon Realm obeyed the edicts of the Heavenly Court. Although they lacked formal bureaucratic appointments, they enjoyed the perks and welfare of the Heavenly Court while being unbound by celestial restrictions. Furthermore, positions were held by the principle of 'survival of the fittest'; in terms of raw power, status, and prestige, they were vastly superior to orthodox civil servants of modest rank like Lin Qi.

Consequently, the Immortal-Demon Realm could be regarded as an extension of the Heavenly Court's domain, and numerous minor worlds treated it as a transit hub on the path toward the Heavenly Court.

For instance, cultivators from minor worlds where spiritual energy was scarce and the maximum cultivation ceiling was hard-locked at Nascent Soul or Soul Formation could, if they managed to locate a suitable spatial conduit, first reach the Immortal-Demon Realm. There, they could cultivate to higher realms before attempting ascension to the Heavenly Court.

For this reason, the Immortal-Demon Realm was not only a melting pot of diverse inhabitants, but its territories and spatial pocket dimensions were exceedingly varied.

On this occasion, Lin Qi had traveled to a location within the Immortal-Demon Realm that lay closest to his target destination. The flow of time here was virtually identical to the world he intended to visit, making it exceedingly convenient for Lin Qi to exert control.

At this moment, Lin Qi's physical flesh sat inside a small pavilion atop an immortal mountain shrouded in swirling spiritual mist within the Immortal-Demon Realm.

Around the exterior of the pavilion, seventy-two defensive formations had been laid out. They served not only to ward off blind demon beasts, fiends, and human immortals from wandering in to disturb him, but also to seal away Lin Qi's aura entirely so outside observers would detect nothing.

One had to admit: in order to descend to the mortal world (and [slack off on the job](<# "Literal CN: 摸鱼 (mō yú) — slacking off / quiet quitting during paid work">)), Lin Qi's preparations had been remarkably thorough.

Thus, the moment Lin Qi's divine soul shifted his thoughts, his physical flesh opened its eyes in real time.

And what Lin Qi meant by "shedding a bit of blood" was quite literally, physically "shedding blood." His eyes were vacant and devoid of focus; in a wooden, dazed manner, he raised his arm, popped a fingertip into his mouth, and bit through the skin.

Do not be fooled by the seemingly meager amount of blood shed. Once a cultivator reached the Soul Formation stage, their divine soul and physical body merged into one, largely devoid of mortal 'blood circulation' in the ordinary sense. For a True Immortal, this was even more pronounced: their physical flesh and divine soul were both high-energy bodies, distinguished only by function.

Consequently, a single drop of a True Immortal's blood was equivalent to the heart's blood of divine beasts like True Dragons. With proper handling, a solitary drop possessed sufficient power to obliterate an entire minor world.

Conversely, if this drop of blood fell into the hands of an enemy of comparable strength, that True Immortal's vital gate would be seized by the opponent.

In this regard, Lin Qi had genuinely [staked a bloody fortune](<# "Literal CN: 下了‘血本’ (xiàle xuèběn) — invested heavily / literally paid in blood capital">).

He held aloft his bitten finger. An abnormal drop of blood tinged with a pale golden luster trickled from his fingertip, falling toward the layered sea of clouds below.

Yet before it could truly touch the clouds, the droplet vanished without a trace.

Accompanying this gesture from the physical body in the Immortal-Demon Realm, countless golden threads sprouted outward from the crown of Guan Xinguang's skull. Like the marionette strings of a puppeteer, some pierced into every joint of Guan Xinguang's body, while others hovered in midair, seemingly assessing new targets.

Regrettably, with so many supreme titans present—virtually the realm's pinnacle combat power gathered in one place—none appeared able to perceive these eerie golden threads. At the very least, not a single person displayed any reaction, including Guan Xinguang himself, who was thoroughly bound by them.

Lin Qi was evidently quite smug regarding this:

[After all, my blood was refined using the blood of [Redacted](<# "Literal CN: 【】 — a taboo supreme celestial entity whose name is redacted/unutterable">)! Had I not done so, how could I have ever fooled those bastards in the Bureau of Heavenly Dao?]

Thinking this inwardly, he did not rashly deploy the golden threads to attack anyone other than Guan Xinguang. He merely guided a single thread with his divine sense, tentatively probing toward the highest tier of the spectator stands where the Untiring Immortal Venerable resided.

Lin Qi's attitude toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable had always been exceedingly prudent.

Although he outwardly acted as if he didn't care about the man, deliberately pretending he couldn't even remember his name, Lin Qi knew in reality that beings possessing a divine spark in the lower realms were almost universally hazardous to cross; there was no telling if they possessed bizarre divine abilities that even a True Immortal could not withstand.

Especially cultivators like this—those who were not originally gods, but acquired a divine spark later in life.

After all, most primitive aboriginal deities of the lower realms had never seen the world. In certain isolated minor worlds, the horizons of local gods were comparable to wild beast tribes, easily swindled by outsiders. Cultivators of the cultivation realm, however, were an entirely different breed: they cultivated from the outset with the explicit ambition of reaching the Heavenly Court, making them reserve cadets for Heavenly Court civil servants. Those capable of ascending were fundamentally monster-tier; those who managed to snag a divine spark midway were even more terrifying—monsters among monsters. Furthermore, they would never readily surrender their weaknesses to anyone, even if the visitor was an emissary from the Heavenly Court.

Therefore, divine spark possessors like Untiring were either branded evil deities by the Heavenly Court, besieged by celestial soldiers and heavenly generals, and smothered in the cradle; or they were co-opted and granted celestial amnesty by the Heavenly Court, soaring to the heavens to become existences that even "connected insiders" like Lin Qi could never hope to touch. Those blessed with exceptional luck (or rather, possessing exceptionally useful divine sparks) could even become full-fledged deities governing the Heavenly Court itself.

As a Heavenly General, Lin Qi was naturally well-versed in such affairs.

Following standard administrative protocol, he should have instructed Ren Ran to file a report via the 'Divine Advent' conduit at the earliest possible moment, leaving it for the Heavenly Court to handle. However, Lin Qi, firstly, had no desire to expose the fact that he had descended to the mortal realm to slack off on paid leave; secondly, he salivated over the resources of this world and didn't want the Heavenly Court noticing this place too early; and thirdly, he possessed immense confidence in himself.

Although Lin Qi was cautious, he believed his own strength was by no means inferior to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's.

[Isn't it just a divine spark?]

[As a Heavenly General, have I slain few 'evil gods'?]

[It's purely because my true physical body cannot enter this world, preventing me from unleashing my full power, that's all!]

[Once I obtain the Dao Bone, never mind a divine spark, even a True God... well, better not bring up True Gods. As a Heavenly Court civil servant, one must refrain from voicing unharmonious remarks.]

Pondering these idle thoughts, Lin Qi gingerly maneuvered the golden thread, creeping up toward the apex of the spectator stands to observe what the Untiring Immortal Venerable was doing.

Yet whether by coincidence or not, the instant the Untiring Immortal Venerable entered Lin Qi's line of sight, the Immortal Venerable's gaze happened to glance right toward the direction of the golden thread.

The golden thread shuddered violently and whipped back in a flash.

Not only this golden thread, but all the other golden threads dancing in the air dared not attack a single cultivator, snapping back into Lin Qi's divine soul in one unified breath.

[...No reaction. He probably didn't notice? But at this level, locking eyes is hardly a coincidence. He might have sensed something on instinct. Before confirming the Dao Bone is securely in hand, it's best not to invite unnecessary complications.]

Lin Qi reasoned.

He was, after all, a 'Heavenly General' who had fought the Demon Realm for years; he still possessed that much composure.

However, his motion of extending the golden threads and retracting them startled Guan Xinguang, whom he possessed. The man was a Mahayana cultivator after all, not trash all the way to the core; excessive fluctuations of power were still perceptible to him, even if his awareness was rather dull.

—"Lord Heavenly General, what is the matter? Just now, I felt as though your spiritual energy experienced some turbulence?"-Guan Xinguang asked tremulously through divine sense.

—"Did it affect your artifact refinement?"-Lin Qi demanded.

—"No,"-Guan Xinguang replied.

—"Then continue!"-Lin Qi commanded rudely.-"The current spiritual energy field is still not large enough! You must expand it further!"

Saying so, he drove several golden threads even deeper into Guan Xinguang's soul.

Guan Xinguang instantly jolted, his spirit reinvigorated.

He felt no discomfort whatsoever; rather, he felt his mental faculties growing sharper and clearer. Vaguely, he could even perceive the voices echoing from the isolated spectator stands across the barrier.

Yet the emotions and words he perceived seemed rather different from what Lin Qi had perceived.

The emotions Guan Xinguang sensed were positive: there was concern over his examination platform's plunge, encouragement for his perseverance in refining, and anticipation for the artifact he was forging. In Guan Xinguang's eyes, none of these sentiments were surprising. As Chief Director of the Department of Works, enjoying the support and adulation of the masses was only natural. It was just that interspersed among them were several discordant noises he despised:

[—"Tsk, to think he actually lost to a Foundation Establishment cultivator."](<# "Speaker: Spectator Hallucination">)

[—"Don't talk nonsense; isn't the exam not over yet?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Hallucination">)

[—"Still, his platform plummeted right out of the sky; how does he have the face to continue? Even if he finishes, he won't have forged anything worthwhile—won't that just be a score of zero? Isn't that losing to Foundation Establishment?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Hallucination">)

[—"Heeheehee, did Lord Chief Director come all the way to the Heavenly Spirit Sect just to take home a [goose egg](<# "Literal CN: 拿鸭蛋 (ná yādàn) — literally 'taking a duck egg,' scoring a zero">)?"](<# "Speaker: Spectator Hallucination">)

[What kind of damned nonsense is this?!]

Blue veins bulged across Guan Xinguang's temples.

He believed the plunge of his platform had been due to carelessness! ...Yes, it was entirely caused by being overly careless and underestimating his opponent! It didn't mean his artifact refinement skills were inferior to that brat's! No, rather, no matter what kind of genius the other party was, he was still ultimately a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator! Thus, even if the plunge of his platform had inflicted minor damage upon the materials in his cauldron and degraded the finished product's quality, that finished artifact was still far beyond anything a Foundation Establishment brat's craftsmanship could ever hope to [fraudulently match](<# "Literal CN: 碰瓷 (pèngcí) — staged collision / fraudulent comparison")!

Instantly, Guan Xinguang's fury began spreading. Temporarily forgetting his resentment over being coerced by Lin Qi, an internally generated urge blossomed within him—a desperate desire to complete this refinement and silence those discordant voices forever.

—"Lord Heavenly General, rest assured,"-Guan Xinguang gritted his teeth.-"I will not only generate the most powerful spiritual energy field, but also forge the most peerless magical treasure!"

—"I have zero interest in what you forge,"-Lin Qi replied with unmasked contempt.

—"I will make every last one of you understand that I am the greatest artifact refiner in this realm!"-Guan Xinguang declared, staring fixedly into the furnace fire.

He seemed to be replying to Lin Qi, yet also speaking to someone else entirely. His glazed eyes burned with unprecedented, fanatical fervor.

[At least there's no need to worry about this coward suddenly fleeing!]

Lin Qi snorted coldly, yet out of lingering unease, he drove several additional golden threads into him before sweeping his perception toward the spiritual energy field outside.

It had to be said, there was a touch of regret. Had Lin Qi not possessed Guan Xinguang's body and instead remained attached to Ren Ran's hairpin, he wouldn't have needed to painstakingly sense the spiritual energies through divine sense; like the rest of the audience, he could have visually perceived the arena's spiritual energy field distribution in direct, intuitive fashion.

Fortunately, Guan Xinguang was a Mahayana cultivator after all.

Just as the man believed himself: if one strictly compared technical artifact refinement finesse, he might not be formidable; but [sufficient thrust can make a brick fly](<# "Literal CN: 力大砖飞 (lì dà zhuān fēi) — with enough brute force, even a brick can achieve flight")! His Mahayana cultivation base was right here. Ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivators... no, ordinary low-to-mid-tier cultivators, and even common refiners below Mahayana, could never hope to surpass him.

And under Guan Xinguang's frantic efforts, the expanse of the spiritual energy field steadily expanded in accordance with Lin Qi's wishes.

For a time, the violet-black spiritual energy field virtually blanketed the entire testing grounds, forcing Ran Deng to dial the "display opacity" of the spiritual field significantly higher; otherwise, the entire arena would have been obscured from sight.

Yet even if visible, there wasn't much worth watching.

Suppressed by Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field, the other examination platforms were pushed down to the absolute bottom of the arena. It was solely due to the protection of the defensive formations that this oppressive field didn't cause the other examinees to fail completely—operating on the pure philosophy of [if I can't test well, nobody else gets to test well either](<# "Literal CN: 我考不好，大家也别想考好 — if I do poorly, I'll drag everyone down with me")!

Fortunately, those examinees had their senses shielded by the platform arrays, remaining oblivious to what was happening outside. Consequently, up until now, not a single one had raised objections or abandoned the exam. Moreover, those who qualified for the Rank 8 testing grounds were largely genuine artifact refinement masters adept at handling unforeseen crises. They likely assumed this turbulent spiritual interference was merely an official part of the examination syllabus, striving desperately to adapt to the unexpected hazards. Regrettably, their spiritual energy still fell short of a Mahayana titan's by a full caliber, rendering them incapable of confronting the spiritual field's pressure head-on.

Under such circumstances, Platform Forty-Four—the sole platform still suspended aloft in midair—became glaringly conspicuous.

The master of this platform possessed merely a Foundation Establishment cultivation base, yet had likewise generated a layer of golden-crimson spiritual energy field.

That spiritual energy field was not large, fitting well with its master's Foundation Establishment cultivation, barely wrapping around the platform itself. Yet it was extraordinarily stable and solid, resembling the toughest shell of a dragon's egg, nearly impossible to shatter from without.

Even though the spiritual field generated by Guan Xinguang had completely encircled it, not a single fissure had appeared.

Instead, the two spiritual energy fields incessantly collided and scraped against one another, producing a horrifying sound like fingernails clawing down glass. Anyone listening closely for even a few moments would feel their ears going deaf.

[Truly a useless piece of trash,] Lin Qi mused inwardly.

He didn't bother continuing to provoke Guan Xinguang this time—the upper limit of a waste's abilities was only so high; no matter how much one whipped him, nothing new would come out. Instead, utilizing the golden threads as his own sensory tentacles, he let them creep outward along the spiritual energy field until they grazed the crimson spiritual field of Platform Forty-Four.

[Just a Foundation Establishment cultivator. No matter how much of a genius he is, even if he can generate his own spiritual field, it cannot possibly be flawless. There must be a loophole, right?] Lin Qi calculated.

This was also the very reason he had continuously egged Guan Xinguang on to expand his spiritual energy field:

Lin Qi required the collision and friction of the two spiritual fields. Only then could he disrupt Platform Forty-Four and force a fissure to form:

Whether a loophole in the formation array, or a psychological vulnerability formed under relentless disruption in Guan Ye's mind.

As long as a crack emerged, Lin Qi was confident he could slide his tentacles within, invading Guan Ye's mind just as he had possessed Guan Xinguang, seizing control of his physical body, and subsequently extracting Guan Ye's spine.

Yet after "probing" with the golden threads for ages, Lin Qi failed to detect the faintest trace of a crack—in every sense of the word, none existed!

[How is this possible?! He's merely a Foundation Establishment cultivator?!] Lin Qi seethed in impotent fury. [Just how on earth is he doing this?!]

\*

Not only was Lin Qi growing furious, but the other 'spectators,' after observing for quite some time, inevitably found their gaze converging upon that lone beacon of golden-crimson light amidst the violet haze.

Fairy Maogui voiced the collective curiosity of the crowd:

—"Your Excellency Ran Deng, Platform Forty-Four seems completely unaffected by the collisions! I wonder what your junior brother is forging? He seems remarkably steady!"-Fairy Maogui inquired.

—"Mmh. His mental fortitude has always been exceedingly steady,"-Ran Deng replied.

—"What is he forging? His spiritual energy field appears extraordinarily steady as well,"-Fairy Maogui pressed.

—"His spiritual energy field is indeed very steady,"-Ran Deng echoed.

—"...So, what is he forging?! The raw materials seem quite exceptional!"-Fairy Maogui pressed further.

—"His technique is indeed exceptionally steady..."-Ran Deng murmured.

Her eyes were glued to Guan Ye's platform; she was visibly more worried about the clash of spiritual fields than Guan Ye himself.

Primarily, the commotion was simply too terrifying. Ran Deng even felt a prick of regret, wondering whether her arrangements had pushed Guan Ye a bit too far.

[But what is wrong with Guan Xinguang?!]

[Has he lost his damned mind?]

[Why on earth would he make a move against Guan Ye in broad daylight before the entire crowd?]

[If he wants to court his own destruction, that's his business—but don't drag my junior brother down with him!]

Recalling the intelligence she had gathered, Ran Deng felt Guan Xinguang wasn't this kind of person no matter how she looked at it; thus, she was rather distracted.

Seeing Ran Deng like this, Fairy Maogui understood her feelings.

Smiling bitterly, she was about to round out her commentary duties herself when the voice of the Untiring Immortal Venerable drifted over once more from the spectator stands:

—"Ye-zi is... Guan Ye is refining a segment of dragon tendon,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Dragon tendon?"-Fairy Maogui hurriedly responded, carefully perceiving the spiritual energy across the testing grounds.-"Indeed, there is unmistakably the aura of dragon breath! It appears to be the tendon of a Flame Dragon. No wonder he initially attempted to deploy Samadhi True Fire; the mutated Liuding Divine Fire is even more suitable! Is it precisely because of the harmonious pairing of flame and material that such an impregnable spiritual field was formed?"

\*

[Flame Dragon Tendon?]

[So that's how it is! I was wondering how on earth a Foundation Establishment cultivator could possess such a stable spiritual field!]

[If he is relying on external power, that explains everything.]

Lin Qi had naturally overheard the exchange between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Fairy Maogui. He promptly relayed it to Guan Xinguang:

—"Knowing what the other party is refining should be of assistance to you, yes?"-Lin Qi asked.

—"Naturally!"-Guan Xinguang replied, though a flicker of confusion crossed his face.-"Dragon tendon, eh... why does that sound somewhat familiar?"

He had completely forgotten what excuse he had used back then when extracting Guan Ye's spine.

Yet to him, that had never been important in the first place. Guan Xinguang's feverish brain was hardly suited for deep contemplation right now; he only desired to defeat Guan Ye as quickly as possible, making that tactless Foundation Establishment brat who dared climb above his head plunge from the sky without delay.

Thinking thus, Guan Xinguang rummaged briefly through his materials and tossed another ingredient into the refining cauldron—one capable of countering a Flame Dragon's power.

To be frank, this material was completely incompatible with the flying sword Guan Xinguang was originally forging and would degrade the finished quality; but a hot-headed Guan Xinguang couldn't care less about that right now.

As the material was added, Guan Xinguang's already violet spiritual energy field took on a deep azure tint, resembling some sort of virulent venom, appearing even more sinister and ominous.

When such a spiritual energy field collided against that crimson spiritual field once more, it produced a sloshing, bubbling sound akin to a swamp. The outer edge of the crimson spiritual field seemed finally unable to endure the corrosion of this energy, beginning to dissolve.

[—"Fourth Junior Brother?!"](<# "Speaker: Ran Deng">)

A worried exclamation drifted faintly from the commentary booth.

That was irrelevant.

—"It's finally beginning to dissolve!"-Lin Qi uttered an exclamation of depleted patience.

Without waiting for the crimson spiritual field to dissolve completely, his golden threads concealed themselves within Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field, scrambling competitively to reach into the interior of Platform Forty-Four.

And then—

—"AWOOOOOGH!"-Lin Qi shrieked in agony inside Guan Xinguang's mind, jolting Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy into disarray.

—"L-Lord Heavenly General?!"-Guan Xinguang cried out.

—"It's eroding... the spiritual energy field is eroding..."-Lin Qi's agonized voice rang out.

—"Yes, exactly! My spiritual energy field is currently eroding and dismantling that brat's spiritual field..."-Guan Xinguang began.

—"[Bullshit!](<# "Literal CN: 放P (fàng pì) — talking shit / farting">)"-Lin Qi's enraged roar exploded once more inside Guan Xinguang's brain, rattling his very brain matter.-"The one being eroded is *your* spiritual energy field! Do you not feel a single damned thing?!"

Guan Xinguang naturally felt nothing. Although a spiritual energy field was the outward spillover of spiritual energy during artifact refinement—and severely damaging it would indeed inflict certain harm upon the refiner—this phenomenon was ultimately not physically connected to the refiner's flesh. Under "minor" erosion without massive spiritual field depletion, a refiner wouldn't necessarily notice right away.

Yet on Lin Qi's end, the agony was bone-deep and heart-wrenching.

His 'golden threads' were manifested from his immortal blood; they were literally part of his physical flesh, and the most sensitive part at that! Consequently, this was virtually akin to having bamboo slivers driven beneath his fingernails, causing him to scream almost uncontrollably while frantically attempting to retract his golden threads.

Yet the rate at which those golden threads were eroded was even faster than Guan Xinguang's spiritual energy field; they were incinerated to ash in a heartbeat! Had Lin Qi not reacted with lightning speed and severed the threads midway, the fire would have burned straight into his core divine soul.

And as if that weren't enough, those dozen-odd golden threads acted like conduits. Originally, the crimson spiritual field and the violet-blue spiritual field merely collided and scraped against one another, eroding bit by bit from the points of contact. Because the violet-blue spiritual field was massive in volume, this erosion was so gradual it was imperceptible. But with these golden threads serving as conduits, the crimson spiritual energy instantly found 'straws' into the violet-blue field, siphoning inside along the trajectory of the threads and accelerating the rate of erosion exponentially!

Lin Qi was genuinely unwilling to acknowledge his own blunder. Having suffered a disastrous loss, he threw all pretensions of True Immortal dignity to the wind, screeching at Guan Xinguang:

—"What on earth did you do?! Why has he gained the upper hand instead?!"-Lin Qi roared.

—"N-No, I... I clearly used materials that counter him!"-Guan Xinguang stammered in utter panic.

Saying so, he unfolded his divine sense to inspect his spiritual energy field carefully, only to discover that his field was indeed suffering severe spiritual energy depletion. Yet this depletion wasn't the "erosion" Lin Qi had claimed, but rather...

—"Refinement!"-Guan Xinguang sensed the activity at Platform Forty-Four, his face turning an ashen iron-gray.-"He is refining it!"

—"What difference does that make?"-Lin Qi demanded dismissively.

Guan Xinguang, however, believed there was a fundamental difference:

—"He is a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator; his own spiritual energy is nowhere near sufficient to refine dragon tendon! Therefore, he is purifying and refining all ambient spiritual energy around him that can possibly be refined!"-Guan Xinguang explained in dread.

Thus, compared to the impure, chaotic spiritual field formed from Guan Xinguang's melted materials, Lin Qi's pure celestial immortal energy was precisely what the other party needed most!

Having puzzled out the theory, Guan Xinguang found it all the more impossible to accept:

—"But... how on earth did he pull this off?!"-Guan Xinguang shrieked in his heart.

To think that with a mere Foundation Establishment body, he was actively refining the power of a Mahayana titan—and even a True Immortal!
