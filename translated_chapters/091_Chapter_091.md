# Chapter 91: Daily Life in the Heavenly Spirit Sect (第91章 天灵派的日常)

Regardless of whatever misunderstanding Zhou Hui had developed, Qingyang Qingyu was at last safely staying in the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Qingyu adapted to life in the Heavenly Spirit Sect astonishingly quickly.

This fact surprised even Qingyu herself immensely.

Back at Eternal Villa, she was accustomed to living in pampered luxury; packing for every outing was practically akin to [relocating her entire household](<# "Literal CN: 搬家 (bānjiā) — moving house">). Even so, when visiting the most exorbitantly opulent Sky City, there were still aspects that left her somewhat dissatisfied.

For this "exchange" to study at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, however, hearing that not even the Untiring Immortal Venerable had any habit of ordering immortal boys or fairy handmaidens about, she naturally could not overstep Master as a visiting disciple. Thus, for the very first time in her life, Qingyu did not bring personal maidservants to wait upon her. By that same token, she merely packed everyday necessities like pillows and teacups, leaving behind that magnificent portable immortal cave-dwelling gifted to her by her young uncle.

Fortunately, she had left it behind; otherwise, she never would have discovered that choosing an immortal cave in an ordinary sect could be so fascinating.

That day, after temporarily dodging the second exam and savoring the delectable spiritual delicacies prepared by Fifth Disciple Lu Shulong—exquisite dishes rarely tasted even within Eternal Villa (and according to Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, Eldest Senior Brother's cooking was even more divine, leaving Qingyang Qingyu completely unable to imagine what heavenly feast that could possibly be)—Qingyang Qingyu followed Ran Deng to select her cave-dwelling.

At this time, thanks to the [brick-moving laborers](<# "Literal CN: 搬砖工人 (bānzhuān gōngrén) — slang for construction workers / manual laborers">) unexpectedly recruited from the Immortals' Bazaar, the "Heavenly Spirit Sect Phase One Cave-Dwelling Construction Project"—built for high-realm disciples within the sect—had already reached completion.

In theory, this phase of construction had budgeted a generous surplus of cave-dwellings. Based on the quota of high-realm disciples recruited the previous year, it would have been more than sufficient to accommodate three more minor admissions cycles.

Who could have anticipated that, with the convening of the Immortal Art Conference, high-realm immortals from every domain would flood into the Heavenly Spirit Sect, many arriving in tow with their own disciples? The available cave-dwellings instantly [stretched taut at the seams](<# "Literal CN: 捉襟见肘 (zhuō jīn jiàn zhǒu) — pulling sleeves to expose elbows; severely strained for resources / shortage">), forcing the Heavenly Spirit Sect to hastily launch "Phase Two Construction."

Regarding this, Ran Deng was already utterly numb.

She had seen right through it all: what were family expectations and parental hopes compared to this? A Master who had (allegedly) renounced ascension was the true root cause of all her stress and endless workload!

First came the renovations of the Library Pavilion and Buyan Palace; then Qianxing Hall and the public bathhouses; followed by the spiritual energy canals and the Phase One cave-dwelling project. Just when she thought she was finally done, the winter vacation brought along the Blood Demon Hall interior decoration project, closely succeeded by the Phase Two cave-dwelling project, and finally... Heavenly Spirit Sect had no "finally." Ran Deng was thoroughly convinced that the moment this was wrapped up, Master would have brand-new homework waiting for her to complete.

From grinding through all these civil engineering projects, Ran Deng had barely cultivated all year, yet her cultivation had forcibly broken through the Golden Core Middle Stage into the Golden Core Late Stage, her spiritual energy so concentrated and dense it bordered on viscous.

Looking at it this way, Ran Deng actually felt grateful that her father had dispatched several clansmen to serve as her deputies.

Just as Master had said: as long as she delegated the tasks downward to keep those clansmen's workload thoroughly saturated, they would have zero spare time to urge Ran Deng to "make progress."

Naturally, as a consummate slacker, Ran Deng would hardly remain satisfied with just this.

Her ideal was not working at all! What was the point of doing repetitive labor for an entire year?!

Having realized this, Ran Deng summarized her year-end work, only to discover that aside from the spiritual canal construction her clansmen had taken upon themselves to accept on her behalf, whether it was Qianxing Hall, the Immortals' Bazaar, or the cave-dwellings, she had been endlessly repeating the construction of living facilities and commercial facilities.

Among them, Qianxing Hall and the public bathhouses were tolerable—they qualified as "landmark architecture," so bespoke designs were warranted. But the retail storefronts and cave-dwellings contained substantial design redundancies; she had only performed customized adjustments due to specific commissions from fellow disciples.

—"Dammit, we should have planned them uniformly like the beginner class dormitories,"-Ran Deng grumbled with regret.-"Or at the very least rolled out a few baseline cave-dwelling blueprints and let them choose for themselves."

Yet complaining was merely complaining; Ran Deng had not actually done so.

She knew that in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, slacking off was permitted, but slacking off without a shred of technical sophistication would only lead to bitter remorse down the line.

For instance, building cave-dwellings: Phase One was built for sect disciples, so slacking off slightly was of little consequence. But Phase Two was designated for high-realm cultivators arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect for the Immortal Art Conference. Not only were those seniors powerful, but they also came with [mountains of fussy bullshit demands](<# "Literal CN: P事 (pī shì) — slang for petty, annoying, troublesome trifles / fussy drama">). If anything fell short of their standards, it was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's face on the line!

It was precisely because Ran Deng saw through such matters with crystalline clarity that she could never truly [lie flat](<# "Literal CN: 躺平 (tǎngpíng) — modern slang: lying flat; completely slacking off and giving up on societal rat races">).

Still, solutions were always devised by human ingenuity.

As that saying went: [laziness drives human progress](<# "Literal CN: 懒惰使人进步 — modern adage: laziness motivates automation and technological convenience">).

This time, Ran Deng was firmly resolved not to design cave-dwellings room by room for each individual. Wanting to slack off yet embarrassed to bother Master for guidance, she gritted her teeth and combed through the catalog of books Master had deposited in the Library Pavilion. Miraculously, she actually discovered a breakthrough solution within a historical chronicle of toys.

This time, with a new martial sister joining the sect, Ran Deng accompanied her to select her future residence:

—"Take a look around the Heavenly Spirit Sect and see if there's any area you fancy; you're free to choose,"-Ran Deng said, unfurling a topographical map of the sect for her to inspect.-"However, I recommend the green zones. These spots boast rich spiritual energy, gorgeous scenery, and are close to the sect center—truly ideal."

Ordinary disciples, naturally, enjoyed no such privilege.

Even disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's advanced class were restricted to designated zones.

But Qingyang Qingyu counted as half a direct disciple, and since she was not settling down here permanently, Ran Deng let her choose at will.

At first, Qingyang Qingyu didn't consider this treatment particularly extraordinary; she enjoyed the exact same privilege back at Eternal Villa.

She did not choose any spot near the direct disciples' caves. Back at Eternal Villa, she would never want visiting relatives staying right beside her private quarters; maintaining such social distance was an unspoken rule among cultivation clans.

Yet Qingyu did not pick any of the prime locations recommended by Ran Deng either. Instead, she gazed toward the outer perimeter of Mount Tianling:
—"The spiritual energy here is also quite vigorous, and there is a vast underground karst cavern? It looks rather wonderful."

—"The location is a bit remote,"-Ran Deng remarked, somewhat surprised by her choice.

—"Doesn't the Heavenly Spirit Sect have the Sundial Clock?"-Qingyu smiled. Having just experienced the sect's automated transit array, she found it astonishingly time-saving.-"Living anywhere is practically identical."

—"In that case, let us choose your cave-dwelling!"-Ran Deng said, pressing no further.

She had always lacked curiosity regarding other people's personal choices, and harbored zero desire to interfere.

Hearing this, Qingyang Qingyu knitted her delicate brows, inquiring:
—"May I construct my own cave-dwelling?"

Aside from ancient clans like Eternal Villa that passed down ancestral cave-dwellings across generations, Qingyu had assumed other sects simply permitted cultivators to excavate their own residences!

Most sects indeed operated that way.

Only a handful of inner-sect disciples and direct disciples inherited cave-dwellings bequeathed by their elders. Once other disciples scouted a suitable living site within sect territory, most simply dug out a cave for themselves (otherwise, why call it a "cave-dwelling"?) and gradually furnished it over time.

While Qingyang Qingyu had refrained from bringing her ostentatious portable cave out of consideration for the Heavenly Spirit Sect's culture, her various furniture and luggage packed several storage pouches to the brim—enough to completely redecorate four or five caves. She naturally preferred utilizing her own furnishings.

Ran Deng was thoroughly accustomed to such mindsets.

Never mind a Golden Core junior like Qingyu; which of the Void Refinement and Body Integration grandmasters arriving at Mount Tianling recently didn't possess an astronomical hoard of personal treasures? If they were allowed to excavate indiscriminately, the Heavenly Spirit Sect would have been dug into an unmitigated disaster.

Ran Deng offered a practiced explanation:
—"Our sect's spiritual spring plumbing is connected directly into every individual cave-dwelling. As such, the structural layouts must adhere to strict regulatory guidelines. However, we have several architectural frameworks available for selection."

Speaking thus, she led Qingyu to inspect the cave-dwelling scale models.

Young Miss Qingyu examined the models with a polite smile, offering no appraisal—evidently rather underwhelmed.

Ran Deng had anticipated this outcome long ago. Once the models were shown, she added:
—"If none of these suit your taste, you may select modular blocks and assemble it yourself."

—"Modules? Assembly?"-Qingyu echoed in bewilderment.

Rather than explaining with words, Ran Deng picked up a scale model, popped the bathroom section right off, plucked the study section from another model, and snapped them cleanly together.

—"Different rooms can be freely combined at will?"-Qingyu's interest was instantly piqued.

Yet Ran Deng's design was clearly far more ingenious than merely snapping pre-built rooms together. Applying a squeeze of spiritual force to a room module, she disassembled it cleanly into dozens of cubic interlocking puzzle blocks.

Ran Deng took one block and slotted it into the study module's flooring; in the blink of an eye, the study expanded significantly in scale.

—"In addition, there are modules for windows, doors, wallpapers, alchemy cauldrons, spiritual herb plots, and so forth available for selection,"-Ran Deng said with a grin, gesturing to a compact toolbox filled with interlocking template blocks.-"Every incoming disciple receives one free toolbox according to their station, but additional ones... *cough cough*, apologies, force of habit—Junior Sister Qingyu may take as many as she pleases."

—"Adhering to standard sect protocol is perfectly fine as well,"-Qingyu replied with a smile, tacitly accepting the title of "Junior Sister."

In the cultivation world, female cultivators suffered no age discrimination, but seniority hierarchy was paramount. In most sects, senior brothers and senior sisters possessed the authority to discipline junior disciples on Master's behalf, and in critical situations could even issue direct commands. Holding the status of "Senior Sister" naturally conferred a distinct advantage.

Ran Deng and Qingyang Qingyu were nearly identical in age and cultivation base. Under normal circumstances, vying for the position of "Senior Sister" would have easily prompted a formal spar. Yet as Qingyu was merely a visiting exchange disciple after all, she took a graceful step back.

Ran Deng was actually indifferent, but she knew that if she called Qingyu "Senior Sister," Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother would pester her to death later on. For the sake of a peaceful, indolent life, she had brazenly claimed the senior title; seeing Qingyu accept it without fuss, she secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

The two maidens exchanged a subtle smile, establishing an initial mutual rapport.

With that resolved, the underlying verbal sparring ceased to matter.

Qingyang Qingyu was thoroughly enchanted by Ran Deng's demonstration. Having never experienced assembling an immortal cave with her own hands, she asked eagerly:
—"May I give it a try?"

—"Of course,"-Ran Deng said.-"Though wait a moment; I need to simulate the environment of your selected site."

Saying this, she wheeled out a rectangular glass aquarium tank.

Through some arcane mechanism, the mountain range Qingyang Qingyu had chosen materialized within the glass tank, and following Qingyu's direction, the projection locked onto the subterranean karst cavern.

—"Are you certain? Subterranean ventilation is quite poor,"-Ran Deng asked once more.

She found this new junior sister's architectural taste rather peculiar.

—"Right here shall suffice; I prefer enclosed spaces. It is far quieter,"-Qingyu said.

Taking the toolbox from Ran Deng, she began assembling the components rather clumsily.

Once she began fitting the pieces together, Qingyang Qingyu realized that the "civil engineering" of an immortal cave was somewhat more arduous than she had imagined. Having to directly interface with both the spirit spring pipeline and external ventilation ducts imposed strict constraints.

[Is this why arbitrary excavation is strictly forbidden?]-Qingyu thought.-[Yet this is genuinely delightful. Just like a toy, I could easily play with this for hours.]

Facing an enthusiastic Qingyang Qingyu, Ran Deng did not rush her, waiting quietly at her side.

In fact, most of the high-realm cultivators and their disciples arriving at Mount Tianling recently had opted to "DIY assemble" their own cave-dwellings. Several had grown so utterly addicted that they spent exorbitant sums purchasing expansion kits.

Upon catching wind of this, Mo Xi had even proposed standardizing the modular sets and selling them in categorized batches at the Immortals' Bazaar. Word had it they sold like wildfire—a [total unscrupulous merchant](<# "Literal CN: 妥妥的奸商 (tuǒtuǒ de jiānshāng) — an outright predatory/shrewd merchant">) through and through.

Nevertheless, Ran Deng had scouted quite a few exceptionally creative talents among those modular DIY cultivators, hiring them to design cave-dwellings to slash her own workload—a flawless win-win.

What brought Ran Deng the greatest pride was that Master had actually commended her upon learning of this initiative.

Having tasted the sweet fruits of success, Ran Deng practiced her [salted fish slacker lifestyle](<# "Literal CN: 咸鱼 (xiányú) — salted fish; slang for a complete slacker">) with even greater righteousness. Gazing at her new "junior sister," she secretly hoped Qingyu possessed architectural flair so she could recruit yet another wage-slave laborer.

Alas, though Qingyang Qingyu was intrigued, it was merely the novelty of a young lady receiving a charming new plaything. With Qingyu's formidable mental discipline, she refused to become ensnared.

After tinkering for a short while, Qingyu recalled her true objective and resolved not to squander precious time on this "toy."

Cleverly selecting several pre-designed rooms that met her general approval, she used the puzzle blocks to modify details she disliked, reshaped them slightly, snapped them together, and placed the completed assembly into the glass tank.

Thus, a cave-dwelling was fully constructed.

Ran Deng looked on with slight disappointment:
—"You won't design it entirely from scratch? Your aesthetic sensibilities seem rather splendid!"

—"['Every domain has its own specialization,'](<# "Literal CN: 术业有专攻 (shù yè yǒu zhuān gōng) — from Han Yu's 'On the Teacher': different people possess mastery in different specialized disciplines">)"-Qingyang Qingyu replied with a gracious smile.-"I trust Senior Sister Ran's designs are supreme; I merely need to tailor minor details to match my living habits."

Ran Deng was visibly impervious to such commercial flattery.

She subsequently instructed Qingyu on how to utilize her temporary spirit token within the Heavenly Spirit Sect's "System" to calibrate Sundial Clock schedules—marking the first time Qingyu formed a concrete concept of the "System" she was slated to study. Then, lifting the glass tank, Ran Deng teleported alongside Qingyu directly to the selected cave location.

Inside that subterranean cavern, the freshly constructed cave-dwelling was already waiting in place.

—"There we go. If you are dissatisfied with any part of the cave in the future, you may modify it at will,"-Ran Deng said, setting the glass tank down at Qingyu's designated coordinates.-"However, external connecting pipelines bear defensive restrictions; those alone cannot be altered. If you absolutely must change them, write down an explanation of reasons and submit it to the Artifact Refinement Hall. They will dispatch disciples to assist with modifications."

—"Understood. Thank you, Senior Sister,"-Qingyu smiled, gazing fondly at the miniature glass tank model she could hardly bear to set down.

Ran Deng briefed Qingyu on a few more administrative details. Seeing there was nothing else, she politely declined Qingyang Qingyu's hospitality, and too indolent even to wait for the Sundial Clock transit, flew straight back to Mount Tianling upon her flying sword.

Qingyang Qingyu's "cave-dwelling decoration," however, was far from over.

After all, the hard furnishings were merely the foundation; the subsequent soft furnishings were the true key to transforming a dwelling into "one's personal sanctuary."

For this phase, there was no need to trouble the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—That said, Ran Deng had mentioned upon departure that the Immortals' Bazaar offered an extensive array of decor goods, which were selling furiously as visiting foreign cultivators settled in.

But the eldest young miss of the Qingyang Clan naturally held no regard for common market wares; the luxurious furnishings she had brought in her storage pouches finally found their purpose.

Without exerting undue effort, Qingyu decorated the entire cave according to her familiar habits back at Eternal Villa until it closely mirrored her original boudoir. The sole chamber she redesigned from scratch was the one she valued above all else: her meditation room.

Qingyu positioned the meditation chamber right in the center of the right-hand karst cavern, assembling it entirely out of transparent crystal glass. Subsequently, she spent an exorbitant sum of spirit stones petitioning Ran Deng to install a specialized spiritual vein formation to assist her cultivation.

Ran Deng had naturally agreed without hesitation, though she felt somewhat puzzled:
—"Why build it entirely out of crystal glass, though? This cave is located inside a sealed subterranean cavern with zero scenery to view. Besides, the array you requested is a starry sky formation; being unable to interface with the outside world might not be ideal."

At the time, Qingyu had merely smiled without answering.

Now, after meticulously arranging her cultivation implements—meditation cushions, incense burners, and the like—she stepped outside the glass meditation chamber, opened a pouch, scattered its contents across the cavern floor, and began channeling her cultivation technique.

Water and wood spiritual energies instantly surged with vibrant life.

Flowing through the cavern in rhythmic harmony, dense carpets of moss sprouted forth across the cavern walls and floor under their nourishment.

Yet this was evidently no ordinary moss. It was speckled with starlight, shimmering with radiant spiritual energy and luminescence—[Star Moss](<# "Literal CN: 星苔 (xīngtái) — Star Moss; an exceptionally rare bioluminescent spiritual moss from deep Southern Domain subterranean caves">). This moss was extraordinarily rare, harvestable only from a few deep subterranean caverns in the Southern Domain. It demanded both a humid environment and dense spiritual qi; even a tiny pinch could fetch an astronomical sum at high-end auctions.

—"I have long wanted to try cultivating it myself; I never expected to find the perfect opportunity here,"-Qingyu smiled with satisfaction.

Qingyu stepped back inside the glass meditation chamber.

Looking outward now, the entire chamber appeared as though suspended amidst the infinite cosmos, while harmonizing flawlessly with the starry sky array Ran Deng had installed, maintaining the cultivation qi circulation at peak perfection.

Furthermore, such an environment was entirely immune to the passage of solar hours or weather.

Qingyu nodded in deep contentment.

Unable to wait another moment, she seated herself upon the prayer mat, eager to experience her brand-new meditation sanctuary.

And that single meditation session lasted an entire night.

Were it not for the fact that she had to report to the Untiring Immortal Venerable for study afterward, Qingyu would have readily opted to enter closed-door seclusion right then and there.

---

In this manner, Qingyang Qingyu's daily routine at the Heavenly Spirit Sect was firmly established.

She concluded her nightly meditation at the first quarter of the Mao hour (5:15 AM), soaked in the cave's spiritual spring for fifteen minutes, and rode the Sundial Clock transit over to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's abode.

At this hour, morning lectures had not yet commenced at Buyan Palace, allowing her to [freeload a warm breakfast](<# "Literal CN: 混一顿早餐 (hùn yí dùn zǎocān) — to casually join in and score a free breakfast">).

During the first few days, the curriculum assigned to Qingyu was not excessively deep, primarily focusing on diagnostic exams and familiarizing herself with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's cultivation methodologies. With Qingyu's formidable aptitude, she mastered it with effortless ease.

During these sessions, she would interact with Immortal Venerable's direct disciples—especially Mo Xi, who spent most of his time by Master's side.

At the sixth quarter of the Si hour (10:30 AM), the morning lessons concluded.

Qingyu joined the Untiring Immortal Venerable to savor a lunch crafted by Lu Shulong.

Her afternoons were delightfully free: she could read in the Library Pavilion, meditate in the Academic Hall, or relax in the bathhouse garden.

Around the You hour (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM), Qingyu would return to her cave after a refreshing bath.

Occasionally she would bring back a cup of Yibei Tea; at other times, she would use a miniature alchemy cauldron purchased at the Heavenly Spirit Sect dubbed the "[Wellness Pot](<# "Literal CN: 养生壶 (yǎngshēng hú) — health-preserving kettle / wellness pot; modern Chinese automated electric brewing kettle adapted to cultivation">)" to automatically brew medicinal tea that augmented her cultivation, drinking it before commencing meditation until dawn.

[Living on one's own isn't that difficult after all!]

The eldest young miss of the Qingyang Clan thought with smug pride, even wishing those clan elders who had lectured her—saying *"your life self-care skills are lacking; you are bound to suffer if wed into the Northern Domain"*—could come witness her splendid independence.

Thus, Qingyang Qingyu happily spent half a month at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Not until the day before hands-on practicum was slated to begin did Qingyu, prompted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's offhand remark—"I promised Excellency Qingyang"—suddenly realize: having stayed for so long, she had completely forgotten to visit her own clan ancestor!

That afternoon, Qingyu nervously hurried through Qianxing Hall. In no mood to browse the Immortals' Bazaar, she arrived at Villa Master Qingyang's temporary cave-dwelling, bracing herself for a thorough reprimand from her ancestor.

Seeing Qingyu, Villa Master Qingyang wore an expression of sheer panic:
—"What are you doing here?! Did you slack off in your studies and get chased back by the Untiring Immortal Venerable?!"

—"How could that be possible?"-Qingyu asked, furrowing her brow.-"I merely thought of how long it had been since I saw you, and came to pay my respects..."

—"I am perfectly fine; I don't need your visits!"-Villa Master Qingyang barked.

Pondering for a moment, he nervously admonished her:
—"Study diligently with Lord Immortal Venerable and showcase your best self! Do not get kicked out for poor performance! But also study slowly and master everything thoroughly—the longer you stay, the better! Do not worry about me or the villa; we wholeheartedly support you studying indefinitely!"

[...Ancestor, don't you realize these two demands are completely contradictory?]

[Studying well means finishing quickly and returning home! Studying slowly means failing to master the material and stalling for time!]

[Ancestor, what on earth do you want from me?!]

Qingyu felt her ancestor's mental state was somewhat unhinged.

Just as she was about to speak, a strikingly handsome youth called out from within:
—"Lord Qingyang, it is time for refinement."

—"Right away, right away!"-Villa Master Qingyang replied in a fluster.

Without even pausing to exchange pleasantries, he merely waved a shooing gesture at Qingyu before scurrying back into the alchemy chamber.

[Just who is the master, and who is the disciple here?!]

Qingyu was struck momentarily speechless by her ancestor's behavior. Then, sensing a gaze resting upon her, she noticed that the gorgeous youth had not departed immediately; instead, he was scrutinizing her with a discerning, appraising stare.

Qingyu naturally knew who this striking youth was.

He was Qu Qianxing.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple "exchanged" for Qingyu.

Unlike Qingyang Qingyu, who [had no desire to return home](<# "Literal CN: 乐不思蜀 (lè bù sī shǔ) — so thoroughly enjoying oneself that one forgets home">), this Second Senior Brother of the Heavenly Spirit Sect hurried back to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's cave-dwelling every single day to make an appearance. Because he was always dashing in and out in haste, aside from his remarkably handsome face, Qingyu had developed no deep impression of him.

Even so, pinned beneath those striking eyes, Qingyu felt a flicker of nervousness.

She suddenly remembered her ancestor remarking in a letter that Qu Qianxing was "not bad." Whenever the ancestor phrased it like that, he undeniably harbored matchmaking ambitions.

[Oh no, I have zero interest in that!]

[Though... considering the encyclopedic erudition of the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's marvelous facilities, it isn't entirely out of the question... wait, what?]

Just as Qingyu was pondering this, she saw Qu Qianxing flash her a smile.

That was by no means a benevolent smile.

Catching that smirk, Qingyu felt utterly disdained.

Only then did Qu Qianxing amble leisurely into the alchemy chamber, stepping up to the alchemy furnace with casual nonchalance.

When cultivators operated an alchemy furnace, they strictly forbade outside interference. Moreover, Qu Qianxing had arrived a moment late. Under ordinary circumstances, anyone behaving like this would have been violently lambasted by the ancestor—even Eternal Villa's most favored disciples would have been [cursed until dog's blood drenched their heads](<# "Literal CN: 骂得狗血喷头 (mà de gǒuxuè pēntóu) — berated mercilessly">).

Yet Qu Qianxing blended seamlessly into the process as though he were an indispensable cog in the machine. Her own clan ancestor even grasped Qu Qianxing's hand:
—"Qianxing-er, come help me inspect the dosage for this..."

[...So that's how it is!]

The razor-sharp Qingyang Qingyu instantly understood the reason behind her ancestor's contradictory instructions.

And she deciphered what Qu Qianxing's mocking smirk meant:

*—I can make the master of your Eternal Villa need me this desperately; so how fares your performance over at our Heavenly Spirit Sect?*

[Fascinating!]

Flinging her sleeves, Qingyu stormed back to her cave-dwelling in a burning huff.

The following day, the Qingyang Qingyu greeting the Untiring Immortal Venerable appeared outwardly identical to her usual self.

Zhou Hui thus remarked without concern:
—"Today, as discussed yesterday, I shall take you to the secret realm for hands-on practicum—"

—"Yes, Master Immortal Venerable, I have made thorough preparations according to your instructions,"-Qingyu said.

Slapping her storage pouch, a glittering mountain of top-grade magical artifacts cascaded onto the floor.

Five complete sets, no less.

Not only sets for Qingyang Qingyu herself and the Untiring Immortal Venerable, but even Mo Xi and Lu Shulong—who had tag-along freeloaded the class—received a set each, with yet another set prepared as a spare.

What was more, a pinnacle-tier [Cloud Chariot](<# "Literal CN: 云车 (yúnchē) — luxurious flying chariot traveling upon clouds">) of the cultivation world was parked grandly outside Immortal Venerable's cave-dwelling.

—"Master, allow me to drive the chariot!"-Qingyu offered with demure sweetness.

*Hmph!* If it were a contest of being "[attentive and tender-hearted](<# "Literal CN: 善解人意 (shàn jiě rén yì) — empathetic, considerate, adept at anticipating others' moods and comfort">)," she, Qingyang Qingyu, might indeed fall short of Qu Qianxing.

But as the eldest young miss of Eternal Villa, why on earth should she compete in waitressing and catering to people?! If they were going to compete, let them compete by [smashing spirit stones!](<# "Literal CN: 砸灵石 (zá língshí) — flexing with cash / pay-to-win whale spending">)
