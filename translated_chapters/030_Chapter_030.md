# Chapter 30: An Undisputed Victory (第30章 毫无疑问的胜利)

How, exactly, did the secret realm come to recognize Qu Qianxing as its master?

Qu Qianxing's stance was simple: as a [straight-A honor student](# "Literal CN: 学霸 (xuébà) — straight-A student / academic overachiever"), if he couldn't even solve a problem he had reviewed before the exam, he might as well resign as Second Senior Brother. Just like Eldest Senior Brother, he should simply pick an aesthetically pleasing branch on the mountain gate's peach tree and string himself up by the neck to call it a day.

After all, formations capable of constituting a secret realm were nothing more than a few dozen fundamental arrays nested repeatedly within one another. In fact, the more intricate the secret realm, the more obvious the formation core became in order to successfully stitch the disparate arrays together.

This sort of theory was something Qu Qianxing had long since mastered down to the marrow.

It was merely that a month ago, he had lacked hands-on trial experience.

For instance, while the Life Gate within a secret realm was certainly an escape route, in certain realms the spiritual flora and fauna surrounding the Life Gate would flourish even more abundantly; without proper preparation, it was actually far more perilous than the Death Gate.

Or for another instance, with identical Ice Formations, depending on the placement of the array eyes, the shapes of the frost flowers condensed would exhibit minute variations.

Or for yet another instance, an excessive pursuit of mechanical contrivance effects for magical artifacts inside a secret realm would necessitate an unusually massive array eye—which, in turn, meant that so long as one seized control of the flow of energy, such realms were actually far easier to commandeer than others.

And this secret realm happened to possess every single one of those issues.

Except Qu Qianxing had already [suffered a humiliating crash](# "Literal CN: 翻车 (fānchē) — modern internet slang: overturned cart, meaning catastrophic failure / blunder") on the chessboard once before; how could he possibly crash twice in a place like this? Not to mention, the coordinates where they entered the secret realm were exceptionally brilliant.

They were stationed directly atop a floating island at the realm's apex that commanded an unobstructed view of the entire layout.

Thus, the moment they stepped inside, the disciples following behind shivered in unison:

[—"So cold!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"And so high up!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Whoa, is this Junior Sister Mo's hometown? Look at all the Snow-Glow Grass!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

Indeed, including the floating island itself, looking down from this vantage point revealed the secret realm blanketed in a dense, shimmering carpet of Snow-Glow Grass. It turned the entire miniature world into what looked like a young girl's pastel-pink dreamscape, where creating the slightest commotion felt almost like a sin.

Gazing across this dreamlike scenery, however, Qu Qianxing knitted his brows.

—"Master's concerns were genuine,"-Mo Xi said in a chilling voice.

Indeed. Had they not known of the Carefree Palace's calculated scheming, Qu Qianxing, upon hearing that this secret realm looked like this, would certainly have considered bringing Mo Bei along.

—"What now? Do we proceed according to the original plan?"-Mo Xi asked.

Qu Qianxing gazed downward for several seconds before countering:

—"Do you think that's even necessary?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

Among the disciples present, at least one-third had emerged victorious in the earlier Immortal Chess tournament. Hearing this question, they broke into identical, mischievous smiles:

—"Look at this secret realm: it's in a textbook state of nine superimposed arrays. The array eye for this sort of formation can't possibly run anywhere else—it's right beneath our feet,"-one disciple pointed out.

—"Don't speak so lightly. According to a ['standard walkthrough'](# "Literal CN: 正常攻略 (zhèngcháng gōnglüè) — standard gaming guide or conventional walkthrough"), reaching the array eye is actually far harder than in ordinary secret realms. Without several months of grinding, you'd never crack it,"-another chimed in.

—"But does this secret realm forbid us from using ['cheese strats'](# "Literal CN: 邪道攻略 (xiédào gōnglüè) — unorthodox gaming strats, cheese tactics, or exploits that bypass intended design")?"-a third grinned.

—"Heheh, Senior Brother Qu even brought his Lightning Pearls out! That stuff was completely banned in the Immortal Chess tournament!"-another disciple added with glee.

Unlike half-hearted opportunists like Yu Feifei, this group had poured their hearts and souls into Immortal Chess. Over the course of a single month, not only had they continually innovated tactical matchups against opponents, but in tackling secret realm formations—taking shameless advantage of the chessboard where no one actually died and no treasures were permanently lost—they had devised all manner of degenerate "cheese strats": things like the "Tile-Flipping Meta," the "Windmill Meta," and the "Treasure Bombing Meta." So much so that when Mo Xi organized the tournament, he was forced to ban a whole pile of degenerate cheese strats purely to preserve spectator entertainment.

Now, looking at this secret realm, what everyone actually worried about was:

—"Senior Brother Qu, is it really fine if we just pack up the entire secret realm and take it with us? What if the other sects get mad?"-one disciple asked.

This question was [dripping with green tea innocence](# "Literal CN: 茶得很 (chá de hěn) — modern Chinese slang for 'green tea bitch' behavior: playing feigned innocence, passive-aggressive coy concern, or mock virtue").

Qu Qianxing shot that disciple a sidelong glance.

Although Qu Qianxing looked like a rare upright gentleman among Zhou Hui's direct disciples, this "uprightness" was strictly reserved for his own martial siblings; toward outsiders, he had never shown a shred of mercy:

—"This secret realm was unearthed in the first place because of our Master; claiming it for ourselves is only natural and proper,"-Qu Qianxing declared coolly.-"Besides, we are obtaining it through sheer strength!"

The other disciples nodded in deep agreement.

Senior Brother Qu's notorious habit of fiercely protecting his own might be infuriating in the eyes of outsiders, but the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were thrilled by it.

—"Alright, stop standing around dazed. You all know what to do, don't you?"-Qu Qianxing looked sternly at the rest.-"We've rehearsed this formation countless times. Don't drag your feet; strive to resolve the issue within [half a shichen](# "Literal CN: 半个时辰 (bàn gè shíchén) — half a double-hour, equal to one hour")."

[—"Yes, Senior Brother Qu!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

Without needing Qu Qianxing to assign specific tasks, they exchanged glances and dispersed toward their respective areas of expertise.

This sort of tacit coordination had been forged on the chessboard.

Though it had only been a month and was still somewhat unpolished, the foundational framework was already firmly established.

However, the mission of these disciples was to provide cover and suppression; their maneuvers were primarily aimed at preventing the secret realm's "guardians" from interfering with Qu Qianxing's array-breaking.

The true crux lay with Qu Qianxing himself.

Qu Qianxing raised his arm. Nine purplish-crimson lightning orbs drifted from his palm, crackling and arcing around him.

This was Qu Qianxing's lifebound treasure:

The Nine-Pillar Pearls.

For reasons unknown, despite possessing a top-grade Gold Heavenly Spirit Root, Qu Qianxing had chosen to specialize in lightning-attribute arts and forged matching treasures, resulting in this strikingly gorgeous celestial scholar perpetually radiating the wrath of thunder.

—"Second Senior Brother, you can do it!"-Mo Xi cheered with a smiling face beside him.-"Outside earlier, you had Master's power reinforcing you, but now you're entirely on your own."

Those words were hardly pleasant to the ear.

Qu Qianxing didn't mind in the slightest.

With only a Golden Core cultivation, he was naturally incapable of teleporting a whole cohort of disciples instantly to their destination, much less battering the Carefree Palace's treasure ship until it listed sideways and forcing another Tribulation Transcendence sovereign to intervene.

For Zhou Hui, however, that feat had been nothing more than casually blowing a puff of breath onto Qu Qianxing's lifebound treasure.

His Master's absolute, insurmountable strength was the dream Qu Qianxing yearned for day and night.

—"However, this secret realm only permits entry to those below Golden Core,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"My own strength is more than sufficient."

Saying so, he flipped his palm downward.

The nine lightning orbs erupted in radiant gold-and-purple brilliance, crashing downward with thunderous, earth-shattering fury.

And the moment the Carefree Palace entourage stepped in, this was the exact spectacle that greeted their eyes.

---

—"Is the Heavenly Spirit Sect planning to destroy this secret realm?!"-Hongying muttered with a deep frown, as the disciples of the Carefree Palace paled in shock.

Su Xin, on the contrary, raised his eyebrows:

—"No, that's not right. Aren't they attacking the array eye?"-Su Xin murmured.

Indeed. The Heavenly Dao Alliance contingent led by Su Xin had entered as the second batch, right behind the Carefree Palace.

Although the Mei-Zhu couple had repeatedly exhorted and cautioned Su Xin to stick close to Qu Qianxing; although Qu Qianxing had cordially extended an invitation to Su Xin prior to stepping into the secret realm; and although, due to the spectacle of their grand entrance, Su Xin had tilted slightly toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect; BUT! None of that could compete with the sheer horror of examinations! Su Xin perpetually suspected that the Heavenly Spirit Sect was an industrial pencil sharpener designed to [grind him into shavings](# "Literal CN: 卷死 (juǎn sǐ) — pun on pencil sharpener (卷笔刀, pencil-rolling knife) and involution (内卷, grinding/rolling), meaning grind someone to death"); hence, he would rather tag along with the Carefree Palace than spend a single second standing beside the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Looking at that lightning now, however, Su Xin still felt a twinge of yearning:

[That move looks so damn cool!]

[If only I could stand over on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's side.]

Hongying thought little of Su Xin's fence-sitting habits, yet she keenly sensed that Su Xin knew something more.

And rightly so. Under the banner of "fairness and upholding justice," the Heavenly Dao Alliance had sent a scouting party ahead into the secret realm. While unraveling the array layout was complicated, seeing through its design was hardly difficult, so they already knew where the array eye was situated.

—In fact, had it not been for that exam, given Su Xin's frivolous and boastful nature, he would have easily leaked the secrets of this realm to both the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Fallen Immortal Palace during the reception banquet. That was how the scene in the "original novel" had unfolded, where Mo Bei led a team into the secret realm. Now, through a bizarre sequence of coincidences, although Mo Bei was indeed inside the secret realm, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's method of breaking the deadlock was completely different from what the calculations of the Heavenly Dao had projected.

As for the Fallen Immortal Palace, they hadn't even secured legitimate exploration slots, let alone known where the array eye was located.

Hongying of the Carefree Palace figured it wasn't too late to find out now. Taking two steps closer to Su Xin, she asked in a coquettish voice:

—"The array eye? Does Senior Su know where the array eye is located?"-Hongying asked softly.

She, a Golden Core cultivator, was addressing a mere Foundation Establishment junior as "Senior"; Su Xin had long grown accustomed to such flattery. There was, however, a peculiar fragrance wafting from Hongying's person. Inhaling it, Su Xin felt no discomfort; if anything, his mind felt remarkably clear, though his desire to show off and talk suddenly spiked:

—"The formations here are rather intricate, so our Heavenly Dao Alliance had array masters analyze them and deduce that the array eye should be directly beneath that floating island,"-Su Xin explained.

—"Oh~?"-Hongying looked at the lightning crashing downward from the floating island. A flicker of anxiety darted through her eyes, yet not a trace showed on her face as she flatteringly replied to Su Xin:-"Looking at it this way, the Heavenly Dao Alliance truly possesses profound righteousness; even knowing where the array eye lies, you didn't touch [a single autumn hair](# "Literal CN: 秋毫 (qiūháo) — autumn hair, meaning the tiniest fraction or slightest particle")."

Anyone could spout flattering pleasantries.

Yet if they truly hadn't touched a hair, they shouldn't have dispatched people into the realm in the first place, but rather sealed the realm and waited for the other sects to arrive together.

Even Su Xin scoffed at that:

—"It's just that their skills were too shoddy! They kept babbling about how diagnosing an array is easy while unraveling it is hard. To reach the array eye, one has to take a massive detour through countless twists and turns. Just look at the Heavenly Spirit Sect—how crisp and decisive their technique is!"-Su Xin said, gazing entranced at the lightning.

Hongying, meanwhile, cast a discreet glance toward a certain woman in her retinue.

That woman was evidently an array master herself. Communicating via divine sense into Hongying's mind, she reported:

[—"Our position isn't ideal; we cannot survey the entirety of the realm. However, judging from several standard layouts of secret realm formations, the likelihood of the array eye lying beneath the floating island is indeed very high. But just as Senior Su said, diagnosing an array is easy while breaking it is hard. An array where the core is placed in such a conspicuous spot actually requires navigating a much longer detour and dismantling far more mechanical traps before one can reach it."](# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Array Master")

She paused here, before speaking haltingly:

[—"...Barring any major unexpected events, our 'designated objective' ought to be situated along the standard path for cracking the formation. After all, the 'Ice Eye' surrounded by Snow-Glow Grass is exceptionally well-suited to serve as part of the defensive mechanisms, and only the spiritual energy of the array eye could sustain that wondrous circulation."](# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Array Master")

Hearing this, Hongying couldn't help biting her crimson lips.

Strict orders had been handed down from above: Mo Bei had to be led to the "Ice Eye" at all costs.

As for the specific insider details, someone of Hongying's cultivation level naturally lacked the qualifications to know; she merely had an inkling that it was connected to the "Heavenly Book."

The so-called Heavenly Book, however, was an open secret in the cultivation world.

The tale traced back ten thousand years. It was said that back then, a section of the Celestial Stairway existed in the world, allowing people to achieve immortality without excessive toil. Alas, it was destroyed during the Great War of Gods and Demons. From that point on, the Tribulation Transcendence stage came into being, requiring practitioners to overcome ninety-nine heavenly tribulations before they could ascend. Then, seven thousand years ago, a great sage arose in this realm; though he had never cultivated, his supreme virtue moved the heavens. Thus, scrolls descended from the sky, coalescing into a path, and True Immortals descended to personally welcome his ascension.

Yet after that great sage strode along the path paved by the Heavenly Book, other cultivators sought to follow him into the celestial realm—only to find that the moment anyone else stepped upon the stairway, it shredded as easily as mundane paper, scattering everywhere in the wind.

Among them, a sharp-eyed, swift-handed supreme power hurled down his pagoda-shaped treasure, pinning down one of the scrolls and giving rise to the Heavenly Book Tower.

However, once that pagoda suppressed the Heavenly Book, it was no longer under its master's control. Whoever wished to enter was required to expend immense spiritual energy, and could only do so on the exact same month, day, and hour of the great sage's ascension each year.

These stringent conditions, ironically, only spurred cultivators on.

Especially when they discovered that the contents of the Heavenly Book were linked to the "future."

Countless cultivators grew obsessed, braving the exorbitant spiritual energy costs to enter the tower and glimpse the Heavenly Book's revelations. Later, however, people realized that even the same person would see entirely different passages upon each entry. Moreover, these glimpses weren't necessarily relevant to the viewer themselves, and reading too much would cause one to bleed from all seven orifices and drop dead on the spot.

At the time, numerous cultivators of formidable cultivation perished within that tower, forcing the Heavenly Dao Alliance to erect a palace around it to station dedicated guards, strictly capping how many times any single individual could read the Heavenly Book.

Nevertheless, not all cultivators possessed the spiritual energy or the obsession to peer into the Heavenly Book.

Particularly when most of the contents had nothing to do with themselves.

Down to the present day, the sect that had perused the Heavenly Book the most was none other than the Palace of Carefree Wandering.

After all, the Carefree Palace was rolling in obscene wealth; they lacked neither the spirit stones to supply spiritual power nor personnel, simply rotating different people inside each time. Furthermore, because they were in commerce, even seemingly trivial tidbits like *"Year X, Month X, Day X, Location X: The weather is clear"* weren't necessarily useless to merchant magnates.

Thus, this year, the Carefree Palace had visited the Heavenly Book Tower once again.

And this time, they had witnessed something rather fascinating:

For instance, Zhou Hui failing his lightning tribulation;

For instance, Su Xin visiting the Heavenly Spirit Sect aboard his cloud carriage;

For instance, Qu Qianxing leading a cohort to explore the secret realm;

For instance, Mo Bei clashing with the "emissaries of the Fallen Immortal Palace" before the Ice Eye surrounded by Snow-Glow Grass, tumbling into the spring, and contracting thermal poison, which forced her to return to the secret realm every year to soak in the medicinal waters;

And for instance, Mo Xi instigating Su Xin to provoke a demon beast, causing Su Xin to be grievously wounded; although bathing in beast blood later enlightened Su Xin and made him focus on his cultivation, the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Mei-Zhu couple forged an irreconcilable feud with the Heavenly Spirit Sect as a result.

Upon learning these fragments, the Carefree Palace had been delirious with joy.

They and the Heavenly Spirit Sect were mortal enemies, and there was nothing sweeter in the world than seeing one's nemesis suffer.

Hence, from the beginning of the year, the Carefree Palace had been eagerly anticipating the unfolding of the Heavenly Book's plot.

Yet certain plot points occurred, while others seemed... not to have occurred at all.

For instance, Zhou Hui indeed failed his tribulation, but the man himself appeared entirely unharmed, still having the vigor to roll out sect-wide unified examinations;

For instance, Su Xin indeed went to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but for reasons unknown, after fleeing back in utter disgrace, he filed a scathing complaint against the Fallen Immortal Palace to his parents, resulting in the Fallen Immortal Palace nearly losing all qualifications to explore the secret realm;

[Why on earth is it the Fallen Immortal Palace that gets screwed over?!]

The Carefree Palace was utterly bewildered.

They analyzed and analyzed, eventually concluding that the issue stemmed from the Mei-Zhu couple having also consulted the Heavenly Book.

However, the Mei-Zhu couple hadn't gone this year, but rather in the year Su Xin was born. What was more, they hadn't poured in that many spirit stones, only enough for a single stroll around the tower that lasted less than an incense stick of time.

Their intention had simply been to pray for blessings for their newborn son, feeling that if their son's future appeared, it would be their good fortune, but if it didn't, no news was good news—it proved their son was living comfortably.

Unexpectedly, from such an idle act, the Mei-Zhu couple actually obtained a glimpse of Su Xin's future. Only, compared to the Carefree Palace's detailed version, their version merely read: *"Su Xin shall face a great tribulation in a secret realm; surviving this tribulation will grant him the fortune to attain personal growth."*

This was naturally good news. Amid their delight, the doting parents grew worried that this so-called "great tribulation" might be too horrific, so they made extensive preparations to shield their child from harm.

The Carefree Palace had every reason to suspect that these very preparations had caused all subsequent deviations.

They practically wished they could shove the Heavenly Book revelations directly into the Mei-Zhu couple's hands and shout, *"Look! Your child's tribulation falls squarely on the Heavenly Spirit Sect! Hurry up and forge a blood feud with them!"* Yet they couldn't actually do that—doing so would only make enemies of the Mei-Zhu couple instead, as the pair would certainly demand: *"If you saw this, why didn't you bring it out earlier?!"*

Yet mere verbal instigation wasn't enough to sway the couple either. Bizarrely, they harbored an inexplicable trust in Zhou Hui, firmly believing that if their boy was to "attain personal growth," it must be due to the noble character of the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

The Carefree Palace had been at their wits' end; their only recourse was to ensure that events inside the secret realm did not derail.

First, they spent a fortune purchasing exploration qualifications to ensure "disciples of the Fallen Immortal Palace" could enter the realm; next, they contacted their sleeper mole inside the Heavenly Spirit Sect to ensure Mo Bei would arrive at the designated location; finally, they dispatched their treasure ship and Sovereign Immortal Chana to monitor the progression of events.

Regrettably, from the moment Qu Qianxing and the others arrived, Hongying had begun to deeply doubt the viability of this plan.

She didn't know if it was just her imagination, but she felt that the Tribulation Transcendence sovereign on their side... was kind of a fraud.

Now, the spectacle inside the secret realm only reinforced her impression.

—"Can an array eye really be forcibly cracked open with lightning?"-Hongying asked skeptically.

—"If it's powerful enough, theoretically it should be possible... right?"-the Carefree Palace array master replied uncertainly.

Had a Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple been here, they would certainly have informed this array master that an ordinary lightning orb naturally couldn't; however, when multiple arrays were superimposed, finding the precise flaw allowed one to pierce straight to the array eye without destroying the realm! They had even coined a name for this technique: the ['Surefire Strike Method'](# "Literal CN: 一击必中法 (yī jī bì zhòng fǎ) — Surefire Strike Method / One-Hit Certain-Kill Strategy; gaming cheese tactic targeting superimposed array cores"). This discovery had directly caused lightning orb items to proliferate across multiple composite-formation chessboards.

The Carefree Palace array master could only speak from her limited knowledge:

—"Although theoretically, a Golden Core's lightning pearls cannot directly obliterate the formation core, but..."-she stammered.

Given Qu Qianxing's grand entrance earlier where he struck the treasure ship with lightning, whether Qu Qianxing truly possessed such monstrous power or not, they had no choice but to assume he did.

Yet if that was the case, they wouldn't have any opportunity to pull underhanded tricks at all—if the entire secret realm fell into the Heavenly Spirit Sect's hands, what dirty tricks were left to play?

—"Still, doesn't it feel like while their lightning looks magnificent, it's not quite as terrifying as it was outside?"-another Carefree Palace disciple piped up.

Hongying looked closely and realized that while Qu Qianxing's display was grand, the lightning arcs struck the earth beneath the floating island like spiritual serpents, piercing downward and faintly tracing the contours of an array.

She analyzed rationally:

—"Or perhaps all his strength is concentrated on unlocking the formation core. Now is the perfect moment—"-Hongying deduced.

She cast a meaningful glance at her subordinates.

The Carefree Palace cohort nodded. Those capable of melee assaults surged toward the floating island, while ranged attackers trained their sights directly on the isle.

—"...Wait a second! Participants attacking one another is a violation of the rules!"-Su Xin protested, realizing a beat too late what they intended to do.

Hongying leaned close and giggled softly:

—"Senior Su is being unfair. The Heavenly Spirit Sect struck first; is this concubine supposed to stand idly by and watch them destroy the entire secret realm?"-Hongying teased.

—"Well..."-wafted by that peculiar aroma, Su Xin's brain momentarily lagged.

That brief hesitation was more than enough. A plume of flame erupted from beneath Hongying's feet, instantly trapping the Heavenly Dao Alliance cohort.

And at that very instant, the Carefree Palace's onslaught arrived right before the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Their reactions are quite fast,"-Qu Qianxing praised casually.

Disrupting the caster was indeed the textbook counter to this strategy; one had to admit Hongying had reacted swiftly. Alas, the fact that Lightning Pearls had been banned in tournament play was proof that this counter didn't actually work.

Qu Qianxing addressed the twin siblings stationed beside him:

—"Mo Bei, Mo Xi: hold them off for [half a ke](# "Literal CN: 半刻钟 (bàn kè zhōng) — half a ke, roughly seven to eight minutes"). I'll be done shortly,"-Qu Qianxing instructed.

[—"Understood!"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei & Mo Xi")

Mo Xi assumed a battle-ready stance, while Mo Bei gazed expressionlessly downward and drew her flexible sword.

A ferocious sword stance instantly flashed through the sky.

Naturally, no matter how long a flexible sword was, it could never reach distant foes. Yet the gale of her blade swept up a drizzle of mist—which instantaneously crystallized into a shower of ice needles!

The encroaching enemies instantly turned into pincushions, screaming as they plummeted from the sky.

—"Yaaah! Junior Sister Mo is too cool!"-Yu Feifei shrieked in euphoric adoration.

Mo Xi and Qu Qianxing shot Mo Bei a speechless look.

—"...Is wiping them out not allowed? Constantly defending is too troublesome,"-Mo Bei murmured blankly.

—"Suit yourself,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

Only then did he recall that this junior sister was no mere chess piece. She wouldn't be restricted to a single set move like her figurine on the board; the arsenal of techniques she could employ now was vastly greater.

And Mo Bei wasn't the only one performing brilliantly.

The other "chess pieces" were not only more numerous but also far more agile. Right as Mo Bei repelled the Carefree Palace attackers, several paper cranes folded from talismans winged their way back from various corners of the secret realm:

[—"Senior Brother Qu, we've neutralized the mechanism at the Qian position!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Reporting from the northwest: we've subdued the demon beasts guarding the protective barrier and reduced the defense to its lowest tier!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"We have reached the diagonal underside of the floating island; the auxiliary array has been completely inscribed!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

—"Excellent!"-Qu Qianxing praised.

He surged greater power into his lightning, piercing through the barrier below, conducting through the subterranean thermal spring of the Ice Eye, amplifying its fury through the auxiliary array inscribed by his martial siblings, and at one stroke blasted clean through the array eye!

Electric currents instantly rippled outward from the array eye like flowing water, clashing against the faint remnants of the realm's original master's aura to seize absolute control over the secret realm.

Over on the other side, the Carefree Palace hadn't even realized that Qu Qianxing was already on the verge of succeeding.

Watching the storm of ice needles rain down like a blizzard, Hongying shrieked in mortified fury:

—"THAT is what they call a 'cultivation furnace'?! Who on earth teaches a furnace moves like that?!"-Hongying screamed.

She yanked out a transmission rune in a desperate bid to cry for help from Perfected Master Zhao aboard the treasure ship. Yet before she could report a word, she felt the earth shudder violently beneath her feet, and a surge of power erupting from beneath the floating island shredded her talisman in an instant.

—"Too late! Looks like this secret realm belongs to the Heavenly Spirit Sect after all,"-Su Xin gloated from the sidelines.

—"Shut up!"-In a fit of blind rage, Hongying kicked Su Xin forward to block the rain of needles.

Unwilling to accept defeat, she stared toward the heavens above the realm, praying for something to reverse the tide:

—"The Sovereign... the Sovereign will never let them do as they please!"-she cried.

---

Outside, Sovereign Immortal Chana had indeed taken action.

In the most literal sense.

Right as the secret realm was ringed by swirling runes, waging a final struggle over its ultimate ownership, a hand suddenly reached out from the treasure ship.

A colossal hand that gripped the secret realm with crushing force.

This hand was so gargantuan that it could actually clasp the entire secret realm within its palm!

—"What a pity. The Heavenly Spirit Sect was still too reckless,"-an onlooker commented, shaking his head with an air of sagely wisdom.-"Since they possessed the ability to claim ownership of the realm, why do it on the very first run? Look at that—they've offended a heavyweight from the Carefree Palace! This one must be at least Mahayana."

—"But if not this time, it would be too late,"-another cultivator countered.-"The results of the initial exploration determine the division of territory going forward; this was witnessed by the Heavenly Dao Alliance. If they claimed ownership later, it would be morally indefensible."

—"It's hardly defensible now either! To try and swallow an entire secret realm whole—they're far too greedy,"-another muttered sourly.

They chatted away in detached amusement, yet failed to notice that after grabbing the secret realm, that colossal hand had frozen dead still.

Evidently, even for a Tribulation Transcendence sovereign, snatching away an entire secret realm across vast distances was no trivial task.

And just after a moment of deadlock, that giant hand suddenly relaxed its grip, as if attempting to shake off the secret realm.

Yet right then, a calm, gentle voice resonated across the firmament:

[—"No running away."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

Accompanying that voice, a crystalline white luminescence began to emanate from the fingertips of that colossal hand, spreading to the wrist, the forearm, the entire arm, extending all the way into the treasure ship.

Instantly, from within the treasure ship erupted a squeal [like a slaughtered pig](# "Literal CN: 如同杀猪般的 (rútóng shāzhū bān de) — colorful Chinese idiom for ear-splitting, undignified shrieking"), stripped of every shred of Tribulation Transcendence dignity!
