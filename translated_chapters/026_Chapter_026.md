# Chapter 26: The Heartless Puppet

[Something feels... distinctly off?]

Mo Bei pondered, gazing at the [Snow-Glow Grass](# "Literal CN: 雪莹草 (xuěyíng cǎo) — exotic luminous grass that sparkles like rainbow frost") carpeting the hillside outside her immortal abode.

These stalks of Snow-Glow Grass were something Mo Bei had "brought over" back when Zhou Hui flattened the Fallen Immortal Palace.

Yet unlike that Mirage Demon—which had merely been stashed with Mo Xi before he brazenly claimed it as his own—Mo Bei's Snow-Glow Grass had been cultivated by the Fallen Immortal Palace specifically for her... and Mo Xi.

It was a gorgeous plant.

Slender and soft to the touch, cool as genuine snow, when planted across wide terraces one discovered it wasn't a sterile pure white. Instead, it was faintly tinged with delicate pastel blues, blushes of red, and soft purples. Spreading out over the precipice before Mo Bei's cave abode, the entire bluff looked as though it had been dusted in a shimmering shower of watercolor-rainbow snow. It was, without question, the most lavish and scenic corner of the humble—*cough*, austere—Heavenly Spirit Sect.

And it didn't end there.

It was said that Snow-Glow Grass was at its most magnificent when its blossoms unfurled.

The buds themselves were hardly conspicuous—just tiny, translucent grains clinging like morning dew across the surface of the blades. But whenever the mountain breeze caught them, they drifted aloft in a swirling flurry, crystallizing in midair into luminous frost-blossoms. To stand amidst them was to bathe in a shower of glittering, incandescent snow.

Yet Mo Bei had never actually witnessed the Snow-Glow Grass in full bloom.

Even though the grass had carpeted the grounds around her quarters for as long as she could remember, and someone had instructed her to water them twice a day on a fixed schedule, Mo Bei had obediently followed orders. No matter how diligently she tended to them, she had never seen them blossom.

In the beginning, Mo Bei hadn't cared much. She didn't particularly adore the grass—though she hardly detested it either, since pretty things were always pleasant to look at. Thus, when they moved to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, she hadn't made any special effort to bring the grass along. Besides, with such vast quantities of it, packing them up was out of the question anyway.

To her surprise, during her second year at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the grass had sprouted into an overwhelming sea once again.

—"You went out of your way to drag these things here too?"-Mo Xi had asked upon seeing them, an expression of undisguised disgust plastered across his face.

Mo Bei had thought about it for a moment back then, choosing not to argue:
—"Perhaps the seeds clung to our robes?"

—"I despise these damned things,"-Mo Xi had spat.-"Don't you think they look just like the dried decorative flowers stuffed inside luxury gift boxes? It makes me sick just looking at them."

—"I think... they're fine,"-Mo Bei had replied, fetching her watering pot to water them.

She had long forgotten who had given the original order—likely someone back at the Fallen Immortal Palace. Mo Bei had always been someone who simply followed instructions and rolled with the punches. Having watered this grass for so many years, it had solidified into second nature, so she casually maintained her daily routine.

Zhou Hui, upon hearing about it, had paid a special visit to examine them.

—"Snow-Glow Grass, is it?"-Zhou Hui had remarked, a rare look of surprise flickering across his face as he surveyed the sprawling sea of pastel frost.-"This flora typically only survives in the southernmost extremes. Away from that climate, it normally perishes. I hadn't expected Bei'er could nurture them so successfully."

—"Is this some kind of ill omen?"-Mo Xi had piped up maliciously.

It was obvious he genuinely wished to rip every single blade out of the ground.

Unfortunately, Zhou Hui saw no need for such extremes:
—"Hardly. It simply brings to mind an intriguing piece of folklore: they say this grass requires a [destined soulmate](# "Literal CN: 命定之人 (mìngdìng zhī rén) — mythical fated lover / destined person") to bloom."

—"A destined soulmate?"-Mo Bei had asked, her curiosity piqued.

—"Just a poetic framework for mate selection. Little more than a mythical legend spun from humanity's biological drive to propagate offspring or an emotional longing for stable domestic partnerships, wouldn't you say?"-Zhou Hui had replied with airy detachment.

At the time, Mo Bei had looked upon her master with a hint of genuine pity.

Word had it that Master had been single for thousands of years, having never experienced romance in his life. Given this clinical mindset, it was readily obvious to the naked eye that he was destined to remain perpetually single; even if he ascended, he wouldn't find a destined soulmate.

Unaware of her pity, Zhou Hui had chuckled:
—"Speaking of which, Mei Zhu... *cough*, a very famous and loving immortal couple once heard this folklore. They tracked down this grass, planted crop after crop, and saw them wither crop after crop, never once able to prove whether they were each other's destined lovers. In my view, there was no need for such obsession. After all, they had already spent tens of thousands of years together. Compared to an empty myth, wasn't it abundantly obvious whether they were fated partners? It only goes to show that rumors are merely rumors."

[That was just couples' bedroom banter and romantic playfulness, wasn't it?!]

Mo Bei had complained inwardly, yet a peculiar flutter of anticipation had sprouted within her heart nonetheless.

She fancied that perhaps one day, amidst a swirling dance of Snow-Glow blossoms, she might encounter her own destined soulmate.

Mo Bei had no idea where such confidence came from. In any case, her enthusiasm for watering the Snow-Glow Grass had soared.

Today, at the [Hour of the Rabbit](# "Literal CN: 卯时 (mǎoshí) — 5:00 AM to 7:00 AM, the crack of dawn"), Mo Bei appeared on the mountain crest outside her cave abode right on the dot.

Yet she wasn't carrying her usual wooden water bucket. Instead, she drew a shimmering, crystalline flexible sword.

This blade had been gifted to her by Master just a few days ago:

—"You have reached the ninth form of the Flying Snow Sword Art, correct? It is just about time for you to synthesize and master it,"-Zhou Hui had said, gently stroking the flat of the blade.-"By the way, do you still water the Snow-Glow Grass every day?"

—"I do, Master,"-Mo Bei had answered.

Zhou Hui had handed the flexible sword to Mo Bei:
—"Then take this blade."

—"This is the [Moon-Water Sword](# "Literal CN: 月水剑 (yuèshuǐ jiàn) — flexible soft sword attuned to water spiritual energy"). It resonates with ambient water spiritual energy to condense pure spiritual dew. When mastered to the peak, it sweeps out crescent sword qi that corrodes all adversaries."

—"Naturally, you are not yet at that stage. But you can start by learning to sense and coalesce atmospheric moisture while practicing your forms. As for the precise metric: match the area you normally water."

—"Each day, in lieu of hauling buckets, once you ensure that every blade of Snow-Glow Grass is coated in dewdrops condensed by the Moon-Water Sword, your practice for the day will be complete."

Mo Bei had bowed her head, accepting the blade:
—"Understood, Master."

She harbored no complaints or complex feelings about it.

Zhou Hui was always like this: his pedagogy intertwined his curriculum with his disciples' daily lives, encouraging them to apply what they learned directly into their living routines, believing that was the superior form of practice.

Mo Bei had long grown accustomed to it, so she gave it little thought.

Yet as a disciple, Mo Bei possessed an immense virtue—one that earned unanimous praise from any elder or teacher:

She was exceedingly obedient.

Or rather, obedient to an absurd fault, like a [clockwork mechanism](# "Literal CN: 定时机关 (dìngshí jīguān) — obedient to a fault, executing commands without fail") (in Mo Xi's exact words).

Mo Bei faithfully executed whatever was asked of her. If told to water the Snow-Glow Grass on schedule twice a day, she watered it diligently without skipping a single day for years. If ordered to copy texts as punishment, she never slacked off, copying every single stroke honestly without ever resorting to outsourcing her homework like certain 'clever' individuals.

That was precisely why Zhou Hui could never fathom how Mo Bei could have betrayed him according to the Heavenly Dao's novel script. It was as absurd as the most well-behaved girl in class abandoning the college entrance exam during twelfth grade just to elope to Annan with some no-name boyfriend she picked up off the street.

Fortunately, Mo Bei's rebellious phase had yet to arrive.

Since Zhou Hui instructed her to replace bucket-watering with sword practice, she practiced her sword diligently.

Gripping the flexible blade, Mo Bei closed her eyes, took a deep breath, and sank into an opening sword stance.

In that instant, every stray thought in Mo Bei's mind vanished without a trace.

—Master had once praised her, saying that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's lack of a specialized sword-cultivation legacy was rather an injustice to Mo Bei. She was a natural-born sword cultivator; her heart shed stray thoughts faster than anyone else's. (Though her detestable twin brother snidely claimed it was merely because Mo Bei never used her brain in the first place)—

The moment her mind emptied, Mo Bei's perception grew extraordinarily sharp. She could sense the gentle flow of spiritual qi, the breathing of the Snow-Glow Grass, and... the gaze of lurkers.

Right, lurkers.

This was the root cause of why Mo Bei had felt something was off lately. For some inexplicable reason, whenever she walked through the sect these days, people were always sneaking glances at her. Some even staked out her spot on a fixed schedule!

Still, this didn't violate any sect regulations in the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

The cultivation techniques of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were open for public exchange. Even the daily drills of direct disciples could be observed by ordinary outer disciples, provided no formal application for closed-door seclusion had been filed. More accurately, Zhou Hui actively encouraged mutual exchange.

It was just that in the past, no one went out of their way to watch Mo Bei.

Disciples universally preferred flocking to Second Senior Brother Qu Qianxing's spellcasting drills. Qu Qianxing's execution was textbook perfection; not only could one learn the proper casting process simply by watching, but he was also willing to answer questions afterward. On days when he was in a good mood, he would even offer hands-on coaching.

Of course, there were also those who came purely to stare at Second Senior Brother himself—though Mo Bei had no clue what was going through those people's heads.

Were these people today just like the disciples who watched Second Senior Brother practice spells?

With her mind cleared of stray thoughts, Mo Bei didn't bother dwelling on the matter. Her divine sense lightly brushed over the onlookers, and having confirmed they bore no malice, she automatically categorized them alongside the ambient weeds.

The next moment, Mo Bei opened her eyes and threw herself into her sword forms without a shred of distraction.

---

[—"Gorgeous."](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"How did we never notice before? Junior Sister Mo is truly worthy of being a direct disciple—the undisputed number one sword cultivator in our sect!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"That's it, my mind's made up! I'm betting all-in on her!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

Down beneath the hillside, several disciples who had snuck over to peek whispered among themselves, blissfully oblivious to the fact that their presence was already blown.

Someone, however, voiced a dissenting opinion:
—"Isn't that a bit reckless? She's only practicing the standard Flying Spirit Sword Art. If we bet on her, her game piece probably won't have any unique killer moves."

—"Who knows? Maybe just like Junior Brother Mo, Junior Sister Mo has a little dragon coiled beneath her skirts too,"-one disciple mused in a swooning tone.-"Judging by Junior Sister Mo's icy demeanor, it might be an ice-blue dragon, wrapped snugly around that slender waist of hers..."

The air fell dead silent for three whole seconds.

Then, without a shred of mercy, that disciple was tackled face-first into the freezing Snow-Glow Grass.

[—"Do you have a death wish, lusting after Junior Sister Mo?!"](# "Speaker: Female Disciple")

[—"Do you not want to survive in the Heavenly Spirit Sect anymore?!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Just imagine what Junior Brother Mo will do to you if he finds out!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

—"Remember! We are here because we appreciate Junior Sister Mo's swordsmanship!"-the tallest girl among them snapped, driving her knee ruthlessly into the back of the offender's skull as she addressed the rest of the group.-"The rules for the chess tournament have already been posted. Every participant must lock in five fixed pieces to use all the way through to the finals. And the combat stats of the pieces directly dictate the tournament difficulty! I trust everyone here is leaning toward drafting Junior Sister Mo?"

Having said her piece, she swept a stern gaze across the circle.

Most of them nodded in agreement.

The girl nodded in satisfaction:
—"Then everyone must have heard the inside scoop: the generated 'pieces' retain an underlying resonance with their real-world prototypes. In other words, the prototype's condition dictates the piece's combat tier! If the prototype gets stronger, the piece becomes stronger too! Therefore, during this period, we must rigorously maintain Junior Sister Mo's optimal condition—"

—"Exactly! We must ensure Junior Sister Mo is the strongest!"-someone cheered, pumping a fist.

—"Right! [Stats, stats, and more stats!](# "Literal CN: 强度，强度，还是强度！ — pure gamer meta mindset: prioritizing tier-list strength over waifu aesthetics") That is our sole justification for drafting Junior Sister Mo!"-another disciple chimed in earnestly.

The girl drove her knee down with extra force:
—"[Simps for looks can drop dead!](# "Literal CN: 颜狗都去死吧！ (yángǒu dōu qù sǐ ba) — internet slang for superficial people who only care about physical appearance")"

---

—"Achoo!"-Mo Bei sneezed out of nowhere after finishing her sword routine.

Dabbing her nose with a handkerchief, she sheathed her sword and prepared to head down the mountain, only to see several disciples who had been lurking at the foot of the hill dash out in a flustered hurry, rushing straight toward her.

[So they really came to study my swordsmanship?]

Mo Bei halted her steps. While waiting for them to arrive, she reviewed all the sword techniques Master had taught her in her mind, mentally rehearsing the effortless, dignified poise Qu Qianxing always displayed when answering disciples' inquiries.

However, these disciples didn't seem to have that demand at all.

The moment the leading girl approached, she thrust a celestial shawl woven from Fire-Silkworm silk into Mo Bei's hands.

—"Junior Sister Mo, you've worked so hard practicing your sword early in the morning! Put this on so you don't catch a chill,"-the girl gushed warmly.

Mo Bei blinked in bewilderment. Before she could process what was happening, another disciple shoved a pouch forward:
—"This is Ice-Needle Tea I harvested myself! I heard it's fantastic for your cultivation... Ah, if you don't care for it, just tell me what tea you prefer! I consider myself quite knowledgeable about spiritual teas, so I can bring some next time!"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Mo Bei took a step back.

Though she hailed from the Fallen Immortal Palace, unlike Mo Xi, she didn't care much for material abundance. What she truly struggled with was confronting direct emotions from others—whether genuine goodwill or sheer malice.

—"What is it you want?"-Mo Bei asked warily.

Cold light reflected in her round, feline eyes, giving her an almost inorganic, doll-like detachment. Her voice rang clear, aloof, and laced with palpable chill, instantly freezing the crowd in their tracks.

Only then did everyone seem to remember why they had always kept their distance from the youngest junior sister.

In contrast to the bubbly, charming Junior Brother Mo, his identical twin sister was notoriously frosty and seldom interacted with ordinary disciples.

It wasn't that anyone disliked her; rather, they viewed her as an unattainable flower atop a frosty peak—a status Mo Bei was fully entitled to as Zhou Hui's direct disciple. Yet regardless of how much they admired her from afar, had it not been for this tournament, none of them would have dared approach her so casually.

—"Junior Sister Mo, we mean no harm!"-the leading girl stammered, flustered.-"We... we just wanted to cheer you on during your sword practice..."

—"Sword practice? Cheering me on?"-Mo Bei echoed, baffled.

[What on earth is there to cheer about for sword practice?]

Mo Bei stared blankly once more, but she quickly recalled the enterprise her twin brother had been tinkering with lately:
—"Are you doing this because of the upcoming chess tournament?"

The gathered disciples broke into bashful, guilty grins.

Mo Bei let out an inward sneer, her expression chilling even further:
—"Rest assured, I will manage my condition during this period and stabilize my performance so I don't drag you down."

[If you're so worried, why not simply refrain from picking my piece?]

—"No, how could it ever be a drag?!"-the girl protested anxiously, terrified that Mo Bei had taken it the wrong way.-"We genuinely admire your swordsmanship! We have absolute faith in your prowess—we're convinced you're definitely the strongest sword cultivator in the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect!"

—"I am not a sword cultivator..."-Mo Bei murmured. (Though Master had mentioned she was suited for the sword, Mo Bei had never officially declared her primary cultivation path).

—"You aren't even a sword cultivator and you're already this formidable? That makes you even more incredible!"-the girl pressed on relentlessly.-"While there isn't much we can do, if there is anything that can boost your combat strength, please don't hesitate to ask!"

The others immediately resumed their enthusiastic swarm:
—"By the way, Junior Sister Mo, I noticed you switched to a new sword! It doesn't look like it's been enchanted with spiritual resonance yet. Do you need a hand?"

—"No... no need—"-Mo Bei stammered.

—"Are you an idiot?! A flying sword is a sword cultivator's [waifu](# "Literal CN: 老婆 (lǎopó) — wife; internet gamer slang for one's beloved weapon/character")! Why would she ever let you touch it?! Junior Sister Mo, I see you always practice your forms around the Snow-Glow Grass—is it because of the terrain environment?"-someone rebuked the previous disciple before turning back to Mo Bei.

[—"Now that you mention it, Junior Sister Mo planted so much Snow-Glow Grass around her cave abode. It must benefit her cultivation!"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Should we plant some across other parts of the sect to give Junior Sister Mo home-field advantage?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

[—"Junior Sister Mo, how do you cultivate this grass?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")

The cacophony of overlapping voices made Mo Bei's head spin completely. She had no idea how to bark at them to back off, so she blurted out an offhand answer:
—"How to plant Snow-Glow Grass? It's simple enough... just toss the cuttings into other soil and they should survive... Ah, Xi'er!"

Catching sight of that familiar figure in the distance, Mo Bei felt profoundly grateful for her brother's existence for the very first time.

—"I promised Xi'er I'd meet him. Urgent business!"-she blurted out, stepping onto her flying sword.

And fled in utter panic.

---

—"I hate those people!"-a moment later, Mo Bei complained to Mo Xi, glaring with wide, round cat eyes.

If she had a tail, her fur would have puffed out completely.

Mo Xi observed her, thinking to himself: [Looks like Master's scheme fell flat! But then again, this girl was always impossibly difficult to handle. Insisting on some 'destined soulmate'—what kind of mythical fantasy beast is that anyway?]

Aloud, he chimed in placatingly:
—"Yeah, yeah, you hate people with ulterior motives the most."

—"Right?! How could one be so easily swayed just because of a game? Affection ought to be pure,"-Mo Bei complained indignantly.-"One shouldn't like someone because of external conditions like looks or talent, nor should it waver over personalities or quirks! One must love the deepest essence of their being—never wavering even across ten thousand reincarnations, recognizing each other's very existence in a single glance—"

—"Wait, without appearance, talent, personality, or quirks, is that person even that person anymore?"-Mo Xi couldn't help but roast her, no matter how many times he had heard this romantic drivel.-"What exactly are they supposed to love about you? That half-ounce soul of yours?!"

—"Soul alone isn't enough; it must be something deeper than the soul,"-Mo Bei insisted.-"Hmph, boys wouldn't understand!"

[...Even if I were a girl, I still wouldn't understand,] Mo Xi thought to himself.

—"Anyway, those people are insufferable! Can't you strike my name from the tournament?"-Mo Bei continued to bristle.-"Make it so nobody can use my piece!"

—"Out of the question. Master considers this a prime opportunity to train the disciples; your participation is mandatory,"-Mo Xi stated mercilessly.

Even if he felt Master's plan had already flopped, he still had to carry out Master's instructions.

Glancing at Mo Bei, Mo Xi added:
—"Just ignore them and be done with it."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Mo Bei huffed coldly, then turned her head and asked:
—"Where are you going, anyway?"

At this moment, they were walking toward the Library Pavilion. The gazes of more and more disciples in the vicinity were making Mo Bei uncomfortable.

—"You followed me all this way without even knowing where I was headed?"-Mo Xi shot her a sidelong look before explaining.-"I'm going to Master's 'Library Illusion Realm' to find Fourth Senior Brother. I need to commission a batch of custom weapons as tournament prizes... If you find it so annoying, you can just head back yourself."

—"Oh,"-Mo Bei blinked, yet didn't make a move to leave.

Mo Xi didn't mind, casually grumbling to himself:
—"Speaking of which, Fourth Senior Brother is truly bored out of his mind. The Library Pavilion is practically finished, yet he remains fixated on the plumbing mechanisms inside. Word is it can pump water without using a single spell, so he wants to build a pipeline connected all the way to the spiritual spring. But he can't find suitable materials."

—"Why go through so much trouble? If there are no materials, just use spells,"-Mo Bei remarked, baffled.-"We've used spells for so long, and no one ever found it inconvenient."

—"Right?! I can't understand him at all,"-Mo Xi sighed.

Yet the moment he stepped into the illusion realm, he immediately called out with boisterous enthusiasm:
—"Fourth Senior Brother, we're here! Ah, Fifth Senior Brother is here too!"

Guan Ye and Lu Shulong were standing before the pump room inside the Library Illusion Realm, deep in discussion with disciples from the Weapon Refinement Hall. Seeing Mo Xi approach, they broke into smiles:
—"Is something the matter?"

Mo Xi's grin widened even further. Instead of stating his business, he planted his hands on his hips, puffed out his chest, and strutted forward smugly:
—"Senior Brothers, do you notice anything different about me?"

[...Ah, here he goes again.]

Mo Bei thought with a deadpan expression.

Ever since Mo Xi gained that 'tattoo,' he had flaunted it to literally everyone he could find. Now he wasn't even sparing Fourth Senior Brother and Fifth Senior Brother, who were usually buried in their research!

Sure enough, Mo Xi preened with smug satisfaction:
—"This isn't an ordinary tattoo—it's the manifestation of a Mineral Spirit! Senior Brothers, you know what that signifies, don't you?!"

[Senior Brothers won't care about this sort of thing anyway, but my damned little brother truly deserves a beating.]

Mo Bei thought to herself, glancing toward Fourth Senior Brother and Fifth Senior Brother. She felt that even if they beat her twin brother to a pulp, it would be entirely justified. Though given their gentle dispositions, they probably wouldn't care... probably?

To Mo Bei's shock, Fourth Senior Brother Guan Ye's face darkened ominously.

Wearing a gloomy, eerie smile, Guan Ye stared at Mo Xi:
—"Now that is truly something to envy. However, I have heard that ordinary mineral spirits cannot fuse directly with a soul. Do you happen to know the underlying principle, Shulong?"

A question mark practically popped over Mo Xi's head. Realizing something was horribly wrong, he backed away several steps.

Yet Guan Ye and Lu Shulong gave him no chance to flee, lunging straight at him:
—"Jie jie jie, don't run!"-Guan Ye cackled.

—"Hee hee, let your senior brothers have a proper look!"-Lu Shulong grinned.

—"No! Stay back, don't come near me!"-Mo Xi shrieked.

—"Don't take off my clothes!"

—"Aaaaaagh!"

Listening to the screeching wails reminiscent of a slaughtered cat echoing from behind her, Mo Bei averted her gaze in sheer secondhand embarrassment—only to unluckily make direct eye contact with a disciple from the Weapon Refinement Hall.

—"...By the way, if I want to enchant a spiritual resonance onto my sword, how should I go about it?"-Mo Bei asked as if the thought had just struck her.

—"That depends on the sword's specifications!"-The disciple's eyes instantly lit up.-"Junior Sister Mo is indeed preparing to upgrade her weapon?! We have several recommended enchantments right here. Would Junior Sister Mo like to come over and take a look?"

[They feel even more passionate than usual...]

Mo Bei inquired as calmly as possible:
—"Did you bet on me to win? Or did you draft my piece for the tournament?"

—"Both!"-The disciple offered a bashful grin.-"I have immense faith in you, Junior Sister Mo! You're definitely the strongest sword cultivator in our entire sect."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

---

Half an hour later, Mo Bei led a thoroughly disheveled Mo Xi out of the illusion realm by his sleeve.

—"I'm going to kill them!"-Mo Xi wept in bitter accusation.

—"Senior Brothers did that because they like you,"-Mo Bei stated factually.

—"That was NOT affection!"-Mo Xi howled in righteous indignance.-"And what were you doing just now?! You didn't even try to rescue me... Wait, you actually got that sword enchanted?! No way... You aren't seriously trying to buff your combat stats, are you?!"

—"...It's only for the duration of the chess tournament anyway. Less than a month,"-Mo Bei muttered, turning her head away.

[This girl was actually swayed that easily?!]

[Is my sister... an idiot?!]

Mo Xi wondered in horror.
