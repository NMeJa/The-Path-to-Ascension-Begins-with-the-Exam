# Chapter 38: Prospective Students (第38章 生源)

Following the morning classroom observation, Zhou Hui escorted the distinguished guests to the Hall of Embracing Emptiness to dine.

Lan Pei immediately voiced his vehement protest:

—"Why must we go to that empty dirt hut?!"-Lan Pei complained.-"Are we supposed to wash down our meal with the [northwest wind](# "Literal CN: 喝西北风 (hē xīběifēng) — idiom meaning to drink the northwest wind; having nothing to eat or living on thin air")?! It's not every day your eldest disciple displays such culinary prowess—why not dine by the hot springs?"

[—"The space is too cramped, and our party is too large."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"Aren't you building a larger one?"](# "Speaker: Lan Pei")

[—"It hasn't been completed yet."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

As Zhou Hui spoke, he cast a distant gaze into the sky.

As they were walking toward the Hall of Embracing Emptiness, they could naturally see the public bathhouse under construction suspended in mid-air above the hall.

Had mortal construction been in progress, the site would naturally be loud, muddy, and thoroughly unfit for entertaining guests. In the cultivation world, however, no such issue existed.

In the cultivation world, there were two primary methods of architectural construction.

The first method was [artifact forging](# "Literal CN: 炼制 (liànzhì) — artifact forging / refining; casting structures in a forge like magical treasures").

The process was largely identical to refining magical treasures. First, designers—typically formation masters and architects—drafted blueprints and miniature scale models. Next, raw materials were cast into an artifact-refinement forge to be meticulously refined. What finally emerged could be paper cutouts like Ran Deng's creations, paper-folded architectural models like the Thought Pavilion, or various other miniature figurines. It didn't even have to be a model—it could simply be a tiny bean that, when placed upon desired ground by a cultivator, would instantly transform into a sprawling estate.

The floating spirit spring sky islands Guan Ye had gifted Zhou Hui previously had been forged in precisely this manner.

The second method was [on-site construction](# "Literal CN: 实地搭建 (shídì dājiàn) — on-site construction; assembling structures directly on premises using spellcraft and enchanted materials").

Superficially, this appeared similar to mortal building practices—assembling structures on the actual premises. The fundamental distinction, however, was that it was erected entirely through spellcraft.

Generally, cultivators needed only to gather sufficient materials, then cast spells upon them or affix talismans. The materials would then autonomously cut themselves and assemble themselves into the final architecture.

Neither construction method was inherently superior to the other.

Strictly speaking, the former was rather taxing on artifact refiners. The refiner's level of craftsmanship directly dictated the structural quality, and the production cycle was relatively longer, but it offered unparalleled portability.

The latter boasted more balanced, uniform quality—there was no risk of substandard structural flaws, though it lacked the dazzling special effects and bonus features forged by master craftsmen. While immobile, its construction cycle was considerably shorter.

Furthermore, the latter possessed another distinct advantage: should modifications be desired midway through construction, fellow cultivators could actively pitch in to assist.

For instance, body cultivators could help haul bricks; talisman cultivators could slap on extra talismans; and the most sophisticated were music cultivators. Should they be willing to play a melody, depending on the varying moods and tempos of different tunes, one could witness the construction materials performing dances to the rhythm, while the laid bricks and tiles would manifest ornate patterns corresponding to the musical score.

When constructing the public bathhouse, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had opted for the latter: on-site construction.

After all, an establishment like a bathhouse required conduits pre-connected to subterranean spirit springs; carrying it around in one's pocket served no purpose. Moreover, merely guaranteeing baseline structural quality was sufficient—there was no need to trouble an architect to attach flamboyant special effects or exotic functions. Conversely, as a public facility slated for use by all disciples in the days to come, having them supervise its construction and invest personal sweat into it was thoroughly essential.

Consequently, gazing upward from Zhou Hui's vantage point, one could observe neatly cut bricks and stones ascending into the firmament in rhythmic cadences, autonomously settling into place upon the half-finished structure. The scene looked for all the world like a [3D modeling wireframe assembly](# "Literal CN: 3D建模的搭建场景 — computer graphics wireframe / 3D rendering construction scene"). Every so often, passing disciples would fly up on swords attempting to help (wreak havoc), only to be beaned by thoroughly annoyed flying bricks and sent scurrying with their heads in their hands. Nearby, several disciples who had just begun studying music theory were strumming out-of-tune melodies on their instruments, providing a rather discordant live soundtrack for the spectacle.

It was... somewhat embarrassing.

—"Are you truly certain you wish to dine up there?"-Zhou Hui asked dubiously.

—"Either way, both are drafty, dilapidated shacks! At least the one up above has local flavor!"-Lan Pei insisted, sidling up beside Ran Zhen in an attempt to enlist an ally.-"Speaking of which, Grandmaster Ran, you haven't visited the spirit spring islands up above yet, have you? All of these were designed by your daughter... Oh, right, your eldest female disciple belongs to the Ran Clan, does she not?"-he asked, looking at Zhou Hui for confirmation.

Indeed: formation master Ran Zhen, who had unexpectedly arrived alongside the Plum-Bamboo couple, was none other than the biological father of Zhou Hui's third disciple—and Eldest Senior Sister of the Heavenly Spirit Sect—Ran Deng.

Only, Ran Deng had zero intention of granting him face merely because he was her father.

It wasn't that Ran Deng harbored any grudge against her father; rather, she had been worked half to death during this period. Having consecutively designed Qianxing Hall, the floating spirit spring islands, and the public bathhouse, the Eldest Senior Sister's popularity within the Heavenly Spirit Sect had skyrocketed to unprecedented heights. Countless disciples begged her to redesign their private caves to interface with the upcoming spirit spring plumbing. The chronically lazy Ran Deng had no desire to deal with anyone, holing up inside her cave dwelling to [play dead](# "Literal CN: 装死 (zhuāngsǐ) — playing dead; ignoring all messages, shirking responsibilities, and hiding away"). Even though her father had traveled ten thousand li to visit, she harbored zero inclination to show her face in public.

Father Ran had already been fretting over when he might see his daughter. Hearing that the aerial architecture currently under construction was actually his daughter's handiwork, his interest was instantly piqued.

—"Deng'er designed it?! Then I must certainly go see it!"-he exclaimed with unvarnished delight.

With two of the four distinguished guests taking a firm stance, the remaining Plum-Bamboo couple were indifferent either way. In truth, hearing that this was the accomplishment of "the unfilial daughter of the Ran Clan who refused to study arrays and was shipped off to Zhou Hui's tutelage for ideological reform" (Ran Deng: "..."), they too found themselves rather intrigued.

—"Then up we go, I suppose?"-Zhou Hui said, shaking his head at his guests' incomprehensible tastes.

—"In that case, Master, I shall have the dishes sent up immediately,"-Yuwen Xuan chimed in briskly from the side. When it came to entertaining guests, he genuinely possessed an uncanny, mysterious knack.-"Speaking of which, the weather is splendid today, and an open-air banquet does possess a certain rustic charm. It's only a pity the bathhouse isn't finished yet, so there's not much scenery to admire while dining. How about I set up some roasting pits down below to grill meats on the spot for the disciples to help themselves to? Looking down from above, it'll provide a lovely [touch of earthly bustle](# "Literal CN: 烟火气息 (yānhuǒ qìxī) — mortal smoke and fire; warmth, vitality, and bustling atmosphere of mortal life")."

Zhou Hui nodded:

—"Very well."

`*`

And so Yuwen Xuan dispatched orders.

When he set his mind to earnest business, he was actually quite efficient.

By the time Zhou Hui's party arrived in the vicinity of the 'construction site,' all labor had halted, the venue had been swept spotless, and banquet instruments, tableware, and delicacies had already been ferried up.

Fortunately, this was a bathhouse. Though unfinished, the foundation was complete and the floor tiles were laid. In particular, the communal bath section had been paved with white jade tiles etched with the silhouette of a Kunpeng. In its unfinished state, it bore an uncanny resemblance to a grand plaza. Once the strings and flutes played and the rich aroma of roast meat wafted from below, it differed little from an authentic banquet hall.

Then again, even if it looked improper, nobody cared—those seated here had [motives far beyond the wine](# "Literal CN: 醉翁之意不在酒 (zuì wēng zhī yì bù zài jiǔ) — the drinker's heart is not in the cup; having an ulterior motive beyond the apparent activity").

[—"In my eyes, this architecture is magnificent and grand! Even without being told, I could recognize the Ran Clan's pedigree behind its construction!"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"Indeed! Even with my meager knowledge of arrays, I can perceive that this design incorporates multiple formations. Truly worthy of a darling daughter nurtured jointly by the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Master Ran!"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

Those present closed their eyes and showered blind praise. At an occasion like this, flattery was never wrong.

Ran Zhen smiled so widely his mouth could scarcely close:

—"You flatter her, you flatter her! Merely my daughter's playful dabbling,"-he chuckled.-"Since childhood, she refused to study proper formations, spending every waking hour playing with tangram puzzles!"-Though he spoke thus, his eyes meticulously surveyed the architecture before him.

And then, his fifty percent joy swelled to a full hundred percent.

No matter what, Ran Deng hailed from the most orthodox formation clan in the cultivation world. When designing architecture, considering arrays was almost second nature and an instinctual baseline. Thus, when drafting this bathhouse, she had indeed taken future utilities and maintenance into account, weaving corresponding formations into the blueprint.

For example, within the bathhouse where the banquet was currently set, that Kunpeng was not merely ornamental—it concealed at least three intertwined formations. Not only did it sweep away dust and grime, but it also warded off evil, purified spiritual qi, accelerated qi circulation, and assisted cultivation!

Even through the critical lens of a professional grandmaster of arrays, Ran Zhen deemed this formation design thoroughly qualified.

—"Truth be told, all gratitude belongs to the Untiring Immortal Venerable!"-Ran Zhen's gratitude was now a hundred percent earnest.-"To think you could educate that [unruly wretch](# "Literal CN: 孽障 (nièzhàng) — karmic obstruction / sinful wretch; traditional self-deprecating Chinese parental term for an unruly child") of mine to such heights!"

—"No, I didn't teach her much,"-Zhou Hui confessed frankly.-"My own mastery of arrays is quite ordinary."

If she wished to study arrays, why would she ever be sent to him? Most of Zhou Hui's instruction to Ran Deng had focused merely on the circulation of spiritual energy. If one had to pinpoint the source of Ran Deng's meteoric leap in formation arts, it likely stemmed from being dragged by Qu Qianxing to play countless games of Immortal Chess recently. While Qu Qianxing gained a comprehensive grasp of secret realm architecture, Ran Deng, racking her brains to craft diverse chessboards, had developed fresh insights into arrays, enabling her to synthesize and apply them seamlessly to the architecture before their eyes.

Thinking of this, Zhou Hui said earnestly:

—"It is rather that Ah-Ran's foundational training was laid well, which enabled such progress."

—"Far from it! How could I not know what she was like at home?"-Ran Zhen laughed bitterly.-"It was only because we were at our wits' end that my late mother entrusted that little menace to your care."

—"...It wasn't quite that dire,"-Zhou Hui said slowly.

Zhou Hui had enjoyed generational ties with the Ran Clan—which meant that while Zhou Hui remained unchanged, the 'Ah-Ran' addressed by his lips had rotated through several generations. For reasons unknown, array masters in this realm were notoriously short-lived; and the Ran Clan, as the pinnacle of array lineages, was the most prominent example. Most were considered fortunate if they reached the lifespan of a Golden Core cultivator.

Such a lifespan was certainly respectable for mortals, but for cultivators, it was tragically brief.

It was only thanks to the Ran Clan's secret inheritance art—passing down the preceding clan head's cultivation to the next generation—that the current Ran Zhen possessed a Mahayana stage cultivation. Theoretically, his designated successor should have been his only daughter, Ran Deng.

Yet Ran Deng adamantly refused to study formations. Even breaking several feather dusters across her back failed to cure her obstinacy.

Coincidentally, back when Zhou Hui was preparing to establish the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he had invited Ran Deng's grandmother—the reigning matriarch of the Ran Clan at the time—to inspect the sect's feng shui. Little did anyone expect that upon laying eyes on the Untiring Immortal Venerable, little Ran Deng felt an extraordinary affinity, clutching the Venerable's thigh in tears and begging to become his disciple.

Thinking their daughter was beyond saving anyway and that placing her under Untiring's wing would at least keep her from going astray, the Ran Clan consented.

What the Ran Clan could never have imagined was that when little Ran Deng begged to become a disciple back then, aside from fancying Zhou Hui's Library Pavilion, she had shrewdly observed that the Untiring Immortal Venerable's 'untiring' nature was [strict with oneself, yet lenient with others](# "Literal CN: 严于律己，宽于待人 (yán yú lǜ jǐ, kuān yú dài rén) — traditional Confucian virtue: demanding high standards of oneself while being tolerant and forgiving toward others")!

In other words, so long as one met Zhou Hui's baseline requirements, Zhou Hui would never force his disciples to grind as tirelessly as he did, nor compel them to study subjects they despised. Instead, he encouraged them to discover their own strengths. Better yet, this Master spent the vast majority of his time in secluded cultivation!

This was infinitely superior to an array clan's single-subject curriculum where failing to learn meant getting beaten black and blue.

And so, Ran Deng had comfortably loafed away in the Heavenly Spirit Sect for over a century, only recently growing somewhat busy due to the sheer volume of facilities needing construction.

Zhou Hui was well aware of Ran Deng's nature, yet had never sought to correct it.

In his view, if the world had room for busybodies destined to toil like himself, it ought to accommodate easygoing, unhurried souls like Ran Deng as well. Besides, it wasn't as though Ran Deng studied nothing. On the contrary, she had dutifully read through all of her clan's array tomes; she simply had no immediate desire to become an array master, harboring far greater interest in diverse miscellanea. But acquired knowledge would never betray a person. Even if temporarily idle, the day would come when it proved its worth.

In fact, in the Heavenly Dao novel script, where Ran Deng was slain by the protagonist due to her meager combat prowess, Zhou Hui had never considered it Ran Deng's fault.

[Is the victim being weak her fault? That is the murderer's fault!]

Therefore, in this lifetime, Zhou Hui harbored zero intention of forcing Ran Deng to change. He merely planned to forge a magical artifact capable of housing divine souls for this disciple; once her 'death' fooled the Heavenly Dao, he would reconstruct a physical body for her, allowing her to continue living her life.

Nevertheless, despite having planned everything out, facing Father Ran's deeply moved gaze still left Zhou Hui feeling slightly guilty.

Especially considering that although he maintained warm relations with the Ran Clan, that bond belonged to Ran Deng's grandmother and Ran Deng herself; with the current clan head, Ran Zhen, he wasn't particularly familiar.

—"Speaking of which, what brings Master Ran to the Heavenly Spirit Sect so unexpectedly?"-Zhou Hui asked, stiffly pivoting the subject.

Ran Zhen's arrival had indeed been unexpected.

Back when the Plum-Bamboo couple announced they were coming to retrieve Su Xin, they had never mentioned that another student's parent would be tagging along for a campus visit.

—"Ah, I could never presume to be called 'Master'! As when my late mother was with us, please simply call me Ah-Zhen,"-Ran Zhen said flusteredly.-"Truth be told, I didn't come on a formal visit. Rather, my eldest disciple in the East Hedge Garden discovered a rather intriguing ancient ruin deep within the Abyss Sea, but was stymied by the ancient formations outside, so he invited me to take a look. Hearing that the Plum-Bamboo Immortal Couple were traveling to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I simply hitched a ride along the way."

—"Isn't that exceedingly perilous?"-Su Mei frowned. Although he had granted Ran Zhen a ride, he was evidently hearing this for the first time.-"Those fellows in the East Hedge Garden are a pack of lunatics. There is no need to get tangled up with them."

Only Su Mei could deliver such an assessment so bluntly.

The so-called [East Hedge Garden](# "Literal CN: 东篱苑 (Dōnglí Yuàn) — elite research circle of top-tier Tribulation Transcendence and Mahayana cultivators exploring ancient ruins; reference to Tao Yuanming's poem: 'Plucking chrysanthemums beneath the eastern hedge'") was an elite research circle composed of topmost cultivators at the Tribulation Transcendence and Mahayana stages. Most of its members had reached the very apex of cultivation; obsessed with the unknown, they spent their days conducting archaeological excavations in the ancient god-demon battlefield deep within the Abyss Sea. Before taking in Yuwen Xuan, Zhou Hui had once been a member of that circle, which was precisely how he had first crossed paths with the Ran Clan.

Remembering this a beat too late, Su Mei hurried to add to Zhou Hui:

—"Ah, forgive me, Untiring. I didn't mean you."

—"No, you are quite right. It is indeed perilous enough there,"-Zhou Hui replied.-"And sorely understaffed."

Which was precisely why he had contemplated applying the online game secret realm model to the Abyss Sea.

—"Still, go I must!"-Ran Zhen replied, his eyes sparkling with unbridled passion. His love for formation research was thoroughly genuine.-"Our Ran Clan's array foundations originated from the Abyss Sea. Who knows—perhaps this discovery will unveil a brand-new formation!"

Alright, this one was already mad.

Come to think of it, if they weren't mad, the Ran Clan wouldn't have flirted with the East Hedge Garden for so many years.

The Plum-Bamboo couple mused inwardly.

—"Besides, this is likely the last thing I can do for my disciples,"-Ran Zhen sighed.

Why did those words sound so ominous?

Those seated around the table exchanged glances.

Finally, Xin Zhu spoke up:

—"Little Ran, you are still so young; surely you aren't about to die?"

—"It is a prophecy from the Heavenly Book,"-Ran Zhen said.-"A close friend of mine visited the Heavenly Book Tower last time and caught a glimpse of my destiny. Though the exact timeframe was unclear, the prophecy foretold that the Ran Clan would be destroyed in my hands. I reasoned that heavenly destiny cannot be altered. If it must come to pass, perishing beneath the Abyss Sea for the sake of a new formation is at least a dignified death for me."

Speaking thus, Ran Zhen raised his wine cup to Zhou Hui:

—"Therefore, Immortal Venerable, I entrust Deng'er into your care."

—"..."-Zhou Hui felt even more guilty.

He knew exactly what was going on with Ran Zhen.

It was written in the Heavenly Dao novel script.

In reality, the Ran Clan's downfall occurred after the Heavenly Spirit Sect's destruction.

And it was precisely because Ran Deng perished that Ran Zhen developed a bitter vendetta against the protagonist. As luck would have it, he 'ran into' the protagonist in an Abyss Sea secret realm, harbored murderous intent, and was counter-killed by the protagonist. Several of his disciples were slain alongside him, resulting in the annihilation of this grand array lineage. Even the supreme array arrayed upon his person was gifted to the protagonist, ensuring the protagonist's future cave dwelling could never be easily breached.

—Thinking back carefully, much like Guan Ye, he was essentially a resource delivery package for the protagonist in the late game.

Zhou Hui lifted his wine cup:

—"There is no need to be so pessimistic. From the sound of it, the Heavenly Book did not specify a timeframe; perhaps it is thousands of years from now?"

—"Indeed! That Heavenly Book nonsense is utterly meaningless—believing in it only scares oneself half to death,"-Lan Pei said.

This old fox couldn't care less about hypocritical mortal etiquette. The moment he sat down, he had clutched his beloved roast chicken and devoured it ravenously. Now that his belly was half full, he finally found the mood to chime in:

—"Didn't you just say your disciple summoned you? Could he possibly harm you? Though the formation may be arduous, surely it won't prove fatal."

—"I thank you both for your auspicious words,"-Ran Zhen said.

Pausing, he smiled wryly:

—"It's just that hearing this prophecy right before my departure threw me into disarray. Thinking carefully, Master Lan Pei's disciple is also stationed there. No matter how severe an injury, surely it can be mended."

—"Which disciple?"-Lan Pei asked instead. But before Ran Zhen could answer, the old fox shook its head.-"Forget it, I don't care to know. They've already graduated anyway. My only request is that when they malpractice someone to death, they don't drag my name into it."

—"How could it come to that?! Every one of your disciples is a renowned divine physician!"-Ran Zhen said.

—"Indeed, it was all thanks to you that our child is alive and kicking,"-Su Mei said.

—"Moreover, one truly shouldn't take the Heavenly Book too seriously,"-Xin Zhu followed after her husband, addressing Ran Zhen.-"A prophecy once claimed our son would suffer a catastrophic tribulation in a secret realm, yet wasn't he miraculously healed by the Divine Physician?"

Lan Pei: "..." It desperately wanted to scream that the little brat Su Xin had nothing whatsoever to do with it—the boy had been bouncing around in full health from the very beginning.

—"Though the part about him maturing is quite true,"-Su Mei said with heartfelt gratification.-"In the past, the mere mention of entering a secret realm for trials made that boy shrink from hardship and shirk duty. Yet after this setback, he actually learned to [turn shame into courage](# "Literal CN: 知耻而后勇 (zhī chǐ ér hòu yǒng) — traditional idiom: knowing shame and then displaying courage; spurred by humiliation to strive harder"), taking the initiative to request a trial run!"

Indeed: that was precisely why Su Xin was absent today.

Upon seeing his parents last night, he had claimed that his ordeal in the secret realm had deeply wounded his pride, but as a real man, he had to confront past traumas, insisting on entering the secret realm once more to reclaim his lost confidence!

The Plum-Bamboo couple had zero understanding of what actually transpired inside the secret realm. Swallowing his tall tale hook, line, and sinker, they joyfully sent their "maturing darling" off to undergo trials.

Only the Plum-Bamboo couple could think like that. Even the newly arrived Ran Zhen sensed something was amiss.

Thus, everyone present reserved judgment on the couple's claims.

In short: as long as they were happy.

Viewed from this angle, did it prove that the Heavenly Book wasn't set in stone?

Unfortunately, the Heavenly Book and the Heavenly Dao were not the same entity, and Zhou Hui had no idea what mapping relationship existed between them, deciding to observe further.

Ran Zhen held similar thoughts.

—"I know one cannot pin the entire future upon heavenly fate. However, having the honor of auditing the Venerable's lecture during this visit to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I feel immensely enlightened. My Deng'er has made tremendous strides under the Venerable's tutelage as well, leaving our Ran Clan with no way to ever repay such kindness,"-Ran Zhen stood up with his wine cup in hand, bowing deeply toward Zhou Hui.-"It is merely that I harbor dread over the Heavenly Book's prophecy. I brazenly beseech the Immortal Venerable: could you perhaps permit several branch disciples of our Ran Clan to enroll in the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Even if not as formal disciples, serving merely as [sweeping and washing boys](# "Literal CN: 洒洗童子 (sǎxǐ tóngzǐ) — menial apprentice / chore-boy tasked with sweeping and washing") would suffice."

[After laying the groundwork for so long and selling so much misery, that was your true goal all along?]

Lan Pei rolled its eyes.

Still, Untiring lecturing in person—no, even without the Untiring Immortal Venerable lecturing in person, word had it that all of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks were personally authored by him, which alone was more than enough! Had its own disciples not already completed their apprenticeships, it would have dispatched a few over for an exchange program... Wait, didn't his clan have several promising little fox cubs? Could he have them disguise themselves as humans and send them over?

Even Master Lan Pei entertained such thoughts, to say nothing of the Heavenly Dao Alliance cultivators, whose hearts stirred with profound temptation.

However, as the overarching coalition governing the realm's immortal sects, the Heavenly Dao Alliance possessed its own pride, naturally unwilling to send their descendants to such a remote, minor sect. Instead, several high-ranking cultivators sent divine sense transmissions to the Plum-Bamboo couple:

—"Perhaps we could test the Untiring Immortal Venerable's pedagogical methods in the Heavenly Dao Alliance? I've heard that examination system is quite remarkable. In the past, we always focused solely on martial duels, neglecting comprehension of core techniques."

—"Indeed,"-the Plum-Bamboo couple affirmed.

They thought of their precious son.

Just traveling out once had resulted in severe injuries. Entrusting Su Xin directly to the Untiring Immortal Venerable was something they simply couldn't bear. But the boy had finally shown signs of improvement (?), and letting him regress was out of the question—wouldn't that mean his recent tribulations were suffered in vain? In that case, let us try teaching him according to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's methods! At the very least, institute monthly exams to test him! Furthermore, to prevent the boy from feeling rebellious, it would be best if all disciples across the entire Heavenly Dao Alliance underwent the exact same testing.

(Su Xin: [Why does it suddenly feel so chilly?])

While the guests harbored their respective calculations, Zhou Hui looked at Ran Zhen, his brow furrowing with difficulty.

In truth, had this request come from anyone else present, he would not have felt so conflicted; only Ran Zhen's plea posed a dilemma.

Because the Ran Clan's catastrophe was triggered by the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"There is really no need..."-Zhou Hui began, seeking a reason to decline. Yet pausing mid-sentence, his thoughts took a turn:

[Actually, it's not entirely unfeasible...]

Granted, the fates of the Ran father and daughter were dictated by the Heavenly Dao, but the destinies of other Ran Clan members were not.

Moreover, descendants of the Ran Clan were all top-tier formation masters. While this profession wasn't known for sprinting speed, their defensive and escape capabilities ranked among the top three in the realm—otherwise, in the Heavenly Dao script, Qu Qianxing wouldn't have entrusted the surviving disciples to Ran Deng to lead their escape, knowing full well her combat strength was negligible. Outstanding formation masters among them could even utilize arrays to deceive lightning tribulations and the Heavenly Dao itself!

If events truly unfolded according to the Heavenly Dao's script in the future, and Zhou Hui needed to deceive the Heavenly Dao by sealing Ran Deng's divine soul into an artifact, having several Ran Clan disciples on the scene would be perfect—they could escort Ran Deng's divine soul back to the Ran Clan.

Thinking to this point, Zhou Hui nodded after all:

—"Very well. The sect's [minor recruitment](# "Literal CN: 门派小招 (ménpài xiǎozhāo) — annual autumn recruitment for rogue cultivators; equivalent of corporate social hiring (社招)") will commence shortly; have the Ran Clan disciples come over then. It will be just in time for the [entrance examination](# "Literal CN: 开学考试 (kāixué kǎoshì) — placement exam administered at the beginning of a school year") and the [new semester](# "Literal CN: 新学期 (xīn xuéqī) — new academic term / semester")'s curriculum."

Ran Zhen was overjoyed, momentarily forgetting to inquire what an "entrance examination" and "new semester" actually were.

The Plum-Bamboo couple exchanged glances, then asked Zhou Hui:

—"Will the Heavenly Spirit Sect's minor recruitment proceed as scheduled?"

—"Naturally,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

The minor recruitment was essentially the annual autumn recruitment conducted by cultivation sects. Depending on the year, sects distinguished between 'major recruitment' and 'minor recruitment.'

A [major recruitment](# "Literal CN: 门派大招 (ménpài dàzhāo) — major sect recruitment conducted once every few years for mortal children with spirit roots within sect territory") was generally conducted once every few years depending on each sect's circumstances, selecting children with spirit roots from towns and clans within their territorial domain, bringing them collectively to the mountain gate for trials, and hand-picking the exceptionally gifted among them as formal disciples.

A [minor recruitment](# "Literal CN: 门派小招 (ménpài xiǎozhāo) — minor sect recruitment conducted annually for rogue cultivators seeking entry"), on the other hand, was akin to [social hiring](# "Literal CN: 社招 (shèzhāo) — corporate recruitment term for hiring experienced talent directly from society rather than campus graduates"). It took place every single year, tailored for rogue cultivators and wandering immortals seeking to align themselves with a sect. They would journey to their preferred sect's mountain gate on their own initiative and kowtow to beg for admission. Generally, it created little fanfare—though some colossal sects with overwhelming applicants would stage theatrical spectacles like the Grand Ascension Assembly.

For a remote, minor sect like the Heavenly Spirit Sect, drawing a dozen-odd applicants during the annual minor recruitment was already considered normal, and most arrived solely upon hearing the Untiring Immortal Venerable's renowned title.

Therefore, in past years, the Heavenly Spirit Sect barely paid attention to recruitment schedules, and virtually every applicant who showed up was accepted—after all, possessing the capability to locate this secluded mountain was already a testament to no small skill.

Zhou Hui looked at the Plum-Bamboo couple:

—"Is there any issue?"

—"...You likely are unaware, but Carefree Palace has recently been smearing your reputation far and wide,"-Su Mei frowned.-"I fear it may impact your sect's minor recruitment."

—"If prospective disciples abandon us over a few stray rumors, there is no need for such people to come in the first place,"-Zhou Hui shook his head unconcernedly, asking in passing,-"Speaking of Carefree Palace, I tasked you two with having the Heavenly Dao Alliance oversee the public administration of the secret realm—did that cause you any trouble?"

—"Not at all; your arrangement actually did us a tremendous favor,"-Xin Zhu replied. Remembering something, a subtle expression crossed her face.-"In truth, Carefree Palace is quite pleased. They believe you acted out of 'fear.'"

—"Fear?"-Zhou Hui tilted his head in bewilderment, expressing sheer incomprehension at such a bizarre line of thought.

`*`

[—"Of course he is afraid! That person is unquestionably terrified!"](# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Cultivator")-a voice proclaimed impassionedly.

A memorial banquet hosted by the Carefree Palace to mourn Sovereign Immortal Chana, who had been slain by Zhou Hui—even though, after spending over a month searching, let alone Sovereign Immortal Chana's remains, they hadn't recovered so much as a single splinter from the Thought Pavilion.
