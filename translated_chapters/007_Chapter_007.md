# Chapter 7: Fortune (第7章 财产)

—"As expected of Second Senior Brother! Your test paper really sold for nearly the price of a Foundation Establishment Pill,"-Mo Xi said adorably, looking up with his round, kitten-like eyes.

Qu Qianxing's face darkened:

—"Don't [play cute with me](# "Literal CN: 卖乖 (mài guāi) — acting obedient or cute to win favor or avoid blame")! If Master hadn't given his consent, I would never have given that paper to you,"-Qu Qianxing snapped coldly.

Mo Xi stuck out his tongue.

[Master didn't give his consent—he just didn't care whether I sold the papers or not.]

[Still, Second Senior Brother is so easy to coax. As long as you bring up Master's name, he'll agree to anything.]

Naturally, Mo Xi wasn't about to say that aloud.

Not only did he keep it to himself, but he even put on an expression of helplessness:

—"There was no other way. Master's standards are so high, and my ability is simply lacking... Second Senior Brother, do you think Master really just did this on a whim?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"It's not as if you only met Master today—you should know better! With our Master's meticulous nature, even a spur-of-the-moment whim wouldn't be treated lightly,"-Qu Qianxing declared proudly.

Mo Xi knew it. In the eyes of his Second Senior Brother, there was simply no flaw in Master.

At that moment, however, Qu Qianxing paused, a peculiar look entering his expression:

—"Still, these things truly don't look like something cobbled together on a whim,"-Qu Qianxing murmured.

As he spoke, he turned his gaze forward.

Floating before Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi was a luminescent white light screen. Green mountains, emerald waters, and floating pavilions drifted across its surface—a complete panoramic map of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Nor was it merely a static map. When Mo Xi reached out and touched the glowing screen, a small building perched on a hill in the lower-left corner illuminated. A new interface popped up before them, filled with densely packed lines of text:

> **[Main Quest: Host a Grand Unified Examination]**
> 
> **[Quest Requirements: The cultivation progress of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's disciples is uneven. Your Master wishes to ascertain their true proficiency. Upholding the principles of fairness, justice, and transparency, organize a Grand Unified Examination for the entire sect!]**
> 
> **[Remarks: Final evaluation will be rated across four grades—[A, B, C, and D](# "Literal CN: 甲乙丙丁 (jiǎ-yǐ-bǐng-dīng) — the Four Heavenly Stems used for ranking or grading")—based on examination preparations, candidate performance, and side quest completion. Please act according to your abilities.]**

Since there was a main quest, there were naturally side quests as well:

> **[Side Quest 1: Expand the Palace of Silence (Part I)]**
> 
> **[Quest Requirements: The Palace of Silence requires an examination venue capable of seating the disciples of the entire sect.]**
> 
> **[Side Quest 2: Deploy Exam-Proctoring Formations]**
> 
> **[Quest Requirements: When the entire sect sits for an examination simultaneously, opportunists looking for shortcuts will inevitably emerge. To prevent regrettable incidents, deploy the following formations in advance (see Appendix) to eliminate all possibility of cheating.]**
> 
> **[Side Quest 3: Construct a Sundial Clock]**
> 
> **[Quest Requirements: Both examinations and daily lectures require more precise timekeeping. Design and construct a sundial clock that divides one [shichen](# "Literal CN: 时辰 (shíchen) — traditional Chinese unit of time equal to two modern hours") into one hundred and twenty equal parts.]**
> 
> **[Side Quest 4: Recompile the Disciples' [Yellow Registers](# "Literal CN: 黄册 (huáng cè) — historically, imperial population and census registers bound in yellow paper; here, the sect's official disciple registry ledgers")]**
> 
> **[Quest Requirements: ...]**

Staring at these quests, Mo Xi let out a heartfelt sigh:

—"Looking at these arrangements, it seems Master isn't planning to hold just one single exam. If I didn't already know that 'Heavenly Secret Exam Paper' business was just a rumor, I would almost believe it myself,"-Mo Xi admitted.

—"Heavenly Secret Exam Paper?"-Qu Qianxing asked, perplexed.

—"Second Senior Brother, you didn't know?"-Mo Xi gave Qu Qianxing an odd look.-"There's an outer sect disciple who, whether by pure luck or some other reason, actually succeeded in Foundation Establishment right after finishing Master's exam paper! Those outer sect disciples started spreading rumors that Master comprehended heavenly secrets during his last heavenly tribulation and hid them inside the exam paper to test us—to see if there's any [Child of Destiny](# "Literal CN: 天选之子 (tiān xuǎn zhī zǐ) — the chosen one of destiny / protagonist trope") among us."

Qu Qianxing listened with a furrowed brow.

He certainly knew about the rumor; in fact, he had been involved throughout the entire ordeal:

Originally, Zhou Hui hadn't cared in the slightest about pursuing the issue of Gan Tang possessing the exam paper. But Qu Qianxing had refused to let it go. Feeling that he had failed to discipline the inner sect disciples properly—thereby allowing the exam paper to leak and causing him to lose face in front of Master—he had insisted on investigating thoroughly. In the end, his search had led straight to his own lazy youngest junior brother.

Throughout that process, Qu Qianxing had witnessed firsthand how a rumor was born from nothing.

Qu Qianxing had always regarded the worldly masses as foolish and hadn't taken the rumor to heart. But now, standing before Master's quest panel and hearing the rumor repeated, a sudden spark of inspiration struck him:

—"Wait, what did you just say?"-Qu Qianxing blurted out.

[—"Child of Destiny?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[—"No, the line before that!"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

[—"Testing us? Or that he comprehended heavenly secrets in the tribulation?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"That's the one!"-Qu Qianxing's eyes shone with realization.-"Of course, I don't mean there are actual heavenly secrets hidden in the exam paper! But don't you think Master's behavior since the tribulation has been rather peculiar? He must have had a profound epiphany during the lightning tribulation! That's why he didn't enter secluded cultivation, why he abandoned the Ascension Platform, and why he's devoting his full attention to rebuilding the sect and elevating our collective strength—all to prepare for his next tribulation transcendence!"

—"That... actually makes a lot of sense..."-Mo Xi nodded blankly.

At the very least, this explanation sounded far more plausible than Zhou Hui's own claim: *"I'm never facing heavenly tribulations again."*

Indeed, Mo Xi hadn't believed for a second that Master would truly abandon tribulations either.

Even though he had studied under Master for the shortest time, he still knew how fanatical Master was about cultivation. For a man like him to say he would renounce tribulations and give up on ascending to immortality was simply out of the question.

What was more, Zhou Hui had never shown any interest in renovating the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the past.

Though the Palace of Silence bore the title of a "palace," that was purely out of cultivation world tradition. Cultivators considered themselves above mortal emperors once they stepped onto the immortal path, so they loved naming their dwellings "palaces" and "halls." Zhou Hui had always been too lazy to waste brain cells on such trivialities, so he had simply adopted the conventional naming style. In reality, the Palace of Silence was nothing more than a large hall capable of holding the inner sect disciples. The other buildings across the Heavenly Spirit Sect were much the same—so much so that every visitor who came to pay respects invariably praised the Untiring Immortal Venerable for being "austere," "resilient," "true to his inner heart," and "returning to pristine simplicity."

Yet this time, Master was actually willing to renovate the Palace of Silence.

Even if it was just for the sake of an examination!

Thinking up to this point, Mo Xi rolled his heterochromatic eyes thoughtfully.

Unlike Qu Qianxing, although he had also noticed Master's "peculiarities," he possessed zero interest in getting to the bottom of them. His only thought was how to exploit the present situation to his advantage.

Chimning in with Qu Qianxing's deductions, Mo Xi offered a beaming smile:

—"Since this examination is all part of Master's grand plan, then naturally I want to make everything as flawless as possible. The spirit stones for renovating the Palace of Silence—"-Mo Xi began.

—"Do not forget: this is Master's punishment for you,"-Qu Qianxing cut in, his expression turning frosty in an instant.

The spirit stones of the Heavenly Spirit Sect belonged exclusively to Master. Even for his most pampered youngest junior brother, misappropriating them was strictly out of the question.

Qu Qianxing managed the sect's finances, and on this matter alone, there was zero room for negotiation.

—"By permitting you to trade the exam papers he authored, hasn't Master already provided you with the solution to your problem?"-Qu Qianxing stated matter-of-factly.-"Doing it flawlessly is your basic duty to begin with."

[That's not it at all! I only scalped those test papers because you refused to give me an excuse to request funding, so I had to make my own money!]-Mo Xi thought.

Yet he could hardly say that aloud.

Right at that moment, a crisp *ding* resonated from the light screen. The characters **[Spirit Stones +1,080]** appeared at the bottom of the interface. Without a doubt, Xiang Yi had just sold another compilation of test papers.

—"See? You will have accumulated enough spirit stones to complete the quest in no time,"-Qu Qianxing said with gratification.

Mo Xi suddenly felt that those spirit stones didn't smell nearly as sweet anymore.

* * *

> **[Spirit Stones +1,080]**

At the very same moment, Zhou Hui was also watching the light screen.

Naturally, this modest income of spirit stones meant next to nothing to the current Zhou Hui.

He had long moved past the days of Qi Condensation, Foundation Establishment, and Golden Core, when he had to scrape by miserably for every meager handful of stones. As a grand powerhouse of the Tribulation Transcendence stage, aside from the Heavenly Dao holding a sliver of leverage over him, there were practically no beings left in this world capable of restraining his will. If he so desired, he could easily stake claim to vast territories or even unify the realm and crown himself Human Sovereign—it was just that Zhou Hui had zero interest in such pursuits.

However, when spirit stones concerned the matter of cultivation, Zhou Hui was by no means modest.

Although the cave abodes of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were crude and its disciples few, giving it the appearance of a third-rate sect, the Heavenly Spirit Mountain range was, in truth, an authentic blessed grotto-heaven. Beneath its peaks lay three full-scale spiritual veins, alongside countless minable spirit stone deposits—the largest of which could easily sustain a major sect for a thousand years.

Yet Zhou Hui had barely mined these veins at all.

In truth, Zhou Hui harbored little desire for a lavish lifestyle. He had chosen this resource-rich spiritual land largely because he had grown up terrified of poverty, developing a [hamster hoarder mentality](# "Literal CN: 仓鼠癖 (cāngshǔ pǐ) — hoarding tendencies, saving resources for a rainy day"). Unless faced with a critical juncture, he refused to touch them. In fact, even at this supposed "critical juncture"—having suffered severe internal injuries from the heavenly tribulation—Zhou Hui still hadn't tapped into them. Instead, he had entrusted their management to Qu Qianxing, intending to pass them down to future generations of the Heavenly Spirit Sect as the sect's foundational ballast.

With Zhou Hui holding such an attitude, Qu Qianxing naturally followed his Master's lead to the letter.

Qu Qianxing had always been exceptionally conservative regarding the excavation of spirit mines. Every year, he formulated strict mining quotas based on sect expenditures, extracting only the exact number of spirit stones required, while subjecting miners to repeated audits to prevent anyone from smuggling out a single stone beyond the permitted quota.

Unfortunately, even precautions like those were useless against certain [cheaters with plot armor](# "Literal CN: 挂B (guà bī) — gaming slang for someone using hacks/cheats").

According to the events of the "novel," after a certain incident, the Protagonist chosen by the Heavenly Dao was penalized by a sect steward and sent to the mines to dig for spirit stones. Even for cultivators, that was grueling manual labor. Who could have foreseen that the Protagonist, upon entering the mines, would act like a rat dropped into a sack of rice? He had conjured an avatar to fool the overseers while devouring massive quantities of spirit stones to repair his mediocre spirit roots, breaking through straight into Foundation Establishment inside the mine! This triggered his expulsion from the sect, leaving him seething with indignation. Upon fleeing the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he had even executed a destructive, scorched-earth excavation on the mine, shattering the spiritual vein beneath and completely draining the resource dry.

Zhou Hui hadn't paid much heed to this "minor matter" previously, but seeing the spirit stone revenue Mo Xi earned from scalping exam papers suddenly jogged his memory.

—"Right. Since it won't be used up anyway, wouldn't it be better to squander it all rather than let someone ruin it?"-Zhou Hui muttered under his breath.

He raised his hands in a posture resembling plucking a zither. The air beneath his fingertips rippled with iridescent white waves, coalescing into—

A keyboard.

There was no helping it; Zhou Hui had grown too accustomed to using one in his previous world. Designing something else on the fly simply felt awkward.

Zhou Hui rattled away on the conjured keyboard, keying the spirit mine's geological data into the light screen. He highlighted the most dazzling, radiant sections of the mine and projected them for the two disciples standing behind him:

—"This way, you won't need to worry about funding for the construction of the Library Pavilion. So long as your architectural designs are sound, you are free to smash not just the Ascension Platform into it, but the entire spirit mine if you so choose,"-Zhou Hui said.

If Zhou Hui had spoken these words to his youngest disciple, Mo Xi, the boy would have ascended on the spot from pure ecstasy. But the two disciples summoned before Zhou Hui at this moment—Third Disciple Ran Deng and Fourth Disciple Guan Ye—showed a distinct lack of enthusiasm.

—"Master, I feel the original Library Pavilion is already quite sufficient,"-Ran Deng said with an air of sincerity, though the exhaustion lingering in her eyes betrayed her desperate desire to slack off.-"When I traveled with my father to visit major sects in my youth, none possessed a pavilion as grand as ours already is. There is simply no need to squander spirit stones. Such gorgeous spirit stones—isn't it wonderful to just leave them resting in the vein?"

Fourth Disciple Guan Ye, on the other hand, was staring at the light screen with gleaming eyes.

What caught his attention was not the massive chunks of supreme-grade spirit stones, but the light screen itself.

—"This light screen appears extraordinarily convenient, Master,"-Guan Ye asked with genuine curiosity.-"Is this a new magical treasure you refined?"

—"Mm,"-Zhou Hui replied, feeling a headache coming on.

Setting Ran Deng aside for the moment, he answered Guan Ye:

—"It is something I constructed... a 'System.'"-Zhou Hui said.
