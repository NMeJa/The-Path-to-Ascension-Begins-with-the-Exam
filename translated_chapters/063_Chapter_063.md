# Chapter 63: A Difficult Problem (第63章 难题)

—"Tsk,"-Qu Qianxing sneered coldly upon hearing the outcry.

The man who had shrieked, along with several others stirring restlessly in the crowd, instantly collapsed to the floor as though struck by lightning, convulsing violently.

Yet the remaining people within Huaixu Hall were not in the least reassured. If their previous anxiety had been sparked by the booming thunder and the agitators' screams, their current dread stemmed entirely from Qu Qianxing's ruthless display.

They stared at Qu Qianxing like panicked small animals, as though the figure before them were not an ethereal beauty, but a ravenous, man-eating tiger.

Seeing their terrified expressions, the cold detachment in Qu Qianxing's eyes deepened.

As the chief administrator of Heavenly Spirit Sect and the designated successor to the position of Sect Leader, even though this Tianling Auction had been personally guided by Zhou Hui and planned by Mo Xi, every single proposal and dossier had crossed Qu Qianxing's desk for final review.

Naturally, that included these "collaborators."

With Qu Qianxing's meticulous nature, he would never handle them casually. He remembered the precise background of every single soul present, including the man who had just screamed at the top of his lungs.

The man was a secondhand broker from Longmen Town in the western Central Domain, possessing a meager cultivation of merely Qi Condensation Level 3. Usually, he scraped a living buying and reselling spiritual poultry, eggs, and ordinary foodstuffs. This time, by sheer coincidence, he had taken the Kunpeng shuttle to the Immortal Bazaar, arriving just as the craze for cakes and pastries exploded. He had reaped astronomical profits, and as an egg-and-poultry supplier to the cake shops, he had earned the qualification to sign this long-term contract.

Theoretically, this man ought to be eternally grateful to the Immortal Bazaar. Yet intelligence reports revealed that prior to this, he had been in frequent, secretive contact with traveling merchants closely affiliated with the Palace of Carefree Wandering, suspected of plotting to use his supplier credentials to pry loose the cake recipes.

In truth, it was not merely him, nor was it solely Carefree Palace. With the auction's spectacular success laid bare for all to witness, numerous commercial guilds and immortal cities across the continent were actively headhunting the creators of the novel commodities showcased at the bazaar, while others brazenly plagiarized and reverse-engineered the products outright.

This fellow had merely heard a few claps of thunder before bellowing hysterically. Who knew whether he was acting out of a guilty conscience, or had simply been bribed to deliberately manufacture panic among the crowd?

Regardless of the motive, Qu Qianxing had zero interest in parsing it.

After all, such was the inherent depravity of human nature.

When something brought them immense profit, everything was sweet and rosy;

Yet the moment disaster loomed on the horizon, they sought only to preserve their own skins, [burning the bridge after crossing the river](# "Literal CN: 过河拆桥 (guò hé chāi qiáo) — destroying the bridge once across; abandoning benefactors after serving one's purpose").

Qu Qianxing was acutely aware that his worldview was extreme and cynical, perpetually shackled by the trauma of his past, rendering him incapable of treating every soul with the serene, impartial grace of his Master. Qu Qianxing did not deny that virtuous people and genuine saints existed within humanity, but once the population base grew large enough, the emergence of such scum was an absolute statistical certainty!

If not for Master being eternally infallible, Qu Qianxing would never have believed Lan Pei's nonsense regarding ascension.

What [giving back to the myriad mortals to sever karmic tethers](# "Literal CN: 回馈万民，了却因果 — returning merit to common mortals to dissolve worldly karmic cause and effect")?

Clearly, it was this wretched world that was indebted to Master, yet Master was expected to humble himself to such an extent? Did trash like this even qualify as "the myriad mortals"? Using them as stepping stones would only soil Master's boots!

—"Second Senior Brother—"-Mo Xi interrupted Qu Qianxing's brooding, deliberately adopting a spoiled, whiny tone.-"What on earth is with that heavenly thunder outside?"

As he spoke, Mo Xi fixed his wide, luminous cat-like eyes on Qu Qianxing, subtly hinting at something.

Seeing this, Qu Qianxing let out a faint sigh.

In truth, he did not need Mo Xi's reminder; he understood priorities.

Now was not the time to purge this flock of opportunists, but rather to address the ominous thundercloud gathered outside.

As for the origin of the thundercloud, Qu Qianxing was not entirely without clues:

Under the protection of the mountain-guarding grand array, ordinary threats could never breach Mount Tianling. However, before departing, Zhou Hui had cautioned Qu Qianxing: he had captured several "god fragments" and implemented several "experimental defensive countermeasures." While these measures ensured no further fragments could slip into Heavenly Spirit Sect concealed inside human vessels, conducting research on a "god" was an act in direct defiance of the Heavenly Dao. The moment saboteurs meddled from the shadows, heavenly thunder would inevitably be triggered.

Of course, having explained matters to such an extent, given the Untiring Immortal Venerable's meticulous prudence, he would hardly leave the sect defenseless.

He had granted Qu Qianxing "some" permissions over the sect's grand array.

Never mind a few measly patches of thunderclouds—even if extraterrestrial demons invaded this very moment, Qu Qianxing possessed the means to annihilate them.

—"Very well. Wait here for a moment; I shall return shortly,"-Qu Qianxing announced.

A flash of electrical luminescence flickered where he stood; when the crowd blinked again, Qu Qianxing was already perched high above the raging thundercloud.

Exclamations of shock rippled through the reception hall.

*Clap, clap!* Mo Xi clapped his hands briskly, recapturing everyone's wandering attention:

—"I imagine that before signing this contract, everyone still harbors doubts regarding our Heavenly Spirit Sect's strength, yes?"-he said with a radiant, disarming smile.-"Everyone hails from different regions; our furthest 'partners' reside tens of millions of li away from Heavenly Spirit Sect. You must also be wondering whether signing this contract is truly wise, or whether attaching yourselves to powers closer to your homelands might be safer, no?"

—"Never! We are not such ungrateful scoundrels!"-Qiao Linggen shouted at the top of his lungs.

It was entirely thanks to the Immortal Bazaar that Qiao Family Village had weathered its existential crisis. How could he possibly turn around after surviving and attach himself to the very cultivation clans that sought to exploit them?

Yet Qiao Linggen's impassioned protest was met with only a sparse scattering of echoing voices.

Startled, Qiao Linggen looked around, only to discover many people shifting their gazes evasively, their expressions conflicted and uncertain.

[Could they truly...?]

Qiao Linggen thought with rising dread.

Mo Xi, however, appeared to have anticipated this long ago. Indifferent to their superficial declarations of loyalty, he merely said with a beaming smile:

—"This is a wonderful opportunity. You may witness our Heavenly Spirit Sect's power with your own eyes, and weigh for yourselves whether betraying us is truly worth it—and whether you can bear the cost."

*BOOM——!*

Right on cue with his words, another deafening roar of thunder detonated across the heavens.

This crash was vastly louder than the first. Rather than merely ear-splitting, it sounded as though the celestial vault itself were being ripped asunder. It was only because Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-guarding grand array remained fully active that the "guests" inside Huaixu Hall were shielded from its destructive shockwaves.

Compelled by Mo Xi's bidding, they had no choice but to crane their necks upward.

Seeing some stubbornly refusing to look up, Mo Xi was not in the least offended. He retrieved several bamboo-dragonfly spiritual artifacts from his wide sleeve, tossing them gracefully into the air, and unfurled eight massive projection screens, live-streaming everything unfolding in the firmament above.

Through the screens, the crowd could now view that patch of thundercloud with terrifying clarity.

It was an exceedingly bizarre mass of vapors: dark crimson tinged with murky, necrotic orange. Viewed in its entirety from midair, it appeared eerily spherical, resembling a colossal severed head, or perhaps a rotting, moldering orange. Except an ordinary head or orange would never pulse with crackling, jagged silver lightning.

And Qu Qianxing stood proudly opposite this filthy miasma, holding a glowing thunder orb aloft in his palm.

[—"Is he... intending to use a thunder orb to counter a heavenly thundercloud?"](# "Speaker: Merchant Cultivator") someone in the hall could not resist murmuring in disbelief.

Hearing this, Qiao Linggen's heart seized with worry as well.

Even a "cultivator" of his meager realm knew that lightning, being an attribute that warded off evil, had virtually no natural counter. Thus, no matter how profound a cultivator's cultivation might be, when facing their heavenly tribulation, they relied primarily on defensive artifacts supplemented by grim physical endurance: survive the onslaught, and you advance; fail to endure, and your soul disperses into flying ash. No one had ever heard of confronting heavenly thunder head-on!

And yet today, before everyone's eyes, a living demonstration unfolded.

Qu Qianxing revolved his thunder orb, channeling celestial purple-gold lightning and hacking downward straight into the thundercloud.

Electrical arcs surged violently from within the thundercloud to intercept it. When the two bolts of lightning collided, they unleashed a catastrophic detonation that sounded capable of tearing the entire world in half.

* * *

—"What is going on?! Didn't you say that man wasn't at Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"-Palace Master Li Hao of Carefree Palace demanded in terror.

Indeed, one did not even need to use one's brain to know this was Carefree Palace's doing. After all, Heavenly Spirit Sect had no other blind, immortal-cockroach enemies besides them.

Yet as Palace Master of Carefree Palace, Li Hao could not see a clear "live broadcast," nor could he even obtain an arcane relay.

Because Heavenly Spirit Sect not only possessed a terrifying mountain-guarding grand array, but after Phase One of the "Spiritual Energy Reservoir" project had reached completion, the entire Mount Tianling region had been shrouded in perpetual mist. Catching so much as a distant rumble of thunder from afar was an utter impossibility.

Under these circumstances, Li Hao felt as though he were [playing blind chess](# "Literal CN: 下盲棋 (xià mángqí) — playing chess blindly without seeing the board; operating entirely in the dark").

Fortunately, he possessed another method of deduction. Thinking thus, Li Hao frowned and looked toward the divine temple ahead.

This divine temple had no name. Situated on the outermost perimeter of Carefree Palace, it served as the sect's mountain gate.

At the same time, this was where Carefree Palace selected incoming disciples and tested their spirit roots.

Consequently, all Carefree Palace disciples harbored a profound impression of this shrine. What struck the disciples as bizarre, however, was that this temple held neither a god nor a divine statue. Bold disciples had asked about this in the past, but Lady Hongyu, who oversaw the spirit root testing, only smiled without answering. Strangely, every disciple who had ever dared ask that question vanished without a trace shortly thereafter. This became Carefree Palace's famous "First Lesson":

Keep your mouth shut! Never ask what you shouldn't ask!

Yet if any Carefree Palace disciple stepped into this temple right now, they would be dumbfounded in horror:

The interior of this temple was plastered wall-to-wall with countless talismans, and intricate formations were inscribed across the entire floor. Meanwhile, Lady Hongyu—dreaded by all incoming disciples—lay convulsing in the dead center of those arrays. Although her body was likewise covered in layers of talismans, those paper charms were being blasted to cinders at terrifying speed by jagged lightning descending from who-knew-where, forcing the talisman masters encircling the array to frantically slap fresh talismans onto her body at an even more frantic pace.

Paying zero heed to Hongyu's agonizing state, Li Hao barked:

—"I am asking you! If he isn't there, who is operating the mountain-guarding grand array right now?!"

Hongyu's eyes bulged as she shuddered violently. She parted her lips, but could only produce choked, rattling noises from her throat:

[—"K-Kh... clack... clack..."](# "Speaker: Lady Hongyu")

Fortunately, the gorgeous youth who had accompanied Li Hao all along answered the question:

[—"It should be his second disciple, Qu Qianxing, no?"](# "Speaker: Gorgeous Youth")

—"Impossible!"-Li Hao exclaimed in disbelief.-"After the previous incident, I dispatched subordinates to investigate thoroughly—that Qu Qianxing is merely at the Golden Core stage!"

—"Yet he is the next Sect Leader of Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-the gorgeous youth sighed.-"And the Untiring Immortal Venerable truly trusts him, actually entrusting the mountain-guarding grand array to a mere Golden Core. In other words, Qianxing currently wields the power of the Tribulation Transcendence realm."

—"How could that be possible?! A Golden Core..."-Li Hao still could not accept it.

After all, power of that caliber was not something one could simply hand over.

Even for Carefree Palace, cultivating a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse required astronomical time and astronomical resources, because only when the mind and physical body reached sufficient fortitude could they withstand power of that tier without detonating.

—"Why could it not be? That is Qianxing, after all! He possesses the [Qi of the Wenqu Star](# "Wenqu Star (文曲星) — the celestial constellation of literature, scholarship, and civil examination genius in Chinese astrology") and the [destiny of a Sage](# "Literal CN: 圣人之资 — innate potential to become a Daoist or Confucian Sage"). Had that face of his not brought him so much trauma, saddling him with unnecessary heart demons, he could have ascended directly to immortality! Never mind a mere sect-guarding array—that is only one-third of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's power, is it not?"-the gorgeous youth remarked wistfully.

One-third of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's power was already monstrous!

In truth, the cultivation world had long speculated: if Zhou Hui could take on three Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses simultaneously, then was one-third of his power equivalent to a standard Tribulation Transcendence cultivator?

Yet for his second disciple to wield that power directly...

—"You seem exceedingly familiar with this Qu Qianxing?"-Li Hao suddenly noticed something amiss in his tone.

—"Mmh, I took a liking to him first. I was only a hair's breadth away from claiming him for myself, but alas, he managed to slip away in the end,"-the gorgeous youth lamented regretfully.

Hearing this, Li Hao shuddered violently.

Gazing at the youth's enchanting visage, Li Hao recalled the other's depraved fetishes—tastes so sickening they disgusted even him, the Palace Master of Carefree Palace—and instantly lost all desire to inquire further.

The youth had no intention of elaborating either, merely continuing:

—"A mountain-guarding grand array can only be transferred to a successor when the sect leader is absent. For Qianxing to unleash power to this extent proves that Untiring has truly departed Mount Tianling."

—"Splendid!"-Li Hao sneered coldly.

Indeed, his objective had never been Heavenly Spirit Sect to begin with.

Even without Qu Qianxing, Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-guarding grand array was not something Carefree Palace could breach. And even if they did assault Heavenly Spirit Sect while the Untiring Immortal Venerable was away, what would come of it? Wait around for the Untiring Immortal Venerable to return and slaughter them to the last man?!

Thus, this thundercloud was merely a probe using a chess piece that had already been exposed. Even if discovered, the Untiring Immortal Venerable would have no evidence to implicate Carefree Palace.

And Carefree Palace's true target lay far beyond Mount Tianling—

Li Hao turned to his retainers and commanded:

—"Notify those people: they may proceed with the next step without hesitation!"

* * *

—"As expected of Second Senior Brother,"-Mo Xi sighed in admiration at the same time.

Within mere moments, the celestial purple lightning belonging to Qu Qianxing had grown vastly thicker and more colossal, coiling above the nine heavens like a flashing dragon of lightning. The original thundercloud had vanished without a trace.

Such overwhelming might truly left one incapable of harboring even a shred of jealousy!

Mo Xi thought with a bitter smile.

In the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Qu Qianxing was the undisputed heir. Everyone knew that he would inherit everything on Mount Tianling—including Mount Tianling itself, the spiritual springs, the mineral veins, the newly constructed spiritual energy reservoir, and the Immortal Bazaar.

Yet not a single disciple took issue with this, not even Mo Xi.

Not merely because being the prospective sect master meant shouldering an astronomical mountain of mundane administrative chores—[Would anyone truly relinquish such vast wealth over that?] Mo Xi thought; [ah, the brain-damaged Eldest Senior Brother was an exception]—but because of Qu Qianxing's terrifying strength.

Qu Qianxing seemed to have possessed an extraordinary past, resulting in a divine sense that surpassed all others, comparable even to a genuine Tribulation Transcendence cultivator. Thus, with only Golden Core cultivation, Qu Qianxing was fully capable of controlling the Tribulation Transcendence-tier mountain-guarding grand array forged by Master.

Therefore, when Zhou Hui had serenely prepared to ascend in previous lives (seven lifetimes ago), it was largely because he had such a disciple; even after his ascension, he never had to worry about Heavenly Spirit Sect lacking a guardian.

Though as for Second Senior Brother serving as a "guardian"...

Haha, only a Master who unconditionally doted on his disciples would ever consider him qualified, right?!

Mo Xi curled his lips in a smirk.

If Mo Xi had truly trusted Qu Qianxing, he wouldn't have gone so crazily obsessed with amassing spirit stones in his past life.

Ah, of course, that was all in the past. As long as Master remained, Xi'er would safeguard the Heavenly Spirit Sect with all his heart!

Thinking thus, Mo Xi turned his round eyes toward the "partners" inside Huaixu Hall.

Even Mo Xi was awestruck by such power, to say nothing of these mortals and cultivators barely distinguishable from mortals. Their faces were ashen, frozen in dead silence, looking as though they had finally realized what kind of monster's lair they had wandered into.

Mo Xi felt deep sympathy for them.

If Master were here, he would surely have the patience to win people over through virtue, convincing them heart and soul, right?

Pity, only he and Second Senior Brother were here...

Neither Qu Qianxing nor Mo Xi believed in rubbish like "repaying kindness." Even if these people had reaped immense profits from the Immortal Bazaar and escaped great hardships, they wouldn't necessarily stand obediently on Heavenly Spirit Sect's side. They wouldn't even recognize it as "benevolence"—some fools would inevitably convince themselves that earning spirit stones was entirely their own "innate talent"!

—"Everyone, why don't you examine this contract once more?"

Waiting for the thunder to subside, Mo Xi spoke with a beaming smile:

—"Perhaps you held some misunderstandings regarding Heavenly Spirit Sect previously. Now, take a good look, and deliberate carefully. I hope to receive a satisfactory answer."

Saying so, he waved his sleeve, sending those contracts floating before everyone once again.

Several people reflexively shrank back in fear, but froze in place.

Mo Xi didn't press them further. He felt these people should be given space to confer and deliberate—though it was ultimately useless and their fate was already sealed, it would at least make them slightly more willing:

—"I shall go summon Second Senior Brother back."

Saying so, he soared into the sky, leaving a crowd staring at one another in stunned dismay.

* * *

High in the sky, Mo Xi said to Qu Qianxing in a sweet, syrupy tone:

—"Second Senior Brother, you are simply incredible!"

—"...It is not over yet,"-Qu Qianxing replied, clutching his thunder orb as though crumpling a contract.

Mo Xi blinked, startled:

—"Not over yet?"

He looked at their surroundings.

At this moment, the entire sky had returned to its pristine state, azure like freshly polished lapis lazuli tiles, without the faintest blemish from the departed thundercloud.

Seeing Mo Xi looking around, Qu Qianxing shook his head:

—"Not Heavenly Spirit Sect. With the mountain-guarding grand array standing guard, no fiends or monsters can breach this place."

Qu Qianxing frowned, looking toward Huaixu Hall:

—"I suspect their true target might be far away—"

—"You mean... the mortal settlements that participated in the Immortal Bazaar?"-Mo Xi's cat-like eyes widened, his adorable smile instantly vanishing.-"I don't doubt for a moment that Carefree Palace and those cultivation sects are despicable enough to do such a thing. But Master must have anticipated this possibility. If a village comes under attack, the coral trees have defensive capabilities, and can even allow us to counterattack when necessary..."

—"That was under the premise that Master was here,"-Qu Qianxing frowned.-"What if they know Master is away, and launch coordinated attacks on all settlements defying them simultaneously? I don't believe they were foolish enough to stage a broad-daylight, unprovoked assault on Heavenly Spirit Sect at such trivial intensity; this was merely a probe. Most likely, they only wanted to confirm what Master was doing so they could launch their true strike. But no matter how formidable my divine sense may be, I cannot multitask continent-wide counterattacks across all fronts like Master."

—"..."-Mo Xi froze. That was indeed a critical problem.

—"Moreover, I fear an even worse scenario: what if, while I am channeling counterattacks through the coral trees to defend those mortals, they gather thunderclouds once again to strike Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-Qu Qianxing muttered in deep contemplation.

Mo Xi's brow furrowed as well.

This was indeed very likely to happen. Yet what struck Mo Xi as bizarre was: why did this scene feel so unnervingly familiar?

Mo Xi felt a sudden wave of dizziness. In his mind's eye, he faintly saw a similar scene under bright sunlight, where Qu Qianxing stood coldly in midair, declaring to him:

*[Only Master is important. And only Mount Tianling where Master resides is important. Consolidate all power of the mountain-guarding grand array here, abandon—]*

Mo Xi blurted out instinctively:

—"Second Senior Brother, you aren't thinking of abandoning them, are you?!"

* * *

Holding the reports submitted by his disciples, Zhou Hui slightly knitted his brows.

Shen Qingqian happened to witness this scene as she strolled over.

—"Um, Immortal Venerable, is there a problem?"-Shen Qingqian asked with concern.

It had now been five days since Zhou Hui had arrived at Blood Demon Hall. He had not yet begun shifting the mountain, but had instead instructed his disciples to wrap the peak layer upon layer in protective formations, making it look like a colossal gift box.

Witnessing this, even with Shen Qingqian's thick nerves, she couldn't help but feel anxious.

What on earth was wrong with that mountain to warrant such solemn caution from the Untiring Immortal Venerable?

—"Nothing..."-Zhou Hui set down the report. Seeing Shen Qingqian's worried expression, he added in explanation:-"What I was reading was not the formation blueprints for the mountain, but the data collection report from the seabed last time."

[They actually did that homework assignment?!]

—"Is there something wrong with the seabed?"-Shen Qingqian asked.

That previous seabed excursion had broadened Shen Qingqian's horizons considerably. If she hadn't gone down to see it herself, she never would have known that so much demonic qi had stagnated at the ocean floor. Even after swimming back and discovering that her cultivation—which had barely budged since entering the Body Integration stage—had ticked up a notch, she hadn't felt particularly thrilled.

—Of course, it was likely also because she was utterly exhausted.

After all, by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's side, as long as one wasn't afraid of hard labor, such methods of increasing spiritual energy seemed commonplace. Because everything was so grounded in rigorous practice, one ended up feeling that breakthroughs in cultivation were only natural.

Shen Qingqian's focus remained entirely on that demonic qi:

—"Could some demon beast be reviving?"

[No wonder the Immortal Venerable had his disciples write reports!] she thought in admiration.

Zhou Hui saw zero need to justify assigning reports:

—"Why would you think that? Stagnation of demonic qi on the ocean floor is completely normal. Especially with the beast tide approaching in a few years, demonic qi is bound to surge during this phase. In truth, conditions are better than I anticipated."

[—"...Then what are you troubled by?"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

—"What concerns me are those teleportation nodes of mine,"-Zhou Hui frowned.-"To prevent those nodes from being compromised by external factors, I integrated spiritual energy sensing mechanisms into them. As it turns out, the spiritual energy readings near several nodes have suddenly spiked."

Saying so, he unrolled an ocean chart that resembled a star map.

Sure enough, several teleportation nodes were glowing brightly.

—"Could it be due to a spiritual energy storm?"-Shen Qingqian asked with persistent curiosity.

Zhou Hui cast a worried glance at the Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall:

—"Spiritual energy storms typically engulf an entire region; they don't light up in isolated, scattered specks like this. Look closely at the positions of these teleportation nodes."

[Is this sect leader's IQ truly all right?]

Shen Qingqian strained her eyes to look, but couldn't discern a thing.

Yet having sensed that trace of pity in Zhou Hui's tone, she reflexively turned to her eldest disciple:

—"Disciple, what do you see?"

—"..."-Bai Wubin looked helplessly at his master.-"These teleportation nodes all appear to be situated near immortal cities and sects. Could they be influenced by crowds returning home? As cultivators gathered in higher numbers, the spiritual energy readings increased accordingly."

—"Then our sect should have lit up as well!"-Shen Qingqian pointed out. But with her disciple's "hint," she finally caught the thread.-"Wait a moment... clustered around towns and cities... Immortal Venerable, oh no! Could they be planning to strike at those mortals from the Immortal Bazaar?!"

[She finally guessed right...]

Utterly exhausted in mind and spirit, Bai Wubin breathed a sigh of relief, only to notice Zhou Hui looking at him with a faint, knowing smile.

Bai Wubin hurriedly dropped his head.

—"Does Fellow Daoist Shen think so as well?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Given my understanding of cultivation clans, they would never allow residents within their sphere of control the leeway to align with anyone else,"-Shen Qingqian shook her head. She was indeed somewhat slow on the uptake, but as the leader of a sect, she still possessed basic political savvy.

In truth, while wandering the bazaar, Shen Qingqian had already worried: if low-cultivation practitioners were rogue cultivators, that was fine; but if they were ordinary civilians, wouldn't they be skinned alive by local cultivation clans upon returning home?

—Even though cultivators treated mortals as insignificant ants, mortals earning spirit stones from a rival sect was an entirely different matter.

Even without Carefree Palace stoking the flames, those cultivation clans and sects would never tolerate such a situation developing within their territory.

Shen Qingqian looked at Zhou Hui with concern:

—"Immortal Venerable, do you intend to return and take a look?"

—"With Qianxing there, he will handle it properly,"-Zhou Hui replied, shaking his head.

—"You truly trust your disciple,"-Shen Qingqian remarked in surprise.

Even she wouldn't dare entrust an affair of this scale entirely to Bai Wubin.

—"Of course I trust Qianxing,"-Zhou Hui said.

[I trust his stubbornness.]

Zhou Hui showed a wry smile. Several thoughts weighed heavily on his heart that he left unsaid:

If Qianxing's obsession were so easily swayed, it wouldn't have led to total team wipes in previous lifetimes.

Yet by that same token, driven by that very obsession, he would resolve this crisis no matter what it took.

* * *

—"Why would I abandon them?!"

Hearing Mo Xi's question, Qu Qianxing's eyes widened in genuine astonishment:

—"Setting aside how much effort Master expended planting so many teleportation nodes! These people are tied directly to Master's grand Dao of Ascension—how could I possibly abandon them?!"

[—"...Is that where your priority lies?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[As expected of Second Senior Brother, the one most obsessed with Master's ascension—his choice is utterly unequivocal!]

The corner of Mo Xi's mouth twitched, accompanied by a trace of confusion:

[Right, Second Senior Brother would obviously choose this!]

[Why on earth did I just hallucinate that Second Senior Brother would abandon everyone, myself included—even if that, too, was for Master's sake?]

Qu Qianxing paid no attention to Mo Xi's bewildered expression, continuing:

—"I worry my power is insufficient to control multiple nodes simultaneously. Thus, our best course of action is to stall for time, allowing that group to carry the contracts back. The covenants will amplify my power, at which point launching counterattacks will be far more effective."

—"But it takes time for them to travel back,"-Mo Xi said blankly.-"Even for the Kunpeng, completing a circuit takes a full day. Ah, I'll go find Eldest Senior Brother and see if he can make the Kunpeng fly faster!"

Saying so, Mo Xi turned to leave, but Qu Qianxing swiftly caught him by the arm:

—"Calling that idiot back is useless. No matter how fast the Kunpeng flies, it will still be too late. I have a better idea."

Saying up to here, Qu Qianxing transmitted a message via divine sense directly into Mo Xi's mind.

Mo Xi's eyes went wide:

—"There is... actually a method like that?!"

—"Enough, hurry along,"-Qu Qianxing urged, giving Mo Xi's back a push.

Mo Xi swiftly soared off toward Buyan Palace. Watching his junior brother's figure vanish into the horizon, Qu Qianxing slowly drifted back down.

* * *

Southeastern Central Domain, entrance of Qiao Family Village.

The villagers gathered beneath the coral tree, gazing anxiously at the village entrance.

There, a group of cultivators clutching various magical artifacts stood watch. Although held at bay by the radiance emitted from the crown of the coral tree, they made no desperate attempt to force their way in, merely glaring hungrily into the village as though awaiting an order.

Finally, the final verdict arrived:

—"Patriarch, word has arrived from Carefree Palace!"

—"'That Immortal Venerable' has departed his immortal cave. Right now is when the protective barrier is at its weakest; we may launch our assault!"-a cultivator dressed in steward's attire reported.

The Patriarch of the Zheng Clan burst into booming laughter, smashing the teacup in his hand to the ground, and roared:

—"Attack!"

Those ravenous cultivators finally unleashed their magical artifacts, crashing against the protective barrier.

The barrier instantly rippled with radiant halos, deflecting the strikes effortlessly. Simultaneously, diverse lines of glowing script surfaced, drifting before the cultivators' eyes.

[—"What is this?"](# "Speaker: Zheng Clan Cultivator")

A body cultivator attempted to smash his way through regardless, but the talisman master beside him shouted:

—"Hold!"

His warning came a fraction of a second too late. The body cultivator was flung backward by his own reflected force, crashing violently into the dirt.

Only then did the talisman master sigh:

—"This protective barrier is in Question-and-Answer Mode. Unless your cultivation exceeds the creator's by a full major realm, you cannot brute-force your way through. The only way in is by answering the question correctly."

—"That's completely foul! How could we possibly solve a question posed by a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator?!"-the body cultivator howled in despair.

—"Not necessarily,"-the talisman master said with an odd smirk.-"To avoid hindering the protected targets from entering and leaving, questions of this sort are generally calibrated to the cultivation realm of the protected occupants—even if they don't know the answer yet, it will be something they are capable of learning."

—"Hahaha, doesn't that make it even easier?!"-another cultivator laughed, eyeing the mortals inside the village.
