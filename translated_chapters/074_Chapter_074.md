# Chapter 74: Completely Under Control (第74章 尽在掌握)

"White Jade Capital?!"

The seated cultivators were struck with utter shock; even those few who were merely here to [eat melons](# "Literal CN: 吃瓜 (chīguā) — snacking on melon seeds / acting as idle spectators") couldn't resist stretching their necks to peer forward.

One female cultivator, known as "Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum," happened to be seated right beside Lan Pei. She turned to him and asked:

—"Is that map real?"

—"I've never heard Zhou Hui mention it,"-Lan Pei said, gazing with equal astonishment at the map laid before Zhou Hui.

—"Yet given Zhou Hui's character, he would never lie,"-interjected another cultivator nearby.-"If he says it's a relic of White Jade Capital, it must be related to White Jade Capital—unless Zhou Hui made a mistake himself."

After all, there was an absurd surplus of bogus information regarding White Jade Capital.

As a lost immortal capital of high antiquity, every few days saw fresh rumors claiming discoveries of "White Jade Capital Ruins" or "Relics of White Jade Capital." There were even scammers declaring, *'I am a True Immortal who was sealed inside White Jade Capital for ten thousand years and only recently escaped. As long as you transfer XXXX spirit stones to me, I will lead you to unearth the ruins of White Jade Capital!'*—it had virtually become a permanent fixture in the cultivation world's repertoire of fraud.

If it were anyone else, even another Mahayana cultivator present in this room, everyone would be deeply skeptical.

Yet this was the Untiring Immortal Venerable!

The Untiring Immortal Venerable, who earned every bit of his progress by honestly comprehending heaven and earth on his own, eschewing all opportunism and shortcuts, whose very cultivation art focused on giving back to nature!

The cultivation world's foremost [model straight-A student](# "Literal CN: 模范优等生 (mófàn yōuděngshēng) — exemplary top student / honor roll model")!

Now that this model straight-A student declared he held [lost ancient real exam papers](# "Literal CN: 上古真题 (shànggǔ zhēntí) — authentic ancient exam questions / past papers") (X) in his hands, naturally not a single person doubted him.

—Though nobody suspected that Zhou Hui had actually obtained White Jade Capital itself.

First, everyone tacitly assumed that White Jade Capital had long been reduced to mere 'ruins'; how could it possibly remain intact? Second, if you tell people a [straight-A top student](# "Literal CN: 学霸 (xuébà) — study god / academic overachiever") has past real exam papers, everyone will readily believe it; but if you claim this top student directly obtained the actual Gaokao exam questions beforehand, heheh—then he isn't a top student, he's a cheat.

—"How could Senior Zhou Hui ever misread a clue?"-another cultivator chimed in.-"I'd wager the likelihood of Senior Zhou Hui deceiving people is greater than the likelihood of him misinterpreting a clue!"

[—"Indeed, that is the Untiring Immortal Venerable after all!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

[—"Speaking of which, I heard the academy Zhou Hui established for mortals is named 'White Jade Capital Academy.' Could there be some profound hidden meaning behind that?"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

[—"There's such a thing?!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

[—"Master Lan Pei, have you heard anything?"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

—"No, I haven't heard a whisper,"-Lan Pei denied blankly.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum shot Lan Pei a look of utter disdain:

—"Haven't you been staying at the Heavenly Spirit Sect all this time? How do you know nothing at all?"

—"I want to know too!"-Lan Pei protested, feeling deeply aggrieved.

He felt as though he had been staying at a counterfeit Heavenly Spirit Sect. How was it that whatever Zhou Hui did, he remained completely in the dark?

Still, even if Lan Pei did know, he wouldn't tell.

Lan Pei merely looked curiously at Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum:

—"What? Are you planning to participate as well?"

—"My ancestors were, after all, mausoleum keepers for the Sacred Lord of White Jade Capital,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said coldly.-"Since it concerns White Jade Capital, I must make a move regardless."

—"Ah, right, you still have that backstory, don't you!"-Lan Pei chuckled.

The origin of the title "Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum" stemmed from her own claim of being connected to the "Sacred Mausoleum"—the purported burial site of the Sacred Lord of White Jade Capital. However, what she claimed to be a relic of the Sacred Mausoleum was merely a tiny stone stele. The script carved upon it didn't look like ancient script, nor did it resemble the script of True Immortals; the heavens only knew where it came from.

Of course, more importantly, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum was completely 'ordinary.'

She was neither extraordinarily beautiful nor notably ugly. She lacked the ethereal immortal grace people imagined a descendant of White Jade Capital would possess, nor did she have the eerie and mysterious aura unique to mausoleum keepers. In outward appearance, she was merely an ordinary woman in her late twenties, hardly even looking like an immortal, with very little presence to speak of. Consequently, nobody believed her claim.

However, this fairy genuinely possessed an extraordinary knack for deciphering bronze and stone inscriptions. Thus, people took to calling her "Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum," using the title with a touch of teasing irony.

Now that Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had attained the Mahayana stage, people were somewhat more inclined to credit her words—or rather, they believed the pedigree of her cultivation realm. Yet old seniors like Lan Pei still took it with a grain of salt.

Sensing Lan Pei's skepticism, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum let out a cold snort:

—"I only fear Zhou Hui has miscalculated. When that map even lures someone like me—possessing neither sect nor factional backing—into entering his tournament, if I lose, so be it; but if I win, it won't bode well for his plans to keep those sects and immortal cities under his thumb!"

—"How do you know Zhou Hui doesn't specifically want a rogue cultivator like you to win?"-Lan Pei smiled.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum curled her lip in disbelief.

That so-called "Central Domain Cultivator Representative" title was obviously just a vanity prize for cultivators who craved fame and prestige to fight over. Not to say that Mahayana cultivators were entirely above such things; yearning for reputation was human nature, an instinct not even the Mahayana stage could fully transcend.

It was just that when Mahayana cultivators clashed, it was like two humanoid nuclear warheads trading blows. Not only did it harm both sides without benefit, but it could easily devastate an entire region of the world. Moreover, Mahayana cultivators were few in number, and everyone knew a bit about each other's backgrounds. Fighting with no real grudge was terribly awkward and embarrassing. That was why everyone usually crouched in the background and sent their juniors forward—after all, "my disciple is more promising than yours" was also a classic way to claim victory!

Yet from any angle, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum felt nobody would want an unremarkable cultivator like herself, lacking both renown and distinctive flair, to become the "Central Domain Cultivator Representative."

Originally, she had no intention of asking for a snub.

Yet once the Untiring Immortal Venerable produced a lead related to White Jade Capital, the equation changed completely!

It wasn't merely because of some grand excuse like being a "descendant of White Jade Capital." Rather, for so many years, she had been relentlessly questioned about her identity as the descendant of a sacred mausoleum keeper. Over time, even she couldn't help doubting whether her father and grandfather had simply spun tales to comfort her—wondering if that stone tablet had nothing to do with the Sacred Mausoleum at all and was just an ordinary ancient stele. Her lifelong dedication to studying ancient scripts had yielded zero corroborating clues, plunging her into deep self-doubt.

This matter had practically become an inner demon, impending her advancement to the Tribulation Transcendence stage.

Now, hearing of an "artifact of White Jade Capital"—what if there were clues within connected to the script on her family's tablet?

Even if there weren't, any White Jade Capital relic produced by someone of Zhou Hui's caliber ought to be related to resisting heavenly tribulations and ascending!

Regardless of what game the Untiring Immortal Venerable was playing, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum was determined to win!

*

Naturally, she was not the only one.

Zhou Hui was thoroughly pleased that the "prize" he offered had achieved its intended effect.

He immediately led the crowd flying toward the void behind Tianqian Mountain.

—Naturally, he could not allow these people to start brawling within the boundaries of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Although Zhou Hui had full confidence that his sect-protecting grand array could contain them, having so many Mahayana cultivators beating the stuffing out of each other inside would cause pointless wear and tear on the array.

—"How shall we fight?"-someone asked with keen interest.

—"Draw lots, perhaps?"-Zhou Hui remarked casually.

Now that his goal of [fanning the flames](# "Literal CN: 拱火 (gǒnghuǒ) — stoking the fire / egging people on") was accomplished and the "reward" tossed out, he had zero interest in the specific fighting process.

It was all a meaningless waste of energy anyway.

The other Mahayana cultivators, however, did not share his apathy—especially the battle-maniacs among them.

At the Mahayana realm, opportunities to go all out were exceedingly rare. Even when they did fight, it was against demonic horrors and foreign entities. It was rare to find an excuse to clash with peers without incurring deadly blood feuds.

They were thoroughly dissatisfied with Zhou Hui's perfunctory attitude:

[—"There must at least be rules! Lest someone refuses to accept their defeat."](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

—"...Fair enough."

Zhou Hui pondered for a moment. He reached into his storage pouch at leisure and lifted out a small wisp of flame.

This wisp of flame was clearly no ordinary flame. It continued to burn brightly even in the void, where there was neither spiritual energy nor any other matter, radiating pale light. If one listened closely, faint weeping sounds seemed to emanate from within, as if crying and begging for mercy.

Zhou Hui paid no heed to those voices—or rather, he expertly pinched them silent.

Then, he elongated and expanded the flame, drawing its light into geometric lines, spinning a colossal three-dimensional polyhedron across the empty void.

—"Let this flame serve as the boundary,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.-"Anyone who voluntarily admits defeat or is knocked outside this flaming boundary during the match shall be considered the loser."

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, what if your flame is extinguished first?"-a cultivator asked with mischievous intent.

—"Then I shall directly acknowledge that person as the victor and present the map to the White Jade Capital relic,"-Zhou Hui replied.

His words prompted everyone to take several hard looks at the flame.

Though anything produced by the Untiring Immortal Venerable was bound to be extraordinary, this flame clearly possessed no small origin!

Only Lan Pei recognized the flame's true origin, leaving him momentarily speechless:

[Little Zhou Hui is really something, squeezing every last drop of utility out of everything...]

—"What's wrong?"-Lan Pei asked, turning to Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum as he noticed her strange expression while gazing in Zhou Hui's direction.

—"Who is that shado... that cultivator beside Immortal Venerable Zhou Hui?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum asked.

—"Beside Zhou Hui?"-Lan Pei glanced over as well.

Because Zhou Hui had intimidated this crowd not long ago, most dared not approach too close. Yet a few didn't care. For instance, right now a small, diminutive figure stood right beside Zhou Hui.

—"You mean Mengshan Old Freak?"-Lan Pei said.

Studying the figure for a few moments, Lan Pei also noticed something amusing:

—"He does look a bit odd. With that expression on his face, one would almost think he's gearing up to stage a [medical malpractice riot](# "Literal CN: 医闹 (yīnào) — staging a violent medical dispute / unruly patient hospital brawl")."

—"That's not what I was referring to... Perhaps my eyes were playing tricks on me?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said.

She averted her gaze and rubbed her eyes. Within those seemingly ordinary eyes, two eerie wisps of ghostly flame flickered faintly.

*

This was Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's trump card.

While Zhou Hui was setting up the flaming arena, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum took inventory of her storage pouch.

As a cultivator specializing in deciphering ancient scripts, her primary discipline was naturally the Dao of Talismans. Moreover, as a rogue cultivator without sect or backing, she always carried her most prized set of talismans on her person, and an annual assembly was no exception.

For a tournament that had erupted out of thin air, this was undeniably an enormous advantage.

She selected a talisman from her stash and ignited it in secret. Instantly, within her ordinary brown eyes, two wisps of eerie blue ghostly flame sprang to life, flickering faintly.

When she looked at the surrounding cultivators through these eyes, she could see them enveloped in multi-colored radiance and mist—their internal spiritual energy and divine senses, and even the outlines of their spirit roots.

Possessing this technique allowed her not only to see through an opponent's techniques, dodge lethal strikes, and deploy matching talismans to exploit their weaknesses, but even to gather complete intelligence on the "enemy" before the bout began.

However, upon reaching the upper three realms, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum rarely used this talisman.

Against those weaker than her, it was unnecessary; against those stronger, she couldn't beat them anyway. Most importantly, after reaching the Body Integration realm, a cultivator's divine soul and physical body fused into one, making each of them shine like a blazing lighthouse—it made her eyes ache just looking at them.

Take the Untiring Immortal Venerable, for example!

In her eyes right now, he resembled an enormous, luminescent world-tree, an entire universe unto himself, appearing even larger than Tianqian Mountain itself.

Staring too long at such an existence felt as though not just her eyes, but even her brain marrow would be roasted dry. Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum struggled to avert her gaze—which was difficult, much like trying to ignore sunlight even with one's back turned. What was more, as though blinded by the intense glare, she even saw double?

No, why would there be a shadow?

This was not physical light; it was spiritual energy and divine sense!

Furthermore, in the void where neither physical light nor a Golden Crow sun existed, how could there be a shadow?

Yet behind that cluster of green light beside the Untiring Immortal Venerable stood a dark shadow. What was more, that shadow was standing upright, with something writhing within the darkness. Looking closer, they appeared to be... characters of script?

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum felt this was utterly absurd, especially after hearing Lan Pei identify him as Mengshan Old Freak.

Mengshan Old Freak was a spirit plant master; his spiritual energy had nothing to do with dark shadows.

Was her vision glitching because Zhou Hui's light was too blinding?

—"The tournament is what matters right now,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said, covering her eyes.

Seeing this, Lan Pei smiled with amusement:

—"You seem quite confident of victory!"

—"I wasn't originally, but after seeing this... arena, I feel I have a good chance,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum replied.

Turning her ghostly flame-imbued eyes toward the flames delineating the arena, she discovered that they were not flames at all, but twisted, writhing script dripping with intense malice.

Yet Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum felt no fear; if anything, she felt her chances of winning were even higher.

Still... why did this script look somewhat similar to the faint characters writhing in the dark shadow clinging to Mengshan Old Freak?

...She must have seen wrong.

Unaware of her internal conflict, Lan Pei asked:

—"Who is your first opponent?"

—"The first is Fairy Xia,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said.

She took the opportunity to verify Mengshan Old Freak's bracket.

Because two Mahayana cultivators needed to be selected, the lots were divided into Red and Blue brackets. Mengshan Old Freak had drawn into the same bracket as Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum, but their placements were far apart; they would only meet if both reached the finals.

[Mengshan Old Freak is a spirit plant cultivator; he'll surely be eliminated early!]

She thought to herself.

For some reason, she let out a sigh of relief.

*

*

*This was a divider for the long and tedious tournament.*

*

*

—"So that's why you blew a hole right through space?"-Tao Zhu asked, thoroughly speechless.

He had only arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect on the third day of the new year.

By that time, not only had the tournament long concluded, but most of the Mahayana cultivators had already departed with their retinues. This left Tao Zhu drowning in regret—not because he missed competing, but because he missed the chance to set up a stall! In his view, during such a sudden, impromptu tournament, someone was bound to be caught unprepared, making it a golden business opportunity to peddle goods.

—"You should have made it a long-term tournament,"-Tao Zhu grumbled.-"Let it run for at least ten days to a half-month, then sell admission tickets and invite cultivators from across the Five Domains and Six Abysses to come watch."

—"To watch you get beaten to a pulp by our Central Domain's top cultivators?"-Lan Pei retorted dryly.

Even if everyone enjoyed the brawl, they certainly didn't want juniors watching!

Besides, could ordinary mortals or low-tier cultivators even spectate?

They had torn open an actual spatial rift here. Any cultivator lacking sufficient cultivation who happened to be nearby—let alone getting sucked in—merely glancing at it from the perimeter would suffer spiritual energy disruption and collapse.

Lan Pei added:

—"Little Zhou Hui asked you here to inspect this spatial hole, not to give tournament coaching."

—"Right, how on earth did you manage this?"-Tao Zhu asked in disbelief.

The group hovered in the void outside Tianqian Mountain, staring at the gaping hole in the sky.

It was a physical "hole."

Inside the hole, points of starlight twinkled, resembling an entirely different universe.

While Mahayana cultivators were all conversant with spatial arts to some degree, being able to punch open a persistent spatial rift in the void that refused to close remained an exceedingly rare feat—though not unprecedented, since the largest rift left behind by the God-Fiend War still cleaved through the very center of the Central Domain!

—"If you really want to know, the final showdown was simply too spectacular,"-Lan Pei explained.-"Over in the Red bracket, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum and Mengshan Old Freak completely demolished Zhou Hui's arena—"

—"Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum and Mengshan Old Freak?!"-Tao Zhu cried out.

He stared in sheer disbelief at the woman and the child who had been standing beside the spatial rift the entire time. He could not fathom how either of these two could possibly be final-match material.

Sensing his disdain, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum and Mengshan Old Freak glared back in unison:

[—"What? Is our reaching the finals that strange?!"](# "Speaker: Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum & Mengshan Old Freak")

—"...Wait, you said the Red bracket. Were there other brackets?"-Tao Zhu turned to ask Lan Pei.

Lan Pei chuckled and said:

—"There was also the Blue bracket. The victor was Patriarch Qingyang of Eternal Villa."

—"Huh? Why him?"-Tao Zhu asked.-"That fellow's combat strength is mediocre at best!"

—"But [he has money](# "Literal CN: 但他有钱啊 (dàn tā yǒu qián a) — but he's filthy rich! — running gag on pay-to-win cultivators")!"-Lan Pei deadpanned.

—"Among our circle, his cultivation isn't anywhere near the highest either,"-Tao Zhu pointed out.

—"But [he has money](# "Literal CN: 但他有钱啊 (dàn tā yǒu qián a)")!"-Lan Pei repeated.

—"His sect's influence isn't the greatest either,"-Tao Zhu persisted.

—"Yet [he has money](# "Literal CN: 但他有钱啊 (dàn tā yǒu qián a)")!"-Lan Pei shrugged.

—"..."-Tao Zhu turned back to Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum and Mengshan Old Freak.-"So which of you won? Did you rely on spirit stones as well?"

—"I won,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum declared, pulling out a fistful of talismans and glaring at Tao Zhu.-"Do you have a problem with that?"

—"No problem at all,"-Tao Zhu said, shaking his head at lightning speed.

Yet he secretly used divine sense to 'private chat' with Lan Pei:

—"Didn't anyone else protest?"-Tao Zhu transmitted secretly.-"In the end, these two were chosen to represent the Mahayana cultivators of our Central Domain?"

He couldn't shake the feeling that they were all going to lose face terribly.

—"Naturally, no one was satisfied. That's why Zhou Hui is already booked through to the Fifth Mahayana Exhibition Tournament; everyone is scrambling for the next slot,"-Lan Pei replied helplessly.-"Judging by this momentum, I suspect every last one of us will eventually get a turn."

[Getting a turn is wonderful!] Tao Zhu imagined his own Immortal Chess piece, feeling thoroughly satisfied.

Except, when his piece debuted, the opposing Mahayana cultivator would have to be like him—someone with "inner beauty." Definitely not a pretty boy who only coasted on looks, nor a glamorous beauty. For instance, Lan Pei's scruffy, bald-tailed fox form would be quite fitting—naturally not the transformed human version, which was sheer false advertising!

Noticing Tao Zhu's gaze, Lan Pei asked suspiciously:

—"What are you looking at?"

—"I was wondering how they managed to tear open this spatial rift,"-Tao Zhu quickly redirected his gaze toward the fairy and the old freak, not having completely forgotten his primary business.-"In theory, neither of these two specializes in combat, right?"

One was a [linguist](# "Literal CN: 语言学家 (yǔyán xuéjiā) — scholar of ancient texts / epigrapher") (X) talisman master; the other was an [arborist](# "Literal CN: 种树的 (zhòngshù de) — tree planter") (X) spirit plant cultivator. Neither looked like frontline heavy artillery.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum and Mengshan Old Freak let out another cold huff in stereo.

Lan Pei said:

—"Don't sell them short, they're genuinely formidable."

In truth, the others had simply been caught off guard, overwhelmed by a sky-blotting barrage of talismans and mutant plants. Furthermore, in the barren void devoid of spiritual energy, it became the ideal battlefield for this kind of [summoning build](# "Literal CN: 召唤体系 (zhàohuàn tǐxì) — summoning archetype / minion-spam strategy") (X).

Lan Pei continued:

—"However, the one truly responsible for this hole is Zhou Hui. Do you know what that madman used to construct the arena? He used the divine fragment he captured last time."

[—"A divine fragment?!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum & Mengshan Old Freak")

This time, all three of them shrieked in unison.

After screeching, Tao Zhu looked in shock at the fairy and the old freak:

—"You didn't know?"

—"No! I didn't know it was a divine fragment! If I had known, how would I have dared to decipher it so recklessly?!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said, her face draining of all color. Anyone finding out they had unknowingly dismantled a god with their bare hands would react the same way.

Shaken to the core, she completely forgot to take offense at Boss Tao's attitude and explained the details of the battle.

As it turned out, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had aimed for the arena from the very beginning.

After all, the talismans in her hands were finite. As a talisman cultivator who [wasn't particularly suited for combat](# "Literal CN: 不怎么擅长战斗 (bù zěnme shàncháng zhàndòu) — comedic modesty for someone who just tore open a dimension") (...), she naturally sought to leverage the battlefield environment. The flaming arena Zhou Hui had erected carried an intense malice, even possessing certain corrupting properties. While insufficient to harm Mahayana cultivators directly, it was more than enough to expose weaknesses during combat.

Upon noticing this, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum repeatedly baited her opponents into directing their attacks into the arena boundary.

Yet after over a dozen matches, that flaming "arena" never once extinguished; it was exceptionally resilient.

[As expected of the power of Tribulation Transcendence!]

Everyone had concluded in the end.

Nobody dreamed it was actually a god. Yet the realization that it was a fragment of a deity only made the Untiring Immortal Venerable appear infinitely more terrifying.

—"I really didn't know it was a god's fragment!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum lamented in deep remorse.-"Never did I imagine Venerable Immortal Zhou would be able to use such a thing, and so casually at that!"

At this moment, even she reverently addressed Zhou Hui as "Venerable Immortal."

This person was clearly more horrifying than any evil deity.

Apparently, she hadn't been the only one. The other Mahayana cultivators had also sensed something amiss and sought to exploit the arena. Coupled with Zhou Hui's promise that "whoever destroys the arena wins first place outright," nobody had held back in the slightest.

—"...Wait, then why did Old Freak have something similar clinging to him?"

Recalling this, the fairy suddenly turned to question Mengshan Old Freak.

—"Huh?"-Mengshan Old Freak blinked, indignant.-"Why drag me into this?! I fought all of you fair and square with the plants I nurtured! I didn't use any cheap tricks outside the rules!"

—"Nonsense! When you fought, a dark shadow floated behind you the entire time! Just like the flames on the arena, it weakened any cultivator fighting you! So we are both guilty!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum accused.-"Even the fact that I defeated you in the end was because I exploited that! I used talismans to disrupt the script within your dark shadow against the script in the arena flames, and that's when space tore open—"

—"Aha! So you really were the one who punched this hole open! And you still try to pin it on me!"-the old freak shouted.

The fairy gasped softly and looked away.

—"So Fairy could actually manifest that divine fragment into characters?"-Zhou Hui's voice echoed from a distance.-"Had I known earlier, I shouldn't have had you all forcefully smash it, but should have sought Fairy's guidance instead."

—"Hey, Zhou Hui, you summoned me here, so why are you dawdling around?"-Tao Zhu complained upon hearing the voice, then froze in realization.-"Wait a moment... forcefully smash it? You intentionally had everyone compete inside just to destroy that thing?!"

—"I merely wished to conduct a stress test,"-Zhou Hui said, smiling.-"Naturally, being able to destroy it is wonderful as well. Has it not torn open the rift I anticipated? Even for me, blasting it open entirely on my own would probably take half a day."

[—"Even this spatial rift was within your calculations?!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

—"Did I not mention that I discovered clues regarding White Jade Capital?"-Zhou Hui said with a calm smile.-"Back in the day, White Jade Capital possessed numerous teleportation arrays connecting the Five Domains and Six Abysses. The arrays leading from the Central Domain to other regions were not limited to the single one preserved in Fengqing City. In this northeastern corner of mine, there was also one. By my estimate, it was right around this position."

—"...Right, here is the blueprint for the teleportation array,"-Zhou Hui said. He took the rubbing of the teleportation array leading to the True Immortal Realm, wiped away the True Immortal Realm coordinates, redrew a fresh copy, and handed it to Tao Zhu.

Hearing that it was the blueprint for a grand cross-domain teleportation array, Tao Zhu's hands trembled as he received it.

After all, the cultivation world had sought to restore those ancient arrays for ages. Yet currently, only a handful of dilapidated arrays led to other continents. Digging them up would sever the connection, and their internal mechanisms couldn't be examined lightly. They could only be used gingerly until the day they broke, at which point who knew if anyone could decipher their inner workings?

Zhou Hui had studied the array for some time, and seeing everything proceed according to plan brought him great satisfaction.

However, another matter caught his attention.

Zhou Hui turned to Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum and asked:

—"You mentioned that Old Freak had script similar to the flame on his body. Is it still there right now?"

*

Eastern Domain.

[—"Zhou Hui! Zhou Hui! Zhou Hui... I will never forgive you!"](# "Speaker: Ancestor of Carefree Palace")

Deep within a grand hall of the Palace of Carefree Wandering, draped in countless talismans, a hollow curse echoed eerily.

In the center of what appeared to be a teleportation array, a clump of [flesh-mush that looked like a censored mosaic](# "Literal CN: 马赛克 (mǎsàikè) — pixelated mosaic / visual censorship effect") lay shattered across the floor, splattered everywhere.

That clump of "matter" was actually alive, crawling with agonizing difficulty toward the outside of the array.

At the end of the direction it was crawling stood a wardrobe. Only, what hung inside was not clothing, but... beauties.

Seductive beauties, gorgeous beauties, pure and innocent beauties—an entire wardrobe of human skins hung like robes.

*

Outside the palace, Li Hao, the Palace Master of Carefree Palace, was issuing orders to his subordinates:

—"...Contract our control over the Central Domain. For now, do not provoke the Heavenly Spirit Sect again. Our immediate priority is our operation in the Abyss Sea. Do not let the Heavenly Spirit Sect notice us."

[—"Yet I heard that the Heavenly Spirit Sect acquired a lead on White Jade Capital?"](# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Subordinate")

—"That's a lie,"-Li Hao stated with absolute certainty. Looking at the rune-covered palace before him, a derisive smile curled his lips.-"If he had truly found White Jade Capital, he would never have dared to strike such a ruthless blow against... our Ancestor."

With that, he withdrew his gaze. Noticing his subordinates still seeming hesitant, he sneered:

—"Yet precisely because our Ancestor has been reduced to such a state, we have no choice but to forfeit the Central Domain. Or would you rather face that ignorant madman yourselves?"

His subordinates dropped to their knees in terror:

[—"This subordinate would never dare!"](# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Subordinate")

—"Then listen to me! A strategic retreat,"-Li Hao said coldly.-"Once our Ancestor has recovered, we will have our revenge!"

[In the worst-case scenario, the only teleportation array leading to the Central Domain is in our hands. As long as we destroy it, even the Untiring Immortal Venerable won't be able to come knocking immediately... right?]

[...Probably.]
