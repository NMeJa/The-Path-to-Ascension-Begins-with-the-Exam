# Chapter 88: Not Even Born Yet (第88章 还没生出来)

The Heavenly Dao protagonist's "[Red Rose](<# "Literal CN: 红玫瑰 (Hóng Méigui) — literary trope from Eileen Chang's novel 'Red Rose, White Rose', symbolizing the passionate, tempestuous lover in contrast to the gentle White Rose">)."

Even with Zhou Hui's mild temperament, merely thinking of this character stoked genuine ire in his heart.

Zhou Hui knew full well that this was venting [misplaced resentment](<# "Literal CN: 迁怒 (qiānnù) — displacing anger onto someone uninvolved / scapegoating">).

It wasn't that there was anything wrong with the young lady herself, but [“the White Moonlight” and “the Cinnabar Mole”](<# "Literal CN: 白月光与朱砂痣 — classic literary contrast popularized by Eileen Chang's novel 'Red Rose, White Rose': the unobtainable pure maiden (white moonlight) versus the passionate, indelible lover (cinnabar mole)">) existed inherently as a foil to one another, naturally inviting comparison at every turn.

For instance: Mo Bei was aloof, ethereal, and coolly elegant, whereas that young lady was striking, voluptuous, and passionate.

For instance: Mo Bei hailed from tragic squalor, possessing nothing to her name and gaining points solely through her [cultivation furnace bloodline](<# "Literal CN: 炉鼎血脉 (lúdǐng xuèmài) — bloodline trait suited for dual-cultivation cauldron/furnace exploitation">), whereas that young lady was born into untouchable nobility—the direct lineage successor of Eternal Villa (who claimed descent from the ancient cultivation realm) and the sole daughter of the City Lord of [Sky City](<# "Literal CN: 天上城 (Tiānshàng Chéng) — supreme floating immortal city in the Northern Domain">), which boasted the highest bloodline in the modern cultivation realm; later in the plot, she even fused with True Immortal blood.

For instance: Mo Bei's sect had been eradicated, and all her surviving senior brothers were world bosses, whereas that young lady had astronomical wealth and endless manpower, turning the entire cultivation realm into the protagonist's stepping stones and support network.

For instance: Mo Bei actually possessed a Nascent Soul cultivation base and was several centuries older than the protagonist, whereas that young lady was several years younger than the protagonist, growing alongside him (as a walking cheat code).

...

Zhou Hui had already been thoroughly disgusted by the idea of [a pig rooting up his family's prized cabbage](<# "Literal CN: 自己家的孩子被猪拱了 — slang idiom: a crude boy (pig) courting/ruining a precious, well-raised daughter/child (cabbage)">), and reading through such comparisons, he inexplicably detected the distinct malice of judgmental in-laws scrutinizing a prospective daughter-in-law.

On the surface, they appeared to be evenly matched Red and White Roses, but in truth, everything about Mo Bei—from her background to her age to her cheat codes—had been thoroughly picked apart and disparaged.

Zhou Hui felt that if whichever wretch dared pick apart his kid like that ever had the gall to stand before him, he would mince their three souls and six spirits into fine paste, bury them in organic refuse to ferment, and use them as fertilizer to nourish Snow-Gleam Grass.

Naturally, even if Zhou Hui vented his resentment, he wouldn't displace it onto Eternal Villa.

As one of the maternal families of the "Red Rose," that villa was an [absolute sucker](<# "Literal CN: 冤大头 (yuāndàtóu) — a gullible person / sucker who suffers heavy losses or is ruthlessly taken advantage of">) as well.

According to the Heavenly Dao script: after slaughtering Heavenly Spirit Sect, the protagonist originally intended to head to Fengqing City to flee via its teleportation array to evade the Heavenly Dao Alliance's investigation. However, upon hearing that Eternal Villa had manufactured a flying vessel capable of traversing the void, he headed to Eternal Villa instead.

At that time, an enraged Mo Xi opened the teleportation array in the depths of the Abyss Sea leading to the Demon Lair to hunt down the protagonist, summoning vast hordes of demons that pursued the protagonist, which caused Eternal Villa to become the very first victim of the demonic calamity.

To withstand the demonic calamity, Villa Master Qingyang stayed behind in the Central Domain alongside the sect's elders and elite disciples, allowing only ordinary disciples to ferry afflicted cultivators aboard the flying vessel toward Sky City in the Northern Domain to report the demonic disaster to the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

The protagonist seized the chance to sneak aboard the flying vessel. Meanwhile, the demon hordes overran Eternal Villa, completely wiping out the Qingyang clan.

Yet during the voyage to the Northern Domain, the vessel encountered a spiritual energy storm; every soul aboard perished. Only the protagonist, utilizing the spatial jump artifact he had found during his secret realm expedition with Xia Bing, successfully reached his destination. Because he delivered the report of the demonic calamity, he was treated with lavish hospitality by Sky City, thereby catching the eye of the Red Rose young miss and sparking a "touching romance."

Incidentally, when it came to "selfless devotion," the Red Rose young miss naturally refused to be outdone by the "White Moonlight."

In the storyline, gifting virtually all of Sky City and the cultivation realm to the protagonist wasn't enough for this eldest young miss; to save the protagonist, she even forcibly swallowed True Immortal blood, ruining her bodily foundation and rendering her unable to bear children. Consequently, believing herself to be imperfect, she voluntarily declared: "Once we rescue Elder Sister Mo Bei, I am willing to serve our husband alongside her." Furthermore, following ascension, she continued caring for the child Mo Bei had borne the protagonist—yes, Mo Bei had even left a child behind for the protagonist.

This degree of [throwing oneself away](<# "Literal CN: 倒贴 (dàotiē) — paying out of one's own pocket to marry down / sacrificing everything for a lover while getting nothing in return">) could probably infuriate Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa back to life, only to drop dead of apoplexy all over again.

Reading this, Zhou Hui even felt a twinge of fellow-sufferer sympathy for Villa Master Qingyang.

At present, however, Villa Master Qingyang knew nothing of this.

In fact, the direct lineage successor of his clan—namely, the future mother of the Red Rose—wasn't even married yet!

Villa Master Qingyang naturally never imagined he would have such an unfilial descendant. Thus, when he arrived at Zhou Hui's immortal cave and met the Untiring Immortal Venerable's faintly pitiful gaze, his heart skipped a sudden beat.

[Why is Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable looking at me like that?]

[Could it be because I rescheduled the academic presentation? Or because my submitted topic isn't up to par? Now that I think about it, it was somewhat hasty. Perhaps I should have picked a far more convincing research topic?]

Villa Master Qingyang thought to himself.

He couldn't help regretting having made such a rash decision to change his topic and reschedule his presentation simply after being provoked by Hermit Zui Liu and that crowd.

[Picking a topic is pure bliss, but drafting the paper is a crematorium!](<# "Literal CN: 选题一时爽，写起来火葬场 — academic internet slang parodying 'X一时爽，全家火葬场': choosing an ambitious research topic feels amazing in the moment, but actually writing it is absolute agonizing torture">)

Even for a Mahayana cultivator, adhering to this format for the first time resulted in flustered scrambling. The thought that his manuscript had to undergo review by the Untiring Immortal Venerable made him all the more anxious.

[Still, even if he is the Immortal Venerable, he wouldn't reject my research proposal and force me to start from scratch, right?]

Villa Master Qingyang gazed uneasily at Zhou Hui.

Amidst this suffocating atmosphere that unsettled even a Mahayana cultivator, the Untiring Immortal Venerable finally spoke:

—"Though it may be somewhat forward of me, regarding the heritage of Eternal Villa—"

—"You mean my paper topic, *Ancient Cultivation Studies*? I admit the scope is a bit broad, but considering how so many people nowadays lack ancient knowledge, especially given the misunderstandings surrounding our Eternal Villa, I felt it necessary to frame the topic on a grander scale—"-Villa Master Qingyang blurted out at rapid-fire speed, entirely forgetting to pause for breath.

Zhou Hui was momentarily taken aback. Fortunately, with his Tribulation Transcendence cultivation base, slowing down the speech playback and parsing its meaning wasn't difficult—(though Tribulation Transcendence cultivation probably wasn't meant to be used like an audio scrubber)—and he realized what Villa Master Qingyang was rambling about.

This left Zhou Hui all the more bewildered:

[I asked about your ancestral heritage—why are you rattling off your thesis proposal?]

Then again, Villa Master Qingyang's topic wasn't entirely unrelated to Eternal Villa's heritage. Could he be hoping for thesis guidance? Was he genuinely this invested in the Immortal Magic Conference?

Come to think of it, he had been exceptionally fixated on Immortal Chess right from the start.

Thinking this far, Zhou Hui felt an uncharacteristic prick of embarrassment. After all, he was the one who had created Immortal Chess and launched this tournament, yet he seemed to be the single person who cared the least about it.

Fortunately, Qu Qianxing was a remarkably dependable assistant. While his master had dispatched him to summon~~(scratch that)~~ invite Hermit Qingyang, he had thoughtfully placed Villa Master Qingyang's topic, the topic and paper of Hermit Zui Liu that had provoked the change, and a comprehensive incident report beside Zhou Hui's desk, right within arm's reach.

Zhou Hui reasoned that since he needed to inquire about personal matters, and the other party clearly sought guidance, he might as well offer a critique!

Reaching out, he casually picked up Villa Master Qingyang's topic proposal.

That single, tiny motion caused every muscle in Villa Master Qingyang's body to lock rigid.

Villa Master Qingyang felt he was being thoroughly pathetic:

[I'm a Mahayana cultivator! I'm not the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple! What is there to be nervous about?!]

Yet that was the Untiring Immortal Venerable! The reputedly strictest Immortal Venerable in the cultivation realm! Rumor had it that back when he first joined the East Hedge Garden, he had corrected the errors of an ancient ancestor several thousand years his senior and lectured the man until he wept.

So many years had elapsed; who knew if the Untiring Immortal Venerable's habit of [tolerating no sand in his eyes](<# "Literal CN: 眼睛容不得沙子 (yǎnjing róngbùdé shāzi) — zero tolerance for flaws, errors, or incompetence">) had mellowed? Perhaps with age, he had grown benevolent and kindly... No, he couldn't be overly optimistic.

After all, he had been that severe back when he had no disciples; now that he had disciples, he would only be harsher!

As everyone knew, teaching brats only made a person increasingly irascible!

At this thought, his initial sliver of regret expanded tenfold. Villa Master Qingyang almost wanted to snatch his paper proposal back and swap it for a much more meticulously prepared one.

—"It seems somewhat lacking in interest perhaps I could approach it from a different angle and incorporate more practical cultivation methodologies that should do the trick..."-his lips moved involuntarily.

[This man truly has genuine academic enthusiasm!]

Zhou Hui thought admiringly.

He had assumed Mahayana cultivators wouldn't bother playing around with the younger generation—so Villa Master Qingyang hadn't guessed wrong; Zhou Hui had originally intended to use him merely as a promotional gimmick. After all, he could hardly hold a sword to two Mahayana cultivators' throats and force them to write academic papers!

It was just that his speaking cadence was rather too rapid, and his bad habit of speaking without pausing for breath was problematic. Did this gentleman lecture his disciples at this exact tempo? If so, the learning comprehension of Eternal Villa's disciples was frankly astonishing.

Regardless of whether Eternal Villa's disciples were fast learners, Zhou Hui possessed more than enough patience.

He listened intently to Villa Master Qingyang's words, chiming in with constructive critique from time to time. Slowly, gradually, he soothed Villa Master Qingyang's agitated nerves until his speech returned to something intelligible to human ears.

And just like that, the two of them inexplicably finalized the core content of Hermit Qingyang's paper.

—"I believe this direction is fairly solid. You may certainly give it a try,"-Zhou Hui concluded.

Hearing those words, Villa Master Qingyang finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Looking once more at his paper proposal—which had been altered beyond recognition—he felt exceptionally pleased. It felt as though if he took it back right now, he could produce an epoch-defining masterpiece.

Thinking this, Villa Master Qingyang could hardly sit still, eager to take his leave:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable..."

Fortunately, Zhou Hui also felt their thesis defense had run its course, and it was about time to pivot to the 'main topic.'

Not wanting to be cut off by Villa Master Qingyang's rapid-fire chatter, Zhou Hui seized a rare lull between his syllables and spoke up:

—"By the way, Villa Master Qingyang, regarding the history of Eternal Villa you recounted just now, there is one detail I do not quite understand."

—"Please, speak!"-Villa Master Qingyang's posture locked rigid once more.

He suddenly felt his confidence in the proposal wavering again.

—"Regarding the portion about White Jade Capital,"-Zhou Hui made a somewhat stiff transition into his intended topic,-"Eternal Villa claims descent from the sacred lord bloodline of White Jade Capital, yet you also claim to possess True Immortal heritage... However, according to what I know, the sacred lords of White Jade Capital were barred from possessing True Immortal bloodlines, were they not? In fact, were they not strictly forbidden from ascending?"

"!" Hearing this, Villa Master Qingyang's eyes went wide.

No longer did he wear that jittery expression of a junior researcher terrified of having his grant proposal rejected by the faculty board; he looked at Zhou Hui with intense solemnity.

—"As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable. You even know of this?"-Villa Master Qingyang said respectfully.

Zhou Hui naturally knew—because this time around, he was the designated "Sacred Lord"!

—"I have merely heard rumors,"-Zhou Hui said, without elaborating on his sources of information, merely inquiring:-"As the inheritors of that lineage, Eternal Villa ought to possess more exhaustive records on the matter?"

—"No. Our records suffered vast gaps following the God-Demon War; they are not terribly detailed either..."-Villa Master Qingyang replied cautiously.

Earlier, during the paper review, despite sweating under the Untiring Immortal Venerable's pressure, he had refused to budge on boasting about Eternal Villa's illustrious origins and magnificent techniques. Yet now, upon hearing Zhou Hui's question, he abruptly lost his bluster, as if completely drained of the urge to boast.

Zhou Hui did not back down; instead, he pressed further:

—"If I am not mistaken, ancient cultivators did not regard the True Immortal Realm with the legendary 'reverence' popularized today, did they? Was there an element of exploitation?"

[—"You have already delved that deeply into the matter?"](<# "Speaker: Villa Master Qingyang">)

—"Is this part classified?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"No, certainly not. But it is far too shocking; if I spoke of it openly, I would merely be treated as an eccentric! Still, if the Immortal Venerable is interested, I might as well share what our Eternal Villa has recorded,"-Villa Master Qingyang gave a wry chuckle. Though he said this, he still appeared somewhat hesitant, pausing for a moment before continuing:—"True Immortals and demons are actually... born of the exact same origin!"

—"Mmh,"-Zhou Hui listened with rapt attention.

Seeing that Zhou Hui showed no inclination to refute him, Villa Master Qingyang continued:

—"Just as we cultivators have both orthodox paths and demonic cultivators, immortals also assume different states based on varying cultivation methods. Upon that foundation, they distinguished the 'Immortal-Demon Realm' and the 'True Immortal Realm.' The Immortal-Demon Realm was likely the birthplace of those 'immortals who once allied with us,' and what is commonly referred to as ascension leads first to the 'Immortal-Demon Realm.' According to our records, that world is rather similar to ours, except its spiritual energy is richer, its species more diverse, and its environment far more chaotic. In that world, one has the opportunity to come into contact with factions from higher realms (the True Immortal Realm or the True Demon Realm). Upon earning recognition from those factions, one ascends once more."

This aligned perfectly with the Heavenly Dao script.

Zhou Hui nodded:

—"I see."

—"...You seem to know all of this already!"-Villa Master Qingyang remarked with an odd expression, observing Zhou Hui's placid demeanor.

Then, sudden comprehension dawned upon him.

Naturally. Given the Untiring Immortal Venerable's temperament, he wouldn't bring up such matters out of the blue unless he had already pieced everything together!

Moreover, during the earlier "Mahayana Exhibition Tournament," the prize offered by the Untiring Immortal Venerable had been a map to the teleportation array of White Jade Capital! Clearly, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had investigated this for ages and possessed deep knowledge.

Villa Master Qingyang convinced himself he had figured it all out.

Consequently, his attitude toward Zhou Hui grew even more reverent.

After all, cultivation aristocratic clans like Eternal Villa had endured across epochs precisely because they held monopoly over this esoteric knowledge. That the Untiring Immortal Venerable could uncover all this proved that even setting aside his colossal Tribulation Transcendence cultivation, his cognitive grasp of the world had reached the absolute zenith attainable in this realm—making him an equal to these ancient clans, and perhaps meaning Heavenly Spirit Sect might endure even longer than them!

Armored with this realization, Villa Master Qingyang was willing to confide further in Zhou Hui:

—"Therefore, you likely understand the origin of White Jade Capital's philosophy—since True Immortals can degenerate into demons, they are by no means superior beings. As an individual, one may ascend in pursuit of better prospects, but as guardians of this realm, we cannot surrender this world to outsiders, nor may we abandon it on a whim."

Saying this, Villa Master Qingyang fluttered his folding fan:

—"Even today, Eternal Villa maintains that same attitude. We do not pursue ascension; enjoying life in this world is more than enough."

[So your insufferable poser swagger is just your way of enjoying life!]

Zhou Hui had his horizons broadened, but he concurred with Villa Master Qingyang's sentiment:

—"Indeed. After retirement... after renouncing ascension, instructing disciples and organizing a few events has made my state of mind vastly more carefree."

Hearing the Untiring Immortal Venerable say this, Villa Master Qingyang wore an expression of hesitating on the verge of speaking.

He seemed to have caught an earth-shattering piece of intelligence: "The Immortal Venerable says he has renounced ascension." Yet that didn't quite tally with the Untiring Immortal Venerable's actions! Then again, to say it completely contradicted his actions wasn't entirely true either.

Villa Master Qingyang was deeply conflicted, unsure whether he should press for clarification.

Zhou Hui didn't grant him the opportunity, focusing only on the crucial point that remained unresolved:

—"However, if that is the case, what is the story behind your 'True Immortal heritage'?"

—"Ha... that is even more irreverent,"-Villa Master Qingyang chuckled. Having reached this point, he didn't conceal anything:-"Though White Jade Capital was comprised of cultivators who chose not to pursue ascension, to our cultivation realm, the True Immortal Realm has always been a higher-plane existence. Even if ancient cultivators chose not to ascend, they maintained contact with the True Immortal Realm, conducting certain research into True Immortal techniques, which were subsequently passed down."

That phrasing was remarkably tactful!

What those ancient cultivators researched certainly wasn't limited to mere "cultivation methods"!

Zhou Hui reflected that if he had been in their shoes, he would definitely have researched far more "thoroughly."

In truth, judging from the records in the Heavenly Dao script where the Red Rose young miss fused with True Immortal blood, Eternal Villa's True Immortal heritage was by no means confined to paper manuals.

Zhou Hui finally [revealed his dagger as the scroll unrolled](<# "Literal CN: 图穷匕见 (tú qióng bǐ xiàn) — the dagger is revealed when the map unrolls; showing one's true hand / getting straight to the real purpose">), no longer bothering to beat around the bush.

Raising his palm, a single drop of sealed "True Immortal Blood" drifted up from his center, suspended midair.

—"With your heritage, can you analyze this?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"...!"-Villa Master Qingyang's jaw dropped slack. Staring dumbfounded at the droplet of blood, he failed for a moment to even register what Zhou Hui had asked:-"Tr-True Immortal Blood?!"

—"Indeed,"-Zhou Hui asked a second time:-"Can you analyze it... and determine its origin?"

—"...You are asking me to analyze this blood?!"-Having finally registered Zhou Hui's words, Villa Master Qingyang asked in breathless excitement.

If his demeanor earlier had been respectful, right now he looked as if he wanted to throw himself forward and wrap his arms around Zhou Hui's thighs.

Zhou Hui could understand his excitement.

Although Villa Master Qingyang claimed Eternal Villa knew everything and had long [lain flat and embraced slacking](<# "Literal CN: 躺平摆烂 (tǎngpíng bǎilàn) — lying flat and letting things rot; completely renouncing ambition and effort">), in reality, this knowledge was merely ancestral lore passed down through generations without empirical verification. And since the only lesson humanity learns from history is that humanity never learns from history, with the passage of millenia, they naturally doubted the veracity of these records.

Especially in a cultivation climate where every single person obsessed over ascension, Eternal Villa was inevitably influenced.

And those most severely affected were figures like Villa Master Qingyang.

He had already cultivated to the Mahayana stage, having reached the absolute peak of his domain. At this stage, unable to ascend, he could only idle away his days in a world that had grown exceedingly tedious to him; bouts of depression were unavoidable.

Zhou Hui's drop of "True Immortal Blood" matched his academic specialization to a tee, granting him the opportunity to advance his research and empirically test whether his ancestors' heritage was accurate.

Yet Villa Master Qingyang was the master of a sect after all; loose and unbothered though he appeared on ordinary days, he understood the gravity of critical matters.

His gaze remained glued to that drop of True Immortal blood, yet his lips spoke:

—"Given sufficient time, I *might* be able to determine this blood's origin. However, the techniques regarding True Immortal blood are the secret legacy of our Eternal Villa, which cannot be casually revealed to outsiders..."

Reaching this point, Villa Master Qingyang felt a pang of despair, certain that Zhou Hui would turn him down.

—After all, this was no different from asking for someone's blood for free. And True Immortal blood was a priceless material even for a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse to refine supreme artifacts or aid ascension—why would anyone part with it easily?

—"Then I leave it in your capable hands,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"I know you would be reluctant, though I have some confi... Huh?!"-Villa Master Qingyang froze.-"What did you just say...?"

[—"Yes. I wish to know its origin."](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

He didn't care about parting with this drop of True Immortal blood. He had no intention of using it to refine anything, and as an experimental sample its volume was far too minuscule. Besides, this drop of blood hadn't even existed in the Heavenly Dao script; all of Zhou Hui's deductions were educated guesses. Who was to say it wasn't a completely trivial entity?

Zhou Hui figured that uncovering its origins was splendid, but failing to do so was fine as well—in any case, he was already planning to inspect the nesting grounds of the Nine-Headed Bird.

Only in the hands of a specialist would this blood yield maximum value.

Thus, Zhou Hui politely offered:

—"Regarding [research funding](<# "Literal CN: 研究经费 (yánjiū jīngfèi) — academic research grant / funding">)... cough, whatever spirit stones and materials you require to study this droplet of blood, you may request them from me."

—"No need, no need! Our Eternal Villa can cover everything!"-Villa Master Qingyang insisted on paying out of his own pocket.

—"It is fine. I am the one commissioning you, so I should provide the funding,"-Zhou Hui asserted firmly, though he was not without conditions:-"However, each quarter, I expect a related academic progress report."

"..." The expression on Villa Master Qingyang's face froze solid for two full minutes.

It brought about that jarring cognitive dissonance of being taken on vacation only to be assigned an essay about it.

Still, for a child who never got to go on vacation, writing a few essays was well worth it! This was the [funding sponsor's](<# "Literal CN: 金主 (jīnzhǔ) — financial backer / sponsor / funding grant agency">) rightful due!

—"I will write the reports,"-Villa Master Qingyang still insisted:-"And I will foot the bill for the materials and spirit stones—do not refuse! Otherwise, I would be too ashamed to take this droplet of blood!"

"..." Zhou Hui found this utterly incomprehensible.

This was the first time he had ever witnessed someone doing work for another while [paying out of their own pocket](<# "Literal CN: 倒贴 (dàotiē) — subsidizing someone else's request / paying to work for someone">).

Yet Villa Master Qingyang felt this was still far from enough.

This was a drop of True Immortal blood after all! Something priceless and unobtainable at any price.

Villa Master Qingyang glanced around in hesitation, his eyes suddenly lighting up when his gaze landed on Qu Qianxing, who had been sitting quietly behind Zhou Hui all along.

—"Though I cannot divulge the specific procedural details to Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable, if you do not mind, you may send your disciple to serve as my research assistant,"-Villa Master Qingyang chuckled warmly.-"While I will not explicitly tutor him in anything, he will have his own two eyes to watch. Whatever he manages to learn is his to keep."

—"You mean... Qianxing?"-Zhou Hui remarked in surprise.

His expression turned even more peculiar:

—"Lord Qingyang, you must not underestimate this disciple of mine. Letting him shadow your experiments is practically no different from handing the entire heritage over to him!"

—"Hahaha, you certainly hold immense confidence in your disciple!"-Villa Master Qingyang laughed as if hearing a marvelous joke.-"If that is the case, if he can absorb it, let him absorb it!" Even as he spoke, he didn't believe for a second that a Golden Core cultivator like Qu Qianxing could glean much substantive knowledge from the complex workings of a Mahayana powerhouse.

—"Still, for the Immortal Venerable to hold such faith in his disciple, his academic marks must be stellar, yes? Splendid! Your disciple can also guide me on how to format these academic reports." Villa Master Qingyang was immensely proud of his keen foresight.

To this, Zhou Hui wore an uncharacteristically guilty expression.

He had already felt he was taking tremendous advantage of the man; now he was letting Qianxing take massive advantage of him too...

Yet research related to True Immortals was a singular karmic opportunity for Qianxing, who was born naturally endowed with [Wenqu Scholar's Qi](<# "Literal CN: 文曲之气 (Wénqǔ zhī qì) — spiritual aura of the Wenqu star, the celestial deity governing civil scholarship, intellect, and imperial exams">). If he missed this chance, he might never encounter another.

Zhou Hui hesitated a moment before finally nodding. Yet he refused to simply take advantage of the man:

—"In that case, Villa Master Qingyang may also dispatch a disciple to study under me. They may choose any one magical technique in which I specialize as an exchange."

—"Truly?! How could I possibly...?"-Villa Master Qingyang instinctively moved to decline.

Just like Zhou Hui, he felt he was taking enormous advantage of the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Yet receiving tutelage from the Untiring Immortal Venerable was a karmic fortune Eternal Villa could never bear to surrender.

[Forget it. When the time comes, I'll simply give Qu Qianxing a few extra pointers!]

Having made up his mind, Villa Master Qingyang said:

—"Since the Immortal Venerable has put it that way, I shall not stand on ceremony. Our Qingyang clan has a direct lineage successor named Qingyang Qingyu. She currently possesses Golden Core cultivation and is passably teachable."

[Qingyang Qingyu? The mother of the Red Rose young miss seemed to bear this exact name?]

Because she was exceptionally beautiful, the protagonist had [fantasized](<# "Literal CN: YY (yìyín) — web novel slang for erotic daydreaming / fantasizing">) about her, which was why her name had appeared in the text.

[Then this is quite opportune. Perhaps with a few pointers—though it likely won't achieve much—I might at least prevent the future Red Rose young miss from throwing herself away quite so abjectly?]

Zhou Hui thought to himself.

After saying this, Villa Master Qingyang glanced at Qu Qianxing once more, a sudden inspiration striking him:

—"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable, your second disciple is not yet betrothed, is he? It so happens my Qingyu has not settled on a suitable match either. Seeing as their age and cultivation bases match so well..."

[Hm? Butterfly-effect the Red Rose completely out of existence just like that?]

Zhou Hui felt a momentary flutter of temptation, only to feel Qu Qianxing tugging firmly at his robes.

—"My apologies. My cultivation has yet to reach fruition; I dare not start a family,"-Qu Qianxing stated flatly.

—"Hahaha, shy, are we?"-Villa Master Qingyang laughed warmly.-"Then we shall discuss it at a later date, at a later date."
