# Chapter 227: The Old Days Reenacted

—"Ah, the tournament? It's my turn already?"-Villa Master Qingyang asked blankly.

It took him a full three seconds to recall that back then, he seemed to have promised Untiring Immortal Venerable that he would participate in the Immortal Arts Assembly.

Come to think of it, the exhibition match between the two Mahayana powerhouses—Villa Master Qingyang and Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum—had all along been hyped up as a major promotional gimmick in the run-up to the Immortal Arts Assembly.

Only, things having progressed to this point, everyone seemed to have forgotten all about it.

At the very least, Villa Master Qingyang had forgotten.

It couldn't be helped; his past year had simply been far too fulfilling! What with researching True Immortal Blood, conducting faculty exchange programs, churning out academic papers, and, on the rare occasion he had a moment of leisure, playing matchmaker to kindle the karmic romance between his great-great-great-granddaughter Qingyang Qingyu and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's second disciple, Qu Qianxing—it truly hadn't left him much time to dwell on tournament affairs.

And once the Immortal Arts Assembly officially commenced, Villa Master Qingyang became busier still: not only did he have to monitor his own clan disciples' match progress and analyze matchups of personal interest, but most detestable of all, there was an irritating "insect" buzzing ceaselessly around his great-great-great-granddaughter, while his preferred candidate for great-great-great-grandson-in-law was nowhere to be found, giving the villa master a massive headache.

However, what had truly captivated Villa Master Qingyang the most was the assembly's "Trial System."

At first, the system had been fairly unremarkable—nothing more than an illusion realm allowing ordinary cultivators to farm items and hone their techniques. For a fully matured high-ranking cultivator like Villa Master Qingyang, it was [better than nothing](# "Literal CN: 聊胜于无 (liáo shèng yú wú) — marginally better than having nothing at all"). Yet ever since the "God-Demon War" DLC rolled out, Villa Master Qingyang had found himself completely hooked.

On one hand, he was astonished by the staggering costs Untiring Immortal Venerable had sunk into engineering an illusion realm of such realistic, breathtaking scale; on the other hand, he was even more floored by the verisimilitude of the God-Demon Battlefield within.

Bear in mind, this was a certified endorsement coming from none other than the Patriarch of Eternal Villa.

In that apocalyptic war tens of thousands of years ago, unlike the other cultivators who had fled to the distant Northern Domain, the cultivators of Eternal Villa had persevered on the battlefield right until the final moment of victory! One could say Eternal Villa was an indelible part of that very battlefield. As a ten-thousand-year-old cultivation clan, Eternal Villa possessed far more ancestral historical records than any other cultivation clan.

Not to mention, Villa Master Qingyang could even be considered half a "firsthand witness."

A span of ten thousand years was indeed vast enough to bring about [the shifting of blue seas into mulberry fields](# "Literal CN: 沧海桑田 (cānghǎi sāngtián) — vast geological and worldly transformations over aeons"). Yet to the cultivation world's apex Mahayana powerhouses, though ten millennia was immense, it was by no means unimaginable. For Villa Master Qingyang, who had lived several thousand years himself, even if he hadn't personally taken part in the fighting, his clan elders had all been direct participants in that historic war; and when he was young, the family estate had still been littered everywhere with scars and remnants of the conflict.

Consequently, upon entering the "God-Demon War" instance dungeon, the first thing Villa Master Qingyang and the disciples of Eternal Villa did was not to hunt demons, but rather to fly straight toward their own ancestral estate.

—"What sort of dilapidated wreck is this?"-a disciple grumbled indignantly upon laying eyes on the condition of their estate.

—"No. Back then, this was exactly what it looked like,"-Villa Master Qingyang corrected solemnly.

Pausing, he explained:

—"The demons had an invasion breach nearby, so our main ancestral house was devastated with particular brutality. The main manor you see today was planned and rebuilt piece by piece in the aftermath of the war."

To be precise, because his clan elders had all sustained varying degrees of crippling injuries during the war, the monumental burden of reconstructing the main manor had fallen square on Villa Master Qingyang's shoulders, spanning the better part of his life.

Yet while he had thought the mess handed over to him back then was already appalling enough, he hadn't imagined that it was actually the hard-won result of his forebears' best efforts to clean up! The site of the God-Demon War "at this present moment" was even more catastrophic: not only was the main manor completely unrecognizable, with demonic qi churning everywhere, but terrifying demons ran rampant, seeking to corrupt the entire space into demonic domain.

Most glaring of all was the center of the main house, where a hideous insect nest stood erect, plastered with teeming demon insect eggs—a sight so sickening a single glance could [drain away all sanity](# "Literal CN: 清空所有的理智 (qīngkōng suǒyǒu de lǐzhì) — HP/sanity bar gaming metaphor").

Seeing this, Villa Master Qingyang muttered under his breath:

—"So that's how it was... No wonder a whopping five tons of finely processed top-grade spirit stones were buried directly beneath the center of the main manor, with strict orders forbidding me from ever moving them, making laying the foundation a nightmare when I rebuilt the estate... Huh?"

[How could Untiring Immortal Venerable possibly know this?!]

He thought suddenly.

The main ancestral manor was the paramount core of any cultivation clan. Successive patriarchs would only divulge the most critical architectural secrets to their successors upon their imminent passing or ascension. Thus, even designated heirs were mostly oblivious to the origins behind certain configurations—for instance, Villa Master Qingyang had never known why five tons of spirit stones were buried there, knowing only that they must not be touched under any circumstance.

True, Untiring Immortal Venerable was undeniably erudite, his knowledge spanning antiquity to the present day. If it were him, pulling off a monumental feat like reenacting the God-Demon Battlefield was certainly jaw-dropping, yet on closer reflection, not entirely beyond his capabilities.

But no matter how realistic Untiring Immortal Venerable's reconstruction was, it shouldn't be so absurdly accurate as to capture even internal family secrets like this, right?

Villa Master Qingyang instantly grew suspicious.

The other disciples of Eternal Villa had no inkling of their patriarch's misgivings. While initially incensed by the wreckage of their ancestral home and the swarming demon hordes, the realization that this was merely an illusion realm quickly calmed their tempers, giving way to eager anticipation:

What cultivator did not harbor the youthful dream of slaying demons and saving the world?

What was more, what the disciples of Eternal Villa were setting out to save was their very own ancestral home! On that count alone, they possessed far greater [role immersion](# "Literal CN: 代入感 (dàirùgǎn) — immersion / sense of personal identification") than any other cultivators.

—"Lord Ancestor, let us take back our territory!"-the current Fifth Master, Qingyang Qingli, requested with soaring fighting spirit.

—"Mm..."-Villa Master Qingyang responded absentmindedly while lost in thought:—"Huh? Wait..."

[You brats are still far too green to take on demons!]

Villa Master Qingyang wanted to stop them.

It wasn't that he distrusted his own clan's disciples; rather, in the context of the God-Demon Battlefield, Eternal Villa was blatantly a high-difficulty raid zone—here lay an active Demon Eye providing a direct highway for demonic legions, yet there was no external backup in sight. Most likely, flying directly to defend White Jade Capital would be vastly safer. Recklessly rushing into battle would only shatter the confidence of these young cultivators who had never once faced genuine demons.

Alas, Villa Master Qingyang's reaction was a beat too slow. The Qingyang clan disciples had already interpreted that absentminded "Mm" as his green light, and charged jubilantly onto the battlefield.

And to Villa Master Qingyang's astonishment, the brats were actually doing remarkably well.

They hadn't foolishly launched a frontal assault on the insect nest. Instead, they split into several squads: one team harassed the hive to bait out the guardian drone bugs; another methodically eradicated roaming fiends; a third lay in ambush near the hive, waiting for its defenses to empty before torching the entire nest; while yet another squad laid down suppression formations to trap the burning stench from alerting other demonic bugs.

Furthermore, they weren't the least bit arrogant or reckless!

[One knows one's own family best](# "Literal CN: 自家人知自家事 (zìjiārén zhī zìjiā shì) — nobody knows family flaws better than family"). Given how pampered, spoiled, and prideful the disciples of Eternal Villa normally were, it was almost impossible for them not to overestimate their prowess or stubbornly insist on foolish vanity over trivial matters—fitting the textbook definition of stereotypical silken-trousered dandy scions in romance novels. Villa Master Qingyang had never been able to do much about it. In fact, he had always felt that so long as they weren't truly rotten at the core, his children possessing a touch more pride than other families was actually a good thing! Had it not been for that stubborn streak, Eternal Villa would never have held out in the Central Domain to the bitter end during the God-Demon War, refusing to surrender their ancestral soil.

Yet now, these children who "merely had a bit too much self-esteem" were facing demons with fiery enthusiasm, but without a shred of bravado. Or rather, they understood the boundaries of their own limits and their enemies' lethality with absolute clarity. Thus, after briskly executing foes within their wheelhouse, they decisively kited enemies they couldn't handle toward clan members of higher cultivation.

One of them even managed to kite an insect-king-tier demonic beast, barreling straight toward Villa Master Qingyang:

[—"Old Ancestor, save me!"](# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple")

That desperate cry caused Villa Master Qingyang's chest to tighten. For reasons unknown, this scene felt [uncannily familiar](# "Literal CN: 似曾相识 (sì céng xiāng shí) — déjà vu / as if having experienced this before"):

He seemed to have witnessed his clan disciples being hunted down by demons before; he seemed to have watched the main manor he personally rebuilt be overrun once more by demonic insects; he seemed to have wept in grief and fury over the annihilation of his clan at the claws of the demon race.

In that fleeting instant, countless tragic vignettes flashed across Villa Master Qingyang's mind. Yet like water slipping through fingers, he couldn't grasp them completely. Only a lingering residue of grief, sorrow, and bitter anguish remained—and it was precisely this emotional surge that compelled his hand to rise, lashing out toward the insect—

—"!"

The bug naturally didn't even have time to utter a sound before it was instantly obliterated into flying ash.

However, the strike carried such devastating force that the Eternal Villa disciple was caught in the shockwave, sent tumbling backward across the ground in a series of somersaults.

Fortunately, the disciples seemed to have anticipated even this scenario; a medical squad materialized beside the victim in the blink of an eye, administering prompt emergency treatment.

Naturally, they also caught onto Villa Master Qingyang's abnormal state.

—"Old Ancestor, it was merely a trivial bug; it couldn't survive even a flick of your sleeve. Could you perhaps... dial it back a notch?"-a disciple asked, timidly observing Villa Master Qingyang's expression.

—"Indeed,"-Villa Master Qingyang replied somewhat hesitantly.

He stared down at the hand that had cast the spell, desperately trying to seize the phantom visions that had just slipped away. He had an unshakable feeling that they held monumental significance for him, yet try as he might, he couldn't recall a single detail.

[Why...?]

[—"Lord Ancestor! Over here, over here!"](# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple")

Before Villa Master Qingyang could make head or tail of it, he heard his disciples' theatrical "wails" once more.

Only this time, Villa Master Qingyang reined in his emotions—partly because the kid looked nowhere near in genuine mortal peril. He waved his hand with measured force, pulverizing the demon fiend, only to watch yet another disciple kite a demonic monster straight toward him:

[—"Old Ancestor!"](# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciple")

Once, twice, three times... With successive waves repeating endlessly, every trace of emotional fluctuation within Villa Master Qingyang went stone-cold numb.

He felt as though he had been reduced to a stationary ballista turret: so long as someone shouted "Ancestor" or "Lord Ancestor," he would automatically discharge a round, exterminating enemies for his clan's disciples.

—"You all seem remarkably practiced at this, don't you?"-Villa Master Qingyang couldn't resist questioning.

—"Not at all, not at all!"-Qingyang Qingli took this as praise. Attempting to feign modesty while the corners of his mouth curled smugly upward, he continued:—"We've merely undergone sufficient tempering inside the Spirit Stone Secret Realm, that's all."

—"The Spirit Stone Secret Realm? Ah, you mean..."-Villa Master Qingyang started.

[That Demon Eye?]

As Patriarch of Eternal Villa, Villa Master Qingyang was naturally aware of the Demon Eye beneath their ancestral secret realm. Back then, it was precisely because an ominous foreboding plagued his heart that he had used having Untiring Immortal Venerable tutor Qingyang Qingyu as a pretext to entrust the Demon Eye to Zhou Hui's handling. And Untiring Immortal Venerable had indeed, in accordance with "his wishes," remodeled the area surrounding the Demon Eye into a peculiar facility akin to the Thousand Stars Secret Realm, observing and exterminating invading demonic aberrations.

Only, Villa Master Qingyang had harbored a long-standing misconception.

He had assumed their family secret realm had merely undergone a "modest" renovation. He thought Untiring Immortal Venerable captured demonic fiends solely to observe the Demon Domain's condition and reinforce the seal. He had absolutely no idea that secret realm had already become a staging bridgehead for counter-invading the Demon Realm!

This was not due to carelessness on Villa Master Qingyang's part, but rather a massive information asymmetry.

After all, the Heavenly Spirit Sect lacked manpower. Thus, when Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu constructed that secret realm, they primarily conscripted labor from Eternal Villa. One could say over ninety percent of the villa's disciples had cycled through the secret realm by now! Villa Master Qingyang had received periodic reports and believed everything was firmly under control. However, terrified that leaked news would spark panic, he had never even told his disciples about the Demon Eye. Consequently, none of the disciples realized that was an authentic portal to the Demon Realm; they had simply been gaming and having the time of their lives, and naturally couldn't provide accurate operational feedback to their patriarch!

Villa Master Qingyang knew now—yet his clan disciples had already fought tens of thousands of rounds against demonic monsters, and had even proactively built their fortified outpost right inside the Demon Realm!

For a moment, Villa Master Qingyang experienced the exact same sensation Zhou Hui had felt upon discovering Qu Qianxing was counter-invading the Demon Realm—except amplified several thousandfold in pure PTSD shock. He nearly bolted off to demand an explanation from Untiring Immortal Venerable on the spot!

Yet casting his gaze across the Eternal Villa disciples, who not only showed zero panic in the face of demonic fiends but conducted their battle formations with methodical precision, an inexplicable sense of relief washed over him.

[Getting accustomed to combat early is a blessing!]

[Perhaps, this time, we will not repeat the same disastrous mistakes...]

Huh? "This time"?

Villa Master Qingyang pressed his temples. That sensation of being on the verge of recalling something surged once more. From this, he was certain: this was neither an illusion nor the effect of some mind-altering spellcraft, but rather that he had genuinely forgotten something of paramount importance.

Yet a spell capable of wiping the memories of a Mahayana powerhouse was by no means ordinary. Recovering memories from such an enchantment was easier said than done. Having reasoned this through, Villa Master Qingyang did not force himself to keep straining his mind, opting instead to pivot his strategy.

—"Enough, I have a firm grasp of your capabilities now!"-Villa Master Qingyang announced loudly.

Refusing to keep standing here like a fixed DPS turret to be 'utilized' by his disciples, he decisively seized back operational command:

—"However, your efficiency is far too low. Now! Follow me, and let us take back our land."

[—"Understood, Old Ancestor!"](# "Speaker: Eternal Villa Disciples")

The Eternal Villa disciples responded with roaring enthusiasm.

Thus, by the time the scheduling notification for the Mahayana-tier matches of the Immortal Arts Assembly reached Villa Master Qingyang, the villa master had already reclaimed control over the ancestral estate grounds, rallied scattered wandering cultivators, and was actively gearing up for a counter-offensive into the demons' native realm!

***

On the other hand, the other contestant of the match, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum, naturally received the identical tournament scheduling notification.

—"Is there even any point in that old Daoist and me holding a match?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum questioned as well.

Unlike Villa Master Qingyang, who was addicted to the God-Demon War, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's "objective" had already been fulfilled:

She had never been hooked on Immortal Chess, nor did she hold any personal stake with Untiring. Her initial agreement to generate hype for the Immortal Arts Assembly was because Untiring had advertised that he held clues regarding White Jade Capital—at the time, Untiring hadn't dared make the news of White Jade Capital public, using it merely as a [carrot to dangle before donkeys](# "Literal CN: 钓驴的萝卜 (diào lǘ de luóbo) — dangling a carrot in front of a donkey; baiting high-tier cultivators") (namely, high-tier cultivators).

What Untiring himself hadn't anticipated, however, was that within less than half a year, he had actually restored the Ascension Stairway! As a result, White Jade Capital seemed rather inconsequential by comparison, serving merely as an incremental milestone reward to incentivize cultivators to comply with the new rules of the Ascension Stairway.

Thus, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum no longer needed to win the Immortal Arts Assembly to gain access to White Jade Capital.

In fact, during this "God-Demon War" instance, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum hadn't bothered with anything else; her very first objective had been to fly straight toward the ten-thousand-year-old White Jade Capital that existed within this battlefield.

And this wasn't true for her alone; the vast majority of cultivators had done the exact same thing.

After all, unlike Eternal Villa, to most cultivators the God-Demon War was merely ancient history. Forcing them to fight demons carried very little visceral reality.

In contrast, the citadel of White Jade Capital was the sacred holy city in the hearts of this world's people. If one were to name the single most electrifying event of the ancient God-Demon War ten millennia ago, it was the Defense of White Jade Capital.

Because of this, in the God-Demon War dungeon, White Jade Capital had become the fiercest battleground between cultivators and demonic legions—and simultaneously the safest haven! Because cultivators of all realms congregated there, even low-stage cultivators could easily [fish in troubled waters](# "Literal CN: 浑水摸鱼 (húnshuǐ mōyú) — opportunistic survival/scavenging in chaotic crowds") within the vast metropolis. Had it not been for this, tracking down Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum would have been nearly impossible; she had always hated bustling crowds, and not a whisper had been heard of her throughout the entire past year.

When Jian Nuo and Qingyang Qingyu finally located her, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum wasn't participating in the war either. She was simply strolling leisurely along the streets of White Jade Capital.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum wouldn't renege on the match on that account, yet her enthusiasm was visibly tepid:

—"I thought the promotional hype was already sufficient, and that Qingyang and I wouldn't need to put our old faces on display,"-she remarked.

—"Please don't say that; everyone is looking forward to it immensely,"-Jian Nuo said with a smile:—"Countless people traveled all this way just to catch a glimpse of Your Ladyship's graceful bearing."

—"Hah!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum sneered coldly.

Even if she had no desire to compete, that didn't mean she was oblivious to the tournament dynamics.

She knew full well that because the high-stage cultivators' matches had devolved into polite displays of "courteous deference," spectators had turned their attention toward the low-stage brackets instead. Coupled with the release of the "God-Demon War" instance, even the spectators had excitedly thrown themselves into the Trial System, leaving virtually no one paying attention to the tournament itself!

Regarding Untiring's decision to roll out such a massive war system right at this juncture, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum even suspected whether the man genuinely wanted to trash his own venue and cancel the Immortal Arts Assembly altogether.

Yet despite grumbling internally, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum did not argue. She still wanted to visit the 'White Jade Capital of reality', after all, so she had to grant Untiring this face. Thus, she asked with faint interest:

—"The time and location of the match?"

—"There is no set time prescribed for the preliminary match between you and Lord Ancestor. You may begin whenever you wish, even right now,"-Qingyang Qingyu explained:—"As for the location, it takes place right within this God-Demon War."

—"Eh?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum froze in surprise.

Qingyang Qingyu attempted to explain:

—"You should be aware that during the God-Demon War, a rather severe internal strife once broke out among the cultivators of our realm, correct?"

—"Hmph, a junior dares lecture me on history?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum scoffed. After all, she styled herself as the "Mausoleum Keeper" of White Jade Capital; though she herself had no idea what she was guarding, she had naturally conducted thorough investigations into that era's history:—"You mean during the early days of the demonic invasion, when certain cultivators of White Jade Capital petitioned the Immortal Realm for aid, while others believed our world's affairs must be resolved by our own hands? One side forcibly summoned a True Immortal while the other sought to stop them, culminating in a disastrous bout of internal strife..."

Historical records of that civil conflict were sparse, but it seemed not to have caused excessive casualties. Because something mysterious occurred during the ritual, it concluded with both factions joining forces to transport the Jian Clan's Sky City away from the Central Domain—and of course, the Celestial Gate was blasted open as well, ushering in the true war.

Thinking up to this point, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum suddenly caught on:

—"Wait a moment. Don't tell me this means Qingyang and I not only have to duel with spellcraft, but must also put on a theatrical performance for everyone to watch?"

—"Your Ladyship is truly perceptive,"-Qingyang Qingyu praised.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had no need for such hollow flattery, especially coming from a junior separated by countless generations.

Seeing the displeasure on Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's face, Jian Nuo hurriedly interjected:

—"How could this possibly be considered acting? This is reenacting history! The environment here is so breathtakingly authentic—does Your Ladyship not wish to uncover all the hidden details of that war?"

—"Untiring never participated in that war; how could he recreate every single detail?"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said skeptically. Pausing, she suddenly scrutinized Jian Nuo from head to toe:—"Speaking of which, you're the young master of Sky City, aren't you? Are you taking part in this play as well?"

[We just said it isn't a play... even if it really is.]

Jian Nuo said helplessly:

—"Yes, I must participate. Not only myself, but even 'Sky City' will be involved."

[—"Could it be..."](# "Speaker: Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum")

—"Indeed. Sky City from back when it was still stationed in the Central Domain has been recreated as well,"-Jian Nuo sighed, pointing in a certain direction.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum followed his finger, but saw only the towering, massive battlements of White Jade Capital; she couldn't spot the Sky City of antiquity, which had been considerably smaller in volume back then. Yet she didn't doubt his words in the slightest—after all, if even White Jade Capital could be faithfully reconstructed, how could Sky City be impossible?

—"What astonishing ambition and scale!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum marveled.

With them going to such extraordinary lengths, refusing to play along would be rather ungracious.

—"Very well! If it's a theatrical reenactment, so be it. Come to think of it, the ancient talisman runes used by White Jade Capital to summon a True Immortal might genuinely be known to no one but myself! Since you two are currently inside White Jade Capital, you are my subordinates—no playing favorites with your ancestor!"-her final sentence was directed pointedly at Qingyang Qingyu.

Qingyang Qingyu felt not the slightest moral baggage regarding betraying her ancestor:

—"Of course not! I happen to be immensely curious about how one summons a True Immortal!"

—"Hmph. If one truly is summoned, don't soil yourselves in terror!"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum boasted.

Naturally, she was merely bragging to impress the juniors. Having yet to step into the Tribulation Transcendence realm, she hardly believed she actually had the capability to summon an authentic True Immortal.

***

Yet, regarding this very matter, there were those who believed it with absolute conviction:

—"I don't know whether the script Mo Xi wrote reflects the authentic reality of the God-Demon War back then, but judging by this situation, it might genuinely pry open the Celestial Gate 'over on that side,'"-Ran Deng said with deep worry, gazing at the Sundial Clock before her.

From the moment Mo Xi submitted his script, a blinding, radiant white light had emanated from the epicenter of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Sundial Clock, piercing straight through the clouds and surging toward the Immortal Realm.

Such an omen filled Ran Deng with unease. Yet Master and Second Senior Brother were both away; even Eldest Senior Brother was nowhere to be found. As the Eldest Senior Sister, she had no choice but to brace herself and hold the fort here alone.

In contrast, Mo Bei was exceptionally sanguine:

—"If an authentic True Immortal truly is summoned, wouldn't that be splendid? Those very 'True Immortals' who hold our destinies in their hands."

Hearing this, Ran Deng glanced at Mo Bei, then turned her gaze back toward the Sundial Clock, cold hatred welling in her eyes:

—"Ah. Indeed."
