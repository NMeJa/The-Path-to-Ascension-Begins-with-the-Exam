# Chapter 206: Return of the Dragon King (第206章 龙王归来)

> On the fifteenth day of the tenth month in the year 293 Before the New Immortal Calendar, the Finals of the 0th Immortal Arts Assembly formally commenced.
> 
> According to historical records, this assembly officially permitted only cultivators from the Central Domain (the central territory of the New Immortal Realm) to participate, hence its formal designation was the 'First Central Domain Immortal Arts Assembly.' However, this gathering had already seized the rapt attention of cultivators across all domains, and five years later, the authentic 'First Immortal Arts Assembly' was convened.
> 
> Nevertheless, the historical significance of the "0th Immortal Arts Assembly" is beyond question.
> 
> It was precisely due to the convening of this assembly that representatives of the Northern Domain's Human Race, the Southern Domain's Demon Race, the Western Domain's Ghost Race, and the Eastern Domain's Spirit Race (Note 1) all journeyed forth. There, they bore witness to the revolutionary superiority of standardized education, either forging alliances with the Heavenly Spirit Sect or importing its pedagogical frameworks, thereby laying the bedrock for the universalization of nine-year compulsory immortal education and the founding of the New Immortal Realm.
> 
> *(Note 1: Compared to the meticulous archives of the other races, the Spirit Race possesses no official records of dispatching delegates to the Central Domain. Only a handful of True Spirits, including Eastern Dragon King Dansha—who remains alive to this day—adamantly insist they personally represented the Spirit Race and crossed paths with Five-Domains Dragon Sovereign Lu Shulong, widely suspected by modern historians to have been an unsanctioned private escapade by supporters of the Dragon Sovereign.)*
> 
> —Excerpt from *History of the New Immortal Realm: Seventh Grade Mandatory Curriculum (Human Race Edition)*

> "If you ask how I felt back then: regret, boundless regret! Had I known what dragging my children to that Immortal Arts Assembly would bring upon my children and the offspring of every living creature under heaven, I would have been the first to execute the idiotic past version of myself."
> 
> —Excerpt from *Diary of a Certain Eastern Domain Dragon King*

***

The fifteenth day of the tenth month, the Xiayuan Festival.

On this day, every living soul throughout the Central Domain could witness a seven-colored aurora cascading from the heavens like gossamer silk, draping gently over the northeastern horizon of the Central Domain. Simultaneously, ceremonial salute cannons detonated like heavenly thunder, their deafening peals echoing across the entirety of the realm.

Hearing those distant cannon blasts, two traveling figures breathed a collective sigh of relief:

—"Is that the ceremonial salute for the opening of the Immortal Arts Assembly?"

—"Judging by that slightly damp, sputtering rumble, it must be. I reckon only the storehouses of the Heavenly Spirit Sect still keep those antique relics. Though I wonder who is presiding over the ceremony—to be so careless as to dig out such ancient junk from the bottom of the chest."

—"Regardless, we managed to catch it in time."

Overhearing snippets of their exchange, a voice suddenly hailed them:

—"Are you two also heading to attend the Immortal Arts Assembly?"

—"Sort of..."-one of the two figures, a young gentleman clad in immaculate white robes, raised his head. Yet upon getting a clear look at the "person" addressing them, he froze,-"A dragon?!"

—"Indeed, [we](# "Archaic royal pronoun: 吾 (wú) — I / We; sovereign dragon speech") are a dragon."

The entity claiming to be a dragon spoke with haughty grandeur.

It certainly looked every bit the part: a serpentine body gliding effortlessly through the dark, swirling mists of the Abyssal Ocean, its golden-crimson scales shimmering brilliantly. Most striking of all was its gossamer tail fin, undulating like a swimming fish fashioned of living cinnabar—graceful, ethereal, and sublime.

This dragon was clearly well aware of its own aesthetic appeal.

Tucking away its three-clawed talons, it descended from the clouds, circling the two figures below like drifting mist before declaring with immense self-satisfaction:

—"Mortal, [thou](# "Archaic second-person: 汝 (rǔ) — you / thou") must rarely have the fortune to lay eyes upon our majestic countenance! [Thou](# "Archaic second-person: 汝 (rǔ) — you / thou") art permitted to express thy reverence and adoration with greater frankness! We shall graciously overlook thy presumption."

The white-robed gentleman wore an expression of acute awkwardness:

—"That's hardly necessary. Actually, the one beside me—"

Before he could finish, he was yanked back sharply by the figure beside him.

That companion was enveloped in a heavy, voluminous azure cloak. The shape beneath appeared bizarre, hardly resembling a standard human frame; wrapped tightly from hood to hem, not only was the face invisible, but one couldn't even determine its true nature.

Tugging the white-robed gentleman behind him, the azure cloak addressed the red dragon:

—"Sounds like you're heading to the Immortal Arts Assembly as well?"

—"Indeed,"-the red dragon replied with mild boredom,-"The humans and demons have broadcast news of this Immortal Arts Assembly across every corner of the world. Rumors even claim an authentic True Dragon is competing in this tournament! We deem this largely a fabrication; how could an exalted True Dragon Sovereign possibly partake in mortals' petty games? We intended to expose their fraudulent lies, only to be trapped within these accursed black mists."

—"You've taken the wrong road. This is the deep interior of the Abyssal Ocean, a considerable distance from the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-the white-robed gentleman noted.

—"What?!"-the red dragon's eyes bulged wide.

[The deep interior of the Abyssal Ocean?!](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[No wonder we've been flying for over half a year without arriving! I told you you were flying in the wrong direction!](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[You kept insisting we were almost there! The ambient spiritual energy here is a complete vortex—even humans couldn't survive in a wretched dump like this!](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[Father's chronic directional blindness has flared up again.](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

A chaotic chorus of muffled complaints erupted directly from inside the red dragon's belly, sounding as though an entire menagerie of creatures was conversing within its stomach.

Irritated, the red dragon whipped its body violently, twisting itself like fried dough twist, prompting a cascade of dizzy squeals from within its abdomen.

Ignoring the mutiny inside, the red dragon pressed the white-robed youth:

—"Where is this Heavenly Spirit Sect?! How does one fly there?!"

—"Flying there from this location is rather convoluted,"-the white-robed youth furrowed his brows,-"The spiritual currents above the Abyssal Ocean are exceptionally volatile, pocked with several massive spiritual vortices. To bypass them, one must first navigate toward the relatively tranquil eye of the Abyssal Ocean, catch the northwestern turbulent crosswind, and circle the perimeter for roughly three thousand kilometers. From there, you should be able to spot the Ascension Stairway. However, do not fly directly toward the Ascension Stairway; the turbulent eddies will scramble your orientation. Instead, you must fly against the current, pressing forward five hundred kilometers through the spiritual gale. Once you spot white cloud layers, you will have cleared the Abyssal Ocean. Then, simply follow the white clouds, skirt around Cloud City, and you'll arrive at the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

Finishing his lengthy explanation in a single breath, the white-robed youth realized it sounded dreadfully complex. He looked worriedly at the red dragon:

—"Did you understand that?"

The red dragon stared back with its pupils completely uncoupled and out of focus, looking utterly dazed.

Yet subjected to the youth's inquiry, it hoisted its snout stubbornly:

—"Such a simple trajectory! How could we possibly fail to grasp it?!"

A mutinous voice promptly piped up from its stomach:

[If you understood, it would be a cosmic miracle.](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[We're doomed. We're going to spend the rest of our lives trapped here.](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[Trusting Lord Father was our greatest mistake.](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

—"Silence, all of you!"-the red dragon roared in humiliated fury.

It had actually descended to brawling with the voices inside its own belly.

Watching this farcical spectacle, the white-robed youth inquired with mild curiosity:

—"Seeing as you are a dragon, you must hail from the Eastern Domain, correct? Did the Northern Domain's grand treasure ships not pass through your territory? I assumed Eastern Domain cultivators could hitch a ride aboard the fleet."

—"Sky City's ships? They set sail far too late; we couldn't be bothered to wait,"-the red dragon lifted its chin haughtily.

—"So... you departed before the treasure ships even arrived?"

—"..."-The red dragon stiffened, its scales flushing crimson with indignant shame,-"H-How could our noble dragon clan possibly deign to ride human conveyances?!"

It appeared this dragon had been lost in the Abyssal Ocean for at least ten days to half a month—if not far longer.

The white-robed youth rather regretted his nosiness. Judging by the oppressive draconic pressure radiating from the red dragon, the beast looked primed to silence witnesses to preserve its dignity, obliterating the unlucky onlookers who had caught it being hopelessly lost.

At this juncture, the azure cloak stepped forward, addressing the irritated red dragon:

—"Cease your fussing, Dansha. Since you're lost yet again, why don't you let us guide you to the Immortal Arts Assembly?"

—"Thou art willing to guide our path? Is this offered as thy humble penance?"-hearing this, the red dragon's temper softened considerably, asking with haughty condescension.

A flurry of tiny whispers resounded from its belly. Whatever was said, the red dragon reacted a beat too late, glaring suspiciously at the azure cloak:

—"How dost thou know our name?!"

The white-robed youth also glanced at the azure cloak, equally curious for the answer.

—"I have heard of it,"-the azure cloak's response was as good as noncommittal.

Yet the red dragon was astonishingly easy to appease; such a vague reply was more than enough to stroke its vanity:

—"Heard of it? Hahaha! Indeed! The illustrious name of Dragon Prince Dansha—even mere mortals ought to have heard it whispered with awe!"

[This fellow is actually a dragon prince?! Though his wits seem rather deficient. I assumed all dragons were as cunning as a certain someone...](# "Jian Nuo's internal thought")

The white-robed youth dared not look sideways, murmuring critiques strictly in his mind.

Meanwhile, the azure cloak had already grasped the youth's arm, leaping lithely onto the crown of the red dragon's head, right between its two horns.

The white-robed youth had assumed that given the dragon's haughty (yet dim-witted) disposition, it would erupt in murderous fury over such an insolent intrusion. Yet curiously, the dragon displayed an instinctive subservience toward the azure cloak's actions, offering not a whisper of complaint.

The azure cloak pointed downward toward the abyssal depths:

—"Down."

—"Down?"

—"You are a dragon; traveling through the Abyssal Ocean is vastly swifter,"-the azure cloak stated as though it were the most natural truth under heaven.

[Is this truly wise?! If the airspace above the Abyssal Ocean is perilous, the depths below are infinitely more terrifying! Normal cultivators avoid submerging into the Abyssal Ocean at all costs!](# "Jian Nuo's internal panic")

The white-robed youth panicked, itching to intervene.

Yet whether this red dragon possessed zero survival instincts regarding the Abyssal Ocean or genuinely harbored zero suspicion toward the azure cloak, it chirped with boundless enthusiasm:

—"Understood!"

Without a second's hesitation, it plunged headfirst straight into the pitch-black waters of the Abyssal Ocean!

***

Fortunately, the azure cloak's navigation proved immaculate.

Or rather, the innate power of the dragon clan truly lived up to its legendary reputation; navigating the Abyssal Ocean proved effortless for the creature, even substantially faster than flight.

In merely half a day, the red dragon and its passengers tore through the abyssal depths, arriving at the outer maritime perimeter of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

To celebrate the convening of the Immortal Arts Assembly, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's undersea port had clearly undergone an opulent transformation—even more dazzling than terrestrial festivities. Through the ink-black oceanic depths, one could spot the luminous port standing like a colossal mountain of light from leagues away. Thousands of siphonophore tube jellies formed endless bioluminescent light ribbons stretching across thousands of kilometers; jellyfish of every conceivable shape and radiant hue were tethered across the harbor like festive lanterns, illuminating the deep sea in iridescent splendor.

Even for a dragon accustomed to the opulent Crystal Palaces of the Eastern Domain, the spectacle left it slack-jawed in sheer awe.

—"...Passable. Though naturally a step below our Crystal Palace,"-Red Dragon Dansha appraised with feigned disdain.

Internally, however, it was already scheming how to rear a swarm of siphonophores to decorate its own royal palace back home.

After all, even dragon palaces rarely featured architecture like the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Firstly, dragons vastly preferred shallow coral seas teeming with vibrant colors, biodiversity, and aesthetic flora; deep-sea creatures were notoriously careless in their physical appearance. Secondly, dragon pearls possessed extraordinary illumination power, bathing entire territorial seas in perpetual daylight; augmented by underwater night-pearls, dragon palaces were blindingly bright day and night, leaving little reason to appreciate bioluminescent jellyfish.

Yet lights flickering in the pitch-black abyss offered an entirely different breed of breathtaking splendor.

Having admired the scenery of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's sea harbor, Dansha puffed up with renewed pride:

—"See! It wasn't far at all! Clearly, we never lost our way!"

The azure cloak offered zero commentary on this boast.

Voices from Dansha's stomach, however, piped up with indignant scorn:

[Is that so? Yet we lingered in that wretched foggy void for over half a year!](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[Lord Father, have you forgotten how you once got hopelessly lost right outside our front gate for several centuries?](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[Had we known, we should have boarded the humans' treasure ship!](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

[Exactly! Without the guidance of these two benefactors, the assembly would have concluded before we ever escaped!](# "Dragon child's muffled voice")

—"Silence!"-Dansha flared with humiliated rage once more,-"Who could have known that place was the Abyssal Ocean?! It lacked the demonic miasma of legend, filled with nothing but black stormclouds! It was entirely forgivable that we misjudged the route!"

It began twisting its massive serpentine body again in frantic agitation.

The passengers perched atop Dansha's head could hardly endure the turbulence.

The white-robed gentleman pleaded:

—"Please refrain from thrashing here; you'll easily collide with other vessels!"

This was no exaggeration.

As they drew closer to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the waters were not only adorned with sprawling jellyfish light ribbons, but packed with private sub-aquatic vessels operated by human cultivators, prompting the Heavenly Spirit Sect to formally codify the *Undersea Maritime Navigation Regulations*.

Speaking of which, credit belonged partly to Villa Master Qingyang.

It was precisely because Villa Master Qingyang had constructed a private laboratory—cave abode—near the harbor for Qu Qianxing that neighboring cultivators had caught the research bug, constantly wanting to borrow it to forge artifacts, test experimental spells, and draft academic theses. Yet borrowing was chronically inconvenient, and cultivators perpetually fretted over proprietary trade secrets leaking; thus, numerous titans had simply followed suit, erecting their own sub-aquatic research lodges.

This dynamic had triggered an explosive real estate boom surrounding the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Whether on terrestrial peaks or beneath the waves.

Had the Untiring Immortal Venerable not strictly banned speculative real estate flipping, Mo Xi felt he could have become the wealthiest tycoon in the cultivation realm purely by trading property deeds.

Now, after more than a year of explosive growth, the undersea perimeter was no longer a lonely transit dock, but had matured into a bustling submarine township—and judging by its trajectory, evolving into a colossal undersea immortal metropolis was merely a matter of time.

Particularly today, the grand opening day of the Immortal Arts Assembly. Even with the Kunpeng shuttles departed, the harbor was packed to the gills with excited crowds freshly emerging from the opening ceremonies:

—"Today's opening ceremony was magnificent! Those performances were utterly sublime!"

—"The best part was zero speeches from long-winded elders!"

—"That's not good at all! I came specifically out of reverence for the Untiring Immortal Venerable, yet didn't get to catch a single glimpse of the esteemed elder!"

—"Still, the exhibition matches were breathtakingly cool!"

—"Naturally! They were contested by the paramount titans of every major sect! Originally I came purely to watch Mahayana titans clash, but who knew virtually every renowned powerhouse in the realm would arrive and stage an exhibition duel! As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable—such staggering prestige and peerless connections!"

—"Even setting aside the assembly tournament, watching the exhibition matches alone was worth the admission price ten times over!"

—"Who was the idiot who claimed the Untiring Immortal Venerable was obscure? The entire cultivation realm bends to his summons!"

—"Not necessarily; there are still the Heavenly Dao Alliance and the Palace of Carefree Wandering..."

—"Do you truly believe the Heavenly Dao Alliance could match today's grand spectacle? Though Carefree Palace might warrant observation."

—"Heh, your news is thoroughly outdated; you have no idea what happened to Carefree Palace..."

...

—"Regardless of those lofty titans, whose duels were far too profound for me to parse anyway, the sword cultivators fielded by the Heavenly Spirit Sect were drop-dead gorgeous!"

—"I had no idea their sword cultivation was so formidable!"

—"Wait, what do you mean by that?! The Untiring Immortal Venerable started out as a sword cultivator!"

—"Rubbish! He was originally a spell cultivator!"

—"How did I hear that he originated as a ghost cultivator from our Western Domain?"

—"Why do you people argue over what the Untiring Immortal Venerable practiced first every single time?! Didn't you notice he is fundamentally an artifact refiner?! Otherwise, how could his disciple possess a Dao Bone?!"

...

—"That magic illusion drama was a first for me; every single contestant looked so dashing inside the projection!"

—"Your news is hopelessly lagging; the Heavenly Spirit Sect has been using this exact format to advertise Immortals' Chess for ages! Though recent productions are vastly more intricate. I heard that following the tournament, they plan to release the *Immortal Arts Assembly Complete Combat Highlights Archive*, alongside specialized compilation reels for individual star athletes!"

—"Damn it all! Why was the Immortal Arts Assembly restricted solely to the Central Domain?!"

—"I want to feature in such dashing illusion broadcasts too!"

—"You'd better figure out how to purchase those illusion jade slips first! I heard pre-orders are already completely sold out!"

—"What?! Already sold out... wait, pre-orders have already opened?!"

...

Enthusiastic chatter reverberated across the seafloor; the serene abyss had never experienced such raucous jubilation.

People were so consumed by debating the ceremonies that virtually no one noticed a "rare" red dragon swimming past their vicinity.

Naturally, these discussions drifted straight into Dansha's ears.

Although seawater could cause acoustic distortions, parsing human speech presented zero difficulty to a marine dragon. Yet to the proud red dragon, these conversations grated excruciatingly upon its nerves.

It began thrashing its body with violent abandon:

—"Curse it all! What opening ceremony?! Why did we miss it?!"

[Isn't it because you got hopelessly lost?!](# "Jian Nuo's unspoken retort")

The white-robed gentleman sorely wanted to blurt this out aloud, yet clinging desperately to the dragon's horns for dear life consumed all his energy, leaving zero room for backtalk.

And the commotion caused by a dragon was no laughing matter. Although Dansha had not deliberately attacked anyone, nor had its thrashing frame struck a single vessel, the colossal waves generated by its writhing tail whipped up massive undersea whirlpools, causing nearby civilian submersibles to spin wildly out of control!

The panicked crowds finally ceased their chatter, crying out in alarm:

—"What's happening?! A tsunami?!"

—"How can there be a tsunami at the bottom of the sea?!"

—"Look over there! What is that?!"

—"A dragon?! There's a dragon?!"

—"Doesn't the Heavenly Spirit Sect have a disciple who is a dragon? Could it be him?!"

Amidst the rising panic, a booming sector-wide public address broadcast resounded across the seabed:

> **"Attention, red dragon! Attention, red dragon! Please observe your navigation corridor! Do not disrupt undersea maritime traffic! Do not disrupt undersea maritime traffic!"**

Had this been any guest who had resided in the Heavenly Spirit Sect for any length of time—even a Mahayana powerhouse, nay, even a Tribulation Transcendence titan—no one would dare blatantly flout this warning. Unfortunately, this red dragon was accustomed to acting lawless and tyrannical across the Eastern Domain's seas; hearing the warning, it only thrashed with greater obstinacy.

Roused by the blaring broadcast, the azure cloak finally stirred.

The azure cloak had appeared thoroughly exhausted throughout the voyage, relying on sheer willpower to guide the path. The moment he had ensured Dansha was on the correct heading and spotted the distant luminescence of the port, he had collapsed into slumber like a puppet whose batteries had run dry, missing the opportunity to restrain the red dragon in time. Had the broadcast not chimed, he likely wouldn't have awakened until disaster struck.

Yet by now, the situation was already beyond salvaging.

—"Dansha...!"

Hearing the broadcast, the azure cloak furrowed his brows, preparing to restrain the beast. Yet before he could act, an unforgiving blade of sword qi descended from above, slicing cleanly through the seawater and severing a long strand of dragon whisker!

The red dragon finally halted its thrashing. Yet it was instantly incensed, radiating a terrifying wave of draconic pressure.

Glaring furiously toward the origin of the sword strike, it roared:

—"Who dares?!"

—"Fifth Senior Brother?"

The owner of the sword qi let out a startled gasp.

Hearing this, the red dragon flared with even greater outrage:

—"Who is thy Fifth Senior Brother?! Cease thy clumsy familial fabrications! Even so, we shall never forgive—"

*Whoosh—!*

Another blistering crescent of sword light cleaved the deep!

Opening its maw, the red dragon spewed a torrential blast of dragon breath in defense. To its utter astonishment, the sword qi severed the dragon breath clean in two, instantly freezing its snout and nostrils before crashing straight toward the crown of its skull!

For a moment, the red dragon found itself utterly cornered.

Fortunately, while the sword qi appeared relentlessly razor-sharp, it held deliberate restraint; striking the dragon's skull, it merely sheared away the heavy cloak enveloping the figure seated between the dragon horns.

As the shredded azure cloak drifted into the oceanic currents, a colossal, transcendent draconic aura descended upon the entire oceanic expanse!

Instantly, countless cultivators collapsed to their knees in uncontrollable awe beneath that suffocating draconic presence, their bodies curling inward. Even the handful of high-tier powerhouses capable of withstanding the pressure could not bear to gaze upon the figure directly, averting their eyes in veneration.

Even so, that single fleeting glimpse etched an indelible, lifelong imprint upon their souls:

It was an unimaginably sublime celestial entity.

Not a 'he,' but an exalted 'Deity.'

A divine dragon suspended midway through mortal transformation: bearing silver-white dragon horns upon his brow, iridescent silver-blue scales shimmering across his cheekbones, and an enormous serpentine dragon tail swaying majestically with the oceanic currents. Such hybrid traits detracted not an iota from his sacred elegance and breathtaking beauty; on the contrary, dropping to one's knees in worship felt like the sole natural response—anything less felt like a blasphemous sacrilege.

Even the haughty red dragon beneath his feet began shivering violently:

—"True Dragon... Your Majesty?!"

Beneath such absolute draconic dominance, it was impossible for the sword wielder to remain unaffected. Yet possessing formidable cultivation resistance, she managed to master her trembling limbs, swimming forward through the currents:

—"Fifth Senior Brother? How did you end up looking like this?"

—She had uncharacteristically used honorifics, clearly shaken by the aura.

—"There are profound reasons..."-the azure cloak—no, Lu Shulong—answered with acute embarrassment. Clutching the shredded remnants of his cloak around his shoulders, he attempted to muffle the terrifying draconic pressure, though with the fabric hacked to ribbons, the effect was marginal at best.

—"Let us return to the sect before discussing this!"-Lu Shulong sighed helplessly.

Glancing at the white-robed companion beside him, Lu Shulong instructed:

—"Young Master Jian, I leave this dragon 'guest' in your capable hands for now. Please do not be bashful; 'entertain' him thoroughly."

—"Eh?"-Jian Nuo blinked in surprise, eyeing the dim-witted red dragon with distinct reluctance.

After all, once separated from Lu Shulong, Jian Nuo doubted this dragon would obey a single word from him.

Perceiving his hesitation, Lu Shulong sweet-talked the deal:

—"Villa Master Qingyang maintains a temporary cave abode nearby. You may head there first; perhaps you might even establish communication with Senior Sister Qingyang Qingyu?"

—"Splendid! Leave it entirely to me!"-Jian Nuo agreed with exuberant delight.

Lu Shulong nodded in satisfaction. Ignoring the trembling red dragon that seemed desperate to stammer something to him, he guided Mo Bei away from the gathered spectators.

***

—"Fifth Senior Brother, what in the world happened?"

Having arrived at a secluded enclave, Mo Bei inquired for the second time.

—"Many things transpired. By the way... Master isn't at the Heavenly Spirit Sect right now, is he?"-Lu Shulong glanced nervously left and right, verifying with Mo Bei in a hushed whisper,-"Do not tell him I have returned yet! I have an extraordinarily detailed explanation..."

—"Keep those explanations buried deep in your heart! You don't need to tell me a single word!"

Mo Bei instantly leaped backward more than a dozen paces.

The very maiden who had been curiously probing Lu Shulong moments prior recoiled violently upon hearing the taboo phrase "do not tell Master," losing every shred of desire to learn anything.

She even bitterly regretted swimming down to the sea harbor to maintain public order.

In truth, Mo Bei had bolted down to the port precisely to evade the grueling post-ceremony social greeting obligations—she detested superficial pleasantries just as much as Ran Deng did. But the one sentenced to punishment was Ran Deng, not her; so Mo Bei had shamelessly slipped away.

In reality, had it not been for competing in the Immortal Arts Assembly, learning that Master was away meant Mo Bei would have never dreamed of returning from the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

And the fact that Lu Shulong had dragged his bizarre, half-transformed frame back was unquestionably because the cursed Immortal Arts Assembly could not be skipped.

—Lu Shulong had been caught red-handed by Master for retaining past-cycle memories while slacking off, sentenced to compulsory participation in the Immortal Arts Assembly. If he dared skip the tournament without authorization, the fate awaiting him was being hung from the peach tree alongside Eldest Senior Brother.

Lu Shulong had no desire to share that fate, which was why he had rushed back on the exact day of the opening ceremony. Yet having done something inexplicable to end up in this half-dragon state, he dared not let Master discover his condition, sneaking about like a burglar trying to slip back into his immortal cave first.

Mo Bei's stance was clear: *When you lot pull suicidal stunts, do not drag me down with you.*

Normally, Lu Shulong would never deliberately provoke his junior sister.

Lu Shulong initially smiled, preparing to let her off the hook. Yet as his gaze swept across Mo Bei, he stiffened momentarily. Suddenly lunging forward, he slung an arm around Mo Bei's shoulders, leaning in to whisper directly into her ear:

—"I'm afraid that won't do at all. Because Second Senior Brother has become the Demon Sovereign—and is currently leading fifty million demon troops, preparing to march upon the Heavenly Realm!"
