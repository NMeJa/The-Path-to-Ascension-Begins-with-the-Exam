# Chapter 116: [Coming Fully Prepared](<# "Literal CN: 有备而来 (yǒu bèi ér lái) — Coming Prepared / Arriving Fully Prepared; chapter title referring to Zhou Hui and Heavenly Spirit Sect's comprehensive infrastructure for harvesting heavenly tribulations and spiritual rain downpours">) (第116章 有备而来)

—"Huh, it can actually flee?"-Zhou Hui muttered in midair, his eyes going wide.

Having undergone tribulations so many times, this was the very first time he had ever witnessed such an absurd spectacle.

Then again, this was also the first time he had ever prepared to capture the heavenly lightning in its entirety.

Under normal circumstances, every time Zhou Hui underwent a lightning tribulation, he acted much like any ordinary cultivator, pondering how to withstand the lightning's destructive might and open a stable ascension corridor. Even when he took action to capture heavenly lightning, it was merely to analyze its power and classifications; he had never once attempted to harvest every single bolt of heavenly lightning down to the last spark.

Thus, this was the first time Zhou Hui had ever encountered a situation where heavenly lightning "escaped."

[What principle does it operate on to flee? Does heavenly lightning possess consciousness? Or is 'someone' remotely controlling it? Where is it trying to escape to? The True Immortal Realm? Or an even higher plane?]

In an instant, all manner of questions and theories surged through Zhou Hui's mind.

Yet these ruminations did not delay Zhou Hui's hands in the slightest.

Controlling his "net of light," he deliberately slackened his pace slightly, tailing behind the thundercloud at a steady speed. During its ascent, the brilliance of the light net subtly dispersed in all directions, causing the "arrow of light" formed by the net to dim, appearing as though it might vanish at any second.

Yet the thundercloud did not slow down because of this. Like water in a bathtub once the drain plug was pulled, it surged toward a specific direction in the sky.

Zhou Hui's divine sense swept toward that direction ahead of the thundercloud.

Within the void, he located a spatial rift.

—"Could it lead to the True Immortal Realm...?"-Zhou Hui murmured in disbelief.

Across several past reincarnation cycles, and even in the early stages of this present lifetime, he had constantly sought the true coordinates of the True Immortal Realm, intending to tear open an ascension corridor by his own strength, yet had never succeeded.

And now, a spatial rift that potentially led to the True Immortal Realm was exposed just like that?

Zhou Hui split off a portion of his divine sense to probe the interior of the passage.

At this moment, the thundercloud also surged near the spatial rift.

Had this been in the past, Zhou Hui would certainly have seized on this final thundercloud to rip open the spatial rift and blaze a trail to the "True Immortal Realm." But at this very instant, an extraordinary glint flickered across Zhou Hui's eyes.

In a flash, the seemingly fading light net abruptly accelerated in its ascent, overtook the thundercloud, wrapped it up tight, and dragged it downward!

The fifth orb of lightning formed just like that.

And this appeared to be the final portion of the lightning tribulation.

Following the capture of this last wisp of thundercloud, the spatial rift in the void collapsed in an instant. The entire sky looked as though washed clean by water. In particular, the massive upside-down reflection of the Heavenly Spirit Mountain range suspended above the sect became crystal clear—every blade of grass and tree vividly discernible, making it virtually impossible to distinguish top from bottom, like two sides of a mirror.

Zhou Hui stood right in the center of this mirror-like expanse, gazing pensively upward.

Witnessing this scene, the cultivators inside the Palace of No Words poked their heads out one after another:

[—"Is the lightning tribulation over?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"What an absolute eye-opener. I never imagined someone could cross a tribulation in such a manner."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Does this count as a successful tribulation? The Untiring Immortal Venerable hasn't ascended either!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"All the heavenly thunder was confiscated—how could he ascend? Rather than calling it a success or a failure, the Untiring Immortal Venerable gave up on ascending this time of his own accord!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Given the Heavenly Spirit Sect's current state, it truly isn't suitable for ascension right now."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"...What a pity."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[If it were me, could I ever bring myself to give it up?]

Even if one's sect is important, ascending is far more important, isn't it?

How foolish. This lightning tribulation "only" had five strikes—it wasn't particularly fierce. Next time, he might never get such an opportunity again.

The cultivators pondered with all manner of mixed feelings.

It was just that being on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's territory, it was inappropriate to voice their thoughts aloud.

Still, others discussed among themselves:

[—"Why is the Immortal Venerable still up there?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Is there something special present?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

As they conversed, they involuntarily drifted toward the open-air aerial balcony outside the academic hall, wishing to observe more closely.

Manor Lord Qingyang also "looked" toward the sky with curiosity—not looking with physical eyes, but sweeping his divine sense upward to check for anomalies.

Yet the instant his divine sense reached a certain altitude, his body shuddered violently as if struck by lightning!

—"Don't go out!"-Manor Lord Qingyang barked in a booming roar.

At his shout, the crowd turned back to look at him in bewilderment.

Yet before anyone could open their mouth to ask, a deafening explosion seemingly capable of tearing the entire Central Domain apart detonated behind them, accompanied by a blinding flash of electric glare!

The crowd whipped their heads around in terror, only to see a brilliant sea of white lightning above—so intense that even the Untiring Immortal Venerable's figure had vanished entirely inside that dazzling radiance.

—"Th-This is a lightning tribulation?!"-someone cried out in horror.

Though he knew full well that if this expanse of light fell in its entirety, the Heavenly Spirit Sect would cease to exist, he still could not resist retreating several paces deeper into the Palace of No Words, as though standing where the sky was out of sight could shield him from harm.

Yet this hydraulic-press-like heavenly thunder, even without striking the earth, could not fail to make its presence felt.

Under the illumination of that blazing glare, the entire Palace of No Words appeared in stark monochrome. Human eyes could no longer distinguish color, and the hair all over the cultivators' bodies stood straight up under the uncontrolled electrostatic charge.

The only thing baffling them was that after waiting several minutes, this horrifying heavenly thunder never actually struck down.

Naturally, the cultivators did not want the heavenly thunder to descend. But with such terrifying lightning hanging precariously overhead, neither falling nor dispersing, everyone felt like prisoners with their heads clamped onto an execution block—dreading the fall of the guillotine blade, yet wishing for a swift and merciful end to the suspense.

[—"Why hasn't the heavenly thunder struck down yet?"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

[—"Has it been blocked by the Untiring Immortal Venerable?"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

[—"The Immortal Venerable can actually block heavenly thunder like that?!"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

[—"How could you presume to fathom the might of a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse? Besides, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation must have activated as well!"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

[—"Right! The Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation!"](<# "Speaker: Panicked Cultivator">)

One cultivator's eyes lit up; he felt he had found a way to verify "how close the guillotine blade hung above their necks."

Glancing around the Palace of No Words, he called out:

—"Where is Little Friend Qu? You should know the status of the Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation, right?"

At his words, the other cultivators showed enlightened expressions and scanned their surroundings to search for Qu Qianxing's figure.

Normally, a peerless beauty like Qu Qianxing would be the most conspicuous center of attention wherever he stood. But under this seemingly world-ending heavenly tribulation, the entire Palace of No Words resembled a stark black-and-white paper cutout silhouette, and people temporarily failed to spot him.

—"He didn't run away, did he?"-a cultivator grumbled in displeasure.

Even high-rank cultivators could hardly maintain a calm composure beneath such an apocalyptic tribulation. That they hadn't erupted into screaming profanities was already the limit of their sanity. If they saw the "person in charge of the Heavenly Spirit Sect" flee, their mental state would surely shatter like an icy glass bottle suddenly doused in boiling water.

It was Manor Lord Qingyang who released his divine sense, swept it across the Palace of No Words, and remarked with a sidelong glance:

—"Isn't he right over there?"

Following Manor Lord Qingyang's pointing direction, everyone saw Qu Qianxing sitting cross-legged right by the doorway closest to the balcony, eyes tightly shut, evidently immersed in deep meditation!

Not only him—Qingyang Qingyu was also sitting cross-legged not far from Qu Qianxing. She must have noticed Qu Qianxing's actions and entered meditative trance along with him.

Manor Lord Qingyang thought with emotional warmth:

[What a match made in heaven—like a golden boy and jade maiden!]

It was quite impressive that even amidst this apocalyptic doomsday scene, he could still manage to [spoon-feed himself sweet shipper candy](<# "Literal CN: 自己找糖吃 (zìjǐ zhǎo táng chī) — finding sugar/candy for oneself; modern fandom slang for seeking out sweet romantic shipping moments between characters">).

—"Why is he meditating in a place like this?"-someone questioned with a bizarre look, stepping forward to "wake" Qu Qianxing.

Seeing this, Manor Lord Qingyang glared at the man:

—"Did the lightning fry your brains? Even two children understand that cultivating at a time like this offers immense benefits, yet you haven't realized it in the slightest?!"

[Immense benefits?] The heavenly thunder is right above our heads! Who has the nerve to cultivate?!

The man wanted to retort.

Yet another person gasped in sudden epiphany:

—"Come to think of it, that's completely true! This Palace of No Words is constructed from Ascension Terrace materials, and Thousand-Species Jade doesn't just assist in defense—it records insights into lightning tribulations and tunes spiritual energy into the optimal frequency for comprehending the Heavenly Dao!"

Hearing those words, many cultivators' expressions stirred. With a rustle, a massive swathe of them sat right down.

Being high-rank cultivators, they knew how to do the math:

Nobody knew whether this heavenly lightning would strike down or not.

If it struck down, everyone would be wiped out together; complaining would accomplish nothing.

If it didn't strike down, what were they waiting for if not seizing the opportunity to cultivate?

Were they at the Integration stage? The Mahayana stage? Were they never going to face a tribulation in their lives?

With such a rare opportunity, were they really going to just stand around gawking?

Moments later, virtually every cultivator in the Palace of No Words had entered a meditative state.

In fact, only Manor Lord Qingyang looked around in utter bewilderment and let out a disdainful scoff:

—"Tsk."

Nothing could be done about it—Manor Lord Qingyang was already at the Mahayana stage, and his clan would never ascend, nor were they willing to enter the Tribulation Transcendence stage. Thus, in this situation, he was the sole person who had no need to cultivate.

...Why was he the only one who didn't need to cultivate?

Manor Lord Qingyang glanced left and right, and tentatively sat down as well.

Withdrawing his divine sense completely, he perceived the terrifying heavenly tribulation through the protective barrier of the Palace of No Words. This time, he clearly sensed that while the heavenly thunder was colossal and cataclysmic, it was also controlled, utterly incapable of harming him.

Was this the effect of Thousand-Species Jade?

No—beneath it all, he sensed an even vaster, tranquil power cradling this heavenly thunder, holding it completely in check.

That power was so deep-rooted and flourishing with life that even heavenly thunder could do nothing against it.

As Manor Lord Qingyang perceived this spectacle, he faintly felt as though he had caught a glimpse of the "Dao."

Ever since Baiyujing disappeared, research into True Immortals had ground to a halt. After Eternal Villa's cultivation techniques reached the Mahayana stage, there was nowhere left to advance; they could only remain frozen in place, refusing to ascend yet not knowing what else they could possibly accomplish. Yet at this very moment, he unexpectedly sensed his own future path.

The "Dao" of the descendants of Baiyujing.

Manor Lord Qingyang hurriedly banished his last stray thoughts and immersed himself in comprehension.

***

In the midst of meditation, one's perception of time warped.

The cultivators could not say how much time had elapsed—it felt like ages, yet also like a mere fleeting moment. All they knew was that the heavenly thunder never struck down in the end.

Nor, however, was it captured.

If Manor Lord Qingyang—the closest onlooker to the heavenly thunder aside from the man himself—were to describe it, he felt that bolt of heavenly thunder had been [kneaded and rolled like dough](<# "Literal CN: 盘 (pán) — kneaded / played with / rolled around in one's hands; slang for obsessively rubbing, polishing, or playing with walnuts/beads until smooth">).

Kneaded into a ball.

Of course, given the sheer volume of this heavenly thunder, even kneaded into a ball, it remained an extraordinarily colossal sphere—one that, if dropped, could instantly blast a second abyss crater into the Central Domain.

Perhaps for this very reason, the Untiring Immortal Venerable did not capture this "lightning ball." Instead, he used the Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation to weave a soft yet immensely elastic net.

When that lightning ball pressed the net down to its absolute limit, nearly touching the roof of the Palace of No Words, the net also reached its maximum tension. Stretched to the breaking point, it snapped violently upward—

The lightning ball bounced upward with even greater speed and momentum than when it descended, like a pellet launched from a slingshot! It even pierced straight through the inverted reflection above the Heavenly Spirit Sect, soaring into the boundless void.

Manor Lord Qingyang's perception ended right there.

Although his divine sense consciously sought to pursue it, the Untiring Immortal Venerable restrained him, gently yet firmly encasing a portion of Manor Lord Qingyang's divine sense—a harmless restriction, much like cupping hands over Manor Lord Qingyang's ears.

And fortunate it was that Zhou Hui did so, for Manor Lord Qingyang was spared hearing a bloodcurdling shriek.

A shriek containing power that even a Mahayana cultivator could not endure.

Had Manor Lord Qingyang heard it, he would surely have dissolved into a pool of foul blood on the spot.

Yet accompanying that shriek, the spatial rift in the sky that had seemingly closed with the capture of the thunderclouds opened once more, and a torrent of things came tumbling down in a heap.

That was... demonic creatures and demonic qi.

Contrary to what Zhou Hui had envisioned, the origin of that heavenly thunder was not the True Immortal Realm, but seemingly the Demon Realm!

Was this a trap?

Or some other unfathomable reason?

Even the Untiring Immortal Venerable, gazing at this spectacle, could reach no immediate conclusion. The only certainty was that had he not captured the thundercloud earlier and instead "ascended" along with this heavenly tribulation, ghost only knew what forsaken plane he would have ascended to.

In truth, even now, with these demonic creatures and demonic qi clattering down, had they fallen upon the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they would surely have contaminated the Heavenly Spirit Mountain region into a catastrophe akin to the Demon God ruins in the depths of the Abyss Sea.

Fortunately, the "lightning ball" was there.

Heavenly thunder was the natural bane of demonic entities, possessing the ultimate purifying effect against them. It was also the only force Zhou Hui knew of capable of transmuting irredeemable demonic qi into pure spiritual energy.

That bolt of heavenly thunder—originally meant to punish Zhou Hui, only to be kneaded into a ball—not only blasted open the spatial rift in a clean [home run](<# "Literal CN: 本垒打 (běnlěidǎ) — home run; modern baseball metaphor for hitting it out of the park">), but also detonated completely upon reaching the other side! As a result, the entire spatial rift crackled with flashing heavenly lightning, and the cascading demonic corpses and demonic qi were purified layer by layer amidst the lightning.

By the time they drifted into the airspace above the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they were so thoroughly purified that not a shred of their original taint remained, completely transformed into a shower of spiritual rain.

The torrential, bucket-dumping kind.

Standing amidst this downpour of spiritual energy, Zhou Hui's silver hair drifted loosely, growing even longer and more silvery-white than before—almost like Rapunzel's hair. This, however, was also a sign that Zhou Hui's spiritual energy reservoir had expanded by another major increment.

Yet Zhou Hui hardly cared about the growth of his spiritual energy. He waited and waited, but saw no sign of any subsequent heavenly lightning.

Instead, the spatial rift gradually knit closed once more.

This time, it sealed shut for real, closing with extreme speed without leaving the slightest trace—seemingly terrified that Zhou Hui might chuck something else up through the crack, or even climb up in person.

With this, Zhou Hui's lightning tribulation came to a completely safe and peaceful conclusion.

—"...'Only' six strikes?"-Zhou Hui muttered in dissatisfaction at the sight.

In the cultivation world, "six" was a special number. While a Sixfold Heavenly Tribulation was an auspicious number that granted tremendous growth upon completion, it also signified that this lightning tribulation ranked among the most formidable in existence.

Only Zhou Hui would feel dissatisfied.

Then again, for the ultimate [grindset arch-king](<# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — king of involution / hyper-competitive overachiever">), he had never seen a heavenly tribulation with anything other than "nine" strikes before!

Even during his first, near-successful lifetime, there were nine strikes of heavenly thunder. Nine was the ultimate extreme number; even for typical Tribulation Transcendence cultivators, Ninefold Heavenly Thunder was extraordinarily perilous, hence designated as an "Immortal Tribulation."

Zhou Hui, however, had started his cultivation career with an "Immortal Tribulation," and from his second life onward, each cycle had grown increasingly absurd.

The most catastrophic was his fifth lifetime, where hundreds of millions of bolts of annihilation thunder descended simultaneously, nearly ripping the entire world and Zhou Hui apart in a single breath, without even granting Zhou Hui a split second to cast a spell.

Having experienced such preposterous tribulations, Zhou Hui found himself almost unaccustomed to a tribulation with "only" six strikes.

Yet that lack of familiarity was unique to Zhou Hui alone.

The cultivators inside the Palace of No Words felt as though they had narrowly cheated death.

Furthermore, for them, the matter was far from over here.

Although the heavenly thunder had been thoroughly neutralized by the Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable through unknown means, before their complexions could recover, the subsequent torrential spiritual rain caused their faces to turn pale and ashen all over again.

Granted, spiritual rain was a marvelous boon.

If it were a gentle drizzle akin to February spring rain, it would be ideal for cultivation—even Integration stage powerhouses could not resist meditating continuously in such rain. But this was a torrential downpour!

Excessive spiritual energy would cause terrestrial flora and fauna to overgrow wildly, even breeding demonic beasts and monsters.

Looking at the broader picture, the Central Domain's spiritual energy environment was already in chaotic turmoil, with spiritual energy storms raging rampantly. If this deluge poured unchecked, the surplus spiritual energy would inevitably spill over into surrounding territories, plunging the entire Central Domain into devastating spiritual storms!

—"No good, we cannot let this 'rain' keep falling!"

Manor Lord Qingyang urgently broke out of his enlightenment state. Snapping his eyes open, he declared:

—"We must halt this downpour!"

[—"Right! Who has spirit-gathering dharma treasures? Fly up and harvest the spiritual energy first!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Who has faster flight speed? Rush to the major immortal cities and alert them to make preparations!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Where are the Daoists proficient in Earth and Wind arts? We must guide the spiritual energy to disperse into the interior!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

The cultivators sprang into action in a flurry.

This time, they had no intention of continuing to lean on the Untiring Immortal Venerable. After all, by conventional wisdom, a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator must enter secluded cultivation for a period after surviving a tribulation to stabilize their cultivation base. While the Untiring Immortal Venerable's method of crossing tribulations was a bit... unconventional, presumably he was no exception.

Since the Untiring Immortal Venerable had already shielded everyone from the most terrifying lightning tribulation and allowed all present to reap insights from it, everyone was naturally willing to handle the subsequent spiritual disaster affairs on his behalf, allowing him to enter seclusion in peace.

Yet just as the cultivators were frantically deliberating emergency response plans for the spiritual disaster, Qu Qianxing also concluded his meditation. Looking at the crowd, he let out a wry smile:

—"Senior predecessors, you actually don't need to go through all that trouble..."

—"Little Friend Qu, you do not comprehend the gravity of this matter,"-a cultivator said solemnly upon hearing him.-"This spiritual storm affects not just the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but the entire Central Domain. We must treat it with utmost vigilance."

—"Indeed,"-another cultivator nodded in agreement.-"We know your Master is exceptionally formidable, but we cannot trouble the Lord Immortal Venerable for every little thing! If an exalted figure like him suffers wear and tear over a minor matter like this and destabilizes his cultivation base, it would be far too tragic."

—"Don't underestimate us! While we cannot compare to your Master, we came to the Heavenly Spirit Sect for the Immortal Arts Assembly to become representative cultivators of the Central Domain. We can't just let the Untiring Immortal Venerable do everything!"-yet another cultivator chimed in.

None of the speaking cultivators seemed to remember that both the heavenly thunder and the spiritual energy storm had been summoned by the Untiring Immortal Venerable in the first place! In a cultivation world where everyone [only swept the snow before their own doorstep](<# "Literal CN: 各人自扫门前雪 (gè rén zì sǎo mén qián xuě) — each sweeps the snow before their own doorstep; common idiom for minding one's own business and ignoring others' troubles">), under normal circumstances not secretly resenting the instigator would already be considered magnanimous—let alone how eagerly they now scrambled to do chores for the [culprit](<# "Literal CN: 罪魁祸首 (zuìkuíhuòshǒu) — chief culprit / instigator; humorously contrasted with 不倦仙尊 (Untiring Immortal Venerable)">) in question.

Consequently, toward Qu Qianxing—a disciple whose cultivation was lower than theirs by who knew how many realms—they treated him entirely with the attitude of an equal peer, without a shred of the haughty posture typical of cultivation world powerhouses.

Qu Qianxing naturally understood the source of their attitude:

[As expected of Master!]

Qu Qianxing marveled inward. Wearing a gentle smile, he said:

—"I thank you all for your kind intentions. However, seniors need not worry. It was precisely out of concern that we might encounter a situation like this that Master had us construct the Spirit Storage Pool beforehand. Judging by the current circumstances, it shouldn't exceed the storage capacity of the Spirit Storage Pool."

Knowing that with his modest cultivation, relying solely on words would carry little persuasion, he simply flicked his finger and summoned the System's light screen, putting the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Spirit Storage Pool on full display for all to see.

And within the light screen, everything became clear at a glance.

Under ordinary circumstances, a massive volume of spiritual energy crashing down upon Heavenly Chasm Mountain and the Heavenly Spirit Sect would churn the surrounding spiritual energy, erupting into a surging torrent like a catastrophic flood. But upon the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Heavenly Chasm Mountain, colossal spiritual energy windmills were already spinning into motion, ingeniously altering the trajectory of the spiritual energy currents. Meanwhile, the aerial reflection overhead was far from a mere aesthetic ornament—it was in fact a spiritual dam, serving to buffer and store spiritual energy.

Furthermore, its storage capacity was stupendously vast. Even though it had suffered damage from the lightning tribulation and had a hole blasted into it, it was still more than adequate to store all the spiritual rain.

[—"So that reflection was actually meant for this?!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Those spiritual energy windmills are incredible! How did they manage to calibrate the spiritual energy so precisely?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Utterly brilliant! I was always envious that the Untiring Immortal Venerable managed to find such an immense geomantic paradise—turns out the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual energy is so stable thanks to the Spirit Storage Pool!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

The cultivators exclaimed in admiration.

Manor Lord Qingyang echoed a few words of marvel, then suddenly seemed to remember something and hurriedly grabbed Qu Qianxing:

—"Put it away quickly! Is this something you can just display to us?!"

The Untiring Immortal Venerable was also something else—handing over something on the level of a Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation to a Golden Core disciple! No matter how much he doted on Qu Qianxing, he shouldn't be this careless!

Qu Qianxing, who in fact truly held command authority over the Mountain-Protecting Grand Formation, replied nonchalantly:

—"The Spirit Storage Pool is no secret. Master said that if anyone needs it, I may freely teach it to them."

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable is truly too magnanimous!"-Manor Lord Qingyang sighed in admiration. Pausing, he turned to Qu Qianxing.-"Have you teach? You know the construction method for the Spirit Storage Pool?"

—"Yes,"-Qu Qianxing nodded.

Immediately, cultivators piped up:

[—"Little Friend Qu, I am immensely interested in this! Would you consider simply hosting a lecture series on it?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Please trouble yourself, Little Friend Qu!"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

This time, their call of "Little Friend" was spoken with absolute, heartfelt sincerity.

Qu Qianxing smiled, just about to agree, when he noticed his light screen flashing. This was a signal that a fellow disciple was requesting communication. And because Qu Qianxing happened to be looking directly at the light screen, the other party's face popped up automatically:

—"Senior Brother Qu, is the lightning tribulation over?"-It was a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect; judging from the background, he appeared to be in the Library Pavilion.-"Can we come out now?"

—"Why are you in such a rush to leave?"-Qu Qianxing's expression instantly turned stern, completely different from his polite courtesy toward "guests."-"The spiritual rain is pouring so heavily right now, when spiritual energy is at its absolute peak—why aren't you meditating properly?!"

—"W-We already meditated... during the tribulation just now,"-the disciple stammered, thoroughly intimidated.-"B-But everyone's foundation is only so deep, and right now we can't quite enter meditation anymore."

[Senior Brother, we really can't swallow another bite!]

This disciple's complaint, had it been heard in any other sect, would have caused others to die of sheer jealousy.

In which other sect could disciples ever be stuffed to bursting on spiritual energy?!

Then again, they weren't truly worth envying—the Heavenly Spirit Sect had its own brand of misery.

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing let out a cold sneer. Never mind the disciple on the Library Pavilion's end; even the surrounding powerhouses felt their hearts skip a beat, and some who belonged to sects could not help being reminded of their own Sect Master Senior Brothers.

—"If you cannot enter meditation, does that mean you have nothing to do?! Did you not gain a single shred of comprehension from this heavenly tribulation?! Tomorrow, the entire sect—every single person—will submit a 10,000-word reflection essay!"

A chorus of tragic wails instantly erupted from the other side of the screen.

Having assigned the homework, Qu Qianxing turned back to the other cultivators:

—"Apologies, seniors. Where were we just now?"

[—"N-No, it's fine. You go attend to your business first."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Right, our matter can wait for later."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

Those cultivators responded with lingering trepidation.

Qu Qianxing naturally had no objection.

Although he didn't mind immediately hosting an instructional course on the Spirit Storage Pool for these cultivators, with the spiritual rain still pouring down and his Master's condition still unknown, Qu Qianxing had actually been suppressing his impatience while humoring the others. His heart had long since flown to Zhou Hui's side. Now that he had the chance, Qu Qianxing excused himself with an apology and departed in a hurry.

However, someone was inspired by this:

—"A reflection essay... Right! Isn't this prime material for an academic paper?!"

The others could not help shooting this cultivator a sidelong glance:

[An academic paper?!]

[How could someone come up with such a brilliant idea?!]

[Now it's mine!]

Inexplicably, the atmosphere inside the Palace of No Words grew scorching and tense with fierce academic rivalry.
