# Chapter 77: The Beginning of the Schoolyard Tyrant Path (第77章 校霸之路的开始)

A sound beating might not be the correct method to solve a problem, but it was certainly the best method to temporarily suppress it.

Especially toward a spirit beast that had bound itself as a servant without permission, only to turn around and try to devour its master without permission.

After Mo Bei gave it a thorough thumping, it became much more well-behaved.

Naturally, this "obedience" was temporary; if this carp ever found another opportunity to turn on its master, it would strike back without the slightest mercy.

Evidently, however, it would not be given that chance.

After she finished pounding the carp, a bamboo slip bathed in starlight drifted down and landed in Mo Bei's hands. The ink upon it was surprisingly fresh.

Even Zhou Hui was quite astonished upon seeing it.

This bamboo slip hailed from his fifth reincarnation—the one where, having endured a heavenly tribulation that annihilated half the world, his remnant soul had no choice but to take shelter in an adjacent spatial rift. Since hiding was just hiding and he was [bored out of his mind](# "Literal CN: 闲得无聊（X） — having nothing better to do / bored stiff (crossed-out meta humor)"), Zhou Hui had synthesized certain spatial principles based on his existing spatial spells, further refined them during his sixth life, and ultimately formulated a cultivation art dedicated to the Laws of Space.

*Soul-Guiding Art* (《引魂功》).

However, this cultivation art was difficult—exceptionally difficult.

Even after Zhou Hui attained the Tribulation Transcendence stage, cleansed his marrow, refined his essence, transformed his physique, and reshaped his mortal body, he remained unsuited for this technique.

Forcing the issue had merely resulted in failing his seventh heavenly tribulation as well, sending him on a [sleepwalking dream tour](# "Literal CN: “梦游”了一番另一个世界 — 'sleepwalking' through another world, referencing his transmigration/reincarnation memories of modern Earth") through another world.

Afterward, Zhou Hui abandoned ascension and shelved his research into spatial magic. He never imagined anyone could practice this art in the near future. Who could have expected that the moment Mo Bei penned the character "Destiny" (命), this technique began stirring restlessly; and once Mo Bei finished thumping the carp spirit, it had grown so agitated that it sought to break free of Zhou Hui's restrictions entirely.

Thinking it over carefully, Mo Bei was genuinely a fitting candidate.

After all, she was born with an innate Extreme Yin Constitution and possessed half Yin Spirit bloodline. Among cultivators, such physique and skeletal aptitude were top-tier; even without undergoing marrow cleansing and essence refinement, she fully satisfied the prerequisite conditions to cultivate it. Even Mo Xi, gifted as he was, could not match her uniquely blessed constitution.

Coupled with obtaining a carp spirit endowed with both celestial qi-luck and innate spatial talent as a pet, allowing her to perceive the Laws of Space more directly and intuitively, she was truly the optimal candidate.

Weighing the matter, Zhou Hui finally relaxed his grip, allowing the manual to soar toward Mo Bei.

He felt a touch of chagrin in his heart, but far more happiness and anticipation.

The chagrin stemmed from the fact that he himself had never accomplished the final step of the technique; having failed to ascend through this art, he had naturally been unable to complete its final stage, nor would he necessarily have the opportunity to finish it in the future.

His happiness and anticipation lay in the fact that this cultivation art was not useless after all; it had been passed down, and to his personal direct disciple at that. With Mo Bei's physiological constitution, perhaps she could cultivate it to the supreme potential he had envisioned, or maybe even complete it one day?

At this thought, Zhou Hui felt another flicker of concern.

Mo Bei's learning capacity was beyond question, but she was rather lacking in originality. For that unverified final step, what if Bei'er encountered an unforeseen crisis and couldn't handle it? Should he continue refining parts of the manual for her?

Mo Bei had no inkling of her "parent's" overthinking and anxieties.

After all, her current cultivation was merely at the middle stage of Foundation Establishment; even if she practiced with all her might, putting the manual's final step of "Ascension" into practice would be an affair countless years down the road.

She merely stowed the manual away carefully, gave the restless carp another whack on the noggin, and stepped off the stage amidst the reverent gazes of the beginner class disciples.

***

—"By the way, did you find out why that carp bound itself to you?"-Mo Xi asked Mo Bei afterward.

—"It said I killed it once before and it didn't want to die a second time,"-Mo Bei replied, thoroughly perplexed.-"Yet after binding itself to me, it discovered I haven't reached the realm where I could kill it."

—"You killed it?!"-Mo Xi exclaimed in shock.

Mo Bei shook her head blankly.

She had never even set foot near that frigid pool before; how could she have ever had the chance to kill it?

Furthermore, had the Library Pavilion's restrictions not taken effect this time, Mo Bei might not have even been able to lay a hand on it!

The twin siblings debated for a long time without reaching a conclusion, turning their gazes together toward their Master.

Zhou Hui propped his chin on his palm, his brows knitting slightly.

In the Heavenly Dao's script, Mo Bei indeed killed this fish—hundreds of years later, when the "protagonist" arrived. Having cultivated until that era, Mo Bei would naturally possess the power of the Nascent Soul stage.

Yet this was something that had not happened yet...

Indeed; the events depicted in the Heavenly Dao's script had yet to transpire.

Across his previous six reincarnations, Mo Bei had died, had betrayed him, had vanished without a trace, but had never once encountered her "destined soulmate."

Zhou Hui estimated that the time simply had not arrived.

After all, the Heavenly Dao's script had factored all six of his past lives into its calculations—down to the precise detail that in his fifth life, after the world was destroyed, his remnant soul hid in a spatial rift and created the unfinished *Soul-Guiding Art*; and then in his seventh life, upon being slain by the "protagonist," he used the *Soul-Guiding Art* to successfully ascend, though his physical body was destroyed and only his remnant soul entered the Immortal-Demon Realm.

—If he hadn't been reduced to a mere remnant soul, how could the "protagonist" have ever thoroughly slain Zhou Hui? It was plain to see that the Heavenly Dao had racked its brains just to arrange for the "protagonist" to kill Zhou Hui twice.

Yet now, the carp spirit claimed Mo Bei had already killed it once?

—"Does this fish possess prophetic foresight?"-he asked.

—"...No,"-Mo Bei shook her head.

The fish's exact words were: *"If your daddy had that kind of ability, would I have been sealed for all those damn years? Would I have bound myself to a green yellow-haired chit like you as my master? Your daddy would have long since... Wait, listen to your daddy finish talking!"*

Mo Bei paid no heed to the foul-mouthed squawking deep within her sea of consciousness, merely keeping her eyes fixed on her Master.

Her Master attached surprising importance to this matter.

—"Why not have this fish project that memory directly for me to see?"-Zhou Hui said.

However, forcefully scouring its memories would inevitably damage the carp's soul, which would indirectly harm Mo Bei as its master.

Zhou Hui resolved to use a gentler approach, having the carp extract the memory itself:

—"Have it review this technique and extract that segment of its memory,"-Zhou Hui said, producing a bamboo slip.

Mo Bei relayed Zhou Hui's instructions, then revealed a rare look of astonishment:

—"...It says it's illiterate!"

—"Use this magical artifact to snip the memory out,"-Zhou Hui adjusted his approach.

—"..., ......"-Mo Bei fell silent for an exceptionally long time.

By the time Mo Xi finished a cup of tea and reviewed the ledger in his hands, Zhou Hui completed an entire cycle of meditation, and the little lamb on his lap finished munching today's flatbread, snuck every last candied fruit from the table, and drifted off to sleep with closed eyes, Mo Bei finally reported in a faint voice:

—"Master, I don't think... I can teach it..."

—"Then take this medicinal pill,"-Zhou Hui said.-"As long as it holds this pill beneath its tongue and recounts its recollection in detail, that will suffice."

—"Master, I don't think that will work either,"-this time, Mo Bei didn't even bother to budge.-"I noticed just now that this fish's linguistic expression is terrible, and its memory isn't much better. Its words are completely jumbled and incoherent. I'm beginning to wonder if what it described was merely a dream."

—"Could it be lying?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"I doubt its IQ is high enough to fabricate such a lie,"-Mo Bei said.-"It's not you, after all."

—"Huh?!"-Mo Xi glared at his twin sister.

—"That is indeed troublesome,"-Zhou Hui sighed, nursing a headache.

He had already sensed earlier that this fish had little brains to speak of, but evidently it was even stupider than Zhou Hui had imagined.

—"Why don't I just sever my contract with it, and Master can examine it with the Soul-Scouring Great Art?"-Mo Bei suggested. She had never cared much for this spirit pet to begin with, and now discovering that it was an illiterate fool only deepened her utter disdain.

Hearing this, the carp panicked at once. Poking its head out from her shadow, it screeched:

—"F\*ck your m—! I'll f\*ck your—"

*SMACK!*

Mo Bei brought her scabbard down with a resounding crack.

—"There is no need to resort to the Soul-Scouring Great Art so hastily,"-Zhou Hui said quite gently.

Although he took this matter seriously, patience was the one thing Zhou Hui possessed in boundless supply.

Having spent seven lifetimes pondering ascension, he naturally wouldn't summarily slaughter a carp when he still had several centuries to spare.

—"It has already reached the Soul Formation stage. Even if it's merely a fish, its brain ought to be fully developed,"-Zhou Hui observed.

Stroking the little lamb on his lap—which was likewise at the Soul Formation stage yet clearly lacking a fully developed brain—he smiled gently:

—"Send it to the schools. Let it start from basic education!"

The little lamb sneezed.

***

Naturally, this did not mean physically dumping the carp spirit into a mortal classroom.

Much as Zhou Hui might have fancied doing so, with the carp spirit's cultivation realm, it would likely take barely a few days before every mortal child in the school was devoured.

Yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect could conduct classes regardless.

—"These are the schools?"-Mo Xi asked curiously.

Although none of this concerned him, as a "good brother who cared deeply for his elder sister," Mo Xi trailed alongside Mo Bei wherever she went.

At this moment, they had arrived on a certain floor of Buyan Palace.

This floor appeared no different from any other level of Buyan Palace, lined with row upon row of classrooms. Yet looking closely inside, one would notice that everything—including the low desks and cushions—consisted of ethereal phantom projections.

These were naturally real-time projections of the various schools scattered across the Central Domain.

After all, Zhou Hui's teleportation arrays were not yet fully operational, unable to instantaneously transport sect disciples to branch campuses across the realm. And the Kunpeng required an entire day's journey, making daily commutes impractical for disciples. Thus, Zhou Hui utilized projection techniques to stream real-time projections of all branch campuses into Buyan Palace. Similarly, projections of the disciples serving as Instructors would manifest inside each respective school, allowing them to oversee classroom dynamics—a form of [remote distance learning](# "Literal CN: “远程教学” (yuǎnchéng jiàoxué) — modern tele-education / remote virtual classrooms").

And when the Heavenly Spirit Sect began its new term, the schools across the lands had naturally commenced classes long ago.

Only, it was currently not class time, leaving all the classrooms empty.

Gazing at the school classrooms, Mo Bei said with an awkward frown:

—"Master, I don't think that fish will listen to lectures obediently..."

She found it awkward to explain in detail that ever since Zhou Hui announced he would make the carp spirit attend classes, the creature had been non-stop cursing her Master.

Only, this fish was not just illiterate, but cursed with an exceptionally impoverished vocabulary. Aside from "die," "f\*ck," and "your mother," it couldn't concoct any new insults, repeating them over and over until it sounded like chanting scriptures.

Ever since following Zhou Hui, Mo Bei had never encountered a creature with such a low IQ; she held zero optimism for its future.

She even felt that forcing sect disciples serving as Instructors to teach this thing would be nothing short of pure torture.

As it happened, Zhou Hui felt the exact same way.

—"I'm not having it sit inside the classroom for lessons,"-Zhou Hui said. Examining the corridor of this floor, he demarcated a spot.-"Place a fish tank right here. Whenever classes begin, lock it inside."

—"Does that count as studying?"-Mo Bei asked.

—"[Read a book a thousand times, and its meaning reveals itself naturally](# "Literal CN: 读书千遍，其义自现 (dúshū qiānbiàn, qíyì zìxiàn) — classic Chinese educational adage: read a text thousands of times until understanding dawns effortlessly"),"-Zhou Hui said, [teaching in accordance with aptitude](# "Literal CN: 因材施教 (yīncái-shījiào) — Confucian pedagogical principle of tailoring instruction to the student's unique nature").-"Since it despises being cooped up, it had best study diligently. Whenever it masters the material, it will be released; if it fails to learn, suppressing it for another few hundred or thousand years hardly matters anyway. In any case, I am in no hurry."

Mo Bei heard the ranting deep within her consciousness dwindle into a whisper. Even though it stubbornly kept muttering in disjointed gasps, a distinct tremor of dread was now woven into its profanity.

***

Zhou Hui had no idea that his casual decision had spawned a fresh ghost story across the branch campuses.

[—"It's true! I just saw the shadow of a colossal fish swim past outside!"](# "Speaker: White Jade Capital School Student")

[—"The school is situated on the seabed to begin with; isn't seeing fish completely normal?"](# "Speaker: White Jade Capital School Student")

[—"But half of that fish's shadow looked like bare bone!"](# "Speaker: White Jade Capital School Student")

[—"You weren't dozing off, were you?"](# "Speaker: White Jade Capital School Student")

[—"No, I saw it too! Only not during class, but last night... I realized I forgot to bring the textbook for one of my courses back to the dorm, and homework was due today, so I came over from the dormitory to grab it. As I was walking down the corridor, I heard the sound of splashing water. And when I stepped into the classroom to search for my book, I heard that sound again. I even glanced around and saw nothing out of the ordinary, but the moment I picked up my book and lifted my head, I saw a gargantuan fish head right outside the corridor window—!"](# "Speaker: White Jade Capital School Student")

[—"Aaaaaah!"](# "Speaker: White Jade Capital School Students")

The students erupted in terrified shrieks.

Hearing the shrieks, Eleventh Lady Mei knitted her brows. Crumpling two small paper balls to stuff into her ears, she returned to her book.

For her, the curriculum was rather heavy.

The school's curriculum was structured according to the [Six Arts of the Gentleman](# "Literal CN: 君子六艺 (jūnzǐ liùyì) — the classical Confucian curriculum: Rites (礼), Music (乐), Archery (射), Charioteering (御), Calligraphy/Literature (书), and Mathematics (数)"):

Rites included standard etiquette, historical lore, customs, and common knowledge of the cultivation world, as well as meditation;

Music encompassed musical theory, though first-year pupils were still learning to read notation;

Archery represented martial arts, though the first grade was still doing "calisthenics"—reputedly because the physical condition of most students was too poor, requiring a solid foundation before they could touch weaponry;

Charioteering involved piloting magical artifacts and machinery, though first-years only learned paper folding;

Calligraphy covered penmanship and composition;

Yet in Eleventh Lady Mei's view, what White Jade Capital School prioritized above all was the [Ninefold Arithmetic](# "Literal CN: 九数 (jiǔshù) — the Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art; advanced mathematics"), which included not only traditional arithmetic, but also upgraded subjects like physics and chemistry.

Therefore, Eleventh Lady Mei was resolved to master it.

She had heard the Instructor say that if one excelled in "Ninefold Arithmetic," outstanding students would be sent for an "internship" during the winter Immortals' Bazaar. When she reached the bazaar, if she could find a way to stay permanently or flee, she would never have to return to Longmen Town again!

Furthermore, Eleventh Lady Mei felt she possessed a bit of talent in this area.

Although she had only recognized a handful of characters at home, she picked up mathematical treatises remarkably quickly, even earning praise from the Instructor. It was nothing like that miserable "Five Archeries" class—as a pampered young lady who had never stepped beyond her courtyard unattended since childhood, merely holding a stance for a single class left Eleventh Lady Mei nearly fainting, let alone practicing full martial fist routines.

Come to think of it, there was another "Five Archeries" class this afternoon, wasn't there?

Eleventh Lady Mei genuinely wished everything could just be replaced with Ninefold Arithmetic.

—"Hey, Shiyiniang~~"

Just as Eleventh Lady was agonizing over the afternoon class, someone suddenly tapped her shoulder.

Muffled by the two paper wads stuffed in Eleventh Lady Mei's ears, the voice sounded faint and broken. Yet that water-snake-like hand resting on her shoulder made her jump up in fright.

Because of this, the loosely fitted paper wads rolled right out of her ears.

—"My, my, stuffing your ears to read, are we?"-that water-snake hand snatched up the paper wads at once. Eleventh Lady looked up to see her suddenly materialized "Grand-Aunt" eyeing her playfully.-"Think everyone is disturbing you?"

God knew what was so funny about that statement, but the flock of girls trailing beside the Grand-Aunt all burst into giggles.

—"...No."

Eleventh Lady Mei knitted her brows, watching this group.

They had only acted cautiously for a single week upon first entering the school; once they realized the curriculum taught consisted of "ordinary" knowledge and that the immortals rarely lingered in person within the school, they quickly grew arrogant once more.

Had it not been for the mandatory teleportation enforced by the coral tree's sundial clock—which unfailingly beamed any enrolled child straight to school—the parents back in town would have already contemplated pulling their children out of classes.

In truth, many children listened to their parents and [rotted away lying flat](# "Literal CN: 摆烂 (bǎilàn) — letting things go to seed / slacking off without ambition") after arriving at school, especially fooling around alongside the Mei family's "Grand-Aunt," even treating the Instructors with flippant disdain.

Eleventh Lady Mei had once overheard the Instructors discussing how the academic discipline of this school was the absolute worst among all branch campuses.

Eleventh Lady Mei felt intensely ashamed.

She did not want to return to Longmen Town, did not want to return to that past life, and wanted even less to be carted off as a second wife for some manager of Shengmo Sect. Thus, she studied with all her might. Yet this conduct seemed to be viewed by the others as a thorn in their side; any child who studied as earnestly as Eleventh Lady Mei was frequently harassed by them.

—What brought Eleventh Lady Mei a measure of relief was that this bunch dared not cause trouble during class hours, allowing her to attend lectures in peace so far.

Yet during recess like this, there was no escaping them.

With disgust, the "Grand-Aunt" tossed the paper wads into Eleventh Lady Mei's face, asking with a sweet smile:

—"Shiyiniang, you don't plan on heading back today either?"

—"I need remedial study for Ninefold Arithmetic,"-Eleventh Lady Mei muttered sullenly.

[—"You're already doing well enough, so why the remedial study?"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")-one girl remarked.-[—"On the other hand, since coming to school, you've barely gone home at all, have you? Could you be trying to latch onto the Instructors here?"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"Teacher Jing who teaches Ninefold Arithmetic is genuinely rather handsome."](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"No matter how handsome he is, his true body isn't here; otherwise you might actually pull something off."](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"Hee-hee, even if his true body were here, it wouldn't matter. Our Shiyiniang has a betrothal after all, right?"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"Speaking of which, I heard from Mother and Father that the immortals from Shengmo Sect will be visiting our town this afternoon! How can Shiyiniang sit still here? Aren't you going home to take a look?"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

The flock of girls gossiped maliciously, their eyes fixed on Eleventh Lady Mei with venomous delight.

—"...I'm not going back. I have classes to attend,"-Eleventh Lady Mei answered in a dry voice. She was infinitely grateful for the existence of the school; at the very least, it provided her with a sanctuary to hide.

Yet today, this group seemed determined not to let her off.

[—"You can just ask for leave!"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"Exactly! Didn't you nearly faint during Five Archeries last time? Why put yourself through such agony?"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"Just use your [monthly flow](# "Literal CN: 葵水 (kuíshuǐ) — traditional Chinese medical euphemism for menstruation") as an excuse, and Teacher will definitely let you leave early!"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

[—"How about we write it for you?"](# "Speaker: Mei Clan Girl")

As she spoke, the "Grand-Aunt" actually reached for the brush and ink on Eleventh Lady Mei's desk.

—"Don't!"-Eleventh Lady Mei scrambled to stop her in a panic. She would rather faint in the Five Archeries class than go back to meet that cursed fiancé!

Yet at this moment, several pairs of hands grabbed her with giggling laughter.

Spells and magic were strictly forbidden among students inside the school, but ordinary mortal scuffles could not be halted—especially ambiguous actions like being held back by others. Consequently, Eleventh Lady Mei was temporarily unable to break free.

What caused Eleventh Lady Mei's blood to run cold was that the "Grand-Aunt" gave a malicious grin, effortlessly penning handwriting identical to her own! She even picked up her personal seal, preparing to stamp the leave slip—

—"Stop!"

With a sudden burst of strength, Eleventh Lady Mei lunged onto the "Grand-Aunt," snatching back her seal.

With that motion of hers, the girls who had been holding her tumbled haphazardly across the floor, knocking the surrounding desks askew, while the "Grand-Aunt" was shoved hard against a nearby desk.

The desks had been reinforced with magic, so there was no worry of serious injury, but the "Grand-Aunt" was visibly enraged by Eleventh Lady's actions.

—"Fine! Mei Eleven, what colossal nerve you have!"-the "Grand-Aunt" hissed, raising her hand high—

—"Enough!"-a cold voice commanded.

Several Instructors had arrived at the doorway at some unknown point, looking over at the scene.

An invisible force froze the "Grand-Aunt's" descending hand in midair.

The "Grand-Aunt" stared blankly at the Instructors. Suddenly her eyes reddened, letting out a delicate whimper:

—"Teacher, you saw it yourself, it was Shiyiniang who struck first! The sisters... the sisters clearly just wanted to play a harmless joke..."

...[The sheer thickness of her skin was truly something to behold](# "Literal CN: 脸皮的厚度也是可以了 — hilarious sarcasm regarding her staggering shamelessness and brass neck").

Everyone was struck with sheer awe at her shamelessness.

And Eleventh Lady only seemed to realize what she had done just then. Staring blankly at her surroundings and seeing the girls sprawled all over the floor, she wore a look of utter disbelief.

Eleventh Lady Mei knew that in the past, she would never have had the strength to break free from so many people holding her back, even if this bunch were delicate young ladies formerly confined to deep boudoirs just like herself...

Right... delicate young ladies... So that was it...

Eleventh Lady Mei understood.

Although she loathed the Five Archeries class, she feared being abandoned by the Instructors and driven back to Longmen Town far more. Thus, even when feeling utterly miserable during every lesson, she diligently practiced to the very end, unlike the others who casually made up all sorts of excuses to skip class.

Therefore, subtle though it was, Eleventh Lady Mei's body was vastly healthier than when she first arrived. At the very least, she was no longer like before, where merely stepping out of the boudoir and walking beneath the sun was unbearable.

Meanwhile, the others' slacking had left them virtually unchanged from their original frail state.

Especially since the "Grand-Aunt" prided herself on status, the girls surrounding her all hailed from "respectable families" back in town, their physical constitutions roughly identical to the former Eleventh Lady Mei. Consequently, the moment she exerted her full strength, she sent all of them tumbling.

[To defeat them... was it actually this easy?]

Eleventh Lady Mei's lips curled into a faint smile. She turned toward the Instructor of the Five Archeries class:

—"Teacher, I will not ask for leave this afternoon. I want to attend class properly!"

—"Hm? Wonderful!"-the female Instructor from Blood Demon Hall blinked, then beamed with a brilliant smile.-"I said it long ago: you actually have great promise!"

—"Wait a moment, aren't we supposed to be discussing what just happened here?"-said the Ninefold Arithmetic Instructor, Teacher Jing—who was none other than Jing Chenjun—with a twitch of his lips.

In his subconscious, however, he firmly wanted to protect Eleventh Lady.

After all, this school's academic discipline was already rotten enough; how could a rare good student be allowed to get bullied?

Just as Jing Chenjun was thinking this, he felt something stir inside his sleeve—

[Wait a moment, I don't even have divine sense, so how could I feel "movement"?!]

Jing Chenjun thought in racing excitement.

Stepping back two paces in secret, he drew the object from his sleeve to inspect it.

It was that imperial jade seal.

On the surface, the imperial seal appeared unchanged, yet faint spheres of light drifted down upon it, making the seal slightly brighter.

Following the trajectory of those light orbs, Jing Chenjun discovered they emanated from Eleventh Lady Mei's body.
