# Chapter 235: Internal Strife and External Peril

In the face of war, whether mortals or immortal cultivators, all were treated like mere grass and mustard.

Even if it was only a "counterfeit" war.

So Jing Chenjun reflected in agonizing despair.

He had long lost count of how many times he had entered the trial system. The moment he materialized, what greeted his eyes was a stone terrace soaked in gore and strewn with mangled severed corpses, accompanied by a sickening stench of blood and putrefaction that made his gorge rise. Yet none of that was the most unbearable part. The true horror was the miasmic demonic qi spreading outward alongside the slaughter of countless demons—corrosive forces that a fragile mortal vessel could not withstand in the slightest. The instant Jing Chenjun logged on, he could feel his vitality draining away in torrential streams.

At times like this, Jing Chenjun could not help but harbor a sliver of grievance, feeling that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had no need to make the simulation *this* realistic.

In truth, by this stage, very few participants could still endure and hold their ground.

After all, the physical avatars inside the trial system were molded based on participants' real-world bodies; their [fleshly resilience](# "Literal CN: □□强度 (肉体强度) — physical body durability") and cultivation realms were virtually identical to reality. Even if one could obtain certain buffs and supply caches through combat, their overall power would not significantly exceed their original realm.

Conversely, this Immortal-Demon War was "authentic history" recorded within the mystic realm. Even though Mo Xi had injected scripted storylines and assigned character roles, it did not alter the overarching trajectory much—not to mention that Mo Xi had zero intention of lowering the difficulty (realism). Consequently, participants in the trial system experienced in real-time the exact horrors felt by the historical combatants of the Immortal-Demon War—nay, it was arguably even more tragic.

Ten thousand years ago, before the catastrophic outbreak of the Immortal-Demon War, this realm had originally plummeted from the celestial heavens precisely to stave off the demon hordes. The living beings of that era had spent their entire lives waging relentless warfare against the demons, possessing a natural "immunity" to this blood-soaked atmosphere. Yet ten thousand years later, because all contact with the outside world had been severed following the war, this realm had enjoyed prolonged peace. To the vast majority of cultivators, the demonic invasion was merely a chapter in ancient history texts; having never tasted such horrific brutality for generations, their psychological endurance and combat tenacity were markedly weaker.

By the time the narrative progressed to the fall of White Jade Capital, few defenders remained along its outer battlements; only high-realm powerhouses could still hold the line.

Consequently, the instant Jing Chenjun "logged on," he was immediately noticed.

—"Kid, why did you come back again?"-The Soul Formation cultivator commanding this sector of the defense line asked in astonishment, looking at Jing Chenjun.—"Didn't we tell you not to log back on?! Your cultivation is so low—you haven't even established your foundation, right? Lingering in this demonic qi environment, not only will your body dissolve into a puddle of bloody water, but if you stay too long, even your brain in reality will be affected!"

—"...Senior hasn't left either,"-Jing Chenjun countered.

—"Ah, well, the battle between the two Mahayana titans hasn't concluded yet, has it? I want to see the outcome,"-the cultivator said.

—"Oh,"-Jing Chenjun murmured, raising his head toward the heavens upon hearing the excuse.

By now, more than half the firmament had been devoured by ink-black and crimson-tinged demonic miasma, completely obscuring the scenery above. Furthermore, the arena where Villa Master Qingyang and the Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum were dueling was not situated here, but rather in the very heart of White Jade Capital.

Yet that central epicenter was infinitely more perilous.

A clash between Mahayana titans was inherently capable of obliterating an entire metropolis or even reducing an entire mortal empire to cinder; it was never an event meant for live frontline spectating. Worse still, this was the bleeding vanguard of the ancient Immortal-Demon War—where demonic qi, celestial essence, and Mahayana shockwaves violently clashed and coalesced into a raging maelstrom of annihilating forces. Forget a mortal like Jing Chenjun; even a Soul Formation titan drawing near would be casually ground into fine powder.

Thus, if one truly wished to watch the duel between the two Mahayana titans, the optimal vantage point was never the doomed historical White Jade Capital on the verge of vanishing, but rather the grand plaza of the Immortal Arts Assembly in the waking world.

There, massive jade viewing screens linked in real-time broadcast the duel from multiple angles, allowing spectators of the Immortal Arts Assembly to watch comfortably according to their preferences.

Yet to a mortal like Jing Chenjun, the Mahayana realm was simply too distant... like unfathomable deities residing in myth.

Even knowing that every gesture of those titans held vastly greater sway over the war than an ant of a mortal like himself—and that this "Immortal-Demon War" might merely serve as a theatrical backdrop prepared for those two supreme grandmasters—Jing Chenjun stubbornly clung to the frontlines out of an inexplicable, unremembered reluctance to surrender. However insignificant, he desperately wished he could contribute his own meager measure of strength.

This bizarre, unyielding reluctance was not unique to Jing Chenjun; many of the cultivators holding the line felt the exact same way.

According to Mo Xi's post-battle statistical analysis, nearly a tenth of the participants exhibited this phenomenon. Although they had not restored the memories of past reincarnation cycles, they resembled vengeful ghouls possessing an obsession that even memory wipes could not extinguish, liable to be triggered whenever specific conditions arose.

The cultivator before him, who claimed he was staying purely to watch the Mahayana duel, was evidently one of them.

Standing atop the outer walls of White Jade Capital alongside Jing Chenjun, it was utterly impossible to catch a glimpse of the central duel. What awaited them here was only an unending tide of demonic beasts and relentless slaughter. Moreover, even without studying history, everyone knew that defending the historic White Jade Capital was an inevitably lost cause.

Jing Chenjun did not expose the man's clumsy lie. Retracting his gaze, he merely sighed:

—"A duel between Mahayana titans... I'd love to witness it live too, but alas, my mortal body simply cannot endure it."

—"Isn't there a [livestream](# "Literal CN: 直播 (zhíbō) — live broadcast / projection") outside?"-The cultivator, completely unaware that he had exposed his true inner motivations, comforted Jing Chenjun:—"Besides, the Immortal Arts Assembly won't be a one-time affair. Once you attain my level of cultivation, you'll naturally be able to linger here much longer."

The cultivator spoke with absolute certainty, as though advancing to the Soul Formation realm were an inevitable milestone in human life; at the very least, in his eyes, Jing Chenjun becoming a Soul Formation master was entirely a matter of course.

This, naturally, was anything but normal.

Hierarchy in the cultivation world was rigid and absolute. In an ordinary small sect, a Soul Formation titan was sufficient to serve as an exalted Grand Elder, revered by low-tier disciples as an 'ancestor.' A typical Soul Formation master would never speak with such gentle warmth to a disciple who had not even achieved Foundation Establishment.

Yet in this trial system, not only this cultivator, but the vast majority of mid-to-high-tier cultivators treated Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples like Jing Chenjun with distinct deference and respect, seemingly oblivious to the chasm in cultivation realms.

This "privileged treatment" was not merely courtesy extended toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Jing Chenjun was well aware of this special treatment, and knew precisely where it stemmed from. Reaching into his robes, he retrieved the item he had endured excruciating agony to deliver:

—"This is the retrofitted blueprint for the spiritual aperture of White Jade Capital's city-defense grand array. The previous energy output was insufficient, so right after dying just now, several senior brothers from our Artifact Refinement Workshop conferred and concluded we could modify the aperture, designing Amplification City-Defense Cannons that can be mounted directly onto it."

—"It can be reinforced even further?"-The cultivator's eyes flashed with hope as he took the blueprint from Jing Chenjun's hands.

The other cultivators defending the ramparts looked over with eager, expectant gazes.

However, upon inspecting the schematic details, the cultivator's face twisted in agony:

—"It's so complicated! Will swapping it out on the fly be in time?"

—"It's really not that complex; the workshop senior brothers just sketched it a bit haphazardly and didn't have time to format the documentation. The actual operation is quite straightforward—even someone like me can do it,"-Jing Chenjun said, rolling up his sleeves.—"Let me demonstrate once. With Senior's grand cultivation prowess, you'll master it the moment you see it."

Yet the cultivator was far less confident in himself:

—"Alright, let me record it with a jade slip first!"

Jing Chenjun smiled, long accustomed to such reactions.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect was truly unique!

Through the Immortals' Bazaar and the Immortal Arts Assembly, Jing Chenjun had completely verified this truth.

From his interactions with external cultivators—whether disciples of formal sects, wandering rogue cultivators, or even elites from prestigious Northern Domain institutions—he noticed that their pedagogical methods were scarcely different from traditional mortals: heavily favoring abstract theory (the humanities) while scorning hands-on practice (engineering). Furthermore, their extreme reliance on the effortless convenience of magical spells caused severe [subject imbalance](# "Literal CN: 偏科 (piānkē) — academic subject imbalance / being lopsided in one discipline while failing another"); procedures that even a mortal like him could comprehend were completely beyond their ability to execute.

This was not because Jing Chenjun was exceptionally gifted, but rather due to the unique pedagogical model of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

If he could achieve it, other mortals could as well. Could mortals, perhaps, also master the spells and arts of immortals?

At the very least, the mortal students of the White Jade Capital Academy were capable of it, performing even better than traditional cultivators.

So Jing Chenjun mused.

He had not realized that after enduring so many tribulations, his yearning for immortals had diminished considerably. Rather than his original, naive desire to 'attain immortality,' when confronted with the inexhaustible demon army, Jing Chenjun found himself wishing far more that 'mortals could possess the capabilities of cultivators.'

Naturally, for the present Jing Chenjun, both aspirations were far too distant. What he had to confront first was that inexplicable, nebulous reluctance carried over from dreams he could not recall.

All Jing Chenjun could manage right now was to properly calibrate the spiritual aperture of White Jade Capital's defense array.

And just as Jing Chenjun had promised, the task was not difficult. In mere minutes—relying on high-precision spiritual energy gauges rather than direct sensory perception—Jing Chenjun concluded the calibration.

—"You must follow the data strictly down to the exact decimal; you cannot do it [by feeling](# "Literal CN: 凭感觉 (píng gǎnjué) — relying on gut feeling rather than exact measurements"),"-Jing Chenjun emphasized at the end.

He understood the deep-seated flaws of these cultivators all too well. Every single one of them, taking advantage of their innate ability to perceive and manipulate spiritual qi, treated spellcraft and the maintenance of magical equipment like novice cooks in a kitchen—tossing in 'a pinch of this' and 'a dash of that by feel.' They transformed what should have been precise mathematical formulas into 'profoundly esoteric mysticism,' performing worse than mortals who knew nothing at all! At the very least, the children of the White Jade Capital Academy performed far better than them.

Yet Jing Chenjun had no idea whether his warning had truly sunk in.

—"I know, I know,"-the Soul Formation cultivator answered casually, though his gaze drifted with deep concern toward Jing Chenjun:—"More importantly, your body..."

—"It's fine, bearable,"-Jing Chenjun replied nonchalantly, glancing down at himself.

In just those few minutes, massive patches of bloody welts had erupted across his skin, resembling giant blooming peony tattoos. In reality, this was the gruesome result of a mortal vessel failing to withstand such overwhelming demonic qi. Had it been anyone else, they would have long collapsed, writhing on the ground in shrieking agony, before dissolving entirely into a puddle of bloody water under the corroding miasma.

What astonished Jing Chenjun himself was that his tolerance for such agony was far greater than he could have imagined. Clearly raised in pampered luxury since childhood, he seemed completely indifferent to the torment of [having a layer of skin peeled off](# "Literal CN: 脱层皮 (tuō céng pí) — shedding a layer of skin; undergoing brutal suffering or flaying"). Even the previous time he had "died" from demonic corruption and was ejected from the trial system, he had faintly felt relieved by his own death.

[Hm? Why would I feel relieved by my own death? As a mortal, I was destined to die anyway.]

Lacking memories of his past life, Jing Chenjun puzzled over it for only a fraction of a second before attributing the mindset to the trial system being "too realistic."

Nonetheless, those bloody welts served as a stark reminder: his time in the trial system was running out.

Thus, Jing Chenjun urged hurriedly:

—"Let's see the array's current performance."

[—"Very well."](# "Speaker: Sky City Guard")

The cultivator nodded, feeling Jing Chenjun's resolve. Come to think of it, this cultivator had also chosen to remain here and fight to the bitter end out of an inexplicable hatred toward the demon race, unconcerned even if he dissolved into bloody water. Besides, this was merely an 'illusionary trial realm'; dying here wasn't true death!

With that thought, the cultivator averted his eyes from Jing Chenjun's bloody welts and barked to the other defenders:

—"Activate the city-defense grand array!"

[—"Understood!"](# "Speaker: Array Cultivators")

The other cultivators shouted back with soaring morale.

They sprinted toward the nodes of the array, preparing to 'ignite' and maneuver the formation using their own spiritual energy to launch an offensive against the demons outside.

Lacking spiritual energy, Jing Chenjun naturally could not participate.

Had this been the past Jing Chenjun, he would have felt dejected. But now that the array had been retrofitted with his own hands, he had no such idle musings, tensely peering over the battlements to confirm the destructive yield of the attack.

Although his vision was constrained by his mortal biology, preventing him from seeing far, this was naturally no obstacle for a disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Jing Chenjun pulled a "thousand-li spyglass" crafted by the Artifact Refinement Workshop from his robes, raised it to his eye, and peered downward.

Now he saw clearly.

Beneath White Jade Capital, behind that churn of black-crimson fog, was an inexhaustible sea of demons. They resembled blistering pustules upon severely scalded, putrefying flesh, or the festering lesions of late-stage cutaneous rot—far exceeding the bounds of mere trypophobia. The sheer sight alone triggered an instinctual, primal horror of agony and death from deep within human nature.

Jing Chenjun felt that no matter how many times he looked upon this scene, it remained utterly unbearable.

Fortunately, he did not have to endure it for long.

While Jing Chenjun was surveying the demon horde, the defenders had activated the city-defense array at maximum speed.

As intricate runes across the formation flared to life, a pillar of pure, radiant white luminescence coalesced at the center and swept mercilessly across the demon ranks below, instantly vaporizing those pustule-like horrors into flying ash.

It even incinerated and purified a portion of the blood mist that had blanketed the city, widening their field of view.

Seeing this, the array cultivators erupted in triumphant cheers.

The leading Soul Formation cultivator slapped Jing Chenjun on the shoulder in sheer delight:

—"Hahaha, splendid work! The array's offensive output has increased multiple times over! At this rate, we might actually hold out until the trial concludes!"

[Can't you people be a bit bolder and actually imagine winning?]

Jing Chenjun offered a wry smile, about to roast him with a sharp retort. But before he could speak, he reflexively flinched at the shoulder the man had just clapped:

—"Pain...!? Agh..."

—"Ah, apologies, apologies!"-The cultivator once again remembered Jing Chenjun's modest—or rather, completely nonexistent—cultivation base and the gruesome blood welts covering him from demonic corrosion. Hastily pulling back his hand, he pleaded:—"I got carried away in the excitement. But you truly ought to leave this place at once! If this continues, your body..."

—"No... it wasn't because of you. Something feels... terribly wrong..."-Jing Chenjun's expression contorted.

As his words fell, something slid out of his sleeve and dropped onto the stone ramparts with a wet thud.

He looked down instinctively, only to discover that it was a chunk of meat.

The flesh on his shoulder had become cooked to mush by the demonic miasma; with that single clap from another person, it had sloughed straight off the bone!

Even hardened immortal cultivators were startled by this gruesome spectacle.

The cultivator stumbled back several paces in horror:

—"Y-You... you..."

—"It's the demonic qi,"-Jing Chenjun said with great difficulty. He found himself shockingly calm, as though the severed flesh that had tumbled off wasn't his own. Through that searing agony, he even grasped certain "knowledge" lingering in the recesses of his mind despite having his memories erased:—"The demonic qi is surging... It must be a demon vanguard of millions... tens of millions... And the source of this demonic qi doesn't seem to be coming from outside the walls, but rather..."

As Jing Chenjun spoke, he turned his head to look toward the interior of White Jade Capital.

Even this simple movement was executed with immense struggle.

In the extreme demonic qi, Jing Chenjun felt he had reached his absolute limit; forget turning his head, even uttering another syllable threatened to make the flesh slough off his frame.

Yet by this point, the cultivators required no warning from Jing Chenjun; those endowed with spiritual energy could already "perceive" far more.

Following Jing Chenjun's gaze toward the center of White Jade Capital, they saw black and white spiritual energies violently tearing at one another, distorting the metropolis into fractured dimensional blocks.

One cultivator cried out in sheer panic:

—"That turbulent spiritual energy... has a teleportation array run amok?!"

[—"...Has the demon race breached the city interior?!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

[—"No, that grayish-white aura doesn't look like pure demonic qi; it contains something else!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

[—"What on earth is happening?!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

[—"Come to think of it, wasn't someone inside the city summoning celestial troops?!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

[—"Ah! If you hadn't mentioned it, I would've completely forgotten! That did seem to be the 'plot'!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

[—"Does that mean the summoning failed?!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

[—"Even if it failed, they wouldn't summon demons out of thin air, would they...?!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivator")

The cultivators clamored in utter confusion.

Meanwhile, the leading Soul Formation cultivator grew increasingly dazed as their chaotic debate echoed in his ears.

He felt as though he had vaguely experienced something identical in the past.

Back then, his cultivation had been even higher than now—he had already successfully broken through to the Void Refinement realm, standing atop a city soaring through the highest heavens as the final line of defense against the demon hordes. Even when pushed to the brink of utter exhaustion with all supplies depleted, he had never surrendered hope. Because he knew that their [son-in-law](# "Literal CN: 姑爷 (gūye) — son-in-law (referring to the City Lord's son-in-law)")—the City Lord's son-in-law—was activating the ultimate summoning array to establish contact with the Celestial Realm. If he succeeded, not only this city, but the entire world would be redeemed!

Yet salvation never arrived; instead, a similar grayish-white aura had torn the entire city asunder...

—"...Senior!"

The Soul Formation cultivator was nearly consumed by the suffocating grip of that nightmare when a voice jolted him "awake."

Or rather, he was startled awake by Jing Chenjun's blood-drenched face.

At this moment, Jing Chenjun could no longer endure the chaotic miasma churning around him, looking completely like a [bloody gourd](# "Literal CN: 血葫芦 (xuè húlu) — a figure drenched head-to-toe in blood, skinned or mangled until unrecognizable").

—"A-Are you alright?"-the Soul Formation cultivator asked in distress.

Jing Chenjun wanted to reply "I'm fine," but the flesh around his throat was melting as well, making speech nearly impossible.

Even so, Jing Chenjun did not depart; he seemed resolved to remain until the bitter end.

Seeing Jing Chenjun like this, the Soul Formation cultivator snapped completely awake, surveying his surroundings and assessing his actual reality:

He did indeed hail from a city in the heavens—Sky City (天上城).

Right, he was a member of Sky City's escort guard.

However, this time Sky City had constructed a massive flying vessel, ferrying a whole ship of cultivators to the Central Domain to witness the Ascension Stairway and take part in the Immortal Arts Assembly. He had merely tagged along as an escort guard, taking the opportunity for a company-paid vacation, which was how he entered this trial "game."

In such a peaceful world, he had obviously never fought against demon legions, nor had he ever battled to his dying breath. Most importantly, Sky City didn't even have a "son-in-law"—Sky City only had an eldest young master! It wasn't as if Eldest Young Master Jian Nuo was going to be married off as a bride, right?

Thus, his feeling just now must have been an illusion induced by the trial—he had simply gotten too into character.

The Soul Formation cultivator persuaded himself.

Yet inexplicably, he did not want to discard the "emotional resonance" stirred by this illusion. A cultivator's intuition told him that this emotion was by no means a bad thing—it existed for the sake of preventing a certain future from ever repeating itself.

Heeding his own conviction, the Soul Formation cultivator cast one last glance at Jing Chenjun—who had dissolved into a bloody gourd yet stubbornly refused to leave—before turning a resolute gaze upon the remaining defenders.

—"Stop making wild guesses!"-the Soul Formation cultivator barked.—"All we need to do is hold the city! Whatever the hell it is, if it's an enemy, we'll blast the f*** out of it together!"

[—"YEAAAH!"](# "Speaker: Defender Cultivators")

The other cultivators roared back without a second thought.

Although they remembered nothing, this visceral emotion was shared among them all.

From that moment on, the runes upon the city-defense array never dimmed again. The pure white beams of light purified the black-crimson mist time and again, obliterating the encroaching invaders.

Jing Chenjun watched quietly from the side.

Yet the longer he watched, the more a gnawing anxiety and despair surged within his chest:

[Is this kind of resistance truly meaningful?]

[If one day I am confronted with real demons, is this all I can do?]

Just as his mind sank into turmoil, a clear, authoritative voice suddenly resonated across the firmament:

—"The trial has entered a critical phase. The Mountain-Protecting Grand Array is about to activate!"

—"All disciples of Heavenly Spirit Sect, hear my command! Anchor your souls, calm your spirits, and prepare for battle—!"

[Is that... Senior Brother Qu?! Qu Qianxing?!]

Hearing that voice, Jing Chenjun's spirit jolted!

Setting aside the Untiring Immortal Venerable, if one were to ask which direct disciple commanded the greatest authority and dread, it was undeniably Qu Qianxing, who oversaw the Heavenly Spirit Sect's academic curriculum. His presence seemed to terrify Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples even more than the Immortal Venerable himself!

Yet at such a despairing juncture, Qu Qianxing's presence was the most reassuring anchor in the world to ordinary disciples.

Jing Chenjun instantly forgot his searing agony and looked toward the origin of the sound. In the skies outside White Jade Capital, radiant luminescence faintly ignited, vaguely coalescing into the monumental silhouette of a [Sundial Clock](# "Literal CN: 日晷钟 (rìguǐ zhōng)").

—"That's a direct disciple of Heavenly Spirit Sect? Are they finally joining the fray as well? Though if I recall, the direct disciples' cultivation levels aren't particularly high..."-The Soul Formation cultivator blinked in surprise. Not quite as thrilled, he glanced at Jing Chenjun before conceding:—"Then again, perhaps not. If even low-tier disciples are this unyielding, the direct disciples must be even more, even more... More... More...?!?"

His words choked violently in his throat.

Because he witnessed the "figures" emerging from the radiance of the Sundial Clock.

It was an endless, suffocating, pitch-black sea of demonic legions stretching beyond the horizon—and amidst the vanguard of that abyssal horde, standing atop a towering command dais, was a peerless beauty so dazzling and incandescent that people dared not gaze upon him directly!

Staring at that unmistakable beauty, Jing Chenjun nearly died of shock on the spot:

[Senior Brother Qu! Which side are you actually on?!]
