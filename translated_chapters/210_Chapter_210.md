# Chapter 210: Immortal Arts Assembly Athletes' Village

Heavenly Spirit Sect, Immortal Arts Assembly Athletes' Village.

—"Whoa, incredible!"

—"Look over there! What is that?"

—"That thing over there looks so fascinating! Shall we go check it out?"

Along the streets of the Athletes' Village, a cluster of female cultivators peered around in wide-eyed wonder, letting out hushed exclamations from time to time.

There was also a senior female cultivator who could barely stomach her martial sisters' gushing, unable to resist chiding:

—"Stop squawking like a flock of birds! You look like a bunch of ignorant country bumpkins who've never seen the world!"

As she scolded them, she nervously glanced at their surroundings.

Truthfully, her anxiety was entirely unfounded.

The passing cultivators appeared completely accustomed to such behavior; most walked past with eyes fixed straight ahead, while those who occasionally glanced their way wore gentle, empathetic smiles—for not long ago, they had worn that exact same 'ignorant bumpkin' expression.

The plain truth was that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's preparations were far too extravagant.

They had actually constructed a dedicated Athletes' Village exclusively for the competitors participating in the Immortal Arts Assembly!

This Athletes' Village was situated amidst the mountain ranges between the Heavenly Spirit Sect and Thousand Star Hall. Sourcing materials on the spot, it was composed of several colossal demonized vines. Dark green and colossal, they entwined around the soaring stone pillars ringing the Heavenly Spirit Sect, resembling an outer palisade of the celestial realm. And the enormous blossoms blooming from these vines clambering up the palisade served as the competitors' living quarters.

This magnificent spectacle was inextricably linked to the fluctuations of spiritual energy around the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Originally, Zhou Hui had secretly reared several demon beasts around his sect for research purposes; affected by the demonic miasma, the surrounding flora had undergone substantial mutation. Then, over a year ago, in order to optimize the spiritual energy circulation around the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Zhou Hui had reconstructed the spiritual energy dam during the Tenth Month Dawn, further stimulating the rampant growth of these demonized plants.

Particularly along the outer perimeter of the Heavenly Spirit Sect: back then, to facilitate the smooth flow of spiritual energy, Zhou Hui had slightly altered the geological structure of the mountains, leaving them barren and jagged like fence posts. Never did anyone anticipate they would become trellises for demonized vines; in a mere six months, those vines had proliferated everywhere, coiling around the desolate stone pillars as though climbing straight to heaven, reminding Zhou Hui of the fairytale *Jack and the Beanstalk* from another world.

Unlike the fairytale, the Heavenly Spirit Sect held zero intention of chopping down these vines. On the contrary, these vines became the finest research specimens for Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples studying demonized botanical mutations. Under the leadership of the direct disciples—particularly Lu Shulong—they dissected and probed these vines from the inside out, tormenting them until the vines wished they could dig up their roots and flee. In the end, having concluded their research, they still refused to let them off; ahead of the Immortal Arts Assembly, squeezing out every scrap of utility, they artificially accelerated the vines to bloom, retrofitting them into competitors' quarters with plans to rent them out to visiting scholars after the tournament.

Setting aside the feelings of the vines, the residential experience here was exceptionally stellar.

First, for the security of the spiritual energy dam, dense formation mist barriers were originally deployed around the "palisade," allowing competitors to enter their living quarters completely undisturbed without fear of prying eyes.

Whether utilizing flying artifacts or ascending the floral spiral staircases along the vines, one could catch the subtle fragrance of vegetation while bathing directly in the rhythmic spiritual currents discharged by the dam. While prolonged closed-door seclusion here might be slightly too 'intense,' for short-term study or pre-battle preparation, it invigorated the spirit and rapidly elevated one's condition to peak form.

Not to mention that standing upon the vines, one could overlook the bustling panoramic view of Thousand Star Hall below, or gaze into the distance at the celestial reflection of the Heavenly Spirit Sect suspended in the sky. In particular, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's sundial clock, shimmering with dazzling brilliance amidst the swirling spiritual currents, resembled a primordial sun that made it impossible to tear one's eyes away.

In contrast, the private spiritual springs inside the rooms, the assortment of automated Dharma artifacts, and the meticulously prepared gourmet buffet cafeteria seemed trivial by comparison—*like hell they were!*

Beyond the breathtaking scenery and rich spiritual energy, it was the thoughtful hospitality down to the minutest detail that moved the tournament competitors to tears.

The vast majority of competitors had never received hospitality of this caliber.

One had to understand: while competitions like the Immortal Arts Assembly were not uncommon across the cultivation world, organizers typically cared solely about the tournament itself, rarely paying attention to backstage logistics, inevitably leaving a catastrophic mess in the wake of every assembly.

Particularly for small and medium sects and loose cultivators.

Lacking influential backing and disregarded in strength, organizers typically treated them as filler props to round out numbers—showing them zero respect, zero care, and zero attention, naturally never bothering to host them properly.

Consequently, it was no exaggeration to say these cultivators practically brought their own rations to compete. Not only did they have to shoulder their own travel fares and living expenses, but once arriving at the venue, they had to scramble to find their own lodging. If funds were tight or luck was poor and they failed to secure a suitable cultivation spot, disrupting their daily breathing cycles, they had already lost half the battle before stepping onto the stage.

Even when encountering wealthy, deep-pocketed organizers who covered all accommodations, matters were rarely much better. No matter how lavish the budget, lodging capacity remained finite, inevitably breeding favoritism; furthermore, when rivals were crammed together, frictions on the stage easily spilled over into brawls off the stage.

In this regard, prestigious major sects fared little better.

They didn't worry about being slighted by organizers, but they engaged in clandestine sabotage over differential treatment and private grudges; a single misstep meant the organizers offended both parties. Consequently, after a single tournament concluded, old hatreds were compounded by a mountain of new grudges, smoothly laying the groundwork for the next tournament's animosity.

By comparison, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Athletes' Village bypassed countless potential headaches right from the outset:

Picturesque scenery, boundless spiritual energy, and egalitarian treatment left all competitors thoroughly devoid of complaints;

Novel public amenities diverted the competitors' focus, preventing them from brooding over petty feuds and even fostering genuine camaraderie through diverse social exchanges;

And continuous, rolling coverage and exclusive interviews from major newspapers made both the elite prodigies of grand sects and lone-wolf rogue cultivators feel deeply valued, simultaneously igniting immense public admiration among the spectators.

Thus, long before the Immortal Arts Assembly officially commenced, public attention was thoroughly captivated by infrastructural wonders like the Athletes' Village.

*(Zhou Hui, whose sole impression of large-scale tournaments came from the Olympic Games: Isn't this just basic standard operation?)*

By comparison, the female cultivators checking in tonight were considered remarkably late to the party. Regarding their starry-eyed marveling, the surrounding cultivators watched them with the amused superiority of internet veterans watching first-timers react to video comments, feeling an odd, vicarious sense of pride.

However, certain cultivators who recognized these women wore peculiar expressions:

—"Aren't those disciples from the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall? Why did they only arrive just now?"

—"The Ten Thousand Beasts Hall?"

—"You don't know them? Are you genuinely a Central Domain cultivator? The Ten Thousand Beasts Hall was once one of the foremost, premier sects of our Central Domain!"

Upon that reminder, the surrounding cultivators finally recalled that the Central Domain indeed harbored such a sect—one that had formerly rivaled the Wanhe Gang for the title of the Central Domain's number one cultivation force.

Though that was hardly surprising.

Over the past year and a half, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had stolen the limelight across every conceivable metric. Sects from every region had converged upon the Central Domain for the sake of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Tireless Immortal Venerable, participating in all manner of Central Domain festivities to the point of appearing closer to the Heavenly Spirit Sect than the local sects, causing native Central Domain factions to become thoroughly overshadowed.

Among them, the Wanhe Gang had fared quite well.

They were originally a coalition assembled from loose cultivators and low-tier practitioners; while vast in numbers and massive in scale, placed in the other four domains they were merely an unruly rabble unfit for polite society. Yet through profound, deep-seated partnerships with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they had secured colossal financial windfalls and expansive networking channels. In particular, their grassroots network reaching deep into the lower strata of cultivators played a pivotal role in establishing courier logistics and transit operations, causing their public reputation to skyrocket.

In contrast, other domains, crowded with arrogant major sects, lacked a syndicate like the Wanhe Gang. Consequently, when loose cultivators in those territories wished to purchase newspapers, academic journals, and specialized tools, the lack of convenient channels sparked immense dissatisfaction among low-tier practitioners, driving many to join the Wanhe Gang and enabling its influence to expand into external domains.

Ah, that was a digression.

In short, factions collaborating closely with the Heavenly Spirit Sect like the Wanhe Gang had reaped massive benefits; whereas sects with zero cooperation like the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall—no, the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall wasn't entirely devoid of cooperation. Half of the dairy milk supply for the Heavenly Spirit Sect's milk tea was provided by the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall! It was merely that this commercial venture expanded so aggressively that numerous outer disciples began questioning whether they had joined a cultivation sect or signed up to herd cattle, causing severe structural crises within a Ten Thousand Beasts Hall unwilling to follow the Heavenly Spirit Sect's reformative footsteps.

Fortunately (?), such 'minor matters' were beneath the notice of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall's Sect Master, Yan Bailang.

Regardless, internal crises within their sect were already rampant; he couldn't perceive what catastrophic impact this issue would have on the sect's trajectory. The sole matter that gnawed at his ego was the precipitous decline in his sect's public prestige.

Yan Bailang felt this caused him immense loss of face—and losing face to the lowly Wanhe Gang was intolerable.

Who could have foreseen it? Back then, he had merely dropped by the Heavenly Spirit Sect to attend the Tenth Month Dawn; yet that visit had somehow enabled the Heavenly Spirit Sect and Wanhe Gang to host an auction, followed by this Immortal Arts Assembly, unconsciously snowballing into the present state of affairs.

Yan Bailang resented the Heavenly Spirit Sect with a burning passion.

Yet while he loathed the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he dared not offend the Tireless Immortal Venerable, and found himself completely incapable of boycotting the Immortal Arts Assembly.

—From an objective standpoint, entering the Immortal Arts Assembly was the single wisest decision Yan Bailang had made, bar none. For only this tournament—strictly restricted to Central Domain cultivators—could draw public attention back to the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall and revive its waning prestige.

Otherwise, what alternative was there? It wasn't as though Yan Bailang could rely on the cultivation manuals he had plundered from his senior sister to deliver a speech at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Academic Hall, could he?!

Precisely due to this conflicted, toxic mindset, Yan Bailang had dragged his feet until the absolute final hour before arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even missing the grand opening ceremonies of the Immortal Arts Assembly.

Naturally, he had zero inkling that he had squandered a golden opportunity to elevate his sect's standing.

A cultivator scoffed in skepticism:

—"What foremost premier sect? With the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Tireless Immortal Venerable standing tall, which sect in the Central Domain dares claim to be foremost?"

—"Speaking of which, this Ten Thousand Beasts Hall didn't even participate in the opening ceremonies, did they?"

—"Indeed! When the athletes marched in, I certainly didn't hear the name of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall called. Are they actually fielding competitors?"

—"Don't speak nonsense. Fairy Xia Bing, the dark horse favored to sweep the Third Arena, hails from the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall! Besides, their sect resides right near the Heavenly Spirit Sect; the spiritual milk in that milk tea you're sipping right now was supplied by them." This came from an exceptionally well-informed cultivator.

—"Then why didn't they attend the opening ceremonies?"

—"Likely because they lived so close that they assumed they had plenty of time, only to end up missing the boat!"

—"Hahaha, can anyone truly be that foolish?"

The gossiping cultivators burst into laughter.

On the other side, the female cultivators of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall had no idea what the crowd was laughing about, but they indeed quieted down.

The woman leading them—the very "Fairy Xia Bing" the crowd had spoken of—noticed this and warmly comforted her martial sisters:

—"Pay them no mind. Let us hurry to our quarters; once we arrive, you can celebrate as much as you like."

Saying this, she cast a soothing look toward the senior sister who had reprimanded the others:

—"There is no need to obsess over the glances of strangers. Rather, the Heavenly Spirit Sect possesses so many rare innovations; having arrived late already, failing to familiarize ourselves with the environment will only leave us further behind."

The woman wanted to protest further, but Xia Bing drew near, whispering gently:

—"Dear Rong Rong, I know you wish to protect me from being berated by the Sect Master upon our return. But hasn't the Heavenly Spirit Sect separated male and female accommodations? No matter how formidable his reach, he cannot bypass the Heavenly Spirit Sect's protective wards. We don't need to remain so wound up."

—"However, who knows if there are his informants lurking among these girls?"-Rong Rong puffed out her cheeks, glaring at her martial sisters. Yet she was inherently kind-hearted, quickly sighing in defeat,-"Damn it, it's all the Sect Master's fault! Why has he become so paranoid and erratic lately? The entire sect has been turned into a foul cesspool; I practically want to advise him to [sever his red dragon](# "Literal CN: 斩赤龙 (zhǎn chìlóng) — Daoist internal alchemy term for halting the 'red dragon' (menstruation); here used sarcastically to tell Yan Bailang to cure his erratic mood swings / male menopause")!"

—"Hey! Rong Rong!"

—"Relax, I'm not an idiot; I wouldn't actually say that to his face. Besides, unlike you, an insignificant nobody like me never gets the chance to speak to that exalted Sect Master."

Rong Rong wrinkled her nose.

Xia Bing breathed a sigh of relief, followed by a bitter smile.

She had chided Rong Rong for being wound up, but in truth, the one whose nerves were stretched to the breaking point was herself. Yet given Yan Bailang's psychotic state, how could Xia Bing afford *not* to be on edge?

A mere six months felt like an entire sixty-year cycle, ushering in earth-shattering evolutions for the Heavenly Spirit Sect, rendering it increasingly magnificent and formidable.

Yet the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall had remained stagnant—no, worse than stagnant, it was actively regressing.

Particularly that bastard Yan Bailang!

Xia Bing thought in despair, gazing toward the dazzling sundial clock suspended in the distance.

Six months ago, when Yan Bailang had ordered Xia Bing and the others into the Beast Soul Tower to temper their strength for the Immortal Arts Assembly, Xia Bing had faintly sensed something amiss. It was merely that back then, she had been agonizing over the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* passed to her by that 'demon,' unsure if she ought to practice it in secret; coupled with the trials of the Heart-Interrogating Stone, she had harbored guilt toward her sect master, doubting her own judgment.

Yet six months later, Xia Bing harbored zero doubts.

Because nearly all the martial siblings who had entered the Beast Soul Tower alongside her had met with catastrophe! Even those who survived had their cultivation crippled—yet this had nothing to do with the Beast Soul Tower. The beasts within were not terrifying at all (the most formidable had long since been devoured by a certain Taotie, its True Immortal blood plundered); rather, they had perished outside the tower!

Such attrition was undeniably unnatural.

Furthermore, Xia Bing had uncovered bizarre notes inside the tower, suspected to have been left by disciples who had undergone the trials in bygone eras. The journals denounced the persecution inflicted by the sect master of their time and questioned the foundational orthodoxy of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, validating Xia Bing's darkest suspicions.

Most crucially, Xia Bing had palpably felt Yan Bailang's latent killing intent directed at her person.

Xia Bing had survived to this day partly due to her meticulous caution in dodging traps, and partly thanks to the Immortal Arts Assembly! Her dazzling performance in the preliminaries had earned her a massive fanbase; newspapers frequently interviewed her, making Yan Bailang hesitant to strike directly lest he tarnish his public standing, coupled with his narcissistic desire to bask in her reflected glory during the tournament.

However, once the Immortal Arts Assembly concluded, all bets were off.

—"There must be a reckoning at this assembly..."-Xia Bing muttered.

Yet how could a newly minted Golden Core cultivator contend with an elder at the Body Integration stage? Even her Golden Core breakthrough had been achieved by secretly practicing the genuine *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* under the guise of the tower trials. Her foremost priority was stabilizing her foundation, not reckless elevation; even with forced breakthroughs, matching Yan Bailang in the short term was impossible.

The mere thought filled Xia Bing with despair.

Dominated by such dark thoughts, Xia Bing held zero interest in sightseeing around the Athletes' Village, only snapping back to reality when Rong Rong called out to her:

—"...Senior Sister Xia, we've arrived!"

—"Hm?"

Xia Bing lifted her eyes, realizing she had unconsciously arrived at the designated suite assigned by the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Aside from Rong Rong watching her with concern, the other disciples had already begun enthusiastically exploring their tournament quarters:

—"There's even a private meditation chamber!"

—"Heavens, the spiritual energy here feels richer than my immortal cave!"

—"This set of musical instruments is so neat; it switches melodies automatically! I want one!"

—"This censer is amazing too; it has a timer and reloads incense on its own! I wonder where one can buy this."

—"I prefer this cold storage cabinet—look, there's milk tea!"

The female cultivators of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall resumed their lively, chattering demeanor.

They looked less like warriors arriving for a blood-soaked tournament, and more like tourists on vacation.

Xia Bing had no idea if there were informants planted by Yan Bailang among them; even if there were, they were thoroughly assimilated at this moment.

Hardly surprising: Yan Bailang treated the Heavenly Spirit Sect like a venomous scourge, strictly forbidding disciples from interacting with them over the past six months, rendering the disciples living nearest to the Heavenly Spirit Sect the most provincial hicks. Any minor discovery was enough to thrill them for hours.

Xia Bing found herself envying their carefree bliss.

A year and a half ago, she had been one of them, dreaming only of sneaking off to play in Thousand Star Hall's secret realm, even plotting to abandon the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall to join the Heavenly Spirit Sect—until she encountered that 'demon,' crossed paths with the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and embarked on a path of rapid promotion...

At this thought, Xia Bing shivered violently.

She suddenly recalled: that 'demon' had been encountered at the Heavenly Spirit Sect! Could it be lurking nearby right now?

How had that creature acquired her sect's core technique, the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, in the first place?

Xia Bing dared not ponder further. Glancing left and right, she refrained from dampening her martial sisters' enthusiasm, whispering to Rong Rong:

—"I feel somewhat fatigued... Fortunately, there are no obligations tonight. Everyone can familiarize themselves with the premises; shall we convene tomorrow to discuss tournament strategies?"

—"Senior Sister Xia is indeed exhausted? No wonder you've been so absentminded. You should definitely rest early!"-Rong Rong agreed anxiously.

Though ignorant of the darker truth, Rong Rong had sensed the suffocating atmosphere within the sect, shielding Xia Bing throughout their journey and guiding her forward now.

As a formerly premier sect, the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall boasted a substantial delegation; on the female side alone, there were competitors spanning multiple cultivation tiers, accompanied by an entourage of attendants.

For factions of this scale, the Heavenly Spirit Sect allocated dedicated residential wings equipped with communal lounges, kitchens, and parlors, while competitors' private quarters branched upward along the vines, boasting isolated meditation suites to ensure total privacy.

During Xia Bing's daze, Rong Rong had clearly gathered the necessary intelligence:

—"Senior Sister Xia, your room is here. I heard the door is protected by a ward; only you can open it."

—"Understood."

Xia Bing replied absentmindedly, resting her palm against the door.

Without applying force, the door swung open smoothly, and a gentle female voice chimed through the chamber:

—"Honored Fairy Xia Bing, welcome to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. We wish you glorious triumph and ideal results in the Immortal Arts Assembly."

Accompanying the voice, a cascade of violet light particles materialized from the void, congealing into a hovering violet card before Xia Bing's eyes. Depicted upon the card was none other than Xia Bing herself, majestically commanding her insect swarm.

—"Whoa! It's a 4-Star Card! Everyone holds high hopes for you!"-Rong Rong gasped.

The other disciples crowded around instantly:

—"Senior Sister Xia's card has manifested already?"

—"Starting at four stars right off the bat?"

—"Honestly, I feel Senior Sister deserves five stars!"

Envy danced across their eyes as they gazed upon the card in Xia Bing's hand.

This was the symbol of an athlete's prestige.

While reaching the finals virtually guaranteed a cultivator qualification to enter the current season's Immortal Chess expansion representing the Central Domain's elite, popularity diverged drastically as preliminaries progressed.

In response, the devious planners of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had seized the commercial opportunity, launching collectible gacha cards tied to the Immortal Chess system, capturing contestants' finest combat moments, assigning star ratings, and releasing them as blind card packs to drive spectators into spending frenzies for their idols.

During the preliminaries, the scheme was subtle; with thousands of contestants, most cards were 1-Star and 2-Star white cards that could easily be traded, and many treated it as a collection mini-game without realizing the predatory malice behind blind packs.

Yet the finals changed everything.

The organizers announced that contestants advancing through the preliminaries would receive their first 'tier ascension,' upgrading cards to 3-Star, 4-Star, or 5-Star tiers based on public popularity, with competitors fighting to elevate their cards into shimmering rainbow-tier "Heaven Cards"—with market value soaring exponentially with each ascension.

Xia Bing receiving 4-Star status from the outset proved her combat prowess was widely recognized.

—"I heard 4-Star status includes perks?"-a disciple asked eagerly.

—"Perks? Is it this?"

Xia Bing hid nothing. Sweeping the card with her divine sense, she confirmed her soul link with the artifact, mastering its operational mechanics and discovering a bundled Scenario Card:

> **[Insect Hive]**  
> **Scenario Card: 2-Star**  
> **Effect: Insect Swarm Capacity +50**

—"What, that isn't particularly formidable."

—"Indeed, fifty additional insects can be stored in an extra spatial pouch; it doesn't compare to Senior Sister Xia's personal Dharma treasures."

—"Naturally! The perk merely reflects popularity; if bundled bonuses were overpowered, wouldn't that compromise tournament fairness?"

The disciples discussed with slight disappointment.

Yet Xia Bing and Rong Rong exchanged a covert glance.

Both had visited Thousand Star Hall's secret realm and experienced similar trial mechanics, understanding that a Scenario Card was never just a meaningless trinket. Having one meant there were many, indicating a comprehensive acquisition and upgrade tree.

*It seems this Immortal Arts Assembly isn't merely a contest of raw brute force!*

*Acquiring more scenario cards to tilt battlefield conditions in one's favor is also a victory condition.*

Xia Bing deduced.

She suddenly felt a pang of nostalgia for the Thousand Star Secret Realm.

With Yan Bailang's psychological deterioration, disciples had long been barred from visiting the Heavenly Spirit Sect's realm.

Yet back then, it was precisely by simulating her insect swarm's evolution inside the secret realm that Xia Bing had mapped out her optimal cultivation path without a master's guidance, subsequently practicing the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* without straying into deviation or exposing herself to Yan Bailang.

Even within the Beast Soul Tower, Xia Bing had endured the grueling trials by imagining defeating beast souls to earn experience points and loot—truthfully, the Beast Soul Tower was far better suited for gamified leveling than the Thousand Star Secret Realm.

Alas, Xia Bing could not voice these insights to her peers.

If the Sect Master discovered her lingering attachment to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, disaster would strike.

Moreover, Xia Bing harbored her own private wish: she desperately wished her sect master would perish on the stage in sheer ignorance. Yet that was virtually impossible—this was merely a 'tournament'; slaying a competitor, let alone the master of a faction like the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, would trigger a catastrophic scandal rocking the cultivation realm.

Sighing inwardly, Xia Bing turned her attention back to the tournament mechanics:

*How does one upgrade these 'Scenario Cards'...?*

—"Ah, here is the tournament bracket?"

Xia Bing stepped deeper into the room.

Mounted upon the wall was an expansive tournament bracket for the Golden Core division, clearly displaying Xia Bing's upcoming opponents, scheduled match times, and an automated alert chime to ensure no competitor missed their duel.

Xia Bing was not prone to carelessness, yet gazing upon the bracket, her serene composure cracked into genuine anxiety:

—"Fairy Mo Bei of the Heavenly Spirit Sect... has also advanced to Golden Core?!"

—"No way!"-Rong Rong shrieked.

The other disciples surged forward, staring dumbfounded at the miniature portrait upon the bracket:

—"She actually broke through as well?!"

—"We assumed Senior Sister Xia could bypass her by advancing to Golden Core!"

—"However, she only recently broke through; her foundation might not be stable, and she won't be accustomed to Golden Core combat mechanics."

—"The exact same applies to our Senior Sister!"

The disciples broke into agitated chatter, many wearing looks of dread, while a select few breathed sighs of relief—mostly Foundation Establishment competitors.

Clearly, nobody wanted to face Mo Bei.

This Little Junior Sister of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had carved a bloody reputation across the preliminaries, becoming everyone's living nightmare; nobody wished to cross paths with her in the arena.

Xia Bing felt the exact same way.

Particularly since her milk tea dealings brought her into contact with Yu Feifei under Mo Bei's command, subjecting her to endless propaganda slogans like "Junior Sister Mo Bei is invincible across the world" and "Junior Sister Mo Bei reigns supreme for ten thousand generations." She felt Mo Bei's rabid fanbase alone was enough to drown her before the match even started.

It was hard to say whether Xia Bing had felt relieved when her breakthrough bumped her from the Foundation Establishment bracket into the theoretically harsher Golden Core arena, purely because it spared her from facing that 'Heavenly Spirit Sect Little Junior Sister.'

*So why on earth did that girl break through too?!*

—"Fortunately, our bracket seeds are far apart; we won't cross paths until the semifinals at the earliest,"-Xia Bing studied the bracket, comforting herself.

—"Right, right! That's ages away; perhaps she'll get eliminated before the semifinals!"-Rong Rong chimed in.

She didn't specify *who* would be eliminated.

The disciples deliberately skirted the subject of Mo Bei, discussing tournament schedules before dispersing.

—Having just arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, their excitement was palpable; compared to stressful duels, endless wonders awaited their exploration.

Xia Bing, true to her words to Rong Rong, felt thoroughly drained and prepared for rest.

Yet it was hardly rest.

Once everyone departed, Xia Bing sat alone in her meditation chamber.

With heavy burdens weighing upon her heart, sleep was impossible; furthermore, Golden Core cultivators held little need for slumber. Thinking of the Sect Master's covetous gaze upon her beast soul, Xia Bing burned to dedicate every second to enhancing her strength.

Yet before she could commence her breathing cycle, her tournament card floated into the air once more, displaying a glowing inquiry:

> **Enter Trial Mode?**

—"What is Trial Mode?"-Xia Bing murmured in bewilderment.

Yet she selected 'Yes.'

After all, she had been pondering how to acquire stronger Scenario Cards; she would never refuse an opportunity to decipher tournament mechanics.

The instant Xia Bing locked in her choice, a gust of wind swept across the room. Blinking her eyes, she discovered her surroundings had transformed entirely.

She stood upon a desolate floating platform. In an instant, Xia Bing understood that this was a combat stage, and across from her stood her opponent—her 'enemy.'

Fearlessly lifting her gaze, Xia Bing froze dead in her tracks:

—"Mo Bei... Excellency?!"
