—"Sis, I told you to put on an act—I didn't tell you to play for keeps!"-Mo Xi yelled frantically.

When Zhou Hui arrived at the 'staff-only backstage' of the Thousand Stars Secret Realm, he saw Mo Xi pacing around in a frantic frenzy, resembling a [scumbag game planner](<# "Literal CN: 狗策划 (gǒu cèhuà) — 'dog planner/game dev', popular Chinese gaming slang for despised dev teams who introduce game-breaking bugs or predatory monetization">) who just watched players uncover a game-breaking bug on the launch day of a new version. He desperately wished he could immediately [roll back the database](<# "Literal CN: 回滚数据库 — database rollback to pre-patch state">) to before the update and distribute a [ten-pull gacha compensation](<# "Literal CN: 发个十连抽 — compensating players with a 10-pull gacha roll to pacify outrage">) to make all the players collectively suffer amnesia.

Unfortunately, his elder sister had no database on her body for him to restore.

Mo Bei sat silently on the ground. Her naturally fair skin appeared even more crystal-clear. In her heterochromatic eyes—identical to Mo Xi's—the darker one seemed to have lost all its luster, resembling a bottomless abyss, while the lighter eye had faded to an even paler hue, taking on the rigid texture of frozen ice. Her entire person truly resembled a [crashed server](<# "Literal CN: 死机的服务器 — crashed computer/server suffering total paralysis">), completely unresponsive.

Mo Bei hadn't actually crashed, of course; she was suppressing murderous intent.

Toward her younger brother, toward everyone else, toward this entire world.

A sinister, chilling voice looped ceaselessly deep within her consciousness:

[—"Kill him! He's just a younger brother—how dare he order you around!"](<# "Speaker: Sinister Voice / Carp Spirit">)

[—"Kill them! People who don't love you have no right to exist!"](<# "Speaker: Sinister Voice / Carp Spirit">)

[—"Kill everyone! Nobody cares about your existence anyway, so you don't need to care about them either!"](<# "Speaker: Sinister Voice / Carp Spirit">)

Accompanied by this voice, Mo Bei felt a thick, viscous malice flowing from her brainstem straight down into her spine, filling her with a genuine urge to draw her sword.

Yet Mo Xi stubbornly pressed closer anyway:

—"Hey, Sis? Are you listening to me? Mo Bei?"-Mo Xi pressed on, refusing to give up.

[You idiot! Don't come near me!]

Mo Bei stared with wide, expressionless eyes. Her ice-colored pupil reflected Mo Xi's face—so similar to hers, yet richly expressive.

His face wore an expression of disdain, but his eyes were filled with tension.

Ah, so he had noticed something was wrong. Naturally. Usually, their emotions were linked; at a time like this, there was no way he couldn't feel it.

He felt the killing intent and still leaned in... wasn't that even more idiotic?!

Just as Mo Bei could barely contain herself from gripping the hilt of her sword, a pair of hands reached over, scruffing the napes of the twin siblings' necks on either side and pulling them apart.

At that very instant, Mo Bei felt the voice deep within her consciousness recede into the distance, as if muffled behind a thick barrier of ice, faint and indistinct.

Mo Bei instantly breathed a sigh of relief.

She relaxed her body and lifted her head:

—"Master,"-she said.

At a moment like this, the only one who could save her was Master after all.

Zhou Hui stood right there. He let go of Mo Xi, but he didn't release his grip on the scruff of Mo Bei's neck, merely asking:

—"Bei'er, feeling any better?"

—"...It's not as noisy,"-Mo Bei answered slowly.

Mo Xi rubbed the back of his neck, looking rather regretful that Master hadn't kept holding him.

However, now was not the time to think about that. Though they spent most of their time bickering and rolling their eyes at each other, to Mo Xi, Mo Bei was irreplaceable family.

—"Master, what happened to Mo Bei?"-Mo Xi asked worriedly.

—"Bei'er's cultivation isn't sufficient to suppress that carp,"-Zhou Hui asked, puzzled.-"Logically speaking, you shouldn't be able to subdue it right now either. How did you get it to recognize you as its master?"

—"Exactly! I only told you to put on an act!"-Mo Xi brought up the old complaint again.-"What are we going to do from now on?"

Mo Bei rolled her eyes at Mo Xi and answered Zhou Hui honestly:

—"I don't know either. At the time, I merely drew my sword, and that carp pounced straight at me..."

Just as Mo Xi said, Mo Bei was only "performing."

After all, Zhou Hui had known from the very beginning that what was suppressed beneath the Ice Eye was a carp on the verge of transforming into a dragon.

Although a carp was a mortal creature, it differed from other mortal beasts by naturally carrying a touch of ability to manipulate fortune and control space. Therefore, unlike other demonic creatures that had to cultivate all the way into Demon Kings—equivalent to a human's Tribulation Transcendence realm—before having a chance to ascend, a carp only needed to gather the Five-Colored Heavenly Flames, burn away its own tail, and it could transform into a dragon at the Soul Formation stage and ascend directly to the upper realm.

In other words, this carp inside the Ice Eye possessed at least Soul Formation cultivation.

Yet this secret realm strictly restricted cultivation to the Golden Core stage and below.

Clearly, the creator of the secret realm never intended for anyone to undo this seal, let alone defeat the carp.

In the Heavenly Dao's script, the protagonist possessed the God-Slaying Sword—a lethal artifact capable of heavily injuring even Zhou Hui (albeit a Zhou Hui who had dropped his guard and completely relaxed his divine soul), which testified to its terrifying power—as his cheat to break open the seal.

Even after breaking the seal, the protagonist struggled immensely against this carp. It was only when Mo Bei, who was afflicted with cold poison and had suppressed her cultivation despite secretly possessing Nascent Soul strength, unleashed her full power that the creature was finally slain.

Of course, immediately afterward, Mo Bei's cold poison flared up, forcing her into yin-yang harmony with the protagonist, gifting several centuries of her suppressed cultivation all at once to him.

Ah, getting off topic.

In short, from this script alone, one could tell that even the biased Heavenly Dao couldn't allow a low-cultivation cultivator to slay the carp spirit with just a divine artifact; external assistance was mandatory, demonstrating its sheer ferocity.

Zhou Hui had no intention of exposing cultivators exploring the secret realm to pointless danger either.

Thus, the restriction he set beneath the Ice Eye possessed an automatic repair function. Even if someone breached the restriction, it would only release the carp for a few minutes before the restriction automatically repaired itself and sealed the carp once more.

Mo Xi, however, felt this self-repairing restriction and the carp spirit itself could be put to good use.

During those few minutes when the seal was broken, the carp spirit could serve perfectly as the secret realm's final BOSS, dealing a crushing blow to the explorers (players), leaving them with deep regret and guiding them onto the level-grinding path of a [second playthrough](<# "Literal CN: 二周目 (èr zhōumù) — New Game Plus / second playthrough in gaming">).

If those players couldn't even survive the few minutes until the restriction repaired itself, Mo Bei would step in and contribute a [story cutscene](<# "Literal CN: 剧情动画 (jùqíng dònghuà) — scripted cinematic / in-game cutscene">), holding out until the timer ran down to seal the carp spirit once more.

It would also conveniently provide an opportunity to collect data on the carp spirit.

This proposal had earned Zhou Hui's approval.

Zhou Hui was never the type who, upon hearing the prophecy that "the child would be pricked by a spindle on a spinning wheel and die," would go and burn all the spinning wheels. On the contrary, knowing that Mo Bei might be fated in her destiny to slay that carp, he felt that getting plenty of early practice was actually a great idea.

Besides, the current Mo Bei had never contracted cold poison.

Taking ten thousand steps back, even if she did catch cold poison one day, it would be far better for her to know how to handle a flare-up herself rather than foolishly waiting for someone else to "rescue" her.

Hmm? Did he just discover another way his pedagogical philosophy diverged from the Heavenly Dao's script?

Zhou Hui wondered to himself.

In any case, the first half of today's raid plan had unfolded roughly according to Mo Xi's blueprint.

The explorers reached the Ice Eye and cracked the restriction.

Although Qingyang Yang of Eternal Villa brought out his clan ancestor's natal magical treasure, his cultivation wasn't remotely sufficient to activate it. Instead, he performed even worse than usual and was defeated in a single instant.

At that point, Mo Bei "had no choice" but to step in.

Originally, all she needed to do was keep the carp occupied until the restriction rebooted to seal it again. Who could have imagined that the carp would charge straight at her and swiftly slide to its knees to recognize her as its master?

Mo Bei hadn't even had time to react.

—"That's not recognizing a master—that's possession!"-Mo Xi commented.

—"No, it's a master-servant contract,"-Zhou Hui said, having inspected Mo Bei's condition.-"If it were possession, Bei'er wouldn't have been able to suppress that murderous intent so easily just now—of course, Bei'er isn't that easily possessed in the first place. Logically speaking, had it been a harmful act, it would have been repelled instead. It was precisely because recognizing a master is completely harmless that it succeeded so easily."

—"Then what was with the situation just now...?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"It seems that after completing the master-servant contract, for whatever reason, that carp regretted its decision and wanted to backlash against its master,"-Zhou Hui observed.

—"Now that Master mentions it, the moment the contract formed, I truly did feel a wave of intense remorse,"-Mo Bei said.

Yet what on earth made a thousand-year carp spirit abruptly submit, only to immediately regret it?

At this point, Master and his two disciples fell silent for a moment.

As humans, they simply couldn't fathom what went through the capricious mind of a carp.

Mo Xi suggested:

—"Why don't we just ask it directly! That carp is about to turn into a dragon; it must have developed spiritual intelligence by now."

—"I can't converse with it,"-Mo Bei replied. She was already lucky not to suffer spiritual pollution.

Zhou Hui closed his eyes in silence.

Immediately, Mo Bei bit her lower lip expressionlessly, while several shrill shrieks erupted from her shadow, accompanied by ripples of bloody crimson spreading outward.

Without a doubt, those were the agonizing shrieks of the carp spirit.

This fish possessed remarkably exceptional spatial manipulation abilities. After submitting, it had concealed itself within Mo Bei's shadow, utterly unwilling to reveal itself before anyone else. Only someone like Zhou Hui could locate it directly.

Yet Zhou Hui shook his head:

—"No good."

—"Even Master can't tame it?"-Mo Xi asked in shock.

—"Its IQ is too low, completely uneducated and uncouth; there is no way to reason with it,"-Zhou Hui said with a frown.-"Yet I cannot severely injure it, as doing so would affect its 'master.'"

—"Then can we forcibly sever the contract?"-Mo Bei asked.

—"We could, but it would be a bit of a waste. It is rare for such a powerful spirit beast to voluntarily bind itself to you,"-Zhou Hui said, frowning.-"If you can tame it and raise it properly, it could even transform into a dragon, and your cultivation would multiply."

Ascending would not even be an issue.

`"..."` Mo Bei remained expressionless.

Clearly, she wasn't interested.

However, if Master wanted her to raise this fish, she wouldn't refuse.

Mo Xi, on the other hand, was quite enticed:

—"Master, how do we tame it?"

Zhou Hui pondered for a moment:

—"Come to think of it, Bei'er's swordsmanship has reached a milestone; it's about time to advance her cultivation slightly. As it happens, when the new term begins, we'll be selecting cultivation manuals and magical treasures for the disciples. Bei'er, you should participate as well!"

—"But there are still several days before the new term starts!"-Mo Xi said worriedly (and jealously), eyeing Zhou Hui's hand still holding the scruff of Mo Bei's neck.-"What do we do in the meantime? Should Master seal that carp?"

—"It would be better for Bei'er to get used to the carp's presence during this time... However, letting it influence her won't do either. We must devise a method that keeps Bei'er safe from its backlash while keeping it in check,"-Zhou Hui said. He sized Mo Bei up for a few minutes and nodded.-"Let's just use [species suppression](<# "Literal CN: 种族压制 (zhǒngzú yāzhì) — biological / species suppression, where a natural predator instinctively terrorizes its prey">)!"

With those words, Mo Xi's eyes went wide, for he watched his elder sister begin to transform in Master's hand.

She turned into a creature with heterochromatic ice-blue and black eyes, pitch-black fur, with white only on her belly and paws...

A cat?!

—"Why on earth?!"-Mo Xi wailed.

Zhou Hui glanced at his youngest disciple, uncomprehending, assuming he was worried about his sister:

—"Rest assured. Once the cultivation manuals are chosen, I'll restore Bei'er to her original form. There won't be any lingering aftereffects."

—"That's not what I meant..."-Mo Xi lamented mournfully.

Mo Bei... the [black clouds draped over snow](<# "Literal CN: 乌云覆雪 (wūyún fù xuě) — traditional Chinese poetic name for a tuxedo cat with black coat on top and white belly/paws">) tuxedo cat shot her younger brother a look of pure contempt:

[Idiot.]

Fortunately (?), no matter the vacation, it was always short.

The New Year holiday was no exception.

Mo Bei hadn't spent many days as a cat before the Heavenly Spirit Sect "started school."

Yet the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were not depressed in the slightest; instead, they eagerly anticipated the start of the semester with immense excitement—not even the opening-of-term exams could dampen their anticipation.

Because this semester, they were finally going to test their spirit roots and allocate cultivation manuals!

In other sects, this was something done the moment one entered the gate, but at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the disciples had been made to study for a full half-year before this began. How could they not gaze through autumn waters in desperate yearning?

However, precisely because of this half-year of study, the disciples had developed a fundamental understanding of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's cultivation techniques:

The Heavenly Spirit Sect primarily centered on the *Five Spirits Technique* created by Zhou Hui himself. This was a breathing method, the foundation of all cultivation techniques, universally applicable to Five-Element Spirit Roots. When practicing this technique, disciples could cycle spiritual qi with heaven and earth far more efficiently; its cultivation speed ranked among the very finest of breathing methods, making it mandatory core heart-law for anyone entering the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Disciples in the beginner class practiced it as their foundational cultivation upon entering the sect.

After practicing the *Five Spirits Technique* for half a year, they could select an auxiliary cultivation technique.

Here, another perk of having an [involution champion](<# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — king of involution / extreme overachiever">) master shone through: because Zhou Hui himself had learned extensively and eclectically, his foundational technique could harmonize with virtually any cultivation art, supporting one another.

Coupled with the vast collection in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion—excluding certain exclusive secret techniques strictly forbidden from theft by other sects—disciples could find almost any technique they wished to study, enough to dazzle their eyes.

Consequently, some disciples had already made up their minds, while others wavered back and forth, not knowing how to choose.

—"I want the Heavenly Spirit Sword Technique!"-Cheng Ziyi declared firmly. Having spent half a year in practical training, he was now thoroughly [molded into the shape of a sword cultivator](<# "Literal CN: 剑修的形状 — internet meme: molded/conditioned into the shape of X">).-"Now that we've come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, what's the point if we don't learn the Heavenly Spirit Sword Technique?"

—"As long as it's a Wood-attribute technique, that's fine with me,"-his roommate Ji Gen said. He seemed indecisive, but had practically made up his mind.-"Though my weapon must be a whip."

—"I haven't decided yet,"-Huang Xiao said with a guilty conscience. After all, it wasn't human and didn't know if the Library Pavilion had a technique suitable for it—there should be, right? There were so many books here, and it had once found an encyclopedia of demon clan spells.

More worried than Huang Xiao was Jing Chenjun. He suspected that what might be exposed was not just his identity, but whether he would be expelled from the Heavenly Spirit Sect altogether. Thus he asked anxiously:

—"How are we supposed to choose? Do they test our spirit roots first, or what?"

[May Heaven protect me, please don't let them test spirit roots first!]

[—"Yeah, I heard the same,"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)-someone chimed in from the side.-[—"Even if it's not talismans, suitable manuals will react to suitable candidates and fly straight over."](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

The one who reached Nascent Soul at a hundred years old... when he walked through his sect's library pavilion, an ancient immortal's posthumous manual flew down,"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)-someone gossiped.-[—"Rosy dawn covered half the sky."](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple">)

Hearing them say this, Huang Xiao and Jing Chenjun grew even more nervous.

—"Doesn't that mean the manuals choose the person?"-Jing Chenjun asked.-"What if no manual chooses me?"

Huang Xiao nodded frantically beside him.

Huang Xiao was not only worried that no manual would pick it, but also terrified that an *Instruction Manual for Demon Cultivation* would come flying straight at its head and expose its true identity.

The other disciples also looked tense. After all, anyone would be anxious on such an occasion, terrified of finding no suitable technique.

—"It won't happen,"-Cheng Ziyi comforted Jing Chenjun.-"Even if you have trash... Five-Element Spirit Roots, so does the Immortal Venerable! He will definitely have a suitable technique for you here."

[The problem is, I don't even have trash spirit roots!] Jing Chenjun thought in dread.

—"Worst comes to worst, isn't the Five Spirits Technique cushioning our fall?"-Cheng Ziyi laughed heartily.-"At the absolute worst, we can learn from Senior Brother Yuwen and advance all the way to Soul Formation purely on breathing methods."

[Is Eldest Senior Brother's path something human beings can emulate?]

[He can even be tied into a butterfly bow and hung from a tree—can you?!]

Cheng Ziyi's comforting words had zero effect.

In fact, half an hour later, Cheng Ziyi himself stood before the Book-Seeking Altar with trembling knees.

—"Junior Brother, write a character,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"Based on the character you write, the manual you desire will fly down to find you."

[So why on earth is it writing a character?!]

[Will a sword manual really fly down upon seeing my handwriting?]

Never in his short life had Cheng Ziyi regretted slacking off on his calligraphy more than at this moment.

Yet there was no retreating. He could only grip the brush and, using the strength of his entire body, write down his character:

"Sword" (剑).

Because he used too much force, he tore through the paper on the knife radical (`刂`), so he had to patch it up with another stroke, leaving an ugly black blob that looked more like a ghost talisman than ever.

Writing this character, Cheng Ziyi could hardly bear to look at Qu Qianxing's expression beside him.

Letting the handsome Senior Brother Qu see such handwriting was agonizing enough of a [social death](<# "Literal CN: 社死 (shèsǐ) — public embarrassment / social death">).

What was even more socially fatal was writing this character in front of everyone.

Yet the moment he finished writing this character, a sphere of golden light enveloped Cheng Ziyi.

Surprised, Cheng Ziyi lifted his head and saw a ray of sword radiance slashing straight toward him. He wanted to dodge, but stubbornly held his ground, reaching for his own sword instead. His movements were too slow, however, and that sword radiance had already cleaved into his forehead.

Cheng Ziyi felt his mind churn in chaos, as if filled with a vast amount of previously unknown knowledge.

Sensing that knowledge carefully, he suddenly understood:

Sword forms!

The sword forms of the Heavenly Spirit Sword!

—"I succeeded! I've learned the Heavenly Spirit Sword!"-Cheng Ziyi cheered.

Fortunately, Qu Qianxing was right beside him.

—"Congratulations,"-the Second Senior Brother of the Heavenly Spirit Sect smiled gently.-"However, you only 'know' the sword technique now. You must practice diligently over a long period before you can master it. Otherwise, even if you know the sword moves, they will be just like your calligraphy—completely unfit to be seen in public!"

This bucket of cold water instantly drenched Cheng Ziyi back to reality on the spot.

Qu Qianxing looked coldly and mercilessly at the other disciples:

—"Next, Ji Gen."

Ji Gen was actually quite similar to Cheng Ziyi; he had already chosen whip techniques.

And Zhou Hui was proficient in virtually all weapons—in Zhou Hui's own words, having lived so long, it was only natural to dabble in every weapon, and even more natural to write an experiential summary (cultivation manual). Furthermore, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's martial skills hadn't rejected even Cheng Ziyi's atrocious handwriting, so they naturally wouldn't turn away Ji Gen, who had only practiced writing for half a year. Ji Gen successfully obtained the *Heavenly Spirit Whip Technique*.

However, Ji Gen's greatest fortuitous encounter wasn't just this.

—"Hmm? Cultivating mutated plants inside one's own body?"-Mengshan Old Freak commented, spotting the flower blooming atop Ji Gen's head out of sheer excitement.-"Untiring, this disciple of yours is rather interesting!"

—"If you find him interesting, I could have him work as an assistant for you?"-Zhou Hui smiled.

Mengshan Old Freak glared at Zhou Hui.

The reason this old freak was still loitering at the Heavenly Spirit Sect was that he had been frightened by that demonic imprint.

—Anyone would feel a chill in their heart upon learning they had been possessed by an unknown entity for who knew how many years. But the Heavenly Spirit Sect possessed the strongest Untiring Immortal Venerable and Divine Physician Lan Pei, so Mengshan Old Freak decided to stay at the sect for a while until he was certain there were no lingering issues before departing.

Today, Zhou Hui having a disciple "run errands" for him was tantamount to having Mengshan Old Freak mentor this disciple.

After all, this was an old monster of the Mahayana stage!

Yet Mengshan Old Freak looked at Ji Gen and reluctantly nodded:

—"Then let him come!"

Ji Gen stood bewildered, completely unaware of how monumental a fortuitous opportunity he had just received.

Next up after Ji Gen was Huang Xiao, who walked up with a guilty conscience and wrote the character "Human" (人).

Huang Xiao heard a scoff of ridicule. Looking up, it saw a colossal maw gaping open toward it, terrifying it so badly that it tumbled right off the platform. Afterward, without waiting for Qu Qianxing to say anything, it slinked back to its seat in disgrace.

—"What manual did you get?"-seeing its state, Cheng Ziyi asked curiously.

—"N-Nothing,"-Huang Xiao shook its head, but let out a silly grin.-"Just a recipe book."

—"A recipe book?"-Cheng Ziyi couldn't understand.

Huang Xiao, however, grinned like a fool, looking down at the item that had appeared in its embrace:

It was a pinch of something resembling flatbread crumbs.

Yet Huang Xiao knew: that was the aura of a Taotie.

For the demon clan, there was no better cultivation material than the aura of an ancient ferocious beast. Relying on their instincts alone, they could embark upon the path of cultivation.

(On the high platform, Zhou Hui glanced at the little black lamb beside his hand.

The little lamb snorted:

—"Consider it my [mending the fold after losing sheep](<# "Literal CN: 亡羊补牢 (wángyáng-bǔláo) — mending the fold after the sheep are lost; humorous pun since Yuwen Xuan is currently transformed into a sheep">). With my legacy, the Delicacy Pavilion had better not fail at cooking meals!")

After Huang Xiao came Jing Chenjun.

Watching his roommates all receive the outcomes they hoped for, a flicker of daring hope sprouted in his heart. With a fluttering heart, he walked up and wrote down his character:

"Immortal" (仙).

In that instant, Jing Chenjun witnessed his entire journey in pursuit of the immortal path:

Being rejected everywhere in search of immortality; arriving unexpectedly in the Central Domain; entering the Heavenly Spirit Sect; doing hands-on practice in the Artifact Refinement Hall; interning at the auction house; earning his teacher certification; instructing mortal children... Finally, he sat upon an imperial throne, looking down at the imperial jade seal before him.

The illusion vanished at that point.

Jing Chenjun stared blankly at the Book-Seeking Altar before him. The character "仙" had disappeared; in its place rested an imperial jade seal.

—"...What does this mean?"-Jing Chenjun's voice sounded somewhat dry.

Was this telling him not to cultivate immortality and to return to be a prince?

Alas, Qu Qianxing couldn't answer his question either.

At this moment, a gentle voice drifted down from above:

—"This is a magical treasure. When you discover the method to use this treasure, that will be the moment you attain the Dao."

That was... the Untiring Immortal Venerable?

Had he received the Untiring Immortal Venerable's validation?

It took Jing Chenjun quite a while to process this.

—"Understood!"

Jing Chenjun gripped that imperial jade seal, bowed deeply toward the high platform in immense excitement, and backed away to his seat.

On the high platform, several visiting cultivators spectating the event wore peculiar expressions:

[—"What was with that child just now?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"A mortal? But carrying grand qi-luck..."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"That's the [Qi of the Human Emperor](<# "Literal CN: 人皇之气 (rénhuáng zhī qì) — sovereign celestial destiny / imperial aura of the destined Human Emperor">), isn't it?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

—"Indeed. That is why I kept him,"-Zhou Hui smiled.-"Perhaps in a few years he will be sent back, but isn't it good to exert a little influence?"

—"Ha, Plum-Bamboo will have a fit if they hear this! They've always taken it for granted that the Human Emperor belongs to the Northern Domain,"-the old freak chuckled.

Yet the cultivators mostly paid it little mind.

They assumed Zhou Hui merely wanted to exert influence over the future Human Emperor, perhaps to reap some benefits from the Northern Domain when the time was right, playing an idle chess move—sects in the Northern Domain had always treated mortal imperial families this way, and since this little prince had somehow wound up in the Central Domain, it wasn't surprising for the Heavenly Spirit Sect to do the same.

They couldn't even be bothered to report it to the Heavenly Dao Alliance. Besides, even if the Plum-Bamboo couple found out, nothing would come of it anyway.

What they didn't know was that Zhou Hui was thinking about something else entirely:

When a mortal dynasty's qi-luck was at its peak, let alone cultivators, even the Heavenly Dao dared not go against it; but when a dynasty declined, not only did bizarre anomalies erupt within the country, but the attracted demons could even trigger turmoil across the cultivation world.

Hence, the cultivation world had historically adopted a policy of indirect interference toward human dynasties, keeping mortal dynasties neither too prosperous nor too destitute.

So was it the Heavenly Dao that determined destiny, or did destiny and qi-luck influence the Heavenly Dao?

Zhou Hui had always been curious.

Until just now, when the jade seal of White Jade Capital—this jade seal naturally didn't control White Jade Capital; cultivators didn't take orders from jade seals, as it was merely bestowed by White Jade Capital to mortal emperors of that era—responded, acknowledging that mortal child as the future Human Emperor.

Zhou Hui couldn't help wanting to see what changes this future Human Emperor, who had never appeared in the Central Domain in his previous reincarnations, would bring him.

Of course, even so, Jing Chenjun was merely an ordinary member among the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Zhou Hui held expectations for the future of all his disciples.

He watched with a smile as all the disciples made their choices. Finally, it was his direct disciple's turn.

Mo Bei had naturally reverted to human form.

Temporarily enduring the murderous intent in her heart and the chill in her body, she walked slowly up to the Book-Seeking Altar.

Her hand was trembling, yet the brush tip didn't waver in the slightest as she wrote down vigorous calligraphy completely at odds with her delicate appearance:

"Destiny" (命).

—"I thought she was going to write 'Love,'"-Mo Xi breathed a sigh of relief.-"Thank goodness she didn't embarrass herself on such an occasion."

—"Xi'er, don't speak of your sister like that,"-Zhou Hui said, shaking his head.

Mo Xi stuck out his tongue.

At this moment, ink began seeping continuously from that character "命," flowing pitch-black onto the ground, submerging the vicinity of the Book-Seeking Altar, and even spreading outwards to form a black frigid pool.

Disciples cried out in shock.

Those closer to the Book-Seeking Altar scrambled backward in panic, only to see a layer of frost condensing beneath their feet.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui's restriction descended from midair, protecting them while containing the further expansion of the frigid pool.

—"F*ck! This daddy is finally out! I'm free! I'm going to slaughter every last one of you! Die, all of you! Die, die, die!"-the colossal carp roared.

It was silver, with a pair of small horns growing on its head, its body slender, already bearing the subtle posture of a dragon. Yet its body was mottled with red splotches like bloodstains, not to mention its tail!

—Starting from the midpoint, all flesh on the tail was completely gone, exposing only crystalline, translucent tailbone.

Looking at this carp, Mo Bei's eyes widened, and then the corners of her mouth curled upward into a smile that [didn't exceed two pixels](<# "Literal CN: 上勾不超过两个像素 — modern digital/gaming humor: a smile barely two pixels wide, subtle and terrifying">):

—"Right. You're out. You're not murmuring in my shadow anymore... That makes things so much easier!"-Mo Bei said, drawing her flexible sword.

Just like a kitty swatting its paws to catch fish, *SMACK!* she accurately whacked this fish right back into the frigid pool.
