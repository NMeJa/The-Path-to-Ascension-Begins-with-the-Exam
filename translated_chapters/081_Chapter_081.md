# Chapter 81: An Old Trick Replayed (第81章 故技重施)

[—"So it is Hermit Zui Liu of Wang Lake? Long have I looked forward to making your acquaintance! Long have I looked forward to it!"](<# "Speaker: Immortal Ronggu">)

[—"Not at all, not at all! To have the honor of meeting the illustrious Immortal Ronggu, I am the one blessed by three lifetimes of fortune!"](<# "Speaker: Hermit Zui Liu">)

The cultivators exchanged beaming pleasantries, the atmosphere thick with the saccharine, hollow scent of [mutual commercial flattery](<# "Literal CN: 商业互吹 (shāngyè hùchuī) — mutual business puffery / trading superficial flattery, internet slang for polite professional back-scratching">).

This spectacle bore a striking resemblance to the deadpan, smile-plastered corporate jargon traded by delegation leaders whenever [prestigious universities—er, sects—](<# "Literal CN: 各大高校（X）门派 — modern university academic conferences crossed out and replaced with cultivation sects">) gathered for magic exchange seminars.

Yet if an unwitting onlooker were to stumble into this pavilion, they would be frightened half to death—for those engaged in this hollow flattery were, without exception, ancient monsters of the Void Refining and Body Integration stages.

Of course, ordinary people could never reach this place to begin with.

This venue was located atop the coral corolla resting upon the celestial Kunpeng's head deep within the Abyss Sea. Rumored to be the private temporary palace of the Tribulation Transcendence titan, the Untiring Immortal Venerable, it had been opened specifically to host the prospective organizing committee members of the upcoming "Central Domain Representative Tournament."

Upon receiving this gesture of hospitality, the Void Refining and Body Integration powerhouses were immensely gratified. The handful of cultivators who had been somewhat reluctant at the outset—such as Hermit Zui Liu—felt especially fortunate that they hadn't declined the invitation.

Hermit Zui Liu had received an invitation letter from Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa shortly after the New Year—indeed, had it not been for this letter, Hermit Zui Liu wouldn't have even realized that another "year" had slipped by in the mortal realm.

The letter mentioned "Immortal Chess" and the "Central Domain Representative Tournament," cordially inviting Hermit Zui Liu to serve as a witness (read: judge).

Hermit Zui Liu had initially scoffed at the request.

As a textbook Body Integration cultivator, Hermit Zui Liu lived in virtually complete seclusion. He resided in the deepest recesses of the hundred-thousand-li Great Wang Mountain. He took no disciples, established no sect, and kept only a few immortal attendants by his side to manage daily chores. Most of his time was spent in cultivation; on the rare occasions he emerged from seclusion, it was merely to procure cultivation materials. He hardly even brewed the spiritual wines he had so cherished in his youth.

This was the typical routine of most Void Refining and Body Integration cultivators.

After all, these two stages represented a critical juncture.

Most cultivation manuals circulating in the cultivation realm today could only take a practitioner as far as the Golden Core stage; those capable of granting [guaranteed admission](<# "Literal CN: 保送 (bǎosòng) — recommendation/admission without entrance exam, modern academic slang comically applied to cultivation manuals">) to the Soul Formation stage were considered peerless divine scriptures. Even so, once cultivators reached the Void Refining stage, blind imitation was no longer an option. They had to forge bespoke divine abilities suited to their individual constitutions, cleanse their marrow, and temper their flesh, reshaping their mortal vessels to the caliber of true immortals before they could hope to advance to the Mahayana stage.

It was an excruciatingly arduous process. Lacking a master like Zhou Hui, most cultivators were forced to grope forward in the dark entirely on their own. The slightest misstep would reduce years of effort to flying ash, leaving them with zero appetite to meddle in secular trivialities.

Naturally, there was another reason: beginning at the Void Refining stage, the population of cultivators plummeted drastically. In the entire mortal realm, there were merely a few hundred such individuals, scattered across the vast corners of the continent. The secular world held nothing left to anchor their lingering attachments. Conversely, this stage demanded astronomical resources; running into a peer almost invariably meant encountering a competitor for scarce treasures. Consequently, any lingering desire for socialization had long since withered away.

Those at this stage who had not severed all social ties were almost exclusively cultivators hailing from colossal sects.

They either possessed ancestral legacies within their sects or simply had mentors guiding them through bottlenecks. Venerated as founding patriarchs within their respective clans and waited on hand and foot by fawning juniors, they naturally possessed both the leisure and disposition to stir up trouble.

Such was precisely the case with the Qingyang clan of Eternal Villa.

Eternal Villa claimed descent from the imperial lineage of the ancient cultivation world's grandest empire, boasting even the bloodline of the Sacred Lord of White Jade Capital. Whether this boast held any truth remained anyone's guess, but their wealth and idle leisure were indisputable facts. Whenever a major event unfolded in the Central Domain, their figures were guaranteed to be front and center.

That was why Hermit Zui Liu had initially thought nothing of the invitation, dismissing it as yet another instance of Eternal Villa's frivolous antics.

However, Villa Master Qingyang had enclosed a coral pearl along with the letter, explaining that this tournament was personally hosted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's Heavenly Spirit Sect, and that planting the coral pearl would summon a dedicated transport shuttle.

This gave Hermit Zui Liu pause.

It wasn't that he had experienced a change of heart, but rather that he shared a cordial friendship with Eternal Villa. Declining Eternal Villa was no big deal, but the tournament was now backed by a Tribulation Transcendence titan. Hermit Zui Liu had never interacted with this venerable senior and had no inkling of his temperament. If the titan belonged to the type who deemed that 'refusing my invitation is slapping my face,' disastrous trouble would follow.

—"It seems I have no choice but to waste a bit of time and make a personal trip to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. At the very least, I must observe the situation before making a final decision,"-Hermit Zui Liu sighed.

In truth, it wasn't much of a waste of time. A Body Integration cultivator enjoyed a lifespan of ten thousand years; venturing out for a few days, or even a few years, was nothing more than a fleeting breath to him.

It was simply that once a man lived as a [cooped-up shut-in](<# "Literal CN: 宅久了 (zhái jiǔ le) — staying cooped up at home as an otaku/shut-in for too long">) for too long, stepping outside transformed into an exhausting chore demanding significant emotional energy.

Hermit Zui Liu remained blissfully oblivious to his own psychological condition.

Having remained in seclusion for centuries, he knew nothing of the Immortals' Bazaar or the winter solstice auction. Yet upon planting the coral pearl, he had been thoroughly awed by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's spatial teleportation techniques and the breathtaking vistas beneath the Abyss Sea, his trepidation mounting by the second—until the celestial Kunpeng arrived.

Unlike the patrons bound for the Immortals' Bazaar or the children heading to the academy, no colossal passenger bubbles emerged beneath the coral trees to ferry these high-tier powerhouses. Instead, accompanied by celestial music drifting through the waters, a shimmering trail of delicate bubbles wove a starlit causeway leading directly toward the floral "corolla" atop the Kunpeng's head.

Body Integration powerhouses naturally required no external aid to move freely through the abyssal depths.

With a subtle blur of his silhouette, Hermit Zui Liu materialized inside the corolla's front reception hall.

Finding several dozen cultivators of his same cultivation realm already gathered here, Hermit Zui Liu stared in momentary shock.

Apart from grand auctions hosted by City in the Skies or grave crises like demon invasions, Hermit Zui Liu hadn't encountered so many peers in centuries.

Fortunately, Hermit Zui Liu was not [socially anxious](<# "Literal CN: 社恐 (shèkǒng) — short for 社会恐惧症 (social anxiety disorder), internet slang for introverts">).

If anything, back when his cultivation was lower, he had been quite the [social butterfly](<# "Literal CN: 社牛 (shèniú) — short for 社交牛逼症 (social god-tier confidence), slang for shameless, hyper-charismatic extroverts">). It was merely that few could walk beside him to reach his current height, forcing him into a solitary existence.

Beholding so many peers once more, Hermit Zui Liu felt an uncanny sensation of returning to the mortal world.

What was more, the atmosphere of the gathering was remarkably pleasant.

Unlike auctions where attendees were locked in ruthless intrigue over coveted treasures, and unlike grim battlefields where they fought shoulder-to-shoulder against demonic beasts, the theme uniting these peers was merely "Immortal Chess"—rendering the entire affair extraordinarily relaxed.

The format of the reception also differed fundamentally from conventional gatherings.

The moment cultivators boarded the Kunpeng, attendants presented each guest with a gift package and an informational booklet.

The gifts appeared rather ordinary at first glance: nothing more than a folding fan, a blank jade slip capable of storing holographic imagery, a cylindrical [insulated jade tumbler](<# "Literal CN: 保温玉杯 (bǎowēn yùbēi) — thermal thermos/travel mug made of jade, parodying Chinese office/middle-aged tea tumblers">), and a commemorative jade medallion denoting their status. Even low-tier cultivators would hardly find them precious. Yet because the packaging was exquisite, the motifs unique, and the items inscribed with the gilded insignia of "The 1st Central Domain Immortal Arts Tournament," they possessed an inexplicable collectible charm.

At the very least, Hermit Zui Liu didn't toss them away, stowing them neatly into his storage pouch instead.

He then flipped through the booklet.

Richly illustrated with text and imagery, the booklet meticulously detailed (and diplomatically embellished) the origins of the tournament, its operational procedures, and its grand historical significance. It made cultivators like Hermit Zui Liu, who had been roped in out of nowhere, feel that this tournament carried profound weight, making it feel like an absolute privilege just to serve as a witness.

Holding this booklet, Hermit Zui Liu finally stepped into the Kunpeng's coral corolla palace.

The breathtaking, otherworldly scenery and the peculiar spiritual energy fountain left Hermit Zui Liu marveling in sincere admiration, thinking it truly befitted the estate of a Tribulation Transcendence titan. Yet what pleased him even more was the absolute freedom of movement.

The reception imposed zero restrictions on the guests. Tables were laden with spiritual delicacies and rare wines for guests to help themselves as they pleased. They were free to converse with fellow peers, inspect the towering presentation boards introducing the tournament rules, try their hand at Immortal Chess, or, if completely disinclined, simply retreat under a quiet coral tree to [wallow in peaceful solitude](<# "Literal CN: 自闭 (zìbì) — internet slang for going non-verbal, curling into a corner, or brooding alone">).

While the Untiring Immortal Venerable had not made a personal appearance—in truth, no one genuinely dared look upon him in such an informal setting anyway—he had invited Sect Leader Zhang Hongjing of the Thousand Harmony Gang to act as master of ceremonies. With this affable host working the floor, there was zero risk of awkward silences, keeping everyone smiling from ear to ear.

In barely half a shichen, Hermit Zui Liu had thoroughly forgotten his initial sensation of being coerced, feeling only immense gratitude that he had come.

—"Regarding this 'academic presentation,' is there a more detailed explanation?"-he even took the initiative to ask Zhang Hongjing.

—"This is a new form of [civil duel](<# "Literal CN: 文斗 (wéndòu) — scholarly contest / battle of wits, contrasted with martial combat (武斗)">) introduced by the Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Zhang Hongjing explained with a genial chuckle.-"After all, this time we are not crudely measuring high and low between rival sects, but selecting the immortals who can best represent the Central Domain. A pure contest of combat spells would be rather unfair to cultivators unversed in violence, so it is necessary to compete on scholarly intellect."

—"Truly worthy of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's visionary mind,"-Hermit Zui Liu nodded in profound conviction.

He had long moved past the stage of proving himself through violent combat, and was currently torturing himself half to death trying to pioneer his own divine abilities. Naturally, he found the Untiring Immortal Venerable's concept of a "civil duel" brimming with transcendent wisdom.

—"The booklet mentions that contestants will present their [theses](<# "Literal CN: 论文 (lùnwén) — academic paper / thesis">) for us to audit and examine their capabilities. What does this [thesis defense](<# "Literal CN: 论文答辩 (lùnwén dabiàn) — oral thesis defense">) mean?"-the other elder, Immortal Ronggu, chimed in to inquire from the side.

—"That is somewhat difficult to explain in words,"-Zhang Hongjing replied with a smile.-"However, the Heavenly Spirit Sect has arranged for several disciples to give demonstrations. Once everyone arrives, you will be able to see for yourselves."

[—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable's esteemed disciple is right here. Why not have him give a demonstration right now?"](<# "Speaker: Yun Guoling">)

At these words, everyone turned their gazes toward the tall, slightly hunched youth standing behind Zhang Hongjing.

That was the Untiring Immortal Venerable's fourth disciple, Guan Ye.

Having just completed the engineering project at Blood Demon Hall, he was riding this Kunpeng flight back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

However, unlike Yuwen Xuan and Mo Xi, Guan Ye was not skilled at socializing with outsiders. Throughout the journey, he had quietly shadowed Zhang Hongjing, offering nothing more than polite nods to the guests as his form of hospitality.

The various cultivators saw that Guan Ye was merely at the late stage of Foundation Establishment and understood he was not the primary host. Even those eager to curry favor with the Untiring Immortal Venerable merely offered passing compliments like "a young talent of immense promise," without forcing further conversation upon him.

Who would be foolish enough to pick a fight in a place like this?

Zhang Hongjing glanced displeasedly toward the speaker, only to discover that it was Young Master Yun Guoling of Cloud City.

This youth was likewise only at the Foundation Establishment stage and theoretically did not belong on this guest roster. However, high-ranking powerhouses occasionally brought junior scions along, and Yun Guoling had managed to hitch a ride precisely by leveraging his status as the young master of Cloud City.

—"Young Master Yun is being far too hasty,"-Zhang Hongjing said with a beaming smile, angling his body to shield Guan Ye.-"Our time is limited, and an academic presentation cannot be prepared in the blink of an eye. How could there possibly be enough time?"

—"Oh? Even the Untiring Immortal Venerable's esteemed disciple cannot manage it?"-Yun Guoling asked with a flippant smirk.-"And here I thought we might have a friendly bout!"

Hearing this, a cold sneer flickered across Zhang Hongjing's lips.

He finally understood: this Young Master Yun wanted to use Guan Ye as a stepping stone!

It made sense. This Immortal Chess tournament would once again select outstanding Foundation Establishment youth to serve as Central Domain representatives. Having missed the opportunity at the previous auction, someone like Yun Guoling was determined to enter this time. Wanting to steal the spotlight and leave a striking impression before the prospective judges was understandable... [the hell it was!]

[How on earth does Cloud City educate their children? Of all the people you could pick to establish your dominance, you chose the Immortal Venerable's disciple?!]

[And they didn't even assign an adult to keep a leash on this spoiled brat!]

[Is this what you people called an apology last time?!]

While internally cursing up a storm, Zhang Hongjing was just about to smooth things over and prevent Cloud City's young master from making an unreasonable scene, when a cold chuckle echoed from behind him.

—"A bout?"-Guan Ye's lips curled into a faint smirk.-"Do you mean to say you wish to deliver an academic presentation right here on the spot?"

—"Are you afraid?"-Yun Guoling asked provocatively.

Yun Guoling's challenge hadn't been an entirely spontaneous impulse.

He had already planned to participate in the tournament and previously felt certain of victory. Yet recently, after visiting the tavern opened by the Heavenly Spirit Sect in Cloud City several times, he discovered that public fervor for this game was borderline fanatical. Virtually everyone was clamoring to register and compete, including numerous formidable practitioners, which had stoked a growing sense of urgency within him.

Yet as a young master of Cloud City, his resources were leagues beyond the ordinary rabble.

For instance, ordinary cultivators remained entirely ignorant of this celestial Kunpeng chartered specifically to ferry the future judges; even if they knew, they could never gain entry. Yet with a single word, Yun Guoling could sneak aboard beforehand to build cozy ties with these organizing committee members.

In fact, under the pretext of being an emissary of Cloud City, Yun Guoling had already ridden this Kunpeng for several trips.

He never expected that today, a disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable would happen to board the shuttle—and at the exact same cultivation realm as himself.

Yun Guoling instantly pegged Guan Ye as a rival, while simultaneously conceiving the ambition to stomp on him to forge his own renown.

Naturally, he wasn't marching into battle unprepared.

Over these past days aboard the Kunpeng, he had thoroughly inquired into the nature of these "academic presentations" and believed he had grasped the essence of the matter: it was nothing more than summarizing one's study experiences and fielding questions from esteemed seniors. As a [cultivation-second-generation](<# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū'èrdài) — parody of 'rich-second-generation' (富二代), spoiled offspring of high-ranking cultivators">), Yun Guoling had been grilled like this by clan elders countless times. Even against a disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, he was confident he would not lose.

What's more, this Guan Ye looked dull and lumbering, clearly resembling someone clumsy with words.

Yet for some inexplicable reason, this "dull and lumbering" Guan Ye was looking at him with a gaze laced with pity:

—"...The ignorant are truly fearless,"-Guan Ye remarked softly.

Normally, he wouldn't have bothered paying heed to an idiot like this. But the fellow had uttered "the Untiring Immortal Venerable's esteemed disciple"—twice! If he did nothing, wouldn't that bring disgrace upon Master?

—"[Jie-jie](<# "Sinister villainous cackle (桀桀), classic trope of evil sect elders in xianxia, comically uttered here by the gloomy, earnest Guan Ye">), then let us have a bout,"-Guan Ye chuckled.

His raspy laughter caused goosebumps to erupt across Yun Guoling's skin.

A deeply ominous foreboding suddenly seized Yun Guoling. Yet having backed himself into a corner, and thrilled that his [reverse psychology](<# "Literal CN: 激将法 (jījiàngfǎ) — provocation tactic / goading maneuver">) had succeeded, he naturally refused to back down.

None of the other cultivators moved to dissuade them; they even held back the anxious Zhang Hongjing.

—"Is it not wonderful for youth to possess such fiery passion?"-Hermit Zui Liu remarked to Zhang Hongjing with an amused grin.

In the eyes of Body Integration ancient monsters, this was truly nothing more than the playful tussle of two "five-year-old toddlers."

Moments later, he received a report over a dozen pages thick from one of these "five-year-old toddlers." He then watched as the youth erected a whiteboard displaying holographic projections of the seabed, crowned with the bold title: *A Dissertation on the Seasonal Fluctuations of Submarine Demonic Qi*.

Hermit Zui Liu flipped through a few pages, only to discover his head spinning in dizzying confusion.

—"You actually carry this on your person?!"-he asked in sheer disbelief.

—"Indeed. This is an analytical report that failed to pass Master's review last time, which I must resubmit upon returning,"-Guan Ye replied earnestly.-"May I trouble the esteemed seniors to review it for me first?"

Witnessing this, Zhang Hongjing breathed a sigh of relief.

He immediately found his prior panic rather comical. As a Void Refining cultivator, he had once been rescued by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's youngest disciple, and this young man was that prodigy's Fourth Senior Brother! How could he possibly be an [easy pushover](<# "Literal CN: 省油的灯 (shěngyóu de dēng) — 'a lamp that saves oil', idiom for someone docile, harmless, or easy to deal with">)?

At this thought, Zhang Hongjing patted the pale-faced Young Master Yun on the shoulder:

—"What on earth possessed you to challenge a disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable to a civil duel?"-Zhang Hongjing sighed.-"That is the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple!"

* * *

There is nothing new under the sun.

Things in this mortal world always recur in an endless cycle.

A strikingly similar scene was about to unfold within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, yet no one sensed it—no, someone did notice, but having failed to grasp what had hit him until the very end last time, he was powerless to sound an early warning now.

At this moment, Su Xin stared blankly at Yuwen Xuan's face, feeling an overwhelming sense of déjà vu.

—"...The tournament is split into two components: civil duels and martial duels,"-Yuwen Xuan explained with meticulous care, lavishly buttering up the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys.-"For the civil component, cultivators wishing to enter must submit their own theses and deliver academic presentations. The organizing committee will audit and evaluate their papers to select the outstanding candidates. Since everyone here hails from the Northern Domain with its countless immortal sects, you must possess an understanding of immortal techniques and cultivation methods far superior to ordinary men. Auditing those papers will surely present zero difficulty."

Listening to this rhetoric, the dread pooling in Su Xin's heart steadily expanded.

Before their cloud carriage had even glided into the mist shrouding the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yuwen Xuan had personally led several disciples out to greet them.

Recognizing him as the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Brother, Su Xin naturally harbored zero suspicion toward Yuwen Xuan's reception.

Yet upon crossing into the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Su Xin was thoroughly flabbergasted by the staggering transformations wrought in merely half a year.

Half a year ago, only the Palace of Silence could be considered imposing (read: terrifying), and that had merely been the product of illusory arts; the rest of the grounds resembled a rundown third-rate sect. Half a year later, not only was the Palace of Silence no longer an illusion (though Su Xin would have vastly preferred it remained unfinished, particularly the examination platforms), but the sect now boasted an elegant Library Pavilion, a colossal public bathhouse and water park, and a magnificent sky-mirror reflection.

Word had it that this was merely a fraction of the scenery; they had yet to inspect the Immortals' Bazaar or Qianxing Hall.

Su Xin was utterly dazzled. Even the haughty Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys lost much of their initial arrogance, praising with genuine sincerity that it "truly befitted the sect of a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator"—though this compliment sounded rather backhanded in practice.

Captivated by the breathtaking vistas, they failed to notice the bizarre expressions on the faces of Mo Xi and the other disciples waiting at the gate when they saw Yuwen Xuan leading the way.

Alas, Zhou Hui had not stepped forth in person to welcome the guests, leaving Mo Xi powerless to stop whatever Yuwen Xuan planned to do.

—Not that Mo Xi wanted to stop him anyway. Noticing that Eldest Senior Brother had seemingly returned to "normal," he breathed a sigh of relief. He then decisively dumped these guests—whom he found rather obnoxious himself—straight into Eldest Senior Brother's hands and beat a hasty retreat to avoid getting caught in the crossfire.

Naturally, Yuwen Xuan didn't immediately pull anything that might summon Master.

On the surface, his itinerary mirrored Mo Xi's original itinerary almost perfectly.

He merely guided the entourage through the Palace of Silence, designated as the venue for the "academic presentations," briefed them on the specific schedule of the tournament, and naturally layered on a healthy dose of [mutual flattery](<# "Literal CN: 商业互吹 (shāngyè hùchuī) — commercial puffery / trading superficial corporate compliments">).

—"Rather, I can hardly wait to witness the scholarly theses of the esteemed envoys,"-Yuwen Xuan declared with vibrant enthusiasm.-"Unlike our Central Domain, your insights must surely represent the highest pinnacle of the immortal cultivation world."

—"Not necessarily..."-Su Xin murmured weakly, trembling as he stared up at the Palace of Silence.

If given the choice, he never wanted to set foot in this horrific place again for the rest of his life. Consequently, he attributed his rising dread upon hearing Yuwen Xuan's praise entirely to his lingering trauma with the palace.

Yet while Su Xin felt this way, the other envoys certainly did not.

They accepted Yuwen Xuan's flattery with shameless righteousness:

[—"Naturally! Compared to the declining Central Domain, our Northern Domain is the true center of the modern cultivation realm!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[—"A pity we don't hold similar tournaments in the Northern Domain, otherwise we would have blown your minds."](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[—"We could suggest organizing one once we return."](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

—"Shut your mouths!"-Su Xin snapped at the Soul Formation cultivators, before turning to Yuwen Xuan with an apologetic bow.-"My apologies. My grand-nephew disciples are far too arrogant. When it comes to cultivation methods, the Heavenly Spirit Sect is far more formidable."

How could they not be formidable when their exams had reduced him to a quivering wreck?

—"Little Grand-Martial Uncle, you are simply too humble. We are merely stating objective facts,"-one of them argued back.

The envoys looked at Su Xin in bewilderment, unable to fathom why this "Little Grand-Martial Uncle" had experienced such a complete personality transplant the moment he arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Perhaps it was because an esteemed "senior" resided here?

Yet they despised seeing their clan's little ancestor bow down to outsiders—especially the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

Granted, the Plum-Bamboo Couple shared close ties with the Untiring Immortal Venerable, but the Heavenly Spirit Sect ought to recognize their own place!

The Plum-Bamboo Couple alone comprised two Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses! That was inherently stronger than the solitary Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Not to mention, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was fundamentally a third-rate sect. Though it possessed many wondrous spectacles as befitted a Tribulation titan's dwelling, its eldest disciple was at the Soul Formation stage just like them. The other disciples were even less worth mentioning—Golden Cores were scarce, and the rest were mostly Foundation Establishment and Qi Condensation rookies who barely amounted to anything.

They truly couldn't comprehend why the Central Domain would entrust such a momentous tournament to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Surely they couldn't rely on the Untiring Immortal Venerable alone!

For grand occasions of this scale, what truly mattered was a sect's foundational heritage.

At least this Yuwen Xuan possessed some self-awareness, showing ample reverence toward the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Yuwen Xuan chimed in right on cue:

—"I too deeply admire the Northern Domain's flourishing prosperity, and only wish I could witness it in person... Ah, how about this!"-Yuwen Xuan proposed with a bright smile.-"This afternoon, our Central Domain's high-tier seniors will arrive to serve as judges for the tournament alongside you esteemed guests."

—"At that time, our Heavenly Spirit Sect will present an academic demonstration to establish the standards and direction for the civil duels. Would you gentlemen care to join us?"-Yuwen Xuan smiled pleasantly, though his tone was subtly laced with a provocation absent before.-"Cultivators arriving from the Northern Domain can surely broaden the horizons of our Central Domain seniors!"

—"Ha?!"-the envoys scoffed, instantly incensed by the perceived slight.-"Join you? You had better not regret asking!"

—"Wait a moment, there is no need for us to do that!"-Su Xin scrambled to intervene, sweating bullets.-"Senior Brother Yuwen, let it go! We are not worthy—"

—"It is quite alright. Those giving academic presentations on our side are merely Foundation Establishment and Golden Core disciples,"-Yuwen Xuan chuckled warmly.-"Surely the esteemed seniors of the Heavenly Dao Alliance can easily accomplish as much."

—"No, we really don't need to..."-Su Xin wailed desperately.

Yet having reached this point, how could the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys possibly back down?!

[—"Little Grand-Martial Uncle, do not douse our own morale while inflating others' prestige!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect clearly refuses to acknowledge our Heavenly Dao Alliance's supremacy!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[—"Then let us show them our true capabilities!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[Wait, no!]

[Why on earth are you fools volunteering to do an academic presentation at the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!]

[Does anyone in their right mind defend their thesis before professors from the rival university next door?!]

At this very moment, Su Xin was struck by the sudden, crushing revelation of just how agonizingly stupid his past self had been.
