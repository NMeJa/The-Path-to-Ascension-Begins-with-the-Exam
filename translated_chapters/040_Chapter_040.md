# Chapter 40: A Minor Problem (第40章 一个小问题)

[—"Mmm, delicious!"](# "Speaker: Huang Xiao")

Huang Xiao downed its portion of beast milk in a single gulp, so utterly delighted that its concealed animal ears nearly popped right out.

Human sects were truly wonderful.

[Good thing Second Younger Sister and I listened to Seventh Grandpa and came to register! Even though learning human script and arithmetic is a huge pain, getting a serving of spirit beast milk every morning makes it totally worth it! I must remember to write a letter home so Third Younger Brother, Fourth Younger Sister, Fifth Younger Sister, Sixth Younger Brother, Seventh Younger Sister, and Eighth Younger Brother can all come together next year!]

Huang Xiao blinked its simple, honest little beady eyes, calculating with shrewd precision.

Still craving more, it cast a lingering gaze toward its roommate's beast milk.

—"Aren't you going to drink yours?"-Huang Xiao asked, licking the milky froth from the corners of its mouth.

—"I am drinking it,"-the roommate replied.

That roommate took a tiny sip of beast milk—merely the delicate nip of a lip—which, in Huang Xiao's eyes, didn't look like he could stomach it at all.

This reminded Huang Xiao of its Seventh Younger Sister.

Its Seventh Sister was just like this roommate: fair, frail, and tiny. Every bite she took was minuscule, and she ate so slowly. Whenever the siblings scrambled for food, she could never beat the others, left with nothing to do but squeak frantically on the sidelines. If Huang Xiao hadn't remembered to yield to this little sister every time, she would have starved to death long ago.

[The sect must have considered the presence of little cubs like this, which is why everyone in the beginner class gets a serving of spirit milk every morning!]

Thinking this way, Huang Xiao's urge to snatch the food dwindled considerably. Instead, it gently nudged the sugar jar on the dining table over.

—"If you can't get it down, just add some sugar,"-Huang Xiao said pityingly.-"You've got to finish every drop. That's the only way to maximize the spiritual energy absorption and lay a solid foundation for your cultivation."

—"...I will,"-Jing Chenjun replied with a thoroughly bewildered smile.

He couldn't fathom why Huang Xiao was looking at him with such pitying eyes. Restraining his appetite, he merely took another delicate sip of the beast milk, barely ensuring that the imperial court etiquette drilled into him since childhood had not gone to waste.

Yet it was truly delicious!

Jing Chenjun had never liked drinking milk before. He disliked milk of any kind—finding it far too gamey, and it always gave him an upset stomach afterwards. Yet this beast milk before him, sourced from who-knew-what spirit beast, was completely different. Not only did it lack even a hint of gaminess, but a single gentle sip filled his mouth with a rich, fragrant dairy aroma, tempting Jing Chenjun so intensely he almost wanted to abandon all royal dignity and chug it down [by the barrel](# "Literal CN: 吨吨吨 (dūn dūn dūn) — onomatopoeia for chugging liquids in massive, unbroken gulps") like his roommate!

[Is this the food of immortals?]

Jing Chenjun marveled inwardly, savoring the flavor lingering on his palate while straining with all his might to discern something else from this delectable taste—namely, the "spiritual energy" Huang Xiao had mentioned.

Alas, no matter how much he strained his entire body, he couldn't taste anything other than sheer deliciousness, leaving Jing Chenjun thoroughly disappointed.

[Why? I've clearly been accepted by an immortal sect, so why can't I sense spiritual energy?]

[Must one truly possess a spirit root?]

Jing Chenjun thought dejectedly.

Yes, Jing Chenjun possessed no spirit root; he was a mortal.

Though a mortal, he was no ordinary mortal—he was the Third Prince of the Yongfeng Empire in the Northern Domain, the largest mortal empire in this entire world. With the Empress having borne no sons, he was only a single step away from the title of Crown Prince.

Yet Jing Chenjun had zero interest in inheriting the imperial throne; he wanted to become an immortal.

From a very young age, Jing Chenjun had realized that in a world with immortals, the so-called "supreme rulers of the realm" and mortal dynasties claiming uninterrupted lineages for ten thousand generations were nothing more than puppets to cultivation practitioners.

With a single command from those cultivation sects, the entire empire had to scramble into motion, every household offering up their children for the immortal sects to pick through—whether they became disciples or indentured servants depended entirely on the immortal sect's whims. If a wicked immortal committed atrocities in the mortal realm, the empire could only report it up to the sects first, begging them to send someone to investigate; the empire itself lacked the power to apprehend them. And if that vile immortal was connected to a major sect, the trouble would be astronomical.

It was only because the Northern Domain had far too many massive immortal sects, none yielding to another, that they chose to prop up a mortal emperor to maintain "impartiality."

Compared to cultivators, however, mortals were far too fragile. To prevent the tragic embarrassment of a mortal dynasty chosen by the major sects being overthrown if they looked away for a split second, the sects had prepared quite a few magical treasures usable even by mortals for their puppet emperors, ensuring the royal family could safeguard themselves.

It was precisely by relying on these magical treasures that Jing Chenjun disguised himself as a fledgling rogue cultivator, visiting immortal sects everywhere in a wild bid to pursue cultivation.

Regrettably, while magical treasures could let him pose as a rogue cultivator for a time, they could not make him a genuine practitioner, much less cause a spirit root to sprout.

Thus, in his quest for immortality, Jing Chenjun had visited many sects across the Northern Domain, only to have his true colors exposed every single time at the "spirit root inspection" trial.

In the most perilous incident, not only was his mortal identity exposed, but that sect even attempted to seize the magical treasures on his person. In the end, Jing Chenjun was forced to trigger an emergency teleportation talisman, which swept him into a spatial rift and deposited him in the Central Domain.

In the perilous Central Domain where even cultivators struggled to survive, Jing Chenjun—a mere mortal who only knew how to rely on magical treasures—naturally found it excruciatingly hard to stay alive. It was at this juncture that he heard news of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's minor recruitment.

Clinging to a do-or-die resolve, Jing Chenjun had knelt to petition before the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate. To his utter shock, he was actually selected!

Watching the peach blossom petal drift into his palm at that moment, Jing Chenjun had nearly believed it was all a dream, but the scorching heat in his palm—verging on painful—told him this was reality.

For the first time in his life, Jing Chenjun forgot his so-called "royal dignity," jumping into the air in a state of utter discomposure.

He had even wanted to hug everyone around him.

Only when he recalled that there was an exam did he regain a shred of composure.

Afterward, Jing Chenjun experienced the most bizarre examination of his entire life.

He was led into an examination hall resembling an underwater palace, seated upon a coral platform enveloped in a bubble, where he began answering questions alongside a multitude of test-takers.

The questions set by immortals were indeed unlike those of mortals; many question formats were entirely new to him. Yet upon closer inspection, they tested candidates' knowledge far more rigorously than vague, sweeping essays, making him itch to suggest to his Imperial Father that they adopt them for the imperial civil examinations!

Furthermore, the scope of the questions was astonishingly broad, encompassing everything from astronomy down to geography.

Thanks to this, although Jing Chenjun couldn't answer the questions regarding spiritual energy and the like, as someone once groomed to be the imperial heir, Grand Scholars had thoroughly drilled him in texts like the *Mathematical Classic*, ensuring he didn't hand in a blank paper—in fact, he felt he had performed quite well!

The coral platform drifted up and down in accordance with his answers, ultimately settling at the middle tier.

Reaching the middle tier proved he was at least average among all test-takers. Considering he had been unable to answer any of the cultivation-related questions, his score on the questions he could answer was actually remarkably high.

And so, Jing Chenjun was admitted!

Though placed in the beginner class, Jing Chenjun had finally fulfilled his lifelong dream of entering an immortal sect, becoming a member of the cultivation world!

—Only later did Jing Chenjun learn that obtaining the peach blossom mark had signified admission all along. The subsequent exam had merely been a placement test! This year, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had abolished the outer and inner sect division, categorizing disciples solely by grades and classes. The placement test only determined which grade and class one was assigned to, and such exams would be held annually. In fact, among the roommates in his four-person dorm, two couldn't even read, yet they had still passed the recruitment and entered just fine.

Under such a "slack" system, the Heavenly Spirit Sect also seemed to have forgotten to check for spirit roots.

Based on Jing Chenjun's experience petitioning dozens of sects, checking spirit roots was clearly the very first step every sect took when recruiting disciples. Yet at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, this seemed utterly unimportant, allowing a fish that slipped through the net like Jing Chenjun to sneak inside.

[Ah, I shouldn't badmouth my own sect like this!]

[Perhaps the Heavenly Spirit Sect didn't check spirit roots because of the Central Domain's complex environment?]

Jing Chenjun speculated inwardly. Sipping his milk, he looked at his roommates with eyes that seemed composed on the surface but were actually scrutinizing them.

The average height in this dormitory was exceptionally tall.

Jing Chenjun had originally thought his own height of seven chi and five cun was not short, but apart from the haughty youth beside him, the other two were both nine chi tall! Among them, the not-so-small "Huang Xiao" wasn't too strange—with a round face and cute beady eyes, he looked simple and honest, seemingly an ordinary cultivator. But the other one, named "Ji Gen," had slender vines wrapped around his body, and a flower bloomed from the crown of his head. He looked less like a human and more like a mountain spirit.

—"Everyone in our tribe is like this,"-Ji Gen had introduced himself quite openly when the roommates first met:-"Because our neck of the woods is surrounded by toxic fog year-round, ordinary mortals can't survive there. So we have no choice but to let 'Guardian Vines' grow on our bodies to ward off the fog. But tribesmen without spirit roots still get sucked dry sooner or later, and feeding the vines day-to-day is agonizing. Fortunately, we heard the Heavenly Spirit Sect didn't look down on us, so they sent me here to study! I hope to find a way so everyone in the tribe won't have to suffer so much."

Sounds like Ji Gen hailed from a Central Domain "mortal" background.

Yet if even "mortals" in the Central Domain looked like this, checking spirit roots really didn't seem all that necessary.

Ji Gen saw nothing abnormal about himself. He chugged half of his spirit milk [with hearty gulps](# "Literal CN: 吨吨吨 (dūn dūn dūn) — onomatopoeia for chugging liquids in massive, unbroken gulps"), then soaked his vines in the remaining milk, watching the vines absorb the other half.

Even after finishing it like this, he still felt unsatisfied, sighing:

—"It tastes great, but the portion is way too small! I need to drink some more water."

As he spoke, so he did.

Ji Gen ran with his cup into the dorm's attached washroom, filled a massive cup of water, and chugged it down in loud gulps, praising:

—"The immortal sect is so wonderful! The living quarters are clean and tidy, and the water source is so close! Back home, we have to walk three or four li just to fetch water!"

—"Indeed, indeed,"-Huang Xiao chimed in, seemingly a local as well:-"Though the spirit spring in our quarters seems to have been channeled in using immortal methods. Disciples before had to fetch water themselves too; we caught a good time."

—"Then that's amazing! I want to learn this! That way our village won't have to brave danger to fetch water!"-Ji Gen said.-"Ah, I need another cup of water."

He went to fill another cup, this time feeding it to the vines on his body.

Huang Xiao seemed almost unable to watch this any longer, saying sympathetically:

—"Brother Gen, if you're still this hungry, you should go check out Delicacy Pavilion. They provide free food for our beginner class as well."

—"...That's probably not a good idea! If I go to Delicacy Pavilion, they'll stir-fry dishes, but if they cook them and I can't finish, wasting food is shameful no matter what,"-Ji Gen hesitated.

Hearing this, Jing Chenjun couldn't help but chime in in agreement:

—"Indeed. The spirit milk here is so delicious, yet I know not why the spiritual food has... that kind of taste. Personally, I feel they would be better off simply supplying raw ingredients."

[—"Right, right!"](# "Speaker: Ji Gen")-Ji Gen nodded in deep agreement.

—"Hmph! What do you lot know!"-the one in the dormitory who had remained silent all along spoke up.

Unlike the silk-and-jade-clad Jing Chenjun, this one wore only a plain white Daoist robe, yet his expression was even haughtier than the royally bred Jing Chenjun, holding his neck high like a swan. He looked every bit like a prominent disciple from a grand sect.

In fact, that was exactly how he had introduced himself.

This cultivator, who called himself "Cheng Ziyi," hailed from the Northern Domain's Xiafei Sect—a major sect that even Jing Chenjun knew of. Cheng Ziyi was the descendant of a sect elder, a shoe-in for the inner sect. However, after watching a projection recording of the Untiring Immortal Venerable defeating Sovereign Immortal Chana (who was also at the Tribulation Transcendence stage), he was filled with profound admiration for the Untiring Immortal Venerable, traveling tens of thousands of li to the Central Domain to petition the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Naturally, a disciple like Cheng Ziyi was not afraid of spirit root inspections; he even lamented why the Heavenly Spirit Sect didn't check them. According to him, he possessed a Gold Heavenly Spirit Root, and had only been relegated to the beginner class because he performed poorly on the arithmetic questions. Next academic year, he would definitely launch a comeback and advance to the advanced class!

Right now, Cheng Ziyi sneered:

—"Only those of you with impure spirit roots still crave mortal food. Once we reach Foundation Establishment, we must practice inedia [bigu], consuming morning dew and dining on clouds and mist—what need is there for such worldly fare! It is surely to help us adapt that the Immortal Venerable instructed Delicacy Pavilion to serve food that is nigh impossible to swallow. This is the Immortal Venerable's deep and benevolent intention!"

[—"So that's how it is."](# "Speakers: Ji Gen & Jing Chenjun")-Ji Gen and Jing Chenjun said with sudden realization.

Only Huang Xiao hung its head in utter shame.

On the day of enrollment, it had walked into Delicacy Pavilion brimming with excitement alongside all the fresh recruits. The moment it smelled the convoluted aroma drifting out from inside, it understood: that was the scent of its Seventh Grandpa!

The Seventh Grandpa who had written a letter telling it to come petition the mountain!

It simply didn't know what sorcery Seventh Grandpa had used to blind the Immortal Venerable's eyes and worm his way into Delicacy Pavilion! One must know: the dishes Seventh Grandpa whipped up were so atrocious that not even the little cubs in their clan were willing to touch them! Yet Seventh Grandpa had stubbornly taken the "God of Cooking" as his life ambition, storming away from the clan in a huff, and ultimately coming to the Heavenly Spirit Sect—where he actually managed to pose as a head cook!

Thus Huang Xiao knew beyond a shadow of a doubt:

There was no way Seventh Grandpa had any deep, profound intention! He was simply, purely, atrociously bad at cooking!

Thinking of how the one "ruining good ingredients" that its roommates condemned was its own grandpa, Huang Xiao felt profoundly apologetic toward them.

And while Huang Xiao was drowning in guilt, Jing Chenjun finally finished that cup of milk using his elaborate imperial etiquette.

Fortunately, as a mortal, this single cup of spirit milk was already enough to keep him from feeling hungry for an entire day.

Jing Chenjun dabbed his mouth and asked his roommates:

—"We have no classes today, and I hear we are allowed to freely tour the sect. I'd like to visit the Library Pavilion first; would anyone like to join me?"

—"Count me out. I don't know how to read; I'll go once I've learned!"-Ji Gen said frankly.

—"I am somewhat interested in Immortal Chess,"-Cheng Ziyi said reservedly, which meant he would accompany him.

Huang Xiao did not answer right away.

It was not very familiar with human script, so in the eyes of outsiders, it couldn't read either. Going to the Library Pavilion seemed rather pointless.

Jing Chenjun realized this a beat too late, suddenly realizing his invitation was only applicable to Cheng Ziyi, and hurried to smooth things over.

Ji Gen, reading the room first, offered an invitation:

—"Brother Huang, if you don't mind, you can come with me. The Senior Brother Xiang who led us to the Heavenly Spirit Sect said we could go to the mineral vein to gather materials, and they provide 'working meals' there."

—"No, I just wanted to ask..."-Huang Xiao stammered hesitantly:—"Does the Library Pavilion have cookbooks?"

—"Hmph, how could the Library Pavilion contain such vulgar trifles!"-Cheng Ziyi scoffed dismissively, but paused and contradicted himself a moment later:—"However, I hear the Untiring Immortal Venerable gathers books from across the mortal realm without distinction. Perhaps such books exist as well."

In short, he just wanted to show off his extensive knowledge of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's deeds.

—"Then I'll go check out the Library Pavilion,"-Huang Xiao said.

Its mind was made up: it was going to study human recipes!

At the very least, it couldn't let Seventh Grandpa continue wreaking havoc on the disciples here!

Otherwise, when it called over its Third Younger Brother, Fourth Younger Sister, Fifth Younger Sister, Sixth Younger Brother, Seventh Younger Sister, and Eighth Younger Brother next year, all of them going hungry together was a minor issue—if the disciples found out they were Seventh Grandpa's blood relatives, that would be a catastrophic disaster! The entire clan's honor would be ruined! Everyone would assume their Huang family could only produce food that even pigs wouldn't eat!

In truth, Huang Xiao hadn't dared go to Delicacy Pavilion to acknowledge Seventh Grandpa to this day, terrified that people would discover their kinship.

It had to vindicate the clan's name!

Thinking this, Huang Xiao apologized to Ji Gen:

—"Sorry, Brother Ji, but I still want to try cooking."

—"That's a wonderful thing!"-Ji Gen clapped Huang Xiao's shoulder.-"If you learn how to cook, I won't have to starve!"

After all, the beginner class disciples were still a long way from inedia; they still had to eat!

`*`

[—"At present, all new disciples have moved into the freshman dormitories. Aside from a few minor disputes, the majority are emotionally stable."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

—"That's good,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

While the disciples enjoyed their days off, the faculty enjoyed no such reprieve. Especially during this period of freshman enrollment, facing countless problems never encountered in the past, there were endless meetings to hold.

In this "expansion of recruitment," because Zhou Hui had the foresight to train the "Teaching Assistant Class," the faculty shortage could temporarily be handled—though classes had transitioned from small seminars into large lecture halls, sect lectures were generally conducted this way regardless, and the disciples saw nothing amiss. The truly most troublesome problem was housing.

It couldn't be helped. The Heavenly Spirit Sect had originally been a minor sect of only three hundred-odd people. This time, they had expanded enrollment by over five hundred disciples in one go, instantly swelling into a mid-sized sect of nearly a thousand souls. While other problems had yet to surface, accommodation was the foremost crisis.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui had lived in another world plagued by population explosions and possessed ample experience in housing the maximum number of people on minimal land. He produced architectural samples of university dormitories and had Guan Ye rush the construction around the clock, finally managing to pack the newly arrived disciples inside.

Guan Ye, however, was rather pitiable, sporting dark circles under his eyes over the past few days.

Even so, Zhou Hui still felt somewhat apologetic toward the freshmen.

—"These dormitories are merely a temporary measure,"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"In the future, we still need to prepare proper immortal caves for them."

—"Master, you're pampering them far too much! In other sects, disciples sleeping on [communal brick beds](# "Literal CN: 大通铺 (dà tōng pù) — a continuous shared raised platform or brick bed where dozens of people sleep side by side") is hardly unheard of,"-Yuwen Xuan said with a curled lip.

—"Then would you like to try sleeping on a communal bed?"-Zhou Hui regarded him with a cool glare.

—"Can I?! Then I want to sleep on a communal bed with Master!"-Yuwen Xuan suddenly grew excited.-"I wanted that ever since I was little! Whenever I saw other sects do it, I was so envious!"

—"Me too!"-Mo Xi blurted out hurriedly:-"I want to sleep with Master!"

—"I can too,"-Lu Shulong chimed in with lingering regret:-"Back when I was still an egg, I slept with Master all the time."

[Ah. These disciples have grown up completely warped. Should I just incinerate them and get a fresh batch?]

Zhou Hui thought with deep mental exhaustion.

—"Alright, jokes aside, we still need to resolve the immediate problem before us,"-Zhou Hui said, gently shoving aside the disciples clamoring around him like spoiled children. He gazed out at the sect map with concern, feeling this recruitment expansion had been a bit too rushed; he hadn't anticipated so many people.

In truth, Zhou Hui hadn't wanted to expand recruitment.

After all, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was destined to be wiped out—why expand enrollment? To provide a few more bodies for the protagonist to slaughter?

Zhou Hui's training of the Teaching Assistants had originally been for other plans.

As for this minor recruitment, it should have proceeded according to past precedent. Even if a few more people showed up due to the secret realm, it shouldn't have been too many—or so Zhou Hui had thought. Thus, while he had intended to carry out educational reforms, he hadn't placed recruitment on his mind.

Yet through a bizarre combination of errors, while carrying out educational reforms, they had accepted this many disciples.

[Could this be the arrangement of the Heavenly Dao?]

Zhou Hui deduced.

According to the "Script of the Heavenly Dao," the Heavenly Spirit Sect indeed underwent a massive recruitment drive during this minor recruitment, but that was after Zhou Hui went into seclusion and handed the sect master's seat to Qu Qianxing.

In the "novel," Qu Qianxing felt that a minor sect of only three hundred people did not match his Master's illustrious renown, and with so few hands, they lacked manpower whenever they needed to search for medicinal herbs and crafting materials for Master. Thus, Qu Qianxing initiated a massive recruitment drive, forcefully transforming the Heavenly Spirit Sect into a prominent sect. By the time the protagonist made his debut, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was already a regional hegemon—which was precisely why it became the protagonist's prime target.

Now, even without Zhou Hui deliberately doing anything, this many people had still arrived.

[Is the script of the Heavenly Dao truly so difficult to overthrow?]

Zhou Hui knitted his brows.

Yet since they were already here, he couldn't very well drive them away. Besides, he was already [lying flat](# "Literal CN: 躺平 (tǎng píng) — giving up the rat race, doing the bare minimum")—yes, Zhou Hui remained convinced he was still lying flat; he hadn't even cultivated seriously over the past few days! (Smug). Since the Heavenly Dao was so insistent, he would just go along with it!

However, Zhou Hui had never planned on mistreating his disciples.

In that other world, land was scarce, populations surged, and capital ran rampant, spawning countless [pigeon cages](# "Literal CN: 鸽子笼 (gē zi lóng) — cramped high-density micro-apartments"). But the cultivation realm lacked everything except land—with tens of thousands of li of uninhabited wilderness, cramming disciples into tiny boxes would be utterly inhumane.

Thinking of this, Zhou Hui cast Qu Qianxing a suspicious glance.

He wondered how Qu Qianxing in the 'novel' had solved the housing crisis during the expansion. Could he truly have listened to Yuwen Xuan's rotten idea and set up communal brick beds? If so, it would be a miracle if bullying didn't break out—even four-person dorm rooms had disputes every three to five days!

—"Master?"-Qu Qianxing noticed Zhou Hui's gaze and asked in confusion.

—"...Nothing,"-Zhou Hui chose not to pursue things that hadn't happened, looking down at the map:-"The kids in the beginner class can squeeze together for now. They have to attend early general lectures together anyway, and being so young, it's best they aren't left alone. However, their living conditions must be improved; strive to reach two to a room. For the intermediate class, guarantee one room per person. Once they reach the advanced class, let them live in shared courtyards for a few months to bond with their fellow disciples, and after that, allocate individual immortal caves."

—"Master, our sect's territory won't be enough for that,"-Ran Deng pointed out with apparent difficulty.

In truth, she simply didn't want to design any more immortal caves. Thanks to the whole business of laying pipes and channeling spirit springs, former disciples were already begging her for renovation blueprints, piling cave remodeling requests on her desk that stretched all the way into next year. If they added caves for hundreds of freshmen, she might as well dig a pit and bury herself alive.

Yet Ran Deng wasn't inventing excuses out of sheer laziness; the difficulty she cited was an objective reality.

Zhou Hui had never intended to build a colossal sect, which was why he chose the site inside the Mount Tianling valley. Housing several hundred people here was fine, but carving out more immortal caves was out of the question—there simply wasn't enough space.

—"There is no need to keep everything inside the valley,"-Qu Qianxing said, unbothered.-"The entire Mount Tianling mountain range belongs to us; we can demarcate zones and let disciples select their own cave locations."

—"The spiritual energy density of each cave would vary wildly then,"-Guan Ye frowned.-"The farther outside the valley, the thinner the spiritual energy becomes. And even with the Natural Chasm mountain range acting as a barrier, being affected is inevitable."

—"Where in this world is there absolute fairness? Don't other sects have their important figures occupying the main peaks while everyone else is shoved into peripheral corners?"-Yuwen Xuan remarked, shooting a sly, teasing look toward Zhou Hui.

Seeing his expression, Zhou Hui grew annoyed and planted a swift kick on Yuwen Xuan's backside.

—"What utter nonsense! In a place like the Central Domain, could spiritual energy truly be in short supply? At least within the boundaries of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, spiritual energy is fully guaranteed, and spiritual energy storms are not unsolvable,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Master intends to alter the flow of spiritual energy and reshape the spirit veins of Mount Tianling?"-Qu Qianxing's eyes lit up instantly.

The power to reshape the geography of an entire region by oneself was something only Tribulation Transcendence cultivators could achieve!

As Master's die-hard fan, Qu Qianxing naturally yearned for the opportunity to observe his Master's divine might up close. But recalling Zhou Hui's condition, his gaze dimmed once more.

—"However, with Master's injuries, it is best not to expend too much strength, right?"-he said dispiritedly.

—"Pei Lan already told you, I am not injured,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"No, Master Pei Lan simply hasn't reached a diagnosis yet, which clearly indicates the injuries are exceptionally complex,"-Qu Qianxing insisted.-"Otherwise, why would Master Pei Lan be the only honored guest to remain in Meditation Hall after all the others departed?"

[That old fox just wants to freeload free food and drinks here!]

Zhou Hui complained inwardly, though he had no desire to keep arguing this pointless topic with his disciple:

—"Never mind. Regardless of whether I am injured, altering the spiritual energy currents of Mount Tianling and blocking external spiritual energy storms is a trivial matter—there is no need for me to act personally."

—"No need to act personally?"-Qu Qianxing's eyes widened.-"Could someone else accomplish such a feat?"

Moreover, how was this a trivial matter?!

Setting aside the spiritual energy distribution, spiritual energy storms were the primary reason why the entire Central Domain was so chaotic and uninhabitable. It was once believed that even if a True Immortal descended, they would be powerless to resolve it—could it truly be as effortless as Zhou Hui made it sound?

—"Of course,"-Zhou Hui said, looking at Qu Qianxing.-"Since we recruited so many disciples, they ought to be put to good use, shouldn't they? After all, it's for the sake of building their own immortal caves."

Indeed. How could anyone expect a semi-retired old man like him to handle every little thing?
