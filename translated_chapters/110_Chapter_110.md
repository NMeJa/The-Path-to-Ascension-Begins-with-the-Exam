# Chapter 110: [Lu Shulong's Regret](<# "Literal CN: 路书泷的懊恼 (Lù Shūlóng de àonǎo) — Lu Shulong's Regret / Chagrin; chapter title revealing Lu Shulong's memories across all reincarnations and the truth behind the counterfeit world of the sixth cycle">) (第110章 路书泷的懊恼)

Up until this year, Lu Shulong had lived for a total of three thousand years.

Even among the Dragon Clan, this lifespan was by no means short.

Yet no one around him thought so; everyone believed he was merely a one-hundred-and-eight-year-old young dragon.

Well, in truth, Lu Shulong himself didn't quite understand whether looping through the exact same stretch of time counted as growing up—especially considering that the three thousand years he had tallied up included his time inside the egg.

Regardless, Lu Shulong possessed every single memory.

He remembered living for four hundred and thirteen years in his first life.

That year, grieved by Master's ascension, he hid alone (one dragon?) inside the alchemy chamber to escape reality, only emerging a beat late when the mountain-protecting grand formation was breached. In the end, he failed to protect his junior brothers, junior sisters, and the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

When restarting in the second cycle, Lu Shulong initially hadn't even realized this was Master's doing, assuming his own True Dragon power had reversed space-time. Yet he didn't confess having his memories to Master. It wasn't because he wanted to secretly save the sect and stun everyone, nor because he feared Master wiping his memories upon discovering the truth, but for a far more practical reason—

He didn't want his homework increased!

For subjects already studied once, Master would naturally impose higher demands 'at his discretion'.

Never mind someone with intact memories like Lu Shulong; take Junior Sister Mo Bei, whose memories were wiped completely clean—merely because her body retained sporadic muscle memory of sword forms, Master unhesitatingly increased her daily practice regimen by thirty percent! Thirty percent! It was even more severe for the other disciples.

Lu Shulong immediately [feigned amnesia](<# "Literal CN: 失忆 (shīyì) — amnesia; pretending to have lost memories across time reversals to avoid catastrophic increases in exam workloads and training regimens">) along with his fellow disciples.

With 'amnesia', you didn't have to shoulder so much extra homework, and you could even earn Master's glowing praise for performing brilliantly on your 'first time learning'. Why wouldn't one 'lose his memory'?

Facts also proved that 'amnesia' was the correct choice.

Although the Lu Shulong of the second cycle failed to save the Heavenly Spirit Sect—because the Immortal-Slaying Sword in the enemy's hands was effective against the Dragon Clan as well, an obvious conspiracy targeting the Heavenly Spirit Sect and Master, and the unprepared Lu Shulong ultimately collapsed in a pool of blood—enveloped by the torrent of light as Master reversed time, Lu Shulong felt at peace.

He felt that enemies having plans and targets was a 'good thing'. As long as at the critical moment, he sacrificed all his cultivation to Eldest... Second Senior Brother, right? Second Senior Brother was more reliable, not to mention Eldest Senior Brother wasn't around every time—yes, sacrifice all his cultivation to Second Senior Brother, temporarily elevating Second Senior Brother's cultivation beyond the Immortal-Slaying Sword's power threshold, and wouldn't they be able to counter-kill?

Thus in the third cycle, Lu Shulong planned everything out from inside his eggshell, simply waiting to be hatched by Master.

The result: when he emerged this time and looked around, he had been given away to someone else?!

Of course, given Zhou Hui's character, the word 'abandonment' did not exist.

Lu Shulong knew Master must have worried about implicating his disciples, which was why he entrusted them to others, meticulously selecting the foster guardians for each and every disciple.

For instance, Lu Shulong's 'adoptive guardian' was the Marine Clan of the Eastern Sea, this realm's Dragon King.

To them, a True Dragon like Lu Shulong was like an immortal to mortals. Consequently, they practically enshrined Lu Shulong, raising him even more conscientiously than an imperial prince of their own clan; whatever Lu Shulong requested, they never refused.

Yet what was the meaning of that?

If it wasn't Master, what was the meaning?!

Lu Shulong thought furiously. At a thousand years of age (including the time inside his egg), he ushered in his [rebellious phase](<# "Literal CN: 叛逆期 (pànnìqī) — rebellious phase / teenage rebellion">).

In that cycle, Lu Shulong went through great pains to track down his scattered brothers and sisters: helping those who could restore memories recover them, and guiding those who couldn't to get to know each other, thoroughly tangling the relationships among the disciples, which directly led to the [field of carnage](<# "Literal CN: 修罗场 (xiūluóchǎng) — shuraba / scene of carnage; here referring to messy, chaotic interpersonal drama and mutual sabotage among the disciples">) in the fourth cycle.

Lu Shulong was actually quite satisfied with this.

Because after the third cycle, Master clearly didn't dare 'release them into the wild' anymore.

At the same time, Lu Shulong was also in despair, because he discovered that even after he and his siblings had done so many things they shouldn't have, even in the fourth cycle where everyone turned against one another, Master never gave up on them.

Even if a single person met with an accident, Master would choose time reversal without hesitation.

—"Clearly you could have just ascended! Just abandon us and ascend! With disciples so hopeless, even if nothing went wrong before and Master ascended smoothly, after Master was gone, we'd still turn heaven and earth upside down! Wouldn't it be better to just act like we don't exist at all?"-Lu Shulong muttered.

Being completely abandoned by Master would of course be the worst outcome for Lu Shulong, but after experiencing four cycles of reincarnation, Lu Shulong only wished his master could be a bit more selfish. After all, direct disciples weren't biological sons and daughters—even if biological, facing ascension, any normal person would have given up!

Thus Lu Shulong was extraordinarily well-behaved in the fifth cycle, doing his utmost not to drag Master down, only begging for Master to ascend as quickly as possible.

Not knowing whether the Untiring Immortal Venerable was also weary, he was unusually hurried in that cycle, crossing his tribulation faster than any previous life. The result, however, was that the heavenly tribulation directly tore the entire world apart—

The worst cycle, the sixth, arrived.

\*

The "Seventh" Cycle.

—"The Southern Domain Saintess? She arrived so quickly?"-Zhou Hui said, somewhat surprised upon hearing Lu Shulong's report.

—"The Southern Domain Saintess!"-Jiuzhai almost leapt to his feet—though such a motion was a bit too stimulating for an old man, so he only symbolically straightened his back a fraction—and demanded of Zhou Hui:—"What did you do to her? Could this be the reason for the shifting of destiny?"

—"You don't know what Untiring did?"-Tao Zhu asked in surprise.

Theoretically, Jiuzhai ought to be able to 'see' anything in this world; he was usually just unwilling to speak, unable to speak, or dared not speak. Rarely did an event occur while Jiuzhai remained utterly in the dark.

This time, however, Jiuzhai admitted defeat.

Ancestor Jiuzhai coughed:—"As a fellow Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, I have long been unable to 'see' Untiring's future. At most, I can infer from others around him."

However, this cycle was an exception.

Although previous cycles deviated from heavenly fate due to Untiring's 'willfulness', they ultimately remained within the range that heavenly fate could correct.

Yet in this cycle, saying Jiuzhai was completely 'blind' was impossible, and the things Zhou Hui did weren't particularly extraordinary—at least not as complex as the third cycle, where he dumped his direct disciples onto others to raise.

After all, Zhou Hui in that life, considering his disciples' future, practically mobilized all his personal connections, begging powerhouses across the Five Domains and Six Abysses. Coupled with his disciples being far too adept at stirring up trouble, all the powerhouses ended up dragged into that chaos, yet even that hadn't changed heavenly fate.

Zhou Hui in this cycle could be considered returning to his origins, merely teaching at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and even—perhaps having thought things through—starting to host auctions and Immortal Conferences. Theoretically, he was comparatively 'resigned to fate'.

Jiuzhai originally thought the Untiring Immortal Venerable had finally given up on cleansing the world, and this life would repeat the rhythm of the first or second cycle. Never did he expect that in the blink of an eye, a massive shift of fortune stirring the entire Southern Domain would arrive.

After sensing the change in fortune, Jiuzhai had divined multiple times, unable to understand why this was happening.

Untiring clearly hadn't done anything special these past few days.

Most infuriating of all, Untiring clearly knew the reason, yet refused to say.

—"Fortune... Rather than being related to the Southern Domain Saintess, it's better to say it pertains to the entire Southern Domain,"-Zhou Hui offered no direct answer to Jiuzhai's interrogation, only shaking his head:—"However, there is no rush. This isn't something that can be achieved overnight. Instruct Qianxing and the others to receive Her Ladyship the Saintess first. I will meet her later."

—"How is there no rush?! You've stirred up such a monumental shift in fortune! How can it not be urgent?"-Jiuzhai, on the contrary, was exceedingly anxious.

As a divination cultivator ([riddle-maker](<# "Literal CN: 谜语人 (míyǔrén) — riddler / person who speaks only in cryptic prophecies; diviners who refuse to give straight answers">)), he actually hated other people being riddle-makers the most.

Yet not only did Zhou Hui ignore him, even Zhou Hui's disciple treated him like thin air.

—"Understood, Master. I will notify Second Senior Brother,"-Lu Shulong said, turning on his heel to flee.

—"Wait, Shulong,"-Zhou Hui stopped him.-"I'll inform Qianxing myself."

As he spoke, Zhou Hui casually snapped his fingers, and the sound of the snap transformed into a plump sparrow, fluttering clumsily toward the outside.

Seeing this, Lu Shulong tried to curve his lips into a smile, but failed.

[Looks like there's no escaping this!]

Lu Shulong thought. Sure enough, Zhou Hui directly posed the question he least wanted to answer:

—"Shulong, you remember everything about my time reversals?"

[—"...Yes."](<# "Speaker: Lu Shulong">)

—"Why didn't you say..."-Zhou Hui was about to ask, but paused, giving up on interrogating to the bottom.

What reason could a disciple have for hiding the truth and being unwilling to confide their real experiences?

Nothing more than finding Master untrustworthy.

True enough—after seven or eight reincarnations, what credibility was left?

Zhou Hui reflected, gazing at his fifth disciple with guilt, yet he still needed to obtain the most critical information from him:—"Then in your memory, how many reincarnations have occurred in total?"

—"Six times; this is the seventh,"-Lu Shulong gave the exact same count as Zhou Hui's tally.

Zhou Hui furrowed his brow slightly.

However, Lu Shulong quickly added:—"However, the sixth reincarnation had 'two'?"

[—"'Two'?!"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

—"Yes... I was just at the entrance and overheard Master's experiences. It seems in Master's memory, because of the fifth cycle's lightning tribulation, the entire world tore apart, so throughout your 'sixth cycle', you were constantly mending the world, right?"-Lu Shulong said with a bitter smile.

Zhou Hui was about to nod, only to see Lu Shulong's bitter smile twist:—"So the 'Master' on our side was indeed a counterfeit!"

—"That side?"-Zhou Hui caught the keyword.

—"Yes. The heavenly lightning of the fifth cycle split the world into two halves, but it might differ from what Master assumed,"-Lu Shulong explained.-"Both incomplete worlds existed."

Saying this, Lu Shulong raised his hand, also generating an illusion.

Originally, the Dragon Clan was also exceedingly skilled in illusions, but whether because Lu Shulong didn't want to reminisce, or because he had been influenced by Ran Deng and Guan Ye recently, what he manifested was a set of little [3D building blocks](<# "Literal CN: 3D建模感觉的小积木 — miniature building blocks with the aesthetic of modern 3D digital modeling, influenced by Ran Deng's gadgets and Guan Ye's blueprints">).

Upon these blocks stood the Heavenly Spirit Sect along with cute dolls resembling Zhou Hui and the disciples. Everyone was originally bickering and noisy, when a heavenly lightning bolt made of paper plummeted down, smashing onto the blocks.

The blocks and everything upon them instantly cracked in two.

Among them, Zhou Hui's miniature figure remained on the left half of the blocks, heavily injured, half of its body blasted into flying ash while the other half remained intact. Even so, after looking around in sorrow, it quickly rallied, gathering the fragments of the blocks and other little figures, attempting to mend and glue them back into their original shape.

—"This was Master's 'sixth reincarnation,'"-Lu Shulong explained.

He carefully set this illusion aside, then placed the other half of the blocks before him:

[—"However, this was our 'sixth reincarnation.'"](<# "Speaker: Lu Shulong">)

Upon the other shattered half of the blocks lay scattered more fragments of the disciples—or rather, their main bodies were on this side. Aside from that, there was also some ash.

The ash of Zhou Hui's doll.

Lu Shulong reached out, collecting those ashes and mixing them with other dust and dirt, sculpting another 'Zhou Hui'. However, this Zhou Hui looked peculiar, likely because it was sculpted from dust—black and filthy.

After sculpting this 'Zhou Hui', Lu Shulong snapped his fingers. The fragments of those disciples 'awakened', instinctively walking toward 'Zhou Hui'.

—"I don't know who did it. Someone sculpted this puppet using Master's lingering aura,"-a rare note of hatred emerged in Lu Shulong's tone.-"However, how could Master ever be counterfeited? Never mind me with memories of previous lives—even everyone else without memories quickly felt a sense of incongruity. Yet no matter where we searched, we couldn't find Master..."

Saying this, Lu Shulong's throat bobbed as he fought to control his emotions.

Yet he could not forget the terror of that time.

When he emerged from his eggshell and saw no Master, only a nonsensical [counterfeit](<# "Literal CN: 伪物 (wěiwù) — fake / counterfeit / puppet impostor">), and after searching the entire world was unable to find the true Master, Lu Shulong felt he was going mad.

The others were the same.

—Master seemed to have misunderstood one point: he appeared to believe the disciples' chaotic feelings regarding the third and fourth cycles were completely wiped away in the fifth cycle's heavenly lightning. In truth, those emotions were preserved intact into the sixth cycle—only within the 'other half of the world where Master was absent'. Thus everyone possessed more or less residual memories and emotions, acting with zero restraint.

Lu Shulong noted in his heart, but this was a part he could not tell Master.

Because everyone had agreed: if they could find the 'true Master' again, this time they would absolutely be 'good children', never doing things Master disliked. Even if Master wanted them to forget everything, they would obediently forget!

Regrettably, Lu Shulong had never learned techniques to control his own memories; he still hadn't forgotten.

[I've truly disappointed Master, haven't I?]

Peeking at Zhou Hui's expression, Lu Shulong said slowly:—"We didn't know what the 'counterfeit' was, so we decided to play along in a theater drama with them to see their ultimate objective."

As Lu Shulong spoke, spotlights shone down upon the little figures. The figures moved, performing like a puppet show, exceedingly cute.

Yet the play they performed was not nearly as cute—especially upon seeing Mo Bei's little figure embracing the strange gray mud together.

This was actually that [Heavenly Dao script](<# "Literal CN: 天道脚本 (tiāndào jiǎoběn) — the dogblood harem cultivation novel plotline imposed on the world">)!

A tempestuous wave surged within Zhou Hui's heart.

He had always believed this messy nonsense was something that would happen in the future and could still be prevented, never imagining these foolish disciples had already done it!

As a result, Zhou Hui watched helplessly as that detestable plot continued to unfold before his eyes, right until the Heavenly Spirit Sect was slaughtered and Lu Shulong's little figure burned down his own alchemy chamber.

—"I don't know what happened afterward,"-Lu Shulong said timidly.

Although Zhou Hui was already suppressing his emotions, terrifying spiritual pressure still swept across the entire cave dwelling.

Under such horrifying spiritual pressure, Lu Shulong saw the black lamb on the ground open one eye, then shut it at lightning speed.

[...I want to [play dead](<# "Literal CN: 装死 (zhuāng sǐ) — feigning death or unconsciousness to evade an awkward situation">) too!]

Lu Shulong thought enviously, yet continued speaking honestly:—"I was too cowardly, not daring to carry on. So after entrusting my power to Second Senior Brother, I bowed out early. Thus I don't know what transpired in 'this side's sixth cycle.'

—"However, after I 'died', I found myself back at Master's side,"-Lu Shulong said.

This point was reflected on the building blocks as well.

Along with Lu Shulong's 'death', he disappeared from the blocks on this side, but his fragment appeared on Zhou Hui's blocks, where 'Zhou Hui' joyfully picked him up and pieced him back.

—"In other words, when you burned yourself to death, you thought you would truly die?"-Zhou Hui glared strictly at Lu Shulong.

"..." That was indeed the case. Because the true Master was 'gone', when Lu Shulong burned himself to death at the time, he felt this cycle could not be reversed, and he was facing permanent death.

—"I'm sorry..."-Lu Shulong said, hanging his head.

He spoke with heartfelt sincerity, for immense remorse truly filled his heart.

He certainly didn't regret returning to Master's side; rather, he regretted his own cowardice.

—In the other half of the 'sixth cycle', he had indeed exited far too early, and thus knew nothing of what transpired.

Had he known death would return him to Master's side, Lu Shulong would have striven to stay until the very end like Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother! That way, he would have known who the culprit repeatedly plotting against the Heavenly Spirit Sect and forcing Master to reincarnate again and again truly was!

Clearly, as a True Dragon, an entity possessing complete memories, he was the most suitable candidate to gather intelligence!

Yet out of dread for that world without Master, he had sacrificed himself to Second Senior Brother early. But although Second Senior Brother was formidable, as a mortal, he had the hardest time retaining memories across every cycle—

[—"Do you know regret now?"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

At this moment, a gentle hand rested upon Lu Shulong's head.

Lu Shulong looked up, seeing Zhou Hui sighing as he looked at him.

Lu Shulong knew Master was completely misunderstanding. Master likely assumed he merely feared death.

However, letting Master continue misunderstanding like this was fine.

His cowardice held no right to be forgiven by Master.

Lu Shulong thought with slumped shoulders, yet couldn't resist shaking his head, nuzzling the crown of his head against that warm palm.

Feeling the soft hair beneath his hand, in an instant Zhou Hui no longer knew how to stay angry at this direct disciple who carried far too long a memory.

Zhou Hui could only say:—"Remember this lesson! Do not squander your life casually."

—"I won't anymore, Master."

[Next time, at least hold out until the very end.]

Lu Shulong made up his mind.

He then said ingratiatingly:—"However, only I was that useless. Judging from how the world was successfully mended by Master in the end, Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother's plan must have succeeded."

Saying this, he blew a breath toward the 'puppet show' half of the blocks, and that half-block shattered into fragments, which were picked up one by one by Zhou Hui's figure and glued into a complete world.

Clearly, this also signified that Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing had ultimately succeeded in destroying that half of the world, allowing Zhou Hui to retrieve the 'materials' to conduct his repairs.

Zhou Hui held zero joy regarding this.

He would rather these foolish disciples hadn't done such stupid things!

—"What plan did you just mention?"-Zhou Hui pressed.-"Why would it escalate to the point of destroying that half of the world?"

He recalled that the Heavenly Dao script contained no such scene! Once the 'protagonist' ascended, the cultivation realm's map was concluded.

—"...I don't know. Our plan was only to investigate the origin of the 'counterfeit' and find the real Master—we didn't know you weren't in that world,"-Lu Shulong said. Regrettably, he knew far too little.

Lu Shulong paused and added:—"However, that was Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother! Investigating to the end, would destroying one or two worlds be surprising?"

Given those two's temperaments, if in the end they couldn't find the real Master, destroying everything wouldn't be surprising at all!

[...Destroying one or two worlds?]

Zhou Hui felt a pang of stomach pain.

Yet he admitted Old Five was speaking the truth. When his eldest and second disciples stirred up trouble, they could cause a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator—who practically no longer counted as human—to develop a stomach ulcer.

No, not just the eldest and second disciples.

If this was something that had already transpired in the past, his entire plan would have to be adjusted.

Gazing at those building blocks, Zhou Hui asked:—"So when you 'departed', were you all deliberately acting according to what others wanted you to do?"

[No, I was acting... and conveniently burned what Heavenly Spirit Sect couldn't give to outsiders.]

[Second Senior Brother was likely acting too.]

[As for the others, it was hard to say.]

[For instance, Eldest Senior Brother didn't seem inclined to follow the script at all; he kept [dancing back and forth on the edge of death](<# "Literal CN: 在作死边缘来回跳动 (zài zuòsǐ biānyuán láihuí tiàodòng) — courting death repeatedly on the brink of disaster">), seemingly testing the other party's bottom line. Eldest Senior Sister was just being a [total slacker](<# "Literal CN: 摆烂 (bǎilàn) — letting things rot / slacking off completely">). Fourth Senior Brother was probably excluded from the plan—after all, his cultivation wasn't high, making him unsuitable for anything. Little Junior Sister and Little Junior Brother had joined the sect too recently; Second Senior Brother refused to tell them anything, only leading Little Junior Brother by the nose. And Little Junior Sister...]

—"Ah, Little Junior Sister!"-Lu Shulong's eyes widened, panicking as he told Zhou Hui:—"I almost forgot! Little Junior Sister's situation was the strangest! She seemed genuinely in love with that guy! Almost like she was possessed!"

Even thinking back carefully, Mo Bei was the strangest in every cycle!

Because Mo Bei's memories were wiped the cleanest! Others had residual feelings that couldn't be eliminated from previous cycles, but Little Junior Sister—while possessing physical muscle memory, remembered nothing in her head, truly starting over in every cycle! Under those circumstances, having no feelings for the counterfeit Master and selling him out directly was far too normal, to the point of nearly ruining Second Senior Brother's plan!

—"Bei'er..."-Zhou Hui breathed a sigh.

Tao Zhu, who had been listening in silence, suddenly asked:—"Wait, the 'Little Junior Sister' you speak of is the 'Mo Bei' who made contact with the Southern Domain Saintess, right? Then is this fortune incident okay? It won't be another 'possession', will it?"

Hearing this question, Lu Shulong wanted to refute, but suddenly saw a faint smile emerge upon Master's face.

Although it was just a faint trace, it seemed a hint of joy had finally returned.

—"Different,"-Zhou Hui said.-"This time, Bei'er is different—"
