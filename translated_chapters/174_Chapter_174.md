# Chapter 174: Shadows of the Past Life

—"Why aren't you dead?"-Mo Bei asked, couldn't care less about Mo Xi's feelings as she followed up with another [verbal knife](# "Literal CN: 补刀 (bǔdāo) — gaming slang for landing the finishing blow / twisting the knife with a follow-up remark").

She wasn't picking a fight this time; she was genuinely puzzled.

It wasn't that Mo Bei looked down on her twin brother. It was just that usually, as long as Mo Xi could use his brain to scam someone, he would absolutely avoid combat. Even the spells he studied were mainly those that looked overpoweringly terrifying on the surface but were actually designed to bluff and intimidate others. He was fundamentally incapable of winning an open, honest fight, let alone fighting continuously for so many days.

Indeed, Mo Xi did not look like he had suffered one bit.

—"Dead? Couldn't you hope for something better for me?"-Mo Xi grumbled, thoroughly dissatisfied. But as he surveyed the surrounding mountain of corpses and sea of blood, he immediately understood what Mo Bei meant.

—"What's going on on your end? Why did you slaughter so many demon beasts? Doing this on someone else's turf isn't great... Wait a minute, this quantity is way too absurd! It's only been a few [shichen](# "Traditional Chinese time unit: 时辰 (shíchen), equal to two modern hours")—how did you manage this?"-he countered.

—"A few shichen?"-Mo Bei stared at Mo Xi, seizing upon the key phrase.

Mo Xi also realized something was off, turning his gaze toward Mo Bei.

Next, Mo Xi explained the situation on his end to Mo Bei.

Mo Xi hadn't suffered any attacks, encountered no obstacles, and undergone zero assessments. He had simply arrived at the entrance of the demon race's treasury smoothly and safely.

Naturally, the demon race's secret treasure was not something they could display to just anyone. Therefore, Shui Huang'er had asked Mo Xi to wait in the front vestibule—a reasonable request; after all, it was a demon secret treasure, and consenting to let them enter the forbidden grounds at all out of deference to Master was already immensely generous. It was an entirely legitimate condition—so Mo Xi had obediently remained in the treasury's front vestibule.

He had waited there alone for a solid five or six [shichen](# "Traditional Chinese time unit: 时辰 (shíchen), equal to two modern hours; roughly ten to twelve hours"), tea refreshed through multiple rounds. Yet Master never emerged; instead, a little thief attempting to pilfer the treasure showed up, so he gave chase—

—"Hold on..."-hearing this, Mo Bei coldly interrupted.

[—"?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"You're saying you arrived right at their treasury, yet didn't cause any mischief at all? You even helped them catch a thief?"-Mo Bei asked.

Her face remained utterly expressionless as always, but her gaze clearly conveyed:

[It would be far more plausible to say you were the thief.]

Having been twins for so long, Mo Xi naturally understood her meaning at a glance.

—"What do you take me for?!"-Mo Xi fumed indignantly. He wanted to argue back, but paused and clicked his tongue with a 'tch,' clearly also feeling that the previous heroic deed had very little to do with his actual character.

—"It wasn't that I wanted to help the demon race,"-he finally told the truth.-"It's that the scoundrel wasn't just after the demon treasure—he also stole you..."

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

Mo Bei stared blankly as Mo Xi curled his lips into a sinister, gloomy sneer, glaring at the corpse beside her feet like a vengeful ghost demanding a soul, his voice dripping with mournful accusation:

—"Because this gentleman here happens to be the fated lover you never stop rambling about!"

Mo Bei instantly broke out in goosebumps, somewhat freaked out by Mo Xi's tone.

Mo Bei couldn't help lowering her head, inspecting the corpse at her feet closely for the first time.

However, having been sliced into two symmetrical halves by her sword, it was genuinely impossible to discern any signs of 'destiny' from the gory cross-section. If she had to admit to anything, when she first laid eyes on him, she indeed had a sensation of her 'heart skipping a beat'—though she had zero doubt it was purely an illusion born of skyrocketing blood pressure.

Put anyone in her shoes: on the verge of breaking through the very next second, only to be abruptly interrupted—anyone's blood pressure would go through the roof!

Mo Bei averted her gaze from the corpse in utter disgust.

—"I'm not an object,"-Mo Bei grumbled, dissatisfied.-"How could I be so easily stolen away?"

—"You weren't an object, but you were even worse than one!"-Mo Xi accused, yet that voice brimming with venomous resentment didn't sound like it was coming from his own chest—at least, not from his present self.-"Babbling endlessly about your 'destined one,' losing your mind the moment you saw a man! You abandoned the sect, betrayed your fellow disciples, and even helped him backstab Master—"

Reaching this point, Mo Xi suddenly froze.

[Mo Bei backstabbing Master?!]

[How could she dare?!]

[Even if she dared, could she actually pull it off?!]

[Given Master's supreme might, he isn't someone a person like Mo Bei could ever backstab!]

Mo Xi thought in utter confusion.

The moment the word "Master" crossed his mind, his resentment-flooded brain abruptly chilled. Not only did he realize he had just uttered something terribly bizarre, but he also recalled earlier anomalies.

[Why was I waiting for Master 'alone'?]

[I didn't come with Master by myself, did I... or did I? Was I?]

Feeling the chaos in his thoughts, Mo Xi pressed a hand to his forehead and circulated his cultivation technique for several cycles. As scorching spiritual energy spread through his body, warmth returned to his freezing limbs, and much of the fog inside his mind cleared away.

—"Sorry, my perception seems to have gone a bit haywire,"-Mo Xi said.-"I... Mo Bei?!"

His eyes went wide as he looked at Mo Bei.

Mo Bei still wore that wooden, dazed expression, yet tears rolled uncontrollably from her eyes.

[—"Mo Bei, Sis... Sister?!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi was terrified out of his wits, the final trace of mental haze instantly vanishing.

There was nothing else for it: having been together since their mother's womb (presumably), this was the first time Mo Xi had ever seen Mo Bei cry.

Mo Bei's temperament—to put it nicely—was aloof; to put it bluntly, she was utterly heartless. Whether treated well or mistreated, whether dealing with the scum of Fallen Immortal Palace or their senior martial brothers and sisters of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, nothing ever seemed to warm her icy, detached heart. By the same token, other people's malice seemed incapable of genuinely hurting her.

She was like an unfeeling, numb doll; barring physiological reasons, tears almost never fell from her eyes.

Yet right now, though Mo Bei's face remained blank, those tears streamed down ceaselessly.

—"Sister, what on earth is wrong with you?"-Mo Xi asked in a panic.

He raised his hands, clumsily trying to wipe away Mo Bei's tears with his sleeves.

Mo Bei parted her lips, a choked, tearful voice straining out from her throat:

—"I don't know. When I heard you say... heard you say that I backstabbed Master, my heart ached so terribly..."

She felt as though she had truly done it.

And that when doing so, her body's emotions, her soul, a piece of her very being had been ripped away.

It didn't necessarily hurt, but it was suffocatingly agonizing—excruciatingly agonizing.

Yet back then, she hadn't even been capable of crying. Only now did she possess the right to shed tears.

—"It doesn't hurt, it doesn't hurt anymore, don't cry..."-Mo Xi stiffly embraced Mo Bei.

For some reason, he also felt an inexplicable, aggrieved urge to weep.

[I did so much, so very much, until in the end I could barely maintain my own physical form... Yet Mo Bei never improved, her whole heart consumed by that filth, turning more and more into a puppet doll. And Master—Master wasn't real either, with no one knowing where he had gone...]

[Ah, what am I thinking?!]

Mo Xi froze once more. Refusing to let this emotion overpower him, he grabbed Mo Bei's hand:

—"There's something bizarre about this place. Let's go find Master!"

Saying so, he pulled Mo Bei toward the direction of the treasury he had come from.

Mo Bei hummed a soft affirmation, wiping the tears from the corners of her eyes.

Yet after taking two paces, she saw Mo Xi halt. Reaching into his sleeve, he produced a specially crafted storage pouch and aimed it at the corpse on the ground.

—"Why are you packing that thing up?"-Mo Bei asked, feeling sickened.

Just looking at the corpse filled her with revulsion now. Especially after what Mo Xi had said, she wished she could resurrect him just to execute him dozens more times using every creative method imaginable.

Mo Xi actually felt the same way.

However, his rationality held the high ground.

—"Our memories and emotions seem to have been compromised, and our current circumstances are very strange,"-Mo Xi said, frowning at the corpses of the other demon beasts.-"Whether this is a demon conspiracy or something else, in any case, this might be a crucial clue. Let's collect it and bring it back for Master to examine first."

Mo Bei had no objections.

In any case, leaving brainwork to Mo Xi was never wrong.

Yet just as Mo Xi tapped the storage pouch to draw the corpse in, and the body visibly shrank as it prepared to enter the pouch, Mo Bei suddenly felt a violent jolt of palpitations.

Before her brain could process it, one of her hands reflexively clamped onto the hand Mo Xi was holding her with, hurling Mo Xi backward, while her other hand gripped her soft sword without hesitation, flicking the two halves of the corpse high into the air.

*BOOM—!*

The corpse unexpectedly detonated, bits and fragments about to rain down like a shower of gore.

Yet Mo Bei did not let that happen.

Flicking her wrist, the soft sword coiled upward with hundreds, thousands of biting, frost-infused sword qi rays. Without missing a single drop, she minced the filth into even finer [minced meat](# "Literal CN: 臊子 (sàozi) — diced meat / minced meat topping for noodles, evoking Lu Zhishen mincing Master Zheng in Water Margin"), freezing every last morsel solid into an icy ball.

Looking closely, even the lean meat, fat, viscera, and bones were sorted apart.

—"All those years of watering the grass weren't wasted,"-Mo Xi praised.

Mo Bei coldly snorted.

Mo Xi tossed aside the ruined storage pouch and fished a book-shaped magical artifact from his sleeve, explaining to Mo Bei:

—"This thing is a bit eerie; I'm worried a regular storage pouch won't hold it. Just in time to test the Sumeru artifact I begged Eldest Senior Brother and Fourth Senior Brother to forge for me last time! I had to copy homework for Eldest Senior Brother for ages to get this—they say it can swallow the entire world!"

Saying so, he slammed a palm against the book.

The book rustled open on its own, pages fluttering with a *rustle-rustle* before gaping into a giant mouth with a ravenous *"Awoo!"*

Mo Bei stared at that mouth, finding it rather familiar.

[This so-called Sumeru artifact that 'can swallow the whole world'—it couldn't be Eldest Senior Brother's stomach pouch, could it?]

Mo Bei deeply suspected her idiot brother had been [freeloaded](# "Literal CN: 白票 / 白嫖 (báipiáo) — getting services or goods without paying; freeloaded / scammed") by Eldest Senior Brother once again.

Still, in this world, there was indeed nowhere more "secure" than Eldest Senior Brother's stomach—assuming you could ever retrieve what went in.

Mo Bei said nothing further.

She watched the frozen lump get swallowed whole by the book, seemingly struggling in futile defiance for a couple of beats before being thoroughly vacuumed inside.

At that instant, both Mo Bei and Mo Xi felt a sense of relief wash over them.

It felt as if a colossal crisis that had plagued them across two lifetimes, unsolvable and inescapable, had finally been completely resolved.

—"Let's go!"-Mo Xi said, extending a hand to Mo Bei.

Mo Bei nodded. She reached to take Mo Xi's hand, but upon seeing Mo Xi's appearance clearly, her eyes widened once again.

Mo Xi had "differentiated" *again*.

Not that Mo Xi had "differentiated" back to his original form. On the contrary, after disposing of "Mo Bei's destined one," his stature visibly shot up by a notch, seemingly restoring his previous height—or even standing a bit taller. Yet his figure had become remarkably slender, his bones appearing soft and supple, possessing the intoxicating grace of *"slender boughs swaying with faint crimson, casting charming silhouettes to flirt with the evening breeze."*

Looking at this, rather than calling it Mo Xi, it was more like a fully matured Mo Bei... No, Mo Bei believed that even if she grew for several more lifetimes, she could never become that flirtatiously alluring.

—"This time, you've definitely differentiated into a woman, haven't you?"-Mo Bei declared with absolute certainty.

—"So that's how it is..."

—"As expected of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. To think it has recorded so much, including the multiple cycles of reincarnation I caused..."

—"Most importantly, a neural network composed of Wood Spirits provides astronomical computing power capable of extrapolating the future, producing 'illusions' that blur reality and fantasy, and accurately projecting short-term futures..."

—"To use all of this merely to test human hearts—isn't that far too wasteful?"-Zhou Hui murmured to himself.

At this moment, he still sat within the white tree-shadow corridor.

To Mo Bei, eighty-seven days had elapsed; to Mo Xi, he had waited for eight shichen; yet to Zhou Hui, it was merely the span of a single cup of tea.

And within this brief cup of tea, the tangled threads of destiny had already been smoothed into neat, orderly alignment—like mechanism chains meticulously calibrated by a master clockwork engineer, intricately yet rhythmically guiding every bough to harmonize in song.

However, whether the Heavenly Dao or the threads of destiny, these were spectacles beyond the sight of ordinary living beings.

Beside him, Shui Huang'er and Tao Zhu saw none of it. All they knew was that the shadows of the branches before Zhou Hui swayed gently, and behind Mo Bei and Mo Xi, reflections suddenly surfaced within their shadows.

Within those projections, "another" Mo Bei and Mo Xi were moving about.

Tao Zhu didn't think much of it upon seeing this.

Having had his own experiences, he roughly guessed that the Mo Bei and Mo Xi in the shadows were likely the twins' souls or astral projections, triggered by latent desires and submerged in an illusion, searching for a way out.

Shui Huang'er, however, stood dumbfounded like a wooden chicken.

Prior to this, being able to perceive what trial an examinee was undergoing and their real-time state had been Shui Huang'er's exclusive privilege. Never did she expect that Zhou Hui would possess this exact authority as well.

This also meant that without even nearing the treasury, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had very likely already gained control over the secret treasure.

Although Shui Huang'er was the one who had offered to present the secret treasure, before she had even presented it, the man was already on the verge of obtaining it on his own! How could that not send a demon's heart leaping into her throat?!

The only detail leaving Shui Huang'er unsure whether to feel reassured or terrified was that, to this moment, the Untiring Immortal Venerable showed zero inclination to seize the secret treasure or harm the Sacred Tree.

That was precisely an "elimination" condition.

The moment any "guest" approaching the secret treasure displayed such an intention during the trial, they would be ensnared by the defensive traps.

Of course, facing the temptation of their own desires, immersed in an illusion where truth and falsehood were indistinguishable—perhaps even living an entire simulated lifetime—many "guests" performed actions that breached all boundaries, repulsive to the point of being unwatchable.

At least in the previous life, a certain "protagonist" had done exactly that; his desires had caused Shui Huang'er's hundreds of years of devotion to crumble to ashes in a single breath—even though that affection had come out of nowhere in the first place.

The Shui Huang'er of this lifetime knew nothing of that disgusting "script." Yet her guests this time—whether the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Mo Bei, or even Mo Xi—had not disappointed her in the slightest.

Especially the Untiring Immortal Venerable: his entire focus seemed to be on how to torment... no, educate his disciples.

Indeed, though Shui Huang'er couldn't comprehend the initial developments on Mo Xi's side, Mo Bei hacking demon beasts for eighty-seven straight days was something her very own Master had specifically assigned!

The Untiring Immortal Venerable evidently felt that this trial of desire was the perfect setup to instruct his disciple.

And conveniently, Mo Bei harbored an eager desire to learn.

It seemed that had that bizarre interloper not interrupted later, Mo Bei could have hacked her way clean through the Sacred Tree, thoroughly enjoying herself the entire time.

Such master and disciples were a sight Shui Huang'er had never witnessed before.

[Only, if this keeps going, what is the point of the trial? If the Immortal Venerable gains the secret treasure's recognition on his own, yet doesn't even value it that much, what am I supposed to do?]

Shui Huang'er thought with growing anxiety.

Fortunately (?), at that moment, Shui Huang'er heard the voice transmissions of the Wood Spirits:

—"Shui Huang'er, hurry and restrain the Untiring Immortal Venerable!"-a panicked Wood Spirit shouted.

[—"That's right! Whatever he is doing, interrupt him immediately!"](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit")

[—"Don't let him continue!"](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit")

—"B-but, the trial is still..."-Shui Huang'er stammered in dazed confusion.

[—"Passed!"](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit")

[—"Consider it passed!"](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit")

[—"Just make him stop right this instant!"](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit")

—"Passed? Really?"-Shui Huang'er chirped happily.

She had assumed that with the rigid stubbornness of those Wood Spirits, they would insist on waiting until the trial's time limit expired before declaring a pass! By then, [the daylilies would have gone stone-cold](# "Chinese Idiom: 黄花菜都凉了 (huánghuācài dōu liáng le) — waiting so long the daylily dish has gone cold; far too late").

However, if the Wood Spirit Elders acknowledged the Untiring Immortal Venerable, shouldn't they simply unlock the Cage of Desire directly? They could even step forth in person to meet the Immortal Venerable and express their approval—why send her to physically pull him back?

Such a thought flashed across Shui Huang'er's mind.

Without time to ponder it deeply, she flew toward Zhou Hui:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, you... Huh?!"

Mid-flight, Shui Huang'er violently recoiled several steps.

She realized that if she took even a few more steps forward, she would be swept into the trial of desire as well!

Shui Huang'er could only halt at a distance.

After all, cheering on Little Sister Mo Bei was one thing, but subjecting herself to that brutally inhumane pedagogy and forcing a cultivation breakthrough driven by her own desires—Shui Huang'er had no such masochistic inclinations.

She could only shout:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, you have passed the trial! You can come out now!"

—"No rush, no rush. I still have a few things left to process on my end,"-the Untiring Immortal Venerable answered.

[There's actually someone who insists on continuing the trial?!]

Shui Huang'er was at a total loss for what to do, only able to relay the Untiring Immortal Venerable's intention to the Wood Spirits:

—"He seems to want to complete the entire course of the trial..."

[—"Stop joking around!"](# "Speaker: Wood Spirit Elder")

On the other end, the Wood Spirit Elder shrieked in absolute hysteria:

—"Do you think he's passing a trial?! He is testing us!"
