# Chapter 69: A Minor Surprise (第69章 一点小意外)

[...All the children?]

[As hostages?]

Master's suggestions could never be wrong.

Indeed. Given the cognitive development of mortals, if someone reached the age of twenty without basic childhood literacy and schooling, achieving profound mastery in mathematics was virtually impossible. Those capable of truly absorbing the knowledge were overwhelmingly young children. Moreover, by casting a wide net, mathematical talent was remarkably easy to identify; three to five years was generally enough to reach a definitive verdict—far simpler and less taxing than divining the elusive presence of innately gifted individuals across the cosmos.

The only thing to worry about was the pushback from the children's parents.

After all, they would be separated for three to five years. He had to devise a method to make them agree "willingly and wholeheartedly"...

Qu Qianxing pondered deeply.

Emotionally, he felt that everything Master proposed was correct, yet his exceptionally sharp intellect never permitted him to blindly obey; his mind automatically ferreted out the vulnerabilities in any proposal and engineered compensatory measures.

In ordinary administrative affairs, an utterly meticulous disciple—or rather, subordinate—like Qu Qianxing was wonderfully dependable and a joy to employ. Yet whenever he was given free rein, he was capable of resorting to downright terrifying measures simply to achieve Master's directives with absolute perfection.

Watching Qu Qianxing's brooding, contemplative expression, Zhou Hui knew immediately that his second disciple was overthinking things yet again.

He tapped Qu Qianxing's forehead with amused exasperation:

—"What are you worrying about?"

—"How to coerce those parents into handing over their children,"-Qu Qianxing replied honestly.

—"?"-Zhou Hui blinked, momentarily caught off guard.-"Coerce? Attending school for half a day every day hardly calls for coercion, does it?"

—"Half a day?"-Qu Qianxing asked, completely baffled.

—"You didn't actually think I intended to bring all those children up to Heavenly Spirit Sect, did you?"-Zhou Hui smiled wryly.-"There is no need, nor is it feasible."

After all, the vast majority of the demographic they were currently discussing were mere mortals. Placing them inside an immortal cultivation sect, even if they could be put to some utilitarian use, would bring zero benefit to those children's future lives.

—"Then what does Master mean...?"-Qu Qianxing asked in perplexity.

Zhou Hui considered explaining the concept of a "school," but after a moment's thought, he rose to his feet and extended a hand toward Qu Qianxing:

—"Come with me,"-Zhou Hui said.

* * *

Toward his most capable and favored direct disciple, Zhou Hui naturally saw no reason to keep the existence of White Jade Capital a secret.

With a single spatial teleportation, bypassing Blood Demon Hall entirely, he brought Qu Qianxing straight into the heart of White Jade Capital.

Qu Qianxing was equally stunned by this primordial ancient metropolis.

—"As expected of Master!"-Qu Qianxing exclaimed.

Qu Qianxing felt that his imperial-top-scholar-level vocabulary became utterly impoverished whenever he stood before Zhou Hui. He even felt that the crude exclamation of [holy crap!](# "Literal CN: 窝草 (wǒ cǎo) — homophonic slang for 'holy f***' / 'holy shit'") favored by Eldest Senior Brother's monster friends was far better suited to expressing his current state of mind.

Hardly knowing how to put it into words, Qu Qianxing stammered:

—"You merely took a brief journey outside, yet you actually uncovered the legendary White Jade Capital?!"

—"...I do not believe this was a mere karmic encounter,"-Zhou Hui said, furrowing his brow slightly.

White Jade Capital was situated so remarkably close to the "Passage to True Immortality"—no, precisely because it was White Jade Capital, an ancient teleportation array leading directly to the True Immortal Realm had been anchored here. This was no coincidence. Taken together with the karmic trap engineered against Yuwen Xuan, Zhou Hui felt that this "serendipitous encounter" seemed specifically prepared for a certain targeted individual.

—"Of course it isn't mere fortune!"-Qu Qianxing declared passionately.-"Everything Master possesses was won through pure, unadulterated strength!"

...Well, that wasn't entirely true either.

Watching Qu Qianxing's flushed, ecstatic face, Zhou Hui realized the boy was incapable of absorbing any reasoned explanation at the moment. Rare as it was to see Qu Qianxing this overjoyed, Zhou Hui simply accompanied him on a leisurely stroll through White Jade Capital.

Yet Qu Qianxing was, after all, the most composed and self-disciplined among all the disciples.

Within a few minutes, he had firmly reined in his excitement and recalled Master's purpose in bringing him here:

—"Master, you do not intend to have those children come to White Jade Capital, do you?"-Qu Qianxing asked, his brow furrowing.-"Setting aside the unformed nature of young children, their parents harbor quite a few ulterior motives. Should this place be exposed, I fear it would place us at a severe disadvantage."

In Qu Qianxing's view, relocating Heavenly Spirit Sect en masse to White Jade Capital would be far more sensible.

Zhou Hui, however, entertained no such ambition.

Mount Tianling was an auspicious cradle of geomancy personally handpicked by him—blessed with mountains, flowing springs, mineral veins, and fertile fields. His rebirth waypoint had also been anchored there—though it could likely no longer be utilized going forward. Why would he relocate when everything was perfectly fine where it was?

—"How could I possibly bring them here? It would be entirely inappropriate,"-Zhou Hui dismissed the idea.

He guided Qu Qianxing toward the Academy of White Jade Capital.

It was a grand palace complex situated in the foremost of White Jade Capital's Five Cities. Its exquisite architectural pavilions, sprawling plazas, and elegant student residences all testified that in antiquity, only the most elite scholars from across the realm possessed the qualifications to set foot here.

Zhou Hui did not linger by the palace classrooms, leading Qu Qianxing instead to the Forest of Steles situated in the rear courtyards.

The Stele Forest recorded the inscribed calligraphy, poetry, and insights left behind by outstanding disciples of ancient eras, though the foremost primary stele stood pristine and bare.

—"This monument is the Student Stele of the White Jade Capital Academy,"-Zhou Hui explained to Qu Qianxing, drawing upon his newly acquired understanding of the city.-"Prior to the start of each academic year, it automatically displayed the names of every eligible disciple across the Five Domains and Six Abysses."

—"The Five Domains and Six Abysses?"-Qu Qianxing was utterly shocked.-"Every single cultivator in the world?!"

Even in the contemporary cultivation world, not a single sect dared claim the capability to census and track every cultivator in existence.

Take Qu Qianxing himself, for instance: he had originally been a [Forgotten Immortal](# "Literal CN: 遗仙人 (yíxiānrén) — mortal possessing spirit roots who missed their karmic window to cultivate, living out an ordinary mortal life").

The term "Forgotten Immortal" referred to "mortals" who possessed spirit roots yet never obtained the karmic opportunity to embark upon immortal cultivation. Endowed with the latent blessing of spirit roots, they might live slightly longer than ordinary mortals, possess sharper intellects, or exhibit preternatural specialization in certain arts. Yet once the window was missed, it was missed forever; they could only live out ordinary, fleeting lives just like common mortals.

Qu Qianxing had originally been destined for the very same fate.

Because his countenance was excessively delicate and striking, he had been constantly targeted by ill-intentioned predators since early childhood. Consequently, his parents had hardly dared allow him to step outside the family courtyard, causing him to miss countless "immortal opportunities." It was not until he passed the imperial examinations with top honors that his name became known across the empire. By then, however, the prime age for cultivating immortality had long since passed. Had it not been for the grotesque, sordid ordeal that unfolded afterward, Qu Qianxing would never have stepped foot into an immortal sect again.

Who could have imagined that in the antiquity of White Jade Capital, they actually tracked the movements of every cultivator in the world?

—"Rather than saying White Jade Capital held dominion over all cultivators, it would be more accurate to say that the cultivators of the Five Domains and Six Abysses nourished White Jade Capital,"-Zhou Hui said.

Indeed. Otherwise, how could a colossal city floating high in the sky—lacking mountains, flowing water, mineral veins, and arable soil—possibly sustain itself?

Relying solely on a handful of high-tier cultivators would certainly never suffice to maintain it.

Thus, White Jade Capital had adopted a remarkably unique methodology: drawing power from every single cultivator across the Five Domains and Six Abysses. This was also the pivotal reason it had managed to preserve its structural integrity even through cataclysms.

Qu Qianxing listened in a daze. Suddenly, he asked with hesitant realization:

—"Master, you said you hold the authority over White Jade Capital. Could it be..."

Hearing this, Zhou Hui gave Qu Qianxing an appreciative glance.

Precisely!

The supreme value of White Jade Capital lay not in its jade masonry, but in the fact that it had once been the grand aggregate of power for all cultivators in this world. Even though it had fallen beneath the earth, so long as that connection was re-established, its celestial functions could be fully restored.

In other words, what Zhou Hui had obtained was far more than mere administrative deed over White Jade Capital.

If he could induce the world's cultivators to willingly and wholeheartedly forge a link with White Jade Capital once more, he would wield administrative control over the supreme tier of authority in this entire world!

That was why Zhou Hui sought to resurrect White Jade Capital.

In that instant, Qu Qianxing was not merely struck speechless; he found it difficult even to breathe.

He looked upon his master with uncomprehending awe, unable to fathom how Zhou Hui, possessing a secret of this magnitude, could stand here with such effortless composure, speaking in a tone as relaxed and casual as if he were merely presiding over Heavenly Spirit Sect's end-of-term examinations.

—"We begin with the Academy,"-Zhou Hui mapped out his blueprint.

His tone held no feverish ambition, only genuine admiration for ancient cultivators:

—"To thoroughly restore the Academy's facilities requires more than mere spiritual energy. Judging by the residual spell matrices, the ancients had already researched [Entering the Dao through Learning](# "Literal CN: 以学入道 (yǐ xué rù dào)") and [Entering the Dao through Teaching](# "Literal CN: 以师入道 (yǐ shī rù dào)"). Consequently, it requires the accumulation of [Scholar's Qi](# "Literal CN: 才气 (cáiqì) — literary / scholarly aura"). Nevertheless, with my current cultivation, several foundational functions can already be engaged. They will serve nicely to assist you in establishing our 'school,' while simultaneously accumulating Scholar's Qi for the Academy."

Zhou Hui then proceeded to explain the modern concept of a "school" to Qu Qianxing.

For the very first time in his life, Qu Qianxing had no mind to absorb what his master was saying.

His mind was still swimming in chaos as he gazed upon White Jade Capital and the Academy before him, feeling as though he were trapped in a surreal dream.

But then, who on earth could remain calm after learning the truth of White Jade Capital?!

Only someone of Master's sublime stature could remain entirely unruffled!

Qu Qianxing's reverence for Zhou Hui deepened by yet another layer.

Of course, for someone who was already a diehard zealot, an increase in reverence was practically redundant.

* * *

Qu Qianxing was, in the end, Qu Qianxing.

Although his mind remained in utter turmoil regarding the revelations of White Jade Capital, when executing the tasks assigned by his master, he was as thoroughly conscientious and responsible as ever—especially since, in his eyes, this entire affair had been precipitated by him in the first place.

By the third day, the official enrollment notice had already been dispatched to all their "cooperative partners."

Yet due to how Qu Qianxing had handled the situation back then, the attitudes of the various recipients who received the notice were night and day.

—"Are the immortals of Heavenly Spirit Sect living saints?"-Qiao Linggen murmured, deeply moved as he read the notice.-"Not only did they erect a barrier to protect our village, they're even establishing a school for us!"

Hearing him say that, a villager standing right beside Qiao Linggen asked nervously:

—"Does it cost spirit stones?"

—"Has your head been flooded with water?"-another villager barked loudly before Qiao Linggen could even open his mouth.-"Someone is willing to come and teach us immortal arts, and you're actually fretting over a few measly spirit stones?!"

—"Heh heh,"-the villager scratched his head with a seemingly honest, rustic chuckle, though inwardly he remained entirely unconvinced.

He figured that with dirt-poor [mud-legged](# "Literal CN: 泥腿子 (nítuǐzi) — barefoot mud-legged peasants / dirt-poor commoners") origins like his own, the children he sired couldn't possibly turn into dragons or phoenixes, let alone master divine immortal arts. It was far more dependable to save up a few extra spirit stones for his sons' bride-prices or his daughters' dowries.

Qiao Linggen shot the villager a faint, knowing smirk. Having lived in the same village for decades, knowing everyone inside and out, he knew exactly what the man was thinking.

—"It costs zero spirit stones!"-Qiao Linggen announced.-"Otherwise, why would I say they are living saints? This school is completely free!"

—"Zero costs across the board?"-a villager asked in disbelief.

—"Well... not entirely zero requirements,"-Qiao Linggen clarified, scanning the notice.-"Those who undergo the 'examinations' with outstanding marks will receive priority recruitment by the Immortals' Bazaar and Heavenly Spirit Sect to work for them."

—"That's an incredible blessing!"-the villagers cheered in excitement.

Even the villager who had initially scoffed at the school couldn't help but feel his heart race with temptation.

After all, that was the Immortals' Bazaar!

Becoming an immortal was a pipe dream, but if their child could secure a job as a hired hand in the Immortals' Bazaar, their livelihood for the rest of their days would be secured.

Now he worried instead whether his own brat was even qualified to enroll:

—"Can any child attend?"

—"Of course—"-Qiao Linggen looked around the perimeter of the coral tree, noticing more and more villagers gathering until practically the entire village was present. He simply projected his voice and read aloud:

> "All children who have reached the actual age of seven years must enroll in the White Jade Capital Preparatory School. The academic curriculum spans six years. Those with outstanding performance shall be eligible for advanced studies."

—"Six years..."-the villagers murmured amongst themselves.

Someone asked loudly:

—"So every child over seven can be sent up to Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"Keep dreaming!"-Qiao Linggen barked, realizing they had completely misunderstood.-"The school is located right where we board the Kunpeng! Four [shichen](# "A shichen (时辰) is a traditional two-hour unit; four shichen equals eight hours") a day, six classes daily, and they come home to sleep in their own beds at night!"

He paused and added firmly:

—"Only those who study well will qualify for 'advanced studies'!"

—"Ah, so they can't go up directly!"-many villagers sighed in disappointment.

Yet upon hearing that not just anyone could go up to the sect, their disappointment was tempered by a profound sense of reassurance:

Now that made sense!

If the immortals carted off every single child in one fell swoop, wouldn't immortal status be utterly worthless?!

Even in mortal kingdoms, when an emperor recruited civil officials, they had to sit for examinations first.

Clearly, the immortals intended to filter out the prime seedlings before investing in advanced cultivation!

Once the villagers realized this "school" functioned like an ordinary academy, a barrage of questions erupted:

—"Does my daughter have to go too?"-a village woman asked.-"Her marriage has already been betrothed and she's practically about to wed. Learning this stuff won't do her any good—"

[—"Exactly! If a girl gets selected, aren't we just handing the benefits over to her in-laws?"](# "Speaker: Villager")

[—"It's not just the girls! My Third Brat is getting older; he probably can't absorb any book learning anyway..."](# "Speaker: Villager")

[—"Aye, they're already at marrying age, let's just let it slide!"](# "Speaker: Villager")

In rustic rural backwaters like this, a ten-year-old child was already considered a small adult—half a full laborer for farm work. The villagers naturally balked at losing such helping hands from their fields and chores.

Qiao Linggen's temper flared as he listened:

—"They must go! Every single one of them! The immortals decreed *all children*, not for you lot to pick and choose!"

Besides, what was wrong with girls? There were plenty of women running shops in the Immortals' Bazaar, and the most profitable storefronts were all owned and operated by women!

—"It's rare enough to encounter such a blessing, so stop concocting your nonsensical excuses!"-Qiao Linggen roared.

As always, he ruthlessly suppressed all dissenting opinions.

—"Not just the children—you adults are going to study in the evenings as well! The entire village is going!"-Qiao Linggen barked with absolute autocratic authority.-"The school will hold [Night Literacy Classes](# "Literal CN: 扫盲班 (sǎománg bān) — literacy classes / anti-illiteracy crash courses") in the evenings to teach reading, writing, and arithmetic! Every single one of you will go and learn something, so that come next harvest, you won't be bickering over who got the better deal or who owes what, creating endless headaches for everyone!"

* * *

While that was the scene in Qiao Family Village, a completely different spectacle unfolded in Longmen Town.

Seeing the announcement appear upon the coral tree, the townsfolk immediately erupted into paranoid chatter:

—"All of them have to go?!"-an anxious townsman cried.-"Isn't this demanding that we [sacrifice virgin boys and girls](# "Literal CN: 献上童男童女 (xiànshàng tóngnán tóngnǚ) — ancient trope of offering virgin boys and girls to demonic deities or corrupt immortals")?!"

—"Surely not? Doesn't it say they only go during the day and come back at night?"-a more level-headed townsman reasoned.-"Besides, it clearly states it is a proper school for learning."

[—"Tch, you actually believe whatever they tell you?"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")

[—"Exactly! Have you ever heard of cultivating immortality right on your doorstep?"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")-the townsfolk argued heatedly.-[—"Furthermore, those immortals have endless sorceries! Who knows if a child who goes out in the morning won't come back at night replaced by an entirely different changeling?!"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")

[—"Even if they aren't replaced, who's to say their souls haven't been tampered with?"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")

A woman began wailing on the spot:

—"Oh, my poor child—!"

Mei Xianyu, the mayor of Longmen Town, arrived tardily, only to be greeted by this hysterical, chaotic spectacle.

—"Nobody has ordered you to send them off yet! What are you wailing for, as if mourning a funeral?!"-he roared, thoroughly irritated.

He then glared fiercely at the merchant beside him:

—"Lou Wu, this disaster is all your doing!"

[And if I terminate the contract with Heavenly Spirit Sect right now and forfeit all those spirit stones, would you agree?]

Lou Wu grumbled inwardly. But when it came to losing out on spirit stones, the very first person who would object was Lou Wu himself. Thus, he maintained an amicable, placating smile:

—"This isn't coercive, is it?"

—"It explicitly specified *all children*, how is that not coercion?!"-a townsman challenged.

Some townsfolk looked toward Lou Wu with a glimmer of hope:

—"Could it be... there is another way?"

—"Indeed. Although it says all children, Heavenly Spirit Sect actually has its own rules: they refuse disciples who belong to other sects!"

Lou Wu's mind worked quickly. Having loitered around the Immortals' Bazaar earlier, he had gathered an earful of rumors regarding Heavenly Spirit Sect. Scanning the notice, he immediately spotted a loophole—and spared himself from being beaten to death by the furious townsfolk.

Lou Wu offered his cunning scheme:

—"So, all you need to do is find a cultivator—doesn't need to be anyone formidable, just someone registered with the Wanhe Gang Rogue Cultivator Guild—and have your child take them as master! That should easily circumvent the requirement."

The townsfolk exchanged glances, finding the logic sound.

Besides, in the Central Domain, what town or village didn't harbor a few destitute, down-on-their-luck cultivators? Impatient villagers immediately swarmed toward Lou Wu:

—"Lou Wu, I recall you possess spirit roots as well, don't you?"

—"You caused this whole mess, you can't shirk responsibility now!"

Seeing the mob surrounding Lou Wu, a few quick-witted townsfolk turned on their heels and sprinted toward the town's Wanhe Gang branch office.

The plaza around the coral tree once again devolved into a clamorous vegetable market.

At that moment, Mayor Mei Xianyu suddenly stomped his foot:

—"Halt!"

—"Silence!"

—"What do you think you are doing?!"-he demanded, glaring at the crowd with bulging eyes.

—"Lord Mayor, our children..."-a townsman stammered.

—"Children, children—all you can see is the tip of your own noses!"-Mei Xianyu scolded.-"Have you already forgotten how, just a few days ago, we were trapped inside this town by Heavenly Spirit Sect for breaching the contract?!"

Hearing this, the townsfolk showed expressions of lingering dread.

They had suffered agonizing torment back then, forced to tear up several lucrative agreements with other factions at great loss and swear vicious heavenly oaths never to breach transactions with Heavenly Spirit Sect again before finally regaining their freedom.

None of them wished to endure that nightmare a second time.

—"But... are we truly to hand our children over just like that?"-an aggrieved townsman asked.

—"Lou Wu's idea is indeed a clever one, and technically doesn't violate regulations,"-Mei Xianyu analyzed with eyes closed.-"However, when has Heavenly Spirit Sect ever been reasonable? If not a single person sends a child, they will undoubtedly lock down our town once more without listening to reason..."

—"Then..."-the townsfolk murmured uncertainly.

—"Quotas!"-Mei Xianyu snapped his eyes open and barked.-"We shall establish town quotas! Proportional to household size, each family may only retain one to three children!"

Hearing this, the townsfolk erupted into heated debate.

—"Lord Mayor, three is far too few! My household has eight children!"-a townsman cried.

—"Aye, that's completely unfair to us! Old Li's family has only one child, so doesn't that mean he keeps his whole family?!"-another protested.

—"Had I known, I would have sired fewer brats..."-someone muttered under his breath.

Yet by seriously calculating the numbers, these townsfolk had clearly resigned themselves to "sacrificing" a few of their children.

—"What are you bickering about?!"-Mei Xianyu sneered.-"Don't forget, my own household has fourteen children!"

At this point, his eyes reddened slightly:

—"Who has sacrificed more than my family...? But there is no other way! For the sake of the town, this is the only path I can take."

Seeing the mayor looking so grief-stricken, the townsfolk gradually settled down.

They completely overlooked the fact that the mayor's fourteen children were the result of taking seven or eight concubines and keeping mistresses outside solely in a desperate quest to sire sons. And out of those fourteen, exactly three were boys—conveniently fitting right within his "one to three" quota!

Seeing the tension dissipate, Mei Xianyu added:

—"Submit the names of the bloodlines you wish to preserve in your households. I can petition Shengmo Sect to accept them as lay disciples. With the protection of an established sect, wouldn't that be infinitely better than some rogue cultivator? Even if Heavenly Spirit Sect comes inquiring, they won't be able to find any fault."

With matters explained to this point, the townsfolk accepted the reality, grudging though they were.

Silently tallying the most suitable "heirs" in their minds, they gradually dispersed.

Once the crowd had thinned out, Lou Wu immediately fawned over the mayor:

—"Lord Mayor, you truly exhaust your heart and soul for this town! Had it not been for your wisdom, I would have nearly committed another catastrophic blunder."

—"Hmph,"-the mayor scoffed with aloof pride.

—"However, doing it this way... won't there be a few too many girls...?"-Lou Wu asked with lingering worry.

It was plainly evident that households would overwhelmingly choose to retain their sons. Lou Wu worried this wouldn't pass muster with Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Isn't having more girls even better?"

Mei Xianyu's mind was stuffed with who-knew-what sordid, gutter filth. He genuinely believed Heavenly Spirit Sect demanded them to "sacrifice virgin boys and girls." Thus, for the sake of the town—scratch that, for his own selfish sake—he was genuinely prepared to discard those children:

—"Could it be they built this school truly intending to teach them anything?"

—"That makes sense too,"-Lou Wu chimed in casually.

Inwardly, however, he was somewhat unconvinced, as he hardly believed Heavenly Spirit Sect was as vile as Mei Xianyu imagined.

Even so, this was a brilliant move!

Whatever Heavenly Spirit Sect intended, even if they truly wanted to recruit disciples, having a roster composed mostly of girls would never do.

After all, wasn't the number of male immortals vastly greater among cultivators?

Lou Wu concluded with supreme confidence, trailing behind Mei Xianyu to resume buttering up the mayor.

* * *

—"Eh?"

Because of these superfluous manipulations, when Qu Qianxing examined the specific enrollment numbers, he couldn't help but blink in bewilderment.

—"What is it?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"It is rare to see an enrollment roster with such a remarkably balanced male-to-female ratio,"-Qu Qianxing remarked, his expression rather peculiar.

After all, in past years, the sect's Major Recruitment had also been overseen by Qu Qianxing.

Unlike the Minor Recruitment's open "mountain-gate trials," Major Recruitment involved Qu Qianxing leading disciples to mortal settlements and familiar cultivation clans throughout the Mount Tianling region to personally select children with suitable spirit roots.

Yet during those Major Recruitments, the children presented to Qu Qianxing were overwhelmingly boys.

—"Because at the grassroots mortal tier, it remains a patriarchal society,"-Zhou Hui observed, entirely unsurprised.

The higher one climbed in the cultivation world, the more raw power flattened physical biological differences, leading to greater gender equality. Instances of female powerhouses taking a dozen male concubines, or a sect's chosen successor being a woman, were by no means uncommon.

Yet neither were they ubiquitous.

For the higher the realm, the fewer female immortals there were.

This was not because women were ill-suited to immortal cultivation, but rather because from the very initial screening stage, the number of female candidates was drastically suppressed.

This was an outcome shaped by societal structure. Whether out of malicious pragmatism—*["Why should a girl cultivate immortality? Raising her so big only for her not to marry off and earn a bride-price, isn't that a total loss?"](# "Speaker: Mortal Clan Mindset")*—or protective goodwill—*["A girl faces danger too easily out in the world, and who knows whether these sects are reputable?"](# "Speaker: Mortal Clan Mindset")*—mortals instinctively hid their daughters away, filtering them out of the recruitment pool entirely. In that regard, having a sect like Blood Demon Hall nearby—which maintained far more accommodating enrollment policies for women—was an extraordinary blessing.

Deprived of the opportunity of Major Recruitment, a young girl seeking to cultivate immortality required monumental karmic luck. After all, a mortal man could set out into the world to seek immortality even in his advanced years, whereas a woman was denied even that autonomy, cutting off even the path of becoming a rogue cultivator.

Cultivation clans, by contrast, treated sons and daughters with far greater parity. After all, they understood that producing an immortal—regardless of sex—could shelter their clan for centuries to come.

Yet at the second stage, even after entering an immortal sect, female cultivators remained at a severe disadvantage.

This was not solely due to internal sect sordidness—though such depravities certainly existed, and in abundance. Even within upright sects, during secret realm expeditions and martial tournaments, the attrition rate among female cultivators was disproportionately higher. In a cultivation world ruled by the law of the jungle, before reaching the Golden Core stage—where physical biological differences were thoroughly erased by spiritual metamorphosis—female cultivators were, in the eyes of many male cultivators, nothing short of prey. Not to mention horrors like cultivation furnaces that commodified human beings into chattel.

(In this respect, female cultivators hailing from mortal backgrounds suffered the cruelest fate. Unfamiliar with the perils of the cultivation world from the start, and compounded by mortal stereotypes of 'ethereal fairy maidens,' the girls specially selected to be 'offered' to immortals were naturally the most graceful and beautiful. Consequently, they were the most prone to premature demise before ever reaching Golden Core.)

Filtered through layer after grueling layer of attrition, the female cultivators who ultimately stood at the pinnacle were few and far between.

—"...I see,"-Qu Qianxing said, his brow furrowing deeply.

He understood the moment he heard it.

After all, though he was not a female cultivator, thanks to that face of his, he had experienced the exact same treatment firsthand.

It was for this very reason that Qu Qianxing, despite possessing an exceptional spirit root, had never been selected by any immortal sect during his childhood.

Gazing at the roster, Qu Qianxing's expression turned increasingly solemn. He took the initiative to inquire of Zhou Hui:

—"Master, that body-refinement cultivation method from Blood Demon Hall—can mortals practice it as well?"

—"With a few modifications, certainly,"-Zhou Hui said.-"After all, we could never abandon [Physical Education class](# "Literal CN: 体育课 (tǐyù kè) — PE / gym class")!"
