# Chapter 175: The Master of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree

Zhou Hui was indeed testing the Wood Spirits.

No—to say he was specifically testing the Wood Spirits wasn't entirely accurate either.

Ask yourself: who, upon getting their hands on an unprecedented [super biological computer](# "Literal CN: 超级生物计算机 (chāojí shēngwù jìsuànjī) — Zhou Hui applying modern computer architecture concepts to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's Wood Spirit neural network"), could resist the urge to run a benchmark and test its performance?

In any case, Zhou Hui certainly couldn't.

He promptly launched the most resource-intensive "game" in his possession:

Modifying the Heavenly Dao.

On this point, Zhou Hui didn't even need to actively hack Saintess Shui Huang'er's administrative privileges over the Sacred Tree. Simply approaching it from the angle of "desire" was enough to prompt the Sacred Tree to execute his commands of its own accord.

And thus, the Wood Spirits suffered terribly.

They thought they had witnessed every conceivable desire: those coveting secret treasures and boundless wealth; those yearning for wanton slaughter; those craving to toy with human hearts; and even arrogant, formidable demon emperors seeking to unify the Three Realms and dominate all living beings.

Yet to an ancient tree that had existed since the dawn of creation, such desires were far too minuscule, far too insignificant. They were as trivial as an ant perched on a leaf craving a few drops of sweet tree sap. And typically, that very sap—those desires—would drown those tiny ants, turning their corpses into nutrients for the Sacred Tree.

Until the Wood Spirits ran into the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

[What in the world was this Immortal Venerable's desire?!]

It not only involved six or seven temporal rollbacks and cosmological reboots, entangling countless, unfathomable cycles of cause and effect, but also reached directly up to the lofty Heavenly Court!

And that wasn't even the most damnable part.

The most lethal factor was that the Untiring Immortal Venerable's desire wasn't merely the alteration of his personal destiny.

If it were solely about him—whether his desire to obtain the secret treasure was to conquer the Heavenly Court and become the Celestial Emperor, or to comprehend the Great Dao and become the sole True God of the three thousand worlds—the required computing load would have been negligible. In fact, with his own cultivation and might, he could have achieved it without needing the secret treasure at all. Both the Sacred Tree and the Wood Spirit Elders could have basked in his enlightenment, and taking a few Wood Spirits along to ascend wouldn't have been out of the question.

Yet the Untiring Immortal Venerable hadn't spared a single thought for himself. He fretted over his disciples; he fretted over the Heavenly Dao; he fretted over the innocent souls implicated by the Heavenly Dao; he fretted over whether his educational reforms could change everything; he even fretted over every single student in the academies!

At this stage, it was practically equivalent to calculating the past, present, and future of living beings across more than half the world.

And a future undergoing radical, colossal upheavals at that.

Could the Wood Spirit Elders pull that off?

If they had that capability, their opening strategy would have been to directly calculate the Untiring Immortal Venerable's win rate against the Heavenly Court and whether he was worth investing in!

In truth, when the Wood Spirit Elders proposed subjecting Untiring to the trial, they had merely intended to probe his personal desire to estimate the direction of his most likely clash with the Heavenly Court from his individual perspective.

Who could have guessed that Zhou Hui's desire would be so colossal, yet so staggeringly specific?

It could be described as entirely devoid of self-interest, yet representing the pinnacle of boundless greed.

Consequently, the moment Zhou Hui engaged his desire, every single Wood Spirit was thrown into a frantic frenzy. Even freshly sprouted tender twigs were assigned dedicated duties. Yet busy as they were, there wasn't the slightest chaos. Like the most flawless melody, each fulfilled its role, weaving an [entirely bug-free program](# "Literal CN: 绝无BUG的程序 (jué wú BUG de chéngxù) — Zhou Hui's immaculate, glitch-free algorithmic framework executed across the Wood Spirit network").

And precisely because of this, the Wood Spirits didn't dare interrupt the "benchmark" haphazardly.

They were terrified that if a single link stalled, the entire "system" would freeze solid. When that time came, losing a few Wood Spirits or having a bunch of demon beasts drop dead was a minor issue; but if it damaged the Sacred Tree's core origin, that would be catastrophic!

That's right—Zhou Hui's "game" had even begun drawing upon the Sacred Tree's core origin!

The Wood Spirits were completely powerless. Unable to abort the "trial" on their own, their only recourse had been to dispatch Shui Huang'er to notify the Untiring Immortal Venerable that he had "already passed the trial," praying the Untiring Immortal Venerable would show mercy and let them off the hook.

Regrettably, the Sacred Tree's Saintess proved entirely useless.

Rather, if she drew any closer to the Untiring Immortal Venerable, she would simply become another variable in the "benchmark," only adding to the computing load.

The Wood Spirits were on the verge of bursting into tears.

Under these circumstances, the leaves of the Sacred Tree rustled with a sound like an exhausted sigh.

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, this old man is merely an ordinary tree. To extrapolate the destiny of an entire world truly exceeds the scope of this old man's abilities,"-an ancient voice spoke.

Accompanying that ancient voice, a tall, handsome young man materialized before Zhou Hui.

The youth had short white hair—a rarity in this world—streaked with tender green highlights. Yet it did not look bizarre in the least; rather, it carried an ethereal, celestial air. His handsome features exuded a gentle, scholarly refinement, and his warm smile made every living being feel as though bathed in a spring breeze, inspiring instant goodwill. Observers would unconsciously overlook his oppressive height of nearly two meters and his frame of dense, corded muscle, mistaking him for a martial cultivator of profound attainment.

However, the tree branches growing across the youth's body gave away his identity as a spirit entity. These branches took distinct forms: for instance, two twigs sprouting from the crown of his head resembled willow fronds or long antennae; the branches emerging from his shoulder blades were as sturdy as the main boughs of an ancient parasol tree, branching downward into delicate twigs; and the shoots around his waist were as supple as creeping vines, resembling fresh tattoos. Yet whether the branches atop his head, the sturdy boughs across his shoulder blades, or the vines around his waist, every single one blazed with roaring demon fire, as though draped in a flaming suit of armor and cloak.

Such a conspicuous youth was naturally spotted by Shui Huang'er and Tao Zhu, who were gazing toward Zhou Hui from afar.

—"Lord Grand Elder?!"-Shui Huang'er cried in astonishment.

She didn't dare flutter about in midair, cautiously bowing low upon the massive tree trunk.

—"The Grand Elder?"-Tao Zhu peered intently at the youth. With his Mahayana stage cultivation, he surprisingly couldn't fathom the other party's depth at all, sensing an unfathomability on par with Untiring.

Tao Zhu instantly formed a guess regarding the youth's identity:

[Could this be the tree spirit of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree?!]

[Ribbit! This must be an ancient fossil that has existed since the dawn of creation, right? He's still kicking?!]

[Untiring actually provoked this antique out of hiding—is this really going to be okay?]

Tao Zhu thought with deep concern.

Whether in the cultivation realm or the immortal realm—across the three thousand worlds, the longer something had lived, the more troublesome it was.

Boss Tao wouldn't dare provoke an existence of this caliber.

He shoved himself backward with all his might, attempting to shrink his plump, burly frame smaller and smaller.

Fortunately, the figure over there had no interest in paying him any mind. He didn't even spare a second glance for his Saintess, simply striding over to sit across from the Untiring Immortal Venerable with hearty, unceremonious swagger.

Zhou Hui looked up at him, likewise surprised by his identity, though for an entirely different reason than Tao Zhu:

—"You are not the tree spirit of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, but... a demigod?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"..."-The entity Zhou Hui addressed as a "demigod" showed a flicker of surprise, chuckling only after a long pause.-"I dare not claim such a title, I dare not! Before your presence, how could I presume to call myself a 'god'?"

Though his tone was humble, it harbored a sliver of pride.

There was a fundamental distinction between the "tree spirit" of the Sacred Tree and the "demigod" of the Sacred Tree.

To put it in Zhou Hui's terms: the former was the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree gaining sentience; the latter was the entire *ecosystem* thriving upon the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree attaining sentience.

Although both were merely spirits gaining sentience in Zhou Hui's eyes, the demigod of the Sacred Tree didn't know that. It was simply delighted that the Untiring Immortal Venerable could see through to its true foundation.

After all, it had been lonely for far too long.

As everyone knew, whether tree spirits or tree demons, they were the entities that found it hardest to "ascend."

Never mind that the Wood Spirit Elders had just been cheerfully spectating the Untiring Immortal Venerable's plight; as a human, Zhou Hui had legs of his own and choices at his disposal. If he resolved to abandon this world, no one could stop him.

As for the Wood Spirits—never mind that they generally lived for hundreds or thousands of years, blessed with vast lifespans without even needing to cultivate—their root networks were anchored deep in this land, and the spiritual energy they absorbed and exhaled was an integral part of this world. The older they grew, the harder it became to ever depart.

Hence, though the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was alive, one rarely heard news of its tree spirit.

Everyone assumed that such an ancient tree spirit had long since dissolved into part of the heavenly and earthly laws, much like the Torch Dragon beneath the Abyssal Ocean.

Yet the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had refused to resign itself to that fate.

Therefore, when heaven and earth collapsed back in ancient antiquity, it had sheltered countless living beings—including demon beasts—allowing them to seed, propagate, and multiply within its body, forming a miniature ecosystem (though the Sacred Tree had no idea what an "ecosystem" was). And the moment that ecosystem formed, the tree spirit injected all of its life force into it, reincarnating into the governing rules themselves. By taking control of the rules of this ecosystem, it acquired half a divine spark.

This was remarkably similar to that 'Creation God' Zhou Hui had encountered, except that the Sacred Tree had executed this transformation voluntarily.

Moreover, the ecosystem of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was still rather modest back then, which was why its divine spark could only count as half. Even so, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree had transformed from an ordinary immortal tree into a sacred tree qualified to govern more than half of the Southern Domain, poised to flourish for ten thousand generations. If it grew for another several tens of thousands of years, it might evolve into an authentic world, at which point it could become a full-fledged nature deity and creation god.

This process had been unimaginably long and arduous. Take its past "reincarnation" as an example: that had been an immense gamble; a single misstep, and it truly would have been assimilated by the cosmic laws.

The "Tree God" of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was naturally exceptionally proud of its own visionary choice.

Regrettably, virtually no living being could comprehend how magnificent His Excellency the Sacred Tree truly was.

Whether the Demon Emperors or the Wood Spirit Elders—let alone the so-called Saintess, who was merely a green girl—every single one of them! None of them had the slightest clue about the fundamental difference between a wood spirit and a demigod!

To them, it was all the exact same thing!!

Of course, the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree didn't necessarily feel compelled to brag—otherwise, it wouldn't have gone tens of thousands of years without ever giving anyone a lecture on the subject, hardly even mentioning it to its own Saintess. But having someone actively point it out scratched an itch in a spot that didn't even exist, leaving it thoroughly elated.

From this, the Sacred Tree confirmed one certainty:

—"For you to see through this old man's identity, you have indeed pierced the truth of the Great Dao,"-the Sacred Tree sighed in admiration.-"My family's Saintess has shallow knowledge and has always been easy to dupe, but who would have thought that she, too, could occasionally make the right choice!"

—"...Lord Grand Elder!"-Shui Huang'er called out, her face flushing bright red.

The Sacred Tree's manner of speaking like an elder discussing his own child brought a smile to Zhou Hui's face.

—"She is far less of a headache than my two rascals,"-Zhou Hui said, turning his gaze toward the demon race's Saintess at last.-"If my kids had half her wit, they wouldn't have disturbed you in the previous lifetime."

However, after seeing the fragments of memory preserved by the Sacred Tree regarding the "protagonist," Zhou Hui no longer put much stock in that "script" where Mo Xi had supposedly fought the "protagonist" in the sixth cycle, burned the Sacred Tree, incurred the wrath of the demon race, and was driven out of the Southern Domain.

This demigod was hardly the sort of entity that could be burned down so easily.

He was currently burning with demon fire on his own body right now!

Sure enough, upon mentioning the previous cycle of reincarnation, the Sacred Tree chuckled.

Since it remembered everything, it naturally possessed many clues even Zhou Hui was unaware of:

—"Clever? Only because this old man kept watch over her,"-the Sacred Tree said with a chuckle.-"Otherwise, that silly girl would have handed over the Taiyi Spirit Dew in a daze without even realizing it! By comparison, your kid at least knew how to fight back!"

Saying so, it cast an appreciative glance at Mo Xi, who was still struggling within his own desire (Master's exam).

To a Sacred Tree that had lived for tens of thousands of years and fostered a menagerie of demon races, the Mo Xi from the previous cycle who practiced demonic arts had been nothing more than an adorably venomous little snake—one that was slightly sick and poisoned at that. Thus, regarding the petty schemes Mo Xi had concocted in his past life to win over the demon race and slay the "protagonist," the Sacred Tree hadn't minded much at all. Even if Mo Xi had plotted to steal the treasure in his past life, hadn't he failed? Not only had he failed, but a layer of his cultivation had been sheared right off by the Sacred Tree, preserved as a specimen in the secret treasure corridor!

What the Sacred Tree actually minded was someone else:

—"On the other hand, the kid who seduced the Saintess was rather bizarre,"-the Sacred Tree continued.-"Though this old man habitually preserved a backup—which was just taken by your disciple—upon my inspection, he was merely an ordinary human. I have no idea how he possessed such uncanny charm to bewitch a phoenix maiden. You must understand, that child hasn't even reached her [mating age](# "Literal CN: 繁育期 (fányùqī) — breeding season / reproductive maturity of magical avian beasts")!"

At this moment, Shui Huang'er was listening in complete bewilderment.

She felt the Grand Elder was speaking ill of her, yet she had zero memory of ever doing anything of the sort.

[It must be some other phoenix.]

Shui Huang'er thought to herself, promptly deciding to treat it as someone else's business.

Here, indeed, only Zhou Hui could truly hold a conversation with the ancient tree.

Especially when mentioning that 'protagonist', Zhou Hui likewise revealed a slightly perplexed expression.

Although he knew it was a conspiracy of the Heavenly Court, how this pawn known as the "protagonist" had been selected and sculpted, and how he came to possess those uncanny abilities, remained an unsolved mystery.

According to Zhou Hui's experience, even if one killed him, a similar entity would simply appear again.

Unless this problem was resolved, this cycle of reincarnation would likely never know peace.

The Sacred Tree sighed as well:

—"I suspect that kid was highly likely dispatched by the Heavenly Court,"-the Sacred Tree sighed.-"Selecting a so-called Child of Destiny to hoard and plunder resources before ascending—they haven't done that just once or twice. It's just, whether because that kid's efficiency was atrocious or because the Heavenly Dao resisted, this world has actually cycled through six or seven reincarnations. I wonder what the Heavenly Court is thinking..."

[—"I did that."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Sacred Tree")

—"The repeated reincarnations of this world—I did that,"-Zhou Hui admitted candidly.-"I couldn't very well let my disciples truly be murdered or abducted, so I had no choice but to resort to a few extreme measures."

—"...!"

The Sacred Tree stared at Zhou Hui in absolute shock.

Not counting the ancient era when it was still a tree spirit, just counting the years since becoming a demigod, it had lived for tens of thousands of years. It had fancied itself having seen everything under the sun, but someone like Zhou Hui... it truly had never seen!

Even the most troublesome Demon Emperors had merely stormed the Heavenly Court! Who in their right mind would [wash the world](# "Literal CN: 把世界洗个六七八遍 (bǎ shìjiè xǐ ge liù-qī-bā biàn) — resetting and rolling back the entire cosmos six, seven, or eight times") six, seven, or eight times?!

Wait a moment... this man repaired the Celestial Stairway, and now possessed a divine spark—could he have already stormed the Heavenly Court as well?!

The old tree thought with profound dread.

—"Rest assured, we shouldn't have to restart this time,"-Zhou Hui quickly reassured the other party upon seeing the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree look as though it were on the verge of a myocardial infarction—though, could plants actually suffer heart attacks?

After all, he still needed this biological computer—cough, Sacred Tree—to help him next!

Zhou Hui pointed toward his two disciples and said:

—"Am I not running the calculations for a new Heavenly Dao right now? A solution is already about to emerge."

—"No, no, no—this old man can't compute it!"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree wailed in distress.

The Sacred Tree finally recalled the original purpose of its visit. Only now did it thoroughly understand why the computing load was so astronomical: this was a madman who could wash and reboot the world seven times just to fight the Heavenly Court! How could the desire of such a being ever be sustained by an old tree like it?!

The Sacred Tree now regretted it—regretted it immensely! Why on earth had it insisted on testing Lord Immortal Venerable?!

If it had simply done as Shui Huang'er suggested—handing over the treasure directly to make a friend—wouldn't everyone be happy and at peace?!

Still, it wasn't too late!

—"This old man's abilities are limited; I cannot keep up with Lord Immortal Venerable's tempo,"-the Sacred Tree said in agony.-"However, this old man possesses a secret treasure called..."

—"Indeed. I also feel it cannot be calculated,"-Zhou Hui agreed, nodding.

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Sacred Tree")

—"You have far too much [redundant data](# "Literal CN: 冗余数据 (rǒngyú shùjù) — superfluous or duplicated data in computing"), while your [memory](# "Literal CN: 内存 (nèicún) — RAM / working memory") is far too small,"-Zhou Hui planned aloud.-"In addition, your [databases](# "Literal CN: 数据库 (shùjùkù) — databases") are insufficient. To compute the Heavenly Dao, I need to first expand some memory, execute a full system cleanup, and then import new databases..."

The old tree didn't understand the jargon, but the old tree sensed extreme danger.

Especially right after learning that this Immortal Venerable had already washed this world multiple times!

[Are you sure you mean a cleanup, and not a clean OS reinstall?!]

—"Ahem, ahem! Why don't you inspect my secret treasure first? Perhaps it can resolve your issue!"-the old tree blurted, grabbing Zhou Hui's arm and forcibly steering him toward the treasury, not even having time to let Zhou Hui call Mo Bei and Mo Xi back from their trials.

—Compared to convincing the Immortal Venerable and completely diverting his attention, letting the Wood Spirit program run for a few more shichen was an acceptable cost.

The Sacred Tree only wanted to hand over the secret treasure as quickly as possible and see this guest off!

Watching all of this, both Tao Zhu and Shui Huang'er were left slack-jawed and wide-eyed:

[There's actually someone who rushes to give away their treasures like this?!]
