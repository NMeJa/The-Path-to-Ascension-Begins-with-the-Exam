# Chapter 4: The Inner Sect Exam Paper (第4章 内门试卷)

—"Ahem,"-Xiang Rong coughed twice, glaring at them.-"Well? Are you buying or not?"

[—"We'll buy."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciples")

The crowd showed no hesitation whatsoever.

If anything, knowing that even inner disciples couldn't solve these questions and had received [punitive copying](# "Literal CN: penal copying (罚抄) — assigning lines or copying mistakes as academic punishment") as punishment only stoked their burning curiosity!

Someone in the crowd attempted to haggle:

—"Xiang Rong, that's dishonest!"-the disciple complained.-"You're taking an inner disciple's punishment, having us do the work for them, and charging us money on top of that?!"

—"Don't talk nonsense! What punishment? I never admitted to any such thing!"-Xiang Rong rolled his eyes.-"Besides, if not for this, when would you lot ever get the chance to work through an inner sect exam paper? ...Hold on, don't rush to snatch the papers just yet. Claim which question numbers you're willing to copy first so I can write them down."

[—"I'll take the first fifty questions? Wait, no—how come this paper has every kind of subject on it? Give me the ones related to alchemy."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Then I'll take the ones on artifact crafting."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"I want cultivation methods..."](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

The disciples clamored over one another, clearly oblivious to the true significance of this exam paper.

Only Gan Tang stared fixedly at the test paper, lost in deep contemplation.

Just then, someone turned to Gan Tang and asked:

—"Gan Tang, which questions do you want?"-the disciple asked.-"If you don't decide soon, they'll all be divvied up."

—"No worry, no worry,"-Xiang Rong said, fishing out another test paper from his robe.-"There's plenty more!"

—"Two different exam papers?"-someone asked skeptically.

—"Naturally! After all, each person's progress in their studies varies, so every single test paper is custom-tailored,"-Xiang Rong babbled shamelessly.

Unaware of the truth, the others wore expressions of naked envy.

They assumed this was the lavish treatment reserved for the inner sect—a privilege an outer disciple could never hope to enjoy.

Not leaving them much room to daydream, Xiang Rong added:

—"However, on this paper, only twenty-odd questions need copying,"-Xiang Rong noted.-"So only one person can buy this one."

Xiang Rong then took out several more exam papers, each with different numbers of questions that required copying.

By now, everyone realized what was going on: the so-called punitive copying wasn't meant for the entire exam paper, but rather for the questions that had been answered incorrectly.

Wait, if that was the case, didn't that mean the owner of the first test paper had turned in a practically blank sheet?!

[How on earth did a clown like that slip into the inner sect?!]

[Someone who just slacks off and fools around ought to give their spot to me!]

The elite outer disciples thought through bitter tears, secretly burning with the desire to track down whoever owned that first paper and usurp their place.

Gan Tang did not care in the least whose papers these were. Quietly scanning the questions on each sheet, she asked:

—"Could you give me a copy of every single paper?"-Gan Tang asked.

Hearing this, Xiang Rong's eyes rolled craftily:

—"A test paper where you don't have to copy out questions doesn't go for that price,"-Xiang Rong countered, smirking.-"Each one will cost... fifty spirit stones."

—"That's way too expensive! These papers all look largely similar, and the questions seem rather elementary. Were they truly written by the Immortal Venerable himself?"-someone tried to bargain.

Xiang Rong paid that person no heed, keeping his eyes solely on Gan Tang.

Gan Tang rapidly tallied her savings in her mind, gritting her teeth:

—"I'll buy them!"-Gan Tang bit out.

* * *

In the end, Gan Tang purchased nine exam papers.

And that was only after Gan Tang had paid an extra hundred spirit stones.

According to Xiang Rong, the degenerates—er, inner disciples—willing to outsource their homework to outsiders amounted to only four or five in total. Procuring test papers from the others required greasing more palms with extra cash. Consequently, Gan Tang emptied every last scrap of her remaining savings just to secure these nine exam papers.

Yet Gan Tang felt it was completely worth it.

She was already at the Great Perfection of Qi Condensation, merely a single step away from Foundation Establishment.

It felt like a jar filled to the brim with water, blocked at the opening by a razor-thin paper membrane; as long as that final obstruction was breached, the water would overflow freely into the open.

Gan Tang wanted to break through that final barrier on her own—and naturally, it was also because she was far too poor. A Foundation Establishment Pill cost several thousand spirit stones, and even then, demand far outstripped supply. There was simply no way Gan Tang could ever afford one. Yet, she had no idea what she was supposed to do.

Gan Tang felt she had made every conceivable preparation, yet her cultivation could no longer advance even an inch. If she still could not establish her foundation under these conditions, there had to be some invisible shortcoming she hadn't noticed.

Yet as an outer disciple who relied primarily on self-study, Gan Tang could neither identify her own shortcomings nor find anyone to ask for guidance.

It was at this moment that Gan Tang laid eyes on these exam papers.

She noticed that the questions covered an immense breadth of knowledge with expansive scope, yet the answers were precise and unambiguous. They were exceptionally well-suited for evaluating oneself and pinpointing unfamiliar knowledge gaps. Gan Tang made her decision on the spot: even if this was [grasping at straws in desperation](# "Literal CN: turning to any doctor in panic (病急乱投医) — seeking any possible remedy when facing an emergency"), she would spend her meager savings to gather every single exam paper she could get her hands on.

However, acquiring the papers was merely the starting point; what mattered far more was summarizing and categorizing the knowledge points after answering them.

After obtaining the test papers, Gan Tang tackled two of them first, cataloging and classifying every question she didn't know or wasn't certain of, and then set out to unearth the answers inside the Library Pavilion.

Even so, this was by no means an easy endeavor.

There were simply too many books in the Library Pavilion; trying to locate the answer to one or two multiple-choice questions among them was exceedingly difficult. Of course, compared to amassing several thousand spirit stones to purchase a Foundation Establishment Pill that might not even exist on the market, this level of difficulty was still relatively accessible.

Today, she once again shuttled diligently between the towering bookshelves:

—"The influence of spiritual springs on cultivation... this question should be related to Water-attribute cultivation methods, right?"-Gan Tang muttered.

—"Incorrect,"-a voice suddenly chimed in to guide her.-"Spiritual springs are produced under the influence of earth veins. If you look under the Earth-attribute categories, you will find it much faster."

Gan Tang jumped in fright.

Turning her head, she realized there was someone sitting beneath a nearby bookshelf.

The person was neither wearing an inner disciple's robes nor carrying an outer disciple's identity plaque. He was likely an unaffiliated [rogue cultivator](# "Literal CN: 散修 (sānxiū) — independent, unsectored cultivator") who had come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to freeload on their books, and he had evidently been in the Library Pavilion for quite some time. Gan Tang had paced back and forth here over the past several days and had spotted him multiple times—it almost seemed as though he lived inside the Library Pavilion. Even so, Gan Tang did not dare underestimate him on that account.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion was famous throughout the entire cultivation world; from time to time, prominent experts would come by to browse their collection.

The figure before her was draped in drab grey, possessing virtually zero presence, yet Gan Tang was entirely unable to see through his cultivation. He was presumably a senior heavyweight at the Golden Core stage or above.

Gan Tang bowed respectfully:

—"Many thanks for Senior's guidance,"-Gan Tang said.

The man gave a slight nod.

Just as Gan Tang was about to depart, she heard him ask out of the blue:

—"Are the books here difficult to find?"-the man asked.

—"...Not really?"-Gan Tang blinked, startled. She indeed found them agonizingly hard to find, but in front of an 'outsider', she would never speak ill of her own sect's Library Pavilion.-"With so many books, searching naturally takes a bit of time. But the repository here is already organized to be quite accessible. Word has it the Immortal Venerable himself personally cast a spell so that even if people misplace or scatter the texts, the books will automatically return to their proper shelves."

—"True. I had thought this would be the most convenient library I'd ever built,"-the man agreed.-"Yet why is it that after returning this time, the classification feels far too unsystematic? Sorting only by Five-Element cultivation methods without even an index catalog, to say nothing of comparing it to an [online search engine](# "Literal CN: 网络查找 (internet search) — Zhou Hui slipping into modern technical terminology from his past lives"). At this rate, compiling teaching materials..."

The man's voice trailed off, sounding as if he were merely talking to himself.

Seeing that he was no longer addressing her, Gan Tang quietly slipped away, offering no further defense on behalf of the Library Pavilion.

After all, she felt no malice from him. From his tone, this senior was most likely just irritated from struggling to find books and was in a foul mood, venting a few passing complaints to whoever happened to be nearby.

In truth, Gan Tang felt the exact same way.

As wonderful as the Library Pavilion was, tracking down books was truly an ordeal!

When Gan Tang first received those exam papers, she had shared the exact same sentiment as everyone else: the questions appeared far too basic, so how could any inner disciple fail to answer them? Yet once she actually began solving the problems and tried to dig to the very root of what she didn't know, she realized she had read far too few books and studied far too shallowly—to the point where she couldn't even determine where such 'elementary' questions originated from.

At this point, never mind copying a question one hundred times; merely hunting down the answers was enough to exhaust someone to death.

Gan Tang's reverence for the Untiring Immortal Venerable climbed yet another tier:

*The Immortal Venerable has read through so vast an ocean of books, yet even after reaching the Tribulation Transcendence stage, he can still draft Qi Condensation knowledge into questions that seem so simple and accessible on the surface. As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable!*

[Hopefully, I'll be able to find the answer shortly.]

Thinking thus, Gan Tang followed the man's guidance and walked toward the Earth-attribute cultivation section.

Yet not long after she walked away, a flamboyant figure stepped out from the shadows between the bookshelves.

Had Gan Tang still been here, she would have recognized him at a single glance: this was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's designated sect master heir, Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing bowed respectfully to the grey-robed man of zero presence:

—"Master,"-Qu Qianxing greeted.

In an instant, a refreshing breeze drifted through the repository. It did not disturb a single page; yet as it brushed over the 'grey-robed man', it seemed to blow away a heavy layer of dust, revealing the pristine, jade-like radiance beneath.

Looking again, the patriarch of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Zhou Hui, was standing right there.

The Immortal Venerable continued to gaze into the distance at the bookshelves, lost in some unknown meditation.

Qu Qianxing had only just arrived and had not overheard the exchange between Zhou Hui and Gan Tang. Catching sight of Gan Tang's departing silhouette, he inquired:

—"Master, that disciple just now appeared to have hit a bottleneck. Should we bestow a Foundation Establishment Pill upon her?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"No need,"-Zhou Hui shook his head.-"That child has no need for a Foundation Establishment Pill. [Letting the water flow naturally to form a canal](# "Literal CN: 水到渠成 (shuǐ dào qú chéng) — idiom meaning when conditions are ripe, success naturally follows") will serve her far better."

—"Master thinks highly of her?"-Qu Qianxing asked cautiously.

—"She is a diligent disciple,"-Zhou Hui glanced at his second disciple, suddenly realizing what he was really getting at.-"However, I have quite enough direct disciples as it is."

Besides, he could barely keep the current ones alive; there was no need to recruit more just to feed the Heavenly Dao another side dish.

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing breathed an audible sigh of relief.

It wasn't that he opposed his master taking in disciples; rather, based on past experience, every time Zhou Hui brought back a new junior brother or junior sister, it either meant acquiring a colossal enemy faction or saddling the sect with an intractable disaster.

To tell the truth, Qu Qianxing was genuinely terrified of Zhou Hui taking in more disciples.

Yet once that relief passed, Qu Qianxing felt curious again:

—"Master, if not to take in disciples, what have you been doing spending all these days in the low-tier technique section of the Library Pavilion?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"In light of your abysmal test scores and foundation of knowledge, I decided I require a set of standardized textbooks,"-Zhou Hui replied.

Qu Qianxing's expression turned instantly awkward:

—"Master..."-Qu Qianxing murmured.

—"And while I'm at it, I'm reorganizing this Library Pavilion. In the past, I never realized it was so inconvenient to use,"-Zhou Hui muttered, before turning his gaze back upon his second disciple.-"Speaking of which, is your 'homework' completed?"

The so-called 'homework' naturally referred to correcting the previous exam paper and completing the punitive copying.

—"It is finished, Master,"-Qu Qianxing replied respectfully.-"It's just that the other junior brothers and sisters have yet to complete theirs, so I haven't collected them all—"

—"Completed by yourself?"-Zhou Hui pressed.

—"Of course I completed it myself!"-Qu Qianxing answered with a hint of humiliation.-"As a Golden Core cultivator, failing to achieve a perfect score was already humiliating enough. I would never stoop so low as to outsource my corrections and punitive copying to someone else! Master, why do you ask—"

—"Nothing,"-Zhou Hui offered no explanation, merely assigning him a chore.-"Since you have finished your homework, go help me tidy up the shattered remnants of the Ascension Platform."

—"The Ascension Platform?!"-Qu Qianxing's eyes went wide.

The Ascension Platform, as the name suggested, was a raised terrace erected to better withstand heavenly tribulations and explore the pathway leading to the upper realm during ascension.

Given Zhou Hui's obsession with ascending, the Heavenly Spirit Sect could cease to exist, but the Ascension Platform was indispensable. Thus, across every reincarnation, the very first structure he always built was the Ascension Platform.

This life was no exception.

During the heavenly tribulation earlier, Zhou Hui had endured it precisely atop the Ascension Platform.

It was the very 'jade platform' where Zhou Hui had started upon waking.

However, no matter how meticulously constructed the Ascension Platform had been, it could never hold back the Heavenly Dao. Zhou Hui had been battered into severe injury by the tribulation lightning, and the Ascension Platform had fared no better, virtually blown to pieces.

Moreover, Zhou Hui had seen the truth behind his inability to ascend within that tribulation, which was why he had never repaired it.

In truth, Zhou Hui had no intention of rebuilding any Ascension Platform ever again. It was merely that the materials comprising that terrace had been amassed across several lifetimes; discarding them outright would be a waste, so he intended to repurpose them.

Yet his second disciple did not see it that way at all.

Zhou Hui watched as Qu Qianxing's face lit up with an unprecedented wave of excitement.

—"Master, are you finally preparing to repair the Ascension Platform?"-Qu Qianxing asked eagerly.

—"Not at all—"-Zhou Hui began.

—"I knew it! When Master said he wasn't going to ascend the other day, it was definitely just nonsense spoken off the cuff—ah, no! I don't mean that Master speaks nonsense! What I mean is, Master had just endured the heavenly tribulation that day, so your mind was inevitably a little bit muddled. Yes, that must be it!"-Qu Qianxing babbled incoherently.

He had clearly been agonizing over Zhou Hui's refusal to ascend for days.

Now that Zhou Hui had finally brought up 'repairing the Ascension Platform', he felt an immense weight lift from his shoulders.

Zhou Hui could not comprehend his agitation in the slightest:

—"That is not the case. Did I not say before that I no longer intend to ascend? Thus, that Ascension Platform is useless. We can use the materials to build something else first,"-Zhou Hui explained.

Like renovating the Library Pavilion, for instance.

—"B-but why?!"-Qu Qianxing cried out.

—"Why what?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"How could you possibly give up on ascending?!"-Qu Qianxing demanded, looking as though he had been betrayed.-"You are not the kind of person who gives up after suffering a few minor setbacks!"

Strictly speaking, those weren't 'a few setbacks', but several actual deaths.

[Why is this boy so utterly obsessed with whether I can ascend or not?]

Zhou Hui gazed at Qu Qianxing in bewilderment, finally noticing his second disciple's extraordinary fixation.

Come to think of it, the 'novel' had mentioned that after Qu Qianxing became Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he labored painstakingly to build the sect into a regional hegemon. At the same time, he resorted to every form of extortion, trickery, and scheming. Beyond mere personal ambition, most of it had been done to procure cultivation and ascension resources for his master, Zhou Hui. In doing so, he ultimately offended the protagonist and was struck down.

Zhou Hui had originally assumed this behavior stemmed purely from Qu Qianxing's 'filial piety'.

He had thought that as long as Zhou Hui abandoned ascending, Qu Qianxing would abandon court-death behavior. But looking at him now, Qu Qianxing clearly harbored a bizarre obsession with having his master ascend.

Now Qu Qianxing pressed frantically:

—"Did Master suffer some fatal injury during the heavenly tribulation? Can it be cured? Whatever medicinal herbs are required, I will scour the earth to find them!"-Qu Qianxing pleaded.

Judging by Qu Qianxing's expression, even if Zhou Hui demanded that he refine his own body into medicine, Qu Qianxing wouldn't hesitate for a heartbeat.

Clearly, not even Zhou Hui could dissuade him with a mere sentence or two.

[As expected of the Heavenly Dao's script—not so easily rewritten!]

Zhou Hui asked curiously:

—"Are you truly so eager for me to depart this world?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Of course I don't mean that!"-Qu Qianxing replied anxiously.-"I only feel that given Master's greatness, there is no reason to squander your energy on useless trifles."

—"Useless trifles? You mean that exam earlier?"-Zhou Hui prompted.

—"...Yes,"-Qu Qianxing hesitated for a moment before admitting honestly.-"While as far as that exam paper itself goes, the question types are extraordinarily innovative and the knowledge coverage is remarkably comprehensive—sufficient to test the true caliber of any examinee—still! That is all the more reason you should not waste your supreme intellect on such pointless things—"

—"I wasn't the one who invented those question formats..."-Zhou Hui said, nursing a headache.

He was about to offer an explanation, but right at that moment, a sudden ripple of spiritual energy caught his attention.

Zhou Hui's frost-tipped eyelashes quivered slightly. He lifted his silver-grey eyes, gazing toward a certain corner of the Library Pavilion:

—"Besides, who says it is useless? Has it not already taken effect?"-Zhou Hui remarked.

Qu Qianxing froze. Immediately afterward, he too perceived the surge of spiritual energy:

—"This spiritual power... seems to belong to that child from earlier?"-Qu Qianxing murmured, astonishment dawning on his face.-"Is she... establishing her foundation?!"

* * *

[Is this... the sensation of Foundation Establishment?]

Gan Tang thought in sheer delight, yet she did not halt the motion of answering questions with her brush.

She felt her luck today was exceptionally good. She had barely arrived at the Earth-attribute bookshelves and flipped through only a few volumes before locating exactly what she sought.

Gan Tang did not simply copy the answer onto the exam sheet and call it a day.

She dutifully prepared to copy it out one hundred times. Moreover, this was no mindless transcription, but copying done to genuinely comprehend and internalize the material. Although her exam paper had been bought at a steep premium and did not require her to do punitive copying for anyone, Gan Tang believed that if the Immortal Venerable demanded this of the inner disciples, he had to have a profound reason.

In fact, Gan Tang had treated every other question she didn't know in the exact same manner.

Her diligence was rewarded.

Through stroke after stroke of copying, Gan Tang solidified her weakest knowledge points while her spiritual energy grew increasingly refined. At long last, at this very moment, the spiritual power within her body finally crested past a certain critical threshold, spilling over into full abundance.

At this moment, Gan Tang felt as though she were submerged in a warm, soothing spiritual spring—though she had never actually bathed in one. It felt so blissfully comfortable she nearly drifted into slumber. Yet she dared not cease the repetitive motion of her brush. Lacking all experience, she had no idea what had triggered her breakthrough, nor what she needed to do to succeed. She only knew to keep copying, clinging fiercely to this state.

Even when the breakthrough to Foundation Establishment was complete, she still did not dare put down her brush. Only when she finished transcribing the very last repetition did Gan Tang finally let out a long breath of relief.

She set down the test paper in her hand, raised her head, and was instantly frightened half to death.

—At some point, with her bookshelf as the epicenter, within a hundred-meter radius—including perched atop the bookshelves themselves—the area was completely swarming with people. It seemed as though the entire outer sect had crowded here in an unruly swarm.

They had refrained from disturbing her earlier, evidently worried about disrupting her breakthrough. Now seeing that she had concluded her Foundation Establishment, they could no longer contain themselves and erupted in questions:

[—"Gan Tang, you established your foundation?! How did you do it?!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"What manual were you reading just now? Could your breakthrough be related to that technique?"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"No, I've read that manual before—it's utterly ordinary! The key has to be what she was writing! She didn't stop writing even during her breakthrough—it must be connected to Foundation Establishment!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

[—"Gan Tang, what were you writing just now?!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

A deafening cacophony of voices battered Gan Tang's eardrums.

Their frantic urgency was only natural.

The vast majority of outer disciples possessed mediocre innate talent; Foundation Establishment was a towering hurdle. Consequently, whenever any disciple successfully established their foundation, they would inevitably be besieged for advice.

Gan Tang, in particular, had broken through in broad daylight before the public eye inside the Library Pavilion, drawing all the more feverish attention.

Yet as the crowd's focus steadily shifted toward the exam papers clutched in her hands, Gan Tang began to panic. After all, she had obtained these inner sect papers through 'illicit channels' and had sworn to keep it secret.

Gan Tang could only hug the papers tightly to her chest, attempting to flee:

—"I... I don't know anything..."-Gan Tang stammered.

—"I recognize that exam paper!"-someone suddenly called out, having spotted the sheets in Gan Tang's grip.

For reasons known only to himself, the person loudly proclaimed:

—"That is an exam paper from the inner sect examination! Word has it the Immortal Venerable authored the questions himself!"-the disciple announced.

[—"Is that true?! Authored by the Immortal Venerable himself?!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciples")

The crowd grew even more hysterical upon hearing this, pressing Gan Tang for confirmation:

[—"Did you establish your foundation because of this test paper?!"](# "Speaker: Outer Sect Disciple")

By this point, it was too late for Gan Tang to conceal or deny anything.

Besides, she didn't want to deny it either.

In Gan Tang's eyes, her breakthrough was entirely thanks to this set of exam papers—she completely overlooked all her own prior toil.

In the end, Gan Tang stammered her admission:

—"...Y-yes. It was thanks to the Immortal Venerable's exam paper that I successfully established my foundation,"-Gan Tang confessed in a trembling voice.

In no time at all, the earth-shattering rumor that *'inner sect exam papers can grant Foundation Establishment'* swept across the entire sect like wildfire.
