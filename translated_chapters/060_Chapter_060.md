# Chapter 60: The End (第60章 结束)

[—"Ten thousand mid-grade spirit stones!"](# "Speaker: Bidder")

[—"Fifty thousand!"](# "Speaker: Bidder")

[—"One hundred thousand!"](# "Speaker: Bidder")

[—"Five hundred thousand!"](# "Speaker: Bidder")

[—"Ten thousand high-grade spirit stones!"](# "Speaker: Bidder")

……

—"Are these people completely insane?! It's a mere set of chess pieces, carved out of ordinary crystal that can't even accumulate spiritual energy! Even with the blessings of two supreme immortals, it shouldn't be hyped to such an absurd price!"-Qingyang Qingli fumed, throwing down the bidding controller in absolute fury.

The others seated in the VIP box stared silently at the discarded controller. This magical artifact had been custom-designed by the Heavenly Spirit Sect specifically for the auction. Featuring two rows totaling twelve buttons, it projected the submitted bid amounts into midair as dazzling fireworks, exceptionally striking to behold.

So then... who was the one who had just spent the last five minutes frantically mashing the "X2 (Double)" button, firing off radiant golden orbs of light that illuminated the entire private room like high noon?

Shu Jiawen couldn't hold back any longer:
—"Brother Qingyang, is the Heavenly Spirit Sect truly this stingy? As someone selected to be the 'Representative of Central Domain Nascent Soul Cultivators' ([*hurk*](# "Literal CN: 呕 — dry heave / retch of disgust")), couldn't you even get your hands on a complimentary set of Immortal Chess figurines?"

Did he actually have to bid on them out of his own pocket?

Was this perhaps some kind of novel scam?

To their surprise, Qingyang Qingli gave his folding fan an arrogant, flourish-filled twirl:
—"How could that be? Every participant receives a set of figurines, tiered into five distinct ranks according to their tournament placement: the standard Bronze Pieces available on the open market; the Deluxe Edition Silver Pieces; the Collector's Edition Gold Pieces; the Diamond Pieces reserved exclusively for the top nine; and the Starlight Pieces exclusive to the top three."

—"Don't worry, I've already purchased one hundred Collector's Edition sets and can gift one to each of you later. Though, since you didn't compete, your sets won't feature the contestants' commemorative inscriptions, which is a bit of a pity,"-Qingyang Qingli boasted.-"Still, fret not! I can always let you feast your eyes on my personal Starlight set. That was personally kneaded by Untiring Immortal Venerable himself, who captured the luminous halos of actual stars from the firmament!"

...[Whoa, I really want to punch his face in!]

Had aggressive actions not been strictly outlawed inside the auction auditorium, fists would have flown right then and there.

Shu Jiawen and the others glared venomously at Qingyang Qingli, sneering:
—"If that's the case, why in the world are you still bidding on this set?"

—"You don't understand—this is the one-of-a-kind limited edition!"-Qingyang Qingli declared.-"As the champion, how could I possibly not own it?"

—"Heh, they're just fleecing gullible fools like you,"-Shu Jiawen scoffed with deep disdain.

Wasn't Heavenly Spirit Sect deliberately granting tournament honors to loaded suckers like Qingyang Qingli just to bait them into buying them as collectibles?!

Right now, the bidders competing down there were probably the very prototypes of the chess pieces themselves!

It had to be!

Shu Jiawen felt confident he had thoroughly seen through Heavenly Spirit Sect's insidious ploy.

Yet regardless of Shu Jiawen's deductions, after an agonizingly fierce bidding war, this functionally useless set of chess pieces was ultimately knocked down for the staggering sum of 580,000 high-grade spirit stones!

And the person who bought it was by no means the "loaded sucker" Shu Jiawen had envisioned.

Thinking it through, even someone like Qingyang Qingli—hailing from the Central Domain's most [refined (filthy rich fools with more money than sense)](# "Literal CN: 最有格调（人傻钱多） — most refined / possessing high taste (loaded suckers with deep pockets)") sect—had dropped out of the bidding early; those who remained in the fray all carried formidable backgrounds.

The winning bidder made no attempt to conceal his identity either—the chess set was hardly some earth-shattering supreme treasure requiring secrecy. Striding forward with open confidence, the buyer opted for on-the-spot collection and walked straight up onto the auction dais to claim the lot.

To everyone's astonishment, he was a Void Refinement cultivator hailing from the Great Wasteland of the Western Domain!

—"Hahaha! I found this chess game extraordinarily fascinating,"-the cultivator boomed heartily:-"It'll make a fine souvenir to take back home, so my boys can see what the heroic champions of the Central Domain look like!"

Many cultivators in the hall swelled with pride at his words. Yet far more shared Shu Jiawen's bitter indignance:

[On what grounds?! If only I hadn't missed it—or arrived late—who knows which of us would have been immortalized as those heroic champions!]

Alas, [the wood was already carved into a boat](# "Literal CN: 木已成舟 (mù yǐ chéng zhōu) — what is done cannot be undone / water under the bridge"); they could only sigh in helpless defeat.

With this roaring inaugural triumph setting the tone, the subsequent auction proceeded at a brisk, steady pace, with virtually no awkward silences or unsold passed lots.

First, the Heavenly Spirit Sect provided superb presentations for esoteric materials and items that usually drew lukewarm reactions; every lot came equipped with detailed analyses and speculative applications from certified appraisers, encouraging many to try their luck. Second, many audience members actively participated in the price-guessing games, drastically enhancing their sense of involvement. Third, the proportion of "everyday magical artifacts" featured by the Heavenly Spirit Sect had surged significantly.

It wasn't merely Immortal Chess. Many merchant stalls from the Immortal Bazaar had taken inspiration, rolling out exclusive limited-edition merchandise. While fundamentally serving as brand advertisements, because the wares could actually be utilized by the majority of attendees, they were remarkably effective at whipping up the crowd's enthusiasm.

Among the most memorable was the ninety-nine-tier cake tower presented by Ganlin Town.

Likewise originating from culinary recipes brought back by Untiring Immortal Venerable from other small worlds, the pastry chefs had introduced ingenious refinements, tuning the flavor profile until it achieved the coveted sweetness of [not too sweet](# "Literal CN: 不太甜 (bù tài tián) — 'not too sweet', the highest compliment in Chinese culinary culture for any dessert, denoting balanced, sophisticated flavor without cloying sweetness"), earning universal acclaim across the cultivation community. In the end, the confectionery masterpiece—baked purely as an extravagant display of technical artistry—was snapped up by an itinerant disciple from a prominent Northern Domain sect, reputedly intending to present it as a longevity gift at his clan elder's centennial banquet.

Another crescendo that swept the auditorium into a frenzy was the couture fashion line debuted by Jinxiu Manor.

Reputedly drawing inspiration from concepts and aesthetic designs Untiring Immortal Venerable had retrieved from other small worlds, their garments had sold like wildfire throughout the Immortal Bazaar. Tonight, they even orchestrated a dazzling [runway fashion show](# "Literal CN: 走秀 (zǒuxiù) — modern fashion runway walk / catwalk modeling") across the auction dais to showcase their luxury couture, driving the assembled cultivators into a near-delirium.

And so, rolling forward through wave after wave of spectacle as the night deepened, the atmosphere inside the auction hall grew ever more uproarious.

*

Alas, wherever light shines, shadows inevitably gather.

Behind the bustling revelry, something sinister was quietly festering.

—"Is this what you called an auction that 'could never possibly succeed'?"-a vile, sinister voice hissed from a secluded corner just beyond the auditorium's rear exit, accompanied by an unsettling static hum.

A passing cultivator attempting to step outside to transmit word to his sect suddenly found his eyes glazing over; he spun in a sluggish circle on the spot, shuffled blankly back to his seat, and only snapped out of the trance after his companions called his name multiple times—yet he had clean forgotten what had just transpired.

Nor was this hypnotic haze confined to low-tier cultivators. Even with his Soul Formation realm cultivation, when Daoist Huang heard that voice, his skull buzzed violently, virtually stripping him of coherent thought.

Yet he could not flee from the voice as the others had.

For the voice was emanating directly from the mark branded onto his hand!

Namely, the brand that Carefree Palace had seared into the back of his hand—ostensibly so they could "personally observe" the auction venue.

Now, the entity on the other end had clearly seen everything with its own eyes—and was exceedingly displeased.

—"M-My Lord..."-Daoist Huang stammered.

He had no idea which powerhouse was stationed on the opposite side of the brand. But since they could converse with him directly inside the auction hall—an act strictly barred by the venue's spiritual wards—without alerting Untiring Immortal Venerable, they had to be an almighty sovereign whose cultivation rivaled Zhou Hui himself!

Daoist Huang naturally dared not offend such an entity, yet he was equally terrified of Carefree Palace discovering his "operational blunders."

Even at this late hour, Daoist Huang stubbornly made excuses:
—"This is nothing more than superficial vanity! Look at that cake tower they just wheeled across the stage—it contained zero rare materials, merely ordinary spiritual flour and sugar! The most expensive components were perhaps a few eggs from Rank-5 spiritual fowl. Though advertised as 'spiritual cuisine suited for Soul Formation cultivators,' it's fundamentally a marketing gimmick that cannot advance one's cultivation realm in the slightest! It's leagues below the priceless treasures Carefree Palace auctioned in years past!"

[—"You know jack shit!"](# "Literal CN: 你知道个P (nǐ zhīdào gè pì) — colloquial swear: you know a fart / you don't know shit")-the voice within the brand rasped with harsh contempt.

It naturally knew that Carefree Palace's previous auction goods possessed far greater intrinsic value—but that had been an involuntary measure forced upon them to seize monopolistic market dominance!

The Central Domain was a godforsaken backwater populated by dirt-poor cultivators whose spending power paled in comparison to the Eastern and Northern Domains. The exact same materials, if presented at an auction of equivalent prestige in other domains, would fetch several times the price. For Carefree Palace, doing business here was practically bleeding money!

Yet the Winter Solstice Auction in the Central Domain was a strategic choke point Carefree Palace was compelled to control.

Those rustic cultivators might be impoverished, but they constantly unearthed inexplicable relics and ancient treasures from the boundless ruins of the Demon Gods. Unable to fetch proper valuations at local auctions, they inevitably parted with them for dirt-cheap scrap prices—which naturally lined Carefree Palace's pockets!

Now, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had completely shattered this predatory cycle.

Untiring wasn't hosting an auction—he was practically running a charity!

Were appraiser analyses and relic secrets something that could be publicly broadcast so casually? Elite appraisers drew annual salaries in the hundreds of thousands of spirit stones, yet he was just tossing out guarded treasure intelligence like candy!

Untiring might be an ignorant amateur in commerce, but could the Abyss Sea Society be ignorant too?

Those veteran appraisers were all their men!

Speaking of which, that old toad from the Abyss Sea Society normally hoarded wealth like a [Pixiu](# "Literal CN: 貔貅 (píxiū) — mythical beast with a voracious appetite that swallows wealth without ever defecating; a miser / hoarder")—no one had ever squeezed a copper of profit out of him. Yet when dealing with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, why in the world did he agree to every single demand?!

Why didn't he just hand over his entire coffin savings to Untiring while he was at it?!

Still, setting aside those gratuitous appraisal lectures, what truly made the "brand" sigh in frustration was Untiring's utilization of worthless scrap.

Indeed, in its eyes, whether the culinary ingredients of the cake or the fabric textiles of the garments, they were all low-grade junk that couldn't command a real price—and objectively speaking, that was the truth. Yet because a certain distinctive flair had been injected into them, they suddenly became wildly coveted objects of desire!

Had Zhou Hui known what the entity was thinking, he would have informed it that this was known as the [product premium](# "Literal CN: 溢价 (yìjià) — price markup over intrinsic production cost / premium").

Having experienced life in another world, Zhou Hui had long concluded that commerce in the cultivation realm was profoundly primitive—a reality evident from the simple fact that money, namely spirit stones, still retained intrinsic practical utility. Even primitive mortals understood using cowrie shells as an abstract medium of exchange, yet immortal cultivators were still utilizing spirit stones that could be directly consumed for spiritual energy. How was that any different from gloriously glorified bartering?!

On such a primitive foundation, commercial transactions among cultivators were exceedingly utilitarian.

From pills to magical treasures, from robes to room furnishings, everything rigidly pursued functional utility—and that utility was measured exclusively in terms of combat efficacy!

Consequently, the entire world was locked in an accelerated, steroid-fueled arms race. That the War of Demon Gods ten thousand years ago had merely obliterated the Central Domain was a stroke of divine fortune; a few more Mahayana and Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses clashing could easily have vaporized the entire mortal realm.

Zhou Hui believed this systemic sickness arose because all worldly resources were hyper-concentrated in the hands of supreme figures like himself.

Everyone scrambled frantically upward in the [rat race](# "Literal CN: 往上卷 (wǎng shàng juǎn) — rolling upward / competitive involution / rat race"), funneling all wealth exclusively toward the topmost handful of powerhouses. What they couldn't acquire legitimately, they stole, plundered, and seized by force. The so-called law of the jungle was nothing more than an unchecked arms race.

Yet humans were ultimately human; deep in their souls, they still yearned for "useless happiness."

A tiny hint of sweetness on the tongue, a tiny touch of aesthetic elegance in attire—plenty of people were more than willing to pay a hefty premium for them.

As long as merchants could reap astronomical profits from such consumer goods, they would no longer cater solely to a microscopic martial elite.

Naturally, if one could establish an enduring, reputable [brand](# "Literal CN: 品牌 (pǐnpái) — commercial brand identity / reputation"), that would be even better.

*That* was the ultimate superweapon for siphoning wealth away from the resource-monopolizing sects.

These macro-economic considerations of Zhou Hui were naturally far beyond what the "brand" on Daoist Huang could fathom.

Yet hailing from Carefree Palace, the largest commercial conglomerate in this world, it faintly sensed the titanic commercial potential lurking within.

Alas, it could not personally transmit these revelations back.

*Still, doesn't the Heavenly Spirit Sect harbor several sleeper moles? Hopefully they will report everything here with absolute fidelity,* the "brand" mused.

Indeed, this "brand" was not some long-range communication relay connecting to distant Carefree Palace sovereigns as Daoist Huang imagined—it *was* an avatar fragment of the entity itself!

The "brand" had had no alternative!

From its perspective, remote puppet-control would have been far safer. Yet somehow, Untiring Immortal Venerable had erected a dense, impenetrable [firewall](# "Literal CN: 防御罩（防火墙） — defensive barrier (firewall)") centered entirely around Mount Tianling; all information entering or leaving the perimeter required explicit authorization from his mountain-protecting grand array [system](# "Literal CN: 护山大阵（系统） — mountain-protecting grand array (system)").

Throughout the duration of the Immortal Bazaar, Carefree Palace had attempted countless infiltration vectors. Whether possessing ordinary cultivators as [zombie hosts](# "Literal CN: 普通的修士（肉鸡） — ordinary cultivators (zombie hosts / botnet compromised machines)") or commanding demonic beasts to launch suicidal kinetic strikes against the barrier, none could breach its perimeter.

Having reached this impasse, how could Carefree Palace ever believe Daoist Huang's ridiculous assurances that "everything is firmly under control"?

Even Palace Master Li of Carefree Palace—the burly giant who had ordered the trade route blockade that fateful day and drafted the entire containment strategy against the Heavenly Spirit Sect—was now drowning in bitter regret.

Who could have foreseen that Zhou Hui would actually accept the hosting rights for the Winter Solstice Auction?!

Wasn't this man obsessed solely with transcending and ascending to the immortal realm?! Why had he abruptly plunged into retail and commerce?!

Naturally, Carefree Palace had initially rested on its laurels, assuming Untiring Immortal Venerable had never engaged in trade and possessed zero experience orchestrating massive commercial gatherings; even if he attempted it, he would surely make an absolute laughingstock of himself.

Who could have imagined Zhou Hui actually harbored peerless genius in this domain, pulling off the entire spectacle with flying colors?

What was worse, his innovative methods far outstripped Carefree Palace's entire repertoire!

Having stalled step by step until this very moment, the party forced into a corner—backed against the wall and compelled to deploy their ultimate nuclear option—was Carefree Palace itself.

The trade route blockade had been a mere smokescreen; they could never surrender the Central Domain market they had spent centuries consolidating entirely to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Enough. Return to the hall,"-the "brand" ordered.

Daoist Huang exhaled an immediate sigh of relief.

He now absurdly felt that the auction auditorium—monitored under Untiring Immortal Venerable's omnipresent surveillance—was the safest haven in existence.

At the very least, the "brand" dared not utter a single sound in there.

Daoist Huang staggered unsteadily toward his seat. While ordinary Wanhe Gang disciples handled the mundane logistics of the auction, high-ranking executives naturally enjoyed a quiet private box to facilitate their participation in the bidding.

Yet the moment Daoist Huang stepped through the rear doors, he felt his physical body take on a terrifying consciousness of its own, steering him straight down toward the densely packed general audience seating below!

*What is happening?!*

—"M-My Lord? Have you taken control of my body?"-Daoist Huang attempted to ask, but his jaws remained clamped shut.

What horrified him even more was that the "brand" suddenly flared scalding hot, like an open flame.

Nor was this blistering heat confined to the brand itself. Radiating outward from the mark, it surged along his meridians, spreading to every remote corner of his limbs; within moments, his entire dantian felt as though it were ablaze, wracking him with searing agony.

Was it truly necessary to go to such lethal extremes "merely" to puppeteer a body?!

Daoist Huang thought in stark despair.

Faint, terrifying conjectures coalesced in his mind, yet with his flesh completely hijacked, he was utterly powerless to resist.

A low-ranking Wanhe Gang disciple stationed at the rear of the audience rows to maintain order snapped to attention as someone approached. But upon realizing the figure was the vice leader of his own gang, he immediately lowered his guard.

—"Vice Leader Huang, what brings you down here?"-the disciple asked respectfully.

To his sheer horror, Daoist Huang heard a raspy, unfamiliar voice rattle from his own throat:
—"I came to inspect the conditions down below."

—"You truly are dedicated to your duties, Elder!"-the disciple flattered without the slightest suspicion.

Despair flooded Daoist Huang's soul as his legs stumbled forward in uneven, jerky strides, weaving through the crowded spectator rows.

Any further, and he would step right onto the auction dais itself!

At this moment, the searing heat inside Daoist Huang reached an unbearable crescendo.

He felt like a living puppet packed to the brim with a Fire Thunderbolt Bomb, primed to swell and detonate at the very next heartbeat!

Yet everyone around him remained completely oblivious. The vast majority of the audience was staring raptly at Qu Qianxing on the dais, while Qu Qianxing himself appeared not to notice Daoist Huang approaching the foot of the stage, wholly absorbed in introducing the next auction lot.

Watching this oblivious bliss, Daoist Huang felt a strange, twisted surge of vindictive ecstasy bubble up within his chest:

[I'm finished! But every last one of these people is finished too! This entire auction is going to be utterly ruined! I wonder what kind of expression Untiring Immortal Venerable will make when he witnesses this?!]

At that thought, Daoist Huang tasted the sweet venom of revenge. He shut his eyes tight, bracing for the Fire Thunderbolt Bomb ticking inside his flesh to erupt, blasting himself, the stage, and every living soul in this hall into nonexistence, leaving neither corpse nor bone!

And so he waited... and waited... and waited...

Yet even when Daoist Huang felt the inferno inside his dantian turn bone-chillingly cold, that catastrophic detonation never arrived.

Filled with dread and disbelief, Daoist Huang cracked open his eyes and lifted his hand to look—only to discover that the sinister black brand had vanished without a trace!

Had Carefree Palace spared him?

Had they aborted their suicide bombing plan?

Daoist Huang's thoughts spun in chaotic confusion. Yet not a single shred of relief touched his heart, only a swelling, suffocating dread. Knowing Carefree Palace's ruthlessness, he didn't believe for half a second they would ever grant mercy—even if they had altered their plans on the fly, they would never have erased the brand seared into his flesh so cleanly.

Then what in the world had just happened...?!

In a daze, Daoist Huang lifted his gaze, locking directly onto Qu Qianxing looking down at him from the auction dais.

Across that breathtaking, exquisite visage curled a faint, mocking smile. Qu Qianxing looked at Daoist Huang as though he were nothing more than a suicidal ant that had blundered straight into a spider's web of its own volition.

A violent shudder racked Daoist Huang's soul as realization struck him like a celestial thunderbolt:

*It was Untiring Immortal Venerable!*

Only Untiring Immortal Venerable possessed the terrifying power to thoroughly dismantle and erase Carefree Palace's "puppeteering brand" in the span of a single breath—even at this late hour, Daoist Huang still believed the brand was merely a remote-control relay.

Yet Daoist Huang felt not the slightest inkling of gratitude. Instead, he subconsciously shifted his terrified gaze toward the private box reserved for Wanhe Gang—only to find his own Gang Leader looking down at him with the exact same freezing, detached contempt as Qu Qianxing!

[—They know! They knew all along! Untiring Immortal Venerable must have known everything I did! Every single thing!]

In that instant, the gnawing unease in Daoist Huang's heart instantly vaporized into pure, paralyzing terror. He spun on his heel and bolted frantically toward the auditorium's exit. True to his Soul Formation realm cultivation, his fleeing form moved like a shrieking gale; in a single blur, he cleared the rear doors and burst out into the night.

Naturally, no matter how foolish Daoist Huang might be, he was not naive enough to believe he could escape on foot alone.

Over the past month and a half, watching the Immortal Bazaar grow more prosperous by the day, a gnawing paranoia had gripped him, prompting him to prepare his escape routes long in advance.

Daoist Huang felt the inner lining of his undergarments. Tucked inside was a high-grade spatial teleportation talisman purchased at exorbitant expense—reputedly forged by an ancient True Immortal, capable of pinpoint teleportation anywhere across a continental landmass.

Even so, Daoist Huang hadn't dared activate it directly inside the auction hall, terrified that Untiring Immortal Venerable would effortlessly nullify it just as he had dispelled the brand. Only after bolting beyond the outer perimeter gates did he finally ignite the talisman.

Fortunately, fortune favored him.

A bout of dizzying spatial vertigo swept through his senses. When clarity returned, he found himself standing before a familiar, opulent palatial compound.

This was none other than the City Lord's Mansion of Fengqing City!

[I actually escaped successfully?! Untiring Immortal Venerable isn't all that terrifying after all!]

A surge of wild euphoria washed over Daoist Huang, though he dared not grow arrogant, scurrying toward the grand gates of the estate.

Of course, reaching this stage, Daoist Huang had no illusions that the City Lord of Fengqing City could shield him. What he needed was the colossal inter-domain teleportation array situated within the side hall of the mansion.

That teleportation array served as the critical link connecting the Central Domain to the Southern Domain!

As long as he obtained the City Lord's permission to jump to the Southern Domain, he would be completely beyond Untiring Immortal Venerable's sphere of influence.

[—I refuse to believe he can hunt me all the way across the Southern Domain!]

Just as Daoist Huang was indulging in this thought, a heavy *thud* resonated behind him, like dead weight crashing onto the flagstones.

Startled out of his wits, Daoist Huang whipped around in panic—only to see Master Chen and several other conspirators who had plotted with him to sabotage the auction arriving through various esoteric teleportation methods!

Daoist Huang gasped in shock:
—"Could you all have also...?"

Master Chen and the others offered no reply, but their ghastly, ash-white faces told the entire tale:

Clearly, their treason had also been exposed (whether they had suffered coercion from Carefree Palace or not), and they had fled for their lives just as Daoist Huang had.

This was certainly no coincidence.

The conspirators had long discussed how to flee the Central Domain if their subversive operations were uncovered and drew Untiring Immortal Venerable's wrath; this very courtyard was their pre-arranged emergency rally point.

Yet looking upon these completely unscathed "accomplices," the heart Daoist Huang had just settled leaped straight into his throat again!

He didn't want to see these co-conspirators at all!

Not in the slightest!

If Daoist Huang had managed to escape because of his meticulous precautions and his priceless True Immortal talisman, what about them? Even if they possessed secret arts to teleport from Mount Tianling directly to Fengqing City, Daoist Huang didn't believe for a second their charms matched his own.

In Daoist Huang's original calculus, several of these fools were meant to serve as the slowest prey, left behind to be devoured by the monster (Untiring Immortal Venerable) to buy him precious escape time!

Now half of them had arrived, with the rest likely already on the way. What did this imply?!

Had Untiring Immortal Venerable deliberately stayed his hand? Or was he preparing to round them all up in a single fell swoop?!

Daoist Huang made terrified conjectures in his heart, yet this was hardly the time or place for a thorough investigation. With his escape route lying right before his eyes, come hell or high water, he had to take the gamble.

—"Let's move,"-Daoist Huang told them.

The group followed Daoist Huang into the depths of the City Lord's Mansion.

The City Lord's head butler who emerged to receive them wore an exceedingly peculiar expression:
—"How did you—never mind. Follow me. The City Lord wishes to see you."

—"Can't you open the teleportation array directly? We are in an extreme rush!"-Daoist Huang urged.

He knew all too well that for a Tribulation Transcendence supreme sovereign, crossing an entire continent took a mere instant.

Daoist Huang was terrified that the moment he blinked, Untiring Immortal Venerable would materialize right before his eyes.

The other cultivators pleaded in panic as well:
—"Indeed! An emergency has occurred at the auction; we must report to Carefree Palace immediately!"

[—"If this is delayed, the consequences will be unimaginable!"](# "Speaker: Conspirator")

Under ordinary circumstances, invoking the name of Carefree Palace was an invincible trump card. Yet the butler merely eyed them with a bizarre gaze—as though watching a troupe of pathetic clowns performing an absurd pantomime—and replied with icy detachment:
—"Out of the question. Our City Lord wishes to see you all."

His attitude unsettled Daoist Huang even further, yet they had no alternative.

The inter-domain array required the City Lord's explicit authorization to activate.

Fine, they would meet him! By any reasonable calculation, the City Lord was in their camp. Could he possibly obstruct them on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's behalf? Wouldn't he fear the wrath of Carefree Palace?

Comforting themselves with such logic, Daoist Huang and the others followed the butler deeper into the mansion.

Yet the butler did not lead them to the formal reception hall customary for entertaining guests; instead, he brought them to the garden in which the City Lord of Fengqing City normally took immense pride.

To Daoist Huang's shock, not only the City Lord, but the patriarchs of several of the city's most prominent cultivation clans were gathered there. Like a flock of geese caught by the throat, they were craning their necks high, staring fixedly into the sky.

Before Daoist Huang could discern what they were gawking at, the butler announced their arrival. The entire crowd whipped their heads around toward Daoist Huang in unison, like a horde of awakened zombies, startling Daoist Huang into a violent shudder.

Among them, Feng Lang, the City Lord of Fengqing City, flushed purple with rage the moment his eyes landed on Daoist Huang and his cohort:
—"You still have the audacity to show your faces here?!"

—"W-What is the matter?"-Daoist Huang asked with intense guilt.

—"What is the matter with the auction?! How did you guarantee it to us?!"-another cultivator roared in accusation. Daoist Huang recognized him—he was the proprietor of a major merchant fleet:—"'Nobody will attend'? 'The Heavenly Spirit Sect can never pull it off'? Then why in the world is every single attendee raking in astronomical fortunes?!"

—"Indeed, Fellow Daoist Huang! I only boycotted the auction because I trusted your assurances! Yet my business rivals all rushed over, and now what am I supposed to do with the stockpiles rotting in my warehouses?! Are you trying to drive me to bankruptcy?!"-another caravan master barked.

The crowd swarmed forward in an instant, a clamorous, raging mob.

Anxious and desperate, Daoist Huang shoved them back, deflecting blame in annoyance:
—"B-But wasn't this what we all agreed upon from the very beginning?! Are you people actually thinking of defecting to the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"

—"Heh, listen to you biting back like a rabid dog!"-the patriarch of a prominent cultivation clan surnamed Zheng snarled:—"To support your scheme, I even introduced the mortal villages near my territory to you! And the result? Those villagers, emboldened by the Heavenly Spirit Sect's newfound protection, are already preparing to tear up our contracts—agreements they never willingly entered into in the first place!"

—"That was..."-Daoist Huang attempted to offer an excuse, but midway through, his thoughts suddenly derailed.

*Wait.*

How did these people know any of this?

Even if news traveled across the Central Domain, given its sluggish communication methods, it should take at least half a month, if not several months! There was no conceivable way they could have received such detailed intelligence in this brief span of time!

Right at that moment, a voice Daoist Huang had heard not long ago drifted through the courtyard air:

> "Sold!"

Subconsciously, Daoist Huang followed the sound. Only then did he notice that a ten-meter-tall blue coral tree had been erected in the center of the garden, who knew when.

—"Isn't that a treasonous betrayal?! Why did you plant one of those as well?!"-Daoist Huang blurted.

For a fleeting second, Daoist Huang thought they had attended the auction in secret.

City Lord Feng Lang sneered with freezing contempt:
—"If not for this tree, we wouldn't even know what you bastards have done!"

—"What do you mean...?"-Daoist Huang started to ask, but saw vibrant images flickering atop the crown of the coral tree.

It was unmistakably a live projection of the auction hall!

The voice Daoist Huang had heard a moment ago clearly originated from that very projection.

—"Could that illusion be...?"-Daoist Huang trailed off.

—"Precisely. Ever since the auction commenced today, glowing illusions materialized atop the crowns of every single blue coral tree,"-Feng Lang stated.

Even the City Lord could not block out the sky with a single hand across Fengqing City; certain rogue factions had secretly planted blue coral saplings. When the Winter Solstice arrived, those coral trees abruptly began [live-streaming](# "Literal CN: 直播 (zhíbō) — live broadcast / live streaming") the auction proceedings, instantly throwing the entire city into an uproar!

Only then did the various sects, merchant fleets, and cultivation clans realize they had been played for absolute fools by Daoist Huang, coming to terms with the catastrophic financial fortunes they had lost by boycotting the event.

They had spent the entire evening watching the broadcast while their hearts bled dry!

And Daoist Huang and his wretched conspirators had walked right into their midst at this exact miserable moment.

Looking upon Daoist Huang and remembering their ruined balance sheets, the mob wanted nothing more than to lynch the conspirators on the spot.

Yet Daoist Huang felt vastly more aggrieved than any of them.

If every coral tree across the domain was "live-streaming," didn't that mean the entire Central Domain knew everything?!

In other words, they could never return to the Central Domain again—not even in hiding! Just like the mob before them, every single cultivator who had boycotted the Immortal Bazaar because of Daoist Huang's machinations would hunt them down to tear them limb from limb!

Daoist Huang literally coughed up a violent spray of blood.

His desperation to flee the Central Domain reached a frantic peak:
—"Cut the crap! Open the teleportation array this instant!"-Daoist Huang demanded, ignoring the furious crowd and barking directly at the City Lord.

The City Lord had been in the middle of hurling accusations, but at that exact second, his expression warped into unadulterated horror. Stumbling backward, he stammered:
—"Wait... Fellow Daoist Huang, you—"

—"I what?"-Daoist Huang snapped, taking a step forward.

Yet all he saw were the onlookers turning deathly pale, scrambling backward in chaotic terror.

Daoist Huang realized something was wrong. He... no, *it* looked down at its own physical form.

His flesh had turned squishy and gelatinous. Like a snowman exposed to a scorching sun, his body was rapidly melting away, exposing the squirming viscera within! Deeper inside, his bones were charred pitch-black, barely providing structural support—yet the instant his gaze fell upon them, they shattered into fine ash!

With their skeletal framework gone, Daoist Huang's internal organs tumbled wetly onto the flagstones.

One of his dislodged eyeballs rolled across the paving stones, still swiveling in its socket, witnessing his "companions" undergoing the exact same grotesque dissolution.

And so, his melting mouth shrieked its final dying words:
—"Haha! So I was already dead all along!"

[—"I was already murdered by Carefree Palace right inside the auction hall!"](# "Speaker: Daoist Huang")

[—"Of course! With heat like that, how could I have possibly survived?!"](# "Speaker: Daoist Huang")

[—"All of you!"](# "Speaker: Daoist Huang")

[—"Your turn will come sooner or later!"](# "Speaker: Daoist Huang")

[—"You are no different from us!"](# "Speaker: Daoist Huang")

That shrieking mouth screamed its bitter, unyielding curse, dissolving into a putrid puddle of melted flesh and blood before the terror-stricken eyes of everyone in Fengqing City.
