# Chapter 122: The Futile Backup Plan (第122章 无用的备用方案)

Setting aside the editor-in-chief who had fled into the night, "the entire cultivation realm is cursing us (Carefree Palace) to high heaven" was by no means an exaggeration.

To be precise, the moment the "Supplementary Issue" of *Spells* dropped, whether cultivators championed the "Controllable Ascension Theory" or rallied behind the "Ascension Reward Hypothesis," both camps turned their artillery barrels in unison, aiming straight at the theses in the supplement and opening fire with blistering diatribes.

> A certain trailblazing Fairy Ruopei:
> —"It is truly unimaginable that someone at such an elevated realm could still possess the cognitive faculties of a mortal without spirit roots! Even after reshaping your physical body from head to toe, did you fail to sever your [root of earthly vexation](# "Literal CN: 烦恼根 (fánnǎogēn) — Buddhist term for worldly troubles and delusions; colloquial slang for earthly hair or male genitalia; here used as a scathing insult on mortal ignorance")?"

> A certain irascible Mengshan Old Freak:
> —"The brains of whoever wrote these papers look like they were fished out of the Abyssal Ocean's river of corpse brine—corrupted down to pure nothingness! If everything relied on heavenly destiny, what the bloody hell are we cultivating for?! Defying the heavens to change our fate, my ass!"

> A certain anonymous cultivator surnamed Su:
> —"My wife said publishing these papers was a sheer waste of paper. I concur."

> A certain sober Ancestor Zui Liu:
> —"To be entirely frank, I refuse to believe that theses with such atrocious formatting could actually appear in a supplement. In particular, regarding the cited data and references—amid an ocean of 'reportedly,' 'sources say,' and 'possibly'—the scant few examples actually provided fail to cite a single verifiable source. I suspect deliberate fabrication and fraud, and strongly recommend a comprehensive audit into the publishing process of these papers."

> A certain undercover Ghost Emperor of the Western Domain:
> —"Hahahahaha, laughing [Zhen](# "Literal CN: 朕 (zhèn) — 'We / Zhen', the ancient imperial first-person pronoun reserved exclusively for the emperor; used here by the Delusion-Entering Ghost Emperor") to death! Is this issue of the supplement a joke book?!"

> A certain Young Master Jian of Sky City:
> —"Compared to the penetrating analyses of the senior predecessors, a junior like myself cannot offer constructive critiques. However, this junior did take note of the unorthodox applications of certain medicinal pills and human furnaces described within. This junior believes the author of these papers engages in rather sinister cultivation methods, and hopes that regarding these specific sections, said author can furnish a convincing explanation."

...

Such scathing critiques flooded the newspaper spreads and reader feedback columns.

In truth, *White Jade Capital Daily* alone simply lacked the physical real estate to accommodate the avalanche of cultivator mockery. Even having transitioned into a daily paper, it was still far from enough. Consequently, this windfall directly benefited a slew of new newspapers launched by other sects; for instance, the Heavenly Dao Alliance's *Tianmeng Weekly* rode this viral wave to surge its circulation, firmly establishing a foothold in the market.

Naturally, the primary battlefield remained *White Jade Capital Daily*.

Cultivators had already grown accustomed to publishing their thoughts in *White Jade Capital*. Most of their primary pen names had built their reputations there, with some cultivators even meticulously cultivating personal personas for their flagship identities. Meanwhile, their more unvarnished [cursing](# "Author markup: 咒骂（X） (zhòumà [X]) — cursing / direct abuse (crossed out in raw text)") candid venting of the soul was carried out via alt accounts, sprinkled liberally across other newspapers.

In today's cultivation realm, outside of the cultivators themselves, perhaps nobody had any clue just how many alt accounts they operated.

Consequently, compiling a comprehensive digest of all the mockery ridiculing the "supplement theses" was truly no easy task.

Truth be told, Palace Master Li Hao of Carefree Palace rarely found himself wishing his subordinates' intelligence-gathering capabilities were slightly worse. Alas, this happened to be Carefree Palace's specialty—especially since it was a matter the big boss had taken a personal interest in. Thus, they monitored every single newspaper across the cultivation world—ah, with the sole exception of *Cultivation World Weekly*, because their own paper was packed exclusively with glowing praise, causing it to completely miss out on this wave of viral traffic—extracting the relevant excerpts and binding them into a thick, hefty scrapbook for their Palace Master's viewing pleasure.

Li Hao read a mere two lines before feeling as though he was on the verge of a cerebral hemorrhage.

—"What is the meaning of this?!"-Li Hao roared in incandescent fury.-"Are they implying there is something wrong with my thesis?!"

Indeed. The paper occupying the central C-position of the "Supplementary Issue" had been personally penned by Li Hao himself.

The remaining papers had likewise been authored by Body Integration and Mahayana "titans" of Carefree Palace.

At least in terms of cultivation stage, the lineup fielded by Carefree Palace was not inferior to the Central Domain, nor to the Northern Domain. This had also been deliberately arranged to facilitate subsequent promotional hype.

—These theses, naturally, hadn't been cobbled together overnight; rather, ever since the academic debate began, Carefree Palace had repeatedly attempted to slip into the fray and stir up trouble, only to be rejected across the board, which ultimately led to today's stunt.

Consequently, Li Hao possessed absolute confidence in his own writing.

He firmly believed that were it not for personal vendettas and the Untiring Immortal Venerable deliberately abusing public authority to settle private grudges by axing his paper, the contents he penned were more than qualified to grace the front page of *Spells*! Even without promotional hype, it should have sent shockwaves across the cultivation world, leaving everyone marveling in awe at the profound erudition he displayed.

Well, people were marveling all right, but the direction seemed somewhat off.

Li Hao wished he could tear this pack of blind Philistines to shreds:

—"This is the quintessential core of our Carefree Palace's cultivation! Deigning to unveil a fraction of it for their eyes was already a monumental grace—what do they mean by this?!"

—"P-perhaps this is Heavenly Spirit Sect's insidious conspiracy?"-a subordinate ventured with difficulty.

In any case, Heavenly Spirit Sect was currently the ultimate [scapegoat pot](# "Literal CN: 背锅 (bèi guō) — carrying the black pot / dumping blame on a convenient scapegoat"); anything and everything could be hurled into it. When deflecting a superior's incandescent fury and burying one's own operational incompetence, it was the handiest tool available.

That subordinate painstakingly fabricated charges against Heavenly Spirit Sect:

—"Perhaps that fellow (the editor-in-chief) was a Heavenly Spirit Sect mole all along? He deliberately steered us into making the wrong decision, then bought off these cultivators to lay siege to us in print, causing our prestige in the cultivation realm to plummet overnight... What a vicious, venomous scheme!"

As he spoke, he was nearly terrified by the conclusion he had spun out of thin air.

Yet lifting his eyes, he discovered his Palace Master glaring at him with murderous savagery:

—"Bullshit!"-Li Hao barked.-"How many cultivators could Heavenly Spirit Sect possibly buy off? Could they buy off the entire cultivation realm?! What's more, this one, that one, and this one over here... they clearly agreed with my thesis in the past!"

This was precisely where Li Hao felt most aggrieved.

His confidence hadn't dropped from the sky; it had accumulated over centuries.

The thesis Li Hao published was a composite hybrid of the "Controllable Theory" and the "Reward Hypothesis," yet fundamentally divergent from both.

He posited that although an individual's innate aptitude dictated what heights they could reach, through external expedients such as medicinal pills and human furnaces, attaining higher cultivation realms was by no means impossible—an aspect that sounded much like the Controllable Theory. Yet regarding ascension itself, ascension was determined by "True Immortals"; if one catered to the rules of the True Immortal Realm, ascending became substantially easier—which leaned toward the Reward Hypothesis.

Consequently, Carefree Palace had always held sway with both camps, and both camps purchased goods from them. Even during IRL Dao debates, whenever cultivators from both factions squabbled, they were willing to heed Carefree Palace's mediation and nodded along with Li Hao's thesis. Who could have foreseen that merely swapping the forum to print, both sides would curse him so mercilessly!

Of course, as Palace Master of Carefree Palace, Li Hao was not entirely oblivious to the psychology of these cultivators.

He knew that the "Controllable Theory" emphasized the sovereign will of the cultivator, dismissing pills and external crutches as unorthodox paths; whereas the "Reward Hypothesis" acknowledged the overarching laws of the Heavenly Dao and cosmic order, rather than any specific True Immortal, so naturally both factions would refute Carefree Palace's stance... Those people had likely merely humored Carefree Palace to save face, unwilling to contradict him in person.

He knew that those people might not care about Carefree Palace's viewpoints at all. To attack their ideological rivals, they simply cherry-picked whichever fragments of Carefree Palace's thesis served their own arguments, hoping to rope Carefree Palace into their camp. Now that the papers had been published without Carefree Palace's brand attached, their intrinsic contempt was laid completely bare.

He knew that many of Carefree Palace's transactions operated in gray or outright black markets. Those willing to purchase such wares had already lowered their moral baselines into the gutter, or were merely hypocrites [burying their heads in the sand](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — plugging one's ears while stealing a bell; deceiving oneself"). Even so, they remained entirely unwilling to drag such sordid realities into the daylight...

Fuck, the more he thought about it, the more furious he became!

—"A pack of hypocrites!"-Li Hao hissed through gritted teeth.

—"P-Palace Master, should we suspend this plan for now?"-someone asked nervously.

—"Suspend what? Press on!"-Li Hao barked coldly.-"Giving up now is how we walk away with nothing."

[—"B-but..."](# "Speaker: Carefree Palace Subordinate")

—"What are you afraid of? Those participating in this debate are merely a handful of archaic fossils from the upper echelons of the cultivation realm. How many of them are there in total?"-Li Hao scoffed. His mind, at this moment, was finally churning with shrewd cunning.-"Besides, this debate is not without merit; it has shown us the staggering influence this medium wields over the grassroots tiers of the cultivation world! As long as we disseminate our arguments among them, even if they lack the standing to speak out today, they will become our staunch supporters tomorrow, perhaps even questioning their own seniors on our behalf. And this one-sided hostility will drive our sympathizers straight to our newspaper, transforming them into our diehard loyalists. However, before that, we must not expose our hand—what is that racket?!"

Just as the Palace Master of Carefree Palace was laying out his new "grand strategy," a sudden commotion erupted outside the hall.

He glared toward the entrance with displeasure.

The messenger stationed at the doorway froze in his tracks. He looked as though he would rather do anything than open his mouth, but had no choice but to bite the bullet and report tremulously:

—"*Spells* just issued an official announcement stating that the 'Supplementary Issue' was not published by them."

—"Hmph, entirely expected,"-Li Hao scoffed. Though irritated, he knew that having provoked such widespread uproar, exposure was merely a matter of time. He remained supremely confident:-"No matter. So long as the Untiring Immortal Venerable is away, they won't deduce that it was our Carefree Palace's handiwork anytime soon—"

—"...However, a newspaper headline has already exposed the origins of the 'Supplementary Issue.'"-the messenger stammered.

With those words, the messenger's head flew into the air.

His body remained locked in its kneeling posture, blood spraying across the flagstones.

Li Hao couldn't care less about the gruesome scene at the threshold. With a flick of his sleeve, the newspaper in the messenger's grip flew into his hand.

There on the front page, the headline read:

> *Who Forged the Spells Supplement? An Audit of Cultivation Realm Suspects*

This publication wasn't *White Jade Capital Weekly*, but an obscure tabloid titled *Weekly Gossip*. Written in an analytical, investigative tone, it painstakingly peeled back each layer of the mystery, connecting disparate clues until pointing squarely at the "culprit": Carefree Palace.

Faced with this, Li Hao could only roar in impotent fury:

—"WHO DID THIS?!"

***

—"As expected of Fairy Xia, this article is written magnificently,"-Mo Xi praised in boundless admiration.

Mo Xi was currently serving as the acting editor-in-chief of *White Jade Capital*, because the original editor-in-chief, Ran Deng, had accompanied Master on an outing, leaving no choice but to entrust this duty to Mo Xi.

During this period, such a massive catastrophe as the "counterfeit supplement" had erupted, entangling the academic debates in *White Jade Capital*. On one hand, Mo Xi was swamped to the point of lightheaded exhaustion; on the other hand, he gleefully imagined just how crushed Ran Deng would be when she returned and discovered she had missed out on such colossal domain-wide gossip—who told her and Eldest Senior Brother to sneak off with Master, even bringing along that sheep on the peach tree, while leaving him behind? Hmph!

Fairy Xia, meanwhile, was an unexpected pleasant surprise.

After reading *White Jade Capital Weekly*, this Mahayana powerhouse had wished to collaborate with the Heavenly Spirit Sect. However, the content she favored diverged slightly from *White Jade Capital*'s editorial tone. Thus, the Untiring Immortal Venerable suggested she launch an independent publication of her own and had Ran Deng mentor her. In the end, Ran Deng recommended a whole mountain of her own "private collection" to Fairy Xia, suggested naming the new tabloid *Weekly Gossip*, and arranged for Fairy Xia to moonlight as a reporter at *White Jade Capital* to familiarize herself with the operational workflow.

Furthermore, during the Untiring Immortal Venerable's absence, Fairy Xia served as another guardian pillar holding down the mountain, preventing troublemakers from inciting chaos at *White Jade Capital*—with the academic flame war reaching fever pitch, someone was bound to try something reckless. If Heavenly Spirit Sect hadn't possessed a Tribulation Transcendence titan presiding, hotheaded cultivators might well have tried to bomb the editorial office of *White Jade Capital* long ago.

Little did anyone anticipate that while *White Jade Capital* remained unscathed, *Spells* took the hit first.

When Qu Qianxing first saw that *Spells* supplement... no, that counterfeit *Spells* supplement, the expression on his face had made Mo Xi's knees turn to water, sending him scrambling out the door on all fours.

He truly hadn't known Second Senior Brother's strikingly handsome face could twist into something so horrifyingly contorted.

What worried Mo Xi even more was that if Master didn't return soon, Qu Qianxing's investigation alone might result in a continental bloodbath.

Fortunately, Fairy Xia had stepped in.

She had practically deduced the mastermind in an instant.

—"You are truly incredible!"-Mo Xi showered her with an endless stream of [rainbow farts](# "Literal CN: 彩虹屁 (cǎihóng pì) — rainbow farts; modern Chinese internet/fandom slang for extravagant, poetic flattery and over-the-top praise").-"Nothing in this world can escape your swiftlets! I was still fretting over what to do, yet who knew you could identify the 'culprit' so swiftly!"

—"Oh, not at all! It wasn't my little darlings who uncovered it."-Fairy Xia said, raising her hand as a wisp of cloud drifted from her sleeve and coalesced into a swiftlet.-"My swallows are merely convenient for transmitting information; they aren't particularly adept at ferreting out hidden conspiracies. Besides, when Carefree Palace orchestrated such a scheme, how could they have let me uncover it so easily? Presumably even the Lunar Phase Mirror of Huanfei Sect—the finest artifact under heaven for peeping—would fail to pierce the truth, wouldn't it?"

—"Then how did you manage it?"-Mo Xi asked, his curiosity thoroughly piqued.

He noticed Fairy Xia glancing down at the newspaper in his hands.

—"Could it be that, just as this article claims, you deduced it purely through raw logical deduction?!"-Mo Xi gasped in awe.-"Doesn't that make you even more formidable?!"

—"Well, as much as I'd love to claim that!"-Fairy Xia laughed merrily, pointing toward an unfamiliar cultivator standing outside the door.-"Allow me to introduce him: that gentleman is the 'former' editor-in-chief of *Cultivation World Weekly*, as well as the mastermind behind this supplement incident. However, he is quite sensible; seeing the writing on the wall, he immediately rushed here to seek my asylum. It just so happened that my *Weekly Gossip* was short on talent, so I took him under my wing."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

The smile on Mo Xi's face froze solid.

Fairy Xia harbored zero prejudice regarding hiring:

—"Once you know the culprit, isn't 'deducing' the rest child's play? I had someone write this article based on the mystery novels Ran Deng lent me. Quite entertaining, isn't it?! I plan to compile *Weekly Gossip* in this serialized style from now on. This must be what the Untiring Immortal Venerable referred to as '[product differentiation](# "Literal CN: 差异化 (chāyìhuà) — product differentiation / unique market positioning; modern business concept taught by Zhou Hui")'?"-Fairy Xia asked with a smile.

—"...Heh, heh, indeed, quite entertaining!"-Mo Xi said, glancing once more at that former editor-in-chief of *Cultivation World Weekly* before lowering his voice.-"Still, with a background like his, you must keep a very close eye on him!"

—"Naturally. Since I've taken him in, I won't allow him to cause your Heavenly Spirit Sect any further trouble,"-Fairy Xia assured him.

—"That's not what I mean. I was referring to his mortal safety..."-Mo Xi muttered.

—"Heh, Carefree Palace won't find it so easy to murder someone under my watch,"-Fairy Xia replied with serene confidence.-"Besides, I'll be staying at your Heavenly Spirit Sect during this period; you could at least have some faith in your sect's mountain-protecting grand array! Carefree Palace couldn't possibly infiltrate this place to cause mischief."

[Is it Carefree Palace I'm worried about?! I'm worried about the person who controls our mountain-protecting grand array!]

[One stray golden lightning bolt from above, and your new editor-in-chief will be vaporized into nothingness!]

Mo Xi thought to himself, his stomach throbbing with acute phantom pains.

Alas, he couldn't openly badmouth his own senior brother before outsiders, so he resolved to keep a vigilant eye on things himself.

—"However, compared to any trouble harboring this unlucky fellow might bring, your Heavenly Spirit Sect's troubles are actually far greater,"-Fairy Xia remarked.-"Surely you don't think this matter ends here?"

—"Of course not!"-Mo Xi replied with a chilled expression.-"Carefree Palace dared pull a stunt like this; we will make them pay dearly."

[Still, once, twice, three times... when on earth will Carefree Palace vanish from our sight for good?]

[In any case, unlike Mo Bei, I don't have a singular, dedicated martial Dao to pursue—my current primary task is refining that dragon-shaped mineral core within my body to thoroughly reconstruct my meridians and dantian, which is a methodical step-by-step process. In the meantime, why not make expanding the Immortal Bazaar, seizing Carefree Palace's trade routes, and displacing Carefree Palace entirely my primary objective?]

[Doing so might even uncover the truth behind Mo Bei's and my origins.]

Mo Xi calculated quietly.

Unaware of Mo Xi's silent ambitions, Fairy Xia warned with deep concern:

—"It is not merely a question of price. Having gone this far, Carefree Palace will certainly not give up. They must have realized the staggering reach of newspapers and periodicals. Even if we've stripped away their skin and turned them into the laughingstock of the cultivation world today, so long as they shamelessly persist in their propaganda blitz, impressionable young cultivators will inevitably fall for their trap—"

—"You are entirely correct! They haven't waited for tomorrow; they have already made their move,"-a voice chimed in from behind.

Mo Xi whipped his head around and spotted Qu Qianxing standing there without anyone knowing when he had arrived. Mo Xi let out a startled "Eek!" and flailed both hands wildly, attempting to block Qu Qianxing's line of sight so he wouldn't see what he shouldn't see.

—"What are you screeching about!"-Qu Qianxing snapped, swatting Mo Xi's forehead to halt his frantic flailing.

Simultaneously, he cast a glance toward the former editor-in-chief of *Cultivation World Weekly*.

In truth, the man could never have set foot inside the Heavenly Spirit Sect without Qu Qianxing's explicit permission. Qu Qianxing had even thoroughly audited his background—he was a once-notorious fraudster in the cultivation realm who joined Carefree Palace because of his silver tongue. He hadn't committed much heinous evil; he simply relied on his glib mouth and his peerless instinct for chasing profit, dodging danger, and trimming his sails to the wind to climb into the executive ranks. Yet the moment he was shoved into the chief editor's chair of *Cultivation World Weekly*, he realized he was out of his depth and prepared his escape route from day one.

Qu Qianxing harbored zero interest in such petty nonentities; he knew who his true enemy was.

As for Mo Xi, while he appeared sharp and calculating when engineering secret realm guidance systems and expanding commercial routes, why did he always indulge in such childish antics?!

[Why does he always act like a spoiled brat?] Qu Qianxing ruffled Mo Xi's hair with helpless exasperation.

—"Second Senior Brother! Let go of me!"-Mo Xi struggled.

Alas, nobody paid any heed to his protests.

Fairy Xia, the only one who could have rescued him, had her attention captured by Qu Qianxing's words:

—"What did you mean just now by 'they have made their move'?"-Fairy Xia asked, turning toward Qu Qianxing.

—"Carefree Palace just issued a domain-wide proclamation, claiming they have invited the Northern Domain's Tribulation Transcendence titan, Goddess Ren Ran, to host a Grand Preaching Assembly on the Isle of No Return in the Boundless Sea, inviting all cultivators under heaven,"-Qu Qianxing reported.

—"Goddess Ren Ran?!"-Fairy Xia gasped in astonishment.-"They actually managed to invite her out of seclusion?!"

—"Is she very formidable?"-Mo Xi asked, clutching his forehead.

—"She shares the Tribulation Transcendence stage with Master; how could she not be formidable?"-Qu Qianxing countered.

Mo Xi let out a disdainful scoff.

[Carefree Palace's Sovereign Immortal Chana was also a Tribulation Transcendence titan! And wasn't he obliterated all the same?]

[Are all Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses created equal?]

—"Goddess Ren Ran is different; the word 'God' cannot be casually appended to someone's name,"-Qu Qianxing explained.-"In truth, Her Holiness is truly extraordinary. Rumor has it that the ancient Demon-God War concluded precisely because she sacrificed her mortal body to seal the Demon Sovereign's head. However, her divine true soul was consequently trapped in this mortal world, unable to ascend. Thus, she is the sole living deity walking this world."

—"Whoa—"-Mo Xi gasped in wonder.-"Doesn't that make her an old hag over ten thousand years old?"

—"Insolent!"-Qu Qianxing snapped, flicking Mo Xi's forehead.

—"Hmph, who told her to side with Carefree Palace?"-Mo Xi grumbled, rubbing his brow.

—"Indeed. Why would such a living goddess be willing to take Carefree Palace's side?"-Fairy Xia mused, equally perplexed.-"I would have thought that even if Carefree Palace sought a powerhouse for endorsement, they would have approached that person in Sky City..."

[—"That person in Sky City?"](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

—"Word has it that person harbors an old grievance with your Master,"-Fairy Xia remarked thoughtfully.

She lifted her gaze, only to be met by two pairs of intensely glittering eyes.

[—"An old grievance?!"](# "Speakers: Qu Qianxing & Mo Xi")-Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi asked in unison, having never heard a single whisper of this before.

Naturally, they knew that before their Master, outside of "cultivation" and "ascension," there were no momentous affairs under heaven. Most likely, even if an old grievance existed, it was purely one-sided on the other party's part; Master had probably forgotten who they were centuries ago.

Yet this was Master's past! *Master's!*

Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi were naturally bursting with curiosity.

Seeing the pair's reactions, Fairy Xia's enthusiasm flared up immediately. She loved recounting these sorts of "tales" most of all—especially to the subject's own disciples. Tossing Carefree Palace's maneuvers straight to the back of her mind, she pulled up a chair, settled into a posture prepared for a lengthy yarn, and drew out her words:

—"Well, that dates all the way back to when your Master was in the Golden Core stage... Ah?!"-Fairy Xia began, settling into a comfortable posture, only to be interrupted by a sudden violent jolt.

[—"?!"](# "Speakers: Qu Qianxing & Mo Xi")

A sudden, violent tremor shattered Fairy Xia's storytelling.

Although an "earthquake" of this intensity could hardly harm cultivators, the sheer fact of an "unpredicted earthquake" sent shockwaves through the cultivators' composure.

—"A [subterranean dragon turning over](# "Literal CN: 地龙翻身 (dìlóng fānshēn) — the earth dragon rolling over; ancient Chinese folkloric idiom for an earthquake")? No, that's not it!"-Fairy Xia gasped, springing to her feet.

She raised her arms; with a rustling flutter of wings, a flock of swiftlets surged outward.

As the swiftlets took flight, Fairy Xia's eyes widened in sheer disbelief.

Like a sleepwalker, she drifted toward the window and gazed up into the sky:

—"Haha, looks like there's no need to worry after all! Forget some Goddess; even if Carefree Palace conjured a True Immortal out of thin air, what your Master has just done ensures they won't stir up so much as a ripple..."

[—"What?"](# "Speakers: Qu Qianxing & Mo Xi")

Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi exchanged glances, then hurried to the window and looked up into the heavens.

***

—"W-what is going on?!"-Li Hao panicked.

The intensity of this "earthquake" was not extreme, but its reach was staggering—the entire cultivation realm had reacted, including the Eastern Domain.

In Li Hao's eyes, Carefree Palace shouldn't have felt any tremor at all.

After all, the Eastern Domain was naturally prone to earthquakes and tsunamis; when constructing Carefree Palace, they had taken this into account with specialized defensive formations. Unless the tremors outside shattered the entire Eastern Domain to pieces, Carefree Palace shouldn't have felt a thing.

So... had the Eastern Domain split in half?!

While harboring this terrifying suspicion, Li Hao stared nervously at the structure before him.

It was a palace.

A palace plastered from top to bottom with yellow talisman papers. The "Ancestor" of Carefree Palace resided within.

For Li Hao, he would rather the Eastern Domain split in half than have this palace collapse.

That "Ancestor" inside hadn't fully regenerated yet; letting him out now would be an absolute catastrophe.

In truth, had Carefree Palace not suffered such staggering humiliation this time, he wouldn't have been willing to come anywhere near this palace in the first place.

Yet as matters stood, to prevent Carefree Palace from becoming the perpetual laughingstock of the cultivation realm, Li Hao had been forced to pull out the backup plan from that meeting—using a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse as the rallying banner to attract cultivators across the world.

However, even as Palace Master of Carefree Palace, compelling a Tribulation Transcendence titan to step forward was no simple task. He had no choice but to beseech this "Ancestor."

Now that matters had proceeded as intended, Li Hao had come to report their progress, only to be struck by this bizarre "earthquake."

[Could this 'earthquake' be some foul omen?] Li Hao thought uneasily.

At that moment, an ear-grating screech—like fingernails dragging across a stone wall—emanated from within the palace:

—"Foul indeed, and it is no longer merely an 'omen'! Now, whatever grand assembly you host will be utterly useless!"

—"What?!"-Li Hao gasped.

—"Look up at the sky,"-the voice instructed.

Guided by the voice, Li Hao tilted his head toward the heavens, then let out a blood-curdling shriek:

—"The... the Ascension Stairway?!"

—"To think Untiring actually excavated this thing,"-the voice murmured with a sigh.

—"Untiring dug up... wait! Isn't that thing buried beneath the Abyssal Ocean?! Then what about our experimental grounds—?!"

Li Hao cried out in sheer, chaotic hysteria.
