# Chapter 163: The Mastermind's Plan

To be precise, that Taotie was one of the [behind-the-scenes masterminds](# "CN: 黑幕 (hēimù) — literally 'black curtain'; shadowy conspirator / manipulator pulling the strings from behind the scenes").

Even though it certainly didn't look like one.

After all, not only had that Taotie been banished to the lower realm, but it had somehow ended up dead. Even its soul had been split into two: one half was sliced apart by humans for experiments, producing a half-human, half-feral-beast child, while the other half was raised in captivity like livestock, waiting for the first half to seek it out so they could devour one another, before being packaged up and shipped back to the Upper Realm in one fell swoop.

No matter how one looked at it, this track record screamed 100% "victim."

Coupled with the universal stereotype that a Taotie knew nothing but eating, no one would ever suspect this beast of being one of the conspirators. Even Zhou Hui had assumed the Taotie was merely being kept in captivity by some True Immortal.

In reality, however, as an ancient ferocious beast of monumental renown, the Taotie was not merely voracious—it was profoundly cunning.

One needed only look at Yuwen Xuan's brain to see that clearly.

That fellow never applied himself to honest work, and his exam scores were an outright insult to his master's pedigree; yet the moment he put his mind to crooked schemes, even a Yin Wraith renowned for sinister cunning like Mo Xi wasn't fit to hold a candle to him.

His original half-soul form was all the more so.

That had been an exceptionally vicious Taotie. For reasons unknown, it had been born uncomfortably close to the Heavenly Court. When the Heavenly Court attempted to capture it back then, it ended up devouring an entire squad of celestial soldiers before fleeing into the junction between the Immortal and Demon Realms, where it eluded capture for several millennia.

Still, being endlessly hunted and besieged by the Heavenly Court made life rather difficult for the Taotie.

Because it devoured anything and everything without discretion, demonic qi had seeped into its brain, plunging it into a semi-deranged state. There was no telling if one day, eating just one more demonic beast might drive it completely insane, leading it to devour itself.

The vicious Taotie had no desire to die; it began scheming a method to cleanse its own demonic qi.

It was during this period that the Taotie caught wind of some news: the Heavenly Court was preparing to abandon an extraordinary yet unruly "minor world," repurposing it as a [flood diversion basin](# "CN: 泄洪区 (xièhóngqū) — flood runoff area; water discharge zone") for the Demon Realm.

Instantly, the Taotie had a bold idea.

It feigned capture by a True Immortal, manipulating the immortal into deploying it as a living weapon to invade this world. Then, it ruthlessly let humans slay it, splitting its soul in two, leaving its old body behind as bait and sustenance for its new self, and charting out its route of return in advance.

That's right: every shred of its seemingly tragic ordeal had been engineered by its own design, all just to obtain a brand-new body!

To this end, the Taotie had stashed its memories away in the Upper Realm, making it convenient for the "reborn body" to devour the other party the moment it ascended... No, that process was properly called "returning to the true body."

Unfortunately, where the Taotie miscalculated was that a portion of its soul had actually been taken in by an anomalous human who defied all conventional rules—and had even been "domesticated." As a result, that soul became so thoroughly saturated with its newfound attachments that the fresh memories overwhelmed and displaced the past ones.

Worse still, upon swallowing those stashed memories, the Taotie discovered that the true body's memories were showing signs of being assimilated.

It actually felt a faint twinge of envy toward the other half of "itself."

*If only what it had encountered back then hadn't been celestial soldiers trying to hunt it down, but Master instead...*

Naturally, the wild, untamed Taotie sneered at such weak-willed sentimentality. Convinced that it had been compromised by the domesticated memories of being raised as a pet, it decided to sever those memories once and for all: it fed plenty of gratuitous intel to a certain author of the Heavenly Scripture. What was more, upon discovering that Zhou Hui rewound time again and again, it grew so thoroughly humiliated and infuriated that it even wrote itself into the "plot."

[If Master finds out about this, I'm completely done for...]

Yuwen Xuan concluded with absolute certainty.

Of course, from Yuwen Xuan's perspective, whatever that Taotie had done, he refused to acknowledge it.

What did that terrifying, ferocious beast have to do with him, an adorable, well-behaved newborn little lamb?

Except Yuwen Xuan also knew full well that even if he deliberately regressed into that foolish little black lamb to plead his case, there was no way he could [play cute to slide by](# "Literal CN: 萌混过关 (méng hùn guò guān) — act adorably innocent to escape accountability / muddle through on cuteness") in front of Master!

In Master's eyes, "reincarnation and rebirth" was hardly an excuse to dodge culpability.

Not to mention, the relationship between him and that Taotie was nowhere near as simple as mere "reincarnation."

—"Had I known, I never would've swallowed those memories,"-Yuwen Xuan muttered.-"Even if it meant discarding seven lifetimes of the past, I should have made a clean break with that cursed bastard once and for all. Now haven't I basically confessed to an unforgivable crime?"

[—"What did you say?!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

Tao Zhu was walking ahead, but a passing gust of wind faintly carried Yuwen Xuan's muttering forward. Unable to catch the words clearly, he turned his head and shouted back in inquiry.

—"Boss Tao, can you crack the secrets of this secret realm?"-Yuwen Xuan asked, making no attempt to conceal his unease and looking rather guilty.

—"Cough, cough! This is Langhuan, you know! *Langhuan!* The legendary imperial library of the Heavenly Emperor!"-Tao Zhu coughed twice, suddenly feeling a bit sheepish himself. But a moment later, he puffed out his chest, completely righteous and self-assured again.-"Do you think it's that easy to crack? Even someone like me will need a bit of time."

—"...But the [Tenth Month Festival](# "Cultural: 十月朝 (Shíyuècháo) — the First of the Tenth Lunar Month, also known as the Winter Clothes Festival (寒衣节); a traditional date for seasonal academic evaluations and ancestor veneration") is almost upon us,"-Yuwen Xuan murmured softly.

This time, Boss Tao heard him loud and clear:

—"The Tenth Month Festival?! You want to crack this secret realm before the Tenth Month Festival? Impossible! Absolutely impossible! What can anyone accomplish in such a pitiful amount of time?! Even spending hundreds or thousands of years cracking it would still be..."-Boss Tao sputtered.

—"Wait a second... the Tenth Month Festival..."-Boss Tao paused, suddenly 'putting two and two together.'-"I seem to recall hearing that Untiring conducts the academic assessment of his direct disciples on the Tenth Month Festival. Don't tell me you want to present the secret realm to your master on that day?!"

Yuwen Xuan averted his gaze.

Yet Boss Tao felt he had "figured it out":

—"Aha! I knew it! Why else would you suddenly bring me here out of nowhere? So you wanted my help just so you could [borrow another's flowers to offer to the Buddha](# "Idiom: 借花献佛 (jiè huā xiàn fó) — presenting a gift or taking credit using someone else's resources")!"-Boss Tao chuckled smugly.

—"That's not it at all,"-Yuwen Xuan retorted with apparent urgency, though he was secretly leaning into the other man's assumption.-"I stumbled across this place purely by chance while out on experiential trials! But my cultivation is meager and my strength too weak to crack it. If I tell Master, he will surely bury his head straight inside and refuse to leave. With the Immortal Arts Assembly underway and Junior Brother facing his tribulation, Master has already been utterly exhausted mentally and physically. I just don't want Master to overwork himself..."

Yuwen Xuan's explanation was a disorganized jumble, yet his heartfelt worry for his master shone through clearly.

Listening to this, Tao Zhu felt a pang of jealousy—raising a little brat wasn't completely without its perks after all. Look at that, even a feral Taotie cub was so well-behaved! No wonder that fellow Untiring adopted them one after another.

—"So you came looking for me to open the door for you?"-Tao Zhu asked sourly.

—"You have the best friendship with Master, and you are the most formidable. I trust you, Boss Tao,"-Yuwen Xuan said with impeccable obedience.

—"Hmph,"-Boss Tao gave a disdainful snort.

Yet deep down, a tense string in his heart finally relaxed.

Boss Tao was Boss Tao after all—a merchant who could build a thriving enterprise even in the treacherous perils of the Abyssal Ocean.

He never believed one could reap benefits without paying a price.

Besides, he had no prior acquaintance with Yuwen Xuan. For the boy to bypass his own master and seek him out with such a windfall was suspicious no matter how one looked at it. Surely it couldn't be because of Jiuzhai's cryptic, superstitious nonsense about them having "the karmic affinity of master and disciple in one lifetime," could it?

Thus, when Tao Zhu followed Yuwen Xuan here, part of it was driven by mercenary greed, but another part was a playful desire to see what games this little brat was trying to pull.

Who could have guessed that this secret realm was genuinely Langhuan?!

The legendary Langhuan!

Amidst his pleasant surprise, the suspicions in Tao Zhu's heart only deepened. He had never hesitated to gauge human nature from the darkest angles (even if the boy before him wasn't human—or at least, not entirely human). He had even entertained the possibility that Yuwen Xuan wanted to bypass his master, swallow Langhuan all for himself, and trick Tao Zhu into opening the gates only to [silence him by murder](# "Idiom: 杀人灭口 (shā rén miè kǒu) — kill someone to prevent secrets from leaking / silence the witness")—and don't say a Void Refinement couldn't kill a Mahayana; this opponent was an ancient ferocious beast!

Now that he had uncovered Yuwen Xuan's "petty agenda"—merely tricking him into opening the door so he could show off in front of his master—Tao Zhu actually breathed a sigh of relief.

Still, Tao Zhu did not let his guard down entirely:

—"Are you truly going to present it to your master? This is Langhuan, you know. If you swallowed it for yourself, it would be an astronomical fortune!"-Tao Zhu probed cautiously.

—"I know this is Langhuan. It's written right over the gate, and you've repeated it several times already,"-Yuwen Xuan replied, growing slightly impatient now as he looked at Tao Zhu as though staring at a senile old geezer.-"In the end, though, isn't it basically just the same kind of thing as our sect's Library Pavilion?"

—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion can hardly compare..."-Tao Zhu muttered under his breath.

Yet upon second thought, he felt a bit uncertain.

While it couldn't compare right now, given Untiring's temperament, the future was truly anyone's guess.

Clearly, however, to a Taotie, every library in the world was pretty much the same:

—"Besides, books don't taste good anyway. If I eat them, Master will be even more furious than before, so what's the point of eating them? I might as well crack it open to make Master happy. Maybe he'll even reward me!"-Yuwen Xuan reasoned.

—"That truly makes sense,"-Tao Zhu remarked.

Fitting logic for a Taotie indeed.

Still, craving a reward... Untiring truly had his ways, managing to domesticate this terrifying Taotie until it behaved like an eager puppy.

With that, the very last shred of Tao Zhu's apprehension evaporated.

—"When your master learns of your filial devotion, he will surely be overjoyed,"-Tao Zhu stated impassively.

—"Of course he will! There are so many books here,"-Yuwen Xuan declared, a hint of smug pride surfacing on his face.

—"No, it isn't merely the books. The very existence of Langhuan possesses immense value in its own right,"-Tao Zhu explained in detail.-"I don't know if your master has ever mentioned it to you, but there is a hypothesis in our cultivation world: that our minor world was once part of the Upper Realm, which plummeted down for some unknown reason and was demoted into a minor world. The existence of Langhuan is practically living proof of that hypothesis."

[Of course I know that.]

It was precisely for this reason that the Heavenly Court designated this minor world as a flood diversion basin for the Demon Realm—they had no desire for Langhuan to endure.

And it was precisely this factor that the Taotie had set its sights on when intentionally descending here!

In fact, in the Taotie's grand plan, it hadn't merely aimed to obtain a fresh body; more crucially, it sought to establish a karmic tether with this world. That way, it could easily return in the future, turning this minor world—which once formed part of the Upper Realm—into its permanent personal feeding trough.

Even Yuwen Xuan had to admit the plan was devilishly tempting.

Yet Yuwen Xuan let none of it show on his face, merely mumbling with breezy nonchalance:

[—"...Ah, I think Master might have mentioned something like that!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

Seeing this, Tao Zhu couldn't help letting out a wry chuckle:

*Indeed, how could anyone expect a Taotie to comprehend such concepts?*

—"Therefore, simply reporting the matter of Langhuan directly to your master would already make him overjoyed. You don't necessarily have to open the gates,"-Tao Zhu told the "ignorant Taotie," explaining the library's value as a final test.

—"Of course I know he'd be pleased! But running over to give him a set of coordinates—forcing him to rack his brains to pry the door open—versus me already opening the door and presenting him with Langhuan's ancient collection on a silver platter... those are two completely different things, aren't they?"-Yuwen Xuan retorted.

He stared at Tao Zhu with eager anticipation:

—"Ah, I'm not asking Boss Tao to completely unlock the main gates either. But finding a way to crack something out of Langhuan in time for the Tenth Month Festival... that should still be doable, right?"-Yuwen Xuan pressed.

—"You certainly know how to present gifts,"-Boss Tao said, feeling sour once again.

—"Then, Boss Tao, shall we begin?"-Yuwen Xuan urged.

With apparent excitement, he pointed toward the very center of Langhuan:

—"I heard that the genuine Heavenly Scripture is hidden in the deepest depths of Langhuan. Even if we can't get the whole book, just a portion—or even a single page—would be enough for me to present to Master!"-Yuwen Xuan declared eagerly.

...Of course, only after altering the final portion of its contents!

Because that text recorded the evidence of someone from the Upper Realm interfering with this world!

It also recorded the origin of the Taotie!

[That is something Master must never, ever see!]

Yuwen Xuan calculated anxiously in his heart.

Suspecting nothing amiss, Boss Tao was brimming with motivation:

—"Heheh, the genuine Heavenly Scripture, is it? Well, I'll need to examine the actual object before I can be sure,"-Tao Zhu chuckled enthusiastically.

The two chatted pleasantly as they strode into the depths of Langhuan.
