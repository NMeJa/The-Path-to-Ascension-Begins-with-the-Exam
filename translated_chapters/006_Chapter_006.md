# Chapter 6: Collateral Damage (第6章 殃及池鱼)

A sect-wide Grand Unified Examination.

This was by no means a spur-of-the-moment whim on Zhou Hui's part.

In truth, the notion had already crossed his mind the moment he felt dissatisfied with his direct disciples' test scores, decided to drill them on foundational knowledge, and went to the Library Pavilion to compile teaching materials. That was when he had sensed the "illicit trade" taking place right beside him—indeed, Zhou Hui had known from the very beginning.

Since he had already resolved to teach from the fundamentals anyway, [herding one sheep was no different from herding a whole flock](# "Literal CN: 放一只羊是放，放一群羊也是放 — idiom meaning once you undertake a task for one person, extending it to a whole group requires little extra effort; might as well shepherd the entire herd").

Besides, in the "novel," it was precisely because the protagonist suffered bullying in the outer sect that the subsequent cascade of disasters unfolded.

Although Zhou Hui didn't believe he could outright alter destiny—after all, even in his past world, school bullying had existed, and even an almighty cultivator at the Tribulation Transcendence stage couldn't keep his eyes glued to every single disciple twenty-four hours a day—there was still no reason to perpetuate unfair treatment toward the outer sect while knowing full well that problems existed. At the very least, he needed to bridge the chasm separating the inner and outer sects.

A standardized, test-oriented education that treated everyone equally happened to fit the bill perfectly.

In short, his principle remained: save as many as he could.

Having made this decision, Zhou Hui hadn't yet settled on when to hold this "Grand Unified Examination." Seeing this little drama play out before him today, he figured [there was no better day than today](# "Literal CN: choosing a day is not as good as bumping into a day (择日不如撞日) — there is no time like the present; seizing the immediate opportunity") and simply took advantage of the moment.

Zhou Hui spoke in a gentle, benevolent tone:

—"Indeed, the fault lies with me. As the Sect Master of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I should never have [favored one while slighting the other](# "Literal CN: treat this one thick and that one thin (厚此薄彼) — showing blatant partiality or favoritism") from the start. Since everyone is brimming with such drive, let the entire sect, from top to bottom, sit for a grand examination,"-Zhou Hui said.

Hearing him say that, even Qu Qianxing—who had been adamant about severely punishing the exam-scalping ring—felt that things had taken a terrible turn.

—"Master, what need is there to go this far?"-Qu Qianxing said, unable to bear it.

[The only ones who did wrong were these few! The other outer sect disciples are entirely innocent!]

—"Why speak of it as if it were a punishment? Isn't this precisely what everyone desires?"-Zhou Hui asked with a faint smile.

In truth, Zhou Hui was well aware that what the disciples wanted was exam papers... no, to be precise, what everyone wanted was "exam papers on par with Foundation Establishment Pills." If they had had the means to purchase Foundation Establishment Pills, they wouldn't have wanted to touch an exam paper with a ten-foot pole.

Yet Zhou Hui did not expose them.

Besides, as a former [champion of the rat race](# "Literal CN: involution king (卷王) — modern slang for someone who out-works, out-studies, and out-grinds everyone else in hyper-competitive environments"), Zhou Hui genuinely believed that exams were a wonderful thing.

Being like him, where even [the qualification to grind in the rat race](# "Literal CN: to involute / roll (卷) — compete in the relentless academic grind") had been stripped away by the Heavenly Dao—now that was true cruelty.

Zhou Hui offered no further explanation to Qu Qianxing, turning instead toward the prime culprit, his youngest disciple:

—"Xi'er,"-Zhou Hui called out.

—"P-Present,"-Mo Xi answered nervously.

—"You feel that copying lines is pointless, don't you?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"I didn't..."-Mo Xi said, his words belying his thoughts.

—"Since you feel it's pointless, you needn't copy them anymore,"-Zhou Hui said.-"In exchange, you shall oversee the preparations for the examination venue."

—"No, Master!"-Mo Xi cried, eyes going wide.

In truth, he didn't dislike the work itself, and deep down he even considered it far better than [punitive copying](# "Literal CN: penal copying (罚抄) — assigning lines or copying mistakes as academic punishment"). But he understood all too well that this was a "punishment"—if he botched it, the consequences wouldn't be as simple as writing lines.

Mo Xi grabbed the hem of Zhou Hui's robe in a panic:

—"Master, I'm sorry! I won't dare do it again! I truly don't hate copying lines! Or... or you can punish me by making me copy them another hundred times! That works too!"-Mo Xi pleaded, looking up at Zhou Hui with misty, tearful cat-like eyes, the tip of his nose turning red.

Regrettably, this held zero effect on Zhou Hui.

—"Copying was never a punishment in the first place, but rather a method to help you memorize better. Since you still view it that way, it proves you are unwilling,"-Zhou Hui said in a gentle voice.-"In that case, wouldn't it be better to do something you are more interested in?"

Giving Mo Xi no chance to speak, Zhou Hui instructed:

—"Although this is the first time the Grand Unified Examination is being held, you have assisted your Second Senior Brother in organizing several sect events in the past. It shouldn't be beyond your abilities. I will provide you with a concrete plan later; simply follow it step by step,"-Zhou Hui instructed.

—"That being said, I expect every single disciple in the entire sect to participate without exception, sitting for the exam in their peak condition, with absolutely zero cheating. In short: fair, just, and transparent,"-he continued.

—"If you cannot even manage this small task, then I truly will have no choice but to assign punitive copying. For instance, having you copy every book on the first floor of the Library Pavilion one thousand times,"-Zhou Hui added with an impassive smile.

Hearing those words, Mo Xi shuddered from head to toe.

Given the sheer volume of volumes stored in the Library Pavilion, even just the first floor would be more than enough to consume the entirety of Mo Xi's lifespan as a Foundation Establishment cultivator.

Dropping all attempts to act cute, he scrambled to bargain for better conditions to complete the task—or rather, he wanted to drag others down into the water with him:

—"Master, the Grand Unified Examination is such a momentous event. I'm afraid I won't be able to handle it alone—?"-Mo Xi ventured cautiously.

—"You may enlist the help of your 'friends' here,"-Zhou Hui said, casting a glance toward the Xiang siblings.

Mo Xi understood instantly: this was "collective punishment." None of the other disciples who had dodged their punitive copying would be escaping either.

Naturally, leaving everything entirely to this group was out of the question.

Zhou Hui turned back to his second disciple:

—"Qianxing, you shall oversee your junior brother,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Yes, Master,"-Qu Qianxing nodded.

He, too, felt that leaving the Grand Unified Examination in the hands of this little junior brother was deeply unsettling.

But Qu Qianxing had not forgotten what Master had originally assigned:

—"However, regarding the matter of gathering the fragments of the Ascension Platform and reorganizing the Library Pavilion you instructed me to do—"-Qu Qianxing began.

—"No matter. I shall simply assign others to handle that,"-Zhou Hui said dismissively.

His expression grew solemn as he looked at the disciples present:

—"Right now, the single most important matter for the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect is this Grand Unified Examination. I expect you to handle it with the utmost diligence and care,"-Zhou Hui said.

[—"Yes, Master."](# "Speaker: Disciples")

The disciples respectfully accepted the command.

* * *

News of the sect-wide Grand Unified Examination spread across the entire sect like wildfire.

Instantly, a chorus of wails and lamentations echoed from disciples across the entire sect. And then—

And then, inner sect exam papers became even more fiercely sought-after!

This was only natural: with an exam looming, how could anyone not prepare frantically? Furthermore, word had gotten out that Lord Immortal Venerable had reformed the examination questions, introducing unprecedented question formats that scrutinized even the most obscure, trivial bits of knowledge. How could anyone not scramble to get their hands on those exam papers to study? By comparison, the rumor that the papers might contain heavenly secrets suddenly seemed trivial.

Moreover, this time it wasn't just the outer sect disciples—even the inner sect disciples began to panic.

Qin Xian of the inner sect's Weapon Refinement Hall was one of them.

There was simply no helping it: on the previous inner sect exam, Qin Xian had scored a miserable ten-something points. Had Eldest Senior Brother not [cushioned the bottom of the ranks](# "Literal CN: cushioned the bottom (垫底) — being at the very bottom of the score rankings, acting as a cushion for everyone else"), Qin Xian would have been the undisputed champion of last place.

If Qin Xian's academic performance had always been that atrocious, it wouldn't have stung so much. The problem was, he was actually considered an elite.

Qin Xian possessed a Single Fire Spirit Root. Such talent would have been sufficient to make him a core disciple even in a major sect, let alone in a third-rate sect like the Heavenly Spirit Sect. It was only because Zhou Hui's direct disciples all possessed extraordinary backgrounds and peerless talent that someone of Qin Xian's caliber was relegated to being an ordinary inner sect disciple.

Qin Xian himself hadn't minded in the slightest. The Heavenly Spirit Sect had few people and even fewer duties; even the Hall of Artifact Refinement, a vital sect hub, barely had any workload. Qin Xian had been able to [slack off to his heart's content](# "Literal CN: catch fish in murky waters (摸鱼) — modern Chinese slang for slacking off or loafing on the job"). His cultivation was also a classic case of [fishing for three days and drying the nets for two](# "Literal CN: 三天打鱼两天晒网 — working in fits and starts; lacking consistency and discipline"). Yet relying solely on the fact that a single spirit root required fewer techniques to cultivate, coupled with his high innate talent, his cultivation progress had consistently remained at the top among his peers.

Unfortunately, such a study attitude proved paper-thin before Zhou Hui's [baseline assessment exam](# "Literal CN: bottom-feeling exam (摸底考试) — diagnostic/placement test given at the start of a school year to determine students' baseline competency"), stripping away all pretenses and revealing his true colors.

Even Qin Xian himself had been frightened out of his wits.

He had always carried the swaggering confidence of an honor student, yet confronted with that exam paper, he had been unable to answer a single thing. He had genuinely felt like a complete failure.

Consequently, although Qin Xian's quota of [punitive copying](# "Literal CN: penal copying (罚抄) — assigning lines or copying mistakes as academic punishment") put him among the heaviest-punished batch, he hadn't dared to outsource it like the others, obediently making corrections and copying every line himself.

He had only just finished copying with great difficulty and was beginning to feel he had gleaned some actual knowledge when news of the Grand Unified Examination struck.

Qin Xian nearly blacked out on the spot.

Once the initial shock subsided, he felt a secret glimmer of relief. At least this offered a chance to wash away his humiliation; otherwise, his miserable score of ten-something points would be nailed to the sect's pillar of shame for all eternity.

The only issue was that the gap between the two exams was far too short. Honest, conventional reviewing simply wouldn't be fast enough.

What's more, Lord Immortal Venerable's exam featured brand-new question types.

[I have to get my hands on the other disciples' exam papers!]

Qin Xian immediately stepped onto his flying sword and sped toward the Heavenly Clothing Workshop.

Every inner sect disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect knew that Xiang Yi of the Heavenly Clothing Workshop was a [money-grubber](# "Literal CN: string of copper coins (钱串子) — slang for someone obsessed with making money"). Not long ago, she had even attempted to buy up the exam papers of disciples who took the test to scalp them to the outer sect. Thus, aside from Qu Qianxing who was tasked with "collecting homework," her collection of exam papers was bound to be the most comprehensive.

To his astonishment, upon arriving at the Heavenly Clothing Workshop, Qin Xian discovered that a full third of the inner sect was already gathered there—and all sharing the exact same objective.

Qin Xian walked over to a familiar inner sect disciple in surprise:

—"Why are you here? Didn't you score fairly well last time?"-Qin Xian asked.

—"You call that fairly well? That was a bare pass,"-the other disciple said dismissively.-"It only looked passable because everyone else scored so dreadfully."

—"...There's still no need to push yourself this hard!"-Qin Xian exclaimed.

[If the class average gets dragged up by the rat race, wouldn't that mean all my progress counts for nothing?!]

—"I didn't want to either. But the outer sect is participating in this Grand Unified Examination too... You've heard that rumor circulating in the outer sect, haven't you?"-the disciple replied.

—"The rumor about heavenly secrets hidden in the exam papers?"-Qin Xian scoffed.-"You don't actually believe that, do you? We all sat for the exam ourselves; we know it contains nothing more than the most elementary knowledge that any Qi Condensation cultivator could master."

—"That's precisely why I'm worried!"-the disciple sighed anxiously.-"Because the questions are so elementary, even outer sect disciples can solve them! Outlandish rumors aside, the fact that one of them broke through to Foundation Establishment because of it is rock-solid truth. If a few more outer sect disciples like that pop up... no, even if their average score is merely comparable to ours, how do you think Lord Immortal Venerable will view us in the inner sect?"

Hearing this, Qin Xian's face instantly turned pale.

As inner sect disciples, none of them wanted to lose face in front of the outer sect they had always looked down upon.

Evidently, Qin Xian was far from the only one thinking this way.

—"No way. How could the outer sect possibly rival us in the inner sect?"-someone chimed in.

—"Hard to say. When it comes to cultivation methods, we're certainly more knowledgeable, but miscellaneous subjects are another story,"-another disciple voiced with deep worry.-"The outer sect has fewer resources than the inner sect, so they dabble in everything—spiritual beasts, spirit fields, alchemy, talisman crafting. Who knows if their comprehensive exam scores might actually turn out higher than ours!"

—"Besides, their enthusiasm for studying has been off the charts lately! I've already had more than a dozen outer sect disciples approach me trying to buy exam papers,"-another inner sect disciple said apprehensively.

[—"Ah, me too."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

With that, a momentary silence fell over the air, and a tension even thicker than before spread among them.

Right at that moment, Xiang Yi stepped out from the Heavenly Clothing Workshop.

Immediately, someone called out to her:

—"Xiang Yi, how are the exam papers selling in the outer sect?"-an inner disciple asked.

—"Extremely well. We've copied several editions, and all of them are sold out,"-Xiang Yi answered. But noticing the unusual tension in the atmosphere, she quickly covered her bases:-"However, as you all know, the outer sect is handled by my brother. He had already gathered a batch of papers and started selling them before the Grand Unified Examination was even announced. By the time I wanted to stop him, it was already too late."

Hearing her explanation, everyone's expressions turned even grimmer.

—"Doesn't that mean the outer sect has already gotten their hands on most of the exam papers?"-a disciple groaned.

—"We're finished! Thanks to that ridiculous rumor, they started reviewing way earlier than us too!"-another added.

[—"We might actually lose to them on the exam."](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciple")

Listening to their defeatist groans, Xiang Yi curled her lips into a smile:

—"It's not as dire as all that. Look at what I have here,"-Xiang Yi said.

As she spoke, she retrieved two exam papers from her storage pouch.

The others were baffled at first, but upon seeing the scores written on them, their eyes widened in shock:

—"Could that be Second Senior Brother's..."-someone gasped.

—"Bingo! Through my connection with Junior Brother Mo, I secured Second Senior Brother's exam paper! And this was never sold to the outer sect!"-Xiang Yi said proudly, producing a stack of books from her storage pouch with a rustling flourish.-"Not just his, either—I have papers from several other senior brothers, complete with their summarized study notes and curated practice questions—"

—"You certainly know how to make a profit,"-Qin Xian couldn't help but remark.

Xiang Yi tilted her chin up:

—"Just tell me: are you buying or not?"-Xiang Yi challenged.

The inner sect disciples present exchanged glances, then declared with resolute conviction:

[—"We'll buy!"](# "Speaker: Inner Sect Disciples")

Even if only to preserve the pride and face of the inner sect, they had no choice but to buy!
