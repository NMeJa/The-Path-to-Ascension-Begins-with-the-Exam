# Chapter 103: [Simple](<# "Literal CN: 简单 (jiǎndān) — simple / straightforward; chapter title">) (第103章 简单)

—"Master, Mo Bei should have arrived in Fengqing City by now, right?"-Mo Xi asked suddenly.

The little black lamb was curled up on Zhou Hui's lap licking its fleece, but rolled its eyes dramatically upon hearing this.

That girl had only left the Heavenly Spirit Sect two days ago, and she had marched off accompanied by an entire army!

Anyone who didn't know better would think she was heading out to wage war!

[How come I never noticed this little brat clinging to his older sister so much before?!]

Yet Zhou Hui happened to be thinking about the exact same thing. Standing up, he said:
—"Indeed, just about."

The little black lamb instantly tumbled from Zhou Hui's lap down to the floorboards, bleating twice in pitiful protest:
[—"Baa... baa..."](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Little Black Lamb)">)

Nobody paid it any mind.

Zhou Hui led Mo Xi over to the long desk in his study to inspect the situation in Fengqing City.

The timing was impeccable; Mo Bei was currently circling high above Fengqing City at the head of her glowing army.

—"Why is she flying in circles up there?"-Zhou Hui frowned, murmuring with concern.-"Is there some problem down below?"

In truth, he could have sent Mo Bei there directly via instant teleportation. However, traveling in such a manner hardly felt like venturing away from home at all, and it would easily foster a dependent mindset in Mo Bei, making her believe that 'Master could appear at any given moment.'

After thorough consideration, Zhou Hui had ultimately decided to have Mo Bei travel through the teleportation array connecting Baiyujing to Fengqing City.

—As a side note, after extensive research, Zhou Hui had finally deduced why the medium-sized teleportation arrays within the Central Domain had remained inoperable, and why their prior investigations centuries ago had yielded no results.

In ancient times, the small and medium-sized teleportation arrays within the Central Domain differed from the cross-domain grand teleportation arrays; they had been centrally controlled by Baiyujing. Their relationship was essentially identical to that between [cell phones and a base station](<# "Literal CN: 手机和基站 (shǒujī hé jīzhàn) — modern telecommunications analogy used by Zhou Hui">). When the base station (Baiyujing) completely vanished, no matter how pristine a cell phone cultivators dug up, none of them could ever figure out how to make a phone call with it.

However, once Zhou Hui secured control over Baiyujing and repaired the 'base station,' many of the medium teleportation arrays could be put back into service as soon as their localized repairs were finished.

The teleportation array in Fengqing City was one of them.

It had been preserved in remarkably good condition. Thus, once Zhou Hui re-entered Fengqing City into Baiyujing's Central Domain cartography, the teleportation array [regained its signal (struck through) was activated](<# "Literal CN: 恢复了信号（X）被激活了 — playful internet/modern joke by the author striking out 'signal restored' for 'activated'">).

The only caveat was that the distance was somewhat far.

After all, there was no reason to build a [high-speed rail terminal (struck through)](<# "Literal CN: 高铁站（X） — modern urban transit analogy; struck out in original CN text">) right in the dead center of downtown.

Prior to the War of Gods and Demons, Fengqing City had served as one of the Central Domain's premier transit hubs, ranking among the foremost immortal megacities alongside modern-day Cloud City. Countless cultivators had resided year-round in its bustling urban core. Consequently, both the medium-sized teleportation array leading to Baiyujing and the cross-domain grand teleportation array leading to the Southern Realm had originally been positioned along the city's outer perimeter ring.

Yet with the postwar decline of the Central Domain and the disappearance of Baiyujing, Fengqing City could no longer sustain its stature as a bustling metropolis. The medium-sized array leading to Baiyujing became utterly useless and was abandoned, while the cross-domain array continued operating, becoming the sole reason for the city's continued existence. As a result, the city's living center progressively shifted toward the Southern Realm grand teleportation array.

In recent centuries, the City Lord of Fengqing City had gone so far as to convert the ancient fortress housing the grand teleportation array into his private City Lord's Mansion. Anyone wishing to utilize the teleportation array had to obtain the City Lord's permission, effectively grasping the city's economic lifeline in his own hands.

As for the medium teleportation array once connecting to Baiyujing, it was forgotten in the barren wilderness, swallowed by overgrown weeds.

Reaching Fengqing City from that site required roughly half a day's flight.

In other words, Mo Bei had spent a full day riding the Kunpeng to Baiyujing, summoned her army from Baiyujing, traveled from `Teleportation Array · Baiyujing Station` to `Teleportation Array · Fengqing City (Old) Station`, set off from the old terminal, flown for half a day, and finally reached Fengqing City.

After running through this entire journey, it certainly gave one the authentic sensation of traveling far from home.

Well, provided one overlooked the elderly father back home keeping watch via real-time [surveillance monitors](<# "Literal CN: 监控 (jiānkòng) — modern surveillance camera analogy">), ready to intervene at a moment's notice.

Zhou Hui did not consider himself overprotective. In his eyes, keeping a close eye on disciples while they were still in their cub stage was only natural—Mo Bei and Mo Xi were genuinely only in their teens; even from a mortal perspective, they were literal children.

Right now, seeing Mo Bei's petite frame mounted atop that towering divine steed made him somewhat anxious:
—"Could it be that she doesn't know how to land a flying horse?"

—"No, Master, I was the one who told her to make an extra couple of laps above Fengqing City! That way, we notify everyone that a direct disciple of our Heavenly Spirit Sect has arrived, putting anyone thinking of stirring up trouble on their best behavior,"-Mo Xi confessed in a hurry, before hesitating nervously.-"...Did I overstep?"

No wonder Mo Xi had pulled Mo Bei aside and talked her ear off before she departed; so he had been acting as her [useless armchair adviser](<# "Literal CN: 狗头军师 (gǒutóu jūnshī) — dog-headed strategist; someone who offers unsolicited, questionable, or crafty tactical advice">)!

—"It is a sound idea,"-Zhou Hui smiled.

Even so, Zhou Hui's gaze continued sweeping through the city below.

Mo Xi noticed this with sharp perception and asked curiously:
—"Master, is there something amiss in the city?"-He followed his master's gaze.

The holographic 'model' of Fengqing City faithfully mirrored the real-time conditions down in the streets.

One could see the luminous army hovering above the city under Mo Bei's lead, while a city full of miniature figurines stood in the thoroughfares, pointing upward and chattering away. At first glance, nothing seemed out of the ordinary.

Mo Xi had initially assumed Zhou Hui was worried Mo Bei might struggle to find a good lodging spot. But looking closer, he realized his master's eyes were neither fixed on the branch of 'A Cup of Sorrow' Mo Bei planned to visit, nor on the potentially troublesome City Lord's Mansion, but rather on an utterly nondescript street.

Mo Xi inspected the street carefully and still couldn't find anything amiss... Hm?

—"That feathered woman is so strange!"-Mo Xi pointed at one of the teal figurines.

Inspected closely, she was rather pretty, but scaled down to the level of tiny pixels, facial beauty hardly stood out.

Naturally, what caught Mo Xi's eye was not her face, but her shadow. Trailing behind this teal-clad woman was a colossal silhouette resembling... an enormous tree?

—"Has she been possessed by some bizarre creature?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"That is a [wood spirit](<# "Literal CN: 木灵 (mùlíng) — an elemental arboreal spirit or nature deity residing within an ancient plant">),"-Zhou Hui replied, also studying the teal figurine.-"And judging by her physical traits, that young lady ought to belong to the Azure Phoenix Clan. They roost upon the largest divine 'tree' in the Southern Realm, serving as priests who worship and tend to it. That wood spirit is likely a detached portion of the divine tree itself—possessing a cultivation at least at the Void Refinement stage."

—"Oh, 'only' Void Refinement!"-Mo Xi scoffed dismissively.

It couldn't be helped. His master was a Tribulation Transcendence supreme, and the experts constantly bustling in and out of the Palace of Silence at Mount Tianling lately were Body Integration and Mahayana powerhouses. To Mo Xi's eyes, Void Refinement truly didn't seem like much—his Eldest Senior Brother was already at the late Soul Formation stage, practically knocking on the door of Void Refinement!

The little black lamb: *...Achoo!*

Seeing his youngest disciple act so blasé, Zhou Hui smiled wryly:
—"A Void Refinement cultivation is by no means low. Though it belongs to the middle three realms, stepping into Void Refinement is essentially securing a ticket to the upper three realms—it marks one's genuine entry into the [academic community](<# "Literal CN: 学术界 (xuéshùjiè) — academic academia; Zhou Hui's scholarly metaphor for high-tier cultivation circles">)."

—"My apologies, Master,"-Mo Xi replied, appearing humble on the surface.

Zhou Hui knew Mo Xi hadn't absorbed a word of it.

The boy was simply too talented to comprehend the plight of ordinary cultivators. However, having now fused with the spirit stone mine essence, achieving Golden Core and thoroughly refining it would not be nearly as effortless as before. Once he hit a bottleneck, he would finally understand the hardships of cultivation.

Zhou Hui smiled inwardly, eagerly awaiting the day his disciple ran into an academic roadblock.

For now, he patiently shared some obscure 'common knowledge' with his disciple:
—"Moreover, plants are entirely unlike humans. Flora enjoy extraordinarily long lifespans, meaning their speed of cultivation is correspondingly far slower than that of humans. It is entirely standard for a plant to take several centuries just to develop sentience and establish a Foundation. Reaching the Void Refinement stage generally means an ancient tree has attained spiritual sentience—it has lived for at least ten thousand years."

—"Whoa, then wasn't it already around before the War of Gods and Demons?"

—"Indeed. There are several existences in the Southern Realm that personally witnessed the War of Gods and Demons,"-Zhou Hui nodded.-"Furthermore, their combat prowess cannot be measured against human standards. When we say it has a 'Void Refinement cultivation,' that only applies when it has departed from its primary trunk to roam on its own. If you were within the domain of its true body, even a True Immortal might not necessarily best it."

At this point, Zhou Hui frowned as he gazed at the teal-clad maiden:
—"Conversely, any young woman capable of escorting such a wood spirit away from the divine tree must be at least of Holy Maiden rank within the Azure Phoenix Clan."

—"A Holy Maiden!"-This time, Mo Xi's eyes widened.

Telling him about "Void Refinement elders" or "beings even True Immortals cannot defeat" elicited nothing but a shrug—after all, could anyone be more formidable than his own master? But "Holy Maiden" was an entirely different story.

Mo Xi managed the Heavenly Spirit Sect's procurement, and he naturally loved collecting commercial intelligence. As such, he maintained a keen awareness of external affairs, particularly regarding the Southern Realm, which served as the Central Domain's primary supplier of goods.

He knew that although the Southern Realm also boasted a Tribulation Transcendence supreme, that powerhouse belonged to the academic (secluded cultivation) faction just as his master once had, rarely stepping out to meddle in secular affairs—and apparently shared a rather cordial friendship with Master.

Furthermore, demonic clans held dominant sway in the Southern Realm. The true rulers of the realm were the faction of the Sacred Tree of Fire Abyss situated in the southwestern corner—namely, the beast clans roosting upon that immense divine tree.

Among them, the Holy Maiden of the Azure Phoenix Clan held an exceptionally prestigious position, roughly equivalent to an imperial princess of a mortal dynasty.

[If I can foster good relations with her, wouldn't I be able to secure the exclusive supply chain for Southern Realm goods?!]
Mo Xi thought with mounting excitement.

After all, the only active cross-domain teleportation array in the Central Domain connected to the Southern Realm, and the resources the Central Domain relied on originated predominantly from the south. Yet because demonic clans were notoriously inept at commerce, trade routes had been firmly monopolized by the Palace of Carefree Wandering from the Eastern Domain, who had severed the lifeline on a whim just last year.

Although Carefree Palace had behaved itself admirably of late and refrained from causing trouble, that did not stop Mo Xi from plotting to seize the Southern Realm's supply chain, [cut out the loathsome middleman](<# "Literal CN: 踢掉中间商 (tīdiào zhōngjiānshāng) — cutting out the middleman broker">), and install himself as the prime distributor instead.

The gaze Mo Xi directed at the teal figurine instantly turned burning hot:
—"Why has she come to the Central Domain?"
[How can I get close to her?]

—"Indeed, why has she come to the Central Domain?"-Zhou Hui echoed with equal curiosity.

Zhou Hui himself didn't harbor many commercial ambitions.

However, lately, whenever he spotted any particularly eye-catching existence, he would subconsciously run their name through the 'Heavenly Dao script' to check whether they were tied to the plot.

And lo and behold, there she was.

In the original 'story,' the Southern Realm was not a central stage, serving merely as a transitional arc.

According to the script, the protagonist had accompanied the red rose maiden to attend Carefree Palace's Treasure Fair in the Eastern Domain. Upon learning that Mo Xi and Mo Bei had once been sighted near the Southern Realm, he rushed over. Unbeknownst to him, the moment he entered the Southern Realm, he was ambushed by demonic beasts, leaving the red rose maiden comatose. The protagonist was forced to journey to the Sacred Tree of Fire Abyss to beg for medicine, which led to his encounter with Shui Huang'er, Holy Maiden of the Azure Phoenix Clan.

Judging by the story's trajectory, this Shui Huang'er had originally been conceived in the 'author's' mind as one of the protagonist's future harem members.

Massive paragraphs in the text had been lavished on close-up descriptions of Shui Huang'er's appearance (predominantly her bust) and her warm, vivacious mannerisms, with the romantic subplot steering steadily into ambiguous flirtation, right up until... until Season 3, Episode 5, Minute 17 of the TV drama adaptation.

Yes, specifying the exact episode and minute was necessary because the turning point had been that jarringly abrupt.

One second, Shui Huang'er was gazing at him with eyes brimming with tender affection, seemingly willing to part even with her clan's sacred treasure without hesitation; the very next second, it was as if Shui Huang'er abruptly realized she was romantically entangling herself with a [slimy garden slug](<# "Literal CN: 鼻涕虫 (bìtìchóng) — slug; colloquial insult for someone spineless, slimy, or repulsive">), whose disgusting mucus had soiled her pristine feathers. She immediately looked like she wanted nothing more than to stomp this slug into paste and scrape its dried innards off the floor.

Oh, right—Zhou Hui had watched the TV drama adaptation for this particular arc.

The actress portraying Shui Huang'er had been unexpectedly committed to her craft, rendering that sudden facial metamorphosis burned into his memory.

Back then, he had only recently started his job at his public institution and had watched the show over lunch with several colleagues. Thus, when reconstructing his memories, he could even recall his colleagues furiously cursing the screenwriters, ranting that adaptations shouldn't faithfully recreate the novel's blunders in moments like this, and insisting that the original author had definitely hired a [ghostwriter](<# "Literal CN: 枪手 (qiāngshǒu) — gunman; internet slang for ghostwriter or substitute test-taker">) here, which caused the story's entire tonal style to derail.

Regardless of how disgruntled they had been, it did not alter the reality that Shui Huang'er had abruptly morphed from a harem member into a minor boss.

And because Shui Huang'er's tonal style had drastically shifted, the overarching plot inevitably suffered a ripple effect.

In short, the protagonist was ultimately unable to acquire the medicine to save the red rose maiden through Shui Huang'er. He was forced to risk infiltrating the grove to steal the medicine, where he ran into Mo Xi, who had arrived seeking a demonic-monster coalition. The two fought a catastrophic battle that ignited the Sacred Tree of Fire Abyss. Consequently, the beast clans refused to ally with either human or demon factions, and the sacred treasure originally slated for the protagonist vanished without a trace. Yet in a stroke of fortune disguised as disaster, the Southern Realm had managed to stay out of the final apocalyptic war, becoming the continent least ravaged by the protagonist and demonic scourges.

—"Looking at it now, this plotline seems rather intriguing,"-Zhou Hui mused.

As a piece of fiction, the jarring twist made zero sense. But from the perspective of the Heavenly Dao script, Shui Huang'er's abrupt awakening warranted thorough scrutiny.

Although Zhou Hui could not confirm whether the Azure Phoenix member currently in Fengqing City was indeed the Shui Huang'er of the script, she was unquestionably worth making contact with.

With this in mind, Zhou Hui took another close look at the teal figurine in Fengqing City.

And right then, he watched as this little figurine hurried forward in a [mutual sprint](<# "Literal CN: 双向奔赴 (shuāngxiàng bēnfù) — running toward each other; mutual pursuit">) straight toward Mo Bei.

At this moment, she truly felt the sensation Elder Tong Ling had spoken of—that 'both the city and its people are dying.'

Living things and inanimate objects were fundamentally different.

The dwelling of the phoenix clan was alive—a divine tree—so even after tens of thousands of years, it only grew grander and more flourishing. But human dwellings were inanimate; no matter how impervious the masonry, none could withstand the relentless erosion of time.

What if it did?

Ah, but prior to that, she should observe the people and sects of the Central Domain first. Based on their actual state, she could decide whether to act alone or seek cooperation with the Central Domain.

Shui Huang'er made up her mind.

Because she laid eyes on the crowd inside the tournament venue.

The female cultivators in the private box grew frantic:
[—"This tournament never banned magical treasures in the first place!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivators">)

[—"Even if she used a treasure bestowed by the Immortal Venerable, so what?!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivators">)

[—"She didn't cheat!"](<# "Speaker: Female Cultivators">)

It seemed that even they didn't quite believe Mo Bei had relied purely on her own sword technique.

[Did she cheat?] Shui Huang'er asked internally.

—"How bizarre. That was pure swordsmanship... wasn't it?"-Elder Tong Ling murmured uncertainly.-"How could a yin spirit be so proficient in swordsmanship? She didn't even employ a single spell."

—"Oh?"-Shui Huang'er hummed softly, turning an intrigued gaze back toward Mo Bei.

Amidst such a hostile roar, an ordinary girl would have been reduced to tears, or at least found it utterly intolerable. Yet the exquisitely beautiful girl on the platform remained completely impassive, as though looking down upon a pond full of croaking bullfrogs.

With a frosty, unbothered expression, she said simply:
—"This is far too inefficient. Come at me all at once!"

With those words, the girl raised her hand. Amidst a wave of gasps, the protective barrier surrounding the arena vanished.

This restriction had been personally crafted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Installed upon the arenas of the Immortal Arts Assembly, it restricted the number of contestants and enforced strict one-on-one combat. Even in lawless Fengqing City, not a single soul had been able to breach this ward.

Yet by actively dispelling the restriction, Mo Bei had stripped away the defensive shield, stepping willingly past the mesh into the beasts' feeding enclosure.

Seeing this, even the employee of A Cup of Sorrow panicked:
—"How could Junior Sister Mo do such a thing—?!"

Even as she cried out, dozens of figures leaped onto the arena stage. Even beyond those, more cultivators charged down from the stands, among whom was actually a Golden Core expert:
—"Stupid bitch, you brought this on yourself—ARGH!"

This time, the crowd finally saw how Mo Bei struck.

A long, supple flexible sword rippled like flowing water through the air, glinting with boundless rainbow light. Yet wherever that rainbow light passed, a blooming mist of blood erupted. That Golden Core cultivator was instantly sliced into seven clean segments.

Witnessing this gruesome sight, the cultivators rushing toward the ring paused dead in their tracks.

Yet Mo Bei's second and third sword strikes had already whipped forth, reducing the entire ring of cultivators crowding the platform into bloody chunks.

[—"M-Murder! She's slaughtering people!"](<# "Speaker: Panicking Cultivator">)

Someone shrieked that utterly moronic platitude. Several scoundrels who had intended to exploit the chaos and overwhelm her with numbers immediately regretted their decision, spinning on their heels to flee.

Evidently, they were a step too late.

Her fourth and fifth sword strikes slashed across the air, mincing the fleeing cowards into ribbons while sweeping cleanly through the spectator stands, harvesting the heads of every scoundrel whose mouths had spewed the filthiest insults just moments before.

Geysers of blood erupted from severed necks like crimson fountains.

Upon the young maiden's exquisite, porcelain face, there was still no expression whatsoever, as if she had merely harvested a basket of snow-glow grass.

Casting a frigid gaze over the petrified crowd, she spoke:
—"I despise racking my brains the most."
—"And since I despise it, let's keep things [simple and direct](<# "Literal CN: 简单直接 (jiǎndān zhíjiē) — title drop: simple / brutally straightforward">)."
—"You claim I am cheating. Indeed, I am cheating."
—"This flexible sword was forged specially for me by my Master. It [weakens against the weak and strengthens against the strong](<# "Literal CN: 遇弱则弱，越强则强 (yù ruò zé ruò, yuè qiáng zé qiáng) — the flexible sword's auto-scaling enchantment: it matches and drags all adversaries down to Mo Bei's realm">), dragging all of my opponents down to the exact same cultivation realm as mine."
—"Therefore, anyone who possesses the confidence to sever this blade, anyone who possesses the confidence to best me in pure swordsmanship—step right up!"
—"Outsiders, demonkin, degenerate gamblers, match-fixers, and all the rest of you who scheme to subvert this tournament by underhanded means—you are all welcome to challenge me!"
—"The moment you defeat me, you earn the right to enter the Immortal Arts Assembly!"

As Mo Bei declared this, she whipped her flexible sword into the empty air for a sixth strike.

A resonant, piercing sword hum rang out above the arena, mingling with the stifling stench of fresh blood, sending a bone-chilling shiver down the spines of everyone present.

Even the female cultivators were rendered utterly speechless.

Only Shui Huang'er eagerly retrieved the fireworks they had handed her earlier, asking with sparkling eyes:
—"What did you say earlier about the rhythm of cheering? What should we do now?"
