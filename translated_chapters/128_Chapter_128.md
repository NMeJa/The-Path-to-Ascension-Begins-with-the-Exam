# Chapter 128: The Ascension Stairway (第128章 升仙梯)

Rewind to several months prior.

Zhou Hui walked steadily along the Ascension Stairway.

At this moment, however, the Ascension Stairway could hardly be considered fully repaired—nor was it something that could be mended so easily.

Unlike the White Jade Capital, which had slumbered intact beneath the earth with all its core mechanisms preserved, merely starved of energy, this Ascension Stairway had suffered a far harsher fate. Whether severely damaged during the ancient Demon-God War, steadily dismantled and leeched of power by Carefree Palace across uncounted years, or a toxic blend of both, by the time Zhou Hui and his disciples uncovered it, it had been smashed to smithereens. A fragment here, a section there—one could scavenge a flight of stairs out of virtually any random room.

Consequently, what Zhou Hui was undertaking could scarcely be called 'repairing'; it was closer to an outright reconstruction.

Once Ran Deng refined Carefree Palace's life plaque with her Soul Flame, Zhou Hui had her transfer control of the Ascension Stairway over to him.

Afterward, Zhou Hui proceeded to the lowest level of the research facility and began ascending the flight of stairs.

With every step he took, the stair beneath his tread reverted to its original form, gleaming with a translucent, lustrous white like pristine jade.

Before long, Zhou Hui had traversed four thousand, four hundred and forty-four steps.

Yet this was merely the beginning.

In truth, those four thousand, four hundred and forty-four steps were merely the longest surviving contiguous segment deep beneath the Abyss Sea, repurposed by Carefree Palace as the 'main thoroughfare' of their experimental facility.

Such a modest altitude amounted at most to scaling roughly two-thirds of Mount Tai. Far from reaching the heavens from the abyssal ocean trenches, even had it stood proudly upon dry surface ground, it wouldn't have come close to touching the sky.

Thus, Zhou Hui did not halt his pace.

Standing on the uppermost tier of the surviving staircase, he raised his foot and stepped into empty air—

*Tap.*

The next moment, Zhou Hui's foot found solid ground.

A brand-new step of white jade instantly coalesced and crystallized beneath his soles, condensing into solid matter.

Zhou Hui continued upward.

The second step, the third step... the thirteenth step.

Just as Zhou Hui placed his weight upon this thirteenth 'newborn' stair, the distant sound of masonry crumbling echoed from a remote corner of the facility.

With the segments of the Ascension Stairway that had served as load-bearing pillars 'returning' to their rightful positions, the [unpermitted construction](# "Literal CN: 违规建筑 (wéiguī jiànzhù) — illegal / unauthorized building structures violating municipal codes") slapped together by Carefree Palace had begun to collapse.

There was no need to worry about the living souls inside.

The precise instant that structure caved in, dozens of children abruptly materialized onto the chairs and benches of a schoolhouse within the 'scale model' of the White Jade Capital.

Ah Huang stood in the courtyard clutching a broom, blinking in utter bewilderment as he looked around.

—"Are these the 'new disciples' Master mentioned?"-Mo Xi stood before a low table, peering into the model of the White Jade Capital. Watching the children inside descend into chaotic frenzy, a wicked smirk crept across his face.-"They look like they're sorely lacking in discipline."

Lu Shulong sniffed the air attentively.

—"No rush. Their state is somewhat irregular; they'll need a course of medicinal pills—like the ones we administered to you and Youngest Junior Sister back then."

—"What's that supposed to mean?"-Mo Xi complained, disgruntled.-"How are we anything like them?"

—"Indeed, quite different. Compared to our Youngest Junior Brother, the [remarkably pure malice](# "Literal CN: 恶意看起来比较清澈 (è'yì kàn qǐlái bǐjiào qīngchè) — a play on the viral meme 'clear and innocent stupidity' (清澈的愚蠢) describing naive university students, flipped here to unadulterated childish mischief") in these little brats looks wholesome—possessing an unpolluted beauty. *Jiejie*,"-Guan Ye chuckled.

Mo Xi dearly wanted to claw the faces off both of these bastard senior brothers.

On the whole, however, with Lu Shulong and Mo Xi keeping watch over these 'experimental subjects' for the time being, nothing could possibly go awry.

Zhou Hui, meanwhile, pressed on with his 'restoration.'

He advanced step by step. As the number of tiers multiplied, the Ascension Stairway soared higher and higher. By the time Carefree Palace's entire testing ground had completely disintegrated, the stairway had regained a portion of its self-repairing function. Even without physical raw materials, as long as Zhou Hui lifted his foot upward, a new step would spontaneously coalesce.

Truth be told, for Zhou Hui, this restoration was a rather tedious affair.

He had found the initial ascent through the Abyss Sea somewhat 'amusing'—there were demonic beasts drawn by the commotion, along with spiritual energy storms, providing occasional 'little pleasant surprises' during his stair-climbing workout.

Yet the higher the steps climbed, the fewer these 'pleasant surprises' became.

And so Zhou Hui plodded upward in dull monotony.

At last, several months later, when he reached the sixty-six thousand, six hundred and sixty-sixth step, he sensed the providential fate between heaven and earth surging violently once more—surpassing even the grand upheaval in the Southern Domain.

Such a ferocious surge of providential fate triggered tremors throughout the Ascension Stairway, sending sympathetic vibrations rolling across the entirety of the Five Domains and Six Abysses.

The tremors were so violent and abrupt that they threatened to shatter the newly formed Ascension Stairway into dust.

Zhou Hui, however, merely let out a faint chuckle.

Standing firmly upon the stairway, he commanded:

—"Halt!"

Instantly, a silvery radiance rippled downward along the engraved patterns flanking both sides of the jade steps, and the tremors ceased at once.

Leaving everything else aside, Zhou Hui looked anxiously toward the two disciples trailing behind him on the stairs:

—"Ah-Ran... are you alright?"

—"N-No, I'm fine,"-Ran Deng answered, sitting upon a step.

From beneath her elbow, the little black lamb emitted protesting squeaks of *ying-ying*.

Ran Deng scrambled to push herself upright.

—"I'm sorry... oof!"

Losing her footing in an instant, she collapsed right back down, using the little black lamb entirely as a floor cushion.

Ran Deng couldn't help it.

The flame between her brows flickered unsteadily, while the palace lantern hovered beside her.

Though she had already refined the life plaque, refining the palace lantern was no speedy process. Across the next nine thousand-odd days, Ran Deng would remain subject to this lingering condition—her spirit exceptionally irritable, her physical strength exceptionally frail.

That was why she had tripped upon the steps when the tremors struck just now, squashing the little black lamb beneath her.

The little black lamb let out a loud bleat of *ying* and shouted:

—"You're just doing this to get back at me!"

Having already been exposed, he naturally had zero interest in continuing the charade.

Besides, if he kept playing the mute lamb, he would only get bullied!

Alas, when it came to his junior sister, Master would forever [play favorites](# "Literal CN: 拉偏架 (lā piān jià) — pretending to break up a fight while physically restraining only one party to let the other land free hits").

Hearing the little black lamb's words, Zhou Hui arched an eyebrow.

—"Retaliating? What did you do to make Ah-Ran want to get back at you?"

—"Is that really the point here?"-the little black lamb asked aggrievedly.

—"Nothing of the sort, Master. Just now, Eldest Senior Brother and I were merely reminiscing about the time you first told us about the Ascension Stairway,"-Ran Deng said with a chuckle.-"The time Eldest Senior Brother sheared off my hair."

—"Oh!"-Zhou Hui recalled the incident as well.

His expression as he looked down at the little black lamb was entirely devoid of sympathy.

[*Ying-ying*, Master truly is biased!]

Tears went *patter-patter* down the little black lamb's cheeks.

Seeing this, Ran Deng briefly forgot that this was her Eldest Senior Brother; she reached out, intending to pat the little black lamb's head. To her surprise, the lamb haughtily twitched his head aside, dodging her hand, and trotted over to Master's feet. Tilting his snout up, he let his tear-streaked face be displayed in full for Master to behold.

This behavior was unmistakably Eldest Senior Brother, yet why did he seem noticeably stupider than before?

The corners of Ran Deng's mouth twitched. She decided not to lower herself to squabbling with a sheep.

—"What happened just now, Master?"-Ran Deng asked.-"An earthquake?"

—"We touched upon the Heavenly Dao,"-Zhou Hui replied.

[—"The Heavenly Dao?"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

—"Indeed,"-Zhou Hui explained in greater detail.-"The original Ascension Stairway collapsed and crashed long ago. One cannot simply construct something similar and call it a day, not even if one uses the original materials. For once the Ascension Stairway reaches a certain altitude, it will touch upon the Heavenly Dao once more, undergoing the Heavenly Dao's ordeal."

This held true not just for the Ascension Stairway, but for other architectural wonders as well.

It was said that when the White Jade Capital was completed, thunder roared for nine hundred and ninety-nine days; and when Sky City was erected in those bygone days, violent gales raged for three whole months.

Yet once such a 'grand wonder' withstood the trial of the Heavenly Dao, it would be bestowed with the blessing of a portion of heaven and earth's cosmic power.

Zhou Hui had no knowledge of what boon the White Jade Capital had received back then, but following Sky City's completion, the world's spiritual energy—which had once centered upon the Central Domain but was thoroughly scrambled by the Demon-God War—surged toward the Northern Domain. Consequently, the Northern Domain became the new premier gathering realm for immortal cultivators.

This was precisely why, even while fully aware that [mega-wonders bankrupt empires](# "Literal CN: 奇观误国 (qíguān wù guó) — 'wonder-building ruins nations', a classic historical adage and Civilization 4X gaming meme where vanity monuments exhaust a state's coffers"), factions always rushed to erect mega-wonders the moment they grew sufficiently powerful.

—"Ah, that sounds almost like a cultivator undergoing a heavenly tribulation!"-Ran Deng remarked.-"So what is the Ascension Stairway's blessing? Does it lead to the Immortal Realm?"

—"No. Leading to the Immortal Realm was merely its original, inherent function; that is not something a cosmic blessing from heaven and earth can bestow."-And besides, where this present Ascension Stairway would ultimately lead was still anyone's guess!

Zhou Hui cast a glance upward along the stairs.

Following the tremors, the 'restoration' of the Ascension Stairway had accelerated.

At the outset, Zhou Hui had utilized the residual materials left by Carefree Palace to repair existing sections;

Next, Zhou Hui had personally forged the parts the stairway lacked;

Now, in the wake of the tremors, without Zhou Hui needing to take another step upward, the Ascension Stairway had begun automatically generating its final stretch.

—"Regardless of where it leads, however, its existence has been acknowledged, and it has received its blessing,"-Zhou Hui said.-"That blessing is likely: 'visible to all living beings across the Five Domains and Six Abysses'!"

Thus, after the tremors, Zhou Hui could sense countless gazes falling upon the Ascension Stairway.

—"This was likely an inherent attribute of the Ascension Stairway originally,"-Zhou Hui sighed with emotion.

For the Ascension Stairway was meant to be egalitarian.

Regardless of high or low station, regardless of supreme or wretched talent, regardless of profound or shallow scholarship—if one possessed the courage to seek knowledge, one deserved the right to attempt the trial.

Such an Ascension Stairway could never be monopolized by a privileged few; it had to be visible to every living creature across the Five Domains and Six Abysses. Only then would they know where to turn when they yearned to ascend.

—"...Master, are you certain these were the rules of the 'original Ascension Stairway'?"-Yuwen Xuan chimed in at this moment.

Zhou Hui looked down to find his eldest disciple staring at him with a peculiar gaze—though conveying such an expression on a foolish sheep's face was no easy feat.

—"Why do you ask?"-Zhou Hui countered curiously.-"If not the original rules, whose rules would they be?"

—"Doesn't that depend on what lies at the summit of the Ascension Stairway?"-the little black lamb said.

Lifting his head, he peered upward:

—"In the past, the Ascension Stairway led to the Immortal Realm, so the True Immortals dictated its rules. The rules today depend upon where the stairway leads—Master, where do you think it leads?"

—"I never presuppose an end goal,"-Zhou Hui answered.

He appraised his eldest disciple.

In truth, he would have preferred to thoroughly investigate his disciple's bizarre condition right now, but this was hardly the opportune moment.

Zhou Hui offered his perspective for the time being:

—"The pursuit of knowledge is not about presupposing an answer and demanding you attain that exact conclusion—that only constricts your future. Rather, the 'answer' simply exists out there; as long as you walk the path, you are bound to encounter it."

Saying so, Zhou Hui cast a glance toward the distance:

—"For instance, our time is running short right now, so let us quicken our pace!"

—"Let us see what awaits at the very summit of this present Ascension Stairway."

Zhou Hui turned around and resumed his ascent up the stairway.

Ran Deng and the little black lamb peered in the direction Zhou Hui had gazed, but spotted nothing. Exchanging a bewildered glance, they hurriedly followed behind Master just as before.

***

Yet in the direction of Zhou Hui's gaze, a cultivator with the appearance of an exquisite woman was riding upon iridescent auspicious clouds, flying at breakneck speed toward the Ascension Stairway.

Behind this female cultivator trailed another figure in hot pursuit:

—"Lady Goddess, Lady Goddess Renran, please wait for me! It's merely an Ascension Stairway, there's truly no need for such haste! Our Carefree Palace has prepared a top-tier banquet in your honor—"

Indeed, this female cultivator rocketing across the sky was none other than the sole divine deity of this world:

Goddess Renran.

And the one pursuing the goddess was naturally no ordinary mortal either, but a fellow Tribulation Transcendence cultivator.

Looking closely, although that cultivator wore a face full of anxiety, seemingly desperate to invite Goddess Renran back, his position remained scrupulously pegged at a distance of a hundred li from her. When the goddess sped up, he sped up; when the goddess slowed down, he slowed down. He resembled nothing so much as a buzzing fly, utterly lacking the imposing grandeur expected of a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse.

Then again, this man had parted ways with his dignity long ago—ever since Zhou Hui had chased him across the world like a frightened rabbit for several straight years.

Eventually, his incessant chatter pushed even the seemingly gentle goddess past her limits.

—"Sikong Yao, what is the meaning of this? You wouldn't happen to be trying to interfere with me and keep me from reaching the Ascension Stairway, would you?"

—"How would I ever dare?"-Sikong Yao retreated several meters mid-air.-"It's just, why must Your Divinity insist on going to the Ascension Stairway?"

—"Indeed, Your Ladyship Goddess, what purpose draws you to the Ascension Stairway?"-another voice queried.

Accompanying this voice, several more Tribulation Transcendence cultivators drifted out from the sea of clouds.

They were none other than the Mei-Zhu Couple, Patriarch Jiuzhai, and an unfamiliar Tribulation Transcendence cultivator.

With this, the majority of the Tribulation Transcendence cultivators in this entire realm were gathered here.

Taking in the scene, Goddess Renran let out a cold laugh.

—"From the looks of your battle formation, you intend to shield Untiring while he seizes the Ascension Stairway!"

—"Now that is an unfair characterization,"-Patriarch Jiuzhai said, wagging his head.-"You know perfectly well that the Ascension Stairway was discovered by Untiring, and it was he who returned it to its rightful place. How could you accuse him of 'seizing' it?"

—"Discovered by Untiring?"-Goddess Renran sneered, glancing toward Sikong Yao of Carefree Palace.

Sikong Yao averted his eyes, putting on an innocent show of complete ignorance.

Nor should he have known.

Sikong Yao was by no means a core pillar groomed with care by Carefree Palace. It was merely that his ancestors had belonged to Carefree Palace, placing him in their camp from birth. Having excelled at fleeing since childhood, he had survived all manner of bizarre rivalries while securing his own lucky opportunities. In the end, he broke through far too rapidly—before Carefree Palace's upper echelon even had time to rope him into their inner circle, he had already reached Tribulation Transcendence.

Carefree Palace didn't dare entrust someone like him with core duties. Thus, despite technically being 'one of their own,' Sikong Yao managed to live the life of an externally hired elder. Especially after being relentlessly hunted by Zhou Hui, his reputation in the cultivation world had cratered, ensuring he was sidelined even further into a glorified hired thug—or so went the official story told to outsiders.

The reality was that Sikong Yao was [slippery as a greased loach](# "Literal CN: 滑不溜丢 (huá bù liū diū) — slick, elusive, and impossible to pin down"). When it came to matters touching upon the realm's deepest secrets, such as the Ascension Stairway testing facility, he perpetually played dumb and refused to touch them with a ten-foot pole. Carefree Palace simply couldn't drag him into the muddy waters.

Yet while Sikong Yao was fast on his feet, his luck left much to be desired.

His presence here today was a prime example:

Earlier, in order to suppress the scandal surrounding the counterfeit *Spells* supplementary issue, Carefree Palace had their 'Ancestors' invite Lady Goddess to the Eastern Domain to host a Grand Preaching Assembly. Under normal circumstances, Palace Master Li himself should have personally welcomed her.

However, because this matter was an [utmost emergency](# "Literal CN: 十万火急 (shí wàn huǒ jí) — burning urgency of ten thousand troops / top-priority dispatch"), there was no time for the Palace Master to fly over leisurely while putting on airs. Yet wanting to display adequate reverence for the goddess, they dispatched Sikong Yao—who, after all, still held the rank of Tribulation Transcendence—to relay the message and escort her.

Who could have guessed that no sooner had Sikong Yao met Goddess Renran than the ground began to tremble, and when he looked up, a heavenly stairway had manifested in the sky toward the Central Domain!

The moment Sikong Yao laid eyes on that heavenly stairway, he knew almost instantly how the damn thing had appeared.

*Untiring Immortal Venerable!*

There was no need to think twice; the only person capable of kicking up such a continent-shaking racket was him!

In that split second, Sikong Yao couldn't help but nurse a deeply aggrieved suspicion that the Untiring Immortal Venerable was targeting him personally—otherwise, why was it that every single time he was tasked with watching over a venue, that man showed up to trash it?!

As for the Lady Goddess, her expression had shifted drastically the instant she saw the Ascension Stairway.

Ignoring Carefree Palace's invitation entirely, she took off toward the Ascension Stairway in extreme haste.

Sikong Yao couldn't very well abandon her, yet he lacked the guts to block her path, so he trailed along at a neither-close-nor-distant radius the entire way. Objectively, this served to drag down the goddess's travel speed—though whether he did so intentionally was truly difficult to tell.

Furthermore, that several other Tribulation Transcendence titans had arrived so quickly, intercepting Goddess Renran at the exact perfect moment, was exceptionally suspicious.

—"Hmph."

Noticing these undercurrents, Goddess Renran's face darkened.

A wave of crushing divine pressure radiated from her form, causing the surrounding Tribulation Transcendence cultivators to strain under the weight.

After all, Goddess Renran was fundamentally different from the cultivators of this realm who had never ascended. She was originally a True Immortal, possessing even a sliver of divine godhood. Thus, even having sacrificed her mortal flesh to seal the Demon Sovereign's head and becoming stranded in this world, with her cultivation dropping back down to Tribulation Transcendence—or rather, the hard cap of this realm was merely Tribulation Transcendence—the divine aura she radiated was not something lower-realm cultivators could look upon directly, even those at Tribulation Transcendence.

Only after subduing the Tribulation Transcendence cultivators present did Goddess Renran demand:

—"Back then, to bring an end to the Demon-God War, how many sacrifices and steep prices were paid! Even the living beings of your realm were weeping and begging—begging us, willing to pay the price of severing the path to immortality if it meant sealing the Demon Realm for good! Only then did we agree! And now, you turn around and seek to reconstruct the Ascension Stairway yourselves—what is the meaning of this?!"

The goddess sneered coldly:

—"Speaking of which, surely you don't believe that just because Zhou Hui restored the Ascension Stairway, you will be able to ascend?!"

This time, Goddess Renran didn't even use the title 'Untiring,' calling Zhou Hui out directly by name.

Hearing her words, the unfamiliar cultivator's brow twitched:

—"Ascension is something I can achieve on my own; I don't need—"

—"Shut up! Jian Haoge, you haven't beaten Zhou Hui a single time in your entire life—what right do you have to utter those words?!"-Goddess Renran snapped.

The cultivator named 'Jian Haoge' wore an expression of profound humiliation.

*[Don't strike someone in the face](# "Literal CN: 打人不打脸 (dǎ rén bù dǎ liǎn) — 'when hitting people, don't hit them in the face', meaning do not expose someone's most humiliating, vulnerable sore spot"), Your Ladyship Goddess!*

The other Tribulation Transcendence titans present wore expressions of utter cringing secondhand embarrassment.

Jian Haoge was the current City Lord of Sky City.

His grandfather was the architect who created Sky City, and his father was the former Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Such an illustrious pedigree had destined Jian Haoge from birth to be surrounded by reverence like [stars revolving around the moon](# "Literal CN: 众星拱月 (zhòng xīng gǒng yuè) — universally adored and centered like stars surrounding the celestial moon"). Coupled with his innate peerless aptitude and brilliant talent, his entire life by all rights should have been spent bathed in ceaseless praise.

Unfortunately, Jian Haoge had been born in the exact same year as Zhou Hui.

Even more unfortunately, Zhou Hui—whose innate talent was supposedly mediocre, whose Daoist title even misled people into thinking his greatest achievement was merely hard work—actually advanced at a speed that wasn't slow in the slightest. In fact, he had thoroughly suppressed the current City Lord of Sky City across their entire lives.

Starting from the Grand Sect Tournament during the Golden Core stage, Zhou Hui had stepped over Jian Haoge to claim first place.

—Prior to that, Zhou Hui, born a mortal with lackluster spirit roots, hadn't even possessed the status to meet Young Master Jian of Sky City.

And thus began Jian Haoge's life of being perpetually suppressed.

Tournament duels, secret realm explorations, cultivation breakthroughs, bottleneck advancements—on every single momentous occasion, Jian Haoge found himself outdone by Zhou Hui by half a head.

Naturally, Jian Haoge's unbroken streak of defeats was partly due to his own stubborn death-seeking tendencies. Had he simply conceded defeat and avoided venues where Zhou Hui might appear, given the vast chasm between their backgrounds, they wouldn't have crossed paths much before the Void Refinement stage. But Jian Haoge refused. He tangled with Zhou Hui across his entire existence. The only realm where he had arguably triumphed was in marrying a wonderful wife.

Now, both had attained the Tribulation Transcendence stage, leaving precious few arenas in which to compete. Yet anyone familiar with their past went to great lengths to avoid mentioning Zhou Hui in City Lord Jian's presence, lest they poke his open wounds.

Only Goddess Renran couldn't care less about such social decorum.

She even gave Jian Haoge a bizarre look:

—"Speaking of which, why are you even here?! I would have thought you loathed him to the bone—even if you showed up, shouldn't you be standing on my side?!"

Jian Haoge's murderous glare right now was sharp enough to butcher this socially tone-deaf goddess ten times over; how could he possibly help her?

—"Forget it. I have no desire to understand the thoughts of you mortals,"-the goddess continued coldly.-"But do you have any idea what will happen if the Ascension Stairway is repaired haphazardly?! If a single thought strays, it won't lead to the True Immortal Realm—it will open into the Demon Realm!"

—"The Demon Realm?"-The others hesitated slightly in their movements.

[—"So get out of my way!"](# "Speaker: Goddess Renran")

Seizing the momentary pause while the others hesitated, Goddess Renran attempted to dash toward the Ascension Stairway. But an aerial rampart suddenly manifested before her, barring her path.

—"Jian Haoge!"-Goddess Renran barked, glaring at the cultivator across from her.

Jian Haoge had unfurled an architectural blueprint. Radiating an imposing, magnificent presence that seemed ready to burst from the parchment was a colossal, majestic city.

The city wall barring Goddess Renran's way stemmed from this very blueprint.

—"No. Though I know not the full story behind this Ascension Stairway, knowing Zhou Hui's sheer execution ability, I will assume for now that he is attempting a new method of ascension."

Jian Haoge's voice was firm and resolute:

—"In accordance with the traditions of this realm, no matter how deep the blood feud, interfering while someone undergoes tribulation to ascend is strictly forbidden! Unless you possess concrete proof that Zhou Hui is unsealing the Demon Realm!"

—"Furthermore, I do not believe Zhou Hui is the type to bring catastrophe upon an entire world just to ascend... Ah, granted, I despise him immensely. No one likes someone whom they unilaterally treat as their lifelong nemesis, only for the bastard to not even spare you a passing glance, right?"

—"Yet I am also rather grateful to him. After all, had it not been for him, I doubt I would have reached Tribulation Transcendence so swiftly."

—"Besides, I am rather curious myself: what secret does that Ascension Stairway harbor that could make you rush over in such frantic panic, so terrified that Zhou Hui might claim it?"

—"Therefore, Your Ladyship Goddess—forgive my offense!"

Jian Haoge declared. With a resounding boom, the ethereal city from the blueprint came crashing down heavily upon Goddess Renran—

*Hmph*, he certainly wasn't retaliating against this ill-mannered goddess for airing his dirty laundry!

Even though he had tangled with Zhou Hui for several millennia, he didn't hold a grudge *at all*! Not! One! Bit! (Heavy emphasis)
