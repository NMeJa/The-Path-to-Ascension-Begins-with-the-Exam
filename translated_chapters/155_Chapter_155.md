# Chapter 155: Refinement (第155章 炼化)

—"Truly stubborn,"-Zhou Hui murmured, gazing toward the distant thunderclouds as he chuckled softly and shook his head.

Although Qu Qianxing felt that this practical exercise was a matter of his own personal grievances and did not wish for his master to intervene, it was impossible for Zhou Hui to truly leave him entirely unattended.

What was more, purely in terms of Qu Qianxing's lightning tribulation, it was already an extraordinarily exaggerated affair. Presumably, every cultivator in the Central Domain at or above the Integration Realm could sense the existence of this tribulation; even mortals across a thousand-mile radius could feel the surging currents of spiritual energy brought about by the storm. For Zhou Hui not to know what was transpiring over there, he would have had to seal away his own senses completely.

Of course, safely overcoming a lightning tribulation of such earth-shaking proportions was by no means an easy feat.

Never mind that Qu Qianxing appeared breezy and composed on the surface, as though refining a "True Immortal" was child's play for him; in reality, he had made himself into a living conductor. On one end was the collapse born of overdrawing his spiritual energy; on the other was the sheer agony tearing through his flesh and meridians as he converted heavenly thunder. Had it been someone with slightly lower endurance, they likely would have blacked out long ago just to escape reality.

What bordered on the truly absurd was that amidst this total-body agony and paralysis, Qu Qianxing still had to execute calculations with surgical precision.

After all, that was heavenly thunder!

Heavenly thunder, which tested every cultivator with cold, absolute impartiality!

It had not descended for the convenience of being exploited by Qu Qianxing; its arrival was meant to subject Qu Qianxing to an even harsher ordeal.

Therefore, to convert the heavenly thunder into lightning of his own, Qu Qianxing had to calculate with absolute exactitude: the quantity his physical body could withstand, the quantity he could convert, the quantity required for refinement, the quantity he could balance, and so forth.

This was a computational workload on par with a supercomputer.

With even the slightest miscalculation, having his meridians shattered and spirit roots destroyed would be considered a stroke of luck; far more likely, his soul would be completely pulverized, scattering to the winds.

Zhou Hui felt deeply helpless at this:

—"What reckless madness..."-he murmured.

When saying this, he certainly didn't reflect on what he himself had done throughout his own cultivation path. Compared to him, Qu Qianxing was actually rather restrained; after all, Qu Qianxing loathed gambling, and anything he dared attempt was generally something he believed he could keep firmly within his grasp.

It was just that Qu Qianxing was, after all, not a god—and even gods encountered unforeseen variables. Furthermore, this stunt of his was pushing things far too close to the absolute limit.

Sensing the spiritual fluctuations of his second disciple, Zhou Hui spoke abruptly:

—"Qianxing, watch out!"-Zhou Hui warned.

Even delivered from far away at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the warning detonated right beside Qu Qianxing's ear.

Almost without thinking, Qu Qianxing diverted a fraction of his power to erect a defensive ward. At that very instant, a burst of blood mist abruptly exploded from his ring finger.

This was an imprint that "Liu Xuenian" had covertly planted when he first approached Qu Qianxing earlier. Although he originally had no intention of striking immediately—planning instead to act on some future day when Qu Qianxing stepped outside Zhou Hui's protective aegis—seeing that he was on the verge of being refined into nothingness, there was no longer any "future" to speak of. Thus, the bloody tumor decisively detonated this mark.

This slight disruption caused Qu Qianxing's rhythm in converting heavenly thunder and refining the "True Immortal" to stall for a fraction of a heartbeat.

—"*Ugh!*"-Qu Qianxing let out a stifled grunt as a trickle of dark crimson spilled from the corner of his lips. Fortunately, thanks to his timely defense, his injury was not severe.

—"Spare me! I beg of you, spare me!"-the bloody tumor wailed in agony at this moment.

In truth, it had tried begging for mercy long ago, but Qu Qianxing's lightning had suppressed it so ruthlessly that it hadn't even had the opportunity to utter a sound.

At this moment, because of its disruption, Qu Qianxing's power had dipped for a split second, yet it was not enough to grant it any chance of escape. Thus, this esteemed "True Immortal" wept and screeched with zero dignity left:

—"Qianxing, Qianxing... I can compensate you for everything! I can even—even tell you the method to return to the Heavenly Court! You can do it! After all, you bear the [Destiny of Wenqu](# "Literal CN: 文曲之命 (wénqǔ zhī mìng) — destiny under the Wenqu Star (Star of Literary and Civil Talent), designating a supreme scholar destined for celestial officialdom"); you were meant to be a member of the upper realm...! *AAAAHHH!"*-the creature screamed, letting out another bloodcurdling cry of torment.

Qu Qianxing looked at it coldly, yet felt a sudden sense of anti-climactic disillusionment:

[Was the thing that destroyed my life back then really nothing more than this?]

[How utterly boring.]

Qu Qianxing had assumed he hated and feared such an existence. He had thought that after killing the other party, he would feel, just like last time, a surge of relief mingled with helplessness and despair.

Yes, helplessness and despair.

Thinking of how the entire world was dominated by monsters like this, while mortals remained completely oblivious—some even sacrificing their own kin for personal gain—Qu Qianxing had felt nothing but despair for such a world.

The reason he had wished so desperately for Zhou Hui to ascend was that, when faced with such formidable foes, he felt that only an existence like his master could fundamentally overturn this unjust world.

Yet this time, Qu Qianxing no longer thought that way.

He had slain it once when he was a mere mortal; now, at the Golden Core realm, he could slay it a second time. This so-called "True Immortal"—it really wasn't all that formidable after all!

In fact, this thing was less promising than the students of their own [White Jade Capital Academy](# "Literal CN: 白玉京学堂 (báiyùjīng xuétáng) — the modern public school system established by Zhou Hui").

Qu Qianxing was certain that none of the academy's pupils would ever be so spineless—and of course, they wouldn't stoop to such bottomless depravity either. Those kids were merely lacking the opportunity to study compared to a "True Immortal."

[...Perhaps there is still hope?]

[Perhaps I don't need to pin all my hopes on Master... No, I knew it all along: my grievances are my own grievances. From the very beginning, I was the one who should see them through.]

[Wishing for Master to ascend was merely my own way of running away.]

[I must be the one to change all this injustice myself!]

Accompanying this realization of Qu Qianxing's, thunder detonated across the heavens with terrifying force.

A colossal streak of lightning illuminated the entire expanse into a world of blinding radiance, leaving not even the subterranean depths untouched.

Amidst such a heavenly tribulation, Qu Qianxing stood proud and unyielding. The lightning could not harm him; it could only become part of his being. Instead, it was that bloody tumor that unleashed an even more ear-splitting screech:

—"*AAAAAAHHH!"*-the bloody tumor howled.

It began to melt beneath that inescapable brilliance where nothing could hide.

Seeing this, Qu Qianxing did not breathe a sigh of relief; instead, his expression grew even more taut and vigilant.

Now was the single most critical juncture of the refinement process.

Slaying the opponent was easy, but refining it—solidifying its memories, soul, knowledge, and everything else to make them entirely his own—depended entirely on this final stroke.

However, the "shortcoming" of Qu Qianxing's insufficient cultivation realm manifested most acutely at this exact moment.

Qu Qianxing felt the spiritual energy in his body beginning to fall short of demand, even starting to [overdraw his lifespan](# "Literal CN: 透支寿数 (tòuzhī shòushù) — burning away years of one's remaining biological life force to fuel cultivation") and soul power. Worse still, he sensed the thunderclouds in the sky outside gradually dispersing; the heavenly tribulation appeared to be drawing to a close.

—"...This lightning tribulation is way too weak!"-Qu Qianxing couldn't help but grumble.

The thunderclouds in the sky flickered with a faint spark of electricity, almost like a pathetic whimper.

If it could speak, it would certainly protest that it had already tried its absolute hardest, maxing out the structural limits of a [Nascent Soul breakthrough tribulation](# "Literal CN: 结婴雷劫 (jiéyīng léijié) — the heavenly tribulation lightning faced when condensing a Golden Core into a Nascent Soul")! To be drained completely bone-dry like this—wait, it seemed someone else had done this exact same thing before!

[*Sob, sob, sob...* Today's cultivators are terrifying!]

Having had a sudden epiphany, Qu Qianxing no longer stubbornly fixated on carrying out the refinement entirely unassisted.

—"Please! I need a melody to restore spiritual energy!"-Qu Qianxing called out toward Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo.

There was no spoken reply from that direction, but the music shifted melody instantly, turning gentle and soothing.

Qu Qianxing then called out into the void:

—"Master,"-Qu Qianxing called.

—"*Hah.*"-The corners of Zhou Hui's lips curled into a faint smile.

Yet he did not immediately step in to provide direct support himself; instead, he fished around inside his sleeve and drew something forth.

It was a droplet of True Immortal Blood.

The droplet of True Immortal Blood that Villa Master Qingyang had refined previously.

Because Villa Master Qingyang had been unable to extract any further information from it, and Zhou Hui had visited the upper realm and obtained more materials, Zhou Hui had wanted to investigate the origins of this blood himself, and had thus reclaimed it.

However, plans rarely kept pace with changing circumstances.

Zhou Hui felt that if he provided the power to assist Qu Qianxing, this refinement would remain imperfect; Qu Qianxing had to carry it through to the end himself.

In that case, it was better to send this already-refined droplet of blood over.

The power of the refined True Immortal Blood could suppress that "True Immortal's bloody tumor," giving Qu Qianxing room to maneuver. Moreover, during the refinement process of this droplet, Qu Qianxing had participated as Villa Master Qingyang's assistant the entire time; thus, he could wield it far more effectively to exert suppression, buying himself extra breathing room and energy.

Judging from how Qu Qianxing had exploited the lightning tribulation, Zhou Hui believed he could pull it off.

Even if he couldn't, the seal on this droplet of True Immortal Blood contained Zhou Hui's own heart's blood, which could also supply a portion of power.

Weighing the situation thus, Zhou Hui unsealed the lid of the vessel containing the True Immortal Blood.

Instantly, the droplet of blood soared outward.

In the direction the blood flew, the air rippled like the surface of water; the droplet sank into those ripples and vanished in an instant.

On the other side, above Qu Qianxing and the others who were stubbornly holding out, ripples similarly spread across the air, and a bead of blood shimmering with luminous runes dripped down from above.

What Qu Qianxing received was not direct reinforcement, but such a single drop of blood. He froze for a moment, then an expression of sudden realization dawned upon him:

—"Ah, so that's how it is,"-Qu Qianxing said, breaking into a grin as he instantly grasped his master's intention.

Of course, at this moment, his brain kicked into overdrive once more, running an entirely new set of computational formulas to determine how best to harness this drop of blood.

At this sight, the corner of Qingyang Qingyu's mouth twitched slightly, and even her pipa strumming hitched for an instant.

Sensing the slight lapse in rhythm, Jian Nuo cast a worried glance at Qingyang Qingyu.

—"No, it's just... I feel like I'm still not working hard enough,"-Qingyang Qingyu said.

[...Wait a second, you want to work even harder?!]

Jian Nuo thought in sheer horror.

Yet this emotional reaction of his did not last long, because in the next moment, that bloody tumor seized his attention... No, it could no longer be called a bloody tumor. Under Qu Qianxing's refinement, with the other droplet of True Immortal Blood taking effect, that bloody tumor had completely lost its sapience—the portion of it that could still be termed a "living entity." This was a blessing, for its malice and depraved desires were thoroughly burned away in the process.

Nor was its shape any longer that of an unsightly bloody tumor, nor of that nauseating murky crimson; instead, it radiated a gorgeous multicolored luminescence, its texture morphing soft and silky.

Indeed, this bloody tumor was ultimately refined into a garment.

A [heavenly robe](# "Literal CN: 天衣 (tiānyī) — celestial garment; seamless robe of immortals, referencing the idiom 'heaven's raiment has no seams' (天衣无缝)").

With this, Qu Qianxing's Nascent Soul breakthrough was fully and thoroughly accomplished, and the mark of an astral constellation faintly materialized upon the center of his forehead—signifying that he was no mere ordinary Nascent Soul cultivator, but had already obtained—or rather, reclaimed—the power of the celestial constellations.
