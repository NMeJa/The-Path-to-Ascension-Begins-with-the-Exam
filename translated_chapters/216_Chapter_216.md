# Chapter 216: Plotting with a Tiger for Its Hide

Xia Bing never expected to receive "assistance" from a sect elder.

She refused to become Yan Bailang's cultivation blood sacrifice. She wanted to protect herself, she wanted to survive, and she even dreamed of turning the tables to strike back. Yet with only a Golden Core cultivation, facing a Sect Master who had attained the Body Integration stage centuries ago, every desperate effort felt utterly futile.

At this exact juncture, the one who extended a miraculous life-saving straw was an elder of her own sect.

Elder Bai Jun of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall sought Xia Bing out immediately after her victory in the preliminary rounds.

At the time, representatives from the Heavenly Spirit Sect were interviewing Xia Bing regarding her impressions of the preliminaries—a practice they apparently referred to as an "interview." Bai Jun had stood nearby with a beaming, genial smile, observing the entire exchange before patiently waiting for the interviewers to depart. Only then did he present Xia Bing with the bone whistle.

—"Fairy Bing!"-Bai Jun had addressed her back then.

Both the moniker and his fawning inflection caused a full wave of goosebumps to ripple across Xia Bing's skin.

After all, Elder Bai Jun was notoriously arrogant and vitriolic; he had practically never looked Xia Bing in the eye before. This sudden, unctuous cordiality filled her with acute revulsion.

For a brief second, Xia Bing failed to mask her grimace.

—"You performed magnificently! It is high time outsiders witnessed the true prowess of our Ten Thousand Beasts Hall,"-Bai Jun continued, seemingly oblivious to Xia Bing's stiff expression. In a tone dripping with uncharacteristic benevolence, he remarked:—"However, you carry far too few respectable Dharma artifacts on your person, leaving you deficient in tactical flexibility."

[Deficient in artifacts?]

[Me?]

[Did you even watch my match?]

Xia Bing muttered inwardly.

While it was true that she possessed few respectable family heirlooms, the Heavenly Spirit Sect seemed determined to prevent discrepancies in material wealth from distorting the playing field between major sects and minor clans. Consequently, they had developed the Trial System. As long as one [ground through enough battles](# "Literal CN: was willing to liver/grind hard enough"), one could acquire a formidable arsenal of "Item Cards."

Having ground the trial simulation for several sleepless days, Xia Bing had accumulated a vast collection of cards.

In her match moments ago, she had deployed those artifact cards with lethal precision. As a beast tamer, she had barely summoned her spiritual pets at all—a calculated move to preserve her true trump cards for later rounds.

However, Bai Jun had merely used the comment as a conversational springboard:

—"Therefore, I have brought several Dharma artifacts to bolster your arsenal,"-he announced, retrieving an exquisite jade casket from his robes and offering it to Xia Bing.

—"...My gratitude to the Elder,"-Xia Bing paused in surprise before accepting the jade box without modesty.

Although she despised the spiteful elders of her sect and had no clue why one would suddenly bestow treasures upon her—perhaps watching her duel had finally opened his eyes to her potential?—free treasures were free treasures; one would be a fool to refuse.

Especially now, with Yan Bailang actively coveting her life.

While the Item Cards granted by the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Trial System were formidable, they were strictly restricted to the tournament arenas. An extra authentic artifact represented an extra sliver of survival. Even if any artifact was practically negligible before the despair-inducing chasm between a Golden Core cultivator and a Body Integration grandmaster, Xia Bing refused to surrender.

Still, Xia Bing harbored zero illusions that Bai Jun would gift her anything genuinely precious.

Accepting the casket, Xia Bing prepared to stow it into her storage pouch, but Bai Jun raised a hand to stop her, a cryptic smirk playing at the corners of his lips:

—"Are you not going to open the casket and inspect it?"

—"?"-Xia Bing shot Bai Jun a wary glance.

An intense foreboding coiled in her stomach. Yet considering that this elder's cultivation rivaled the Sect Master's, crushing her was no harder than squashing an ant; he had no need to tamper with a gift to harm her. Steeling her nerves, Xia Bing opened the jade casket per his instruction.

Lying within the casket was the bone whistle.

Compared to the opulent jade box, the whistle was utterly unremarkable and grotesque. Yet Xia Bing's sharp instincts told her immediately that her ominous premonition stemmed entirely from this object.

Xia Bing refrained from touching it, merely examining its exterior:

—"Judging by the structure of this bone whistle, it appears to be refined from spinal vertebrae. Given the slightly recurved osteophytes around the perimeter, it should belong to some serpent... or flood dragon species, correct?"

—"Indeed. It is a segment of... dragon bone,"-Bai Jun drawled, deliberately prolonging his syllables.

—"Wow, dragon bone,"-Xia Bing offered a hollow gasp of cooperation, though inwardly she wondered whether deploying this item in the ring would summon the Eastern Realm's dragon clans to beat down her door.

She had already guessed it was dragon bone.

As a (formerly) renowned beast-taming sect of the Middle Realm, whose Sect Master commanded a four-clawed five-eyed green-faced flood dragon, an ordinary flood dragon bone wouldn't warrant such fanfare from Bai Jun. It was bound to be true dragon bone.

Yet dragon bones weren't astronomically unheard of; several noble dragon households inhabited the Eastern Realm's oceans, and encountering a loose dragon relic through serendipity wasn't entirely impossible.

Seeing through Xia Bing's emotionless performance, Bai Jun deepened his timbre:

—"A *True Dragon's* bone."

—"Wow, an actual True... True Dragon?!"-Xia Bing's eyes widened in genuine disbelief.

Bai Jun smiled complacently:

—"You are familiar with the founding history of our sect, yes?"

—"I am... Our Founding Ancestor received enlightenment from a True Immortal amidst the Abyssal Ocean. He carried the carcass of a ferocious beast upon his shoulders for nine hundred and ninety-nine days. Just as his physical vitality was utterly spent, the ropes across his shoulders snapped, and the carcass dropped to the earth. Only then did he discover that beneath his feet lay a rare Five Blessings Land, where he established our sect, founding the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall,"-this was the legendary myth taught to every disciple upon entry. Suddenly, a realization struck Xia Bing:—"Could it be that the Five Blessings Land was formed because..."

Bai Jun nodded solemnly:

—"Precisely. This was a truth the Founding Ancestor discovered only after raising the mountain gates. To unearth an exceedingly rare Five Blessings Land within the Middle Realm was certainly aided by unique geomancy and converging spiritual leylines, but the fundamental root was an adult True Dragon buried deep beneath the earth, whose primordial essence nourished the soil."

—"Furthermore, it is the corpse of an adult True Dragon whose cultivation stood at the True Immortal stage or higher!"

Xia Bing breathed in shock:

—"I had no idea..."

—"How could you possibly know? This is a supreme state secret reserved exclusively for those who attain the seat of Sect Master,"-Bai Jun sneered softly.

He continued his revelation:

—"Back then, when the Founding Ancestor discovered the True Dragon's remains, he recognized it was both a celestial windfall and an apocalyptic catastrophe. If word leaked, not to mention the myriad sects across the realm, even the dragon clans of the Eastern Realm would mobilize in full force to seize the ancestral remains. Thus, he erected the Beast Soul Tower—not merely to suppress ferocious beasts, but to mask the aura of the True Dragon's corpse! Fortunately, our Founding Ancestor was an anointed chosen one favored by heaven, and our sect was a sanctuary blessed by a True Immortal. The baleful aura of the ferocious beasts genuinely smothered the presence of the dragon corpse. Throughout the millennia since our founding, even our next-door neighbor, the transcendent tribulation-tier powerhouse Immortal Master Fujuan, failed to detect our secret."

—"So that is how it was,"-Xia Bing subconsciously cast a glance in the direction of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

She understood the Founding Ancestor's reasoning, but as for whether Immortal Master Fujuan had genuinely remained in the dark, she harbored deep doubts.

After all, wasn't his Fifth Disciple a literal True Dragon?

[...Wait, if this is a supreme secret that must be concealed from all living souls, why are you telling me?!]

Panic flared within Xia Bing's chest as she stared warily at Bai Jun.

Bai Jun was still rambling with self-satisfaction:

—"Sheltered by the True Dragon's remains, our sect naturally reaped boundless blessings. The Sect Master's green-faced flood dragon, my three-winged dragon-horse, and the spiritual serpents nurtured by countless inner disciples were all successfully hatched and mutated under the influence of that residual draconic energy. If mere draconic vapor possesses such miraculous efficacy, imagine the power of the True Dragon's corpse itself!"

—"Were one to forge divine blades or supreme artifacts from those remains, they would become legendary relics that even True Immortals could not resist. Of course, to beast tamers like us, weapons are extraneous; what we refine are supreme treasures that amplify our beast mastery—such as the bone whistle I have just bestowed upon you."

—"Do not look down upon its modest size. It was meticulously crafted from five prime vertebrae situated near the dragon's tail. Because the materials are exceedingly rare and the forging process perilous beyond measure, the ancestors exhausted their lifespans to craft merely three whistles in total. On the day they were completed, torrential tempests engulfed half of the Eastern Realm. It was only because our sect was situated in an isolated wilderness, and Immortal Master Fujuan had yet to establish the Heavenly Spirit Sect, that no prying eyes detected the phenomenon. Sadly, down to this era, through various historical tribulations, this solitary whistle is the only one remaining."

Hearing this, Xia Bing immediately pushed the jade casket back toward him:

—"...Elder, since it is an irreplaceable heirloom of our sect, this disciple dare not accept it."

—"Are you certain?"-Bai Jun did not take back the box. Instead, he smiled thinly:—"All feathered, furred, scaled, and shelled creatures of this world trace their ancestry to dragons. A bone whistle forged from the dragon's prime vertebrae can dominate all living beasts the moment it is blown. Not to mention unawakened spirit beasts and demonic monsters, even cultivators several realms above your own can be suppressed. With this in your grasp, vanquishing 'every single competitor at the Immortal Arts Assembly' will be as effortless as turning your hand."

[Such a terrifying supreme treasure, deployed merely for tournament glory?]

[What is your true agenda?!]

Xia Bing could tell Bai Jun was speaking with loaded undertones, but she had zero desire to decipher them.

Every elder in the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall harbored vile, treacherous schemes. Xia Bing had no intention of getting entangled in their web:

—"Then all the more reason I cannot..."

—"Back then, Yan Bailang used it as well. He spoke exceedingly highly of its efficacy!"

—"The Sect Master used it too?!"-Xia Bing's eyes widened in horror.

In that fleeting second, she desperately wanted to shut down her cognitive faculties. Yet uncontrollably, the mocking words spoken by that "fiend" within the Beast Soul Tower echoed through her mind:

*"Who told you that? Ancestor Yu Si was a woman. She simply lacked a spine. After painstakingly writing the Ten Thousand Beasts Manual, her leadership was usurped by her junior brother."*

[...So what that fiend said was the absolute truth?!]

[Back then, Yan Bailang used this exact bone whistle to assassinate his senior sister and seize the sect leadership through bloody usurpation?!]

She was utterly doomed. She had stumbled upon a lethal conspiracy far more dangerous than knowing a True Dragon corpse lay buried beneath their mountain!

Yan Bailang had already marked her for death, but if he discovered she knew the truth of his patricidal usurpation, she wouldn't even have a corpse left to bury!

Clutching the jade casket, Xia Bing froze solid like a statue.

Bai Jun, however, appeared thoroughly satisfied with her terror:

—"Ponder it carefully... Naturally, as our sect's supreme heritage, this bone whistle is not gifted to you permanently, but loaned. Once we return to the sect, you must surrender it. Therefore, during the Immortal Arts Assembly, reflect deeply upon who your true opponent is, and how you ought to utilize this whistle."

—"..."

—"Think carefully. Take your time. This old man shall not disturb you further."

Watching the paralyzed Xia Bing, Bai Jun let out a cold snort, turning his back to depart.

—"Wait, Elder!"-Xia Bing called out frantically.

Bai Jun paused and slowly turned his head. He was met with the sight of Xia Bing forcing the corners of her mouth upward into an awkward, clumsy smile of subservience, struggling to ask:

—"May I inquire... if I must deal with a high-tier cultivator—say, one above the Void Refinement stage... what exact steps must I take?"

[Above Void Refinement... isn't that the Body Integration stage?]

[This girl genuinely harbors treasonous intent!]

This time, genuine astonishment rippled across Bai Jun's features.

Although he had deliberately provoked her with hidden insinuations—even preparing for the possibility that she might refuse to take the bait—he never anticipated Xia Bing would be so remarkably receptive.

Bai Jun couldn't help but scrutinize Xia Bing with newfound respect.

The elegant maiden stood half-cloaked in the shadows. While her features portrayed apprehension and anxiety, the depths of her eyes gleamed with chilling resolve and burning ambition.

This sight instantly reminded Bai Jun of the young Yan Bailang centuries ago.

*"She is my senior sister..."* the youth had stammered back then, his face torn with hesitation yet his posture leaning into temptation.

By comparison, this girl was even more decisive!

Then again, throughout the history of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, aside from rare anomalies like "Ancestor Yu Si," which generation of Sect Masters had ever been saints?

Musing thus, Bai Jun spoke aloud:

—"Your cultivation is indeed somewhat meager. Even with the bone whistle, you cannot unleash its full draconic might. However, there are alternative methods, particularly when facing a fellow beast tamer. While a high-tier cultivator can resist the whistle's direct psychic vibration, his spiritual beast cannot. As long as you strike when his spiritual power is depleted and his mental state is at its lowest ebb, instigating a violent backlash within his beast will guarantee success ninety-nine times out of a hundred."

—"...Should you genuinely require assistance, you may notify me."

Bai Jun lowered his voice, flashing a sinister, venomous grin:

—"Compared to a paranoid madman whose sanity is crumbling, I place my bets on you."

***

[Place your bets on me?]

[More like Yan Bailang's recent bouts of madness have pushed the elders into feeling actively threatened, prompting them to strike first!]

[Yet even at this desperate stage, those insidious old vipers refuse to risk dirtying their own hands, seeking instead to turn me into their disposable blade!]

Xia Bing analyzed the situation with razor-sharp clarity.

She had not placed an ounce of trust in Bai Jun merely because he had extended an olive branch.

In Xia Bing's eyes, within the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, the elders and the Sect Master were cut from the exact same cloth—they were all cannibals. The only distinction was that Yan Bailang devoured people out in the open, while the elders consumed their victims through covert machinations.

For instance, while the elders monopolized the greatest celestial resource (the True Dragon's remains), they relentlessly extorted and squeezed the ordinary disciples dry of every last drop of spiritual oil. Another example: they had aided Yan Bailang in murdering the previous Sect Master to secure the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, yet only core lineages were permitted to cultivate the authentic manual, while non-core disciples were fed fragmented, corrupted, and deliberately sabotaged versions. Furthermore, throughout the entire sect hierarchy, every key managerial post was monopolized exclusively by the elders' biological kin and disciples.

Speaking of which, back when the Thousand Stars Secret Realm opened, the sect had dispatched an overseer named Bai Tuo to guard the entrance and confiscate the disciples' spoils! Judging by his surname, he was obviously Bai Jun's relative, and the spirit serpent he raised was likely bathed in the True Dragon's residual draconic energy.

Consequently, despite sharing the same sect banner, Xia Bing never considered herself aligned with the elders, nor had she ever contemplated collaborating with them.

She was certain that gifting her the bone whistle was steeped in malicious intent.

And yet... [MONOLOGUE]It is an offer I cannot refuse.[MONOLOGUE]

Xia Bing caressed the jagged surface of the bone whistle in silent contemplation.

The chasm separating the Lower Three Realms from the Upper Three Realms was wider than the celestial firmament. Even with the authentic *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and endless toil, Xia Bing couldn't guarantee she would ever survive to reach the Body Integration stage. More critically, Yan Bailang would never grant her the time to grow.

Returning to the sect after this tournament would almost certainly seal her execution.

Therefore, even knowing the bone whistle was a lethal trap laid by the elders, Xia Bing had no choice but to pinch her nose and swallow the bait.

—"Even under the most optimistic assumptions, this Immortal Arts Assembly is my sole opportunity... No, given Yan Bailang's deteriorating mental state, can he even hold his sanity together until the tournament concludes?"-Xia Bing murmured to herself.—"My chance might only come once. During this exhibition match..."

[—"What chance?"](# "Speaker: Rong Rong")

The abrupt question startled Xia Bing, nearly causing her to drop the whistle.

Jerking her head up in alarm, she breathed a heavy sigh of relief upon seeing Rong Rong's round, endearing face leaning over her shoulder.

However, the torment consuming her heart was something she could never reveal to Rong Rong.

Not because she feared Rong Rong would leak the secret, but because an ordeal that even Golden Core Xia Bing could not solve was utterly insurmountable for Foundation Establishment Rong Rong. Furthermore, dragging an innocent girl into a catastrophic conspiracy from which no benefit could be gained would only result in Rong Rong being silenced by the elders. Xia Bing held zero faith in the elders' moral integrity; she naturally had to keep Rong Rong shielded.

—"I was pondering the exhibition match,"-Xia Bing shared only the portion that could be voiced safely.—"You must have noticed it as well. When I paid my morning respects earlier, a faint scent of fresh blood lingered in his quarters. Who knows whom he mutilated this time."

Rong Rong was ignorant of the underlying truth, feeling only bewildered apprehension:

—"I don't know what is wrong with the Sect Master lately. It feels as though he has lost all rational thought."

—"After the exhibition match, he will descend into even deeper madness. And his opponent happens to be that Yuwen Xuan."

—"Is Yuwen Xuan truly that formidable?"

—"Did you not encounter him in the Trial System?"-Xia Bing asked, slightly surprised.

—"No,"-Rong Rong shook her head.—"I already cleared the main trial storylines and fought all the major bosses! Could it be that because our cultivation realms differ, the trial system assigns different encounters?"

—"Probably,"-Xia Bing conceded.

Such administrative nuances could only be answered by the Heavenly Spirit Sect's trial architects.

Xia Bing's focus lay elsewhere:

—"In any case, compared to Mo Bei's lethal sword offensive and Lu Shulong's overwhelming might, Yuwen Xuan's combat style is sheer psychological torture. He relies on an endless barrage of consumable artifacts and talismans to slowly grind opponents to dust. I have matched against his trial avatar more than a dozen times, yet I haven't devised a single winning strategy. I haven't even forced him to draw a personal weapon."

—"He's that terrifying?"

—"Rather than terrifying, he is profoundly cunning. I purchased the projection jade slips recording his preliminary bouts, and his modus operandi has remained identical throughout. Whether he looks down upon his opponents or the Heavenly Spirit Sect simply boasts absurdly bottomless wealth, this Eldest Senior Brother has never displayed his true martial strength. Or rather, he has no need to; by relying purely on talismanic saturation and defensive artifacts, he wears opponents down until they lose their tempers entirely."

As she spoke, Xia Bing retrieved an image projection jade slip from her pouch.

A slender young man draped in a crimson-lined black robe beneath an ostentatious black fur mantle materialized within the illusory projection. He wore a Taotie mask covering the upper half of his face, obscuring his eyes and leaving only a faintly mocking smile visible at the corners of his lips. Floating motionless mid-air, he flicked spirit talismans from his fingertips as if spirit stones were free, gradually draining his adversary's spiritual reserves—resembling a wicked, oversized cat toying mercilessly with trapped prey.

Xia Bing felt an inexplicable flicker of familiarity gazing upon the figure.

Regrettably, the man concealed his face behind the mask even on promotional tournament posters—rumor had it that because he was perpetually punished by being strung up from the mountain peach tree, Yuwen Xuan refused to let the public witness his face.

Regardless, Xia Bing held no interest in Yuwen Xuan's true visage.

—"Wow, so this is the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Brother?"-Rong Rong watched the projection for a few moments, nodding in realization:—"What a troublesome opponent! Even with the realm disparity, our Sect Master is bound to suffer quite a frustrating ordeal."

—"Especially since our Sect Master didn't even know what an exhibition match was, let alone conduct preliminary reconnaissance on his adversary... Which is precisely why this match is our sole opportunity."

—"?"

—"I mean to say that after this bout concludes, the Sect Master's wrath will spiral completely out of control,"-Xia Bing explained, casting a protective glance toward Rong Rong as she finally delivered her true warning:—"Therefore, you and the other junior disciples must absolutely refrain from attending this match!"

—"Eh?!"

—"It is entirely predictable. When encountering an opponent like that, whether he wins or loses, the Sect Master will be thoroughly humiliated and enraged. He will almost certainly seek scapegoats to vent his fury. If one is merely beaten, that is fortunate; but if your spiritual pets are slaughtered or worse, the damage is irreversible."

Hearing this, Rong Rong hesitated, looking up at Xia Bing with concern:

—"B-But Senior Sister Xia, will you be safe remaining there alone?"

—"It is perilous, of course, which is why I intend to request several sect elders to accompany me. With the elders present, even if the Sect Master erupts, he will be held in check,"-Xia Bing smiled reassuringly.

—"..."

Rong Rong felt an indescribable sense of unease regarding Xia Bing's arrangements.

Xia Bing gave her no time to dissect the feeling, shoving a heavy storage pouch into Rong Rong's hands:

—"Just in case, remain on standby here! If the Sect Master's madness exceeds all bounds, take these resources and come find a way to rescue me."

—"Understood!"

Hearing that she had been assigned to "standby duty," Rong Rong ceased her questioning.

That did not mean Rong Rong was entirely at ease.

Although she refrained from entering the arena to avoid triggering the Sect Master's temper, she couldn't bring herself to wait obediently in the Athletes' Village. Instead, she rushed to the perimeter plaza outside the battle arena. The Heavenly Spirit Sect's public tournament broadcasts were broadcast everywhere—particularly for a high-profile exhibition bout featuring their own Eldest Disciple—allowing Rong Rong to watch the live projection amidst the plaza crowds.

Truth be told, it was an exceedingly boring duel.

Yan Bailang clearly intended to crush Yuwen Xuan with overwhelming force. As a Body Integration grandmaster facing Yuwen Xuan, who stood "only" at the Void Refinement stage, he theoretically commanded absolute dominance.

Yet his companion beast—the four-clawed five-eyed green-faced flood dragon, likewise possessing Body Integration strength—behaved bizarrely. The moment it slithered into the ring, it cowered in terror, exhibiting an instinctive, paralyzing dread toward Yuwen Xuan. Consequently, Yan Bailang had to divert massive amounts of his own spiritual power just to coerce and stabilize his mount.

Conversely, as the lower-realm combatant, Yuwen Xuan ought to have seized the opportunity while the grandmaster was distracted to launch aggressive counteroffensives. Yet he merely floated leisurely, casually flicking a talisman here and prodding the flood dragon there, treating the entire duel like child's play.

This dull standoff dragged on for an entire hour.

Initially, a massive crowd had congregated before the broadcast screen. Now, aside from a small cadre of suspicious, rabid partisans screaming frantically for Yuwen Xuan, the general spectators had dispersed to watch other matches.

Because Rong Rong stubbornly held her ground, a passerby had even thrust a glowing crimson coral glowstick and a decorative paper fan emblazoned with Yuwen Xuan's portrait into her hands.

Rong Rong felt they had grievously misinterpreted her presence.

Still, watching Yan Bailang's complexion turn progressively ashen and demonic, Rong Rong felt profoundly relieved that she had not attended the live arena.

Yet was Senior Sister Xia truly safe? Would the Sect Master truly restrain his fury out of deference to the elders?

Just as Rong Rong's anxiety threatened to boil over, Yan Bailang abruptly called for a halt—and conceded the match!

[Conceded?!]

Rong Rong's eyes nearly popped out of her head.

She knew intimately how obsessed her Sect Master was with glory and vanity; one could say he had journeyed to the assembly entirely to bask in public adulation. Never in her wildest dreams could she imagine he would concede so abruptly!

Even the spectators and Yuwen Xuan's ardent fan club in the plaza were thoroughly underwhelmed:

[—"Wait, Lord Yuwen won?!"](# "Speaker: Bewildered Fan")

[—"Winning like this doesn't feel satisfying at all!"](# "Speaker: Disappointed Fan")

[—"Seriously! It felt as though his opponent didn't even exert half his power!"](# "Speaker: Disgruntled Cultivator")

[—"Indeed! For the Sect Master of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, failing to control his spiritual beast is disgraceful enough, but he's a Body Integration titan! Why didn't he just engage in direct spell warfare? Don't tell me his spiritual energy couldn't overpower a Void Refinement junior!"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

[—"I was hoping to witness Lord Yuwen's authentic martial prowess!"](# "Speaker: Frustrated Spectator")

The crowd grumbled in dissatisfaction, while the projection cameras tracked Yan Bailang's retreating figure, seemingly investigating why the grandmaster had forfeited.

Yan Bailang ignored everyone.

Staggering off the stage, he hurried toward the private athlete lounge with erratic, uneven steps. When Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples approached for a post-match interview, he brushed past them without a word. The moment he stepped inside the lounge, Yan Bailang cast a heavy isolation restriction across the doorway.

[—"Ah, does he not know that the assembly lounges have automated live broadcasts and restrictions are disabled?"](# "Speaker: Perceptive Spectator")

someone in the plaza remarked in amusement.

Of course he didn't know!

The man had never bothered reading the tournament rulebooks, so how could he possibly know such mundane administrative details?

Rong Rong thought bitterly. Yet she had no attention to spare for idle gossip, because within the broadcast projection of the lounge, she spotted Xia Bing standing by the wall—accompanied, as promised, by several sect elders.

Seeing that everything aligned with Xia Bing's calculations, Rong Rong attempted to comfort herself:

[With the elders present, the Sect Master will surely exercise restraint—]

In the very next instant, Yan Bailang's green-faced flood dragon suddenly whipped its massive serpentine tail, sweeping violently toward everyone inside the room! Its ferocious vigor and lethal momentum were completely contrary to the cowering pet seen in the arena!

The elders of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall were seasoned combatants; despite the surprise attack, they hastily erected protective barriers. Only Xia Bing, possessing the lowest cultivation, was caught unprepared and sent crashing across the floor by the dragon's tail!

—"Senior Sister!"-Rong Rong cried out in terror.

She watched as Xia Bing crashed heavily against the wall. A small object slipped from Xia Bing's fingers, rolling clattering across the floorboards—it was none other than the grotesque bone whistle Rong Rong had seen earlier!

The instant Yan Bailang laid eyes on that bone whistle, his expression twisted into pure demonic frenzy!

Discarding all pretense of dignity, he lunged forward, snatching the bone whistle into his clawed fingers, and bellowed in rabid accusation:

—"I knew it! You! All of you want to murder me!"

[—"Murder?!"](# "Speaker: Plaza Spectators")

A collective uproar detonated across the spectator plaza.

—"S-Sect Master, you have a guilty conscience!"-Xia Bing coughed out a mouthful of fresh blood, yet leveled an unyielding finger at him in public condemnation:—"Was it not you and the elders who conspired together to murder the previous Sect Master first?!"

[...Wait a minute! Don't suddenly air our sect's scandalous, bloody patricidal dirty laundry on a live public broadcast for the entire cultivation world to watch!]

Rong Rong's mind collapsed into absolute chaos.
