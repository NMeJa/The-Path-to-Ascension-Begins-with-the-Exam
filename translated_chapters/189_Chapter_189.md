# Chapter 189: Dao Bones

Southern Domain, Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

Tao Zhu stared blankly at the jade slip before him.

His expression was like that of a toad about to be boiled alive in lukewarm water: dazed, yet with eyes wide open, tinged with two parts despair, three parts bewilderment, and five parts innocence, brimming with [pure and untainted stupidity](# "Meme: 清澈的愚蠢 (qīngchè de yúchǔn) — popular internet slang describing an earnest, innocent, yet completely clueless gaze, originally used for college graduates entering the workforce").

When Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai arrived riding a white crane, this was the exact scene that greeted him.

He couldn't help but suspect that Tao Zhu's soul had been snatched away by something.

Yet if such a thing had genuinely happened, whatever could snatch the soul of a Mahayana powerhouse was no petty trick. Jiu Zhai dared not approach recklessly; instead, he fished a Soul-Stabilizing Talisman from his sleeve, let out a sharp cry of "Halt!", and cyan-blue flames burst forth from the talisman's calligraphy. The entire talisman fluttered like an azure butterfly straight toward Tao Zhu's body.

However, just as this azure talisman drifted slightly above Tao Zhu's crown, before it could even flutter downward, a long, fleshy tongue suddenly shot out from Tao Zhu's mouth. In one swift snap, it curled around the azure talisman and yanked it back into his mouth—or perhaps, even deeper down.

—"Bleurgh!"-Boss Tao's mouth instinctively chewed twice before he began violently gagging and vomiting.

A cluster of cyan flames erupted from his throat, splattering everywhere.

Fortunately, this pure flame was neither filthy nor scalding; it would not combust on ordinary objects. Instead, it was bitterly frigid. As soon as it struck the ground, the low table, the jade slip, and the hems of their robes, it instantly crystallized into a thin sheen of frost.

Tao Zhu was also frozen "awake" by the chill. He glared at Jiu Zhai, sputtering:

—"What the hell are you doing?! Cough, cough, cough!"-Tao Zhu rasped.

—"I saw you looking like your soul had flown away and wanted to summon your spirit back. Who knew you'd actually eat it?"-Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai chuckled.-"Still, not a waste. The Soul-Stabilizing Talisman achieved its intended purpose after all."

—"Ptui, ptui! Whose soul flew away?! I was merely lost in thought... confirming the blueprints for the teleportation array. Yes, just confirming the blueprints,"-Tao Zhu defended himself.

Yet as his gaze fell back onto the blueprints, his eyes glazed over once more.

Seeing this, Jiu Zhai frowned:

—"The teleportation array? The one you're building? Is there some kind of problem with it?"-he asked, cautiously scrutinizing the blueprints for several breaths, attempting to discern whether there were any soul-snatching traps woven into them.

Traps there were none; tidy array formations and dense mathematical calculation formulas, however, were aplenty.

As a Divination cultivator who consorted with the *I Ching* day in and day out, computing the boundless Great Dao of Heaven and Earth, Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai was naturally quite different from the copper-scented Boss Tao. He absorbed those formation formulas almost instantly, even praising:

[—"As expected of Untiring. What a gorgeous array formation."](# "Speaker: Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai")

—"Untiring?"-Tao Zhu scoffed.-"This wasn't drawn by Untiring at all! It was drawn by his disciple—and not even the Ran family one, but the one with trash spirit roots at the Foundation Establishment Stage!"

—"How is that possible?"-Jiu Zhai said, stunned.

Yet there was no need to go find Untiring to confirm it. As Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai, capable of divining the past, present, and future, he only had to open his celestial inner eye a sliver to immediately discern who had drafted these blueprints. His blurted "As expected of Untiring" a moment ago had merely been a reflexive stereotype.

Yet once he confirmed the blueprints' true origins, Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai uttered for the second time:

—"How on earth is that possible?! How could a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator draft blueprints of this caliber? And in less than a single year?!"-he cried in disbelief.

—"What's so impossible about it?"-Tao Zhu said sourly.-"[Like master, like disciple](# "Literal CN: 有其师必有其徒 (yǒu qí shī bì yǒu qí tú) — as is the teacher, so is the pupil"). Wasn't Untiring just like that back in the day?"

—"Stop talking nonsense! While Untiring was certainly exceptional back then, he never reached such an absurd degree,"-Jiu Zhai disagreed.

—"Why don't you try saying that to City Lord Jian?!"-Tao Zhu retorted, clearing his throat to mimic Jian Haoge's pretentious aristocratic tone:-"'Untiring's cultivation can be achieved through sheer effort? Can mere effort outstrip the 9,576 top-grade spirit stones I burned to ashes?! You tell me if this makes any damn sense?!'"

—"He only burned 9,576 spirit stones? Far more than that,"-Jiu Zhai said, raising an eyebrow.-"Just to break through to Void Refinement before Untiring, he spent eight thousand spirit stones at my divination booth alone."

—"He said those words back when he had only just met Untiring... Wait a moment, Jian Haoge only had to pay eight thousand for a divination at your place?! You charged me ten thousand last time!"-Tao Zhu blurted.

—"The things being calculated were different. Besides, back then I already foresaw that Jian Haoge, just to cling to Zhou Hui, would buy affinity-binding talismans several thousand more times, not to mention countless other curse and blessing talismans. Could you match that volume?"-Jiu Zhai countered.

—"Hiss, Jiu Zhai, your heart is even blacker than mine!"-Tao Zhu sighed.

He and Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai exchanged a knowing look that only crooked merchants could truly appreciate.

Still, the two youths they were reminiscing about had long since become Tribulation Transcendence titans. Although neither of those two would hold grudges over such ancient history anymore, it was best mentioned as little as possible.

Jiu Zhai pulled the topic back:

—"Still, the reason City Lord Jian was able to nip at Untiring's heels was because the gap between them had not yet widened beyond comparison. No matter how monstrous Untiring was, it remained within the bounds of reason. But if Untiring had written something like this during his Foundation Establishment Stage, he wouldn't have survived to this day,"-he said, tapping the blueprints resting upon the low table.

—"That is true,"-Tao Zhu finally admitted, gazing at the blueprints with a heavy sigh.

A Foundation Establishment cultivator, in the span of a mere single year, had reconstructed and designed a large-scale teleportation array blueprint that even Mahayana powerhouses found obscure and difficult to comprehend.

How could that be possible?!

This was not discrimination; it was an objective, cold fact.

The act of cultivating immortality was never merely about absorbing spiritual qi and slinging celestial spells. If anything, such spells and spiritual energy were the most superficial exterior of all. At its core, cultivation was fundamentally a process of "remodeling" the physical body.

Remodeling an imperfect body into perfection; remodeling a short-lived vessel to possess infinite lifespan; remodeling a frail frame into indomitable might; remodeling an ignorant, dull mind into supreme intellect and wisdom.

Because of this, from a biological standpoint, a Mahayana cultivator and a Foundation Establishment cultivator were practically two entirely distinct species.

Never mind having Zhou Hui's personal guidance or putting in enough blood and sweat—Guan Ye designing a large-scale teleportation array within a single year was roughly as absurd as [a pocket calculator designing a manned lunar rover in three minutes](# "Tech metaphor: 便携计算机用三分钟设计出了载人月球车 — absurd satirical contrast between low-spec hardware and high-spec computational engineering").

Even the supercomputers themselves—the Tribulation Transcendence and Mahayana powerhouses—found it utterly outrageous.

It was just that during this recent period, Tao Zhu had had his worldview shattered and rebuilt by Zhou Hui and his disciples so many times that his cognition of reality had developed a slight warp; he felt that no matter how preposterous a development was, it was entirely par for the course.

Tao Zhu attempted to find a reasonable explanation for Guan Ye:

—"Still, I heard Peilan mention that the child [Entered the Dao through Craftsmanship](# "Cultivation concept: 由器入道 (yóu qì rù dào) — attaining enlightenment and stepping onto the Great Dao through the mastery of artifacts/tools rather than standard spiritual root cultivation"). I suppose he's different from ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivators."

—"Entered the Dao through Craftsmanship? He possesses a Dao Bone?"-Jiu Zhai asked in surprise.

But soon, Jiu Zhai shook his head:

—"Even so, a Dao Bone is by no means a shortcut that yields overnight results. If anything, precisely because one possesses a Dao Bone, all convenient Dharma treasures and spiritual elixirs along the cultivation path must be cast aside; one can only rely on their own two feet to trudge step by step to the destination."

—"Tsk!"-Tao Zhu, choked into speechlessness, promptly abandoned all further thought.-"It's not this, and it's not that! Well then, omniscient Lord Ancestor Jiu Zhai, why don't you use those all-seeing eyes of yours that perceive everything under heaven to tell me: why on earth are Untiring and his disciples so utterly abnormal?"

—"Can't see,"-Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai answered bluntly.

—"Heh. And you call yourself the Southern Domain's sole Tribulation Transcendence titan?"-Tao Zhu sneered.

One couldn't blame him for treating Jiu Zhai and Untiring so differently. Despite both being Tribulation Transcendence titans, Jiu Zhai spent most of his time acting like an old charlatan who freeloaded meals and drinks; this couldn't be spoken, that couldn't be divined—what use was he?!

Jiu Zhai did not take offense at this "foolish layman's" attitude. As always, he explained patiently:

—"I am hardly omniscient. The will of Heaven is difficult to fathom, and hexagrams shift across ten thousand variations, let alone when the divination subject themselves harbors fundamental anomalies. Setting aside that monster Untiring, every single one of his disciples boasts a bizarre and miraculous origin."

—"Take his 'plain and unremarkable' fourth disciple, for example: there has always been a celestial veil blinding my eyes. Based on my experience, an existence of an even higher tier than myself must have targeted him, plundering his future. Originally, I couldn't comprehend why. But having seen these blueprints today, and having heard you mention the Dao Bone, I finally understand."

Speaking to this point, Jiu Zhai shook his head:

—"To be targeted by an existence of that caliber, no wonder he was fated to suffer the agonizing torment of having his bones extracted and his marrow ripped out nine times over—a fate from which not even Untiring could save him."

* * *

**Heavenly Dao Alliance · Department of Works**

—"Dao Bone?"-Guan Xinguang bowed his head.-"This department does not possess such a material."

—"You don't?"-Goddess Ren Ran frowned, glaring at him.

Guan Xinguang was the Chief Director of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works, as well as the foremost authority of the artifact refinement guild across this mortal realm.

Perhaps influenced by his vocation, Guan Xinguang possessed a dull, wooden, ruddy square face. Burly and thick-waisted, he looked less like a Mahayana powerhouse and more like an honest, simple mortal artisan incapable of uttering a lie.

Consequently, whenever people commissioned the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works to forge artifacts, they always felt thoroughly at ease.

High-level cultivators even held a certain stereotype: if one desired top-tier luxury items, they could naturally visit the Palace of Carefree Wandering; but if spirit stones were tight and one merely needed a few Dharma artifacts to get by, coming to the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works was the most sensible choice.

Naturally, if the artifacts purchased here broke down quickly, nobody would complain. After all, the spirit stones spent seemed minimal, and the cultivation world hardly issued quality guarantee certificates anyway. The Heavenly Dao Alliance projected a thoroughly righteous image, and the Chief Director of the Department of Works possessed a face that looked incapable of deception—so next time, they would simply continue buying here.

Having lingered in this realm for so long, Goddess Ren Ran had inevitably absorbed some of these local notions.

She glared at Guan Xinguang, yet did not suspect him of lying either. Instead, she muttered strangely to herself:

—"How odd. How is it that I heard it was in the Department of Works...?"-she murmured.

Saying this, Ren Ran seemed about to turn and depart to question someone else and clear things up.

Seeing this, Guan Xinguang secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

Right at that moment, the golden tassels dangling from the buyao hairpin atop Goddess Ren Ran's head swayed slightly, brushing past her ear.

The Goddess's expression suddenly twisted, her gaze snapping ice-cold and fierce:

—"How did you know the 'Dao Bone' I spoke of was a material?!"

—"This..."-Guan Xinguang's entire body stiffened.

Indeed: why had his immediate, reflexive response been "material"?

A Dao Bone was an innate aptitude unique to cultivators who [Entered the Dao through a specific vocation](# "Cultivation lore: 由X入道 — reaching enlightenment through secular crafts like forging, medicine, or brewing"). Whenever people evaluated it, it was almost always discussed alongside cultivation spirit roots and skeletal talent. Barring a tiny handful of heinous, bloodthirsty demonic cultivators, virtually no one ever treated a "Dao Bone" as a crafting material!

Furthermore, as the Chief Director of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works, Guan Xinguang naturally hailed from the most orthodox of righteous sects. Under no circumstances should his instinctive reaction to "Dao Bone" have been "crafting material." In theory, upon hearing of a Dao Bone, he should have assumed she was inquiring whether the Department of Works housed any geniuses possessing such a Dao Bone.

Piecing this together, Goddess Ren Ran was consumed by the fury of having been duped. Her spiritual might, brimming with lingering celestial power, surged outward, causing the surrounding air to thicken like viscous syrup.

Forced two steps back by this crushing pressure from a Tribulation Transcendence titan, Guan Xinguang desperately tried to explain:

—"S-Sky City has been repairing voidships recently, and someone proposed that tempering it with a 'Dao Bone' would be ideal. I-I merely attended several meetings, caught wind of it by chance, and was influenced by the rumor,"-Guan Xinguang stammered. Sweat rolled down his temples, making him look all the more like an aggrieved, miserable simpleton.

But Goddess Ren Ran no longer believed a single word.

After all, when she had originally descended to the mortal realm, she hadn't been like Lin Qi, merely seeking to play hooky from work. She had genuinely believed that lower-realm mortals were ignorant and required divine enlightenment before coming down. The legendary tales about her were even seventy percent true: she had indeed voluntarily sacrificed thirty percent of her essence blood during the ancient Demon-God War, causing her cultivation base to plummet, which had forced her to remain stranded in this realm.

Yet the inhabitants of the lower realm had hardly shown her unquestioning obedience because of her sacrifice. On the contrary, they had committed countless infuriating atrocities, and anyone who proactively approached her merely wanted to pry for intelligence regarding the Upper Realm. This had led Goddess Ren Ran to become thoroughly disillusioned with this mortal realm; her initial thought of bringing a select few who performed admirably back to the Heavenly Court during the demonic invasion had long since vanished without a trace.

A type like Guan Xinguang—who dared to "belittle and deceive her"—was precisely the kind of person Goddess Ren Ran despised most.

Losing all patience to listen to Guan Xinguang's excuses, Ren Ran raised her hand, shooting a streak of golden light straight into his skull.

Guan Xinguang's eyes bulged in terror, but in the next second, his expression turned utterly slack and vacant—though given how dull and wooden he looked by default, there wasn't much noticeable difference.

Seeing this, Ren Ran also felt a flicker of concern. She interrogated him with meticulous care:

—"Were you the one who received the Celestial Edict back then?"-she asked.

[—"Yes."](# "Speaker: Guan Xinguang")

—"You didn't extract the bone according to the Celestial Edict?"-she demanded.

[—"No, this humble one dared not disobey! This humble one carried it out immediately!"](# "Speaker: Guan Xinguang")

—"Then the Dao Bone is in your hands?"-she pressed.

[—"It is not."](# "Speaker: Guan Xinguang")

—"Huh?!"-Ren Ran raised her voice.-"Since you were the one who extracted the bone, how is it not in your hands?!"

—"T-There wasn't one! There was no Dao Bone from the very beginning!"-Kneeling upon the ground, Guan Xinguang answered uncontrollably:-"This humble one extracted the bone and refined it strictly according to the Celestial Edict, but no matter how it was refined, it was nothing more than ordinary mortal bone!"

—"Are you saying the Celestial Edict was mistaken?!"-Ren Ran snapped in disbelief.

—"Of course I would never dare! But that was truly not a Dao Bone! This humble one can take you to see it!"-This time, Guan Xinguang offered the proposal through his own conscious volition.

He was certain that he could not endure a second round of soul-searching interrogation from this "Goddess."

Under the crushing oppression of terror, this Chief Director of the Department of Works made no attempt to conceal anything, leading the Goddess straight into the department's most vital and heavily guarded secret refinement chamber—a forbidden ground that even the Plum-Bamboo immortal couple might not be permitted to enter.

The Heavenly Dao Alliance was truly worthy of being the largest alliance across the cultivation world.

Regardless of the craftsmanship of the artisans within its subordinate Department of Works, the hardware facilities, at least, were unquestionably top-tier.

Especially this forbidden sanctum.

Here, an entire pocket cave-heaven had been lavishly forged. Within this dimension stood ninety-nine towering bronze pillars stretching between heaven and earth, carved with all manner of profoundly enigmatic runes. Vigorous celestial fire flowed downward along the channels of the runes, forming a colossal artifact-refinement array across the bronze floor.

At the very center of this magnificent array, bound tightly by layers of bronze chains, hung—astoundingly—a human spine.

Yet this spine was exceedingly frail and slender. It appeared to belong to nothing more than an ordinary human youth whose skeletal aptitude wasn't even particularly good, leaving the bone's coloration murky and mottled. It seemed thoroughly unworthy of such a grand setting.

No matter who looked upon it, no one would ever consider this to be a Dao Bone.

—"How could this be?"-Having inspected the actual object, Goddess Ren Ran was still unwilling to believe it. She glared at Guan Xinguang, demanding:-"Could it be that you coveted the Dao Bone and casually swapped it out with some random bone?!"

—"An item specifically designated by a True Immortal—how would this humble one ever dare?!"-Guan Xinguang cried out in grievance.-"This humble one acted strictly in accordance with the Celestial Edict! You can even compare this spine against the designated body to verify whether it originated from him!"

—"Pfft. Who knows how many years ago that person died..."-Ren Ran sneered.

—"No, that useless trash is still alive!"-Guan Xinguang said, his eyes reddening.-"Somehow, he was picked up by the Untiring Immortal Venerable, who grafted another spine onto him and accepted him as a disciple!"

—"Untiring again?!"-The Goddess revealed an expression of mingled horror and revulsion.

Seeing this expression, Guan Xinguang felt a twinge of vindictive gratification. Seizing the opportunity, he poured out his bitter grievances:

—"This humble one naturally dared not doubt the Celestial Edict, yet not only did I fail to obtain a Dao Bone, but I also offended the Untiring Immortal Venerable for nothing! That lawless Immortal Venerable even dared to smash the Palace of Carefree Wandering and fiercely shields his own! For the past century, this humble one hasn't dared to close his eyes for a single night, terrified that the Immortal Venerable would come knocking on my door!"-he whined.

—"Yet how could the Dao Bone be fake? Could Untiring have..."-the Goddess muttered to herself.

In any case, Ren Ran's single-track mind simply could not accept the notion that there was no Dao Bone.

Seemingly unable to bear her [utter lack of brain cells](# "Literal CN: 脑干缺失 (nǎogàn quēshī) — 'missing a brain stem'; popular Chinese internet hyperbole for someone being breathtakingly brainless or oblivious") any longer, a voice echoed out from Ren Ran's buyao hairpin:

—"You keep talking about some Celestial Edict. Who on earth issued this Celestial Edict wanting this section of Dao Bone?"

Hearing this voice, Guan Xinguang stole a puzzled glance at the top of Ren Ran's head, but dared not inquire further.

Ren Ran paid no heed to Guan Xinguang's reaction, replying simply:

—"It was Lord Tian Yan."

—"Tian Yan, is it?"-the voice mused.

The owner of this voice was, of course, Guardian Demon-Subduing Heavenly General Lin Qi, who had skipped work to descend to the lower realm.

As a fellow member of the Heavenly Court, Lin Qi naturally knew of Tian Yan; if anything, they enjoyed quite a friendly rapport. Tian Yan was an official of the Bureau of Heavenly Dao, in charge of the Heavenly Dao across the three thousand minor worlds.

Whenever a True Immortal sought to descend to the mortal realm to undergo tribulations, they had to go through his hands to modify the target minor world's Heavenly Dao and script their destined life trajectory. As such, he was a figure even True Immortals fawned over.

Since Tian Yan had the authority to modify the Heavenly Dao, he was naturally adept at deducing celestial destiny; his prophecies virtually never missed their mark.

Lin Qi did not believe Tian Yan could make a mistake either.

Peering through Ren Ran's eyes at the frail human youth's spine, he suddenly asked:

—"Could it have been intentional? What if his goal all along was to forge the true Dao Bone?"

—"What do you mean?"-Ren Ran asked, perplexed.

—"In other words: this bone was always nothing more than an ordinary human bone. But using this as a karmic catalyst, the bone's owner would be taken in as a disciple by that Untiring you mentioned, ultimately growing the exact Dao Bone he desired?"-Lin Qi theorized.

—"How could a Dao Bone be so easy to grow?! Untiring isn't a god—how could he possibly make his disciple grow a Dao Bone...?"-Ren Ran questioned.

—"Since you know he isn't a god, why are you so utterly terrified of him?"-Lin Qi asked.

—"I..."-Ren Ran froze, tongue-tied.

Lin Qi found Ren Ran's mentality utterly fascinating.

She seemed to look down on Untiring, yet attributed every single sinister deed in the universe to him. Just what kind of mental gymnastics was this?

And while these two were debating, Guan Xinguang mulled over their conversation and timidly offered evidence:

—"Th-This Lord Upper Immortal (?) might actually have a point."

—"What did you say?!"-Ren Ran snapped, glaring at Guan Xinguang once more.

Guan Xinguang hurriedly lowered his head, though a calculation had already formed in his mind:

While he did not know who the voice inside the buyao hairpin belonged to, it was undeniably an existence that even Goddess Ren Ran could not rebut. Aligning himself with that figure would surely not be a bad move.

Thinking thus, Guan Xinguang fished a sheet of paper from his sleeve—astoundingly, it was this week's edition of the *White Jade Capital Weekly*:

—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect announced that Untiring's fourth disciple Guan Ye, the original owner of this spine, reconstructed the manufacturing blueprints for a large-scale teleportation array despite possessing only Foundation Establishment cultivation. He is currently preparing to construct a teleportation array connecting the Heavenly Spirit Sect to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, and is actively recruiting array masters and artifact refiners,"-Guan Xinguang explained, though his words carried a trace of thinly veiled jealousy and dread.

Foundation Establishment Stage! A large-scale teleportation array!

This was an achievement Guan Xinguang couldn't even dare to dream of, and it was the very reason why hearing "Dao Bone" had caused him to reflexively think of "crafting material." Otherwise, over a century had passed; although he had panicked for several days back then upon hearing Guan Ye had been taken in by Untiring—even ruthlessly silencing the subordinate clan head who carried out the task and slaughtering every single disciple in that forge—Untiring had never come knocking on his door. He had assumed Untiring couldn't be bothered to investigate the matter, and had pretended nothing had ever happened. But now, that Guan Ye had risen to prominence! Not only did he frequently publish papers in academic journals, but he was now spearheading a mega-engineering teleportation project. Even if his master didn't stand up for such a gifted artifact refiner, would others truly refrain from sticking up for him?

For days on end, Guan Xinguang had been unable to enter meditative seclusion whenever he thought of this. That was why, when Goddess Ren Ran arrived to inquire, he had assumed his past crimes had finally [come to light](# "Literal CN: 东窗事发 (dōngchuāng shìfā) — 'the secret plotted at the eastern window is exposed'; traditional idiom for a conspiracy being unmasked").

Looking at it this way, was the Goddess actually on his side?

Guan Xinguang stole a glance at Goddess Ren Ran's expression.

Ren Ran's face, however, was exceptionally grim. Snatching the *White Jade Capital Weekly* from Guan Xinguang's hands, she flipped through it roughly before tearing it into shreds:

—"Lord Heavenly General, since the Dao Bone is gone, let us change our approach—"-she urged.

Clearly, she accepted this explanation, yet wanted nothing to do with the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Lin Qi, however, suffered from none of Ren Ran's psychological baggage. He asked with genuine curiosity:

—"Why change the approach? Since the owner of that Dao Bone is still alive, and has even become a grand array master at Foundation Establishment—"

[—"A-Artifact refinement master."](# "Speaker: Guan Xinguang")

—"Fine! An artifact refinement master,"-Lin Qi dismissed carelessly.-"That proves he truly possesses the Dao Bone now nine times out of ten. What's more, it's far superior to ordinary human bone! On that page, the one dragging a crimson spine behind him is him, right? That looks like the spine of some spiritual beast—wouldn't that be even better?"

[—"Your Lordship means..."](# "Speaker: Goddess Ren Ran")

Lin Qi felt Ren Ran was genuinely hopeless at reading the room, but nevertheless made his brazen proposal:

—"Since Tian Yan wants that Dao Bone anyway, why don't we just seize this opportunity to rip it out?"

At this proposal, Ren Ran and Guan Xinguang shuddered in unison.

It was as though Lin Qi hadn't suggested extracting the spine of a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator, but had instead demanded they charge solo into the headquarters of the demon army to claim the head of the Demon Emperor's most favored concubine.

—"You're unwilling?"-Sensing their resistance, Lin Qi asked with clear displeasure.

—"It isn't that, Lord Heavenly General,"-Goddess Ren Ran struggled to reply, attempting a tactful refusal.-"The main issue is that none of us are acquainted with the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and it is the territory of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. That tree existed even when this land was still in the Upper Realm, sheltering two-thirds of this world's demon race. If we barge straight in, seizing the Dao Bone would probably... not be difficult, but I fear it would incite massive upheaval across this realm!"

—"That is true,"-Lin Qi mused.

He was arrogant by nature, so he saw nothing wrong with barging into a Tribulation Transcendence titan's domain to rip out their disciple's spine, nor did he consider throwing this mortal world into chaos to be any grand disaster.

However, Lin Qi was worried about offending Tian Yan and the Bureau of Heavenly Dao he represented.

Lin Qi knew that the Heavenly Dao of such minor worlds was meticulously scripted. If he threw it into chaos and forced the Bureau of Heavenly Dao to work overtime, those bastards would definitely hold a grudge against him. The next time he wanted to visit another minor world for vacation ([crossing tribulations](# "Satirical pun: 度假 (dùjià, vacation) crossed out and replaced with 渡劫 (dùjié, undergoing heavenly tribulations); Lin Qi treats celestial tribulations as paid vacations")), he feared the bureaucrats at the Bureau of Heavenly Dao would script him some [melodramatic, god-awful dog-blood fate](# "Literal CN: 狗血的命运 (gǒuxuè de mìngyùn) — melodramatic, absurdly tragic soap-opera destiny"), turning it into an actual agonizing tribulation!

Lin Qi asked in a dilemma:

—"Is there any justifiable reason to reasonably get close to him?"

Of course there wasn't.

Goddess Ren Ran wanted to say as much. Sensing Lin Qi's hesitation, she was secretly overjoyed—while she had brought Lin Qi down to deal with Untiring, that was under the prerequisite of not using herself as the vanguard! Thus, she prepared to add a few more persuasive words to make Lin Qi abandon the notion of using the Dao Bone entirely and descend using an alternate method.

Yet before the Goddess could formulate her argument, another newspaper was slipped right into her hand:

—"You wish to go to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree? You could try this method!"

[Who?!]

Goddess Ren Ran looked up at the speaker.

Without anyone noticing when he had arrived, a tall, handsome, dashing young man stood right beside them. He was draped in an iridescent, dazzling robe that didn't quite suit him, making him look exceptionally conspicuous.

Yet whether it was Goddess Ren Ran, Guan Xinguang, or even Lin Qi himself, the moment their gazes drifted away from this young man, the suspicion in their hearts vanished like chalk wiped away by a blackboard eraser.

Unanimously, they ignored the young man who had materialized out of thin air, their entire attention falling upon that newspaper.

And that very newspaper seemed, in their minds, completely natural—an object that had existed there from the very beginning.

The front-page headline blared:

> ### *To Construct Large-Scale Teleportation Array, Heavenly Spirit Sect Plans to Open Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination*

[Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination? What on earth is this?]

Guan Xinguang suddenly felt a sense of foreboding far more terrifying than when he had been mind-controlled by the Goddess just moments ago.

Lin Qi, however, had already skimmed through the article with lightning speed, crying out in sheer delight:

—"Isn't this the perfect, legitimate opportunity to get close to the Dao Bone?!"

Accompanied by these words, Goddess Ren Ran also turned an eerie, predatory gaze toward Guan Xinguang beside her.

Guan Xinguang shuddered violently.

He felt as innocent—and utterly helpless—as a plucked quail skewered over a roasting spit.
