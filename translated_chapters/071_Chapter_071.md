# Chapter 71: Final Exams and Winter Break (第71章 期末考试和寒假)

Did Zhou Hui know what the Palace of Carefree Wandering was up to?

Naturally, he did.

After all, the "spies" planted by Carefree Palace were all delicate, charming children—not that the ordinary towns and villages of the Central Domain lacked comparable beauties, but the difference was like that between pampered greenhouse blossoms and vigorously thriving wildflowers. One didn't even need divination; the contrast was obvious at a single glance.

Yet Zhou Hui didn't bother to intervene.

To "cover their tracks," Carefree Palace had deliberately chosen children who either possessed no spirit roots at all or had murky, fragmented ones. Most of them were utterly incapable of cultivation. Had this sudden event of opening the preparatory school not occurred, [one didn't need half a brain cell to guess](# "Literal CN: one could think with their knees to know (用膝盖想) — an idiom meaning something blindingly obvious") what sinister purpose Carefree Palace had in hoarding such children.

Rather than letting that happen, it was far better to have them study properly.

In any case, the curriculum taught within the school consisted of upright, wholesome knowledge. Furthermore, barring a handful of specialized classrooms, an absolute restriction against spellcasting was placed across the entire school interior—neither innate spellcasting nor the use of magical artifacts was permitted. There was no need to worry that these "sleeper moles" could harm their classmates.

And if worst came to worst, should these kids insist on causing trouble, simply piling on a bit more homework and assigning heavier problem sets would suffice.

Thus Zhou Hui calculated.

The "moles" of Carefree Palace mingled like black sheep among a flock of white lambs, gazing at Zhou Hui with burning ambition, entirely unaware of what awaited them.

Even if they did catch on, however, that would be at least half a year down the road.

For now, it was merely enrollment registration.

Besides, Zhou Hui was busy—genuinely busy.

He had just taken over White Jade Capital, then had to inaugurate the preparatory school. And now that the school's affairs had wrapped up, an even more crucial matter lay in wait:

Final exams!

Indeed, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's final exams had arrived.

This examination would comprehensively assess the strength of the students admitted during the autumn minor recruitment and directly reflect the efficacy of Zhou Hui's newly implemented teaching methodology. He couldn't afford to be careless.

As for whatever Carefree Palace's conspiracy was?

Did that even matter?

***

—"Time flies so quickly,"-Mo Bei sighed deadpan.-"It feels like the sect's minor recruitment was just yesterday, yet today is already the final exam."

—"That's because you haven't done a single thing for the past half-year!"-Mo Xi [roasted](# "Literal CN: 吐槽 (tucao) — to snarkily call out, retort, or complain") without ceremony.

Mo Xi felt his foolish older sister was utterly useless.

Over the past half-year, apart from assisting Eldest Senior Sister in drilling a sword formation during the Winter Clothes Festival, she hadn't been of the slightest use—whether during the Immortals' Bazaar auction or guarding the sect while Master was away on "travel"! Even Yi Bei Tea was being managed for her by other disciples; she was practically a useless sect mascot.

—"You certainly did everything,"-Mo Bei retorted coldly.-"You just didn't have time to do your schoolwork. When your exam scores tank, how do you think Master will look upon you?"

—"I—I reviewed!"-Mo Xi stammered, taking a step back.

Still, why did he and Mo Bei have to sit for the exam in the first place?!

All the senior brothers and sister above them had clearly been exempted from this exam—mostly because Ran Deng, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong had yet to return from Blood Demon Hall, while Qu Qianxing had long surpassed the level where his knowledge could be measured by written tests.

Among the direct disciples, only their pair of twins had to sit for the exam. And Mo Xi had never once beaten the stubbornly literal Mo Bei on a test.

Just thinking about this gave Mo Xi a pounding headache.

Mo Bei, on the other hand, glanced at her younger brother with nonchalant dismissal:

[This guy only has petty, street-smart cleverness. Why can't he understand?]

[Before Master, everything else is illusory. Only grades are real... right?]

Thinking this, Mo Bei couldn't help but look toward their Master and senior brothers in the distance.

—"What's wrong?"-Mo Xi asked, debating whether to squeeze in some last-minute cramming before the exam started, but unable to resist getting distracted when he noticed his twin sister's gaze.

—"I'm looking at Eldest Senior Brother,"-Mo Bei said. In the entire sect, from top to bottom, he was the sole individual who never had to worry about grades.

—"Right! Why doesn't Eldest Senior Brother have to take the exam?! If he took it, someone would be guaranteed to finish dead last!"-Mo Xi grumbled resentfully, following Mo Bei's gaze before pausing with a bizarre expression.-"...Though Eldest Senior Brother has indeed been acting weird lately."

—"Right?"-Mo Bei agreed.-"He's actually keeping that sheep, claiming it's an important spiritual pet of Master's that can't be eaten... Since when has he ever cared about that?"

—"There's something even more horrifying!"-Mo Xi glared resentfully at the little lamb in Zhou Hui's arms.-"Just a moment ago, when he walked up beside Master, he casually shoved that sheep straight into Master's embrace!"

[—"...Is *that* the only thing you care about?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

[—"Isn't that horrifying enough?! It's one thing for Eldest Senior Brother not to eat the sheep! But he actually let Master hold it!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[It's definitely horrifying. A sixteen-year-old boy acting like an unweaned infant, obsessing over Master's embrace... Competing with a baby lamb for favor has completely exceeded my realm of comprehension.]

—"Why don't you turn into a cat, claim you've been cursed, and burrow into Master's chest yourself?"-Mo Bei suggested.

—"Great idea... Too bad Master could unravel any ordinary curse in a heartbeat, couldn't he?"-Mo Xi agonized.

[...You're actually going to do that?!]

Mo Bei turned her head in speechless exasperation and walked toward the coral exam platforms.

She felt that chatting with this foolish younger brother for even one more minute carried the grave risk of dragging down her own IQ.

Dragging down her exam scores would be disastrous.

***

[Has this child's IQ dropped?]-Zhou Hui wondered as he looked down at the little lamb nestled in his arms.

Zhou Hui felt that ever since Yuwen Xuan turned into a lamb, his behavior exhibited a degree of regression. For instance, he constantly demanded hugs and cuddles, becoming even clingier than he had been as a human toddler.

That said, to claim he had gone completely stupid wasn't accurate either.

Zhou Hui glanced at the "Yuwen Xuan" standing beside him.

This humanoid "Yuwen Xuan" was naturally not the real thing, but a clay puppet Yuwen Xuan had sculpted before sneaking off to Blood Demon Hall with Zhou Hui, terrified that his younger brothers and sisters would find out.

The craftsmanship was remarkably fine; it hadn't given itself away right to the very end.

And after turning into a lamb, Yuwen Xuan had craftily employed remote puppet-control techniques. He had the fake figure pose as him, while the actual lamb he had transformed into was either held in the arms of the fake "Yuwen Xuan" or cradled by Zhou Hui, feigning the look of an ordinary spiritual pet. Miraculously, he had managed to keep up the deception until now.

[If you have the time and cunning to cook up these crooked schemes, why can't you put a fraction of that effort into studying?]

[A Taotie's brain might not have developed properly, but the brain of his original human body had developed just fine, hadn't it...? Ah, and now it has regressed right alongside it.]

Zhou Hui sighed softly and rubbed the base of the little lamb's ears.

—"Ying-ying,"-the little lamb let out a tender bleat.

Looking at Yuwen Xuan like this, he might very well feel that if he had regressed, so be it—living as a sheep wasn't bad at all. He was utterly devoid of ambition.

Zhou Hui couldn't help but apply a bit more pressure with his fingers.

—"Ying!"-the lamb squealed.

Likely pinched a bit too hard, it jumped up. Yet instead of running away, it forcefully buried its head and ears into Zhou Hui's chest like an ostrich with its tail feathers sticking out, vigorously flailing its little hind hooves.

[No. The more I look at him, the stupider he looks.]

Zhou Hui patted the lamb and looked up.

Qu Qianxing clearly hadn't realized that the foolish little sheep was actually his Eldest Senior Brother. He was conscientiously patrolling the examination grounds:

—"Master, it appears the beginner class disciples have not failed your earnest efforts,"-Qu Qianxing reported.-"Their study progress is faster than that of new disciples in previous years, and their fundamentals are firmer as well."

—"The first half of the year was merely to familiarize them with the routine,"-Zhou Hui said.-"The second half will be the true test."

Though he spoke thus, as his divine sense swept across the disciples' test papers, he was quite pleased.

—"Still, everyone's receptivity to the mathematics and science curriculum is better than I anticipated,"-Zhou Hui remarked.-"It seems promoting it across the preparatory schools shouldn't pose any problems either."

Qu Qianxing instantly turned serious.

Qu Qianxing currently served as the "Director of Studies" for the preparatory schools, a role roughly equivalent to a dean of academic affairs.

Now that Master held concrete expectations for the school's pupils, Qu Qianxing immediately resolved to record the current beginner class disciples' grades as the benchmark target for the school children to strive toward in their first academic year.

—"I will give it my all, Master,"-Qu Qianxing promised.

—"?"-Zhou Hui gave Qu Qianxing a bewildered look. Hadn't he just refrained from demanding anything of this child?

Qu Qianxing didn't explain. His divine sense swept across the coral platforms before he frowned and said to Zhou Hui:

—"However, if this continues, the ones in the direst straits will actually be the newly admitted disciples in the intermediate and advanced classes."

—"No matter. If they still can't keep up next semester, they can simply repeat the grade,"-Zhou Hui said, ruthlessly and without mercy.

***

Entirely unaware of Sect Master Zhou Hui's ruthless decree, the disciples of the advanced class were already dripping with cold sweat.

Their anxiety stemmed precisely from the beginner class disciples.

Take Zhu Rong of the Alchemy Department, for instance.

Before stepping into the examination hall, he had actually been brimming with confidence.

After all, before arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Zhu Rong already possessed a Golden Core cultivation, and had scored well enough on the baseline placement assessment at the start of the semester to enter the advanced class. Subsequently, he had lived through the spiritual energy reservoir crisis and the Immortals' Bazaar—though the Alchemy Department hadn't stolen the spotlight at the bazaar, they had refined massive batches of stable-quality pills to stabilize market prices. Zhu Rong felt he had improved by leaps and bounds compared to before.

For example, answering the test paper in front of him felt far smoother than that initial baseline assessment.

However, mere "feeling" was utterly useless!

Sitting atop his coral platform, Zhu Rong watched blankly as the platforms beside him floated upward one after another. Before long, a vast empty void opened around and beneath him, to the point where he could clearly see the crowds of onlookers gathered below Buyan Palace.

—"Stay calm, stay calm... Maybe the beginner class questions are just exceptionally easy,"-Zhu Rong muttered to himself.

After all, the advanced class, intermediate class, and beginner class did not share the same test paper; in fact, even disciples within the same class didn't receive identical papers. The total score was simply fixed at 500 points across the board. Every set interval, the coral platforms shifted up or down according to score and rank fluctuations.

Thus, it was entirely possible that the beginner class questions were simpler, resulting in a higher average score, which meant everyone's platforms flew higher...

Like hell they did!

Had this been Zhu Rong's first day at Heavenly Spirit Sect, he might have successfully fooled himself with that logic. But after staying here for over half a year and gaining a profound understanding of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's abilities as well as the sect's study atmosphere, he could no longer deceive himself.

Especially regarding the Untiring Immortal Venerable's terrifying prowess!

This supreme powerhouse's brilliance manifested not merely in martial might, but even more so in his question-crafting standards.

If a test was designated as the same difficulty tier, it was truly the exact same difficulty tier, calibrated precisely against the disciples' capabilities.

Within Buyan Palace lay a "Simulation Examination Hall." Before exams, many disciples would enter it to "cultivate." Zhu Rong had followed along inside before. If one selected "identical difficulty" for trial runs, one could score the exact same numerical mark across several consecutive sessions without encountering a single identical question. It could drive an examinee to total despair.

After going a few times, Zhu Rong refused to return. He couldn't fathom what was wrong with Heavenly Spirit Sect's original disciples—running inside from dawn till dusk, practicing what they termed [problem-sea tactics](# "Literal CN: 题海战术 (tíhǎi zhànshù) — the practice of grinding massive volumes of exam questions to master patterns"). It was practically asking to suffer...

At this thought, Zhu Rong's body jerked. His eyes widened as he scanned his surroundings.

He noticed that those at the exact same elevation as himself were indeed newly admitted disciples from the intermediate and advanced classes. Meanwhile, the coral platforms of the original Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples had long since soared high into the sky above.

Naturally! With others working that hard, it would be strange if their grades weren't outstanding!

Compared to them, what did his paltry bit of progress amount to?

If this kept up, wouldn't it be a matter of time before the beginner class disciples overtook him completely?

Zhu Rong was instantly drenched in cold sweat.

***

Equally drenched in cold sweat were intermediate class disciples like Zhao Yuegui.

Zhao Yuegui's scores were slightly better than Zhu Rong's, placing his coral platform somewhat higher, yet it remained visibly below the elevation of the beginner class. Furthermore, Zhao Yuegui had "figured things out" earlier than Zhu Rong.

As early as the Immortals' Bazaar, watching Jing Chenjun's series of business maneuvers, Zhao Yuegui had already realized these "junior brothers" were not to be underestimated.

Back then, however, that realization wasn't particularly tangible. Besides, the midterm exam for the intermediate and advanced classes had consisted of submitting a social practice report rather than a written test, allowing them to dodge a bullet and avoid directly comparing test scores with the beginner class.

Now that they were placed side by side, the disparity became painfully obvious.

[Besides, aren't these arithmetic questions way too absurdly difficult?! This was supposed to be our forte as Eastern Domain cultivators, yet Heavenly Spirit Sect's test questions are utterly ridiculous...]

Zhao Yuegui grew all the more anxious.

Zhao Yuegui was well aware that their "science" textbooks were no different from those used by the beginner and advanced classes.

After all, arithmetic was not a mainstream cultivation method. Aside from diviners who specialized in the *Book of Changes*, even artifact refiners and alchemists who utilized arithmetic as a tool merely learned enough to get by. Typically, a cultivator only thoroughly patched up this tool upon reaching the Void Refinement stage, when they needed to divine heavenly mandates to charge toward the upper three cultivation realms.

But the Heavenly Spirit Sect was different; right from the outset, it placed extraordinary emphasis on mathematics and physical sciences.

Moreover, unlike the purely commercial arithmetic taught by Carefree Palace to serve bookkeeping and trade, Heavenly Spirit Sect seemed to integrate extensive principles of Heaven and Earth into the curriculum. This drastically deepened the difficulty, making it excruciatingly hard to master.

Consequently, regardless of cultivation realm, any disciple who arrived at Heavenly Spirit Sect had to start from scratch in math and science.

Yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect seemingly wished to avoid stirring resentment among the "high-level cultivators" recruited during the minor intake. Thus, they had only implemented the full-time, departmentalized curriculum in the beginner class. The intermediate and advanced classes followed the traditional arrangement of most cultivation sects: large lecture halls where skipping class was effortless, instructors rarely assigned homework, and discipline was nowhere near as strict as in the beginner class.

—Then again, disciples in the intermediate and advanced classes were at least at the middle stage of Foundation Establishment. Aside from the occasional genius, most were over thirty years old; micromanaging them like children would hardly have been appropriate.

As a result, when Zhao Yuegui worked in the Artifact Refinement Hall, he could distinctly feel that in arithmetic, he lagged behind Heavenly Spirit Sect's original disciples.

Still, those disciples had spent years in the Heavenly Spirit Sect and were senior brothers and sisters; lagging behind them was only natural!

But after the midterm exams, when Jing Chenjun and the others earned top marks and secured qualifications for "field practice" at the Immortals' Bazaar, Zhao Yuegui vaguely sensed that these "junior brothers" also possessed remarkably solid foundations in math and science!

As expected, it was the curriculum that made the difference!

He had heard that the sect recently opened a preparatory school for mortal kin and was recruiting assistant instructors. Not only would there be personal pedagogical training from direct disciple Qu Qianxing, but there was even a chance to receive instruction from the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself. Should he give up his "winter break" and go sit in on the classes?

Zhao Yuegui began to seriously contemplate the matter.

***

One week later.

Inside the classroom for assistant instructor training—playfully dubbed the "winter break cram school" by Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples—Jing Chenjun was startled to spot Zhao Yuegui's figure.

—"Senior Brother Zhao, you came too?"-Jing Chenjun greeted cheerfully, pulling Cheng Ziyi to sit down beside Zhao Yuegui.-"Aren't you going home for winter break?"

—"Heh heh, we're cultivating immortality now; what's the point of going back?"-Zhao Yuegui gave a dry chuckle.-"Only a sect as extravagant as Heavenly Spirit Sect would actually let disciples return home."

—"That is true,"-Jing Chenjun sighed.

Cultivators were originally supposed to sever mortal ties. Even disciples who contemplated returning to their hometowns were prepared to spend years unable to go back. However, after the daily Kunpeng Special Express Line was inaugurated, traveling home had suddenly become remarkably convenient.

Jing Chenjun's roommates who were Central Domain locals, like Huang Xiao and Ji Gen, had managed to procure coral seeds for their families during the Immortals' Bazaar, having their parents and clansmen plant coral trees in their hometowns. Now, a round trip took them barely a single day.

For this "winter break," Huang Xiao and Ji Gen had happily gone home to celebrate the New Year.

That left only Jing Chenjun and Cheng Ziyi in the dormitory. As natives of the Northern Domain, they naturally couldn't enjoy the perks of the Kunpeng transit line. Furthermore, given both Jing Chenjun's and Cheng Ziyi's unique family backgrounds, neither was in any rush to return.

Seeing they had nothing to do, Jing Chenjun had dragged Cheng Ziyi along to Buyan Palace.

Zhao Yuegui hadn't wanted a junior brother from his cohort to catch him attending remedial classes. Feigning that he hadn't come to study, but was merely interested in the "assistant instructor side gig," he asked Jing Chenjun:

—"Did you come for the assistant instructor post as well?"

—"Yes! My final exam scores this term turned out quite well, so our instructor suggested I give it a shot,"-Jing Chenjun replied happily.

Indeed, although it was nicknamed a winter break "cram school," only top honor students from the beginner class were eligible to attend.

Realizing this fact, Zhao Yuegui felt a pang of jealousy in his heart, though he couldn't very well voice it. He merely offered a dry laugh:

—"Junior Brother Jing is certainly active! Interning at the Artifact Refinement Hall on ordinary days, not missing out on the Immortals' Bazaar, and now taking a part-time teaching job. Which one is truly your passion? Being too fickle isn't good; you ought to settle on a path early."

Zhao Yuegui had started out being sour, but by the end, his tone held genuine sincerity.

Indeed, when cultivating immortality, identifying the path beneath one's feet was of paramount importance.

Yet Jing Chenjun had sorrows only he knew.

As a mortal with no spirit roots, it was never a matter of him choosing a path—rather, every single path rejected him. Even fields like artifact refinement and commerce that seemingly required less spiritual power would inevitably hit a ceiling the deeper one went.

—"I understand. Thank you for your guidance, Senior Brother,"-Jing Chenjun gave a self-deprecating smile.

Hearing Jing Chenjun thank him so earnestly, Zhao Yuegui felt a bit embarrassed:

—"Though I suppose telling you this now is a bit premature. I heard you all won't choose cultivation manuals until next semester, right? Once your manual and spirit roots are determined, your path will be set. Trying different things now is actually quite good."

—"..."

Spirit roots? Cultivation manuals!

[Senior Brother Zhao truly has a knack for [touching a raw nerve](# "Literal CN: 哪壶不开提哪壶 (lifting the kettle that isn't boiling) — bringing up the exact topic someone wants to avoid")!]

Jing Chenjun's current "impressive final exam scores" had only been achieved by meticulously selecting electives that required zero spellcasting. Consequently, he felt immense trepidation regarding the "manual selection" that all beginner class disciples eagerly anticipated for next semester, terrified that his rootless condition would be exposed and he would be expelled from the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

He did not want to discuss this topic in the slightest!

—"Honestly, even if you don't choose the right path right away, it doesn't matter, does it?"-Cheng Ziyi chimed in from the side. He had been sitting there impatiently, but spoke up upon hearing Zhao Yuegui's words.-"Who knows? You might just stumble upon your path when you least expect it."

Reaching this point, Cheng Ziyi puffed out his chest:

—"Take me, for instance. I feel I'm doing quite well right now!"

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Zhao Yuegui")

Zhao Yuegui stared blankly, wondering what sudden nerve Cheng Ziyi was tripping on. Before Cheng Ziyi could explain, however, an exquisite immortal crane suddenly flew into the classroom.

Paying no heed to the stares of the other disciples, the crane circled a few times above Cheng Ziyi's head.

Cheng Ziyi glared at the crane with annoyance for a moment:

—"Ah, a letter from my family. We'll talk when I get back."

He rose and strode out of the classroom.

Once Cheng Ziyi had left, Jing Chenjun chuckled and whispered to Zhao Yuegui:

—"Cheng Ziyi performed quite well on the practical exam this term; he was selected for the sword cultivator reserve squad."

—"So that's it! He was just trying to show off!"-Zhao Yuegui snorted dismissively.-"It's just the reserves; what's there to be smug about?"

Though he had to admit, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's sword cultivators were indeed rather dashing.

—"Still, I didn't expect him to stick with it,"-Jing Chenjun sighed.

He still remembered how Cheng Ziyi had wanted to quit after just two days of sword practice half a year ago, and how he had clamored, "I'll make it to the intermediate class on the next exam!" Yet that very same Cheng Ziyi had persevered in practicing swordcraft for an entire semester. After two exams, he no longer viewed the beginner class as an insult to his dignity. He had come to realize what he was learning was far from useless, even willing to accompany Jing Chenjun to this "cram school" to master the material on firmer footing.

Then again, Cheng Ziyi could certainly pull it off—after all, he possessed an exceptional spirit root!

At this thought, a faint bitterness stirred within Jing Chenjun's chest.

[Why is the Heavenly Dao so unfair? Why must there be such a divide between those with spirit roots and those without?]

Jing Chenjun couldn't help but wonder.

Surrounded daily by these "immortals-in-training" within a cultivation sect, it was impossible not to think this way.

Still, Jing Chenjun was not one to wallow in such thoughts. Shaking off his gloom, he ceased dwelling on a question with no answer. Just as he was about to steer the conversation with Zhao Yuegui toward something else, a furious roar from Cheng Ziyi echoed down the outside corridor:

—"Go fuck your goddamn motherfucking bullshit straight to hell! Like hell I'm going back!"

Because that preceding curse was so earth-shatteringly resonant, the entire classroom fell deathly silent.

This allowed the latter half of the communication on Cheng Ziyi's side to carry in with absolute clarity:

[—"Tsk, tsk. Junior Brother Cheng, what a foul mouth. Stuck in that petty little backwater sect, have you even abandoned basic manners? That's why I said you shouldn't have gone to a destitute wasteland like the Central Domain in the first place—let alone a sect no one's ever heard of like the Heavenly Spirit Sect."](# "Speaker: Senior Brother from Xiafei Sect")

These words caused expressions of fury to flash across the faces of most disciples in the classroom.

Even if they weren't Central Domain natives, they were disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. How could they allow someone to slander their sect like this?

Cheng Ziyi was all the more infuriated, seemingly about to roar again:

—"You fucking—"

Yet his curse failed to leave his lips. The person on the other end seemed to have cast some spell, their voice cutting in a step ahead:

[—"Are you going to bring up that Immortal Venerable again? That only proves your absolute ignorance! Even if the Heavenly Spirit Sect has one Tribulation Transcendence elder, what does that count for? Our Xiafei Sect boasts six Mahayana powerhouses! Even if you disliked your own sect, choosing another Northern Domain sect would have been fine—at least you'd be closer to the Heavenly Dao Alliance. But a tiny sect in the Central Domain? I bet they rarely even see a Void Refinement cultivator! They're just relying on one or two senior figures to keep up appearances..."](# "Speaker: Senior Brother from Xiafei Sect")

The voice chattered on continuously. Empowered by some bizarre spell, interrupting it proved impossible.

Listening to this, Jing Chenjun knitted his brows. He wasn't particularly enraged by the caller's words—as Cheng Ziyi's roommate, he had long known what Cheng's family attitude was like. Rather, he worried that if that scum kept rambling, it would sour everyone's impression of Cheng Ziyi.

Just as Jing Chenjun was wondering how to intervene, he noticed a senior sister in the classroom waving at him.

He had met that senior sister at the Immortals' Bazaar; she was extraordinarily well-informed.

Jing Chenjun leaned over, and the senior sister whispered a few words into his ear.

Jing Chenjun's eyes lit up in pleasant surprise:

—"Really?"

The senior sister nodded.

Jing Chenjun rushed out into the corridor at once.

Seeing him emerge, Cheng Ziyi hadn't yet realized his conversation had been overheard. Flustered, he scrambled to muffle the endlessly squawking crane, only to see Jing Chenjun sprint straight past him to the architectural schematic panel at the end of the corridor without even glancing his way.

The rooms in Buyan Palace were dynamically movable, often resulting in classrooms from the previous period vanishing into thin air. Hence, at the end of every corridor stood an updated architectural schematic panel. Students needed only tap the map to navigate to their desired classroom.

Yet Jing Chenjun didn't relocate himself. Instead, as if solving a [Klotski sliding puzzle](# "Literal CN: 华容道 (Huarong Dao) — a classic Chinese sliding block puzzle where tiles slide along grid tracks"), he shifted the panels on the schematic map.

In tandem with his movements, the classrooms behind them rumbled aside, and the ceiling above slid away into nothingness. Jing Chenjun raised his hand and pointed toward the sect's mountain gate for Cheng Ziyi.

Completely bewildered, Cheng Ziyi turned his gaze toward the mountain gate—and his eyes instantly went wide.

Right at this moment, the torrent of chatter spewing from the "crane" came to a temporary pause. The speaker on the other end was evidently able to see the surroundings, asking Cheng Ziyi:

—"Junior Brother Cheng, are you listening to me?"

—"I heard you. You just mentioned the Heavenly Dao Alliance and what great powerhouses in the cultivation world, right?"-Cheng Ziyi sneered.

Grabbing the crane by the neck, he swung it around to face the mountain gate:

—"Then tell me: in which Northern Domain sect—or even the Heavenly Dao Alliance itself—could you ever witness a spectacle like *this*?!"

The crane had initially been thrashing about, but upon getting a clear look toward the mountain gate, it suddenly froze stiff in the posture of a roasted duck.

A sky-blanketing canopy of celestial rosy glow!

That was the quintessential signature of high-tier titans taking to the skies.

Accompanied by the rosy clouds that flooded the entire midday firmament, delegations from various major sects—just among those the "crane" could recognize—included the Plum-Bamboo Immortal Couple of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, Central Domain powerhouses like Eternal Villa, the Eastern Domain's Yuke Xuan, and a vast swarm of figures it didn't even recognize who were all at least at the Mahayana stage, flying across the sky in a grand, sweeping procession!

It felt as if [a pantheon of immortals had descended to pay birthday homage](# "Literal CN: 群仙拜寿 (qúnxiān bàishòu) — a classical motif where all immortals gather to offer felicitations and gifts to supreme deities").

Even this "crane," bursting with northern domain major-sect elitism, was struck dumb. It asked in dazed stupefaction:

—"Junior Brother Cheng... y-you're truly at the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Then who are all these immortals...?!"

—"Naturally, with the New Year approaching, they've all come to pay respects to our Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable,"-Jing Chenjun leaned in, beaming from ear to ear.
