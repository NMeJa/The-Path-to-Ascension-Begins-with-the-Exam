# Chapter 73: Fanning the Flames (第73章 拱火)

[← Previous Chapter](072_Chapter_072.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](074_Chapter_074.md)

---

Was Immortal Chess truly that important?

It was not important, yet it was extraordinarily important.

The reason it was not important was that Immortal Chess was merely a game; no matter how entertaining it was, it was still just that.

Had the Heavenly Spirit Sect remained an obscure, backwater sect huddled away in its remote corner, even if every single piece on the board depicted their own disciples as the "Representative Cultivators of the Cultivation World" or concocted an entire pantheon of "Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors," not a single soul would have spared it a second glance.

Yet today's Heavenly Spirit Sect was vastly different from before.

Not only had it hosted the most wildly successful auction in recorded history, but far more critically, under the pretext of that auction, it had planted coral trees—teleportation transit hubs—across the entire Central Domain, rooting them straight into the spheres of influence of every major sect. Even mere mortals could now travel back and forth within a single day.

—Naturally, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had always been capable of teleporting straight into the core of any major sect at a whim. Yet without a conspicuous, tangible marker like a "coral tree" staring them in the face, the vast majority of cultivators had never maintained a clear, visceral awareness of that reality.

After all, a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse was simply too distant—distant enough to feel like a mythical legend.

Take, for instance, the earlier incident where the Palace of Carefree Wandering blockaded the trade routes and demanded everyone take sides. Were those sects truly ignorant of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's terrifying renown? Of course they knew. They knew full well that to a Tribulation Transcendence titan, physical distance was utterly meaningless. Yet every single one of them still harbored wishful thinking:

[It's so far away anyway.]

[I won't be the first bird to stick my neck out.]

[Would a Tribulation Transcendence supreme being really descend upon our little sect in person?]

[Our sect still has two Mahayana elders; we can at least put up a decent fight!]

[Our mountain-protecting grand array was left behind by an ascended patriarch; there's nothing to fear!]

[Even if he is in the Tribulation Transcendence stage, he can't possibly slaughter everyone in the world, can he?]

In short, it was merely the ants [plugging their ears while stealing a bell](# "Literal CN: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — burying one's head in the sand / self-deception"), comforting themselves with the delusion: *He can't see me.*

Yet the moment those coral trees were planted into the soil, everything changed. It signified that not only was the Untiring Immortal Venerable watching them, but he had actively planted his markers right in their front yards.

The most terrifying part was that these markers were not one-time consumables!

Even after the auction concluded, the Heavenly Spirit Sect made no move to retrieve those coral trees. Instead, they forged binding pacts with the local mortals, using the coral trees to designate them as protected wards under their aegis.

At that, the vast majority of sects could no longer sit still.

More than a few sects began harboring the exact same existential terror as Palace Master Li of Carefree Palace:

*The Untiring Immortal Venerable couldn't possibly be planning to conquer the world, could he?!*

Even if he wasn't conquering the world, ruling the entire Central Domain seemed an absolute certainty.

Once burdened with such paranoia, they naturally strained their minds to dissect the hidden meaning behind every single move made by the Heavenly Spirit Sect. And Immortal Chess, serving as the grand opening spectacle of the auction, was parsed and over-analyzed countless times—especially the specific identity behind every chess piece.

Some believed the pieces represented cultivators who had earned the Untiring Immortal Venerable's personal approval.

Others believed the pieces represented sects with whom the Heavenly Spirit Sect sought friendly alliances.

Naturally, there were even those who arrived at an extreme interpretation: the Heavenly Spirit Sect was preparing to enact sweeping retribution against the Central Domain sects for their previous "taking-sides incident," and Immortal Chess was, in truth, an amnesty roster.

In short, no matter how wild their conjectures ran, Immortal Chess was an exceptional probe.

At the very least, through it, one could gauge the Untiring Immortal Venerable's attitude toward their sect. Furthermore, by striving to join this [All-Star](# "Literal CN: 全明星 (quán míngxīng) — All-Star roster") lineup, they could publicly signal that they harbored zero hostility toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and that the previous neutrality during the blockade had merely been a "misunderstanding."

Fairy Xia summarized with a sigh:

—"Everyone was frightened out of their wits by the Immortal Venerable."

—"So complicated! Why must you all turn a little toy into something so utterly convoluted?"-Mengshan Old Freak complained, sprawling limply across the edge of the low table.-"Wouldn't it be better to just say whatever is on your mind directly?"

By this moment, they had already dismissed the juniors of their respective sects and arrived outside Zhou Hui's cave dwelling by themselves.

The current gathering resembled their casual reunions of yesteryear, though the attendance was far larger than in previous years. Roughly twenty-some cultivators were present, the surplus consisting almost entirely of Central Domain high-stage powerhouses—essentially a summit of supreme leadership.

Mengshan Old Freak clearly despised this kind of summit.

He grumbled to Fairy Xia:

—"You too, acting as a matchmaker for such meaningless nonsense."

—"It is a misfortune of the clan!"-Fairy Xia sighed.-"You all know I haven't managed mundane affairs for ages. Then suddenly they knelt outside my cave entrance in a weeping heap, crying that Senior Untiring was going to beat down their gates tomorrow. I thought the sky had collapsed..."

—"And how does that not count as a major crisis?"-a middle-aged cultivator with figure-eight eyebrows chimed in.-"When Carefree Palace was stirring up trouble, every last one of you played dead. Now that Untiring has wrapped up the auction, you all come scurrying out of the woodwork. Aren't you just taking advantage of Untiring's good temper?"

—"Precisely! If you ask me, Untiring should have taken full control of the Central Domain long ago!"-another female cultivator declared boisterously.-"Saves a bunch of mountain monkeys from crowning themselves king."

[You aren't Central Domain cultivators, so [it's easy for you to talk when your back isn't aching](# "Literal CN: 站着说话不腰痛 (zhànzhe shuōhuà bù yāotòng) — easier said than done / talking lightly when unaffected")!]

The Central Domain cultivators led by Fairy Xia wore rather sour expressions.

Yet none dared to utter a word of protest.

Do not be fooled by the notion that cultivators are aloof from fame and worldly concerns. Upon reaching their cultivation realm, every single one of them was an overlord capable of dominating an entire region. Even seemingly wandering eccentrics like Master Lan Pei and Mengshan Old Freak could permanently terminate an entire avenue of development in medicine or spiritual plants with a casual remark.

If Mahayana powerhouses possessed such sway, what need was there to speak of the Tribulation Transcendence realm perched above them?

In the cultivation world, the strong commanded absolute reverence. A Tribulation Transcendence cultivator was an uncrowned sovereign; even if they never showed their face, all cultivators would instinctively operate by their rules.

Hence, there existed a tacit understanding among Tribulation Transcendence titans: [Kings do not meet kings](# "Literal CN: 王不见王 (wáng bù jiàn wáng) — supreme sovereigns never meet directly").

Only the Northern Domain, serving as the heart of the modern cultivation world, housed four Tribulation Transcendence cultivators. The Eastern Domain, owing to the unique circumstances of the Palace of Carefree Wandering, had held three (one of whom had already been slaughtered by Zhou Hui). The remaining three domains—Central, Southern, and Western—each possessed only a single Tribulation Transcendence titan.

Without a shadow of a doubt, Zhou Hui was meant to be the default "law" of the Central Domain.

It was merely that the Central Domain's history was unique, and Zhou Hui had previously paid little heed to administrative rule, giving the various sects room for "independent choices." Now that the Untiring Immortal Venerable wished to reclaim his rightful authority and lay down the rules for the Central Domain, nobody possessed the standing to utter a single complaint.

Fairy Xia forced a smile:

—"That is why we have come to consult Senior Untiring: how exactly should this game of chess be played?"

—"Our Eternal Villa is entirely different!"-at that moment, a strikingly handsome young cultivator chimed in.-"Unlike these unpromising cowards, I came purely for Immortal Chess!"

He cast a coquettish wink toward Zhou Hui:

—"Although my grand-disciple clinched first place in the inaugural Immortal Chess tournament, he is by no means the most exceptional cultivator our Eternal Villa has to offer. Lord Immortal Venerable, won't you consider me instead?"

[You're even more unpromising than the rest of us!]

Everyone present glared at him in exasperation.

—"I do happen to have a set of rules,"-Zhou Hui said with a faint smile.-"Why don't we all play a round?"

The gathered powerhouses instantly straightened their postures, their expressions turning solemn.

***

—"Your Heavenly Spirit Sect seems to have rather few people!"

While the founding ancestors sat in formal reverence before the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Immortal Venerable, Young Master Yun Guoling of Cloud City was casually surveying his surroundings with a dismissive gaze, asking in an ill-intentioned tone.

Such insolence would have been more than enough to prompt Fairy Xia to strangle this unworthy descendant to death the moment they returned home.

Yet Yun Guoling felt nothing amiss whatsoever.

Because of Fairy Xia's status, he was accustomed to seeing high-stage cultivators come and go. Yet because his own cultivation was too meager, he could not fathom the monumental gulf between the Mahayana and Tribulation Transcendence realms—to him, both were titans capable of crushing him to dust with a flick of their aura. In this regard, he was not even the equal of a pampered playboy like Su Xin. Although Su Xin had the Plum-Bamboo couple backing him in the Heavenly Dao Alliance, that central cradle of the cultivation world was ruthlessly obsessed with hierarchy; each realm was divided into tiers, each corresponding to strict privileges, where insubordination was unthinkable. Thus, when Su Xin arrived at Mount Tianling, he hadn't dared to act recklessly.

On the contrary, core disciples of middling "immortal metropolises" and "major sects" had grown so accustomed to seeing their ancestral patriarchs flex unrivaled majesty that they genuinely believed their ancestors were omnipotent sovereigns under heaven.

This mindset was not limited to Yun Guoling; even City Lord Yun Mingyue of Cloud City harbored similar thoughts.

*What Heavenly Spirit Sect? Never heard of it.*

*Untiring Immortal Venerable? Tribulation Transcendence? Sounds formidable enough, but he's never appeared in our ancestor's Cloud Pool, so he can't be that important! Just a loose rogue immortal outside the mainstream upper echelons of the cultivation world.*

*Besides, aren't Tribulation Transcendence titans chased all day by celestial lightning tribulations, having zero leisure to micromanage mortal trifles? So what if we leaned toward Carefree Palace?*

Even when subordinate cultivation clans reported that several villages within their sphere of influence had signed trade pacts with Mount Tianling, Yun Mingyue had paid it no heed. He had even flown into a rage, feeling that the Heavenly Spirit Sect was encroaching on his private domain.

Fortunately, before he had the chance to take action, news arrived that the cultivation clans of Fengqing City had been eradicated root and branch for besieging mortal villages.

Only then did Yun Mingyue realize that the other party possessed a genuine Tribulation Transcendence patriarch.

He had hastily rushed over to hug his clan ancestor's thighs, never expecting that instead of upholding "justice," the ancestors turned pale with fright upon hearing the news, cursed him furiously, and dragged him straight to their doorstep to apologize.

And after personally arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect—witnessing the boundless mist shielding the exterior, the inverted celestial reflection within, a spiritual energy environment entirely alien to the outside world, and seeing his own ancestor holding his breath alongside other clan patriarchs before the Untiring Immortal Venerable—Yun Mingyue finally recognized that he had likely offended someone who must never be crossed.

Intellectually, he understood. Yet deep in his heart, resentment and aggrieved indignation lingered:

*Even if you are a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, can you simply seize another's ancestral domain by force?*

Yun Mingyue vividly recalled the tearful lament of City Lord Feng Lang of Fengqing City:

[—"Brother Yun, today it is a few villages; tomorrow, how will it not be our very cities?!"](# "Speaker: Feng Lang")

Thus, seeing his great-grandson Yun Guoling's haughty attitude, Yun Mingyue made no move to correct him.

Fortunately, the reception disciple dispatched by the Heavenly Spirit Sect—naturally Xiang Rong, Mo Xi's premier right-hand assistant for entertaining such "distinguished guests"—maintained a good-tempered smile:

—"During the winter break, there are indeed fewer people around."

[—"Winter break?"](# "Speaker: Yun Guoling")

—"Yes, our sect implements winter and summer breaks,"-Xiang Rong explained with a chuckle.-"One month in the winter, two months in the summer. Disciples are permitted to return home."

—"Whoa..."-several young cultivators beside Yun Guoling gasped in awe.

Even Yun Guoling's face betrayed a flicker of envy. Born in Cloud City, he naturally cared little about visiting home, but the concept of winter and summer breaks filled him with sheer jealousy—three whole months without having to cultivate! (Naturally, he was entirely oblivious to the staggering volume of daily coursework endured by Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples.) Still, he sneered aloud:

—"Cultivators of the Dao, yet still clinging to mortal dust with [impure six senses](# "Literal CN: 六根不净 (liùgēn bù jìng) — the six sensory organs not purified / lingering worldly attachments")!"

Coming from Yun Guoling's mouth, such words carried zero weight.

After all, as the pampered young master of Cloud City, he had never stepped beyond his family estate since childhood. In moments like these, such second-generation cultivators conveniently forgot to mention "severing mortal ties."

—"Ah, what you say is quite true,"-Xiang Rong replied placidly, offering no debate.

Seeing his nonchalance, Yun Guoling felt a burst of irritation and wanted to say more, but Xiang Rong gestured toward the floating stairs:

—"Esteemed guests, this way please."

The destination Xiang Rong was guiding these high-stage cultivators' "entourages" toward was none other than the public bathhouse.

Yes, the very bathhouse meant for bathing.

Perhaps because Zhou Hui had used the unfinished bathhouse to entertain the Plum-Bamboo couple prior to its completion, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had developed an unspoken tradition of hosting important guests at the bathhouse.

Naturally, half a year had passed, and the bathhouse was now entirely complete.

Aside from a few private suites at the apex reserved exclusively for the Untiring Immortal Venerable, the colossal bathhouse hovering above Huaxu Palace was open daily to the entire sect.

The public bathhouse comprised three tiers in total, each taking the shape of a massive circular disc, stacked gracefully in suspended elevation.

The first tier was a colossal [water amusement park](# "Literal CN: 水上游乐场 / 空中水上乐园 — aerial water amusement park"), completely open without partitioned boundaries, outfitted with spiraling slides and diverse aquatic recreation apparatuses;

The second tier served as the bathhouse restaurant, divided into male and female sections, featuring both private chambers and communal baths, along with tea bars, pastry shops, entertainment pavilions for Immortal Chess, and tranquil meditation chambers—the disciples' favorite venue for whiling away their leisure hours;

The third tier was a breathtaking bathhouse garden. Not only did it feature spirit spring pools endowed with diverse therapeutic properties, artfully separated by lush spiritual vegetation, but flowering vines cascaded over the disc's perimeter, draping all the way down to Huaxu Palace far below. The entire third tier resembled a colossal hanging flower basket suspended in midair, presenting a feast for the eyes even from afar.

Without question, the third-tier garden was the ideal venue for entertaining guests.

Thus, Mo Xi had cleared the third tier of ordinary disciples, directing Xiang Rong to guide the guests up via the floating stairs.

Yet whether it was the floating stairs or the first-tier aquatic park, both utilized an open architectural design. As the guests ascended, they naturally caught sight of off-duty disciples splashing playfully in the water below.

The younger cultivators watched the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples zooming down the winding water slides, their eyes nearly glued to the spectacle.

[That looks like so much fun...]

Initially, these young cultivators merely harbored such innocent thoughts. Yet they were not the sort to indulge in frivolous play, so they told themselves they were definitely not envious (probably). Instead, it was the rich spiritual energy permeating the air that drew their rapt attention.

[—"...Spirit springs?"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Cultivator")-someone asked in stunned surprise.

—"Indeed,"-Xiang Rong introduced with pride.-"This is the masterpiece of our Senior Sister Ran Deng and Senior Brother Guan Ye: the Aerial Water Park. It is powered by four low-grade spirit springs and three colossal spiritual energy water pumps, achieving a fully closed self-circulating system..."

Yun Guoling had zero interest in engineering theory:

—"Wait, you allow ordinary disciples to use spirit springs for frivolous games?!"

—"This is not frivolous play; it is an integral component of their cultivation,"-Xiang Rong corrected solemnly.

[Bullshit! You literally just called it a 'water amusement park'!]

Yun Guoling fumed silently. The disciples of other sects stared down at the water park, their eyes practically crimson with envy.

Even the higher-stage elders among the entourage were secretly shaken.

As the primary controllers and distributors of resources in the cultivation world, they understood far better than juniors what it meant to open spirit springs so lavishly to rank-and-file disciples—it was a terrifying demonstration of how [fabulously wealthy and overbearing](# "Literal CN: 豪横 (háohèng) — swaggeringly wealthy and domineering") the Untiring Immortal Venerable truly was.

Spirit springs, spiritual energy, material resources—this was the universal language that administrators of sects and immortal cities comprehended most acutely.

[Since they possess such abundant spirit spring resources, why must they encroach upon our territory?!]

Yun Mingyue couldn't resist complaining in the depths of his heart.

This unspoken grievance received its answer during the banquet that followed.

As an array of exquisite spiritual delicacies was wheeled out, Yun Mingyue and the others were treated to the long-forgotten satisfaction of "ordinary mortal appetite."

This differed vastly from the banquets typically hosted by high-stage cultivators.

As the City Lord of Cloud City who frequently entertained foreign dignitaries, Yun Mingyue knew the drill well. Banquets hosted by figures like Fairy Xia featured transcendent spiritual teas and divine fruits—ten-thousand-year celestial peaches, five-thousand-year ginseng fruits, and the like.

Undeniably, a mere sliver of such treasures was enough to send ordinary cultivators into a frenzy. Yet it had nothing to do with satisfying culinary appetite; it was purely for advancing cultivation. Furthermore, no matter how delectable such treasures were, a low-stage cultivator would feel bloated and bursting at the seams after merely licking the skin. Yearning to eat yet physically unable to consume it was an agonizing torment.

The spiritual delicacies of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, however, were crafted to genuinely satisfy mortal appetite.

Xiang Rong introduced each dish with exuberant warmth:

—"Consider this [borrowing flowers to offer to Buddha](# "Literal CN: 借花献佛 (jièhuā-xiànfó) — using another's resources to show hospitality"). Many of the ingredients for today's banquet hail from the villages surrounding Cloud City. Owing to the unique regional climate, the spiritual crops grown there boast a remarkably sweet and tender flavor—"

Such praise carried no sense of offense; on the contrary, it filled the Cloud City guests with immense pride.

Even City Lord Yun, who had been brooding over grievances moments earlier, began to second-guess himself due to the youth's utter candor:

*Could we have overthought this? Were they simply looking to purchase cooking ingredients?*

—"Great-Grandfather, couldn't we actually maintain friendly ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-at this moment, Young Master Yun Guoxin of Cloud City whispered softly to Yun Mingyue.-"I don't think they harbor any hostility toward us! When you think carefully about it, they only signed purchasing agreements with a few mortal villages; they never truly occupied those territories! Didn't we simply overthink things?"

—"Overthink things?"-Yun Mingyue snorted coldly under his breath.

He glanced at Yun Guoling, who had acted insolently earlier, only to find the youth prickling his ears to eavesdrop on their exchange. Rather than talking back as usual, the moment he noticed Yun Mingyue's gaze sweeping over, he hurriedly buried his head in his bowl and resumed gorging himself.

[...A single meal of spiritual delicacies was enough to buy you two off?]

Yun Mingyue thought with disdain. Watching Xiang Rong step away to fetch another course, he lowered his voice to lecture his two chosen heirs:

—"This is not a matter of overthinking, but a question of tangible interests. Regardless of circumstances, our Central Domain's primary teleportation array resides in Fengqing City, firmly in the grasp of the Palace of Carefree Wandering. Even if the Heavenly Spirit Sect can temporarily resolve material shortages, are we supposed to sever all contact with the other domains forever?"

—"But Ancestor Xia..."-Yun Guoxin frowned.

—"Ancestors have their own considerations,"-Yun Mingyue replied.-"While we follow her lead, there is no need to rush into declaring our stance."

Yun Guoling and Yun Guoxin exchanged a glance, instantly discerning that their "Great-Grandfather" had already shifted his stance.

His attitude had certainly not been this malleable when he was weeping on his knees beside Fairy Xia's Cloud Pool earlier.

Sycophantic though it sounded, raw strength—including sheer wealth—was indeed what mattered most!

Blissfully unaware of his great-grandsons' internal mockery, Yun Mingyue continued his lecture:

—"One must take the long view—"

*BOOM!*

A deafening roar abruptly detonated across the horizon, violently severing Yun Mingyue's speech.

Similar conversations unfolding across neighboring tables were shattered in an instant.

Startled gasps erupted as everyone turned toward the sound, only to witness the northeastern sky, under broad daylight, blooming with an unnatural shade of violent violet-crimson, as if the celestial vault itself were tearing open:

[—"What happened?!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Cultivator")

[—"Isn't that the direction of Tianqian Mountain?!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Cultivator")

[—"Could Tianqian Mountain be collapsing?!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Cultivator")

The cultivators of the various sects fell into immediate disarray.

Conversely, the younger disciples of Mount Tianling wore calm, unbothered expressions, thoroughly accustomed to such spectacles.

Carrying a newly prepared platter of delicacies to the table, Xiang Rong smiled reassuringly at the Cloud City entourage:

—"Esteemed lords, please pay it no mind. The venerable immortals must be competing for the honor of becoming the strongest chess piece!"

[—"Chess piece?!"](# "Speaker: Cloud City Cultivators")

***

Rewinding time by fifteen minutes.

—"The Grand Immortal Arts Competition of the Strongest Central Domain Cultivators?"-the high-stage powerhouses asked in utter bewilderment.

—"Indeed. Did everyone not feel that the initial Immortal Chess roster was rather hasty? That Immortal Chess alone cannot determine representative cultivators? In that case, why don't we allow all sects and immortal metropolises to hold a proper contest?"-Zhou Hui proposed with a warm smile.

With a casual wave of his sleeve, he unfurled a shimmering light screen, partitioning the Central Domain into distinct territories:

—"We can divide the region into eight competition zones. Within each zone, interested sects and cities can compete across cultivation tiers to determine the strongest cultivators, establishing them as the 'Representatives of the Central Domain' and immortalizing them as the chess pieces for our next edition of Immortal Chess."

—"Hahaha, brilliant idea!"-Mengshan Old Freak roared with laughter.-"Settle it with pure fist-power!"

The other cultivators did not speak immediately, their eyes fixed intently upon Zhou Hui.

Tournaments of immortal arts were commonplace among the major sects; they did not believe for a second that the Untiring Immortal Venerable was merely organizing an ordinary contest of martial prowess.

Sure enough, Zhou Hui continued:

—"It will not solely be about martial strength, but will also encompass prestige, moral reputation, and past deeds."

—"And what does that entail?"-Fairy Xia inquired.

—"Put simply, if someone ascends purely through martial violence yet turns out to be an obscure hermit nobody recognizes, discontent will inevitably brew. Therefore, I propose the competition be divided into two distinct components: a [civil contest and a martial contest](# "Literal CN: 文斗和武斗 (wéndǒu hé wǔdǒu) — civil contest of wits/pedagogy and martial contest of arms"). The martial contest will feature standard round-robin duels to crown the strongest combatants. The civil contest, on the other hand, will allow the general public to become intimately acquainted with them."

Reaching this point, Zhou Hui laid bare his grand design:

—"For instance, introducing one's sect, introducing one's cultivation techniques... Ah, I do not mean to force you to expose your core secret scriptures, but there are surely aspects that can be made public, yes? Such as your most renowned stances, or your personal path of cultivation—"

—"I know! Just like the background lore section on Immortal Chess, right?!"-Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa interjected eagerly. He appeared genuinely, wholeheartedly invested in Immortal Chess.

—"Precisely,"-Zhou Hui smiled warmly.-"I will host public specialized lecture series at Buyan Palace for these cultivators, and compile their teachings into published volumes to generate momentum for the martial contests."

—"Furthermore, to govern the 'civil contest,' I will require each of you to dispatch elite disciples to form an organizing committee to vet all presented techniques—nobody wishes to see an evil cultivator crowned, after all. These disciples will also collaborate to draft the accompanying referee regulations and judicial standards."

In doing so, the glaring weakness plaguing the Heavenly Spirit Sect—its dearth of intermediate cultivators in the [middle three realms](# "Literal CN: 中三境 (zhōngsānjìng) — the intermediate cultivation realms: Nascent Soul, Soul Formation, and Void Refinement") (Nascent Soul, Soul Formation, and Void Refinement) and its inability to exert influence over that stratum—would be resolved in one fell swoop.

Simultaneously, a rigorous academic atmosphere would be cultivated across the realm.

After all, the current community academies served as primary schools; if the Heavenly Spirit Sect's beginner classes were counted as secondary and vocational schooling, the "higher university" tier was entirely vacant. Theoretically, that tier ought to be manned by disciples of the middle three realms. In the Heavenly Spirit Sect's current generation, only Yuwen Xuan had attained Soul Formation—though that fellow... best not mention him! Nevertheless, his second disciple Qu Qianxing would soon mature. Zhou Hui's ambition for Qianxing was certainly not to graduate from a secondary normal school and become a primary school dean; he needed to lay the foundations for a grand university first. Furthermore, Ran Deng and Guan Ye's technical proficiencies were rapidly approaching that threshold.

Zhou Hui reflected with meticulous foresight.

None of the high-stage cultivators perceived Zhou Hui's "nefarious pedagogical scheme." Instead, they viewed it through the familiar lens of territorial influence:

Be it "specialized lectures" or an "organizing committee," were these not premier platforms to promote their respective sects? Moreover, emerging victorious in this tournament would not only wipe away past grievances with a stroke of the pen, but would also signify earning the Untiring Immortal Venerable's personal blessing! It would herald a complete reshuffling of authority among all sects and immortal cities in the Central Domain!

That Immortal Chess was truly profound beyond measure!

The Central Domain powerhouses exchanged glances, their divine senses practically sparking with friction.

Little wonder—they were Mahayana titans attending an annual summit; those gathered here were quintessential political animals. They cared deeply about their sects' standing and thrived on such covert clashes of prestige.

—"Tch, sounds like an agonizingly drawn-out schedule,"-Mengshan Old Freak complained, failing to sense the brewing storm. He seemed to prefer throwing hands immediately.

—"It encompasses the entire Central Domain, after all. I believe providing sufficient preparation time—selecting the Foundation Establishment and Golden Core representatives in five years, followed by the middle three realms in ten years—is quite reasonable,"-Zhou Hui remarked.

No one raised an objection.

The cultivation world's perception of time was inherently vast; five or ten years was merely a blink of an eye to these ancient eccentrics.

In fact, it was rather brisk.

It was only because Zhou Hui had lived a lifetime as a mortal in another world that his sense of temporal urgency had sharpened. Coupled with his incurable nature as an [involution champion](# "Literal CN: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — hyper-competitive grindset master / relentless overachiever") who refused to stay idle, he was disinclined to let matters drag on.

Zhou Hui smiled:

—"However, there is one duel that can be held this very instant."

[—"What?"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

—"The Mahayana realm representative cultivators of the Central Domain!"-Zhou Hui said, smiling warmly at the gathered masters, his gaze lingering particularly on Villa Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa.

Had this fellow not been clamoring non-stop to join the Immortal Chess package?

Mengshan Old Freak's eyes lit up, and he cheered:

—"Splendid! The most renowned Mahayana cultivators of the Central Domain are all assembled right here! We can settle who is the strongest right now!"

—"Isn't that a bit too hasty?"-a cultivator protested reluctantly.

They were regional hegemons; how could they simply spar at a moment's notice?

Even Foundation Establishment juniors had to undergo both civil and martial contests!

—"It is not hasty in the least,"-Zhou Hui countered smoothly.-"As the supreme representatives of the cultivation world, you should naturally lead the charge. Surely none of you wish to hear people say in the future that the Central Domain's Mahayana representatives were filtered through petty rounds of elimination? On the contrary, every senior present is already a representative cultivator of the Central Domain, an immortal of the highest order. Crafting your chess pieces is merely a matter of sequential order."

Hearing this, everyone thought:

*That is quite true!*

At their echelon, they were already the undisputed representatives of the Central Domain without needing to prove it; it was merely a question of who went first...

Wait, sequence mattered immensely!

Who knew how many iterations this tournament would run? Anyone could see that the earlier one debuted, the greater the celestial glory and attention commanded by those pioneering Mahayana chess pieces!

And then, Zhou Hui added:

—"Immortal Chess is contested between two opposing factions: Red and Black. Therefore, we can select two Mahayana cultivators to serve as the respective champions of the Red and Black camps. They will also act as the promotional figureheads for the entire grand tournament, letting the world witness what caliber of cultivator they should revere as role models..."

*Role models!*

This time, before Zhou Hui could even finish, someone blurted out with urgent enthusiasm:

[—"Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable, do you have a suitable sparring ground here?"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Cultivator")

—"You may proceed to Tianqian Mountain,"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"Starting right now?!"-Mengshan Old Freak, who had been whining earlier, was dumbfounded by this sheer efficiency.
