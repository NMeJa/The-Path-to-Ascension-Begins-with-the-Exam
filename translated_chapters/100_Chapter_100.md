# Chapter 100: [Practical Function](<# "Literal CN: 具体作用 (jùtǐ zuòyòng) — specific purpose / concrete practical function; chapter title">) (第100章 具体作用)

—"Master, what kind of pills are you preparing to refine?"-Mo Xi asked, tugging at Zhou Hui's hem.

Because of the "nightmare," Mo Xi clung to his Master exceptionally tightly, almost squishing the little lamb nestled in the crook of Master's elbow, so angering the black lamb that it let out indignant *ying-ying* bleats, trying to gnaw on Mo Xi's hair.

Yet because Xi'er was naturally clingy to begin with, Zhou Hui didn't think anything was amiss.

—"I am preparing to refine karmic fortune,"-Zhou Hui answered.

He took out from his sleeve the dharma treasure gourd that had helped Qu Qianxing gather karmic fortune and aimed it at the alchemy furnace.

Instantly, purplish-red liquefied crystalline karmic fortune, resembling foul clotted blood, surged out from the gourd like a violent torrent.

Seeing karmic fortune of a color similar to that in his dream, Mo Xi instinctively gripped Master's hem even tighter. But he soon noticed that this karmic fortune was different from that in the dream; there was far—

Far, far more of it!

The gourd seemed bottomless; it poured continuously for half an hour without emptying, to the point where the interior of the largest alchemy furnace in the alchemy chamber—reputedly capable of refining an entire miniature world—was nearly filled to the brim.

[Where on earth did Master get so much karmic fortune?!]

Mo Xi felt his teeth ache just looking at it.

In his nightmare, that terrifying karmic fortune choking the sky above Heavenly Spirit Sect had required sacrificing the entire sect, including Fifth Senior Brother with his True Dragon body, to gather.

Yet with a casual pour from Master, several hundred times more karmic fortune than in the nightmare came pouring out.

[How many Fifth Senior Brothers would have to be sacrificed to the heavens to make this much?!]

Mo Xi instinctively glanced over at Lu Shulong.

Lu Shulong was speaking with Qu Qianxing; receiving such a ghostly, somber stare from Mo Xi, he couldn't help shivering.

Lu Shulong looked back in bewilderment, only to see the little black lamb take a mouthful of Youngest Junior Brother's hair, whereupon the two little fools immediately began tussling with each other. This was also a common (?) sight, nothing worth fretting over. What truly drew Lu Shulong's gaze was the purplish-red karmic fortune inside the alchemy furnace.

—"Second Senior Brother, what did you and Master do? How did you collect so much karmic fortune?"-Lu Shulong asked.

Qu Qianxing also glanced at the alchemy furnace, but had no desire to mention how he had suddenly "comprehended" a demonic art, only speaking vaguely:

—"Oh, we went on a trip to the Immortal Arts Assembly."

—"The Immortal Arts Assembly? Is the Immortal Arts Assembly such a terrifying competition?"-Lu Shulong exclaimed in shock.-"With this much misfortune, didn't every single contestant in the entire Central Realm's arenas get wiped out in a [total party kill](<# "Literal CN: 团灭 (tuán miè) — total party kill / wiped out to the last man; gaming slang for an entire team being eliminated">)?"

—"How could that be?!"-Qu Qianxing was actually startled by Lu Shulong's words!

Every contestant in the Immortal Arts Assembly dead?

What tier of demonic cult trap would that even be?!

Their Heavenly Spirit Sect could declare war on the entire cultivation world right this second!

However, speaking of how this karmic fortune had been gathered, Qu Qianxing's entire face lit up.

—"Back then, Master used an... art of unknown origin. With just a gentle cycle of circulation, he stirred all the nearby karmic fortune,"-Qu Qianxing recalled the scene at that time, rarely displaying an excited expression.-"Not only the karmic fortune generated by the Immortal Arts Assembly, but even the forgotten resentful grievances from the ancient battlefield beneath the Abyssal Sea were churned up. The sky at that time was like a reflection of the ancient battlefield of gods and demons, forming a corona of karmic fortune. Truly: *'Immortal clouds and abyssal shadows glow ablaze with the sun; how many years have passed as stars shift and seasons turn.'*"

—"That is truly a pity; I didn't get to see it with my own eyes,"-Lu Shulong sighed.

He then showed some worry:

—"Unfortunately, that karmic fortune is misfortune, right? If Master uses it, will it cause some bad repercussions for him?"

—"That is precisely what I admire the most!"-Qu Qianxing said excitedly.-"Under such circumstances, Master was still able to control it! I felt I was familiar enough with that cultivation technique, circulating it almost like a bodily instinct, yet even I couldn't catch the exact instant of karmic transformation to halt it midway. But Master was only testing it for the very first cycle, yet he terminated it at the most precise moment, and even reversed the direction of the karmic fortune, gathering every single drop into the gourd without leaving a trace!"

Yes, exactly as Qu Qianxing said.

At that time, Zhou Hui had obtained Qu Qianxing's "demonic art" and tested circulating it for one cycle, immediately drawing in karmic fortune from half the Central Realm. If converted directly into cultivation, it would have been enough to summon a heavenly tribulation straight onto Zhou Hui.

However, Zhou Hui had endured tribulation after tribulation, mastering control over his own cultivation to a razor-sharp degree. At that moment, he precisely pinned down the circulation of the technique, preventing the karmic fortune from actually being absorbed by himself, and instead smoothly guided it into the gourd.

Also, because the volume of this karmic fortune was so immense, and as "misfortune" it wasn't suitable to leave sitting for long, Zhou Hui changed his plans, bringing Qu Qianxing, Qingyang Qingyu, and the others back early, heading straight for the Alchemy Hall to refine and neutralize this karmic fortune.

This was why Mo Xi had spotted Zhou Hui and the others in the Alchemy Hall.

At this point, Qu Qianxing felt a pang of melancholy:

—"I wonder how many lifetimes I will have to cultivate before I can achieve such [effortless mastery](<# "Literal CN: 举重若轻 (jǔ zhòng ruò qīng) — handling heavy weights as if they were light; effortless composure and mastery">)."

—"...Leave that sort of demonic art to Master in this lifetime. Second Senior Brother, you have other goals!"-Lu Shulong countered upon hearing this.-"Cultivation requires singular focus; the greatest taboo is being [fickle and divided](<# "Literal CN: 朝三暮四 (zhāo sān mù sì) — three in the morning and four in the evening; blowing hot and cold / shifting goals and lacking focus">). Isn't that what Second Senior Brother always lectures us about?"

—"That's easy to say..."-Qu Qianxing sighed.

For some reason, seeing that "demonic art" handled like a plaything in Master's hands—started whenever he wanted, stopped whenever he pleased, extracting only what was needed and severing what was not—Qu Qianxing, amidst his admiration and yearning, felt a profound sense of regret deep within his soul.

This regret naturally did not stem from being unable to cultivate such a "demonic art," but from being not strong enough himself.

[If I had mastered this technique like that back then, perhaps I truly could have fulfilled my wish...?]

[Wait... Back then? Perhaps? Fulfilled my wish?]

Qu Qianxing was perplexed by this sudden thought for a long time, unable to make sense of why he couldn't let go of this regret.

Seeing Qu Qianxing's dispirited look, Lu Shulong attempted to comfort him:

—"Second Senior Brother..."

—"Wait, how did you know it was a 'demonic art'?!"-Qu Qianxing suddenly lifted his face, looking sharply at Lu Shulong.-"I didn't tell you what kind of art it was, did I? Why does it feel like you're very familiar with it?"

—"...One glance is enough to tell!"-Lu Shulong defended himself, pointing at the alchemy furnace stained in reddish-black.-"Don't forget, I am a dragon after all. I'm much more sensitive to karmic fortune than you are, Senior Brother."

—"Still..."-Qu Qianxing started.

—"Shulong,"-Zhou Hui's voice called out.

—"Ah, Master is calling me,"-Lu Shulong said.

Without waiting for Qu Qianxing to say anything more, Lu Shulong hurried toward Zhou Hui.

Qu Qianxing wanted to say something further, but looking at the karmic fortune in the furnace, he held his tongue.

An inexplicable technique, a sudden pang of regret... everything felt strange today. It was better not to overthink things.

Qu Qianxing told himself this and circulated the *Nine Thunder Nirvana Scripture* Master had bestowed upon him. As golden lightning flowed through his meridians, the unpleasant misfortune was swept clean along with it.

Qu Qianxing instantly felt refreshed and invigorated:

[Right, Master is still here; there's nothing to worry about.]

***

—"So, all of this karmic fortune is being given to me?!"-Lu Shulong's eyes widened as he asked in astonishment.

—"Not given entirely to you—it is for you to refine,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"What's the difference between that and giving it to me?"-Lu Shulong asked bluntly.

In the major sects, the Alchemy Hall was a [lucrative sinecure](<# "Literal CN: 肥缺 (féiquē) — fat post / lucrative sinecure; a comfortable position with high perks and profit">), and that was just how things worked.

After all, alchemy was a probabilistic affair. No matter which sect handed ingredients to an alchemist, they would always budget a "failure allowance." Add to that the remuneration paid upon successful concoction, and generally speaking, if an alchemist's skill was high enough, roughly one-fourth of a batch ended up lining their own pocket.

Naturally, Lu Shulong would never deliberately fail to pocket illicit profits, but Zhou Hui had always believed that innovation and refinement in pills were essential. In the past, he had been exceptionally generous to the Alchemy Hall. Although in the past year he had begun requiring the Alchemy Hall to submit project proposals, as long as Lu Shulong provided sound reports and experimental data for each trial round, virtually none were ever rejected.

Even so, Lu Shulong was still stunned by Zhou Hui's generosity this time.

This was karmic fortune!

Karmic fortune!

Among all resources in the cultivation world, karmic fortune was the hardest to gather.

As an entity capable of swaying the destiny of oneself and others, never mind the claim that misfortune was inherently bad—even without refining it, releasing just a tiny wisp during battle was enough to alter the outcome of a fight! For certain demonic arts, even a sliver of karmic fortune was valuable enough to warrant slaughtering an entire sect or an entire domain.

And the process of refining this karmic fortune was itself a massive elevation for the alchemist.

Especially for the Dragon race.

Just as Lu Shulong had said, a True Dragon inherently possessed immense karmic fortune.

In theory, with a True Dragon's blessing, Heavenly Spirit Sect should have summoned wind and rain at will. Alas, whether because Lu Shulong was too young—speaking of which, as fellow divine beasts, Lu Shulong was much younger than Yuwen Xuan, yet for some reason appeared far more mature in interpersonal worldly ways; could it be because the Dragon race was an innate primordial spirit, endowed with greater [cranial volume](<# "Literal CN: 脑容量 (nǎo róngliàng) — brain capacity / cranial volume">) than a Taotie? Zhou Hui was perplexed by this as well—or because this world lacked sufficient spiritual energy, Lu Shulong didn't actually have much karmic fortune value.

However, if he were allowed to refine this karmic fortune, things would be completely different.

Karmic fortune attracted karmic fortune.

At least two-thirds of the karmic fortune here would be refined and absorbed by Lu Shulong himself, becoming part of his own destiny.

Therefore, Lu Shulong wondered if he had misunderstood Master's intentions.

Even if Master wanted to refine this karmic fortune, there was no need to use such a "wasteful" method.

Lu Shulong asked in surprise:

—"I thought you intended to alter Second Senior Brother's karmic fortune?"

—"I do have that intention, but in this state, do you think your Second Senior Brother could swallow it directly?"-Zhou Hui sighed.

Lu Shulong looked at the cauldron as well.

Indeed.

Eat this, and he'd immediately fall into demonic deviation again.

Lu Shulong fell silent.

Zhou Hui continued:

—"However, there is a way to deal with this misfortune. Back then, having your Second Senior Brother purify this misfortune using the method for refining True Immortal Blood was effective, but later I realized I had taken the wrong approach. Compared to True Immortal Blood, when it comes to transforming karmic fortune, the effect of True Dragon blood might be even greater—"

Saying so, Zhou Hui produced a drop of blood.

It was a drop of pale golden blood, imbued with rich karmic fortune. In theory, such blood belonged only to an adult dragon commanding its own aquatic domain, yet Lu Shulong smelled his own scent upon it.

He realized: that was the [essence heart-blood](<# "Literal CN: 心头血 (xīntóu xuè) — blood from the tip of the heart, the purest vital essence of a cultivator or divine beast">) he had offered Master previously!

Back then, Master had said he would tailor a set of cultivation techniques for him; it seemed the results were finally here.

Zhou Hui indeed said:

—"I used the Taotie heritage as a foundation and modified it. You can use this technique to refine karmic fortune, transforming the misfortune into purer karmic fortune, and then concoct pills for your Second Senior Brother."

Yet Lu Shulong was still somewhat hesitant:

—"Even so, during the refinement process, I will still consume the vast majority of the karmic fortune."

Or rather, with the Taotie heritage incorporated, he would devour even more.

That way, instead of helping Second Senior Brother alter his luck, he would be the one reaping the greatest benefits.

—"Isn't that what Second Senior Brother was supposed to compensate Fifth Senior Brother for anyway?!"-Mo Xi suddenly blurted out.

Everyone looked at Mo Xi in bewilderment.

—"What are you talking about, Xi'er?"-Zhou Hui asked, puzzled.

—"Ah, no, nothing,"-Mo Xi froze for an instant, slapping a hand over his mouth.

Just a moment ago, for some reason, he had mistaken the nightmare for reality.

Zhou Hui gave Mo Xi a strange look, appearing thoughtful. Qu Qianxing, on the other hand, took the initiative to speak:

—"Still, that's not wrong! Since we're troubling Shulong to concoct pills for me, he ought to receive compensation in the first place."

Lu Shulong looked at Qu Qianxing and gave a faintly bitter smile.

—"If that's the case, then I won't be polite,"-he said slowly.

***

—"Xuan'er, don't you think several of your junior brothers were acting a bit strange just now?"-Zhou Hui asked, walking slowly toward his immortal abode and kneading the black lamb in his hands like a hand-warmer.

The little black lamb was kneaded so comfortably that it let out little snorts, only answering after a good while:

—"I don't think there's anything strange about them. Haven't they always been uncute like that?"

—"...Says who? Your junior brothers are all very cute,"-Zhou Hui countered.

[It's just that they seem to have retained some unnecessary residual emotions, especially Xi'er,] Zhou Hui thought to himself.

However, regarding wiping his disciples' memories with every reincarnation cycle, Zhou Hui wasn't certain it was right.

Although this was an inevitable condition of the [*Seven-Life Cyclic Metempsychosis Art*](<# "Literal CN: 七世转轮功 (Qī Shì Zhuǎnlún Gōng) — Seven-Life Cyclic Metempsychosis Art; Zhou Hui's core rebirth cultivation technique">), and he personally didn't want his disciples to remember those painful memories, from the disciples' own perspective, did they truly consider forgetting a good thing?

Just like in the third life, when they had bitterly resented Zhou Hui for "abandoning" them.

That belated [rebellious teenage phase](<# "Literal CN: 叛逆期 (pànnì qī) — rebellious phase / adolescent defiance stage">) had even bled into the fourth life.

No one would want their path chosen for them, right? Even if the person making the choice was their most revered Master.

Yet looking down at the little black lamb in his arms, exposing its belly and nudging Master to rub it, Zhou Hui felt he was overthinking things.

Among all his disciples, the one who bore grudges the most wasn't Qu Qianxing with his eidetic memory, nor was it the outwardly petty Mo Xi, but Yuwen Xuan, his eldest disciple.

If someone said a bad word about him, even if this brat forgot the content, he would eventually find a chance to retaliate.

In the third and fourth lives, during the "abandonment by all" and mutual betrayal, the one who blackened the most severely was this guy.

Yet looking at its foolish appearance now, it clearly remembered nothing at all; otherwise it wouldn't be so well-behaved right now.

Thinking this, Zhou Hui felt relieved.

By this time, he had returned to his immortal abode. He placed the little black lamb onto the soft cushion before the low desk and patted its back:

—"Go play on your own!"

Yet the little black lamb hadn't had enough coddling; it trotted behind Zhou Hui on its little hooves: *clop-clop-clop*.

It followed Zhou Hui into the study, watching Master place a glass tank onto the low desk.

The little black lamb hopped repeatedly on the floor before finally leaping onto the desk, discovering that inside the glass tank was a "scale model" of the White Jade Capital, its buildings micro-carved miniature replicas in white jade.

Looking closely, however, one would notice that not only were the buildings identical to the White Jade Capital, but there were even "tiny figures" moving about inside, as though they were its residents.

In truth, they were the guardians of the White Jade Capital.

If Yuwen Xuan had accompanied Zhou Hui to the very end of the trials in the White Jade Capital, he would have seen the soldiers lining both sides of the grand avenue, waiting as part of the trials for the arrival of the new "Holy Sovereign."

Now that the "New Holy Sovereign" had emerged, these guardians of the past no longer needed to test anyone. Coupled with the city's lack of energy, they had nearly faded into nonexistence. Only because Zhou Hui had nourished them with his own spiritual power for a time were they sustained, though they wandered along the street sides like ghosts, appearing somewhat lost and bewildered.

—"Master, these ghosts consume a lot of spiritual power, don't they?"-Yuwen Xuan observed for a while before saying.-"Why don't I just eat them up? That way you won't have to spend a drop of spiritual power at all."

As it spoke, drool practically dripped from its mouth.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui smiled:

—"Why don't you try taking a bite and see?"

—"..."-The little lamb saw Master's smile, instantly slurped its drool back in, and neatly tucked its four legs underneath itself.

Its intellect had dropped after turning into a lamb, but it wasn't completely gone! The little black lamb knew full well what fate awaited it if it ate those soldiers! It certainly didn't want to be hung on a peach tree for a hundred years to become [smoked mutton jerky](<# "Literal CN: 烟熏羊肉干 (yānxūn yángròugān) — smoked dried mutton jerky">)!

Zhou Hui smiled, once again bringing out his karmic fortune gourd and aiming it at the "model" of the White Jade Capital.

Naturally, Zhou Hui wouldn't pour misfortune into it.

This time, what surged from the gourd was no longer purplish-black karmic fortune, but sparkling, iridescent colors.

Zhou Hui was meticulous with this karmic fortune.

He carefully allocated different colors of karmic fortune to different locations in the White Jade Capital. The areas receiving the most karmic fortune were the White Jade Capital's Academy and its Martial Arts Drill Grounds.

The Academy was empty for now, showing no immediate effects.

However, once the karmic fortune condensed over the Drill Grounds, the soldiers wandering the streets seemed to sense something; no longer roaming aimlessly, they slowly marched toward the Drill Grounds.

The figures of the soldiers began to gradually solidify.

Watching this, the little black lamb gulped:

—"Master, this is..."

—"Karmic fortune from the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"Qianxing's condition wasn't great, but the young lady of the Qingyang clan is quite good, not to mention she possesses the bloodline of the White Jade Capital. Thus, we still gathered relatively pure karmic fortune. Although the volume is still small, the Immortal Arts Assembly has only just begun. If this continues, the army of the White Jade Capital can be completely revived."

Speaking of this, Zhou Hui grew somewhat cheerful:

—"I originally expected the academy or the secret realms to yield results first, but I hadn't expected the Immortal Arts Assembly to produce such direct effects."

[What?]

[There was actually an objective like this?!]

[...You clearly said you were just taking us out to have fun!]

The little black lamb's eyes went wide, glaring at Zhou Hui with a profound sense of betrayal.

Zhou Hui paid no heed to his eldest disciple's expression, murmuring calculations:

—"Still, it's not just the Drill Grounds. The academies, academic halls, and even the academic journals are crucial to reviving the Academy. Today's performance by that contestant at the Immortal Arts Assembly was a [shameless hard advert](<# "Literal CN: 硬广 (yìng guǎng) — hard advertising / direct, blatant product placement">) for the journal; hopefully it will boost its circulation..."

***

In truth, that "hard advert" did far more than promote journal sales—it caused the journals to be snapped up until not a single copy remained!

At that time, not long after the seal on that layer had been broken, while matches at the Immortal Arts Assembly were still ongoing, clever individuals had already caught on and rushed straight to Heavenly Spirit Sect's Immortal Chess Pavilion in Cloud City, buying out all the journals.

By the end of the first day's matches, the tale of the Nascent Soul tier—*"Immortal Venerable secretly restrains the Master of Ceremonies; Lu Zhen holds the arena to challenge the Northerners in succession"*—spread across the entire venue and was even circulated as a storyteller's yarn. Not just in Cloud City, but across the entire Central Realm, journals were completely sold out.

Discussing the contents of the journals became one of the hottest topics:

[—"That journal is related to the Immortal Arts Assembly!"](<# "Speaker: Excited Cultivator">)

[—"Isn't that obvious? Everyone knows the journal is an arena where high-stage grandmasters engage in literary duels."](<# "Speaker: Skeptical Cultivator">)

—"No! I mean the arena stages are related to the journal! Not only is Manor Lord Qingyang's paper tied to the 'Bones of the True Immortal' map, but look at Hermit Zui Liu's paper *A Study on the History of White Jade Capital Folding Fans*—it interacts directly with today's Qi Condensation arena map, [Broken Bridge](<# "Literal CN: 断桥 (Duàn Qiáo) — Broken Bridge; Qi Condensation stage arena map corresponding to Hermit Zui Liu's paper">)! And this paper here..."-the first cultivator insisted.

[—"Whoa, incredible!"](<# "Speaker: Astonished Cultivator">)

[—"Thinking carefully, it's not surprising, is it? Isn't arranging the arena stages according to the grandmasters' papers very fitting for the occasion?"](<# "Speaker: Pondering Cultivator">)

[—"So, if we use the cultivation techniques or immortal arts of those authors, we'll have an advantage during the matches?!"](<# "Speaker: Hopeful Contestant">)

[—"What immortal arts does Eternal Villa excel at?"](<# "Speaker: Inquiring Cultivator">)

[—"Offering a massive reward for works by Manor Lord Qingyang!"](<# "Speaker: Eager Buyer">)

Such discussions spread across the Central Realm's cultivation world.

As mentioned earlier, news in the cultivation world could travel very slowly, or extraordinarily fast.

Evidently, news of the Immortal Arts Assembly was the hottest of hot topics, the kind that warranted every participant sending out hundreds of origami cranes (not limited to paper cranes, but other communication talismans as well) to broadcast their thoughts.

These discussions quickly swept the entire Central Realm.

Naturally including the major sects.

Especially sects ambitious about achieving good results; they certainly wouldn't miss these tidbits.

However, their ambitions lay not in the preliminary rounds, but in the subsequent regional tournaments, and their deductions were tailored accordingly.

For instance, Thousand Beasts Hall had a similar discussion:

—"...That is everything that took place at the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-a disciple reported faithfully.

—"It seems the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Heavenly Spirit Sect aren't entirely on friendly terms after all,"-Yan Bailang sneered.

Elder Bai Jun said:

—"It is merely that the Immortal Venerable shares a decent relationship with the Dao companion couple; his dealings with the rest may not amount to much. Hall Master, should we seize this opportunity to curry favor with the Heavenly Dao Alliance...?!"

—"Are you an idiot? Is the squabble between such towering figures something we can meddle in?"-Yan Bailang saw things quite clearly in this matter.-"Rather than that, it would be better to inquire into the Organizing Committee roster and understand what cultivation techniques those judges specialize in."

—"We are already gathering that information,"-Bai Jun said sheepishly.

Yan Bailang gave a perfunctory nod and looked at the others:

—"Do any of you have other thoughts?"

—"Hall Master, I believe our focus shouldn't just be the Organizing Committee,"-Xia Bing stepped forward to speak at this moment. Her securing a placement in the Immortal Arts Assembly was already beyond dispute, so she analyzed the matter with utmost diligence.-"Rather, it is precisely because this is the first issue of the journal that there are so many papers from the Organizing Committee. In the future, these journals will likely feature more papers from the competing grandmasters, won't they? If so, in the regional tournaments, the arenas might very well be adjusted according to the papers of the grandmasters engaged in the literary duel."

—"Makes sense,"-Yan Bailang nodded.

His expression when looking at the journal turned complex:

—"Then this journal is truly something extraordinary!"

Preaching the Dao and imparting knowledge always carried a certain amount of karmic merit.

However, no cultivator ever treated this as their primary path of cultivation. After all, anyone who had ever taught disciples knew: it drained one's spirit and drained one's lifespan. You could pour your heart out and still fail to produce a decent disciple, not to mention the adage about how you could [teach the apprentice and starve the master](<# "Literal CN: 教会徒弟饿死师父 (jiào huì túdì è sǐ shīfu) — common proverb warning that once disciples learn all the master's secrets, they put the master out of business">). It was universally recognized as an endeavor of high effort and low return. Unless one's cultivation reached a dead-end bottleneck and needed a push from karmic merit, most people wouldn't actively invite trouble upon themselves.

But this journal was marvelous!

Tied to the Immortal Arts Assembly like this, countless people would voluntarily learn even just to win matches. The quality might not be top-tier, but it made up for it by [scaling through sheer volume](<# "Literal CN: 走量 (zǒu liàng) — moving high volume; prioritizing sales/scale quantity over per-unit margin">)!

As long as one taught, without having to take responsibility—could there be a better method of [mass harvesting](<# "Literal CN: 收割 (shōugē) — harvesting / shearing chives (割韭菜); internet slang for exploiting and milking a captive audience">) (X) teaching than this?

Yan Bailang had originally considered the "literary duel" a burden, but seeing the potential harvest the journal could bring, his heart couldn't help burning with desire.

Unfortunately, he had always been on the "learning" end, lacking a suitable thesis topic.

However, things would be different in the future. The project he was currently "researching" would yield him ample rewards—in every aspect!

Thinking of this, Yan Bailang's pupils trembled as he gazed toward his "research subject."

Xia Bing felt a sudden chill from his gaze.

Yet blissfully unaware, she offered a smile:

—"Hall Master."

Yan Bailang naturally wouldn't betray any deeper emotion. Smiling approvingly at the maiden he outwardly trusted the most right now, he said:

—"Indeed. Whether for victory in the literary duel or for intelligence on the martial arena stages, we must gather relevant intelligence. Bai Jun—"

[Why does he always pick on me?!]

—"I shall see to it, Hall Master,"-Elder Bai gave a dry smile.

He was an old hand who had followed Yan Bailang for many years, knowing full well what kind of advice his Hall Master favored most.

Rolling his eyes with a sly smile, Bai Jun said:

—"However, such information is surely kept confidential by Heavenly Spirit Sect, making it very difficult to collect. I actually have a good idea—"

The others looked at him.

Bai Jun said:

—"In this Immortal Arts Assembly, Heavenly Spirit Sect acts as both referee and contestant. Therefore, wouldn't it suffice if we set our sights firmly on their disciples? If we know who their participating direct disciples are—"

—"First Disciple Yuwen Xuan, Fifth Disciple Lu Shulong, and Sixth Disciple [Mo Bei](<# "Literal CN: 莫悲 (Mò Bēi) — Mo Bei; sixth disciple of Heavenly Spirit Sect, public tournament alias of Mo Xi">),"-Xia Bing cut in.

[—"What?"](<# "Speaker: Bai Jun">)

—"Information on Heavenly Spirit Sect's direct disciples participating in the Immortal Arts Assembly,"-Xia Bing said, taking out several thin sheets of paper.-"They have no intention of concealing this matter at all, and have already begun [generating hype](<# "Literal CN: 造势 (zào shì) — generating momentum / whipping up publicity and hype">)."
