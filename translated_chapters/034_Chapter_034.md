# Chapter 34: Gains and Losses (第34章 得与失)

—"Hmph, this so-called trial is nothing special after all,"-Qingyang Yang of the Eternal Villa sneered, lifting his chin.

He was currently standing inside the trial chamber leading into the secret realm.

This was a cubical chamber measuring three paces by three paces by three paces. The surrounding walls were fashioned from rough mountain rock; starkly distinct from the magnificent grand hall outside, it had clearly been hewn directly out of the sheer cliffside. As a result, it was exceedingly dark and gloomy. Such caverns (rooms) situated amid mountains dense with Yin energy were naturally the most prone to attracting [malevolent mountain specters and wicked wraiths](# "Literal CN: 魑魅魍魉 (chīmèi-wǎngliǎng) — demons, monsters, and evil spirits of mountains and waters").

In fact, inside this little chamber, more than a dozen pitch-black hands had already reached out from the shadows.

Some sprouted razor-sharp claws, viciously swiping toward Qingyang Yang's face; some were extraordinarily long and spindly, stretching straight out to strangle his throat; others were exceedingly thick and stout, bristling with coarse black hair, attempting to seize his legs.

Yet no matter how gruesome these hands appeared, they were now frozen dead in their tracks. Everything in the room, including the stone walls, was coated in a sparkling layer of crystalline white frost, looking as though the entire chamber had been struck by a polar blizzard.

Qingyang Yang, however, debonairly fluttered his Jade-Bamboo Snow-Gold Fan, pointing it toward the air with theatrical flair:

—"Shatter—Achoo!"

Amid his sneeze, those frozen specter hands promptly shattered into sparkling ice crumbs, and the door on the opposite end of the room swung open.

—"Heh heh, nothing more than child's play,"-Qingyang Yang repeated in a distinctly congested, nasal voice.-"Now let us see where they assign me after passing this trial. I only hope Heavenly Spirit Sect doesn't [be a sore loser](# "Literal CN: 玩不起 (wán bù qǐ) — can't take a loss / poor sport") and dump me straight into the most brutal zone."

Refusing to abandon his suave routine, he continued fanning himself as he prepared to stride toward the open door. But amid the swirling ice dust he had conjured, he spotted three blood-red light orbs descending slowly from the ceiling, hovering in midair.

Simultaneously, a translucent screen of light prompted:

> **【Please select your reward.】**

—"There are rewards too?"-Qingyang Yang gasped in surprise.

He first examined the light orb closest to him:

> **【Generate Frost Ring: Within its radius, Ice-attribute spell ATK +30%】**

—"Hmph, even without this, my offensive power is already plenty high,"-Qingyang Yang scoffed, though the ravenous gleam that flashed through his eyes told an entirely different story.

After gazing lingeringly at this orb for several seconds, he shifted his eyes to the second light orb:

> **【Upgrade Snow-Gold Fan: Fan ATK +100】**

And then the third light orb:

> **【Obtain Ice-Coagulation Pill: For 30 seconds after ingestion, CRIT DMG ×200%】**

—"Whoa!"-Qingyang Yang exhaled in awe.

During his recent stay under detention at Mount Tianling, he had fallen head over heels for their Immortal Chess, so he understood with crystal clarity what these numerical values signified.

All three were unequivocally magnificent treasures! Sadly, every single option had a caveat trailing behind it:

> **【Effective within the secret realm only.】**

Then again, if these benefits could be taken outside the secret realm, they would hardly be handed over so lightly to an "outsider" like him. For them to function inside the realm was presumably achieved through the supreme divine might of the Untiring Immortal Venerable!

—"Hmph, do they imagine such external trinkets can shake my resolve?"-Qingyang Yang sneered disdainfully.-"Power that cannot be permanently retained is nothing but an illusion. I shall never fall for such tricks!"

Proclaiming this grandly, he ignored the rewards and strode boldly toward the doorway.

Yet no sooner had he reached the threshold than his boots froze, and he promptly moonwalked straight back:

—"On second thought, these 'rewards' aren't being offered to me alone, are they?"-Qingyang Yang reasoned aloud.-"If I refuse, wouldn't that mean falling a full step behind everyone else? How sinister! I nearly fell right into their trap."

Having put on a whole one-man monologue show to rationalize his turnaround, he scrutinized the three rewards and finally locked in **【Upgrade Snow-Gold Fan】**.

The instant his choice was registered, the other two light orbs vanished into thin air. The chosen orb floated upward, traced a jaunty arc in midair, and slammed solidly into the fan in Qingyang Yang's grip, erupting in blinding radiance.

As the glare subsided, Qingyang Yang felt the fan ribs turn three degrees colder. Upon the already crystalline jade-bamboo, delicate snow-white frost patterns had settled; when he fluttered the fan once more, miniature snowflakes visibly drifted from the folds.

Delighted by the effect, Qingyang Yang couldn't wait to fan himself:

—"Now this is stylish—Achoo!"

A nonstop volley of sneezes and a frozen fringe of sideburns did nothing to dampen Qingyang Yang's enthusiasm. He fiddled with the fan for a good long while before finally parting with the trial room and stepping outside.

Stepping out of the chamber, a crisp gust of glacial air rushed against his face. Qingyang Yang opened his eyes to behold a vast plain of Snow-Glow Grass stretching in all directions.

Although he had only caught a fleeting glimpse that day, Qingyang Yang knew that the Snow-Glow Grass plains sat at the mid-tier layer of the secret realm—the absolute ideal environment for an Ice Spirit Root cultivator like him to unleash his full prowess. It seemed Heavenly Spirit Sect hadn't shortchanged him with some barren corner. Qingyang Yang was thoroughly satisfied, yet a pang of worry quickly crept in.

Namely, his sense of direction was utterly atrocious. On ordinary days, finding paths and tracking prey were chores his fellow sect disciples handled for him.

Stranded in the middle of such a boundless grassland, Qingyang Yang hadn't the faintest clue which way to walk.

Right at that moment, several glowing cursors popped up:

> **【What do you plan to do next:】**  
> **【Find Companions】**  
> **【Obtain Spirit Herbs】**  
> **【Slay Demonic Beasts】**  
> **【Earn Spirit Stones】**

Each option was accompanied by a gigantic, dazzling arrow pointing toward a different direction—a truly [nanny-level degree of hand-holding](# "Literal CN: 保姆级的保护 (bǎomǔ jí de bǎohù) — nanny-level protection / spoon-fed guidance").

—"There really was no need to take it to this extreme,"-Qingyang Yang grumbled.

Nevertheless, he carefully weighed the choices.

As an elite disciple of the opulent and refined Eternal Villa, Qingyang Yang harbored zero interest in spirit herbs or spirit stones—honestly, what kind of bizarre secret realm offered an option to 'earn spirit stones' anyway? Given his personality, gallantly slaying demonic beasts was his true romantic calling. Yet even an insufferable [B-King](# "Literal CN: Bking (bī-wáng) — internet slang for an obsessive poseur / someone desperate to flex, look cool, and act like a haughty king at all times") like Qingyang Yang knew that challenging unknown beasts alone was a hazardous proposition. Furthermore, out of basic cultivation paranoia, he didn't entirely trust this magical guidance system; interacting directly with real people felt far safer.

—"Find Companions?"

Qingyang Yang locked in his choice and followed the glowing cursor forward across the grass.

He hadn't walked far when the tall grass ahead began to rustle violently. Preceded by a foul gust of predator stench, a four-eyed, white-browed demonic tiger pounced out from the brush!

[Is Heavenly Spirit Sect trying to screw me over?!]

In a panic, Qingyang Yang hoisted his fan to repel the beast, when a girl's sharp reprimand rang out:

—"A-Miao, no insolence!"

At that shout, a charming young maiden dressed in the gray "school uniform" of Thousand Beasts Hall sprinted from behind the bushes and threw her arms firmly around the four-eyed tiger's neck.

Rather than mauling anyone, the fierce white-browed tiger merely began purring like an oversized house cat, rubbing its snout affectionately against the maiden's cheek.

Qingyang Yang breathed an instant sigh of relief.

Examining the girl's face closely, he recognized her as one of the Thousand Beasts Hall disciples who had stayed at Mount Tianling alongside him:

—"Ah, Miss Rong... of Thousand Beasts Hall?"

—"Fellow Daoist Qingyang?"-Rong Rong blinked in surprise, offering an awkward smile.

Her awkwardness stemmed not merely from her pet startling a stranger, but also from being recognized by Qingyang Yang.

Rong Rong recalled that they had only exchanged names once when first ushered to Heavenly Spirit Sect; afterward, she had barely interacted with the disciples of Eternal Villa. She hadn't expected him to actually remember her.

By contrast, Rong Rong had completely forgotten this fellow's name—Qingyang... what was it again?

In any case, cultivators from Eternal Villa all dressed identically: the men looked like pampered young lords, the women looked like wealthy aristocratic heiresses, and every last one of them was surnamed Qingyang. Addressing him as "Fellow Daoist Qingyang" was guaranteed to never go wrong. Surely he wouldn't insist on her calling him by his full given name, right?

Rong Rong fretted inwardly.

Fortunately, the others caught up right at that moment:

[—"Rong Rong, an acquaintance?"](# "Speaker: Disciple")-someone called out as two more cultivators stepped out from the tall grass.

At the sight of them, Qingyang Yang let out a dry smirk:

—"Well, well. It seems the major sects entering this realm have assembled in full!"

He remarked thus because the two newcomers were disciples from the Thousand Harmony Gang and the Heavenly Spirit Sect respectively.

Naturally, several other minor sects had also participated in this expedition, but in the haughty eyes of an Eternal Villa aristocrat, sects without renowned pedigrees practically didn't exist.

Nobody had any interest in correcting him. The Thousand Harmony Gang disciple merely chuckled:

—"A sheer coincidence, sheer coincidence."

It truly was a coincidence.

Unlike Qingyang Yang, who had [staged an entire one-man opera](# "Literal CN: 那么多戏 (nàme duō xì) — so dramatic / making so much drama") in the preliminary chamber and wasted half the day, the others had entered the secret realm much earlier. After exploring for quite some time, they had happened across one another; unable to find their own sect mates, they had cheerfully joined forces as a party.

—"Still, I never anticipated finding a Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple in here,"-Qingyang Yang said.-"I assumed today was reserved exclusively for us outside sects. It appears I thought too much."

—"Shall I see myself out, then?"-the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple retorted with a radiant smile.

—"I wouldn't dare, I wouldn't dare! This is your Heavenly Spirit Sect's turf after all—"

Qingyang Yang fluttered his fan as he spoke, whipping up another drift of frost that made his nostrils tickle. This time, however, Qingyang Yang pressed his lips together with heroic resolve, forcibly swallowing the sneeze to preserve his aristocratic poise.

Seeing this spectacle, the Thousand Harmony Gang disciple's eyes lit up:

—"Could it be that Brother Qingyang possesses an Ice Spirit Root?"

Qingyang Yang frowned slightly at the form of address:

—"You could say that,"-he replied stiffly; he disliked being addressed so familiarly by strangers.

—"Fellow Daoist Shen, look at that..."-the Thousand Harmony Gang disciple turned toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect cultivator.

That disciple promptly extended an invitation to Qingyang Yang:

—"Fellow Daoist Qingyang, would you be interested in joining us?"

Only then did Qingyang Yang learn the reason this group had banded together.

As it turned out, ever since Qu Qianxing claimed this secret realm, it had naturally been opened to Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples first. This disciple, known as "Fellow Daoist Shen," had been exploring the realm over the past few days in search of spiritual herbs.

Just yesterday, she had inadvertently stumbled upon a Rank 5 Gu Diao guarding a rare spirit herb. Completely unable to best it alone, and with all her sect siblings occupied with their own affairs, she had been unwilling to merely sit and wait, and thus resorted to putting together a temporary [pickup group](# "Literal CN: 野队 (yěduì) — PUG / random pickup group formed on the spot").

—"Sounds like we're getting a free ride?"-Qingyang Yang said with a light chuckle.

A spirit herb guarded by a Rank 5 Gu Diao was guaranteed to be of exceptional grade—something ordinary cultivators would never stumble upon.

—"That depends on whether we can actually beat it first!"-Disciple Shen answered briskly.-"Besides, the path there isn't safe in the slightest—"

As if on cue to prove her words, a rustling noise arose from the grass. Yet before the cultivators could even assume defensive stances, Rong Rong's four-eyed tiger had already pounced.

Within mere moments, the massive tiger padded back with a captured Eshou clamped between its jaws. It deposited the beast obediently at Rong Rong's feet, rubbed against her ankle, and curled its broad tail affectionately around her shins.

—"Good boy!"-Rong Rong praised immediately.

She gave the tiger's head a vigorous rub and popped a Beast Feed Pill into its mouth.

—Demonic beasts successfully tamed by Thousand Beasts Hall would never carelessly consume food found in the wild. Disciples possessed specialized techniques for blending nutritional formulas into these "feed pellets" for their companions.

While the giant tiger happily crunched its pill, two faint red light orbs and one pale yellow light orb materialized above the Eshou's carcass.

—"Huh? Slaying a minor critter like this yields rewards as well?"-Qingyang Yang asked in surprise.

—"You haven't tried it yet?"-the Thousand Harmony Gang disciple asked, somewhat astonished.

Qingyang Yang kept his mouth shut, refusing to expose his utter [noob nature](# "Literal CN: 菜鸟本质 (càiniǎo běnzhì) — rookie/newbie nature") any further.

Rong Rong, on the other hand, was quite generous:

—"If you're interested, you could... help me pick one? To tell the truth, I'm feeling rather torn!"

Hearing her offer, the group peered over in curiosity, discovering the three choices laid out:

> **【Might Aura: ATK +1%, unusable outside the secret realm】**  
> **【Gale Pill: Pet Speed +10, unusable outside the secret realm】**  
> **【Eshou Materials: Will assist in dismantling; can be taken out of the secret realm.】**

—"These three rewards are rather modest, I suppose,"-though obviously inferior to the opening room's rewards, for a minor critter like an Eshou, such loot was already quite decent.

Qingyang Yang remarked dismissively:

—"What is there to hesitate over? That Gale Pill is obviously superior."

—"But the Eshou's materials will vanish..."-Rong Rong hesitated.

—"That rabbit doesn't have [three ounces of meat](# "Literal CN: 没三两肉 (méi sān liǎng ròu) — practically no meat at all / negligible value") on its bones anyway; let it vanish!"-Qingyang Yang insisted.-"On the contrary, your tiger is magnificently imposing. Stacking a bit more speed on it will make it far deadlier, giving us much better odds against that Gu Diao."

—"..."-Only a spoiled rich young master could say something like that.

Rong Rong smiled ruefully.

Through these options, she had already seen through the Heavenly Spirit Sect's insidious "scheme."

In a standard secret realm trial, prey like an Eshou would naturally be packed up and carried away by Rong Rong. Here, however, she was presented with two alternative options to empower herself. Although this enhancement was temporary and confined strictly to the secret realm, it granted the possibility of hunting higher-tier prey—yet it simultaneously meant surrendering the Eshou's materials.

Right now, it was merely an Eshou—a rabbit with barely "three ounces of meat," as Qingyang Yang put it. But what about subsequent harvests? What about rare spirit herbs? Artifact-crafting materials? Or far more precious prey? Would cultivators continuously sacrifice tangible gains to empower themselves in pursuit of greater glory, only to suffer defeat in the end and leave with empty hands?

Heavenly Spirit Sect was leveraging external cultivators to clear their secret realm for them, enticing cultivators to voluntarily surrender their gathered materials—and charging them an admission fee on top of it!

Rong Rong perceived the trap clearly, yet in the end, she still selected **【Gale Pill】** to buff her spirit pet.

After all, she had already resolved to challenge that Gu Diao.

[As long as we successfully fell that Gu Diao and I carry away my share of the materials, I won't have lost!]

[Yes! As long as I cash out in time, I won't lose!]

Rong Rong clenched her fists tightly.

*

—"I won!"

Drenched in blood, Xia Bing of Thousand Beasts Hall collapsed into the Snow-Glow Grass, golden light orbs scattering across the ground around her.

These were the rewards for felling a Rank 4 Hou beast.

Demonic beasts were generally categorized into thirteen ranks. They possessed no formal cultivation realms, classified strictly by lethality; a Rank 4 Hou beast boasted combat prowess equivalent to the Foundation Establishment Late Stage—categorically not something Xia Bing, a mere Foundation Establishment Middle Stage cultivator, could hope to defeat.

The secret to Xia Bing's victory was, naturally, that she had stacked an absurd number of buffs.

Even so, Xia Bing's intestines had been ripped out during the bout.

Confronted with such a dire situation, Xia Bing dared not delay. She crawled past the golden orbs spawned by the Hou's corpse, successfully locating a Rank 5 Five-Leaf Seven-Star Grass behind it.

Under ordinary circumstances, Xia Bing would never have borne to use such a precious spirit herb on herself.

She would rather have shoved her spilled intestines back inside, taken her chances with crippling complications, and slapped on whatever low-grade salve she had on hand, deferring future problems to the future. Yet at this moment, Xia Bing didn't hesitate for a heartbeat: she chewed the herb to a pulp and plastered it directly over her lacerated belly.

Instantly, a soothing coolness washed over the searing agony, suffusing her entire abdomen as surplus spiritual energy nourished her battered flesh.

[As expected of a Rank 5 spirit herb!]

Xia Bing marveled inwardly, hastily arranging her limbs into a meditative posture to absorb every single drop of spiritual sap without waste.

Then again, only in this secret realm would she dare do such a thing. In any other realm, she would have been murdered for her treasure the moment she collapsed; here, without her explicit permission, onlookers couldn't even view the contents of her "rewards."

Only after her wounds were fully healed did Xia Bing finally turn her attention to inspecting her loot with peace of mind.

The payoff from her high-stakes gamble was lavish.

Excluding the one she had consumed, she had obtained three additional Rank 5 spirit herbs, a high-quality Rank 4 monster core, and various assorted beast materials such as pelts and razor claws. Naturally, among the chaotic cluster of light orbs were also copious combat buffs that would allow Xia Bing to unleash even more terrifying might inside the realm.

However, her time was running out.

Heavenly Spirit Sect did not permit outsiders to camp inside the secret realm; once two shichen (four hours) elapsed, trialees were forcibly evicted. Watching the countdown ticking away on her light screen, Xia Bing knew she would be forcibly booted from the realm in a matter of minutes—she had previously witnessed a Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple attempting to loiter past the timer, only to be summarily kicked out. Given their ruthless enforcement even on their own disciples, they certainly wouldn't bend the rules for other sects.

Therefore, unless Xia Bing had lost her mind, she ought to abandon those fleeting, ethereal buffs and claim the tangible, physical spirit herbs and monster materials she had bled to acquire.

Yet Xia Bing stared at the spoils, lost in deep thought.

Just as she was wavering, rustling footsteps echoed from the grass, and several figures stepped out.

Even knowing they couldn't harm her here, Xia Bing reflexively assumed a defensive posture, sizing up the newcomers. The party looked thoroughly bedraggled—even the disciple of Eternal Villa, who clearly valued face above all else, was splattered in gory beast blood. Yet their expressions were relaxed and adorned with broad grins; evidently, their haul was considerable.

Furthermore, Xia Bing noticed that one among them was a disciple from her own Thousand Beasts Hall, seemingly named "Rong Rong."

—"Eek!"-Rong Rong jumped in fright at the sight of Xia Bing.

Almost reflexively, she threw herself in front of the four-eyed, white-browed tiger, stammering frantically:

—"Th-This was caused by the secret realm's rewards! It—It's just a normal cat!"

The excuse carried a distinct [guilty-conscience giveaway](# "Literal CN: 此地无银三百两 (cǐ dì wú yín sān bǎi liǎng) — 'no 300 taels of silver buried here'; a clumsy cover-up that exposes the exact secret one is trying to hide") flavor. After all, that demonic tiger was already twice her size; no matter how one looked at it, no ordinary house cat could ever mutate into such a beast.

Xia Bing understood the girl's paranoia. In Thousand Beasts Hall, exceptional beast cubs were prime targets for senior disciples to extort or seize outright.

In response, Xia Bing raised her wide sleeve. A swarm of crimson spirit insects surged forth from her cuff, blotting out the sky like fiery sunset clouds.

—"These are also ordinary bugs; they only morphed like this thanks to the secret realm's rewards,"-Xia Bing remarked with a bright, knowing smile.

This, too, was a blatant lie.

Ordinary bugs would never mutate into such a terrifying swarm from a handful of temporary buffs.

Yet the younger girl visibly relaxed.

Since everyone harbored dirty little secrets, they could speak on level ground.

—"Big Sis, looks like your haul was quite splendid!"-the Thousand Harmony Gang disciple on Rong Rong's team called out cheerfully.

—"Decent enough,"-Xia Bing didn't bother hiding it, simply waving Rong Rong over.-"Rong Rong, come here for a moment. There's something I wish to discuss with you."

—"Yes, Senior Sister Xia?"

Rong Rong briefed her teammates and walked toward Xia Bing. Tactfully, the party retreated just far enough out of earshot while remaining in sight, clearly watching out for Rong Rong's safety.

That alone proved one thing:

—"It appears your haul was quite handsome as well?"-Xia Bing asked.

—"I-It was alright, I suppose!"-Rong Rong had indeed helped fell the Gu Diao and claimed her rightful share of the rewards, but she wasn't so naive as to broadcast her exact loot to an unfamiliar senior sister.

Xia Bing didn't pry further, merely asking:

—"Do you plan to take it all outside?"

—"Why wouldn't I? If I don't take it out, wouldn't this entire run be meaningless?"-Rong Rong replied in bewilderment.

—"Have you forgotten who is waiting right outside? That bastard Bai Tuo!"-Xia Bing reminded her grimly.-"Forget fifteen stalks of herbs—once he sees good stuff, he'll confiscate every single scrap he lays eyes on!"

Rong Rong's face instantly drained of color.

Slaying the Gu Diao had yielded handsome spoils; her share included two stalks of Rank 6 spirit herbs. By standard valuation, a single stalk was worth twenty Rank 4 herbs, but knowing Bai Tuo's shameless greed, he was genuinely capable of pocketing both without batting an eye.

—Even losing a single stalk would leave Rong Rong mourning in agonizing heartbreak for an entire month.

—"Rather than taking it outside, it's far better to bank it inside the secret realm,"-Xia Bing guided patiently.-"I've inspected the system: this realm features a '[Save Function](# "Literal CN: 存档 (cún dàng) — save file / save game progress")'. As long as you choose to leave all your gains behind this run, you can resume with the exact same loadout next time you enter and continue exploring. During the time we're offline outside, we can even apply to have the realm refine our banked spirit herbs into elixir pills."

—"Next time? Senior Sister Xia, you plan to come back?"

—"Why wouldn't I? This secret realm is open to the public, isn't it?"

Xia Bing directed the latter question intentionally toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple in Rong Rong's group.

That disciple replied with a sunny grin:

—"Naturally it is open, though nominal fees will apply."

—"Why didn't you mention this earlier?!"-The one with the most explosive reaction was the Eternal Villa disciple, who looked primed to buy a membership card on the spot.-"How much does it cost?!"

—"Future solo entries certainly won't be free. A single run will cost around one hundred spirit stones each time, I believe!"-the disciple stated.

That price was undeniably steep for ordinary cultivators.

Yet the Eternal Villa disciple whipped out his coin pouch without hesitation, boasting grandly:

—"Just that pittance? Sign me up for an appointment!"

—"Don't give it to me; I'm not in charge of registration. Go find the receptionist at the front entrance,"-the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple waved him off in mild disdain.-"Besides, I'd suggest you purchase a Monthly Pass instead. You get ten runs a month at a ten percent discount. An Annual Pass gives a fifteen percent discount! Though word has it the most economical option is a whole-sect group package, calculated based on disciple headcount and scheduled runs—volume discounts apply! I'm not sure about the exact pricing for that, but you can have your team leader ask at the front desk."

—"Oho! There's an option like that too?!"

Not only was the Eternal Villa disciple sorely tempted, but even the Thousand Harmony Gang disciple beside him wore a look of intense longing.

By contrast, the two Thousand Beasts Hall disciples wore deeply conflicted expressions.

—"Steward Bai will never agree,"-Rong Rong murmured under her breath.

—"Forget Bai Tuo, none of the other stewards would agree either. In their eyes, we low-tier disciples are no different from the beasts they pen—only the exceptionally prized pedigrees deserve extra feed,"-Xia Bing sneered cuttingly.-"Not that it matters. I'll pay my own spirit stones. I won't take this run's rewards outside; I'll wait until Bai Tuo isn't stationed here, then return to claim them."

—"Then what about Steward Bai's quota..."-Rong Rong whispered.

—"Why pay heed to his unreasonable demands? As long as he can't fish out a single stalk of spirit herb from our robes, what could he possibly do to us?"-Xia Bing said, her tone dripping with [frustration at her junior's timidness](# "Literal CN: 恨铁不成钢 (hèn tiě bù chéng gāng) — exasperated that iron cannot instantly become steel; disappointed that someone lacks ambition or courage").

Rong Rong was struck with sudden enlightenment.

That was indeed a brilliant solution!

In secret realm trials like this, senior team leaders exploiting juniors was practically a time-honored "tradition." In relatively reasonable sects, stewards would issue task quotas for specific herbs; if disciples secured the requested flora, their mission was deemed a success, and any additional spoils were theirs to keep—a policy found only in the most generous sects. Unfortunate souls in sects like Thousand Beasts Hall, where discipline was tyrannical and leaders insatiably rapacious, faced the equivalent of a full-body strip search upon exiting. Any rare treasures found would be seized, and disciples were expected to kowtow and thank their superiors for the privilege; only items successfully smuggled past detection truly belonged to them.

However, if the secret realm proved barren or disciples were simply too weak to harvest anything, an elder couldn't force them to lay spirit herbs like hens laying eggs. They had no choice but to spew a few venomous curses and let it go.

Now that Heavenly Spirit Sect offered a "Save Function," it neatly bypassed the nightmare of high-tier exploitation upon exit!

—Provided, of course, that Heavenly Spirit Sect remained scrupulously fair.

Rong Rong and Xia Bing weren't particularly worried about that. Following their experience being "held captive at Mount Tianling," their faith in the credibility of the Heavenly Spirit Sect under the Untiring Immortal Venerable had skyrocketed to unprecedented heights.

Still worrying that her younger peer might lose her nerve, Xia Bing pressed on:

—"Besides, even without those two spirit herbs, this secret realm is still an incredible bargain. Have you considered whether starting over from scratch and picking different rewards might cause your tiger to evolve into an entirely different form?"

—"Ah, absolutely, absolutely!"-Once again, the Eternal Villa disciple piped up in high agitation. He bit the edge of his fan, unleashing an icy gale that dusted everyone in white frost, and sighed melodramatically.-"Next time I reset my run, I will never choose such an unromantic option again!"

The rest of the group collectively rolled their eyes at him.

Even so, everyone understood Xia Bing's point.

For low-tier cultivators, choosing a cultivation path was akin to gambling blindly in the dark, with no way to know whether a decision was good or disastrous. But the three choices presented after every battle granted them profound insights into how they should cultivate going forward.

For Thousand Beasts Hall disciples in particular, it revealed the evolutionary branches available to their spirit beasts.

Caressing her tiger's fur, Rong Rong's gaze hardened with resolve:

—"In that case... I'll purchase a Monthly Pass!"-The lump sum for an Annual Pass was simply beyond her current savings.

*

Similar discussions were unfolding across every corner of the secret realm.

Disciples from various sects made differing choices based on their respective circumstances: some consulted their masters directly, some gave detailed debriefs to their sect leaders, and others—like Thousand Beasts Hall—pocketed and banked every single scrap of their harvest in secret.

Furthermore, Xia Bing and Rong Rong were clearly not the only ones in Thousand Beasts Hall who had made that calculation. When they exited the realm and came upon a spitting-mad Steward Bai, they discovered that out of more than a dozen disciples dispatched on this trial, only one single "honest kid" had sheepishly produced three stalks of Rank 2 herbs; everyone else had returned virtually "empty-handed."

—"Useless trash! Every last one of you is useless trash!"-Bai Tuo screamed, spewing spittle.

The Thousand Beasts Hall disciples waited patiently until his tirade ran its course before putting on aggrieved faces:

—"There were hardly any good things inside the secret realm, or perhaps Heavenly Spirit Sect deliberately targeted us! No matter how hard we searched, we couldn't find a thing!"

—"Then how come other sects had harvests?!"

—"Did they?"

—"If they didn't, why are they all lining up at the counter to book future runs?!"-Bai Tuo interrogated furiously.

—"It's probably for the trial experience itself, isn't it?"-A disciple rolled his eyes thoughtfully, launching into a vivid recounting of the trial mechanics—naturally omitting every scrap of their actual loot—hoping to sway leadership into purchasing a Sect Group Pass.-"You see, if we pick the right rewards, we might test out our spirit pets' perfect future evolution! So, Martial Uncle Bai..."

—"In your dreams!"-Bai Tuo snapped without a second thought, glaring with slitted eyes.-"You lot slack off on your cultivation all day, only ever dreaming of such [crooked methods and degenerate shortcuts](# "Literal CN: 歪门邪道 (wāimén xiédào) — crooked gates and depraved paths / underhanded tricks and unorthodox bypasses")! What respectable, orthodox sect would ever—"

—"Give me an Annual Pass!"

Right at that moment, the delegation leader of Eternal Villa had also finished listening to her disciples' reports. In fact, Eternal Villa had been far more sophisticated: many disciples carried recording magical devices on their persons, recording their trials in real time to broadcast a live stream directly to their elder. That very elder now strode to the reception desk without an ounce of hesitation.

—"Very good,"-the Heavenly Spirit Sect receptionist greeted with a warm smile.-"One Annual Pass grants three hundred and thirty-three trial runs—"

—"No, I am not buying just one card. I am buying one for every single disciple in our entire sect!"-the female Nascent Soul elder of Eternal Villa countered.-"And not for one single year, either—"

As she spoke, she unfastened her storage pouch. A deafening, crystalline avalanche of high-grade spirit stones cascaded forth, nearly burying the Heavenly Spirit Sect receptionist beneath an glittering mountain of wealth.

Only then did the elder declare in a booming voice:

—"I wish to purchase a one-hundred-year quota for our entire manor."

Her voice echoed through the magnificent hall, stunning every cultivator present into petrified silence.

—"...Th-The Eternal Villa is an exception; they're just [filthy rich fools with more money than sense](# "Literal CN: 人傻钱多 (rén shǎ qián duō) — people are stupid and their money is abundant; loaded idiots / suckers with deep pockets")!"

Bai Tuo grumbled indignantly, his greedy gaze utterly unable to tear itself away from that blinding, mountain-sized pile of spirit stones.

Consequently, he completely failed to notice the Thousand Beasts Hall disciples behind him exchanging a subtle, knowing glance.
