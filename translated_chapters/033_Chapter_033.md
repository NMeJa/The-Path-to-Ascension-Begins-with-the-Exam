# Chapter 33: Opening the Secret Realm (第33章 开放秘境)

—"An envoy from the Heavenly Dao Alliance has finally arrived?"-Sect Leader Zhang Hongjing of the Thousand Harmony Gang asked in delighted surprise.

Originally, for a "mere" secret realm trial capped below the Golden Core realm, there was no need for the leader of the "Number One Gang of the Central Domain," the Thousand Harmony Gang, to make a personal appearance. But who told this matter to get tangled in the feud between the Palace of Carefree Wandering and the Untiring Immortal Venerable? Zhang Hongjing had no choice but to step forward and declare a stance.

In truth, Zhang Hongjing had no stance whatsoever. He was merely at the Void Refinement stage; how could he dare take a stance against sects backed by bona fide Tribulation Transcendence patriarchs? In his eyes, both sides were venerable ancestors; neither could be offended.

[I knew it. The moment I heard Carefree Palace bought up exploration slots back then, I said we shouldn't send anyone!]

Zhang Hongjing thought with deep grievance.

The Thousand Harmony Gang was hailed as the foremost faction in the Central Domain, but in reality, it was nothing more than a loose alliance of rogue cultivators. Although the number of practitioners beneath its banner was massive, most had merely registered their names. Whenever a major event occurred, they gathered to consult together (or rather, find someone to take the blame); plenty of them had even established their own independent sects on the side.

Zhang Hongjing, too, had been young and naive back in the day. Back when he was a mortal, he had roamed the martial arts world. After embarking on the path of cultivation, he assumed the cultivation world was much like the mortal Jianghu, going about calling everyone "brother" and slapping shoulders. Renowned throughout the Central Domain as an [“old nice guy”](# "Literal CN: 老好人 (lǎo hǎorén) — an overly obliging, conflict-averse people-pleaser who can never say no") and everyone's dear friend, he had thus muddied his way into founding a gang, muddied his way into becoming a leader burdened with endless obligations and zero privileges, muddied his way into watching this gang balloon into the Central Domain's number one faction, and muddied his way into watching the troubles pile up endlessly.

Case in point: right now.

—"Is the Heavenly Dao Alliance finally stepping in to manage things?"-Daoist Huang, a Vice Sect Leader of the Thousand Harmony Gang, rumbled in a gruff, resonant voice.-"Good timing! Let them arbitrate fairness. There's no reason in the world for the Heavenly Spirit Sect to hog the secret realm all to themselves!"

—"Ahem. Weren't we just asking the Heavenly Spirit Sect to release our people?"-Zhang Hongjing coughed.

—"And we're just letting the secret realm go like that?!"-Daoist Huang shrieked at the top of his lungs.

[Of course we let it go like that! What, you want to compete against a Tribulation Transcendence patriarch?!]

Zhang Hongjing thought, nursing a throbbing headache.

If not for several disciples exploring the secret realm being detained at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Zhang Hongjing would have grabbed his people and bolted immediately. Why would he ever entangle himself in this mess?

What was worse, the longer they lingered here, the stranger the situation grew.

Back when news first broke that the secret realm had recognized an owner, people like Daoist Huang had grumbled and muttered under their breath, yet none dared voice any real objection. Recently, however, they had suddenly begun asserting claims over the secret realm's ownership, acting as if they wouldn't hesitate even to offend the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

It wasn't just the Thousand Harmony Gang either; other sects were behaving the exact same way.

People clamored one after another, grumbling that Heavenly Spirit Sect monopolizing the secret realm was unfair. Within the Thousand Harmony Gang, muddle-headed fools like Daoist Huang were even shouting about demanding justice.

Yet... fairness?

When has the cultivation world ever possessed any concept of fairness?

To say nothing of a secret realm, even when stumbling upon a slightly rare spiritual herb out in the wilderness, the strong routinely bullied the weak, and whoever had the martial might to pluck it owned it. How come no one ever mentioned fairness then?

Zhang Hongjing silently roasted them in his heart, but couldn't be bothered to open his mouth to dissuade them.

He knew speaking up would achieve nothing anyway. Back when he heard that the Heavenly Spirit Sect had secured the lion's share of exploration quotas and Carefree Palace had muscled in, he had already written off this secret realm entirely. Yet a faction within the gang had stubbornly disagreed, spouting nonsense like: "The secret realm has no master!" "When the big bosses eat meat, there will always be a bowl of broth left over!" "Why shouldn't we fight for more slots? As Sect Leader, how can you disregard the interests of the gang's members?!" Well, look at them now—they were about to turn into the broth themselves!

In any case, if these fools truly offended the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the only unlucky sucker facing the music would be him, the Sect Leader!

Zhang Hongjing had investigated thoroughly: that day, Sovereign Immortal Chana, who tried to seize the secret realm, hadn't merely lost a severed hand—even his ashes had been scattered! Drifting across Xiaoyao Lake for all eyes to witness!

(Sovereign Immortal Chana: No! What scattered across Xiaoyao Lake was the Thought Pavilion! Not my ashes!)

Once he met with the Heavenly Dao Alliance representative, he would simply ask them to help retrieve his gang's disciples and nothing more!

Grab the disciples and run as fast as possible, never meddling in this cursed affair again!

Zhang Hongjing steeled his resolve—until he came face to face with Hall Master He Yong of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Central Domain Branch.

He Yong beamed warmly:

—"Sect Leader Zhang, joyous news! Splendid news!"

—"What joyous news?"-Zhang Hongjing inquired warily.

Viewing He Yong's demeanor, his suspicion flared.

One had to realize that Su Xin of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had also been inside the secret realm, his current condition still unknown. On top of that, while other sects didn't dare confront the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they were more than emboldened to demand an explanation from the Heavenly Dao Alliance. The sheer anxiety had caused Hall Master He to shed his hair in colossal handfuls, hiding away from visitors day and night, longing for nothing more than to hang a sign reading "I Do Not Exist" around his neck.

Yet now, Hall Master He bore not a trace of sorrow. The locks atop his head were sleek, glossy, and luminous, carrying with them a smile that was just as oily.

—"It was all a misunderstanding!"-He Yong announced.-"On that day, Sovereign Immortal Chana suddenly struck, interfering with the internal environment of the secret realm and causing minor injuries to several disciples. Heavenly Spirit Sect felt they bore some responsibility as well, which was why they escorted those disciples back to their sect for convalescence. They have made a full recovery! Everyone need only visit the gate to bring your disciples home."

—"That's wonderful!"-Zhang Hongjing breathed a sigh of relief.

Catching sight of Daoist Huang seemingly about to speak from the corner of his eye, Zhang Hongjing hastily used his spiritual energy to suppress the man, preventing that scoundrel from uttering another superfluous syllable.

Yet Zhang Hongjing had been entirely preoccupied with Daoist Huang, never expecting another Vice Sect Leader—the Thousand Harmony Gang had at least a dozen Vice Sect Leaders—Daoist Master Chen, to leap forward:

—"And what of Heavenly Spirit Sect monopolizing the secret realm? Is it just going to end like this?"

[I think the only thing ending is your life!]

[No, our Thousand Harmony Gang is done for! Why in the world was I so insane as to found this gang in the first place?!]

Zhang Hongjing genuinely felt like fainting on the spot.

—"And just what do you all propose to do?"-Sure enough, He Yong's spiritual prestige crashed down like toppling mountains and surging seas, pinning everyone present—including Zhang Hongjing—so firmly they could not even lift their heads.-"Their disciple claimed it by virtue of genuine capability! His Master never intervened to help him, nor was any rule violated. If you have the guts, send your own disciples to take it!"

Only then did the crowd remember that this good-tempered Hall Master was also a Body Integration powerhouse, a mere half-step away from the Mahayana realm!

Muttering meekly, they dared not utter another word, though whether their hearts were truly convinced remained anyone's guess.

However, He Yong retracted his divine ability a moment later, letting out a cold snort:

—"Nevertheless, having you make this trip for nothing would indeed look somewhat unbecoming. Therefore, our Heavenly Dao Alliance specially negotiated with the Heavenly Spirit Sect. The Heavenly Spirit Sect is exceedingly generous as well; they have agreed to grant your disciples another opportunity to resume their trial. All harvests acquired within the secret realm shall belong entirely to them."

Hearing this, the crowd's eyes widened in astonishment.

One had to understand that in the cultivation world, regarding any resources or magical treasures, finders were keepers and might made right. For someone who had already swallowed the meat into their mouth to be willing to let others have a taste was undeniably beyond generous.

Surveying their stunned expressions, He Yong felt immensely smug inside.

What "negotiation"? There had naturally been no such thing! How would he ever dare initiate a conversation with that terrifying Immortal Venerable? Rather, it was the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself who, whether out of face for the Plum-Bamboo Immortals or due to other considerations, had proactively taken a step back.

He Yong had also breathed a sigh of relief. He felt the Heavenly Dao Alliance now had an official explanation to give the outside world; even if Carefree Palace came looking for trouble, they would have plenty of justification to push back.

Yet there were still those whose brains were not quite working. Having finally regained the chance to speak, Daoist Huang actually asked with unyielding persistence:

—"Then does the secret realm belong to Heavenly Spirit Sect after all?"

—"Shut your mouth! If it doesn't belong to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, is it supposed to be given to you?!"-Zhang Hongjing barked.

Zhang Hongjing's magical treasure slammed directly into Daoist Huang's skull, driving him deep into the dirt like a tent peg.

If you want to [push your luck](# "Literal CN: 得寸进尺 (dé cùn jìn chǐ) — take an inch and ask for a mile"), you'd better check who you're dealing with first!

That was the Untiring Immortal Venerable, a man who offended Carefree Palace without blinking an eye!

Watching this spectacle, He Yong let out a cold smirk. In his opinion, executing such a fool wouldn't be an injustice in the slightest. However, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had left instructions regarding this matter, so it would be improper for him to overstep:

—"Naturally, the secret realm belongs to the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-He Yong replied.-"If you have the ability, go make the secret realm recognize you as master instead. However, the Immortal Venerable is benevolent. Considering how perilous the Central Domain's environment is, while secret realms suitable for low-tier cultivators are few and far between, he has decided to charge merely a nominal maintenance fee and open the secret realm to all practitioners across the Central Domain."

[—"What?!"](# "Speaker: Sect Elders")

The others found this even more unbelievable.

If allowing them to keep their trial spoils earlier was letting them have a lick of the meat, this was practically inviting them to take a full bite!

Was the Untiring Immortal Venerable a living Bodhisattva or something?

Yet they couldn't pry any deeper rationale out of He Yong either. Only after the Heavenly Dao Alliance's party departed did they begin heatedly debating:

—"Could it be that the Heavenly Spirit Sect is scared?"-someone asserted with conviction.-"They don't want to offend us?"

—"Bullshit! They're not even afraid of Carefree Palace, why would they fear us?"-Clearer heads were also present.-"In my view, that secret realm must hold very little exploration value. Hoarding it only invites hostility, so they simply threw it open."

[—"Even if it's a low-tier secret realm, it could still be reserved for training their own disciples!"](# "Speaker: Sect Elder")

[—"Perhaps it's even a [chicken rib](# "Literal CN: 鸡肋 (jīlèi) — tasteless to eat yet wasteful to discard; something of little practical value") to low-tier disciples?"](# "Speaker: Sect Elder")

[—"That sounds about right."](# "Speaker: Sect Elder")

This speculation instantly gained the upper hand. After all, in a cultivation realm governed by the law of the jungle, people fundamentally refused to believe in the existence of any "good samaritans." True altruists had died out long ago; for Zhou Hui to reach Tribulation Transcendence, how could he possibly possess such a soft heart?

Thinking along these lines, much of the resentment dissipating from the crowd was replaced by schadenfreude:

[No matter how formidable your Heavenly Spirit Sect is, all you grabbed was a worthless secret realm, and you offended Carefree Palace in the process! What a monumental loss!]

It wasn't just them either. Once the news spread and word of Untiring Immortal Venerable's dirt-cheap entry terms circulated, whether sect members or rogue cultivators, the vast majority drew the exact same conclusion. The [green-eyed jealousy](# "Literal CN: 红眼病 (hóngyǎnbìng) — pink-eye / jealousy and envy at others' success") subsided, preemptively defusing a storm before it could even gather.

Naturally, that was a matter for later.

Zhang Hongjing merely watched these people [act like clowns](# "Literal CN: 作妖 (zuòyāo) — stir up mischief / act up dramatically") with cold detachment, feeling an infinite wave of exhaustion wash over his heart. Alas, having been hoisted onto his current throne, stepping down wasn't something he could do simply because he wanted to.

He merely sighed:

—"Regardless, since the Venerable has granted this opportunity, let the disciples finish their incomplete trial."

*

Zhang Hongjing's decision was naturally the decision of the other sects as well.

Whether everyone considered the secret realm useless or not, [since they were already here](# "Literal CN: 来都来了 (lái dōu lái le) — one of China's iconic Four Great Excuses: 'Since we've already come all this way, we might as well...'"), they might as well run the trial!

In any case, this run didn't cost a single spirit stone.

While the higher-ups' reactions were noncommittal, the disciples could only be described as ecstatic:

—"We can resume the trial?!"-Rong Rong of the Thousand Beasts Hall inquired with delight.

She had thought this secret realm trial had gone down the drain, and had moped over it for days!

To a mid-tier disciple like her, who only possessed Foundation Establishment cultivation, a secret realm trial was an exceptionally rare opportunity.

After all, secret realms were scarce in this world, and those suitable for low-tier disciples were even rarer. Many of them had long been monopolized by grand sects, completely out of reach for ordinary cultivators.

Of course, Thousand Beasts Hall wasn't considered a small sect in the Central Domain either. Its numbers were only slightly fewer than the Thousand Harmony Gang's, but the ties among its members were vastly tighter; it was a bona fide major sect, and it even owned several secret realms of its own.

However, a sect like this inevitably maintained rigid hierarchies. Its secret realms were reserved exclusively for direct disciples and core inner sect seedlings groomed for greatness; for someone like Rong Rong, who had broken through to Foundation Establishment through her own efforts to enter the inner sect, gaining entry was virtually unthinkable.

The reason Rong Rong had managed to secure a spot as an expeditionary trialee this time was threefold: first, the newly opened secret realm was uncharted territory with unknown internal hazards, necessitating a batch of "vanguard pioneers" (read: [cannon fodder suicide squad](# "Literal CN: 敢死队 (gǎnsǐduì) — dare-to-die corps / vanguard suicide squad")); second, the sect leadership had caught wind that this realm was contested between Carefree Palace and Heavenly Spirit Sect, meaning there would likely be no good spoils to reap; and third, Rong Rong's father had bribed a sect manager with thirty whole stalks of fifty-year-old Rank 3 Red Cinnabar Grass—the premier ingredient for high-grade beast medicine—just to obtain this chance.

Who could have guessed that no sooner had the secret realm opened than Qu Qianxing of the Heavenly Spirit Sect subjugated it? Worse yet, the clash between Sovereign Immortal Chana and the Untiring Immortal Venerable left the trialees injured. Fearing that releasing them willy-nilly would cause misunderstandings, Heavenly Spirit Sect had simply bundled them all up and ferried them back to their mountain gate, waiting for their respective sects to claim them and confirm their conditions one by one. Thus, Rong Rong and the others had found themselves stranded at Heavenly Spirit Sect.

[Sure enough, if a secret realm isn't even worth sending elite disciples to, it's never going to be an easy ride.]

Rong Rong had initially lamented with vexation.

She didn't harbor much resentment toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect. After all, it wasn't her first day on the path of cultivation; during ordinary training, her harvests were routinely snatched away by "seniors" and "elder martial brothers." If she wanted to hold grudges, her revenge blacklist would stretch into the next lifetime. In the current predicament, she could only chalk it up to her own lack of skill.

Of course, another major reason was the wonderful atmosphere of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Although they weren't permitted to leave Mount Tianling, aside from the captives from Carefree Palace, Heavenly Spirit Sect placed no curfew or house arrest on ordinary sect disciples like Rong Rong, letting them wander and sightsee at leisure.

Rong Rong had lingered in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion so long she forgot to return. She could scarcely believe such a treasure trove of manuals could ever be opened to outsiders, to the point where she slightly regretted joining the Thousand Beasts Hall in the first place! Disciples from other sects felt much the same. Even the snooty disciples of Eternal Villa, whose noses were usually pointed at the clouds, had stepped into the array simulation room of the Library Pavilion to play a round of Immortal Chess—and refused to step foot outside ever since!

Just yesterday, Rong Rong had overheard cultivators from other sects badgering Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, asking why they couldn't turn themselves into chess pieces too, complaining that this was blatant discrimination!

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, remarkably even-tempered, had replied that even within their own sect, any disciple who failed the sect examinations could forget about getting a custom piece; if they wanted one so badly, why not sit for the next unified exam?

Lo and behold, that bunch actually looked sorely tempted! Who knew, they might genuinely sneak over to Heavenly Spirit Sect (another school) just to sit for the exam!

The sect elders waiting outside the secret realm likely never dreamed in their wildest nightmares that their disciples were [so happy they forgot all about home](# "Literal CN: 乐不思蜀 (lè bù sī Shǔ) — so happy in a new place that one no longer misses home (referencing Liu Shan of Shu)")!

It was only when today's announcement regarding the resumption of the secret realm trial came down that Rong Rong and her peers finally remembered how they had arrived at Mount Tianling in the first place. The final lingering trace of vexation vanished into thin air, replaced instead by a tinge of regret:

[Why couldn't they hold us for a few more days? I haven't finished reading my book (haven't finished playing that match)!]

Naturally, the sect elders who had been [drinking the northwest wind](# "Literal CN: 喝西北风 (hē xīběi fēng) — suffering in the cold with nothing to eat") outside the secret realm for days on end were greeted by a crowd of radiant, rosy-cheeked disciples:

[—"Detained? Not really, I felt we were pretty free!"](# "Speaker: Sect Disciple")

[—"Master, listen to me! Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion is colossal! I even found that *The Complete Compendium of Hundred Herbs of the Black Abyss* you always talked about. Why didn't we come visit Heavenly Spirit Sect sooner?"](# "Speaker: Sect Disciple")

[—"You have no idea! Heavenly Spirit Sect has a game called Immortal Chess, it's so much fun! I've established some rapport with their disciples now; next time, let's come play together!"](# "Speaker: Sect Disciple")

...

The masters of each sect truly had to exert every ounce of their willpower to keep from fainting in sheer rage.

—"I know you lot had quite the grand time, but don't forget the rules of our Thousand Beasts Hall!"

Thousand Beasts Hall hadn't placed much importance on this trial to begin with. Their team leader was merely an elderly Golden Core cultivator named Bai Tuo, sporting a bald pate and a bulbous, rosacea-red nose. He looked nothing like a decent person, and the way he glared at disciples was remarkably identical to looking at pigs fattening in a sty:

—"Once you exit the secret realm, whether you found anything or not, each of you must hand over ten stalks of Rank 4 medicinal herbs."

The disciples of Thousand Beasts Hall felt as though a bucket of freezing water had been dumped over their heads.

—"Ten stalks?! Don't the regular rules say only five are required?"-someone asked in indignation.

The old cultivator sneered:

—"You think while you were idling carefree at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I was just twiddling my thumbs here? Do you know how many spirit stones I spent to bail you ungrateful brats out?"

[Total nonsense! Heavenly Spirit Sect never blocked outside news! You were the ones who cowed in terror after witnessing the clash between Carefree Palace and Untiring Immortal Venerable, not daring to lift a finger! And in the end, it was Heavenly Spirit Sect who proactively made the concession!]

The disciples present were seething with fury, yet utterly helpless.

After all, at the end of the day, they still had to return to their sect.

Bai Tuo was primarily in charge of personnel assignments within the sect. Offending him meant not only the risk of being banished to some dead-end mountain peak, but far worse—being designated as cannon fodder and dispatched on suicide missions.

[—"Ten stalks, though! Why doesn't he just go rob someone?!"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"Doesn't this mean I'm working for him for free?!"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"I heard that secret realm is a total chicken rib; we might even have to pay out of pocket."](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

[—"It shouldn't be that bad. Although I only caught a brief glimpse back then, inside the secret realm—"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

The last speaker was abruptly hushed. The others glared at him, furiously throwing frantic glances until the disciple finally caught on, instantly switching to a mournful sigh:

—"Yeah, such a useless secret realm... who knows if we can even harvest that many spirit herbs,"-the disciple hastily sighed.

Thanks to the concerted efforts of these disciples with their [own clever agendas](# "Literal CN: 别有用心 (biéyǒuyòngxīn) — harboring ulterior motives / hidden agendas"), even more people became convinced that the secret realm held nothing of real value.

Rong Rong said nothing, merely feeling an overwhelming sense of dull disillusionment.

How wonderful the Heavenly Spirit Sect was!

Aside from dreading exams, their disciples didn't seem to have a single care in the world.

She couldn't stop the thought from surfacing, yet dared not dwell on it. After all, not only was Rong Rong's entry-level cultivation technique tied to Thousand Beasts Hall, but her family also shared countless intricate ties with the sect; betraying her own sect was completely out of the question.

In this stifling, slightly warped atmosphere, the disciples of Thousand Beasts Hall and other sects reorganized their ranks, preparing for the secret realm trial.

Unlike the first time when the secret realm was "wild and untamed," however, this time the realm had an owner.

Whether to flaunt their ownership or not, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had spent a few days erecting a grand hall situated atop the very mountain peak where the secret realm had once manifested—the one that had been sliced flat by the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

—The mountain was no longer named "Nameless Peak"; Heavenly Spirit Sect had rather crudely christened it Mount Qianxing, and the structure atop it was dubbed Qianxing Hall.

Fortunately, Qianxing Hall had been cut out exquisitely.

Yes, cut.

Because the construction deadline was far too tight, this grand hall was said to have been cut out of paper by Eldest Senior Sister Ran Deng of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Word had it this was merely a preliminary mockup, to be filled in gradually later on.

Yet rushed as the schedule was, because it bore the name "Qianxing," Ran Deng felt she could not do an injustice to her Second Senior Brother's handsome face. Thus, her mastery of paper-cut window flowers (read: divine architectural origami) was displayed to breathtaking perfection, utterly peerless and magnificent.

Once Ran Deng finished cutting, she simply blew a gentle breath toward the mountain peak, and an incomparably resplendent palace materialized atop the sheer, vertical cliff. Ran Deng then strung up countless luminous orbs across its eaves. Looking up toward the summit from the base of the mountain, it genuinely carried the celestial aura of "a thousand stars."

However, this also meant that certain individuals stepping into Qianxing Hall was an absolute insult to the structure—both in appearance and in spirit.

Case in point: Steward Bai Tuo, team leader of the Thousand Beasts Hall.

—"Entering the secret realm requires registering names?!"-Bai Tuo barked loudly.-"I've never heard of such a preposterous rule!"

—"We must ensure the personal safety of every cultivator entering the secret realm, so personal information is naturally required,"-the receptionist disciple at Qianxing Hall was merely at Qi Condensation Level 7, yet faced with a Golden Core cultivator like Bai Tuo, he remained neither servile nor haughty.-"If you do not wish to provide your details, you are free to decline entry into the secret realm."

—"Such insolence!"-Bai Tuo roared, slapping his teal gourd.

The disciples of Thousand Beasts Hall instinctively took two steps back.

Thousand Beasts Hall, as the name suggested, specialized in beast taming. Bai Tuo's chosen beasts were a nest of finger-thick vipers, ordinarily nurtured within the teal gourd. With a single slap to the gourd, they would dart out to strike, their venom sealing the throat upon drawing blood—a lethal terror even disciples of his own sect dreaded.

Yet no matter how Bai Tuo slapped his gourd today, there was not the slightest reaction.

—"Fighting is strictly prohibited within Qianxing Hall—in fact, across the entirety of Mount Qianxing,"-the receptionist disciple remarked with a beaming smile.-"Our Immortal Venerable cast the ward upon this realm in person."

Hearing his words, the others present hastily tested their abilities. Sure enough, they discovered that all offensive spells were completely suppressed.

[As expected of a Tribulation Transcendence titan!]

A chill seized the hearts of the cultivators present; some even contemplated fleeing the mountain at once. Much of Bai Tuo's arrogance deflated, yet he was still adamantly unwilling to register his personal details.

To say nothing of comprehensive dossiers, in the cultivation world, even one's true name was not lightly disclosed. Who knew what sinister curses or tracking arts someone might weave with a true name?

Consequently, in the cultivation world, courtesy names, Daoist monikers, and aliases were rampant, as everyone avoided uttering their true names.

Those who dared speak their true names fell into two categories: the first were low-tier disciples below the Golden Core realm, who lacked access to such esoteric curses among their peers, while higher-tier masters didn't need to expend such effort to crush them, rendering concealment moot; the second were supreme titans like Zhou Hui, whose true names and even Daoist titles outsiders dared not utter, as doing so was tantamount to exposing one's presence to his senses.

Yet while Bai Tuo refused to disclose his own name, he showed no such delicacy toward his sect's disciples.

Heavenly Spirit Sect had no intention of concealing anything, laying out the rules and requirements for exploring the secret realm with crystal clarity, leaving it entirely up to their own discretion. Immediately, some disciples wanted to [beat a retreat](# "Literal CN: 打退堂鼓 (dǎ tuìtáng gǔ) — idiom meaning to withdraw, chicken out, or back out midway"):

[—"In that case, maybe we should just forget it—"](# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple")

—"You say forget it, so we forget it?! This is a privilege our sect fought tooth and nail to secure!"-Bai Tuo lied through his teeth.-"Every single one of you must go in, and bring out at least fifteen stalks of Rank 4 medicinal herbs!"

Well, look at that. In the blink of an eye, the quota had grown by another five stalks.

Under the leering glare of this manager, the Thousand Beasts Hall disciples could only yield with bitter reluctance. Glancing at one another, nobody wanted to be the [first person to eat crab](# "Literal CN: 第一个吃螃蟹的人 (dì yī gè chī pángxiè de rén) — the first brave person to try something new and untested"). In the end, Rong Rong stepped forward.

—"How do we register?"-Rong Rong asked. She had searched the desk before him and found neither paper nor brush.

—"Please give me a spirit stone,"-the receptionist requested.-"A low-grade one will do just fine, preferably on the smaller side."

Baffled, Rong Rong rummaged through her storage pouch.

The universal standard for spirit stones in the cultivation world was roughly the size of a pinky finger. However, there was no dedicated imperial mint; most spirit stones were issued by sects owning spiritual mines, stamped with their sect insignia—the most universally recognized spirit stones in the realm being those minted by Carefree Palace. Occasionally, ordinary cultivators discovered minor ore veins in the wilderness, in which case their spirit stones were hand-polished by themselves.

How to maximize spiritual energy extraction while polishing a spirit stone into standard specifications was a research topic pursued by every impoverished cultivator.

The leftover shavings and residue produced from polishing spirit stones circulated among low-tier cultivators as "micro-currency."

Rong Rong's storage pouch contained quite a few such spirit stone fragments. Most gleamed with pastel hues of blush pink and sky blue, preserved by Rong Rong purely because she found them pretty.

She picked out a fingernail-sized wafer and handed it to the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple:

—"Will this do?"

—"It will do."-Yet the disciple did not pocket it. He merely swept a horsetail whisk across the spirit stone chip, inquired about a few details regarding her identity, and returned the chip into her hands.-"Keep this fragment safe. Your secret realm data has been inscribed inside."

—"Secret realm data?"-Rong Rong repeated in bewilderment.

Receiving the spirit stone fragment, she watched as a translucent screen of light materialized before her eyes:

> **【Name: Rong Rong】**  
> **【Cultivation: Foundation Establishment Middle Stage】**  
> **【Sect: Thousand Beasts Hall】**  
> **【Class: Beast Tamer】**  
> **【Quest: Complete Secret Realm Trial】**

Seeing this, Rong Rong came to a vague realization. During her stay at Mount Tianling, she had noticed Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples carrying similar information plaques, though theirs were carved from special peach wood talismans that could even teleport them anywhere according to the Sundial Clock.

[Still, what purpose does this serve in a secret realm?]

Rong Rong wondered in curiosity. Unfortunately, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple had no intention of elaborating, merely stepping aside to reveal the small doorway behind him:

—"Please step this way. As long as you pass a minor preliminary trial, you may enter the secret realm."
