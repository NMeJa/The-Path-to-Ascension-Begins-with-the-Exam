# Chapter 135: Secrets of the Heavenly Dao (第135章 天道的秘密)

[← Previous Chapter](134_Chapter_134.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](136_Chapter_136.md)

---

Summon Qu Qianxing over?

Hearing Lu Shulong's suggestion, Zhou Hui shook his head, utterly bemused:

—"No need,"-Zhou Hui said.-"Calling him over won't be of much use anyway. Your Second Senior Brother possesses no relevant memories."

—"Then, Master, have you not considered restoring Second Senior Brother's memories?"-Lu Shulong inquired.-"Although Second Senior Brother's memories were wiped clean by reincarnation, if one insists on tracing them back, there must be a way, right?"

After all, back in the third reincarnation, Lu Shulong had managed to do just that.

At present, however, Lu Shulong wore a look of utter innocence, appearing completely devoid of selfish motives.

His persuasion sounded both sincere and remarkably reasonable:

—"Regarding the sixth reincarnation, I feel Second Senior Brother would understand it best. He was likely the one pulling all the strings from behind the scenes. If Second Senior Brother were to regain his memories, countless problems would surely be solved effortlessly."

Having said this, Lu Shulong let out a soft sigh, exuding the faint, delicate fragrance of a [white lotus](# "Literal CN: 白莲花 (bái liánhuā) — white lotus; slang for feigning innocence, vulnerability, and pure-hearted helplessness"):

—"It's all because I am too useless. If only I remembered a little more, Master wouldn't have to suffer such distress."

—"I would much rather none of you ever remembered anything,"-Zhou Hui replied, furrowing his brows as he looked at Lu Shulong.-"Having to re-experience painful things all over again... no, more than once. You must have suffered immense agony."

In truth, Lu Shulong's circumstances were actually considered relatively fortunate.

Granted, Yuwen Xuan had initially wanted to eat him, but back then Lu Shulong was merely an egg with no conscious awareness.

Aside from that, Lu Shulong likely hadn't encountered anything too dreadful. By comparison, however, the pasts of Qu Qianxing, Guan Ye, and the twins Mo Bei and Mo Xi were anything but easy. If they were to suddenly regain their memories, the experience would be sheer living hell.

Whenever Zhou Hui thought about this, he was never truly certain whether initiating these repeated cycles of reincarnation had been the right choice.

—"It would be more accurate to say that your master was simply too incompetent,"-Zhou Hui murmured.

Lu Shulong's eyes snapped wide, a flash of indignation crossing his face:

—"Master! Do you still not understand?! We weren't stirring up trouble for no reason—everyone would rather endure all that agony than lose even the slightest fragment of our memories regarding you! Especially Second Senior Brother!"

—"So you do know you were stirring up trouble, you evil dragon,"-Zhou Hui said with a rueful chuckle, lightly tapping Lu Shulong's forehead.

It wasn't that Zhou Hui failed to understand these children's feelings. But just because they claimed they were willing to bear it did not mean they deserved to suffer such agony.

—"If restoring your memories were an absolute prerequisite for defying destiny, I would have intervened long ago without hesitation. But looking at things now, there doesn't appear to be any such necessity. On the contrary, an abrupt restoration of memories would only bring unexpected complications,"-Zhou Hui gave a wry smile.-"Especially when it comes to your Second Senior Brother. If he can pull off all those outrageous stunts without a shred of past memories, I truly shudder to imagine what he might do if he regained seven full lifetimes of them."

Even Lu Shulong couldn't find a single word to refute that.

Throughout the third and fourth reincarnations, Second Senior Brother had been the one who went off the deepest end. Although Lu Shulong hadn't witnessed the sixth reincarnation himself, a Qu Qianxing who could casually decide to sacrifice Lu Shulong had clearly gone completely off his rocker.

—"However, as long as he remains by Master's side, Second Senior Brother is harmless,"-Lu Shulong decided to speak a fair word on Qu Qianxing's behalf.-"I believe that even if he regains his memories while at your side, he would still be a perfectly normal Second Senior Brother."

[...Isn't that even worse?!]

[So we really have to keep a [tight-fillet curse](# "Literal CN: 紧箍咒 (jǐngūzhòu) — the golden fillet curse placed on Sun Wukong in Journey to the West to keep him obedient") strapped to Qu Qianxing's head forever?!]

[He's the Second Senior Brother, not the Eldest Senior Brother!]

[Ah, right. Our family's Eldest Senior Brother is also someone who can't function without a tight-fillet curse... No, a tight-fillet curse is too lenient. That fellow is someone who can't function without a muzzle.]

As this thought crossed Zhou Hui's mind, his stomach—which theoretically could no longer feel physical pain—began to ache with sympathetic phantom agony once more.

Zhou Hui let out a long sigh:

—"Once the Heavenly Dao is amended this time, I am absolutely going to retire! I'm completely serious this time."

He spoke as though his previous declarations about [lying flat](# "Literal CN: 躺平 (tǎngpíng) — modern slang for opting out of the rat race to relax") and retiring hadn't been serious.

Lu Shulong, however, froze in astonishment:

—"Amending the Heavenly Dao?! Master, you can actually accomplish even something like that now?!"

[...Right, I never did mention it to Shulong.]

—"Thanks to your Eldest Senior Sister, after repairing the Ascension Stairway, I managed to grasp a thread of possibility,"-Zhou Hui pondered for a moment, then looked toward Lu Shulong.-"Would you like to take a look?"

[—"Look?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

—"Yes. As a True Dragon, you should be capable of 'seeing' it. It just so happens that I also require a third party to assist me in calibrating the coordinates,"-Zhou Hui said, extending his hand toward Lu Shulong.

Lu Shulong stared at Zhou Hui in utter disbelief.

Only after several seconds did he tremblingly place his hand into Zhou Hui's palm.

There was no helping it; it had been far too long.

Lu Shulong had lived through seven entire lifetimes! Not only had he struggled himself, but he had also watched his fellow martial brothers and sisters struggle, and had even watched his all-powerful Master struggle—yet none of them had been able to overturn the fate they so desperately needed to change.

Consequently, even when Master spoke with such certainty, Lu Shulong still found it difficult to believe.

Yet Zhou Hui had never once disappointed the expectations of his disciples.

The instant Lu Shulong's hand settled into Zhou Hui's palm, he realized everything around him had changed.

No, calling it a "change" was not entirely accurate.

White Jade Capital was still there; the vast earth below was still there; the cultivation world still existed. Yet they had all transformed into something resembling a child's crude stick-figure doodle, so primitive that only lines remained—to the point where he and Master had both become little stick figures within the drawing.

The Ascension Stairway, however, eerily retained its original form.

Amidst the chaotic mess of lines, it appeared remarkably solid and tangible. The stick-figure clouds and mist could no longer obscure its true body. One could see it extending endlessly toward the highest heavens; lamentably, it had been severed in the middle. Otherwise, it would have connected directly to a similarly solid "island" suspended above.

Only now did Lu Shulong notice that floating within the "sky" were several similar "islands."

Some "islands" were filled with singing birds and fragrant blossoms; some were laden with rare treasures and exotic jewels; others bore resplendent, golden palaces. Yet there were also "islands" that were utterly desolate, and some completely choked with dense demonic qi.

—"Do you see them? Those are the 'upper realms,'"-the stick-figure Master explained, his tone identical to his usual lecture voice, completely devoid of any agitation.-"They were originally situated in different spatial dimensions. But because the Ascension Stairway exists in a different dimension, once one possesses sufficient cultivation—or is an inherently high-dimensional being like your Dragon Race—one can employ specific methods to drag them into a single plane for observation."

Lu Shulong listened in silence. There were many terms in Zhou Hui's explanation that he didn't understand in the slightest, such as "dimensions" and "high-dimensional organisms." But Lu Shulong did not ask. He knew all too well that the moment he asked, countless follow-up lectures would be waiting for him.

When necessary, Lu Shulong had no objection to ruthlessly suppressing his superfluous curiosity.

In any case, Master would explain the most crucial parts on his own.

Zhou Hui continued:

—"As for our world, it is only because I adjusted the observational angle that it manifests in this two-dimensional planar form. However, such a simplified display actually makes it easier to grasp the key points. Look over there—at those coordinates, the traces of the Heavenly Dao can be easily discerned."

Lu Shulong's expression grew increasingly solemn as he strove with all his might to appear like an attentive, diligent student listening to a lecture.

As for what he had actually discerned... Heh.

At this moment, Lu Shulong's only prayer was that Master was merely letting him look, and wouldn't force him to write a lab report. Otherwise, he wouldn't be able to produce a single character.

[As expected, for this kind of coursework, having Second Senior Brother here would be infinitely better!]

Yet under the sheer terror of the word "report," Lu Shulong dared not let his thoughts drift too far afield. He concentrated fully on absorbing Master's words. After all, it wasn't as if everything was beyond his comprehension; certain aspects were actually quite fascinating:

—"...Theoretically, the Heavenly Dao of every world is woven into existence by the world itself,"-Zhou Hui said.-"Every move and action of all living beings is governed by the Heavenly Dao, yet simultaneously weaves the Heavenly Dao anew. For instance, the Divine-Demon Ruins below: even in such utter chaos, new threads of the Heavenly Dao are continually being generated."

Hearing this, Lu Shulong craned his neck to look downward. He saw cluster upon cluster of tangled black lines, chaotic and disorderly.

Lu Shulong nodded with profound solemnity, putting on the appearance of someone who had just experienced a deep epiphany.

—"However, vermin have crawled in,"-Zhou Hui said, reaching out to grasp one of the lines.

It was a pristine white silk thread. It looked pure and immaculate, even radiating a lustrous pearlescent glow. Yet within Zhou Hui's grip, it behaved like the silken thread of a spiderweb—sticky, viscous, and clinging.

This time, Lu Shulong finally understood.

He watched this silk thread drape downward, tangling into the chaotic lines below and becoming an integral part of this world, making it impossible to distinguish which portion was the original Heavenly Dao and which was this invasive thread.

Even as a member of the Dragon Race, Lu Shulong felt an instantaneous wave of goosebumps crawl across his scalp, feeling as though he were a tiny insect trapped at the center of a spider's web.

—"Then let's just sever it!"-Lu Shulong said.

And he immediately acted upon his words.

His hand abruptly morphed into a draconic claw, swiping violently toward that silk thread.

Yet those dragon claws—capable of shredding the hardest purple steel like flimsy paper and crushing the densest boulders into fine powder with ease—could not shake the thread in the slightest. The mere moment of contact unleashed a colossal repulsive force.

Lu Shulong was jolted backward several steps.

He stared at that silk thread in shock, unable to comprehend how Master could casually hold such a thing in his hand with such effortless poise.

Evidently, if Zhou Hui wanted to sever it, he was not without means.

Zhou Hui acknowledged as much:

—"That is indeed one viable proposal. If there truly were no other alternative, simply severing it and then painstakingly combing through every section it modified should allow us to alter the Heavenly Dao. However, this is an undertaking requiring immense time and infinite patience—"

[Since when have you ever lacked patience, Master? Isn't this kind of prolonged, meticulous labor precisely your greatest specialty?]

Lu Shulong grumbled internally.

Yet from Zhou Hui's tone, he sensed a faint sliver of fury.

Master was angry!

Fortunately, this wrath was not directed at Lu Shulong, but rather at the "master" of that silk thread.

Yanking on the silk thread, Zhou Hui let out a cold sneer:

—"In fact, the plan I personally favor is simply yanking those fellows up there down by force!"

As he spoke, Zhou Hui gave a sudden, violent tug.

The silk thread stretched taut to its absolute limit, causing the floating "island" anchored to its opposite end to violently tremble.

Lu Shulong's eyes bulged in astonishment.

Yet in the very next moment, Zhou Hui released his grip:

—"Just joking. This is not a literal 'thread.' No matter how immense one's physical strength, it cannot be dragged down in such a manner."

—"I actually feel as though you almost pulled it off, Master,"-Lu Shulong said in awe.

—"Impossible. You saw wrong,"-Zhou Hui replied flatly.

Seeing that Lu Shulong still looked ready to argue the point, Zhou Hui gave the "thread" in his hand a gentle twist. The "thread" instantly transformed into an elongated "pathway."

Not only that, but the world was no longer rendered as a chaotic mess of doodles, but rather as endless crisscrossing pathways. Countless living beings crawled along these narrow ribbons like tiny ants, making the "Heavenly Dao" resemble a gigantic spiderweb even more vividly.

—"This is how the Heavenly Dao appears from an alternate observational perspective,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"Have you noticed? It is not a 'thread.'"

[I don't understand.]

[I haven't understood a single thing from the very beginning.]

Lu Shulong abandoned his resistance entirely. Nodding with grave solemnity, he pretended to have understood completely, asking:

—"In that case, how do you intend to proceed, Master?"

—"[A certain great sage once said: "In truth, there were never roads upon the earth; where many people walk, a road is formed,"](# "Literal CN: quote by Lu Xun (鲁迅) from his 1921 classic short story 'Hometown' (故乡)")"-Zhou Hui recited, gazing toward the direction connected by the ribbon.-"What's more, a road originally existed here in the first place. As long as we determine where they hail from, we can weave a brand-new Great Dao from below and march straight up."

[Mhm, so the only difference is between dragging them down to beat them to a pulp, versus climbing up ourselves to beat them to a pulp!]

Lu Shulong felt that he understood once again.

—"As expected of Master! That's simply magnificent!"

This was far better than merely snipping that "thread." After all, having endured seven full reincarnations, Lu Shulong felt that unless he tore the bastards who tampered with the Heavenly Dao limb from limb, it would be impossible to dispel the rancor burning in his chest.

Clutching Zhou Hui's sleeve, Lu Shulong pleaded:

—"Master, when the time comes, please take me with you!"

—"It won't be that fast,"-Zhou Hui replied with a rueful smile.-"Weaving a path that ascends requires an astronomical amount of providential qi. It cannot be accomplished overnight. In fact, over this recent period, all I've managed to do is pinpoint their coordinates."

—"Providential qi? Didn't Master harvest a tremendous amount of it recently?"-Lu Shulong asked.

—"Compared to the Heavenly Dao, that pittance amounts to virtually nothing,"-Zhou Hui said, shaking his head.

Moreover, the providential qi from the Southern Domain was somewhat like an advance loan; until the Demon Race Academies yielded tangible academic results, there wouldn't be much more incoming providential qi. Meanwhile, in the Central Domain, final examinations had just concluded, making it difficult to generate fresh providential qi in the short term. He wondered whether launching summer cram schools or extracurricular interest clubs might generate some providential qi...

Watching Master's deeply contemplative expression, Lu Shulong suddenly shivered.

[An ominous premonition.]

[A dread premonition that extra homework is about to be assigned.]

Lu Shulong hastily glanced around, desperately seeking a way to divert Master's attention. But all around them was the tangled mess of the "Heavenly Dao"—how could he possibly...?

—"M-Master, what is that over there?"-Lu Shulong asked, pointing in a particular direction.

Hearing this, Zhou Hui lifted his eyes absentmindedly, only to freeze:

—"A turning point...?"

[—"A turning point?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

—"Yes. An opportune turning point that brings providential qi. What's more..."-Zhou Hui stared intently at that slender thread. Over these past few days, he had been continuously combing through the Heavenly Dao. Even without the specialized divination expertise of Patriarch Jiuzhai, he had managed to deduce a few patterns.-"What's more, it seems to be related to a 'guest'?"

If one were to speak of notable guests in the near future, that would have to be Jian Nuo, wouldn't it?

It should be him... right?

---

While Zhou Hui was pondering this, Jian Nuo was indeed barreling straight toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

He seemed not to have considered the direction of Eternal Villa in the slightest, nor did he slow his pace for any reason whatsoever. Following the route most commonly traveled by cultivators, he charged straight for the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the Central Domain. In fact, much like the fresh Sky City blossoms he unfailingly sent to Qingyang Qingyu every single day, he arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect precisely at the crack of dawn the following morning, while the dew was still fresh.

—"Is that brat out of his mind?!"-Villa Master Qingyang cursed.

Villa Master Qingyang had naturally received Jian Haoge's "notification."

In truth, even if Jian Haoge hadn't frantically contacted various factions across the realm, Eternal Villa had separate intelligence channels to learn of Young Master Jian's travels.

Villa Master Qingyang had recently been residing in a temporary immortal cave near the Heavenly Spirit Sect. He had even hesitated for three whole seconds, contemplating whether he ought to make a trip back to Eternal Villa for Jian Nuo's sake.

However, he abandoned that notion almost immediately:

If the visitor were merely the Young Master of Sky City, no matter how modest his cultivation realm, he would still warrant Villa Master Qingyang making a dedicated trip back. But if he was merely a "wild punk pursuing my family's precious child," what right did such a fellow have to command his attention?!

Besides, the brat might not even head to Eternal Villa at all—he would likely run straight to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to pester his child!

In that case, giving him any pleasant face was even more out of the question!

So Villa Master Qingyang firmly resolved.

Naturally, allowing the brat to approach his child unimpeded was completely out of the question.

Consequently, Villa Master Qingyang established a united front with his own disciples, decreeing that if Jian Nuo went to Eternal Villa, they would employ every conceivable method to obstruct him and ensure he never got the chance to approach Qingyu.

As for the scenario where Jian Nuo came to the Heavenly Spirit Sect first, he had already coordinated with the Heavenly Spirit Sect to ensure he could appear at the earliest possible moment:

It was not that Villa Master Qingyang intended to roll up his sleeves and enter the ring personally. Rather, within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, the one who would inevitably step forward to welcome the "Young Master of Sky City" was bound to be Qu Qianxing. Thus, Villa Master Qingyang wanted to witness that wild punk feeling [thoroughly inadequate and humiliated](# "Literal CN: 自惭形秽 (zìcán-xínghuì) — feeling ashamed of one's own unworthiness and inferiority when compared to someone exceptional") before Qianxing'er!

Of course, Qianxing'er was flawless in every way—except that he was far too modest!

*(Heavenly Spirit Sect direct disciples: Second Senior Brother? Modest?!)*

If Jian Nuo was too thick-skinned and Qianxing'er was too humble to [put the brat in his place](# "Literal CN: 给那小子好看 (gěi nà xiǎozǐ hǎokàn) — give him a harsh lesson / give him what for"), then Villa Master Qingyang could step in as Qianxing'er's spokesperson, teaching Jian Nuo exactly what kind of male immortal was truly worthy of his family's Qingyu!

—"How is that any different from stepping into the arena yourself?"-Qingyang Qingyu said, rubbing her temples in exasperation.-"Venerable Ancestor, could you please refrain from ruining my reputation?"

Only then did Villa Master Qingyang realize he had voiced his private schemes aloud.

—"Ruining your reputation?! I heard that punk actually dared to tell the City Lord of Sky City that you were his wife—now *that* is ruining your reputation!"-Villa Master Qingyang fumed indignantly.-"If you were a maiden from an ordinary mortal family, you would be utterly unmarriageable!"

—"That... is indeed true,"-Qingyang Qingyu conceded, furrowing her brows and nodding.

While she didn't care excessively about conventional marital reputations, for Jian Nuo to blabber so carelessly without any formal betrothal was undeniably reckless and thoughtless. She intended to confront him directly; if he refused to mend his ways, he truly wasn't someone suitable for marriage.

Seeing that Qingyu agreed with his perspective, Villa Master Qingyang's complexion improved considerably.

Pausing briefly, his eyebrows knotted together again:

—"Weren't you researching the secret realm system inside the Spirit Stone Secret Realm? Why did you come running to the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Don't tell me it was for that punk?"

—"A mere coincidence. I was scheduled to submit my research report around these few days anyway,"-Qingyu explained.-"It just so happened that Senior Brother Qianxing informed me that Jian Nuo was coming to look for me, so I simply decided to stay."

[Qianxing'er, why must you be so honest and straightforward?!]

Hearing this, Villa Master Qingyang looked toward Qu Qianxing with profound grief and exasperation.

Qu Qianxing appeared entirely oblivious to Villa Master Qingyang's inner turmoil, merely smiling politely:

—"News reports indicate that Young Master Jian has reached Qianxing Hall. He should arrive at our mountain gate in about a quarter of an hour. This junior shall head out first to... Master?"

Qu Qianxing looked over in astonishment, only to see Zhou Hui standing there, accompanied by a haggard, listless Lu Shulong.

—"Our guest is arriving, yes? I would like to go take a look,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Master?"-Qu Qianxing arched his brows in utter perplexity.

The visitor was "merely" the Young Master of Sky City. According to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's standard protocol, he was naturally far from worthy of Zhou Hui welcoming him personally.

Even someone like Su Xin, brandishing the banner of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, had only been received by Lu Shulong.

Had Yuwen Xuan not stirred up chaos, Su Xin wouldn't have even caught a glimpse of Zhou Hui.

What merit or virtue did Jian Nuo possess that could warrant Master welcoming him in person?

Rumor had it that his great-grandfather and Master had an exceptionally terrible relationship—wait, was it truly terrible?

In that fleeting instant, countless suspicious theories flashed through Qu Qianxing's mind. Yet it was inappropriate to ask outright, so he merely directed his gaze toward Lu Shulong.

Lu Shulong offered a frail, sickly smile.

Because Jian Nuo hadn't arrived immediately, he had been dragged by Master to calculate the Heavenly Dao for an entire day and night! At this point, forget Young Master Jian—even if it were a [young moth](# "Literal CN: 简蛾子 (Jiǎn ézi) — a pun on 简公子 (Young Master Jian) swapping 'gong' (公) for 'e' (蛾, moth), rhyming with 出幺蛾子 (stirring up demonic tricks)"), as long as it spared him from calculating that cosmic nightmare any longer, he would gladly run out to welcome it with open arms!

And so, each harboring their own divergent thoughts, the group arrived before the mountain gate of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

The Sundial Clock was punctual as always. Accompanying the ringing of the bell, a majestic, grand herd of winged celestial steeds appeared before the mountain gate of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Those unaware might have assumed an invading army had come to lay siege to the sect.

And the spirited, dashing young man leading the charge was none other than Jian Nuo.

—"He is indeed a true 'Young Master Jian,'"-Zhou Hui murmured with a faint smile, reminded of an old acquaintance.

—"Hmph, [gaudy ostentation](# "Literal CN: 敷张扬厉 (fūzhāng-yánglì) — idiom meaning to show off brazenly with excessive fanfare and theatrical display")!"-Villa Master Qingyang snorted in disgust.

Fortunately, Jian Nuo dismounted from his celestial steed almost instantly, gliding down toward the mountain gate.

Even though Zhou Hui had stepped out in person, he naturally would not stride forward to greet a mere junior.

Standing at the very front as the public face of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Qu Qianxing stepped forward:

—"Welcome—"-Qu Qianxing began.

Jian Nuo bypassed Qu Qianxing without even sparing him a single glance.

His expression was flushed with excitement, his eyes fixed unwaveringly straight ahead, as though nothing else in the world could register in his vision. Words tumbled from his lips:

—"I've finally met you—"-Jian Nuo uttered, his voice trembling with fervor.

—"Hold on! What do you think you're doing?!"-Villa Master Qingyang barked fiercely, pulling Qingyang Qingyu behind him to shield her. He resolved that if this punk dared act insolent toward Qingyu in his presence, he couldn't care less whether he was the heir of Sky City or the flesh-and-blood scion of a Tribulation Transcendence titan—he would give the boy a thrashing he'd never forget!

Yet Jian Nuo walked right past Villa Master Qingyang without blinking an eye.

Now, even Qingyang Qingyu tilted her head in curiosity, peering toward the rear.

Behind them stood the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Lu Shulong.

Lu Shulong had looked on the verge of coughing up his own soul just a moment ago. Seeing Young Master Jian's manic demeanor sent a shiver of revulsion down his spine, causing him to instinctively take two steps back and hide behind Master.

However, this maneuver had zero effect. Young Master Jian fell to his knees with blazing fervor—right before the Untiring Immortal Venerable:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable! I have finally met you!"

—"I grew up listening to your legendary deeds! I revere and adore you so, so very much!"

[Mhm, mhm... WAIT, WHAT?!]

[Weren't you here to see Qingyang Qingyu?!]

Everyone's eyes bulged in shock—especially the two disciples of the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

[Whoa... What kind of [battlefield of jealous rivalry](# "Literal CN: 修罗场 (xiūluóchǎng) — Asura field; popular ACG slang for an intense, awkward romantic/interpersonal clash or jealousy showdown") is this?!]

As the apparent center of this drama, Qingyang Qingyu glanced at Lu Shulong, who was currently scrambling out from behind Master in a panic to shield him, and then looked at Qu Qianxing's expression on the other side. She was practically tempted to let out a loud whistle!
