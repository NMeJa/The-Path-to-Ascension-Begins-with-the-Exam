# Chapter 168: Misunderstanding

In the end, Boss Tao was still dragged off to the Southern Domain.

His reason for traveling to the Southern Domain was naturally not because of Mo Xi's flattering compliments, which had been cut from the exact same cloth as Yuwen Xuan's, but because of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's invitation.

No one could refuse the Untiring Immortal Venerable; Tao Zhu was no exception.

Furthermore, Zhou Hui hadn't given Boss Tao much time to think it over in the first place.

The very second after Tao Zhu gave his reluctant assent, the entire party was already standing in the Southern Domain.

Such speed was astonishingly rapid—faster even than walking from the living quarters of an immortal cave into its courtyard.

Even as a Mahayana titan, Tao Zhu found it impossible to adapt to this manner of spatial transition—no, precisely because he was at the Mahayana stage, possessing greater dimensional "mass," he was far more capable of perceiving Zhou Hui's power than an ordinary person, and consequently even less capable of adapting to it.

Thus, the moment the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the others teleported to the Southern Domain, Boss Tao's body went completely limp.

In a display of sheer "fragility," he complained:

—"Croaaak, Untiring... no, could you please not teleport like this? [These old bones of mine](# "Literal CN: 一把老骨头 (yī bǎ lǎo gútou) — these old bones / an old codger's frail body")... can't adapt to it,"-Tao Zhu wheezed.

—"I will keep that in mind next time,"-Zhou Hui replied in all seriousness.

Tao Zhu didn't believe him for a second.

He knew Zhou Hui's temperament inside out. He knew the fellow's reply was completely sincere, but his method of "keeping it in mind" would inevitably have issues: for instance, next time, the "instantaneous teleportation" would likely become a teleportation "lasting thirty whole seconds."

Deliberately dragging out the transition time like that would only prolong the motion sickness caused by the spatial shift; it was far better left undone.

Therefore, Boss Tao intended to discuss this matter with Zhou Hui in detail. Yet before Tao Zhu could open his mouth, Mo Bei beat him to it, grabbing hold of Zhou Hui's sleeve and crying out in excitement:

[—"Master, it's so magnificent!"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

What Mo Bei was referring to was naturally the center of the Southern Domain, the sacred land of the demon race:

The Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

This tree was indeed extraordinarily magnificent.

Rather than calling it a tree, it was more apt to describe it as a mountain—a city.

The diameter of this titanic tree was larger than an entire metropolis, and its height surpassed that of a mountain range. Due to its sheer altitude, the foliage of this gargantuan timber displayed distinct aspects across different elevations:

For instance, the boughs at the very summit glowed a luminous golden-red, blazing furiously into the clouds like the flames of the [Golden Crow](# "Mythological: 金乌 (Jīnwū) — the three-legged solar bird representing the sun in ancient Chinese mythology"); only the venerated sovereigns of the demon race, the Phoenix clan, were permitted to dwell there.

Beneath the golden-red canopy hung boughs cloaked year-round in vibrant reds and yellows, looking like perpetual autumn yet brimming with abundant spiritual energy, providing a habitat for various spiritual beasts.

Further down beneath that autumn canopy were lush green leaves, emerald and luxuriant. Upon its sprawling boughs roosted not only diverse demon clans and rare beasts, but actual trees and tangled vines sprouted from the wood as well, growing upon the Sacred Tree just as they would on solid ground, creating the illusion of several layered forests stacked one atop the other.

Beneath this "forest" lay what appeared to be the tree's root system. The colossal trunk seemed to erupt from the soil, sprawling across tens of thousands of *li* of [land](# "Translation note: The Chinese raw contains a voice-input typo '徒弟' (disciples) for '土地' (land)"). Furthermore, owing to the lush canopy above, the central trunk received virtually no sunlight, remaining shrouded in perpetual darkness year-round and attracting insects and shade-loving creatures to nest within.

Yet this was still not the entirety of the Sacred Tree.

If one were to slice through the layer of fallen leaves beneath the tree—a bed accumulated over tens of thousands of years—one would discover that the so-called "earth" was merely a portion of the tree's own withered boughs and foliage. The gargantuan trunk extended downward; after passing through the dusky, pitch-black sections that lay buried beneath dead leaves as if dead themselves, deep beneath the subterranean strata it gradually shifted into white, silver-white, and pure silver, radiating a pristine luminescence like the moon itself. As it sprawled and grew beneath the thick layers of geological bedrock, it formed a spectacle even more magnificent than the canopy above. It was said that these root veins permeated the entirety of the Southern Domain, with several of the thickest taproots leading directly to pocket worlds inhabited exclusively by the demon race—the so-called Demon Realm.

And that was still not the end.

Unsatisfied with merely dominating the entirety of the Southern Domain, the roots of the Sacred Tree continued their downward descent until they penetrated clear through the southern continental crust, extending all the way into the pitch-black Abyssal Ocean. Yet the roots steeped within the Abyssal Ocean did not lose their pristine radiance; instead, they ignited into roaring flames just like the boughs at the canopy's summit, radiating that very same Golden Crow brilliance.

Due to the bizarre temperature contrast between the freezing Abyssal Ocean and the fiery blaze of the Sacred Tree, even the Phoenix clan could not survive in these waters, making it the Southern Domain's sole forbidden ground. Nonetheless, demon legends whispered that deep within the heart of those flames, figures resembling divine beings had occasionally been glimpsed coming and going.

Naturally, at depths so profound, even the mightiest demons could never reach them, to say nothing of anyone else.

Thus, what Mo Bei was marveling at was merely the terrestrial portion of the Sacred Tree.

Even so, this portion alone was more than enough to stun any outsider setting foot in the Southern Domain for the first time.

However, upon hearing this exclamation, Zhou Hui instinctively glanced at his disciple. Confirming that this was Mo Bei rather than Mo Xi was what truly caught him by surprise.

After all, rather than calling his youngest female disciple aloof and cold, it was more accurate to say she lacked emotional sensibility—as though in her eyes, anything in this world was just fine.

Whether it was White Jade Capital or the immortals' bazaar beneath the Abyssal Ocean, neither had managed to draw the slightest emotional reaction from her.

Yet today, she had actually praised the Sacred Tree as "magnificent"...

[Wait a moment—could this child actually have a penchant for botanical scenery?]

Although she claimed the Snow-Glow Grass outside her door had been brought along "somehow or other," the fact that she had tended to those grasses day in and day out for years on end—on top of completing Zhou Hui's academic assignments—already spoke volumes.

In fact, before the expansion of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mo Bei's immortal cave had actually been the most picturesque spot in the entire sect.

Zhou Hui realized this in a sudden flash of insight.

He turned to Mo Bei to confirm:

—"Do you like this scenery?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Like it?"-Mo Bei lifted her face expressionlessly, her tone barely differing from usual.-"I suppose... it's alright?"

Yet her sparkling eyes betrayed her true feelings.

Sadly, Mo Bei herself did not understand her own emotions. Owing to her upbringing, she had suppressed her feelings far too severely for far too long, eventually losing the ability to define her own emotions and mistaking other people's impressions for her own.

At this moment, facing Zhou Hui's intrigued gaze, she released her grip on her master's sleeve and spoke slowly:

—"Naturally, if we're talking about magnificence, our White Jade Capital is even more magnificent,"-she added. At least, that was what everyone said.

[It really wouldn't matter if you liked the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree better.]

Zhou Hui wanted to say as much, but considering it might place even greater pressure on Mo Bei, he swallowed the words back down.

[Had I known this child actually liked places like this, I shouldn't have sent her to the Western Domain back in the third life.]

[Thinking back now, that was probably the place she despised most.]

[Ah well, what's done is done; I must pay closer attention in the future.]

[Ideally, I should devise a method that allows this child to travel and gain worldly experience without worrying about her Extreme Yin Constitution, so she can witness more sights that stir her soul and discover her own preferences.]

Zhou Hui mused in his heart with quiet self-reproach.

He offered a reassuring smile to Mo Bei, who was looking up at him uneasily:

—"White Jade Capital is magnificent, of course; it is a miracle of human cultivation civilization,"-Zhou Hui said, comforting her.-"Yet the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree is by no means inferior; it is a miracle of the demon race. The two are much the same."

Before Mo Bei could react to Zhou Hui's remark, a clear voice rang out:

[—"As expected of the Immortal Venerable—wholly devoid of the arrogance common to ordinary humans!"](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

Accompanying this voice, several clusters of flame drifted down from the towering treetops. Sizzling and crackling as they burned, they transformed into a group of charming demon youths. The one manifested from the blue flame was none other than Shui Huang'er, who had previously visited the Central Domain.

Leading the flock of youths, she first performed a deep, formal bow toward Zhou Hui and Tao Zhu, then beamed foolishly at Mo Bei.

Mo Bei instinctively retracted all emotion, adopting an expression identical to Mo Xi's guarded look.

—"I already said there was no need to come out specially to greet us,"-Zhou Hui said to Shui Huang'er.

This was a polite pleasantry.

Purely from the standpoint of the Untiring Immortal Venerable and Boss Tao—one at Tribulation Transcendence, the other at Mahayana—paying a joint visit, the demons of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree could hardly turn a blind eye.

It was also out of consideration for their sensitivities that Zhou Hui had not teleported directly into the interior of the Sacred Tree, but had paused in the open skies outside, effectively announcing their arrival to their hosts.

The demon race naturally appreciated the courtesy and dispatched only the familiar Shui Huang'er to receive them.

—"Rest assured, Lord Immortal Venerable, I understand completely! You wish to inspect how the Heavenly Spirit Sect's teaching materials are being promoted across the Sacred Tree, don't you?"-Shui Huang'er said with a radiant smile.-"We didn't announce your arrival, guaranteeing that you can verify the most authentic, unfiltered situation."

Saying so, she guided Zhou Hui and his party toward the interior of the Sacred Tree.

If this had been the human race, any so-called "unfiltered situation" would have required a discount, more or less involving some degree of [vanity projects and window dressing](# "Literal CN: 面子工程 (miànzi gōngchéng) — face-saving engineering / superficial vanity projects to impress higher-ups").

Yet the demon race was genuinely forthright; when Shui Huang'er said unfiltered, she meant 100% unfiltered. She led Zhou Hui into the "forest" section along the middle tier of the Sacred Tree, which was also the region where the greatest concentration of ordinary demons dwelt.

Yet barely had they stepped into this "forest" when the faint sound of wailing drifted through the air.

And it wasn't just a single child—it sounded like scores of children sobbing in chorus.

Mo Bei immediately shuddered expressionlessly.

Mo Xi, on the other hand, furrowed his brows and asked directly:

[—"What is with all this crying?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"[School starts today](# "Literal CN: 今天开学 (jīntiān kāixué) — the school term begins today"); isn't crying completely normal?"-Shui Huang'er asked, utterly baffled by the question.

Right as she spoke, a "child"—one whose upper torso resembled a human toddler, but whose lower half bore a long, green serpent tail—darted out from the dense foliage, slithering down the massive branch as swiftly as flowing water.

As it slid, it wailed at the top of its lungs:

[—"I don't wanna go to school!"](# "Speaker: Snake-Demon Child")

Regrettably, its protest proved entirely futile. From the spot where the "child" had burrowed out, a colossal serpent tail whipped forth, snagging the "child" in a single sweep and reeling it back while a cursing adult voice barked:

[—"Don't wanna my foot! This is the clan leader's direct order!"](# "Speaker: Adult Snake-Demon")

—"No! You can't sell me out like this!"-the "child" shrieked in heartbreaking despair.

And this spectacle was evidently not limited to the snake demons. In the chaotic intervals of this hunt-like scenario, several young demons in the forms of various small animals scampered frantically across the forest—some succeeding in their escape, others dragged back kicking and screaming.

This sight rendered even Zhou Hui thoroughly speechless.

Dense though she was, Shui Huang'er eventually noticed the shift in atmosphere:

[—"...Lord Immortal Venerable, is something amiss?"](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

There was no need for Zhou Hui to explain; one of Shui Huang'er's attendants whispered something softly in her ear.

Shui Huang'er's expression cleared into sudden realization:

—"Now that you mention it, that makes sense! Are human children willing to attend school voluntarily? How truly admirable! Sadly, some of our demon clans are, as you humans put it, uncivilized, and aren't accustomed to the whole academy concept. No matter, we have already implemented counter-measures. This way, please!"-Shui Huang'er chirped enthusiastically.

Hearing her words, Zhou Hui was instead seized by an ominous premonition.

Still, [since they were already here anyway](# "Literal CN: 来都来了 (lái dōu lái le) — iconic Chinese catchphrase of resignation: 'we're already here anyway, might as well see it through'"), he might as well see just how unhinged the demon race could get.

And so, following Shui Huang'er a short distance further, they were instantly stunned by the scene before their eyes.

The forest was still a forest, except that from virtually every branch hung a demon, with silencing spells sealing their voices. Thus, stepping into this clearing, one could only hear the wind rustling through the treetops while beholding silent, bizarrely shaped monsters dangling from the boughs—looking for all the world like some macabre suicide grove, or the [emergency pantry](# "Literal CN: 储备粮仓库 (chǔbèi liáng cāngkù) — emergency food ration storage / stockpile") of some primordial archdemon.

—"How is it? Pretty good, right? This is the disciplinary method we learned from the Heavenly Spirit Sect! No matter how disobedient a child is, after hanging up there for a few days, they become obedient,"-Shui Huang'er proclaimed with triumphant pride.

[...No, our Heavenly Spirit Sect has no such disciplinary method.]

Zhou Hui wanted to defend his sect, but then noticed Tao Zhu secretly cowering back, looking as though he were terrified Zhou Hui might string him up next.

[Old Tao, what kind of reaction is that?!]

Zhou Hui facepalmed.

Failing to receive the desired reaction from the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and lacking the courage to interrogate him, Shui Huang'er turned to Mo Bei instead:

[—"Sister Mo Bei, what do you think?"](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

—"Very good,"-Mo Bei murmured blankly. Gazing upon the bodies swaying gently in the breeze, she recalled her Eldest Senior Brother whom she had seen hanging from a tree branch barely minutes ago.

At this moment, whatever aesthetic awe she had harbored for the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree vanished without a trace, leaving only a sigh:

[—"I feel as if I've returned to my own sect."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

To Shui Huang'er, this sounded like the highest form of praise:

—"Then I really did get it right!"-she giggled in delight.
