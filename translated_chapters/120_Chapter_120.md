# Chapter 120: [The Trend](<# "Literal CN: 潮流 (cháoliú) — Trend / The Tide / New Fashion; chapter title referring to the unstoppable continental cultural and academic craze ignited by White Jade Capital Daily and the Immortal Arts Assembly">) (第120章 潮流)

—"Look at this! This is my article,"-Su Xin said smugly, waving the freshest issue of the *White Jade Capital Week... Daily*.

He was currently lounging inside the [Carefree Pavilion](<# "Literal CN: 逍遥阁 (xiāoyáogé) — Carefree Pavilion; premier luxury entertainment complex in Sky City operated by Carefree Palace">) in Sky City.

Even in Sky City, the Carefree Pavilion was the premier entertainment venue—bar none.

Here lay the finest wines, the most enchanting music and dances, the most breathtaking beauties, the rarest treasures, and the most... ah, the things that followed were somewhat gray and illicit. Unless Carefree Palace was eager to forge a mortal blood feud with the Mei-Zhu couple, they wouldn't dare let Su Xin clap eyes on those services—in fact, letting him even catch wind of them was strictly out of the question.

Consequently, Su Xin and fellow [cultivation scions](<# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū-èrdài) — cultivation second-generation; the privileged, spoiled children of high-ranking immortal elders">) of his ilk generally engaged only in comparatively "wholesome" pastimes inside the Carefree Pavilion.

Even so, this flock of second-generation immortals lingered day and night, indulging themselves until they virtually treated the pavilion as their second home.

Lately, however, these pampered scions had shown little interest in freshly auctioned treasures and found themselves thoroughly unenthusiastic about the beauties' newly rehearsed dances. Their attention had been monopolized entirely by the tempestuous squabbles filling the columns of *White Jade Capital Daily*.

Granted, when it came to the academic content, it was entirely a case of [the words knowing them, but them not knowing the words](<# "Idiom: 它认识他们，他们不认识它 — colloquial expression for complete scientific/academic illiteracy; you can look at the characters, but you have no idea what they mean">). Yet they intimately recognized the cultivators from the Northern Domain! Moreover, those cultivators were very likely their own masters, grandmasters, great-grandfathers, or ancestral patriarchs! To actually witness their own solemn, righteous, immovable "Ancestral Grandmasters of every variety" rolling up their sleeves to enter the mud pit and scream at others in public—how could that fail to make the blood of these young scions boil with excitement?!

—Not to mention, the other information published in *White Jade Capital Daily* was extraordinarily captivating in its own right.

Su Xin was precisely among their number.

As a "cultivation second-generation" far more authentic and pedigree-rich than the rest, he stood upon the very front lines of [eating melon drama](<# "Literal CN: 吃瓜 (chī guā) — eating melons / eating melon seeds; internet slang for gossiping, following drama, or being a spectator to public spectacles">). The moment he discovered his father had actually adopted his name as a pen name, he was propelled straight into a state of ecstatic frenzy.

Su Xin had shamelessly commandeered the article penned by his Tribulation Transcendence father, brazenly boasting across town that he had authored it himself.

His [frivolous clique of fair-weather drinking buddies](<# "Literal CN: 狐朋狗友 (húpéng-gǒuyǒu) — fox-friends and dog-pals; disreputable cronies / frivolous drinking companions">) promptly chimed in with a chaotic chorus of empty flatteries:

[—"As expected of you! Absolutely magnificent!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"If even an article by you can get published, it looks like those Central Domain cultivators aren't all that impressive after all!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"Do you even know how to speak human words?! What do you mean 'even an article by you'?! Our Big Brother Su is at the very least the top dog among the Northern Domain youth, alright?! His article being published is the most natural thing in the world!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"Right, exactly! Big Brother Su is the most accomplished talent among our Northern Domain juniors! He can even participate in high-level debates alongside grandmasters!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

The circle of foppish scions erupted into a wave of thoroughly insincere, uproarious laughter.

Evidently, even brainless, hedonistic fops would never take Su Xin's claims seriously.

A high-stakes debate between supreme powerhouses—for a Foundation Establishment nobody like Su Xin to muscle his way in? What kind of cosmic joke was that?!

Fortunately, precisely because Su Xin had flaunted it so ostentatiously, everyone immediately ruled out the correct answer—that the article had been authored by the Tribulation Transcendence titan Su Mei himself. They merely assumed Su Xin had coerced one of his parents' disciples or grand-disciples into using his name as a pseudonym. Ironically, this spared Su Mei from the agonizing [social death](<# "Literal CN: 社死 (shèsǐ) — social death; extreme public humiliation or exposure of an embarrassing secret">) of having his sockpuppet stripped away.

The other cultivation scions were even somewhat envious that Su Xin's name was printed in the newspaper.

[Having supreme generational seniority is truly magnificent! You can bully your parents' disciples whenever you feel like it.]

The others couldn't help thinking enviously to themselves, their mouths growing increasingly unhinged without a filter:

[—"Big Brother Su! The ultimate victory in this great debate rests entirely on your shoulders!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"We have full faith that you will represent our Northern Domain to thoroughly crush those Central Domain scumbags!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"We suspect Big Brother Su's research paper will be published in *Spells* any day now as well! Show those bastards what a true genius looks like!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

Su Xin had initially listened with a beaming grin, but by the end, his smile was becoming somewhat difficult to maintain.

Pen names in the newspaper were fine—one could choose whatever alias they fancied, and some cultivators even operated several [sockpuppets](<# "Literal CN: 马甲 (mǎjiǎ) — vest / sockpuppet; online pseudonym or alternate persona">) simultaneously. But the academic journal *Spells* was not something to be trifled with. Su Xin had taken merely a single glance at that publication and felt his head was on the verge of detonating. Furthermore, judging by his parents' demeanor, if they were to publish a thesis in that journal, they would unquestionably sign their true, illustrious names.

Most critically, that journal was personally presided over by the Untiring Immortal Venerable. While senior sect masters borrowing disciples' names might be overlooked, any act of encroaching upon or stealing another person's research findings against their will was strictly forbidden. Not only would the victim be privately notified, but the violation would be publicly broadcast in both *Spells* and *White Jade Capital Daily*, exposing the offender before the entire cultivation realm!

This very point had been clarified by the Untiring Immortal Venerable in person when someone raised suspicions during the debate regarding whether certain manuscripts involved plagiarism or improper appropriation.

That proclamation had caused the roaring flame wars to halt for an issue or two, effectively redirecting the trajectory of the continental debate. It dramatically dampened the noise of personal slander and [regional carpet-bombing](<# "Literal CN: 地图炮 (dìtúpào) — map cannon; indiscriminate attacks targeting an entire region or demographic group">), steering the discourse toward genuine doctrinal disputes over the Great Dao.

For instance, the crux of the debate was presently focused between the Central Domain's [*Controllable Ascension Theory*](<# "Literal CN: 飞升可控论 (fēishēng kěkòng lùn) — Controllable Ascension Theory; thesis proposing that ascension is an autonomous, controllable physical migration to higher-energy realms rather than divine judgment">) and the Northern Domain's [*Ascension Reward Hypothesis*](<# "Literal CN: 飞升奖赏说 (fēishēng jiǎngshǎng shuō) — Ascension Reward Hypothesis; traditional dogma asserting ascension is a moral and spiritual reward granted by True Immortals and the Heavenly Dao">).

The former posited that ascension was a natural, controllable progression once a cultivator's cultivation reached full fruition—an autonomous migration driven by an aspiration toward higher realms and superior cultivation environments, journeying voluntarily toward worlds blessed with vastly richer spiritual energy (such as the True Immortal Realm). As such, it was fundamentally controllable.

The latter maintained that ascension was a reward bestowed by True Immortals and the Heavenly Dao in recognition of a cultivator's strength and moral virtue, with heavenly tribulation lightning serving as the divine ordeal prior to receiving that reward.

In truth, the Northern Domain's *Ascension Reward Hypothesis* had historically reigned as the mainstream orthodoxy.

After all, the ancient cultivation realm had been replete with accounts of paragons of virtue—some of whom had never cultivated a single day in their lives, yet were personally escorted into the Heavenly Court by True Immortals. While cultivators did not believe True Immortals held absolute dominion over ascension itself, they believed ascension adhered to certain cosmic laws of the universe: only those who conformed to those laws were permitted to ascend, rendering it an existential "reward."

Conversely, the *Controllable Ascension Theory* was a hypothesis that had surged in popularity during the twilight era of ancient antiquity, shortly before the God-Demon War. Following the disappearance of White Jade Capital, the theory had languished in obscurity for a period. Yet with the total extinction of True Immortals while cultivators continued their pursuit of the Dao, this notion had once again regained immense traction.

However, the Central Domain cultivators' staunch defense of this doctrine differed somewhat from the traditional camp.

Many of them had personally witnessed the Untiring Immortal Venerable capturing heavenly lightning in the palm of his hand, their [three views](<# "Literal CN: 三观粉碎 (sānguān fěnsuì) — world outlook, values, and philosophy of life shattered to pieces">) shattered into dust. During the fiery debates, they had unconsciously gravitated toward this school of thought, leading them to be labeled by Northern Domain scholars as the "Controllable Faction."

Naturally, regardless of which faction was correct, neither was a doctrine that someone of Su Xin's caliber had any business [pestering](<# "Literal CN: 碰瓷 (pèngcí) — porcelain bumping; claiming false connection / trying to latch onto / punching way above one's weight">).

Strictly speaking, anyone below the Void Refinement stage lacked even the fundamental qualification to participate in this discussion.

Consequently, the cultivation scions gathered in the pavilion were merely shouting mindlessly for kicks.

Had this been two years ago, Su Xin would certainly have joined the rabble in their mindless clamor, oblivious to how he was [hoisting himself onto a roasting spit](<# "Idiom: 架在火堆上 (jià zài huǒduī shàng) — placing oneself over the fire; backing oneself into an impossible corner">). In all likelihood, he might genuinely have pulled off the monumental folly of coercing his parents' disciples or grand-disciples into ghostwriting a thesis for him and submitting it to *Spells*.

However, ever since that harrowing examination ordeal at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, while Su Xin could hardly be said to have undergone the "colossal transformation" foretold in the Heavenly Book's prophecy, he had at least learned to thoroughly investigate all caveats and rules before opening his mouth whenever matters concerned the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Regarding the academic journal *Spells*, for instance, Su Xin had extensively cross-examined his parents concerning the prerequisites and prohibitions for publication.

Thus, Su Xin remained acutely, soberly aware: bragging with an article penned by his father in the newspaper was harmless fun, but if he dared harbor designs upon *Spells*, he would find himself plunged straight back to that day on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's coral examination platform... no, into a predicament infinitely more agonizing than that!

Su Xin burst into loud laughter:

—"Ha! What's so fun about publishing a paper in that journal? Not a single word in it is entertaining!"-Su Xin declared.

[—"But anyone capable of publishing on those pages is a grand titan of the Void Refinement or Body Integration stage!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-one scion urged with an envious, seductive cadence.-[—"If you could publish an article in *Spells* and have your name listed side-by-side with them, wouldn't that prove our Big Brother Su is on the exact same footing as those grandmasters?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

Su Xin shot a deep, piercing glare at the speaker and sneered coldly:

—"Why should I have to publish an article in a journal filled with incomprehensible gibberish just to be on equal footing with them? So long as someone is a disciple of my parents, even if they are a Mahayana cultivator, won't they still have to obediently address me as Junior Brother when they see me?! Body Integration and Void Refinement juniors are only worthy of calling me Grand-Martial Uncle!"-Su Xin scoffed.

Such brazen, arrogant swagger elicited a resounding cheer from the rest of the gang.

Even if someone secretly muttered in their heart: [Is having a pair of Tribulation Transcendence parents truly so impressive?!]

Pardon me, but in the cultivation realm, it really was precisely that impressive.

At the very least, not a soul dared actually pressure Su Xin into publishing an academic thesis.

Nevertheless, after being ruffled by this exchange, Su Xin lost all enthusiasm for flaunting his (father's) article.

He tossed the newspaper aside and curled his lip:

—"Alright, enough of this drivel. What other entertaining things have been happening lately?"-Su Xin asked.

[—"Hey, what interesting things could possibly happen here?!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-someone replied.

[—"Exactly, it's been boring to death lately."](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"That's just Sky City for you. Nothing monumental ever occurs."](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

Sky City was undeniably a celestial capital that every cultivator under heaven yearned for. Yet in the eyes of these stimulation-seeking scions, who practically wished for a Second God-Demon War to erupt just for excitement, the city was boring enough to grow weeds.

Particularly with the raucous debates recently raging across *White Jade Capital Daily*, seeing their ancestral elders mobilized into action made their own hearts itch with restless energy, desperate to do something of their own.

Ignited by this continent-wide frenzy, their curiosity regarding the Central Domain reached unprecedented heights, turning into open fascination.

[—"If only we had an Immortal Arts Assembly like the Central Domain!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"Indeed! Watching the match reports is so thrilling!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"How about we play a round of Immortal Chess?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

Someone piped up with the suggestion.

Their Immortal Chess sets had not been purchased during this current wave of popularity, but were an "internal edition" Su Xin had brought back when he visited the Central Domain the previous year. At the time, it had sparked a massive craze among Sky City's cultivation scions. Yet the Central and Northern Domains were separated by staggering distances, and Su Xin's chess set came with zero customer after-sales support. Once everyone grew weary of the fixed maps, they only brought it out for an occasional match whenever they were dying of sheer boredom, lacking their former fanatical zeal.

This time, however, the venomous squabbles among supreme cultivators led to astronomical sales of *White Jade Capital Daily*, granting young disciples across major sects the fortune of reading the paper and learning about developments in the Central Domain.

High-altitude doctrinal debates held little meaning for the younger generation beyond popcorn entertainment. What truly captured their imagination were the tournament reports on the Immortal Arts Assembly. Above all, a cultivation realm newspaper offered far more than mere text:

Right at that moment, for instance, someone retrieved the newspaper Su Xin had cast aside, meticulously smoothed out the creases, and laid it flat across the table.

Instantly, a miniature battle arena materialized above the parchment. Two competitors stood at opposite ends of the platform, reenacting the most dazzling highlight moments of their duel in tandem with the accompanying analytical commentary.

Watching the entire segment play out was virtually indistinguishable from a curated short-video livestream.

—Even if, in the cultivation realm, not a soul understood the concept of a "short-video livestream."

Consequently, the underlying motive driving countless sect disciples to acquire *White Jade Capital Daily* had long since shifted: they bought the paper solely to watch the Immortal Arts Assembly!

Through this spectacle, the Immortal Arts Assembly had drawn the undivided attention of the entire cultivation world.

Under these circumstances, precisely as Qu Qianxing had duped the Central Domain elders, anyone who seized victory in the Immortal Arts Assembly would truly become an undisputed representative cultivator of the Central Domain.

Yet that was merely what ordinary cultivators fixated upon.

These cultivation scions considered themselves a cut above the rest:

To them, the Immortal Arts Assembly was nothing more than an elaborate mechanism for selecting new figurines for their Immortal Chess boards!

Thus, their passion for Immortal Chess reignited with a vengeance. They even attempted to recreate every single match upon their "internal beta" boards.

[—"What figurines competed today?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"Are there any pieces we can use as temporary substitutes?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"What map was the arena this time?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

They discussed the tactical layouts with feverish enthusiasm.

Seeing their fervor, Su Xin curled his lip:

—"Give it a rest. Today's match was a [terrain kill](<# "Literal CN: 地形杀 (dìxíngshā) — environmental kill / terrain kill; winning by manipulating the map geography to eliminate an opponent">); it's impossible to recreate,"-Su Xin said.

Otherwise, he wouldn't have spent his time solely boasting about his (father's) article.

Having arrived with the freshest copy of the paper, Su Xin had naturally devoured every shred of content within.

The others had not yet seen it—when it came to delivery speed, the Mei-Zhu couple received the newspaper earlier than anyone else in the Northern Domain, bar none. The scions devoured the report with relish, exhaling wistful sighs:

[—"Simulating these duels has gotten harder and harder lately."](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"Indeed. There are too many maps, and the contestants' moves have grown far more diverse."](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"It seems those Central Domain cultivators truly have some skill!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

[—"I wonder what the new Immortal Chess edition will look like."](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-Having reached this thought, someone turned to Su Xin and asked,-[—"Speaking of which, Big Brother Su, are you still unable to buy the Auction Version of Immortal Chess?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

Su Xin's expression instantly darkened.

Another scion smacked the tactless fool across the back of the head:

[—"What do you mean 'unable to buy'?! Big Brother Su merely disdains to buy it!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

Disdaining it was hardly the case.

Rather, during the period of the auction, Su Xin had happened to grow weary of the (internal beta) chess set. Furthermore, the winter solstice had just passed, New Year celebrations were approaching, and Sky City was brimming with endless festivals and revelry. Su Xin had been completely occupied carousing and indulging in debauchery with his drinking buddies, completely missing the window to purchase the chess set.

Only when the Immortal Arts Assembly commenced did Su Xin remember the matter and instruct his subordinates to make the purchase. To his dismay, that particular edition of Immortal Chess had been pulled from circulation due to "sparking disputes," becoming a [discontinued collector's edition](<# "Literal CN: 绝版 (juébǎn) — out of print / discontinued edition; highly sought-after rare collector's item">). Presently, the major Immortal Chess parlors across the Central Domain were promoting the "Internal Beta Edition" and the "Virtual Character Edition."

As a self-proclaimed "hardcore veteran gamer," being unable to acquire the discontinued edition immediately dampened his enthusiasm.

Although he had dispatched agents to purchase the "Virtual Character" sets he lacked, he refused to trouble the Mei-Zhu couple to reach out directly to the Untiring Immortal Venerable on his behalf.

Alright, the truth was that Su Xin was simply [terrified out of his wits](<# "Literal CN: 怂 (sǒng) — chicken / cowardly / scared stiff">).

Even now, he routinely suffered nightmares featuring the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Yuwen Xuan, and that godforsaken examination hall! He would never dare let the Immortal Venerable discover he was [sapping his willpower through idle trifles](<# "Idiom: 玩物丧志 (wánwù-sàngzhì) — sapping one's lofty ambitions by dallying with frivolous toys/trifles">) to chase down discontinued chess sets. What if the Venerable said: *"Let us administer an examination first to see if you have studied well"*?! Don't think for a second the man wouldn't do it! During his period of "recuperating from injuries" at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Su Xin had witnessed with his own two eyes how the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples lived their daily lives!

Consequently, without the Mei-Zhu couple pulling strings, Su Xin was relegated to standard shipping speeds even when utilizing the Heavenly Dao Alliance's logistics.

His frivolous drinking companions, however, were oblivious to Su Xin's internal agony and dread. They assumed, just like before, that Immortal Chess had been deemed too violent, bloody, and unpeaceful, prompting the Mei-Zhu couple to forbid Su Xin from playing.

The Mei-Zhu couple had done similar things countless times. The last time they used such a pretext was when someone lured Su Xin to watch an underground Dark Spell Tournament. In their righteous wrath, the couple had purged the entire illicit underground arena network of Sky City, launching an anti-vice and anti-crime campaign that lasted an entire decade.

It was during that sweep that everyone realized the Mei-Zhu couple wished for their son to remain a well-behaved, obedient darling. Anyone attempting to lead that sweet boy astray was thoroughly unforgivable—only certain ignorant factions in the Central Domain still suffered from an information deficit regarding this fact.

Yet with certain matters, the more forbidden something was, the more intoxicating it became.

Because (they believed) Immortal Chess had been banned by the Mei-Zhu couple, the other cultivation scions instantly found it just as thrilling as an underground Dark Spell Tournament. Furthermore, these crafty young foxes noted that while the Mei-Zhu couple had refused to buy Su Xin the new edition, they hadn't confiscated his old set—doubtless out of courtesy to a fellow Tribulation Transcendence titan like the Untiring Immortal Venerable! Thus, they could still play it covertly.

Opportunities to flex in front of Su Xin were exceedingly rare!

Consequently, the scions scrambled through every conceivable channel to purchase various editions of Immortal Chess from the Central Domain.

Unfortunately, during this period, the entire cultivation world was desperately scrambling to purchase goods from the Central Domain. Even the Heavenly Dao Alliance couldn't guarantee immediate delivery, let alone second-rate underground channels.

Rarely encountering something their immense wealth and status couldn't instantly procure, the scions were naturally as frantic as ants upon a hot pan.

Abandoning the useless Su Xin, they turned to another member of their circle:

[—"What about you? Didn't you claim last time that your Immortal Chess order would arrive in merely three days? How many days has it been now?!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

The young man's face flushed crimson:

—"Wh-who could have known it would be this slow?! In all likelihood, it's just like the newspaper earlier—the Heavenly Spirit Sect suffered another inventory shortage! Small, backwater sects in the middle of nowhere are always like that, constantly running out of stock! You should know that back when I wanted the [Spider Lily](<# "Literal CN: 彼岸花 (bǐ'ànhuā) — Equinox Flower / Red Spider Lily (Lycoris radiata); mythical flower said to bloom along the shores of the underworld river in Buddhist and Chinese folklore">) from the palace of the [Ruwang Ghost Emperor](<# "Literal CN: 入妄鬼帝 (rùwàng guǐdì) — Delusion-Entering Ghost Emperor; the sovereign reigning beneath the blood cliff in the Western Domain introduced in Chapter 119">) beneath the blood cliff of the Western Domain, Carefree Palace took a mere three days to fetch it for me!"-the youth retorted defensively.

The moment Su Xin heard him sneer that "Heavenly Spirit Sect is a small, backwater sect," his brow furrowed.

He had completely forgotten that he had spewed virtually identical words in the past.

However, as he heard the rest, his expression turned exceedingly strange:

—"...You went through Carefree Palace?"-Su Xin asked.

—"Indeed, Carefree Palace's specialized trade route!"-the youth boasted smugly.-"Only because my clan possesses intimate ties with Carefree Palace am I permitted to utilize it! No matter what commodity under heaven one desires, they can procure it at maximum speed."

—"Then did they bother telling you that the wares of the Heavenly Spirit Sect happen to be the *one* thing on earth they cannot buy?"-Su Xin asked.

The youth froze:

—"That can't be! Even with the Untiring Immortal Venerable present, Heavenly Spirit Sect is merely a Central Domain sect. How could they possibly refuse Carefree Palace—"-the youth stammered.

—"Why couldn't they?"-Su Xin sneered coldly.-"Why don't you try saying those words directly to Carefree Palace? Just last year, the Untiring Immortal Venerable slaughtered one of their Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses! Not to mention that centuries earlier, their entire Treasure Fair was overturned by Lord Untiring!"

—"H-how could that be...?"-the youth stammered, his face draining of color.

Su Xin instantly began to strut and preen.

Only now did he recall that upon returning home last year, his mind had been entirely preoccupied with Immortal Chess and the secret realm, causing him to completely forget to boast about the monumental death of Carefree Palace's Tribulation powerhouse!

Strange as it was, while rumors of that incident had circulated in other regions, Sky City—sitting at the very heart of the cultivation realm—had shielded and suppressed all intelligence regarding the matter.

Perhaps high-tier powerhouses were well aware, but these young scions had never heard a peep about Carefree Palace's [scandals](<# "Censored text: 丑闻 (chǒuwén) — scandals / dark secrets">).

Seizing this golden opportunity, Su Xin vividly recounted how he had ventured into the secret realm, how Carefree Palace had launched a treacherous ambush, and how the Untiring Immortal Venerable had slaughtered Carefree Palace's Sovereign Immortal.

Listening to his grand delivery, one would almost believe he had personally stood on the battlefield witnessing the Untiring Immortal Venerable's divine majesty, rather than being bound hand and foot inside the secret realm, tied up like a [rice dumpling](<# "Literal CN: 绑成粽子 (bǎng chéng zòngzi) — wrapped like a zongzi; trussed up tightly from head to toe">) by a Carefree Palace enchantress to serve as a human meat-shield against flying needles. The sole reason Su Xin hadn't mentioned the affair when he first returned was precisely due to that humiliating dark history. Yet after more than half a year of mental polishing, the story had been reshaped into a valiant tale of him being a dashing hero rescuing a damsel, bravely assisting Junior Sister Mo Bei against Carefree Palace inside the secret realm!

Because the narrative contained a blend of genuine truth and fabrication, the listeners couldn't dismiss it as pure bragging, and their skepticism began to waver.

[—"So Lord Untiring is truly that terrifying?!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-one scion marveled.

—"Of course! Otherwise, how could a single tribulation of his ignite an academic debate across the entire cultivation realm?"-Su Xin argued with supreme conviction.-"Even that journal was founded by him! Only titans at the level of my parents possess the qualification to serve as peer reviewers for those pages!"

[—"Whoa!"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-the group gasped, their belief inching up another notch.

Someone else asked with even greater curiosity:

[—"This Junior Sister Mo Bei you speak of—could she be this piece from the internal beta set?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-he asked, lifting the miniature figurine of Mo Bei from the chessboard.

—"Quite adorable, isn't she?"-Su Xin bragged smugly.

[—"Ah, but word has it she is a terrifying demoness who painted Fengqing City red with rivers of blood,"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)-someone chimed in.

Su Xin's expression stiffened slightly.

The speaker, seemingly utterly blind to social cues, pressed on:

[—"Besides, you were trapped inside the secret realm at the time. How could you be so certain that the Untiring Immortal Venerable truly executed Carefree Palace's Tribulation powerhouse?"](<# "Speaker: Frivolous Cultivation Scion">)

—"Three times,"-Su Xin said coldly.

The young man blinked in confusion:

[—"What?"](<# "Speaker: Plant Scion">)

—"You have provoked me three times today,"-Su Xin said, bearing a vicious grudge.-"First, you egged me on to submit a thesis to *Spells*. Second, you claimed I was incapable of buying Immortal Chess. Third, you questioned my experiences at the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

Speaking thus, Su Xin grabbed the man by the collar, hauling him into the air, before turning his gaze toward the companion who possessed "special channels with Carefree Palace":

—"I recall you were the one who brought this fellow along last time? What is the meaning of this? Is he a [shill](<# "Literal CN: 托儿 (tuōr) — shill / plant; someone planted to manipulate or bait targets">) for Carefree Palace?!"-Su Xin demanded.

—"Wh-what are you talking about?"-that companion's expression suddenly grew exceedingly bizarre.

—"Heh, you people have immense guts!"-Su Xin sneered, hurling the man violently across the room, smashing him into the companion.-"How dare you try to use [this young master](<# "Literal CN: 小爷 (xiǎoyé) — this young master; arrogant, entitled first-person pronoun used by pampered noble scions">) as your pawn?!"

The companion took the full impact of the flying body, the two tumbling together in a heap on the floor.

To their horror, however, a crimson [exotic flame](<# "Literal CN: 异火 (yìhuǒ) — exotic / primordial flame; heavenly or inherited spiritual flame possessing devastating properties">) ignited across Su Xin's palm. It was an ancestral divine art inherited from Xin Zhu—legend held that a mere spark of this "Candle Fire" could ignite any living creature like a wax taper, burning until nothing remained but ash.

Naturally, Su Xin's cultivation was nowhere near his mother Xin Zhu's realm. Even so, this was the direct legacy of a Tribulation Transcendence titan. Should a cultivator of the same realm be touched by even a fleck of that flame, they would find it completely inextinguishable, capable of incinerating the greater part of their cultivation base!

—"Y-you dare?! This is the Carefree Pavilion!"-the man shrieked in terror.

—"Indeed, this is the Carefree Pavilion. But this is *also* Sky City!"-Su Xin sneered coldly.

Without a shred of mercy, he flicked the exotic flame straight at him. The youth was instantly engulfed in blazing crimson fire, unleashing blood-curdling screeches.

Su Xin paid his agonizing screams no heed, sweeping his cold gaze across the rest of the trembling room:

—"Go fetch the manager of Carefree Pavilion at once! I want to interrogate him face-to-face and find out what Carefree Palace is scheming!"-Su Xin commanded.

***

Carefree Palace was not scheming anything nefarious at all.

Their "ancestor" had not yet fully recovered from his injuries. Having recently heard that Zhou Hui had captured heavenly lightning bare-handed, how could they possibly dare provoke him? In the Central Domain, the Carefree Pavilion was behaving more meekly and obediently than a tucked-away tortoise.

Yet, confronted by the grand academic debate sweeping across the entire cultivation realm, the Palace Master of Carefree Palace was gripped by a colossal, agonizing dilemma:

—"How in the world can we get *White Jade Capital Daily* or *Spells* to publish an article from our Carefree Palace?!"-the Palace Master lamented.-"Just a single paper will do!"
