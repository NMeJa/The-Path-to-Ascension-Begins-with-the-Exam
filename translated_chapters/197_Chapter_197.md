# Chapter 197: Misfortune and Fortune

—"All examinees have entered the examination hall,"-Ran Deng announced.

Serving as the Heavenly Spirit Sect's representative, she was seated at the commentary booth. Yet before this gathering of domain titans, a mere Golden Core cultivator like her appeared much like an automated talking cardboard cutout.

Ran Deng, for her part, was exceedingly content with her role as a decorative background board. Adopting an entirely emotionless [monotone script-reading delivery](# "Literal CN: 捧读 (pěngdú) — reciting aloud from a script; deadpan teleprompter reading without emotional inflection"), she intoned:

—"Esteemed guests, please look closely: the examination platforms before them have undergone adjustments,"-Ran Deng began.-"This is no illusion, but rather real-time modifications tailored to each examinee's individual forging habits.

—"Although candidates are strictly prohibited from bringing personal belongings into this examination—all tools and raw materials being provided directly by the testing arena—the platforms and implements will adjust within regulatory boundaries to suit each candidate's needs, ensuring they can perform in their optimal state,"-Ran Deng continued.

—"Likewise, the examination arena is outfitted with the highest-tier defensive noise-canceling arrays,"-Ran Deng said.-"While guaranteeing the safety of all candidates, these arrays also isolate them from all external disruptions. For instance, my voice right now cannot penetrate into the interiors of their platforms.

—"Subjectively, the examinees will perceive themselves to be inside an enclosed, private forging chamber, allowing them to focus single-mindedly on their work,"-Ran Deng added.-"Honored guests need not worry about disturbing them. Naturally, attempting to offer guidance or aid someone in cheating is equally impossible (laughs)."

Ran Deng's closing joke—was she truly telling a joke?—elicited zero reaction from the spectator gallery.

Fearing Ran Deng might feel awkward, Fairy Maogui stiffly picked up the conversational baton:

—"This point seems somewhat different from the Heavenly Spirit Sect's usual examinations, doesn't it?"-Fairy Maogui asked.-"I've heard that during internal Heavenly Spirit Sect exams, examinees are not permitted to block external disruptions?"

—"That is intended to temper the disciples' character, cultivate their mental fortitude, and hone their spirits, teaching them to disregard irrelevant gazes from bystanders, distinguish what truly matters, and remain wholly focused when facing challenges in critical moments,"-Ran Deng explained with righteous pomposity.

This high-sounding rationale unexpectedly resonated with quite a few domain powerhouses.

Many titans nodded repeatedly, finding her reasoning remarkably sound. As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's design—every detail truly held profound pedagogical depth! A few even began contemplating erecting an identical spectator gallery back at their own sect grounds.

Ran Deng kept her eyes half-closed as she continued brazenly spouting nonsense on her Master's behalf:

—"As for this qualification examination, it belongs to the category of a 'critical moment,'"-Ran Deng continued.-"After all, the foremost objective of a qualification exam is to assess the technical proficiency of the candidates. Thus, isolating them from unnecessary interference is only natural."

[...If you truly believe that, don't design spectator stands in the first place—no, just cancel the spectator gallery entirely!]

Had any examinee overheard Ran Deng's reasoning, they undoubtedly would have let out such an agonized wail!

Setting aside someone like Guan Xinguang—who feared that stepping into an enclosed space meant emerging as a puddle of pus and melted blood (Zhou Hui: *If I truly wanted to do that, an enclosed space wouldn't be necessary in the first place*)—no sane cultivator enjoyed having their exams live-streamed to the public.

Yet Ran Deng granted Guan Xinguang his wish after all, turning her attention toward his movements. Truth be told, he was the Chief Director of the Department of Works and a Mahayana cultivator; most of the 'spectators' at this examination had come precisely on his account. Ignoring him was out of the question—otherwise, her commentary would truly become white noise, and the assembled titans might even find it annoying.

Having finished introducing the fundamental rules of the testing grounds, Ran Deng simply directed everyone's gaze toward Guan Xinguang:

—"The theme of this examination is the 'Dao,'"-Ran Deng announced.-"An exceedingly vast topic indeed; I wonder how our master artifact refiners will interpret it. However, His Excellency the Chief Director of the Department of Works appears to harbor no hesitation regarding this theme whatsoever. Upon ascending the platform, he ignited the flames of his forging furnace without the slightest hesitation. An exceptionally pure purple-gold flame.

—"By the way, for this examination, we have prepared a full ninety-nine types of fire for examinees to choose from,"-Ran Deng continued.-"But as we all know, fire is the most explosive and difficult to control among the Five Elements. Depending on one's cultivation realm and karmic luck, even the exact same designated flame may exhibit vastly different properties at different moments and in different states. Fairy Maogui, what are your thoughts on the furnace flame chosen by Chief Director Guan?"

Fairy Maogui smiled faintly and replied:

—"In my view, His Excellency the Chief Director's luck today is quite good,"-Fairy Maogui said.-"Though I am an alchemist, the Dao of Alchemy and the Dao of Artifact Refinement share notable commonalities—fire, for instance. Fire can be considered the fundamental bedrock of concoction and forging; a fine furnace of flame directly determines the quality of the final product. As we can see, the furnace flame selected by the Chief Director today is purple. It seems that even the Chief Director of the Department of Works did not act overconfidently, wisely choosing the gentlest and most versatile [Extremity Earth Fire](# "Literal CN: 极罗地火 (Jíluó Dìhuǒ) — Supreme Net / Boundless Earth Fire; a widely used, exceptionally stable subterranean alchemy and forging flame")."

—"What a pity. It seems Chief Director Guan doesn't intend to show off his flashy skills, opting to pass the exam using the most fundamental method instead,"-Ran Deng sighed with theatrical regret.

—"Perhaps that is His Excellency the Chief Director's 'Dao'?"-Fairy Maogui suggested.

She was entirely unaware of the feud between Guan Xinguang and the Heavenly Spirit Sect, nor did she share much personal rapport with this 'master artifact refiner' from the Department of Works. Completely oblivious to the filthy deeds he had committed in the shadows, she simply relied on her shallow impression of him as an honest, straightforward man. Given Maogui's gentle disposition, she naturally wouldn't speak ill of Guan Xinguang on such an occasion.

Fairy Maogui attempted to offer a few polite compliments:

—"Although I know little about His Excellency the Chief Director, in my memory, the magical treasures produced by the Department of Works are predominantly high in quality and reasonable in price, devoid of sensationalist gimmicks meant merely to catch the eye,"-Fairy Maogui ventured.-"Perhaps crafting unpretentious, practical magical treasures that meet the everyday needs of the majority of cultivators is this master refiner's true pursuit?"

In other words: when actually counting them up, Guan Xinguang possessed zero world-shaking signature masterpieces.

Though Fairy Maogui tried her best to maintain a praising tone, when it came to the Department of Works' forged products, this was truly the absolute limit of what could be praised.

The domain powerhouses in the spectator gallery exchanged knowing smiles.

People were inevitably prone to comparisons.

While they harbored no malice toward Guan Xinguang—and might even be on friendly terms with him—when it came to tallying their own personal achievements and magnum opuses, cultivators were ready to [beat each other's dog brains out](# "Literal CN: 狗脑子都打出来 (gǒu nǎozi dōu dǎ chūlai) — fight each other until their dog brains spill; a colorful idiom meaning to brawl viciously and mercilessly without holding back").

Noticing the subtle shift in the gallery, Fairy Maogui cleared her throat and continued:

—"As a reward for his down-to-earth pragmatism, karmic luck has evidently smiled upon him today. Look at this cauldron of flame—full yet not explosive, vigorous yet completely steady; its initial state can only be described as flawless,"-Fairy Maogui said, her tone sounding considerably more genuine when praising the flame.-"Most importantly, it has mutated! Look at that golden radiance gleaming through the purple—without question, it has transformed into [Extremity Golden Flame](# "Literal CN: 极罗金焰 (Jíluó Jīnyàn) — Supreme Net Golden Flame; the rare, mutated golden state of Extremity Earth Fire"). One must understand that as the most stable of flames, Extremity Earth Fire has the lowest probability of mutation. Yet once it mutates, the resulting Extremity Golden Flame becomes one of the [Ten Divine Flames](# "Literal CN: 十大神火 (shí dà shénhuǒ) — the ten legendary mythical flames of the cultivation realm") best suited for crafting and forging, multiplying the chance of producing supreme-grade pills... ah, supreme-grade magical treasures by several folds!"

By the end of her explanation, even Fairy Maogui's tone carried a hint of wistfulness.

The other cultivators in the stands deeply empathized with her sentiment.

Encountering such a flame during pill concoction or weapon refinement was naturally a coveted stroke of fortune; alchemists and refiners might even scrap their original plans on the spot to forge an even higher-tier treasure. But how long was an examination session? It was utterly impossible to craft high-grade pills or treasures in such a limited window. Even if an examinee voluntarily forfeited the exam, whether this arena was suitable for prolonged refinement was another question entirely, not to mention that the materials on hand were merely standardized, generic supplies provided by the testing hall, making it impossible to produce high-grade items regardless. Therefore, no matter how extraordinary the flame was, all it could do in the end was slightly inflate an exam score—an absolute waste of such a cauldron of fire!

Consequently, a wave of regretful sighs rippled through the spectator stands.

Amid these sighs, Guan Xinguang's examination platform began to slowly rise.

—"It seems the examination platform acknowledges Chief Director Guan's 'performance' as well,"-Ran Deng drawled lazily.

Nobody had much to say to that.

While 'good fortune' was certainly enviable, achievements attained through sheer luck were inherently non-replicable. Aside from provoking envy, jealousy, and spite, it offered little practical reference value.

Some spectators even lost interest in watching Guan Xinguang's subsequent forging steps.

[What is so interesting about a foregone conclusion anyway?]

—"It's just a cauldron of furnace fire; is that really worth all this fuss?"-Sikong Yao muttered in bewilderment at the refiners' reactions.

As a combat-oriented—or rather, escape-oriented—cultivator who neither concocted pills nor forged artifacts, he felt that this furnace flame could neither strike an enemy nor boost one's cultivation base. Compared to the *true* [Ten Divine Flames](# "Literal CN: 十大神火 (shí dà shénhuǒ) — the ten legendary mythical flames of the cultivation realm"), it fell miles short.

Naturally, someone like Sikong Yao had no business being here in the first place.

Had it not been for Zhou Hui's 'invitation,' he wouldn't have bothered watching such an utterly tedious exam at all.

While Zhou Hui took no issue with Sikong Yao's frivolous attitude, Ren Ran was visibly displeased.

She knew full well how Guan Xinguang's 'good fortune' had come about, and she had been thoroughly relishing the envious remarks of the others. Unfortunately, this batch of spectators fell short—their level of envy and jealousy was nowhere near high enough. To make matters worse, Sikong Yao's dismissive expression stepped squarely on her nerves.

Ren Ran glared at Sikong Yao:

—"Do you have a problem with it?"-Ren Ran snapped.

—"Ah, no, none at all,"-Sikong Yao said hastily. Yet he paused, unable to resist offering his two cents:-"I just feel that making such a fuss over a trivial thing isn't worth it. After all, according to my [Law of Conservation of Karmic Luck](# "Literal CN: 气运守恒定律 (qìyùn shǒuhéng dìnglǜ) — a satirical pseudo-scientific law parodying the Law of Conservation of Energy"), when someone gains karmic luck, someone else is bound to lose karmic luck and run into misfortune."

As he spoke, he shrunk back into his seat, as though that unlucky bastard were about to be him.

Incidentally, Sikong Yao had picked up the term 'conservation law' yesterday while passing by Buyan Palace, overhearing Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples discussing something about 'energy conservation,' and had promptly purloined it for his own use.

Yet this bizarre law of his prompted Ruwang Ghost Emperor to turn his head in amusement.

—"Hearing your words, does that mean sticking close to an unlucky person is advantageous?"-the theatrical mask over Ruwang's face asked.-"When others lose their karmic luck, can you reap it in their stead?"

—"How could that be possible?"-Sikong Yao replied nervously.-"Sticking close to an unlucky ghost will only make you even more unlucky, alright?!"

[In that case, your karmic luck isn't conserved at all—it only flows out and never flows in!]

Ruwang and Ren Ran averted their gazes simultaneously, having entirely lost the courage to speak with His Excellency Misfortune.

Whether Sikong Yao's [Law of Conservation of Karmic Luck](# "Literal CN: 气运守恒定律 (qìyùn shǒuhéng dìnglǜ) — a satirical pseudo-scientific law parodying the Law of Conservation of Energy") and [Principle of Contagious Misfortune](# "Literal CN: 倒霉传染原则 (dǎoméi chuánrǎn yuánzé) — the satirical idea that bad luck is infectious and spreads like a disease") held any truth remained unknown, but his [crow's mouth](# "Literal CN: 乌鸦嘴 (wūyāzuǐ) — crow's beak / jinx; someone whose pessimistic predictions always end up coming true") was undeniably lethal.

Even as they spoke, a plume of cyan flame suddenly surged upward from below.

—"What happened?!"-Sikong Yao leaped to his feet in an instant.

Even without knowing alchemy or artifact refinement, he could tell this flame was anything but normal.

Gazing downward, Sikong Yao spotted a forging furnace ablaze on one of the examination platforms. The flame wasn't excessively large, however, and shouldn't spread to engulf the entire arena—besides, the Untiring Immortal Venerable was sitting right here.

Reassured by that thought, Sikong Yao sat back down, only to leap to his feet a split second later [like his backside had caught fire](# "Literal CN: 火烧屁股 (huǒ shāo pìgu) — fire burning one's butt; jumping or bolting in extreme haste or panic").

This time, it was because of the killing intent radiating from Ruwang Ghost Emperor.

Sikong Yao shot a nervous glance toward Ruwang. *I bear no grudge or enmity against this fellow, so what in the world is he emitting such terrifying bloodlust for?* Looking closer, he realized Ruwang was staring fixatedly toward that burning platform. Though the mask concealed his face, making his expression unreadable—the design on the theatrical mask had shifted into half fury and half worry—the armrest of his seat had been crushed to powder in the Ghost Emperor's grip.

Sikong Yao heard Ruwang murmur in tense agitation:

—"...Ye-zi!"-Ruwang said.

[Ye-zi?]

[...Someone affiliated with Ruwang?]

[Wait, isn't that platform occupied by Untiring's disciple? That disciple named Guan Ye.]

[Tsk. He's Untiring's disciple; why are you getting so worked up? Anyone who didn't know better would think you gave birth to him...]

[Wait, hold on a second—Untiring's disciple?!]

Sikong Yao instantly dove behind his seat:

—"His bad luck is his own doing! It has nothing to do with me!"-Sikong Yao squeaked.

Witnessing this spectacle, even Ren Ran couldn't help but feel that Sikong Yao being chased and hunted by Untiring for eight solid years wasn't an injustice in the slightest.
