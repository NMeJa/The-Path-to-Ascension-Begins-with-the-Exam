# Chapter 178: The Trial of Desires

Unaware of what their Master was doing on the other side, within the illusion realm of desires, Mo Bei followed Mo Xi as they arrived at the demon race's "treasure treasury."

—Naturally, it was a fake treasure treasury.

This was a component of Mo Xi's trial of desires.

Yet neither of them knew this was a trial; what was more, the parameters of this trial had even been seized and steered by their Master. Mo Bei and Mo Xi still assumed this was merely the demon race's 'hospitality.'

Still, that it could fool the sharp-witted Mo Xi was partly due to the "authenticity" of the treasure treasury.

Although according to the "setting," Mo Xi lacked the qualifications to accompany "Master" into the deepest recesses of the treasure vault to inspect the genuine secret treasures, the front hall was already resplendent and magnificent enough to satisfy anyone's imagination of a grand treasury.

Even Mo Bei felt her heart race at the sight.

She surveyed her surroundings, speaking with utter incredulity:

—"You stayed here for several hours?"-Mo Bei pressed,-"And you didn't do anything at all?"

[—"Not a single stray thought?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"...I feel like you are questioning my integrity,"-Mo Xi protested indignantly.

—"I never question things that do not exist,"-Mo Bei retorted.

Only toward her twin brother would she unreservedly unleash her razor-sharp, acerbic tongue. Utterly disregarding his reaction, she walked straight up to the blossoming plant resting beside the tea table, openly displaying her admiration:

—"This potted flower is truly gorgeous,"-she murmured.

*

—"Of course it's gorgeous, that's an [Ice Feather Orchid](# "Literal CN: 冰羽斛 (bīngyǔhú) — a legendary frost-dendrobium"), isn't it? I heard it only blooms once every five thousand years; I thought the species had gone extinct!"-Tao Zhu exclaimed.

This trial of desires functioned like an immersive fantasy projection. Normally, aside from the examinee, only the 'examiners' could view the scenarios unfolding inside; hence, previously only Zhou Hui and Shui Huang'er knew the contents of Mo Bei and Mo Xi's trials.

It was only because the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree directly broadcasted the duo's illusion realm that Boss Tao finally caught sight of their situation.

Yet rather than the trials of the two youths, Boss Tao's gaze was instantly seized by the "treasure treasury."

He couldn't help clicking his tongue in marvel:

—"Now *this* is what a proper treasure vault ought to look like!"

What Taiyi Spirit Dew, what suns, what Primordial Suns—treasures were treasures, but they were far too high and mighty, not something a tiny golden toad like him could ever stomach.

However, his phrasing was muddled and vague, easily leading one to misunderstand that the demon race's genuine forbidden sanctuary didn't look like a proper treasury at all.

Shui Huang'er did indeed misunderstand. Yet having just received Zhou Hui's solemn commitment, she was in high spirits and took no offense, even taking the opportunity to offer a polite compliment:

—"Our demon race is inept at preserving relics, never able to rival the treasure vaults of you humans,"-Shui Huang'er offered politely.-"And naturally, it pales even further before Untiring Immortal Venerable's personal collection!"

—"Ah? What treasures does Untiring have?"-Tao Zhu asked blankly.

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er")

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

Tao Zhu and Shui Huang'er blinked at each other in blank astonishment, discovering that a golden toad and a phoenix shared zero common language.

Only after a long while did Tao Zhu ask:

—"The treasure treasury Untiring's disciple is currently in... wasn't that an illusion crafted by you all?"

—"It is indeed an illusion. But just like how within your own desire, you believed you had uncovered a secret method to slip into the treasure vault, the illusion realm of desires is generated entirely from your own memories and capabilities,"-Shui Huang'er explained patiently.-"We cannot interfere, nor can we perceive overly specific details."

At this point, she had nothing left to hide.

Yet she could not fathom why Tao Zhu assumed the place Mo Xi occupied was the demon race's treasury.

They were literally standing inside the actual treasury right now!

Besides, anyone thinking with half a brain would never use a real treasury to test someone's desires—what if an examinee had harbored no such designs originally, only to be seduced by the actual treasures?!

[A toad's brain truly is microscopic.]

The phoenix maiden complained inwardly, trying her utmost to help him understand:

—"In other words, the treasure vault in Young Master Mo Xi's desires should be constructed entirely from his own memories."

—"Indeed so,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree confirmed.-"A trial of desires is, after all, an 'exam'; there must be a [crux to solve the question](# "Literal CN: 题眼 (tíyǎn) — the critical eye/key to cracking an exam problem"). In the case of a treasure vault, as long as one's eyes are not blinded by greed, one should easily spot the inconsistencies."

Hearing this, Boss Tao froze, then abruptly lunged forward, trying to grab Zhou Hui's arm:

—"Untiring! You actually hoard so many treasures, yet you [kept me in the dark so cruelly!](# "Literal CN: 瞒得我好苦 (mán de wǒ hǎo kǔ) — kept me deceived in bitter ignorance")"

—"Kept what from you?"-Zhou Hui dodged Boss Tao's grasp, frowning.-"I don't recall owning such a mess of miscellaneous clutter."

—"Quit bluffing me! I know you've stashed away heaps of treasures—even that Five Emperors Silk-Bound Gold-Jade Coiled Dragon Seal rumored to have vanished during the demonic catastrophe is—"

Reaching this point, Boss Tao trailed off, a look of bewilderment washing over his face.

The "Five Emperors Silk-Bound Gold-Jade Coiled Dragon Seal" he spoke of was an exceptionally renowned antique. Reputedly belonging to the Human Sovereign before the ten-thousand-year demonic catastrophe, it was documented across numerous ancient ruin archives—a treasure that antique dealers like Tao Zhu dreamed of laying their hands on.

At this very moment, that coiled dragon seal rested right beside Mo Bei's left hand, utterly ignored by the twins.

Yet Tao Zhu also knew that just as the twins cared nothing for some coiled dragon seal, Untiring cared even less.

After all, though this imperial seal sounded prestigious, it was "merely" proof of the Human Sovereign's mortal lineage. First, it recorded no cultivation techniques; second, it channeled zero spiritual power. At most, it possessed archaeological value—precisely the sort of dead antique Untiring found completely uninteresting.

Tao Zhu was dead certain that had Untiring acquired the coiled dragon seal, he would have dragged it out long ago to trade with him for "more practical book fragments," or simply liquidated it for funds; he would never have kept it around to fondle.

And similar "valueless" antiques with "illustrious pedigree but zero utility" made up fully half of the front hall in Mo Xi's "fantasized treasury"—many of which even Tao Zhu couldn't identify.

Tao Zhu instantly cooled down:

—"Looking closely, it really doesn't resemble Untiring's hoard... Is it truly not the demon race's collection? Many items appear ancient beyond measure; could they not be relics passed down from your era?"

—"We have no interest in collecting human rubbish,"-Shui Huang'er scoffed.

Seeing the Immortal Venerable dismiss them with indifference, she naturally deemed those artifacts utterly worthless.

—"It's not rubbish, it's antiques!"-Boss Tao protested, desperately trying to salvage his profession's dignity. Still somewhat reluctant to let it go, he muttered,-"Bizarre... Not Untiring's, not the demon race's—these things certainly can't be fabricated out of thin air..."

Even if Mo Xi had read related historical accounts, his brain couldn't possibly conjure up such hyper-realistic physical artifacts.

Boss Tao pondered intently, then struck upon a sudden flash of inspiration:

—"Speaking of which, didn't you rescue those two little brats of yours from Fallen Immortal Palace? Could these collections belong to Carefree Palace?!"

—"Aha! Carefree Palace actually hoards such treasures without leaking a single scrap! If I ever get the chance, I must go inspect their headquarters' treasure vault!"-Boss Tao hollered.

Evidently, even though this trial no longer concerned him, Boss Tao's avaricious desires had been triggered even more violently.

Judging by Boss Tao's stance—itching to storm Carefree Palace this very second—if he ever gained access to Carefree Palace one day, he would never be content with merely "inspecting."

Yet were these antiques genuinely Carefree Palace's?

[Most likely not.]

Zhou Hui mused in his heart.

Although Mo Bei and Mo Xi were considered "high-priced commodities" in Fallen Immortal Palace, no matter how 'cherished' they were, in the eyes of their 'breeders,' they were nothing more than a pair of pre-Golden Core cultivation furnaces. Before being fully cultivated, they didn't even possess the qualifications to attend Carefree Palace's high-tier auctions. Consequently, Mo Bei and Mo Xi's horizons had been trapped within that tiny, suffocating world.

Zhou Hui had only realized this during the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grand Auction in this reincarnation cycle.

He considered this a personal failing as their master.

Fortunately, through the experience accumulated at the auction and Tao Zhu's guidance in this cycle, Mo Xi's horizons were broader than in previous lifetimes. Moreover, having broken through to Golden Core ahead of schedule, he had severed the shackles of the Extreme Yin Constitution; no different from any ordinary cultivator, he had finally earned the right to 'freedom.'

Once current matters were resolved, sending Xi'er out on a traveling journey to broaden his horizons would be a fine idea.

Zhou Hui resolved quietly in his heart.

Of course, that belonged to the future.

Returning to the matter at hand: even if Mo Xi possessed broader horizons than in previous lifetimes, he could never fabricate such an extravagantly opulent treasure vault out of thin air.

Crucially, Mo Bei had clearly never seen those relics either.

In this cycle of reincarnation, Mo Bei and Mo Xi had never been separated. Theoretically, had Mo Xi encountered those items, even if Mo Bei hadn't paid close attention, she would have recognized at least a few. Furthermore, this treasury was manifestly Mo Xi's trial of desires; while it could bewitch Mo Xi, it should have held little sway over Mo Bei, whose material desires were relatively ascetic. Had Mo Bei seen these items before, she would have quickly spotted the flaws and shattered the illusion.

Combined with the "protagonist" that had appeared earlier, as well as Mo Xi's bewitching, slender figure so drastically different from his current life...

—"...Echoes of the previous cycle?"-Zhou Hui murmured softly, forming a hypothesis.

*

Mo Xi, for his part, noticed nothing amiss about this treasure vault.

Although every treasure here was manifested from his subconscious desires and theoretically should have obstructed his judgment, the truth was that until that so-called "Mo Bei's fated one" appeared, Mo Xi hadn't spared these artifacts a single passing thought.

Er, not now either.

Leading Mo Bei into the front hall of this treasure vault had indeed brought Mo Xi a comforting sense of security.

Yet this security had nothing to do with these lifeless antiques; rather, in Mo Xi's mind, his Master was somewhere within this "treasury."

As long as Master was present, they were completely safe.

Even if the demon race harbored insidious conspiracies, they couldn't budge Master in the slightest.

—"We'll just wait here for Master to return,"-Mo Xi declared,-"Think carefully about everything that happened, so you can report ([tattle](# "Literal CN: 告状 (gàozhuàng) — file a complaint / snitch / tattle")) to Master later."

—"Mm,"-Mo Bei nodded.

She too breathed a sigh of relief, confident that once Master returned, every mystery would be resolved.

Yet because of this, Mo Bei felt a twinge of lingering regret:

She had fought continuously for so many days, slaughtered countless demonic fiends, and stood a mere hair's breadth from a breakthrough, only for the moment to be derailed by an absolute idiot. Who knew when the next opportunity for breakthrough would arise?

At the thought, Mo Bei wanted nothing more than to drag that "fated one" out and mince him all over again.

Regrettably, Mo Bei knew that no matter how many times she slaughtered him, that lost opportunity would never return.

She could only meditate and see if she could recapture the ethereal sensation from before.

Mo Bei sighed softly.

She settled into a chair beside the Ice Feather Orchid, attempting to circulate her heart cultivation method.

Seeing this, Mo Xi did not disturb her. He picked up the teapot, poured a cup of tea for Mo Bei, placed it at her elbow, and sat down in the chair across from her.

Just before entering meditative stillness, Mo Bei opened her eyes and glanced at Mo Xi. She was startled to see a luminescent screen pop up before him, densely crowded with flickering numerals.

—"Is that... the 'system'?"-Mo Bei asked.-"Can it be accessed even outside the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"Of course. It was developed by Master in the first place; as long as we're near Master, it can be accessed anywhere,"-Mo Xi replied, fingers flying as he tapped and entered numerals, his gaze growing increasingly intense.-"Besides, Master assigned me a task; I have administrator permissions."

—"A task?!"-Mo Bei sat bolt upright.

She had only asked in passing, harboring little interest in where Mo Xi's "system" had come from, but upon hearing his words, she instantly smelled a rat.

—Or rather, as an outside observer, though Mo Bei did not realize this was an illusion realm constructed for Mo Xi's desire examination, she had always possessed razor-sharp intuition regarding Mo Xi's "abnormalities."

Yet sharp as she was, Mo Bei was notoriously indulgent toward this foolish brother of hers.

If Mo Xi merely wanted to slack off or seek his own death, so long as it didn't drag her down with him, Mo Bei would watch from the sidelines with cool detachment.

Unless it concerned Master's directives—

—"That doesn't add up. I thought what Master values most right now is your cultivation progress,"-Mo Bei pointed out.-"He would definitely want you to consolidate your cultivation base as quickly as possible, not assign you busywork."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

A flicker of hesitation surfaced on Mo Xi's face.

Mo Bei's logic was impeccably sound: in Master's eyes, for them disciples, cultivation was always priority number one!

Yet as Mo Xi's gaze drifted back to the luminous screen, he was once again seduced by his own desires:

—"Even if it's a task, since Master assigned it, it naturally helps consolidate my cultivation... Yeah, that's it."

—"What task?"-Mo Bei pressed for clarification.

Mo Xi, however, fell silent. Only after a protracted pause did he reply in a slow, deliberate murmur:

—"To control... the demon race?"

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"Control... the demons of the Southern Domain's Sacred Tree, pulling them into our camp,"-Mo Xi declared.

He curled the corners of his lips, his heterochromatic eyes narrowing into slits, exuding a dangerous, enchanting allure that had never existed in this reincarnation cycle.

*

As Mo Xi uttered those words, Shui Huang'er's brows knit tightly together.

She felt the ripples of Mo Xi's desires, and it was far more than mere desire:

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, your disciple's desires are expanding, attempting to drag the demons upon the Sacred Tree into their vortex."

[And why?]

[Why would Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple harbor a desire to "control the demon race"?]

[This is far worse than snooping after secret treasures!]

Shui Huang'er thought, thoroughly perplexed.

From her perspective, having just reached an alliance with the Immortal Venerable, only to immediately discover that his disciple harbored designs against her kin—the feeling was utterly foul.

Yet compared to this conscious indignation, Shui Huang'er's anger stemmed from a primal instinct beyond her control. She felt an eerie certainty that she had lived through something identical before: back then, Mo Xi had also perpetrated something similar, even exploiting someone else's far more sickening desires... Had the Great Elder not intervened, would everything have been destroyed...?

Shui Huang'er clutched her throbbing, feverish head, lost in fragmented thoughts.

—"No need to worry,"-however, this time, the Sacred Tree's Great Elder did not intervene. Instead, it soothed her,-"What this child refers to as 'control' this time is most likely the 'curriculum plan' you have been relentlessly requesting from the Immortal Venerable."

—"A curriculum plan?"-Shui Huang'er echoed in bewilderment.

Stifling the instinctive repulsion and aversion churning within her toward Mo Xi, she leaned in to closely inspect the contents of the illusion realm radiating from Mo Xi's desires—and froze solid.

Within that illusion realm, provocative, incendiary slogans blazed with blinding clarity:

> *"Don't you want to attend class?"*
>
> *"Don't you want to study?"*
>
> *"Are you trying to reject the [tyranny of being hung from tree branches](# "Literal CN: 被挂在枝头的暴政 — referencing Yuwen Xuan being publicly suspended from the peach tree at the Heavenly Spirit Sect as an academic punishment")?"*
>
> *"Now, shall we play a game?"*
>
> *"If you can achieve victory in this game, then you are a qualified demon!"*
>
> *"Even without studying, you will master sufficient skills!"*
>
> *"Come! Win your freedom!"*

—"Wait a second! This isn't encouraging them to study, this is [urging them to drop out!](# "Literal CN: 劝退 (quàntuì) — advising someone to quit or withdraw; opposite of 劝学 (encouraging learning)")"-Shui Huang'er shrieked in deadpan disbelief.
