# Chapter 35: Mo Xi's Colossal Miscalculation (第35章 莫喜的大失策)

Clutching the Cloud Mountain Snow-Needle Tea that he had finally gotten his hands on, Mo Xi returned from outside, practically glowing with smug delight.

He was thrilled not merely because he had finally procured the precious tea leaves he coveted, but even more so because he had turned a handsome profit.

It wasn't an astronomical sum—nowhere near a single year's yield from his spirit stone vein—yet even that amount was sufficient to sustain the sect's operating expenses for an entire year.

Regrettably, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's operational scale was far too small. The few disciples dispatched outside for procurement were merely ordinary disciples—scant in number and sorely lacking in commercial acumen. It was solely under Mo Xi's remote direction that they had managed to turn a tidy profit before the Carefree Palace cottoned on and plugged the regulatory loophole. If only their headcount had been larger and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's transport corridors smoother, seizing those commercial trade routes to forge Mo Xi's very own commercial empire wouldn't have been out of the question.

—"Keep dreaming!"-Mo Bei scoffed dismissively. She found her younger brother's "ambitions" utterly ungrounded in reality; how could securing one microscopic victory send him daydreaming about world conquest?—"Commercial empire, my foot! If our sect could just receive letters and parcels with a shred of convenience, we'd already be thanking the Heavens!"

That, in truth, was hitting the nail on the head.

From Master down to the disciples, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was [homebody to the bone](# "Literal CN: 宅属性 (zhái shǔxìng) — otaku / indoor homebody nature; innate inclination to stay holed up at home").

Because the sect was entirely self-sufficient internally, they placed virtually no emphasis on transport infrastructure. Even when external goods were needed, they simply dispatched a few disciples on procurement errands to haul the cargo back [in the flesh](# "Literal CN: 肉身 (ròushēn) — by physical body / manual footwork without relying on automated logistics").

For instance, the tea Mo Xi wanted had required him to lend the Xiang siblings an origami paper kite magical artifact to send them out on the shopping run. To make matters worse, a minor hiccup along the road had delayed Xiang Rong and Xiang Yi halfway, forcing them to resort to alternative measures to relay the tea leaves to the beacon tower on Mount Tianling's outer perimeter as swiftly as possible—this beacon tower being the maximum safe distance Mo Bei and Mo Xi were permitted to venture. Even so, Mo Bei had worried about her brother and tagged along to fetch the tea leaves together.

—"What does it matter? It's not like I buy this kind of thing often anyway,"-Mo Xi declared smugly, patting his storage pouch.-"Since I turned a tidy profit this time, I couldn't resist buying a little extra. It's plenty to keep our entire sect drinking tea for a hundred years!"

[...Do tea leaves even keep for that many years?]
Mo Bei pondered skeptically.

It suddenly occurred to her that Mo Xi's inability to step outside Mount Tianling might actually be a blessing in disguise. Otherwise, within a single year, he would squander the entire spirit stone vein Master had gifted him down to the bedrock—and likely manage to bankrupt himself into debt slavery along with it.

Unaware of Mo Bei's private fretting, Mo Xi was scanning his surroundings in high excitement, looking eager to nab some passing senior brother to flaunt his spoils. Regrettably (or perhaps fortunately), no eligible target crossed their path. However, as they passed by the Hall of Embracing Emptiness, Mo Xi craned his neck and slowed his stride.

—"Strange. Do we have guests today?"-he murmured.

The Hall of Embracing Emptiness was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's main hall, possessing the signature hallmark of Heavenly Spirit Sect architecture:

Vast, cavernous, and utterly spartan.

To put it more plainly: it was a drafty, echoing hall devoid of furniture, which meant even Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples avoided stepping inside. Normally, it served strictly for receiving distinguished guests.

(Distinguished Guests: [...Excuse me?])

Last time, they had prepared to receive Su Xin here, but Su Xin had fled in panic, leaving the Hall of Embracing Emptiness to resume its uninterrupted vacancy.

Today, however, numerous disciples were bustling in and out, with quite a few lingering outside just to watch the commotion.

An idle disciple, overhearing Mo Xi's question, chirped in reply:

—"Senior Brother Mo, you haven't heard? The people from Eternal Villa have arrived, and they're currently negotiating a massive business deal with Second Senior Brother!"

—"A massive business deal?!"-Mo Xi exclaimed in astonishment.

He had spent the past few days cooped up in the Water Mirror Pavilion, engrossed in coordinating with the outside world, and was genuinely oblivious to the opening of the secret realm.

In his eyes, his Second Senior Brother was so prim, proper, and austere that he could be enshrined on a temple altar; by no stretch of imagination did he seem the type to dabble in commerce.

Seeing that Mo Xi was truly in the dark, the disciple relayed the entire sequence of events.

And with every word that left the disciple's lips, the radiant smile on Mo Xi's face vanished until not a trace remained.

*

A quarter of an hour later, Mo Xi rode his flying sword and barged into Zhou Hui's immortal cave:

—"Master, I heard you gave the secret realm to Second Senior Brother?!"

Zhou Hui was currently examining the Pavilion of a Single Thought he had acquired last time. Hearing Mo Xi's breathless interrogation, he looked at him in mild perplexity:

—"When your Second Senior Brother made the secret realm recognize him as master, weren't you standing right beside him?"

[Did this boy bump his head somewhere?]

—"That was Second Senior Brother's own capability; I acknowledge that!"-Mo Xi protested frantically.-"But this current business model—you taught him that, didn't you?!"

*Sob sob!* In one fell swoop, the revenue had completely dwarfed all his "hard work" over the past few days!

[This is cheating!]

Carrying the tea leaves as she strolled in behind him, Mo Bei sensed her brother's thoughts and couldn't resist rolling her eyes:

[What imaginary phantom is this idiot brother of mine even waging war against?!]

—"Business model?"-Zhou Hui couldn't instantly parse the clutter inside Mo Xi's head.-"I did instruct him to open the secret realm, but that is merely a 'test' to see whether we can achieve an efficient recycling and reuse of the realm's resources, providing everyone with a superior environment for academic study and realm exploration."

—"Efficient recycling and reuse?"-Mo Bei echoed the unfamiliar terminology in confusion.

Zhou Hui offered no immediate explanation. Raising a hand, he beckoned two prayer mats to float over to his low desk, gesturing for the twin disciples to take a seat.

[Ah, misery. Master is about to lecture!]
Mo Bei and Mo Xi thought in unison.

Yet since Master had made his intentions clear, they could hardly excuse themselves. Furthermore, Mo Xi still wanted an "explanation." Resigned, the twin siblings sat down on the opposite side of the desk.

Zhou Hui set aside the paper model (prototype) of the Pavilion of a Single Thought, producing another multi-colored paper model (prototype) from beneath the desk.

Judging by its contours, it was unmistakably the Qianxing Secret Realm.

—"I've been analyzing the architecture of the Pavilion of a Single Thought lately, and tried my hand at crafting this,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"As expected of Master! To achieve such flawless perfection after a mere casual inspection,"-Mo Xi praised with a honeyed tongue, despite feeling thoroughly aggrieved.

However, his underlying resentment bled over to Mo Bei. Subconsciously adopting the exact same sweet smile as Mo Xi, Mo Bei chimed in:

—"As expected of Master! Working so diligently at your advanced age."

—"What kind of blasphemy are you spouting?!"-Mo Xi barked, immediately pinching Mo Bei's arm.

Mo Bei curled her lip.

It wasn't as if she could control it.

Whether it was an innate trait of being twins or some foul tampering by the Fallen Immortal Palace to make their "merchandise" more marketable, Mo Bei and Mo Xi possessed a degree of telepathy that let them hear each other's thoughts. Furthermore, whenever they were in close proximity, their actions influenced one another—such as unconsciously mirroring the exact same expression, mimicking identical gestures, or even delivering spoken lines that sounded like pre-rehearsed banter.

In such moments, the emotionally detached Mo Bei was influenced far more heavily by the emotionally volatile Mo Xi.

At the very least, due to the conditioning of the Fallen Immortal Palace, Mo Bei tended to let herself synchronize expressions with Mo Xi.

Identical silly grins.

It was just that Mo Bei's remarks were usually far more deadpan and demoralizing—which was precisely why their names were "Bei" (Sorrow) and "Xi" (Joy).

Even though Mo Xi had altered his cultivation manual and constitution, the twins still reflexively adopted identical postures when seen together outside.

Zhou Hui had long grown accustomed to the duo's comic [crosstalk routine](# "Literal CN: 相声 (xiàngsheng) — traditional comedic double-act / crosstalk").

—"It is precisely because of advanced age that one must study more and observe more,"-Zhou Hui replied good-temperedly.-"Besides, I have merely scratched the surface."

Saying so, he lifted a corner of the secret realm's paper model (prototype).

One could clearly see that the model not only faithfully reproduced the realm's topography, but also featured tiny paper figures leaping and hopping within, rendering the real-time dungeon-clearing progress of the trialees.

Because of this fidelity, everything was exceptionally vivid.

Above the heads of all the trialees floated red and blue status bars. Clicking on those bars revealed dense arrays of numerical data—including the mysterious "HP" and "MP" that completely baffled Mo Bei and Mo Xi, as well as stats they could vaguely comprehend, like Attack Power, Speed, and Defense.

Mo Xi had a 'sudden revelation':

—"Master, you are gathering intelligence on the disciples of other sects, aren't you?"

Mo Bei eyed Mo Xi coldly. [The Heavenly Spirit Sect isn't preparing to go to war with other sects; why on earth would Master collect that kind of intelligence?]

Zhou Hui's vision was indeed far more macroscopic than Mo Xi imagined:

—"Not only them, but all living entities,"-he explained, casually tapping open the stat panel of a demonic beast.

—"In the past, people merely categorized these creatures roughly into nine grades and thirteen ranks, winging it in combat based on gut feeling. In my view, much more precise quantitative data is required,"-Zhou Hui stated.

This could be considered an occupational OCD acquired from his experience in the other world!

Just think: back when launching rockets into space, figures were calculated down to five decimal places. Yet here, fatal life-and-death battles were addressed with: *"The cultivation is roughly around Soul Formation," "The lightning tribulation's power is about what a Nascent Soul can tank," "This spirit herb can probably cure a Golden Core."* It was just very... *erm*.

Children raised on native cultivation schooling could not fathom Zhou Hui's psychological torment for the time being.

Mo Xi undeniably belonged to the demographic exceptionally sharp with numbers—his crowning academic subject was, after all, *The Mathematical Classic*—yet even he asked with a puzzled expression:

—"Even if we know these numerical values, what of it?"

—"It allows us to simulate the secret realm's environment to the maximum possible fidelity,"-Zhou Hui replied.

Rather than launching into complicated theoretical exposition, Zhou Hui simply pulled out a sheet of paper, folded it several times, and tossed it into the model realm.

The origami paper instantly materialized within the realm as a Bi Fang bird, soaring about in smug triumph. Several cultivators promptly locked onto it, and following an arduous struggle, emerged victorious, sending light orbs scattering across the turf.

Such a faux bird naturally yielded no authentic beast materials. Consequently, the virtual prizes among the dropped loot were visibly far more enticing, prompting the cultivators to naturally forfeit the Bi Fang's physical materials.

Mo Xi's eyes ignited with brilliance:

—"Brilliant! What a masterstroke! Even if the entire secret realm gets picked clean, we can sustain it indefinitely using illusion arts! Furthermore, the combat prowess of the beasts can be dialed in at will. If we introduce an in-game cash shop where trialees can purchase weapons and medicinal pills with spirit stones, wouldn't that rake in infinitely more wealth than mere Annual and Monthly Passes?!"

[Why did Master hand such a magnificent concept to Second Senior Brother?! If he had given it to me, I'd have milked every other sect bone-dry in minutes!]

Little did he know, Zhou Hui looked at Mo Xi with a rueful, helpless smile.

[I already gifted him an entire spirit stone vein. Why is this boy still so obsessed with money?]

It seemed this boy could never be allowed to touch online gaming virtual currencies, microtransactions, or financial speculation without ironclad oversight; it would bode ill for him, and worse for the world.

—"Why must you [drain the pond to catch all the fish](# "Literal CN: 竭泽而渔 (jié zé ér yú) — fish by emptying the pool; kill the goose that lays golden eggs / unsustainable exploitation")?"-Zhou Hui guided Mo Xi gently.-"How do you know that allowing so many cultivators into the secret realm isn't actually to nourish and preserve it?"

—"Nourish and preserve?"-Mo Xi asked, utterly perplexed.

To his thinking, with crowds rampaging about burning, slaying, and pillaging (so to speak), the secret realm would be utterly ruined before long.

—"Do you remember the very first lecture I gave you all regarding the origin of spiritual energy?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Pop quizzes were Mo Xi's absolute worst nightmare, yet he answered obediently:

—"[Qi generates all things; the five zang organs generate the five qi](# "Literal CN: 一炁生万物，五藏生五炁 — classic Daoist cosmology from the Book of Changes and Huangdi Neijing; spiritual qi engenders all matter, while the internal organs cultivate and replenish qi")."

"Qi" here referred to spiritual energy.

The passage meant: *All myriad things arise from spiritual qi, while the five visceral organs generate spiritual qi.*

—"Correct,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

This was also the single greatest divergence between the cultivation world and the other world.

In the other world, humans consumed energy and could not synthesize energy out of nothing; they were active agents of the [law of entropy increase](# "Literal CN: 熵增定律 (shāngzēng dìnglǜ) — the Second Law of Thermodynamics; isolated systems naturally progress toward maximum disorder").

When it came to spiritual qi, however, living fauna did not exist purely as "consumers."

While a cultivator certainly absorbed ambient qi from the atmosphere, once that qi circulated through their meridians and dantian, it condensed into far denser, richer spiritual energy. In this dynamic, a cultivator was actually a *producer* of spiritual energy.

The higher a cultivator's realm, the more spiritual energy they generated.

At a Tribulation Transcendence stage like Zhou Hui's, even if he settled on a barren, desolate island completely devoid of spiritual qi to cultivate for a few years, that island would blossom into lush spiritual flora teeming with spirit beasts. The fact that the mineral vein beneath Mount Tianling was so luxuriantly rich could hardly be separated from Zhou Hui's passive radiance.

—Naturally, cultivators who solely poached monster cores, stripped spiritual herbs, and relied on pill-popping to force breakthroughs did not belong to this category. Such individuals merely plundered the fortune of Heaven and Earth for selfish gain—consuming everything while producing nothing. Regrettably, given the prevailing trends of the cultivation realm, reckless cultivators who cut corners in this manner were growing increasingly prevalent.

The same logic applied to spirit beasts and spiritual herbs; their relationship was fundamentally symbiotic.

—"Since you understand that spiritual qi is generated through cultivation, why do you fixate solely on spirit stones without noticing that we can guide the cultivators' methods of practice?"-Zhou Hui pointed out.

He gestured for his disciples to look back at the tiny figures who had slain the origami Bi Fang.

Speech bubbles popped up above the paper figures:

> *"I received spiritual incense that aids cultivation; if we begin meditating now, our efficiency will surpass normal times!"*  
> *"Then what are we standing around for?!"*

The paper figures immediately sat in meditation. Mist-like spiritual energy wafted from their bodies, settling gently upon the adjacent Snow-Glow Grass. Though imperceptible in the moment, over time, that Snow-Glow Grass would flourish with newfound vigor.

—"Speaking of which, Master also instructed me to practice my sword arts while watering the plots. Does the spiritual energy expended there work the same way?"-Mo Bei asked.

Zhou Hui nodded:

—"Precisely."

—"This method is far superior to merely sealing off a secret realm and leaving its ecosystem to grow at a crawl,"-Zhou Hui elaborated in detail.-"Simply locking it away effectively severs cultivators from the ecological cycle. But if we guide them appropriately—directing them to engage in combat and meditation in the most optimal locations—we can curb the wasteful destruction caused by rampaging beasts while nurturing the exact spiritual flora and fauna we require."

—"If all goes well, this methodology could even be applied to remediating the ancient god-demon battlefield,"-Zhou Hui sighed softly, gazing into the far distance.

Everyone knew that the Central Domain had once been the most prosperous, opulent cradle of this world. Tragically, the ancient war between the divine and demonic races had riddled that expanse with turbulent, toxic miasmas, rendering it uninhabitable to mortals to this day.

Although cultivators ventured into the ancient battlefield, their excursions were largely confined to relic-hunting and treasure-seeking.

Yet if this secret realm model were deployed, they might just be able to nibble away at the corruption and revive the Central Domain.

Such was Zhou Hui's aspiration.

Observing this, Mo Bei's lips curled into a faint smile.

She reflected that it wasn't just her brother whose imagination ran wild; Master's grand design was on a truly staggering scale.

—"...Even so, you could have entrusted the task to me,"-Mo Xi mumbled, still somewhat reluctant to concede.-"Compared to Second Senior Brother, when it comes to manipulating illusions and calculating numerical statistics, I'm clearly far more adept."

—"Since you possess such keen interest, you are welcome to assist your Second Senior Brother,"-Zhou Hui raised no objection.

—"That's not what I mean! What I'm saying is: Master, by helping Second Senior Brother like this, the spirit stones he earns instantly blew past mine! It's not fair!"-Mo Xi cried out.

With things like this, how was he supposed to brag to Master about the meager spirit stones he had scraped together?!

Mo Xi felt like a kid who had worked part-time all summer vacation to scrape together cash to buy his master fine tea—only to discover the family hotel had been handed over to Second Senior Brother to manage, and his hard-earned tea wasn't even worth a single glass of wine from the restaurant.

Zhou Hui had no inkling of what Mo Xi had been up to—though had he wished to know, a simple glance would suffice. In principle, however, Zhou Hui believed in respecting his disciples' privacy.

He assumed Mo Xi was merely squabbling over pocket money.

[He already owns an entire mine; what could this child possibly be greedy for?] Looking over at the blank-faced Mo Bei beside him, a thought struck Zhou Hui.

—"Come to think of it, favoring only Qianxing is indeed improper. As it happens, that Qianxing Hall... *pfft*, *cough cough*... that Qianxing Hall is rather empty as well,"-Zhou Hui mused, his eyes drifting toward the tea leaves cradled in Mo Bei's arms.-"I recall a couple of refreshments that young women quite enjoy... Bei'er, would you be interested in running a little venture with A-Ran to earn some pocket money? Lest you accuse me of playing favorites."

—"As for operational concepts..."-Zhou Hui raised his jade-white fingers, plucking two glowing orbs of memory from his mind. One was labeled **[Food Street](# "Literal CN: 小吃街 (xiǎochī jiē) — pedestrian street lined with snack stalls and street food vendors")**, and the other **[Cinema](# "Literal CN: 电影院 (diànyǐngyuàn) — movie theater")**. Passing both memories to Mo Bei, he added:-"You two may use these for reference."

—"Master?!"-Mo Xi wailed in tragic indignance.

That night, when Ran Deng crawled back into her immortal cave, she spotted a clear glazed cup resting upon the desk.

The cup held a juice(?) concoction formed of three distinct layers—vivid crimson, golden amber, and emerald green—with morsels of pulpy fruit settled at the bottom. It was strikingly gorgeous.

Her youngest junior sister sat clutching an identical cup filled with milk(?), expressionlessly sipping through a hollow straw.

—"What manner of treat is this?"-Ran Deng asked curiously.-"Miracles abound; you actually remembered me."

—"Pocket money,"-Mo Bei replied.

—"What pocket money... Mmm, delicious!"-Ran Deng took a sip, her eyes lighting up with delight.

Her brains felt as though they had been squeezed to the last drop, leaving her exhausted enough to sleep for three days and three nights. A single mouthful of sweet liquid felt like it had extended her lifespan by three days.

—"Bestowed by Master. Yours is fruit tea, and mine is boba milk tea,"-Mo Bei said, taking another sip of her milk tea, clearly quite enamored with it.-"Master suggested we could set up shop in Qianxing Hall to sell these. Whatever we earn will be our pocket money. He thought it might prove popular."

—"It'll be wildly popular, no question—I certainly can't refuse it,"-Ran Deng affirmed decisively.

Fishing out a plush cushion, she flopped onto it. Sipping her fruit tea, she pondered how to slack off:

—"Still, opening a shop sounds terribly troublesome... mm, it'll disrupt our cultivation. Why don't you have your brother find a couple of people to run it for us?"

Such was Ran Deng: even when it came to minting wealth, she found the effort bothersome.

Mo Bei suspected that Mo Xi's praise of *"Eldest Senior Sister always remembers you whenever good fortune strikes"* was purely because Eldest Senior Sister was too lazy to do things herself.

With that thought, Mo Bei shook her head:

—"I'm afraid that won't fly. Mo Xi is practically pickled in jealousy right now. We can certainly fleece some tea leaves off him, though; that fool bought enough tea to last us a hundred years."

—"Why on earth did he buy that much tea? Squandering spirit stones like that makes it awkward to approach him. Then again, this is pin money Master specifically reserved for us girls; letting Youngest Junior Brother meddle would hardly be proper,"-Ran Deng drawled languidly.-"Fortunately, the sect's minor recruitment drive is starting soon. When the time comes, I'll request a few disciple quotas from Second Senior Brother and select some female disciples to handle the labor."

[So in the end, aren't you still burdening another senior brother?]
Mo Bei continued roasting her internally while sipping her milk tea.

—"This is called mutual assistance,"-Ran Deng said without glancing up, as if reading her junior's mind.-"What need is there for ceremony between martial siblings? For example... Wait a minute. Seeing me drag myself back looking like a corpse, aren't you going to ask what I was doing?"

[—"What were you doing?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"Hmph,"-Ran Deng gave a dissatisfied huff before continuing:-"Ye-zi actually had a rare stroke of brilliance and figured out a way to honor Master, so he asked me to help him craft a [【...】](# "Literal CN: 【】 — authorial redaction/blank used for comedic suspense before the next scene")."

—"A [【...】](# "Literal CN: 【】 — authorial redaction/blank used for comedic suspense before the next scene")?!"-Mo Bei widened her eyes in rare shock, repeating the word Ran Deng had just uttered.

—"Indeed,"-Ran Deng said, watching her doll-like junior sister's stunned expression with smug satisfaction.-"Given Fourth Senior Brother's rigid, single-track mind, wasn't it a miracle he conceived such a genius concept? Figuring he wouldn't come up with another spark of inspiration like that in this lifetime, I put in genuine effort."

Ran Deng burrowed deeper into the plush cushion, her voice heavy with impending sleep:

—"Still, first Qianxing Hall, now the [【...】](# "Literal CN: 【】 — authorial redaction/blank used for comedic suspense before the next scene")... I feel like I'm about to drop dead."

The slumberous Ran Deng naturally failed to notice Mo Bei turning a deeply conflicted gaze toward Master's immortal cave:

Whether Eldest Senior Sister would drop dead was up in the air; what Mo Bei knew for certain was that her idiot brother was on the verge of dying from sheer fury.

—"A gift?"-Zhou Hui echoed with considerable intrigue, looking at his fourth disciple, Guan Ye.

This reaction made Mo Xi, who had been throwing tantrums and rolling on the floor in protest all afternoon, flare with jealousy all over again:

—"Master has never been this interested in my gifts!"

—"Because your Fourth Senior Brother doesn't have [as many twisted, crafty schemes](# "Literal CN: 花花肠子 (huāhuā chángzi) — flowery intestines; scheming tricks / manipulative angles") as you,"-Zhou Hui looked warmly at his disciple; to him, a gift from Guan Ye was indeed an exceptional rarity.-"Could it be that your cultivation has finally made progress? Ah..."

Zhou Hui's tone trailed off in disappointment.

With a single glance, he discerned that Guan Ye's cultivation hadn't advanced in the slightest—it remained in that same stagnant, half-dead state. On the other hand, Mo Xi's cultivation had surged ahead, already surpassing Guan Ye's.

Yet this was simply the harsh reality.

Judging solely by spiritual root aptitude, Guan Ye was the least endowed of them all—worse even than Zhou Hui's original baseline. He possessed a trash spiritual root, coupled with a damaged dantian that could not retain spiritual energy. Reaching the Foundation Establishment Middle Stage was purely thanks to practicing Zhou Hui's specialized cultivation manual and grinding with relentless diligence.

As a staunch representative of the hard-work faction, Zhou Hui refused to believe his disciple was incapable of cultivating. Yet while pushing himself through grueling effort felt natural, watching someone else crawl along at a snail's pace inevitably stirred an anxious itch in his heart—so much so that he wished he could swap spiritual roots with the boy.

Fortunately, this disciple possessed another specialty.

At the mention of cultivation, Guan Ye's face turned pale:

—"My apologies, Master. This disciple has made no progress..."

—"No, it was Master who was overly hasty. Simply proceed at your own pace,"-Zhou Hui had no heart to reprimand a child who worked diligently without visible fruit.

—"Though it is not cultivation,"-Guan Ye began,-"through researching the library illusion realm Master provided, I have gained some insights. I ask Master for your guidance."

—"The library?"-Zhou Hui echoed in surprise.

He had assumed that once the library pavilion was erected, only Ran Deng would lounge inside reading miscellaneous trivia books. Had Guan Ye also read something and prepared a book report?

Zhou Hui speculated inwardly, refraining from asking directly after his previous faux pas.

Guan Ye, however, produced a wooden box filled with colorful paper cutouts.

—"Wait, aren't those Eldest Senior Sister's paper cutouts?"-Mo Xi couldn't help piping up.-"Don't tell me you built something like Qianxing Hall too? But wasn't that mostly Eldest Senior Sister's doing?"

—"Yes. I am rather dull-witted; only Eldest Senior Sister could design that caliber of architecture, so I had no choice but to seek her assistance. However, the mechanism nestled inside is what I truly wish to present to Master,"-Guan Ye replied without taking offense. Turning nervously toward Zhou Hui, he asked:-"Master, would you mind if something were installed beneath your immortal cave?"

Beneath Zhou Hui's immortal cave, there was currently nothing.

The query sounded rather peculiar.

In truth, Zhou Hui's cave dwelling sat at the pinnacle of Mount Tianling's highest peak, the Heavenly Spirit Peak. Spread across the valley below was the entirety of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, with the most conspicuous structure being the Hall of Embracing Emptiness in the center.

By asking this, Guan Ye seemed intent on placing a structure suspended in midair—beneath Zhou Hui's immortal cave, yet hovering directly above the Hall of Embracing Emptiness.

This only stoked their curiosity as to what he had fabricated.

Mo Xi craned his neck, squinting at the contents for a long while; to his eyes, they looked like ordinary window paper cutouts of plum blossoms, orchids, bamboo, and chrysanthemums.

Zhou Hui didn't press him, indulging his disciple's suspense:

—"By all means."

Guan Ye sat bolt upright. Pulling a small folding fan from his wide sleeve, he gave the paper cutouts a brisk wave.

—This technique was far beyond the reach of a Foundation Establishment cultivator, meaning it, too, had been pre-enchanted by Ran Deng.

The paper cutouts took flight on the gust, swelling rapidly as they soared.

When resting in Guan Ye's palms, they had been mere scraps of paper. Mid-flight, they expanded into vast floating landmasses. By the time they anchored at their designated coordinates, some were lush with Snow-Glow Grass, others blanketed in emerald bamboo, and others ablaze with blossoming peach trees. Their sole shared feature was that each isle cradled a spirit spring flowing with distinct properties.

And winding between these floating islands like coiling dragons were colossal jade conduits!

—"Are those... spirit springs?"-Mo Xi asked in bewilderment. Turning an even more baffled gaze toward his fourth senior brother, he demanded:-"How on earth did you pull that off?!"

It wasn't that Mo Xi looked down on his senior brother. Diverting the natural flow of a spirit spring was something neither Fourth Senior Brother nor Eldest Senior Sister could achieve—even if they brought in their Soul Formation Eldest Senior Brother, he would likely fail.

In the cultivation world, weight was categorized into two varieties: the physical mass of mundane matter, and spiritual mass.

Physical mass was simple enough for cultivators; as long as one practiced the appropriate techniques, even a slender maiden could possess the might to uproot mountains. But redirecting a spirit spring required exerting spiritual energy far surpassing the spring's innate spiritual density. Thus, while cultivators could store an entire spirit spring inside a spatial ring, they could never suspend it aloft in midair, for doing so demanded a continuous, uninterrupted output of spiritual force equivalent to the entire spring itself.

[Perhaps... Master could?]
Mo Xi looked toward Master, yet undeniably, Guan Ye had accomplished it as well.

—"Because I used no spiritual energy,"-Guan Ye explained matter-of-factly.-"I redirected the spirit springs through purely physical methods. Much like how other sects alter mountain topography to reroute watercourses."

—"I don't think that's the same thing at all!"-Mo Xi retorted. When other sects did it, it took Nascent Soul powerhouses carving tectonic plates, whereas Guan Ye was a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator who hadn't touched the terrain!

Mo Xi knew he could never replicate it.

Zhou Hui was equally stunned—though the source of his astonishment differed entirely from Mo Xi's. He understood precisely how Guan Ye had done it:

—"Running tap water?! You laid plumbing pipes?! And one of them leads all the way into the secret realm?!"

This civil engineering project was utterly mind-boggling!

Unaware of the exact reason for Zhou Hui's shock, Guan Ye assumed Master found his handiwork devoid of technical elegance, and blushed:

—"Yes. It was the plumbing pipes depicted in the library. Seeing those existing blueprints, Shulong and I dismantled and studied them. We realized they operated without spiritual energy, making them ideal for channeling spirit springs. It was just that we struggled to find a suitable alloy capable of bearing spiritual fluid."

—"Ah!"-Mo Xi blurted as memory struck him.

He recalled Guan Ye complaining about this very problem back then, and how he had mocked him for wasting time on such foolish pursuits.

—"I experimented with numerous metallurgical formulas, and ultimately adapted the casting techniques of the Ascension Platform to synthesize a serviceable alloy,"-Guan Ye's blush deepened.-"Later, inside the secret realm, Master remarked that the Ice Eye was well-suited for cultivation. Remembering that Master also uses spirit springs in his daily routine, I took the liberty of laying pipes..."

—"How did you resolve the water pump?"-Zhou Hui inquired with acute interest.-"And the piping routes?"

—"I modified the base design of the water pump found in the library,"-Guan Ye produced a set of engineering blueprints.-"Here are the schematics. Master, please inspect them."

Zhou Hui accepted the schematics. What he saw scarcely resembled a conventional water pump: forged entirely from purple gold, it was gigantic in scale, resembling an alchemical furnace or ritual cauldron in silhouette. Even its mechanical principles bore only a loose kinship to an industrial pump.

Guan Ye had overhauled the pump's baseline mechanics—using pure physical hydraulic pressure to draw water from the spirit springs and the Ice Eye, while incorporating artifact-refining spiritual mechanisms to maintain the conduits and preserve water purity.

It was evident that Guan Ye had seamlessly synthesized his artifact-forging expertise into the design.

—"Good. Splendid!"-Zhou Hui praised warmly.

Naturally, what thrilled him wasn't simply the aerial bathhouses his disciple had erected—truth be told, bathing solo in midair was rather taxing on one's modesty; it might be better to have Ran Deng tack on a public bathhouse open to the entire sect (Ran Deng: [...])—what truly delighted Zhou Hui was Guan Ye's phenomenal capacity for learning and engineering.

This was a youth who had never been taught a day of foundational physics!

He suddenly realized his prior worries about Guan Ye were entirely superfluous. Even if this child were dropped into the other world, he would comfortably secure a place in Zhou Hui's aerospace research laboratory!

No—training him in this world might yield even greater wonders. Today he hand-forged a hydraulic pump; tomorrow he could hand-forge a space rocket!

Zhou Hui was thoroughly pleased.

Sensing this, Guan Ye finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Consequently, neither of them noticed Mo Xi's eyes, which had turned entirely bloodshot:

[Fourth Senior Brother, and here I thought you were an honest man!]

[How dare you [backstab](# "Literal CN: 背刺 (bèicì) — backstab; modern gaming slang for betrayal by an ally") me!]
