# Chapter 119: [Pointless Arguments](<# "Literal CN: 没有营养的争论 (méiyǒu yíngyǎng de zhēnglùn) — Pointless Arguments / Nutrition-less Squabbles; chapter title referring to the chaotic domain-wide flame war ignited in White Jade Capital Weekly">) (第119章 没有营养的争论)

—"Why is your newspaper printing every single piece of rubbish that comes its way?!"-Qu Qianxing demanded of Ran Deng right to her face, directly in front of Zhou Hui.

It was currently breakfast time, which had recently evolved into a new daily ritual among the Heavenly Spirit Sect's direct disciples.

Back when Zhou Hui wasn't in closed-door seclusion, the direct disciples had always loved concocting all manner of excuses to pay their morning respects. Ever since Zhou Hui had (in his own mind) retired, the disciples' free time had paradoxically shrunk. Yet even on days without lectures, they would make a point of finding a spare window to stop by their Master's quarters every single day—usually timing their visits with the three daily meals to [mooch a free bite](<# "Literal CN: 蹭饭 (cèng fàn) — to mooch a meal / freeload food">).

Qu Qianxing and Ran Deng typically mooched breakfast.

Since Zhou Hui ate breakfast each day at the crack of dawn, only Qu Qianxing and Ran Deng were ever around at such an ungodly hour.

Yet despite arriving at the same time, the two possessed diametrically opposed sleep schedules.

Qu Qianxing modeled his schedule after Zhou Hui's: rising early and beginning his morning meditation early, arriving at Zhou Hui's quarters first thing in the morning brimming with boundless vitality to pay his formal respects.

Ran Deng, on the other hand, had no predictable schedule whatsoever. Or rather, she seized every available opportunity to slack off—not by meditating, but by sleeping. The consequence of sleeping her fill during irregular hours was that she couldn't fall asleep when normal people rested. Instead, she simply pulled all-nighters drafting engineering schematics—claiming her inspiration flowed best at that hour—and soldiered through until dawn, dragging herself over to Master's table to mooch breakfast before stumbling back to bed on her last breath.

Consequently, seated on opposite sides of the morning dining table, one could behold Qu Qianxing, radiant as a fresh blossom glistening with morning dew, and Ran Deng, looking as though she had absorbed a full night's moon essence and was about to have her mortal soul ascend straight to the heavens.

Faced with Ran Deng's ghostly state, Qu Qianxing couldn't even be bothered to lecture her. Adopting the principle of [out of sight, out of mind](<# "Literal CN: 眼不见为净 (yǎn bù jiàn wéi jìng) — what the eye doesn't see, the heart doesn't grieve over; out of sight, out of mind">), he merely flipped through the latest copy of *White Jade Capital Weekly* she had brought over, taking the opportunity to discuss its highlights with Master.

Yet today, the instant Qu Qianxing opened the paper, his attention was violently arrested by the headline plastered across page two:

>*Innovative Cultivation?! [Sensational Grandstanding](<# "Literal CN: 哗众取宠 (huázhòng-qǔchǒng) — seeking the clamor of the crowd to win favor; sensationalism / playing to the gallery">) by Central Domain Cultivators*

The moment Qu Qianxing saw that title, his brow furrowed deep.

Whether Central Domain cultivators were genuinely grandstanding or not remained to be seen, but with such a [sweeping regional carpet-bomb](<# "Literal CN: 地图炮 (dìtúpào) — map cannon / area-of-effect carpet-bombing; internet slang for indiscriminate attacks or generalizations targeting an entire group or region">), the author had certainly succeeded in commanding maximum attention right off the bat.

Sure enough, beneath the bombastic headline, a cultivator writing under the pen name "Fake Fish" had cursed every single Mahayana cultivator across the Central Domain up and down. The piece accused them of academic misconduct and an obsessive pursuit of flashy novelty for novelty's sake, raised blatant suspicions that they were ghostwriting theses on behalf of their disciples, and even went so far as to cast doubt upon the editorial integrity of *Spells*.

Qu Qianxing's blood pressure skyrocketed with every word.

*Spells* was an academic journal managed and coordinated directly under his watch. This was practically pointing a finger straight between his eyes and spitting curses into his face!

—"Why would you ever publish something that is so blatantly an irrational, bad-faith temper tantrum?"-Qu Qianxing demanded of Ran Deng, slamming the newspaper down onto the table.

—"How is it an irrational temper tantrum? It made plenty of sense to me,"-Ran Deng said, curling her lips.-"There really *are* mighty elders ghostwriting research papers for their disciples, aren't there? It wasn't that egregious before, but during this round of treatises on the heavenly tribulation, it stuck out like a sore thumb. Several authors were visibly nowhere near Heavenly Spirit Sect. Had they actually witnessed Master crossing his tribulation with their own two eyes, they couldn't possibly have written such preposterous drivel!"

—"Be that as it may, that is orthodox academic discourse! Furthermore, if you ask me, this article right here is the one written by an elder who never saw Master cross his tribulation and ghostwrote for someone else!"-Qu Qianxing snapped, slapping the front page.-"No matter how you look at it, this author is simply [flying into a rage from sheer humiliation](<# "Literal CN: 恼羞成怒 (nǎoxiū chéngnù) — flying into a rage out of shame and embarrassment">). If she had been at Heavenly Spirit Sect... no, if she had set foot in the Central Domain and watched even a single match of the Immortal Arts Assembly, she would never have uttered such utter rubbish!"

—"And so what if she hasn't?"-Ran Deng arched an eyebrow.

Qu Qianxing glared at Ran Deng.

—"This is a wonderful sign. It proves that people from other domains have started paying close attention to the Central Domain and are eager to make their voices heard,"-Ran Deng argued with absolute conviction.-"Would you prefer this grand elder squabble with the other grandmasters directly within the pages of *Spells*, or do you think it's better to deny her any platform to speak at all?"

—"But this is blatantly bad-faith troublemaking—"-Qu Qianxing protested.

—"That's simply because she has no idea what is actually happening in the Central Domain. No one possesses omniscience; perspectives are bound to be skewed. Rather than leaving her with bottled-up fury and nowhere to vent it, forcing her to find some other outlet, I don't mind *White Jade Capital Weekly* serving as the arena,"-Ran Deng continued, clearly having anticipated Qu Qianxing's objections well in advance.-"Do you have any idea how many sects and syndicates have hatched plans to print their own newspapers ever since *White Jade Capital Weekly* became a continental bestseller? Some have already put ink to parchment."

As she spoke, Ran Deng rummaged through her storage pouch and produced several folded sheets of newsprint.

Among them, a publication titled *Cultivation World Weekly* stood out with glaring prominence. Its layout, typeface, and column formatting bore an uncanny resemblance to *White Jade Capital Weekly*, but upon closer inspection, the publisher was none other than the Carefree Palace.

Qu Qianxing hadn't anticipated this turn of events at all and froze mid-breath.

—"Since it is impossible to gag every mouth in the world, taking advantage of our first-mover advantage to seize continental attention and cement the universal perception that we are the 'most impartial' and 'most authoritative' publication is an absolute necessity. This is merely step one,"-Ran Deng said, smoothing out the folds of *White Jade Capital Weekly* as she spoke with leisurely deliberation.-"Even if a war must be fought, it ought to be fought on *our* home turf. I read a quote in Master's library once that left a profound impression on me: [If we do not occupy the propaganda battlefield, our enemies surely will](<# "Quote from Zhou Hui's modern library (originally Chairman Mao): 宣传的阵地，我们不去占领，别人就会去占领 — ideological battlegrounds must be seized proactively">)."

—"...Are you certain you can keep this 'battlefield' under control?"-Qu Qianxing asked, staring intently at Ran Deng.

—"Yeah, it really is a giant pain in the neck,"-Ran Deng surrendered without a second of hesitation.-"How about I just quit? Seeing as everyone has recognized how important newspapers are anyway, I certainly won't be running short on gossip to read..."

—"Don't you dare!"-Qu Qianxing barked.

Catching the playful, teasing glint in Ran Deng's eyes, he suddenly realized he had walked straight into her trap.

Qu Qianxing's face instantly flushed crimson.

—"I am going to show this article to the others right now. There will be an absolute avalanche of rebuttal submissions incoming, and you had better review every single one with utmost diligence! Not an ounce of slacking off!"-Qu Qianxing threw down the command, ostensibly weaponizing Ran Deng's greatest weakness against her, before hastily beating a retreat.-"Just you wait!"

—"Oh, I'm so terrified!"-Ran Deng scoffed.

In any case, when it came to heavy academic submissions, Ran Deng had already recruited dedicated peer reviewers.

She beamed with mischievous satisfaction as she watched Qu Qianxing flee in disarray.

—"Ah-Ran, don't tease Qianxing too harshly,"-Zhou Hui finally said with a faint smile.

However, his silence throughout their banter had already made his stance crystal clear:

He supported Ran Deng.

The reason he hadn't stated it outright was that, in Zhou Hui's memory, his third disciple was the absolute nemesis of his second disciple.

As the de facto leader of the direct disciples and the most revered senior brother in the eyes of his junior siblings, Qu Qianxing had, in truth, never once emerged victorious in any argument or competition against Ran Deng.

Owing to her chronic laziness, Ran Deng had indeed been micromanaged and targeted by Qu Qianxing for a period in the past. Yet in retrospect, it was obvious that Ran Deng had mapped out every single one of Qu Qianxing's emotional trigger points. Every encounter saw her cheerfully dancing upon his bottom line, leaving Qu Qianxing utterly powerless to do anything about her.

When Ran Deng established the newspaper, she had preemptively secured full executive autonomy from Zhou Hui, clearly having prepared for situations just like today so Qu Qianxing would be blocked from interfering from the very start.

To this, Ran Deng made no secret of having deliberately hedged against her Second Senior Brother:

—"Can't help it! Second Senior Brother and I share an irreconcilable feud!"

—"A feud?!"-Zhou Hui's hand paused mid-motion as he looked toward Ran Deng.

—"Of course! [I was the one who arrived first!](<# "Anime meme / White Album 2 reference: 是我先来的 — 'I was the first one there, the first one to know him, why did someone else take the spot?'">) I was the one who met Master first! I was the one who began learning things from Master first—I still remember Master personally teaching me the first time I ever drew! So why on earth did *he* get to become 'Second Senior Brother'?!"-Ran Deng grumbled.

Speaking of this, Ran Deng genuinely harbored a deep sense of grievance.

Her clan and Zhou Hui were family friends of many generations, so Ran Deng felt as though she had already "known" Master before she was even born into this world. It was only because her clan had its own ancestral inheritance that she hadn't formally taken Zhou Hui as her master. It wasn't until she had a falling out with her family and was sent to Zhou Hui's side that she formally knelt and apprenticed. Yet during that awkward window where she knew him without formally bowing, Qu Qianxing had suddenly materialized out of nowhere and snatched the title of Master's "Second Disciple"! The mere thought of it still made Ran Deng's teeth itch with indignation.

Zhou Hui, however, couldn't see what practical difference there was between being the second disciple or the third disciple.

Even though he had listened to Ran Deng whine about it countless times.

—"You're harping on that again?"-Zhou Hui remarked, [not knowing whether to laugh or cry](<# "Literal CN: 哭笑不得 (kūxiào-bùdé) — torn between laughter and tears / amused exasperation">).

For a fleeting second, he had almost suspected that Ran Deng, like Lu Shulong, had awakened memories of their previous lives.

According to the Heavenly Dao's script, Ran Deng had met her end after Qu Qianxing ordered her to escort the other disciples to safety. He had wondered whether recovering such memories might kindle genuine resentment toward Qu Qianxing.

However, based on Zhou Hui's observations, his two female disciples appeared to be the farthest beings in existence from ever recovering their past-life memories:

One was too lazy to think, while the other had entirely given up on thinking. Their brains seemed to lack a storage drive altogether, cleaner than a freshly formatted disk.

Zhou Hui considered this a blessing... probably?

Seeing Master gazing at her with a wry smile, Ran Deng seemed to misinterpret his look.

She hurriedly raised her hand in solemn vow:

—"Though when real crisis strikes, I will definitely listen to Second Senior Brother!"

—"...There is no need to be *that* obedient,"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"Eh?"-Ran Deng's eyes went wide as saucers.

Looking at his disciple's sparkling eyes, Zhou Hui decided it was best not to encourage the girl to escalate her squabbles with her senior brother any further. Changing the subject, he asked:

—"Setting that aside, do you have any free time lately?"

—"M-Master, you heard Second Senior Brother just now. With all those incoming submissions, I'm going to be utterly swamped!"-Ran Deng stammered.

—"I heard Fairy Xia paid you a visit recently? Couldn't she serve as a reviewer? Besides, her innate spell seems exceptionally well-suited for information gathering,"-Zhou Hui noted. Seeing Ran Deng's eyes darting around as she searched for another excuse to shirk work, he added quietly,-"What I need to do next concerns your father."

Ran Deng immediately pulled a face of pure distaste:

—"What happened to my old man? Because I am definitely not going home!"

—"Your father likely has no time to mind you for the moment anyway,"-Zhou Hui said, massaging his aching temples.-"Do you recall Siwu? That—"

—"That [deviant gay guy](<# "Literal CN: 变态基佬 (biàntài jīlǎo) — perverted / deviant gay guy; internet slang for homosexual man, picked up by Ran Deng from Zhou Hui's modern memory files">) who disrupted the Immortal Arts Assembly, got disciplined by Master, fell madly in love with Master because of it, and shamelessly lingered at our Heavenly Spirit Sect refused to leave?"-Ran Deng's eyes lit up with blazing excitement once again.

[Your impression of Siwu is profoundly twisted! And where on earth did you pick up the term "gay"?!]

—"...He did not come here voluntarily,"-Zhou Hui said helplessly.

Omitting the part where the little black sheep (Yuwen Xuan) had stirred up trouble, he briefly recounted how he had fished Siwu out of the Abyssal Ocean.

Zhou Hui concluded:

—"Thus, he is not infatuated with me. He simply had nowhere else to turn."

—"Tsk!"-Ran Deng clicked her tongue.

—"Siwu's true objective in heading to the Abyssal Ocean... and indeed, in coming to our Central Domain, was that the East Hedge Garden uncovered artifacts pertaining to True Immortals,"-Zhou Hui continued.-"Yet despite him having lost his way for so long, the East Hedge Garden has shown zero activity whatsoever. That is far too abnormal."

Hearing this, Ran Deng dropped her careless expression, her demeanor turning solemn:

—"I recall when Father visited the Heavenly Spirit Sect over half a year ago, he mentioned going to help the East Hedge Garden unravel the ancient formations deep within a set of ruins. Could he have—"-Ran Deng trailed off.

—"It is most likely intimately connected. At the very least, Master Jiuzhai can no longer 'glimpse' their whereabouts,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Master—"-Ran Deng gasped.

—"Rest assured, we shall set out to find him shortly,"-Zhou Hui comforted her.-"However, I may require you to handle a few preparations..."

***

While Zhou Hui and Ran Deng were already setting to work over this nascent crisis, Qu Qianxing was making his way toward the Academic Hall of the Palace of Silence.

His head was still pounding over the newspaper.

Qu Qianxing had no idea that Ran Deng was busy extracting "vengeance" for an ancient grudge.

Even had he known, he wouldn't have cared in the slightest. After all, whoever knelt to apprentice first won the seniority; who told Ran Deng to dilly-dally for so long on her own?

Compared to petty domestic grievances, Qu Qianxing was far more concerned about the fallout that blasted article would cause.

And just as he had anticipated, the moment he stepped into the Academic Hall, he beheld more than a dozen mighty elders brandishing newspapers, locked in furious, spittle-flying debate.

[Don't these people ever sleep?]

[...Ah, right, they genuinely don't.] They didn't even need to meditate; they could remain brimming with restless energy right up until their lifespans expired.

Reflecting on this, Qu Qianxing suddenly felt that giving these grandmasters something to occupy their time wasn't such a bad idea after all.

Most of them did nothing but burn themselves out in endless internal friction over an impossible ascension, offering not a shred of tangible contribution to the cultivation realm.

Qu Qianxing thought caustically.

Oblivious to the contempt with which Qu Qianxing regarded them, the cultivators swarmed him the moment he entered, waving their papers and demanding answers in much the same manner Qu Qianxing had confronted Ran Deng earlier:

—"Little Friend Qu, what on earth is the meaning of this article?!"-one elder demanded.

—"Are you simply going to sit by and allow this wretch to slander the authority of *Spells* right within your own newspaper?!"-another shouted.

—"Seniors, pray calm yourselves,"-Qu Qianxing said, offering a serene, measured smile.-"This is merely a 'primer.'"

—"A primer?"-several cultivators asked in unison.

—"Indeed. Did none of you notice the headline? The author is clearly not a Central Domain cultivator, but hailing from another domain entirely,"-Qu Qianxing said, taking the prime opportunity to [fan the flames](<# "Literal CN: 拱火 (gǒng huǒ) — stoking the fire / fanning the flames / instigating conflict">).-"Note how insufferably haughty the tone is, carrying on as if they alone were the true center of the cultivation world."

[—"Now that you mention it, that is true..."](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Elder">)

[—"It must be a Northern Domain cultivator! Very likely someone from Sky City! Only those bastards carry around that insufferable, look-down-their-noses attitude!"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Elder">)

[—"Tsk, what gives them the right?! Our Central Domain has been the heart and soul of the cultivation world since ancient antiquity!"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Elder">)

[—"Precisely! When White Jade Capital was rising to cosmic prominence, the Northern Domain was still chewing on snow!"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Elder">)

Qu Qianxing listened with a beaming smile to their regional grievances and prejudices. Only when their complaints tapered off did he smoothly continue:

—"Precisely so! They fail to understand that the arguments published in *Spells* are not merely novel—they have undergone rigorous empirical verification! Every single thesis has been subjected to repeated experimentation throughout the Immortal Arts Assembly. On the foundation of those trials, you esteemed seniors went on to publish brand-new treatises. During this peer review cycle, the reason you felt certain submissions were hopelessly obsolete is the definitive proof of the immense progress you have achieved through relentless refinement!"-Qu Qianxing declared.

Hearing this flattering yet razor-sharp analysis, the corners of every cultivator's mouth involuntarily curled upward.

Thinking it through carefully, wasn't that precisely the reality?!

Ever since the Immortal Arts Assembly commenced, they had authored countless papers, debated and polished their theories with peers of identical cultivation, watched tournament competitors put their theoretical constructs into live combat practice, and rectified shortcomings. Without even realizing it, they were no longer the cultivators they had been merely months ago!

—"It would be an immense tragedy if your hard-won achievements were not witnessed by the entire cultivation realm! What is more, the Immortal Arts Assembly is meant to select the 'Central Domain Representatives'! It is not enough for merely our own people to acknowledge your brilliance; the entire cultivation world must behold your true might,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"And this very article has served the exquisite role of [casting a brick to attract jade](<# "Idiom: 抛砖引玉 (pāozhuān-yǐnyù) — tossing out a crude brick to elicit valuable jade; offering a modest initial contribution to provoke brilliant responses">)."

Watching his poised, authoritative bearing, not a soul could have guessed that merely moments ago, he had been squabbling over this very article with his junior sister before Master, only to be verbally disarmed and forced to flee in shame.

With supreme confidence, Qu Qianxing concluded:

—"Moving forward, *White Jade Capital Weekly* will select far more rigorous and compelling rebuttal manuscripts to vindicate everyone's honor. We shall never permit such misunderstandings to persist—"-Qu Qianxing promised.

[—"Haha, wonderful! That is good to hear!"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Elder">)

The cultivators nodded heartily. Yet several wore deeply contemplative expressions, some traded meaningful glances, and others clutched *White Jade Capital Weekly* while sporting exceedingly sinister grins.

No matter how one looked at them, not a single one appeared inclined to meekly sit by and wait for *others* to vindicate their honor.

Seeing their reactions, Qu Qianxing felt thoroughly reassured.

***

However, there were certain individuals who required zero instigation to spring into action.

—"What kind of utter nonsense is this?!"-a furious voice screamed.

Beneath a blood-red cliff somewhere in the Western Domain, a piercing screech suddenly tore through the air. The crows perched atop the crag pecking at corpses let out hoarse, panicked caws, abandoning the rotting carrion in their beaks to scatter into the distance.

Deep within a cavernous karst abyss beneath the cliff hung an inverted, blood-red palace. The master of this palace was furiously scattering a pile of smoldering ashes across the floor. If one were to magically restore those burnt fragments, they would reveal the unmistakable form of an issue of *White Jade Capital Weekly*.

—"How dare it claim that [Zhen](<# "Literal CN: 朕 (zhèn) — imperial first-person pronoun 'We' reserved for emperors">) ghostwrote?! That Zhen committed academic fraud?!"-the palace master roared in incandescent fury.

Judging from his outburst, he clearly wasn't the primary target of the attack, but merely one of the countless casualties caught in the crossfire of the regional carpet-bombing. Even so, the barbed words had scraped against his rawest nerve.

—"The disciples in Zhen's palace practice the cultivation arts bestowed by Zhen! Naturally, they represent Zhen's sacred legacy! What fault could there possibly be in Zhen using their names to propagate Zhen's Great Dao?! How does that count as fraud?!"-the entity howled, his voice vibrating through stone.

Minions knelt in droves across the stone floor. Yet far from attempting to soothe their lord's fury, several seized the opportunity to stoke the flames:

[—"They are unquestionably insulting you, Your Majesty!"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

—"Precisely! It is insulting me!"-the entity addressed as Your Majesty roared.-"It looks down upon Zhen's 'Great Dao'! It slanders Zhen as a fraud! They presume that they alone represent orthodoxy! Yet in the matter of ascension, is not whoever ascends the true orthodoxy?! What if Zhen utilized a disciple's name? To gaze upon Zhen's divine teachings is their supreme honor!"

[—"Kill him!"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

[—"Slaughter them all!"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

A chaotic chorus of bloodthirsty cries echoed throughout the palace. Someone even leaped at the chance to broaden the theater of war:

[—"Not only the scoundrel who penned this drivel, but also the newspaper that dared publish it! And the creature who founded that rag—they are all heaping insults upon your head! Slaughter them all, Your Ma—"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

Before the speaker could finish his sentence, his head and four limbs detached from his torso and crashed wetly onto the flagstones, blood erupting in geysers across the chamber!

—"What raving madness are you spewing?! Do you wish for Zhen to start a war with Lord Untiring?!"-"His Majesty" cried out, his voice trembling with latent terror.

An instant of dead silence engulfed the palace.

—"Besides, Lord Untiring printing this article must have his profound reasons!"-"His Majesty" continued, pacing back and forth before his tone abruptly pivoted into ecstatic revelation.-"Wait, no! Zhen misunderstood! This is a golden opportunity to preach my Dao! So long as I publicly denounce this article and eviscerate their claims, will not the entire world come to know my Great Dao?!"

[—"Your Majesty is brilliant!"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

[—"Your Majesty's wisdom knows no bounds!"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

Another round of chaotic, fawning adulation swept through the hall.

"His Majesty" paid them no mind, barking:

—"Bring ink and brush!"-His Majesty bellowed.

Instantly, crimson skeletal frames crawled out with eerie clattering sounds, presenting parchment and brush upon their bended spines.

A sycophantic retainer asked gingerly:

[—"Your Majesty, shall you still submit under that disciple's name? Or perhaps swap to several other disciples so they cannot seize any handle against you?"](<# "Speaker: Western Domain Cultist">)

—"No need. This is not an academic paper. Like the opponent, Zhen shall simply adopt a pen name,"-His Majesty sneered.

***

Naturally, there were several other mighty cultivators who flew into identical rages like this "brilliant Majesty"—most notably the very grandmaster who had rejected Fairy Ruopei's thesis. Though they were hardly as "sagacious" as His Majesty, all of them resolved to pick up their brushes and launch blistering counterattacks in print.

However, when it comes to open flame wars, people easily lose all semblance of rationality once the blood rushes to their heads.

Particularly since Fairy Ruopei had been the first to unleash the nuclear option of regional carpet-bombing, the rebuttal submissions were naturally unhinged. Even after being meticulously vetted by the editorial department of *White Jade Capital Weekly*, the banner headline of the very next issue read:

>*The Stubbornness of Old Antiques?! [Clinging to Antiquated Relics](<# "Literal CN: 抱残守缺 (bàocán-shǒuquē) — cherishing the broken and guarding the incomplete; stubborn conservatism / clinging to archaic ways">) Among Northern Domain Cultivators*

Well then. Even the headline formed a [perfect poetic antithesis](<# "Literary parallelism: 《创新性修仙？！中境修士的哗众取宠》 vs 《老古董的坚持？！北境修士的抱残守缺》 — exactly mirrored syntactic structure and rhythm">) with the original.

This happened to coincide precisely with the period when Northern Domain cultivators, having turned their gaze toward the Central Domain due to the heavenly anomaly, had heard rumors of the academic journal and newspaper and placed bulk subscription orders. The moment their copies of *White Jade Capital Weekly* arrived in their hands, this headline provoked incandescent fury across the north:

[—"What is the meaning of this?!"](<# "Speaker: Northern Domain Cultivator">)

[—"Do they think our Northern Domain has no champions left alive?!"](<# "Speaker: Northern Domain Cultivator">)

[—"Utterly intolerable bullying! If anyone qualifies as old antiques, it is those Central Domain fossils who are the true antiques!"](<# "Speaker: Northern Domain Cultivator">)

[—"Exactly! Even the very name of their newspaper is White Jade Capital!"](<# "Speaker: Northern Domain Cultivator">)

Without Fairy Ruopei even needing to mount a second assault, cultivators across the Northern Domain snatched up their brushes in droves.

In the blink of an eye, fiery salvos flew back and forth across the columns of *White Jade Capital Weekly* in an uproarious spectacle. The enthusiasm of titans and grandmasters across practically the entire cultivation world was whipped into a frenzy: some argued passionately, some stirred the pot, and some simply pulled up chairs to watch the drama unfold.

Someone even took advantage of the domain-wide chaos to purchase a classified ad in *White Jade Capital Weekly* searching for a pen pal they had lost touch with several centuries ago!

Within the Heavenly Dao Alliance, Lady Xin Zhu, a supreme powerhouse of the Tribulation Transcendence stage, was a [pure drama goblin](<# "Literal CN: 乐子人 (lèzǐrén) — fun-seeker / drama goblin; internet slang for someone who lives to watch chaos and drama for entertainment">). Watching these domain-wide flame wars unfold, she was laughing herself sick.

Brandishing the freshest issue of the paper, Xin Zhu hurried to share the joy with her husband:

—"Look at this! Didn't I tell you that article last time was blatant flame-bait? Sure enough, this issue features five or six pieces openly and covertly rebutting it! I heard that publishing once a week can't even contain the volume of arguments anymore, and they are preparing to transition into a daily paper... Su Mei, what in the world are you doing?!"-Xin Zhu called out.

She stared with a bizarre expression at her husband.

There sat Lord Su Mei—a leisure-loving Tribulation Transcendence titan who had crossed the threshold ages ago, had long abandoned all ambition of advancing his cultivation, and was thoroughly resolved to idle away his days at home [enjoying sweets with his son](<# "Literal CN: 含饴弄子 (hányí-nòngzǐ) — playing with children while eating candy; enjoying the peaceful idleness of domestic family life">). Yet right now, he was perched before his study desk as though preparing for a breakthrough of centuries past! Mountains of ancient tomes were piled before and behind him; several volumes floated suspended in mid-air, automatically flipping to the exact pages Su Mei required. Cross-referencing these ancient texts, Su Mei was scribbling away with ferocious intensity.

Faced with his wife's sharp interrogation, it took Su Mei a good long moment to snap back to reality.

—"Ahem, cough! I saw how delightfully hilarious their squabbles were, and my fingers grew a bit itchy..."-Su Mei chuckled dryly.

Xin Zhu was struck utterly speechless.

She had never imagined that the [melon-eating gossip circus](<# "Literal CN: 吃瓜吃到自己身上 (chīguā chīdào zìjǐ shēnshàng) — eating melons until one eats one's own melon; spectator drama landing on one's own doorstep">) would land straight upon her own household!

—"Don't worry! I certainly won't publish under my own name—that would be far too undignified!"-Su Mei promised loudly, carefully reading his wife's changing expression.-"I used our Xin'er's name! I guarantee not a single soul will ever find out!"

With that, Su Mei fawningly shoved his half-written manuscript in front of her face:

—"Take a look! Isn't the scolding magnificent? Doesn't it capture the majestic flair of back when our clans tried to force arranged marriages upon us, and I roundly cursed out those [meddlesome aunts and gossiping matrons](<# "Literal CN: 三姑六婆 (sāngū-liùpó) — three aunts and six grannies; meddlesome women / busybodies">)? Even after all these centuries, I feel my cursing prowess hasn't degraded in the slightest!"-Su Mei fawned.

—"Are you an idiot?"-Xin Zhu couldn't help but ask.
