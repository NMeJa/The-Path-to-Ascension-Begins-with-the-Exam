# Chapter 205: Each Performing Their Duties (第205章 各司其职)

Why did this bastard always have to do superfluous things?!

Why did he have to make such a big fuss about cleaning up the Heavenly Robe?! Once the affair was over, couldn't he just sneak it back into place?!

Ran Deng nearly fainted on the spot.

She couldn't count how many times she had bitterly regretted collaborating with Yuwen Xuan.

To be frank, having known him for two hundred years... no, across seven or eight lifetimes spanning thousands of years, Ran Deng had never managed to comprehend the true depth of Yuwen Xuan's intellect. This "person's" brain was like a kite caught in a gale: when the wind was favorable, it could soar into the stratosphere, but the moment you looked away, its string would snap, or it would plunge nose-first into the dirt, even tangling itself with other bizarre, unidentified debris. In Master's memorable words: Yuwen Xuan suffered from an [incurable affliction of the head](# "Literal CN: 头有贵恙 (tóu yǒu guì yàng) — an exalted sickness in the head; politely calling someone a lunatic").

Take right now, for instance. Ran Deng couldn't tell whether Eldest Senior Brother was trying to frame the 'thief' or trying to drag her down with him—or perhaps in Yuwen Xuan's mind, there was zero difference between the two.

One had to understand: when Ran Deng stole the Heavenly Robe, in order to ensure Yuwen Xuan wouldn't bolt the moment he put it on, she had specifically branded the garment with her own spiritual energy tracking sigil! In Master's eyes, that mark was as glaringly obvious as writing her name across the fabric in black ink with a calligraphy brush!

Ran Deng had no desire whatsoever to be strung up from the peach tree alongside Eldest Senior Brother!

She still possessed a sense of shame!

Fortunately, Master did not spare the celestial robe a second glance, granting Ran Deng a temporary reprieve. Instead, he strode forward, seized the little black lamb by the scruff of its neck, hoisted it into the air, and forced its jaws apart.

—"Ying?!"

The little black lamb stared at Master with massive, round, black-pearl eyes, looking the very picture of wide-eyed innocence.

Witnessing this, Zhou Hui let out a weary sigh. Yet he raised his other hand, plunging it mercilessly straight down the little black sheep's throat!

—"Ying-wuuugh!"

The little black lamb's strangled yelp was choked right in its gullet.

It looked to be in excruciating agony, its eyes rolling back into its skull, its tiny cloven hooves flailing frantically in midair. Only its jaws dared not exert force; on the contrary, it stretched its mouth open as wide as possible, terrified that the rows of razor-sharp fangs no ordinary lamb possessed might nick Zhou Hui's arm, causing glistening streams of saliva to spill from its lips and drag down in long, gooey strands.

Yet even with its utmost efforts, the tiny lamb's mouth was nowhere near as wide as Zhou Hui's fist.

Furthermore, as a 'nursing lamb,' its body was only marginally larger than a Maine Coon cat; thus, the length of its head and its entire torso combined did not exceed an adult human male's forearm. Yet as long as one could stomach the densely packed layers of razor fangs inside its throat, Zhou Hui's entire arm plunged all the way in, as though reaching into a bottomless, yawning black void.

Zhou Hui rummaged around inside that dark void, seeking to fish out whatever this 'sheep' had just swallowed.

The process was clearly far from pleasant; the little black lamb looked on the verge of rolling its eyes back and fainting into oblivion.

The spectacle was too gruesome to watch.

Confronted with this scene, Ran Deng took several quiet steps backward, striving with all her might to shrink her presence to absolute zero.

In truth, compared to that idiotic sheep, what Ran Deng cared about most was the saliva-drenched celestial robe on the ground. It was just that she wasn't certain whether stepping forward to retrieve it was wise; her instincts told her it was an exceptionally terrible idea.

—"...Is that Eldest Senior Brother?"-a voice suddenly inquired from behind.

Ran Deng nearly leapt out of her skin.

Whipping her head around, she found that Guan Ye had walked over at some point, his gaze fixed on the black sheep, appearing utterly incapable of processing the situation:

—"How did he turn into a sheep? No, wait, how could that sheep be him? Isn't that the sheep Master has kept by his side for the past year?"-Guan Ye asked, thoroughly perplexed.

—"Yes, isn't that completely obvious?"-Ran Deng saw no need for debate.

—"No, why?"-Guan Ye's honest, literal-minded brain failed to make the connection.

Evidently, had the little black lamb not spoken aloud in Eldest Senior Brother's unmistakable voice, Guan Ye would never have deduced that Eldest Senior Brother had turned into a sheep, not even in a thousand years.

—"There is no 'why,'"-Ran Deng was in no mood to offer detailed explanations,-"That's Eldest Senior Brother. Since it's Eldest Senior Brother, is it ever surprising when he does something completely batsh*t?!"

—"Mind your language,"-Guan Ye corrected her mildly.

He cast a curious glance at Ran Deng, wondering why Eldest Senior Sister was suddenly so short-tempered.

Yet Ran Deng's reasoning successfully persuaded Guan Ye, even allowing him to grasp the situation with lightning speed.

Considering the individual in question was 'Eldest Senior Brother,' it was entirely understandable for that bastard to do anything bizarre. Unsurprisingly, he had evidently pulled another stunt worthy of being hung from the peach tree bough for half a year.

This seemed to be the most routine occurrence in the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Guan Ye breathed a quiet sigh of relief, deciding there was no need to waste further worry.

Until several minutes later, when Zhou Hui called out to him:

—"Ye-zi, come have a look at this."

—"Yes, Master?"

Guan Ye walked over, watching as Zhou Hui withdrew his arm from the little black sheep's throat, clutching an object in his grasp.

Zhou Hui was evidently indicating that Guan Ye should inspect that object.

Guan Ye did not look at it immediately. Instead, out of ingrained habit, he reached into his storage pouch, retrieved a flask of spirit spring water and a towel woven of fire-asbestos silk, and handed them to Zhou Hui:

—"Master, please wipe your hands."

This was an automatic reflex.

He was accustomed to tending to Lu Shulong, and Lu Shulong, as an alchemist, perpetually took advantage of the dragon clan's thick hide and coarse flesh to handle lethal toxins bare-handed (the dragon himself claimed that tactile authenticity gave him a superior feel for ingredients). Lu Shulong remained completely immune, but he routinely endangered everyone around him; consequently, whenever Guan Ye saw someone with soiled hands, he instinctively prompted them to wash.

Furthermore, Zhou Hui's hand wasn't merely dirty right now—his entire forearm was drenched in glistening, slobbering saliva, prompting a strong urge in Guan Ye to submerge Master entirely in a vat of water and scrub him vigorously with a stiff brush.

Eldest Senior Brother's drool was very likely more terrifying than ordinary poison!

Guan Ye held this deeply prejudiced conviction.

Zhou Hui evidently shared a similar sentiment. Although with his supreme cultivation base, neither poison nor filth nor dust could ever genuinely taint his person—a single passing breeze would disperse the moisture entirely—Zhou Hui nevertheless cleansed his hands with the spirit spring. Rather than drying them with the towel, he flashed a minor fire cantrip to bake them dry, lowered his sleeves, and accepted the towel.

Holding the towel, Zhou Hui first wiped the little black lamb's snout, cleaning away the long, gooey strands of drool trailing from its lips, before using the cloth to wipe the artifact he had just retrieved from the sheep's belly.

—"Master, I have other towels..."-Guan Ye watched Zhou Hui's actions and the little black sheep's snout with a deeply conflicted expression,-"Once it's soiled, you can just throw it away!"

—"Ying!" [I'm not dirty at all!]

The little black lamb bleated indignantly in protest. Once it adopted the persona of a sheep, it evidently forgot its human linguistic faculties.

Yet Guan Ye paid no further attention to the sheep, his eyes drawn completely to the artifact in Zhou Hui's palm.

It was an array disc.

Its design was a model Guan Ye had never encountered before—hardly surprising; in a world untouched by industrialization, the vast majority of goods were handcrafted. Not only did they vary from artisan to artisan, but even two identical pieces crafted by the same refiner using identical materials bore distinct variations.

In comparison, this array disc looked remarkably standardized.

The true problem lay in its materials.

—"Is this... a 'Dao Bone'?"-Guan Ye asked, suppressing a wave of visceral discomfort.

The tail-like spine protruding behind his back stood erect in instinctive vigilance.

That spine was also a Dao Bone.

Not merely his spine; the very instant Guan Ye had successfully refined the Immortal Binding Rope, every single bone throughout his entire skeleton had transformed into Dao Bone.

Yet Guan Ye himself possessed little conceptual grasp of the matter. The truth regarding the 'Dao Bone' had only been explained to him by Master following the examination. In terms of Guan Ye's personal experience, the primary change was that he could now command his spine with effortless fluidity.

It was not as though Guan Ye had been unable to control his spine previously; if he deliberately concentrated or experienced intense emotional spikes, the vertebrae would react slightly.

However, that spine had not originally belonged to Guan Ye. First and foremost, the dimensions were mismatched. Normally, Guan Ye felt a pronounced foreign-body sensation; during overcast and rainy weather, the junction where bone met skin ached noticeably. If he accidentally bumped it or moved the lower tailbone, the pain intensified, creating the unnerving sensation that it might peel away from his flesh at any second.

Yet since this spine had evolved into a "Dao Bone," Guan Ye could distinctly feel that it had become an inseparable part of his own body.

It no longer ached, nor did it feel alien; like his arms or legs, Guan Ye could control it with absolute ease, swishing and lashing it at will. He could even emulate Lu Shulong, using his tail—tailbone—to effortlessly coil around an object and deposit it anywhere within reach.

And compared to these mechanical perks, the greatest benefit of this tailbone was that Guan Ye's ability to absorb ambient spiritual energy had skyrocketed.

For the longest time, Guan Ye had been notoriously inept at siphoning environmental spiritual qi.

This was partially due to his crippled spirit roots; yet even before his spirit roots had been ruined, his impure multi-element roots had been ill-suited for cultivation. For an cultivator of his lackluster aptitude, absorbing spiritual energy was like trying to breathe through a nose clogged by a severe head cold; having his roots crippled had merely downgraded him from a mild cold to acute pneumonia.

With this Dao Bone, however, Guan Ye's 'nasal passages' cleared completely whenever he forged—his siphoning capability became gloriously unobstructed, creating a sensation of seamless harmony with heaven and earth. A breakthrough in his cultivation seemed imminent.

As for the exact timing of his breakthrough, it required Guan Ye to develop a deeper mastery over the Dao Bone's applications.

Naturally, Guan Ye had no intention of wasting time; in truth, the moment the examination concluded, he had been itching to enter secluded cultivation in the Artifact Refinement Workshop for several hundred days. It was only because Eldest Senior Brother had evidently caused another scene that appeared somewhat linked to him that he had followed along.

Even so, along the entire walk, Guan Ye had diligently followed Master's instructions, cycling his spiritual energy again and again to scour his newly formed Dao Bones and familiarize himself with their state.

It was precisely in this hyper-attuned state that the moment Guan Ye sensed the array disc, he immediately detected the striking similarity between its material and his own Dao Bone:

It was an innate sensation of autonomous spiritual respiration. Though the breathing cadence differed, it was undeniably respiring.

—"So it truly is a Dao Bone?"-Zhou Hui raised his palm, letting the array disc hover above his fingers,-"Although my initial impression suggested similarities to an ancient immortal corpse, it leans much closer to an authentic Dao Bone."

Hearing Zhou Hui's remark, Guan Ye grew slightly hesitant.

He worried his amateur assessment might be flawed, inadvertently skewing Zhou Hui's judgment:

—"Master, may I examine this array disc more closely?"-Guan Ye asked, reaching out to touch the disc.

Before Guan Ye could make contact, however, something clamped down sharply on his hand.

—"Ying-ying!"-the little black lamb bleated.

—"Speak human,"-Zhou Hui frowned.

—"Don't touch it! That is a True Immortal's possession! If your cultivation base is too low, the Heavenly Court will trace you!"-Yuwen Xuan finally remembered his linguistic faculties.

Zhou Hui cut him a sideways glance:

—"You seem remarkably well-informed."

—"I overheard it from that 'thief,'"-Yuwen Xuan answered with an impeccably sincere countenance.

Had he been in human form, his handsome visage and solemn tone could have easily deceived many. Yet coming from a tiny black lamb, the delivery felt painfully contrived.

Naturally, he was lying through his teeth.

His knowledge regarding the array disc stemmed from the primordial Taotie's past-life memories. That ancient Taotie had clashed and collaborated with True Immortals and demons countless times, even participating in the ancient God-Demon War; consequently, it possessed formidable familiarity with celestial implements.

Thus, when Yuwen Xuan cloaked himself in the Heavenly Robe and tailed Ren Ran into the treasure ship, he had swiftly located the array disc hidden in her vanity. After concocting his 'culinary recipe,' he had filched the disc, mimicked Ren Ran's voice to deliver it to Lin Qi, and deployed it as the bait to lure his prey step by step into the slaughterhouse.

Yuwen Xuan believed his scheme had been [flawless like heavenly garments](# "Literal CN: 天衣无缝 (tiānyī-wúfèng) — seamless celestial robe; completely airtight"). Yet even with a sheep's brain, why did it feel as though Master had already seen through everything?

He watched as Master let out a cold smirk, asking:

—"And where is this 'thief' now?"

—"No idea. If even you couldn't find him, Master, then once he's gone, he's gone!"-Yuwen Xuan replied on pure reflex.

Ferocious beasts were notorious for refusing to shed a tear until they saw the coffin.

Even if his human rationality faintly sensed that the jig was up, his ferocious beast instincts habitually lied as easily as breathing—let alone when no physical evidence remained!

—"Hmph,"-Zhou Hui scoffed coldly, resolving to discipline this wretched creature later.

He did not press further regarding the True Immortal's whereabouts, turning instead to verify the array disc's technical telemetry with Yuwen Xuan:

—"Is it safe for me to hold this?"

—"Completely safe. I used the celestial qi you acquired during your previous trip to the Heavenly Realm to tamper with it slightly. As long as the spiritual energy channeled into it is sufficiently colossal, the Heavenly Court will assume it is their own True Immortal operating it,"-Yuwen Xuan replied.

Especially since Lin Qi had already handled it.

Naturally, he deliberately omitted the fact that under these circumstances, the only individual truly doomed was Ren Ran. As an exiled convict, unauthorized distribution of her surveillance disciplinary disc to third parties—even fellow True Immortals—compounded her administrative guilt exponentially. Though given her current predicament, Ren Ran probably no longer cared.

Zhou Hui paid no heed to that aspect either, his focus drawn to a different absurdity:

—"Tampered with it? *You?!*"

—"Master, you look down on me! I know how to refine artifacts too, ying!"-the little black sheep protested indignantly.

—"If you dare call that artifact refinement..."-Zhou Hui's lips twitched slightly.

Nearby, Guan Ye wore an expression of acute professional insult, adamantly refusing to validate Yuwen Xuan's 'craftsmanship.'

Yet Yuwen Xuan genuinely did possess 'refinement' capabilities, and formidable ones at that—particularly concerning spatial Dharma treasures. The sole hitch was that his refinement apparatus was not a forge, but a cooking pot; and his methodology was not standard metallurgical technique, but dumping raw ingredients into broth and simmering them like a culinary banquet.

To any orthodox artifact refiner, his brand of 'refinement' was an intolerable affront.

Zhou Hui was currently more concerned that Yuwen Xuan's 'tampering' had inflicted irreversible structural damage upon the disc. Casting aside reservations, he lightly tapped the disc; instantly, the artifact disassembled into dozens of floating components, its interior runes unrolling into a dense, sprawling tapestry that made even Guan Ye's eyes spin with dizziness.

—"...What kind of unholy mess is this?!"-Zhou Hui skimmed the runic architecture, his complexion darkening by the second before glaring sharply at Yuwen Xuan,-"The core is strikingly elegant, yet the exterior is an incoherent disaster! Did you scribble all of this?!"

—"I didn't!"-Yuwen Xuan cried out in aggrieved defense,-"Master, do you truly believe I'm capable of writing that much text?! Even if I could, I'd be too lazy to write it!"

[You sound remarkably proud of that!](# "Zhou Hui's unspoken thought")

Both Zhou Hui and Guan Ye stared at the little black lamb with deeply conflicted expressions.

The little black sheep raised a cloven hoof, pointing toward a specific cluster of runes:

—"I only used celestial qi to alter this tiny section."

Guan Ye leaned in to inspect that specific segment with immense effort.

He struggled to parse it.

Guan Ye felt that just as Master had observed, the core of this array disc was exquisite, virtually devoid of flaws; at least at Guan Ye's current proficiency, he could find zero avenues for refinement.

In contrast, the outer runes were an absolute trainwreck—equally impossible to tamper with, but solely because they were so horrifyingly bloated and convoluted that one had no idea where to even begin. Crucially, these were clearly not the work of ordinary talisman masters or refiners; on the contrary, judging by the obscure, archaic script, the author(s)—given the vast stylistic discrepancies, it was clearly the work of multiple contributors—possessed profoundly encyclopedic runic knowledge, worthy of being hailed as grandmasters. Yet they suffered from the chronic flaw of traditional artisans: an incurable desire to show off! Where things could be streamlined, they wrote with absurd verbosity, terrified that outsiders might actually understand their work. Layering multiple disparate runes on top of one another created a catastrophic structural nightmare, making modifications nearly impossible. It was a minor miracle the disc functioned at all!

Amidst this chaotic pile of runic garbage, the section Yuwen Xuan had modified was shockingly elegant.

He had deployed merely two runes. Not only did they slot seamlessly into that bloated heap without disrupting original functions, but they flawlessly achieved the exact objective Yuwen Xuan desired.

—"You actually did this?"-Zhou Hui looked on with genuine disbelief.

Even more incredulous than when he had first heard Yuwen Xuan claimed to have modified the disc.

The little black lamb sharply detected Master's astonishment, proudly tilting its fuzzy little head:

—"Yes!"

—"How did you do it?"

—"Intuition."

—"Why did you choose these two specific runes?"

—"I guessed."

Hearing this, Zhou Hui couldn't resist rapping the fluffy head with his knuckles.

Yet following that knock, Guan Ye let out a rare, mildly sour murmur:

—"Eldest Senior Brother is always like this... arriving at answers through pure intuition that take the rest of us hours of grueling calculations to derive!"

The little black lamb, having initially attempted to shield its head with its stubby hooves, puffed up with instant arrogance upon hearing his junior brother's 'flattery' (Guan Ye: *No, I certainly didn't mean it that way*).

—"You and I operate on fundamentally different planes,"-the little black sheep boasted smugly.

Although Zhou Hui sorely wanted to deliver another crisp rap to this unruly disciple, a realization suddenly struck him that he had never considered before:

Was Yuwen Xuan's 'intuition' truly mere 'intuition'?

Back before mandatory exam papers were instituted, the phenomenon had been less obvious.

Whenever theoretical principles were involved, Yuwen Xuan had simply answered with complete ignorance, making instruction appear futile.

Now that exam papers were routine, Yuwen Xuan's scripts remained an utter disaster, frequently scoring zero. Yet unlike in the past, Yuwen Xuan would occasionally 'blindly guess' one or two correct answers—devoid of workings, devoid of derivation steps. When asked, his answer was invariably 'intuition.'

On one occasion, Zhou Hui had forgotten to cast a 'luck nullification' ward over the exam papers, and Yuwen Xuan had miraculously scored a perfect hundred through pure 'intuition.'

Zhou Hui had always assumed Yuwen Xuan was incapable of learning; yet viewing the situation now, perhaps it wasn't that he couldn't learn. After all, relying strictly on luck and intuition could never successfully modify an authentic Heavenly Court array disc.

It could only be said that a ferocious beast's brain operated on an entirely different architecture from a human's—much like computers running completely disparate operating systems. Operating under arcane, inscrutable rules, as long as sufficient data was fed into the machine, it could calculate the correct output through some bizarre, inexplicable shortcut—though the steps remained a mystery even to the beast itself. Because the underlying principles were so obscure, even orthodox magic systems chalked the result up to dumb luck.

[Ah, how fascinating... I really want to dissect it and see what's inside.](# "Zhou Hui's internal contemplation")

—"Ying? Master?"-the little black lamb bleated with sudden trepidation.

A sudden, bone-chilling shiver ran down its spine. Looking up into Master's eyes, it sensed a terrifying crisis—infinitely more perilous than when Master had caught it red-handed moments prior!

Roused by that bleat, Zhou Hui finally recalled that this was his own disciple, not an experimental subject to be dismantled willy-nilly.

Speaking of dismantling...

—"You didn't tamper solely with this array disc, did you?"-Zhou Hui scrutinized the little sheep, his voice softening into an unnervingly gentle cadence,-"There is also the mountain-protecting grand array... To think I was unaware that you had dismantled it and refined its components into your internal viscera?"

Hearing this, the little black lamb trembled even more violently, yet stubbornly attempted to play dumb:

—"Master, what are you talking about? I know nothing!"

It would never confess!

Although Master had already designated Second Senior Brother to inherit the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Second Senior Brother was far too frail, and his memories and powers were essentially outsourced—he couldn't protect the sect at all!

Thus, Yuwen Xuan had recently been clandestinely refining the entire sect, plotting to convert the Heavenly Spirit Sect into an extension of his stomach space. Should villainous intruders arrive in the future, he could eat whoever he pleased!

As for the mountain-protecting grand array, that had merely been the preliminary phase of his refinement, far from complete...

Wait a moment—Master wasn't planning on ripping the refined components out of his flesh, was he?! That would hurt like hell!

The little black lamb scrambled backward several paces.

Yet Zhou Hui's hands were lightning-fast, scooping the lamb into his arms and stroking its fleece with alarming gentleness:

—"Since you claim you didn't, then you didn't,"-Zhou Hui smiled warmly,-"Next, I plan to decipher and reverse-engineer this array disc. It is quite intriguing; a portion of its magic remains tethered to the Heavenly Court. Perhaps I might extract critical intelligence from it."

—"...As long as you are happy, Master,"-the little black lamb shivered beneath Master's caressing palm.

It loved being petted by Master; the dilemma was that the timing was horribly wrong!

It had committed so many 'crimes' and adamantly refused to confess! Under normal circumstances, Master should never be petting it with such angelic tenderness! Not knowing what Master was scheming was sheer psychological torture!

In contrast to the little black sheep's ungrateful panic, Guan Ye was brimming with eager enthusiasm:

—"Master, when you disassemble the array disc, may I serve as your assistant? Or observe and study from the side?"

Zhou Hui rarely denied his disciples' desire for academic advancement, yet on this occasion he shook his head:

—"No. The Immortal Arts Assembly is imminent. Regarding the tournament procedures we discussed previously, I plan to network the event with the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree's academic system. If I am to decipher this array disc, that assignment must fall upon your shoulders. Furthermore, your Dao Bone has only just coalesced; merely watching me work will not yield substantial breakthroughs. You must get your hands dirty to make genuine progress."

—"Yes, Master,"-Guan Ye naturally had zero objections.

Gazing upon the array disc in contemplation, Zhou Hui added:

—"However, your Eldest Senior Brother gave me an intriguing idea: the functionality of the mountain-protecting grand array and this array disc could very well be incorporated into the tournament trials. When the time comes, Ye-zi, come retrieve the relevant components from me. Additionally, I shall instruct Ruwang to assist you; if you encounter difficulties, do not hesitate to consult him."

—"Understood..."

—"Wait a minute, Master! Why call in an outsider when you have me?!"-the little black sheep piped up, perking its stubby tail,-"Running the Immortal Arts Assembly requires more than raw technical skill! It demands supreme interpersonal charisma! Little Seventh is still down in the Southern Domain, isn't he?! Who else could possibly handle it besides me?!"

The current, gentle Master terrified the Taotie; slipping away was by far the wisest move!

Besides, the Immortal Arts Assembly was a monumental undertaking long in preparation. Executing it with flying colors would make it far harder for Master to settle old scores later!

The little black sheep plotted with a belly full of schemes.

Yet Zhou Hui smiled at it with boundless affection:

—"Sending you to the Immortal Arts Assembly would be an utter waste of talent. Seeing as you know so remarkably much about this array disc, I have already decided: you shall serve as my apprentice and assistant during this disassembly!"

—"Assistant? Me?!"

—"Indeed. Assistant and apprentice. I shall dismantle every single rune of this array disc and explain each one to you in exhaustive detail,"-Zhou Hui smiled warmly at the little sheep. While he couldn't dissect this disciple, conducting an experiment to map the operational architecture of a ferocious beast's brain and plumb its academic potential was entirely fair game,-"You must study diligently. When we conclude, there will be a comprehensive examination."

Study?! Examination?!

The little black lamb wore an expression as though struck by apocalyptic lightning:

—"You might as well hang me from the tree!"

—"You are already hanging from the tree, are you not? Although it is merely a portion, is that portion not still up there?"-the temperature in Zhou Hui's voice plummeted to subzero,-"Splitting off a fragment without my express permission... I think leaving that half hanging up there indefinitely sounds like an excellent arrangement."

...Now *this* was their normal Master.

The little black lamb breathed a quiet sigh of relief internally, yet struggled obstinately:

—"Ying... B-But can you truly entrust the entire Immortal Arts Assembly to Fourth Junior Brother? Even if you have faith in him, Fourth Junior Brother cannot handle it! Is this punishing me, or punishing Fourth Junior Brother?!"

[I don't care about that at all...](# "Guan Ye's internal thought")

Guan Ye sorely wanted to say those words aloud, yet the mere thought of handling hospitality and public relations for the Immortal Arts Assembly made his scalp tingle with acute dread.

Particularly having just passed the qualification examination—perhaps because of his newfound fame, a massive swarm of cultivators had mobbed him moments ago, desperate to converse. Merely recalling that suffocating scene made Guan Ye feel claustrophobic.

Reluctant though he was to admit it, he genuinely wished to avoid hospitality duties at all costs.

—"You needn't worry about that,"-Zhou Hui scoffed coldly, his gaze suddenly pivoting toward a forgotten figure lurking in the corner,-"Ah-Ran, you shall oversee the areas where your Fourth Junior Brother falls short."

—"Eh? Me?!"-Ran Deng shuddered violently, protesting on pure reflex,-"B-But what Fourth Junior Brother isn't good at, I'm not good at either..."

Ran Deng's social anxiety was only marginally better than Guan Ye's—just a tiny sliver better; otherwise, she wouldn't have repeatedly sought out her unreliable Eldest Senior Brother for assistance.

Zhou Hui fixed his gaze upon her, wearing a serene, gentle smile:

—"Or would you prefer to be hung from the peach tree alongside your Eldest Senior Brother?"

Ah... it was punishment! She hadn't escaped retribution after all!

—"I'll do it. I will do it,"-Ran Deng conceded with utterly dead eyes.
