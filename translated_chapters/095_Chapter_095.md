# Chapter 95: [Day Off](<# "Literal CN: 休息日 (xiūxirì) — day off / rest day; chapter title">) (第95章 休息日)

[—"Master Untiring, regarding the True Immortal..."](<# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingyu">)

Qingyang Qingyu had rushed back to the Heavenly Spirit Sect in a frantic hurry.

She hopped down from her flying sword, traversed the courtyard, and stood at the entrance of the immortal's cave dwelling. Catching sight of that jade-white figure, she had been just about to ask about the "True Immortal Blood project" her ancestor was researching. Yet three pairs of eyes turning toward her made her swallow the remaining words right back into her stomach.

The first pair was naturally the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself.

His gaze, during examinations and questioning, could indeed be terrifyingly severe, making one dare not meet his eyes. Yet in ordinary moments, it radiated an infectious, gentle warmth that compelled whoever saw it to relax their guard, or even pour out their deepest thoughts.

Having stayed in the Heavenly Spirit Sect for quite some time, Qingyu was no longer as terrified of the Untiring Immortal Venerable as she had been upon their first meeting.

She even dared to shout and holler at the Untiring Immortal Venerable's doorstep.

So right now, she naturally hadn't swallowed her words because of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's gaze.

What made her lose her voice were the other two pairs.

One pair was pitch-black like deep ink, yet held a soul-stirring, breathtaking radiance. At first glance, it made one want to dive headlong into it like a moth to a flame, yet on second glance, one would discover the unmasked scrutiny and mockery lurking within, snapping a person into ice-cold sobriety in an instant.

This was Qu Qianxing's gaze.

Every time Qingyang Qingyu met this peerless beauty's eyes, her head felt as though it had been struck by a wooden club, rapidly jolting her awake from her emotional turbulence:

[Wait... what did I just come rushing over here for?]

[To accuse Master Untiring of not disclosing the True Immortal Blood to me?]

[I was the one who was so resistant to studying at the Heavenly Spirit Sect in the first place, which was why I never properly inquired with Ancestor!]

[I was also the one who chose to study the 'Secret Realm Guidance System'—and that truly is an immensely useful field of scholarship!]

[To still covet knowledge related to the 'True Immortal' on top of that... isn't that being far too greedy?]

Such feelings instantly overpowered her earlier urgency, leaving Qingyang Qingyu finding it awkward to press the question.

Yet what made Qingyang Qingyu abandon the thought entirely was that final pair of eyes.

It was a pair of pupils with bizarre horizontal slits. However, these eyes were enormous, round, and dewy-moist—thoroughly adorable. Especially set upon the face of a fluffy little black lamb, it was even cuter.

Qingyang Qingyu knew of this little black lamb.

It had been [hung from the branches](<# "Literal CN: 挂在树枝上 (guà zài shùzhī shàng) — Yuwen Xuan's punishment for losing bets and failing exams, suspended from the mountain gate peach tree">) at the mountain gate for over a month!

For some inexplicable reason, disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect loved gathering around to watch it. Later on, after everyone discovered that this sheep had hung there for five or six days without eating a bite and still hadn't died, all kinds of rumors sprouted. Some believed it was a demon beast that had gambled alongside Eldest Senior Brother; some believed it was the mortal incarnation of the [God of Failing Exams](<# "Literal CN: 考试不及格之神 (kǎoshì bù jígé zhī shén) — superstitious moniker given to the lamb by disciples fearful of flunking">); others believed it was a supreme sacrificial offering dedicated to the God of Examinations. Speculation ran wild without consensus, but there was never any shortage of superstition:

Some disciples had gone so far as to set up incense burners beneath the tree and begun bowing in worship.

Nobody knew what they were praying for or what results came of it, but black smoke billowed in thick clouds. Whether due to being smoked out by the heavy fumes or not, the lamb's fleece seemed to grow even blacker, and its eyes appeared even more tearful and dewy.

Now, this very lamb was staring at Qingyang Qingyu with those teary, dewy eyes.

Yet Qingyu felt her scalp tingle with numbness.

Looking at this cute little lamb, she felt none of the tender affection the other female disciples did; she sensed only an uncanny terror, harboring the ominous premonition that if she said one superfluous word right now, she would be completely finished.

Qingyu dutifully clamped her mouth shut, shuffling her feet to the side to steer clear of the little black lamb's malice-laden glare.

Of course, this exchange of glances had transpired in a mere flash of sparks.

Zhou Hui had not even noticed that his disciples could communicate so much through eye contact alone. Seeing Qingyang Qingyu look as if she wanted to say something only to shut her mouth upon seeing Qu Qianxing, he sensed nothing amiss, regarding it as entirely normal:

[My second disciple is simply too handsome. People forgetting their lines before they get used to his [critical face-shot](<# "Literal CN: 美颜暴击 (měiyán bàojī) — modern Chinese internet slang: an overwhelming aesthetic critical hit from someone's extreme beauty">) is only natural.]

Zhou Hui had never paid attention to what actually struck others dumb about Qu Qianxing.

Mainly because Qu Qianxing never wore that kind of expression in front of him.

At this moment, Zhou Hui assumed Qingyu had simply been dazzled by Qu Qianxing's appearance.

—"Qingyu, is something the matter?"-Zhou Hui asked with a smile.

—"I..."-Qingyu parted her lips, staring at Qu Qianxing and that lamb, not daring to utter the words 'Master, I want to learn everything!' in front of these two.

Only as she racked her brain for an alternative topic did she notice something else:
—"Your attire today is...?"-she asked.

—"Oh, I was just preparing to head out,"-Zhou Hui said, smoothing the hem of his robe.-"The place we are going will be quite crowded, so I would rather not attract too much attention."

In keeping with this intention, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had not donned his usual white robes patterned with subtle silver embroidery, but had instead worn a plain gray Daoist robe. Qu Qianxing was similarly dressed in unassuming garb.

However, both of their faces and auras were so conspicuous that even after changing clothes, the first thing Qingyang Qingyu noticed was still their faces.

[Does this disguise serve any purpose whatsoever?]

Naturally, Qingyang Qingyu wasn't tactless enough to voice such a question aloud. She merely asked out of curiosity:
—"It is truly rare to see Master leave the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

The Untiring Immortal Venerable's reluctance to step outdoors was renowned.

In the past, he secluded himself for cultivation, so much so that even high-tier cultivator gatherings could rarely invite him. Recently, the Immortal Venerable seemed to have altered his disposition, growing fond of hosting grand events, yet those had mostly taken place on Mount Tianling.

Even his recent trip accompanying Qingyu to her clan's spirit palace had been strictly for pedagogical purposes.

This time, however, Zhou Hui seemed to be heading out simply to stroll about:
—"The preliminary rounds of the Immortals' Gathering Tournament are about to commence,"-Zhou Hui said.-"I intend to visit Cloud City to take a look. Would you like to come along?"

[So the preliminary rounds of the Immortals' Gathering Tournament are finally ready?]

Qingyang Qingyu came to a sudden realization.

However, the "preliminaries" had nothing to do with elite disciples like herself.

After all, to ensure the diversity of participants—or rather, to save face for certain sects so they wouldn't miss out on participating entirely—every sect that registered was allotted a certain quota of participant slots.

Whether through internal sect competitions or selection by elders, their disciples could bypass the preliminary screening and proceed straight to the regional tournaments.

Beyond that, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had constructed massive combat arenas in major immortal cities and cultivator gathering hubs. Those arenas also held a certain quota of slots, but those slots were directly controlled by the Heavenly Spirit Sect, completely free of shady backroom deals.

Whether rogue cultivators, sect disciples, demon beasts, or mortals, anyone could step onto the arena to issue challenges. Depending on the total number of challengers, slots were awarded proportionally to the top contenders.

Following the extensive promotion in the preceding period, nearly the entire Central Domain was buzzing about the Immortals' Gathering Tournament.

For a time, everyone was signing up, making it practically a realm-wide tournament for all cultivators across the Central Domain.

Evidently, the liveliest spectacles of the preliminaries were precisely these open arena matches.

Among them, Cloud City, as the immortal city with the densest concentration of cultivators, would clearly see the fiercest competition for tournament slots.

No wonder the Untiring Immortal Venerable wished to see it for himself.

[Still... is he taking that sheep along too?]

Qingyang Qingyu wondered in bewilderment.

She cast another glance at the little black lamb trotting around Zhou Hui's feet, even pawing at Zhou Hui's robe hem with its hooves in an attempt to clamber up Zhou Hui's thigh. In the end, she dared not ask what on earth was going on with this sheep.

Naturally, Qingyang Qingyu wouldn't pass up this rare opportunity to go on an outing with the Immortal Venerable—rarely were his other (annoying) disciples absent; perhaps along the way, she might find an opening to ask about the matters weighing on her mind? Ignoring the chilling glare Qu Qianxing shot her way, Qingyu brightened up:

—"The Immortals' Gathering Tournament?!"-Qingyu's eyes sparkled as she spoke in the tone most guaranteed to please an elder.-"Wow, I've been looking forward to it for ages! Please take me along, Master!"

*

Cloud City was indeed bustling.

Almost the entire city's cultivators had turned out in full force. Because there were simply too many applicants, the arena at the immortal bazaar hadn't even begun matches before the sheer volume of people flocking over to scout the battlefield (and gawk at the commotion) caused several shutdowns due to street congestion.

For this reason, Cloud City had simply abandoned using the downtown district entirely.

Out in the sky beyond the city walls, they had layered clouds into tiers of cloud platforms, stacking all the way from the city gates up to the entrance of Maiden Rosy Cloud's Cloud Pool. A competitor had to fight their way through one hundred and eight cloud platforms to earn the title of Cloud City's Strongest.

Naturally, that was something that would only take place during the finals of Cloud City's regional tournament.

During the preliminary phase, competitors were still in the standard stage of challenging and defending the arenas. Yet even before the matches officially began, some cultivators couldn't wait any longer and scrambled onto the stages to duel. Cultivators were constantly knocked flying off the cloud platforms, forcing Cloud City to weave an array of soft, fluffy cloud-beds drifting below to catch anyone sent hurtling down. The cloud-bed would instantly morph into a cage upon impact, publicly putting that unlucky fellow on display for one [ke](<# "A traditional Chinese unit of time roughly equivalent to 15 minutes">), hoping to deter reckless troublemakers—though the effect was rather negligible.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable and his disciples descended near one of the cloud platforms.

[This is probably an arena for the Nascent Soul division?]-Qingyu thought as she watched several Nascent Soul cultivators clashing above.

Although no one in the Untiring Immortal Venerable's entourage happened to be at this cultivation stage, it didn't matter in the slightest. The Immortal Venerable certainly hadn't come to enter anyone into the tournament; he was purely here to enjoy the spectacle.

Thus, the three people and one sheep landed on the promenade near the stage.

This promenade wound spirally upward around the cloud platform.

The promenade was divided into two levels. The lower level was livelier, packed with shops and vendor stalls where people could stroll at will, while the upper level was quieter, equipped with tea seating. Regardless of the level, both faced the arena, allowing spectators to watch the battles unfold at any moment. When the blood rushed to one's head, one could even vault over the railing and leap straight onto the platform to become a challenger.

Carrying the little lamb in his arms, Zhou Hui settled near the railing with his disciples.

Zhou Hui inspected the stage with a critical eye and gave a slight nod, appearing reasonably satisfied.

The little lamb, meanwhile, craned its neck, eyes glittering as it gazed toward the vendor stalls, looking as though it desperately wanted to leap from Master's arms and charge over, only to be firmly held down by Zhou Hui.

Qingyang Qingyu couldn't help but look toward the vendor stalls as well, discovering pills, magical artifacts, and even [candied hawthorn skewers](<# "Literal CN: 糖葫芦 (tánghúlu) — traditional candied hawthorn skewers on a bamboo stick, popular mortal street treat">)—following the Immortals' Gathering Tournament, such mortal delicacies seemed to have experienced a renaissance across the cultivation world—[Wait, can a sheep even eat this?]

Just as Qingyu was pondering this, she saw Qu Qianxing walk over and purchase two candied hawthorn skewers.

Only then did Qingyu realize that nobody expressed astonishment at Qu Qianxing's face, nor did anyone pay attention to their group. Evidently, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had cast an obscuration spell.

[Then what on earth was the point of changing into plain clothes...?]

[—"Here."](<# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing">)

At that moment, Qu Qianxing held something out.

Lost in thought, Qingyang Qingyu instinctively took it, only to realize it was one of the candied hawthorn skewers Qu Qianxing had just bought.

Qingyu froze for a second, then flushed and voiced her thanks.

She felt Qu Qianxing wasn't quite as detestable after all.

Meanwhile, Qu Qianxing carried the other skewer over to the Immortal Venerable. Just as Qingyu assumed he was going to offer it to the Immortal Venerable—and was wondering in disbelief if the Immortal Venerable would actually eat such a thing—she saw Qu Qianxing hold the skewer right up to the little black lamb's snout.

[...So I'm receiving the exact same treatment as a sheep?]

Qingyu suddenly didn't want the candied hawthorn skewer anymore.

Qu Qianxing asked:
—"Master, can this sheep eat can—ah, a step too late,"-Qu Qianxing asked.

Just as Qu Qianxing was about to inquire, he saw that adorable little lamb stretch its maw wide open to a thoroughly un-adorable degree, gulping down all four candied hawthorns off the bamboo skewer in a single bite.

—"It doesn't matter. This fellow can eat anything,"-Zhou Hui replied.

Of course, eating all that would only delay his return to human form.

Zhou Hui cast a cool look at his foolish eldest disciple contentedly chewing on the hawthorns:
—"Still, don't spoil him too much."

—"Pfft,"-Qu Qianxing chuckled.

That smile shone so brightly in the crowd it was as though a spotlight had illuminated him, though unfortunately none of the surrounding bystanders could truly perceive it.

—"Back then regarding Eldest Senior Brother, Master warned me in the exact same words!"-Qu Qianxing laughed.

The little black lamb's chewing instantly ground to a halt.

Its face froze, staring wide-eyed at Qu Qianxing with a comical expression.

Zhou Hui stroked the fleece on its back and shot Qu Qianxing a profound look, appearing to simply reminisce about the past:
—"You overfed him far too much back then,"-Zhou Hui remarked.-"Looking terrified that Xuan'er might starve to death."

—"Because back then, I thought Eldest Senior Brother suffered from some mad mania... Actually, even now I wonder how on earth he could survive outside the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Qu Qianxing sighed, patting the little black lamb's head.-"In any case, this lamb is far cuter than Eldest Senior Brother."

Listening from the side, Qingyu was utterly dumbfounded.

[The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Brother is a [mentally impaired simpleton](<# "Literal CN: 痴儿 (chī'ér) — mentally challenged simpleton / fool">)?!]

No wonder she had never laid eyes on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Brother since arriving! And the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect clearly looked to Second Senior Brother as their sole leader, speaking of Eldest Senior Brother without much reverence. So this was the truth?!

Still, with a half-step immortal like the Untiring Immortal Venerable around, ordinary congenital deficiencies could surely be cured. For the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Brother to show such poor results, it had to be an incurable condition!

Qingyu immediately classified this matter as a taboo subject within the Heavenly Spirit Sect. As an exchange student, she resolved to hear less and ask less about Yuwen Xuan in the future, lest she touch upon a sore nerve.

—"Baaah!"-the little black lamb bleated in protest.

Unaware of what misunderstandings the "outsider" had concocted, merely hearing his junior brother evaluate him like this made the little black lamb utter a bleat of protest.

It gulped down the hawthorns and sugar in its mouth in one swallow, spun around, and pointed its [marshmallow-like rump](<# "Literal CN: 棉花糖似的屁股 (miánhuātáng shì de pìgu) — fluffy white-tail sheep rump comparison">) straight at Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing couldn't care less.

Having finished feeding (teasing) the lamb, Qu Qianxing wore a deeply satisfied expression. Glancing around, he said:
—"Master, it is too crowded down here. It is unsuited for a pampered young lady like Junior Sister Qingyu. Let us find a tea booth to sit down,"-Qu Qianxing suggested.

[You're just using me as an excuse because you don't want your Master standing in the crowd!]

Qingyu saw through it with absolute clarity.

Even so, she agreed with Qu Qianxing's suggestion.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable stood amidst the crowd like pure moonlight. Though the throng failed to notice him, Qingyu, watching from the side, was perpetually worried that moonlight might shine too brilliantly and drive some of the more perceptive individuals into madness.

—"Master Untiring, let us head up to the second floor to take a look!"-Qingyu chimed in.

With both speaking disciples making the request, Zhou Hui naturally would not refuse them.

Although he still wished to look around the ground floor a bit more, he nodded:
—"Very well, to the second floor... However, once we are up there, do not strike up conversations with people at random. I have no desire to disturb them,"-Zhou Hui instructed.

[Them?]

Qingyu wondered curiously.

Yet the moment she reached the second floor, she understood what the Immortal Venerable meant.

A group of people was already gathered here, though whether they wanted to be conspicuous or inconspicuous was hard to say.

To say they "wanted to be conspicuous," it was because they had commandeered several tables in a private booking posture, falling just short of driving away everyone nearby—though doing so would disrupt the tournament, and the Untiring Immortal Venerable had explicitly stated that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's arena would grant privileges to no one; daring to do so would be an open provocation to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, so they couldn't quite go that far.

To say they "wanted to be inconspicuous," it was because at the center of this group sat a handsome cultivator with white hair and white robes—at first glance, his demeanor was actually somewhat similar to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's. Yet compared to the Immortal Venerable's gentle warmth, this person's disposition was considerably aloof and cold, looking rather unapproachable. Such a person ought to have been exceedingly eye-catching, yet while several people glanced toward the group with dissatisfaction, none gave this white-haired cultivator a second look. Evidently, like Zhou Hui, he had cast a spell to conceal his appearance and cultivation, preventing others from noticing his presence.

Nevertheless, Qingyang Qingyu could determine that this person was definitely a high-tier cultivator, and could even ascertain that he was an eminent figure from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court. After all, only they would adhere to a set of "ancient rites" that even Eternal Villa no longer observed.

[Are big shots' '[incognito tours](<# "Literal CN: 微服私访 (wēifú sīfǎng) — emperor or high official traveling in plain clothes to inspect the realm secretly">)' nowadays always conducted in such an awkward, contorted fashion?]

Qingyang Qingyu thought cynically to herself.

Eternal Villa had never cared for the fellows from the Ceremony Court, as they were constantly questioning Eternal Villa's orthodox legitimacy.

Qu Qianxing naturally noticed this group as well. Turning directly to Zhou Hui, he asked:
—"Master, who are these people...?"-Qu Qianxing asked directly.

[Is it really wise to discuss them so openly? Didn't Master just say not to strike up conversation with them?]

Qingyu looked toward Qu Qianxing.

Zhou Hui, however, replied nonchalantly:
—"I do not know them. But judging by his cultivation, he is at the Late Mahayana stage. Judging by his scent, he seems somewhat reminiscent of White Jade Capital,"-Zhou Hui answered nonchalantly.

Only then did Qingyu realize:

The Untiring Immortal Venerable had said "do not strike up conversations"; "discussing them behind their backs" was evidently perfectly fine.

In fact, let alone "discussing them behind their backs," even when the Immortal Venerable led them over and—finding no vacant tables—simply sat down right inside the cleared zone of the other party and ordered a pot of tea, the other side showed zero reaction whatsoever. Clearly, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had completely erased their presence from the other party's cognitive perception. As long as their side did not actively step forward to provoke them, the other side would never "see" them.

—Even the tea waiter's perception of their presence had been erased in that fleeting instant.

A chill ran down the back of Qingyang Qingyu's neck as she watched.

The Immortal Venerable had stated it himself: the other party was a Late Mahayana cultivator! Could even a Mahayana's senses be veiled to such a terrifying degree?!

Qingyu suddenly remembered something her ancestor had once said:

> "In the cultivation world, cultivation differences within the same realm are not vast. Even if research directions differ and there are distinctions between who excels in combat and who does not, there is no insurmountable chasm between one Mahayana and another.
> 
> "The only exception is a single realm: Tribulation Transcendence.
> 
> "Tribulation Transcendence is Tribulation Transcendence because cultivators have no further stages above Mahayana. Yet ascension is not determined purely by cultivation base. The end result is that the strongest Tribulation Transcendence might be roughly comparable to a True Immortal. For instance...
> 
> "The Untiring Immortal Venerable's might could very well rival a True Immortal."

[Ancestor... is it truly *just* rivaling?]

[That Late Mahayana over there cannot even sense the Immortal Venerable in the slightest!]

Qingyu thought with deep uncertainty.

Seeing that their conversation would not alert the other party, she shared her deduction with the Untiring Immortal Venerable:
—"Judging from their posture when sipping tea, they should be people from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court,"-she shared her deduction with Zhou Hui.

—"Speaking of which, didn't something like that happen?"-Qu Qianxing said, sitting just a few seats away from the other party as he unreservedly scrutinized them.-"Because Eldest Senior Brother provoked their emissary, the Heavenly Dao Alliance prepared to dispatch Lord Siwu from the Ceremony Court. Could this be him?"

Hearing this, the little black lamb's ears twitched, poking half of its little head above the table to peer toward the other party.

—"Lord Siwu?"-Qingyu also boldly looked toward that Late Mahayana cultivator, carefully searching her mental archive. Although she had never met him in person, as an heir of Eternal Villa, she had been made to study dossiers on key figures from major factions, at least needing to know their appearances to avoid committing social blunders outside:—"Now that you mention it, white hair and white robes... Lord Siwu does seem to match this appearance."

—"Hmph,"-Qu Qianxing scoffed.

He was clearly dissatisfied by the fact that this fellow had [copied his Master's aesthetic](<# "Literal CN: 撞形象 (zhuàng xíngxiàng) — aesthetic/character design clash; Qu Qianxing's indignation at another cultivator sporting white hair and white robes like Zhou Hui">).

—"What does he mean by coming to the Central Domain and heading here first instead of visiting our Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-Qu Qianxing muttered.

—"Perhaps he heard news of the Immortals' Gathering Tournament and came to watch the commotion?"-Zhou Hui remarked, suspecting nothing.

He had no particular thoughts about this fellow.

The reason he was unwilling to let the other party sense him was simply because he had come out today to [steal half a day of leisure](<# "Literal CN: 偷得浮生半日闲 — stealing half a day of leisure from this floating life; Zhou Hui just wanting a relaxed day off">). Encountering high-tier cultivators of comparable cultivation, especially ones from the Northern Domain, would inevitably entail polite social formalities, which Zhou Hui found immensely tedious.

Especially after confirming this person was an incoming "guest" from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court destined for the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Zhou Hui found it even more bothersome. He had no wish to cut his outing short, nor did he want to go sightseeing with a stranger, so he deepened the concealment barriers around them by several layers.

Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu exchanged a glance, offering no comment on their Master's words.

Clearly, whichever route one took, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was much closer to Fengqing City; there was absolutely no reason to take a detour to Cloud City first.

Still, since Master didn't care, it was not their place to say anything.

[When this Lord Siwu arrives at the Heavenly Spirit Sect later, I must have everyone stay sharp... He feels like trouble. We cannot give him any excuse to find fault,]-Qu Qianxing calculated in his mind.

Sensing someone watching him, he turned his head and discovered it was the little black lamb in Master's arms.

[Right,]-Qu Qianxing added as an afterthought:—[Since the Heavenly Dao Alliance dispatched another elder solely because they failed to deliver an adequate [academic presentation](<# "Literal CN: 做报告 (zuò bàogào) — scheduling an academic thesis presentation; Qu Qianxing plotting to subject Lord Siwu to the same grueling academic defense as Su Xin">), we might as well arrange a presentation for this elder too. We cannot favor one over the other.]

He proceeded to scheme what pretext to use to make the elder present a paper, as well as the hospitality standards and itinerary.

Meanwhile, Lord Siwu, sitting nearby, felt an inexplicable chill run down his spine.

[Is some scoundrel plotting against me?!]

He thought suspiciously. Just as he was about to give instructions to his guards, a deafening roar of cheers erupted from below, cutting his words short.

The preliminary rounds of the Immortals' Gathering Tournament had begun.
