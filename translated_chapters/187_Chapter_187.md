# Chapter 187: Invoking the Deities

[—"Aaaargh! Guan Ye, that absolute bastard!"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

Upon learning that Guan Ye had obtained Master's permission to undertake the construction of the large-scale teleportation array, on this very night at [the third quarter of the Ox Hour](# "Literal CN: 丑时三刻 (chǒushí sānkè) — approximately 2:45 AM, the dead of night when all vegetation sleeps"), a terrifying howl like that of a wolf echoed from the direction of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate.

Those terrible shrieks caused even the children burning the midnight oil to shrink deeper beneath their blankets, none daring to cast a single glance toward the mountain gate.

Consequently, they failed to discover that the one letting loose these bloodcurdling screams was neither demon nor vengeful ghost, nor was it some disciple heartbroken and jealous in romance, but rather their usually dignified, elegant (too lazy to move an inch) Eldest Senior Sister, Ran Deng.

Dressed in white robes with her hair hanging loose, pale from deficient qi and blood and bearing dark circles under her eyes, Ran Deng was in a frail, bedraggled state. Yet crimson firelight flickered and flared around her body. She stood beneath the ancient peach tree like a vengeful female ghost, lacking only hammering nails into a [voodoo curse doll](# "Literal CN: 钉小人 (dìng xiǎorén) — nailing paper effigies of enemies to a tree trunk, a traditional hexing practice") on the trunk.

—"That bastard, bastard, bastard!"-Ran Deng cursed, though hampered by an excessively refined upbringing and a severely deficient vocabulary of insults, her offensive power was pitifully lacking, leaving only pure grievance overflowing.-"It's fine if he wants to perform in front of Master! But why does he have to drag me into the water every! single! time?! The year before last, last year too—especially last year! What was the point of arbitrarily installing running water pipes, leaving me stuck drafting the sect's plumbing renovation blueprints to this very day without finishing! This year is even more infuriating! Master already said he would keep him by his side, so what is he still [grinding](# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn) — short for 内卷 (neijuǎn, involution): hyper-competitive, exhausting overworking") for?! What large-scale teleportation array?! Does he think I'm too idle? Or does he think our Array Pavilion is too idle?! Our internal sect construction is already suffocating enough as it is—how does he have the nerve to drag us into the mud? Their Artifact Refinement Hall has no time, so we must be rolling in free time, is that it?!"

Indeed, the target of Ran Deng's burning resentment was none other than the "large-scale teleportation array construction" Guan Ye had proposed.

After all, with a thing like a "teleportation array," anyone can tell just from the name that an array is, at its core, an *array*.

Therefore, when it came to actual construction, it had very little to do with Guan Ye's Artifact Refinement Hall; rather, it required a massive workforce of array-specialized cultivators to do the grunt work. Naturally, the burden landed squarely upon Ran Deng's head.

Ran Deng was already half-dead from refining her soul-fire. Upon hearing this news, she had half a mind to drag Guan Ye straight down to hell with her.

Unfortunately, Guan Ye had been kept right at Zhou Hui's side the entire time. Ran Deng couldn't even scold him to his face—she could hardly protest in front of Master that they shouldn't build a large-scale teleportation array, could she?—and was left with no choice but to sneak out in the middle of the night to "weep in secret."

It was just that her incessant, nagging blubbering was truly too grating, to the point where even Yuwen Xuan, hung up in the peach tree, could no longer bear to listen.

—"If you want to weep, go cry at the head of Old Four's bed. What does coming to my turf have to do with anything?"-Yuwen Xuan said, letting out a yawn.

—"This is the mountain gate, a public area! Just because you've hung here so long, you really take this for your bedroom?!"-Ran Deng sobbed, glaring resentfully at Yuwen Xuan to vent her misplaced anger.-"Don't you usually guard against the rest of us like thieves? If anyone so much as brushes against Master's sleeve, you can squabble for half a day. Why are you playing blind when it comes to this alone?!"

—"Old Four is different from the rest of you. He's an upright, serious guy whose head is packed with rigid, boring trivia. He doesn't crave Master's hugs, nor does he try to [cuddle up to Master](# "Literal CN: 贴贴 (tiētiē) — modern ACG/internet slang for being physically affectionate, clingy, or snuggling up close"). Why would I care what he does?"-Yuwen Xuan scoffed dismissively.-"Besides, what do I stand to gain by fighting over this? Follow Master around listening to his lectures while forbidden from sleeping, or take Old Four's place to build that godforsaken teleportation array? Neither option is worth the trouble!"

—"Then do I deserve to be dragged into this mess?! Wuwuwu..."-Ran Deng hugged the peach tree and wailed.

[So your grand idea is to drag *me* into it?]

Yuwen Xuan rolled his eyes.

He understood now: Old Four had made Youngest Junior Sister (crossed out) *Former* Youngest Junior Sister unhappy, so the Former Youngest Junior Sister had come to make him unhappy.

Sure enough, junior brothers and sisters were the most troublesome creatures in existence—he'd be better off swallowing them whole, one by one.

Dark thoughts swirled through Yuwen Xuan's mind, yet out loud he coaxed her nonetheless:

—"Although it's quite a hassle, it's not like you strictly have to do the heavy lifting yourself. Didn't Master teach you that if you truly detest doing something, you can just delegate it to others?"-Yuwen Xuan said.

—"Master told me that in private! How do you know?"-Ran Deng asked in astonishment.

However, she had no intention of interrogating him on the details. The Heavenly Spirit Sect held few secrets from Yuwen Xuan; even if he didn't know at the moment, he was bound to find out sooner or later.

Wiping the corners of her eyes, Ran Deng sniffled:

—"And who says I haven't recruited others? Not only did I conscript this year's incoming freshmen, but every disciple in the Array Pavilion has been mobilized! I even exhausted all my personal connections, reaching out to array masters who share close ties with our Ran Clan."-Ran Deng said.

[—"Then what are you still crying about?"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

—"B-But no matter how much you exploit others, there are still some miserable tasks you can't avoid doing yourself,"-Ran Deng wept.-"Especially building a large-scale teleportation array. Theoretically, that's not something a mere Golden Core cultivator like me has any business overseeing! Most of the array masters recommended by my clan are Void Refinement or Soul Formation grandmasters. They only agreed to come out of respect for my father; they hardly hold any regard for me. And if they look down on me, they'll look down on Fourth Junior Brother, who's only at Foundation Establishment, even more! If powerhouses like that are sent to help build the array, it'll be a miracle if they don't cause trouble, let alone be put to effective use. When Fourth Junior Brother suffers a loss, won't I be the one taking the fall? But I'm terrible at social diplomacy! If only there were a suitable person to help me intimidate and cow them, wuwuwu~~"

—"Ha! Even if you whine to me about this kind of thing, what could I... wait!?"-Yuwen Xuan replied lazily, but halfway through the words, realization struck him. He couldn't help asking with a bizarre expression:-"Oi, you wouldn't be thinking of having me help you, would you?"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

Ran Deng gave no reply, but her pleading gaze spoke volumes.

—"But I'm still hung from this tree. Even if I wanted to help you, there's no way,"-Yuwen Xuan pointed out.

—"...Eldest Senior Brother, the only one I can turn to is you,"-Ran Deng persisted in her plea.

Yuwen Xuan grasped her intention completely, recoiling in sheer horror:

—"Are you out of your mind?! Master hung me here with his own hands! Without his command, even if the [King of Heaven](# "Literal CN: 天王老子 (tiānwáng lǎozǐ) — traditional idiom for the highest conceivable cosmic authority") himself showed up, I wouldn't dare come down!"-Yuwen Xuan exclaimed.

Especially since this time there was only a single rope around him—not even enough to restrain a cat. Yet he had hung here for so many days. Was it because he couldn't get down? Of course not! He didn't dare come down!

—"Master's command naturally cannot be defied,"-Ran Deng said with her eyes squeezed shut.-"However, Master only hung your physical body from the tree. He never said your soul couldn't wander around."

She was obviously spouting bald-faced sophistry.

Although Zhou Hui hadn't spelled out certain rules, if disciples required every single boundary to be spoon-fed to them, then this flock of disciples truly had nothing but sheep brains—they might as well be tossed into a pot and stewed together to save everyone time.

Yet tonight, Ran Deng seemed dead set on helping Eldest Senior Brother "break out of prison."

Not only did she try to persuade Yuwen Xuan with words, but she also produced an exquisite treasure box from her sleeve:

—"If Eldest Senior Brother is truly worried about Master's punishment, you can simply pinch off a corner of your soul and use this object to conceal it,"-Ran Deng coaxed.

Saying so, Ran Deng flipped open the lid of the treasure box.

Resting inside was an incomparably gorgeous garment.

Yet as stunning as this robe was, it exuded a distinctly ominous aura.

If Qu Qianxing were here, he would undoubtedly recognize it: this "Heavenly Robe" was precisely what he had refined not long ago using that "True Immortal Lord" from Carefree Palace!

Back then, Qu Qianxing had slain the True Immortal who had perpetually schemed to usurp his mortal vessel, finally breaking through to the Nascent Soul realm and reclaiming his astral constellation power, using the True Immortal's blood to refine the foe into a Heavenly Robe.

However, a Heavenly Robe of such origins inevitably left an unsettling taste in anyone's mouth. No matter how magnificent it appeared or how formidable its stat buffs were, wearing it gave one the eerie sensation of draping oneself in human skin. Who knew if wrapping oneself in such a garment might cause them to gradually become part of it?

Even outsiders harbored such misgivings, to say nothing of Qu Qianxing, whose first half of life had been thoroughly wrecked by the robe's "raw material."

Consequently, after reporting to Zhou Hui, Qu Qianxing had sealed the garment away like a cursed relic, disdainfully stashing it in a forgotten corner of his cave dwelling's storage vault, seemingly intending to let this Heavenly Robe never see the light of day again.

Who could have guessed that merely a few days later, Ran Deng would fish it out?

—"I went through enormous pains to infiltrate Second Senior Brother's cave dwelling to swipe this thing. If he ever finds out, my credibility will be utterly ruined, won't it?"-Ran Deng sighed.-"This Heavenly Robe was purportedly refined from the Tribulation Transcendence avatar of Carefree Palace. You know the history of bad blood between Carefree Palace and Master, right? They managed to stay hidden for so many centuries that not even Master could locate where Carefree Palace was stationed—this fellow must have been behind that trick. A garment refined from him inherited similar properties: donning it can either draw everyone's undivided attention onto the wearer, or make everyone completely overlook the wearer's existence... But most importantly, it is the sole existence confirmed capable of deceiving even Master."

—"No one knows I stole it. If you help me, this robe is yours, Eldest Senior Brother,"-Ran Deng said, fixing her eyes intently upon Yuwen Xuan.

—"Whoa,"-Yuwen Xuan let out a whistle.

Truth be told, this was an exceedingly enticing offer.

Qu Qianxing might despise this garment, but Yuwen Xuan didn't mind it in the slightest!

Most crucial of all was that attribute—"capable of deceiving even Master." That was a feat even gods couldn't necessarily accomplish!

The things Yuwen Xuan wanted to do behind Master's back were far too numerous to count. To him, this Heavenly Robe was practically the supreme divine artifact.

However, before agreeing, Yuwen Xuan widened his eyes as if seeing Ran Deng for the very first time, scrutinizing this "former youngest junior sister" of his from head to toe:

—"I never would have guessed, Junior Sister! Here I thought you were just a docile, pampered young lady! The kind who wouldn't even let out a squeak when getting butchered (in past lifetimes). Who knew you had the guts to pull off something like this?"-Yuwen Xuan marveled.

Letting down a senior brother hung from a tree, stealing a dharma treasure from Second Senior Brother to do it, and even plotting to pull the wool over Master's eyes.

This was a "grand heroic feat" that even Yuwen Xuan had never dared to contemplate.

Truly worthy of being the sect's one and only heroine to have ever hung from that peach tree.

—"Heh,"-Ran Deng let out a chilling, spectral chuckle.

Eldest Senior Brother clearly failed to comprehend that when it came to slacking off, humans were willing to do anything.

Northern Domain, the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

[—"Has Siwu still not returned?"](# "Speaker: Goddess Ren Ran")

[—"N-No, not yet!"](# "Speaker: Court of Ceremonies Cultivator")

—"Then why did I hear rumors that someone spotted him at the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Could he have turned traitor?"-Goddess Ren Ran demanded coldly.

Before her, the cultivators of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Court of Ceremonies knelt in trembling terror across the floor.

There was no helping it. Ordinary cultivators had no standing whatsoever before a Tribulation Transcendence titan. Moreover, Goddess Ren Ran was a bona fide [banished celestial](# "Literal CN: 谪仙人 (zhéxiānrén) — an immortal deity banished from the heavenly realm to live in the mortal world"), while the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Court of Ceremonies was the very department responsible for communicating with the Heavenly Court. With their supreme chief, Siwu, having "vanished," the Court of Ceremonies cultivators possessed even less backbone when facing Her Ladyship.

To make matters worse, at this delicate juncture, yet another terrifying ancestor arrived on the scene:

—"Turned traitor? Betrayed who, exactly? Since when did our Heavenly Dao Alliance and the Heavenly Spirit Sect become hostile factions?"-a woman's voice chimed in.

Accompanying those words, a woman in red robes materialized in the center of the Court of Ceremonies hall.

The newcomer was none other than Xin Zhu, the Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse presiding over the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Although the outside world referred to Xin Zhu and her husband Su Mei collectively as the "Plum-Bamboo Couple," subtly giving the impression that Su Mei stood at the helm of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, everyone within the Alliance knew that between the two, the truly formidable one to cross was Xin Zhu. Consequently, some insiders privately joked that the Plum-Bamboo Couple was actually "Xin Zhu and Her Useless Man"—and recently, they had even appended "and Their Useless Kid."

Seeing Xin Zhu's arrival, several cultivators of the Court of Ceremonies visibly breathed sighs of relief, while others put on even more terrified expressions. The few who had been about to answer earlier clamped their mouths shut, instinctively shrinking deeper into the shadows.

Noticing the shift in atmosphere, Goddess Ren Ran furrowed her brows.

Yet she had no leisure to attend to others, for Xin Zhu was staring at her with a half-smile:

—"Nor did I know since when our Heavenly Dao Alliance's Court of Ceremonies became Her Ladyship's personal subsidiary,"-Xin Zhu noted.

—"What a way to phrase it,"-Goddess Ren Ran snorted coldly.

Before Xin Zhu, she was forced to rein in her temper:

—"I am merely dissatisfied. I have matters to report to the Heavenly Court, yet upon arriving at the Court of Ceremonies, I was told Chief Siwu has been missing for half a year! It is difficult for me not to suspect that the Court of Ceremonies has grown negligent in its offerings to the Heavenly Court!"-Goddess Ren Ran asserted.

[—"Is that so? I assumed you had anticipated Elder Siwu's disappearance!"](# "Speaker: Xin Zhu")

—"What do you mean by that?!"-Goddess Ren Ran glared.

Xin Zhu scrutinized Goddess Ren Ran's eyes, realizing that this Goddess genuinely seemed unaware of Siwu being attacked in the Abyssal Ocean.

However, that did not prove the Heavenly Court was innocent.

Xin Zhu had long discovered that this Goddess—purportedly having sacrificed everything to remain behind in this mortal world—was more akin to a pretty puppet; many behind-the-scenes machinations were kept strictly hidden from her.

Of course, barring the absolute worst scenario, the Heavenly Dao Alliance had no desire to stir up disputes with the Heavenly Court.

Recalling certain intelligence Untiring had shared with her, Xin Zhu sighed in her heart.

Her demeanor softened slightly:

—"I mean to say, I assumed Her Ladyship was already well aware of Elder Siwu's situation! After all, Elder Siwu's karmic fortune was bestowed by the Heavenly Court, and his itinerary was directed by the Heavenly Court. His every move ought to fall under the watchful gaze of the Upper Realm, shouldn't it? Could it be that the exalted lords of the Upper Realm failed to divine that he was ambushed and gravely wounded in the Abyssal Ocean, rescued by Immortal Venerable Untiring, and has been recuperating at the Heavenly Spirit Sect for the past six months?"-Xin Zhu remarked.

—"...!"-Goddess Ren Ran choked on her words, caught completely flat-footed.

She truly had no knowledge of Siwu's plight.

However, Xin Zhu's words reminded Goddess Ren Ran: the previous "Immortal Edict" had indeed mentioned that if an accident befell the Court of Ceremonies, she should not act rashly for the time being and simply let the Court of Ceremonies proceed according to protocol.

Yet with Siwu's "disappearance"—while it technically counted as an accident—the Court of Ceremonies' operations had completely stalled because his whereabouts were unknown; it hadn't reached the stage of "following protocol" at all.

Goddess Ren Ran had waited for several full months without a single whisper of news before being forced to come knocking on their door.

Furthermore, what was throwing Goddess Ren Ran into turmoil wasn't just the Court of Ceremonies, but also Carefree Palace.

"That person" in Carefree Palace had recently vanished without a trace as well!

The reason behind his disappearance was completely unknown: one day they simply discovered a pool of bloody water inside his chambers, and no one had seen him ever since. Although Carefree Palace still had its Palace Master and wouldn't descend into total chaos, anxious dread was unavoidable. Particularly since the previous Tribulation Transcendence lord had been slain by Untiring right in front of everyone's eyes, Carefree Palace dared not admit that another Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse had perished. Thus, they had pleaded with Goddess Ren Ran to investigate "that person's" whereabouts.

Upon learning this news, Goddess Ren Ran had completely panicked.

Although she had been left in this mortal realm following the God-Demon War, most of the time she was akin to a newspaper correspondent stationed in some backwater countryside: every now and then she received letters (Immortal Edicts) from the Upper Realm and chatted occasionally with her colleague stationed in this world (that certain someone in Carefree Palace), living in leisurely comfort.

Now, communication with the Upper Realm was temporarily cut off, and her colleague had abruptly vanished. Goddess Ren Ran suddenly felt as though she had been abandoned alone in the Great Wasteland—her entire composure was unraveling.

No! In truth, the turn for the worse had begun even earlier!

Ever since Untiring repaired the Stairway to Immortality and the "Immortal Edicts" from above grew increasingly sparse, everything had taken a terrible downturn.

This was the true reason Goddess Ren Ran had barged into the Court of Ceremonies.

Yet having arrived with aggressive bluster, facing Xin Zhu's questioning, she couldn't counter-interrogate with righteous indignation—spoiling the superiors' plans would be disastrous—and was instead forced to cover for the Upper Realm.

—"H-How am I supposed to know how the Upper Realm instructed Siwu? That is an internal matter of your Heavenly Dao Alliance's Court of Ceremonies! You said yourself this place isn't under my jurisdiction—what right do I have to know?"-Goddess Ren Ran stammered, lacking confidence.-"However, even if Siwu was gravely injured, how can you be so at ease leaving him at the Heavenly Spirit Sect? Currently, the Court of Ceremonies doesn't even have a chief administrator. How are we supposed to receive Immortal Edicts? How do we pray to the Upper Realm? Right now, it's difficult for me to even conduct a prayer ritual!"

—"Lord Siwu's injuries are extremely severe. Only Divine Physician Peilan could stabilize his condition, and Her Ladyship refused to come to the Heavenly Dao Alliance, so we could only leave him at the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Xin Zhu replied with a smile.

She then swept her gaze over the assembled Court of Ceremonies cultivators:

—"As for the Court of Ceremonies here? As long as there is no need to host large-scale rituals for the time being, they are naturally more than adequate. Surely they aren't so incompetent that they cannot even assist Her Ladyship with a prayer ritual, are they?"-Xin Zhu added.

—"O-Of course!"-the Court of Ceremonies cultivators hastily chimed in.-"Whatever Her Ladyship requires, we will certainly fulfill it!"

[...This situation won't do.]

Seeing this, the Goddess gritted her teeth in secret.

Although the Court of Ceremonies was not technically her personal department, it practically was. After all, the Court of Ceremonies possessed the function of communicating with the Upper Realm, enjoying a transcendent status across the entire Heavenly Dao Alliance; in the face of an "Immortal Edict," even the Plum-Bamboo Couple had to comply.

Yet judging by Xin Zhu's current demeanor, taking advantage of Siwu's "disappearance," she had clearly seized control of the Court of Ceremonies.

Wait... could it be that Xin Zhu and Untiring colluded to deliberately detain Siwu at the Heavenly Spirit Sect?!

Goddess Ren Ran glared suspiciously at Xin Zhu, then let out a cold sneer:

[—"Very well! I wish to pray to the 'Guardian Demon-Subduing Heavenly General'!"](# "Speaker: Goddess Ren Ran")

—"The Guardian Demon-Subduing Heavenly General?"-Xin Zhu blinked in surprise.

The True Immortals worshiped by the Court of Ceremonies naturally had established titles. The "Guardian Demon-Subduing Heavenly General" mentioned by Goddess Ren Ran was not within the scope of regular worship—or rather, was not a common contact.

Yet Goddess Ren Ran put on an air of deliberately making things difficult:

—"Never heard of him? This Heavenly General is an ever-victorious commander who campaigns against the demon race! The demonic tide of this realm is fast approaching; I wish to pray to him for peace and protection. Surely you aren't incapable of doing even this?"-Goddess Ren Ran challenged.

Hearing this, Xin Zhu surveyed the Court of Ceremonies crowd.

After a good while, an elderly cultivator stepped forward from among the practitioners, cupping his hands:

—"This humble one has spent some wasted years in the Court of Ceremonies and once had the honor of presiding over a similar ritual,"-the old cultivator said, his eyes lowered, appearing completely unacquainted with the Goddess.

Seeing this, Xin Zhu breathed a sigh of relief:

—"Then trouble yourself to preside over the ritual for Goddess Ren Ran!"-Xin Zhu said.

[—"Understood!"](# "Speaker: Old Cultivator")

Hearing this, Goddess Ren Ran gave a cold snort, appearing somewhat dissatisfied yet forced to accept it.

Yet as she followed behind the old cultivator toward the inner hall of the Court of Ceremonies, she exchanged a subtle glance with him, a cold sneer rising in her heart:

[Ignorant fools of a minor world, indeed!]

[They don't even realize that the one I'm contacting this time is a Heavenly General directly under the command of the Heavenly Court.]

However, Goddess Ren Ran hadn't done this purely to give Xin Zhu a hard time. This "prayer" was in truth an emergency communication channel, allowing her to establish real-time contact with the highest-ranking superior she could reach.

After all, "[a day in heaven is a year on earth](# "Literal CN: 天上一天，地上一年 (tiānshàng yītiān, dìshang yīnián) — traditional Taoist mythological proverb: time flows vastly slower in the celestial realm compared to the mortal world")"—to say nothing of the Heavenly Court's dead-slow bureaucratic efficiency, merely having a dispatch sit on an official desk in heaven for a few hours would equate to several mortal months. Heaven only knew what Untiring might accomplish in that time. It was far better to speak directly to the higher-ups and have them descend an Immortal Edict immediately...

No—it would be best if they dispatched someone down directly!

Goddess Ren Ran plotted furiously.
