# Chapter 114: [Merit](<# "Literal CN: 功德 (gōngdé) — Karmic Merit / Heavenly Merit; chapter title referring to the astronomical cosmic merit summoned by Zhou Hui's pedagogical reforms that forces an ascension tribulation">) (第114章 功德)

—"A conspiracy! This is an absolute conspiracy on the part of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"-Shu Jiawen of Feather Guest Pavilion shouted at the top of his lungs.

He had originally looked forward to the convening of the Immortal Arts Assembly with super-intense anticipation.

After all, during last year's Winter Solstice, due to personal reasons ([pride and prejudice](<# "Literal CN: 傲慢和偏见 (àomàn hé piānjiàn) — pride and prejudice; Shu Jiawen's aristocratic arrogance from the wealthy Eastern Domain that initially caused him to arrive late to the Winter Solstice Auction">)), Shu Jiawen had only arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect after the auction opened. Although he had thoroughly enjoyed himself throughout the entire auction—precisely because of that, before he could even have his fill of fun, the auction had come to an end.

Most infuriating of all, that scoundrel Qingyang Qingli of Eternal Villa had participated in the Immortals' Bazaar the entire time without breathing a single word to him! Worse, Qingli had secretly snatched the championship of the Soul Formation bracket in the Immortals' Chess tournament. Not only did he receive his own custom commemorative chess piece, but a specialized illusory projection hologram had been crafted in his honor. Qingyang Qingli had been so ecstatic that he strutted around bragging and flexing everywhere. In the end, Shu Jiawen had teamed up with several of his pals to meticulously select a [white burlap sack](<# "Literal CN: 白色麻袋 (báisè mádài) — white burlap sack; the custom sack chosen to match Qingyang Qingli's aesthetic before ambushing and thrashing him">)—specifically Qingyang Qingli's favorite color—slipped it over his head, and gave him a sound thrashing, which finally caused that fellow Qingyang to exercise a modicum of restraint.

Even after beating Qingyang Qingli, however, the Immortals' Chess tournament had concluded, and the next one wouldn't be until next year.

Though cultivators enjoyed immense lifespans, that only applied to times of secluded cultivation. When one had something tantalizing dangling right in front of them, even a single year felt interminably long.

It was precisely then that Shu Jiawen caught wind of a rumor: the sects of the Central Domain were profoundly dissatisfied with the representative cultivators selected from that Immortals' Chess tournament. That was the ostensible pretext, at least. As for the shadowy politics behind the scenes, Shu Jiawen neither cared nor gave a damn—his ancestral home was Feather Guest Pavilion, a colossal sect of the Eastern Domain; what did the political shifts of the Central Domain have to do with him? Regardless, they had petitioned the Untiring Immortal Venerable to host the Immortal Arts Assembly to elect the cultivators who could truly and legitimately represent the Central Domain.

Shu Jiawen had instantly grown thrilled!

At that moment, he conveniently chose to forget that Feather Guest Pavilion was an "Eastern Domain sect." Instead, he argued vehemently and passionately that his clan had been stationed at the Abyss Sea ever since the God-Demon War, meaning their presence in the Central Domain spanned longer than that of certain "locals." Naturally, they were more than qualified to participate in the Immortal Arts Assembly and represent the Central Domain, and so on. Through sheer coaxing and badgering, he finally secured an entry quota for Feather Guest Pavilion.

Once he secured that quota, Shu Jiawen was astonished to discover that the disciples under his wing had suddenly "come back to life."

This was an exceedingly rare sight.

Because coming to the Central Domain was not something most of these disciples had ever wanted.

Perhaps during the ancient era of the cultivation world, major immortal sects took immense pride in having an enclave in the Central Domain. In particular, possessing even a tiny guildhall in White Jade Capital was practically a badge of honor for high-ranking immortal sects.

Yet after the God-Demon War, White Jade Capital had plummeted from the heavens, the Central Domain had been poisoned by wild spiritual energy storms, and the entire territory had degenerated into a restricted wasteland. Stationing in the Central Domain turned into a wretched chore. Although Shu Jiawen had volunteered to come here for his own personal reasons, the other disciples had not. In their eyes, they had been "exiled" from the flourishing, prosperous Eastern Domain to the Central Domain. With Feather Guest Pavilion being one of the premier giants of the Eastern Domain, that sense of psychological disparity was all the more glaring.

Consequently, the atmosphere in Feather Guest Pavilion's Central Domain guildhall had always been abysmal. The more ambitious disciples scrambled to curry favor with the stewards and pulled every connection possible to transfer back to the Eastern Domain. The more pessimistic ones abandoned all hope, wallowing in hedonism, slacking off, and just muddling through one day at a time.

But the Immortal Arts Assembly had ignited them.

Those disciples who still harbored ambition figured: if they achieved a respectable ranking, they might catch the eye of a sect powerhouse, making a transfer back to the Eastern Domain much easier. Even if that failed, the tournament prizes were quite handsome and undeniably beneficial for cultivation.

Those disciples who had given up on ambition thought: whether it's the Immortal Arts Assembly or Immortals' Chess, they're both super fun! Joining the bustling spectacle has zero downsides—at the very least, it gives you bragging rights to boast about later.

And regardless of what these disciples had originally thought, once they arrived at the tournament arena, swept up in the fiery, electric atmosphere, they couldn't help but become motivated and proactive.

Of course, this was a wonderful thing.

What sect elder or teacher would ever want to watch the disciples under their tutelage drift through life like walking corpses?

It was just... while these youngsters had indeed become proactive, why on earth did they have so many questions?!

[Why on earth do they have so many questions?!]

Shu Jiawen thought with immense chagrin.

He felt the sect tournaments he had experienced in the past were never like this!

Back then, no matter how monumental the competition was, all he had to do was distribute the prepared magical artifacts, give the disciples a few words of routine exhortation, and when the battle commenced, he could [sit firmly on the fishing terrace](<# "Literal CN: 稳坐钓鱼台 (wěn zuò diàoyútái) — sitting firmly on the fishing terrace / remaining calm, detached, and unruffled while watching events unfold">), leisurely watching the disciples fight it out.

Yet for this Immortal Arts Assembly, before each round began, he had to dissect academic papers, deduce which map the platform arena had loaded, and even map out tactical strategies for these young brats. After the matches, he had to conduct post-match reviews, summarize their blunders, and formulate preparations for the subsequent round—

—"Not to mention, I have to fight in matches myself, you know!"-Shu Jiawen blurted out, thoroughly exasperated.

—"Shh! Keep your voice down,"-the cultivator standing beside Shu Jiawen urged, grabbing his arm and making a shushing gesture.

Saying so, the cultivator cast furtive, sneaky glances all around. Only after confirming that no one else was paying attention to them did he let out a sigh of relief.

—"Is that really necessary?"-Shu Jiawen said, unable to resist rolling his eyes at the display.-"Even if your disciples saw you, so what? You're looking up reference materials here for their sake anyway!"-Though he also lowered his voice.

The cultivator... Master Chunan sneered coldly:

—"I'm not like you. My disciples happen to have boundless faith in my competence."

—"A master whose competence is so trusted, yet who doesn't even comprehend the diffusion mechanics of Golden Core pill poison—and you call yourself an alchemy cultivator!"-Shu Jiawen mocked snidely.

—"Shut up! I brew pills! I don't research biological chemical warfare!"-Chunan snapped back.-"And aren't you in the exact same boat? If you have the backbone, don't come to Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion—"

[—"Chirp?"](<# "Speaker: Ink-Painted Bird">)

A crisp birdcall cut off Chunan's squabble.

He and Shu Jiawen instantly clamped hands over each other's mouths, looking to the side in sheer panic. They saw a tiny bird rendered in black ink fluttering toward their bookshelf. As if verifying something, it circled the shelf once. Apparently failing to find the book it was searching for, it continued flying upward.

Down beneath the bookshelves, several disciples stood talking among themselves—though whether they were Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples was hard to say. At any rate, they didn't look up, entirely absorbed in their own discussion.

Even if they had looked up, however, they likely wouldn't have noticed Shu Jiawen and Chunan.

Because over this recent period, there were simply far too many visiting cultivators in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion. Almost every tier of the colossal bookshelves was packed with movable ink ladders of varying heights. There were those conducting deep research into academic thesis topics, those seeking inspiration for papers, and those 'masters' like Shu Jiawen and Chunan who were frantically [cramming at the eleventh hour](<# "Literal CN: 临时抱佛脚 (línshí bào fó jiǎo) — clasping Buddha's feet at the eleventh hour / desperately cramming at the last minute">). It had actually produced the bizarre spectacle where the higher tiers of bookshelves (housing more profound cultivation arts and immortal techniques) were far more crowded and bustling than the lower tiers (housing elementary arts).

Realizing this, Shu Jiawen and Chunan exchanged a look and released their hands from each other's faces.

—"Which is why I said, it's a conspiracy on the part of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"-Shu Jiawen grumbled with bitter resentment, returning right back to his original premise.-"They must have orchestrated this whole tournament just to show off their Library Pavilion."

—"Who on earth would go through all this trouble just to show off a library?"-Chunan couldn't help but roast him.-"Do you think Heavenly Spirit Sect's Immortal Venerable is like you, or that brain-dead moron Qingyang Qingli?"

—"Untiring Immortal Venerable naturally wouldn't act on a whim. But I agree with Brother Jiawen's words—in doing this, he must have a profound reason,"-a third voice suddenly chimed in.

Startled out of their wits by the sudden voice, Shu Jiawen and Chunan nearly tumbled off their ink ladders.

Turning around, they discovered it was Daoist Heju, whose cultivation was roughly on par with theirs. However, his sect, Cloud Azure Sect, was merely a minor sect in the Central Domain, far outside the social circle of disciples from prestigious sects like Shu Jiawen's. Normally, he rarely conversed with them.

Rather, for him to approach them directly meant he truly had serious business.

Daoist Heju made no attempt to conceal his intent. Getting straight to the point, he said:

—"I imagine both of you are also troubled by the barrage of bizarre questions from your respective disciples! Before forcing yourselves to rack your brains for answers, have you two looked at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks?"

[—"Textbooks?"](<# "Speakers: Shu Jiawen & Master Chunan">)

—"Yes, precisely these,"-Daoist Heju said, rummaging through his storage pouch and producing a thick stack of book scrolls.

Shu Jiawen and Chunan looked upon them in utter astonishment:

[—"Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples are required to study this many cultivation arts?!"](<# "Speakers: Shu Jiawen & Master Chunan">)

—"You misunderstand. Several among them are miscellaneous subjects,"-Daoist Heju explained with a wry smile.-"Extremely useful miscellaneous subjects."

—"What do you mean?"-Master Chunan asked, his curiosity thoroughly piqued.

He picked up a textbook and flipped through it.

Then, Chunan's face flushed bright red as he discovered that the issue of pill poison diffusion wasn't even a Golden Core-level problem—it was something that could be resolved with an elementary, Qi Condensation-stage wind-attribute minor cantrip!

Yet this kind of [severe subject imbalance](<# "Literal CN: 偏科 (piānkē) — academic subject imbalance / being lopsided in one's study of specific subjects while neglecting others">) was extraordinarily common in the cultivation world.

The cultivation world possessed no standardized curriculum. Everyone either fumbled through things on their own or relied on their masters' oral teachings. Even when cultivation manuals were written down, due to the underdeveloped state of printing—and sometimes it had nothing to do with printing; certain techniques simply defied textual description and could only have their experiential insights recorded into jade slips, which, if handed to the wrong person, couldn't even be transmitted and would only get them killed—various cultivation manuals and arts suffered horrific loss over time. Cultivators ended up severely lopsided, constantly [reinventing the wheel](<# "Literal CN: 反复造车轮 (fǎnfù zào chēlún) — repeatedly reinventing the wheel">).

For instance, if Chunan and Shu Jiawen hadn't seen this textbook, they too would have spent days researching and reverse-engineering a similar minor spell before passing it on to their disciples—monstrously inefficient.

Naturally, Master Chunan would never admit this aloud. He cleared his throat:

—"They certainly study a rather broad miscellany, but it is quite intriguing."

—"Isn't it?"-Daoist Heju remarked eagerly. Letting out a soft sigh, he added,-"For such exquisite teaching materials to be confined solely to the Heavenly Spirit Sect is truly too great a pity."

—"What are you getting at...?"-Shu Jiawen asked with a furrowed brow.

—"I heard that Eternal Villa is attempting to import this curriculum,"-Daoist Heju said with brazen shamelessness.-"You two have always been on amicable terms with Eternal Villa. Could I trouble you to put in a good word on our behalf, so that our Cloud Azure Sect might get a piece of the action as well?"

—"Eternal Villa wants to introduce Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks?"-Chunan blurted out in shock, missing the point entirely.

Yet examining the contents of the textbook once more, he felt it made eminent sense.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable truly lived up to his reputation as an Immortal Venerable renowned for encyclopedic erudition. Put anyone else in his shoes, and they could never compile such a comprehensive suite of pedagogical materials. Using it to patch up the holes in disciples' foundational knowledge was simply perfect—and, incidentally, he could use it to tutor himself, sparing him from running around in circles answering every weird question his disciples hurled at him.

—"I'll... ask Qingyang later, perhaps?"-Master Chunan offered hesitantly. Mostly for his own sanity.

Seeing through Chunan's true motive at a single glance, Shu Jiawen couldn't help but remark:

—"Is that really necessary, Chunan? Doesn't your Moon-Welcoming Valley have its own textbooks? And you're the foremost authoritative alchemy sect in the Northern Domain."

Indeed, Master Chunan's background was quite similar to Shu Jiawen's.

He hailed from Moon-Welcoming Valley in the Northern Domain. Unlike Shu Jiawen's quasi-exile, however, Moon-Welcoming Valley, as an alchemy sect, required a vast array of medicinal ingredients, many of which did not grow in the north. Thus, Moon-Welcoming Valley established outposts across every domain to cultivate or harvest those ingredients, and all Moon-Welcoming Valley disciples took turns rotating across regions to assist.

For that reason, Moon-Welcoming Valley disciples harbored relatively few regional prejudices and were daring in utilizing local resources.

—"But we only excel at pill refining!"-Chunan protested at this moment.-"Branching out into other miscellaneous subjects to round out our foundations is wonderful. Besides, this suite of textbooks from the Heavenly Spirit Sect doesn't touch upon proprietary secrets that require confidentiality; as foundational enlightenment for disciples, it couldn't be better."

Reaching this point, Chunan gritted his teeth:

—"With this many textbooks to study, let's see if those little brats still have the energy to bug me with so many damn questions!"

Hearing this, Shu Jiawen paused. Remembering the horde of disciples who had been pestering him relentlessly of late, his heart wavered.

—"That sounds wonderful! Let's head over and inquire right now!"-Daoist Heju urged impatiently.-"Not long ago, I spotted Villa Master Qingyang over by the Academic Lecture Hall!"

[You're way too impatient!]

Shu Jiawen furrowed his brows, about to say something, when suddenly, an earth-shattering boom cut off his thoughts.

Everyone inside the Library Pavilion instinctively raised their heads, looking toward the source of the thunderous blast:

[—"What was that just now?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"Thunder?"](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

[—"What kind of thunder could possibly be that loud..."](<# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator">)

Amidst the chaotic murmurs, the high-tier cultivators suddenly realized what was happening. They gasped in shock:

[—"Could it be... a lightning tribulation?!"](<# "Speaker: High-Tier Visiting Cultivators">)

*Time rewinds to half a shichen earlier.*

Atop the Star-Gazing Platform, Shui Huang'er was excitedly reporting to Qu Qianxing:

—"Our first livestream achieved phenomenal results! I heard that after watching the premiere, tons of demons came forward to register! Now we actually have to worry about whether we'll even have enough instructors to teach them all!"-Shui Huang'er chirped.

—"Just how many is 'tons'?"-Qu Qianxing asked, his brows furrowing at Shui Huang'er's vague description.

Although Zhou Hui had established the strategy of [educational aid](<# "Literal CN: 支教 (zhījiào) — educational volunteer aid / teaching support program dispatched to underdeveloped regions; Zhou Hui's modern pedagogical outreach initiative for the demon race">) for the demon race, when it came to concrete implementation, his disciples naturally wouldn't let mundane administrative chores trouble their Master. Thus, the logistics of execution fell onto the shoulders of Heavenly Spirit Sect's 'chief housekeeper,' Qu Qianxing.

Having participated in the establishment of the White Jade Capital Academies, Qu Qianxing already possessed considerable experience in such matters. Even so, he still required concrete, definitive data before he could systematically arrange specific class formats, textbook distribution, and related logistics.

However, the demon race's situation was rather peculiar.

They had an overwhelming number of distinct clans and their governance was utter chaos. Many tribes operated entirely independently, often divided under the rule of Third Great King, Fourth Great King, or Fifth Great King. Even clan chieftains had no clear tally of how many demons served under them. Having never conducted a [demon census](<# "Literal CN: 妖口普查 (yāokǒu pǔchá) — demon demographic census; parody of the modern national population census (人口普查)">), when it came to popularizing education, merely calculating the headcount was a monumental headache.

Shui Huang'er could only offer the sweeping generalization "tons."

—"It's not that I insist on prying into your demon race's internal affairs, but based on clan populations and inheritance traditions, we at least need a baseline,"-Qu Qianxing said with a sigh.

Seeing Shui Huang'er's utterly clueless expression, he simply drew out a sheet of paper and swiftly drafted a formatted table with quick strokes of his brush:

—"Hand this to the demon tribes seeking to enroll, and have them fill it out."

—"Oh, wow! So there's this much meticulous care involved!"-Shui Huang'er exclaimed in admiration.

She studied the table intently, leaning forward to discuss the specifics with Qu Qianxing, when nearby came the sound of a dry cough accompanied by a deliberately imposing spiritual pressure meant to intimidate.

Qu Qianxing and Shui Huang'er looked over in surprise, discovering Villa Master Qingyang and Qingyang Qingyu of Eternal Villa.

That spiritual pressure—devoid of actual hostility, merely conveying displeasure—had originated from Qingyang Qingyu.

She now glared at Qu Qianxing with wide, almond eyes, complaining:

—"You're discussing curriculum matters? I was under the impression that our Eternal Villa had petitioned first, long before the demon race."

—"Yes, precisely, our Qingyu was clearly here first,"-Villa Master Qingyang chimed in supportively.

—"This is not a matter of first-come, first-served,"-Qu Qianxing said.-"We are currently compiling the teaching materials for Eternal Villa as well. It does not conflict with the demon race."

—"Is that so?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked, casting a suspicious glance at Shui Huang'er.

Shui Huang'er offered her a bright smile.

Qingyu's expression softened slightly, but she still turned back to Qu Qianxing:

—"Even if they don't delay each other, I still hope you can expedite it. If Heavenly Spirit Sect has any demands, name them freely; our Eternal Villa's ancestral treasury is by no means inferior to the demon race's."

—"Right, our Qingyu is infinitely better than some demon vixen,"-Villa Master Qingyang declared proudly.

Qu Qianxing gave a helpless smile:

—"It is not a question of price. Your respective matters simply do not conflict."

—"Who says? At the very least, your time is limited!"-Qingyu muttered.

Villa Master Qingyang added with heavy emphasis:

—"Indeed! The daughters of our Qingyang clan will never tolerate sharing a husband with another!"

[—"......"](<# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing">)

[—"......"](<# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingyu">)

[—"......"](<# "Speaker: Shui Huang'er">)

—"What kind of looks are those?!"-Villa Master Qingyang demanded.

He realized that all three juniors were staring at him with expressions that utterly defied description.

—"Ancestor, please stop talking,"-Qingyang Qingyu's voice was cold enough to drop icicles.-"And preferably, don't let me see your face for the next few days."

—"Human males truly are the most bizarre creatures,"-Shui Huang'er remarked, doing her best to be 'diplomatic.'

—"There's no helping it; they can't achieve parthenogenesis,"-Elder Tong Ling observed.

Villa Master Qingyang felt he was being mercilessly bullied.

*But surely Qianxing-er wouldn't be so scathing, right?*

Villa Master Qingyang turned tearful eyes toward Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing indeed offered him a way out:

—"I never expected Villa Master Qingyang to pay this humble junior a visit at this hour! I assumed that since Master published an article in *Arts & Sorcery* relating to your thesis today, you would be much more interested in visiting the Academic Lecture Hall?"

A face-saving out had been offered, but the intent to shoo him away was clearer than day.

[You think I'd leave you alone with two gorgeous girls—wait a minute, Untiring Immortal Venerable's paper?!]

Villa Master Qingyang wavered.

He glanced at Qingyang Qingyu, attempting to signal with his eyes, *'I believe you can triumph over that demon vixen!'* only to see Qingyang Qingyu cry out in shock:

—"Untiring Master published a paper?!"

—"Indeed,"-Qu Qianxing nodded.

Qingyang Qingyu instantly abandoned Qu Qianxing and Shui Huang'er, sprinting down the stairs in a single breath.

In the blink of an eye, she had vanished without a trace.

Villa Master Qingyang stood frozen in place in dead silence.

—"To be frank, regarding Eternal Villa's curriculum, I actually had a few matters I wished to discuss with her,"-Qu Qianxing said slowly.-"Or perhaps, Villa Master Qingyang, you could relay them on her behalf?"

—"...No, I'd better head down as well,"-Villa Master Qingyang said with an air of ancient sorrow.

[When one's own descendant is so uncompetitive, what can an old man do? I can't exactly marry Qu Qianxing in her stead, can I?! Even if I were willing, Qianxing-er wouldn't agree!]

Thoroughly weathered by life's vicissitudes, Villa Master Qingyang turned to depart, only to see a blinding, piercing flash of light streak across the Star-Gazing Platform, accompanied by an explosive, cataclysmic roar.

Every single feather on Shui Huang'er's body stood on end.

The three (four) people (demons/spirits) present had evidently witnessed similar scenes before. Practically in unison, they gasped:

[—"A heavenly tribulation?!"](<# "Speakers: Qu Qianxing, Shui Huang'er, Villa Master Qingyang, Elder Tong Ling">)

Having confirmed this fact, Villa Master Qingyang stared at the roiling clouds spreading across the sky, muttering:

—"Wait, out of nowhere, why would there be a heavenly tribulation? Whose is it?"

—"Could it be... Untiring Immortal Venerable?"-Shui Huang'er stammered, trembling all over.

As a member of the demon race, even a proud Azure Phoenix harbored innate, instinctual terror toward heavenly lightning. She curled entirely behind Qu Qianxing, wishing she could burrow straight beneath Qu Qianxing's writing desk.

At this moment, however, not even Villa Master Qingyang had the leisure to indulge in petty jealousy on behalf of his great... great-granddaughter.

He gazed up at the sky, still utterly baffled:

—"But how could it happen at a time like this?!"

Even if the Untiring Immortal Venerable had suddenly sensed the celestial nexus and realized he could ascend, it should have been after the Immortal Arts Assembly! Otherwise, what would happen to his disciples? What would happen to the Central Domain?

Tong Ling, however, spoke in the seasoned tone of someone (some tree) who had seen it all:

—"No, this is not an active confrontation with tribulation! Rather, it is the accumulation of [colossal cosmic merit](<# "Literal CN: 大功德 (dà gōngdé) — colossal cosmic merit / astronomical karmic merit accumulated through educating and elevating the entire world">), which even the Immortal Venerable's cultivation base can no longer suppress, coalescing into an Ascension Heavenly Tribulation."

—"Colossal cosmic merit?!"-Villa Master Qingyang gasped, sucking in a sharp breath.

He examined it closely. Sure enough, the hues of that thundercloud were distinct from any he had ever witnessed before—it faintly radiated [five-colored divine radiance](<# "Literal CN: 五色光芒 (wǔsè guāngmáng) — five-colored divine radiance; auspicious celestial light infused into heavenly clouds, signifying merit-based tribulations">)!

Villa Master Qingyang remarked in sheer envy:

—"Just what kind of deed did Immortal Venerable perform to amass such immense merit?"

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing instinctively glanced at the table in Shui Huang'er's hands.

It was said that mere fleeting thoughts of benevolent intent from Shui Huang'er back then had gathered massive amounts of providential destiny. Now that this educational initiative was about to be enacted, was it any wonder that the merit was being cashed in?

...No, it was far more than that!

Qu Qianxing turned his gaze toward Villa Master Qingyang.

Over the days the Immortal Arts Assembly had been underway, the textbooks had not only been popularized among the demon race, but had also caught the eyes of numerous Central Domain sects. Though few had directly knocked on the door to inquire, more and more were secretly stirring with eager anticipation. This, too, formed a part of the providential merit!

Reaching this thought, Qu Qianxing was about to speak, when he suddenly noticed Villa Master Qingyang's face light up with an expression of sudden, ecstatic realization!

—"I know!"-he bellowed at the top of his lungs.

[—"?"](<# "Speakers: Qu Qianxing & Shui Huang'er">)

—"I know! It must be because of that paper!"-Villa Master Qingyang shouted excitedly.-"My research concerned White Jade Capital and True Immortal blood, but because my academic prowess was insufficient, I could only write a fragment! Now that Immortal Venerable has thoroughly deciphered it and synthesized it into a paper, an [epoch-defining masterpiece](<# "Literal CN: 旷世神作 (kuàngshì shénzuò) — epoch-defining masterpiece / once-in-an-epoch divine opus">) has been born, naturally accumulating astronomical merit!"

[You certainly know how to stretch a connection...]

Qu Qianxing was completely dumbfounded by Villa Master Qingyang's [wild headcanon](<# "Literal CN: 脑补 (nǎobǔ) — brain-filling / headcanon / fabricating elaborate internal explanations without basis">).

And Villa Master Qingyang couldn't sit still for another second:

—"No! I must go read that epoch-defining masterpiece at once!"

—"Are you truly just going to read the paper?"-Qu Qianxing asked skeptically.

Yet Villa Master Qingyang's silhouette had already vanished into thin air.

And Qu Qianxing's skepticism was undeniably on the mark.

A quarter of a shichen later, everyone in the Academic Lecture Hall knew that the paper penned by the Untiring Immortal Venerable—having delved into the mysteries of True Immortals (Villa Master Qingyang: *'I contributed the research materials, you all better remember that!'*)—had accumulated astronomical merit, beckoning forth a heavenly tribulation.

And in the days to come, this theory would proliferate throughout the entire cultivation world, firmly believed by all due to the awe-inspiring magnitude of that thunderous tribulation.

Qu Qianxing, who had borne witness to the entire birth process of this urban legend from start to finish:

[...Whatever makes you happy.]

---

Zhou Hui had no inkling of this peculiar backstory, nor did he have the time to care about it right now.

As the individual at the center of the storm, he had already soared into the airspace above his immortal cave, looking upon that colossal thundercloud with an expression devoid of both sorrow and joy:

—"So it has finally arrived, after all."
