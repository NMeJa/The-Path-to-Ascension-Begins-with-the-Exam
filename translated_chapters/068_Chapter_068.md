# Chapter 68: An Amusement Park? (第68章 游乐场？)

—"C-Chosen by destiny?"-Shen Qingqian stammered, still reeling.

—"Think nothing of it. Just take it as the treatment you rightfully deserve,"-Zhou Hui consoled her, seeing how thoroughly overwhelmed with terror and awe she remained.

Yet this was not mere empty reassurance.

Upon receiving the jade signet of White Jade Capital and acquiring administrative control over the entire imperial city, Zhou Hui had gained vast lore regarding White Jade Capital and the ancient cultivation realm. Naturally, this included the "trial" he had just traversed.

The rules were actually surprisingly simple:

When White Jade Capital possessed a master, the Main Avenue was merely an ordinary thoroughfare.

When White Jade Capital lost its master, any living being who set foot upon the Main Avenue toward the Thirteenth Pavilion was deemed a challenger.

There were three rules to the challenge:

> First, one must be a native inhabitant of this world and possess at least one-half human bloodline. The challenger cannot borrow the strength of any other living being, and must walk the entire journey alone.
>
> Second, one must possess sufficient cultivation and endurance to traverse the avenue. The road itself will recreate the greatest hardships, the most terrifying enemies, and the deepest inner demons encountered throughout that cultivator's path of cultivation.
>
> (This second point was highly suspect. Zhou Hui admitted the road was indeed long, but not a single 'hardship,' 'enemy,' or 'inner demon' had manifested. Zhou Hui couldn't tell whether White Jade Capital had simply run out of energy, or if it had judged his cultivation journey as being just that boring—though he leaned toward the former possibility. In other words, the trial was practically nominal. [Main Avenue of White Jade Capital: ...])
>
> Third, one must have zero blood ties to the True Immortal Realm, and harbor no intention whatsoever of settling in the True Immortal Realm.

Only by fulfilling these three criteria could one walk to the very end of the Main Avenue.

Finally, upon reaching the city gates, one could push them open using a "self-created cultivation technique" that had made a "major contribution to this world."

Whoever opened the gates would obtain complete ownership of White Jade Capital.

Simple, wasn't it?

The trials of that road were virtually non-existent; only the third condition—'harboring no intention of settling in the True Immortal Realm,' which was tantamount to 'abandoning ascension'—was somewhat demanding.

Yet judging solely by the fact that Shen Qingqian had devised the *Blood Demon Art* (a technique name that sounded like an outright demonic cult) before reaching Soul Formation—refining baleful qi into spiritual power to temper the physical body—Zhou Hui believed she was more than qualified to pass the trial.

Regrettably, she lacked a single thread of [karmic opportunity](# "Literal CN: 机缘 (jīyuán) — fateful opportunity / serendipitous destiny"), blindly overlooking the colossal treasure right at her doorstep without ever realizing it.

*Tsk. 'Karmic opportunity' once again?*

Zhou Hui's gaze chilled slightly.

Regrettably, though Zhou Hui thought as much, he was no selfless saint so devoted to pure fairness that he would hand away treasures already resting in his palm. While a measure of guilt played into his decision to cooperate with Blood Demon Hall, the core prerequisite was that this body-refinement sect was genuinely useful.

Otherwise, Zhou Hui had ten thousand ways to escort Blood Demon Hall out of the spatial rift without them ever knowing what they had missed, all while singing his praises with heartfelt gratitude.

At this moment, the intelligence Zhou Hui disclosed to Shen Qingqian was also carefully curated.

He didn't inform her about acquiring administrative control over White Jade Capital, focusing solely on outlining the city's current greatest problem.

—"Furthermore, this 'cooperation agreement' does carry a certain element of dragging Blood Demon Hall into deep water,"-Zhou Hui said to Shen Qingqian.-"Because in its current state, White Jade Capital is by no means easy to restore. You must have noticed, haven't you? White Jade Capital is completely drained of spiritual energy. That means all its facilities—such as the colossal alchemy furnaces, artifact refinement cauldrons, defensive barriers, and even the Twelve Pavilions—cannot be activated."

—"Even you cannot do it?"-Shen Qingqian asked in shock.

—"If it were merely destroying White Jade Capital to retrieve the ancient 'relics,' of course I could,"-Zhou Hui replied placidly.-"However, to restart and operate it anew, my cultivation alone is vastly insufficient."

—"...Then Blood Demon Hall won't be nearly enough either!"-Shen Qingqian muttered.

She understood Zhou Hui's meaning.

If this were simple "exploration"—the sort typical of the cultivation world when delving into secret realms, merely smashing open the gates, destroying the seals within, and looting every treasure bare—Zhou Hui could obviously pull it off with ease.

For instance, in the southeastern quarter of White Jade Capital stood a massive alchemy furnace thirty-three meters tall. Although the flames within had long extinguished, thirteen layers of seals remained intact on its exterior—the hallmark of an alchemist mid-refinement. It signified that the ancient alchemist had departed before having the chance to retrieve the immortal pills inside. Simply shattering the seals to extract those ancient pills, even if unusable for oneself, would fetch an astronomical sum at Heavenly City's Feathering Auction on provenance alone.

Yet the Untiring Immortal Venerable had no intention of doing that.

What he saw was not the pills inside the furnace; what he wanted was to bring that colossal alchemy furnace back into operation so it could continue concocting pills.

That had nothing to do with treasure hunting.

On the contrary, it was a bottomless money pit requiring a mountain of spirit stones dumped into it just to stay afloat!

Shen Qingqian's heart swayed like a pendulum, swinging from one extreme dread straight into another.

—"Immortal Venerable, you wish to restore White Jade Capital?"-she asked with great difficulty.-"While I do not doubt your capabilities, for a city that has been dormant for tens of thousands of years, isn't that a bit..."

—"No, not restore it—merely exploit White Jade Capital's promotional gimmick,"-Zhou Hui replied, waving his hand.

[—"Gimmick?"](# "Speaker: Shen Qingqian")

—"Sect Master Shen, during the Tianling Auction, I believe you visited our secret realm?"-Zhou Hui countered.

—"Yes, I went to play,"-Shen Qingqian answered, nodding in bewilderment. In truth, it had been quite fun.

She had formed a deep impression of the Qianxing Secret Realm; had Blood Demon Hall possessed a suitable secret realm of their own, she would have wanted to build one herself.

—"Yet you must have also noticed that even before the auction, that secret realm's visitor capacity had reached its absolute limit,"-Zhou Hui said.-"In fact, reservations are already booked solid six months in advance."

—"That is true..."-Shen Qingqian realized, her eyes widening.-"You intend to remodel White Jade Capital into something like that? But this isn't as simple as a mere secret realm! When the time comes, everyone will demand that you surrender White Jade Capital!"

—"Naturally, I will not display it out in the open,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.-"Instead, I plan to announce that the Heavenly Spirit Sect has acquired the ruins of White Jade Capital and distribute treasure maps, claiming that cultivators need only accomplish such-and-such to unlock the grand gates of White Jade Capital."

[...Why does this suddenly smell like a total scam?]

Shen Qingqian thought in a daze.

Observing Shen Qingqian's dazed expression, Zhou Hui figured conceptual explanations were tough for a body cultivator to grasp. He pulled up a light screen, projecting a structural blueprint of the Abyssal Ocean Fiendgod battlefield ruins.

—"Sect Master Shen, you must be quite familiar with this war ruin surrounding your residence,"-Zhou Hui posed a surprise pop-quiz question.-"Then, please point out a widely known area suitable for exploration that remains virtually unvisited, and explain why."

—"Ah? What?"-Caught off guard by the sudden question, Shen Qingqian instinctively sat up straight, answering:-"If you ask me, an area suitable for Nascent Soul cultivation that nobody visits... would probably be Black Abyss Lake. Around that lake are copious artifact refinement materials desperately needed at Foundation Establishment. It should be an ideal exploration site, but because the demonic qi is too oppressive, only Nascent Soul cultivators and above can withstand it. Yet those materials are like [chicken ribs](# "Literal CN: 鸡肋 (jīlèi) — chicken ribs; something of little value to oneself, tasteless to eat yet a pity to discard") to Nascent Soul cultivators. Unless they deliberately go to gather materials for their juniors, hardly anyone ever ventures there."

—"Improper exam answer format, and the phrasing needs conciseness, but the content analysis is quite good,"-Zhou Hui graded her.-"It is evident Sect Master Shen has acquired considerable insights regarding ruin exploration!"

Shen Qingqian exhaled in relief.

A bizarre sense of peace washed over her—the relief of barely scraping a passing grade under a master's pop quiz.

Zhou Hui continued:

—"For an area like this, if I use the artifact refinement cauldrons of White Jade Capital as bait and assign corresponding quests, do you think those Nascent Soul cultivators will continue to ignore it?"

As Zhou Hui spoke, he tapped on the screen. A glowing quest line emerged from the location of the refinement cauldrons, tracing upward until it reached Black Abyss Lake as Shen Qingqian had described. Over Black Abyss Lake appeared mission nodes labeled "Harvesting Zone," "Refinement Zone," and "Exploration Phase." An icon of a tiny figure bustled across these sectors, steadily reigniting the artifact refinement cauldrons below piece by piece.

—"...You plan to trick Nascent Soul cultivators into serving as human fuel?"-Shen Qingqian gasped in sudden realization.

—"No, please refer to it as an [amusement park](# "Literal CN: 游乐场 (yóulèchǎng) — theme park / amusement park"),"-Zhou Hui corrected her with a straight face.-"A Grand Amusement Park Development Plan centered around White Jade Capital, with the Fiendgod ruins as an auxiliary attraction."

[This amusement park reeks of devouring people whole!]

Shen Qingqian thought to herself.

She immediately shivered at her own sacrilegious thought.

* * *

Of course, this "Amusement Park Development Plan" was merely a preliminary draft.

Even the Untiring Immortal Venerable could hardly transform White Jade Capital into an "amusement park" overnight—though given Zhou Hui's personal hobbies, whatever "amusement park" existed in his mind was thoroughly suspicious.

Shen Qingqian was well aware that for the present, the only thing she could do was maintain absolute secrecy.

At the very least, not a whisper of news regarding White Jade Capital could leak out from Blood Demon Hall!

In truth, Zhou Hui had only disclosed this to Shen Qingqian alone.

Thus, after signing that agreement, Shen Qingqian proactively requested that Zhou Hui seal the spatial passage below along with the grand gates in the underground hall leading to White Jade Capital, and even place protective barriers around Blood Demon Hall's outer perimeter:

After all, most cultivation sects were [homebodies](# "Literal CN: 宅 (zhái) — indoor homebodies / stay-at-homes") anyway. Having collected sufficient materials and resources from the Immortals' Bazaar, they could comfortably wait until Zhou Hui completed his "preparations."

Naturally, Shen Qingqian did not breathe a word to anyone—not even to Bai Wubin, her most trusted head disciple.

Internally, Shen Qingqian merely announced that Heavenly Spirit Sect had undertaken a renovation project for Blood Demon Hall's spatial rift as well as running water engineering, meaning they would be busy with construction all the way until spring. Seizing the opportunity, she assigned workloads to every disciple so they would have no idle time for wild speculation.

Zhou Hui was quite satisfied with Shen Qingqian's cooperative attitude.

According to the original plan, Zhou Hui left Ran Deng, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong behind to install running water pipelines and construct new cave dwellings for Blood Demon Hall, while he returned to the sect alone.

...Well, not entirely alone.

Yuwen Xuan was with him.

He snuggled smugly in Zhou Hui's arms, licking the flatbread around his neck, and even let out two boastful bleats at his junior siblings. Alas, Ran Deng and the others merely assumed it was a spiritual beast Master had captured on a whim, paying his bragging zero mind.

* * *

Mount Tianling was bustling as usual.

The disciples had no idea their master had discovered a ten-thousand-year-old treasure trove, had no intention of hauling out the relics to sell, and was instead preparing to pour massive funds into it.

They followed their schedules methodically—attending lectures, finishing classes, hands-on practice, and exam review, their timetables packed to the brim.

After all, once midterms ended, finals were right around the corner.

Because bizarre rumors were still circulating among the introductory class disciples, no one wanted to flunk and end up hung from the peach trees by the mountain gate!

Zhou Hui observed the bustling silhouettes of these disciples with deep gratification. Watching them for a full quarter of an hour, he finally nodded in satisfaction and flew toward his own cave dwelling.

At the entrance to his cave dwelling, his adorable twin disciples, Mo Bei and Mo Xi, were already waiting.

Not that Zhou Hui had told them to come.

In fact, when Zhou Hui wished to return, if he didn't care about the "journey," it was merely a matter of a single teleportation. Furthermore, Zhou Hui disliked pomp and circumstance, never bothering to notify his disciples to welcome him.

However, once he entered the boundaries of Mount Tianling—especially after conducting an inspection of the disciples' study habits—his direct disciples naturally sensed their master's return. They gathered outside his cave entrance on their own accord to greet him.

Mo Xi in particular.

—"Master!"

The moment he spotted Zhou Hui, he bounded over like an eager puppy—only for a little lamb to sneeze directly into his face, spraying flatbread crumbs all over him!

—"What on earth is this?"-Mo Xi demanded, his cat-like eyes wide as he glared accusingly at the lamb nestled in his master's arms.-"A sheep?! Why is there a sheep? It's just a wild beast without an ounce of spiritual power! Master, are you keeping it as a pet? And why is there a pancake looped around its neck?!"

[—"Baa baa!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Lamb)")-Facing Mo Xi's skepticism, the lamb actually raised its chin with immense smugness.

Not cute in the slightest!

—"Ah, this is your Eldest Senior Brother's pet,"-Zhou Hui replied offhandedly.

—"A pet? Are you sure it's not food?!"-Mo Xi exclaimed.

[—"Baa!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Lamb)")

The lamb craned its neck, snapping its teeth in an attempt to bite Mo Xi!

Zhou Hui paid no mind to the squabble between his youngest disciple and the pet... no, his eldest disciple. Looking around with a hint of bewilderment, he asked:

—"Where is the other one?"

Ordinarily, upon his return, all the disciples would come out.

—"...Master, do you mean Eldest Senior Brother?"-Mo Bei asked curiously.

She thought to herself that there should be "two" missing, as neither Eldest Senior Brother nor Second Senior Brother had shown up.

However, using the counter for animals ("one"), she figured Master was more likely asking about Eldest Senior Brother. Mo Bei reported:

—"Eldest Senior Brother hasn't come. In fact, while you were away, Eldest Senior Brother has stayed obediently inside his cave dwelling. Because he's been behaving far too well, I suspect he's plotting some conspiracy."

[—"Baa?!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Lamb)")

The lamb stared at Mo Bei in utter shock.

[Holy crap, Mo Bei, what's wrong with you?! Talking behind my back to Master like this?! Are you really my cute little junior sister?!]

—"Exactly! Eldest Senior Brother has been extremely suspicious lately—no gambling, no hunting down wild game, quiet as if he didn't even exist!"-Mo Xi chimed in. When it came to snitching on their Eldest Senior Brother behind his back, he and his sister stood on an ironclad united front.-"I bet he's definitely plotting something huge!"

[—"Baa baa!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Lamb)")

[Number Seven, just you wait!] the lamb bleated furiously.

Zhou Hui paid it no mind, casually pinching the base of the lamb's ears and shaking his head:

—"Your Eldest Senior Brother... never mind, leave him aside for now. I was asking about your Second Senior Brother."

When he returned to the sect, Yuwen Xuan's absence—well, it would be a miracle if he *could* come—was quite common. But Qu Qianxing failing to greet him was extraordinarily rare.

Hearing his question, Mo Xi and Mo Bei exchanged a glance.

Mo Xi wore a rare expression of concern:

—"Second Senior Brother probably didn't sense your return, Master. Lately... he's been very busy."

—"Busy?"-Zhou Hui frowned.

—"Yes. Qu Qianxing has practically moved into Buyan Palace lately, hardly getting any rest,"-Mo Xi sighed.-"Seeing him exhaust his mind and energy like that, I'm genuinely worried about his health..."

—"...Bei'er, take this little lamb to your Eldest Senior Brother's cave dwelling,"-Zhou Hui instructed, smoothly depositing the little lamb into Mo Bei's arms.

He said to Mo Xi:

—"Come, take me to see your Second Senior Brother."

—"Yes, Master."

[—"Baa baa!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Lamb)")

Watching Zhou Hui and Mo Xi fly away, the little lamb thrashed violently, but slender yet surprisingly ironclad arms held it firmly in place.

—"No can do. Master ordered me to take you to Eldest Senior Brother's cave,"-Mo Bei murmured, stroking the lamb's horn buds.-"I wonder if we can have roasted lamb tonight... Ah, you're wearing a flatbread. Could you be [lamb paomo with pita bread](# "Literal CN: 羊肉烤馕 (yángròu kǎonáng) — roasted lamb with flatbread / nang")?"

Mo Bei let out an expressionless *slurp*.

The lamb thrashed with even greater frantic terror:

[Screw you! Badmouthing me behind my back wasn't enough, now you want to make me into dinner?!]

[Wait! Why the hell would I roast myself?!]

* * *

The Star-Gazing Platform in Buyan Palace had been newly completed during the recent campus renovations.

Although intended as a classroom for Astromancy, the introductory class had yet to roll out relevant coursework, and none of the intermediate or advanced disciples specialized in this field, leaving it largely unused.

However, in the wake of the "Coral Tree Defense Array" incident, it had unexpectedly found its purpose.

When Zhou Hui followed Mo Xi onto the Star-Gazing Platform, he saw drafted calculations scattered everywhere across the elevated terrace. A group of Dharma-cultivating disciples were furiously flicking abacuses with heads bent low, forming a veritable [Human Computer Array](# "Literal CN: 人肉计算机方阵 (rénròu jìsuànjī fāngzhèn) — human computer array / flesh-and-blood calculation phalanx"). Meanwhile, Qu Qianxing sat upright before the Sky-Gazing Mirror, revolving the Astrolabe in front of it.

The pointer on the astrolabe flickered across a succession of names. Looking closely, one would discover they were the names of every inhabitant in the mortal villages that had signed "cooperation agreements" with Heavenly Spirit Sect.

For instance, right now, the astrolabe displayed all the villagers of Qiao Family Village—including unborn babes still in their mothers' wombs.

Qu Qianxing was searching for the "Destined Answerer."

Indeed, this was the inevitable "price" he had to pay after opting for a "question-and-answer" defense array that day.

Because the "questions" he had embedded could only activate if answered by the villagers across "past, present, or future," one could hardly expect villagers who couldn't read a single character to solve problems that stumped even Grandmaster mathematicians in the "past or present." Thus, his only choice lay in the "future."

Qu Qianxing had to divine which unborn children possessed sufficient mathematical talent, and then teach them the solutions in time to prevent the defensive barrier's energy from backfiring.

—Naturally, Qu Qianxing could have simply left it alone. He knew such minor backlash was trivial to his Master. But leaving behind loose ends was entirely contrary to his character; if he did something, he demanded perfection.

Yet even for Qu Qianxing, deducing so many destinies and innate talents all at once was excruciatingly onerous.

He hadn't rested once in days. Despite his Golden Core cultivation, thick dark circles had formed under his eyes, giving him a distinct air of haggard, fragile beauty.

Yet Qu Qianxing paid no heed to his physical state. His vision darkened in wave after wave—a clear warning sign of mental exhaustion—yet he refused to close his eyes and rest. Hearing footsteps approach from behind, he simply asked without turning around:

—"Are the calculations for Group C finished? Set them beside me, and use the fate chart in the upper left corner to calculate...!"

A pair of cool hands gently covered Qu Qianxing's eyes.

Qu Qianxing instinctively moved to pull away, but the crisp, cool fragrance mingled with ink wafting from behind soothed his muddled mind. He blinked in a daze:

—"Master?"

—"Qianxing, it is about time you rested,"-Zhou Hui sighed.

Yet to Qu Qianxing's ears, that sigh sounded almost like a reprimand. He hastily pleaded:

—"I will be done very soon..."

—"Rest,"-Zhou Hui insisted firmly.

Accompanied by that quiet command, Qu Qianxing felt his center of gravity fall backward. He instinctively wanted to resist, but enveloped in that gentle, cool fragrance, his entire body and soul gradually settled into peace.

[Perhaps... in an embrace like this, slacking off for once isn't so bad after all?]

[Ah, in the end, I've ended up troubling Master again.]

Qu Qianxing thought in chaotic embarrassment.

Faintly, Qu Qianxing heard Master's voice asking his junior brother in a low tone:

—"Xi'er, what occurred during these days? Tell me in detail."

—"Understood, Master,"-Mo Xi recounted in full detail how Qu Qianxing had handled the incidents caused by Xiaoyao Palace, before adding:-"Though it was necessary, if I did such things, it'd be normal. But given Second Senior Brother's character, doing something like that... was he triggered by something? What on earth did Second Senior Brother experience in the past?"

What had he experienced?

Qu Qianxing let out a cold, silent scoff. He didn't listen for his master's reply, knowing Zhou Hui would reveal nothing to his youngest disciple. Not that Qu Qianxing cared about keeping it secret; he simply felt it held zero value worth discussing.

After all, it had been a shallow, erroneous mortal life.

And all the errors stemmed from a face.

A breathtakingly beautiful face.

One hundred years ago, in a distant mortal empire, there lived an exceptional youth. He sat for the imperial examinations and scored first place overall. Yet solely because of his dazzling face, he was denied the title of [Zhuangyuan](# "Literal CN: 状元 (zhuàngyuán) — Top Scholar / first place in imperial civil examinations") that rightfully belonged to him, relegated instead to [Tanhua](# "Literal CN: 探花 (tànhuā) — Third Scholar; traditionally chosen partly for their youthful, handsome appearance"). Thereafter, still because of this face, he was assigned to the Hanlin Academy—a prestigious, leisurely post where no one wished for him to handle practical governance.

[—"Such a stunning man. What a pity it would be not to treat him delicately."](# "Speaker: Courtiers of the Mortal Empire")

Everyone said as much.

Yet when stripped to the bone, everyone merely wished for him to serve as a flawless [decorative flower vase](# "Literal CN: 花瓶 (huāpíng) — decorative flower vase; slang for someone valued solely for physical beauty with no substantive contribution")—offering visual ornamental value and performing zero actual function.

Unfortunately, the youth was no flower vase, nor had he any desire to be one. During a rebellion that nearly toppled the empire, the youth took up a sword, donned armor, and quelled the revolt. When the emperor asked what reward he desired, he declined all honors and riches, requesting instead to be dispatched to local provinces for practical administration.

His "insolence" offended the emperor. The handsome youth was exiled to a destitute, flood-and-drought-stricken county to serve as a lowly magistrate. Yet the youth was overjoyed; he believed he could finally realize his grand ambitions. Employing myriad methods, he repaired the levees, tamed the floods, and brought prosperity to the once-impoverished county.

It was also during the process of repairing the levees that the youth first encountered "immortal arts."

Worthy of a top scholar's intellect, he actually mastered those "immortal arts" and integrated them into the engineering of the levees, successfully halting a once-in-a-century catastrophic flood.

Yet when the populace praised him, their focus remained fixed on his face;

When the imperial ministers commended him, it was still his face;

When the emperor rewarded him once more, the praise was still directed entirely at his face.

The youth wanted none of those praises. He sought only to disseminate his flood-control engineering techniques throughout the realm. Yet the emperor declared it unnecessary: future floods would be handled by an "immortal."

Indeed. Where there were "immortal arts," how could there not be "immortals"?

With a mere wave of the immortal's hand, floodwaters would recede, and submerged lands would be restored to fertile fields.

Yet the youth found no peace in that prospect. He reasoned that since the immortal had failed to arrive in time when the flood struck previously, the levees remained an absolute necessity. [Self-reliance is better than praying to immortals](# "Literal CN: 求仙不如求己 (qiú xiān bù rú qiú jǐ)").

Yet his counsel met with universal rejection. The emperor deemed him ungrateful; the ministers suspected he was embezzling state funds; even the commoners he had saved grumbled that he was meddling and creating unnecessary trouble.

As for the immortal—the immortal declared that the youth possessed spirit roots and demanded he accompany him to cultivate immortality.

The youth harbored no trust toward "immortals" and wished to refuse.

Yet everyone rejoiced. Believing that refusing an "immortal" was an unpardonable blasphemy, they forced the youth to submit.

The emperor decreed that the youth be bound with thick ropes; the ministers shoved him into a prison cart dubbed the "Immortal Sedan"; and the common people hoisted the "Immortal Sedan" aloft, delivering the youth straight into that blood-drenched cavern of horror.

And so, the youth—

* * *

Qu Qianxing opened his eyes.

He stared blankly at the starry canopy above for several minutes before realizing he was no longer trapped in that terrifying nightmare, but in Buyan Palace of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, right beside his master.

Wait, Master?!

Before drifting off, had Master returned?

Qu Qianxing sat up in confusion and looked around. The entire Star-Gazing Platform was deserted, with drafted calculations scattered across the floor. The disciples who had come to assist had presumably been dismissed by Mo Xi. But the most important person—his master—sat cross-legged on a cushioned mat, holding one of his astromancy draft notebooks and reading it with quiet absorption.

Judging by the distance and their posture... had he truly slept with his head resting on Master's lap the entire day?!

Qu Qianxing's cheeks flushed warm. With an effort, he croaked:

—"Master..."

—"Awake?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Naturally, Zhou Hui knew Qu Qianxing had roused, but he had reached an intriguing section and had no wish to set the notebook down just yet.

To be fair, when he had taught Qu Qianxing surface integrals, he had never imagined the boy would apply them to gravitational and spiritual pulls between celestial bodies, using them to calculate destiny charts and innate talent.

Yet in the cultivation world, it was shockingly reasonable!

Zhou Hui turned to the next page.

Watching Zhou Hui's movements, Qu Qianxing felt uneasy, yet he spoke solemnly:

—"Master... this decision was made by me. Therefore, please let me take full responsibility to the very end!"

He had certainly not orchestrated those events just to have Zhou Hui return and clean up his mess.

If anything, having Master sweep up his aftermath felt like an admission of defeat.

—"Of course you must see it through to the end,"-Zhou Hui said, finally laying down the astromancy manuscript upon hearing Qu Qianxing's words.-"However, the way you are currently handling it—isn't it far too inefficient?"

—"...This disciple is incompetent!"-Qu Qianxing lowered his head.-"At the time, I lacked absolute certainty in halting Xiaoyao Palace, which was why I resorted to the most painstaking method. Reviewing it now, there should have been superior approaches..."

Whereupon Qu Qianxing launched into a fifty-thousand-word tactical and strategic self-criticism.

Zhou Hui listened with a gentle smile, suddenly struck by an odd thought:

[Who claimed this child takes after me the most? Surely I was never this stubborn and extreme?]

Believing himself to be exceedingly "flexible," Zhou Hui listened through Qu Qianxing's self-criticism and nodded:

—"Your analysis is excellent. However, because the angle was skewed from the very start, it is equivalent to zero analysis."

Qu Qianxing froze stiff on the spot.

—"In truth, I do not object to your method,"-Zhou Hui said, setting the draft back onto Qu Qianxing's desk.-"However, your final cleanup is far too inefficient, and entirely lacking in foresight."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

—"Why go to the painstaking trouble of calculating all this? Divining the futures of others not only drains mental energy, but readily cuts into your lifespan! You didn't study mathematics for this, did you?"-Zhou Hui sighed, looking at Qu Qianxing.

Qu Qianxing also knew this was a clumsy, brute-force method, but he had conceived of no alternative solution.

—"What Master means is..."-Qu Qianxing asked for guidance.

—"Why not simply have all our 'cooperation partners' send over their children, assign suitable disciples, and teach them all together?"-Zhou Hui remarked breezily.-"Wouldn't that resolve everything?"

—"Ah?"-Qu Qianxing was struck dumb.

[Splendid! Now I also know which facility in White Jade Capital to restore first!]

[It has to be the academy!]

Zhou Hui mused with utter delight.

The great and venerable Master never once paused to ponder: *what kind of "amusement park" on earth begins with a school?!*
