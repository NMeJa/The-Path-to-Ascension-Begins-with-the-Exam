# Chapter 61: Unexpected Harvest (第61章 意外收获)

—"Ah, caught an unexpected little thing,"-Zhou Hui said, raising his hand.

He was not in the auction hall at this moment, but situated by the spiritual spring near his cave-dwelling.

In truth, ever since the broader situation stabilized during the midpoint of the Immortal Bazaar, Zhou Hui had spent the vast majority of his time here.

Strictly speaking, the blame lay with his fourth disciple, Guan Ye. The boy had plumbed the spiritual spring to such an excessively convenient spot that ignoring it was impossible; once seen, one couldn't resist sliding in for a soak. Especially with the arrival of the Winter Solstice and the plum blossoms in full bloom—although they bloomed equally vibrant in the height of summer, certain things brought a sweeter sense of contentment when experienced [in their proper season](# "Literal CN: 应季 (yìngjì) — according with the season / seasonal").

Zhou Hui spent two [shichen](# "Literal CN: 时辰 (shíchen) — traditional two-hour time unit (four hours total)") here every day, feeling quite conscious of how terribly he was squandering time.

If he weren't constantly clutching his disciples' test papers, reports, and lesson plans, the scene would have looked even more like retirement.

Tonight, Zhou Hui didn't bother grading reports or papers. Instead, he projected the visual feed of the auction, propping the shimmering illusion beside the spiritual spring.

After all, this was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's maiden auction; Zhou Hui remained rather invested in its reception and results.

Accompanying him to watch the [livestream](# "Literal CN: 直播 (zhíbō) — live broadcast / streaming")—or rather, also currently soaking in the hot spring—was a bald-tailed old fox, and a belly... oh, Boss Tao.

Boss Tao was submerged comfortably beneath the water, leaving only his colossal white belly floating on the surface, swelling and contracting in rhythmic heave-hos. It tyrannically monopolized two-thirds of the spring, crowding poor Master Lan Pei into the far corner. Zhou Hui had no desire to plunge in and wrestle those two for pool space, so clutching a teacup with his robes hitched above his knees, he merely dipped his calves into the warm water.

—"This auction was quite well run,"-Master Lan Pei praised. Though crammed into the corner, he was cradling a small wine jug, drinking with serene contentment. Watching the auction projection, he marveled:—"Who knew Untiring harbored such commercial talent? Both the bazaar and the auction were refreshingly novel."

He even flicked his bald tail, swatting Tao Zhu's protruding belly, playfully [teasing and putting him down](# "Literal CN: 拉踩 (lā cǎi) — internet slang for hyping one party up while stepping on another"):
—"A certain someone spent so many centuries in commerce, yet can't even match Untiring, a complete newcomer to the trade!"

—"Hmph. I am an [epigrapher](# "Literal CN: 金石学家 (jīnshí xuéjiā) — scholar of ancient bronze and stone inscriptions / epigrapher and antiquarian"); business is merely on the side,"-Tao Zhu retorted, not bothering to bicker with Lan Pei, his muffled voice bubbling up from beneath the surface accompanied by *gurgle-gurgle* air bubbles.

Don't let Boss Tao's rotund girth fool you; his posture had always been extraordinarily nimble.

He perpetually toggled between being "the finest epigrapher among merchants" and "the greatest merchant among epigraphers."

This time, he was thoroughly convinced by Zhou Hui.

Although industry peers seemed to think he had taken a colossal loss—donating several master appraisers exclusively for the Tianling Auction's use, providing free appraisals and detailed breakdowns without charging a copper—those people were hopelessly short-sighted; in terms of vision, they couldn't even match the golden toad residing inside his belly!

Though the Abyssal Ocean Society appeared exceedingly generous on the surface, the treasures their appraisers leaped out to analyze weren't actually rare; they were merely the fundamental textbook basics of an appraiser's craft. Yet at the Tianling Auction, it established an authoritative, professional reputation, essentially running an "[advertisement](# "Literal CN: 广告 (guǎnggào) — commercial advertisement")" for his appraisers (in Untiring's words). Never mind ordinary cultivators; lately, even the number of high-tier powerhouses pulling strings to hire his appraisers—even specifically booking them by name—had skyrocketed.

Of course, for the Abyssal Ocean Society, appraisal services were merely a sideline.

More crucially, analyzing those treasures served as [casting a brick to attract jade](# "Literal CN: 抛砖引玉 (pāo zhuān yǐn yù) — offering a modest initial contribution to induce valuable contributions from others / priming the pump").

Although on the auction floor it seemed the appraisers analyzed an endless stream of treasures, in truth, Tao Zhu and Zhou Hui had meticulously pre-screened every single item: common everyday substances, rare materials that required strokes of sheer karmic fortune to obtain, and hazardous materials surrendered for safe disposal and recycling.

After all, ordinary cultivators rarely brought out the private treasures they possessed.

First, their worldly experience was lacking, whereas cultivation materials came in myriad baffling varieties; they feared being swindled out of rare assets. Second, the cultivation world was a ruthless jungle; even with the Heavenly Dao Alliance or Carefree Palace acting as guarantors, without knowing the true market value, cultivators dreaded revealing an exotic relic only to be murdered and silenced for their loot.

Consequently, unless backed into a corner where they desperately needed spirit stones to save their lives, the vast majority of cultivators behaved like [hamsters](# "Literal CN: 仓鼠 (cāngshǔ) — hamsters / obsessive hoarding behavior"), hoarding every scrap of material in their storage pouches until it rotted away rather than taking it out, spoiling good things for nothing.

Yet with the Abyssal Ocean Society's appraisers taking the lead at this auction, the little appraisal "secrets" prized by low-tier cultivators were demystified before the public eye. Cultivators no longer felt bashful about comparing notes with peers. Naturally, they gained a far deeper understanding of their own stockpiles, becoming willing to trade high-value materials they would never use themselves for hard spirit stones.

And indeed, there were quite a few treasures among them that left even the Abyssal Ocean Society clicking their tongues in wonder.

Not only did the Heavenly Spirit Sect [strike it rich hand over fist](# "Literal CN: 赚得满盆满钵 (zhuàn de mǎn pén mǎn bō) — earning enough to fill bowls and basins / raking in huge profits"), but Boss Tao, as an early investor, also reaped astronomical windfalls. He didn't even mind Lan Pei's prickly jibes, merely turning to Zhou Hui:
—"Untiring, let's keep running this Winter Solstice Auction next year!"-He struggled to hoist his head above the water, fixing Zhou Hui with earnest, pleading eyes.

Zhou Hui had originally only planned to oversee a single Winter Solstice Auction.

After all, his primary objective had been tracing the origin of the brand on Yuwen Xuan's hand. Now that he had achieved a rough answer, the auction had outlived its utility to him.

However, Zhou Hui had always been someone who saw things through from beginning to end.

This auction and the Immortal Bazaar had woven countless new karmic threads; Zhou Hui naturally wouldn't abandon them halfway. At the very least, he would wait until the conventions and regulations of the bazaar and auction had taken deep root in people's hearts and become established tradition.

However!

This was, after all, merely a sideline. The disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were still far too green; having them participate in a bit of social practicum was fine, but squandering too much energy on commerce was unacceptable—even this time, Zhou Hui hadn't allowed the bazaar to monopolize much of his disciples' time. Their primary vocation was studying!

And as the sect's Immortal Venerable, his primary duty was naturally educating them into pillars of talent.

*That*, of course, was Zhou Hui's post-retirement "relaxation."

Unlike Boss Tao, Zhou Hui could never derive joy from counting piles of spirit stones.

—"You and the Wanhe Gang will handle the mundane logistics; our Heavenly Spirit Sect will only provide the Kunpeng and the venue,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Deal, deal, deal! We will handle it!"-Boss Tao nodded vigorously, his head bobbing up and down.

He now looked upon Zhou Hui as though gazing upon the God of Wealth Himself, wishing he could enshrine him on an altar.

Lan Pei scoffed at the sight, edging closer to the rim of the spring to signal his utter disdain for such vulgar company.

Just as the three supreme powerhouses were chatting harmoniously while watching the auction proceedings, Zhou Hui suddenly raised his hand:
—"A few little bugs."

As he spoke, he lifted his hand in the gesture from the opening, as though snatching something out of thin air.

Lan Pei hadn't even registered what was happening:
—"What bugs? Even if there were bugs, Brother Tao would have swallowed them whole!"-Lan Pei laughed aloud—only to be drenched head to toe by a massive wave raised as Boss Tao vaulted out of the pool, turning into a bona fide [drowned fox](# "Literal CN: 落水狐狸 (luòshuǐ húli) — soaked fox / playing on the idiom 'drowned dog' (落水狗)").

Lan Pei shook his fur violently, glaring furiously at Boss Tao, only to see that Tao Zhu had leaped out of the spring, splashing half the water onto the stone bank beside Zhou Hui, staring with hyper-vigilance at Zhou Hui's fist that seemed to clasp something invisible.

—"There really are 'bugs' here?"-Lan Pei asked in astonishment.

For a moment, he wondered if his Mahayana cultivation had been forged out of cardboard; he hadn't sensed a thing.

—"Caught them from the auction hall,"-Zhou Hui explained.-"They hid exceptionally deep. Had they not suddenly unleashed an ultra-intense surge of spiritual power, even I wouldn't have noticed them."

As Zhou Hui spoke, he held the "bugs" firmly with one hand while tracing a small formation with the other, tossing the "little bugs" into it.

His movements were blindingly swift; before the words had fully left his mouth, the formation had already taken shape.

Those few "little bugs" immediately scurried, attempting to take flight, but no matter how they struggled, they could not breach the containment array's perimeter.

*Ah, so it was over at the auction hall.* In that case, it was only natural Lan Pei hadn't noticed.

Lan Pei embraced his [unbothered slacking](# "Literal CN: 心安理得的摆烂 (xīn'ān lǐdé de bǎilàn) — lying flat without guilt"); he had entered the Dao through medicine, so combat was never his forte anyway.

Lan Pei even bantered with Zhou Hui:
—"To deceive your eyes and ears is rare indeed. Just what kind of method is it?"

Saying so, he leaned his snout in to take a closer look, only to discover that what Zhou Hui had caught wasn't "bugs" at all, but several tiny clusters of flame. Crackling with crisp *snap-crackle-pops* inside the containment array, they darted about aimlessly as though devoid of intellect; whenever they collided, they merged into a larger, more violently blazing inferno.

—"Is this soul fire?"-Lan Pei asked, reaching out a claw to touch it.

One of the flickers seemed to sense his aura, darting straight toward Lan Pei's claw.

—"Not just soul fire—it possesses divinity!"-Tao Zhu said, eyes wide.

—"D-Divinity?!"-Lan Pei instantly yanked his claw back.

The cluster of flame slammed into the barrier of the array, flaring with a burst of blinding light like a dying candle, before dimming down.

Lan Pei no longer dared look down upon this flame.

Anything tied to "Gods" was profoundly, incomprehensibly esoteric.

Gods and Immortals—though the two were constantly grouped together as *Shenxian*, even cultivators struggled to articulate the precise distinction between a "God" and an "Immortal." Their raw power seemed comparable, and certain gods could even be counted as immortals.

Strictly speaking, a "God" could interfere with cosmological entities such as the Heavenly Dao and fate itself, whereas even ordinary True Immortals had inescapable fates.

It seemed only by becoming a "God" did one obtain certain unique [divine authorities](# "Literal CN: 特殊权柄 (tèshū quánbǐng) — special cosmological authorities / divine dominions").

Consequently, cultivators could only cultivate toward Immortality; they could not directly ascend to Godhood—unless they encountered extraordinary karmic fortunes along the path, stumbling upon divine relics tied to divine authorities right from the very start.

Of course, only greenhorns just setting foot upon the immortal path indulged in romantic fantasies about ascending to godhood in the mortal flesh.

As Mahayana powerhouses, they knew encountering divine relics wasn't particularly difficult—especially in the Central Domain polluted by the ancient War of Demon Gods. Rummaging casually through the ancient ruins of the Abyss Sea, the majority of magical artifact shards carried faint remnants of lingering divinity.

Yet likely owing to the brutal nature of that war, those divine remnants were mostly tainted with venomous malice.

The malice of mortal men was terrifying enough; how much more horrifying was the malice of a god? Any living creature touching that malice—whether mortal or cultivator, spirit race or demon beast—was either killed or crippled. Even survivors bore catastrophic curses that spanned generations.

As a physician, Lan Pei had witnessed countless gruesome tragedies, so he naturally wouldn't touch it rashly.

Even so, "divinity" remained an intoxicating temptation for cultivators—the higher one's realm, the more irresistible the allure.

After all, who knew if it might just be a chance to [ascend to the heavens in a single step](# "Literal CN: 一步登天 (yī bù dēng tiān) — reach the peak overnight / skyrocket to heaven") (physically)!

Looking upon the embers, Lan Pei still felt a covetous itch:
—"A flame bearing divinity! It could surely forge an extraordinary magical artifact!"

He wasn't suggesting blindly; flames and malice typically countered one another, possessing immense destructive lethality. Even with lingering traces of malice, it was prime material for artifact refinement.

—"Extraordinary indeed,"-Tao Zhu sneered coldly.-"This thing could easily claim tens of thousands of lives!"

—"Eh?"-Lan Pei asked blankly.

—"I didn't let it detonate, but it hid too deeply earlier; by the time I noticed, it was already too late,"-Zhou Hui sighed, shaking his head.-"Those hosts couldn't be saved. Worst of all, had they remained in Qianxing Hall, I might have found a way, but they fled too quickly. Now that I've settled these flames, they are beyond rescue."

—"You actually wanted to save them?"-Tao Zhu asked incredulously.

—"Testimony is always better when comprehensive. Asking just Him alone leaves me somewhat uneasy,"-Zhou Hui said, poking the flame with his fingertip and kneading it into a ball, eliciting helpless *sizzle-sizzle* whines from the fire.

Hearing Zhou Hui's words, Tao Zhu and Lan Pei were utterly shocked:
[—"What do you mean, 'asking just Him alone'?"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")
[—"Could this thing actually possess sapience?!"](# "Speaker: Lan Pei")

Tao Zhu and Lan Pei instantly retreated three meters back.

An item bearing divinity was hazardous enough; one possessing sapience was exponentially more lethal.

That meant this god was still alive.

Living in a state worse than death.

Torn into pieces, maimed and incomplete, harboring lingering hostility toward all worldly existence—especially loathing cultivators who wished to usurp its godhood or refine it into tools.

It would surely employ every conceivable method to make any living being approaching it die faster and more gruesomely.

Zhou Hui, however, was entirely unfazed, casually kneading the ember:
—"No need to be so alarmed. Though His malice is obvious, His resentment is actually quite faint. It likely isn't residual malice from ancient ruins, but a fragment torn from a living, healthy deity."

*Isn't that even more dangerous?!*

*That is equivalent to offending an active, living god!*

Lan Pei and Tao Zhu stared speechlessly at Zhou Hui, debating whether they should flee before Zhou Hui dragged them down—though it was probably already too late.

Having been analyzed with such surgical precision, the ember could naturally no longer play dead.

It demanded in furious indignation:
—"Zhou Hui, brat! You bully and humiliate me—aren't you afraid of retribution?"

—"Not at all. In fact, I rather welcome 'retribution,'"-Zhou Hui replied placidly.-"Ever since the War of Demon Gods, this minor realm has been thoroughly sealed. The sole way out is the Great Dao of Ascension. Yet even a mere fragment of yours remains so lucid, which means your main body must be exceptionally intact! If such a body existed within this world, I would have known long ago. That means you are interfering here from 'another world.' I am exceedingly interested in how you managed that!"

After all, back in his fifth life when his lightning tribulation had shattered half the world, Zhou Hui had realized that standard ascension was out of the question!

In his sixth life and in this life before seeing the script of the Heavenly Dao, his primary research topic had been "how to locate spatial rifts and [smuggle his way into the immortal realm](# "Literal CN: 偷渡到仙界 (tōudù dào xiānjiè) — stowing away / illegally crossing the dimensional border to the immortal realm")."

Even though Zhou Hui had abandoned thoughts of ascension now, with his academic thirst for knowledge, since the answer had been delivered straight to his doorstep, he didn't mind conducting a bit of research.

Sensing Zhou Hui's emotions, the ember actually shuddered.

—"D-Don't come near me!"-the flame stammered, pressing itself flat against the edge of the containment array.-"W-When I was severed, I was completely cut off from the main body! My memories are incomplete, holding virtually nothing of importance! Even if you research me inside out, you won't discover how my true form interferes in this world!"

Zhou Hui eyed Him with a chilling smirk:
—"Yet there is one thing you definitely know: how to conceal yourself inside a host without being detected!"

And that point was likely connected to the brand on Yuwen Xuan.

No, not "likely"—*definitely*.

Zhou Hui possessed supreme confidence in his own strength. Across this mortal realm, entities capable of bypassing his perception and planting a brand within the perimeter of his divine sense were exceedingly rare. Yet within a short span of time, so many had cropped up. That was obviously no coincidence.

They undoubtedly originated from the same source.

Yet the origin of the brand on Daoist Huang was clear as day. Given how frantically they were prancing about, the mark had evidently been planted by Carefree Palace—even Zhang Hongjing had caught onto them and warned Zhou Hui beforehand.

Then had the brand on Yuwen Xuan also been planted by Carefree Palace? For what purpose?

Furthermore, if they had planted Yuwen Xuan's mark with such exquisite stealth, why had they configured Daoist Huang's mark like a crude suicide bomb, as though terrified Zhou Hui wouldn't figure out who had tampered with his eldest disciple?

Even if Carefree Palace were utterly devoid of brains, they wouldn't be this absurd, would they?

Questions swirled in Zhou Hui's mind, but his hands moved without the slightest hesitation. He traced several arrays in rapid succession, trapping the flame layer upon layer, setting up all manner of "examination" postures.

Lan Pei and Tao Zhu shivered on the sidelines.

If minutes ago they had feared the "deity" represented by that flame, now their dread had shifted entirely to the man before them; for the flame, they felt only pity.

Naturally, that pity wasn't enough to plead on its behalf.

Lan Pei and Tao Zhu exchanged a glance. Ignoring the fact that the auction "livestream" hadn't ended, they silently grabbed their bath towels and slipped out of the courtyard.

Walking down the path, they could still hear the flame's bloodcurdling shriek echoing behind them:
—"SZZZ-AAAAAAH!"

—"AHHH——!"

Somewhere in the Eastern Domain, inside a majestic celestial palace resplendent as the heavenly court, an identical shriek of agony rang out.

In an opulent hall, a woman was rolling on the ground in wretched agony, completely stripped of her usual glamorous poise. Her jet-black hair was splayed across the floor, rapidly streaked with silver strands, while fine wrinkles sprouted across her face—which had been maintained in her early thirties—making her look older and older.

Seeing such horror, the female cultivator attendants swarmed in a panic, helpless:
—"Mistress! Mistress, please hold on!"-an attendant cried in panic.
[—"Where is the healer? Has the physician not arrived yet?"](# "Speaker: Attendant")
[—"What use is a physician in this state? Go summon the Palace Master!"](# "Speaker: Attendant")
[—"She was fine just moments ago, how did it suddenly..."](# "Speaker: Attendant")

Amidst their chaotic panic, a violent gale blew in, sweeping all the attendants a dozen paces away from the woman.

Simultaneously, dozens of talismans slammed onto the woman along with the gust, finally stabilizing her condition.

The woman now appeared in her forties, her body trembling with lingering spasms of pain. Refusing to remain sprawled on the ground, she pushed herself up on trembling legs, looking toward the palace entrance.

There stood an eight-foot burly giant and a gorgeous youth. The talismans had come from the youth's hand.

The woman's gaze swept past the youth, looking away as though seeing nothing.

She knelt solely before the eight-foot giant:
—"Xiao Yu thanks the Palace Master for saving my life!"

—"I told you long ago not to act recklessly!"-the burly giant known as the "Palace Master" roared in fury.-"Whose rotten idea was it to blow up the Tianling Auction hall?! Now look at how you've been countered!"

[But you didn't object back then either!]

Naturally, the woman dared not say such a thing aloud. She only pleaded tearfully:
—"P-Palace Master, forgive me."

The burly giant snorted coldly.

Seeing he wasn't rebuking her further, the woman gathered her courage:
—"B-But what the Lord God bestowed was merely shed divine remains; in theory, there should be no remaining connection. That... that man shouldn't have been able to sense me—"

—"He didn't sense you. Given Zhou Hui's temperament, he is likely purely researching that fragment,"-the gorgeous youth said upon hearing this.-"And as the '[Divine Emissary](# "Literal CN: 神使 (shénshǐ) — divine messenger / emissary of the god"),' Xiao Yu shares sensory empathy with the god. Even a severed, discarded fragment of the deity will reflect back onto her body. This is an inevitable price."

The eight-foot giant listened in silence, his brow furrowing. Without speaking aloud, he sent a divine sense transmission—one the woman could not hear—to the youth:
—"Will this implicate Carefree Palace?"-the giant asked:—"I mean, could she become Zhou Hui's puppet and destroy Carefree Palace from within?"

As he spoke, he cast a cold, indifferent glance at the woman. Clearly, had the youth answered with a single 'yes,' the woman would have lost her life in the very next heartbeat.

Fortunately, the youth shook his head:
—"Not for now. As she said, that fragment was discarded by the god; tracing it back to its root is virtually impossible. Even for Zhou Hui, even if he discovers there is a Divine Emissary, he cannot establish control through such a faint link. If anything, that 'God' has a different plan for Zhou Hui, and this remnant was left behind deliberately!"

—"A different plan?"-the burly giant asked via divine sense, casting an odd glance at the woman.

Kneeling on the ground, the woman had heard none of their silent exchange. Seeing the giant standing there without a word, she said tremulously:
—"I... amidst the agonizing wails of the Lord God's fragment, I heard a piece of information."

[—"What is it?"](# "Speaker: Palace Master Li")

—"That... that man seems to be leaving the Heavenly Spirit Sect—for [ten days](# "Literal CN: 一旬 (yī xún) — a ten-day period in the traditional lunar calendar") at least, or a month at most."

[—"Oh?"](# "Speaker: Palace Master Li")

Hearing this, the giant's eyes lit up.

—"...Immortal Venerable, this is your luggage?"-Sect Leader Shen Qingqian of Blood Demon Hall asked with visible difficulty.

They had arranged long before that after the auction, Zhou Hui would visit Blood Demon Hall to see if there were suitable Flame Crystal Flowers (an excuse). Three days had passed since the auction concluded, and attendees were departing; it was time for Blood Demon Hall to fulfill its promise.

Yet Shen Qingqian never imagined Zhou Hui would bring this much luggage!

Moved down from Mount Tianling were items enough to fill two entire warehouses—and these were only the items unsuitable for storage pouches! For instance, Shen Qingqian saw a flame sealed inside a glass fish tank filled with water, constantly letting out wretched shrieks. According to Zhou Hui, spatial transfer would compromise the precision of these experimental tools, so they had to be transported directly.

As for the items that could go into storage pouches... put it this way: Zhou Hui's fifth disciple, Lu Shulong, was carrying a colossal sack packed with all manner of storage pouches.

[Does Untiring Immortal Venerable truly intend to just look around Blood Demon Hall? He isn't planning to conquer our Blood Demon Hall, is he?!]-Shen Qingqian thought in sheer panic.

—"Don't mind it. I am merely preparing thoroughly in case unforeseen circumstances arise,"-Zhou Hui comforted her, observing Shen Qingqian's troubled expression.

[...That is way too thorough! You are a Tribulation Transcendence supreme sovereign; what kind of unforeseen circumstances could possibly harm you?!]-Shen Qingqian muttered internally, though she wasn't so unhinged as to say that aloud to a powerhouse she dreaded. She merely chuckled dryly:
—"With an almighty sovereign like you present, even if unforeseen circumstances arise, the other party will be the miserable one, right?"

Accompanied by her words, somewhere in the boundless cosmic void, a colossal sneeze echoed out!
