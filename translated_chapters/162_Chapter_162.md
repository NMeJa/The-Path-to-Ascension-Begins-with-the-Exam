# Chapter 162: Behind the Scenes (第162章 黑幕)

—"Master, did Eldest Senior Brother get into trouble again? For you to suddenly seek him out,"-Qu Qianxing inquired.

His tone was calm and measured, yet for reasons unknown, Zhou Hui could sense a heavy undercurrent of murderous intent steeped in every syllable.

Zhou Hui cast a peculiar glance at his second disciple.

—"Yuwen Xuan has not caused trouble,"-Zhou Hui answered.-"At least, I have not yet confirmed it."

*Not yet confirmed... is that so?*

The other disciples exchanged meaningful glances.

Zhou Hui disliked harboring malicious suspicions regarding his disciples. Consequently, his phrase "not yet confirmed" was generally reserved for situations where he had already deduced that a disciple was up to no good, but merely lacked concrete evidence. Regrettably, there was a ninety-nine percent probability that such cases would ultimately turn into "confirmed"—a rule that was hardly limited to Yuwen Xuan, but applied equally to every disciple under his roof.

Thus, in a split second, everyone became firmly convinced that Eldest Senior Brother had definitely committed some atrocious deed and fled the scene.

However, on this occasion, they had truly wronged Yuwen Xuan.

Zhou Hui had not actually uncovered anything; it was merely that while attempting to rewrite the Heavenly Dao under the guidance of the Miniature World's Goddess, he had occasionally sensed that although his eldest disciple's (human) physical body remained in place, his soul had drifted into the spatial rift between the Upper Realm and Lower Realm.

—Yuwen Xuan had been inside a dream at the time, attempting to devour his own true form.

Zhou Hui naturally wished to grab Yuwen Xuan to ask what in the world had happened.

To his surprise, Yuwen Xuan had already made a run for it.

At this point, Qu Qianxing asked with thoughtful solicitude:

—"Shall we go capture Eldest Senior Brother and drag him back?"-Qu Qianxing asked considerately.

—"That will not be necessary; it is hardly so urgent,"-Zhou Hui replied after a moment's contemplation.-"Besides, with Boss Tao keeping an eye on him, he should not be able to do anything too terrible."

*Not be able to?*

Lu Shulong's brow twitched violently.

He remembered that in the third lifetime, Yuwen Xuan had inherited the Abyssal Ocean Society from Boss Tao, managing the Central Domain so that it was [airtight as an iron barrel](# "Literal CN: 经营得像是铁桶一样 — fortified so securely that nothing can penetrate"). Forget the Palace of Carefree Wandering—not even a mosquito could slip inside.

What was most infuriating was that even in that third lifetime, Eldest Senior Brother had been the sole entity who enjoyed Master's personal care and watchfulness.

Yet because Master had not raised him personally from childhood, Master only stepped in to discipline him whenever Eldest Senior Brother stirred up continent-shattering trouble in that lifetime. Boss Tao was clearly not the sort of person who knew how to care for a child either, leaving Eldest Senior Brother in that life appearing exceedingly haughty, aloof, and unapproachable. He [refused to give face even to Boss Tao](# "Literal CN: 连陶老板的面子都不卖 — wouldn't even grant Boss Tao face/respect"), listening only to Master (out of sheer dread of Master's terrifying might), while simultaneously harboring a latent resentment toward Master—the sort that quietly bided its time for an opportunity to devour him whole.

Since Yuwen Xuan had treated even Master that way in that lifetime, he had naturally shown zero mercy toward his fellow junior brothers and sisters.

He had treated their youngest junior brother and sister as mere instrumental Petri dishes, coerced the Ran Clan into constructing extravagant palaces for him, and fought Second Senior Brother—who was desperately scouring the world for Master—in a bitter war across the Eastern Domain, each using the Central and Northern Domains as their respective operational bases. The two had been [brawling tooth and nail](# "Literal CN: 掐得死去活来 — fighting each other to the death"), nearly boiling the boundless ocean dry and coming within an inch of slicing Lu Shulong into [cured jerky](# "Literal CN: 做成肉干 — slicing someone thin and drying them into jerky").

Just thinking back to Eldest Senior Brother from that lifetime made Lu Shulong shudder down to his scales. How on earth could Master possibly believe Boss Tao was capable of keeping Eldest Senior Brother under control?

[What on earth gave Master such a massive delusion?!]

Qu Qianxing held the exact same view.

However:

—"Since Master has spoken, we shall leave Eldest Senior Brother in Boss Tao's hands for now,"-Qu Qianxing said.

In his mind, he made an immediate note to deploy scouts to place the Abyssal Ocean Society under round-the-clock surveillance shortly.

Qu Qianxing smiled and asked:

—"Though, since Master deems Eldest Senior Brother's situation non-urgent, do you have more pressing matters to instruct us upon?"-Qu Qianxing asked, smiling.

—"No, you have all done splendidly,"-Zhou Hui replied with deep gratification.

Although Qu Qianxing and Yuwen Xuan undergoing their respective heavenly tribulations in succession had been somewhat unexpected and stirred up quite a commotion, on the whole, it had not derailed the grand strategy.

[Having a flock of disciples capable of independent initiative really saves so much trouble!]

Zhou Hui thought with great relief.

It was truly remarkable that he could still maintain such an optimistic thought even after enduring seven full reincarnations.

On the contrary, Zhou Hui was somewhat dissatisfied with himself:

—"It is rather that I have encountered some issues in my own cultivation that must be resolved,"-Zhou Hui said.

To be precise, he had hit a snag in rewriting the Heavenly Dao.

Yet that was only natural.

Scripting the Heavenly Dao had never been an easy undertaking. Even native deities were incapable of mastering it with complete flexibility—which was precisely why old gods superseded by new gods were a [dime a dozen](# "Literal CN: 比比皆是 (bǐbǐ jiē shì) — ubiquitous / found everywhere"). Furthermore, this world's Heavenly Dao had been running for uncounted eons, with earlier eras possessing virtually zero documentation. It was, in every sense of the term, a genuine [mountain of shitcode](# "CN: 屎山代码 (shǐshān dàimǎ) — tech slang for legacy spaghetti code: fragile, undocumented, horrifyingly convoluted code that somehow still runs, which nobody dares to touch").

Zhou Hui was not the sort of cultivator who coasted on sheer prodigy-level talent to begin with. If he wished to rewrite the Heavenly Dao, he would need prolonged, continuous practice.

Therefore, Zhou Hui resolved to devote a dedicated block of time to deep research.

Indeed. Ever since he first pried into the Heavenly Dao last year, he had been "resting" for such an extended period; it was high time he got some real work done.

Regarding Zhou Hui's decision, the disciples had no surprising reactions whatsoever.

Their Master had always been this way. No matter how lofty one's cultivation realm, it was never an excuse to slack off; there were always endless research projects to tackle. If anything, going an entire year without "running into problems" was already a remarkably long record.

[As expected of Master—even with the power to ascend to the Upper Realm within his grasp, he never relaxes for a single moment.]

Qu Qianxing thought to himself in similar fashion, inquiring respectfully:

—"Then is Master preparing to enter secluded cultivation?"-Qu Qianxing asked respectfully.

—"No. Promoting the Heavenly Spirit Sect's educational curriculum is equally paramount,"-Zhou Hui answered.

Or rather, it served as an indispensable resource for rewriting the Heavenly Dao.

Tampering with the Heavenly Dao might yield an influx of karmic destiny, but it was even more likely to consume vast reserves of it. If Zhou Hui wished to conduct repeated trial-and-error experiments, sufficient karmic destiny was an absolute necessity.

Judging by recent developments in the Southern Domain, standardizing and expanding educational curricula proved to be an exceptionally stable revenue stream for karmic destiny.

—"I shall be keeping a constant eye on this matter,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Furthermore,"-he paused for a brief beat, turning his gaze back toward Qu Qianxing,-"regarding the issue of the Demonic Domain, you may proceed according to your current approach. However, do not overextend yourself. If three of the towers are breached, come find me immediately."

—"Understood, Master,"-Qu Qianxing nodded.

Lu Shulong's eyes widened in disbelief. He had not imagined that Second Senior Brother's scheme to "invade the Demonic Domain" would receive further backing from Master.

Zhou Hui then addressed the remaining disciples:

—"Proceed with the Immortal Arts Assembly and the Grand Auction as planned. However, paramount above all remains the pedagogical work of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the White Jade Capital Academy. Under no circumstances may anyone slack off,"-Zhou Hui instructed the rest.

—"Understood, Master,"-the disciples answered in unison.

—"Master, just leave it all to us!"-Mo Xi chirped with a sunny smile.

Zhou Hui nodded in satisfaction.

***

After briefing his disciples and waiting for them to take their leave, Zhou Hui once again commenced his "practice sessions" in rewriting the Heavenly Dao.

He shifted spatial dimensions, observing the world from an entirely different vantage point.

—"As expected, it is still a complete mess,"-Zhou Hui let out a helpless sigh.

Just as he had witnessed previously when bringing Lu Shulong along, lines and threads of every description were tangled into a chaotic mass, resembling a ball of yarn shredded by a cat, utterly impossible to untangle.

In truth, normally no one would even attempt to untangle it.

Having observed the Goddess's operations, Zhou Hui knew that the so-called "tidying" of the Heavenly Dao practiced by deities amounted to nothing more than injecting a root-level override command to forcibly sever every developmental path they disliked, coercing the world into whatever trajectory the gods desired.

That sort of [blunt, heavy-handed](# "Literal CN: 简单粗暴 (jiǎndān cūbào) — simple and crude / brute force") method was something Zhou Hui fundamentally disliked.

To his mind, the Heavenly Dao had originally been authored by the living mortals and creatures of the earth below. Forcibly violating their collective will to reshape the world into a plaything catering to divine whims was entirely unacceptable—in fact, the emergence of new gods to overthrow the old was precisely the manifestation of mortal resistance reaching its absolute limit.

Of course, an utter absence of governance would not do either.

The current Heavenly Dao of this world was a prime example of an unmanaged mess. Stripped of the hardcoded scripts forcibly patched in by heavenly deities, the remainder was merely the byproduct of unrestrained, chaotic evolution. That was precisely why it had degenerated into such an incoherent snarl—to the point where one could hardly comprehend how it even managed to execute without crashing.

—"A complete rewrite is out of the question; that is virtually indistinguishable from brute-force control. The most critical step is to locate the original baseline architecture,"-Zhou Hui murmured to himself.-"Only then can we optimize upon that foundation."

And on this very problem, the "Heavenly Dao script" strangely provided a clue.

According to Zhou Hui's observations, unlike the synthetic pocket realm of the Goddess, this mortal world had possessed exceptionally close ties to the Heavenly Court in its infancy. Its original architecture was inextricably linked to the Heavenly Court—which was precisely why the deities of the Heavenly Court, despite lacking direct sovereign control over this world, were still able to inject and modify its underlying scripts.

Conversely, by latching onto that very "script," one might be able to excavate the innermost core.

Thinking thus, Zhou Hui began parsing out the extraneous "threads" that did not belong to this world.

This was also the underlying rationale behind Zhou Hui permitting Qu Qianxing to proceed with his scheme to "invade the Demonic Domain":

After all, according to the Goddess, the moment mortal beings birthed intellect and self-awareness, they inevitably spawned inner demons, which in turn spawned ever more demonic horrors. Tracing this thread into the depths could excavate the primordial origins linking the mortal realm to the Demonic Domain, proving immensely beneficial for reconstructing the original baseline architecture.

And so long as Qu Qianxing continued to "invade the Demonic Domain," he was bound to tug on that very thread.

Yet what caught Zhou Hui's attention was far more than that. Strangely enough, while sorting through this "thread," Zhou Hui noticed that Qu Qianxing's spiritual aura felt rather peculiar.

Whether it was due to his entanglement with the Demonic Domain or not, Zhou Hui observed that Qu Qianxing's karmic destiny actually stretched deep into the heart of the Demonic Domain.

*Was this a projection of the past, or did it herald some impending future?*

Regrettably, as a mortal human, Qu Qianxing possessed no memories of past reincarnations. Then again, those memories were hardly pleasant in the first place, and Zhou Hui had no intention of letting him recover them so casually.

—"In any case, let us observe for now,"-Zhou Hui noted down.

A situation remarkably similar to Qu Qianxing's was that of Yuwen Xuan.

However, Yuwen Xuan's circumstances were far easier to diagnose. That Taotie clearly maintained ties to the Heavenly Court—it might very well possess a designated "master," which was a parasitic leash that needed to be severed.

In truth, Yuwen Xuan was far from alone; within the cultivation world, there were several such puppet figurines.

Such as Siwu; such as Goddess Ren Ran.

—"These virus-like contaminants..."-Zhou Hui muttered, glaring unkindly at those parasitic threads.

While tracing them deeper was certainly an option, as Zhou Hui studied them, a "splendid" notion suddenly struck him.

Zhou Hui waved his sleeve, and several scrolls of paper drifted into his hands.

Zhou Hui began to "code."

Although he had no desire to forcibly tamper with the Heavenly Dao belonging to the native beings of his own world, when it came to external invaders, he had zero intention of showing mercy.

Indeed.

Zhou Hui was currently reverse-engineering the Heavenly Scriptures.

Injecting interference into the Upper Realm's "Heavenly Dao"!

From this perspective, Zhou Hui was truly the exact [problem child](# "Literal CN: 问题儿童 (wèntí értóng) — problem child / troublesome kid") that Ancestor Jiuzhai fretted about!

***

Elsewhere, deep in the Abyssal Ocean.

The problem child's number-one problem disciple was currently leading Boss Tao before a colossal structure.

Boss Tao held his breath, his usually massive protruding belly sucking in completely:

—"I-It is actually the [Langhuan Forbidden Grounds](# "Mythology: 琅嬛 (Lánghuán) — the mythical celestial library of the Heavenly Emperor where secret divine books and sacred cosmic records are kept")! To think such a place lay hidden in the depths of the Abyssal Ocean!"-Boss Tao gasped, his breath hitching.

—"Is it truly fine for you to bring me here without informing your master?"-Tao Zhu asked in intense agitation.

—"I felt Boss Tao was the most suitable candidate to handle this,"-Yuwen Xuan replied with a beaming smile.

*What kind of joke was this?!*

[I am here to destroy the evidence and wipe away every trace!]

[How could I ever let Master lay eyes on this thing?!]

Yuwen Xuan thought to himself, [feeling guilty and lacking confidence](# "Literal CN: 心里发虚 (xīnlǐ fāxū) — having a guilty conscience / lacking confidence inside").

Should it be called 'wholly as expected'? After devouring his past memories, Yuwen Xuan had discovered an enormous number of things that Master could never, ever be allowed to know.

For example: he was very likely one of the [sinister masterminds](# "CN: 黑幕 (hēimù) — literally 'black curtain' or 'inside conspiracy'; behind-the-scenes manipulator / architect") behind that Heavenly Dao script...
