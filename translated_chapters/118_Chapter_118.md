# Chapter 118: [Flaming Up](<# "Literal CN: 烧起来了 (shāo qǐlái le) — Flaming Up / Things Are Heating Up / A Flame War Has Begun; chapter title referring to academic drama igniting in the press as rejected powerhouses publish scathing open flame-war exposes in the newspaper">) (第118章 烧起来了)

It took Fairy Ruopei a full [half-shichen](<# "Literal CN: 半个时辰 (bàn gè shíchen) — one hour in modern time">) to finally make sense of what her grand-disciple was rambling about:

—"So the anomaly in the Central Domain was actually caused by the Untiring Immortal Venerable facing his heavenly tribulation?"

—"At the time, you and quite a few fellow cultivators were guests at the Heavenly Spirit Sect, so you witnessed the spectacle, gained insights, and penned them into an article?"

—"And then you wanted their publishing house to print it?"-Fairy Ruopei summarized.

There were simply too many [bizarre points to roast](<# "Literal CN: 槽点太多 (cáodiǎn tài duō) — too many roastable points / absurdities / things to complain about">) and far too many crucial revelations packed together, leaving her with a mountain of things to say and an ocean of questions to interrogate—yet she had no idea where to even begin.

However, one thing was certain: what Bo Jiu had been whining about was undoubtedly the most utterly trivial aspect of the entire affair.

Yet the man himself didn't feel that way at all. With dead earnestness, he corrected her:

—"Wrong! It's not a common 'insight,' it's an [academic thesis](<# "Literal CN: 论文 (lùnwén) — research paper / academic thesis; modern academia concept ported into Xianxia by Zhou Hui">)! Nor is it a mere publishing house, but an academic journal! This directly concerns my future standing within the academic circles of the Central Domain and the Organizing Committee!"-Bo Jiu insisted.

*Theses? Journals? Standing in the academic circle? What in the world was that ghost?*

[The tally of incomprehensible vocabulary had increased once again.](<# "Internet meme: 听不懂的名词增加了 — More jargon I don't understand has been added to the pile">)

By this point, Fairy Ruopei was ninety percent certain that her grand-disciple's brain had been fried silly by heavenly lightning.

She had half a mind to find other cultivators in the Central Domain to ask instead. But watching her grand-disciple bathed in snot and tears, looking as if he might commit suicide at any second, Fairy Ruopei conceded:

—"Fine, fine. Let me take a look at that... what-do-you-call-it 'insight'... thesis of yours,"-Fairy Ruopei said.

Perhaps from this "insight," she might glean what had actually taken place back then.

—"You want to see it?!"-Bo Jiu gasped in horror.

Even though he was the one who had started complaining in the first place, the moment Fairy Ruopei asked to read it, his expression twisted into the nervous dread of a student having his homework inspected by a teacher.

Clutching his thesis papers, he stammered:

—"Y-You want to read it? B-But you are all the way in the Northern Domain, and there is no way to hand it over to you right now. Wouldn't reading it like this be a bit too troublesome?"-Bo Jiu mumbled.

—"Then forget it,"-Fairy Ruopei said coldly.-"If you have nothing else to report, I shall go look up someone else. Fairy Xia in Cloud City should be far more..."

—"Wait! Grandmaster!"-Bo Jiu cried out. In the end, he truly could not bear to linger in [manuscript revision hell](<# "Literal CN: 改稿地狱 (gǎi gǎo dìyù) — paper revision hell; the unending torment of endless reviewer revisions">) any longer.-"Grandmaster... please, do take a look for me!"

As he spoke, the sheets of his thesis manuscript floated into the air, wriggling like elongated caterpillars before the mirror's focus.

Just as Bo Jiu had said, trying to read a manuscript like this was thoroughly inconvenient.

Yet before the cultivation world developed superior long-distance telecommunications, deploying a supreme spiritual treasure of the Moon Phase Mirror's caliber for such a purpose was already an exorbitant luxury.

Fairy Ruopei forced herself to read on with patient endurance.

Unfortunately, she failed to find what she originally sought—such as a description of how the Untiring Immortal Venerable had crossed his tribulation or what havoc it had wrought across the Central Domain. Just as Bo Jiu had said, this was not an "insight," but a "thesis."

It was a dead-serious paper classifying the various types of heavenly lightning in tribulation, critiquing their destructive power, and including analyses of tribulation-crossing methods and corresponding defensive artifacts.

It was... [thoroughly mediocre](<# "Literal CN: 很普 (hěn pǔ) — very run-of-the-mill / completely basic / thoroughly mediocre; internet slang">).

Granted, for a Void Refinement cultivator, possessing this degree of understanding regarding heavenly lightning was commendable. But looking at it from another angle, there wasn't a single innovative perspective inside—it was all material Fairy Ruopei had read centuries ago.

[Did you truly witness the Untiring Immortal Venerable crossing his tribulation?]

[From the heavenly tribulation of a supreme Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse, this was the only meager scrap of insight you managed to produce?]

Fairy Ruopei cast a suspicious glance at her grand-disciple. Even if she had not personally witnessed the Untiring Immortal Venerable's tribulation, simply looking at what the Central Domain had been turned into made it obvious that the circumstances were anything but ordinary. Yet her own grand-disciple was still mechanically analyzing according to classical textbook teachings on tribulation lightning, making one wonder whether the brain inside his skull was merely an ornamental decoration.

If there was anything novel about this "thesis," it was its formatting.

In the past, most research on cultivation techniques in the cultivation world resembled vague "epiphanies," cluttered with excessive amounts of extraneous fluff. With some cultivators' manuals, one couldn't even tell whether the author was writing his autobiography or genuinely attempting to teach a technique—less than ten percent of the text contained anything useful. If a fledgling cultivator blindly attempted to practice according to such texts, learning nothing was the best-case scenario; more often than not, it would [lead them straight to their deaths](<# "Literal CN: 把人坑死 (bǎ rén kēng sǐ) —坑 / screw people over fatally / get people killed">).

Whoever had devised the structure of this thesis—Ruopei certainly didn't believe her useless grand-disciple could have invented it—had made the focal points razor-sharp. Even though it felt like [a newly branded method of padding word count](<# "Literal CN: 换了一种水的方式 (huàn le yī zhǒng shuǐ de fāngshì) — switched to a different method of watering/padding">), at least it never drifted too far off topic.

Fairy Ruopei followed the thesis to straighten out her own line of thought, clarifying how she ought to question him.

Yet before that, her grand-disciple was still gazing at her with a foolish, expectant grin:

—"Venerable Grandmaster, what do you think?"-Bo Jiu asked.

—"Rest assured,"-Fairy Ruopei replied.-"Even if this gets published, it will not exert the slightest influence upon your standing in that whatever-circle."

[—"!"](<# "Speaker: Bo Jiu">)

Bo Jiu's face instantly collapsed into [the bitterness of a dried gourd](<# "Literal CN: 一脸苦瓜相 (yī liǎn kǔguā xiàng) — a bitter gourd face; wearing a mournful, distressed expression">).

Looking like [a drowned dog](<# "Literal CN: 落水狗 (luòshuǐgǒu) — a dog fallen into water; utterly pitiful and wretched">), he pleaded:

—"Grandmaster, where on earth did I write it poorly?"-Bo Jiu whined.

—"This is no longer a question of good or bad,"-Fairy Ruopei sighed.-"Do you possess not even an ounce of original thought? Take this section on tribulation lightning classifications, for instance: you witnessed the Untiring Immortal Venerable's lightning with your own eyes, did you not? Was his lightning truly as standard as the textbooks describe?"

—"No, it was the most bizarre lightning I have ever witnessed in my life..."-Bo Jiu admitted. However, in order to force the types and colors to align with the examples found in the cultivation realm's classical treatises on lightning tribulations, he had exerted tremendous effort to [ana(forcefully)lyze(cobble) and cla(together)ssify](<# "Literal CN: 分（强）析（拼）处（硬）理（凑） — ana(force)-lyze(cobble) pro(hard)-cess(shoehorn); author comedic wordplay splitting 'analysis and processing' (分析处理) with 'forcefully cobbling together' (强拼硬凑)">) them.

—"Are you an idiot? At a time like this, you ought to be bold!"-Fairy Ruopei reprimanded sharply.-"Tell me everything you saw during that heavenly tribulation in detail—"

—"Back then..."-Bo Jiu began.

***

And so, it was not until three full days had passed that Fairy Ruopei finally reappeared before the assembled cultivators of the Northern Domain.

Her haggard, utterly drained appearance gave everyone a profound fright:

—"Is the situation in the Central Domain truly so dire? To think it exhausted Fairy's mind and spirit to such an extent!"-one cultivator gasped.

—"No, the Central Domain... is fine,"-Fairy Ruopei replied in a daze.

Seeing the expressions of utter disbelief on everyone's faces, she could only let out a bitter smile, at a complete loss for how to explain.

She could hardly admit that she had initially intended to inquire about the Central Domain's affairs while "incidentally" tutoring her grand-disciple, only to grow more and more irritable as she taught, until in the end she simply seized the "thesis" to write it herself—and even spent half an eternity fixing the formatting!

Naturally, when a dignified Mahayana supreme powerhouse stepped onto the field, it was [a dimensional strike from a higher plane](<# "Literal CN: 越级打击 (yuèjí dǎjī) — punching down across cultivation realms / dimensional smiting">).

According to Bo Jiu's report, a mere fifteen minutes after submitting the thesis, it had passed the "preliminary review"—yet another brand-new term, though Fairy Ruopei no longer had the energy to decipher what it meant.

Ruopei desperately wanted to rest, but knowing a whole crowd was eagerly awaiting her findings, she had no choice but to brace herself and come out to deal with them.

Fairy Ruopei skipped the explanation entirely and reported the conclusion directly:

—"The Central Domain is truly fine... It was merely the Untiring Immortal Venerable facing his heavenly tribulation."

—"A heavenly tribulation? A tribulation produced such a terrifying commotion?!"-someone exclaimed in shock.-"Then did the Immortal Venerable... ascend?"

That was phrased as auspiciously as possible.

To cultivators, after such an apocalyptic cosmological anomaly, if one failed to ascend, it was practically synonymous with perishing entirely.

—"He did not ascend,"-Fairy Ruopei let out a faint, melancholic sigh.

The cultivator paused, a mournful expression washing over his face:

—"Is that so? Our cultivation world has lost yet another mighty elder."

—"And here I thought the Untiring Immortal Venerable was the cultivator most likely to ascend! He was always so hardworking!"

—"In the end, it seems he was still lacking that sliver of karmic opportunity!"

—"...Wait a moment, what is with this mourning routine of yours?!"-Fairy Ruopei barked, catching on a beat late.-"The Untiring Immortal Venerable is alive and kicking! He even captured the heavenly lightning and put 'samples' on public exhibition!"

That was also the true reason behind Fairy Ruopei's sigh.

She was eaten up with bitter envy! She cursed the heavens that she hadn't possessed the karmic luck to personally observe such an unprecedented tribulation spectacle, while her dull-witted grand-disciple stood right there and couldn't even write a proper thesis!

Hearing Fairy Ruopei's words, the cultivators exchanged bewildered looks:

—"Your meaning is that this magnificent celestial phenomenon was caused by Untiring Immortal Venerable's heavenly tribulation? And he survived it, and even captured the tribulation lightning... Isn't that a successful tribulation? Why didn't he ascend?!"

—"I told you, he captured the heavenly lightning, so naturally the spatial corridor could not open,"-Fairy Ruopei said.

The cultivators grew all the more astounded.

One of them blurted out:

—"Does that not mean that so long as the Untiring Immortal Venerable releases the heavenly lightning, he can open the ascension corridor at any time?!"

—"A splendid research direction! Far superior to that idiotic topic of 'Study on the Types of Tribulation Lightning'!"-Fairy Ruopei pointed directly at the man and bellowed.

The air froze in excruciating awkwardness.

After a moment of dead silence, Fairy Ruopei covered her face in mortification:

—"Forgive me. I have gone slightly [possessed](<# "Literal CN: 魔怔了 (mózhèng le) — gone mad / obsessed / brain-fried by a single fixated topic">)."

Helping her grand-disciple "revise" his paper over the past few days had truly caused her brain to shut down, forcing every single thought to veer in that bizarre direction.

Fairy Ruopei took a deep breath, striving to maintain a normal demeanor:

—"I shall display the scenes of the Central Domain observed through the Moon Phase Mirror for you. However, the Untiring Immortal Venerable tidied up the aftermath splendidly; word has it that all effects of the heavenly tribulation were confined within the borders of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. And due to their mountain-protecting grand array, I cannot peek inside at will without the master's consent. As for the rest of the Central Domain, nothing appears amiss."

As she spoke, she magnified the view within the Moon Phase Mirror. Sure enough, the Central Domain looked no different from ordinary days.

Except that rain was falling everywhere.

—"Spiritual rain?!"-the cultivators recognized it at a glance, exclaiming in wonder.

—"Indeed, that is the aftermath of the heavenly tribulation,"-Fairy Ruopei nodded.-"According to cultivators who were on site, this was the final phenomenon that manifested. They speculate that the ascension corridor must have cracked open for a brief instant at that moment."

The expressions of the cultivators turned increasingly shocked, mingled with acute regret.

Earlier, hearing that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had crossed his tribulation, people had marveled, but as one of the very pinnacle cultivators in the entire world—and the one most devoted to cultivating for ascension—he was the sort where hearing one day that he had ascended or died in tribulation would surprise no one; at most, they would offer a few mournful sighs as they just had.

But spiritual rain generated by a heavenly tribulation was a monumental blessing that benefited an entire domain!

Many cultivators felt their hearts itch with longing, desperately regretting that they hadn't been in the Central Domain.

Now, they were even more impatient to learn every detail of the Immortal Venerable's tribulation:

—"Regarding Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's tribulation, could you describe it in greater detail?"

—"Indeed! What happened back then? Wasn't it said that the heavenly lightning was captured? Where did the spiritual rain come from? What caused such a spectacular celestial phenomenon?"

—"Who exactly was at the Heavenly Spirit Sect at the time?"-this one was determined to gather first-hand intelligence himself.

Fairy Ruopei's head throbbed from the barrage of questions:

—"Wait, wait! Even if I were to describe the details, my account would hardly be comprehensive... Ah, right! You can go find Lord Mei and Lady Zhu!"

The cultivators were thoroughly dissatisfied with this brush-off:

—"Lord Mei and Lady Zhu are swamped right now,"-they protested. It was precisely because the Heavenly Dao Alliance had no clue what had happened that they had come seeking Fairy Ruopei in the first place!

Fairy Ruopei clearly knew this.

—"Lord Mei and Lady Zhu have clearly pursued the wrong direction, but that does not matter,"-Fairy Ruopei said.-"I am not telling you to ask them to describe what happened, but to request that they order an academic journal called *Spells*. It has already been confirmed that many research papers regarding the heavenly tribulation will be published within."

One could only pray those papers wouldn't all be of the same dismal caliber as her grand-disciple's.

—"Papers? Journals?"-the cultivators echoed in confusion.

Just like Fairy Ruopei previously, they were completely bewildered.

However, with so many cultivators gathered, there were bound to be well-informed individuals among them:

—"Are you referring to the Central Domain's *Spells* journal? I thought that was founded purely for the Immortal Arts Assembly!"

—"I happen to have a copy right here,"-another cultivator pulled out an inaugural issue.-"My junior brother mailed this over, telling me to read it as casual entertainment. I haven't even had the chance to look through it yet!"

The cultivators paid no heed to his explanation and crowded around to pass it along.

The first group to read froze right on the spot:

—"Ah, it is an article by Master Qingyang of Eternal Villa!"

—"A powerhouse is truly a powerhouse—it is written with such lucidity!"

—"To think there exists such an understanding regarding True Immortals! Eternal Villa truly lives up to its heritage as the bloodline of White Jade Capital; it seems they genuinely held back some profound secrets."

—"Who would have thought the Central Domain would publish such an 'entertaining' publication? Isn't doing all this for a mere game slightly over the top?"

—"Do not underestimate this publication; it was founded by the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself. Who knows if he harbors deeper intentions?"

—"Hey, stop hogging it to yourselves!"

—"Wait a moment, based on the nature of this publication, does this not imply that high-ranking cultivators across the Central Domain will publish analyses of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's tribulation?!"

—"Heavens! Was that what you meant all along, Lady Ruopei?!"

The crowd of cultivators erupted into chaotic chatter.

They all turned to look toward Fairy Ruopei, only to discover that she was already treading atop an immortal cloud, flying straight in the direction of the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

***

—"Purchase the *Spells* journal?!"

When [Xin Zhu](<# "Literal CN: 辛烛 (Xīn Zhú) — Lady Zhu; senior leader of the Northern Domain Heavenly Dao Alliance, wife of Lord Mei, and a supreme Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse">) heard this request, she was utterly baffled.

Over the past few days, she had roughly investigated the cause behind the Central Domain's anomaly, though she hadn't yet found the time to inquire with Zhou Hui personally. Right at that moment, she saw Fairy Ruopei leading a swarm of cultivators storming in with aggressive momentum.

She had assumed this mob had come to interrogate her about the Central Domain as well! Yet when they opened their mouths, they actually wanted her help procuring the *Spells* journal from Zhou Hui!

Fairy Ruopei even attempted an explanation:

—"The *Spells* journal is a publication founded by Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable dedicated to the research of spellcraft..."

—"I know. You are referring to this, yes?"-Xin Zhu waved her sleeve toward a bookshelf behind her.

Several magazines intermixed with newspapers floated down onto Xin Zhu's desk.

Fairy Ruopei stared in astonishment, discovering that issues from the inaugural volume to the latest release were all present. In addition, there were unbound newsprint broadsheets uniformly titled *White Jade Capital Weekly*. The front page blazoned headline news covering major affairs across the Central Domain, appearing exceedingly fascinating.

Fairy Ruopei tore her gaze away with immense difficulty to avoid reading further.

She marveled:

—"Truly worthy of the Heavenly Dao Alliance! So everything has been under Lord Mei and Lady Zhu's watchful eye all along?"

—"Hardly. The journals were mailed over by Zhou Hui; it seems he gifted copies to all Tribulation Transcendence cultivators and the Mahayana figures he is relatively familiar with,"-Xin Zhu said.

What she didn't mention was that *White Jade Capital Weekly* was something she had subscribed to herself—and she had ordered three copies.

Her foolish husband and son were constantly squabbling with her over the papers, and once finished, they never returned them to their proper place, wandering off to share them with their [pack of disreputable cronies](<# "Literal CN: 狐朋狗友 (hú péng gǒu yǒu) — fox-friends and dog-cronies; disreputable drinking buddies / rowdy clique">) until her collection was missing half its issues. That was the sole reason she had specifically ordered multiple subscriptions.

Ruopei's arrival served as a timely reminder.

—"Indeed. Rather than asking Zhou Hui directly, reading the journals and newspapers is actually faster,"-Xin Zhu muttered to herself.-"That fellow Zhou Hui is always far too concise whenever anyone asks him anything, acting as if whatever he knows ought to be common knowledge to the rest of us... *Tsk!* Please wait a moment!"

Her final sentence was directed toward Fairy Ruopei and the others.

With that, Xin Zhu retreated into the inner chamber to retrieve her water mirror and contact the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Unlike Fairy Ruopei, whose contact had caused her to vanish for three days, Xin Zhu returned in no time at all, carrying a stack of newspapers and a journal manuscript.

—"This is the latest issue of *White Jade Capital Weekly*. However, because everyone else across the Central Domain was equally desperate to learn what occurred, word has it that the moment the papers reached the retail storefronts, they were instantly sold out. This was the final stock preserved at the *White Jade Capital Weekly* editorial office; I hear they are currently rushing reprints,"-Xin Zhu explained.

She pulled out three sheets from the [steaming-hot papers](<# "Literal CN: 热腾腾的报纸 (rètēngtēng de bàozhǐ) — steaming-hot newspapers; fresh off the magical printing press">). After a second thought, she took another half of the remaining stack. She paused again, and took yet another half of what remained.

Finally, Xin Zhu set down the pitifully thin final few sheets before Fairy Ruopei and the cultivators.

*How infuriating!*

Yet the woman was a supreme Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse—what recourse did they have?

Some cultivators snatched up those few newspapers with lightning speed, terrified Xin Zhu might change her mind, and privately began an ["elegant and quiet" scramble](<# "Literal CN: 优雅且安静的抢夺 (yōuyǎ qiě ānjìng de qiǎngduó) — dignified yet ruthless silent tussle among high-ranking cultivators trying to read first">) over reading rights.

Although Ruopei desperately wished to join the fray, she reined in her desires, fixing her eyes solely upon the manuscript Xin Zhu held in her hand, suspected to be the journal proofs.

It was not because she was so deeply enamored with scholarship, but because within it lay the paper she had (helped her disciple "revise").

—"Apologies, but you cannot view this one,"-Xin Zhu stated.-"According to Zhou Hui, these are merely manuscripts that passed the preliminary review. They must still undergo peer review before they can be approved for publication; I cannot disclose them to unrelated persons."

Speaking of this, Xin Zhu felt a mixture of exasperation and amusement.

As an "unrelated person," she originally had no right to view them either. The only reason Xin Zhu had obtained them was because she had agreed to assist Zhou Hui as an [academic reviewer](<# "Literal CN: 审核人 (shěnhérén) — peer reviewer / journal referee">).

When Zhou Hui initially mailed them the journals, he probably harbored this very calculation all along!

Right now was merely the opportune moment to reel her in.

Fairy Ruopei was entirely oblivious to the "covert dealings" transpiring between supreme Tribulation Transcendence figures. Suppressing her anxious impatience, she inquired:

—"Then when will it be formally published?"

—"Rest easy, it will be very soon,"-Xin Zhu comforted her.-"Word has it that, just as you predicted, this issue of *Spells* consists mostly of papers written by cultivators who observed Zhou Hui's tribulation. Because it is unrelated to the Immortal Arts Assembly and constitutes an unexpected emergency, they plan to produce it as a [special supplementary issue](<# "Literal CN: 增刊 (zēngkān) — supplementary issue / special issue / zengkan">) alongside *White Jade Capital Weekly*. You should be able to see it in half a month."

Yet *White Jade Capital Weekly* had already hit the shelves, while *Spells* was still trapped in review!

Ruopei felt a twinge of dissatisfaction.

Still, considering that she had spent three whole days merely (helping) write the paper, a lengthier review process was understandable—especially when it was being reviewed by a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse like Xin Zhu.

Moreover, given the Untiring Immortal Venerable's relentless diligence, he would undoubtedly review the manuscripts personally. Furthermore, when contacting Bo Jiu, she had heard that Patriarch Jiuzhai, the supreme Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse of the Southern Domain, was also a guest at the Heavenly Spirit Sect—could he be reviewing manuscripts as well?

[My own manuscript was going to be peer-reviewed by Tribulation Transcendence supremes spanning three domains?!]

The moment this thought crossed Ruopei's mind, she couldn't help but feel a surge of thrilling excitement mixed with nervous anxiety.

—"Lady Xin Zhu! Once this issue of *Spells* is published, you must notify me at the very first moment!"-Ruopei pleaded, trembling as she clasped Xin Zhu's hand.

Xin Zhu shot Ruopei a bewildered look, failing to comprehend why the other woman was so overwrought.

—"In truth, if you wish to understand what happened, I believe the newspaper is far more suitable,"-Xin Zhu advised.-"I feel those academic papers have digressed quite far into the weeds."

—"It does not matter. I wish to see it at the earliest possible instant!"-Ruopei declared with unyielding resolve.

***

In truth, Fairy Ruopei did not have to wait half a month. Because during the [third round of review](<# "Literal CN: 三审 (sān shěn) — tertiary review / final review stage in peer review">), Bo Jiu's "thesis" was [slapped with a rejection](<# "Literal CN: 打回来了 (dǎ huílái le) — sent back / desk-rejected / rejected by reviewers">) after all.

—"On what grounds?!"-Fairy Ruopei shrieked.

Her sonic wave carried offensive power right through the mirror, forcing Bo Jiu to clamp his hands over his ears.

Perhaps because this paper had fundamentally been [ghostwritten by his advisor (X)](<# "Literal CN: 导师（X）代笔 (dǎoshī dài bǐ) — thesis advisor / mentor ghostwriting for the student; academic parody with (X) striking out mentor to imply grandmaster">), Bo Jiu's mindset had become completely serene, practically [zen](<# "Literal CN: 佛系 (fóxì) — Buddha-like / indifferent slacker mindset / unbothered">). He attempted to soothe his grandmaster:

—"I hear there were simply too many brilliant submissions, and they weren't necessarily penned by the authors themselves. For example, a certain cultivator hailing from the [Feisha Sect](<# "Literal CN: 飞沙门 (Fēishā Mén) — Flying Sand Sect; prominent cultivation sect in the Western Domain">) in the Western Domain obviously recruited his master to—"

—"Are you saying I am inferior to his grandmaster?!"-Fairy Ruopei glared with wide eyes.

Daojun Bo Jiu snapped straight as a board, shaking his head stiffly.

—"Granted, I know the thesis topic itself was [dogshit](<# "Literal CN: 狗X (gǒu X) — dogshit; self-censored profanity">), but given how brilliantly I revised it, it shouldn't have been rejected!"-Fairy Ruopei muttered indignantly.-"Even if other Mahayana cultivators got involved, could they truly write better than me?!"

Bo Jiu, who had chosen the topic: "..."

—"Unacceptable! I must demand a clear explanation!"-Fairy Ruopei muttered darkly under her breath.

Ignoring her grand-disciple still waiting on the other side of the Moon Phase Mirror, she charged aggressively all the way back to the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

—"So that article was written by your... disciple?"-Xin Zhu exclaimed in surprise. However, since the piece had already been rejected, she didn't mind disclosing a bit of insider news.-"I personally passed you, but your... disciple's thesis arguments were deemed too obsolete by certain individuals..."

—"Certain individuals? Which individuals?!"-Ruopei demanded, glaring fiercely at Xin Zhu.

Even though Ruopei's cultivation lagged far behind Xin Zhu's, Xin Zhu still subconsciously took half a step back.

Xin Zhu let out a wry smile:

—"In matters like this, it is best not to get to the bottom of everything. I feel your proficiency is not far behind the others in this issue. Revise a few details; while you missed this round, you should be able to make the next issue."

—"Understood!"-Ruopei spat coldly.

[What did you understand?]

Xin Zhu wondered in bewilderment. Looking at Ruopei's departing back, nothing about her posture suggested she was going home to revise a paper.

And indeed, Fairy Ruopei did not revise the paper.

Instead, she appeared in *White Jade Capital Weekly*.

Under the pen name ["Fake Fish"](<# "Literal CN: 伪鱼 (Wěi Yú) — Fake Fish / Counterfeit Fish; pen name adopted by Fairy Ruopei, mocking the Bo Fish (薄鱼) and her grand-disciple Bo Jiu (薄九)">), Fairy Ruopei took to the newspaper, calling out a whole flock of *Spells* journal peer reviewers by name and cursing them up and down, while simultaneously [strafing](<# "Literal CN: 扫射 (sǎoshè) — spray-firing / strafing; internet slang for attacking an entire group or demographic indiscriminately">) the crowd of mighty elders who had ghostwritten theses for their disciples.

—"Ah, I forgot: even if I didn't tell her, she could look through the Moon Phase Mirror and see for herself,"-Xin Zhu realized in hindsight.

Holding the newspaper, she sighed in mock distress:

—"How am I going to explain this to Zhou Hui?"

Though her words sounded troubled, her face was completely plastered with [pure popcorn-munching drama spectator glee](<# "Literal CN: 看热闹 (kàn rènao) — watching the bustling circus / spectator enjoying front-row drama">).

Ever since stepping into the Tribulation Transcendence realm, it had been an extraordinarily long time since she had enjoyed such delightful drama.
