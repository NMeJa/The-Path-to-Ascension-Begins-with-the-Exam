# Chapter 147: Passing the Buck (第147章 一些甩锅)

[← Previous Chapter](146_Chapter_146.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](148_Chapter_148.md)

---

—"This isn't my fault,"-Lu Shulong said.

Qu Qianxing was provoked to laughter by sheer exasperation:

—"Not your fault? Did this rift sprout out on its own, then?"

—"There was a hole there to begin with!"-Lu Shulong attempted to pass the buck, but under Qu Qianxing's stern glare, his voice dwindled to a murmur.-"I merely... accidentally enlarged it a tiny bit..."

—"It's all my fault!"-At that moment, Jian Nuo stepped forward voluntarily.-"It only turned out like this because my 'friends' were coming."

—"Heh,"-Qu Qianxing glared at them, letting out a frigid sneer.

Lu Shulong and Jian Nuo instantly shrank their shoulders. Lu Shulong even took a step backward, while Jian Nuo held his ground with a stiff neck, looking as though he were gallantly trying to shield Lu Shulong.

At this spectacle, the face of Qingyang Qingyu's maid twitched violently.

For reasons unknown, it suddenly reminded her of a scene from a popular storybook she had read recently: the male head of the household confronting his primary wife to defend his concubine.

The resemblance in their aura, above all, was uncanny:

A dignified, aristocratic, yet incandescently furious primary wife;

A delicate, ethereal, yet hopelessly unrepentant concubine;

And a master of the house desperately trying [to smooth things over](# "Literal CN: 和稀泥 (hé xīní) — 'mixing mud'; muddling through or playing mediator to gloss over conflicts").

Alas, she was a professional; she could not burst out laughing on an occasion like this.

Besides, on closer reflection, the situation wasn't amusing in the slightest.

First of all, that master of the house attempting to smooth things over was very likely to become their Young Miss's husband—which meant the master of *their* household.

Secondly, the place where a hole had been punctured... no, where a spatial rift had been ripped wide open, was Qingyang Clan territory—the very secret realm their Young Miss was currently renovating!

And finally, most fatal of all, this prospective "master" had attempted to drag his gang of disreputable cronies there, exposing this rift for all to see!

Thinking up to this point, the gaze the maid directed at Jian Nuo was infused with venomous hatred.

Indeed, she too believed that this entire disaster was Jian Nuo's fault.

Yet the entire ordeal had indeed originated with Jian Nuo.

The story traced back half a month prior (in this world's time), just after the Heavenly Spirit Sect had concluded the academy's spirit root testing in White Jade Capital.

At the time, Zhou Hui was supervising Shen Qingqian's thesis writing—an ordeal that, frankly, was excruciating even for the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Sect Leader Shen wrote in agony, and Zhou Hui guided her in even greater agony.

After all, some knowledge existed solely in Shen Qingqian's brain... No, Zhou Hui suspected Shen Qingqian possessed no such organ at all; her techniques, her cultivation methods, and her martial skills were entirely stored inside her muscles!

Synthesizing the core of cultivation from a person who had no earthly idea what she actually knew required vastly more patience than inventing an entirely new manual from scratch—and delivered zero sense of accomplishment. Only Zhou Hui possessed the superhuman patience required to communicate with Shen Qingqian.

As a result, Zhou Hui naturally had no spare time to entertain Jian Nuo.

Not that a senior of the Untiring Immortal Venerable's stature should ever have had to entertain a junior in the first place; even if Jian Haoge had arrived in person, he would hardly have received such red-carpet treatment. (Jian Haoge: *Hmph, as if I would ever visit Zhou Hui's house as a guest!*)

However, Qu Qianxing was also swamped.

Following the successful spirit root assessment, countless cultivators clamored for the White Jade Capital Academy's textbooks. Qu Qianxing had seized the momentum to propose tours of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's pedagogical methods, leaving him spinning like a top with zero time to chaperone Jian Nuo around.

Going down the list of direct disciples: Ran Deng was half-dead from her soul fire ordeal; Guan Ye and Mo Bei were fundamentally unsuited by temperament for social hosting; and Mo Xi was submerged in endless preparations for the Immortal Arts Assembly grand finals—a massive event held alongside an auction and the Immortals' Fair, requiring monumental logistical effort.

In the end, the task of hosting Jian Nuo fell squarely onto Lu Shulong's shoulders.

—"Actually, you could ask Senior Sister Qingyu to host you,"-Lu Shulong had told Jian Nuo at the time with a sullen, resentful expression.-"Don't you have feelings for her? Having her show you around would be the perfect chance to kindle affection."

—"As lovely as that sounds, absolutely not!"-Jian Nuo replied resolutely.-"Qingyu has her own work to do. I didn't come rushing here just to become a nuisance to her."

[...So being a nuisance to *me* is totally fine?!]

Lu Shulong stared coldly at Jian Nuo. Just as Jian Nuo was about to add something more, an attendant hurried over and handed a letter into Jian Nuo's hand.

Skimming the contents, Jian Nuo's face instantly contorted as though he were nursing a severe toothache.

He turned back to Lu Shulong, asking sheepishly:

—"Would you mind hosting a few more people?"

[—"?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

—"A few of my 'friends' are heading this way,"-Jian Nuo said with a wry grimace.

Jian Nuo watched Lu Shulong's gaze turn frigid enough to freeze water, eyeing him as though evaluating a heap of unwanted clutter.

Yet Jian Nuo was entirely helpless.

To keep up appearances and mask his true motives, the social circle he cultivated consisted of thoroughly disreputable figures.

There were undercover spies from the Palace of Carefree Wandering, as well as outrageously spoiled second-generation cultivators. A cocktail of those two varieties was terrifying in the extreme.

Jian Nuo knew this all too well.

That was why he had bolted to the Central Domain without breathing a single word to them. Outwardly, slipping away fit his persona as a dissolute, untamed playboy; in truth, he simply hadn't wanted that pack of troublemakers tagging along.

Yet whether because their brains had short-circuited or because someone had deliberately egged them on, they had actually pursued him all the way here! Worse still, Jian Nuo had no way to dissuade them, because they had slipped out in secret—several having literally run away from home—intending to present Jian Nuo with a "pleasant surprise."

Fortunately, as the heir to Sky City, Jian Nuo possessed no shortage of eyes and ears across the city.

The moment those "friends" departed Sky City, Jian Nuo had been alerted.

Yet because they hadn't formally notified him, he had no pretext to order them back. Thus, he could only look toward Lu Shulong with an apologetic wince.

—"Those fellows are indeed too willful; even I never anticipated they would chase after me,"-Jian Nuo floated a suggestion.-"Honestly, there's no need to fuss over entertaining them. As long as we keep them from getting up to bizarre mischief, it'll be fine. Alternatively... we could arrange an exam for them."

—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect's examinations aren't something just anyone can take!"-Lu Shulong snapped in exasperation.

That was an examination question bank painstakingly crafted by Master himself for his disciples! Did this Young Master Jian truly believe any [stray dog or cat](# "Literal CN: 阿猫阿狗 (ā māo ā gǒu) — riff-raff; any random nobody") could peer at those questions?

Besides, the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the White Jade Capital Academy had already wrapped up their testing and were currently on summer vacation. What kind of logic would dictate holding a special exam just for them?

Still, they couldn't simply be left to run wild.

Furious as Lu Shulong was, he knew better than to leave a pack of [unruly brats](# "Literal CN: 熊孩子 (xióng hái zi) — 'bear kids'; spoiled, undisciplined children who run amok") completely unsupervised.

After all, across seven full lifetimes, his senior and junior martial brothers and sisters had shown Lu Shulong precisely what catastrophic heights spoiled brats could achieve when allowed to run amok—not to mention that he himself was an unruly [brat of a dragon](# "Literal CN: 熊（龙）孩子 — pun on 'bear child' (spoiled brat) and dragon child").

Letting those clowns cause chaos was out of the question.

In particular, this was currently a critical window for the Heavenly Spirit Sect [to peddle courses](# "Literal CN: 卖课 (mài kè) — internet slang for selling commercial courses/educational packages"). Second Senior Brother was straining every nerve to demonstrate the superiority of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's modern pedagogical model to various sect leaders, promoting their textbooks and teaching methods far and wide.

If a mob of privileged cultivation scions swooped in at this exact moment, scored [like a pile of crap](# "Literal CN: 像是一坨x似的 — uncensored internet slang for a pile of shit"), and wound up afflicted with traumatic psychological scarring like a certain someone, was that not deliberately sabotaging the business? Was it wrecking the venue or wrecking the venue?

Lu Shulong pondered for a moment, deciding not to aggravate Second Senior Brother further.

—"I know of a place,"-Lu Shulong finally proposed after some deliberation.-"It's ideal for entertaining guests, and it'll let them have the time of their lives."

—"Honestly, there's no need to make them *too* happy,"-Jian Nuo sighed.-"What place is this?"

—"Senior Sister Qingyu's place—"

—"I just told you, I will not disturb Qingyu,"-Jian Nuo's expression instantly sobered.

He couldn't even bear to disturb Qingyu himself, let alone let his gang of worthless scoundrels near her.

In fact, even back in Sky City, he had never permitted that crowd [to prance around](# "Literal CN: 舞到面前 (wǔ dào miànqián) — internet slang: flaunting or posturing right in front of someone's face") before Qingyu.

At Jian Nuo's hyper-protective reaction, Lu Shulong cast him a faint, knowing smirk:

—"Rest easy, it won't trouble Senior Sister Qingyu in the least. If anything, Senior Sister Qingyu happens to be in dire need of test subjects... no, closed-beta testers."

—"Test subjects? Closed-beta testers?"-Jian Nuo asked, utterly baffled.

---

—"Wait, wait a second! Isn't this a spatial fissure leading to the Demon Realm?!"

A few minutes later, Jian Nuo grabbed Lu Shulong by the arm, hissing in a hushed yet deeply alarmed query.

They were currently inside the Qingyang Clan's Spirit Stone Secret Realm.

When Jian Nuo arrived, a cluster of Qingyang Clan disciples was huddled inside an elevated command room, peering downward through a transparent glazed wall. One was tensely sliding something across a table, while the others crowded around offering tactical backseat commentary:

[—"Put an arrow tower over there!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Clan Disciple")

[—"Build a fire-element restriction first!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Clan Disciple")

[—"Quick, quick, that one is about to breach the command center! Deploy the 'Heroic Spirit'!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Clan Disciple")

[—"The defensive grid is paper-thin and you haven't placed a hero yet! What are you doing?!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Clan Disciple")

This atmosphere... felt remarkably similar to an Immortal Chess parlor?

Yet this wasn't how Immortal Chess was played. Was it a similar sort of game?

Although Jian Nuo didn't play Immortal Chess much himself, he considered it rather wonderful that his disreputable friends were addicted to it. It was far healthier than their previous vices and offered educational benefits; at the very least, those pampered wastrels—who usually relied on hired escorts to carry them through secret realms—had managed to memorize several secret realm topography classifications.

Perhaps a novel game could distract and pacify them... couldn't it?

Just as Jian Nuo was entertaining the thought, he walked over to the glazed wall beside the control console and glanced downward.

Then, he froze stock-still.

A swarm of colossal insects was flapping its wings, flying through slender, labyrinthine corridors below.

They were monstrously grotesque.

Not only did these bugs boast elongated antennae and oily, lustrous wings, but their heads were topped with contorted human faces that continually emitted eerie wails resembling human infants!

A monstrosity this twisted had to be of the demon race!

It was definitely a demon!

Yet why were there demons here... Demons?!

Suddenly, he remembered!

Jian Nuo glanced at the surrounding spirit-stone walls, glistening with the unmistakable fragrance of astronomical wealth, and his complexion turned pale and rigid.

He yanked Lu Shulong and demanded:

—"How did they get out?"

—"You think those are real demonic creatures?"-Lu Shulong asked with a half-smile, looking at Jian Nuo with quiet amusement.

—"Are they not?"-Jian Nuo hesitated for a fraction of a second, yet as he watched those demonic insects, the wariness in his eyes didn't abate in the slightest—even when the bugs were incinerated to flying ash by a flame ward the very next second.-"Wasn't this Spirit Stone Secret Realm constructed specifically to seal the final corridor to the Demon Realm? There is no reason to simulate combat against the demon race here of all places. Couldn't you hold it somewhere safer?"

—"You are remarkably well-informed,"-Lu Shulong remarked in mild surprise.

—"Anyone who pays the slightest attention would have heard of this!"-Jian Nuo insisted.

—"And yet, the God-Demon War occurred so long ago that most people treat it as mere myth. Especially the notion of the 'final demon corridor'—the general consensus is that it ceased to exist long ago,"-Lu Shulong said.-"Even the Qingyang Clan youngsters assume this is merely a new game."

—"'What people assume' and 'actually vanished' are two entirely different matters! It hasn't even been ten thousand years yet, has it? ...Wait. 'Assume'? They *assume* it's a game?! So these demonic creatures are genuinely real after all?!"-Jian Nuo stared at Lu Shulong.

Lu Shulong offered an enigmatic smile.

In Jian Nuo's eyes, that smirk was indistinguishable from the sinister grin of an arch-villain preparing to unleash the demon race to annihilate mankind.

Jian Nuo demanded in fury:

—"Releasing demons? How dare you?! Did Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable not stop you?! Even foolish antics must have limits—"

—"It was my idea. Do you have an objection?"

A third voice cut into the conversation.

Jian Nuo looked up to find Qingyang Qingyu standing there, having arrived at some unknown moment. Though she had to look up slightly given their height difference, her gaze bored into him with an imperious, icy disdain.

Jian Nuo instinctively broke into a foolish, besotted smile:

—"As long as you like it, even the destruction of the world would be righteous."

Lu Shulong silently took a step back, feeling thoroughly suffocated by the sickening display.

Qingyang Qingyu, however, let out a cold snort:

—"Stop acting like a man bewitched by lust; you clearly don't believe that. When it comes to opinions that conflict with your fundamental principles, you had best speak plainly from the start."

—"No, not at all!"-In moments like this, Jian Nuo's wits proved remarkably agile.-"If you chose to do this, you must have your reasons. Besides, it just occurred to me: for a secret realm of such paramount importance, this must have received the explicit approval of Villa Master Qingyang and the Untiring Immortal Venerable! They must have discovered something amiss!"

[Why didn't that occur to you when you were aggressively interrogating me a second ago?]

Lu Shulong retreated three more steps.

He truly felt he shouldn't be here.

Qingyang Qingyu let out a soft chuckle:

—"You guessed correctly. Although I was deeply bewildered when Ancestor and Master Untiring entrusted this secret realm to me, their decision was clearly grounded in profound wisdom. At least, after conducting measurements over this period, I discovered that the centuries of spirit stone suppression never truly sealed that 'Demon Eye.' It merely plugged the demonic qi below like a cork. But so long as the Demon Eye remains here, demonic qi and demonic creatures will continue to gather—surging forth in an unending, inexhaustible torrent."

—"How could that be... No, should I say 'as expected'?"-Jian Nuo muttered under his breath.

Since the True Immortals were petty enough to maintain a blacklist to retaliate against those who offended them millennia ago, why wouldn't they feed the Qingyang Clan fraudulent instructions for sealing the Demon Eye (the portal to the Demon Realm), while secretly waiting in the shadows for the next war to ignite?

Jian Nuo clenched his fists.

Forcing a strained smile onto his face, he offered:

—"So, you're clearing out the demon nest down below like this? Truly worthy of Qingyu—to accomplish such a feat."

—"..."-Qingyang Qingyu paused briefly before shifting the subject.-"By the way, what brought the two of you here?"

Jian Nuo looked at Qingyang Qingyu, then back down at the demonic swarm below.

While he doubted his good-for-nothing friends possessed the intellect to deduce the truth, the problem was that a few spies from the Palace of Carefree Wandering were mingled among them! If they leaked the situation here to Carefree Palace, matters would become unimaginably complicated.

Jian Nuo had never wanted to bring his friends into Qingyu's presence to begin with; now, he was even more desperate to decline.

However, Lu Shulong was even less willing to host those bizarre degenerates.

Before Jian Nuo could utter a word, Lu Shulong had already passed the buck:

—"Actually, Jian Nuo has a request."

He laid out Jian Nuo's dilemma in exhaustive detail.

—"I advise against having them come over,"-Qingyang Qingyu said, her stance aligning closely with Jian Nuo's.

Jian Nuo brightened immediately:

—"Indeed! The secrets here are hardly suitable for external eyes."

—"That is a secondary concern,"-Qingyang Qingyu muttered.

—"...Senior Sister Qingyu, did your secret realm system run into technical difficulties?"-Lu Shulong inquired keenly.

—"No!"-Qingyu replied instantly.

Yet seeing even Jian Nuo glance over in astonishment, Qingyang Qingyu tugged anxiously at a stray lock of hair by her temple.

—"Fine... There is indeed a slight issue,"-Qingyang Qingyu admitted.-"The enemies' combat stats are rather unstable, making it impossible to accurately measure the monster classifications at the entrance. Occasionally, an entity spawns that cannot be eradicated no matter how much force is applied... At this stage, it is still manageable since the spawns are low-tier demons. But if a high-tier demon appears while the disciples 'playing the game' possess insufficient cultivation, they won't be able to hold the line. Therefore, I cannot take in outsiders for 'closed beta testing' just yet."

—"It's completely fine; it was merely a passing whim of mine,"-Jian Nuo eagerly took the blame upon himself, sparing no effort to disparage his "friends."-"Besides, even if the system were complete, they wouldn't be fit to participate. Their cultivation realms were piled up purely through medicinal pills; real demonic creatures are far beyond what they can handle."

Hearing Jian Nuo speak like that, Qingyang Qingyu grew somewhat displeased instead:

—"No, this has nothing to do with cultivation realm. If anything, what I am trying to build is a system operable even by those with zero cultivation..."

Jian Nuo listened with complete incomprehension written across his face.

—"Come to think of it, why is it failing to measure accurately?"-Lu Shulong asked.

He had caught on: Qingyang Qingyu wasn't personally opposed to the idea; she had simply hit a developmental roadblock. So long as this problem was solved, he could merrily pass the buck after all!

Thus, Lu Shulong asked:

—"Could you let me examine the relevant data models?"

[You're trying to show off in a moment like this?! You clearly said you had no desire to become Qingyu's mount!]

*(Lu Shulong: I never wanted to in the first place...)*

Jian Nuo glared daggers at Lu Shulong.

Yet Qingyang Qingyu appeared even more flustered than Jian Nuo:

—"I can solve it myself. Just let me think it through."

—"I never offered to solve it for you, Senior Sister Qingyang!"-Lu Shulong replied with an obedient, innocent demeanor.-"Don't forget, I am merely an alchemist. Even as a direct disciple, Master wouldn't cram advanced knowledge beyond my level at this stage. It's just that I am more accustomed to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mode of thinking; perhaps I can offer a bit of inspiration."

Hearing this, Qingyang Qingyu wavered.

She had genuinely been stuck on this bottleneck for an eternity. She had gathered mounds of data following the methodologies Zhou Hui taught, yet the measurements remained frustratingly inaccurate, yielding zero progress. Yet she was loath to consult Zhou Hui directly, because when he had touched on the subject, he hadn't elaborated—as though treating it as a fundamental knowledge point his disciples had already mastered. At least, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples treated it as basic common sense. Having always prided herself on being an academic ace, Qingyang Qingyu had worked herself into a stubborn corner, unwilling to run crying to the Untiring Immortal Venerable so soon.

Still, getting a minor hint wouldn't count as cheating... right?

Unsure of who she was even competing against, Qingyang Qingyu hesitated for several moments before finally nodding.

—"Very well. Then I trouble you to take a look."

Qingyu retrieved the recorded data scrolls.

Lu Shulong began reviewing them with a pleasant smile, but gradually, his expression grew stern—and then chillingly severe.

Lu Shulong's physical appearance belonged to the pure, ethereal, delicate sort; coupled with slightly melancholic, drooping doe-like eyes, he was breathtakingly handsome, yet lacked the domineering, [bloodbath aura](# "Literal CN: 我花开后百花杀 (wǒ huā kāi hòu bǎi huā shā) — 'When my flower blooms, all other blossoms wither' — famous line by Huang Chao denoting ruthless dominance") that radiated from Qu Qianxing.

And yet, when such a Lu Shulong turned stern, an eerie, reptilian chill coiled around him, evoking an instinctual urge in onlookers to back away in dread.

Qingyu naturally didn't back down, yet she couldn't help adding in unease:

—"I suspect my dataset is simply not comprehensive enough."

—"...No. This is not a matter of incomplete data; you failed to categorize them properly,"-Lu Shulong said, furrowing his brow.-"What on earth is this chaotic mess of demonic qi figures? Did you think logging numbers was all it took?! You have to use your brain and classify them! Subdivide their species, identify morphological and energetic commonalities, and deduce data patterns from there—"

Mid-sentence, Lu Shulong abruptly halted, suddenly remembering that Qingyang Qingyu was not a disciple under his own Alchemy Hall.

He looked at Qingyu and sighed with melancholy:

—"This isn't your fault. Presumably Eternal Villa has never offered Biology classes... To be fair, even Heavenly Spirit Sect only introduced them recently! Though Master always emphasized systematic summaries in the past. In that case, allow me to demonstrate how to categorize biological organisms... no, demonic entities, to extract statistical values. Bring me a specimen of a demonic creature. Ideally, a living one."

—"A specimen?"-It was Qingyu's first time hearing the term, though she grasped the general intent.

At this point, Qingyang Qingyu no longer dared dream of "independent research."

Looking at Lu Shulong's demeanor, she felt that failing to grasp such "elementary common sense" was practically a heinous crime.

Qingyang Qingyu stammered:

—"You mean a demon? No... none were captured."

Even if she had captured one, she wouldn't have kept it around!

Who would ever feel comfortable keeping a live demon at their side?!

—"If there isn't a live one, surely there's a dead one?"-Lu Shulong asked.

[“...”](# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingyu")

Qingyu's sense of shame deepened further. Precisely because she had diligently disposed of every demon carcass each time... Wait, not every single one! Last time, Qingyang Qingli seemed to have slain one and discarded it in a corner of the secret realm.

—"There should be one remaining. Please follow me,"-Qingyu said hurriedly.

She led Lu Shulong (and Jian Nuo) to the location where the demonic carcass had been tossed. Yet looking at the remains, Lu Shulong still appeared thoroughly unsatisfied.

—"It's far too mangled... Whatever, it'll have to do,"-Lu Shulong said.

With that, Lu Shulong slid several petite, scalpel-like willow-leaf dissection blades from his sleeve, turning to Qingyu:

—"I will only explain this once. Watch closely."

—"When encountering a demon, we generally begin our examination with the demon core, observing its condition..."-*Rrrrip!* He sliced effortlessly through the organ storing the creature's demonic energy, flicking the demon core free.

—"If the condition of the demon core is compromised, we move on to studying its brain. Take care not to damage it..."-As he spoke, he made precise incisions across an area bearing little resemblance to a human form, cleanly cleaving open the cranium.

—"Notice that demons possess a degree of mimicry; relying purely on external morphology is insufficient. In such cases, we must thoroughly map its internal organ classifications..."-Lu Shulong meticulously dissected its vascular network.

—"Following that..."

...

Qingyang Qingyu and Jian Nuo watched in dead silence.

Their expressions shifted from calm observation to unease, from unease to sheer horror, and finally to catatonic numbness—until Lu Shulong glared up at them:

—"What are you standing there gaping for? Take notes!"

—"Yes!"-Qingyu hastily pulled out a jade slip.

Jian Nuo mechanically rummaged through his storage pouch as well, yet at this moment, he deeply regretted revealing his cards to Qu Qianxing so early. The thought from earlier resurfaced with terrifying clarity:

[No matter how you look at him, this dragon is an absolute arch-villain!]

[And he claims this horrifying butchery is standard Heavenly Spirit Sect curriculum?!]

[Is the Heavenly Spirit Sect truly okay?!]
