# Chapter 31: Injury (第31章 伤)

East Domain.

Within the Palace of Carefree Wandering, time inside the Thought Pavilion stood perpetually frozen.

This three-story pavilion stood at the very center of Xiaoyao Lake. The moment one stepped onto the long corridor leading to the pavilion, one could see nightfall slowly descending, a clear, luminous moon forever hanging in the distant, deep-blue firmament, casting the pavilion into a sharp silhouette.

Whenever a crisp breeze blew past, only the bamboo forest before the pavilion would rustle. Upon closer inspection, one would discover that every single bamboo joint was composed of broken swords. Some remained razor-sharp, while others were decayed and rusted, every sword notch across their surfaces testifying to the fierce, high-intensity battles they had once endured.

Passing through this bamboo forest brought one to the Thought Pavilion.

Yet without the master's express permission, ordinary cultivators could never reach the pavilion's threshold. People could only linger in vain along the long corridor, wander aimlessly across the lake, hesitate amidst the bamboo grove, and hover helplessly in midair. The more desperately one attempted to approach, the more the pavilion stood quietly beneath the moonlight like a paper silhouette, almost for eternity.

On this day, however, a bloodcurdling shriek erupted from that serene silhouette, startling a flock of waterbirds into panicked flight.

—"Aaaaaah—!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana shrieked in agony.

Sovereign Immortal Chana could not care about anything else; he only felt that his hand hurt, hurt, HURT!

It was only thanks to a medicine cultivator beside him swiftly pulling a packet of medicine from a storage pouch and scattering it over the junction where Sovereign Immortal Chana's arm plunged into the cloud mirror that the searing agony eased by a fraction, allowing Sovereign Immortal Chana to recover a sliver of his faculties.

Regrettably, anyone familiar with Sovereign Immortal Chana knew that this sovereign's head was purely ornamental; the meager brain matter inside was barely better than nothing. In most circumstances, him thinking was far worse than him not thinking at all.

Having finally managed to string thoughts together, the sovereign did not ponder how to extricate himself; only boundless, torrential hatred surged into his chest.

[Zhou Hui! Zhou Hui! ZHOU HUI!!!]

From childhood to adulthood, he had never suffered such a humiliating setback!

It had always been him snatching treasures from others! Who dared to cut off his hand?!

Yet Sovereign Immortal Chana could not break free for the moment, and his arm still throbbed with scorching, fiery torment, so this hatred inevitably shifted onto the people around him:

—"What are you all standing around dazed for?! Hurry over and help me!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana snarled.

—"In a situation like this, how are we supposed to help?"-someone protested awkwardly.

The reason the Carefree Palace had chosen the most brainless Sovereign Immortal Chana to oversee the scene was precisely because of his signature technique:

[Hand Turning Heaven and Earth](# "Literal CN: 手转乾坤 (shǒu zhuǎn qiánkūn) — Sovereign Immortal Chana's spatial divine ability, allowing him to reach across space via communication media to seize objects regardless of distance or mass").

As long as there was a communication medium like a cloud mirror, he could grasp objects across vast distances. Furthermore, no matter how distant that object was or what its mass might be, to Sovereign Immortal Chana it was utterly meaningless, feeling no different than a mustard seed resting within arm's reach.

Clearly, this technique possessed immense strategic value for the Carefree Palace.

It was also the very foundation that gave Sovereign Immortal Chana the confidence to swagger so brazenly.

Yet this was the first time the Carefree Palace cohort had ever seen Sovereign Immortal Chana suffer such a devastating blow—resembling a [mischievous brat](# "Literal CN: 熊孩子 (xióng háizi) — unruly, destructive spoiled child / brat") who shoved his hand into a jar to grab a scorpion, only to find he couldn't pull his hand back out.

However, spatial techniques were the most dangerous to directly interfere with among all celestial arts. One clumsy maneuver could leave a person with their head and body severed in two. Moreover, how were they supposed to help in this predicament? Drag Sovereign Immortal Chana by the arm and yank him back?

Thus, even with several Mahayana cultivators present, they were like [a rat trying to drag a tortoise](# "Literal CN: 老鼠拉龟 (lǎoshǔ lā guī) — Cantonese idiom for having no idea where to grab hold or start on an intractable problem"), utterly at a loss for where to begin.

And having reached this stage, Sovereign Immortal Chana didn't appear to want to flee either:

—"Spiritual energy! Give me spiritual energy! I want to kill that bastard Zhou Hui!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana roared, glaring with eyes webbed in crimson veins, stripped of every shred of an 'Immortal Sovereign's' dignity.

The cultivators present looked at one another. Ultimately unable to defy his command, they began channeling their cultivation to transmit spiritual energy. Right at that moment, an urgent shout rang out:

—"Don't touch him!"-a voice barked.

Accompanying this reprimand, a sword qi like moonlight swept toward Sovereign Immortal Chana's arm. Before anyone else could react to stop it, the sword qi cleaved his arm clean in two, causing blood to geyser outward like a fountain!

Simultaneously, the cloud mirror connecting the two locations crashed to the floor, unable to maintain its coherence, evaporating into billows of thick mist.

Yet even so, the newcomer seemed unsatisfied. Several more sword qi slashed past until the mist forming the cloud mirror was thoroughly shredded into nonexistence. Only then did a shadow materialize within the Thought Pavilion.

It was faintly the silhouette of a slender swordsman, the projection of a sword casket on his back being his most defining feature. But a shadow was ultimately just a shadow; the man clearly had no intention of revealing his true self.

—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable excels most at tracking spiritual energy,"-the shadow spoke up.-"If you make a move, you'll be [registered on the Venerable's ledger](# "Literal CN: 记载仙尊的账上 (jì zǎi xiānzūn de zhàng shàng) — marked on his personal ledger; targeted for ruthless retaliation")!"

The several Mahayana cultivators present immediately turned green, retreating a massive stride away from Sovereign Immortal Chana.

—"Nonsense!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana bellowed, clutching his severed arm.

In truth, once the arm was severed entirely, the agonizing pain had actually receded considerably. Yet looking down at his mangled stump, even knowing there were immortal arts to regenerate it later, he felt he had lost face completely.

—"Sikong Yao, you're using public duty to settle a private grudge!"-he howled at the shadow.

—"I've never had private ties with you; where would a grudge come from?"-the shadow known as Sikong Yao replied helplessly.

—"Don't think I don't know! You've always regarded this Thought Pavilion as your personal property, even imitating True Immortal Yinian's sword techniques!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana sneered.-"Pity, what a pity! Your swordsmanship could never hold a candle to the True Immortal; only your escape arts [surpassed the master](# "Literal CN: 青出于蓝 (qīng chū yú lán) — indigo comes from the blue plant yet is bluer than blue; the student surpasses the master"). Did it feel enjoyable being hunted by Zhou Hui for seven full years? You actually have the nerve to shamelessly cling to life!"

—"It was quite enjoyable, actually. After all, I survived in the end, didn't I?"-Sikong Yao replied nonchalantly.

In his current shadow form, he didn't have a face anyway.

—"On the contrary,"-Sikong Yao offered earnest advice.-"you had better leave the Thought Pavilion immediately and head to the Hall of Pure Heart to beg your ancestral master for a talisman. I cannot guarantee that merely severing your arm will preserve your life."

—"Hahaha! Sikong Yao, you admitted it! You chopped off my arm for revenge after all!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana barked, simply not the type to listen to reason.-"I'm not like you, a coward who only knows how to run! If you hadn't interfered just now, I might not have lacked the strength to fight him!"

—"Young people nowadays are far too confident..."-Sikong Yao muttered.

Right then, whether seeking to interrupt this pointless squabble or genuinely unable to hold it in any longer, a Mahayana cultivator stammered tremulously:

—"Sh-Shadowless Sword Venerable... why... why is your shadow turning a bit white?"

Hearing this, the crowd focused their gaze on Sikong Yao's shadow. Sure enough, that deep black shadow appeared faintly suffused with a lunar white glow.

—"[Holy crap!](# "Literal CN: 窝草 (wō cǎo) — playful phonetic homophone for 我操 (wǒ cào, holy shit / WTF)")"-the shadow shrieked, instantly vanishing.

Before anyone could look for him, a frantic warning came drifting from far down the lakeside corridor:

—"RUN! ZHOU HUI IS HERE!"-Sikong Yao yelled.

The voice was actively drifting—on the first syllable he was at the midpoint of the corridor, and by the final syllable he was already at the far terminal! A corridor that would take an ordinary cultivator four to five double-hours to traverse even with permission had been cleared by him in the span of a few words!

The others lacked Sikong Yao's ungodly reaction speed. Hearing his cry, many blankly raised their heads to peer out the window, wondering where on earth Sikong Yao had gone.

Yet instead of catching sight of Sikong Yao (or his shadow), they saw the moon—once hanging far on the horizon—now suspended directly outside the window, like a colossal, jade-colored eye gazing emotionlessly down at them all.

—"Aaaah!"

Everyone present jumped out of their skins. Like Sikong Yao, scores of cultivators bolted frantically down the stairs.

In their wake, they faintly caught a soft sigh:

[—"He got away again."](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

Hearing that voice, who among them didn't know who it was? They sprinted even faster!

Yet there was also a "brave man" completely devoid of fear—namely, Sovereign Immortal Chana.

—"Haha! Zhou Hui! You actually dare to chase me all the way here!"-Hearing that voice, Sovereign Immortal Chana turned ecstatic instead of fearful, a vicious grin splitting his face.-"This is my turf!"

—"Theoretically so, but is this truly your 'turf'?"-a gentle voice mused with bemused curiosity.-"The Carefree Palace's teaching methods are truly peculiar. Pumping a kid full of hot air is one thing, but he's already reached Tribulation Transcendence, yet you still haven't taught him how to construct his own mini-world? Can one truly ascend like this?"

—"Shut up!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana shrieked; he detested this pedantic lecturing tone most of all.-"I don't need those useless procedures! This Thought Pavilion was left behind when our Carefree Palace's True Immortal Yinian ascended! It's infinitely superior to someone like you who can never ascend!"

—"Since you came, then stay here forever!"-he bellowed, flinging his arms wide.

The shadow beneath him stretched and ballooned accordingly, sweeping across the entire Thought Pavilion.

In that instant, the Thought Pavilion was drained of all color, becoming just like its exterior—reduced to stark black and white, resembling a two-dimensional paper silhouette.

Even the ordinary cultivators who had failed to escape the Thought Pavilion, the bamboo forest, and its perimeter lost all color alongside it, freezing in place with terrified expressions etched upon their faces.

This was the legendary ultimate killing strike of True Immortal Yinian: [All Perish in a Single Thought](# "Literal CN: 一念俱灭 (yīniàn jù miè) — True Immortal Yinian's domain technique where all existence within the realm is reduced to a static black-and-white silhouette").

Sovereign Immortal Chana's cultivation fell far short; even having refined the Thought Pavilion, he could not achieve true "All Perish in a Single Thought." Yet through the innate might of the Thought Pavilion, he could freeze time in a [fleeting instant](# "Literal CN: 须臾刹那 (xūyú chànà) — Buddhist terms for infinitesimal units of time, roughly equivalent to a split-second").

At this moment, within the entire Thought Pavilion, only Sovereign Immortal Chana could move.

—"Zhou Hui, I know you're here! I'm going to slaughter you—!"-Clutching his stump that continued to gush blood, Sovereign Immortal Chana staggered to his feet.

At this moment, his hair was disheveled, his body soaked in gore, and his expression twisted into utter madness. Rather than an immortal sovereign, he looked far more like a vengeful ghoul.

Yet Sovereign Immortal Chana remained oblivious to his horrific appearance.

—"Zhou Hui—!"-Sovereign Immortal Chana croaked, his mind holding only this singular, terminal obsession.

He did not even notice that as he strode forward, the colors across his body were draining away bit by bit, ultimately blending into the paper-silhouette pavilion. Only the dark-red bloodstains on the floor radiated a bewitching, eerie glow beneath the pale blue moonlight.

*

Sovereign Immortal Chana's disappearance was not quiet or unnoticed.

Though he himself had not realized his own dissolution, every cultivator currently within the Carefree Palace felt the vanishing of the Thought Pavilion.

Yes—at the exact moment Sovereign Immortal Chana vanished, the Thought Pavilion vanished alongside him.

This Thought Pavilion was situated at the center of Xiaoyao Lake, and Xiaoyao Lake was the very heart of the Carefree Palace. Whether the disciples of the Carefree Palace were taking off or landing aboard treasure ships, or soaring past on flying swords, they could always see that exquisite pavilion resting in the lake's center, glowing with azure moonlight, a self-contained world of its own.

For such an extraordinary structure, even a cultivator who had barely stepped into the Qi Condensation stage could sense the unique spiritual aura permeating that domain.

Yet this small realm was disappearing.

It had stood there for hundreds, thousands of years, yet now, like a ball of ink-soaked paper tossed suddenly into water, vast plumes of ink [bled outward](# "Literal CN: 洇开 (yīnkāi) — ink soaking into absorbent paper or bleeding into water"), dispersing like smoke.

The people of the Carefree Palace could only watch on in helpless dismay.

At this moment, two Tribulation Transcendence elders currently residing in the palace, alongside several dozen Mahayana cultivators, joined forces to intervene, yet could not slow the dispersion of that "ink" by even a fraction. Everyone could only watch powerlessly as the dark-green Xiaoyao Lake turned into a pool of dead water.

—"Can it really not be saved?"-a beautiful mature woman asked, heartbroken and distraught.

Sikong Yao—no longer a shadow, but a white-robed sword immortal carrying a sword casket on his back, looking remarkably youthful—shook his head in silence.

In truth, he felt that this loss was entirely acceptable.

They had merely lost the Thought Pavilion.

Sikong Yao knew that in the past, Zhou Hui had wanted to smash the Carefree Palace's headquarters to bits; he simply hadn't been able to locate the palace's exact coordinates, which had led to Zhou Hui walking him like a dog for seven full years.

Fortunately, Sovereign Immortal Chana had been obsessed with pomp and ostentation, occupying that Thought Pavilion day in and day out. And the Thought Pavilion was a miniature space refined by a True Immortal, forming its own self-contained world completely decoupled from the surrounding Carefree Palace grounds; otherwise, Zhou Hui could have pushed the boat along the current and uprooted the entire Carefree Palace in one fell swoop!

Speaking of which, hadn't rumors claimed that after founding his own sect, Zhou Hui had mellowed out considerably?

Why was his strike so devastatingly lethal?!

This time was completely different from when Zhou Hui chased him. This time, Zhou Hui had straight-up annihilated a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator—and the one he killed was of "noble" birth, with thousands of tangled ties to the Carefree Palace's highest echelon. This was setting the stage for an irreconcilable blood feud!

Sikong Yao appraised the situation with a strange sense of detachment in his heart.

However, this wasn't appropriate to voice aloud; it was too easy to offend people.

In fact, under the current circumstances, someone was already glaring daggers at Sikong Yao.

—"Didn't you go to save my disciple?!"-the beautiful woman interrogated him sharply, refusing to give up.

—"I was a step too late,"-Sikong Yao replied, raising an eyebrow.-"What? Did you want me to be buried alongside him inside?"

Resentment flooded the woman's face, and she seemed about to say something, but a booming command shattered the tension:

—"Enough!"-a deep, resonant voice thundered.

The speaker was an eight-foot burly man with wide, glaring eyes. Bulging muscles strained against his silken robes, giving him the distinct vibe of a bandit masquerading as a wealthy landlord. Yet what was truly terrifying was his spiritual aura—so vast it could practically be described as blanketing heaven and earth. It made his already hulking frame appear all the more colossal; merely standing there, he resembled an immovable mountain peak.

—"What are you bickering for?! Pointing fingers now is too late!"-the burly man bellowed in fury.

Sikong Yao held his tongue.

—"Then did my disciple die for nothing?!"-the beautiful woman wept bitterly, unwilling to let it go.

—"How could that be?!"-the burly man gnashed his teeth.-"Zhou Hui provokes our Carefree Palace time and time again! We conceded repeatedly, yet he refuses to quit while ahead! I shall personally dispatch a missive to the Heavenly Dao Alliance to demand where the Heavenly Dao stands?!"

Those around listened in silence, knowing this matter would certainly not conclude with a mere denunciation.

—"Furthermore, starting today, blockade all trade routes into the Central Domain! Prohibit all grain, spirit pills, talismans, and commodities from entering the Central Domain! I want to see: Zhou Hui might not care about our Carefree Palace, but can the sects of the Central Domain endure it?! Across these Five Domains and Six Abysses, I will leave Zhou Hui nowhere to stand!"-the burly man decreed.

*

Central Domain, Mount Tianling.

—"Master, how did you accomplish this?"-Guan Ye, Zhou Hui's fourth disciple, asked, staring curiously at the miniature paper house on the desk.

It was an exquisite paper model crafted from black and white paper: a white lake, a black corridor and pavilion, with only the bamboo grove before the pavilion fashioned from iron thorns. There were even tiny paper figurines running between the pavilion and bamboo, their mouths agape in terror as if fleeing. It looked remarkably "cute."

Well, if one didn't know the origin of this paper house, one might indeed find it cute.

Unfortunately, Guan Ye and the others had sat with Zhou Hui before the jade screen and watched the entire farce from start to finish—including the jade emitting smoke at the end, which condensed into this very paper model.

That was right: this paper house was the Thought Pavilion. Zhou Hui had snatched it over, entirely intact!

Any cultivator with a shred of common sense knew that capturing it whole was infinitely harder than destroying it—at least the Carefree Palace believed the Thought Pavilion had been utterly pulverized by Zhou Hui.

Yet Guan Ye didn't care much about how Zhou Hui had seized it; he only cared about how this paper house had been constructed.

—"Is this a dharma treasure?"-Guan Ye asked curiously.

—"Not entirely. Its rules are already [self-consistent](# "Literal CN: 规则自洽 (guīzé zìqià) — self-consistent rules; closed system of natural and spatial laws operating independently"),"-Zhou Hui replied, gazing at the paper house with genuine admiration.-"Worthy of a [mini cave-heaven](# "Literal CN: 小洞天 (xiǎo dòngtiān) — pocket cave-heaven; miniature self-contained realm") crafted by a True Immortal. A pity..."

—"Rest assured,"-Zhou Hui smiled at Guan Ye.-"this is required coursework once you enter the Void Refinement stage. But before that, you have a mountain of fundamentals to lay, and they must be rock-solid. Otherwise, even if I leave good things behind for you, you'll end up squandering them just like this fellow did."

Zhou Hui's smile gave Guan Ye an instant headache.

—"Second Senior Brother seems to have brought the secret realm under control and should be returning shortly. I'll go receive him,"-Guan Ye announced, feigning composure as he glanced at the jade screen, shooting a meaningful glance at his fifth junior brother.

—"I'll go too!"-Lu Shulong chimed in hurriedly.

—"Then I might as well go too,"-Ran Deng said, attempting to slip away as well.

Yet before they could flee, Yuwen Xuan, who had been propping his chin in his hand and watching Zhou Hui with a playful grin, suddenly piped up:

—"Master, what did you mean by 'leave good things behind'?"-he asked.

Zhou Hui raised an eyebrow.

—"Ascension, of course! What else could it be?"-Ran Deng curled her lip, feeling the question was absurd.

Yuwen Xuan said nothing, merely fixing his gaze on Zhou Hui.

—"Just as your junior sister said, what else could it be?"-Zhou Hui smiled gently at him.-"Could it be that I'm dying? Regrettably, given the lifespan of a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, if you don't cultivate diligently, I'll still be around long after you're all dead and gone."

—"Then I'm relieved,"-Yuwen Xuan nodded with solemn gravity.-"With the way Master made his move just now, I thought you had given up on us!"

—"What are you talking about?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Because ever since you founded the Heavenly Spirit Sect... no, ever since you picked up Old Two, your conduct has grown increasingly cautious,"-Yuwen Xuan accused.-"Though you don't say it, I know you wanted to leave a retreat route for these little brats! I even seriously considered that if these guys kept dragging your feet, I'd just bump them off."

Ran Deng and her two junior brothers shuddered in unison.

In that instant, they felt genuine killing intent radiating from Yuwen Xuan. The Eldest Senior Brother was dead serious!

—"You haven't been [hung from the mountain gate](# "Literal CN: 挂山门 (guà shānmén) — traditional martial sect punishment of stringing an errant disciple from the sect gate for public display") for three days; are your bones itching again?"-Zhou Hui was amused to the point of exasperation.

—"Fortunately, the way Master acted just now didn't look like you were considering our futures at all,"-Yuwen Xuan nodded with mock gratification.

Zhou Hui didn't want to speak; his temples throbbed.

How to put it regarding this eldest disciple? He was always exceptionally sharp at the exact moments he shouldn't be.

The moment Zhou Hui decided to deal with Sovereign Immortal Chana, he indeed hadn't considered the Heavenly Spirit Sect—after all, the sect's annihilation was already decreed by Heaven, so why give the Carefree Palace face?! Zhou Hui even felt that rather than dying mysteriously at the hands of some so-called "protagonist," it was better to pull off a massive heist before kicking the bucket!

Of course, this wasn't appropriate to voice aloud.

Otherwise, Yuwen Xuan might rush ahead of him to pull off something huge first.

—"Why consider so much?"-Zhou Hui sneered.-"He was merely an [inflatable doll](# "Literal CN: 充气娃娃 (chōngqì wáwá) — inflatable doll; Zhou Hui's modern derisive metaphor for cultivators artificially pumped full of pills and qi without real foundation") pumped full of hot air; a wave of my hand and he was gone."

—"As expected of Master, truly formidable—"-Yuwen Xuan drawled.

Suddenly, he lunged forward, throwing his arms around Zhou Hui in a flying tackle!

Zhou Hui instinctively raised a hand to shove the rascal away, but the spiritual pressure bearing down from that side made Zhou Hui momentarily unable to summon force, and Yuwen Xuan caught him in a dead bear hug around the waist!

—"Mm-hmm, a wave of your hand and he was gone?"-Yuwen Xuan buried his face against Zhou Hui's waist, tilting his head up to peer at his master.-"Master, your spiritual energy depletion doesn't tell that story, does it?!"

Zhou Hui smacked him upside the head with a palm.

Zhou Hui had not lied; even a dozen more of those watery Tribulation Transcendence hacks wouldn't be enough to make him break a sweat.

What had genuinely cost Zhou Hui effort was snatching the Thought Pavilion intact, and using the pavilion's coordinates to deduce the Carefree Palace's exact location—which had failed, indicating that the Carefree Palace had an array grandmaster even more skilled than himself.

However, this had indeed slightly overdrawn his spiritual power. Coupled with Yuwen Xuan's rather unique method of channeling spiritual energy—even back when the boy was a child, Zhou Hui couldn't dodge the brat hugging his leg unless he focused his full attention—Yuwen Xuan had caught him completely off guard.

Zhou Hui considered this nothing more than their usual daily roughhousing, but then Ran Deng spoke up, her voice trembling:

—"In other words... Master just exhausted all his strength?"-Ran Deng choked up.

[Huh? What are you crying for?]

Zhou Hui stared at Ran Deng in bewilderment, only to discover that not just Ran Deng, but Guan Ye's and Lu Shulong's eyes were glistening with tears as well!

[—"Even I know an enemy of that caliber couldn't force Master to overdraw his power!"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

[—"Not to mention, to prepare for ascension, Master hasn't utilized his power like this in ages..."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

[—"Eldest Senior Brother, could Master be..."](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

—"Yes!"-Yuwen Xuan slipped down from Zhou Hui's waist, sat upright on the floor, nodded with profound solemnity, and put on a face of tragic grief.-"Though it sounded absurd when I first heard you discussing it with Old Two, based on my observations during this period, Master was indeed heavily injured in the last Heavenly Tribulation!"

[...Why don't you just say I'm already dead?!]

—"Who was heavily injured?"-The corner of Zhou Hui's mouth twitched.

Though he was slightly injured, the concept of an "injury" to a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator was completely beyond the comprehension of low-tier cultivators. Strictly speaking, it would hinder his ascension, but it certainly wouldn't hinder him from beating his disciples.

—"Didn't I already say?"-Zhou Hui laughed out of sheer exasperation.-"I gave up on ascending! So even if I used a bit more strength just now, it's hardly a big deal, right?"

—"Since you gave up on ascending, why did you say 'leave things behind'?"-Yuwen Xuan questioned sharply.-"You just said that if you don't ascend, your lifespan will outlast us all. Why would you need to 'leave things behind'?"

[Where did this [contrarian troll](# "Literal CN: 杠精 (gàngjīng) — modern internet slang for someone who compulsively argues, nitpicks, and contradicts every statement for the sake of arguing") come from?! That was because the Heavenly Dao decreed...]

Zhou Hui wanted to explain, yet found himself unable to explain.

Yet seeing Zhou Hui seemingly at a loss for words, Ran Deng and the others became all the more convinced!

To make matters worse, at some point on the jade screen displaying Qu Qianxing's group, the jade figurine representing Qu Qianxing also turned its head, peering out beyond the screen:

—"Master is heavily injured?!"-Qu Qianxing's voice drifted out from the screen.

[When on earth did this turn into a two-way live stream?!]

Zhou Hui glared at Yuwen Xuan in utter disbelief. Extending his divine sense toward the giant peach tree outside the mountain gate, he began shopping for which branch was best suited to hang Yuwen Xuan from!
