# Chapter 82: A Brutal Contrast (第82章 惨烈的对比)

—"...In summary, the main body of the Abyss Ocean has not been contaminated by demonic qi. In fact, in tandem with ocean current dynamics, it possesses a certain capacity for self-purification. Considering the current state of the divine-demonic ruins, a reverse-remediation strategy may be implemented..."-Guan Ye explained methodically.

Though he was thoroughly inept at socializing and knew only how to bury his head in labor, when it came to academic thesis presentations—even outside his primary domain of expertise—given sufficient time to prepare, Guan Ye could execute them with near-flawless precision.

After all, he had been rigorously forged under his master's relentless tutelage.

This was not even Guan Ye's unique talent, but rather a baseline survival skill beneath Zhou Hui's banner.

Barring Yuwen Xuan, whose [mind was profoundly afflicted](<# "Literal CN: 脑子贵恙 (nǎozi guìyàng) — polite satirical euphemism for 'troubled in the head' or having a screw loose">), every single disciple was required to master it. It wasn't that Yuwen Xuan was truly incapable; according to Guan Ye's observations, Eldest Senior Brother understood the core mechanics perfectly well in his heart and could even spot nuances others overlooked. It was just that whenever he was tasked with delivering a standardized report, he inevitably went completely off the rails, as though born without the faintest concept of what 'rules' were.

From the very beginning, Zhou Hui had placed immense weight on assessing his disciples' cultivation progress.

Particularly after that fateful examination over half a year ago following Master's failed tribulation transcendence, Master had rebranded his 'assessments.' When he wasn't demanding sit-down exams, he was requiring them to submit formal reports every few days. The formatting had also evolved—becoming more standardized, more granular, and far more ruthless than ever before.

Having faced such a terrifyingly strict Master year in and year out, in Guan Ye's eyes, the Void Refining and Body Integration powerhouses present were practically no different from [frogs](<# "Literal CN: 青蛙 (qīngwā) — wide-mouthed, blank-eyed amphibians staring vacantly at a pond">). They couldn't even compare to Second Senior Brother Qu Qianxing, who was merely at the Golden Core stage.

They could provide zero constructive guidance on research direction, nor did they abruptly cut into his exposition to cross-examine minute factual errors down to the bedrock. They couldn't even maintain an imposing facade from start to finish to exert psychological pressure on the presenter and induce errors at weak points.

This flock of so-called supreme experts merely resembled frogs: mouths agape, heads craned upward, listening in vacant stupor. The occasional questions they posed were of such [zero intellectual substance](<# "Literal CN: 没营养到了极点 (méi yíngyǎng dào le jídiǎn) — completely devoid of nutritional value, meaning intellectually vacuous or elementary">) that even an apprentice disciple from the Artifact Refinement Hall could answer them in their sleep.

[It seems I shouldn't expect to extract much rehearsal value from these seniors.]

[Once I return, I had better get Second Senior Brother to review it one more time before daring to see Master.]

Guan Ye sighed inwardly, though not a trace showed on his face. He calmly continued presenting to the frogs—[scratch that](<# "Author meta-humor notation (X) crossed out">), supreme experts.

* * *

Hermit Zui Liu and his peers remained entirely oblivious to the fact that they were being looked down upon by a mere Foundation Establishment Late Stage junior; they were listening with utter relish.

If at first they had merely been enjoying the spectacle—curious to see who would prevail between Zhou Hui's disciple and Cloud City's succession candidate—they were now completely enthralled by Guan Ye's presentation.

At the very least, Hermit Zui Liu had never imagined that a topic like "demonic qi distribution" could yield so many intricate nuances.

Cultivators in the past had naturally monitored demonic qi as well, but it amounted to little more than a cursory glance: as long as nothing went wrong, all was well. Even the more conscientious practitioners merely warned nearby mortals to stay clear, waiting until people actually turned up dead before taking official action.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's modus operandi, however, was fundamentally different.

They continuously monitored demonic qi concentrations down to precise numerical values, analyzing what variables influenced its expansion and contraction. They devised preventative countermeasures while simultaneously putting the energy to practical use; for instance, Guan Ye noted that while forging several specific types of magical artifacts, he discovered that a certain concentration of demonic qi proved extraordinarily suitable.

Of course, had an ordinary Foundation Establishment disciple spouted such claims, Hermit Zui Liu would have dismissed them out of hand—perhaps even roundly scolding the youth for his wild, fanciful delusions.

Yet Guan Ye's report was another matter entirely.

It was exhaustively detailed, rigorous, and backed by solid evidence. Even a Body Integration titan like Hermit Zui Liu, without having conducted firsthand verification, wouldn't dare assert that the youth's analysis was definitively flawed.

([Obviously! This is meant for a Tribulation Transcendence Master!])

Furthermore, Guan Ye's presentation methodology was another key factor that commanded belief.

Not only did he employ a wealth of pictorial projections to corroborate his points, but an array of charts, bar graphs, and line curves rendered the data legible at a single glance. Even cultivators who had never conducted relevant research could follow along effortlessly. Combined with vivid case examples and practical demonstrations, it left even Body Integration grandmasters with the distinct impression that they were genuinely learning something new.

Take, for example, the slide Guan Ye had just flipped past detailing the impact of demonic qi content on demonic artifacts. On one hand, Hermit Zui Liu marveled at the graphical drafting techniques and yearned to master how such charts were plotted; on the other hand, he was fascinated by the specific numerical values displayed within. He felt almost overwhelmed by the feast of information. It was only out of concern for his dignity as an elder that he refrained from shouting at Guan Ye to pause so he could get a closer look.

Evidently, he was far from the only one who felt this way.

Once the session transitioned to the Q&A segment, Hermit Zui Liu discovered that everyone else harbored an entire mountain of questions they wished to [humbly seek guidance on](<# "Author meta-humor notation (X) crossed out">)—inquire about:

[—"Could you explain that demonic qi concentration distribution map one more time?"](<# "Speaker: Cultivation Senior">)

[—"Why are those specific indices used as the baseline for the artifact crafting matrix? How were they designed?"](<# "Speaker: Cultivation Senior">)

[—"Replay the footage of the Abyss Ocean fissure boundary! Is it true that monster beast aquaculture is viable there?!"](<# "Speaker: Cultivation Senior">)

A chaotic deluge of inquiries instantly submerged Guan Ye.

Furthermore, because every person present was an exalted elder and patriarch of the cultivation world, Guan Ye couldn't enforce discipline the way he did back in his Artifact Refinement Hall. There were few people by his side who could assist him—Zhang Hongjing was clearly useless in a setting like this, and even he harbored some personal curiosity regarding the presentation's contents. Guan Ye could only strain to cope with these "esteemed seniors."

Amidst these wildly inefficient inquiries, the celestial Kunpeng finally arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Taking advantage of the moment when everyone's attention was captivated by the breathtaking, colossal coral reef barrier, Guan Ye seized his chance to slip away.

—"Senior cultivators, if you are interested in the formatting of academic reports, you may visit our sect's Palace of Silence, where dedicated workshops are held,"-Guan Ye said with uncharacteristic briskness, displaying a rare stroke of nimble wit.-"I must attend upon Master, and shall take my leave."

The cultivators were filled with deep regret.

They still harbored countless questions, yet they could hardly forcibly detain this prized disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Now, they genuinely believed in the title of "the Untiring Immortal Venerable's prized disciple" from the bottom of their hearts. Even if the young man was merely at Foundation Establishment Late Stage, in the realm of demonic qi research, Guan Ye was unmistakably qualified to stand on his own two feet. They didn't even realize that this investigative report was merely an "extracurricular interest assignment," completely outside Guan Ye's primary specialization.

The crowd watched helplessly as Guan Ye made his exit.

Right at this moment, Hermit Zui Liu suddenly remarked with cheerful enthusiasm:

—"By the way, hasn't the young master of Cloud City yet to deliver his academic presentation?"

A look of sudden realization dawned across the cultivators' faces. Whirling around with gleaming eyes, they excavated Yun Guoling, who was desperately trying to bury himself inside the coral garden.

If one were to describe Yun Guoling's current state of mind, it was regret—gut-wrenching, agonizing regret!

If he had still harbored a sliver of indignation when Zhang Hongjing sighed, *"What on earth possessed you to challenge a disciple of the Untiring Immortal Venerable?"*, the moment Guan Ye began his presentation, Yun Guoling's mind had devolved into a frantic internal loop: *[How on earth did I dare challenge the Untiring Immortal Venerable's prized disciple after overhearing just a few stray sentences?!]*

He finally grasped the reality with brutal clarity: the paltry scrap of knowledge tested by his clan ancestors during ordinary cultivation was nowhere near enough to even fill the gaps between this man's teeth! No—ordinary "cultivation quizzes" and the Heavenly Spirit Sect's "academic presentations" were completely different beasts!

That "report" was nothing like casually flipping through a manual, reciting it by heart, and calling it a day once you had an epiphany. It required months of gathering multidimensional data and compiling empirical analysis. Was writing this kind of report the Heavenly Spirit Sect's daily routine?!

What proved truly terrifying was that Guan Ye clearly possessed far more than just a single report.

When challenged on the spot, he had casually whipped out a presentation deemed safe for public viewing, which meant he undoubtedly possessed classified material reserved for private debriefing with his Master.

[Were all Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples [terrifying to such an extent](<# "Literal CN: 恐怖如斯 (kǒngbù rú sī) — iconic xianxia meme expressing hyperbolic awe and dread">)?!]

[Truly worthy of being the Untiring Immortal Venerable's prized disciples!]

[So what on earth gave me the delusion that I could compete with the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple on scholarly grounds?!]

For the first time in his pampered life, Yun Guoling experienced such visceral regret.

Had they not been stranded in the fathomless depths of the Abyss Ocean with nowhere to flee, he would have jumped overboard and swum for his life. Even so, he had strained with futile desperation to wedge himself into the coral grove, praying that everyone would forget his existence.

Prior to this, Yun Guoling had almost believed he had pulled it off.

The ancient powerhouses had appeared thoroughly intrigued by Guan Ye's presentation, bombarding him with so many questions that they hadn't finished even after arriving at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Catching sight of the magnificent barrier reef from afar, Yun Guoling had looked upon it with as much affection as if it were his own ancestral home, savoring the dizzying ecstasy of surviving a mortal catastrophe.

Yet who could have foreseen that despite reaching their destination, he would still be dragged out into the open!

Why?!

Even the target of his provocation hadn't bothered clinging to the grudge—why hadn't these old monsters forgotten about him?!

Yun Guoling naturally didn't dare question Body Integration seniors directly. With a face as white as paper, he stammered:

—"We have already arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. There is no time..."

—"How could there be no time? We have no other arrangements scheduled next, do we?"-Hermit Zui Liu turned to Zhang Hongjing for confirmation.-"—Rest? Are you underestimating the stamina of Body Integration cultivators? In half a day, one barely has time to take a light nap. Sightseeing can wait until later. Let us proceed directly to that Palace of Silence and let Young Master Yun deliver his academic presentation! Lest the venerable Yun Clan accuse us of playing favorites and neglecting their beloved scion."

[No, please, feel free to neglect me to your hearts' content,] Yun Guoling thought miserably.

Yet Hermit Zui Liu's proposal met with roaring approval from the other cultivators.

These ancient powerhouses were currently riding a wave of surging excitement.

Experts at the Void Refining and Body Integration stages were all devoted to researching and cultivating their own personalized divine abilities. Even a pure body cultivator like Shen Qingqian had stammeringly codified her training methodology into a codified *Blood Demon Manual*. Consequently, this was the single most critical juncture in a cultivator's lifespan for 'founding a cohesive theoretical framework of one's own.' Yet traditional cultivation manuals had always prized esoteric ambiguity—mysterious upon mysterious. Understanding them was excruciating; committing them to writing was even more excruciating; synthesizing those epiphanies to deduce one's supreme divine abilities was the pinnacle of agony.

Today, Guan Ye's presentation methodology had provided them with a dazzling epiphany, making them realize they could approach the dilemma from an entirely fresh angle.

Unfortunately, before they could thoroughly digest the material, Guan Ye had departed to attend upon his Master. These titans couldn't very well shamelessly march over to seek guidance directly from the Untiring Immortal Venerable—not that saving face mattered much, but the oppressive aura of a Tribulation Transcendence sovereign was simply too terrifying.

Conveniently enough, wasn't there another volunteer right here who had aggressively demanded to deliver a presentation?

Hermit Zui Liu assumed that since Young Master Yun had displayed such overflowing confidence at the outset, he must at least have conducted some rudimentary research in this field!

Thus, a flock of old grandmasters happily escorted—[scratch that](<# "Author meta-humor notation (X) crossed out">), kidnapped—Yun Guoling and headed briskly toward the Palace of Silence. Little did they imagine that an entirely different "pleasant surprise" awaited them there.

* * *

[—"The Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys also wish to deliver an academic presentation?"](<# "Speaker: Hermit Zui Liu">)

[—"Let us witness the contemporary academic style of the Northern Domain."](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

[—"Haha, commendable courage!"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

Upon arriving at the Palace of Silence, meeting Su Xin's group, and hearing of their intentions, Hermit Zui Liu and the others broke into hearty laughter.

They harbored no malice whatsoever; they were simply dazzled and thoroughly impressed by the Heavenly Spirit Sect's hospitality and itinerary thus far.

Disembarking from the celestial Kunpeng, these cultivators had traversed the exclusive VIP passage for high-tier masters to reach Qianxing Hall, been teleported to the Heavenly Spirit Sect via the Sundial Clock, and flown on their swords directly from the mountain gate to the Palace of Silence. The wonders they witnessed along the way had left them clicking their tongues in marvel, completely overhauling their perception of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. In their eyes, this was truly worthy of being the host of the Central Domain Immortal Chess Tournament! The evaluation proceedings belonged nowhere else!

Yet all that had merely been a fleeting glance. At present, what had etched the deepest impression upon the cultivators—aside from Guan Ye's thesis lecture aboard the Kunpeng—was this very Palace of Silence.

They had bypassed the ground floor examination hall that had inflicted severe psychological trauma on Su Xin, flying directly upon their flying swords to the observation terrace situated beneath the Palace of Silence's observatory.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's Grand Academic Hall was located right there.

It was a colossal, semi-open pavilion: one half extended outward into a scenic terrace lush with spiritual flora, while the other half was a functional lecture hall equipped with numerous breakout podiums and a central keynote stage, capable of hosting over thirty symposiums simultaneously.

Granted, this multifunctional hall had not yet been put to formal use, but the colossal pillars displayed vibrant projections of cultivation techniques alongside promotional broadcasts for the Immortal Chess Tournament, cultivating a rich and dignified atmosphere.

Most notably, the central keynote stage was illuminated with glowing spirit-script: *Keynote Symposium of Northern Domain Cultivators*.

The moment the Central Domain powerhouses arrived and beheld this sight, they assumed the Heavenly Spirit Sect had specially adjusted the itinerary just for their sake!

Naturally, they were brimming with eager anticipation, hoping to witness a brilliant exposition comparable to Guan Ye's.

Little did these grandmasters realize that their genial smiles took on an entirely different connotation in the eyes of the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys.

[—"Why are they all Void Refining and Body Integration powerhouses?!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[—"No, more importantly, why did they come all the way here specifically to listen to our sermon?"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

[—"I get it! The Heavenly Spirit Sect was resentful of our Heavenly Dao Alliance from the start—they want to make us look like fools!"](<# "Speaker: Heavenly Dao Alliance Envoy">)

Several Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys whispered anxiously amongst themselves.

—"I suspect there might be a misunderstanding here,"-Su Xin originally wanted to correct them, but suddenly realized this was a golden opportunity to dissuade them.-"—Since we know they are targeting us, how about we just drop it?!"

As long as they didn't step onto that stage, anything else was fine.

—"No, Little Grand-Martial Uncle! Precisely because we have seen through the Central Domain's haughty attitude, we must ascend that stage all the more—to let them know that we Northern Domain cultivators are by no means empty vessels!"-declared Qi Jie passionately. He was the highest-cultivated among the envoys, Su Xin's nominal deputy, and in reality the official representative dispatched by the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Su Xin genuinely suspected the fellow had been afflicted by some mind-altering spell.

Yet the others clearly didn't think so.

Standing backstage behind the podium and beholding a sea of Void Refining and Body Integration masters seated below, their spirits surged with inexplicable fervor instead.

Su Xin could not fathom this bizarre euphoria in the slightest:

—"Are you people not afraid of humiliating yourselves before such an audience?"-he asked.

—"We are not contestants, but organizing committee members,"-Qi Jie stated.-"What we have prepared to expound upon is the Northern Domain cultivation world's profound understanding of the Dao. What could possibly be humiliating about such a topic? In fact, it serves as the perfect opportunity to set the keynote tone for this tournament."

And, more importantly, a golden opportunity to carve out his own fame.

[...Are you serious? Where did this completely baseless confidence even come from?]

Su Xin wanted to say something.

Yet someone barged in right at that moment:

—"Brilliantly spoken! As expected of seniors hailing from the very center of the cultivation realm! When I traveled to the Northern Domain for experiential training, I wished nothing more than to bring that sublime cultivation atmosphere back to the Central Domain!"

[Why is there another idiot fanning the flames?!]

Su Xin glanced up, only to see Yun Guoling, the young master of Cloud City, strutting over.

Su Xin naturally recognized him. In his childhood, Su Xin had accompanied his parents to several banquets at Cloud Pond, while Yun Guoling frequently gallivanted across the Northern Domain—particularly Sky City—on sightseeing excursions, which the boy naturally pompously termed "experiential training."

Yun Guoling now fawned over the Heavenly Dao Alliance cultivators:

—"If permissible, this humble one is willing to assist the esteemed seniors on stage."

—"Haha, as expected of Young Master Yun! You truly possess keen discernment!"

Yun Guoling's lavish flattery inflated the Heavenly Dao Alliance envoys' confidence to new heights.

Su Xin dragged Yun Guoling aside and hissed:

—"Have you lost your mind? Or did you take a commission from the Heavenly Spirit Sect to dig a pit for us?!"

He no longer believed for a second that Yuwen Xuan's invitation had been a harmless coincidence.

Yet Yun Guoling replied with a miserable grimace:

—"I only wanted to [cling to your coattails](<# "Literal CN: 抱大腿 (bào dàtuǐ) — 'hug thick thighs', slang for piggybacking on someone powerful to freeload or survive">) to skate by, otherwise I would have had to take the stage alone... But surely your Northern Domain is the glorious center of the cultivation world—you won't lose, right?"-He looked at Su Xin with desperate hope.

[You think far too highly of our Northern Domain.]

Su Xin wanted to blurt this out, yet he loathed admitting in front of a creature like Yun Guoling that his own side couldn't hold a candle to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

In the end, Su Xin could only watch helplessly as several utter buffoons mounted the podium.

* * *

What followed was an unmitigated disaster.

Even Su Xin could see it clearly.

—He saw it with his eyes, not his ears.

As an ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivator, Su Xin naturally couldn't comprehend what gibberish Soul Formation practitioners were preaching about. But he could see with piercing clarity that the grandmasters seated below started off brimming with interest, grew visibly impatient after five minutes, and devolved into complete, unmasked boredom after ten minutes. A loose, suffocating aura of *wasting precious lifespan* steadily saturated the hall.

Witnessing this spectacle, Su Xin's toes curled so hard in secondhand embarrassment that he could have [excavated a brand-new Palace of Silence](<# "Literal CN: 用脚趾抠出一个新的不言宫 — popular internet slang 'cringing so hard your toes dig out a three-bedroom villa', adapted to the Palace of Silence">).

He genuinely couldn't fathom how Qi Jie, standing at the primary podium, managed to keep droning on—the man even seemed quite thrilled with himself!

Of course, had the lecture ended right then and there, the Heavenly Dao Alliance members might never have realized just how humiliating their performance was. Yet the Heavenly Spirit Sect had cruelly instituted a mandatory Q&A segment immediately following the lecture.

Su Xin clearly overheard the murmurs rippling through the audience:

[—"With that level of competence, where did they get the audacity to take the stage?"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

[—"Seems the Northern Domain's caliber isn't all that impressive after all!"](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

[—"Here I thought some grand revolution had swept the cultivation world while I was in secluded cultivation! Turns out it's the exact same old clichés."](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

[—"Don't be too hard on them; they are merely a few Soul Formation juniors, after all."](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

[—"True enough. Let us just toss out a casual question and get it over with."](<# "Speaker: Central Domain Senior">)

Su Xin felt his cheeks burning like fire.

He didn't know whether he should feel mortified or relieved.

Still, since these Body Integration powerhouses had decided not to make things difficult, it should wrap up very quickly... right?

Thinking thus, Su Xin clearly failed to comprehend:

From Soul Formation to Void Refining, and from Void Refining to Body Integration—these two chasms weeded out ninety-nine point nine-nine percent of all cultivators, ruthlessly discarding every practitioner incapable of forging their own unique path. The gulf in comprehension regarding cultivation techniques was unimaginably vast and cruel.

Thus, when one of the grandmasters casually tossed out an offhand question, Su Xin watched as the radiant excitement on Qi Jie's face abruptly froze. It drained to a ghostly pallor, then darkened from pale to a sickly, livid green.

A deathly silence began to spread.

After waiting for roughly half a tea's time, another genial, easygoing cultivator chuckled:

—"Old fellow, your question is far too tricky. Ask something simple instead! Earlier, you mentioned..."-He posed an alternative question.

This time, Qi Jie managed an answer, but barely a trickle.

He stammered a few arid, disjointed sentences, glancing toward his peers in hopes of reinforcement. Who could have guessed that his companions were even more clueless than he was, allowing the suffocatingly awkward silence to spread once more.

Watching this unfold, Su Xin felt on the verge of suffering a [heart attack from sheer secondhand embarrassment](<# "Literal CN: 心肌梗塞 (xīnjī gěngsè) — myocardial infarction; internet slang for dying of unbearable stress or cringe">).

Unable to stomach the sight any longer, he bolted out onto the exterior observation terrace, letting the howling wind chill his burning face for half an hour before scurrying back.

Su Xin returned right on cue. The agonizing Q&A session had finally concluded. The grandmasters up front were chatting casually amongst themselves, while the Heavenly Dao Alliance contingent—including Yun Guoling—slumped in the rear seats like ashen corpses. They looked so utterly drained that a gentle breath could have scattered them into dust.

—"Is it over? I just went outside to transmit a communication talisman to notify my family of our safe arrival,"-Su Xin inquired with feigned nonchalance.-"So what are we doing next? Surely on the first day there is no need to discuss serious matters, right? It should be about time to head back and rest."

He did not want to remain in this mortifying venue for another second.

—"...Due to the insistent demands of these grandmasters, the Heavenly Spirit Sect is preparing to conduct a demonstration lecture on the standard format of academic reports,"-someone croaked aridly.

In reality, the grandmasters' exact words had been: *"After listening to half a day of useless drivel, surely you should show us something of actual value!"*

Coming on the heels of that mortifying Q&A, this comment had dealt devastating emotional damage to the envoys' shattered psyches.

Fortunately, since Su Xin hadn't heard it, Qi Jie felt there was still room for [face-saving rationalization](<# "Literal CN: 挽尊 (wǎnzūn) — abbreviation of 挽回尊严 (salvage dignity / save face), common internet slang for desperately trying to restore one's bruised ego">):

—"Even if we couldn't answer, does that mean the Heavenly Spirit Sect can? ...They even went so far as to send up a mere Foundation Establishment disciple."

Su Xin looked up. Sure enough, a female Foundation Establishment cultivator had stepped onto the stage.

—"I am Gan Tang,"-the young woman declared with graceful composure.-"The topic of today's presentation is *The Writing Format of Academic Reports*..."

[...Why does that name sound familiar? And why is it linked to an exceedingly dreadful memory?]

Su Xin shook his head vigorously as if trying to banish a traumatic shadow, then turned to his 'grand-nephew disciples' with a cold smirk:

—"Are you certain? That is a Foundation Establishment disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

Hearing this, Yun Guoling shrank deeper into his chair, vividly recalling his traumatic ordeal aboard the Kunpeng.

Having witnessed the Northern Domain's so-called "academic foundation," he had engaged in comprehensive self-reflection and was already contemplating forfeiting the tournament altogether.

Qi Jie, still failing to see the light, attempted to shoot back:

—"No matter what, I could hardly be inferior to a mere Foundation Establishment junior!"

Unfortunately, that proclamation was slapped in the face mere minutes later.

Gan Tang's presentation was straightforward; it barely touched upon cultivation at all, serving purely to instruct on the structural format of academic theses and reports. Yet the template examples she selected were lucid and intuitive, resolving everyone's core perplexities to the greatest possible extent.

Listening to this lecture, Qi Jie finally understood what the Heavenly Spirit Sect's "theses" actually were.

Su Xin understood as well.

Su Xin turned to Qi Jie with a cold sneer:

—"Look easy to you?"

He felt he would rather sit through another written exam than ever write one of those damned theses—to say nothing of the oral defense that followed.

Qi Jie covered his face and fled in shame.

His frantic retreat prompted several fellow envoys to follow suit.

Yet no one paid any heed to the quiet commotion in the back. The grandmasters were listening with utmost satisfaction.

Particularly because Gan Tang's presentation was purely about formatting structure. Unlike Guan Ye's, it was not cluttered with a mountain of specialized data and analytical breakthroughs that might distract the seniors into attributing the presentation's excellence solely to its subject matter.

This allowed the grandmasters to grasp directly and intuitively just how systematically this thesis framework could organize knowledge and what immense hassle it could spare them. Not only for their own cultivation techniques—more importantly, if they handed this down to their apprentices, disciples, and retainers, how neat, powerful, and impeccably detailed would their progress become!

Especially with the Heavenly Dao Alliance's "traditional cultivation world model" serving as a stark, [brutal contrast](<# "Literal CN: 惨烈的对比 (cǎnliè de duìbǐ) — chapter title motif">) right beforehand.

Once Gan Tang's presentation concluded, the crowd swarmed once more.

This time, they cornered Yuwen Xuan:

[—"Does the tournament still require additional assistants?"](<# "Speaker: Cultivation Senior">)

[—"We can dispatch our own disciples over to study!"](<# "Speaker: Cultivation Senior">)

[—"Whatever terms you require, everything is negotiable!"](<# "Speaker: Cultivation Senior">)

Yuwen Xuan beamed with a radiant smile:

—"Please, esteemed seniors, there is no need to rush. We can discuss everything at our leisure—"
