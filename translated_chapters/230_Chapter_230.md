# Chapter 230: Divine Feast

Is this... a banquet?

Am I... the master here? Have I arrived to convene and host this banquet?

No, wrong! Is this cognitive influence?!

Zhou Hui's gaze glazed over for a fleeting instant before turning entirely lucid in the very next heartbeat.

Yet the scenery around him did not dissipate.

At this moment, Zhou Hui stood atop a towering white jade platform, surrounded by a boundless sea of clouds. White jade low tables were arranged across the dais, laden with a dazzling variety of delicacies. Looking downward from the head throne, the jade steps descended straight into the rolling sea of clouds, where another jade terrace level with the mist held chime bells and grand musical instruments—resembling the moments preceding a grand banquet, waiting only for distinguished guests to take their seats before entertainers stepped forward to perform music and dance.

Naturally, the reality was far less delightful, for this was one of the secret realm's checkpoints.

A quarter of an hour ago, Zhou Hui's party had departed the Genesis Machine, returning to the secret realm to resume their [main quest](# "Literal CN: 主线任务 - gaming slang for main storyline quest"): locating Ran Deng's father, Ran Zhen.

Although Zhou Hui had ascertained Ran Zhen's coordinates through the Genesis Machine, without familiarity with the secret realm's layout, even he could not teleport about at will. Stepping into a trap himself was of little concern, but triggering a spatial snare that brought disaster upon his companions would turn a rescue mission into manslaughter.

Thus, Zhou Hui had no choice but to clear each of the secret realm's checkpoints in sequence.

The banquet here was undoubtedly one of them.

—"This feels like a very standard setup,"-Zhou Hui mused silently,-"employing cognitive influence to ensnare visitors in an endless feast so they can never break free? Still... am I overthinking it? This is a secret realm in the vicinity of the Genesis Machine; surely they wouldn't assume mere gluttony could bewitch incoming cultivators? It isn't as if everyone is the bottomless pit from my household..."

Yet just as Zhou Hui was pondering this, he noticed his "friends" marching toward the low tables with glazed, entranced expressions—including Jiuzhai, who currently floated as a glowing orb in the shape of a withered, rotting orange.

—"Ah, crap. Almost forgot,"-Zhou Hui muttered, snapping his fingers.

Instantly, as if their collars had been yanked violently from behind, everyone lurched backward in an awkward stumble, their expressions suddenly shifting into that of people jolting awake from a dream.

[—"What happened?"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

[—"Just now? Why did I..."](# "Speaker: Peilan")

[—"This place is utterly evil!"](# "Speaker: Sikong Yao")

A chorus of horrified cries broke out from the esteemed circle of Tribulation Transcendence and Mahayana cultivators.

Zhou Hui looked at them with slight perplexity:

—"The cognitive modification here is indeed rather potent; even I nearly succumbed. Still, it merely tempts one to eat a few bites. Given enough time, one ought to regain clarity, no?"

After all, none of them were greenhorn fledglings who had just stepped onto the immortal path. If anything, their over-the-top panic was what truly surprised Zhou Hui.

Yet everyone looked at Zhou Hui with even greater bewilderment.

—"Just wanting to sit at the table and eat a few bites?!"-Tao Zhu's eyes bugged out.-"I thought I was a plate of stir-fried bullfrog!"

*"Ribbit!"*

For the very first time, a croak of fervent agreement sounded from inside his belly.

—"I thought I was a pot of braised fox,"-Peilan stroked his slightly balding tail with mournful relief,-"and was even fretting over how to scald off my own fur, lest the deities find it unpleasant on the palate."

As the foremost divine physician of the realm, the singular ailment he remained completely powerless against was his own patchy alopecia.

Sikong Yao wore an equally shaken, relieved expression:

—"I wasn't even food! I believed myself to be the furnace fire beneath the meat cauldron, and nearly incinerated my own physical body!"

Hearing everyone's hallucinatory horrors, Tao Zhu glared at the dazzling spread of dishes atop the tables, dread creeping into his voice:

—"Since we all perceived ourselves as food, does that mean these dishes..."

—"No. The muscle grain does not match,"-Zhou Hui said calmly.

—"Untiring Immortal Venerable, sir... you seem exceptionally familiar with delicacies crafted from human flesh?"-Sikong Yao's expression twisted into profound terror.

It wasn't that he possessed a delicate aversion to cannibalistic cuisine—he was, after all, a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator bred in the ruthless den of the Palace of Carefree Wandering, long since numbed to grotesque atrocities. Rather, seeing an upright pillar of righteousness like Untiring speak of such butchery with such seasoned, clinical expertise was what chilled his bones to the marrow!

`?`

Zhou Hui shot him a baffled glance.

Zhou Hui was indeed familiar. To prevent a certain glutton in his household from secretly ingesting abominations without his knowledge, Zhou Hui had once dedicated an entire intensive study period to cataloging the cellular textures and striations of every conceivable edible and inedible "ingredient."

And so, he nodded naturally:

—"I am very familiar. Why?"

Whereupon Zhou Hui watched in growing perplexity as Sikong Yao turned deathly pale, hunching his shoulders as though desperately wishing to disintegrate into thin air.

[What is wrong with this fellow? Is there any need for such terror? Or has the cognitive influence lingered?]

Zhou Hui opened his mouth to inquire. While Sikong Yao could hardly be classified as a "friend," Zhou Hui had conscripted him by force, and thus felt an instructor's duty of care toward his physical well-being. Little did he realize that the more benevolence and concern he radiated, the deeper Sikong Yao's psychological trauma plunged!

Fortunately, Jiuzhai chose that moment to speak:

—"Whether it contains mortal flesh is beside the point. If required, it certainly could, for this is a divine feast."

[—"A divine feast?"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

[—"Sacrificial offerings dedicated and consecrated to the gods."](# "Speaker: Jiuzhai")

—"I obviously know what sacrificial offerings are! The question is, why would a divine feast be laid out here?! Could there be some evil god?"-Tao Zhu asked nervously, before pausing to look at Zhou Hui.-"Wait, didn't you say this place is connected to the Heavenly Court? How could there be an evil god?"

—"Cease your blathering! It is no evil god!"-Jiuzhai corrected even more agitatedly than Tao Zhu.-"This is a consecrated rite offering tribute to the celestial gods!"

—"What celestial gods?"-Zhou Hui asked.

Zhou Hui inspected the banquet table with clinical scrutiny, confirming he had never seen comparable offerings in any scripture:

—"Neither the ceremonial placement rules nor the varieties of delicacies align with any sacrificial rites within my knowledge. This does not appear to be an offering dedicated to the deities and immortals of the Heavenly Court known to us. If anything, the grade of the dishes on this table is astronomical! The raw ingredients could never be nurtured by the current spiritual energy of our mortal domain. Even a... True Immortal would likely be unable to withstand the raw spiritual potency contained within, would they not?"

—"It's actually that magnificent?!"-Tao Zhu's eyes lit up like lanterns.

—"Naturally it is magnificent—because you and I only possess the qualifications to be served as garnish!"-Peilan gave Tao Zhu's sleeve a warning yank.-"If you rashly jump onto that table, Heaven only knows what grisly end awaits you."

—"I know that! I'm not some reckless greenhorn."

—"Heh. And how did that golden toad end up swallowed into your belly all those centuries ago? Clearly, your memory is nonexistent."

—"Stop bringing up ancient history!"

[Excuse me... are you two completely blind to the fact that there is actually someone standing right here who is qualified to sit at that table?!]

Sikong Yao had zero interest in Tao Zhu and Peilan's bickering. Shifting his gaze away from Zhou Hui in sheer self-preservation, he harbored a sneaking suspicion that Untiring Immortal Venerable could consume this entire divine feast without suffering the slightest spiritual indigestion.

Zhou Hui certainly could.

Precisely because he could, he had felt that fleeting spark of temptation, causing his perception to momentarily falter.

Yet Zhou Hui did not attribute this to his personal martial prowess. Rather, when repairing the Ascension Stairway and seizing control of this world's Heavenly Dao, he had assimilated a fragment of divinity. And as far as Zhou Hui knew, the essence of his current state was closer to a primordial native deity of this realm rather than an immortal official certified by the Heavenly Court—making him an explicit target for celestial eradication. Yet inside a secret realm leading into the Heavenly Court, there existed a "divine feast" consecrated to celestial gods, leaving Zhou Hui profoundly curious as to who the intended recipient truly was.

Thus, he fixed an unblinking gaze upon Jiuzhai:

—"So, for whom are these sacrificial offerings prepared? And how do you know of them?"

Prompted by Zhou Hui's inquiry, the others ceased their chatter, suddenly waking up to the crux of the matter.

Delicacies that even Zhou Hui had never laid eyes upon, prepared in the solemn ritual of a divine feast—yet Jiuzhai recognized them instantly! It seemed the old charlatan harbored secrets of immense depth!

—"Do not look at me like that,"-Jiuzhai sighed. Ever since Untiring refused to let him remain behind at the Genesis Machine, he knew the man had grown suspicious.-"It is not that I deliberately concealed it; I simply never knew how to broach the topic. In truth... my lineage originates from the Heavenly Court."

`?!`

Peilan and Tao Zhu's eyes popped wide.

Although Jiuzhai had already sported the withered guise of a doddering elder back when they first stepped into the cultivation world, neither had ever stopped to contemplate his true age. As long as it hadn't sprouted mold, who on earth would care about the vintage of a dried tangerine peel!

Zhou Hui was also somewhat surprised:

—"Like Goddess Ren Ran?"

—"Pah, pah! Who would ever resemble that brainless little broad?! We are the [Chroniclers of this realm!](# "Literal CN: 记录者 - record keepers / chroniclers")"-Jiuzhai uttered words that sounded embarrassingly [chuunibyou](# "Literal CN: 中二病 - eighth-grader syndrome / grandiose delusions of cosmic importance"), before elaborating in detail:—"The heritage of our Shenzhi Altar dates back to a Heavenly Court far preceding the God-Demon War. We were dispatched to this world to observe and record the fluctuations of local time and space..."

—"Aha! Aren't you an undercover mole?!"-Tao Zhu yelled.

—"Who is a mole?! With the wretched condition of our world, spacetime was already a tangled disaster to begin with! Add to that an absurd immortal who resets the wheel of reincarnation seven times just because his disciples kicked the bucket—if we didn't call in external reinforcements every now and then, how was anyone supposed to survive?!"-Jiuzhai shot back, before pausing slightly to tack on:—"Supposedly... it used to be effective."

[—"Used to?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"The Heavenly Court today is not what it once was,"-Jiuzhai shook his head, casting a furtive, anxious glance at Zhou Hui.-"Allegedly, back when the Shenzhi Altar was first erected, the authorities granted almost every petition to maintain this world's integrity. Nowadays, however... one cannot shake the feeling that something catastrophic happened up there. That is why I haven't reported your affairs to the higher-ups! Not a single syllable!"

If anything, Shenzhi Altar had been practicing "selective recording" since before the God-Demon War.

Whether the celestial bureaucracy simply didn't care or had failed to notice remained a mystery.

Jiuzhai harbored a lingering suspicion that the Heavenly Court might have forgotten his existence entirely.

Zhou Hui was not particularly concerned with bureaucratic neglect; his focus leaned entirely toward academic inquiries:

—"I see. Since your lineage hails from the Heavenly Court, this divine feast belongs to the Heavenly Court as well? Yet if they are already the Heavenly Court, what manner of deity could they possibly be consecrating sacrifices to?"

—"On this point, even our Shenzhi Altar possesses no historical records,"-Jiuzhai admitted.-"We only know that by ancestral decree, the Celestial Emperor conducts a Sky-Tour every five hundred years to perform a sacrificial rite. The divine feast prepared for the occasion is tailored according to the specific wish prayed for that year. Because no name of an enshrined deity is ever invoked, people generally regard it as a symbolic ceremony. However..."

At this juncture, Jiuzhai glanced over his shoulder toward the direction of the Genesis Machine.

—"Are you suggesting they are sacrificing to a machine? But machines don't eat food!"-Tao Zhu stared begrudgingly at the exquisite yet forbidden delicacies.

—"That is why it is called a ceremony,"-Jiuzhai explained, striving for academic completeness.-"According to lore, once the sacrificial rites conclude, the offerings are divided and shared among the assembled immortals, signifying that the deity has accepted the tribute..."

In all honesty, Jiuzhai felt he had strayed completely off-topic. This useless trivia was merely part of the mandatory curriculum at Shenzhi Altar; during his studies, he had frequently doubted its practical value. Even if it coincidentally proved relevant today, it had zero bearing on resolving their current predicament. Surely they couldn't just devour the divine feast, complete the sacrifice, and stroll out of the secret realm...

Just as this thought crossed Jiuzhai's mind, a resounding, wet chewing sound abruptly cracked through the silence!

Everyone jolted, turning in unison toward the low tables.

Right before their disbelieving eyes, atop the side table flanked beside the head seat, the centerpiece dish—an entree resembling a roasted suckling pig—vanished!

And it vanished in an exceptionally eerie manner.

Before their eyes, the "pig" appeared to be brutally torn apart by sharp, invisible fangs, exposing the tender meat within. Then, one chomp, two chomps, three chomps... gone!

Though termed a "suckling pig"—setting aside the fact that ordinary pigs do not sprout horns, and aside from its plumpness it bore little resemblance to a swine, appearing more like a spirit beast from an alien realm—its sheer volume and meat were not something that could be polished off in two or three bites. That was decidedly not a maw belonging to a humanoid entity!

—"Does this qualify as a deity feeding?"-Peilan asked.

—"With table manners like that, it's definitely an evil god, right?"-Tao Zhu added.

—"That makes no sense!"-Jiuzhai cried in utter disbelief.

Zhou Hui, meanwhile, stared at the empty platter with an increasingly peculiar expression. For some unfathomable reason, he found this boorish feeding technique and thoroughly unrefined chewing sound dreadfully familiar.

As the "suckling pig" was completely devoured, the spiritual energy upon the terrace... no, far more than mere spiritual energy, an indescribable essence was draining away, undergoing an arcane transmutation as it surged toward the invisible diner across the table.

Jiuzhai sensed it as well, attempting to reconcile himself with "reality":

—"Power is circulating... Could it truly be a god?"

[A god? Unlikely.]

Yet Zhou Hui harbored an uncanny premonition; that eerie sense of familiarity was only intensifying.

And in the very next heartbeat, that familiarity peaked:

The remaining dishes atop the side table vanished like clouds swept before a tempest. Immediately following, the spread atop the neighboring head seat—the very throne Zhou Hui had almost occupied during his momentary cognitive lapse—began to exhibit unnatural movement. As though abruptly springing to life, the dishes crept forward millimeter by millimeter, then executed a sudden airborne leap, landing squarely upon the side table whose centerpiece had just been devoured, before vanishing once more in a whirlwind of phantom mastication!

Evidently, it was not the "master" of the head seat partaking of the meal; someone was brazenly stealing food!

Zhou Hui felt a lecture rising straight to the tip of his tongue.

—"Surely not?"-Zhou Hui muttered, desperately attempting to grant his eldest disciple the benefit of the doubt.-"Didn't that boy set off to locate his Second Junior Brother?..."

Yet deep down, a profound unease gnawed at him.

Pinching his fingers, Zhou Hui initiated astromantic calculations to divine just where on earth his household glutton had wandered.

Yet before his divination could yield an answer, spurred by the frantic evaporation of the feast atop the head throne, the spiritual energy upon the terrace churned into a violent maelstrom! This time, however, the power did not flow toward the unseen diner across the table. Instead, it surged straight toward Zhou Hui!

Luminescent particles settled over Zhou Hui's hem, draped across his shoulders, and dusted his silver hair like a snowfall of pure light, bathing him in a brilliant silver radiance akin to a true, transcendent deity!

***

[—"No reaction? The sacrifice failed?"](# "Speaker: Immortal Official")

At the opposing edge of the cloud sea, atop an open-air sacrificial altar, several figures stood exchanging baffled glances before the vacant platform.

Had Yuwen Xuan still been perched within the spatial fissure high above, he would have instantly recognized this as the very altar he had appraised earlier as "temporarily beyond his capabilities to devour." At this juncture, the majority of the sacrificial retinue had already dispersed, leaving only a handful of leaders standing at the forefront, watching the sacrificial table atop the terrace with bated breath.

As for the offerings adorning the sacrificial table, they were identical not only to the spreads near the banquet's head seats in the celestial gardens, but also to the spread upon the cloud terrace within the secret realm.

If there was any distinction, it was that while these offerings were freshly prepared and presented only moments ago from the highest tier of spiritual beasts and flora, they were now completely devoid of warmth and spiritual luminescence—resembling shoddy papier-mâché replicas that retained nothing but their outer contours.

Under normal circumstances, such phenomenon signified that the gods had accepted and partaken of the tribute, and were about to bestow divine blessings. Yet after waiting for what felt like an eternity, the dignitaries had witnessed not a single trace of the customary "divine miracles."

The black-bearded middle-aged sovereign at the forefront, crowned with a ceremonial crystal imperial mortarboard with jade bead tassels, finally let his impatience show, demanding in a frigid tone:

—"What is the meaning of this, Tian Yan?"

—"Your Majesty, please be at ease,"-the purple-robed immortal addressed as "Tian Yan" consoled gently. His countenance was extraordinarily refined, his brow and eyes bearing a fifty-percent resemblance to Qu Qianxing, yet his air was cultured and scholarly, lacking Qu Qianxing's breathtakingly sharp, venomous glamour.-"Perhaps the divine power bestowed this time is not channeled through the direct manifestations of the past..."

—"Why don't you just state it plainly: this year's wish cannot be granted?"-another immortal clad in magnificent golden armor sneered.

—"What could possibly be ungrantable?"-Tian Yan countered with a mild smile.-"His Majesty the Celestial Emperor undertook this Sky-Tour sacrifice to pray for the three thousand minor worlds, seeking peace, health, and prosperity for all mortal subjects. What heavenly deity would ever decline such a noble prayer?"

[As expected of the chief official of the Ministry of Celestial Destiny—his mouth is packed with nothing but absolute drivel.]

Wasn't the actual catalyst for this Sky-Tour the fact that the Celestial Emperor had suffered a waking nightmare in broad daylight, sensing his heavenly mandate was running out, prompting him to enact the Sky-Tour rites to interrogate fate regarding his future?

Such was the consensus circulating silently among the other immortals present.

Naturally, not a soul dared voice such thoughts aloud. Given that the sacrifice had yielded zero divine response, speaking the truth would be tantamount to poking the Celestial Emperor in the chest and declaring: *Your prayer was rejected; you'd better start fitting yourself for a coffin and decide which of your children will carry the pall and usurp the throne!*

Thus, regardless of whatever private scheming churned in their hearts, none of the immortals dared stir up trouble. When pressed, they could only parrot Tian Yan's sycophantic drivel:

—"In-indeed, that makes sense. Even without an instantaneous sign, the heavenly gods will surely bless the Heavenly Court with enduring peace and stability."

Regrettably, the Celestial Emperor's visage remained stormy.

Evidently, what he craved was not the enduring stability of the Heavenly Court, but his own immortal longevity and eternal reign.

—"Tian Yan,"-he commanded.

—"This minister is here,"-Tian Yan answered at once.

—"Since the sacrifice has yielded no result, proceed with the original plan. Recall Our children to the Heav..."-The Celestial Emperor paused, his brows knitting tightly together:—"No. Dispatch them to the Immortal-Demon Realm first. The... embryo you crafted, was it stationed there?"

—"It is."

—"Then see to it!"

So declaring, the Celestial Emperor cast one final, grim glance at the cold, leached offerings upon the altar, exhaled a heavy sigh, and stepped down the terrace.

Instead of descending toward the main gates, however, he directed his stride toward the side exit.

An attendant ventured in a hushed whisper:

—"Your Majesty, the banquet..."

The Celestial Emperor shot him a blistering glare, instantly choking the servant's voice into silence. Another courtier kicked him while he was down:

—"The sacrifice itself has failed; what significance remains in a banquet?"

[—"Then the banquet..."](# "Speaker: Attendant")

—"Let it continue!"-the Celestial Emperor replied this time.-"The banquet is an integral part of the sacrificial rites; it must not be neglected. However, We shall not attend. In the days to follow, We must enter closed-door seclusion at Jingxin Cliff here in Xuanpu. Ensure that no one disturbs Us!"

—"By Your Majesty's command!"

Ministers and attendants chorused in assent, bowing deeply until well after the Celestial Emperor's silhouette had completely vanished. Only then did they straighten their backs, sinking into a heavy, wordless silence.

The stifling atmosphere was finally shattered by the True Immortal clad in golden armor.

Sneering at Tian Yan, he challenged:

—"Lord Tian Yan, shall we head to the banquet together?"

—"I think not,"-Tian Yan replied with a placid smile, seemingly deaf to the sarcasm dripping from the man's tone.-"Since His Majesty has issued his decree, I naturally must..."

Mid-sentence, his body lurched sideways in an unnatural spasm, nearly crashing flat against the flagstones!

Nearby courtiers scrambled to catch him:

—"Lord Tian Yan—!"

—"No, I am unhurt,"-Tian Yan steadied himself, raising a hand to ward off their assistance, before clasping his hands toward his colleagues:—"I have urgent affairs to attend to and must take my leave."

With that, he departed the sacrificial altar in hurried strides.

An attendant vaguely caught him muttering the word "seal," though none could fathom what it portended.

With Tian Yan gone, the atmosphere grew even more awkward.

Someone addressed the golden-armored warrior:

—"Celestial General, will you be attending the banquet?"

—"I am! Of course I am! I'm not like those bastards harboring stomachs full of filthy schemes! With free food and wine waiting, why wouldn't I go?!"-the Celestial General barked, glaring at his peers.-"None of you are planning to concoct excuses to ditch, are you?"

[—"How could we?"](# "Speaker: Immortal Official")

No one wished to stir up trouble at such a delicate juncture. Furthermore, with matters in such a precarious state, if the senior officials failed to appear, wouldn't they be broadcasting to the entire assembly of immortals that the sacrifice had gone catastrophically awry?

And so, the remaining high-ranking immortal officials prepared to make their way toward the feast. To their bewilderment, before they had taken even a few strides, several celestial soldiers came sprinting in sheer hysteria:

—"Celestial General! Crisis on the front lines! The demons... the demons are storming the Heavenly Court!"

—"WHAT?!"-the Celestial General roared in shock.

Abandoning all thought of the feast, he stormed away, cursing a blue streak.

The remaining immortals stared after his departure in dumbfounded stupor. One ventured:

[—"Is this banquet still going to continue or not?"](# "Speaker: Immortal Official")

[—"This..."](# "Speaker: Immortal Official")

Before anyone could formulate a response, several celestial attendants came hurrying over, looking at the officials with anxious faces, desperately wanting to deliver a report yet unsure of whom to address.

An official with an approachable demeanor inquired:

—"Whom are you seeking? Lord Tian Yan? The Celestial General? Both have already departed..."

—"N-no, my Lord,"-the lead attendant stammered.-"We came to seek guidance from Your Excellencies."

[—"Guidance?"](# "Speaker: Immortal Official")

—"Though the honored immortals have only just taken their seats, the banquet delicacies have already been completely, entirely... devoured clean! Even the backup reserves of ingredients! How are we to proceed with the remainder of the feast?!"

[—"WHAT?!"](# "Speaker: Immortal Officials")
