# Chapter 191: Diverse Manifestations of PTSD

Guan Ye was an honest child.

Or so Zhou Hui believed.

This time, it wasn't a matter of Zhou Hui's teacher-goggles filter. Anyone who knew and understood Guan Ye would agree with this assessment.

In the Heavenly Spirit Sect, even Yuwen Xuan—who loved raising hell most of all—would, in the worst-case scenario, rather try his luck with Qu Qianxing when picking an accomplice for mischief than order about this rigid, dull Fourth Junior Brother. Because Guan Ye would ignore him one hundred percent of the time.

Yet it was precisely this honest child who did the most terrifying things imaginable.

Take, for example, the third lifetime. And the fourth lifetime.

In the third lifetime, Zhou Hui mistakenly assumed he was the sole target of the Heavenly Dao's calculations. To avoid dragging down his disciples, he accepted no disciples in that lifetime, instead fostering them out to other places to be raised.

Among them, Guan Ye, Mo Bei, and Mo Xi were sent to the sole Tribulation Transcendence cultivator in the Western Domain: the [Delusion-Entering Ghost Emperor](# "Ruwang Ghost Emperor (入妄鬼帝)").

The Delusion-Entering Ghost Emperor was the master of Bi'an Tian—the Realm of the Other Shore—and, true to his title, was a ghost cultivator.

For Mo Bei and Mo Xi's Extreme Yin Constitutions, his cultivation technique was essentially [fighting poison with poison](# "Literal CN: 以毒攻毒 (yǐ dú gōng dú) — curing toxicity with toxicity, here using ghost arts to stabilize Extreme Yin"). In that lifetime, Zhou Hui didn't know the twins possessed yin spirit bloodlines, though from a yin spirit perspective, it was a [perfect match for their major](# "Literal CN: 专业对口 (zhuānyè duìkǒu) — modern college graduate slang for finding a career matching one's university major"). So long as Mo Bei and Mo Xi inherited that lineage of techniques, if they ever ran into anyone plotting against them, it was anyone's guess who would devour whom.

The only issue was that the academic ethos at Bi'an Tian was rather poor, and the Delusion-Entering Ghost Emperor's own moral baseline wasn't particularly high. Zhou Hui was deeply worried his children would be corrupted by their schooling.

Out of this consideration, Zhou Hui sent his most steadfast, conscientious disciple, Guan Ye, along with them.

Of course, for Guan Ye, this had also been the best choice.

—Or so Zhou Hui believed at the time.

Because in every lifetime Guan Ye could never escape the agony of having his bones extracted. Dragging that exposed segment of spine, he inevitably drew strange, alienating stares wherever he went. Bi'an Tian, however, was the supreme sanctuary of ghost cultivators. That ghostly place was teeming with all manner of bizarre demons and ghouls; beings running about with half their skeletons exposed were everywhere, so there was no need to worry about aesthetic discrimination.

Furthermore, the Delusion-Entering Ghost Emperor had originated as Zhou Hui's contracted ghost slave before Zhou Hui granted him his freedom. He therefore owed Zhou Hui a heavy debt of gratitude. To repay the favor and sever past karma, he possessed ample motive and capability to shelter the disciples Zhou Hui entrusted to him, as well as the appropriate techniques to help Guan Ye harmonize with his ill-fitting replacement spine.

Most importantly, Guan Ye was the most honest and upright among Zhou Hui's disciples. Zhou Hui believed that even amidst Bi'an Tian's atrocious learning environment, Guan Ye would guard his Dao heart, cultivate in tranquil seclusion, set a sterling example for Mo Bei and Mo Xi, and keep the two youngest from being led astray.

Nor did Guan Ye disappoint Zhou Hui's expectations.

Unseduced by Bi'an Tian's debauched atmosphere, he remained as diligent and hardworking as he had been in the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Compounded by ghost cultivation techniques that yielded rapid advancements and the Ghost Emperor's almost doting deluge of resources, in the third lifetime, Guan Ye's cultivation actually reached a peak across all reincarnation cycles: the Nascent Soul Middle Stage! His cultivation had surpassed even Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing in that lifetime.

Unfortunately, whether because cultivation had progressed too smoothly or because he had ultimately been tainted by Bi'an Tian's ghost cultivators, Guan Ye took vengeance as the sole goal of his entire existence.

He sat idly by while Yuwen Xuan abducted Mo Bei and Mo Xi, confronting the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City camp led by Qu Qianxing and Jian Nuo. Meanwhile, Guan Ye secretly obliterated and refined the branch halls of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, refining his mortal enemy into a ghost slave! He also covertly colluded with Qu Qianxing, having Qu Qianxing offer the Department of Works as a bargaining chip in exchange for intelligence on Yuwen Xuan. Yet when Qu Qianxing demanded the location of Zhou Hui's secluded cultivation retreat, Guan Ye flatly refused him. The resulting butterfly effect burned down that lifetime's Child of Destiny's hometown, and caused the fellow to "accidentally" breach the protective formations around Zhou Hui's retreat, allowing Qu Qianxing to pursue him there. Everything repeated itself, terminating that cycle of reincarnation.

The fourth lifetime was the lifetime in which Zhou Hui was deserted by all his disciples.

In that lifetime, burdened with crushing resentment, the disciples reborn across multiple cycles tore into each other in endless feuds. Before the "Child of Destiny" even arrived—before Mo Bei and Mo Xi could even enter the sect—they had already wrecked the Heavenly Spirit Sect and ground half of Zhou Hui's old life away. In this, Guan Ye played an immense, pivotal role.

Don't misunderstand: Guan Ye had not betrayed him.

In fact, regarding Zhou Hui saving his life, Guan Ye was grateful with his very soul across every reincarnation, caring nothing for whether he was "released into the wild" or formally accepted as a disciple—unlike Yuwen Xuan, Qu Qianxing, and Lu Shulong.

Precisely because he felt this way, he found his other martial brothers in the third lifetime—who had raised hell demanding to locate Zhou Hui and get a clear explanation—utterly incomprehensible. He even harbored a fury toward them no less venomous than toward his mortal enemies.

—"It was all because of your willful disobedience that Master failed his ascension in the previous life!"

Thinking along these lines, Guan Ye unified his grand cause of revenge with his objective of stopping his martial siblings from slaughtering each other.

In that lifetime, Guan Ye could be said to have "voluntarily" let his spine be extracted. He refined the cultivation clan he belonged to into ghost slaves, resumed the ghost cultivation arts of the third lifetime, and, upon entering the Heavenly Spirit Sect, slowly refined the mountain-protecting grand array while laying deadly mechanisms inside his martial siblings' immortal caves.

What was truly fatal was that because Guan Ye was so honest by nature, no one ever genuinely guarded against him. Even Zhou Hui never imagined Guan Ye could commit any villainy. In fact, not wanting to watch his disciples stress and quarrel, Zhou Hui had even entrusted a portion of the mountain-protecting array's administrative authority to Guan Ye. Thus, Zhou Hui had the distinct privilege of witnessing the grand finale:

At the crucial moment right before Zhou Hui entered secluded cultivation, Guan Ye took a blade and stabbed every single one of his blood-crazed martial siblings to death, one by one—save for the missing Yuwen Xuan and Ran Deng, who had [lain flat like a salted fish](# "Literal CN: 咸鱼躺 (xiányú tǎng) — slacking off completely like a salted fish") for three lifetimes! Every single Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple was refined into a ghost slave. The mountain-protecting array was inverted into a spiritual energy battery to assist Zhou Hui's ascension!

Only, where on earth would Zhou Hui have had the mood to ascend at that moment?!

Standing before the blood-drenched Guan Ye, the fact that Zhou Hui hadn't personally manifested as heavenly lightning to blast this disciple into ash was already testament to his consummate cultivation and pedagogical restraint.

It was hard to say whether Zhou Hui in the fifth lifetime—who spared no expense to ascend at breakneck speed—had developed [PTSD](# "Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder") from Guan Ye, dreading that if he hesitated even a moment, the Heavenly Spirit Sect would turn into a slaughterhouse all over again.

Incidentally, because Guan Ye had been too busy raising hell in the fourth lifetime, his cultivation was the complete opposite of the previous cycle: the lowest across all lifetimes, permanently stalled at Foundation Establishment Early Stage. Relying entirely on consummate refinement arts and clockwork mechanisms, he had forcefully refined the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect into his construct! Looking back now, that had been the first subtle manifestation of his terrifying talent for artifact refinement.

As for the sixth lifetime—namely, the tribulations he inflicted upon the so-called 'protagonist' in the Heavenly Dao's novel script—compared to the 'pleasant surprise' he delivered to Zhou Hui in the fourth lifetime, they were hardly worth mentioning.

—This wasn't to say Guan Ye was only harsh with his own siblings; toward the 'protagonist', he had been an absolute vengeful ghoul. Especially after the Heavenly Spirit Sect was annihilated, when (Guan Ye believed) all his martial brothers and sisters had perished and he was left utterly destitute and alone, he refined himself into a puppet, consolidated the realm's largest villainous demonic faction, and drove the 'protagonist' into an inescapable dead end. In truth, he was far more of a hidden side-quest final boss than Mo Xi, who held the designated mid-boss role. In fact, because that arc was far too ['tormenting for the protagonist'](# "Literal CN: 虐主 (nüèzhǔ) — web novel trope where the protagonist is relentlessly tortured, humiliated, and driven to despair"), much of it was censored and cut when adapted into the television drama. And since Zhou Hui was hardly the sort of person to catch up on the source material of such pulp trash, he didn't know specifically what Guan Ye had done. Alas, the 'protagonist' [lacked all martial ethics](# "Literal CN: 不讲武德 (bù jiǎng wǔdé) — internet meme from Master Ma Baoguo: failing to observe martial sportsmanship/ethics"), cheating directly with heavenly lightning to blast Guan Ye until his soul scattered to the winds, which forced Zhou Hui to expend massive effort in subsequent resets to gather his fractured soul back together.

It was also through these lifetimes that Zhou Hui realized how terrifyingly obstinate Guan Ye was when it came to "vengeance."

Or rather, precisely because he was too "honest," Guan Ye had a [one-track mind](# "Literal CN: 一根筋 (yī gēn jīn) — single tendon/wire; stubborn, inflexible, single-minded"). Incapable of regulating his own emotions, he could never let go of a grievance once acknowledged. His previous silence had merely been dormant suppression; the longer it brewed, the thicker the rancor became, impervious even to the cleansing wash of cyclic reincarnation.

Therefore, Zhou Hui didn't believe a single Guan Xinguang would be enough to quell Guan Ye's hatred.

In the final analysis, Guan Xinguang was merely a puppet of the Upper Realm.

Even killing him would not achieve complete vengeance.

Thus, in Zhou Hui's original plan, once he untangled the Heavenly Dao and investigated the state of the Heavenly Court, he would take his disciple straight to the mastermind's doorstep to settle the score.

Nevertheless, letting Guan Ye vent a breath of fury beforehand wasn't a bad thing either.

Zhou Hui was genuinely worried that if Guan Ye bottled it up for too long, he would bottle himself into a breakdown all over again.

—"The idea of a qualification examination is exceedingly good,"-Zhou Hui praised, expressing great approval of Ran Deng's initiative.-"Even without the teleportation array project, we could consider establishing it on a long-term basis."

The only point that puzzled Zhou Hui was:

—"How did you convince Guan Xinguang to come sit for the exam?"

After all, Guan Xinguang had a guilty conscience. Like a [turtle shrinking into its shell](# "Literal CN: 缩头乌龟 (suōtóu wūguī) — a coward hiding his head like a turtle"), he had holed up inside the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works, refusing to step out. Without hard evidence, the Mei-Zhu couple would naturally shield their own subordinate—this had nothing to do with personal friendship. Thus, for Zhou Hui to let Guan Ye claim his vengeance back then, he truly would have had to slaughter his way through the Heavenly Dao Alliance and completely burn bridges with the Mei-Zhu couple.

—"...I don't know either,"-Ran Deng mumbled, hanging her head.

She didn't dare confess what she had unleashed Eldest Senior Brother to do.

Ran Deng reported with ninety percent truth and ten percent falsehood:

—"Purely because we were short on hands, I thought of using qualification examinations to recruit talent. As Master knows, regarding professional qualifications and rankings recognized across the cultivation world, people have trusted the Heavenly Dao Alliance's accreditation for millennia. So I tapped a few connections on my end to see if I could persuade the Department of Works, while giving Fourth Junior Brother an opportunity along the way. Who would have guessed that word quickly arrived from that side saying, seemingly due to other matters, that scoundrel actively volunteered to take the qualification examination!"

—"Hmm. Su Mei did mention that Goddess Ren Ran visited the Department of Works for some unknown reason..."-Zhou Hui said, furrowing his brows.

Hearing this, Ran Deng grew somewhat worried:

—"Master, could there be some hidden problem?"

—"They're delivering themselves to our doorstep. What problem could there be?"-Zhou Hui countered, letting out a soft chuckle.

Ran Deng looked up at Master's finely sculpted profile, unable to resist curling the corner of her lips.

[In the Heavenly Spirit Sect, isn't the only truly harmless one just me?]

she muttered silently under her breath.

She had genuinely been a little spooked by Guan Ye's reaction earlier.

Yet no matter how silent she tried to be, Ran Deng's aura could never elude Zhou Hui. Fortunately, Zhou Hui didn't seem to catch the words clearly, casting a curious glance at his 'most harmless' disciple:

—"What was that?"

—"Nothing!"-Ran Deng denied, shaking her head vigorously.

Zhou Hui smiled and didn't press the matter, pivoting to ask:

—"Speaking of which, why did you rush all the way here? Didn't I say I would return?"

After all, a three-day journey took Zhou Hui merely an instant; Ran Deng making this trek was hardly cost-effective.

—"Isn't it because communication with Master has been spotty? You clearly agreed a week ago that you would return immediately, yet you never came back. One glance and anyone could tell you were lost in research,"-Ran Deng complained.-"The Tenth Month Festival is only a few days away; we must have the qualification examination questions ready before then."

—"Ah. My apologies,"-Zhou Hui said with a rueful smile.

He had indeed procrastinated a bit. Knowing he could return in the next split-second, he hadn't felt in any rush. Meanwhile, the hardware upgrade on the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree was pressing; he kept thinking he would return after finishing this stage, then after finishing the next stage. Without realizing it, Ran Deng had already arrived, and he still hadn't returned.

Today, it was solely because the Mei-Zhu couple had contacted him regarding the Department of Works suddenly volunteering to sit for the qualification exam that Zhou Hui had paused his adjustments to the Sacred Tree and noticed Ran Deng's arrival.

Zhou Hui attempted to make amends:

—"As it happens, I expanded the Sacred Tree's database over these past few days... that is to say, the Langhuan library received an upgrade. There are plenty of intriguing records inside that can be incorporated into the exam question bank. Ah-Ran, come have a look with me?"

[...Does this mean the exam questions are being dumped on me as well?!]

Ran Deng knitted her brows, wanting to decline.

She had traveled here to drag Master back to write the questions, not to contract the entire workload herself!

Before she could speak, she heard Master ask:

—"By the way, when you left, what was Xuan'er... what was Yuwen Xuan doing? I heard Qianxing and Shulong are both away? Is it really alright leaving only him behind?"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

Ran Deng's heart gave a violent jolt. Yet not a single ripple showed on her face; instead, she replied in an impeccably steady tone:

—"At least when I departed, Eldest Senior Brother was hanging obediently from the tree. Why? Why does Master suddenly ask about Eldest Senior Brother?"

—"It simply crossed my mind,"-Zhou Hui said.-"I don't believe I've ever left him alone to watch the house."

—"Hahaha, Eldest Senior Brother isn't a dog! Leaving him alone for a few days, could he possibly tear down the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-Ran Deng laughed.

Hard to say.

Even the most honest Guan Ye could stir up a cosmic catastrophe when left unattended, let alone Yuwen Xuan.

Thinking carefully, during the wildest lifetimes in the reincarnations, on the surface it looked like Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing were both raising hell. Yet whenever settlement time arrived, Yuwen Xuan had vanished without a trace. Although he had calculated against himself, piecing it all together, that fellow seemed to have the most skeletons in his closet.

Zhou Hui pondered.

Originally, he had wanted to ask Ran Deng a few more questions about conditions in the sect. Unwittingly, however, their steps brought them into the palace where Zhou Hui was temporarily residing. Dozens of panels glowed brightly, displaying the fruits of Zhou Hui's recent labors.

Ran Deng immediately let out an exaggerated gasp:

—"Whoa! Are these the records from Langhuan? It's my first time seeing them!"

Saying so, she strode toward the debugging screen of the newly connected Yin Spirit Cloud terminal.

[You misunderstand...]

Zhou Hui was about to explain, only to see Ran Deng lean close to the screen. After skimming a few lines, her expression turned serious.

—"Do you have any thoughts?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"No, Master's work is far too profound; I don't understand much of it,"-Ran Deng answered honestly, though she clearly had an ulterior scheme.-"However, Master, don't you feel these immortal remains and yin spirits... make exceptionally fitting exam questions for artifact refiners? Specifically for Fourth Junior Brother."

As Ran Deng spoke, she retrieved the formation plate she carried on her person and casually projected a model upon it for Zhou Hui to inspect.

*The most harmless disciple?*

Zhou Hui looked at the formation plate, then at his third disciple, falling into deep contemplation.

* * *

Something was wrong!

The atmosphere lately was completely, utterly wrong!

Or so Su Xin thought.

Clutching his little cloak, he surveyed his surroundings with utmost caution.

Of course, as the sole offspring of two Tribulation Transcendence titans and the little ancestor of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, it was virtually impossible for Su Xin to be in any genuine danger.

Indeed, he was situated safely aboard a gargantuan treasure ship.

The vessel boasted sixty-six colossal sails woven from mermaid shark-silk, an eighteen-story compound deck citadel, and a keel forged from the skeleton of an ancient vicious flood dragon. Soaring through the azure sky, it resembled a mobile aerial fortress.

Yet far more staggering than the ship itself was the fact that at this very moment, roughly one-third of the cultivators across the Northern and Eastern Domains (data sourced from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's most recent cultivation census) were aboard this vessel, sailing in magnificent, sweeping procession toward the Central Domain!

And their destination was merely an Immortal Arts Assembly in the Central Domain.

Even Su Xin had no idea how the travel entourage had exploded into something so absurdly grandiose.

He couldn't even recall how the expedition had originally been decided.

It seemed to have started when Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable repaired the mythical Ascension Stairway, sending shockwaves across the entire cultivation realm!

For the glorious cause of ascension, countless cultivators yearned to travel to the Central Domain to investigate firsthand, hoping to negotiate with the Untiring Immortal Venerable for rights concerning the Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital. Consequently, major sects across the Northern Domain organized tour groups to head south, clamoring for the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City to take the lead—after all, the vast majority lacked the pedigree to speak face-to-face with a Tribulation Transcendence titan.

Annoyed to wit's end, the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Sky City finally saw City Lord Jian decide to forge a grand treasure ship.

Up to that point, it was merely an ordinary diplomatic tour of the cultivation world.

As for Su Xin, he had reserved his berth early on, though it had nothing to do with the Ascension Stairway; he was simply freeloading on the ship to attend the Immortal Arts Assembly. To that end, he had already mentally prepared himself for a ship full of unapproachable high-stage seniors with whom he could never converse.

What nobody anticipated was that as Central Domain newspapers became runaway bestsellers and academic journal debates grew white-hot, legions of cultivators caught the travel bug for a Central Domain excursion. Passenger tickets quickly sold out, and calls to expand the ship's capacity grew deafening. In the end, a thoroughly exasperated City Lord Jian simply dusted off the original architectural blueprints of Sky City, constructing a vessel allegedly capable of housing the entire cultivator population of Sky City! Anyone who wished to go was welcomed aboard.

At once, the passenger manifest became an utter mishmash.

There were lofty visionaries seeking an audience with the Ascension Stairway; there were scholars traveling to Heavenly Spirit Sect for academic debates; there were opportunists with ulterior motives purely catching a free ride; and there was a massive horde of aimless sightseers [joining the commotion](# "Literal CN: 凑热闹 (còu rènao) — joining in the bustling fun/excitement") just to watch the Immortal Arts Assembly.

While several high-stage seniors felt this degraded the ship's elite pedigree, Su Xin was overjoyed.

After all, the vessel made ports of call across multiple immortal cities in the Northern and Eastern Domains, stopping and starting along a voyage that would take roughly half a month to reach the Central Domain. That journey was boring to death; having more cultivators aboard to help him [hunt for fun](# "Literal CN: 找乐子 (zhǎo lèzi) — seeking amusement / having a good time") was a blessing!

And throughout the first leg of the voyage, Su Xin had indeed spent his days in blissful delight.

New passengers boarded constantly, everyone passionately debating the Immortal Arts Assembly and fresh tidbits from the newspapers (a few eccentric souls even debated boring academic journals). Under Su Xin's instigation, they even hosted Immortal Chess tournaments aboard. Everything was bustling and lively—far more entertaining, in Su Xin's eyes, than any Carefree Palace auction!

Regrettably, starting three days ago, a subtle shift occurred in the ship's atmosphere.

Su Xin didn't know where this shift had come from. Three days ago hadn't been any special date; if pressed, it was merely the day a new issue of *White Jade Capital Weekly* hit circulation. Su Xin had developed the habit of reading the papers himself, but apart from feeling a prick of envy over the headline that Heavenly Spirit Sect was building a large-scale teleportation array to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree—if only there were a direct express teleportation array connecting Sky City to Heavenly Spirit Sect's Qianxing Hall, that would be absolute perfection—there was nothing else that caught his attention.

Yet others seemed to think differently. Back then, several fellow Daoists who read the paper displayed bizarre expressions upon finishing the pages.

From that day forward, participants in the Immortal Chess tournaments plummeted. Interest in the Immortal Arts Assembly waned. Instead, people whispering in corners with newspapers grew, as did those burying their heads in books. Most absurd of all were the ship's Artifact Refinement and Formation Rooms. As a titanic vessel designed to accommodate tens of thousands, the ship naturally featured artifact workshops, alchemy chambers, meditation rooms, formation halls, and even a miniature secret realm. Yet a dense, swarming mob now besieged those facilities, fighting tooth and nail for reservation slots!

—"...Is all this really necessary?"-Su Xin muttered.

Carefully sidestepping two cultivators fiercely arguing whether array flags should be refined using Earth Fire or Heavenly Fire, he traversed the stifling, oppressive corridor and located his companions on the other side of the lounge hall.

To Su Xin's absolute horror, this crew of cultivation second-generation trust-fund scions—fellow slackers just like him—were also striking poses of deep, agonizing contemplation! The table that was normally piled high with spiritual snacks and Immortal Chess paraphernalia was now buried beneath an avalanche of books!

Trembling, Su Xin reached out a finger and flipped through the titles, greeted by the following volumes:

> *Introduction to Artifact Refinement*
> 
> *Encyclopedia of Formations*
> 
> *Analysis of Ran Clan Formations*
> 
> *Heavenly Spirit Sect Secret Exam Scrolls*
> 
> *Heavenly Spirit Sect Entrance Mock Exam*
> 
> *Selected Real Exam Questions of the Heavenly Spirit Sect*

...

Su Xin asked in a raspy, cracked voice:

—"What on earth are you all doing?!"

—"Doing what?"-a chubby boy holding *Introduction to Artifact Refinement* replied.-"Preparing for the Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination, of course!"

—"Why the hell are you taking that?!"-Su Xin blurted.

—"Didn't you read the newspaper?"-another buddy countered.-"The Heavenly Spirit Sect is preparing to construct a large-scale teleportation array. If you earn a qualification certificate, you can participate in the project!"

—"Of course I know that! But never mind if others want the qualification—why are you joining the circus?!"-Su Xin's voice rose an octave.-"Are you planning to go [move bricks](# "Literal CN: 搬砖 (bānzhuān) — moving bricks; slang for doing menial, hard manual labor on a construction site") on a construction site?!"

—"Aiya, my little ancestor, you don't understand. Knowledge of large-scale teleportation arrays has been lost for ages. Now that the Heavenly Spirit Sect has recreated it, being able to get our names on it provides massive prestige and boost to our cultivation!"-one cultivator piped up.

This person was of relatively modest pedigree among the scion clique; his reasoning reflected the consensus of the vast majority of cultivators aboard.

Su Xin could understand his motives, but the others left him utterly baffled.

Su Xin turned his gaze toward the chubby boy who had spoken first. That fellow was also the blood kin of a cultivation titan. While he couldn't match Su Xin's imperial pedigree, he belonged to the tier that could coast on [nepotism](# "Literal CN: 拼爹 (pīn diē) — competing via fathers; relying on parental prestige/nepotism") all the way to guaranteed admission at the Void Refinement stage!

The chubby boy grinned:

—"I don't care much for large teleportation arrays, but according to leaks in the gossip tabloids, the qualification exam is being held as an entertainment side-event for the Immortal Arts Assembly! Word is, magical treasures and formations crafted by qualified candidates will be deployed in the Immortal Arts tournament! Isn't that neat?"

[—"Exactly! Refining artifacts—who doesn't know how to do that? What if we qualify?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Even if we can't compete in the Immortal Arts Assembly, bringing home a professional qualification certificate is pretty awesome."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Right! It's the very first batch of certificates! More than enough to flex in front of professional artifact refiners!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The flock of second-generation trust-fund brats chattered excitedly.

They seemed to treat the qualification certificate like tournament [merchandise](# "Literal CN: 周边 (zhōubiān) — anime/gaming/event merchandise and souvenirs"), firmly convinced it was within easy reach. Su Xin couldn't comprehend where in the world their confidence came from. From that copy of *Introduction to Artifact Refinement* in their hands?!

Su Xin glared at the others:

—"You lot too? But if I recall correctly, none of you have ever studied artifact refinement or formations in your entire lives?!"

—"Heheh. Never studied it, sure, but after playing Immortal Chess for so long, I feel like I might have latent formation talent!"-one of the scions chuckled.

[Talent your ass?!]

[Apologize to every formation master under the heavens right now!]

[And traveling all the way to the Central Domain just to sit for an exam—are you all out of your minds?!]

Su Xin felt his Heavenly Spirit Sect Exam Phobia flaring up once more:

[Why does it feel like the Heavenly Spirit Sect is holding an exam every damn time?!]
