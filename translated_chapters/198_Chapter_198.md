# Chapter 198: The Fall

Naturally, Zhou Hui wouldn't [snuff out](# "Literal CN: 噶了 (gá le) — northern slang meaning to off, whack, or kill") Sikong Yao over a single [crow's mouth jinx](# "Literal CN: 乌鸦嘴 (wūyāzuǐ) — crow's mouth; pessimistic utterance that brings bad luck").

What was more, he already possessed a thorough understanding of the sheer depth of Sikong Yao's 'misfortune' and jinxing capabilities.

Back then, when Zhou Hui agreed to mediation by the [Meizhu couple](# "Literal CN: 梅竹夫妇 (Méi-Zhú fūfù) — Plum and Bamboo couple; senior mediator powerhouses of the Heavenly Dao Alliance") of the Heavenly Dao Alliance and called off his hunt for Sikong Yao, setting aside the reality that he genuinely couldn't locate Carefree Palace's headquarters—making continued pursuit a futile effort—he had also indeed discovered that Sikong Yao hadn't committed any grave atrocities. At most, the man was merely a minor lackey [aiding and abetting the tyrant](# "Literal CN: 助纣为虐 (zhù zhòu wéi nüè) — helping King Zhou of Shang do evil; acting as a pawn for villainous schemes"). Yet even on that basis, the reason Zhou Hui had relentlessly hunted Sikong Yao for eight solid years was purely because Sikong Yao was too catastrophically unlucky.

Wherever this man fled, vicious criminal cases tied to Carefree Palace would erupt without fail, leaving Zhou Hui with no choice but to suspect him. Yet whenever Zhou Hui investigated further, the crimes were genuinely unrelated to Sikong Yao; occasionally, the man even ended up lending a helping hand. But the moment Zhou Hui's impression of Sikong Yao began to warm, another suspicious case linked to him would instantly surface.

And so, as Zhou Hui hunted Sikong Yao along that entire journey, he conveniently obliterated countless criminal dens—both related and unrelated to Carefree Palace—along the way. By the later stages, Zhou Hui was essentially [walking Sikong Yao like a bloodhound on a leash](# "Literal CN: 故意遛着 (gùyì liù zhe) — deliberately walking him around like a dog to flush out quarries") to hunt for clues regarding Carefree Palace. Through this, he came to understand that a cultivator's karmic fortune could truly be so... wondrous.

In Zhou Hui's view, Sikong Yao's misfortune wasn't true misfortune; the man possessed a textbook [accident-prone constitution](# "Literal CN: 事故体质 (shìgù tǐzhì)").

Wherever Sikong Yao went, he easily ran into all manner of perilous troubles. Yet these crises always provided sufficient room to maneuver; handled properly, they might even turn into serendipitous opportunities.

Sikong Yao had consistently handled them with great finesse. That was precisely why he had been able to seize various serendipities and attain Tribulation Transcendence on his own without ever getting entangled in Carefree Palace's darker depths.

—Of course, Sikong Yao's situation wasn't particularly unique.

To reach the heights of Tribulation Transcendence, every single individual present possessed extraordinary, peerless strengths. Even in Ren Ran's case, having an exceptional reincarnation pedigree was a formidable strength in its own right.

Ah, getting a bit sidetracked.

—"Sit properly,"-Zhou Hui said, shooting Sikong Yao an amused glance.-"Ye-zi's luck isn't particularly good, but it isn't dreadfully terrible either. The examination arena is outfitted with comprehensive defensive formations; genuine danger will not arise. Even if his karmic luck is poor, the situation is by no means completely beyond salvation."

He directed his latter sentence toward Ruwang.

Ruwang appeared introverted, but that was strictly in front of his former master.

In truth, ghost cultivators lacked a physical body to serve as an emotional buffer, causing their temperaments to become exceptionally extreme. This extremity differed from that of demons dominated by worldly desires; rather, it stemmed from the direct, uninhibited amplification of their own joys, sorrows, fury, and affection.

For instance, Ruwang's current 'concern' toward Guan Ye.

Fortunately, at this moment, voices drifted over from the commentary booth:

—"...A slight issue has arisen at Platform Forty-Four. Ah, it's my Fourth Junior Brother,"-Ran Deng said in a remarkably relaxed tone.-"It seems his furnace flame has also mutated."

—"This mutation is hardly a good one,"-Fairy Maogui's voice sounded deeply anxious and concerned.-"Your junior brother... this Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple chose Samadhi True Fire. Such a flame is the high-tier fire most familiar to all alchemists and artifact refiners. Highly versatile and exceptionally stable, it is the most dependable choice for crafting high-tier elixirs... ahem, magical treasures. Yet few realize that the seemingly stable Samadhi True Fire can also mutate. Out of tens of thousands of cauldrons of Samadhi True Fire, there are always a few instances of [furnace blowout](# "Literal CN: 烧炉 (shāolú) — runaway furnace combustion leading to vessel explosion or scorched ruins")..."

Fairy Maogui didn't cite precise metrics like 'percentages.' It was evident that her commentary drew from personal experience, which was precisely why it caused her far greater worry than cold numbers ever could.

—"In this situation, points won't be deducted, right?"-Fairy Maogui asked Ran Deng anxiously.

—"As long as an accident is not caused by the candidate's operational error, points will not be deducted, and they may start anew,"-Ran Deng answered placidly, appearing not in the least worried about her junior brother.-"However, the lost time cannot be recovered. After all, karmic luck is also a part of cultivation."

Hearing this, Maogui wore a regretful expression.

If that was the case, this Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple's score couldn't possibly be very good, Maogui mused.

After all, artifact refinement was identical to alchemy: the longer the refinement process, the superior the resulting quality. Therefore, for a timed qualification examination, examinees had to plan their schedule meticulously from the very start, ideally crafting a magical treasure that maximized every second of the allotted window without a single shred of waste.

And the candidates had indeed planned accordingly.

Consequently, when an abrupt mutation caused a failure like this, the loss of time was a minor concern compared to the disruption of the entire blueprint. If their schedule was tight, they might even be forced to completely change the item they intended to forge; such a last-minute alteration would naturally deal a devastating blow to their final score.

Yet Maogui harbored no objections toward the examination regulations.

—"True enough; karmic luck is a component of cultivation,"-Fairy Maogui sighed.

Especially for alchemists and artifact refiners, if someone's luck was excessively abysmal, it truly wasn't recommended that they pursue this path.

Yet which cultivator dared claim their luck was flawless? Even someone blessed with a Heavenly Spirit Root like Maogui had her own share of woes.

—"I had thought the Untiring Immortal Venerable didn't care about karmic luck!"-Fairy Maogui remarked.-"Ah, I don't mean anything negative about the Untiring Immortal Venerable; I hold him in supreme reverence. I simply felt that if anyone here could achieve ['man triumphs over the Heavens'](# "Literal CN: 人定胜天 (rén dìng shèng tiān) — human determination surpasses divine decree"), it would surely be him."

Yet within his examination arena, karmic luck was treated as part of cultivation!

—"What Master opposes is treating karmic luck as the sole path to success and abandoning one's own efforts as a result,"-Ran Deng replied.

It was difficult for her to explain to outsiders that in her Master's eyes, karmic luck was much like spirit roots and spiritual veins—merely a constant variable in cultivation to be scientifically recorded and categorized. If karmic luck and serendipity could be quantified, Master would likely construct a mathematical formula tailored to each individual's circumstances.

Ran Deng couldn't be bothered to elaborate further, merely adding:

—"Besides, my junior brother hasn't given up either!"-Ran Deng said.

—"Hasn't given up?! But his furnace has already..."-Fairy Maogui trailed off.

Fairy Maogui's eyes widened as she turned her gaze toward Guan Ye's direction.

---

[Today's luck is indeed rather poor. First a bug in the Demon Clan learning system, then the examination medallion, and now the furnace fire. Had I not been forewarned by Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai, I certainly would have jumped in fright.]

Guan Ye thought in his mind with detached indifference.

His physical movements were equally detached, as though this had nothing to do with him.

When the refining cauldron let out an explosive screech that drew the eyes of the entire arena, Guan Ye was in the middle of processing the bones of a [Wenyao fish](# "Literal CN: 文鳐鱼 (wényáoyú) — a mythical flying fish with bird wings from Mount Taiwu in the Classic of Mountains and Seas, heralding abundant harvests"). This was one of the raw materials he intended to forge today; because the fish was small and packed with fine spines, it required extreme meticulousness and patience.

Even so, that ear-splitting detonation didn't cause the carving blade in his hand to deviate by a single micrometer.

Only after executing a flawless, beautiful incision did Guan Ye raise his eyes to glance at the cauldron leaping with flames dozens of meters high.

—"Cyan-red flame, furnace temperature seven hundred degrees; it can hold for another thirty-five to thirty-seven seconds,"-Guan Ye evaluated in a low murmur.

While making this calculation, he donned specialized gloves resistant to both freezing cold and extreme heat. In less than a second, he hauled out timber covered in a layer of white frost from the lower tier of the supply rack. Taking twenty-three seconds, he applied pinpoint force to cleave it into seven blocks so uniform they looked measured by a caliper. Next, spending eight seconds, he evenly sprinkled combustion accelerant over them, ignited them, and used three seconds to affix them evenly along the interior walls of the cauldron.

The entire operation took thirty-five seconds—just right.

Upon the addition of these burning wooden blocks, the flames inside the cauldron visibly mellowed.

Though they still burned fiercely, they no longer emitted horrifying screeches, and the flame core stabilized considerably.

Guan Ye had evidently anticipated this outcome. With methodical calm, he retrieved additional materials to perform follow-up adjustments on the flame, even finding time in between to continue preparing his original crafting ingredients according to schedule, appearing wholly unhindered in his planned progression.

From that point on, there was little left to narrate.

Artifact refinement was fundamentally a monotonous affair.

Whether adjusting furnace flames or treating ingredients, even though practice had made Guan Ye second nature and every single step was executed with textbook perfection, compared to the dazzling, unpredictable clashes of the Immortal Arts Assembly, the testing arena of a qualification exam offered minimal entertainment value.

However, the "spectators" of this examination were predominantly professionals after all.

The mutation of Samadhi True Fire was evidently a subject most refiners had never researched.

And Guan Ye's handling method made the sharper master refiners realize something profound.

At first, they watched Guan Ye's operations with the gloating amusement of masters testing their disciples and watching them struggle. But as the fire was brought under control, their demeanor turned deadly earnest.

By the time the flames retracted back into the cauldron and the core transformed into a flawless, unblemished crimson, the entire spectator gallery had fallen into absolute, pin-drop silence.

Some cultivators sensed the uncanny mood and looked around, only to spot a crowd of domain powerhouses stretching their necks like [ducks awaiting the roast](# "Literal CN: 待烤的鸭子 (dài kǎo de yāzi) — stretching necks in rapt, motionless attention like hung roasting ducks")... ahem, staring fixatedly at Platform Forty-Four like eager disciples watching their master demonstrate a monumental supreme technique, terrified of missing the slightest detail. Seeing this scene, even spectators who weren't artifact refiners clamped their mouths shut.

---

—"...It can actually be handled like that?"-Fairy Maogui murmured.

As an alchemist, she too had watched with rapt attention until the flame core shifted hue before remembering to speak:

—"So treating mutated Samadhi True Fire like this can transform it into [Liuding Divine Fire](# "Literal CN: 六丁神火 (Liùdīng Shénhuǒ) — Six Ding Divine Fire; one of the legendary Daoist divine fires, notably used in Lord Laozi's Eight Trigrams Furnace")... Ah, is this something that can be openly displayed? Untiring Immortal Venerable."

Fairy Maogui looked toward the high platform of the spectator stands.

In her eyes, Guan Ye's technique bordered on leaking core sect secrets. If the Untiring Immortal Venerable took displeasure, he possessed the power to detain everyone present.

...Surely he wouldn't, right?

Fairy Maogui felt a prickle of fear, which was why she couldn't ask Ran Deng, instead seeking confirmation from the one she deemed the 'ultimate authority.'

—"Since he is willing to employ this method during an examination, it should naturally be within the bounds of what can be publicized, wouldn't it?"-Zhou Hui replied, seemingly somewhat surprised by her question. After a brief pause, he appeared to grasp Fairy Maogui's underlying apprehension and added:-"This treatment method was not taught by me; he most likely figured it out on his own."

—"So that's how it is,"-Fairy Maogui said, her tone growing notably enthusiastic.-"To achieve such a feat while merely in the Foundation Establishment Stage—your disciple is truly a peerless genius!"

Following her words, a wave of praise erupted from the spectator gallery as well, the tense atmosphere easing considerably.

Evidently, everyone had harbored concerns similar to Maogui's.

Yet since this 'little trick' was conceived independently by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple, it didn't constitute anything as grave as a breach of sect secrets. At most, it was a naive youth who didn't realize how momentous his technique was and carelessly showed it off.

Naturally, since he was the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple, the titans present took pride in their standing and wouldn't let him suffer a loss; when they returned, sending some gifts to thank him for his 'imparted knowledge' would suffice.

Furthermore, through this single display, the crowd became far more convinced regarding Guan Ye's drafting of the 'large-scale teleportation array' blueprints.

Prior to this, most had shared Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai's skepticism, refusing to believe a Foundation Establishment disciple could draft such blueprints—assuming the Untiring Immortal Venerable was merely fabricating hype for his own disciple. But in the craft of artifact refinement, observing the fluidity of one's hands laid the truth bare. Especially after witnessing Guan Ye's execution, refiners naturally recognized the immense labor embedded within; master instruction alone could never teach this level of muscle memory, particularly since the Untiring Immortal Venerable wasn't renowned for artifact forging in the first place.

Having understood this, many in the stands looked at Guan Ye with even hotter gazes:

An artifact refiner blessed with such talent, even with a trash spirit root, was a treasure worth roping in early on!

One thing was certain: regardless of how this exam concluded, Guan Ye's fame would spread across the cultivation world.

Yet the one most astonished was seated right next to Zhou Hui.

—"Ye-zi... excels at... artifact refining?"-Ruwang Ghost Emperor turned his head, confirming each word one by one.

—"I only learned of it in this cycle myself; his talent in this aspect is quite decent,"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"We naturally knew his artifact refining talent was decent; We personally tested him back then! Did you not assign him to the artifact refinement workshop for that very reason?"-the theatrical mask over Ruwang's face panicked even faster than the man himself, blustering noisily without even using his usual drawn-out operatic cadence:-"Yet even if his talent is decent, it shouldn't be decent to the point of inventing his own technique out of thin air?!"

—"If he didn't have it in this cycle, that doesn't mean he lacked it in other cycles,"-Zhou Hui smiled faintly.

Ruwang Ghost Emperor let out a low "Ugh."

—"What does that mean?"-Sikong Yao asked curiously.

Without Ruwang's neck turning in the slightest, the theatrical mask upon his head snapped around to the back of his skull with a *thump*, glaring at Sikong Yao like a second face with a hideously ferocious expression.

Sikong Yao instantly regretted running his mouth:

—"You don't have to say it if you don't want to,"-Sikong Yao squeaked.

—"There is nothing that cannot be said,"-Zhou Hui said, leaning back against his chair as he gazed at the meticulous Guan Ye.-"Among my disciples, Ye-zi has always been the most diligent and hardworking. Consequently, he is also the one who inherited my mantle most thoroughly. Even if he lost his memories, the skills he mastered were never lost in the slightest; as long as the necessary conditions are met, he can employ them... I originally thought Qianxing could achieve this as well, yet Qianxing appears to have been wiped the cleanest—even cleaner than Bei'er. Even after being tampered with, Bei'er still remembered sword stances through muscle memory!"

Toward the end, Zhou Hui's voice faded into a quiet murmur.

His final doubt was uttered for himself.

Even if it hadn't been spoken to himself, Sikong Yao wouldn't have understood it anyway.

Ruwang Ghost Emperor, on the other hand, understood perfectly. Yet he naturally wouldn't bother answering Sikong Yao; his mask simply swiveled back to the front of his face, shifting into a smug, triumphant grin:

—"Jiejiejiejie! Ye-zi is a boy raised by Us! How could he possibly be compared to the brat raised by that useless second-generation cultivator trash of Sky City?!"-Ruwang sneered.

—"...During the third cycle, when those kids ran amok, you and Jian Haoge didn't participate, did you?"-Zhou Hui paused, casting a suspicious glance at Ruwang Ghost Emperor.

Ruwang Ghost Emperor slowly turned his entire body around, facing the wall with both his face and his mask, refusing to utter another sound.

Zhou Hui shook his head with a bitter smile.

—"Wait a moment—did you say reincarnation cycles?! You know about the reincarnation cycles?!"-Goddess Ren Ran finally reacted, several beats late.

She glared venomously at Zhou Hui, unable to fathom how he 'also' knew about the reincarnation cycles.

After all, Ren Ran herself had never noticed the time rewinds; she had only learned about it from Heavenly General Lin Qi when she sought his aid. Because this matter implicated her in 'dereliction of duty,' she had always considered it the utmost 'top secret.'

Yet now, hearing it casually mentioned by Untiring, how could she not feel both terror and suspicion!

However, Goddess Ren Ran's thought process had always diverged wildly from normal people. When she first realized she had failed to notice the time rewinds and committed 'dereliction of duty,' her instinct had been to [pass the buck](# "Literal CN: 甩锅 (shuǎi guō) — shift blame; dump the pot") onto Zhou Hui. Yet at the time, she was unwilling to acknowledge that Zhou Hui wielded such immense power, so that claim had merely been lip service.

Now, hearing Zhou Hui speak of reincarnation cycles (time rewinds) with her own ears, she felt everything clicked:

—"It was indeed you who rewound time—and rewound it that many times! That's it—your power... the reason your power surpasses everyone else must have been gained from those endless time rewinds!"-Ren Ran proclaimed.

Hearing her accusation, Zhou Hui's eyes widened slightly before a faint smile spread across his lips:

—"Oh my, I've been discovered?"-Zhou Hui said.

Ren Ran froze instantly.

She seemingly hadn't anticipated that her random blame-shifting would hit the bullseye.

—"Rewinding time seven times—indeed, that was my doing,"-Zhou Hui smiled, yet not a shred of warmth touched his eyes.-"My power naturally stems from time."

Perhaps observing Guan Ye's state had stirred old memories, for Zhou Hui disclosed slightly more information today than ever before:

—"It seems the Heavenly Court is also aware that this world's time is somewhat unstable? Looking back, at the very beginning, I was just like Ye-zi is now—completely oblivious to time's abnormalities, quietly cultivating on my own."

Indeed. If Zhou Hui's power held any 'secret,' this was it.

Because this world had shattered into pieces in antiquity, time and space were riddled with countless loopholes. Thus, even without a supreme titan like Zhou Hui rewinding time, localized or even widespread temporal anomalies routinely occurred across this realm. It was merely that mortals and cultivators progressed linearly along the timeline, leaving them unable to remember.

Yet while memories might slip away and hard-earned fruits might be forgotten, most traces still lingered behind.

Initially possessing mediocre spirit roots and subpar talent, Zhou Hui was no different from anyone else—except that he was far more [involutionary](# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn) — hyper-competitive, obsessively grinding and self-improving"). Perhaps in another world, he would have achieved greatness as well; but just like the 'otherworldly realm' in his dreams, the moment his spacecraft soared toward the stars and his ideals were realized, his mortal lifespan had already run dry. Only in this world did the fissures of time become Zhou Hui's supreme serendipity. The traces of his grueling diligence inevitably remained etched upon him, allowing his progress to be just a tiny bit faster than anticipated.

Simultaneously, Zhou Hui was never one to blindly grind in ignorance. He acutely detected that 'tiny bit faster,' thereby noticing the world's temporal flaws. He persistently sought to unravel this mystery, culminating in his *[Seven-Life Reincarnation Wheel Sutra](# "Literal CN: 《七世转轮诀》 (Qīshì Zhuǎnlún Jué) — Seven Lifetimes Transmigration Wheel Formula; the supreme temporal scripture created by Zhou Hui capable of rewinding the world")*, which possessed the power to rewind the entire realm a full seven times.

Moreover, those seven lifetimes had not been spent in vain.

Although in terms of Zhou Hui's original objective, every cycle had ended in complete failure, his power never vanished. Instead, it accumulated with each cycle of time. If in his first life he was merely twice as strong as cultivators of the same realm, across seven lifetimes of accumulation, it was no simple sevenfold increase—it was a qualitative transformation born of quantitative accumulation. Zhou Hui's current power was already something peers of his realm couldn't begin to compare with.

Comparatively speaking, Zhou Hui's disciples had possessed cultivation bases that were too low, the frequency of time rewinds was too dense, and their memories had been repeatedly erased and rewritten, leaving behind few lingering experiences. Yet they were not completely barren.

Especially Guan Ye.

Even if his memories showed no sign of recovery, the fruits of his diligent study and research would never betray him.

For instance, this 'little trick' for handling mutated Samadhi True Fire had nothing to do with Zhou Hui; Guan Ye had developed it himself during the sixth cycle. Though the memories of that cycle had been wiped and Guan Ye remembered nothing, when confronted with the Samadhi True Fire issue in this lifetime, he subconsciously thought: *[If I do this and that, it seems I can resolve it?]* Given his personality, he inevitably ran repetitive experiments, thereby mastering the technique once more.

It was precisely upon this 'bedrock' that Guan Ye was able to draft the large-scale teleportation array.

His accumulation was not merely from this lifetime, but from over three thousand years of forgotten time.

(Which made Zhou Hui somewhat puzzled as to why Qu Qianxing, who was equally diligent, had seemingly reaped no dividends from time.)

This 'secret of Untiring's cultivation' was hardly a secret in truth; yet even if one knew about time rewinding, whether one could exploit it was an entirely different matter.

After all, some people could write several thousand words one day, only for their brain to vanish alongside a lost computer document the next! One hardly needed the memory reset of a time rewind to suffer total amnesia.

Regrettably, when Zhou Hui spoke the absolute truth, Ren Ran refused to accept it instead.

—"H-How could you possibly achieve that?"-Ren Ran demanded.

In her heart of hearts, she refused to believe a mere mortal—a lower realm cultivator who hadn't even ascended—could manipulate time. That was something she, blessed with 'heaven-bestowed divine arts,' had never been able to accomplish.

And a mundane word like "diligence" was an answer Ren Ran could never accept.

Ren Ran could only reason within the narrow confines of her own mind:

—"C-Could it be Cha Na and I? That's it—you slaughtered Cha Na of Carefree Palace and him! You killed so many Tribulation Transcendence titans; naturally you could plunder their power! That's how you gained the ability to rewind time! Unacceptable—I must report this to the Heavenly Court! Someone like you cannot be allowed to... Mmph!"-Ren Ran sputtered.

One truly wondered whether Ren Ran's skull contained any actual brains.

It was one thing to convince herself of such nonsense, but she actually blurted it out loud and sprang to her feet as though truly preparing to run off and report. Yet before she could make a move, an immense pressure slammed down onto the crown of her head, forcing her back into her seat.

The disparity in power was akin to an adult swatting an infant.

Ren Ran's eyes widened in horror. She discovered crimson blood-mist enveloping her surroundings, sealing both her movements and her voice.

And this blood-mist hadn't even been cast by Untiring—it was Ruwang Ghost Emperor!

At some point, Ruwang's theatrical mask had vanished, leaving only a face identical to Untiring's staring at her without a single trace of emotion.

Ren Ran heard Ruwang ask Untiring:

—"Silence her... permanently?"-Ruwang asked.

Hearing this, that terrifying smile on Untiring's face deepened (from Ren Ran's perspective).

Only now did Ren Ran realize her own sheer stupidity—why on earth had she probed Untiring so bluntly to the bottom? [For a being wielding the power to rewind time, extinguishing me would be as effortless as lifting a finger, wouldn't it?!]

Ren Ran thought in sheer terror. Like a fish tossed onto dry land, she gaped her mouth in vain, entirely unable to make a sound. And naturally, no one here could save her; that bastard Sikong Yao—whom Ren Ran had considered to be on her side—had crawled beneath his chair at some point, wearing an expression that said he would never interfere under any circumstances.

[No, I still have... the Heavenly General! Lord Heavenly General!]

Ren Ran remembered the rescuer she had summoned. Her eyes darted frantically, peering toward the examination platforms below. In her eyes, her sole savior, Lin Qi, was down there on one of those platforms. If he knew...

*BOOM!*

Once more, an explosive detonation shook the entire testing arena.

This time, it wasn't another examinee's flame mutating, but rather the platforms of the two whose flames had already mutated!

After treating the Samadhi True Fire and obtaining a mutated flame, Guan Ye's examination platform had naturally earned the qualification to ascend. Thus, without anyone realizing it, Guan Ye's and Guan Xinguang's platforms had reached the exact same height.

This was originally nothing remarkable—merely a coincidence. As their performances varied, the platforms would continue to shift.

Yet whether because the magical treasures being forged by the two were too formidable or their mutated flames were too domineering, when the two platforms reached the same horizontal plane, despite the ample physical distance separating them, a spatial collision erupted across the void! The spiritual energies utilized in their respective forging clashed and grappled with one another!

The two spiritual energy fields influenced each other, causing the air in the testing grounds to faintly warp and distort.

From the commentary booth, Fairy Maogui's anxious voice drifted over:

—"...An unfortunate development has occurred! The forging spiritual power from both candidates has leaked outward, causing mutual interference..."-Fairy Maogui announced.

—"Lord Heavenly General..."-Ren Ran murmured, watching with eager anticipation.

She couldn't care less what was happening; she was simply ecstatic that something had erupted to divert Untiring's and Ruwang's attention away from her.

The bigger the commotion, the better!

Ideally, it would severely injure or even slay Untiring's disciple!

Ren Ran cursed viciously in her heart. The pitiful trickle of brain matter inside her skull seemingly failed to realize that if something truly happened to Guan Ye, setting aside Untiring, Ruwang would never let her live.

And Fairy Maogui's analysis seemed to lean precisely toward the outcome Ren Ran hoped for:

—"Such an occurrence usually happens when the treasures being crafted mutually counter each other, thereby requiring conflicting materials that generate opposing spiritual fields during refinement,"-Fairy Maogui explained.-"Although this state does not last long, the dire part is that the side crafting the stronger treasure will exert a devastating impact on the weaker side, resulting in the other's refinement failure and potentially even inflicting backlash upon the refiner."

—"Particularly since both candidates just 'upgraded' their furnace flames, the cauldron fires are exceptionally susceptible to disruption from spiritual fields. If the forces fail to maintain equilibrium, it could easily trigger a [catastrophic runaway disaster](# "Literal CN: 恶性事故 (èxìng shìgù) — vicious accident / catastrophic runaway failure")!"-Fairy Maogui warned.

In other words, the disparity in artifact refinement caliber would directly dictate the severity of damage from the accident!

Whether because Ruwang was also concentrating on listening to this analysis, Ren Ran felt the spiritual pressure suppressing her loosen considerably. Struggling, she sneered:

—"Untiring, Ruwang—wouldn't it be better if you went to save your child... Mmph!"-Ren Ran sneered, before being cut off.

In her view, a Foundation Establishment Guan Ye going up against a Mahayana Guan Xinguang was certain death.

Yet between Ren Ran and Ruwang alone, the disparity in strength was painfully stark.

Ruwang's spiritual pressure intensified once more, laced with fierce hostility, causing Ren Ran to spit out a mouthful of blood.

Hiding beneath his chair, Sikong Yao stared at Ren Ran with an expression of utter disbelief and pity, finding her inability to read the room entirely beyond comprehension—even harboring doubts whether she had a masochistic streak.

Fortunately, Ruwang truly had no inclination to waste time on Ren Ran.

He evidently harbored the exact same worry as Ren Ran, turning anxiously toward Zhou Hui:

—"Master... cancel... the exam..."-Ruwang urged.

*BOOM... BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!*

Before he could finish his sentence, sounds like celebratory cannon fire detonated in midair!

Ruwang whipped his head around in panic, only to see purple-gold flames and deep crimson flames exploding across the sky. Billowing dense smoke enveloped the airspace of the testing arena, and an examination platform plunged straight down out of the smoke—

—"Ye-zi..."-Ruwang gasped, moving to catch the falling platform.

Yet halfway through, he froze.

Because that was by no means Platform Forty-Four.

And Fairy Maogui's astonished voice rang out across the hall:

—"A platform has fallen! It's Chief... Chief Director Guan's platform!"-Fairy Maogui cried.
