# Chapter 96: [A Joke](<# "Literal CN: 玩笑 (wánxiào) — a joke / jest / prank; chapter title">) (第96章 玩笑)

The Grand Immortals' Gathering had finally begun!

Cloud City Nascent Soul cultivator Lu Zhen stood upon the gallery promenade, gazing out toward the distant sky-canopy.

Accompanied by the resounding swell of immortal music, the sky-canopy was screening the [promotional trailer](<# "Literal CN: 预告PV (yùgào PV) — promotional video / game trailer">) for Immortals' Chess.

This edition of the trailer had been adapted from the opening video of the Heavenly Spirit Auction. It likewise began with the ancient cultivation realm ruined during the Demon-God War, yet after the Untiring Immortal Venerable's... hand (the man himself still had not shown his face on screen) cast aside the wine chalice, there appeared majestic projections of the Central Domain's various celestial cities and sects, alongside blank silhouettes representing key figures from each major power.

Although these sect silhouettes smelled suspiciously of commercial advertising, they were unquestionably far more compelling than the previous version.

Cultivators let their imaginations soar, dreaming that their own likenesses might fill in those blank silhouettes, erupting in frenzied cheers.

And as if answering the crowd's fervor, a floral fragrance bearing a crisp hint of ice crystals drifted through the air. A gentle breeze mingled with flower petals and shimmering spiritual rain swept over the cultivators at both ends of the promenade, not only making their bodies feel light and their spiritual energy circulate more smoothly, but also inducing a mild, pleasant buzz in their minds. Fighting spirit surged within their chests, making them itch to leap into the arena that very instant.

Of course, spellcraft influences of this degree were universally accepted within the cultivation world.

Cultivators needed only to circulate their own spiritual energy for a single cycle to purge this slight mental sway, which would even temper and solidify the foundations of their spiritual energy and divine sense—regarded as a small fringe benefit of the Immortals' Gathering.

However, most cultivators preparing to compete did not dispel these effects; maintaining a spark of battle fervor allowed them to perform far better in the ring.

Lu Zhen did not extinguish that fighting spirit.

Even as his thoughts stirred while gazing out toward the arena, his grip tightened around the bound scroll in his hand.

It was an academic journal titled *Arts & Sorcery* (《法术》).

This journal had only been published a few days ago, chief-edited by the Untiring Immortal Venerable of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. It compiled "theses" authored by Late Mahayana cultivator Villa Master Qingyang and members of the Immortals' Gathering organizing committee; rumor had it that the lowest-ranked contributor was at least at the Void Refinement stage.

The journals were offered for sale at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's various Immortals' Chess parlors at a rather steep price, and thus sales had been lackluster.

Lu Zhen had never expected he would actually understand it.

He had bought it purely to [bask in the celestial aura](<# "Literal CN: 蹭一口高阶修士的仙气 — rubbing off some celestial energy / basking in the reflected glory of high-tier cultivators">) of high-tier grandmasters.

In fact, the majority of people who bought this journal shared Lu Zhen's exact mindset.

Yet upon receiving the journal, Lu Zhen had been pleasantly surprised.

Although about eighty percent of the content was indeed entirely beyond his comprehension, it did not feel like reading [heavenly gibberish](<# "Literal CN: 像是看天书 — reading a book from heaven; reading an incomprehensible, arcane text">) as he had feared. Some topics were even quite "fascinating." Even if Lu Zhen currently lacked the ability to fully grasp them, he vaguely realized that once he reached those realms, these papers would provide genuine, tangible assistance.

And the paper that was theoretically supposed to be the most arcane—the "thesis" penned by Villa Master Qingyang—the Nascent Soul realm Lu Zhen had understood in its entirety!

Evidently, to help promote the Immortals' Gathering, Villa Master Qingyang had chosen a relatively entry-level topic:

*On the Differences Between Spiritual Energy and Immortal Energy* (《论灵力和仙力的不同》).

Of course, a grandmaster's definition of "entry-level" was worlds apart from an ordinary cultivator's "entry-level."

Lu Zhen had never heard of the arguments presented within; they were a complete revelation.

Most precious of all, Villa Master Qingyang's "thesis" was structured nothing like traditional cultivation manuals and secret scriptures—in fact, the entire journal's writing style parted ways with antiquity, crystal-clear at a glance and remarkably pleasant to read. The prose was plain and accessible, explaining profound truths in simple terms, supported by copious practical examples, and even revealed a clever little trick for refining immortal artifacts.

Among Central Domain cultivators, who among those who had explored the Abyss Sea didn't possess one or two broken remnants of magical treasures left behind from the Demon-God War?

Yet most of the time, such remnants were virtually [chicken ribs](<# "Literal CN: 鸡肋 (jīlèi) — chicken ribs: something of little value or taste, yet too pitiful to discard">).

After all, they could no longer be repaired, nor could they be reforged, and practically no shop would purchase them. Yet throwing them away felt wasteful, as these shards still retained traces of spiritual energy or were forged from precious materials; for rogue cultivators lacking sect patronage in particular, discarding them felt positively sinful.

Now, having read Villa Master Qingyang's thesis, Lu Zhen discovered that some of the "scrap" he had hoarded over the years could actually be refined, and the immortal energy sealed within could even be drawn out! He had tried it himself, and it actually succeeded!

Through this, Lu Zhen had obtained a pair of magical artifacts imbued with immortal energy.

Gripping this pair of artifacts, Lu Zhen felt his odds of victory tick up by another notch.

He looked toward the arena platform with keen anticipation.

At this moment, a sturdy yet well-featured Nascent Soul cultivator appeared at the center of the arena. With a deep, resonant voice amplified by sound transmission, he called out:

—"Fellow Daoists, thank you for waiting!"

—"The First Central Domain Grand Immortals' Gathering Tournament officially begins now!"

—"All who aspire to become the Central Domain's 'Nascent Soul Cultivator Representatives,' you may now take the stage! No restrictions on origin, no restrictions on background, no limits on attempts—so long as you are a cultivator of our Central Domain! Hold the platform for ten consecutive rounds, and you shall be the master of this arena!"

—"Now, we invite the 'future Platform Master' of this arena to step forward—!"

The Nascent Soul host bellowed at the top of his lungs.

The crowd erupted in another round of thunderous cheers.

Lu Zhen paid no attention to what the host was saying, his eyes fixed intently on the arena.

The tournament's arena was no ordinary fighting ring.

After all, it originated from "Immortals' Chess," and the chessboards had been crafted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable to train his disciples in secret realm exploration, complete with diverse topographies and secret realm environmental templates.

Although the commercial chessboards sold outside only featured a few fixed, classic illusion environments, for players of Immortals' Chess, utilizing the map's environment in combat was the absolute fundamental of fundamentals.

And since the victors of this Immortals' Gathering would become living "chess pieces," they naturally would not fight upon an empty, featureless stage.

The rules for this competition had already been leaked half a month ago.

Lu Zhen had known back then that the arena platforms would replicate various terrains from Immortals' Chess, requiring cultivators competing in the martial arena to battle on stages mirroring chess maps.

In other words, the map played a vital, decisive role in combat.

That was precisely what Lu Zhen cared about.

True to the intelligence, even as the host was speaking, the scenery atop the arena began to transform.

Black sand swept across the entire platform, faintly revealing beast skeletons and shattered array carvings. A colossal skeleton that had lost half its torso loomed like a towering mountain at the edge of the arena, gazing toward the gallery promenade with hollow, pitch-black eye sockets. Under its crushing pressure, even the burly host appeared downright petite.

Seeing this spectacle, gasps of dread rippled through the audience.

Lu Zhen, however, cheered inwardly.

It was the "Immortal's Bones" map!

Although this map was not his absolute forte, it did not counter his elemental arts either. What was more, according to Villa Master Qingyang's thesis, there were aspects here that could be studied and exploited—though because he had acquired the journal so late, Lu Zhen hadn't had time to research every detail thoroughly.

Lu Zhen did not hesitate.

Almost the very instant the host shouted "...'future Platform Master' step forward—!", he vaulted over the railing and flew straight toward the center of the arena.

A collective gasp rose from the crowd at Lu Zhen's decisive move.

Without question, Lu Zhen was the very first participant. By the time he touched down in the middle of the platform, other cultivators hadn't even made up their minds whether to step onto the stage!

The host let out a startled exclamation as well.

Whether this host carried some identification artifact on his person or not, despite Lu Zhen never announcing his name, he was already introducing him in an excited bellow:

—"Oh! The first challenger has already appeared!"

—"Cultivator Lu Zhen of our Cloud City!"

—"Though originally hailing from the Northern Domain's Xiafei Sect, he has resided in our Cloud City for a full three hundred and sixty years! An authentic local through and through! Let us see whether he can seize victory!?"

Beneath the host's boisterous introduction, Lu Zhen broke into an exaggerated grin, flashing a brazen, provocative gesture toward the spectator gallery—the very picture of a reckless brute.

Yet Lu Zhen was no brute.

His timing and provocative taunt were deliberate calculations made after analyzing the competition's rules:

The tournament employed a challenge-ladder format:

Initially, any two cultivators would enter the arena to duel. Once a cultivator secured ten consecutive victories, they would become that arena's Platform Master and ascend to the Spirit Terrace hovering diagonally above the stage;

Each Spirit Terrace was furnished with protective arrays, spirit delicacies, and nourishing pills, on par with the finest cave dwellings on that level of the Cloud Pavilion. The Platform Master enjoyed the finest treatment during the tournament, though this privilege was not permanent;

Whenever another cultivator racked up ten consecutive wins, they would challenge the Platform Master, seizing the title upon victory;

And when the preliminary rounds concluded, the reigning Platform Master of each arena would naturally secure qualification for the regional finals.

In Lu Zhen's assessment, while the rules sounded as though one could hop on at any moment, there were two optimal windows to take the stage.

The first was right as the tournament opened.

While everyone was still cautiously probing one another and hesitant to go all-out, seize victory early, then [rest and wait at ease](<# "Literal CN: 以逸待劳 (yǐ yì dài láo) — waiting at ease for an exhausted enemy; 36 Stratagems">) atop the Spirit Terrace. Moreover, if a Platform Master held the stage from the preliminaries to the very end, they would become an undisputed representative of the local cultivators, at least within this celestial city.

The drawback was that as Platform Master, one's combat techniques would be exposed incrementally, growing increasingly perilous toward the latter stages.

The second optimal window was right before the preliminaries concluded.

By that time, everyone who wanted a shot would have tried, allowing a rough assessment of all participants' combat prowess. In particular, the Platform Master's arsenal would be almost entirely laid bare. Taking the stage then would be [reaping the fisherman's spoils](<# "Literal CN: 渔翁得利 (yúwēng dé lì) — the fisherman benefits when the snipe and clam grapple; swooping in to take the prize with minimal risk">); a single victory practically guaranteed qualification with minimal danger.

However, fame gained this way was modest, and on less hotly contested platforms, observers might question the legitimacy of such a victor.

And as a mature Nascent Soul cultivator, Lu Zhen declared: [adults don't make choices—I want both!](<# "Literal CN: 小孩子才做选择，成年人全都要 — famous internet meme derived from Stephen Chow's movie 'Hail the Judge': only children make choices, adults want them all">)

He could test the waters by defending the platform first. If the opponents were [total scrubs](<# "Literal CN: 太菜 (tài cài) — gaming/internet slang for being terribly weak or unskilled">), he'd defend all the way to the end; if the competition proved too fierce and the pressure mounted, he could forfeit, gather intelligence, switch combat styles, and challenge again as the preliminaries neared their close.

With this battle plan mapped out, Lu Zhen had vaulted onto the stage without a second thought.

He played the role of a hotheaded brute, taunting the crowd.

Instantly, several cultivators, incensed by his antics—whether genuinely enraged or merely feigning fury, though Lu Zhen hoped they were genuinely livid, since fools so easily baited looked like [utter noobs](<# "Literal CN: 菜鸡 (càijī) — weak chicken / rookie / noob">)—leapt onto the platform.

And reality proved Lu Zhen's plan to be wildly successful.

Whether because the first ten challengers were all reckless hotheads or because no one dared reveal their full strength early on, Lu Zhen clinched ten consecutive victories with ease, barely showing his hand.

—"Aha! Who could have imagined our arena's Platform Master would emerge so swiftly!"-the host announced excitedly.-"Please, Cultivator Lu Zhen, ascend to the Spirit Terrace!"

Amid cheers that were rather lukewarm, Lu Zhen ascended to the Spirit Terrace.

Evidently, even the spectating crowd hadn't wanted a "Platform Master" crowned so early, thirsting for more dramatic, blood-pumping clashes.

Lu Zhen paid it no mind.

He walked up to the ostentatious, oversized throne at the highest point of the Spirit Terrace and seated himself. The dense spiritual energy sent a refreshing shiver through his body, but even better was the panoramic view.

Looking down upon the arena and gallery promenade from this height, everyone below looked like tiny insects under his absolute dominion.

[This feeling is far too exhilarating, but I must not get intoxicated by this seat and forget my original plan,]-Lu Zhen cautioned himself.-[If my techniques get analyzed too thoroughly, it's better to yield this seat and make a strategic retreat.]

Yet pausing for a beat, Lu Zhen decided to indulge himself just a little:

[Still, relaxing a bit right now can't hurt.]

Lu Zhen reasoned that the preliminary rounds of the entire tournament would last a month; the probability of formidable foes surfacing in the first two days was practically negligible.

[At least for the first couple of days, there's nothing to worry about.]

Or so Lu Zhen believed.

Yet no sooner had he settled into his seat, as if deliberately singing a counter-tune to his thoughts, a voice sneered coldly:

[—"What Grand Immortals' Gathering? Nothing special."](<# "Speaker: Nie Tongzi">)

Along with this voice, a figure flew onto the arena platform.

It was a rather handsome youth, though a pair of peach-blossom eyes carried an unmistakable demonic flair. But the moment his feet touched the stage, the shadow beneath him seemed to come alive, transforming into billows of black mist that wrapped around his body.

Witnessing this sight, the crowd erupted in palpable commotion:

[—"Demonic qi!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"There is demonic qi on this person!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Is he of the Demon Race?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

That "demon" clearly wanted this exact reaction. Wearing a sinister grin, he challenged the host:

—"What? You just proclaimed no restrictions on background, no limits on origin. Are you going to bar me from competing?"

[Whew, a troublemaker here to crash the stage!]

Lu Zhen froze for a second before grasping the situation immediately, followed by a surge of fresh curiosity:

*Still, this Immortals' Gathering was organized by the Central Domain's highest-tier cultivator, the Untiring Immortal Venerable, backed by virtually every sect and high-ranking expert in the region. Just which faction did this fellow belong to, to have such [suicidal courage](<# "Literal CN: 那么勇 (nàme yǒng) — so brave/bold; internet slang questioning someone's reckless audacity">)?*

* * *

—"How did this demon slip in?"-Qu Qianxing asked with a deep frown, gazing at the exact same scene.

Although he was not the direct manager of this tournament—the entire event had been planned by Mo Xi with technical support provided by Ran Deng, leaving Mo Xi so swamped today that he was running in circles, remotely coordinating the progress of multiple venues and completely unaware that Master had secretly slipped out with Second Senior Brother for a stroll—Qu Qianxing was thoroughly displeased that Master had to witness a scene spiraling out of control.

What incensed him even more was that, theoretically, contingency protocols existed for precisely this sort of incident.

Particularly in a place like Cloud City, which already had Late Mahayana powerhouses presiding over it. Even though this was merely a preliminary venue, numerous patrol cultivators should have been stationed around, ready to arrive at a moment's notice. Yet several minutes had elapsed, and not a single patrol guard had shown up.

The host was clearly stalling for outside intervention as well, dragging out his words as he read out the intruder's identity:

—"This is... Nie Tongzi, hailing from the Central Domain's Abyss Sea. He was once renowned throughout the Southeast Chaotic Stone Forest, though five hundred years have passed without word of him. Who would have guessed he would also grace our Immortals' Gathering today... Haha..."

—"How about it? Am I not far more qualified to represent the Central Domain than those so-called 'Central Domain cultivators' from the North?"-the cultivator named Nie Tongzi demanded arrogantly, utterly unconcerned about outside interference.-"A tournament like this was tailored from the ground up for someone like me!"

[...What utter rubbish. If we only counted those born and raised in the Central Domain, we might as well cancel this Immortals' Gathering altogether!]

the host thought to himself.

After all, following the Demon-God War, the Central Domain's mortal population had dwindled to near extinction, and the vast majority suffered from demon blood or demonic corruption. Trying to select untainted children fit for cultivation was an uphill battle. Only larger cultivation sects relied on affiliated cultivation clans to replenish their numbers, while most depended on small-scale recruitments of outside cultivators to fill vacancies.

For this reason, cultivators like Lu Zhen, who originated elsewhere but had resided in the Central Domain for centuries, were already regarded as [pure, red-blooded locals](<# "Literal CN: 根正苗红 (gēn zhèng miáo hóng) — roots straight and sprouts red; orthodox, bona fide locals">)—or according to the more radical Central Domain perspective: [anyone who's braved the forbidden zones of the Abyss Sea demon-god ruins three times is one of our own!](<# "Literal CN: 去过三趟渊海魔神遗迹的禁区，就是俺们中境人 — humorous grassroots definition of Central Domain residency">)

Why, even the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself wasn't directly born in the Central Domain!

On the contrary, corrupted cultivators like "Nie Tongzi" were beings that righteous cultivators would never accept as Central Domain brethren. After all, walking the demonic path entailed all manner of inhumane atrocities; they were hardly even "human" anymore.

However, sensing the ominous demonic qi radiating from the man, the host lacked the confidence to subdue him on his own. Thus, while placating him with noncommittal drivel, he desperately prayed for reinforcements.

Yet for reasons unknown, the patrol guards failed to appear, and several cultivators in the audience could no longer contain their fury.

After all, those gathered here had come for the glory of the Immortals' Gathering. How could they tolerate a demonic fiend strutting about so arrogantly?

[—"A mere demon dares brag so insolently here?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Allow [this grandfather](<# "Literal CN: 洒家 (sǎjiā) — rustic self-reference used by burly warriors/monks in classical Chinese fiction">) to take you down!"](<# "Speaker: Bald Cultivator">)

Bellowing thus, a bald, burly man vaulted onto the arena platform. Without another word, he hoisted a long halberd and smashed it down toward Nie Tongzi.

Nie Tongzi giggled with shrill glee.

Neither dodging nor evading, the black mist swirling about him surged toward the burly man, submerging him in the blink of an eye.

The burly man vanished without a trace, and the volume of the black mist grew noticeably larger.

—"What, only this much skill?"-Nie Tongzi laughed mockingly, turning back to the host:—"One by one is far too slow. Why don't you have them all come at once?"

The spectators along the gallery promenade hadn't even processed the burly man's disappearance, but hearing these taunts, fury ignited like wildfire:

[—"Who will go slay him?!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"We cannot let a demon run amok in our Cloud City!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"If we let such a wretch become Platform Master, it will be the everlasting shame of our tournament division!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

[—"Demons must be executed by all who cross their path!"](<# "Speaker: Spectator">)

Chaotic roars flooded the entire promenade. Without question, had this "demon" still been on the walkway, the enraged crowd would have torn him to shreds. Yet at this moment, the crowd found itself bound by the competition's rules.

The arena here hosted single duels—strictly one-on-one. Consequently, restrictive barriers sealed the platform's perimeter; so long as two combatants occupied the stage, no third party could enter.

The result was that the cultivators, [like the Calabash Brothers trying to save their Grandpa](<# "Literal CN: 葫芦娃救爷爷 (Húluwá jiù yéye) — Calabash Brothers saving Grandpa; classic Chinese animation meme referring to trickling in one by one and getting picked off sequentially">), leaped onto the stage one after another, only to be swallowed by the black mist one by one.

Yet rescue never arrived.

—"Master, let me go up!"-Qu Qianxing petitioned Zhou Hui.

Although he was currently at the Golden Core realm, he showed not the slightest dread toward this Nascent Soul demonic cultivator, wanting nothing more than to step up, discipline the rogue, and ascertain why the patrol guards hadn't arrived.

—"No need,"-Zhou Hui said, shaking his head.-"He has no intention of harming anyone."

*No intention of harming anyone? But those cultivators who vanished...*

Qingyang Qingyu's eyes widened in disbelief.

Zhou Hui glanced at Qingyang Qingyu with a faint smile, then blew a gentle puff of air toward the arena.

Instantly, Qu Qianxing and Qingyang Qingyu saw through the illusion: Nie Tongzi's body was radiating pure white luminescence. It wasn't demonic qi at all, but immaculate spiritual energy! And within the black mist behind him lay a massive spatial rift; the defeated cultivators had merely been safely contained inside that fissure.

Yet that was not the crux of the matter. The crux was that around the perimeter of this arena, a shroud of spiritual energy had been draped at some unknown moment, shimmering with iridescent light. Patrol guards passing by seemed entirely oblivious to it, automatically steering clear whenever they neared the barrier.

Qingyu found this sight strikingly familiar—

Ah, wasn't this identical to their current situation?

The Untiring Immortal Venerable was sitting with them squarely inside the cordoned-off private area of Elder Siwu from the Ceremony Court, yet Elder Siwu appeared completely oblivious to their presence.

Naturally, the Untiring Immortal Venerable would never do something as absurd as crashing his own tournament.

Yet on this very promenade sat another supreme powerhouse capable of pulling off such a feat—seated right beside them.

Thinking this, Qingyu glanced toward the neighboring table.

That white-robed Mahayana cultivator was gazing at the arena with an impassive expression, looking almost bored, as if whatever unfolded on stage was utterly beneath his concern.

—"...It's Elder Siwu?"-Qingyu whispered with a furrowed brow.-"Why would he do such a thing?"

—"Who knows? Perhaps he's playing a joke?"-Zhou Hui remarked casually.

—"That is hardly funny,"-Qingyu said.

Yet she knew that if the other party was truly "playing a joke," there was genuinely nothing anyone could do to hold him accountable.

After all, judged by his underlying spiritual energy, that Nie Tongzi was indeed not a demonic cultivator.

The competing cultivators weren't dead either, merely stowed away inside a spatial rift.

Although Elder Siwu had sealed off the arena, he hadn't actually harmed a single soul, to say nothing of his prestigious status within the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ceremony Court. If he claimed it was merely "a joke," no one could touch a hair on his head—at worst, Cloud City and the Untiring Immortal Venerable who hosted the gathering would lose a bit of face, and that was "all."

No matter how furious the Untiring Immortal Venerable became, he couldn't simply kill the man like he had slain that fellow from Xiaoyao Palace... could he?

At this thought, Qingyang Qingyu suddenly felt a flicker of anxiety.

From the perspective of Eternal Villa, she did not wish to see the Untiring Immortal Venerable clash with the Heavenly Dao Alliance. After all, the Heavenly Dao Alliance was nominally the leader of the cultivation realm, and the "talents" currently migrating to the Central Domain were supplied by the Northern Domain; the Qingyang family naturally wished to preserve peace.

Still... even if this Siwu died, he wouldn't be missed!

As an elder of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, what kind of petty shenanigans was he pulling?! And from the Ceremony Court, of all places!

And with conduct like this, he still had the gall to accuse Eternal Villa of neglecting ancient etiquette and possessing impure lineage!

Officials from the Ceremony Court capable of stooping to such depths were the ones who needed to relearn every single tenet of etiquette from scratch!

Qingyang Qingyu fumed in silent indignation.

Qu Qianxing had also deduced the crux. Yet rather than punishing Siwu, he felt the pressing priority was restoring the tournament to normal order.

Qu Qianxing petitioned Zhou Hui once more:

—"Master, let me go up! I will teach him a lesson under the guise of an ordinary contestant."

—"No need, let us wait and watch,"-Zhou Hui replied, glancing toward the arena with an intrigued expression.-"Do not underestimate the contestants. This Platform Master is far more interesting than you imagine."

—"The Platform Master?"-Qu Qianxing looked up toward the Spirit Terrace in confusion.

He hadn't thought particularly highly of the Platform Master who had recklessly leaped onto the stage at the start. Furthermore, those first ten bouts had hardly been spectacular, leaving the distinct impression that the man had claimed the seat purely on luck.

Still, since Master said so, did that fellow truly have some secret trick up his sleeve?

Qu Qianxing seated himself once more.

The group turned their eyes back to the arena... with the exception of the little black lamb.

Although the little black lamb also tilted its small head, staring toward the arena with dark, beady eyes, the shadow beneath its body seemed to possess a life of its own. It crept quietly toward the other side of the tea tables, slithering up the legs of the chair until it reached Lord Siwu's side.

* * *

—"Hahaha, nothing special after all,"-Nie Tongzi laughed.

On the arena, Nie Tongzi's black mist had already "devoured" seven or eight cultivators.

Although Zhou Hui had employed a specialized technique to let his disciples perceive the truth behind Nie Tongzi, in the eyes of everyone else, those cultivators had literally been swallowed alive!

Consequently, after five or six cultivators had [sent themselves to their deaths](<# "Literal CN: 送死 (sòngsǐ) — courting death / throwing one's life away">), subsequent challengers hesitated. Under Nie Tongzi's mocking jeers, the tally of swallowed cultivators halted at the number eight, with not a single soul willing to step forward.

Seeing this, Nie Tongzi pointed his spearhead directly at the host and Lu Zhen:

—"No one is coming up! How about it—will you fight? Or will you have the Platform Master come down directly?"

—"...There is no such rule,"-the host stammered, drenched in cold sweat.

He looked despairingly toward the sky, but reinforcements never came. Having witnessed Nie Tongzi "devour" so many cultivators with effortless ease, he had completely lost all faith in defeating the fiend.

The host could only cast a hopeless glance toward the Spirit Terrace.

Atop the Spirit Terrace, Lu Zhen's complexion had turned stiff.

[What rotten luck! How could I run into this kind of calamity?!]

He thought in exasperation. Yet Lu Zhen knew there was nowhere to hide! As the reigning "Platform Master," he would inevitably become the target of this "demon's" feast; stepping up to face him directly would at least let him die with some shred of dignity.

Besides, it wasn't as if he had no path to victory.

After all, this map was "Immortal's Bones"...

Recalling the technique he had researched, Lu Zhen let out a sigh:

—"Very well! Since no one else is willing to challenge, allow this one to accept the challenge ahead of time!"

Speaking thus, he soared toward the arena platform.

Seeing him approach, Nie Tongzi burst into loud laughter. Without even waiting for the host to announce the start of the match, he commanded the billowing black mist to strike at Lu Zhen.

Lu Zhen's mind was racing with the new spellcraft he had researched. Catching sight of the black mist, his heart clenched, and he reflexively raised his hands to weave an intricate hand-seal.

Accompanied by his hand-seal, a blinding white light erupted. A colossal skeletal palm suddenly slammed down from above, [squashing Nie Tongzi into meat paste](<# "Literal CN: 拍成肉泥 (pāi chéng ròuní) — smashed into mincemeat / squashed flat like a fly">)!

It happened in the fraction of an instant.

Even Lu Zhen himself hadn't registered what had just occurred.

Lu Zhen blinked in dazed stupor before realizing that the colossal palm slamming down from the heavens belonged to the towering skeleton placed on the arena as mere "scenery"!

—"Haha, my research succeeded!"-Lu Zhen cried out to the host in wild ecstasy.

—"Congratulations...?"-the host replied, his mind still entirely frozen.

Naturally, the audience hadn't registered it either; only after a full half-minute did a deafening roar of cheers tear through the venue.

And amidst those thunderous cheers, a voice reverberated with terrifying, apocalyptic might:

[—"Who dared harm my disciple?!"](<# "Speaker: Lord Siwu">)
