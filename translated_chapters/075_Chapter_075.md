# Chapter 75: Progress? (第75章 进步？)

Zhou Hui temporarily had no time to pay heed to the Palace of Carefree Wandering.

To be precise, Zhou Hui had no idea where the Palace of Carefree Wandering was actually located.

This matter was, in itself, a rather 'bizarre tale.' Everyone clearly knew that the headquarters of Carefree Palace was situated upon the Endless Sea in the Eastern Domain, yet not a single soul knew its exact coordinates.

Neither frequent patrons nor the disciples of Carefree Palace themselves possessed this knowledge.

All of them arrived at Carefree Palace only after receiving 'recognition.' As for those who lacked Carefree Palace's recognition, rumor had it that even if they strolled right past the front gate, they would be completely unable to perceive the palace itself.

Without a doubt, a 'terrorist' like Zhou Hui was entirely disqualified from approaching.

Even though he had kited Sikong Yao, Carefree Palace's Tribulation Transcendence elder, across the realm for seven full years back in the day, and even though he had recently seized their Thought Pavilion, he still remained ignorant of where Carefree Palace was physically located.

—Naturally, had he known, he would have stormed over and smashed Carefree Palace to rubble long ago.

Yet precisely because of this, setting aside 'personal grievances,' Zhou Hui was genuinely intrigued—from a purely technical perspective—as to how Carefree Palace managed to deceive heaven and cross the sea, slipping past his senses entirely.

It was the sort of interest that warranted an academic research grant.

And right now, he seemed to have caught the faint, elusive wisp of a clue.

First had been the imprint on Yuwen Xuan; next came the 'kindling' gouged from the members of the Wanhe Gang who had defected to Carefree Palace and been branded with imprints; and now, there was Mengshan Old Freak.

After Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum noted that Mengshan Old Freak had once possessed a shadow similar to the flames (the divine fragment), Zhou Hui located a third, similar imprint upon Mengshan Old Freak's person.

It was situated just below the nape of Mengshan Old Freak's neck—a blind spot the bearer would never discover on their own.

—"Since when did I have something like this on me?!"-Mengshan Old Freak shrieked in horror.

He clutched the back of his neck in panic, scanning his own physical body over and over again.

Yet even with Mengshan Old Freak's Mahayana stage cultivation, he was utterly unable to detect anything amiss.

He had no idea when this imprint had been planted on him, how long it had accompanied him, or what kind of influence it had exerted over him. To any cultivator, nothing could possibly be more terrifying.

Zhou Hui plucked the imprint off:

—"Fairy, what do you make of this?"

—"In my view, it is a character—a smeared character,"-Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum said, inspecting the imprint while once again enduring Zhou Hui's [light pollution](# "Literal CN: 光污染 (guāngwūrǎn) — modern internet slang for blinding visual aura") (X).-"It looks somewhat similar to the script I saw in the 'flames' and the 'shadow', but it has been defaced; I cannot be certain."

Zhou Hui paused upon hearing this, then produced the imprint he had taken from Yuwen Xuan—having plucked it off after pinpointing the location of the Flame Crystal Flower:

—"What about this one?"

—"It's also a charac... Ugh!"

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum let out a muffled cry of agony. She clutched her eyes and stumbled a step backward as fresh blood trickled from between her fingers.

Fortunately, the mortal realm's foremost divine physician stood right beside them.

Lan Pei swiftly retrieved a vial of spiritual elixir and had Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum apply it to her eyes, preventing any permanent damage. If anything, prior to the blood flowing, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had felt her [clairvoyance](# "Literal CN: 灵视 (língshì) — spiritual vision / sight into the supernatural") sharpen noticeably.

—"This is the power of a true god. A slight brush can enhance one's vision, but ['see no evil'](# "Literal CN: 非礼勿视 (fēi lǐ wù shì) — from the Analects of Confucius: 'look not at what is contrary to propriety'")—stare at it too long and you'll go stark mad,"-Tao Zhu warned, thoroughly accustomed to this sort of crisis. He merely cast a peculiar sidelong glance at Zhou Hui.-"Where did you get your hands on such a potent divine seal?"

—"By chance,"-Zhou Hui replied evasively.

In his heart, he was certain: the three imprints were distinct.

Judging by Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum's reaction, the one on Yuwen Xuan was by far the most troublesome.

Being unable to endure even a single glance clearly indicated that its divinity was the most potent.

As for the imprints Carefree Palace had planted on the Wanhe Gang disciples, because those individuals had died without leaving so much as ash behind, it was unfortunate that they could not be compared against the imprint on Mengshan Old Freak. Nevertheless, both were evidently inferior to the one on Yuwen Xuan.

After all, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had gazed directly at Mengshan's imprint without suffering any adverse reaction, and had even been able to harness the flames and shadows brought forth by those imprints.

Analyzing it from this angle, the flame imprint and the imprint upon Old Ancestor Mengshan did not seem 'compatible.' In fact, when the two collided, they had produced a colossal explosion that tore open space itself.

[Hmm, perhaps the energy yield of that explosion could be calculated and harnessed...]

Seeing Zhou Hui's train of thought already veering from sinister conspiracies toward pure academic physics research, Tao Zhu frowned:

—"Hey! Untiring, let me warn you: you'd better not look too deeply into this."

[—"...?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"When your realm hasn't reached that level, prying into divine matters never ends well,"-Tao Zhu warned sternly.-"Even if you care deeply about it, wait until after you ascend!"

Hearing him say that, Mengshan Old Freak immediately withdrew the hand touching the back of his neck, looking thoroughly spooked.

—"..."-Zhou Hui merely smiled, responding with sweet obedience.-"I understand."

His attitude was unmistakably [I admit fault, but I won't change](# "Literal CN: 我知错我不改 (wǒ zhī cuò, wǒ bù gǎi) — popular Chinese internet meme about defiant pseudo-apologies").

Tao Zhu rolled his eyes, his round belly heaving, too exasperated to pay him any further mind.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum, however, cast several peculiar glances at Zhou Hui.

Looking at the Untiring Immortal Venerable's demeanor, could one really say his 'realm hadn't reached that level'? Why did she feel that the Untiring Immortal Venerable, holding those imprints so effortlessly, was far more formidable than those twisted glyphs?

In her estimation, the only reason the Immortal Venerable did not suffer immediate harm was that those imprints (glyphs) harbored indelible malice that automatically attacked anyone who looked upon them; the Immortal Venerable, on the other hand, held zero malice and had even restrained his own terrifying might.

Nonetheless, Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had no desire to get entangled in this affair, so she kept her thoughts to herself.

Nor did Zhou Hui have any intention of continuing to debate this with a group opposed to his research.

He steered the conversation back toward what the others considered a safe direction:

—"Very well! Since we have no further leads on this matter for now, continuing to discuss it is pointless. Let's return to the teleportation array. Old Tao, what do you think?"

—"It looks far more feasible than before,"-Tao Zhu said, frowning at the teleportation array blueprint.-"The only issue is that the materials are exceedingly difficult to find!"

After all, this was a massive teleportation array capable of instantaneous transit between two continental landmasses. Setting aside its mind-boggling architectural complexity, the required materials were supreme heavenly treasures—extraordinarily difficult to track down even for the apex cultivators of this world.

—"For instance, ten-thousand-year sacred lotus root, supreme five-element spiritual tallow, root veins of the Jianmu tree, tears of the Zhulong torch dragon, and thousand-year Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul..."

—"Ah, as for the Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul, I have it,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"You have it?!"-Tao Zhu's eyes widened to the size of saucers.-"Are you certain? This is actually trickier than the Jianmu tree or the Torch Dragon—at least people know where those two can be found! But the Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul is different. It sounds like a dragon, but it is actually the spiritual soul of an ice-attribute carp spirit that lived for a thousand years! It sounds like a lower-tier material, but precisely because it originates from a mortal beast, it's far rarer—after all, any beast with that level of cultivation would have leaped through the Dragon Gate to become a true dragon long ago! To have not turned into a dragon, yet possess that cultivation and an ice attribute... that's just..."

—"No matter, I have it, and we share quite a history,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.

[After all, in the Heavenly Dao's script, it was the very thing that constituted his 'fatal weakness' and allowed the protagonist to kill him!]

*

Heavenly Spirit Sect, Warehouse.

—"Sis, help me take inventory over there!"

At that moment, Mo Xi called out to Mo Bei.

Mo Bei paused in her tracks upon hearing his voice, frowning at the gilded, glittering chests of various sizes that filled the room.

These were the New Year gifts left behind by the visiting cultivators who had come to 'pay New Year calls.'

Because their visits carried undertones of 'pleading guilty' and currying favor, the gifts were extraordinarily lavish. Every single one had been meticulously selected; tossing any random trinket among them into the outside world would incite Nascent Soul and Soul Formation cultivators to fight to the death over it.

In the eyes of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, however, these were naturally worthless baubles, usually handed off to his disciples to handle.

—Here, 'disciples' almost exclusively referred to Mo Xi.

Among Zhou Hui's disciples, only Mo Xi welcomed every kind of dharma treasure, medicinal pill, and artifact that could be converted into spirit stones with open arms. In Mo Xi's own words: even when he knew an item didn't belong to him, just counting them brought him pure, unadulterated joy.

Naturally, Mo Xi had never allowed anyone else to interfere with this task before, not even his own twin sister.

Mo Bei had merely been passing by by coincidence, so being dragged in to do manual labor today caught her somewhat by surprise.

—"Is there an issue with the items here?"-Mo Bei asked.

She casually flipped open a chest, and the dazzling radiance of the spirit stones inside nearly blinded her eyes.

—"Nothing's wrong, just some high-grade spirit stones. Totally boring,"-Mo Xi said dully. He was currently tinkering with a hefty, ornate box, trying to see what was inside.-"Just count up the quantity so they can be stored in the warehouse. I'm swamped, and you have nothing to do anyway, so lend a hand, will you?"

[Spirit stones? Boring? Did those words just come out of Mo Xi's mouth?!]

Mo Bei didn't count the spirit stones. Shutting the lid, she sat atop the chest, studying her twin brother:

—"You seem to have changed lately."

—"Changed? Changed how?"-Mo Xi asked, bewildered.

He gave the box a firm press. Unlocking some hidden mechanism, the box popped open, and a mineral core radiating five-colored spiritual brilliance instantly illuminated the entire room. Mo Xi immediately clutched it to his chest, unable to bear parting with it:

—"Whoa! The mineral core of a Ninth-Rank Crimson Essence Ore! Not even the auction managed to procure this! I've wanted this forever—wonder if Master will let me have it!"

The black dragon 'tattoo' on his body stirred restlessly, looking as if it longed to lick the shimmering stone.

After logging the gift into the official registry, Mo Xi fished out a small personal notebook from his robes and jotted down the item, planning to find an opportunity later—such as scoring well on an exam—to wheedle it out of Master or Second Senior Brother.

[I overestimated this fellow.]

[He hasn't changed in the slightest!]

Mo Bei let out a sigh, but nonetheless remarked:

—"Your tastes have gotten pickier."

In the past, a handful of spirit stones would make him leap for joy; now, nothing short of a rare treasure caught his eye.

Then again, it made sense. After being gifted an entire spirit stone vein by Master, followed by the auction and the secret realm, colossal fortunes of spirit stones had passed through his hands. Mo Xi seemed to have grown numb to spirit stones, and his threshold for ordinary treasures had skyrocketed.

He had also become far harder to raise.

[If his condition keeps 'deteriorating' like this, what on earth will become of him?]

Mo Bei sighed with the detached amusement of a bystander.

Thanks to their twin telepathy, Mo Xi naturally sensed that Mo Bei was ridiculing him.

He glared at Mo Bei:

—"My taste is leagues better than yours! Are you still looking for that ['Destined Person'](# "Literal CN: 命运之人 (mìngyùn zhī rén) — fated lover / destined soulmate") of yours? You might as well give up on such a fantasy creature!"

—"What fantasy creature?!"-Some expression finally appeared on Mo Bei's face.

For some reason, her brother could tease her about anything else, but her 'Destined Person' was strictly off-limits.

—"I can feel it,"-Mo Bei insisted.-"He definitely exists, and he's getting closer and closer to me!"

—"Getting closer? What is he, an urban legend?!"-Mo Xi sneered.-"A fantasy creature who doesn't care about your background, doesn't care about your looks, doesn't care about your personality, doesn't care about your cultivation, doesn't care about your swordsmanship, doesn't care about anything about you, and only cares about your 'true self'?!"

—"Of course..."-Mo Bei looked ready to argue back, only to freeze unexpectedly. Then, her cheeks inexplicably flushed pink, and she stammered,-"...I think he should at least appreciate my swordsmanship."

[—"?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"I think my swordsmanship is quite good. I've already crossed the threshold, Master praised me, and everyone says I'm formidable,"-Mo Bei said slowly.-"If he doesn't even like my swordsmanship, it proves he isn't looking at the real me at all!"

—"..."-Mo Xi stared strangely at his sister for a good while, then burst into laughter.-"Going by that logic, your Destined Person is everywhere! Just grab any random disciple playing Immortal Chess in our sect, and they'll adore your swordsmanship."

—"Only liking my swordsmanship won't do either! What if I lose a match? They'd immediately switch allegiances!"-Mo Bei frowned.

—"Wanting it yet not wanting it. You're so high-maintenance,"-Mo Xi scoffed.

—"Romance is inherently troublesome,"-Mo Bei huffed coldly.-"An [overgrown toddler](# "Literal CN: 巨婴 (jùyīng) — giant infant / childish adult") who at your age still schemes about turning into a cat to snuggle in Master's arms naturally wouldn't understand."

—"What?! Who's turning into a cat?!"

—"Don't think I don't know. You've been secretly practicing transformation arts lately."

—"I have not!"

...

In the end, it was an origami crane that cut short the twin siblings' supremely foolish, meaningless bickering.

The paper crane fluttered before Mo Bei, opening its beak to deliver a message, but Mo Bei clamped its beak shut with lightning reflexes. Unfolding the crane before it could utter a sound, she scanned its contents.

Promptly summoning her flying sword, she prepared to depart.

Mo Xi protested:

—"Wait! I still need your help!"

—"This is also part of what you asked me to 'help' with,"-Mo Bei said.-"Someone has reached the final stage of Second Senior Brother's secret realm. Didn't you ask me to go put on a 'show'?"

Mo Xi blinked in surprise:

—"They finally reached the finale?"

*

Heavenly Spirit Sect, Qianxing Secret Realm.

Several cultivators stood before the 'Ice Eye,' surrounded by clusters of Snow-Luster Grass, conversing with weary sighs:

[—"This must be the final puzzle, right?"](# "Speaker: Raid Team Cultivator")

[—"It should be."](# "Speaker: Raid Team Cultivator")

[—"To think that after circling all the way around, we ended up right back where we started!"](# "Speaker: Raid Team Cultivator")

[Anyone hearing this without knowing better would think they had gone through endless trials and tribulations!]

Xia Bing thought to herself.

In reality, this was the safest, yet also the most enjoyable secret realm she had ever explored.

The entire process felt like playing a game.

Yet the rewards reaped from this game were solid and tangible.

In just half a year, Xia Bing's cultivation had solidified by an entire layer. Even without the secret realm's combat buffs, her spirit pets exhibited mottled red patterns in the outside world, steadily evolving toward the form Xia Bing desired. As long as she had a bit more time, she would undoubtedly be able to guide these spirit pets into their ideal state.

Consequently, even though Xia Bing could now be considered thoroughly riding the winds of success in Thousand Beasts Hall:

She had already been promoted to manager in Thousand Beasts Hall, overseeing an entire mountain peak and a contingent of low-tier disciples. The spirit sheep and divine cows she grazed were already sufficient to fulfill a substantial portion of Mount Tianling's purchasing orders.

—Though these orders seemed perpetually insatiable, especially since Yibei Tea had expanded its business further following the auction, reportedly opening branch stalls across every major immortal city.

With this steady stream of orders, coupled with having successfully gilded her credentials during the Winter Clothes Festival and latched firmly onto Sect Leader Yan Bailang's coattails, Xia Bing was no longer an insignificant pawn that anyone could bully in Thousand Beasts Hall. She practically had whatever she desired.

Even so, Xia Bing still preferred coming to Qianxing Secret Realm to grind levels.

This excursion was no exception.

In truth, over the course of their half-year exploration, Xia Bing and her regular teammates had long discovered the lair of the secret realm's strongest adversary. To be safe, however, they had decided to exercise caution, gearing up more thoroughly before challenging it.

Xia Bing's luck had also been quite good, having arrived right in time for the New Year promotional sales.

This was the very first time Xia Bing had ever heard of cultivators celebrating the New Year just like mortal folk. Even so, she was fond of Qianxing Hall decked out in festive decorations, vibrant and overflowing with joy—a sight that reminded her of a happy childhood that had never actually existed.

Xia Bing and her teammates had taken advantage of the discounts to go on a massive shopping spree, completely overhauling their equipment.

And now, they stood before the secret realm's Ice Eye.

This Ice Eye was visibly, conspicuously problematic.

It sat atop a frigid ice cavern, surrounded by Snow-Luster Grass. A deep pool of freezing water, any cultivator lacking sufficient cultivation who drew close would suffer the corrosion of cold poison. Yet it appeared to be the true core of the secret realm; looking upward, one could see the floating island suspended above, like a colossal eye quietly gazing down upon everyone below.

Unquestionably, something was deeply amiss with this Ice Eye, yet it seemed to exceed the capabilities of the disciples exploring the secret realm. Thus, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had placed restrictive barriers sealing the perimeter of this Ice Eye, ensuring that only by following the secret realm's guided quest steps and exploring every corner would one finally be led back here.

Based on clues scattered throughout the realm, everyone was already mentally prepared for what lay beneath.

—"According to the builder's records, he sealed a carp spirit beneath this spot that was on the verge of turning into a dragon,"-said Rong Rong, Xia Bing's junior sister and companion from Thousand Beasts Hall. She was carefully reading through a small notebook—her self-authored walkthrough guide, in which she had meticulously cataloged every clue gathered around the secret realm.-"By utilizing its dragon-transformation power, he created this pool of unfrozen water."

—"How utterly wicked,"-remarked another companion, Qingyang Yang of Eternal Villa, fluttering his folding fan before the frigid ice cavern.-"The poor creature was about to turn into a dragon, only to be sealed in a place like this and turned into a formation eye."

—"Hence, this carp spirit is estimated to harbor immense resentment,"-Rong Rong explained.-"Anyone lacking Nascent Soul cultivation who falls into this frigid pool will inevitably be invaded by cold poison—yet to dispel this poison, one must use this very water, forcing victims to plunge into the cold pool every few years."

*Hiss.* Everyone present shivered upon hearing this.

Merely standing beside this cold pool was freezing enough; what on earth would it feel like to fall in?

Worse yet, having to voluntarily take a dip every few years—what kind of sadistic trial was this?!

—"However, if we undo the seal, the spiritual energy released from the carp spirit's dragon transformation will be enough to elevate all our cultivations by a layer,"-Xia Bing pointed out.-"Furthermore, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's final secret realm quest explicitly states: 'Release the Carp Spirit,' and that they will handle the aftermath."

—"What's with this tone assuming we definitely can't beat it?"-Qingyang Yang grumbled, displeased.

The disciple from the Heavenly Spirit Sect chuckled:

—"It's not looking down on you; it's our Heavenly Spirit Sect providing a safety net. The quest rewards are split into three tiers: the baseline reward is absorbing the dragon qi upon releasing the carp spirit; the second-tier reward is claiming the treasure beneath the cold pool; and the top-tier reward is claiming everything! If Brother Qingyang can truly defeat it, that carp spirit belongs to Eternal Villa."

—"Heh, just don't regret it later,"-Qingyang Yang said.

With a sharp *snap*, he closed his fan and retrieved a dharma treasure from his sleeve:

—"For this raid, I went so far as to borrow my ancestor's natal dharma treasure! Never mind a carp—even if it were a true dragon, it wouldn't escape!"

—"Then we wish Brother Qingyang the best of luck!"-the others laughed.

Nobody noticed that throughout this conversation, not a single person present harbored any doubt that if they failed to defeat the carp spirit, the Heavenly Spirit Sect would step in to clean up the aftermath.

In the cutthroat, treacherous cultivation world, cultivators encountering such a scenario would normally never trust another sect; they would even suspect it of being an elaborate death trap.

Yet within Qianxing Secret Realm, nobody harbored such suspicions.

At least in the hearts of this group of secret realm explorers, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had already garnered a degree of institutional credibility rivaling their own sects.

No—in the eyes of cultivators from certain factions, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was even more trustworthy than their own sects!

[—"Isn't that just because you're secretly cultivating the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and don't trust your own sect?"](# "Speaker: Mind-Interrogation Stone")

At that moment, a voice suddenly echoed within Xia Bing's mind.

Hearing this voice, Xia Bing didn't bat an eye, calmly burying the Mind-Interrogation Stone deeper into the recesses of her consciousness. Turning to the others with a radiant smile, she asked:

—"In that case, shall I break the seal?"

Accompanied by the cold pool water erupting high like a geyser, a crystalline, translucent creature—half dragon, half carp—soared out from the depths of the frigid pool, exuding an aura of bone-chilling frost.

*

Mo Bei stood atop the high floating island, gazing down upon this scene with an impassive expression.

She was waiting.

Waiting for the moment the raid team's strength fell short.

*

A carp leaping through the Dragon Gate.

The power of ascension.

—"There is a method of ascension where one fuses the Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul with their own soul, borrowing that power to ascend,"-Zhou Hui was currently explaining to Tao Zhu.-"The feasibility seems quite high. The only catch is poor compatibility; thus, the ascender must bare their soul and soak their spirit in the dragon pool water that sealed the carp. In that moment, one is completely defenseless. If someone happens to obtain a 'God-Slaying Sword', it is virtually an instant kill."

—"What kind of braindead method is that?!"-Tao Zhu sputtered.-"Wait a minute... you said you got your hands on the Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul. Don't tell me you originally planned to use this method?!"

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum chimed in as well:

—"I think you'd better give up on that idea!"

Relying on a carp to carry a colossal tree into the heavens—that was a flat-out impossibility!

Zhou Hui chuckled upon hearing their reactions:

—"So everyone thinks it's impossible after all?"

[Yet in the Heavenly Dao's script, he had seemingly gone completely insane and actually done it!]

In his first six lifetimes, he had never once considered such an absurd method—though in those six lives, he had never acquired the secret realm, nor had he possessed any information regarding it.

Consequently, Zhou Hui had been somewhat curious: if he were to lay flat as a [salted fish slacker](# "Literal CN: 咸鱼 (xiányú) — slacker / laying flat"), how would the Heavenly Dao force him to use the Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul?

Now, however, before that bizarre ascension path could even manifest, the Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul was being put to use in an unexpected place.

No, perhaps it wasn't so strange after all.

The Dragon Gate was inherently a type of spatial rift; for a carp to transform into a dragon, it had to traverse space. Borrowing its power to construct a teleportation array was, surprisingly, remarkably sound.

—"Putting that ascension method aside for now, at least we have one of the materials,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Yeah, I'll think of ways to track down the rest,"-Tao Zhu replied.-"Still, they probably can't be gathered all at once."

—"It doesn't matter; I'm merely letting you know the possibility exists,"-Zhou Hui noted.-"Besides, we don't need such a massive array right from the start. Lost teleportation arrays are scattered throughout the Central Domain; it would be better to first test repairing the smaller arrays within the Abyss Sea!"

—"Wait a second, Untiring... the prize you gave out earlier wasn't a map of teleportation array ruins, was it?!"-Lan Pei asked in delayed realization.-"You're using those people as free labor to search for suitable arrays?!"

—"Is there a problem with that?"-Zhou Hui smiled gently.

—"Why do I feel like you're more of a fox than I am?"-Lan Pei muttered under his breath.

Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum stood aside with total nonchalance, casually patting the storage pouch containing her share of the prize.

She had no objections whatsoever to this reward.

After all, most of White Jade Capital's teleportation arrays had been constructed in what were bustling hubs at the time, virtually all of which were tied to ancient ruins. What did it matter if she was being used to confirm the locations of teleportation arrays?

In fact, if Zhou Hui possessed the methods to repair teleportation arrays, it would save her immense trouble, making traveling back and forth between ruins far easier.

Tao Zhu folded the teleportation array blueprint neatly and slipped it carefully into his storage pouch:

—"Then I'll head down beneath the Abyss Sea first to consult Patriarch Ran of the Ran Clan about this... As for that Dragon-Saliva Ice Soul, don't touch it yet. Wait until we've discussed things before retrieving it."

Tao Zhu felt he worried endlessly for this old friend.

How could this man invent so many suicidal death-seeking methods without ever repeating a single one?!

—"Rest assured, I won't touch it,"-Zhou Hui replied. He had never intended to follow the bizarre method provided by the Heavenly Dao anyway.-"Normally, I only use it to give my disciples some practice...!"

At this point, Zhou Hui froze, his gaze turning in the direction of his sect.

—"What happened?"-Tao Zhu's heart instantly leapt into his throat.

—"...It seems the plan for how to utilize that carp will have to be revised,"-Zhou Hui said with an odd expression.-"My kid seems to have made that carp spirit recognize her as its master."
