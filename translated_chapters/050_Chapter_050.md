# Chapter 50: Another Path (第50章 另一条路)

[All according to plan.](# "Literal CN: 计划通 (jìhuà tōng) — Japanese meme 'keikaku doori', meaning everything is proceeding exactly as plotted; a direct nod to the previous chapter title")

Those merchants had indeed become completely rooted to the spot the instant they laid eyes upon Qianxing Hall.

[Once they actually step inside the secret realm, they will be even more reluctant to leave!]

Zhang Hongjing thought to himself.

At this moment, he sat inside the ["Sovereign Suite"](# "Literal CN: 人皇套房 (Rénhuáng Tàofáng) — Heavenly Spirit Sect's extravagant room naming scheme, literally 'Human Emperor Suite'") of a small pavilion perched atop the secret realm's floating island—or at least, that was the grandiose name the Heavenly Spirit Sect had bestowed upon the lodging—gazing out through an expansive, transparent glazed glass wall at the undulating, tiered landscapes of the realm below.

The scenery was breathtakingly exquisite.

Hardly surprising, given that this was the very spot the Heavenly Dao had meticulously prepared for the fateful first encounter between the Child of Destiny and his [First Love White Moonlight](# "Literal CN: 初恋白月光 (chūliàn bái yuèguāng) — web novel trope for the pure, unattainable, idealized first love who forever haunts the protagonist's memories").

—Yes, Mo Bei's canonical narrative role was that of the First Love White Moonlight.

Within the grand script orchestrated by the Heavenly Dao: aside from Mo Bei surrendering the majority of her cultivation upon their very first meeting via [methods unsuitable for detailed description](# "Literal CN: 不能详写的方式 — meta-satire of web novel censorship guidelines prohibiting explicit intimate dual cultivation scenes"), treating the protagonist with exceptional favor in the sect afterward which predictably triggered waves of bullying against him, and ultimately sacrificing her Master to fulfill their tragic romance; in the subsequent story arcs, Mo Bei was constantly abducted by her blackened, demon-corrupted twin brother Mo Xi, abducted by demonic beasts, and abducted by the demon race. This convenient recurring absence systematically manufactured an environment devoid of the [official wife](# "Literal CN: 正宫 (zhènggōng) — the main wife / Empress in an imperial harem"), granting the protagonist ample breathing room to foster passionate romances with a red rose, a yellow rose, a white rose, and indeed an entire botanical garden of maidens—all while hypocritically declining their advances with deep 'regret' on the grounds that he already had a wife (the kind where he did absolutely everything with them, yet took zero responsibility afterward).

It was not until the Child of Destiny was on the verge of ascending to the True Immortal Realm that Mo Bei was finally 'rescued'—only to perish a few chapters later during a war against the demon race while shielding the protagonist. Weeping bitter tears, the protagonist had no choice but to refine her mortal remains into a magical artifact as an eternal 'keepsake', conveniently employing it to facilitate his own ascension alongside the persistent red rose who had tagged along behind him the entire way and refused to be chased off, nursing an everlasting 'unfulfilled void' deep within his aching heart.

Word had it that this romantic subplot was actually wildly popular. Whenever Mo Bei's name was brought up on forums in another world, it inevitably sparked vicious [shipping wars](# "Literal CN: 党争 (dǎngzhēng) — fandom shipping faction warfare"). As a side note: beyond the sprawling botanical garden of romantic contenders, Mo Xi had once disguised himself as his twin sister to frame the protagonist, thereby accidentally getting dragged into the shipping wars himself—and in one infamous forum popularity poll, he had miraculously surpassed his own sister to claim the number one spot!

Zhou Hui's reaction to this was simple: *What in the unholy hell is this garbage?!*

Even setting aside the fact that Mo Bei was his disciple, he found the entire premise too atrocious to look at. Whenever he had accidentally overheard audio snippets of related television adaptations in his past life, he would promptly shove earplugs into his ears, which was why his recollection of the finer plot details remained rather fragmented.

Ah, the topic had wandered off track again.

Zhang Hongjing, of course, had no inkling that this secret realm was supposed to be the staging ground for some 'peerlessly tragic romance.' Viewed purely through the discerning eyes of a veteran cultivator who had traversed the Five Domains and Six Abysses, the realm was engineered with breathtaking ingenuity.

It resembled an exquisitely designed bonsai miniature, layered upon a foundation of softly iridescent Snow-Glow Grass. At first glance, it appeared as a unified, seamless panorama; yet upon closer inspection, a single hillside, a cave, or even a modest puddle constituted a self-contained pocket world of its own, brimming with subtle details worthy of careful savoring. Little wonder cultivators could enter and exit dozens of times without ever growing weary of it.

However, maintaining such an intricate 'bonsai' was also exceptionally arduous.

It demanded an immense volume of spiritual energy and a relatively enclosed environment. Hence, in the 'novel,' several sects had only opened the realm once every few decades in a bid to preserve it, yet even that had failed to halt the steady degradation of its internal ecology.

And once it crossed paths with the protagonist, the secret realm was doomed beyond salvation.

Spiritual herbs exceeding two hundred years of age were uprooted down to the last fiber; spirit beasts above Tier 4 were slaughtered to extinction; the realm core was shattered; and the demon sealed within was released. The place was stripped utterly bare and laid waste. Yet one couldn't even blame the protagonist—after all, since the realm couldn't be sustained indefinitely anyway, why not let the protagonist plunder every single scrap before its total annihilation?

Yet now, despite hordes of cultivators cycling in and out on a daily basis, the secret realm's ecology remained entirely unharmed.

In fact, the ambient spiritual energy was even denser than during Zhang Hongjing's previous visit.

Indeed: shortly after the secret realm had opened, back before Heavenly Spirit Sect had erected scenic lodging upon the floating island and while cultivators above Golden Core were still barred from entry, Zhang Hongjing had used a specialized magical treasure to suppress his cultivation and slipped inside for a covert inspection.

He harbored no ill intentions; he merely wished to discern what the Untiring Immortal Venerable was truly aiming to accomplish.

After all, once this secret realm opened to the public, vast swarms of rogue cultivators would inevitably flock here. As the leader of the sect boasting the largest concentration of rogue cultivators, Zhang Hongjing, in his capacity as Sect Leader of the Thousand Harmony Gang, needed to ensure the safety of his people.

During that single excursion, Zhang Hongjing had been thoroughly captivated by the Untiring Immortal Venerable's genius—especially that combat simulation framework capable of replicating high-tier battles. It had left Zhang Hongjing bitterly lamenting why no such secret realm had existed back when his own cultivation was still modest.

For rogue cultivators devoid of masterly guidance, their only recourse was to bumble blindly through cultivation and forge dharma weapons purely on gut instinct. Yet certain techniques, once practiced, could never be abandoned midway even if flawed, and certain weapons, once forged, could never be reverted to raw materials. This resulted in catastrophic waste for rogue cultivators who were already strapped for resources, inflating their true cost of cultivation far beyond that of coddled sect disciples.

Consequently, a secret realm capable of simulating developmental trajectories—even if it yielded modest materials, or even if it yielded nothing at all—would still see legions of cultivators lining up at the door.

Zhang Hongjing had even felt a strong urge to remodel several secret realms under the Thousand Harmony Gang's control along similar lines.

The only reason he hadn't carried it out was that the internal factions of the Thousand Harmony Gang were far too convoluted. Every single secret realm was entwined with the vested interests of rival protectors and elders. Even if he secured the Untiring Immortal Venerable's permission, replicating it would have been near impossible.

Yet Zhang Hongjing knew that if he himself was this moved, those merchants would be even more enraptured.

As long as he brought them here, cold, hard profit would do all the talking.

Why, even Boss Tao of the Abyssal Ocean Society had put on that display!

(Even if the precise reason for Tao Zhu's agonizing heartbreak differed somewhat from that of the other merchants.)

—"Once we secure the merchant syndicates' backing, all that remains are several pivotal sects and immortal city administrators,"-Zhang Hongjing planned, gazing out over the panorama.-"First, Thousand Beasts Hall has nowhere else to turn; they are guaranteed to back Heavenly Spirit Sect. Furthermore, Eternal Villa's attitude is exceptionally favorable toward this side as well. That secures two highly advantageous foundational bases. As for the others, a bit of light intimidation should suffice. What truly warrants concern is the attitude of Lord Immortal Venerable himself—who knows whether this was merely a fleeting whim, or just casual talk..."

[Even if it was just casual talk, I must hammer it into stone!]

Zhang Hongjing resolved in his heart.

If he failed to seize this opportunity to [hug the Untiring Immortal Venerable's thigh](# "Literal CN: 抱大腿 (bào dàtuǐ) — slang for attaching oneself to a powerful patron / piggybacking on a titan"), he would sooner or later be forced to side with Carefree Palace. At that point, it would no longer be a question of moral bottom lines, but whether he could even retain control over his own life.

He was desperate to avoid falling into such a plight at all costs.

Yet Zhang Hongjing remained blissfully unaware that as he pondered this, his shadow beneath the guest room's radiant illumination twisted in a grotesque, unnatural contortion. At the head of the shadow, bubbles resembling boiling mire welled up, churning and popping. Moments later, bloodshot eyeballs emerged at the shadow's ear and crown, fixing their inorganic, dead stares onto the unsuspecting Zhang Hongjing.

Zhang Hongjing was still engrossed in calculating his grand patron-courting enterprise:

—"Still, word has it that Lord Immortal Venerable made improvements to Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-protecting grand formation(?). If the intelligence from Thousand Beasts Hall holds true, we won't even need Lord Immortal Venerable to intervene in person to cow them! The genuine dilemma actually lies with hosting the auction itself..."-Zhang Hongjing calculated.

Without Carefree Palace, and if Heavenly Spirit Sect merely lent its name as a figurehead, staging this auction solely on the Thousand Harmony Gang's shoulders would be practically impossible.

Although several factions had expressed interest in collaborating, every single one looked more than a little suspicious.

The mere thought of it gave Zhang Hongjing a throbbing headache.

—"Forget it. I'll draft the plans after I've formally met with Lord Immortal Venerable and discerned his true stance! Anything said before then is premature,"-Zhang Hongjing sighed.-"Indeed, the crucial pivot is still the Untiring Immortal Venerable."

Gazing down at the Snow-Glow Grass glistening like freshly fallen snow in the night, he gave a wry smile and muttered to himself:

—"At the very least, I mustn't waste such an extortionate room. I had better make the most of the experience,"-Zhang Hongjing muttered.

Pulling over a nearby prayer mat, Zhang Hongjing assumed a meditative posture.

Instantly, he felt brisk, vital spiritual energy flowing around him.

While this ambient spiritual energy might not match the raw concentration of the cultivation world's renowned grotto-heavens and blessed lands, its liveliness and refineability surpassed them by far.

Zhang Hongjing knew this was the doing of the cultivators within the realm.

Although the hillside lodges outside and the private meditation rooms inside Qianxing Hall commanded steep prices, there was a way to spend the night within the secret realm without consuming one's time limit—and in fact earn bonus experience points: namely, ["Tranquil Meditation"](# "Literal CN: 静心打坐 (jìngxīn dǎzuò)") without popping pills or consuming external consumables.

When meditating cultivators condensed their own spiritual power, they simultaneously accelerated the ambient circulation of spiritual energy around them, purifying it, enhancing spatial qi circulation, and generating an atmosphere far more conducive to cultivation for themselves and others. Consequently, most sects maintained dedicated 'Tranquility Halls' or 'Meditation Chambers' to have disciples meditate in collective groups.

The theory was sound enough, but the practical results were severely limited.

After all, even a cultivator of Zhang Hongjing's level wrestled with endless worldly worries, to say nothing of lesser cultivators. Truly achieving tranquility was an exercise in futility; distracting stray thoughts were an absolute certainty.

Particularly for cultivators burdened with miscellaneous Four-Element or Five-Element Spirit Roots: after toiling for half a day without absorbing the desired elemental energy, irritability was inevitable. And emotions were contagious. While the ideal scenario held that a congregation of disciples meditating without stray thoughts could accelerate the condensation and refinement of spiritual energy, the reality was that just one or two agitated disciples in a meditation hall were enough to infect everyone around them with restlessness, yielding an efficiency far worse than meditating alone.

Even a room packed with Heavenly Spirit Roots could not evade this pitfall.

Hence, countless cultivators preferred [popping pills like candy](# "Literal CN: 嗑药 (kèyào) — slang for relying heavily on pills/elixirs") or plopping themselves directly over a spirit spring. That was essentially equivalent to violently blasting raw spiritual energy down one's meridians; while it boosted cultivation levels, it contributed zilch to condensing ambient spiritual power or stimulating spatial qi circulation.

Transplanted into this secret realm, however, the dynamic underwent a profound shift.

Cultivators ventured here primarily for exploration; whether their meditation absorbed surplus spiritual energy was secondary. Consequently, they found it far easier to quiet their minds, and even if their meditation yielded suboptimal results, they were far less prone to frustration.

More importantly: as they meditated, the interface panel was generating experience points!

Meditating for two shichen and watching one's [EXP bar](# "Literal CN: 经验条 (jīngyàn tiáo) — MMORPG experience bar") tick upward by a visible notch—which could later be redeemed for combat buffs—left everyone feeling thoroughly pleased with themselves. Even the lowest-efficiency five-root cultivator sitting through an entire night would never feel their efforts had been in vain.

All these positive feedback loops compounding together rendered the secret realm's ambient spiritual energy vibrantly active.

Naturally, when it came to the precise quantitative values of spiritual energy, far more intricate calculations were involved, though that was beyond what Zhang Hongjing's naked eyes could discern.

Nor was Zhang Hongjing in any mood to ponder such profound principles at this moment.

As an honored guest occupying the 'Sovereign Suite,' he resided at one of the realm's highest vantage points, where the spiritual energy was most vibrant. Even though his cultivation stood leagues above the realm's average threshold, enveloped in this spiritual atmosphere, he effortlessly cast aside his earthly worries and plunged headlong into a wondrous, ethereal trance.

The spiritual energy he absorbed soon fed back into the secret realm as well.

Had anyone activated their 'Spirit Eyes' at that moment, they would have seen purple spiritual qi coalescing into iridescent auroras, condensing into violet clouds around Zhang Hongjing. Even as it nourished Zhang Hongjing's dantian, it billowed outward, feeding back into the surrounding space.

Alas, good times never lasted.

Just as Zhang Hongjing was savoring this rare respite from worldly anxieties, thoroughly absorbed in cultivation, that pair of slanted eyes within his shadow snapped open once more!

And this time, accompanying the opening of those eyes, Zhang Hongjing's shadow seemed to evaporate, exuding wisps of black qi. Even as it corroded the resplendent violet spiritual clouds, it spun out like gossamer threads, slowly winding itself around Zhang Hongjing's body. It cast his form into an unprompted shroud of shadow that grew progressively thicker. And as the shadow deepened, the two eyes slowly crept forward, crawling from the shadow onto his robes, and from his robes toward his bare skin.

Yet before they could make contact with Zhang Hongjing's flesh:

—"Hah!"-Zhang Hongjing roared, his eyes snapping wide.

Violet arcs of lightning erupted across his body, blasting outward against the creeping shadows and the two slanted eyes draped over his frame.

Amidst the thunderous glare, the shadows dissolved like dust, vaporized in an instant. Yet the pair of eyes merely blinked before bulging to their absolute limit. The eyeballs protruded grotesquely, laced with angry red capillaries, while crimson blood vessels resembling optic nerves writhed rapidly below, not only latching fiercely onto Zhang Hongjing's robes, but spreading across his flesh at terrifying speed.

—"Aaaaargh—!"-Zhang Hongjing shrieked.

Clutching his skull, his own eyes bulged with bloodshot veins. Countless chaotic voices erupted inside his mind—voices that sounded as though they were being produced by his very own vocal cords, systematically usurping the thoughts within his consciousness.

At that terrifying juncture, a crystal-clear realization struck Zhang Hongjing:

He was about to be replaced!

He was about to be usurped by the entity emitting those chaotic cacophonies!

Sheer, paralyzing terror seized Zhang Hongjing's body. He couldn't even fathom when or by whom he had been ambushed into such a horrific state.

It was at that very instant that a faint voice echoed:

—"...A [Heart-Devouring Ghost](# "Literal CN: 噬心鬼 (Shìxīn Guǐ) / 噬心魔 (Shìxīn Mó) — insidious parasitic demon planted by Carefree Palace into shadows to replace dissenters")? Carefree Palace's tricks are always the same tired old routine,"-a voice observed coolly.

Accompanying those words, a hand rested gently atop Zhang Hongjing's crown. Instantly, a bone-chilling frost stabbed into the Thousand Harmony Gang leader's skull like an icicle.

It was freezing, agonizing—yet remarkably invigorating.

Zhang Hongjing felt the horrific cacophony recede into the distance, regaining a shred of his cognitive faculty.

He saw cyan flames igniting across his body—not scorching, but freezing to the bone—burning(?) the two slanted eyes until they trembled violently. He watched the slanted eyes detach and fall from his torso. And he caught sight of the hem of a white robe fluttering before him, its owner seemingly perched with dangling feet, looking down at him.

—"Immor... Untiring Immortal Venerable, save me!"-Zhang Hongjing pleaded on instinct.

He ultimately stopped short of actually throwing himself forward to hug the thigh—chiefly because the proportions of those legs looked distinctly off. Instead, he painfully raised his head to look upward.

Zhang Hongjing beheld no legendary silver-haired immortal. Instead, a youth of fifteen or sixteen years gazed down at him with clear, heterochromatic cat pupils, looking as utterly innocent as a kitten batting at a cockroach with its paws.

—"Are you out of your mind? Does a trivial nuisance like this warrant my Master stepping out?"-the youth remarked proudly. Bending down, he scooped up the pair of eyeballs without the slightest hesitation.

Yet Zhang Hongjing stared at the eyeballs with lingering dread, thinking:

[You call this a trivial nuisance?! That is clearly a demonic creature!]

Zhang Hongjing opened his mouth to retort, only to suddenly realize that this youth possessed merely a Foundation Establishment cultivation base.

[A Void Refinement cultivator like me actually got outdone by a Foundation Establishment brat... Wait, no! He may be at Foundation Establishment, but that spiritual flame is something no Foundation Establishment cultivator could ever possess! From the resonance I sensed, does it contain both a Mine Spirit and a Yin Spirit?!]

Setting aside bizarre oddities like a Yin Spirit for a moment—a Mine Spirit! That was an entity found solely within top-grade spirit stone mines! And to nurture it into a natal magical artifact, one practically had to gift an entire spirit mine to the recipient! Even the wealthiest cultivation clans showering their most pampered heirs wouldn't dare pull off such an absurdly prodigal stunt.

Yet this youth referred to the Untiring Immortal Venerable as 'Master'?

Zhang Hongjing suddenly recalled a piece of intelligence he had gathered from Thousand Beasts Hall—a scrap he hadn't been sure would prove useful, but now seemed exceedingly pertinent. Hesitating, Zhang Hongjing ventured:

—"Are you Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's most favored youngest disciple, Mo Xi?"

The youth paused visibly, whereupon his grin blossomed into radiant delight.

—"You've got sharp eyes!"-Mo Xi beamed gleefully:—"That's right! I am Mo Xi, my Master's most favored youngest disciple!"

In high spirits, Mo Xi generously explained the reason for his appearance:

—"For now, the adjustments for this secret realm happen to be under my management. So when I detected an anomaly in the spiritual energy circulation, I came over to investigate."

—"...This secret realm was given to you as well?"-Zhang Hongjing asked in astonishment.

[It was clearly the second disciple who secured this secret realm, yet in the blink of an eye, it was handed to the youngest disciple? Talk about outrageous favoritism!]

Rolling his eyes, Mo Xi countered:

—"You have a problem with Heavenly Spirit Sect's administrative regulations?"

He would never admit it! In truth, he was doing [unpaid labor](# "Literal CN: 打白工 (dǎbáigōng) — working for free / uncompensated overtime") for his Second Senior Brother.

The reality was that due to the recent influx of new disciples, sect administrative affairs had exploded. Trivial daily matters could hardly be brought to bother Zhou Hui, while Eldest Disciple Yuwen Xuan was an utterly unreliable brute, meaning the entire mountain of paperwork collapsed onto the shoulders of Heavenly Spirit Sect's de facto second-in-command, Qu Qianxing.

Unable to juggle the secret realm's management, Qu Qianxing had dumped it onto Mo Xi.

At the time, he had delivered his pitch thus:

> "Youngest Junior Brother, you are a member of this sect. To enjoy rights, one must fulfill obligations. I hear from Master that you possess quite the knack for this sort of thing, so I am entrusting it to your care! Compensation? There is none. However, revenue generated here will be incorporated into the Heavenly Spirit Sect treasury. You wouldn't want to disappoint Master, would you?"

[Damned Qu Number Two!]

[That black-hearted, tax-collecting housekeeper!]

[If Master hadn't designated that guy as the next Sect Master and instructed me to assist him—and if this project, though paying zero monthly allowance, weren't likely to yield a massive year-end bonus red envelope—I wouldn't have bothered touching it with a ten-foot pole!]

Mo Xi thought with indignant resentment.

Zhang Hongjing suspected nothing of the sort—after all, when a Master gave away an entire mine, what was a secret realm?—yet he did not miss the critical piece of information slipped into the conversation:

—"The spiritual energy circulation was... detected?"-Zhang Hongjing asked, unable to miss the crucial detail.

Furthermore, the moment an issue cropped up here, it was detected over there immediately. Was it because he was a 'VIP' who happened to be observed? Or was the entire realm being monitored around the clock?

Under normal circumstances, it would likely be the former?

Zhang Hongjing's chronic habit of wanting to avoid dark secrets flared up again:

—"My apologies. I did not mean to pry into the secret realm's confidential affairs..."-Zhang Hongjing said hastily.

—"It's hardly a secret. With so many cultivators cycling in and out of the secret realm each day, how much spiritual energy they consume and how much they refine naturally has to be calculated in real time,"-Mo Xi stated nonchalantly:—"You didn't think this secret realm could regenerate indefinitely no matter how people trample it, did you?"

—"I assumed Lord Immortal Venerable had cast some divine art,"-Zhang Hongjing said blankly.

—"Is that really necessary? For a mere secret realm like this?"-Mo Xi chuckled.

Zhang Hongjing, however, stubbornly clung to his view.

He was convinced that even if Mo Xi possessed the means to govern this secret realm, it was entirely via some magical treasure bestowed by Zhou Hui, much like the flame that had just incinerated the unholy entity from his body. This deduction wasn't entirely mistaken; after all, the underlying 'Secret Realm System' had indeed been constructed by Zhou Hui, while monitoring telemetry data and live operations were handled by Mo Xi.

Yet this differed fundamentally from Zhou Hui personally controlling and operating it himself.

After all, once Zhou Hui established the underlying foundation, he could redirect his own computational resources(X) toward other projects. Routine management was delegated to his disciples; he merely needed to cast an occasional glance to ensure nothing derailed on a macro level.

There was no need to elaborate on such nuanced distinctions to Zhang Hongjing.

Nor did Zhang Hongjing possess the guts to pry further.

Having resolved the anomaly on Zhang Hongjing, Mo Xi turned on his heel to leave:

—"Since the bug is resolved, I'm heading back!"-Mo Xi said, turning on his heel to leave.

—"Wait, Fellow Daoist Mo!"-Zhang Hongjing cried, reacting with lightning reflexes to grab the hem of Mo Xi's robe.

He was shamelessly flattering the boy by addressing him as 'Fellow Daoist.'

Yet since the other party was 'Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's most favored youngest disciple,' cultivation realm, age, and generational seniority were utterly trivial irrelevancies.

Zhang Hongjing pressed urgently:

—"Fellow Daoist Mo, as Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's most cherished disciple, you must surely know, right? What is Lord Immortal Venerable's actual attitude toward our visit...?"

Evidently, Zhang Hongjing's stellar popularity was no accident.

In just a few exchanges, he had already discerned that Mo Xi took the greatest pride in being 'Master's most favored disciple'—a claim that was undeniably worth boasting about.

Yet Mo Xi was clearly not someone who could be bought over with a couple of sweet words.

—"Rest easy. My Master received your visiting card and is already prepared to grant you an audience,"-Mo Xi offered the good news first, before casting a half-smiling, half-mocking glance at Zhang Hongjing:—"But are you certain you still want to see my Master? Especially after what just happened? Aren't you afraid Carefree Palace will truly slaughter you?"

—"...Even without this incident, Carefree Palace wants me dead anyway!"-Zhang Hongjing gritted his teeth.

He did not believe the monster from earlier was an isolated, sudden event.

At the very least, it couldn't have been planted recently.

Ever since he had failed to immediately comply with Carefree Palace's demand to pick a side, Zhang Hongjing had spent every waking day dreading their retaliation. He layered dozens of defensive wards over himself daily. He held reasonable confidence in his own methods; under such heightened vigilance, even if a Mahayana cultivator sought to ambush him, Zhang Hongjing believed he would have received some warning.

Yet that demonic entity had materialized without a sound, deliberately choosing the precise moment he entered deep meditation. Had the spiritual energy in this secret realm not been extraordinarily pure and vibrant—making that wisp of demonic qi reek as conspicuously as someone letting rip a foul fart in pristine air—he might very well have succumbed completely.

Zhang Hongjing was certain this demon hadn't attached itself to him recently; rather, it had lain dormant as a parasitic seed within his vicinity for many years.

In other words, Carefree Palace had plotted against him long ago.

—"Not necessarily,"-Mo Xi offered an explanation on Carefree Palace's behalf, intimately familiar with their playbook from his years in Fallen Immortal Palace:—"The [Heart-Devouring Demon](# "Literal CN: 噬心魔 (Shìxīn Mó) / 噬心鬼 (Shìxīn Guǐ) — insidious parasitic demon planted by Carefree Palace into shadows to replace dissenters") is considered quite a precious demonic creature. It can parasitize a person's shadow for centuries without being detected, awakening solely under specific emotional triggers—such as the moment you firmly resolve to oppose Carefree Palace. Only then will it usurp your soul and replace you. In other words, as long as you remained obedient, Carefree Palace wouldn't have lifted a finger against you."

—"But isn't living like that no different from being dead?"-Zhang Hongjing lamented with a bitter grimace.

Hearing his reply, Mo Xi blinked and sat back down in front of Zhang Hongjing:

—"So, you want my Master to save you?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"Claiming I don't harbor such thoughts would be an outright lie,"-Zhang Hongjing rasped:—"Yet I know my own place. I do not ask Lord Immortal Venerable to aid me without cause; I only beg for an opportunity."

—"Good attitude,"-Mo Xi smiled:—"And you've done rather well so far. If you can help us pull off this auction smoothly, I really could plead your case before Master."

He deliberately threw him a favor here.

Or more precisely: Mo Xi's willingness to come in person was naturally because Zhang Hongjing's conduct had already pleased Zhou Hui.

Yet favor aside, the information Mo Xi let slip sent Zhang Hongjing into ecstasy:

—"Pull off the auction... Meaning, Lord Immortal Venerable didn't just mention it as a fleeting whim for fun?!"-Zhang Hongjing asked in disbelief.

—"Does my Master look like the type to joke about something like this?"-Mo Xi frowned.

—"Of course not, of course not!"-Zhang Hongjing was so moved he nearly wept:—"But Lord Immortal Venerable possessing the genuine intent to host it, versus having no interest in hosting it, are two completely different matters!"

—"Is it really that serious? Are you people starved for inventory to such an extent?"-Mo Xi asked curiously.

—"Inventory? That is merely a minor issue,"-Zhang Hongjing shook his head:—"The genuine crisis is the trade routes!"

***

—"Trade routes? What he means is merchandise transportation! That is indeed a major problem,"-Zhou Hui remarked with a nod after listening to Mo Xi's report.

—"Master thinks it's a major problem too?"-Mo Xi asked in surprise.

—"Traveling the roads is no simple feat,"-Zhou Hui reminisced with a touch of nostalgia:—"Even I was only able to travel to various lands virtually unimpeded once I reached Void Refinement."

This issue was quite similar to the communication bottleneck.

In the cultivation world, transportation posed no challenge for high-tier cultivators; in the blink of an eye, they could traverse an entire continent. But for mortals and low-tier cultivators, transit was a mortal ordeal, capable of claiming lives at any moment.

Due to the hazards of demonic beasts, spiritual miasmas, and treacherous terrains, carving out a road in this world was thousands of times harder than in another world. Thus, among the Five Domains, only the Northern Domain had formed a unified, gargantuan mortal empire—largely because their geographic conditions were gentle and their dense population of cultivators held demonic creatures in check.

Conversely, the Central Domain possessed the harshest, most unforgiving environment.

Setting aside other tribulations, the turbulent spiritual storms lingering from the ancient Fiendgod War rendered safe aerial flight impossible even for cultivators. Zhou Hui's boast of 'traveling unimpeded after Void Refinement' was actually a subtle humblebrag; in truth, without meticulous preparations, even Mahayana powerhouses would not dare recklessly traverse the Central Domain's heartland.

When even flying through the sky was this perilous, carving out navigable pathways across the earth was naturally a monumental feat.

—"Do you know why past Winter Solstice Auctions were always held in Fengqing City in the southeast?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Because that is where the largest teleportation array connecting to other domains is located,"-Mo Xi replied without hesitation:—"However, Carefree Palace blockaded the trade routes this year, rendering that teleportation array largely moot. Changing the venue shouldn't be difficult, right?"

—"On the contrary, it is exceedingly difficult,"-Zhou Hui said placidly:—"Precisely because the teleportation array sits there, anyone wishing to depart the Central Domain travels in that direction. That is why countless trade routes were forged toward it over time. But to abruptly switch to another location means there are no roads at all."

After all, those trade routes had been paved with the blood of countless generations.

—"...In that case, do we have no choice but to hold the auction in Fengqing City?"-Mo Xi asked.

—"That will not be necessary,"-Zhou Hui replied:—"I know of another way."

—"Will Master take action?"-Mo Xi asked excitedly.

—"No, not me,"-Zhou Hui shook his head:—"Your Eldest Senior Brother."

—"Eh? Eldest Senior Brother?"-Mo Xi gasped.

—"Indeed. Go summon him here,"-Zhou Hui instructed.

Mo Xi, however, began dragging his feet, asking with a reluctant pout:

—"Master, are you letting him down already?"

[Eldest Senior Brother has only been hanging for five days this time!]
