# Chapter 203: Array Eye (第203章 阵眼)

Lin Qi soared along the path indicated by the array disc, blissfully unaware.

No, he wasn't entirely 'unaware.'

From the moment he departed the 'mountain gate,' Lin Qi had felt an intense surge of unease. Had his physical body still been intact, this sensation would have been called [creeping dread](# "Literal CN: 心惊肉跳 (xīnjīng-ròutiào) — heart trembling and flesh jumping; palpitations of dread"). He could distinctly forebode that something exceedingly terrible lay in wait for him.

To this, however, Lin Qi merely let out a disdainful scoff:

—"Mere [cheap tricks](# "Literal CN: 故弄玄虚 (gùnòng-xuánxū) — deliberately mystifying; blowing smoke")!"-Lin Qi sneered.

This was not reckless bravado on Lin Qi's part. Although a True Immortal's premonitions were generally razor-sharp, Lin Qi had ample reason for his skepticism.

After all, who was Lin Qi? He was a Demon-Suppressing Guardian Heavenly General!

As an exalted Heavenly General, he had seen more demons than ordinary mortals had seen people, navigated more ambushes than a normal person had walked paths, and witnessed countless horrors wrought by the demonic realm. Even when directly facing the Demon Sovereign and his billion-strong legion, the unease Lin Qi had experienced had never been as suffocating as this. Could a paltry lower-realm sect truly be more terrifying than the Demon Sovereign himself?

Furthermore, specializing in mental manipulation as he did, Lin Qi knew several methods for tampering with another's emotions, bluffing grandly to [subdue enemy forces without fighting](# "Literal CN: 不战而屈人之兵 (bù zhàn ér qū rén zhī bīng) — Sun Tzu's Art of War: subduing the enemy without battle").

Thus, Lin Qi concluded that his current unease was merely the influence of a similar psychological spell.

In reasoning this way, Lin Qi conveniently overlooked the fact that when he faced the Demon Sovereign's army, the Heavenly Court had also deployed a billion celestial soldiers; countless heavenly generals and soldiers like himself were present, meaning he had never actually been in grave mortal peril.

Rather, Lin Qi had enjoyed smooth sailing throughout his entire existence, never once teetering upon the knife-edge between life and death. Consequently, he had grown rather doubtful that an 'ill omen' would ever genuinely befall his own person.

And it was not as though he was truly fearless.

—"Hahaha!"-Lin Qi laughed boisterously, raising his voice to declare,-"The cardinal mistake of emotional manipulation is 'overplaying one's hand.' The very instant the target realizes their emotions have spiraled out of control and parted ways with common sense, the spell has already failed!"

Lin Qi failed to realize that his behavior was no different from an ordinary mortal whistling loudly in the dark while walking down a nighttime alley to bolster their own courage.

Or perhaps he had already realized it, but his spells were powerless to soothe his nerves—for this was no mere emotional sway, but an authentic, unvarnished premonition.

Lin Qi could only force down the ominous foreboding and seek to distract himself.

Holding the array disc, he compared it against the roadside signs:

—"Ah, this road leads toward Water Mirror Pavilion, while this one leads toward Buyan Palace... crystal clear..."-Lin Qi murmured.

This was yet another detail that unsettled him:

The directional signposts throughout the Heavenly Spirit Sect were far too clear.

Just like the layout map at the mountain gate, every crossroads within the Heavenly Spirit Sect featured signposts informing travelers of their precise location. Should anyone still manage to lose their way, they could summon the paper cranes perched among the roadside trees, which were more than happy to guide the way—though in the shadow realm of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, these paper cranes lay scattered beneath the boughs like withered leaves, appearing utterly harmless.

Yet this level of clarity struck Lin Qi as profoundly contrary to common sense.

After all, throughout the cultivation world and the immortal realm alike—be it inside sect secret realms or the Heavenly Court itself—interiors were invariably built with formations nested within formations and mechanisms layered upon mechanisms. The guiding philosophy seemed to be making everyone hopelessly lost; even disciples within the same sect rarely knew how to navigate another peak. This made the Heavenly Spirit Sect appear shockingly 'candid.'

Even though the array disc aligned perfectly with the signposts, it still left Lin Qi muttering with suspicion.

—"The first array eye is Water Mirror Pavilion..."-Lin Qi looked toward the direction indicated by the disc and the signs to confirm.

The thoroughfare leading to Water Mirror Pavilion was wide, level, and unobstructed.

All roads throughout the Heavenly Spirit Sect were designed this way.

Even though immortal cultivators vastly preferred sword-kinesis flight, the terrestrial thoroughfares were wide enough for six horse carriages to travel abreast. Furthermore, Water Mirror Pavilion had genuine need for such capacity.

Although Water Mirror Pavilion had once lacked popularity in the old Heavenly Spirit Sect, information had become synonymous with wealth ever since the opening of the Immortals' Bazaar. Many merchants felt that even Kunpeng shuttling once a day across the Central Domain was too sluggish. Consequently, they required water mirrors to negotiate business transactions with far-flung partners. Mo Xi had secured Zhou Hui's permission to open Water Mirror Pavilion to the public, converting the facility into a bustling communications center.

Thus, in the present Heavenly Spirit Sect, one could see crowds of hurried merchants traveling between the market or the sect interior and Water Mirror Pavilion at any hour of the day, making it one of the liveliest arteries in the sect.

Naturally, none of this bustling activity could be seen within the 'mirror side' of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

To Lin Qi, the desolate emptiness felt distinctly unnerving.

—"Looks like quite a distance. Hmph, don't think you can scare me off with mere distance,"-Lin Qi talked to himself, yet abruptly wheeled around and flew toward Buyan Palace instead.

This was by no means retreat; it was "necessary prudence."

After all, Buyan Palace was the sole location within the Heavenly Spirit Sect with which Lin Qi possessed any familiarity.

Over recent days, both Ren Ran and Guan Xinguang had cowered aboard the grand treasure ship out of terror toward the Untiring Immortal Venerable, refusing to set foot outside. This had left Lin Qi with no opportunity to properly survey his surroundings until Guan Xinguang sat for the examination.

Now, before causing trouble, he naturally needed to assess Buyan Palace to confirm whether the 'outer surface' and the 'mirror side' matched with complete fidelity, lest unexpected discrepancies leave him helpless to react.

On the other hand, the Academic Hall within Buyan Palace was the 'core' among the seven array eyes, and the sole location from which one could safely depart the mountain-protecting grand array without performing additional tasks (theoretically).

Although Lin Qi refused to admit that he had been easily spooked into abandoning his schemes and fleeing, securing his sole escape route before executing his plan was elementary common sense.

It had to be said that Lin Qi was an exceedingly prudent immortal.

This was precisely the professional caliber of an illustrious, rising star Heavenly General.

Praising himself in his thoughts, Lin Qi glided lithely toward Buyan Palace at a velocity so rapid it made one wonder if he was fleeing for his life.

Fortunately, as the place where Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples attended lectures and sat for exams, Buyan Palace was not far from the mountain gate.

Flying for merely a few minutes, Lin Qi spotted the exquisite flying eaves and interlocking dougong brackets of Buyan Palace in the distance.

A stream of profanity slipped past Lin Qi's lips:

—"Holy f*ck! What kind of demonic monstrosity is that?!"-Lin Qi blurted in sheer shock.

It was no monstrosity. It was a dragon.

Yes, a dragon.

A silvery-white True Dragon was coiled languidly atop Buyan Palace. It was remarkably slender, graceful, and stunningly gorgeous—especially under the cool moonlight, where every single one of its pristine scales shimmered with a lunar radiance. It was so breathtaking that one instinctively held one's breath, terrified of waking the sublime creature.

After his startled yelp, Lin Qi indeed held his breath immediately, fixing a sharp gaze upon the massive dragon head resting atop the apex of Buyan Palace.

Only upon confirming that the dragon was fast asleep did Lin Qi let out a breath of relief.

[Damn it all, the Heavenly Spirit Sect actually harbors a dragon—and a True Dragon at that!](# "Lin Qi's mental monologue") Lin Qi dared not make a sound aloud this time, complaining bitterly in his mind: [Did that brain-dead woman invite me to this realm purely to play a sick joke on me?!]

This was the first time Lin Qi had begun questioning Ren Ran's true motives.

Had Ren Ran not consistently demonstrated utter stupidity, Lin Qi would have genuinely suspected she bore a grudge against him and had dug an elaborate pitfall just to watch him make a fool of himself.

After all, True Dragons occupied an extraordinarily unique standing in the Heavenly Court.

Disregard all the myths claiming the dragon clan merely pulled carriages for immortals; those were invariably branch offshoots of the clan, creatures closer to spirit beasts or demon beasts. The only beings permitted to use True Dragons to draw their divine chariots were entities possessing true godhood, and even then, only those few closest to the Primordial Ancestors. Yet to pull chariots for those exalted few, such True Dragons held official Heavenly Court civil service appointments ranked at the very pinnacle, far beyond Lin Qi's meager rank.

Naturally, at that echelon, a True Dragon's combat prowess was not something an ordinary Heavenly General like Lin Qi could dare provoke; a single healthy, adult True Dragon possessed sufficient might to obliterate an entire legion of Lin Qis into fine ash.

—"Wait a moment... how did a True Dragon squeeze itself into this mortal realm?"-Lin Qi wondered in bewilderment.

Even his own True Immortal physical vessel had been barred from descending.

Lin Qi scrutinized the True Dragon several more times, discovering that it appeared incomplete—merely a 'portion' of a True Dragon. Much like how Lin Qi had severed his soul from his mortal shell, a piece of this True Dragon had been separated from its whole.

—"Still formidable nonetheless... could it be a remnant from the ancient God-Demon War?"-Lin Qi stared tensely at the dragon. He desperately wanted to gauge its precise 'scale,' yet the dragon clan possessed uncanny sensory acuity; sweeping his divine sense across it recklessly would easily rouse it from slumber. Lin Qi could only abandon the attempt with reluctance, murmuring in speculation,-"I wonder how much power it truly wields... it might very well represent the ceiling of this mortal realm."

On this point, the blame genuinely rested on Ren Ran.

Ren Ran was chronically steeped in arrogance. She operated on the premise that the Heavenly Court was the ultimate answer to all problems; mortal beings were supposedly helpless before celestial soldiers and generals, requiring zero thought. She hadn't even bothered to research the Untiring Immortal Venerable she intended to suppress, let alone Untiring's disciples.

Consequently, Ren Ran had no inkling (or perhaps knew but couldn't care less) that Untiring's disciples included a dragon, making it impossible for her to pass such intelligence to Lin Qi.

Without question, Ren Ran was a teammate so pigheaded as to redefine incompetence—the quintessential nightmare for any military commander.

Whether Lin Qi would recognize this in the near future or remain oblivious forever was anyone's guess.

At this moment, however, Lin Qi merely harbored mild complaints toward Ren Ran. He had yet to grasp what he was truly up against, even nursing suicidal ambitions toward this celestial dragon:

—"Regardless, judging by its position, it must be the sect's guardian beast. If I dismantle the mountain-protecting grand array, I might even have a chance to tame it,"-Lin Qi muttered under his breath.

With this grand ambition, Lin Qi felt an even stronger urge to investigate Buyan Palace's critical array eye.

Yet he did not lose his head.

To avoid rousing the dragon, Lin Qi suppressed his aura to the absolute minimum, rendering himself virtually translucent. Even the array disc he 'held' faded into invisibility. In this state, drifting like an imperceptible wisp of breeze, he cautiously glided toward the Academic Hall situated at the very pinnacle of Buyan Palace.

According to the array disc, the key array eye for escaping the mountain-protecting grand array lay right there.

Lin Qi's extreme caution yielded dividends; he was adept at concealing his presence, particularly in the face of mortal danger.

Thus, he approached Buyan Palace with ease, and even upon drawing exceedingly close, he did not disturb the dragon's slumber.

Yet Lin Qi failed to find the array eye.

More accurately, before Lin Qi could step inside Buyan Palace's Academic Hall, his attention was completely seized by the figure resting upon the terrace outside.

It was a beauty.

A peerless, breathtaking beauty.

This peerless beauty was also fast asleep, reclining languidly against the massive dragon claw resting upon the terrace. His head was tilted slightly to the side, silky raven hair spilling across the steps to reveal a slender, snow-white neck, appearing utterly defenseless.

Yet upon laying eyes on this beauty, Lin Qi wore an expression every bit as stunned as when he had first seen the dragon.

—"This is..."-Lin Qi gasped, his eyes widening in disbelief.

Lin Qi stared with wide eyes—though he currently possessed no physical eyes. This reaction was clearly not born of lust for the beauty's countenance, but because he recognized him!

—"Is it him? It must be him, right?"-Lin Qi muttered uncertainly.

Yet his uncertainty did not linger, for a beauty of this caliber could hardly be matched across three thousand mortal realms; to have seen him once was to remember him forever, rendering misidentification impossible.

—"But shouldn't he be in the Bureau of Heavenly Dao...? No, if he were still in the Bureau of Heavenly Dao, how could there have been zero word of him for so long?"-Lin Qi murmured to himself.

To be fair, Lin Qi was not personally acquainted with this beauty, not even knowing his name.

The sole occasion he had laid eyes on him was during the Heavenly Court's Grand Pill-Tasting Assembly.

—The Grand Pill-Tasting Assembly was one of the grand millennial galas of the Heavenly Court (measured in celestial time). On that occasion, the celestial alchemy chambers opened their doors, allowing True Immortals to sample the latest heavenly elixirs—a rare, coveted employee benefit that brought together every officially appointed True Immortal in the Heavenly Court.

With his modest seniority, Lin Qi had only attended a handful of times.

The assembly that had left the deepest impression on him was the one where he had caught sight of this beauty.

It was not that Lin Qi harbored lecherous designs, but that during that year's assembly, everyone had been buzzing about the mysterious new beauty who had arrived at the Bureau of Heavenly Dao. Whispers claimed he hailed from an extraordinary background (though none could pinpoint where) and was an unprecedented genius, perhaps destined to be parachuted straight into the supreme post of Bureau Chief.

Yet rumors were merely rumors. After that day, this beauty seemed to have dissolved into thin air within the immortal realm; not only was he never seen in the Heavenly Court again, but even the Bureau of Heavenly Dao ceased all mention of his name.

Lin Qi had never imagined he would cross paths with him in this lower world.

A creeping sensation washed over him, as though he had stumbled into a secret he was never meant to know:

—"How did he get banished down here...? Wait, now that I think about it, didn't someone's intelligence report mention that one of Untiring's disciples was a 'Wenqu Star'? Could that 'Apostate Wenqu Star' be him?!"-Lin Qi's mind raced.

The Bureau of Heavenly Dao was the paramount department responsible for weaving the Heavenly Dao across all realms, and its officials were renowned for peerless divination and celestial arithmetic. In terms of understanding the cosmos, calling them 'omniscient' was hardly an exaggeration; they thoroughly deserved the moniker of 'Wenqu.'

Nay, given the extraordinary status he had held even within the Bureau of Heavenly Dao, labeling him merely a 'Wenqu Star' was almost an insult.

Lin Qi's mind was a swirling vortex of question marks:

[What in the world is the Bureau of Heavenly Dao scheming? No, wait a minute—didn't they say he fell to demonic corruption? Why is he still here? But there shouldn't be another Wenqu Star, right? Or is he like this dragon, merely a severed fragment of the Wenqu Star?](# "Lin Qi's mental monologue")

Lin Qi scrutinized the sleeping countenance in deep contemplation.

Perhaps his gaze was far too brazen; the beauty let out a soft murmur, eyelashes fluttering like delicate cicada wings.

Yet alongside this tiny stir, the breathing of the colossal dragon coiled around Buyan Palace grew noticeably heavier, and an immense, crushing draconic pressure instantly enveloped Lin Qi.

Although the beast had clearly not awakened and bore no deliberate malice, it was nonetheless the authentic draconic pressure of a True Dragon, nearly squashing Lin Qi flat as a sheet of paper on the spot.

Only then did Lin Qi notice that the beauty cradled in the dragon's claw was no captured princess; he looked like the True Dragon's master!

Terrified, Lin Qi snapped his gaze away and retreated at top speed, flying far into the distance.

Only when the beauty was completely lost to sight did the crushing draconic pressure dissipate.

Lin Qi felt as though his flickering flame of soul light was on the verge of snuffing out.

—"A Wenqu Star, a True Dragon, a Dao Bone, and a sect master who possesses true godhood... what kind of freak sect is this Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"-Lin Qi belatedly realized he had kicked an impossibly colossal hornet's nest.

Yet he had no way out.

Lin Qi had originally presumed the key array eye at Buyan Palace was a clean escape route—naturally, he hadn't assumed a key array eye would be completely unguarded; ordinary mountain arrays always had guardians, but ordinary guardians were powerless against Lin Qi. Who could have fathomed that the Heavenly Spirit Sect's guardians would be a True Dragon and a Wenqu Star?!

Lin Qi hovered above the thoroughfare for a long while, lacking the courage to go back and probe further.

The prudent Heavenly General did not believe his current diminished strength was capable of testing entities of that caliber.

Hesitating, he flew backward. Yet when he reached the three-way intersection connecting Water Mirror Pavilion and Buyan Palace, Lin Qi discovered to his horror that the road he had arrived on had vanished!

Or rather, the mountain gate had disappeared.

A shroud of pitch-black mist concealed the road leading to the mountain gate. The outer perimeter of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had grown hazy and distorted, as if swallowed whole by something colossal.

—"...Could the ill omen have been pointing to this?"-Lin Qi gasped softly.

He stared at the enveloping black mist for a long while, then looked toward Buyan Palace coiled by the dragon.

Suddenly, he let out two boisterous chuckles:

—"Hahaha! But thinking this will make me cower is utter foolishness! No actual threat has materialized, which means the master of this realm has either failed to detect me or dares not face me in open combat! What do I have to fear?! Sealing my escape route simply means that even if I wished to spare the Heavenly Spirit Sect, it is no longer an option! Just watch how I dismantle this mountain-protecting grand array!"-Lin Qi proclaimed boldly.

With that, Lin Qi hesitated no longer, turning toward Water Mirror Pavilion without looking back.

He simply refused to believe it!

Could this Heavenly Spirit Sect truly harbor anything more terrifying than a True Dragon and a Wenqu Star?!

***

Or perhaps it truly did.

What kind of unholy [blessed land](# "Literal CN: 风水宝地 (fēngshuǐ bǎodì) — geomantic treasure trove; blessed feng shui location") was this cursed Heavenly Spirit Sect?!

Lin Qi drifted in utter exhaustion, his soul letting out a wail for what felt like the hundredth time.

Surrounding him were endless, boundless mirrors.

These mirrors were fashioned from every conceivable material: some were colored glaze, some were liquid water, some were carved of ice crystals, and some were formed purely of warped, shimmering air. Yet their sole shared trait was that every single one could reflect a figure.

Frankly, in the 'mirror side,' this trait alone was deeply eerie.

Consider this: when one stepped into Water Mirror Pavilion, one saw countless mirrors reflecting countless figures, yet in reality there wasn't a living soul in sight. Walking through the hall, only one's own echoing footsteps resounded. Even without suspicious phenomena, the scene exerted immense psychological terror.

Furthermore, it was not as though this place was devoid of "suspicious phenomena."

At the very least, Lin Qi could sense the dense yin energy here threatening to assimilate what little celestial qi remained within his soul.

Naturally, the glorious, valiant Heavenly General did not fear underworld trickery; moreover, since he currently lacked a human form, reflections in mirrors could hardly frighten him.

At the time, Lin Qi had sneered disdainfully and flown into Water Mirror Pavilion without care.

And then he had gotten hopelessly lost inside for several shichen... No, wait, perhaps it hadn't been several shichen; judging by the array disc, the actual time he had spent inside was brief. Yet as a True Immortal, he had not fully surrendered his sensory perception, so it subjectively felt like several shichen. If he lingered longer, the perceived passage of time would stretch even further—drifting for days, years, or even centuries was entirely plausible.

And this perceptual time distortion was merely a minor annoyance; the true nightmare was spatial chaos.

Countless mirrors replicated countless spatial dimensions, and to an energy entity like an evil spirit or ghost, these dimensions were not illusory, but tangibly real.

Consequently, whether according to Lin Qi's divine sense or the array disc's display, the array eye should have been right at his fingertips, yet he couldn't find a single trace of it.

Those mirror dimensions not only scrambled the true location of the array eye, but the reflections they cast were deceitful.

Lin Qi tried observing the mirrors, only to see a feeble cluster of flame drifting amidst layers of reflections. Yet the instant he tried looking away, he caught fleeting glimpses of figures within the glass: sometimes an adolescent youth in Heavenly Spirit Sect robes, sometimes a young maiden, and sometimes his own true form.

Amidst such unsettling illusions, locating anything useful was an exercise in futility.

—"This is definitely not mortal yin energy,"-Lin Qi concluded grimly.

He was right.

This was Mo Xi's personal domain, and Mo Xi was a yin spirit born from an ancient immortal's corpse. At his core, he was an entity of the exact same tier as Lin Qi. While Mo Xi could never defeat Lin Qi in a direct contest of brute force, finding an item belonging to the Heavenly Spirit Sect inside the personal domain of the cunning Mo Xi was anything but simple.

—"Damn it! If my physical body were here, I would smash this wretched place to pieces!"-Lin Qi seethed with unprecedented longing for his true vessel.

He even began to regret being overly finicky about demanding a 'perfect vessel.' Had he known, he would have simply lowered his cultivation and descended directly; he certainly wouldn't be as bound and crippled as he was now.

Gazing into the mirrors, Lin Qi thought thus, retaining a blind confidence in his own might.

—"Wait, what is this?"-Lin Qi noticed a discordant impurity within his golden radiance.

Staring at it for a moment, he remembered: this was the flame he had harvested when he stoked the lingering obsession of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's eldest disciple at the 'mountain gate.'

A flash of inspiration struck Lin Qi:

—"As the eldest disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, no matter how marginalized he is, he must possess some clues, right?"-Lin Qi reasoned.

He released that tiny wisp of flame.

Sure enough, the flame wavered in confusion midair before drifting toward a specific mirror.

Landing upon the glass, the flame coalesced into the phantom of a handsome young man. The moment this phantom appeared, the attractive youth who had been flickering on the periphery of Lin Qi's vision stepped squarely into the mirror's reflection, scowling with deep disgust:

—"Eldest Senior Brother, what did you come to my place for?"-Mo Xi asked in annoyance.

Witnessing this, Lin Qi felt even more validated in his deductions regarding Yuwen Xuan.

The young man—or rather, the eldest disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Yuwen Xuan—paid no heed to his junior brother's sour face, slinging an arm affectionately around his shoulders:

—"I need a favor from my beloved Little Junior Brother. I heard you acquired quite a few good things at the Immortals' Bazaar. I want some to marinate... cough, maintain a treasure,"-Yuwen Xuan wheedled.

—"..."-Little Junior Brother eyed his Eldest Senior Brother sideways, thoroughly unwilling.

—"Those ingredients won't go to waste! I just need to marinate it... cough, I mean soak it, just enough to absorb the flavor, and this treasure will become crystal clear, able to see through falsehood and reality,"-Yuwen Xuan persuaded him earnestly,-"And I'm not doing this for myself! I'm planning to present it to Master for dinner!"

—"Fine, then..."-Little Junior Brother nodded reluctantly.

The two in the mirror bustled about, fetching a massive vat resembling a fish tank and mixing a concoction within.

—"What is this? Looks like an echo of a past conversation?"-Lin Qi pondered.

He overlooked the bizarre word choices in Yuwen Xuan's dialogue, chalking them up to verbal slips while coaxing his junior brother. Instead, the 'treasure maintenance' caught Lin Qi's full attention.

'See through falsehood and reality'—wasn't that precisely what Lin Qi needed right now?!

Lin Qi grasped the method to crack this array eye!

Thus, the moment the two in the mirror finished preparing the contents of the massive vat, Lin Qi hesitated not for a second, diving straight into the vat with all his might!

Following his plunge, Lin Qi's sense of time blurred. He seemed to spend an eternity within, yet it also felt like a mere heartbeat. Perhaps this was the marinating—no, soaking duration Yuwen Xuan had spoken of. Yet it certainly did him no harm; on the contrary, Lin Qi felt enveloped in exceptionally dense spiritual energy. All the power he had lost to various mishaps surged back into his soul, and he began glowing with dazzling golden radiance once more, as if he had just enjoyed a restorative hot spring therapy.

Following this radiance-restoring therapy, the paste inside the vat began swirling, coalescing into a vortex. Simultaneously, the array disc responded vigorously to this vortex.

—"Hahaha, I found it! The array eye!"-Lin Qi laughed triumphantly.

Without mercy, he unleashed an assault upon the vortex. The vortex and the surrounding space fractured instantly, forming an arcane passage that evidently led to the next array eye.

And shattering alongside this vortex were the countless mirrors.

Lin Qi was delighted to hear the sound of those tormenting mirrors shattering. As he departed through the spatial fissure, he specifically glanced backward.

He saw countless mirror shards floating in midair, each reflecting an image of 'Little Junior Brother.'

These myriad reflections of 'Little Junior Brother' were watching him with chilling, sinister smiles. Their lips parted, yet no sound emerged.

Only the shape of their lips silently mouthed a word:

—"Fool."
