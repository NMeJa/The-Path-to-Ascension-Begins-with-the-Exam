# Chapter 192: Path of Retreat

Su Xin's feelings were of little consequence.

Since even the cultivation second-generation trust-fund scions were brimming with enthusiasm over the qualification examination, to say nothing of other cultivators.

In Zhou Hui's view, no matter where in the cosmos humans resided, the vast majority yearned for their 'selves' to be acknowledged by others. To that end, people spared no effort in sorting themselves into categories, slapping on labels, assigning numerical scores, and even tagging themselves with a price.

This was precisely why various 'IQ tests' and 'personality quizzes' proved so wildly popular, and why the annual [Gaokao essay prompt](# "Literal CN: 高考作文 (gāokǎo zuòwén) — National College Entrance Examination essay prompt, an annual cultural phenomenon and trending topic in China") invariably became a trending viral topic—after all, when you couldn't do math, you just couldn't do math, but the essay was something that everyone (or so they believed) could write.

So long as they were not genuinely placed upon an auction block, people hardly minded pinning an inflated price tag onto themselves.

Yet if the scores of such tests truly had the power to dictate one's future, then all that joy would instantly transmute into agony.

A ten-thousandfold agony.

At least, such was the case for the artifact refiners of the Department of Works.

—"Why do we have to take the Heavenly Spirit Sect's qualification exam?"-an artifact refiner from the Department of Works grumbled to his companions via divine sense.-"By conventional practice, shouldn't our Heavenly Dao Alliance be the ones conducting qualification certifications for cultivators under heaven?"

—"There's no helping it—who told our Chief Director to sign us up for the exam?"-another artifact refiner replied.

The artifact refiners covertly stole a glance at their superior, whose complexion had been dreadful for days on end.

That superior was clutching a copy of *Heavenly Spirit Sect Artifact Workshop Product Catalog*—an item that had recently completely sold out of print because artifact refiners were scrambling to scrutinize the Heavenly Spirit Sect's finished goods to deduce the likely contents of the qualification exam. Whether anyone actually deduced the examiners' pedagogical tendencies was anyone's guess, but what was certain was that the product order queue for the Heavenly Spirit Sect's artifact workshops had already been booked solid fifty years into the future—though that was a story for later. At this very moment, Guan Xinguang was also clutching a copy of the catalog; yet though his eyes were glued to the parchment, his gaze was utterly vacant and his face was ashen-black, bearing the ghastly look of a scholar who had failed the imperial examinations ten years in a row.

Evidently, this Chief Director was not particularly keen on taking an exam either.

—"...It's likely on account of the large-scale teleportation array,"-someone analyzed.-"Who would have thought that the Untiring Immortal Venerable would actually reconstruct large-scale teleportation arrays? You should all know what a colossal disruption this will bring to existing transit networks! If they erect teleportation arrays across the Five Domains and Six Abysses, the fractured land will virtually be stitched back together into a unified whole, completely upending the geopolitical landscape of the entire cultivation realm. If we do nothing, I fear the Heavenly Spirit Sect will become the center of the world going forward, while the Heavenly Dao Alliance completely loses its voice. It's likely for this very reason that the Chief Director wants to participate in the qualification exam—to prove his capabilities and muscle into the construction of the large-scale teleportation array."

—"Even so, there's no need for him to personally take an exam, is there?"-someone still grumbled in dissatisfaction.-"Don't the Mei-Zhu couple share an excellent relationship with the Untiring Immortal Venerable? Wouldn't it suffice to have them figure out a way to secure our share of the pie directly from the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

[—"What tier of formation do you think a large-scale teleportation array is? It would be a miracle if they ever made something like that public."](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Hmph, isn't the Untiring Immortal Venerable famous for his motto of '[education without discrimination](# "Literal CN: 有教无类 (yǒu jiào wú lèi) — Confucius' famous pedagogical maxim: teaching everyone regardless of social status or background")'? He even opened access to the Ascension Stairway—why couldn't he do the same for the teleportation array? At the end of the day, it's just fake generosity."](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Don't speak so soon! Who knows, maybe the Untiring Immortal Venerable truly is that generous! But even if the Mei-Zhu couple strike a bargain with the Untiring Immortal Venerable, what does that have to do with our Department of Works?"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"That's true too. Starting from last year, what with auctions and the Immortal Arts Assembly, the Heavenly Spirit Sect has collaborated heavily with the Mei-Zhu faction. I hear the Central Domain branch of the Heavenly Dao Alliance has [stuffed its pockets to bursting](# "Literal CN: 吃撑了 (chī chēng le) — ate until bursting; slang for making an obscene fortune / reaping immense profit")! Yet they didn't toss a single drop of grease to our department."](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Ah, no wonder that bastard recently started strutting around like a peacock! Back then, he pulled every string and called in every favor trying to get transferred back from the Central Domain, but recently he hasn't uttered a single peep about it. Instead, it looks like he looted some ancient secret realm—his entire wardrobe of magical treasures has been upgraded from head to toe!"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Is the Central Domain really that profitable?"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Just take a look at the sales figures of Immortal Chess and you'll know."](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

...

The artifact refiners carried on their discussion.

Even though they conversed through divine sense without uttering a single audible sound, the atmosphere grew noticeably heated.

For the sake of wealth, the repulsion many felt toward sitting for the qualification exam was finally no longer as stubborn as it had been at the start; some even cast a few genuinely earnest glances at the artifact-refining manuals clutched in their hands.

Regrettably, the stimulation was far from sufficient.

Or rather, for the artifact refiners of the Department of Works, no amount of stimulation could ever be sufficient.

Only those who had not yet surrendered hope would yearn madly for change. The overwhelming majority, once having resigned themselves to their lot in life and acquiesced to their predetermined future, became [frogs boiled in lukewarm water](# "Literal CN: 温水中的青蛙 (wēnshuǐ zhōng de qīngwā) — the boiling frog syndrome; complacent people failing to react to slow, deadly changes"), petrified of any alteration to their environment.

The craftsmen of the cultivation realm were particularly prone to this.

By virtue of walking the path of cultivation, they had attained lifespans far exceeding those of mortals; yet the nature of their labor condemned them to repeat the exact same mechanical chores across decades and centuries. Among them, ninety-nine point nine nine nine... percent would never achieve the threshold of [Entering the Dao through Craftsmanship](# "Literal CN: 以器入道 (yǐ qì rù dào) — attaining enlightenment and reaching the Dao through mastery of artifact crafting"). It merely bred an insidious familiarity with a tepid routine, until at last they sank into the mire, thoroughly forfeiting their future.

Of course, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's qualification exam was hardly so transcendent a cause, so the cultivators gave up even faster.

Even if the lure of wealth had sparked a fleeting shred of earnestness, the moment they glanced over the exam syllabus—which the Heavenly Spirit Sect had published in the newspapers for the benefit of candidates—endless grumbling erupted anew:

[—"Isn't the syllabus they designated far too broad, and far too elementary?"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Exactly! Didn't they say this was for constructing a large-scale teleportation array? Then they ought to be testing the refining and processing of high-grade materials!"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Perhaps they simply don't want to leak the true crafting techniques of teleportation arrays?"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Even so, there's way too much foundational trivia! Stuff like 'Cauldron Care and Furnace Maintenance' shouldn't even be tested in the first place!"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"Pfft, isn't it just because you don't know how to do it?"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

[—"As if you know how? Never mind the Department of Works, even the cauldrons in my personal clan have specialized immortal attendants to maintain them!"](# "Speaker: Department of Works Artifact Refiner")

...

Going on like this, it seemed they wouldn't have the slightest inclination to do any preparation before the qualification exam.

But none of that mattered. In truth, the person least willing to sit for this exam was none other than the Chief Director of the Department of Works, Guan Xinguang.

Guan Xinguang had been coerced.

His reason for participating in the examination had absolutely nothing to do with teleportation array blueprints, Department of Works welfare perks, internal power struggles within the Heavenly Dao Alliance, or any such grand narratives. It was purely, unequivocally forced.

Forced by two deities.

—"No! Absolutely not!"-back when he first heard Goddess Ren Ran's demand, Guan Xinguang had refused hysterically.-"I've already gravely offended the Untiring Immortal Venerable over extracting that spine! If I rashly present myself before him—no, the moment I leave the Heavenly Dao Alliance, he might slaughter me!"

—"You're overthinking it,"-the goddess said, utterly dismissive of Guan Xinguang's terror.-"It was merely the spine of a low-tier cultivator with pathetic aptitudes."

—"Yet he still took him in as a disciple!"-Guan Xinguang argued.

[—"Perhaps out of momentary pity?"](# "Speaker: Goddess Ren Ran")

Goddess Ren Ran remained unconvinced.

Her reaction was also the reaction of the vast majority of people across the cultivation realm.

Most cultivators would never believe that someone would antagonize a Mahayana titan over the unjust treatment suffered by a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator—even if that someone was a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse.

Guan Xinguang, however, was dead certain:
—"Even if it was mere pity, he will definitely kill me! He will! Just think of what he did to the Palace of Carefree Wandering! He is a madman!"

As Guan Xinguang said this, he shivered uncontrollably.

There was no helping it. Back when Zhou Hui upended Carefree Palace's auction, Guan Xinguang had been present on the scene. And most unfortunately, because he shared certain commercial dealings with Carefree Palace's stewards at the time, he had been seated inside Carefree Palace's exclusive VIP box. Consequently, when Zhou Hui obliterated the auction hall, Guan Xinguang's physical body was pulverized directly into mincemeat; his soul had only been preserved thanks to the intervention of the Mei-Zhu couple.

Yet compared to the 'minor inconvenience' of having his physical body destroyed, what lingered far more indelibly in Guan Xinguang's memory was how Zhou Hui had flown into a rage in the first place.

Although the outside world possessed no fewer than nine hundred and ninety-nine versions of the grudge between the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Palace of Carefree Wandering—ranging from rumors that Untiring's first love had been refined into a cultivation furnace by Carefree Palace, to tales that Untiring himself had been abducted and trafficked by Carefree Palace in his youth, each rendition wilder than the last—Guan Xinguang knew the truth: the reason Zhou Hui had flown into a fury was exceedingly 'ordinary,' yet utterly mind-boggling.

The object of his wrath had been a Golden Core stage 'furnace.'

The girl wasn't even the auction's main event; she was merely a complimentary gift. Furthermore, she had raised no objections herself. She had stepped onto the auction stage beaming with delight, had been bundled as a free gift beaming with delight, had gained a Void Refinement master beaming with delight, had thrown herself into his arms beaming with delight, and was then gifted away by her new master to one of his Golden Core disciples beaming with delight.

Oh, when being gifted away to a subordinate, that furnace had seemed slightly less delighted.

After all, by following a high-stage cultivator, even a furnace could glean some scraps of benefit; but if her master was merely a peer at the same cultivation stage, she would be reduced to nothing more than a consumable husk to be plundered dry.

Yet who cared about the displeasure of a furnace?

Since she had willingly come out to sell herself, whatever fate befell her was entirely her own doing!

The sole problem was: the Untiring Immortal Venerable cared.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable had stepped forward and demanded: Who granted the right to barter and sell fellow kin like cattle? Who granted the right to bestow human beings like commodities on a whim? Who granted the right to refine living, breathing souls into cultivation furnaces?

Everyone present thought the Untiring Immortal Venerable was out of his mind.

Still, in deference to his monstrous cultivation, everyone offered an avalanche of sweet-talk in an attempt to pacify the Untiring Immortal Venerable's fury; even the furnace stammered timidly that she was entirely willing.

Regrettably, the Untiring Immortal Venerable had not come in search of answers. He had come to pass judgment.

After uttering inquiries that received no satisfactory answers, the Untiring Immortal Venerable overturned the auction stage with a single strike, shredded Carefree Palace's VIP boxes with a second strike, and crushed the entire venue into pulverized dust with a third strike.

Before being shredded into mincemeat, Guan Xinguang's final gaze had locked onto Untiring.

At that moment, the Immortal Venerable stood amidst roaring wind and thunder. He held his head high, gazing down upon everything with cold, silver-gray eyes—looking more merciless than the Heavenly Dao, and more ruthless than heavenly tribulation lightning.

He believed no one would ever dare forget that spectacle—if they survived, that is.

Guan Xinguang had survived. He could never forget that scene. Pulverized along with his flesh were all his petty schemes; not only did he not dare seek vengeance, he even severed all ties with the Palace of Carefree Wandering for a time.

Alas, Guan Xinguang's dread did not last very long.

Just as all cultivators knew full well that the Heavenly Dao hung overhead yet still committed transgressions; just as they knew their actions would invite heavier tribulation lightning yet could never sever their greed; just as they knew their conduct might bar them from ascension or bottlenecks yet stubbornly persisted on their path—Guan Xinguang, in the process of reconstructing his physical body, also reconstructed thoughts that should never have been reconstructed.

Thus, when he received the Celestial Edict and learned that a low-aptitude disciple in a distant branch clan beneath his lineage actually possessed a Dao Bone, greed that should never have arisen bloomed once more.

That was a 'Dao Bone'!

Word had it that Sky City's successful forging back in the day had relied on a Dao Bone as its core material!

As an artifact refiner, as a Mahayana cultivator, how could he bear to let it slip through his fingers?

At the end of the day, it was merely a Foundation Establishment cultivator.

Even the Heavenly Dao would surely agree that a Mahayana cultivator like him was far more important to this world, wouldn't it?

So Guan Xinguang reasoned. Yet in his heart, a faint unease gnawed at him. He didn't even understand why he felt uneasy—after all, he had done similar things for thousands of years. Thus, he had not dared to face the owner of the Dao Bone in person, commanding his subordinates to carry it out instead.

Even so, he had not escaped fate.

At the exact moment he received that worthless, ordinary spine, Guan Xinguang received news that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had retrieved the 'scrap' whose bone had been extracted. Soon after, he heard that the 'scrap' had been taken in as the Immortal Venerable's disciple.

The instant he learned of that news, Guan Xinguang felt those terrifying silver-gray eyes from his memory gazing right at him.

Nowhere to run.

—"He will definitely kill me! What does a Mahayana matter? Hasn't a Tribulation Transcendence titan from Carefree Palace already been slain by him?! Right now, Carefree Palace is thrown into utter chaos—who knows if the other one was slaughtered by him as well!"-Guan Xinguang said incoherently, pouring out the dread he had harbored for over a hundred years. Regarding the mysterious disappearance of a certain someone in Carefree Palace, he held the exact same view as Goddess Ren Ran.-"Right now, he's just waiting for the right moment to kill me! The only reason he hasn't struck yet is because he lacks 'evidence.' But the moment I appear before him, he'll know I was behind it all! He will definitely kill me!"

He looked imploringly toward Goddess Ren Ran:
—"Goddess, you don't necessarily need me, do you? If the goal is merely to use the qualification exam to get close to Guan Ye and seize the Dao Bone, anyone else can do it! Our Department of Works lacks anything but artifact refiners—plenty of them possess decent craft! I'll just order all of them to take the qualification exam! I beg of you, don't make me leave the Heavenly Dao Alliance."

—"Utterly spineless,"-Goddess Ren Ran grumbled in disgust.

Yet just as Guan Xinguang had pointed out, this task did not strictly require the Chief Director of the Department of Works. Seeing his half-crazed state and unwilling to push him over the edge, Goddess Ren Ran was prepared to let him off the hook.

Lin Qi, however, chimed in:
—"No. Make him take it."

—"Lord Heavenly General?"-Ren Ran asked.

—"The more certain he is that he will be slain, the more it means that by sending him to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he can draw our target straight out, no? That saves us plenty of trouble,"-Lin Qi sneered.-"Besides, he's merely a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, yet you all act as if the Celestial Emperor himself has descended. It makes me rather curious about this so-called Un-Grinding fellow."

—"It's Untiring,"-Goddess Ren Ran corrected.

She naturally would not oppose the Lord Heavenly General's request. Thus, Ren Ran looked down at Guan Xinguang and let out a cold snort:
—"Whining about being killed, being killed, being killed—are you looking down on me? Since I'm having you participate, I will naturally guarantee your life."

—"But..."-Guan Xinguang stammered, his eyes darting around.

He truly had no faith in Goddess Ren Ran. After all, the number of Tribulation Transcendence powerhouses Zhou Hui had slain was hardly limited to one.

—"I am no ordinary Tribulation Transcendence cultivator! I am a True Immortal!"-Ren Ran saw through Guan Xinguang's petty thoughts and snapped in irritation.-"Even if my power is bound at present, you think I cannot beat Untiring?!"

—"That wasn't what I meant..."-Guan Xinguang replied insincerely.

Goddess Ren Ran glared at him:
—"Then make your choice. Do you wish to be slain by me right now, or head to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to be slain by Zhou Hui?!"

[Can I choose neither?]

The corners of Guan Xinguang's mouth twitched.

—"Ren Ran, don't be so harsh,"-Lin Qi spoke up. This time, he switched to an 'external broadcast' voice, speaking gently to Guan Xinguang:-"Esteemed Artifact Refiner, it is only natural that you harbor misgivings when facing one of the strongest beings in this realm. How about this: I shall grant you an additional divine blessing."

With those words, the hairpin atop Ren Ran's head dissolved into golden radiance and descended upon Guan Xinguang.

Guan Xinguang's spirit instantly jolted.

He felt his body enveloped in a divine blessing—a barrier that neither the Heavenly Dao nor tribulation lightning could breach. Boundless courage surged within his chest; he felt capable of withstanding even a True Immortal, so what did a mere Untiring—

Those cold, silver-gray eyes flashed across Guan Xinguang's mind, and he shuddered violently.

Lin Qi clicked his tongue, amplifying the flow of divine power as he spoke:
—"What? Do you believe that Immortal Venerable is stronger than an active, currently serving Heavenly General?"

[Right, why hasn't Untiring ascended?]

[Since he hasn't ascended, he can't possibly be genuinely stronger than a True Immortal, right?]

[Besides, I've secured the backing of two immortals... although Ren Ran is an abandoned relic, the one she brought along seems to be an active True Immortal! Didn't he say so himself—an active Heavenly General? Now that I have the aid of a Heavenly General, why should I fear Untiring?]

Guan Xinguang's thoughts spun in frantic disorder. The lingering dread in the depths of his heart gradually evaporated, just as it had back when he heard the Celestial Edict and plotted to extract the Dao Bone.

—"Indeed, why should you fear?"-Lin Qi's voice continued echoing in his mind.-"Rather than worrying about a mere Untiring, shouldn't you let everyone under heaven know who the foremost artifact refiner of this realm is? You are the Chief Director of this realm's craftsmen! You are the realm's foremost artifact refiner! The large-scale teleportation array project should rightfully belong to you, and this qualification exam should rightfully be headed by you! You must make those fools understand this..."

Listening to those whispering murmurs, the expression on Guan Xinguang's face turned fanatical.

* * *

—"Don't head in that direction,"-Sikong Yao said.

He tugged at his cloak, striving to conceal his aura, and deliberately avoided looking toward the treasure ship's VIP suites—where Ren Ran and the artifact refiners of the Department of Works were currently gathered.

—"The immortal qi over there is way too suffocating,"-Sikong Yao muttered, casting an anxious glance behind him.

Behind him stood a surging, bustling crowd.

Although these people wore plain attire and appeared unremarkable, their sheer numbers—men and women, old and young, spanning from Qi Condensation all the way to Mahayana—made such a massive gathering look exceedingly peculiar.

Fortunately, this port was the treasure ship's final stop in the Eastern Domain.

From this city, passing through teleportation arrays into the Southern Domain and onward to the Central Domain, the treasure ship would not make another port of call, sailing straight to the Heavenly Spirit Sect. As a result, the crowd boarding here was exceptionally dense and [fish and dragons intermingled](# "Literal CN: 鱼龙混杂 (yú lóng hùn zá) — fish and dragons mixed together; a motley crowd of diverse individuals of varying backgrounds"), providing prime cover.

Even so, Sikong Yao remained on edge:
—"Disperse as we did on the way here. Come find me only when it's time to disembark—and remember! Do not approach the VIP suites, and do not approach anyone from the Heavenly Dao Alliance! If I catch you, you will be executed without mercy."

As he spoke, a wave of Tribulation Transcendence pressure washed over everyone's heads.

The crowd froze. Even Mahayana cultivators dared not ask questions, silently scattering in every direction, leaving only three to five individuals trailing behind Sikong Yao like servants.

Following Sikong Yao to a secluded corner, they inquired in hushed, cautious tones:
—"Lord Sikong, are we truly heading to the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"

—"You're already aboard the ship, and you're only asking this now?"-Sikong Yao raised an eyebrow.

—"Heheh,"-the man offered a bitter smile.-"Isn't it because we've run out of options? If we remain in the Palace of Carefree Wandering, I fear we'll end up as artifact refinement fodder."

—"Besides, I never joined Carefree Palace of my own free will in the first place,"-another somber-faced man chimed in. His feet tapped against the deck with a rhythmic *thud-thud* of hollow wood; beneath his robes, his lower legs were actually a pair of mechanical wooden prosthetics.-"If we don't escape now, I'm afraid we'll never have another chance."

—"Be that as it may, having left Carefree Palace, we truly have no idea how we're going to survive,"-a woman with half her face scarred sighed softly.

The atmosphere instantly grew oppressive.

Yet just as their words revealed, this group hailed from the Palace of Carefree Wandering.

Or rather, they were defectors from Carefree Palace.

This entire matter traced back to the 'disappearance' of a certain Tribulation Transcendence titan in Carefree Palace.

As everyone knew, Carefree Palace once possessed three Tribulation Transcendence titans. Sikong Yao was one of them; but because the Untiring Immortal Venerable had demolished their auction hall years ago and hunted him across the realm for several years until he lost every shred of dignity and became a laughingstock, he had long lost all standing. Last year, another titan, Sovereign Immortal Chana, was slain by the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Consequently, the only Tribulation Transcendence figure who actually ran the Palace of Carefree Wandering was a certain 'Old Ancestor.'

Yet a month ago, that 'Old Ancestor' had also mysteriously vanished.

Palace Master Li Hao of Carefree Palace was gripped by terror and launched an internal investigation and purge. Yet the harsher his methods became, the more hollow and desperate he appeared. Furthermore, internal feuds were already rampant within Carefree Palace; upon realizing that the Old Ancestor had vanished, many 'clever individuals' grew restless, turning Carefree Palace into a bloodbath where countless lives were extinguished.

In such a slaughterhouse, many naturally sought to escape—and craftsmen comprised the largest contingent.

The Palace of Carefree Wandering owned the realm's largest sales network and operated its most standardized, massive magical treasure trade; naturally, it maintained a vast horde of craftsmen to produce, repair, and forge artifacts.

To Carefree Palace, however, even the most talented among these craftsmen were mere tools for churning out profitable merchandise. They could hardly be considered members of the cultivation world, nor could they be called artifact refiners; they were merely 'craftsmen.'

These craftsmen performed slave labor, their lives disregarded by all. Yet when Carefree Palace turned into a killing field, they inevitably became collateral damage and sacrificial pawns.

It was precisely at this juncture that Carefree Palace's final Tribulation Transcendence titan, Sikong Yao, appeared before them, promising to lead them away from Carefree Palace and designating a destination for their flight.

Although everyone harbored some doubts regarding this 'destination.'

Someone ventured hesitantly:
—"We hold no grievances against the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but Lord Sikong, why did you choose the Heavenly Spirit Sect? We all assumed your relationship with the Untiring Immortal Venerable was... not great."

—"How is it not great? He and I share a very profound bond,"-Sikong Yao asserted with absolute conviction.

Looking at Sikong Yao's dead-serious expression, the others couldn't help but doubt the truth of the rumors.

Then they heard Sikong Yao add:
—"After all, he passionately chased me for seven full years, and still never managed to catch me!"

[Is that something to brag about?!]

[You're actually getting proud of that?!]

[Are you sure the first thing the Untiring Immortal Venerable does when he sees you isn't cleaving you in half?!]

The crowd was struck utterly speechless.

The next second, however, Sikong Yao wiped away his smile, adopting a solemn tone:
—"Regardless of my personal grudges with Untiring, the Heavenly Spirit Sect is your best sanctuary. Given your Carefree Palace background, the moment your identities are exposed, no one would dare harbor you; even if someone had the guts, they would face retaliation later. Only the Heavenly Spirit Sect couldn't care less about that, and only they can protect you.
—"Most importantly, your luck is quite good. Untiring's side is clearly starved for manpower—they've even rolled out a qualification exam! So long as you haven't committed anything unforgivable, I expect he will be more than happy to take in a batch of craftsmen delivered right to his doorstep.
—"At the very worst, you can pass the qualification exam first.
—"The capabilities of Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples have always received high praise, especially recently, as major sects across the Northern Domain have begun adopting his teaching methods. I reckon the value of this qualification certificate will only skyrocket. Carrying that certificate, you'll be considered officially legitimized when you venture out—at the very least, in the immortal cities of the Central Domain, you'll never lack for opportunities."

Hearing this, the craftsmen were completely persuaded.

Finally, Sikong Yao looked at them and added:
—"As for why I'm helping you, it's because I want to use you to earn a favor with Untiring! Considering I've finally done a good deed, maybe he'll let me off the hook? Speaking of which, I haven't done any heinous villainy... well, nothing beyond the average cultivator's baseline, right? Why must he lump me in with Carefree Palace for retribution every single time?! Can't I just quit being Carefree Palace's guest elder?!"-he muttered at the end.

Hearing him speak so candidly, the craftsmen of Carefree Palace breathed a collective sigh of relief.

After all, having emerged from that dog-eat-dog den, had Sikong Yao harbored no ulterior motives, they would have worried about some deeper trap!

Someone flattered:
—"Lord Sikong, we will definitely clarify matters to Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable on your behalf—that you merely coasted along in Carefree Palace and never took part in any grave atrocities."

[Wait, why does that sound so wrong?!]

—"Save it. Only if you pass the Heavenly Spirit Sect's qualification exam will you count as useful,"-Sikong Yao sneered.

—"We will do our utmost!"-the crowd solemnly pledged.

After all, this concerned their very future; they had no choice but to give it their all!

The craftsmen didn't linger long around Sikong Yao, quickly dispersing to prepare for the qualification exam.

Little did they know that the moment they left, Sikong Yao's expression darkened.

—"Still, why the hell did the higher-ups get involved?"-he muttered, pacing in circles like an anxious hamster.-"Even though I don't think Untiring will lose, I'd better prepare my escape route just in case!"
