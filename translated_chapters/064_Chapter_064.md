# Chapter 64: An Evil Sect (第64章 邪派)

Mount Tianling.

—"Immortal, here is my covenant,"-someone said.

When Qu Qianxing returned to Huaixu Hall, several individuals smartly presented contracts already bearing their signatures.

Qu Qianxing was not at all surprised to find that the foremost among them were the very individuals who had attempted to scream and whip up panic earlier when the thunder clapped.

A textbook case of [fearing might rather than cherishing virtue](# "Literal CN: 畏威而不怀德 (wèi wēi ér bù huái dé) — respecting brute force while having zero gratitude for kindness").

Qu Qianxing asked coldly:

—"Have all of you verified the contents of the contract? Are they acceptable to you?"

—"Yes, Immortal, we find every term acceptable!"-someone offered a groveling, flattering smile.

[As if you'd let us disagree anyway!]

The man thought dismissively to himself.

Qu Qianxing paid him no heed, merely casting his gaze across Huaixu Hall before reiterating:

—"This contract concerns our future collaboration. If there are clauses you find onerous, speak up now. Whosoever breaches this covenant shall face severe punishment."

Accompanying those words, the thunder orb floating beside Qu Qianxing crackled with a burst of electric glare.

Yet because of that burst of lightning, many fell into even deeper silence.

When Qiao Linggen's gaze met Qu Qianxing's, however, he gritted his teeth, picked up his contract, and spoke:

—"Immortal Qu, there are several points in this contract I do not quite understand."

With him taking the lead, a scattered few spoke up to raise questions as well.

The chill in Qu Qianxing's eyes softened slightly.

—"...It seems your concerns share substantial overlap,"-Qu Qianxing said, retrieving a jade seal from his sleeve.-"How about this: we shall execute temporary contracts. I will dispatch disciples to liaise with each of you directly regarding your questions, and we shall sign the definitive covenant only once both parties are satisfied."

Saying so, he pressed his stamp onto those contracts, then asked:

—"Does anyone else wish to enter into a temporary contract?"

Seeing his amicable demeanor, several more raised their hands.

Only after finalizing the temporary contracts did Qu Qianxing state:

—"However, as of right now, I need you all to immediately return to your respective settlements with your contracts. Only then can the covenants take effect—most notably the clauses regarding the protection of your assets—"

—"Could it be... the Zheng Clan has finally made their move?!"-Qiao Linggen jolted in shock, instantly putting the pieces together.

Qu Qianxing gave a slight nod.

Qiao Linggen leaped to his feet. At the thought of Qiao Family Village's peril, he could not wait a single second to rush back, stammering out in a fluster:

—"Thank you for the guidance, Immortal! I shall head back this instant!"

There were several others just like him, mostly those who, like Qiao Linggen, had signed temporary covenants.

By contrast, those who were the most opportunistic fence-sitters exchanged furtive glances, showing no inclination to budge.

One of them even spoke up:

—"Immortal Qu, I am merely a humble rogue cultivator. Even if I don't return, it hardly matters..."

—"Whosoever does not return immediately shall be deemed an enemy of the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Qu Qianxing said coldly, his thunder orb flashing with lightning once more.

The man clamped his mouth shut in terror.

Only then did Qu Qianxing nod, turning to Qiao Linggen:

—"Rest assured. The coral trees possess defensive capabilities. Those who harbor malice toward you cannot easily breach your dwellings. There is ample time for you to make your return."

* * *

Naturally, some scoffed at Qu Qianxing's words.

They believed that with Heavenly Spirit Sect being so far away, [distant water could not quench a nearby fire](# "Literal CN: 远水救不了近火 — a slow remedy cannot solve an immediate crisis"). What could a single coral tree possibly hold back?

Yet that tree truly did hold them back.

Outside Qiao Family Village, cultivators were running around in circles in utter futility.

—"It's just a single question! Not one of you can solve it?!"-the Zheng Clan Patriarch raged in impotent fury.

—"There's nothing we can do, we can't even read it!"-his subordinate cried in agony.-"The script is bizarre, completely unlike anything we've ever seen! If I recall correctly, doesn't this village carry half-demon blood? Could it be some unique beastfolk script?"

Hearing this, the Patriarch immediately glared at several beastfolk cultivators hired from outside.

Those beastfolk cultivators shook their heads vigorously:

—"We've never seen this. This isn't the lingua franca of any of the major demon tribes. Perhaps some remote, obscure tribe invented it themselves?"

—"Makes sense! Look, there are even a few Han characters mixed in! It must be that those barbarians were bad at studying, so they used symbols as makeshift substitutes for words!"-someone declared in sudden enlightenment.

Another voiced confusion:

—"Qiao Family Village has been here for centuries, yet I've never heard of them having their own script! In that village, barely anyone can even read!"

—"Wait, what about finding cultivators who practice the [Language Comprehension Spell](# "Literal CN: 通语术 (Tōngyǔ Shù) — Universal Language Comprehension spell")?!"-another offered a suggestion.-"I recall the Chen Clan has one!"

The so-called "Language Comprehension Spell" was a universal translation art. Cultivators who mastered this spell could not only understand the speech of beasts such as cats and dogs, but could even listen to the whispering of plants and trees. Given its immense convenience, quite a few cultivators practiced it.

Hearing this, the Zheng Clan Patriarch's eyes lit up:

—"Hurry and invite him over!"

The Chen Clan, like the Zheng Clan, was a cultivation clan of Fengqing City currently liquidating the "rebels" within their territory, so they had naturally encountered identical problems.

The Chen cultivator specializing in the "Language Comprehension Spell" was already suffering from a splitting headache; upon seeing the Zheng Clan's barrier question, his head practically swelled twice as large.

—"...Incomprehensible,"-the cultivator muttered as if sleepwalking.

—"How could it be incomprehensible?"-the Zheng Clan Patriarch asked suspiciously.-"Doesn't the Language Comprehension Spell perceive the mind of the speaker or author and translate any language?"

—"Yes, I can perceive it! I understand every individual concept, but I cannot comprehend the meaning!"-the cultivator was practically in tears.-"Whenever I try to follow the author's train of thought, my head buzzes violently!"

[—"...Could it be the demonic tongue?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator")

[—"Even if it were demonic, this village is filled with mortals. There is no way they could learn it, so that makes no sense."](# "Speaker: Cultivator")

People engaged in puzzled speculation, arriving at no conclusion.

In the end, the cultivator of Language Comprehension wept:

—"I can only confirm... this is probably, maybe, perhaps... arithmetic...?"

...Could arithmetic produce this kind of effect?!

The Zheng Clan Patriarch was stunned. Yet looking at the unbreachable barrier, he burst into boisterous laughter:

—"If it's arithmetic, that makes it simple! Carefree Palace is famed for commerce—the one thing they never lack is bookkeepers!"

* * *

And so, the pressure shifted squarely onto Carefree Palace.

In truth, it wasn't just the Zheng Clan Patriarch. Having instigated this Central Domain-wide purge by clans and sects against low-tier cultivators, Carefree Palace naturally monitored conditions on the ground around the clock. The various questions displayed on the glowing screens were dispatched to Carefree Palace at the earliest possible moment.

Confronted with those bizarre questions, Carefree Palace did just as the Zheng Clan had, first mobilizing linguistics experts to analyze them. After barely discerning the literal Chinese meaning behind each character, however, people assembled them together only to discover in despair that even though they recognized every single word, they still could not comprehend the meaning of the sentences.

Nevertheless, everyone also realized that these were probably, maybe, perhaps arithmetic problems.

Fortunately, Carefree Palace at least understood that ordinary bookkeepers could never hope to solve problems of this caliber. Paying exorbitant sums, they hired several prominent arithmetic masters of the cultivation world.

After indirectly understanding the questions via translation, the arithmetic masters sank into deep contemplation:

—"Are these problems truly arithmetic? They are entirely unprecedented..."

—"Not necessarily. Look at this problem—isn't this a standard equation? Through the 'Language Comprehension Spell,' I can visualize geometric figures, three-dimensional concepts... Fascinating, truly fascinating!"

—"Excuse me, where might one enroll to study this?"-the arithmetic master inquired earnestly.

The Palace Master of Carefree Palace was on the verge of losing his mind:

—"I hired you to solve the questions, not enroll in school!"

After debating among themselves for half the day, the arithmetic masters ultimately chose to [pass the buck](# "Literal CN: 推锅 (tuī guō) — shift blame or responsibility"):

—"Our capability falls short. For knowledge of such unfathomable depth, we estimate only diviners who study the *Zhouyi* could calculate the answers!"

—"Go summon the finest diviners,"-the Palace Master of Carefree Palace said weakly.

—"By the way, what is the provenance of these problems...?"-the arithmetic master asked, still consumed by burning curiosity.

The Palace Master of Carefree Palace lacked even the strength to throw them out.

Naturally, such an "orthodox frontal approach" hardly suited the ethos of Carefree Palace. They had never been the sort to honestly answer questions to attain results—though if a direct assault yielded fruit, they wouldn't shy away from that either.

Tossing those problems to the diviners and arithmetic masters to tackle, Li Hao straightened his frame and interrogated his subordinate:

—"Those useless wastes haven't given up just because they couldn't solve the questions, have they?"

—"Of course they haven't given up, they are currently assaulting the defensive formations,"-the subordinate replied with difficulty.-"However, that formation is worthy of being forged by a Tribulation Transcendence... and employs the most rigorous Question-and-Answer defensive architecture. Conventional methods cannot deceive the questions to slip inside at all."

[Damn it! This kind of Q&A formation was originally Carefree Palace's specialty!] When robb... doing business, they frequently ran into similar wards, yet Carefree Palace had never suffered such frustration; in the cultivation world, there were few entities that possessed broader trivia knowledge than them!

In fact, Carefree Palace found itself wishing those formations were orthodox contests of raw power instead!

That way, with the Untiring Immortal Venerable absent and relying solely on Qu Qianxing, a vulnerability or two would inevitably emerge.

—"Idiots! Is our Carefree Palace the kind of brainless sect that relies on head-on collisions to survive?!"-Li Hao, cutting an eight-foot musclebound figure whose very brain seemed packed with brawn, berated his subordinate.-"If you can't break it from the outside, can't you shatter it from within?!"

—"Didn't I tell you to bribe internal moles? It is high time they earned their keep!"

Speaking to here, Li Hao tacked on an extra grievance:

—"Speaking of which, I wonder how that bunch dispatched to Heavenly Spirit Sect is faring. Not a shred of news from them—a bunch of freeloading wastes who know only how to stuff their faces!"

* * *

Abyssal Ocean, Kunpeng.

Unaware of the Carefree Palace Master's grievances, high above the Abyss Sea aboard the Kunpeng, a crowd of those very "freeloading wastes" was chaotically venting their resentment:

[—"This is outright extortion!"](# "Speaker: Merchant")

[—"Exactly! This covenant is nothing short of a [deed of servitude](# "Literal CN: 卖身契 (mài shēn qì) — an indentured servitude contract or deed selling oneself into slavery")!"](# "Speaker: Merchant")

[—"What kind of righteous sect is Heavenly Spirit Sect?!"](# "Speaker: Lou Wu")

Qiao Linggen was already on pins and needles over the plight of his village, and this whining only unsettled his nerves further.

He could not help but sneer:

—"Since you feel coerced, why did you sign the contract?"

—"Because not signing wasn't an option, obviously! Anyone with eyes could see that refusing to sign meant certain death!"-someone retorted defensively.

He was none other than the one who had taken the lead in screaming "an ill omen!" when the thunder roared earlier.

Qiao Linggen recalled that the man was named "Lou Wu," a secondhand poultry and livestock peddler. When he first arrived at Mount Tianling, his circumstances were much like Qiao Linggen's—nervous, impoverished, and desperate. Back then, they could still converse cordially, and Qiao Linggen had offered him plenty of guidance, even introducing him to the supply channels for the cake shops. Once the man struck it rich, however, he associated exclusively with merchants hailing from prominent immortal cities and prestigious sects.

Qiao Linggen had no regard for him either, merely mocking:

—"Every one of us came here today after agreeing to the preliminary terms; there was never any coercion. And if you had issues with specific clauses, you could have signed a temporary contract!"

—"You think your temporary covenant is a blessing?"-Lou Wu sneered dismissively.-"Chances are they made a note of your names, preparing to settle scores with you down the road!"

Hearing him say that, several people who had signed temporary contracts showed uneasy expressions.

Qiao Linggen looked at Lou Wu as if staring at an idiot:

—"A single streak of lightning from them could take your life; what need would they have for such petty posturing? In my eyes, you're the one who did something guilty to be acting so defensive!"

—"What did you say?!"-Lou Wu rolled up his sleeves and jumped to his feet.

Qiao Linggen did not back down an inch:

—"I said you're colluding with Carefree Palace!"

Seeing that the two were about to trade blows, bystanders hastily intervened to pull them apart.

—"Lou Wu has overly grandiose ambitions; why bother provoking him?"-someone advised Qiao Linggen.

Qiao Linggen held his chin high:

—"I just can't stand his attitude—stuffing his face while complaining about the food."

Yet in his heart, Qiao Linggen knew his sense of righteousness wasn't actually that grand. The reason he found Lou Wu so insufferable right now was purely because he was terrified.

Yes, Qiao Linggen was afraid!

He feared not only the Zheng Clan assaulting the village, and not only that the coral tree might fail to shield them; what he feared even more, in truth, were his own villagers.

Qiao Linggen knew his village inside and out. He knew there were several ruffians in the village cut from the exact same cloth as Lou Wu. Usually they caused no trouble because Qiao Linggen kept them in line, but now that he was away, if the Zheng Clan did something, would those scoundrels sell out the village?!

Just pondering that possibility gave Qiao Linggen a splitting headache.

* * *

The ruffians of Qiao Family Village certainly did not disappoint Qiao Linggen's "expectations."

They had attempted a backstab, but the backstab had failed miserably.

At this moment, they stood staring blankly at the glowing screen before them, which displayed the exact same question as on the outside.

Yes, venturing from inside the village to the outside also required solving the problem!

The barrier was actually bidirectional!

A problem that even arithmetic masters could not solve was naturally far beyond the reach of local thugs:

[—"What are these bizarre drawings?"](# "Speaker: Village Ruffian")

[—"A heavenly scripture?"](# "Speaker: Village Ruffian")

[—"H-Heavenly scripture?! Then won't the immortals punish us?!"](# "Speaker: Village Ruffian")

The thugs exclaimed in horror.

Originally, they had planned to wait for the outside cultivators to breach the perimeter before surrendering the village. To their shock, those cultivators had dallied outside for an entire day, yet remained completely incapable of opening the dome.

Having kept watch all day and confirmed temporary safety, the villagers finally went to sleep with eased minds—perhaps not entirely at ease, but they couldn't just stand stupidly beneath the coral tree forever. Only a few sentries were left behind. And these few ruffians, long bribed by the outside cultivators, crept stealthily to the perimeter of the defensive formation, attempting to slip out.

—In the instant they stepped through, like opening a door, wouldn't a temporary gap in the formation appear?

The ruffians, knowing nothing of array theory, took it for granted.

Never did they expect that while the dome barred outsiders from entering, it simultaneously barred insiders from leaving.

Seeing this, the cultivator waiting outside to receive them wore a dark, flickering expression, sneering:

—"Look at your cowardly faces! This is merely an arithmetic problem, but we lack a suitable arithmetic master on our end, so it cannot be solved for the time being."

[—"Th-Then what should we do?"](# "Speaker: Village Ruffian")

—"Are you fools?! Can't you just dig up the coral tree directly?!"-the cultivator incited them.

—"Dig up the coral tree?!"-the thugs were dumbfounded.-"B-But Uncle Qiao hasn't returned yet. Without the coral tree, wouldn't he..."

—"Keeping him from returning is precisely the point! Think about it: once he returns and discovers what you've done, do you think he'll spare you? Better to dig up the coral tree before he gets back and welcome our masters into the village! With that contribution, you'll be the stewards of this village!"-the cultivator seduced them.

Swayed by his words, the thugs grew tempted, yet still hesitated:

[—"B-But..."](# "Speaker: Village Ruffian")

—"No buts! Once this opportunity slips by, you'll never get another,"-the cultivator sneered.-"And don't worry about that village chief of yours. Think about him living in luxury at Mount Tianling, wining and dining like an immortal—for all you know, he'd love nothing more than never returning!"

That line finally convinced the thugs.

Fetching several jars of wine, they went over to chat with the villagers on sentry duty, got them thoroughly drunk, and only then grabbed shovels and strode toward the coral tree.

It was just that their movements were far too slow.

After a day and a night, it was ample time for the Kunpeng to deliver Qiao Linggen home.

Qiao Linggen returned to Qiao Family Village via the sundial clock. Looking up, the very first sight that greeted his eyes was the thugs clutching iron shovels, poised to start digging.

He roared in fury:

—"What do you think you are doing?!"

Simultaneously, accompanied by Qiao Linggen's arrival, the grand formation enveloping the village flared with brilliant light, and a booming voice as resonant as a deity echoed:

> 【Contract detected.】
> 
> 【Temporary Contract validated!】
> 
> 【Henceforth: Whosoever infringes upon 『Qiao Family Village』 infringes upon our Heavenly Spirit Sect.】

Accompanying that decree, the defensive formation instantly flipped into an offensive array! Celestial purple-gold lightning whipped around its perimeter, crashing down upon the cultivators who had been ceaselessly assaulting the barrier.

Those cultivators were instantly blasted by the lightning, shrieking in agony.

Their cultivation was only fit for bullying mortals; the elder specifically hired by the Zheng Clan was merely at the Golden Core stage. How could they withstand the assault of celestial golden lightning? That they weren't wiped out in a single wave was already Qu Qianxing showing mercy.

The most pathetic among them was the Patriarch of the Zheng Clan.

Originally, backed by Carefree Palace and banking on Heavenly Spirit Sect being located on the far side of the continent, he refused to believe Heavenly Spirit Sect could do a thing to him, even strutting over in arrogant complacency to witness the "fate of those who defied them" in person. Now, enveloped in the celestial lightning and electric glare, he truly regretted it—regretted it down to his marrow.

He could not help but recall the horrifying deaths of Daoist Huang and the others from Wanhe Gang.

Only, because of Daoist Huang's dying accusations, the Zheng Clan Patriarch had previously marveled at Carefree Palace's ruthlessness, fearing to disobey Carefree Palace's orders and meekly complying with whatever they demanded.

Now, under this celestial lightning, his mind suddenly grew sharp:

Carefree Palace was terrifying indeed, capable of making Daoist Huang die such a gruesome death!

Yet what kind of monstrous existence was Heavenly Spirit Sect—against whom even Carefree Palace was helpless, and from whom Daoist Huang had been desperate to flee the Central Domain?!

What [leopard guts had he eaten](# "Literal CN: 吃豹子胆 (chī bàozi dǎn) — eaten leopard gallbladders; a Chinese idiom meaning to possess reckless, suicidal audacity") to dare provoke them?!

Alas, the Zheng Clan Patriarch had figured this out far too late!

A streak of purple-gold lightning crashed onto his body. Even as he wailed in agony, a purple-gold brand, resembling a convict's mark, burned deep into the Zheng Clan Patriarch's forehead!

* * *

Yet Qiao Family Village was merely the most ideal scenario.

Not all "partners" possessed the leadership of Qiao Linggen, nor were all villagers cooperative with only a traitor or two; there were far worse situations.

Among the worst, none surpassed Longmen Town.

This town was not a matter of one or two traitors, but a betrayal spanning from Lou Wu, who signed the covenant, to the entire town—from the town mayor to the common townsfolk, a total betrayal by all.

It turned out that after Lou Wu made a fortune at the Immortal Bazaar, he was unsatisfied with merely being a supplier. Witnessing how well the cakes sold, he conceived other designs.

Coincidentally, Shengmo Sect, a sect near Longmen Town, had also attended the Immortal Bazaar.

—That sect had not attended out of support for Heavenly Spirit Sect, nor was it even neutral; it was a sect entirely aligned with Carefree Palace. However, its sect leader was extremely suspicious; while approving of Daoist Huang's claims, he still felt it was safer to see things with his own eyes, and thus dispatched personnel.

Confronted by the flourishing spectacle of the Immortal Bazaar, Shengmo Sect conceived wicked designs. While serving as moles reporting intelligence on the bazaar to Carefree Palace, they leveraged their connections in Longmen Town—as a local sect, many of their cultivators were selected from Longmen Town, naturally possessing intricate kinship ties—to approach Lou Wu, instructing him to pry into the recipes of several popular shops or recruit their proprietors.

Even the merchants supplying Lou Wu chose to throw their lot in with Shengmo Sect, intending to manufacture and sell the goods themselves once Lou Wu acquired the recipes. It could be said that every figure holding real power in the town had chosen the path they deemed most profitable for themselves.

To be fair, making such a choice was reasonable enough.

Zhou Hui had never intended to bind every merchant.

Thus, after the Immortal Bazaar, some merchants felt that visitors would inevitably dwindle following the auction, and preferred to develop business back in their home territories; they signed short-term contracts and returned home early.

Heavenly Spirit Sect placed no obstacles in the way of those merchants—with the exception of recipe thieves. Consequently, Lou Wu, who had yet to finalize his contract, found that even though he had stolen the recipes, upon returning to the town via the coral tree, he could not utter a single word regarding their contents.

Alas, Lou Wu harbored wicked designs.

Not only did they covet the recipes, but they also lusted after the revenues of the Immortal Bazaar and attempted to serve as Carefree Palace's internal spies. Thus, they had Lou Wu pretend to sign a long-term covenant, infiltrating the ranks of Qiao Linggen and the others.

Such a Lou Wu and such a Longmen Town would naturally suffer no external assault.

While Qiao Family Village was under siege, Longmen Town, theoretically speaking, faced zero hostility. Yet when Lou Wu returned to Longmen Town, he witnessed a crowd jumping and shouting at the town entrance, locked in utter chaos.

—"What happened?"-Lou Wu asked blankly.

—"You're finally back!"-seeing Lou Wu, the town mayor, who had been standing silently by the entrance, pointed an accusatory finger in fury.-"Starting yesterday, this coral tree shot out a beam of light that engulfed the entire town, barring our own townsfolk from entering or leaving!"

[—"How could that be?"](# "Speaker: Lou Wu")

Lou Wu attempted to step out of the town, only to see the glowing screen illuminate, displaying several bizarre symbols.

This time, it wasn't advanced mathematics—it was advanced physics.

Though to Lou Wu, there was zero difference.

—"What kind of ghost is this?"

Lou Wu threw a punch at the question, attempting to resolve the matter with brute force.

Yet accompanied by that strike, a counterforce flung him backwards—one dozens of times greater than his own strength—sending him tumbling head over heels dozens of times until he crashed right in front of the coral tree!

Even worse, the coral tree emitted a voice:

> 【Contract detected.】
> 
> 【Contract invalid!】
> 
> 【Longmen Town's Lou Wu, Mei Xianyu, Lou XX... and others have committed severe breaches of contract!】
> 
> 【Remedy within three contractual days!】
> 
> 【Failure to do so shall result in formal breach-of-contract prosecution!】

Accompanying that announcement, several streaks of golden lightning crashed into the crowd, striking each named individual with surgical precision. It did not hurt, but they discovered in horror that shackles of golden lightning had clamped around their necks!

—"What is the meaning of this?!"-the Mayor of Longmen Town, namely "Mei Xianyu," shouted in fury.

—"It's the covenant! Heavenly Spirit Sect's covenant!"-seeing the golden lightning, Lou Wu instantly realized what had happened, panicking.-"What we did breached the contract, so we are being punished!"

He looked toward the reverse-defensive dome:

—"Chances are, this barrier is too! If we do not make amends, I fear we will never be able to step out of this town for the rest of our lives!"

—"What?!"-Mei Xianyu roared indignantly.-"How dare they?!"

He lashed out with a kick at the coral tree, only to be flung backwards into another massive somersault.

Hearing this, several townsfolk erupted in protest:

[—"What kind of talk is that?! Lou Wu is the one who signed the contract; what does it have to do with us?!"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")

[—"Exactly! We've never even set foot in this Heavenly Spirit Sect!"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")

[—"Are we supposed to be locked inside this town for the rest of our lives too?!"](# "Speaker: Townsfolk")

[...You may never have set foot in Mount Tianling, but the supply agreement included your goods!]

[When the town held discussions back then, every one of you agreed to it!]

Lou Wu thought bitterly to himself.

Heavenly Spirit Sect naturally wouldn't sign a contract with a secondhand merchant out of thin air. Lou Wu had undeniably obtained the authorization of these people and secured their notarized proof before heading to Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Thus, these people were not entirely uninvolved.

Only, they refused to see it that way. One townsman immediately grabbed a shovel and marched toward the coral tree:

—"I'm going to dig up this cursed piece of rubbish!"

*BOOM—!*

A bolt of purple-gold lightning crashed down, blasting the offender unconscious on the spot! The purple thundercloud did not disperse; instead, like a vigilant prison warden, it hovered above the town, glaring down menacingly at every single soul below.

The townsfolk were thoroughly cowed.

Having witnessed Qu Qianxing unleash his divine might in person, Lou Wu was the most terrified of this lightning.

—"M-Mayor, our only choice is to tear up our contract with Shengmo Sect,"-Lou Wu said tremulously.-"We cannot afford to offend the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"

[Imprisoning an entire town's residents for failing to reach a contract! Suppressing the town with heavenly lightning!]

[...What kind of righteous sect acted like this?!]

Lou Wu could not help but think.

This time, it wasn't slander; it was his genuine, heartfelt thought.

* * *

Heavenly Spirit Sect, Buyan Palace, Spell Cultivator Classroom.

Mo Xi silently watched a group of people flipping through notebooks, placing question after question onto the sandbox map representing the teleportation nodes. Some problems were rejected, while others were approved and set down.

Those teleportation nodes glowed with different hues: some were white, indicating defense against external invasion; some were black, indicating the imprisonment of contract-breakers; and some were multicolored, indicating that the local populace had reached no unified consensus, requiring targeted defense of key objectives only.

The rest was fine, but gazing upon the black lights, even Mo Xi's expression twitched slightly.

Mo Xi could not resist confirming with Qu Qianxing:

—"Second Senior Brother, is it truly necessary to go this far? For those who showed no sincerity in collaborating, teaching them a minor lesson should suffice; why force them...? Weren't you worried earlier that your power wouldn't be enough?"

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing sneered coldly:

—"It is merely [killing the chicken to scare the monkey](# "Literal CN: 杀鸡儆猴 (shā jī jǐng hóu) — punishing one to deter others"). If we do not make an example of them, these foolish masses will never learn their lesson!"

[Foolish masses...]

[Second Senior Brother, did you suffer some profound trauma somewhere in the past?]

Mo Xi desperately wanted to ask, but didn't dare. Qu Qianxing never spoke of his past, though rumors said he had once served as an official in the mortal realm's largest empire in the Northern Domain—something must have happened back then, surely?

Mo Xi speculated internally, but held his tongue so as not to provoke his Second Senior Brother, who looked to be in a foul mood. Instead, he flashed a nonchalant smile:

—"I was only concerned about tarnishing our Heavenly Spirit Sect's reputation. It hardly feels like the conduct of a righteous sect."

—"Is our Heavenly Spirit Sect a righteous sect?"-Qu Qianxing retorted.-"In this Central Domain, where even the Heavenly Dao Alliance holds little sway, every sect has skeletons in its closet. Why cling so obstinately to the notion of 'orthodoxy'?"

Speaking to here, Qu Qianxing suddenly noticed a disciple's movements beside him:

—"Wait! Do not take that notebook! I haven't solved that conjecture written there yet! Only deploy questions I know how to solve, just in case!"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi gazed at those thick stacks of notebooks in sheer awe.

In a daze, he thought:

[Ah... I didn't even realize we were an evil sect all along!]

* * *

Zhou Hui had no idea that simply by stepping out for a trip, Heavenly Spirit Sect had transformed into an evil sect.

And that the deed was done by his most trusted second disciple, no less.

Naturally, Zhou Hui had no leisure to worry about that right now. He stood before the "back mountain" of Blood Demon Hall, observing the "mountain" wrapped tightly in myriad formations.

—"The defensive preparations are complete. I am now preparing to move the mountain,"-Zhou Hui told the disciples of Blood Demon Hall.-"No matter what happens, remain calm."

—"Rest assured, Immortal Venerable,"-Shen Qingqian replied with solemn gravity.-"We are fully prepared!"

Watching the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples toil for so many days, everyone in Blood Demon Hall had braced themselves mentally:

For a Tribulation Transcendence powerhouse to exercise such caution, this was no ordinary mountain! At the very least, a fiend must be sealed within!

Body cultivators feared no danger!

All of Blood Demon Hall stood ready to fight and defend their sect.

Zhou Hui stood before that mountain and gently flicked his sleeve.

The vegetation, gravel, and layers of soil across the mountain were peeled away as effortlessly as a bridal veil, exposing what lay beneath...

A head!

Yes, that was no mountain at all, but a titanic head! Wedged within this spatial rift, it reached the heavens above and touched the earth below, occupying nearly the entirety of the spatial pocket!

What was most horrifying was that it was actually alive!

Judging from the subtle tremors, it was still breathing. Only, its eyes were tightly shut, seemingly asleep. And the "caves where Flame Crystal Flowers grew" mentioned by Shen Qingqian were actually this monstrosity's nostrils!

Its gaping maw pointed directly toward Blood Demon Hall—positioned precisely to bite off the lower half of the colossal ancient demon skeleton that formed the hall itself!

As if providing proof, several human skeletons lay scattered near its teeth, making it look as though it could snap open its jaws and devour Blood Demon Hall at any given moment.

Shen Qingqian had believed she was prepared, but when she saw that titanic maw gaping toward her sect, she stumbled backward several paces. Had Zhou Hui's swift breeze not held her upright, she would have collapsed onto the ground on the spot.

To say nothing of the other Blood Demon Hall disciples. Staring at this gargantuan head, they could not help but scream in sheer terror:

[—"WHAT IN THE WORLD IS THIS MONSTROSITY?!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciples")
