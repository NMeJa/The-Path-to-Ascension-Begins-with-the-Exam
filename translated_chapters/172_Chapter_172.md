# Chapter 172: The Foremost Desire

—"[Offering courtesy without cause is a sure sign of treachery or theft](# "Chinese Proverb: 无事献殷勤，非奸即盗 (wú shì xiàn yīn qín, fēi jiān jí dào) — showing unprompted hospitality or flattery conceals sinister motives"),"-Tao Zhu sent a divine sense transmission to Zhou Hui.

They were currently walking along the corridor of the Sun Wheel Palace, heading toward the forbidden grounds of the demon race deep within the palace.

The scenery here was exceptionally beautiful.

Snow-white tree silhouettes and jade-tinted breezes wove together into the corridor's balustrades and eaves. Golden-crimson sunlight draped down like gauzy curtains—gentle, warm, and brimming with spiritual energy. Simply walking along this path, one felt a soothing warmth radiating from their dantian, while the spiritual energy within their body behaved more obediently than it ever had in their life.

For a cultivator, this was practically a monumental karmic opportunity.

Yet when the [pie falling from the sky](# "Chinese Idiom: 天上掉馅饼 (tiān shàng diào xiànbǐng) — an unexpected windfall or stroke of luck") was this massive, it instead caused one's heart to drum with suspicion.

Boss Tao felt deeply ill at ease. He questioned Zhou Hui to confirm:

—"Or could it be that this little phoenix is actually your illegitimate daughter looking to show filial piety to you?... No, that's not right. Even if you were the Demon Emperor's biological father, he'd have no reason to treat you this well. Or did you save the entire demon race from impending extinction?"

—"Quit talking nonsense,"-Zhou Hui replied.

[—"Then I can't wrap my head around why they'd present their secret treasure. In theory, they shouldn't even let us near it."](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

—"Indeed it is,"-Zhou Hui replied with a wry smile.

In truth, Zhou Hui was harboring doubts of his own.

Although one of his objectives in coming here was indeed the "secret treasure of the demon race," even he knew that based solely on his current cooperation with the demons, convincing them to surrender their treasure was impossible; even catching a single glimpse of it ought to have been exceedingly difficult.

Zhou Hui knew all too well the dismal caliber of his own social skills. To prevent misunderstandings with the demon race, when Mo Xi had suggested bringing Boss Tao along on the trip to the demons, Zhou Hui had nodded and accepted the recommendation.

In his view, the slick, worldly Boss Tao was far more likely than himself to persuade the demons to part with their secret treasure.

Who could have guessed that after journeying all the way to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, and even facing a minor obstacle at the gates of the Sun Wheel Palace, the demon Saintess would act as though she had been conquered by some unknown force, stubbornly insisting on gifting him the secret treasure?

[What kind of third-rate male-lead web novel development is this...?]

Wait a moment. Speaking of third-rate male-lead web novels, it was not entirely unrelated.

Thinking this, Zhou Hui let his divine sense sweep faintly over his two disciples.

Mo Bei and Mo Xi were also currently examining their surroundings.

Among them, Mo Bei was purely curious. She clearly took a liking to the corridor woven from white tree shadows; her habitually dull expression rarely held a faint trace of a smile. Of course, she did not squander the superb cultivation environment here either. Without waiting for her master's instructions, the spiritual energy in her meridians had already begun circulating briskly, draping a thin layer of white frost over her glossy hair like a translucent silk veil.

As for Mo Xi, he was one part curious, two parts worried, and seven parts vigilant.

He naturally understood this was a prime spot for cultivation, all the more so since he had just condensed his core and needed consolidation most, yet he had zero desire to partake in this karmic opportunity.

Compared to this, Mo Xi was far more worried that the demon race harbored sinister designs on his master.

Intensely suspicious by nature, he naturally refused to believe Shui Huang'er harbored pure goodwill. Even though he knew that someone of his master's stature was hardly a subject a newly minted Golden Core novice like himself could fret over, his master was so [pure, noble, and untainted by worldly grime](# "Chinese Idiom: 光风霁月 / 霁月清风 (jì yuè qīng fēng) — clear breeze and bright moon; noble, upright, and innocent character") that if Master inadvertently fell into a trap, harming even a single strand of hair would be a tragedy.

The more Mo Xi thought, the more anxious he grew. Right at this moment, he heard his master's gentle voice resonate within his mind:

—"Xi'er, don't let your thoughts run wild."

[—"But..."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"There are no 'buts.' The most important thing for you right now is to consolidate your cultivation base,"-Zhou Hui said, worriedly using his divine sense to perceive the emotional turbulence of his youngest disciple.-"Ever since arriving here, you've seemed extraordinarily strange. Is there a reason?"

—"I..."-Mo Xi's eyes widened.

Indeed, ever since arriving in the Southern Realm, he had been plagued by bizarre visual hallucinations, auditory whispers, and inexplicable emotions; it was only when he broke through to the Golden Core stage earlier that he had shaken off those strange illusions.

Now that his core was formed, while the symptoms were no longer as severe as before, he remained somewhat restless and uneasy.

[...Yet right now is hardly the time to whine to Master!]

[Who knows what that demon vixen is plotting! Even if I can't lend a hand, I certainly can't drag Master down!]

—"Master, please don't worry,"-Mo Xi strove to infuse his tone with persuasion.-"Though I did feel slightly unwell earlier—likely affected by the demonic qi?—after condensing my core, all that discomfort has been purged. I'm completely fine now."

As he said this, he even circulated a cycle of spiritual energy for Zhou Hui to inspect.

Yet his master seemed extraordinarily ill at ease, never releasing the divine sense link.

Mo Xi had no choice but to obediently circulate his spiritual energy cycle after cycle.

Fortunately, after doing so, Mo Xi's emotions genuinely stabilized. Recognizing that the only thing he could currently do was indeed to consolidate his cultivation base, he gradually suppressed his suspicions deep down, dedicating himself to diligent practice just like Mo Bei.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui still did not completely sever his divine sense.

He was not merely supervising his disciples' practice; rather, throughout this process, uneasy in his heart, he conducted several thorough divine sense examinations over both disciples.

After all, Mo Xi shared considerable ties with a certain "third-rate male-lead web novel protagonist" and the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. Thus, prior to condensing his core, Mo Xi had not only been corroded by fiendish qi and displayed signs of cultivation deviation, but also carried the possibility of awakening past-life memories.

Even Mo Bei had spoken strange words earlier.

Fortunately, Mo Xi did not seem to be lying.

Perhaps all that fiendish qi had been thoroughly incinerated by the demon fire during Mo Xi's core condensation; this time, Zhou Hui detected not the slightest anomaly. To his pleasant surprise, he even discovered that the quality of Mo Xi's golden core was exceptionally superb, having gained a mutated attribute from the demon fire. In the future, this would not only facilitate greater mastery of human mental techniques, but also yield [twice the result with half the effort](# "Chinese Idiom: 事半功倍 (shì bàn gōng bèi) — achieve double the result with half the work") when practicing demon spellcraft.

[It seems I can set my mind at ease?]

On the surface, Zhou Hui breathed a sigh of relief at last.

His actions, however, told a different story.

After conducting an exhaustive examination of both disciples using 'conventional methods' and conveniently slapping on a whole suite of protective, warding, exorcising, blessing, and enhancement spells for good measure, Zhou Hui only then sluggishly began to withdraw his divine sense. In the middle of doing so, as if suddenly struck by a thought, his divine sense abruptly surged outward.

As Zhou Hui's divine sense expanded, gossamer threads reflected within his pupils.

Those were the trajectories of the "Heavenly Dao."

Zhou Hui had obtained the authority to view the Heavenly Dao and had learned methods for editing the Heavenly Dao from the "Creator Deity" of that other miniature world. However, considering the Heavenly Dao's far-reaching impact on all living beings, he had refrained from reckless action, never modifying it rashly.

This was Zhou Hui's first time inspecting the Heavenly Dao outside the Ascension Stairway. He had done so only because the realization had suddenly struck him: since the "fated destiny of the past life" had once played out upon the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, the Heavenly Dao ought to have left behind traces.

This inspection proved him right.

Zhou Hui saw chaotic, tangled threads blanketing the Sacred Tree, coiling layer upon layer around Mo Xi's body, Mo Bei's body, and even Shui Huang'er's body, wrapping them into near-complete silken cocoons.

—"I see. The quickest method to alter 'destiny' is to directly trigger the events stipulated by that 'destiny,'"-Zhou Hui realized in an epiphany.

The reason he had not noticed this at the Heavenly Spirit Sect was obviously that the sect's "script" had long since been scrambled beyond recognition by his hands, severing those threads entirely.

Here at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, however, the 'destiny' still persisted, even exerting an influence upon Mo Xi.

—"Then right now, should I snip these threads?"-Zhou Hui mumbled.

He gave those slender threads a tug.

In the eyes of anyone unable to see these threads of destiny, this action must have looked bizarre in the extreme.

Sure enough, a bewildered voice drifted out from beneath that thick 'cocoon':

[—"Master?"](# "Speaker: 'Mo Xi'")

Perhaps due to the interference of the threads of destiny, "Mo Xi's" voice sounded slightly distorted.

Suspecting nothing amiss, Zhou Hui replied gently:

—"Wait a moment. Let me see how to alter it."

* * *

—"No! Rather than altering it, don't you feel you've forgotten something?!"-Boss Tao cried out in righteous indignation.

Regrettably, his voice failed to reach Zhou Hui's ears.

Although Tao Zhu was still in the corridor of the Sun Wheel Palace, a layer of thin, circular bars now enclosed his perimeter in the shape of a cage, penning him inside exactly like a chicken or duck.

For a Mahayana cultivator, Boss Tao had not suffered such treatment in ages. Yet this cage clearly possessed formidable tricks; with his immense power, he was surprisingly unable to break free.

In fact, had Boss Tao not been so fat, he would have been hoisted up by this cage to hang aloft, just like the phoenix perched above him.

Naturally, Shui Huang'er was free.

She was seated upon a bird perch woven from white tree shadows, dangling her legs as she watched Boss Tao:

—"Toad Immortal, you'd best stop struggling. The more you thrash, the tighter it binds you. If you can't be gotten out in the end, it won't be easy for me to account to the Lord Immortal Venerable."

As she spoke, the cage actually constricted slightly, making Tao Zhu's bulky frame all the more cramped.

—"Who are you calling Toad Immortal?!"-Tao Zhu wailed miserably. Glaring furiously at Shui Huang'er, he demanded,-"Treating us like this—just what are you plotting?!"

—"Let's be reasonable here. That you ended up like this is entirely your own fault,"-Shui Huang'er scoffed.

Tao Zhu blinked his beady little eyes, briefly betraying a guilty expression.

The reason he had been cooped up in a place like this like a barnyard fowl was that one moment he had been suspecting the demons of vile schemes and urging Untiring to be cautious, and the next moment he had noticed a faint "loophole" in the flow of spiritual energy along both sides of the corridor—a loophole that seemed to allow one to slip into the forbidden grounds ahead of time and snatch the secret treasure.

Boss Tao was certainly no fledgling fresh out of the nest; under circumstances like these, he naturally realized that the "loophole" could be a trap. Yet he looked again and again, perceived with divine sense time and after time, only to discover that the "loophole" was supremely compatible with his own cultivation methods and magical arts—it was a "loophole that only he could discover." Not to boast, but when it came to his signature secret skills, even Untiring might not be able to replicate them.

Conversely, so long as he took a tiny risk, he could slip through the "loophole" and claim the secret treasure a step ahead. When that happened, whatever traps these demons had prepared would be utterly powerless.

Reasoning thus, greed triumphed over intellect for a fleeting moment, and Tao Zhu executed a "minor trick."

Immediately afterward, he found himself locked inside a cage.

Untiring, standing practically within arm's reach, completely failed to notice Boss Tao's predicament. His entire attention was devoted to his two disciples; no matter how loudly Boss Tao screamed and hollered, Untiring remained completely oblivious.

—"This really was a trap!"-Boss Tao fumed. [Fierce of visage but faint of heart](# "Chinese Idiom: 色厉内荏 (sè lì nèi rěn) — outwardly fierce but inwardly cowardly; putting on a brave front"), he attempted to intimidate Shui Huang'er:-"Is it wise for you to scheme against Untiring and me like this? I don't matter—I'm merely the humble boss of the Abyssal Ocean Society with a bit of pull among major factions. But Untiring is not an entity you can provoke lightly! Setting aside his terrifying personal might, his social network is extraordinarily tangled. Don't be fooled by how he holed up at home all day; he maintains considerable ties with Sky City, the Heavenly Dao Alliance, and even the master of the Great Wilderness's 'Ghost Domain.' If anything happens to him on demon territory, it means you'll be going to war with the entire human race!"

Tao Zhu's tone was blatant bluffing, yet Shui Huang'er evidently bought it:

—"Although I previously thought the Lord Immortal Venerable was widely revered, I never imagined he commanded such staggering prestige. As expected of a sage of the human race; it seems my bet was not misplaced..."-She sank into deep thought, her voice trailing off to a murmur.

Boss Tao did not catch Shui Huang'er's final muttered remarks, but that did not hinder him from [borrowing the tiger's terror](# "Chinese Idiom: 狐假虎威 (hú jiǎ hǔ wēi) — the fox borrowing the tiger's might; bullying others by flaunting powerful connections").

—"Then why aren't you hurrying to let us out?!"-he barked, puffing out his potbelly.

—"What do you mean 'let you out'? You make it sound as if I captured you,"-Shui Huang'er scoffed.

—"Are you saying you didn't?!"-Tao Zhu grabbed the bars around the cage.

—"Our intention in presenting the Supreme Treasure is entirely sincere,"-Shui Huang'er said in earnest.-"It's just that since it's the 'Supreme Treasure' of our demon race, we can hardly hand it over willy-nilly! Even the treasure itself possesses great dignity; naturally, it must see whether its prospective master is worthy of it."

—"Then what I am right now is..."-Tao Zhu realized something, his complexion turning sour.

—"Failed the trial of ownership,"-Shui Huang'er stated bluntly.-"The treasure's trial is very common—nothing more than amplifying the desires at the bottom of a living creature's heart, forcing anyone who approaches it to bare their true soul. Therefore, the moment you manifested greed, you were already disqualified."

Saying so, Shui Huang'er shot Tao Zhu a contemptuous glance.

Greedy fellows like this were commonplace among both demons and humans; Shui Huang'er had seen plenty of them.

Had it not been out of deference to the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Shui Huang'er would not have bothered to explain so much.

Boss Tao could not care less about Shui Huang'er's contempt.

He knew his own nature better than anyone. Having cultivated all the way to the Mahayana stage, Boss Tao had long since mastered the art of confronting his less-than-flattering personality traits with absolute equanimity, even treating them as virtues.

Yet this time, he was consumed by bitter regret! Regret that he had been so tantalizingly close to obtaining the Supreme Treasure!

Why couldn't he have held back just a little longer?

That was the Supreme Treasure of the demon race!

Boss Tao was so wracked with regret he nearly wept aloud.

Yet he paused, sensing something was amiss.

—"You said it triggers 'desire,' but does Untiring look anything like someone plagued by desire?"-Boss Tao accused.-"You aren't playing favorites, are you?"

[I'd love to play favorites, but...]

Shui Huang'er sighed:

—"The Supreme Treasure's influence is completely impartial; even I have no way to alter it. Right now, the Lord Immortal Venerable is indeed being troubled by his own desire. It's just that his greatest desire at this moment happens to be concern for his disciples, which is why his attention is entirely focused on them. Haven't you noticed he's completely forgotten you exist?"

[You didn't need to say that part out loud!]

The flesh on Tao Zhu's jowls quivered, and he couldn't help but question the sincerity of his friendship with Untiring.

—"His foremost desire is actually the safety of his disciples—as expected of the Lord Immortal Venerable,"-Shui Huang'er murmured, feeling all the more vindicated in her decision.

Since the Lord Immortal Venerable cared so deeply for his disciples, would demons who received his pedagogy count as disciples too? She truly had brilliant foresight!

—"No wonder he could foster such a wonderful person like Little Sister Mo Bei,"-Shui Huang'er said delightedly.

[This phoenix seems to harbor questionable designs on Untiring's disciple, and her romantic inclinations seem a bit peculiar too.]

Tao Zhu snidely roasted her in his heart with detached indifference.

Still, now that he had grasped the situation and understood that he was officially disqualified, he couldn't be bothered to waste any more mental energy. Instead, he [let himself rot](# "Modern slang: 摆烂 (bǎilàn) — letting things rot; giving up and doing nothing productive") and plopped down, waiting to see how Untiring would crack this puzzle.

If it was Untiring, he should be able to break the deadlock successfully... right?

Just as Tao Zhu was pondering this, he saw Untiring sit down cross-legged in front of Mo Xi over on the other side.

—Naturally, without Zhou Hui's authorization, neither Boss Tao nor Shui Huang'er could perceive the "Heavenly Dao" with the naked eye. From their perspective, they merely saw Zhou Hui gazing with concern at his two disciples and casting protective spells over them, while the previously agitated Mo Xi quietly began cultivating, evidently consolidating his cultivation base under his master's guidance.

Yet Untiring now suddenly sitting cross-legged himself, looking as if he were about to meditate, was rather inexplicable.

He did not look like he was transferring power to his disciple.

—"Haha! I knew it! This should be Untiring's top-priority desire after all!"-Seeing this, Tao Zhu blinked in surprise before bursting into hearty laughter.-"No matter how addicted he is to raising kids, this is indeed his number-one priority!"

—"What are you talking about?"-Shui Huang'er asked, utterly baffled.

—"Cultivation, of course!"-Tao Zhu said smugly.

[—"The one desire Untiring can never refuse—it really could only be this!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

* * *

[—"Master?"](# "Speaker: 'Mo Xi'")

"Mo Xi" called out once more.

Seeing his master suddenly sit down to meditate, he too was evidently somewhat puzzled.

—"Wait a bit longer; let me study this thoroughly,"-Zhou Hui replied upon hearing him, dangling the 'slender threads' in his hand.

Indeed, in the end, he had not decisively snipped these 'slender threads' to crudely tear that shoddy script to pieces; instead, he took this opportunity to study them.

Zhou Hui felt this was a golden opportunity to research how to code and script the Heavenly Dao.

After all, hadn't he searched for the Heavenly Scripture in the first place for this very purpose? This was naturally of the utmost priority.

"Mo Xi," however, appeared somewhat anxious:

—"Yet isn't this the wrong time? Weren't the demons going to present the Supreme Treasure to you? We cannot afford to waste time here... Look, Her Reverence the Saintess is waiting for you as well."

He pointed toward "Shui Huang'er."

Yet it was not the Shui Huang'er perched on the bird stand above Tao Zhu's head, but rather the "Shui Huang'er" wrapped in threads of destiny like a cocoon.

—"You desire the Supreme Treasure dearly, don't you?"-"Mo Xi" coaxed enticingly.-"That is what we came here for."

To this, Zhou Hui merely replied with cool detachment:

—"Right now, this matter is more important. Let them wait."

—"Didn't you hear what the demon Saintess said? This was part of the trial all along."

Zhou Hui did not speak via divine sense this time, and his gaze swept deliberately or inadvertently across Tao Zhu and Shui Huang'er above him.

* * *

Shui Huang'er's eyes widened in shock, and she slapped a hand over her mouth.

Boss Tao froze:

[—"You could see me this whole time?!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

In that split second, Boss Tao entertained the murderous urge to strangle Untiring to death—if only he had the strength to beat him.
