# Chapter 72: The Essence of New Year Celebrations Lies in Awkward Small Talk (第72章 过年的精髓在尬聊)

—"Wait a moment. This year's annual gathering isn't being hosted by me, is it?"-Zhou Hui asked in confusion.

It was only three days ago, right after the Heavenly Spirit Sect's final exams concluded, that Zhou Hui received this request from his "old friends"—and even then, it had been forwarded through Master Lan Pei.

Had it not been for this communication, Zhou Hui would have long forgotten about any "annual gathering."

Or rather, it had never been important to him in the first place.

In previous years, Zhou Hui spent most of his time in secluded cultivation. Only on rare occasions when he emerged from seclusion to gather information and happened to cross paths with the New Year would he drop by to have a look.

In truth, most high-tier cultivators did the same.

After all, to evade lightning tribulations and karmic entanglements, cultivators at or above the Mahayana stage typically employed various methods of [deceiving the heavens](# "Literal CN: 瞒天过海 (mántiānguòhǎi) — crossing the sea under a false sky / masking one's presence from the heavenly order") to live in seclusion. If they had no specific sect, tracking them down was extraordinarily difficult—take Lan Pei and Tao Zhu, for instance; had they not delivered themselves to his doorstep, even Zhou Hui would have had to expend considerable effort to locate them.

Thus, the function of the annual gathering was similar to a [neighborhood committee](# "Literal CN: 居委会 (jūwěihuì) — modern Chinese community residents' committee"). At the very least, it fixed a time and location so that whenever high-tier cultivators emerged from seclusion and wished to inquire about news, they had a place to go.

Yet for that very reason, not many people usually attended the annual gathering of high-tier cultivators.

At least whenever Zhou Hui went, he only ever saw a dozen or so cultivators, and those dozen were generally from a fixed set of prominent sects, such as the Plum-Bamboo Immortal Couple.

Zhou Hui had always suspected that these [normies](# "Literal CN: 现充 (xiànchōng) — modern slang for people living fulfilling social lives (from Japanese 'riajuu'); here referring to established elder cultivators") organized this gathering every year simply because they saw their younger sect disciples celebrating so boisterously and, being elders who couldn't awkwardly butt in, needed an excuse to mingle among themselves.

Consequently, each year's gathering was organized by only a few regular figures.

Someone like Zhou Hui would certainly never be the host.

—"If I recall, this year it is..."-Zhou Hui glanced at the hand gesture Lan Pei was making beside him before confirming,-"...hosted by Fairy Xia of Cloud City at the Cloud Pool?"

—"So you do remember! Then why haven't you shown up?"-the old fellow on the other side of the cloud mirror demanded, letting out a drunken hiccup.-"Don't give me that 'can't make it' excuse! Cloud City is right here in the Central Domain; for you, isn't it just a blink of an eye? Get over here! Right now!"

—"...I have business to attend to,"-Zhou Hui replied, utterly uncooperative.-"Besides, the New Year is still some time away, isn't it? Why have you already started drinking?"

—"Does that old freak ever have a single day all year round where he isn't drinking?"-Lan Pei chimed in dismissively from the side.-"He might as well drown dead in a wine vat."

—"Lan Pei is there too? Fine! You're ostracizing me!"-the figure Lan Pei called the 'old freak' bellowed.-"Just you wait, I'm coming over right now."

—"Wait..."-Zhou Hui knitted his brows.

He had no desire whatsoever to have a drunken sot barging onto Mount Tianling!

Yet the person across the cloud mirror had already changed:

[—"Senior Zhou..."](# "Speaker: Cultivator at Annual Gathering")

—"What is going on with you all?"-Zhou Hui asked with a frown.

Listening to the other person's explanation, Zhou Hui finally understood that this pack of [social butterflies](# "Literal CN: 社牛 (shèniú) — internet slang for extreme extroverts / socially fearless individuals") was already drinking away at the Jade Garden. As they chatted, the conversation inevitably drifted to the Immortals' Bazaar orchestrated by Zhou Hui and Tao Zhu, whereupon the drunken Mengshan Old Freak, fueled by alcohol, took it upon himself to harass Zhou Hui.

—"It is so rare for Senior Zhou to take an interest in organizing an event like this; everyone is terribly curious. We wanted to see Senior Zhou and ask what it feels like to re-enter the mortal world!"-the person on the other end said.

Zhou Hui remained unimpressed:

—"It feels like nothing special."

As expected, these people simply had too much free time on their hands. It was bad enough that they used him as gossip material, but now they were treating him like entertainment for their annual gathering!

Was him hosting a single auction really that novel?

Normally, given Zhou Hui's personality, he would have simply terminated the communication and ignored them entirely.

Yet Zhou Hui paused, a sudden mischievous impulse welling up within him.

[Since these old fellows have nothing better to do anyway, wouldn't it be better to put them to good use?]

—"Of course. If you genuinely wish to hear it, you can come pay New Year respects to me,"-Zhou Hui said, his eyes curving into a smile.

[—"Ah?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator at Annual Gathering")

The other side froze.

—"Is that not how it works?"-Zhou Hui said deliberately.-"If you want advice, visiting in person naturally shows proper sincerity! Not to mention, during New Year, juniors pay respects to seniors. Who among you is worthy of receiving New Year respects from me?"

—"You're the one who said it, Senior Untiring!"-another female immortal took over across the cloud mirror, laughing charmingly.-"When we arrive at Mount Tianling, you can't throw us out!"

Without waiting for Zhou Hui's reply, she turned her head toward the others and shouted:

—"Senior Untiring says he will play the host—!"

A chorus of excited cheers erupted from the other end of the cloud mirror, and then the communication abruptly cut off.

Seeing this, Lan Pei chuckled with a grin:

—"You've [poked a hornet's nest](# "Literal CN: 捅了马蜂窝 (tǒng le mǎfēngwō) — stirring up trouble / provoking a swarm") now. Every year, their gathering is either at the Cloud Pool or the Jade Garden; they've been bored out of their skulls for ages. Now that they hear they can trash a new place, of course they'll swarm right over."

—"That is precisely why I let them come,"-Zhou Hui smiled.-"It just so happens to give everyone something to do."

He then glanced at Lan Pei:

—"Speaking of which, Master Lan Pei, you have stayed at our Heavenly Spirit Sect for quite a while now, haven't you? Would you be interested in doing something else?"

"?!"

Lan Pei twitched his hidden tail, suddenly overcome with an ominous premonition.

***

Regardless of whether Zhou Hui had acted on a whim or harbored ulterior motives, entertaining the topmost titans of the cultivation realm certainly could not be handled carelessly.

—Even if Zhou Hui still appeared rather indifferent on the surface.

He assigned Mo Xi to oversee guest hospitality, then rummaged through the sect warehouse to prepare several special artifacts for Mo Bei, sending her to Blood Demon Hall to swap Lu Shulong back to cook.

Learning of this arrangement, Qu Qianxing found it rather odd:

—"Under normal circumstances, isn't Eldest Senior Brother usually in charge of banquets?"

He specifically glanced at the "Eldest Senior Brother" trailing behind Zhou Hui like a [door god](# "Literal CN: 门神 (ménshén) — guardian deity pasted on entrance doors; here, standing guard stoutly and silently"), wondering why Eldest Senior Brother hadn't clamored about Master playing favorites this time, nor actively volunteered for any task.

Zhou Hui patted the back of the "lamb" in his arms:

—"The mounts of this batch of guests will be exceptionally precious. I am worried he will sneak off and eat them."

[—"Ying!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Lamb form)")

[Who would eat them?!]

The little lamb let out a bleat of protest, though it had zero intention of moving.

Having fought so hard to secure the most coveted "seat" in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, why would he ever do manual labor?

Yuwen Xuan felt he could happily remain a spiritual pet for the rest of his life!

Nevertheless, Zhou Hui's reasoning dispelled Qu Qianxing's doubts.

Although Eldest Senior Brother was remarkably skilled when it came to managing divine beasts, having him look after the mounts of supreme cultivators genuinely felt like [having a wolf herd the sheep](# "Literal CN: 让狼放羊 (ràng láng fàng yáng) — setting a wolf to guard sheep / trusting a predator with prey"). Fifth Junior Brother was indeed far more reassuring.

Thus, Zhou Hui brought Mo Bei to the warehouse to select five items:

A Six Realms Soul-Guiding Lantern, a spool of Ten-Thousand-Crystal Puppet Silk, a dozen Five Domains Teleportation Talismans, a bottle of Nine-Revolution Soul-Restoring Powder, and a Thousand-Thread Star-Guiding Pearl.

Among them, the first two were weapons for Ran Deng and Guan Ye, granting them the means to protect themselves in moments of crisis.

The latter three were life-saving measures in the event of unforeseen disaster: the teleportation talismans could return them to Mount Tianling in an instant; the soul-restoring powder could snatch a life back from the brink so long as the soul hadn't dispersed; and the star-guiding pearl ensured that even if they were tragically cast adrift into other minor worlds, Zhou Hui could still pinpoint their coordinates and retrieve them.

—With Ran Deng and Guan Ye already present, the reason Zhou Hui insisted on having the more combat-capable Lu Shulong and Mo Bei guard the site was naturally not a lack of trust in Blood Demon Hall, but concern over 'other factors.'

After all, that was the place where someone had deliberately set a trap to capture the Taotie. Who knew in what manner the mastermind behind that trap might appear?

Therefore, a baseline level of vigilance was imperative.

Zhou Hui also instructed Mo Bei:

—"If an unusual situation truly arises, report back immediately. Do not try to be brave."

—"Yes, Master,"-Mo Bei nodded.

As long as it didn't involve the bizarre script of the Heavenly Dao or some so-called "Destined Person," Mo Bei was quite reliable in every aspect.

She had even taken first place on this term's final exams.

Zhou Hui swapped Mo Bei and Lu Shulong with complete peace of mind.

And by the time Zhou Hui had his disciples finish basic preparations, the guests had already arrived outside the mountain gate of Mount Tianling.

***

—"Hah—! Untiring is truly planning to take the Heavenly Spirit Sect to the big leagues!"-Mengshan Old Freak exclaimed, clutching his wine gourd.

As the saying goes, [birds of a feather flock together](# "Literal CN: 物以类聚 (wùyǐlèijù) — things of a kind come together"). Although Zhou Hui had never been fond of attending annual gatherings in the past, the members of the gatherings he did attend were mostly "freaks" like himself... no, legends.

After all, in the eyes of ordinary cultivators, the Untiring Immortal Venerable—who possessed Five-Element Spirit Roots yet forged his own cultivation method and blazed a path all the way to Tribulation Transcendence—was certainly "legendary" enough.

In fact, only Zhou Hui himself remained oblivious to it, but the "Jade Mountain Gathering" or "Cloud Pool Gathering" he attended was considered nothing short of a myth in the cultivation realm.

Many Mahayana cultivators dreamed of sneaking into this annual gathering, viewing it as proof of qualification among the "premier" elite cultivators.

Zhou Hui cared little for fame. Those he associated with—even as mere nodding acquaintances—were naturally figures akin to himself.

Therefore, the annual gatherings he attended never included the kind of "Mahayana cultivators" churned out through medicinal pills like those in the Palace of Carefree Wandering; they were either powerhouses who had genuinely relied on their own prowess, or experts who commanded peerless unique crafts.

And Mengshan Old Freak was one such figure.

Despite being called an "old freak," Mengshan Old Freak's appearance was merely that of a seven- or eight-year-old child. Seated atop the back of a sika deer whose antlers bloomed with dazzling flowers, he looked exceedingly adorable. Judging solely by appearances, no one could have ever guessed that this toddler was already at Mahayana Great Perfection, let alone a master of spiritual botany.

Arriving before the mountain gate of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Mengshan Old Freak first cast a surprised glance at the sundial clock in the sky and the inverted reflection of Mount Tianling, then poured a bit of wine from his gourd beneath the peach tree at the mountain gate:

—"Little Peach Immortal, looks like you'll have your work cut out for you from now on!"

*Pop~*

The sound of blossoms unfurling echoed from the branches.

This peach tree, which had begun blooming during the autumn minor intake and had not ceased even into the winter break, burst forth with even more blossoms. Furthermore, the petals transformed from pale pink to vivid crimson, glowing bright red as though flushed with wine.

—"Senior Mengshan, you're recklessly feeding someone else's child again. Careful or Senior Untiring will settle scores with you!"

The one who arrived immediately after Mengshan Old Freak was an old woman riding upon a multi-colored feathered garment, known to the world as "Fairy Xia."

Her voice was sweet and charming, resembling that of a young maiden, yet she addressed everyone as "Senior" at every turn—not only calling Zhou Hui senior, but even addressing Mengshan, a Mahayana powerhouse with the appearance of a toddler, as senior. It sounded exceedingly bizarre.

However, this Fairy Xia was also the builder of Cloud City, the largest immortal metropolis in the Central Domain. Although she no longer managed affairs, she offered her proudest creation, the Cloud Pool, to entertain high-tier cultivators every year, enjoying immense popularity in the upper echelons of the cultivation realm.

Hearing her accuse him, Mengshan Old Freak chuckled slyly:

—"How could wine from my cellar ever harm a living being? If you don't believe me, go ask your little lotus blossoms and see what they say!"

—"You still dare mention that?!"-Fairy Xia flared up at once.-"Just because you poured in far too much wine last time, my lotus pool turned into some rainforest secret realm, nearly swallowing my disciple whole!"

—"That's just your disciple being utterly useless,"-Mengshan Old Freak curled his lips.

Seeing that Fairy Xia seemed ready to nag him further, he hurriedly waved his hand:

—"I won't bicker with you over this at someone else's doorstep. Since we're already here, let's call Untiring to open the gates!"

—"Wait a bit longer; let us wait until everyone arrives,"-Fairy Xia said instead.-"The mist grand array laid out before Senior Untiring's gates is extraordinarily subtle. I worry some seniors won't be able to make it through."

—"If they can't even get past this, what kind of 'seniors' are they?"-the old freak sneered.

Sitting backwards on his sika deer, he peered behind him.

The mist before Mount Tianling was meant to obscure outsiders; once one actually reached the mountain gate and looked outward, not a single wisp of fog was visible. Hence, the view here was exceptionally clear. In the distance, one could see swarms of retinues riding dragons, riding phoenixes, and riding carriages drawn by all manner of rare beasts and exotic fowl soaring through the sky—an absolute [wild demonic riot](# "Literal CN: 魔神乱舞 (móshén luànwǔ) — chaotic pandemonium of gods and demons").

Mengshan Old Freak laughed at the spectacle:

—"Good grief, how lively! Still... why does it feel like there are way more people attending this gathering than in previous years? What's more, every single one of them has brought a flock of juniors along to [broaden their horizons](# "Literal CN: 涨见识 (zhǎng jiànshí) — expand experience / gain knowledge of the world"). It never felt like this in previous years!"

Indeed, previous gatherings had always consisted of familiar faces. This year, however, saw the addition of many Mahayana cultivators from local Central Domain sects—to say nothing of the juniors they brought in tow.

Even the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself likely hadn't anticipated that there would be so many people.

Fairy Xia glanced back and chuckled softly:

—"Of course there are many people. They came specifically for Senior Untiring; how could the crowd not be large?"

—"Huh?"-Mengshan Old Freak blinked in bewilderment.-"But Untiring had no intention of attending the gathering in the first place!"

After all, it had been their side that proactively reached out to him.

—"Sigh, as expected, Senior Mengshan is rather naive,"-Fairy Xia laughed coquettishly.-"Do you really think bringing up Senior Untiring out of the blue was just a coincidence? It was blatantly deliberate! Even egging you on to contact Senior Untiring was almost certainly done with ulterior motives!"

Mengshan Old Freak's round eyes widened.

Carefully recalling the course of events while drinking earlier when everyone suddenly brought up the Untiring Immortal Venerable, he indeed sensed subtle traces of manipulation.

Mengshan Old Freak slapped his thigh:

—"Dammit! This old man was actually used!"

Mengshan Old Freak glared at Fairy Xia:

—"Since you knew, why didn't you warn me?!"

—"Because I wanted to see Senior Untiring as well!"-Fairy Xia said with a beaming smile.-"In truth, even if the others hadn't brought it up, I would have steered the conversation in that direction sooner or later."

However, that so-called "business" was hardly anything major. Everyone merely wanted to fool around in good fun; even if Senior Untiring hadn't come, it wouldn't have mattered.

Yet bringing up the Untiring Immortal Venerable so impatiently during the gathering felt far too rushed!

Could someone with ulterior motives have steered the topic?

Furthermore, Senior Untiring agreeing so readily to let everyone come to Mount Tianling was hardly his usual style.

[I hope nothing unpleasant happens right in the middle of New Year!]

Fairy Xia thought worriedly to herself.

Mengshan Old Freak had always been oblivious to social atmosphere, so naturally he couldn't deduce so much from an ordinary conversation.

He merely glared at Fairy Xia in confusion:

—"You all have business with Untiring? What business?"

—"That is... ah, everyone has practically arrived. Let us knock on the gate!"-whether deliberately or not, Fairy Xia left her sentence unfinished, glancing behind her before letting out a soft laugh.

She then paid no heed to Mengshan Old Freak, who was scratching his ears and cheeks in frustration, and simply extended her hand.

It was a hand as fair and tender as that of a young maiden.

With that hand, she plucked a wisp of golden-red cloud, folded it several times, transformed it into a visiting petition, and sent it flying into the mountain gate.

With the arrival of this visiting petition, the peach blossom gate swung open in response, welcoming the immortals within.

And thus unfolded that very scene of the "gathering of immortals paying homage" that left the disciples struck dumb in awe.

***

Upon sensing the sheer number of visitors paying respects to the mountain, Zhou Hui was indeed taken aback.

He had assumed it would be a modest gathering of a dozen familiar acquaintances; who could have imagined an entire horde arriving with their whole families in tow? Could they have come purely to mooch New Year red envelopes?

And after Mengshan Old Freak relayed Fairy Xia's remark about having "business," Zhou Hui felt even more puzzled.

He looked at the crowd and asked:

—"Business? What business do you all have with me?"

No one answered.

The atmosphere inexplicably froze into silence.

Fairy Xia merely shot a glare at Mengshan Old Freak, somewhat regretting having teased him before the mountain gate. Who would have thought he would report the entire conversation directly to Senior Untiring?

Yet "that matter" truly was a trivial, insignificant trifle.

Had it been like before—mentioned casually amidst drunken laughter and flushed cheeks at a banquet—it would have been quite amusing. But claiming to have come specifically for it made it seem rather impolite, perhaps even utterly bizarre.

Of course, given their levels of cultivation, being a bit bizarre hardly mattered.

What truly made everyone hesitant to speak was Senior Untiring himself!

Those familiar with the Untiring Immortal Venerable knew his personality was not cold; in fact, he was even rather warm-hearted. But to those unfamiliar with him, standing before an immortal with silver hair, porcelain skin, icy heart, and jade bones—resembling a celestial figure sculpted from ice and snow—even if he did not put on an aloof facade, ordinary people would subconsciously dread offending him.

Furthermore, Fairy Xia felt that after not seeing him for a time, the Untiring Immortal Venerable's accumulated prestige seemed even heavier.

Previously, she had merely felt he was difficult to approach. Seeing him across a cloud mirror was fine, but standing in the flesh before the Untiring Immortal Venerable, even when those pale eyes gazed over with a smile, it compelled one to reflect on whether they had committed some grave error.

Fairy Xia had no idea that if she voiced these sentiments aloud, every single disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect would surely cling to her thighs in fervent agreement:

[—"Exactly, exactly! The Lord Immortal Venerable is just that majestic!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Especially in the exam hall! I don't dare glance toward the proctor's podium even once!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

[—"Lately, he feels even more terrifying!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciple")

Although cultivators not belonging to the Heavenly Spirit Sect had never endured the trials of the examination halls, they were inevitably affected by that lingering aura of dread.

This was also the second mistake made by Fairy Xia and the others:

They truly should have come alone like Mengshan Old Freak.

Had it been a simple reunion of old friends, the atmosphere would have been far more relaxed. But bringing a flock of disciples made the occasion feel stiff and formal, while low-stage disciples couldn't bear the spiritual pressure radiating from Zhou Hui, holding their breath in muted silence and driving the atmosphere into an even deeper freeze.

Yet Zhou Hui appeared completely oblivious to this awkward chill. Instead, he swept his gaze across the crowd, recognizing the faces of numerous behind-the-scenes patriarchs from various Central Domain sects.

His smile deepened:

—"Could it be that everyone has come to [hold me to account](# "Literal CN: 问罪 (wènzuì) — demand an accounting / bring someone to task for an offense")?"

"?!"

—"Because the Immortals' Bazaar I organized and the subsequent schools encroached upon your sects' spheres of influence, you have come to hold Zhou to account?"-Zhou Hui smiled.

Faced with such an inquiry from the Untiring Immortal Venerable, quite a few people broke out into a cold sweat.

—"How could that be?!"-someone immediately defended themselves.-"Senior Untiring organizing the auction was a wonderful deed! It resolved our [pressing crisis](# "Literal CN: 燃眉之急 (ránméi zhī jí) — emergency so urgent it singes one's eyebrows")! How could we ever blame you?"

[—"I was in secluded cultivation earlier; once I found out what my brats had done, I wanted nothing more than to break their legs!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Patriarch")

[—"Indeed! As fellow Central Domain cultivators, we are absolutely on your side!"](# "Speaker: Central Domain Mahayana Patriarch")

The Mahayana titans of the Central Domain voiced their loyalty in a flurry of overlapping declarations.

Their attitude left the flock of juniors behind them dumbfounded.

Given their cultivation levels, while they might not oversee daily affairs in their respective sects, their influence was colossal. Belonging to the untouchable rank of 'founding ancestors,' they typically stood high above the clouds like true immortals, figures ordinary disciples feared to offend with even a single glance. Who could have imagined they would be so subservient before another cultivator?

Was it merely because of his Tribulation Transcendence realm?

Or was it because he was the "Untiring Immortal Venerable"?

The sect masters of those sects turned pale, subconsciously re-evaluating their perception of the Untiring Immortal Venerable and the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Zhou Hui merely maintained a faint, detached smile.

He knew full well that these apologies only materialized after he successfully hosted the auction and reached his tentacles into these sects' territories. Had there been no auction, these "founding ancestors" would never have proactively delivered their faces to him.

This world was just that pragmatic.

—"What is the point of saying all this now?!"-Mengshan Old Freak couldn't bear to watch and shouted.-"If you knew you were fellow Central Domain cultivators, you should have stood on Untiring's side from the very start... Ah, wait a minute, how is the Palace of Carefree Wandering involved in this again?"

Mengshan Old Freak asked with delayed realization.

It turned out he had been completely ignorant of the whole story behind the auction, merely tagging along and causing chaos after hearing others talk about it.

Yet that did not prevent Mengshan Old Freak from naturally inserting himself into the matter.

He rolled up his sleeves:

—"Untiring, are you going to beat up Carefree Palace again? Count me in this time! Let's play together!"

[Is the Palace of Carefree Wandering something you just beat up for fun?!]

Everyone present wore bizarre expressions, thinking in unison.

Fairy Xia hurriedly stepped in to smooth things over:

—"Senior Mengshan, Senior Untiring did not mean that! Right, Senior Untiring?"

—"How does the saying go? [Beating the kids during New Year—doing it just because one has time to kill](# "Literal CN: 过年打孩子，闲着也是闲着 — a folk idiom meaning finding an excuse to punish someone just out of boredom or convenience"),"-Zhou Hui said with a beaming smile.

"!"

[You're truly going to beat them up?!]

—"Unfortunately, Carefree Palace is not my child, so they are not on my turf... or rather, they shouldn't be on my turf, should they?"-Zhou Hui swept his gaze across everyone once more, asking in a slow, deliberate tone.

—"Of course not! They are in the Eastern Domain,"-at this moment, the Plum-Bamboo Immortal Couple caught on under the frantic winks of Fairy Xia—whose eyelids were practically spasming—and chimed in with smiles,-"They cannot make it to the Central Domain for the time being."

—"Indeed. If they come to the Central Domain, our Heavenly Dao Alliance will stand entirely on your side,"-Xin Zhu added.

[...You two are here to [fan the flames](# "Literal CN: 拱火 (gǒnghuǒ) — incite trouble / stoke the fire") too, aren't you!]

Fairy Xia thought in sheer exhaustion, gazing nervously toward Zhou Hui.

The Untiring Immortal Venerable seemed to give face to the Plum-Bamboo couple, letting the matter slide:

—"True enough. I suppose I can only let them live a few years longer."

Live a few years longer...

—"Cough, cough! The auction!"-Fairy Xia blurted out abruptly. At this point, she no longer cared whether it was appropriate; changing the subject was top priority, lest this figure actually run off to thrash the neighbor's unruly brat during New Year.-"We were discussing the auction earlier. While there was a slight misunderstanding, Senior Untiring, what you did was truly magnificent! There was only one small thing that everyone complained about."

—"Complained?"-Zhou Hui finally looked toward Fairy Xia, whose words were jumping all over the place.

—"Yes, complained!"-Fairy Xia emphasized.-"What you did was simply too unfair! That is why everyone was so dissatisfied and gathered together. That was the 'business' I mentioned to Senior Mengshan!"

—"Oh? What did I do that was unfair?"-Zhou Hui smiled.

—"Immortal Chess!"

Fairy Xia finally blurted it out:

—"How could you determine the roster for the 'Representative Cultivators of the Central Domain' based on a single auction? Our Cloud City is the very first to object!"

As she spoke, she looked toward the high-tier cultivators of the various Central Domain sects.

Those cultivators were still immersed in the tense atmosphere from earlier; how could they dare utter a word? As a result, Fairy Xia had to call them out one by one:

—"You complained about it earlier too, didn't you, Patriarch Wu?"

—"Right... our sect didn't get a single piece... though if the Lord Immortal Venerable thinks we don't deserve one, then forget it..."-Patriarch Wu stammered.

—"Excellency Qingyang?"

—"Ah, well, I feel capping the selection at the Soul Formation stage is a bit unfair,"-Excellency Qingyang ventured.

—"Burning Heaven Sword Venerable?"

—"Selecting representative cultivators solely through a chess tournament seems a bit off... but if the Immortal Venerable thinks so..."-Burning Heaven Sword Venerable mumbled.

The founding ancestors present once again wore utterly flustered and embarrassed expressions.

Everyone thought to themselves: Fairy Xia was usually so perceptive and considerate; why was she so completely unreliable today? Those complaints regarding Immortal Chess were merely meant to serve as a bridge to find an opportunity to mend relations with the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Who truly cared about... alright, thinking carefully about it, they actually cared quite a bit!

Especially after understanding the rules and seeing the chess pieces, discovering that their own sect didn't have a single one!

Or discovering that only their juniors had pieces.

Did they, the founding ancestors, not deserve one?!

It was just that bringing up this matter under these circumstances felt awkward no matter how one looked at it.

Looking at this bunch, Mengshan Old Freak couldn't help but blurt out:

—"Just for *this*?! Are you all a bunch of [brain-dead morons](# "Literal CN: 沙雕 (shādiāo) — internet slang for goofy, ridiculous idiots / silly fools")?!"
