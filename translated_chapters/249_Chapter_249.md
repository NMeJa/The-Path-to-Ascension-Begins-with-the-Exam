# Chapter 249: It's Over

This wait lasted several minutes.

At first, the Demon Sovereign merely raised an eyebrow, a mocking sneer still plastered across his face. But gradually, the sarcasm drained away. His expression turned solemn, and the swirling demonic qi began to recede. Next, his gaze grew distant and blank as he began muttering under his breath. Finally, his brows knitted tightly together, his eyes shut, and he seemed to enter a state of deep meditation.

[—"He's hooked."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Yeah, completely hooked."](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

The disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect agreed in unison.

They could tell at a single glance: Second Senior Brother had entered his "study mode." For the time being, he wouldn't spare them a second thought.

Still, waiting around like fools wasn't an option either.

Ran Deng turned her gaze toward their side's Qu Qianxing:

—"Second Senior Brother, since you've 'convinced yourself,' isn't it about time to destroy this whole mess? You can't seriously be planning to feed it to Eldest Senior Brother, can you?"

She pointed toward the Blood Jade.

—"Let me examine how this thing was crafted,"-Qu Qianxing also lapsed into contemplation. Fortunately, he didn't dwell in that state for long; after all, the taboo lore of the demon race held little allure for him.-"I have a general grasp of it now. A crooked craft relying on bloodlines, an existence manifested through sacrifice. Creating it is easy, and destroying it is just as simple. It's just..."

Qu Qianxing's hesitating gaze lingered upon the young man within the Blood Jade, before turning with deep guilt toward Mo Bei:

—"If the Blood Jade is destroyed, the one inside..."

After all, that was flesh and blood shed from Mo Bei's own body!

—"Tch, having a bleeding heart *now*?"-Guan Ye sneered coldly.

—"I don't care,"-Mo Bei said frigidly. Looking at the youth whose facial features resembled her own, rather than being unfeeling, she felt disgusted. Thinking about how she and Mo Xi had likely been born in a similar manner, she only wanted to dispose of him as quickly as possible.-"If Second Senior Brother doesn't want to do it, I can do it myself."

—"...Then I'd really be utter scum,"-Qu Qianxing smiled bitterly.

He had exploited his Youngest Junior Sister, yet now he had personally abandoned the scheme, only to expect her to slaughter that child with her own hands. A self like that wouldn't atone for his sins even if he died ten thousand times over.

—"Even right now, you're scum enough,"-Guan Ye mocked bluntly. Yet he too rubbed his temples as he looked toward the Blood Jade.-"Killing him outright is indeed somewhat inhumane. The kid hasn't even truly lived! How about I refine him into a ghost servant for Little Junior Sister? It could be considered a form of returning..."

—"No! That's disgusting!"-Mo Bei let out a horrified wail.

—"Fourth Senior Brother, your standards are downright repulsive,"-even Lu Shulong couldn't resist roasting him.

Thus, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect fell into a quandary. None of them wanted Mo Bei to personally kill her own child, yet having someone else dispose of their junior sister's offspring was just as bizarre. For a moment, they were at a complete loss.

—"Um... are you talking about the Blood Jade?"-at this moment, Qingyang Qingyu suddenly asked in a quavering voice.

She possessed no memories of past reincarnations—even if she did, having died long before the demon invasion in those cycles, she wouldn't have possessed profound realizations anyway. Consequently, she and Jian Nuo had quietly remained in the corner all along, watching the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples squabble in utter incomprehension.

Yet seeing them debate how to destroy that Blood Jade (and the young man within), Qingyang Qingyu couldn't help but voice her own plea.

Gazing deeply at the Blood Jade, she felt an inexplicable palpitation in her heart:

—"Once it's dealt with, could you give me a piece? I... seem to feel something?"-Qingyang Qingyu couldn't explain the origin of her sensation; she could only attribute it to her thirst for knowledge.

—"Ah, if it's too precious, I can pay for it... I will pay any price,"-Qingyang Qingyu added hurriedly.

—"That's right, that's right! Any price is fine, I'll pay it!"-Jian Nuo also stared at the Blood Jade with a tangled expression, feeling an inexplicable reluctance to part with it.

—"..."-Hearing this, Qu Qianxing fixed them with a profound, solemn gaze.

He had never anticipated that without recovering their past-life memories, both Jian Nuo and Qingyang Qingyu would react this way. This was undoubtedly because the Blood Jade had been condensed from their daughter. He just hadn't expected that even with her human form long erased, and with Qingyang and Jian Nuo lacking any recollection, they would still share an instinctual resonance with their "daughter."

Although scheming against Sky City hadn't originally been part of the Demon Sovereign's core agenda—being merely a sub-plot to breach the Heavenly Court's restrictions over Langhuan—Qu Qianxing hadn't stopped the Heavenly Court's conspiracy once he learned of it. Instead, he had gone with the flow, mercilessly using Jian Nuo's cornered daughter and refining her into the Blood Jade.

[What an absolute scoundrel I was!]

Qu Qianxing closed his eyes, then reopened them, speaking in a heavy tone:

—"The Blood Jade was forged specifically to seal the Celestial Emperor. The power contained within is terrifyingly vast, far beyond what any mortal vessel can withstand. Furthermore, the Blood Jade and the...'person' inside are merged too deeply; they cannot be completely separated."

—"Then..."-Qingyang Qingyu knitted her brows upon hearing this.

She didn't want to give up.

—"Hear me out,"-Qu Qianxing insisted firmly.-"I currently have a way to extract the Jade Essence of the Blood Jade and give it to you. However, the soul of that young man will be mixed in as well. Direct absorption is impossible. But... if in the future you marry someone and give birth to a child, the Jade Essence and the youth will be born as your children, carrying away a portion of the power. At that point, the remaining power can be utilized by you."

[And that way, you can also avoid dying in childbirth.]

—"C-Children?!"-Qingyang Qingyu exclaimed in shock.-"That's a bit..."

—"Yeah! Talking about children and all, isn't it way too early for that?"-Jian Nuo blushed furiously.

Qu Qianxing shot him a look reserved for fools. Even after recovering the memories of several reincarnation cycles, he held zero tact toward this sworn brother of convenience:

—"That depends entirely on the mother, including whom she chooses as the father. None of this has anything to do with you."

—"Ack, why are you like this?!"-Jian Nuo whined aggrievedly.

—"However, before the children are born, you will be corroded by the formidable power of the Blood Jade, enduring excruciating agony. And after childbirth, the vast majority of that power will be carried off by those two children. To accept this Blood Jade merely for the meager dregs of power left behind is a profoundly uneconomical transaction,"-Qu Qianxing looked earnestly at Qingyang Qingyu.-"Are you certain you wish to proceed?"

—"..."-Hearing his words, Qingyang Qingyu did not answer immediately.

When it came to giving birth to two children, any normal person would hesitate.

Qu Qianxing sighed. He didn't press her, but prepared himself to destroy the Blood Jade. After all, he was the one who stirred up this karma; it was only natural that he tie up the loose ends.

Yet Qingyang Qingyu did not waver for long. She had always been a decisive person:

—"Please, give it to me!"-Qingyang Qingyu said softly, pressing a hand to her chest.-"I don't know why, but I feel that if I let it go, I will regret it sooner or later."

—"..."-Seeing her unwavering resolve, Qu Qianxing hesitated instead.

It felt as if the sins he had committed were being shouldered entirely by this innocent woman who knew nothing of the truth.

—"But..."-Qu Qianxing started to say, but Jian Nuo suddenly cried out as if struck by a revelation:

—"Wait a second! Does it have to be Qingyu?! You can transfer the Jade Essence of the Blood Jade to me! Let me bear this power! My physique is far sturdier than Qingyu's, so it shouldn't matter!"

—"Then prepare to be torn to shreds by that power! Men don't possess the capability to gestate children. You cannot convert the Blood Jade into new life and give birth to it,"-Qu Qianxing replied coldly.

—"That's not necessarily true,"-Yuwen Xuan chimed in abruptly, pawing at the little gray lamb with his hoof, adopting the tone of someone who wanted nothing more than to stir up chaos.-"Speaking of which, I know an obscure gender-reversal cultivation technique that allows men to bear children. Wouldn't that solve the problem completely?"

—"Ugh,"-Jian Nuo's face turned deathly pale. Yet glancing at Qingyang Qingyu who was staring at him, he braced himself nonetheless:-"If... if there's no other way, I... I can give it a shot..."

—"Give it a shot my foot,"-Qu Qianxing sneered.-"Though it does remind me: we could actually split the Jade Essence and the souls between a man and a woman, each bearing half the burden. The suffering wouldn't be nearly as severe, and since the once-unified souls naturally attract each other, the children could ultimately be born without issue."

*Hiss.* Jian Nuo revealed an intensely tempted expression.

If they proceeded according to Qu Qianxing's proposal, wouldn't that practically mean Qingyang Qingyu had to marry him?

Yet looking at Qingyang Qingyu's furrowed brow, Jian Nuo painfully rejected the proposal:

—"Wouldn't that be [taking advantage of someone's plight](# "Literal CN: 趁人之危 — taking advantage of someone when they are in danger/vulnerable")? I... I still can't bring myself to do that..."

—"No one forced you to,"-Qu Qianxing glanced sideways at him.-"What I meant was, I can take responsibility. This Blood Jade was born because of me in the first place..."

—"[A friend's wife is not to be toyed with](# "Literal CN: 朋友妻不可戏 — traditional idiom: one must never seduce or flirt with a friend's wife")!"-Jian Nuo bristled instantly, shouting in outrage.-"Qianxing, I misjudged you! How could you—"

—"Shut up!"-Qingyang Qingyu snapped. Rubbing her temples, she turned to Qu Qianxing.-"Stop teasing him, and stop testing me, 'Second Senior Brother.' I know you feel guilty and want to personally handle the Blood Jade, but doing so would be irresponsible to me, and equally irresponsible to the Blood Jade... If you truly wish to resolve this matter properly, entrust 'them' completely to me! If I truly give birth to children in the future, how about you act as their teacher and take charge of their future?"

—"..."-Qu Qianxing pursed his lips, falling silent.

Qingyang Qingyu broke into a triumphant smile. She knew she had convinced him.

On the contrary, Jian Nuo was still hesitant:

—"Qingyu, do you really want to bear it all by yourself? It sounds terribly painful..."

—"I won't know until I try,"-Qingyang Qingyu said resolutely. She glanced at Jian Nuo, breaking into a playful smirk.-"Isn't this a good thing for you? Who knows, maybe because I can't endure the torment, I'll want to rid myself of these two souls early by giving birth, and agree to be with you because of it!"

—"Am I, Jian Nuo, the kind of scoundrel who kicks people when they're down?!"-Jian Nuo proclaimed with righteous indignation.

[This guy is actually an idiot, isn't he?]

Both Qingyang Qingyu and Qu Qianxing thought simultaneously as they stared at Jian Nuo, exchanging a helpless look.

Regardless, Jian Nuo's opinion had never mattered anyway.

Qingyang Qingyu was about to finalize the matter with Qu Qianxing, but there was a gaze she simply could not ignore.

Qingyang looked over with a wry smile:

—"...Junior Sister Mo Bei, you've been staring at me this whole time. Do you have any objections?"

—"...I will also take responsibility,"-Mo Bei murmured slowly, not daring to meet Qingyang Qingyu's eyes.-"Senior Sister Qingyang, if you truly cannot bear that power, you can use me. After all, I possess an Extreme Yin Constitution. As a cultivation furnace, I can help you refine the power and ease the pain."

—"Th-That won't be necessary!"-Qingyang Qingyu was stunned, visibly flustered.-"I'm actually quite traditional. I can't really accept dual cultivation with another woman, especially treating you as a furnace at the cost of harming you..."

—"Dual cultivation doesn't necessarily require physical contact, and serving as a furnace won't necessarily harm me. It's just my selfish desire to take responsibility. After all, no matter how you look at it, a part of the blame lies with me,"-Mo Bei said.

She wished she could shirk the blame.

To push everything onto the 'Child of Destiny' who seduced her, onto the 'Heavenly Court' that engineered it all, onto the coldhearted Second Senior Brother who exploited her. But when all was said and done, in that lifetime, it was her own [love-struck brain](# "Literal CN: 恋爱脑 (liàn'ài nǎo) — infatuated to the point of total foolishness, romance-obsessed") that betrayed the sect and acted as the catalyst for the entire catastrophe—one where not even Mo Xi falling to demonhood could pull her back.

She wanted to sever everything with a single sword slash, yet there were still people who cared about that Blood Jade, and the souls within were indeed innocent. While she could never make amends to Master, she could at least offer some assistance to Qingyang Qingyu.

Mo Bei coaxed Qingyang Qingyu:

—"Right, Senior Sister Qingyang, aren't you deeply interested in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's cultivation manuals? Compared to my martial siblings who dabble in all sorts of random side pursuits, I am probably the purest. If we dual-cultivate, you can research me however you like, oh!"

[So please, at least let me do something to make amends!]

—"Really?!"-Qingyang Qingyu's eyes lit up instantly.

She could no longer refuse Mo Bei's offer:

—"Then I shall trouble Little Junior Sister! While avoiding any harm to you, I will thoroughly research the cultivation technique... *cough cough*, research how to cultivate it."

—"Qingyu?"-Jian Nuo stared blankly at Qingyang Qingyu, suddenly feeling as though he had acquired several new romantic rivals out of thin air.

Regrettably, no one paid any mind to Jian Nuo's emotional turmoil. Having reached an agreement, Qu Qianxing drew forth a ribbon of golden-red liquid from the Blood Jade, which condensed into gossamer threads like crimson silk and settled gently onto Qingyang Qingyu's wrist.

—"Refining it takes time, and destroying something this massive also takes time. We need time,"-Qu Qianxing cautioned.

Qingyang Qingyu nodded as she gazed at the crimson mark circling her wrist like a blood-jade bangle.

In the end, regarding how to handle the debts accrued from past reincarnation cycles, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had reached a consensus. And after settling their internal affairs, they finally had a moment to resolve certain external "issues."

***

—"[Not bad, not bad. Better late than never](# "Literal CN: 亡羊补牢，犹未为晚 — mending the fold after the sheep are lost is not too late")!"-as the crimson threads of the contract wrapped around Qingyang Qingyu's wrist, someone clapped their hands in approval.

The disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect froze in place. As if only just remembering there were outsiders present, they turned their heads slowly, fixing their gazes on the few "outsiders" on the scene.

Tao Zhu, Sikong Yao, and Jiuzhai wore varying expressions.

They stood bolt upright, resembling dignified seniors patiently waiting for their juniors to reflect upon their mistakes. But upon closer inspection, one would notice that their lower bodies had been thoroughly frozen solid by the Blood Jade.

As it turned out, while the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples were confronting their debts from past cycles, the Blood Jade had not spared the "outsiders." Whether commanded by the Demon Sovereign's side or the uncorrupted side, the Blood Jade had crept toward the feet of Sikong Yao and the others as if under their collective direction, pinning them firmly to the floor.

Though the cultivation of these disciples was ostensibly far inferior to their own, Sikong Yao and the others felt unmistakable killing intent.

Then again, for this group of disciples to appear in such an eerie realm outside the Three Thousand Worlds, casually debating terrifying topics like world destruction, they had clearly ceased being constrained by standard cultivation logic.

What did they want to do by trapping them here?

No matter how one looked at it, it certainly wasn't anything good.

Regrettably, the debate on that side had finally concluded. Seeing them suddenly turn their heads around, Sikong Yao and the others felt their hearts clench in fright, wishing they could faint dead away like Ran Zhen resting in Ran Deng's arms.

Only Tao Zhu bit the bullet. Banking on his long-standing friendship with Zhou Hui, he tugged at his robe hem and smiled cheerfully as if nothing had happened:

—"Since the problem is resolved, isn't it about time for us to head back? Lingering in a place like this isn't an option either. Your Master must be worried sick about you."

—"Yeah, it really is about time to head back,"-Ran Deng agreed.

—"Indeed, indeed! Your father and your clan disciples need treatment as well,"-Tao Zhu beamed.

The atmosphere noticeably relaxed.

In the very next heartbeat, however, Ran Deng wiped the smile clean off her face, staring earnestly at Tao Zhu:

—"So then, Senior Tao, Senior Sikong, and Elder Jiuzhai... how do you plan to explain us to Master?"

—"Ah..."-Cold sweat broke out across Tao Zhu's back.

[No way, is even Ran Deng being this ruthless? We were the ones who rescued this little girl's father!]

—"I am of course deeply grateful to the seniors. Without you, my father might have been tormented even longer,"-Ran Deng said with a wry smile, as if reading their minds.-"Besides, everything here was caused by Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother. I am purely a victim! If anyone should be punished upon returning, it's them. I don't want Master thinking I'm in cahoots with them in the slightest."

—"Hey!"-Yuwen Xuan yelled indignantly.

He was thoroughly displeased by his junior sister drawing the line right in his face.

Qu Qianxing didn't possess skin as thick as Yuwen Xuan's; when it came to his own blunders, he at least owned up to them.

The issue was that while Ran Deng had no intention of shielding Qu Qianxing and the others, there were certain details she desperately needed to keep Tao Zhu and the rest from mentioning.

For instance: where Lu Shulong and Mo Bei's terrifying powers came from, how Qu Qianxing was able to confront his Demon Sovereign self... and so forth.

Though Ran Deng was the most innocent, at this stage, she couldn't possibly extricate herself completely.

In fact, what Ran Deng didn't know was that she was also saddled with horrifying crimes bordering on being the prime culprit: "almost forcing Master to ascend to godhood," "the fake Master during the demon invasion cycle was a projection of divine nature," and "therefore, the fake Master was essentially caused by Ran Deng."

Yet not knowing didn't matter. When it came to destroying incriminating evidence, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were seasoned veterans.

Even the well-behaved ones were no exception.

Ran Deng took a few steps closer to Tao Zhu, putting on the harmless act of a meek young maiden:

—"However, Seniors, as you surely understand... since I ended up appearing in this inexplicable place, telling Master that I did nothing wrong won't hold water either! I don't ask you to put in a good word for me, but at the very least, could you please not tell Master what you witnessed here?"

[...That sounds like a junior pleading for mercy, but what kind of junior pleads for mercy after binding their elders to the floor?!]

Tao Zhu's mouth twitched dryly.

And looking past Ran Deng, what exerted even greater pressure upon him were Yuwen Xuan and Qu Qianxing. One was a divine beast while the other had fallen to demonhood, both possessing cultivation that blew past the Tribulation Transcendence realm, exuding suffocating dread. Worse, this whole mess was their doing in the first place. Consequently, Ran Deng's polite request felt even more like an outright threat—agreeing to Ran Deng alone was easy enough, but what about these two? They couldn't very well tell them to their faces: "I'm going to tattle about you two to your Master!"

—"I didn't see anything, I didn't hear anything, and I know absolutely nothing,"-while Tao Zhu was still hesitating, Sikong Yao said with [complete shamelessness](# "Literal CN: 很光棍 (hěn guānggùn) — being completely blunt, brazen, and unbothered by pride").

He didn't care about things like "an elder's dignity" in the slightest, nor did he want to wade into the muddy waters of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Whatever they demanded, he agreed without missing a beat.

Unfortunately, these little terrors were far from satisfied.

—"Really?! Senior Sikong, you are wonderful!"-Ran Deng beamed with delight.

Behind her, Lu Shulong transformed back into human form, fumbled inside his sleeve, and produced a parchment scroll:

—"Since that's the case, let's sign a contract, shall we?"

—"Huh?!"-Sikong Yao's eyes bugged out.

—"You're a Tribulation Transcendence mighty figure. You didn't think you could just brush it off with a couple of verbal promises, did you?"-Lu Shulong smiled sweetly.-"We'd like a little security too, Senior~~"

—"...You're a True Dragon, aren't you? Are you seriously afraid I'll trick you?"-Sikong Yao smiled through gritted teeth.

He could throw away his face, but signing a soul-contract with a True Dragon was practically throwing away his life!

While ignorant fools fantasized about taming dragons by signing beast contracts, those in the know understood that once such a contract was inked, there was no telling who would end up as the "slave"! As the most sacred of divine beasts, True Dragons had always been intertwined with sovereign imperial authority. Even uttering a single lie before a True Dragon could court divine retribution, let alone a binding contract—that was handing your life over on a silver platter.

And that was precisely what Lu Shulong intended.

To put it bluntly, they were merely juniors. Once they returned to Master's side, they wouldn't be able to pull any tricks. Not silencing the witnesses right here and now was already an act of immense mercy—they certainly couldn't let them actually go back and tattle!

—"I'm just a little dragon, my strength is still very puny,"-Lu Shulong played the innocent, cute routine.-"So I can only trouble the seniors to suffer a bit of grievance, just to give me some peace of mind!"

[You have peace of mind, but what about us?! Besides, where are you small?! Your dragon form earlier was big enough to choke half the Abyssal Ocean!!]

The other "people" were no better—a den of fiends, demons, and monsters who yielded nothing to evil dragons. Only their blind Master would ever treat them like sweet, well-behaved little darlings!

Tao Zhu and the others were almost provoked to laughter out of sheer exasperation.

Right as the standoff peaked, Patriarch Jiuzhai's brows twitched, and he shot Tao Zhu a meaningful glance.

Whatever Jiuzhai conveyed, Tao Zhu's eyes widened slightly, his expression softened, and he actually squeezed out an awkward, strained smile toward Lu Shulong, starting to haggle:

—"Since things have come to this... fine. However, the terms cannot be drafted arbitrarily. We must specify each clause one by one..."

—"Tao Zhu, have you lost your mind?!"-Sikong Yao stared at him in disbelief.

Tao Zhu grunted twice:

—"What choice do we have?"

—"Boss Tao is truly straightforward!"-Lu Shulong praised, though suspicion stirred in his heart.

He didn't believe for a second that Tao Zhu was someone so easy to talk into compliance.

As if confirming Lu Shulong's premonition, the Demon Sovereign, who had been captivated by the shared memories, suddenly opened his eyes and looked toward the void:

—"...Someone is approaching."

Qu Qianxing's brows knit together:

—"It seems to be..."

—"Master?!!!"

The moment that word left his lips, every single Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple present wore an expression of sheer panic!

[—"Master? How could Master know we're here?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Isn't this too fast?! What do we do? I haven't even thought of an excuse yet!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Why don't we escape right now?"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"He probably tracked us down through Boss Tao?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Aaargh, we really should have silenced the witnesses first... Is there still time to do it now?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

—"You little brats, you're crossing the line—"-Tao Zhu sputtered.

Amidst the pandemonium, a sudden gust of fetid wind blew from behind. As everyone turned their heads, they discovered a colossal, bloody maw that swallowed the entire horizon yawning wide behind them:

*Gulp!*

A single bite.

It swallowed everything in sight.

When Zhou Hui arrived in this spatial realm, all he saw was a mutilated Blood Jade dragon vein and a foolish black lamb whose cheeks bulged like an overweight hamster stuffing its cheek pouches to bursting.

—"Where are the others?"-Zhou Hui asked, knowing full well the answer.

—"Dunno,"-the little black lamb shook its head, not daring to open its mouth, squeezing words through its belly with immense difficulty.-"What are you asking, Master? From start to finish, I was the only one here!"

—"Heh. Just you, is it? Then was this done by you as well?"-Zhou Hui asked, gazing off toward the Blood Jade dragon vein.

—"..."-The little black lamb hesitated for a fraction of a second.

Yet its clone was ultimately a part of itself; when settling accounts, they were bound together anyway.

Yuwen Xuan finally nodded:

—"Yes! It was all me! Every wicked deed from the past reincarnation cycles was done by me!"

—"..."-Hearing this, Zhou Hui actually let out a gentle chuckle, patting the black lamb's head tenderly.-"Not bad. You've even learned to shield your junior brothers and sisters. You truly have grown."

—"Master..."-Stroked like that, the black lamb's tail began to wag happily.

Right until it felt that warm, jade-like hand glide gently down toward its neck:

—"However... didn't I also say? Lying is strictly forbidden."

—"Wait, no, Master, I wasn't shielding—"

Yuwen Xuan scrambled to explain, but it was already too late. Before its words could finish, a familiar *crick-crack* echoed through the void.

It was the sound of its neck being snapped clean in two.

The Immortal Arts Assembly of the Heavenly Spirit Sect began smoothly, was conducted smoothly, and concluded smoothly.

Yet given the vast lifespans of cultivators, a single magnificent tournament provided enough fodder to be discussed for years, decades, all the way until the convening of the next assembly.

Not to mention, this edition of the assembly possessed not just talking points, but earth-shattering revelations.

Perhaps because of this, after attending the assembly and returning home, the leadership of numerous major sects held clandestine meetings and backchannel consultations. Whatever consensus they reached, they astonishingly cast aside their historical prejudices and joined hands to promote the Heavenly Spirit Sect's educational system across the realm.

According to grapevine rumors, these sects had gained great fortune during the tournament, obtaining intelligence regarding the ancient restrictions. Consequently, they forged a grand alliance centered around Sky City, with the Heavenly Spirit Sect providing technical guidance, partnering with the Demon Clan to reconstruct and repair the primordial restrictions and fuse the Five Domains and Six Abysses back into a single unified realm.

At any rate, in the wake of the Immortal Arts Assembly, the cultivation world, dormant for centuries and millennia, began to churn with unprecedented vitality. From disciples of righteous sects to rogue cultivators of unorthodox paths, and even ordinary mortals who had nothing to do with cultivation, everyone felt the ripple effects—fortunately, in a distinctly positive manner.

Centuries later, looking back, posterity would refer to the First Immortal Arts Assembly as the dawn of the "First Cultivation Revolution."

Naturally, those living through it had no such grand perspective. Aside from the select few powerhouses who had awakened their memories, ordinary cultivators after the tournament still buzzed enthusiastically about the event itself:

[—"Untiring Immortal Venerable is terrifyingly strict."](# "Speaker: Cultivators")

Most discussions concluded in such lingering dread.

Then again, after hosting such an immensely successful Immortal Arts Assembly, who would have thought the "Strict Untiring Immortal Venerable" would still be dissatisfied with his personal disciples' performance, stringing all of them up from the peach tree at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate?!

The pedagogical effect of [killing the chicken to scare the monkeys](# "Literal CN: 杀鸡儆猴 (shā jī jǐng hóu) — punishing someone severely as an example to warn others") proved far too effective. Forget perplexed cultivators from outside sects—even internal disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect had taken detours around the main gate recently, terrified of witnessing something they shouldn't.

This rendered the vicinity of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate uncharacteristically deserted. Only seven personal disciples dangled from above like a bunch of gourds, swaying gently in the breeze—a pity that no one dared steal them.

These "gourds" weren't quiet either, constantly emitting pointless squawks.

For example, right at this moment, [Gourd Brother No. 7](# "Literal CN: 七娃 (qī wá) — the seventh child from the classic animation 'Calabash Brothers' (葫芦兄弟); the (x) indicates a humorous self-correction") (strike that) Mo Xi was fake crying:

—"I wasn't even involved at the very end, why do I have to be hung up here too?! *Waaaah*, Master, I'm innocent!"

—"So noisy,"-Mo Bei rolled her eyes. Master wasn't even around; she genuinely didn't know who her idiot brother was putting on this act for.

—"Aiyo, Youngest, there's no reason to talk like that,"-the beast dangling from the very highest branch grumbled. Having grown the most accustomed to hanging from these boughs, it not only had the stamina to swing back and forth like a pendulum, but even reached down with a foot to stomp on the junior brother below.-"Even your senior sister is up here. There's no reason to leave you out!"

—"Eldest Senior Brother still has the nerve to talk?! It's clearly all your fault!"-Lu Shulong dodged Yuwen Xuan's stomping hooves, protesting indignantly.-"Eldest Senior Sister, Fourth Senior Brother, and I are completely innocent!"

—"What kind of talk is that?! After all the effort I made to cover for you in the end!"-Yuwen Xuan stomped even harder.

It was visibly having a grand time. Usually, it was hung up here all alone; now that a whole flock of junior siblings were dangling alongside it, this was practically a reward.

Ran Deng couldn't stomach its smug swagger:

—"You call that covering for us?! You just wanted to use the opportunity to eat us!"

[—"Exactly! Back then, Fourth Senior Brother was knocked unconscious by the foul stench from your maw!"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

—"What foul stench?! I had just devoured a celestial banquet! Every breath I blew out was pure immortal qi! Fourth is steeped in ghostly qi from head to toe, isn't it completely natural for him to faint?! The fact that I didn't accidentally [cross him over into the afterlife](# "Literal CN: 超度 (chāodù) — release a ghost into reincarnation; exorcise / purify") was only because I exercised restraint!"-Yuwen Xuan barked back.

—"...Argue among yourselves. Leave me out of it,"-Guan Ye snorted coldly.

—"Leaving Fourth aside, didn't Second's clone fail to hold on and temporarily vanish as well?"-Yuwen Xuan added, swinging over to poke at Qu Qianxing beside him.-"Right, Second?"

Qu Qianxing had his eyes shut, playing dead. Seeing Yuwen Xuan swinging over like a trapeze artist, he spoke up:

—"Stop fooling around. It's almost time for Master's inspection."

The moment these words fell, the "gourds" fell dead silent.

After all, they weren't all Yuwen Xuan. Being hung up for the first time was deeply humiliating. Even though the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect steered clear out of deference to their dignity, Master's daily inspection remained an excruciating ordeal.

Yet Master cared for them dearly, making a point to visit every single day.

—Though had Zhou Hui not come, they would have been unhappy as well.

Today was no exception.

Shortly after Qu Qianxing spoke, the silver-haired Immortal Venerable strolled over from a side path as if taking a leisurely walk, looking up at the tree full of disciples with a sigh.

—"A month has passed already. Have you reflected upon your actions?"-Zhou Hui asked.

He asked this almost every day, randomly calling upon one disciple to deliver a self-criticism. This was precisely the part the disciples found most agonizing.

Only one person... one beast was the exception.

—"I have, Master! Every day, every hour, every minute, I am reflecting deeply!"-Yuwen Xuan piped up eagerly.

The tone sounded completely unrepentant.

Worse, it made it sound as though none of them had reflected in the slightest.

The others couldn't help but grind their teeth in secret, convinced that if they hung up here for even one extra shichen, it would be entirely the fault of their unreliable Eldest Senior Brother.

Zhou Hui normally ignored Yuwen Xuan, but today he seemed to be in a slightly better mood than usual, surprisingly inquiring:

—"And what, pray tell, have you reflected upon?"

—"Eh?"-Yuwen Xuan clearly hadn't anticipated Master actually indulging him. Stunned for a moment, he began fabricating on the fly:-"I shouldn't... shouldn't have left the Taotie's remnant soul alone. From the very beginning, I should have devoured it alongside Second's clone! Then we wouldn't have wasted so much breath with a mountain of nonsense, dragging things out until a whole crowd of relevant and irrelevant bystanders gathered to watch the show—"

—"...Is that what you call reflection?"-Zhou Hui furrowed his brows.

Another voice spoke up:

—"According to my statistical analysis of biological emotions and human linguistic habits, this organism's sentiment of reflection accounts for a mere 0.15926 percent. Its primary objective is program optimization, in order to scheme for its next crime."

—"What do you mean 'scheme for its next crime'?!"-Yuwen Xuan bristled, shouting in outrage. Then he paused, eyeing Zhou Hui's back with suspicion.

Standing there was a tiny child.

It appeared merely five or six years old, with chubby round cheeks that looked immensely adorable. Its features were well above average, destined to be an exquisite beauty, yet its face was entirely devoid of life. In particular, its silver pupils gleamed with a cold, metallic luster, bearing not the slightest resemblance to a human.

Then again, no normal child would utter words like "program optimization" or "scheme for a crime."

Yuwen Xuan suddenly had an extremely ominous premonition:

—"Wait a minute... Master, who is this kid?"

The others also looked toward the child with curiosity.

—"Your junior brother,"-Zhou Hui announced calmly.

Yet those words fell like a clap of thunder. Yuwen Xuan's swinging ceased instantly, as if he had frozen stiff on the spot.

The other disciples revealed looks of panic or disbelief, chiming in all at once:

[—"M-Master, where did this junior brother come from?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Yeah, where did you accept this disciple?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Why did you suddenly decide to take in another disciple?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

Where did he come from? From that "Genesis Machine," of course.

Being so devoid of human nature, which had caused the Heavenly Dao's chronic malfunction, it truly was an entity in dire need of instruction.

Zhou Hui didn't intend to offer a lengthy explanation here, merely stating:

—"An emergency situation. Originally, I planned to wait until you came down before bringing it up. But seeing how you won't be coming down anytime soon, and your 'Little Junior Brother' has already taken human form, I brought him over so you can get acquainted. Furthermore, if you have any urgent duties that must be completed, you may delegate them to him; he will perform them in your stead."

[—"Took human form? He's not human?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Wait, Master, what do you mean by 'in our stead'?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

[—"Master, you're joking, right?! Junior Brother is so small, how could he possibly replace us in our work?!"](# "Speaker: Heavenly Spirit Sect Disciples")

A clamor erupted from the tree branches, escalating from confusion to outright horror. In particular, a certain beast perched at the tree's pinnacle finally connected the dots, letting out an undignified, blood-curdling howl:

—"NOOOO! Master, I really have reflected upon my mistakes!"

This time, its shrieks were more sincere than ever before in its life.

Regrettably, it was already too late.
