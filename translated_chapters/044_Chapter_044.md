# Chapter 44: Fitting Session (第44章 试衣)

—"Master, did you summon me for some instructions?"-Yuwen Xuan asked outside Zhou Hui's cave dwelling, bowing his head in an attitude of utmost reverence and obedience.

Under normal circumstances, he was naturally nowhere near this well-behaved.

As Zhou Hui's Eldest Disciple and the living being who had accompanied the Untiring Immortal Venerable the longest, Yuwen Xuan had watched with his own eyes as the Heavenly Spirit Sect was founded from scratch. He treated the entire territory of Mount Tianling as his personal backyard, provoking demons and teasing monsters here since childhood. There was virtually nothing he dared not do. Tossing fireworks into Master's cave dwelling in the dead of night was something he had pulled off twice—though his resulting fate had been exceedingly tragic.

Naturally, on ordinary days, Yuwen Xuan barged into Master's cave dwelling whenever he pleased; not even Zhou Hui entering secluded cultivation could stop him. Yet there was precisely one scenario that made Yuwen Xuan dread stepping inside:

Namely, when his master actively summoned him.

Based on Yuwen Xuan's years of experience in [courting death](# "Literal CN: 作死 (zuòsǐ) — recklessly courting disaster"), whenever Master sought him out of his own accord, it never boded well. Either the misdeeds he was currently committing had been exposed, or past misdeeds had come back to haunt him, or perhaps misdeeds he hadn't even had time to execute yet had somehow leaked prematurely.

Yuwen Xuan stood at the entrance of Zhou Hui's cave dwelling, pondering for a long time without reaching a conclusion.

The list of punishable offenses he had committed was simply too long to tally in a single sitting.

To make matters worse, his master urged from within:

—"What are you doing standing at the doorway? Come inside."

It didn't sound particularly furious.

*Fine, let's test the waters with a "minor matter" first—*

—"I'm sorry, Master! I shouldn't have returned the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* to their disciple, much less egged that girl on to usurp the sect master!"-Yuwen Xuan blurted.

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

*Not this one?*

Yuwen Xuan thought for a moment, pulling out something slightly more severe:

—"Is it the yellow weasel matter, then? His background is actually quite clean, and he doesn't go around letting rip random farts! Aside from his cooking tasting horrible, there's no major problem with him. I thought he made a splendid chef—didn't he even stimulate the young disciples to become self-reliant and strive diligently for progress?"

—"...Oh? Then explain in finer detail the entire process of how you installed a weasel who can't cook into the kitchen,"-Zhou Hui said slowly.

*Finished! As expected, the most atrocious one was exposed.*

Yuwen Xuan shouted at the top of his lungs:

—"Master, I won't dare do it again! I'll never play cards with those little monsters ever again!"

Saying so, he dropped cleanly into a [five-point prostration](# "Literal CN: 五体投地 (wǔ tǐ tóu dì) — casting five points of the body (knees, elbows, head) to the ground in total submission") flat on the floor. His practiced posture was so smooth it was heartbreaking to behold.

It was fortunate he dropped so fast, for a wind blade grazed right over Yuwen Xuan's crown, pulverizing the bronze hairpin holding his topknot together. A cascade of long black hair scattered across the floor.

One second slower, and what tumbled would have been Yuwen Xuan's head.

Only now did Zhou Hui's voice brim with genuine fury:

—"You gambled again?!"

—"N-no, that didn't count. I really only played two rounds of cards with them during New Year,"-Yuwen Xuan said, sounding deeply aggrieved.

As for how two rounds of cards had managed to forfeit fifty years of the cafeteria's head chef tenure, Yuwen Xuan kept his lips sealed tight.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui did not interrogate him further.

To be precise, Zhou Hui did not continue berating Yuwen Xuan because faint voices drifted from inside the cave dwelling, as though someone was speaking to Zhou Hui.

Yuwen Xuan's sharp, sword-like brows instantly arched.

*There's someone else in Master's cave dwelling?*

[Ah, could one of the little ones have come to tattle on me?]

[Dammit, when I usually "care for" them so much! How could they do this?!]

In his heart, Yuwen Xuan was already scheming his next blueprint for "caring for" his junior brothers and sisters.

After a moment, Zhou Hui's voice spoke from within:

—"Never mind. I'll settle accounts with you later. Come inside first."

—"I know my wrongs! Master, just let me kneel here and reflect upon my misdeeds!"-Yuwen Xuan yelled, playing dead on the floor.

He wasn't foolish enough to walk inside so easily!

He had a feeling that merely having his neck snapped was no longer sufficient to satisfy Master lately. What if Master sliced him in two next time and hung him from a tree for several months? Being hung up was trivial; being forbidden from going outside was the real headache.

He still wanted to attend the Winter Solstice Festival Auction!

Just as Yuwen Xuan was pondering this, a soft chuckle drifted out from inside.

*Eh? Mo Xi?*

Then came Zhou Hui's stern bellow:

—"Get in here!"

Fine. It seemed that if he didn't go in, Master was going to step out.

Only then did Yuwen Xuan scramble to his feet and drag his feet reluctantly inside.

As it turned out, the scene inside the cave dwelling was nothing like the [three-court joint trial](# "Literal CN: 三堂会审 (sān táng huì shěn) — a joint tribunal of three high judicial courts; an intense interrogative grilling") Yuwen Xuan had braced himself for.

Zhou Hui's bedchamber was in utter disarray, looking as though it had just been ransacked by Yuwen Xuan—or rather, hit by burglars. Scattered robes lay everywhere. Zhou Hui stood beside a folding screen, absorbed in adjusting the sleeves of a long robe draped over it. Qu Qianxing stood nearby holding several folded garments; seeing Yuwen Xuan enter, he rolled his eyes as though looking at a monumental idiot. Further back, Mo Xi sat atop a massive clothing trunk swinging his legs; the moment he saw his Eldest Senior Brother, he ducked his head, his shoulders quivering violently as he struggled agonizingly to stifle his laughter.

Seeing his two junior brothers' expressions, how could Yuwen Xuan fail to realize:

[I dug my own grave!]

It seemed Master hadn't summoned him to reprimand him at all?

Yuwen Xuan instantly felt his vigor return. His skin had always been remarkably thick anyway, so he tossed his earlier blunders out of mind. Flashing a mocking grimace at Mo Xi, he shamelessly sauntered up to Zhou Hui's side.

—"Master, what are you doing?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

Along the way, he subtly gathered spiritual power in his legs, ready to bolt the instant Zhou Hui showed any sign of attacking.

Zhou Hui did no such thing. Seeing Yuwen Xuan approach, he pulled down the robe he was adjusting and draped it over Yuwen Xuan's shoulders:

—"Put it on and let me see."

[—"Ah?"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

Completely bewildered, Yuwen Xuan found himself shoved into the robe by his master.

—"My, it looks rather good!"-Mo Xi said.

—"I don't think so. This robe definitely makes people look bulky,"-Qu Qianxing mused:—"It only looks decent because Eldest Senior Brother's shoulders are broad and square. On someone of similar build whose posture isn't as upright, it will look awfully bloated."

—"Would a different color work better?"-Zhou Hui remarked, scrutinizing Yuwen Xuan:—"Change it to black."

—"Wouldn't it be too conspicuous while flying on swords? Besides, it's far too gloomy,"-Qu Qianxing replied.

—"Then try this one,"-Zhou Hui said, dragging over another long robe and draping it over Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan stood there with a face full of question marks.

—"Ah, this scarlet looks gorgeous!"-Mo Xi cried, leaping down from the trunk as if eager to try it on himself. But comparing his height to Yuwen Xuan's, he slumped back down dejectedly:—"I can't do it. I can't pull it off."

—"Yes, most people cannot carry off this hue,"-Qu Qianxing tilted his head:—"Yet it is indeed very striking."

—"This one is certainly not suitable for everyone, but for Advanced Class sword cultivators, it should be quite fitting,"-Zhou Hui said:—"Disciples who can reach the Advanced Class can at least perceive sword intent. Sword cultivators of this cultivation stage are almost invariably tall and slender, barring exceptions... Still, scarlet is rather overbearing. Xi'er, bring over that white crossed-collar jacket with the hidden silver Luan-bird motifs we looked at earlier."

—"Right away!"-Mo Xi answered, hopping down from the trunk and beginning to rummage inside.

And so, completely baffled, Yuwen Xuan was treated like a [dress-up doll](# "Literal CN: 换装娃娃 (huànzhuāng wáwá) — dress-up doll / mannequin"), trying on garment after garment before finally realizing that Zhou Hui planned to design standardized uniforms for the sect's disciples. Because Yuwen Xuan was taller and broader (relative to Qu Qianxing, at least), he had been dragged in as a live fitting model.

—"Is there really a need for this?"-Yuwen Xuan scoffed. Raising his arms, he obediently allowed Zhou Hui to adjust him while grumbling:—"Every sect's tailors are different anyway. The moment anyone walks outside, a single glance tells you which sect they belong to."

This was a solid truth.

Although "school uniforms" were not a trend among cultivation sects, disciples relied on the sect for food, lodging, and daily necessities. Yearly clothing was distributed seasonally as fixed benefits. In a cultivation world where commerce was underdeveloped, sects predominantly relied on internal production and consumption. Such vast quantities of apparel naturally could not be outsourced to third parties. Hence, every sect possessed dedicated logistical departments akin to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Tianyi Pavilion, where master tailors and apprentices followed hereditary techniques. They saw no need to put excessive thought into ordinary disciples' clothing, so designs and color palettes were largely identical. Stepping outside, anyone could identify their sect at a glance.

Thus, one could not judge a sect solely by its upper echelon; their true aesthetic taste was best reflected through their rank-and-file disciples.

For instance, bottom-tier disciples of the Heavenly Dao Alliance predominantly wore blue short tunics bearing a massive character for "Heaven" across their backs, resembling franchise store employees and [completely devoid of swagger](# "Literal CN: B格尽失 (bī-gé jìn shī) — internet slang for losing all aura, coolness, and pretension").

Another example was the Palace of Carefree Wandering. Their low-level disciples wore a yellow-and-white palette. While nominally a prestigious combination, whether due to fabric quality or other factors, it exuded a bizarrely cheap vibe.

The most pitiful were the bottom-tier disciples of Thousand Beasts Hall. Draped in drab, dusty gray—ostensibly for ease of beast-taming—they looked virtually indistinguishable from trash bags. If the cultivation world held an award ceremony for the worst sect uniform, the crown would undeniably belong to them. Any ordinary disciple with a few spirit stones to spare would scramble to purchase decent clothes for themselves.

Looking back, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's former "uniforms" were indeed far from unified.

Because the sect had been small, the workload at Tianyi Workshop was relatively light, leaving enough idle time for eccentric indulgences. Disciples' attire largely followed their assigned "departments."

For example, Ran Deng favored pastel yellow, so garments in the Array Hall typically resembled little ducklings. Guan Ye favored black, turning the Artifact Refinement Hall into an unbroken sea of pitch-black. Lu Shulong favored green, to the point where even the hats in the Alchemy Department were [green](# "Literal CN: 绿帽子 (lǜ màozi) — wearing a green hat is a famous Chinese idiom for being cuckolded, making the disciples' uniform hilarious"). In contrast, disciples unassigned to any specific department fared slightly better, copying Zhou Hui's pristine white robes—looking as though they were [wearing sackcloth and mourning hemp](# "Literal CN: 披麻戴孝 (pī má dài xiào) — traditional funeral mourning attire").

—"It is better to standardize,"-Qu Qianxing assessed. He would never oppose Zhou Hui's suggestions regardless.

—"Still, I never imagined Master would have so many clothes!"-Mo Xi chimed in.

With slight astonishment, he gazed upon the trunks crowding the cave dwelling. All of these had been pulled from Zhou Hui's storage pouch. Their styles, colors, and patterns were plentiful enough to open an entire boutique, boasting even greater variety than the garment pavilion run by the Carefree Palace in the Eastern Domain.

Reflecting carefully, Mo Xi realized that Zhou Hui's fashion sense had always been exquisite. Yet because he favored white most of the time and conducted himself with such austere restraint, he projected an aura of frugal simplicity, making it unimaginable that he possessed such an immense wardrobe.

Zhou Hui, however, regarded it as entirely natural:

—"Equipment is something one must keep in reserve to be prepared against eventualities. Leaving aside how many are required to face Heavenly Tribulations, even an ordinary secret realm trial would turn sour if an accident caught you unprepared."

During the last tribulation, for instance, at least nine immortal robes crafted from top-tier precious materials and embroidered with distinct defensive talismans had been incinerated into flying ash under the celestial lightning.

In the cultivation world, garments were genuine consumables—the higher one's realm, the more true this was.

At this point, Zhou Hui glanced toward Yuwen Xuan:

—"Therefore, my intention to standardize our 'school uniforms' is not merely for outsiders to gawk at, but for far more practical functions."

Such as warding off demonic corruption.

—"Understood—"-Yuwen Xuan answered, devoid of interest.

Mo Xi also found the explanation rather dull.

He had assumed he might uncover some juicy gossip about Master!

Only Qu Qianxing remained wholeheartedly convinced:

—"As expected of Master. To amass such a vast arsenal of equipment in preparation for battle... Hm? Did I misspeak?"-Before he could finish, he noticed Yuwen Xuan eyeing him with a half-smile.

Yuwen Xuan seemed to have just realized something extraordinarily amusing. Sporting an odd grin, he said:

—"Where do you think these clothes came from? Collected? Purchased? Custom-ordered? Ancestral treasures of True Immortals obtained through blood and sweat?"

—"Are they not?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Of course not,"-Yuwen Xuan announced:—"Master sewed every single one with his own hands!"

[—"!"](# "Speakers: Qu Qianxing & Mo Xi")

Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi widened their eyes simultaneously.

They stared at Yuwen Xuan, then turned in sheer panic to look at Zhou Hui.

Zhou Hui looked back at them in perplexity:

—"Is that strange?"

—"No, but... why did Master make all of them yourself?"-Qu Qianxing's expression showed a rare hint of bewilderment.

—"Because you can't buy them!"-Zhou Hui stated as though it were self-evident.

In the cultivation world, the higher one's realm ascended, the harder it became to purchase clothes.

In fact, given how primitive commerce was in this realm, even low-tier cultivators struggled to buy decent apparel.

Particularly after Zhou Hui had offended the Palace of Carefree Wandering, it had become virtually impossible!

Moreover, as Zhou Hui had just stated, garments were equipment and consumables. Decorative pieces could be commissioned from tailors, but robes boasting specialized functions—or worn to withstand heavenly lightning tribulations—required high-grade materials that could only be processed by someone possessing commensurate cultivation. This was not an online game from another world; how could one effortlessly round up a flock of tailors at the identical cultivation realm to sew for you? Much less could gear drop from slaying monsters or finishing quests. While secret realms might occasionally yield one or two True Immortal relics, it was a case of [too many monks and little porridge](# "Literal CN: 僧多粥少 (sēng duō zhōu shǎo) — scarce resources with too many claimants")—there was never enough to go around. Besides, no matter how miraculous a "relic" was, it was still a leftover; who knew whose corpse it had been stripped from? Wearing it would hardly bring peace of mind.

Therefore, one had no choice but to craft them personally!

—"E-even this one?"

Having heard Zhou Hui's reasoning, Mo Xi still found it impossible to swallow. Pulling out an exquisitely embroidered robe covered in intricate patterns, he asked:—"Master embroidered this with his own hands?!"

—"Yes. It isn't difficult; practicing for fifty years is plenty to master the craft,"-Zhou Hui said matter-of-factly, gazing encouragingly at his two disciples:—"Of course, your cultivation realms are still modest, so your primary task is cultivating. At your level, peers of similar cultivation are abundant, and there will always be those skilled in tailoring, so you need not fret. But once you reach Void Refinement and beyond, peers of equal realm become exceedingly scarce. Even if they exist, they will hardly deign to perform such chores for you. Fortunately, upon reaching that stage, a standard lifespan spans three to five millennia. Setting aside fifty years to master a trade is by no means a losing bargain."

*Is this a matter of whether it's a losing bargain?!*

—"...I could just find a Dao companion,"-Mo Xi whispered.

Hearing this, Zhou Hui chuckled:

—"That is indeed an option. Though there is a fifty percent chance it will result in you doing double the embroidery."

***

Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Zhou Hui paid no mind to his two traumatized disciples. Glancing at the intricately embroidered robe in Mo Xi's hands, something seemed to strike him. Rummaging through the trunk, he pulled out another garment boasting comparable craftsmanship and beckoned to Yuwen Xuan:

—"Come here. Try this one on."

—"Right away, Master!"-Yuwen Xuan answered gleefully. Seeing his two junior brothers disheartened seemed to brighten his mood considerably.

Of course, he had seemingly given no thought to what he would do for clothes once he reached the Void Refinement stage—or perhaps he had, given how close he was to that threshold.

Yuwen Xuan's solution was simple:

—"Master, speaking of which, you haven't sewn clothes for me in ages! Once I break through to Void Refinement, would you help me one more time? Just like when I was a boy, I want a Taotie pattern embroidered across it."

As he spoke, he made a face at the two junior brothers glaring at him.

Zhou Hui missed his eldest disciple's petty antics. Smoothing Yuwen Xuan's sleeves with a slight furrow of his brow, he said:

—"Very well."

—"V-very well...?"-Yuwen Xuan's eyes widened, never expecting Zhou Hui to agree so readily.

—"Then try this one on next. I believe this style is particularly suited for Taotie motifs,"-Zhou Hui said, sounding thoroughly in earnest as he handed over another robe.

—"Master, you spoil him far too much!"-Mo Xi protested listlessly.

Draped in the robe, Yuwen Xuan felt utterly lightheaded, adrift in a surreal haze, completely missing Zhou Hui's searching gaze.

[None!]

[Absolutely none!]

By now, he had cycled Yuwen Xuan through dozens of garments. Among them were robes imbued with evil-repelling arrays, demon-exorcism talismans, and even tribulation-lightning properties—the sort that would summon a celestial thunderbolt to strike him down if he harbored even a trace of demonic corruption. Yet none produced the slightest reaction.

—Indeed, Zhou Hui was not merely selecting uniform cuts; he was also scrutinizing Yuwen Xuan's condition.

He suspected that in the Heavenly Dao's script, "Mo Xi colluding with demons" was connected to Yuwen Xuan.

[After all, the only one capable of leading his younger brother astray is this brat!]

[Not long ago, this rascal even egged Mo Xi into hiring outer-sect disciples to ghostwrite his punishment copying!]

Setting aside petty everyday mischief, Yuwen Xuan was the only person who treated Mount Tianling like his personal backyard, knew its monsters and demons better than Zhou Hui himself, could wipe out the demonic entities beforehand, and possessed the influence to convince Mo Xi to bypass Qu Qianxing and betray the sect.

Disciples might obey Qu Qianxing on daily affairs, yet in critical moments, every one of them—Qu Qianxing included—placed boundless faith in their formidable Eldest Senior Brother. If Yuwen Xuan were tainted by demonic qi, catastrophe would truly follow.

Naturally, Zhou Hui believed they trusted him even more.

Zhou Hui could have simply voiced his suspicions without beating around the bush; Yuwen Xuan would have obeyed without protest. However, he had no reason to resort to forceful measures, and more importantly, speaking of events that had not yet occurred might alert... the Heavenly Dao.

Hence, Zhou Hui had chosen this roundabout method.

None of the garments he had Yuwen Xuan try were ordinary robes. As Zhou Hui had noted, they were "consumables capable of withstanding heavenly tribulations." Their fabrics and runic patterns were meticulously paired; cycling through them was arguably more thorough than an ordinary soul search.

Yet Yuwen Xuan displayed not the slightest abnormality.

*Had he overthought things? Or had the time simply not arrived?*

Zhou Hui didn't know whether he ought to feel relieved. Passing the final prepared garment to Yuwen Xuan, he said:

—"Try on this one with the coiled-dragon pattern. Let me see if the silhouette works."

If all else failed, he would grant Yuwen Xuan's request and craft an inner garment enchanted with surveillance arrays, forcing Yuwen Xuan to wear it twelve [shichen](# "Cultivation time: 1 shichen = 2 hours; 12 shichen = a full 24-hour day") a day. That would surely reveal any anomalies.

Just as Zhou Hui was pondering what materials would be suitable for the new garment, he heard Yuwen Xuan speak:

—"Master, this isn't a coiled-dragon pattern. It's the Moonlit Acacia Wood motif."

—"Did I grab the wrong one?"-Zhou Hui turned around.

Yet Yuwen Xuan had already thrown it on with a cheeky grin, babbling as he dressed:—"Didn't Master say all his robes serve functional equipment purposes? What is this one's effect? 'Moonlit Acacia'... could it be for finding a Dao companion—?!"

Yuwen Xuan never finished his sentence, for he saw himself begin to glow.

To be precise, the silver-white lunar motifs embroidered along the hem radiated brilliant radiance. The foliage beneath the moon began to rustle, and a crimson thread reached out from beneath the tree into reality, coiling and dancing around Yuwen Xuan.

Everyone stared blankly at this sight. Yuwen Xuan was the first to react.

—"Oh my!"-Cradling his cheeks, he mimicked a delicate feminine trill:—"Could it be that Master and I share a crimson thread of three-lifetime destined love? How scandalous! Doesn't this [violate the cardinal human relationships](# "Literal CN: 乱了伦常 (luàn le lúncháng) — subverting Confucian familial hierarchy, here mockingly feigning romantic incest between master and disciple")?"

*Wow, that was punchable.*

Evidently, Qu Qianxing and Mo Xi felt the exact same way.

One summoned his lightning pearls while the other summoned his red dragon, aiming them squarely at Yuwen Xuan.

—"No matter. Slaying you will prevent any violation of cardinal ethics from occurring,"-Qu Qianxing declared coldly.

—"Precisely,"-Mo Xi grinned:—"Eldest Senior Brother, you may pass away in peace! We shall never permit Master's pristine reputation to suffer the slightest blemish."

[Do they truly respect this Eldest Senior Brother of theirs?]

In that fleeting instant, Zhou Hui found himself questioning the interpersonal bonds among his disciples.

Yet the crimson thread did not reach toward the robe's creator as the disciples had imagined. Instead, it coiled around Yuwen Xuan's own wrist.

A brand flared into illumination upon his palm.

Black, hideous, and unmistakably sinister at first glance.

—"What is this?"-Yuwen Xuan cried in alarm.

—"Didn't you ask what function this robe serves? This is it,"-Zhou Hui said coldly:—"Detecting the location of artificially concealed 'opportunities.'"

Its greatest virtue was its active detection: having sensed the brand, it had reached out directly for Yuwen Xuan's hand.

Zhou Hui had rarely worn this garment, so he had nearly forgotten it possessed such an ability.

Then again, he had never imagined that the anomaly on Yuwen Xuan would operate in the most inconspicuous guise of an "opportunity." Without a targeted test, even if it lay right before one's eyes, one would merely dismiss it as a random stroke of luck!

—"An artificial opportunity?"-Yuwen Xuan's expression changed:—"Someone plotted against me? What kind of opportunity was planted?!"

—"We must examine it directly. May I?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Of course! Leaving something like this unchecked is far more terrifying!"-Yuwen Xuan frowned, spreading his arms wide and opening his divine sense, leaving himself completely undefended before Zhou Hui.

In this state, Zhou Hui could effortlessly tear his soul to shreds.

Seeing this, Zhou Hui revealed a faint smile. The next moment, he reached out and probed directly into Yuwen Xuan's soul, seizing the ugly brand and reading its contents.

—"The Winter Solstice Festival? An auction?"-Zhou Hui murmured softly.

*Just that?*

Zhou Hui scrutinized it repeatedly, but found no further information.

Yet this was precisely why "artificially planted opportunities" were so insidious: masquerading as ordinary occurrences, they merely implanted a subtle psychological suggestion, prompting the target to act on their own while believing the decision was entirely their own idea, perceiving no abnormality whatsoever.

Thus, Zhou Hui looked toward Yuwen Xuan:

—"You plan to attend the Winter Solstice Festival Auction?"

—"Yes,"-Yuwen Xuan replied in a daze. Because his soul remained tethered in Zhou Hui's grasp, he appeared somewhat sluggish:—"Someone very, very important told me to purchase an item."

—"What item?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"The Flame Crystal Flower,"-Yuwen Xuan replied.

—"...Purchased for what purpose?"-Zhou Hui pressed.

—"To give to...???"-Yuwen Xuan's eyes went wide, staring at Zhou Hui in utter blankness.

Zhou Hui withdrew his hand.

—"Wait, wait, wait?! Why did I want to buy that flower?!"-Yuwen Xuan clutched his head, crying out.

—"Hehe, Eldest Senior Brother, did you gamble with someone again and rack up a debt?"-Mo Xi chuckled:—"What 'very important person' sent you on an errand? Could it be a lady?"

Qu Qianxing cast a disdainful look at Yuwen Xuan, evidently harboring the identical suspicion.

—"No! It's not a woman!"-Yuwen Xuan shouted. In his memory, this was an indispensable treasure that he had to obtain no matter what, of paramount importance. Yet right now, compared to this "paramount importance," Yuwen Xuan felt it far more urgent to clear his name. Grabbing Zhou Hui's arm, he pleaded:—"Believe me, Master! I wouldn't gamble with women of dubious origin and end up owing such bizarre debts—"

—"Yes, I believe you,"-Zhou Hui said.

Because it was Zhou Hui who had instructed Yuwen Xuan to purchase it.

In multiple past cycles, Zhou Hui had dispatched Yuwen Xuan to procure it—a critical ingredient required to heal the injuries left behind by the Heavenly Tribulation, an indispensable material for ascension!

And it was during the search for that very material that Yuwen Xuan had vanished without a trace.

Yet in this lifetime, Zhou Hui had never issued that command. How, then, had Yuwen Xuan formed that identical resolve?

Interference from the Heavenly Dao?

No. The Heavenly Dao's meddling would never take such a crude, direct form. It demanded "rationality"—there had to be a "rational" vector that allowed this brand to "rationally" manifest upon Yuwen Xuan.

In other words, since it was an *artificially planted* opportunity, there had to be a *person* behind its creation.

Zhou Hui's brows furrowed deeply.

—"What should we do, Master? Should we gouge it out?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

—"Gouge it out! Of course we have to gouge it out!"-Yuwen Xuan yelled, gripping a dagger as if ready to excise it himself:—"How can I keep an opportunity of unknown origin on my person?!"

—"No,"-Zhou Hui stayed Yuwen Xuan's hand:—"Judging from the information, there is nothing that actively harms you. Gouging it out would leave us completely in the dark. We might as well pay a visit when the time comes."

—"Indeed, let's go check out Eldest Senior Brother's destined romance!"-Mo Xi clamored, thrilled by the chaos.

Yuwen Xuan flashed Mo Xi a throat-slitting gesture.

—"Enough. That is a matter for winter,"-Zhou Hui clapped his hands nonchalantly:—"Let us finish the task at hand first."

And so, the disciples exchanged glances and resumed discussing sect uniforms.

Even Yuwen Xuan, the victim in question, merely cast occasional looks of frustration and bewilderment at his palm.

To them, this was simply a harmless trace left behind by someone Yuwen Xuan had provoked at an unknown time, which happened to be fortuitously detected while trying on Master's robes.

Merely an inconsequential, random little episode.

Hardly anything of importance.

During the hour of the dragon (辰时, 7:00–9:00 AM).

Looking utterly drained, Cheng Ziyi returned to the dormitory from outside, slamming his sword onto the floor the moment he stepped through the door.

—"I'm not going! I'm not going anymore! I'm definitely not going tomorrow!"-he bellowed.

Cheng Ziyi felt he had never suffered such colossal humiliation in his entire life! It was one thing to spend every day practicing sword forms by condensing water mist over vegetable patches, but because the mist he condensed failed to meet standard specifications, he had been dragged off to a patch of barren wasteland to drill in isolation, lest he damage those two cabbages!

[Are those two cabbages more important than me?!]

[Being an uncool sword cultivator like this, I refuse to do it!]

Seeing Cheng Ziyi in this state, Jing Chenjun glanced toward Ji Gen, who had entered behind him. Ji Gen shook his head. He had been assigned to farm labor, which was practically far more exhausting than Cheng Ziyi's drills, but he was accustomed to it and found nothing worth complaining about.

On the other side of the room, Huang Xiao poured water and wrung out a damp towel, handing it to Cheng Ziyi:

—"There, there, we won't practice anymore! Speaking of which, the [Winter Clothes Festival](# "Literal CN: 十月朝 (Shíyuèzhāo) — the 1st day of the 10th lunar month, a traditional festival marking the onset of winter and the distribution of seasonal clothing") is just around the corner. I heard they're issuing winter garments soon. Let's head over to Tianyi Workshop this afternoon to check it out."

—"Are you a child? Getting so worked up over a few garments,"-Cheng Ziyi retorted reflexively.

Even reduced to this state, the man couldn't resist picking a fight.

Everyone in the dorm had grown accustomed to his prickly demeanor and exchanged wry smiles. Just as they were about to speak, a clamor erupted from outside:

[—"In the sky! Look up at the sky!"](# "Speaker: Neighboring Disciple")

[—"It's sword cultivators!"](# "Speaker: Neighboring Disciple")

Disciples in the neighboring dormitory could be heard hollering. Curiosity piqued, Huang Xiao stepped out onto the balcony to look, and promptly began yelling as well:

—"Hey, hey, come out quick! Look at the sword cultivators!"

—"What's so special about sword cultivators? Don't we see them every single day?"-Jing Chenjun asked in bewilderment.

—"Not ordinary sword cultivators! The clothes!"-Huang Xiao shouted back.

—"The clothes?"-Jing Chenjun and Ji Gen dashed over, prompted by Huang Xiao's incoherent excitement. Then they too gasped in awe, waving frantically back at Cheng Ziyi:—"Come out and look, quick! So handsome!"

—"Just sword cultivators. Don't think you can fool me,"-Cheng Ziyi said coldly.

[I'm not looking! What a bunch of fools...]

Cheng Ziyi told himself, yet his eyes couldn't resist stealing a glance through the window toward the open sky—and froze.

Just as the others had said, a squadron of sword cultivators was gliding across the sky.

No, "gliding across" was hardly accurate.

These people were shamelessly [flaunting](# "Literal CN: 显摆 (xiǎnbai) — shamelessly showing off")!

On their upper bodies, they wore tight-sleeved, straight-collared scholar tunics in brilliant scarlet. Their waists were cinched impossibly trim above dark red, waist-high pleated skirts, while tall black boots stepped firmly upon flying swords. Draped over their shoulders were silver cloaks that billowed like white wings in flight, accented at the cuffs with flashes of vibrant crimson that flared open in midair like courting white cranes.

Several cultivators even swooped right past the dormitory balconies on purpose.

[—"Those are the new robes for this season, right?!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Disciple")

[—"Will they issue those to us too?!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Disciple")

[—"Drop-dead handsome! When I get my own flying sword, I'm flying just like that!"](# "Speaker: Spectating Disciple")

Amidst the chorus of excitement, Cheng Ziyi stealthily picked up his sword from the floor and tied it back to his waist.

Catching his movement out of the corner of his eye, Jing Chenjun fought hard to suppress a smirk:

—"Since there's such a commotion, shall we head over to Tianyi Pavilion to take a look?"

—"Yeah,"-Cheng Ziyi nodded.

There was simply no helping it. Sword cultivators were truly far too handsome! Dressed like that, who could possibly resist?!
