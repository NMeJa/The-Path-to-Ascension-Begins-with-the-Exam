# Chapter 55: Strange Collaborations Have Multiplied (第55章 奇怪的联动增加了)

Zhou Hui's decree caused a chorus of groans and wails to erupt across the entirety of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Yet although the disciples of the Beginner Class complained with their mouths, their emotions were not in turmoil—in truth, they were even somewhat looking forward to it.

After all, before enrolling in—er, being recruited by—the Heavenly Spirit Sect, this batch of disciples had more or less heard about the legendary Grand Unified Examinations. Given how exaggerated the rumors had been, they were mentally braced to accept any arrangement thrown at them. What was more, the syllabus handed out at the start of the semester had already clearly indicated it; everyone had long possessed psychological expectations.

Not only that, the Beginner Class disciples harbored ambitions of their own.

They had been sorted into the Beginner Class due to low cultivation realms, dismal scores in a specific subject, or similar deficits. Although the midterm examination would not result in re-streaming the classes, many still longed to show off their mettle—to let everyone know that landing in the Beginner Class had merely been a fluke of poor exam-day performance, not an inherent lack of ability!

Most important of all, because the vast majority of the Beginner Class possessed only Qi Condensation cultivation and were still interning internally within the sect, they were ineligible to participate in the preparations for the Winter Solstice Auction. Naturally, this left an unbearable itch in their hearts.

Word had it that if one performed exceptionally well on this upcoming examination, a cohort of disciples would be selected to intern at the immortals' bazaar! This served as a tremendous shot in the arm for everyone; their passion for studying soared to the heavens!

And so, the Heavenly Spirit Sect bore witness once more to the apocalyptic spectacle of six months prior:

No matter where one walked, groups of disciples could be seen clutching textbooks and cramming furiously. With the influx of new recruits, the sheer number of study fiends was even greater than before. Merely strolling through the Heavenly Spirit Sect during this time instilled a bone-deep terror: if you didn't study, you were dead.

Yet among these disciples, while some engaged in rote memorization, others relied on petty cleverness.

When Zhao Yuegui received a message talisman from Jing Chenjun, he was quite taken aback:

—"Aren't you preparing for the midterm exam?"-Zhao Yuegui asked.

Zhao Yuegui himself was currently overwhelmed and burnt to a crisp over the midterm examinations.

As a member of the most [involuted](# "Literal CN: 内卷 (nèijuǎn) — hyper-competitive rat race / academic grind") Artifact Refinement Hall, he was not only carrying out a [social practicum](# "Literal CN: 社会实践 (shèhuì shíjiàn) — field internship / practicum requirement") at the immortals' bazaar—which required submitting a thirty-thousand-word report—but also had to turn in an inspection dossier on artifact materials or an original blueprint for a magical treasure to the Artifact Refinement Hall! The crushing pressure made Zhao Yuegui want to pack his bags and flee for his life.

Yet whenever Zhao Yuegui cast his eyes toward the abyssal ocean beyond the bazaar, he couldn't bear to let go.

The Kunpeng!

Several months ago, it was his sheer misfortune of falling asleep on the back of a Kunpeng that had caused him to be dumped from the Eastern Domain all the way into the Central Domain. He had subsequently been "blessed" to witness the scene where the Immortal Venerable crushed the Palace of Carefree Wandering and Qu Qianxing claimed the secret realm, which led him to join the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Never in his wildest dreams had he expected that the Heavenly Spirit Sect actually harbored a Kunpeng of its own!

On the day the Kunpeng unveiled itself, Zhao Yuegui had stood before the Buyan Palace, watching the Kunpeng soar up from the back mountain and plunge into the void behind Heaven-Cleaving Chasm. His blood had boiled with pure ecstasy.

In that instant, he had almost wanted to prostrate himself before the Immortal Venerable.

Based on his understanding of the Heavenly Spirit Sect accumulated over this period, Zhao Yuegui knew that if he were to explain his plight to the Untiring Immortal Venerable, there was a very high probability that the revered Immortal Venerable would genuinely dispatch the Kunpeng to fly him home. Even if not a Kunpeng, he would use other divine arts to send him back.

Yet Zhao Yuegui had pondered and brooded for days on end, and in the end, he did not do so.

Quite the contrary: a far grander ambition had sprouted within him.

He wanted to pilot a Kunpeng too!

Of course, this didn't mean Zhao Yuegui intended to change careers and become a beast tamer. (Even if he had harbored such thoughts, after asking around about the lethal working conditions in the Rare Beast Enclosure, he only wanted to run for the hills.) Rather, he wanted to tame the Kunpeng through the methods of an artifact refiner!

Though the two sounded [completely unrelated](# "Literal CN: 风马牛不相及 (fēng mǎ niú bù xiāng jí) — having nothing to do with each other, like wandering horses and oxen that never cross paths"), it was by no means impossible.

At the very least, currently the "bubbles" carrying passengers and the "coral islands" upon the Kunpeng's back were both forged by the Artifact Refinement Hall. Senior Brother Guan, who oversaw the Artifact Refinement Hall, had also mentioned that he hoped to discover a more effective, spiritual-energy-conserving method to pilot the Kunpeng rather than relying solely on the revered Immortal Venerable.

Because of this, Zhao Yuegui grew anxious over whether his academic grades would stand out before Senior Brother Guan. After all, if he waited until the specific research project began recruiting to start trying hard, it would definitely be too late!

Case in point: during this current cycle, Zhao Yuegui had failed to get picked for the R&D of the Kunpeng's "passenger transit system."

Yet even with a clear goal in mind, actually grinding through the effort felt miserable, and writing reports was doubly agonising. In particular, Zhao Yuegui hailed from a background as a loose immortal who was accustomed to carefree, undisciplined wandering; he didn't even know how to pick a thesis direction, and just thinking about it made his hair fall out in clumps.

Therefore, upon receiving Jing Chenjun's request for a guided tour, Zhao Yuegui had dashed out immediately to receive them.

—He was obviously not brimming with burning warmth toward Jing Chenjun, whom he had met only a handful of times; he simply wanted to catch a breath of fresh air.

Yet whenever a few Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples gathered together, opening their mouths inevitably meant talking about exams.

Although they had joined the sect at the exact same time, Zhao Yuegui exploited his status as an Intermediate Class disciple, unceremoniously venting his own stress in the form of lecturing his junior brothers.

—"At the Heavenly Spirit Sect, exams are of paramount importance,"-Zhao Yuegui lectured Jing Chenjun and the others.-"These grades determine how various departments select you. Even setting aside the distant future, it will at least directly impact your practicum placements for the second half of the semester."

—"Precisely, Senior Brother Zhao. That is the very reason we came,"-Jing Chenjun replied with a refined smile.

He had clearly come well-prepared:
—"We heard that disciples who excel in this midterm exam will be selected for practicum assignments at the immortals' bazaar. Therefore, I boldly deduced that this test paper will likely contain questions related to the bazaar and the auction. We hope Senior Brother Zhao can guide us on a tour of the bazaar's various departments."

[You can even pull a trick like that?!]

Zhao Yuegui choked for a beat, suddenly realizing that this junior brother was not to be underestimated:
—"...I don't mind, but can you Beginner Class students even enter the bazaar?"

—"Normally, of course not,"-Jing Chenjun explained.-"However, on the [rest-and-bathing day](# "Literal CN: 休浴日 (xiūyù rì) — designated rest and personal hygiene day held every ten days in ancient Chinese academies and bureaucracies") of every ten-day cycle, we are permitted free movement. Just like the time limit for the secret realm, we are granted three [shichen](# "Literal CN: 时辰 (shíchen) — traditional Chinese two-hour time block; three shichen equals six hours") to explore the bazaar."

...In other words, while others used their free time for recreational shopping, you lot were using it for academic fieldwork?!

Zhao Yuegui felt all the more that these kids were [frighteningly involuted](# "Literal CN: 卷得厉害 (juǎn de lìhai) — excessively competitive academic grinders").

Still, he was hardly petty enough to refuse their request.

Upon closer thought, strolling around might genuinely help him find a thesis topic of his own.

—"Fine then. Where do you want to look?"-Zhao Yuegui asked.

—"The artifact auction house!"-Cheng Ziyi blurted out immediately.

—"Can we visit the spirit plant market?"-Ji Gen asked, shaking the flower blooming atop his head.

—"I want to check out the ferry docks,"-Huang Xiao said.

—"...Doesn't doing that defeat the point and turn into ordinary sightseeing?"-Jing Chenjun intervened.
They had debated this topic prior to leaving without reaching a consensus. Because Jing Chenjun was unfamiliar with the cultivation world and had no idea where to begin, that was his true rationale for seeking out Zhao Yuegui:
—"Senior Brother Zhao, could you trouble yourself to take us to the places most likely to recruit disciples for internships? In my view, we in the Beginner Class likely have zero leverage to be picky about jobs. It will almost certainly be a matter of whichever department is understaffed taking us, won't it?"

—"You certainly have sharp eyes!"-Zhao Yuegui chuckled.

He suddenly felt that letting his junior brothers experience the brutal realities of society early on was quite healthy:
—"Spot on. How could the sect practicum ever let you pick and choose? Besides, with your meager cultivation bases, you can't be of much help anyway. If we're talking about a place that is currently in utter chaos, [desperate enough to borrow a cat's paw for help](# "Literal CN: 猫的爪子都想借用 (māo de zhuǎzi dōu xiǎng jièyòng) — Japanese/Chinese idiom meaning so overwhelmingly busy that even a useless cat paw would be welcomed as extra labor"), there is only one destination—"

***

—"This broken sword of yours could probably fetch around seventy spirit stones... No, I wouldn't recommend submitting it to the auction."

—"The quality of these spirit fruits is decent; they can sell for ten spirit stones per jin."

—"Demonic Blood Stones again? Though the grade is pristine, the supply is simply too vast. The market price has been marked down repeatedly..."

—"Hmm? No, you misunderstand. This is merely the Market Guidance Price. In other words, as long as it does not constitute predatory dumping or malicious competition, you may sell higher or lower as you please. Our counter only handles registration. Of course, if you are willing to sell certain items to us directly, that is fine too, but we only acquire them at the official guidance price."

A quarter of an hour later, Zhao Yuegui stood with his junior brothers before the entrance of the Commodity Registration Office.

This was indeed the busiest spot of all.

With the passage of days and the Wanhe Gang's widespread distribution of coral beads, more and more merchants poured into the immortals' bazaar every day. Moreover, the goods they brought became increasingly complex and bizarre, making pricing harder and harder.

Yet the reputation of the immortals' bazaar was firmly established alongside it.

To begin with, this bazaar was guaranteed by the supreme might of a Tribulation Transcendence titan; conducting transactions here meant never worrying about highway robbery or murder. The market guidance was equitable, allowing many cultivators unsure of their goods' worth to sell at fair prices without fear of being swindled. Consequently, many treated this venue as an appraisal institute—whether they intended to sell or not, they would drop by to check the guidance price.

For this reason, the Commodity Registration Office for cultivators below the Golden Core realm saw queues of merchants stretching so long they packed an entire district! It got so chaotic that Zhou Hui had been forced to make emergency adjustments, specifically opening a thousand-person hall dedicated solely to appraisal, along with an automated search system that cataloged common items for self-service inquiry.

Even so, the Commodity Registration Office remained boisterously crowded every single day—even more frantic than the bazaar itself!

There were even exceptionally shrewd individuals who abandoned physical trade altogether, arbitraging price discrepancies by buying low and selling high based on the market guidance price—giving rise to the rudimentary embryonic form of a modern financial market!

Witnessing the sea of people, the four youths stood with their jaws dropped.

—"So many people..."-Ji Gen muttered, the vines grafted onto his body shivering nervously.

—"I... I have so little worldly experience,"-Huang Xiao lamented, clutching his head.-"I don't think I can appraise prices."

—"What on earth are you daydreaming about?! How could they ever let you handle appraisal?"-Zhao Yuegui laughed scornfully.

He pointed to the staff behind various counters and explained:
—"The ones appraising rare treasures over there are heavyweight experts specially invited from the Abyss Sea Association. The ones evaluating agricultural produce here are veteran traveling merchants with three hundred years of trading experience in the Central Domain. The revered Immortal Venerable had to tap into Boss Tao's network to recruit these masters! Did you really think just anyone could do this job?"

The four beginner-class cubs widened their eyes.

When they looked at the registration clerks behind the counters again, their gazes were filled with profound veneration.

—"Then, Senior Brother Zhao, what can we actually do?"-Jing Chenjun inquired.

—"As for you lot,"-Zhao Yuegui chuckled,-"those who know arithmetic can help keep the books."
He pointed to an accounting assistant behind the counter who was wielding seven or eight talisman brushes simultaneously, scribbling furiously without pause.

—"Those with physical strength can haul cargo."-He pointed toward the back door, where laborers were lugging crates.

—"Those who are fast on their feet and sharp of tongue can serve as guides."-He looked toward the entrance, where a guide was patiently explaining procedures to bewildered merchants.

—"Those who study the sword can maintain order."-Everyone turned their gaze toward a security guard who was currently being bossed around as an errand runner.

Clapping his hands, Zhao Yuegui concluded:
—"Looking at it this way, there's quite a lot you can do!"

[What do we do? Looking at it like this, we don't want to come for a practicum at all!]

[It feels like coming here just to shop after the exams are over would be a hundred times more fun!]

The enthusiasm of the four little ones was brutally crushed.

Conversely, Zhao Yuegui's pointing and gesturing drew the glances of several merchants nearby.

—"Are you also an exalted immortal of the Heavenly Spirit Sect?"-several merchants approached Zhao Yuegui, asking him deferentially:—"Could you perhaps introduce us to your sect's Fairy Mo... or rather, Fairy Yu?"

—"Ah?"-Zhao Yuegui was dumbfounded.

He had no idea who on earth they were talking about.

Fortunately, the guide who had originally been explaining things to these merchants rushed over in a hurry:
—"...Stop harassing our sect's junior brothers! I've already told you countless times: the recipe for *A Cup of Sorrow* is not for sale! That is a personal gift from the revered Immortal Venerable to Junior Sister Mo—how could it ever be sold to outsiders?!"

—"We don't necessarily have to buy that exact recipe,"-the merchant reasoned.-"We merely wish to sell similar tea beverages, that's all."

—"Although there is no rule strictly forbidding it, why... why are you so fixated on this?!"-the guide exclaimed in exasperation.

In truth, given how simple milk tea's recipe was, imitating it was extraordinarily easy.

As long as one had tasted it once, one could basically whip up something similar. What was more, the cultivation world possessed no concept of copyright; it was easy to envision that in the near future, knockoffs like *One Cup of Tea* and *Two Cups of Tea* would pop up everywhere.

However, in the immortals' bazaar, out of deference to the organizers, no one dared to sell blatant counterfeits for the time being—mainly due to the terrifying pressure of the Immortal Venerable. Yet evidently, certain merchants were unwilling to wait even that long, seeking to acquire an official "license" right off the bat!

The guide responsible for receiving this batch of merchants was an inner sect disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Having never encountered such a situation before, she was thrown into total disarray, completely at a loss on how to handle it.

Selfishly speaking, she definitely didn't want the second milk tea shop in existence to be birthed by guests she had personally hosted. Otherwise, how could she ever face Junior Sister Mo Bei back at the sect?!

The guide pressed anxiously:
—"Weren't you here to sell sugar?"

—"But sugar can't fetch a good price!"-the merchant replied aggrievedly.-"That's precisely why we wanted to discuss a partnership! We heard that *A Cup of Sorrow* acquired a massive batch at a price slightly above the guidance price, didn't they?"

They had made the trip specifically after catching wind of this rumor.

—"That was because their sugar was of exceptional quality! Furthermore, *A Cup of Sorrow* has already signed a purchase contract and requires no more supply."

—"At least take a look first! The quality of our sugar is excellent too!"-the merchant urged.

—"The goods from our Mount Yunzhong are all top-notch,"-several adjacent merchants chimed in, clearly having traveled together:—"Not just the sugar, our spiritual wheat is of peerless quality as well, far superior to the common wares you typically see! Lumping everything into the exact same guidance price—isn't that a bit unfair?"

—"Didn't I just explain this?!"-the guide said, her throat parched from talking:—"The market supply of spiritual wheat is overwhelmingly vast—literally every single sect cultivates it! Slight variations in quality simply cannot move the needle on the guidance price. If you refuse to accept it, you are free to price it higher on your own and see if anyone buys it!"

—"It's precisely because nobody knows the quality of our goods that we want the guidance price to be set a little higher!"-the merchants insisted, refusing to yield.

...

Seeing that the two sides were on the verge of a screaming match, Zhao Yuegui took the opportunity to pat his junior brothers' shoulders, gesturing for them to slip away while no one was paying attention. Yet his palm slapped empty air.

Zhao Yuegui saw that Jing Chenjun was actually walking toward the brawl. He hissed under his breath in panic:
—"What are you doing? This kind of dispute happens every single day here; talking is completely useless!"

—"I have an idea,"-Jing Chenjun said.

Clearing his throat until the merchants shifted their gazes onto him, Jing Chenjun smiled warmly:
—"Gentlemen, since you possess such immense confidence in your goods, why not produce some finished goods from them?"

—"That's exactly what I was thinking!"-the sugar merchant slapped his thigh.-"Which is why I wanted to launch a milk tea of our own, guaranteed to taste completely different from *A Cup of Sorrow*! We just hope to receive the fairy's blessing—"

[—"I already said..."](# "Speaker: Guide Sister")

The guide was about to snap back, but Jing Chenjun gently tugged at her sleeve.

—"What is the point of everyone selling milk tea?"-Jing Chenjun smiled.-"Besides, from what I see, your inventory contains no spiritual tea, and sourcing suitable spiritual milk on short notice is hardly convenient, is it? Rather than peddling a shoddy, low-budget knockoff, why not sell food that you gentlemen can collaborate to produce together?"

—"Do you mean sweet pastries like sugar-crisp cakes?"-one merchant asked, clearly having pondered this before.-"We considered such ideas, but pastries cannot be conjured out of thin air. We don't possess that kind of culinary craftsmanship."

[And you thought you possessed the craftsmanship for milk tea?!]

The guide sister thought with righteous fury, yet she had to admit: seemingly, only milk tea could be learned on the spot in five minutes.

—"Ordinarily, making pastries well is indeed difficult, but there are recipes as simple as milk tea,"-Jing Chenjun explained, adopting an expression of pious reverence as he bowed toward the sky:—"Our revered Immortal Venerable has deposited their recipes in the Library Pavilion, accessible to anyone who wishes to view them."

—"Even we can view them?"-the merchants asked in astonishment.

—"If you gentlemen are interested, why not accompany me to take a look?"-Jing Chenjun invited.-"The Library Pavilion has a satellite borrowing branch right inside Qianxing Hall; it is exceedingly convenient."

The merchants exchanged glances, visibly intrigued by the phrase "the Immortal Venerable's recipes."

Furthermore, the Commodity Registration Office had a luggage depository right on site, making checking their cargo effortless. Without much debate, the merchants agreed.

Even the guide sister was intrigued by Jing Chenjun's pitch, deciding to skip work—er, accompany her valued guests:
—"I shall accompany you as well. This is my solemn duty."

[—"Of course."](# "Speaker: Jing Chenjun")

Jing Chenjun nodded gracefully, welcoming all.

Afterward, he led the group out of the Commodity Registration Office.

***

Having reached the immortals' bazaar, there was naturally no need for coral trees to serve as teleportation hubs. To emphasize the cohesive unity of the "Heavenly Spirit Sect Immortals' Bazaar," the Untiring Immortal Venerable had engineered a far more ingenious spatial architecture.

Thus, when guests disembarked from the Kunpeng and rode bubbles to the docks, they entered distinct bazaar sectors tailored to their respective cultivation realms. Each sector had its own layout and topography; yet regardless of which bazaar one advanced through, reaching the far end invariably led into the exact same central hall.

This was a colossal hall capable of accommodating tens of thousands of people simultaneously, rising so high that looking upward revealed no ceiling.

It was said that when the Winter Solstice arrived, the grand finale auction would be staged right here.

Prior to that date, visitors were by no means forbidden from entering or exiting. On the contrary, following the seemingly endless spiral staircase encircling the perimeter of the hall upward, after merely a few minutes of walking, one would find themselves standing at the apex of the grand hall—

Qianxing Hall.

Indeed, the auction hall connected to the immortals' bazaar was located at the very "bottom" of Qianxing Hall (in the sense of spatial magic, of course, rather than actual physical coordinates)!

As a result, guests could effortlessly access the amenities of Qianxing Hall. The influx was so overwhelming that the secret realm had been forced to implement unprecedented crowd control: VIP members were granted priority access, while ordinary visitors were required to make advance reservations. That said, reservation slots were reportedly booked solid until two months after the Winter Solstice Auction!

Fortunately, Jing Chenjun was not leading the guests on a tour of the secret realm.

They headed straight for the Library Pavilion's temporary borrowing branch.

After the secret realm opened, Zhou Hui had noticed an increasing number of rogue cultivators visiting the Heavenly Spirit Sect Library Pavilion to borrow manuals. To prevent disruptions to the daily activities of sect disciples and to accommodate these wandering cultivators, Zhou Hui had simply established several satellite borrowing points throughout Qianxing Hall, allowing rogue cultivators to interface directly with the Heavenly Spirit Sect Library Pavilion and select whatever books they desired.

Naturally, the interior decor of such satellite branches could not compare to the main library at sect headquarters, but everything that mattered was present.

The borrowing branch Jing Chenjun led them into resembled an ordinary little room at first glance. Several bookshelves stood along the walls, holding frequently consulted volumes that were reportedly rotated every month. At the center of the chamber stood a manual search console for books.

—"Like this: since what you intend to sell is spiritual wheat... which is to say flour, and sugar, simply write those words upon the console."

Jing Chenjun gave step-by-step guidance.

He was actually quite delighted with this mechanism, having secretly tested it previously to confirm that even someone like him, completely devoid of a spirit root, could operate it seamlessly.

Moreover, Jing Chenjun had practiced calligraphy since early childhood; his brushwork was impeccably elegant.

Once Jing Chenjun wrote "flour," "sugar," and "recipe" across the console in fluid, beautiful calligraphy, a colossal scroll unfurled behind the desk, revealing an astronomical array of densely packed culinary recipes!

Seeing this, Jing Chenjun added the words "simple preparation," and more than half of the recipes instantly vanished.

—"You may select from here,"-Jing Chenjun suggested.-"I recommend this Rice Cooker Sponge Cake. Well... even I can make it."

(By the way, after helping Huang Xiao forge an air fryer, Zhao Yuegui had gone on to forge electric rice cookers. While the dark culinary abominations of the Delicacy Pavilion had shown zero improvement in the short term, quite a few disciples—including those of royal mortal descent like Jing Chenjun—had mastered a handful of simple recipes.)

Jing Chenjun demonstrated the process to the merchants.

Watching the demonstration, the merchants were genuinely captivated:

[—"It looks quite simple."](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"Many of these recipes are meticulously detailed; perhaps it really can be made?"](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"The cooking vessel looks rather strange."](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"By the way, what on earth is a 'rice cooker'?"](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

—"If you are interested, our Heavenly Spirit Sect Artifact Refinement Hall sells them!"-Zhao Yuegui had originally been staring dumbly at Jing Chenjun's maneuvering, but at this moment his business instincts kicked in, and he pitched with blazing enthusiasm:—"Furthermore, as for ovens, blenders, and the like, we can custom-forge them to order!"

These were lucrative revenues for the Artifact Refinement Hall!

—"Oh, that's right! There isn't a single cake shop in the entire bazaar!"-The guide sister finally recalled how a proper commerce guide ought to perform, chiming in:—"As the first shop of a brand-new product category in the market, your taxes can be discounted by fifty percent! What is more, between now and the end of November, customers across all bazaars will vote and rate the stalls; the shop with the highest score will be promoted into Qianxing Hall in December, open to cultivators of every realm!"

These perks made the merchants tremble with excitement:
[—"Why didn't you say so earlier!"](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"I think this sponge cake is brilliant."](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"Could a recipe from the revered Immortal Venerable ever be wrong?"](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"Still, is this cake a bit too simple? Perhaps we could challenge something a tad more advanced?"](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

[—"Let's go back to town and recruit Master Liu! Although he is a mortal, his hand-pulled noodles can be stretched thinner than a strand of hair. Crafting pastries like this will be like taking candy from a baby!"](# "Speaker: Mount Yunzhong Merchant")

The merchants huddled together in fervent, excited planning.

Seeing this, Jing Chenjun and the others refrained from disturbing them further, slipping quietly out of the room.

—Zhao Yuegui was the lone exception. He was cornered and pinned down by the merchants, who peppered him with inquiries regarding how these rice cookers, ovens, and magical "artifacts" were sold, whether they could be rented, and related commercial terms.

***

What Jing Chenjun, the merchants, and the others failed to notice—not that it mattered if they had—was that their actions had fallen into the watchful eyes of observant bystanders.

Several female cultivators who had been casually shopping in Qianxing Hall observed the scene from afar for quite some time. Afterward, they quietly found another borrowing branch and inscribed upon the search console:

> Thread. Fabric. Textile.

To their disappointment, very few entries appeared. A large portion were texts already native to the cultivation world, while the rest bore bizarre, incomprehensible titles such as *Spacesuit Structural Design* and *History of Spacesuit Development*.

—It couldn't be helped. Zhou Hui had possessed no past-life memories while in the other world, so he could not intentionally memorize the intricacies of every modern industry. Even recipes only existed because such content was ubiquitous across the internet, allowing Zhou Hui to "accidentally" retain them.

When it came to textiles and fabrics, however, Zhou Hui's knowledge was exceedingly narrow, confined at most to brand names and logos.

—"It seems that even the revered Immortal Venerable has areas where he is not proficient!"-the female cultivators sighed.

[—"Indeed. What is all this bizarre gibberish?"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

One of them flipped through the volumes at random, but one of the books suddenly caught their eye.

To be precise, the design sketches inside arrested their attention:

[—"My, these garments look so strange."](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"With bare arms and exposed legs... the Immortal Venerable didn't accidentally slip some banned books in, did he?!"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

—"Don't make wild guesses! These garments clearly belong to an established aesthetic system; they must be observations the Immortal Venerable gathered during his travels across some minor realm... Wait, hold on, look at this!"

—"I know, I know! You're just utterly infatuated with the Immortal Venerable, refusing to let any of us utter a single word that might tarnish his pristine image!"

—"No! I'm talking about the page just now! Yes, the dress in this sketch... Manifest!"

In response to the female cultivator's invocation, the dress depicted in the sketch materialized before their eyes in a three-dimensional projection. Not only could they inspect the structural tailoring of the garment on the spot, but it also recreated the tactile texture of the fabric (from Zhou Hui's memories).

Touching the dress, the female cultivator beamed with pleasant surprise:
—"This exact weave! Don't you think we could try this with our unsold, low-priced silk thread?"

[—"Truly!"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"And this decorative edging looks gorgeous! We could totally embellish skirt hems and cuffs with it!"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"It feels like it would make lovely hairpin accessories on its own as well."](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

[—"What is this called? Are there more patterns?"](# "Speaker: Female Cultivator")

—"Let me check... Ah, what a strange name... It's called '[lace](# "Literal CN: 蕾丝 (lěisī) — modern Chinese phonetic loanword for lace; completely alien and exotic to ancient cultivators")'?"

—"Let's search using this word as a clue! Who knows—maybe we could open a specialty boutique of our own?"

***

—"Junior Brother, I owe you a debt of gratitude,"

Meanwhile, along the highest corridor of Qianxing Hall, the guide expressed her thanks to Jing Chenjun.

—"You are too kind, Senior Sister. It was merely a sudden flash of inspiration,"-Jing Chenjun replied with a seemingly modest and honest smile.

He naturally harbored private calculations of his own.

Even though he believed he could piece together a respectable score on this midterm exam, his total inability to cast spells was a fatal flaw. Jing Chenjun suspected that no matter how high his test scores were, no department would want to recruit him. Sowing seeds of goodwill in advance could never go wrong.

Sure enough, the senior sister serving as their guide smiled with immense satisfaction. Looking at Jing Chenjun, she asked:
—"Would you be interested in writing a report?"

—"A report?"-Jing Chenjun echoed.

—"Yes. Concerning the events of today, write about areas where you feel the immortals' bazaar can be improved,"-the senior sister explained.-"Although this is the midterm exam assignment for disciples of our... [Commerce Department](# "Literal CN: 商务部 (shāngwù bù) — corporate business/commerce department; a hilariously modern bureaucratic term instituted by Zhou Hui")—I still find the name Master picked so strange!—disciples from other halls are welcome to participate as well. If your proposals are adopted, it will not only add bonus points to your year-end evaluation, but you will also receive a [scholarship](# "Literal CN: 奖学金 (jiǎngxuéjīn) — academic scholarship / merit stipend")—which is to say, a handsome reward of spirit stones!"

—"There are even spirit stones to be earned?"-Ji Gen asked with naked envy.

—"Indeed! Our Commerce Department is exceedingly wealthy! And Junior Brother Mo Xi is also very kind and approachable."

The senior sister said this with a sunny smile, looking at the fresh junior brothers.

Nobody noticed that when she mentioned "Junior Brother Mo Xi," her fingers behind her back crossed into a giant X, signaling that her final sentence was a blatant lie and begging the heavens not to hold it against her!

How could the four innocent cubs ever comprehend the treacherous depths of the human heart? Their interest was instantly piqued.

—"However, I have never written a report before,"-Jing Chenjun hesitated slightly.

—"No matter, I can teach you the formatting; it is exceedingly simple,"-the senior sister offered with escalating warmth:—"Oh, right, you came to tour the facilities, didn't you? I ended up delaying your plans. How about this: on your next rest-and-bathing day, come over and I will personally guide you on an extensive tour of the bazaar. I'll let you see firsthand how our Commerce Department operates! Moreover, don't let our chaotic busyness deter you—whether it is bonus points or spirit stone stipends, the rewards are remarkably lavish—"

[What splendid fresh laborers delivered right to my doorstep; how could I ever let them escape?]

The senior sister thought, an evil glint flashing in her eyes.

As for how in the subsequent days of the immortals' bazaar, every single Commodity Registration Office came to feature an adjacent Library Pavilion satellite branch—earning glowing acclaim from visiting merchants—that was a story for later.
