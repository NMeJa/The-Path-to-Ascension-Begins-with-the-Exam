# Chapter 219: Behind Every Troublemaking Brat Is an Enabling Parent

—"What manner of monstrosity did you just unleash upon the realm?"

The moment Zhou Hui returned, Old Ancestor Jiuzhai demanded point-blank.

Naturally, this was not Old Ancestor Jiuzhai's true physical body.

Having divined some catastrophic cosmic omen, the old fossil had abruptly fled from the Middle Realm back to the Southern Realm right as the Immortal Arts Assembly commenced. Subsequently concluding that even the Fire Abyss Divine Tree—allied with Zhou Hui—was far from safe, he had scurried all the way back to his ancestral fortress at the Divine Decree Altar.

Even so, Jiuzhai remained deeply unsettled. In particular, after observing abnormal fluctuations across the karmic destiny of the entire micro-world, he became thoroughly convinced that Fujuan was at the bottom of it. (In truth, his intuition was spot-on. While Zhou Hui had performed no overt action, the phenomenon was triggered by Qu Qianxing administering examinations to the demon hordes; of course, neither Jiuzhai nor Fujuan was privy to that fact at this moment.) Eager to discern what manner of cosmic meddling Fujuan had committed to distort karmic destiny, Jiuzhai dispatched an avatar to monitor the situation.

It was an exceedingly hideous avatar.

Minuscule—barely the size of a ping-pong ball—shriveled with countless wrinkles and sporting an identical white beard to Jiuzhai's, whether viewed from afar or inspected up close, it resembled nothing so much as a desiccated, moldy orange.

Its singular virtue was exceptional energy efficiency.

It expended virtually zero spiritual energy, maintained near-zero spiritual presence, and rarely attracted notice. Even if someone spotted it, nobody would ever fathom that this was the avatar of a transcendent Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster.

At the very least, Yuwen Xuan had failed to detect it.

Consequently, when Yuwen Xuan was setting his ancient demonic traps in the arena, Jiuzhai had caught him red-handed from the very inception. Otherwise, with Zhou Hui utterly engrossed in deciphering array discs, he truly might have failed to notice. This was precisely the outcome Yuwen Xuan had engineered; the youth was a master at evading the surveillance of his Master and fellow disciples, never expecting an unexpected ambusher lurking in the grass.

Yet when Jiuzhai lodged his grievance with Zhou Hui, Zhou Hui did not immediately string his Eldest Disciple up from the mountain peach tree.

The core reality was that if the foul schemes Yuwen Xuan prepared had been rooted in genuine malice—recklessly pursuing the absolute worst-case scenario purely to sate his appetite—the catastrophic fallout could never be resolved by dangling from a branch. It would have pushed Zhou Hui to the brink of [righteous patricide](# "Idiom: 大义灭亲 — executing one's own kin for the greater good"), tearfully executing his Eldest Disciple and escorting the soul to reincarnate so he could re-educate the brat starting from prenatal schooling.

Fortunately, Yuwen Xuan still retained a shred of humanity.

Not much, but enough to pass the baseline.

Furthermore, upon careful reflection, Zhou Hui realized that his Eldest Disciple had always harbored complete indifference toward outsiders, yet had repeatedly gone out of his way to provoke the disciples of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall. While his primary objective was food, he had undeniably treated that child as a playmate!

(*"Gan Tang": A playmate...? If being unilaterally toyed with can be defined as a playmate, then I suppose so?*)

An interpersonal relationship cultivated by Yuwen Xuan outside the sect entirely on his own initiative!

Realizing this, Zhou Hui felt an unexpected wave of fatherly pride and emotion.

He had always felt that his disciples were flawless in every regard, save for being far too introverted, insular, and xenophobic!

Even the most reliable and grounded Qu Qianxing—perhaps scarred by the traumas of his youth—harbored excessive paranoia toward outsiders, appearing utterly incapable of forming genuine, intimate friendships, let alone the younger disciples.

Who would have thought that the most fiercely possessive Yuwen Xuan would unexpectedly forge a bond with a friend? Even if that friend was already dead—and had died twice—it proved that the child's social faculties were completely intact. Perhaps it was because he had governed them with far too heavy a hand, denying these youths the chance to venture out into the world. After all, children only formed meaningful camaraderie by roaming the wide world!

He himself wasn't proficient at socializing, yet hadn't his own lifelong friendships been forged during his youthful travels?

In his burst of paternal joy, Zhou Hui had granted Yuwen Xuan permission to venture into the world.

Zhou Hui shared this heartwarming realization with Jiuzhai.

Using his dried-orange avatar, Jiuzhai contorted his features into an indescribably twisted grimace:

—"Your... friend?"

—"?"-Zhou Hui returned a puzzled glance. Then, as if struck by sudden realization, an expression of profound hurt surfaced upon his features:—"So all this time, Jiuzhai, you never considered me a friend?"

—"..."

In that singular split-second, Jiuzhai felt a suffocating, lethal wall of killing intent crash down upon him.

This was entirely natural. Whenever anyone witnessed an ethereal, silver-haired peerless grandmaster display such wounded vulnerability on account of a dried-up citrus peel, their immediate instinct was to slice that citrus into marmalade.

Even Jiuzhai himself suffered a fleeting wave of acute, soul-deep guilt.

—"N-No, that is not what I meant,"-Jiuzhai stammered with great difficulty.—"I was merely startled that you actually considered me a friend. In truth, I always assumed you viewed those around you... *cough*, people like us, as trivial hindrances along your grand path of cultivation!"

—"How could I ever entertain such thoughts?"-Fujuan answered with utter sincerity:—"Jiuzhai, you are naturally my friend."

...Truth be told, hearing this did not bring much joy.

Back when he first met Fujuan, the boy had been a child; now, they were "peers."

Jiuzhai's internal emotions were exceedingly convoluted.

Yet such sentiments could never be voiced aloud. Inflicting emotional "damage" upon Fujuan a second time would undoubtedly cost him his life.

Zhou Hui did not neglect the others standing nearby. Turning toward Ruwang—who had been radiating overt killing intent toward Jiuzhai moments ago—he declared:

—"Ruwang, you are my friend as well."

A suspicious flush of crimson swept across Ruwang's face (which was an exact replica of Fujuan's own countenance). Yet before anyone could study it closely, a vibrant red Beijing opera mask snapped down to cover his entire visage, and the figure shrank back into the deep shadows.

Zhou Hui continued his grand distribution of affection:

—"Peilan, and Old Tao, both of you are included."

—"Completely devoid of sincerity,"-Peilan scoffed, though the fur on his bald tail bristled in visible fluster.

Tao Zhu rubbed his round belly, chuckling merrily:

—"I shall definitely pass word of this to City Lord Jian later, just to make him drown in lifelong regret."

—"?"-Zhou Hui blinked:—"A-Jian is naturally my friend."

Having distributed an entire wholesale batch of ["Friend Cards,"](# "Modern dating/pop slang: 发朋友卡 (giving friend cards) — friend-zoning or indiscriminately handing out friend labels") Zhou Hui swept his gaze across the gathering once more, checking whether any "fish had slipped through the net."

At that exact moment, Zhou Hui's gaze locked abruptly onto Sikong Yao, who was lingering awkwardly at the periphery of the crowd.

A violent shiver jolted down Sikong Yao's spine.

Fortunately (?), Fujuan's gaze merely lingered upon him for a brief second before silently, impassively drifting away without uttering a single syllable.

[...What in the world is this faint sense of devastation?!]

While it was true they certainly were not friends, being so quietly and thoroughly ignored was profoundly demoralizing!

Sikong Yao felt he shouldn't even be standing here today.

Then again, his presence had never been voluntary.

To be frank, he had wanted to slip away days ago.

Earlier, Sikong Yao had led a cohort of artisans from the Xiaoyao Palace to "gift" to Zhou Hui. Zhou Hui had accepted them without fuss, even "inviting" him to observe the Master Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination. By the standard "common sense" of the cultivation world, their past grievances were considered completely reconciled.

Furthermore, the artisans Sikong Yao delivered were exceptionally competent; all had secured various tiers of professional certification, precisely fulfilling the Heavenly Spirit Sect's acute manpower needs.

The complication lay in that during that examination, a monstrous scandal detonated: Guan Xinguang, the Chief Overseer of the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Ministry of Works, was exposed as the culprit who had extracted the spinal bone of Fujuan's Fourth Disciple, Guan Ye! Consequently, Goddess Ranran—who had accompanied him—fled the scene without even offering a polite farewell (or so Sikong Yao deduced; as for the deeper cosmic conspiracies, Sikong Yao maintained that he neither knew nor wished to know, praying fervently not to be dragged into the crossfire). This placed Sikong Yao in an excruciating dilemma.

Sikong Yao had intended to depart right after the qualification exams concluded, but immediately following the event, Fujuan had mysteriously "vanished," denying Sikong Yao any opportunity to offer a formal goodbye.

Admittedly, Fujuan had placed zero physical restrictions upon Sikong Yao's movements; slipping away without saying goodbye was entirely feasible. Yet how could Sikong Yao be so tactless? What if his departure was misinterpreted as fleeing alongside Ranran's conspirators to escape retribution? Wouldn't all his groveling be for naught?! Most torturous of all, for reasons known only to the heavens, Fujuan had arranged Ruwang's guest quarters directly adjacent to his own! Daily, a figure bearing Fujuan's exact face glared at him with sinister, ghastly malice, instantly evoking the harrowing years he spent being hunted down. Sikong Yao harbored deep suspicions that if he took two steps too quickly, the man would pursue him to the ends of the earth.

Fortunately, Sikong Yao's greatest virtues—aside from running fast—were an impossibly thick skin and an easygoing temperament.

Seeing that departure was temporarily out of the question, he settled in comfortably, spending his days indulging in gourmet feasts and spectating the Immortal Arts Assembly. He found these past few days infinitely more relaxing than life back at the Xiaoyao Palace.

Until today, when Fujuan—who had been hiding who-knows-where—abruptly summoned him.

Sikong Yao had dashed over with his heart in his throat, only to find that it didn't look like an execution.

Still, watching a cluster of ancient monsters whose ages were impossible to calculate gathered together playing childish games of declaring friendships was somewhat excessive. Were they intentionally bullying him for lacking friends? Besides, wasn't his lack of companions entirely Fujuan's fault?! Because he had been relentlessly chased by Fujuan for years, losing every shred of dignity, and high-tier grandmasters valued face above all else, Sikong Yao's superficial social circle had cut ties with him completely; no high-tier gathering ever deigned to invite Sikong Yao anymore!

Overwhelmed with self-pity, Sikong Yao cleared his throat, interrupting Zhou Hui's ongoing wholesale distribution of friendship cards:

—"Immortal Master, what instructions do you have for this humble one?"

—"Ah, it concerns the demonic miasma,"-Zhou Hui had not ignored Sikong Yao maliciously; he simply felt the previous topic bore zero relevance to him (Sikong Yao: *enough already!*). Reminded by the question, Zhou Hui finally recalled the business at hand.

He gestured toward their surroundings:

—"Specifically, right here."

—"Demonic miasma?"-Sikong Yao looked around the arena platform.

They were currently standing at the exact center of a tournament battle ring in the assembly venue.

While tournament rings were built to standardized architectural specifications, making internal orientation confusing, Sikong Yao had not loafed around in vain over the past few days. Relying on the extraordinary mnemonic faculties of a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster, he had memorized every district flawlessly. Furthermore, when it came to "demons," an exceptionally conspicuous event had just detonated.

—"Do you refer to the incident where Sect Master Yan Bailang of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall fell into demonic possession?"-Sikong Yao inquired.

This was undeniably the first monumental scandal since the assembly opened.

Though the press was delayed today, barring unforeseen variables, it would dominate the headlines of every newspaper across the realm tomorrow.

Never mind the melodramatic tragedy of conspiring with elders to butcher his senior sister and usurp the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*; Yan Bailang's transformation into a demon alone was sufficient to fuel cultivator gossip for half a year. Crucially, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had broadcast the entire horror live to the world; discussions were currently raging from Thousand Star Hall all the way to the sect's mountain gates.

Regrettably, Sikong Yao had always regarded Upper Three Realms matches as terribly dull, having wandered off to the Middle Three Realms rings instead. Afterward, he had shared drinks with a newly acquainted Eastern Realm dragon noble, perfectly missing the live broadcast. Only when the crowds poured into the tavern did he discover what sensational mayhem had unfolded, and before he could review the recorded projections, Fujuan had summoned him.

Even so, Sikong Yao found nothing extraordinary about the dirty secrets of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall:

Far more vile, disgusting atrocities transpired within the Xiaoyao Palace on a daily basis! Dragging out any random skeleton from their closets was enough to shatter an orthodox cultivator's Dao heart, not to mention that their currently "missing" ancestor hardly resembled a righteous cultivator. Sikong Yao had long suspected that the ancestor was actually...

Sikong Yao halted his train of thought in the nick of time.

Knowing too much did not lead to happiness; that was his supreme law of survival within the Xiaoyao Palace.

Focusing strictly on the facts, Sikong Yao remarked:

—"Although I did not witness Sect Master Yan's demonic fall with my own eyes, judging by the accounts, his descent into madness is hardly surprising, is it?"

—"It is not surprising. The issue lies in the methodology of his transformation..."-Zhou Hui replied. Rather than elaborating, he addressed Jiuzhai:—"However, if even Sikong Yao, who is most adept at sensing peril, cannot detect anything, it truly is harmless, is it not?"

—"Pardon?"-Sikong Yao blinked.

He inexplicably felt as though he were an old rooster thrown into a tomb to test the air quality.

—"Hmph, you are merely striving to shield your own disciple!"-Jiuzhai snapped scornfully.

Zhou Hui saw nothing biased in his defense:

—"Xuan'er was indeed somewhat excessive. Yet had it not been for him, even I might have failed to notice that the previous reincarnation cycle left behind such a colossal vulnerability. If you claim I am deflecting blame, it is solely for my own oversight."

—"Your temperament is simply..."

—"Wait, wait!"-at this point, even Peilan and Tao Zhu could hold their tongues no longer.

Like Sikong Yao, they had been summoned without context. Their intelligence was even less current than Sikong Yao's; neither paid attention to tournament proceedings and hadn't heard the gossip surrounding the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, nor would they have cared if they had.

Tao Zhu asked:

—"Can you at least explain clearly what transpired?"

Zhou Hui remained evasive:

—"It is no grand calamity..."

—"You call this 'no grand calamity'?!"-Jiuzhai's voice turned icy.

A unique pulse of spiritual energy radiated from the dried-orange avatar, sweeping across the entire arena platform.

Instantly, wherever the spiritual energy washed, wisps of dark crimson demonic miasma manifested, forming intricate, spiderweb-like sigils and threads that coiled across the flagstones, waiting for their next victim.

This was merely the diluted residue.

When Yuwen Xuan originally laid the trap, the density had been exponentially thicker. However, not wishing his Master to discover the scheme, the Demon Subduing Array used to seal Yan Bailang had been pre-planned as part of his cleanup protocol. With layer upon layer of containment arrays laid down, the demonic imprints had been buried beneath the surface; had they not been excavated in time, even Zhou Hui would have struggled to unearth them after a while. This was precisely why Jiuzhai had scrambled to assemble everyone immediately after the match to "bear witness."

Jiuzhai suspected that Fujuan's shameless favoritism would lead him to sweep the entire affair under the rug.

In Jiuzhai's view, Fujuan failing to halt his Eldest Disciple upon discovering the demonic trap was already absurd beyond measure; yet afterward, Fujuan had actually set that Taotie free! Set him free!

Even maternal pampering had to have limits!

Jiuzhai resolved to enlist everyone to publicly condemn Fujuan's reckless negligence.

And indeed, the moment the others laid eyes on the demonic imprints, genuine shock gripped their hearts.

—"Demonic miasma imprints?!"-Sikong Yao barely suppressed his reflexive instinct to bolt.

—"These appear exceptionally lethal,"-Peilan frowned.

—"How could there be so many...?"-Old Tao's belly tightened in alarm.

—"Remnants of the Sixth Reincarnation cycle,"-seeing that further evasion was impossible, Zhou Hui laid out the truth.

Specifically, the events of the Sixth Cycle.

—"...In that cycle, I assumed the entire world had been shattered to pieces. I never realized that in corners hidden from my perception, a tedious melodrama had unfolded—demons invaded this world, etching profound scars into reality. Therefore, the fault is entirely mine. When I restructured time and space, my craftsmanship was far too unrefined, failing to realize that demonic corruption had sunk this deep,"-Zhou Hui reflected self-critically, still neglecting to mention Yuwen Xuan's exact role.

—"So your Eldest Disciple is entirely blameless, is that it?!"-Jiuzhai fumed in exasperation.

—"I am not saying he bears zero blame, but overall, no tangible catastrophe materialized..."

—"Hah! No catastrophe? The one who mutated into an abomination is still rotting over in the lounge!"-Jiuzhai glared toward the private quarters, filling in the missing details regarding Yuwen Xuan's arena exploits for the rest of the gathering.

Hearing the full account, aside from Ruwang, who had known all along, everyone stood completely shell-shocked.

Especially Sikong Yao.

He had never heard a whisper that this world had been rebooted multiple times, let alone learning right off the bat that the very bedrock of their current realm had been kneaded together with demonic miasma! As for what Yuwen Xuan did... did Yuwen Xuan's actions matter in the slightest?! Was he even half as terrifying as his Master?!

A hybrid Taotie merely wanted to eat a person (and failed to eat him), digging an arena-sized trap and politely filling it back in afterward—why, that was practically as gentle and adorable as a fluffy little lamb!

By comparison, Fujuan, who had rebooted the world seven times—what kind of Primordial Demon Sovereign was he?!

Even the legendary Demon Emperor couldn't pull off such an apocalyptic feat!

Sikong Yao scrambled to inch several yards further away from Zhou Hui. He suddenly felt that surviving Fujuan's pursuit for all those years was a monumental cosmic miracle worthy of erecting a commemorative monolith!

Sikong Yao thought thus, and Tao Zhu and Peilan shared identical sentiments.

Although Old Tao had heard from Jiuzhai in broad strokes about what Fujuan had done, hearing it in such granular detail imparted a visceral sense of reality to those past cycles, making Fujuan appear progressively more monstrous. In contrast, his Eldest Disciple seemed remarkably benign.

Rather, that Fujuan had trained a ferocious Taotie to the point where it had to sneakily induce someone into demonic corruption before daring to eat them was already a miracle that defied all cosmic reason. Besides, hadn't he just set the beast free?

As long as the Taotie went to terrorize other realms instead of tormenting him, that was fine.

Old Tao mused, feeling strangely "tolerant" toward Yuwen Xuan.

—"Still, to think that lad possessed such methods—quite impressive, honestly!"-Old Tao remarked, surveying the arena with admiration.

—"That is your sole reaction?!"-Jiuzhai stared in sheer disbelief.

—"What else should I do? Drag the boy back for execution?"-Tao Zhu countered:—"Just as Fujuan noted, no genuine harm was inflicted. There is no need to be excessively draconian."

...You truly are worthy of being that Taotie's temporary foster guardian!

In that past cycle, your Abyssal Ocean was snatched away by that Taotie brat, and you look as though you were quite happy about it?!

Jiuzhai suddenly realized he had recruited the wrong witnesses for this trial.

—"I am not demanding the lad's execution,"-Jiuzhai clarified. In truth, he wouldn't mind executing Yuwen Xuan, but voicing it would never earn Fujuan's assent; thus, his current goal was merely to force the "guardian" to shoulder administrative accountability:—"The issue is that Fujuan's Eldest Disciple is far too abnormal. He can actually excavate demonic imprints buried in the seams of time and space! What if he orchestrates identical stunts elsewhere? If nobody is there to clean up the aftermath, the consequences are unthinkable! At the very least, we must uncover the source of this knowledge, restrict his actions, and forbid him from wandering freely!"

—"There is no need to treat him like a living weapon..."-Zhou Hui demurred.

—"That lad is undeniably a living weapon, but who among us here isn't?"-Tao Zhu waved a dismissive hand, asking curiously:—"Though for Yuwen Xuan to achieve this—it truly wasn't taught by you?"

—"I do not know."

—"What does 'do not know' mean?"

—"Recently, I discovered that the child... that primordial beasts process cognition through principles fundamentally alien to ordinary organisms. How he deduced the method to unearth demonic miasma is beyond my knowledge, nor can I confirm whether it was a synthesis of the knowledge I imparted,"-Zhou Hui sighed:—"Were he not Xuan'er, I would genuinely love to dissect and inspect his cerebral architecture."

Hearing this, Tao Zhu leaned close to Old Ancestor Jiuzhai, whispering in a conspiratorial shout:

—"Look at that. You still expect him to discipline his disciple? Don't you feel this man is infinitely more dangerous than his student? If you wish to imprison hazardous elements, you ought to lock him up first!"

[Don't you think I would if I could?!]

Old Ancestor Jiuzhai's white beard twitched violently in silent fury.

He held no personal vendetta against Yuwen Xuan; as a diviner, his instinct was simply to neutralize latent cosmic threats. Yet reminded by Tao Zhu, the brutal truth struck him:

Indeed, who in this universe could ever be more dangerous than Fujuan?

To think he had actually grown accustomed to Fujuan's existence.

Tragic.

Jiuzhai let out a dejected, withered sigh, looking more desiccated than ever.

Zhou Hui, observing Jiuzhai's deflated state, seemed to recall something. He opened his mouth to inquire, but before he could speak, Peilan finally finished synthesizing all the information.

Bizarrely enough, Peilan appeared to be the sole rational individual among this gathering (even though he wasn't strictly human).

—"Rather than bickering over crimes that have yet to materialize or assigning retrospective blame, isn't the most pressing priority this demonic miasma right before our eyes?"-Peilan remarked, tugging at the yellowish whiskers beside his snout:—"Since Yuwen Xuan possessed the means to unearth it, other entities might unearth it as well. These residual scars represent a colossal vulnerability in our world's fabric. In the worst-case scenario, they could invite a second demonic invasion!"

—"That is precisely why I assembled you all here,"-Zhou Hui nodded in agreement.

Mentioning this, he had no further attention to waste on Jiuzhai. Zhou Hui knew intimately that the danger was infinitely more severe than Peilan imagined!

Peilan merely feared *if* someone exploited this flaw; Zhou Hui knew with absolute certainty that someone *would*.

The Heavenly Court.

The Sixth Cycle had been a cosmic script orchestrated by the Heavenly Court using this micro-world's heavenly laws. They were undoubtedly cognizant of this demonic vulnerability. Even if the sect guarded the spatial rift within the Spirit Stone Secret Realm of Qiangu Manor, when the celestials sought to fulfill their "prophecy," manipulating this loophole would be more than sufficient to trigger an apocalyptic demonic invasion.

Zhou Hui frowned:

—"This vulnerability naturally cannot be left open. The optimal resolution would be for me to restructure the world once more, thoroughly cleansing every trace of residual miasma..."

[Isn't that far too absurd?!]

[Do you view this universe as a toy, disassembling and washing it whenever you feel like it?!]

Everyone stared at Zhou Hui in sheer, unadulterated terror.

Fortunately, Zhou Hui continued:

—"Regrettably, the fabric of this world is already exceedingly fragile; I fear another reset would result in total collapse. Therefore, this time, I intend to adopt a conventional approach: constructing a grand sealing array to permanently entomb the hidden miasma."

This process was roughly equivalent to pouring liquid concrete over cat litter; even termites couldn't bore their way through.

Hearing his explanation, the grandmasters exchanged uneasy glances.

To be frank, they held zero faith that anything Fujuan termed "conventional" would ever be conventional.

—"An array? For formation matters, you should seek out the Ran Family!"-Tao Zhu rubbed his belly with an expression of acute reluctance:—"Besides, isn't their child already in your custody?"

Can you please not phrase it as though I were a kidnapper?!

—"That is precisely why I have summoned you all,"-Zhou Hui sighed with grand solemnity:—"Everyone gathered here possesses at least Mahayana cultivation, standing as the supreme elite of our micro-world..."

—"Fujuan, that is quite enough!"-Peilan cut him off. Fujuan's fawning flattery sent icy chills rippling across his fur; compounded by the wholesale distribution of "Friend Cards" earlier, he had an overwhelming premonition that a horrifying chore was about to be dumped onto their heads.

Peilan demanded warily:

—"Speak plainly. What do you want?"

Zhou Hui beamed with a gentle, radiant smile:

—"I simply believe that if it is all of you, you will undoubtedly be capable of journeying to the Heavenly Court to retrieve Ran Zhen, the Clan Head of the Ran Family."

—"...Wait a moment, where did you say the head of the Ran Family is?!"

—"THE HEAVENLY COURT?!"
