# Chapter 101: The Newspaper (第101章 报纸)

[—"Right now, the entire Central Realm has its eyes locked onto our Heavenly Spirit Sect!"](<# "Speaker: Ran Deng">)

[—"Therefore, hiding anything is utterly pointless; rather than concealment, why not showcase it openly and generously for publicity?!"](<# "Speaker: Ran Deng">)

—"In order to achieve a flawless, total victory, we must seize control of [public opinion](<# "Literal CN: 舆论 (yúlùn) — public opinion / narrative discourse">) first!"-someone proclaimed with stirring passion.

A burst of applause immediately erupted inside the room hung with a signboard reading *[White Jade Capital Weekly](<# "Literal CN: 白玉京周报 (Báiyùjīng Zhōubào) — White Jade Capital Weekly; popular weekly newspaper founded by Ran Deng">)*.

Guan Ye sat in the front row with his legs crossed, wearing a completely blank expression that screamed *Who am I? Where am I? What am I supposed to be doing?*

He didn't think there was anything wrong with the words themselves; what was wrong was the person speaking them.

The one speaking was Ran Deng.

Yes, Heavenly Spirit Sect's Eldest Senior Sister, Ran Deng.

After having been martial siblings for a solid one hundred and seventeen years, Guan Ye possessed an unforgettable, bone-deep understanding of his senior sister's level of laziness. In any case, ever since Guan Ye entered Heavenly Spirit Sect, he had never once seen Ran Deng do anything of her own initiative; on the contrary, she was constantly trying to shirk homework and cut corners to slack off.

The most outrageous incident was once when Ran Deng brought snacks into the Scripture Depository to "enter seclusion." Because this sort of "seclusion" of hers was a common occurrence, nobody paid it much mind. Who could have imagined that once she finished all her snacks, she was too lazy to come out? She hadn't brought any inedia pills either, and actually starved herself for half a month, coming within an inch of dying from spiritual energy exhaustion! In the end, it was Master—who was genuinely in secluded cultivation—who suddenly sensed that one of his disciples was about to perish, abruptly halting his meditation and dragging Ran Deng out of the Scripture Depository before catastrophe struck. The only fallout was that it incensed Qu Qianxing so much he didn't give Ran Deng a single pleasant look for thirty straight years.

Therefore, when Ran Deng came looking for Guan Ye, saying she needed a dharma device for printing periodicals to establish *White Jade Capital Weekly*, Guan Ye had assumed it was Master's instructions.

He never imagined this was actually Ran Deng's own idea.

—"It can't be helped; a thing as convenient as a [newspaper](<# "Literal CN: 报纸 (bàozhǐ) — newspaper; modern mass media periodical introduced from Zhou Hui's library">) actually didn't exist in our world,"-Ran Deng had said.

Half a month ago, she had sat before the periodical racks in the "library" constructed from Master's memories, leafing through that thing called a "newspaper" while arguing logically to Guan Ye:

—"With this, we can spread Master's ideas much better, so we won't be framed or exploited by others."

Guan Ye stared into Ran Deng's eyes:

—"What's your honest thought?"-Guan Ye asked.

—"I want to read [juicy gossip](<# "Literal CN: 八卦 (bāguà) — juicy gossip / tabloid rumors">) from all across the world,"-Ran Deng answered with utmost honesty.

Guan Ye turned to leave, only to have his ["tail"](<# "Literal CN: 尾巴 (wěiba) — tail; refers to Guan Ye's oversized prosthetic spine protruding from his lower back">) grabbed by Ran Deng.

—Namely, that section protruding outside his back because of the oversized, ill-fitting replacement spine.

—"Wait a second, don't leave! Weren't you the one who asked for the honest truth?!"-Ran Deng protested.

—"Master instructed me to craft a '[color inkjet printing dharma device](<# "Literal CN: 彩色喷绘印刷法器 (cǎisè pēnhuì yìnshuā fǎqì) — color spray-painting/inkjet printing magical apparatus">)' for an important academic journal, not for Senior Sister to fool around with,"-Guan Ye said with utter seriousness.

—"I'm not fooling around! Didn't I already give you legitimate reasons?"-Ran Deng said.-"Journals are sold to high-tier almighty cultivators, and very few people can understand them. But newspapers and periodicals are sold to the general populace, wielding influence over public opinion. They can also guide the public narrative for those who can't understand the journals, preventing people from deliberately distorting Master's intentions."

Saying this, Ran Deng handed over a newspaper:

—"Take a look and you'll understand,"-Ran Deng urged.

—"..."-Guan Ye wordlessly perused the newspaper carefully.

Ever since Zhou Hui entrusted the "library" to Guan Ye and Ran Deng, Guan Ye's primary attention had been focused on technical books in [STEM fields](<# "Literal CN: 理工科 (lǐgōngkē) — science and engineering / STEM">); he hadn't paid attention to these "sheets of paper" for a while. However, after carefully reading through several newspapers, Guan Ye agreed with Ran Deng's reasoning.

—"It is indeed very useful,"-Guan Ye said.-"Why don't you report directly to Master? Master would gladly approve, and the funding would be covered by our sect."

—"No way,"-Ran Deng wore a look of utter disgust.-"If the sect provides funding, Qu Qianxing will definitely demand to audit the paper! In that case, whether my newspaper could keep its eighty percent gossip layout would be anyone's guess—wait, wait, wait! Why are you walking away?!"

[—"I have no desire to print layouts that are eighty percent gossip."](<# "Speaker: Guan Ye">)

—"This is necessary guidance!"-Ran Deng cried out.-"Do you think everyone is as rigid and stiff-necked as you and Second Senior Brother?! If we made it purely academic, isn't the journal enough? The masses love vulgar... lively and entertaining things! Take these newspapers, for instance: compared to this XX News whatever, this Entertainment Daily is so much more fun, right? Shulong."

Lu Shulong had been flipping through that Entertainment Daily; suddenly called on, his eyes went wide.

Guan Ye gave a cold snort:

—"Ah Long isn't even human; his opinion isn't representative,"-Guan Ye scoffed with a cold snort.

—"Then use that stiff head of yours to think carefully about what ordinary people prefer to discuss in their spare time! I know you'd rather see them pass the time with [advanced calculus](<# "Literal CN: 高等数学 (gāoděng shùxué) — higher mathematics / advanced calculus">), but the only person who would ever do something like that is Old Second! Not even Master would do that,"-Ran Deng curled her lip.

*Pfft!* Lu Shulong burst out laughing to the side.

He set down the pile of newspapers to join Ran Deng in cajoling:

—"Fourth Senior Brother, just agree to Eldest Senior Sister! It's so rare for Eldest Senior Sister to actively want to do something! She's even paying out of her own pocket,"-Lu Shulong pleaded.

—"Precisely because she's doing it on her own, I'm worried it's just a [fleeting fad](<# "Literal CN: 三分钟热度 (sān fēnzhōng rèdù) — three-minute enthusiasm; a fleeting fad / short-lived burst of interest">),"-Guan Ye said, not giving his senior sister an ounce of face.-"Even with Second Senior Brother presiding over the journal and Eternal Villa assisting, they're running around in circles. Eldest Senior Sister's newspaper sounds like it'll have an even larger circulation, won't it? Can she even handle the workload?"

Ran Deng instantly puffed out her chest:

—"How could I possibly do it myself? I plan to hand it over to Yu Feifei, the manager of One-Sorrow Tea, to run!"

She hadn't even started, yet she had obviously already mapped out her route for slacking off.

At this attitude of hers, Guan Ye rolled his eyes, wanting only to leave.

Yet Ran Deng held fast to his "tail," refusing to let go:

—"Please, Ye-zi! Last time you needed to lay the spirit spring plumbing, didn't I help out without a second word?"-Ran Deng pleaded, clutching his "tail" tightly.

—"That's right, Fourth Senior Brother! Even though Eldest Senior Sister has her own selfish motives, she surely also wants to repay Master and ease his worries,"-Lu Shulong chimed in, hugging Guan Ye's tail as well.

—"Exactly, exactly! Didn't Master put me in charge of One-Sorrow Tea? The profits from that are astronomical! If I don't do something for the sect, my conscience will prick me to death!"-Ran Deng added.

The two of them looked at Guan Ye with pleading eyes.

Guan Ye felt as though his spine were about to be ripped out all over again.

He tried swishing his "tail" to shake the two of them off, but failed. Instead, Lu Shulong's thoughts came transmitting through the "tail" in fragmented waves:

[It's rare for Eldest Senior Sister to actively do something; this is something that never happened in the 'past.']

[Even though Eldest Senior Sister is always the first to perceive Master's intentions, she is also always the first to give up.]

[Whether it was leaving or dying, she was too lazy to resist. Yet only this time, she's doing something she never did before. Even if she really is just playing around, maybe it could create a miracle?]

...Although Ran Deng's behavior was abnormal, it surely wasn't to that extent.

Guan Ye's lips twitched as he met Lu Shulong's gaze.

That ill-fitting spine—who knew what creature it had originally belonged to, but it possessed an uncanny affinity with Lu Shulong.

Back when Lu Shulong hatched from his egg, he had imprinted on Master as his parent, clinging to Master relentlessly. If Master let go of him even for a moment, he would wail uncontrollably—and it was the sort of wailing that summoned torrential flash floods, infuriating Eldest Senior Brother so much that he felt even if he couldn't eat the whelp, he ought to toss him into the Abyssal Sea.

It was only when Guan Ye's "tail" accidentally brushed against little Shulong that Lu Shulong, for the very first time, let go of Master's robes to grab Guan Ye's "tail," finally liberating Master.

Miraculously, Guan Ye could also perceive Lu Shulong's needs through the "tail." As a result, Lu Shulong spent half of his childhood growing up while hugging Guan Ye's "tail."

Because of this, among all the martial brothers and sisters, Guan Ye and Lu Shulong shared the closest bond.

Lu Shulong was naturally grateful for Guan Ye's care.

Yet Guan Ye was equally grateful for Lu Shulong's existence.

Back then, Guan Ye had viewed himself as complete trash, having almost entirely lost the will to live. He felt that even though he had been saved, a piece of garbage like him could never repay Master. It was the newly hatched Lu Shulong who gave Guan Ye a reason to remain by Master's side.

Yet even between the "closest" martial brothers, Guan Ye never felt he truly understood Lu Shulong.

Rather, Guan Ye felt Lu Shulong was the most incomprehensible of them all.

He didn't know whether it was the racial barrier between "human" and "True Dragon," but aside from Lu Shulong's basic physical needs, when it came to his psychological thoughts, Guan Ye found them completely baffling.

Particularly in recent years, merely drawing near Lu Shulong made Guan Ye sense an abyss-like melancholy emanating from him, cold and bottomless like the Abyssal Sea, threatening to drown everyone in its depths.

Yet Lu Shulong acted as if nothing were wrong. No matter how Guan Ye questioned him, he would only stare back with clear, innocent eyes, infuriating anyone who looked at him.

Right now, however, Lu Shulong's eyes were no longer clear; they were somewhat dark and somber, carrying a hint of probing and resolve.

Seeing him like this, Guan Ye let out a sigh.

—"Fine, I'll collaborate,"-Guan Ye finally relented with a sigh.

However, to print a newspaper, the number of printing presses allocated for the journals certainly wouldn't suffice; new ones would have to be forged from scratch.

Guan Ye mentally calculated the Artifact Refinement Hall's workload, then looked at Ran Deng:

—"However, Senior Sister, even if it is 'your' private newspaper, before I agree, I will review the content once. It cannot all be weird, bizarre nonsense—"-Guan Ye insisted.

—"Rest assured, I'll give priority advertising to the Artifact Refinement Hall!"-Ran Deng said happily.

—"That's not what I'm talking about!"-Guan Ye snapped.

---

The above was the reason why Guan Ye was currently sitting in the editorial office of *White Jade Capital Weekly*.

Still, a fleeting fad was at least a burst of enthusiasm.

This time, Ran Deng had indeed displayed unprecedented initiative. Not only had she personally taken time to design the layout for every page of this weekly and set the newspaper's editorial tone, but she had also personally convened this mobilization meeting, and even planned to oversee the publication of the first few issues herself.

Watching this "energetic" Ran Deng, Guan Ye couldn't help harboring the thought: [Just who on earth is this?]

If Master hadn't been presiding over Heavenly Spirit Sect, Guan Ye would have suspected his senior sister had been [possessed by an old monster](<# "Literal CN: 夺舍 (duóshě) — soul-possession / seizing the body">).

Unaware of Guan Ye's silent roasting, Ran Deng said cheerfully:

—"Alright, let's examine the specific articles."

—"The front-page headline for this issue naturally belongs to our venerable Master! We must let everyone know about the Bureau of Rites' foolish blunder! However, remember to separate him from the Heavenly Dao Alliance; offending the Heavenly Dao Alliance outright isn't wise. We should concentrate our firepower on that Si Wu fellow."

—"The secondary focus will be... whatever academic content is in the journal. Although it's rather boring, this is what establishes our newspaper's professional authority, and what will make people read it repeatedly. For this section, we can invite authoritative figures for further analysis. For instance, Manor Lord Qingyang's paper could be interpreted by Fairy Qingyu—that offers both a selling point and authority! For other papers, we can invite disciples of various mighty experts to review them, and select those with the highest public appeal for recurring commissions. But don't make it all positive reviews; we can also find dissenting arguments to stir up debates. The livelier and noisier it is, the better."

—"Still, these reports will inevitably pique people's interest in Heavenly Spirit Sect. My advice is: don't be afraid! Announce our information boldly and openly, leaving no room for others to misunderstand—"-Ran Deng said in an orderly, methodical fashion.

Just then, someone raised a hand:

—"Milady Ran Deng, I have a proposal,"-Yu Feifei spoke up, raising her hand.

It was Yu Feifei.

This self-proclaimed Captain of Mo Bei's Guard Brigade had been borrowed from Mo Bei's side by Ran Deng to serve as the editor-in-chief of *White Jade Capital Weekly*, thanks to her outstanding performance managing One-Sorrow Tea.

This could be considered a promotion.

Though in Yu Feifei's eyes, having to step away from her duties as captain of the guard brigade was quite a sacrifice.

And this sacrifice wasn't made for the sake of a "promotion," but for another grand objective.

At this moment, Yu Feifei declared:

—"Perhaps we could open a dedicated long-term column for this? For example, how about running commentary on the outstanding disciples of major sects? That way, we can quietly slip in our sect's elites—such as Junior Sister Mo Bei... and Milady Ran Deng."

—"You can leave me out,"-Ran Deng curled her lip, but encouraged,-"keep going."

—"Whether it's Milady Ran Deng or Junior Sister Mo Bei, you are both infinitely superior to those undeserving frauds from other sects!"-Yu Feifei said smoothly, glossing over her "minor slip-up.-"However, our Heavenly Spirit Sect has kept too low a profile in the past, causing your excellence to remain unknown. Those empty-reputation frauds are the perfect stepping stones! We can run a few issues praising the most famous 'young heroes' and 'fairies' familiar to the Central Realm, casually dropping Junior Sister Mo Bei's name among them to first plant the concept that she is one of the 'Central Realm's sect elites' in everyone's minds. Afterward, we can hype her according to her tournament performance—"

Guan Ye could tell: this girl was trying to protect Mo Bei!

Indeed, with Heavenly Spirit Sect currently in the spotlight, disciples participating in the Immortal Arts Assembly would inevitably face intense scrutiny.

Eldest Senior Brother and Shulong were fine; those two non-humans essentially lacked shame nerves altogether. But Little Junior Sister was still a girl, after all; if she suffered a setback due to the judgmental stares of others, it would be too much of a pity.

This girl's proposal was to use others to deflect attention, ensuring Little Junior Sister's debut wouldn't be overly jarring.

Moreover, Little Junior Sister had friends... this counted as a "friend," right? In any case, it was truly heartwarming that someone cared for Little Junior Sister like this.

Guan Ye nodded in approval.

As someone who preferred to keep a low profile, Guan Ye liked this method very much.

—"A decent idea..."-Ran Deng mused, but then shook her head.-"We can promote Eldest Senior Brother and Fifth Senior Brother that way, but not Mo Bei."

—"Why?!"-Yu Feifei exclaimed in disbelief.

[Because Master seems to have other plans for Bei'er.]

Ran Deng thought to herself.

It had started back during the Celestial Chess match. Normally, in such a competition, Master would never have let Mo Bei—who needed to conceal her Extreme Yin Body—stand in the limelight. Yet Master had entered Mo Bei into the internal sect tournament, making her the leader of the sword cultivators, and this time had even placed Mo Bei alongside those two monsters, Eldest Senior Brother and Fifth Senior Brother!

Just as Lu Shulong had said, Ran Deng was always the one quickest to discern Master's intentions.

After all, compared to the other six disciples, Ran Deng possessed the highest emotional intelligence—even if it occasionally leaked like a sieve.

When she saw Master enter Eldest Senior Brother and Fifth Senior Brother into the Immortal Arts Assembly, even without knowing Master was gathering karmic fortune, Ran Deng understood that Master—who usually cared nothing for fame or fortune—for some reason had to win this tournament, and win it in spectacular fashion.

After all, those two were the strongest among the martial siblings!

—Qu Qianxing was formidable too, but he was ultimately at the mortal human level, unlike those two monsters.

Yet Bei'er had been grouped alongside those two monsters!

Master clearly held extraordinary expectations for Mo Bei.

Naturally, Ran Deng wouldn't say any of these observations to the other disciples.

She could only analyze Master's reasons from her own perspective:

—"It's a matter of educational approach. Because that child Bei'er... is a plant,"-Ran Deng explained.

[—"A plant?"](<# "Speaker: Yu Feifei">)

—"Yes. A plant that has surrendered its right to choose, accepting whatever others give it,"-Ran Deng assessed Mo Bei.

When Ran Deng wasn't reading, she observed people—not just Master.

Her senior and junior martial brothers and sisters were naturally subjects of her observation.

When Mo Bei and Mo Xi first arrived at Heavenly Spirit Sect, Ran Deng had observed them for a long time. Especially Mo Bei, being the second female disciple Master had taken in; Ran Deng had originally hoped to grow closer to her.

In the end, Ran Deng quickly discovered that the pair of twins were practically a set of [ghost dolls](<# "Literal CN: 鬼娃娃 (guǐ wáwa) — ghost dolls; eerie, lifeless porcelain dolls">). Not being particularly fond of horror stories, she didn't dare delve into their inner world, deciding that being a pair of [plastic sisters](<# "Literal CN: 塑料师姐妹 (sùliào shī-jiěmèi) — plastic martial sisters; superficial friendship that looks shiny but lacks genuine emotional depth">) was quite enough.

Yet that didn't prevent Ran Deng from understanding Mo Bei:

—"But this also means that if you give her expectations, she is willing to grow. The stronger the expectation, the harder she will strive to respond. Therefore, vague reports that lump her in with the crowd are meaningless. We must let her know that she is our sect's [facade](<# "Literal CN: 门面 (ménmiàn) — facade / public face / poster child">)! Our golden signboard! A presence loved by all! And then, she will grow into exactly that,"-Ran Deng declared.

—"How could anyone possibly..."-Yu Feifei instinctively started to object.

Yet recalling her interactions with Mo Bei over these past days, she faltered.

That Mo Bei... might actually obediently do just that.

—"Still, wouldn't that be a bit too harsh on Junior Sister Mo Bei?"-Yu Feifei finally asked after a long pause.

—"If we only treat her as a decorative storefront signboard, that would indeed be harsh of us,"-Ran Deng countered.-"However, if the wish is for her to be loved by everyone, from her own perspective, that ought to be something worth celebrating too, wouldn't it?"

[If she becomes someone loved by all, will she perhaps learn to love herself a little?]

—"Isn't that demand of yours [forcing the impossible](<# "Literal CN: 强人所难 (qiǎng rén suǒ nán) — forcing someone to do the impossible / demanding too much">)?"-Guan Ye couldn't help chiming in this time.

—"On the contrary, I believe Junior Sister Mo Bei is worthy of everyone's love,"-Yu Feifei asserted firmly.

[...Is there something wrong with your head?]

Guan Ye glared at Yu Feifei, finding her completely unreasonable.

He wanted to say something to dissuade Ran Deng from adopting such an aggressive strategy that would heap immense pressure on Little Junior Sister, but out of the corner of his eye, he caught a flash of sword light outside the window. A sect disciple came crashing through the window together with his flying sword, smashing straight into the opposite wall and tumbling into a pathetic heap.

The disciple didn't even have time to stand before shouting breathlessly:

—"Huge news! Breaking news! Junior Sister Mo has won the preliminary tournament championship of the Immortal Arts Assembly in [Fengqing City](<# "Literal CN: 风清城 (Fēngqīng Chéng) — Fengqing City; southern city linked to White Jade Capital via an ancient spatial teleportation array">)!"

[—"What?!"](<# "Speakers: Ran Deng, Guan Ye & Yu Feifei">)

---

Time rewound to the day the Immortal Arts Assembly was held.

After Zhou Hui finished organizing the karmic fortune within White Jade Capital, he casually summoned an azure bird from within White Jade Capital, sending it to fetch Mo Bei.

—"Why call her here?"-The little black lamb voiced its displeasure.

It felt dinner time was approaching, and it wanted to eat with Master—the kind of dinner where Master ate and it secretly stole bites.

—"It's about time she went out for some real-world experience,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Real-world experience?"-The little black lamb's ears twitched, then snapped straight up.-"Master, Master, I want to go too! Take me with you!"

[Don't you dare think of abandoning me to take Junior Sister out on an excursion!]

—"Go tell your Little Junior Sister; if she's willing to take you along, you may go,"-Zhou Hui said.

—"Why do I need her permission? Obviously..."-The little black lamb paused, its not-too-bright sheep brain finally catching on.-"You aren't going?"

—"If I tagged along, how could it be considered real-world experience?"-Zhou Hui countered.

—"But haven't you always been overprotective of those two? Acting like they'd be abducted the moment they stepped outside... Well, those two really aren't that smart, so they'd definitely get abducted!"-The little black lamb laughed, baring two rows of sharp little teeth.

[You have the gall to mock your junior brother and sister when you look even dimmer than they do?]

Zhou Hui roasted him in his heart, but still explained:

—"Now that White Jade Capital has been partially restored, letting them venture outside is no longer an issue."

As he spoke, he tapped the southeast corner of the White Jade Capital "model."

In that location stood a well-preserved teleportation tower, precisely the tower representing Fengqing City.

The massive spatial teleportation array connecting Fengqing City to the Southern Realm had been passed down from the ancient era, so White Jade Capital still retained a seat for it.

As Zhou Hui tapped his finger, several miniature soldiers marched toward the teleportation array, lighting it up.

With the illumination of that teleportation array, a new "city model" manifested.

It was Fengqing City!

However, compared to the Fengqing City of tens of thousands of years ago, the present Fengqing City was much smaller, and its center had shifted—originally, the teleportation array had merely been an auxiliary facility of the city, whereas the modern teleportation array was situated inside the City Lord's Estate at the heart of the city.

This caused the projected image to appear slightly fragmented.

Yet for Zhou Hui, it was more than enough.

Whichever disciple went to Fengqing City, Zhou Hui could perceive their movements at any moment, fish them back at any time, assist them with divine arts, or refrain from interfering directly and simply deploy soldiers from White Jade Capital to reinforce them.

In other words, Mo Bei and Mo Xi could finally travel a bit further without Master hovering beside them.

—"Baa, still overprotective,"-the little black lamb grumbled petulantly.

Losing interest in this, it trotted down from the table on its little hooves, aiming to drag Master's favorite prayer mat into a cozy nest and bury itself comfortably inside. To that end, it even sneakily filched the fruit platter from Master's desk.

[Is this brat planning on taking root right here?]

Zhou Hui noted the little black lamb's movements and said deliberately:

—"Rest assured, I will be overprotective of you as well. Although the Soul Formation stage matches are scheduled later, your turn will come."

—"?! I have to compete too?!"-The little black lamb froze in shock.

—"Shulong has to as well. I told you both last time, didn't I? As expected, you weren't listening—"-Zhou Hui cast a stern glance at Yuwen Xuan.

The little black lamb instinctively shrank back, but unable to care about being scolded, it pleaded anxiously:

—"B-But how am I supposed to compete in this state?!"

—"Eat fewer strange things and work hard on transforming back,"-Zhou Hui replied.

—"Baa!"-The little lamb let out a mournful wail.

Unfortunately, it had no opportunity to bargain further, for Mo Bei had already arrived at the entrance of Zhou Hui's immortal cave.

Stepping through the doorway, Mo Bei's first words were:

—"Did I hear Eldest Senior Brother's voice?"

The little black lamb buried itself deeper into the prayer mat.

Zhou Hui glanced at the little black lamb and smiled:

—"He was here just a moment ago."

—"Oh,"-Mo Bei nodded.

She asked nothing further, seemingly satisfied with the answer.

If it had been Xi'er, he would have probed to the very bottom. This child was simply like this, seeming completely devoid of curiosity toward anything.

Zhou Hui sighed softly and said:

—"Bei'er, I plan to send you on a trip to Fengqing City."

—"Yes, Master,"-Mo Bei had no objections whatsoever, her expression unchanged. However, she had clearly misunderstood just like Yuwen Xuan.-"When do we set out?"

—"No, I am not going, Mo Xi is not going, and neither are your other senior brothers and sisters,"-Zhou Hui said.-"You will go alone... No, not entirely alone. You will lead a group of disciples from our sect. As their captain!"

—"?!"-Mo Bei finally showed a look of astonishment, her eyes widening as she stared at Zhou Hui.

Ever since arriving at Heavenly Spirit Sect, this was the very first time she was being dispatched outside "alone."

Not to mention, without Mo Xi by her side.

Zhou Hui did not elaborate further, only saying:

—"I don't know if Mo Xi told you, but although the Immortal Arts Assembly has begun, the situation in Fengqing City is somewhat chaotic. I hear cultivators from outer realms are attempting to infiltrate, and someone needs to deal with it. Conveniently, you already competed in our internal sect tournament, so competing against our own disciples again holds little meaning. Go participate in Fengqing City's Immortal Arts Assembly! And while you're at it, help Mo Xi resolve the chaos over there."

[Master, are you serious?]

Mo Bei stared blankly at Zhou Hui, her lips trembling several times.

Theoretically, she knew she ought to refuse, because she wasn't good at handling such complicated matters at all; she would surely make a complete mess of things! Moreover, wasn't Fengqing City an area once deeply controlled by the Carefree Palace? Wouldn't going there be dangerous?

Yet Mo Bei was, after all, Mo Bei.

That obedient [automaton](<# "Literal CN: 人偶 (rén'ǒu) — puppet / doll / automaton; someone who moves only upon command">).

She fundamentally could not defy her "master."

—"Understood, Master,"-Mo Bei heard her own voice answer obediently, just as it always did.
