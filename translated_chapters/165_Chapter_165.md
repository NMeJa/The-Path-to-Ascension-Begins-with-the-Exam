# Chapter 165: Battle of Spells

—"Where are you?"-Zhou Hui inquired, looking at the characters.

"You," of course, referred to the "Heavenly Scripture."

This Heavenly Scripture could read the pre-compiled Heavenly Dao scripts, clearly functioning as a reading port of the Heavenly Dao. Moreover, its primary host port was very likely the crucial key through which the True Immortals modified this world's Heavenly Dao—perhaps held in the hands of some True Immortal, or even located somewhere in the Upper Realm. Naturally, Zhou Hui was exceptionally curious about the location of its "central server."

Regrettably, the Heavenly Scripture gave no answer to Zhou Hui's question.

It wasn't that it refused to reply; the contorted strokes squirmed like tadpoles, only to be violently wiped away before they could even coalesce into words. In their place, even more bizarre and nonsensical characters surfaced—no... those hardly looked like words at all, but rather like some kind of spray paint splattered across the Heavenly Scripture, forming grotesque patterns.

—"Could someone actually be modifying the Heavenly Scripture?"-Zhou Hui murmured.

He grew rather cheerful at this speculation.

He had originally come searching for a pathway to the Upper Realm; if he could seize this opportunity to catch someone red-handed, that would be ideal.

Or perhaps, with this opportunity, he wouldn't even need to sit for the Upper Realm's [civil service exams (lol)](# "Literal CN: 公务员考试（x） — civil service examination; modern Chinese internet slang using (x) as a self-deprecating humorous strikethrough/aside mark") to secure clearance, unlocking another Upper Realm map much closer to the Heavenly Court!

Zhou Hui mused optimistically. His magical power coalesced into an enormous writing brush, which began writing across the uncanny paper floor.

* * *

Meanwhile, in the depths of the Abyssal Ocean, within the Langhuan Forbidden Grounds.

—"So that's how it is! To think it was actually like this!"-Tao Zhu exclaimed, clicking his tongue in wonder.

He looked less like the boss of the Abyssal Ocean Society who had dominated the Abyssal Ocean for millennia, and more like an intern who had just stepped into an antique shop, finding everything utterly fresh and novel.

Small wonder he was like this.

The Langhuan Forbidden Grounds was both within his imagination and far beyond his wildest dreams.

In the conventional perception of ordinary folk, Langhuan—as the imperial library of the Heavenly Emperor—ought to be packed with books everywhere.

In truth, that was essentially the case.

There truly were books everywhere here—books of every conceivable kind, piled high in every conceivable corner:

Paper-bound volumes stacked full on bookshelves, tea tables, stone steps, and flooring;

Bamboo scrolls crammed across flower trellises, bamboo groves, ancient boughs, and grassy meadows;

Stele inscriptions carved in bone and jade paving courtyards, plazas, lotus ponds, and colossal boulders;

Engravings in bronze and stone filling pillars, palace walls, massive cauldrons, and sheer precipices...

Indeed, there were books everywhere, yet they were not entirely mere books either.

The entire Langhuan Forbidden Grounds was a complete minor world, possessing mountains, waterways, trees, and blossoms. Yet every single creation was constructed in a form capable of bearing texts; wherever one walked, reaching out a hand allowed one to pull out a book.

Furthermore, these "bookshelves" forged of blossoms, flora, peaks, and groves were far more than just shelving—they were "books" in their own right.

In the Langhuan Forbidden Grounds, a pebble casually picked off the path was a book, the grain of the stone concealing a profound allegory; a leaf drifting down from a bough was a book, the veins of the foliage carrying a heartbreaking poem; a handful of water scooped from a pond was a book, every single droplet holding a philosophical debate on cosmic truth.

If a book lover were to step into this forbidden sanctuary, they would likely find themselves [unable to take a single step forward](# "Literal CN: 寸步难行 (cùnbù nánxíng) — hard to move even an inch / completely paralyzed; here meaning overwhelmed by endless reading material")!

For with every step taken, they would encounter something they yearned to read, losing themselves entirely without any desire to leave. Someone like Ran Deng in particular could probably manage to starve herself to death right here.

Regrettably, the two fellows who had broken into the long-sealed Langhuan Forbidden Grounds this time were both individuals with zero interest in reading.

Boss Tao marveled all along the way, yet his eyes reflected nothing but spirit stones; in reality, he was constantly calculating how many spirit stones each of these books could fetch if taken outside.

As for Yuwen Xuan, he was even worse than the [money-grubbing](# "Literal CN: 铜臭入脑 (tóngchòu rùnǎo) — the stench of copper coins invading one's brain; obsessed with filthy lucre") Boss Tao.

He held not the slightest interest in books of any kind. His sole desire was to doodle, scribble graffiti, and—ideally—eat them.

Yet, perhaps owing to how completely insensitive to romantic elegance these two were, they surprisingly encountered not a single decent obstacle along their journey. They simply marched straight in, penetrating directly into the heart of Langhuan.

This was also where the "Heavenly Scripture" enshrined in Langhuan was located.

Legend held that the "Heavenly Scripture" here was the complete edition of that Heavenly Scripture in the Heavenly Scripture Pagoda, which had drifted down and scattered from the Upper Realm. It recorded everything in this world, including the past, the present, and the future. From this book, one could obtain all desired knowledge.

Naturally, studying demanded a price.

Consider that the Heavenly Scripture Pagoda pinned down merely a few pages, yet it caused the vast majority of cultivators in the cultivation world to suffer endlessly, leaving them terrified of approaching. What then of the entire Heavenly Scripture?

Tao Zhu was genuinely worried that a single glance would cause him to bleed to death from his seven orifices.

Yet to Tao Zhu's complete astonishment, upon actually arriving before the Heavenly Scripture, he discovered that the Heavenly Scripture was not a book at all—it was a tree.

A withered, dead old tree.

It had once been luxuriantly lush and deeply rooted. Its massive roots had seized firmly upon a parcel of earth, forming the very bedrock of Langhuan; its titanic branches had hoisted up a dome of sky, creating the celestial canopy of Langhuan. Or rather, it was Langhuan itself.

Yet such a tree had already perished.

Its boughs and trunk were desiccated and withered, without a single leaf remaining. Its roots were riddled with gaping holes, barely able to anchor to the earth below. Most tragic of all was its trunk: it had practically been snapped in two at the waist, with only a slender strip of bark stubbornly connecting top and bottom.

Even so, this dead tree still propped up the entirety of Langhuan, preventing the realm from collapsing completely.

Beholding this sight, Tao Zhu let out an agonized cry:

—"How could it have ended up like this?! What a waste! An absolute tragedy! Like this, nothing can be read at all!"-Tao Zhu grieved in heartbreak. His entire portfolio of moneymaking schemes would have to be restructured.

Refusing to accept this, Boss Tao turned to confirm with Yuwen Xuan:

—"What state was this secret realm in when you found it? How on earth did it... What are you eating?!"-Tao Zhu asked.

—"Leaves,"-Yuwen Xuan replied, casually plucking off another one.

Tao Zhu's eyes bulged wide. Striding over to Yuwen Xuan's side, he finally noticed that behind this withered old tree, a tiny fresh bough had newly sprouted.

It seemed this tree hadn't perished completely after all; it had found new life.

Only, the color of the new foliage was somewhat yellowish-black, looking rather sickly and unhealthy.

What was more, the single leaf sticking out at the very tip had just been plucked off and eaten by Yuwen Xuan.

Yet the newborn sprig refused to be devoured so passively. Struggling, it actually managed to sprout another tiny leaf—which was promptly plucked off again by Yuwen Xuan.

—"What do you think you're doing?!"-Boss Tao bellowed in fury at the sight.-"Something so precious is—*croak!*"

He abruptly let out the croak of a frog.

Naturally, this sound hadn't come from Boss Tao himself; rather, his belly had convulsed violently, venting that bizarre screech.

—"...It's crying out to someone for help?"-Tao Zhu murmured, looking solemn as though having received some warning.

The moment the next leaf sprouted, he plucked it even faster than Yuwen Xuan, stuffed it into his mouth, and chewed vigorously. Yuwen Xuan glared at him with a disgruntled expression, but considering he still needed to use this fellow later, he raised no objection.

Tao Zhu's face, however, went completely blank—as though he had made contact with an unknown cosmos, his brain freezing up because it couldn't process the sheer volume of data.

Only after several minutes did he finally snap back to his senses.

Boss Tao took a deep breath, stepped back a few paces from this newborn sprig, and cast an awestruck glance at Yuwen Xuan.

As expected of someone possessing half of a Taotie's soul. Eating this stuff didn't affect him in the slightest.

—"...You just guard right here. Don't let it sprout any more,"-Boss Tao instructed Yuwen Xuan.

Knowing that this tree was actually sending out a distress signal to "someone," Tao Zhu naturally dared not be careless.

After all, this was the "Heavenly Scripture"!

What kind of existence would a Heavenly Scripture cry out to for help? Even the rank of a True Immortal seemed insufficient. A deity? An ancient demon god?

If such an existence were provoked, it would inevitably trigger earth-shattering catastrophe.

Yet having come this far, abandoning the endeavor was out of the question for Tao Zhu.

Boss Tao dared to excavate lost treasures in the perilous depths of the Abyssal Ocean—where anything could exist and danger lurked in every shadow—and even turn them into trade commodities. This level of hazard was something he was more than willing to confront.

Since he knew there was another location capable of contacting this Heavenly Scripture, Boss Tao's pace accelerated dramatically.

He had to refine and subjugate the Heavenly Scripture before the "someone" summoned by the tree could make their move.

The newborn sapling seemed to understand Tao Zhu's words; dispirited, it dared not sprout any new leaves. However, the instant Yuwen Xuan shifted his gaze, it immediately squeezed out a leaf from an inconspicuous nook—only for Yuwen Xuan to devour it in a flash.

Meanwhile, Boss Tao pulled out chains strung thick with heavy copper coins, laying down layer upon layer of restrictive formations around the entire withered old tree.

After draping the old tree from top to bottom with copper coins, Tao Zhu prudently positioned himself at a safe distance from the trunk. Puffing his belly out high, he unleashed a loud:

—"*Croak!*"-Boss Tao bellowed.

This frog croak boomed like a bronze bell, deafening to the ears.

Accompanying this croak, a cloud of dark green venomous mist spewed from Boss Tao's mouth, showering down upon the "Heavenly Scripture." A coat of dark green sap instantly enveloped the surface of the withered tree like creeping moss, spreading rapidly toward the root network and boughs in an attempt to engulf the entire tree.

Naturally, the Heavenly Scripture refused to be refined so meekly. In a burst, it sprouted several new leaves at once, desperate to voice its protest. Unfortunately, no sooner did they sprout than they were devoured by Yuwen Xuan once more.

The newborn sprig shook with righteous fury.

Alas, having only just begun to regrow, its innate strength was far too frail—even if it was the Heavenly Scripture. In short order, the dark green toxic mist crept up to the joint connecting it to the old tree.

Right at that moment, however, an anomalous power entered the fray.

A tiny cluster of silver-white brilliance burst forth from the fresh sprout, bathing the newborn sprig in a translucent, pearlescent glow. Sweeping downward along the branch, it collided directly against the dark green venomous mist.

Witnessing this, Yuwen Xuan's eyelid twitched violently. A deeply ominous premonition suddenly seized him.

...No, in truth, even when he first saw the Heavenly Scripture's leaves, Yuwen Xuan had already felt rather uneasy; but he had assumed it was merely the innate power of the Heavenly Scripture, so he had recklessly devoured it regardless.

Now, however, with the appearance of that white light, that premonition amplified tenfold.

It was an uncanny, hair-raising sensation that made his heart leap into his throat.

—"Boss Tao, someone seems to be interfering here!"-Yuwen Xuan warned anxiously.

—"I sense it too!"-Tao Zhu replied, maintaining his mist-spewing state as his voice emanated directly from his belly.-"Don't talk to me! You'll disturb my technique!"

Saying so, Tao Zhu ramped up the output of toxic mist, forcing the silver-white glow back by an inch or two.

Seeing this, rather than feeling reassured, Yuwen Xuan felt the terrifying dread in his chest grow even stronger.

Yet the silver-white light showed no sign of rushing to compete with Boss Tao. It merely held steady upon the newborn bough, glowing ever brighter until, beneath the dark green canopy of the trunk, it resembled a resting silver moon.

Bathed in this soft yet luminous radiance, the sprig trembled and finally produced another leaf.

A pearlescent, translucent, exquisitely beautiful leaf.

Yuwen Xuan plucked it without mercy all the same, stuffing it into his mouth.

This time, he didn't swallow. Instead, a look of sheer, unadulterated horror overtook his face as he shrieked at Tao Zhu:

—"Boss Tao, stop! Stop doing that! It's...!!!"-Yuwen Xuan screamed in terror.

—"I told you not to distract me!"-Tao Zhu snapped, cutting Yuwen Xuan off before he could finish.-"I think I can take this!"

[No, you can't.]

Yuwen Xuan thought in absolute despair.

He glanced back toward the passage they had arrived from, desperately wanting to bolt. Had he still been the little black lamb, he might well have done just that. Unfortunately, he was currently in human form, and his human intellect told him that even if he ran, there was nowhere to hide.

Yuwen Xuan tilted his head back, standing rooted to the spot with a face of total despair.

Meanwhile, Boss Tao still had no inkling of who he was dueling. Refusing to yield, he roared at the top of his lungs:

—"*Croak!*"-Tao Zhu bellowed.

Another wave of toxic mist gushed forth, surging to stain the Heavenly Scripture. Yet that sliver of silver-white no longer merely defended; in that very split second, it surged violently across the trunk. The sprayed venom actually served as a conducting medium for the silver-white radiance, accelerating its spread tenfold.

In an instant, even the withered trunk and boughs of the ancient tree began exuding a gentle silver light.

Meanwhile, Tao Zhu's bulging belly felt as though it had taken a vicious punch. Letting out a pitiful "croak-whimper," he stumbled back a step, a trickle of blood spilling from the corner of his mouth.

[It's over! I failed!]

[What on earth is on the other side? How could it be this powerful?!]

A look of sheer dread surfaced on Tao Zhu's face as well, his eyes staring in hopeless dismay at the inexorably expanding silver light.

Yet from within the glowing tree trunk drifted a rather familiar voice:

[—"Huh? Old Tao?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

Accompanying these words, a pair of shapely, elegant hands reached out from the fissure in the tree trunk, grasped the local space, and tore it wide open. Immediately after, a silver-robed figure stepped out through the rent in the trunk.

He looked for all the world like an ancient deity who had just emerged from an arboreal world after being sealed for tens of thousands of years.

Upon clearly identifying the figure's face, Boss Tao cried out in sheer shock:

[—"Untiring?! How is it you?!"](# "Speaker: Tao Zhu")

—"That is precisely what I was going to ask..."-Zhou Hui replied, staring curiously at Tao Zhu. Then he averted his gaze, surveyed the surroundings, and ultimately let his eyes rest upon Yuwen Xuan, who stood rigidly frozen behind the tree.

Although Yuwen Xuan had [plugged his ears while stealing the bell](# "Idiom: 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn'ěr-dàolíng) — plugging one's ears to steal a bell; burying one's head in the sand / self-deception"), attempting to conceal his silhouette behind the trunk, he was not a little black lamb anymore. With a frame that large, there was simply nowhere to hide—not to mention that the newborn sprig was vigorously beaming light right at him, illuminating Yuwen Xuan's exact location like a spotlight.

Seeing that escape was impossible, Yuwen Xuan stammered in a dry, raspy squeak:

[—"M... Master."](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan")

—"Heh,"-Zhou Hui chuckled softly.

* * *

On the day of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grand recruitment exams that year, every aspiring disciple who arrived to pay homage at the mountain gate beheld the magnificent peach tree towering before the entrance—and the Eldest Senior Brother hanging suspended from its boughs.
