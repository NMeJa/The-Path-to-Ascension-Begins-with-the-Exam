# Chapter 105: [Superfluous](<# "Literal CN: 多余 (duōyú) — Superfluous / The Extras; chapter title referring both to ordinary background cultivators who feel like superfluous extras in the cultivation world and the extra audience tagging along">) (第105章 多余)

—"Alas, why are there no matches today?"-someone lamented.

Hearing her words, many female cultivators showed expressions of deep agreement.

Today was a rest day for the Immortal Arts Assembly, but the female cultivators of the "Mo Bei Fan Club" had not taken the day off to rest. Instead, they had gathered inside the Yi Bei Tea shop.

Or rather, it was precisely because today was a rest day that they could gather in the teahouse at all; had it been like previous days, they would have long since rushed to the Immortal Arts Assembly arena to cheer for Mo Bei.

Business at Yi Bei Tea had naturally been affected as well—in a very good way.

Now that Mo Bei had become the center of attention in Fengqing City, well-informed individuals had naturally taken it upon themselves to educate the public on the background of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Youngest Junior Sister. Among the facts, the detail that "Yi Bei Tea was a personal gift from the Untiring Immortal Venerable to his disciple" had certainly not been overlooked, attracting a massive wave of customers to the shop.

Thus, when the female cultivators took a rare day off from the tournament arena and returned to their "headquarters," they found that the line for Yi Bei Tea stretched all the way from the head of the street to the very end.

Fortunately, regular customers enjoyed privileges.

On the top floor of Yi Bei Tea was a small balcony reserved for staff and strictly closed to ordinary guests. However, the "Mo Bei Fan Club" were hardly outsiders, so it had been converted into a private gathering spot for the girls.

Even so, the female cultivators felt somewhat lacking in energy.

Especially Jiang Feiyan, president of the "Mo Bei Fan Club (Fengqing City Branch)."

Jiang Feiyan was the daughter of a chess parlor owner in Fengqing City. Her family had first settled here in her great-grandfather's generation.

It was said that her great-grandfather possessed extraordinary talent and had once been favored by a major sect, accepted as an inner disciple. Regrettably, his cultivation stalled at the late stage of Soul Formation without advancing an inch further. Furthermore, after having several disputes with his sect, he had ultimately become disheartened, moving to Fengqing City to open a chess parlor, take a wife, and carry on his family bloodline.

Regrettably, the Jiang family never produced another descendant with decent talent after him.

Jiang Feiyan's grandfather, father, and even Jiang Feiyan herself all possessed mottled Three-Element Spirit Roots—the sort who could scrape by as outer disciples in an orthodox sect, but could never hope to enter the inner sect.

Yet for a family like the Jiangs, who owned an established business in an immortal city, going off to be exploited as outer disciples held no appeal whatsoever. The meager pittance of spirit stones that ordinary sects provided to outer disciples was something their own household could comfortably afford. Consequently, they had no intention of joining any sect, choosing instead to quietly and honestly run their family chess parlor.

Families like Jiang Feiyan's formed the fundamental bedrock of every major immortal city.

Yet because they were not mortals, they were not harassed by demons or monsters; nor were they promising cultivators capable of ascending to higher realms or venturing forth to vanquish evil fiends. As a result, even within this world's legendary storybooks and folk chronicles, they remained an entirely overlooked demographic, as though they did not exist at all.

Jiang Feiyan took no issue with this. She knew her own limits and harbored no delusions of seeking out secret realms or eradicating demonic beasts—wasn't simply living a good life fine? At baseline, she was guaranteed to live two hundred disease-free, pain-free years; why make life miserable for herself?

Still, as long as one was human, one was bound to have a little bit of... "ambition."

Jiang Feiyan's greatest passion was playing chess.

She was quite skilled at it too. While she had not reached the level of entering the Dao through chess, by the age of seven or eight she was already capable of beating some of the old patrons who frequented her family's parlor.

It was also during that period that she noticed men were universally [sore losers](<# "Literal CN: 输不起 (shū bù qǐ) — unable to afford losing; sore losers who cannot take a defeat gracefully">).

Initially, the parlor's patrons loved to dote on and tease her, eager to sit across the board from a cute little girl. But as Jiang Feiyan began winning more and more frequently, the patrons' moods soured. Eventually, her father simply forbade her from playing against customers altogether.

Jiang Feiyan had been deeply annoyed, yet she could understand his reasoning.

After all, patrons were paying customers; they came to enjoy themselves, not to entertain her.

Yet there were no other chess parlors in the city. Even if there were, showing up at someone else's doorstep would look like she was [challenging a rival dojo](<# "Literal CN: 踢馆 (tīguǎn) — kicking the dojo sign / gate; raiding or challenging a rival establishment">). Thus, Jiang Feiyan rarely played against outsiders, usually playing only with her family members or close female friends who shared the hobby.

That remained the case until last year, just before the winter solstice, when a distant cousin brought back a box of Immortal Chess from the Tianling Auction.

Jiang Feiyan quickly became utterly obsessed.

Back then, her favorite piece was Junior Sister Mo Bei. At the time, however, it was purely because this piece was incredibly strong and handled exceptionally smoothly; using her, Jiang Feiyan easily crushed every opponent.

Playing solely against her family could no longer satisfy Jiang Feiyan. She couldn't help wishing that Immortal Chess would become just a little more popular.

As if answering her prayers, when spring arrived, the Heavenly Spirit Sect announced it would host the Immortal Arts Assembly, and opened a teahouse in Fengqing City that doubled as an Immortal Chess parlor.

Upon hearing the news, Jiang Feiyan dashed over that very day in high spirits.

A crowd of male cultivators had already gathered around, though at the time no gambling syndicates were involved yet, so the atmosphere in the hall was relatively pleasant, far from deteriorating into the foul cesspool it would later become.

When those male cultivators saw Jiang Feiyan asking for a match, they burst out laughing, exactly like the patrons who used to tease little girls in her family's parlor. At that moment, Jiang Feiyan felt an ominous premonition.

And indeed, reality unfolded just as she feared. After she won against several people in succession, the male cultivators' expressions darkened, muttering and grumbling suspicions that she was cheating. When she made an accidental misplay under that hostile atmosphere, she was ridiculed mercilessly by the surrounding "spectators." Spiteful remarks like "Girls aren't suited for chess" and "This was never made for you people to play" began flying thick and fast.

It was there that Jiang Feiyan wished, for the very first time, that she possessed martial prowess like Mo Bei's.

Naturally, such a "petty matter" hardly warranted slaughter. Besides, the Heavenly Spirit Sect seemed to take note of the situation quickly, soon establishing a private tea lounge exclusively for female patrons on the second floor of Yi Bei Tea, where they could play Immortal Chess without interference.

Thanks to her outstanding chess skills, Jiang Feiyan was even hired as an external consultant for Immortal Chess at Yi Bei Tea.

Even so, regarding her previous experience, Jiang Feiyan still felt somewhat [chafed and resentful](<# "Literal CN: 意难平 (yì nán píng) — an unresolved injustice or lingering grievance that one cannot let go of">).

It was also during this time that Jiang Feiyan gradually heard various rumors concerning Junior Sister Mo Bei. Only then did she learn that the pieces in Immortal Chess mirrored the real combat prowess of actual figures, and discovered how Junior Sister Mo Bei had attained honor as the Heavenly Spirit Sect's number one sword cultivator.

Consequently, Jiang Feiyan developed an even deeper admiration for "Junior Sister Mo Bei," resolving to cheer for her during the Immortal Arts Assembly.

—Taking the opportunity to visit the legendary Immortal Fair was, in truth, an even more enticing goal she yearned for.

What Jiang Feiyan had never expected was that this very "Junior Sister Mo Bei" would personally come to Fengqing City, severing heads and painting the arenas red with those scoundrels who disrupted order.

This was truly far more... far cooler than she had ever imagined!

Throughout these past few weeks, Jiang Feiyan felt as though she were living in a constant festive holiday.

Regrettably, no matter how wonderful a festival was, it inevitably had to come to an end. Everyone knew that the situation in Fengqing City had been firmly stabilized by Junior Sister Mo, and that the "festival" was drawing to a close. Thus, sitting on the small balcony of Yi Bei Tea on this rest day, Jiang Feiyan felt not only a sense of lethargy born from weeks of excessive excitement—cheering was physically exhausting work, after all—but also a tinge of regret that the celebration was slipping away.

Yet upon hearing someone complain about there being no matches today, Jiang Feiyan spoke up nonetheless:
—"It's already been half a month straight. Even if she's Junior Sister Mo Bei, she needs proper rest!"

—"Indeed, Little Sister Mo Bei has been on the arena stage every single day; she must be exhausted!"-another agreed.

[—"She definitely needs a rest."](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

Jiang Feiyan's remark was immediately met with a flurry of overlapping agreement.

Although everyone wished the "festival" would go on forever, they were also somewhat worried about Mo Bei's physical endurance. In particular, during that earlier stretch when dozens of challenges were issued each day, just watching was exhausting; it was a miracle Mo Bei had endured it all.

The girl who had complained initially felt somewhat embarrassed, her face reddening as she defended herself:
—"I didn't mean Little Sister Mo Bei, I meant the regular matches... Don't you feel that under Little Sister Mo Bei's influence, the matches in the Immortal Arts Assembly have become interesting too?"

[—"True, those people have finally started competing for real."](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"It's hilarious, though! Quite a few are actually trying to imitate Little Sister Mo Bei, attempting to [overwhelm all technique with pure brute force](<# "Literal CN: 一力降十会 (yī lì jiàng shí huì) — one strength overcomes ten techniques; brute force over technique">)."](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"Hahaha, what do they think the Immortal Chess maps are for? Background scenery?"](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"Oh no, I'm suddenly worried—could the cultivators representing our Fengqing City arena turn out to be the weakest of all arenas?"](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"Weakest isn't certain yet, but dumbest is definitely confirmed!"](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"Tee-hee, I feel like even if I went up there, I might beat them [black and blue](<# "Literal CN: 落花流水 (luò huā liú shuǐ) — falling flowers and flowing water; thoroughly routed / smashed to pieces">)!"](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"You might actually! You really know those recent maps inside and out."](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

The female cultivators bantered and giggled amongst themselves.

They settled back into their usual easygoing atmosphere. Yet it was all just talk; not a single one among them actually entertained the thought of signing up for the tournament.

Grand titles like "Representative Cultivators of the Central Domain" were simply too far removed from their everyday lives. The most thrilling thing they had ever done was project their own modest dreams onto Mo Bei, feeling as if seeing Mo Bei claim victory was equivalent to attaining what they themselves desired.

As "ordinary bystanders" of the cultivation world who didn't even belong to a sect, they were merely superfluous pieces of the world's background scenery.

Once Mo Bei departed Fengqing City for good, they would return completely to their mundane routines, at most finding some way to travel to the finals venue to cheer for Mo Bei when the time came—if it was Mo Bei, she would definitely make it to the finals, perhaps even take the championship! Not a single soul doubted that.

Right as the female cultivators were idly passing the time as usual, the door to the rooftop balcony opened.

[Did someone order something again?]

Jiang Feiyan thought to herself, subconsciously turning her head to look.

Then, she let out a piercing shriek:
—"Junior Sister Mo Bei!"

Startled by the scream, everyone turned around in astonishment, only to find a doll-like maiden standing at the threshold. In response to the shrill cry, she glanced over with an expressionless face, accompanied by several junior brothers and sisters standing behind her.

Under Mo Bei's aloof, indifferent gaze, Jiang Feiyan regained a sliver of composure, though the excitement thumping in her chest did not diminish in the least.

After all, even as the fan club president, Jiang Feiyan had scarcely had any opportunity to interact with Mo Bei at close range over these past few weeks.

Although Mo Bei had been residing inside Yi Bei Tea during this period, aside from a single meal arranged by the teahouse manager a few days ago where the fan club members dined together with her, they had no idea where she stayed.

Moreover, considering the intense combat Mo Bei endured every day, even if they had known where she lived, none of the fan club members would have dared disturb her.

—Though they casually tossed around affectionate terms like "Junior Sister" and "Little Sister," after witnessing Mo Bei in battle, not a single one dared treat her as an ordinary little sister; deep down, they held immense awe and reverence for her.

Still, the closer one observed Little Sister Mo Bei, the harder it was to fathom how such a petite frame could harbor such terrifying combat power!

Jiang Feiyan thought as she gazed at Mo Bei.

Not only was Mo Bei diminutive in stature, her facial features were exquisitely delicate, looking almost as though she had been crafted specifically to satisfy male cultivators' stereotypical aesthetic fantasies. Consequently, when Immortal Chess first debuted, aside from [meta slaves](<# "Literal CN: 强度党 (qiángdù dǎng) — strength/power faction; players who pick characters purely based on top-tier combat stats and meta efficiency">) like Jiang Feiyan, most girls initially favored other game pieces.

It was only through this recent tournament slaughter that the ranks of the Mo Bei Fan Club had multiplied exponentially.

Mo Bei herself seemed entirely oblivious to such things. Most of the time she wore a blank expression; rather than aloof, she appeared slightly dazed.

Even now, an ordinary person encountering a flock of their own fans would show at least some reaction, but Mo Bei showed none.

She merely glanced over when her name was called, and upon realizing the group had no urgent business with her, turned her head back and marched toward the edge of the rooftop balcony, moving just like a puppet programmed with fixed instructions.

Yet the fans all yearned to exchange a few words with Mo Bei. Someone couldn't help asking:
—"Lady... Mo Bei,"-she didn't dare say "Little Sister" after all, asking excitedly,-"did you come up here for something?"

[This place belongs to her in the first place. What's so strange about her coming up whenever she pleases?]

Jiang Feiyan thought to herself, yet she too looked toward Mo Bei with flushed anticipation.

Mo Bei never initiated conversation with others, but whenever someone asked her a direct question, she almost invariably answered:
—"I am taking them out to conduct the [practical exam portion](<# "Literal CN: 实操部分 (shícāo bùfen) — practical hands-on application section">) of their [midterm examinations](<# "Literal CN: 期中考试 (qīzhōng kǎoshì) — midterm examination; Zhou Hui's modern standardized academic testing system striking even on external missions">)."

[—"Midterm exams?"](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

[—"Practical exam?"](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

The female cultivators froze in bewilderment at her response. Only then did their attention shift to the several disciples trailing behind Mo Bei.

Those disciples wore thoroughly devastated expressions, looking as though they had just been trampled and ravaged by something horrifying.

Though the girls could not fathom what "midterm" meant, they vaguely understood "examination." A wave of sympathy instantly washed over the female cultivators—still, why on earth did they have to take exams even when dispatched on an external assignment to Fengqing City?

Just as Jiang Feiyan was about to voice this question, she heard someone ask:
—"What is a midterm exam? Can we participate too?"

—"Ah, Shui Huang'er!"-Jiang Feiyan cried out.

She recognized the girl as a newcomer who had joined recently. She seemed completely infatuated with Mo Bei, and because she had been exceptionally proactive during the past half month of cheering—possessing remarkable talent in music and dance, which contributed greatly to choreographing cheering rhythms—she had quickly become a core member.

Even so, knowing full well that Little Sister Mo Bei had sect examinations to conduct, asking "Can we participate too" was outrageously presumptuous! A fan club had to maintain fan club etiquette! What if Little Sister Mo Bei grew annoyed with them because of this?!

Jiang Feiyan thought in a panic.

She looked toward Mo Bei and the others, only to see that several Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples indeed wore bizarre, conflicted expressions at Shui Huang'er's request. Only Mo Bei retained her completely deadpan countenance.

Mo Bei looked at Shui Huang'er, then nodded:
—"Sure."

—"W-Wait a moment, Senior Sister Mo Bei, this is our sect's examination!"-one Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple objected.

—"Does that really matter?"-Mo Bei said, thinking back to Su Xin, who had traveled tens of thousands of li to take exams at the Heavenly Spirit Sect.-"It's not as if nobody has ever done it before. Master warmly welcomes outsiders coming to take our sect's exams."

[The Immortal Venerable actually allows it?... Wait, no! What kind of lunatic voluntarily wants to take exams?! And someone from another sect, no less?!]

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples exchanged dumbfounded glances.

Yet with Shui Huang'er standing right before their eyes as a living example, they could hardly dispute it.

Still... another disciple spoke up in deep humiliation:
—"Other exams aside, having outsiders watch our practical exam is a bit inappropriate, isn't it?"

—"What is inappropriate about it?"-Mo Bei asked in confusion. Then, her gaze turned slightly stern as she looked at them:-"If you perform poorly merely because bystanders are present, then it is indeed inappropriate... inappropriate for you to be disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

The disciples instantly turned pale with terror:
—"That wasn't what I meant, Senior Sister Mo Bei!"

—"We will take the exam properly!"

—"Yes! No matter what happens, even if the heavens collapse and the earth shatters, we will take the exam properly!"

After all, during this period, they had been so preoccupied with watching Mo Bei's matches that they had completely forgotten the existence of midterm exams. Last night's written exam had already been a total disaster; if today's practical exam was botched as well, while they wouldn't actually be expelled from the Heavenly Spirit Sect, they were very likely to be [held back a year](<# "Literal CN: 留级 (liújì) — repeating a grade / held back; Zhou Hui's modern schooling system introducing grade retention to cultivation sects">).

—That was right: the Heavenly Spirit Sect had recently implemented a grade-level system, and with it came grade retention. Imagining having to attend classes next semester with their former junior brothers and junior sisters, and being forced to address their current classmates as "Senior Sister" and "Senior Brother," everyone felt that hanging from trees for a while to maintain their senior brother status was actually quite a bargain.

Seeing the terrified expressions of these Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, the female cultivators felt slightly embarrassed, wondering if they had intruded upon something deeply important. Yet this only stoked their curiosity about the Heavenly Spirit Sect's "midterm exams" even further.

Jiang Feiyan debated whether she ought to politely decline, lest she come across like some obsessive stalker.

Mo Bei, however, had always been direct and cared little for what others might think.

Having already unsheathed her flying sword, she spoke to the crowd:
—"If you are interested, follow along."

Saying so, she stepped onto her flying sword and soared into the sky.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples naturally dared not delay, scrambling in a fluster to follow.

—"Let's go too~~"-Shui Huang'er said cheerfully, unceremoniously following right behind them.

With her taking the lead, the other fan club members hesitated no longer, joyfully taking to the air. Fortunately, having shuttled back and forth between the Immortal Arts Assembly arena and Fengqing City over the past several days, most of them carried flying artifacts on their persons, so there was no fear of being left behind—though keeping pace with Mo Bei's speed proved somewhat challenging.

Jiang Feiyan resolved that in the future, sword-flight training would definitely be added to the fan club's official curriculum.

* * *

Flying on swords was remarkably convenient, after all.

In merely a quarter of an hour, Mo Bei and her group had departed the boundaries of Fengqing City, arriving above a village situated beyond the city walls.

This settlement did not look drastically different from most villages surrounding immortal cities, aside from having noticeably more houses under construction and a dazzling, lustrous coral tree standing proudly at its center.

Jiang Feiyan's eyes lit up the instant she spotted that coral tree.

Because she had been looking into ways to travel to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to cheer for Mo Bei during the finals, she had researched numerous travel routes. The most highly recommended route was naturally using the coral trees distributed by the Heavenly Spirit Sect to board a Kunpeng bound for the Immortal Fair, and from there proceeding to the tournament venue.

Yet due to the foolish actions of Fengqing City's City Lord and certain cultivation clans, Fengqing City—despite being one of the representative metropolises of the Central Domain—lacked even a single public coral tree, a fact that had caused Jiang Feiyan no small amount of distress.

[To think there was actually one planted so close to Fengqing City!]

Jiang Feiyan marvelled.

She took a closer look at the settlement, noticing a stone stele standing at the village entrance inscribed with the words "Qiao Family Village."

[—"Isn't Qiao Family Village..."](<# "Speaker: Fan Club Members">)

Whispers arose from behind. Jiang Feiyan glanced back and found several girls from cultivation clans murmuring among themselves. Jiang Feiyan had vaguely heard that these clans had clashed with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and several clan patriarchs had perished as a result. However, such matters sounded far too distant, and those girls were hardly core figures within their clans, so she hadn't paid it much mind.

[Nothing bad will happen, will it?]

Jiang Feiyan worried inwardly.

Mo Bei, however, paid no attention to the hushed whispers behind her. She descended straight toward the village, yet rather than landing near that splendid coral tree, touched down at the village entrance.

There, a young woman dressed as a mortal village girl was already waiting:
—"Teacher Mo Bei,"-the maiden addressed her neither servilely nor arrogantly, her face lighting up with pleasant surprise,-"you've arrived! I saw news of you in the newspaper; they say you were extraordinarily majestic."

Hearing this, members of the fan club furrowed their brows. They disliked outsiders speaking in such a casual, spectacle-watching tone, especially since this maiden was clearly a mere mortal. Even now that order had been restored to the Immortal Arts Assembly, she had no way of attending the arena—although Qiao Family Village was merely a quarter-hour of [flying sword travel distance](<# "Literal CN: 剑程 (jiànchéng) — sword-flight distance; distance measured by flying sword travel time">) from Fengqing City, traveling it on foot would take a mortal several full days.

Mo Bei, for her part, treated everyone exactly the same. She gave a casual nod and asked:
—"Is the examination site prepared?"

—"It is ready! Everyone has been eagerly awaiting the arrival of the immortal masters!"-the maiden replied.

Seeing that Mo Bei had no desire for idle chatter, the maiden wasted no words either.

She crisply led the group toward the back of the village, stopping before a vast expanse of agricultural fields.

These plots were clearly prime spiritual fields. Yet what grew inside was not ordinary grain, but much taller plants bearing thick purple stalks.

—"Is this... sugarcane?"-Shui Huang'er asked in surprise.

As a member of the Southern Domain demon race, Shui Huang'er was not so ignorant as to fail to recognize sugarcane. In fact, the Central Domain's sugarcane had originally been introduced from the South. Yet even in the Southern Domain, Shui Huang'er had rarely seen prime spiritual fields used to cultivate such a mundane crop.

Human cultivators far preferred using spiritual fields to grow considerably more lucrative items—such as medicinal herbs for alchemy.

As far as Shui Huang'er knew, several sugarcane-producing demon clans in the Southern Domain had signed contracts with humans to convert their fields into medicinal herb farms. She had never imagined that humans themselves would actually plant crops like this in spiritual soil.

"..."

Mo Bei glanced at Shui Huang'er and the others, suddenly recalling that this crowd appeared to be regular patrons of Yi Bei Tea.

How had Eldest Senior Brother and Mo Xi phrased it? Making sure customers could drink tea with peace of mind, or something along those lines?

—"The crops grown in this field are the source of sugar for Yi Bei Tea,"-Mo Bei explained in as much detail as she could muster.-"To ensure our customers can enjoy [safe, certified tea](<# "Literal CN: 放心茶 (fàngxīn chá) — reassuring/safe tea; referencing modern food safety standards and transparent consumer supply chains">), we signed supply contracts with model demonstration villages like Qiao Family Village. We provide standardized cultivation methods to guarantee a steady supply of spiritual energy for plant growth, and select optimized cultivars—this season's sugarcane was bred by Fifth Senior Brother using special medicinal formulas. It not only matures earlier, but also yields significantly higher sugar content."

"Ohhh!"

The female cultivators from the city uttered sounds of enlightened realization, though whether they had truly grasped any of it remained an open question.

The Qiao Family Village girl, however, cast a thoughtful glance at these cultivators, seemingly pondering something.

Shui Huang'er asked curiously:
—"Then, what does this sugarcane have to do with the 'midterm exam' you mentioned?"

—"This is the exam content,"-Mo Bei announced.-"Today, we must harvest all several hundred mu of sugarcane in these fields."

Hearing this, the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples covered their faces in utter despair.

This was precisely why they hadn't wanted outsiders tagging along to watch!

As reserve sword cultivators, daily agricultural drills were one thing, but for their practical midterm examination to literally be [harvesting sugarcane](<# "Literal CN: 收甘蔗 (shōu gānzhe) — harvesting sugarcane; manual agricultural labor as a practical sword cultivation exam">)!

There was nothing sword-cultivator-like about this at all!
