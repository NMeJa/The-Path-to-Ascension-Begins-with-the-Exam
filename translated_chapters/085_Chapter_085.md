# Chapter 85: The Neighbor's Messy Business (第85章 邻居家的破事)

The Beast Soul Tower was constructed even before Thousand Beasts Hall itself existed.

Legend held that back in the ancient past, Patriarch Qubi, the first-generation sect master of Thousand Beasts Hall, encountered a ferocious beast. Although rescued in the nick of time by a True Immortal, the beast's soul was so ferociously malevolent that even the True Immortal was at a loss for how to extinguish it completely.

Yet if that ferocious beast's soul were left abandoned in the deepest depths of the Abyss Sea Ruins to be eroded by chaotic spiritual qi and demonic essence, it was feared that not only might the soul resurrect, but it would mutate into an even more horrifying monstrosity.

The True Immortal had no choice but to temporarily seal the ferocious beast's soul inside a stone stele. Instructing Patriarch Qubi to carry it upon his back and depart the Abyss Sea Ruins, the immortal decreed that whenever the stele grew so heavy that it could no longer be borne, that place would be the ideal site to seal the ferocious beast.

And so, Patriarch Qubi carried that ponderous stone stele upon his shoulders and departed the Abyss Sea Ruins.

Because shouldering the stele rendered him entirely unable to travel via spells or magical artifacts, Patriarch Qubi walked for a total of nine hundred and ninety-nine days. His shoulders wore raw until they bled, scabbed over, wore raw again, and scabbed over once more... By the time his shoulders had grown calluses as thick and tough as rhinoceros hide, he finally emerged from the ruins and arrived at the northeastern corner of the Central Domain.

There, in an inconspicuous patch of mountain forest, that stone stele finally slipped from Patriarch Qubi's shoulders, sinking into the earth as though taking deep root, never to be pried loose again.

Following the immortal's instructions, Patriarch Qubi constructed the Beast Soul Tower around the stone stele to seal the ferocious beast's soul.

Afterward, he did not depart. Instead, by researching the sealed beast soul, he comprehended a method to share cultivation with spirit beasts so both could advance in tandem—and upon that very foundation, he established Thousand Beasts Hall.

This was the foundational history of the sect that every disciple of Thousand Beasts Hall had heard.

Yuwen Xuan had heard it too.

Naturally, no one had made a special effort to recount it to him. Rather, in his unruly youth, he had been far too mischievous, [running wild all over the mountains](<# "Literal CN: 满山乱跑 (mǎnshān luànpǎo) — running wild all over the mountains; defined here as a 100,000-kilometer radius around Zhou Hui's immortal cave">)—and "all over the mountains" here meant running wild within a radius of a hundred thousand kilometers centered squarely on Zhou Hui's immortal cave on Mount Tianling. He had simply happened to be roosting in a treetop when an instructor of Thousand Beasts Hall was telling ancient tales to disciples, allowing him to freeload a free bedtime story.

Yuwen Xuan had no idea whether the tale was true or not, but the Beast Soul Tower indeed possessed a remarkably formidable seal. Not only could Yuwen Xuan not get in, but even Zhou Hui couldn't enter.

Of course, Yuwen Xuan suspected that Zhou Hui couldn't enter not because he lacked the ability, but simply because he had no desire to barge in.

His master was clearly powerful enough to teleport instantaneously to any corner of this world—be it the ocean floor of the Abyss Sea or the boundless cosmic void. Yet so long as another party staked out their own territorial boundaries, laid down warding restrictions, and committed no hostile act to provoke him, his master would ordinarily abide by the rules with scrupulous honesty, even if the other party was a mere mortal.

—"This is the fundamental principle of being human,"-Zhou Hui had once stated.

Unfortunately, Yuwen Xuan possessed a brain.

Not particularly large, but fully capable of cogitation.

He was convinced that the true "fundamental principles of being human" were definitely not what his master preached. After all, the cultivation realm was a ceaseless battlefield where the weak were meat for the strong. If one judged by his master's set of "fundamental principles," more than half the realm couldn't even qualify as humans, meaning there was no need to respect their human rights in the slightest.

Under Zhou Hui's painstaking tutelage of over two hundred years, Yuwen Xuan naturally identified far more strongly with his human identity.

He had even summarized his own personal principles of being human:

First: Master is paramount—I would sooner devour myself than ever lay a tooth on Master.

Second: Junior brothers and junior sisters are also very important—devouring them means losing Master forever.

Third: Do one's utmost not to eat creatures possessing humanoid intellect capable of human-like cognition, nor eat entities bearing human form; Master might regard both as fellow humans, and devouring them could incur Master's thunderous wrath.

Fourth: Rare and precious entities must be devoured as quickly as possible; if you eat them too slowly, they might just mutate into a junior brother or junior sister.

Fifth: Consuming enough food to sustain bodily functions is sufficient; eating too much makes one resemble a [glutton](<# "Literal CN: 饭桶 (fàntǒng) — rice bucket; a derogatory term for a useless glutton who contributes nothing but food consumption">), rendering one far less adorable than those troublesome junior brothers and sisters.

Armed with these five tenets, Yuwen Xuan genuinely fancied himself a "principled human" far superior to the overwhelming majority of mortals!

Ah, getting sidetracked.

In any case, no matter which of Yuwen Xuan's principles one consulted, the Beast Soul Tower fell under none of the restrictions. It was squarely categorized as fair game for consumption—not to mention, it smelled extraordinarily fragrant!

Unbelievably fragrant.

Yuwen Xuan couldn't verify whether that True Immortal's seal was authentic, but he was dead certain that the seal had never been completely air-tight, allowing a mouthwatering aroma to waft out at every waking moment.

Now, his master was exceptional in every conceivable way, save for one: his worldly desires were far too austere. He held zero enthusiasm for gourmet food or drink, preparing rations for Yuwen Xuan that merely met the bare minimum of being edible. The dynamic felt exactly like living in a household where one's mother—~~scratch that~~, father—possessed abysmal culinary skills, while the neighbor's kitchen whipped up Michelin-grade feasts. Every single day on the dot, an intoxicating fragrance would drift across the yard, reducing the hungry child to tears of craving. Given Yuwen Xuan's innate nature as a dyed-in-the-wool foodie, the fact that he hadn't defected to become the neighbor's adopted son was already the supreme testament to his filial devotion toward his master. Thus, occasionally clambering onto the neighbor's windowsill was entirely understandable, and inexplicably picking up the art of beast taming was even more forgivable.

—Even to this day, Zhou Hui still couldn't comprehend how his eldest disciple had ever become a beast tamer, merely chalking it up to the innate gifts of a Taotie. (Ordinary Taoties: *No, we are strictly responsible for eating beasts, not raising them!*)

However, if one spent enough time clinging to the windowsill, one would inevitably catch the eye of the neighbor—or rather, the neighbor's child—and receive some illicit treats.

Two hundred years ago, Yuwen Xuan had succeeded once.

A little girl from the neighbor's household had noticed Yuwen Xuan's presence and forged a temporary master-servant contract with him:

Yuwen Xuan was the master, and the little girl was the servant—on matters of hierarchy, Yuwen Xuan would never let himself get the short end of the stick!

Following that child, he had successfully sneaked into the neighbor's [cafeteria](<# "Literal CN: 食堂 (shítáng) — cafeteria/canteen; Yuwen Xuan's irreverent foodie term for the sacred Beast Soul Tower">)—~~scratch that~~, the Beast Soul Tower—and snuck a clandestine bite.

Oh, the sheer, transcendental deliciousness!

Even after two hundred years, Yuwen Xuan had never forgotten it!

Regrettably, the neighbor's little girl had been an utter blockhead—a fact proven beyond doubt by her sheer audacity in signing a contract with a Taotie like Yuwen Xuan. Not long after, she was murdered by her junior martial brother, causing Yuwen Xuan to lose all access to the neighbor's cafeteria.

Yuwen Xuan never gave up hope. Over the course of two centuries, he had concocted countless schemes to revisit that "cafeteria."

Unfortunately, the deceased girl's junior brother guarded the cafeteria like a hawk. Worse yet, he was an extraordinary coward who didn't even possess the guts to step inside himself, causing all of Yuwen Xuan's painstaking efforts to end in dismal failure.

Until recently.

Although Yuwen Xuan didn't understand the exact sequence of events, his master had somehow thoroughly battered the neighbor's cowardly wretch right in his fragile self-esteem. Seasoned with a pinch of malice bugs as extra spice, that craven coward who had murdered his own senior sister had finally mustered the nerve to reopen the cafeteria—~~scratch that~~, the Beast Soul Tower—that he had dreaded confronting for centuries.

As expected of Master!

And as expected of himself!

Casting a wide net, laying snares everywhere, and waiting with supreme patience truly were the vital first steps of a master hunter. Look at that—hadn't a child just waltzed straight into the cafeteria?

Yuwen Xuan thought, bursting with gleeful exhilaration.

His divine sense unfurled outward from the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* he had gifted to Xia Bing, greedily licking the ambient air inside the Beast Soul Tower:

[This is the exact fragrance!]

[Grand Cafeteria, I've finally come home!]

*

Xia Bing shivered.

An inexplicable wave of unease washed over her, though she had no idea where the foreboding sensation originated.

Still, standing inside the Beast Soul Tower that tested the candidates for next sect master, feeling this sort of tension was only natural.

Or so Xia Bing thought.

Widening her eyes, she curiously took in her surroundings within the Beast Soul Tower.

As a pure human being, Xia Bing naturally did not find the air inside the Beast Soul Tower sweet or appetizing in the slightest. She detected only a pungent stench of blood and rank beast musk.

This implied that within this tower—which theoretically no one had entered for two hundred years—lethal combat had taken place not long ago.

Even without that nauseating odor, the Beast Soul Tower itself was profoundly unsettling.

The interior lacked a single window. It was intensely gloomy and composed almost entirely of endless corridors, resembling a vast, pitch-black labyrinth—and the dreadful sort where no treasure chests awaited at the end of the hall.

Even Xia Bing, who thoroughly relished exploring the Thousand Stars Secret Realm, found this place stifling and oppressive.

The other disciples felt it even more acutely.

Especially the few who had entered in the same cohort alongside Xia Bing.

Unlike Xia Bing, who had experienced "bizarre fortune," these disciples were "core disciples" earnestly groomed by Thousand Beasts Hall. Naturally, they had not participated in that chaotic, unauthorized secret realm expedition earlier, and having been sheltered within the sect compound all their lives, they had never even heard of the Thousand Stars Secret Realm. Although Thousand Beasts Hall occasionally arranged routine trials, genuine opportunities for live combat were far too sparse. As a result, they were quickly unhinged by the tower's eerie atmosphere, succumbing to paranoid whispers:

[—"Haven't we already walked down this corridor?"](<# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple">)

[—"Why is there nothing here?"](<# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple">)

[—"Are we truly moving forward? There's nothing at all."](<# "Speaker: Thousand Beasts Hall Disciple">)

They murmured among themselves.

Listening to their anxious debate, Xia Bing's expression also clouded with concern.

According to the established procedure of the Beast Soul Tower, participating disciples entered from the very top of the tower, navigating layer by layer through the intricate maze—~~scratch that~~, corridors—and defeating the various spirits lurking within the labyrinth.

Those spirits originated from the spirit beasts of past trial-takers who had failed and perished here.

A new trial-taker had to command their own spirit beast to slay those adversaries, absorbing strength to empower themselves until they reached the bottommost floor. There, they would challenge the sealed ferocious beast soul, devouring a portion of it to attain the supreme legacy and peak cultivation.

However, this expedition was not a true succession trial, but merely a preparatory training session for the Immortal Arts Tournament—or at least, that was what the current sect master, Yan Bailang, had claimed. Consequently, Xia Bing and her companions were not required to complete the entire grueling gauntlet.

All Xia Bing's group had to accomplish today was traverse the first floor and exit through the rear door on the opposing flank.

Frankly speaking, under ordinary circumstances, this trial was by no means insurmountable.

The so-called "ordinary circumstances" meant running into a beast soul after traversing a single corridor, or stumbling upon the skeletal remains of a demonic beast after rounding a corner.

Based on Xia Bing's dungeon-crawling experience, when there was combat, time passed much faster and party morale held up far better.

Yet the trial of the Beast Soul Tower seemed completely different from what she had envisioned.

At present, they had wandered the tower for half an hour without encountering a single beast soul. Instead, faint, intermittent chewing noises drifted from the distance, sounding as though some ravenous predator were gnawing on bones, ratcheting up the psychological dread among the explorers.

Xia Bing felt she had to take charge.

—"Stick to the right!"-Xia Bing instructed.-"No matter what kind of labyrinth it is, if you keep turning right, you'll always find your way out."

This was a navigation technique she had overheard while exploring the Thousand Stars Secret Realm.

Her tone was steady and resolute. While the other disciples were floundering in helpless confusion, they exchanged glances and decided to follow Xia Bing's guidance.

Their subsequent "luck" proved quite decent.

Following Xia Bing's instructions, after making three consecutive right turns, they finally encountered a colossal wolf soul.

Because they had grown accustomed to finding nothing during the initial stretch, several disciples were visibly startled and fell into a brief panic. At that moment, the fruits of Xia Bing's "special training" in the Thousand Stars Secret Realm shone once more.

—"Calm down,"-Xia Bing called out.-"In narrow corridors, giant beasts of this size are very easy to counter. First, we—"

Methodically and briskly, she took command of the battle.

*

—"So very similar..."-Yan Bailang muttered—so very similar to 'Senior Sister'!

At this precise moment, Yan Bailang was observing the disciples' entire trial progress through an ice mirror.

Xia Bing's display caused Yan Bailang to knit his brows, evoking memories he was utterly loath to revisit.

—"Wasn't this girl originally an outer sect disciple? Her aptitude was only passable... Hmph, hard to say. Perhaps she absorbed enough spiritual energy when she accompanied me to attend the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Winter Clothes Festival, which boosted her cultivation speed?"-Yan Bailang mused, recalling Xia Bing's dossier.-"Still, her strength is far too lacking, far too lacking."

If it had been "Senior Sister," she wouldn't have required anyone's assistance to slaughter all the beast souls single-handedly. This disciple before his eyes had to rally the others just to barely scrape a victory.

Even so, Yan Bailang still activated a talisman and cast it into the ice mirror.

He had never intended to let this batch of disciples grow into their full power anyway.

Let alone yielding the seat of sect master—he wouldn't even permit them to outshine him for the sake of the Immortal Arts Tournament!

Especially a disciple harboring the potential of that person—his senior sister, the former sect master of Thousand Beasts Hall. That was an absolute impossibility.

The strongest figure in Thousand Beasts Hall had to be him, and him alone!

Yan Bailang thought with chilling resolve, staring at Xia Bing and the other disciples through frosty, murderous eyes.

His reason for sending this group of disciples inside was naturally not what he had publicly proclaimed—to enhance their power for the Immortal Arts Tournament. If that had truly been his sole objective, whether sending disciples to other trial grounds or organizing intensive sparring drills, countless methods existed to foster their strength. There was zero necessity to unseal that tower—

[—"Indeed! My skeletal remains are still inside, aren't they?"](<# "Speaker: Inner Voice / Senior Sister's Specter">)

[—"Poor little Bailang, ever since that day, you haven't even possessed the courage to step inside and take a look!"](<# "Speaker: Inner Voice / Senior Sister's Specter">)

[—"You're terrified, aren't you? Terrified that I'm still there... waiting for you right there."](<# "Speaker: Inner Voice / Senior Sister's Specter">)

An icy voice reverberated within Yan Bailang's sea of consciousness. The ghastly, unavenged visage of the woman he had framed and murdered materialized before his eyes like a vengeful specter.

[This is just the Heart-Interrogating Stone! It's nothing more than the Heart-Interrogating Stone!]

[I conquered my inner demon centuries ago!]

—"Shut up!"-Yan Bailang snarled.-"If I could slaughter you once, I can naturally slaughter you a second time!"

Spitting those vicious words, he glared venomously at Xia Bing's reflection in the ice mirror, as though conflating the young girl's silhouette with the murdered woman of his past:

—"Not only that, you will become my nutrient... Yes, exactly what the former sect master planned to do to us back then! So long as I consume them! So long as I consume you! I will inevitably break through! Effortlessly stepping into the Mahayana stage! Even Tribulation Transcendence will be well within my grasp!"-Yan Bailang hissed.

Yes! Why hadn't he done this from the very beginning?

Countless preceding sect masters had already done the exact same thing!

All one had to do was cast disciples into the Beast Soul Tower, allow their spirit beasts to devour enough beast souls to amass immense cultivation, and then butcher them, feeding their corpses to one's own spirit beast to effortlessly absorb all that cultivated power!

Granted, this shortcut carried the grave hazard of inner-demon erosion leading to incurable madness! But he already had the Heart-Interrogating Stone lodging in his brain—what difference did one more inner demon make? Besides, he had killed that woman over two hundred years ago, hadn't he?! And hadn't he lived in perfect safety all this time?!

Yan Bailang rationalized with manic, tangled depravity.

Yes, precisely so. Yan Bailang intended to employ a forbidden evil art native to Thousand Beasts Hall.

Although the succession trial of Thousand Beasts Hall was already gory and brutal by design, human malice was infinitely more wicked. Previous sect masters who found themselves permanently bottlenecked had invented an even more heinous shortcut: dreading the terrifying beast soul and unwilling to wager their own lives, they would cast disciples into the Beast Soul Tower to clear the lethal trials on their behalf, ultimately harvesting those disciples as personal livestock.

Yan Bailang had long yearned to execute this plan. Aside from Xia Bing, whom he had unexpectedly "favored" due to her rapid advancement, the other disciples had been meticulously cultivated specifically with this slaughter in mind.

The only obstacle that had stayed Yan Bailang's hand was not his nonexistent conscience, but his paralyzing fear of the past.

He had constantly dreaded that opening the Beast Soul Tower's doors would unleash the vengeful retribution of the senior sister he had murdered, prompting him to keep the tower sealed tight.

Yet over the past half year, between the Heart-Interrogating Stone's unrelenting day-and-night torment, the cultivation world's diplomatic ostracization, the impending pressure of the Immortal Arts Tournament, and finally the malice bugs that Yuwen Xuan had maliciously sprinkled into his path, he had at last conquered his cowardice. He had swung open the doors to the Beast Soul Tower and set his bloody scheme into motion.

What Yan Bailang failed to perceive was that the instant his resolve hardened, the Heart-Interrogating Stone embedded within his sea of consciousness was being dyed a brilliant, dripping shade of crimson.

*

—"E-Eat it?"-Xia Bing asked, her expression turning utterly bizarre.

Entirely oblivious to the sect master's murderous designs, the disciples had just slain the wolf soul and were presently dividing the spoils.

Yet when Xia Bing observed how the other disciples handled the soul fragments, she was thoroughly horrified. She watched in shock as these disciples commanded their spirit beasts to devour portions of the wolf soul—and one of the disciples' spirit beasts was an herbivorous rabbit!

—"Aren't you going to feed yours?"-one disciple asked in a slightly ambiguous tone.

Glancing at Xia Bing's insect swarm—insect tamers were counted among the elites of beast tamers, and this swarm of fiery crimson insects had just displayed dazzling combat prowess—he said with open envy:

—"Devouring this wolf soul will boost your swarm's strength by another solid tier!"

—"Y-Yeah,"-Xia Bing agreed.

[But they'll also lose control that much faster!]

Xia Bing was well aware that entering the Beast Soul Tower was meant for absorbing beast souls. But according to the instructions set forth in the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, there were strict, scientific methodologies for consumption!

This was an ethereal soul, after all! While spirit beasts weren't like human beings whose spiritual qi raged intensely upon death, the murderous hostility and demonic malice born of slaughter still lingered within the remnant soul. Devouring it raw and untreated like this was nothing short of a direct descent into madness.

Within the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, only a Taotie possessed the anatomical constitution to devour raw beast souls without suffering demonic backlash.

[Do you people seriously believe your spirit beasts are Taoties?!]

Xia Bing thought in silent bewilderment.

She did not venture to interrogate them directly, simply sealing her share of the beast soul inside a specialized spirit soul canister:

—"My insect swarm fights better when kept in a hungry state. I'll feed them once our excursion concludes."

The other disciples sensed nothing amiss. After all, everyone's spirit pets differed, each governed by unique rearing regimens.

However, having sensed something suspicious, Xia Bing casually prodded the others with a few inquiries regarding their cultivation techniques.

Following the preceding battle, Xia Bing had earned subtle authority among these disciples. Furthermore, they were comrades selected to represent the sect in the upcoming Immortal Arts Tournament. While they might one day compete for the seat of sect master, to these juniors still lingering in the lower three realms, such a milestone was far too distant to warrant petty factional backstabbing. Consequently, they answered Xia Bing's inquiries without the slightest defensiveness.

Listening to their explanations, Xia Bing kept a gentle smile plastered across her face, giving away nothing, yet her heart sank steadily like a stone.

It was completely different!

Their *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* was fundamentally different from her own!

Xia Bing had always possessed keen survival instincts—a fact proven by the reality that after receiving the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* from the "demon," she hadn't dared cast a single glance at the entity, remaining completely oblivious to this day that the "demon" was in fact Yuwen Xuan.

Having received a scripture from an unknown "demon," how could Xia Bing not be on guard?

Back then, she had cross-referenced that manual against her own *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* (the Foundation Establishment edition distributed to outer sect disciples). She discovered that while the core contents prior to Foundation Establishment were largely identical, the fine details diverged. Most noticeably, the version bestowed by the demon was vastly more detailed, rigorous, and logically sound.

At the time, Xia Bing found this perfectly reasonable.

As an outer sect disciple, she naturally wouldn't be entrusted with the sect's deepest secrets, making it unsurprising that she had received a simplified edition. Afterward, Xia Bing had borrowed copies from other disciples for comparison, arriving at the exact same conclusion before finally daring to cultivate it in earnest.

Yet now, upon questioning these disciples, Xia Bing discovered that the so-called "direct disciple" edition bestowed by the sect master was even more stripped down than the outer sect's version! It omitted massive swathes regarding the proper nourishment and temperamental conditioning of spirit pets. Even the cultivation enhancement section relied on crude, violent brute-force consumption, while haphazardly incorporating numerous dangerous practices that the "demon's version" explicitly warned against as suicidal hazards!

If this had occurred half a year ago when Xia Bing first received the demon's edition, she certainly would have assumed the demon's version was fraudulent—a sinister trick designed to corrupt her. But after undergoing the trials of the Thousand Stars Secret Realm, her perspective had completely flipped.

Once one grew familiar with that secret realm, one would realize that its true brilliance lay not in "ordinary" resources like spirit herbs, ore veins, or spirit stones, but in its astronomical array of status buffs. By testing various buff combinations, an examinee could accurately observe what consequences their chosen cultivation trajectory would yield, allowing them to make timely course corrections.

Through this empirical testing, Xia Bing had verified the authenticity of the demon's *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*. She found that it was incomparably superior to her simplified edition, enabling steady, risk-free breakthroughs simply by following its steps. In fact, she had grown so terrified of advancing too quickly and exposing her anomaly that she deliberately suppressed her cultivation, choosing to cultivate primarily in external secret realms.

Xia Bing had naturally assumed that the direct disciples under the sect master would possess the complete demon's edition.

Yet what was going on with these core disciples? They seemed to be cultivating an even cruder, more suicidal version than the outer sect!

She initially wanted to probe further, but a voice echoed within her sea of consciousness:

[—"Tch, you dare suspect the sect master?"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone">)

[—"When you yourself are nothing more than a thief."](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone">)

[—"You don't seriously believe this absolves your sin of illicitly practicing the sect's technique, do you?"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone">)

The Heart-Interrogating Stone interrogated her, yet its voice was feebler than ever before—resembling the dying gasp of an inner demon on the verge of dissolution. Indeed, when anyone discovered that their revered elder was plotting to slaughter them and their peers, whatever guilt they harbored over stealing a technique would vanish into thin air.

This sudden realization jolted Xia Bing:

[The sect's *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* is a deadly trap!]

[How could a man like him ever willingly surrender the seat of sect master?!]

[Is this trial truly meant for the Immortal Arts Tournament?!]

Uncovering a single flaw detonated a cascade of subsequent doubts. In truth, Xia Bing had never placed much faith in Yan Bailang to begin with. She knew all too well how she had secured his favor, and given Thousand Beasts Hall's nepotistic, backstabbing power structure, her sense of sect loyalty was virtually nonexistent.

[—"Isn't this a golden opportunity? Since he's exploiting you, why not exploit him right back?"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone / Demon Voice">)

[—"Just devour the beast soul down below, and you will become the sect master."](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone / Demon Voice">)

[—"Just like the 'demon' promised."](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone / Demon Voice">)

At that moment, the Heart-Interrogating Stone abruptly shifted its timbre, whispering to her in the deep, rumbling cadence of the "demon."

Xia Bing's footsteps halted mid-stride.

Yet immediately after, the Heart-Interrogating Stone reverted to its original voice, shrieking:

[—"Are you insane?! You'll get yourself killed!"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone / Original Voice">)

[—"Thousand Beasts Hall is blatantly rotten to the core! Since you possess the authentic *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, your best move is to pack up and flee immediately!"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone / Original Voice">)

[—"After all, he's scheming to butcher you, and you stole his technique—no, the demon said it wasn't even his technique to begin with, so you owe him nothing! Once you break free from Thousand Beasts Hall, you can cultivate entirely on your own, or even establish your own sect!"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone / Original Voice">)

This counter-proposal was equally tantalizing to Xia Bing.

And so, within her sea of consciousness, the Heart-Interrogating Stone began arguing with itself in two distinct voices, locked in a fierce tug-of-war over Xia Bing's thoughts.

Xia Bing endured this splitting headache the entire way. It was a miracle that she could still coordinate with the others to navigate the winding corridors, eliminate ambushing beast souls, and confront the guardian beast soul stationed at the hub of this floor.

Indeed, to maintain the seal on the ferocious beast soul at the very bottom, a guardian beast soul was stationed at the center of every floor of the Beast Soul Tower to enforce the warding boundary. Only by defeating the guardian and claiming its token could one delve deeper.

Under ordinary pretenses, Xia Bing's group was merely attending a training session to bolster their combat prowess for the tournament; they had no obligation to slay the guardian beast. Moreover, defeating it offered no harvestable soul, as the guardian was an integral component of the seal itself.

Yet when they reached the center of the first floor, the two squabbling halves of the Heart-Interrogating Stone suddenly spoke in unison:

[—"Strike it down!"](<# "Speaker: Heart-Interrogating Stone Dual-Voice">)

—"Strike it down, descend to the bottommost floor, claim the ferocious beast soul, and the seat of sect master will be yours!"-one voice urged.

—"Strike it down, feign boundless ambition to hoodwink Yan Bailang, and only then can you make a clean escape!"-the other voice argued.

Since even the conflicting voices in her head had reached a consensus, Xia Bing naturally raised no objections.

Turning to the other disciples, she proposed:

—"Since we've already come this far, why don't we test our mettle against the challenge? Let Lord Sect Master witness that we possess the strength to delve into the next floor!"

This proposal was met with enthusiastic approval from the rest of the cohort.

Rallying their strength, they defeated the guardian beast soul, unlocking the warding seal leading to the lower level.

The moment their feat concluded, Yan Bailang's voice echoed through the corridors of the Beast Soul Tower:

—"Splendidly done! You have successfully conquered the trial of the first floor!"-Yan Bailang's voice proclaimed.

[—"Press onward! I eagerly await you claiming even greater power!"](<# "Speaker: Yan Bailang">)

[—"I hold supreme expectations for every one of you!"](<# "Speaker: Yan Bailang">)

—"Yes, Lord Sect Master!"-Xia Bing replied with an elegant, deferential bow.

Separated by the ice mirror, both parties wore smiles of radiant, mutual deceit.

*

—"Hahaha, they finally opened the way down!"

Unbeknownst to the schemers trading hypocritical pleasantries above, down on the second floor of the Beast Soul Tower, manic laughter erupted.

Upon hearing this dreadful cackling, the soul of a Rank 4 Hou beast spun around in sheer terror to flee, only to be enveloped by billowing black mist and herded in a specific direction. Before long, the vast majority of beast souls roaming the second floor had been corralled into a single corner, shivering together like piglets trapped inside a pen.

—"Which one should I start eating first?"-the black mist muttered, thick drool pattering down like rain.

From the swirling darkness, a colossal claw reached out, lunging toward the nearest beast soul.

Yet before the claw could make contact, it abruptly shrank, morphing into a slender little cloven hoof. The billowing expanse of black mist collapsed inward with a soft pop, condensing into a diminutive black lamb that landed upon the stone floor with a dull *thud*.

[—"Ying-ying?!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb) / Literal CN: 嘤嘤 — high-pitched bleat of distress">)

Yet even though it was merely a tiny lamb, the colossal beast souls towering over it displayed expressions of sheer, unadulterated horror. Now that the perimeter of black mist had dissipated, they scattered in all directions like startled birds.

Seeing his feast dispersing, the little lamb grew even more frantic:

—"[Ying, don't run, ying-ying!](<# "Literal CN: 嘤，你们别跑嘤嘤 — distressed lamb bleating as Yuwen tries to talk in sheep form">)"-the lamb bleated in panic, scrambling on all four hooves to pursue the nearest fugitive.

He yelled in desperate pursuit, but right at that moment, a terrifying voice rang out from behind:

—"Xuan'er, you seem to be having quite the delightful time."

The black lamb froze dead in his tracks.

Slowly, stiffly, he turned around.

Standing there was a silver-haired celestial child appearing no older than three or four, gazing at him with a warm, radiant smile. In his small hands, the child was cradling an enormous switch snapped from an ancient peach tree—one three to four times taller than the celestial child himself.

[—"Ying!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb) / Literal CN: 嘤 — terrified bleat">)

In the blink of an eye, the black lamb bolted across the floor, fleeing for his life exactly like the terrified beast souls before him.
