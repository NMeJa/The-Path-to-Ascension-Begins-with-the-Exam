# Chapter 181: [Nor Grieved by Personal Sorrows](# "Literal CN: 不以己悲 (bù yǐ jǐ bēi) — alluding to Fan Zhongyan's famous line in Yueyang Lou Ji: 'Not pleased by external gains, nor saddened by personal losses' (不以物喜，不以己悲); directly puns on Mo Bei's (莫悲) name and character arc")

[What on earth is that fellow stubbornly persisting for?]

[Ah, don't tell me... he's worried the "test results" will be seen by Master?]

[That guy who only knows how to put on airs in front of Master...]

Half a [shichen](# "Literal CN: 半个时辰 (bàn ge shíchen) — traditional Chinese time unit; half a shichen equals one full hour") ago, Mo Bei had thought coldly to herself.

She held immense disdain for this sort of "targeted, sudden burst of effort" from Mo Xi.

Still, if Mo Xi insisted on it, it wasn't as if she would interfere.

Mo Bei paid no further heed to her idiot younger brother, turning her head to inspect the "program" he had just coded.

How should one put it? Even though during written examinations Mo Bei's mathematics and talismanic runes were fairly decent—sometimes even earning an 'Excellence'—the moment it came to practical execution, she became utterly lost.

[Do Second Senior Brother, Mo Xi, and I really have the same kind of head?]

Staring at the "code" before her eyes for merely a few minutes, Mo Bei felt her concentration beginning to drift.

It was precisely at this moment that she suddenly sensed a chill creeping over her body.

—This was normal. As top-grade cultivation furnace material endowed with the Extreme Yin Constitution, not only was Mo Bei's body temperature inherently low, but her entire being was year-round corroded by an ethereal chill.

In the past, Mo Bei would never have paid attention to this.

She was used to it.

Much like the gazes that fell upon her—as a "commodity," Mo Bei had never bothered to distinguish whether those eyes were filled with admiration, affection, lechery, greed, or other motives; keeping her mind completely blank was simply easier.

However, after taking part in the Immortal Arts Assembly, Mo Bei had begun to pay heed.

After all, in battle, killing intent, hostility, fighting spirit, and even subtle glances were all excellent means of predicting an opponent's movements.

Most importantly, once she confronted them head-on, Mo Bei discovered that so long as she refused to place herself in the position of a "commodity" and instead stood as an autonomous human being capable of action, those negative emotions weren't actually so terrifying. They were like furtive, creeping glances—staring right back at them, open and upright, was by far the simplest, most effective way to deal with them.

The faint, ethereal chill now clinging to her was the same.

When Mo Bei sensed it carefully, she felt it bore an uncanny resemblance to the unease she had previously detected coming from Mo Xi—

No, it had literally peeled right off of Mo Xi!!

Back when this chill had not yet clung to Mo Bei, she could only sense a vague disquiet. But now that it had actually tainted her own person, Mo Bei was able to more vividly "see" the frigid vapor.

It took the form of dark-crimson, icy mist.

Though of a warm hue, it was like a setting sun over an endless glacier—chilling to the point of a stinging ache.

This invisible icy mist had clearly congregated beside Mo Xi at first. Yet as Mo Xi struggled to confront "his future self," that crimson mist had also been peeled away sheet by sheet, drifting aimlessly through the air until portions of it surged toward Mo Bei.

Mo Bei vaguely recalled Mo Xi's somewhat eerie state prior to his breakthrough.

[...I see. Because you couldn't corrode Mo Xi, you came looking for me instead? There really are pests like this from time to time!]

Mo Bei thought coldly.

One could say this was a commonplace occurrence.

Back when they were put up for sale as a packaged "commodity," the definitively female Mo Bei had been the primary item, while Mo Xi was more akin to a "free bonus gift" meant to double the package's value. Yet precisely because this "bonus gift" seemed more affectionate and pleasing, quite a few people were drawn to the "bonus gift" first.

Even upon reaching the Heavenly Spirit Sect, there were still plenty of such fellows.

It was precisely based on this that Mo Bei considered Mo Xi an idiot.

What meaning was there in vying for such toxic "affection"?

Especially when Mo Xi occasionally displayed petty, jealous squabbles to win favor—something Mo Bei found utterly incomprehensible.

...Incomprehensible?

"..."

Mo Bei looked toward the glowing light-screen.

She seemingly ceased paying attention to her surroundings, letting the thoughts in her head disperse alongside the runes and numerals drifting across the light-screen, returning to a state of complete mental emptiness—the very state she had maintained most often in the past.

This state was actually extraordinarily dangerous.

Any manner of [monsters and demons](# "Literal CN: 牛鬼蛇神 (niúguǐ-shéshén) — ox-ghosts and snake-spirits; all kinds of wicked, evil beings or corrupt influences") could easily invade her consciousness.

Yet this state was also very "safe."

Because the Extreme Yin Constitution was fundamentally an extraordinarily "receptive" physique. Despite possessing equivalent cultivation, its combat strength was mediocre; even if one mounted a desperate defense, one still couldn't defeat the opponent. In that case, just as one emptied one's thoughts to avoid paying heed to others, it was simply better to empty oneself entirely.

No one can harm the air, right?

Mo Bei had once believed so.

Yet Mo Bei also remembered that the first time Master detected this mindset of hers, he had looked at her and exhaled a long, heavy sigh.

She had thought Master would preach at her [in exasperation over unmet potential](# "Literal CN: 恨铁不成钢 (hèn tiě bù chéng gāng) — exasperated that iron cannot become steel; frustrated by someone failing to live up to expectations"), just as Mo Xi did, or comfort her with heartfelt earnestness like those people who attempted to become her "friends." But Master had done neither.

Master had merely stated that Mo Bei possessed a natural talent for swordplay, and assigned her a massive pile of relevant coursework.

Seeing the sheer volume of that homework, Mo Xi had gasped in horror. Back then, he hadn't yet placed much trust in Master, so he had adamantly insisted that Master was deliberately tormenting Mo Bei.

Mo Bei, however, hadn't cared in the slightest.

Mindless and heartless as she was, this method of study suited her perfectly.

And so, like a puppet programmed with preset motions, mindless and heartless, she practiced the sword techniques Master taught a hundred times, a thousand times, ten thousand times, ten million times—until the sword forms whipped up sword winds, the sword winds condensed into sword qi, the sword qi birthed sword intent, and the sword intent merged seamlessly into her spiritual energy, permanently anchored at her side.

Now, even when Mo Bei remained utterly devoid of mind and heart, that sword intent formed an airtight, impenetrable web of swords around her. Any entity harboring malice that dared approach would be shredded into mincemeat.

Or rather, when Mo Bei was empty of heart and mind, her sword intent was at its most ruthless.

Right now, it was precisely so.

The moment that scarlet, icy mist merely drifted close to Mo Bei, it was instantly encircled by fine, dense sword intent, ruthlessly sliced, condensed, purified, and annihilated into nothingness.

At once, several shrill, agonizing shrieks erupted from within the crimson mist. Faint silhouettes shattered into fragments, and the mist's bloody hue faded considerably in an instant. Following this trajectory, complete purification seemed only a matter of time.

Right at that moment, an unexpected mutation abruptly occurred.

A cold snort echoed from who-knew-where, and amidst the icy mist severed and scattered by the sword intent, a translucent, crystalline flower suddenly "bloomed."

With the first came a second, a third...

Flowers of icy crystal drifted everywhere through the sword qi, just as if the Snow Glow Grass outside Mo Bei's residence—which had never once bloomed—had suddenly burst into full blossom, scattering petals far and wide.

And wherever the "blossoms" landed, real Snow Glow Grass sprouted forth.

Yet unlike the Snow Glow Grass Mo Bei cultivated—pale, elegant, and ethereal as pastel watercolors—these stalks of Snow Glow Grass were of a deep, visceral scarlet, as though coalesced from tears of blood. Listening closely, one could even catch the sound of faint weeping—and that weeping bore a terrifying resemblance to Mo Bei's own voice.

When Mo Bei snapped back to awareness, she was already standing upon this carpet of scarlet Snow Glow Grass.

—"Hmph!"-Mo Bei uttered an irritated scoff.

[Have my weaknesses been mapped out?]

She could perceive that the opponent—the crimson icy mist—seemed intimately acquainted with her sword techniques. From the very beginning, it had identified the vulnerabilities within her sword intent, targeting them as the focal point of its assault to drag her into this sea of Snow Glow Grass.

The adversary was truly no simple matter!

Why was it so acutely aware of the weaknesses in her sword forms? Some of those weaknesses she hadn't even noticed herself.

Though ever since she first sensed that disquiet, Mo Bei had felt this "unease" was familiar to the point of making her flesh creep.

So Mo Bei mused.

However, rather than self-reflecting on her shortcomings or pondering the enemy's identity, Mo Bei preferred direct action.

In fact, before her conscious mind had even fully caught up, her hand had already drawn the flexible sword at her waist, sweeping out an airtight web of sword light that draped over her surroundings:

*Clang!*

Before long, the sword web collided with something, ringing out with a piercing resonance of clashing metal in midair.

Mo Bei immediately locked onto that position, sending several arcs of sword qi whistling in that direction.

A rapid succession of metallic collisions rang out.

It sounded distinctly like someone trading blows with Mo Bei. Though the afterimages moved too fast for the eye to catch, judging from the light, shadows, and rhythm of the maneuvers, they were strikingly similar to Mo Bei's own sword forms... No, that *was* Mo Bei's swordplay.

A moment later, a corpse plummeted from the sky. Astoundingly, it was another Mo Bei!

Or rather, "future Mo Bei"?

Her figure was slightly taller and more slender than the current Mo Bei, possessing a bit more flesh and distinct feminine curves, yet her wooden, blank face was identical to the present Mo Bei—if anything, the current Mo Bei was noticeably more animated.

Of course, even if this "future Mo Bei" wished to appear animated, she could no longer do so, because she had already been impaled clean through, collapsing like a limp puppet into the blood-red grass.

Yet Mo Bei did not let down her guard over this victory. The flexible sword in her hand danced like an agile serpent, twirling gracefully in midair as she pressed her attacks against several other locations without a pause. Wherever her sword forms swept, colossal blocks of ice congealed.

Frozen within those massive ice blocks were astonishingly several more Mo Beis.

There were also Mo Beis who had not been frozen.

Yet they were little different from the ones encased in ice: all shared the same wooden expression, vacuous eyes, and streaks of bloody tears trailing from the corners of their lids. The only distinctions lay in their attire. The one who had just been slain still wore the uniform of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, but among the others, some wore ordinary Daoist robes, some wore the revealing "commodity attire" of the Fallen Immortal Palace, and some wore normal clothes yet dragged heavy shackles on both hands and feet. The most terrifying one, for reasons unknown, wore the [phoenix coronet and embroidered robes](# "Literal CN: 凤冠霞帔 (fèngguān xiápèi) — the traditional ornate ceremonial attire of a wealthy Han Chinese bride") reserved for a bride, while blood steadily dripped and gushed from her abdomen.

Facing these eerie duplicates, Mo Bei did not hesitate for a single second.

Nor did she care that every time her sword struck down another "self," clearer weeping echoed within her mind:

[—"I don't want to do that!"](# "Speaker: Future Mo Bei")

[—"There is no turning back anymore..."](# "Speaker: Future Mo Bei")

[—"Hurry and kill me! I don't want to go on like this!"](# "Speaker: Future Mo Bei")

[—"Why must it be 'me'?!"](# "Speaker: Future Mo Bei")

[—"Am I even still me?"](# "Speaker: Future Mo Bei")

Yet these chaotic voices failed to disrupt Mo Bei's sword forms in the least.

If anything, her swordplay grew sharper and more decisive with every pass.

After all, each "self" embodied a vulnerability in her own swordsmanship. This manner of live combat was far more effective than slaughtering endless hordes of demonic beasts. The more she swung her sword, the cleaner her strikes became, and the purer her spiritual energy grew—

Finally, the spiritual energy enveloping Mo Bei transformed into a blustering blizzard of absolute zero. Within her heterochromatic eyes—which had once matched Mo Xi's golden and brown pair—that brown eye turned completely into an icy, crystalline blue!

*

—"Ah, she broke through!"-Shui Huang'er shrieked.-"Little Sister Mo Bei broke through too!"

—"And that's not all! Her realm seems to be skyrocketing non-stop! Early Golden Core? Mid Golden Core? Late Golden Core? Golden Core Great Perfection?! Aaaah—!"

Shui Huang'er squealed at the top of her lungs, looking as thrilled as if she were the one breaking through.

Indeed, this method of advancing was extraordinarily rare. Even Tao Zhu wore an expression of astonishment:

—"That's how sword cultivators walking the [Dao of Emotionlessness](# "Literal CN: 无情道 (wúqíng dào) — the Path of Emotionlessness/Heartlessness; a cultivation path requiring the severing of mortal passions and worldly attachments") are. Though their pace of advancement is slightly slower, the moment they break through, they are instantly invincible among peers of the same realm,"-Boss Tao explained, racking his brain to recall trivia.—"Wait, Bujuan, you actually let your disciple practice the Dao of Emotionlessness? Don't you utterly despise such cultivation methods that suppress human nature?"

—"No. I never had her practice the Dao of Emotionlessness. Could she be... Oh no!"-Zhou Hui cried.

Watching Mo Bei's condition, Zhou Hui revealed a rare expression of fluster.

With a flick of his wide sleeve, his figure vanished from where he stood, leaving Tao Zhu and Shui Huang'er blinking at each other in bewildered silence.

*

Yet Mo Bei did not feel the slightest shred of joy over her breakthrough in realm.

In truth, the instant she broke through, her sword motion froze mid-swing. Her face, which had still possessed some semblance of expression, abruptly turned deathly pale—becoming utterly blank and wooden, bearing virtually no distinction from the surrounding "Mo Beis."

What was more, a drop of bloody tear rolled down from the eye that had just crystallized into icy blue.

Beholding this sight, a mocking voice sneered:

[—"Hehehe, remember now?"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"...No, that's not right. You don't have a 'remembering' function at all. Those memories have simply been crammed into your mind, so you should understand by now, shouldn't you?"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"You know about your foolish deeds in the previous cycle, don't you?"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"You betrayed Master!"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"You betrayed the Heavenly Spirit Sect!"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"You brought about everyone's deaths!"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"You even killed yourself!"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")-the voice accused.

At first, it was mere derisive mockery, but with each mounting accusation, the voice cracked into weeping:

[—"How can you still be alive?!"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

[—"Why didn't you just die before any of it happened?!"](# "Speaker: Illusory Voice / Lingering Cycle Memory")

Accompanying this shrill denunciation, all the "Mo Beis" sprang into motion. Wielding identical sword forms, they lunged toward the frozen Mo Bei standing stock-still—

Zhou Hui rushed over just in time to face this exact scene.

He moved to intercept the convergent sword strikes, but mid-motion, he sensed something. An expression of surprise surfaced on Zhou Hui's face. Twisting his wrist, he didn't intervene to protect Mo Bei; instead, he scooped up a bead of blood-colored light from beside her.

It was fortunate Zhou Hui acted with such swiftness. In the very next split-second, colossal pillars of frigid ice detonated around Mo Bei, blasting every single "Mo Bei" and the carpet of Snow Glow Grass into powdery particles of ice crystals.

Even the entire illusory space of the desire trial was shattered into oblivion by the blast.

Mo Bei found herself back upon the shaded promenade of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. Around her, glittering crystalline ice particles drifted and swirled, resembling a heavy snowfall.

Amidst the dancing ice crystals, Mo Bei still stood dazed and motionless, like a pillar of frost.

Looking at Mo Bei in this state, Zhou Hui breathed a soft sigh:

—"Bei'er."

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"Bei'er, pay no mind to those memories,"-Zhou Hui said, alighting beside Mo Bei.-"They are merely phantoms of a past cycle. Everything has already passed. There is no need to grieve over things that never truly existed in your memory..."

Zhou Hui reached out a hand, intending to cover his disciple's eyes and erase those memories along the way.

Yet a pair of ice-cold hands caught Zhou Hui's arm.

—"No, Master. Your disciple is unfilial."

—"I am not sad. I don't feel the slightest bit of sadness."

So Mo Bei spoke, lifting her face.

Indeed, there was no grief upon her face. Although a bead of bloody tear clung to her cheek, her expression held only empty void. Even her heterochromatic golden and blue eyes lacked focus, seeming to look at Zhou Hui, yet also gazing into some unknown distance:

—"I only want to kill!"

—"I want to kill that foolish self from the previous cycle! And Eldest Senior Brother and Second Senior Brother too!!"

—"And of course, what I want to kill most of all... are the scumbags who manipulated my destiny, treated me like a puppet, and toyed with my life!!!"

Zhou Hui listened quietly to his disciple's venomous, hate-filled voice, letting out another soft sigh.

—"Bei'er,"-he said, gently patting the crown of her head.

—"Master..."-Mo Bei whispered, feeling the touch atop her head. Trembling, she threw her arms around Zhou Hui's waist, hugging him with a force so desperate that even Zhou Hui at the Tribulation Transcending stage felt a twinge of pain. She finally burst into tears:—"I don't want to become like that! I want to protect you! I want to protect the Heavenly Spirit Sect! I want to protect everything I have right now!"

—"I know,"-Zhou Hui said gently.
