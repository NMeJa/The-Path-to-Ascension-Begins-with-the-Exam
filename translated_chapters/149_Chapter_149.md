# Chapter 149: Ying (第149章 嘤)

[← Previous Chapter](148_Chapter_148.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](150_Chapter_150.md)

---

—"Master, how did you return early?"-Qu Qianxing asked.

Upon spotting Zhou Hui's paper origami bird, Qu Qianxing panicked for an instant.

Yet unlike his martial brothers and sisters, who panicked because 'Master caught them red-handed doing something wicked after coming back early,' his panic stemmed purely from the terror of: 'My homework isn't polished to absolute perfection yet, and Master is conducting an unscheduled inspection!'

Indeed, Qu Qianxing felt absolutely zero guilt about his actions.

In his mind, he was simply executing the homework assignments Master had left behind:

*Safeguard the sect*—a slight hiccup had nearly occurred, but there had been ample time for remedial action. Right now, he was wrapping up the loose ends alongside Lu Shulong and Qingyang Qingyu; at the very least, the disaster hadn't escalated further.

*Take good care of the sect disciples*—setting aside those who already had coursework, every idle disciple in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, down to the introductory classes, had been rotated into Eternal Villa's spirit stone secret realm. Their summer internships and summer vacation excursions were fully booked back-to-back.

*Keep his fellow disciples in line*—every disciple was swamped up to their necks with zero time to cause mischief. Right now, the only party suffering from "issues" was someone else (the Demon Realm).

*However! As an honor student, was merely finishing the homework enough?!*

For a three-thousand-word essay, he was someone who routinely submitted fifty thousand words. Naturally, he couldn't afford to slack off on any other assignment!

—"I apologize, Master. My schedule planning lacked reasonable foresight,"-Qu Qianxing confessed with palpable strain.

Indeed, Master had already announced he would return. Given Master's temperament, he would inevitably return ahead of schedule—how could Qu Qianxing have failed to factor that into his calculations?!

—"Grant me half a month more... no, just ten days!"-Qu Qianxing pleaded, making a rare request for an assignment deadline extension.-"I will completely conquer the insect nest sector and establish a permanent forward outpost!"

—"Wait, I never assigned you such a task, did I?"-Zhou Hui's voice emanated from the paper origami bird.

The little bird drew everyone's attention.

Upon hearing its voice and realizing this was a paper attendant dispatched by the Untiring Immortal Venerable, however, quite a few people wore the expressions of guilty students subjected to a surprise pop quiz by their teacher. Lu Shulong, in particular, shrank backward furtively into the crowd.

Zhou Hui did, in fact, act every bit like an instructor conducting a pop inspection.

Dropping its patterned, paper-cut wings, the origami bird perched upon Qu Qianxing's shoulder:

—"Let's hear it, then,"-Zhou Hui said.-"Why did you do this?"

—"Because this was the optimal strategy I could devise,"-Qu Qianxing answered.

He had never once feared being questioned by Master.

Methodically and crisply, Qu Qianxing laid out his reasoning:

—"I am not as omnipotent as Master. I lack the power to reseal a spatial rift that has already ruptured. Furthermore, Master's original teachings clearly pointed out the way forward: [channeling is superior to damming](# "Literal CN: 堵不如疏 (dǔ bù rú shū) — ancient proverb from Yu the Great taming the floods: diverting/dredging water is far better than building blockades")! Rather than attempting to evade the problem, it is far better to confront it head-on. Offense is the best defense."

—"Therefore, I constructed an outpost directly at the rift. While this inevitably creates a permanent opening, it anchors and stabilizes the local spatial framework, preventing secondary collapses from spawning additional fissures elsewhere. If a rift connecting to the Demon Realm were to erupt in an arbitrary, unguarded location, *that* would truly be a catastrophic disaster. By comparison, the Spirit Palace is the most advantageous battleground we could ask for."

—"As for assaulting the demonic creatures, that was an unexpected bonus."

—"Originally, I only intended to reorganize the tunnels excavated by the disciples of Eternal Villa and erect a defensive barricade, 'conveniently' exterminating a few fiends near the breach so they wouldn't obstruct construction. What I did not anticipate was that once we actually got to work, I discovered this territory has no 'master' whatsoever!"

Hearing this, the little bird blinked its paper-cut eyes:

—"No 'master'?"-the paper bird repeated.

—"To put it accurately, the demons lack a cohesive, centralized regime,"-Qu Qianxing analyzed.-"They exist in a primitive, Darwinian state of [the weak falling prey to the strong](# "Literal CN: 弱肉强食 (ruòròu qiángshí) — law of the jungle"). Although entities like 'Demon Sovereigns' and 'Demon Emperors' ostensibly exist, in truth they merely exert control over their immediate domains. Governing independently in fragmented autonomy leaves vast swathes of the Demon Realm as unclaimed territories. Fortunately, the sector beyond this rift happens to be just such a lawless buffer zone. As for the granular details, I have compiled an exhaustive report that I will present to you upon our return."

The origami bird nodded in approval.

`!`

Having rushed over the moment she heard the Untiring Immortal Venerable had arrived, Qingyang Qingyu's eyes widened in utter disbelief:

[You wrote a report?!]

[When on earth did you write it?!]

[Why didn't you give me a heads-up beforehand?!]

Qingyang Qingyu realized she had suffered a catastrophic strategic blunder.

Qu Qianxing continued smoothly:

—"Furthermore, I discovered that aside from the demonic insect nest, other species of fiends are nowhere near as plentiful as ancient lore suggested. In fact, even the demonic insects inside the nest are not as terrifying as historical records describe. In my assessment, this is likely because they have not yet reached 'maturity.'"

—"According to the demonic qi historical curves Master had us track, beast tides consistently coincide with peak years of ambient demonic qi. Evidently, demonic energy profoundly dictates the reproductive cycles and physiological states of beast hordes. Thus, I boldly hypothesized: could demonic qi exert an even more direct influence upon demonic lifeforms? Should the insects here multiply into greater numbers and grow more formidable—or even coalesce into an unstoppable Demonic Tide—halting them would become impossible."

—"Hence, rather than allowing that scenario to materialize, it is vastly superior to erect an airtight, fortified defense network beforehand."

Perched on his shoulder, the little paper bird nodded once more.

—"Naturally, investigating the origin of the insect nest is also one of our primary research objectives,"-Qingyang Qingyu piped up at lightning speed, hurriedly adding her own contribution.-"Judging by the structural architecture of that hive, its formation is decidedly unnatural! Moreover, demonic anatomical structures warrant rigorous scholarly contemplation—regarding this line of inquiry, I shall submit a supplemental research paper shortly."

`!!`

Having just sprinted over to enjoy the drama, Mo Xi's eyes bulged in horror.

[Why are you submitting a paper too?!]

To the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Second Senior Brother 'jumping the gun' and pulling ahead in academic overdrive was practically routine.

Even Mo Xi, after competing against him a few times, had conceded that [he simply couldn't out-involute him](# "Literal CN: 卷不过 (juǎn bùguò) — unable to compete in the cutthroat academic rat race / involution").

But Qingyang Qingyu was just an exchange student!

Sure, she was a terrifyingly talented test-taker, but at the end of the day, she was still an exchange student!

If even an exchange student was grinding this hard, wouldn't that make real direct disciples like himself look utterly useless?!

—"I... I will submit a report as well,"-Mo Xi stammered out hesitantly.-"Senior Sister Qingyang's map editor incident provided me with considerable inspiration. I would like to synthesize a comprehensive summary..."

[Are you openly filing a formal grievance against me right to Master's face?!]-Qingyang Qingyu glared daggers at Mo Xi.

Mo Xi promptly shifted his gaze away.

`!!!`

Yet someone was even more shocked and outraged than Qingyang Qingyu: Lu Shulong.

*Never mind Second Senior Brother and Senior Sister Qingyang—Mo Xi, what business do you have jumping into this dogpile?!*

*You aren't even part of this project group!*

Lu Shulong sucked in a sharp breath through his teeth, throwing up both hands in total surrender:

—"Then I shall write one too. As for my thesis topic..."-Lu Shulong lapsed into dead silence for a full quarter of an hour before grinding out with immense difficulty:-"From the perspective of an objective bystander, I shall evaluate the merits and pitfalls of the Secret Realm Guidance System."

[That's just another grievance report!]-Qingyang Qingyu and Mo Xi glared at Lu Shulong in unison.

Without question, Lu Shulong's thesis topic would catch Mo Xi right in the crosshairs of collateral damage.

Be that as it may, Lu Shulong had successfully locked in his thesis topic. The crushing academic pressure now cascaded entirely onto the sole remaining individual in the room without a proposal.

`!!?`

Jian Nuo's eyes went round as saucers:

—"D-Do I have to write one too?!"-Jian Nuo blurted out.

He wasn't the Untiring Immortal Venerable's disciple!

Besides, this horrific contraption called a 'research paper' sounded downright nightmarish.

—"No need,"-the little bird chirped.

Zhou Hui couldn't fathom how this situation had devolved into everyone frantically clamoring to turn in academic theses.

Still, young people striving for self-improvement was a commendable thing.

By comparison, Jian Nuo paled beside Jian Haoge! Back in the day, whenever confronted with a similar scenario, Jian Haoge would never have conceded defeat—even if he knew absolutely nothing, he would have stubbornly scraped together something to present.

Zhou Hui sighed nostalgically in his thoughts.

A cold shiver instantly ran down Jian Nuo's spine.

Belatedly, an awful realization dawned on him:

[Did I just make a catastrophic life choice?]

He heard the Immortal Venerable ask in an exceedingly flat tone:

—"Your friends should be arriving soon, shouldn't they?"-the Immortal Venerable asked.-"Go ahead and keep them well-entertained."

It sounded like ordinary, polite hospitality, yet why did it reek so distinctly of being written off as an unteachable lost cause?!

Jian Nuo scrambled to salvage the situation:

—"I... I can also—"-Jian Nuo began.

Regrettably, he was a step too late. Zhou Hui had already turned his attention back to instruct Qu Qianxing:

—"I merely dropped by to survey the situation. Since you have drafted a coherent plan, do not rush. Proceed according to your original design. However, I have a recommendation: since you have advanced this far, why not contemplate how to integrate your foundational cultivation technique into the engineering of these fortifications? Metal is both absolute offense and impenetrable defense. While mastering the arts of lightning and thunder, never lose sight of your core spiritual root. Only when properly harnessed..."

Once the discussion delved into such intricate technical specifics, the Immortal Venerable's impromptu pedagogical lecture was evidently not going to conclude anytime soon.

Jian Nuo's shoulders slumped in utter defeat.

Lu Shulong gave his shoulder a deceptively warm, reassuring pat.

Jian Nuo immediately looked back at him with pleading eyes.

Lu Shulong offered a saintly, gentle smile:

—"Just focus on giving your 'friends' a proper reception!"-Lu Shulong said warmly.

---

In truth, Jian Nuo's "friends" arrived far later than anticipated.

A full half-month prior, Jian Nuo had received word that they had departed Sky City. At Jian Nuo's traveling pace, they ought to have arrived by the second or third day—and had they arrived back then, perhaps that whole business of blasting open a rift into the Demon Realm might never have happened (probably). Jian Nuo figured that no matter how much they dawdled, they should have reached the destination within a week. Who could have imagined that this gang of second-generation cultivators would party their way all the way from the Northern Domain down to the Central Domain? They had even attended several lavish banquet galas hosted by the Palace of Carefree Wandering in the Eastern Domain, dragging their feet for more than half a month before finally staggering into the Central Domain.

In other words, by the time they reached the Central Domain, they were already partied out.

Consequently, the entourage merely made a brief transit through Fengqing City without stopping, rushing straight toward Eternal Villa.

By this time, the regional qualifiers for the Immortal Arts Assembly had long concluded, while the grand continental finals had yet to commence. On the surface, Fengqing City appeared to have returned to its original baseline state—though certain shifts, once set in motion, could never be undone. Of course, such profound developments were entirely irrelevant to this frivolous crowd of pleasure-seeking scions. Thus, in the eyes of these second-generation cultivators, this was the absolute dullest lull imaginable.

The moment the group laid eyes on Jian Nuo, they began whining in a chorus of grievances:

[—"Why is the Central Domain so run-down and shabby?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"The Southern Domain might be a backwater too, but at least it had exotic charm!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"And it's nowhere near as bustling as *White Jade Capital Daily* claimed!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Exactly! We checked out the tournament arena in Fengqing City—it was totally underwhelming."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Though I nearly ran into my Martial Grand-Uncle nearby. Scared the living daylights out of me!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Are those newspapers printing outright fabrications? I'm going to file a formal complaint!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Their deafening yammering gave Jian Nuo a splitting headache.

Or rather, Jian Nuo had already been nursing a massive migraine.

It all went back to that wretched research paper.

Ever since everyone had been assigned theses to write, the atmosphere had devolved into frantic, hair-trigger agitation. It made Jian Nuo feel like an utterly useless loafer loitering around in empty space. Even the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples who had arrived as reinforcements were assigned experimental reports. The entire subterranean work site resembled purgatory more than actual hell! To an uninformed onlooker, one would think the demons were on the verge of overrunning the outpost any second!

Through this traumatic realization, Jian Nuo recognized his catastrophic blunder.

He thought to himself that even though he had been nominally 'exempted,' under such an all-consuming academic peer-pressure cooker, he simply *had* to turn in something.

Yet only upon digging deeper did he realize how insurmountably difficult it was!

He couldn't even determine a viable thesis proposal for step one.

Jian Nuo's head felt ready to explode, and on top of that, he had to deal with this flock of [deadweight](# "Literal CN: 冤种 (yuānzhǒng) — internet slang for exasperating, troublesome burdens / suckers") "friends."

This pushed Jian Nuo into extreme irritability, completely stripping away whatever patience he had left [to play along with their social pleasantries](# "Literal CN: 虚与委蛇 (xūyǔwēiyí) — dealing with someone insincerely; going through polite motions"):

—"If you find it so dreadfully boring, how about you pack your bags and get the hell back home?"-Jian Nuo snapped, his expression darkening as he glared frigidly at the crowd.

His "friends" instantly fell dead silent.

Jian Nuo didn't bother sparing them another glance. Letting out a frosty scoff, he strode inward.

Yet a rebuke of this caliber was hardly enough to deter such thick-skinned scions:

[—"Did something happen? I've never seen Young Master Jian look so gloomy."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"What else could it be? He obviously failed to woo the girl!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"I told you from the start, women from the Qingyang clan are simply—"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Hush! Young Master Jian will skin you alive if he hears that!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The scions muttered behind his back, not daring to flaunt their gossip to Jian Nuo's face.

After all, as the undisputed leader of this clique, Jian Nuo possessed absolute, unquestioned authority.

Seeing Jian Nuo's sullen misery, however, one individual in the crowd breathed a covert sigh of relief:

[Even though Jian Nuo falling for a woman of the Qingyang clan and idolizing the Untiring Immortal Venerable was his original 'character setting,' judging by current circumstances, his progress is hitting a brick wall! That's for the best. At least there's no need to worry about him being recruited over to their side!]

The person harboring these inner calculations was a remarkably alluring young man.

His features were strikingly exquisite, yet his physique was delicate and frail, his skin paper-white as though a sudden gust of wind might blow him away.

Such an ethereal figure appeared rather out of place among a rowdy pack of pampered cultivation scions.

The companions flanking him treated him with exceptional solicitude. Seeing him linger at the rear in a daze, several stepped forward to support him:

—"Xuenian, are you feeling unwell again?"-one asked with concern.

—"No... *cough, cough!*"-the beauty addressed as "Xuenian" shook his head, only to erupt into a violent fit of coughing, causing anxious panic to ripple through the faces of several peers.

[—"Xuenian, are you alright?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Was the journey truly too rushed after all?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"I said from the start, you never should have brought Xuenian along!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"And leaving Xuenian alone in Sky City wouldn't be utterly pitiful?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Liu Xuenian managed a strained, fragile smile:

—"It is quite alright. You have all taken wonderful care of me along the journey... We should hurry along. Young Master Jian already seems to be in foul spirits; making him angrier would be terribly improper,"-Liu Xuenian said softly.

—"Tch! That guy Jian Nuo..."-someone grumbled in immediate annoyance.

—"It is not Young Master Jian's fault,"-Liu Xuenian spoke up in Jian Nuo's defense.-"Being away from Sky City, it is only natural he feels out of his element. Having an irritable temper is entirely understandable."

[—"Xuenian always stands up for Young Master Jian!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Well, it's the truth, isn't it? I heard the Qingyang clan was some prominent noble house handed down from the ancient White Jade Capital, but seeing it in person, they're completely underwhelming! The Central Domain can't hold a candle to the Northern Domain. Young Master Jian is practically slumming it out here."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Indeed! We should just urge him to give up on her. There are plenty of fish in the sea!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"He'll never listen. Surprisingly enough, Young Master Jian is a hopeless romantic. It'd be far better to persuade him to marry her and whisk her back to Sky City as soon as possible. Surely Young Master Jian wouldn't want his beloved suffering out here either!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

—"Yet the young lady will not agree so easily,"-Xuenian sighed softly.-"Surely we cannot just abduct her by force, can we?"

`...`

Xuenian's seemingly offhand remark caused the second-generation cultivators to snap their heads around.

—"Whatever is the matter?"-Xuenian tilted his head with an innocent, bewildered look.

[—"Brilliant idea!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

Someone suddenly cheered under their breath.

This appeared to startle Xuenian.

Yet if these spoiled scions shared consensus on nothing else, they were in 100% agreement when it came to stirring up reckless chaos:

[—"Exactly! Wouldn't everything be solved if we simply snatched that Qingyang woman away?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Jian Nuo will thank us on his knees!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Not necessarily, right? Jian Nuo is surprisingly conservative about courtship."](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Doesn't matter! Once we snatch her out, Jian Nuo can 'rescue' her from us! With a bit of [back-and-forth heroics](# "Literal CN: 一来二去 (yīlái-èrqù) — over repeated interactions / through back-and-forth contact"), their romance will ignite like wildfire! Besides, once she's transported back to Sky City, Eternal Villa won't have any grounds to keep her!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

[—"Pure genius!"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

The cluster of scions chuckled like giddy buffoons.

Blended into their midst, Xuenian covered his mouth with his sleeve, wearing a faint smile that appeared like an apologetic smirk on the surface, yet harbored a chillingly sinister undertone.

Right at that moment, Jian Nuo's voice barked from ahead:

—"What are you all still standing around over there for?"-Jian Nuo glared impatiently at the bunch loitering in the distance. Were this not the territory of the Qingyang clan—where leaving them unsupervised risked untold debauchery—Jian Nuo wouldn't have spared them a second thought.

Naturally, scheming to [cook the raw rice into porridge](# "Literal CN: 生米煮成熟饭 (shēngmǐ zhǔchéng shúfàn) — create a fait accompli; make it an irreversible done deal"), the entourage had no intention of tipping off Jian Nuo in advance.

Instead, one fellow stepped up with a shifty, rat-like grin:

—"By the way, Young Master Jian, we've been here this long—how come we haven't seen Miss Qingyang yet?"-the scion asked slyly.

—"Qingyu has a research paper to write,"-Jian Nuo replied curtly. He wasn't about to let his precious crush interact with these degenerate clowns!

—"A paper? That trendy fad that was 'popular' in the newspapers a while back?"-an illiterate scion asked.

Liu Xuenian smiled gently:

—"Yet now that we have arrived, it would hardly be polite not to greet our host, would it?"-Liu Xuenian asked softly.

Jian Nuo glanced at Xuenian, raising an eyebrow:

—"Naturally, you shall meet her,"-Jian Nuo replied.-"She has even prepared special arrangements to entertain you all!"

—"What is it? What has sister-in-law prepared?"-someone asked eagerly, hyping the mood.

—"Until we are wed, do not address her like that!"-Jian Nuo shot him a venomous glare.

Only after the scion shrank back with an abashed grimace did Jian Nuo add:

—"She prepared demonic fiends to entertain you."

[—"Demonic... what?"](# "Speaker: Cultivation Scion")

—"Demonic fiends,"-Jian Nuo enunciated word by agonizing word.-"Fiends have crawled out from beneath the Qingyang clan's seal. They require you to go save the world. Do you have the guts?"

The second-generation cultivators looked at one another in stunned, flabbergasted silence.
