# Chapter 238: Behind the Scenes

[← Previous Chapter](237_Chapter_237.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](239_Chapter_239.md)

—"That Demon Sovereign... just what is his background?!"-Tian Shou, the so-called Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King, demanded with immense difficulty amidst the raining deluge of lightning.

After battling for half a day, he hadn't even cleared up who his opponent actually was.

Yet one couldn't truly chalk it up to Tian Shou's arrogance—though he was indeed exceptionally arrogant—but rather that celestial generals of the Heavenly Court possessed boundless lifespans; and conversely, high-tier demons were exactly the same.

Although low-tier demons were like tiny insects that lived in the morning and perished by evening, fit only to serve as [cannon fodder](# "Literal CN: 炮灰") on the battlefield, demons of the Demon Emperor caliber were like the True Immortals of the Heavenly Court—entities possessing near-infinite lifespans.

Consequently, in the great war between immortals and demons, the opposing commanders on both sides were virtually always old acquaintances; the next war was merely the continuation of the last. Even when a "newcomer" cropped up, they required tens of thousands of years of tempering before growing formidable enough for their names to be remembered by either the Demon Realm or the Heavenly Court.

Naturally, as long as time was sufficiently vast, "accidents" would inevitably occur.

Yet what the Immortal and Demon Realms lacked least of all was time. When time was stretched to a certain magnitude and the sample size of "accidents" grew large enough, even such anomalies began to display a predictable regularity.

For instance, at this very moment, though the Heavenly Court's Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King had been taken aback by the sudden deluge of lightning, he swiftly regained his composure and turned to his strategist hailing from the Ministry of Celestial Destiny:

—"Has another deity or high immortal fallen into demonic corruption?!"-Tian Shou demanded.

The strategist gave a start:

—"N-No, surely not?"-the strategist replied nervously.

—"No? Bullshit!"-Tian Shou cursed crudely.-"This father hasn't just stepped onto the battlefield for the first day! These Demon Emperors, Demon Sovereigns, and Celestial Demons who suddenly crop up out of nowhere with transcendent might—out of every ten, thirteen of them fell from 'above'!"

—"H-Heavenly King, sir, one cannot speak so recklessly,"-the strategist stammered, growing even more anxious.

Evidently, he was not anxious because he believed Tian Shou was wrong, but rather terrified that such treasonous talk would be overheard. He cast furtive glances left and right, petrified that someone might be paying attention.

Fortunately, beneath the sky where thunder crashed down like a torrential gale, the surrounding celestial soldiers and generals were straining with all their might to uphold defensive barrier arrays against the lightning strikes. Compounded by the deafening roar of thunder, no one noticed what blasphemous remarks General Tian Shou had just uttered.

Even so, the strategist broke into a cold sweat, attempting to "correct" his superior:

—"Look at what you are saying, Heavenly King, sir,"-the strategist pleaded.-"Our Celestial Realm doesn't possess even a single wisp of demonic qi, so how could anyone fall so easily?"

—"Who knows?"-Tian Shou sneered.

He had no intention of avoiding other ears, because what he had just voiced was, in truth, an open secret acknowledged throughout the highest echelons of the Heavenly Court.

Indeed: Celestial Demons and high-tier demons were transformed from gods and immortals.

Yet unlike what Tian Shou suggested, such transformation was not the conventional "fall" of succumbing to spiritual trials along the path of cultivation, nor was it the "contamination" by demonic qi claimed by the strategist. Rather, it was a naturally occurring "evolution."

After all, the lifespans of gods and immortals were far too protracted, measured casually in units of tens of thousands of years. As long as they did not "perish," their existence was inexhaustible. But what in reality was truly eternal? At the very least, living beings were incapable of it.

Although one of the goals of cultivating immortality was to modify one's physical body from the very outset to withstand the erosion of endless eons, when even the cosmos itself was not necessarily eternal, how could mortal cultivators forge an eternal vessel that had never been seen, never been known, and never existed?

Hence, even upon ascending to immortality, whether these immortals were still "themselves" at the terminus of boundless epochs was exceedingly difficult to say.

This was especially true for the cohort that attained godhood. Almost all of them had completely stripped away their humanity and mortal desires, turning into concrete matter—such as the sun, the moon, and the stars—or dissolving outright into conceptual entities, becoming one of the Three Thousand Great Daos, literally 'attaining the Great Dao' in the most physical and absolute sense.

Yet this was considered the fortunate outcome—at least for the mortal world.

Far more gods and immortals, grappling with the agonizing riddle of the 'self,' chose either to sever their doubts and negative emotions, discarding them into the void to coalesce into Celestial Demons, or chose to embrace madness directly, becoming Celestial Demons themselves.

For this reason, despite possessing infinite lifespans, the officials across the Heavenly Court—including the Celestial Emperor himself—had undergone several silent [changes of administration](# "Literal CN: 换届 — transition of government leadership / election term turnover") over the ages.

And this time, another "change of administration" seemed close at hand.

Thirty celestial years ago, during a sacrificial rite, the Ministry of Celestial Destiny divined that the Celestial Emperor was destined to encounter a catastrophic calamity in the future. Thereafter, the Heavenly Court undertook countless measures to avert this fate, yet none seemed of any use; the ill omen persisted without wavering, to the point that the Celestial Emperor entertained thoughts of abdicating his throne...

—Or rather, judging from his organization of the Sky-Tour Ceremony, perhaps he was merely seeking a scapegoat onto whom he could transfer that inescapable doom?

Only, that ceremony had failed as well.

Furthermore, once the Celestial Emperor truly abdicated, the aftermath would be entirely unpredictable. If he possessed a method to reclaim the imperial throne, that was one thing; but if the ill omen came to pass, a new Celestial Emperor would inevitably trigger seismic convulsions across the upper ranks of the Heavenly Court—in that regard, it was no different from the mortal realm.

Because of this, the immortal officials of the Heavenly Court had all grown somewhat restless of late.

Pondering up to this point, Tian Shou seemed to arrive at a conclusion:

—"Speaking of which, this Demon Sovereign who suddenly popped out of nowhere... could he have some connection to the Celestial Emperor?"-Tian Shou muttered.

[—"Huh?"](# "Speaker: Strategist")

—"Could it be otherwise?"-Tian Shou glared at the strategist. He harbored zero trust toward these strategists hailing from the Ministry of Celestial Destiny.-"The Celestial Emperor's Sky-Tour Ceremony just failed, and immediately such a formidable Demon Sovereign emerges on the frontlines. It hardly feels like a coincidence."

—"The Sky-Tour Ceremony failed? Ah, no, I mean..."-the strategist stammered.

The celestial banquet at Xuanpu was not something a mere army advisor could attend, so he had no knowledge of the ceremony's outcome—nor did he want to know! Looking at General Tian's loose-tongued manner, knowing too much felt like an express ticket to being dragged into unspeakable disaster.

Rather, General Tian's total lack of filter was already troublesome enough, and Lord Tian Yan happened not to be here to rein him in...

The strategist thought in sheer chaos.

He parted his lips, completely at a loss for how to pacify—or muzzle—his commander. Fortunately—or perhaps unfortunately—at that exact moment, a bolt of heavenly thunder seemed to sense Tian Shou's insolence and crashed unceremoniously straight toward their position!

This bolt was exceptionally ferocious, blasting right through the defensive barrier array in a single strike. Even as a Heavenly General, Tian Shou did not dare take it head-on, dodging nimbly to the side.

The lightning slammed directly into the spot where Tian Shou had just stood, violently tearing open a rift in the fabric of space itself. Splatters of purple-gold electric sparks showered outward, splashing onto Tian Shou's cloak.

Yet although Tian Shou was arrogant, he possessed centuries of seasoned combat instincts after all, remaining sharp and alert in critical moments.

He ripped off his cloak with a brutal tug and hurled it away, watching as the fabric was utterly annihilated amidst the crackling electric glow.

—"Where the hell did such wicked power pop up from?!"-Tian Shou roared even louder.-"Fuck your whole damn lineage!"

Amidst the falling thunder and his superior's raging fury, the strategist scrambled several paces back in utter disarray, still unable to formulate a response. He could only grumble desperately in the privacy of his own heart:

[It wasn't me who called down this lightning! If you want to fuck someone, go fuck him!]

[Besides, he's much prettier!]

[Or is it because he looks too much like Lord Tian Yan that you don't dare to fuck... wait?!]

The strategist froze, casting another glance toward Qu Qianxing's direction.

Relying on the transcendent visual acuity of a True Immortal, he could clearly discern Qu Qianxing's face. Only now did he notice that the Demon Sovereign's facial features truly bore an uncanny, inexplicable resemblance to Lord Tian Yan! It was merely because their temperaments were entirely different that no one had noticed it before—could it be, just as General Tian said, that this Demon Sovereign had truly descended from the Heavenly Court?

As the strategist pondered this, he couldn't resist staring at the Demon Sovereign for a few extra moments—only to see the Demon Sovereign lift his eyes, looking straight back at him.

Indeed: since he could see the Demon Sovereign, the Demon Sovereign could naturally see him with equal clarity.

The strategist watched as that exquisitely gorgeous face curled into a chilling, bloodcurdling smile, and the lightning pounded down from the heavens with even more frenzied fury!

Fortunately, owing to the previous "oversight," the celestial army's array masters dared not allow their commanding general to be exposed to the lightning a second time. Even if it meant leaving far more ordinary celestial soldiers exposed beneath that terrifying tempest, they decisively contracted the defensive array's perimeter to fortify its structural integrity.

Thanks to this desperate measure, the barrier barely managed to withstand the raging bombardment.

The strategist thus escaped with his life.

Yet compared to that breathtaking but terrifying Demon Sovereign, the truly perilous crisis facing the strategist right now was the Heavenly General standing right beside him.

Simply because he had not immediately answered the hysterical general, an iron-like hand reached out the very next second, clamping viciously onto his skull!

—"I'm asking you a question! Didn't you hear me?!"-Tian Shou bellowed.

The strategist harbored zero doubt that if he failed to provide a satisfying answer, his skull would be crushed into paste in the very next instant—this Heavenly King had certainly done similar things in the past.

Yet he genuinely knew nothing about that Demon Sovereign!

Let alone the fact that this might involve classified secrets of the Heavenly Court—though he didn't believe the frontline turmoil had anything to do with the change of celestial leadership, since General Tian insisted on it, then so be it!

The strategist's brain spun at breakneck speed. Though he still knew nothing, with his meager life on the line, he unhesitatingly threw his superior under the bus:

—"Lord, Lord Tian Yan..."-the strategist stammered.

—"Huh?"-Tian Shou grunted.

—"Look closely at that Demon Sovereign's facial features. Don't you think he bears a slight resemblance to Lord Tian Yan?"-the strategist merely stated the objective fact he had just noticed, yet he put on an air of 'I know a great deal, but this is as much as I am permitted to say,' attempting to shift the blame onto his superior—behaving precisely like his colleagues back in the Ministry of Celestial Destiny.

—"Resembles Tian Yan...?"-Tian Shou muttered.

Sure enough, Tian Shou bought the bait. He finally released his iron grip on the strategist's head, narrowing his eyes to scrutinize Qu Qianxing's direction.

That single glance triggered a catastrophic reaction. For powerhouses of immense magical might, merely uttering an opponent's true name could kill, let alone making direct visual contact. Furthermore, unlike his strategist, Tian Shou was an overt commanding general of the celestial host—a target of sufficient status for Qu Qianxing to commit substantial power to assassinate.

Thus, the exact heartbeat their gazes locked, a purple-gold orb of ball lightning detonated right before Tian Shou, engulfing him entirely in dazzling, blinding radiance!

[—"Lord Heavenly King—!"](# "Speaker: Strategist and Guards")

The strategist and the surrounding guards shrieked in horror, yet not a single soul dared step forward.

They knew with crystal clarity that brushing against even a tiny sliver of that wicked lightning was sufficient to swallow them whole.

Moreover, Tian Shou did not require their assistance in the first place.

As one of the highest-ranking active generals of the Heavenly Court, if Tian Shou were so easily slain, the Heavenly Court would have been overthrown countless times over long before today.

Therefore, although the ball lightning appeared monumental in scale—seemingly capable of vaporizing anything it touched into flying ash—after engulfing Tian Shou, it appeared to grind to a halt right there. It neither detonated outward nor dissipated, merely emitting futile static *sizzles* like a fixed celestial ornament.

Witnessing this, the strategist barked in panic at the array masters:

—"Reinforce the defensive array!"-the strategist commanded.

[—"Yes, sir!"](# "Speaker: Celestial Array Masters")

The array masters replied with ashen faces, obeying the order to bolster the defensive barrier once more.

Only, this time, the barrier was not oriented outward—it was directed inward.

Sparing no expense and overdrawing their spiritual energy amidst the falling lightning, the array masters fortified the barrier facing toward their own commanding general!

This was undeniably the correct decision. For the moment their defensive barrier was strengthened, using the ball lightning as its epicenter, the entire surrounding space—and as they were currently positioned high in the sky, this 'space' encompassed hundreds of li of the firmament—violently shuddered, erupting into a catastrophic roar akin to the shattering of dimensions:

*BOOM—!*

The sound was so colossal it completely overpowered the relentless thunder booming across the heavens.

A beam of golden radiance blossomed outward from the core of the ball lightning. In the same breath that it pulverized the lightning orb, it shattered the sea of clouds beneath the celestial generals' feet. Had the defensive array not been reinforced, even the celestial soldiers and array masters would have been shredded into dust.

And the source of that blinding light was Tian Shou himself.

He had expanded more than five times in size, adopting the colossal stature of a Giant Spirit God. His skin had transformed into an impenetrable, lustrous gold, thick and unyielding like a towering statue of invulnerable metal, such that the remaining purple electric sparks could only flicker uselessly across his surface before guttering out. He truly lived up to his title as the Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King.

Yet this Heavenly General had not emerged entirely unscathed.

Leaving aside the fact that he had been forced to reveal his 'true form,' the opulent golden armor adorning his body had been shredded to pieces within the ball lightning, leaving his metallic musculature exposed to the open air—to say nothing of the invisible internal trauma.

—"*Krk...*"-Tian Shou opened his mouth, exhaling a plume of acrid black smoke.

Seeing this spectacle, every celestial soldier present couldn't help retreating several paces.

Although duty dictated they ought to step forward and express concern for their commander, doing so at this moment looked like a surefire way to have his fury vented upon them.

In truth, even without anyone stepping forward, Tian Shou was already incandescent with rage.

—"Brat! You dare lay hands on this father?!"-Tian Shou roared, his fierce gaze sweeping across the subordinate celestial generals under his command:—"What are you all still standing around in a daze for?! Hurry up and annihilate those demons!"

In his mouth, the grand war between immortals and demons sounded no different from a vulgar street brawl.

In truth, it was barely different.

While the low-tier demons on the opposing side lived in the morning and died by evening, the Heavenly Court's official army [quotas](# "Literal CN: official establishment/quota (编制) — coveted permanent government civil service tenure in China") were strictly limited. Furthermore, the Heavenly Court was loath to squander its genuine, tenured celestial troops in the bottomless pit of the frontline. Hence, they drafted cohorts of newly ascended cultivators to serve as [line-filler babies](# "Literal CN: line-filler babies (填线宝宝) — gaming/military internet slang for low-cost, disposable cannon fodder drafted merely to hold a trench or battle line"), dangling the carrot of celestial civil service tenure before them to keep them fighting.

Naturally, possessing neither tenure nor proper stipends, these line-fillers were celestial soldiers in name only. Both their training and their gear were whatever they had brought along upon ascension—fighting on self-funded salaries. One could hardly expect them to be disciplined or well-drilled.

Thanks to this marvelous matchmaking mechanism, the frontline between immortals and demons had perpetually ground on in an endless, inconclusive stalemate.

Of course, as the Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King, Tian Shou possessed regular troops under his command.

Yet those regular troops were precious beyond measure. Losing even one or two would ignite massive public backlash within the Heavenly Court; not even a Heavenly General would deploy them lightly. Furthermore, this battlefield was not meant to be a decisive engagement; a Demon Sovereign had simply emerged out of nowhere to disrupt the established equilibrium, and Tian Shou had merely come down to investigate, harboring no intention of committing his regular legions.

It was just that this Demon Sovereign's specifications were somewhat beyond all expectations.

Tian Shou circulated the immortal power within his vessel, attempting to purge the residual tribulation lightning from his meridians. Within those electric currents, he could faintly detect the presence of divine power—the power of cosmic rules. Fortunately, the opponent was still exceedingly green and the power was not yet fully mature; otherwise, erasing him in a single strike would have posed no difficulty at all.

Realizing this, even Tian Shou lacked confidence in his own army—he was naturally unconcerned about the line-filler babies dying, but feared that these cannon fodder would collapse at first contact just like low-tier demons, failing to pose any meaningful threat.

Thus, Tian Shou mobilized his immortal power, supplementing his orders in a booming voice that rang clear across the entire celestial host:

—"If we emerge victorious in this battle, those who perform outstandingly shall be granted official regular army status!"-Tian Shou proclaimed.

This promise was met with a roar of thunderous cheers, and the charging celestial soldiers instantly fought with far greater fervor than before.

Moreover, right as Tian Shou shattered the ball lightning, the lightning across the heavens thinned considerably. It was indeed an ideal window for a counter-offensive.

Seeing this, the strategist attempted to blend into the main host and slip away:

—"I'll go direct the—"-the strategist began.

—"You, wait!"-Tian Shou barked, his colossal palm clamping onto the strategist's head once more.-"Get in touch with Tian Yan for me!"

He glared fiercely in Qu Qianxing's direction:

—"I want to know just what the hell his background is!"-Tian Shou growled.

The strategist truly felt like bursting into tears on the spot!

—"Lord Demon Sovereign, are you all right?"-on the other side of the battlefield, a certain demon inquired with palpable anxiety.

Qu Qianxing expressionlessly wiped the corner of his lips. The streak of blood bloomed like a brilliant crimson petal, contrasting against his already breathtakingly gorgeous face to make him look all the more stunningly otherworldly.

Yet no matter how mesmerizing that streak of blood appeared, it remained undeniable proof of Qu Qianxing's injury.

Just moments ago, the exact heartbeat Tian Shou shattered the ball lightning, Qu Qianxing had suffered severe spiritual backlash, coughing up a mouthful of blood.

[As expected, was it still pushing too hard?]-Qu Qianxing thought, staring at the crimson stain on his fingertip.

—"You shouldn't have forced it in the first place!"-Ran Deng shouted hysterically directly inside Qu Qianxing's mind, terrified by the mouthful of blood he had just spat.-"Think about what your actual cultivation realm is! What is the proper amount of power you should be channeling? Yet leaving aside the power originally stored within the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array, you channeled all of the new power Master transmitted over! Do you think your physical vessel isn't breaking down fast enough?!"

—"I know..."-Qu Qianxing mumbled.

As the most outstanding among the direct disciples of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Qu Qianxing naturally understood what consequences awaited someone who over-channeled power far exceeding their realm.

—In truth, had it been anyone else, even if granted power vastly eclipsing their cultivation base, they would have been utterly incapable of unleashing such a "full-screen AoE" strike like Qu Qianxing. After all, even if their reserves expanded, the spells most cultivators knew were limited to just a handful; no matter how amplified, there was an insurmountable ceiling, rendering them incapable of rivaling true supreme powerhouses. Only an [honor student who started pre-studying advanced calculus in the second year of middle school](# "Literal CN: 初中二年级 (Grade 8) studying 高等数学 (college-level advanced mathematics) — a classic Chinese academic try-hard / involution trope") like Qu Qianxing could execute wide-area heavenly lightning strikes rivaling those of Master himself.

Unfortunately, while magical arts could jump grades, the mortal flesh could not.

To use a computer as an analogy, a cultivator's physical vessel was akin to hardware. The process of gradually modifying one's mortal body through cultivation was fundamentally equivalent to upgrading the hardware specifications of this human computer to support more demanding software functions—namely, higher-tier spells.

Qu Qianxing could pre-study future curricula, but he was even clearer on the operating capacity of his current hardware specifications. He could even calculate precisely down to the minute and second in what manner his vessel would overload—and suffer fatal system collapse—after casting spells beyond his operational limit.

Qu Qianxing had zero intention of committing suicide.

Hence, his initial lightning strike had merely been a tentative probe—testing the strength and defensive coverage of the celestial soldiers and generals, while serving to intimidate the enemy ranks.

—"However, after actually doing so, I realized my physical body is far stronger than I understood,"-Qu Qianxing spoke to Ran Deng in his heart, actively analyzing his own condition.-"When casting lower-tier spells, I didn't notice it. But when wielding power far beyond my limits, it feels as though something deep within my divine soul is bolstering my physical body, even allowing me to utilize that power efficiently. I never knew..."

Never knew... had he?

Qu Qianxing's movements froze.

He stared blankly at the blood on his fingertips, feeling the searing ache radiating from his internal organs. It was the visceral agony born of overdrawing power and suffering the resultant backlash that damaged his viscera—and a comparable agony was something he had experienced once before.

It was the first time he had slain the "High Immortal" of Carefree Palace who coveted his physical body.

Qu Qianxing had slain him within a certain prison.

It was called a prison, yet it had been decorated with lavish opulence.

Not only was it adorned with carved beams and painted rafters, outfitted with furnishings more magnificent than an emperor's bedchamber, but even the floors and railings were swathed in multiple layers of thick, plush golden down. It looked for all the world like an exquisitely delicate, resplendent birdcage.

Qu Qianxing had been dressed in opulent finery and locked at the very center of that birdcage like a meticulously pampered songbird. Barring his departure, whatever he desired would be delivered before him without delay.

In truth, had Qu Qianxing not displayed such fierce, unrelenting defiance, he wouldn't even have needed to stay inside this golden cage; he would have enjoyed a measure of "bounded freedom."

For everyone within the mortal empire knew that this breathtakingly beautiful youth had caught the eye of a "High Immortal" and was about to be escorted to his immortal grotto. Toward such [immortal fortune](# "Literal CN: 仙缘 — fated encounter or blessing bestowed by an immortal"), there was not a single mortal who did not harbor burning envy or outright jealousy.

Yet Qu Qianxing himself felt only humiliation.

Abject, suffocating humiliation.

At that time, Qu Qianxing did not even know the true nature of this "High Immortal," nor did he understand that the entity merely coveted his stunning mortal husk. Yet whether the opponent was a genuine deity or some higher-order existence, simply because that being had spoken, Qu Qianxing was packaged like an object to be gifted away. To refuse was branded as "not knowing what was good for him." He was betrayed by the very common folk he had wished to protect, badgered by friends and kin, and locked within this gilded cage by his own mentors.

Qu Qianxing could not help but feel profound disappointment—disappointment in the empire he had wished to serve and in his own kinsmen.

Yet beyond disappointment, Qu Qianxing remained utterly unwilling to bow to fate, and especially unwilling to bow to the destiny of being traded away like chattel. Thus, under the pretext of wishing to learn about immortals, he requested relevant archives and scrolls from the imperial family.

Believing Qu Qianxing had finally submitted, the mortals were overjoyed. The imperial court genuinely delivered vast troves of texts concerning immortals.

It was only then that Qu Qianxing discovered the truth: the great empire he had believed to be the grandest domain in the world was, in the eyes of cultivators, nothing more than an oversized anthill. In fact, from its very founding, the empire had been a puppet of the cultivation world. Cultivators had deliberately selected mortals utterly incapable of cultivating the Dao to serve as emperors, ensuring that the imperial lineage would remain eternally subservient to immortal factions merely to maintain their secular throne. For them to trade away a subject like him in exchange for their own security was entirely natural.

Such imperial archives naturally contained no methods to combat cultivators.

If the righteous path was barred, then only crooked and sinister methods remained.

Books concerning immortals inevitably mentioned the wicked fiends, monsters, and ghosts vanquished by cultivators, along with their forbidden arts. Naturally, in imperial records, such sinister rites were fragmented and incomplete. Had an ordinary mortal read them, they would never have been able to reconstruct them, let alone cast them.

Yet was Qu Qianxing's aptitude for learning comparable to that of an ordinary mortal?

Prior to this, he had already independently deduced and self-studied basic immortal arts. Cross-referencing them against these fragmentary records, Qu Qianxing actually reverse-engineered several forbidden arts! Among them, he selected an archaic ritual that burned his own flesh and blood as an offering to amplify his power and summon an ancient deity.

To this, that "High Immortal" had curled his lips in a sneer of utter contempt:

—"*Pfft.* A mere mortal—do you honestly believe a little trick you cobbled together at the last minute can contend against me?"-the "High Immortal" had sneered.

—"How would we know without trying?"-Qu Qianxing sneered coldly, and ignited his own flesh and blood.

Amidst the excruciating agony of overdrawing his mortal flesh and divine soul to their absolute limits, Qu Qianxing seized a future for himself—and heard the voice that redeemed his entire existence:

—"...Is it you who is calling for help?"-a voice inquired.

Fortunately, it was an "illusion"; had these demons and celestial legions descended upon the real mortal realm, the catastrophic devastation unleashed would be beyond Qu Qianxing's comprehension.

Moreover, Qu Qianxing's true physical vessel was already exposed on the frontline of the war. He dared not wager that the mastermind lurking behind the scenes hadn't noticed him. If his action of leaving the mystic realm to return home was witnessed by the enemy, it might very well expose the sect's genuine coordinates.

This was precisely why Qu Qianxing had risked overdrawing his power in his true physical form.

Though home was mere inches away, he dared not return to the Heavenly Spirit Sect to re-enter this illusion via projection for his "experiments."

Now that they had been inexplicably dragged to this unfamiliar domain, these demons—who possessed zero organization or discipline to begin with—doubtless wanted to retreat all the more, didn't they?

The problem was that this illusion sat right at his sect's doorstep; Qu Qianxing had no intention of letting them run amok.

Furthermore, with celestial soldiers charging toward them and his desire to probe the limits of his own power, he could not allow the demon ranks to fall into chaos.

—"How could that be?!"-to his surprise, those demons responded with impassioned fervor:—"We shall follow Lord Demon Sovereign to fight the celestial soldiers to the death!"

—"Fight to the death!"-other demons roared at the top of their lungs.

Qu Qianxing cast an astonished glance at them, hardly believing these creatures were such loyal, obedient subjects.

Yet noticing the awe and greed burning within their eyes, Qu Qianxing realized that his earlier display of heavenly lightning had intimidated the demons just as effectively as it had terrorized the celestial soldiers.

Although that brief exchange had concluded with Qu Qianxing coughing up a mouthful of blood, hadn't the Heaven-Protecting Heavenly King on the opposing side suffered a setback as well?

The demons had clearly gained substantial confidence in victory.

Qu Qianxing did not care in the slightest about the demons' loyalty, but he certainly wouldn't pass up an exquisite opportunity to exploit these cannon fodder:

—"Very good! Then charge!"-Qu Qianxing commanded.-[Best if you all perish together with the celestial soldiers!]

—"Yes, Lord Demon Sovereign!"-the demons roared with menacing fury.

And possessing far greater momentum was the furious shriek ringing inside Qu Qianxing's mind, nearly piercing straight through his brain tissue:

—"Qu Qianxing!"-Ran Deng shrieked in his mind.

Ran Deng clearly did not want Qu Qianxing acting recklessly.

She felt that both of her senior brothers were impossibly exhausting to manage; between the two of them, she—the junior sister—felt more like their exasperated mother.

Unfortunately, Eldest Senior Brother never listened to Ran Deng, and neither would Second Senior Brother.

—"Ran Deng, stop screeching; my head aches,"-Qu Qianxing said coldly.-"Rather than watching the spectacle here, you should be scrutinizing the spatial vectors much more closely. To have transferred us here, that 'mastermind behind the scenes' is bound to have left lingering traces of power. This is our prime opportunity to drag him out into the light."

—"You, you, you—!"-Ran Deng was so infuriated she could barely speak.
