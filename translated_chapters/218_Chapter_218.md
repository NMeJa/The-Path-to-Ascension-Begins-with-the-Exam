# Chapter 218: Yuwen Xuan

The "promising" Xia Bing was, in fact, enduring immense agony.

She admitted she was taking a massive gamble.

She was gambling that Yuwen Xuan fought in reality exactly as he did in the Trial System; gambling that even with a full realm difference, Yuwen Xuan could effectively grind away Yan Bailang's spiritual energy; gambling that this duel would push Yan Bailang into absolute exhaustion.

Or rather, if Xia Bing had to predict whether any match throughout the entire Immortal Arts Assembly could cause Yan Bailang to carelessly drain his spiritual reserves—allowing her to utilize the True Dragon bone whistle to subjugate him—it was exclusively this "exhibition match."

It wasn't that the other combatants were weak. Rather, when it came to matches in the Upper Three Realms, even though Immortal Master Fujuan had striven to construct optimal combat stages for them, the high-tier powerhouses still maintained their dignity, adhering primarily to "stopping upon a decisive strike." Barring blood feuds, they rarely fought to the bitter end, making Upper Three Realms duels ironically less thrilling than lower-realm clashes. Xia Bing never expected Yan Bailang to exhaust himself in conventional tournament bouts.

Yuwen Xuan's combat style, however, was exceptionally idiosyncratic. Coupled with the fact that the assembly had just commenced, and Yan Bailang disdained using the Trial System—possessing zero understanding of tournament rules or his opponent—there was a high probability the grandmaster would be caught off guard, squandering all his reserves against Yuwen Xuan and exposing a fatal vulnerability.

Xia Bing believed that even if the elders had laid some treacherous trap for her, should the Sect Master genuinely fall into an irrecoverable disadvantage, they would never pass up an opportunity to seize power. When that moment came, she could [reap the fisherman's spoils](# "Idiom: 坐收渔翁之利 — profiting while two adversaries exhaust each other").

Naturally, that was the ideal scenario.

In the mortal world, affairs rarely unfolded according to one's desires. Xia Bing never assumed everything would proceed smoothly, preparing contingency plans for various catastrophes. Yet no matter how she weighed the scales, this exhibition bout appeared to be her single best opportunity.

And initially, the match indeed progressed according to Xia Bing's projections.

Though it appeared to be a dreadfully dull, stationary shootout, Xia Bing could discern that Yan Bailang wasn't refusing to overpower his junior; rather, shortly after the duel began, he had fallen into some bizarre trap, leaving both him and his green-faced flood dragon in an erratic state that forced them to expend several times—even tens of times—more power just to sustain a standard offensive output.

Had his opponent been an equal or slightly lower-tier cultivator, Yan Bailang would never have dragged out the contest. He would have graciously conceded long ago, claiming that disarming traps wasn't his forte. In truth, because beast tamers relied on traps to capture spiritual beasts and assist them in combat, they were the most proficient profession at utilizing terrain advantages; an intelligent beast tamer would never wage a war of attrition against an unknown foe amidst a known trap.

Yet precisely because Yuwen Xuan was a junior who had only recently advanced to the Void Refinement stage, conceding the moment adversity struck was far too humiliating on every conceivable level. Thus, Yan Bailang ground on and on, failing to find a face-saving exit.

Had the bout continued for another fifteen minutes, Yan Bailang would have completely depleted his spiritual reserves.

In fact, several elders and Xia Bing had originally been seated in the assistant gallery adjacent to the ring. Seeing Yan Bailang flounder, the elders had grown restless, with Bai Jun explicitly gesturing for Xia Bing to blow the bone whistle and ["strike while he was sick to take his life."](# "Idiom: 趁他病要他命 — ruthlessly striking an enemy while they are weak or ill")

Naturally, Xia Bing wasn't foolish enough to sabotage her Sect Master under the watchful eyes of thousands. Claiming she had left the whistle in the athlete lounge, she excused herself to evade them, only for the elders to shadow her, issuing increasingly brazen demands for her to strike.

And right at that moment, Yan Bailang forfeited!

Whether he had caught wind of something or not, he charged into the private lounge, lashing out with violent attacks without uttering a single word.

Caught off guard, Xia Bing had been sent crashing across the room by the flood dragon's tail, and the bone whistle had rolled from her grasp.

Judging by the speed and crushing momentum of the dragon's strike, Yan Bailang's spiritual energy was nowhere near depleted.

[Ah, the absolute worst-case scenario!]

Xia Bing realized bitterly.

Yet having drawn the arrow to the bowstring, she had no choice but to loose it.

Considering this was the Heavenly Spirit Sect's tournament grounds, they surely wouldn't stand idly by and watch a competitor get butchered in cold blood!

As long as she firmly claimed the moral high ground—and the truth was genuinely justifiable—even if she was branded a traitor to her sect after losing, she wouldn't be completely hounded out of the cultivation world.

This calculation flashed through Xia Bing's mind in a fraction of an instant.

—"S-Sect Master, you have a guilty conscience! Was it not you and the elders who conspired together to murder the previous Sect Master first?!"-Xia Bing cried out in thunderous accusation.

Struggling backward, she pressed her back against Elder Bai Jun:

—"Elder Bai has already revealed everything to me! We only wished to avenge Ancestor Yu Si, our previous Sect Master!"

—"...Avenge?"-Yan Bailang froze. He twisted his neck stiffly, glaring with unfocused, bloodshot eyes at Bai Jun and Xia Bing before bursting into wild, manic laughter:—"Hahaha! Avenge?! Was it not all of you who goaded me into slaughtering my senior sister in the first place?! Today, you merely wish to replay the exact same trick!"

[Ah, he took the bait!]

Xia Bing noted inwardly.

She knew that the athlete lounges of the Immortal Arts Assembly were monitored by observational formations. Even if what transpired here could not be broadcast in real time, the footage would be reconstructed afterward. Ignorant of this, Yan Bailang had cheerfully confessed to his crimes!

Patricide and murdering one's own master were absolute taboos across the cultivation world. Xia Bing refused to believe that after witnessing this indisputable "evidence of guilt," the righteous sects would still return her to Yan Bailang's clutches!

Regrettably, the cunning old foxes among the elders were not so easily outmaneuvered.

With a sweep of his broad sleeve, Bai Jun hurled Xia Bing straight out from behind him, tossing her directly before Yan Bailang's boots:

—"Utter nonsense! Complete slander! This is solely this wretched girl's treasonous conspiracy! I only pursued her upon noticing her suspicious behavior, discovering that she had stolen my bone whistle in an attempt to orchestrate an assassination!"

—"*Ugh...*"-Xia Bing vomited a mouthful of fresh blood.

What was truly disastrous was that the layers of defensive talismans and artifacts she had meticulously equipped had already suffered a third of their integrity during Yan Bailang's initial tail strike. Now, with Bai Jun's vicious toss, another quarter shattered into dust.

It couldn't be helped; the chasm in cultivation was far too astronomical.

Before a Body Integration grandmaster, she was no different from an insect. No matter how sturdy the chitin shell, it took merely a single stomping boot to crush it to paste.

Bai Jun hadn't executed her directly just now; he had simply thrown her to Yan Bailang to serve as a scapegoat to vent the madman's wrath!

If this continued, before the Heavenly Spirit Sect could intervene, she would already be a corpse.

[Speaking of which, when will the Heavenly Spirit Sect take notice of what is happening here?]

Xia Bing wondered frantically.

***

—"Wait a moment."

—"Eldest Senior Brother?"

—"Activate the Grade-A Defensive Barrier, disperse the spectator stands, and only then proceed inside,"-the avatar of Yuwen Xuan instructed.

The exhibition duel had been agonizingly dull; the sole unexpected twist was Yan Bailang's sudden forfeiture. Consequently, post-match surveillance cameras were locked entirely onto Yan Bailang—fully automated lenses driven by spiritual energy—broadcasting the turmoil within the private lounge in live, unfiltered clarity.

The perimeter security guards and tournament staff had naturally witnessed the internal feud erupting within the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall. Just as Xia Bing had calculated, though the spectacle was juicy gossip, they could not permit a murder to stain the Immortal Arts Assembly. As gracious hosts, they had a duty to mediate, prompting the security detachment to fly swiftly toward the lounge.

Yet before they could approach the doorway, Yuwen Xuan called them to a halt.

Adopting an expression of profound, magnanimous concern for public welfare, Yuwen Xuan explained:

—"Those inside are Body Integration grandmasters, and the dispute touches upon their sect's deepest domestic privacy. It is best to exercise extreme caution."

—"Understood..."-the guard captain hesitated.

While the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples possessed modest personal cultivations, within the perimeter of the Grand Mountain-Protecting Array, they held absolute administrative authority. They could briefly suppress even a Mahayana powerhouse until Immortal Master Fujuan arrived to resolve the crisis. Handling a few Body Integration elders, though demanding, was well within their capacity to issue a stern reprimand.

Surely the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall would wish to avoid airing their domestic scandals before the realm?

Yuwen Xuan continued smoothly:

—"Having just crossed hands with Yan Bailang, I perceived that his mental state is severely compromised. If we barge in recklessly and trigger an escalation, innocent spectators in the vicinity might suffer collateral harm."

Besides, if the constables arrived at the very beginning, how could the melodrama unfold? That was how mortal storybooks were written, after all.

The avatar of Yuwen Xuan reasoned serenely. With his own eyes, he watched as his imperceptible true body—accompanied by Yan Bailang's Heart Demon—slipped quietly into the private lounge.

The poor security disciples detected none of this. They held zero reason to question their Eldest Senior Brother.

—"Eldest Senior Brother is truly far-sighted."

—"Yet Fairy Xia appears to be in dire peril..."-another guard murmured with lingering concern.

Glancing at the broadcast projection screen, Yuwen Xuan smiled thinly:

—"Rest assured. That maiden is far stronger than you or I imagine."

As if providing immediate confirmation to his words, a piercing, screeching whistle detonated from within the private lounge.

***

Xia Bing, naturally, would never entrust her survival to outside rescue.

Even if rational logic suggested the Heavenly Spirit Sect would not allow competitors or spectators to perish on their grounds, she knew the world was plagued by endless variables. She would never wager her life on a mere "perhaps"!

Therefore, the instant Bai Jun hurled her before Yan Bailang, while countless thoughts raced through her mind, Xia Bing did not plead, did not explain, and did not even pause to wipe the blood from her lips. Instead, guided by near-instinctive muscle memory drilled through thousands of rehearsals, she formed a lightning-fast hand seal.

In tandem with her seal, the bone whistle clutched in Yan Bailang's palm let out a shrill, deafening shriek!

The sound caused everyone inside the room to recoil in shock. Yan Bailang's murderous aura was cut clean in half; in frantic panic, he nearly flung the whistle away, only to catch himself and clamp down on it with white knuckles.

—"Back then, Yan Bailang used this exact bone whistle to assassinate the previous Sect Master! Such an unspeakable secret, such an heirloom whistle—how could I possibly know of it or possess it if the elders hadn't delivered it into my hands?! And now you seek to abandon me?!"

Xia Bing shouted her scathing condemnation, seizing the momentary chaos to execute a tactical roll into an obscure corner of the room that was difficult to target.

Hearing this accusation, Yan Bailang glared with bloodshot, bulging eyes at the gathered elders.

—"Sect Master, do not listen to this wretched girl's sowing of discord!"-one elder hurriedly disclaimed. Yet their gazes lingered intently upon the vibrating bone whistle, subconsciously calculating the weapon's psychological suppression over Yan Bailang.

That whistle was by no means an empty bluff. Accompanying the note, a suffocating miasmic haze tainted with the pungent, brackish stench of ancient ocean water swept silently through the chamber, inundating the entire room like an imperceptibly rising tidal wave.

—"This is... Beast Miasma?!"

—"You are controlling Beast Miasma?! How are you doing this—no! How *could* you possibly do this?!"

—"After escaping the Beast Soul Tower back then, that senior sister... that wretched woman claimed it would never be needed again, deliberately refusing to transcribe this divine technique into the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*! Even I never mastered it! Where did you learn this?!"

Yan Bailang's eyes bulged with raw terror and fury as he bellowed at Xia Bing.

[But this is a technique recorded right inside the Ten Thousand Beasts Manual!]

Xia Bing noted inwardly. Only now did she realize that the manual she had obtained was vastly more authentic and complete than the corrupted text possessed by the Sect Master.

—"Naturally. Even if it were useless, how would she dare hand me a flawed knockoff?"-Yuwen Xuan gloated softly.

Having slipped into the room alongside the Heart Demon, he remained completely concealed, squatting comfortably in a corner to enjoy the chaotic performance with relish.

The Heart Demon, by contrast, desperately wished for them to stop brawling.

Countless spectators outside were watching this! The Ten Thousand Beasts Hall's reputation was thoroughly ruined today!

Regrettably, even if she intervened, nobody would heed her words.

Besides, seeing a young girl driven to such desperate extremes, did such a decrepit sect genuinely deserve to endure?

The Heart Demon pondered with complicated sorrow, suddenly spotting something peculiar:

—"What she is performing seems somewhat different from the original technique, isn't it? That art was created specifically to subdue ferocious beast souls within the tower—by channeling the residual breath of the souls, namely 'Beast Miasma,' it purges the miasma to soothe their wrath. Back then, I... Ancestor Yu Si only cleared the Beast Soul Tower trial by pacifying those beasts. Yet now, the miasma isn't dissipating; it's multiplying exponentially!"

—"..."-Yuwen Xuan cast a deep, contemplative glance at the Heart Demon.

—"She adapted the technique,"-he explained, displaying a rare trace of concern for the operational state of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall (his private cafeteria).—"That girl never encountered the primordial beasts of the Beast Soul Tower (since they were eaten by me). Facing ordinary beast souls, pacifying them through opportunistic tricks is pointless; what matters to her is maximizing her raw combat power. Furthermore, she is an insect tamer. Insects possess negligible intellect, so inverting the art to amplify the miasma and ignite the insects' frenzied rage is vastly more beneficial for her combat style."

—"I see... She improved the technique!"-the Heart Demon's eyes sparkled with genuine pride and warmth.

Did mentors inevitably favor disciples of this caliber?

—"Hmph!"

Yuwen Xuan gave a cold snort, turning his eyes back to Xia Bing.

Xia Bing was dedicating every shred of her concentration to manipulating the art.

Just as Yuwen Xuan had deduced, the technique she was invoking was an inverted adaptation of the "Disperse Miasma Art" from the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*. Instead of calming spirits, it excited the miasma released by beast souls, driving her insect swarm (and spiritual pets) into a berserk battle frenzy.

This time, however, what Xia Bing commanded was not an insect swarm, but "Dragon Miasma."

From the moment Bai Jun mentioned that the bone whistle was fashioned from True Dragon remains, Xia Bing had conceived this application. In her subsequent private tests, she confirmed that a True Dragon was indeed a True Dragon; even a small segment of tail vertebra, severed from a soul long extinguished for millennia, could produce terrifying plumes of miasma if handled correctly. No wonder this whistle could dominate all living creatures!

Because she was manipulating the miasma rather than the whistle itself, whether the artifact rested in her palm was entirely inconsequential.

What was more, Xia Bing had made meticulous preparations in this lounge beforehand!

She had deliberately planted subtle artifacts designed to dampen sound, suppress dragon aura, and blunt the whistle's resonance—which, in truth, served to diffuse the miasma throughout the chamber. Given Yan Bailang's habitual paranoia, he had hesitated when inspecting the room's fixtures and left them untouched, allowing them to function with ruthless efficacy now.

Under Xia Bing's delicate steering, within moments, the miasma inside the lounge grew so dense it mirrored the ocean trenches. The crushing atmospheric pressure and toxic density were unbearable to mortal senses. Even Xia Bing herself had swallowed specialized antidote pills beforehand just to endure the atmosphere, while Yan Bailang and the elders could only resist through sheer spiritual grit.

Yet at this moment, no one had attention to spare for Xia Bing.

—"Did you think... this was enough to kill me?!"

*Huuu—*

Yan Bailang had begun by roaring at Xia Bing, but as the miasma thickened, the bone whistle in his grip began whistling spontaneously once more.

This sound, however, was not the shrill piercing screech of a blown whistle. Rather, it resembled wind rushing through hollow chambers—a rhythmic, resonant breath... No, something was literally *breathing*!

Yan Bailang felt the bone whistle in his fingers awaken into life. Not only was it drawing breath, but he could distinctly feel the rhythmic pulsing of a heartbeat beneath the bone!

Yan Bailang nearly hurled the whistle across the room.

He had always dreaded and mistrusted this object; now, his revulsion spiked beyond all limits.

Yet facing the elders and Xia Bing, reason held his arm rigid. If the whistle was this terrifying in his own hands, he dared not imagine the apocalyptic consequences if it fell into someone else's grasp!

For a frozen moment, Yan Bailang remained paralyzed.

He resembled an amateur beast tamer gripping a lethal viper by the neck—his palms coated in an unnaturally slick, repulsive slime, every nerve screaming at him to cast the venomous horror away, while intellect warned that dropping it invited instant, fatal retribution.

Under this suffocating tension and the washing tide of dragon miasma, his demonic corruption actually subsided by several degrees.

Yet half of his previous unhinged ferocity had been fueled by those demonic imprints; with their influence blunted, his aura noticeably waned.

Sensing this, the elders exchanged furtive glances:

—"Sect Master, are you unharmed?"

—"Despicable! For this girl to commit such insubordination is an outrage against heaven!"

—"That whistle possesses a foul curse! Sect Master, surrender it to this old man!"

Feigning deep fraternal concern, the elders closed in around Yan Bailang. While their mouths spouted self-righteous condemnations of Xia Bing, not a single one spare a glance for the culprit herself. Their predatory ambition and treachery were laid bare for all to see.

Just as Xia Bing had anticipated: grant these elders an opening, and they would never let Yan Bailang walk away alive.

If Xia Bing understood this, Yan Bailang grasped it tenfold.

—"Stay back! All of you, stay back!"-Yan Bailang snarled, commanding his green-faced flood dragon to coil protectively before him.—"Do not think that by manipulating that wretched girl, you can replay your ancient coup—"

The elders naturally disregarded his ramblings.

Sneering inwardly, they offered hollow platitudes:

—"Sect Master, what are you saying? Was it not you who begged for our aid to seize the sect leadership back then? How has this become our burden?"

—"Indeed. We have supported you steadfastly throughout the centuries, yet you repay us with baseless suspicion. Is this not excessive?"

—"Hand the whistle to us, and we may yet discuss terms—?!"

Just as their boots stepped within striking distance of Yan Bailang, they froze solid.

Or rather, they were ensnared by the miasma.

The bone whistle let out another piercing screech, and the dragon miasma that had previously coiled around Yan Bailang suddenly surged like a wrathful serpent toward the elders, wrapping violently around their limbs!

The elders were caught completely unprepared—no, worse.

Because the miasma had already flooded the chamber like deep sea water, the elders had been burning their spiritual reserves merely to withstand the ambient toxicity. Believing Xia Bing's wrath was trained exclusively on Yan Bailang, they had willingly endured the discomfort to witness his demise. Relaxing their vigilance, they suddenly found themselves the primary targets—frogs boiled in warm water, now utterly incapable of mobilizing their spiritual energy!

Right at that split second, Xia Bing's sharp voice rang out:

—"Now!"

—"Fool! You are targeting the wrong people!"-Bai Jun roared, glaring furiously toward the corner.

Then, his heart plunged into absolute ice.

The maiden lay crouched against the floorboards, her impassive features illuminated by eyes that burned with terrifying, brilliant resolve:

—"I am not mistaken. It was always you."

—"When you approached me, I wondered: even if I succeed, will I merely become the next puppet Sect Master?"

—"If the queen bee chosen for the colony is diseased from the very inception, every generation of brood born thereafter will be defective!"

—"As long as you parasites remain, the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall will never heal!"

In the cultivation world, this was doubly true.

Setting aside disparities in martial might, by lifespan alone, a high-tier cultivator outlived generations of mortal disciples. A sect governed by cultivators whose moral compass was rotten became a living, inescapable purgatory.

The moment Xia Bing learned the truth, she understood:

Slaughtering Yan Bailang alone was useless. Unless these decrepit monsters were purged down to the root, the past, the present, and the future would remain forever damned.

Therefore, from the very beginning, Xia Bing's strategy had never contemplated collaborating with the elders. She had marked them as prime prey, specifically luring their core leaders into this confined deathtrap.

Though variables had complicated the execution, the operation had finally been dragged back onto its intended track!

Xia Bing shouted:

—"Sect Master, strike now!"

Yan Bailang finally understood.

Earlier, he had never imagined Xia Bing was plotting against the elders, failing to seize the initial window (Xia Bing: *utterly incompetent, useless, and brainless*). Only when Xia Bing openly condemned them did the truth click into place.

—"Hahaha! Worthy of being my chosen heir!"-Yan Bailang bellowed in manic glee.

At this juncture, he didn't mind offering Xia Bing empty promises; after all, he still needed her to channel the dragon miasma! Even so, he dared not surrender the whistle, instead commanding his green-faced flood dragon to lunge without hesitation at the weakest elder—opening with an unreserved killing blow!

The elder was sent crashing through the air, painting the floorboards with a spray of crimson blood.

—"Yan Bailang, you dare?!"-Bai Jun shouted in fury.

The remaining elders were seasoned combatants; frantically, they attempted to summon their personal spiritual beasts to repel the assault.

To their horror, Yan Bailang's offensive was exponentially swifter and more savage than they had ever witnessed. Every strike was aimed ruthlessly at their defensive weaknesses, deploying specialized Dharma artifacts explicitly tailored to neutralize their techniques.

It looked as though he and Xia Bing had rehearsed this assassination for decades!

Naturally, that was an impossibility.

Yet the preparation was undeniably genuine.

When drafting her plans, Xia Bing knew with absolute certainty that compared to disposable disciples like herself, Yan Bailang considered these elders his true existential threat!

Though they had helped him usurp the throne, they held his patricidal crime over his head, occupied key administrative nodes, eroded his sovereign authority, commanded power equal to his own, and possessed the very bone whistle capable of suppressing or executing him!

One could say that more than half of Yan Bailang's "cultivation breakthrough anxiety" stemmed entirely from these elders.

Consequently, what Yan Bailang spent centuries plotting in secret was precisely how to butcher these elders down to the last man.

Given an opening, he would strike at them with ravenous brutality.

Unlike the ancient schemers who refused to dirty their hands and relied on disposable pawns, Yan Bailang's psychological resilience was thoroughly unhinged—when backed into a corner, he personally threw himself into the slaughter!

Especially now.

Plagued by the paranoid delusion that *"treasonous subjects are constantly plotting my execution,"* compounded by the lingering demonic imprints from the arena, his mind hovered on the precipice of madness. With Xia Bing handing him his lifelong dream on a silver platter, Yan Bailang could no longer contain his volcanic fury:

—"Heh, why would I not dare?!"

—"I have wanted to butcher you ancient scourges for centuries!"

—"You were the ones who handed me the bone whistle! You were the ones who urged me to commit the crime! You were the ones who reaped the greatest spoils! Why was I the only one whose cultivation bottlenecked?! Why was I the only one tormented by a Heart Demon?! Why only me?!"

—"Why?!"

—"M-Madman!"-Bai Jun barely parried a strike, staggering backward several paces without finding the space to summon his spiritual pet.

This lunatic had genuinely lost all sanity; this combat style was pure mutual destruction!

And when had Yan Bailang become this formidable?!

He was supposed to be a mediocre waste propped up by the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and mountains of sect resources!

Bai Jun thought in agony.

Yet what terrified him even more was Xia Bing.

The longer the melee dragged on, the more the surrounding miasma resembled the crushing abyssal sea, suffocating his lungs and paralyzing his meridians until channeling spiritual energy became an agonizing chore.

The bone whistle had never possessed such an ability!

How could a mere Golden Core junior accomplish such an impossible feat?!

Bai Jun naturally could not fathom that when Ancestor Yu Si authored the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, she had drafted it while facing a Taotie, designing the arts against primordial beasts infused with True Immortal blood. The inherent potency of the technique was monstrous; now refined and adapted by Xia Bing, suppressing a handful of decrepit Body Integration elders was well within its parameters. Had Xia Bing possessed even mid-Void Refinement cultivation, she wouldn't have needed Yan Bailang's blade to subjugate them all.

Bai Jun, however, would never have the chance to learn this.

Clashes between high-tier cultivators were measured either in centuries or in fleeting seconds.

This duel was decidedly the latter.

With Xia Bing preemptively suppressing the elders' mobility, and Yan Bailang unleashing near-demonic murderous frenzy, the elders—who had spent centuries living in bloated luxury—crumbled like dry clay. Within breaths, bodies littered the floor.

After a single desperate parry, Bai Jun found himself the sole survivor.

This was solely because he had stationed himself near the rear to monitor Xia Bing.

Looking up, Bai Jun saw the colossal, cold reptilian pupil of the green-faced flood dragon hovering inches before his nose.

—"S-Stop!"-Bai Jun stammered in mortal dread.

Biting his tongue savagely, a piercing, shrill whistle burst from between his lips!

Aside from the whistle lost during Yu Si's murder and the one gifted to Xia Bing that had fallen into Yan Bailang's hands, this was the third and final True Dragon bone whistle—the ultimate trump card Bai Jun had kept hidden for himself!

Just as Xia Bing sought to let them slaughter each other, the elders had held this final relic to ensure they reaped the ultimate spoils.

Had he not been backed into the absolute brink of death, Bai Jun would never have deployed this treasure.

Instantly, the acoustic vibration manifested into a visible shockwave, slamming squarely into the green-faced flood dragon! Radiating pure True Dragon draconic majesty, the sonic blast not only tore Xia Bing's dragon miasma to shreds, but violently ruptured the flood dragon's primary blood vessels, causing the colossal serpent to collapse to the floorboards like an earthworm stamped on its vitals!

Triumph flared within Bai Jun's eyes. Before he could utter a word of gloating, he saw Yan Bailang, his vision blinded with crimson bloodlust, charging forward without a shred of hesitation!

In this decisive second, the ferocious beast soul fragment Yan Bailang had consumed to counter Yuwen Xuan proved its worth. Even without his spiritual companion, his physical form radiated an overwhelming, suffocating beastly pressure, descending upon Bai Jun like an avalanche!

Refusing to submit to death, Bai Jun blew into the whistle with his dying breath, attempting to crush the madman with draconic majesty—

*BOOOOOOM!*

The collision of two titanic forces unleashed a blinding shockwave that completely engulfed the chamber in incandescent light.

Had this clash occurred anywhere else in the world, obliterating several mountain ranges and a major metropolis would have been effortless.

—"Are they all dead?"-amidst the blinding brilliance, the Heart Demon asked with profound anticipation.

Yuwen Xuan sneered softly:

—"You still exist, so how could he be dead? If you are that eager, drag him into demonic corruption with your own hands!"

The Heart Demon ignored his taunt.

Just as Yuwen Xuan had noted, as the radiance dispersed, though the room was strewn with fallen corpses and Yan Bailang lay battered upon the floor drenched in wounds, the ragged rise and fall of his chest proved he was still breathing.

At that moment, someone struggled to their feet.

It was Xia Bing!

—"She survived?!"-the Heart Demon gasped in disbelief.

Though not a direct participant, for a Golden Core cultivator to be swept into the lethal epicenter of a Body Integration clash—no matter how flawed the combatants—the residual shockwave should have squashed her like a bug.

Yet Xia Bing stood breathing, and was the very first to rise, appearing to be the sole individual possessing lingering vitality.

The Heart Demon stared in shock, before sudden realization dawned upon her. Turning her head, she gazed wide-eyed at Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan gave zero acknowledgment, staring impassively at Xia Bing.

Xia Bing had no idea how she had survived either.

When the two titanic energies collided moments ago, she had steeled herself for death.

Fortunately, she had made worst-case preparations: the authentic *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* had been entrusted to Rong Rong, accompanied by a comprehensive farewell letter. With the corrupt upper echelons wiped out, whether Rong Rong possessed the ambition to assume leadership or chose to depart with trusted peers to found an independent lineage, a brighter future awaited them!

Yet to her astonishment, that apocalyptic force had not touched a hair on her head.

At the critical moment, an impenetrable defensive barrier seemed to have materialized over her form, deflecting every shred of lethal energy.

Standing amidst the smoking ruin, Xia Bing surveyed the carnage in a daze, feeling as though she were walking through a dream:

Had she succeeded?! Had her desperate gamble actually worked?!

Had she succeeded in driving the Sect Master and corrupt elders to slaughter one another to the death?!

Had she succeeded in walking out alive?!

Xia Bing knew intimately that the mathematical probability of her surviving this ordeal was practically zero. Standing here breathing, she could barely comprehend that victory was hers.

Ultimately, the voice that snapped Xia Bing back to harsh reality was Yan Bailang's raspy wheeze:

—"Xia Bing? You... you survived?"-Yan Bailang rasped, glaring at her with a mixture of terror and murderous malice.

Yet in his shattered state, he couldn't even manage to crawl.

Forcing a sickeningly sweet tone, he coaxed:

—"Worthy of being my most treasured disciple, the successor I chose long ago... I knew you would stand by my side."

[Nonsense!]

[Your very first question betrayed your true thoughts!]

[You fully intended to slaughter me moments ago!]

Xia Bing noted with razor-sharp clarity.

Yan Bailang, however, shamelessly pressed on, dangling empty promises:

—"Come, lend me a hand! It appears that in this state, I cannot continue competing and must return to recuperate. When that occurs, you may very well have to serve as Acting Sect Master... What are you doing?!"

Indeed, what was she doing?

Xia Bing drew a gleaming steel blade from her storage pouch—she lacked the spiritual energy to command her insects, leaving physical steel as her sole recourse—and strode steadily toward Yan Bailang.

Reason warned her that she had no need to strike. The wisest move was to turn on her heel and exit the chamber immediately.

After all, whether outsiders had detected the commotion earlier, such an apocalyptic explosion had surely alerted the entire tournament venue by now.

The cultivation world placed paramount value on filial hierarchy. Regardless of how justified her vengeance was—even if Yan Bailang had committed the exact same atrocities—murdering one's own Sect Master was an indelible taboo that would invite relentless public condemnation against her ascension.

Moreover, in Yan Bailang's crippled state, executing him was superfluous. Once the entire realm learned of his crimes, even if he survived, he would be stripped of his leadership. Conversely, residual elder lineages still lingered back at the sect; relying on Xia Bing's current strength, purging them would be an uphill battle. The optimal strategy was to have him carried back, squeezing his remaining political utility dry to reorganize the sect before legitimately assuming the mantle of leadership.

The intelligent path was to walk out the door and play the filial disciple to the very end.

And yet, why could she not bring herself to do it?

Why could she not stomach allowing this piece of human garbage to draw another breath on this earth?

[MONOLOGUE]Ah, it must be because I refuse to allow a brand-new vicious cycle to begin all over again![MONOLOGUE]

Thinking thus, Xia Bing raised her blade high—

—"*Tsk*, another foolish girl,"-Yuwen Xuan observed the tableau with cold detachment, critiquing:—"She shouldn't strike. If she wishes to become Sect Master, her hands must remain visibly immaculate. Besides, does she truly believe my meal is so easily slaughtered?"

—"Senior Brother Yuwen,"-the Heart Demon spoke softly.

—"I told you not to call me 'Senior Brother Yuwen.'"

—"I know what I must do,"-the Heart Demon smiled gently at Yuwen Xuan:—"I am sorry for delaying you for so long."

With those words, she transformed into a streak of crimson radiance, hurtling toward Xia Bing.

Yuwen Xuan flared in sudden fury:

—"Again?! You fool, why do you always make the exact same choice—?!"

Yet despite his bellow, he did not raise a hand to halt her, merely tracking the scarlet streak with his eyes.

Across the room, Xia Bing's blade never descended.

Not because she hesitated at the final threshold, but because an invisible, telekinetic hand clamped around her wrist and throat, hoisting her off the floorboards!

—"Y-You... you still haven't exhausted your strength..."-Xia Bing gasped, staring wide-eyed at Yan Bailang.

—"Hahaha! How could I ever dare exhaust my strength, you treacherous slut?!"-Yan Bailang sat upright, his expression twisted into demonic malice:—"You harbored foul designs all along—"

—"*Hmph!*"-Xia Bing let out a cold, mocking smirk.

She couldn't be bothered to recite his sins again. Instead, the final stone crushing her heart rolled away:

She had chosen correctly!

Had a monster like Yan Bailang returned to the sect, not only would she have been slaughtered, but every innocent disciple would have been dragged into his bloodbath!

She held zero expectation that this realm would ever grant them justice. At the very least, upon hearing of her demise, Rong Rong would lead the survivors far away from the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall!

***

—"Senior Sister Xia..."

Before the massive projection screen in the central plaza, Rong Rong watched the feed in paralyzed horror.

For reasons unknown, despite such bloody mayhem erupting in an athlete lounge, the Heavenly Spirit Sect had made no move to terminate the broadcast, airing the entire tragedy live to the realm.

Rong Rong had witnessed every second.

Sudden realization struck her like a thunderbolt. With trembling hands, she retrieved the storage pouch Xia Bing had entrusted to her, pulling out the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and the thick parchment folded within.

Reading merely the first few lines, despair washed over Rong Rong's features. She spun around, torn between fleeing back to the sect or sprinting toward the lounge to aid Xia Bing—yet whichever direction she chose, the path was completely blocked.

Throughout the broadcast, an immense crowd had congregated behind Rong Rong.

Practically every cultivator in the district was packed shoulder-to-shoulder before the screen.

It was human nature to gravitate toward scandalous gossip, particularly when it exposed the darkest secrets of what had once been the foremost beast-taming sect of the Middle Realm.

Murmurs rippled across the assembly:

[—"Tsk, who knew the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall was this utterly filthy?"](# "Speaker: Gossiping Cultivator")

[—"Honestly, isn't this common practice among major sects?"](# "Speaker: Cynical Cultivator")

[—"Common? Your sect must be terrifying!"](# "Speaker: Shocked Cultivator")

[—"This Xia Bing has quite some backbone."](# "Speaker: Impressed Cultivator")

[—"Backbone? She's butchering her own Sect Master, what backbone is that?!"](# "Speaker: Orthodox Moralist")

[—"Hey, Yan Bailang committed patricide first, can you blame her?"](# "Speaker: Sympathetic Cultivator")

[—"What right does a junior have to pass judgment? Doesn't that make her identical to Yan Bailang?"](# "Speaker: Moralizer")

[—"Hah, Yan Bailang held back a hidden reserve after all!"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

[—"Xia Bing let her guard down too early!"](# "Speaker: Spectator")

[—"Villains get ground down by worse villains."](# "Idiom: 恶人自有恶人磨 — evil people will inevitably be tormented by worse evil")

—"Senior Sister Xia only wished to protect our sect! How can you call her an 'evil person'?!"-Rong Rong's eyes flushed crimson as she shouted in desperate defense.

Nobody paid her any heed, though a few cast inquisitive glances her way:

[—"You're from the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall? Then you..."](# "Speaker: Inquisitive Passerby")

[—"What in the world is that?!"](# "Speaker: Stunned Spectator")

A collective gasp detonated through the crowd.

The spectators instantly abandoned Rong Rong, craning their necks toward the screen.

At the exact split-second before Yan Bailang could snap Xia Bing's neck, a brilliant crimson silhouette materialized in Xia Bing's place before Yan Bailang's clawed grasp! Accompanying her emergence, countless glowing red swallowtail butterflies drifted through the air like burning embers of karmic flame!

[—"A-A demon?!"](# "Speaker: Terrified Cultivator")

[—"How could a demon appear in the assembly venue?!"](# "Speaker: Alarmed Cultivator")

Pandemonium erupted in the plaza, while on the screen, Yan Bailang let out a bloodcurdling shriek:

—"S-Senior Sister?!"

The crowd's uproar exploded tenfold:

[—"Senior sister?!"](# "Speaker: Bewildered Crowd")

[—"The one he murdered centuries ago?!"](# "Speaker: Shocked Cultivator")

[—"If he butchered her, how is she here?!"](# "Speaker: Confused Spectator")

[—"Are you dense? She's obviously returned to claim his life!"](# "Speaker: Enlightened Cultivator")

[—"Ah! No wonder Xia Bing was acting so erratic! Having been acknowledged as the next Sect Master, why would she insist on killing Yan Bailang? She was being influenced by an ancient spirit!"](# "Speaker: Speculating Cultivator")

Bizarrely, Xia Bing's moral standing was rehabilitated in an instant.

Rong Rong merely found their chatter deafening.

***

In the ruined lounge, Xia Bing had no knowledge of how wildly public opinion was fluctuating. Clasping her bruised throat, she tilted her head back, staring blankly at the crimson figure standing in her place within Yan Bailang's grip.

—"Grandmaster...?"-she stammered hesitantly.

Though Xia Bing had never possessed a formal master, in deference to the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*, the title was entirely appropriate.

Hearing that whispered call, the crimson demoness turned her head, offering Xia Bing a faint, gentle smile.

Xia Bing returned a dazed, trembling smile of her own.

Immediately after, a cool pair of hands covered Xia Bing's eyes. An inexplicable sense of profound security washed over her soul. The crushing exhaustion born of surviving this harrowing ordeal came crashing down like a tidal wave; incapable of sustaining consciousness any longer, she crumpled softly to the floor.

Releasing her gaze from Yuwen Xuan—who had shielded Xia Bing's eyes—the Heart Demon finally turned her crimson gaze back upon Yan Bailang.

Yan Bailang had descended into absolute madness.

To be precise, having endured relentless psychological trauma throughout the entire day, holding his sanity together until now was already an extraordinary feat of endurance; even the Heart Demon suspected that corrupting him from the outset might not have been so simple.

Yan Bailang didn't believe he was mad; he believed he had finally pierced through the cosmic veil.

He laughed hysterically:

—"Haha! Hahahaha! I knew it! I knew that little slut was you!"

—"Otherwise, how could she command arts absent from the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual*?! Otherwise, why would she conspire with me to butcher the elders?!"

—"And yet! Why?! Why are you still alive?!"

—"You were butchered by my own hands! AAAAARGH—!"

Yan Bailang bellowed, pouring every ounce of his strength into throttling her throat. Had it still been Xia Bing, her neck would have snapped instantly; to a Heart Demon, physical force was completely meaningless.

Under his frenzied grip, crimson butterflies fluttered outward through the gaps between his fingers, settling upon his shoulders, chest, and arms.

The moment they touched his flesh, the butterflies ignited into blazing karmic flame, wrapping Yan Bailang in an inferno.

—"Naturally, I cannot be counted among the living, Junior Brother. Therefore, I have come to escort you home."

The Heart Demon spoke with sweet, ethereal elegance. Swarms of butterflies erupted from her form, enveloping Yan Bailang in an inescapable cocoon of fire.

Engulfed in flames, Yan Bailang seemed oblivious to the heat, muttering in dazed bewilderment:

—"Escort me...?"

—"Indeed. Look upon your own vessel."

Yan Bailang subconsciously lowered his gaze, discovering himself submerged in fire. Amidst the burning, his physical body was melting, shedding its human geometry to sprout grotesque vestigial limbs, sensory nodes, and matted bristling fur.

An agonizing wave of primordial mutation dominated his consciousness, wrenching manic shrieks and weeping laughs from his throat:

—"Ha! Hahaha! I have become a demon! Hahaha... Aaargh! I am a demon!"

—"No, no! I refuse to be a demon!"

—"Hahaha! Power! I possess ultimate power! I am a demon!"

He spouted contradictory, unhinged wails, drifting further and further from humanity.

The Heart Demon watched the transformation with icy detachment. Yet as Yan Bailang mutated, ancient, buried memories flooded her consciousness:

She saw herself witnessing her kind senior sister become their master's cultivation blood feast; she saw herself in paralyzing terror forming a pact with Yuwen Xuan; she saw herself with Yuwen Xuan's aid executing their master; she saw herself defying Yuwen Xuan's warnings to author the *Ten Thousand Beasts Manual* and entrust it to the elders to reform the sect; she saw herself ambushed and butchered by her junior brother, her soul shattered into dust, leaving merely a lingering wisp trapped within Yan Bailang's heart—finally returning under the moniker of a "Heart Demon" through Immortal Master Fujuan's Heart-Inquiring Stone.

—"Ah, I am not a heart demon,"-she realized softly.

Yuwen Xuan let out a cold snort.

—"And I am no Ancestor Yu Si,"-she continued.

Yuwen Xuan's brow furrowed in mild confusion.

—"I am Gan Tang,"-she declared.

Indeed, "Gan Tang" was her true birth name—a name far too sweet and feminine, deemed unfit by the patriarchal elders to lead a grand sect. Yet it was the name that belonged to her.

—"Considering the era when I fell, I should have reincarnated long ago, shouldn't I? Perhaps in my next life, I might still bear this name?"-she... Gan Tang mused with boundless hope.

—"Hmph, stop daydreaming. You are a residual sliver of a broken soul; where would you find a reincarnation?"-Yuwen Xuan scoffed mercilessly.

Gan Tang took no offense, bursting into a radiant, joyful laugh.

Just as she had in life.

Witnessing her smile, the mutated mass that had been Yan Bailang shrieked in shrill terror:

—"What are you smiling at?! What plot are you brewing?! What are you going to do?!"

By now, all semblance of human form had vanished; it was merely a grotesque, undulating mass of cancerous meat.

Gazing upon the aberration with deep revulsion, Gan Tang turned toward Yuwen Xuan:

—"The time has come, Senior Brother Yuwen."

Yuwen Xuan remained silent. Only the blind, deaf aberration let out a muffled gurgle:

—"What Senior Brother Yu... what senior brother do you have?! Could it be Yuwen Xuan?!"

Those sounds were never fully articulated, devolving mid-breath into the screech of metal grinding against glass.

Accompanying that cacophony, dazzling formation sigils detonated outside the lounge, freezing the screech and the horror permanently into the abomination once known as Yan Bailang—sealing it eternally within the heart of the Grand Demon Subduing Array as a heap of rotting meat.

In that identical instant, the crimson maiden dissolved into a cloud of red butterflies, leaving not even a splinter of the peach branch behind. Only her final, lingering whisper echoed in the quiet air:

—"Thank you, Senior Brother Yuwen."

***

Outside the private lounge, disciples from the Formation Hall meticulously locked the final array cipher into place.

—"The Demon Subduing Array is complete!"

—"Eldest Senior Brother, we can enter to rescue the survivors!"

Reporting their success, they looked toward their Eldest Senior Brother, who had stood vigil over the perimeter throughout—only to gawk in shock upon finding a maiden draped across his arm: none other than Xia Bing of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall.

—"There is no need to enter; this is the sole living soul remaining,"-Yuwen Xuan replied coldly, tossing Xia Bing casually into the arms of the nearest disciple.—"Deliver her to the infirmary, and notify the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall... as well as the Heavenly Dao Alliance."

—"Then, the inside..."-the flustered disciple asked, bracing Xia Bing's limp form.

—"Do not step inside; demonic pollution is exceedingly lethal,"-Yuwen Xuan instructed dismissively.—"Notify Ran Deng and let her decide how to dispose of it."

—"Eh? But Eldest Senior Brother, wasn't this matter..."

—"The one presiding over the Immortal Arts Assembly is Ran Deng, not me."

Turning his back to the crowd, Yuwen Xuan gave a lazy wave of his hand and strolled into the distance, leaving the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples staring at one another in dumbfounded silence.

***

No one witnessed Yuwen Xuan collapsing heavily onto the grassy earth the moment he vanished from public sight.

—"Stupid beyond belief."

He cursed softly, though who the target was remained unclear.

Yuwen Xuan was genuinely exhausted.

Today, he had manifested ancient demonic traps, fought an exhibition match, squatted to spectate the melodrama, and executed the final containment—most critically, sustaining the manifestation of that Heart Demon... that 'Gan Tang' had drained an enormous portion of his own soul energy. He was practically hollowed out, yet hadn't managed to ingest a single morsel.

The core tragedy was that he hadn't gotten to eat.

And yet...

[MONOLOGUE]I have no appetite.[MONOLOGUE]

It was all the fault of these foolish mortals.

Yuwen Xuan had noticed it long ago: whenever he witnessed humans engaging in foolish emotional entanglements—sacrificing themselves for a sect, experiencing "heartwarming" peer camaraderie, refusing neat and tidy vengeance, or making irrational, self-destructive choices—an overwhelming sensation of fullness washed over him, far more profound than consuming True Immortals or ancient deities. His all-consuming, bottomless hunger simply evaporated into nothingness.

Thus, having painstakingly waited for Yan Bailang to fully demonize, he hadn't taken a single bite, casually dumping the carcass onto Ran Deng.

—"Ending up like this, I really am human after all!"

Yuwen Xuan muttered, raising an arm to stare blankly at his human hand.

Truth be told, right now, he preferred turning into a sheep.

A stupid little black sheep that contemplated nothing and did nothing but chew grass. That was infinitely simpler.

Yet for reasons unknown, try as he might, he couldn't trigger the transformation.

—"Why is that?"

Closing his eyes, Yuwen Xuan mumbled lazily into the breeze.

He felt that simply falling asleep here was quite pleasant—preferably waking up as a sheep.

At that exact moment, a cool, gentle palm rested lightly upon his forehead, accompanied by a helpless sigh:

—"Honestly, Xuan'er, why do you always refuse to eat when you should, yet recklessly ingest garbage when you shouldn't?"

—"..."

In a rare display, Yuwen Xuan did not snap back with a witty retort. Instead, he nuzzled his head gently against the palm.

The owner of that voice let out a soft, resigned chuckle, slipping his arms beneath Yuwen Xuan's shoulders to lift the youth onto his lap.

—"...Master, I am in human form right now,"-Yuwen Xuan reminded him quietly.

Yet he displayed zero intention of pulling away; instead, he wrapped his arms tightly around his Master's waist, clinging like a spoiled child.

Zhou Hui shook his head with tender exasperation:

—"You never grow up."

—"I am grown up,"-Yuwen Xuan buried his face in his Master's robes, mumbling indistinctly:—"I even feel ready to accept a disciple—for example, that girl named Gan Tang under my wing isn't bad. The one who helped me farm jellyfish. I think she would make an excellent serv... *cough*, disciple."

—"You wish to take an apprentice?"-Zhou Hui remarked in surprise.—"Isn't it a bit premature?"

—"*Aww...*"-Yuwen Xuan let out a whiny, wheedling whimper.

Given his current adult physique, the sound was objectively sickening.

Only his Master wouldn't hold it against him:

—"At the very least, wait until you return from your Second Junior Brother's post."

—"Why must I wait until I return from that bastard second... Wait! Master, you agree to let me venture outside?!"-Yuwen Xuan sprang upright in astonishment, pressing:—"To leave this micro-world entirely, all on my own?!"

He conveniently scrubbed the part about visiting his Second Junior Brother from the itinerary.

Zhou Hui seemed not to notice the omission, answering calmly:

—"Was that not our original agreement? Once your cultivation was sufficient, you would be permitted to roam the realms."

In truth, Yuwen Xuan had possessed that qualification long ago. It was merely because he had abruptly mutated into a black sheep, compounded by past reincarnations where he had vanished while dispatched on procurement errands, that Zhou Hui had hesitated to let him off the leash.

Now, however, Yuwen Xuan had clearly discovered his own methodology to suppress his gluttony, and possessed his own moral code when dealing with mortals. Zhou Hui saw no further need to cage him.

Perceiving the shift in Zhou Hui's demeanor, Yuwen Xuan leaped into the air:

—"Master, you are the best! I shall go pack my travel gear this instant—!"

With a joyful *poof*, he transformed into a chubby, exuberant black lamb, bounding and hopping merrily into the distance.

Zhou Hui stared in silence at the bouncing silhouette of the round black sheep, suddenly wondering whether he had just made a terrible, catastrophic mistake.
