# Chapter 86: [Some Past History](<# "Literal CN: 一些过去 (yīxiē guòqù) — some past history / events from the ancient past; chapter title">) (第86章 一些过去)

From the deepest depths of the Beast Soul Tower, muffled, agonizing shrieks faintly drifted up.

Immediately following them came the sound of pathetic, [ying-ying bleating](<# "Literal CN: 嘤嘤 (yīngyīng) — high-pitched bleating and wailing of the distressed black lamb">) and sobbing.

Outside, the disciples of Thousand Beasts Hall who had barely managed to pass the trial wore unnatural, strained expressions. Not daring to steal a second glance toward the passageway leading down to the lower levels, they hastened their footsteps.

Even the sect master of Thousand Beasts Hall was muttering under his breath in doubt:

[Could there truly be a monster on the second floor that weeps with such ying-ying cries? Or has some unknown mutation occurred down there?]

Yet due to "historical reasons," Yan Bailang was utterly unwilling to conduct a personal investigation. Feigning as though nothing whatsoever had transpired, he stowed away the water mirror.

In truth, had they stolen so much as a single peek, they would have discovered that the scene below wasn't horrifying at all—in fact, it was rather comical.

After all, the sight of a three-year-old child thrashing a diminutive lamb with a [peach branch switch](<# "Literal CN: 桃木 (táomù) — branch or stick of peach wood, traditionally used to ward off evil, but here used by Zhou Hui as a disciplinary switch">) was actually quite adorable—provided he wasn't whipping it quite so savagely.

Those "ying-ying" sobs were naturally issuing from the little black lamb:

—"[Ying-ying, ying... I haven't even started eating yet, ying... ying-ying...](<# "Literal CN: 嘤嘤，嘤……我都还没开始吃嘤…嘤嘤…… — Yuwen Xuan weeping in his lamb form">)"-the little black lamb sobbed in distress.

He had just received a sound thrashing from his master, his little [bum](<# "Literal CN: 小PP (xiǎo PP) — humorous internet slang abbreviation for buttocks/rear end">) beaten black, blue, and swollen. Of course, for a cultivator, such an injury was hardly a major issue; even missing limbs could be reattached. Yet Zhou Hui felt his eldest disciple required a far more indelible lesson and had consequently applied a spell. Now, the little black lamb's backside wouldn't heal for at least half a year—even if he transformed back into human form, he would still be forced to maintain this tender state.

In truth, the physical pain was bearable; it was the sheer humiliation that stung.

Yet even after things had reached this point, this dyed-in-the-wool foodie had clearly failed to engage in any proper self-reflection. The reason for his weeping was laughably pure:

—"At least... at least wait until I've finished eating before thrashing me, ying..."-the lamb wept pitifully.

[Is your entire grievance really just that you didn't get to eat?!]

Zhou Hui felt that giving this [bratty child](<# "Literal CN: 熊孩子 (xióngháizi) — bear child; mischievous, trouble-making brat">) one beating was far from enough; he desperately needed a second round.

Yet whether it was because the little black lamb kept droning on with foolish laments like "didn't get to eat" and "wait until I finish eating before thrashing me," Zhou Hui's own stomach began to churn, emitting an audible *gurgle*.

Right—his current avatar still contained a drop of the Taotie's essence heart-blood!

The exact same insatiable hunger.

Before realizing it, he had been fine, but the moment he took note of it, the hunger flared up with ferocious intensity, as if his stomach were about to digest itself. Simultaneously, the entire Beast Soul Tower suddenly smelled overwhelmingly fragrant, akin to a weary commuter trudging home from midnight overtime and stumbling upon a roadside street-food stall.

—"Ying? Master, your stomach is growling?!"-Yuwen Xuan asked with keen ears.-"Are you hungry?"

—"Indeed,"-Zhou Hui replied, regarding him with a cool, deadpan stare.

If this little scoundrel dared to propose something along the lines of "Let's devour all the beast souls in the cafeteria~~(scratch that)~~ Beast Soul Tower together," he would thrash him a second time on the spot.

Fortunately, Yuwen Xuan didn't dare go that far after all.

It wasn't that he had cured his death-seeking tendencies. Rather, even having turned into a sheep, in Yuwen Xuan's underlying logic, the food his master consumed was fundamentally, completely different from what he ate.

While teardrops went *pitter-patter* down his snout, the little black lamb used a small hoof to rummage through the wool on his fleece, miraculously unearthing a storage pouch from within.

Gripping the storage pouch between his teeth, he proffered it before Zhou Hui:

—"Master, you can eat this,"-the little black lamb offered.

Zhou Hui peered inside, only to discover completely normal human cuisine—the pastries, refreshments, and snacks that Yuwen Xuan routinely prepared.

—"You carry these on you? Then why haven't you eaten them?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"These can't fill my stomach anyway,"-the little black lamb explained.-"They're just for fooling myself whenever my mouth gets greedy."

He gazed up at Zhou Hui with pitiful, sorrowful eyes:

—"Master, this time I truly didn't do it on purpose,"-the little lamb added, trying to look as innocent as possible.

[You swindled that sect's young lady half a year ago, and you dare claim it wasn't on purpose?!]

Zhou Hui knew this rascal was [acting cute](<# "Literal CN: 卖萌 (màiméng) — weaponizing cuteness / acting cute to elicit sympathy and escape punishment">) to solicit sympathy. Yet holding the little black lamb's storage pouch, feeling the dull cramps churning in his own stomach, and gazing upon the lamb's adorable, wide-eyed expression, he couldn't help but soften slightly.

This child was truly starved to his wit's end.

Although he had previously known that the Taotie soul exerted an influence, he had never imagined that the sensation of hunger could be so agonizingly tragic. Especially for a child, resorting to "slightly" unscrupulous means was almost inevitable—after all, it was raw survival instinct.

Once they returned, this problem had to be solved once and for all.

Zhou Hui engaged in sober self-reflection, feeling that he might have been a bit too harsh on his eldest disciple.

—"You must be famished as well,"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"Fine. I suppose I can let you eat a little..."

—"Truly?!"-The little black lamb's ears instantly pricked straight up.

In the next breath, he kicked out all four little hooves, eager to bolt toward the opposite end of the corridor—

*Smack!*

Zhou Hui's hand moved with lightning speed, hoisting him up by the scruff, and a peach branch switch came down hard squarely upon his little bum.

—"Ying! M-Master, you hit me again!"-The little lamb's eyes went wide as tears streamed down anew.

He glared at Zhou Hui with an accusatory gaze, looking as though he had suffered the ultimate betrayal.

—"Regardless of the circumstances, stealing food from the neighbor's home is fundamentally wrong!"-Zhou Hui eyed him coolly.

Especially considering that the beast souls within the Beast Soul Tower formed an integral component of the seal. Although many were the aggrieved souls of disciples who had failed the trial, who was to say that when undergoing the ordeal, they hadn't harbored the resolve to guard this tower even in death?

At the very least, as part of an ecological cycle, they ought to serve as nourishment to strengthen the disciples of their own sect, rather than being inexplicably devoured by an outside Taotie.

—"But..."-The little lamb's ears drooped down limply.

—"Besides, this meager amount of beast souls couldn't possibly fill your belly anyway,"-Zhou Hui said.

Hoisting the lamb by its tail, he carried it toward the entrance leading down to the next level:

—"Come. Let us go see what can truly satisfy an empty stomach,"-Zhou Hui said.

The little black lamb's eyes instantly ignited with brilliant light.

*

—"I knew it! I just knew Master could open it!"-Yuwen Xuan cheered, dancing in wild ecstasy.

They were currently standing at the barrier seal of the Beast Soul Tower's lower sanctum. That very seal—which ostensibly required vanquishing the guardian beast soul and presenting the special spiritual medallion of the Thousand Beasts Hall sect master to unlock—offered not the slightest resistance at this moment. It stood wide open like the gates of a food warehouse, inviting them to stroll right in and retrieve the provisions hidden within its deepest recesses.

At this juncture, Yuwen Xuan acutely felt the inconvenience of having a sheep's physique.

He desperately wished to shower his master with elaborate, poetic praise, but alas, his current sheep brain was pitifully impoverished, utterly unable to scrounge up any serviceable adjectives. Yuwen Xuan could only express his jubilation through sheer shock:

—"What 'True Immortal seal'?! Before Master, it isn't even worth a glance!"-Yuwen Xuan proclaimed in awe.

—"There was no True Immortal. Back then, I was merely at the Soul Formation stage,"-Zhou Hui corrected him flatly.

The little lamb tilted its sluggish head, utterly incapable of processing Zhou Hui's words for a moment.

Watching Zhou Hui deftly undo the warding restrictions with practiced familiarity, he pondered hard for a long while before an unbelievable realization finally surfaced:

—"Wait a moment, Master... Are you saying that the 'True Immortal' who defeated and sealed that ferocious beast back then... was actually you?!"-Yuwen Xuan asked, stunned.

—"What lies below cannot be considered any particularly exceptional ferocious beast either—at least, it pales far in comparison to a Taotie,"-Zhou Hui did not deny it, continuing smoothly.

Yuwen Xuan still couldn't wrap his mind around it:

—"B-But according to the lore of Thousand Beasts Hall, I always assumed that was an event from the era of the Demon God War?"-Yuwen Xuan asked, bewildered.

—"Indeed. It sounds much grander that way,"-Zhou Hui said.

With the conversation reaching this point, even with his slow sheep brain, Yuwen Xuan grasped the truth.

In the cultivation world, where people's lifespans stretched endlessly long, the history of a sect was naturally the longer the better. Ideally, it was best if the sect had existed since the dawn of creation itself—that was the ultimate hallmark of venerable heritage!

Regrettably, the history of most cultivation sects wasn't particularly long. In fact, sects whose lifespans surpassed the ten-thousand-year mark of the Body Integration stage could be counted on one hand. Thus, the vast majority of sects resorted to shamelessly grasping at straws, forcibly forging tenuous connections to the ancient cultivation world, the Fallen Capital of White Jade, and primordial antiquity.

Thousand Beasts Hall was a prime example.

The moment buzzwords like "True Immortal," "Abyss Sea Ruins," and "Ferocious Beast" were strung together, it sounded precisely like a mythical tale from around the era of the Demon God War, when True Immortals still descended upon the mortal plane.

That certainly possessed far more prestige and mystique than "ran into a rather formidable spirit beast in the Abyss Sea and was rescued by a Soul Formation cultivator."

Thinking this through, Yuwen Xuan suddenly erupted in righteous indignation:

—"They erased Master's credit!"-Yuwen Xuan fumed.

[The current sect master was a thief, and the founding sect master was a thief as well!]

—"No, no. Although that tale is slightly exaggerated, it is broadly accurate in outline,"-Zhou Hui said.-"In fact, I ought to thank that... whatever his name was, for enabling me to discover such a [geomantically blessed treasure trove](<# "Literal CN: 风水宝地 (fēngshuǐ bǎodì) — geomantically blessed sanctuary / auspicious location with rich spiritual veins">) like Mount Tianling for the Heavenly Spirit Sect."

As it turned out, Thousand Beasts Hall's founding legend was not pure fabrication out of thin air.

As long as one swapped out the "True Immortal" for "Zhou Hui at the Soul Formation stage," the rest of the narrative was more or less identical:

Back then, while exploring the Abyss Sea, Zhou Hui had by chance encountered Patriarch Qubi, the founder of Thousand Beasts Hall, being hunted down by a ferocious beast. Naturally, he hadn't possessed such a resounding title back then either, but was merely an unremarkable Void Refinement cultivator.

[...] Void Refinement? But Master was only at Soul Formation when he rescued him?!

[Isn't Soul Formation an entire major realm lower than Void Refinement?!]

[Ah, it's Master. Never mind then, totally normal.]

The little black lamb had initially wanted to voice his confusion, but after rolling the thought through his head, he obediently kept listening to his master's tale.

Zhou Hui at Soul Formation had rescued Patriarch Qubi at Void Refinement. Yet since it was, after all, a ferocious beast capable of mauling a Void Refinement cultivator, Zhou Hui had sustained minor internal injuries. Moreover, that ferocious beast was indeed peculiar; back then, Zhou Hui had been nowhere near as richly knowledgeable as he was today and had been temporarily unable to seal the beast's soul completely. Thus, just as recounted in Thousand Beasts Hall's legend, he sealed the beast soul inside a stone stele, pointed Patriarch Qubi in a general direction, and instructed him to shoulder the stele to seek a [Land of Fivefold Blessings](<# "Literal CN: 五福之地 (wǔfú zhī dì) — Land of Fivefold Blessings; an auspicious, geomantically blessed paradise made up on the spot by Zhou Hui">) to enact the final seal.

Hearing up to this point, Yuwen Xuan suddenly decided to forgive Thousand Beasts Hall for erasing his master's credit and falsely claiming the advent of a True Immortal.

Under those circumstances, anyone rescued by someone whose apparent cultivation was a whole realm lower than their own—who not only slew the ferocious beast but also bestowed a sealing method resembling a celestial trial, which upon completion not only secured the seal but also granted enlightenment into a cultivation manual—would naturally suspect their savior's true cultivation, wondering if his mortal form were merely a disguised incarnation.

Furthermore, the cultivation realm adored the trope of "encountering immortals." It was exceedingly easy to understand why that founding patriarch had genuinely believed he had met a True Immortal.

Not to mention, his master was already like a True Immortal... no, he looked far more like a True Immortal than an actual True Immortal!

Yuwen Xuan reflected to himself.

Zhou Hui, however, felt he had accomplished nothing worthy of praise.

At the time, he certainly hadn't proposed that procedure to "test" Patriarch Qubi. Rather, the condition of that beast soul was genuinely ill-suited to linger in the Abyss Sea Ruins—every additional minute it stayed brought another minute of peril. That was why Zhou Hui had resorted to gentle deception, coaxing Patriarch Qubi into shouldering the stone stele and fleeing the ruins.

Had he not put on the airs of an unfathomable prophet back then, assuring Patriarch Qubi that a Land of Fivefold Blessings lay in a certain direction where the beast could be sealed, the man would never have possessed the courage to wander about carrying a stele imbued with a ferocious beast soul upon his back.

Naturally, Zhou Hui hadn't simply washed his hands of the matter either. After dressing his injuries, he had pursued him out of the ruins without resting, intending to properly deal with the beast soul once and for all. What he hadn't anticipated was that Patriarch Qubi's luck was phenomenally absurd—in a mere matter of days, he had miraculously stumbled upon an actual "Land of Fivefold Blessings."

—"A few days?"-Yuwen Xuan's ears twitched.

[Wait, why do I recall the story saying he walked for nine hundred and ninety-nine days, suffering an ordeal more arduous than Master's own ancient quest for the Dao?!]

—"Around seven days, perhaps? It might have been even shorter,"-Zhou Hui verified his own recollection.-"After tending to my wounds, I immediately set off in pursuit without a moment's delay. Yet when I caught up with him seven days later, the seal had already taken root."

Speaking up to here, even Zhou Hui felt that Patriarch Qubi had been a genuine [luck god](<# "Literal CN: 欧皇 (ōuhuáng) — European Emperor; internet gaming slang for an individual blessed with miraculous RNG luck">).

He had encountered mortal peril, yet was rescued in the nick of time; he had been duped into seeking a nonexistent Land of Fivefold Blessings, yet miraculously discovered one in just seven days; and most outrageous of all, though eroded by the beast soul, he gained profound enlightenment from it to synthesize an entire cultivation method, establishing Thousand Beasts Hall upon that foundation.

Such cosmic serendipity was truly impossible to emulate.

Even in retrospect, Zhou Hui found it nothing short of mind-boggling.

—"In truth, that talk of a 'Land of Fivefold Blessings' was pure nonsense I made up on the spot. But when I discovered he had genuinely stumbled upon an auspicious spiritual treasure trove and successfully sealed the beast soul, I felt too embarrassed to show myself and confess that I had lied to him,"-Zhou Hui recalled the past.-"More importantly, he had just gained enlightenment into his technique; stepping forward right then would have made me look like an envious villain harboring sinister motives."

Back then, his skin had still been far too thin.

Gazing at the Beast Soul Tower, Zhou Hui reflected with a sigh.

Had he not been squeamish about having his little white lie exposed back then, he never would have allowed Patriarch Qubi to construct the Beast Soul Tower in such an abhorrent fashion—relying on the sacrificed souls of disciples to maintain the seal.

Nonetheless, Zhou Hui had still been exceptionally responsible back then.

Worrying that the spiritual vein of this so-called "Land of Fivefold Blessings" might prove incapable of permanently containing the beast soul, he had conducted a thorough investigation. Although in the end he merely confirmed that Patriarch Qubi's luck was simply off the charts—miraculously managing to hold the beast soul fast—as an unexpected bonus, he followed that spiritual vein all the way to the grand spiritual vein of Mount Tianling.

Eventually, when establishing the Heavenly Spirit Sect, after comparing several candidate sanctuaries, Zhou Hui ultimately chose that very location.

—"...Ying. So having this neighbor wasn't a coincidence after all,"-Yuwen Xuan murmured.

—"In the pursuit of immortality, there is no such thing as coincidence—only karmic serendipity,"-Zhou Hui said indifferently.

Lifting his head, he announced:

—"...We have arrived."

After discussing the origins of Thousand Beasts Hall along the descent, Zhou Hui and his eldest disciple—in the eyes of any outsider, a three-year-old child leading a diminutive lamb—finally reached the very bottom of the Beast Soul Tower.

Due to their diminutive stature, the trek had taken a fair bit of time.

As if welcoming their arrival, a barrage of shrill, horrific shrieks erupted from the base of the tower. It sounded like the screech of a night owl intermingled with the howling fury of a typhoon, or countless fingernails scraping across a chalkboard—exceedingly unkind to the ears.

Yet this was only capable of terrifying disciples who came to undertake the trial.

The instant Zhou Hui released a sliver of his spiritual aura, the dreadful screeching abruptly choked off—like a rooster seized by the neck and having its throat slit—ceasing completely.

Standing upon the stone steps, the little black lamb craned his neck to peer downward.

He had entered this place once before in the distant past. However, back then he had been remarkably young—even smaller than he was now, whether viewed as a Taotie soul or from a human perspective. Consequently, he had been forced to coordinate closely with his contract partner at the time—the previous female sect master of Thousand Beasts Hall—just to bite off a tiny fragment from that colossal soul. That was the sole reason he hadn't devoured the entire monstrosity in one gulp back then.

Two hundred years later, that beast soul still appeared just as colossal. Yet this time, having received Zhou Hui's tireless academic education for over two centuries, Yuwen Xuan finally managed to dredge up the name of this "ferocious beast" from within a sheep brain that couldn't even pass a Foundation Establishment exam:

[Guiche](<# "Literal CN: 鬼车 (Guǐchē) — Ghost Chariot, an ancient mythical nine-headed bird of ill omen in Chinese folklore">).

Or commonly known as the [Nine-Headed Bird](<# "Literal CN: 九头鸟 (Jiǔtóuniǎo) — Nine-Headed Bird, a mythical beast associated with bad fortune, said to have had its tenth head severed by a deity, leaving a perpetually dripping neck">).

Speaking strictly by species, it genuinely couldn't be considered a top-tier ferocious beast.

In reality, this bird originally possessed ten heads, but one was said to have been severed by a deity, leaving behind only a perpetually bleeding neck stump. Miraculously, this distinct feature had actually become "hereditary"—every single Nine-Headed Bird inherited this bleeding trait.

The bird's intrinsic combat strength was not particularly terrifying; it possessed no extraordinary divine abilities, and its malevolent aura was only potent enough to harm small children. Thus, it was never classified as a supreme calamity beast.

Even now, severed from the True Immortal Realm and with the mortal plane's spiritual energy diminished compared to the ancient past, they still survived comfortably. Not only in the Abyss Sea, but occasionally one could even encounter such beasts wreaking havoc in mortal kingdoms.

Of course, being a fellow "ferocious beast," its malevolent aura was far more suited to lining a Taotie's belly.

Especially since this particular specimen was abnormally, gargantuously massive.

—"Was it truly this enormous back then?"-Zhou Hui murmured, observing the bird's beast soul.

—"Master thinks it's huge too?"-Yuwen Xuan asked in surprise.

—"...Indeed. For a Nine-Headed Bird, it is rather excessively colossal,"-Zhou Hui said.-"I always assumed it was merely because my cultivation was too low back then that I perceived it as exceptionally formidable."

—"Perhaps it was fattened up by Thousand Beasts Hall over all these years?"-the little black lamb suggested.

Gazing at the massive bird, drool dripped uncontrollably from his mouth in steady droplets:

—"It looks so delicious!"-Yuwen Xuan drooled.

Had his stubby tail not been firmly pinched in Zhou Hui's grasp, he would have pounced forward in the very first second.

—"Thousand Beasts Hall?"-Zhou Hui's brows furrowed slightly.

Come to think of it, that hypothesis held a great deal of merit.

From the very moment Zhou Hui pursued Yuwen Xuan into this tower, he had sensed the dense, rancorous resentment permeating the interior.

With just a touch more observation, Zhou Hui understood exactly what had transpired here.

Yet Zhou Hui was no champion of justice; he harbored no ambition to purge every single injustice under heaven.

Back then, he had observed the cultivation technique synthesized by Patriarch Qubi for a period, confirming that the art was somewhat shortcut-driven, rather grueling, and overly eager for quick gains. Yet he had found no justification to intervene—what Dao another chose to cultivate was their own sovereign prerogative. Even though he had anticipated that the manual might turn sinister depending on the practitioner's disposition, that was still no justification to snuff out the possibility beforehand.

Thus, as long as Thousand Beasts Hall refrained from trafficking children for blood sacrifices, Zhou Hui had no intention of meddling.

[A single Heart-Interrogation Stone is enough. I have no qualification to pass judgment on others...]

Zhou Hui reminded himself inwardly.

—Indeed, back during the Tenth Month Festival, Zhou Hui had discerned the rot within Yan Bailang and intentionally slipped that Heart-Interrogation Stone into his possession, with a deliberate touch of malice aforethought.

Beyond that, however, Zhou Hui had no desire to interfere excessively.

Yet sometimes, one was fine so long as one didn't look; the more one looked, the more one's blood boiled!

The little black lamb, however, was utterly incapable of empathizing with Zhou Hui's inner turmoil (especially while wearing the appearance of a three-year-old child). Stamping his little hooves, he pleaded:

—"Master, Master, let's go down and eat!"-the little black lamb urged.

Gazing upon that enormous mound of soul essence below, his eyes were glued to it as though staring at a platter of golden [roasted chicken](<# "Literal CN: 烤鸡 (kǎojī) — roasted chicken; Yuwen Xuan viewing ancient calamity beast souls purely as succulent poultry dishes">).

—"Very well. Down we go,"-Zhou Hui nodded.

*

[Dining together with Master truly is the absolute best!]

Yuwen Xuan reflected in sheer bliss.

Just look back at the last time he snuck into the cafeteria! Sure, he had managed to lick a single bite, but in truth, he had been beaten half to death before fleeing, and his little partner had perished shortly afterward, depriving him of any further chance to sneak in.

Coming with Master, however, was an entirely different universe. Not only were all warding locks rendered mere decorative props, but once they arrived below, the terrifying beast soul of this colossal bird obediently let him feast on it, behaving exactly like a real roasted chicken.

Zhou Hui hadn't even lifted a finger; merely stepping before the stone stele binding the beast soul was enough to reduce the horrifying entity to an obedient, cooked state, slumping submissively onto the ground and permitting the little black lamb to tear off a chunk!

—"Ying-ying! Ying!"-the little black lamb cheered, attempting to extol his master while vigorously chewing on soul essence.

Unfortunately, his master was wholly unreceptive:

—"Don't speak while eating,"-Zhou Hui patted the lamb's head.

Seeing the little black lamb eat with such ravenous relish, Zhou Hui's already famished stomach growled even louder.

Observing that this roasted chicken~~(scratch that)~~ Nine-Headed Bird possessed zero capacity to resist before him, he sat cross-legged upon the stone floor with satisfaction.

Zhou Hui had no intention of wildly gnawing away with atrocious table manners like his eldest disciple. Instead, he felt this was the perfect opportunity to cultivate the Taotie's heritage technique.

—His current avatar had been formed specifically to experience the Taotie's inheritance; he had simply been so occupied with thrashing his disciple earlier that he hadn't found the time.

Moreover, this present environment was actually more fitting than his secluded immortal cave. It could also grind away a portion of the Nine-Headed Bird's malevolent energy, so that when those children undergoing the trial descended to assimilate the beast soul in the future, they would suffer far less torment and enjoy a higher rate of survival.

Regrettably, Zhou Hui was not a roundworm residing in Yan Bailang's gut. He merely assumed Xia Bing and the others were participating in an ordinary Thousand Beasts Hall trial, accepting that a slightly higher mortality rate was simply the price of pursuing power. Little did Zhou Hui suspect that even under the agonizing interrogation of the Heart-Interrogation Stone, Yan Bailang was still calculating such vile, sordid schemes.

Nor did Zhou Hui realize that the principle he so casually grasped was in fact the most vital lost secret of the *Ten Thousand Beasts Art*: so long as the beast soul's malevolent energy was shaved down beforehand, assimilating the beast soul would drastically elevate the breakthrough success rate, entirely circumventing the mortal peril of death.

In fact, the moment Zhou Hui circulated that ancestral heritage technique, an extraordinarily fierce Taotie aura surged through the entirety of the Beast Soul Tower.

To say nothing of the Nine-Headed Bird before him, every single soul across all the other levels of the Beast Soul Tower dropped prostrate to the floor, shivering uncontrollably like tiny critters resigned to their fate and awaiting the slaughter.

The little black lamb's hooves went weak under the dreadful pressure, collapsing onto his side.

[—"Ying?"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb) / Literal CN: 嘤 — startled bleat under Zhou Hui's overwhelming Taotie aura">)

Yet the moment he sensed this terrifying presence emanated from his master, he brushed it off without a care, scooting closer to Zhou Hui's side and resuming his happy gnawing on the beast soul.

Indeed, Zhou Hui would never lay a hand on innocent beast souls.

In his eyes, the only entity within the Beast Soul Tower that could be safely ground down was this Nine-Headed Bird. It should have been slain back in antiquity to begin with, but due to its sheer enormity, and because an entire sect now relied on it for breakthroughs, snuffing it out entirely was inconvenient. However, devouring a portion of it was perfectly fair game.

And so, as the cultivation method circulated, a layer of the Nine-Headed Bird's malevolent aura was smoothly licked away.

The once-ferocious beast soul of the nine-headed beast became even more listless and withered, curling into a corner at the tower's base and trembling in terror.

—"Eh?"

At that moment, Zhou Hui opened his eyes.

—"No wonder this creature was so immense—"-Zhou Hui murmured, rising to his feet and forming a hand seal toward the Nine-Headed Bird.

Seeing this, the Nine-Headed Bird completely abandoned its previously submissive posture of quietly being bitten, eaten, and licked. Shrieking shrilly once more, it lunged ferociously toward Zhou Hui!

Yet while Zhou Hui's physical form had shrunk into a child, his cultivation hadn't weakened in the slightest. How could he possibly fail to overpower the soul of a bird he had already slain back when he was merely a Soul Formation cultivator?

In a single exchange, the beast soul collapsed wretchedly to the ground, and a single drop of blood flew out from within the soul.

—"What is that?"-The little black lamb instantly dropped the soul chunk he was chewing, trotting over.-"It looks so delicious!"

[—"Ying!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb) / Literal CN: 嘤 — weaponized cute bleating to beg for food">)

—"You cannot eat this!"-Zhou Hui sealed the droplet of blood, waving Yuwen Xuan away.-"If you are hungry, go gnaw on the Nine-Headed Bird. This bird possesses regenerative properties; it will be enough to feed you for a very long time."

A ferocious beast whose severed neck could endlessly drip blood in perpetuity could, in theory, provide an inexhaustible supply of sustenance for a Taotie.

Yet with a Taotie's finicky palate, how could he fail to sniff out the true source of the aroma that had attracted him in the first place?

—"But I feel that drop of blood is so much better!"-Yuwen Xuan protested.

—"No,"-Zhou Hui refused flatly.

After all, this was [True Immortal Blood](<# "Literal CN: 真仙之血 (zhēnxīan zhī xuè) — True Immortal Blood; pure, unpolluted celestial blood containing boundless divine power">).

Unlike divine blood or demonic blood, it carried neither divine taint nor demonic corruption—only boundless, untainted power.

One needed only look at the Nine-Headed Bird to see: it was precisely because of this droplet of blood that it had grown far more malevolent and massive than its kind. If fed to a Taotie, he feared that even without the other half of his soul, Yuwen Xuan might ascend straight into the celestial realm on the spot.

Sensing Zhou Hui's unyielding firmness, the little lamb whimpered a couple of [ying-yings](<# "Literal CN: 嘤嘤 (yīngyīng) — submissive whimpering of a lamb admitting defeat">) and ceased pestering him.

After all, the list of things his master forbade him from eating was endless.

It wasn't as if this were his first day dealing with it.

As for this Nine-Headed Bird, though it had lost a bit of "seasoning," it was still thoroughly edible, and sufficiently hearty to sustain him for an eternity.

Calculating thus, the little black lamb turned his gaze back toward the Nine-Headed Bird.

The soul of the Nine-Headed Bird shivered aggrievedly, trying its utmost to shrink its colossal bulk even smaller.

Zhou Hui naturally lacked the carefree simplicity of the little black lamb. He had just declared that "there is no coincidence, only karmic serendipity." While this occurrence hadn't been his serendipity, but rather Patriarch Qubi's karmic fortune back then, hadn't he determined the location of the Heavenly Spirit Sect precisely because of this incident?

Was it truly not a mere "coincidence"?

[Once we return, I should visit the location where I first encountered the Nine-Headed Bird to investigate—even though thousands of years have already elapsed...]

Calculating in his heart, Zhou Hui said to Yuwen Xuan:

—"Xuan'er, we are heading back."

—"Ying?"-The little black lamb's eyes went wide, soul essence still clamped between his jaws.-"Ying-ying!"

[I haven't eaten enough yet!]

—"Rest easy, I shall devise a solution,"-Zhou Hui assured him.

[Right, I still have to resolve the issue of this child's perpetual starvation.]

Pondering this, Zhou Hui casually instructed:

—"As for Thousand Beasts Hall... continue keeping an eye on them."

If this was not a "coincidence," having such a sect lingering near his doorstep for generation after generation left an unsettling itch in his heart.

Yuwen Xuan, however, clearly interpreted those two statements as permission to visit the cafeteria~~(scratch that)~~ whenever he pleased.

—"Ying! Rest assured! I will watch them like a hawk!"-The little black lamb spat out the soul fragment from his mouth, declaring smugly.-"Just like two hundred years ago when I made that silly girl sign a master-servant contract with me, this current girl's life is actually in the palm of my hoof right now!"

—"...Ha?"-Zhou Hui paused, turning his gaze toward Yuwen Xuan.-"What did you say you did to someone else's daughter?"

Only now did the little black lamb realize he had become far too carried away in his smugness.

He took two slow, cautious steps backward, gazing in utter despair as his master once again tightened his grip on that peach branch switch.

[—"Ying-ying!"](<# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan (Black Lamb) / Literal CN: 嘤嘤 — desperate, terrified bleating">)

[Master, I don't dare anymore!]

[My bum hasn't even healed yet!]
