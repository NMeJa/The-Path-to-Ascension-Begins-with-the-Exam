# Chapter 242: Gathering Spirit

Regrettably, the disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were unaware that someone capable of directly [summoning Master on speed dial](# "Slang: 摇人 (yáo rén) — calling in backup / ringing someone up") was standing right beside them. Or rather, even if they had known, the disciples had no desire to trouble Master.

Aside from the remorse of having dragged Master through repeated cycles of reincarnation, there was, even more so, the burning hatred to claim vengeance with their own hands.

At least, such was the case for Lu Shulong.

For this reason, he could temporarily set everything else aside, darting off in a single breath to find Guan Ye as Ran Deng had requested.

Truth be told, his Fourth Senior Brother was rather difficult to track down.

Although they had entered the "illusion realm" together, unlike Lu Shulong and Mo Bei—who directly slaughtered demons to farm experience points—Guan Ye possessed little interest in honing needless combat techniques. He only cared about sharpening his professional skills. As one of the top [hyper-involution grinders](# "Slang: 卷王 (juǎnwáng) — king of involution; an ultra-competitive workaholic / overachiever") among the Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples, Guan Ye naturally had an exhaustive "study plan" for this. Consequently, rather than mindlessly farming at the Ascension Stairway or White Jade Capital like everyone else, he had flown straight toward the depths of the Abyssal Ocean, where demonic qi roiled like thunderclouds.

That said, Guan Ye's physical body was still back in Water Mirror Tower, so he hadn't exactly been unreachable before. However, now that Qu Qianxing had violently severed the spatial connection between the two dimensions, Guan Ye and Mo Bei's divine souls were stranded on this side, plunged into a state of total communication blackout.

Thinking of this, Lu Shulong's teeth itched with resentment:

—"I really want Master to hang Second Senior Brother from the tree for once..."-Lu Shulong grumbled through gritted teeth.

Fortunately, Lu Shulong was not entirely without clues. He hadn't entered the "illusion realm" in his true body initially, but had instead piloted a bone dragon calculated by the trial system. In the "future" Mo Bei had witnessed, this bone dragon had been forged by Guan Ye himself, establishing a profound resonance between them. So long as Lu Shulong utilized it properly, tracking Guan Ye down with a bit of effort was entirely feasible.

Employing this method, Lu Shulong once again possessed the bone dragon and soared toward the depths of the Abyssal Ocean.

Whether because the bond between a ghost-refining master and his bone dragon was exceedingly intimate, or because Lu Shulong and Guan Ye shared ample tacit understanding as martial brothers—Lu Shulong preferred to believe the latter—not long after entering the Abyssal Ocean's territory, Lu Shulong spotted a white banner with purple glyphs rustling briskly in the wind: a Ten Thousand Demon Banner. Beneath the banner lay the corpses of two or three demons.

With a single glance, Lu Shulong recognized that the forging technique of this Ten Thousand Demon Banner was unmistakably the handiwork of his most familiar Fourth Senior Brother.

—"Looks like I found the right place,"-Lu Shulong breathed a sigh of relief. Yet gazing at the demon corpses not far off, his brow furrowed once more.-"Still, the demonic qi around here is terribly dense. I just hope no brainless demons came looking for trouble with Fourth Senior Brother."

Still, finding the first banner was enough.

Among all manner of magical artifacts, banners served as tools for deploying area-of-effect spells; a solitary banner was rarely potent on its own. They were typically deployed in sets of several, dozens, or even hundreds and thousands to form large-scale regional formations. Thus, finding a single banner meant one could find the rest, and in turn locate the banner's master.

Moreover, unlike a formation master like Ran Deng—who might lay an array to kill foes, defend territory, or simply serve as garden decor that improved courtyard air quality—an artifact refiner like Guan Ye employed formations with singular purposes and methods: strictly to aid in refining.

Take this Ten Thousand Demon Banner Lu Shulong had discovered: standing briskly here, it continuously refined the surrounding demonic qi to expand its own power, resonating in tandem with distant banners of the same set to convert the entire region into a colossal refining furnace. Consequently, by tracing the currents of demonic qi, locating the heart of the array was effortless.

Lu Shulong already had pressing business with Fourth Senior Brother and was now anxious about Guan Ye's condition. Naturally, he didn't dawdle, accelerating along the flow of demonic qi straight toward the center of the array.

With his bearings accurate, he smoothly discovered the second banner, the third, the fourth... Yet as he drew ever closer to the center of the refining array, Lu Shulong felt no relief at all; instead, his heart climbed higher and higher into his throat.

For no other reason than: far too many had died.

Lu Shulong referred to demon corpses.

Of course, an abundance of demons in the Abyssal Ocean was only natural.

Ten thousand years ago, the primary demonic eyes had opened within the Abyssal Ocean, causing half of it to become demonized. So long as the concentration of demonic qi was high enough, new demons would randomly spawn; no matter how many one slaughtered, they could never be eradicated. This was precisely why Lu Shulong had worried Guan Ye might be in peril.

Yet judging by the scene before him, the wretched ones were clearly the demons.

If the vicinity of the first banner had only one or two demon corpses, then with each subsequent banner discovered, the surrounding corpses multiplied exponentially: four or five within the second banner's perimeter, sixteen or seventeen around the third, hundreds near the fourth... Near the center of the array, the demonic qi had actually thinned out, practically swept clean. In theory, the air should have turned fresh; correspondingly, however, the originally white fabric of the banners was drenched in blood to the point of blackening, looking as though droplets of gore were about to drip down. The demon corpses were stacked into literal mountains and seas of flesh—nothing about it appeared fresh in the slightest.

[Is this truly Fourth Senior Brother?]

Lu Shulong's eyelids twitched uncontrollably. Yet the Ten Thousand Demon Banners were undeniably Guan Ye's handiwork, and with the situation so bizarre, he couldn't possibly leave things unattended. Steeling himself, he arrived at the center of the array.

Guan Ye was standing right there.

He looked no different from usual: standing slightly hunched, dragging his tail-like crimson spine behind him, his eyes fixed with rapt focus on the magical artifact he was forging, as though nothing in the universe could distract him.

Yet Lu Shulong's eyelids twitched once more. For reasons he couldn't name, something felt terribly off.

Before Lu Shulong could ponder further, however, the main banner at the center of the ground emitted a warning hum at his approach, interrupting Guan Ye's movements.

Guan Ye turned around, his eyes flickering like ghostly flames.

—"Xiao Long?"-he called out.

—"F-Fourth Senior Brother,"-Lu Shulong greeted dryly, attempting to locate the source of his unease.-"You handled all these demons...? Ah, speaking of which, why are you in your original body's form?"

That was it! In this "illusion realm," most of the direct disciples had entered manifesting the boss forms from the "worst possible future" calculated by the trial system. For example, Lu Shulong was a bone dragon, while Guan Ye was supposed to be a half-human, half-ghost ghost-refiner whose appearance looked almost as miserable as a bone dragon, rather than this form nearly identical to his original body.

Lu Shulong felt he had found the reason for his unease.

—"Working in this form is much easier than a bag of bones, and the tactile feel is more familiar,"-Guan Ye replied quite normally.-"Besides, with the cultivation base simulated here, shifting one's appearance into anything is effortless."

—"Ghost-refining can even reshape a physical body? Amazing!"-Lu Shulong said cautiously.-"And those demons were all killed by you as well?"

—"More or less,"-Guan Ye nodded indifferently.-"Though it actually doesn't have much to do with ghost-refining arts. While ghost arts excel at slaughter, the reason the Ten Thousand Demon Banners achieved this effect is primarily that we already had the baseline data—remember? Master once had us monitor the demonic qi metrics in the Abyssal Ocean and built a demonic qi flow model based on it. Combining that model with ghost arts yielded lethality far beyond my expectations, creating a demonic qi vacuum in one fell swoop. Given a few weeks more, I should be able to thoroughly seal the demonic eye here, permanently cutting off the regeneration of demonic qi."

—"Oh, I see! So what's amazing isn't the ghost arts, but Fourth Senior Brother!"-Lu Shulong fawned obediently.

Still, he finally breathed a little easier.

With this tone of voice, this was undoubtedly his Fourth Senior Brother; at least he hadn't been possessed by some bizarre entity.

Oblivious to Lu Shulong's worry, Guan Ye looked at him quizzically:

—"What about you? Why did you suddenly come running over? Didn't you say you wanted to fight demons? And why did your true body come over as well?"

Lu Shulong's true body was naturally present.

To track down Qu Qianxing, he had charged all the way into higher-dimensional space; naturally, when descending, he came in the flesh. Thus, Lu Shulong had shifted back into human form, seated atop the bone dragon's back. Coupled with his cool, melancholic aura, he looked like a sovereign demon monarch.

Regrettably, before his Master, senior brothers, and senior sisters, Lu Shulong had always behaved like a meek white pony rather than an august white dragon, lacking even an ounce of True Dragon presence.

Now, he finally remembered his original reason for coming:

—"Ah, Fourth Senior Brother, listen to me! Second Senior Brother, he..."

Lu Shulong rapidly recounted everything that had transpired earlier, liberally seasoning the narrative with a mountain of personal grievances.

Guan Ye listened in complete silence.

Yet as Lu Shulong's tale unfolded, Guan Ye's already expressionless face grew increasingly somber. His eyes turned murky and indistinct, flickering like trembling ghostly flames, blending seamlessly with the surrounding mountains of demon corpses and seas of blood. He looked for all the world like a native arch-demon capable of devouring an entire classroom of children.

Only after Lu Shulong finished speaking did Guan Ye let out two chilling sneers:

—"Jie-jie... Has he finally shown his face?"

His tone sounded exactly like a terrifying demonic cultivator who, after capturing his prey, intended to perform live vivisection while keeping the victim conscious.

By all rights, Lu Shulong was the person in the entire world most accustomed to Guan Ye's peculiar laugh, yet cold sweat still broke out across his back. The heart he had just settled climbed right back into his throat. He couldn't shake the feeling that something about Guan Ye's reaction and that sentence was profoundly wrong.

Even though he wasn't opposed in the slightest to vivisecting the mastermind behind the scenes.

Paying no heed to Lu Shulong's reaction, Guan Ye continued to confirm:

—"So the situation now is that Eldest Senior Sister has decided to pool all available power against the enemy, and she needs me to assist her in calculating the exact coordinates of the destiny threads while laying out an array to transmit the power?"

—"Y-Yes?"-Lu Shulong replied, somewhat hesitantly.

—"Good! Then let's get to work!"-Guan Ye nodded crisply, voicing zero personal thoughts on the matter, as straightforward as ever.

He was the epitome of a man of action. Even as he nodded, the unidentified contraption he was crafting in his hands clicked into motion, emitting a radiant glow that expanded outward. In tandem with the array's spreading light, the Ten Thousand Demon Banners gave off a soft purple halo in resonance. The two illuminations converged in midair, churning the ambient spiritual energy, demonic qi, and other forces to construct a colossal magic array across the sky.

Lu Shulong watched dumbly, taking a long moment to process the sight:

—"Eh? Starting right now? Senior Sister hasn't even finished preparing on her end..."

—"No matter. She prepares hers, and we prepare ours,"-Guan Ye continued adjusting the mechanism in his hands.-"She is still far too gentle—always willing to exert at most three tenths of her strength when she has ten. In a matter like this, how could the meager strength of the Immortal Arts Assembly's participants ever suffice? If the real world cannot be mobilized, we must at least harness the ten thousand years of accumulated resentment within this illusion realm. Only then can we make the mastermind feel genuine pain!"

—"I don't think Eldest Senior Sister holds back out of gentleness. She's just..."

Lu Shulong instinctively started to roast her, but as Guan Ye's words echoed in his mind, realization struck him like lightning. He finally understood why everything felt so terribly wrong.

His hair standing on end, Lu Shulong stared at Guan Ye in sheer horror:

—"F-Fourth Senior Brother, you... you've regained your memories?!"

This was Lu Shulong's absolute worst nightmare.

Even more terrifying than Qu Qianxing regaining his memories.

If Lu Shulong disliked Qu Qianxing recovering his memories out of fear that his senior brother would settle accounts over their third-cycle grievances, then regarding Guan Ye recovering his memories, Lu Shulong's reaction was pure, unadulterated psychological trauma.

Who asked Guan Ye to have [wiped out the entire sect](# "Gaming Slang: 团灭 (tuánmiè) — total party kill / wiped out the entire group") that one time?!

Heaven only knew how the others viewed that incident, but for Lu Shulong, the blow had been devastating.

It was roughly equivalent to waking up in the dead of night to discover that your best bro had turned into a vengeful ghoul, standing rigidly at the head of your bed with a dripping, bloody cleaver, quietly sizing you up to figure out the best spot to hack into your flesh.

Between that sheer terror and the dread of Master discovering one had multiple lifetimes of memories and deciding to make one make up three thousand years of backlogged homework, it was hard to say which was more horrifying.

Noticing Lu Shulong's terror, Guan Ye grinned, baring his teeth:

—"Jie-jie... Does it matter whether I've regained my memories? Could it be that you did something unspeakable that shouldn't be remembered?"

That smile looked every bit like a psychopathic mass murderer.

—"N-No, absolutely not!"-Lu Shulong backed away several paces in fright.

—"...There's really no need to look so guilty,"-Guan Ye sighed at Lu Shulong's panicked state.-"Rest easy. What you did has already been written off. I won't settle accounts with you a second time."

[That's not the point at all...] Was he afraid of settling accounts? He was terrified of his Fourth Senior Brother suddenly turning into a bloodthirsty fiend!

Lu Shulong thought this inwardly, but dared not voice it aloud.

In truth, there was no need for him to say it. As the most earnest, upright disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Guan Ye was already reflecting on his own misdeeds:

—"Besides, in the fourth cycle, the fault was predominantly mine. First, forgetting the bonds of brotherhood over grievances of a past life was already a grave taboo. Second, failing to consider Master's feelings and arbitrarily purging the sect—forcing Master to expend his power to rewind time once more—was an unforgivable crime..."

—"Fourth Senior Brother!"-Lu Shulong cried.

—"No matter. When this is over, I'll go [carry the bramble and plead for punishment](# "Idiom: 负荆请罪 (fùjīng-qǐngzuì) — carrying thorned brambles on one's bare back to beg for forgiveness; offering a humble, contrite apology") before Master!"-Guan Ye offered a bitter, self-deprecating smile.

Lu Shulong frowned. He felt that the disciple Master least wished to see recover his memories was precisely Guan Ye—because it was simply too agonizing!

Guan Ye, however, possessed zero self-awareness regarding this.

He had recovered his memories upon entering the "God-Demon War" dungeon instance... No, as early as when he learned of the "worst possible future" from Mo Bei, there had already been signs of Guan Ye's memories loosening. It was just that Lu Shulong had been present at the time and secretly devoured his soul-threads. After entering the God-Demon battlefield, however, whether it was the methods of ghost-refining or the hauntingly familiar battlefield itself, every single stimulus had steadily eroded the locks on Guan Ye's memories.

At the same time, it had enticed Guan Ye toward demonic corruption.

Especially the memories of the sixth cycle.

In the sixth cycle, to avenge the sect, Guan Ye had stopped at nothing, modifying his own body in exchange for mechanism arts, yet had never even caught a glimpse of the mastermind's shadow. He had merely watched, step by step, as the entire world was corroded by demonic qi, transforming into something virtually identical to this ten-thousand-year-old illusion realm of the God-Demon battlefield. In the end, the mechanism arts he had painstakingly comprehended were seized by the so-called "protagonist." Even Guan Ye himself considered that lifetime exceptionally tragic.

No Master, no sect, no future—only hatred and obsession.

Compared to that lifetime, the fratricidal carnage of the third and fourth cycles felt almost like ordinary sibling roughhousing when Master wasn't around to discipline them, positively brimming with warmth and tenderness (Lu Shulong: *...*).

Guan Ye had inevitably retained immense resentment from that lifetime. That hatred was engraved onto the deepest bedrock of his soul; no matter how many times Zhou Hui rewound time, he could never wash that agony and malice completely clean.

And when Guan Ye entered the near-demonic realm of the Abyssal Ocean, that resentment had become his fatal weakness.

After all, amidst demonic qi, even the most cheerful optimist would develop negative emotions of dread and anxiety if submerged for too long, to say nothing of an entity like Guan Ye, who was already steeped in bitter grievances. All along the way, hauntingly familiar voices and phantasms had assailed him, nearly dragging him down into the frigid abyss of the sixth cycle's memories to be lost forever.

Fortunately, at the critical juncture, the spiritual energy channeled from the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array (Master) had guided him.

Not only did that influx of spiritual energy grant Guan Ye greater strength to withstand the demonic qi's erosion, but the familiar sensation also revitalized his spirit, pulling him back from the vortex of dreadful memories and reminding him that the "present life" was what mattered most. Indeed: in this lifetime, Master was still here, martial brothers and sisters had not turned against one another, and everything in the Heavenly Spirit Sect was thriving. What excuse did he have to drown in the grievances of the past?

However! Refusing to drown in the past did not equate to forgiving the hatred!

Especially when clues regarding the enemy had appeared before his eyes [so fresh and vivid](# "Internet Slang: 水灵灵 (shuǐlínglíng) — fresh and juicy; Chinese internet slang used ironically for something shockingly conspicuous or blatantly dropped right in front of someone")!

Purple luminescence flickered within Guan Ye's eyes as he looked up toward the sky veiled in demonic qi. Even though the area was draped in murky gray demonic mist—making it virtually impossible to see the sky or the Ascension Stairway leading to the Heavenly Court—Guan Ye had already pinpointed exactly where his enemy was located. If only for the futile agony of that sixth cycle, he had no intention of letting that culprit off.

—"Since you're here, help me out,"-Guan Ye said, tearing his gaze away to instruct Lu Shulong.

—"Okay!"-Lu Shulong answered with instant obedience, only then asking,-"Fourth Senior Brother, what do you need me to do? Perfect this array? Come to think of it, what on earth are you forging here?"

—"A demonic qi conduit,"-Guan Ye replied.-"Do you remember? Back when Master sought to channel the chaotic spiritual energy of the Central Domain, he constructed a spiritual energy levee and had us collect copious amounts of demonic qi data from beneath the Abyssal Ocean to prevent subterranean turbulence from causing a crisis. It's just that Master is too powerful, so nothing has ever gone wrong. That makes this 'illusion realm' exceptionally rare. Even though it portrays events from ten thousand years ago, it also simulates the worst possible state our world could face. So I originally planned to seize this opportunity to simulate how to construct a demonic qi canal to channel and purify demonic qi, which would also let me write a paper on the subject."

[...Did the other martial brothers and sisters know about this plan?]

[Why in the world are you guys so hyper-competitive?!]

Lu Shulong stared at Guan Ye in mounting horror, feeling that compared to this, his earlier self—who had been merrily slaughtering monsters a few hours ago—was a total idiot.

Guan Ye continued:

—"It's just that an 'unexpected emergency' has arisen. Rather than purifying it, concentrating the demonic qi and supplying it as raw power to Second Senior Brother is naturally far better. That fellow is still the 'Demon Sovereign,' right?"

—"Concentrate demonic qi?! Is that even possible?"-Lu Shulong blurted out.

—"Jie-jie, I'm currently at my absolute peak!"-Guan Ye said with rare, overflowing confidence.-"I am both the supreme ghost-refiner and the possessor of all the accumulated knowledge across multiple reincarnations, along with all the resentment gathered over the cycles..."

Guan Ye gazed into the heavens with venomous eyes, his voice raspy and cold:

—"Since he desires to plunge this realm and every living being upon it into the abyss, then let him taste the full weight of it all!"

\* \* \*

—"Such immense power!"

On the other side, Ran Deng gasped in shock, staring at the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array.

Rays of light blazed from within the formation like a miniature sun rising slowly into the sky. The piercing radiance threatened to blind anyone who looked directly at it, and the spilling spiritual energy was potent enough to obliterate any creature that drew near. Fortunately, the capacity of this Mountain-Protecting Grand Array had been engineered with an upper limit capable of storing several times Zhou Hui's total power, allowing it to contain every single drop of accumulated spiritual energy. Otherwise, with Ran Deng and Mo Xi's modest cultivations, handling such a titanic volume of spiritual energy would have been fundamentally impossible.

At this moment, Ran Deng had no inkling of what "surprise" her Fourth Junior Brother was preparing on the other side. This influx was merely the spiritual energy Mo Xi had harvested from the guests attending the Immortal Arts Assembly, yet this amount alone already left Ran Deng deeply uneasy.

Glaring at Mo Xi, she demanded:

—"Where did all this spiritual energy come from?! Just what did you tell our 'guests'?!"

After all, while numerous cultivators had attended the Immortal Arts Assembly—including no shortage of high-tier powerhouses from Northern Domain prominent sects—judging by how blatantly these "grand figures" had slacked off during the high-tier exhibition bouts, they were clearly unwilling to expend significant strength on a "trivial tournament."

Thus, even Ran Deng, who had hatched the idea, had estimated that gathering the equivalent of a single Tribulation Transcendence titan's power for Qu Qianxing—not Master's caliber, of course, but roughly on par with Ren Ran—would already be miraculous, and that was only feasible because the mountain grand array was continuously pumping out energy.

Ran Deng had never expected external cultivators to return all the energy they had drawn from the mountain array, let alone part with a major fraction of their own reserves.

Yet the energy accumulated right now could only have been amassed if the vast majority had emptied their pockets!

—"I just spoke normally!"-Mo Xi said.-"I told them it was part of the Immortal Arts Assembly's event. I could hardly announce that we were declaring war on the Heavenly Court, could I? If I actually said that, I was afraid they'd gift our Heavenly Spirit Sect to the Heavenly Court on a silver platter first!"

Yet given how little faith Mo Xi had in human nature, he was clearly just as unsettled by such an overwhelming influx of spiritual energy. As such, he had investigated the underlying reasons while collecting it:

—"Still, the mental state of several titans is rather peculiar."

—"Peculiar?"-Ran Deng asked.

—"Yeah. I don't know if the trial system's impact was too profound, but they seem to genuinely believe the demon race is going to invade,"-Mo Xi replied, frowning.

He waved his hand, and the water mirror displayed several clips of "surveillance footage."

Naturally, this "surveillance footage" came strictly from public areas.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect wasn't foolish enough to plant monitoring artifacts in private quarters; that would be an invitation to offend everyone. Moreover, when Zhou Hui was around, no such artifacts were needed anyway—with a sweep of his divine sense, he knew what every ant within a thousand li of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was doing.

Regrettably, Mo Xi did not yet possess such terrifying prowess. So when he felt something was amiss, he could only pull up public surveillance feeds, attempting to divine the hidden reasons behind the crowd's movements.

As it turned out, what resonated from the monitoring artifact first was Mo Xi's own broadcast voice:

> "Distinguished participants and audience members! Thanks to your relentless struggle and dedication, the 'Trial: God-Demon Battlefield' has entered its climactic final battle. We firmly believe that the warriors holding the frontline will achieve ultimate victory—dawn is within sight! Those warriors who have already exited need not despair: you may proceed to the various public squares across the Immortal Arts Assembly to spectate and utilize the plaza arrays to send reinforcement to the fighters you support! Naturally, the Immortal Arts Assembly administration will also distribute material resupply packages to all warriors who participated in the trial and provided reinforcement. Details can be viewed on the announcement boards. This is your final and largest opportunity to obtain combat items in this tournament. A fleeting chance never returns—give it your all, everyone~~!"

Hearing this deadpan monotone recitation, Ran Deng's expression grew exceedingly subtle:

—"What in the world is this nonsense?"

—"Isn't this what we agreed on?"-Mo Xi said gloomily.-"Since we couldn't disclose the truth, we could only use the pretext of trial reward distributions to trick... *cough*, convince everyone to 'voluntarily' surrender their spiritual energy. Naturally, we have to provide a massive pile of 'rewards.' Looking at the ledgers, we might even take a loss! What's most infuriating is that I had to advance this sum from my own private vault! Heaven knows whether Second Senior Brother will reimburse me in the end—even though this is for his sake, I suspect he'll be completely [unresponsive to tears and bribes](# "Idiom: 油盐不进 (yóuyán-bùjìn) — impervious to oil and salt; obstinate / impossible to persuade"). This might end up coming entirely out of my own pocket..."

[...Actually, what I meant was, couldn't you have read that script with at least a shred of emotion? Make it sound like an authentic tournament procedure so we could scam... cough, convince even more people?]

Ran Deng wanted to roast him, but seeing Mo Xi's mournful look and remembering that this little miser had actually dipped into his own private vault—not to mention that the trial system's debugging and scriptwriting had all fallen on Mo Xi's shoulders—demanding authentic acting chops on top of that felt a bit excessive.

Ran Deng sighed, hoping only that Mo Xi's deadpan script-reading wouldn't arouse the participants' suspicions and cause them to catch on.

As it turned out, Ran Deng had overthought it.

Evidently, the vast majority of participants and spectators demanded no acting flair from the tournament announcer. If anything, after enduring such a brutal defense campaign, Mo Xi's flat monotone was just what they needed to break free from the suffocating "realism" of the battlefield and return to the safe confines of the Immortal Arts Assembly.

At least, such was the case for low-tier students like Jing Chenjun.

—"...Why does it sound like a game?"-Jing Chenjun murmured in a daze, looking up.

He attempted to glance toward the transmission artifact broadcasting the war bulletin, but that simple movement made darkness swim before his eyes. Fortunately, his worried roommate beside him caught his arm.

Supporting Jing Chenjun, Cheng Ziyi asked nervously:

—"Are you alright?"

—"I'm fine..."-Jing Chenjun waved a hand at Cheng Ziyi, straightening his posture.

Seeing that he was truly steady, his roommates breathed a sigh of relief.

Huang Xiao, the gentlest of Jing Chenjun's roommates, puffed his cheeks and glared with bean-like eyes:

—"It *was* a game to begin with—just an entertainment side-event for the Immortal Arts Assembly! You guys took it far too seriously, immersing yourselves way too deeply! Especially you, Jing Chenjun—you're clearly a low-tier student, yet you held out so long before exiting that you ended up in this state! Going too far is as bad as falling short; this will affect your future cultivation!"

—"My apologies,"-Jing Chenjun smiled wryly.

He and Cheng Ziyi exchanged glances, both offering helpless, wry grins.

Truth be told, neither of them understood why they had been so fiercely obsessed with resisting the demon hordes, to the point of forgetting that this was merely a tournament activity—an entertainment side-event. Yet they had fought as though genuinely standing against an apocalyptic invasion, determined to hold out until the very moment of annihilation. As if failing to do so would leave some ancient regret forever unavenged.

Yet in the end, they were merely low-tier disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Forget them; even cultivators of middle-tier realms like Golden Core, Nascent Soul, and Void Refinement had been forcefully "logged off" the instant celestial soldiers and divine generals tore open the sky—thanks to which they didn't have to worry about being stranded in the "illusion realm." Still, exiting in such a manner left a hollow void in Jing Chenjun's heart. Hearing Mo Xi's announcement, he had barely been able to register the words, only able to listen helplessly to Huang Xiao's nagging.

In the end, it was Ji Gen, the roommate with the least presence in the dorm, who interrupted Huang Xiao's lecture:

—"Alright, alright. Since we've already exited, why don't we head to the plaza too?"

—"Go again?!"-Huang Xiao squeaked.

—"We at least have to claim the final rewards, otherwise wasn't all our bitter holding out for nothing?"-Ji Gen winked at Jing Chenjun as he pushed open the dormitory door.

Huang Xiao opened his mouth to nag further, only to be nearly blown backward by the roar of noise from outside.

Only then did Huang Xiao and the others realize that following this deadpan event announcement, practically everyone had mobilized!

Whether brooding over grievances tangled in accumulated past-life obsessions like Jing Chenjun, or like Huang Xiao, who harbored no lingering pain in his soul; whether genuinely invested in the tournament and trial system, or simply tagging along with the crowd for the excitement—in short, the moment people heard the broadcast, they surged toward their nearest public squares. Even the top-tier titans made appearances, causing the tournament thoroughfares—engineered by the Heavenly Spirit Sect to accommodate several times the actual capacity—to become completely gridlocked.

—"Isn't this a bit exaggerated?!"-Huang Xiao gasped in shock, watching the congested avenue where they could barely advance an inch. A sudden chuckle escaped him.-"*Pfft*, with this many people, even if demons actually invaded, we wouldn't need to fear, right?"

—"If sheer numbers could win, we wouldn't have met such a wretched end,"-Cheng Ziyi replied dejectedly. As a "sword cultivator" who had formally practiced swordsmanship for barely a year, his user experience inside the trial system had been abysmal.-"Against those invaders, I felt no different from an insect."

Indeed. Before celestial soldiers and demonic hordes, cultivators and mortals alike were no different from insects.

Such were Jing Chenjun's thoughts, yet he felt no despair—if he had despaired, he wouldn't have held out until the very end. On the contrary, he felt he had at least been of some use, and [pushing his luck](# "Idiom: 得寸进尺 (décùn-jìnrǐ) — give an inch and take a mile / pushing one's luck"), an even bolder thought took root: precisely because they were "all the same," perhaps when the worst-case scenario truly arrived, "mortals" could achieve the exact same feats as cultivators? Could equally defend their own world?

Harboring this conviction, Jing Chenjun murmured as though speaking to himself:

—"There's no need to think that way right now. After all, we're only in our second year. Yet even now, we were able to be of some use, weren't we? Then what about several years, or decades from now...?"

—"Right!"-Cheng Ziyi instantly perked up, displaying the straightforward mindset befitting a sword cultivator.-"Cultivation is a long journey to begin with! Just because I can't do it now doesn't mean I won't be able to decades or centuries from now!"

Mortals couldn't live for centuries... but that wasn't necessarily set in stone!

Although those without spirit roots were all mortals, cultivators often granted tiny "perks" to mortal royalty and aristocracy like Jing Chenjun to streamline their rule; a lifespan longer than common mortals was one such privilege.

And precisely because these rulers enjoyed exceptionally long lifespans, mortal society, much like the cultivation world, saw virtually zero fundamental change across centuries, millennia, or tens of millennia. This was precisely the outcome cultivators desired: they had no wish to emerge from a decades- or centuries-long seclusion, take a stroll in the mortal world, and find every familiar thing gone. Thus, most long-lived entities, whether cultivators or True Immortals, leaned toward keeping the realms and domains under their governance in a state of eternal stagnation.

Conversely, since such life-extending techniques existed, the premise for popularizing them existed as well, didn't it? If mortals could master skills and techniques that required no spiritual energy—just as Jing Chenjun had—they too could be of tremendous use. And without question, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was striving precisely toward this goal!

In some obscure, fated way, it seemed he had come to the absolute right place!

Jing Chenjun thought in a daze, his spirit—previously weighed down by the gloom of the God-Demon War—finally revitalizing.

It was also at this moment that Huang Xiao cried out in surprise:

—"Jing Chenjun, why is your storage pouch glowing?!"

—"Storage pouch?"-Jing Chenjun touched his pouch blankly.

A cultivator's storage pouch was a manifestation of spatial magic. Items placed inside existed within a separate, static pocket of space and typically underwent no change, let alone glowing so intensely that light bled into the outside world.

Yet the brand-new pouch Jing Chenjun had purchased during this Immortal Arts Assembly was currently radiating dazzling luminescence.

Flustered, Jing Chenjun pulled open the pouch, only to discover that the glowing object was none other than the imperial jade seal he had obtained at the start of the academic year when choosing his cultivation track! Back then, he had panicked slightly, fearing his identity as mortal royalty had been exposed and he would be expelled.

Yet the Untiring Immortal Venerable had said that when Jing Chenjun learned how to wield this jade seal, that would be the moment he attained the Dao. However, no matter how much Jing Chenjun racked his brain, he had never deciphered how to use it; he only knew it occasionally exhibited strange reactions toward the students of the White Jade Capital academy. Unable to uncover its operational method, Jing Chenjun had simply resigned himself to stashing it inside his pouch.

Now, however, this jade seal was blazing with light.

—"So this was what was glowing?"-Cheng Ziyi and the others exclaimed in astonishment. As his roommates, they naturally knew Jing Chenjun had been agonizing over how to use this imperial seal.-"Since it's lighting up now, does that mean it's finally recognized you as its master?"

—"It was already mine to begin with,"-Jing Chenjun said, a trace of reluctance in his voice.-"And just like before, I can sense zero operational instructions from the seal."

Yet this time, Jing Chenjun felt no disappointment, for he could sense that for once, the jade seal was reacting directly to him—to his true self. Could it be endorsing the thoughts he had just harbored?

For reasons unknown, Jing Chenjun felt extraordinarily certain.

Ji Gen agreed:

—"Rather than recognizing a master, it feels more like Jing Chenjun generated some form of power."

—"I-Indeed. It seems very powerful,"-Huang Xiao answered tremulously, shrinking behind Ji Gen.-"Though it doesn't seem to be spiritual energy...?"

None of them knew that this radiance was naturally not spiritual energy, nor any ordinary force, but a form of "divine authority."

An "authority" belonging to the Human Sovereign.

As a member of the monster race supplanted by humankind, Huang Xiao's instinctual dread of this "authority" was entirely natural. Fortunately, this authority rested in Jing Chenjun's hands: this youth, who had yet to truly become the Human Sovereign, harbored no hatred toward his demon roommate, wishing only to interact on equal footing in the future. Thus, with a single shift of Jing Chenjun's intent, the radiance's suppression upon the demon race diminished drastically.

Huang Xiao breathed a tiny sigh of relief.

Cheng Ziyi, oblivious to Huang Xiao's emotional fluctuation, asked with a frown:

—"If you don't know how to use it, or even what kind of power it is, what are we supposed to do? Just watch it dissipate into nothing? Ah, or will it keep glowing forever?"

—"...Let's take it to the plaza!"-Jing Chenjun said in a low, resolute voice.

[—"Ah?"](# "Speaker: Cheng Ziyi")

—"Didn't the Immortal Arts Assembly's finale event ask us to supply power?"-Jing Chenjun had already made up his mind. He didn't know what this power was, but he decided to send this radiance (authority) out as a testament to his resolve.-"I suspect this power didn't appear without reason; it's likely connected to the trial system. Since that's the case, we might as well return it. Consider it my final contribution to the 'God-Demon War.'"

[—"Good idea!"](# "Speaker: Cheng Ziyi")

[—"Then let's go! To the plaza."](# "Speaker: Ji Gen")

His roommates voiced their approval, following Jing Chenjun as they merged into the surging crowd heading toward the plaza.

\* \* \*

If low-tier disciples like Jing Chenjun—regardless of whether they carried heavy resolve—merely believed they were contributing to an "event activity" of the Immortal Arts Assembly, then the high-tier titans understood what they were doing far more clearly.

Thus, upon arriving at the plaza reserved exclusively for powerhouses of the Body Integration Stage and above, Villa Master Qingyang remarked without the slightest surprise:

—"*Heh*, quite the full turnout!"

He referred to the titans attending this Immortal Arts Assembly: whether from the Northern Domain, the Central Domain, or elsewhere, approximately ninety percent of these powerhouses had actually arrived on scene!

This was an extraordinarily rare sight.

After all, among them, some had come for the Ascension Stairway, some to assess the Heavenly Spirit Sect and the Untiring Immortal Venerable, and others for magical technique journals and networking with fellow high-tier cultivators. In comparison, the Immortal Arts Assembly itself was merely a polite pretext. Although they had dutifully participated in matches out of respect for the Untiring Immortal Venerable, those were merely exhibition bouts; very few had taken them to heart like Villa Master Qingyang, to say nothing of gathering en masse to compete like low-tier cultivators.

Thus, to assemble this group of high-tier cultivators in one place usually required an occasion personally hosted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself.

Yet today, a mere public broadcast aimed at the general populace had drawn virtually all of them out!

Villa Master Qingyang couldn't help but chuckle:

—"Turns out you were all playing the trial system too!"

Indeed, the trial system... Or rather, the God-Demon War of ten thousand years ago. Only that battlefield could evoke the demonic invasion that was supposed to occur three hundred years from now, but which they had actually lived through in past reincarnation cycles.

That invasion had been so catastrophic that even a mortal like Jing Chenjun harbored emotions that could never be thoroughly erased, to say nothing of these titans who stood merely half a step from Tribulation Transcendence.

Upon entering the trial system, Villa Master Qingyang and Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had recovered their memories one after the other. However, caught between the pincer attacks of celestial soldiers and demon legions, they ultimately hadn't held out until the end—nor was there any need to hold out until the end. Seeing the situation devolve into utter absurdity, and having received Qu Qianxing's private divine transmission urging them to withdraw, the two had departed the battlefield without hesitation.

This was undeniably the correct choice, sparing the two titans from the crisis of being trapped within the illusion realm.

Still, Villa Master Qingyang remained worried about Qu Qianxing's predicament. Hearing the Heavenly Spirit Sect's broadcast announcement, he had decided to drop by the plaza to assess the situation, only to find that nearly every high-tier cultivator had shown up.

Villa Master Qingyang naturally didn't believe these people had gathered purely for the spectacle; he knew in his heart that their circumstances were almost certainly identical to his own.

And indeed, at this moment, these high-tier titans had discarded every shred of their customary composure. Some looked deeply pensive, still drowning in profound reminiscence; some stared blankly, unwilling to accept their own memories; while others paced restlessly, fretting over the present and future of themselves or their sects.

In such a state, upon seeing Villa Master Qingyang, they naturally had zero appetite for banter. Someone demanded directly:

—"Qingyang, just what in the world is going on?!"

—"Don't you know what you remember yourself? What are you asking me for?!"-Villa Master Qingyang retorted.

Someone else attempted to ask in a calmer tone:

—"We didn't mean that. We only wanted to ask what those memories actually are..."

—"Are you certain those are genuine memories?"-a gruff voice interrupted rudely.

Villa Master Qingyang glanced over, discovering the speaker was an elder from a major sect in the Northern Domain. He wasn't an evil person per se, merely sloppy in his conduct, with frequent dealings with Carefree Palace. When confronted with demonic invasions and Heavenly Court conspiracies, where such a person stood was hard to say. Judging by his pale complexion, his personal ending had likely been far from satisfactory, making him reluctant to accept reality.

[Could it possibly be as wretched as mine?!]

[Never mind having my sect exterminated—the daughter of my most pampered granddaughter paid out of pocket to simp, throwing herself and the entire clan away!]

Remembering the past (future) he had glimpsed, Villa Master Qingyang felt his stomach ache. Consequently, he had zero patience to indulge the man, merely sneering:

—"At your level of cultivation, can you not judge for yourself whether it's real? If you harbor doubts, what did you come here for?!"

Seeing Villa Master Qingyang flare up, another elder chuckled with apparent politeness:

—"Venerable Qingyang, you misunderstand. We merely regained our memories all of a sudden and are slightly disoriented. We also do not know what the Immortal Venerable intends with these instructions."

—"..."-Villa Master Qingyang was momentarily struck dumb.

How was he supposed to know what the Untiring Immortal Venerable was thinking?

Although judging by how the Untiring Immortal Venerable had instructed him to research True Immortal Blood, host the Immortal Arts Assembly, and recreate the God-Demon War, Villa Master Qingyang sensed the Immortal Venerable was guarding against the Heavenly Court, he didn't believe openly declaring war on the Heavenly Court in public was wise. Even with memories of having been manipulated in a past life, yearning for the Heavenly Court and the Heavenly Dao was engraved into the very marrow of cultivators—the higher their realm, the deeper that reverence ran. Speaking recklessly might only invite needless trouble upon the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

Moreover, the Untiring Immortal Venerable himself had said nothing. He had organized this tournament casually; the "illusion realm" of the God-Demon battlefield had appeared casually; even the transmission (broadcast) earlier had sounded utterly casual, just like a phase in a real game. Villa Master Qingyang felt that if one claimed the Untiring Immortal Venerable was trying to recruit or rally everyone, that was definitely not the case.

Mulling this over, Villa Master Qingyang remained silent for a moment.

However, regarding the recovery of memories, every cultivator felt differently and naturally harbored different thoughts. It was only because high-tier titans were long unaccustomed to exposing personal secrets, and didn't yet know the status of others, that they had been unable to converse candidly.

Now, with Villa Master Qingyang's pointed retort confirming that everyone was in the exact same boat, and with most people teeming with newly awakened memories and bursting with the urge to express themselves, demanding a definitive answer was no longer necessary.

Thus, without waiting for Villa Master Qingyang to speak, someone else shot back on Qingyang's behalf:

—"What does the Untiring Immortal Venerable intend?! He kindly helped you recover your memories, yet you dare suspect him of harboring ulterior motives?!"

—"I did not mean that..."-the previous elder muttered, looking somewhat sheepish and secretly bewildered. He couldn't fathom why this fellow was so agitated. Even on the Heavenly Spirit Sect's home turf, there was no need to suck up to the Untiring Immortal Venerable so blatantly; that Immortal Venerable was notoriously [impervious to both cajolery and coercion](# "Idiom: 软硬不吃 (ruǎnyìng-bùchī) — eats neither soft nor hard; unyielding / immune to flattery or pressure")—flattery yielded nothing anyway.

What this elder hadn't anticipated was that regarding the recovery of their memories, the vast majority of titans present were, in truth, profoundly grateful.

After all, among the titans attending this Immortal Arts Assembly—setting aside the Central Domain sects that had been wiped out at the very onset of the demonic invasion—most were elders from orthodox, righteous sects of the Northern Domain. Following the fall of the Central Domain, these people had struggled bitterly to halt the demons, yet had still watched their sects crumble into ash in the end. Virtually all of them carried indelible regrets. Now that they had been granted a second chance, how could they not feel gratitude?

Voices rose in succession:

[—"I see! It must be that the Immortal Venerable recovered his memories first and gave us this hint."](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"But how come I remember the Heavenly Spirit Sect perishing very early on in that lifetime?"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"No! It's precisely *because* the Heavenly Spirit Sect perished that it makes sense... Ah, I don't mean it was good they perished, but rather that back in that lifetime, I found it strange why a Tribulation Transcendence titan like the Untiring Immortal Venerable vanished so abruptly! Thinking about it now, he must have divined the heavenly secrets, faked his death to deceive destiny, and rewound time to guide us!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"Are you saying even the time rewinding was done by the Untiring Immortal Venerable?!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"In this entire realm, aside from the Untiring Immortal Venerable, who else could possibly accomplish such a feat?!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"As expected of the Untiring Immortal Venerable!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

The high-tier cultivators clamored back and forth, remarkably deducing the reality of the temporal reincarnation cycles. Regrettably, what they had recovered was merely the memory of the single cycle that had caused them the most searing agony. Consequently, their deductions remained worlds apart from what Zhou Hui had actually done. Even Villa Master Qingyang felt something was off as he listened: although he didn't know the exact causes and effects, having spent considerable time around the Untiring Immortal Venerable, he always felt the Immortal Venerable was not someone driven by self-sacrificing altruism—certainly not enough to rewind time for an irrelevant bunch of high-tier cultivators.

Still, the matter of rewinding time six times solely for his own disciples was something no ordinary person could ever guess in a million years without witnessing it firsthand.

It was hard to imagine what expressions these cultivators would make if they ever learned the truth.

Yet in the face of an existential crisis, the truth was both crucial and entirely irrelevant. When learning of a grim future, people always tended to think toward the best possible interpretation, particularly along angles that flattered themselves; even Mahayana cultivators were not exempt.

They sighed in admiration:

[—"Reflecting carefully, the Untiring Immortal Venerable historically never involved himself in worldly affairs, yet recently he repaired the Ascension Stairway, recruited vast numbers of disciples, founded magic journals to preach the Dao, and now organized this Immortal Arts Assembly. Clearly, it was all to awaken and alert us!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"Untiring Master's righteousness is boundless!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"Indeed! If we are to repel foreign foes, preparations must begin now. There isn't much time left for the young ones to dawdle."](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"In that case, the trials of this Immortal Arts Assembly are truly a stroke of divine genius! Not only did they awaken our memories, but they also tempered the combat awareness of the younger generation. When that 'future' arrives, we will possess the strength to fight rather than being caught unprepared!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

Listening quietly to the discussion, Villa Master Qingyang's eyelids twitched:

[...The future?]

[Was that 'illusion realm' truly just an illusion?]

[Would the demon race and Heavenly Court really wait three hundred years like in their memories?]

Villa Master Qingyang recalled everything inside that "illusion realm." As "representatives" of Mahayana-stage bouts at the Immortal Arts Assembly, he and Fairy of the Sacred Mausoleum had stood at the absolute frontlines, directly facing the descent of celestial soldiers and the onslaught of demon armies. That biting, lethal aura prevented Villa Master Qingyang from deceiving himself like the others.

He felt the situation was vastly more dire, yet since the Untiring Immortal Venerable had communicated nothing, he didn't know how best to help. The only thing he could do was comply with the broadcast.

*Since the Untiring Immortal Venerable asked to consolidate spiritual energy, he naturally has his reasons.*

Villa Master Qingyang pondered. Just as he was about to urge the others to follow suit, the atmosphere had reached such a fever pitch that without him needing to utter a word, someone had already raised a similar question:

[—"Since the Untiring Immortal Venerable created this trial system to temper everyone, but now celestial soldiers and demon hordes are clashing violently and the battlefield is virtually lost, with most people including Qingyang having exited, hasn't this become meaningless?"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

—"Are you dense? Even though it's a trial, it's also a recreation of the ancient God-Demon War ten thousand years ago! How could the very first Immortal Arts Assembly rewrite history? If they won too easily, far from tempering the youth, they'd look down on the Heavenly Court and demon race and think themselves invincible!"-another cultivator countered immediately.-"The current outcome is just right: suffering total defeat and being expelled from the battlefield will spur them to push themselves harder and grow stronger!"

[—"Then why request that everyone contribute their power...?"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"Even though we lost, things must still be brought to a close! We cannot plunge everyone into total despair. Besides, didn't the war end ten thousand years ago as well? At a time like this, we naturally need united persistence and effort, concentrating everyone's strength to seal that chaos! Didn't you see the Untiring Immortal Venerable's second disciple standing right there? He must be the one designated to conclude it all!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"True! To resist external enemies, unity among the major sects is paramount. This is a full-dress rehearsal!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"No wonder power was channeled from the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain grand array earlier! I thought it was a perk for the juniors, but it was likely meant to inspire them as well!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[—"The Untiring Immortal Venerable possesses such profound foresight!"](# "Speaker: High-tier cultivator")

[...What you guys say makes so much sense!]

[I'm almost convinced the Untiring Immortal Venerable actually arranged it this way.]

The corners of Villa Master Qingyang's mouth twitched.

Someone asked:

—"Then should we do it too?"

—"Of course!"-several voices answered simultaneously this time, followed by solemn explanations:-"Not to mention repaying the Untiring Immortal Venerable's grace in rewinding time and awakening our memories, solely for the momentous significance embedded within this trial, we cannot treat it lightly! We must set an example for the younger generation."

As they spoke, someone took the lead, sitting cross-legged in a meditative posture and funneling power into the formation array.

With someone leading the charge, other grateful cultivators sat down one after another, followed by those who had previously hesitated. Soon, three-quarters of the high-tier cultivators were contributing their power. With the atmosphere pushed to such heights, even the high-tier cultivators harboring selfish agendas had no choice but to surrender their spiritual energy:

[To hell with it!] At any rate, they had already absorbed a portion of the energy sent out by the Heavenly Spirit Sect's grand array earlier; they had to at least return that portion, plus a little extra on top. After all, they were figures of status; they couldn't allow rumors to spread that they coveted another sect's power!

Under the influence of these complex motives, the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array of the Heavenly Spirit Sect blazed with radiance far more blinding than ever before.

\* \* \*

—"See? I told you they were acting bizarre!"

Mo Xi said, watching the titans in the water mirror.

Speaking so openly in public without attempting to conceal themselves, these high-tier cultivators had naturally been caught in full view by Mo Xi.

It was just that Mo Xi couldn't fathom their behavior:

—"Babbling about demon invasions, defying the Heavenly Court, and the fate of the future... What on earth did they experience inside that trial?"

[...They gained memories of their past lives. But hasn't Little Junior Brother recovered his memories?]

Ran Deng shot Mo Xi a sidelong glance.

She hadn't accompanied Master to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree and naturally didn't know the deep connection between Mo Xi and that reincarnation cycle. Having died early in that cycle, Ran Deng was even more oblivious to the fact that Mo Xi had once fallen into demonic corruption, prompting his remnant soul to actively shield his true self from regaining memories to prevent self-tainting.

Yet to Ran Deng, this was largely inconsequential.

[Junior brothers and junior sisters are much cuter when they don't remember anything,]-Ran Deng firmly believed.

—"No matter. Master having us construct the trial system inherently had profound meaning. Those titans likely divined something and are merely offering assistance,"-Ran Deng offered an ambiguous explanation.

Mo Xi glanced at Ran Deng, neither confirming nor denying his belief, and simply summoned the program coding interface:

—"In that case, let me add a new feature. It'll keep a record of these generous benefactors, making it convenient for Second Senior Brother to return the favor later."

With a flurry of inputs on his end, colossal leaderboard projections blazed to life across the various public squares, titled the "Merit Contribution Leaderboard." It was further subdivided into the Overall Leaderboard, Realm Leaderboard, Sect Leaderboard, and Regional Leaderboard, ranking cultivators by their cultivation background and the volume of power they contributed as a public "record."

This "record" proved extraordinarily effective.

The moment the leaderboard appeared, the power within the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array multiplied several times over in an instant!

It was now vastly greater even than the power Zhou Hui had funneled out through the mountain array earlier!

—"You're truly too much!"-Ran Deng felt her chest ache in exasperation.-"This amount of spiritual energy is already ridiculous! Why didn't you consider whether I can actually transmit all of it over?!"

—"If there's extra, just leave it inside the mountain grand array. Who would ever complain about having too much?"-Mo Xi beamed mischievously.

Ran Deng rolled her eyes, too lazy to bicker with him, and focused all her concentration on channeling the power across.
