# Chapter 148: The Invaders (第148章 入侵者)

---

—"Heh,"-Qu Qianxing scoffed, glaring daggers at Lu Shulong the moment the words "biological experiment" were uttered.

Lu Shulong returned his glare with an expression of pure, unadulterated innocence.

He hadn't said anything wrong. Biology was, in fact, a mandatory foundational course within the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

That being said, a "dissection class" was considered the pinnacle of advanced curriculum.

After all, the course required handling diverse corpses and severed remains, including those of various monsters and fiendish demons. If one carelessly came into contact with even the slightest trace of such corruption, their very nascent soul could be eroded away.

Only because Lu Shulong was a True Dragon could he remain immune to the vast majority of such contamination.

Consequently, only disciples who had gained admittance into the Alchemy Hall—and subsequently passed rigorous vetting to guarantee their temperament and willpower—were permitted to study the craft in depth.

Still, it wasn't out of the question to teach it to Qingyang Qingyu.

Judging by Villa Master Qingyang's exhaustive research into the True Immortal Blood, his methodologies actually aligned far more closely with Lu Shulong's.

Although Qingyang Qingyu had never systematically undergone such training, the core cultivation arts of Eternal Villa granted her considerable inherent resistance to such impurities.

Arriving at this conclusion, Qu Qianxing let out a cold huff:

—"Fine. Categorizing demons to calculate their demonic energy ratings is indeed a valid methodology. But what happened next? Merely cataloging demons wouldn't have ripped open a spatial fissure. Or did you attempt to capture live specimens, only for some catastrophic abomination to break loose?"

As he spoke, Qu Qianxing's expression turned increasingly grim.

Puncturing a hole in a Demon Realm rift was one thing, but provoking an apex fiend was another entirely! Especially with Master away in the Upper Realm, they couldn't possibly allow Master to return only to find a Second Demonic Incursion devastating the Central Domain!

—"No, nothing like that happened..."-Lu Shulong protested weakly.

—"Senior Brother Qu, you underestimate me far too much,"-Qingyang Qingyu couldn't help but interject, having remained silent until now.-"I may lack practical experience and fall short of perfection, but I still possess baseline combat strength."

Not only did she possess it—she had it in overwhelming abundance.

To be precise, because demons were pouring forth in an endless tide while Qingyang Qingyu lacked a reliable means to track their movement patterns, and because the ones manning the command towers were her clan's low-tier disciples... if one or two high-order fiends slipped through the perimeter, she could hardly expect low-tier disciples to tear high-ranking demons apart with their bare hands, could she?

Consequently, when Qingyang Qingyu designed the layout, she suffered from severe [insufficient firepower anxiety](# "Literal CN: 火力不足恐惧症 (huǒlì bùzú kǒngjùzhèng) — 'firepower deficiency phobia'; a modern military meme describing the pathological urge to hoard overwhelming firepower").

The arsenal of items and deployable "heroes" she prepared had reached excessive, overflowing proportions.

However, just as that seasoned "teaching assistant" Mo Xi had warned her at the very beginning, players—er, cultivators—were quintessential [gluttons for punishment](# "Literal CN: 犯贱 (fànjiàn) — asking for trouble / cheap servility; perverse pleasure in self-inflicted hardship"). If a challenge was made too simple, cultivators would promptly lose all appetite for exploration and combat, finding the entire ordeal utterly flavorless.

At first, Qingyang Qingyu hadn't quite believed it. How could anyone possibly dislike reduced difficulty? She had even conducted a dedicated trial run, only to find that the [poor sucker](# "Literal CN: 冤种 (yuānzhǒng) — wronged sucker / chump") disciples shouted "So satisfying!" as they slaughtered everything in sight—and then logged off and left far earlier than usual.

Armed with this experience, even though Qingyang Qingyu had crafted items far superior to anything the current demonic wave could endure, she didn't provide them outright. Instead, she dumped them into the "In-Game Shop," requiring the disciples commanding the battle to redeem them using spirit stones.

Did the disciples of Eternal Villa lack spirit stones? Naturally not.

The result: after a handful of testing cycles, Qingyang Qingyu discovered that she had fully recouped the spiritual energy expenditure incurred while fabricating those items!

In an instant, a door to a whole new world swung wide open before Qingyang Qingyu.

She had attained an enlightened epiphany regarding secret realm game design.

This time was no exception. While Qingyang Qingyu found dissecting demonic abominations thoroughly revolting, she had never lacked the spirit of self-sacrifice for the sake of cultivation—or rather, academic scholarship. Not only did she give the proposal serious consideration, she even planned to integrate the research into her secret realm framework.

—"Capturing live demons for dissection..."-Qingyang Qingyu had mused at the time.-"How exactly should we go about it?"

Seeing her hesitation, Jian Nuo had immediately volunteered in a bright, chirpy cadence:

—"...Allow me! It sounds quite intriguing. I haven't tried capturing live demons before!"

Had his facial expression not been so utterly ghastly, his offer might have sounded somewhat convincing.

Naturally, Jian Nuo had no desire to capture or dissect demonic creatures.

He had even begun suspecting that Lu Shulong's True Dragon bloodline had already been corrupted under the True Immortal's curse, and that he was now intentionally steering them toward dreadful depravities.

Yet without hard evidence, Jian Nuo was unwilling to disrupt Qingyu's academic pursuit, leaving him no choice but to throw himself onto the frontline.

Alas, Qingyu completely failed to appreciate his painstaking sacrifice:

—"There is no need,"-she had replied.-"According to Junior Brother Shulong's explanation, captured specimens should be as fresh and pristine as possible. If you were sent out to hunt them down by hand, combat would be inevitable. That would inflict physical damage on the demons—or kill them outright—which would defeat the entire purpose of specimen collection."

[...Why do I feel like you care more about the demons' personal safety than mine?!]

Jian Nuo had stared at Qingyang Qingyu with wide, disbelieving eyes.

—"By comparison, it would be far better to integrate trap items directly into combat encounters,"-Qingyang Qingyu had continued.-"I can modify the map. Whenever a 'Commander' triggers a trap, the demons will drop into cages beneath freshly excavated trenches, delivering pristine, fresh specimens right to us... Though that lacks operational flexibility. Perhaps it would be better to place map-editing tools into the shop as well? That way, the 'Commanders' can excavate trenches routing the demons straight toward the testing grounds. Hmm, and adding a Demon Bestiary should boost their collection enthusiasm."

—"Q-Qingyu?"-Jian Nuo had stammered.

Listening to Qingyang Qingyu murmur a deluge of jargon he could barely comprehend, Jian Nuo had felt a wave of genuine horror wash over him.

[Why does it feel like Qingyu has been assimilated by Lu Shulong and turned thoroughly sinister?!]

*(Lu Shulong: [If anyone assimilated her, it was Mo Xi, that fake cat, alright?!])*

And so, the very next day, the Qingyang clan disciples who arrived at the Spirit Palace for the [closed beta test](# "Literal CN: 内测 (nèicè) — closed beta testing") were pleasantly surprised to discover that the secret realm system had received an update, introducing an entirely new gameplay loop.

The objective was to capture demons using traps and route them to designated recovery zones.

Everyone enthusiastically began playing around—er, exterminating fiendish invaders.

Initially, Qingyang Qingyu had supervised them for a spell. Seeing that their enthusiasm was largely consistent with prior sessions, she didn't linger. Instead, she accompanied Lu Shulong to dissect the freshly harvested demonic specimens, with Jian Nuo tagging anxiously along.

Yet what Qingyang Qingyu could never have anticipated was that the very second she turned her back, the disciples began [stirring up shenanigans](# "Literal CN: 作妖 (zuòyāo) — acting up / stirring up demonic chaos / causing shenanigans").

Or rather, has there ever existed a gamer who *didn't* try to break the game?

When first introduced to the mechanics, everyone dutifully followed the tutorial prompts, deploying traps and shepherding demons to the specified locations. But they swiftly unearthed whole new uses for the map-editing tools:

[—"Why are you digging a hole here? This doesn't even lead to the destination!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

—"Heh heh, are you dense? Haven't you noticed these earth bugs can't fly?"-a disciple chuckled.-"Dig a pit here, slap a flame emitter right above it—hahaha! Charcoal-roasted bug skewers, coming right up!"

—"Whoa, [that's so deliciously shameless!](# "Literal CN: 你好骚哦 (nǐ hǎo sāo o) — 'you're so provocative/shameless'; iconic Chinese internet meme marveling at someone's outrageously unorthodox tricks")"-another disciple gasped in admiration.

[—"Let me try! I want to test the frost towers!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"Frost won't work, will it? They'll just crawl right out... Whoa, you dug way too deep!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

...

[—"Just try it! Let's see how deep we can actually dig!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"Tsk, ran out of spiritual energy. The editor won't let me build."](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"Hold on, doesn't the shop sell spiritual energy restoration potions? Chug some and try again!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

...

[—"Impressive! You built an entire labyrinth all on your own!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"The way these monsters get hopelessly lost in there looks so stupid!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

—"Tsk, ran out of items,"-a disciple grumbled.-"Time to [whale a bit more](# "Literal CN: 再氪一点 (zài kè yīdiǎn) — top up a little more cash; spend more spirit stones to buy gacha/shop items")."

...

[—"Conserve your firepower, you lot! There aren't enough monsters left to kill!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"I haven't had nearly enough fun yet!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"The monster spawn rate has been pathetic lately. Is Auntie Qingyu intentionally trying to cut our playtime short?"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

—"I have an idea..."-one disciple whispered conspiratorially.-"What would happen if we used the excavation tool directly on the monster spawn entrance?"

...

[—"It worked! New monsters are pouring out!"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

[—"W-Wait a second, isn't that an exploit?! Isn't that against the rules?"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Disciple")

—"How is that breaking the rules? Since Auntie Qingyu provided these tools, they were obviously meant for us to use!"-another disciple barked back.

...

---

—"And so, they ended up turning what was originally a small fissure into a bottomless crater?"-Qu Qianxing asked, facepalming in utter exasperation.

In truth, it was far more than just a crater; they had engineered an entire subterranean labyrinth down below.

Moreover, while these disciples brazenly claimed "Auntie Qingyu permitted it," they were acutely aware that they were exploiting a game-breaking bug. They had banded together to keep Qingyang Qingyu entirely in the dark until she finally realized that the rate of artifact wear and spiritual energy consumption had gone wildly off the charts.

By then, of course, the damage was irreversible.

Recalling all this, Qingyang Qingyu's cheeks flushed crimson with sheer mortification.

She nodded slowly:

—"Yes... this was entirely my design flaw..."

—"No, it isn't!"-Jian Nuo protested anxiously upon seeing her rare display of despondency.-"If my 'friends' hadn't been planning to visit, things wouldn't have devolved into such a tangled catastrophe. Qingyu would have had far more time to think through and polish her designs."

[Actually... I completely forgot why you came here in the first place.]-Qingyu thought to herself.

Qu Qianxing also shot Jian Nuo a look reserved exclusively for annoying nuisances.

Perhaps Qingyang Qingyu's maid might attempt to invert black and white to defend her young mistress, but in Qu Qianxing's eyes, Jian Nuo was merely an incidental catalyst. The root cause lay squarely in Qingyang Qingyu's structural oversight.

Then again, the reason Qingyang Qingyu had developed such habits owed plenty to Lu Shulong's influence—and perhaps even more to the absent Mo Xi.

[What a joyous occasion. It's only been a few months, and Qingyang Qingyu has already become a thoroughly qualified disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect!]-Qu Qianxing mused with razor-sharp sarcasm.

—"Actually, I think tearing this hole completely open was a blessing in disguise,"-Lu Shulong piped up from the side, desperately attempting [to salvage the situation](# "Literal CN: 找补 (zhǎobu) — patch up a blunder / smooth over mistakes").-"Second Senior Brother, you didn't see it—there was an entire nest of demonic insects beneath that aperture! If we hadn't excavated it, and let that abomination multiply uncontrollably, sooner or later this secret realm wouldn't have been able to suppress the swarm. *That* would have triggered an actual Second Demonic Incursion!"

—"I believe Brother Shulong is entirely correct!"-Jian Nuo chimed in immediately.-"If it were merely an ordinary demonic eye, leaving it sealed would cause it to dissipate eventually. But in its current state, leaving it unpurged is out of the question!"

At this point, Jian Nuo clenched his fists in righteous indignation:

—"Just who on earth misled Eternal Villa all those years ago?!"

—"I have already dispatched personnel to examine our clan's archives regarding the True Immortal who guided our family in sealing the demonic eye,"-Qingyang Qingyu sighed.

She didn't harbor much hope, however.

The True Immortal who had advised the Qingyang clan in constructing the subterranean labyrinth was an allied collaborator during the God-Demon War. He had presumably returned to the True Immortal Realm immediately following the war's conclusion. Even if they uncovered his identity, tracking him down would be impossible.

Besides, even if they could find him, what then? A single dismissive remark claiming he had no idea a demonic nest lurked underneath would effortlessly absolve him of all culpability.

Qingyang Qingyu looked toward Qu Qianxing:

—"Right now, that demonic nest is our immediate concern. Judging by the swarming insects, an insect Broodmother lurks in its deepest recesses, and perhaps even more horrifying monstrosities... Senior Brother Qu, shouldn't we report this to Master Untiring as soon as possible?"

On a matter of such gravity, Qingyang Qingyu was not about to put on a brave face.

After all, this concerned the survival of the entire mortal realm.

—"Right, Villa Master Qingyang must also be notified,"-Lu Shulong added. They could hardly expect Master to shoulder the burden entirely alone!

—"...Perhaps I should report this to the old man in my family as well,"-Jian Nuo muttered under his breath.

—"No rush!"-Qu Qianxing blurted out with uncharacteristic urgency.

—"The situation isn't urgent?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked, bewildered.

—"Indeed, no rush,"-Qu Qianxing replied smoothly, though his thoughts were spinning at breakneck speed.

He couldn't say it!

He couldn't possibly reveal that Master had conveniently ascended to the Upper Realm and was currently unreachable to handle this crisis!

If word of this leaked out, it would bring nothing but catastrophic trouble to Master and the Heavenly Spirit Sect!

Especially given those short-sighted opportunists across the domain—they would never believe Master could simply take a trip to the Upper Realm and come back. Instead, they would immediately train their greedy sights on a Heavenly Spirit Sect stripped of its supreme guardian, or even [pass the buck](# "Literal CN: 甩锅 (shuǎiguō) — shifting blame") onto the sect's head. After all, Qingyang Qingyu's unsealing of the Spirit Palace's demonic eye and subsequent design of the secret realm system had indeed been authorized by Master. If things went awry, malicious actors might brazenly smear Master's untarnished reputation.

By that same token, they couldn't invite Villa Master Qingyang or City Lord Jian over either.

Confronted by one Tribulation Transcendence titan and one Mahayana powerhouse, Qu Qianxing would never be able to maintain the ruse.

Yet Qu Qianxing had no desire to stress-test their supposed "friendship."

From that perspective, upon discovering that the demonic passage had evolved from a small hole into a massive rift, Lu Shulong's decision to immediately dispatch word to him was remarkably sensible.

Weighing the advantages and drawbacks, Qu Qianxing pivoted briskly:

—"How is Junior Sister Qingyu's data collection progressing?"

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingyu")

—"The classification of demon species, the empirical data metrics, and the specimen harvesting experiments!"-Qu Qianxing pressed, fixing his gaze squarely upon Qingyang Qingyu.-"During the entire time the disciples of Eternal Villa were busy excavating a highway straight into the Demon Realm, surely you weren't sitting on your hands doing nothing?"

—"I... of course I did!"-Qingyang Qingyu raised her voice defensively, though her tone gradually softened.-"In fact, it progressed remarkably smoothly..."

In reality, Qingyang Qingyu had made substantial breakthroughs in data collection. Moreover, through the process of dissecting demonic carcasses, her deepened understanding of fiendish anatomy had unexpectedly resolved numerous personal cultivation bottlenecks.

The original techniques of the Qingyang clan had been created in imitation of True Immortals. Consequently, certain meridian pathways had never made logical sense—there appeared to be fundamental anatomical divergences between mortal cultivators and True Immortals, which also accounted for how they drew spiritual energy versus immortal qi.

Yet after thoroughly dissecting the inner physiology of various demonic species, Qingyang Qingyu had bizarrely felt an inkling of comprehension... Wait a second. Why on earth would studying demonic physiology grant insight into True Immortals?!

Qingyang Qingyu had pondered this bizarre anomaly in silence.

She hadn't dared to voice the question aloud, simply continuing her dissections in quiet concentration, growing more proficient by the day.

Had she not been so thoroughly absorbed in her anatomical research, she wouldn't have taken so long to discover what her mischievous disciples were doing behind her back.

Qu Qianxing cared little for the intricate specifics. He remarked airily:

—"Since data collection proceeded smoothly, that proves your secret realm system is not under particularly immense strain?"

—"It is currently within manageable tolerances..."-Qingyu admitted.

—"Then have you conducted a stress test?"

—"A stress test?"

—"Evidently not,"-Qu Qianxing sighed, shaking his head at Qingyang Qingyu without waiting for an answer.-"In other words, you have obtained zero baseline metrics. Your comprehension of the subterranean conditions is inferior even to the disciples beta-testing your system. Running crying to Master under such circumstances—how are you any different from a child running to mommy and daddy the second you cause trouble?"

Qingyang Qingyu was struck dumb, her cheeks burning with even deeper mortification.

Jian Nuo attempted to defend her:

—"Wait, this situation is entirely different! This concerns a full-scale demonic incursion..."

—"And so, you intend to scurry home to your great-grandfather?"-Qu Qianxing offered a dazzlingly gorgeous yet venomously scathing smile.-"Weeping to him: 'I'm sorry, Honored Grandfather, I've caused a catastrophic disaster'?"

[...]-Jian Nuo's blood pressure spiked just hearing the hypothetical scenario.

Having effortlessly dismantled the first two, Qu Qianxing finally shifted his gaze toward Lu Shulong.

Lu Shulong had been watching Qu Qianxing's performance with great amusement, but the moment that sharp gaze landed on him, he folded like a cheap tent:

—"Understood, Second Senior Brother! I will immediately order every disciple in the Alchemy Hall to come assist in processing those demonic carcasses!"

—"Wait, but..."-Qingyang Qingyu began, wanting to say she didn't need their help.

—"A [stress test](# "Literal CN: 压力测试 (yālì cèshì) — stress test; evaluating system stability under extreme loads") evaluates the stability of your system under worst-case parameters to prevent far deadlier crises in the future,"-Qu Qianxing explained in crisp, methodical detail.-"For example, should our Master be away and unreachable while the rift expands, we would face total annihilation. Therefore, while our seniors are still within reach, the rift remains relatively modest, and the situation is controllable, we must utilize our available spiritual energy and personnel to ascertain the precise ceiling of demonic forces we can withstand."

Qingyang Qingyu had zero objections left.

After all, the vast majority of Eternal Villa's disciples were already committed to the "testing phase"—and their wallets had already been thoroughly harvested for a round of spirit stones. If they were to conduct a full-scale "stress test," they truly required reinforcements from the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Besides, so much has happened in the Heavenly Spirit Sect over the past year. It is summer vacation, after all—it's high time everyone had some proper 'fun'!"-Qu Qianxing concluded with an angelic smile.-"Otherwise, how will they ever survive next semester's coursework?"

And that was the complete, unvarnished story behind why more than half of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's disciples had vanished into thin air.

---

When Zhou Hui first returned, his [deep concern had clouded his judgment](# "Literal CN: 关心则乱 (guānxīn zé luàn) — deep care and concern disrupt one's rational composure").

Upon hearing the words "Demon Realm rift," however, he regained his customary composure. [Pinching his fingers in divination](# "Literal CN: 掐指一算 (qiāzhǐ yīsuàn) — Daoist hand calculation/divination"), he quickly grasped the general sequence of events.

—"Honestly, not a single one of these junior brothers can let anyone rest easy,"-Yuwen Xuan sighed from the side.

He hadn't actually understood what had transpired, but as Master's most troublesome disciple, Yuwen Xuan never passed up an opportunity to belittle the others—especially Qu Qianxing.

Opportunities to bully Qu Qianxing were far too rare to squander.

—"Old Second always looks like such an upright, dignified gentleman, yet he quietly stirred up such a monumental catastrophe,"-Yuwen Xuan muttered.

—"This was not Qianxing's doing,"-Zhou Hui defended Qu Qianxing.-"On the contrary, he enacted contingency measures precisely because of my absence. It was my oversight for failing to prepare a safety net for him."

Next time an emergency arose, he should authorize Qu Qianxing to contact City Lord Jian as a fallback guarantee.

Then again, who could have anticipated that no sooner had he stepped out... no, calculating the timeline, trouble had already brewed before he had even departed.

[Before 'ascending' next time, I really ought to have Jiuzhai cast a hexagram.]

Zhou Hui mused with a slight furrow of his brow, swiftly folding a small paper bird to inform Qu Qianxing of his return.

At least that would allow Qu Qianxing to set his mind at ease.

—"Tch, Master always plays favorites with him,"-Yuwen Xuan grumbled under his breath. Patting his storage pouch, he asked for permission:-"If there's nothing else for me to do, Master, may I head back to my cave dwelling?"

*Hehehe.* While Master had collected the divine corpse, who was Yuwen Xuan? He had, in fact, secretly pocketed some choice "goodies." Before Master noticed, he needed to stash them away securely.

And Zhou Hui truly hadn't noticed. He waved a dismissive hand:

—"Go on."

Yuwen Xuan promptly took several bouncing, lamb-like frolicking steps before abruptly remembering his humanoid form. Freezing in his tracks, he cleared his throat with several dry coughs, stealing a furtive glance back at Master.

Zhou Hui still hadn't registered his idiotic behavior.

Seeing this, Yuwen Xuan actually felt a twinge of petulant annoyance.

—"What's wrong? Can't locate Old Second?"-Yuwen Xuan asked.

He surmised that Master's paper messenger bird had reached Qu Qianxing's vicinity, and that some unexpected development had captured his Master's undivided attention.

Zhou Hui was indeed profoundly astonished.

In fact, he didn't even have the energy to formulate a spoken reply. Instead, he flicked his sleeve, relaying the visual feed perceived through his paper bird into a holographic illusion before Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan felt as if he were soaring through the air from a bird's perspective, gazing down upon Qu Qianxing and the others standing inside what appeared to be a command control chamber.

[What's the big fuss? Aren't they alive and kicking?]-Yuwen Xuan thought.

Yet he soon noticed that the surrounding environment was completely wrong.

Far from merely wrong—it was utterly unhinged!

Qu Qianxing and his companions were not inside the Qingyang clan's spirit stone secret realm at all. Judging by the suffocating demonic miasma and the hostile, grotesque terrain, they were unmistakably inside the Demon Realm itself!

Had it been only that, it wouldn't have been so shocking.

The real problem was that the indigenous debris of the Demon Realm—the demonic carcasses, jagged bones, and foul pollutants—had been assembled into a colossal, foreboding labyrinth. Meanwhile, the native fiends were scurrying about like battered heroes desperately fleeing for their lives, ducking and dodging in frantic terror as various defensive towers descended from above to bombard and obliterate them!

As for the mastermind orchestrating this slaughter like a supreme Demon King... it was none other than Qu Qianxing and his crew!

...Ah, wait, wrong genre! More accurately, Qu Qianxing looked like a breathtaking beauty abducted by a mountain bandit demon king.

Except this beauty was a lethal [femme fatale](# "Literal CN: 蛇蝎美人 (shéxiē měirén) — 'snake-and-scorpion beauty'; a deadly, ruthlessly alluring mastermind"). He stood waiting cold-bloodedly for the demons to perish, systematically harvesting every scrap of valuable material—[flaying their hides and ripping out their sinews](# "Literal CN: 抽筋扒皮 (chōujīn bāpí) — tearing out tendons and skinning alive; ruthless dismemberment").

Before him, the fiends possessed zero ability to resist.

—"What kind of ungodly mess is this?!"-Yuwen Xuan blurted out.

This time, Zhou Hui nodded in heartfelt agreement.

He watched as a cluster of demons fled under the lethal barrage of automated sniper towers, cowering and shrieking in despair before dissolving into flying ash.

—"How is this a demonic plot?"-Zhou Hui said, clutching his stomach in sheer exhaustion.-"If anything, it looks like we invaded the Demon Realm."

They had even constructed forward watchtowers and were methodically slaughtering the local "demons."
