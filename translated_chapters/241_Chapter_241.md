# Chapter 241: Prerequisite for Unity

Fortunately(?), Ran Deng was spared from pondering how to persuade Lu Shulong, for Qu Qianxing granted her no further opportunity.

As if having overheard Lu Shulong's grumbling, golden lightning from the other dimension once again interfered with this side of reality.

The lightning struck the water mirror Lu Shulong had painstakingly reassembled, causing it to detonate as though it were a solid slab. Shards of the "glass" ricocheted like streaks of electrical arcs, inflicting secondary collateral damage upon the already devastated Water Mirror Tower, while a stray fragment zipped mercilessly toward Lu Shulong's face.

Fortunately, being a True Dragon conferred a rather thick hide: electrical arcs potent enough to blow a Nascent Soul cultivator's head off merely sliced a small gash across his delicate, fair visage.

It all unfolded in the blink of an eye. Before Ran Deng could even react, Qu Qianxing's voice spoke within her mind:

—"Don't listen, don't look, don't speak, don't move."

—"What is the meaning of this...?!"-Ran Deng demanded.

She received no reply.

In that very instant, Ran Deng felt her psychic link with Qu Qianxing sever completely. More than that, the connection between this world and the "illusion realm" was cut off as well—evidently, Qu Qianxing refused to permit Ran Deng any further interference with the situation over there.

—"Wait a moment!"-Ran Deng cried in alarm.-"Do you intend to perish together with them?!"

Naturally, Qu Qianxing gave no response.

The link between them had been cut with absolute finality, and the spatial connection between the two dimensions was severed just as thoroughly. It was as if Master had never opened a gateway between the two spaces to begin with; even through the trial system, no method could be found to enter that "illusion realm" again.

Ran Deng instantly flew into a rage:

—"Qu Qianxing! It doesn't matter if you die, but how am I supposed to account for this to Master?! And the others—Fourth Junior Brother, Little Junior Sister, and Junior Sister Qingyang are all over there! How am I supposed to explain this to Master afterward?!"

Alas, her fury lasted only a fleeting second. The words she shouted in her outburst served instead as a sharp reminder of her own responsibilities... as the Eldest Senior Sister of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"*Tsk*, that bastard Qu Qianxing!"-Ran Deng cursed under her breath. Forcing herself to steady her emotions, she turned toward Lu Shulong.-"Shulong, assemble the disciples managing the Immortal Arts Assembly at once and see how many people are still stranded over there and haven't returned. I'll take charge of the mountain grand array just in case... Shulong?"

Ran Deng frowned as she looked at Lu Shulong, belatedly recalling that he seemed to have suffered "splash damage" from the shattered water mirror shards. To Ran Deng's eyes, it was merely accidental splash damage; the shattering mirror had intentionally or unintentionally spared Ran Deng, so she measured the harm against a routine mirror rupture without thinking much of it.

Lu Shulong, however, felt quite differently.

He hung his head, clutching the cut on his cheek. While the injury was negligible—far less alarming than the dragon scales covering his body—his complexion was terrifyingly dark. If one had to summarize his grievance in a single sentence, it would be: [Even Master has never hit me!](# "Pop-culture allusion: Gundam's Amuro Ray famous line — 'Even my own father never hit me!' (连我爸爸都没有打过我！)")

Past grudges and ancient grievances surged into Lu Shulong's heart all at once.

Thinking back, aside from Yuwen Xuan, Second Senior Brother was the one in the entire sect who bullied him the most viciously: in the third cycle, for example, Qu Qianxing had conspired with an outsider (Jian Nuo) to ruin Lu Shulong's plans; in the fourth cycle, Qu Qianxing had clung stubbornly to the previous lifetime's grievances, provoking Fourth Senior Brother into an extreme overreaction where he decided the sect was utterly hopeless and [knifed everyone](# "Slang: 刀了大家 (dao le dajia) — slaughtered / knifed everyone; Chinese internet gaming slang"); and in the sixth cycle, Qu Qianxing had gone so far as to demand that Lu Shulong sacrifice his very life under the guise of locating the true Master, forcing Lu Shulong into an early exit—leaving him completely unable to render Master any aid despite retaining his reincarnation memories!

And most infuriating of all! Qu Qianxing had never once been punished!!!

Master was usually fair. Even though he showed blatant favoritism toward Eldest Senior Brother (or so Lu Shulong firmly believed), whenever Eldest Senior Brother committed an offense, Master never let him off lightly. When Eldest Senior Brother bit Lu Shulong a few times in childhood or locked him inside an alchemy furnace, Lu Shulong merely had to drag his tail—bearing tooth marks or covered in furnace soot—to snitch to Master. A single complaint was enough to get Eldest Senior Brother strung up from the rafters for days; if Lu Shulong exaggerated the severity just a bit, that brute wouldn't be allowed down for half a month or even a full month.

For instance, that time Lu Shulong was almost genuinely refined into pills, Yuwen Xuan was strung up for an entire year. Master didn't even permit him to come down for New Year's Eve dinner, leaving him suspended until Yuwen Xuan bawled genuine tears of remorse and reflected on his misdeeds.

By contrast, all of Qu Qianxing's "bullying" had taken place during the temporal rewinds when Master was absent. Because Lu Shulong didn't want to expose his secrets, he hadn't dared to snitch at all. Not to mention, snitching wouldn't necessarily have yielded results anyway—after all, the chaos of the third cycle had indeed been instigated by Lu Shulong, and the fourth cycle was merely its aftershock. But Lu Shulong simply could not swallow this grievance:

[Why should Second Senior Brother be the only one who never gets punished?!]

Not only had Qu Qianxing escaped unpunished, but in the sixth cycle, he had even swindled his body away! Heaven only knew what deeds he had committed with that dragon vessel afterward; judging by how relentlessly Master was investigating the matter, that cycle must have ended in an absolute catastrophe! And what about Qu Qianxing? He suffered amnesia! He had cleanly forgotten every single deed he committed across the sixth cycle and the lifetimes before it... No, it wasn't mere forgetfulness. Lu Shulong could tell that Qu Qianxing had deliberately severed his own memories and power. As a result, the Qu Qianxing of this lifetime was once again the immaculate, pristine model student in Master's eyes, the upright Second Senior Brother of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, completely untainted by that scumbag who bullied his junior brother and swindled his body!

Lu Shulong originally hadn't paid it much mind, but connecting it all together sent a roaring surge of fury straight to his head, erupting into a deafening dragon roar:

—"Qu! Qian! Xing!"-he bellowed, a deafening draconic roar erupting from his throat.

In that very instant, his body transmuted entirely into a full draconic form.

—"Wait a moment!"-Ran Deng cried in panic at the sight.

She didn't know what Lu Shulong intended to do, but her immediate instinct was to stop him. Qu Qianxing was already acting reckless enough; she couldn't allow Fifth Junior Brother to run off and add to the chaos, leaving behind an uncontrollable mess.

Yet in his current state, how could Lu Shulong possibly listen to anything Ran Deng said? Disregarding everything, he soared straight upward.

Fortunately, Lu Shulong in this form was exceedingly long, allowing Ran Deng to grab hold of his tail in her panic.

Upon grabbing it, Ran Deng nearly lost her grip: the tail was as slick as an aquatic creature's, its texture resembling water, yet slightly denser. It was bone-chillingly frigid like melting slush; apply even a fraction of force, and her fingers would sink directly into it and pass straight through, letting the icy slush slip between her knuckles.

At the same time, the surrounding air grew icy yet viscous, and the field of vision darkened drastically, as though plunged into the bottom of a deep lake... No! It didn't just feel like it—it was precisely that!

Ripples abruptly spread across the air above Water Mirror Tower, splashing colossal waves of energy that coalesced into a perilous vortex.

Lu Shulong was charging headlong toward that very vortex.

—"Spatial magic?!"-Ran Deng gasped, shocked out of her wits as she released Lu Shulong's tail.

She recognized that Lu Shulong had torn open a spatial fissure, and the realm on the other side wasn't even the void filled with spatial turbulence, but the so-called "Divine Realm."

Naturally, the "Divine Realm" was not literally "the dwelling of the gods"—if Lu Shulong possessed such capability, Zhou Hui wouldn't have needed to ascend in the first place; he could simply have had Lu Shulong escort him to godhood.

In the cultivation world, the "Divine Realm" generally referred to "spaces that only divine beings could enter"—or, in Zhou Hui's terminology, a "higher-dimensional space."

Cultivators possessed no concept of "spatial dimensions"; they only knew that such spaces were absolute forbidden zones of death. Merely entering... no, merely touching the boundary of such a space would reduce one to fine powder, or worse, trigger a collapse into a black hole capable of annihilating everything nearby or even an entire minor realm. What was more, the greater one's power, the more catastrophic the collapse would be; for True Immortals in particular, the consequences of trespassing into the Divine Realm were indescribable. Only a handful of mythical divine beasts (higher-dimensional entities) like True Dragons could freely come and go.

Ran Deng had no desire to court death at such a moment; she decisively pulled back her hand.

Nor did Lu Shulong intend to bring Ran Deng into the "Divine Realm." His tail and entire physique were shifting into a state resembling water and spirit matter—something humans could touch, but could never grasp—and with Ran Deng's own strength, approaching that spatial rift was fundamentally impossible.

Ran Deng, however, did not give up completely. The flame upon her forehead flickered, and a wisp of life-fire descended onto the silvery-white mane at the tip of Lu Shulong's tail.

Ran Deng wasn't certain whether her life-fire would be affected by spatial dimensions, but it was worth a shot. At worst, it would merely extinguish and cause her a bit of internal recoil; she couldn't simply abandon her Fifth Junior Brother after he suddenly went mad, could she? She wouldn't be able to account for it to Master later!

As it turned out, Ran Deng's gamble paid off.

The "life-fire" had originally been refined by Old Ancestor Jiuzhai to divine the future—a flame capable of piercing across temporal and spatial dimensions. When Untiring Immortal Venerable, who could rewind time and space, bestowed this flame upon his disciple, it had been further reinforced. It would not be snuffed out by a "mere" dimensional ascension, nor would it extinguish upon entering the "Divine Realm."

On the contrary, after that wisp of life-fire followed Lu Shulong into the "Divine Realm," the liquid throughout Water Mirror Tower rippled once more. A colossal draconic silhouette surfaced across every liquid face, shimmering indistinctly. It looked like an airborne reflection, yet also like the shadow of a creature beneath the water. In truth, it was neither above nor below: it was the shadow cast through by the life-fire illuminating Lu Shulong within the other dimension.

—"What is that fellow doing?"-Ran Deng muttered in dissatisfaction.

Grumbling, Ran Deng roughly tidied the shambolic Water Mirror Tower.

She gathered the shattered "water mirrors" strewn across the floor and condensed them once more, forming a gargantuan mirror encircling the entire interior of the tower, giving off the distinct vibe of an IMAX theater—even though Ran Deng had never seen a 3D movie, and cultivation projection techniques were inherently unconstrained by physical screens. In any case, Lu Shulong's figure became considerably sharper. Without looking closely, one would feel as though the entire Water Mirror Tower had been dragged into the "Divine Realm."

Alas, human eyes were similarly bound by dimensions. No matter how much cultivators practiced, until they attained godhood, they could only perceive entities belonging to their native dimension. Thus, to Ran Deng's eyes, the entire Divine Realm appeared almost completely pitch-black aside from Lu Shulong.

Ran Deng furrowed her brows.

She was unsure whether she should directly interrogate and stop Lu Shulong, or quietly leave the life-fire attached to him and wait for Master to return so she could snitch on him directly.

—Ran Deng leaned toward the latter.

After all, the God-Demon battlefield had invaded the "illusion realm," Qu Qianxing had engaged the True Immortal commander in battle, the link between the "illusion realm" and the Heavenly Spirit Sect had been severed by his own hand, and Fourth Senior Brother, Little Junior Sister, and disciples from various other sects were still over there! The entire Immortal Arts Assembly was currently in a state of semi-suspension; she didn't know how to explain matters to the competitors, nor did she know whether the Immortal Realm and Demon Realm might breach into this world. The mere thought of the mountain of crises awaiting her handling made Ran Deng want to faint. As for this Fifth Junior Brother who refused to help and chose to flee, Ran Deng almost wanted to release him into the wild and wash her hands of him entirely.

Before Ran Deng could make up her mind, however, a piercing shriek rang out:

—"Eldest Senior Sister! What have you done to my Water Mirror Tower?!"

Mo Xi's projection flickered into existence beside Ran Deng.

Startled by his sudden entrance, Ran Deng gasped:

—"How did you get 'over here'?"

—"Used a bit of external assistance,"-Mo Xi brushed past the subject hurriedly. Before Ran Deng could press further, he launched into his grievance:-"Besides, how I got 'over here' isn't the point! I've been trying to reach you two this whole time, and nobody answered me! Sure enough, Fourth Senior Brother tossing me that bronze bell and claiming someone would rescue me if I rang it was a total scam! Not a single one of you paid any attention to me!"-As he spoke, he indignantly jiggled the bronze bell tied to the tip of his hair.

Ran Deng immediately felt a pang of guilt.

Mo Xi had been ringing that bell ever since Water Mirror Tower was affected by the illusion realm, but they had simply had no bandwidth to spare for him. On top of that, Qu Qianxing and Lu Shulong had completely wrecked Water Mirror Tower into its current state.

For a moment, Ran Deng didn't know how to explain, clumsily trying to block Mo Xi's line of sight:

—"Listen to me..."

—"What is that?!"-Mo Xi, however, had already spotted it, crying out in shock.-"...Shards?"

—"So, listen to my explanation...!!"-Ran Deng said with immense difficulty.

She had no idea what Qu Qianxing and Lu Shulong were doing either, making any convincing explanation impossible. Racking her brains for an excuse, she subconsciously looked in Lu Shulong's direction.

Ran Deng's eyes widened.

She noticed that the "shards" Mo Xi had gasped at were not necessarily from his Water Mirror Tower, but certain projections within the "Divine Realm."

As Lu Shulong advanced, Ran Deng's life-fire illuminated the scenery along his path. Although most sights in this dimension could not be parsed by mortal eyes, the life-fire was ultimately Old Ancestor Jiuzhai's trans-dimensional instrument for peering into the future; it simplified those sights into forms Ran Deng could comprehend.

And the area illuminated by the life-fire appeared, in Ran Deng's eyes, to resemble a...

Garbage dump?

Here lay all manner of shattered, seemingly abandoned relics. Some had degraded into dust-like powder, while others still reflected dim gleams like broken mirrors—evidently the "shards" Mo Xi had noticed.

Ran Deng surveyed these objects with curiosity, her life-fire happening to shine upon a shattered, necklace-shaped piece of junk, prompting her to look a second longer.

And with that single glance, Ran Deng witnessed the entire lifetime of a female celestial:

She saw the other's mother weeping and her father's demise; she saw the girl escorted to the Heavenly Court; she saw her receive celestial indoctrination; she saw her endure bullying; she could feel two diametrically opposed emotions festering within her heart—simultaneously harboring intense hatred for the Heavenly Court and True Immortals, yet yearning desperately for the strength of her oppressors. She sought to pad her credentials to climb higher up the hierarchy, joining the mortal God-Demon War for that purpose, only to discover she had been completely cast aside as an expendable pawn. Helpless, she was still compelled to execute the Heavenly Court's agenda... Wait, a variable had emerged in this world, a colossal variable, and she had to ensure the Heavenly Court—

[—"Soul, return home~~"](# "Cultural allusion: 魂归来兮 (hún guī lái xī) — classical soul-summoning chant from the Songs of Chu (楚辞), sung in dramatic operatic cadence")

Suddenly, an operatic falsetto shouted.

Ran Deng felt the scruff of her fate seized with tremendous force. In the next breath, she "snapped back" into her physical body, wearing an expression of someone jolting awake from a nightmare.

—"What on earth was that just now?!"-Ran Deng gasped, still shaken.

She had merely looked for an extra second, yet felt as though she had lived through the other person's entire lifetime—even feeling as if she had become her, compelled to fulfill her objectives.

—"Merely projections. Just like shadows cast on a wall when candlelight illuminates an object, the manifestation of this space in another dimension,"-a cold voice answered.-"Your cultivation is simply too low, making you easily susceptible to influence. Better not look too closely."

—"Y-Yes, I won't look, I won't look,"-Ran Deng replied tremulously. She withdrew her gaze, belatedly realizing that someone had just saved her. Looking toward her savior, she beheld an incomparably familiar face paired with an entirely unfamiliar aura.

—"...Ruwang Ghost Emperor?"-Ran Deng called out, a beat late.

Although one would never mistake him for Master at a glance, undeniably, beholding that face which brought her the greatest sense of warmth instantly granted Ran Deng immense peace of mind.

Finally calming down, she inquired:

—"Why are you here? Weren't you with Master..."-entering the illusion realm?

—"His Majesty Ruwang is still inside the illusion realm; this is merely his projection,"-Mo Xi explained on Ruwang Ghost Emperor's behalf.-"Master and the others seem to have discovered something very important, but have yet to find your father, so they entrusted His Majesty Ruwang to keep watch, as well as look after our situation here."-These words were the excuse Ruwang had given Mo Xi, and Mo Xi hadn't doubted them.

Nor did Ran Deng.

Since he was someone Master trusted, Ran Deng naturally placed her trust in him as well.

Moreover, Ran Deng swiftly connected "Master's important discovery" with the "surging power pouring through the mountain grand array." Delighted, she asked:

—"So the power in the mountain grand array really was bestowed by Master? Will he be returning soon?"

—"*Hmph*."

Ruwang Ghost Emperor gave no direct answer, merely staring at the pitch-black projections of the "Divine Realm" around them.

—"Youngsters intruding into perilous domains,"-the theatrical mask atop his head drawled in operatic cadences,-"what might your intentions be, pray tell?"

—"Ah, that..."-Ran Deng choked up once more.

Yet facing that face identical to Master's, she didn't stutter for long, answering with practiced speed:

—"A minor situation arose. For reasons unknown, the God-Demon battlefield suddenly shifted into the illusion realm. To prevent all the participants of the trial system from being implicated, Second Senior Brother exhausted his strength, willingly sacrificing himself to shut the connection between the two worlds. But doing so left him in mortal peril. Fifth Junior Brother simply couldn't bear the thought, so he entered the 'Divine Realm' to assist Second Senior Brother."

—"Is that so?"-Mo Xi asked, astonished.

Even though their Heavenly Spirit Sect was like a family where martial siblings lived in harmony and boundless affection, why did he feel that the two senior brothers Eldest Senior Sister spoke of would never do such a thing? No—it wasn't that they wouldn't do it, but rather they would never do it for the reasons Eldest Senior Sister claimed! If they did, there had to be an entirely different motive.

Mo Xi couldn't resist pressing further:

—"Second Senior Brother, for the sake of everyone? Fifth Senior Brother, for the sake of Second Senior Brother?"

—"Indeed!"-Ran Deng replied with solemn gravity. Her eyes remained locked onto Ruwang Ghost Emperor as she strained to project the most innocent and earnest demeanor of her entire life.-"My sect's senior and junior brothers are just that affectionate and brimming with a spirit of selfless sacrifice."

*Selfless sacrifice?*

Mo Xi broke out in a full body of goosebumps.

Ran Deng, however, kept her gaze rigidly forward.

What a joke. One could afford to speak a shred of truth before a little junior brother, but before an outsider—especially an outsider who was Master's friend—how could she possibly expose the disgraceful truth of her fellow disciples, including the sect master heir, acting utterly lawless, reckless, and self-willed?! Did the Heavenly Spirit Sect no longer care about its dignity? Did she, the Eldest Senior Sister, no longer need her face?! Even if it were pitch-black, she would paint it pure white!

She could only pray that fellow Lu Shulong would live up to expectations and not pull any bizarre stunts...

—"Found it!"

Just as Ran Deng was praying in her heart, Lu Shulong's cheer echoed from the other end of the mirror.

*Found what?*

Ran Deng instantly looked over in nervous apprehension, only to find Lu Shulong coiled atop a flight of stairs in the middle of the garbage mountain.

The stairway was also part of the garbage mountain. It originally appeared to be made of colored glaze, but lay shattered and dilapidated, looking as though it had plummeted from the heavens, broken almost beyond recognition. Only a few fragmented shards remained clinging to the twisted original frame, feebly refracting the glow of the life-fire.

Having just suffered at the hands of a broken necklace, Ran Deng naturally dared not underestimate this stairway.

She refrained from looking directly, merely sweeping her divine sense across the steps.

Ran Deng could vaguely "perceive" White Jade Capital, the Ascending Heaven Stairway, and the confronting immortals and demons reflected across the shattered glaze—could this be the "illusion realm" Qu Qianxing had just sealed off?

As if answering Ran Deng's conjecture, the stairway abruptly vibrated. Accompanied by thunder that boomed even across a dimensional divide, a barrage of electrical arcs flashed, surging with a majestic momentum that seemed poised to puncture the dimensional barrier and blast the other space to bits.

Witnessing the electrical arcs, Mo Xi blinked in surprise before recognizing the existence of that realm:

—"Ah, Fifth Senior Brother really did go find Second Senior Brother!"

—"Of course! Your Fifth Senior Brother was worried sick about your Second Senior Brother! He insisted on going to save him; even I couldn't dissuade him!"-Ran Deng fabricated without batting an eyelid.-"Fortunately, Shulong is a True Dragon after all, possessing such a method of traversing spatial realms; I certainly have broadened my horizons today. Now that they have rendezvoused, I can finally rest easy. Compared to matters over there, what is more important right now is for our Heavenly Spirit Sect to prepare as well. Mo Xi, come lend a hand!"

Saying so, Ran Deng seized the chance to change the subject, attempting to nonchalantly snuff out the life-fire attached to Lu Shulong—compared to her junior brother's whereabouts, preventing him from continuing to embarrass the sect before a distinguished guest was far more pressing.

Yet across a dimensional gap, Ran Deng's manipulation hit a snag: her life-fire failed to extinguish smoothly, merely wavering slightly. Worse still, Ruwang Ghost Emperor did not shift his gaze in the slightest, nor did Mo Xi move.

Mo Xi even asked:

—"Huh? Why did Fifth Senior Brother stop?"

Indeed, Lu Shulong had spotted the stairway leading to the illusion realm, yet showed zero intention of tearing open another spatial rift to join Qu Qianxing's side. On the contrary, he produced several recording jade slips from his person, aiming them at the stairway and arranging them meticulously up and down, doing Heaven only knew what.

Seeing this, Mo Xi raised an eyebrow:

—"Eldest Senior Sister, Fifth Senior Brother..."-Mo Xi trailed off.-"He isn't actually trying to help Second Senior Brother, is he?"

—"No!"-Ran Deng cut in.

—"Fifth Senior Brother's actions look an awful lot like..."-he's just watching the spectacle?

—"Not at all!"

—"Then what on earth is he..."

—"Impossible!"

Ran Deng fired off a [triple denial](# "Internet meme: 否认三连 (fouren sanlian) — rapid-fire 'No, wasn't me, impossible'"), not even knowing what she was denying anymore. She only felt bewildered as to why this little junior brother, usually so adept at reading the room, was suddenly acting so oblivious!

Unable to silence Mo Xi, she could only force out an excuse:

—"Shulong, he... he has always been very cautious. He must feel that barging into the battlefield recklessly is too hazardous, so he's making preparations. Yes, that must be it."

Inwardly, she suspected that Lu Shulong merely wanted to record some compromising dirt to hand over to Master later—that foolish dragon couldn't possibly be so petty, could he?

Lu Shulong was oblivious to his Eldest Senior Sister's inner slander, busy adjusting the jade slips in his claws:

That's right! He was just that petty.

Lu Shulong's fit of rage was, after all, merely a fit of rage. Frankly speaking, Lu Shulong possessed the gentlest temperament among all of Zhou Hui's direct disciples—bullied by Yuwen Xuan from infancy to adulthood, he had no room to cultivate a bad temper. Today, he had suddenly acquired strength far surpassing the past; before his swelling ego could even burst, he was struck by a sharp blow, causing him to vent like a deflating balloon and charge into another dimension in a fit of pique.

Yet as he soared high enough, that breath of anger dissipated.

With his head cool once more, Lu Shulong realized that going to find Qu Qianxing was utterly meaningless:

Once he found him, he couldn't very well blast him back with lightning, could he?! Setting aside whether he could win the fight, such an act was far too childish; if he accidentally fouled up something important, it would ruin his image in Master's heart.

Still, [now that he was already here](# "Literal CN: 来都来了 (lai dou lai le) — classic Chinese social justification: 'since we're already here...'"), Lu Shulong didn't want to turn back immediately.

After all, even for a True Dragon, reaching this "Divine Realm" demanded immense effort.

Lu Shulong recalled that the last time he visited was back in the third cycle, when he obtained a portion of the draconic inheritance from the Boundless Ocean dragons of the Eastern Domain. Only then did Lu Shulong learn that as a True Dragon, he could traverse the Divine Realm to journey to other worlds, or even ascend directly to the Heavenly Court.

[If I could take Master to ascend directly, would Master not abandon me?]

Lu Shulong had reasoned back then, and after strenuous effort, discovered the lost method of opening a pathway into the Divine Realm.

Alas, Lu Shulong in that lifetime was far too weak. Upon entering this space, he felt as though he were bound by invisible filaments; the further he flew, the tighter they strangled him. Having flown barely a few hundred meters, Lu Shulong felt he was on the brink of perishing, leaving him no choice but to retreat and stir up trouble for his martial siblings instead.

Entering the "Divine Realm" once more in this lifetime, aside from his momentary fit of anger, was also driven by his desire to test his newfound strength. And indeed, this time had gone extraordinarily smoothly: he no longer felt that strangling constriction, soaring an immense distance in a single breath, and even smoothly locating the entrance to the "illusion realm" sealed off by Second Senior Brother.

Lu Shulong felt it would be a pity to leave just like that, but going down to help Second Senior Brother went entirely against his grain.

Most importantly, looking at Qu Qianxing's wretched state, that fellow clearly had no desire for anyone's help. Even if he did accept "help," it would be the kind where jade and stone burned together—such as demanding Lu Shulong surrender his dragon remains to serve as casting materials.

Remembering the sixth cycle, Lu Shulong gritted his teeth once more.

He was even less inclined to jump down and court bad luck, though observing what Second Senior Brother planned to do before deciding was fine.

Besides, it was an undeniable fact that Qu Qianxing had dragged Fourth Senior Brother, Little Junior Sister, and competitors who hadn't evacuated in time into this mess. Recording the evidence along the way so he could snitch to Master afterward was entirely justifiable, right?

Scheming with fierce relish, Lu Shulong adjusted the angle of his recording jade slips.

Yet peering through the jade slips, Lu Shulong felt as though he caught sight of something indistinct.

He cautiously slowed his movements, widening his eyes to peer forward.

When widening his eyes proved insufficient, he squinted like someone with severe myopia.

Unlike Ran Deng, Lu Shulong, who could enter the "Divine Realm" directly, was essentially a species native to this higher plane. In theory, his vision was unobstructed, laying the entire garbage mountain bare before his eyes. Yet right now, Lu Shulong felt something interfering with his sight, rendering him unable to discern something crucial.

It seemed relying on himself alone wouldn't cut it.

Lu Shulong abruptly twisted his serpentine body, shaking Ran Deng's spark of life-fire down in front of him:

—"Eldest Senior Sister, you're watching, aren't you? In that case, why not look a bit further? Ignite this entire place!"

—"*Eeeh?!*"-Ran Deng fired off rapidly.-"You mean attacking from the spatial periphery to assist Second Senior Brother?"

Rather than asking Lu Shulong, she was explaining to Ruwang Ghost Emperor.

In reality, across a dimensional barrier, Ran Deng's voice could not reach Lu Shulong at all; only the motionless life-fire clearly conveyed Ran Deng's refusal.

Lu Shulong, however, seemed determined to drag Ran Deng into the water:

—"This is for the sake of our Heavenly Spirit Sect. If you don't believe me, shine it brighter and look around."

Saying so, he held the life-fire aloft, proactively funneling a massive surge of spiritual energy into the flame.

Ran Deng cast a hesitant glance at the Ghost Emperor. Before Lu Shulong explained what he was scheming, Ran Deng didn't want to humor him. But having just proclaimed her absolute trust in her martial brother, refusing now would look exceedingly bizarre, puncturing her lie.

Ran Deng sighed helplessly:

—"Fine, let me see..."

She slowly intensified her life-fire.

This was no simple task. After all, manipulating her life-fire across a dimensional divide was exhausting. Ran Deng's reluctance to indulge Lu Shulong, aside from annoyance at his recklessness, stemmed from how taxing the endeavor was; she already felt maintaining the flame was hard enough.

Yet having a face identical to Master's watching nearby was an entirely different story—not to mention the Heavenly Spirit Sect's dignity was on the line.

Ran Deng had no choice but to concentrate, striving to govern that wisp of flame.

The only silver lining was that so long as she governed it, the spiritual energy Lu Shulong supplied was abundant to the point of excess, sparing Ran Deng from providing energy herself.

And so, that ember of life-fire wobbled arduously several times before emitting a sharp *pop!* From a tiny spark, it flared three to four meters high like a blazing torch, the illuminated radius instantly expanding from a tiny patch to the breadth of an entire grand hall.

As the life-fire's illumination spread, things that had previously been imperceptible came into view.

They vaguely resembled fine, thread-like filaments.

Ran Deng subconsciously squinted as well.

Fine threads flickered in and out of sight, like drizzle on a freezing snowy night, refracting intermittent reflections in the lightning and life-fire. Descending from the sky of the higher dimension, they draped across various objects atop the garbage mountain—particularly the broken stairway—seeming to penetrate the spatial barrier that even thunder could not shatter, sinking straight down into the fractured world within the stairway.

This sight felt strangely familiar...

—"The Heavenly Dao? Right, it's the Heavenly Dao!"-Ran Deng murmured.

She remembered!

After repairing the Ascending Heaven Stairway, Master had begun researching the Heavenly Dao, seeking to unravel the origin of reversing reincarnation and destiny. Ran Deng had visited a few times and vaguely glimpsed similar phenomena—reputedly the "threads" through which the Heavenly Court altered the Heavenly Dao. Master had even traced these "threads" back to their source, visiting the Heavenly Gate to investigate.

Ran Deng had not participated in Master's journey to the Heavenly Gate.

At the time, having just acquired her life-fire, she was half-dead and uninvolved in sect affairs, whereas that damnable Eldest Senior Brother, after gorging himself silly alongside Master at the Heavenly Gate, had strutted over to flaunt his round, distended belly before wandering off. To this day, Ran Deng couldn't fathom why a trip to the Heavenly Gate could satisfy Eldest Senior Brother's appetite—just what on earth had he scavenged to eat?!

*Ah, getting sidetracked.*

In any case, after returning from the Heavenly Gate, Master seemingly stopped visiting the Ascending Heaven Stairway to study those "threads." Knowing Master, he was likely already capable of handling them—or at least had advanced to the next research phase, meaning this world no longer needed to fear those "threads."

Yet now, these "threads" had manifested in this place and penetrated into the "illusion realm"—evidently a terrible omen!

Then Second Senior Brother...!

Ran Deng peered nervously into the illusion realm, striving to trace the trajectories of those "puppet strings." To her slight relief, the "threads" had not settled upon Qu Qianxing, nor had they fallen upon the demons or other living beings; rather, they landed squarely upon the celestial soldiers and heavenly generals.

Those celestial troops appeared outwardly unchanged, yet looking down from the higher-dimensional space, the filaments formed a colossal spiderweb. Within this web, whether demon or celestial soldier, once snared by the threads, escape was impossible. Woven further, it would eventually bind the entire illusion realm.

This did not mean Qu Qianxing was powerless to resist.

On the contrary, only upon seeing those fine threads did one realize what Qu Qianxing was truly battling.

Thunder detonated again and again, not in mindless barrages, but striking each individual filament with surgical precision, determined to shred that colossal web.

Regrettably, Qu Qianxing's power remained insufficient.

Naturally so.

No matter how brilliant Qu Qianxing was, no matter how immense his talent, no matter how suddenly he acquired power, his foundational cultivation remained where it was; a head-on clash of pure strength was simply too much of a stretch. To make matters worse, Qu Qianxing disdained resorting to other tactics. Lightning that seemed capable of tearing space apart could not sever those barely perceptible filaments; electrical arcs merely clung to the threads, causing that invisible web to transform into a literal, physical electrical grid.

Watching this, Ran Deng grew frantic.

Had her life-fire not lacked offensive properties, she would have sprayed all her flames downward in tandem with the lightning to see if she could burn those damnable threads away.

Lu Shulong evidently shared similar thoughts.

He was even angrier, with a lingering sense of post-traumatic dread.

Lu Shulong recalled that when he first entered this "Divine Realm" in the third cycle, he felt as though something invisible was constricting him—evidently, it was these very threads!

Fortunately, whether because he retreated fast enough or because the entity laying the threads hadn't noticed him, he had escaped; otherwise, being [sliced into eel cutlets](# "Literal CN: 鳝片 (shanpian) — sliced swamp eel; culinary metaphor for being chopped into thin ribbons") by these seemingly fragile threads was well within the realm of possibility.

And these threads still existed even now.

Lu Shulong called out loudly:

—"Senior Sister, do you see those 'destiny threads'? The threads that weave the Heavenly Dao and manipulate fate—they once controlled our destinies as well, until Master severed them. Their presence here is no coincidence, but unfortunately, we lack Master's skill; we can barely even... see them."

As he spoke, Lu Shulong swiped his claws irritably at the filaments.

Yet the threads that had once bound him seemed endowed with sentience, gracefully evading his swipe.

Not only could he not touch them, he couldn't even see them clearly.

Where the life-fire did not shine, the threads shimmered indistinctly. Even among True Dragons possessing trans-dimensional might, only a handful of breeds could discern the trajectory of fate, and Lu Shulong was not among them—otherwise, he wouldn't have watched his sect cycle through reincarnation so many times.

Ran Deng's life-fire, however, was tailor-made for this occasion.

Lu Shulong egged Ran Deng on:

—"Thanks to Senior Sister refining this life-fire, we can investigate this mystery. If you could burn them away, that would be even better—and if you can't, it doesn't matter, so long as you pinpoint their coordinates, I can handle them! If we can sever the destiny threads in this space, the celestial soldiers and generals below won't be a problem anymore. Senior Sister, you wouldn't want Second Senior Brother to suffer a major mishap, would you?"

[Flattering on one hand and threatening on the other—you don't sound like you're genuinely helping at all!]

Ran Deng wanted to roast him, but held her tongue due to Ruwang's presence.

She knitted her brows helplessly:

—"Even if you say so, never mind burning them, merely locating them is difficult!"

Ran Deng wasn't making excuses. In truth, even without Lu Shulong's "plea," she had proactively guided her life-fire toward the destiny threads. However, the life-fire carried zero offensive force and poor adhesion... or rather, the destiny threads themselves were problematic: they were fundamentally non-flammable, slick as oil, and freezing as rain. The life-fire could barely touch them, and even when it did, it was instantly snuffed out, proving extraordinarily difficult to manage.

Yet Ran Deng and Lu Shulong were communicating across a dimension: Ran Deng could see and hear Lu Shulong, but Lu Shulong could not hear Ran Deng's voice, nor could he offer any advice.

Mo Xi inquired:

—"If even the life-fire cannot burn them, how did Second Senior Brother locate these threads? It should be far harder for him to see them clearly over there, right?"

—"...Perhaps Master taught him?"-Ran Deng answered without much thought.

A genius like Qu Qianxing possessing bizarre skills was hardly surprising; wasn't the lightning he was currently wielding also something he wasn't supposed to have learned yet? So even if he could suddenly perceive destiny threads...

—"No, that's not it. He didn't see them—he *calculated* them,"-Ran Deng paused, her reasoning clicking back into gear. While Second Senior Brother was versatile, he wasn't omnipotent like Master; his methods always left a traceable trail.-"Come to think of it, the spatial anomaly data we compiled earlier... that's it! As these filaments enter the space, they inevitably interfere with the realm itself, causing shifts in spatial metrics. Second Senior Brother had me compile those statistics earlier; he must have calculated their trajectories using that—"

Mo Xi asked:

—"Then can your life-fire burn the destiny threads that way too?"

—"Are you kidding me?! I'm not a monster like him!"-Ran Deng cried out, flipping through the dense sea of numbers.

Never mind how many thousands of tangled destiny threads there were, these things weren't even constants—they were variables! She could not fathom how Qu Qianxing managed to instantly calculate the point of impact for every single thread and strike each with precision using lightning! With such an immense computational load, wouldn't his brain explode?!

Ran Deng couldn't understand, but she never tormented herself over such things. She glanced at Mo Xi:

—"Can the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree calculate it? The trial system can be halted now, which should free up considerable computational throughput, right?"

—"There is throughput, but..."-This time it was Mo Xi's turn to stammer. His practical "arithmetic" was decent, but when it came to advanced higher-dimensional mechanics, he was out of his depth.-"What formulas do we even use to compute this?"

—"Let me substitute a few spatial formulas first to see?"-Ran Deng murmured. She genuinely engaged her brain.

Fortunately, as an architect (read: formation master), her grasp of spatial mechanics was quite solid. While she still couldn't reverse-engineer Qu Qianxing's full calculation method, she noticed several details:-"If these spiritual energy variables are plugged in, we should be able to calculate the spiritual energy value of the destiny threads, and then... Huh? Strange..."

—"What is it?"-Mo Xi asked nervously.

—"I was thinking: if Second Senior Brother can calculate both the positions and shifting trajectories of the destiny threads, he should know the exact force required to snap them! In that case, maintaining this deadlock makes no sense!"-Ignoring the flashes of lightning, Ran Deng looked up once more toward Qu Qianxing on the other side.-"Even if his power isn't enough to sever all the destiny threads, he could easily pluck the threads off the most troublesome heavenly generals!"

—"Perhaps Second Senior Brother feels that wouldn't be efficient enough?"

—"Is grinding down a stalemate efficient?! If he exhausts his strength, it's truly over! No, wait, before he even runs out..."-Ran Deng rapidly scribbled a long string of numbers on paper, crying out:-"Dammit! He can't possibly want to perish together with them, can he?!"

—"Eh?"-Mo Xi gasped.

Ran Deng explained in frantic haste:

—"Spatial rifts are inherently fragile! Two colossal forces repeatedly wrenching back and forth will cause the space to destabilize further. If spiritual energy is manipulated precisely and the timing is seized to make the space detonate in a single instant, then even a True Immortal on the other end of the destiny threads will not escape death! Make no mistake—with Second Senior Brother's calculation prowess, he can definitely calculate when that godforsaken 'window' arrives!"

—"...How could that be?"-Hearing this, Mo Xi's complexion turned pale.

—"Sounds like quite a decent solution, doesn't it?"

A third voice chimed in at that moment.

It was Ruwang.

His tone was brimming with nonchalant detachment:

—"Regardless of the underlying cause, it is an undeniable fact that Qu Qianxing is currently leading demons against celestial troops. Even if he triumphs, the Heavenly Court will hold him accountable afterward and bring trouble upon this world. Conversely, if he takes down the entire space along with the enemy commander in mutual annihilation, the blame can be laid entirely upon him—or even more cleanly, written off as an accident on the immortal-demon frontlines. Should the Heavenly Court dispatch investigators, we would have a solid pretext to offer..."

—"Out of the question!"-Ran Deng and Mo Xi objected in unison.

Ran Deng glared at Ruwang:

—"Perhaps as a Tribulation Transcendence grandmaster you have other considerations, but we cannot sacrifice Second Senior Brother."

—"Indeed, Master would be heartbroken,"-Mo Xi added sorrowfully.

—"Not just heartbroken—we cannot allow him to exhaust his strength for our sake again!"-With Master's habit of rewinding time whenever someone died in past cycles, this time would surely cause him to reset everything once more!

Ran Deng bit her lip, turning toward the other end.

Suddenly, her life-fire leaped, enveloping Lu Shulong's head.

—"Wah! Senior Sister, what are you doing... *waaah!*"-Lu Shulong let out a pitiful wail.

Yet he swiftly discovered that the life-fire was as warm as when it rested upon his mane, bearing no intent to harm him. Instead, as the flame burned atop his head, packets of information were transmitted across—precisely Ran Deng's deduction from moments ago.

—"Eh? Second Senior Brother wants to die? Don't joke around! It doesn't matter if he dies, but what about Master?!"-Once Lu Shulong grasped the situation, he arrived at the exact same conclusion as Ran Deng and Mo Xi. Turning around, he prepared to charge down the stairway into the illusion realm to stop Qu Qianxing.

—"Don't go! Second Senior Brother won't pay you any heed!"-Ran Deng stopped him.

—"Then do we just leave that fellow alone?!"-Lu Shulong shouted back.

—"Of course not! So go find Guan Ye!"-Ran Deng commanded.

—"Fourth Senior Brother? But Second Senior Brother won't listen to him either, will he? We'd be better off finding Eldest Senior Brother... Dammit! In every crisis like this, that glutton is nowhere to be found!"

—"While Guan Ye can't persuade Qu Qianxing either, when it comes to who on that side can calculate Second Senior Brother's power trajectory and coordinate with me to deploy an array, he is the only one,"-Ran Deng said.

Fortunately, she had merely been too lazy to think in the past, not genuinely devoid of ideas.

Ever since resolving not to remain idle and useless in reincarnation, Ran Deng had brought every ounce of her wit and competence to bear in this lifetime:

—"Doesn't Second Senior Brother want mutual destruction? Then we simply deny him the opportunity! After all, the spatial rift will only rupture and cause spatial collapse if the two opposing forces grind against each other for long enough. We only need to lay down an array and provide Second Senior Brother with sufficient power to sever all the destiny threads in one fell swoop!"

—"Ah, give Second Senior Brother power?"-Lu Shulong sounded distinctly reluctant.

Ran Deng didn't waste breath coaxing him, simply dumping all the spatial destiny thread data into Lu Shulong's mind in one massive packet.

Lu Shulong fell silent for two full seconds.

As the apparent second-ranked honor student of the Heavenly Spirit Sect (on the surface), Lu Shulong felt he *could* calculate it if pushed, but there was no telling how long it would take. More importantly, he possessed negative interest in mathematics and physics, let alone calculating such excruciatingly tedious variables—otherwise, he wouldn't have bothered pretending to have amnesia in front of Master.

—"I'll go find Fourth Senior Brother,"-Lu Shulong said decisively.

Watching Lu Shulong swish his tail, bypassing Qu Qianxing to venture deeper into the illusion realm, Ran Deng turned to Mo Xi.

—"Is there anything I can do?"-Mo Xi asked without hesitation.

He inwardly cursed his young age and modest cultivation, unable to provide substantial strength. He wondered how Mo Bei was faring over there; he should have asked Eldest Senior Sister to tell Fifth Senior Brother to look for Mo Bei too—that girl's strength was vastly superior to his own.

Mo Xi pondered, harboring zero hesitation when it came to volunteering his twin sister.

Ran Deng, however, didn't place much stock in the meager strength of the two youngest disciples; she had other plans:

—"Go assemble all the participants of the Immortal Arts Assembly. Tell them... it is the final phase of the trial system."

—"You mean... pooling everyone's strength?"-Mo Xi's eyes widened.

—"Precisely. Relying solely on us certainly won't suffice, but with grandmasters from so many sects gathered here, that's an entirely different story,"-Ran Deng said decisively.-"What's more, the mountain grand array possesses the power channeled by Master! They have undoubtedly been bolstered as well. So long as we concentrate all this power, [wrestling arm-to-arm with a True Immortal](# "Idiom: 掰手腕 (bai shouwan) — arm-wrestling; matching strength / going toe-to-toe") is by no means impossible!"

—"Understood!"-Mo Xi said.

His figure vanished in an instant, evidently departing for the "main server" at the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree.

Ran Deng did not idle either. Gathering power was one thing, but transmitting it across was another. Qu Qianxing had closed the spatial link between the two worlds; fortunately, the power coursing through the mountain grand array continued to flow without discrimination. Ran Deng therefore decided to use the mountain grand array as a conduit, having Guan Ye draw a corresponding array on the other side to regulate the power transmission—which would also serve to rein in Qu Qianxing. While this power was to be funneled to Qu Qianxing, they couldn't let him run wild either. Thus, she needed to design an array equipped with sufficient safeguards to hand over to Guan Ye.

Mulling over the mechanics, Ran Deng dared not squander a second, producing her customary array drafting tools and sketching formulas and diagrams on the spot.

With the crisis so pressing, she momentarily even forgot that an "honored guest" bearing Master's exact face was still standing inside Water Mirror Tower.

[Truly rare,]-Ruwang thought lazily.

As the entity who knew best the state of previous lifetimes—particularly the third cycle—Ruwang had watched these unfilial disciples tear each other to shreds. Now, he witnessed them truly united for the very first time.

And indeed, they were united for the exact same person.

That person had toiled across multiple reincarnation cycles for these youngsters; it was about time he saw just how far his disciples were willing to strive for his sake.

Ruwang suppressed the urge to notify Untiring, settling back into his posture of an amused spectator:

[—"Let us see just how far you can go."](# "Speaker: Ghost Emperor Ruwang")
