# Chapter 139: A Special Invitation (第139章 特别邀请)

[← Previous Chapter](138_Chapter_138.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](140_Chapter_140.md)

---

—"Sooner or later, I'm going to butcher that pair of shameless adulterers!"-Shen Qingqian, Sect Leader of Blood Demon Hall, cursed furiously at the very moment the invitation from the Untiring Immortal Venerable arrived.

Not a single disciple beside her attempted to dissuade her. On the contrary, they chimed in with furious agreement:

[—"Exactly! Not a single person in the Peerless Alliance is a good thing!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"What kind of dogs are they, daring to [fabricate nonsense to touch our Sect Master's coattails](# "Literal CN: 碰瓷 (pèngcí) — staged crash / clout-chasing extortion; here meaning concocting false scandals and defamatory gossip")!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Aside from fabricating lewd rumors, they don't know how to do anything else, do they?!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

[—"Next time I see them on the dueling platform, I'm going to beat their dog heads in!"](# "Speaker: Blood Demon Hall Disciple")

Watching this spectacle unfold, Bai Wubin felt a throbbing headache coming on.

Yet when it came to the root cause of Shen Qingqian's fury, even someone as even-tempered and steady as Bai Wubin couldn't help but feel his blood boil.

It was "just" a few rumors.

In truth, ever since Blood Demon Hall had been founded, it had been persistently besieged by a haze of shadowy rumors.

This was tied directly to Shen Qingqian's origins.

As mentioned previously, Shen Qingqian had not been born inside a spatial rift staring at the severed head of an ancient demon sovereign. Once upon a time, she too had belonged to an ordinary cultivation sect. It was just that the sect master of that faction—who also happened to be Shen Qingqian's senior martial sister—had been far more interested in securing a handsome Daoist partner than actually managing a sect. Consequently, she had handed over the entire sect as a wedding dowry to join the Peerless Alliance, even attempting to offer Shen Qingqian as a social favor to grease the wheels. It was because of this betrayal that Shen Qingqian had severed ties and walked away.

Regarding that godforsaken ordeal, Shen Qingqian had never made a fuss about it. The Peerless Alliance, however, considered her departure an unforgivable act of treason. In particular, Shen Qingqian's former senior sister and the Peerless Alliance protector whom Shen Qingqian had flatly rejected refused to let the matter rest. They hounded Shen Qingqian relentlessly, forcing Blood Demon Hall—which hadn't even been called Blood Demon Hall back then—to relocate several times before finally retreating into the desolate spatial rift beneath the Abyssal Ocean.

Yet the Peerless Alliance's harassment of Shen Qingqian did not stop there.

They were incapable of capturing Shen Qingqian, but smearing her reputation was well within their capabilities. As a result, all manner of sordid, absurd rumors sprouted like weeds. It was only when Shen Qingqian achieved the miracle of rocketing from Soul Formation straight to the Body Integration stage in a mere five hundred years that the slander ceased on the surface. That is to say, it ceased on the surface only because the underhanded whispers grew all the more vicious—some even claimed she had sold her body to the demon race. From there, they pivoted to defaming Shen Qingqian's sect. It was precisely under these infuriating circumstances that Shen Qingqian, in a fit of fiery rage, had rebranded the sect as Blood Demon Hall, adopting the [edgy adolescent defiance](# "Literal CN: 中二病 (zhōng'èrbìng) — 'middle school 2nd-year syndrome' / chuunibyou; flamboyant adolescent delusions of grandeur and rebellion") of: *'You claim I've fallen into demonic ways? Fine, I'll truly show you what a demon looks like!'*

However, while Shen Qingqian had the formidable backing of the Body Integration stage to sustain such brazen defiance, her sect, Blood Demon Hall, did not.

After all, Blood Demon Hall was modest in scale and consisted predominantly of female cultivators. The Peerless Alliance was the exact opposite: as the largest coalition of cultivation sects across the southwestern Central Domain, virtually every cultivator who was not a rogue immortal belonged to its ranks. Even a powerhouse syndicate like the Wanhe Gang felt that [even a mighty dragon cannot crush a local serpent](# "Idiom: 强龙不压地头蛇 (qiáng lóng bù yā dìtóushé) — even a powerful outsider cannot suppress an entrenched local villain") in the southwest.

Under such conditions, even though not every individual in the Peerless Alliance harbored malice toward Blood Demon Hall, running into a single villain was troublesome enough. What was worse, sometimes it wasn't even active malice—the mere curious, appraising gazes people cast at "female body cultivators" was sufficient to generate endless distress.

Thus, wild though Shen Qingqian's personal conduct might be, she remained exceptionally cautious toward the outside world. She routinely kept her disciples shielded from rumors and peril, disguising Blood Demon Hall as an utterly unremarkable, run-of-the-mill body-refinement sect.

The issue lay with this recent Immortal Arts Assembly.

Because the tournament was hosted by the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Blood Demon Hall, as a cooperative partner, naturally had to show their support.

Shen Qingqian hadn't let down her guard just because she had established ties with the Heavenly Spirit Sect. She had still adhered to her standard prudence, dispatching mostly male disciples alongside only a small handful of female disciples to compete.

Yet the female cultivators of Blood Demon Hall were simply too [hyper-competitive](# "Literal CN: 卷 (juǎn) — hyper-involuted / excessively hardworking and competitive").

To avoid drawing unnecessary attention, the quotas allocated to female cultivators were painfully limited. Every girl had to rigorously prove her absolute supremacy before she was even permitted out the front door. As a result, the female disciples sent out into the world were all peerless powerhouses among their peers. Once they stepped onto the tournament grounds of the Immortal Arts Assembly, they swept across the arena like an unstoppable hurricane. They tore through every bracket and tier of the southwestern regional preliminaries, sweeping first place across every single cultivation realm and securing two-thirds of the regional qualification slots for the continental finals!

The southwestern region erupted into sheer bedlam.

The Peerless Alliance felt as though their faces had been thoroughly slapped off.

As the supreme overlord coalition of the southwest, they had actually been utterly trounced by a tiny fringe sect—and by a band of female cultivators, no less!

In an instant, all manner of filthy slander regarding Shen Qingqian was dredged up anew, this time dragging her disciples into the mud alongside her.

Shen Qingqian was so incandescent with rage that she wanted to march over and overturn the entire Peerless Alliance.

Bai Wubin was practically [balding from stress](# "Literal CN: 头秃 (tóutū) — shedding hair in stress / going bald from anxiety").

In truth, he fully supported giving those scoundrels in the Peerless Alliance a thorough thrashing. It wasn't as if they couldn't beat them—the alliance leader and his wife who had tormented Shen Qingqian centuries ago had been stuck at the early Void Refinement stage for ages. The problem was that as the regional hegemon of the southwest, the Peerless Alliance still had one or two Mahayana patriarchs and seven or eight Body Integration ancestors in reserve. Those old fogies rarely showed their faces on ordinary days, but if their alliance leader were butchered, they would undoubtedly intervene.

To make matters worse, Shen Qingqian was exceptionally stubborn. She refused to publicize Blood Demon Hall's alliance with the Heavenly Spirit Sect, nor was she willing to borrow the Untiring Immortal Venerable's terrifying prestige to intimidate those petty clowns. She insisted on resolving the dispute through sheer strength alone.

Bai Wubin genuinely worried that before Shen Qingqian managed to resolve the rumors, she would end up getting herself killed first.

It was precisely at this fraught juncture that the invitation to "inspect the learning outcomes of White Jade Capital Academy" arrived. For Bai Wubin, it was an immense relief.

Making a trip to the Heavenly Spirit Sect was for the best; perhaps it would allow Master to cool her head.

Even so, Bai Wubin couldn't help but feel bewildered. Why would the Immortal Venerable summon them to "inspect learning outcomes"? Shouldn't matters regarding examinations be handled by the teaching disciples?

Bai Wubin reported the matter to Shen Qingqian.

Upon hearing the summons, however, Shen Qingqian's face instantly twisted into an expression of mortal terror, as if facing an apocalyptic army.

She hadn't looked even a fraction this panicked when she was screaming curses at the Peerless Alliance moments ago.

—"Fu Ning, get over here,"-Shen Qingqian called to the disciple in charge of liaising with the Heavenly Spirit Sect on White Jade Capital Academy affairs.-"What was that teaching evaluation you mentioned a while back? I heard the mortal students' exam scores affect your teaching evaluation grades? Those evaluation grades won't affect Master, will they?"

[...Master, do you hear what's coming out of your mouth? Is that something a responsible teacher should say?]

Fu Ning was rendered utterly speechless by Shen Qingqian.

Yet Shen Qingqian was genuinely terrified down to her soul.

She feared neither heaven nor earth, but the moment she laid eyes on the Untiring Immortal Venerable, her legs began to tremble. In the beginning, it had been pure intimidation born of his overwhelming spiritual majesty; now, it was sheer horror that the Immortal Venerable might open his mouth and grill her on cultivation theory.

This was, in fact, the lingering trauma from drafting the White Jade Capital Academy textbooks.

Because body cultivation techniques focused on the physical vessel rather than spirit roots, even an omnipotent academic maniac like Zhou Hui who had mastered hundreds of arts had never personally cultivated a female body. Having happened upon a veteran professional like Shen Qingqian, he had naturally entrusted the girls' [Five Arts of Charioteering](# "Literal CN: 五驭 (wǔyù) — the Five Arts of Charioteering from the classical Six Arts; here adapted into foundational physical education and body refinement") curriculum to her.

Shen Qingqian had initially felt tremendously proud.

Praise from the Untiring Immortal Venerable was hard to come by! Furthermore, spreading her cultivation techniques—even to mortal children—would allow her to accumulate cosmic merit. Shen Qingqian naturally had no reason to refuse.

Until it came time to write the syllabus.

Shen Qingqian's blood-refinement manuals naturally could not be fed directly to children; they required structural adaptation from the ground up into a standardized textbook suitable for mortal youths. This was far more complex than merely dialing back the daily workout reps. With the Untiring Immortal Venerable's ruthless pedagogical rigor, he had naturally cross-examined Shen Qingqian relentlessly on the fundamental theoretical mechanics, interrogating her until she felt it was harder than breaking through to Body Integration had ever been:

[I just felt this way was right, so that's how I trained!]

[Where do all these 'whys' come from?!]

[What 'differing physiological effects of various elemental qi currents on the five viscera and six bowels'—you might as well just strike me dead with heavenly lightning!]

In the end, the textbook adaptation had only been completed with the direct assistance of the Untiring Immortal Venerable. By the time that curriculum was finalized, Shen Qingqian felt as though an entire layer of her skin had been peeled off, leaving her hollow and spiritually vacant.

The disciples of Blood Demon Hall, however, had been overjoyed.

They discovered that after surviving that academic "tribulation," their Sect Leader's lecture style had become vastly easier to comprehend. The lessons were now noticeably stripped of vague nonsense like *"go by feeling,"* *"once you feel it you'll know,"* and *"just feel it carefully and you'll understand."*

Consequently, over the past six months, the disciples of Blood Demon Hall had made astronomical strides in their cultivation, laying the very groundwork for their sweeping dominance in the Immortal Arts Assembly.

Of course, someone like Shen Qingqian was utterly oblivious to such subtle cause-and-effect dynamics.

Hearing the Untiring Immortal Venerable's summons—cross that out, *invitation*—her train of thought derailed entirely, exactly like a student who hadn't done her homework being called on by name by the homeroom teacher.

—"It's over!"-Shen Qingqian muttered under her breath.-"Could it be that the kids at White Jade Capital Academy bombed their Charioteering exam, and the Immortal Venerable thinks it's all my syllabus's fault?!"

—"Master, that shouldn't be the case,"-Fu Ning said calmly.-"Actually, the children tested quite well, so our teaching evaluation grades were quite decent too."

—"If they were decent, why didn't you say anything?"-Shen Qingqian demanded, her maternal-mentor instincts kicking into high gear.-"If you earned good marks, how could you possibly not come brag about it?"

It was because compared to their glorious sweep in the Immortal Arts Assembly, the academy's teaching evaluations really weren't something to write home about.

[It wasn't that we didn't pass the evaluation... we just didn't win!]

Fu Ning thought with profound grief.

In truth, competition among the instructional posts at White Jade Capital Academy was hyper-involuted to an insane degree.

After all, for the disciples of Blood Demon Hall, teaching at White Jade Capital Academy was an exceptionally lucrative assignment.

Because the academy was operated by the Heavenly Spirit Sect, Blood Demon Hall disciples dispatched there received the full compensation package of Heavenly Spirit Sect instructors. Not only were there ample spirit stones and welfare stipends, but they also had dedicated faculty quarters and could regularly stroll through the Immortals' Bazaar for recreation. That was infinitely more entertaining than being cooped up inside a subterranean spatial rift beneath the Abyssal Ocean.

To protect this cushy post, the disciples of Blood Demon Hall worked with frantic diligence, terrified of being sent back.

Anywhere else, ordinary cultivators would have been crushed into dust by the insane work ethic of Blood Demon Hall's disciples. But what kind of place was the Heavenly Spirit Sect? It was the ancestral homeland of academic tryhards!

The native instructors of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were already the most obsessively hardworking cohort in the sect. The moment they saw Blood Demon Hall's disciples putting in overtime, they immediately panicked:

[If we lose to you outsiders, where will the Heavenly Spirit Sect put its face?!]

[We won't even need Master to punish us; we'll hang ourselves from the peach tree!]

And so, if you kept students five minutes past the bell, I would keep them ten minutes; if you refused to dismiss class on time, I would hold mandatory remedial study sessions over the weekend! Thus, the various instructors had violently escalated the workload, turning themselves into an unstoppable engine of academic pressure.

Under such conditions, Blood Demon Hall's disciples naturally couldn't achieve a total blowout; managing to keep their footing without being outcompeted was already an accomplishment.

Yet Fu Ning's silence only deepened Shen Qingqian's misunderstanding.

—"The Immortal Venerable wouldn't be demanding that I rewrite the syllabus from scratch, would he? I really can't write another word!"-Shen Qingqian wailed, turning a pleading gaze toward Bai Wubin.-"How about this: I won't go. You go to the Heavenly Spirit Sect in my stead and tell the Immortal Venerable that I fell ill."

—"Master, you are at the Body Integration stage,"-Bai Wubin said, staring at Shen Qingqian with utter helplessness.-"Barring [the Five Decays of Heaven and Man](# "Literal CN: 天人五衰 (tiānrén wǔshuāi) — the five signs of decay signaling the death of celestials/deities"), Body Integration cultivators do not fall ill."

—"Then tell him I'm undergoing the Five Decays of Heaven and Man!"

—"Master, that is the omen of impending death..."

—"I'm already on the verge of dying! If I have to write another textbook, I will definitely die!"-Shen Qingqian wailed in despair.

---

In the end, of course, Shen Qingqian still brought Bai Wubin and her disciples along to the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Bellowing and whining was one thing, but that was hardly grounds to decline an invitation from the Untiring Immortal Venerable.

She was simply burdened with the dread of a truant student about to be interrogated in class, leaving her nerves stretched taut.

Fortunately, once aboard the Kunpeng shuttle, Shen Qingqian swiftly realized the purpose of the invitation was vastly different from what she had imagined. There were numerous high-tier cultivators aboard the vessel, all seemingly traveling for the sake of White Jade Capital Academy's exams as well.

Still, when had these lofty, top-tier powerhouses ever cared about mortal affairs?

—"Do you know what's going on?"-Shen Qingqian asked Bai Wubin.

As reliable as always, Bai Wubin had already gathered intelligence prior to departure:

—"I heard that Young Master Jian Nuo of Sky City has arrived at the Heavenly Spirit Sect,"-Bai Wubin said.

—"Just a young master from Sky City? That hardly warrants all this, does it?"-Shen Qingqian asked, raising an eyebrow.

After all, even if it were Jian Nuo, his cultivation realm was not particularly high.

He was merely at the Nascent Soul stage. For Void Refinement and Body Integration grandmasters to swallow their pride and fawn over him would be a bit much.

—"I heard that Young Master Jian is exceptionally intrigued by the examinations of White Jade Capital Academy,"-Bai Wubin explained further, lowering his voice.-"And that examination is being held inside the First City of White Jade Capital!"

—"White Jade Capital?"-Shen Qingqian's eyes widened.

She was naturally well aware of the recent continental fervor surrounding the Ascension Stairway and White Jade Capital. However, having known about White Jade Capital's existence long beforehand and realizing that the Untiring Immortal Venerable had merely been waiting for an opportune moment to unveil it, she wasn't quite as stunned as the rest of the world.

In truth, she was actually far more interested in the Ascension Stairway. Not that she wished to ascend—she merely wanted to test how high she could climb in a single breath using raw physical power.

Nevertheless, for White Jade Capital Academy's examinations to be hosted directly inside White Jade Capital was something Shen Qingqian had never anticipated.

[...Wait. Come to think of it, why did he name it White Jade Capital Academy right from the start? What on earth has the Immortal Venerable been scheming all along?]

Shen Qingqian finally began to pay attention to the actual nature of the test:

—"Speaking of which, White Jade Capital Academy's final exams are already over—your teaching evaluation grades have already been issued. What kind of test are they conducting right now?"

[...Only now did you think to ask?]

The corner of Bai Wubin's mouth twitched.

—"It's a spirit root assessment,"-Bai Wubin answered.

—"A spirit root assessment?"-Shen Qingqian asked in bewilderment.-"Isn't White Jade Capital Academy a school for mortals?"

To adapt the Blood Demon Manual into a regimen suitable for mortals, she had wracked her brains to exhaustion!

—"Even though they are mortals, they can still operate magical artifacts; furthermore, the things they can achieve are surprisingly vast,"-Fu Ning chimed in to explain.-"According to the Immortal Venerable, as long as a living being in this realm possesses a soul, they possess spiritual essence. As for how to channel that spiritual qi..."

She paused mid-sentence, noticing Shen Qingqian's blank, utterly glazed-over stare.

Considering her master's limited brain capacity, Fu Ning decisively switched to the public explanation everyone understood:

—"...Furthermore, mortal academy though it is, that doesn't mean none of the children possess cultivation potential. The Heavenly Spirit Sect grants priority enrollment to promising cultivator seedlings discovered within the academy."

—"Ah! When you put it that way, this year is a [Grand Recruitment Year](# "Literal CN: 大招之年 (dàzhāo zhī nián) — a major recruitment year when sects prioritize inducting new disciples from affiliated mortal settlements")!"

This time, Shen Qingqian understood immediately.

After all, this was standard practice for most cultivation sects.

Almost every sect had affiliated mortal villages under its wing. Even in the Northern Domain with its massive mortal empires, cities and towns fell under the spheres of influence of various cultivation factions.

Typically, cultivation sects were responsible for policing these regions against rampaging demons and uncontrollable calamities, while the mortal villages paid protection fees—cross that out, *offered incense and tribute*. Naturally, when recruitment season rolled around, these sects gave priority admission to outer and inner disciples selected from the mortal settlements in their territory. Such a cycle was known as a "Grand Recruitment Year."

Shen Qingqian had embarked upon the immortal path through this very mechanism, and virtually all her disciples had been recruited in a similar fashion.

[Though I suppose we can forget about recruiting anyone this year, can't we?]

Shen Qingqian frowned in thought.

Due to its hidden geographic location, Blood Demon Hall possessed no direct subordinate mortal villages. They had always operated on the model of [salvaging overlooked gems from rivals' territory](# "Literal CN: 捡漏 (jiǎnlòu) — picking up overlooked leaks / snapping up gems missed by others"). That was another major reason why their sect had so many female disciples: setting aside incompatible manuals, the southwest was heavily dominated by body-refinement sects that favored men, meaning their quotas were overwhelmingly male. Unless a girl was extraordinarily exceptional, entering a major sect was nearly impossible, so Blood Demon Hall scooped them up instead.

This year, however, Blood Demon Hall was being actively targeted by the Peerless Alliance. They would undoubtedly sabotage any recruitment efforts and smear Blood Demon Hall's name among the mortals, making recruitment virtually impossible.

—"Ugh, what a headache!"-Shen Qingqian decided not to dwell on it, asking instead:-"Still, is there really any need to go all the way to White Jade Capital for something like this?"

—"I heard they require more precise numerical data collection..."-Fu Ning said.

Once again, she slipped into academic jargon that Shen Qingqian couldn't fathom—and this time, even Bai Wubin was left scratching his head.

—"It's likely meant to showcase White Jade Capital as well,"-Bai Wubin analyzed, drawing upon the prevailing consensus among outside cultivators he had spoken to.-"First, to entertain Young Master Jian; second, to open a portion of White Jade Capital for outsiders to observe. Of course, since they aren't scaling the Ascension Stairway to reach it, it's merely a sightseeing tour to give everyone a concrete concept of White Jade Capital."

[...I don't think the Untiring Immortal Venerable has that intention at all.]

[Even if that was part of it, it certainly isn't the most important purpose.]

[The true purpose is precisely what the Heavenly Spirit Sect announced: a pure spirit root assessment for the mortal academy.]

[The Immortal Venerable places an extraordinary amount of importance on those mortal children!]

Fu Ning mused inwardly.

Still, even if she explained this to outside cultivators, would any of them ever comprehend it?

Fu Ning exchanged glances with the other disciples who served as instructors at White Jade Capital Academy, realizing there was an immense cognitive divide between themselves and their fellow sect members. Setting aside their master—whose mental wavelength had always been beyond their comprehension—even Senior Brother Bai viewed things through the conventional lens.

Amid Blood Demon Hall's hushed deliberations, the Kunpeng did not head toward Mount Tianling as usual. Instead, it came to a halt in an unfamiliar sector of the Abyssal Ocean.

The seawater here was an eerie, abyssal blue, its aura turbulent and dense—evidently close to the most deeply corrupted depths of the Central Domain.

Yet the cultivators aboard were hardly surprised. The Ascension Stairway had risen from ancient ruins within the Abyssal Ocean; arriving here meant they had come to the right place. They simply waited quietly for the transit bubbles to emerge.

This time, however, the disembarkation protocol was entirely different from before.

Normally, when heading to the Immortals' Bazaar, passengers aboard the Kunpeng disembarked all at once regardless of cultivation, carried by bubbles toward the market entrances matching their respective tiers. This time, because there was neither a colossal coral reef nor a sprawling bazaar, disembarkation proceeded in carefully segregated batches.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect paid no heed to the high-ranking cultivators. Instead, they allowed the children of White Jade Capital Academy to disembark first!

—It was only at this moment that the gathered cultivators realized there were mortal children aboard the Kunpeng.

Before their eyes, translucent, shimmering bubbles drifted upward through the deep water, each carrying a child inside. They were dressed in uniform scholarly robes, their backs held arrow-straight. Though nervousness peeked through on some faces and others appeared stiff with tension, every single pair of eyes shone with undisguised yearning, hope, and an extraordinary vitality.

Somewhere among the crowd, an incredulous voice slipped out:

[—"Are those... mortals?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator Spectator")

---

[—"Is this the immortal city?"](# "Speaker: Academy Student")

Simultaneously, the children of White Jade Capital Academy asked in hushed whispers inside their bubbles.

They were completely unaware that the dark ocean water outside carried demonic corruption potent enough to erode them down to the bone in a fraction of an instant. Shielded safely within the softly glowing bubbles, they swayed gently as they floated toward the sky.

These bubbles did not dock along the seabed as usual. For the first time, they breached the ocean surface, rising out of the Abyssal Ocean and soaring straight up into the heavens—of course, given the unfathomable depth of the Abyssal Ocean, such an ascent was by no means simple. It was powered directly by the spellcraft of the Untiring Immortal Venerable. Yet such profound spatial magic was something even the Body Integration powerhouses aboard the Kunpeng could not replicate; the children simply had no concept of how miraculous it was.

The wide-eyed children merely watched in awe as the world above grew radiant. Then, the towering white jade stairway manifested before their eyes, and higher still, a colossal celestial city of white jade sprawled across the clouds.

Although the Ascension Stairway was visible from every corner of this realm, confronting a stairway that pierced the cosmos face-to-face was an entirely different experience.

Children who had only studied for half a semester completely forgot all the poetic praises they had learned in [the Six Arts of Calligraphy and Literacy](# "Literal CN: 六书 (liùshū) — the Six Scripts / Six Principles of Chinese character composition; here referring to primary school literacy class"), uttering only pure, heartfelt gasps:

[—"It's so huge!"](# "Speaker: Academy Student")

[—"Can we really go up there?"](# "Speaker: Academy Student")

[—"Look, there are so many immortals too!"](# "Speaker: Academy Student")

Indeed, a vast throng of cultivators had already gathered near the Ascension Stairway.

They had not arrived aboard the Kunpeng; rather, they were proactive individuals who had rushed toward the Ascension Stairway the very instant it manifested, attempting to climb it.

Unfortunately, it was by no means that simple.

Though Zhou Hui had climbed it effortlessly alongside Yuwen Xuan and Ran Deng on that fateful day—seemingly repairing the stairway without meeting any resistance—in reality, ordinary cultivators quickly discovered that walking upon the Ascension Stairway was vastly more grueling than exploring the perilous depths of the Abyssal Ocean.

To date, not a single person had managed to climb past the thousandth step.

And yet on this day, whether these cultivators knew about White Jade Capital Academy's exams or not, they watched in disbelief as a procession of children, riding in glowing bubbles and lined up in orderly rows, floated effortlessly past them toward the celestial city above. It was impossible for their expressions not to contort with envy, jealousy, and bitter resentment.

Faced with such piercing, covetous stares, the children grew all the more anxious.

—"What are you afraid of? We worked hard to earn our place here,"-a stern voice rang out.

It was Eleventh Lady Mei from the Sixth Academy.

Hearing her words, children from other schools merely felt their spirits bolster slightly. But the children hailing from the Sixth Academy wore far more complicated expressions.

Indeed, they had worked hard.

What the outside cultivators did not know was that qualifying for this spirit root assessment depended strictly on final examination scores. Several core subjects had mandatory cutoff lines; otherwise, even if a child possessed a spirit root, they were required to study until they achieved passing marks before they were permitted to take the assessment.

Yet compared to other academies, for the Sixth Academy—which had started out with the worst study habits of all—to have qualified so many students, it was undeniable that Eleventh Lady Mei's aggressive methods of [exhorting them to study](# "Literal CN: 劝学 (quànxué) — 'Encouraging Study' / Exhortation to Learning, alluding to Xunzi's classical treatise; here referring to Eleventh Lady Mei's aggressive academic drill-sergeant methods") deserved the lion's share of credit.

In this moment, even those who usually loathed Eleventh Lady Mei's strictness couldn't bring themselves to hate her anymore.

Had it not been for her ruthless prodding, they might never have had the chance to stand here today.

—"Why did Father and Mother say school was useless?"-someone muttered quietly.-"Have they ever seen a sight like this?"

Hearing this, many of the children sank into deep contemplation.

Whatever the case, they were overwhelmingly grateful that the ones standing here today were themselves, and not their brothers.

Yet among all the children, the ones harboring the most complex thoughts and expressions were the undercover moles who had been abandoned by Carefree Palace. They had previously believed they were fundamentally different from the mortals around them; they had already witnessed the "grand spectacles" of the cultivation world, and believed that if they performed meritorious deeds, they would surely earn a chance to join their ranks.

Even if those immortal lords treated them without a shred of respect and regarded them as disposable playthings, who told them to have bad luck and be born without spirit roots? To serve immortals in mortal flesh was already considered their greatest fortune.

Until this very moment.

Looking at the naked jealousy and burning envy written across the faces of those high-and-mighty cultivators, they finally realized that here in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, there was another path open to them—a path where they did not have to sell their dignity.

Amid these intricate thoughts far beyond their tender years, the academy students finally arrived before the lowest city of white jade.

And that once-unattainable celestial fortress swung its grand gates wide, welcoming every single one of them without distinction.
