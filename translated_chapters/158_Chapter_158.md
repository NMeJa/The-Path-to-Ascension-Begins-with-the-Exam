# Chapter 158: Unrest (第158章 骚动)

Zhou Hui silently watched her laugh uproariously, utterly baffled as to where the goddess found the humor.

—"My apologies, I was impolite,"-the goddess apologized shyly once her laughter subsided.-"What you said is quite true. Following the completion of world creation, the primordial deities truly do, alongside nature's manifestation, attain [spirit-sentience](# "Literal CN: 成精 (chéngjīng) — spirit-monsters gaining sentience/transformation, used humorously for gods")... *pfft*... *cough*. They are generally rather willful nature gods. Later, sapient beings are born. Initially, they worship these nature gods, but as their civilizations advance, 'mortal gods' come into being. For reasons unknown, mortal gods tend to be stronger, eventually driving the nature gods out..."

—"'For reasons unknown'?"-Zhou Hui repeated.

—"Yes, I don't know... Uh, based on experience? Generally speaking..."-the goddess stammered.

She wasn't lying. She possessed copious experience functioning as a god, but she had never once stopped to ponder the *why*. To her, everything simply unfolded naturally. That was precisely why hearing Zhou Hui's "spirit-monster hypothesis" had provoked such helpless laughter.

In truth, there was nothing inherently wrong with her attitude.

Most of the time, "everyone" operated purely on empiricism—and that empiricism included "gods."

Yet beneath Zhou Hui's gaze today, an inexplicable pang of guilt washed over the goddess, even though she genuinely had no idea what fundamental laws governed the "historical precedents" she had witnessed countless times.

Seeing her reaction, Zhou Hui grasped the situation and let out a soft sigh.

The goddess felt even guiltier.

—"Mortal gods displace nature gods. And after that?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"They take charge of the rules and become the objects of faith for living beings,"-the goddess murmured hesitantly, before her expression turned solemn.-"However, once a world develops to that stage, *they* inevitably arrive."

—"*They*?"-Zhou Hui prompted.

—"The Heavenly Court,"-the goddess said in a cold voice.

She began to explain in greater detail:

—"The moment sapient beings begin to think, they inevitably attract inner demons, and other fiendish monsters follow in their wake. When that happens, True Immortals descend into that world under the righteous guise of slaying demons and expelling evils, winning the reverent acclaim of the world's inhabitants. Just as you postulated, if deities are rules attaining sentience, then once the thoughts of the populace are tainted and guided, the deities born at that stage inevitably belong to the Heavenly Court. The cosmic rules of that world—namely, its Heavenly Dao—fall entirely under the Heavenly Court's control."

—"...Is this what your world went through?"-Zhou Hui could not resist asking.

He had assumed her miniature world was stripped away purely through brute force; he had not expected it had traversed the "legitimate procedures" of the Heavenly Court.

—"Even for a dust-mote world, tearing it away from its original trajectory is no trivial feat,"-the goddess said resentfully.-"The Heavenly Court's interventions are, in truth, calculated methods to seize and subjugate a world."

She was visibly reluctant to dwell upon those nauseating memories. Her only aim right now was to repay Zhou Hui's assistance by sharing everything she knew, giving him a foundational awareness:

—"Furthermore, the Heavenly Court possesses numerous methods to reshape a world. For instance, providing a so-called path of ascension so candidate deities of that world can assume posts in the Heavenly Court, or even stripping away divine authorities under the pretense that someone has 'descended to the mortal dust.'"

Hearing this, Zhou Hui frowned and turned his gaze upon her.

[Could Qianxing be...]

—"Just as you suspect,"-the goddess confirmed with a nod.-"Your disciple is in precisely that situation."

She elaborated further:

—"In theory, stellar authority over constellations belongs natively to each individual minor world. The proof is that even in worlds devoid of the Heavenly Court's meddling, 'Celestial Officials' still exist—or rather, beings naturally gifted at commanding that specific power. But if the Heavenly Court brands these Celestial Officials as having 'descended to the mortal realm,' intending to summon them back later... No, they don't even need to summon them back. As long as the myriad mortals perceive that these figures originated from the heavens above, that portion of divine authority falls into the Heavenly Court's grasp."

Zhou Hui let out a cold scoff:

—"My, what a splendid track record of good deeds they have."

—"That is why reclaiming this portion of authority, as your world has done, is extraordinarily difficult. Or rather, this is the very first time I have ever witnessed it! To actually reclaim it... As the 'god' of this world, your divine godhead will naturally be elevated,"-the goddess remarked admiringly.

Hearing this, Zhou Hui remained nonchalant:

—"Stellar authority, you say... That was Qianxing's doing. It has nothing to do with me."

—"You are far too modest. Constellation authority must anchor itself to a divine godhead. If your world lacked your presence, even if the stars wished to return to their rightful stations, they would only lose their way!"-the goddess chuckled.

She regarded Zhou Hui with curious eyes:

—"However, an existence like yourself—attaining a divine godhead in a mature world without passing through the Heavenly Court—is extraordinarily rare. At first, I assumed you were like me, hatched directly from a newborn world!"

—"Hatched from a newborn world?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Indeed. Didn't you ask about Creator Deities earlier? That is how we come into being,"-the goddess said.-"Following your hypothesis, I suppose you could call it a world attaining spirit-sentience, haha."

The goddess gave a dry chuckle, only to notice Zhou Hui staring at her thoughtfully.

—"Is something the matter, Lord Immortal Venerable?"-she asked.

—"A world attaining spirit-sentience... In other words, you possess a certain understanding of a minor world's primordial source?"-Zhou Hui inquired.-"Would you mind if I posed a few questions regarding the genesis of a world?"

The smile froze on the goddess's face. Having observed this Immortal Venerable for quite a while, she had realized he was not merely terrifyingly capable, but also possessed an obsessive streak. Coupled with her recent "experience," the last thing she wanted was to answer any of Zhou Hui's "academic" inquiries.

—"...Al-Although we are called ancestral gods, we are actually very weak,"-the goddess said, timidly showing vulnerability.-"Just as nature gods are ultimately supplanted by mortal gods, the purpose of an ancestral god is merely to guide a world's birth, ultimately feeding back into the world itself. Generally speaking, those who attain godhood later, like yourself, are vastly stronger."

She gazed at Zhou Hui reverently—partly hoping he would lose interest, and partly out of genuine awe.

Yet Zhou Hui continued to scrutinize her in deep contemplation. His curiosity regarding divine godheads had nothing to do with power or weakness; it was purely academic curiosity. Naturally, it was *only* curiosity—he couldn't very well dissect the goddess! That wouldn't help matters in the slightest.

The goddess, however, felt her hair stand on end, especially when she noticed Zhou Hui parting his lips to probe further.

—"*Cough, cough*! Theoretical matters aside,"-the goddess blurted out in haste,-"as an incompetent deity who failed to protect her own world, this is all I know regarding the Heavenly Court. I hope it proves useful to you! B-But this is all common knowledge anyone could learn with enough digging anyway. The 'way of being a god' I truly wished to share isn't this—it's how to compile the Heavenly Dao!"

—"Compile the Heavenly Dao?"-Zhou Hui's attention was finally diverted.

—"Yes. Thanks to your aid, this world has finally regained some stability,"-the goddess said.-"However, due to the prior devastation, this world's Heavenly Dao is an utter mess. If left as is, living beings will never be able to establish lasting order. Thus, I intend to recompile the Heavenly Dao from scratch. I believe this process might offer you some inspiration."

—"That is precisely what I require,"-Zhou Hui said.-"My sincere thanks."

And indeed it was.

Zhou Hui had long sought to rewrite the Heavenly Dao, but he had only gained actual access to it recently after acquiring his divine godhead. Moreover, he was no innate deity endowed with innate omniscience from birth. Even when gazing upon that tangled rat's nest of a Heavenly Dao, he lacked the means to revise, debug, and extract advantageous data from it. That was why he had previously resorted to the simplest, most brutal, and most reckless measure available: risking exposure to the Heavenly Court to slay the god manipulating the Heavenly Dao.

Yet that had merely been an expedient stopgap.

That so-called deity had clearly been nothing more than a glorified gatekeeper. Without an effective mechanism to oversee his own world's Heavenly Dao, a similar incident would recur sooner or later.

In truth, setting future threats aside, that Heavenly Dao was already cluttered with an enormous quantity of external scripts. While Zhou Hui was familiar with the portion comprising the "main storyline," much like a shoddy third-rate web novel, its "background subplots" were choked with an absurd tangle of loose threads that even the original author had forgotten: the victims of retaliation from the God-Demon War; the mystery of Sky City Lord Jian Haoge's disappearance in the "main plot"; the seemingly forgotten patriarch of the Ran Clan; and countless other messy loose ends.

Even someone like Zhou Hui felt as though he were staring at an [ancestral mountain of spaghetti code](# "Literal CN: 祖传的代码shi山 (ancestral mountain of shit code) — programmer slang for a horrific, unmaintainable legacy codebase inherited across generations"); merely looking at it gave him a headache.

At a juncture like this, observing how another deity debugged and rewired a Heavenly Dao was utterly indispensable.

Zhou Hui had a mountain of questions:

—"Regarding the Heavenly Dao—"

—"It is nearly impossible to describe in mere words!"-the goddess interrupted firmly.-"Fortunately(?) my world is severely damaged, so I will rebuild it starting from the most rudimentary fundamentals. However, I can only perform this once, and I must concentrate fully throughout; I will be unable to answer any questions."

—"...Very well,"-Zhou Hui conceded with palpable regret.

The goddess finally let out a silent sigh of relief:

—"Then I shall begin. Lord Immortal Venerable, please guide me... Ah, please observe—"

With that, the goddess began calibrating and compiling the Heavenly Dao of the miniature world.

Zhou Hui stood quietly to the side, fixing his gaze upon her just as she had asked.

He had no intention of missing a single detail.

***

While Zhou Hui was absorbed in studying how to rewrite the Heavenly Dao, on the other side of the sect, several of his disciples were pacing in circles like ants on a hot skillet.

—"Has Master still not returned?"-Guan Ye asked.

Mo Bei, who had just flown down from above Zhou Hui's immortal cave upon her flying sword, shook her head expressionlessly.

Guan Ye knitted his brows:

—"Strange. I never heard anything about Master heading out!"

Mo Bei offered additional context:

—"Master likely hasn't left the mountain. I saw that his desk remained untidy, with freshly dipped brush and ink still wet. His outer robe was draped across the folding screen as well. It feels as though he temporarily stepped into some mustard-seed space. What's more, when I peeked inside just now, I caught a faint, familiar wisp of divine sense brushing past. Master ought to have 'seen' me and knows the situation out here, yet he hasn't emerged."

Guan Ye's face instantly looked as though he had been struck by lightning:

—"So Master intends for us to handle this ourselves?!"

—"Presumably. It wasn't particularly difficult to begin with,"-Mo Bei said flatly.

Guan Ye glared at her:

—"If you think it's not difficult, why don't you handle it?"

—"You are my senior brother. For me to take the stage would be [overstepping my bounds](# "Idiom: 越俎代庖 (yuè zǔ dài páo) — stepping outside one's role to handle someone else's affair")."

—"No, no, Little Junior Sister is the sect's public face right now! I'm certain everyone would much rather see you."

—"Fourth Senior Brother's presence carries far more authority."

—"Little Junior Sister is far more popular."

—"..."

—"..."

After mutually dodging responsibility for a good while, Guan Ye and Mo Bei turned their heads in unison toward the Palace of Silence and let out a collective sigh.

Even gazing from afar, they could keenly perceive the overwhelmingly powerful and chaotic tangle of divine senses permeating the area.

Those belonged to the heavyweights of various sects who had come to ["seek scripture"](# "Metaphor: 取经 (qǔjīng) — borrowing instructional wisdom or educational experience, alluding to Journey to the West").

Following the White Jade Capital spirit root assessments administered across the primary schools, numerous sects had displayed keen interest in adopting the standardized textbooks. A relentless stream of sect managers and high-tier titans had arrived at Heavenly Spirit Sect to negotiate curriculum imports and conduct instructional observation tours.

Naturally, inter-sect "diplomatic relations" of this sort had never had anything to do with Guan Ye or Mo Bei. With their all-capable future Sect Master Qu Qianxing in charge, every affair was run with clockwork precision; on the rare occasion a detail slipped through, Mo Xi was there to patch it up.

Yet who could have foreseen this?

Their impeccably reliable Second Senior Brother had suddenly broken through and triggered his heavenly tribulation!

It wasn't that Qu Qianxing wasn't ready to undergo tribulation. Given his cultivation, he could have breached his current bottleneck at any time; he had merely been refining his foundation to absolute perfection, biding his time for the ideal opportunity.

This sudden breakthrough indicated that he had stumbled upon a magnificent stroke of karmic fortune, allowing nature to take its course.

Guan Ye reasoned thus, feeling genuinely thrilled for his second senior brother.

The problem, however, was that Second Senior Brother's tribulation was far too colossal and theatrical! For a "mere" Nascent Soul tribulation, the lightning clouds blanketed an enormous expanse of territory, drumming up an apocalyptic spectacle worthy of ascending to the upper three realms. Overlooking it was an absolute impossibility.

Such a thunderous commotion naturally arrested the full attention of the visiting sect titans.

Just as they had once scrambled to observe the Untiring Immortal Venerable's heavenly tribulation, they fixated upon Qu Qianxing's tribulation with equal—if not greater—intensity.

After all, the Untiring Immortal Venerable's tribulation had been far too high-tier; none of them could fathom how he had accomplished it. But Qu Qianxing was the Immortal Venerable's designated successor, undoubtedly privy to his master's authentic teachings—as evidenced by the staggering commotion. Furthermore, Qu Qianxing was "merely" forming his Nascent Soul. Many visiting powerhouses, confident in their own cultivation realms, believed they stood a fair chance of deciphering his methods.

Self-serving motives aside, Qu Qianxing's cultivation progress served as the ultimate testament to the Untiring Immortal Venerable's pedagogical prowess. While Qu Qianxing was exceptionally brilliant on ordinary days, when it came to a "grand final exam" like a breakthrough tribulation, the visiting sect stewards eager to study teaching methodologies were dying to inspect Qu Qianxing's final "exam score."

Thus, the titans abandoned their scheduled itinerary and rushed in a collective mob to spectate Qu Qianxing.

Yet Villa Master Qingyang firmly shut the door in their faces.

Eternal Villa had never signed up for any instructional showcase program, so naturally he had no intention of throwing open his family's vital secret realm. What was more, Villa Master Qingyang's justification was unassailable:

—"A heavenly tribulation is an affair of supreme consequence! How could anyone permit spectators? What if your presence disrupts Qianxing'er's mental state?"

Having declared this, Villa Master Qingyang sealed the entire Spirit Stone Secret Realm with formidable restrictions, ensuring not even a mosquito could slip inside.

Faced with this lockdown, the titans had no choice but to retreat to Heavenly Spirit Sect, seeking firsthand intelligence on Qu Qianxing. This unexpectedly trapped Guan Ye and Mo Bei in a corner so harrowing they desperately wanted to flee.

Under the current circumstances, even Lu Shulong was stationed over at Qu Qianxing's side. Not a single direct disciple capable of managing "diplomatic PR" was present, leaving behind only the two individuals least suited for the task: Guan Ye and Mo Bei.

To make matters worse, beyond the direct disciples, most of Heavenly Spirit Sect's inner and outer sect disciples had also scampered off to the secret realm to help out. The entire sect was practically deserted, suffering a catastrophic personnel shortage.

And most fatal of all, while everyone was clamoring to learn the specifics of Qu Qianxing's tribulation, Guan Ye and Mo Bei had been sequestered in their caves refining artifacts and practicing swordplay, completely deaf to worldly affairs. They had only discovered that the person undergoing tribulation was their own second senior brother when external guests came knocking! How could they possibly possess any updated information?!

Why couldn't those people just question Eternal Villa directly?!

Eternal Villa certainly knew far better!

Massaging his temples, Guan Ye groaned:

—"Have you still not managed to contact Mo Xi?"

—"No,"-Mo Bei shook her head.-"Not anytime soon."

After all, that fool of a brother had excitedly run off to meddle in Second Senior Brother's business. Word had it he had gotten his hands on an academic paper a few days back. Mo Bei reckoned that given her idiot brother's academic capability, there was no chance he would show his face in public before squeezing that paper out.

Having completely given up hope on locating Mo Xi, Mo Bei turned to Guan Ye:

—"Fourth Senior Brother, didn't you go looking for Eldest Senior Sister earlier? She should be around, right?"

While Eldest Senior Sister could hardly be considered an extrovert, she was at least the scion of a prominent formation clan. Her social etiquette was beyond reproach, making her an adequate substitute in an unfortunate pinch like this.

Guan Ye, however, let out a weary sigh:

—"Given her current state, dragging her out of her cave dwelling would be downright cruel."

Recalling Ran Deng's recent half-dead, comatose condition, Mo Bei fell silent as well.

—"...It seems we have no choice but to bite the bullet and handle it ourselves,"-Guan Ye sighed.

Mo Bei's face contorted in overt revulsion.

She would rather slaughter every last one of them than make small talk with a crowd. It was far too exhausting; she truly had no idea how Mo Xi managed to revel in it.

Mulling it over, Mo Bei's expression suddenly brightened with rare animation:

—"Fourth Senior Brother, haven't we forgotten someone?"

—"..."-Guan Ye followed Mo Bei's gaze toward a certain direction.-"Please, no! It's rare enough that he hasn't caused trouble recently... Or rather, he's been so unnervingly quiet of late that I constantly suspect he's brewing some apocalyptic disaster, even if I have no proof."

—"Then will you entertain the guests?"-Mo Bei countered.

—"..."-Guan Ye fell into dead silence.

He stared toward the Palace of Silence, watching the dense throng of guests bustling to and fro.

Finally, after an agonizing fifteen minutes of hesitation, Guan Ye forced the words through gritted teeth:

—"Shall we... go invite Eldest Senior Brother?"

***

[—"The thunderclouds have dissipated. It looks like the tribulation succeeded?"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"That goes without saying! That second disciple's cultivation was already brimming at full capacity. If anything, it's strange that he only triggered his tribulation now."](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"As expected, Heavenly Spirit Sect possesses secret arts of their own."](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"A pity we couldn't get close enough to observe."](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

[—"Still, isn't it rather odd? Why would a direct disciple of Heavenly Spirit Sect undergo tribulation inside Eternal Villa?"](# "Speaker: Visiting Cultivator")

The visiting cultivators engaged in lively discussion.

They harbored no malice.

At this stage, the sects interested in Heavenly Spirit Sect's textbooks and willing to visit in person were largely predisposed toward goodwill. Their chatter was born of pure curiosity.

Villa Master Qingyang, on the other hand, was growing increasingly distressed as he eavesdropped on these discussions.

At that moment, he was lurking in a secluded corner, just as eager as Guan Ye and Mo Bei to avoid contact with any living soul.

Yet unlike the socially reclusive pair, Villa Master Qingyang was a certified [social butterfly](# "Literal CN: 社牛 (shèniú) — social extrovert/butterfly who fears no crowd"); he harbored zero fear of conversing with people. His reluctance to step out stemmed entirely from the fact that the Spirit Stone Secret Realm was Eternal Villa's ancestral domain. Compared to the utterly clueless Guan Ye and Mo Bei, Villa Master Qingyang was the one person who knew *exactly* what had transpired down there.

And precisely because he knew, his head throbbed with agony.

There was nothing inherently secret about Qianxing'er undergoing tribulation. If anything, as Qu Qianxing's interim master, Villa Master Qingyang felt an inexplicable surge of pride; had the abyss below not led to the Demonic Realm, he would have loved nothing more than to erect a stage on the spot for Qu Qianxing to show off!

Yes—if only the abyss below wasn't the Demonic Realm!

Why was it that during his brief absence, Qingyu and Qianxing'er had managed to mount an expedition straight into the Demonic Realm?! And the Untiring Immortal Venerable hadn't made the slightest move to stop them! What was more, Qianxing'er's tribulation seemed to involve eradicating a demonic abomination that had possessed the young master of the Northern Domain's Liu family. (Or so he had heard; they would likely have to explain to the Liu family later how their precious child had vanished into thin air.)

With all these complications compounding one another, even a seasoned titan like Villa Master Qingyang felt overwhelmed.

While he wholeheartedly supported Qu Qianxing and trusted both his daughter's and Qu Qianxing's prowess to keep the Demonic Realm in check, outsiders didn't know that!

Heavenly Spirit Sect was currently teeming with a chaotic mix of guests. While their enthusiasm for acquiring textbooks was sincere enough, who knew how they would react if they caught wind of the Demonic Realm!

In short, Villa Master Qingyang firmly believed this was the worst possible moment for full disclosure. Yet with Qu Qianxing's lightning tribulation having drawn every pair of eyes in the vicinity, Villa Master Qingyang had had no choice but to resort to a crude, heavy-handed total lockdown.

Even so, he knew full well that barricades couldn't stop rumors forever. Even if the Untiring Immortal Venerable personally issued an injunction, meddlesome busybodies would inevitably poke around to confirm what had happened in the Spirit Stone Secret Realm.

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, please manifest your divine presence!"-Villa Master Qingyang prayed, pressing his palms together.

Evidently, he was just as desperate to [pass the buck](# "Idiom: 甩锅 (shuǎi guō) — shift blame or pass the buck").

As if answering Villa Master Qingyang's prayer, the rumble of thunder rolled across the heavens.

*Hmm? Thunder?*

Villa Master Qingyang looked up in astonishment, witnessing colossal clusters of thunderclouds coalescing across the sky.

[Didn't Qianxing'er just finish his tribulation?]

[No, wait—someone is undergoing tribulation right here in Heavenly Spirit Sect!]

[It shouldn't be the Untiring Immortal Venerable, right? He 'underwent tribulation' just recently!]

[Then who is it?!]

Villa Master Qingyang's mind spun in utter confusion.

Seeing the spectacle unfold, the other cultivators were immediately distracted, murmuring in astonishment as they looked up at the gathering thunderclouds.

Amidst the swirling commotion, Heavenly Spirit Sect's fourth disciple Guan Ye and seventh disciple Mo Bei flew upon their swords toward the vicinity of the Palace of Silence.

Villa Master Qingyang wasn't sure if it was just his imagination, but Guan Ye and Mo Bei appeared thoroughly ash-faced, as though they had just suffered some unspeakable catastrophe.

Yet Guan Ye's announcement suggested quite the opposite.

—"Everyone, please remain calm. This thundercloud is due to my Eldest Senior Brother, Yuwen Xuan, undergoing his breakthrough tribulation,"-Guan Ye announced with a grim, blackened face.-"Because he is breaking through to the Void Refining Stage, it may appear slightly dramatic."

Yet his declaration only plunged the crowd into deeper bewilderment—especially Villa Master Qingyang.

Villa Master Qingyang racked his brain, realizing he had almost zero impression of Heavenly Spirit Sect's eldest disciple. The man seemed to be an ordinary youth with negligible presence. But why was he triggering a tribulation at this exact moment?! Wasn't the timing far too coincidental? Or did the Heavenly Spirit Sect simply make a habit of undergoing tribulations in batches?!

Naturally, that wasn't the case at all.

Guan Ye, face dark as charcoal, stared at the clamoring cultivators and recalled the grand speech Yuwen Xuan had delivered when they had rushed to his cave dwelling just moments prior:

—"To make people overlook a storm, simply whip up an even greater storm! Since neither of you knows what happened to Number Two, I'll engineer a massive event that you *do* understand. That way, you'll have an easy explanation for everyone!"

—"...Wait, Eldest Senior Brother, we wanted you to go out and handle the guests, not start shit!"

Such was the baffling origin of Yuwen Xuan's sudden tribulation.

What was more, when this fellow wished to trigger a tribulation, it seemed whatever he willed came to pass. The moment Yuwen Xuan uttered those words, the sky-wide storm clouds had coalesced obediently like an on-call summon beast, drumming up a spectacle even more absurdly theatrical than Qu Qianxing's earlier display.

Witnessing this, Guan Ye couldn't even summon the energy to feel jealous.

He only felt that their Eldest Senior Brother was once again [begging to be hung up](# "Literal CN: 欠挂 (qiànguà) — deserving to be hung up from the ancient peach tree at the mountain gate by Master Zhou Hui for discipline")!
