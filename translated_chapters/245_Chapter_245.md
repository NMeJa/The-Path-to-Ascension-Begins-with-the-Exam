# Chapter 245: Dead End

[← Previous Chapter](244_Chapter_244.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](246_Chapter_246.md)

Above the Nine Heavens, within the so-called Immortal Realm, a tiny cluster of golden light was fleeing in panic.

It was Tian Yan, and it was also Tian Shou. It was both, or perhaps, it was neither.

In the end, Qu Qianxing had miscalculated.

No, calling it a miscalculation wasn't entirely accurate. Qu Qianxing had set out from the very beginning to obliterate Tian Yan, and Tian Yan had similarly sought to kill Qu Qianxing. In the duel between those two, that unlucky celestial general, Tian Shou, had been nothing more than a pawn in their game from start to finish.

Yet precisely because neither of them had paid any mind to Tian Shou, this fellow had somehow managed, through sheer absurd resilience, to preserve a sliver of remnant soul. It was merely that, bound tightly by the destiny threads, it was difficult to tell whether this remnant soul was Tian Yan or Tian Shou.

Having fallen to such a state, the remnant soul had no inclination to figure out who it actually was.

Surviving was the only thing that mattered!

Not daring to linger at the altar where spatial fissures remained, the remnant soul fled outward as fast as it could.

Yet regarding the direction of their flight, a subtle contradiction was tangled within the remnant soul:

[—"Where is a vessel? We must find a new vessel immediately!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"What's the rush! Revenge comes first!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"Revenge? Now?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Isn't that spatial fissure still right there? Those people below will definitely come up to confirm 'my' death..."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"Then isn't that all the more reason to run?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Run from what? This is the Heavenly Court, and the Feast of Immortals is underway! As long as we let them know what happened here, never mind a few insects from below—crushing that entire world into dust would be child's play!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"But..."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"There are no 'buts'! Finding a vessel after we've taken our revenge will be far safer."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

"Thinking" thus, the remnant soul flew toward the banquet grounds at its absolute fastest speed.

Having fallen to such a wretched state anyway, it couldn't afford to worry about anything else for the moment. Surviving was paramount.

Yet before the remnant soul could reach the banquet grounds, a wave of palpitations seized it.

No, it no longer had a heart, so there was nothing to "palpitate."

Even so, the remnant soul's sensation was as though it had been struck by Qu Qianxing's lightning all over again. Every strand of soul flared and puffed out, causing its soul body to swell by an entire ring. If it flared any further, even this sliver of remnant soul wouldn't hold together.

Yet ignoring its own condition, the remnant soul gazed toward the banquet grounds in shock and doubt:

—"What is happening? Such a monstrous, ferocious aura,"-the remnant soul muttered, bewildered.

What was worse, this malevolent aura could eat immortals!

It was still some distance from the venue, yet it felt as though a layer of its meager celestial power had been licked away! At the remnant soul's current size, if it flew any further, it would likely be devoured into nothingness before it even reached the banquet.

What had happened at the feast?

What had happened to the other immortals?!

Doubt and terror gripped the remnant soul, and within its nonexistent brain, internal bickering erupted once more:

[—"Something is wrong! Run!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"We cannot run! No matter how low your intelligence is, you can't be completely devoid of brains! If the celestial feast is ruined, the Heavenly Court won't escape either. Even if we find a vessel by then, returning will just be sending ourselves to our deaths!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"But going forward is death as well!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Not fleeing immediately does not mean having to fly straight in! Can 'I' not take a look from afar, to at least learn roughly what happened at the banquet?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

The remnant soul "thought" thus, and acted accordingly.

It pulled tight its flared soul body, shrinking its "form" into a single speck as much as possible. Detouring around the direct path to the banquet, it swayed in the opposite direction toward the higher mountain peaks, attempting to gaze down from the skies to see what had transpired in the courtyard where the feast was being held.

Yet once one noticed a problem and went looking for answers, problems appeared everywhere one looked.

Just now, the remnant soul had been consumed entirely with fleeing for its life, failing to notice that the entirety of Xuanpu had descended into utter chaos.

The chaos here stemmed primarily from the spirit beasts.

As the garden of the Celestial Emperor, Xuanpu was the place where celestial qi was most abundant even within the Immortal Realm. Consequently, it gathered all manner of spirit beasts and celestial beasts, even including divine beasts whose traces could no longer be found in the outside world. Yet because their cultivation had reached the level of attaining the Dao and achieving immortality, and under the intimidating majesty of this place's master, they naturally would not brawl and slaughter like monsters of the lower realms. Not only did the various species live in harmony, but even those naturally ferocious by nature would put on a docile, peaceful demeanor, as though their sharp fangs and claws were merely decorative.

At this moment, however, these seemingly tamed celestial and spirit beasts had fallen into a state of sheer madness.

As if encountering a calamity unseen in tens of millions of years, they sought only to flee at all costs from this celestial garden that still drifted with immortal mist. To that end, they trampled and crashed into one another, knocking the divine beasts guarding the garden into dizzying circles until they couldn't extricate themselves. The celestial beasts lagging behind tore into and slaughtered one another—not merely because others blocked their escape path, but seemingly in an attempt to delay their own demise through the deaths of their companions.

Yet such fighting only drew that bizarre malevolent aura all the faster.

One could see defeated celestial beasts collapsing to the ground with pitiful wails. Before they had even perished, the fur and flesh on their bodies vanished abruptly, as though chewed and swallowed by an invisible maw. In just a few bites, nothing remained but a bare skeleton, without so much as a single drop of marrow left behind.

Thus, the entire garden seemed to have turned into an invisible ferocious beast's dining hall. Spirit beasts, celestial beasts, and even divine beasts appeared to have become dishes on a plate, where being devoured clean in the very next moment was entirely within the realm of possibility.

Yet in such an eerie situation, not a single immortal official seemed to have noticed.

Leaving aside the immortal officials attending the ceremony—who might be at the banquet itself, as not one remained outside—the celestial servants and maidservants hurried to and fro. Some carried heaps of food overflowing from platters as they rushed toward the banquet grounds, while others sprinted back to the kitchens and prep stations clutching piles of empty tableware.

They were so frantic that even the celestial soldiers and heavenly generals guarding the garden dared not slacken, joining the ranks of the food-delivery brigade.

When the remnant soul attempted to question them, it received only vague, panicked replies:

[—"Hurry, hurry! The dining tables are about to be empty again!"](# "Speaker: Celestial Servant")

[—"We must serve the dishes immediately! Otherwise the 'Host' of the banquet will fly into a rage again!"](# "Speaker: Celestial Servant")

[—"Without dishes, we will become the dishes!"](# "Speaker: Celestial Servant")

[—"Sob, sob, sob... I don't want to be eaten!"](# "Speaker: Celestial Maid")

The servants were terrified, yet dared not stop their footsteps. They couldn't see the anomalies, nor did they care about them. They merely gathered all the food they could possibly lay their hands on and ferried it relentlessly to the banquet. Fortunately, this was the largest garden in the Immortal Realm. Though the Celestial Emperor rarely visited, he hosted the gathered immortals here every few hundred years. Consequently, specialized storehouses held stockpiled ingredients sufficient to sustain several minor worlds for centuries. For the moment, there was no fear of running out, and thus no one knew what would happen once the food was gone.

The outcome would likely not be pleasant.

The remnant soul pondered uneasily, taking ever greater care to steer clear of that all-devouring malevolent aura:

[—"Judging by their reactions, some evil entity has altered the 'rules' of Xuanpu."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"An evil entity? What evil entity could run amok in the Celestial Emperor's garden?! Even 'I' cannot fully command the rules here."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

The "I" referred to here was, of course, Tian Yan.

As the author of the Heavenly Dao across numerous minor worlds, Tian Yan was the existence within the Heavenly Court most familiar with "rules." Yet he was not the Celestial Emperor himself; in the Heavenly Court, his rules could by no means override the Celestial Emperor.

In truth, even in the minor worlds, Tian Yan could not act entirely as he pleased. After all, he represented "righteousness," "heaven," and "principle." He could not permit the Heavenly Dao to become overly unjust. Even when plundering resources, he could only drain the pond to catch the fish in a sustainable manner, maintaining at least superficial fairness. Tian Yan had always felt that this was precisely why his scripts constantly ran into various complications.

Yet entities such as demonic beasts and evil spirits had no such constraints.

They had no need for the guise of righteous principle. They cordoned off domains as their private hunting grounds, and established rules primarily to better filter their prey—rules that were blatantly nefarious at a glance.

Naturally, never mind entering the Celestial Emperor's garden, even ascending to the Heavenly Realm was an act of gross irreverence toward the Celestial Emperor for such an evil entity.

[—"...Yet there are exceptions."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

The remnant soul paused, before speaking again:

—"Ordinary monsters and demons certainly cannot ascend, but those primordial beasts born of heaven and earth before the Great Desolation are another matter entirely,"-it said.-"Don't forget, divine beasts and ferocious beasts were arbitrarily divided back then solely based on whether they aided humankind. In essence, there was no distinction between them. And the Heavenly Realm places no restrictions on divine beasts, nor can it truly constrain ferocious beasts."

[—"In fact, 'I' even raised one."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"'I'? Can what 'I' did even be called raising?! 'I' couldn't restrain it at all! Nor could 'I' kill it! 'I' was nearly eaten by it! In the end, out of sheer desperation, 'I' had to use the resources of Langhuan as bait to entice it into tearing itself apart, splitting off a smaller version to devour itself—and that plan seems to have failed..."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Wasn't that because 'I' was too useless?! Couldn't even deal with a handful of demons, forcing 'me' to deploy a ferocious beast to clear out that trash!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"A handful of demons?! The demons multiplying so fast was entirely your fault! If you hadn't plundered so ruthlessly, depleting the spiritual energy of so many minor worlds until people couldn't cultivate immortal arts, causing demonic horrors to run rampant and nourishing the demon race until our celestial soldiers were driven back in retreat!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Don't speak as if you didn't enjoy the spoils harvested from those realms!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

The remnant soul began to bicker with itself.

Its internal contradictions could no longer be suppressed, causing this temporarily cobbled-together soul to nearly split apart at the seams.

Regrettably, the instinct for survival once again suppressed the conflict between the merged souls. It—or they—ultimately did not fracture, bringing the squabble to a temporary halt.

Only after a good while did the remnant soul resume its "self-soliloquy":

[—"Going by that, the one making trouble right now is that ferocious beast you kept?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

—"It wasn't kept by me, at best it was a partnership! That vile beast doesn't understand human speech at all and constantly breaks its promises,"-the part of the remnant soul belonging to Tian Yan vehemently denied, unwilling to shoulder the blame, though unable to shake it off entirely.-"However, in a place like Xuanpu, incapable of cunning, stubbornly clinging to its innate nature—a nature that is nothing but 'eating'—there is only one..."

[—"Taotie..."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul")

Having reached this point, the remnant soul once again looked instinctively toward the banquet grounds.

It had flown to a certain altitude by now. Although this distance was not yet sufficient to look down into the interior of the banquet venue, across this span, there were no longer any trees, flowers, or palace walls to obstruct its line of sight.

And the "Host" stirring up trouble at the banquet seemed to have no intention of concealing its form, exposing itself brazenly amidst the swirling celestial mist.

It was a ferocious beast like a small mountain.

It bore menacing ram horns, its body resembling a titanic ram, while its head was human—that of an exceedingly handsome young man. Yet it inexplicably gave off the impression of a savage predator. With a face full of drunkenness, clutching a massive cauldron brimming with fine wine, it looked like an idiot monster who had drunk too much, couldn't help revealing its true form, and was throwing a tantrum at an immortal feast.

Regrettably, while this ferocious beast might indeed be an idiot, it was by no means on the level of an ordinary monster.

With just that single glance, half of the remnant soul's already meager soul body vanished in an instant.

What was even more terrifying was that, accompanying the vanishing of the remnant soul's body, the ferocious beast smacked its lips as though sensing something. It then rolled its eyes, which had lost focus from heavy drinking, and looked straight toward the remnant soul.

"..."

It found nothing.

A look of perplexity surfaced on the ferocious beast's handsome young face:

—"Strange. Did I just eat something weird?"-it muttered.

As it muttered to itself, the malevolent aura already saturating the garden expanded by another ring.

Immediately, numerous weaker spirit beasts cried out in sorrow and burst into clouds of blood mist. Yet not a single drop of blood touched the ground. In the instant they burst, it was as if a ravenous tongue had swept everything clean without leaving a single drop behind. Only a violently corrosive liquid resembling saliva fell out of nowhere, forcefully licking away a layer of every living being and patch of soil it touched.

The immortals and divine beasts in the garden were relatively powerful and did not get devoured outright, but the lucidity in their eyes grew even fainter. Their movements in cooking, preparing, and serving food became ever more zealous. If not for an invisible restriction maintaining a bare minimum baseline, they would likely be no different from those mindless spirit beasts, offering themselves up directly onto the chopping block.

Yet that malevolent aura remained unsatisfied.

Like a colossal maw, it held all of Xuanpu in its mouth, licking it thoroughly. In particular, wherever the remnant soul had passed, not only the air but even the fabric of space was almost digested away. The malevolent aura seeped through the fragile spatial barriers, corroding toward other dimensions.

On the other side of space, the remnant soul let out a terrified cry:

[—"Even space itself is about to be eaten! Is Taotie such a horrifying ferocious beast?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Presumably so. Records originally stated that it could swallow an entire world... Thinking back now, a world itself might not refer solely to the physical matter existing within it, but could even encompass space itself."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"! You dared to raise such a thing?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

The remnant soul began squabbling again, and the portion considering itself Tian Yan hastily defended itself:

—"I told you, that hardly counts as raising it!"-the Tian Yan portion retorted defensively.-"Besides, how many eons have passed since the era of primordial desolate beasts? That was during the Chaos Era before even the Divine Court and the Demon Court! Even those desolate beasts that survived are far from what they once were, not to mention that when it was discovered back then, it was merely a severely injured cub. It had nothing left except a shred of devouring instinct, unable to even access its ancestral memories. I never taught it anything either, only letting it eat trash [thoroughly corrupted by demonic qi]. Under normal circumstances, it should have been eroded by demonic qi, growing increasingly deranged until it devoured everything it could reach, before ultimately reverting back into primordial chaos!"

—"In fact, ten thousand years ago it was on the verge of ruin. But its size was too gargantuan. If it truly lost its sanity completely, the Heavenly Court might have had half of itself chewed off. That was why I tricked it into Langhuan—firstly, to utilize it to weaken Langhuan's spiritual barrier, and secondly, to preserve its sanity so I could drive it toward a safer location later on. However..."

The remnant soul paused here.

There was no need to speak further. Langhuan's sheer unpredictability was already plain to see: even Qu Qianxing, a discarded vessel, had surpassed the original body. Thus, whatever a naturally uncontrollable Taotie transformed into would come as no surprise.

If someone told the remnant soul right now that this Taotie had eaten the library and consequently become the wisest entity between heaven and earth, it would believe it.

[—"What do we do now? Go to the Heavenly Court?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"No! Taotie is running amok here, and who knows what has become of the Celestial Emperor and the other immortal officials... Even if they are unharmed, with the garden thrown into such chaos, rather than handling the crisis, they are far more likely to assign blame. 'I' would not escape heavenly punishment either. Even if 'I' am to return to the Heavenly Court, 'I' must restore at least seventy to eighty percent of our power, so that the Celestial Emperor cannot bear to discard 'me.'"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"Can 'I' still recover now?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"How much power do you have left? Your reaction was fast enough when you saw Taotie just now, tearing open space in an instant. Is it enough to fly to another world?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

—"...That is the authority of a military general. After all, 'I' have always been on the most perilous battlefields, so 'I' have a trick or two for escaping,"-the portion of the remnant soul considering itself Tian Shou replied slowly. It didn't really wish to speak, but the remnant soul was currently one entity; forcibly tearing them apart meant neither could survive, so it ultimately answered,-"However, not much of my strength remains. At most, I can teleport through one or two more worlds... Do you have a way to restore our power?"

—"The Immortal-Demon Realm..."-the portion of the remnant soul considering itself Tian Yan replied, equally reluctant yet resigned.-"'I' prepared a top-grade vessel there. Rebirth can be achieved immediately."

[—"Pfft, a vessel? You haven't forgotten how 'I' was reduced to this state, have you?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

—"It is different from that disobedient piece of discarded trash. This time, the vessel is an unfeeling infant that has not yet developed a sense of self. Furthermore, 'He' is far more perfect than that one, birthed from a maternal host meticulously chosen and cultivated using natural treasures harvested from Langhuan. Although it is a bit earlier than anticipated, if rebirth succeeds smoothly, it will be stronger than the original 'us' combined,"-the portion belonging to Tian Yan explained with brimming confidence.

The Tian Shou portion, however, had already developed psychological trauma over that particular location:

[—"Langhuan again...?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

The remnant soul had an exceedingly terrible premonition.

Yet before the remnant soul could deliberate carefully, it suddenly felt as though it had been bitten. A massive chunk of its soul body went missing, and despite having no physical form, a pain akin to being mauled by a savage beast ripped through it.

Only then did it notice that the spatial barrier had already cracked open. That malevolent aura was relentlessly pursuing it.

[—"Flee!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"To the Immortal-Demon Realm!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

Scrambling in blind panic, it transformed its remaining form into a streak of lightning, swiftly vanishing into the spatial rift.

After the remnant soul fled, the malevolent aura did not continue the chase, merely swaying lazily a few times within the spatial rift like a sluggish tiger's tail.

Within the garden, the ferocious beast faced its new guests:

—"Yo, Old Fifth, Little Sixth. What brought you two here?"-it inquired with a beaming smile.

Yet its gaze swept past these two uninvited guests, fixing solely upon the direction from which they had arrived—

Namely, the spatial rift at the altar. Furthermore, it stealthily reined in a portion of its malevolent aura.

At least, it reined in the aura around the young girl it addressed as Little Sixth. But as for the white dragon addressed as "Old Fifth," it paid no heed to the aura surrounding him. Not only did it ignore it, but the concentration of malevolence around the dragon actually intensified, looking as though it wanted not only to devour his draconic qi, but swallow the white dragon whole as well.

Old Fifth—who was, of course, Lu Shulong—couldn't help protesting loudly:

—"Eldest Senior Brother! If you bite me again, I'm going to tattle to Master when I get back!"

Hearing this, Yuwen Xuan breathed a sigh of relief and grinned mischievously:

—"Tattle when you get back? That means Master isn't returning yet? Splendid! Old Fifth, come let me take a few bites. You've grown so massive, it won't be easy to go back and see Master looking like this anyway, right?"

At present, Lu Shulong was indeed quite large... or rather, quite long.

After all, this was the Heavenly Realm, where spiritual energy was extraordinarily abundant. Lu Shulong had no need to suppress his physical vessel. Moreover, even in the Heavenly Court, the status of a True Dragon commanded greater deference. Thus, after carrying Mo Bei through the spatial rift, he had revealed the true form of an adult dragon. Gleaming with a brilliant silver luster, he looked like a river beneath moonlight, coiling across the skies of Xuanpu like the Heavenly River itself.

Yet when it came to size, Lu Shulong and the current Yuwen Xuan were [six of one and half a dozen of the other](# "Literal CN: 半斤对八两 (bànjīn duì bāliǎng) — half a catty versus eight ounces; an even match").

Lu Shulong leveled his accusation:

—"You're the one to talk, Eldest Senior Brother! Just how much have you eaten?! How did you get this huge?! Looking like this, you're the one who can't go back at all!"

—"Don't you go talking nonsense to Master when you get back! I haven't eaten a single thing I shouldn't have!"-Yuwen Xuan immediately denied, pointing toward the banquet venue.

The immortal officials within the feast appeared completely lost in a drunken stupor, trapped within the ferocious beast's domain. Yet true to Yuwen Xuan's words, they were at least still in one piece and had not been eaten.

Things with sapience—humans—were best left uneaten.

This ferocious beast had actually held onto this bottom line. If the remnant soul were to learn of this, it would surely be horrified by such an anomaly.

Yuwen Xuan, on the other hand, was quite proud of himself:

—"The reason my form is so big is that I took back my true form and brought it under control. Master knows about this... right? A-Anyway, he'll definitely be proud of my growth!"

[If you've really grown, stop constantly thinking about eating me!]

[Is this level of intelligence any different from when I first hatched?!]

Lu Shulong roared inwardly.

Yet before Lu Shulong could voice his protest, Mo Bei spoke coldly:

—"I believe Master will be proud of both Senior Brothers' 'growth,'"-she remarked.-"But even if he doesn't say it aloud, he will probably feel a bit melancholic seeing you two grow up so fast, wouldn't he? After all, Master doted so heavily on your cub forms."

Yuwen Xuan and Lu Shulong froze instantly—especially Yuwen Xuan.

Only he knew how much sweet treatment he had enjoyed ever since turning into a little lamb. If Master saw him in this current state, he definitely wouldn't like it; at the very least, he wouldn't be willing to hold and cuddle him anymore.

[You at least need to be a size Master can actually carry!] Mo Bei thought flatly.

At that thought, the wine cauldron in Yuwen Xuan's hands crashed to the ground, and the food at the feast no longer smelled appetizing.

He eyed his junior brother and sister darkly, contemplating whether he should simply silence the witnesses.

Except if his junior brother and sister vanished, what if Master took it hard again...

Unaware of what terrible thoughts were brewing in Eldest Senior Brother's mind, Mo Bei saw that both senior brothers had "calmed down" and ceased fooling around. Feeling that normal conversation was finally possible, she seized the moment to ask:

—"Eldest Senior Brother, since you have been here the whole time, did you see anything emerge from the altar over there?"

—"What's what? How would I know when you phrase it like that?"-Yuwen Xuan asked, rolling his eyes.

—"It's just..."-Mo Bei didn't know how to describe it either. She hadn't fought Tian Yan personally, nor did she quite know what she was tracking down. However, knowing that Second Senior Brother shared a certain connection with that entity, Mo Bei used terms Yuwen Xuan could understand:-"Roughly... a scent very similar to Second Senior Brother?"

—"I've never eaten Old Second, how would I know what he tastes like?"-Yuwen Xuan scoffed.

Even Mo Bei could tell that Yuwen Xuan was unwilling to cooperate.

Yet this Eldest Senior Brother was simply like this: as long as Master wasn't around, he was the sect's greatest menace.

At times like this, only the tried-and-true method would work. Mo Bei sighed:

—"It doesn't matter if you didn't see it, Eldest Senior Brother. At worst, our sect will just be exterminated again. We've been wiped out several times anyway, so everyone should be used to it by now. I just wonder how much longer Master can hold out?"

If she could help it, Mo Bei truly didn't want to bring Master into this, but only this worked on Eldest Senior Brother—and on the other martial brothers and sisters as well.

Hearing those words, Yuwen Xuan's expression darkened in an instant.

—"What did you lot do this time?"-Yuwen Xuan demanded in a chilling voice.-"Why is it that in the brief time I slipped out for a drink, you managed to push things to the brink of sect extermination again?!"

He paused, his gaze growing increasingly hostile:

—"It's like this every single time. You aren't doing this on purpose, are you?"

Indeed, every past instance of sect extermination had occurred after Yuwen Xuan stepped out the door.

—"Who would do such a thing on purpose? Unlike you, Eldest Senior Brother, if the sect is destroyed, we die too! I'm suspecting this might be your conspiracy instead!"-Lu Shulong fired back. Although he knew the exterminations had nothing to do with Yuwen Xuan, he was simply aggrieved.

[This Taotie has caused so much disaster—why is it that no one besides Master can discipline him?!]

[He ought to be strung up on the Southern Heavenly Gate!]

Yet now was not the time to let this wretched Eldest Senior Brother cause further trouble. Grinding his teeth, Lu Shulong explained anyway:

—"Besides, we weren't the ones looking for trouble first. That massive spatial fissure over at the altar certainly wasn't torn open by us! Who knows, maybe your reckless drinking brought this retribution upon us!"-Lu Shulong added, intentionally shoving the blame onto Yuwen Xuan.

Yuwen Xuan couldn't be bothered to argue with Lu Shulong's nonsense. Frowning, he questioned:

—"Then are you useless? If someone comes knocking on your door, hit them back! Didn't Master give you power? He must have given it, right? That power was enough for me to dominate this entire garden! You lot..."

Having said this much, Yuwen Xuan abruptly trailed off.

Indeed, Yuwen Xuan had also received the power bestowed by Zhou Hui, though in a manner distinct from the others.

The power received by Qu Qianxing, Ran Deng, and the participants of the Immortal Arts Assembly had been channeled through the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Yuwen Xuan, however, had no intention of inheriting the sect, and had moreover fled up to the Heavenly Realm. His connection to the mountain array was far from strong, so the power he received through it shouldn't have been excessive. But he had Master [cooking him private meals](# "Literal CN: 开小灶 (kāi xiǎozào) — lighting a small stove; giving someone special privileges, private tuition, or secret perks")!

As Zhou Hui's most "favored" eldest disciple—and a Taotie to boot—Zhou Hui had always given him extra meals in the past, even feeding his eldest disciple with his own flesh, blood, and spiritual energy. Thus, when Yuwen Xuan feasted on the celestial banquet, the karmic cause-and-effect was instantly attributed to Zhou Hui. And when Zhou Hui acquired divine power, Yuwen Xuan received the dividends of the "Chief Eldest Disciple" directly, without needing the Mountain-Protecting Grand Array.

This was one of the genuine reasons why, despite being a ferocious beast, he could single-handedly suppress so many spirit beasts, celestial beasts, and even immortal officials throughout the Celestial Emperor's garden.

Naturally, even Yuwen Xuan understood that what he was doing flew in the face of universal order. If the Heavenly Court were to uncover his origins, it would be a monumental disaster that implicated the entire sect.

Yet he had gained power and pulled off something like this—so what about his junior siblings?

In his view, these junior brothers and sisters were hardly any better than him. Only Master would mistake this pack of little scoundrels for good children.

Yuwen Xuan narrowed his eyes:

—"Wait. Did you lot beat someone up, and now, fearing the Heavenly Court's wrath, you're planning to silence the witnesses?"

—"Heh heh, look at what you're saying, Eldest Senior Brother. What 'silencing witnesses'? You make it sound as though we did something wicked,"-Lu Shulong said with a laugh, casting a glance at the wrecked, chaotic garden.-"Though that thing fled from the altar, so it most likely witnessed everything you're doing right now. You're the one who needs to silence witnesses!"

—"Hmph,"-Yuwen Xuan snorted coldly.

Narrowing his eyes, he picked up the bronze wine cauldron he had dropped earlier and tapped on it idly, as if weighing his options.

Seeing this, Lu Shulong and Mo Bei exchanged glances, both feeling a bad premonition.

Clearly, their Eldest Senior Brother's unwillingness to reveal the whereabouts of the "target to be silenced" was not merely a childish whim, but rooted in his own reasoning.

In truth, while Yuwen Xuan appeared to be a pure [chaos-seeker](# "Literal CN: 乐子人 (lèzǐrén) — someone who does things purely for amusement or drama"), there was always a reason behind what he did—the kind of reason that got him hung from a tree by Master afterward.

Yet precisely because Yuwen Xuan always maintained an attitude of [hung on the tree today, doing it again tomorrow](# "Literal CN: 每次被挂在树上也‘下次还敢’ — even after being hung from the tree, defiantly vowing to do it again next time"), most people in the sect genuinely respected this Eldest Senior Brother. Even Qu Qianxing would acknowledge him at critical junctures.

Because no matter who it was, and no matter how deeply they revered Master, there would always be times when they couldn't or didn't want to carry out Master's instructions. And at such times, Eldest Senior Brother's "reasoning" was genuinely very "reasonable."

Unfortunately, the current situation seemed to fit "such times" perfectly.

Eldest Senior Brother clearly knew the target was an enemy who needed to be silenced, yet refused to disclose their whereabouts—a move reeking of a "reasoning" known only to him.

Listening to this reasoning gave one the distinct impression that they would be dragged into the mud alongside him.

Mo Bei's lips twitched, half-wishing to cut him off.

Regrettably, Yuwen Xuan spoke first:

—"...That soul whose scent resembles Old Second most likely fled toward the Immortal-Demon Realm. In principle, I don't feel like bothering with it, because there is already a creditor waiting for it there."

—"A creditor?"-Lu Shulong asked, puzzled.

—"What other debt does he owe?"-Mo Bei said, deeply displeased.-"Regardless of what else he owes to whom, if he doesn't die by my hand, I won't feel at ease. Not to mention, I have other debts to settle."

—"You don't need to worry about that,"-Yuwen Xuan laughed.-"That thing owes plenty of debts. But its greatest transgression was forcing us through so many cycles of reincarnation, wouldn't you say? Do you think anyone who has offended us could possibly meet a good end?"

Having said this, Yuwen Xuan paused, repeating with deliberate emphasis:

—"The ones it offended are *us*!"

* * *

The remnant soul had no idea it had been deliberately "released."

After several spatial transmissions, it successfully arrived at its destination: the Immortal-Demon Realm.

What met the remnant soul's eyes at this moment was an endless expanse of palaces, resplendent in gold and jade, wreathed in celestial mist, rivaling the Heavenly Palace itself.

[—"Such ostentation grows more and more outrageous."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

The remnant soul gave a mocking sneer, before resuming its "self-soliloquy":

[—"It cannot be helped, this is the Immortal-Demon Realm! Governance is loose here, so any level of pomp is normal."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"Then are we just going to waltz in like this? What I mean is, with such grand pomp, the defenses must be airtight. And we are so weak right now—can you truly walk in nonchalantly?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Not 'you,' nor 'we'—it is 'I'!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

The remnant soul struggled to maintain the unity of its soul body.

Yet the fact that such doubts arose indicated that a portion of the soul body could no longer access memories. Its condition was dire indeed.

Precisely because of this, the remnant soul felt it had to secure a new body as quickly as possible.

Only with a physical vessel could it purge the foolish, unruly portion within its soul.

The remnant soul exercised its patience:

—"Besides, no matter how strict the defenses here are, they are entirely useless against 'me.' For the 'Sovereign' of this realm is the 'Child of Destiny' chosen by me."

[—"Heh, you couldn't even control your discarded vessel or the pet you raised, and now you boast about a 'Child of Destiny'?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

—"I said, not 'you,' but 'I.' Furthermore, those were neither ordinary pets nor standard vessels,"-the Tian Yan portion explained patiently.-"Precisely because he is the 'Protagonist,' there is no need to worry. Rather, what thoughts he harbors do not matter in the slightest! As you know, the so-called Child of Destiny is merely a shell used by celestial officials when descending to the mortal world for amusement. As long as he walks the path of 'Heavenly Destiny' tailored by the Ministry of Celestial Destiny, his fortune is limitless, obtaining whatever he desires. Yet precisely because he acts according to Heavenly Destiny, he is forever bound by the destiny threads—lower than a puppet. He could never betray 'me.'"

[Are you sure about that?]

[There are quite a few foolish immortals who play this boring 'Child of Destiny' game.]

[Just how many immortal officials in the Heavenly Court have been controlled by you like this?!]

A chill ran through the Tian Shou portion of the remnant soul.

To be reduced to such a state was, in plain terms, the masterpiece of this control freak Tian Yan... Ah, it couldn't think any further. Any more thought and the soul would scatter. This sliver of soul could barely maintain its sanity as it was.

Better to obtain a body first. Only with a body could accounts be settled!

In a subtle manner, unity was reached within the remnant soul, and it flew toward the center of the palace.

And just as the remnant soul's Tian Yan portion had guaranteed, all defensive formations of the palace were as good as nonexistent to it. The cultivators within the palace, regardless of cultivation or station, turned a blind eye to it, even treating it with absolute deference.

Without the slightest obstruction, the remnant soul made its way straight to the center of the palace. According to the feng shui layout, this should have been the residence of the palace's mistress.

Yet there was no mistress here.

Or rather, the former mistress of this place had transformed into a humanoid chunk of blood-jade amber. Only her exterior retained a few traces of her original form, resembling an enlarged sculpture of a female immortal. Her interior, however, had become a translucent crystal condensed from flesh and blood. Faintly visible within the heart of the crystal was a youth, body curled up like an infant enveloped in amniotic fluid.

Only the youth's age was no longer suitable for an infant.

He—or she—appeared around fifteen or sixteen years old, just one step shy of adulthood, possessing an exquisite face that yielded nothing to Qu Qianxing. Fortunately, this face bore no resemblance to either Qu Qianxing or Tian Yan; rather, it was roughly seventy percent similar to Mo Bei and Mo Xi, imbued with a sacred, divine grace they lacked that compelled onlookers to prostrate in worship.

It was precisely for this that the remnant soul had come.

Catching sight of the blood-jade amber from afar, its eyes lit up (if it had eyes). Accelerating its flight, it pressed impatiently before the amber, sizing up the youth within with great care.

[—"Truly a magnificent vessel!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Naturally. I spent tens of thousands of years in Langhuan, using the yin souls of ancient True Immortals to repeatedly temper and select a suitable maternal body, then gathered an entire dozen bloodlines of the Celestial Emperor to serve as the paternal body..."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"A dozen?"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Time in Langhuan is far too chaotic. Entering the 'wrong time' means certain death."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

[—"Hmph, 'I' certainly took great pains."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

The remnant soul gave a cold snort.

The portion leaning toward Tian Shou referred to itself as "I" once more. It was plain to see that it was immensely satisfied with this vessel, thereby adopting the assumption that it, too, was the creator of this body.

Now that the consciousness of the remnant soul had achieved unity once more, all that remained was possessing the vessel.

Had it been the Tian Yan of the past transferring into his meticulously nurtured new body, he would certainly have selected a prime geomantic sanctuary where celestial qi gathered most densely, erected the safest and most formidable formations, commanded the most reliable and capable celestial attendants to stand guard, and bathed, meditated, and performed rituals for nine hundred and ninety-nine days before transferring on the most auspicious day.

Yet it currently had no such luxury. It felt deep regret:

—"What a pity, it is still too rushed. This shell still requires some years to fully mature. Accompanied by a proper ritual, transferring into it would have been far more perfect."

—"Nothing in this world is flawless, this is good enough,"-the remnant soul retorted carelessly to itself. Circling the blood-jade amber several times with frantic impatience, it asked grumpily,-"How do we melt this amber? Wouldn't getting the body sooner be better? Does 'I' enjoy being in such a weak state so much?"

—"? Reason dictates that it should be completely 'eaten' before the vessel finishes maturing. But for our use, it isn't so troublesome. Enter directly, and it will melt... !?"

The remnant soul attempted to touch the amber. The result was a sharp *hiss* as a portion of its soul body vanished, sending its internal mind screaming once more:

[—"Fuck! If we could just go straight in, would I have needed to ask you?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

[—"Wait, this sensation of the soul vanishing..."](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Yan)")

Why did it feel as though it had just experienced this exact sensation moments ago?

—"My, what rare guests."

While the soul was screaming in disarray, a laughing masculine voice came from behind:

—"For the Lord of Celestial Destiny and the Heavenly General to grace my humble abode together, this lowly immortal was remiss in welcoming you. But My Lords really ought to have given advance notice, rather than sneaking in like thieves. If an accident were to occur, it would be quite difficult for this lowly immortal to explain things to the Heavenly Court, wouldn't it?"

—"Tunhuo?"-the remnant soul asked suspiciously, turning around (though it possessed no head) to look.

Standing at the entrance of the palace vestibule at some unknown point was a young man.

Backlit by the light, his face was obscured, but the black-and-red Twelve-Ornament Imperial Robes adorning his figure were sufficient to proclaim his identity:

The Sovereign of the Immortal-Demon Realm, the former Child of Destiny who had once entered Langhuan—

Tunhuo.

Yet whether he was the sovereign here or a Child of Destiny, the Immortal-Demon Realm was theoretically subordinate to the Heavenly Court. Even if the Sovereign of the Immortal-Demon Realm held a station higher than ordinary True Immortals, upon encountering high-ranking celestial officials like Tian Yan and Tian Shou, he should still treat them with utmost decorum.

Yet Tunhuo's words carried not the slightest hint of deference.

—"Accident? What do you mean?"-the remnant soul demanded. The portion belonging to Tian Yan attempted to channel its ability to manipulate destiny threads—an ability that, despite having been largely stripped away by Qu Qianxing, should still have been effective against a "Child of Destiny," a puppet thoroughly hijacked by Heavenly Destiny. Yet those destiny threads felt as though they had plunged into a bottomless abyss, yielding no reaction no matter how hard it pulled.

Instead, the tactile feedback of the destiny threads transmitted an abnormal aura:

—"Wait. You... you are not Tunhuo?"

—"How am I not? Humans have a phrase, [inferring the meaning from the name](# "Literal CN: 顾名思义 (gùmíng-sīyì) — taking words at face value / understanding the meaning from the title"), don't they, Lord of Celestial Destiny?"-Tunhuo asked.-"When you chose a Child of Destiny with a name like 'Tunhuo' [Swallowing Calamity](# "Literal CN: 吞祸 (Tūnhuò) — Devouring Disaster / Swallowing Calamity"), you should have known that rather than Heavenly Destiny, he was far more susceptible to being dominated by my divine authority. Ah, no—you knew from the very beginning. Only, in your arrogance, rather than him being dominated by me, you wanted him to follow your 'script' to seize my strength and devour me instead, didn't you?"

"Tunhuo" chuckled thus, taking two steps toward the remnant soul.

Though still a considerable distance from the remnant soul, accompanying "his" movement, a blood-red luminescence shone upon "his" face, unveiling "his" features.

It was a familiar countenance.

The very same handsome young face the remnant soul had seen moments ago upon that colossal ferocious beast in Xuanpu.

At this moment, the remnant soul also realized that with the other party's appearance, the air within the palace chamber had become identical to that of Xuanpu—suffused with ravenous malevolence and overwhelming aggression.

The remnant soul shrieked, attempting once more to unleash its escape arts:

[—"Didn't you say there wouldn't be a problem—aaaaagh?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

This time, the portion belonging to Tian Shou failed. The moment it began channeling the spell, the adjacent blood-jade amber seemed to be triggered, erupting into a crimson luminescence—the origin of the red glow reflected on the face of "Tunhuo," or rather, Taotie. It thoroughly enveloped that portion of the remnant soul, locking its existence in place.

Worse still, the remnant soul felt the excruciating agony of being torn apart.

[—"Tian Yan, fuck your—?!"](# "Speaker: Remnant Soul (Tian Shou)")

The moment it was enveloped in crimson light, the portion belonging to Tian Yan had severed the soul without the slightest hesitation, transforming into several threads of soul and darting in the direction opposite to Taotie!

Yet this portion of the remnant soul did not escape either. Black demonic qi suddenly erupted, sealing off the path on the other side.

And within that curtain of demonic qi, an exquisite face emerged—a face that filled the remnant soul with dread and an overwhelming urge to retreat: the face of Qu Qianxing.

Only, compared to that "Demon Sovereign" standing amidst golden lightning, who radiated an awe-inspiring, righteous aura even when shrouded in demonic qi, the Qu Qianxing here was like a withered black peony petal drifting upon a pool of blood, possessing a tragic, desolate, bone-chilling beauty.

This Qu Qianxing fixed his gaze upon the remnant soul, his lips slowly curling into a chillingly beautiful, hair-raising smile:

—"You have arrived... far earlier than planned..."

Wh-What did that mean?!

The remnant soul wished to ask.

Yet it could no longer produce any sound. Like an insect trapped within blood-jade amber, its soul threads twitching in vain, it froze entirely within the barrier woven of ferocious beast malice and eerie demonic qi.
