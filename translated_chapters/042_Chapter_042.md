# Chapter 42: Internal Strife (第42章 内斗)

For Guan Ye, of course, the cafeteria issue was merely a minor problem.

In the cultivation world, the cafeterias of most sects were notoriously unpalatable.

Although Zhou Hui viewed the sect as a school and the disciples as his students—believing that even the children in the beginner class were the future of the cultivation world who needed proper instruction and nurturing—other cultivators held no such sentiment.

Other cultivators regarded a sect as a permanent job.

The kind where, once you joined, switching careers was practically impossible.

Even the twelve- or thirteen-year-old children picked from mortal towns during the sect's grand recruitment drives were viewed as junior wage slaves.

At the end of the day, this world had no concept of child labor laws. In mortal eyes, twelve or thirteen was already considered a young adult: children of commoner households were expected to help with chores and raise younger siblings, while wealthy households were already [arranging marriages](# "Literal CN: 议亲 (yìqīn) — negotiating marriage alliances"). Under such circumstances, wasn't finding a job at that age completely normal?

Joining a cultivation sect to pursue immortality was merely a job that came with pre-employment vocational training attached.

Anyone below the Middle Stage of Foundation Establishment was categorized as an [unpaid apprentice](# "Literal CN: 学徒工 (xuétú gōng) — low-level indentured apprentice laborers who work for room and board without formal employee status").

The sect cafeteria was simply the feeding ground for these apprentices.

Furthermore, given how brutally arduous cultivation was, at least half of these apprentices would never obtain [permanent employee status](# "Literal CN: 转正 (zhuǎnzhèng) — transitioning from probation/apprentice to a permanent, full-benefit post"), remaining apprentices for the rest of their lives.

Naturally, the cafeteria would never provide refined delicacies for such apprentices. Terrible food was industry standard; Heavenly Spirit Sect's cafeteria blended right in, completely unremarkable.

Ah, the sole exception was that their head chef happened to be an old yellow weasel.

But so long as that detail wasn't exposed to the light of day, it was hardly a big deal. In Guan Ye's eyes, at least Eldest Senior Brother hadn't installed a skunk in the kitchen, so it didn't count as entirely outrageous.

Guan Ye had far more pressing matters to attend to.

After wrapping up his instructions to the new disciples regarding the research of small household appliances, he walked into the inner Artifact Refinement Hall and nodded to the veteran employees (X):

—"Let's have a brief meeting."

—"Another major project?"-Qin Xian asked routinely.

Two meetings ago when Guan Ye said those words, the Immortal Venerable had tasked him with constructing the Library Pavilion, granting the Artifact Refinement Hall authorization to tap into the sect's mineral veins;

The last time Guan Ye said those words, the Immortal Venerable had tasked him with outfitting the entire sect with spirit spring plumbing, granting the Artifact Refinement Hall authorization to tap into the sect's spirit springs;

So, what was it this time?

Although Qin Xian felt he had been run ragged lately, the exhaustion was profoundly worthwhile.

Truth be told, not only did Qin Xian feel his artifact refinement craftsmanship had advanced by leaps and bounds, his cultivation was even faintly showing signs of a breakthrough. The progress he made over these past few months surpassed everything he had achieved across years of [slacking off](# "Literal CN: 摸鱼 (mōyú) — catching fish / slacking off on company time").

Reflecting on this, Qin Xian couldn't help but look forward to the new assignment.

Guan Ye did not keep him in suspense, nodding:

—"Master wishes to construct a Spiritual Energy Dam near Mount Tianling."

[—"A Spiritual Energy Dam?"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciples")-the crowd echoed in unison.

—"Yes."

Guan Ye strode into the center of the Artifact Refinement Hall's conference chamber. Disciples immediately cleared away the assorted blueprints and scratch calculations, revealing the formation simulation platform underneath.

Unlike the formation simulation platform in the Library Pavilion, however, this platform was more than five times larger, allowing all the Artifact Refinement Hall disciples to crowd around it with plenty of room to spare. Furthermore, what it displayed was not Immortal Chess, but a three-dimensional topographical map of the Mount Tianling mountain range.

At present, the map was covered in a network of pipes and water reservoirs—the plumbing schematics for Mount Tianling's immortal caves.

—"Has this been saved?"-Guan Ye asked, examining the blueprint.

—"Saved multiple times,"-Qin Xian answered.—"Though this proposal still feels somewhat flawed."

—"Then we'll discuss it next time,"-Guan Ye said.—"Following Master's new plan, we might very well have to redesign everything from scratch anyway."

A collective sharp intake of breath hissed through the room.

The disciples of the Artifact Refinement Hall winced, yet none voiced any complaint.

A fickle, erratic [Party A](# "Literal CN: 甲方 (jiǎfāng) — corporate client who constantly changes project requirements") was infuriating, but the Immortal Venerable was different. The Immortal Venerable undoubtedly had his profound reasons.

Guan Ye retrieved a jade slip from his storage bag and slotted it into the groove at the corner of the formation table. Instantly, a new map unfurled across its surface.

It was a complete three-dimensional map of the Central Domain.

The disciples present gasped in awe once more, for they understood the true worth of this map:

Given the Central Domain's ancient history, treacherous terrain, and harsh climate, no other sect—not even the Heavenly Dao Alliance—could possibly obtain such an exhaustive map.

In the entire cultivation world, Zhou Hui was likely the only person who possessed one.

—Because he had measured it all by himself, inch by inch.

Consequently, the entire landscape of the Central Domain could be seen with pristine clarity on this map: the towering Heaven-Cleaving Chasm mountain ranges encircling the borders, the black-hole-like Abyss Sea churning in the center, the sprawling ruins of ancient battlefields, and the shattered remnants of the Ascending Heaven Stairway suspended in the northeastern sky.

Opportunities to view the entirety of the Central Domain so intuitively were vanishingly rare. The disciples widened their eyes, striving to examine what those perilous zones—places that had only ever existed in myths and legends—actually looked like. Unfortunately, Guan Ye gave them little time to gawk; he swiftly enlarged the projection, cropping the view until only the northeastern corner remained: the region where Mount Tianling was situated.

This area was intimately familiar to all of them.

A small portion of Mount Tianling bordered the Abyss Sea, and ancient battlefield ruins dotted the peaks. Yet the spiritual energy storms laden with ferocious, concentrated resentment could not ravage this land as they did elsewhere, nor were there recurring demonic waves or beast tides like in other parts of the Central Domain. This was because the convoluted terrain of Mount Tianling and the soaring Heaven-Cleaving Chasm formed a massive protective angle, while the spirit springs, spirit veins, and mineral lodes below generated a natural defensive shield that intercepted the storms. This had nurtured a lush valley brimming with pure spiritual energy: the domain of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Such a paradise had not fallen into Zhou Hui's lap by chance, of course.

In the furthest corner, one could see that the Heaven-Cleaving Chasm, which encircled the Central Domain like an impenetrable palisade, was missing a piece. And blowing through that breach was not mere spiritual energy storms, but something far more lethal—celestial demons.

Legend held that this breach was the very gateway through which foreign celestial demons had invaded this world. Even after the ancient Demon-God War concluded, several demon progenies remained entrenched there, awaiting the next invasion by their kin.

Until Zhou Hui arrived.

Zhou Hui had taken a fancy to this spatial rift, judging it the most suitable coordinates for his ascension. He briskly [stir-fried](# "Literal CN: 爆炒 (bàochǎo) — high-heat wok stir-fry; Zhou Hui casually fried/obliterated the celestial demons") several foreign celestial demons, constructed his immortal cave and Ascension Platform on the site, and repurposed the rift as an observation deck to conduct research on spatial mechanics and the cosmic void beyond this mortal world.

It wasn't that no further demons had been drawn toward the rift; rather, after Zhou Hui captured several of them and ruthlessly dissected and dismantled them in the name of scientific inquiry, outside the fissure even the demonic miasma commonly drifting through the void could scarcely be detected anymore.

*Ahem.* That was straying too far afield. Back to the Artifact Refinement Hall's engineering agenda.

—"...Mount Tianling shields us from the spiritual energy storms blowing from the Abyss Sea, but as you all undoubtedly know, storm intensity attenuates along a gradient,"-Guan Ye explained, pointing at the map.—"Outside our inner Tianling Valley, the surrounding perimeter remains a zone of turbulent spiritual fluctuation. Constructing cave dwellings along the outer perimeter is not only hazardous, but actively detrimental to cultivation."

—"To address this, Master provided a 'very simple' proposal: construct a Spiritual Energy Dam."

—"Just as mortals build dams to store water and prevent floods, we can construct a spiritual energy dam to accumulate qi when it is overly abundant and release qi when it is scarce. This will regulate the spiritual energy equilibrium across the entire mountain range, establishing a tranquil buffer zone far calmer than the surrounding lands."

—"Of course, executing this is nowhere near as simple as it sounds. To withstand the spiritual storms, the facilities we must construct are as follows—"

Guan Ye waved his hand, projecting several immortal engineering devices onto the map, and proceeded to explain each one in exhaustive detail.

Finally, he asked:

—"Do you have any questions?"

—"No, I'd like to digest this first,"-Qin Xian said, raising his hand, his eyes still glued to the map.—"To think the Immortal Venerable devised such an approach... and that even we are capable of building it."

—"Because the storms in our region are relatively mild, the scale of the project isn't overly massive—or so Master said."-Guan Ye recalled Master's words, shaking his head.—"That is why he provided this initial engineering schematic as an illustrative example. For grander projects in the future, we will have to design and build them entirely on our own."

[—"...The Immortal Venerable thinks far too highly of us,"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")-a disciple chuckled bitterly.

Yet if this was Master's expectation, then one day, it must be accomplished.

Guan Ye resolved inwardly.

Looking at his fellow disciples in the Artifact Refinement Hall, he did not rush to pressure them, merely saying:

—"As for fabricating the facilities, we can discuss and study that at our own pace. However, if this proposal is to be implemented, preliminary fieldwork is required to collect baseline data. We must assemble an exploratory surveying team."

—"Naturally,"-Qin Xian nodded.—"I'll check which nodes on the running water project have already been finalized and pull a few suitable hands from there. A few of the newly arrived disciples look quite promising as well; we can have them fill in the gaps."

—"No. My meaning is that even if we must suspend non-critical nodes on the running water project, we must scrape together the personnel,"-Guan Ye said with deadly seriousness.—"As for the new disciples, anyone who scored eighty or above on the Arithmetic Classic can be given a trial."

—"But they haven't even learned the most fundamental basics... Is it that urgent?"-Qin Xian realized something was amiss.

—"If possible, it would be best to have a foundational data framework compiled by the [Double Tenth](# "Literal CN: 十月朝 (shíyuè cháo) — the 1st day of the 10th lunar month, an ancient autumn festival for honoring ancestors and seasonal sacrifices") Autumn Festival,"-Guan Ye said.

—"Double Tenth?! Aren't there barely any days left until then?!"-Qin Xian exclaimed.

Double Tenth referred to the first day of the tenth lunar month. It had only been on the thirtieth of the eighth month that the newcomers paid respects at the mountain gates. Allocating their department assignments alone had consumed a full ten-day period. At present, everyone was scrambling in a frenzy over educating the fresh recruits, the Artifact Refinement Hall had countless other obligations, and surveying was meticulous work that could hardly be rushed in a day or two. A mere fortnight left everyone thoroughly panicked.

The disciples erupted into anxious clamor.

[—"Senior Brother Guan, why the rush? Is there something wrong with the project?"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")-a disciple asked urgently.

[—"Is a massive spiritual energy storm sweeping toward us? Is that why the dam is needed immediately?"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"No, if a spiritual storm were large enough to hit here, the entire Central Domain would be facing annihilation! I bet it's an ultra-powerful celestial demon invasion, and the Immortal Venerable needs to hoard spiritual energy to save the world?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

Guan Ye listened to their wild conjectures without interrupting, before declaring with grave solemnity:

—"Far more severe than that!"

The disciples' eyes bulged. They felt their apocalyptic theories of cataclysmic spiritual storms and celestial demons were already as terrible as it got—what on earth could possibly be worse than that?!

—"When Master proposed the dam project, he presented three distinct solutions,"-Guan Ye said with heavy gravitas.—"Our Artifact Refinement Hall forging the necessary infrastructure is merely one of them. The other two are 'planting' a living spiritual levee, and 'laying out an array' for a qi-storing reservoir. You understand what that implies, don't you?"

Every disciple in the Artifact Refinement Hall sucked in a cold breath.

Their expressions curdled into something far uglier than when they heard their design schematics might be scrapped.

[—"In other words, this project could be snatched away by someone else?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciples")-they roared in outrage.

[—"Laying out an array... that would be the Array Hall, right? And planting is..."](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Our sect's spiritual plants are overseen by Senior Brother Lu—that's under the Alchemy Department!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Aren't they supposed to be our brother departments?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"Damn it! Senior Brother Guan engineered the spirit spring plumbing, bringing Senior Sister Ran countless cave renovation commissions! Isn't their Array Hall busy enough with that?!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

[—"And Senior Brother Lu! We thought he had such a close bond with Senior Brother Guan! Who could have imagined he would backstab us like this!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciple")

The Artifact Refinement Hall disciples bellowed in righteous fury.

Only then did Guan Ye clear his throat:

—"Personal friendship is one thing; official business is another. You understand the rules of our Heavenly Spirit Sect, don't you? Master's guidance was never something just anyone could have."

Of course, that had not been Zhou Hui's original intent in the slightest.

Zhou Hui's exact words had been:

> "Although conventional wisdom dictates that the Artifact Refinement Hall's forged facilities are the safest route, Ye-zi, you folks are already quite busy over there, aren't you? So I also have two rather interesting, unconventional alternatives we could try."

That considerate concern had been decisively ignored by Guan Ye.

Guan Ye fixed his gaze on everyone in the hall:

—"Even when facing our closest martial brothers, where Master's matters are concerned, we must never yield an inch! Though time is exceptionally tight, we will seize this project no matter what!"

[—"Understood!"](# "Speaker: Artifact Refinement Disciples")

The disciples of the Artifact Refinement Hall roared with combative solidarity.

***

[—"Sounds pretty lively in the conference chamber. What are the seniors discussing?"](# "Speaker: New Disciple")

[—"Maybe there's some new artifact to forge?"](# "Speaker: New Disciple")

[—"They've already contracted the entire sect's spirit spring plumbing. What could possibly be more important than that?"](# "Speaker: New Disciple")

In the outer Artifact Refinement Hall, the new disciples listened to the faint clamor filtering out from the conference chamber, chatting leisurely. A few occasionally huddled in front of Zhao Yuegui to inspect his pot. None of them paid much heed to the impassioned shouts echoing from within.

This was standard operating procedure for the artifact refinement halls of other sects as well.

Under normal circumstances, the artifact refinement and alchemy departments were the [most lucrative departments](# "Literal CN: 最多油水 (zuì duō yóushui) — departments with the fattest perks, kickbacks, and resource allocations") in any sect.

After all, in a cultivation world where commerce was underdeveloped, most sects relied entirely on self-sufficiency. For immense recurring demands like the monthly allocations of spirit stones, medicinal pills, and magical artifacts distributed to disciples, it was naturally impossible to purchase everything from external markets; they had to be forged and refined in-house by their own alchemy and artifact refinement halls.

Consequently, in ordinary sects, positions within these two departments were almost invariably reserved for disciples with the strongest nepotistic backing.

A rogue cultivator like Zhao Yuegui would never have had the slightest ghost of a chance.

Yet Zhao Yuegui felt his luck was downright miraculous. After successfully passing the entrance trials, he had happened upon the Heavenly Spirit Sect outfitting the entire mountain with spirit spring plumbing. The Artifact Refinement Hall was critically short-staffed, allowing him to exploit a loophole and slip right in (or so he believed).

Even if the work was somewhat exhausting for now, it meant he would move into a cave dwelling blessed with a spirit spring far earlier than anyone else! In the Eastern Domain, such a luxury was something he wouldn't have dared dream of in his wildest fantasies.

Thus, even after receiving a harsh dressing-down from Guan Ye moments earlier, Zhao Yuegui felt no lingering resentment.

—Besides, as an artifact refiner, holding oneself to rigorous standards was only right and proper.

Zhao Yuegui felt that way, and the other new disciples shared virtually identical sentiments.

Everyone felt they were truly blessed by celestial fortune!

To gain entry so effortlessly into the notoriously exclusive Artifact Refinement Hall!

The new disciples ambled happily about the Artifact Refinement Hall like dyed, multicolored chicks bought from a night market, blissfully savoring their final moments of freedom.

They failed to notice that nestled among them were several individuals of a distinctly different feather. These figures had watched the conference chamber with grave expressions for a long time, and the instant the chamber doors swung open and senior disciples emerged to announce future arrangements, they slipped away into the shadows without making a sound.

***

[Is the chess room in this direction?]

Cheng Ziyi muttered inwardly, peering down the sprawling corridors of the Library Pavilion.

He had been wandering lost in this place for nearly the entire afternoon.

And to think he had specifically turned down Huang Xiao's invitation to visit the Artifact Refinement Hall for this!

Half the reason Cheng Ziyi came to the Library Pavilion was for Immortal Chess. From the moment he stepped foot into the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he had heard endless tales of its renown. Rumor had it that not only did everyone who played it become utterly obsessed, but even Su Xin of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had carried off several sets for himself.

That was Su Xin!

The golden standard for the Northern Domain's [second-generation cultivators](# "Literal CN: 修二代 (xiū èrdài) — privileged scions born to wealthy/powerful cultivation parents")!

Although Cheng Ziyi held little respect for the fellow's habit of throwing his weight around on the coattails of his Tribulation Transcendence parents, the young master's ability to set cultural trends could not be underestimated. If friends back in the Northern Domain wrote asking about the phenomenon and Cheng Ziyi couldn't answer a single question, they would inevitably suspect he had enrolled in a fraudulent Heavenly Spirit Sect.

That was why Cheng Ziyi wanted to verify it for himself.

Unfortunately, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Library Pavilion was simply too massive. Cheng Ziyi had wandered for half an afternoon without catching a glimpse of the place. Worse, because he had shouted loudly upon first entering the pavilion, he had been struck with a silence curse, complete with an inkblot plastered over his face. To avoid social humiliation, he had been forced to dodge around people, resulting in endless dilly-dallying that kept him from finding the way to the chess hall.

[Perhaps... I should just come back next time?]

Just as Cheng Ziyi was contemplating retreat, he suddenly noticed several figures slipping furtively into a room across the corridor. Instinctively glancing over, his eyes caught the plaque above the door: "Array Hall."

[I heard Immortal Chess is housed in the Array Hall... Could this be it?]

Cheng Ziyi trotted over in delight, only to be stopped cold right at the entrance.

—"Kid, what do you think you're doing?"-the disciple snapped with a hostile glare:—"This isn't a place you should be!"

With that, he slammed the Array Hall door shut.

Unable to speak due to his silence curse, Cheng Ziyi flared with indignation. As the descendant of a Xiafei Sect elder, he had never suffered such disrespectful treatment in any sect! Besides, the chess rooms in the Library Pavilion were public areas—by what right was he barred entry?!

He was going in, no matter what!

Naturally, a mere wooden door could never stop Cheng Ziyi.

Even if Cheng Ziyi was only at the sixth or seventh layer of Qi Condensation and had yet to formally select a core cultivation manual, his background was deeply rooted! If even a mortal prince walked around laden with protective treasures, what need was there to mention a textbook cultivation scion like Cheng Ziyi?

Rummaging through his storage pouch, Cheng Ziyi produced a talisman and slapped it squarely onto his forehead with a crisp *smack*.

In an instant, his physical form dissolved into a dark shadow, sliding seamlessly beneath the crack of the door right into the "Array Hall."

Yet the interior of the Array Hall was nothing like what Cheng Ziyi had heard. There were no formation tables lined up with disciples huddled over chess matches; instead, a single colossal table sat in the center, surrounded by a tight cluster of people.

Cheng Ziyi crept forward, melding his shadow into the shadows cast by the crowd.

Only then did he discover, to his awkward dismay, that what lay atop the table appeared to be a three-dimensional map of Mount Tianling.

And the matters being discussed were unmistakably unrelated to playing chess:

[—"...Looks like his choice is solely their Artifact Refinement Hall's proposal!"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")

[—"Outrageous! Shouldn't a qi-storing reservoir naturally be spearheaded by our Array Hall?!"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")

[—"Ah-Deng said she's too exhausted, has far too much on her plate, and already wants to decline."](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")

[—"Sister Ah-Deng is only exhausted because of Artifact Refinement's plumbing! They build pipes, control springs, and get praised by Grandmaster, while our Array Hall has been reduced to interior decorators renovating caves!"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")

[—"Exactly! If this continues, the Array Hall will become a mere appendage of the Artifact Refinement Hall!"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")

[—"It was fine before we arrived, but since the Clan Patriarch sent us here, we cannot let Ah-Deng fall into such a state! Sister Ran Deng is Guan Ye's senior sister after all!"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")

—"..."

Listening to this heated discourse, Cheng Ziyi's shadow shifted back two cautious steps.

Only because Cheng Ziyi was currently a shadow did his face not turn stark white:

[Disaster!]

[I've stumbled right into the secret conspiracy meeting of a sect civil war!]

Cheng Ziyi concluded in horror.

Although he didn't fully comprehend the technical jargon they were bandying about, piecing together keywords like "Guan Ye," "Ran Deng," "fall into such a state," and "monopolize," combined with his firsthand experience of the Xiafei Sect's internal intrigues and the myriad gossip circulating among Northern Domain scions, Cheng Ziyi was utterly convinced his assessment was bulletproof!

In the dark underbelly of cultivation sects, this was the one thing that was never in short supply!

After all, even a single mortal family could scarcely avoid domestic courtyard scheming! A sect inevitably possessed one Sect Master, multiple elders, and their respective direct disciples. Consequently, no matter how harmonious a sect appeared on the surface, conflicts over the division of resources were unavoidable. Outwardly, they smiled and exchanged pleasantries; behind the scenes, they were beating each other's brains out!

It was precisely because Cheng Ziyi had grown sick and tired of witnessing the filthy, underhanded machinations within the Xiafei Sect that he had fled to the Central Domain.

Never in his wildest dreams had he imagined that even the Untiring Immortal Venerable's Heavenly Spirit Sect would be plagued by the exact same disease!

No—precisely because it was the Untiring Immortal Venerable's sect, it was bound to happen!

Because a legendary figure as monumental as the Untiring Immortal Venerable was destined to ascend any day now! His direct disciples were still young, which meant they had already plunged into vicious, bloody infighting over power, status, and legacy!

How tragic! If the Heavenly Spirit Sect were destroyed by internal strife, how heartbroken would the ascended Immortal Venerable be?!

(Zhou Hui: *Excuse me, I haven't ascended yet!*)

Cheng Ziyi thought with deep, agonizing grief, concocting an entire Shakespearean tragedy of factional civil war in his head.

Yet however profound his grief and wild his imagination, he understood full well that if anyone discovered him eavesdropping on this "conspiracy scene," he would certainly be [silenced](# "Literal CN: 灭口 (mièkǒu) — eliminating witnesses to keep a secret")!

Thus, Cheng Ziyi tiptoed his shadow toward the doorway with utmost care.

The quality of his talisman was exceptionally fine. Only when his shadow was on the verge of slipping out the door did one of the "conspiring" disciples suddenly look up, glaring sharply in his direction:

—"Who's there?!"

Paying no heed to anything else, Cheng Ziyi bolted through the exit at top speed.

The disciple yanked the door open, catching only a glimpse of a sprinting silhouette vanishing down the hall.

[—"What should we do? Should we chase after him?"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciple")-someone inside asked.

The disciple shook his head:

—"No need. Most likely someone from Artifact Refinement or Alchemy. In any case, the competing proposals for the 'Spiritual Energy Dam' aren't a secret; it doesn't matter if they know."

[—"Right! Our Array Hall will never yield an inch!"](# "Speaker: Ran Clan Disciples")

***

While the Array Hall disciples burned with fighting spirit, on the other end, Cheng Ziyi returned to his dormitory quarters panting heavily.

His roommates had long since returned, huddled together in lively discussion.

Seeing Cheng Ziyi's disheveled state, they all looked somewhat taken aback.

—"What happened?"-Ji Gen asked, pouring a cup of water and handing it to him.

—"..."-Cheng Ziyi paid no attention to his manners, chugging the water down in great, frantic gulps: *glug, glug, glug*.

Upon leaving the Library Pavilion, the silence curse placed upon Cheng Ziyi had naturally dissipated.

Looking at his roommates, he wanted to blurt out everything he had just witnessed, but after a second thought, he suppressed the urge.

Before entering the Heavenly Spirit Sect, these roommates of his were either rogue cultivators or mortals. Even that Jing Chenjun, who seemed to possess some pedigree, didn't appear to hail from any major sect—Cheng Ziyi suspected he was merely a junior disciple from some reclusive hermit clan. They certainly possessed no inkling of the filthy, dark secrets lurking behind the facade of grand cultivation sects. Why shatter their innocent romantic illusions of immortal life?

Furthermore, if this leaked out, being silenced alone was one thing; he could not drag innocent bystanders down with him!

After an impassioned bout of heroic self-admiration, Cheng Ziyi forced himself to speak in a calm, steady tone:

—"I nearly missed the Sundial Clock's teleportation window, so I had to sprint... What were you all discussing that seemed so cheerful?"

—"We're discussing which department to apprentice in,"-Huang Xiao answered without suspicion.

As mentioned earlier, disciples in the beginner class were akin to apprentices. The sect did not distribute spirit stones, medicinal pills, and cultivation resources to them every month for nothing. Although Zhou Hui believed they should focus primarily on their studies, the vast majority of this session's beginner disciples were adults, so they still needed to perform odd chores.

Even so, the Heavenly Spirit Sect was far more lenient in this regard than other sects. Apprenticeships didn't demand stellar academic scores; so long as one filled out an expression of intent form and the target department had an opening, one could head over to apprentice (run errands).

—"I want to go to the Artifact Refinement Hall,"-Jing Chenjun said.

Hearing this and recalling the "conspiracy" he had just eavesdropped on, Cheng Ziyi couldn't help but give Jing Chenjun a lingering, pitying look.

—"But Senior Brother Guan looks so terrifying,"-Huang Xiao said, shrinking back slightly.

—"That is merely necessary strictness; I find it quite admirable,"-Jing Chenjun replied.

In truth, it was primarily because he had observed the Artifact Refinement Hall's workflow and realized that tasks involving calculation and measurement were things he could easily perform. If he switched to a post requiring active spellcasting, his mortal identity would be exposed in minutes.

Cheng Ziyi parted his lips, but ultimately refrained from warning him.

On second thought, high-level conspiracies wouldn't reach lowly handymen for the time being. He would simply remind Jing Chenjun not to look at things he shouldn't, and if necessary, he could even have Jing Chenjun help gather intelligence! Cheng Ziyi plotted shrewdly.

Meanwhile, Huang Xiao spoke up:

—"I'm going to the Beast Enclosure."

[—"I thought you would go to the Delicacy Pavilion?"](# "Speaker: Jing Chenjun")-the others asked in surprise.

—"My younger sister already went there,"-Huang Xiao explained:—"Besides, if one wants to eat good food, someone has to keep a close eye on the ingredients!"

[—"Our future three meals depend entirely on you!"](# "Speaker: Roommates")-everyone declared solemnly.

—"Haha, Brother Huang is heading to the source of meat, while I wish to go to the other source of ingredients!"-Ji Gen chuckled, the little flower atop his head swaying.—"I'm going to the Spirit Herb Fields."

He had come to the Heavenly Spirit Sect specifically to learn how to better control the flora grafted onto his body, so this was an obvious choice.

Cheng Ziyi felt this was for the best; these two naive simpletons ought to stay as far away from political conspiracy as possible.

—"What about you?"-Huang Xiao finally turned to Cheng Ziyi.—"Does Fellow Daoist Cheng have any plans?"

—"I shall become a sword cultivator!"-Cheng Ziyi proclaimed proudly, lifting his chin high.

[—"Ooh!"](# "Speaker: Huang Xiao and Ji Gen")-Huang Xiao and Ji Gen gasped in awe.

—"Very fitting for Fellow Daoist Cheng,"-Jing Chenjun agreed with a nod.

[With handwriting like that... aside from becoming a sword cultivator, his only other option would be drawing talismans, wouldn't it?]

Having received his roommates' affirmation, Cheng Ziyi puffed out his chest.

Exactly—he would become a sword cultivator!

If one day the Heavenly Spirit Sect met with catastrophe due to internal strife, that would be the moment for him, the righteous sword cultivator, to turn the tide and save the day!

Only that would do justice to his profound reverence for the Immortal Venerable!

***

[As expected, sword cultivators are truly magnificent!]

The very next day, Cheng Ziyi realized he had made an incomparably brilliant choice.

Having submitted his expression of intent form that morning, he was led out that very afternoon for his apprenticeship trial by Senior Yan Fei, one of the sect's most outstanding sword cultivators.

The initial destination of their apprenticeship was to observe Senior Sister Mo Bei's sword practice.

[Truly worthy of being the Immortal Venerable's direct disciple!]

With every fluid sweep of Senior Sister Mo's soft sword, minute water droplets condensed into a fine mist of frost, draping over the pale, rainbow-tinted Snow-Glow Grass in a display of transcendent beauty!

Furthermore, word had it that this senior sister never formed cliques or joined factions, maintaining an indifferent, aloof expression even toward her own twin brother. She was the absolute paragon of the aloof, untouchable sword cultivator—so completely different from everyone else!

Cheng Ziyi thought with mounting exhilaration.

What thrilled him even further was that after the demonstration concluded, Senior Yan Fei distributed a soft sword similar to Senior Sister Mo Bei's to every single apprentice disciple—though it was a lower-tier version, it was still capable of churning up misty water sprays.

—"Senior Brother, I have a Fire Spirit Root,"-one disciple protested hesitantly.

—"No matter, this is a practice sword,"-Yan Fei answered with a beaming smile.

[—"Practice?"](# "Speaker: Apprentice Sword Cultivator")

—"Regardless of your spirit roots, you all aspire to reach Lady Mo Bei's realm, don't you?"-Yan Fei asked.

[—"Yes!"](# "Speaker: Apprentice Sword Cultivators")

—"Excellent, follow me!"

Yan Fei timed his words to the second.

Clearly having planned this in advance, he activated the teleportation function of the Sundial Clock.

And so, in the very next instant, the aspiring sword cultivator cadets found themselves standing beside a lush, verdant... spirit herb field.

Yes, a literal spirit herb field.

Cheng Ziyi spotted his roommate Ji Gen standing right on the opposite side of the field, excitedly waving at him. To maintain the solemn dignity of a sword cultivator, however, Cheng Ziyi ignored him completely.

Yan Fei looked upon these apprentice sword cadets striving mightily to keep their composure and maintain straight faces, nodding in deep satisfaction.

—"Alright, let us begin practice!"

—"Divide into pairs of two and line up along the field ridges. Yes, just like that."

—"To start, swing your swords at the spirit fields fifty times a day, until you can evenly control the water droplets falling upon the vegetable patches!"

—"Keep at it."
