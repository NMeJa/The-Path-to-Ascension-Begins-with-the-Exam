# Chapter 190: Ran Deng's Plan

—"You already settled it?!"-Ran Deng cried in disbelief.

At this moment, she was on the crane terrace of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree. Before her stood a flock of unintelligent spiritual cranes; startled by her outburst, several timid ones immediately took flight, while a few bolder ones craned their elegant necks to peek over.

Ran Deng flurriedly covered the bronze communication mirror in her hand, ducking into a corner.

Only then did she lower her voice and grind her teeth at the other side:

—"Yuwen Xuan! Don't you dare fool me!"

Naturally, Ran Deng found it hard to believe.

After all, it had been less than thirty-six [shichen](# "A traditional Chinese unit of time equal to two hours; thirty-six shichen equals seventy-two hours, or three full days") since she parted ways with Yuwen Xuan.

Three days ago, after sending Eldest Senior Brother off, she had been preparing to head to the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree to see Master. Unexpectedly, before she could even depart, the "omniscient" Old Ancestor Jiu Zhai somehow caught wind of the news that she was going to see Master. Claiming he had stayed at the Heavenly Spirit Sect for far too long, he wanted to [catch a ride](# "Literal CN: 趁个车 (chèn ge chē) — hitch a ride / carpool; modern colloquialism applied humorously to immortal travel") back to the Southern Domain.

Ran Deng had no way to refuse.

On one hand, she rejoiced that she hadn't traveled alongside Eldest Senior Brother; on the other, she worried this old Divination cultivator might have seen through something. Trembling with apprehension the entire way, she accompanied him aboard a Kunpeng to Fengqing City, took a large-scale teleportation array from Fengqing City to the Southern Domain, and then rode a white crane across the Southern Domain, spending a full three days before just barely reaching the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree—which was also the absolute fastest travel time normal cultivators in the cultivation world would take for this journey.

She never expected that she would have barely stepped off the white crane, before even having time to straighten her robes and documents to pay respects to Master, when she received a transmission from Eldest Senior Brother informing her that the deed was done.

Ran Deng [believed him like hell](# "Literal CN: 信他个鬼 (xìn tā ge guǐ) — 'believe his ghost'; idiom meaning not believing a single word someone says / believing a likely story")!

—"Did you really go to the Heavenly Dao Alliance? Fine, even if you are at the Heavenly Dao Alliance, how did you pull it off so fast?"-Ran Deng asked anxiously.-"You didn't use some forbidden art... like using that so-called 'Beast-Taming Art' of yours on humans, did you? And then, when Master gets furious later, planning to make me [take the blame](# "Literal CN: 背锅 (bēiguō) — carry the black pot; modern slang for taking the blame / being the scapegoat")?!"

—"What kind of person do you take me for?"-Yuwen Xuan grumbled in displeasure.-"Since I promised you, I will naturally get it done."

As he spoke, he deliberately angled the mirror to let Ran Deng see the grand hall of Yingju Hall in the Heavenly Dao Alliance behind him, as well as Guan Xinguang and Goddess Ren Ran who were right beside him.

What relieved Ran Deng was that while the complexions of those two didn't look too pleasant, they clearly remained compos mentis, seemingly still discussing something; they hadn't been turned into Eldest Senior Brother's livestock. In truth, it wouldn't have mattered even if they had become livestock, but the next step required a mentally sound Chief Director of the Department of Works—she couldn't let her Eldest Senior Brother run wild.

Fortunately, Yuwen Xuan had no intention of employing extreme measures, merely boasting smugly:

—"As for how I accomplished it, it is naturally because I have done many good deeds, accumulating virtue and benevolence until Heaven and Earth were moved. Thus, the Heavens rewarded me, dropping a massive snack right out of the sky—"

[—"A snack?"](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

—"Mhm, exceptionally delicious meat,"-Yuwen Xuan said, licking the corners of his lips, giving Ran Deng a wave of cold chills.

Ran Deng suddenly had no desire to know what this "exceptionally delicious meat" actually was.

However, Yuwen Xuan seemed worried that someone might covet his food and had no intention of elaborating, merely continuing:

—"Not only is that meat delicious, it's also wonderfully kindhearted. Without waiting for me to do anything, it actually instigates the target to jump straight into the bowl alongside it."

Ran Deng didn't understand what nonsense Eldest Senior Brother was babbling, but she understood the core message:

The deed was done!

Even though Ran Deng didn't believe for a second that Yuwen Xuan had done nothing.

—"Since you're done, how about coming back quickly?"-Ran Deng suggested.

—"No way! What if someone steals my meat?"-Yuwen Xuan replied, guarding his food with a vigilant expression.

[What do you mean 'your meat'?! Which cell of that piece of meat(?) ever agreed to enter your stomach?!] That was precisely what Ran Deng was worried about:

—"Come back first! Don't go eating random things!"

—"I will return—together with my prey,"-Yuwen Xuan insisted stubbornly.-"Rather than worrying about this, you should make sure Master and Old Fourth are ready. Especially Old Fourth: if he can't bring himself to strike, then even his spine will belong to me."

Having said this, Yuwen Xuan decisively cut off the transmission.

—"Yuwen Xuan!"-Ran Deng shouted in fury.

She rubbed the mirror repeatedly several times, but was utterly unable to contact Yuwen Xuan again. She could only resentfully make her way toward the depths of the Sacred Tree, praying in her heart that her abominable Eldest Senior Brother wouldn't do anything reckless.

***

Guan Ye greeted Ran Deng at the entrance of the Sun Wheel Palace.

For Guan Ye, this was exceedingly rare behavior.

It wasn't that Guan Ye didn't respect his senior sister or senior brothers; rather, he had an inherently solitary disposition, locking himself in the artifact refinement workshop most of the time and rarely engaging in social pleasantries. Furthermore, given the temperaments of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's direct disciples and the profound camaraderie among them, there was never any need for deliberate acts of courtesy or intimacy; if someone did so, one would instead have to be wary of an ulterior motive.

Guan Ye was no exception right now.

Sure enough, his primary purpose was not to welcome her, but to ask straight to the point:

—"Senior Sister, did you arrange that 'Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination'?"

[—"And the Formation Master Qualification Examination."](# "Speaker: Ran Deng")

—"Right, and formations... Senior Sister, what did you arrange these two exams for?"-Guan Ye asked in dissatisfaction.

Indeed, both examinations were Ran Deng's brainchild.

When Guan Ye first saw this project proposal, he felt as though he had been blindsided by a flying boulder.

—"Isn't it all because you suddenly announced you wanted to build a large-scale teleportation array?!"-Ran Deng was even more dissatisfied than Guan Ye.-"Manpower in the sect was already strained, and then Second Senior Brother suddenly poached a whole crowd of people to construct defense measures for the Demon Eye—and now he has even vanished along with Shulong! The personnel coordination for the entire sect has fallen squarely on my head. If I don't resort to special measures, how am I supposed to find anyone?"

—"...I don't need that many assistants. Just a handful is enough; I can handle it myself,"-Guan Ye said dryly.-"Besides, I never asked you for help. Master merely told you to contact a few artifact refiners to come over, yet you took the initiative to demand responsibility for manpower, making it so absurdly grandiose."

Guan Ye's words sounded like someone receiving free help while still being ungrateful.

Yet anyone familiar with Guan Ye knew this wasn't his intention; he simply didn't know how to speak tactfully.

In truth, Guan Ye had never intended to burden Ran Deng.

With Guan Ye's temperament, he would have loved nothing more than to lock himself in the artifact refinement workshop, finish all the work entirely by himself before bringing it out, without troubling a single soul.

It was just that a large-scale teleportation array truly exceeded his personal capacity and required numerous permits, which was why Guan Ye had no choice but to consult Master in advance. Even so, aside from the array blueprints, Guan Ye had drafted an execution plan grueling enough to work himself to death, attempting to shoulder as much of the workload as humanly possible and only outsourcing the tiny portion where help was strictly unavoidable.

Zhou Hui naturally wouldn't stand by and watch Guan Ye push himself to destruction, intending to contact a few powerhouses to serve as assistants. And because it involved connections within the formation master network, Ran Deng had learned of the matter.

In past years, Ran Deng would have grumbled non-stop while dutifully obeying Master's instructions, refusing to invite the slightest bit of extra trouble for herself—or rather, not even wanting to do the tasks Master assigned.

This year, however, Ran Deng had other ideas.

No—more accurately, after recovering the memories of her previous lifetimes, Ran Deng had other ideas.

She didn't want to die cluelessly like in her previous lives, having accomplished nothing, merely watching Master fruitlessly cycle through reincarnation again and again, unable to ascend. She wanted to gain control over the circumstances within her grasp, once and for all.

Huh? Sounds contradictory?

It being contradictory was precisely the point.

Because she, Ran Deng, had always been so useless and contradictory, perpetually stuck in a state of wanting to [lie flat](# "Slang: 躺平 (tǎngpíng) — lying flat; opting out of the rat race, slacking off") yet unable to do so due to a lingering sense of responsibility. It had to be because the soul-fire at the center of her forehead hadn't been fully refined—meaning she hadn't been thoroughly cured and wasn't [salty enough](# "Pun: 咸 (xián) — meaning both literally salty (like cured salted fish) and figuratively slacking off (from 咸鱼, salted fish)").

Mulling over these random thoughts, Ran Deng didn't pause her steps, walking toward the depths of the Sun Wheel Palace where Master resided.

As she walked, she said:

—"Actually, that examination isn't too troublesome."

—"How is it not troublesome? You said it yourself: the Heavenly Spirit Sect was already short on hands, and now we have to divert resources to run an examination. Won't that make things even more chaotic? Not to mention the Immortal Arts Assembly is right around the corner..."-Guan Ye said anxiously.

At this point, Guan Ye didn't say anything like "I can handle it myself"—that standard was reserved solely for himself.

Thus, if any Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple were to witness this scene, they would find it thoroughly spine-chilling:

Senior Brother Guan Ye, the one least afraid of trouble, was unwilling to hold exams because they were "too troublesome," while Senior Sister Ran Deng, the one who dreaded trouble the most, insisted it was "not troublesome" and fought tooth and nail to hold them.

Yet even though both suffered from [social anxiety](# "Cultivation pun: 社恐 (shèkǒng) — modern term for social anxiety/phobia"), 'willing but lacking the energy' and 'not wanting to face it' were two entirely different emotions. In terms of organizational prowess, Ran Deng far surpassed Guan Ye.

She, in particular, knew how to save effort and cut corners.

—"It's precisely because of the Immortal Arts Assembly that holding qualification exams is so convenient!"-Ran Deng said.-"After so much publicity, cultivators not just from the Central Domain, but from all over the world have flooded into the vicinity of the Heavenly Spirit Sect for the assembly. Most of them can't participate in the tournament anyway, and since they're idling around with nothing better to do, why not have them take an exam en masse so their trip isn't wasted?"

[...Are you even speaking human words?]

Guan Ye was instantly stunned by Ran Deng's baffling train of thought.

He stared at Ran Deng, feeling as if she had been possessed by Master, and couldn't help taking a step back.

Ran Deng continued:

—"Besides, in our Heavenly Spirit Sect, examination halls and question banks are already readily available. Hosting a qualification exam is merely an incidental endeavor—it could even serve as an appetizer for the Immortal Arts Assembly."

—"B-But..."-Guan Ye stammered.

From the perspective of a person with common sense, he couldn't shake the feeling that something was deeply wrong.

Ran Deng stepped closer toward Guan Ye, fixing her gaze onto his eyes:

—"Lastly, don't misunderstand. Although this exam is to recruit suitable assistants for the large-scale teleportation array, I am definitely not doing it for your sake. Guan Ye, you haven't forgotten who I am, have you?"

[—"Eldest Senior Sister?"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"No, I mean my profession!"-Ran Deng retorted.

[—"... ... ...A spoiled playboy?"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

`!`

Ran Deng jabbed a command flag straight into Guan Ye's head. Fortunately, it was Guan Ye—he was probably the most pain-tolerant disciple in the entire Heavenly Spirit Sect. Even with the sharp tip of the command flag stabbed dead center into his forehead, causing blood to stream down half his face, he didn't utter a single sound.

On the contrary, Ran Deng declared loudly:

—"I am a formation master! A formation master of the Ran clan!"

[—"Ah..."](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"Ah your head! Ah what?! When you submitted the large-scale teleportation array to Master... no, when you were researching that thing, you didn't give me a heads-up beforehand, and I only learned about it from other people! Do you have any idea how much of a slap in the face that was for me? Right now, my Formation Hall and my clan have practically exploded—almost everyone is hounding me about this!"-This was the honest truth.

Compared to the extra workload, the interpersonal complications currently causing trouble for Ran Deng were far worse.

Yet Ran Deng also knew that with Guan Ye's oblivious mind that understood zero worldly wisdom, he never would have thought of that.

Wearing an expression of someone who had clearly just realized this, Guan Ye clenched his jaw and said slowly:

—"I'm sorry."

—"This isn't something a simple 'I'm sorry' can solve,"-Ran Deng crossed her arms.

She somewhat regretted rushing out too quickly without bringing a pair of male cousins from the Formation Hall to pose as her little maidservants. They had been chattering incessantly and annoying her to death recently anyway; if she had brought them along as background noise, she would have had much more of the domineering presence of an [arrogant and villainous female secondary lead](# "Trope: 刁蛮恶毒女配 (diāomán èdú nǚpèi) — arrogant, shrewish female antagonist in romance/drama web novels").

For now, Ran Deng could only rely on herself, using her height of one meter sixty-seven to bully her junior brother of one meter eighty-six:

—"Guan Ye, in my capacity as the heir of the Ran clan, I declare war on you!"

`"..."`

—"Let us settle this once and for all in the qualification examination! If I win, you must publish a statement in the newspapers acknowledging that I am the strongest formation master in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and surrender the entire large-scale teleportation array project to our Formation Hall..."

—"You want to take charge of this project?"-Guan Ye's eyes widened in astonishment.

[Did I really go too far?]

Ran Deng thought to herself, yet she still braced herself and nodded:

—"That's right! What of it?"

—"Senior Sister, are you alright?"-Guan Ye showed a worried expression instead.-"Even though taking the initiative to host qualification exams was already bizarre, now you actually want to take charge of the teleportation array project. You haven't been possessed by some unclean spirit, have you?!"

—"Just say whether you agree or not!"-Ran Deng snapped, flying into a rage from embarrassment.

—"I agree—about the teleportation array, that is,"-Guan Ye said earnestly.-"I didn't know that my building the teleportation array would bring Senior Sister so much trouble; otherwise, I would have handed it over to you from the very beginning. But it isn't too late now, right? Whether it's presiding over the construction of the large-scale teleportation array or the empty vanity of having designed it, there's no need for any test or duel, nor any need for victory or defeat. If you need it, just take it all. All I ask is that you let me continue participating in the construction."

—"Huh? You designed this teleportation array! Do you really feel no indignation whatsoever?"-Ran Deng pressed.

—"Why would I?"-Guan Ye asked, puzzled.-"This teleportation array was commissioned to forge a better alliance with the demon race anyway. In the final analysis, it's because we disciples are too useless—unable to shift locations freely like Master and constantly delaying Master's research—that such a teleportation array is necessary. Therefore, who designed it and who oversees its construction doesn't matter at all. What matters is how much we can learn from it."

—"I knew you'd say that,"-Ran Deng muttered.

It was precisely because this fellow always cared only for craftsmanship itself while ignoring everything else that he had been plotted against by that bastard from the Heavenly Dao Alliance's Department of Works, having even his spine ripped out, wasn't it?

Ran Deng thought to herself.

Ran Deng, of course, knew who Guan Ye's enemy was.

Or rather, with the exception of Mo Xi and Mo Bei who had entered the sect latest, all the other disciples knew the truth.

After all, the place where Master picked up Guan Ye was no secret, and given Guan Ye's condition at the time, he couldn't possibly have been far from the place where his bones were extracted. Thus, as long as one looked into it, it was trivial to uncover whom that estate belonged to—

Back then, Zhou Hui had been visiting the Mei-Zhu couple at the Heavenly Dao Alliance when he found Guan Ye in a mass grave nearby. Near that mass grave was a minor cultivation clan; although the clan patriarch was merely at Foundation Establishment, he happened to be a distant relative of Guan Xinguang, Chief Director of the Department of Works in the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Even though Zhou Hui had left immediately to heal Guan Ye without punishing the culprit, had they wished to avenge him afterward, they could have done so right away. The reason it was never carried out was that shortly after Guan Ye was rescued, Guan Xinguang of the Department of Works—perhaps catching wind of the matter—immediately used "internal housekeeping" as an excuse to "investigate." Claiming his distant relative had been corrupted by inner demons and committed widespread atrocities, he executed the relative, casually purged the relative's entire family, and even fabricated a demon captive to pin the blame on.

And because Guan Xinguang had acted so ruthlessly and thoroughly, if Zhou Hui wanted to seek vengeance for Guan Ye by continuing to investigate the matter without hard evidence, he would inevitably have to tear up all pleasantries with the Heavenly Dao Alliance, potentially even damaging his relationship with the Mei-Zhu couple.

Guan Ye hadn't wanted Master to go that far for his sake; dragging his ailing body, he had bitterly pleaded with Zhou Hui, repeatedly declaring that he "wanted to take revenge with his own hands," which finally persuaded Zhou Hui not to pursue the matter further.

Yet Guan Ye was only at Foundation Establishment, whereas Guan Xinguang was already at the Mahayana Stage. It wasn't that Guan Ye could never catch up to Guan Xinguang—given Guan Ye's sheer perseverance, nobody doubted he could eventually reach Mahayana—but rather because Guan Ye's foundational bones and meridians were heavily damaged, and entering the Dao through craftsmanship was a cultivation path that demanded immense time. Everyone worried that before Guan Ye could grow to fruition, Guan Xinguang might meet his demise first.

Furthermore, did Guan Ye truly have the heart to seek vengeance?

This was what everyone in the Heavenly Spirit Sect had always worried about.

After all, looking at Guan Ye's current stubborn obsession—caring solely about the craft while disregarding worldly trifles like fame, reputation, and the opinions of others—he might very well tell himself, [What's in the past is in the past; why waste energy on superfluous matters? Isn't forging a few more artifacts far sweeter?](# "Meme: 不香吗 (bù xiāng ma) — 'isn't it fragrant?'; popular slang meaning 'isn't that way better?'"), thereby convincing himself to let his mortal enemy off the hook.

The problem was: even if one's mindset became 'broad-minded', would the trauma inflicted upon him genuinely vanish?

A tragedy doesn't become a comedy simply because its protagonist is a smiling fool; it only appears that much more tragic.

Even if Guan Ye tried not to think about it, his crippled meridians, his ill-fitting spine, and the scars branded into the depths of his heart would whisper to his subconscious, until one day during a breakthrough, they would manifest as an insurmountable inner demon, once again obstructing his Great Dao.

Ran Deng didn't know what plans the others had for this—perhaps Master had a better method—but this time, when Ran Deng decided to settle her own affairs 'once and for all', she discovered she could resolve Guan Ye's troubles 'once and for all' along the way.

In martial might, he couldn't compete yet. But in artifact refinement, would Guan Ye ever fear losing?

This was Junior Brother Guan, who could design a large-scale teleportation array at Foundation Establishment!

In this entire mortal realm, probably only Master was qualified to teach him!

Thus, Ran Deng had painstakingly set the stage, even risking being strung up on the tree herself to let Eldest Senior Brother down so he could invite another crucial actor. Yet she hadn't anticipated that the actor in her own household would prove so difficult to handle.

Ran Deng could only adopt a tough stance:

—"Still, you say you'll hand the project over and that's it? Wouldn't that make me look like I have [no face](# "Cultural idiom: 面子 (miànzi) — social face / pride / prestige")? Right now, in my capacity as the Eldest Senior Sister of the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I command you: you must take the Artifact Refiner Qualification Examination!"

[—"...Very well."](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"No throwing the match! You must participate with utmost seriousness!"

—"I am serious about every exam,"-Guan Ye replied.

Ran Deng looked at Guan Ye and knitted her brows, still feeling something was missing.

She believed he would take the exam seriously, but merely having an exam-taker's attitude wasn't enough!

She had to instill in her junior brother the burning desire to take "first place."

Only then would he conquer Guan Xinguang in the artifact refinement qualification exam, thoroughly crushing the man and eradicating his inner demon in advance.

Yet Ran Deng didn't want Guan Ye to know before the exam that the outcome was tied to his blood vendetta, because she didn't want to place too much pressure on her junior brother and force him to dredge up those hateful memories.

Just as Ran Deng was agonizing over how to 'motivate' Guan Ye with a few more words, a voice drifted from behind:

—"Ah-Ran, Ye-zi, what are you two doing outside?"

—"Ah, Master,"-Ran Deng scrambled to bow.

She only just realized that she and Guan Ye had lingered in the corridor for far too long—long enough that Master had come out to see what they were up to.

As soon as Zhou Hui stepped out, he noticed the command flag still planted in Guan Ye's head:

—"What is going on with Ye-zi's head..."

—"My hand slipped a bit. Heheh, just slipped a bit,"-Ran Deng hurriedly shoved Guan Ye behind her, offering a clumsy cover-up, yet showing no intention of pulling the command flag out.

—"Pranks should have their limits,"-fortunately, Zhou Hui didn't press the matter. However, his next sentence made Ran Deng wish Master had asked a few more questions about the command flag instead:

—"Still, Ah-Ran, you've arrived at the right time. The Heavenly Dao Alliance just contacted me, saying their Department of Works also plans to participate in the qualification examinations—including the Chief Director of the Department of Works, Guan Xinguang."

—"Master!"-Ran Deng cried out.

Flustered, she almost reached out to cover Master's mouth, but realizing how utterly irreverent that would be, she dropped her hand in despair and turned to look at Guan Ye's expression.

Just as Ran Deng had feared, the moment Guan Ye heard Zhou Hui's words, his face turned ash-white and his body froze stiff:

[—"The Department of Works? Guan Xinguang?"](# "Speaker: Guan Ye")

—"J-Junior Brother, perhaps this is a mis..."-misunderstanding.

Ran Deng had no idea how to explain, when she heard Master's voice echo in her mind:

`"Don't panic; your junior brother is not that fragile."`

—"Master?"-Ran Deng looked up at Zhou Hui in astonishment.

She saw Master slightly furrowing his brows, watching Guan Ye with focused attention, before she turned her gaze back to Guan Ye.

Ran Deng saw her Fourth Senior Brother—whose honesty often struck her as somewhat cowardly—cover his face with his hands. His shoulders began to tremble violently, causing that section of spine protruding outside his back, which rarely ever moved, to twitch and rattle along with him, tapping rhythmically against the floor, thud, thud, thud.

[He's not crying, is he?]

Ran Deng wanted to comfort him:

—"Jun..."

—"So that's how it is... So that's how it is! Senior Sister, this was your true intention behind wanting me to take the exam all along, wasn't it?"-Guan Ye asked. His voice was hoarse and grating, like the caw of a raven perched upon a withered branch, making Ran Deng jump in fright.

Yet what terrified Ran Deng even more were the eyes peering at her from between his fingers.

Those were the eyes of a vengeful ghoul.

Eyes sealed away for thousands of years, ravenously longing to devour flesh and soul.

They were so bottomless, like an abyss, that Ran Deng subconsciously shrank behind Master.

Seeing this, Guan Ye's shoulders shook even more violently. A sound akin to a vengeful demon tore from his throat:

—"[Jiejie, jiejiejiejie!](# "Onomatopoeia: 桀桀 (jiéjié) — classic sinister, cackling laughter of evil cultivators, villains, and ghouls in Xianxia novels")"-Guan Ye laughed maniacally.-"Senior Sister, rest assured. I will treat this 'examination' very, very well!"
