# Chapter 180: [Not Swayed by External Gains](# "Literal CN: 不以物喜 (bù yǐ wù xǐ) — alluding to Fan Zhongyan's famous line in Yueyang Lou Ji: 'Not pleased by external gains, nor saddened by personal losses' (不以物喜，不以己悲); directly puns on Mo Xi's (莫喜) name and character arc of letting go of external wealth to rely on his own internal foundation")

The direct disciples of the Immortal Venerable hardly felt tormented.

In fact, they were even slightly jealous.

—"This function is quite nice!"-Mo Bei remarked, speaking in a rare tone of praise.—"Why hasn't Master set one up in our Heavenly Spirit Sect classrooms?"

—"He did. The 'skill system' in the secret realm is essentially the same thing,"-Mo Xi replied.

Several floating holographic panels hovered before him, densely packed with numbers that made Mo Bei dizzy just looking at them—in this respect, Mo Bei had to concede that no matter how hard she tried, she couldn't match Mo Xi. Although they were twins, the partitions of their brains seemed to have developed in diametrically opposed directions. As Mo Xi keyed in data, he continued explaining:

—"However, our Heavenly Spirit Sect's secret realm cannot achieve those two points so flawlessly—'reading the students' real-time data' and 'simulating developmental progression based on their data.' It merely simulates the completed moves after mastery. Or rather, even if Master possesses spells that could help us simulate post-growth states, the required volume of spiritual energy would simply be far too colossal, far beyond what Heavenly Spirit Sect's resources could ever support."

Mo Bei paused, momentarily stunned:

—"Even Master can't do it? Then this is..."

—"It's likely the power of the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, and connected to the demon race's sacred treasure?"-Mo Xi guessed with pinpoint accuracy.—"Their entire demon population lives upon the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree, essentially functioning as part of the Sacred Tree itself, so gathering their data is comparatively simple. But being able to simulate growth progression to such a degree, and simulating so many demons simultaneously—that is genuinely astonishing! The sheer volume of computation alone is on an incomprehensible scale. It is truly difficult to imagine what could be accomplished if this power were fully harnessed!"

—"Heh,"-Mo Bei responded coldly.

She now had no doubt that what Mo Xi was doing was indeed Master's command.

—Channeling such monumental power purely into educating low-tier demonlings—only Master could ever be capable of something so outrageously extravagant.

Yet whether out of instinct or some twin telepathy (though Mo Bei felt she possessed no such damned thing), Mo Bei couldn't shake the feeling that Mo Xi's state was somewhat off.

Faintly, indefinably off.

Mo Bei couldn't help but stare a few moments longer at Mo Xi's alluring, bewitching face.

[—"...Do you want to give it a try too?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

[—"Eh?"](# "Speaker: Mo Bei")

—"I said, since you're so interested, why don't you give it a shot too?"-Mo Xi had noticed Mo Bei's gaze, but seemed to have misread her intent. He pointed at the glowing screen.—"On a grand scale, it can project what you will grow into; on a smaller scale, it can predict how much longer until you break through."

[...If not for a certain meddlesome brat, I would have broken through already.]

Mo Bei thought deadpan, refusing flatly:

—"No need. My cultivation direction is crystal clear, and my progress is well within my grasp. I certainly won't pull the kind of stunt where I rush off into someone else's territory to abruptly break through and cause Master headaches."

—"Why is it that you only become articulate when you're roasting me?"-the corner of Mo Xi's mouth twitched.—"Besides, I'm not talking about boring trifles like [score estimation](# "Literal CN: 估分 (gūfēn) — estimating exam scores after taking a test"). Think bolder: integrate your heart method and swordsmanship to compute what form you'll take once you reach Nascent Soul or Void Refinement."

Having said this, Mo Xi paused, his tone turning bizarrely sly:

—"Don't you want to see? Your future self—even if you aren't interested in that, what your future self looks like determines what your partner will be like. Especially after you just slaughtered your own [fated lover](# "Literal CN: 命运之人 (mìngyùn zhī rén) — fated lover / destined partner; romance trope")?"

Mo Bei furrowed her brows:

—"Slaughtered... Why do you insist that thing had to be my partner?"

Truth be told, she hadn't given the slightest thought to this so-called "fated lover" nonsense in a very long time.

Lately, if she wasn't practicing her sword forms, she was preparing for the Immortal Arts Assembly. She was running around in dizzying circles. On top of that, among the direct disciples, Eldest Senior Brother, Second Senior Brother... and even this [aggrieved sucker](# "Literal CN: 冤种 (yuānzhǒng) — internet slang for a pitiful chump / sucker") of a younger brother had all advanced ahead of schedule. This would undoubtedly draw even more continental scrutiny to the Immortal Arts Assembly, ratcheting up her own pressure. Forget that [half-wit](# "Literal CN: 二百五 (èrbǎiwǔ) — Chinese slang for a fool, blockhead, or half-wit") from earlier—even if a breathtakingly handsome immortal genuinely appeared and made her heart flutter, she had neither the time nor the inclination to give him the time of day.

—"I'd like to think my taste isn't that atrocious,"-Mo Bei stated.

[—"Is that so?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi wore an intricate, conflicted expression.

That look made Mo Bei's chest tighten uncomfortably.

A sudden wave of irritation washed over her; she had no desire to pursue this idiotic topic with her brother any further.

Before Mo Xi could say another word, Mo Bei cut in:

—"Rather than me, shouldn't you be testing it on yourself instead?!"

[—"Me?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"Am I wrong?"-Mo Bei sneered.—"Sure, you broke through, but among all of Heavenly Spirit Sect's direct disciples, you're the one with the least planning for your life."

[—"How so?!"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi instinctively opened his mouth to retort, only to realize... that seemed to be the exact truth.

Mo Bei was unquestionably a sword cultivator. Fifth Senior Brother did alchemy; Fourth Senior Brother did artifact refinement. Although Eldest Senior Sister was chronically unwilling, her primary discipline was still formations. As for Second Senior Brother, though his curriculum as a spell cultivator was eclectic and vast, claiming that man lacked a life plan was utter nonsense.

Only Mo Xi had no clear path. His current heart method had been specifically assigned by Master purely to shed his Extreme Yin Constitution; it was merely a transitional fix. Once he had fully attained a healthy physique, he would be free to choose whatever suited his passion. Yet when it came to his passions, for now, wasn't it just treasures and spirit stones? But becoming a merchant like Boss Tao didn't quite sit right with him either; he always felt he needed to accomplish things that would be far more useful to Master...

At this thought, Mo Xi felt a headache coming on. Throwing caution to the winds, he blurted out:

—"Wait, isn't there still Eldest Senior Brother to [anchor the rock bottom](# "Literal CN: 垫底 (diàndǐ) — being at the bottom of the rankings / cushioning the bottom")?"

—"Eldest Senior Brother? Are you certain you want to be like him?"-Mo Bei asked in genuine shock.

"..." Mo Xi choked.

Only after a long silence did he grumble dejectedly:

—"No. No matter how low I sink, I haven't reached the point of [forsaking my humanity](# "Literal CN: 不做人 (bù zuò rén) — refusing to be human / abandoning human form and decency; referencing Yuwen Xuan's beastly antics and turning into a black lamb")."

—"You're right. Perhaps I should take a look at my own future,"-Mo Xi conceded at last.

[Excellent. The fool talked himself into a corner.]

Mo Bei thought coolly.

She wanted to see what kind of shady trick lay behind the "future self" Mo Xi had wanted her to see.

—This was not doubting Master.

Since it was a task assigned by Master, Mo Bei had complete faith that there was nothing amiss with the trial itself. If something felt wrong, the problem had to lie with Mo Xi.

For instance, coaxing her to test this simulation program was deeply suspicious.

[In that case, why not let Mo Xi step inside and see for himself?]

Mo Bei reasoned.

To be frank, Mo Bei hated using her brain, but when it came to manipulating her younger brother, she was an expert through lifelong habit.

Nor did Mo Xi harbor the slightest suspicion toward Mo Bei—he simply didn't believe his blockhead elder sister possessed the cranial capacity for schemes.

Mo Xi even pleaded with Mo Bei:

—"Take over for a moment!"

—"Alright,"-Mo Bei nodded.

Arithmetic and Talismanic Runes were core foundational courses at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. While Mo Bei couldn't [code simulation programs from scratch](# "Literal CN: 徒手搓程序 (túshǒu cuō chéngxù) — hand-rolling / coding programs with bare hands") like Mo Xi, she was entirely capable of handling basic maintenance to ensure stable operation, provided she didn't touch the core codebase.

And so, after briefing Mo Bei on a few operational caveats and manually inputting his own parameters, Mo Xi vacated the operator's seat.

By now, aqueous ripples had begun spreading across the center of the "grand treasure vault front hall."

[Future Mo Xi... is it?]

Gazing at the undulating ripples, Mo Bei peered forward with unavoidable curiosity, watching as a figure emerged from within.

"Whoa~ ♥"

Mo Bei let slip a sound she would never normally make.

Stepping out from the ripples was a magnificent immortal lord with the appearance of a twenty-five or twenty-six-year-old youth, radiating the imposing aura of the Void Refinement stage.

He stood a full eight feet tall, his skeletal frame impeccably proportioned. Though slender, he was neither fragile nor delicate; lean muscle granted him a poised, heroic posture. The once-androgynous features had fully matured into striking, handsome elegance, tinged with a sharp edge. Only the round, heterochromatic cat-like eyes and the smile lingering at the corner of his lips remained largely unchanged—warm, approachable, yet carrying a faint touch of innocence, evoking the captivating aesthetic of an exquisite swindler.

Yet even those heterochromatic eyes had evolved from one gold and one brown into one gold and one crimson. The crimson eye looked as though it were condensed from primordial abyssal flame, and were it not partially veiled by loose bangs, it seemed poised to set the world ablaze.

This version of Mo Xi, while undeniably recognizable as Mo Xi, bore not the slightest resemblance to Mo Bei.

Especially when contrasted against the androgynous creature standing right beside her.

Mo Bei was immensely satisfied with this outcome.

As if sensing Mo Bei's approval, that "future Mo Xi" even turned his head to offer her a gentle smile.

He then turned to face "his present self":

[—"Care to bestow your instruction?"](# "Speaker: Future Mo Xi")

[—"Please."](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

Mo Xi was evidently thrilled with his "future self" as well, cupping his hands with sparkling eyes.

His "future self," however, showed no such courtesy toward "his present self." With a faint smile, he raised a hand to unleash an opening technique: crimson flames coalesced into the shape of a blazing dragon, pouncing straight toward Mo Xi.

Caught unawares, Mo Xi took the fiery dragon head-on, sent flying backward to slam hard against the rear wall.

—"...Do you need me to dial down the difficulty for you?"-Mo Bei asked.

—"No need,"-Mo Xi pushed against the ground, struggling to stand.

Yet "future Mo Xi" gave him no opening whatsoever. Raising his palm, the fire dragon surged toward Mo Xi once more, blasting him backward again.

This impact was brutal.

Mo Xi was completely engulfed in flames, enveloped in golden fire, emitting the smell of scorching fat—which is to say, the aroma of roasted meat.

—"Are you really sure you don't need it dialed down?"-Mo Bei asked again.

—"N-no need..."-Mo Xi gasped.

Smelling the charred roast meat and listening to Mo Xi's pained groans, Mo Bei didn't feel very optimistic:

—"Failing to beat your future self is hardly a big deal. It's not like you're obligated to master this subject anyway, are you?"

—"Shut up! Are you part of the trial too?!"-Mo Xi snapped.

This time, he didn't rush to stand, nor did he hurry to douse the flames. Instead, he strained to unfurl a barrier of fire, attempting to intercept the fire dragon plunging toward him once again.

Yet Golden Core against Void Refinement was simply too overwhelming. Regardless of one's proficiency with a technique, the chasm between cultivation realms could not be bridged. The fiery barrier was obliterated in an instant. Mo Xi felt an excruciating agony that seared all the way into his internal organs, the pain so intense he nearly wanted to give up just as Mo Bei had suggested—

[No, I can't give up!]

[If I give up now, I'll truly never win!]

The dregs of reason remaining in his skull screamed at Mo Xi.

As the programmer of this "Exhortation to Learning program," Mo Xi naturally knew the secret to passing the exam:

It was simple—just endure.

Just as Mo Bei had said, one's present self could never defeat one's idealized future self—otherwise, wouldn't studying be completely pointless?

Therefore, the core objective of this battle had never been defeating the opponent from the start; it was refusing to give up when faced with the crushing setbacks delivered by that "ideal."

No matter how excruciating, no matter how grueling, as long as one held out and demonstrated an unyielding determination to seize this "future," one could inversely obtain this skill temporarily from that "future."

[So I just have to hold out for five minutes...]

[Hiss... isn't five minutes a bit too long? Even if it was Master's syllabus requirement, repeatedly dying and reviving for five straight minutes isn't something an ordinary person—or demon—could endure, right? Maybe I should adjust it shorter?]

Mo Xi thought in agony, instinctively glancing in Mo Bei's direction.

And as he was thinking this, the fire dragon collided with him once more, burrowing straight into his scorching flesh until it felt as though his very soul were being incinerated.

[It hurts, it hurts so much... Tell Mo Bei to shorten the time...]

Mo Xi thrashed violently. Just as the words were about to burst from his mouth, he caught sight of the faint, seemingly gentle yet chillingly detached smile on the face of "his future self."

[Come to think of it... this guy could vaporize me in a single instant, couldn't he?]

A Void Refinement master incinerating a Golden Core cultivator—and one who had just broken through without even consolidating his cultivation base—was as effortless as turning a hand.

[Yet every single one of his strikes deliberately spared my life, almost as if he specifically wanted me to experience maximum agony...]

—"I-I see... As expected of 'me'..."-Mo Xi let out a tragic, breathless laugh.

No one understood oneself better than oneself.

The opponent clearly knew that far more than simple death, Mo Xi could not tolerate pain. That was why he had deliberately deployed these torture-like tactics; the flames coating his body were not ignited to incinerate Mo Xi, but burning specifically to maximize his suffering.

—"Don't even think about it!"-Mo Xi snarled.

Trembling, Mo Xi slapped the storage pouch at his waist with force. Several top-grade spirit stones flew out instantly, continuously replenishing his spiritual energy.

Although these spirit stones could not extinguish the flames, the influx of spiritual energy allowed Mo Xi to better withstand the blaze. At the very least, physically, the torment felt slightly diminished.

"Future Mo Xi," however, let out a soft sigh:

—"Is this wise? Even if you gave up, Master wouldn't actually refuse to teach you flame-manipulation arts. But those top-grade spirit stones—you handpicked those from your collection, didn't you? Consuming them in a setting where you can't earn any money in return... don't you feel it's an awful deal?"

—"T-talking so big... Isn't it just because you only have this one... attack method?"-Mo Xi fired back.

—"No. I merely pity you,"-"future Mo Xi" chuckled.

He snapped his fingers. The flames abruptly shifted to an azure hue.

With that transformation, Mo Xi's eyes widened in horror. The drain on his spiritual energy surged violently; no matter how he circulated his cultivation, he could not resist the flame's corrosion. The handful of top-grade spirit stones—wealth sufficient to fund months of inter-domain teleportation between the Southern and Central Domains—were drained dry in a split second, crumbling into grayish-white powder.

Mo Xi had no leisure to mourn the loss. He felt as though he were on the verge of melting entirely, yet death would not claim him—there was only agonizing, unrelenting pain.

[Five minutes... could it really be this long?]

[No, relying on external items won't hold. I need more, far more spiritual energy...]

Mo Xi's thoughts spun in chaos.

Across Mo Xi's slender frame, a miniature fire dragon began swimming gracefully.

This was the mine spirit bestowed upon Mo Xi by Zhou Hui. In order to counteract Mo Xi's sex-mismatched Extreme Yin Constitution, this mine spirit served as an active link to the sect's entire spirit stone vein, capable of channeling an endless torrent of spiritual energy to Mo Xi even across tens of thousands of miles spanning two continental domains.

—At the cost of draining the spirit stone mine dry.

—"Whoa, that's even more wasteful,"-"future Mo Xi" remarked at the sight, his sigh deepening.—"You might as well give those wasted spirit stones to me! Perhaps I'd grant you a quick end."

Evidently, even the "idealized self" conceived in Mo Xi's mind had not been stripped of his money-grubbing nature.

His flames latched onto the dragon-shaped mine spirit as though attempting to seize the vein itself, setting the fiery dragon ablaze.

—"Aaaagh!"-Mo Xi finally screamed in agony.

The fire dragon thrashed, and Mo Xi thrashed alongside it. He felt as though his three ethereal and six corporeal souls were burning to ash.

[No, I can't... At this rate, I truly won't be able to hold out!]

Double vision swam before Mo Xi's eyes. Biting down hard on his lip, he coughed up several plumes of violet flame.

Standing to the side, "future Mo Xi" offered a smiling, gentle exhortation:

—"Stop struggling. Wouldn't it be better to give up early..."-before blinking in sudden shock.-"...Wait, what are you doing?!"

To his astonishment, Mo Xi's spiritual aura began to plummet, and he sensed Mo Xi actively severing the conduits channeling the spiritual power.

The dragon-shaped mine spirit leaped wildly, on the verge of tearing free from the confines of Mo Xi's body.

To be precise, Mo Xi was severing his tether to the spirit stone vein.

Because Mo Xi had realized that this flame wielded by "his future self" fed on external spiritual qi to burn. If he shut out external spiritual energy and relied purely on his own golden core, he could actually hold out longer.

Yet to ensure Mo Xi had sufficient energy to counteract his Extreme Yin Constitution, Zhou Hui had personally bound the mine spirit to Mo Xi's body so he could receive replenishments from the vein anytime, anywhere. In this contest, that boon had inadvertently become the weapon utilized by "his future self."

If Mo Xi wished to alleviate this scorching agony of extreme Yang, severing his connection to the mine was the swiftest way.

Yet even "future Mo Xi" could not comprehend this move:

—"Have you lost your mind?! That is an entire mineral vein! For a worthless trial like this, is there any need to go to such lengths?!"

—"I-I've already formed my Golden Core. In th-theory, I've already refined the mine spirit, and the Extreme Yin Constitution is... no longer an obstacle,"-Mo Xi gasped between spasms of pain, a look of fierce resolve washing over his features.—"In the first place, I never should have relied on external crutches—"

As he spoke those words, Mo Xi's chaotic mind cleared with startling lucidity.

No, he had actually been lucid all along:

That was right. This trial was indeed of little consequence—merely an exercise to urge young dunces who hated studying to face their choices honestly. As an "examiner," theoretically speaking, sacrificing so many spirit stones and the mine vein gifted by Master just to secure a victory was hardly worth the price.

Yet just as failing an exam wouldn't prompt Master to withhold flame-manipulation arts, even if he severed the connection temporarily, that mineral vein wasn't going to become someone else's property!

By comparison, leaving a record of failure in the database would be infinitely worse!

[What if Master saw it? What if he saw that I gave up?]

[My resolve won't be something so fragile and meager...]

Mo Xi reasoned through the agony, enduring the blistering inferno.

Finally, the instant the fiery dragon broke free from his body, the flames that had been consuming him reshaped themselves into the form of a dragon. This meant that Mo Xi had temporarily obtained the fire spiritual energy of the Void Refinement stage. Under its empowerment, Mo Xi's demeanor took on a distinctly masculine, valiant air.

In exchange, "future Mo Xi's" demeanor turned somewhat soft and effeminate, resembling the Mo Xi of old.

Mo Xi beamed in delight, declaring to "his future self":

—"Haha! Bring it on again!"

*

—"Well then, the kid is actually having a blast. Truly worthy of being your disciple,"-Tao Zhu remarked.

Unquestionably, Mo Xi's "performance" had been watched by Zhou Hui and the others from start to finish. They had seen every step of how Mo Xi overcame "his future self."

Watching his disciple, Zhou Hui breathed a quiet sigh of relief.

A satisfied smile graced his lips:

—"This will do the child good. His golden core has begun to stabilize."

—"Isn't that the truth? He roasted for half a [shichen](# "Literal CN: 半个时辰 (bàn ge shíchen) — traditional Chinese time unit; half a shichen equals one full hour")! Even artifact forging isn't conducted like that,"-Tao Zhu muttered, the fat on his cheeks jiggling.

He was certain now—absolutely certain that every child from the Heavenly Spirit Sect harbored some form of [masochistic streak](# "Literal CN: 被虐心理 (bèinüè xīnlǐ) — masochistic complex / glutton for punishment").

Who on earth went to such horrifying lengths just to prove their resolve?!

—"Half a shichen..."-Zhou Hui furrowed his brows slightly. He turned toward the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree:

—"Venerable Sacred Tree, how are things on Bei'er's side?"

—"That child's constitution is too extraordinary; the entanglements of the past have surged toward her,"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree replied, wearing a grave expression.—"To borrow your phrasing, the computational load is rather immense."

Hearing this, Zhou Hui turned a worried gaze in that direction.

Before the glowing screen, Mo Bei—who was supposed to be guarding Mo Xi's trial—was seated cross-legged in deep meditation. Yet all around her, gorgeous clusters of Snow Glow Grass had sprouted in profusion, as if preparing to bury her from sight.

—"Should we pull that child out?"-the Fire Abyss Sacred Tree asked.

Zhou Hui studied the scene, beginning to reach a hand toward Mo Bei inside the illusion, only to halt halfway.

—"No. The child insists on facing it herself,"-Zhou Hui said, half in a wry smile and half in quiet pride.—"Honestly, every single one of them is so stubbornly willful."
