# Chapter 131: The Gamblers (第131章 赌狗们)

[← Previous Chapter](130_Chapter_130.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](132_Chapter_132.md)

---

Zhou Hui's decision was swiftly proclaimed to the world.

Not only did the Tribulation Transcendence titans publicly endorse it, but Zhou Hui himself published the announcement in *The White Jade Capital Daily*.

Front page, top headline.

Once again, *The White Jade Capital Daily* and the academic journal *Spells* fell into a state where demand vastly outstripped supply.

Subscribers multiplied several times over yet again.

To cope with the astronomical printing demands, Guan Ye had no choice but to construct a colossal gold-flecked inkstone within the Heavenly Spirit Sect. He channeled spiritual spring water to create an ink pool and fired a lifelike, peach-tree-shaped brush pot right beside it.

Tens of thousands of brush monkeys resided here.

Among them, the few little brush monkeys that had originally ground ink for Zhou Hui naturally became the chieftains of this unprecedented primate clan.

Perched like miniature tarsiers, they wore custom crystal spectacles forged specifically for them by the Artifact Refinement Hall. With immense gravity, they sat upon the sixth branch on the eastern side of the peach tree brush pot—a branch that happened to connect directly to the newly constructed Four Treasures Terrace.

Whenever the editorial staff of *The White Jade Capital Daily* and *Spells* finalized the content for a new issue, they would carry the draft layouts to the Four Treasures Terrace and respectfully present them to the brush monkey chieftain.

Upon receiving the drafts, the monkey chieftain would conduct an initial editorial review. Should there be any typos or formatting irregularities, it would screech in indignant protest, refusing to budge until the editors corrected every last error.

Once satisfied, the monkey chieftain would take the approved text and push up its crystal spectacles.

Instantly, light beamed from the crystal lenses, projecting a scaled layout directly onto the opposing cliff wall.

Simultaneously, the brush monkeys below sprang into motion.

Agile little monkeys deftly retrieved matching movable type from type cabinets and aligned them precisely within the projection's coordinates, forming a complete printing plate across the stone face. Meanwhile, other little monkeys bustled about—some hauling spiritual spring water, some grinding ink, and some stealthily gulping down mouthfuls of ink. Long rolls of paper glided across the printing plate like winding serpents, rolling off fresh newspaper sheets one after another.

—As an aside, movable type printing had actually existed in this world for ages, but it had only ever been popular in the mortal realm. Many immortal spells could only be recorded by living souls or beings endowed with spiritual intelligence. Coupled with the relatively small population of cultivators and the sheer diversity of cultivation methods, mass printing had never been a pressing necessity.

With the founding of *The White Jade Capital*, however, printing would likely take the cultivation realm by storm.

Fortunately, the core technology was already in place; all it required was the cooperation of spirits and sprites.

It was only because Zhou Hui had held classes for the little brush monkeys and enlightened their spiritual intelligence that they were able to assist in the first place.

Even so, this remained an incomplete solution. Brush monkeys had limited reproductive rates, leading the Heavenly Spirit Sect to scour the world for brush spirits, ink elementals, book sprites, and similar creatures to keep pace with the ever-expanding printing workload.

Luckily, this was merely a matter of time—and in the cultivation world, time solved most problems. After imprinting enough treatises penned by supreme titans, this "living ink" and "special movable type" were bound to attain sapience on their own.

—"However, compared to the booming sales of the *Daily* and *Spells*, the hottest seller this time is actually Immortal Chess,"-Mo Xi reported.

Ever since the day Zhou Hui returned, Mo Xi had spent at least two shichen every day exclusively reporting work progress: summarizing the high-level debates among supreme cultivators from the preceding period, the petty schemes of the Palace of Carefree Wandering, the reactions of various sects across the realm, and so forth, as if brimming with boundless passion for his duties.

The other disciples, however, saw right through him:

[This little junior brother's skin is itching for a thrashing again! He just wants to hog Master all to himself!]

Still, Mo Xi had never lacked for petty cleverness. He methodically organized key bullet points and compiled the most crucial articles, preparing enough material that reading ten reports a day would last half a month. It left his peers lamenting that if he applied even a fraction of this ingenuity to his studies and cultivation, he wouldn't be forced to cram at the last minute before every exam.

Unfortunately for his siblings, while Zhou Hui withheld comment on Mo Xi's study habits, he was exceedingly satisfied with Mo Xi's work ethic.

Intrigued by the report, Zhou Hui asked:

—"Immortal Chess is selling wildly? So quickly?"

—"As expected, was this already within Master's calculations?"-Mo Xi said, his eyes sparkling with admiration.

Zhou Hui waved a hand nonchalantly:

—"It cannot strictly be called 'expected'..."

Rather, it was an inevitable consequence.

After all, Zhou Hui had proposed the concept of an academic impact factor ($X$) for *Spells*.

Specifically, it depended upon a paper's influence and the number of cultivators citing and utilizing it.

In practice, however, one quickly realized that although the papers in *Spells* appeared lively and provoked furious academic debates, the number of low-tier cultivators who actually cited and applied them approached zero.

It wasn't that the quality of these papers was lacking; rather, it was an ecological dilemma inherent to the cultivation realm.

First and foremost, beyond the rudimentary classifications of spirit roots and cultivation realms, the cultivation world possessed virtually no unified standards.

Even realm classifications were notoriously unreliable.

For instance, Zhou Hui's divine sense at the Golden Core stage and an ordinary cultivator's divine sense at the Golden Core stage were entirely different concepts. Inquiring with cultivators of Zhou Hui's generation, such as Jian Haoge, would plunge them straight into an existential crisis.

The root cause of this disparity traced back to the cultivation world's obsession with karmic fortune.

Even disciples of the same great sect practiced vastly different mental cultivation methods depending on their personal karmic encounters. Consequently, for what appeared to be the exact same Ice Condensation Art, there might be dozens of underlying techniques driving it, each operating on completely different principles. The higher one climbed toward the peak, the more pronounced this phenomenon became. Inconsistent foundational textbooks led to fractured understanding at the upper tiers. To put it simply, there was no [standardized script or axle width](# "Literal CN: 书同文，车同轨 (shū tóng wén, chē tóng guǐ) — Emperor Qin Shi Huang's policy of unifying written characters and vehicle axle widths across China, here meaning universal academic and curricular standards")—everyone operated on disparate assumptions.

This also meant that inter-sect exchanges were largely limited to superficial spell demonstrations; exchanging core cultivation manuals was virtually impossible.

What was more, because cultivation manuals governed the allocation of resources and competitive breakthroughs, most techniques were jealously guarded secrets. Unless a cultivator hit an insurmountable bottleneck, stood on the brink of death, or prepared for impending ascension and sought to accumulate merit, they would never pen their proprietary techniques for the public eye.

Consequently, a suitable cultivation manual could fetch astronomical sums at auction, yet was also the most prone to going unsold.

Zhou Hui's academic lecture hall had already been a rare innovation.

Even so, discussions merely orbited the Immortal Arts Assembly, never straying far from traditional Dao Discourse Assemblies: deducing fragments of others' techniques from peripheral clues to glean secondary experience.

Such a system inevitably forced every cultivator to reinvent the Dao from scratch during their own journey.

It also resulted in the papers published in *Spells* having minimal real-world impact.

No matter how loudly titans argued, once the debate concluded, everyone retreated to their private research. Scarcely any disciples within their own sects even utilized the theories proposed in those papers—even within grand sects boasting thousands of disciples, only a tiny minority ever mastered a technique to their patriarch's level.

When all was calculated, the papers boasting the highest citation rates were actually those related to the Immortal Arts Assembly.

After all, the tournament arenas had been designed around the theories published in those papers. To improve their odds of advancing, contestants were forced to grit their teeth and dissect the research, naturally providing a steady stream of citations.

As for cultivators unable to travel to the Central Domain to spectate in person, their natural alternative was to purchase Immortal Chess to study the matches.

—"Master's timing was also impeccable. The regional qualifiers just concluded, and the immortals advancing to the grand finals have all been decided,"-Mo Xi praised gleefully.-"It is the perfect moment to roll out the new edition of Immortal Chess."

What Mo Xi referred to was the *Immortal Chess: 1st Immortal Arts Assembly Expansion Pack*.

This release featured exclusive chess pieces for every finalist, along with twelve specialized game boards.

For the time being, these pieces were not differentiated by cultivation realm, instead utilizing standardized base attributes. Each piece was programmed with only the contestant's three signature spells, allowing enthusiasts who couldn't attend the assembly to step into the finalists' shoes on the board and simulate the tournament's outcomes.

The expansion pack complemented the Immortal Arts Assembly perfectly.

Unsurprisingly, it met with overwhelming acclaim upon release.

Yet what truly ignited the explosion in sales was the Ascension Stairway and Zhou Hui's public notice.

The supreme powerhouses of the cultivation world quickly realized that the Immortal Arts Assembly offered an effective avenue to boost the citation rates of their papers, prompting them to purchase Immortal Chess through every available channel.

—"In fact, it is not just Immortal Chess itself. Multiple immortal cities and market fairs have proactively petitioned to franchise our Immortal Chess Guild and build dedicated Immortal Chess parlors,"-Mo Xi reported.

Shaking his head, Zhou Hui replied:

—"Their crooked wits certainly turn fast when it comes to that sort of thing."

—"Master, are you displeased?"-Mo Xi asked cautiously.

Lying nearby in a drowsy state, the little black lamb let out a cold snort.

[Little Junior Brother truly has nothing but petty cleverness—he never grasps the core point!]

[Master went through all the trouble of repairing the Ascension Stairway and declaring that access to the White Jade Capital would depend on one's impact factor in *Spells*. He obviously didn't do it just to promote a board game!]

Rather, Master sought to compel cultivators to produce genuine substance and reflect deeply on the practical dissemination and application of spellcraft.

Yet shifting an entire civilization's mindset was hardly a matter that could be resolved overnight.

Zhou Hui had never expected them to grasp his true intent immediately.

—"No, the fact that they could even think of Immortal Chess is already quite good. It shows they are actively searching for solutions,"-Zhou Hui sighed.-"At the very least, they are willing to study it. When you think about every cultivator clutching an Immortal Chess booster pack in hand, it is rather amusing."

—"Ah, it is likely far more than one,"-Mo Xi noted.

Zhou Hui turned his gaze toward his youngest disciple:

—"More than one?"

—"Yes,"-Mo Xi's eyes darted left and right, not daring to meet his master's gaze, as he admitted haltingly.-"Everyone's enthusiasm is boiling over. Many people bought over a dozen expansion packs... Everyone is desperate to pull Fifth Senior Brother and Mo Bei from their booster packs!"

—"...What?"

---

[—"Not in this booster pack either?!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator A")

[—"My dear Little Junior Sister!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator B")

[—"Who's *your* Little Junior Sister? She's the Heavenly Spirit Sect's Little Junior Sister!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator C")

[—"The Heavenly Spirit Sect is too vile! They must have deliberately reduced the drop rates of their own disciples' pieces to conceal their true strength!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator B")

[—"Who said that? There are tons of pieces for Yan Fei and Yu Cong! I've already opened over a dozen sets! If you just want to study how the Tianling Sword Art performs on the new maps, they're more than enough!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator D")

[—"Can they even begin to compare to Junior Sister Mo Bei?!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator B")

[—"Speaking of which, just how are you supposed to pull her?! I've consulted every diviner in the entire city, and not a single one was accurate!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator E")

[—"Save your breath. I heard Patriarch Jiuzhai of the Southern Domain is currently a guest at the Heavenly Spirit Sect. His sheer presence alone is enough to jam the prophetic calculations of every diviner in the world! How could anyone divine what's inside a sealed booster pack?!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator D")

[—"Could it be that Little Junior Sister isn't even in this batch of packs at all?!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator A")

[—"This is rigged!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator B")

Such fervent discussions erupted among the cultivators.

When Shu Jiawen stepped into his own sect's Yuke Xuan, this was the exact spectacle that met his eyes.

Shaking his head at the sight, Shu Jiawen remarked:

—"Utterly disgraceful. Where has the mental discipline of these juniors gone? To lose all reason over a mere chess piece or two—none of them will ever achieve greatness!"

—"It might help if the corners of your mouth weren't curled up to your ears,"-Qingyang Qingli scoffed.

Shu Jiawen hurriedly patted the corners of his mouth, but the grin across his face only widened.

Never had he felt so utterly triumphant.

Ever since Zhou Hui's heavenly tribulation, every faction seeking intelligence or eager to purchase *The White Jade Capital Daily* and *Spells* had to find reliable distribution channels in the Central Domain. The Palace of Carefree Wandering, which typically served as the primary broker, had been rendered useless for reasons known to all, and had subsequently pulled that counterfeit journal fiasco. As a result, commercial trade routes across the Central Domain were being completely redistributed.

Among them, Yuke Xuan, where Shu Jiawen was stationed, gained immense prominence as a branch of a reputable great sect from the Eastern Domain, managing to carve out a host of lucrative new business ventures.

Proxy-purchasing Immortal Chess sets was one such enterprise.

Although Shu Jiawen had come to the Central Domain of his own free will, he hated the thought of his branch being viewed by sect disciples as a place of exile. Now, with these booming businesses, his disciples would hold their heads high and strive onward even after the Immortal Arts Assembly concluded—an ideal development in Shu Jiawen's eyes.

Of course, the massive sales of Immortal Chess brought him personal joy as well.

After all, Shu Jiawen had competed in the Immortal Arts Assembly himself, and his own piece was included in this release. Moreover, he (in his own mind) had already locked in victory for the Immortal Arts Assembly, guaranteeing he would be featured in the official standard edition.

The better Immortal Chess sold, the greater his renown would spread!

Especially after hearing that Immortal Chess parlors had even opened in Sky City, he was just one step away from achieving realm-wide fame!

This brought Shu Jiawen boundless satisfaction.

—"Hmph. The champion last time was clearly me,"-Qingyang Qingli muttered indignantly, eyeing Shu Jiawen's smug expression.

—"Heheh, it is precisely *because* the 'champion' last time was you that it's now our turn to sit on the throne!"-Shu Jiawen didn't bother arguing, merely dropping a sarcastic jab.

However, it wasn't the Heavenly Spirit Sect that had barred Qingyang Qingli from competing—since that previous tournament had been a "pilot trial," participants were technically permitted to enter the Immortal Arts Assembly. Rather, it was an infuriated Shu Jiawen and their peers, along with an equally furious Patriarch Qingyang!

It was entirely Qingyang Qingli's own fault for strutting around and flaunting his championship so shamelessly that his relatives and friends had beaten him black and blue.

At the thought of this, Shu Jiawen cast a curious glance at Qingyang Qingli:

—"Speaking of which, what happened to your face? Has it still not healed from last time? I recall I didn't hit you that hard!"

—"...Don't you know how much force you put into it?"-Qingyang Qingli retorted, sporting a prominent [black eye](# "Literal CN: 熊猫眼 (xióngmāo yǎn) — panda eye / black eye").

Yes, a black eye.

Sporting a freshly punched black eye, Qingyang Qingli looked utterly ridiculous—so much so that he hadn't dared wear his signature white robes today, opting instead for drab gray attire in hopes of avoiding notice.

Standing next to Shu Jiawen in his blazing crimson robes, however, the effort was entirely wasted.

Shu Jiawen remained puzzled:

—"But I distinctly remember I punched you on the left side..."

—"Heheh,"-Qingyang Qingli sneered coldly.

Naturally, he wouldn't admit that Shu Jiawen's memory was entirely correct. Shu Jiawen had indeed struck his left eye, and for a cultivator, an injury of that caliber was trivial; Qingyang Qingli had dispelled the bruise that very evening.

His current shiner was the direct result of scrambling with his nieces and nephews to play the secret realm crafted by Qingyu, only to be caught red-handed by the girl.

Speaking of which, that girl Qingyu was truly vicious!

She had clearly noticed Qingyang Qingli sneaking in, yet said nothing. Instead, she buried a brutal trap inside the secret realm, dealing Qingyang Qingli a resounding blow that forcefully kicked him out of the instance.

[Wretched girl! You'd be better off marrying into Sky City in the Northern Domain!] Qingyang Qingli cursed inwardly. [Go marry that playboy who loiters around Carefree Palace all day—let's see if you don't cry your eyes out!]

Of course, he only dared think it.

If Qingyu genuinely intended to marry Jian Nuo, Qingyang Qingli would likely be the very first to jump out in vehement opposition.

If only for his own freedom to game in her secret realms, he would object to the death!

[Dammit, just when will Qingyu's secret realm be completed?!]

[I can't participate in the Immortal Arts Assembly, and I can't play in the secret realm—I feel like I'm going to die!]

Qingyang Qingli wailed in the depths of his soul.

Watching the kaleidoscopic shifts of emotion across Qingyang Qingli's face, Shu Jiawen warily took two steps back.

—"S-Sorry. I truly didn't think..."-Shu Jiawen genuinely believed that his playful thrashing had left a bruise lasting this long, driving his friend toward an emotional breakdown. Come to think of it, the Immortal Arts Assembly had been underway for quite some time, yet Qingyang Qingli had barely shown his face. Given his playful nature, that was utterly uncharacteristic. It seemed sporting a black eye truly wounded his vanity, especially since the fellow was notoriously obsessed with his own looks.

Reflecting on this, Shu Jiawen offered apologetically:

—"How about this: I'll give you a pack from the Immortal Chess booster boxes I ordered."

As it happened, Shu Jiawen and Qingyang Qingli had visited the Immortal Chess parlor today precisely to pick up the booster packs Shu Jiawen had ordered.

Due to staggering demand from the Eastern and Northern Domains, Shu Jiawen had placed a bulk order for several hundred packs in one go.

Seizing the opening, Qingyang Qingli shamelessly latched on:

—"Just a single pack of Immortal Chess? Aren't you being a bit too stingy?!"

—"Even if you wanted to buy one off the shelf right now, you couldn't!"-Shu Jiawen retorted dismissively.-"Immortal Chess is in an absolute frenzy right now. Everyone has to pre-order and wait two or three days to claim their stock."

—"Isn't that shortage caused entirely by [scalping proxies](# "Literal CN: 代购 (dàigòu) — proxy purchasing agents / resellers") like you?!"-Qingyang Qingli shot back.

He trotted over to the parlor's front counter to inquire, only to discover that was indeed the case.

Without standing on ceremony, Qingyang Qingli held out his palm toward Shu Jiawen:

—"Hand one over, then!"

—"Hmph."-Shu Jiawen curled his lip, deciding to forgive the fellow for the sake of that ridiculous black eye.

He even pulled out four different booster packs and asked Qingyang Qingli:

—"Which one do you want?"

—"Aren't they all the same?"-Qingyang Qingli asked, eyeing the packs suspiciously.

—"Just how long have you been cooped up inside?!"-Shu Jiawen gasped in shock.

He had no idea Qingyang Qingli had simply been holed up inside Eternal Villa, hopelessly addicted to the secret realm system, and genuinely believed the blame lay with himself.

Softening his tone, Shu Jiawen patiently explained:

—"This expansion pack is different from previous releases. Consider how vast our Central Domain is! Each regional qualifier had no shortage of exceptional cultivators. Not only the victors, but even defeated contestants have substantial fanbases. Presumably wishing to avoid playing favorites, the Heavenly Spirit Sect designed this expansion pack to feature not just the grand finalists, but also the most popular competitors from every regional division."

—"However, a single booster pack contains only five sets, totaling twenty-five chess pieces. Naturally, they couldn't squeeze every contestant into one pack. Thus, they categorized them by role and distributed standout competitors from each region into different packs according to [karmic drop rates](# "Literal CN: 机缘 (jīyuán) — karmic affinity; here used as gacha drop RNG"). To ensure fairness, every booster pack guarantees one set of grand-finalist pieces—though precisely which one you pull is left entirely to fate."

—"I see,"-Qingyang Qingli recalled the heated debates among cultivators when they first entered the parlor, suddenly catching on.-"Doesn't that mean pulling the contestant you actually want depends entirely on luck?"

—"Precisely,"-Shu Jiawen nodded.

He nudged the four packs forward:

—"However, pieces from different regions are distinguished by the packaging color. You can buy according to the contestants from whichever division catches your eye."

—"But I haven't watched the Immortal Arts Assembly, so I have no clue who's strong,"-Qingyang Qingli complained.-"Why don't you introduce the stronger contestants to me?"

—"Honestly, it might be better if you don't know! Even if you know who's good, you aren't guaranteed to pull them anyway. You're better off picking a pack at random and enjoying the surprise,"-Shu Jiawen advised.

Yet he was only saying so in passing; Shu Jiawen was clearly all too eager to dissect the current state of the Immortal Arts Assembly with his friend:

—"Judging by regional qualifiers, the Northwest's Cloud City naturally boasts plenty of powerhouses. Even Vice City Lord Lu Mingyao entered the fray, not to mention rising stars like Lu Zhen. Buying the Northwest pack is guaranteed solid value."

—"In the Southwest, you have factions like the Wushuang Alliance, with plenty of formidable cultivators. Oh, right—a massive dark horse emerged this time! The Blood Demon Hall went on an absolute killing spree in the Southwest. What's more, their ranks are mostly female cultivators... Heheh, you catch my drift."

—"As for the Northeast, your Eternal Villa serves as the local stronghold. Furthermore, Xia Bing and Rong Rong of the Thousand Beasts Alliance performed exceptionally well—and they're striking beauties, making them immensely popular."

—"Then there's the Southeast, home to Fengqing City's teleportation array connecting to the Southern Domain. Consequently, there are quite a few demon cultivators there. If you're looking for fun [tech cards](# "Literal CN: 对策卡 (duìcè kǎ) — situational counter cards in TCG/board games"), picking that region's pack is well worth it."

Qingyang Qingli's head spun listening to the breakdown:

—"Your introduction isn't much better than no introduction at all... Hmm,"-he stared at the four booster packs for a moment, deciding to shift angles.-"Which region's pack sells the best?"

Generally speaking, the best-selling pack corresponded to the region universally recognized as the strongest!

Shu Jiawen, however, wore a rather bizarre smile:

—"The Southeast."

—"Southeast... Why?"-Qingyang Qingli sensed something amiss the moment he heard it.-"There aren't many powerhouses around Fengqing City, are there?"

Even if there were formidable individuals among the demon race, a divide still separated them from humans. The cultivation realm had not yet popularized [beastkin enthusiasts](# "Literal CN: XX控 (XX kòng) — censored slang for monster-girl / beastkin fetishes"), so cultivators wouldn't necessarily support them.

—"Strong contenders are few, but alas, the [gacha addicts](# "Literal CN: 赌狗 (dǔgǒu) — gambling dogs; slang for gacha addicts / pack-ripping gamblers") are legion!"-Shu Jiawen laughed.-"Who told the Untiring Immortal Venerable to dispatch his sect's strongest sword cultivator to the Southeast? Everyone has gone utterly mad trying to pull 'Little Junior Sister'!"

—"Untiring Immortal Venerable? Strongest sword cultivator?"-To Qingyang Qingli, it sounded like an alien tongue.

—"You don't even know that? Tsk, tsk, you've missed out on so much excitement!"-Shu Jiawen marveled.-"In this Immortal Arts Assembly, the faction stealing all the limelight is the Heavenly Spirit Sect. As expected of disciples under the Untiring Immortal Venerable, even their ordinary disciples are terrifyingly competent, turning the Northeast division into a bloodbath. But the most formidable of all are the Untiring Immortal Venerable's two direct disciples—"

Shu Jiawen proceeded to detail Mo Bei and Lu Shulong's performances in the tournament.

—"Of course, Lu Shulong is exceptionally strong! Plenty of people buy packs just for his pieces, ranking the Northeast division's sales second overall. But when all is said and done, he is a pill cultivator—a support. Junior Sister Mo Bei, on the other hand, is a sword cultivator... How do they put it in Immortal Chess terminology again? She's the [main DPS](# "Literal CN: 主输出 (zhǔ shūchū) — primary damage carry / main DPS in gaming")! What's more, her innate skill completely ignores realm suppression, making her viable on literally any map, which only makes her all the more coveted—"

At this point, Shu Jiawen saw Qingyang Qingli's hand reach unerringly toward the Southeast division's booster pack.

—"Hey, hey! Don't tell me you're going to gamble too?!"-Shu Jiawen cried out.-"There are no strong pieces in the Southeast except Mo Bei!"

—"If I'm going to play chess, I naturally want the strongest piece!"

—"As if you can just pull her on command! This pack might not even—*AAAAAAAHHH?!*"

Shu Jiawen let out a shriek that instantly caught the attention of everyone in the parlor.

Heads snapped around, and unified shouts erupted across the room:

[—"It dropped!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator A")

[—"Someone hit the jackpot!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator B")

[—"That fellow Daoist looks unfamiliar. He rarely comes here, right?"](# "Speaker: Cultivator C")

[—"He doesn't just rarely come! I caught a snippet just now—I accidentally overheard Brother Jiawen explaining the Immortal Arts Assembly to him! That means it's his very first time here—a complete rookie!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator D")

[—"Heavens! Pulling Little Junior Sister on his very first buy!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator E")

[—"I—I want to pre-order another bundle of booster packs!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator F")

[—"Me too!"](# "Speaker: Cultivator G")

Following the chorus of gasps, another frenzied buying spree swept through the hall.

Qingyang Qingli watched in stunned disbelief:

—"Aren't they being far too insane?!"

—"You only say that because you [pulled the jackpot in a single roll](# "Literal CN: 一把就出了 (yī bǎ jiù chū le) — hit the rate-up jackpot on the very first pull / beginner's luck")!"-Shu Jiawen ground his teeth, glaring with red-eyed jealousy at the chess piece standing before Qingyang Qingli.

One had to know that Shu Jiawen had ripped booster packs until he had collected virtually every piece in the entire Central Domain before finally pulling Mo Bei's piece.

Shu Jiawen sighed:

—"And it isn't just the Central Domain—the other domains are even crazier!"
