# Chapter 217: The Malicious and Gluttonous Nature of the Taotie

Yuwen Xuan, that [goddamn bastard](# "Literal CN: XX / censored expletive")!

The Heart Demon cursed in the deepest recesses of her soul.

While ordinary mortals witnessed only a tedious, stilted duel between Yan Bailang and Yuwen Xuan, in a dimension imperceptible to mortal eyes, an incomparably arduous—and far more "spectacular"—clash was taking place.

At the very least, visually speaking, it was infinitely more "splendid."

First of all, the arena was fundamentally different.

Unlike the preliminary rounds, the finals of the Immortal Arts Assembly featured no messy pre-rendered battlegrounds. Instead, competitors utilized "Scenario Cards" mid-combat to switch the terrain to whatever environment granted them a tactical advantage, enhancing both the dynamism and spectacle of the match.

Regrettably, Yan Bailang possessed zero tactical awareness of this feature. Having never bothered to study the foundational tournament rulebook, he naturally remained ignorant of "Scenario Cards," remaining completely rooted to the spot while commanding his green-faced flood dragon to brawl like a stationary turret.

Conversely, on the opposing side, Yuwen Xuan—despite fighting on home turf and possessing intimate familiarity with Scenario Cards—had zero intention of invoking a battlefield modifier either. Floating motionlessly at the opposite end of the ring, he flicked talismans and activated defensive artifacts, matching his opponent's stationary turret stance with equal stubbornness.

Thus, what ought to have been a breathtaking exhibition duel was reduced by these two into the most dreadfully boring turn-based grind, with the arena remaining an empty circular slab of plain white marble from beginning to end—an absolute zenith of dullness.

Yet the moment one shifted to a demonic perspective, the entire battlefield was turned completely upside down.

The physical arena was still the same platform, but the entire fabric of space—from the atmosphere to the flagstones—was stamped with pitch-black and blood-crimson sigils, hanging densely across the grounds like heavy, fully bloomed clusters of poisonous datura blossoms.

Had any grandmaster with experience plumbing the deepest trenches of the Abyssal Ocean witnessed this spectacle, they would have been struck with utter terror, immediately demanding the total quarantine of the sector.

For these chilling imprints were the indelible scars left behind exclusively by massive, catastrophic demonic corruption following a fiendish invasion.

Even amidst the Primordial God-Demon Battlefield within the Abyssal Ocean, such marks could only be encountered in the innermost dead zones. They were lethal beyond comprehension; brushing against the faintest wisp was sufficient to shatter one's mental clarity and induce irreversible demonic possession. They were virtually impossible to purge, persisting unabated after ten thousand years—the prime culprit behind the chaotic spiritual energy plaguing the modern Middle Realm.

Yet at this moment, these apocalyptic demonic imprints were laid out in the realm's most spiritually abundant sanctuary, utterly invisible to all.

No, not entirely invisible.

Yuwen Xuan appeared to be hovering motionlessly mid-air, but if one could perceive these demonic scars, they would realize he wasn't stationary at all. Rather, he was shifting with exquisite precision to evade every single imprint, his exaggerated caution giving the visual illusion of remaining frozen in place.

After all, in his eyes, these demonic imprints were nothing more than rancid scraps and leftovers preserved across an entire cosmic reincarnation cycle. Even for a Taotie—especially an aristocratic Taotie who had always enjoyed high-grade gourmet dining—he held zero desire to consume expired garbage that would upset his stomach!

Besides, if he gave himself food poisoning, Master would give him an earful.

By contrast, his adversary was entirely oblivious. While Yan Bailang was also standing rooted to the spot, he had parked himself directly in the thickest cluster of demonic imprints! His robes and flesh were plastered with overlapping dark sigils, while his green-faced flood dragon, writhing and lunging across the ring, was in an even more pathetic state: thick demonic corruption wrapped around its azure scales layer upon layer, resembling a bloated green caterpillar tightly cocooned in a spider's web, helplessly awaiting the arrival of the apex predator.

Worse still, Yan Bailang was already halfway plunged into demonic possession.

Demonic imprints of this caliber could corrupt even a fully armored, high-tier cultivator upon contact, let alone Yan Bailang, whose psyche was already half-corrupted. His spiritual energy was furiously transmuting into black demonic miasma, and the fractures across his mental defenses had physically manifested into miniature demonic black holes, spewing out plumes of suffocating miasma that began billowing outward.

In this grotesque state, Yan Bailang was poised not merely to fall into demonic madness, but to collapse at any second into a living gateway to the Demon Realm.

The one holding the final line of defense amidst this crisis was, ironically, Yan Bailang's own Heart Demon.

Watching the miasma billow out of control, she frantically pulled a beast flute from her sleeve, attempting to suppress the eruption.

This bone flute was a relic wielded by "Yu Si" during her lifetime, bearing zero draconic heritage. According to Yan Bailang's memories, it had been refined from the carcass of the very first monster beast Yu Si had hunted and tamed in her youth, of exceedingly mediocre craftsmanship.

However, an inner demon's weapons were conjured from memory and manifested from condensed demonic energy. Material grade was irrelevant; it served merely as an operational medium to facilitate the demon's manipulation of mental states and miasmic currents.

The Heart Demon blew the beast flute, striving on one hand to suppress Yan Bailang's murderous malice and choke off the production of demonic miasma;

On the other hand, she manipulated the escaping miasma, shaping the dark mist into crimson swallowtail butterflies and guiding them with her melody. She prevented them from scattering into the spectator stands, instead directing them over the poisonous datura imprints to swarm toward herself, cloaking her form in a shimmering gown of living red wings.

Truth be told, absorbing this demonic energy dramatically amplified the Heart Demon's strength.

Her spiritual form began to mutate.

She shifted from the youthful maiden whom Yan Bailang remembered clearing the Beast Soul Tower into the mature woman he dreaded recalling—the senior sister he had murdered with his own hands. Across the areas where fatal wounds had been inflicted—her face, her neck, her arms—intricate demonic markings crystallized into existence. Her clear, bright eyes flushed into deep crimson, and two jagged demonic horns sprouted from her brow.

Yet even as her demonic power swelled, the savage bloodlust and predatory instincts innate to fiends began eroding her rational thought—strange, did a heart demon even possess rationality to begin with?

*"Drag him into the abyss! Let him become a demon! He doesn't deserve to be human!"*

*"Ahhh, it hurts, being butchered by his hands hurt so terribly! He must taste the exact same agony!"*

*"Look at him, even he knows he is no longer human!"*

*"Kill him! Tear him apart!"*

*"Indeed, what are you hesitating for?"*

That final sentence, however, snapped the Heart Demon instantly back to clarity.

Because that voice had not originated from within her consciousness, but from the outside world.

Steadying her focus, the Heart Demon discovered that besides the Yuwen Xuan brawling in the ring, another Yuwen Xuan was seated directly opposite her, watching her with the amused curiosity one might display toward an entertaining plaything.

Yuwen Xuan chuckled:

—"Am I seeing things? You are actually protecting him?"

—"I am not protecting him! I am protecting the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall!"-the Heart Demon hissed in vehement denial.

She couldn't care less if Yan Bailang dropped dead in the very next second, but it could not happen beneath the gaze of the entire world.

Only now did the Heart Demon realize that beyond her burning desire for vengeance, an obstinate obsession to "safeguard the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall" lingered in her heart:

—"If this piece of human scum falls into demonic corruption in a place like this, the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall is completely finished. Every cultivator in the realm will assume our sect is a nest of fiends—and what's truly fatal is that our sect genuinely cannot withstand an investigation,"-especially that Beast Soul Tower.

Reaching this point, the Heart Demon glared suspiciously at Yuwen Xuan:

—"That isn't your true objective, is it? But turning the arena—your own sect's home ground—into such a cursed abomination, have you lost your mind?!"

—"I didn't manufacture this; it was already here,"-Yuwen Xuan explained indolently.—"In a past reincarnation cycle, the entire Middle Realm was breached and slaughtered. It was only through our Master's boundless might, reversing time and rewriting spatial fabric, that everything appears as though it never happened. Yet demonic imprints are not so easily erased; residual fragments remained trapped in the seams of time and space. I merely employed specialized techniques to force them to manifest into reality."

Lacking the requisite background lore, the Heart Demon could make neither head nor tail of this cosmic exposition.

Yuwen Xuan finally offered something she could comprehend:

—"So you needn't worry. These faint residual imprints cannot inflict harm upon ordinary spectators."

—"Then that scum..."

—"He is an exception. He fell into demonic corruption in that prior cycle as well, meaning he already carries original demonic imprints in his soul. Now, his situation is far worse. One could say that with a mere nudge from you, he would instantly transform into an authentic demon,"-Yuwen Xuan glanced casually at Yan Bailang before returning his gaze to the Heart Demon:—"And you can rest easy. Precisely because this is our Heavenly Spirit Sect's territory, the moment he mutates, I can instantly devour him clean, obliterate all physical evidence, and dispose of the kitchen scraps! I will publicly declare that his demonic corruption was strictly Yan Bailang's personal misdeed, bearing zero connection to the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall."

—"..."

The Heart Demon did not move a muscle.

She didn't even understand why she was hesitating.

Naturally, she didn't want to forgo her revenge. Whether driven by fiendish instinct or sheer revulsion toward Yan Bailang's atrocities, she burned with the desire to end his life.

Yet simultaneously, a subtle intuition stayed her hand.

She felt that slaughtering Yan Bailang or triggering his demonic fall would not bring closure; rather, something far more sinister would unfold, and she had zero desire to see it transpire... Then again, it was entirely possible that she was simply terrified of dying.

Once Yan Bailang perished, she would undoubtedly be devoured as well, wouldn't she?

The Heart Demon hardly viewed ending up in someone's stomach as a "reward."

[...Wait a minute, which of us is the actual Heart Demon here?!]

Why was she, an inner demon, struggling bitterly to restrain herself from meaningless slaughter, while an ostensible "human" was relentlessly goading her to corrupt someone into a fiend? Rumor had it that Immortal Master Fujuan of the Heavenly Spirit Sect was an upright, peerless grandmaster; did he have any inkling that his Eldest Disciple spent his days [coercing decent souls into ruin](# "Literal CN: forcing good women into prostitution / goading righteous folks into depravity")?

Of course, the Heart Demon dared not voice such thoughts aloud. Instinct warned her that if she dared utter a single disparaging word regarding Immortal Master Fujuan, her fate would be infinitely more agonizing than simply being eaten.

She scrambled for an excuse to deflect:

—"...Hearing you say that only worries me more! He is exceptionally attuned to demonic energy. If he surrenders his humanity, the power he gains upon turning will be catastrophic, and he will recover his faculties far too quickly. When that happens, who devours whom becomes an open question!"

This was a legitimate concern for an "extrinsic" heart demon. Such demons did not view themselves as an extension of the host, but rather as the host's sworn nemesis; she naturally refused to let a monster like Yan Bailang retain his ego after falling into demonic madness.

—"Once he turns, does it matter in the slightest which of you controls the flesh?"-Yuwen Xuan asked, flashing two rows of gleaming, razor-sharp fangs.—"The moment he loses his human body and mutates into a fiend, his soul will suffer an apocalyptic shock, completely demonized. In that singular instant, the host inevitably loses all human reason, while a coherent demonic ego has yet to awaken—he will be nothing more than a mindless slab of meat. I will seize that exact window to gulp you both down in one bite! You don't have to worry about a thing."

[..."I" would rather continue worrying, thank you very much!]

The Heart Demon thought in horror.

She genuinely had zero desire to become a snack!

Fortunately, Yuwen Xuan scrutinized her with a frown of mild distaste:

—"Then again, you do have a point. You think far too much, already exceeding the behavioral parameters of a standard heart demon. Even if he turns into a mindless slab of meat, eating a demon like you would somewhat violate my dining principles."

[You actually possess principles?!]

The Heart Demon glared at Yuwen Xuan, only to see an object hurtling through the air toward her face.

Subconsciously reaching out, she caught it in her hands. It was a peach tree branch, adorned with a crude bow tied from several strands of human hair.

—"This is a 'Possession Effigy,'"-Yuwen Xuan announced.

The Heart Demon examined it thoroughly, completely unable to discern anything resembling an "effigy."

—"It isn't an edible ingredient, so I didn't bother carving it meticulously. As long as it functions, that's enough,"-Yuwen Xuan remarked flippantly.—"However, I personally snapped it from the very highest branch of our mountain gate peach tree. It is extraordinarily precious, you know!"

She couldn't detect a shred of preciousness.

And where did the hair tied around it come from?

It couldn't possibly be your own, could it?!

The Heart Demon asked tremulously:

—"What... what is the function of this effigy?"

—"It can provide you with a temporary physical vessel. If your hands are swift enough, in that exact split-second when his soul shatters upon demonic transformation, you can extract his core consciousness into the doll—just like a sea cucumber spitting out its own entrails,"-Yuwen Xuan clapped his hands in delight.—"Brilliant, isn't it? That way, you can enact your personal vengeance, while I obtain a freshly cleaned, pristine ingredient without being forced to ingest the bizarre garbage Master forbids me from eating. A win-win for everyone!"

[So to you, a person's "self-awareness" is considered bizarre garbage?!]

[And why on earth should I enthusiastically gut and clean myself just to make it easier for you to eat me?!]

The Heart Demon's mind screamed with inward complaints as she gazed upon the wooden stick with complicated emotions.

Undeniably, this crude effigy could not sustain a soul for long. Even if she possessed it, it would only last a fleeting moment, leaving her ultimate fate unchanged—death was the only conclusion.

Her choices were merely between being devoured by Yuwen Xuan, or briefly inhabiting a peach twig alongside her sworn enemy's consciousness before both of their souls dispersed into nothingness.

She didn't particularly fancy either option.

Yet this entity before her—who acted infinitely more like a demon than any true demon—had firmly resolved not to let her off the hook today. He had practically laid out the pots, pans, and seasonings right beside the ring, patiently waiting for the meal to cook.

Yuwen Xuan clapped his hands impatiently:

—"Alright, problem solved! Hurry up and make your move! It would be best if I could enjoy my meal before this match concludes."

—"..."

The Heart Demon offered no reply.

Resolving to die was never an easy task, even for an inner demon.

—"Speaking of which, that bastard is dragging his feet as well,"-Yuwen Xuan remarked, completely indifferent to the psychological welfare of the inedible portion of his meal. Turning toward the edible yet uneviscerated portion in the arena, he grumbled:—"I assumed he would want to establish his dominance by attacking me with everything he had the moment the duel began! To think I painstakingly laid this trap for nothing. Once his spiritual energy ran dry, even without your intervention, I could have made him tumble into demonic form in an instant."

And a thoroughly marinated demon at that.

—"...It is impossible. He will never exert his full strength,"-the Heart Demon murmured softly.

Yuwen Xuan cast a puzzled glance at her:

—"I thought he was ambitious?"

—"Ambition does not surpass his desire for self-preservation,"-the Heart Demon naturally understood Yan Bailang's psychological trauma.—"Back then, he struck me... his senior sister down precisely when her spiritual reserves were completely exhausted. Consequently, he lives in mortal terror of suffering the identical fate. Ever since that day, whenever he engages in battle, he invariably holds back thirty percent of his power."

—"Hmph, even against an opponent like me, who stands an entire realm below him?"

—"A personal disciple of Immortal Master Fujuan—his Eldest Disciple, no less—who would dare treat you as a mere Void Refinement junior?"-the Heart Demon countered.

She felt that the cultivation world's ingrained wariness toward the Heavenly Spirit Sect was thoroughly justified. Whatever Immortal Master Fujuan's true strength might be, even as a newly born heart demon, she could perceive that Yuwen Xuan's combat prowess was absurdly beyond normal bounds.

What kind of ordinary Void Refinement cultivator could manifest ancient demonic imprints to construct a lethal trap?!

You are this monstrously capable, does your Master have any idea?!

The Heart Demon now believed that Yan Bailang's paranoia was far from sufficient; if he truly wished to preserve his life, he should have forfeited the Immortal Arts Assembly altogether.

The Heart Demon continued:

—"However, Yan Bailang genuinely did not want to waste this tournament bout. Prior to the match, he made exceptionally thorough preparations—measures tailored as though he were preparing to face a powerhouse an entire realm *above* himself. He even swallowed a soul fragment of the ferocious beast from the Beast Soul Tower that he had hoarded for centuries—the very fragment 'Yu Si' bestowed upon him when they conquered the tower together. Yet for reasons unknown, the moment he stepped into the ring, he couldn't unleash an ounce of that power."

—"A ferocious beast's soul fragment? He swallowed that kind of trash?"-Yuwen Xuan raised an eyebrow, bursting into a mocking grin:—"Then failing to unleash it is entirely natural! Right now, every drop of blood in his veins should be screaming at him to flee from me as far as possible!"

After all, the ferocious beast within the Beast Soul Tower—the one infused with a drop of True Immortal blood—had been eaten by Yuwen Xuan.

Devoured down to the very last bone.

When the remnants of that beast's soul encountered Yuwen Xuan, running for their lives was their only instinct; how could they muster the courage to fight him?

Recalling that nine-headed bird—specifically, that drop of True Immortal blood Master had confiscated and subsequently gifted to that bastard second brother—Yuwen Xuan couldn't resist licking his lips.

Never mind that he had already consumed ancient deities and an entire True Immortal!

The most delectable delicacy was always the one he hadn't gotten to eat yet, wasn't it?!

Even if it was merely a single drop!

Yuwen Xuan lamented inwardly.

Witnessing the expression on Yuwen Xuan's face, the Heart Demon stumbled backward several paces in sheer terror.

What was truly disastrous was that perhaps because Yuwen Xuan's longing was too intense, his avatar brawling with Yan Bailang in the arena mirrored that exact same predatory expression.

Instantly, both Yan Bailang and his green-faced flood dragon froze solid.

From the deepest recesses of their instinct, they sensed an absolute, crushing suppression radiating from the apex of the cosmic food chain.

During that fleeting second of paralysis, the demonic imprints that had already coiled tightly around them sank deeper into their spiritual meridians, initiating an all-out erosion of their souls.

At this rate, even without the Heart Demon's intervention, Yan Bailang would plunge into demonic madness on his own.

[No!]

*SCREEECH—!*

In a blind panic, the Heart Demon blew frantically into her beast flute. Under extreme duress, the melody emerged so shrill and piercing that it resembled a screeching whistle.

Only after the sound echoed did the Heart Demon realize what she had done.

—"You genuinely intend to save him?!"-Yuwen Xuan's voice turned chillingly sinister, resembling a massive predator whose food bowl had just been violently kicked over.

—"N-No, I..."-the Heart Demon stammered incoherently. Her action had been purely reflexive, devoid of conscious thought.

Regrettably, Yuwen Xuan possessed zero concept of [cherishing the fair sex](# "Literal CN: 怜香惜玉 (liánxiāng-xīyù) — showing tenderness and mercy toward beautiful women"), particularly when the entity before him was not a fair maiden, but an inner demon.

With a flick of his finger, the Heart Demon felt an irresistible force yank violently at her throat, sending her tumbling helplessly to the floor at Yuwen Xuan's feet.

Yuwen Xuan bent down, clutching a fistful of the Heart Demon's bangs:

—"To be frank, whether in the past or the present, I have never understood why you constantly let him off the hook. A piece of human garbage like that lacks even the most fundamental capacity for remorse. Even without external interference, he is bound to fall into demonic madness—believe it or not, before this day ends, he will collapse into a full-fledged demonic abomination! And a remarkably troublesome one at that. When that occurs, a useless heart demon like you, who can't even perform her own duties, will become nothing more than his fertilizer."

—"...I... I genuinely didn't mean to..."-the Heart Demon gasped, struggling against his grip.

Indeed, she knew better than anyone how utterly irredeemable Yan Bailang was; killing him warranted not a shred of pity.

Yet she felt with equal certainty that now was not the ideal moment to strike him down. Unfinished business remained waiting for her to fulfill; therefore, she refused to throw her existence away merely to satisfy Yuwen Xuan's gluttony.

This enigmatic intuition was something the Heart Demon found impossible to articulate.

Yuwen Xuan gave her no opportunity to explain:

—"However, if he mutates in some remote corner or mutilates someone, our Heavenly Spirit Sect will be dragged into an administrative nightmare! If you continue to obstruct my meal, my only choice is to slaughter you right now!"

As he spoke, he wrenched her bangs with increasing brutality.

—"Ah!"-the Heart Demon cried out in pain.

Fortunately, a wave of loud boos echoing from the spectator stands abruptly broke the standoff:

[—"What kind of garbage is this?!"](# "Speaker: Furious Spectator")

[—"This clown calls himself a Body Integration grandmaster?!"](# "Speaker: Outraged Cultivator")

[—"Fuck your ancestors, refund our spirit stones!"](# "Literal CN: 艹你爹，退灵石！ — furious tournament spectators screaming profanities and demanding refunds for spirit stones spent on tickets")

Those enraged jeers finally injected life into the arena that had remained dead and dreary from the start. Yuwen Xuan released the Heart Demon, turning his head to glance at his avatar in the ring:

—"? He forfeited?"

Smoothing down her hairline, which felt on the verge of being ripped bald, the Heart Demon looked toward the stage in equal astonishment.

She discovered that Yan Bailang had indeed conceded the match.

Ignoring the torrential rain of curses from the crowd, wrapped in spiderweb-like demonic imprints, Yan Bailang staggered off the web-spun deathtrap of an arena, fleeing directly toward the athlete lounge.

[I am finished.] The Heart Demon's immediate instinct was to scurry several paces away from Yuwen Xuan.

She had zero desire to discover how Yuwen Xuan reacted upon watching a meal that was already inside his food bowl sprint away on two legs.

Yet in the very next instant, through her empathetic resonance with Yan Bailang—an innate talent of heart demons, allowing them to perceive their host's negative emotions more acutely than anyone else—she realized that Yan Bailang's emotional tempest was entirely unlike that of an athlete who had suffered defeat.

Of course, the emotions were wholly toxic:

Terror!

Fury!!

Murderous intent!!!

Yet this boiling malice was not directed toward Yuwen Xuan's avatar in the arena, but focused entirely on the private lounge! The Heart Demon recalled that several elders of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, alongside that young maiden named Xia Bing, were waiting inside that room...

[Ah, he misunderstood!]

The Heart Demon realized in hindsight, touching her beast flute.

The shrill, discordant note she had blown in her panic to halt the demonic energy had succeeded in stabilizing the miasma, but having neglected Yan Bailang's mental state, the sound had reached his ears.

That sound was far too piercing, resembling the screech of a whistle. A seasoned beast tamer might have discerned the subtle acoustic difference, but Yan Bailang's craft had decayed for centuries, and plagued by intense paranoia, the sound instantly triggered his darkest, most shameful memory.

He had naturally projected the assassination plot onto Xia Bing and the elders!

Inevitably, he had chosen to strike preemptively:

—"I knew it! You! All of you want to murder me!"

—"S-Sect Master, you have a guilty conscience! Was it not you and the elders who conspired together to murder the previous Sect Master first?!"-Xia Bing fired back.

Watching the scene unfold on the broadcast, Yuwen Xuan let out a scornful scoff:

—"Look at that. As a fellow disciple of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall, she has far more backbone than you!"

The Heart Demon had no idea whom Yuwen Xuan was referring to with "you."

Was he addressing "Yu Si," or the Heart Demon?

If the former, Yuwen Xuan had explicitly stated that she was not Yu Si, but a heart demon; if the latter, the Heart Demon was not a disciple of the Ten Thousand Beasts Hall.

Or perhaps, even Yuwen Xuan occasionally misspoke.

Naturally, that was the least important detail. The Heart Demon wholeheartedly agreed with Yuwen Xuan's verdict:

Xia Bing possessed far more backbone than she ever had.
