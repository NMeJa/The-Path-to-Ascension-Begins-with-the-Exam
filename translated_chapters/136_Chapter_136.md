# Chapter 136: The Challenge (第136章 挑战)

[← Previous Chapter](135_Chapter_135.md) | [Table of Contents](INDEX.md) | [Next Chapter →](137_Chapter_137.md)

---

—"Qingyu, I feel like we're even more of a [match made in heaven](# "Literal CN: 天造地设的一对 (tiānzào-dìshè de yī duì) — a pair crafted by Heaven and fashioned by Earth; destined soulmates") now!"-Jian Nuo declared.

[—"Because I took the Untiring Immortal Venerable as my master?"](# "Speaker: Qingyang Qingyu")

[—"Precisely."](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo")

—"I really appreciate how exceptionally 'frank' you are,"-Qingyang Qingyu replied with a beaming smile.

Throughout it all, Qingyang Qingyu had never quite understood why everyone in Eternal Villa—from top to bottom—unanimously opposed her prospective engagement to Jian Nuo, acting as though she were some utterly naive village girl who could be duped and swept away by a few sweet words from a man.

Granted, there certainly existed people in this world whose brains turned into instant mush the moment they encountered the opposite sex, and Qingyu readily conceded that she was indeed a sheltered young lady lacking worldly experience and ignorant of the cold, fickle ways of the mortal world. Even so, could a young woman who calmly tallied up her pursuer's resources and weighed whether he would be advantageous to her personal cultivation ever be described as "cute" in any sense of the word?

In Qingyu's eyes, if things were truly as her venerable ancestor feared—that she had fallen madly in love with Jian Nuo and insisted on marrying him for passionate romance—*that* would be the true disaster.

Naturally, Qingyu made no effort to conceal her [transactional calculation](# "Literal CN: 市侩 (shìkuài) — town merchant / petty broker; someone driven purely by mercenary pragmatism and profit") in front of Jian Nuo.

What satisfied Qingyu immensely was that Jian Nuo didn't care about this in the slightest. From that perspective, Jian Nuo was the genuine [lovestruck romantic](# "Literal CN: 恋爱脑 (liàn'àinǎo) — 'love brain'; popular modern slang for someone blinded by romance who prioritizes infatuation over logic"). Still, so long as it didn't impede her cultivation, she didn't mind his sentimentality. Furthermore, he always expressed his desires with absolute clarity, never indulged in baffling or eccentric antics, and occasionally proved rather entertaining. Only for those reasons was Qingyu willing to give this marriage serious consideration.

Take this incident, for example.

Truth be told, Qingyu had initially felt somewhat displeased by Jian Nuo's bizarre decision to dash all the way to the Central Domain out of nowhere.

She had believed that keeping "romance" from interfering with their respective lives and cultivation was a shared, unspoken tacit understanding between them. This sudden stunt had clearly crossed that boundary.

Yet when it turned out that Jian Nuo had actually come because of the Untiring Immortal Venerable, Qingyang Qingyu breathed a sigh of relief instead.

If this were a romance dating sim, a little floating prompt above Qingyu's beating heart would have undoubtedly flashed: *Favorability +15*.

Jian Nuo was indeed "frank," never missing an opportunity to [push his luck](# "Literal CN: 顺着杆子往上爬 (shùnzhe gānzi wǎng shàng pá) — climbing up the pole; seizing every inch given to advance further"). He said with sheer delight:

—"You like that about me? Splendid! Lady Qingyu, why don't we just get engaged... right now?"-Jian Nuo said.

As he spoke, however, his voice grew progressively quieter.

After all, the immense pressure of a Mahayana powerhouse glaring daggers into the back of his neck was simply too terrifying. Even though Jian Nuo was well-accustomed to bickering with a Tribulation Transcendence titan like Jian Haoge, he was utterly incapable of withstanding spiritual pressure of this magnitude.

He realized that if he indulged in his customary [smooth-talking cheekiness](# "Literal CN: 口花花 (kǒuhuāhuā) — glib, flirty banter; teasing sweet talk")—or rather, "candidly expressed his true feelings"—he might genuinely be beaten to death on the spot.

Fortunately, his Lady Qingyu, benevolent as a true celestial maiden, gracefully skipped over the topic.

—"I never knew before that you liked the Untiring Immortal Venerable so much,"-Qingyu said with a light laugh.

—"Revere! It is *reverence*! Using 'like' is somewhat disrespectful!"-Jian Nuo explained earnestly.-"Though in truth, I have idolized the Untiring Immortal Venerable ever since I was a boy. In fact, my daily schedule back in Sky City was deliberately modeled after the venerable Immortal Venerable."

—"Ah, that actually makes sense!"-Qingyu's eyes widened.

She gave it some careful thought and realized that, at the very least, his "eccentric quirk" of waking up at dawn aligned with that claim.

However, Villa Master Qingyang was not about to let a slip of the tongue slide so easily:

—"So that gives you the right to be disrespectful toward my Qingyu?!"-Villa Master Qingyang barked fiercely.

Jian Nuo braced himself against Villa Master Qingyang's murderous glare, looking affectionately at Qingyu:

—"What I feel for Qingyu is love,"-Jian Nuo replied.

Villa Master Qingyang's face contorted as if he had just been [force-fed a choking mouthful of dog food](# "Literal CN: 被狗粮塞了喉咙 (bèi gǒuliáng sāile hóulóng) — stuffed to the throat with dog food; modern slang for being subjected to cringe-inducing public displays of romantic affection").

Qingyu smiled radiantly, acting entirely as though she hadn't heard the line.

—"Still, why would you revere the Untiring Immortal Venerable?"-Qingyu asked curiously.-"After all, everyone in the cultivation realm knows that the relationship between... City Lord Jian and the Untiring Immortal Venerable isn't particularly harmonious."

[Everyone knows? Why the hell didn't WE know?!]

The Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples present cried out inwardly in utter bewilderment.

Truth be told, given the Untiring Immortal Venerable's consistent style, everyone had always assumed their Master was the quintessential lone-wolf cultivator! Even Lu Shulong, who possessed the memories of seven lifetimes, had never been able to figure out what price Master had paid during the third lifetime to convince all those major factions to foster his disciples and treat every single one of them like their own flesh and blood.

Seen now, it was simply because Master's social network was terrifyingly vast.

Even his supposed "arch-nemesis" was never truly an enemy at all.

Jian Nuo's subsequent words only corroborated this.

At the mention of his great-grandfather, Jian Nuo's expression turned slightly awkward:

—"It's precisely because he brought him up so constantly that I came to revere him... Although none of what he said sounded like pleasant praise, you know my great-grandfather's abrasive temperament. He isn't the type to erase another's achievements. So the more I listened, the more I felt Immortal Venerable was truly extraordinary. Anyone capable of enduring my great-grandfather's foul temper must be a remarkable person."

—"Especially once I began cultivating,"-Jian Nuo continued, his eyes gleaming.-"If one wishes to surpass Great-Grandfather, merely learning from Great-Grandfather will never suffice. A superior role model would naturally be someone even more formidable than him! Thus, I delved even deeper into the Untiring Immortal Venerable's lore, and I even entertained thoughts of apprenticing under him—though with Great-Grandfather around, that was obviously impossible. Moreover, I never had the karmic opportunity to meet Immortal Venerable in person, which has always been my greatest regret!"

—"Who would have imagined that thanks to Qingyu, I would finally be granted this opportunity! Qingyu, you truly are my lucky star!"

Jian Nuo gazed at Qingyu with burning eyes.

Yet that little speech caused everyone listening to feel a sudden twinge of pity for Jian Haoge.

Just how much good karma had that old man burned through in his past lives to end up with such a rebellious descendant?!

Qu Qianxing appraised Jian Nuo with a calculating gaze, turning his head toward Lu Shulong:

—"In what way does this idiot merit Master personally stepping out to greet him?"-Qu Qianxing muttered.

—"I haven't the faintest idea,"-Lu Shulong replied. He had originally been scowling at Jian Nuo, but now cast a teasing sidelong glance at Qu Qianxing.

[An 'idiot'...?]

[To think the day would come when I'd hear Second Senior Brother evaluate Jian Nuo with that word.]

After all, during the third lifetime, Qu Qianxing and Jian Nuo had shared an exceptionally close camaraderie, to the extent that outsiders collectively referred to them as the ["Qian-Nuo Young Masters of Sky City"](# "Literal CN: 天上城千诺公子 (Tiānshàngchéng Qiān-Nuò Gōngzǐ) — collective honorific blending Qu Qianxing and Jian Nuo"). One became the City Lord of Sky City, the other the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, expanding their factions' influence across the cultivation realm to the absolute zenith. It had taken Lu Shulong considerable effort back then just to secure an audience with Qu Qianxing, let alone awaken his past memories.

The reason Lu Shulong had been willing to sacrifice himself to Qu Qianxing during the sixth lifetime was precisely because Second Senior Brother had proven so overwhelmingly capable in the preceding lives, giving Lu the feeling that if anyone could rewrite destiny, it was Second Senior Brother.

Reflecting on it now, Second Senior Brother was indeed rewriting destiny—though toward which direction remained somewhat questionable.

Lu Shulong let out a soft sigh.

—"Based on an observational framework I cannot comprehend, we saw that he seemed to have stirred the future currents of the Heavenly Dao, capable of bringing Master the karmic fortune he desires,"-Lu Shulong explained in a low voice.-"And in truth, this Young Master Jian did bring along the 'things' Master wanted, didn't he?"

Following those words, Qu Qianxing glanced displeasedly toward the other side of the hall.

Naturally, they were no longer standing at the mountain gate.

They were gathered inside Huaixu Hall.

Qu Qianxing had originally organized the reception in accordance with the standards for "the child of Master's acquaintance" and "the fiancé of our temporary junior sister," preparing a flawless banquet that left no room for fault or complaint.

Following the initial pleasantries, the guests had relaxed, breaking into casual chatter with those around them.

That was how Jian Nuo ended up conversing with Qingyang Qingyu in the adjacent seat—Qu Qianxing had no intention of wading into a bizarre romantic battlefield, so he had deliberately arranged their seats together—while Qu Qianxing and Lu Shulong sat side by side.

However, the arrangement at the dais was entirely outside Qu Qianxing's designs.

Qu Qianxing had originally assigned his own seat right beside Master. Yet at this moment, Zhou Hui was seated at the head of the banquet, conversing warmly with several cultivators whose spiritual energy felt peculiar and who appeared almost like mortals.

Those people were Young Master Jian's "retinue."

No, calling them mere retainers was inaccurate. Jian Nuo had brought several high-stage cultivators, but unlike ordinary cultivators, they were all those who had [achieved the Dao through unconventional craft](# "Literal CN: 由XX入道 (yóu XX rùdào) — entering the Dao through specialized mundane mastery, such as craftsmanship, farming, or medicine").

The so-called "craft" could be culinary arts, agriculture, or any number of mundane disciplines.

Zhou Hui's fourth disciple, Guan Ye, and Divine Physician Peilan both belonged to this category.

For that reason, Peilan was currently mingling right beside Zhou Hui, and Patriarch Jiuzhai had joined them as well.

Yet Guan Ye and Peilan were considerably more fortunate than these newcomers.

Guan Ye hailed from a cultivator background and was Zhou Hui's direct disciple; once he entered the Dao, he could continue his cultivation smoothly. Peilan was of demon beast stock, possessing innate ancestral bloodline inheritances; for her, "entering the Dao through a craft" was merely a change in cultivation methodology.

For those born as ordinary mortals, however, the reality was entirely different.

In the ancient cultivation era, it hadn't been an issue. The True Immortals seemed exceedingly fond of those who entered the Dao through worldly crafts, refusing to pigeonhole them into conventional tiers like Qi Condensation or Foundation Establishment; as long as they "entered the Dao," they could generally ascend straight to the heavens.

Once the Ascension Stairway was severed, however, such "paths to ascension" were essentially extinguished.

If their luck was poor, such enlightened artisans would waste their lives away in the mortal realm, at best living somewhat longer than ordinary mortals. If their luck was good, they might gain entry into an immortal city.

Yet because they hailed from mortal stock and had never undergone formal training in Qi Condensation or Foundation Establishment, they were regarded in immortal cities merely as "mortals endowed with spiritual energy." They were left with two choices: either abandon their craft to cultivate from scratch—only to be strangled by their mediocre spirit roots and achieve nothing for the rest of their days; or pursue their chosen path to the bitter end, endlessly honing the worldly skills that had led to their initial enlightenment. Naturally, what they actually produced remained virtually identical to what they had crafted before.

Seeing these individuals, even Zhou Hui was somewhat astonished:

—"How did you manage to bring these masters along? Did your great-grandfather have no objections?"-Zhou Hui asked in surprise.

—"He didn't seem to notice,"-Jian Nuo answered with a smile.-"However, I felt that regarding Immortal Venerable's doctrine of 'academic impact factor,' catering to their needs might prove even easier than merely designing maps for Immortal Chess."

—"...You truly are far cleverer than your great-grandfather,"-Zhou Hui remarked in genuine surprise.

(Far away in the Northern Domain, Jian Haoge: *Achoo!*)

It was precisely this shrewd move by Jian Nuo that deepened Qu Qianxing and Lu Shulong's discontent.

—"What on earth do you think this Young Master Jian's true angle is?"-Qu Qianxing muttered to Lu Shulong.-"He claims he came for Master, yet he appears utterly sincere toward Qingyu. But if he came for Qingyu, why is he bending over backward to curry favor with Master...?"

[—"He's a grown adult. He probably wants both."](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

—"Tsk!"-Qu Qianxing clicked his tongue.

Both handsome youths let out a synchronized click of their tongues, entirely unbefitting their refined visages.

Over at the neighboring table, Qingyang Qingyu posed a similar question:

—"Young Master Jian, you traveled all this way; surely you didn't come merely to 'tour' the Untiring Immortal Venerable and call it a day? Do you have any other business?"-Qingyang Qingyu asked.

—"Ah, I do have a request,"-Jian Nuo nodded.

At last, he tore his gaze away from Qingyang Qingyu and looked earnestly toward Zhou Hui. Sensing his gaze, Zhou Hui looked back.

Jian Nuo stated his request:

—"I hope to participate in the Heavenly Spirit Sect's examinations—specifically, to have a contest against His Fifth Highness, Lu Shulong,"-Jian Nuo requested.

[—"Wait, ME?"](# "Speaker: Lu Shulong")

Lu Shulong's eyes bulged in shock, his expression looking as if he had been casually walking down the road only to be flattened by a falling meteor.

Zhou Hui also gave Jian Nuo a quizzical look:

—"Why Shulong?"-Zhou Hui asked, puzzled.

[Exactly, why me?!]

[Whether you want to win over Qingyang Qingyu or catch Master's eye, having a match against Second Senior Brother makes the most sense!]

It wasn't that Qu Qianxing was the favorite; rather, Qu Qianxing was the official heir of the Heavenly Spirit Sect and consistently the highest-scoring academic ace among Zhou Hui's disciples. If someone wanted Zhou Hui to take notice, Qu Qianxing was unequivocally the best benchmark.

As for his reasons regarding Qingyang Qingyu, that went without saying.

Lu Shulong pondered the situation in sheer bewilderment.

—"I wish to gauge my own competency,"-Jian Nuo explained, his reasoning sounding remarkably sound.-"Having finally made the journey to the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I wouldn't dare make the presumptuous request to become Immortal Venerable's disciple. I merely wish to know where the gap lies. Ah... if my academic progress isn't lagging too far behind, might I be permitted to stay at the Heavenly Spirit Sect for a brief period to seek guidance on a few questions from Immortal Venerable?"-Jian Nuo looked toward Zhou Hui with flushing cheeks.

[...You literally just laid out your whole 'presumptuous' scheme for everyone to hear!]

The disciples in the hall were itching to smash their teacups on the floor.

While no squad of armed axemen was about to burst into the banquet, dragging this Young Master Jian out for a sound thrashing seemed entirely warranted.

Hearing this, Villa Master Qingyang arrived at a conclusion quite similar to Lu Shulong's:

—"Since that is your wish, why Little Lu? Bounding forward to challenge Immortal Venerable's fifth disciple hardly seems appropriate,"-Villa Master Qingyang remarked.-"If you wish to compete, you, as your great-grandfather's successor, should naturally compare notes with the Heavenly Spirit Sect's heir, Qu Qianxing."

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing offered Jian Nuo a faint, razor-sharp smile.

Qu Qianxing hadn't originally wanted to meddle in this messy farce, but once it touched Master, matters were entirely different.

Catching Qu Qianxing's smile, Jian Nuo shivered violently.

A bizarre sensation washed over him—an instinctual terror, [like a mouse freezing before a cat](# "Literal CN: 老鼠见了猫 (lǎoshǔ jiànle māo) — visceral, instinctual dread").

[How strange... Even when facing a Tribulation Transcendence titan like Great-Grandfather, I've never felt this kind of dread?]

Jian Nuo mused in bewilderment.

Fortunately, he had never intended to provoke Qu Qianxing in the first place—though at this moment, Jian Nuo had not yet realized that he had already stepped on Qu Qianxing's most sacred taboo.

—"How could I dare cross swords with the Untiring Immortal Venerable's prize disciple?"-Jian Nuo said with a wry smile.-"The reason I chose His Fifth Highness is because Lord Lu Shulong delivered outstanding performances during the Immortal Arts Assembly and various competitions. He serves as the ideal benchmark."

[In other words, you think I'm a [soft persimmon](# "Literal CN: 好欺负 / 软柿子 (ruǎnshìzi) — an easy target / pushover who can be bullied or walked over"), don't you?!]

Lu Shulong was generally easygoing—at least on the surface—but at this moment, real irritation flared within him.

—"Ah, of course, this may be somewhat discourteous toward His Fifth Highness,"-Jian Nuo added with polished diplomacy.-"How about this? As compensation for humoring my little indulgence, let this golden crown serve as my apologetic gift to His Fifth Highness."

With those words, an attendant stepped forward, reverently presenting an ornate golden crown upon a silk cushion.

Zhou Hui had originally been listening with an amused smile, but the moment his gaze fell upon the golden crown, his brow twitched ever so slightly.

Upon it, he detected an aura subtly resembling that of Lu Shulong.

Yet it was merely a resemblance—and extraordinarily faint.

Had someone not possessed Zhou Hui's level of cultivation, it would have been virtually impossible to discern.

Zhou Hui couldn't help casting another glance between the golden crown and Jian Nuo, only to find Jian Nuo staring intently at Lu Shulong, seemingly searching for some reaction upon Lu's face.

Lu Shulong, however, noticed nothing amiss with the golden crown at all. He said coolly:

—"Why do you keep calling me 'His Fifth Highness'?"-Lu Shulong asked, displeased.

—"Isn't that how the Sea Race addresses you...?"-Jian Nuo asked in confusion.

—"The Sea Race?"-Lu Shulong murmured thoughtfully.

He was indeed acquainted with the Sea Race. After all, during the third lifetime, Zhou Hui had sent him to the Eastern Domain's Sea Race. However, because Zhou Hui had not established the Heavenly Spirit Sect in that life and had never formally taken him as a disciple, he had not been the "fifth disciple"; the Sea Race had simply addressed him as "Your Highness."

Lu Shulong disliked that title.

Staying by Zhou Hui's side was all he ever wanted; he had no desire to be any sort of "Highness." Naturally, hearing "His Fifth Highness" grated on his nerves.

Jian Nuo caught this nuance and immediately corrected himself:

—"Ah, was it merely an unauthorized moniker coined by fanatic admirers from the Immortal Arts Assembly? That was my discourtesy, Lord Shulong,"-Jian Nuo apologized.

For some inexplicable reason, a faint, almost imperceptible trace of disappointment laced his voice.

Yet as the young master of Sky City, he maintained strict control over his expressions, allowing none of it to show on the surface.

Only Qingyu and Qu Qianxing cast him a lingering, perceptive glance.

—"Hmph,"-Lu Shulong said, unwilling to let matters drop so easily.-"Address me however you please! But taking an examination is out of the question. Our Heavenly Spirit Sect's exams concluded long ago."

—"That's just how it is. We can hardly convene a special examination solely for your sake!"-Lu Shulong raised his chin.

—"Ah, that is true,"-Jian Nuo murmured, lowering his head.

This time, he forgot to conceal his despondency. Watching him, Qingyu was strangely reminded of a [drenched dog](# "Literal CN: 被浇了水的狗 (bèi jiāole shuǐ de gǒu) — looking like a soaked puppy drooping its ears in defeat").

Lu Shulong finally felt a sliver of satisfaction.

—"However, traveling all this way with a heart eager to learn is truly commendable,"-Zhou Hui said with a gentle smile.

Lu Shulong instantly shot upright, staring nervously at his master.

He had a terrible premonition.

Sure enough, his master never disappointed when it came to testing:

—"While the Heavenly Spirit Sect's examinations have indeed concluded, the exams for the White Jade Capital Academy have not yet finished, have they?"-Zhou Hui remarked.

[No way! Master, you wouldn't actually make me sit for an elementary school exam, would you?!]

Lu Shulong stared at Zhou Hui in utter terror.

[—"White Jade Capital Academy?"](# "Speaker: Jian Nuo")

—"The White Jade Capital Academy is a school Master established for the children of mortal towns across the Central Domain,"-Qu Qianxing explained.-"They are currently undergoing their end-of-term examinations. If Young Master Jian is interested, perhaps you might go have a look?"

In truth, Qu Qianxing's suggestion carried deliberate malice.

After all, having already stated it was an academy for mortals, expecting a high-tier cultivator to participate was an outright slight—especially given the other party was the young master of Sky City. Jian Nuo's attendants instantly wore indignant scowls.

—"A mortal academy? No wonder White Jade Capital Daily made no mention of it..."-Jian Nuo muttered under his breath, before brightening up immediately.-"If Immortal Venerable does not mind, I would be more than willing to take it!"

[You'd actually go that far?! Even kissing Master's ass should have its limits!]

Lu Shulong's rage meter spiked.

He forced a thin smile:

—"Very well, then shall I accompany Young Master Jian?"-Lu Shulong offered sweetly.

[Let's see if you can even solve those physics problems!]

—"Qianxing, Shulong, stop fooling around,"-Zhou Hui called a halt.-"Knowledge is not a [tree without roots](# "Literal CN: 无根之木 (wúgēn zhī mù) — something lacking fundamental grounding"). The academic curriculum of the White Jade Capital Academy is something cultivators have never studied. Forcing someone to sit for their exam out of the blue serves no purpose."

Zhou Hui turned his gaze toward Jian Nuo:

—"However, as this academic year comes to a close, a 'Spirit Root Assessment' will be administered to those mortal students,"-Zhou Hui continued.

—"A spirit root assessment? But mortals do not possess spirit roots!"-Jian Nuo exclaimed in surprise.

—"It is not that they lack them entirely, but rather that their roots have not reached the threshold necessary for cultivation, rendering them virtually undetectable,"-Qu Qianxing elaborated.-"However, Master intends to log and cross-reference this data. Thus, serving as an examiner actually poses quite a challenge."

—"That sounds fascinating!"-Jian Nuo's eyes sparkled.-"Please permit me to participate!"

—"Good child. If you are interested, by all means participate,"-Zhou Hui smiled warmly.-"In truth, you needn't take any exams. If you have questions, you may come directly to ask me."

Hearing this, Qu Qianxing let out a sigh of relief.

Clearly, Zhou Hui had not agreed to take Jian Nuo on as an apprentice; he had merely permitted him to audit.

Yet Jian Nuo still wore an expression of rapturous joy:

—"Thank you, Lord Immortal Venerable!"-Jian Nuo bowed gratefully.

Watching Jian Nuo, Zhou Hui couldn't help musing that this boy was hardly as rebellious as Jian Haoge made him out to be:

—"Speaking of which, the academic atmosphere in the Northern Domain must be quite robust,"-Zhou Hui remarked.-"No matter who visits from there, they actively demand to take examinations."

—"No matter who?"-Jian Nuo asked in confusion.

—"The scion of the Plum-Bamboo couple came all the way to sit for our 'end-of-term examination' last year as well,"-Lu Shulong chimed in with deliberate relish.

—"...Su Xin?"-Jian Nuo's mouth twitched.

First came sheer disbelief, followed immediately by an expression of visceral revulsion.

Evidently, Jian Nuo thoroughly loathed being mentioned in the same breath as Su Xin.

Yet Lu Shulong found Jian Nuo considerably weirder.

Su Xin had merely been duped by Eldest Senior Brother, whereas Jian Nuo's background and motives were genuinely bizarre!

This guy had literally traveled from the Northern Domain to the Central Domain of his own accord just to take an exam!

---

—"Lord Qu Qianxing, might I trouble you for a moment of your time?"-Jian Nuo called out.

After finalizing the schedule, Jian Nuo was escorted by Qu Qianxing to a separate courtyard to settle in.

Originally, guest accommodations were located within the Heavenly Spirit Sect's standard guest quarters. However, Qu Qianxing harbored the selfish desire to isolate this lunatic scion as far away as possible, lest he suffer a sudden mental breakdown and bite someone.

Jian Nuo himself had no objections whatsoever. Because this courtyard happened to be close to Qingyang Qingyu's residence within the Heavenly Spirit Sect, he was immensely grateful for Qu Qianxing's "thoughtfulness."

Just as Qu Qianxing finished settling him in and turned to depart, Jian Nuo called out to him.

—"What is it?"-Qu Qianxing looked at him coldly.

Away from Master, he couldn't be bothered to maintain a polite facade.

Jian Nuo didn't mind Qu Qianxing's frosty demeanor at all. He merely cast a glance at his retainers, who immediately retreated several paces, whereupon Jian Nuo raised a soundproof barrier.

Seeing this elaborate display, Qu Qianxing's brows knit slightly.

—"In truth, my visit this time—apart from wishing to meet the venerable Immortal Venerable whom I have always revered—is also linked to the Qingyang clan,"-Jian Nuo said.

—"I could tell,"-Qu Qianxing remarked with biting sarcasm.

[Because you want both!]

Unaware of Qu Qianxing's sarcasm, Jian Nuo misconstrued his response as validation. His eyes shone with eager delight:

—"As I suspected! Immortal Venerable has also uncovered the curse afflicting the Qingyang clan and the True Dragon, and is preparing to break it, isn't he?!"-Jian Nuo asked with breathless fervor.

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Qu Qianxing")

[...Eh?]

Qu Qianxing stared at Jian Nuo, keeping his bewilderment strictly to himself.

Only Jian Nuo continued babbling with mounting excitement:

—"Previously, I assumed Immortal Venerable taking in the True Dragon was merely a coincidence! I never imagined Qingyu would also have the good fortune to become his disciple. As expected, nothing can escape Immortal Venerable's piercing gaze—he must have uncovered that ancient secret from ten thousand years ago! Well, of course he did. He even repaired the Ascension Stairway; how could he possibly not know? I only wonder how he intends to shatter that curse! And the Palace of Carefree Wandering... the spokespersons of 'Them'! This humble junior has investigated Carefree Palace for many years and secured a handful of clues. I hope I might be of assistance to Immortal Venerable. I know it is somewhat presumptuous, but I wonder if I might be permitted to take part?"

With those words, Jian Nuo looked at Qu Qianxing with boundless, shining anticipation.

[What in the actual hell are you talking about?!]

Qu Qianxing's face remained completely, rigidly blank.
