# Chapter 202: Door-to-Door Delivery

[Even if he's the bearer of a Dao Bone, just how on earth could he have pulled this off...]

[This makes no sense!]

Lin Qi thought in excruciating agony.

At this moment, he existed in a divine soul state, appearing to outsiders as a cluster of radiant golden light. Reason dictated that he shouldn't feel a physical sensation like "pain." Yet presently, not only had his form shrunken by an entire caliber compared to before, but he was also incomparably dim, trembling uncontrollably—looking extraordinarily 'pained' even to the naked eye.

The reason Lin Qi was reduced to this miserable state was that he had invaded Guan Ye's body.

Back then, when Guan Xinguang tossed Guan Ye's original spine into the refining furnace, to any cultivator, even a single drop of blood falling into enemy hands could bring lethal peril, let alone a physical part as critical as a spine. Guan Ye had inevitably suffered a momentary fissure in his spiritual energy field and divine soul barrier, allowing Lin Qi to slip within.

Lin Qi had prepared to possess Guan Ye, biding his time for a suitable opportunity to extract the other party's Dao Bone.

In any case, a host's condition wouldn't truly affect a 'True Immortal' like Lin Qi. Just like a Mahayana titan like Guan Xinguang, whom Lin Qi punished whenever he pleased—whether Guan Xinguang suffered cerebral concussions or harbored rebellious thoughts, none of it could affect Lin Qi in the slightest. If anything, when certain unlucky hosts showed a bit too much [unyielding defiance in the face of death](# "Literal CN: 临死不屈 (lín sǐ bù qū) — refusing to yield even unto death"), it merely spared Lin Qi the trouble of doing the deed himself.

Matters like this were something Lin Qi was thoroughly familiar with and accustomed to.

What he never anticipated was that the instant he invaded Guan Ye's body, he suffered his very first disastrous stumble.

He was slammed dead-on by a surging torrent of super-high concentration spiritual energy!

One had to understand: spiritual energy was inherently a high-energy polymer (in Zhou Hui's terminology), while a living being's 'soul' was likewise a form of energy body. In the absence of a physical body to act as a buffer, when the two energies violently collided, a soul could easily be assimilated by the spiritual energy, swept straight into the furnace to be refined together!

It was only because Lin Qi was a True Immortal whose divine soul had undergone thorough "evolution" during cultivation—possessing immense energy mass—that an immediate tragedy had been averted.

—This was a blessing for Guan Ye as well; with an impurity that massive inside, Heaven only knew what monstrosity would have emerged from the furnace.

Yet even though Lin Qi wasn't refined, the experience was agonizing. It was virtually equivalent to taking a shower naked under a high-pressure fire hose amidst the polar freeze of Antarctica. Lin Qi immediately felt as though a layer of his skin had been blasted clean off—though he currently possessed no skin.

After several minutes of dazed disorientation, Lin Qi finally came to his senses:

—"This bastard! Using his own physical body as a semiconductor for spiritual energy?!"-Lin Qi gasped.

—"Has he lost his mind?!"-Lin Qi shrieked inwardly.

Although Guan Ye possessed a physical body, in the eyes of a True Immortal, a Foundation Establishment body was virtually no different from having none at all. This meant that by doing this, Guan Ye was facing the exact same agony of being blasted by a high-pressure fire hose.

—"Even taking ten thousand steps back, is he not afraid of blasting his own spirit roots to ruins?!"-Lin Qi thought in utter bewilderment.

Then, he discovered that Guan Ye truly wasn't afraid.

Because Guan Ye's spirit roots had already been completely destroyed back when his spine was extracted. Now, his spirit roots—just like his spine—were haphazardly patched and stitched together from miscellaneous materials by his Master. Regrettably, Zhou Hui was no Goddess Nüwa and could not reshape a flawless set of spirit roots for Guan Ye; consequently, the efficiency of these trash roots in absorbing spiritual energy for cultivation was abysmal. Yet precisely because Zhou Hui had painstakingly selected the highest-grade materials available even to a Tribulation Transcendent to stitch them together, the structural toughness of the newly formed spirit roots and meridians was terrifyingly resilient, virtually impossible to shatter through ordinary means.

This resulted in Guan Ye having zero worry about ruining his current body, recklessly thrashing himself without reservation.

As long as his mental fortitude could endure it, he was capable of withstanding any energetic collision.

Through multiple cycles of reincarnation, Guan Ye's pain tolerance had become extraordinarily formidable. He himself was completely oblivious to where this resilience had come from, even laughing and remarking to Lu Shulong:

—"My nerves are rather dull; I'm naturally unfazed by pain. Probably because of that, I managed to survive having my spine extracted."-Guan Ye had said with a grin.

Lu Shulong had merely responded with a thoroughly complicated expression, declining to comment.

However, no matter how much others ached for him, they were not Guan Ye himself; thus, nobody knew what level of excruciating agony he faced during artifact refinement. Lin Qi was the very first person to experience it in real-time synchronization.

Naturally, no sane person would ever wish to experience it.

Under this torrent of spiritual energy, Lin Qi held out for merely fifteen seconds—yet those fifteen seconds felt longer than the thousands of years he had lived in the past.

In that split second, Lin Qi nearly 'summoned' his true physical body to descend.

Even though the lower realm's energy capacity differed and the formidable flesh of a True Immortal could not squeeze into this world (in Lin Qi's view), it was more than enough to tear open a rift, pulverizing the torment-inflicting body of Guan Ye into dust, along with Buyan Palace, the Heavenly Spirit Sect, and everything else he detested. As the price, Lin Qi's physical flesh and divine soul would sustain considerable injury, requiring at least tens of thousands of years (in Heavenly Court time) to fully recover.

In fact, Lin Qi had genuinely attempted to do just that.

He struggled desperately for a few seconds, realizing he could not disrupt Guan Ye in the slightest—the man wasn't even afraid of this level of blinding pain, so why would he be thrown off by other trivial mishaps? Meanwhile, Lin Qi couldn't break free from the torrent of spiritual energy, getting drawn closer and closer to the refining furnace. Seeing that he was about to become part of the refinement materials, in desperation, he had indeed 'summoned' his true body.

Yet whether it was because the ambient spiritual energy was too turbulent, disrupting the summoning, or because he had transmitted the wrong coordinates amidst his blinding agony, his physical body did not immediately respond, forcing him to struggle in torment for a good while longer.

It was at this moment that Lin Qi 'keenly' noticed a spatial rift ahead of him.

Because the pain was truly unbearable, Lin Qi didn't even bother probing where the spatial rift led, bolting straight into the fissure and arriving at the opposite side.

Fortunately, the other side of the rift was exceedingly tranquil, the flow of spiritual energy so gentle it could even be described as tender, finally allowing Lin Qi's agony to subside.

Even so, Lin Qi rested for an entire cup of tea before barely gathering his wits.

Yet he remained extremely frail.

[That Heavenly Spirit Sect disciple is definitely abnormal! How on earth could a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator accomplish this?!]

Lin Qi naturally refused to admit that he had suffered a disastrous loss at the hands of a Foundation Establishment junior, fuming indignantly:

[It must be that Tribulation Transcendent master of his trying to prop up his vanity, secretly pulling strings behind the scenes!]

This was the exact same rationalization Guan Xinguang always resorted to whenever the topic of 'Guan Ye drafting blueprints for the large-scale teleportation array' came up.

Back then, Lin Qi had mocked Guan Xinguang for his inferior skills, resorting to petty excuses for self-consolation; who knew he would end up reduced to the exact same state?

Yet Lin Qi possessed zero self-awareness.

He soothed his bruised ego with this third-rate excuse, barely gathering his spirits to survey his surroundings.

Then, he saw the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

An upside-down Heavenly Spirit Sect.

Lin Qi stared blankly. Focusing his gaze on his surroundings, he realized he was dangling upside down from the highest bough of the ancient peach tree at the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain gate.

Lin Qi vaguely remembered that someone had been hung here before—seemingly the Heavenly Spirit Sect's eldest disciple.

Rumor had it that this eldest disciple possessed extraordinary talent, brilliant wisdom, and was a thoroughly fine lad. Yet because of an academic blunder—not for doing poorly, but for performing entirely too well and stealing the limelight from his junior brother—he had been strung from the bough as public punishment.

—Because the reason was so bizarre, Lin Qi had taken an extra look at the time.

Only, there was nobody on this bough right now; only frayed ropes and a tattered banner hung from the tip, fluttering in the wind.

Moreover, not only was the bough empty, but looking toward the sect's interior, the grounds were desolate and silent—starkly contrasting the bustling atmosphere he had witnessed when possessing Guan Xinguang. Only chilly moonlight bathed the various pavilions, slicing the architecture into stark halves of black and white, appearing somewhat eerie.

In stark contrast to this eerie quiet, the spiritual energy here was extraordinarily pure, cycling in orderly fashion along specific pathways. Merely standing here refreshed the spirit.

—"This place is..."-Lin Qi murmured in bewilderment.

Then, as if realizing something, he burst into boisterous laughter:

—"So that's how it is, so that's how it is... Isn't this the interior of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's mountain-protecting grand array?!"-Lin Qi exclaimed.

Indeed, this was the mountain-protecting array.

The "inverted sky-reflection Heavenly Spirit Sect" visible from below.

This was the sect's spiritual underbelly—the 'inner-side space.' All the infrastructure, specialized facilities, and energy pipelines inconvenient to place on the 'outer surface' were installed here. Calling it the core of the Heavenly Spirit Sect's spiritual network was by no means an exaggeration.

—"I knew it! How could a mere Foundation Establishment cultivator manipulate energy of that magnitude?! He was merely a puppet figurehead pushed out by the sect! In reality, it was that Untiring fellow who secretly mobilized the sect's spiritual energy to rig the exam! This rift leading into the core array is absolute proof of his cheating!"-Lin Qi deduced with immense confidence.

With his narrow cognitive baseline, there was no way he could fathom why someone would willingly endure that degree of excruciating pain just to continue forging and cultivating.

Lin Qi could only rationalize that the boy was a puppet, just like the pawns he controlled to die on the front lines.

Having uncovered the "truth," Lin Qi felt considerably refreshed.

Regrettably, this 'truth' provided zero practical assistance to Lin Qi's current predicament.

After all, he couldn't very well crawl back through the rift into that lethal torrent of spiritual energy to expose the 'truth.' Furthermore, mountain-protecting arrays were typically controlled by a sect's core figures—such as the Untiring Immortal Venerable and his second disciple—forming a sealed loop. Getting in was difficult, and getting out was just as hard; without paying a price, even a True Immortal would struggle to break free.

Worse still, there wasn't a single living soul in this godforsaken place, meaning no physical puppets to possess. His signature spells were completely unusable; his soul had just been stripped of a 'layer of skin,' his strength drastically diminished.

[Should I just summon my physical flesh after all?] Lin Qi pondered once more.

He was beginning to lean toward giving up.

Without question, returning to find Guan Ye and extracting his Dao Bone was virtually impossible now. With no gains to be made, Lin Qi judged that spending further effort on this world wasn't worth it. Better to self-detonate the Heavenly Spirit Sect, escape back to the upper realm, and submit a report explaining the anomalies to appease his superiors.

The only issue was that this scheme would inflict substantial losses upon himself; now that the excruciating pain had subsided, he grew hesitant.

It was at this moment that a sarcastically mocking voice drifted over faintly:

—"And here you claimed... lower-realm mortals were incompetent... turns out you're... just the same?"-the voice broke and crackled.

—"Who's there?!"-Lin Qi gasped, recognizing the speaker.-"Ren Ran?!"

—"Yes..."-'Ren Ran's' voice drifted from an indeterminate location, sounding distant and muffled.-"Where... are you? The puppet is dead, the exam is over, but... I cannot sense your aura on him... Where are you trapped...?"

[—"..."](# "Speaker: Lin Qi")

Lin Qi refused to answer.

—"Fine... don't say anything if you don't want to... I've been placed under watch as well, and won't be able to act later..."-'Ren Ran' sighed, unbothered by Lin Qi's silence.-"But you seem able to move freely... perhaps you can... help..."

Ren Ran's voice grew increasingly faint, eventually fading away.

Lin Qi didn't care to hear more anyway.

He had been thoroughly exasperated by the failure of his scheme; hearing this only aggravated him. Just as he turned to leave, a cluster of starlight descended from above, glittering as it formed an astral formation before condensing into a physical array disc.

Seeing this, Lin Qi inexplicably let out a sigh of relief.

He could sense that this disc not only carried Ren Ran's magical aura, but also the unmistakable aura of the Heavenly Court. It was evidently the trump-card treasure Ren Ran had received upon descending—a perk reserved exclusively for 'field personnel' like her.

Seeing this array disc, Lin Qi finally confirmed beyond doubt that the speaker had been Ren Ran.

—Voices could be forged, but the aura of this disc was impossible to fake!

Otherwise, given Ren Ran's typical lack of wits, Lin Qi would never have believed she could pull off anything clever.

Lin Qi considered 'Ren Ran's' earlier words, concluding her meaning was:

Guan Xinguang was dead, the exam was over, and failing to sense Lin Qi's aura on Guan Ye, Ren Ran suspected he was trapped elsewhere. But because she was under watch by the Untiring Immortal Venerable and couldn't move freely, she had somehow delivered her treasure to Lin Qi, hoping he could use its power to escape and rescue her.

As for how Ren Ran located him—as a True Immortal, even an idiotic one, having a few inscrutable tracking tricks was completely normal.

—"At least the array disc genuinely belongs to the Heavenly Court,"-Lin Qi muttered.

Whether he would rescue Ren Ran after escaping would depend entirely on his mood.

Musing thus, Lin Qi accepted the astral array disc.

To an outsider, it looked like a cluster of dim golden light enveloping the disc, hovering in midair.

Lin Qi meticulously inspected the disc inside and out, especially the exclusive seal of the Bureau of Heavenly Dao on the back.

The seal confirmed it was a standardized treasure forged by the Bureau of Heavenly Dao. As a crucial department responsible for weaving the Heavenly Dao, they never distributed artifacts lightly; even when issued, recipients were only granted usage rights, while structural control remained firmly with the bureau, preventing alteration of its internal mechanisms.

Similar array discs were things even Lin Qi's military strategists were only temporarily granted during major campaigns, required to return them immediately afterward.

—"Quite a stroke of luck that she got her hands on such a treasure. Was she so stupid that the Bureau of Heavenly Dao had to provide specialized guidance?"-Lin Qi sneered with a prick of jealousy.

By now, all suspicion in his heart had vanished.

He channeled his immortal spiritual energy into the array disc.

Though hard to acquire, using it was straightforward: one merely focused on a goal and channeled energy.

Lin Qi's first thought was naturally: *How do I find the array eyes of the mountain-protecting array?*

A star lit up on the disc, corresponding to a direction within the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

—"Hmph,"-Lin Qi grunted with satisfaction, noting the star's direction.

Instead of moving immediately, he held the disc and focused on: *How do I seize control of the mountain-protecting array?*

Several more stars illuminated, forming a clear route map. Based on his understanding, destroying the array eyes along this path would grant control over the entire grand array.

Lin Qi smiled in triumph.

Better yet, he noticed a massive jade screen standing beneath the peach tree at the mountain gate, displaying a complete layout map of the Heavenly Spirit Sect.

It had been erected for outside guests attending the assembly and qualification exams.

Although there were no guests in the array's mirror side, as a reflection, it mirrored everything from the outer surface with meticulous detail, including this map—which now proved immensely convenient for Lin Qi.

Comparing the astral route to the map, he identified the array eyes:

—"Starting from Water Mirror Pavilion, then Sword Mountain... the one overgrown with Snow Glow Grass? Then the Pill Concoction Chamber, the Artifact Refinement Workshop, the Formation Pavilion... wait, the Formation Pavilion is located right inside Buyan Palace where the exam was held?"-Lin Qi murmured.

Had he known more about the sect, he would have recognized that every single array eye was a domain overseen by one of Zhou Hui's direct disciples.

Naturally, Lin Qi was oblivious. He read the next location:

—"From the Formation Pavilion in Buyan Palace to the Rare Beast Enclosure? Hmm? Why is there a red 'DO NOT APPROACH' warning over the Rare Beast Enclosure?"-Lin Qi wondered aloud.

Fortunately, the map provided detailed footnotes for such 'forbidden zones' to prevent reckless thieves from barging in under the assumption that treasures lay within.

—"The Rare Beast Enclosure is managed by the sect's eldest disciple, housing various demon beasts and even ferocious primordial beasts, which is why outsiders are warned away?"-Lin Qi scoffed.

He hardly believed a sign warning of [vicious beasts within](# "Literal CN: 内有恶犬 (nèi yǒu è quǎn) — beware of vicious dog; traditional warning sign") could deter a True Immortal. He read further:

—"Exiting the Rare Beast Enclosure, then returning to Buyan Palace's Academic Hall... Wait! It indicates I must first proceed to the residence of the second disciple, Qu Qianxing, retrieve an artifact bearing his spiritual aura, and only then return to the Academic Hall?"-Lin Qi double-checked.

Wait... the interpersonal dynamics of the Heavenly Spirit Sect seemed quite intriguing!

Lin Qi sank into thought.

Though no grandmaster of formations, he understood basic mechanics: the final array eye was invariably the most critical core. But accessing it required an item belonging to the second disciple, indicating Qu Qianxing held administrative authority over the mountain array!

In contrast, the eldest disciple was not only banished to herd beasts, but regularly strung up from the mountain gate tree!

As a True Immortal specializing in manipulating human hearts, Lin Qi felt he had glimpsed the unsightly, sordid underbelly of this sect.

Previously, he wouldn't have cared about lower-realm politics. But now his puppet was dead, his original prey was gone, and even if he broke the array, he couldn't find a suitable vessel in short order.

—"Isn't this delivering himself right to my doorstep?"-Lin Qi sneered coldly.

He had heard this eldest disciple had reached the Void Refinement Stage at age two hundred, ahead of the favored second disciple! Clearly a genius with peerless talent and bone structure! Rumors spoke of him being uniquely gifted; though not a freshly molded body made from a Dao Bone, it was an outstanding alternative vessel.

Besides, possessing the eldest disciple didn't mean giving up on the fourth disciple!

Ultimately, that Dao Bone would still belong to him!

Thinking of this, Lin Qi burst into uproarious laughter.

He soared to the apex of the peach tree, his golden light trembling as he radiated mournful celestial melodies alongside his voice:

—"Feeling terribly wronged, aren't you? Feeling thoroughly indignant? Even though you are the eldest disciple, you've been hung in a place like this! You are clearly far more outstanding than your junior brothers! Your Master is exceedingly unfair, isn't he?"-Lin Qi projected his voice persuasively.

As he spoke, a faint sigh drifted through the branches, followed by the indistinct outline of a figure.

It wasn't the real Yuwen Xuan, but his 'lingering obsession.'

As the guardian of a sect, the mountain array was also a chronicler. It mirrored buildings and recorded intense spiritual reactions; powerful arrays even captured potent soul imprints, forming mirror phantoms of disciples.

The Heavenly Spirit Sect's array possessed this function, though its short history meant it lacked sufficient 'data' to coalesce vivid phantoms on its own.

Lin Qi's spellcraft was accelerating this process.

—"Yet this isn't solely the work of my spellcraft; this is an emotion inherently present within you!"-Lin Qi chuckled, delighted to see that when he mentioned 'Master,' the phantom of a young man materialized at the end of the rope, glaring at him with a look of utter disgust.

Naturally, this was not the true Yuwen Xuan, but a phantom formed from indignant resentment.

If someone were emotionally dull, unwilling to confront their inner demons or face their negative emotions, they might not even notice such a phantom forming.

Lin Qi didn't know if Yuwen Xuan was that sensitive, but he hoped he was, as it made him easier to manipulate.

Even if he wasn't, it didn't matter. The real Yuwen Xuan on the outside wouldn't know this conversation was occurring in the array's mirror space, yet his obsession would lay bare all his secrets to Lin Qi.

Once Lin Qi broke the array, possessing Yuwen Xuan with this intel would be child's play.

—According to Lin Qi's vast experience, that was how it always worked.

Thus, Lin Qi sought only to provoke Yuwen Xuan's obsession:

—"Actually, the moment I crossed the spatial rift and arrived here, I faintly sensed the aura of resentment. That was your emotion, wasn't it? Truly pitiful..."-Lin Qi continued cooing.

[—"Ying!"](# "Speaker: Yuwen Xuan's Phantom")

Ying?

Lin Qi blinked in bewilderment at the bizarre sound. In the next instant, Yuwen Xuan's phantom lunged forward in a furious assault.

Yet a phantom was merely a phantom—and a 'mortal's' phantom at that.

The moment it touched Lin Qi's golden light, it began melting, rapidly condensing into a tiny clump of dark-red flame enveloped within the golden radiance.

Lin Qi smirked, yet felt slightly underwhelmed:

—"Tsk, not enough! I thought it would be stronger. Seems this eldest disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect is rather cowardly; no wonder his Master chose the second disciple... Brr, so cold,"-Lin Qi shivered.

Lin Qi felt a foul, fishy breeze blow past, chilling the back of his neck...

Wait, he currently had no neck!

Lin Qi scanned his surroundings warily, but spotted nothing amiss. He merely felt the surrounding light dim slightly, accompanied by strange noises.

—"Not only cowardly, but overly sensitive as well!"-Lin Qi concluded that this shift was caused by the eldest disciple hanging from the bough on the 'outer surface.'

Though the real person couldn't perceive his phantom or what transpired in the mirror space, sensitive individuals would naturally feel an instinctive ripple when part of their emotional aura was stolen, triggering minor spiritual weather phenomena.

Sensitive, yet possessing immense spiritual power.

Lin Qi grew increasingly pleased with his newly chosen vessel.

—"Very well, I shall linger here no longer. With a temperament like his, there must be plenty more obsessions scattered across the sect to harvest!"-Lin Qi laughed heartily.

He flicked his golden light with self-imagined swagger and soared along the star path into the depths of the sect.

Only, what Lin Qi did not know was that the instant he departed the mountain gate and vanished into the path ahead, the entire mountain gate was plunged into pitch-black darkness, as if someone had snuffed out a candle!

And within that darkness, everything in the surroundings began to contort and mutate:

The lush woods outside the mountain gate transformed into rows of gigantic, razor-sharp teeth, densely packed and terrifyingly jagged;

The smooth road before the gate turned into a moist, glistening tongue, crimson and drenched in slobbering saliva;

The colossal peach tree at the gate melted into an enclosed oral cavity, its highest bough serving as the dangling uvula!

And the path Lin Qi had eagerly flown into was none other than the esophagus at the far back of the throat!

At this moment, a warm, moist breath exhaled from the gullet, carrying a muffled, grumbling complaint:

—"Annoying bastard, nearly made me blow my cover,"-Yuwen Xuan muttered in annoyance.

—"Hold back, hold back. Premium ingredients require premium culinary methods,"-Yuwen Xuan reminded himself.

—"If you're going to eat, you must eat the whole thing. Must hold back!"-Yuwen Xuan resolved hungrily.
