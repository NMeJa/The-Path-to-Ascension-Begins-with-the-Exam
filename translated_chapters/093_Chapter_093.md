# Chapter 93: [Channeling Is Better Than Blocking](<# "Literal CN: 堵不如疏 (dǔ bùrú shū) — ancient Chinese wisdom from flood control: dredging and channeling a flood is far more effective than trying to erect dams to block it; addressing root causes rather than suppressing symptoms">) (第93章 堵不如疏)

[Could it be that the Untiring Immortal Venerable is actually filthy rich?]

Qingyang Qingyu pondered to herself.

For the eldest young miss of the Qingyang clan to arrive at the conclusion of "rich," it certainly couldn't mean ordinary wealth. Even among the eminent cultivation clans of the Celestial City, very few could ever catch the eye of the Qingyang family.

In theory, the Heavenly Spirit Sect should never have qualified for such an appraisal under any circumstances.

After all, in terms of sheer scale, it was merely a second-rate minor sect. Its relatively grand facilities—such as the Library Pavilion, Wordless Palace, Immortals' Bazaar, Qianxing Hall, and the communal bathhouses—had all been erected within just the past six months. To true old-money magnates like Eternal Villa, not only was the number of [grand monuments](<# "Literal CN: 奇观 (qíguān) — wonders / grand architectural monuments; also an allusion to civilization-building wonders">) sorely lacking, but it also lacked historical foundation.

Yet upon actually interacting with the Untiring Immortal Venerable and his direct disciples, Qingyang Qingyu noticed that this lord Immortal Venerable simply couldn't be bothered with the trivialities of daily living.

Lord Immortal Venerable would frequently slip into an epiphany of cultivation while sipping tea and reading; thus, in his eyes, whether the spiritual tea he drank was fifty-year-old wild tea leaves or ten-thousand-year-old Jade Dew Silver Needles was utterly irrelevant. In either case, the spiritual energy they provided was negligible.

But when it came to cultivation itself, things were entirely different.

The formation stones for his cave-dwelling had to be aged stones dredged from the spiritual eyes of the Abyssal Ocean, with pure, unblemished dark crimson considered optimal. His meditation cushion had to be woven from ten-thousand-year gold-rimmed fragrant bamboo growing beside icy springs blended with the silk spun by a ninth-rank Jade-Bellied Five-Eyed Spider during gestation—and Qingyu recalled that her ancestor possessed only nine strands of such silk, which he had painstakingly forged into a defensive artifact. As for everyday cultivation materials, it was even more exorbitant: various top-grade spirit stones and spiritual ores were consumed like disposable office supplies.

What was even more terrifying was that this lavish treatment was not exclusive to the Immortal Venerable alone.

While the Untiring Immortal Venerable deeply abhorred resorting to external shortcuts like alchemical pills or furnace cauldrons to inflate cultivation, whenever his disciples engaged in legitimate study, the materials he provided were invariably the absolute best. For instance, Ran Deng's modular prefabricated cave-dwelling hadn't generated a single copper in revenue yet, but research alone had reportedly incinerated half a mineral vein and a mountain of rare materials. By comparison, Mo Xi's bonded mineral spirit was practically economical—since Mo Xi's cultivation was still low and his level of refinement modest, the spirit stones he burned through were relatively minimal.

Furthermore, following the "education reform," research projects initiated by regular Heavenly Spirit Sect disciples were treated with equal seriousness. Regardless of rank or seniority, disciples could submit their own cultivation projects; provided an idea demonstrated innovation and high practical utility, it could secure massive research grants.

This put Qingyang Qingyu in quite a dilemma.

Her initial approach of buying gifts with ordinary spirit stones to farm favor had been simple yet effective.

But measured against the Immortal Venerable's actual standards—completely unconcerned with lifestyle luxuries, yet absurdly particular about cultivation materials—it seemed whatever she gifted carried the distinct risk of [patting the horse's flank only to get kicked by its hoof](<# "Literal CN: 拍马屁拍到马腿上 (pāi mǎpì pāi dào mǎtuǐ shàng) — backfiring flattery; buttering someone up inappropriately and receiving a harsh rebuff instead">).

—"...Might as well just help the Artifact Refinement Hall build a brand-new facility,"-Qingyu muttered to herself.-"They have quite a backlog of projects; their existing floor space probably isn't enough, right?"

[—"A splendid idea."](<# "Speaker: Villa Master Qingyang">)

—"Ancestor!"-Qingyu gasped in surprise.

Even though both were staying right here in the Heavenly Spirit Sect, her venerable ancestor spent all his time either cooped up in his temporary cave-dwelling or lurking around the Wordless Palace; Qingyu had almost thought her ancestor had abandoned her!

This time, however, Villa Master Qingyang had clearly come specifically for Qingyu. Having paid a special visit to her cave-dwelling, he lavished praise on her plan to splash cash on an experimental laboratory building:
—"Make it a large one. That way, we can dispatch more disciples here on exchange programs in the future."

[Are you planning to turn 'exchange students' into a permanent tradition?!]

Qingyu felt an urge to roast him, yet found herself hardly surprised.

To say nothing of Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable's encyclopedic erudition, Qingyang Qingyu had attended two lectures at the Wordless Palace and discovered that even the beginner-level curriculum was delivered with astonishing lucidity, unveiling profound depths from simple fundamentals. Such pedagogical mastery was something even major sects failed to achieve.

It was only natural that her ancestor's heart would itch with desire.

—"Is an exchange program really enough?"-Qingyu asked.-"Personally, I feel the 'beginner class' textbooks could be popularized among all our newly inducted disciples, regardless of whether they belong to the inner or outer sect. After all, even if the Heavenly Spirit Technique isn't used as a primary cultivation manual, it serves brilliantly as an auxiliary foundation. It can lay down a solid baseline for children before they advance, making it far easier for them to cultivate our Qingyang clan's primary techniques once they reach the appropriate age. Most remarkably, though this curriculum was authored by Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable himself, it is simple and approachable—anyone with basic literacy can understand it."

—"Oh?"-Villa Master Qingyang asked. He had genuinely never paid attention to the Heavenly Spirit Sect's beginner textbooks. (Qingyu had only audited the sessions because the Untiring Immortal Venerable personally conducted training for the instructors.) Still, for Qingyu—who was utterly obsessed with cultivation manuals—to praise it so highly, he had no doubt regarding the Immortal Venerable's prowess. Yet after pondering carefully, he shook his head:
—"However, that is their Heavenly Spirit Technique; how could the Untiring Immortal Venerable possibly permit another sect to utilize it?"

Lineage and inheritance of techniques were, after all, an exceedingly solemn matter.

As the saying went: *[teach the apprentice and starve the master](<# "Literal CN: 教会徒弟饿死师父 (jiāo huì túdì èsǐ shīfu) — common Chinese idiom warning that sharing trade secrets allows pupils to outcompete and displace their masters">).* Typically, as long as the creator of a technique remained alive, they would rigorously vet and hoard their successors. Common manuals circulating freely in the market were almost exclusively relics whose original creators had long since perished.

Zhou Hui, however, was clearly cast from a different mold.

Qingyu shook her head:
—"I already inquired. If it is merely the beginner curriculum, the Untiring Immortal Venerable doesn't mind it being disseminated externally at all. Currently, the Heavenly Spirit Sect's beginner classes operate under a policy of [education without discrimination](<# "Literal CN: 有教无类 (yǒu jiào wú lèi) — famous Confucian principle from the Analects: providing education to all without distinction of social class or background">). Deeper techniques require advancing into the intermediate classes before one may study them. That is precisely why I suggested the beginner curriculum is ideally suited for widespread introductory schooling."

—"...Education without discrimination, hm? Is he trying to accumulate karmic merit?"-Villa Master Qingyang muttered.

Villa Master Qingyang naturally wouldn't agree immediately.

This was a matter concerning the long-term future of Eternal Villa; he would naturally have to audit a couple of lectures and obtain the Heavenly Spirit Technique to scrutinize it thoroughly first.

Qingyu understood this well and did not press him.

Instead, Villa Master Qingyang cast an eccentric glance at his clan's heir:
—"Still, it's quite rare to see you this enthusiastic about such an affair." In the past, Qingyu frequently studied various manuals and might comment favorably on exceptional ones, but rarely pursued them further. This time, she had actually taken the initiative to consult the Untiring Immortal Venerable, indicating serious intent.

Recalling rumors of Qingyu's recent efforts to curry favor with the Immortal Venerable, Villa Master Qingyang couldn't help feeling a twinge of worry:
—"You aren't planning to defect and enter the Untiring Immortal Venerable's tutelage, are you?! Let me warn you, that is outright betrayal! Unless, of course, you form a Dao companionship with someone from the Heavenly Spirit Sect; then you could study their arts openly and honorably. Personally, I think Qianxing-er is splendid—earnest, dependable, and he possesses precisely the sort of handsome face young maidens adore—"

—"I may appreciate handsome faces, but unless that face is attached to my own neck, it is utterly meaningless,"-Qingyu retorted peevishly.

Why on earth would she want to tie herself to a haughty prig whose eyes were glued to the heavens?!

Did she look like someone who thought her life was too peaceful and needed extra misery?

Qingyu was hardly the self-sacrificing type. Given the choice, rather than someone she fancied, she would far prefer someone who doted on her—not to mention she felt zero romantic attraction toward Qu Qianxing.

Her ancestor, on the other hand, couldn't stop spouting 'Qianxing-er' this and 'Qianxing-er' that. [If you're so fond of him, why don't you marry him yourself?!]

Qingyu roasted him in her heart. Seeing that Villa Master Qingyang was about to say more, she blurted out in haste:
—"If you force me to marry Qu Qianxing, I'd rather go to... the Celestial City!"

*What was the name of that fellow in the Celestial City who had been courting her again?* She couldn't recall it for the life of her.

Faced with this "threat," Villa Master Qingyang instantly shut his mouth.

Having lost all appetite for idle chatter, Qingyu asked listlessly:
—"Venerable Ancestor, did you make this special trip just to peddle your temporary apprentice to me?"

[Did you forget that I'm your actual great-(great-great...)-granddaughter?!]

—"What nonsense are you talking?"-Villa Master Qingyang grumbled indignantly.-"I heard you finished the initial theoretical foundations and are preparing to get hands-on experience building a 'Secret Realm Guidance System.' But since it's secret realm construction, you need a secret realm to begin with! So I just visited Lord Untiring Immortal Venerable and secured a secret realm for you, allowing the Immortal Venerable to tutor you directly within it."

As he spoke, Villa Master Qingyang fished into his sleeve, retrieved a padlock of mutton-fat white jade, and handed it to Qingyu.

—"Ancestor!"-Qingyu cried, genuinely touched.

Though his nagging about marriage was annoying at times, her ancestor truly cared for her after all!

Not only had he kept close tabs on her academic progress, but he had promptly provided the absolute finest resources.

Take this jade lock: it was in fact the "key" to the warding arrays of a secret realm.

As descendants of the ancient Lord of Baiyujing, Eternal Villa possessed numerous archaic secret realms—which was precisely the source of their astronomical wealth. Most of these realms remained sealed behind ancient wards, with different jade "keys" designating varying tiers of secret realms. Among them, a mutton-fat white jade padlock undoubtedly represented the highest possible grade.

To think Ancestor was willing to bestow such a supreme secret realm upon her... *Hm?*

Qingyu's gaze caught the engraved patterns at the base of the mutton-fat jade lock. These markings signified the specific realm serial number—and the number before her looked extraordinarily familiar. Come to think of it, wasn't this entire jade lock itself oddly familiar?

—"Wait a second, Ancestor, isn't this the warding key to the 'Spirit Palace'?!"-Qingyu interrogated.-"Did you truly prepare this for me?! Ancestor?!"

Qingyang Qingyu snapped her head up, only to find her ancestor had vanished like morning dew.

*

Mo Xi finally understood what Second Senior Brother meant by "extravagantly expensive yet thoroughly useless materials."

For today's practical lab, the eldest young miss of the Qingyang clan had casually produced an entire secret realm to serve as her instructional textbook, making secret realms look like pebbles one could pick up off the side of the road.

Yet what stunned Mo Xi above all else was the interior of this secret realm.

Rather than a realm, it was more like a magnificent palace.

A palace of pure spirit stones.

The entire structure was constructed from massive, monolithic, glittering spirit stones, radiant enough to blind anyone who laid eyes upon it.

Even though Mo Xi possessed an opulent mineral vein of his own, his breath hitched in his throat when he beheld this palace.

Thanks to Immortals' Chess, Mo Xi had believed he possessed decent insight into realm design—though usually he merely drafted normalized map requirements for Ran Deng to implement, only to be scolded by Eldest Senior Sister for being too wildly impractical. Yet even in his most demented brainstorms, he had never conceived of building a secret realm out of raw spirit stones!

[What on earth were they thinking?!]

Plainly speaking, spirit stones were merely natural storage vessels for spiritual energy. Their hardness was mediocre, their structural defenses feeble, and their tensile resilience abysmal—they were utterly unfit to serve as construction materials. Using them to build houses—let alone palaces—meant even an ordinary cottage risked collapsing under its own weight, requiring countless reinforcement spells just to hold its shape. Yet as energy-conducting capacitors, their tolerance to spellcraft was notoriously poor; casting magic upon them easily agitated the internal spiritual current, causing them to shatter into dust. It was the epitome of spending twice the effort for half the result.

In short, unless one suffered from [ten years of cerebral thrombosis](<# "Literal CN: 十年脑血栓 (shí nián nǎoxuèshuān) — modern Chinese internet slang denoting severe brain rot or utter insanity">), no sane architect would ever build a palace out of spirit stones.

Mo Xi couldn't help casting a profoundly complicated expression at Qingyang Qingyu:
—"I thought Eternal Villa's aesthetic was slightly more... understated?"

[Don't you think building a secret realm out of spirit stones screams gaudy nouveau riche?](<# "Literal CN: 暴发户 (bàofāhù) — parvenu / nouveau riche who flaunts garish wealth without taste or restraint">)

Qingyu's personal aesthetic didn't care for this monstrosity either; however, as it concerned her ancestral home, she naturally couldn't allow an outsider to mock it.

Qingyu explained:
—"It has nothing to do with style; this secret realm was originally a mineral vein."

[—"?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi">)

—"The Demon-God War reportedly began when someone planted [Demon Eyes](<# "Literal CN: 魔眼 (móyǎn) — interdimensional rifts connecting to the demon realm">) to summon demonic legions,"-Qingyu explained.-"Our cultivation world could not withstand them and was forced to summon True Immortals and divine deities. At that time, the entire Central Domain became a cataclysmic battleground. Demon Eyes tore open everywhere, spewing forth infinite demons and monsters, which ultimately caused the fall of Baiyujing. Eternal Villa, as descendants of the Lord of Baiyujing, was able to survive precisely because the Demon Eye near them opened deep beneath a spiritual vein. The overwhelming concentration of spiritual energy happened to pin down the fiends, enabling our ancestors to hold out until the war concluded."

—"I see..."-Mo Xi began, before trailing off.-[Like hell I do! What does any of that have to do with building a palace out of spirit stones?!]

Mo Xi was utterly baffled.

Furthermore, he caught onto something far more horrifying:
—"Wait a second—does that mean that Demon Eye is still sitting under the mineral vein right now?!"

Demon Eyes across the cultivation realm naturally hadn't vanished on their own.

Just like the teleportation matrices and Heavenly Ladders leading to the True Immortal Realm, they had been prime tactical targets during the war; anything that could be smashed was pulverized, and anything that could be annihilated was wiped out. Only when deities declared this mortal realm must rest and recover—sealing the path to ascension—were True Immortals and demons prevented from invading anew.

Yet this Demon Eye had remained entombed beneath a mineral vein while humanity maintained the upper hand—meaning it had never been destroyed at all!

—"Following the war, many people did indeed harbor that concern,"-Qingyu added in explanation.-"So my ancestors stacked even more spirit stones atop the vein to suppress and seal the Demon Eye, which ultimately grew into this palace."

—"...Incredible,"-Mo Xi murmured, utterly speechless.

[You stacked spirit stones to suppress a Demon Eye, and then just got carried away playing house, didn't you?!]

[The pleasures of the rich are indeed so unpretentious and simple.](<# "Literal CN: 有钱人的乐趣就这么朴实无华 (yǒuqiánrén de lèqù jiù zhème pǔshí wúhuá) — famous Chinese internet meme originating from the short video series 'The Boring Life of Uncle Zhu' (朱一旦的枯燥生活): 'The life of the rich is often so unpretentious, simple, and boring'">)

Qingyu coughed twice, clarifying that they hadn't merely been playing around:
—"Naturally, this palace was meticulously engineered. During the Demon-God War, our Qingyang clan consulted a True Immortal and, following his instructions, commissioned grand array masters to construct ninety-nine Reverse-Spirit Formations throughout the palace to pump spiritual energy backward into the Demon Eye. As long as it was continuously infused for 9,999 years, the Demon Eye would be eradicated entirely. So much time has elapsed since the war that the Demon Eye has surely withered away by now... Even if it lingers, it cannot stir up any meaningful trouble."

*That may not be the case.*

Zhou Hui thought to himself as he gazed at the palace.

According to the script of the Heavenly Dao, a century from now, after the Heavenly Spirit Sect was annihilated, Mo Xi would commune with the demon race and unseal this exact Demon Eye. Consequently, the entire Spirit Stone Palace would be corrupted by demonic miasma, transforming into a breeding ground for demonic horrors.

Ten thousand years ago, Eternal Villa had survived thanks to this spiritual vein; ten thousand years later, they would face demonic doom precisely because of it. Whoever penned that script possessed a truly morbid sense of humor.

Therefore, when Villa Master Qingyang requested yesterday that Zhou Hui construct a "Secret Realm Guidance System" for this palace while tutoring Qingyu, Zhou Hui had been genuinely startled.

He had previously wondered how Mo Xi could have ever become entangled with the Demon Eye beneath this palace; now, it had delivered itself right to his doorstep.

Yet thinking it through carefully, Zhou Hui felt something was amiss.

Had he not "dreamed"—had he not glimpsed another world and read the script of the Heavenly Dao—he would most likely still be in secluded cultivation obsessing over how to ascend! He certainly wouldn't be building secret realms, creating "secret realm operating systems," and Villa Master Qingyang would never have invited him to install a guidance system in his family's realm.

In other words, the present scenario was unique to his current trajectory.

Then how had Mo Xi become entangled with this palace in the original timeline? Was his present assignment to renovate this palace an inevitable convergence of fate? Or had Zhou Hui's interventions caused history to self-correct, circling right back to the starting point?

Regrettably, there was clearly no answer to this question.

The original Heavenly Dao script had never explained how Mo Xi managed to open the Demon Eye; it simply treated Mo Xi as a born sociopath who had colluded with the demon race long before the protagonist appeared. Treated as pure narrative "setting," it was never elaborated upon; re-reading that "novel" now would yield no clues whatsoever.

When Zhou Hui received the request yesterday, he had uncharacteristically hesitated.

—"Why this particular secret realm?"-Zhou Hui had asked curiously.-"Surely your esteemed villa possesses realms far more perilous or dysfunctional? Why not have me renovate those instead?"

—"Haah, truth be told, Lord Immortal Venerable, that realm makes me feel the most uneasy,"-Villa Master Qingyang had replied with a wry smile.

[—"Uneasy?"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)

—"...Six months ago, during my routine inspection of our secret realms and their warding seals,"-Villa Master Qingyang sighed,-"when my divine sense swept across the 'Spirit Palace Secret Realm,' a violent palpitation gripped my heart without warning. In that split second, I felt as though I would die because of this place..."

Hearing those words, Zhou Hui's brow furrowed.

It seemed the "Heavenly Dao" was indeed grinding along as usual!

Generally, a Mahayana cultivator did not suffer random "palpitations." Especially for an orthodox powerhouse like Villa Master Qingyang, reaching this realm of cultivation granted a subtle resonance with heavenly omens. Even their casual remarks could be subconscious premonitions, and a visceral physical reaction like palpitations strongly hinted that he had caught an echo of the future.

Indeed: his entire clan butchered, himself slain in battle, leaving behind only a single surviving descendant—such a tragedy was more than enough to disturb the spiritual senses of a Mahayana cultivator.

—"A premonition?"-Zhou Hui had murmured.

—"No. If anything, it felt as though it had already happened in the past,"-Villa Master Qingyang remarked.

[—"?!"](<# "Speaker: Zhou Hui">)-Zhou Hui looked at him, startled.

*Happened in the past?* If it had already occurred, how could he still be standing here?

Besides, according to the Heavenly Dao's script, that tragedy belonged to this lifetime... didn't it?

Villa Master Qingyang parted his lips, seemingly trying to articulate the sensation, but found it impossible to put into words. Waving his hand, he sighed:
—"Treat it as premonition or sheer hallucination if you wish. Regardless, that Demon Eye fills me with profound dread. I hope Lord Immortal Venerable can lend a hand to construct a guidance system for this secret realm—not for exploration, but to ensure its superior maintenance!"

—"Mmh."-Zhou Hui displayed hesitation once again.

He wasn't inclined to refuse.

Since Villa Master Qingyang had spoken so earnestly, and Zhou Hui had offered it in exchange for instructing his second disciple, it was unbecoming to back out.

What made him hesitate was whether to let Mo Xi participate.

Since the Qianxing Secret Realm was maintained by Mo Xi, [herding one sheep was no different from herding two](<# "Literal CN: 赶一只羊是赶，赶两只羊也是赶 — doing a task for multiple people requires no more effort than doing it for one">). When instructing Qingyang Qingyu, Zhou Hui naturally brought Mo Xi along, occasionally including Ran Deng, Guan Ye, and Lu Shulong as well.

However, since this secret realm was tied to "the future where Mo Xi colluded with the demon race"—and according to the script, Mo Xi was the catalyst for Eternal Villa's extinction—the theoretically safest course of action was to bar Mo Xi from ever touching this "Spirit Palace Secret Realm."

Yet even though Zhou Hui had diverted fate's trajectory, the secret realm had still arrived at their doorstep just like the Qianxing Secret Realm, practically begging to snuggle up to Xi-er. Would mere quarantine and prohibition keep Mo Xi safe? Wouldn't that just be another farce of [Sleeping Beauty being pricked by a spindle](<# "Reference to the fairy tale Sleeping Beauty, where attempting to ban spindles only ensures the cursed event happens">)?

—"Lord Immortal Venerable, if you feel it inappropriate, I can select another realm,"-Villa Master Qingyang had ventured tentatively, seeing Zhou Hui hesitate for so long.

—"No, it isn't that I find it inappropriate,"-Zhou Hui had shaken his head.-"I merely had a rather audacious idea; I simply wonder if you would be receptive to it."

*

[—"Master?"](<# "Speaker: Mo Xi">)

—"Nothing,"-Zhou Hui said, opening his eyes as he returned from his reverie.

Looking at the two disciples before him, he offered no commentary on the historical background of the labyrinth they had just discussed, nor did he reveal anything regarding the Heavenly Dao's scripted future. Instead, he instructed Qingyu to dive straight into practical application:
—"Very well. Now that we are inside this secret realm, do you know what the first step should be?"

—"Locate a spiritual energy nexus suitable for serving as the central processing unit (CPU)!"-Mo Xi blurted out, jumping to answer first.

—"Incorrect. You must first have a system architecture plan for what you intend to build!"-Qingyang Qingyu countered.-"Only then do you seek a suitable central processing unit based on that blueprint." (Truth be told, Lord Untiring was impeccable in every aspect of his lectures, save for his rather bizarre terminology.)

Thinking this, Qingyu drew a thick stack of papers from her storage pouch:
—"This is the project proposal I drafted for a defensive perimeter alert system. Master Untiring, please review it."

[...Why are you so practiced at this?!]

[I'm the actual direct disciple of the Heavenly Spirit Sect here!]

Mo Xi [puffed up his cheeks like a pufferfish](<# "Literal CN: 鼓成河豚 (gǔ chéng hétún) — modern Chinese meme for pouting or sulking furiously">):
—"That's wrong! Following Master's methodology, one must first identify the requirements before designing a system... Ah, you cheated! You're from the Qingyang clan, so of course you know the requirements beforehand!"

—"This isn't an exam,"-Qingyang Qingyu said with a smile.

Yet her face clearly had the word 'VICTORY' stamped across it.

—"...This system blueprint is quite well-crafted; it's evident you have paid close attention recently,"-Zhou Hui remarked, flipping through the stack of proposals.-"However, regrettably, just as Xi-er pointed out, before drafting a blueprint, one must understand the client's requirements."

Hearing this, Mo Xi instantly puffed up with smug pride.

Qingyu felt somewhat unconvinced:
—"I do know Ancestor's requirements. He has always felt deeply uneasy about this secret realm, so he wants an upgraded maintenance array apparatus. He even hired several array masters to inspect it in the past; my proposal fully satisfies his specifications."

Precisely because of this, when Qingyu had first received the realm, she suspected her ancestor was using her as a front to serve his private ends!

—"However, you do not know *my* requirements,"-Zhou Hui smiled gently.

Qingyu's eyes widened in shock.

Come to think of it, that was true: since Ancestor's ultimate objective was enlisting the Untiring Immortal Venerable to construct the secret realm system, the Immortal Venerable naturally possessed his own concrete vision.

Could his vision surpass whatever she had conceived?

*—Without a shadow of a doubt.*

Even so, Qingyu raised her competitive eyes:
—"Master Untiring, your requirements are...?"

—"I want to smash open the mineral vein below,"-Zhou Hui said with a smile.

—"Eh? But isn't there a Demon Eye underneath?"-Mo Xi asked, bewildered. He and Senior Sister Qingyu had just discussed this; surely Master hadn't failed to overhear them?

—"Correct. We are going to inspect the condition of that Demon Eye. And if there are still demons on the other side, we might as well let them through,"-Zhou Hui said.

[—"?!"](<# "Speakers: Mo Xi & Qingyang Qingyu">)

This time, both Mo Xi and Qingyu goggled their eyes in unison.

Zhou Hui concluded calmly:
—"What I want you two to build is a [Tower Defense System](<# "Literal CN: 塔防系统 (tǎfáng xìtǒng) — modern gaming genre adapted into sect defense architecture">)."
