# Chapter 11: Top Priority

—"How could you two possibly be [secretly studying](# "Literal CN: 偷偷的学习 — classic Chinese high school trope of classmates pretending to slack off while secretly pulling all-nighters to study")?!"-Mo Xi demanded in utter disbelief.

If Mo Xi were forced to pick the two people across the entirety of the Heavenly Spirit Sect who were the absolute laziest when it came to cultivation, it definitely wouldn't be Eldest Senior Brother Yuwen Xuan, who had [cushioned the bottom of the ranks](# "Literal CN: 垫底 (diàndǐ) — being at the very bottom of the score rankings, acting as a cushion for everyone else") in dead last. Nor would it be Eldest Senior Sister Ran Deng, who was practically a bookworm spirit incarnate. No, it would unquestionably be the brother and sister standing right before his eyes.

After all, in order to conduct his business more effectively, Xiang Rong had voluntarily forfeited his qualification to become an inner sect disciple.

This was closely tied to their origins.

The Xiang siblings were 'locals'—the children of mortal mountain folk living in the vicinity of the Heavenly Spirit Sect. Yet unlike other disciples whose spirit roots had been tested and recruited through official channels, these twins had the misfortune of being born in an off-year when the Heavenly Spirit Sect wasn't holding disciple admissions. To make matters worse, a famine struck. Because their family was dirt-poor, their parents prepared to sell Xiang Yi off to a sixty-year-old bachelor in the village.

The moment Xiang Rong caught wind of this, he grabbed Xiang Yi by the hand and fled the village. Back then, the two nine-year-old children trekked several hundred li of rugged mountain paths before finally collapsing near the ancient peach tree by the sect's mountain gate.

Evidently, they hadn't come seeking immortality. Rather, someone back in the village had once frightened them with local ghost tales about the tree, and they had come specifically looking for a place to die.

Fortuitously, the siblings were discovered to possess spirit roots. The elder brother, Xiang Rong, had three spirit roots predominantly focused on Fire, Wood, and Metal, while his younger sister, Xiang Yi, had Metal, Fire, and Wood. For a third-rate little outfit like the Heavenly Spirit Sect, this barely met the threshold for entering the inner sect. However, if they wished to achieve anything significant—such as reaching the Nascent Soul stage or beyond—it would require arduous, dedicated cultivation, mountains of spiritual herbs and medicinal pills (spirit stones), and a little bit of 'good fortune.'

Once the siblings grasped their situation, they calculated shrewdly: one chose to remain in the outer sect while the other entered the inner sect. Cooperating with each other, they leveraged the disparity in resources between the inner and outer sects to run an arbitrage trade, raking in spirit stones.

Poverty had scarred them to the bone. Even after becoming cultivators, they harbored zero yearning for the ethereal Three Thousand Great Daos. They saw themselves as little more than mayflies who were born at dawn and dead by dusk, seeking nothing more than a fleeting measure of peace and security.

And now, these two were actually studying!

Mo Xi felt that if they had been huddling together plotting how to stab him in the back, it would have been vastly easier to stomach!

—"It's just a single Grand Unified Examination!"-Mo Xi demanded, glaring at the pair with bitter exasperation.-"Master never even said the exam scores would affect anything! Why are you two grinding so hard?!"

—"We have no choice but to study!"-Xiang Yi lamented mournfully.-"Everyone else is studying!"

As Xiang Yi spoke, she pulled two bamboo slip ledgers from her drawer. This time, they were genuine accounting books.

Xiang Yi had always been meticulous in business. Not only did she log the sales figures of the test papers, but she had also recorded who the buyers were, how many copies they purchased, and their follow-up feedback and suggestions.

Mo Xi took the ledgers and flipped through them, his expression instantly contorting into something exceedingly complicated.

Because he had been tasked with the logistical preparations for the sect-wide exam over the past few days, he had obtained the Heavenly Spirit Sect's most comprehensive population [Yellow Registers](# "Historical imperial household census ledgers bound in yellow paper; here used for the sect's official personnel registry") from Qu Qianxing. Comparing the two ledgers, it took no effort at all to figure out who had bought the test papers.

And just as Xiang Yi had said, every single disciple across the entire sect who was scheduled to take the exam had bought a copy. What was more—

—"Isn't this Huang Du old Uncle Huang from the Delicacy Pavilion? What is a cook buying test papers for?! And this Zhang Xi—he's just a handyman who tends the spirit herb fields, isn't he? Why the hell is he joining in the frenzy?!"-Mo Xi spluttered.

Xiang Yi had asked them the very same question herself:

—"They said they are members of the Heavenly Spirit Sect too, so they are entitled to take the exam,"-Xiang Yi explained.-"Even if they aren't required to this time, who's to say the Immortal Venerable won't make them take exams in the future?"

—"Never mind cooks and handymen, what about these names? Their names aren't even on the Yellow Registers!"-Mo Xi snapped.

—"Those are rogue cultivators traveling through our sect,"-Xiang Yi replied.-"They only bought them because they revere the Immortal Venerable's great renown."

—"And why are there advance reservations?!"-Mo Xi asked.

—"Those are from our affiliated cultivation clans,"-Xiang Yi answered.-"They're placing advance orders for their own younger generation."

Mo Xi pressed his fingers against his temples, at a complete loss for words.

—"If you are displeased, should I go cancel those advance orders right now?"-Xiang Yi asked, cautiously studying Mo Xi's expression.

—"No..."-Mo Xi muttered. The renovations on the Palace of Silence were already operating at a steep deficit; there was no way in hell he was going to turn away money.

Looking at the ledger, virtually anyone who had the slightest reason to buy a test paper had bought one. This was precisely the outcome Mo Xi had dreamed of, yet why didn't he feel the least bit happy?

Xiang Yi didn't dare say too much, only continuing cautiously:

—"Precisely because everyone is grinding their hearts out, we estimate the sect-wide average score will shoot up by a massive leap compared to last time,"-Xiang Yi explained.-"If I score too low, I'm terrified I might get kicked out of the inner sect."

—"...Master never said that would happen,"-Mo Xi said dryly.

Yet given the obsessive importance Zhou Hui had placed on exams lately, even Mo Xi couldn't fully guarantee that such a disaster wouldn't come to pass.

—"Whatever! If you want to study, go ahead and study!"-Mo Xi snapped, turning away from Xiang Yi and glaring at Xiang Rong instead.-"What about you, then? You certainly don't need to worry about getting expelled from the sect!"

After all, as an outer sect disciple, there was nowhere left to demote him.

In the cultivation world, the 'disciple' aspect weighed far heavier for inner sect disciples. They were regarded as true students; yet because they hadn't formally apprenticed under a personal master, if their academic performance was abysmal, getting booted from the inner sect was a very real possibility.

Outer sect disciples, on the other hand, were virtually indistinguishable from handymen. It was essentially a 'job,' not unlike a state-owned enterprise in another world—though the compensation and benefits were vastly inferior. As long as a disciple didn't commit an egregious crime, they generally wouldn't be expelled from the sect.

Xiang Rong could [lie flat](# "Literal CN: 躺平 (tǎngpíng) — modern Chinese youth philosophy of refusing to participate in the hyper-competitive rat race") with total peace of mind.

—"I don't have to worry about being expelled, sure,"-Xiang Rong smiled wryly,-"but if my exam score is too abysmal, it might ruin my future business!"

[—"Ruin your business?"](# "Speaker: Mo Xi")

—"Exactly! After all, I'm the one hawking the exam prep papers,"-Xiang Rong explained helplessly.-"If I end up [cushioning the bottom of the ranks](# "Literal CN: 垫底 (diàndǐ) — being at the very bottom of the score rankings, acting as a cushion for everyone else") in dead last, even though the exam papers I sell have nothing to do with my own wits, people will still question their credibility! In fact, not just the test papers—they'll start doubting the efficacy of the spiritual pills and magical artifacts I sell too!"

That actually made... an infuriating amount of sense!

For the first time in his life, Mo Xi found himself refuted by the Xiang siblings until he hadn't a single word in rebuttal.

His face flushed beet-red. He clenched his fists, unclenched them, and finally whipped around with a violent flick of his sleeve.

—"Do whatever you want!"-Mo Xi barked.

With that, Mo Xi stormed out of Xiang Yi's quarters.

The Xiang siblings exchanged a bewildered glance. Yet within a fraction of a second, Xiang Yi picked up the problem set once more.

[—"Shall we continue?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Yi")

[—"Let's."](# "Speaker: Xiang Rong")

—"The formation of the Abyssal Ocean occurred during the...!"-Xiang Yi began reciting.

Xiang Yi was right in the middle of reading the question when a loud *BANG* echoed from the doorway. She looked up, only to find Mo Xi standing by the threshold once again, glaring at her with his mismatched cat-like eyes.

The fine hairs on the back of Xiang Yi's neck stood on end. By sheer instinct, she tried to hide the problem set behind her back again.

Mo Xi, however, simply held out an open palm.

—"Hand them over!"-Mo Xi demanded.

[—"What?"](# "Speaker: Xiang Yi")

—"Those new test papers you got your hands on, plus that problem set—duplicate a copy for me,"-Mo Xi demanded.-"...No, wait. Every single test paper and reference answer sheet you have. Give me a copy of all of them!"

The Xiang siblings stared in silence.

Neither Xiang Rong nor Xiang Yi was a fool. They didn't dare ask why Mo Xi wanted these study materials, merely working at top speed to duplicate everything he requested onto jade slips before handing them over.

Then, with utmost deference, they saw off this jinx of a demon whose only redeeming quality was his handsome face.

---

—"Master! Youngest Junior Brother has started studying!"-Lu Shulong announced excitedly, bounding right up before Zhou Hui.

Zhou Hui watched his fifth disciple, Lu Shulong, dash eagerly to his side.

This disciple of his was also a breathtaking beauty.

Yet unlike Qu Qianxing's bright, striking elegance, Lu Shulong belonged to the melancholic aesthetic.

He likely possessed a trace of demon-beast bloodline, which accounted for his silken, silvery-blue hair. Set against his exquisite features, however, it didn't look bizarre in the slightest. His slender, willow-leaf brows seemed perpetually knit in gentle sorrow, his pale glazed-glass eyes were ever shrouded in a soft mist, and a delicate beauty mark rested beneath the corner of his left eye, giving him a naturally gloomy, tragic aura.

Perhaps influenced by this very countenance, the Heavenly Dao had maliciously scripted a melancholic fate to match his looks:

When the Heavenly Spirit Sect was annihilated in the original plot, Lu Shulong had originally been dispatched by Qu Qianxing to evacuate the treasures in the alchemy chambers and storerooms. Yet upon hearing the news that Zhou Hui had been slain, Lu Shulong ignited the alchemy chambers with True Samadhi Fire, burying himself alongside the Heavenly Spirit Sect. In doing so, he had conveniently constrained the protagonist's loot to a reasonable threshold that prevented the [progression web novel](# "Literal CN: 升级文 (shēngjí wén) — web novel genre focused on steady leveling up, stat increases, and power progression")'s power economy from collapsing.

In truth, however, anyone who actually interacted with Lu Shulong quickly realized that his personality bore zero relation to his looks.

Lu Shulong loved to laugh. He possessed a bright, sunny grin that flashed a prominent canine tooth—a smile that almost felt like an insult to his tragic beauty, yet carried such infectious warmth that anyone who saw it couldn't help but smile along.

At the very least, from an elder's perspective, Zhou Hui much preferred a smiling Lu Shulong.

Zhou Hui smiled warmly as well.

—"How do you know that child has started reviewing?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"I was just passing by the Palace of Silence when I saw Youngest Junior Brother hiding in a corner [secretly studying](# "Literal CN: 偷偷的学习 — classic Chinese high school trope of classmates pretending to slack off while secretly pulling all-nighters to study")!"-Lu Shulong reported gleefully.-"He looked so incredibly earnest that I didn't want to disturb him. He'll definitely get a fantastic score on the next exam!"

[...Whether Mo Xi's scores turn out well or not remains to be seen, but your relationship with him is certainly wonderful.]

Zhou Hui mused with an amused smile.

Up to this point, his second, third, fourth, and fifth disciples had all spoken up on their youngest junior brother's behalf, either directly or indirectly. It appeared that despite Mo Xi's laundry list of terrible habits, his popularity among his martial siblings was genuinely beyond reproach.

Granted, it was precisely because the direct disciples of the Heavenly Spirit Sect were so close-knit that the tragic spectacle of [Calabash Brothers saving Grandpa](# "Literal CN: 葫芦娃救爷爷 — Chinese pop-culture meme from the classic animation 'Calabash Brothers', describing foolishly rushing in one by one to rescue someone, only to be picked off and captured sequentially") had occurred in the original timeline, allowing the enemy to wipe them out root and branch. But that wasn't their fault.

Zhou Hui was still thoroughly pleased with the harmonious camaraderie among his disciples.

Pondering whether he ought to give his youngest disciple a graceful way out, Zhou Hui turned toward Mo Bei, who was standing nearby:

—"Then Xi'er should be just about regretting taking on so many tasks by now, shouldn't he?"-Zhou Hui asked.

—"Eh? Ah!"-Mo Bei started, blinking blankly.

She had been spacing out, reacting a beat too slow to realize Zhou Hui was speaking to her.

Mo Bei was Mo Xi's twin sister, possessing a face virtually identical to his, save for the fact that the colors of their heterochromatic eyes were mirrored.

Yet unlike Mo Xi's lively expressiveness, Mo Bei appeared completely blank and dazed—like a lifeless porcelain doll. Exquisite as she was, her vacancy gave the impression that she was devoid of all worldly passions and mortal desires.

Looking at her vacant, unresponsive state, it was almost impossible to fathom that in the future, she would become so utterly [lovestruck](# "Literal CN: 恋爱脑 (liàn'ài nǎo) — internet slang for someone completely blinded by infatuation, tossing aside reason, loyalty, and morals") over a man that she would tear her own life apart—willingly joining his harem, betraying the Heavenly Spirit Sect for him, and even divulging her master's fatal weaknesses to the enemy.

Though strictly speaking, the foreshadowing wasn't entirely absent.

Right now, for instance, Mo Bei threw her younger brother under the bus without a second's hesitation:

—"Mo Xi has not engaged in any self-reflection,"-Mo Bei stated flatly.-"Not only that, he has devised a renovation plan that cuts corners on materials to siphon spirit stones. He clearly requires further discipline from Master."

—"What sort of plan?"-Zhou Hui asked with keen interest.

Mo Bei provided an exhaustive explanation of Mo Xi's scheme for completing the sect missions.

Upon hearing the details, Zhou Hui found himself caught between amusement and dismay:

—"That child certainly doesn't lack clever tricks,"-Zhou Hui remarked, chuckling softly.-"The idea itself isn't bad at all. Pity his cultivation level is... hah. Well, letting him taste a bit of hardship won't do him any harm."

Hearing this, Mo Bei promptly lapsed back into her doll-like standby mode.

Lu Shulong shrugged his shoulders, silently observing a moment of mournful silence for their youngest junior brother in his heart, just as Ran Deng and Guan Ye had before him.

[Youngest Junior Brother would be better off strung up in the ancient peach tree like Eldest Senior Brother! At least that way, death would be quick and clean.]

Lu Shulong's own academic grades were terrible as well, so he didn't dare say another word on the matter. Besides, neither he nor Mo Bei had come here today because of Mo Xi; they had come for the 'proper business' their master had assigned:

—"Master, regarding the Grand Unified Examination at the Palace of Silence... do we need to keep it confidential from the guests?"-Lu Shulong asked.

[—"Guests?"](# "Speaker: Zhou Hui")

—"The guests from the Heavenly Dao Alliance,"-Lu Shulong explained.-"I have already confirmed the schedule with them. They will be arriving on the exact day of our Grand Unified Examination. But with an event like the Grand Unified Examination, and with Youngest Junior Brother's grand plan making things so... elaborate, I fear it might cause a stir among those guests."

—"It matters not,"-Zhou Hui replied coolly.

The fact that they were arriving right on the day of the Grand Unified Examination was almost certainly no mere 'coincidence.' In that case, whether it startled them or not was entirely beside the point.

—"Receive them as you normally would,"-Zhou Hui instructed.-"If they don't ask, act as though nothing out of the ordinary is happening. If they express interest in the Grand Unified Examination, permit them to observe. However, no matter what happens, the Grand Unified Examination remains our top priority. Do not allow a handful of 'guests' to disrupt our disciples' examinations."

—"Understood,"-Lu Shulong and Mo Bei replied in unison.
